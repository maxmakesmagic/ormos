Longtime **Magic Online** player Dracc0n put his own spin on Modern control in a recent Premier Event with an Isochron Scepter control deck! With a wide array of control elements and several utility spells to put on a Scepter, Dracc0n sliced through the competition and took down the tournament. Could this be a new force in Modern? Check out the deck for yourself!

Dracc0n's Scepter Control 

Modern – Magic Online Premier Event #3314381
