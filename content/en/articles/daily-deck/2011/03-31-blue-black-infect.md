Blue-Black Control may have won the Barcelona Grand Prix, but perhaps the deck that is the most fun is Blue-Black Infect. Championed by pros like Brian Kibler, the deck uses a blue-black control core with cards like Doom Blade and Mana Leak to control the game, but finishes the opponent off with poison. How? Skithiryx, the Blight Dragon or Phyrexian Crusader. Look for your control opponents to begin using the strategy against you at Friday Night **Magic**! Read more about Grand Prix Barcelona in the [official event coverage](/en/events/2011/gpbar11/scheinin-beats-them-black-and-blue).
