![](https://i.ormos.uk/8b/8f/8b8f621c665e1aaf35101fefa1d38cef6449cf8b21a100bfea6462178e5f76f8.jpg)

In the first round of quick questions this weekend, Japanese Legend Kenji Tsumura boldly stated that the relatively unknown amateur Yuuya Watanabe would win this Grand Prix… and wouldn't you know it? He was right. With a huge [Demonfire](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonfire) off the top, Watanabe snatched the last game of the match away from Yuusuke Iwasaki to become the Grand Prix Kyoto Champion. Watanabe was last seen heading due east carried high on the shoulders of his many friends and supporters.

## Sunday, Mar 18: 6:51 p.m. - Quarterfinals Recap

*by blisterguy*

  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/72/0a/720ad27297bec64f8f94e2bc21636deda97cc26b629a65a61eec7d52ba8f3e12.jpg)*Motokiyo Azuma*

The first match finished was between Ren Ishikawa and Tsubasa Tomii. In both games, Tomii was able to swing until lethal with a [Phyrexian Totem](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Totem), Ishikawa unable to locate enough instant speed removal to neutralize the threats. The final straw fell with a [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand) delayed [Detritivore](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detritivore) just long enough for the Totem to run past for the kill.

Tsubasa Tomii defeats Ren Ishikawa 2-0

Motokiyo Azuma managed to get the Pickles lock in place to take Game 1 from Atsushi Wada with little meaningful resistance. In Game 2, Wada tried to set up a [Detritivore](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detritivore) for two, but managed to get the worst of it when Azuma redirected each one of it's triggers with his Shapeshifter and [Willbender](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Willbender) duo. Wada tried to recover from this disaster with a [Wrath of God](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wrath+of+God), only to have that also taken from him by a [Draining Whelk](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Draining+Whelk).

Motokiyo Azuma defeats Atsushi Wada 2-0

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c5/e4/c5e4a95de9e55c2f49831ce2960624717c85e8ddf486952f09530923e926cc7f.jpg)*Yuusuke Iwasaki*

In the match of the Urzatrons, Yuuya Watanabe got off to an early start with a [Sulfur Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sulfur+Elemental) getting feisty. Anything Naoki Shimizu threw down to stop it was either [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand)ed or [Repeal](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Repeal)ed, but that can only delay the defense for so long. However, the damage had been done and a [Demonfire](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonfire) finished him off. Both players succeeded in getting their 'Trons assembled in Game 2, but Watanabe robbed Shimizu of his with a timely [Annex](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Annex). Shimizu started grinding away at Watanabe's hand with a [Muse Vessel](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Muse+Vessel), but could only shake his head at the steady stream of [Compulsive Research](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsive+Research)es and [Tidings](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidings) Watanabe seemed to be drawing. Eventually, Watanabe dropped a [Bogardan Hellkite](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bogardan+Hellkite) and when Shimizu tapped out to [Chord of Calling](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chord+of+Calling) up an answer, Watanabe took the spell and the match with a [Commandeer](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Commandeer).

Yuuya Watanabe defeats Naoki Shimizu 2-0

A triple mulligan won Game 1 for Iwasaki, Ide being completely unable to mount an offensive that meant anything in the face of [Loxodon Hierarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Hierarch) and friends. Another slow start from Ide made things look bleak until he topdecked a [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain) for a third turn [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon), locking Iwasaki out of the second game. Ide's offence in Game 3 was shut down by an [Orzhov Pontiff](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Orzhov+Pontiff), giving Iwasaki all the time he needed to get him larger army online and marching all over Ide's for the win.

Yuusuke Iwasaki defeats Katsuhiro Ide 2-1

Against Motokiyo Azuma Yuuya, Yuuya Watanabe continued his trend of getting in early with [Sulfur Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sulfur+Elemental)s, attacking with a pair of them and again replying to the potential solutions with more [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand)s and [Repeal](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Repeal)s. Watanabe still succeeded in clearing a path for it though, including killing a [Teferi, Mage of Zhalfir](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%2C+Mage+of+Zhalfir) with one of his own. ## Sunday, Mar 18: 9:38 p.m. - The Finals

*by Event Coverage Staff*

  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/19/1b/191b8cb091ae478852eb78e96119232b280c43274e3d805fddfe950ca22833ec.jpg)

And now finally, I get to sit down and catch my breath. Trying to cover all of the Quarterfinals and Semifinals at once is no easy task, and wouldn't have been possible with the help of local Westerner and good man about town, Eli Kaplan.

As an amateur in the finals, Yuuya Watanabe is already going to make out big here this evening, but after the amateur win in Amsterdam last weekend, he's got to have his eyes on the prize as well. Curiously enough, Watanabe was also singled out earlier this weekend by Kenji Tsumura as the person who would win this tournament. Yuusuke Iwasaki is no amateur, but is no doubt eyeing up the trophy just as much as Watanabe is.

Iwasaki got the start he would want, with a [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant) and a [Saffi Eriksdotter](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Saffi+Eriksdotter) dealing damage while Watanabe was still developing his mana. A [Castigate](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Castigate) denied Watanabe a [Repeal](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Repeal), leaving him chain casting [Compulsive Research](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsive+Research)es in search of an answer. With Watanabe's mana tapped down, Iwasaki also pushed a [Loxodon Hierarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Hierarch) into play to wind it up a notch, and then harried Watanabe further with another [Castigate](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Castigate). Counting seven mana in play and another land in hand, Iwasaki wasted no time in removing a [Bogardan Hellkite](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bogardan+Hellkite) from the game. Yet another Compulsive research and an [Electrolyze](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrolyze) later, Watanabe still hadn't found an answer to Iwasaki's beaters and was forced to shuffle up for Game 2.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2a/9c/2a9cf4d8dec000efbb9a3073f67bd37497d9289db90c674c4c60006bba59ab84.jpg)*Yuusuke Iwasaki*

Watanabe started this one with an Urza's Tower, and an [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island). Iwasaki on the other hand, practically dumped his hand on the table within the first two turns, playing out an [Elves of Deep Shadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elves+of+Deep+Shadow), a [Wall of Roots](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Roots) and good ol' [Saffi Eriksdotter](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Saffi+Eriksdotter). Not content just attacking on the ground, Iwasaki also went for the hand with a [Castigate](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Castigate), taking a [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand) to allow [Teysa, Orzhov Scion](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teysa%2C+Orzhov+Scion) to make it into play as well.

Things were looking grim for Watanabe. He was down a game and facing an offensive force of females that threatened to finish the tournament some time in the very near future. Iwasaki played out the last two cards in his hand, an [Essence Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Essence+Warden) and the [Crypt Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crypt+Champion) that would combine with Saffi to give Iwasaki as much life as he could ever want. From what seemed to be out of nowhere, Watanabe had a [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) for the Champion. On Iwasaki's next attack step, a [Sulfur Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sulfur+Elemental) sprang forth, killing a Teysa token and crushing the [Essence Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Essence+Warden). Another Elemental later and the board was suddenly clear. Iwasaki's draw steps yielded land, while Watanabe promptly played an end of turn Hellkite, untapped and attacked with it an finished the surprised Iwasaki off with a [Demonfire](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonfire).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5a/5d/5a5dde3654d3a60454c1b40aacbf2773b4195cfa83ac57ba6835485fa665f924.jpg)*Yuuya Watanabe*

So the scores were now even. Iwasaki again came out with an early Saffi, and a duo of [Castigate](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Castigate)s snatching a matching pair of Serated Arrows. Two [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise) were making Iwasaki's attack force look much less formidable, so when he played his last move, a [Persecute](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Persecute), right into a [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak), he was once again playing off the top. Watanabe then killed the two Birds with one [Electrolyze](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrolyze), drawing into his Urzatron set and ending the turn with naught but a [Demonfire](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonfire) in hand. Mind you, that's a pretty good spell to have when you have a great deal of mana at your disposal, you have to admit.

In a crushing blow, Iwasaki ripped a third [Castigate](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Castigate) off the top to knock the [Demonfire](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonfire) out of the Watanabe's hand, leaving him visibly frustrated. A couple of turns later, Iwasaki had drawn a [Ghost Council of Orzhova](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Council+of+Orzhova) that would leave Watanabe very low on life, and placed it on the stack. Suddenly, a [Bogardan Hellkite](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bogardan+Hellkite) appeared on the stack over it, attempting to clear the board of any potential victims of the Ghost Council. Saffi bravely saved the [Elves of Deep Shadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elves+of+Deep+Shadow) from a fiery death, meaning Watanabe was still not out of the woods yet. He untapped, drew his card and began to count his mana. The Hellkite flew over for a bite, and a huge [Demonfire](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207053819/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonfire) off the top finished Yuusuke Iwasaki off, giving Yuuya Watanabe the win, the second Grand Prix won by an amateur in a row.

Yuuya Watanabe defeats Yuusuke Iwasaki 2-1 to become the Grand Prix Kyoto 2007 champion.
