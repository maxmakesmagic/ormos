Pro Tour Champion and Player of the Year Bob Maher's had plenty of success in his career, and he shows no sign of stopping. In his way is Pierre "Caribou" Malherbaud, one of France's best. Malherbaud bested William Jensen in the first round, who was playing [Tinker](https://web.archive.org/web/20030508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tinker) just like Maher. He's confident in his Sligh deck. 

### Game 1

A mulligan for Malherbaud, and they were off. Maher started with island and Key. Malherbaud showed mountain and Lackey. Maher betrayed none of the weakness in his hand, playing Monolith and a second Key. Malherbaud summoned the pain, Lackeying out a Piledriver, then playing [Jackal Pup](https://web.archive.org/web/20030508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jackal+Pup) and [Seal of Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20030508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seal+of+Fire). Maher needed something, and fast. He had [Upheaval](https://web.archive.org/web/20030508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Upheaval) in hand, but decided to wait another turn and eat the six damage. He dropped a Dynamo and passed it back.

Malherbaud bashed in and dropped Fanatic and Port. Maher untapped, drew, and calmly laid [Masticore](https://web.archive.org/web/20030508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Masticore).

"Look what I drew." – Bob Maher

Malherbaud gave him a golf clap for his accomplishment. With a million mana on board, Maher was able to ride [Masticore](https://web.archive.org/web/20030508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Masticore) to victory.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d8/9e/d89e19573e48c5aac3ca57834ca7db502ab98c890c6be258a35e96c47c654316.jpg)

"You're much better than Jensen, he never drew that one." – Pierre Malherbaud

Maher 1 – Malherbaud 0

### Game 2

Malherbaud mulliganed a sketchy one-lander into a much better one-lander. Maher also mulliganed. Malherbaud again opened with Lackey, but Maher had the [Seal of Removal](https://web.archive.org/web/20030508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seal+of+Removal) to slow it down. Malherbaud replayed it, showing he hadn't draw a second land.

Maher exploded. He dropped [City of Traitors](https://web.archive.org/web/20030508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=City+of+Traitors), then [Grim Monolith](https://web.archive.org/web/20030508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Monolith), then [Tinker](https://web.archive.org/web/20030508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tinker) for Dynamo, and finally [Masticore](https://web.archive.org/web/20030508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Masticore). Malherbaud still didn't have a second land. He nodded sagely.

"Much better than Jensen." – Pierre Malherbaud

The farce was mercifully short-lived.

**Final Result:** Bob Maher defeats Pierre Malherbaud 2-0
