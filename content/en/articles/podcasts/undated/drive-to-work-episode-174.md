**Episode 174**: Meeting Expectations (15.0 MB)

One of the jobs of design is to make things that match what the audience expects of the design. In today's podcast, I talk about this element of the design process.

*Magic: The Gathering* Head Designer Mark Rosewater shares stories, insights, and more while driving to work. Listen in and learn more about *Magic*!
