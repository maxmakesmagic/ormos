# Play *Streets of New Capenna* Now!

[*Streets of New Capenna*](https://web.archive.org/web/20220504000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/streets-of-new-capenna) is available now on *MTG Arena*! Catch up on the latest set with the [Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2022/03-24-streets-of-new-capenna-card-image-gallery), then hop in to play today. Plus, you can get started with the latest cards using the code "PlaySNC" to receive three Store packs of *Streets of New Capenna*.

# Alchemy Rebalancing Arrives Tomorrow

The newest Alchemy rebalancing changes arrive this week on May 5. Check out [the complete rebalancing announcement](/en/articles/uncategorised/2022/05-04-alchemy-rebalancing-for-may-5-2022) for all the cards and changes coming to Alchemy and Historic tomorrow.

This game update will also include fixes for many of the *Streets of New Capenna* card-related issues that were identified during *Streets of New Capenna* Early Access and over the weekend.

# Explore More for Explorer

The Explorer format has launched. The newest format to *MTG Arena* is live with events and other ways to play. Learn all about the format in [the latest State of the Game article](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/04-21-mtg-arena-state-of-the-game-streets-of-new-capenna) and jump in with the Explorer Metagame Challenge later this week, May 6–9. Play Best-of-Three matches to claim gold and *Streets of New Capenna* packs!

# Reach the *Magic* World Championship

You can now qualify for the *Magic* World Championship in *MTG Arena*! The new Premier Play system offers players paths to reach the highest levels of competitive *Magic*. Check out [*MTG Arena* Premier Play in 2022](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/04-21-mtg-arena-premier-play-in-2022) for complete details.

# Arena Open May 14–15

The next Arena Open is next week, featuring *Streets of New Capenna* Sealed and Player Draft. You can practice now with the new cards to prepare for the event, which will feature two formats:

*   Day 1 – S*treets of New Capenna* Sealed (Best-of-One and Best-of-Three)
*   Day 2 – *Streets of New Capenna* Player Draft (Best-of-Three)

For this Arena Open we've adjusted some of the gem rewards from Day 2 to Day 1, giving more players more rewards even sooner:

*   Gem rewards on Day 2 for earning 0-5 wins have all been reduced by 5000 gems.
*   Earning 7 wins on Day 1 in Best-of-One now also awards 5000 gems in addition to Day 2 access.
*   Earning 4 wins on Day 1 in Best-of-Three now also awards 6000 gems in addition to Day 2 access.
*   Earning one win on Day 1 in Best-of-Three now awards 1500 gems, up from 1000.

Check out [the May 2022 Arena Open article](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/05-04-may-arena-open-streets-of-new-capenna-mixed-limited) for full event details to be ready!

# *MTG Arena*'s First Play-In Event

Our first [Qualifier Play-In event](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in), which can earn you an invitation to this month's Qualifier Weekend, is set for May 21! Compete in Best-of-One Phantom *Streets of New Capenna* Sealed matches for prizes, and reach six wins for an invite to the May 28–29 Qualifier Weekend!

What can you qualify for? Learn about the $200,000 Arena Championship with our [*MTG Arena* Premier Play announcement](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/04-21-mtg-arena-premier-play-in-2022) to learn all the ways you can secure a seat at a Qualifier Weekend.

# Family Means Business—and Festivals!

Get ready to fete the five crime families of *Streets of New Capenna*! The [Rise of the Five Families Festival](https://web.archive.org/web/20220504000000/https://magic.wizards.com/mtga-snc-rise-of-the-families) explores each family's particular way of doing business and exposes how they ascended to the ritzy heights of New Capenna's upper crust.

What's in it for you? Here's the deal: you can score golden age showcase card styles that'll prove you're a big shot and not some chump with a lot of chin music.

Each event's format is Alchemy Singleton with Best-of-One matches, but with a unique emblem twist inspired by each family!

The first two events feature the Maestros and the Obscura back to back.

**Rise of the Maestros: May 12–15**

*   Emblem: "Once during each of your turns, you may cast an instant or sorcery spell from your graveyard by sacrificing a creature in addition to paying its other costs. If a spell cast this way would be put into your graveyard, exile it instead."
