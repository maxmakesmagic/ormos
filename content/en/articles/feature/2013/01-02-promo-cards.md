Okay, enough fooling around. It's Wednesday already, and we haven't had any *Gatecrash* preview cards in Arcana. This will not stand! Today, we have the best kind of preview cards: alternate-art event promos!

### Prerelease Cards

When you go to [your local prerelease](https://web.archive.org/web/20130102000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prerelease-facts), you'll have a choice of five guilds: Orzhov, Dimir, Gruul, Boros, and Simic. When you select your guild, you'll get a Guild Prerelease Pack, which will contain five *Gatecrash* booster packs, one booster pack for your specific guild, an achievement card, a Spindown Life Counter, a letter from your new guildmaster, a guild sticker, and an alternate-art foil Prerelease card. And you'll be able to play your Prerelease card in your Prerelease deck! So here's what each guild is getting.

### Launch Weekend

*Gatecrash* goes on sale on Friday, February 1, 2013. And that means [Friday Night **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20130102000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=events/magic/fnm) has a special card for everyone, while supplies last!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/bf/12/bf12b31a0f36ba6bfaf4ec38b72428c07c6b1fe1fc0e2579e9ffd8970ffa158c.jpg)

### Buy-a-Box

At participating stores, there's a special promotional card for the first people to reserve a whole box of *Gatecrash*!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9b/78/9b7816bd0153a987dddb0bfb1929df6c691e17ff091eaa90347b2dfa8c801a8d.jpg)

### League

Once *Gatecrash* is on sale (on February 1, which we already told you about), the [*Gatecrash* League](https://web.archive.org/web/20130102000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/league-facts) will start at your local store. And at the end, there will be cool prizes including this cool Soldier token!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d4/70/d470e843b464b88d9636b336f05876b66a912a02ba1d7279d6dc9e7b187c08f7.jpg)

### Game Day

[*Gatecrash* Game Day](https://web.archive.org/web/20130102000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/gameday-facts) is February 23-24, 2013. And while supplies last, all participants will receive Zameck Guildmage.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/90/25/90250754d9954589fc8f06ff06bbd62a8c49d9676f795e14e04f99c43bd51a3e.jpg)

But that's not all! Because the Top 8 will receive this full-art Firemane Avenger!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d0/91/d0915899da37b04a5f578dbf525dc248064630f9032356d02b2b6cdb528c0ee4.jpg)
