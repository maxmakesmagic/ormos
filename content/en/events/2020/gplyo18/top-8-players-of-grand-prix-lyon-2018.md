After fifteen rounds of Modern, with a total of 2,050 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gplyo18/gplyo_t8_kowalski.jpg)

**Name:** Grzegorz Kowalski

**Age:** 26

**Occupation:** Wannabe *Magic* pro

**Hometown:** Wroclaw, Poland

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Four Grand Prix Top 8s, two Pro Tour Top 16s, second at the 2017 World *Magic* Cup with Team Poland.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Red-Green "Fork" Eldrazi. It's a great deck that we figured out before the Pro Tour. In the end, nobody from the team played it, except for me. Deck knows that I trusted it and is paying off dividends.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Forked Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forked+Bolt). Without it the deck doesn't exist!

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Engineered Explosives](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engineered+Explosives). Mass removal is great. It's also fun to kill [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) with Explosives playing only a two-color deck.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gplyo18/gplyo_t8_carvalho.jpg)

**Name:** Márcio Carvalho

**Age:** 32

**Occupation:** Platinum pro

**Hometown:** Lisbon, Portugal

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Three Pro Tour Top 8s, a ton of Grand Prix Top 8s, Top 4 at MOCS, World Championship finals.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Abzan, because I like the playstyle.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Liliana of the Veil](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+of+the+Veil), just too powerful.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Tireless Tracker](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tracker), grind master.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gplyo18/gplyo_t8_bausch.jpg)

**Name:** Joshua Bausch

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Nurse

**Hometown:** Frankfurt am Main, Germany

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Third at Nationals 2017.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Mono-Green Tron. I think it was the best metagame call and I tested it a lot with friends for the Pro Tour.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulamog%2C+the+Ceaseless+Hunger). It was there when it was needed.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Grafdigger's Cage](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grafdigger%27s+Cage). I argued with my friends and ended up the only one to run it. Never boarded it once.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gplyo18/gplyo_t8_cappiello.jpg)

**Name:** Davide Cappiello

**Age:** 23

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Turin, Italy

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Some PPTQ wins, some Grand Prix Day 2s, PTQ victory on GP Madrid's Sunday.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Tron is too powerful, only big stuff.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Karn Liberated](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn+Liberated), 7 mana and it will also make the coffee for you.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

I don't need any card in my sideboard.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gplyo18/gplyo_t8_juric.jpg)

**Name:** David Juric

**Age:** 25

**Occupation:** Student?

**Hometown:** Maastricht, Netherlands, from Slovenia originally

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

12-3 at Grand Prix London in Limited, 10-5 at Grand Prix Birmingham in Modern.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Jeskai Control, not Tempo.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Secure the Wastes](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Secure+the+Wastes), as a burn spell // chump blockers split card. I won multiple games due to 4–8 surprise damage from the tokens.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Izzet Staticaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Izzet+Staticaster) single-handedly won a match against Black-Red [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One) by blanking [Bloodghast](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodghast) and making [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One)s die to [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt)/[Lightning Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Helix).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gplyo18/gplyo_t8_de-togni.jpg)

**Name:** Marco De Togni

**Age:** 29

**Occupation:** Project manager

**Hometown:** Bologna, Italy

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Three Pro Tour appearances, Top 8 at Italian Nationals.

Abzan. I'll always play what Reid Duke plays.

[Liliana of the Veil](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+of+the+Veil). Just too good against so many decks.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gplyo18/gplyo_t8_penko.jpg)

**Name:** Matic Penko

**Age:** 31

**Occupation:** Pro slacker

**Hometown:** Ljubljana, Slovenia

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Slovenian World *Magic* Cup team member twice, with one Top 16 finish.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Big Tron. I was playing it before you guys decided to ban my [Eye of Ugin](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eye+of+Ugin), and since Lantern won the Pro Tour I was hoping it would be popular.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

The one and only [Karn Liberated](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn+Liberated), but only on turn three, or [Expedition Map](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Expedition+Map) when I had two Tron parts.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Nature's Claim](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nature%27s+Claim) to kill opponents' [Stony Silence](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stony+Silence). [Thought-Knot Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thought-Knot+Seer) was awesome as well, but when [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20180218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) is legal I'm not sure how good it is going to be.
