Go to the "Selecting *Eight Edition*" promo page [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20020725000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/vote8e/home).

I received an email from one of my old **Magic** [teammates](https://web.archive.org/web/20020725000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=sideboard/strategy/20020716a) regarding the results of one of the previous *Eighth Edition* votes a few weeks ago. "I strongly suspect this is fixed," he said, hinting that the "weaker" card winning was not a realistic possibility in his eyes.

You hurt my feelings. If R&D wanted a particular card in or out of the set, they'd just put it in, and there wouldn't even be a vote. There's no elaborate setup here, folks; all there results are pure 100% public opinion.

That said, hang on to your [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20020725000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise).

You have spoken, and by a 2-to-1 differential, Birds and [Vine Trellis](https://web.archive.org/web/20020725000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vine+Trellis) beat out [Llanowar Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20020725000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Elves) and [Utopia Tree](https://web.archive.org/web/20020725000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Utopia+Tree). [Vine Trellis](https://web.archive.org/web/20020725000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vine+Trellis) will be going into Eighth as the Elves' replacement. Keith Parkinson's [Spined Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020725000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spined+Wurm) also cruised to victory.

This week's vote pits two cards against one another that are really quite similar, except one has an extra line of text that tacks ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless2.gif) onto the mana cost. So the question boils down to… Uncounterable or no?

*Ice Age*'s [Jokulhaups](https://web.archive.org/web/20020725000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jokulhaups) vs. *Invasion*'s [Obliterate](https://web.archive.org/web/20020725000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Obliterate). Choose your favorite red reset. Should be interesting.

The flavor text submission will be run a little differently. This week, we're accepting submissions for [persecute](https://web.archive.org/web/20020725000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=persecute), which will feature D. Alexander Gregory's art from *Urza's Saga*. But this time, we want *real-world literary quotes*. Here's a chance for all you real-world fans to shine.

Two rules:

*   The flavor text should be from a source at least 100 years old, for copyright reasons. The older, the better.
*   The source should be clearly attributed, so it can be verified and credited.

You can submit your quote [here](#).

This week's art choice is for a blue enchantment, [Steal Artifact](https://web.archive.org/web/20020725000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steal+Artifact). The choice is between John Coulthart's *Fifth Edition* piece, and Peter Bollinger's from *Seventh Edition*.
