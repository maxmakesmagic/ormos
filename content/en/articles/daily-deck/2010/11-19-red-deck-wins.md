Red Deck Wins is a famous name for red decks that run the gamut from beatdown to burn oriented. Sigurd Eskeland used this version, heavy on burn, to Top 16 the European Championships. The deck was designed by John Ormerod, and was later made famous by Dan Paskins. Learn more about the latest burn deck, *Premium Deck Series: Fire & Lightning*, [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20110216023038/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/ProductArticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/pdsfirelightning/productinfo).
