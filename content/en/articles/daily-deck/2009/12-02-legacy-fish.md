Team China emerged from the 2009 Team World Championships the victor in Rome, Italy. The team competition featured three member teams competing in one format a piece: Standard, Extended, and Legacy. For China, it was Bo Li battling Legacy and his deck of choice was a Fish build. A powerful strategy featuring cheap blue Merfolk backed by Lords and counters, he was very successful despite not having played much Legacy prior to the event itself. For more information on the 2009 World Championships, [visit the official event coverage](/en/events/2009/worlds09/all-roads-lead-to-victory-for-coimbra-china).
