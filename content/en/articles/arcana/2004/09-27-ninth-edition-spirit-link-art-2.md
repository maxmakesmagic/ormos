In the [Selecting *Ninth Edition* Wrap-up article](https://web.archive.org/web/20040927000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/selecting9e/wrapup) you saw the final art for [Dancing Scimitar](https://web.archive.org/web/20040927000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dancing+Scimitar) and [Mahamoti Djinn](https://web.archive.org/web/20040927000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mahamoti+Djinn). As you can see, they turned out terrific; if you voted on the Selecting *Ninth Edition* sketches over the last few months, they just prove how good an art director you were. 

  
*Ron Spears' [Dancing Scimitar](https://web.archive.org/web/20040927000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dancing+Scimitar) and Greg Staples' [Mahamoti Djinn](https://web.archive.org/web/20040927000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mahamoti+Djinn)* 

Well, here's more proof of your artistic eye. Behold Kev Walker's amazing [Spirit Link](https://web.archive.org/web/20040927000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spirit+Link).

  
*[Spirit Link](https://web.archive.org/web/20040927000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spirit+Link) art by Kev Walker* 

Watch Magic Arcana in the coming weeks for more previews of the final art from *Ninth Edition*.
