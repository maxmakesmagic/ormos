Pauper is **Magic Online**'s format that uses only commons, but that doesn't mean it isn't a format capable of quick wins. \_Shaper\_ finished 2nd in a Pauper Premier Event with a Pauper version of a deck that current **Magic** R&D intern [Gerry Thompson played at Pro Tour *Return to Ravnica*](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/10-20-video-deck-tech-elemental-combo-with-gerry-thompson) . This deck's plan is to get a [Kiln Fiend](https://web.archive.org/web/20131218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kiln+Fiend) or [Nivix Cyclops](https://web.archive.org/web/20131218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nivix+Cyclops) on the battlefield and then play a lot of cheap instants and sorceries to quickly make its power huge. 

Lee Sharpe  
 Sr. Business Intelligence Analyst—**Magic Online**
