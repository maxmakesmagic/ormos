The 1999 World Championships were a coming out party of sorts for the nation of Germany. Kai Budde took the individual title with his Mono-Red Wildfire deck list that relied on Covetous Dragon to beat his opponents down. He also managed to lock up Player of the Year with his victory, leaving the previous year's winner (American Jon Finkel) in 2nd. The German national team managed to finish 2nd in the team portion of the competition, and Kai's teammate Dirk Baberowski won the Rookie of the Year title. When all was said and done, Germany had taken down 75% of the titles on the line, and had been in the Finals of all of them. The 2010 World Championships will broadcast live from Japan this weekend!
