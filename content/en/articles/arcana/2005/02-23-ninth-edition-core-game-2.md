Last week we teased you with some packaging art of [*Ninth Edition* boosters](https://web.archive.org/web/20050223000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/ninth-edition-product-shots-2005-02-14). This week we bring you a look at the packaging of the *Ninth Edition* Core Game box. Here's what you get with that Core Game product, intended for new players. 

<table><tbody><tr><td>&#10;						</td>&#10;					<td>&#10;						<p><i>The Core Game contains everything you need to learn how to play <b>Magic</b>! It contains:</i></p>&#10;						<ul><li><i>two 33-card decks,</i></li>&#10;							<li><i>a 14-card pack with more advanced cards for expanded game play,</i></li>&#10;							<li><i>a collectible premium card,</i></li>&#10;							<li><i>two play guides to take you and your opponent through the game step by step,</i></li>&#10;							<li><i>an easy-to-read rulebook for complete rules reference,</i></li>&#10;							<li><i>two playmats with scorekeeping counters,</i></li>&#10;							<li><i>a CD-ROM featuring exciting games that cover the key aspects of <b>Magic</b> play, plus the <b>Magic</b> Online program,</i></li>&#10;							<li><i>and a credit for online versions of the cards included in the game.</i></li>&#10;						</ul></td>&#10;				</tr></tbody></table>

And, because we like ya, here's one more booster image we didn't show you [last week](https://web.archive.org/web/20050223000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/ninth-edition-product-shots-2005-02-14). It appears to feature the same art as the Core Game box above -- some sort of winged woman with a sword. Huh.
