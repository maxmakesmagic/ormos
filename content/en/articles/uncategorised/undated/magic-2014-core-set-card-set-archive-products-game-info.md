## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0c/2f/0c2ffdb47c7edb560123ff23f3b8d1c45880168493edbc5d461b0dbaf8da56b8.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/06/d2/06d2728e97fb5cd704841beca95c551a433edca146afedefa10bb16cc7e23665.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, nine 15-card booster packs, 80 basic land cards, one card box, two deck boxes, a learn-to-play insert, and a special-edition life counter.

(\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5e/e8/5ee80a728f46a286a0c4a1258f278f86d2dcce7c0f72ab59ca71da4a8fd983f6.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 cards, a foil premium rare card, two sealed 15-card booster packs, a strategy insert, and a Magic learn-to-play guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717015522/http://magic.wizards.com/node/155266)

(\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5b/b5/5bb55486be7422e155940fb3683a44dcbd32c2d77861ac637eac0e1a75131ce0.png)

### EVENT DECK

Battle like the best, right out of the box. Includes a tournament-ready 60-card deck plus a 15-card “sideboard” to swap in as needed, depending on what you expect your opponent will play.

[DECKLIST](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717015522/http://magic.wizards.com/node/155296)

(\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a0/3d/a03d536b5bf4ce5f280219f391587522903d50e05029ee6e60b304b70f752f89.png)

### BOOSTER BATTLE PACKS

Want to play with a friend right now? Just choose which of the two 22-card decks you’ll each play, insert up to five cards from the two included booster packs—and begin! It’s that easy.

(\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a4/43/a4430f9a9298a7f6def7d979de032f07a0dfe3ce9da6a65f1f444dabb9cafdb1.png)

### DECK BUILDER'S TOOLKIT

The support you need to build your own **Magic: The Gathering** decks. Includes 125 semi-randomized cards suitable for building several different decks, four 15-card booster packs from a variety of recent **Magic** sets, 100 basic land cards, a deck builder’s guide, a **Magic** learn-to-play guide, and a card box.
