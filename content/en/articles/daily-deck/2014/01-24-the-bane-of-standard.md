It should be no surprise that Ali Aintrazi came up with another wacky five-color deck. As always, Ali's deck tries to "go over the top" of most decks in the format, which gives him a much superior late game.

![Axebane Guardian](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Axebane%20Guardian&options=)In this case, Ali's deck abuses [Axebane Guardian](https://web.archive.org/web/20140124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Axebane+Guardian) in combination with [Sylvan Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20140124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Caryatid) to generate a lot of colored mana very quickly. That mana tends to turn into huge spells, like [Angel of Serenity](https://web.archive.org/web/20140124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel+of+Serenity), [Sylvan Primordial](https://web.archive.org/web/20140124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Primordial), [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation), and [Rakdos's Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20140124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos%27s+Return). In the meantime, it stalls with "normal" spells like [Jace, Architect of Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20140124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Architect+of+Thought); [Mizzium Mortars](https://web.archive.org/web/20140124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzium+Mortars); and [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict).

At the [StarCityGames](http://www.starcitygames.com/) Open in Orlando, David McDarby bravely chose to run Ali's brew. He was 5–1 before the wheels fell off. Apparently the deck has a reasonable matchup against Mono-Black Devotion, in no small part due to [Blood Baron of Vizkopa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Baron+of+Vizkopa). Normally, that would just be [Devour Flesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20140124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devour+Flesh)ed, but Ali's deck has sacrificial fodder with his mana walls and [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize)s in his sideboard.

If you want to go big without using [Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx](https://web.archive.org/web/20140124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nykthos%2C+Shrine+to+Nyx), [Axebane Guardian](https://web.archive.org/web/20140124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Axebane+Guardian) is probably the best way to do it.
