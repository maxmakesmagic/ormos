The 2008 State Championships have come and gone, leaving a wake of fun and awesome new Standard decklists in their path!

Check out the top finishers below. Don't forget, you can test each decklist thanks to our [Sample Hand Generator](https://web.archive.org/web/20130624094147/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/294). Just click on the card fan in the upper right of each decklist and you can see what your opening hand could be, along with mulligans and the first three cards you'd draw.

Attendants at this year's State Championships each got a special foil Dauntless Dourbark (pending supply). Winners earned a free year's worth of events with participating tournament organizers!
