After 15 rounds of Standard play the original field of 799 players has been whittled down to but eight people. These eight are the best of the best, the cream of the crop, the literal one percent (well, close enough). Learn more about them here!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsev15/t8_corderovalle.jpg)

### **Name**: Marcos Antonio Cordero Valle

**Age**: 40

**Occupation:** Accountant

**Hometown:** Seville, Spain

**Clan:** 

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Spanish National Top 8, played five Pro Tours.

**What deck did you play and why? How did Fate Reforged influence your choice?**

The one my friends were able to lend me on Friday evening, which was White/Blue Heroic. Not sure how the set influenced that …

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**

I prefer three [Erase](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Erase)s and I'd remove the [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise)s from the sideboard. The main deck is fine.

**What is the most important card in your deck?**

[Favored Hoplite](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Favored+Hoplite).

### **Name**: Martin Jůza

**Age**: 27

**Occupation:** Professional tourist

**Hometown:** Plzen, Czech Republic

**Clan:** Jeskai

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

With this Top 8 I just got over 400 Pro Points lifetime.

**What deck did you play and why? How did Fate Reforged influence your choice?**

Jeskai Aggro. I only had two days to prepare so I asked friends for help. Dave Shields gave me a great Jeskai list and I tuned it a little to fit my style. [Wild Slash](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Slash), [Valorous Stance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valorous+Stance), and [Outpost Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Outpost+Siege) are some great additions from Fate Reforged!

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**

For now, none. It depends on how the metagame evolves during the next week.

**What is the most important card in your deck?**

It's really a combination of cheap removal and card advantage which makes the deck good, no specific card.

### **Name**: Carlos Ballester García

**Age**: 21

**Occupation:** Information technician

**Hometown:** Villena, Alicante, Spain

**Clan:** Abzan

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Day 2 at Pro Tour Journey into Nyx, Top 64 at Grand Prix Bilbao 2013.

**What deck did you play and why? How did Fate Reforged influence your choice?**

Red/Green Devotion/Monsters, which was the deck I knew the best and which performed the best.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**

I would play the third copy of [Crater's Claws](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crater%27s+Claws). It's too powerful!

**What is the most important card in your deck?**

[Genesis Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genesis+Hydra) and [Crater's Claws](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crater%27s+Claws).

### **Name**: Alexandre Habert

**Age**: 23 very soon

**Occupation:** Delivery man

**Hometown:** Toulouse, France

**Clan:** Sultai

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Some money finishes, in each of the possible Top X categories except for Top 8.

**What deck did you play and why? How did Fate Reforged influence your choice?**

Sidisi-Whip. I tested a lot with my team and it seemed the best deck to play in a metagame which we expected toi be very red-dominated.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**

Take [Stain the Mind](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stain+the+Mind) out of the sideboard to make room for two copies of [Nissa, Worldwaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%2C+Worldwaker). [Stain the Mind](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stain+the+Mind) was not as good as expected. Not at all!

**What is the most important card in your deck?**

[Sidisi, Brood Tyrant](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sidisi%2C+Brood+Tyrant) tied with [Whip of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos) of course.
