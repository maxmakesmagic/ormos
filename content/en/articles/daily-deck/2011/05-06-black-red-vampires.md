The story in Standard lately has focused on Caw-Blade and Blue-Red-Green, but plenty of players are innovating outside of the box. Like Robert Moody. He made the Top 8 of the TCGPlayer.com Wizards World Series in Anaheim, California recently with this take on Black-Red Vampires. The key addition to the deck? A twin set of Hero of Oxid Ridge, which prevent pesky Squadron Hawks from getting in the way of his Vampire attacks.
