*Mirrodin Besieged* has brought some new tools to the world of Extended, and for Bant players around the world one in particular has had a huge impact: Mirran Crusader. The unassuming 2/2 becomes a monstrous powerhouse when combined with Bant's exalted creatures. After all, thanks to double strike, the 2/2 quickly becomes a 3/3 or 4/4—which is equivalent to an 8/4 when left unblocked! It doesn't take long to take an opponent out with stats like those!
