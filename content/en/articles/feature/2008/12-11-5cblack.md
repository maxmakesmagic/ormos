Jakub Slemr had a very successful career on the **Magic** Pro Tour, with the crown in his resume being his World Champion title in 1997. He used a combination of rainbow lands to supplement a powerful black weenie strategy with the best splash cards the other four colors could provide. With Five Color Control now a popular strategy in the world of modern Standard, there is the real possibility a deck playing all five colors could regain the championship again in 2008.
