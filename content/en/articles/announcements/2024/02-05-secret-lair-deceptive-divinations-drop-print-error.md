The version of the card Circular Logic found in the *Deceptive Divination* drop, part of the [*Secret Lair* Winter Superdrop 2024](/en/articles/announcements/2024/02-05-winter-wonders-abound-in-the-secret-lair-winter-superdrop-2024), was inadvertently printed as a sorcery. The error is found in both foil and non-foil versions.

However, we assure you that you can still cast it as an instant and the Oracle rules text remains accurate.
