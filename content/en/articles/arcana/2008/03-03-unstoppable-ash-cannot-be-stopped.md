That boggart in its branches never had a chance, right? But the Ash has gone through a lot to get where he is. Here's the description given to the artist:

> Action: Show a fierce towering Ash treefolk charging toward camera. He has several hatches embedded in him from relentless Boggart attacks. BUT each of the hatchet handles has grown and sprouted leaves as if they were a part of him all along. Seeing that hes slung one or two of these lil' bastard Boggarts off of him would be a nice touch. Mood: that which does not kill me...

Mouse over the art to see what the Unstoppable Ash has already shrugged off.
