Learn more about Grand Prix Milwaukee's Top 8 competitors—including who they are, what they've done in the game, and more—by taking a look through their profiles!

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmil16/t8_severson2.jpg)Name:** Eric Severson 

**Age:** 24 

**Hometown:** Davis, CA 

**Occupation:** Math PHD student 

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Gold pro with Team East-West Bowl; three Grand Prix Top 8s 

**What did your Sealed Deck look like?** **What was the best card?** Red-White Aggro. I had [Veteran Motorist](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Veteran+Motorist); Scry 2 makes every hand with it, a Mountain and a Plains great. I went 7-2. 

**What colors were your draft decks, and what was your record? What were the best cards?** Red-Green Aggro and then Red-White Aggro; 3-0 with both. The best two cards in those drafts were [Renegade Freighter](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Renegade+Freighter) and then [Key to the City](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Key+to+the+City). 

**What card do you most want to open in this Top 8?** Skysovereign, Flagship Consul, but a powerful *Invention* would be more fun. 

**What was your favorite moment of 2016?** Narrowly surviving a car crash two months ago and now rallying back to another great finish.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmil16/t8_weitz2.jpg)Name:** Benjamin Weitz 

**Age:** 26 

**Hometown:** St. Paul, MN 

**Occupation:** Graduate Student 

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Three Grand Prix Top 8. Gold Pro and qualified for 2017 *Magic Online* Championship Series. I also cohost the podcast [Allied Strategies](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Allied+Strategies)! 

**What did your Sealed Deck look like?** What was the best card? Black-Red Aggro, Inventor's Goggles was the best card. 

**What colors were your draft decks, and what was your record? What were the best cards?** I went 3-0 in my first draft with Blue-Black Energy. [Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel) is great, especially when you hit [Noxious Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noxious+Gearhulk). Realistically, [Gearseeker Serpent](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gearseeker+Serpent) is how I actually won. 

Second draft I went Black-Red Aggro. [Night Market Lookout](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Night+Market+Lookout) plus [Sky Skiff](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sky+Skiff) is a good combo. Chandra helped too.

**What card do you most want to open in this Top 8?** [Skysovereign, Consul Flagship](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skysovereign%2C+Consul+Flagship). It's just the best card. 

**What was your favorite moment of 2016?** Making Gold in the penultimate round of Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon* after going 4-4 on Day 1. Also, my entire team made gold or platinum.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmil16/t8_burkhart2.jpg)Name:** Corey Burkhart 

**Age:** 24 

**Hometown:** Alta Loma, CA 

**Occupation:** Game Designer 

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Five Grand Prix Top 8s; my last three Grand Prix I've finished 2nd, 9th and now Top 8 here. Four weeks and three formats. 

**What did your Sealed Deck look like? What was the best card?** I had a solid black-green deck. I played poorly and ended 7-2. Best card was [Verdurous Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Verdurous+Gearhulk). 

**What colors were your draft decks, and what was your record? What were the best cards?** Blue-Black Aggro (a very bad deck with two [Torch Gauntlet](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torch+Gauntlet)s). Best card was [Ovalchase Daredevil](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ovalchase+Daredevil). Second draft was Jeskai [Panharmonicon](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panharmonicon). It had a bad curve but powerful cards. The best card was [Whirler Virtuoso](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whirler+Virtuoso). 

**What card do you most want to open in this Top 8?** [Skysovereign, Consul Flagship](https://web.archive.org/web/20161211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skysovereign%2C+Consul+Flagship). That card is messed up in Limited. 

**What was your favorite moment of 2016?** Watching Luis Scott-Vargas Top 8 his third Pro Tour in a row – it was better than my three Grand Prix Top 8s this season.
