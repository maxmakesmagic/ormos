Ryuma Narumiya has earned a slot at Japan Nationals thanks to this entirely green list that netted a Top 8 finish during National Qualifiers. Good old green; the deck simply plays out its creatures, including some that hand out free Eldrazi Spawn tokens like Nest Invader, then overwhelms the opponent with Overrun or Eldrazi Monument. Even the lands pitch in! Khalni Garden's innocuous 0/1 Plant token gets a lot more deadly when it's got +3/+3 and trample! It's the perfect deck to take to your Friday Night **Magic**.
