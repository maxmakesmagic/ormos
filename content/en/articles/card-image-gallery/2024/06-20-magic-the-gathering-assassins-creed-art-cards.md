In 30% of [*Magic: The Gathering® – Assassin's Creed®*](https://web.archive.org/web/20240620000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/assassins-creed) Beyond Boosters, available for preorder now from [your local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20240620000000/https://locator.wizards.com/) and online retailers like [Amazon](https://gtly.to/ij6DQ-0Zb), you can find an art card showcasing 1 of 20 illustrations from the set. In an additional 5% of Beyond Boosters, the art card is gold stamped with an artist's signature or the Planeswalker symbol.

Learn more about Beyond Boosters—and the other awesome things you can find inside them—from [Collecting *Magic: The Gathering® – Assassin's Creed®*](/en/articles/feature/2024/06-18-collecting-magic-the-gathering-assassins-creed) and check out the art cards themselves in the [*Magic: The Gathering® – Assassin's Creed®* Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20240620000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/assassins-creed/card-image-gallery?cigfreshness=all-cards&cigproduct=all-products&cigrarity=art%20card) (non-stamped versions have the same art but without the artist's signature or Planeswalker symbol).
