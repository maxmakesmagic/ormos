Hellow and welcome to Timmy Week here on **magicthegathering.com**. I've been excited about this particular column for awhile now.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ed/62/ed62636b0fceb405d0b5b24664a8595861d121b1ac28463429a2d736ab71fdf5.jpg)

Who is Timmy? Timmy plays the game to have fun. Many players will see people playing wacky cards or offbeat combos and ask a question like, "Why is he playing with that?" Timmy doesn't play **Magic** to solve metagames or to win tournaments. Timmy just wants to have a good time.

There are four types of Timmy. A quick summary:

*   Power gamers like to play big spells and big creatures. They equate quantity of damage, power/toughness, and spells' effects with how much fun the game is.
*   Social gamers play to spend time with their friends. They enjoy multiplayer formats and treat **Magic** as something to do while hanging out.
*   Diversity gamers want to see what **Magic** has to offer. They'll play a different format every day of the week and happily move along over and over. You'll rarely ever see a diversity gamer playing the same deck twice.
*   Adrenalin gamers live for the thrill of the moment. They like coin-flip cards, Erratic Explosions, and great topdecks.

Like a lot of people, when I think about Timmy, I think about the power gamer. I remember playing Magic on a picnic table during recess. I remember casting [Shivan Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Dragon) and [Baron Sengir](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baron+Sengir). I remember loving every minute of it.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9d/dc/9ddc4f9683ad9e01fe220708d1cc5119f4be3dfa4b5ad4cb5d794ffd2d1bc691.jpg)  

One card sprang to mind when I found out there was going to be a Timmy Week:

![Where Ancients Tread](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Where+Ancients+Tread)

  

[Where Ancients Tread](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Where+Ancients+Tread)!

I want to play with Where Ancients Tread because it's fun. I'm trying to get into the Timmy mindset here, and I can't think of a better way to do it. Timmy is not satisfied with his 5-power monster. Timmy wants his 5-power beater to deal 5-damage to something when it comes into play.

Now it's time to decide what we can put into our [Where Ancients Tread](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Where+Ancients+Tread) deck.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9f/19/9f19af362d36cc311c2b4008da2e80a2ed537e2446a337a4b699a1311e70cf30.jpg)  

**[Druid of the Anima](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Druid+of+the+Anima)**: I want to cast big goofy monsters. I don't want my opponents to overrun me with little dudes before I can play the fat. Acceleration is important. The Druid will also help fix my mana.

**[Llanowar Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Elves)**: I'm playing Llanowar Elves for the same ramping purpose as Druid of the Anima.

**[Devoted Druid](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devoted+Druid)**: This lovely individual ramps our mana extremely quickly.

**[Elvish Visionary](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Visionary)**: I want chump-blockers for the early stages of the game. As the game progresses my Visionary can combo with my [Stampeding Wildebeests](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stampeding+Wildebeests) or be sacrificed to my [Caldera Hellion](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Caldera+Hellion).

**[Stampeding Wildebeests](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stampeding+Wildebeests)**: These guys are a lot of fun here. I can return an [Elvish Visionary](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Visionary) or [Civic Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Civic+Wayfinder) back to my hand and replay it every turn. If I get [Where Ancients Tread](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Where+Ancients+Tread) into play I can start bouncing 5-power monsters back to my hand to [Lava Axe](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lava+Axe) my opponent every turn.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/79/06/79064c110038cb25282855650ec193f9ce1aebf51b4c3c2348ed22964efc049a.jpg)  

**[Civic Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Civic+Wayfinder)**: This respectable 2/2 will smooth out my mana and help me survive the early stages of the game.

**[Caldera Hellion](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Caldera+Hellion)**: The Hellion will clear my opponent's board and make way for the fatty boom-booms.

**[Flameblast Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flameblast+Dragon)**: I looked through [Gatherer](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/http://beta.gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Default.aspx) to find cards that just screamed "TIMMY!", and this screams it with fire.

**[Deus of Calamity](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deus+of+Calamity)**: I love dropping the Deus on people. It's a very underplayed card that's a lot of fun to play with.

Once we mash all this fat together the list looks like this:

I keep [Elvish Visionary](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Visionary), [Civic Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Civic+Wayfinder), [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest), [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain), [Deus of Calamity](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deus+of+Calamity), and 2 [Stampeding Wildebeests](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stampeding+Wildebeests) on the play. I play my [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest), and my opponent plays a [Swamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swamp). I draw a [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain), play my [Elvish Visionary](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Visionary), and draw another [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain). My opponent plays a [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain) and a [Ravenous Rats](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ravenous+Rats), and I discard one of my Mountains. On my turn I draw [Flameblast Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flameblast+Dragon), play my [Civic Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Civic+Wayfinder), and get a second [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest). My opponent plays [Blightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blightning) on his turn, and I discard [Flameblast Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flameblast+Dragon) and [Deus of Calamity](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deus+of+Calamity). On my turn I attack with [Civic Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Civic+Wayfinder) and play my [Stampeding Wildebeests](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stampeding+Wildebeests). My opponent evokes a [Shriekmaw](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shriekmaw) to kill the 5/4. I draw another [Deus of Calamity](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deus+of+Calamity), play my second copy of [Stampeding Wildebeests](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stampeding+Wildebeests), and pass. My opponent plays [Lavaborn Muse](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lavaborn+Muse) and passes. I return [Elvish Visionary](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Visionary) back to my hand and draw another [Elvish Visionary](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Visionary). I play both Visionaries, drawing a land and another [Stampeding Wildebeests](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stampeding+Wildebeests), and attack with [Stampeding Wildebeests](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stampeding+Wildebeests). My opponent simply passes the turn back. I return a Visionary and draw another land, then attack with my [Stampeding Wildebeests](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stampeding+Wildebeests), replay the Visionary drawing a [Where Ancients Tread](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Where+Ancients+Tread), and play my second Wildebeests. My opponent untaps and plays [Nyxathid](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nyxathid). On my turn I return both Visionaries and draw a [Flameblast Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flameblast+Dragon). I attack with both [Stampeding Wildebeests](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stampeding+Wildebeests), and my opponent is forced to try to make some trades. After the dust settles in the Red Zone I play [Flameblast Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20090312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flameblast+Dragon). My opponent fails to find any help on the top of his library and concedes the game.

3-2
