With every new **Magic** set comes a new set of Intro Packs. We've already shown you what's on the front of them; now let's dive into them and see what they're made of!

As of this publishing, ***Magic** 2014* has not yet entered Gatherer but it will later today. Until then, you can see the new cards in our [Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218214856/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/magic2014coreset/cig), or check back after it goes live in Gatherer and we'll update this arcana to have the mouseover for new cards.
