"The deck is really good against fair aggro decks or grindy midrange decks, but it has a hard time beating burn spells or countermagic," Michael Bonde explained when the video coverage team interviewed him at the beginning of the day. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsto14/Michael-Bonde.jpg)

*Michael Bonde* 

  

Bonde's list is very similar to the one ran by several members of Team ChannelFireball Pantheon at Pro Tour *Khans of Tarkir*. The deck didn't put up a strong performance there and had fallen a bit out of favor since then, but Bonde thought it would still be a strong choice for the metagame. 

His current record is 5-2, so he'll have to win his last two matches of the day to make it to Day 2. Here is Bonde's decklist:

In a way, Green Devotion is kind of the last remaining bastion from the old Standard, and it's a tried-and-true strategy. Filled with lots of ways to generate mana and lots of ways to utilize that mana, it features the dream start of [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic) into [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix) into [Polukranos, World Eater](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Polukranos%2C+World+Eater), which can be particularly deadly when combined with [Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nykthos%2C+Shrine+to+Nyx).

The most interesting card choice in his deck is [See the Unwritten](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=See+the+Unwritten), which is included in lieu of [Genesis Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genesis+Hydra). "In testing, I found that [See the Unwritten](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=See+the+Unwritten) always has a huge impact," Bonde said. "It can get double [Doomwake Giant](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doomwake+Giant) and give -4/-4 to the entire opponent's board, or you can get [Hornet Queen](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hornet+Queen) and beat most decks in the metagame. It also puts cards in the graveyard for [Pharika, God of Affliction](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pharika%2C+God+of+Affliction)." 

Another interesting card is [Hornet Nest](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hornet+Nest). When asked when he boards it in, Bonde answered that he was mainly planning to bring it in against [Goblin Rabblemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Rabblemaster) deck. It's a sweet way to deal with the Goblins indeed, but maybe Bonde wasn't brave enough: at least one Platinum pro is playing a Green Devotion deck with [Hornet Nest](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hornet+Nest) in the maindeck today. Watch out for the bees!
