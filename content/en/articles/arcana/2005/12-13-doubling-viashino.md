The original art description for **Doubling Season** called for the art to depict a double batch of *viashino* instead of saprolings. The artist, Wayne Reynolds, duly created this initial sketch:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/56/ff/56ff595e852bff8bf553c5972a19ce76f28458f8a269b23f8483f53e1bd9e74e.jpg)  
*Doubling Season sketch by Wayne Reynolds*

While the sketch was awesome, it was later decided that the card should depict saprolings, since it's what most of the token-generating cards in *Ravnica* produce, and would be an in-color race (viashino are traditionally red-aligned). Here's the second sketch.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0b/3e/0b3e604d461af2beef93249c0a24b4541ead73764d688c5eb76072c5b1b8262d.jpg)  
*Doubling Season sketch by Wayne Reynolds*

This sketch led to the final art on the card. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/55/f1/55f130cde7541795194057f314c33d7da7a6e45fba74a38d8b60e143ba716781.jpg)  
*Doubling Season final art by Wayne Reynolds*
