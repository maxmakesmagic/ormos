Note: Due to load and stability issues, we are not going to run championships for the *Weatherlight* Release events. Instead, we will give away Online sets to the winners of the 2x and 4x events. See the event information below for more details.

On Wednesday, December 12th at 9:00am PST, *Weatherlight* goes on sale in the Magic Online store.

NOTE: *Weatherlight* becomes legal in ALL Classic-based Constructed formats the day it goes on sale (December 12). The Classic-based formats are Classic, Tribal Wars (Classic), Classic with Vanguard, Prismatic, Singleton, and Freeform.

*Weatherlight* Release events begin Friday, December 14 and will have 4 components:

Release Leagues  
 Draft Queues  
 Premier Event "No Top 8" Flights  
 Premier Event Flights (2x and 4x Prizes)

### *Weatherlight* Release Leagues

SIGNUP: December 14, 9:00am – December 21, 12:00pm (the final 1 week, 2x league will start no later than 12:00pm on December 21). Release Leagues will have a maximum of 256 players each and will auto-spawn (as soon as a league reaches 256 players, another will begin registration).

LEAGUE DURATION: 1 week

PRODUCT: 1 *Mirage* Tournament Pack and 3 *Weatherlight* Booster Packs.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 2x prizes, Participation Avatar: Peacekeeper; Records of 5-0 get Prize Avatar: Morinfen.

Avatars will be distributed Approx. 3 weeks after the last league concludes

*NOTE: The regularly schedule downtime will still take place on Wednesday, December 19 at 8:00 am PST. Plan your league games accordingly as no compensation will be issued if you lose a game due to the servers going down for maintenance.*

### *Weatherlight* Draft Queues

QUEUES BEGIN: December 14, 12:00pm

FORMATS: Booster Draft

PRODUCT: 1 *Mirage* Booster, 1 *Visions* Booster, 1 *Weatherlight* Booster or 3 *Weatherlight* Boosters.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 4-3-2-2

*NOTE: The regularly schedule downtime will still take place on Wednesday, December 19 at 8:00 am PST. Queues will be down during the downtime.*

### *Weatherlight* "No Top 8" Premier Events

DURATION: December 14, 2:00 pm – December 21 12:00 pm (last starts at 12:00 pm). "No Top 8" Flights will have 32 players per flight and will start every ½ hour or 1 hour (depending on demand). Each flight will have a minimum of 24 players, maximum of 32 players. Each flight will be 5 rounds.

PRODUCT: 1 *Mirage* Tournament Pack and 3 *Weatherlight* Booster Packs.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product 

PRIZE SUPPORT: As there is no top 8, prizes are based on total match points at the end of the tournament. Each match win awards 3 points, each draw awards 1 point, and each loss awards 0 points.

### Important information regarding system crashes and refunds for Premier Events

If during your event the system crashes, 1 of 3 things will happen:

1.  The system will come right back up. In this case:

    *   Events will continue and additional time will be given to allow players to finish the event
    *   No Compensation will be given

2.  The system cannot recover the event and "finishes" (prematurely ends) the event, or a WOTC Tournament Coordinator must "finish" (prematurely end) the event.

    *   We will accept the prize payouts "as is" - This means that whatever prize payouts and invites the system awards, we will not change what the system does.
    *   No changes will be made to these events from what the system automatically pays out as prizes or invitations

    If the event is in TOP 8:

    *   No product or tickets will be refunded
    *   All players keep the product used to enter the event as well as the drafted cards from the TOP 8 draft.

    If the event is still in Swiss Rounds:

    *   Players can [request a refund through Customer Service](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=161).
    *   We will refund your Product and tickets.

3.  The event is cleared or cancelled by the system. If an event is cancelled this means the event can no longer be viewed in the premier event listings.

    *   The system will automatically refund your tickets.
    *   Players can [request a refund through Customer Service](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=161).
    *   We will refund your Product.
