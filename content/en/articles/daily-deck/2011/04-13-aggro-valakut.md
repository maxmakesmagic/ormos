Grand Prix Dallas-Fort Worth gave the Standard format plenty of interesting new decks. While Caw-Blade might have won the title, Texan Justin Corbett's team of six players took an Aggro Valakut list into battle and did very well. Using a beatdown plan powered by Bestial Menace and Hero of Oxid Ridge, the deck can kill as early as the fourth turn. And if that plan doesn't work, the players switch gears and use Valakut as a turbo win condition! Of the six who played the deck, five made Day 2. Read more about Grand Prix Dallas-Fort Worth in the [official event coverage](/en/events/2011/gpdal11/shiels-stands-alone-in-dallas).
