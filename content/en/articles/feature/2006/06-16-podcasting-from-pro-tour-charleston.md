Welcome to [Pro Tour-Charleston](https://web.archive.org/web/20060616000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/kajiharu80-puts-char-charleston)! The best **Magic** players in the world have flocked to South Carolina to try their hands at Team *Ravnica* Block Constructed, and Randy Buehler's prowling the event floor to bring you all the info straight from the players' mouths. With 525 players, there's sure to be plenty of stories, tall tales, anecdotes, and bad-beat stories to fill the airwaves.

New podcasts will be posted here throughout the tournament. You can also get the podcasts delivered directly to your computer by subscribing to the **Magic** Podcast via iTunes (information below).

**WHAT IS PODCASTING?**

Podcasting is delivering audio and video content on demand so that it can be enjoyed at the user's convenience. The main benefit of podcasting is that listeners can sync content to their media player and take it with them to listen whenever they want to. But they're not just for iPods…the files are ready to watch or listen to on just about any computer.

To subscribe to the **Magic: The Gathering** podcast, follow these easy steps.

1.  Go to [http://www.apple.com/podcasting/](http://www.apple.com/podcasting/)
2.  Download iTunes version 6
3.  Launch iTunes.
4.  From the dropdown menus, select Advanced -- Subscribe to Podcast
5.  When prompted for a URL, enter **[http://www.wizards.com/magic/rsspodcast.xml](https://web.archive.org/web/20060616000000/http://www.wizards.com/magic/rsspodcast.xml)** and click OK.

Once you subscribe to the podcast, iTunes will automatically check for new files and download them to your computer. To ensure that you get timely updates, set your Podcast Settings to check for new episodes every hour (the Settings button is in the lower-right corner of the iTunes window, or you can find it under Edit -- Preferences -- Podcast).
