In this week's feature article, Monty Ashley celebrates the ten-year anniversary of the first World Championships. The finals match between Zak Dolan and Bertrand Lestree involved many cards familiar to modern players ([Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20040823000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise), [Icy Manipulator](https://web.archive.org/web/20040823000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Icy+Manipulator), [Wrath of God](https://web.archive.org/web/20040823000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wrath+of+God)) and some you may not have heard of -- at least in a long time.

Today's Magic Arcana tests your knowledge of old-timer **Magic** art. Each of these closeups are from cards used in the final match of the first World Championships. Click "See answer" to reveal each card.
