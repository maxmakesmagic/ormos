Let's talk for a second about [Metallic Mastery](https://web.archive.org/web/20110405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metallic+Mastery), which represents the Phyrexians' ability to use the Mirrans' own technology against them.

![Metallic Mastery](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Metallic+Mastery)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/44/9f/449feb4b8806b9d7a6184ed54ebdf7d4fe283f949d46d7bbe0160504974df097.jpg)

[Metallic Mastery](https://web.archive.org/web/20110405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metallic+Mastery) | Art by [Erica Yang](https://web.archive.org/web/20110405000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Erica+Yang%22%5D)

The art showcases a Phyrexian furnace worker holding a familiar-looking artifact from *Scars of Mirrodin*.

![Ratchet Bomb](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ratchet+Bomb)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0b/4b/0b4bed43cb1e8cc591a6dff1f22518e59c1ad87e65d5a180506c69c96d412c80.jpg)

[Ratchet Bomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20110405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ratchet+Bomb) | Art by [Austin Hsu](https://web.archive.org/web/20110405000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Austin+Hsu%22%5D)

Now that that's out of the way, let's look a little more closely at the rest of the card. The furnace worker, and even the furnace background, were taken from concepts in the *Mirrodin Besieged* style guide. Observe!

![The furnace layer from the Mirrodin Besieged style guide](https://i.ormos.uk/42/71/427114d2e16f4ece57d6562b662683e91acc8a6eac7a61d403a4e3188f7b11d1.jpg)  
![Phyrexian furnace worker from the Mirrodin Besieged Style Guide](https://i.ormos.uk/9d/9e/9d9e5aa397d78ed55003e3dddbf928800331cae6554112ca8e744aab00f0fad4.jpg)
