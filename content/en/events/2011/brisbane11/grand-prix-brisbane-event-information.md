Sun, surf and Standard?? 

Yes indeed, after a long absence, Constructed Magic makes its triumphant return to an Australasian Grand Prix. Just up the road from the World Famous Surfers Paradise, the Queensland capital of Brisbane will play host to players from around the country and the world, deck builders and card slingers alike, all fighting it out for the crown of Grand Prix champion and a slice of the $30,000 prize pool. 

But of course it’s a Grand Prix weekend so that’s not all! The weekend will also feature an action-packed public event program that will appeal to all players, from those new to the game to those not so new. With events like Multiplayer Madness, Learn to Play Intro Challenge, a Pro Tour Qualifier, and a few surprises thrown in there really will be something for everyone. In addition, local stores will set up shop in ’Vendor Alley’ where they will have just the thing to complete your deck of doom, and world-renowned artist Izzy will be there to sign cards and chat. Add to that the chance to hang out with players from around the globe and its going to be one heck of a great weekend.

*The **Magic: The Gathering** Grand Prix tournament series is a great reason to get out of the house, catch up with old friends, meet some new ones, and play **Magic**. Grand Prix tournaments are world-wide open events that allow all **Magic** players to participate and experience the thrill of a large-scale competition. In addition to $30,000 in cash prizes, Pro Tour invitations for the top 16 finishers, and exclusive foil promo cards for participants, Grand Prix feature a host of other activities, including public events and artist signings (prizes subject to change). Find out more about the Grand Prix [tournament schedule here](/en/events/2002/welcome/grand-prix#1).*

The main tournament at Grand Prix Brisbane is open to **Magic** players of all ages.

Minors must have a parent or guardian's signature, and the release form for minors can be [downloaded here](https://media.ormos.uk/95/17/95172028576cb86460d2c8e05c71319eeca651e971b000630e221988d4d4c286.pdf) (12k PDF) and will be available onsite. **Tournament Format**  
 

*   [Standard](https://web.archive.org/web/20130708023701/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Resources.aspx?x=judge/resources/sfrstandard)
*   Deck lists are required.
*   Players must use the same deck on Day 2 that they used on Day 1.

**Tournament Structure** 

*   Modified Swiss-style (50-minute rounds).
*   Rules Enforcement Level (REL) is Competitive (Day 1) and Professional (Day 2)
*   If 799 or fewer players register for the event, all players with an X-2-0 or better record OR the top 64 players—whichever number is greater—will advance to the second day of the event.
*   If 800 or more players register for the event, all players with an X-2-0 or better record OR the top 128 players—whichever number is greater—will advance to the second day of the event.
*   The Top 8 players after the final Swiss round on Day 2 will advance to the Top 8 single-elimination finals.
*   Top 8 playoff matches will be best 2 of 3, and have no time limit. (Players are still expected to play at a normal pace and complete playoff matches in a reasonable amount of time.)
*   Where necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will still be used to determine final order in the standings.

Byes will be given to players in the Grand Prix based on the following criteria *(Byes are not cumulative throughout the season or year. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Players that accumulate multiple byes to a particular Grand Prix tournament receive only the bye for the greatest number of rounds)*:

**Third-Round Byes**  
 

*   Players with a [Players Club](/en/events/2006/playersclub/pro-players-club-guidelines-and-procedures) level of 4 or higher
*   Players with a DCI Total rating of 2050 or higher. Byes are based on DCI Total ratings published on October 12, 2011.
*   Winners of Grand Prix Brisbane Trial tournaments.

**Second-Round Byes**  
 

*   Players with a [Players Club](/en/events/2006/playersclub/pro-players-club-guidelines-and-procedures) level of 2 or 3
*   Players with a DCI Total rating of 1950 or higher. Byes are based on DCI Total ratings published on October 12, 2011.

**First-Round Byes**  
 

*   Players with a [Players Club](/en/events/2006/playersclub/pro-players-club-guidelines-and-procedures) level of 1
*   Players with a DCI Total rating of 1850 or higher. Byes are based on DCI Total ratings published on October 12, 2011.

**Trial winners**  
 People who win a Trial event are awarded 3 byes in the Grand Prix that it feeds. Trial byes are not cumulative with other byes and do not pass down.

**Getting to Brisbane**  
 Flying is the most convenient mode of transportation for most visitors traveling to Brisbane. Domestic flights are also easy and convenient, cutting down on journey time.
