Lorwyn is an idyllic world where nature thrives in its most pristine state. The sun never drops below the horizon, and midsummer never ends. The creatures of fable who dwell there know nothing of gloom or malice—

—but they are consumed with *rivalry*.

**Magic**'s *Lorwyn* set hits a store near you on the [weekend of October 12-14](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-launch-parties-4). Be sure to mark your calendar for the release events going on that weekend, where you can get your hands on the set, play some *Lorwyn* sealed and/or booster draft, pick up a promo foil, and compete for store prizes.
