![](https://i.ormos.uk/bf/e4/bfe4b806b78b186731be93795fd96394f417c2b741b18e72407a614a7a4d1051.jpg) 

  

Naturally, your first thought was "What? Play Network?! I want to be a part of that!" Well, [feel free](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/welcome). And with that out of the way, how about that Chandra Nalaar in the background? It was drawn by Richard Whitters. You might also have seen it on the half-decks that were made available through mysterious convention-related means last summer.

But the drawings are, in our opinions, *really, really cool*. And the half-decks didn't really use all of them. So here they are, in all their glory!
