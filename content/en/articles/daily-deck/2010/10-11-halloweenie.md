It's October, and that means Halloween is just around the corner. We here at Daily Decks happen to be big fans of holidays, and in honor of the creepiest holiday of them all (depending on how creepy you feel Christopher Columbus and his Day are .... ), we present a 60-card Singleton deck themed to Halloween. It's got the big day, of course (All Hallow's Eve), an assortment of scary bodies (Werewolves, Ghouls, Witches, Warlocks, Insects, oh my!), the result of a long night of trick-or-treating (Soul Feast, Vampiric Feast), something to carry the goods in (Jandor's Saddlebags), and even a Jack-o'-Lantern (Reaper King)! This year is no doubt going to be the best Halloween ever ....
