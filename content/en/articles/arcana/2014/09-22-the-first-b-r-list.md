Today we released yet another in a long line of [banned and restricted announcements](/en/articles/feature/2014/09-23-september-22-2014-dci-banned-restricted-list-announcement), dating back virtually to the game's beginnings. The [modern banned and restricted list](https://web.archive.org/web/20140922000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/gameinfo/gameplay/formats/bannedrestricted) is heavily curated, always watched, and typically very sensible.

The first list might have been, well, a little soft in some of those areas.

The first list was actually published on USENET way back in the day (think of it as a precursor to Internet message boards, forums, or even reddit). You can actually see the full text of it still, [right here](https://web.archive.org/web/20140922000000/http://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/firstdcifloorrules.txt), preserved for posterity's sake.

By and large it looked similar to the modern-day Vintage restricted list (at least as far as with cards that had actually been printed at the time) except for a few interesting nuggets.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?multiverseid=12&type=card)](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?multiverseid=12&type=card)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?multiverseid=214&type=card)](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?multiverseid=214&type=card)

You could probably add [Icy Manipulator](https://web.archive.org/web/20140922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Icy+Manipulator), [Gauntlet of Might](https://web.archive.org/web/20140922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gauntlet+of+Might), [Rukh Egg](https://web.archive.org/web/20140922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rukh+Egg), and [Ali from Cairo](https://web.archive.org/web/20140922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ali+from+Cairo) to the list of oddities, but power levels varied a little bit back in *Magic's* early days. [Icy Manipulator](https://web.archive.org/web/20140922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Icy+Manipulator) was actually one of the best cards in the game early on, and [Rukh Egg](https://web.archive.org/web/20140922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rukh+Egg) suffered from confusion and unfortunate wording that led people to get 4/4 Rukh tokens simply by discarding it.

As for [Dingus Egg](https://web.archive.org/web/20140922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dingus+Egg) and [Orcish Oriflamme](https://web.archive.org/web/20140922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Orcish+Oriflamme)? The Oriflamme we can at least sort of blame on the fact that the Alpha printing was misprinted as costing only 1R. Sort of.

*   [Shahrazad](https://web.archive.org/web/20140922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shahrazad)

Ante cards were banned from tournaments from the get-go, and subsequently removed from the game altogether. [Shahrazad](https://web.archive.org/web/20140922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shahrazad) was banned for being weird and time consuming and really poor for tournament play. I think we're all better off for that particular move.
