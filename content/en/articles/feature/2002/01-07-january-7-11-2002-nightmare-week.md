The theme week for January 7-11, 2002 was "Nightmare Week". It was the second week of **magicthegathering.com**'s existence and its first theme week.

### Monday

*   **Making Magic: [A Nightmare to Remember](/en/articles/makingmagic/2002/01-07-a-nightmare-to-remember)**, by Mark Rosewater. Making the "nightmare" mechanic and previewing *Torment*.
*   **Feature: [A Man Named Chainer](https://web.archive.org/web/20020107000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/tales-beyond-veil-2005-06-13)**, by Rei Nakazawa. The background of [Chainer, Dementia Master](https://web.archive.org/web/20020107000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chainer%2C+Dementia+Master) and the Cabal.

### Tuesday

*   **Serious Fun: ["Chainge" Is Good](/en/articles/seriousfun/2002/01-08-chainge-is-good)**, by Anthony Alongi. Previewing [Chainer, Dementia Master](https://web.archive.org/web/20020107000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chainer%2C+Dementia+Master) without showing any actual nightmares.

### Wednesday

*   **Uncommon Knowledge: [Here And Gone](/en/articles/uncommonknowledge/2002/01-09-here-and-gone), by Ben Bleiweiss**. Previewing [Faceless Butcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20020107000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faceless+Butcher)

### Thursday

*   **House of Cards: [Giving You Nightmares...](/en/articles/houseofcards/2002/01-10-giving-you-nightmares), by Jay Moldenhauer-Salazar**. Nightmare theme decks.

### Friday

*   **Latest Developments: [Slithery Sky](/en/articles/latestdevelopments/2002/01-11-slithery-sky), by Randy Buehler**. Developing the nightmares.
