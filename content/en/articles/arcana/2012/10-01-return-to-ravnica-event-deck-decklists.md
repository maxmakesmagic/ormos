Now that the *Return to Ravnica* Prerelease is behind us, why not look to the future? All the way to October 26, 2012, when the *Return to Ravnica* Event Decks will be on sale at an MSRP of $24.99! Each deck will be Standard-legal and contain a sixty-card deck and fifteen-card sideboard.
