The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on July 10, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by Paul Paul Paul Paul Paul Paul Paul Paul.

# What You Might Have Missed This Weekend

1.  [Vintage Cube #25 – My Final Draft . . .](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BpTyYRLReCI) | Paul Cheon

Not final as in "I will never draft again," but final as in "before starting to work at Wizards of the Coast and drafting all the time." We hope to get Paul streaming again in some official capacity, but we're going to let him figure out where the bathroom is first.

1.  [*Magic: The Gathering* Reveal Their SDCC Exclusive](http://sknr.net/2017/07/07/magic-gathering-reveal-sdcc-exclusive/) | Skewed & Reviewed | Gareth

That Nicol Bolas poster art is enough to get me interested, though the card images shared in the article are smaller than most people can make out without one of those fake television computers that people shout "enhance" at a million times. So let's fix that. Here are the six individual cards for your viewing pleasure.

1.  [2017 Chesley Award Winners](http://file770.com/?p=36192)

In particular I want to draw your attention to Best Gaming Related Illustration, where [Ryan Yee](https://web.archive.org/web/20170710000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Ryan+Yee%22%5d) won for [Die Young](https://web.archive.org/web/20170710000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Die+Young) in *Kaladesh*. The card [Yee has called his favorite *Magic* piece](https://www.instagram.com/p/BRvwGcuDUfA/) is beautiful, haunting, and now a Chesley Award winner. If you haven't heard me say it before, Chesley Awards are a massive deal in the science fiction/fantasy art world. Congratulations to Ryan Yee!

1.  [Modern Ban Watchlist](https://collected.company/2017/07/08/modern-ban-watchlist-138/) | Masters of Modern | Ben Bateman and Alex Kessler

It's certainly nothing official, but it's fun fodder for discussion. Which, I suppose is the point.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Blue-Red Control](https://www.channelfireball.com/articles/hour-of-devastation-is-exactly-what-ur-control-needs/) | Channel Fireball | Joel Larsson

Larsson thinks *Hour of Devastation* is bringing Blue-Red Control to the next level—and he means that in two different ways. One, the new set brings some new toys, particularly [Abrade](https://web.archive.org/web/20170710000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrade) and [Supreme Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20170710000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Will). Two, the actual card [Hour of Devastation](https://web.archive.org/web/20170710000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hour+of+Devastation) gives the deck a sweeper unlike any the deck has seen before. It might even be the best sweeper in Standard.

So expect this deck to not only continue kicking around in new Standard, but devastating opponents left and right.
