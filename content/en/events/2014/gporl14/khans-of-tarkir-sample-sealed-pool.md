Limited Grand Prix always begin with Sealed: Opening six booster packs and building a 40 cards deck from it, including basic lands. At Grand Prix Orlando, it's *Khans of Tarkir*'s time to shine in the spotlight as its first weekend of Premier *Magic*. 

Whether you made it to your local Prerelease events, celebrated Friday Night *Magic* with its release last weekend, or you're just catching up on everything now, it's clear at a glance that *Khans of Tarkir* presents some challenging questions for players to answer in Limited. Here, six booster packs of the set yielded a mix of powerful but colorful cards – [Villainous Wealth](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Villainous+Wealth) and [Sultai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sultai+Ascendancy), [Duneblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duneblast), Bear's Companion and [Temur Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temur+Charm) all – as well as several lands that might help us cast them. In less-colorful choices, cards like [End Hostilities](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=End+Hostilities), [Bloodsoaked Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodsoaked+Champion), [Hardened Scales](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hardened+Scales), and [Rattleclaw Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rattleclaw+Mystic) pull us in different directions. 

Will you build the deck the mana fixing can support? Would you find a way to play just the strongest cards possible? Can you assemble a machine to fight through nine rounds of Sealed matches?

Take your best shot and we'll share what some of the game's greats had to say about building this pool:
