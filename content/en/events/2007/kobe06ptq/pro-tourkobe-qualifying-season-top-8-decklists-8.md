![](https://www.wizards.com/protour/images/qt_lg.jpg) Refine your Sealed Deck and Booster Draft skills to earn a ticket to Kobe, Japan, by winning a Pro Tour Qualifier! 

The full *Ravnica* block is on display in all its might this season. The Pros got off and running in the format at [Pro Tour-Prague](/en/events/2006/ptpra06/osawas-wurms-flog-prague), with not one but two draft viewers available for careful study of the intricacies of *Ravnica*.

The decks linked here are the Top 8 Booster Draft decks from North American Pro Tour Qualifiers. Coming July 21, the Top 8 drafts switch to *Coldsnap*.

You can test each decklist thanks to our [Sample Hand Generator](https://web.archive.org/web/20130624094147/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/294). Just click on the card fan in the upper right of each decklist and you can see what your opening hand could be, along with mulligans and the first three cards you'd draw.

Decklists will be updated weekly during the PTQ season. The season runs until August 27. To find a PTQ near you, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20130701084910/https://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/events/protour?x=protour/kobe06/qualifiers). [Pro Tour-Kobe](https://web.archive.org/web/20130701084910/https://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/events/protour?x=protour/kobe06/facts) is scheduled for October 20-22, 2006, and the format is *Time Spiral* Booster Draft.
