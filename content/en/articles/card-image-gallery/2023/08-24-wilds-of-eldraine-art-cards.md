[*Wilds of Eldraine*](https://web.archive.org/web/20230824000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/wilds-of-eldraine) Set Boosters, available for preorder from [your local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20230824000000/https://locator.wizards.com/) and online retailers like [Amazon](https://gtly.to/oDSipyfgz), include a special art card depicting 1 of 81 pieces of artwork from the set. In addition, 1 in 10 art cards opened may be gold stamped with the artist's signature or a Planeswalker symbol.

You can learn more about Set Boosters and the other amazing products from *Wilds of Eldraine* in our [Collecting *Wilds of Eldraine*](/en/articles/feature/2023/08-15-collecting-wilds-of-eldraine) article and check out the art cards themselves below (non-stamped versions have the same art but without the artist's signature or Planeswalker symbol).
