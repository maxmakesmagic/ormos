The month is nearly over, so once again it's time to take a trip in our Friday Night *Magic* time machine and see what promos we have in store for the coming months.

**September**

We're far from done with Dragons, and this sturdy Bird Monk, [Orator of Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20150827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Orator+of+Ojutai)—the Dragoniest [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20150827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) ever—reminds us why. You'll be able to get your copy come September, whether you choose to employ Dragons or some other inferior creatures to do so.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_s6idki155h.jpg)

**October**

What's in the bag? WHAT'S IN THE BAG?!?!

The spookiest month gets a spooky upgrade to [Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20150827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price) with new art by Scott Murphy. Look away if you're squeamish, because John Doe has the upper hand.

**November**

It might be cold outside, but your opponents will be feeling the heat at FNM with this Zoltan Boros take on the premium removal spell [Roast](https://web.archive.org/web/20150827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roast).
