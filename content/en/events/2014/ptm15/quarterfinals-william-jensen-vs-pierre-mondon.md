Just because you've made it to the Top 8 of a Pro Tour doesn't mean you're playing for the same thing as your opponent. While winning it is, as always, the goal of every player it's become more and more important to consider what else comes along with victory: World Championship slots, World *Magic* Cup captainship, and Pro Players Club benefits create victories that mean more than "just" a Pro Tour trophy. 

**The Players** 

If you hadn't heard of 20th-ranked William Jensen yet this season, you've been living under a rock. The former Pro Tour player had returned to the game around his induction into the Pro Tour Hall of Fame, and embarked on a journey that's demonstrated his skill is more than worth of the game's hallowed few. Setting a new seasonal Grand Prix Top 8 record with eight this season, doubling his previous lifetime total, he's been on a march that earned him Platinum status in the Pro Players Club and, now, a potential seat at this year's World Championship.

Pierre Mondon has been heating up this season, with a Top 8 at Grand Prix Philadelphia and win at Grand Prix Oklahoma City. With his qualification for Pro Tour *Magic*2015 coming from that Top 8 it's been some time since he's played at the Pro Tour, but it hadn't stopped him from finding his first Top 8 there as well. While just Top 100 would have earned him Silver status – good enough for an invite to Pro Tour Khans of Tarkir– he is after much more returning to the venue for the Sunday stage. 

**The Decks** 

Jensen, like many of his teammates, brought White-Black Midrange to bear this weekend. With plenty of removal at its disposal, it works to maintain control of the battlefield until [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s, [Blood Baron of Vizkopa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Baron+of+Vizkopa), or [Obzedat, Ghost Council](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Obzedat%2C+Ghost+Council) lets it pull away with the win. Cards like [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion) and [Last Breath](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Breath) are just two of the many ways the white half of the deck complements the dominating power of [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie) and [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s. 

Mondon's Jund Planeswalkers was a deck he picked up from *Magic Online*. After finding success in Daily Events, Mondon decided to take it to the Pro Tour. With a 9-1 Constructed record going into the Top 8, it was a choice that worked out well for him. By casting powerful spells, including Planeswalkers such as [Vraska the Unseen](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vraska+the+Unseen) and [Xenagos, the Reveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xenagos%2C+the+Reveler) with removal and [Rakdos's Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos%27s+Return) to answer aggression and control, Mondon marched through decks of all types Friday and Saturday. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/qf_jensen_mondon.jpg)

*No. 20 Ranked Player William Jensen was looking for a win to cap off an incredible season with White-Black Midrange. Pierre Mondon, however, was playing for Platinum with Jund Planeswalkers.* 

  

**The Games** 

An early [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) revealed multiple copies of [Chandra, Pyromaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Pyromaster) in Mondon's hand, but it was a few more turns before one would appear in play. Missing a land drop on the fourth turn, Jensen was already behind when [Xenagos, the Reveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xenagos%2C+the+Reveler) appeared to begin producing haste-fueled tokens. [Banishing Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banishing+Light) was Jensen's answer as he drew out of his missing lands with [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections), though the Satyr still in play put him down to 13 life. 

[Chandra, Pyromaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Pyromaster) began to tick up for Mondon as Jensen kept trading life for cards every turn with [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections). Unfortunately, [Rakdos's Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos%27s+Return) is exactly the kind of card that fights that: Return for three mana meant Jensen went down to one card and 5 life, then 4 when he again used [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections). After killing off the Satyr token, Jensen dropped to 3 life still looking for a way out. [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie) revealed [Golgari Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golgari+Charm) and [Dreadbore](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dreadbore) but it was too little too late: two turns later Chandra had burned Jensen to death. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/qf_mondon.jpg)

*Mondon's Planeswalkers can do some serious damage over the course of the game.* 

  

Jensen led off the second game with an early [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress), clearing Xenagos the Reveler and leaving [Mizzium Mortars](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzium+Mortars), [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle), [Read the Bones](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Read+the+Bones), and [Rakdos's Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos%27s+Return) behind. A pair of [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) set Jensen up to draw plenty of cards, but Mondon began his own digging with [Read the Bones](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Read+the+Bones). 

[Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay) destroyed one of Jensen's Connections – though it was just replaced it next turn – and was followed by [Sylvan Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Caryatid) for Mondon, setting up [Chandra, Pyromaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Pyromaster) and [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle) set to Swamp: It was a roundabout way to deal with another one of Jensen's [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections). 

When Jensen cast [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) it revealed an updated hand: two copies of [Mizzium Mortars](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzium+Mortars), [Rakdos's Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos%27s+Return), and [Vraska the Unseen](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vraska+the+Unseen), the latter-most he decided to have discarded. [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall) dealt with Chandra, but [Rakdos's Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos%27s+Return) for three again ripped Jensen's hand down to one. Dropping to 9 by using Connections, Jensen cast [Banishing Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banishing+Light) to unlock his second and quickly refilled what he had lost. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/qf_jensen.jpg)

*Jensen relies on his connections to find him the answers.* 

  

[Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie) confirmed just two copies of [Mizzium Mortars](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzium+Mortars) sat in Mondon's hand, and it wasn't long before one was used. Another [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie) revealed Mondon had drawn his third [Mizzium Mortars](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzium+Mortars), but it was a freshly drawn [Chandra, Pyromaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Pyromaster) that killed the second Zombie. Again, [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall) handled the fiery Planeswalker and Jensen played his own with [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion). 

[Mizzium Mortars](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzium+Mortars) reset Jensen's first wave of Soldiers, and [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie) confirmed the third Mortars was still there but it didn't matter: the next turn Mondon drew and revealed [Rakdos's Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20140803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos%27s+Return), with plenty of mana to burn Jensen out on the spot. 

"Good luck," Jensen said as he extended the hand.

"Congrats on what is it... your fifth Top 8?" Mondon asked.

"Yeah." Jensen, ever stoic, left the stage for the final time this season.

**Pierre Mondon defeats (20) William Jensen 2-0 and advances to the Semifinals!**
