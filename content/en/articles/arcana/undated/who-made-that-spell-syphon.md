Sometimes there are subtle things happening in the background of a **Magic** card. These can be [irrelevant](/en/articles/arcana/2002/06-12-surfs-up), or they can be the secret focus of the spell. Take, for example, Spell Syphon.

At the center of Spell Syphon is a massive whirlpool, sucking in magic fire. But what's causing the whirlpool in the first place? Mouse over the image to see!

[![](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/1615_SpellSyphonFull.jpg)](#image)  
*Spell Syphon art by John Avon*

Here's what's actually happening in Spell Syphon according to the art description: 

> Action: A bunch of merrows are swimming in a swift circular motion (barely visible through the murky water) to create a large whirlpool. The whirlpool is sucking in several gobs of magical flame. Maybe shapes of faces can be seen in the flame-gobs. Focus: the whirlpool sucking in the spell energy
