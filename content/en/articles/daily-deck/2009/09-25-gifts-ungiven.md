Featuring the skins payout, in which each player won money for winning a match instead of receiving a large payout based on finish at the end of the event, Pro Tour–Philadelphia was one for the record books. Upstart Gadiel Szleifer finally sealed the deal with a win featuring his Gifts Ungiven deck. The powerful combination of Spirits and Arcane spells backed by the tutor engine of Gifts Ungiven allowed him to outplay his opponents all weekend long. [Read more about the event in the official event coverage archives](/en/events/2005/ptphi05/szleifer-seizes-sunday).
