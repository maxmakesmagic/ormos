Every Tuesday through September 29, at 6:00 p.m. Pacific (UTC-7), the newest episode of the Modern Super League's first season airs live on [twitch.tv/magic](http://www.twitch.tv/magic).

If you haven't already, check out Randy Buehler's [article](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/06-30-standard-super-league-finals-and-the-return-of-vintage) that gives an overview of what the Super League schedule is going to look like over the next few months as it builds towards the Super League Championships!

Follow the links below to take you to each player's decklist!
