Morningtide, as you may have heard, is a class-based set. We don't mean that in the sense of "snooty, monocle-wearing creatures", but in the sense of "everyone's got a job".

Three of those jobs are Rogue, Shaman, and Warrior. Rogues look like this:

![Rogue](https://i.ormos.uk/3a/df/3adf886f2bd5d8d96b4dce54c23c92bb5162c0414c76f6d1eb14ee4f1501d02a.jpg)*[Rogue Poster in PDF (1.7MB)](https://web.archive.org/web/20080107000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/1498_MORPoster_Rogue.pdf)*

Sneaky, aren't they? Shamans look like this:

![Shaman](https://i.ormos.uk/64/81/648153428e5490c617980bfe19aacc1c096279d8bcf3df81dbf94422613e8803.jpg)*[Shaman Poster in PDF (2.1MB)](https://web.archive.org/web/20080107000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/1498_MORPoster_Shaman.pdf)*

And Warriors look like this:

![Warrior](https://i.ormos.uk/34/f9/34f9bbc36fd9002a488646b6c83bf91f484c459d9554896e8abab0beaac695f3.jpg)*[Warrior Poster in PDF (1.8MB)](https://web.archive.org/web/20080107000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/1498_MORPoster_Warrior.pdf)*

Tune in tomorrow for the other two classes!
