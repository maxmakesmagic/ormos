Several tournaments happened last weekend in North and South America! Let's start in Chile, where hometown player Luis Navas won [Grand Prix Santiago](https://web.archive.org/web/20131108000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/chilean-navas-shows-madcap-skills), thus keeping the trophy in-country. Waaaay up north, in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, [Joel Lim won the Vintage half](https://web.archive.org/web/20131108000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/lims-fish-reel-deal-philly) of Eternal Weekend playing the deck Reel Fish, while [Ari Lax took down Legacy](/en/articles/event-coverage/2013/11-02-legacy-coverage-of-eternal-championships-2013) with Death and Taxes. Congratulations Luis, Joel, and Ari! For his win, Luis will be invited to Pro Tour *Born of the Gods* in Valencia, Spain, and if you'd like to join him there, one way is to win a Pro Tour Qualifier (PTQ)! This season's format is *Theros* Limited. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#bog)! Good luck; have fun!
