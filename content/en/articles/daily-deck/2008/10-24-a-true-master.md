Fans of the Esper shard will be pleased to see the fortunate forty shipped the way of Justin "Googs" Meyer in the Top 8 draft of the recent Grand Prix–Kansas City. The Minnesota native had many of the blue-white-black shard's usuals like Windwright Mage, Tower Gargoyle, and Tidehollow Strix, but where things got *really* interesting were with his rares. He managed to nab not one, not two, but *three* copies of Master of Etherium, a huge behemoth based on the number of artifacts he was playing and a free pump effect for his army to boot! You can read more about Justin [in the Grand Prix–Kansas City coverage](/en/events/2008/gpkc08/heroics-in-the-heartland). Better yet, you can start playing *Shards of Alara* Limited right now on **Magic Online**! Find out how you can get involved with casual drafts, Release Events, and more [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2008/10-13-shards-of-alara-release-events).
