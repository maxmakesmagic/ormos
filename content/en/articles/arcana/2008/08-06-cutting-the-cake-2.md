On August 2, two people named Morgan and Kristen were married in Fayetteville, Arkansas. Why do we mention that? Well, because of this:

![The Groom's Cake](https://i.ormos.uk/87/91/87917080b3d7eb2b6204179a0e99a99353e4a35e5df5c467093f155faf70686e.jpg "The Groom's Cake") 

That's the [Groom's Cake](http://www.google.com/) right there. It was made, we're told, by Rick's Bakery in Fayetteville. And they did a pretty good job, too! Wanna see him cutting into it?

![Cutting the Cake](https://i.ormos.uk/7e/39/7e3996188f0930db686a61a3674596ffa4d84c53d76c3ff38827fa6cb03e8dba.jpg "Cutting the Cake") 

If we had to guess, we'd theorize that the cake symbolizes a "new hand", and by cutting into it, Morgan is seeing what his new life will hold. Or it could be that he just really likes **Magic**. Let's take a look at the happy couple!

![Sharing the Cake](https://i.ormos.uk/6f/34/6f341fc5c89ef8fe2541c5690436250e3e9ecff641e7179aab847bd64a16f814.jpg "Sharing the Cake") 

Congratulations, Morgan and Kristen. The **magicthegathering.com** team wishes you all the best!
