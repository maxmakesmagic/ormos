Extended is a format known for crazy combo decks, and the recent Pro Tour–Austin didn't disappoint! Kuan Tian, who already has a respectable deck design pedigree, combined Thopter Foundry and Sword of the Meek to gain tons of 1/1 flyers. He spliced the combo into a Gifts Ungiven control skeleton and managed to finish in Day 2! Read more from Pro Tour–Austin in the [official event coverage](https://web.archive.org/web/20091029000000/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/ptaus09/welcom).
