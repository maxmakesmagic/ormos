The first major Legacy event featuring *Scars of Mirrodin* was held this last weekend in Nashville, TN. The StarCityGames.com Open series made a stop there, and some 200 people took a crack at defining the new world of Legacy. Rion Marmulstein used the popular Survival Madness strategy to make the Top 8, but added a new, *Scars*-fueled combo. By getting Phyrexian Devourer and Triskelion into the graveyard, Rion gives his Necrotic Oozes the ability to ping an opponent directly over and over again. Only a single player was able to survive the combo on the weekend!
