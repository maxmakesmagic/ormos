The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on February 23, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by winning. In whatever form that takes.

# Today's Must

1.  [What Does Winning in Commander Look Like?](http://www.starcitygames.com/article/34613_What-Does-Winning-In-Commander-Look-Like.html) | StarCityGames.com | Sheldon Menery

For me, it looks like my opponents mutually agreeing the game is over while I take a 10-minute turn, but I suppose that's why people throw fruit at me. For others, there are a lot of things that look and feel like winning in Commander—from finally pulling off that nine-card combo to everyone having fun to decks doing the thing they were designed to do, even if that thing is losing in style.

It's a good reminder that there are a lot of ways to enjoy *Magic*.

# After That, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [Heartfelt Thoughts from Pro Tour *Aether Revolt*](http://www.mtgmintcard.com/articles/writers/eduardo-sajgalik/heartfelt-thoughts-from-pro-tour-aether-revolt) | MTG Mint Card | Eduardo Sajgalik

Pro Tour *Aether Revolt* Top 4 competitor Eduardo Sajgalik recounts how he ended up on Team MTG Mint Card, how he found his deck, and just how he ended up on a *Magic* card (sort of).

1.  [The Gatewatch Era, So Far](http://www.starcitygames.com/article/34615_The-Gatewatch-Era-So-Far.html) | StarCityGames.com | John Dale Beety

Beety recounts the Gatewatch Era, as he calls it—the storytelling epoch *Magic* is currently in, following the Gatewatch as they traipse around the multiverse (yes, traipse), righting wrongs and making up nicknames for one another.

1.  [Five Hidden Gems of *Aether Revolt*](http://www.hipstersofthecoast.com/2017/02/five-hidden-gems-aether-revolt/) | Hipsters of the Coast | Brendan McNamara

Still can't crack *Aether Revolt* Draft? Yeah, me neither. Maybe it's time to look at things a bit differently. Brendan McNamara tilts his head to the side and squints to find five cards in *Aether Revolt* that we all might be undervaluing.

1.  [Against the Odds: Warped Statuary (Modern)](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/articles/against-the-odds-warped-statuary-modern?src=wizards) | MTGGoldfish | SaffronOlive

Saffron's been all over [Inspiring Statuary](https://web.archive.org/web/20170223000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inspiring+Statuary) since it was printed—so why not Modern? In one of his craziest brews ever, he tries to go off with [Warp World](https://web.archive.org/web/20170223000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warp+World) and [Inspiring Statuary](https://web.archive.org/web/20170223000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inspiring+Statuary) to, well, let's just call them mixed results.

1.  [A Guide to Sigarda, Host of Herons Commander / EDH for *Magic: The Gathering*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v8HuTVldPD8) | Tolarian Community College | The Professor

[Sigarda, Host of Herons](https://web.archive.org/web/20170223000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sigarda%2C+Host+of+Herons) is a bit of a weird bird. Or Angel. The Professor explores what you can do with her for Commander, complete with creepy-sounding opening montage that might haunt your dreams if you listen to it before bed.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [TGNYN's Blue-Black Eldrazi](/en/events/2017/competitive-standard-constructed-league-2017-02-21/competitive-standard-constructed-league)

And now for something completely different. *Magic Online* player TGNYN went 5-0 with this Blue-Black (and colorless) Eldrazi deck in a Standard Competitive League, pairing removal, disruption, and Eldrazi to form one monster of a deck.

What's most interesting is the blue, which, besides some sideboard cards, is here almost exclusively for [Baral's Expertise](https://web.archive.org/web/20170223000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baral%27s+Expertise), a powerful card that only pops up now and then. But when it does, it always signals something gross. In this case, casting [Baral's Expertise](https://web.archive.org/web/20170223000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baral%27s+Expertise) can let you immediately drop a [Thought-Knot Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170223000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thought-Knot+Seer), allowing you to snag one of the cards you just bounced. Between that and [Transgress the Mind](https://web.archive.org/web/20170223000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transgress+the+Mind), it can be difficult for your opponent to put together a coherent game plan, what with the Eldrazi always reaching into your brain, and all.
