This feature match highlights the raw power of an all-in strategy against an unprepared field. Red mages have been asking questions of the other colours - mostly 'What was your life total again?' - since the dawn of **Magic**, and the current Standard format is no exception. Scry is a powerful mechanic when it digs you closer to the next burn spell. Black-based decks in Standard are particularly vulnerable to burn, as two of their key strategic cards - [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) and [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) - play very comfortably into a burn deck's game plan. [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel) and [Blood Baron of Vizkopa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Baron+of+Vizkopa) can provide some relief, but they come at the higher end of the mana curve, which is always a risk... 

**Game 1** 

Liu opened with [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) showing Tan with a [Magma Jet](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Jet) and [Chandra's Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Phoenix). He summoned a [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s, which was promptly [Shock](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shock)ed. A second [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) took a [Searing Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blood), but Tan had his Phoenix and a [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) (in addition to the [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) damage) to keep the pressure up regardless. Liu had an [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay) for the Phoenix, then cast an [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) to try and draw into some more answers. Tan didn't give him the time, pushing through more burn damage and recycling his Phoenix to take the first game. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/r7_tan.jpg)

*"Are you a little burned, or is that just a deep Tan?"* 

  

**Raymond Tan 1 Yi-liu Liu 0** 

Liu's opening [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) took out a [Chandra, Pyromaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Pyromaster), and an [Ash Zealot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ash+Zealot) was met with an [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay). 

A [Magma Jet](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Jet) and a [Warleader's Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warleader%27s+Helix) went to Liu's head, and a [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike) took out a Nightveil Spectre. A [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm) knocked Liu to a precarious 4, but Tan's [Chandra's Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Phoenix) barely entered the red zone before it was shot with a [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall). "No more charms!", pleaded Liu, as he tested the waters with a [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize). Seeing the coast was clear, he summoned a [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel) and then followed it up with another, gaining a little breathing room. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/r7_liu.jpg)

*Liu, barely containing his overwhelming joy at this most fortuitous of matchups.* 

  

Unfortunately for Liu, his room ran short as Tan threw a freshly drawn [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike) at him, reanimated his Phoenix and finished things off in short order with another flurry of burn to the dome. 

**Raymond Tan 2 - Yi-liu Liu 0**
