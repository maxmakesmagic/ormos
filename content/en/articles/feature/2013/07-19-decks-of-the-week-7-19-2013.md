Prereleases occurred for ***Magic** 2014* all across the globe last weekend, and today it goes on sale! It's hard to know what the set will do to Standard, which is this season's PTQ format. Get ready for your local PTQ by checking out these decklists from recent events and find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#theros). Good luck; have fun!
