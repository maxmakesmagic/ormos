Fresh off his victory at the Star City Games Invitational, Pat Cox shares his deck choice for Pro Tour Nagoya: White Weenie featuring [Puresteel Paladin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Puresteel+Paladin). Designed by PT champion Mark Herberholz, Cox aims to equip a [Sword of War and Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+War+and+Peace+) as soon as possible.
