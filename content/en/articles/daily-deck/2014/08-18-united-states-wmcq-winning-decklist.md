World *Magic* Cup Qualifiers happened all over the world this past weekend. One of the first to punch his ticket to the World *Magic* Cup was Isaac Sears, who won his WMCQ in the United States with Rabblemaster Red. Congratulations to Isaac! See the full schedule of World *Magic* Cup Qualifers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20140818000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/wmcq/schedule).
