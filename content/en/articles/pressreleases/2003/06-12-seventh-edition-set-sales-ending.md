### Parting Is Such Sweet Sorrow

* * *

It's time for yet another phase of ***Magic** Online* to go into effect -- ending the sales of a digital set. We all knew it would happen eventually, and that time is less than two months from now.

<table><tbody><tr><td><p>On July 28, 2003 at 12 midnight PDT, the <i>Seventh Edition </i>set will no longer be available at the <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20030612000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com&amp;origin=magic_magiconline_news052903"><i><b>Magic </b>Online </i>Store</a>. This also means that all <b>Limited</b> events that currently use <i>Seventh Edition </i> cards will begin using <i>Eighth Edition</i> cards at the same time. This includes draft queues, leagues, and Limited events in the <b>Premier Events</b> room.</p>&#10;<p>Then, on September 1, 2003, the <i>Eighth Edition </i>set will rotate into Standard format in both physical <b>Magic </b>events and <i><b>Magic </b>Online </i>events. As a reminder, this <i>doesn't</i> mean you won't be able to use your <i>Seventh Edition </i>cards in <i><b>Magic </b>Online </i>anymore. The Extended Constructed format will allow them in sanctioned play, along with many casual formats (Open, Tribal Wars, and Prismatic). Also, keep in mind that older cards that are reprinted in Standard-legal sets are allowed in Standard. For example, your <i>Seventh Edition </i><a>Birds of Paradise</a> that you spent so much time tracking down is still legal (reprinted in the <i>Eighth Edition</i> set)<b><a href="#deckeditor">**</a></b>.</p>&#10;</td>&#10;<td><img src="https://i.ormos.uk/55/77/5577b47d6223bb63576f0e2b5398d79c20743deae863884b418468d5718e308d.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

At the same time we say good-bye to the *Seventh Edition* set, the new *Eighth Edition* core set will become available for purchase. For those of you wondering what the development team has been working on, now you know. Within *one month* of the online *Scourge* release, the new core set will be available as digital objects. Ah, I hear the testing crew tapping cards as we speak.

So, on July 28 it's out with the old, in with the new. Come midnight that day, it's all about the eight.

* * *

**\*\***To find a Standard-legal card from an older set on the **Deck Editor** screen, you need to have either **Extended** or **Open** selected from the **Sets** dropdown list. (The **Sets** list filters your cards based on set, not on card legality.) You also need to have the **Show all versions** check box selected.
