Historically speaking, white-black-red has been one of the least successful color combinations in **Magic** tournament history. However, VirgoIII finished in 4th place in a recent Modern Premier Event with a white-black-red deck that looks exceptionally powerful and contains some of the strongest cards Modern has to offer. 

[Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant), and [Liliana of the Veil](https://web.archive.org/web/20140509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+of+the+Veil) offer quite a bit of control over a game, but often at the expensive of paying life that some decks can take advantage of. This deck, however, has [Ajani Vengeant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ajani+Vengeant) and [Lightning Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Helix) to recoup some of that life. This deck also features highly efficient cards in [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos) and [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20140509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls), both of which have a big impact while being hard to remove. Throw in some of the most efficient one-mana spells in Modern and you have a very powerful deck.
