1,900 players showed up bright and early yesterday morning for Grand Prix Madrid and after 9 rounds of Swiss, there are only six players remaining with a perfect 9-0 record.

Looking over their lists, two things become clear: Firstly, we still don't see [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy) at the very top of the field. Secondly, the format is wide open! Among the top decks we can find the most expected deck just once, a Blue-Red Delver (with a splash of white) piloted by (5) Ivan Floch, as well as old favorites like [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin), [Scapeshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scapeshift). [Slippery Bogle](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slippery+Bogle)s, and Zoo. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpmad14/9-0-players.jpg)

*After this round, only 3 players will remain with a perfect record!* 

  

The standout deck is Ricardo van den Bogaard's build that features [Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut%2C+the+Molten+Pinnacle), but that doesn't run [Scapeshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scapeshift). Instead, he tries to overpower opponents with a combination of [Through the Breach](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Through+the+Breach) that gives him access to powerful creatures like [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan) and [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Aeons+Torn). Thanks to [Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut%2C+the+Molten+Pinnacle), he does have an alternative way of finishing games. We will definitely talk to van den Bogaard over the course of the day to learn a little more about his thinking process when he first put the deck together. 

Without further ado, here they are, the 9-0 lists of Grand Prix Madrid 2014:
