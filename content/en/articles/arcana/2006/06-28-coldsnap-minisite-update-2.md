![](https://i.ormos.uk/68/7b/687bdfbc334bed31a01a2fcd3585a6c7413e753fa74308988e9c6b4a1fb84aa0.jpg)Today? Three words. More. *Coldsnap*. Goodies.

We've chipped a bit more chilly content for you out of the glaciers of northwestern Terisiare, and hung them out to dry on the [*Coldsnap* minisite](https://web.archive.org/web/20060628000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/coldsnap/home). Here's what we found:

*   NOW: “The Battle of Kjeldor”: Original fiction by *Coldsna*p flavor text writer Monty Ashley, featuring more art from *Coldsnap*!
*   NOW: Volume 2 of 2 of that meticulously-scanned classic *Ice Age* comic!
*   LATER: Original fiction by *Coldsnap* flavor text writer Adam Lee!

Turn up your collar, [head on over](https://web.archive.org/web/20060628000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/coldsnaplore/home) and check it out!
