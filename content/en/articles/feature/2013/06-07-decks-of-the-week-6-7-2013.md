Exciting news for Modern players, as *[Modern Masters](https://web.archive.org/web/20140413152922/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/modernmasters/productinfo)* releases today! There'll be a [Limited Grand Prix featuring it](/en/events/2013/lasvegas13/grand-prix-las-vegas-event-information) later this month, but since the current PTQ season is Standard, let's help you keep up on that metagame. Check out these Top 8 Standard decks from recent PTQs and WMCQs, as well as various decks from recent **Magic Online** events. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#theros). Good luck; have fun!
