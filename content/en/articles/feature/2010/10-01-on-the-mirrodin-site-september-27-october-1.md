Welcome back to "On the Mirrodin Site," where we show off the week's highlights from the [Mirrodin Site](https://web.archive.org/web/20140223104428/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/). This week, the Phyrexians took their invasion to the site itself, allowing people to decide which faction they would stand with.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/activity/da550_thumb.jpg)  

To see the Phyrexian version of the site (which comes with its own updates, field reports, wallpapers, screen savers, and so on), just go to the [Mirrodin Site](https://web.archive.org/web/20140223104428/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/) and click "Change Faction!"
