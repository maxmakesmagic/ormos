After two months of Modern staples as Friday Night *Magic* promos, September returns us to the world of Standard with a new promo from *Dragons of Tarkir*. First, let's start with those two major Modern months.

**July**

Participate in an FNM in July, and you might just walk away with one of the best removal spells ever printed. Path to Exile is a near-universal staple in Modern, and July will grant you an opportunity to stand out further from the pack with this new version—now featuring art from Raf Sarmento.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/EN_cardpromo_PathofExile.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_promo_PathtoExile.jpg)

Path to Exile Promo | Art by [Raf Sarmento](https://web.archive.org/web/20150630000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Raf%20Sarmento%22%5d)

**August**

Do you see? Do you see? Your vision of the future will be 20/20 come August, when [Serum Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20150630000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serum+Visions)—with art by Dan Scott—comes to an FNM near you.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/EN_cardpromo_SerumVisions.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_160402.jpg)

Serum Visions Promo | Art by [Dan Scott](https://web.archive.org/web/20150630000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Dan%20Scott%22%5d)

**September**

The trip back to Standard will be softened with this sturdy Bird Monk, [Orator of Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20150630000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Orator+of+Ojutai)—the Dragoniest [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20150630000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) ever. You'll be able to get your copy come September, whether you choose to employ Dragons or some other inferior creatures to do so.
