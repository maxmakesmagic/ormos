So here we are in Commander Preview Week. But preview cards don't normally get assigned to Arcana. We got to [break the news](/en/articles/arcana/2011/06-01-sol-ring) that [Sol Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20110607000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sol+Ring) is in all five decks, but that was a special case. Luckily, however, [UltraPro](http://www.ultrapro.com/) has a couple of new things coming out that tie with ***Magic: The Gathering** Commander*. Let''s take a look!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e4/af/e4af5dcf47ec9770008c4e5e62ccca8c6c97fcf60809ba2e0cd6248c9d12774f.jpg)  

That's the new Commander Deck Box. It holds 100 sleeved cards, which you'll agree is the sort of thing you want in a product with that name.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/78/45/78455a45f31bfa5c11e49b1ad169aa74286cbf2f03fc214fb745cb9a0321825e.jpg)  

Classy! You might be wondering why it's shaped kind of like a trapezoid. Well, that's in case you have any oversized cards you want to include with your deck!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/71/9b/719bf1966f4eb257a739b2b42245da7f4d39f07d3827f139419a61214961a818.jpg)  

As you might remember, the five ***Magic: The Gathering** Commander* decks each come with three oversized foil commanders, which are also included at regular card size. So let's say you want to bring along your oversized commander. Or some *Archenemy* Schemes. Or *Planechase* Planes. Or maybe you've got some Vanguard cards you like to carry around. Any combination of those will fit back there!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/50/0f/500fdc119b6076fab24e68eb3cabd5b1db374807295460d43ea6477b39ae9e27.jpg)  

Did you notice something odd about that last picture? Aside from the presence of Vanguard cards, which were just thrown in there because they were handy. They look a little shiny, don't they? Almost as if they were... in sleeves!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f0/29/f02928837dbac42178da3cb757157bc576217e8807165d1d18135fd141a9f5f0.jpg)  

So that's the other part of the announcement. Commander Deck Boxes *and* oversized sleeves!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/72/e1/72e1d1e6fd206865d1a6b3bf13ca908a4e3d423ddc746471ff682f763a450052.jpg)  

That's coming from UltraPro. Keen!
