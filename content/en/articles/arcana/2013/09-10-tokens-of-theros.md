There are a number of tokens in *Theros*, and we've already shown you a few, but now it is time to show them all to you.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/60/a5/60a5d804313b19bf6925ece554946025d3e462d7e7398c3e74b52519484c2226.jpg)

It's a bird! It's a plan—... No it's just a bird. You'll have to keep an eye out for the card which makes this token.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b6/76/b6762b255fd289b0d98593d6b3cad53ce8666cc0528874aae2e9a3fef8ab0f7e.jpg)

We debuted this adorable token at the PAX Worldbuilding panel and it remains one of my favorite tokens ever.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/24/20/24203bfa9f330504fce99105c59ab3ce6b4bc21b80d9b551295bdf0b40ffbab8.jpg)

What could make white enchantment creature Cleric tokens? Heliod of course!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/18/6d/186d0c5cf69c598d8de6ad48e40b5e042f55919bd61893d6dd29a814a4e6e00a.jpg)

In ancient mythology it was the god of the sea (Poseidon/Neptune) who created the horse. Which I always found odd because you'd think they'd make an animal which could swim better. In any case, I'm sure it's coincidence that this Elemental features a horse emerging from water.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0b/d6/0bd60e3ce4ef56381577027ff4282ffa652faf2bb96bbf349d17441aa5237c55.jpg)

This Golem is forged by the Hammer of Purphoros, the god of the forge.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6c/e3/6ce3128fec8010c74f19ccd9b029905c7ee0c7e37cb069c373f19416c7a17d7f.jpg)

I love this Harpy flying over ruins of some city in a cloudy stormy sky.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/32/a1/32a1a81c501f950920124f2da028def0613c9a7ea9edaf6ad12b00ca51b5b543.jpg)

Xenagos's Satyr token featuring the red and green background.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/92/f1/92f117122bf06b7fe00c290b4c534355ba1b4310ee6221bc653bc4ec7b0b6713.jpg)

A red Soldier token!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/95/23/95234abeb715dd666fa574b072de3c8f2808772b03276648e6d78f4297c4e4c8.jpg)

And a white Soldier token!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/32/0c/320c06c55ba9f116b6abdd29daccd0d6969a73f6a5127930d5a51b6f2abf0aae.jpg)

And another white Soldier token! So many Soldiers!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ed/11/ed11e727a5d0fb569100e9d4aec26df3b9c0d4547660a02c22091e24f11a4b73.jpg)

Lastly we have Elspeth's new emblem.
