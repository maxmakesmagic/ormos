The 2014 World *Magic* Cup Qualifiers (WMCQs) are underway, as players from 72 countries vie for one of their country's coveted slots into the World *Magic* Cup.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/wmcq_forarticle.jpg)

The format for the World *Magic* Cup Qualifiers is Standard Constructed. You can see Top 8 Standard decklists from some of the WMCQs below.

More decks will be added after the second round of qualifiers (August 30-31), and the third round of qualifiers (13-14). For more information about World *Magic* Cup Qualifiers, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20140919000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/world-magic-cup-qualifiers-event-types-events).
