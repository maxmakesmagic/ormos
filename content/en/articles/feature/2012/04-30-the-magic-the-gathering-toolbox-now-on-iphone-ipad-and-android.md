With the *Avacyn Restored* release happening at your local game store in a few short days, now is the perfect time to download the **Magic: The Gathering** Toolbox! A no-cost download that is now available for iPhone, iPad, and Android, the **Magic: The Gathering** Toolbox includes a life counter, a deck builder, an event finder, and more. Any players who download the app for the first time will have access to the *Avacyn Restored* set included with their download. 

### iPad Screenshot

![](https://i.ormos.uk/55/8e/558e72376fd6d035130971c1e196de0d165764fe89597fb32cbbf3e9c4c20d96.jpg)  

Don't worry, if you have the original iPhone app, just update it and *Avacyn Restored* will be added to the deck builder and card database at no cost! You can find more information and screenshots [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20120430000000/http://archive.wizards.com/%20http://staging.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/feature/192b&date=4/30/2012), or check out the [original announcement](https://web.archive.org/web/20120430000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/magic-gathering-toolbox-2012-01-05) for even more screenshots. 

Now go download the app and head to your local game store for the *Avacyn Restored* release!
