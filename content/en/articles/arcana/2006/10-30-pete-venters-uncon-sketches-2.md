[![](https://i.ormos.uk/9f/5b/9f5b315e3aa0c2666969ddf6ffa8e066cac70523671c84d88d0f8525b468b50d.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20061030000000/http://www.wizards.com/uncon) [UnCon](https://web.archive.org/web/20061030000000/http://www.wizards.com/uncon) is the online convention run by and for Wizards community. Every year it cranks out virtual tonnage of games, contests, tournaments, roleplaying, and up-close-and-personal interaction with your favorite gaming deities. 

This year’s UnCon was no different. In one Wizards message boards thread, [Pete Venters took requests](https://web.archive.org/web/20061030000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/showthread.php?t=723365) for quick **Magic**-themed sketches, and then took it upon his mighty Sharpie to blast out an even [ten of them](https://web.archive.org/web/20061030000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/showthread.php?t=727066) for UnCon participants to enjoy.

Missed UnCon? Missed your chance for Pete Venters to draw your favorite **Magic** beastie? We recommend you tune in to next year’s UnCon. In the meantime, check out the sketches [from this thread](https://web.archive.org/web/20061030000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/showthread.php?t=727066)! (Click the “Show” button in the thread to see each sketch!)
