Side events are open to the public. All side-events are sanctioned events unless otherwise noted.

### Daily Events (Friday – Sunday)

**Daily Drawings**  
 There will be **Magic**TM product, **Magic** art prints, special promotional items and more! Get your drawing tickets at the information booth and each time you play in a Side Event! (No cost for tickets)

**8-Player Pick Up Single-Elimination Tournaments**  
 These tournaments will be added as requested (*subject to space and judge availability*).  
 Registration: 11:00AM – 11:00PM  
 Finalist will win 8 boosters for first place, and 4 boosters for second place.

**Prizes**  
 Events offer a prize pool of 2 boosters per player. Events with 29-players or less pay to top-4, and 30 or more pay to top-8. Qualifier Tournaments offer a prize pool of 3 boosters per player, paying to at least the top 8.

**Registration**  
 Registration for each event begins 1 hour prior to the listed start time at the tournament registration desk in the side-events area.
