## Nexus of Fate Update

We realize that [Nexus of Fate](https://web.archive.org/web/20180807000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nexus+of+Fate) hasn't worked its way into as many decks as we like, so we are releasing more into the wild on *MTGO.* Check out this [Tumblr post](http://wizardsmtgo.tumblr.com/post/176534688819/update-nexus-of-fate) for more information.

* * *

## Next Cube Spotlight Series Announcement Tomorrow

Check mtgo.com tomorrow for an article on the next Cube in the Cube Spotlight Series, written by its creator! These cubes offer a unique synergistic look around some of your favorite cards.

* * *

## Pauper Gauntlet Information

The highly anticipated Pauper Gauntlet starts this Wednesday! R&D Play Designer Dan Musser has all the information you could ever want in this [nifty article](https://web.archive.org/web/20190221122215/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/magic-online/pauper-gauntlet-2018-08-01).

* * *

## Chaos Draft Information

We know how excited everyone is for the Chaos Draft, and it is almost here! There'll be a behind-the-scenes dev blog coming later this week so keep an eye out for it.

* * *

## MOCS Playoff

MOCS Playoff event is this weekend, August 11 at 7 a.m. PT, and the format is Modern. To enter you must have finished with six or more wins in a MOCS Monthly event that has occurred since the previous MOCS Playoff event. The top 2 finishers will be invited to the 2018 Magic Online Championship, Pro Tour *Guilds of Ravnica* and the Pro Tour Challenge at that event.

We hope to see you there!

* * *

## PTQ Sunday

We're having a Pro Tour Qualifier event on August 12, at 7 a.m. PT. This event will feature *Core Set 2019* Sealed and entry will cost a Chandra or Jace PTQ Finals Entry Token. **NOTE**: This is the last chance to use Chandra PTQ Entry Tokens before they expire!

Learn more about *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20180807000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers).

## Downtimes August 29 and September 26

The next two downtimes will be August 29, 3 a.m.–noon PT/-7 from UTC and August 26, 3 a.m.–noon PT/-7 from UTC. Please note that there are no more 'extended' or 'normal' downtimes; in the new world with fewer downtimes, they're all the same length of time.
