If you like playing big mana spells early by using mana accelerants, you're going to love Kettle's Big Mana Block deck. Used to Top 8 a **Magic Online** Premier in the *Shards of Alara* Block Format, the deck plays cards like Keeper of Progenitus and Knotvine Mystic to hasten big mana spells like Titanic Ultimatum. Imagine the horror on an opponent's face when they pass the turn, comfortably protected with a Cancel in hand only to find a blazing X of uncounterable doom coming right at them from an over-sized Banefire!
