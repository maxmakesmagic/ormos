M**agic** players have contributed to the creation of more than just Forgotten Ancient and Crucible of Worlds. And we don't just mean the card submissions of **Magic** Invitational winners, either.

Other elegant mechanics, submitted via You Make the Card and the **Magic** Invitational, have made their way onto cards without even being chosen as the winner. 

For example, the *Ravnica* card Spectral Searchlight is based on a mechanic sent in during [You Make the Card 2](/en/articles/youmakethecard2/2003/06-23-you-make-the-card-2-step-5). 

![Spectral Searchlight](https://i.ormos.uk/20/11/20112f7b19932b37bd58f285aefb99eb2a28e4e787a858df9daa66c40692b012.jpg "Spectral Searchlight")

Although the original submitted mechanic had the artifact tap to grant one *colorless* mana, the printed version grants one mana of the targeted player's choice. That way it can act either as a *Ravnica*-friendly mana fixer, a la Darksteel Ingot, a flexible mana-granter for a teammate, a la the [Two-Headed-Giant](/en/events/2005/2hg/two-headed-giant-tournament-fact-sheet)-friendly Fertile Ground, or a tricky way to "ping" your opponent for damage, a la Granite Shard (or Rod of Spanking). Nice mechanic!
