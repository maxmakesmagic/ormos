If you haven't checked out [May's Open Events](/en/articles/feature/2006/05-02-open-events-may-2006), you may not know that a 4x Tribal Wars Open is coming up this Saturday at 9am PST. If you're curious about the metagame, I [recapped the last big Tribal 4x Open](/en/articles/intotheaether/2006/01-19-some-tribal-decks-from-the-4x-open) back in January, which featured Mono-White Clerics, No-Stick Wizards, Tooth and Nail Shaman, Elves, and Goblins. A recent 2x event (on April 9th, with 27 players) was a little less diverse, featuring No-Stick Wizards, Clerics, Tooth and Nail Shaman, and Goblins. If your deck can tussle with these currently dominating tribes, then you have a good chance at bringing home the bacon (er, packs).

For those who are new to the Tribal Wars format, here are the basics:

1.  1/3 of your deck must be creatures of a particular type.
2.  Banned Cards: Circle of Solace,Endemic Plague, Engineered Plague, Peer Pressure, Skullclamp, Tsabo's Decree, Umezawa's Jitte, Unnatural Selection

Towards the end of my [Tribal Wars Raging](/en/articles/intotheaether/2005/09-22-tribal-wars-raging) column, I listed some tips new players may find useful (though note this was written before Umezawa's Jitte was added to the banned list). In particular, Aether Vial seems to be quite underutilized and may be key to making the more color-intensive tribes more viable.

As another helpful tool, I went into Gatherer and did some Excel and Access tricks in order to produce a list of all the Tribes (Creature Types) currently available for MTGO Tribal Wars, and their current (pre-*Dissension*) populations. I've also grouped and color-coded them according to how challenging each Tribe is for building decks around.

### Singleton 4x Open

*Sunday, May 7th, 83 players*

Singleton is one of the lesser-played formats, so it was great to see a pretty good turnout for the Open!
