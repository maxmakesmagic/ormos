Welcome to another edition of [Sketches](https://web.archive.org/web/20040722000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/fullarchive&tablefilter=sketches:)! This week we take an interactive look behind the scenes of the art of *Fifth Dawn*'s **[Bringer of the Red Dawn](https://web.archive.org/web/20040722000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bringer+of+the+Red+Dawn)** by Christopher Moeller. 

### 1\. Art Description

Once a card is designed, an art description is written to tell the artist what the card will do, what its flavor is, and what the mood of the illustration should be. Here were the instructions given to Christopher for the project:

>  *Location: Oxidda mountain chain.  
>  Action: Show a huge crystalline humanoid bull in an aggressive pose.  
>  Focus: On the beast (5/5). Mood: Powerful.  
>  Notes: This magical beast is made of 100% crystal that looks like ruby or garnet.*

The cycle of *Fifth Dawn* Bringers were all concepted as manifestations of the energy of Mirrodin's five suns. They are crystalline to show that their power and their nature transcends the metal ecology of Mirrodin.

Christopher Moeller has been a consistent contributor to the world of **Magic** card art since *Urza's Saga*, with over eighty artist credits to date. You can see his work such cards as [Bloodstoke Howler](https://web.archive.org/web/20040722000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/sketches-bloodstoke-howler-2003-02-10), [Expunge](https://web.archive.org/web/20040722000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Expunge), the cycle of *Nemesis* [Seal of Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20040722000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Seal%2Bof%2BFire), [Meddling Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20040722000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meddling+Mage), [Pernicious Deed](https://web.archive.org/web/20040722000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pernicious+Deed), the cycle of *Odyssey* [Sphere of Law](https://web.archive.org/web/20040722000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Sphere%2Bof%2BLaw), [Siege-Gang Commander](https://web.archive.org/web/20040722000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege-Gang+Commander), and [Liar's Pendulum](https://web.archive.org/web/20040722000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liar%27s+Pendulum), among many others.
