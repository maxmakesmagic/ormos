You've only seen four *Ravnica: City of Guilds* preview cards this week, and already we're clamoring to show you their cool full-size art and the sketches that went into their creation.

On Monday you saw **[Agrus Kos, Wojek Veteran](/en/articles/feature/2005/09-05-life-in-the-big-city)**, a heroic investigator from the red-white Boros Legion. Here's the art behind it, by Donato Giancola.

*Agrus Kos, Wojek Veteran sketch by Donato Giancola*

*Agrus Kos, Wojek Veteran final art by Donato Giancola*

Also on Monday you saw **[Selesnya Guildmage](/en/articles/makingmagic/2005/09-05-city-planning-part-i)**, a preview of the new "hybrid" mana costs from the green-white Selesnya Conclave guild. Here's the art behind it, by Mark Zug.

![Selesnya Guildmage sketch by Mark Zug](https://i.ormos.uk/f3/45/f345a74178cc2b94c2522e793354ab8d841017978e78bd6f6770fadbc8c14262.jpg "Selesnya Guildmage sketch by Mark Zug")

*Selesnya Guildmage final art by Mark Zug*

On Tuesday you saw **[Carven Caryatid](/en/articles/seriousfun/2005/09-06-this-week-a-very-special-blossom)**, a new, tougher Wall of Blossoms. Here's its art by Jim Nelson.

![Carven Caryatid sketch by Jim Nelson](https://i.ormos.uk/51/2e/512ecea2dec873e6c122e21e8299434f82e6d15661e118ab7a57bfdccce67d94.jpg "Carven Caryatid sketch by Jim Nelson")

*Carven Caryatid final art by Jim Nelson*

Last but not least, today's preview is of **[Sunhome, Fortress of the Legion](/en/articles/theplaysthething/2005/09-07-the-boros-legion-brings-the-beatings)**, a mighty nonbasic land that blesses your Boros or any other troops with double strike. Here's its art by Martina Pilcerova.

![Sunhome, Fortress of the Legion sketch by Martina Pilcerova](https://i.ormos.uk/bf/74/bf745cdae620c49aaaa1a7e7bd43b9066aa690408e3372a984f0408b86a0c0b9.jpg "Sunhome, Fortress of the Legion sketch by Martina Pilcerova")  
*Sunhome, Fortress of the Legion sketch by Martina Pilcerova*

Expect more *Ravnica* card and art previews over the next two and a half weeks. Of course, you'll love the art of the special *Ravnica: City of Guilds* **[prerelease card](https://web.archive.org/web/20080416125625/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/prereleases)** -- but you'll have to be a bit patient for that.
