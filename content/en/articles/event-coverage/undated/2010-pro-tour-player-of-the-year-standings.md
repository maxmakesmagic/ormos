*Updated February 12, 2011* 

![Magic: the Gathering Pro Tour](http://www.wizards.com/protour/images/pt_lg.gif "Magic: the Gathering Pro Tour")  
 Congratulations to Brad Nelson, the 2010 Player of the Year. Nelson defeated Guillaume Matignon in a best-of-seven playoff at Pro Tour Paris after the two ended the 2010 season with the same number of Pro Points. 

The player with the most Pro Points over the course of a season is identified as the Pro Player of the Year. In addition to the title, the Pro Player of the Year receives invitations to certain tournaments throughout the next season. That player also receives travel and accommodations to each of the next season’s Pro Tour events and to the World Championships. 

This page shows all players who have received at least 10 Pro Points in the 2010 season.
