**Magic Online** Championship winner and StarCityGames.com writer Reid Duke worked hard to come up with an exciting deck for the Legacy portion of the StarCityGames.com Invitational a few weeks ago... and he found it! This blast from the past features several cards that haven't seen play in ages, topped off by [Pox](https://web.archive.org/web/20120103000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pox)! Take a look for yourself!
