### Jérémy Dezani

**Age:** 23  
**Hometown:** Paris/Orleans in France  
**Occupation:Magic**/Tennis

 

**Previous **Magic** accomplishments:**  
 Four GP Top 8s with one win, and Pro Tour *Theros* champion.

**What deck did you play and why did you choose it:**  
 Mono Blue. This deck gave me $40,000, so I couldn't reasonably make another choice.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them:**  
 No change; I love my build choices.

**What is the most important card in your deck:**  
 In general, [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) and [Thassa, God of the Sea](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thassa%2C+God+of+the+Sea). Against Mono Black, [Bident of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bident+of+Thassa); it's a crazy card in this matchup.

### Niklas Kaltenböck

**Age:** 24  
**Hometown:** Graz, Austria  
**Occupation:** Student, work for people with special needs

 

**Previous **Magic** accomplishments:**  
 None.

**What deck did you play and why did you choose it:**  
 White Weenie with a splash for [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm). I don't play Constructed normally, so I don't know the format, so aggro was the way to go for me. I chose it because it punishes slow draws and [Brave the Elements](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brave+the+Elements) is awesome.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them:**  
 Definitely cut [Wear & Tear](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wear+%26+Tear) and [Renounce the Guilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Renounce+the+Guilds) for something else. Maybe 1 [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay) (for bluffing reasons, flip it before every match).

**What is the most important card in your deck:**  
[Brave the Elements](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brave+the+Elements).

### Johan Prinzell

**Age:** 31  
**Hometown:** Stockholm, Sweden  
**Occupation:** Student

 

**Previous **Magic** accomplishments:**  
 None to speak of.

**What deck did you play and why did you choose it:**  
 Red devotion with a white splash. With 4 [Chained to the Rocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chained+to+the+Rocks) and 4 [Mizzium Mortars](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzium+Mortars) it has a good game versus Mono Blue. With 4 [Assemble the Legion](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Assemble+the+Legion) in the sideboard it has a good matchup versus Mono Black. I expected lots of these decks.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them:**  
 4 [Burning-Tree Emissary](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning-Tree+Emissary); in retrospect 3 was just a mistake.

**What is the most important card in you deck:**  
[Mizzium Mortars](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzium+Mortars).
