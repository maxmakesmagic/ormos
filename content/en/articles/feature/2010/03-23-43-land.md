The Legacy format allows players to use nearly all **Magic** cards that have ever been printed! With the exclusion of a few overly powerful cards on its banned list, Legacy covers all sets in the game's history. That means you get some interesting decks. Chris Woltereck recently won a large Legacy tournament, the StarCityGames.com Open in Indianapolis, using a deck playing over 40 lands! The goal of his deck is to put as many lands onto the battlefield as possible using [Exploration](https://web.archive.org/web/20100323000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exploration), [Manabond](https://web.archive.org/web/20100323000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Manabond), and [Life from the Loam](https://web.archive.org/web/20100323000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Life+from+the+Loam). From there it can beat people down with [Mishra's Factory](https://web.archive.org/web/20100323000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mishra%27s+Factory) or [Mindslaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20100323000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindslaver)-lock them using [Academy Ruins](https://web.archive.org/web/20100323000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Academy+Ruins). Sound like fun? Well now you can play too, because Legacy is coming to **Magic Online** soon!
