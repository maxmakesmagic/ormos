### Wu Xuan

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpbei17/gpBeijijng2017_Top8WuXuan.jpg)

**Hometown:** China  
**Age:** 33  
**Occupation:** Office Assistant  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Friday Night Magic Top 8

**Day 1 Summary:**  
 9 – 0 (2 Byes). I had a Black-Red deck with [Archfiend of Ifnir](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archfiend+of+Ifnir) and [Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer).

**Day 2 Summary:**  
 I went 2-1 with a Blue-Red Fling deck and 1-1-1 with a White-Black deck with nothing spectacular.

**What was your favorite memory this weekend?**  
 I loved every moment that I won with [Fling](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fling)!

**What is your favorite rare, uncommon and common in *Amonkhet* Limited, and why?**  
[Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer), because well, you tell me... I also like [Bone Picker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bone+Picker) because it is like a one mana card. As for my favorite common, it is [Fling](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fling)!

**Trials are a centric theme in *Amonkhet*. What was the biggest trial/challenge you faced this weekend?**  
 The Day Two Booster Draft portion was very difficult. It is very different from other Booster Draft formats.

### Yi Shimin

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpbei17/gpBeijijng2017_Top8ZhiYimin.jpg)

**Hometown:** Shanghai, China  
**Age:** 26  
**Occupation:** IT Sales  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** PTQ Finalist and Top 16 at Grand Prix Hong Kong 2015

**Day 1 Summary:**  
 8 – 1 (2 Byes). My Blue-Black control deck had [Pull from Tomorrow](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pull+from+Tomorrow) and [Cruel Reality](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cruel+Reality).

**Day 2 Summary:**  
 My score for Draft 1 was 2-1 and I had a Black-White deck with double [Bontu the Glorified](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bontu+the+Glorified). Then, I went 2-0-1 with a 5-color deck which had [Glyph Keeper](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glyph+Keeper) and [Heaven // Earth](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heaven+%2F%2F+Earth).

**What was your favorite memory this weekend?**  
 My teammate Chen Yang drew with me and I was very happy to make the Top 8. However, it was also sad that he came in 9th.

**What is your favorite rare, uncommon and common in *Amonkhet* Limited, and why?**  
[Liliana, Death's Majesty](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana%2C+Death%27s+Majesty) is great. I have won all my games on Magic Online whenever I cast her. [Lay Bare the Heart](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lay+Bare+the+Heart) is a great card that helps me win sideboard games. My favorite common is [Final Reward](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Final+Reward), and I especially love it when I used it to kill [Angel of Sanctions](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel+of+Sanctions) forever!

**Trials are a centric theme in *Amonkhet*. What was the biggest trial/challenge you faced this weekend?**  
 I had to sacrifice sleep in order to practice more on *Magic Online* for this event.

### Kelvin Chew

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpbei17/gpBeijijng2017_Top8KelvinChew.jpg)

**Hometown:** Singapore  
**Age:** 30  
**Occupation:** MTG Mint Card Pro Player  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Top 8 at Pro Tour *Return to Ravnica*. Two other Grand Prix Top 8s. Three-time Singapore National Champion.

**Day 1 Summary:**  
 7 – 2 (3 Byes). I played a four-color deck (no black) which had [Pyramid of the Pantheon](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyramid+of+the+Pantheon) and [Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer).

**Day 2 Summary:**  
 I went 6-0, drafting Red-White both times. The best cards were [Magma Spray](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Spray) and [Heart-Piercer Manticore](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heart-Piercer+Manticore) \[respectively\].

**What was your favorite memory this weekend?**  
 I mulliganed to 5 but won that game with a lucky topdeck to keep my Top 8 dream alive.

**What is your favorite rare, uncommon and common in *Amonkhet* Limited, and why?**  
[Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer), [Bone Picker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bone+Picker) and [Magma Spray](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Spray).

**Trials are a centric theme in *Amonkhet*. What was the biggest trial/challenge you faced this weekend?**  
 Having only done around 10 drafts, I didn't feel I was prepared but Jason Chung and Zen Takahashi gave me a lot of tips. Team MTG Mint Card and Team MTG Bent Card also provided a lot of advice during our discussions.

 I had a lot of great food!

[Combat Celebrant](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Combat+Celebrant) because it is spicy, [Cryptic Serpent](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Serpent) because it is dreamy, and [Fan Bearer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fan+Bearer) because it is like a removal spell on a creature.

 I didn't get enough sleep this weekend and it is difficult to stay awake sometimes.

**Age:** 28  
**Occupation:** *Magic Online* Player  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** This is my first Grand Prix Top 8!

**Day 1 Summary:**  
 6 – 2 - 1 (2 Byes). [Scaled Behemoth](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scaled+Behemoth) was great in my White-Green deck. It helped me win a lot of games.

**Day 2 Summary:**  
 My first draft was Black-Green splash Blue and I had [Liliana, Death's Majesty](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana%2C+Death%27s+Majesty).  I can't decide which one I like more, [Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer), [Angel of Sanctions](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel+of+Sanctions) or [Archfiend of Ifnir](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archfiend+of+Ifnir), but my favorite uncommon and common are [Edifice of Authority](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Edifice+of+Authority) and [Emberhorn Minotaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emberhorn+Minotaur).

**Age:** 36

[Gideon of the Trials](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon+of+the+Trials) and [Oketra the True](https://web.archive.org/web/20170507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oketra+the+True) was great in my White-Green deck.

 I had crazy good rares in my Sealed Deck.

 Winning my first Booster Draft was very tough, but once I did that I knew I had a good chance of making Top 8.
