## *Core Set 2021* Launch Super Event

So, you've scoured the previews and are ready to test your mettle against the best players around, eh? Here's your chance! We've got one more special *Core Set 2021* Launch Premier Event coming up in addition to our regular release events. Similar to the Super Qualifiers, this large scheduled event features "double" prizes for the Top 8 qualifiers (though there are no invitations awarded for this event), including complete *Core Set 2021* sets. If you want to brush up on the set before joining, Leagues start on Thursday morning. Have fun!

**Start Times**:

*   Friday, July 3, at 7:00 a.m. PT

**Format**: *Core Set 2021* Sealed Deck  
**Product**: 6 *Core Set 2021* Booster Packs. Product opened in Limited events *will* be added to players' collections.  
**Location**: The Limited lobby under Premier Events  
**Entry Options**:

*   40 Event Tickets
*   400 Play Points
*   40 *Magic Online* Qualifier Points

**Size**: 129–672 players  
**Duration**: A number of rounds determined by entries, with Swiss pairings. Each round plays up to 50 minutes, followed by a single-elimination Top 8.

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format Challenges are the first step toward qualifying for Premier Play through your favorite *Magic* formats. To learn more about Premier Play qualification, click [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020).

*Magic Online*'s Format Challenges now run once on Saturdays and once on Sundays for each Constructed format! On weekends featuring Showcase Challenge events, the Format Challenge that starts at either 6 a.m., 8 a.m., or 10 a.m. Pacific time is pre-empted by the Showcase Challenge—Format Challenges scheduled at other times remain in place. *NOTE: The Pauper Challenge is never pre-empted.*

## Build Notes

*   [Bolas's Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20200630000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bolas%27s+Citadel), [As Foretold](https://web.archive.org/web/20200630000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=As+Foretold), and other cards that allow casting spells without paying their mana cost can once again allow players to cast X spells. (Please note that per rule 107.3b in the Comprehensive Rules that in these circumstances X must be 0.)
*   [Containment Priest](https://web.archive.org/web/20200630000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Containment+Priest)'s ability now correctly only applies to creatures entering the battlefield without being cast if [Containment Priest](https://web.archive.org/web/20200630000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Containment+Priest) is already on the battlefield. Notably, casting [Exhume](https://web.archive.org/web/20200630000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exhume) and returning a [Containment Priest](https://web.archive.org/web/20200630000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Containment+Priest) will no longer exile the other creature returned.
*   [Conclave Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20200630000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Conclave+Mentor)'s replacement effect will no longer apply to itself and give itself an extra counter if an effect would cause it to enter with one (e.g. [Renata, Called to the Hunt](https://web.archive.org/web/20200630000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Renata%2C+Called+to+the+Hunt)).
