Announcing the Champions of Kamigawa site!

Conveniently located at [www.magicthegathering.com/kamigawa](https://web.archive.org/web/20040811000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/displayexpansion.asp?set=chk&page=1), the site will expand to contain stories about the background of the Kamigawa world, wallpapers and screensavers, and an archive of cards previewed on **magicthegathering.com**.
