*   **[Sketches: Grinning Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20040707000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/sketches-grinning-demon-2002-09-17)** - See the development of the art of Ol' Grinny.

*   **[*Onslaught* wallpaper and screensavers](https://web.archive.org/web/20040707000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/wallpaper-and-screensavers-2002-10-03)** - A [Grinning Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20040707000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grinning+Demon) desktop wallpaper is featured as one of the goodies linked from the *Onslaught* product page.

*   **[Sketches: Havoc Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20040707000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/sketches-havoc-demon-2003-01-28)** - Check out how the look of the Thomas Baxa demon came to be.

*   **[The new look of tokens](https://web.archive.org/web/20040707000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/new-look-tokens-2003-09-24)** - A peek at token promo cards in the new card face, featuring the [Promise of Power](https://web.archive.org/web/20040707000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Promise+of+Power) demon token.

And now let's add to the list -- here's a new demonic tidbit for today.

### *Mirrodin* Demon Horns

Although they were painted by three different artists, the look of demon horns in *Mirrodin* block is very consistent. Compare the jagged, curving horns of [Reiver Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20040707000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reiver+Demon), the [Promise of Power](https://web.archive.org/web/20040707000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Promise+of+Power) token card, and of course, [Demon's Horn](https://web.archive.org/web/20040707000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demon%27s+Horn):
