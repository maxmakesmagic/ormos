We've shown you some peeks at Wizards of the Coast meeting rooms in the past. [Mark Rosewater's 80,000 Words article](https://web.archive.org/web/20080626000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/mr240) showed a couple of the entertaining names, like "Graceland" and "The Bat Cave". But did you know that the meeting rooms actually contain themed artwork? Let's look!

![Graceland](https://i.ormos.uk/81/86/81865955253cabeb75ef4c5eb767892b4e75b2668211cd9f08b86b3af810eebd.jpg)

Here's Graceland. As you can see, it's got an enormous orange Elvis on the wall. He's a little distracting,

![The Bat Cave](https://i.ormos.uk/b4/9a/b49aa354955394de4926ed7fd965bf2be59794fa8c46d992ea16d90d8140afa0.jpg)

The Bat Cave has images from the old Batman serials. And, logically enough, it's right next to a meeting room named "Wayne Manor". There's no tunnel from one to the other, though. Here's the mural in Wayne Manor:

![Wayne Manor](https://i.ormos.uk/46/1a/461a50d056786130a89511ce247d1395e12f5f98bb6c72e8a677bda72132f5a1.jpg)

This is more the Batman that people expect. And there's Bat-Mite! If you don't know who Bat-Mite is, there is a world of weirdness waiting for you. We don't really have space to get into it here.

![Scrubland](https://i.ormos.uk/e3/92/e392aa7ff864ff901ee93a674284f806029004a5fc776962632be38454504857.jpg)

A number of meeting rooms around **Magic** R&D are just named after cards. Aaron Forsythe's office used to be [Castle Sengir](https://web.archive.org/web/20080626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Castle+Sengir), and here's [Scrubland](https://web.archive.org/web/20080626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scrubland).

One final note: these meeting rooms are all on the third floor, which is fantasy-themed. Here's a picture of one of the meeting rooms on a different floor, which has a different theme (except for the statue).

![Matrix](https://i.ormos.uk/f8/f4/f8f45721ad6973a238a7cd43412c56e5f0b47703f951c5b1477de98561a80d01.jpg)
