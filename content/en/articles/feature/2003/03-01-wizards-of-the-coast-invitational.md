Which leads us to the upset of the day. The very next round was Dev Team vs. Ex-Pro Tour, with Arjona and Doyle against Buehler and Forsythe. Most of us shook our heads in sympathy for the beating about to ensue. None of us realized at the time that it was Randy and Aaron about to be pummeled, losing with Ramon and John at 49 life. Towards the end of the game, there were a half-dozen people surrounding the Dev Team monitors to watch the win. Even more amusing was when Jeff Donais admitted that they could've won a turn earlier had they not been listening to everyone's advice.

All the big *LegionsTM* cards came out to play today. Charlie Catino begged for turns to get the last land he needed to put Akroma, Angel of Wrath into play. When that land was dropped, she proved her name by sending Bill Rose and Worth Wollpert to the graveyard. John Doyle let Phage the Untouchable reach out and touch someone twice in the same Two-Headed Giant game for a victory. I admonished Michael Elliott for being rude when he played Kilnmouth Dragon and amplified it by revealing the Imperial Hellkite in his hand. To top that off, Mark Rosewater had Mistform Ultimus in play, giving their team all three of the set's dragons at the same time. Ramon Arjona got a first-turn Dark Supplicant into play, which became a turn four Scion of Darkness. And Bane of the Living decided it was the black version of Wrath of God more than a few times.

The Standard portion was quite the array of fun versus serious decks. White weenie, 'tog, mono-black control, Sligh--you name it, we saw it. Including, well, whatever it was that Fred Royal was playing. In the end, it was red-green beats and red-white Slide that won the day.

Overall, the most fun was just being around these people who love the game and hearing them as they played. Some of my favorite quotes and moments: 

*   "This is going to be the longest weekend of my life."--Tyler Bielman as the first round finished after almost three hours.
*   Ramon letting everyone in on the secret that the shuffler is actually a goblin named Grog that lives in his Linux box.
*   Richard remarking that a good strategy would be to remove all stops and yields before you moved to a new computer.
*   "I can win if he just does nothing for the next nine turns."--Alan Comer, when he had a single 1/1 on the board and his opponent was at 9 life.
*   "I can't believe I lost to Alan."--Randy Buehler, who had constructed his deck specifically to beat Alan.
*   "You know, you guys are never going to live this down if they beat you."--Jess Roberts to Aaron and Randy as they played Ramon and John.
*   Calling the waitress back at the Keg after cashing out because John and Ramon showed up to tell Jess, me, and the Testmonkey Twins that they'd just finished round three. (We'd already been there for an hour-and-a-half.)
*   "I'm going to lose."--Charlie Catino, while he was at 27 life and Bill McQuillan was at 7 life (Quill was playing 'Tog).
*   "I knew I was going to win because he wouldn't have Compost in his deck."--Aaron Forsythe commenting on his loss to Jeff Donais who, unfortunately for Aaron, did.

After a personally gruelling week, a company-wide Nerf war and a day of watching these folks play **Magic** definitely left me on a high note. And, since I'm not going to be around for the rest of the Invitational, I'd just like to thank the participants, the organizers, and the community for a great event.

So, join us Saturday at 9am PST and Sunday at 8am PST for the rest of the action.
