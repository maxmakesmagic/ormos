Elves were all the rage coming out of [Pro Tour-Berlin](/en/events/2008/ptber08/lsv-elves-equals-champion) but headed to North Carolina States, Nathaniel Muesing had the tribe in mind for Standard. His build, running a shy 16 lands, overwhelmed opponents with efficient green creatures like Wolf-Skull Shaman, Nettle Sentinel, and Imperious Perfect. His late game was shored up with Overrun, huge amounts of Elf tokens via Elvish Promenade, and even drawing a ton of cards with Regal Force. You can find Nathaniel’s list and more in the [2008 State Championship archive](/en/events/2008/all/state-championships-2008-top-8-decklists).
