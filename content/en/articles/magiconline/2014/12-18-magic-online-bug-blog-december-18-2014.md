What Is the *Magic Online* Bug Blog?

While the [Known Issues List](https://web.archive.org/web/20141218000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-known-issues) is simply that—a running list of issues we expect players to encounter most often—this accompanying “Bug Blog” is the list’s companion. Here you’ll find updates regarding emergent new issues, details around issue statuses when applicable, known issue workarounds, and a running tally of Known Issues List updates. The Bug Blog will be updated each Thursday, in conjunction with the [Known Issues List](https://web.archive.org/web/20141218000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-known-issues) on the same day.

**Known Issues List Updates**

This week saw several items being updated or fixed during the Wednesday, December 17 deployment. In addition to this, two new known issues have been added to the [Known Issues List](https://web.archive.org/web/20141218000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-known-issues). Refer to the [Known Issues List](https://web.archive.org/web/20141218000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-known-issues) to see the most recent updates.

* * *

**Fixed During the Wednesday, December 17 *Magic Online* Client Update**

**Client**

*   Limited Deckbuilding | Cards that sometimes did not appear when moving from drafting to deckbuilding now appear

* * *

**Updated During the Wednesday, December 17 *Magic Online* Client Update**

**Client**

*   Collection | Deck automatically saving now only happens as needed instead of with every card change

**Server**

*   Events | If a player gets an error when submitting deck, client will check with server to verify card pool and update if needed

* * *

**Newly Added Known Issues as of Thursday, December 18**

**Game Play**

*   MMA | [Deepcavern Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20141218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deepcavern+Imp) forces you to pay the echo cost

**Client**

*   Trade | Using Shift + Click in List View in a trade causes the client to crash
