![](https://i.ormos.uk/90/05/90058cc0f5305c2c71e90b46ec5b8c48f5811276cab0de21c7e5870170c20fef.jpg)

Grand Prix-Nottingham was kind of an odd event as it doesn't feed the upcoming Pro Tour in Hawai but an unspecified Pro Tour next year. As such it made a welcome diversion before the avalanche of Goblins hits with the start of the Extended season. Instead, the players had to cope with an avalanche of Green. The guild structure of the Ravnica expansion meant that forests occurred in fully 88% of the 559 sealed decks bashing it out on day one.

64 players emerged from that minefield and then we got to see how the brand new multi-color set would draft. The results were fairly chaotic with players often straddling two guilds, but the obvious archetypes emerged -- although with differing results.

At first glance it seemed like a relatively unknown Top 8 although a little further digging revealed it contained two previous Grand Prix winners and another player with three appearances at the final stages of a Grand Prix. In the end it was the amateur Czech player Nikola Vavra and up-and-coming Dutch player Roel Heeswijk who battled it out in a tricksy match between Vavra's green-black-white deck and Heeswijk's blue-black deck.

Vavra won the first and then looked in a very strong position in the second when [Congregation at Dawn](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Congregation+at+Dawn) fetched the best creatures in his deck. Heeswijk was able to deal with the first two but a swamp-walking [Sewerdreg](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sewerdreg) managed to finally do in Heeswijk despite him bouncing it an amazing four times. A final desperate effort to gain life with [Ribbons of Night](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ribbons+of+Night) was countered as Vavra used a [Last Gasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Gasp) to kill his own [Stinkweed Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stinkweed+Imp). It was a fantastic end to a riveting game and congratulations go out to Nikola Vavra, champion of Grand Prix-Nottingham!

## Sunday, Oct 16: 7:40 pm - Top 8 draft: Roel van Heeswijk

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4f/11/4f11bbff75aa764edea61085b66d2c6afd028952f3e1923e609c51d78ba4f582.jpg)*The final draft table is set*

Roel told me before the draft that he was somewhat biased towards the blue/black Agro deck; with lots of fliers and cheap blockers. After the draft, Roel was shaking his head because he expected the draft to go a lot better for him. He told me that he's usually afraid that he's going into a certain color combination as a result of his bias, and that's why he was afraid to go into his favourite colors in this draft, even thought it may have been right. "Now to build the sealed deck", Roel jokes; as he was still deciding his colors during deck construction rather than during the draft. He could splash green for [Gather Courage](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gather+Courage), or white for [Conclave Equenaut](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Conclave+Equenaut), [Auratouched Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auratouched+Mage) and [Benevolent Ancestor](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Benevolent+Ancestor), but he had enough cards for a blue/black deck without any splashes. ## Sunday, Oct 16: 8:26 pm - Quarterfinals: Alfred Benages vs. Philip Reich

Philip won the die roll and elected to play first.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/48/0b/480bd6112b7a1a02ff82c02e1bbe74454a152c3cf77c9b0692ea6bc857f8a00b.jpg)*Alfred Benages*

His hand was a quick keep, and while Alfred's was slightly slower, it was also worth playing with. A mountain on turn one signalled the fast Boros legion, but there were no plays for on the first three turns. For Benages, who was also red white, there was a [Golgari Signet](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golgari+Signet) and the first creature of the game in the form of [Nightguard Patrol](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightguard+Patrol). Reich matched it with a [Screeching Griffin](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Screeching+Griffin), and had a Sunhome, Fortress of the Legion for turn five in order to attack in the air for four.

Benages turn four had brought a [Benevolent Ancestor](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Benevolent+Ancestor), and his five saw a Skynight Legionnaire who immediately hit the red zone. There was still no fourth land though. As Reich continued with the beats in the air with a lone double-striking [Screeching Griffin](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Screeching+Griffin), Benages built an increasingly threatening board, with a [Boros Guildmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Guildmage) joining the team, with haste for an extra 2 points of damage on attack.

Once Philip was down to just eight he started making additional creatures, in the form of a War Torch Goblin. He also had a [Faith's Fetters](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faith%27s+Fetters) to keep Alfred's [Boros Guildmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Guildmage) under locks. With the life totals at 12 each, the early rush of the Boros had largely ended, and it was time for the turns of big scary Falter-esque effects and burn.

Benages cast a [Viashino Fangtail](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viashino+Fangtail) and played a [Selesnya Sanctuary](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Selesnya+Sanctuary), suggesting that the off-colour Signet was not quite as off colour as it had initially appeared. Having drafted R/W/g in the swiss, it appeared that the same strategy was Benages' plan for the top eight.

![Benevolent Ancestor](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Benevolent+Ancestor)

Reich continued to attack. His flyer went unopposed, while [Benevolent Ancestor](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Benevolent+Ancestor) blocked the Goblin. [Rally the Righteous](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rally+the+Righteous) was then played on Screeching Griffin to untap it and make it larger. Due to radiance it did also untap Alfred's wall, allowing him to prevent two damage, but with the double strike coming from Reich's land, the attack was still for six.

On the attack back, Benages went in with his Fangtail and Legionnaire, before playing [Loxodon Hierarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Hierarch). The Elephant rare was plenty enough to elicit the scoop from Reich, who was all of a sudden in a race he was definitely losing, at 1 life, without the means to finish off his opponent.

Alfred Benages won game 1.

Philip again elected to play first, hoping that tempo in the pseudo mirror would be the deciding factor. A turn-two [Thundersong Trumpeter](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thundersong+Trumpeter) from Reich seemed like a powerful start when it was unopposed until Benages made a [Centaur Safeguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Centaur+Safeguard) on his turn three. He followed up with a [Viashino Fangtail](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viashino+Fangtail), but found it quickly hit by [Faith's Fetters](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faith%27s+Fetters) from Benages.

The following turn saw [Ordruun Commando](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ordruun+Commando) from Reich, looking to get in for some damage. Benages had a [Skyknight Legionnaire](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skyknight+Legionnaire), with just the same plan. Thundersong Trumpeter kept the Centaur down, but the flyer kept coming in.

The Minotaur cracked back, and Reich played a Griffin. For Bernages, it was just [Concerted Effort](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Concerted+Effort), which would make his Centaurs (and anything else as long as the Legionnaire was around) fly. This they duly did on the following turn, to trade with Reich's Screeching Griffen.

Reich had a rare of his own in answer with a [Firemane Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firemane+Angel), but when a [Nightguard Patrol](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightguard+Patrol) showed up for Benages, suddenly it didn't look quite as hot. Each of Benages' creatures would now have flying, first strike and vigilance. After some thought, Reich used his [Thundersong Trumpeter](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thundersong+Trumpeter) to stop [Skyknight Legionnaire](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skyknight+Legionnaire) from blocking, then attacked with just his Minotaur, unopposed.  -Turn 2 Think for ages, then play [Golgari Rot Farm](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golgari+Rot+Farm), and discard [Plains](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plains).

Nikola made a token with his land, while Ricard activated [Vedalken Entrancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vedalken+Entrancer).  A topdecked [Tidewater Minion](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidewater+Minion) came down for Ricard, but [Clinging Darkness](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clinging+Darkness) and a [Mortipede](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mortipede) activation took care of it. So Nikola had a 5/4 that could smash through Ricard's creatures, while Ricard had two win mechanisms on the table: a 3/2 flyer to attack a life total (unlikely to work), and a [Vedalken Entrancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vedalken+Entrancer) to attack a library (more likely to work).

Nikola played [Fists of Ironwood](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fists+of+Ironwood) on his [Golgari Rotwurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golgari+Rotwurm) and swung with all of his guys. ## Sunday, Oct 16: 10:45 pm - Finals: Nikola Vavra vs Roel Heeswijk

![](https://i.ormos.uk/93/0e/930ec4ffc4e1323f2c5d6496af3b7269969400ef4f89acdc0c2895b27608fe96.jpg)*Roel Heeswijk*

Well, this is it and England is guaranteed a home win as …. Oh wait, I'm dreaming. England's hopes of defending a GP title on its home turf ended in round thirteen, sigh.

Instead we have an interesting battle between the Czech amateur Nikola Vavra and up-and-coming Dutch player Roel Heeswijk. Vavra has been playing for around four years but mainly only casually with friends. Making the final here guarantees him a massive pot of amateur money to go with the main prize. Heeswijk has had a pretty good year on the Pro Tour. He finished 40th in London, picked up $875 from Philadelphia and managed 8th place at the team PT in Atlanta. You might not recognise the name now, but he sounds like a player going places.

Vavra's deck straddles green and takes cards from both Selesnya and Golgari guilds while Heeswijk has an aggressive Dimir deck.

Vavra won the die roll (after a few ties) and elected to start. He rushed out of the blocks with an [Elvish Skysweeper](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Skysweeper) and [Transluminant](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transluminant). The Skysweeper is okay against Heeswijk's black-blue deck so he took it down with [Clinging Darkness](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clinging+Darkness). Vavra kept up the curve with [Fists of Ironwood](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fists+of+Ironwood).

Then Heeswijk's deck kicked into action as he followed [Halcyon Glaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Halcyon+Glaze), a card that has really impressed me this weekend, with [Moroii](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moroii). Vavra is in this final for a reason though and negated the [Moroii](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moroii) with a [Clinging Darkness](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clinging+Darkness) of his own. Heeswijk made another flier in [Tattered Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tattered+Drake) (another 4 point bash with the Glaze) as Vavra ramped up his mana with [Farseek](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Farseek).

![Halcyon Glaze](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Halcyon+Glaze)

Heeswijk ran out of creatures and swung with [Tattered Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tattered+Drake). The life totals stood at 10-9 in Vavra's favor. He attacked back and [Moroii](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moroii) took Heeswijk down to 6 in his upkeep. Vavra had really followed up on his early start. A [Stinkweed Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stinkweed+Imp) meant he had to [Disembowel](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disembowel) the Glaze.

The Czech player passed another turn with no play. In contrast Heeswijk refilled his hand with gas from a [Compulsive Research](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsive+Research). His own crippled [Moroii](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moroii) was slowly killing him though. It was tight. He was at 5 life and an attack from the Drake dropped his opponent to 6.

Vavra had four cards in hand and five land on the table. Was he waiting for that sixth land to bust out Groodion or Corruptor. One of the lands was Vitu-Ghazi so he could at least start kicking out some Saprolings while he waited.

Heeswijk transmuted a [Dimir Infiltrator](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dimir+Infiltrator) and went for an ominous [Peel from Reality](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peel+from+Reality). However Vavra had a fantastic play in [Faith's Fetters](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faith%27s+Fetters) on the [Stinkweed Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stinkweed+Imp). It allowed him to send through the Saprolings to put Heeswijk at two life and put him back up to a precious ten life.

[Moroii](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moroii) took the Dutchman down to one life and he was in trouble. He brought out a [Sadistic Augermage](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sadistic+Augermage) to block, but it wasn't enough as Vavra killed it with [Clinging Darkness](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clinging+Darkness) and that meant one little Saproling would sneak through for the final point of damage.

Those early monsters, combined with a generous dollop of removal, had been enough to overcome what had seemed like a very good start from the Dutchman.

Vavra 1-0 Heeswijk

Vavra's draw was much slower in Game 2 and Heeswijk was able to ramp up to his signature card, [Junktroller](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Junktroller) on turn 4. He followed it with a [Stinkweed Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stinkweed+Imp) as Vavra dug a [Swamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swamp) from his deck with [Farseek](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Farseek).

Unfortunately Heeswijk appeared to have forgotten to drawn [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island)s and he passed turn 5 with just a cheeky nibble with [Stinkweed Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stinkweed+Imp). The fatties decided to show up from Vavra's deck as he summoned a [Golgari Rotwurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golgari+Rotwurm).

Heeswijk finally found his [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island) and a [Tattered Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tattered+Drake) entered play. He'd assembled an irritating defence to try to bust through. A [Fists of Ironwood](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fists+of+Ironwood) on the Rotwurm made it more likely. Heeswijk threw the [Junktroller](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Junktroller) in the way, which allowed Vavra to finish it off with [Clinging Darkness](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clinging+Darkness).

![Stinkweed Imp](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Stinkweed+Imp)

Heeswijk attacked back with the Drake and then Transmuted an Infiltrator into a [Last Gasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Gasp). [Stinkweed Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stinkweed+Imp) was back on defense. It was unlikely Stinky would get to block as Vavra cast Congregation at Dawn to find his three best monsters - [Keening Banshee](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Keening+Banshee), Sewerdreg and Drooling Groodion - and put them on top of his library.

Heeswijk looked in trouble here. [Keening Banshee](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Keening+Banshee) nobbled the Imp and let the Rotwurm and accompanying saprolings through for seven. Heeswijk dredged back the [Stinkweed Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stinkweed+Imp) and left the [Tattered Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tattered+Drake) on defence.

Vavra was firmly on offence. He sent his entire team into the fray. Heeswijk had a lot to think about. What would he block with his Drake and crippled Imp? Would he use the [Last Gasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Gasp) with both a Groodion and swamp-walking [Sewerdreg](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sewerdreg) on the way?

Heeswijk felt he had to. It seemed like the right decision when [Brainspoil](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainspoil) took down the Groodion. Heeswijk still had problems as the [Sewerdreg](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sewerdreg) entered play. A Vedalken Dismisser time-walked his opponent and kept the [Sewerdreg](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sewerdreg) off his back for a turn. He was still behind in the race as the saprolings nipped through to put him at 9. The [Sewerdreg](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sewerdreg) returned. Heeswijk dredged back Stinky and hit with the Drake to leave the scores at 11-9 in Vavra's favor.

The Czech player finally got the joys of an unknown draw step and found a [Stinkweed Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stinkweed+Imp) of his own. Blue is always the color of cute tricks, and a [Peel from Reality](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peel+from+Reality) to bounce the [Sewerdreg](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sewerdreg) again and bring back the Dismisser was especially nice. Vavra had picked the best three monsters from his deck and it seemed like Heeswijk had almost weathered the storm.

The Dutch player attacked with his [Tattered Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tattered+Drake), looking to level the life totals. Vavra thought for a while and then block it with Banshee and [Stinkweed Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stinkweed+Imp). What was going on here? Heeswijk regenerated the Drake and the Banshee appeared to have died for nothing.

[Faith's Fetters](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faith%27s+Fetters) put in another fantastic timely appearance for Vavra, shutting down the Imp and clearing the way for an alpha strike to drop Heeswijk to a slender 3 life. The Fetters had of course given Vavra a precious 4 life. Heeswijk took that four life straight off as the Dismisser returned to activate the Glaze and return the [Sewerdreg](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sewerdreg) to the top of Vavra's library for the umpteenth time.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a3/08/a3086f717c502c8d54d27b567898030198ca15f5723f5da4a52dd3d2b328f4ec.jpg)*Nikola Vavra*

Vavra recast the [Sewerdreg](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sewerdreg) and again Heeswijk had to look for an answer.

Again Heeswijk refused to lay down and die. He cast [Ribbons of Night](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ribbons+of+Night) on the [Stinkweed Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stinkweed+Imp), mainly to gain the life. Vavra was having none of that as he turned a [Last Gasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Gasp) on his own Imp. No life gain for Heeswijk. He was at 3 life. Vavra had a 3/3 swampwalker. It was not good.

Nervously Vavra pushed the [Sewerdreg](https://web.archive.org/web/20210615001206/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sewerdreg) sideways.

"Damage on…?"

Heeswijk smiled ruefully and extended his hand.

Nikola Vavra had just picked up his first Grand Prix title!

Nikola Vavra beat Roel Heeswijk 2-0 and is the champion of Grand Prix-Nottingham.
