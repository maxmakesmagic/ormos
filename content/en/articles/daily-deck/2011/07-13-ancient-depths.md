You're looking at one of the exciting new decks from **Duels of the Planeswalkers** 2012. Why is it so exciting? Let's forget for the moment the deck introduces a new planeswalker character, Kiora Atua, who has yet to be seen on the card side of **Magic**; how about the fact the deck uses green acceleration to cast giant blue fatties? Or the fact that it may be one of the decks used in the upcoming Digital Face-Off? What's that? You don't know what the upcoming Digital Face-Off is? Well then you need to [read up](https://web.archive.org/web/20110713000000/http://community.wizards.com/magicthegathering/blog/2011/07/07/digital_face_off)!
