There's really no column this week, folks. If you want to know what's going on inside ***Magic** Online,* all you have to do is read the [Announcements folder](https://web.archive.org/web/20040309000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/forumdisplay.php?s=&forumid=217) in the message boards.

I thought I might have a few more answers for you in today's column, but it seems I've been putting my foot in it of late. Instead of writing up something I might have to correct several times, contributing to the confusion and frustration we've all experienced today, I'll stick to what I do know.

The ***Magic** Online* team will be meeting Tuesday, March 9 at 9:00 AM (0900 hours) Pacific Standard Time to discuss what's going on. This was originally scheduled to decide if we were ready to continue bringing features back to the live ***Magic** Online* server. As we don't even have the beta server up at this time, the answer to that question is a resounding "no". So, the topic of conversation will be what the dev team came up with during the night and what we're going to do.

As soon as I can after that meeting, I'll post whatever I have to the Announcements folder. If I have enough for a full article, it'll also show up on the [***Magic** Online* page](https://web.archive.org/web/20050307092752/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline).

I'm sorry I don't have any more information for you at this time and I'm sorry for the continued delay in returning you to the live server. All I can tell you for certain right now is to watch the [Announcements folder](https://web.archive.org/web/20040309000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/forumdisplay.php?s=&forumid=217) of the ***Magic** Online* message boards for the latest information about what's going on with the game.
