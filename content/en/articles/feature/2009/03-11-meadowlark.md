Looking for a fun deck for Standard? Ken Yukuhiro took this interesting list to a solid finish at Pro Tour-Kyoto. He sought to abuse Mistmeadow Witch with numerous comes-into-play creatures like [Reveillark](https://web.archive.org/web/20090311000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reveillark), [Sower of Temptation](https://web.archive.org/web/20090311000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sower+of+Temptation), and [Mulldrifter](https://web.archive.org/web/20090311000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mulldrifter).
