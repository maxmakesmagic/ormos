As you know, Zendikar is a dangerous place, what with all the deadly perils and traps and whatnot. There are a few cards which demonstrate how the land itself provides dangers to the unwary adventurer.

Consider Rumbling Aftershocks. Apparently it's set in a part of Zendikar where the slightest tremor can cause giant boulders to plummet down onto helpless passersby.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/ad/d9ad8b257f7cf5b0338acc956a548219358051d2daa96de65792a99cc01970f0.jpg) 

Rumbling Aftershocks | Art by [Tomasz Jedruszek](https://web.archive.org/web/20100215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22tomasz+jedruszek%22])

Can you identify the place these two goblins are standing? Click here!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/86/b3/86b38b345c821b60c1307adf1c653a6f82fa0705b537bb73178d3971d883e0c9.jpg) 

Teetering Peaks | Art by [Fred Fields](https://web.archive.org/web/20100215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22fred+fields%22])

They're journeying through the teetering peaks! Too much magic in this area could easily dislodge one of those boulders. Better step carefully!
