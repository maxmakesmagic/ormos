Tomorrow (or even today, depending on your part of the world and when you read this) is that glorious day of the week we like to call Friday (or *Freitag*, *Vendredi*, or *Viernes*, if you're feeling international). And with Friday comes Friday Night *Magic*.

And with Friday Night *Magic* comes FNM Promos! Let's take a look at the promos you can pick up at FNM over the next few months.

# November

Seriously, how gross is this [Brain Maggot](https://web.archive.org/web/20141023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brain+Maggot) art? The same art you can pick up in November at FNM, of course.

# December

December might be winter for the northern hemisphere, but [Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20141023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames) is sure to keep many people nice and toasty. Or just plain dead.

# January

A new year makes a good time to add new art to your...Goblin deck? Kev Walker updates the most frenzied of Goblins. I wouldn't expect your opponents to do much blocking once you get your hands on a few of these [Frenzied Goblin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frenzied+Goblin)s.
