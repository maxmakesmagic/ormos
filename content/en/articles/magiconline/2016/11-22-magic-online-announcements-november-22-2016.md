**Upcoming *Amonkhet* Standard Pro Tour Qualifier Final on November 26**

Attention all Pro Tour hopefuls: a Pro Tour Qualifier (PTQ) is approaching! The first Standard Pro Tour Qualifier of the *Amonkhet* season will take place on this Saturday, November 26.

Remember, you are no longer required to enter into the PTQ Finals immediately following the Preliminary that you qualified through, thanks to the redesigned PTQ system that *Magic Online* Events Manager Lee Sharpe announced [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/11-16-pro-tour-qualifier-changes).

Learn more about *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20161122000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers#AER_schedule).

**The Year of Modern Flashbacks Continues with *Magic 2013* Starting Tomorrow**

It's 2016: The Year of Modern Flashbacks! If you want to build up a collection for Modern, these Flashback Drafts add everything you draft to your collection once the event is over. Even if Modern isn't your thing, you can enjoy a leisurely Draft through the planes of the past with rotating formats each week!

Triple *Magic 2013* Flashback Drafts start tomorrow and run until Wednesday, November 30 and are followed up by *Return to Ravnica* Drafts. Get the full schedule [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/magic-online-limited-events#YearOfModernFlashbacks) and mark your calendar for the formats you're most excited about!

**Upcoming Standard MOCS Monthly Event on Friday, November 25**

A *Magic Online* Championship Series (MOCS) Standard Monthly Event is coming up on Friday, November 25! Players can spend 35 Qualifier Points (QPs) to enter the event. The series will contain 24 Monthly Events, each of which qualify top players for one of the four Playoff Events. These Playoff Events qualify top players for the *Magic Online* Championship, a Pro Tour, and the Pro Tour Challenge event at that Pro Tour.

Monthly and Playoff Events also award top players MOCS Leaderboard Points for the year as a whole. Top players on the Leaderboard at the end of the 2017 MOCS will qualify for the year-end Championship event.

We look forward to seeing you on November 25 for the latest Monthly Event! Learn more about the 2017 *Magic Online* Championship Series [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20161122000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2017-magic-online-championship-series).
