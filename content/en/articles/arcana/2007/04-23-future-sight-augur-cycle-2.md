The cycle of [*Future Sight* "augurs"](https://web.archive.org/web/20070423000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/gatherer/index.aspx?term=augur&Field_Name=on&Field_Rules=on&Field_Type=on&setfilter=All%20sets) are creatures adept at prophecy, the art of foretelling the future. Their value comes from their dual uses—they can hang around to attack, block, and fight another day, or they can accelerate to their prophesied demise to useful effect.

Each of the augurs is depicted employing his or her method of soothsaying. Check it out:
