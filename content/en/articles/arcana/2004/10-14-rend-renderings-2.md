[Rend Spirit](https://web.archive.org/web/20041014000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Rend%2BSpirit)  
*[Rend Spirit](https://web.archive.org/web/20041014000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rend+Spirit) art by Stephen Tappin*
