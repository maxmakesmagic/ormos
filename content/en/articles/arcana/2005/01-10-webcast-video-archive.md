Magicthegathering.com's [Tournament Center](https://web.archive.org/web/20080503034345/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/tournamentcenter) provides you with extensive coverage of **Magic** events all over the world. In addition to play-by-play blog-style reports and individual results, magicthegathering.com brings you live video of high-level **Magic** play as it happens.

But what if you missed the *live* video stream? What if you want to see video from a tournament from a while ago? 

You want our **[Webcast Video Archive](/en/events/2005/webcastarchive/webcast-video-archive)**.

Check it out. Drown yourself in several gigabytes of **Magic** coverage since early in the 2003-2004 season. Watch the pros pilot their decks to and through top eights of four Pro Tours and a World Championship. The [Webcast Video Archive](/en/events/2005/webcastarchive/webcast-video-archive) is required watching for students of the game.
