The penultimate round of Great Britain Nationals, and here's how the four feature matches went down, minute by minute, as it happened.

Kevin Blake UB control versus Joe Jackson RUG Daniel Royde Eldrazi Green versus Jason Savage mono-red Daniel Harborne mono-red versus Scott Macpherson RUG Tim Pinder vampires versus Carrie Oliver RUG

5.12 Tim has double [Kalastria Highborn](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kalastria+Highborn) against Carrie, plus [Bloodghast](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodghast) and [Vampire Lacerator](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vampire+Lacerator). GAME Tim 1 - 0 Carrie.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Kalastria+Highborn)](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kalastria+Highborn)

5.13 Scott falls to 3 with [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) in play, uses [Obstinate Baloth](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Obstinate+Baloth) to get back to 7.

5.15 Royde uses [Green Sun's Zenith](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Green+Sun%27s+Zenith) to fetch a second [Overgrown Battlement](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overgrown+Battlement).

5.16 Double [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) from Daniel Harborne gives him GAME 1-0 over Scott.

5.17 Royde casts [Terastodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terastodon) against Savage.

5.18 Kevin Blake casts [Grave Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grave+Titan) against Joe Jackson, follows up with [Karn](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn) [Liberate](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liberate)d.

5.19 Carrie Oliver leads with [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise), but Tim kills it with [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember). No second land for Carrie, facing double [Kalastria Highborn](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kalastria+Highborn).

5.26 [Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm) from Scott clears the board, casts a [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer) of his own.

Against a red deck.

5.40 Savage kills the Baloth with [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember), Savage at 14, Royde at 23.

5.42 [Green Sun's Zenith](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Green+Sun%27s+Zenith) for six fetches [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan). Royde surely can't lose now.

5.44 [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan) gets Royde to 15 mana. That means [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20110820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Aeons+Torn). MATCH Royde 2-1.
