It's almost June, so it's time to announce the promos that Friday Night *Magic* players can look forward to over the next three months. They are shiny, and soon they can all be yours.

**June**

Looking for something? Well, if that something is a land (that's right, any land), [Sylvan Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20160530000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Scrying) will help you find it—with new art from Christine Choi.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/gI3A0sa68F.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/c4rdar7_gI3A0sa68F.jpg)

[Sylvan Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20160530000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Scrying) Promo | Art by [Christine Choi](https://web.archive.org/web/20160530000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Christine+Choi%22%5d)

**July**

Play in an FNM this July, and this could be you! Wait, *yours*, not you. (Wizards of the Coast does not endorse, condone, or engage in freaky magical person-stretching.) This painful-looking take on [Spatial Contortion](https://web.archive.org/web/20160530000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spatial+Contortion) from Joseph Meehan is July's promo card, and it's ready to twist your opponent's creatures into oblivion—or your own into damage-dealing monstrosities!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/idfSc4kUN4.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/c4rd4r7_idfSc4kUN4.jpg)

[Spatial Contortion](https://web.archive.org/web/20160530000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spatial+Contortion) Promo | Art by [Joseph Meehan](https://web.archive.org/web/20160530000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Joseph+Meehan%22%5d)

**August**

As if you need another reason to side with the Eldrazi. Last month was [Spatial Contortion](https://web.archive.org/web/20160530000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spatial+Contortion), this month is one of the best ways to cast it. Throw in a touch of colored mana, and you have a pretty versatile land with pretty art from one Jonas De Ro.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/QBLLRzefys.png)
