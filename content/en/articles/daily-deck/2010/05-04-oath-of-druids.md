The Grinders—single elimination qualifier tournaments held before U.S. Nationals each year—have yielded some of the most innovative decks in **Magic**'s history. Perhaps the most famous was the first deck to utilize Oath of Druids in a competitive format. This version was played by Adrian Sullivan of the Cabal Rogue. Now recognized as one of the game's most powerful cards, Oath of Druids had just been released in the *Exodus* set. What innovative new decks will you cook up for your National Qualifiers this year? For more information on National Qualifiers, visit the [official site](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2011-national-qualifiers).
