With so much on the line as the Pro Tour season concluded, Craig Wescoe defeated Dusty Ochoa in the Finals of Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze* here in San Diego. Wescoe's Green-white Tokens deck featuring Voice of Resurgence powered him to a 3-0 win and a Pro Tour title, and with it a seat at the **Magic** World Championship and Platinum status in the Pro Players Club.

Believed to be heavy underdog based on deck archetypes, Wescoe's blisteringly fast starts and ability to recover from Ochoa's Supreme Verdicts due to the Voice of Resurgence and Advent of the Wurm overwhelmed Ochoa and his Blue-black-white Control deck. 

Wescoe wasn't the only person taking home a trophy this weekend. Josh Utter-Leyton earned Player of the Year honors due to his Top 8 appearance, edging past two-time winner Yuuya Watanabe down the stretch on Saturday. Felipe Tapia Becerra of Chile won the Rookie of the Year title, becoming the first player from Latin America to win that award. 

In addition, all 16 slots for the [**Magic** World Championship](/en/events/2013/2013/2013-magic-world-championship) have been decided, as well as the [71 National Champions for the World **Magic** Cup](/en/events/2013/invitations2013/2013-world-magic-cup-invitation-list) . Join us for Worlds Week in Amsterdam on July 31-August 4 for those two epic tournaments!
