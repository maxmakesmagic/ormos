Poison is back in Standard! Although poison has been fairly absent in Standard recently (Inkmoth Nexus aside), **Magic Online** player Sopet has found quite a bit of success in recent Daily Events with a unique blue-black poison decklist.

This deck aims to get a quick poison start, leading off with Blighted Agent, Plague Myr, Phyrexian Crusader, and the ubiquitous Inkmoth Nexus. Once the deck has established an early board presence, Mutagenic Growth and Livewire Lash help surge the size of its creatures to maximize its poison output.

Livewire Lash is a card that is particularly strong in Sopet's deck. The creature that the Lash is attached to deals the damage—doling two poison counters out every time it's targeted by a spell. If you just so happen to have a Mutagenic Growth or two, Livewire Lash might just become lethal!

What's really unique about Sopet's version, though, is that, on top of the poison engine, the deck also features control elements! Mana Leak can help you play a tempo or control game, and Curse of Death's Hold helps get token blockers out of the way. Also at the top end is the relatively unplayed Corrupted Conscience—an excellent way to deal with an opposing Titan or Praetor!

Poison has a lot of fans who have been looking to return it to Standard popularity. If you've been looking for a fun, powerful, and relatively budget-friendly deck to check out for Friday Night **Magic** tonight, take a look at Sopet's decklist!

Sopet's Blue-Black Infect
