![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/ptogw/ptogw_t8_brown.jpg)

### Andrew Brown

**Age:** 23

**Hometown:** Los Angeles, California

**Occupation:** Game Designer

**Limited Record:** 4-2

**Constructed Record:** 8-1-1

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**

Pro Tour *Dragons of Tarkir* 18th Place

Grand Prix Denver Champion 2015

Silver Pro

**Did You Team up with anyone to practice for this Pro Tour? Who?**

Team East West Bowl

Special shouts to Eugene Hwang and Ben Weitz

**What Modern deck are you playing and why?**

Eldraggo \[Blue-Red Eldrazi\]

It uses [Eye of Ugin](https://web.archive.org/web/20160207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eye+of+Ugin) to power out powerful starts, plus [Vile Aggregate](https://web.archive.org/web/20160207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vile+Aggregate) = [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20160207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf).

**Are you on the side of the Eldrazi or the Zendikari?**

Eldrazi, not close. Get dunked Zendikar clowns.

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?**

Dream come true, and validation for countless hours of play.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/ptogw/ptogw_t8_dickmann.jpg)

### Patrick Dickmann

**Age:** 26

**Hometown:** Cologne, Germany

**Occupation:** Student

**Limited Record:** 3-3

**Constructed Record:** 9-0-1

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**

Grand Prix Antwerp winner

Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*, 3rd place

**Did You Team up with anyone to practice for this Pro Tour? Who?**

I want to thank my beloved girlfriend, who's always supporting me, my good friends Philipp and Giuseppe who tested and helped out a lot, as well as Frank Karsten's last-minute invite to the Affinity Aficionados.

**What Modern deck are you playing and why?**

Affinity, I've been playing it for years and really enjoy the playstyle! Also, Twin was banned.

**Are you on the side of the Eldrazi or the Zendikari?**

Zaendikari … the bad guys can't always win!

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?**

It'd be my greatest *Magic* achievement and probably lock me for Team Captain of Germany!
