The word "dinosaur" means terrible lizard, and while Molder Beast and Alpha Tyrranax are not proper dinosaurs but Beasts, they are pretty terribly frightening if you're seated across from them at the *Scars of Mirrodin* Limited table. That's what many of Ari Lax's opponents found out this past weekend at Grand Prix–Nashville. There he managed to finish 2nd place using a draft deck based around the green creatures. Ari's name for the deck? Dinosaurs, of course! Read more about Grand Prix—Nashville [here](/en/events/2010/gpnas10/xmas-comes-early-for-gerry-t).
