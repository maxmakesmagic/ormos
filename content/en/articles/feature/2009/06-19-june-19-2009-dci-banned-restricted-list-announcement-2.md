**Announcement Date:** June 19, 2009  
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2009

**Vintage**  
 Thirst for Knowledge is restricted

Crop Rotation is no longer restricted  
Enlightened Tutor is no longer restricted  
Entomb is no longer restricted  
Grim Monolith is no longer restricted

**Standard, Extended, Legacy, *Shards of Alara* Block Constructed**  
 No changes

Changes to **Magic Online**–only formats are now announced monthly in the [**Magic Online** Announcements forum.](http://forums.gleemax.com/forumdisplay.php?f=667)

For an explanation of this announcement, read the [Latest Developments](https://web.archive.org/web/20130903232710/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/archive.aspx?tag=latest%20developments&description=latest%20developments) column next Friday, June 26. For the complete list of all banned and restricted cards by format, click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20130408054028/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=judge/resources/banned).
