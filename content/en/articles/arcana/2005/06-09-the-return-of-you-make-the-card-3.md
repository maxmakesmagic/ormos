The promotion that brought you the chance to design Forgotten Ancient and Crucible of Worlds is back in action in [You Make the Card 3](/en/articles/youmakethecard3/2005/06-08-you-make-the-card-3-step-4-mechanic-vote). Rejoin **Magic**'s Head Designer, Mark Rosewater, as a virtual member of R&D and vote on one of ten mechanics submitted by **Magic** fans just like you. Ready?
