### FAT PACK

Includes a guide with pictures of all cards in the set, a card box with panoramic art, eight 15-card booster packs, 40 basic lands, a special-edition life counter, and excerpts from *Alara Unbroken*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/96/ba/96bae3b14ee0369e435c0d0217a6ff4e7e42f222df99f55664feda6a1e950c8a.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/44/7a/447a1d837b8ff6027c74c5563259cbcf297cdab899f43288f85af8809c5b8b03.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes a 41-card preconstructed deck, a rare foil card, a strategy insert, a learn-to-play insert, and one 15-card *Alara Reborn* booster pack.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/87/b5/87b5adba9d914246003e19333d2119158d1bc139751b2ff6b2133b575f30a6ff.png)*Alara Reborn* is a 145-card set featuring the new Cascade mechanic, multicolored equipment, and a twist on cycling that will help you dig through your deck for answers to dangerous new threats.

**Set Name:** *Alara Reborn*  
**Block:** Set 3 of 3 of the *Shards of Alara* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 145

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044820/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/EN_MTGARB_FAQ.rtf)

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Aaron Forsythe (lead), Mark L. Gottlieb, Alexis Janson, and Brian Tinsman

**Final Game Design and Development:** Matt Place (lead), Erik Lauer, Dave Guskin, Alexis Janson, and Mike Turian, with contributions from Mark L. Gottlieb
