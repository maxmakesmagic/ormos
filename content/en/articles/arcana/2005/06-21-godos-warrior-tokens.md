The *Saviors of Kamigawa* sorcery **Rally the Horde** adds a potentially enormous horde of angry Warrior tokens to your team. 

 ![Rally the Horde](https://i.ormos.uk/9b/1c/9b1c2419d65344b899058dbf13b30830c9b37bd3a1096536ef3d60a9aa96b8c5.jpg) 

Notice who's doing the rallying in that art -- it's Godo, that warlord of bandits (also illustrated by Paolo Parente), riding his water-buffalo-like beast and brandishing Tenza, his trademark spiked-log-on-a-chain (*also* illustrated by Parente):

![Rally the Horde](https://i.ormos.uk/ae/6f/ae6f5414308b931c00b9815d1af10339026915be6208a2017ef6009e963255e6.jpg) 

Next Monday, June 27th (the day after [Regionals weekend](https://web.archive.org/web/20110219103702/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/regionals)), *Saviors of Kamigawa* releases in ***Magic** Online* (with [release events](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603201311/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Article.aspx?x=magic/magiconline/article061705) shortly thereafter). At that point you'll be able to see this art for the Warrior tokens you create with Rally the Horde, illustrated by Mark Brill:

![Warrior token art](https://i.ormos.uk/1b/3d/1b3d5db03f58f73d11676fadce9251de0833d06acef6e5689e045cc62bdf10da.jpg)
