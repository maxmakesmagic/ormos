### Game 1

![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpsap02/a959.jpg)*世界選手権で学んだ白青繰る石田格* 奇しくも白系ウィニー同士のミラーマッチとなった。

福地が《不屈の部族/[Tireless Tribe](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tribe)》、 4 枚の《拒絶魔道士の代言者/[Spurnmage Advocate](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spurnmage+Advocate)》、石田が《陽光尾の鷹/[Suntail Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Suntail+Hawk)》《敬愛される司祭/[Beloved Chaplain](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Beloved+Chaplain)》に続いて《不屈の部族/[Tireless Tribe](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tribe)》《拒絶魔道士の代言者/[Spurnmage Advocate](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spurnmage+Advocate)》からディスカードによる《金切るときの声/[Battle Screech](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battle+Screech)》フラッシュバックと序盤から激しい展開になる。

後続が続かない福地が《栄光/[Glory](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glory)》で強引に押し切ろうと試みるが、《金切るときの声/[Battle Screech](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battle+Screech)》の分だけクリーチャーの絶対数が微妙に届かない。

![Battle Screech](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Battle+Screech)

6 ターン目《栄光/[Glory](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glory)》を持つ福地の攻め手が止まった時、制空権を握った石田が 1 本目を取ることになった。

**石田 1-0 福地**

### Game 2

先攻土地 1 枚でやむを得ずマリガンした福地の 2 度目の手札は《聖餐式/[Divine Sacrament](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divine+Sacrament)》、 3 枚の《虹色の断片/[Prismatic Strands](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prismatic+Strands)》という苦しいもの。

![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpsap02/a960.jpg)*独自の《狡猾な願い/Cunning Wish》チューンの福地貴弘* しかたなくキープするが、続けて引いてくるのは《不屈の部族/Tireless Tribe》《聖餐式/[Divine Sacrament](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divine+Sacrament)》・・・・

そんな福地を尻目に石田は豪華 3 枚の《金切るときの声/[Battle Screech](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battle+Screech)》で鳥トークンを量産し、ただ《虹色の断片/[Prismatic Strands](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prismatic+Strands)》を連発して耐える福地を追い詰めていく。  
 《綿密な分析/[Deep Analysis](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deep+Analysis)》で《カーターの怒り/[Kirtar's Wrath](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kirtar%27s+Wrath)》、《真理の領域/[Sphere of Truth](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphere+of+Truth)》に対する《ねじれの光/[Ray of Distortion](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ray+of+Distortion)》と万全の手札を整えた石田。

![Envelop](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Envelop)

いつまでたっても《不屈の部族/[Tireless Tribe](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tribe)》 1 体のままの福地は《狡猾な願い/[Cunning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cunning+Wish)》からも《虹色の断片/[Prismatic Strands](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prismatic+Strands)》を使って、ただ 2 枚の《カーターの怒り/[Kirtar's Wrath](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kirtar%27s+Wrath)》に望みを託そうとする。

結局その望みは届かなかったのだが、実は石田はそれすらも打ち砕く《被覆/[Envelop](https://web.archive.org/web/20020825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Envelop)》までも手にしていたのだった。

**石田 2-0 福地**

**Final Result:石田 2-0 福地**
