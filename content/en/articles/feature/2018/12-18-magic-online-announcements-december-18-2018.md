## State of *Magic Online* 2018

It's time to [leap](https://web.archive.org/web/20181218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leap) ahead and address the [elephant](https://web.archive.org/web/20181218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=201843) in the room: "What's going on with *Magic Online* now that MTG Arena is in the house?" The simple answer is MTGO will continue to offer a vastly different *Magic* experience in players' favorite formats. But that's not all!

Check out Chris's [article](/en/articles/magiconline/2018/12-14-state-of-magic-online-2018) to get the lowdown on MTGO events, 2019's increased prize pool, and more.

* * *

## Vintage Cube Returns!

The time has finally come—Vintage Cube is back! It's nothing like any other cube out there, and it's overflowing with iconic cards from *Magic*'s entire 25-year history. Draft the super-deck of your dreams starting tomorrow, December 19.

This time around, you'll have through January 17 to try it out. See the full card list and the most recent changes [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/2016/11-30-vintage-cube-cardlist).

* * *

## UMA Draft Events Extended

If you missed the *Ultimate Masters* Draft action, not to worry! The Intermediate Draft league has been extended on MTGO, now ending December 26. The Phantom events will continue until January 11 to allow players practice time for Grand Prix Vancouver and MagicFest Prague.

You can also check out Alli's [release article](/en/articles/magiconline/2018/11-28-ultimate-masters-magic-online-edition) to catch up on everything you need to know about the set's arrival and consequent *Magic Online* updates.

* * *

## Saturday MOCS Playoff

This Saturday, December 22, there will be a *Magic Online* Championship Series Playoff Event! The format will be Standard, and things will kick off at 7 a.m. PT. Only players who have qualified via a recent MOCS Monthly Event are eligible for entry, and the winner will receive (among other things) invitations to both the 2018 *Magic Online* Championship and as well as Mythic Championship London and its associated Mythic Championship Challenge.

Learn more about MOCS events [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20181218000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2018-magic-online-championship-series)!

* * *

## Sunday Mythic Championship Qualifier

On Sunday, there will be a *Guilds of Ravnica* Sealed MCQ Finals at 7 a.m. PT. This event costs 1 Limited PTQ Finals Token.

Learn more about *Magic Online* Mythic Championship Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20181218000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers).

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format challenges are the first step toward qualifying for premier play through your favorite *Magic* formats. To learn more about Format Focus events and premier play qualification, click [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2018/11-12-2019-magic-online-championship).

The *Magic Online* Format Challenges continue every weekend for Modern, Vintage, Legacy, and Pauper formats.

## Downtime January 16

The next downtime will be January 16, 3 a.m.–noon PT/-8 from UTC. Please note that there are no more 'extended' or 'normal' downtimes; in the new world with fewer downtimes, they're all the same length of time.

## Build Notes

Duel Scene | [Savage Stomp](https://web.archive.org/web/20181218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Stomp) and [Price of Fame](https://web.archive.org/web/20181218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Price+of+Fame) correctly have their costs reduced when their conditions are met.

Duel Scene | [Living Lore](https://web.archive.org/web/20181218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+Lore) will now correctly count the converted mana cost of each side of a split card only once.
