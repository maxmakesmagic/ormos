## PLAYERS COMMITTEE VOTING RESULTS

Listed below are the votes cast by members of the 2005 *Magic*Pro Tour Hall of Fame Players Committee. Voters were asked to vote for a single candidate. All players with 100 or more lifetime Pro Points as of August 1, 2005, were eligible to vote.

As the top vote-getter, Olle Råde becomes the fifth member of the 2005 Hall of Fame class.

**Olle Råde: 16**  
 Dirk Baberowski, Noah Boeken, Kamiel Cornelissen, Yann Hamon, Nicolai Herzog, Anton Jonsson, Mattias Jorstedt, Mark Justice, Raphael Levy, Eivind Nitter, Antoine Ruel, Olivier Ruel, Bram Snepvangers, Terry Tsang, Tom van de Logt, Jelger Wiegersma

**Chris Pikula: 15**  
 David Bachmann, Randy Buehler, Daniel Clegg, Brian Davis, Antonino De Rosa, Igor Frayman, Tommi Hovi, William Jensen, Osyp Lebedowicz, Gabriel Nassif, Steven O'Mahoney-Schwartz, Brock Parker, Dave Price, Ben Stark, David Williams

**Dave Humpherys: 9**  
 Rob Dougherty, Justin Gary, Brian Hacker, Eugene Harvey, Scott Johns, Brian Kibler, Ben Rubin, Tomi Walamies, Gary Wise

**Rob Dougherty: 7**  
 Dave Humpherys, Darwin Kastle, Zvi Mowshowitz, Masashi Oiso, Michael Pustilnik, Alex Shvartsman, Matthew Vienneau

**Mike Long: 5**

**Svend Sparre Geertsen: 3**
