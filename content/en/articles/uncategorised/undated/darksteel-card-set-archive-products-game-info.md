## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/49/46/4946e5caade022d1001c636e29d050c29290f3309a260fde3c898c61b3270af6.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, six 15-card booster packs, two premium cards, a special-edition life counter, and Th*e Darksteel Eye* novel.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/49/46/4946e5caade022d1001c636e29d050c29290f3309a260fde3c898c61b3270af6.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/49/46/4946e5caade022d1001c636e29d050c29290f3309a260fde3c898c61b3270af6.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719043940/http://magic.wizards.com/node/976486)

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e2/a1/e2a1ab1e9a36a0b3930b104527e1a510e5252d7d5bda8fd0f53696302728e25e.png)

This 165-card deck introduces two new abilities to the *Mirrodin* block: Indestructible and Modular.

**Set Name:** *Darksteel*  
**Block:** Set 2 of 3 of the *Mirrodin* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 165

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Chinese Simplified, Spanish
