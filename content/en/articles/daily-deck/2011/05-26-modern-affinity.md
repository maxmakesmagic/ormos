The Community Cup is at hand, and a new format has been announced for the online bash: Modern. The format will feature all card sets released since the debut of the "modern" card frame in *Eighth Edition*. Here's a look at an early example of a list for such a format, a Modern take on Affinity. Read the official announcement [here](/en/articles/feature/2011/05-19-2011-magic-online-community-cup-details).
