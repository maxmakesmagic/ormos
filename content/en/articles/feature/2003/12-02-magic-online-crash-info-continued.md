### Leagues Up - Premier Events Still Down

* * *

Here’s an update on various ***Magic** Online* issues as of Tuesday morning.

We believe we have narrowed down the cause of last week’s crashes. Our technical evidence indicates a correlation between **Premier Events** play and system instability which leads us to believe there’s a nasty bug somewhere in our **Premier Events** code, although we’re not sure exactly where. The good news is this means we are now comfortable turning leagues back on. The bad news is it may be a while before we run any more Premier Events. We are currently trying to put together a *Mirrodin* Release Event plan that doesn't involve any Premier Events and we hope to have something ready to announce tomorrow. (We’ll definitely say more about this topic by the end of the week).

Here’s what we’re doing for those of you who were in the leagues that were cancelled last week: 

*   Everyone gets to keep whatever product they opened during the league (you already have those cards in your account's collection).  
  

*   For each league you were in, you will receive two event tickets and enough product to join a new league of the same kind. (For example, if you were in an *Eighth Edition* league you’ll get 5 *Eighth Edition* boosters plus two tickets.) This product will be granted to you automatically sometime in the near future.  
  

*   If your league was in week 3 or week 4 we will award prizes based on the standings at the time of the crash. (This product will also be automatically granted sometime soon.)

If you managed to pre-register for a *Mirrodin* Release Event in the hour that they were up, you will also be getting your product back automatically at the same time the league product goes out.

Meanwhile, we haven't forgotten about the invisible "CVP" booster packs. We hope to have our fix to that bug ready to implement during the next downtime and we will let you know for sure if the fix is going in before the update is implemented.

That’s today’s update--hopefully we’ll have more to say tomorrow.
