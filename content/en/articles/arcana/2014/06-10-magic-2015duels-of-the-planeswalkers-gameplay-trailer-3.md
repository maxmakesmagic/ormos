Over the weekend, we debuted the gameplay trailer for ***Magic** 2015—Duels of the Planeswalkers* over on Gametrailers.com. It offers a first look at [new features](https://web.archive.org/web/20140610000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/1470), improved gameplay, and a far more frightening AI—not to mention the terrifying visage of Garruk hunting bigger game: You.
