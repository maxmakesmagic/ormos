On Monday, February 27th at 9:00 am PST, *Guildpact* goes on sale in the Magic Online store.

NOTE: *Guildpact* becomes legal in ALL Constructed formats the day it goes on sale (February 27). All casual rooms and constructed queues will use *Guildpact* starting on February 27. Constructed Premier events will not run until March 8 and will use *Guildpact* starting on that date.

*Guildpact* Release events begin Wednesday, March 1 and will have 4 components:

Release Leagues  
 Draft Queues  
 Premier Event Flights (2x and 4x Prizes)  
 Release Championship

***Guildpact* Release Leagues**

SIGNUP: March 1, 9:00am – March 8, 9:00am (the final 1 week, 2x league will start no later than 9AM on March 8). Release Leagues will have a maximum of 256 players each and will auto-spawn (as soon as a league reaches 256 players, another will begin registration).

LEAGUE DURATION: 1 week

PRODUCT: 1 *Ravnica, City of Guilds* Tournament Pack and 3 *Guildpact* Booster Packs.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 2x prizes, Participation Avatar: Teysa, Orzhov Scion; Records of 5-0 get Prize Avatar: Rumbling Slum. 

Avatars will be distributed Approx. 3 weeks after the last league concludes

*NOTE: The downtime will take place on Wednesday, March 1 at 8:00 am PST (instead of the usual Thursday time).*

***Guildpact* Draft Queues**

QUEUES BEGIN: March 1, 1:00 pm. 

FORMATS: Booster Draft

PRODUCT: 3 *Guildpact* Boosters, or 2 *Ravnica, City of Guilds* Boosters and 1 *Guildpact* Booster.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 4-3-2-2 and 8-4

***Guildpact* Premier Events (2x Flights)**

DURATION: March 2, 9:00 am – March 8 9:00 am (last starts at 9am). 2x Flights will have 32 players per flight and will start every hour, on the hour. Each flight will have a minimum of 24 players, maximum of 32 players.

PRODUCT: 1 *Ravnica, City of Guilds* Tournament Pack and 3 *Guildpact* Booster Packs, top 8 booster draft. The booster draft format will be 2 *Ravnica, City of Guilds* Boosters and 1 *Guildpact* Booster.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product (Events that start between the hours of 10 pm and 5 am will be "Nix Tix events and require 0 event tickets)

PRIZE SUPPORT: 2x Prizes, Top 8

All who participate in a 2x Prize Premier Event Flight will get the Teysa, Orzhov Scion avatar. Top 2 will get a Rumbling Slum avatar. In addition, the 1st place finisher will qualify for the *Guildpact* Release Event Championship on Saturday, March 11 @ 9:00 am PST.

***Guildpact* Premier Events (4x Flights)**

DURATION: March 2, 9:00 am – March 8 9:00 am (last starts at 9am). 4x Flights start every 3 hours. Each flight will have a minimum of 24 players, maximum of 128 players.

PRODUCT: 1 *Ravnica, City of Guilds* Tournament Pack and 3 *Guildpact* Booster Packs, top 8 booster draft. The booster draft format will be 2 *Ravnica, City of Guilds* Boosters and 1 *Guildpact* Booster.

ADMISSION: 5 Tix, Product (Events that start between the hours of 10 pm and 5 am will require 2 event tickets)

PRIZE SUPPORT: 4x Prizes, Manual Pay to Top 16 (Prizes for 9-16 place will be paid out within 72 hours of event completion)

All who participate in a 4x Prize Premier Event Flight will get the Teysa, Orzhov Scion avatar. Top 8 will get a Rumbling Slum avatar. In addition, the Top 4 finishers will qualify for the *Guildpact* Release Event Championship on Saturday, March 11 @ 9:00 am PST.

***Guildpact* Release Event Championship**

START TIME: March 11, 9:00 am PST 

PRODUCT: 1 *Ravnica, City of Guilds* Tournament Pack and 3 *Guildpact* Booster Packs, top 8 booster draft. The booster draft format will be 2 *Ravnica, City of Guilds* Boosters and 1 *Guildpact* Booster.

ADMISSION: FREE – (No Tix, No Product, Invitation only)

PRIZE SUPPORT: 6x, Manual pay to Top 64

The winner of the Championship will also receive a complete set of Magic Online Premium *Guildpact* cards.

Second, Third, and Fourth Places will each receive a complete set of regular Magic Online *Guildpact* cards.

Although players may have multiple accounts qualified for the Championship event, players not permitted to have more than 1 of their accounts entered in the event. Players are not permitted to have secondary accounts played by another person. If a player is discovered to have more than 1 account playing in the Championship either before, during, or after the event, appropriate penalties will be applied (including disqualification from the event with no refund of product or event tickets).
