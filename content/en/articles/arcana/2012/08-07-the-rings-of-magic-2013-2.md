You've probably seen the five [rings of ***Magic** 2013*](https://web.archive.org/web/20120807000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&set=%7C[%22magic+2013%22]&name=+[ring]+[of]), and you've also probably noticed that each of them its own distinctive style. But did you know that all five were illustrated by the same person? And here they are now, for your observational pleasure!
