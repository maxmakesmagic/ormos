***Aether Revolt* launches for *Magic Online* tomorrow, January 25!**

*Aether Revolt* will officially arrive on *Magic Online* following the downtime tomorrow, January 25. We will no longer be running Prereleases in *Magic Online* and will instead be launching directly into events. For details on the new release structure and what events will be available, [check the announcement here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/01-12-aether-revolt-launches-january-25-on-magic-online).

**Extended downtime tomorrow, January 25**

There will be an extended downtime on Wednesday, January 25, for *Magic Online*. Events close at 12:00 a.m. PT/8 a.m. UTC (that's midnight Pacific), and store activity and trades end at 2:45 a.m. leading up to the system coming down at 3:00 a.m. The system is estimated to return from downtime at 12:00 p.m.

**Build notes**

We will have a new build of *Magic Online* live tomorrow after the downtime. Here are the changes to expect:

General | *Aether Revolt* is deployed to Magic Online.

General | Corrected all links in the EULA to direct to the proper pages.

General | New Product Window settings will now be remembered more consistently.

General | Updated copywrite year in legal text.

General | Added the *Kaladesh* prestige avatars to qualifying accounts, which you can read about [in this article](/en/articles/magiconline/2016/11-09-magic-online-event-updatesnovember-2016#avatar).

General | Fixed an issue causing Unhinged land art to appear distorted.

Collection | The promo version of [Time Spiral](https://web.archive.org/web/20170124000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Time+Spiral) now includes art.

Collection | You can now find flip cards when searching for text present on the bottom of the card.

Duel Scene | Fixed an error that was preventing players from tapping newly played lands.

Commander | [Thelon of Havenwood](https://web.archive.org/web/20170124000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thelon+of+Havenwood) is providing bonuses to Thallids with spore counters correctly.

Commander | Added a commander damage display. [Learn more in Alli Medwin’s article.](/en/articles/uncategorised/2017/01-18-aether-or)

Modern | Fixed an error that would sometimes cause [Hero of Bladehold](https://web.archive.org/web/20170124000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero+of+Bladehold)'s tokens to enter the battlefield but not be attacking.

Modern | Fixed the error causing [Saffi Eriksdotter](https://web.archive.org/web/20170124000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Saffi+Eriksdotter)'s delayed trigger to have no effect.

Modern | Discarding [Bridge from Below](https://web.archive.org/web/20170124000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bridge+from+Below) to the ability of [Insolent Neonate](https://web.archive.org/web/20170124000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Insolent+Neonate) now correctly creates a zombie token.

Modern | [Porphyry Nodes](https://web.archive.org/web/20170124000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Porphyry+Nodes) no longer causes a soft-lock when there are two creatures tied for the lowest power and the controller has selected to Always Yield to the triggered effect.

Modern | [Revenge of the Hunted](https://web.archive.org/web/20170124000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Revenge+of+the+Hunted) now correctly forces all of the opponent's creatures to block the targeted creature.

Modern| Fixed an issue that caused [Rite of Ruin](https://web.archive.org/web/20170124000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rite+of+Ruin) to occasionally sacrifice the wrong number of lands.

Standard | Players can now view the top seven cards of their library when playing [Deploy the Gatewatch](https://web.archive.org/web/20170124000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deploy+the+Gatewatch), even if there are no planeswalkers in those cards.

Standard | Hitting CTRL+Z after selecting lands for [Fork in the Road](https://web.archive.org/web/20170124000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fork+in+the+Road) no longer has any effect.

 

NOTE: Tomorrow’s update will introduce a bug that affects resolving the abilities for Narset Transcendent, Domri Rade, and Scrying Sheets that reveal cards from the top of your library. [Full details on how to work around this issue can be found on the MTGO Tumblr](http://wizardsmtgo.tumblr.com/post/156320779929/bug-warning). We expect to have a fix out before the weekend.
