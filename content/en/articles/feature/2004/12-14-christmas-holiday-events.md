We are planning some special events for the Christmas Holidays. These events will run from Monday, December 20 through Monday January 3. We will be continuing to give away the Fallen Angel Avatar as prizes for most of these events. Events include:

*   2 week CHK League with 2x Prizes
*   "Nix Tix" Draft queues
*   Full Schedule of Premier Events
*   32 person, Auto-spawning CHK Limited Events
*   Prismatic Tournament with 4x Prizes
*   Standard Tournament with 6x Prizes
*   The first ever Singleton format Premier Event
*   Extended Tournament with 6x Prizes
*   CHK Limited Events with 6x Prizes

### Holiday Leagues

SIGNUP: December 20, 9:00 AM – January 3, 9:00 AM (the final 2 week, 2x league will start no later than 9AM on January 3). Holiday Leagues will have a maximum of 256 players each and will auto-spawn (as soon as a league reaches 256 players, another will begin registration).

LEAGUE DURATION: 2 weeks

PRODUCT: CHK Tournament Pack and 2 CHK Booster packs. Players may add 1 CHK booster after the first week.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 2x prizes

### *"Nix Tix"* Draft Queues

"Nix Tix" QUEUES START: Thursday, December 23 9:00 AM  
 "Nix Tix" QUEUES END: Monday, December 27 12:00 AM

"Nix Tix" QUEUES RESTART: Thursday, December 30 9:00 AM  
 "Nix Tix" QUEUES END: Monday, January 3 12:00 AM

ADMISSION: 0 Tix, Product

All draft queues will require 0 event tickets from Thursday, December 23 at 9:00 AM through Monday, December 27 at 12:00 AM **and** from Thursday, December 30 at 9:00 AM through Monday, January 3 at 12:00 AM "Nix Tix" Draft Queues will have a normal prize payout.

### Holiday Premier Events

DURATION: December 20 7:00 AM – January 2 11 00 PM.

All Scheduled Premier Events during this time period will have minimum 24 players, maximum 64 players. Formats include Standard, Extended, Prismatic, Singleton, CHK Limited, 8E Limited and MRD Limited.

All Events will be 1x Prizes.

All Top 8's will get a Fallen Angel avatar.

### Note about Premier Events and crashes:

As a general rule, tickets paid for an event will be automatically refunded, any product (in the case of Sealed Deck events) will not be *automatically* refunded by the system, but may be refunded by Tech Services. Make that you make note of the event number for the event in which you are playing so that any product refunds can be quickly resolved.

If a game crash cost you an entry fee and possibly prizes, visit [http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=company/feedbackform](https://web.archive.org/web/20060812221910/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=company/feedbackform) and supply them with as many details as you can. These would include tournament number, date, game numbers, time and opponents.

As far as Premier Event prizes are concerned, if a crash causes an event to cancel, prizes are normally not paid. Sometimes the system will "finish" an event and declare it over, sometimes events get stuck and we have to manually finish the event. In these cases, prizes that are paid automatically by the system are final.
