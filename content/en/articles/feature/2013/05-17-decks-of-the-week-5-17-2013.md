Congratulations to Quan Zhou for his win at [Grand Prix Beijing](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/zhou-brings-back-beijing) and to Sam Pardee for his victory at [Grand Prix Portland](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/its-pardee-time)! Both Quan and Sam are now invited to Pro Tour *Theros*, and if you would like to compete against them there you can do so by winning a Pro Tour Qualifier event! Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#theros)! Good luck; have fun!
