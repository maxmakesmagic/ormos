Pro Tour Hall of Fame member Mike Turian was the gunslinger at the Denver, Colorado Prerelease for *Scars of Mirrodin*. His Standard deck of choice? Whitecraft—a White Weenie strategy powered by the new keyword metalcraft. Mike plays white's familiar bear-type creatures backed by equipment, but metalcraft allows him to explode onto the battlefield with cards like Mox Opal and Memnite. From there it's all downhill for his opponents!
