Burning on the outer fringes of Standard are constant reminders of the powers of combo decks. For Daniel Villamizar, the strategy was good enough to take him to the Top 8 of a Pro Tour Qualifier for Amsterdam in Boston. His combo centers around taking tons of extra turns with Time Sieve, Time Warp, and Open the Vaults. Cards like Howling Mine and Prophetic Prism help fuel the combo, while Angelsong and Pilgrim's Eye provide some stalling along the way.
