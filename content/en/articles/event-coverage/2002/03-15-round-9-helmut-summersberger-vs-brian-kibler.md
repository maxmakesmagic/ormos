Helmut Summersberger vs. Brian Kibler

Alex Shvartsman

### Game 1

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5e/92/5e92dee3bbb0542993ec7130e5da5fccbf1de54d0191b75ffc8208a40dc40414.jpg)

Summersberger was not off to a good start. He threw away a one-land hand, only to draw six non-land cards. The five land hand was finally acceptable, allowing him to summon a first turn [Basking of the Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basking+of+the+Wurm) and putting a [Basking Rootwalla](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basking+Rootwalla) into play. 

Kibler went on riding his Looter. He discarded [Fiery Temper](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Temper) to kill off [Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel) and summoned a [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog). Once he cleared the board completely, Aether Bursting the Wurm token and using [Fiery Temper](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Temper) on the Rootwalla, Kibler was able to go on the offensive. Kibler dropped a second [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog), prompting the Austrian player to concede game 1.

### Game 2

Summersberger kept a reasonable hand, but his first turn [Careful Study](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Careful+Study) did not turn up anything useful, forcing him to discard an [Arrogant Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrogant+Wurm) and a Forest. Both players summoned [Cephalid Looter](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cephalid+Looter)s on turn 3, and Summersberger followed it up with yet another Looter – so the game might come down to which player can take advantage of their madness cards better. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c4/f7/c4f76663d628abf8bc19ebcfdee6d25abb52a33d04494d8de671c3d639037b65.jpg)

At the end of Kibler's turn Summersberger activated the Looter to put [Basking Rootwalla](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basking+Rootwalla) into play. Kibler had a perfect answer, casting [Violent Eruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Violent+Eruption) to kill all the creatures his opponent had in play. Kibler used Edict to kill the Wurm token and went on gaining card economy via the Looter, discarding [Circular Logic](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Circular+Logic)s and burn spells. 

Both players cast [Compulsion](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsion) and were cycling through their libraries quickly. Kibler got out a [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog) and was only a turn or two away from victory. Summersberger was holding an [Upheaval](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Upheaval) but spent several turns unable to drop a land despite cycling through two cards a turn with [Compulsion](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsion). Kibler was able to attack for the win before his opponent ever got to the point where he could cast [Upheaval](https://web.archive.org/web/20020315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Upheaval).

Despite being forced to play with twenty six lands in his deck game 1, Summersberger lost to poor land draws – though it seems like the matchup should be in Kibler's favor anyway.

**Final Result:** Kibler 2 – Summersberger 0
