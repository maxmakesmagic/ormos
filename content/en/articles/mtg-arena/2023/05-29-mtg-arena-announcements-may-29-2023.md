## Congratulations to Benjamin Broadstone, the Arena Championship 3 Winner!

[![Benjamin Broadstone, Winner Arena Championship 3](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/1920x1080_AC3_Ben_Broadstone_Winner_Graphic.png)](https://www.magic.gg/events/arena-championship-3) 

This past weekend featured the [Arena Championship 3](https://www.magic.gg/events/arena-championship-3), the pinnacle of competitive achievement in *MTG Arena*, and Benjamin Broadstone walked away with the championship title, $30,000 in winnings, and an invitation to this year's *Magic* World Championship!

Check out [the decklists from the tournament](https://www.magic.gg/decklists/arena-championship-3-standard-decklists)—including the Rakdos Breach deck used by Benjamin—and [visit twitch.tv/Magic to see the broadcasts from Arena Championship 3](https://www.twitch.tv/magic). 

## Start Your Historic Journey to Arena Championship 4

Reaching the Arena Championship as a competitor is a challenge, but if you have the skills, we have the paths that will take you there—and your first step on one of those paths happens this weekend!

[![Qualifier Play-In logo](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/logo_play-in_left.png)](https://www.magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) 

Compete in [the Historic format](https://web.archive.org/web/20230529000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/historic) in the [Best-of-One Qualifier Play-In event Saturday, June 3](https://www.magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in), to earn an invitation to the June Qualifier Weekend, your springboard to Arena Championship 4! 

You'll have another option to reach for the big events on Friday, June 9, with the Best-of-Three Qualifier Play-In. The format is Traditional Historic (Best-of-Three) as well.

Then, with invitation in hand, you'll be ready for the [Qualifier Weekend event, June 10–11](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend). The format is Traditional Historic (Best-of-Three), so start planning and practicing now!

## New Alchemy Rebalances Tomorrow, May 30

![Alchemy logo](https://media.wizards.com/2021/images/daily/urj5kf9j5pz5.jpg) 

We have a couple of rebalances coming Tuesday, May 30, to the Alchemy format.

### Mono-Red Rebalances

Mono-red aggressive decks are among the top performing decks on the Alchemy ladder, especially in Best-of-One matches. Most cards in mono-red are replaceable with others, but Kumano Faces Kakkazan, Traumatic Prank, and burn spells such as Lightning Strike are unique effects that are played in almost every version of mono-red.

We are only rebalancing Kumano Faces Kakkazan and Traumatic Prank to directly target mono-red aggro and avoid weakening red's removal in other decks.

*   Kumano Faces Kakkazan is the most efficient one-drop in mono-red that plays well with both creature-centric and spell-centric builds with its second chapter and by triggering prowess. **Etching of Kumano loses haste.**

    ![Kumano Faces Kakkazan](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/card_a-kumano_faces_kakkazan_hufupr85inln.png)

    ![Etching of Kumano](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/card_a-etching_kumano_pl7rucltrede.png)

*   Traumatic Prank efficiently answers large creatures that are otherwise supposed to be strong against red decks. **The cost increases to 3R (from 2R).**

    ![Traumatic Prank](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/card_a-traumatic_prank_thiquwlph6ni.png)
