### Entry Fee

The entry fee for all Pro Tour—Kuala Lumpur Qualifier tournaments in Japan is ¥3,500.

### Tournament Structure

The structure for all Pro Tour—Kuala Lumpur Qualifier tournaments will be modified Swiss-style, with the Top 8 players playing in a single-elimination round for prizes and invitations.

All Pro Tour—Kuala Lumpur Qualifier tournaments will be run using Sealed Deck for the Swiss rounds, with the single-elimination portion consisting of Booster Draft. Product for the Sealed Deck portion of the tournament will consist of 1 *Lorwyn* tournament pack and 2 *Lorwyn* booster packs per player; for the Booster Draft, each player will receive 3 *Lorwyn* booster packs. For information on these formats, please refer to the DCI™ Universal Tournament Rule and **Magic** Floor Rules documents on the [DCI Document Center](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/welcome-to-the-dci-document-center).

### Advancement

*   All players may participate in all Swiss rounds.
*   The top 8 players after the final Swiss round will advance to the Top 8 single-elimination finals.

### Top 8 Finals

*   All Top 8 matches will be best 2 of 3.
*   Top 8 matches will have no time limit.
*   The Top 8 players will be seated randomly at the draft table immediately prior to drafting; the Top 8 bracket will be determined using this random seating.
*   When necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will be used to determine final order in the standings.

### Notes

*   All Pro Tour—Kuala Lumpur Qualifier tournaments are run at Rules Enforcement Level: Competitive, and are 32K.
*   All Universal Tournament Rules and **Magic: The Gathering** Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this format document.
*   Players are responsible for bringing their own pen, paper, card sleeves, and appropriate counters.

### Invitations and Eligibility

Players already holding an invitation to Pro Tour—Kuala Lumpur 2008 are prohibited from participating in any Pro Tour—Kuala Lumpur 2008 Qualifier tournaments, with the exception of Grand Prix tournaments. Players may have earned invitations through performance at a previous Pro Tour, Player's Club membership level, winning a previous Qualifier or Grand Prix tournament, or by being an invited DCI player (valid upon the date of the official ratings invitation announcement for the appropriate Pro Tour). 

### Prizes

*   The winning player of each Pro Tour—Kuala Lumpur Qualifier tournament in Japan will receive an invitation to the Pro Tour and a round-trip air ticket from his/her closest airport to Kuala Lumpur.
*   Product prizes to be determined by the organizer.
*   The Pro Tour—Kuala Lumpur Qualifiers will also feature a special Amateur prize schedule. The Amateur prizes will be awarded to the highest-finishing amateur players *in addition to any other prizes those players receive from the main tournament prize schedule* . The highest-finishing amateur players will receive additional booster packs as follows.

    **Amateur Award Prize Schedule**

    <table><tbody><tr><td><b>Finish</b></td><td><b>Prize</b></td></tr><tr><td>1st</td><td>12 packs</td></tr><tr><td>2nd</td><td>8 packs</td></tr><tr><td>3rd – 4th</td><td>4 packs each</td></tr><tr><td>5th – 8th</td><td>2 packs each</td></tr></tbody></table>

    A player is considered to have amateur status as long as he or she has never participated in a Pro Tour tournament. Even if a player has been awarded pro points from a Grand Prix, as long as that player has not been to a Pro Tour, he or she is still considered to be an amateur.
