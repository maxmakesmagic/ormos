The Grinders—single elimination qualifier tournaments held before U.S. Nationals each year—have yielded some of the most innovative decks in **Magic**'s history. Perhaps the most famous was the first deck to utilize [Oath of Druids](https://web.archive.org/web/20100504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oath+of+Druids) in a competitive format. This version was played by Adrian Sullivan of the Cabal Rogue.
