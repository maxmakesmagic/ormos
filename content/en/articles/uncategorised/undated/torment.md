## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/bc/fd/bcfd29492a37affc63b096a12ea2433f413ddb699dc049befae9060f4f56ecaf.png)

This 143-card set includes two new game mechanics (Madness and Nightmares) as well as additional cards for the Flashback and Threshold mechanics of *Odyssey*.

**Set Name:** *Torment*  
**Block:** Set 2 of 3 of the *Odyssey* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 143

**Languages:** English, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719050329/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/faq/torment) (or download as [RTF](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719050329/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/products/Torment_FAQ_EN.rtf))

**Game Design:** Bill Rose (lead) and Mike Elliott, with Odyssey concepts by Richard Garfield and Mark Rosewater

**Game Development:** Henry Stern (lead), Randy Buehler Jr., Michael Donais, Bill “Quill” McQuillan, and Brian Schneider, with contributions from Paul Barclay and Brady Dommermuth
