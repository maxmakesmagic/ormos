The set *Weatherlight*, fittingly, is where the *Weatherlight* Saga began in earnest. And, like *Tempest* after it, *Weatherlight* had sequences of card art that told little pieces of the story.

The most notable of these is on the cards [Desperate Gambit](https://web.archive.org/web/20020617000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desperate+Gambit) and [Debt of Loyalty](https://web.archive.org/web/20020617000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Debt+of+Loyalty). In the former, [Gerrard Capashen](https://web.archive.org/web/20020617000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Gerrard%2BCapashen) is battling [Maraxus of Keld](https://web.archive.org/web/20020617000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Maraxus+of+Keld), and looks to be losing. But [Starke of Rath](https://web.archive.org/web/20020617000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Starke%2Bof%2BRath) is running up in the background.

[Debt of Loyalty](https://web.archive.org/web/20020617000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Debt+of+Loyalty) reveals that Starke backstabbed Maraxus, killing him. The flavor text shows Starke's true nature, however…
