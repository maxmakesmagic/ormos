Nicholas Rausch has you covered, and his deck is a proven winner! Manaless Dredge, a Dredge deck for Legacy that doesn't need mana to operate, allows Nicholas to fill up his graveyard without ever having to tap mana! From there he can beatdown with [Ichorid](https://web.archive.org/web/20110722000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichorid) or use [Bridge from Below](https://web.archive.org/web/20110722000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bridge+from+Below) to swarm his opponents to submission.
