Bill Tsang was in an enviable spot. By reaching the Top 8 he qualified for Pro Tour *Magic Origins* in Vancouver, Canada. As one lone remaining Canadian of the event his travel partners gave him some good-natured hassle as the match got underway, sharing that Tsang had slept throughout the long drive down. “He was well-rested for this tournament,” one said.

But there was plenty of wear from the challenges he had faced thus far.

The number twenty-one-ranked Andrew Cuneo was looking to win the first Grand Prix of his long career, having found just a finalist slot in Lincoln in 2012. After dispatching the storied and talented Yuuya Watanabe in the quarterfinals in a decisive match, Cuneo looked well-positioned to advance further.

**The Decks**

Tsang’s deck was made to take advantage of the worst opponents could offer. With multiple copies of [Act of Treason](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Act+of+Treason) and [Collateral Damage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collateral+Damage) to go along with, his aggressive deck topped out with [Icy Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Icy+Blast) and other potent removal to cleave a path to victory.

Cuneo’s deck was using similar colors and aggression but leaned on a longer game. With [Warden of the Eye](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warden+of+the+Eye) to bring back powerhouse cards like [Jeskai Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Charm) and a surprising Howl of the Horde, Cuneo used evasive creatures and burn into a deadly one-two combo to win games.

**The Games**

Cuneo led off with a morph on turn three, which Tsang wasted no time clearing with Bathe in Dragonfire. [Summit Prowler](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summit+Prowler) and [Hordeling Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hordeling+Outburst) gave Tsang a larger presence against Cuneo’s long morph that followed, but with five mana up on Cuneo’s side Tsang used [Singing Bell Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Singing+Bell+Strike) to clear the way.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpcle15/gpcleveland-2015-SF-tsang.jpg)  
*Bill Tsang raced against Andrew Cuneo, and used Falter-effects to get the job done.*

Mastery of the Unseed gave Cuneo a way to make a blocker anyway, and one Goblin token was eaten. On his next turn, Tsang unleashed the full fury of this deck with [Act of Treason](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Act+of+Treason) into [Icy Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Icy+Blast) that left Cuneo without any ways to block. Tsang overran the first game from there.

The second game was a similar race, with [Valley Dasher](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valley+Dasher) punching in against Cuneo’s Wandering Champion. Creatures traded back and forth after that, but Cuneo’s Watcher of Roost looked to tip the race in his favor.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpcle15/gpcleveland-2015-SF-cuneo.jpg)  
*Andrew Cuneo could prepare for Icy Blast, but multiple [Act of Treason](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Act+of+Treason) was an act from Bill Tsang that couldn’t be followed.*

At least it looked favorable until back-to-back copies of [Act of Treason](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Act+of+Treason) with a [Collateral Damage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collateral+Damage) in between handily took the game away for Tsang.

“So many Act of Treason,” Cuneo said in surprise. “Good luck in the finals.”

Tsang shook his hand. “Thanks.”

**Bill Tsang defeated Andrew Cuneo, 2-0.**
