*Sideboard Magazine* #48 is now available!

Contains:

*   A full-color *Eighth Edition* card encyclopedia
*   Coverage of National Championship tournaments from the US, Canada, Japan, Germany, and more
*   Coverage of the JSS Championships and several Grand Prix events
*   A look at *Onslaught* Block Constructed by Brian Kibler
*   Several "Top 10" lists to commemorate the 10th Anniversary of **Magic**
*   a "photomosaic" cover illustration of Black Lotus made up of hundreds of tiny pieces of card art
*   **Magic** puzzle by Mark Gottlieb
*   …and much more!

Look for it premier stores, premier events, or subscribe by clicking the ad below.
