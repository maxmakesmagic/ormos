It's no secret that R&D member Ken Nagle loves Commander. He has a deck for almost everything—even planeswalkers! If you've ever wanted to cast Creeping Renaissance naming planeswalker and return seven of them to your hand, this planeswalker-themed deck (using Progenitus as the Commander) is the deck for you! Doubling Season doubles the counters planeswalkers get on the way in, allowing most of them to use their "ultimate" immediately, and Rings of Brighthearth works overtime letting you get two activations of out of each planeswalker each turn. Ken's deck contains a whopping twenty-three planeswalkers. Can you figure out which ones are missing?
