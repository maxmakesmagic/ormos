**Vanguard**  
 The following Vanguard avatars will change. The new starting hand size and life totals for each are listed below:

Arcbound Overseer – 7 cards, 23 life (from 6, 23)  
 Elvish Champion – 7 cards, 15 life (from 8, 21)  
 Goblin Warchief – 7 cards, 22 life (from 7, 17)  
 Ink-Eyes, Servant of Oni – 6 cards, 17 life (from 6, 13)  
 Seshiro the Anointed – 7 cards, 19 life (from 7, 25)

For an explanation of the changes from **Magic** R&D, tune in to Aaron Forsythe's "Latest Developments" column on **magicthegathering.com** on Friday, June 3, 2005.
