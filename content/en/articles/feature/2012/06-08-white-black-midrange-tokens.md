Jessie Smith, or [@Smi77y](http://twitter.com/smi77y) as he is on Twitter, shares his latest brew ready to battle in Standard. Here are a few of his thoughts on it:

> The WB Tokens strategy has always been one that hasn't been capable of taking down tournaments; however, the metagame has changed a bit as we now have a "King of the Hill" deck to combat against. Instant-speed interaction is something the WB Midrange strategy has lacked in the past, but I'm leaning toward fixing this issue with the inclusion of the new almighty [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel), [Midnight Haunting](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Midnight+Haunting), and some additional instant-speed removal.
> 
> The idea is that [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak)s are generally too powerful against us, particularly when we lean toward a midrange version of white-black. The key plays here are that you can cast an end-of-turn [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel) and/or [Midnight Haunting](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Midnight+Haunting) to either bait the [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) or create board presence, both of which are largely beneficial to us. Once we have board presence, we generally have a fast clock to beat down our opponents.

Read [his complete article](http://www.starcitygames.com/magic/standard/24227_Battling_The_King_Of_The_Hill.html) about the deck on StarCityGames.com!
