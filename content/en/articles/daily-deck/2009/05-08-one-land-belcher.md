Notice anything out of the ordinary about this deck? Astute viewers will pick up on the fact the deck has but a single land: a lonely Taiga. That's because it's based on a similar deck from the world of Vintage that centers around landless mana like Simian and Elvish Spirit Guides, Tinder Wall, Land Grant, and others to drop a fast Empty the Warrens or Goblin Charbelcher. With just a single land in the deck, and that land being a Mountain, the 'Belcher is practically guaranteed to be lethal every time it activates. Rukcus tuned this version to take a top spot in a recent Premier Event on **Magic Online**.
