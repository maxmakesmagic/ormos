Maybe it should have been called Pro Tour Thassa. Rising French star Jeremy Dezani and his Mono-Blue Devotion deck filled with Thassa, God of the Sea, Master of Waves, and an army of blue creatures defeated Team Revolution teammate Pierre Dagen playing a nearly identical deck to earn the title of Pro Tour *Theros* champion. 

Devotion was a major theme throughout the weekend in Dublin. Both the Standard and Booster Draft formats were filled with Devotion cards thanks to the introduction of *Theros*. Dagen and Dezany showed their devotion to their team and country with masterful performances, re-asserting France as a major power in **Magic**. The weekend began with Magic's ultimate tribute to devotion—the induction of legendary players Luis Scott-Vargas, William Jensen, and Ben Stark into the **Magic** Pro Tour Hall of Fame. 

Congratulations to Jeremy Dezani, the Pro Tour *Theros* champion! If you missed any of Sunday's action, [watch the replay here](/en/events/2004/webcast/magic-the-gathering-webcast-schedule).
