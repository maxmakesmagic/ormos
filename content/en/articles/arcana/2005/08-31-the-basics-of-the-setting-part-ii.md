Following the lead of [Monday's Arcana](/en/articles/arcana/2005/08-29-the-basics-of-the-setting), today we explore more of *Ravnica* through its amazing basic land art. We should point out that selecting only one sample of the four pieces for each basic land was incredibly difficult -- they're all beautiful.

Without further adieu, Mountain and Swamp, you're up.
