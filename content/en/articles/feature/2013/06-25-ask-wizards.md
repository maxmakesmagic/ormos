Ask Wizards is a weekly feature that allows you to ask us questions! If you'd like to submit your question please email it to AskWizards@wizards.com. We aren't able to answer every question we receive but if your question is good then it might show up in the coming weeks!

 **Q: When is the next Prerelease? - Alex.**

**A: Trick Jarrett, Editor-in-Chief of DailyMTG.com**

The ***Magic** 2014—Core Set* prerelease is fast approaching on July 13, 2013. Be sure to check with your [local store](https://web.archive.org/web/20130625000000/http://locator.wizards.com/) to find out when they’re running theirs!

After that you’ll be looking for the *Theros* Prerelease on September 21, 2013! That’s going to be awesome!
