### Angel Solache

**Age:** 24  
**Hometown:** Houston, TX  
**Occupation:** Salesman

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
 Won FNM x3

**What deck are you playing, and why did you choose it?**  
 Abzan because it's good

**What are your best and worst matchups?**  
 Mardu is the worst; Red-Green Monsters is a good matchup

**What card, in the maindeck or sideboard, won you the most matches this weekend?**  
[Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos)

 Play another Brimaz

**What's the best American holiday? \[Hint: It's probably Thanksgiving, right?\]**  
 Christmas

### Randall Gay

**Age:** 25  
**Hometown:** Houston, TX  
**Occupation:** Digital Marketing Professional

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
 Day 2 Pro Tour *Khans of Tarkir*

**What deck are you playing, and why did you choose it?**  
 Abzan Midrange - I am comfortable with this style of deck, it has very few bad matchups, and [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino).

**What are your best and worst matchups?**  
 Best – White-Blue Control. Worst - Reanimator.

**What card, in the maindeck or sideboard, won you the most matches this weekend?**  
[Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino)

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**  
 I would like a maindeck answer to [Hornet Queen](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hornet+Queen)—maybe [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight), and I'd like to try [Whip of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos).

**What's the best American holiday? \[Hint: It's probably Thanksgiving, right?\]**  
 Festivus - for the rest of us.

### Jeremy Frye

**Age:** 22  
**Hometown:** College Station, TX  
**Occupation:** Texas A+M student

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
 Top 8ed PTQs

**What deck are you playing, and why did you choose it?**  
 Temur Monsters. I love the color combination and thought it was a powerful deck.

**What are your best and worst matchups?**  
 Best - Any control deck. Worst - Green Devotion decks.

**What card, in the maindeck or sideboard, won you the most matches this weekend?**  
[Stubborn Denial](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stubborn+Denial)

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**  
 I would take out the Temur Ascendency for a [Temur Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temur+Charm). The Ascendency only helps already-good matchups.

**What's the best American holiday? \[Hint: It's probably Thanksgiving, right?\]**  
 Christmas

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsna14/GP%20SA%20Top%208%20Profile%20-%20Ryan%20Scullin.jpg)

### Ryan Scullin

**Age:** 24  
**Hometown:** Narragansett, RI  
**Occupation:** (F)unemployed

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
 Top 33 Grand Prix Chicago

**What deck are you playing, and why did you choose it?**  
 Mardu Midrange. I had the most experience with it, and [Tymaret, the Murder King](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tymaret%2C+the+Murder+King) and [Bloodsoaked Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodsoaked+Champion) weren't good enough.

**What are your best and worst matchups?**  
 Best - UW Heroic. Worst - [Whip of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos) decks.

**What card, in the maindeck or sideboard, won you the most matches this weekend?**  
 Tie between [Crackling Doom](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crackling+Doom) and [Hushwing Gryff](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hushwing+Gryff).

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**  
 Find room in the sideboard for [Liliana Vess](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+Vess). You lose midrange mirrors to having fewer/worse cards, and Chandra rarely does anything the turn she comes down.

**What's the best American holiday? \[Hint: It's probably Thanksgiving, right?\]**  
 Right!

### Gareth Aye

**Age:** 24  
**Hometown:** Knoxville, TN  
**Occupation:** Dad, Software Engineer at Mozilla Firefox

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
 2 PTQ Top 8s, Top 64 at JSS Championship 2005, Top 16 at Tri-State Regionals 2004

**What deck are you playing, and why did you choose it?**  
 Blue-Red Aggro. I thought Red would be well-positioned for Game Ones, and that doing something different with the transformational sideboard would cause some opponents to mis-sideboard.

**What are your best and worst matchups?**  
 Not totally sure. I get uncomfortable when I see [Whip of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos).

**What card, in the maindeck or sideboard, won you the most matches this weekend?**  
[Goblin Rabblemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20141201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Rabblemaster)

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**  
 Not sure.

**What's the best American holiday? \[Hint: It's probably Thanksgiving, right?\]**  
 Thanksgiving! I cooked it this year for my family!
