Gen Con is fast approaching and **Magic: The Gathering** will have a giant presence this year, and we aren’t just talking about the oversized **Magic** game that will be featured each day! 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9b/66/9b66b7fe9dd616273a2b47d480dadb409fb41d3c3e445daa2c5d3447687eb1ad.jpg)

With a plethora of cool foil promo cards, a video contest, limited advanced sales of *From the Vault: Exiled* (details below), a *Duels of the Planeswalkers* live "Game with Fame" event, **Magic** tournaments, and other activities, this Gen Con will be one to remember! The action starts Thursday, August 13 in Indianapolis.

### Magic Tournament Highlights

*   [**Magic Online** Live](/en/articles/uncategorised/2009/04-02-2009-magic-online-live-series) event leading to **Worlds** in Rome
*   [Vintage Championship](/en/articles/event-coverage/2011/06-02-2012-vintage-championship-event-information): Friday, 11 a.m.
*   [Legacy Championship](/en/articles/event-coverage/2011/06-02-2012-legacy-championship-event-information): Saturday, 10 a.m.
*   *Shards of Alara* Block Championship: Thursday, 5 p.m.
*   *Duels of the Planeswalkers* Game with Fame: Thursday-Saturday, 1-3 p.m.
*   Oversized **Magic**, featuring Wizards R&D members Ken Nagle and Mark Gottlieb playing with members of the audience, plus head judge Sheldon Menery and master of ceremonies Brian David-Marshall. Thursday-Saturday, 11 a.m. - 1 p.m. and 2 - 4 p.m.; Sunday 11 a.m. - 1 p.m.
*   Pro Tour–Austin Qualifier
*   Mega **FNM**: Friday, 7 p.m.
*   Grand Melee: Saturday, 5 p.m.

### *From the Vault: Exiled* Convention Promotion

Wizards of the Coast will be selling a limited quantity of **Magic: The Gathering** *From the Vault: Exiled* card boxes at Gen Con Indy 2009.

*   Only 100 boxes will be sold each day.
*   Attendees wishing to purchase *From the Vault: Exiled* are required obtain a marked card. The marked card allows you to purchase *From the Vault: Exiled*.
*   Cards can be obtained at the Serra Angel Sculpture located by the Westin Skybridge on the 2nd floor of the Indianapolis Convention Center. 100 Cards will be available beginning at 9 a.m. **Please, do not begin forming a line earlier than this**.
*   Cards will be redeemed for *From the Vault: Exiled* at the Wizards of the Coast booth (1721) beginning at 3 p.m. and ending at the close of the exhibit hall each day. You **must** turn in your marked card in order to purchase your copy of *From the Vault: Exiled*. No Card, No Purchase, No Exceptions.
*   You **must** turn in your marked card the day you receive it. Cards will not be allowed to roll over to the following day.
*   The limit for *From the Vault: Exiled* is **one per person** for the duration of the show. Your badge will be marked to show you have purchased your copy. Please have your badge out to help in this process.
*   *From the Vault: Exiled* will retail for MSRP ($34.99) plus applicable IN state sales tax.

If you’ve ever thought of going to Gen Con, this is the year to do it. No matter how you like to experience **Magic**, there will be something for you. 

For complete information about Gen Con, visit [www.gencon.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20090724000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.gencon.com/2009/indy/default.aspx&origin=).
