The **Magic** World Championships begin today! The first format on the docket? Standard! **Magic Online** tournaments have been dominated by Delver of Secrets based decks over the past few weeks, either in Mono-Blue Illusion or Blue-Red Counterburn varieties. Could today be its breakout performance? Check out Zilli Jr's sample decklist taken from the Top 8 of a recent **Magic Online** Premier Event, and stay tuned to Worlds coverage all weekend long to find out what decks end up on top!

Zilli Jr's Delver Illusions
