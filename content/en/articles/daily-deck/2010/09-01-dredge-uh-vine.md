David Ochoa managed to earn a slot on the U.S. National team as an alternate with his innovative Dredge-Uh-Vine deck. The game plan? Use Fauna Shaman to fill the graveyard with Vengevines, then use Renegade Doppelganger to copy them as they re-enter the battlefield from the graveyard following the casting of two cheap creatures in the same turn. As a backup plan? Use Extractor Demon to mill your opponent out, or to fill up your graveyard with beefy Vengevine goodness. Powerful! Read full coverage of U.S. Nationals [here](/en/events/2010/usnat10/utter-leyton-crowned-u-s-champ).
