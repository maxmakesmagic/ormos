*What's your favorite **Magic** Vampire, and why?*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4d/df/4ddf17870927d1fa29f7c99e3b74f59ad7b39456776c837fd321939967d75c7b.jpg)

**Mike Turian:** [Skeletal Vampire](https://web.archive.org/web/20091020000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skeletal+Vampire) is my favorite **Magic** Vampire. I was a developer on *Guildpact*. It was the first **Magic** expansion I worked on, and I was excited to see a Vampire in the set. Not only a Vampire, but a Vampire Skeleton! And it has amazing art by Wayne Reynolds.

![Skeletal Vampire](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Skeletal+Vampire)

  

I really enjoy that Skeletal Vampire is an expensive creatures that is tough to kill. On top of that I love the idea of a Vampire that brings along his own bats.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/mtg/images/daily/activity/act302_skeletalVampire.jpg)  

The *Guildpact* development team was searching the perfect flavor of the Bat eating Vampire when we came upon the idea that the [Skeletal Vampire](https://web.archive.org/web/20091020000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skeletal+Vampire) could not only eat Bats to save himself, but would also eat Bats to make ... more Bats!
