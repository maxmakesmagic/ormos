The **FlavOracle** is a document that houses the official flavor text for every **Magic** card (basic sets, expansion sets, and promotional cards).

Full FlavOracle: [TXT](https://web.archive.org/web/20020321000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/images/mtgcom/docs/flavoracle.txt) - [DOC](https://web.archive.org/web/20020321000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/images/mtgcom/docs/flavoracle.doc)

In addition, MagicTheGathering.com is running an ongoing feature that allows readers to submit flavor text for older cards. Each person may submit one entry per card. After a screening process, a popular vote will determine the eventual winner for each card, and the winning text will be added into the FlavOracle.
