Atlanta played host to pro-level **Magic** for the second weekend in a row, and nearly 1,300 players showed up for the *Theros*-block Limited [Grand Prix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140530000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/stern-stands-tall-atlanta) there. Jon Stern battled his way through a Top 8 that included longtime Pro Tomoharu Saito and three players from the [Top 25 Rankings](/en/articles/makingmagic/2014/05-29-top-25-rankings-may-29-2014) to claim another Grand Prix trophy. Congratulations, Jon! The coming weekend brings the last Pro Tour Qualifiers (PTQs) and the last Grand Prix (in Manchester, England, and São Paulo, Brazil) for the season that feeds into Pro Tour ***Magic** 2015* . Find one of the last PTQs this season with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#m15). Good luck; have fun!
