 **Q: Does Nykthos produce six mana from Nightveil Specter or three? —Jack D.**

 **A: Mike McArtor, copy editor for DailyMTG:** 

Just three. [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter) provides three toward your devotion to blue, so with just it and [Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nykthos%2C+Shrine+to+Nyx) you could produce ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif). It provides three toward your devotion to black, so with just it and Nykthos you could produce ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/black.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/black.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/black.gif).

BUT! [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter)*also* provides three toward your devotion to blue and black, which will be important if you're playing with it and, say, the card that Gavin Verhey previews in [today's ReConstructed](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/rc/283).

[Kelly Digges](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/archive.aspx?author=kelly%20digges) helpfully explains hybrid mana symbols and devotion like this: "You're not summing your individual devotions to the two colors—you're counting all the mana symbols that are either or both of the two colors. [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter) has three mana symbols on it, so the most it can add to your devotion to anything is three—but it adds that three if you're counting blue, black, blue and black, or any other combination that includes blue or black." (Thanks Kelly!)

  

 **Q: Some friends and I play a lot of casual Commander games and are wondering about the ruling of hybrid cards in a deck that doesn't have one of the colors in it. For example, the card Bioshift costs one blue or one green; can that card be included in, say, a mono-green Commander deck? We would also like a ruling on cards like Profit amp; Loss. —Matt G.**

 **A: Mike McArtor, copy editor for DailyMTG:** 

In order for a card to be included in a Commander deck all of the colors of its entire color identity must fit within the color identity of your commander. Color identity [is defined](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/resources.aspx?x=magic/rules/100cardsingleton-commander) as "…the set of colors the card is and the colors of any mana symbols appearing on the card." Hybrid mana symbols count as both colors, so, to use your example, [Bioshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bioshift) is both green *and* blue, meaning it can't be included in a mono-green deck.

The same is true of a split card, where both halves have to be within your commander's color identity in order to include it in your deck, even if you only want to use one side or the other. Going back to your examples, [Profit amp; Loss](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Profit+%1Aamp%3B+Loss) is both white *and* black, meaning you can't include it in a Commander deck unless your commander includes both white and black in its color identity. It gets even more restrictive when you look at split cards like [Ready amp; Willing](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ready+%1Aamp%3B+Willing), where your commander must have black *and* green *and* white in its color identity to include the card.

Color identity extends to the text box (but does not include reminder text, like that for the [extort](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;text=+%5Bextort%5Damp;format=+%5B%22Return+to+Ravnica+Block%22%5D) mechanic), so, for example, [Scholar of Athreos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scholar+of+Athreos)'s color identity is white *and* black. Oh, and double-faced cards? Yup, both sides count for color identity, whether because of a color indicator (as on [Cloistered Youth](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloistered+Youth)/[Unholy Fiend](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unholy+Fiend)) or because of mana symbols (as on [Daybreak Ranger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daybreak+Ranger)/[Nightfall Predator](https://web.archive.org/web/20140121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightfall+Predator)).
