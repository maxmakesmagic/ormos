## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d4/db/d4db08f990eb2430a37d6bda529259f6c5d1d91c7c88af4110ed24bcd2052b17.png)

This 119-card set, the fourth Magic expansion, features many cards that pair tempting Magic abilities with damaging penalties.

**Set Name:** The Dark  
**Number of Cards:** 119

**Languages:** English, Italian

**Design Team:** Jesper Myrfors, with contributions from Steve Bishop, Glenn Elliott, Beverly Marshall Saling

**Development Team:** Skaff Elias, Jim Lin, Chris Page, Dave Pettey
