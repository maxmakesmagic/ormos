### Invitation List

SINGAPORE Nationals Invitation list comprises the following:

*   The current SINGAPORE National Champion and members of the current SINGAPORE national team (including alternate).
*   Players from the SINGAPORE with Pro Tour Players Club level 2 or higher.
*   Winners of invitations through Regional Championships.
*   Winners of invitations through SINGAPORE National Qualifiers.
*   The Top 75 DCI Composite–ranked players from the SINGAPORE on June 10, 2009.
*   The Top 75 DCI Total–ranked players from the SINGAPORE on June 10, 2009.

All players on the list below are already qualified for SINGAPORE Nationals and are not permitted to play in SINGAPORE Regional Championships and/or SINGAPORE Nationals Qualifier.
