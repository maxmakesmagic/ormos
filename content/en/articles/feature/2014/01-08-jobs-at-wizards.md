Welcome to 2014! Today's Arcana is a reminder that we are always looking to hire people. I do not know of a single day where we did not have open positions listed on our website.

As of this writing there were fifteen listings on the Wizards careers page, ranging from a [content manager](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/content-manager-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1) (working for me!) to a [Japanese support member](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/japanese-support-csr-work-home-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1) to art director roles for both **[Magic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/art-director-magic-rd-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)** and **[D&D](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/sr-marketing-art-director-dungeons-dragons-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)**.

To see the full list of positions we're hiring for, visit [our Careers page](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers) on the company website.
