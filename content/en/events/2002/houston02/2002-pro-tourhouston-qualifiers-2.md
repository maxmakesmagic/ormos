### LAST CHANCE QUALIFIER

Complete information on the Last Chance Qualifier for Houston is available [here](/en/events/2002/houston02/2002-pro-tourhouston#sides)

### ENTRY FEE

Entry fee varies from location to location. Check with local organizer for specific information.

### Tournament Structure

The structure for Pro Tour–Houston Qualifier tournaments will be modified Swiss-style, with the Top 8 players playing in a single-elimination format for prizes and invitation(s).

### Tournament Format

The format for Pro Tour–Houston Qualifier tournaments is Odyssey Block Constructed.

### Invitations, Eligibility, and Prize Money

Players already holding an invitation to Pro Tour–Houston are prohibited from participating in any Pro Tour–Houston Qualifiers, with the exception of Grand Prix tournaments. Players may have earned invitations through cumulative past performance in the Pro Tour, winning a previous Qualifier or Grand Prix tournament, or by being an invited DCI player (valid upon the date of the official ratings invitation announcement for the appropriate professional tournament).

### Prizes

*   Invitations to Pro Tour–Houston (exact number of invitations determined by tournament location)
*   Product prizes to be determined by the organizer
*   Some Qualifiers offer cash prizes. See individual schedules for details.
