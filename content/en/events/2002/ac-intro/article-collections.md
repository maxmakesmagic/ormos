### Article Collections

[**Magic Invitational Reports**](https://web.archive.org/web/20090604033319/http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/article.asp?x=ac_invitational) - All the reports from **Magic's** All Star Game, known as the **Magic** Invitational. These are the tournament reports of the star players who have attended the events.

[**Week in Review**](/en/events/2004/archives/archives) - Summary of the week's interesting events plus looks at upcoming events, trivia, play of the week and other miscellaneous goodies. Author Alex Shvartsman is a pro tour regular and the most accomplished Grand Prix player in the world.

[**Wise Words**](/en/events/2004/archives/archives) - Weekly article series discussing a variety of **Magic** topics, including pro player gossip, strategy advice and recent events in the **Magic** playing community. Author Gary Wise was member of the Pro Tour New York 2000 champion team and is an outspoken pro tour regular.
