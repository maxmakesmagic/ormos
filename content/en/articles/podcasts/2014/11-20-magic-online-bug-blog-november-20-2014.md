What Is the *Magic Online* Bug Blog?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/mtgo/icon_bugblog.jpg)

While the [Known Issues List](https://web.archive.org/web/20141120000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-known-issues) is simply that—a running list of issues we expect players to encounter most often—this accompanying “Bug Blog” is the list’s companion. Here you’ll find updates regarding emergent new issues, details around issue statuses when applicable, known issue workarounds, and a running tally of Known Issues List updates. The Bug Blog will be updated each Thursday, in conjunction with the [Known Issues List](https://web.archive.org/web/20141120000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-known-issues) on the same day.

Known Issues List Updates

This week, several Server and Game Play known issues have been scheduled for the *Magic Online* update on Wednesday, December 3. In addition to this, there was a fixed issue that was accidentally not added when it was fixed during the November 5 deployment. Updates can be found in the tables below.

**Newly Scheduled for the Wednesday, December 3 *Magic Online* Client Update**

**Game Play**

*   C14 | Artifact creature tokens created by C14 cards show no art when zoomed or in the preview pane
*   C14 | [Nahiri, the Lithomancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nahiri%2C+the+Lithomancer)'s first ability attaches the equipment to previous tokens if they exist
*   C14 | Offerings display blue text identifying a chosen opponent before it is chosen. The cards function correctly.
*   C14 | [Song of the Dryad](https://web.archive.org/web/20141120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Song+of+the+Dryad) enchanting another Aura appears incorrectly. The card functions correctly.
*   General | A copy of a spell that divides damage among multiple targets doesn't display the distribution among the copy's targets
*   General | [Cruel Ultimatum](https://web.archive.org/web/20141120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cruel+Ultimatum)'s resolution can crash the game, causing it to restart
*   General | Lands in M15 frames that produce mana of two differnet colors have the wrong background colors in the rules text box
*   General | [Trinisphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20141120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trinisphere)'s effect does not apply to spells with X in the mana cost

**Server**

*   Limited Deckbuilding | Submit button is greyed out even though deck is legal
*   Play Lobby | Legacy Cube is just named "Cube"

**Newly Added Fixed Known Issues as of Thursday, November 20**

**Game Play**

*   M15 | Convoke reveals you are casting a convoke card, even if you cancel before tapping any creatures —fixed 11/5
