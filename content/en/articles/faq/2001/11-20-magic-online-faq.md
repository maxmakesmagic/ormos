### ANSWERS

### GENERAL QUESTIONS

**What is the release date?**  
 A: ***Magic** Online* officially released on June 24, 2002.

**What languages will the game be in?**  
 A: We will launch in English in 2002 and will follow with additional languages. French, German, and Italian are projected within a year of release.

**Which expansions will be available at launch?**  
 A: All expansions from the *InvasionTM* set forward, including the *Seventh EditionTM* basic set. At the release, the *Seventh Edition, Invasion, PlaneshiftTM, ApocalypseTM, OdysseyTM,* and *TormentTM* sets will be available to players. (The *JudgmentTM* set will be available soon after.)

**Will card collation in booster packs be identical to the paper-based product?**  
 A: Collation will be as close as possible. The mathematical equations will be the same, but real life production errors will not be duplicated.

**Will there be premiums?**  
 A: Yes.

**Will there be unique online cards?**  
 A: No.

**Will the cards have a border color?**  
 A: Yes, true to paper-based cards.

**What play formats will ***Magic** Online* support?**  
 A: At launch: Constructed, Limited (Sealed Deck, Booster Draft, and Rochester Draft), and various Multiplayer formats.

**What tournament and league formats will be offered at launch?**  
 A: Both Constructed and Limited tournaments will be available at launch. Leagues will be Sealed Deck Format using different combinations of product.

**Can I create my own group or team online?**  
 A: Yes. Go to the **Clans** room to form your own group.

**Is there a buddy list to see when my friends are online?**  
 A: Yes.

**Do I have to be logged on to ***Magic** Online* to see upcoming tournaments?**  
 A: No, these will also be posted on the [***Magic** Online* web site](https://web.archive.org/web/20041207152325/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline).

**Can I start tournaments and leagues?**  
 A: No. Only tournament organizers and administrators can begin tournaments and leagues.

**How will trading work? Is it secure?**  
 A: Right click on a player's name and select **trade**. If the player accepts, the **Trade** screen opens where you can look through each other's cards marked as tradable. Each of you clicks on cards you want, and can discuss possible trades in the chat window. If both parties confirm the trade, the transaction and cards go into a verification process for one day, then the cards are transferred. Trading takes place on the ***Magic** Online* server and is secure.

**Will there be a chat function? Voice chat? What equipment do I need?**  
 A: Chat, private chat, and blocks are built into the game. We are working on voice chat, but it won't be available at launch. The only special equipment you would need is a microphone for voice chat.

**Will the program animate the combat? (Will I see a representation of what the cards are doing?)**  
 A: ***Magic** Online* doesn't animate combat or spells, but does show the cards in each zone and which cards are targeting what.

**How many users can the program support? Total accounts, simultaneous?**  
 A: Almost limitless. It all depends on the number of servers we have, and that is dependant on demand.

**How do I know who I'm playing against?**  
 A: Player names and profiles are available to you. Just like with any other online experience, we cannot guarantee the accuracy of the info players provide. The exception will be certain accounts we assign for specific players.

**Are there age requirements?**  
 A: Yes. You must be 18 years of age or older. If you're between the ages of 13 and 18 years, you must have parental consent to play ***Magic** Online*. See the Terms of Service for legal restrictions and regulations.

**Can I have an account if I am under 13?**  
 A: Due to certain restrictions, we can't allow players under the age of 13 to play ***Magic** Online*.

**Can I be a ***Magic** Online* judge?**  
 A: Since the game handles all the aspects of judging, judges aren't needed. 

**Will the constraints of online card interactions affect offline card development?**  
 A: Not in any noticeable way. 

### GAME PLAY QUESTIONS

**Can I play against the computer?**  
 A: No. Just like in paper-based **Magic**, you need another real player to have any sense of victory or defeat.

**Will it enforce rules? Which rules?**  
 A: Yes. ***Magic** Online* enforces the comprehensive **Magic** rules.

**How do you prevent online play/tournament cheating?**  
 A: ***Magic** Online* is the equivalent of having a Level 5 **DCI** judge at your table. It will not allow illegal moves. Many other methods are in place to insure tournaments and casual play are as cheat-free as possible (anti-flood, tournament player randomizations, etc.).

**Will it keep stats (player, card, clan, etc.)?**  
 A: Yes. You may even view some of your past games played.

**If I have stats, do I level up? Gain better abilities?**  
 A: No, but your avatar might change.

**Will there be online ratings and rankings?**  
 A: Yes. The online ratings/rankings are separate from the standard **DCI**, but are formatted and calculated the same.

**What if someone drops from a tourney?**  
 A: Just like in paper-based tournaments, that person is removed from the tournament lists. If there's an uneven number of players, someone will get a bye.

**What if someone gets disconnected during a tourney?**  
 A: During tournament games, each player has only a certain amount of time to play. While the game can't continue until the disconnected player comes back, that player's "shot clock" continues to run down (a player's shot clock only runs when he or she has priority). If the player rejoins, the game continues. If not, the other player is awarded a match win when the disconnected player's clock runs out. See the ***Magic** Online* help files for more information.

**How does shuffling work online? Can I "mana weave"? Is the shuffling random? Won't I get mana-screwed if it's completely random?**  
 A: The shuffling is random, so you can't stack your deck. As long as you build a good deck, you shouldn't get mana-screwed often.

**If I don't own a card, how will I see what cards my opponent is playing? Are all the cards downloaded on my machine? How much disk space will that require?**  
 A: All the cards are downloaded to your machine. At launch, the card images only take a few hundred MB of space.

**Can I forfeit a match? Can I concede? Will that affect my ratings? Can I mulligan?**  
 A: Yes, yes, and yes. Tournaments on ***Magic** Online* function just like regurlar **Magic** tournaments.

**How will cards that let me go through my opponents deck/library/hand/graveyard work?**  
 A: The computer will show you what cards you get to go through and then prompt you with legal actions you can take. 

**Can I see a replay of my match? Can I watch other people play?**  
 A: Yes and yes.

**Can a friend and I save a match during play and return to finish it some other time?**  
 A: Yes.

**Can I save my own matches to replay them later?**  
 A: Yes.

### TECHNICAL QUESTIONS

**What are the technical requirements?**  
 A: 

*   Windows 95, 98, 2000, NT (Service Pack 4), or XP
*   PII 333 or faster
*   64 Megs of RAM
*   Mouse
*   SVGA, High-Color (16bit)
*   4x CD ROM
*   600 MB of hard drive space
*   Internet connection (56k or faster)

**Will I have to buy upgrades when they come out?**  
 A: No. Your software is updated automatically each time you log on. 

**Can I create a macro to play for me (i.e., a "bot")?**  
 A: No.

**Is this programmed in TCP/IP or UDP and why?**  
 A: TCP/IP. After evaluating the pros and cons, this was chosen as the best method.

**I heard that TCP/IP games create huge amounts of lag. Will this game slow to a halt with many players?**  
 A: With only a small amount of animation, ***Magic** Online* doesn't have the same issues as most other TCP/IP games.

**Does this game run on a Mac? Linux? Windows 2000? Will it ever?**  
 A: Wizards of the Coast currently supports ***Magic** Online* only on Windows platforms. If we develop the software for other systems, we'll make sure to announce it to the community.

**How do you handle denial of service attacks? What if my opponent gets my IP address and then spams me to keep me from completing a match? What can I do?**  
 A: We have built-in systems to prevent this, from the simple added feature of "Block User" to internal safeguards.

**What's a "digital object"?**  
 A: Each digital card is unique with its own individual tracking number. Just like in real life, no two digital cards are the same. You own yours and we track its every move.

**Where are digital objects stored?**  
 A: In our database and master server.

**How do I know no one will steal digital objects?**  
 A: Our secure master server has two separate layers of firewalls with every security measure taken to protect the digital objects and accounts. We also track and log every move of every digital object.

**What if your system crashes? Is my collection safe? What happens to the tourney in progress?**  
 A: The back-up and redundant systems take over in event of system failure. Even if the back-up systems were to fail, we have every detail recorded. As soon as the system comes back online everything goes right back to where it was.

**Is ***Magic** Online* going to be available on other platforms (i.e., console or handheld)?**  
 A: We currently have no plans to port ***Magic** Online* to any other platforms. If we do decide to, we'll make sure to notify the community.

**Can I access my account from any computer? Are there any drawbacks (decklists, etc.)?**  
 A: You can access your account from any computer that has ***Magic** Online* installed. Decklists aren't stored on the server, but they aren't very large and many can be stored on a single floppy disk.

**My friend said the he can hack the TCP/IP stream. Is this true?**  
 A: We have many security systems in place to prevent hacking. And, as new hacking techniques appear, we will address them.

**What happens if you catch players hacking? Will you roll back the servers? Will I ever possibly lose my cards due to a server malfunction? What will happen when your server is eventually hacked or crashed?**  
 A: If we ever have to roll back the servers, we will have detailed and redundant backup systems to guarantee you do not lose your cards or accounts.

**Can I run my own ***Magic** Online* server?**  
 A: No.

**Can I play this privately over a LAN?**  
 A: No. You have to connect to the ***Magic** Online* server to play.

**My Netscape browser is having problems connecting to the *Magic Online* Store. What's wrong?**  
 A: You probably have Netscape Navigator version 4.x or an older version. (Netscape 4.0 often comes with the Netscape Communicator download.) Update your browser to version 6.0 to fix the problem.

### PLAYER QUESTIONS

**Will players ever be able to redeem individual cards?**  
 A: No. The logistics involved in a single-card redemption program prevent it from ever being implemented.

**Will players ever be able to trade in physical cards for online cards?**  
 A: No. The only way to get online cards is to buy them or win them in the normal fashion. Physical **Magic** cards will never be ported to ***Magic** Online* through a trade-in or redemption system.

**Since *Magic Online* can randomize cards already (shuffler), why does the game maintain the pack distributions used in the physical game?**  
 A: The pack distributions for cards are maintained in ***Magic** Online* in order to mirror the physical game as closely as possible.

**Will pre-*Invasion* sets ever be available for online play?**  
 A: There are no plans to release older sets at this time.

**How long are sets available for purchase on the *Magic Online* Store?**  
 A: Since we try to mirror the physical **Magic** game as much as possible, there is no specific time period that an online set is available for purchase. However, sets are available for no less than a year after becoming available on the ***Magic** Online* Store.

**Are there any plans to integrate *Magic Online* with the DCI Player Rewards program?**  
 A: There are no plans to integrate ***Magic** Online* with the DCI Player Rewards program at this time.

**In the physical **Magic** game, having more than one DCI number falls under the Cheating rules. Why isn't this the same for players with more than one *Magic Online* account?**  
 A: The anonymous nature of the Internet makes enforcing a multiple account rule impractical. For this reason, we have decided to accept that some players will choose to have multiple accounts.

**Why is playing for ante not allowed on *Magic Online?***  
 A: Ante is no longer a part of the physical **Magic** game, so there's no reason for it to be a part of ***Magic** Online.*

**Why can't I change my user name?**  
 A: Your user name is our primary means of player identification. Allowing players to change their names would invite abuse of our systems and rules.
