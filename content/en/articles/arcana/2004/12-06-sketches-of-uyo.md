The art for [Uyo, Silent Prophet](https://web.archive.org/web/20041206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Uyo%2C+Silent+Prophet) originated as packaging art for *Champions of Kamigawa*. 

  
*Champions of Kamigawa packaging art sketch 1 by John Bolton* 

  
*Champions of Kamigawa packaging art sketch 2 by John Bolton* 

  
*[Uyo, Silent Prophet](https://web.archive.org/web/20041206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Uyo%2C+Silent+Prophet) final art by John Bolton* 

As the art pointed toward a Moonfolk with a powerful magic-manipulation ability, R&D made it into the legend you know today.
