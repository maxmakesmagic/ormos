The dust had settled after five rounds of *Zendikar* Block Constructed and three rounds of *Rise of the Eldrazi* Booster Draft; 141 players emerged with at least five wins. When we looked at the decklists of the continuing players, we found that the top five archetypes had held their positions.

**Blue-White Control - 38**

Ninety-five players were cut to 38, yet the power of [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20100529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) and [Gideon Jura](https://web.archive.org/web/20100529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon+Jura) still looms throughout over one quarter of the room. 

**Devastating Red – 21**

Twenty-one of the original 57 red decks were still winning. 

**Monument Green – 20**

This was the breakout deck of this weekend. The combination of token generators, [Eldrazi Monument](https://web.archive.org/web/20100529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Monument) and [Beastmaster Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20100529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Beastmaster+Ascension) had carried almost half of the players who chose to play it onward to Day Two.

**Green Eldrazi – 9**

We were still seeing [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20100529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Aeons+Torn) and his crew of Eldrazi monstrosities, fueled by Eldrazi Spawn, annihilating most things in their paths as nine of the original thirty-four players battled Day Two.

**Koros - 9**

Coming in as the third aggro-deck in this metagame, Koros rounded out our top five archetypes with nine of the 28 Day One players continuing on.

With five more rounds of *Zendikar* Block Constructed still to come, we would soon see which decks would come forth as the ones to beat.
