Well, the votes are in and no one name managed to run off with the victory. This means that the top three vote-getters will have a runoff to see what [Mr. Babycakes](https://web.archive.org/web/20020924000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=/magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/features/MrBabyCakes6.jpg&origin=mtgcom_feature_92) will officially be called. Those names are: (in alphabetical order)

*   Evolutionary Force
*   Forgotten Ancient
*   Verdant Nexus

The deadline for the run-off is midnight (EDT), Sunday, September 29th, and afterwards we'll vote for the winning flavor text.

We are in the home stretch of "[You Make the Card](https://web.archive.org/web/20041226203216/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/columnarchive&column=youmakethecard)." If you haven't participated yet, I highly encourage you to cast a vote.
