Remember [a couple of weeks ago](https://web.archive.org/web/20100809000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/dryads-favorite-guy-2010-07-28) when we showed you that ***Magic** 2011*'s [Dryad's Favor](https://web.archive.org/web/20100809000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dryad%27s+Favor) contained a reference to ***Magic** 2010*'s [Borderland Ranger](https://web.archive.org/web/20100809000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Borderland+Ranger)? Well, today we've got another refugee from the previous core set.

Let's take a look at [Chandra's Outrage](https://web.archive.org/web/20100809000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Outrage) (from a safe distance):

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/511_chandrasoutrage.jpg)

[Chandra's Outrage](https://web.archive.org/web/20100809000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Outrage) | Art by [Christopher Moeller](https://web.archive.org/web/20100809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Christopher+Moeller%22%5D)

Something seems to have incurred her anger. And that something is ...

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/511_snappingdrake.jpg)

[Snapping Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20100809000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapping+Drake) | Art by [Todd Lockwood](https://web.archive.org/web/20100809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Todd+Lockwood%22%5D)

Aha! So *that's* why [Snapping Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20100809000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapping+Drake) isn't in ***Magic** 2011*! Clearly Chandra burned it to the ground before it could take a card slot.
