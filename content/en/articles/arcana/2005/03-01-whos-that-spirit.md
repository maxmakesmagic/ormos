The *Betrayers of Kamigawa* card **[Twist Allegiance](https://web.archive.org/web/20050301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Twist+Allegiance)** is a spell that causes creatures to desert their masters and defect to the enemy side. The art by Wayne Reynolds shows a chaotic, pitched battle between mortals and spirits in which a huge, angry spirit looms above the battlefield:

[Twist Allegiance](https://web.archive.org/web/20050301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Twist%2BAllegiance)  
*Who's that roaring kami?*
