Did you attend a *Fate Reforged* Prerelease this weekend? Did you and your compatriots manage to save Ugin as part of the Prerelease activity? Did you get to crack one of these little bad boys as a reward? 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/EN_Ugin_Booster_01.png)

Each of these boosters contained two alternative-art cards, one *Fate Reforged* land, and a token. There were 26 cards plus 10 possible basic lands you could have received. If you were very lucky, you might have opened this beauty: 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/uginthespiritdragon.png)

Not every booster contained a rare card, but if yours did, it could have been Ugin or any one of these seven cards, four from *Khans of Tarkir* or three from *Fate Reforged* (plus Ugin, for a total of eight possible rares): 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/AltarOfTheBrood.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/jeeringinstigator.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/GhosrtfireBlade.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/GrimHaruspex.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/jeskaiinfiltrator.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/masteryoftheunseen.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/wildcall.png)
