Join in on the fairy-tale fun of [*Wilds of Eldraine*](https://web.archive.org/web/20230815000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/wilds-of-eldraine) with two new promo cards! Starting September 8, 2023, in Japan and October 27, 2023, everywhere else, [your local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20230815000000/https://locator.wizards.com/) will be able to provide these borderless anime versions of *Magic* cards: Ice Out and Pyroblast.

**Pyroblast Promo (Japanese)**

To receive these promos, just head down to your local WPN game store after the dates specified above. While supplies last, when you spend your region's equivalent of $50 USD or more (¥5,000 or more in Japan) on sealed *Magic: The Gathering* product in-store, you can receive these two anime borderless promos illustrated by Takuma Ebisu. With new cards and new artwork, your local game store has plenty to enjoy.

*Wilds of Eldraine* releases on September 8, 2023, and is available for preorder now! Find [your local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20230815000000/https://locator.wizards.com/) and prepare to discover the many secrets of the wilds.
