**Magic Online** continues to welcome *Innistrad* to Standard with some innovative takes on what the new cards can do. BDeCandio7 used a blue-red-green shell with Snapcaster Mage to rebuy spells and the team-up of Daybreak Ranger / Nightfall Predator and Mayor of Avabruck / Howlpack Alpha to punish slow opponents.
