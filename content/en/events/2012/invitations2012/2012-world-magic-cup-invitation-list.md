Each national team invited to the [2012 World **Magic** Cup](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2012-world-magic-cup) will consist of the following players:

*   The winner of each of the three (3) [2012 World **Magic** Cup Qualifiers](/en/events/2012/qualifiers/2012-world-magic-cup-qualifiers) run in that country.
*   The top ranked player at the conclusion of the 2011-2012 Professional Points Season from that country that is not already invited based on the above criteria. If two or more players are tied, ties will be broken as follows:

*   The tied player with the greatest number of Pro Tour Top 8 finishes during the 2011-2012 Professional Points Season, or
*   The tied player with the greatest number of Grand Prix Top 8 finishes during the 2011-2012 Professional Points Season, or
*   The tied player with the highest Pro Tour finish during the 2011-2012 Professional Points Season, or
*   The tied player with the highest point total at the conclusion of the 2012 Seasonal Award Season #1.

If you believe that your finish at a World **Magic** Cup Qualifier is in error, please visit [www.wizards.com/Magic/PlaneswalkerPoints](https://web.archive.org/web/20140217222015/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/PlaneswalkerPoints). After logging in, click the "Appeals Form" link at the bottom of the page and fill out the form.

Players who have (1) won a 2012 World **Magic** Cup Qualifier or (2) are the top Professional Points player invited to the World **Magic** Cup (invitations awarded on May 14, 2012) may not compete in further 2012 World **Magic** Cup Qualifiers.

All invitations are subject to the requirements limitations set forth in the [**Magic** Premier Event Invitation Policy](https://web.archive.org/web/20140307214247/http://www.wizards.com/wpn/Document.aspx?x=Magic_The_Gathering_Premier_Event_Invitation_Policy).
