Mike Hron, Matt Severa, and Justin Cohen had plenty to celebrate after they won the trophies this weekend. Hron has now won his third Team Grand Prix in a row that he’s attended; Pro Tour finalist and 2015 Rookie of the Year Justin Cohen has now finished in a Grand Prix Top 16; and Matt Severa has hit Silver and got himself qualified for a few more Pro Tours to come.
