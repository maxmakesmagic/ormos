Join the *Gatecrash* League and experience the newest **Magic: The Gathering** set in an exciting format!

The top finishers at the end of the league will get an alternate art Soldier token!

### Where Can I Join on February 1, 2013?

*Gatecrash* Leagues are happening at local stores all over the world! Check out our [tournament locator](https://web.archive.org/web/20130117011358/http://locator.wizards.com/) to find a *Gatecrash* League near you. Contact your local store to confirm the event venue, start time and date.

### What Happens There?

When you join the *Gatecrash* League, you will be given the opportunity to play with the newest cards in an on-going, five-week league. There will be prizes, and players can complete a league badge for fun and excitement. 

The suggested format for the *Gatecrash* league is **Sealed Deck** — Each player receives six *Gatecrash* booster packs to use for building a deck. Please check with your local store to confirm the league format.

Over the course of the league, you will be able to add more boosters to your league deck.

Each participant should play three matches per week, for a total of fifteen over the course of the league. Players joining the *Gatecrash* League late can play extra matches to catch up to the current match cap of three times the current total of weeks of the league.

### What Do I Get?

Each player will receive a league badge, and each week, if they play all three of their matches, they'll be rewarded with a guild symbol sticker they can attach to their badge.

At the end of the league, there will be cool prizes including an alternate art Soldier token. Your local store may offer other prizes as well. Be sure to contact them to confirm the details of their league.

### Have Questions?

Players and tournament organizers who have general questions about Leagues or any other Wizards of the Coast offerings should contact Customer Service at [www.wizards.com/customerservice](https://web.archive.org/web/20130117011358/http://www.wizards.com/customerservice), or call 800-324-6496 in North America, or check the global contact list for their local Customer Service office at [www.wizards.com/GlobalCS](https://web.archive.org/web/20130117011358/http://www.wizards.com/globalcs).
