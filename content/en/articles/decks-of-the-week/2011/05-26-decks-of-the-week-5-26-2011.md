N*ew Phyrexia* has begun making its impact on the Standard metagame. Pro Tour Philadelphia Qualifiers in North Carolina and Kansas have reported their Top 8 decks and the early movers and shakers are Batterskull, Sword of War and Peace, Dismember, Deceiver Exarch, Spellskite, and Despise. *New Phyrexia* [prerelease events on **Magic Online** begin Thursday](/en/articles/feature/2011/05-17-magic-online-new-phyrexia-prerelease) at 10 a.m. PT, with the set going on sale Monday, May 30.
