![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gprich14/qh_froehlich.jpg)

***Eric Froehlich**: Matej "Big Z" Zatlkaj* 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gprich14/qh_lau.jpg)

***Gaudenis Vidugiris**: [Empty the Warrens](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Empty+the+Warrens). Because people are really bad at playing around it. Last round someone set [Meddling Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meddling+Mage) on [Grapeshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grapeshot), then I untapped and cast [Empty the Warrens](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Empty+the+Warrens) for 12.* 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gprich14/qh_zvi.jpg)

***Zvi Moshowitz**: [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite). So many decks, when they do their thing, need to target something. Boggles just folds ... and Twin can’t go off either if it’s in play.* 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gprich14/qh_leyton.jpg)

***Josh Utter-Leyton**: [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize). It’s universally good versus combo ... it forces uninteractive decks to interact with you.* 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gprich14/qh_ravitz.jpg)

***Josh Ravitz**: [Fulminator Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fulminator+Mage). It’s used in the most matches; and even some places where you wouldn’t think. I side them in against both [Living End](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+End) and Affinity.*

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gprich14/qh_parke.jpg)

***Jamie Parke**: [Dispel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dispel+) #4 and [Flame Slash](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flame+Slash) #4. It's boring, but the extra copies are important. [Flame Slash](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flame+Slash) takes out [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) even when [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) doesn't ... and the extra one-mana counterspell is key against all the control decks.*
