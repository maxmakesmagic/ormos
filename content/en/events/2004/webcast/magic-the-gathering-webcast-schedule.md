### Pro Tour *Theros* Webcast Schedule

The live video webcast from [Pro Tour *Theros*](/en/events/2013/ptths13/dezani-proclaims-allez-les-bleus-at-pro-tour) features Rich Hagon, Brian David-Marshall, Tim Willoughby, Rashad Miller, Marshall Sutcliffe, and Zac Hill calling the action. 

A complete replay of each day from Dublin will be starting approximately 60 minutes after the conclusion of the live feed. The stream will be posted here with no information on the day's results, perfect for those trying to time-shift their live viewing experience.
