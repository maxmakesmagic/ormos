### General Information

**What is Magic Player Rewards ?** 

Magic Player Rewards is a free program for DCI members participating in sanctioned Magic: the Gathering tournaments. It rewards participation in sanctioned events in several ways:

*   Textless spells and premium cards mailed directly to the player
*   Clear Plastic Magic Player Rewards membership card with DCI number
*   Bi-weekly MPR HTML newsletters sent to your email address

Other benefits may be added in the future and will be announced on the [Magic Player Rewards homepage](https://web.archive.org/web/20050516000000/http://www.wizards.com/magicrewards/) and through emails sent to program members. A more detailed description of how textless spells and premium cards are awarded is found here.

**Who can join Magic Player Rewards? How much does it cost?** 

This program is open to any DCI member, worldwide, that is in good standing. We only ask for the following:

*   Your DCI number
*   A current mailing address (so that we can send you member benefits), and
*   A valid email address (so that we can use to contact you about the program)

Also, you must have played in one sanctioned tournament in the past year that was properly reported to the DCI.

**How do I sign up for Magic Player Rewards?** 

Click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20050516000000/https://membership.wizards.com/login.aspx?ReturnURL=/signup/signup.aspx) to register for the program.

**Why is an email address required?** 

By having your correct email address, we can send you benefit notifications, along with other tournament information and news items in the bi-weeklys MPR newsletter. You can choose not to have these notifications and news items to you, but we think you'll find these to be a solid benefit. The email address is also required in case we need to contact you regarding the status of your membership or changes in the program.

If a benefit mailing is returned to us, or if an email is returned due to an invalid email address, we may withhold or cancel membership benefits. Please update any change in your personal information by going to [webapp.wizards.com/dcimember](https://web.archive.org/web/20050516000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://webapp.wizards.com/dcimember/&origin=).

**How do I get a DCI Number?** 

If you have participated in a sanctioned tournament, the tournament organizer (this is sometimes the store owner) or event official, such as a judge, should have given you a DCI number. If you have lost or forgotten your DCI number, please go to our [online help](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_alp.php). 

To participate in sanctioned DCI events and to sign up for Magic Player Rewards, you need a DCI number. You can get a DCI number any place that holds sanctioned DCI events. You won't be able to enroll in Magic Player Rewards until your tournament organizer submits the registration portion of the card (where you fill out your name and address information) unless you use the activation part of your new DCI card to activate online.

**How do I find sanctioned tournaments?** 

The [Tournament Locator](https://web.archive.org/web/20050516000000/http://locator.wizards.com/) is the best way to find sanctioned tournaments in your area.

**How long will it take to process my enrollment request?** 

Requests that are submitted with complete and accurate information and a valid email address will be processed within 7 days of receipt.

**How can I see that my enrollment has been processed?** 

Go to the [personal information page](https://web.archive.org/web/20050516000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://webapp.wizards.com/dcimember/&origin=) and type in your DCI number. You will see the list of the memberships currently active with the DCI on the lower left column. Make sure Magic Player Reward Active is mentionned there.

### Detailed Information on Magic Player Reward Benefits

**When do you ship out the textless spells, premium and membership cards?** 

Benefits are shipped out to all eligible players three times a year: early March, early July and Early November. Membership cards are sent with the player's first shipment of textless spells. Currently, there is a six-week delay between the File Preparation Date and the day that we ship out the textless spells and premium cards. Because this is a worldwide program, we are shipping textless spells and foil Magic cards and membership cards to players in more than 60 countries, with different address formulations and benefit quantities. All this adds up to a complex fulfillment job, requiring significant time for preparation. We appreciate your patience with this delay.

**How do I access my match history?** 

You will need your DCI number to access your match history. Go to [Personal Statistics page](https://web.archive.org/web/20050516000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://webapp.wizards.com/dcimember/Default.asp&origin=) and follow the instructions on that page. Once you enter the Personal Statistics area, find the 'My Stuff' box in the upper right hand corner of the page, and click on "Ratings History". At the bottom of the next page will be three links, **Constructed, Limited,** and **Classic.** Click on one of these links to see your detailed individual match history.

Your participation in team events **does** count toward earning textless spells and premium cards.

**I signed-up for Magic Player Rewards last week, but I still can't see my membership in the membership information box of my DCI page. What can I do?** 

First, if you have already received the email with the confirmation link, please do **not** sign-up for Magic Player Rewards again. I you have not received any confirmation email with a link to click, make sure to remove wizards.com from your spam filter. This confirmation email is sent by dciautoemailer@wizards.com. If you have received this confirmation email, use the [Customer Service contact link](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/ask.php?) and include in your email your name, your DCI number and the date you filled out the Magic Player Rewards registration form. We'll send you back instructions on what to do.

**What are textless spells and Premium cards?** 

Textless spells are a special type of Magic: the Gathering cards. These are [tournament legal](https://web.archive.org/web/20101010220009/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dci/announce/dci20040923a) well-known spells (like Terror and Fireball) that feature new and enlarged art and don't have any text on them besides their name. Since any Magic player worth their salt knows what these classic spells do, you should have no problem using them in your constructed decks. **What happened to the token cards this program used to send out?** 

After five years of sending out tokens through the Magic Player Rewards program, we decided it was time to try something different. Hence, the textless spells were created.

**How do I get the textless spells and premium cards? How many will you send me?** 

Textless spells and premium cards are mailed out three times a year directly to Magic Player Reward members. The number of textless spells and premium cards that are sent out is determined by the number of sanctioned events that a member participates in and are properly reported to the DCI at the time we prepare the file for the mailing.

*   For every **5 tournaments** a player has listed in the DCI database, he/she receives **1 new random textless spell** and **1 random older card** wrapped together; and
*   For every **20 events**, you receive a **premium foil card**.

The number of cards we send to you for each of the three mailings per year depend on two dates: your Sign-up Date and the File Preparation Date. Your Sign-up Date is the date that we activate your membership. The File Preparation Date is when we have to send the file to our printer so that you can receive your mailing. At that time, we count the number of sanctioned events you have participated in since your sign-up date. We determine the number of textless spells and premium cards to send you by counting the number of tournaments you've participated in and have been properly reported by the tournament organizer. We then subtract the number of cards that have already been sent to you in previous mailings, and send you the difference.

**I signed up for Magic Player Rewards several weeks ago. I've played in over five tournaments, but still haven't received any textless spells. When will you be sending them to me?** 

Textless spells and premium cards are sent out three times a year to all eligible players. If you have played in more than five reported sanctioned events, you will receive token cards in the next scheduled mailing.

**What is the Magic Player Rewards membership card?** 

This clear plastic card lists your name and DCI number; it is designed to be more durable that the paper DCI cards that are distributed at card shops. This card also shows your DCI number, which is the number that you should use in all DCI sanctioned tournaments you participate in. 

**What is that barcode on the front of the card?** 

We continue to look for ways to enhance the tournament experience of all our players. A common complaint among players at larger events such as prerelease events, Pro Tour Qualifiers and Grand Prix events, is the length of time it takes to register for the event. In the future, we hope to introduce a method for tournament organizers to enroll you for an event simply by scanning the bar code on your Magic Player Reward Membership card. We will let members know when and where they will be able to use this feature.

**When do you send out the membership card?** 

We send you a membership card with your first shipment of textless spells. The card is valid up until the end of the 2006, and will be renewed regularly as long as you continue to play in tournaments and qualify to receive textless spells.

**How do I receive the MPR biweekly HTML newsletter?** 

Make sure you do not click "remove me from the mass contacts list" when you sign up or change this online when you update your information.

### Other Questions about Tournaments

**How can I check on the status of the tournaments I've played in?** 

You can visit [webapp.wizards.com/dcitournament/](https://web.archive.org/web/20050516000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://webapp.wizards.com/dcitournament/&origin=) to look up the status of almost any DCI-sanctioned event you've played in. If you know the organizer's name, you can search by his or her name and the tournament date. Otherwise, you can search by metro area (in the U.S. only) or city and the date of the event. Just remember to change the tournament status from "Rated" to "All" before you start to search.

Below is a brief description of the status codes you'll see or the events listed on the tournament search page:

*   **Received (RCD):** The tournament report from the event has been received by the DCI. Barring any problems:

*   *a report submitted on paper* may take up to two ratings runs (approximately two - three weeks) to move from being listed as Received to being included in the ratings; and
*   *a report submitted electronically* will be included in the next available ratings run, provided it is received at least 48 hours prior to the date ratings are run (Monday).

*   **Processed (PRO):** Matches from this event have been processed and included in the ratings.
*   **Not received (NOT):** Matches have not yet been received from this event, but it isn't late yet.
*   **Delinquent (DLQ):** Matches have not yet been received from this event, and they are late.
*   **Cancelled (CNC):** The organizer has cancelled the sanctioning for this event.
*   **Invalidated (INV):** The match results for this tournament have been invalidated because results were corrupted and irretrievable, did not meet DCI requirements, or violated DCI policy. For example, Magic events received with fewer than 8 players are invalidated. Additionally, organizers participating in events outside the parameters of the 3-judge system (8-16 players) are invalidated.

QuickSanctioned tournaments (those taking place as side events at Premier events such as Pro Tour Qualifiers or prereleases) and some international events are not listed in our database until we receive the results, so they will not show up on this search. To check on the tournament status for these events, be sure to gather the following information from the event organizer so you can verify its status later, if necessary:

*   Organizer's Name
*   Event Name
*   Event Format
*   Event Date
*   QuickSanctioning number or DCI sanctioning number (this is often in the format xx-xx-xxxxxx)
*   Premier Event Name (examples: Pro Tour-Chicago, *Invasion* prerelease)

**What can I do if the tournament is listed as DLQ (delinquent)?** 

If you have questions about the status of event results that are delinquent, it is usually better to check with the tournament organizer for event information -- including results submission-before contacting the DCI. If you have not received a satisfactory response from a tournament organizer regarding an event results submission, *and its status is DLQ* check out the [DCI Match History Appeals process](https://web.archive.org/web/20110308031859/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dci/appeals).

*As it often takes two to three weeks to process event results, please do not file a missing tournament inquiry sooner than six weeks after an event has occurred.* 

**Do Friday Night Magic tournaments count toward Magic Player Rewards?** 

Yes, Friday Night Magic tournaments are sanctioned, so they do count toward Magic Player Reward benefits. For more information about Friday Night Magic, go to the [Friday Night Magic home page](https://web.archive.org/web/20050516000000/http://www.wizards.com/fnm/).

**Do Arena matches count toward Magic Player Rewards?** 

No, Arena league matches are unsanctioned, and do not count toward receiving Magic Player Reward benefits. For more information on Magic: the Gathering Arena League, go to the [Arena home page](https://web.archive.org/web/20050516000000/http://www.wizards.com/Arena/).
