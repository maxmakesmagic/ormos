Our final **Magic** Game Day deck this week comes from Saitama, Japan. Shun Ikeda played what looked like an ordinary Bant deck. Of course, when the opponent least expected it, Time Warp or Savor the Moment hit the stack and Ikeda happily took an extra turn while a hapless opponent could only watch in horror. Add to that the interaction of Fertile Ground and Garruk Wildspeaker's untap ability, and the "downside" of Savor the Moment didn't bring him down!
