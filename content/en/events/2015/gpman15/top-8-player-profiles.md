After sixteen rounds of Modern, only eight players remained in the running for the title of Grand Prix champion. About time to get to know them a little better, isn't it?

* * *

 

### Tzu-Ching Kuo

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/T8-Kuo-Tzu-Ching.jpg)

**City:** Taipei  
**Age:** 33  
**Occupation:** Globetrotter  
**Score for Day One:** 8-1 (2 byes)  
**Score for Day Two:** 4-1-1  
**Pro Tour and Grand Prix Top 8s:** 11 GP Top 8s  
**Other Achievements:** Won 2012 World Magic Cup, National Champion 2002, 2009, 2010, 2012, 2013, 2014  
**What deck are you playing today and why?**  
 Abzan Midrange, because Cheng Tung-Yi told me this deck is good (built by Chen Liang)  
**What is your MVP this weekend?**  
 Maindeck [End Hostilities](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=End+Hostilities)

### Lee Shi Tian

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/T8-Lee-Shi-Tian.jpg)

**City:** Hong Kong  
**Age:** 27  
**Occupation:** Accountant  
**Score for Day One:** 8-1 (3 byes)  
**Score for Day Two:** (4-0-2)  
**Pro Tour and Grand Prix Top 8s:** 3 Pro Tour Top 8s, 5 GP Top 8s (including this one!)  
**Other Achievements:** Last place in Worlds Championship 2014. Hong Kong Captain for 2009, 2013 and 2014.  
**What deck are you playing today and why?**  
 Umbrella Revolution, with only a couple of cards different from my PT Honolulu Top 8 list. Sometimes we just need to persist until the day we succeed! (PS. Cheep up Yui!!)  
**What is your MVP this weekend?**  
[Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy). Yet I was a fool for not playing maximum [Torment](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torment)ing Voice before.
