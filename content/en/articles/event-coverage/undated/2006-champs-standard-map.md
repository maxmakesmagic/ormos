 Congratulations to 2006 Champs Triple Crown winners Thomas Evaristo (Delaware), Brett McCleaf (Kansas), Guillaume Daoust (Quebec), Robert Kadlec (Indiana), and Wilbert Yeung (Rhode Island). Each Triple Crown winner will be sent a case of *Time Spiral* booster packs and a bevy of foil promotional cards, including Sol Ring, Regrowth, Vampiric Tutor, and Ball Lightning.

 Mouse over the map to see the Champs winners in your state or province, and then click on a state or province to see the Top 8 decklists.
