The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on July 19, 2016. Today's Update is brought to you by [these sweet San Diego Comic-Con *Magic* promos](/en/articles/feature/2016/07-19-san-diego-comic-con-2016-magic-promos).

# Today's Must

1.  [There's No Problem with Politics in Commander](http://generaldamagecontrol.com/theres-no-problem-with-politics-in-commander/) | General Damage Control | Chris "EDH Ghost"

Yesterday there was an article on StarCityGames.com by Erik Tiernan titled "[The Problem with Politics in Commander](http://www.starcitygames.com/article/33302_The-Problem-With-Politics-In-Commander.html)," in which he covered some discussion points on politics in Commander that he had had with EDH Ghost (aka Chris). Chris, in turn, wrote a rebuttal of sorts. Both are excellent reading on what it means to be political in a game of Commander and how useful, or not, those distinctions are.

# After That, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [Commander Review: *Eldritch Moon* Part 1 (White, Blue, Black](http://www.mtggoldfish.com/articles/commander-review-eldritch-moon-part-1-white-blue-black)) | MTGGoldfish | Tomer Abramovici

Let's stick with Commander for the moment. There are so few Commander set reviews these days, every one is like a precious little gem, at least compared to straight-up set reviews. If you're into Commander, savor this. *I said savor it!*

1.  [5 Cards That Get Better with *Eldritch Moon*](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/5-cards-that-get-better-with-eldritch-moon/) | ChannelFireball | Brian Braun-Duin

Minus the cheeky ending (and it is oh-so cheeky), this is a good look at how the format can shift once *Eldritch Moon* is introduced. Just because a card already in print isn't seeing play now doesn't mean it won't when the metagame shifts. Here are 5 4 cards the current leader in the Grand Prix Master standings think will get better in just a few days.

1.  [The Greatest Sealed Pool Ever](http://www.magictheamateuring.com/mta-episode-182-the-greatest-sealed-pool-ever/) | Magic the Amateuring | Maria Bartholdi and Meghan Wolff

This one is worth it just to hear tales of Maria's busted Sealed pool at the *Eldritch Moon* Prerelease. However busted you're thinking, it was better than that. Additionally, Maria and Meghan answer some listener questions and have some fun with flavor text from *Shadows over Innistrad* and *Eldritch Moon*.

1.  [*Magic: The Gathering* Office Hours: Sorin Markov](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L-q0oLZmj8Y&feature=youtu.be) | Tolarian Community College | The Professor

There's a lot of staring in this one. And Sorin goes positively metal. But with hair like that, why wouldn't he?

1.  [The BUG with Twelve Legs](http://www.hipstersofthecoast.com/2016/07/bug-twelve-legs/) | Hipsters of the Coast | Jerry Mee

This article isn't terribly topical—there's no *Eldritch Moon* content to be found—but I found this article about a Legacy Dreadnought/[Stifle](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stifle) deck to be really funny and highly entertaining. Heck, the graphics and captions alone were enough to make me chuckle before I had my coffee. Even if Legacy isn't your thing, this is a funny, informative read that's worth your time.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Blue-Red Tutelage](http://magic.tcgplayer.com/db/article.asp?ID=13399&writer=TCGplayer+Staff&articledate=7-19-2016)

Remember when [Sphinx's Tutelage](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Tutelage) decks were all the rage? Pepperid...er, I remember. Because they always trounced my control decks packed with removal.

Well, Seth Manfield wants to bring us back to those harrowing days with a new take on the archetype boosted by some *Eldritch Moon* standouts. [Bedlam Reveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bedlam+Reveler) and [Curious Homunculus](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Curious+Homunculus) give the deck reasons to play creatures, while [Scour the Laboratory](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scour+the+Laboratory) provides some much-needed card draw. [Nahiri's Wrath](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nahiri%27s+Wrath) and [Unsubstantiate](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unsubstantiate) keep the board clear and, of course, [Sphinx's Tutelage](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Tutelage) provides the win condition.

Is this deck good? Maybe. Is it better than a [Thing in the Ice](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thing+in+the+Ice)/[Pyromancer's Goggles](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyromancer%27s+Goggles) deck? Who knows! But it does look like a lot of fun. For the person playing it. The opponent will probably be miserable.
