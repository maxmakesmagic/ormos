Congratulations to Pro Tour Hall of Famer Brian Kibler, your Pro Tour *Dark Ascension* champion. Kibler and his Wolf Run Ramp deck defeated Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa in a five-game final to earn him his second Pro Tour title. In the semifinals, Kibler played one of the most memorable matches in **Magic** history when he beat Pro Tour Hall of Famer Jon Finkel in five incredibly tense games. Complete coverage of Pro Tour *Dark Ascension* can be found [here](/en/events/2012/ptdka12/kibler-triumphs-in-epic-pro-tour).

Brian Kibler 

1st Place - Pro Tour Dark Ascension, Standard Constructed
