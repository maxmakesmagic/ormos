**Announcement Date:** June 20, 2011  
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2011  
**Magic Online Effective Date:** June 29, 2011

**Standard**  
[Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110620000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) is banned.  
[Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20110620000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic) is banned.

*Exception:* The deck list for the "[War of Attrition](https://web.archive.org/web/20110620000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/new-phyrexia-event-decks-deck-lists-2011-05-19)" Event Deck will be legal in Standard if kept completely intact. That deck, which went on sale on June 10, contains two copies of [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20110620000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic).

On **Magic Online**, [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110620000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) and [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20110620000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic) will both be banned in Standard as of the June 29, 2011 downtime. There will be no exceptions made for the Event Deck containing [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20110620000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic), as that deck will not be sold online.

***Scars of Mirrodin* Block Constructed, Extended, Legacy, Vintage**  
 No changes

Changes to **Magic Online**–only formats are now announced monthly in the [Magic Online Community Group blog](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-gatecrash-qualifier-season-top-8-standard-decklists-43).

For the complete list of all banned and restricted cards by format, click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20110620000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=judge/resources/banned).

For an explanation of the B&R changes from R&D member Aaron Forsythe, click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20110620000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/standard-bannings-explained-2011-06-20).
