Last week, [we looked at some of the new art](/en/articles/arcana/2014/11-05-new-art-in-commander-2014-edition) in *Commander* (2014 Edition), but we had such an overwhelming amount of new art that we couldn't fit it all in to a single Arcana (or, for that matter, [two Arcanas](/en/articles/arcana/2014/10-29-a-wizards-best-friend)).

So, due to popular demand, we've come back for another go-round with new art from the latest *Commander* release. Enjoy!

# Tornado Elemental

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Tornado%20Elemental)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tornado%20Elemental)

Originally printed in *Fifth Dawn*, [Tornado Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tornado+Elemental) made a return entry in *Planechase*. It wasn't until this year, however, that this bomb-y force of nature changed its art. [Richard Wright](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5B%22Richard%20Wright%22%5D) was definitely right on with his new vision of what a tornado brought to life would look like.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/cardart_tornadoelemental.jpg)

[Tornado Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tornado+Elemental) | Art by [Richard Wright](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Richard%20Wright%22%5D)

# Wellwisher

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Wellwisher)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wellwisher)

[Wellwisher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wellwisher) looks so unassuming at first, but anyone who has ever played an Elf deck knows this innocuous 1/1 can completely dominate some games, gaining dozens of life over multiple turns. [Karl Kopinski](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5B%22Karl%20Kopinski%22%5D) provides an update to the classic [Christopher Rush](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5B%22Christopher%20Rush%22%5D) look.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/cardart_wellwisher.jpg)

[Wellwisher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wellwisher) | Art by [Karl Kopinski](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Karl%20Kopinski%22%5D)

# Cathodion

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Cathodion)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cathodion)

Any time an artifact set comes around, it seems Cathodion sticks its amusing head up to say hello. Given the red deck's artifact theme, it's no surprise to see Cathodion return. What might have been a surprise was that Cathodion got a third new take in its third printing. This time, [Izzy](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5B%22Izzy%22%5D) takes on the built-to-die Construct with pleasing results.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/cardart_cathodion.jpg)

[Cathodion](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cathodion) | Art by [Izzy](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Izzy%22%5D)

* * *

Additionally, your art-sense might have been set tingling when you saw these two "new" pieces. They sure look new, but maybe you couldn't tell why they were so familiar? Well, these two pieces have actually never seen a physical printing before, even though they did get to see the light of day in *Vintage Masters* in the digital realm. In fact, so did [Desert Twister](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desert+Twister) (shown last week). But we wanted to point them out to you completists out there, because we're nothing if not thorough.
