The *Alara Reborn* Prerelease was fun, right? [Kelly Digges](/en/articles/seriousfun/2009/04-28-fi-r-st-impressions) seems to have enjoyed it. And judging by this smattering of responses to @[dailymtg](https://web.archive.org/web/20090428000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.twitter.com/dailymtg&origin=), some of you did as well!

Here's how it started:

> (Kelly) Had a great time at the Prerelease. Any of you have good stories from the weekend?

And here are some of our favorite 140-character stories:
