### Blue

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f8/a8/f8a8588c1b5475624186c19686587e3b7b2b93f9654fb7475142701feaea6ec6.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4c/6c/4c6ce3a8f63fe519ecb5e086a5aa7186a72be53e690f6c63d52fab899f598a4b.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ad/90/ad90168c10a875a4cb2d1d02b54f52304d11955b2bcd0d807e0210cf5650839b.jpg)

What new to bring to an Island? My original idea was that Islands would just be huge chunks of Phyrexian wreckage on the landscape. What made it blue? Well, it would always be shown raining on the chunk! Yeah. That didn’t pan out. We recentered on texture and shape. Acid rain seemed right to me for a post apocalyptic blue, and Cranford was into the idea of weathered porous stone. Those two ideas ended up combining into what I called ‘tunnel-in-tunnel’ igneous structures. The idea was that geologic turmoil would force up these magma spouts, which would freeze immediately in the remaining water of the oceans, and then cough up another form through that. The result would be foreboding igneous structures, almost ominously organic in shape, that would be pitted and worn from above by the incessant acidic rains. The look of blue-aligned peoples would also be determined by this direction. They wear guttered helms and fluted armor over leather drapes to shunt the acid rains away from their flesh as quickly as possible. Normal metal would be rusted and corroded from above, and some sort of magical or at least highly acid-resistant blue metal would be prized for its ability to withstand the rains. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ee/43/ee4307f3b3faa6dd6e8449bf353574da3f988a3e7d76f810939d92643f56fddb.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7a/87/7a875120cbe671e869f22e9c0ac7866849809ab51b4c7e58bbf55655ac69b7c0.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/71/f1/71f1dbed01344bdcb0cdf2054419cf0bb4a860661cf461d6ecb75cc0cafb5791.jpg)

Any person or creature not protected would show the consequence or exposure: perforated wings, clumps of missing hair, a scar or stripe for each drop of precipitation that met their skin.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/13/0a/130acf996a1d7dc0ee7674346b07cdf8218287f8cb84d49536397cb2695532dc.jpg)

![Voidmage_Husher_640](https://i.ormos.uk/9d/28/9d287ceb8af7b840aa7f29443f13ccd1524b280c9f5de6ef40ab68db0462cff6.jpg)  
*Chippy Dugan’s Voidmage Husher is a fantastic example of blue treatment. Corroded metal, guttered armor, and if you look closely, some scarring on her face and thigh from the acid rains.*
