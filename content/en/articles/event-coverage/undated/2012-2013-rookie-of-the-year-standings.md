![Magic: the Gathering Pro Tour](https://i.ormos.uk/72/33/7233580ab42233846709a253f2c477d01df8a3ce0e4a4de5f531a79594c6b5fb.jpg "Magic: the Gathering Pro Tour") 

Congratulations to [Felipe Tapia Becerra, the 2012-13 Rookie of the Year](/en/events/2013/ptdgm13/2013-rookie-of-the-year-felipe-tapia-becerra)! The player with the most Pro Points at the end of a tournament season who has not participated in a Pro Tour or World Championship tournament in any previous season is identified as the Rookie of the Year. 

This page shows all rookie players who have received at least 5 Pro Points in the 2012-2013 season (from May 14, 2012 to May 19, 2013).
