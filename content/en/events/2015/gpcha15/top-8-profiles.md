* * *

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpcha15/Top-8-Zach-Jesse.jpg)Name:** Zach Jesse

**Age:** 30

**Hometown:** Richmond, VA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 Several Grand Prix Top 8s. Did well at several Pro Tour but no Top 8s

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 “Griselshoalbrand” – The deck is ridiculous. Honestly though, I played it because I wanted to have fun

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
 Nourishing Shoul. The deck couldn’t operate without it. I also like [Worldspine Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Worldspine+Wurm) as nothing feels better than putting a 15/15 into play.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon). It’s even better when you cast it on turn 1 or 2.

**What was the most interesting play you made so far this weekend?**  
 Lots of turn 2 kills. I hard cast [Griselbrand](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Griselbrand) three times, twice in one game. I’ve also spliced onto Arcane more than any other time in my life. I kill my opponent in response to end step Deciever Exarch multiple times.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpcha15/Top-8-Wesley-See.jpg)Name:** Wesley See

**Age:** 23

**Hometown:** Indianapolis, IN

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 States 2014

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Blue-Red Splinter twin because [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) is a busted card.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon). See above.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
 A tie between the third [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) and the first [Keranos, God of Storms](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Keranos%2C+God+of+Storms) simply because they’re both unfair in different ways.

**What was the most interesting play you made so far this weekend?**  
[Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) targeting [Sower of Temptation](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sower+of+Temptation) to steal my opponent’s manlands in combat.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpcha15/Top-8-Sam-Pardee.jpg)Name:** Sam Pardee

**Age**: 25

**Hometown:** Berkeley, CA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 Five previous Grand Prix Top 8s with one win.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Blue-Red [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin). They took my [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod)s away!

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand). The card is so punishing on the play and enables from absurd tricks.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Teferi, Mage of Zhalfir](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%2C+Mage+of+Zhalfir). No one knew what it did and it enables from sweet blowouts.

**What was the most interesting play you made so far this weekend?**  
 Using Teferi to allow me to [Vendilion Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vendilion+Clique) away my opponent’s Rending Volley.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpcha15/Top-8-Donald-Smith.jpg)Name:** Donald Smith Jr.

**Age:** 20

**Hometown:** Cut Off, LA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 22nd Grand Prix Las Vegas. I have a winning record versus Tom Ross.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Burn. Burn has a fighting change versus all the decks while being good versus Grixis decks.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Flame Javelin](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flame+Javelin). It dodges [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek), kills Deciever Exarch, doesn’t trigger [Eidolon of the Great Revel](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eidolon+of+the+Great+Revel), and hits the face for 4.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile). And extremely tempo efficient catch-all answer.

**What was the most interesting play you made so far this weekend?**  
 Cast [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) targeting my opponent.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpcha15/Top-8-Michael-Malone.jpg)Name:** Michael Malone

**Age:** 24

**Hometown:** Destine, FL

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 TCG Player Jacksonville 5k Winner  
 Top 32 Grand Prix Nashville  
 Day 2 Pro Tour *Dragons of Tarkir*

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
[Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company) Elves. It’s fantastic against the “fair” decks and after sideboarding the big mana decks (Red-Green Tron and Amulet Bloom) get reasonable.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
 Collect Company. It allows me to go over the top of everything in terms of card advantage and board presence. Honorable mention to [Ezuri, Renegade Leader](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ezuri%2C+Renegade+Leader): That card is sick.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Bow of Nylea](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bow+of+Nylea). The card is great versus Affinity and Burn. Deathtouch plus trample is always nice as well.

**What was the most interesting play you made so far this weekend?**  
 Casting [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company) into double [Elvish Archdruid](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Archdruid) to pump my creatures three times with Ezuri’s [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) ability.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpcha15/Top-8-Ian-Bosley.jpg)Name:** Ian Bosley

**Age:** 25

**Hometown:** Charleston, SC

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
  
**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Abzan Company. Strong combo deck with grind potential (and I love the [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company)).

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company)/[Fiend Hunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiend+Hunter). Grind advantage engine that can combo kill and instant speed removal with [Chord of Calling](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chord+of+Calling)/[Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Tidehollow Sculler](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidehollow+Sculler). Too good against combo and land decks.

**What was the most interesting play you made so far this weekend?**  
[Chord of Calling](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chord+of+Calling) for a chain of Eternal Witnesses against Jund with an active [Gavony Township](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gavony+Township), or swinging my [Melira, Sylvok Outcast](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Melira%2C+Sylvok+Outcast) into a [Griselbrand](https://web.archive.org/web/20150614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Griselbrand) and conceding.

 I played “Robots” this weekend because I’m ana ggro player and it doesn’t get more aggressive than ‘bots.
