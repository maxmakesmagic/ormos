# Which *Dragons of Tarkir* card do you think will have the biggest impact on Standard?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_lee.jpg)  
**Lee Shi Tian, Back-to-Back Pro Tour Top 8s this Season:** [Sidisi, Undead Vizier](https://web.archive.org/web/20150410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sidisi%2C+Undead+Vizier). It means midrange decks can have better access to their silver bullets and trumps.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_lax.jpg)  
**Ari Lax, Pro Tour *Khans of Tarkir* Champion:** [Thunderbreak Regent](https://web.archive.org/web/20150410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thunderbreak+Regent). There are other cards worth mentioning, but that's the one which defines the entire metagame.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_Nakamura.jpg)  
**Shuhei Nakamura, Five Pro Tour Top 8s:** [Dragonlord Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20150410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai). The card is great. It's hard to remove when you play it, and easy to protect after that.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_turtenwald.jpg)  
**Owen Turtenwald, Chasing his Second Player of the Year Title:** [Dromoka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dromoka%27s+Command). It seems like a pretty safe answer. It's a popular, exciting spell and all of its modes are relevant.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_shenhar.jpg)  
**Shahar Shenhar, World Champion Two Years Running:** [Dragonlord Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20150410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai). I think any deck with access to blue and white will want it, and Blue-Black Control may splash for it. It conveniently survives a [Crux of Fate](https://web.archive.org/web/20150410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crux+of+Fate).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_chapin.jpg)  
**Patrick Chapin, Pro Tour *Journey Into Nyx* Champion:** [Thunderbreak Regent](https://web.archive.org/web/20150410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thunderbreak+Regent). It's a very efficient creature and exactly what a number of decks were looking for.
