The Magic: The Gathering® Grand Prix tournament series is an international circuit of large-scale Qualifier tournaments featuring cash prizes and multiple invitations to Pro Tour™ events.

![](http://www.wizards.com/global/images/grandprix_atlanta03_facts_picMain_en.jpg) **Grand Prix—Atlanta is presented by Southeast Magic and Dragon\*Con** 

**TOURNAMENT FORMAT AND SCHEDULE**  
 The format for Grand Prix–Atlanta is Standard. All DCI™ Constructed-format rules, **Magic**® Floor Rules, and all subsequent updates will be enforced.

**Note:** Eighth Edition will be legal for this tournament, instead of Seventh Edition.

**Friday, August 29:** Registration from 9:00 A.M.-9:00 P.M.  
**Saturday, August 30:** Registration from 7:00-8:45 A.M.  
 Tournament begins at 9:00 A.M. The Top 64 players advance to competition on Sunday  
**Sunday, August 31:** The Top 64 players begin play at 9:00 A.M.

www.Dragoncon.org

**TOURNAMENT**  
 The main tournament at Grand Prix–Atlanta is open to **Magic** players of all ages.  
*In the event that a player who wins an invitation to a Pro Tour stop at a Grand Prix has already qualified for an invitation, the invitation shall be extended to the next-best finisher in that Grand Prix. Previous qualification may be through a Qualifier tournament, through performance at a professional tournament, **or** by being an invited DCI™ player (valid upon the date of the official rank announcement for the appropriate professional tournament).*

*   **Tournament Structure**
     The structure for Grand Prix–Atlanta will be modified Swiss-style (50-minute rounds), with the Top 64 players advancing to the second day of competition for prizes and invitations. The top 8 previously uninvited Players will receive invitations to Pro Tour-New Orleans.

*   **Judges**
     The Head Judge for Grand Prix–Atlanta will be Sheldon Menery, Level 3.

    If you wish to volunteer as a judge at Grand Prix–Atlanta, please go to www.southeastmagic.com/gpatlanta2003/judges.htm for contact information and requirements.

*   **Tournament Divisions and Prizes**
     Grand Prix–Atlanta will feature the standard “main-tournament” prize schedule as well as a special “Amateur" prize schedule. The Amateur prizes will be paid to the highest-finishing amateur players *regardless of their standing in the Grand Prix.* All prizes are in U.S. Dollars.

*   **Amateur Status**
     A player is considered to have amateur status if he or she has 0 lifetime pro points. If a player has ever been awarded ANY pro points from a Grand Prix, Pro Tour, or World Championship event, that player no longer has amateur status, may not participate in the Amateur Championship or Junior Super Series tournaments, and is not eligible for amateur awards at Grand Prix or other tournaments.

*   **Tournament Byes** *(Byes are not cumulative throughout the season or year)*
     Byes will be given to players in the Grand Prix based on the following criteria:

    > ***Third-Round Byes***
    >
    > *   Players with 28 or more professional points in the 2003 Yokohama Pro Standings.
    > *   DCI players with a Constructed rating of 2000 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI Constructed ratings published on June 18, 2003.
    >
    > ***Second-Round Byes***
    >
    > *   Players with 14 or more professional points in the 2003 Yokohama Pro Standings.
    > *   DCI players with a Constructed rating of 1900 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI Constructed ratings published on June 18, 2003.
    >
    > ***First-Round Byes***
    >
    > *   Players with 7 or more professional points in the 2003 Yokohama Pro Standings.
    > *   DCI players with a Constructed rating of 1800 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI Constructed ratings published on June 18, 2003.
