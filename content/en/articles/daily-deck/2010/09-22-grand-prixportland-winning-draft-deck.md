Martin Juza is one of Europe's (and the Pro Tour's) very best players, and he proved it again using ***Magic** 2011* Booster Draft at Grand Prix–Portland. There the Czech player took on a Top 8 featuring big names like Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa and David Ochoa to finish in 1st place. Read full coverage of the event [here](/en/events/2010/gppor10/juza-reigns-in-northwest).
