As *Shadowmoor* card previews continue, we thought we'd spend a little more time with card's little brother, the token. Here are the remaining tokens, soon to be appearing in boosters everywhere!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c5/db/c5db24cd4235fa80f368d631359ca7fb7c7d097147150f067a846ee702058459.jpg)

A green *and white* Elf Warrior makes an appearance, cohort of preview cards like [Rhys the Redeemed](https://web.archive.org/web/20080518030759/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/shadowmoor/spoiler).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fd/9c/fd9c4b1ed61d380078cb9d6c961121abcad39e32bca67cfb6e74e7d8ad2d9881.jpg)

A 1/1 red Elemental token with haste! Let's hope he doesn't bring along too many of his buddies...

![](https://i.ormos.uk/39/3b/393ba275b97698bfd94f82185808645abf3b860c36d96e7673bbb4a8986819d2.jpg)

Interesting. A 1/1 flying Spirit token, floating through *Shadowmoor*'s eternal night.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/21/48/2148bf0b4cf7dbc81dbf731dae3b9efb298173d7ea5665dc12c3e3dea76b20cb.jpg)

What's this? A 1/2 Spider token with reach! Somebody page guest columnist [Jon Becker](https://web.archive.org/web/20080410000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/db30)!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/87/4b/874bf80ce054c9368fd0e01dfed8df60be9d04c87232ca27f4fdb189b90be412.jpg)

[Oona's](https://web.archive.org/web/20080410000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/db31) Faerie Rogue children seem right at home here in *Shadowmoor*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/32/67/326751833b7056fe0cc7e7b446eab78cb32349ab55516b678cdb37684917078e.jpg)

Finally, we leave you with something a bit smashier. This 4/4 hasty Giant Warrior means business... and his business is definitely smashing face!

Can’t wait for *Shadowmoor*’s release on May 2? Don’t miss your first chance to play with *Shadowmoor* cards at the [Prerelease](https://web.archive.org/web/20080410000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prereleases&dcmp=ILC-MTGCOMPRE) on April 19 and 20!
