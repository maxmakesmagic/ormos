![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gphir03/a947.jpg)Local Boy Takao Higaki is tearing up this Grand Prix with [Tinker](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tinker). If he keeps his current pace, he'll have a title of his own before the day is done. First he must best Katsuhiro "Blitzschlag" Mori, former Rookie of the Year and the fastest hands in the east. Mori is playing a [Burning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Wish)[Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog) deck that's packed with hate for creature decks. He's ill-equipped to handle [Tinker](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tinker). In fact, he drew with Akira Asahara in round 7 rather than risk his Day 2 against it.

### Game 1

Higaki earned the right to play first, a huge advantage. Mori added to it by mulliganing. Higaki started with [Ancient Tomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Tomb), [Grim Monolith](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Monolith) and [Metalworker](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metalworker). Mori dropped [Urborg Volcano](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Urborg+Volcano). Higaki made [Saprazzan Skerry](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Saprazzan+Skerry) and showed a pair of [Masticore](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Masticore)s to untap his Monolith. He was setting up for a huge [Stroke of Genius](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stroke+of+Genius). Mori [Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fire)d the [Metalworker](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metalworker) to keep things reasonable. Higaki drew five.

Mori dropped a third land and waited for Higaki's move. Higaki dropped [Voltaic Key](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voltaic+Key), [Thran Dynamo](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thran+Dynamo) and [Tangle Wire](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tangle+Wire), with three left over for Mori's [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak). Mori was trapped under the Wire. Higaki [Tinker](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tinker)ed up [Mishra's Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mishra%27s+Helix), earning the concession.

Higaki 1 – Mori 0

### Game 2

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gphir03/a948.jpg)Higaki practically fell out of his chair when Mori started Game 2 with just an [Urborg Volcano](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Urborg+Volcano). His [Ancient Tomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Tomb) and [Defense Grid](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Defense+Grid) fell onto the table of their own accord. Mori had no follow-up, and Higaki was free to resolve [Metalworker](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metalworker).

Mori untapped, dropped his third land, and tapped all three, putting down [Nightscape Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightscape+Familiar) and aiming [Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fire) at the [Metalworker](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metalworker). The judge reminded him that his lands were [Urborg Volcano](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Urborg+Volcano) and two islands. Worse, he couldn't take back his Familiar, and so was unable to dispatch the Worker. Given the contents of Higaki's hand, it looked like he would win regardless, but suddenly the game was a slaughter.

He untapped, then displayed four artifacts with the [Metalworker](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metalworker). The eight mana gave him two [Thran Dynamo](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thran+Dynamo)es, and that let him play [Stroke of Genius](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stroke+of+Genius) for five. That, in turn, handed him [Voltaic Key](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voltaic+Key) to untap the [Metalworker](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metalworker) and show three more artifacts to give him a [Planar Portal](https://web.archive.org/web/20030125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Planar+Portal). Mori packed it in.

**Final Result:** Takao Higaki defeats Katsuhiro Mori 2-0
