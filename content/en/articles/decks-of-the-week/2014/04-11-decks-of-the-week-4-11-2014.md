The finals of [Grand Prix Phoenix](/en/events/2014/gpphx14/bernis-devotion-pays-off-in-phoenix) featured two of the defining decks of this Standard format: Black Devotion and Blue Devotion. In the end, it was Robert Berni piloting Black who won the day. Congratulations, Robert! *Journey into Nyx* is in previews (the [Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424042028/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/journeyintonyx/cig) updates daily), and will undoubtedly have an impact on Standard when it releases on May 2. But for the Pro Tour ***Magic** 2015* Qualifier season, the format is Sealed Deck. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#m15). Good luck; have fun!
