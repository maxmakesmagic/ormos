This week's "Sketches" takes an insider's look at that [coastal piracy](https://web.archive.org/web/20030805000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=coastal%2Bpiracy)-esque Sliver from *Legions*, [Synapse Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20030805000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Synapse+Sliver). **Magic** art vet Thomas Baxa was tapped to illustrate the card.

### 1\. Art Description

These were the instructions given to the artist:

"Location: Riptide project  
 Action: Show a very powerful sliver wrapped around a victim it has ensnared. Its prey is going limp as the sliver sucks the energy from the head of its victim through its specalized talon.  
 Notes: See the style guide for the sliver concept, but feel free to give it some slight variations, especially in the talon which is equipped to leech mind energy."

### 2\. Sketches

Here is Thomas's original pencil sketch. You can see how he nailed the twist on the normal Sliver talon, which you can see on [Talon Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20030805000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Talon+Sliver) or [Root Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20030805000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Root+Sliver).
