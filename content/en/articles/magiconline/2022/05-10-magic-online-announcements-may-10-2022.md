## *Streets of New Capenna* on *Magic Online*

Shimmy on over to [the release article](/en/articles/magiconline/2022/04-26-streets-of-new-capenna-magic-online-edition) to learn all about *Streets of New Capenna*'s *Magic Online* release. *   **Redemption Reprint Guarantee Date**: September 7, 2022
*   **Redemption Cutoff Date**: October 12, 2022

## Alternative Play for *Streets of New Capenna* Season

Here's the schedule for the alternative play experiences you can expect during the *Streets of New Capenna* season*.* All events run for a week, except where noted:

*   May 11: ***Modern Horizons 2*** – We're bringing back *Modern Horizons 2* Draft for two weeks! This will be non-Phantom, so it's your chance to nab a ritzy [Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ragavan%2C+Nimble+Pilferer), [Urza's Saga](https://web.archive.org/web/20220510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Urza%27s+Saga), or even a [Solitude](https://web.archive.org/web/20220510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Solitude)!
*   May 25: ***Proliferate Cube*** – Our very own Carmen Handy is back with another energizing update to *Proliferate Cube*! You can count on this being one infecting experience!
*   June 1: ***Vintage Cube*** – We've bookended this calendar with Moxen! *Vintage Cube* is back, powered up with *Streets of New Capenna*, and will run for four awesome weeks of cubing action.

## *Modern Horizons 2* Draft May 11–25

*Modern Horizons 2* Draft returns for a two-week run from May 11–25. This event is non-Phantom, so cards you open will be added to your collection! Entry options include 25 Event Tickets, 250 Play Points, or 4 Event Tickets plus 3 *Modern Horizons 2* boosters.

## Caleb Gannon's *Powered Synergy Cube* Ends Tomorrow

Popular *Magic* Content Creator Caleb Gannon has brought his very own cube to *Magic Online*! *Powered Synergy Cube* combines The Power 9 with a plethora of synergistic and flexible combo pieces that come together to create some amazing experiences! Caleb's *Powered Synergy Cube* runs from May 4–11 on *Magic Online*, and you can read all about it and check out the full card list in [Caleb's announcement article](/en/articles/magiconline/2022/05-03-spotlight-cube-series-caleb-gannons-powered-synergy-cube).

## Showcase Challenge Weekend

Players looking to prove their mettle against the best players in *Magic Online* will want to use their QPs to enter *Magic Online* Champions Showcase (MOCS) events such as the returning Showcase Challenges this weekend. These MOCS events are a set of linked events designed to highlight the best players on *Magic Online* each season, culminating in the Champions Showcase and its prize pool of $70,000 and a future Championship invitation.

On Saturday, May 14, we have the Pauper Showcase Challenge at 8 a.m. PT (15:00 UTC), and on Sunday, May 15, we finish things off with the Legacy Showcase Challenge at 8 a.m. PT (15:00). Each of these events requires 40 QPs to enter. The Top 8 finishers from each event will earn a qualification token to enter the appropriate Showcase Qualifier in August.

Learn more about Showcase Challenge events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mocs-step-1-showcase-challenges-last-chance-events).

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format Challenges are the first step toward qualifying for Premier Play through your favorite *Magic* formats and provide excellent prizes for those looking to test their mettle in their favorite formats. [Learn more about Premier Play qualification](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021) to get started today.

*Magic Online*'s Format Challenges run once on Saturdays and once on Sundays for each Constructed format! On weekends featuring Showcase Challenge events, the Format Challenge that starts at either 6 a.m., 8 a.m., or 10 a.m. Pacific time is pre-empted by the Showcase Challenge—Format Challenges scheduled at other times remain in place.

*NOTES:* 

*   *Vintage and Standard Challenges are never pre-empted.*
*   *Saturday Legacy Challenges require 32 players and give prizes to Top 32.*
*   *Sunday Legacy Challenges require 64 players and give prizes to Top 64.*
