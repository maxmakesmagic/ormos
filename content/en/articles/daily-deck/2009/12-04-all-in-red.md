A popular strategy during last Extended season, All-In Red fell out of favor during Pro Tour-Austin. It made a solid resurgence during the Extended day of competition at Worlds in Rome, however. Italy's own Mario Pascoli used the deck for a respectable finish, ritualing out powerful first-turn plays like Magus of the Moon or even Deus of Calamity. For more information on the 2009 World Championships, [visit the official event coverage](/en/events/2009/worlds09/all-roads-lead-to-victory-for-coimbra-china).
