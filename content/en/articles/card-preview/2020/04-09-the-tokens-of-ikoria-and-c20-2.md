A set filled with monsters deserves a menagerie of too-cool tokens with eye-popping art. Check out this gigantic collection of a combined 31 full-art tokens and one emblem that you can create with cards from *Ikoria: Lair of Behemoths* and *Commander (2020 Edition)* just below.

If you haven't yet seen the cards that make these tokens, step cautiously through these creature-packed [Card Image Galleries for IKO](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2020/04-10-ikoria-lair-of-behemoths) and [C20](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2020/04-07-commander-2020-edition)—just watch out for giant footprints!
