**Announcement Date:** September 20, 2011  
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2011  
**Magic Online Effective Date:** October 12, 2011

**Modern**  
[Blazing Shoal](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blazing+Shoal) is banned.  
[Cloudpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudpost) is banned.  
[Green Sun's Zenith](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Green+Sun%27s+Zenith) is banned.  
[Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder) is banned.  
[Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain) is banned.  
[Rite of Flame](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rite+of+Flame) is banned.

**Extended**  
[Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) is banned.  
[Mental Misstep](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mental+Misstep) is banned.  
[Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder) is banned.  
[Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain) is banned.  
[Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic) is banned.

**Legacy**  
[Mental Misstep](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mental+Misstep) is banned.

**Vintage**  
[Fact or Fiction](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fact+or+Fiction) is no longer restricted.

***Scars of Mirrodin* Block Constructed**, **Standard**  
 No changes

Changes to **Magic Online**–only formats are now announced monthly in the [Magic Online Community Group blog](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-gatecrash-qualifier-season-top-8-standard-decklists-43).

For the complete list of all banned and restricted cards by format, click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424103806/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=judge/resources/banned).

For an explanation of the B&R changes from R&D member Erik Lauer, click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/feature/161b).
