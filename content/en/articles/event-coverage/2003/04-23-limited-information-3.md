Two similarly powered first-pick worthy cards, Pacifism and Snarling Undorak, are clearly the top two choices. However, with the poll not demonstrating a winner by a significant margin, what is the correct decision? What factors should one be considering? As usual, the pros had some strong opinions:

**Brian Kibler**: I'd take Pacifism out of this pack. While green is the strongest color in *Legions* and thus makes Undorak more attractive, white is so under-drafted right now that you can end up with some truly ridiculous decks if you draft it aggressively enough. For reference, just take a look at my [Grand Prix-Boston Top 8 monstrosity](/en/events/2003/gpbos03/top-8-decks).

**William Jensen**: The only two possible considerations are Undorak and Pacifism. I would take the Undorak because it wins more games than Pacifism and green is the deeper color. Although I like white, it is shallow in *Onslaught* and green is deep right on through. 

**Anton Jonsson**: The pack has two cards: Pacifism and Undorak. Basically, the pick comes down to which one you think is better. My choice is Undorak unless I know that white won't be popular, which is difficult knowledge to have. There aren't really any green cards that detract from drafting Undorak either.

**Ben Stark**: I think Snarling Undorak and Pacifism are very close and you could choose one over the other freely. The rest of this pack from green and white is Serpentine Basilisk, Vitality Charm and Daunting Defender. Since Basilisk and Charm are both better then Defender, you should take Pacifism.

**Mike Turian**: I would take the Undorak. Green, while a little over-drafted, is still better than white. While they both are their color's premier commons, the Undorak does what it does better than the Pacifism. While Pacifism is solid removal, if you end up pairing white with either black or red then you will certainly have better removal. Undorak is one of the best common creatures in the set. It hits hard and goes well with the tribal theme of *Onslaught*.

**Gary Wise**: From this week's pack, I've got to go with the Undorak. It fills the crucial-in-beasts four-slot, is great early and late and is in my draft color of choice for the moment and while I can see those with pre-draft inclinations towards white saying Pacifism, with green's strength in *Legions*, that's where I most want to be. 

**Scott Wills**: I'm going with Undorak. On top of the fact it's pretty much the lone green card (the next three picks are probably white-red), it's also a great card in its own right. It's capable of winning games, it fits in your curve really well as you always have three drops but often don't have good drops until turn five in green and finally you have the obvious combo with Krosan Vorine, which you'll hopefully pick up in *Legions*.

It's clear that the two cards are similar in power and that there are three main considerations: the power level and depth of their respective colors, the contents of the pack and the needs of the archetype to which the card belongs. Everyone seems to agree that green is deep in both *Onslaught* and *Legions* and that white is comparatively shallow in *Onslaught*, though better in *Legions*. The advantage of taking a white card where color depth is concerned is that people will be steering away from the color for that very reason. Evaluate the risk accordingly. Ben and Scott, the two people who brought up the specific contents of the pack, both advocate cooperation but disagree on what their neighbors downstream will take out of the pack. Ben is the only one who considers the cards close enough in power for this to be the determining factor, but clearly one should be keeping it in mind. Mike, Gary, and Scott all brought up good points regarding archetype. Simply put, they feel Snaring Undorak is more vital to a green deck than Pacifism is to a white deck. While I initially thought Pacifism was the better choice because of the value of removal, reflection on these arguments and its inability to handle utility creatures makes the archetype argument more clear to me. Objectively, the pros have spoken: Snarling Undorak.  
 To those who selected Erratic Explosion, you cheated yourself out of a lot of card quality with that pick. Yes, removal is good, but the Explosion is between weak and unplayable in the wrong deck (namely one with one drops, Wave of Indifference, etc.) and regardless will never be on the same level as either Pacifism or Undorak. Even if you want to force red for some reason, you can't do it here.
