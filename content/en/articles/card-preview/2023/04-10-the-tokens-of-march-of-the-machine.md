Fight the Phyrexian invasion and save the Multiverse with 62 full-art tokens, four emblems, three double-faced Incubator // Phyrexian tokens, and two status marker cards from across [*March of the Machine*](https://web.archive.org/web/20230410000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/march-of-the-machine) products.

You can find 18 tokens, three double-faced Incubator // Phyrexian tokens, three emblems, and two different Treasure tokens for the Phyrexians and the defenders in Draft and Set Boosters. In Collector Boosters, you'll find all these as double-faced tokens in traditional foil.

The [five Commander decks from *March of the Machine*](/en/articles/announcements/2023/04-04-march-of-the-machine-commander-decklists) feature the remaining 42 tokens, two status marker cards (The Monarch and City's Blessing), and one emblem. These appear on the 10 double-faced tokens in each deck, with different tokens and combinations in each depending on the deck's theme, and often with Draft Booster tokens on the back or front face (or both faces in the case of the Incubator // Phyrexian tokens).

Prepare for battle and bolster your collection! Preorder *March of the Machine* booster displays, Bundles, Commander decks, and more from your [local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20230410000000/https://locator.wizards.com/) and online retailers like [Amazon](https://gtly.to/XkWuLY3ab).

Visit the [*March of the Machine* Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20230410000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/card-image-gallery/march-of-the-machine) to see the cards in the set, including the ones that create these tokens. You can also stop by the [*March of the Machine* Variant Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20230410000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/card-image-gallery/march-of-the-machine-variants) for a look at the Booster Fun and promo versions of *March of the Machine* cards, then check out [*March of the Machine* Commander Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20230410000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/card-image-gallery/march-of-the-machine-commander) and [Planechase Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20230410000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/card-image-gallery/march-of-the-machine-planechase) to see the cards in the five Commander decks!
