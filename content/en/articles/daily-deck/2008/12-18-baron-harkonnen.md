For our fourth installment of Rerun Week, Daily Decks brings you a rerun inside a rerun! Early in **Magic**'s history, famed deckbuilder Adrian Sullivan created a powerful control build that combined card draw, counterspells, and removal to answer every single threat his opponent could play. Gaea's Blessing then allowed him to reshuffle the most important cards back into his deck to be replayed later. The above list is a modern interpretation of the deck, a rerun given to Gaudenis Vidugiris for use at the 2007 Pro Tour-Yokohama. The new build works just like the classic, and is even rerunning the beloved Gaea's Blessing.
