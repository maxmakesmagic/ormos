A picture is worth a thousand words. So this article is around five thousand words long. \*wink\* Five players show off their impressive draft decks as we enter the last round of competition before the Top 8 is born.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/Banners-Han-Bing.jpg)

 *Han Bing* 

  

**The Mardu Horde** is fighting alongside Han Bing! He is playing his win-and-in match now, and he's relying on his [Dragon-Style Twins](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragon-Style+Twins) and [Utter End](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Utter+End) to bring him there. Sneaky morphs such as [Ruthless Ripper](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ruthless+Ripper) and [Horde Ambusher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Horde+Ambusher) will aid him in his quest. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/Banners-Joe-Soh.jpg)

 *Joe Soh* 

  

**The Temur Frontier** is backing up Joe Soh! Playing his win-and-in match against Tamada Ryoichi, he is glad to have [Hooded Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hooded+Hydra), [Kheru Spellsnatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kheru+Spellsnatcher) and [Meandering Towershell](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meandering+Towershell) on his side. [Bear's Companion](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bear%27s+Companion), [Snowhorn Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snowhorn+Rider) and [Trap Essence](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trap+Essence) are also Temur's signature cards. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/Banners-Yuuki-Ichikawa.jpg)

 *Yuuki Ichikawa* 

  

**The Abzan Houses** would be proud of Yuuki Ichikawa. His Green White Black deck contains signature cards such as [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) and [Duneblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duneblast), complemented by a pair of [See the Unwritten](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=See+the+Unwritten)! 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/Banners-Huang-Hao-Shan.jpg)

 *Huang Hao Shan* 

  

**The Jeskai Way** is the best way! Huang Hao Shan shows off his [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider), [Kheru Spellsnatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kheru+Spellsnatcher) and [Thousand Winds](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thousand+Winds), complemented by a pair of [Efreet Weaponmaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Efreet+Weaponmaster)s and the backbreaking [Jeskai Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Charm). 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/Banners-Ken-Yukuhiro.jpg)

 *Ken Yukuhiro* 

  

**The Sultai Brood** supports Ken Yukuhiro. Aside from it's leader [Sidisi, Brood Tyrant](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sidisi%2C+Brood+Tyrant), he also has [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) and a pair of [Rakshasa Vizier](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Vizier) to go with the rest of his Delve cards.
