Rich Hagon and Brian David-Marshall take a look back at the big stories from [Pro Tour *Theros*](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/dezani-proclaims-allez-les-bleus-pro-tour) in Dublin, examining the break-out Standard and Booster Draft strategies and paying tribute to the newest class to be inducted into the Pro Tour Hall of Fame. You'll definitely want to check it out before going to your next Friday Night **Magic**!
