**Date**: July 17 - 19  
**Event Location**: Hotel Vincci Granada  
**Address:** Avda. Constitución 18  
 18012 Granada

The **Magic: the Gathering** Spain National Championship is an invitation-only event, open to citizens or foreign players resident in Spain from Jan 1st 2009 who have won an invitation to Nationals through DCI Ratings or from qualifying events such as Regionals or National Qualfiers.

**Registration Requirements** 

Players must have a signed waiver and consent form to compete in Spain Nationals. These forms will be available will be available onsite at the event or can be obtained by writing to dci@devir.es

Minors (those under 18 years of age) must have a parent or guardian's signature on these forms. Minors will not be able to register or play unless we receive a waiver signed by a parent or guardian. *No exceptions!*

To register, all participants must present one of the following forms of identification:  

*   A driver's license, or
*   A passport, or
*   DNI

**Registration times and locations**  
 Registration for Spain Nationals will open at the event venue from 16- 22pm Friday July 17 and between 8:30 am-9.00 am Saturday July 18

**Format**  
 

*   Standard and Booster Draft

    *   1 *Shards of Alara*, 1 *Conflux*, and 1 *Alara Reborn* booster pack will be used for drafting
*   Deck lists are required
*   Check [www.thedci.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20090709025029/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.thedci.com&origin=) for Standard and Booster Draft format rules

**Tournament Length**  
 

*   Day 1 will consist of 3 rounds of Standard, 3 rounds of Booster Draft and second draft with 3 round of Swiss play.
*   Day 2 will consist of 3 rounds of Booster Draft, and the Top 8 finals.
*   12 Swiss Rounds total, 60 minutes per round
*   Top 8 will be Standard
*   Top 8 single-elimination finals have no time limit; however, players are expected to play at a reasonable pace and finish their matches within 90 minutes.

**Advancement**  
 

*   All players may participate in all Swiss rounds
*   After round 12 the top 8 players advance to the top 8 single-elimination finals

**Top 8**  
 

*   Standard format
*   Top 8 matches will be best 3 of 5
*   Includes playoffs for 3rd and 4th

**Invitations**  
 

*   The Top 4 players receive invitations to the 2009 World Championships in Rome Italy. The top 3 players will form the Spain National Team and compete in the teams section the 4th placed player will be the team alternate.

**Notes**  
 

*   40K, REL Competitive
*   Players are required to use the same Standard deck for day 1, day 2, and Top 8.
*   All Universal Tournament Rules and Magic: the Gathering Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document
