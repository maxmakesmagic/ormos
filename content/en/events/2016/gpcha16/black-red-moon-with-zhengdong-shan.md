With nine rounds of Modern in the books, only twelve players remain undefeated here at Grand Prix Charlotte. One of these players, Zhengdong Shan, managed to achieve his flawless Day 1 record with an exciting new strategy that's clearly a force to be reckoned with.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpcha16/gpcha16_br-moon_shan-1.jpg)  
*Zhengdong Shan*

It makes sense to attack opponents' mana bases In a format where three and four color decks run rampant. [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) has always been the card of choice for decks with this plan of attack. Previously, [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) strategies have augmented their strategy with Blue cards, but Shan decided to break the mold and use powerful Black removal and disruption to control the board once he locks his opponent out of casting spells.

I sat down with the creator and pilot of this innovative strategy to talk about Black Red Moon and how it attacks what people are playing right now in Modern.

“I play a lot of Modern on Magic Online,” said Shan, “I noticed that most of my opponents were playing three, four, five colors, or trying to assemble tron. I thought that [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) would be in a good spot and I thought that the black cards were better at cleaning up than Blue cards. I played the deck a lot on Magic Online and won a lot of matches.”

Playing Black with [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) also means that Shan gets to play [Liliana of the Veil](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+of+the+Veil), one of the best cards in Modern. Liliana is especially good in this type of deck because Shan can use the +1 ability to discard extra copies of [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) or discard spells that are no longer relevant against an opponent with no lands in play.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpcha16/gpcha16_br-moon_shan-2.jpg)  
*Zhengdong Shan's innovative Black Red deck is making a big splash this weekend!*

As far as the matchups go, Shan was confident in his deck's ability to beat opponent's with ambitious mana bases.

“Black Red is good against creatures and [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) wins a lot of games against Jeskai Harbinger, Jund, and Abzan.”

Shan's deck has some exciting interactions that have caught a lot of his opponents by surprise, “[Goblin Dark-Dwellers](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Dark-Dwellers) with [Boom // Bust](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boom+%2F%2F+Bust) has been very good for me. Boom is very good at blowing up leftover basic lands when I already have [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) in play. Once I have [Boom // Bust](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boom+%2F%2F+Bust) in my graveyard I can use [Goblin Dark-Dwellers](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Dark-Dwellers) to get ahead on the board while also destroying all lands. Then my opponent needs to topdeck everything and start from the beginning again.”

I asked if there was anything Shan would change about the deck going forward.

“I had one [Slaughter Games](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slaughter+Games) in my sideboard before and the [Dark Petition](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Petition) in my main deck is very good with sideboard cards that can win the game by themselves. [Scapeshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scapeshift) can be more difficult than I expected and I think one copy of [Slaughter Games](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slaughter+Games) would help a lot.”

It's clear than Shan is on to something. Will Black Red Moon continue to dominate a field of Modern decks with ambitious mana? Stay tuned to continuing coverage of Grand Prix Charlotte to find out!
