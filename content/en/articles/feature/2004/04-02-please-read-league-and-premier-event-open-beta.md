### Updated: April 5, 2004

* * *

We're going to begin open beta testing of ***Magic** Online* with leagues and premier events next Tuesday around noon (1200 hours) Pacific Daylight Time. The purpose of the open beta is to load test the system with fixes to the league and premier events code. Which means that we need to make sure we get plenty of players to test.

So, here's how things are going to work:  
  

*   At 8:00 AM (0800 hours) Pacific Daylight Time on Tuesday, April 6, ***Magic** Online* will go into "no-pay" mode. (Queues will stop spawning games.)  
  

*   At 10:30 AM (1030 hours) PDT the ***Magic** Online* Store will be shut down.  
  

*   Around 11:00 AM (1100 hours) PDT the live server will go down.  
  

*   The system will be down for about an hour. After 12 Noon (1200 hours) PDT, whenever you log on to ***Magic** Online* you'll actually be logging on to the beta server--not the live server.  
  

*   For the following 48 hours or so (midday on Thursday) any time you log on to ***Magic** Online,* you'll be on the beta server.  
  

*   On the beta server, your collection won't have any cards in it initially. (Your real collection will be waiting for you back on the live server.)  
  

*   Only the **Leagues** and **Premier Events** rooms will have anything running in them. You won't have to provide any tickets or product to enter in leagues or tournaments.  
  

*   Around 11:00 AM (1100 hours) PDT on Thursday, April 8, the beta server will go down.  
  

*   The system will be down for about an hour. After 12 Noon (1200 hours) PDT, you'll be back on the live server.  
  

*   **You'll have to close and restart ***Magic** Online* to update the client on Tuesday.** If you don't, you won't connect to any server until the live server comes back up on Thursday. (The same goes for getting back on the live server Thursday.)

Unlike the last beta, the server should be fairly stable. However, part of what we need to test is that tournaments and leagues restore correctly when the servers do go down. ***While you're on the beta server, the game will come down occasionally so the team can make sure this is working.*** If things are going well, the tournaments and leagues should automatically restore. This is the big issue we're testing, so it may happen fairly frequently.

All leagues on the beta server will be set to run for two weeks. The main task is to make sure the leagues restore correctly when the server bounces. However, we also would like to see some leagues finish. ***So, at some point during the 48 hours of beta, the dev team will bounce the server and advance the clock on the game ahead by a week.*** We've never done this before, so we're not sure if the leagues will actually survive or not.

Since we need players to stay in events for several rounds--and especially until we can cut to a true top 8--we're doing a couple things. First, events and leagues won't be queued up to spawn as soon as one fills up. Instead, the dev team will manually create events and leagues during the beta. They'll try to make sure demand is met, but their first priority is making sure players are where the team needs them to test the code.

Second, there's some real incentive to playing in these events and leagues. If you're in a league or tournament that finishes and your final standing is one that would normally give out prizes, you'll get those prizes on the live game. (For leagues, that's a top 128 finish and for premier events that's usually a top 8 finish.) After we bring the live servers back up, we'll grant those prizes to players who won them during beta. Those prizes will match whatever the prize payout was listed as on the beta server. *This won't happen immediately after we go back to the live server.***Note:** We won't be running any *Invasion-Planeshift-Apocalypse* or *Seventh Edition* events.

As always with the beta server, we need players to help us test, but not players who engage in disruptive behavior. Anyone from Wizards of the Coast can kick players from the game and, once booted, those players will probably not return until we go back to the live server. This won't affect your account on the live server in any way, so warnings will not necessarily be issued. Please be respectful of the other people on the system.

### Dude, Where's My Collection?

***During the open beta--Tuesday, April 6 to Thursday, April 8--you won't have access to your normal collection.*** The only cards you'll have are any you open when you participate in any Limited-format event.

Your real collection will be sitting on the live server during beta. It will be waiting for you when you log back in on Thursday after we switch back to the live server. We'll post announcements when the switches from live to beta and from beta to live happen to help avoid confusion.

The store will also be down during beta. As soon as the live server comes back up, so will the store.

### Other Notes

There are a lot of things waiting on the results of open beta that we'd like to be able to move forward with, so we want to get this started as soon as possible.

Compensation for crashed queues is one of those items. The dev team hasn't nailed down exactly what happened with that script, so we'd rather get the open beta started rather than waiting until we can get the script fixed. If you're still waiting for draft compensation, I'm sorry. We'll get your refund to you as soon as we can after we have the results of the open beta.

Viridian Zealot avatars also haven't been given out yet. One of the reasons is that players can't use them, anyway, due to the avatar-changing bug. Since we have a fix for that bug and it's waiting until the open beta test is complete, we'll be granting those after open beta finishes.

We'll also be deciding what to do about general compensation and *Darksteel* release events after open beta finishes.

There's a lot riding on this test and we're sorry to put the ***Magic** Online* community through this. We hope the free play and prizes somewhat makes up for not having access to your collection for a few days and that the possibility of restoring both leagues and premier events is worth it as well.

Thank you for your continued support.
