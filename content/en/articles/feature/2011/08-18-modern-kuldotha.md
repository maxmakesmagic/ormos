Last week it was [announced](/en/articles/latestdevelopments/2011/08-12-welcome-to-the-modern-world) that the upcoming [Pro Tour Philadelphia](/en/events/2011/philadelphia11/magic-weekend-philadelphia-event-information) will feature the Modern format. So what might a Modern deck look like? Here's one from Wizards of the Coast's own Lee Sharpe, based around [Kuldotha Rebirth](https://web.archive.org/web/20110818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuldotha+Rebirth). Lee's goal is to plunk as many creatures onto the table as quickly as possible, then use [Haze of Rage](https://web.archive.org/web/20110818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Haze+of+Rage) or [Goblin Bushwhacker](https://web.archive.org/web/20110818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Bushwhacker) to hit the opponent for lethal! The deck list comes from the 2011 **Magic Online** Community Cup, where Lee was a competitor on behalf of the Wizards team.
