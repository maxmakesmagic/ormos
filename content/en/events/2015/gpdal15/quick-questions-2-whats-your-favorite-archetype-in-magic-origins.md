# What's your favorite archetype in *Magic Origins*?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpdal15/gpdfw2015_mclaren.jpg)  
**Shaun McLaren:** The black-red sacrifice deck, with [Act of Treason](https://web.archive.org/web/20150726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Act+of+Treason) and [Enthralling Victor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enthralling+Victor). You can get the [Act of Treason](https://web.archive.org/web/20150726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Act+of+Treason)s late. There's a lot of hidden sacrifice outlets. Obviously there's [Nantuko Husk](https://web.archive.org/web/20150726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Husk), but there's also [Blazing Hellhound](https://web.archive.org/web/20150726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blazing+Hellhound) and [Fiery Conclusion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Conclusion). With [Fleshbag Marauder](https://web.archive.org/web/20150726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleshbag+Marauder)s you can steal their guy and sacrifice it and make them sacrifice another. If you can get those, it's a really good archetype.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpdal15/gpdfw_15_bkibler.jpg)  
**Brian Kibler:** I'm still figuring it out. I generally like aggressive decks in this format, because Renown really punishes people for falling behind, and I'd rather be the one doing that rather than having it done to them.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpdal15/gpdfw_15_Sigrist.jpg)  
**Mike Sigrist:** I like a few. I've had a lot of success with white-red and white-blue, and blue-black is decent because it's underdrafted a lot.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpdal15/gpdfw_15_webb.jpg)  
**Andrew Webb:** Anything with white. I really like it because the common creatures are very good overall.
