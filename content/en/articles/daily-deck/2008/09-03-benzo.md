In honor of the Hall of Fame ballots being tabulated this week, Daily Decks is examining current Hall of Famer's most famous creations. Perhaps Rob Dougherty's most memorable accomplishment is the reanimator list he used to finish second at [Pro Tour–Houston in 2002](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603171824/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=). It's not just the fact that Rob was able to put a 7/7 Verdant Force, Phantom Nishoba, or Symbiotic Wurm into play as early as Turn 2 that made his performance special. It was the fact that he was joined in the Top 3 by his Your Move Game teammates Justin Gary and fellow hall of famer Darwin Kastle, the most dominating team performance the Pro Tour has ever seen!
