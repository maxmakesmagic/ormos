### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare card.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ff/94/ff94ae8a4a59a100213e93f52b1796ac96e204d080f421a590b407ba9b38edca.png)

### TWO-PLAYER STARTER GAME

Want to play with a friend right now? Just choose which of the two 30-card decks you’ll each play—and begin! It’s that easy. Also includes a rulebook, play guide and *Fifth Edition* booster pack.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ff/94/ff94ae8a4a59a100213e93f52b1796ac96e204d080f421a590b407ba9b38edca.png)

### STARTER DECKS

Battle like the best. Includes 60 tournament-ready cards and a rulebook.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ac/cf/accfeb20c7a751b8ce06c5ef62559a9d55d63c8d3c9a3f25523a1290456588ff.png)*Fifth Edition* contained a record-setting 449 cards. It added 188 cards to the basic set, removed 128, and featured new artwork and flavor text on many of the cards.

**Set Name:** *Fifth Edition*  
**Number of Cards:** 449

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, Chinese Traditional

**Design:** Richard Garfield, with contributions from Charlie Cateeno, Skaff Elias, Don Felice, Tom Fontaine, Jim Lin, Joel Mick, Chris Page, Dave Pettey, Barry "Bit" Reich, Bill Rose, and Elliott Segal

**Development:** Skaff Elias, Mike Elliott, William Jockusch, and Bill Rose
