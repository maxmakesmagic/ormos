If you're just getting here, you should know that we've been showing off some of the *Zendikar* basic lands this week. We showed [Plains](/en/articles/arcana/2009/08-10-zendikar-plains) on Monday, [Islands](/en/articles/arcana/2009/08-11-zendikar-islands-2) on Tuesday, [Swamps](/en/articles/arcana/2009/08-12-zendikar-swamps) on Wednesday, and [Mountains](/en/articles/arcana/2009/08-13-zendikar-mountains-2) on Thursday. Today, of course, is Forests.

But it's *also* time for the Wallpaper of the Week! So be sure and scroll down past the forests to get to your **five *Zendikar* Basic Land wallpapers**.
