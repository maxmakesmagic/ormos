The *Unhinged* card **[Johnny, Combo Player](https://web.archive.org/web/20050516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Johnny%2C+Combo+Player)** depicts the innovating mage flinging a whirlwind of spells. Each of the **Magic** cards in the art is a spell with hefty "[Johnny](https://web.archive.org/web/20050516000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/mr11)-style" combo potential.

Ready to test your squinting powers? Identify all the cards in the art of [Johnny, Combo Player](https://web.archive.org/web/20050516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Johnny%2C+Combo+Player).
