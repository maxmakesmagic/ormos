The Standard format is under way with the brand new *Scars of Mirrodin*! Dan Jordan won the StarCityGames.com Standard Open in Charlotte, North Carolina playing Blue-Red-Green Control. The deck is the latest iteration of Turbo Land from the previous Standard format. Using mana acceleration and card draw Dan sets up a powerful end game that uses Jace, the Mind Sculptor, Volition Reins, and Frost Titan to seal up the victory.
