If you're a **Magic** player in Europe, you don't want to miss any of the five Grand Prix scheduled in 2007. With multiple formats represented, you're certain to find the perfect event for you. Along with the two-day main event, the schedule will be filled with wall-to-wall side events for all players. 

### [Amsterdam](/en/events/2007/gpams07/amateurs-annihilate-amsterdam): March 10-11

![](https://i.ormos.uk/20/2f/202fc65ee4d8c0239c116717f341cde002d3ebdb5be1a776a6a2927984c5ea1d.jpg)Michael Havlik and Richard Hornansky are the 2006 Grand-Prix Amsterdam Champions!  
  
  
  

### [Stockholm](/en/events/2007/gpsto07/enter-the-russians): May 5-6

![](https://i.ormos.uk/04/e4/04e4d6f8233edf4d8323e5ff168649c2ab6a761e018075ab6a2d91edda6f9205.jpg)Congratulations to Nicolay Potovin, Grand Prix-Stockholm champion!  
  
  
  
  

### [Strasbourg](/en/events/2007/gpstra07/saitou-seizes-strasbourg): May 19-20

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ca/38/ca3871141930e7db12253b941a977992508b794ac96337f07ec56cb95aac085a.jpg)Saitou wins Grand Prix Strasbourg !  
  
  
  
  

### [M-Fest](https://web.archive.org/web/20121111093925/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/m-fest)

 ***Do You want to go to the largest Magic: The Gathering Festival in the UK?***  
Sure, It's *not* a Grand Prix but there will be plenty of events going on from 26th to 29th of July with something for everyone. Why not come and join us for 4 days of serious **Magic** fun! Also with regular feeder tournaments happening across the UK from April to July offering you the chance to earn fabulous prizes just for turning up at M-fest, can you afford to miss out?

### [Firenze](/en/events/2007/gpfir07/kaneko-dominates-in-florence): September 8-9

![](https://i.ormos.uk/20/d4/20d4c4b65e91cfa22ff56186cc46acff83606c60320bf3d8d20d9751ce487f42.jpg)Kaneko Dominates in Florence!  
  
  
  
  

### [Krakow](/en/events/2007/gpkra07/cheon-conquers-in-krakow): November 3-4

![](https://i.ormos.uk/27/4d/274da2b91a139c0301a500163ffdc979421c14238b450610cac5c49a707a37ae.jpg)Cheon Conquers in Krakow
