### List Of Changes

* * *

*   **Bug Fix (system)**--A memory leak bug was located and repaired after the February update. This bug was responsible for several server crashes during the past week. The issue should be resolved in this update.  
  

*   **Bug Fix (game)**--The Team Sealed Deck format introduced in the February update distributed cards among players inappropriately when the games concluded. This should now work as intended. All cards are provided by the team captain before the event, and remain owned by the captain after the event is completed.  
  

*   **Bug Fix (game)**--Several card rules issues with the forthcoming *LegionsTM* expansion have been located and fixed since the February update.  
  

*   **Art Changes**--The *Legions* booster art will be included for download by the client.  
  

*   **Prize Changes**--This update includes a new prize payout structure that enables tournament coordinators to run draft queues that pay 4/3/2/2 instead of the standard 8/4. This change also incorporates the prize payout for the planned large tournament.
