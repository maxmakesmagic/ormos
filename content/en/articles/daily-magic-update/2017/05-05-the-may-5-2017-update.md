The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on May 5, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by a weekend full of Grand Prix. Three of them, to be exact.

# What to Watch This Weekend

1.  [Grand Prix Richmond](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-richmond-2017) | [twitch.tv/magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic) | 10 a.m. ET/7 a.m. PT/2 p.m. UTC

It's here! We finally have the first high-level tournaments featuring *Amonkhet* Limited this weekend. There are three Grand Prix, but it's Richmond that will be streamed on [twitch.tv/magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic). I'm sure I'm supposed to make some kind of clever reference to Trials here, but I'm just way too excited to finally see these cards in action.

Also, I couldn't come up with anything clever to reference the Trials.

Like, spoiler alert, unassuming [River Boa](https://web.archive.org/web/20170505000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=River+Boa) being No. 2 on the list or, still a spoiler alert, [Ophidian](https://web.archive.org/web/20170505000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ophidian) clocking in at No. 9. There are more recent cards on this list (in fact, they make up the bulk of the list), but if I keep just listing off fun ones, that sort of defeats the point of watching the video. So do that instead of reading my words.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Antoniocfa's Four Color No-Cat](/nob/events/2017/competitive-standard-constructed-league-2017-05-04/competitive-standard-constructed-league)

Want to know the reason why the Copy Cat deck was so strong? It was because the combo was almost an incidental way to win. Often the deck could grind through everything with planeswalkers, creatures, and some Oaths.

Well, Antoniocfa took the deck's Plan B (which was more of a plan A2, if we're honest) and ran with it to a 5-0 finish in a Competitive *Magic Online* Standard League.

Seriously, look at the deck below and find the differences. They basically swapped out 4 [Felidar Guardian](https://web.archive.org/web/20170505000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Felidar+Guardian) for a [Cast Out](https://web.archive.org/web/20170505000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cast+Out), 2 [Blossoming Defense](https://web.archive.org/web/20170505000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blossoming+Defense), and an extra [Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170505000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer). Everything else might as well have been in the deck prior.

The deck is, very nearly, a four-color Superfriends deck, packing nine copies of four planeswalkers. Worth noting? Despite the combo going away, [Saheeli Rai](https://web.archive.org/web/20170505000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Saheeli+Rai) is actually the only planeswalker Antoniocfa played four copies of. The scry ability is very strong with [Nissa, Steward of Elements](https://web.archive.org/web/20170505000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%2C+Steward+of+Elements), and the copy ability is still *very good* with [Rogue Refiner](https://web.archive.org/web/20170505000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rogue+Refiner), [Servant of the Conduit](https://web.archive.org/web/20170505000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Servant+of+the+Conduit), and, yes, [Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170505000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer).

So if you had Copy Cat all ready to go for *Amonkhet* Standard, I wouldn't take it apart just yet. There's definitely life in those 71 cards yet.
