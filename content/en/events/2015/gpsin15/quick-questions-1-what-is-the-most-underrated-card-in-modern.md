# What is the most underrated card in Modern?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsin15/GPSing_QQ2_Duke.jpg)  
**Reid Duke (11 Grand Prix Top 8s):** [Auriok Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150627000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auriok+Champion). It's good against [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20150627000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin), Jund and Grixis and most of these decks have a huge problem killing.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsin15/GPSing_QQ2_Hayne.jpg)  
**Alexander Hayne (Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* Champion):** [Simian Spirit Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20150627000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simian+Spirit+Guide). It enables the most broken plays in Modern.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsin15/GPSing_QQ2_JChung.jpg)  
**Jason Chung (Pro Tour *Dragons of Tarkir* Top 8):** [Leyline of Punishment](https://web.archive.org/web/20150627000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leyline+of+Punishment). It's a better card at stopping Grishoalbrand than [Leyline of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20150627000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leyline+of+the+Void).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsin15/GPSing_QQ2_Bo.jpg)  
**Sun Bo (3 Grand Prix Top 8s):** Relic of [Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20150627000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Progenitus). It's excellent against all the Blue Red decks, any deck with [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20150627000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) and also keeps [Goryo's Vengeance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150627000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goryo%27s+Vengeance) in check.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsin15/GPSing_QQ2_Juza.jpg)  
**Martin Jůza (22 Grand Prix Top 8s):** Geez, I wish I knew! Maybe [Become Immense](https://web.archive.org/web/20150627000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Become+Immense) since its great in Infect and Zoo?

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsin15/GPSing_QQ2_Indrakesuma.jpg)  
**Taufik Indrakesuma (Grand Prix Manila Finalist):** [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20150627000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas). People expect it much lesser than [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150627000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon).
