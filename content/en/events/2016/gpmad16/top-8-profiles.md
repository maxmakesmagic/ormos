After 15 rounds of Standard, with a total of 1,448 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmad16/t8_gobern.jpg)  
 Name:** Luis Gobern

**Age:** 23

**Occupation:** Snake handler

**Hometown:** Lisbon, Portugal

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Team Portugal at the 2016 World Magic Cup.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Red-Green Marvel splashing [Nahiri, the Harbinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20161204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nahiri%2C+the+Harbinger), because I initially disliked the format and wasn't skilled enough to play White-Blue mirrors all day.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I think the deck is really good right now.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20161204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel) and [Emrakul, the Promised End](https://web.archive.org/web/20161204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Promised+End) of course, but the best card is [Nahiri, the Harbinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20161204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nahiri%2C+the+Harbinger).

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmad16/t8_cammilluzzi.jpg)  
 Name:** Marco Cammilluzzi

**Age:** 30

**Occupation:** Business analyst

**Hometown:** Rome, Italy

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Bomberboss and Matteo Moure's babysitter. \[Editor's note: Also, four Grand Prix Top 8s and a Pro Tour Top 8.\]

**What deck did you play and why?**

Red-Green Marvel because [Emrakul, the Promised End](https://web.archive.org/web/20161204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Promised+End) is just a nonsense card.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

If it were possible I would like to play five [Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20161204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel).

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Tireless Tracker](https://web.archive.org/web/20161204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tracker) for sure!

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmad16/t8_legendre.jpg)  
 Name:** Nicolas Legendre

**Age:** 23

**Occupation:** Not working at the moment, ex-technician

**Hometown:** Nantes, France

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

None!

**What deck did you play and why?**

I played Black-Red Aggro because I like to play aggro and to use burn spells! Also, I figured that there would be many White-Blue players at the Grand Prix, and the matchup against them is great.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I'm not sure about the number of [Galvanic Bombardment](https://web.archive.org/web/20161204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Bombardment)s. Overall fine sideboard, just change some numbers in respect to the expected matchups.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Fiery Temper](https://web.archive.org/web/20161204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Temper)!

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmad16/t8_ramirocano.jpg)  
 Name:** Adrian Ramiro Cano

**Age:** 28

**Occupation:** Student of computer science

**Hometown:** Mallorca, Spain

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

…

**What deck did you play and why?**

White-Blue Flash, because it's the best option against Marvel and Black-Green Delirium.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I would take out one [Blessed Alliance](https://web.archive.org/web/20161204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blessed+Alliance) and would ad one [Immolating Glare](https://web.archive.org/web/20161204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Immolating+Glare). It's very good against [Smuggler's Copter](https://web.archive.org/web/20161204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smuggler%27s+Copter).

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20161204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar).

**Occupation:** English teacher
