[![Rakdos the Defiler](https://i.ormos.uk/4c/3f/4c3f383fd4c699728bbdf4b87b3bd9d75ee5f7b0745676da64b354cda4e35002.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/dissension-ranks-2006-04-10)

D*issension's* **Rakdos the Defiler** (previewed in [today's feature article](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/dissension-ranks-2006-04-10) by Rei Nakazawa) causes players to sacrifice "non-Demon" permanents. While references to "[Goblin Taskmaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Goblin%2BTaskmaster)" and "[Gempalm Strider](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Gempalm%2BStrider)" and "[Takeno, Samurai General](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Takeno%252C%2BSamurai%2BGeneral)" have been relatively common in **Magic**, use of the "non-" prefix, specifying everything that *isn't* a member of a subtype, has been much rarer. 

Most of the references to "non-" are on older cards that specified "non-Wall creatures" before the defender keyword was created to mean "this creature can't attack." [Nettling Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nettling+Imp), [Siren's Call](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siren%27s+Call) and [Aggression](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aggression) gain some flavor by specifying "non-Wall" -- it wouldn't do to have your [Wall of Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Stone) die because it couldn't heed the [Norritt](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Norritt) or got in a fight with a [Basilisk](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basilisk), right? 

![Catacomb Dragon](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Catacomb+Dragon)

*Mirage's* [Catacomb Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Catacomb+Dragon) is a cool early example of a card that specifies "non-Dragons" -- anything that gets in a tussle with it that isn't a dragon or artifact creature gets so freaked out that its power is cut in half.

![Frontline Strategist](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Frontline+Strategist)

*Onslaught* Block's heavy emphasis on tribal interactions, especially combined with Black's selfish fifth of the color pie, led to several instances of the exclusionary "non-". [Cruel Revival](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cruel+Revival), [Deathmark Prelate](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathmark+Prelate), [Noxious Ghoul](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noxious+Ghoul), and [Call to the Grave](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Call+to+the+Grave) all punish non-Zombies. However, also see [Frontline Strategist](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frontline+Strategist), which "[Fog](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fog)s" all non-Soldiers for battlefield victory.

![Harbinger of Spring](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Harbinger+of+Spring)

**Rakdos the Defiler** is the only card that refers to "non-Demons", but Kamigawa Block has its share of tribally-aware demons and spirits. When [Yukora the Prisoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yukora+the+Prisoner) dies, he takes all your non-Ogres with him. [Harbinger of Spring](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Harbinger+of+Spring) has protection from non-Spirits, and [Rend Flesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rend+Flesh) rends, well, any fleshy creatures, i.e. non-Spirits.

Most recently, the introduction of the Aura subtype to replace "local enchantments" has caused Oracle rewording of the term "global". For example, [Opalescence](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Opalescence) and [Tranquil Domain](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tranquil+Domain) now refer to "non-Aura enchantments". But we suggest, when you head to the [prerelease](https://web.archive.org/web/20060410000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prereleases), that you keep track of non-Demons first...
