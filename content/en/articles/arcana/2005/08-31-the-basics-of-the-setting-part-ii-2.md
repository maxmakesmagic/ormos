Following the lead of [Monday's Arcana](https://web.archive.org/web/20050831000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/basics-setting-2005-08-29), today we explore more of *Ravnica* through its amazing basic land art. We should point out that selecting only one sample of the four pieces for each basic land was incredibly difficult -- they're all beautiful.

Without further adieu, [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20050831000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain) and [Swamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20050831000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swamp), you're up.

Still drooling? Don't worry, you can collect all your *Ravnica* basics in tournament packs at the [prerelease](https://web.archive.org/web/20050831000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/prereleases), September 24-25.
