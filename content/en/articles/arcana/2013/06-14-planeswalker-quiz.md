Since I'm still figuring out what sort of questions work best for this sort of thing, let me know what you think! I've got a nice mixture of questions for this quick quiz, let me know which you like best and worst either [on Twitter](http://www.twitter.com/trickmtg) or via the author email below!

**2\. Six of a Kind** - There are six Planeswalkers with \[0\] cost abilities. Among them, what color isn't represented?  
  
*Difficulty: Medium*

[White](#) - WRONG Nope, both Gideon Jura and Gideon, Champion of Justice have zero activation abilities. 

[Blue](#) - WRONG Sorry, not here either. Jace, Memory Adept mills a player for ten cards and of course Jace, the Mind Sculptor allows you to Brainstorm for zero. 

[Black](#) - WRONG Sarkhan the Mad reveals the top card for zero. 

[Red](#) - WRONG Sarkhan the Mad reveals the top card for zero. 

[Green](#) - WRONG Garruk Relentless has only zero activation abilities. 

[None](#) - Correct! Sarkhan the Mad covers red and black, Garruk Relentless is green, and then Gideon and Jace are white and blue respectively.
