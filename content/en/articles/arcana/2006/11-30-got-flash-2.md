So you're a time-bending chronomancer, consulting with a being from some dimension outside of time, and this being is about to let you in on one of its chronomantic secrets. In other words, you have Mystical Teachings on the stack—what kinds of cards can you tutor up?

Since *Time Spiral*, the flash keyword has shortened the Oracle text of several cards. Which ones now say “flash” on them matters a lot for Mystical Teachings, so let's take a trip through the pre-*Time Spiral* cards that were affected by the new flash keyword.
