## Ravnica Remix Drafts Tomorrow

Available tomorrow, March 13, Ravnica Remix is a digital-exclusive Phantom experience made from two lists of cards from *Guilds of Ravnica* and *Ravnica Allegiance*, curated by our very own *Magic* R&D. The two sets are mixed into two different packs. The set will be drafted in A, B, B style, meaning the second and third packs are the same as one another and different than the first pack.

The contents of the first pack include a higher density of multicolored cards. The second and third packs have more monocolored cards and mana fixing. Ravnica Remix encourages players to mix and match guilds and discover playstyles not possible in either GRN or RNA draft.

The Ravnica Remix league entry options will match our other Phantom offerings: entry will be 10 Event Tickets or 100 Play Points.

Some cards from those sets have been omitted from Ravnica Remix packs. The full list of cards found in Ravnica Remix packs is below.

## Vintage Super League Playoffs

It's time for VSL Playoffs—don't miss your chance to get a weekly dose of ridiculously powerful *Magic* every Tuesday evening. You don't have to play (or even understand) Vintage to enjoy VSL—in fact, it's a great way to learn more about one of *Magic*'s deepest formats!
