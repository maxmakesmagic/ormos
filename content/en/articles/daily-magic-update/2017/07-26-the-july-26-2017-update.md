The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on July 26, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by Standard metagame speculation and my own [Fraying Sanity](https://web.archive.org/web/20170726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fraying+Sanity).

# Today's Must-Read

1.  [God-Pharaoh's Gift](http://magic.tcgplayer.com/db/article.asp?ID=14067&writer=Seth+Manfield&articledate=7-26-2017) | TCGPlayer | Seth Manfield

This deck came out of seemingly nowhere to win a *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifier. While it does have some weakness against aggro (which has gained strong support from *Hour of Devastation*), this deck obviously has something to it based on its performance. I guess we'll see this weekend at the Pro Tour whether this is going to be a surprise contender!

# After That, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [Azorius Fraying Sanity](http://www.mtgmintcard.com/articles/writers/soh-weng-heng/azorius-fraying-sanity) | MTG Mint Card | Soh Weng Heng

With [Startled Awake](https://web.archive.org/web/20170726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Startled+Awake) and [Fraying Sanity](https://web.archive.org/web/20170726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fraying+Sanity) in the same Standard, it was only a matter of time before brewers started trying to crack that formula open. Soh attempts to make this into a [Persistent Nightmare](https://web.archive.org/web/20170726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Persistent+Nightmare) for their opponents.

1.  [Standard Mono-Red Is Back](https://www.channelfireball.com/articles/standard-mono-red-is-back/) | ChannelFireball | Corey Burkhart

Corey joins the ongoing conversation surrounding this deck with his own take on the deck.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Robert Anderson's 1st-Place Grand Prix Toronto Deck](https://web.archive.org/web/20170827045623/http://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/gptor17/top-8-decklists-2017-07-23)

Robert took two of *Hour of Devastation*'s most powerful tribal cards and showed just how powerful they can be . . . without any other cards from those tribes. Honestly, it shouldn't be a huge surprise that [Crested Sunmare](https://web.archive.org/web/20170726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crested+Sunmare) and [Pride Sovereign](https://web.archive.org/web/20170726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pride+Sovereign) are powerful in their own right. I'm sure Robert would have loved to open a [Regal Caracal](https://web.archive.org/web/20170726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Regal+Caracal) in pack three, but it's pretty obvious now that it would have been overkill. Well done!
