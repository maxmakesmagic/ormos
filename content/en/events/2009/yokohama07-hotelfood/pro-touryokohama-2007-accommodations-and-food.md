### Pacific Convention Plaza Yokohama

April 20-22, 2007

### PLAYER ACCOMMODATIONS

NOTE: The following hotels are not endorsed by or affiliated with Wizards of the Coast. The hotels on this list are taken from guidebooks and provided here for your convenience only.

The travel time from these hotels to the venue by train and/or on foot is somewhere between 20-35 minutes.

When contacting the hotels from outside of Japan, dial +81 (country code) and omit the first 0 from the phone/fax number.

**Western-Style Accommodations**  
 These are standard Western-style hotels, but expect the rooms to be very small.

### PLACES TO EAT

**At the Venue**  
 The building in which the venue is located (Pacifico Yokohama) has a convenience store (7:00-23:00) and several cafeterias (10:00/11:30-17:00/18:00).

**South of the Venue (5-10 min. walk)**  
 There are two large adjacent shopping mall compounds (Queen's Square Yokohama and The Landmark Tower Yokohama) that have numerous restaurants of various cuisines.

**Queen's Square: at! (1st/2nd/3rd 1-5F; Station Core B1-B3F)**  
 $-$$$ fast food (McDonald's, Subway, KFC), Starbucks and other cafes, various cuisines, convenience store

**Queen's Square: Queen's East (B1F & 2-4F)**  
 $-$$ various cuisines

**Landmark Tower: Landmark Plaza (1-5F)**  
 $-$$$ fast food (McDonald's, Wendy's). Starbucks and other cafes, Sizzler and other various cuisines

**Landmark Tower (tower proper) (1-5 F)**  
 $$-$$$ vegetarian and other cuisines

**Landmark Tower: Dockyard Garden (B1F-B2F)**  
 $-$$ pubs, various cuisines

**Landmark Tower: Queen's Tower A (1-2F)**  
 $-$$ Hard Rock Café and other Western cuisines

**Yokohama World Porters (1-5F)**
