Earlier this season, I finished 9th at [Grand Prix Charleston](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/bolding-thunders-to-victory-in-charleston) with Green-White Humans. 

  

![Silverblade Paladin](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Silverblade+Paladin)

In the weeks that followed, Rakdos decks with four copies of [Knight of Infamy](https://web.archive.org/web/20130111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+Infamy) became popular, and it became much more difficult to win with small white creatures. 

Today, I found a deck that plays much the same kind of game as the Green-White Humans deck, but incorporates more green creatures like [Strangleroot Geist](https://web.archive.org/web/20130111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strangleroot+Geist) and [Thragtusk](https://web.archive.org/web/20130111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thragtusk) that can be particularly problematic for Rakdos. The deck still uses [Silverblade Paladin](https://web.archive.org/web/20130111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silverblade+Paladin) and [Sublime Archangel](https://web.archive.org/web/20130111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sublime+Archangel) to turn any attacker into a huge monster, but it also supports a variety of five-mana creatures that earn the title all by themselves. 

My one criticism of the deck is that I think it doesn't have enough mana sources. I would cut [Dryad Militant](https://web.archive.org/web/20130111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dryad+Militant) for another [Avacyn's Pilgrim](https://web.archive.org/web/20130111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avacyn%27s+Pilgrim) and add at least one more land.
