Mono-black Control has been a popular archetype even before it entered the mainstream with the release of the set *Torment* from the *Odyssey* block. Dunkle\_stille brings the latest update to the deck for use in recent **Magic Online** Premier Events. The deck was good enough to make the Top 4 of the event.
