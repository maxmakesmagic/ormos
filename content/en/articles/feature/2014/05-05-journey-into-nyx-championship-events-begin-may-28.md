The **Magic Online** release of *Journey into Nyx,* the capstone set in the *Theros* block, happens Monday, May 12. That means it's time to gear up for the capstone event of the set: the *Journey into Nyx* Championships!

There will be two *Journey into Nyx* champions, one for the Limited format and one for the *Theros* Block Constructed format.

**Limited Format Events**  
 Events begin on Wednesday, May 28, with the start of the [*Journey into Nyx* Championship Limited Qualifiers](#limited), which run through Tuesday, June 3. The Limited format Champion will be crowned after the [Limited Finals](#limitedchamp) on Saturday, June 7.

***Theros* Block Constructed Format Events**  
[*Journey into Nyx* Championship *Theros* Block Constructed Qualifiers](#block) begin on Wednesday, June 4, and run through Tuesday, June 10. Then, on Saturday, June 14, the top players will compete in the [*Theros* Block Constructed Finals](#blockchamp).

**Participation Promo Card Prize**  
 All players who participate in *Theros* Block Constructed or Limited Qualifier events will receive a regular promo version of [Hall of Triumph](https://web.archive.org/web/20140505000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hall+of+Triumph). All players who participate in a *Theros* Block Constructed or Limited Championship events will receive a premium promo version of [Hall of Triumph](https://web.archive.org/web/20140505000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hall+of+Triumph)!

As we did with both [the *Theros* winners](https://web.archive.org/web/20140505000000/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=magic/online/championship/theros/winners) and [the *Born of the Gods* winners](/en/articles/feature/2014/05-01-magic-online-born-of-the-gods-championship-winners), we will celebrate our *Journey into Nyx* champions with a winners' page featuring their conquering decks, plus highlights from the matches!
