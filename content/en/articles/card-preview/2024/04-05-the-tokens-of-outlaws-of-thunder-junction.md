**Editor's Note: Language has been added to reflect that the plot helper card appears in the place of ad cards on select tokens in Play Boosters.**

There's a big ol' haul of tokens to swipe from [*Outlaws of Thunder Junction*](https://web.archive.org/web/20240405000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/outlaws-of-thunder-junction), with 57 tokens, 12 bounties, and 3 helper cards across *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* products all featuring glorious full art.

In Play Boosters, you can find 30 tokens and 1 plot helper card across three card sets, with 18 tokens and the plot helper card from *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* (OTJ), 7 tokens from The Big Score (BIG), and 5 tokens from Breaking News (OTP). The plot helper card from Play Boosters can be found on the backs of select tokens (making them double sided) where an ad card would otherwise appear.

In Collector Boosters, you'll find the same 30 full-art tokens as traditional foil double-sided tokens. (The plot helper card is not available in Collector Boosters.)

[*Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Commander decks](/en/articles/announcements/2024/04-04-outlaws-of-thunder-junction-commander-decklists) (OTC) also have tokens to rassle up. There are 27 tokens, 12 bounties, and 2 helper cards with the OTC set code across all four Commander decks, with 10 double-sided tokens in each deck. (Bounties feature bounty rules on the back.)

Set out on a Western journey of your own when *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* releases worldwide on April 19, 2024. You can preorder now from [your local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20240405000000/https://locator.wizards.com/), online retailers like [Amazon](https://gtly.to/Q0IT0FBOU), and elsewhere *Magic* products are sold.
