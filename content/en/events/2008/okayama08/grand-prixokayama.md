###  TOURNAMENT SCHEDULE

**Friday, November 21:** Trials and registration for Grand Prix—Okayama to be held at the Okayama Shi-min Kaikan, near Okayama station. Registration for Trials will begin at 10:00; registration for the Grand Prix will be held from 16:00-20:00.  
**Saturday, November 22:** Registration from 8:30-9:00 on-site.  
 Tournament begins at 9:30. If less than 800 players participate in the event, the Top 64 players advance to competition on Sunday. If 800 or more players participate, the Top 128 players advance to Sunday.  
**Sunday, November 23:** The Top 64 (or 128) players begin play at 9:00. After six rounds, the Top 8 will advance to the single-elimination finals to determine the Grand Prix—Okayama champion. See below for a list of additional Public Events scheduled for Sunday.

###  TOURNAMENT FORMAT

• Day 1: Sealed Deck (1 *Shards of Alara* tournament pack+ 2 *Shards of Alara* booster packs per player)  
 • Day 2 and Top 8: Booster Draft (3 *Shards of Alara* booster packs per player)  
 • Deck lists are required.  
 • Deck registration and deck swap will be performed for the Sealed Deck portion of the tournament.

**TOURNAMENT LENGTH**  
 • Grand Prix—Okayama will be run using Swiss rounds, with 50 minutes per round. Players will receive 5 extra turns after time is called in a round to complete their match.  
 • The number of Swiss rounds on Day 1 will be determined based on attendance and the number of byes in the tournament, and will be announced at the start of Day 1.  
 • Day 2 will have 6 Swiss rounds (regardless of the number of players advancing to Day 2) followed by a cut to the Top 8.

**ADVANCEMENT**  
 • All players may participate in all Swiss rounds of Day 1.  
 • If the number of players on Day 1 is 799 or less, the Top 64 players at the end of Day 1 will advance to the second day of competition for prizes and invitations. If the number of players on Day 1 is 800 or more, the Top 128 players will advance to Day 2.  
 • The Top 8 players after the final Swiss round on Day 2 will advance to the Top 8 single-elimination playoff round.

**PLAYOFF ROUND**  
 • Top 8 matches will be best 2-of-3.  
 • Top 8 matches will have no time limit; however, players are still expected to play at a reasonable pace and complete their matches in a timely manner.  
 • The Top 8 players will be seated randomly at the draft table immediately prior to drafting. The Top 8 bracket will be determined using this random seating.  
 • Where necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will be used to determine final order in the standings.

**JUDGES**  
 The Head Judge for Grand Prix—Okayama will be Takeshi Miyasaka (Level 3, Japan).

**NOTES**  
 • Grand Prix tournaments have a DCI KValue of 40K.  
 • Day 1 of the Grand Prix will be run under Rules Enforcement Level: Competitive; Day 2 will be REL: Professional.  
 • All Universal Tournament Rules and **Magic: The Gathering** Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document.  
 • Players are expected to bring pen, paper, card sleeves, and appropriate counters for use in play.

### INVITATIONS AND ELIGIBILITY

• Grand Prix—Okayama is open to **Magic** players of all ages.  
 • The Top 16 players will receive invitations to Pro Tour—Kyoto, scheduled for February 27-March 1, 2009. In the event that a player who wins an invitation to a Pro Tour stop at a Grand Prix has already qualified for an invitation, the invitation does not pass down under any circumstances. Previous qualification may be through a Qualifier tournament, through performance at a professional tournament, or by being an invited DCI player (valid upon the date of the official rank announcement for the appropriate professional tournament).

###  TOURNAMENT BYES

*(Byes are not cumulative throughout the season or year)* 

Byes will be given to players in the Grand Prix based on the following criteria:  
***Third-Round Byes***  
 • Players with a Players Club level of 4 or higher.  
 • Winners of a Grand Prix—Okayama Trial tournament.  
 • DCI players with a Limited rating of 2000 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI Constructed ratings published on the Wednesday before the Grand Prix (November 19, 2008).  
***Second-Round Byes***  
 • Players with a Players Club level of 2 or 3.  
 • DCI players with a Limited rating of 1900 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI Constructed ratings published on the Wednesday before the Grand Prix (November 19, 2008).  
***First-Round Byes***  
 • Players with a Players Club level of 1.  
 • DCI players with a Limited rating of 1800 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI Constructed ratings published on the Wednesday before the Grand Prix (November 19, 2008).
