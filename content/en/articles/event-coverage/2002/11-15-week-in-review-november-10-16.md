### Pro Tour-Houston

![](http://www.wizards.com/protour/images/pt_lg.gif)Your Move Games. Your Move Games. Your Move Games. Can you say that three times fast? You'd have to, if you want to talk about Pro Tour-Houston. YMG always seemed to perform best in the Extended format. Justin Gary made Top 8 of PT-Rome, with Humpherys barely missing the Top 8 also. In New Orleans, Kastle and Humpherys both made Top 8 with several teammates finishing in Top 16.

This time around, Justin Gary, Rob Dougherty and Darwin Kastle took the top three places after fourteen rounds of Swiss, then defended them in the Top 8, not losing a single game to players outside the team. Moreover, they did this with three different decks! Each player worked off his own strengths. Justin Gary continued to use the Oath of Druids strategy that netted him a second place finish in the Masters last season. Darwin Kastle practically invented black-green and succeeded playing this archetype, even finding room for Living Death in his sideboard. Rob Dougherty designed the Benzo deck for PT-New Orleans. He and Dave Humpherys came up with the current version of the deck and Rob used it successfully during the tournament.

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/pthou02/405.jpg)Throughout much of the tournament, Kastle was in the lead, starting the event with a 10-0 record. Although this did not yet guarantee Kastle a spot in the Top 8, in a true display of team camaraderie, he conceded a match to Justin Gary, who was three points behind, when they were paired up in eleventh round. The gamble almost cost too much for Darwin, who lost the next two matches and had to win in the final round to make Top 8 - but he did win that crucial match. Kastle, who is the only player to have competed in every single Pro Tour, was in danger of falling off the gravy train if he did not place in Top 32 in Houston. He is now safe for several more Pro Tours. In a related story, Pro Tour veteran Dave Price was also in danger of finally falling off the Pro Tour after he failed to earn a Top 64 finish this weekend. Price played in a Saturday PTQ but lost in the semifinals. He then played in another PTQ on Sunday and won that one, safe for another Tour, and still the king of the qualifiers.

Next to YMG's unprecedented dominance, perhaps the second greatest story of the weekend is Bob Maher and the Dutch/Japanese connection. Victor van den Broek and other Dutch players approached Maher at 1 AM the night before Pro Tour and they offered him their deck in exchange for a percentage of his winnings. Maher decided to take a look and was immediately impressed with their very strange Sutured Ghoul reanimator strategy. He stayed up testing the deck until about four in the morning and finally settled into playing it. Despite having little practice, Maher outperformed the deck's designers, earning his second Pro Tour Top 8 for the season and a very comfortable spot in the Player of the Year race. The deck was apparently first designed by Tsuyoshi Fujita, who did not attend this Pro Tour but emailed the idea to Kamiel Cornelissen. Cornelissen and his Dutch teammates then worked on the concept, showing up with the most successful original deck of the tournament. I say successful because the event certainly did not lack originality. There was even an Erratic Explosion/Draco deck out there.

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/pthou02/389.jpg)John Larkin was the most successful member of Kai Budde's current playtest team, with a Top 4 finish. Baberowski was next at the tail end of Top 24, overreaching his Phoenix Foundation teammate in the Player of the Year race. Kai himself failed to make Day 2 for the first time in eons. He was paired up against Ben Rubin in the final round of Swiss, where Rubin refused to take an intentional draw that would allow both pros to play on Day 2. Instead, Rubin defeated Budde, eventually earning himself a 42nd place finish. Anyone is allowed a bad day at the Pro Tour. Even Kai.

In the very first round of the tournament, the game's top two legends clashed as Budde was paired up against Finkel. Although Budde won the matchup, it was Finkel who went on to a 6-1 record on Day 1, winning every match after that. Finkel earned a total of zero dollars on the Pro Tour last season and was barely qualified for this one, but he seems to be making a comeback. Although his Day 2 was a bit less impressive, he still finished in the Top 64. Could he make a comeback and threaten Budde's dominance? We shall see in a few months at Pro Tour-Chicago. Another old school player to make a return at this Pro Tour was Kyle Rose. After having skipped several events, Rose returned to earn a ninth-place finish.

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/pthou02/914.jpg)Invitational winner Jens Thoren did poorly at the Pro Tour, but more than made up for it by winning the Masters. He defeated Gary Wise in the finals, with Raphael Levy and Eric Froehlich rounding out the Top 4. Although $30,000 will surely help pay for Walamies's ticket, this talented deck designer will have to work hard to re-qualify for the Pro Tour. With so many points falling off his rating now that Pro Tour-New Orleans is a year behind us, Walamies was in bizarre position of being qualified for Masters but not for the next Pro Tour. He failed to earn a Top 32 finish and now has to look to the PTQs or hope for the ratings invite.

Finally, perhaps the saddest story of the weekend was that of Matt Vienneau. Matt was qualified for this tournament on the strength of his DCI rating but apparently did not realize this, and did not attend. Pat Chapin called him on Thursday night when the rest of us realized he wasn't in Houston, but only got the answering machine. Hopefully Matt will join us for the next PT.

### Congratulations

YMG had more to celebrate than just large amounts of cash (over $80,000 total!) won at this Pro Tour. Rob Dougherty and his wife Krista had a baby boy, Ryan, just a little while prior to the Pro Tour. Also, Chad Ellis and his fiancee got married the weekend of the Pro Tour! Rather than re-tell the story, I will quote Chad directly from an email I got just as I was finishing up this article:

*Alex,* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/41/0c/410cf6df654a7d203b498e57180e21fe1d2562effa960a3645db39f0e47a8118.jpg)*I hope this isn't too late... but there's a bit of a story behind the YMG dominance of last week's PT. The weekend of the PT was also the weekend I got married! When we scheduled the wedding several months ago, we had Rob call up his contacts at WotC to find out when Houston would be held, and scheduled our wedding around it so that our YMG friends could attend. Unfortunately, conflict with a football game caused WotC to move the PT to "our" weekend, by which time it was impossible for us to do anything.* 

*We decided to hold a pre-wedding YMG get together in Montreal the weekend before. Rob (along with wife Krista and baby Ryan), Darwin, Danny and Tony Bayer drove up to Canada and the five of us had a typical bachelor-party weekend. (That is, we drafted and played Extended to all hours.) They also gave me a wedding present of all the rares from the drafts and Rob threw in several hundred tokens so I could trade them with Europeans.* 

*Call it karma, but giving up a practice weekend seems to have paid off... or did it? Rob and Darwin certainly had an amazing PT, but Justin Gary won the whole thing in the end... in any case, all three of them can afford to buy me a better wedding present now! :)* 

*Hugs,  
 Chad* 

*P.S. In true YMG style, our wedding wasn't exactly normal... we had a medieval theme (heck, Rob and Krista already took Star Trek), with over 120 guests in costume. Unfortunately I don't have a lot of pictures yet, but here's one...* 

Heartfelt congratulations to both Rob and Chad from all of us at the *Sideboard*!

### Magic Trivia

Last week's question:

*How many **Magic** expansions share names with **Magic** cards from other sets?*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/53/12/5312ffae830f21e9a4afa3c7718c5f67063035a467ec4b780ce5e346a88b46fc.jpg)*Visions*, *Prophecy*, *Torment*, *Apocalypse* and *Onslaught* are all both the names of **Magic** sets and single cards. Visions is a card from *Legends*, Prophecy is a card from *Homelands*, Torment is a card from *Stronghold*, Apocalypse is a card from *Tempest*, and Onslaught is a card from *Exodus*.

### Bad Play of the Week

"Anyhow, Gary thought about what to do next. If he swung with just the \[Jareth, Leonine\] Titan, Patrick might have a trick to get through the next turn for the ten damage that equaled Wise's life total. If he swung with the Tephraderm, Sullivan would be forced to block (since the Titan would reduce him to two life), and would be unable to swing for the win.
