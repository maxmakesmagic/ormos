## 2001 Latin America Championship Coverage

![Latin America Championship](https://i.ormos.uk/cd/78/cd7803f330ffe4700ec466e3220161e18449ee05f72f4711b6e2c7b0dcd7bd33.jpg)![Latin America Championship](https://i.ormos.uk/7e/2c/7e2c09cc30f0caedb0f71c0a0b26515b201a9e203e29ded0b56d089c1eb900a4.jpg)

![Scott Richards, Latin American Champion](https://i.ormos.uk/41/45/4145d5cc7cc29a08ad8b91c8f65a7619ba9d52fa8b268c2c628667d22fe7c8e5.jpg)[Spanish coverage at Necro Magic](http://www.necromagic.com/torneos/latino/01/)

[2001 European Championship Coverage](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721050653/http://archive.wizards.com/sideboard/event.asp?event=EURO01)

Scott Richards somehow managed to escape a nightmare matchup against Ponza in five gruelling games, and wound up in the finals against Argentinian National Champion Diego Ostrovich. Piloting his Counter-Rebels deck expertly, Richards took the final match in four games. Congratulations to Scott Richards, 2001 Latin American Champion!
