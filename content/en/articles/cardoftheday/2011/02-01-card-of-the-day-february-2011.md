From rules manager Matt Tabak: A <a>Myr Welder</a> walks into a bar and imprints <a>Mimic Vat</a> and our friend the <a>Copper Myr</a>. Wait, you've heard this one? Well, then you know that <a>Myr Welder</a> and <a>Mimic Vat</a> both have a pair of linked abilities. The second ability on each only refers to cards exiled by the matching first ability. The <a>Myr Welder</a> won't be able to use the <a>Mimic Vat</a>'s activated ability to produce token copies of <a>Copper Myr</a>. <p></p>&#10;      </div>&#10;    </td>&#10;  </tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

<table>&#10;  <tbody><tr>&#10;    <td colspan="2">&#10;      <b>Card of the Day - Thursday, February 03, 2011</b>&#10;    </td>&#10;  </tr>&#10;  <tr>&#10;    <td colspan="2">&#10;      <div>&#10;        <p>&#10;          </p><div>&#10;            <div>&#10;              <a>&#10;                <img alt="Rot Wolf" src="https://i.ormos.uk/1e/6f/1e6f75fcd3e853fb7a61ab2a9ce48db2ddf54d4955a9669bc672628b87eef3c3.jpg">&#10;              </a>&#10;            </div>&#10;          </div>&#10;          <b>&#10;            &#10;            <a>Rot Wolf</a>&#10;          </b> – <i>Mirrodin Besieged</i> common. Remember that cute <a>Copper Myr</a> from yesterday? Well today it blocked a <a>Rot Wolf</a>, then had <a>Giant Growth</a> cast on it again. It's now a 2/2 (a 4/4 with two -1/-1 counters on it). Cleanup rolls around, and the effect form <a>Giant Growth</a> wears off. You'd expect to draw a card right? Afraid not. <a>Rot Wolf</a> just has a regular triggered ability, not a delayed one. It has to be on the battlefield when the creature it damaged goes to the graveyard to trigger. <p></p>&#10;      </div>&#10;    </td>&#10;  </tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

<table>&#10;  <tbody><tr>&#10;    <td colspan="2">&#10;      <b>Card of the Day - Wednesday, February 02, 2011</b>&#10;    </td>&#10;  </tr>&#10;  <tr>&#10;    <td colspan="2">&#10;      <div>&#10;        <p>&#10;          </p><div>&#10;            <div>&#10;              <a>&#10;                <img alt="Virulent Wound" src="https://i.ormos.uk/c7/34/c73477e952e4a1496f54958cbecd87fa54a44e03f4a044ce784f7b3d687be935.jpg">&#10;              </a>&#10;            </div>&#10;          </div>&#10;          <b>&#10;            &#10;            <a>Virulent Wound</a>&#10;          </b> – <i>Mirrodin Besieged</i> common. From rules manager Matt Tabak: Let's say you have a 1/1 creature that is targeted by <a>Virulent Wound</a>. In fact, let's make it an adorable <a>Copper Myr</a>. You cast <a>Giant Growth</a> in a somewhat futile attempt to save it. During cleanup, the +3/+3 from <a>Giant Growth</a> wears off (at the same time damage does), the 0/0 Myr heads to the graveyard. It's still &quot;this turn,&quot; so you'll receive a poison counter from <a>Virulent Wound</a>.<p></p>&#10;      </div>&#10;    </td>&#10;  </tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

From rules manager Matt Tabak: The damage a creature with infect deals to <a>Melira's Keepers</a> won’t result in any -1/-1 counters, but that doesn’t mean the damage is prevented. If that creature has lifelink, its controller will still gain life. If that creature has deathtouch, <a>Melira's Keepers</a> will be destroyed. <p></p>&#10;      </div>&#10;    </td>&#10;  </tr>&#10;</tbody></table>
