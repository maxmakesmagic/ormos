Regionals is on the horizon, and it promises to be an exciting weekend! All over North America, thousands of players will bring their best Standard decks to the table in an effort to earn the opportunity to compete at their National Championships. Of course, there's still plenty of time to get involved! [Check out the worldwide schedule for Regionals](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/regional-championships-2008), plus a first wave of lists from Regionals in Tokyo.

Looking for more places to play Standard? The Pro Tour Qualifier season for Austin is now under way, and the first results are pouring in. Find out what was hot at the double PTQ in Richmond, Virginia, or see what was played in San Diego and Standish, Maine. Naturally, we also have the latest and greatest from **Magic Online**. Make sure to check out the Pauper and 100 Card Singleton lists from recent Premier Events too!
