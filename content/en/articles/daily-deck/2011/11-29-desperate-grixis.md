At this year's **Magic** World Championships, Patrick Chapin innovated an exciting new blue, black, and red control deck. Patrick used some powerful new *Innistrad* cards to fuel his strategy, but they weren't all cards people had pegged as Constructed worthy when the set came out. Desperate Ravings and Olivia Voldaren were two new cards in particular that shocked the audience. If you're looking for more on this deck, check out [the Worlds Coverage](/en/events/2011/worlds11/japan-stands-tall-at-worlds-again) page!

Patrick Chapin's Grixis Control 

Standard – 5-1 at the 2011 World Championships
