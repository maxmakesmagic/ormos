The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on August 24, 2016. Today's Update is brought to you by Modern. So much Modern.

# DailyMTG is Getting a Facelift

1.  Changes coming Thursday

You're going to notice some cosmetic changes coming to DailyMTG, likely tomorrow, making the page easier to read, better for scanning, and overall less cluttered. Basically, we're making it just plain look better.

The changes coming aren't particularly functional, though you will notice, for example, that the white box doesn't pop up anymore when you hover over an article. Additionally, color schemes, general layout, and the location of articles aren't changing. If you looked in one place for something before, it should still be there.

What we are doing is making things more readable. This includes making the text larger, increasing the content well size (the area where the main content lives), and decreasing the sizes of icons to make it so that you don't have to scroll down just to see the rest of the article block.

We're constantly looking to improve the website, and we hope this is one step in making it more readable.

Now, the other part of this is that, in order to make these changes, we'll be in maintenance mode for a while on Thursday. That means, for the most part, you won't notice any service or article interruptions. What we're actually doing is publishing DailyMTG earlier than normal—at 6 a.m. PT. So you'll get articles two hours earlier for that day only. The look and feel changes won't go into effect till later that day, and chances are good I'll tweet ([@blakepr](http://www.twitter.com/blakepr)) about it when they are up and running.

There are still a couple check points that need to be passed, so things can still shift, but you can always check with me on Twitter for updates.

Otherwise, articles will go up as normal, we'll still be delivering *Magic* strategy and news (the new Friday Night *Magic* promo comes on Thursday!), and the sun will still be rising in the east. See you Thursday.

# Deck of the Day

1.  The Return of Amulet?

The other day, Matt Nass, pro player and streamer, streamed (as streamers do) a new take on Amulet, the deck formerly known as Amulet Bloom until one half of the deck's namesake—[Summer Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20160824000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summer+Bloom)—was banished to the banned list. Since then, we haven't really seen much of the deck.

But with [Grand Prix Indianapolis](/en/events/2016/gpind16/grand-prix-indianapolis-2016-fact-sheet), [Lille](/en/events/2016/gplil16/fact-sheet-grand-prix-lille-2016), and [Guangzhou](/en/events/2016/gpgua16/grand-prix-guangzhou-2016-fact-sheet) all happening this weekend (and with video coverage happening at Grand Prix Indianapolis), Nass tested out a version of the deck to see if it still had legs. And, [based on the results of his stream](https://www.twitch.tv/mattnassty/v/85170840) (head to the 48-minute mark for roughly the start of his Amulet testing), it seems it does.

The innovations include finding more ways to play land that are slightly less explosive than [Summer Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20160824000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summer+Bloom), but still effective. [Explore](https://web.archive.org/web/20160824000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Explore) is a longtime ramp staple that is only one-third of a [Summer Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20160824000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summer+Bloom), but it draws a card. The real find is [Journey of Discovery](https://web.archive.org/web/20160824000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Journey+of+Discovery), which, for one more mana, is effectively two-thirds of a [Summer Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20160824000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summer+Bloom). That's about the best rate you'll get these days. While it's certainly less explosive, the Amulet deck was a bit *too* explosive before. Now it might be just about right. At any rate, in honor of this worse-but-still-effective substitute, I now dub this deck Amulet Journey. Because I'm creative like that.
