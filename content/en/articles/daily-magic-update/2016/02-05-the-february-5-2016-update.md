Merry Chri...I mean, Happy Pro Tour Weekend! Pro Tour *Oath of the Gatewatch* is finally here, and by the time you're reading this, [it's already happening](http://giphy.com/gifs/excited-ron-paul-its-happening-rl0FOxdz7CcxO)!

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Ascending to New Heights](/en/events/2016/modern-constructed-league-2016-02-04/modern-constructed-league)

If, as many pros are predicting, [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20160205000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay) will be on the decline in Modern, now seems like a pretty good time to start breaking out the three-mana permanents, right? And since [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20160205000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy) is one of the most powerful three-mana permanents around, it might be time for a re-emergence. Parjona certainly thought so, and turned in a 5-0 performance in a Modern League with this masterwork.

How does it work? With an Ascendancy in play, untapping either [Fatestitcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20160205000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatestitcher) or an active [Faerie Conclave](https://web.archive.org/web/20160205000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faerie+Conclave) with every spell nets you mana and cards every time, while growing that creature. That finds you more [Fatestitcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20160205000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatestitcher)s, which nets more mana and more attackers. Eventually, you swing with a large enough creature and your opponent is dead in a flurry of cantrips.
