Years ago, there was a magazine called *Duelist*, and it was all about **Magic**. Its covers were frequently adorned with brand new paintings, like this alternate view of Phyrexian Negator by [Carl Critchlow](https://web.archive.org/web/20120329000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22carl+critchlow%22]):

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ea/8e/ea8e9b3d1459c12ea0444cf3e4f0927df149d6ceb99bc2fdc6680a1701afe036.jpg) 

  

 ![5559](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&MultiverseId=5559&options=) 

  

Well, guess what? We recently found the files for the original art for a few of these covers. So here, for the first time, is this neat Negator without all those pesky words all over it!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ca/8b/ca8bfa05ed52a028f8ac1b79d9c5dd981602961823f6a4a07a4b01ea841c4c63.jpg) 

Duelist #40 Cover | Art by [Carl Critchlow](https://web.archive.org/web/20120329000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22carl+critchlow%22])

Now that we've established the theme of what we're doing here, here's Duelist #38:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ad/52/ad52c6a7064cfa23fff58197feadeccc9faaa1248e494bdb264180e1ad352f93.jpg) 

  

 ![3452](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&MultiverseId=3452&options=) 

  

That art is an alternate version of Hammer of Bogardan, done by [Ron Spencer](https://web.archive.org/web/20120329000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22ron+spencer%22]), the man who did the original. And here it is without the words getting in the way!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1e/5d/1e5d59e780630cbc91a126c8e6328889f30afecee1d5fd56fc6a0acb86d2cfdd.jpg) 

Duelist #38 Cover | Art by [Ron Spencer](https://web.archive.org/web/20120329000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22ron+spencer%22])
