Google just launched its gaming themes for iGoogle, and **Magic: The Gathering** is one of the 25 featured games! You can see the cool stuff [right here](https://web.archive.org/web/20090331000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.google.com/help/ig/gaming/#mtg&origin=).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5c/8a/5c8aca0465ec3d21d3a6404d76ea1ca0dd0d4337a4296f6c635d0cd85bc3d2f3.jpg) 

  

The themes change depending on the time of day, and there are twelve different themes. They feature artwork from **Magic**'s planeswalkers and giant monsters and there are even some preview images from *Alara Reborn*!

The announcement came at a big, fancy Google party. So if you've ever wondered what Nicol Bolas would look like projected on a wall, the answer is this:
