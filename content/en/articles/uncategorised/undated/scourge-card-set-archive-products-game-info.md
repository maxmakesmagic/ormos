## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d7/f0/d7f001582ee04bc4bb843de2bccb80c37a0fc74d11e85148b2feea2230db79b2.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, six 15-card booster packs, one foil basic land card, one random foil card, the *Scourge* novel, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d7/f0/d7f001582ee04bc4bb843de2bccb80c37a0fc74d11e85148b2feea2230db79b2.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d7/f0/d7f001582ee04bc4bb843de2bccb80c37a0fc74d11e85148b2feea2230db79b2.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1f/35/1f352a74093dc9abcf7aab5cd44ad947b665f45319436ca2e2dd96729fb23e37.png)This 143-card set includes randomly inserted foil-finished premium cards and new mechanics like Storm, Landcycling, Warchiefs, and even a few creatures that morph for free.

**Set Name:** *Scourge*  
**Block:** Set 3 of 3 of the *Onslaught* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 143

**Languages:** English, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044257/https://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/faq/scourge) (or download as [RTF](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044257/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/Scourge_FAQ_4-7.rtf))

**Game Design:** Brian Tinsman (lead) and Worth Wollpert

**Game Development:** Randy Buehler Jr. (lead), Brandon Bozzi, Mike Elliott, Brian Schneider, and Henry Stern
