One Dragon that doesn't fit the normal mold is the rarely-seen Nalathni Dragon:

![Nalathni Dragon by Michael Whelan](https://i.ormos.uk/78/2e/782e46b7c6c4b5bc38d1af3f61ad1473203c0460dbd10e497db8b34f237866c6.jpg)

The card was created as a giveaway for the Dragon\*Con convention in 1994 (hence the dragon expansion symbol). It was also distributed in *The Duelist* #3 (much to the annoyance of people that paid/traded heavily for them after Dragon\*Con).

Not only is Nalathni Dragon the smallest Dragon ever made, it also has the very out-of-flavor ability of banding (and is the only red creature with the ability).

Nalathni Dragon is legal in Type 1 tournaments. The card's Oracle text:
