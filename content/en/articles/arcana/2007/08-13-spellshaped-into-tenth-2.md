What do these *Tenth Edition* creatures have in common?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ce/9d/ce9db1db72e1a3455a1461815b4bde1b656404d36dcc9b28b96bf2e852baac28.jpg)

They are all creatures "shaped" by spellshapers in *Future Sight*: [Cloudseeder](https://web.archive.org/web/20070813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudseeder), [Skirk Ridge Exhumer](https://web.archive.org/web/20070813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skirk+Ridge+Exhumer), [Sparkspitter](https://web.archive.org/web/20070813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sparkspitter), and [Llanowar Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20070813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Mentor). 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7b/83/7b8332b177102bb96496fd949b2fc6bc5dfdcbb267a6dadb01ea3ee5a3ed18ec.jpg)

**Magic Online** creates tokens when you activate these spellshapers, but you might use spare copies of the creatures from *Tenth Edition* as their tokens in the tabletop world.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4a/f6/4af6fd7c2ae79b413160848841b19ca578b7b939d303732e546552fe10e51976.jpg)

[Sliversmith](https://web.archive.org/web/20070813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sliversmith) makes a [Metallic Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20070813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metallic+Sliver) token, which of course doesn't appear in *Tenth Edition*. A vanilla, artifact Sliver would have been pretty strange all by itself in a core set!

But the biggest mystery is [Goldmeadow Lookout](https://web.archive.org/web/20070813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goldmeadow+Lookout). It creates a token with the name of no existing **Magic** card. *Future Sight* indeed?
