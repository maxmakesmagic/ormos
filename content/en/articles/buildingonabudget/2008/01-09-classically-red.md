Hello everyone, and welcome to a new year for Building on a Budget! For those just reading this column for the first time, Building on a Budget is a column dedicated to building decks that cost 30 tickets or less using **Magic Online**. I'm Ben Bleiweiss, and you can find my bio and blurb at the bottom of the page. My user ID on **Magic Online** is BuildingonaBudget, so look me up some time if you want to help playtest for this column!

This is my third calendar year working on Building on a Budget! It seems like only yesterday that I first started working on Building on a Budget, so I guess that's a good sign—time flies when you're having fun!

Speaking of fun, I had a blast playing around with the Classic format on **Magic Online** this past week. For those tuned in at the end of last year, I ran a poll asking you, the reader, which format you'd most like to see me play at the beginning of this year. That poll was too close to call, so I held a runoff between the two top picks. Here are the results of that runoff:

The biggest problem I had when approaching Classic was an immense pool of cards. Every card on **Magic Online** is legal for Online play, with the three exceptions of Gleemox (banned), [Flash](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flash) (restricted) and [Vampiric Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vampiric+Tutor) (restricted). This left every base set from *Seventh* through *Tenth*, *Mirage*, *Visions*, *Weatherlight*, *Masters Edition*, and all expansion sets from *Onslaught* through *Lorwyn* as places to draw from (plus a few promo cards like [Sliver Queen](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sliver+Queen), [Morphling](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morphling), and others). Let me tell you—it is daunting to start from scratch when building a deck with that many cards to choose from!

 

![Skullclamp](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Skullclamp)

In the end, I took a look at which cards tickled my fancy in the common and uncommon slots from each color. Red in particular stuck out in my mind, in one of two forms—either as a Goblin deck or as a straight burn deck. There are a lot of amazing common burn spells available right now ([Incinerate](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Incinerate), [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt), [Fireblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fireblast), [Rift Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rift+Bolt)) which made red burn a very attractive, appealing, cheap choice. By the same token, the Goblin engine is almost exclusively made up of commons and uncommons ([Goblin Warchief](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Warchief), [Goblin Ringleader](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Ringleader), [Skirk Prospector](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skirk+Prospector), [Goblin Matron](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Matron)), making a budget version of a Goblin deck (no Piledrivers, no fetch lands) a possibility.

What pushed my choice over the edge was the inclusion of [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp). Now, I don't know who among you played when [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp) was legal in Standard, but the card is three degrees of broken. Each time you play a one-toughness creature, you're looking at another card. Put it on a two toughness creature, and draw extra cards when it dies/is blocked. Play two [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp)s on a two-toughness creature, and draw four cards. [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp) is banned in every paper format except Vintage, and for good reason—it is one of the most potent card-drawing cards ever printed in **Magic**.

Well, it's legal in Classic and I found them all over the place at four-for-a-ticket. If [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp) is legal in Online Classic, I sure as heck was going to find a way to work it into every deck I built on a Budget.

In order to make this deck budget, the green had to go entirely—there's just no cheap way to get green and red reliably and still fit in the budget of the key cards (the Goblins) in this deck. Also, [Goblin Piledriver](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Piledriver) just isn't affordable. While several other key Goblins (Warchief, Matron) are available cheaply right now thanks to the [*Elves vs. Goblins* duel deck](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/expansion/elvesvsgoblins), a set of Piledrivers alone would take up most of the cost of the deck.

There is another deck that was banned out of existence in other formats—the [Goblin Recruiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Recruiter) / [Goblin Charbelcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Charbelcher) deck. With this combination, you can put all of the Goblins in your deck on top of your library, and then activate your Charbelcher to deal that much damage (or double that much if you get a [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain)) to your opponent. I found Charbelchers available for half a ticket each.

This gave my deck multiple degenerate combos—[Skirk Prospector](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skirk+Prospector) plus [Mogg War Marshal](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mogg+War+Marshal) (three extra mana), [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp) plus most of my Goblins (reusable card drawing), [Goblin Warchief](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Warchief) (to reduce the cost of other Goblins and give them haste), a singleton tutorable [Goblin King](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+King) (more damage, [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain)walk) and Sharpshooter, plus the Recruiter/Charbelcher kill. This was a much more competitive deck than I'm used to playing in the casual room, but there weren't enough people playtesting Classic in the serious rooms to test there. There were plenty of other players playing Classic in the casual room, and so I tested my Goblin deck there.

I turn up a [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain) at the end of my remaining Goblins, so I would have dealt over 20 damage anyhow.

Record: 11-1

With the 11-1 record under my belt with Goblins, I decide to switch to a classic burn deck. I still want to us [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp), but I add in several creatures that aren't necessarily from the Goblin deck—[Mudbutton Torchrunner](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mudbutton+Torchrunner) ([Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) plus draw two cards), [Slith Firewalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slith+Firewalker) (the guy just keeps getting bigger), and [Scorched Rusalka](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scorched+Rusalka) (to get through that last few damage). I make my burn suite an impressive package of [Rift Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rift+Bolt), [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt), [Fireblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fireblast), [Char](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Char), and [Incinerate](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Incinerate).

I have enough burn to take down a forest full of wet trees. Let's see what kind of kindling I found!

**Game 1: jdderda (Mono-Black Discard)**

I draw eleven [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain)s, and end up dying to [Liliana Vess](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+Vess) and [The Rack](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Rack), having never drawn enough burn to kill Liliana before she got up in counters. I do get double [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp), but no creatures to go with it.

Record: 0-1

**Game 2: cadaverous knight ([Muse Vessel](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Muse+Vessel))**

Very similar to my old [Muse Vessel](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Muse+Vessel) deck, but I burn him out the turn before he takes away my entire hand, and strips all of my lands but two. He ends up with double [Char](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Char), [Slith Firewalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slith+Firewalker), [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp), and [Mudbutton Torchrunner](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mudbutton+Torchrunner) under his [Muse Vessel](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Muse+Vessel) before I take him down to zero.

Record: 1-1

**Game 3: Knights84 (Mono-Red Burn / LD)**

He gets [Slith Firewalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slith+Firewalker) and casts [Molten Rain](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Molten+Rain) on my [Mishra's Factory](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mishra%27s+Factory). I [Incinerate](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Incinerate) his Firewalker, and he casts [Stone Rain](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stone+Rain). He then plays [Vulshok Sorcerer](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulshok+Sorcerer), and I drop [Mudbutton Torchrunner](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mudbutton+Torchrunner). He drops another Sorcerer, shoots me, and I drop another Torchrunner. He accidentally shoots his own Sorcerer and concedes, but we look at our hands and determine that I was going to win the game (I had double [Fireblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fireblast), double [Rift Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rift+Bolt) and [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) to his [Shock](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shock), Sorcerer, Firewalker, [Shock](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shock), meaning I could throw 17 damage at him (plus the Torchrunners, either attacking or being killed for 3 damage) before he could even get close to throwing 14 more damage at my head.

Record: 2-1

**Game 4: TJLAPEYROUSE (Griffins)**

I get stuck on one mana, with double [Scorched Rusalka](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scorched+Rusalka) and a [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp). It takes until my second Rusalka until I draw a second mana. Record: 3-1

**Game 5: dark\_cowboy30 (Black-Green Elves)**

I kill a [Wellwisher](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wellwisher) with a [Mudbutton Torchrunner](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mudbutton+Torchrunner) and a [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp), and get a [Slith Firewalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slith+Firewalker) up to 3/3. I then aim [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) at dark\_cowboys30's head, get triple [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp) on my Firewalker, and finish the game by untapping, unsuspending a [Rift Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rift+Bolt), throwing double [Incinerate](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Incinerate) at the dome, and then saccing four [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain)s to double [Fireblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fireblast) (17 points of direct damage)

Record: 4-1

The Rusalkas aren't that great, since A) I want to sacrifice my creatures to [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp) when possible, and B) I don't have a lot of creatures. I decide to run [Martyr of Ashes](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Martyr+of+Ashes) in their stead, as a way to sweep the board of early creatures, and to have a creature with more general utility for this deck.

I've also ended up with [Char](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Char) in my hand to end several games—at three mana, it is (surprisingly enough) very unwieldy for this deck. Most of the rest of my burn costs one or zero ([Fireblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fireblast)), and I've had more than enough of it. What I'm light on are creatures, especially with [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp) in my deck. I take out the [Char](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Char)s (making the deck completely rare-free) and put in [Dwarven Blastminer](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dwarven+Blastminer)s—people love their nonbasic lands, and the Blastminer also gives me an out in case my opponent drops some sort of [Story Circle](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Story+Circle) or [Circle of Protection: Red](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Circle+of+Protection%3A+Red).

Record: 8-2

I had a lot of fun playing Classic with these two decks—even though both are red, they function very differently. There's a fun thrill of getting across 20 points of attacking damage on turn four or five with Goblins, just as there is a perverse pleasure taken in dealing 20 points of burn in a single turn. For those who hate playing with rares at all, definitely try out the pure red burn deck—it was very fun to play, it proved competitive, and it had no rares. If you like Goblins, take advantage of two of the most broken cards in **Magic Online** ([Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp) and [Goblin Recruiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20080109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Recruiter)) while they are cheap and allowed, and win yourself a few games!
