One thing I love about *Magic Online* is that you can play any format at any time. Most *Magic* players can't play Vintage, because they just don't have the means to acquire the highly sought after "Power Nine[\[1\]](#_ftn1 "")." It's a whole different story on *Magic Online*, however. Extremely rare cards like the Power Nine, [Mana Crypt](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Crypt), [Mishra's Workshop](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mishra%27s+Workshop), and many others are readily available, making the Vintage format much easier to get into digitally than in face-to-face *Magic*.

Today's deck is a Vintage deck that is an innovative take on a classic Vintage archetype usually known as Stax. The name came about because it used to play [Smokestack](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smokestack). The deck archetype consists of artifact mana as well as a lot of "lock out" cards like [Chalice of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chalice+of+the+Void), [Lodestone Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lodestone+Golem), and [Sphere of Resistance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphere+of+Resistance). The idea behind the deck is to play a ton of mana acceleration and then spells that make your opponent's spells cost more. Combine that with [Wasteland](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland) and [Strip Mine](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strip+Mine) and your opponent will never have enough lands to play their spells, thus locking them out of the game.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Lodestone+Golem)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lodestone+Golem)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Sphere+of+Resistance)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphere+of+Resistance)

Montolio's take on the deck earned him a 4-0 finish in a *Magic Online* Vintage Daily. This version is a little different from traditional Stax decks. Instead of large artifact creatures like [Steel Hellkite](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steel+Hellkite) or [Duplicant](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duplicant), this deck plays smaller creatures like [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager) and [Phyrexian Revoker](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Revoker). Low mana creatures are a smart move, as you can usually cast one early and attack with it until the game ends. [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager) is especially strong, because as the game goes on you will likely have lots of artifacts in play that are no longer useful—like [Tangle Wire](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tangle+Wire) or the Moxen.

The Vintage metagame is full of creatures like [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer) and [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage), so [Sword of Fire and Ice](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+Fire+and+Ice) is another nice addition to this deck. Even [Batterskull](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Batterskull)—a card that usually only makes an appearance alongside [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic)—is good here because it's pretty easy for you to accelerate to five mana with this deck.
