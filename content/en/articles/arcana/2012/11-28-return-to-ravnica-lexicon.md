Welcome to the *Return to Ravnica* edition of the Lexicon, in which we explore the meanings of some of the more unusual words we use in the names of those cards. While many offbeat names in **Magic** (like "dracogenius") are simply made up, we often dig around dictionaries for real little-used and archaic words for inspiration.

* * *

 ![Azor's Elocutors](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Azor%27s%20Elocutors&options=) 

**el•o•cu•tion** \[el-*uh*-**kyoo**-sh*uh* n\]  
*noun*

1.  a person's manner of speaking or reading aloud in public: *The actor's elocution is faultless.*
2.  the study and practice of oral delivery, including the control of both voice and gesture.

  

* * *

 ![Archon of the Triumvirate](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Archon%20of%20the%20Triumvirate&options=) 

**tri•um•vi•rate** \[trahy-**uhm**-ver-it, -*vuh*-reyt\]  
*noun*

1.  *Roman History*. the office or magistracy of a triumvir.
2.  a government of three officers or magistrates functioning jointly.
3.  a coalition of three magistrates or rulers for joint administration.
4.  any association of three in office or authority.
5.  any group or set of three.

  

* * *

 ![Azorius Justiciar](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Azorius%20Justiciar&options=) 

**jus•ti•ci•ar** \[ juh-**stish**-ee-er\]  
*noun*

1.  a high judicial officer in medieval England.
2.  the chief political and judicial officer in England from the reign of William I to that of Henry III.

  

* * *

 ![Necropolis Regent](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Necropolis%20Regent&options=) 

**ne•crop•o•lis** \[*nuh*-**krop**-*uh*-lis, ne-\]  
*noun*

1.  a cemetery, especially one of large size and usually of an ancient city.
2.  a historic or prehistoric burial ground.
