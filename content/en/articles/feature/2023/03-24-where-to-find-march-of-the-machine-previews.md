It's time for [*March of the Machine*](https://web.archive.org/web/20230324000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/march-of-the-machine) previews! To help our readers and preview seekers, we've created this handy guide to preview season.

Tune in to [the official *Magic: The Gathering* YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC8ZGymAvfP97qJabgqUkz4A) or [twitch.tv/magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic) on March 29 beginning at 9 a.m. PT (12 p.m. ET) to get your first deep dive into the cards, mechanics, and big reveals of *March of the Machine* ahead of the global release April 21!

This page will help you follow along with everything coming by giving you the outlets where upcoming *March of the Machine* previews are planned on each day. The goal is to help you find what you're looking for every day and avoid engaging in previews if you choose.

Below, you'll find a list of dates as well as any outlets previewing a card on that date. Once the previews are live, we'll update this article so that you can easily find them. If the link simply goes to the front of a website or social media site, that means they probably haven't previewed the card yet.

And if you just want to see all the cards that have been officially previewed in one place, you can always check out the Card Image Galleries for *March of the Machine*, Multiverse Legends cards, variant and Booster Fun treatments, Commander deck cards, and Commander deck–exclusive Planechase cards when those are published. Note that these galleries are updated every morning, Monday through Friday, once previews begin, so they contain all cards previewed the previous day (and earlier), but not the day of (and we'll add links when these galleries go live on March 29).

For the most up-to-date listings, bookmark this page and check back regularly for all the latest previews from *March of the Machine*!
