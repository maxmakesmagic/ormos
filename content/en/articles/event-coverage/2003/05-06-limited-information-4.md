A card as powerful as Sparksmith comfortably coming in first is to be expected. Is the solution to the dilemma simply to take the most powerful card? Why would one take Cabal Archon? The pros have the answers.

**Dirk Baberowski**

Clearly, the only two cards worth considering are Sparksmith (which is a far better red card than Insurrection) and Cabal Archon. When making this difficult decision, there are some factors to consider that are not in the cards.

First, ask yourself what the person to your right could have possibly picked from this pack. The only uncommon in *Onslaught* that beats Sparksmith in terms of card quality is Slice and Dice. However, it is not unreasonable to put him on having picked Centaur Glade, Death Pulse, or Infest. Although any of those cards are far weaker than the Sparksmith, he may have been trying to avoid color conflicts, just like you are.

Second, consider where you want to go. If you take the Cabal Archon, you are in a strong position to cut black entirely (besides the Pangolin you passed in pack 1). If you take Sparksmith, you may or may not be sandwiched between two red drafters. The player on your left may have opened a Shock, Sparksmith, Solar Blast, or Lavamancer's Skill as well and now get passed a third pick Insurrection. Also, it is generally very difficult, if not impossible, to force the players behind you out of red in O-O-L draft. After all, the fact that they are red behind you only means that they will get none of the red cards in *Legions*, which are poor anyway.

Third, there is a consideration that is unique to this draft format. Due to morph and cycling, you always, ALWAYS have twenty-two playable cards, so you do not have to worry about having a good deck with a good mana curve as much as you used to. Instead, you have to worry about having cards that break games in half. Sparksmith is one such example, as are Daru Stinger, Lavamancer's Skill, and Timberwatch Elf. Cabal Archon is not in that category, but there is a fourth consideration.

I generally see black-red as a poor archetype. It has few synergetic cards and has to rely on power alone. Therefore, picking the ‘Smith means there is a chance I will give up Cruel Revival (or the Sparksmith) and the simple equation of Cruel Revival + Cabal Archon\>Sparksmith finally tips the scale towards Archon, making it my pick for the week.

**Kai Budde** 

I would take Sparksmith. People are paying way too much attention to their first pick. A lot of people don't understand that this draft format does not work like old ones. It is totally reasonable to fight for colors as you always end up with twenty-two playables, no matter what. Your deck is defined by your early picks and then it has a bunch of fillers, which you always get regardless of color. Taking worse cards to stay out of your neighbor's colors is not a good plan in this format. Even if I am sure that the person feeding me took Slice and Dice, I will take Sparksmith anyway. 

**Ken Krouner** 

There are a number of quality cards in both packs. My initial thought was that your pick in the second pack would be dependant upon what you think the player to your left will take out of the pack you opened. The player to your right either over-valued Centaur Glade and took it, or took Slice and Dice. Since the color you want most with green is red, you can be fairly confident if you go into red at this point, you will be sharing.

Additionally, you are going to be sending a red signal out of the second pack, like it or not. Sparksmith is clearly the best card in the pack, but I would take Cabal Archon here. Neither Slice and Dice nor Centaur Glade lend themselves well to white decks, so I believe you can begin moving toward the Cleric tribe early and have one of its most powerful weapons.

I would take Sparksmith because I think it is the best card in the pack. I know passing a red rare as strong as Insurrection might send weird signals, but I actually think it would be worse if you took the Insurrection. There are few rares anyone takes over Sparksmith and Insurrection is not one of them. The person on my right should realize that the only common worth taking over Insurrection would be Sparksmith and even if that's not clear, there is still a Cruel Revival in the pack to make it more obvious. **Gabriel Nassif**

I would take Sparksmith. My plan would be to go black-red if it keeps coming or switch to blue-red, the best combination, if it does not. Clerics are very good, but Cabal Archon is unimpressive.

**Jeroen Remie**

If you take the Sparksmith, you simply take the best card. Even if the person in front of you is red, which he must be, by taking Slice and Dice, he will only cut you off for one pack, since *Legions* red is not as good. You will still get lots in your second color, either black or something that comes late and get a good deck. In the second pack, you have a huge shot to get good red, since it is being cut by two people in pack one. In short, take Sparksmith.

I would take the Sparksmith from the pack. Red-black is a fine color combination and seeing these two red bombs in the pack is a sign of good things to come. There is the chance that the person next to you took Slice and Dice, but this risk is not nearly as great as the reward of having a Sparksmith.

**Victor van den Broek**

I would take Sparksmith. There are a number of uncommons in other colors that people could have taken (i.e. Infest, Death Pulse, Improvised Armor). In those cases, you are in a good position to draft red. If they DID take a red uncommon (Slice and Dice or Lightning Rift), then you still do have a Sparksmith. If you get totally cut off from the right, you might be able to reap the benefits from the left. Red is mediocre in *Legions* anyway, so it does not matter too much.

* * *

Sparksmith is the clear winner among both the public and the professionals. Some interesting topics have emerged from the discussion, however. The pros disagree on the power of black-red, with Dirk citing it as a reason to take Cabal Archon while Gabriel, Mike and Jeroen specifically support the archetype. Everyone agrees on the power of the cleric deck, but the value of Cabal Archon is in question. Ken seems to think it is very important, while the others who voiced an opinion disagree. They unanimously think Sparksmith would have been the correct pick out of the pack barring the presence of Slice and Dice, which makes it difficult to compile a list of the cards one might have taken over it. Assuming ignorance of the uncommon runs, the list could include Centaur Glade, Death Pulse, Infest, Improvised Armor, Lightning Rift, Smother and Venomspout Brackus. Determining the number of cards the player feeding you may have taken yields the odds of retaining color cooperation with your Sparksmith pick. This is critical if you want to cooperate and are willing to sacrifice card quality, but Kai and Dirk present a strong argument against that very theory. Their arguments combined with red's quality in *Legions* cement Sparksmith as the correct choice.

This week it is once again your first pick of an *Onslaught-Onslaught-Legions* booster draft. You do not know of any of the players. Which card do you draft?
