Looking for more ways to use your Titans? Kamiya Shogo used this blue, red, and green deck at a recent Standard tournament in Japan. The game plan was to control the flow on the ground by accelerating mana using Cultivate and Rampant Growth. Destructive Force helped keep things calm, and Frost Titan or Primeval Titan batted cleanup to finish the opponent off!
