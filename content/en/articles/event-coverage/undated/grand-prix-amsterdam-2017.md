## Grand Prix Amsterdam 2017

Day 2 Coverage

Earlier on Saturday morning, 1,170 players arrived in Amsterdam to play in the main event, either hoping to cast a turn-four Ulamog via Aetherworks Marvel or with a way to beat the archetype. In the end, it seemed that the combination of aggressive, humongous creatures with countermagic, discard, and/or artifact removal was good enough. The Top 8 featured one Aetherworks Marvel deck, four black-green variants, and three other decks: Esper Vehicles, Red-Green Pummeler, Blue-Red Control.

Among the four black-green variants, a Delirium/Constrictor variant stood out. Looking like an evolution of Shaun Mclaren’s deck from Grand Prix Montreal, it was piloted by teammates Lukas Blohon and Thomas Hendriks, both of whom made it to the Top 8. In the end, the Pro Tour Eldritch Moon champion bested everyone, giving him another trophy to take home.

Congratulations to Lukas Blohon, champion of Grand Prix Amsterdam 2017!
