Argentum Armor has drawn a lot of interest from players since being previewed as a part of the *Scars of Mirrodin* set. Now that tournaments can use the new set, players are finding neat ways to utilize the card! Check out Chet Norton's White Weenie deck from the [TCGPlayer.com](http://magic.tcgplayer.com/index.asp?wwwlink=yes) Championship Series held in Boston recently. By using lots of cheap creatures he's able to quickly pile up counters on his Quest for the Holy Relic. That in turn allows him to search up and equip the costly artifact. Soon after he's walloping his opponent for +6/+6 and the loss of a permanent every turn!
