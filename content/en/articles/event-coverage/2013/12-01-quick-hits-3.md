**Do you have a favourite niche archetype in *Theros* draft?**

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/smith_qh.jpg)

Robert Smith, GP Seattle 2012 Champ — *“Mono-red with 14 land and 3-4 [Dragon Mantle](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragon+Mantle)s. Play as many [Bronze Sable](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bronze+Sable)s and [Satyr Rambler](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Satyr+Rambler)s as you have to.“* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/turtenwald_qh.jpg)

Owen Turtenwald, Trophy Hunter — *“I don't have one, I just always force Blue-Black.”* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/samms_qh.jpg)

Phil Samms, Canadian Icon — *“Black-Blue Tempo. Just not hyper-aggressive.”* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/parke_qh.jpg)

Jamie Parke, Worlds 2008 Finalist — *“Black-Green. It has the most powerful cards, but you need to draft to survive the early game.”* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/jensen_qh.jpg)

William Jensen, Hall of Famer — *“Does blue-black count as "niche"? If not, then no.”* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/hayne_qh.jpg)

Alexander Hayne, Miracle Man — *“Blue-Red Scrye. [Flamespeaker Adept](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flamespeaker+Adept) is so powerful. [Aqueous Form](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aqueous+Form) is the card you really want, but you have access to a lot of Scry effects with [Omenspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Omenspeaker), [Titan's Strength](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Titan%27s+Strength), [Rage of Purphoros](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rage+of+Purphoros) and so on.”*
