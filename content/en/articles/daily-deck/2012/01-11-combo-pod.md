The Modern PTQ season is officially underway, and **Magic Online** is providing no shortage of tournaments for the new format. In a recent **Magic Online** Premier Event, Feefyfohfum brought a crazy Birthing Pod concoction to the table, cruising all the way to first place. The deck can both beat down by playing a midrange game, or just combo out of nowhere with Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker and either Pestermite or Deceiver Exarch. Could this deck be what Modern players have been waiting for? Try it out yourself in an upcoming [PTQ qualifier](/en/events/2012/barcelona12-qualifiers/qualifiers-for-pro-tour-avacyn-restored-in-barcelona)! 

Feefyfohfum's Combo Pod
