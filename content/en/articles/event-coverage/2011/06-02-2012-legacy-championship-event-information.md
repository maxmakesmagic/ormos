**Where**  
 GEN CON Indianapolis  
 Indiana Convention Center  
 TCG Hall  
 100 South Capitol Avenue  
 Indianapolis, Indiana

**Date/Time**  
 Saturday, August 18, 2012  
 10:00 AM

**Cost**  
 The entry fee for the 2012 **Magic: The Gathering** Legacy Championship is $24.00. Please note that admission to GEN CON is also required. Please visit [www.gencon.com](http://www.gencon.com/) for more information.

**Format**  
 

*   Legacy Format
*   No proxies of any kind are allowed
*   Open to all players

**Tournament Length**  
 

*   Swiss rounds, 60 minutes per round
*   Number of rounds based on attendance according to the following chart:

**Advancement**  
 

*   All players may participate in all Swiss rounds.
*   The Top 8 players after the final Swiss round will advance to the Top 8 single-elimination finals.

**Top 8**  
 

*   Top 8 matches will be best 2 of 3.
*   Top 8 matches will have no time limit.
*   Where necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will still be used to determine final order in the standings.

**Notes**  
 

*   REL Competitive, Planeswalker Points multiplier: 2x
*   All the **Magic: The Gathering** Tournament Rules and the **Magic: The Gathering** Infraction Procedure Guide are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document.
*   Players must bring pen, paper, card sleeves and appropriate counters.

**Prizes**  
 

*   Winner receives receives an alternate-art framed **Brainstorm trophy**
*   Second receives one set of *Revised Edition* Dual Lands (10 lands, 1 of each)
*   Boosters and other prizes for other top finishers:

*   3rd – 4th place: *From the Vault: Exiled* box set, 8 Italian *Legends* boosters and 2 Chinese *Portal: Three Kingdoms* boosters.
*   5th through 8th place: 4 Italian *Legends* boosters and 1 Chinese *Portal: Three Kingdoms* booster.

Other Legacy-format public events will be offered at Gen Con. You can check out the full schedule at [www.pastimes.net](http://www.pastimes.net/).
