As you might have heard, R&D members fanned out and went to local Friday Night **Magic** events last weekend. And they showed off *Rise of the Eldrazi* Intro Packs! So we thought now would be a good time to show you the decklists. Note that the actual *Rise of the Eldrazi* cards will remain a mystery for now. But at least you get the card names!
