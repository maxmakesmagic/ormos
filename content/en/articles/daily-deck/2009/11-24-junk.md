Matej (mah-tie) Zatlkaj (zaht-uhl-kye) is one of the nicest players on the Pro Tour, and with a Top 8 at Pro Tour-Berlin last year, proved he's one of the better players as well. At the 2009 World Championships, his deck of choice was a Junk aggro deck. It featured many of the game's most powerful green and white cards (Elspeth, Knight-Errant, Baneslayer Angel, Lotus Cobra) but also included a touch of black for Maelstrom Pulse. For more on the 2009 World Championships, [visit the official event coverage](/en/events/2009/worlds09/all-roads-lead-to-victory-for-coimbra-china).
