While this is the end of *Dragons of Tarkir*’s turn in the spotlight, *Magic Origins* is ready to take over. With its card image gallery filled to the brim just before Grand Prix Montreal, several players have already turned their thoughts forward to what’s coming soon.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmon15/gpmon15_qh_hayne-sigrist.jpg)  
*Alexander Hayne and Mike Sigrist*

“I’m excited for the new [Siege-Gang Commander](https://web.archive.org/web/20150705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege-Gang+Commander), Chandra’s parents,” No. 17-ranked Alexander Hayne said, referring to Pia and Kiran Nalaar. Mike Sigrist, staring Hayne down on the right above, had a different tack in mind: “Erebos’s Titan.”

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/origins_askdf9aj2399v/en_agXuQWdNoH.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/origins_askdf9aj2399v/en_ZFYxrThEf5.png)  
*Pia and Kiran Nalaar/Erebos's Titan*

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmon15/gpmon15_qh_lax.jpg)  
*Ari Lax*

“There are too many cards,” No 7-ranked and Pro Tour *Khans of Tarkir* champion Ari Lax said, with a grin on his face. “There are literally twenty cards I’m excited for. Each of them do something interesting.”

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmon15/gpmon15_qh_egashira.jpg)  
*Kenji Egashira*

Kenji Egashira, better known to say hello of friends as *Magic Online* streamer Numot the Nummy, knew exactly what we wanted to see first. “The 6/6 with renowned 6,” he said, meaning the biggest of the baddies coming in *Origins*: Outland Colossus.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/origins_askdf9aj2399v/en_wAl5Htekhk.png)  
*Outland Colossus*
