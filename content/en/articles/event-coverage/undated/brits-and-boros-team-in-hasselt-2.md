Grand Prix Hasselt is over. All 1066 competitors managed to fight through the cold to get to the venue, but only one managed to fight through the others to get to the trophy: Sam Gomersall.

In the last premier event using the triple-Ravnica draft format, a Boros deck won once again. It was the first time in seven years that an Englishmen won a Grand Prix - back then it was some long-haired heavy-metal guy whose playing skills are best described as "rubbish." This time, the title was well deserved.

Quite a few former world champions had shown up, but in the end, none of them got in. We had a fairly inexperienced top 8, that was won by the most experienced of them.

The European pros will be getting another chance soon, as GP Dortmund is only three weeks away - but by then, Guildpact will be added to the mix, and we will have a completely different format. It will be exciting to see who can adjust fast enough.

## Sunday, January 29: 7:33 p.m. - Top 8 Drafting with Sam Gomersall

*by Rogier Maaten*

As Sam is the most experienced player in this top8 (he now has three GP top8's under his belt, while the other seven players had only two GP quarterfinal appearance combined), I decided to cover his draft.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b4/d1/b4d1d517c53d6b946754f4cd6422fa4b225e650c9a10755519ffeb61d20f3a9a.jpg)*Half of the Top 8 Draft table, Sam Gomersall at far right*

Opening one of the best cards in the set, which also turns out to be in the guild that is wide open is quite nice. First pick [Flame Fusillade](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flame+Fusillade) over some green cards ([Carven Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Carven+Caryatid), [Pollenbright Wings](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pollenbright+Wings), [Farseek](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Farseek)) was followed by [Veteran Armorer](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Veteran+Armorer) out of a mediocre booster (Julien Goron, on Sam's right took [Drift of Phantasms](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drift+of+Phantasms) from this pack). In the next booster [Bramble Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bramble+Elemental) was the card Sam drafted, above [Scatter the Seeds](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scatter+the+Seeds). The next few picks were all Boros: [Galvanic Arc](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Arc) 4th, then [Wojek Embermage](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wojek+Embermage), Sell Sworn Brute and [Flash Conscription](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flash+Conscription). Finally a [Frenzied Goblin](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frenzied+Goblin) came 13th.

Sam proved his skills at opening booster again, as this time Moldervince Cloak was an easy pick. The cloak is powerful enough to splash for, and a good first pick. Goron replied with a [Stasis Cell](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stasis+Cell) that would keep the Fangtail from whittling down his life total.

The game had ground down to a stall that could only favor Goron. [Flight of Fancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flight+of+Fancy) took a [Tidewater Minion](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidewater+Minion) to the skies and found a second Entrancer. [Clinging Darkness](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clinging+Darkness) removed the threat of the Trumpeter and there really didn't seem like any way for Gomersall to pull out of this.

Goron used the [Tidewater Minion](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidewater+Minion) and Entrancer to rip four cards from Gomersall's library and he conceded to save time.

Gomersall 1-1 Goron

And so it comes down to the final game. To win Gomersall would have to bust through enough early damage before Goron locked up the game with his high toughness monsters. In this game he would at least have the advantage of going first.

A [Sell-Sword Brute](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sell-Sword+Brute) showed up on turn two but only got to attack once before the inevitable [Drift of Phantasms](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drift+of+Phantasms) appeared on Goron's side. The Englishman went to plan B and summoned a [Viashino Fangtail](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viashino+Fangtail). A missed land drop proved very painful for Goron as it enabled to Gomersall to get a [Firemane Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firemane+Angel) into play without having to worry about [Induce Paranoia](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Induce+Paranoia). Against the first-striking Angel a [Stinkweed Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stinkweed+Imp) was an irrelevance.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a4/f8/a4f83677575a0db3aed274464166019387ddd4ca26d9b556c084f7a0a6c895b5.jpg)*Sam Gomersall is the 2006 Garnd Prix Hasselt Champion!*

I think it was pretty much game at that point. The Angel was simply too big for Goron to block. It got in for two hits before the Imp had to sacrifice itself and block.

[Disembowel](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disembowel) gave the Frenchman some hope as he could take out the Fangtail and bring the Drift into the game as a blocker. That plan went down in flames as [Rally the Righteous](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rally+the+Righteous) ripped the Angel straight through the wall. A [Vedalken Dismisser](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vedalken+Dismisser) bounced the Angel to buy a turn, but it couldn't even done that as Gomersall simply showed the [Flame Fusillade](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130191642/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flame+Fusillade) he was holding in his hand.

Sam Gomersall beats Julien Goron 2-1 and is champion of Grand Prix Hasselt.
