It's almost July, so it's time to announce the promos that Friday Night Magic players can look forward to over the next three months. They are shiny, and soon they can all be yours.

**July**

Play in an FNM this July, and this could be you! Wait, yours, not you. (Wizards of the Coast does not endorse, condone, or engage in freaky magical person-stretching.) This painful-looking take on [Spatial Contortion](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spatial+Contortion) from Joseph Meehan is July's promo card, and it's ready to twist your opponent's creatures into oblivion—or your own into damage-dealing monstrosities!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/idfSc4kUN4.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/c4rd4r7_idfSc4kUN4.jpg)

[Spatial Contortion](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spatial+Contortion) Promo | Art by [Joseph Meehan](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Joseph+Meehan%22%5d)

**August**

As if you need another reason to side with the Eldrazi. Last month was [Spatial Contortion](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spatial+Contortion); this month is one of the best ways to cast it. Throw in a touch of colored mana, and you have a pretty versatile land with pretty art from one Jonas De Ro.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/QBLLRzefys.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/QBLLRzefys.jpg)

[Crumbling Vestige](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crumbling+Vestige) Promo | Art by [Jonas De Ro](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Jonas+De+Ro%22%5d)

**September**

The Eldrazi hits just keep on coming. Didn't the Gatewatch win? Yet here we are months later still facing down some [Flaying Tendrils](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flaying+Tendrils) with new art from Svetlin Velinov.
