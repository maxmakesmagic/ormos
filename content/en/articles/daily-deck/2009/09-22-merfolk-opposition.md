Alex Borteh made the Top 8 of U.S. Nationals, then a few months later repeated the feat at the World Championships with his innovative Merfolk Opposition deck. Early Merfolk backed by a smattering of counters allowed him to control the battlefield. His end game featured Opposition to lock his opponent out of the game. [Read more about the event in the official event coverage archives](https://web.archive.org/web/20090922000000/http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/event.asp?event=worlds2001).
