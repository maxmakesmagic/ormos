*Khans of Tarkir* Known Issues

The following are a list of known issues around the release of *Khans of Tarkir* on *Magic Online*. We are working right now to resolve everything on the below list. In the meantime, we want to get this list out to players so they are aware of what we're up to and so they know how to avoid problematic bugs. As of now, the entirety of the below list have planned fixes launching on Wednesday, October 22, during downtime.

*   [Sidisi, Brood Tyrant](https://web.archive.org/web/20141014000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sidisi%2C+Brood+Tyrant)'s trigger creates multiple zombies if multiple creatures are put into the graveyard at the same time.
*   When activating abilities mana should be automatically consumed
*   Raid does not trigger when creatures are placed onto the battlefield from the opponent's library
*   [Ashcloud Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20141014000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashcloud+Phoenix) returns under the wrong player's control when controlled by a player other than its owner
*   [Clever Impersonator](https://web.archive.org/web/20141014000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clever+Impersonator) doesn't trigger the raid ability of the opponent's creature that it copies
*   If a planeswalker would take trample damage, and is removed from the battlefield before damage, it crashes the client
*   Some cards that were printed at a rarity other than common outside of *Magic Online* are showing up as legal in the Pauper format, even though they are not commons on *Magic Online*.

Morph Clarification

Lately, there has been some confusion around how the morph mechanic works on *Magic Online*. Players have been asking, "Why would I want my morph overlay to have the foil treatment?" Or, "If a morph card is bounced, why does it return to the same position on the battlefield?" This concern is justified, as both of these things could give your opponent valuable information as to which morphs you have lying in wait. Fortunately, your opponent doesn't know either of these things!

Both of the aforementioned morph effects are only visible to the morph's owner. To your opponent, your foil morph will look just like any other morph overlay. When a bounced morph returns to the field, it will be in a random position to your opponent, but retain the same position it previously did from your point of view. Check out all of the morph improvements we made with the implementation of *Khans of Tarkir* in Digital Editor, Allison Medwin's, most recent [article](/en/articles/magiconline/2014/09-16-r-d-feature-khan-we-make-it-better-a-magic-online-story).

Extended Downtime on October 22

There will be an extended downtime on Wednesday, October 22, for *Magic Online*. Events close at 12:00 a.m. Pacific (07:00 UTC), midnight, and store activity and trades end at 2:45 a.m. Pacific (09:45 UTC) leading up to the system coming down at 3:00 a.m. Pacific (10:00 UTC).

Please plan ahead!

Downtime for October 15, 2014

*   All tournaments will close at 5:00 a.m. Pacific (12:00 UTC)
*   Store and trade activity will be suspended at approximately 7:45 a.m. Pacific (14:45 UTC)
*   The system will be down from 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. Pacific (15:00 to 19:00 UTC)
