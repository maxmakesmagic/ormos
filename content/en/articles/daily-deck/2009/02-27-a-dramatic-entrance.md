Dramatic Entrance? Impromptu Raid? Your eyes might not believe it, but those were the exact cards Ajnar25 used to put giant fatties into play for significantly less than cost. The payoff? Fifth place in a recent **Magic Online** Premier Event. What does the future hold for Ajnar's Mono-Green build? No one can say for certain, but a likely bet would be that it will rhyme with "Brogenitus."
