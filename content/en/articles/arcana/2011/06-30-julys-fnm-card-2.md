It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20110630000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=events/magic/fnm)! And since Friday is the first day of July, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

![Jace's Ingenuity](https://i.ormos.uk/f7/97/f79778adc314f3c7102534a3d8ab2d8ede6f3806dfe6fa39f1370c42c684a362.jpg)  

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards often feature original flavor text and updated wordings.

Since tomorrow is the first of July, that means the [Standard bannings](https://web.archive.org/web/20110630000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/feature/148a) go into effect. To remind you, that means that [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110630000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) is banned. And [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20110630000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic) is banned *unless it's part of the [War of Attrition](https://web.archive.org/web/20110630000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/new-phyrexia-event-decks-deck-lists-2011-05-19) deck list*. If this is the first you're hearing of this, [Aaron Forsythe's explanation](https://web.archive.org/web/20110630000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/feature/148) is highly recommended.
