**Question: What *Torment* common is your first draft pick?**

[Faceless Butcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20020216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faceless+Butcher). The only time I wouldn't consider picking it is if I'm drafting white-black.  
 -**Itaru Ishida**

[Basking Rootwalla](https://web.archive.org/web/20020216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basking+Rootwalla). It's probably the best card for a red-green deck-easily as good as [Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20020216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel), maybe better.  
 -**Katsuhiro Mori**

Probably [Faceless Butcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20020216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faceless+Butcher). It's just plain good.  
 -**Masahiko Morita**
