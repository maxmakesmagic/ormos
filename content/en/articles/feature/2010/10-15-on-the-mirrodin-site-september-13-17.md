Welcome back to "On the Mirrodin Site," where we show off some of the recent highlights from the [Mirrodin Site](https://web.archive.org/web/20140223104428/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/).

### Field Reports

When the Mirrodin Site launched, it contained [field reports](https://web.archive.org/web/20140223104428/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/#blogs) from three characters: [Ria of Bladehold](https://web.archive.org/web/20140223104428/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/#!blog,499), [Farris of the Anvil](https://web.archive.org/web/20140223104428/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/#!blog,475), and [Kessla of Temple Might](https://web.archive.org/web/20140223104428/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/#!blog,472). Recently, one character's story received another chapter:

*   [Farris of the Anvil's story](https://web.archive.org/web/20140223104428/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/#!blog,533), part 2

Two new characters' stories have begun to be told, but they're on the Phyrexian Faction. Spooky!

*   [Roxith, Thane of Rot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140223104428/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/#!blog,538), part 1
*   [Unctus of the Synod](https://web.archive.org/web/20140223104428/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/#!blog,537), part 2
*   [Unctus of the Synod](https://web.archive.org/web/20140223104428/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/#!blog,565), part 1

That's only a brief sampling (we didn't even mention the [awesome concept art](https://web.archive.org/web/20140223104428/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/#swamps,hotspots;B4;0;)), so be sure to check the [Mirrodin Site](https://web.archive.org/web/20140223104428/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/) every day to see what's new. And check back here on Friday to see if there's anything you missed.
