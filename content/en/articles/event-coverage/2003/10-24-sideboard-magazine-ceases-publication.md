After seven years, Sideboard magazine has ceased publication as of its 49th issue (now available). People with outstanding subscriptions will receive a full $18 refund. With 97% of our player base on the internet, we felt it would be more effective to devote our resources to delivering more timely content through Magicthegathering.com. By shifting our content to an online venue, will have greater flexibility to offer localized magic content for people around the world.

We plan to combine the content on Sideboard.com with MagicTheGathering.com (read more [about the plan](/en/articles/feature/2003/10-08-the-future-of-magicthegathering-com)); for now, Sideboard.com is on a hiatus, with no updates planned except for live Event Coverage.

The next events to be covered are Grand Prix Lyon (October 25-26), Pro Tour New Orleans (October 31-November 2), and Grand Prix Shizuoka (November 8-9).
