*Zendikar* Game Day has come and gone for 2009. Tim Green of [Carrollton, Georgia](https://web.archive.org/web/20091105000000/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Magic/Magazine/Events.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/zendikargameday/carrollton) took an innovative Standard build of Goblins all the way to the top 4. The deck featured powerful Goblins like Warren Instigator as well as Goblin Razerunners and Siege-Gang Commander. A tiny splash of white allows for Intimidation Bolt and Path to Exile to deal with opponent's blockers. You can see many more Standard deck lists from Game Day [here](/en/events/2009/zendikargameday/zendikar-game-day-2).
