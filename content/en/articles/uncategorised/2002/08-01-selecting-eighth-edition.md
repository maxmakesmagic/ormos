Our Week 7 vote was a three-way battle between Worship, Pariah, and Kismet. I saw some message board threads comparing Pariah to Ralph Nader (Nader a pariah? Hmmm…), its only purpose being to leech votes from the more-worthy Worship, allowing Kismet to steal the vote.

Fear not, Worship fans, for the love is strong, and the life-saving enchantment will live again in *Eighth Edition*.

On the art side, the *Seventh Edition* Goblin Chariot beat out the *Starter* version, and it will get to keep its chair in the base set.

We're still sorting through flavor text submissions from the first entries (Aladdin's Ring and Orcish Artillery), but we're ready to accept even more! This week's card is Verduran Enchantress, a classic "Johnny" card, which will appear in *Eighth Edition* sporting Rob Alexander's *Seventh Edition* art. We want player-written text on this one, folks, so get cracking and submit here.

The art vote this week is another showdown between two pieces by the same artist. Mark Tedin did both the *Urza's Saga* and *Seventh Edition* illustrations for Phyrexian Colossus, and we want to know which one of those should appear in *Eighth*.

![Phyrexian Colossus](https://i.ormos.uk/6f/1f/6f1f33c13f7a04558dcd989c21df3a581286442ee7f8bd629fc8b2c028d4f821.jpg)

As for the card choice, we're sticking with red on the heels of Jokulhaups and Obliterate (which is still accepting votes, by the way). Nonbasic land "hosers" have never been a big part of the base set, but R&D wants to include one this time around. But which one? The old school Blood Moon, last seen in *Chronicles*, or the pesky 1/2 Dwarven Miner from *Mirage*? Remember that the one that wins will be reintroduced to Extended -- as well as Standard -- when *Eighth Edition* is released…

![Blood Moon vs. Dwarven Miner](https://i.ormos.uk/ed/48/ed48ae9aefb66bc6e5b731aafc59444df298814b78ba340eae4cd4bc2fc8e621.jpg)

And that's that for this week… Remember that your vote counts! Be sure to cast it [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20050312043028/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/vote8e/home), and tune in next week for another exciting set of choices!
