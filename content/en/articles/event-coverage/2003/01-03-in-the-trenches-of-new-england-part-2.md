This is the second installment in my series on New England Pro Tour Qualifiers. This week’s tournament was one of my PTQs (I was the event organizer). I held it at the Knights of Columbus hall in Brighton, MA. The Knights has been a hot spot for **Magic** tournaments since the early days of PTQs (to the endless delight of the pizza shop next door). 

We had 99 players, which is about average for Boston (around 160 is the most I’ve drawn, and our worst event was in the 50s). Seven of these players almost missed the event entirely. The first vehicle in their caravan got into an accident on the way to the PTQ. It was bad enough that an ambulance had been called, but it turned out all were fine. Not to be deterred my such a minor event, they pressed on. Unfortunately, they didn’t arrive until the first round had begun. Given the circumstances, we allowed them in with round one losses. You have got to respect the determination of **Magic** players.

I must admit, I’ve really enjoyed working on this series so far. It’s been really good to spend time talking to the PTQ players. I’ve heard (and watched) some great stories, and seen interesting deck choices and tech. The only sad part for me is that I do more interviews than I can put into the articles. For all the players who took the time to talk to me who are not featured here: thank you. I’ll try to work you in next time.

JJ loves his Enchantress decks. While he has never qualified for the Pro Tour, he has made multiple PTQ Top 8s playing Enchantress in Extended. Besides the PTQ finishes, JJ’s claims to **Magic** fame are qualifying for U.S. Nationals in 1999 and being the 1998 Connecticut State Champion.

He is a Your Move Games Providence player, and works with a team that calls themselves “Tech House”. The challenge of events like PTQs is what keeps him in the game. “If there were not events of this nature, I would give up **Magic**.” This was JJ’s first PTQ of the season, and he’s off to a good start.

Rob: “Who’s on team Tech House?

JJ: “Myself, Rich Shay, Andy Lamb, and Bowman Horton.”

Rob: “How did you guys prepare for the PTQ?”

JJ: “Separately, for the most part. I play Enchantress during the Extended season no matter what, so my team decided to go other routes.”

Rob: “Why were you so sure you wanted to play Enchantress?

JJ: “I’ve been playing it for years. I did well with it last year. I got a 9th place and two Top 8’s. All of the bad matchups are gone now, so...”

Rob: “When was the last time you played a deck other than Enchantress in Extended?”

JJ: “ Two years ago, I played Trix.”

Rob: “Do you always play combo in Extended?”

JJ: “Well, I guess you could consider Enchantress a combo deck. In that case, yes.”
