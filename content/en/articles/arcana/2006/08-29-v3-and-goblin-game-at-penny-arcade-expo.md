***Magic** Online* Version 3.0 was demoed at last weekend’s [Penny Arcade Expo (PAX)](https://web.archive.org/web/20060829000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.pennyarcadeexpo.com/&origin=mtgcom_arcana_1152), a convention run by the guys behind the [Penny Arcade web comic](https://web.archive.org/web/20060829000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.penny-arcade.com/&origin=mtgcom_arcana_1152). Visitors to the Bellevue, Washington convention got to see screenshots of V3 and see the interface in action.

But that’s not all.

### “The Goblin Game”

PAX was also the site of Wizards of the Coast’s announcement of a new game code-named “The Goblin Game.” Wizards’ next foray into the online world, “The Goblin Game” is a web-based strategy game in which factions of goblins vie for dominance across a fantasy empire.

The yet-to-be-officially-named “Goblin Game” will hold open beta this December.
