*Updated May 23, 2013* 

![Magic: the Gathering Pro Tour](https://i.ormos.uk/72/33/7233580ab42233846709a253f2c477d01df8a3ce0e4a4de5f531a79594c6b5fb.jpg "Magic: the Gathering Pro Tour") 

Congratulations to [Josh Utter-Leyton, the 2012-13 Pro Tour Player of the Year](/en/events/2013/ptdgm13/player-of-the-year-josh-utter-leyton)! The player with the most Pro Points over the course of a season is identified as the Pro Player of the Year. This page shows all players who received at least 10 Pro Points during the 2012-2013 Pro season (May 14, 2012 to May 19, 2013). 

In addition, the points listed below are the Professional Points totals that will be used to determine 2013-14 Pro Players Club levels and invitations to top tournaments.

*   [2012-2013 Rookie of the Year Race](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2012-2013-rookie-of-the-year-standings)
