**Question: What *Torment* common is your first draft pick?** 

Faceless Butcher. The only time I wouldn't consider picking it is if I'm drafting white-black.  
 -**Itaru Ishida**

Basking Rootwalla. It's probably the best card for a red-green deck-easily as good as Wild Mongrel, maybe better.  
 -**Katsuhiro Mori**

Probably Faceless Butcher. It's just plain good.  
 -**Masahiko Morita**
