<table><tbody><tr><td><h3>HAWAII CONVENTION CENTER</h3> MARCH 3-5, 2006</td></tr></tbody></table>

Artists will be signing from 10 a.m. - 1 p.m. and 2 p.m. - 5 p.m. on March 3-5.

![](http://www.wizards.com/events/images/Glen_Angus.jpg)**Glen Angus**  
 Glen Angus has just recently moved into the video game biz by starting at Raven as a digital painter/concept artist. The previous 10 years have been divided between teaching as a professor of Graphic Design at his hometown college in Canada, and painting fantasy art for the role-playing and card game industry. Much of his work can be found on **Magic: The Gathering** cards, in **Dungeons & Dragons** books, and Dragonlance covers. Glen also dabbles in the Children's art genre-doing work for Cricket magazine, Harry Potter packaging, and personal pieces like The "Filling Valhalla" poster (winner of the 2001 Summit Illustration award (www.summitawards.com) Check out his work at www.gangus.net.

![](http://www.wizards.com/events/images/rk_post2.jpg)**rk post**  
 Just prior to graduation from Northern Illinois University in 1994, rk post started freelancing the fantasy gaming industry doing interior illustration for several major game companies, gradually teaching himself how to paint with acrylics and eventually oils. rk garnered a staff illustration position at TSR (a company purchased by Wizards of the Coast soon after) in the fall of 1996 painting cover illustrations for games and novels. WotC and rk have parted company in the winter of 2000 and now he doubles as a full time freelance illustrator still contracting occasional assignments through the subsidary of Hasbro, Inc., and as a 2D and 3D artist for Gas Powered Games.  
 rk has contracted and work through White Wolf, WizKids, Fanpro, Microsoft (Mythica, X Box), LucasFilm, 20th Century Fox (Aliens VS Predator), Sega (Dreamcast), Nintendo, Ballantine Books, Science Fiction Book Club, Hasbro (televsion and animation), Marvel Entertainment, DC Comics, the History Channel, and Blizzard Ent (Daiblo 2). rk post has his collected works available in a hard cover art book, Postmortem: the Art of rk post through Cartouche Press (www.cartouchepress.com). New and continuing work can also be seen at www.rkpost.net.
