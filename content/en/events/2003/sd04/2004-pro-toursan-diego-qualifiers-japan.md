### Entry Fee

The entry fee for all Pro Tour—San Diego Qualifier tournaments in Japan is ¥3,500.

### Tournament Structure

The structure for Pro Tour—San Diego Qualifier tournaments will be modified Swiss-style, with the Top 8 players playing in a single-elimination format for prizes and invitations. 

### Tournament Format

The format for Pro Tour—San Diego Qualifiers tournaments is *Mirrodin*TM Block Limited. For information on this format, please refer to the DCITM[Tournament Rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20051218140009/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dci/utr/intro) page.

### Invitations and Eligibility

Players already holding an invitation to Pro Tour—San Diego are prohibited from participating in any Pro Tour—San Diego qualifiers, with the exception of Grand Prix tournaments. Player may have earned invitations through cumulative past performance in the Pro Tour, winning a previous Qualifier or Grand Prix tournament, or by being an invited DCITM player (valid upon the date of the official ratings invitation announcement for the appropriate professional tournament.) Please refer to the [Pro Tour—San Diego Invitation List](/en/events/2003/sd04/2004-pro-toursan-diego#invites) for a list of qualified players.

### Prizes

•
*   Invitation(s) to Pro Tour—San Diego (the exact number of invitations is determined by tournament location; see below)•
*   The winner of each Pro Tour—San Diego Qualifier tournament will receive a cash prize of US $500. (Cash prize awards will be distributed to all winners at the end of March.)•
*   Product prizes will be awarded to the Top 8 players of each Pro Tour—San Diego Qualifier tournament as follows: The winner of each tournament will receive 32 booster packs of *Darksteel*TM. The finalist will receive 20 booster packs of *Darksteel*. The 3rd- and 4th-place players will each receive 15 booster packs, the 5th- and 6th-place players will each receive 8 booster packs, and the 7th- and 8th-place players will receive 5 booster packs each.

### Registration Information

Preregistration is required for all Pro Tour—San Diego Qualifier tournaments. Players must register no later than 10 days before the event they wish to participate in. Players may register by contacting Ron Foster, Wizards of the Coast Japan Organized Play Manager, at ron.foster@wizards.com
