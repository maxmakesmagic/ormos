Looking for a fun way to sneak Emrakul, the Aeons Torn onto the battlefield? Why not consider the hideaway lands from the *Lorwyn* block? With Windbrisk Heights and Mosswort Bridge, you'll have yourself a 15/15 in no time! As a backup, just in case you don't find one of your lands, you can try to hit with Summoning Trap. Of course, Knight of the Reliquary helps to make sure you've always got the land you want, so it shouldn't be that difficult!
