## *THE BROTHERS' WAR* SET MASTERY

*   33x *The Brothers' War* packs
*   5x Mastery Orbs (Each can be redeemed for a card style or sleeve on *The Brothers' War* Mastery Tree.)

## *THE BROTHERS' WAR* MASTERY PASS

![BRO Mastery Pass](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/82nDyweny1.png)

### AVATAR

*   Urzan Automaton

### CARDS AND PACKS

*   20x Packs (4x, *The Brothers' War*, 4x *Dominaria United*, 3x *Streets of New Capenna*, 3x *Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty*, 3x *Innistrad: Crimson Vow*, 3x *Innistrad: Midnight Hunt*)
*   10x *The Brothers' War* mythic rare individual card rewards (ICRs)
*   Level 81+: 1x Uncommon ICR

### CARD SLEEVES

*   *1x The Brothers' War* card sleeve
*   1x Exquisite *The Brothers' War* card sleeve
*   5x Additional *The Brothers' War* card sleeves (obtainable from *The Brothers' War* Mastery Tree)

### CARD STYLES

*   25x Mastery Orbs (Each can be redeemed for a card style or sleeve on the *The Brothers' War* Mastery Tree.)
*   15x Common card styles
*   10x Uncommon card styles

### EVENT TOKENS

*   1 Player Draft token (Can be redeemed for either a Premier or Traditional Draft)

### GOLD AND GEMS

*   4,000 Gold
*   1,200 Gems

### PETS

*   Common Urzan Soldier
*   Uncommon Urzan Soldier
*   Rare Urzan Soldier

## HOW MANY LEVELS ARE THERE IN *THE BROTHERS' WAR* SET MASTERY?

*The Brothers' War* set mastery goes up to level 80. All players receive rewards through level 66, while players with the Mastery Pass will receive rewards up to level 80—and beyond!
