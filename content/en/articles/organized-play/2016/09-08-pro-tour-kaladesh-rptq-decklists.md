# Pro Tour *Kaladesh* RPTQ Top 8 Decklists

We have a few sets of Top 8 decklists for you today from the Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers (RPTQs) that took place on August 21 and August 29. The Top 4 players from each of these RPTQs earned themselves invitations and airfare to compete in [Pro Tour *Kaladesh*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-kaladesh-event-information), which takes place October 14–16 in Honolulu, Hawaii.

Every player who participated in an RPTQ during August's rounds received this program-exclusive deck box.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/3RxJ18J5bV_box.png)

And the Top 16 players from these RPTQs also received a program-exclusive playmat, featuring the *Kaladesh* key art with Saheeli Rai.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/h9W61XJDhk_playmat.png)
