Another great event in Kobe, Japan has crowned its champion, and the victor was hardly a first-timer on the professional stage. No, the champion was former Player of the Year Shouta Yasooka, who defeated former World Champion Makihito Mihara in the finals. Yasooka didn't lose a game in the Top 8, sweeping each match he played. His powerful deck of choice was Faeries. Read [full coverage](/en/events/2011/gpkob11/holy-cow-shouta-back-on-top) of the event in the archives.
