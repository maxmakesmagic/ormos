Nathan Holiday showed us that, as when making omelets, to win a Modern tournament you've got to break a few Eggs. Meanwhile, in Europe, a trio of Swedes rose to the top of a Team Limited tournament by… beating another team of Swedes. Congratulations go to Nathan for his victory at [Grand Prix San Diego](https://web.archive.org/web/20130322000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/holiday-serves-eggs-gp-san-diego) and to Jonathan Bergström, Tomas Westling, and Fredrik Carlsson for defeating their countrymen to win [Grand Prix Utrecht](https://web.archive.org/web/20130322000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/swedes-sweep-grand-prix-utrecht). Going back to Modern, that's the format for this season's PTQs to get you into Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze*! Check out recent PTQ results to get an idea of the Modern metagame and find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#dgm)! Good luck; have fun!
