Stone Rain is the most frequently-reprinted card in **Magic**. This champion of reprinting appeared in 18 sets, beating out other cardboard-happy contenders like Counterspell and Disenchant (neither of which made the cut to *Eighth Edition*). Specifically, Stone Rain was printed in: 

*   all the base sets from *Alpha* to *Eighth Edition*,
*   plus the large sets *Ice Age*, *Mirage*, *Tempest*, and *Mercadian Masques*,
*   plus the starter-level sets *Portal*, *Portal Second Age*, *Portal Three Kingdoms*, *Starter 1999* and *Starter 2000*.

The question is, can you identify **Stone Rain** from its art through the ages?
