Let's talk about Thespian's Stage. As you may already know, "thespian" is a highfalutin' word for "actor," and the card shows two actors, well, acting:

 ![Thespian's Stage](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Thespian%27s%20Stage&options=) 

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/72/6f/726f689a6c3a04fea0bc932408a70d720d1e432b225991f539584566bab315a6.jpg) 

Thespian's Stage | Art by [John Avon](https://web.archive.org/web/20130131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22john%20avon%22])

Now, because this is Arcana, you may have already guessed that the actors are significant. In fact, they're acting out an important moment from the history of Ravnica. Let's look closer!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3e/8e/3e8eebb5c03a51d36ea8b1103db83be4a7865f8a346c02698a81852195ac6c76.jpg)

It's Szadek, Lord of Secrets and Agrus Kos, Wojek Veteran, from the original *Ravnica* block! And they're fighting, which means this is probably the moment when Agrus Kos arrests Szadek, breaking the Guildpact!

You can see what it's like to watch this play in [the Uncharter Realms article](/en/articles/feature/2013/01-16-the-absolution-of-the-guildpact) that previewed Thespian's Stage. And you can read the whole story of the original *Ravnica* Cycle in eBook form. More information is [right here](https://web.archive.org/web/20130711060011/http://www.wizards.com/magic/merchandise/product.aspx?x=mtg/merchandise/ebooks).
