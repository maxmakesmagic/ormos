Zainuddin looked extraordinarily hesitant about his decision to keep his opening hand. In the end, he knocked thrice on his library and kept, and was presumably rewarded with a land when he breathed a huge sigh of relief, and summoned [Vaporkin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vaporkin), and followed it with an [Ordeal of Nylea](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ordeal+of+Nylea). Sugaya had a [Phalanx Leader](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phalanx+Leader) and a [Crackling Triton](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crackling+Triton) to keep pace.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gpkl14/r12_zainuddin.jpg)

Sugaya, under pressure from the increasingly enormous flier, cast a [Cavalry Pegasus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cavalry+Pegasus) and used a [Voyage's End](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyage%27s+End) on the [Vaporkin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vaporkin) to both stem the beatings and stop Zainuddin from getting too far ahead on lands. Zainuddin had a [Nylea's Disciple](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nylea%27s+Disciple) to clog the ground, then bestowed a [Nimbus Naiad](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nimbus+Naiad) on it and attacked. Sugaya took the damage for a turn before casting a [Gift of Immortality](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gift+of+Immortality) on his Pegasus, drawing a wry look from Zainuddin as he pondered how to get past the now-recursive blocker.

Zainuddin re-cast his [Vaporkin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vaporkin) and found a pair of [Fleetfeather Sandals](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleetfeather+Sandals), while Sugaya summoned a [Wingsteed Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingsteed+Rider), [Silent Artisan](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silent+Artisan) and a [Traveling Philosopher](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Traveling+Philosopher). Both players were slowed to a crawl by relatively weak offences and relatively strong defence on both sides of the board. An [Aqueous Form](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aqueous+Form) for Zainuddin resolved the stalemate and let him take the first game in short order.

**Shamsul Bahrin Zainuddin 1 - Hironobu Sugaya 0**

Both players summoned out cheap creatures but couldn't find any profitable attacks, Zainuddin with a [Vaporkin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vaporkin) and a [Nessian Courser](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nessian+Courser), Sugaya with a [Cavalry Pegasus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cavalry+Pegasus), [Lagonna-Band Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lagonna-Band+Elder) and a Wingsteed Pegasus. Zainuddin bestowed a [Leafcrown Dryad](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leafcrown+Dryad) on his [Vaporkin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vaporkin), making an imposing flier and crashing it in at Sugaya.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gpkl14/r12_sugaya.jpg)

Sugaya visibly considered attacking back with his team, prompting Zainuddin to throw his hands up in the air to try and dissuade him. Sugaya, smiling, reconsidered and just sent his fliers over, leaing his Elder back to slow down the Courser if necessary. Sugaya summoned a [Soldier of the Pantheon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soldier+of+the+Pantheon), while Zainuddin went for a [Prophet of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prophet+of+Kruphix), which was promptly [Voyage's End](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyage%27s+End)ed.

Sugaya tried a [Gift of Immortality](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gift+of+Immortality) on his [Wingsteed Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingsteed+Rider), building it to a 4/4 before attacking with his Rider, Pegasus and [Soldier of the Pantheon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soldier+of+the+Pantheon). Zainuddin flashed in a bestowed [Nimbus Naiad](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nimbus+Naiad), creating a tremendous blocker for both the Soldier and the Pegasus. Sugaya shook his head in frustration and flashed a God's Willing that he had been holding in anticipation of, well, anything but a surprise 5/5 flying blocker. Regardless of his choices, Sugaya could get Zainuddin to 1 - not enough to stop him from swinging back for lethal the next turn.

**Shamsul Bahrin Zainuddin 2 - Hironobu Sugaya 0**
