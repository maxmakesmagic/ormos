In Japan, Wizards distributes provides extra prize support for "**Magic** Premier Shops" that contribute to the game and the community above and beyond the level of a normal retail store. That means keeping a certain level of inventory on hand, running more than the usual number of in-store events, and periodically pulling in new players.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8f/6f/8f6ff9806d85a1b97550042697810b24d43d4020f50fb9a0b19fcaffe69fe6f5.jpg)  

If this all seems somehow familiar, you either knew it already or you remember [this Arcana from three years ago](https://web.archive.org/web/20120312000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/japanese-mps-lands-2009-01-12), which showed the prize support that was being used at the time. Now, however, they're using these!
