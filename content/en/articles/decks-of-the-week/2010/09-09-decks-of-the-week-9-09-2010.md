How about that new Extended format? The new four-year Extended made its premier-event debut at Pro Tour–Amsterdam last weekend with an extremely varied metagame. Paul Rietzl used a White Weenie deck created by Gabriel Nassif to [take home the trophy with a perfect 9-0 Top 8 performance](https://web.archive.org/web/20100909000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/rietzl-sweeps-victory-amsterdam). Check out the Top 8 decks below, as well as all Extended decks that earned 18 points or more across the 10 rounds of Swiss play plus Top 4 and Top 8 lists from various Public Events held in Amsterdam.
