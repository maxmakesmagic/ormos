*   **MAGIC 2015 “GARRUK’S REVENGE” EXPANSION**

    ******Q: What is in the *Garruk’s Revenge* Expansion?******

    ****A: In Garruk’s Revenge, players take on the role of Garruk himself, helping him seek his revenge across the fan-favorite plane of Alara. With four new campaign nodes and an expanded explore node, players will take on the role of Garruk, using his deck to battle their way through challenging foes. With every victory Garruk’s deck improves, and players will earn cards from Alara to later modify their own deck creations as they hunt their prey across the planes of the Multiverse.****

    ******Q: What changes have you made to the game with this expansion?******

    ****A: The biggest change is one that our players and fans directly asked for and we delivered on. Now, every card in the game is earnable. Players who have unlocked access to the game’s five initial chapters (Innistrad, Theros, Ravnica, Zendikar, and Shandalar) now have the opportunity to earn additional cards by winning multiplayer matches. Previously, some cards could only be acquired by purchasing a “Premium Booster.” Going forward, however, these cards will instead be available in “Battle Boosters,” which can be earned by winning multiplayer matches.****

    ******Q: What happened to the “Premium Boosters?”******

    ****A: Previously, some cards could only be acquired by purchasing a “Premium Booster.” Going forward, however, these cards will instead be available in “Battle Boosters,” which can be earned by winning multiplayer matches. The content of the former Premium Booster card pool and the Battle Booster card pool is the same. This change is only in name, not in content. Just like with the Premium Boosters, there are 14 Battle Boosters available.****

    ******Q: What are you doing to help players who purchased some or all of the Premium Boosters that are now earnable as Battle Boosters?******

    ****A: We are committed to providing great play experiences for our players and fans. For players who had previously purchased a Premium Booster, Card Collection Bundle, or the Complete Bundle, we want to show our appreciation for their dedication and patience. When they update their game with the latest patch, they’ll see some exciting content we’ve unlocked for them as a bonus.****

    *   ******Purchased Premium Boosters:** To thank them for purchasing Premium Boosters, we've given players the new *Garruk's Revenge* Expansion content as a bonus.****
    *   ******Purchased the Card Collection Bundle:** We’ll be including the Alara Card Collection and the Battle Booster Collection as a bonus.****
    *   ******Purchased the Complete Bundle:** *Garruk’s Revenge* Expansion content and the Battle Booster Collection as a bonus.****

    ******Q: What if I want to get all of the cards right away instead of playing through?******

    ****A: It’s easier than ever to get all of the cards right at your fingertips. Players can purchase entire card collections by plane for $4.99 each or all of the 14 Battle Boosters in the Battle Booster Collection for $9.99. Each collection purchase provides the maximum number of copies of each card (4 copies of each common, 3 of each uncommon, 2 of each rare, and 1 of each mythic). Players who previously purchased 1-13 of the Premium Boosters will only earn the remaining cards in the card pool. Players who previously purchased all 14 of the Premium Boosters will not earn any additional cards.****

    ******Q: Why did you not include “Two Headed Giant” with this expansion?******

    ****A: Our goal is to always provide our players with the best experience possible and in prioritizing that, we focused on ensuring that all cards were earnable within the game before adding additional play modes. Two-Headed Giant is a fan-favorite play style and one that we are definitely looking to bring back in future iterations of the game.****

    ******Q: Why was there a delay with the Xbox One release?******

    ****A: As always, our goal is to provide players and fans with the best play experience possible and sometimes that means making tough decisions like the one to post-pone the release of the game on Xbox One. While we’re not going to comment on the cause of the delay, we’re very excited about this upcoming release and proud of the partnership we have with Microsoft Game Studios and the Xbox One team.****

    ******Q: Can I use the cards from Garruk’s Deck in my own collection?******

    ****A: No, but you can come back to the Garruk’s Revenge Expansion and test out your own decks. The cards in Garruk’s Deck are only available for play in the Expansion against the AI of the Expansion.****

    ******Q: Can I play with Garruk’s Deck against the Hunt Bigger Game Campaign or in Multiplayer?******

    ****A: No, but you can take the cards earned in the Expansion from the Alara Collection and use them to build new and exciting decks of your own.****

    ******Q: What are the latest Achievements available to players in Garruk’s Revenge?******

    ****A: There are five new achievements available for players in Garruk’s Revenge available on Steam, iTunes, Xbox One, Xbox 360 and GooglePlay:****

    *   ******Welcome to the Club:** Add an unlocked expansion card to a deck.****
    *   ******Deconstructor:** Defeat the final expansion boss.****
    *   ******Murderous Tendencies:** Control creatures with a combined total power of 20 or more with the Garruk deck.****
    *   ******Conflux Capacitor:** Complete the Alara collection.****
    *   ******Fly My Pretties:** Have ten or more Thopter artifact creature tokens on the battlefield under your control.****

    *   ******MAGIC: THE GATHERING - DUELS OF THE PLANESWALKERS FRANCHISE******

    ******Q. What is Magic 2015— *Duels of the Planeswalkers* ?******

    ****A. Magic 2015— *Duels of the Planeswalkers* is our latest version of the digital game in the **Magic: The Gathering** franchise. Building on the success of the original Duels of the Planeswalkers, **Magic** 2013 and **Magic** 2014, **Magic** 2015— *Duels of the Planeswalkers* will introduce a full, openended deck building experience through a comprehensive deck builder, the ability to customize and supplement your deck as you open booster packs featuring new and exciting cards, a thrilling story that puts players at the center of the action as they try to outwit the Multiverse’s greatest hunter, Garruk Wildspeaker, and, as always, a fun learntoplay experience for those new to Magic. These and other exciting new features on Xbox One, Xbox LIVE® Arcade, PC/Steam®, iPad® and on Android™ tablet.****

    ******Q. What is the storyline of Magic 2015— *Duels of the Planeswalkers?*******

    ****A. In Magic 2015, players will find themselves dropped into the wilderness at the heart of the story: You’re not just on Garruk’s list – you’re next. You will have to confront the deadliest hunter in the Multiverse. But, how will you defeat Garruk before he finds you.****

    ******Q. What platforms are available for Magic 2015— *Duels of the Planeswalkers* ?******

    ****A. Magic 2015— *Duels of the Planeswalkers* is available on Xbox One, Xbox 360, PC through Steam®, iPad® and on Android™ tablet.****

    ******Q. Is additional downloadable content available?******

    ****A. Yes. Magic 2015— *Duels of the Planeswalkers* offers a variety of downloadable game content including booster packs, premium boosters and foil conversions.****

    ******Q. Can I purchase additional cards for my collection?******

    ****A. Yes. In Magic 2015— *Duels of the Planeswalkers* , players can obtain more cards in two ways. First, they can unlock additional boosters with up to 6 cards by defeating opponents. Second, players can get additional cards by purchasing more boosters or Premium Boosters, or through special promotional unlocks.****

    ******Q. What game modes are available?******

    ****A. Magic 2015—*Duels of the Planeswalkers* features a full single-player campaign game vs. AI opponents. Magic 2015—*Duels of the Planeswalkers* also offers online multiplayer play with three and four-player free-for-all play on all platforms with the exception of iOS. On iOS, you can play against up to one other player and up to three AI opponents.****

     ******Q. Is there trading in Magic 2015— *Duels of the Planeswalkers* ?******

    ****A. Magic 2015— *Duels of the Planeswalkers* does not support trading; you can unlock all the cards in the game by simply playing, through purchasing additional boosters, or through special promotional unlocks.****

    ******Q. What are the multiplayer freeforall rules?******

    ****A. Magic 2015— *Duels of the Planeswalkers* features freeforall play, meaning the last player remaining wins. You can choose to play ranked games against random opponents or unranked games against your friends.****

    ******Q. How many players can play in a multiplayer game?******

    ****A. On all platforms with the exception of iOS, up to four players can play online with up to four players playing in a free-for-all game format. On iOS, you can play against up to one other player and up to three AI opponents.****

     ******Q. Is Magic 2015— *Duels of the Planeswalkers* suitable for gamers unfamiliar with Magic: The Gathering or trading card games?******

    ****A. Yes! Magic 2015— *Duels of the Planeswalkers* is ideal for gamers regardless of their familiarity with Magic: The Gathering or other trading card games. Magic 2015— *Duels of the Planeswalkers* is the best way to start playing the **Magic: The Gathering** trading card game.****

    ******Q. What features are there for new players?******

    ****A. Magic 2015— *Duels of the Planeswalkers* includes a tutorial where you learn while you play, ingame hints, and even an ingame rulebook and glossary.****

    ******Q. Can I create new decks or modify my deck?******

    ****A. Magic 2015 – *Duels of the Planeswalkers* offers players the opportunity to completely customize their decks. Based on a player’s initial color pairing selection, players can then add more cards to their collection through gameplay, purchased boosters or special promotional unlocks. As a player’s collection grows, they can have up to 30 custom created decks at any given time and continue to modify them infinitely. Players can opt to have the AI “auto complete” a deck for them out of their collection based on their collection as a whole or any number of cards. Additionally, players can either add land cards to their decks manually or allow the AI to auto complete their lands.****

    ******Q. What cards are featured in the game?******

    ****A. Magic 2015 – *Duels of the Planeswalkers* features select cards from throughout the history of **Magic: The Gathering** up through the **Magic 2015—Core** Set.****

    ******Q. Are there achievements or trophies and, if so, what type?******

    ****A. There are 30 achievements worth 1000 points on Xbox One and 400 points on Xbox 360, respectively. There are twenty achievements to be earned in the base game Steam® and twenty achievements on iPad. Each achievement is earned in a different way, including ranked match wins and meeting unique victory conditions on Xbox 360. The twenty achievements on iPad are worth a total of 200 points.****

     ******Q. Is Magic 2015 – *Duels of the Planeswalkers* challenging for the experienced players?******

    ****A. Magic 2015 has an advanced AI that can be adjusted to provide challenging game play for even skilled Magic: The Gathering players. The AI gradually increases the difficulty level as players progress through the game. Also, Magic 2015 – *Duels of the Planeswalkers* has ranked headtohead competitive play on Xbox 360.****
