*Mirrodin* has folded words like "[Mox](https://web.archive.org/web/20031015000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=47446)" and "[Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20031015000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=373335)" back into common *Magic* parlance. Why is this exciting? Because the original Moxen and Lotus form six of *Magic* 's so-called Power Nine, a nonuple of the most rule-breakingly powerful cards ever to grace cardboard. In case you've heard the term but don't know the cards, here's a rundown of the original Power Nine:
