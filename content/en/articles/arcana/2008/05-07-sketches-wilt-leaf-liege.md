Welcome to another edition of [Sketches](https://web.archive.org/web/20080507000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/fullarchive&tablefilter=sketches)! You know, not all sketches come in the traditional "pencil on paper" format. Some artists, like Jason Chan, deliver color sketches that are closer to a final version. Sometimes, they even give us more than one option!

### 1\. Art Description

Here's the original art description for [Wilt-Leaf Liege](https://web.archive.org/web/20080507000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wilt-Leaf+Liege):

> Color: Green and white  
>  Location: A stretch of mossy, dead woods lit by elvish lanterns (your design)  
>  Action: Show a elvish knight mounted on a \[Jelly\] version of a cervin (deerlike creature). The elf is armored and armed. Perhaps she has a dog companion who looks up at her as she patrols the perimeter of the elvish haven.  
>  Focus: the elf knight  
>  Mood: Noble, innocent, earnest

### 2\. Sketches

Jason turned in three options for our creative team to choose from:

![Wilt-Leaf Liege Sketches](https://i.ormos.uk/a2/bb/a2bb650e6a5cb3b89d3213801915d16d140b954694713660abe3d595677da05e.jpg)

The creative team liked all three and Art Director Jeremy Jarvis ended up just telling Jason to go with A or C.
