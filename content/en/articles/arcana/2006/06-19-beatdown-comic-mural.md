![](https://i.ormos.uk/8d/87/8d87fa69c8e8ef4749e6b4bb372627e8facd13ef6bb9b89755cdff221762d8ff.jpg)We've already seen that mural-master Greg Stapes created the [*Ravnica* block mural](https://web.archive.org/web/20060619000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/arcana/1076), but there was an earlier piece of Staples mural mastery. The [*Beatdown Gift Box*](https://web.archive.org/web/20060619000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/expansion/beatdown) featured two prebuilt decks, one in green and red centered around [Ehrnam Djinn](https://web.archive.org/web/20060619000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ehrnam+Djinn), and the other decked out in black and blue with [Sengir Vampire](https://web.archive.org/web/20060619000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sengir+Vampire) at the helm.

For that product, Greg had the assignment of illustrating these two iconic beasties battling it out. He painted a full mural-style “comic” of the entire fight. Ready to scroll?
