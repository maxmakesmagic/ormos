The August 2008 [Friday Night Magic](https://web.archive.org/web/20080729000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Link%20to:%20http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/fnm) promo card is an alternate-art [Desert](https://web.archive.org/web/20080729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desert). The art featured in this promo was illustrated by Glen Angus who passed away in July 2007. This piece of art was actually commissioned for the *Odyssey* set, but it got bumped during an eleventh hour frenzy of art swapping. 

Our story starts with this piece, which needed a home:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/77/1f/771fea84da08b014aa782deaecbbe93889614a7724b9004716b8825624944d7f.jpg)*Art by Terese Nielsen*

That art ended up on [Luminous Guardian](https://web.archive.org/web/20080729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Luminous+Guardian), which had the playtest name “Tireless Tribe”. 

![Luminous Guardian](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Luminous+Guardian)

The art [Luminous Gaurdian](https://web.archive.org/web/20080729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Luminous+Gaurdian) used to have got bumped to a card with playtest name “Scars of the Earth”.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/13/41/1341dfb0854c0260d46a4c3d792e22b471c9416f1b89949876a587a7123ad7f4.jpg)*Art by Christopher Moeller*

As you may recognize, “Scars of the Earth” became the card [Need for Speed](https://web.archive.org/web/20080729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Need+for+Speed). 

![Need for Speed](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Need+for+Speed)

Now the original art from [Need for Speed](https://web.archive.org/web/20080729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Need+for+Speed) (“Scars of the Earth”) needed a home. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/62/ac/62accb7786c7d239ba0f2b28504d368fc43f2d5dea358d154273aa276b322b23.jpg)*Art by Glen Angus*

Eventually that art ended up on [Shadowblood Ridge](https://web.archive.org/web/20080729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shadowblood+Ridge), which had the playtest name “Moorlands”. 

![Shadowblood Ridge](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Shadowblood+Ridge)

The original art for “Moorlands” ended up getting bumped from the set. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/25/f2/25f231f1ca696a232e6676f4653455572177f8629d72adf0c30e486adfd16129.jpg)*Art by Glen Angus*

But, thanks to the magic of promos, the displaced piece finally found the great home it deserved. Here’s how it all comes together on the [Friday Night Magic](https://web.archive.org/web/20080729000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Link%20to:%20http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/fnm) August promo: 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b2/6d/b26d1988d6464552a69d138c13dd5d5ff08fe631c750c322b28265ea1b4cfe5e.jpg)
