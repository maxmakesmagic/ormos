Think about Gem of Becoming.

  

 ![Gem of Becoming](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Gem%20of%20Becoming&options=) 

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c2/9e/c29ec1cf00bbf8d44c87f907478256bb2577d3e64599bd19f0b07f8821735171.jpg) 

Gem of Becoming | Art by [Jack Wang](https://web.archive.org/web/20120823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22jack+wang%22])

It's not exactly the thing hanging between the horns of Nicol Bolas, Planeswalker, but it's certainly the same sort of thing. So here's the question: why is it a Gem of *Becoming* exactly? Click here for the answer!

 ![Pools of Becoming](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Pools%20of%20Becoming&options=) 

  

Because it's from the Pools of Becoming on Nicol Bolas's private meditation plane! The pools have changed a little since we last saw them, but that decaying tower in the lower left of the gem's art looks awfully familiar....
