## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0e/bc/0ebc4e215731f0aee0b1f4fe4f0c8e78405e547a47921ed1c4313a08ec0ad07d.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/05/e6/05e659e3c1873432822c40c530bbd41a8703ab974f29da11960344f7ee149a7b.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, nine 15-card booster packs, 80 basic land cards, one card box, two deck boxes, a learn-to-play insert, and a special-edition life counter.

(\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/32/ec/32ec23bedd75217b9bf7b3d99c017b334c8483075c6484c7f91cd2af8f00b79e.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 cards, a foil premium rare card, two sealed 15-card booster packs, a strategy insert, and a Magic learn-to-play guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160709213937/http://magic.wizards.com/node/155306)

(\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ab/ec/abec605d0ee93e11794e5eba1b62d5802a554e236d4de0f50524549d2d247082.png)

### EVENT DECK

Battle like the best, right out of the box. Includes a tournament-ready 60-card deck plus a 15-card “sideboard” to swap in as needed, depending on what you expect your opponent will play.

[DECKLIST](https://web.archive.org/web/20160709213937/http://magic.wizards.com/node/155336)

(\* Not available in all languages)
