Burnished on the chest of the silver golem Karn is the symbol of his creator, Urza.

[![Karn, Silver Golem](https://i.ormos.uk/61/92/619267a5f81d796c08a9c0deb90773c9016d111bd88acd66bf67fc0a38f17f54.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071203000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Karn%252C%2BSilver%2BGolem) *[Karn, Silver Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20071203000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn%2C+Silver+Golem) art by Mark Zug*

You can see other shots of the symbol wherever Karn is depicted, and in many of the cards where Urza is depicted, which is rather a lot throughout the Weatherlight Saga.

Check out the *Urza's Legacy* art of [Blessed Reversal](https://web.archive.org/web/20071203000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Blessed%2BReversal). Karn and Jhoira stand behind Urza, who casts a spell to protect them from a swarm of attacking angels controlled by Radiant. Urza's symbol can be seen on a medallion on his chest.

[![Blessed Reversal](https://i.ormos.uk/10/25/10250d2036eb9604349d7501d42b25691685da0c8817b0e72cb6838abf7f1b89.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071203000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Blessed%2BReversal) *[Blessed Reversal](https://web.archive.org/web/20071203000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blessed+Reversal) art by Pete Venters*

The symbol is somewhat covered up on [Brawl](https://web.archive.org/web/20071203000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brawl) and [Jaded Response](https://web.archive.org/web/20071203000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jaded+Response), but you can see better detail on it on Urza's costuming on [Stroke of Genius](https://web.archive.org/web/20071203000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stroke+of+Genius). Note the blueprint of Karn on the wall, behind Urza.

![Brawl](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Brawl)

![Jaded Response](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Jaded+Response)

[![Stroke of Genius](https://i.ormos.uk/4b/ec/4beca2420ff213bd9ce7fdfa42f6975b486f6605d160d2dee529015de7c13076.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071203000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Stroke%2Bof%2BGenius) *[Stroke of Genius](https://web.archive.org/web/20071203000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stroke+of+Genius) art by Stephen Daniele*

Urza can be seen magically burning his symbol into the metal surface of an artifact creature in the art of Brand.

[![Brand](https://i.ormos.uk/af/03/af032cee53bdb7f83ab06d7b7a81a99dd34a4a9857ff1d39a087e4d4846241e0.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071203000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Brand) *[Brand](https://web.archive.org/web/20071203000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brand) art by Donato Giancola*
