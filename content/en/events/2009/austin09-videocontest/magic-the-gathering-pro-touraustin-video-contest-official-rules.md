NO PURCHASE NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. The Magic: The Gathering® *Pro Tour Austin Video Contest* ("Contest") is governed by these Official Rules. Contest begins at 9:00AM Central Time on Friday, October 16, 2009 and ends at 7:00 PM Central Time on Saturday, October 17, 2009 ("Contest Period"). The Contest provides entrants with the opportunity to create a short film portraying the entrant's experiences playing or spectating at the Magic: The Gathering® Pro Tournament ("Film") using a Flip Mino camera supplied by "Sponsor" (as defined below) or participant's own video camera, and to submit the Film for consideration. Eligible Films will be reviewed by representatives of "Sponsor" (as defined below). You must have a YouTube account and agree to comply with any terms of use applicable to such account in order to participate in the Contest or win a prize. Membership on YouTube is free, and can be obtained by logging on to www.youtube.com and following the onscreen instructions to open an account. The Sponsor of the Contest is Wizards of the Coast LLC, 1600 Lind Ave SW, Suite 400, Renton WA 98057 ("Sponsor"). By participating in the Contest, each entrant unconditionally accepts and agrees to comply with and abide by these Official Rules and the decisions of Sponsor, which shall be final and binding in all respects. 

**ELIGIBILITY: OPEN ONLY TO INDIVIDUALS ATTENDING PRO TOUR AUSTIN WHO HAVE A DCI NUMBER. You need not be playing in the Pro Tour to enter.** To sign up for a DCI Number during the Contest Period, go to the Wizards Information Booth located in Exhibit Hall 5 at the Austin Convention Center during Pro Tour Austin. Employees, officers and directors of Sponsor, and their parents, subsidiaries, and affiliated companies, advertising, promotion, or production agencies, and event vendors (collectively, "Contest Entities"), and each of their IRS dependents and immediate family members (spouse, parents, children, siblings) and individuals residing in their same household of each (whether or not related), are not eligible to participate. By participating, entrants agree to these Official Rules. Void where prohibited, taxed or restricted by law. All federal, state, and local laws and regulations apply. 

**HOW TO ENTER:** To enter:  
 1. Read and sign a statement agreeing to these official rules at the Wizards Booth in Exhibit Hall 5 at the Austin Convention Center at the Pro Tour Austin.  
 2. Sponsor will loan you a Flip Mino video camera ("Camera") or you can use your own video camera or video camera on your phone or iPod to shoot a Film that meets the Film Requirements (as described below). *Note:* There may be a delay or wait time in obtaining a loaner Camera due to a limited supply.  
 3. You must bring the Camera back within ninety (90) minutes after checking it out. If you keep it longer, your entry may be disqualified.  
 4. Go to the Video Booth located near the Wizards Booth and upload your Film to YouTube, or use other means to upload your film to YouTube.  
 5. Provide Wizards with the link to your Film by emailing the URL to Wizards at austinptfilm@wizards.com.  
 Failure to return the Camera as described above, to comply with the ninety (90) minute time limitation, or malicious use of or damage to the Camera may result in your suspension from the DCI, disqualification from the contest, and any other rights or remedies Sponsor may have.

Each Film that is submitted into the Contest must meet the following "Film Requirements" (any Film that, in Sponsor's sole and absolute discretion, violates the following criteria will be disqualified):  

*   Films must be no shorter than 30 seconds and no greater than 2 minutes in length;
*   Films must NOT include third parties/people, who have not expressly authorized entrant to display their image, likeness or voice in the Film or otherwise use such image, likeness or voice in accordance with these Official Rules. Third Parties include but are not limited to celebrities, friends and minors who have not obtained parental consent prior to such participation. In the event an entrant cannot provide Sponsor, upon Sponsor's request, proper releases from such Third Parties, then Sponsor reserve the right, in Sponsor's sole discretion, to disqualify the applicable Film or seek to secure the releases and clearances for Sponsor's benefit.
*   Films must NOT contain material which is (or promoting activities which are) sexually explicit, obscene, pornographic, violent, self-mutilating (e.g., relating to murder, the sales of weapons, cruelty, abuse, etc.), discriminatory (based on race, sex, religion, natural origin, physical disability, sexual orientation or age), illegal (e.g. underage drinking, substance abuse, computer hacking, etc.), offensive, threatening, profane, or harassing; or material that contains any derogatory references to any Contest Entities.
*   Films must not contain the last name, phone number(s), address, or email address of any person, including the entrant.
*   Films must not contain or otherwise make available any unsolicited or unauthorized advertising.
*   Films must be entirely original to the entrant and must be filmed onsite at Pro Tour Austin.
*   Films must NOT include third party trademarks, logos, insignia, location signage, photographs, artwork, or sculpture, except for those belonging to Sponsor.

You may enter only once. Entries must be received and uploaded no later than 7:00 PM Central time on October 17, 2009. Each entrant must be the rightful owner (or have authorized use) of the YouTube account associated with the entrant's Film. Entrants must provide all information requested to be eligible to win. Incomplete, unreadable, or unintelligible entries will be disqualified. Entries will not be acknowledged by the Sponsor.

**FINALIST SELECTION/NOTIFICATION:** At the conclusion of the Contest Period, three (3) winning Films will be chosen from all eligible Films. The winning Films will be selected based on the following judging criteria: Entertainment value (50%), highlighting Magic: The Gathering (25%), and highlighting the host city of Austin, TX (25%).The judging of Films will be conducted under the supervision of Sponsor whose decisions are final and binding in all respects relating to winner selection. The entrants who submitted the three (3) winning Films, as determined by the judging criteria indicated above, will be announced on or about Saturday, October17, 2009 at 8:00 PM Central Time in Exhibit Hall 5 at the Austin Convention Center**.** Winners need not be present to win. If winners are not present, winners will be notified by email using the contact information provided through their DCI registration within three (3) days and will be required to respond (as directed) to the notification attempt within three (3) days (or a shorter time if required by exigencies). The failure to respond timely to the notification, or if the winner notification is returned as unclaimed or undeliverable to any potential winner, it may result in forfeiture of participation in the Contest and, in such case; Contest Entities may select an alternate winner according to the aforementioned judging criteria. Winners may be required to execute and return an affidavit of eligibility, a liability release and, unless prohibited by law, a publicity release and other related documents required by the Contest Entities.
