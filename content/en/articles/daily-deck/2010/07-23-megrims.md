Many players recognized Liliana's Caress as similar to Megrim when ***Magic** 2011* was released. At the 2010 Finland National Championship, Hannu Vallin realized everyone's dreams by making the Top 8 combining the Caress with the original. To get opponents discarding cards, Hannu used Blightning, Burning Inquiry, Inquisition of Kozilek, and even Mind Rot. Combined with additional burn like Lightning Bolt and Burst Lightning, Vallin made the Top 8 of the event!
