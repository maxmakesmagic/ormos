The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on March 14, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by [dogs. So many dogs](#item9).

# "No Changes"

1.  A few people took on fast reactions to the Banned and Restricted announcement yesterday

*   [No changes to the Banned List!](http://www.pantheonmagic.com/no-changes-to-the-banned-list/) | Pantheon Magic | Andrew Cuneo
*   ["No Changes" to the Banned and Restricted List](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/no-changes-to-the-banned-restricted-list/) | ChannelFireball | Matt Sperling
*   [Podcast 112: Standard No Changes, ChannelFireball Grand Prix](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/articles/podcast-112-standard-no-changes-channel-fireball-grand-prix) | MTGGoldfish | SaffronOlive and ChazV

There were others and there will be more, but these represent the hot takes—the quick reactions that came out before a consensus could coalesce. In fact, I'm not even sure how Sperling got his out the door so fast. Dude can *write*.

# After Those, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [From New Jersey to No New Bans](http://manadeprived.com/first-strike-17-new-jersey-no-new-bans/) | First Strike | Kar Yung Tom, Robert Lombardi, and Bryan Gottlieb

Robert Lombardi had a stellar [Grand Prix New Jersey](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-new-jersey-2017) finish, so there's that. There's also "no changes" to debate, the ChannelFireball Events Grand Prix takeover, and players with family priorities learning to balance their time. It's a meaty episode.

1.  [Channel PV—Modern Faeries](http://www.channelfireball.com/videos/channel-pv-modern-faeries/) | ChannelFireball | Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa

With *Modern Masters 2017 Edition* releasing this Friday, there's a lot of Modern content coming out right now—but you'd be remiss to bypass PVDDR, perhaps the greatest Faeries players of our generation, playing his favorite deck full of fluttery 1/1s.

1.  [Examining *Modern Masters 2017* Limited](http://www.starcitygames.com/article/34723_Examining-Modern-Masters-2017-Limited.html) | StarCityGames.com | Matt Higgs

Oh, did we mention *Modern Masters 2017 Edition* releases this weekend? We did? Strange. Well, at any rate, Higgs takes a spin through the Limited format as well as the implications for Cube.

1.  [Mastering *Modern Cube*](http://www.hipstersofthecoast.com/2017/03/mastering-modern-cube/) | Hipsters of the Coast | Zach Barash

These kinds of articles fascinate me. They're only relevant for a short time—the window when *Modern Cube* is available for play—but they're wildly useful during that time. If you're enjoying the [*Modern Cube*](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/03-02-the-magic-online-modern-cube) on *Magic Online*, then this is a helpful little article to navigate the ins and outs of the format.

1.  [The Return of Nonsense](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/the-return-of-nonsense/) | ChannelFireball | Luis Scott-Vargas

This was posted juuuuuuuust after the update went live yesterday, but we could all use a bit more nonsense. LSV covers a potpourri of topics, from terrible mana and [Exclude](https://web.archive.org/web/20170314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exclude)s to ranking Reid Dukes to ranking the discard steps. It's LSV stream of consciousness, which we could all use a bit more of.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [KADASHI6351's Grixis Improvise](/en/events/2017/competitive-standard-constructed-league-2017-03-13-0/competitive-standard-constructed-league)

Willy Edel [is a believer](https://twitter.com/bazardebagda/status/841658444910346241), maybe you should be too?

KADASHI6351 recently went 5-0 in a Competitive Standard League with this improvising monster of a deck, punishing people with quick [Maverick Thopterist](https://web.archive.org/web/20170314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Maverick+Thopterist)s, shutting them down with cheap [Herald of Anguish](https://web.archive.org/web/20170314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Herald+of+Anguish)es, bleeding them dry with [Unlicensed Disintegration](https://web.archive.org/web/20170314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unlicensed+Disintegration), and poking them to death with [Servo Schematic](https://web.archive.org/web/20170314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Servo+Schematic)s.

The real star of the deck is probably [Tezzeret's Touch](https://web.archive.org/web/20170314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tezzeret%27s+Touch), which churns out a fast 5/5 to pressure opponents right out of the gates. And while there's only one in the deck, improvising with or attacking with a [Key to the City](https://web.archive.org/web/20170314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Key+to+the+City), then drawing a card when it untaps, has got to be a great feeling.

The deck is oddly resilient to artifact destruction, as its best cards aren't actually artifacts, just artifact-enabled. Considering the power packed into this deck, it seems like a real potential player in the format.
