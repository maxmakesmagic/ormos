Gentlemen and ladies, start your calculators. Today's Magic Arcana takes a look at some of details of the *Ice Age* set from a numerical or factoid perspective.

[![Ice Age](https://i.ormos.uk/cd/68/cd68b06e0a66ef7e111c6b5bb293dc13021af79f1b9eee344377d18e478a3646.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/expansion/iceage)

*   **Release date:** June, 1995.

*   **Number of cards:** 383.

*   **New mechanics:** Cumulative upkeep, cantrips, and snow-covered lands.

*   **Alphabetical first and last cards:** [Abyssal Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Abyssal%2BSpecter); [zuran spellcaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=zuran%2Bspellcaster).

![Elkin Bottle](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Elkin+Bottle)

*   **White mana symbol:** [Redesigned](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/white-mana-symbol-2003-02-06).

*   **Plains art:** A [mural](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/arcana/68).

*   **Number of cards involving single-surface topology:** [1](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/single-surface-topology-2002-03-29).

*   **Number of instants:** 53.

*   **Number of sorceries:** 29.

*   **Number of lands:** 33 (including 3 of each basic land).

*   **Number of enchantments:** 95 (including enchant creatures and enchant lands).

*   **Number of non-creature artifacts:** 39.

*   **Number of artifact creatures:** 6.

*   **Number of those artifact creatures that count as walls:** 3.

![Polar Kraken](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Polar+Kraken)

*   **Number of creatures that aren't artifacts:** 128.

*   **Largest creature:** [Polar kraken](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Polar%2Bkraken).

*   **Card with the highest converted mana cost:** [Polar kraken](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Polar%2Bkraken).

*   **Number of cards with "snow" in the name:** 11.

*   **Number of cards with "ice" in the name:** 4 ([Justice](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Justice) and [Fiery Justice](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Justice) don't count).

*   **Number of cards with "winter," "glacier" or "blizzard" in the name:** 1 each ([Winter's Chill](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Winter%27s+Chill), [Glaciers](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glaciers), and [Blizzard](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blizzard)).

*   **Number of cards that mention banding:** 11.

*   **Number of cards that mention cumulative upkeep:** 30.

![Skeleton Ship](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Skeleton+Ship)

*   **Number of legends:** 4.

*   **Number of cards printed in *Ice Age* that were also printed in *Eighth Edition*:** 25.

*   **Number of *Ice Age* cards currently restricted in Type 1:** 2 ([Demonic Consultation](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonic+Consultation) and [Necropotence](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Necropotence)).

*   **Number of *Ice Age* cards currently banned in Type 1:** 1 ([Amulet of Quoz](https://web.archive.org/web/20040804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Quoz)).
