Did you enjoy the *Rise of the Eldrazi* Prerelease? If you were in Richmond, Virginia, you had the chance to play against R&D member Mike Turian, who was taking on challengers with this take on White Weenie featuring the new set. Inspired in part by a deck list Mike got from fellow R&D member Joe Huber, it features standbys like Day of Judgment and Path to Exile while highlighting new power cards like Gideon Jura, Wall of Omens, and Transcendent Master.
