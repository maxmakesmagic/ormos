StarCityGames.com columnist Abe Sargent has taken one of his favorite old archetypes and given it a Commander twist: Equinaut! By using creatures like Whitemane Lion and Fleetfoot Panther that can immediately return themselves to owner's hand combined with cards like Equilibrium, Azorius Æthermage, and Momir Vig, Simic Visionary, Abe's Jenara, Asura of War deck can set up a lot of creature-manipulation fun. Check out [Abe's article](http://www.starcitygames.com/magic/commander/23061_the_kitchen_table_367_equinaut_commander_deck.html) and use this deck for your own Commander inspiration!

Abe Sargent's Equinaut 

Commander
