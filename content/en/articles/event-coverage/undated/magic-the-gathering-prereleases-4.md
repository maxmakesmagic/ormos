Held two weeks before each new **Magic** card set goes on sale, Prereleases provide a way to play in a large-scale event in a low-stress environment. You get to play with the cards before you can buy them, special promotional cards are given out to attendees and the top players can win even more cards. So have fun!

Prereleases are Limited events, meaning you build your 40-card (minimum) deck from the packs that you get after paying your entry fee (this can be either Sealed Deck or Booster Draft format). Along with the possibility of playing in one large tournament at the event, Prereleases also run flights of smaller tournaments to provide more opportunities to see the new cards – you get a new set of cards for each flight you play (each flight requires another entry fee).

Prereleases are sanctioned by the DCI, which is the governing body for all **Magic** tournaments – from the smallest local events up to the high-level Pro Tour. Playing in a sanctioned event means your results are recorded and begin to give you a player rating among the thousands of **Magic** players worldwide. If you don’t have a DCI number yet, the event staff on site can set you up. For many players, prereleases are a fun and easy way to play in their first sanctioned events.

### Prizes

Additional packs of **Magic** cards of the set being showcased are awarded to top players. Special commemorative prerelease foil cards are given to each participant while supplies last. Additional prizes may be awarded at the discretion of the tournament organizers.
