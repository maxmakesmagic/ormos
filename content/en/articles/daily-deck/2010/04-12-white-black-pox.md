The Extended season is winding down, but Grand Prix–Houston recently crowned a champion. One of the most innovative decks in the Top 8 was Charles Lancaster's white-black Pox deck. Using Flagstones of Trokair as well as creatures like Bloodghast and Kitchen Finks to minimize the effects of the card on himself, Charles fought his way to a star-studded Top 8. Read more about the event in the [official coverage](/en/events/2010/gphou10/yurchick-first-at-last).
