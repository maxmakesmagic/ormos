* * *

One thing you'll notice after the October 31 update is that there are some changes to the ***Magic** Online* tournaments. Because of the nature of running tournaments on a round-the-clock basis and the amazing response we've had, we've realized that the current structure creates an unfair environment for a majority of ***Magic** Online* players.

Under the original structure, a few ***Magic** Online* players win enough product in tournaments to effectively pay half price for boosters while everyone else has to pay full price. Our desire is to make ***Magic** Online* a fun and fair environment for all players of all skill levels, not to generate a huge rift between the top competitive players and those just entering the game.

The [new prize schedule](https://web.archive.org/web/20021030000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/tourprize&origin=magic_magiconline_tourchange) no longer depends on how many players are in a tournament. This means that you don't need to know how many players are competing to understand what prizes you're playing for.

The new minimum size for events in the **Sanctioned Tournaments** area will be 24 players. (This doesn't include the 8-player rooms.) Running Swiss tournaments with 16 to 23 players often means cutting to a Top 2 playoff. This new minimum size means the finals will never cut to less than the top 4 players, and will more often result in a full Top 8 playoff.

With the launch of the *OnslaughtTM* set, we've created a new schedule of events in the **Sanctioned Tournaments** area. If you look at the [new schedule](https://web.archive.org/web/20021030000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/events06302002&origin=magic_magiconline_tourchange), you can see that there are only 4 hours on any given day that don't have an event running. At any time of the day, the most you'll ever have to wait for a new event to launch is one hour.

The cost to enter Premier Events has increased to 10 tickets for Constructed events and 5 tickets for Limited events. While these tournaments are also affected by the new prize schedule, the prizes will actually be much bigger in some cases and may also award non-booster prizes. For example, the *Onslaught* launch events this weekend will award double the normal prize schedule to winners.

While the changes to the structure and prize schedule may seem severe at first glance, the impact is actually far less broad than it appears. The only tournaments significantly affected are those in the non-8 player **Sanctioned Tournaments** rooms. The most popular rooms in the **Sanctioned Tournaments** area (the 8-player rooms) have the same prize schedule as they've always had.

For the health of the game and the community, we believe these changes are necessary. We want to ensure that everyone has an equal opportunity to enjoy the game.

Thank you for playing,  
  
 The ***Magic** Online* Team
