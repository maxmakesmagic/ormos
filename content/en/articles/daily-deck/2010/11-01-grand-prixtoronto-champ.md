Grand Prix–Toronto saw a host of stars playing in its Top 8, including the likes of Ben Stark, Eric Froehlich, and the hottest player in the world right now: Brad Nelson! But it was hometown hero Jonathan Smithers who managed to take the title. He credited his success to staying flexible in the draft portion and reading the signals he was handed by those sitting around him. Read [full coverage](/en/events/2010/gptor10/hometown-hero-seizes-the-initiative) of the event for more!
