It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20110901000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=events/magic/fnm)! And since it's the first Friday in September, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

![Teetering Peaks](https://i.ormos.uk/88/87/8887904eb3b0624fd92c2b153688ea5157cf845152b690b968888c730b806fcc.jpg)  

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards often feature original flavor text and updated wordings.
