Brian David-Marshall checks in with *Magic* Brand Manager Mark Purvis about the *Magic: The Gathering - Commander* decks coming out June 17. Based on the popular 100-card singleton format created by the *Magic* community, Commander decks offer a great opportunity to jump in on an entirely different way to play *Magic*. There are five decks, with 51 new cards spread across all five.
