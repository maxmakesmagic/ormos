*Rivals of Ixalan* introduces us to six new tokens, though calling two of them tokens might be a bit of a stretch.

First up, the tokens, all of which are produced by cards you've met:

![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/rix/en_uFXjevfdSn.png)![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/rix/en_it7lbrrNfw.png)

![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/rix/en_d10yUrbm43.png)![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/rix/en_emZEVtYuDj.png)

Then the last two. First, Huatli's amazing, absurd emblem. If you ever put one of these on the table, you're probably on your way to victory.

![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/rix/en_eLQMf4RB6k.png)

Finally, should you garner the City's Blessing, this token will mark your ascendancy to this higher level.

![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/rix/en_2MWLzaZo23.png)
