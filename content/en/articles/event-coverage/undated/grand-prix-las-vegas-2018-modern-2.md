## ARTICLES COVERAGE

Day 1 Coverage

It’s finally here – Grand Prix Vegas 2018. One of the most-hyped events on anyone’s calendar whenever *Magic* takes a tour through the desert, Vegas this year is no different, with two Grand Prix and a host of other events including an actual *Beta* draft on Sunday. More than 2,000 players turned out for the Modern event starting on Friday, and *Dominaria* Limited will follow starting on Saturday.

Of course, with an event like this all the biggest names in the game are out – chasing either pro points or *Beta* packs (Hall of Famers Ben Stark and Martin Jůza were the first two qualifiers) – and the entire weekend will feature the best of both the professional *Magic* world as well as the realm of content creators.

And today Modern takes center stage, as a format that defies a solution continues to evolve in front of our eyes – and you can watch three full days of coverage live at [Twitch.tv/Magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic).
