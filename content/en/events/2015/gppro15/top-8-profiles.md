### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppro15/top8_sacks.jpg)**Neal Sacks

**Age:** 24

**Hometown:** Lynnfield, MA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 37th grand Prix Cleveland 2015; 70th Grand Prix Las Vegas 2015

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Abzan Aggro because [Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion) into [Anafenza, the Foremost](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anafenza%2C+the+Foremost) into [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) is the best curve in the format.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Rakshasa Deathdealer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Deathdealer): Early game attacker, last game threat. What more could you ask for?

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Arashin Cleric](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arashin+Cleric): I only played mono-red twice, but the 1/3 body and 3 life made that matchup much easier. [Drown in Sorrow](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drown+in+Sorrow) actually had more applications.

**What was the most interesting play you made so far this weekend?**  
 I pumped [Rakshasa Deathdealer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Deathdealer) to an 8/8 then used [Dromoka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dromoka%27s+Command) to make it 9/9 and fight a [Dragonlord Atarka](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Atarka) for the win.

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppro15/top8_mcclain.jpg)**Josh McClain

**Age:** 25

**Hometown:** Iowa City, IA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 Grand Prix Detroit 2013 winner; four total Grand Prix Top 8s

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Abzan Megamorph. Mike Sigrist said it was good.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Den Protector](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Den+Protector): Between the evasion and the ability to rebuy cards, it does everything you could ever want.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price): People are trying to cast giant creatures. Killing them for two mana is great.

**What was the most interesting play you made so far this weekend?**  
 Using a single [Den Protector](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Den+Protector) to get back two more [Den Protector](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Den+Protector)s to attack through an army of 0/3s and 1/1s.

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppro15/top8_severson.jpg)**Eric Severson

**Age:** 23 (As of yesterday!)

**Hometown:** Berkeley, CA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 Finalist Grand Prix Portland 2014. Six 11-4 finishes at other Grand Prix since then. Will be a Silver Pro if I win this tournament!

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Bant Megamorph: Did really well for me at Grand Prix Toronto and I only brough cards for this or Abzan, but I didn’t like Abzan in testing.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Disdainful Stroke](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disdainful+Stroke): Went up ot three to help the Red-Green Devotion matchup. Never played it, but they countered a lot of [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion)s and finally an [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon) to win my last game.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Mastery of the Unseen](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mastery+of+the+Unseen): Going to three is great versus Esper Dragons but also the Abzan morph decks, who have cut down on [Dromoka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dromoka%27s+Command). It lets you keep up [Disdainful Stroke](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disdainful+Stroke) while developing your board.

**What was the most interesting play you made so far this weekend?**  
 Round 9 versus Ross Merriam. I curved [Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion) into morphed [Rattleclaw Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rattleclaw+Mystic). He played [Anafenza, the Foremost](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anafenza%2C%C2%A0the+Foremost) which I attacked into with the Lion. He blocked, not realizing I coul dunmorph, make five mana, and monstrous to blow him our in combat.
