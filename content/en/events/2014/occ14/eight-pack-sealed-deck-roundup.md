Narrowly behind the Community team, the Wizards team was looking to turn things around, and their first opportunity was going to come in the first event of the day: Eight-Booster Sealed Deck. To generate the eight packs used in the format, the community as a whole voted during the lead up to the Community Cup as to which packs would make the most...entertaining decks.

Here are the packs that the community chose:

> — Mariah Pagliocco (@mariahnikol) [September 13, 2014](https://twitter.com/mariahnikol/status/510842984137379840)
