 H: Play Wild Dogs

 T: Play Island

 T: Play Island

H: Play Gaea's Cradle

 H: Play River Boa

Turn four:  
 T: Play Rishadan Port

T: Port the Cradle in Hernandez' upkeep

H: Rancor the Wild Dogs

T: Submerge in response

H: Attack with the Llanowar Elves and the Boa

Turn five:  
 T: Play Island  
 T: Play Treachery on the Boa  
 T: Play Somnophore

H: Play Wild Dogs  
 H: Attack with Llanowar Elves

 T: Attack with the Somnophore, lock down the Elves  
 T: Play Island

T: Port the Cradle during Hernandez' upkeep

H: Attack Wild Dogs

T: Block the Wild Dogs with Boa

H: Giant Growth the Wild Dogs

Turn seven:  
 T: Get the Wild Dogs from Hernandez  
 T: Attack Somnophore, lock down the Elves again  
 T: Play Island  
 T: Cycle Miscalculation

T: Port the Cradle during Hernandez' upkeep

![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/461.jpg)H: Play Treetop Village

Turn eight:  
 T: Attack with Somnophore and Wild Dogs  
 T: Play Masticore

T: Port Treetop Village during Hernandez' upkeep

H: Play Forest

Turn nine:  
 T: Attack with the Masticore, the Somnophore, the Wild Dogs

Hernandez scoops.

Tsang 1 - Hernandez 1
