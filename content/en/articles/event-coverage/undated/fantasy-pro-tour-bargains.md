### 

I always like previews before Pro Tours. Last time, I brought you a preview of playtest groups. For Chicago, I want to show you a different side of the Pro Tour. Every individual Pro Tour has a Fantasy Pro Tour that goes along with it. We see all the big names at the top of the points list and these are often the backbones of our teams. I wanted to list some of the supporting cast members you might like to include on your team. Here they are in alphabetical order:

### Joseph Crosby 11 points

I mentioned Joe Crosby in my Limited articles on StarCityGames.com. Joe is an incredibly strong theorist, probably the strongest I talk to on a regular basis. However, his skills do not end when he reaches the table. He is a methodical player who doesn’t miss much, but is far from slow. In fact, other than his Florida compatriot Ben Stark, I would say Joe Crosby is the closest to the complete package I have played with. Trust me, 11 points for Joe is a steal. I have picked Joe for every Fantasy PT in which he was qualified, that won’t stop now.

### Nick Eisel 5 points

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/gpla02/895.jpg)Sooo, ThatsGameBoys. Nick has been showing the world what it means to draft Onslaught Limited. He Top 8’d both Grand Prix-LA and Grand Prix-Philly, which were virtually the same format as Chicago (there is no sealed portion in Chicago). He writes a lot and makes his theories known and you know he puts a lot of time into being number one on Magic Online. Nick knows his Onslaught and for five points, you could do a lot worse. The only thing holding him back is his lack of ability to follow my Limited theories…

### Sigurd Eskeland 6 points

The man won a Pro Tour. Granted, it was Constructed and Lin Sivvi was the broken card of choice back then but still, he has a good game. Despite winning a Constructed PT, he is known as a Limited specialist. Rumors from across the sea tell me that he has been practicing hard. In fact, there have already been rumors of a team forming for the next Team PT including Sigurd and German powerhouse Patrick Mello. Six points is not bad for this PT champ.

### Jon Finkel 16 points

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/MI01/969.jpg)Here is one of three players on this list that will cost you more than 10 points. Jon’s game may have faltered a little, but Rochester Draft has always been his best format. He is one of the best to ever pick up the cards and I am sure he doesn’t want his star burning out just yet. Expect to see him at the top of the standings in this event.

### Jürgen Hahn 0 points

Canada’s National Champion. He skipped out on Worlds, but Chicago is a lot closer than Sydney. It was no fluke that he won the crown in Toronto this year. If you are shy a 0 pointer, this man should definitely be on your team. There aren’t a whole lot of 0s you are eager to jump on, this should be one.

### Jens Krause 10 points

I am going to defer to Kai Budde on this one, as I am not extremely familiar with this particular Jens:

“Jens Krause is pretty much a newbie on the international scene. He has played in one Pro Tour and didn’t do very well there. This season, however, he seems to be on fire. He started off by reaching the semifinals at GP-Hamburg. Next up was GP-Copenhagen. Day 2 was Onslaught Rochester draft, the very format of the upcoming PT. Jens cruised through the first draft, 3-0’ing table 1. Jens defeated Bob Maher to finish 1st in the Swiss and then walked over Florent Jeudon and myself in the quarter- and semifinals. In the finals, Bob Maher gave him his first real loss of the tourney, but that GP boosted Jens’s rating up to 2144, ranking him third in the world.” 

### Mike Long 8 points

Down, but not out. That seems to be the story of Mike Long’s career. Every time you think you have seen the last of the Dark Lord, he rears his head again. Mike is a very strong player and has a tendency to surprise everyone. Rochester Draft is also traditionally his strong suit. He made Top 8 at 1999 Pro Tour-LA. If you were ever going to pick Mike, this should be the tournament.

### Christian Lührs 3 points

I am not that familiar with Christian either, so I will again let my esteemed colleague Kai field this one: 

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/Worlds2001/837.jpg)“Christian Lührs got frustrated with the Magical cards roughly two years ago. He posted excellent finishes by making the Top 8 at 1999 New York, the Top 4 at 1999 Chicago and Top 4 with Patrick Mello and Stephan Valkyser at the Team PT in 2000 New York. He decided to stop going to overseas events he was qualified for, concentrated on university and fell off the gravy train. He wouldn’t even travel to PTQs outside of Hamburg. The local shop had a PTQ for PT-Chicago and he attended that one – and won, unsurprisingly. He did work on the format with Patrick Mello, Marco Blume, me and other locals and is well-prepared, which makes this three-time Top 8 finisher quite a bargain for just 3 points.” 

### Zachary Parker 5 points

The Baby Faced Assassin is the terror of Southeast PTQs. Zach, while only 14, has managed to place 1, 1, 1, 2 in his last 4 PTQs and finished Top 32 in GP-New Orleans. I don’t want to shame this kid by saying he is good for his age; he is flat out good. He will win games based simply on underestimation, but he will win other games based on trash talk. This kid can keep up with the best of them.

### Ben Ronaldson 5 points

Ben decided to focus more on tennis than Magic, but he still knows how to game. His little hiatus will hopefu blade\_sliverlly prove to lower his value for the Fantasy PT and not for the game. While he is primarily known as a Constructed player, he did spend a good amount of time living with Gary Wise. Despite this, I feel he has more solid tournament finishes in him. I kid the Wise, because I love him.

### Ben Stark 15 points

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/GPDET01/186fm13Stark.jpg)This Ben, while less well known, is one of the most complete gamers I have met in my time on the Pro Tour. Ben is another Southeastern phenomenon. He has given up slots in the finals of more PTQs than I have attended. He usually is capable of riding his rating to PTs. This year, Ben is finally starting to come into his own. He had a fantastic 8-1 start at PT-Houston and this was after winning the Last Chance PTQ to make it. Expect a lot more to come from this player. The one thing you have to ask yourself: the whole world agrees he is one of the best drafters in the world. Why does he not do better in Pro Tours? Hopefully he can overcome this psychological hump.

### James Stroud 0 points

I need a zero, I’m holdin on for a zero… okay, I’ll stop now. James Stroud is an extremely old school player. When you last met this man, he was about 60 lbs heavier and a lot more hardcore into the game. He took a break, but decided to come back right along with good friend Dave Williams. He won two PTQs and designed the deck that Dave, Bob Maher, and he played in GP-New Orleans. It is clear that James did not lose his game with his weight.

### Cole Swannack 9 points

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/GPBRI01/a927.jpg)Speaking of young phenomenon, Cole has turned the continent of Australia on its head. He won his National Championships in New Zealand and he finished Top 24 at Worlds. He games constantly on Magic Online and is frequently seen chatting it up with some of the top pros in the game. Cole has the poise and talent of someone several years older than he. A fine pick at 9 points.

### Michael Thomas 0 points

Mike is a name you may not have heard. He played on the Pro Tour so long ago, they weren’t even giving out points yet. He was the first player in history to reach the height of a 2000 Limited rating. Most recently he teamed with Ed Fear and Adrian Sullivan in Grand Prix-New Jersey and they barely missed Day 2. Mike is from Buffalo, NY and I play with him on a regular basis and I seem to be incapable of beating him. His methodical style of play has allowed him to defeat me time after time. The biggest flaw in his game is his lack of confidence. He is making my team and he should definitely make yours.

### Joe Weber 2 points

The last surviving member of Team Sped keeps hanging around. Joe was always the most devoted to the game as well as the best drafter on the team. Joe has made Top 16 at two Pro Tours and has solid finishes in several GPs. Most recently he made Top 32 at GP-LA. Expect a solid finish from this old timer.

### David Williams 7 points

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/Worlds2001/693.jpg)Last, and far from least, is Dave Williams. Dave, as we all know, has taken some time off from the game. He kept up with Magic Online, and his comeback has reflected his practice. He hasn’t missed a Pro Tour since his return, and has been at the top of the Grand Prix circuit. Dave was forced out of the game in his prime, but it seems as though he still has some fight left.

Now, you obviously can’t include all of these, but once you have your all-stars, look at how many points you have left and check this list to fill in the holes. This may look like a list of has-beens and wannabes, but this is a list of some of the best players you may not have heard of. If you sit down across from them, you will soon learn they are neither has-beens nor wannabes.

Your strongest picks on this list are most likely Christian Lührs, Sigurd Eskeland, Dave Williams, and Ben Stark in that order. All are bargains at their point values, so keep these guys in mind when making your team.
