![](https://www.wizards.com/protour/images/qt_lg.jpg) Punch your ticket to Prague by winning a Pro Tour Qualifier! 

We're trying something a little different with this season. Instead of showing the Top 8 Booster Draft decks like we've done in past seasons, we'll showing you the Sealed Decks those players built to make it to the Top 8. But don't get too comfortable with these decks, as *Guildpact* will be added to the card pool for all qualifiers after February 3.

You can test each decklist thanks to our [Sample Hand Generator](https://web.archive.org/web/20090602023708/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/294). Just click on the card fan in the upper right of each decklist and you can see what your opening hand could be, along with mulligans and the first three cards you'd draw.

Decklists will be updated weekly during the PTQ season. To find a PTQ near you, [click here](/en/events/2006/prague06/pro-tourprague-06-qualifiers-4). [Pro Tour-Prague](/en/events/2006/prague06/2006-pro-tourprague) is scheduled for May 5-7, 2006.
