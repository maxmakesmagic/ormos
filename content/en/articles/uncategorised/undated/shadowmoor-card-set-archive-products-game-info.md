### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide, card box, eight 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, one random Pro Tour player card, a special-edition life counter, and the *Shadowmoor* collection of short stories.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d4/43/d443bfb0332945c64ca8a2379b5f8579163c31e92b13d4d45347f6bcb1cb110f.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b0/07/b007855636a8dba3e4d320e5fe095fbe3bb440898d77c7ce30547069515d0c55.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/eb/69/eb698bee20699ab504d737ec15ff54820b5c8efa9070ed744b7c4ca09264c564.png)

This 301-card expansion set features randomly inserted premium cards, mono-colored hybrid cards, and four new game mechanics: Wither, Persist, Untap, and Conspire.

**Set Name:** *Shadowmoor*  
**Block:** Set 3 of 4 of the *Lorwyn-Shadowmoor* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 301

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

[FAQ ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719043931/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/MTGSHM%20FAQ%20042108.doc)

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Mark Rosewater (lead), Sean Fletcher, Mark L. Gottlieb, Devin Low, and Ken Troop

**Final Game Design and Development:** Aaron Forsythe (lead), Alexis Janson, Devin Low, Kenneth Nagle, Matt Place, and Steve Warner, with contributions from Jake Theis and Mark L. Gottlieb
