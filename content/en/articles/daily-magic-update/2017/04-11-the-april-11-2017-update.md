# Synergy of the Day

1.  It's Madness!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_CauMBC9mh1.png)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Noose+Constrictor)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noose+Constrictor)

 

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Fiery+Temper)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Temper)

 

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Incorrigible+Youths)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Incorrigible+Youths)

Back when madness first debuted with *Odyssey*, there were a number of decks built around [Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel) and the ability to discard cards with flashback, like [Roar of the Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roar+of+the+Wurm), or cards with madness, like [Arrogant Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrogant+Wurm). The deck could hit hard and fast and leveraged the lower costs of these cards when they were discarded.

Fast forward to today, and many of the same parts are present. [Noose Constrictor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noose+Constrictor) steps in for [Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel). [Incorrigible Youths](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Incorrigible+Youths) does a decent [Arrogant Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrogant+Wurm) impression, [Fiery Temper](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Temper) is, well, still [Fiery Temper](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Temper), and Honored Hydra is basically just an updated [Roar of the Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roar+of+the+Wurm).

Who knows if we can actually make it work, but the pieces are clearly there to start something brewing.
