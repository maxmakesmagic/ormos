Last week, we used the official **Magic** group in the Wizards Community to [show the packaging for the physical *Duels of the Planeswalkers* decks](https://web.archive.org/web/20100429000000/http://community.wizards.com/magicthegathering/blog/2010/04/19/duels_of_the_planeswalker_decks_available_june_4th:), which will be available in stores June 4.

Today, we're going to use Arcana to show you the decklist for one of them. Behold, **Teeth of the Predator** – the Garruk Wildspeaker Deck!
