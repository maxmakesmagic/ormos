**What’s the most unexpected card you’ve seen this weekend?**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/eternalweekend14/QQDuke1.jpg)

***Reid Duke:** "[Fireblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fireblast) out of Delver, I was at four life and I was definitely going to win the game, then I got [Fireblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fireblast)ed by a Delver deck."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/eternalweekend14/QQFabiano1.JPG)

***Gerard Fabiano:** "[Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise). I thought it was a myth."* 

 

 

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/eternalweekend14/QQJones1.jpg)

***Kevin Jones:** "[Fireblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fireblast) from my Blue/Red Delver opponent. I never saw it coming and it almost cost me the match."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/eternalweekend14/QQShenhar1.JPG)

***Shahar Shenhar:** "[Helm of Obedience](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Helm+of+Obedience). I was playing against Reanimator and I had Grafidigger's Cage in play. I wanted to play around [Echoing Truth](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Echoing+Truth), so I decided to play [Rest in Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rest+in+Peace). My opponent had [Helm of Obedience](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Helm+of+Obedience) and, suddenly, I no longer had a deck."* 

 

 

 

 

 

Roland Chang has apparently been surprising plenty of opponents with his [Fireblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fireblast) this weekend. Be sure to check out the Randy Buehler's deck tech with him from last night!
