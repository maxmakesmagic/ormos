**Modern MOCS Monthly on Sunday, March 26**

A *Magic Online* Championship Series (MOCS) Modern Monthly Event is coming up on March 26! Players can spend 35 Qualifier Points (QPs) to enter the event. The series will contain 24 Monthly Events, each of which qualify top players for one of the four Playoff Events. These Playoff Events qualify top players for the *Magic Online* Championship, a Pro Tour, and the Pro Tour Challenge event at that Pro Tour.

Monthly and Playoff Events also award top players MOCS Leaderboard Points for the year as a whole. Top players on the Leaderboard at the end of the 2017 MOCS will qualify for the year-end Championship event.

We look forward to seeing you on March 26 for the latest Monthly Event! Learn more about the 2017 *Magic Online* Championship Series [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20170321000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2017-magic-online-championship-series).

**Extended downtime tomorrow, March 22**

There will be an extended downtime on Wednesday, March 22, for *Magic Online*. Events close at 12:00 a.m. PT/7 a.m. UTC (that's midnight Pacific), and store activity and trades end at 2:45 a.m. leading up to the system coming down at 3:00 a.m. The system is estimated to return from downtime at 12:00 p.m.

**Build notes**

Duel Scene | Improved display feedback on buttons in the prompt box.

General | Fixed several crashes players could encounter on log in.

Duel Scene | Visual indicator improvements have been made to cards that cause different effects across multiple targets.

Limited | [Monstrous Onslaught](https://web.archive.org/web/20170321000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monstrous+Onslaught) now triggers correctly off of controller’s creatures instead of owner’s creatures when cast via cards such as [Gonti, Lord of Luxury](https://web.archive.org/web/20170321000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gonti%2C+Lord+of+Luxury).

Modern | The first ability of [Sorin, Solemn Visitor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170321000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorin%2C+Solemn+Visitor) now correctly lasts until its controller's next turn.

Modern | Colored mana from two different [Cavern of Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20170321000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cavern+of+Souls) cards now show separately.

Modern | [Vanish Into Memory](https://web.archive.org/web/20170321000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vanish+Into+Memory) now correctly causes the caster to discard.

Modern | [Reality Smasher](https://web.archive.org/web/20170321000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reality+Smasher)'s triggered ability now only triggers once for each spell that targets it.

Modern | Cards that reduce the costs of other cards, such as [Goblin Electromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170321000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Electromancer), now correctly affect alternate costs like buyback and multikicker.

Vintage | Spell copies created by [Eye of the Storm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170321000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eye+of+the+Storm) no longer trigger [Eye of the Storm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170321000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eye+of+the+Storm).

Vintage | [Natural Balance](https://web.archive.org/web/20170321000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Natural+Balance) now allows players with less than four lands to search their libraries.

Vintage | Selecting a split card to exile from your hand with [Scroll Rack](https://web.archive.org/web/20170321000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scroll+Rack) now returns the correct number of cards to your hand.
