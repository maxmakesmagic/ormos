Earlier this week, [I covered Makihito Mihara's Standard deck](https://web.archive.org/web/20140220000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/perfect-curve-2014-02-18-0) with *Born of the Gods*. Today, I have Aaron Lewis's decklist, which is absolutely chocked full of *Born of the Gods* cards! 

![Meletis Astronomer](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Meletis+Astronomer)

Aaron used [Hero of Iroas](https://web.archive.org/web/20140220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero+of+Iroas) to bestow [Eidolon of Countless Battles](https://web.archive.org/web/20140220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eidolon+of+Countless+Battles) and [Hopeful Eidolon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hopeful+Eidolon) on the cheap. [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos) unsurprisingly makes an appearance and works pretty well with [Eidolon of Countless Battles](https://web.archive.org/web/20140220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eidolon+of+Countless+Battles). [Meletis Astronomer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meletis+Astronomer) keeps the gas flowing. Having a cheap way to trigger heroic, such as [Ethereal Armor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ethereal+Armor) or [Ordeal of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ordeal+of+Thassa), is incredibly important, as playing multiple spells per turn is the easiest way to get ahead. 

If you enjoy building a giant monster, then you should check out Aaron's deck. Although Standard has a lot of removal, bestow mitigates the downside of creating a huge creature. I would like to see some of the sideboard [Gods Willing](https://web.archive.org/web/20140220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gods+Willing)s make their way into the main deck, though. Being able to protect your creature or force through lethal damage seems really important for this type of deck.
