Invitations to Pro Tour-Honolulu are awarded in the following manner:

*   The 2008 World Champion.
*   The 2008 Pro Tour Player of the Year.
*   The Top 50 players from Pro Tour Kyoto.
*   Players with 2009 Pro Tour Players Club level 6 or higher.
*   Players with 2009 Pro Tour Players Club level 4\* & 5\* (invitation for level 4 & 5 players are not awarded until June 5, 2009).
*   Players with 2009 Pro Tour Players Club level 3\* are invited to 1 Pro Tour or World Championship of their choice in the 2009 season (invitation for level 3 players are not awarded until June 5, 2009).
*   The Top 16 finishers of each Grand Prix tournament that feeds Pro Tour Honolulu.
*   Players who earn invitations through Pro Tour Honolulu Qualifier tournaments.
*   The Top 100 DCI-ranked players in the Constructed format on April 22, 2009.
*   The Top 100 DCI-ranked players in the DCI Total format on April 22, 2009

\* Players with Pro Tour Players Club levels 3, 4 and 5 are allowed to play in Pro Tour qualifiers if their *only* invitation to the Pro Tour is due to their level in the Players Club (see below)

If you believe that your rating or finish at a Pro Tour Qualifier or Grand Prix is in error, please Visit [https://webapp.wizards.com/login.aspx?target=personal](https://web.archive.org/web/20090901060757/https://webapp.wizards.com/login.aspx?target=personal). After logging in, click the "Appeals" link and fill out the form.

The following players have 2009 Pro Tour Players club level 3 and have no other invitation to the Pro Tour. They are allowed to play in Pro Tour Qualifier Tournaments that feed Pro Tour Honolulu (including the Pro Tour Honolulu Last Chance Qualifier). Level 3 members receive 1 invitation to the Pro Tour of their choice in 2009. They will not receive their invitations to Pro Tour Honolulu until June 5, 2009.
