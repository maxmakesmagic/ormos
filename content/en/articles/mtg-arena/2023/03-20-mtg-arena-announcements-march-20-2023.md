## *Shadows over Innistrad Remastered* Arrives Tomorrow!

![Shadows over Innistrad Remastered logo](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/en_hero_sir.jpg) 

Innistrad haunts *MTG Arena* once more when *Shadows over Innistrad Remastered* arrives tomorrow, March 21!

Check out all the cards in the set by visiting the [*Shadows over Innistrad Remastered* Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/03-14-shadows-over-innistrad-remastered-card-image-gallery).

In addition to introducing awesome cards from *Shadows over Innistrad* and *Eldritch Moon* to *MTG Arena*, the set is accompanied by Shadows of the Past, [a selection of cards from the original *Innistrad* block](https://web.archive.org/web/20230320000000/https://stage.magic.wizards.com/en/news/mtg-arena/shadows-of-the-past-card-list-and-schedule) (*Innistrad*, *Dark Ascension*, and *Avacyn Restored*) which appear in Store packs.

Shadows of the Past cards will also appear in packs used in *Shadows over Innistrad Remastered* Limited events—but there's a twist! In these events, [themed subsets](https://web.archive.org/web/20230320000000/https://stage.magic.wizards.com/en/news/mtg-arena/shadows-of-the-past-card-list-and-schedule#schedule) of Shadows of the Past cards will appear and change up the challenge each week on this schedule:

*   March 21–28: Creature Type Terror!
*   March 28–April 4: Fatal Flashback!
*   April 4–11: Morbid and Macabre!
*   April 11–18: Abominable All Stars!

## April 1–2 Arena Open: *Shadows over Innistrad Remastered*

![April 1–2 Arena Open Shadows over Innistrad Remastered, artwork Ulrich of the Krallenhorde by Slawomir Maniak](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/wrup7oyegi8l.jpg) 

We're closing in on the next Arena Open, scheduled for April 1–2. Flex your skills with the new *Shadows over Innistrad Remastered* set in Limited matches for a chance to earn not only May Qualifier Weekend invitations, but as much as $2,000—no April Fool's joke here!

This Arena Open falls in the Shadows of the Past schedule, and that means the theme in effect will be Fatal Flashback! Check out [the theme's card list and details on Shadows of the Past](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2023/03-13-shadows-of-the-past-card-list-and-schedule#schedule) for more info.

## Qualifier Play-In Best-of-One April 1

![Qualifier Play-In logo](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/logo_play-in_blue_850.jpg) 

There's a lot happening on the April 1, including a [Best-of-One Qualifier Play-In event](https://www.magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) in the Explorer format. Qualifier Play-Ins let you jump into competitive play by winning an invitation to each month's Qualifier Weekend event.

On April 7, there will be a Best-of-Three Qualifier Play-In with another chance to compete in the Explorer format and claim an invitation to the [April Qualifier Weekend](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) happening immediately following the Play-In on April 8–9.

## Premier Play Schedule

All times listed are Pacific time (UTC-07:00).

### Qualifier Events

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events.

Qualifier tokens will be delivered through [the *MTG Arena* player inbox](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/12-12-mtg-arena-release-notes-alchemy-the-brothers-war#inbox). Remember to claim them before the event starts!

    *   Format: Explorer
*   **Qualifier Play-In** – **Best-of-Three**
