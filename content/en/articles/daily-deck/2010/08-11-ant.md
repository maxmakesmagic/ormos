With the banning of Mystical Tutor from the format, many thought combo decks like Ad Nauseam Tendrils had had their day in the sun. At Grand Prix–Columbus, Ari Lax proved otherwise. The young player burst to an undefeated record after the first day of competition playing a Mystical Tutor-less version of the ANT deck. Read more at the [official coverage](/en/events/2010/gpcol10/saitos-legacy-continues).
