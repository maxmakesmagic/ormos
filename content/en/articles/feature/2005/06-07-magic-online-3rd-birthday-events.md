In celebration of the 3rd Birthday of Magic Online, Wizards of the Coast will offer the following events on Friday, June 24, 2005:

***"Nix Tix"* Draft Queues**

"Nix Tix" QUEUES START: Friday, June 24 12:00 AM PST  
 "Nix Tix" QUEUES END: Friday, June 24 11:59 PM

ADMISSION: 0 Tix, Product

All draft queues will require 0 event tickets on Friday, June 24, 2005. "Nix Tix" Draft Queues will have a normal prize payout.

**CHK Block Constructed Open**

START TIME: Friday, June 24, 2005 2:00 PM

FORMAT: CHK Block Constructed Format

ADMISSION: 6 Tix
