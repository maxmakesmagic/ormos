The first Yotian Soldier appeared in *Antiquities*. The flavor text explains that Urza created the soldiers to protect his allies while he was busy elsewhere.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e2/ad/e2ad047bed0baf6212bf35691ed5c3a07fb220fabad2bd2f46312524b322c771.jpg) 

Yotian Soldier | Art by [Christopher Rush](https://web.archive.org/web/20110324000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22christopher+rush%22])

 ![Yotian Soldier](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Yotian%20Soldier&options=) 

  

So how could this creature show up in the *Mirrodin* set when it's native to Dominaria? What are the odds of Memnarch creating exactly the same creature as Urza made on a different plane? The flavor text on the *Mirrodin* version explains:

> *Poets dream the verses of otherworldly stories. Artificers dream the blueprints of otherplanar artifacts.*
