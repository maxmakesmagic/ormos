**December 1 - January 3**  
  

* * *

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b5/ae/b5aec9a0f6d539942bc3469cde2b6de7db1247b99a7b29def4566f88ab7c0b3a.jpg)

Build a tribal deck based on *Lorwyn*'s eight tribes and play in a store near you, at home-–wherever–-until January 3, 2008 and determine which race will Rule. 

### START DATE

The battle begins Saturday, December 1, 2007 at one-day in-store events around North America. We'll continue to compile player-submitted results from around the world throughout the month of December.

### TRIBAL RULES

*   Choose a *Lorwyn* tribe: [Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20071210212508/http://ww2.wizards.com/gatherer/index.aspx?term=elf&Field_Type=on&setfilter=Standard), [Elementals](https://web.archive.org/web/20071210212508/http://ww2.wizards.com/gatherer/index.aspx?term=elemental&Field_Type=on&setfilter=Standard), [Faeries](https://web.archive.org/web/20071210212508/http://ww2.wizards.com/gatherer/index.aspx?term=faerie&Field_Type=on&setfilter=Standard), [Giants](https://web.archive.org/web/20071210212508/http://ww2.wizards.com/gatherer/index.aspx?term=giant&Field_Type=on&setfilter=Standard), [Goblins](https://web.archive.org/web/20071210212508/http://ww2.wizards.com/gatherer/index.aspx?term=goblin&Field_Type=on&setfilter=Standard), [Kithkin](https://web.archive.org/web/20071210212508/http://ww2.wizards.com/gatherer/index.aspx?term=kithkin&Field_Type=on&setfilter=Standard), [Merfolk](https://web.archive.org/web/20071210212508/http://ww2.wizards.com/gatherer/index.aspx?term=merfolk&Field_Type=on&setfilter=Standard), or [Treefolk](https://web.archive.org/web/20071210212508/http://ww2.wizards.com/gatherer/index.aspx?term=treefolk&Field_Type=on&setfilter=Standard).
*   Only use Standard legal sets: *Tenth Edition*, *Coldsnap*, *Time Spiral*, *Planar Chaos*, *Future Sight*, and *Lorwyn*.
*   At least one-third of the cards in your Tribal deck must have the creature type of your chosen tribe (For example, a sixty-card Elf deck must have at least twenty cards with the Elf creature type.)
*   Tribal Instant / Sorcery / Enchantment cards and cards with changeling count toward your total.
*   You may build more than one deck and represent more than one tribe.
*   There is NO sideboarding in this format.
