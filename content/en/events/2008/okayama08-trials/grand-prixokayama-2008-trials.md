### Entry Fee

The entry fee for Grand Prix—Okayama Trial tournaments varies by location.

### Format

  

*   The format for all Grand Prix—Okayama Trial tournaments is Sealed Deck with Booster Draft finals. Tournaments held before October 3, 2008 will use 1 *Shadowmoor* tournament pack and 2 *Eventide* boosters for the Sealed Deck portion of the Trial, and 2 *Shadowmoor* booster packs and 1 *Eventide* booster pack for the Booster Draft. Trials run on October 3, 2008 and later will use *Shards of Alara* packs for both the Sealed Deck and Booster Draft portions of the event. For information on these formats, please refer to the DCI™ Universal Tournament Rule and **Magic** Floor Rules documents on the [DCI Document Center](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/welcome-to-the-dci-document-center).
*   Deck lists are required.

### Tournament Structure

  

*   The structure for all non-retail Grand Prix—Okayama Trial tournaments will be modified Swiss-style, with the Top 8 players playing in a single-elimination round for prizes and invitations. (In the event a Grand Prix–Okayama Trial tournament has 16 or fewer players, the Top 4 players will advance to the single-elimination round.)
*   Each round in the Swiss portion of the tournament will be 50 minutes.
*   The number of Swiss rounds will be determined based on attendance according to the following chart:

    <table><tbody><tr><td><b>Attendance</b></td><td><b>No. of Rounds</b><br><b>(non-retail)</b></td></tr><tr><td>8 – 16</td><td>4</td></tr><tr><td>17 - 32</td><td>5</td></tr><tr><td>33 – 64</td><td>6</td></tr><tr><td>65 – 128</td><td>7</td></tr><tr><td>129 – 226</td><td>8</td></tr><tr><td>227 – 410</td><td>9</td></tr><tr><td>411 or higher</td><td>10</td></tr></tbody></table>

### Advancement

  

*   All players may participate in all Swiss rounds.
*   When necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will be used to determine final order in the standings.

### Top 8 Finals

  

*   The Top 8 players after the final Swiss round will advance to the Top 8 single-elimination finals.
*   All Top 8 matches at Grand Prix—Okayama Trial tournaments will be best 2 of 3.

### Notes

  

*   All Grand Prix—Okayama tournaments are run at Rules Enforcement Level: Competitive, and are 24K.
*   All Universal Tournament Rules and **Magic: The Gathering** Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this format document.
*   Players are responsible for bringing their own pen, paper, card sleeves, and appropriate counters.

### Prizes

  

*   The winning player of each Grand Prix—Okayama tournament in Japan will receive an third-round bye at Grand Prix—Okayama (to be held November 22-23, 2008). If the winner of a Grand Prix—Kobe Trial already has a third-round bye, the bye does not pass down. Byes are only usable for the appropriate Grand Prix and cannot be "carried over" to another Grand Prix tournament.
*   Additional product prizes may be awarded, as determined by the organizer.

### Registration Information

  

*   Players who want more specific local tournament or registration information should contact their local organizer.
*   Information in this document is specific to Japan; Trials held outside Japan may have different entry fees, etc. For information regarding tournaments outside Japan, please contact the local organizer.
