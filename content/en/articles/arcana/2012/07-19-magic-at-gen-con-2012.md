Are you going to be at [Gen Con](http://www.gencon.com/2012/indy/default.aspx)? Maybe I'll see you there! And maybe you'd like to know about some of the **Magic** things on the schedule.

In fact, there's going to be **Magic** all weekend long. Just as you'd expect at the Best Four Days in Gaming, right? The [full schedule is here](http://www.pastimes.net/events/convention/gencon-2012/), and it's full of Limited and Constructed events of all varieties. Prizes include packs, uncut **Magic** sheets, foil sets, and more. And thirty-two events feed Sunday's **[Gen Con Championship](http://www.pastimes.net/events/1373/)**, a single-elimination draft showdown with big prizes.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/08/aa/08aa043f8535594a6588b28c12357f5feebe1e6f278641c00b3bade19e655847.jpg)

### The World Magic Cup

If you're in the [World **Magic** Cup](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2012-world-magic-cup), you probably already know about it, because it's a tournament featuring teams from more than fifty countries. It's being held Friday through Sunday in Hall G. And the whole thing will be streamed on a live video webcast at [www.wizards.com/webcast](https://web.archive.org/web/20120719000000/http://www.wizards.com/webcast).

  

### The 2012 Vintage Championship

The [2012 Vintage Championship](/en/articles/event-coverage/2011/06-02-2012-vintage-championship-event-information) starts at 10:00 AM on Friday, August 17, and is open to anyone with a Vintage deck. And the winner will receive framed alternate art for Timetwister!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a8/0d/a80da09dee274768ce504cb401625e0cc7f78a33d1221dedd1cb89e1447bd649.jpg) 

Timetwister | Art by [Matt Stewart](https://web.archive.org/web/20120719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+[%22Matt+Stewart%22])

  

### The 2012 Legacy Championship

The [2012 Legacy Championship](/en/articles/event-coverage/2011/06-02-2012-legacy-championship-event-information) starts at 10:00 AM on Saturday, August 18, and is open to anyone with a Legacy deck. The winner of the Legacy Championship will get framed alternate art for Brainstorm!
