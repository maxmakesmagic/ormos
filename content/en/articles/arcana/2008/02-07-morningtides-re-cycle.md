Re-deem the Lost. Re-search the Deep. Re-vive the Fallen. Re-lease the Ants. Re-cross the Paths. Five *Morningtide* cards that re-cursively re-ward you for re-peatedly clashing.

![Morningtide's Re-Cycle](https://i.ormos.uk/a8/4f/a84ffff1508024d6c17d01eb04cba803ca11bfb9de05509e3d9a398ce8abab0d.jpg)

Ah, but can you name the *sixth* re-card that comes back to your hand if you win the clash?
