With Lingering Souls and Intangible Virtue out of the picture, it's time for some new cards to shine in *Innistrad* Block Constructed. With the Pro Tour just around the corner, players have been revising the decks over and over. One of the big contenders? Red-green beatdown!

Taking a page out of the Standard playbook, Xsebishopp's red-green deck features an aggressive curve that is poised to strike down any deck that stumbles along. Strangleroot Geist and Mayor of Avabruck can come down on turn two, threatening plenty of early damage. Predator Ooze and Daybreak Ranger are very scary turn-three plays, either becoming unstoppable attackers or ways to pick off each of the opponent's creatures.

The unique thing about this version, though, is that it's not just the early game that you have to worry about. Sure, Kessig Wolf Run can deal plenty of extra damage later on, but there's more than that. Even if an opponent can deal with the early onslaught, a full set of Vorapedes means this deck can consistently land a monstrous turn-five threat. Flayer of the Hatebound even makes an appearance at six mana to close out of the game.

The creature secretly helping this deck hit that five- and six-mana point? Dawntreader Elk! The innocuous two-drop helps push the deck into casting turn-four Vorapedes. As one of the few forms of land acceleration in the format, the Elk is looking to become a block staple.

If you're interested in giving the deck a try, check out *Innistrad* Block Constructed tournaments on **Magic Online**. And with the Pro Tour right around the corner, what *Avacyn Restored* cards might fit into this deck? Take a look at the [card image gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20121227074702/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/avacynrestored/cig) and start thinking about the possibilities!
