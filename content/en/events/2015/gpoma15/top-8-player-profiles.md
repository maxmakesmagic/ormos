# Pascal Maynard

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpoma15/TOP8_maynard.jpg)

**Age:** 21

**Occupation:** Store manager and card player

**Hometown:** Quebec City

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

4 Grand Prix Top 8s

**What deck did you play and why?**

Blue-Red Delver with [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide)s.  Because I wanted 8 [Monastery Swiftspear](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Swiftspear)s.

**Would you have made any changes to your deck and why?**

Cut [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer), maindeck [Tidebinder Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidebinder+Mage).  Probably a few other things too.

**Which card was your maindeck MVP so far this weekend?  What about your sideboard?**

Tidebinder Mage sideboard; [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) maindeck.

**What are you most excited about for *Fate Reforged*?**

# Andrew Elenbogen

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpoma15/TOP8_elenbogen.jpg)

**Age:** 21

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Ann Arbor, Michigan

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

2 GP Top 32s

2 GP Top 16s

1 GP Top 64 (all Limited or Modern)

**What deck did you play and why?**

Tron.  I disliked everything I tested, so I audibled to Tron because it’s the deck I’m the most familiar with.

**Would you have made any changes to your deck and why?**

Yes.  I am only playing the fourth [](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Relic+of+Progenitus)Relic of Progenitus over the fourth [Wurmcoil Engine](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wurmcoil+Engine) because of a deck registration error.  Also, there should be a [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) in the board for [Scapeshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scapeshift).  I’d cut a [Combust](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Combust) for it.

**Which card was your maindeck MVP so far this weekend?  What about your sideboard?**

Maindeck: [Wurmcoil Engine](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wurmcoil+Engine).  The entire plan versus Delver is this card and it worked repeatedly.

Sideboard:  [](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nature%27s+Claim)Nature's Claim for versatility; [Slaughter Games](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slaughter+Games) for power.

**What are you most excited about for *Fate Reforged*?**

Ugin, the Spirit Dragon.  He is going in this deck as a two-of!!

Slaughter Games .  . .not even sure about it.

Anafenza should be my Prom date.
