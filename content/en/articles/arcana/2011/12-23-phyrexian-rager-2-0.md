Hhere's the art description that was given to [Stephan Martiniere](https://web.archive.org/web/20111223000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&artist=+[%22Stephan+Martiniere%22]) for Phyrexian Rager.

  

### Art Description

> Color: Black creature
> 
> Action: We'd like you to show us this world's version of the creature called "Phyrexian Rager". Reinterpret it through the lens of the styleguide. (Don't include the blue-skinned humanoid in the reference.)
> 
> Focus: the Phyrexian rager
> 
> Mood: It's fascinated by how you experience pain.
> 
> Notes: This is a purely Phyrexian creature.

Because the world had already seen a Phyrexian Rager in *Apocalypse*, there was an art reference for Stephan to work from. Here's what the original rager looked like.
