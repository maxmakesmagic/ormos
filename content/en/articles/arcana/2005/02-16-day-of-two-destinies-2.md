Daren Bader illustrated *Betrayers of Kamigawa's* legendary enchantment, **[Day of Destiny](https://web.archive.org/web/20050216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Day+of+Destiny)**. Not only does the art take a cool perspective from under one samurai's thundering horse, it depicts two -- count 'em, *two* -- legendary creatures from *Champions of Kamigawa*.

Can you recognize the two legends? Click around on the larger image below to find them.
