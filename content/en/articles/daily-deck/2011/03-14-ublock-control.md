*Scars of Mirrodin* Block Constructed is available to play on **Magic Online**! It is a fascinating world of archetypes including Mono-Black Poison, Mono-Red, Mono-Blue Control, Black-Green Control, and of course Blue-Black Control. VinnieD used this take on that infamous color combination for a control strategy that went undefeated in a recent Block Constructed tournament online.
