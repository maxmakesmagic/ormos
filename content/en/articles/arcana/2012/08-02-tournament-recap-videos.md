First up, we've got a new video feature called "The Final Match." Jacob Maynard won [Grand Prix Columbus](/en/events/2012/gpcol12/rookie-maynard-ravages-modern-in-columbus) recently, and he sat down with Rashad Miller to go over the final match in detail.
