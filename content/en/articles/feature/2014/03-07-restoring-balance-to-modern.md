![Restore Balance](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Restore+Balance)

Restore Balance has made a splash at every single Modern Pro Tour but tends to get overshadowed by the other combo decks in the format. As Alfonso Barcelona Cabeza has shown us, [Restore Balance](https://web.archive.org/web/20140307000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restore+Balance) got a great card from *Theros*—[Thassa, God of the Sea](https://web.archive.org/web/20140307000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thassa%2C+God+of+the+Sea). 

Thassa is in the sweet spot for [Restore Balance](https://web.archive.org/web/20140307000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restore+Balance) at three mana. The deck uses cascade cards like [Ardent Plea](https://web.archive.org/web/20140307000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ardent+Plea) and [Violent Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20140307000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Violent+Outburst) to only cascade into [Restore Balance](https://web.archive.org/web/20140307000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restore+Balance), which you cast immediately. If you've been picking up your lands with [Borderposts](https://web.archive.org/web/20140307000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&name=+%5Bborderpost%5D) (or can sacrifice them to [Greater Gargadon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140307000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Greater+Gargadon)), your opponent will have few permanents after [Restore Balance](https://web.archive.org/web/20140307000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restore+Balance). 

Meanwhile, cards like Thassa, Planeswalkers, Borderposts, and [Ardent Plea](https://web.archive.org/web/20140307000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ardent+Plea) (which actually matters due to devotion!) stick around, so you'll be in a great spot.
