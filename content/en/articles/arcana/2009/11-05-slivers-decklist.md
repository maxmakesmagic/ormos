All of the cards will be foil, which means that the following cards will appear in foil versions for the first time: Metallic Sliver, Winged Sliver, Clot Sliver, Heart Sliver, Acidic Sliver, Victual Sliver, Armor Sliver, Barbed Sliver, and Heartstone!

Incidentally, our feature article on Monday, November 9 will be by Ken Nagle, and he'll explain the thought process that went into choosing these cards. So stop by on Monday for that!
