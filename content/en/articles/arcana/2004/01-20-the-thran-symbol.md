![The Thran symbol](https://i.ormos.uk/a2/09/a20930beab6b99ba7df48cb58f63ed7e0966443418d9eede01b01824bb3b94e5.jpg)

The Thran, an extinct race to which the Phyrexians belonged before they became half-mechanical monstrosities, once ruled Dominaria. They created powerful artifacts long before the times of *Mirrodin* or the *Urza Saga* block, and crafted ornate designs into their creations.

Buried in a **Magic** artist's style guide from the *Urza's Saga* days is this tidbit about the Thran: the Thran symbol, an icon representing the Thran culture and technology (see the symbol at right). This symbol is depicted in a few **Magic** cards; for example, see the art of *Urza's Legacy's* Thran War Machine and *Urza's Saga's* Thran Quarry, below.

Learn more about the Thran in [*The Thran*](https://web.archive.org/web/20040120000000/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=products/magicnovel/216000000), a novel by J. Robert King.
