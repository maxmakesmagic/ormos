This is our last edition of Arena Announcements for 2022! We won't have announcements for December 26 or January 2 while our team is on holiday break, but we'll be returning on January 9!

## Arena Decathlon Returns January 1–21!

![Thrilling Encore art by Alex Konstad; Arena Decathlon January 1–16 with finals on January 21](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/decathlon_hero.jpg)

Thrilling Encore | Art by: [Alex Konstad](https://web.archive.org/web/20221219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+[%22Alex%20Konstad%22]) 

The huge, multievent Arena Decathlon returns to usher in 2023 with play and prizes January 1–16, capped by a finals competition on January 21 in which you can win one of every card in the forthcoming *Phyrexia: All Will Be One* set!

Check out all the details on [the Arena Decathlon page](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/12-19-the-arena-decathlon-kicks-off-the-new-year-with-ten-events-finals-and-prizes).

[![Qualifier Play-In Best-of-One January 7, 2023](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/logo_play-in_blue_850.jpg)](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) 

The first [Best-of-One Qualifier Play-In](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) of 2023 happens on January 7! Sharpen your Alchemy skills over the holiday break and start your Premier Play journey fresh in the new year!

## Play *Arena Cube* until January 1

[![The Arena Cube Draft, December 13, 2022–January 1, 2023](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/h2d9de8kk7g.jpg)](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/11-28-the-arena-cube-draft-december-13january-1) 

The latest *Arena Cube* is under way! In *Arena Cube*, you open and pass around packs just like a draft, except the card pool isn't from a single *Magic* set but rather a curated selection of some of the best cards available in *MTG Arena*. [See the cards and play in *Arena Cube*](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/11-28-the-arena-cube-draft-december-13january-1) before it's gone on January 1.

## Premier Play Schedule

All times listed are Pacific time (UTC-08:00).

### Qualifier Play-Ins

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

#### January

*   **Best-of-One Qualifier Play-In**
     January 7, 6 a.m. PT–January 8, 6 a.m. PT (UTC-08:00)
    *   Format: Alchemy
*   **Best-of-Three Qualifier Play-In**
     January 13, 6 a.m. PT–January 14, 3 a.m. PT (UTC-08:00)
    *   Format: Alchemy

### Qualifier Weekends

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to the Arena Championship 1 held in the fourth quarter of 2022.

Qualifier Weekend Day One and Day Two events open to entries at 6 a.m. PT and close to new entries at 8 a.m. PT (UTC-08:00) each day. You must enter within this 2-hour window—late entries cannot be accommodated.

#### January

*   **Qualifier Weekend**
     January 14, 6 a.m. PT–January 16, 4 p.m. PT (UTC-08:00)
    *   Format: Alchemy (Best-of-Three)

### Arena Open

The Arena Open Day 1 entry window begins at 6 a.m. PT and closes to new entries the following day at 3 a.m. PT (UTC-08:00).  Day 2 entry window is 2 hours only, from 6 a.m. PT until 8 a.m. PT (UTC-08:00). 

*   **Arena Open January 21–22** 
    *   Day 1, January 21: *Kaldheim* Sealed Best-of-One and Best-of-Three
         All participants will receive the Memory Lapse sleeve:
         ![Memory Lapse sleeve](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/sleeve_memorylapse.png)
    *   Day 2, January 22: *Kaldheim* Player Draft 
         There are two Player Draft events on Day 2.

### Arena Championship 2

The [Arena Championship](https://magic.gg/events/arena-championship-2) is an invitation-only, two-day virtual event for players who earn invitations through Qualifier Weekend events.

*   [**Arena Championship 2**](https://magic.gg/events/arena-championship-2)March 18–19, 2023
    *   **Format**:
         Swiss: ONE Draft + Historic
         Top 8: Historic
