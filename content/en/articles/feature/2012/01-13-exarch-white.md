TCGPlayer.com columnist Justin Vizaro is all about putting spins on established archetypes. This week, he looked at the white-blue [Moorland Haunt](https://web.archive.org/web/20120113000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moorland+Haunt) deck in Standard and puts his own spin on it. If you're looking for a fun, budget-friendly deck for your FNM tonight, give this new take on an established favorite a try!
