Love playing creatures? This is definitely the Standard deck for you! Wiks managed to make the Top 8 of a recent **Magic Online** Premier Event with this green-white list. Every card in it was either a land or a creature save for just eight cards. Path to Exile keeps potential blockers out of the way, while Shield of the Oversoul makes threats into impervious monsters! This take on Green-White is one that knows how to get into the red zone.
