Hello everyone, and welcome to the final installment of Daily Decks for the year. For today's deck, we're going to be looking at a Modern deck that uses a mechanic from *Battle for Zendikar*: processing. The process mechanic has begun to take off in Standard with [Wasteland Strangler](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland+Strangler), but this is the first time I've seen a Modern deck that exploits the strategy.

[Wasteland Strangler](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland+Strangler) is a card that I like to compare to [Flametongue Kavu](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flametongue+Kavu), which was a very dominant card in its time. It was a straight two-for-one and a card that you simply had to play in your deck, even if was just to combat opposing [Flametongue Kavu](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flametongue+Kavu)s. [Wasteland Strangler](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland+Strangler) is not nearly as dominant as the Kavu was, mostly because it's pretty hard to exile your opponent's stuff. You have to hope they exile something on their own (with delve perhaps) or you have to be actively trying to exile their cards with either the ingest mechanic or white enchantments such as [Silkwrap](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silkwrap) or [Stasis Snare](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stasis+Snare).

Exiling things is much easier in Modern. As you can see from EternallyRamza's list, he is playing six one-mana artifacts that exile entire graveyards. In Modern, players are filling their graveyards with fetch lands and efficient spells very quickly, so an activation of one of these artifacts could exile many cards. There are plenty of removal spells that exile, such as [Magma Spray](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Spray) and [Anger of the Gods](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anger+of+the+Gods), so you are very likely to have food for your [Wasteland Strangler](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland+Strangler) over the course of a game.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Relic+of+Progenitus)](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Relic+of+Progenitus)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Oblivion+Sower)](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Sower)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Wasteland+Strangler)](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland+Strangler)

Another really cool interaction that this deck has is [Oblivion Sower](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Sower) with [Relic of Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Relic+of+Progenitus) and [Nihil Spellbomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nihil+Spellbomb). You're going to be exiling many lands, and [Oblivion Sower](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Sower) puts them all onto the battlefield under your control. After you cast a Sower, it's possible to have enough mana for [Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulamog%2C+the+Ceaseless+Hunger).

[Eldrazi Temple](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Temple) is great in this deck, and this card makes it possible to cast an [Oblivion Sower](https://web.archive.org/web/20151211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Sower) on turn three. If you get enough lands from the Sower, Ulamog can come out as early as turn four! That's pretty impressive.
