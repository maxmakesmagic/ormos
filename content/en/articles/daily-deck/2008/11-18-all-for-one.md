Many a player has enjoyed a game of the format Highlander or Singleton in **Magic**, wherein players may only play a single copy of any non-basic land card in their deck. Jia Wu took things one further, taking this list all the way to the State Championship title in Alaska. In 75 cards, only four were played in a quantity greater than one-of. Is there something to the immense amount of flexibility offered by having a possible one of numerous different answers in your hand? Jia would certainly tell you there is!
