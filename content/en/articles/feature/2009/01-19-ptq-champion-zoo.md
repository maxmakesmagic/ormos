The first PTQs for Honolulu have started rolling in, and Bradley Carpenter earned a ticket to the tropical Hawaii playing the tried and true Zoo. His build used [Tidehollow Sculler](https://web.archive.org/web/20090119000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidehollow+Sculler) instead of the sometimes popular [Shadow Guildmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20090119000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shadow+Guildmage), but the truly innovative card he added to the mix was a full playset of [Lightning Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20090119000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Angel)!
