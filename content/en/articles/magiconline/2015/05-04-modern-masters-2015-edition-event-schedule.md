**Draft Like A Master**

*Modern Masters 2015 Edition* takes players back to some of the most remarkable planes from recent history, including Zendikar, Mirrodin, Ravnica, Lorwyn, Kamigawa, and Alara. Featuring new artwork on a number of cards, every card in *Modern Masters* can be added to your favorite Modern-format decks. But first, enjoy a fresh Limited experience that combines some of *Magic*'s most iconic cards in new ways.

*Modern Masters 2015 Edition* goes on sale Thursday, May 28, on *Magic Online*! *Modern Masters 2015 Edition* is a set that takes essential Modern cards and combines them into an exciting Draft environment. To celebrate both the Constructed and Limited appeal of this set, the *Magic Online* team has made plans that will please Limited and Modern Constructed fans alike.

Limited queues will be running through the Wednesday, July 8, downtime. There's going to be a lot to play around with in this set, so why not try taking *Modern Masters 2015 Edition* for a test run in the Phantom [Draft](#MM2draft) and [Sealed](#MM2sealed) Events? Getting at least 2 wins in either Phantom Event earns players enough Phantom Points to gain entry into another. With enough skill, you can play endless Phantom Limited through the July 8 downtime! Or, perhaps you wish to raise the stakes and get your hands on some sweet, digital booster packs. If so, head on over to the *Modern Masters 2015 Edition* [6-2-2-2](#MM26222) and [8-4](#MM284) Draft Queues. Once you're feeling confident, participating in the scheduled events can win players big prizes (not to mention a Dark Confidant Avatar).

The [64-Player Single Elimination Draft Event](#64playerdraft) begins Thursday, May 28, at 10:30 a.m. The event is scheduled to run every 2 hours, on the half hour, through Tuesday, June 16, 10:30 p.m.—make sure you don't miss out! Victory can earn you big booster pack prizes and a healthy stack of [*Magic Online* Championship Series](https://web.archive.org/web/20150504000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/2015-magic-online-championship-series) Qualifier Points.
