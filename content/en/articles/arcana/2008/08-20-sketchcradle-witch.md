Welcome to another edition of [Sketches](https://web.archive.org/web/20080820000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/fullarchive&tablefilter=sketch)! For Elf Week, we're going to take a look at how [Seedcradle Witch](https://web.archive.org/web/20080820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seedcradle+Witch) came to be.

### 1\. Art Description

Here's the original art description:

> **Location:** A field of nettle-wheat near the border of a creepy forest  
> **Action:** Show an elf "nature-priestess" of your design, male or female. He/she is a healer and a master of herbalism, a mage who gets his/her power from nature. Show this figure casting a spell; perhaps leaves made of light swirl around his/her

### 2\. Sketches

Steven Belledin received that art description and gave this to the creative team for their approval:

![Seedcradle Witch sketch](https://i.ormos.uk/57/72/577259a361f56a9331850939ab090c63e67a173ef3c0b40ce18d7c35eb27842d.jpg)

The sketch looked promising, and Steven was given the go-ahead to proceed.

### 3\. Final Art

In the end, Steven took the bottom sketch and filled it out nicely, as you can see.

[Seedcradle Witch](https://web.archive.org/web/20080820000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Seedcradle%2BWitch)
