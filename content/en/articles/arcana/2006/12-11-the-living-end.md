*Time Spiral* features a 6-card cycle of suspend cards that mimic the functions of powerful spells from times past. [Lotus Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20061211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotus+Bloom) and [Ancestral Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20061211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Vision) saw a lot of play at [Worlds](https://web.archive.org/web/20061211000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/mihara-dutch-crowned-world-champions). One that was encountered less often was [Living End](https://web.archive.org/web/20061211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+End), a suspend throwback to the *Tempest* wrecking-ball, [Living Death](https://web.archive.org/web/20061211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+Death).

![Living Death](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Living+Death)

The art of [Living Death](https://web.archive.org/web/20061211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+Death) depicts a host of zombies rising from their resting places and shoving a living man down into an open grave. The dead burying the living—disturbing!

![Living_Death_640](https://i.ormos.uk/87/e7/87e76a40a029c4d778d711432b78c5973c38c56209883c0d6e1ce72097206698.jpg)  
*[Living Death](https://web.archive.org/web/20061211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+Death) art by Charles Gillespie* 

Now check out the art of *Time Spiral’s* [Living End](https://web.archive.org/web/20061211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+End).

![Living_End_640](https://i.ormos.uk/68/4c/684c04194ce74301c939b2708aa9c15e1207890dbc67cda25ea378072b9f5c77.jpg)  
*[Living End](https://web.archive.org/web/20061211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+End) art by Greg Staples* 

You can see that this is another scene of zombies rising from their graves, this time with the “suspend visual cue” of the blue time-barrier. Note that there’s also a little reference to the original [Living Death](https://web.archive.org/web/20061211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+Death) art: Check out the rose growing from the zombie-head in the foreground of [Living Death](https://web.archive.org/web/20061211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+Death). It’s mimicked in the flowers on the head of the zombie in the background of [Living End](https://web.archive.org/web/20061211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+End).

Note also that the “suspend visual cue” was added in post-production. Greg Staples’s original didn’t have the electric-blue barrier to represent the spell appearing from a rift in time; it was just a shot of these zombies rising out of the earth.
