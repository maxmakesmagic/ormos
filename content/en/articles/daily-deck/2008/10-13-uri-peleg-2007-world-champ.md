Uri Peleg was a well respected Israeli pro who had worked with some of the game's biggest and brightest. Prior to the [2007 World Championships](/en/events/2007/worlds07/historic-wins-for-peleg-swiss-at-worlds-3), however, he hadn't managed a breakthrough performance. That all changed as he bested three separate formats on the weekend before hoisting the title of 2007 **Magic** World Champion. The deck that took him to the title through the Top 8? The above Doran list!

You can live the dream too, by qualifying for the [2008 World Championships](/en/articles/event-coverage/2011/10-10-magic-weekend-san-francisco-and-2011-worlds-event-information), to be held in Memphis, Tennessee December 11-14. How? By participating in the Last Chance Qualifier tournaments being held on [**Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20090511064658/http://www.wizards.com/magic/digital/magiconline.aspx)! The events feature Standard and Limited formats and run from Wednesday, October 15 through Thursday, October 16. For more information, check out the official announcement [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2008/10-03-2008-world-championships-qualifier-on-magic-online).
