### Derek Woloshyn

**Age:** 30  
**Hometown:** Chicago, IL  
**Occupation:** Category Analyst

 

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
*Gatecrash* Prerelease Top 8… so none.

**What was your record and best card from the Sealed Deck:**  
 6-2, [Agent of the Fates](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Agent+of+the+Fates). The card just wins games.

**What was your record and best card from the first draft pod:**  
 3-0. [Spiteful Returned](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spiteful+Returned). Bestow on a creature with intimidate is awesome.

**What was your record and best card from the second draft pod:**  
 2-0-1. [Wingsteed Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingsteed+Rider). Gets out of control very quickly.

**What is your favorite card from *Born of the Gods* for Limited and why?**  
[Hunter’s Prowess](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hunter%E2%80%99s+Prowess). Drawing cards in green feels really good.

**What card from *Theros* has changed in value the most (better/worse) with the addition of *Born of the Gods*?**  
[Voyage’s End](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyage%E2%80%99s+End) has gotten more important because there is less bounce with *Born of the Gods*.
