### A Discussion About Clan & Player Names

* * *

Every once and a while, I find threads on the Message Boards that strike me in a significant way. Sometimes it's because of the humor, other times because it speaks to an important issue. Generally, it's because I see intelligent discussion going on between members of the ***Magic** Online* community.

Since building our community is one of my goals for this site, I feel responsible to point these discussions out. There are two reasons for this.

The first is that there are plenty of players who check out the ***Magic** Online* site that never read the Message Boards. Since the boards are such a good source of information for players of all skill levels, it's good for the community to have many people visiting it regularly--even if only to lurk.

The second reason is to bring issues to the ***Magic** Online* community's attention. Yeah, I know, that's not necessarily my job. And some members of the community do their best to do it themselves. But if it helps a newbie out, makes people speak out about their concerns, or brightens someone's day, it's something I like to foster.

And I'm sure people are going to say my third reason is so I don't have to come up with my own content. Well, you caught me there. Although it does make my job a little easier, I pose this question: Whose voice is more important to ***Magic** Online--*mine or our community's? And if you think our community's voice isn't important to the game, can you blame me for trying to make it a bit more so?

So, without further ado, I'm going to link to a current discussion on [offensive clan and player names started by Random Ranter](https://web.archive.org/web/20030219000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://boards.wizards.com/tcg/ultimatebb.php?ubb=get_topic;f=114;t=002653&origin=magic_magiconline_otb021903). One of my main reasons is to say that we need you to tell us about your concerns, and that Wizards does pay attention--whether there's a public statement about it or not.
