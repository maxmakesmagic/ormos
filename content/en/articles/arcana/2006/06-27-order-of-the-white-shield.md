Lucilde Fiksdotter is the leader of the Order of the White Shield, an elite cadre of Kjeldoran knights who fought the forces of the necromancer Lim-Dûl. Lucilde herself was quoted in the flavor text of several *Ice Age* and *Alliances* cards—her words carried inspiration to all her knights in the long winter before Freyalise's thawing World Spell.

You'll see more from the Order of the White Shield, from their ally King Darien, and from the shadowy agents of Stromgald, at the [*Coldsnap* prerelease](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-prereleases-3). A host of dangers, including the Rimewind mage Heidar, the madman who wants to end the Thaw and ring in the dawn of a new ice age, present many opportunities for the White-Shielders to ride into righteous combat again.

Lucilde Fiksdotter may actually be depicted here, on the *Ice Age* art of Order of the White Shield.
