We just might have stumbled upon a new [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20150206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) in *Fate Reforged*. His name is [Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20150206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang).

I'm not joking. This is my conclusion after testing for the Pro Tour. I will explain why.

First, good size.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang)

You can tell that a 4/5 is great news in Modern by looking at [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino). Remember the biggest creatures for each mana cost. One, [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20150206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl). Two, [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20150206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf). Three, [Anafenza, the Foremost](https://web.archive.org/web/20150206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anafenza%2C+the+Foremost).

Why is [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20150206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) so strong? Because it becomes a 4/5 right away with land, creature, instant, and sorcery in the graveyard. A 4/5 is bigger than pretty much anything in the format. Not dying to one burn spell or [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20150206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay) is great. In a midrange matchup, whoever has the toughest creatures alive decides the match.

Second, it's playable for one or two mana on turn thee.

You think I'm joking? Picture this: Activate fetch lands each turn, casting discard spells, play one removal, and you already have five cards in your graveyard. You can play Tasigur and still have mana left up. I know this is a best-case scenario, but Tasigur's six mana is way cheaper than you think. It also slows down your opponent's [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20150206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf)!

Third, card advantage.

Although with some uncertainty, you get one card for four mana. Tasigur isn't only good for his size. His ability brings him to a different level. He comes out approximately the same turn as [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20150206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf), has about the same size, and does more!

You're starting to like this guy, huh?
