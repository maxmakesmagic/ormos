You know what creature type is awesome? Goblin! Judging by his taste in Commander decks, TCGPlayer.com columnist Cassidy Silver seems to agree. In a recent article, Cassidy writes about decks built on a theme—and in this case, that theme is goblins! If you've been looking to goblin your opponent out on Commander, take some inspiration from Cassidy's deck.

Cassidy Silver's Kiki-Jiki Commander
