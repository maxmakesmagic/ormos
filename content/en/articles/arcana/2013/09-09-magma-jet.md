Last week it was revealed that we are reprinting Magma Jet in *Theros*, and there was much rejoicing. Not only because the card is back (and boy do I love casting Magma Jet) but also because of the gorgeous new art by Maciej Kuciara.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/tcg/products/ths/obasdfkjw8324lz/tmCCTRI2YFo_EN_LR.jpg) 

  

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/arc1316_adsad_magmajet.jpg) 

Magma Jet | Art by [Maciej Kuciara](https://web.archive.org/web/20130909000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22maciej%20kuciara%22])

Let's take a look at the art description that brought this beautiful painting to life:

Color: Red spell  
 Location: Your choice  
 Action: This spell represents a damaging blast of molten lava. Start with the reference on pg. 49B. Using the reference as inspiration, design a female cleric from Akros. She's summoning a blast of molten lava out of the ground. It shoots up like a fiery geyser. She's in a dynamic pose as she casts her spell. The location is your choice. Maybe she’s on a rocky hillside with a Greek-inspired temple in the background.  
 Focus: The lava  
 Mood: I wield the power of fire!
