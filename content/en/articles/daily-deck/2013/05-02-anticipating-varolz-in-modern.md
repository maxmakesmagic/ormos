Recently, osmanozguney finished 3–1 in a Modern Daily event with a black-green aggro deck with Death's Shadow that used Dark Confidant, Overgrown Tomb, fetch lands, Putrid Leech, Thoughtseize, and Dismember to pay life to make it big early in the game. 

 ![Varolz, the Scar-Striped](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Varolz,%20the%20Scar-Striped&options=) 

While this is a relatively interesting strategy in Modern, in that it isn't all over, a 3–1 finish with a Tarmogoyf and Dark Confidant deck might not ordinarily warrant closer investigation. However, *Dragon's Maze* is about to come out, and Varolz, the Scar-Striped has been hyped as part of a two-card combo with Death's Shadow to turn Death's Shadow into a one-mana "Aura" that gives +13/+13, which can be, as we say in the business, "big game." 

The question has been finding a shell for this combo, and there are some who think it's all hype because the cards aren't independently good enough. I'm optimistic about this combo specifically because I think each card is good on its own, and they fit so well in the shell osmanozguney has built that I wonder if it was being tested specifically in anticipation of Varolz, the Scar-Striped. 

Thoughtseize and Inquisition of Kozilek are perfect for making sure your opponent can't kill one of your creatures before you invest your +13/+13 and, incidentally, Tarmogoyf doesn't make a bad Aura after it's dead, either. 

The question is, what can be cut to make room for Varolz, the Scar-Striped? Unfortunately, I don't have an easy answer for that. All the cards in this deck look both good and important, and I expect it to be more a question of trimming than a direct replacement. Maybe I'd try three Varolz, the Scar-Stripeds over a Liliana, an Inquisition of Kozilek, and a Dismember to start. 

osmanozguney's Death's Shadow
