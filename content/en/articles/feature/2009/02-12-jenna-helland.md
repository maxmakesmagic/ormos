Jenna Helland is a writer and designer for Wizards of the Coast in Seattle, Washington. Her works include "The Cloudbreaker" in *The Shadowmoor Anthology, The Planeswalker's Guide to Alara* (with Doug Beyer), *Path of the Planeswalker* (with Brady Dommermuth and Doug Beyer), and *[The Fanged Crown](https://web.archive.org/web/20090212000000/http://archive.wizards.com/dnd/Product.aspx?x=dnd/products/frnovel/239577400).*
