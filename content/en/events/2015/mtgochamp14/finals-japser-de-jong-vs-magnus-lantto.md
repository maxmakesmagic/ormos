Both Jasper De Jong and Magnus Lantto, the first and second-seed of the Top 4 players, showed off their knowledge of multiple formats all weekend. The two represent some very different strategies in all of the Constructed formats, which leads to some very interesting match-ups in the finals.

**Vintage**

The two players both 3-0'd the Vintage portion of their tournament with blue control decks looking to gain advantage through token-generating creatures. While De Jong preferred a light number of [Monastery Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Mentor)s, Lantto was all about [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer), and the [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy) or four that go along quite nicely with it.

Magnus Lantto was in the driver's seat for the entirety of Game 1, leading with a blind call on [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy) to hit De Jong's [Force of Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+of+Will). De Jong, who had to mulligan to six, was card light, and Lantto's plays left him with few resources to combat what Lantto was capable of doing.

After a few turns, Lantto found a [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer) after a Gush, and the elemental tokens that followed made quick work of De Jong.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/mtgochamp14/mtgochamp14finals_dejong.jpg)  
*Jasper De Jong looks at some sideboarding options after a brutal first game.*

The second game wasn't any better, as De Jong went to six cards, had his [Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain) hit with [Mental Misstep](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mental+Misstep), and was left with four lands and a [Force of Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+of+Will). Lantto, meanwhile, had a hand full of gas. While De Jong was able to effectively "[Ancestral Recall](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Recall)" with a [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm), shuffling back some of his lands for the three spells he found off of the powerful cantrip, Lantto undid the damage with [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer) and another [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy), stripping De Jong of anything relevant and locking up the match.

**Modern**

While De Jong demonstrated he could beat Elf Company, Lantto's deck, with his Melira & Company deck on Friday, this was not what happened in Game 1. Playing a land tapped rather than untapped left De Jong without the ability to leave white mana up when [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company) in response to [Chord of Calling](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chord+of+Calling) for three. This proved devastating, as [Eternal Witness](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eternal+Witness) got back a [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile) that he could no longer cast, while Lantto found [Ezuri, Renegade Leader](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ezuri%2C+Renegade+Leader), which cemented the win.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/mtgochamp14/mtgochamp14finals_lantto.jpg)  
*Magnus Lantto steamrolled through the Vintage portion of the finals. Will he do the same for the Modern portion?*

The second game went about as fast, with De Jong missing a land drop on a critical third turn, while Lantto flooded the field with elves. Multiple [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company) and [Ezuri, Renegade Leader](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ezuri%2C+Renegade+Leader) locked up the game, the match, and the championship title for Lantto.

*Congratulations to Magnus Lantto, 2014 Magic Online Champion!*
