The main thing about Team Draft is the seating arrangement in the pod. Given two teams (Team A and Team B) each with three players, seating alternates between the teams as you go around the table. No communication, verbal or non-verbal, is allowed between players during the draft.

With the exception that communication between team members is allowed during match play, all other rules are identical to standard [Booster Draft Format](http://wotc-mx1.bkom.com/content/gameinfoformatsboosterdraft).
