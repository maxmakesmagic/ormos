The latest Extended Pro Tour Qualifier on [**Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20110414083445/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Digital/MagicOnline.aspx?) was taken down by blue-black Faeries. The archetype proved powerful in Standard and is putting up some respectable numbers in the Extended world. It relies on Faeries like Spellstutter Sprite, Mistbind Clique, and Bitterblossom to beat the opponent down while disrupting their day-to-day operations. Mana Leak and Cryptic Command help keep an opponent down by stopping their big spells dead in their tracks!
