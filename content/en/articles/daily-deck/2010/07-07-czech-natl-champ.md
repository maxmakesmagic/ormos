Good ole' red was what Petr Nahodil relied on to take the title of 2010 National Champion for the Czech Republic! His version featured both Smoldering Spires and Teetering Peaks and had a pair of Earthquakes to help keep the ground clear. Petr will captain his national team at the [2010 World Championships](https://web.archive.org/web/20121103022300/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/worlds) to be held in Chiba, Japan in December.
