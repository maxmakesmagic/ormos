You may know [Jedit Ojanen](https://web.archive.org/web/20050329000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jedit+Ojanen) as a 7-mana blue-and-white legend from *Legends*: 

![Jedit Ojanen](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Jedit+Ojanen)

But he also starred in a 1996 comic book from Armada / Acclaim Comics written to tell his story:

  

During issue 1 of 2 of Jedit's story, he encounters other creatures seen on **Magic** cards. He spots a [Fire Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20050329000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fire+Drake) and gets a [Healing Salve](https://web.archive.org/web/20050329000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Healing+Salve) from passing [Desert Nomads](https://web.archive.org/web/20050329000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desert+Nomads).
