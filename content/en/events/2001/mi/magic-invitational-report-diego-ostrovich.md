For me, the process of qualification for the Invitational started many months before Worlds, in February. After spending summer vacation with some other **Magic** players, we decided to create a new team, with the best players of the country, which finally ended consisting on Matias Gabrenja, Jose Barbero, Gabriel Caligaris and me, and we started testing for our Nationals by March. Since we were friends of some guys from Chile, we decided to start testing with them, and then a testing group from Brazil joined us, and we ended up with a b-u-g madness deck that won me my second National Championship in a row, and Gabriel the 3rd spot on the National Team. After this tourney, we tested a lot of Standard for Grand Prix-Sao Paulo, but the alliance between the countries was broken, so we tested on our own.

We finished with a u-b Psychatog that Gabriel Caligaris used to take the crown, and after that, we talked with some other guys and we created the mailing list that's still working. The list consisted of all the players we knew were qualified for Worlds, and nowadays, I add the players that are qualified for the Pro Tours and take out the ones that are not qualified.

That´s how what´s now called ¨Bandana¨ (which originally was LatinTesting, the name of the list. Bandana was our local team from Argentina) was created.

Well, you all know the rest of the story concerning our team, so I won´t bore you any more with our team´s history and I´ll go to the Invitational, that´s why you´re reading anyway.

After many hours of flight, I met Carlos at the airport. We went to the hotel, and we decided to go out to eat something, which wasn´t the best of the ideas, because after a 40 minute walk, we ended up at a deli which wasn´t really exciting.

We came back to the hotel and took some rest. After some time, Carlos went to call home and he found some other players in the lobby and some Wizards´s staff. After he came back to the room, they called us for a draft and we agreed happily.

The day after, we went for sightseeing after a tour of WotC Headquarters, which was really nice, and we ended up talking with Carlos, Mark Rooswater, Alex Shvartsman and Brian Davis, and then we went to the restaurant, which was very good.

Well now on to the tourney:

For the Two-Headed Giant portion, all of us were teammed with our roomates, so in the first round Carlos and I faced Gary Wise and Einvind Nitter. Both Carlos and I were mana screwed this game and lost because of that. They had built their decks better though.  
 0-1

### 4th round: Alex Shvartsman - blue-green madness

1st game: He got a blue-green madness hand and he destroyed me without much resistance  
. 2nd game: After I take a mulligan, I played my second land after he had Arrogant Wurm, Looter, Rootwalla and Wonder in play - try to figure out the rest.  
 0-4

### 5th round: Itaru Ishida - 4 color black

His deck was supposed to absolutely crush Psychatog, since it had Persecute, Deep Analysis, Burning Wish, Duress, Haunting Echoes, Pernicious Deed and more.  
 The first game, he just destroyed my hand and when I had almost recovered, he killed me with 2 Grizzly Fates and 2 flashbacks. In the second game, I killed him with an Atog after an Upheaval. In the third game, I had control of the game the whole time and killed him with Atog and Familiar beatdown. 

1-4 And I won my first match!
