*What's your favorite Constructed deck of all time?*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4d/df/4ddf17870927d1fa29f7c99e3b74f59ad7b39456776c837fd321939967d75c7b.jpg)

**Mike Turian:** Pick just one! What a difficult question! Should I pick Angry Hermit? Slugageddon? The Car Deck? Green-White Maro? Squirrel-Opposition? They all have a special place in my heart, but I will pick [Red-Green Madness](/en/articles/event-coverage/2008/12-06-magic-the-gathering-2). I loved the feel of drawing from the Red-Green Madness deck. 

I played hundreds of games with the deck. [Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20090929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel), [Call of the Herd](https://web.archive.org/web/20090929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Call+of+the+Herd), [Violent Eruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20090929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Violent+Eruption), and [Basking Rootwalla](https://web.archive.org/web/20090929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basking+Rootwalla) gave the deck explosive starts.

Starting with a [Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20090929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel) in your hand would unlock the sheer power of the deck. I loved the deck so much that when a reporter asked to take a picture of me with an opening hand, I took the green-red deck and constructed the perfect hand. Even though you could only see the backs of the cards in the photograph, I will always know how amazing that draw was.
