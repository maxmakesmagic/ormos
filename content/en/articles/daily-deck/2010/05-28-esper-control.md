Eric Froehlich is one of **Magic**'s great players, having teamed with even the likes of Jon Finkel in days gone by. But his career of late has taken him away from the bright lights of the Pro Tour, and it was a surprise to see him sitting down to battle at Grand Prix–Washington D.C., the largest such event ever held in North America. But if you thought Eric was rusty, you needn't have worried; he ripped off a 9-0 run, undefeated through Day 1! His deck was a Conley Woods concoction to serve as a counter to the White-Blue Control and Jund lists popping up all over, and it would make the perfect concoction for your upcoming Friday Night **Magic**! For more information on Grand Prix–Washington D.C., visit the official [event coverage](/en/events/2010/gpwas10/nelson-king-of-the-capital).
