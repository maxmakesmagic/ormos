Alongside the draftable *Commander Legends* set, we're releasing two entry-level Commander Decks featuring both new cards and reprints. The decklists were revealed earlier on [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/), but we've also provided them here, just in case you're the type who likes reading things twice.

***NOTE: Decklists pull the newest printing of each card automatically from Gatherer, printings which are not in this product. Decklists are not card-for-card product displays but rather interactive lists of the cards you can expect to see in each deck.***
