## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/03/c4/03c4647c8fe51ab8c0ccdd1d22b997a1471dbcafe0a519f2e719152aa8717cb3.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f3/fc/f3fc3126c19bc50e7c50ad734ce056e0d6a1c7d2a0aec28b7a5196debab0805b.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the pack as well as two full core set deck boxes, nine 15-card booster packs, 80 basic land cards, and a special-edition life counter.

(\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/45/c5/45c5b1acb0620ae9833b7a6174ec24859fc765b1087daa7f4f8f96b1ebf5f0e1.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 cards, a foil premium rare card, two sealed 15-card booster packs, a strategy insert, and a **Magic** learn-to-play guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160623121353/http://magic.wizards.com/node/155356)

(\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ff/b7/ffb75f5f45a32ec7226fdffaa9e5beb8ff61d6dea168aa5c3adecade82266b69.png)

### EVENT DECKS

Battle like the best, right out of the box. Includes a tournament-ready 60-card deck plus a 15-card “sideboard” to swap in as needed, depending on what you expect your competitor will play.

[DECKLIST](https://web.archive.org/web/20160623121353/http://magic.wizards.com/node/155346)

(\* Not available in all languages)
