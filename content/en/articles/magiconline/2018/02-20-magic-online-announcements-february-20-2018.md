## *Magic Online* Championship Coming Up!

It's the most wonderful tiiiiime… of the yeeeeear! Well, at least if you love watching people play awesome digital *Magic*. The *Magic Online* Championship takes place March 2–4 right here at Wizards HQ in Renton, Washington. Featuring 24 of the best MTGO players in the world, a $200,000 prize pool, and a roster of great coverage personalities, this really is the biggest MTGO moment of the year.

All three days of the tournament will stream live on [twitch.tv/magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic). Check out [Mike Rosenberg's article here](/en/articles/organized-play/2018/02-15-magic-online-championship-soon) for more information.

* * *

## *Theros* Flashback Drafts Start Tomorrow

It's time to return to the land of gods and heroes, enchantments and myths. That's right: *Theros* Flashback Drafts are here! Strive to show how heroic you are by bestowing a blessing on one of your monstrosities! Something about devotion!

*Theros* Flashback Drafts run February 21 to 28, and can be found in the Limited Leagues area of the *Magic Online* Play Lobby. Enjoy, and may all your Gods be creatures when you need them!

## Team Modern Super League Continues

With two exciting weeks already on the books, Team Modern Super League is in full swing. Among many highlights, we've already seen the first activation of [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20180220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) in the new era of Modern! Spoiler alert: it's a pretty good *Magic* card.

TMSL airs Tuesdays (like this one!) at 5 p.m. on [twitch.tv/magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic). Learn more about Team Modern Super League [here](https://vintagesuperleague.com/tmsl-info/), or check out the video below.

## MOCS Monthly This Saturday

A *Magic Online* Championship Series Monthly Event will take place this Saturday, February 24, starting at 7 a.m. The format will be *Rivals of Ixalan*-*Ixalan* Sealed, and players can enter the event with 35 Qualifier Points (QPs). Top finishers qualify for the Playoff Event on May 5 (the format for which will be Modern).

Monthly and Playoff Events also award top players MOCS Leaderboard Points for the year. Top players on the [Leaderboard](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/magic-online-leaderboard-points) at the end of the 2018 MOCS will qualify for the year-end Championship.

* * *

## PTQ Finals Event This Sunday

A Pro Tour Qualifier Finals Event will kick off this Sunday, February 25, at 7 a.m. The format will be *Rivals of Ixalan-Ixalan* Sealed. A red or blue PTQ Finals Entry Token is required to join this event, and the winner will qualify for Pro Tour *Dominaria*.

Learn more about *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20180220000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers).

## Build Notes

Limited | [Ambuscade](https://web.archive.org/web/20180220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ambuscade) now functions correctly.

Sideboard | You are again able to submit sideboard changes as many times as you want before your opponent submits.

Collection | Closing the "Added to your collection" window will no longer crash the client in rare cases.

Modern | The trigger from [Reality Smasher](https://web.archive.org/web/20180220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reality+Smasher) is functioning correctly again.

Modern | [Exhaustion](https://web.archive.org/web/20180220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exhaustion) correctly prevents creatures and lands from untapping.

Modern, Limited | [Teferi, Mage of Zhalfir](https://web.archive.org/web/20180220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%2C+Mage+of+Zhalfir) correctly grants flash to all creatures you own that aren't on the battlefield.
