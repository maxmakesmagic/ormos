## ROOKIE OF THE YEAR STANDINGS: 2013-14

*Updated October 30, 2014*

Congratulations to Raymond Perez Jr., 2013-2014 Rookie of the Year!

*   [2013-14 Player of the Year Race](https://web.archive.org/web/20160604025147/http://magic.wizards.com/node/231291)

The player with the most Pro Points at the end of a tournament season who has not participated in a Pro Tour or World Championship tournament in any previous season is identified as the Rookie of the Year. These are the final 2013-2014 Premier Play Rookie of the Year standings.

This page shows all rookie players who have received at least 10 Pro Points in the 2013-14 season (from May 20, 2013 to August 3, 2014).

*(Note: Due to the findings of an investigation, the previous Rookie of the Year title was revoked and given to Raymond Perez Jr., the next player with the most Pro Points.)*
