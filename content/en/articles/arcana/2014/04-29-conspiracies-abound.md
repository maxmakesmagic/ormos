This past weekend, Tokyo played host to the Niconico Chokaigi convention. **Magic** was there, and among the things we showed was a sneak peek at a new legendary creature in *Conspiracy*.

And since we showed him in Japanese at Niconico Chokaigi, here's your first look at Brago, King Eternal *in English*!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c5/98/c5989425c61da3d8d0123582d06f1e79d46080a2f0fbcb7c769a4483f6cfa44e.jpg) 

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b3/7c/b37ccfdb85bda739add9c16afe2de688bd950128e583519fd1477ddf7d98377c.jpg) 

Brago, King Eternal | Art by [Karla Ortiz](https://web.archive.org/web/20140429000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22karla%20ortiz%22])

And for those who missed the roll out, we had a bit of a puzzle quest and social media hunt that slowly revealed the full Dack Fayden card for *Conspiracy*. Players were able to decode, puzzle crack, and figure out our hints as we slowly revealed Dack.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/26/66/2666eee13762e87d00164b4d9d72853782b9e054555706dd6a275ca12c514320.jpg) 

  

The truth is, *Conspiracy* doesn't hit shelves until June 6, 2014, so don't let this peek into the future distract you from the fact that *Journey into Nyx* hits shelves this Friday!
