### Important!

  

*Last update: 10:02 p.m. London time* 

**Friday's start of Pro Tour-London will go on as scheduled. Heightened security in London has suspended service for the Underground (the Tube) but bus and national rail service has resumed. Please follow local emergency services' instructions, or visit http://www.tfl.gov.uk/ for London transit updates. Please use caution when traveling to the event site. Provisions will be made Friday morning for players unable to arrive Thursday in time for registration. We will post additional information here as it becomes available.** 

Come join Wizards of the Coast for our next Pro Tour stop in London, England. This information will help you to prepare now to attend in July. Pro Tour London will be held July 8-10, 2005.

###  Mandatory Registration

Player registration will occur at Hotel Ibis Earls Court London (47 Lillie Road) 16:00-20:00 on Thursday, July 7th.

Players must have a signed [waiver and consent form](http://www.wizards.com/dci/downloads/consent_form_london_2005.pdf) to compete at Pro Tour London. Forms can be downloaded at **wizards.com/protour** and will be available onsite. Minors must have a parent or guardian’s signature on these forms.

Bring these forms with you to registration. We will make forms available for competitors at registration, if necessary.

**At registration, all participants must present one of the following forms of identification upon request:** 

*   a driver’s license, or
*   a passport, or
*   a copy of their birth certificates *and* photo IDs (school IDs are acceptable).

If you anticipate any difficulty registering during the official registration hours, please contact Andy Heckt at (425) 254-2941 or andy.heckt@wizards.com.

###  Format and Schedule

*All Universal Tournament Rules and Magic: the Gathering Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document* 

**Format** 

*   Booster Draft
*   1 *Champions of Kamigawa*TM, 1 *Betrayers of Kamigawa*TM, and 1 *Saviors of Kamigawa*TM booster pack will be used for drafting.
*   Competition will consist of 5 Booster Drafts, each followed by 3 rounds of competition.
*   Check [www.thedci.com](http://www.thedci.com/) for Booster Draft rules.

**Round Length** 

*   15 Swiss Rounds total, 55 minutes per round
*   Top 8 single-elimination finals have no time limit
*   When time is called, the active player will finish the turn and five additional turns will be taken
*   Stalling and slow play rules will be strictly enforced during the tournament.

**Advancement** 

*   All players may participate in the first 2 drafts and Swiss rounds of Friday's competition.
*   Players with 12 or more match points after round 6 will advance to draft 3. Draft 3 will take place on Friday immediately following round 6 (See schedule below).
*   The top 8 players after round 15 will advance to Sunday's top 8 single-elimination finals.

**Top 8** 

*   Top 8 matches will be best 3 of 5
*   The Top 8 players will be seated randomly at the draft table immediately prior to drafting
*   The Top 8 bracket will be determined using this random seating.
*   Where necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will still be used to determine final order in the standings.

**Notes** 

*   48K, REL 5
*   Players are responsible for knowing all DCI rules and [high-level tournament rules](http://www.wizards.com/protour/images/highlevelrules.pdf).
*   Players must bring pen, paper, card sleeves and appropriate counters.
