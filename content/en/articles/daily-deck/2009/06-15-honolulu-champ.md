Quietus Spike is not the type of rare most players think of when they think "bomb" but for Kazuya "The Chief" Mitamura, it was more than enough. He used the equipment, combined with Vithian Stingers, to take down the Top 8 at the recent Pro Tour-Honolulu. For more about Pro Tour-Honolulu, including all the top decklists, [read the official event coverage](/en/events/2009/pthon09/mitamura-finds-gold-in-paradise).
