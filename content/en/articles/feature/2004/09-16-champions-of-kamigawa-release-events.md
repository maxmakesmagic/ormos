Monday, October 25 9:00 am PST, *Championships of Kamigawa* goes on sale in the MTG:O store.

NOTE: *Champions of Kamigawa* becomes legal in ALL Constructed formats the day it goes on sale (October 25). All casual rooms and constructed queues will use *Champions of Kamigawa* starting on October 25. Constructed Premier events will not run until November 2 and will use *Champions of Kamigawa* starting on that date.

*Champions of Kamigawa* Release events begin Thursday, October 28 and will have 4 components:

Release Leagues  
 Draft Queues  
 Premier Event Flights (2x and 4x Prizes)  
 Release Championship

***Champions of Kamigawa* Release Leagues**

SIGNUP: October 28, 9:00am – November 2, 9:00am (the final 1 week, 2x league will start no later than 9AM on November 2). Release Leagues will have a maximum of 256 players each and will auto-spawn (as soon as a league reaches 256 players, another will begin registration).

LEAGUE DURATION: 1 week

PRODUCT: CHK Tournament Pack and 2 CHK Booster packs.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 2x prizes, Participation Avatar: Seshiro the Anointed, Records of 5-0 get Prize Avatar: Eight-and-a-Half-Tails. 

Avatars will be distributed Approx. 3 weeks after the last league concludes

***Champions of Kamigawa* Draft Queues**

QUEUES BEGIN: October 28 3:00 pm

FORMATS: Booster and Rochester Draft

PRODUCT: 3 CHK Boosters

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 4-3-2-2, 8-4 (Booster Only)

***Champions of Kamigawa* Premier Events (2x Flights)**

DURATION: October 29 9:00 am – November 2 9:00 am (last starts at 9am). 2x Flights will have 32 players per flight and will auto-spawn (as soon as a 32 person flight begin, another will begin signing up and will start when full) 

PRODUCT: CHK Tournament Pack + 2 CHK Boosters Sealed deck, top 8 booster draft

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 2x Prizes, Top 8

***Champions of Kamigawa* Premier Events (4x Flights)**

DURATION: October 29 12:00 pm – November 2 9:00 am (last starts at 9am). 4x Flights start every 3 hours. Each flight will have a minimum of 24 players, maximum of 128 players.

PRODUCT: CHK Tournament Pack + 2 CHK Boosters Sealed deck, top 8 booster draft

ADMISSION: 5 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 4x Prizes, Manual Pay to Top 16 (Prizes for 9-16 place will be paid out within 72 hours of event completion)

The winner of the Championship will also receive the framed, original artwork\* for the *Champions of Kamigawa* card *Time Stop* by artist Scott Fischer.

In order to remain eligible for the prize, all qualified players must email molworldsqt@wizards.com by Wedensday, November 3, 2005 at 12:00 PM PST with the following information: Full Legal Name, Full Address, Phone, and DCI number with their Username as the subject. (Account passwords will not be requested--never give your password to anyone.) In addition, during the tournament, Wizards of the Coast staff (marked with the Wizards logo beside their online name) may ask players for their name and/or contact information. Do not give out your information to anyone who is not a Wizards of the Coast Staff member (marked with the Wizards logo beside their online name).

Failure to provide accurate information may result in significant penalties, including suspension from both online & physical **Magic** tournaments.
