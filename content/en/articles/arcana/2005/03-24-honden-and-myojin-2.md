Each of the [Honden](https://web.archive.org/web/20050324000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/default.asp?first=1&last=100&term=honden&Field_Name=on&Field_Rules=on&Field_Type=on&setfilter=ChampionsofKamigawa&colorfilter=All&output=summary&sort=name&x=36&y=27) from *Champions of Kamigawa* is a shrine dedicated to the worship of a powerful, godlike kami. The name of each Honden corresponds to the name of a legendary spirit -- [Myojin](https://web.archive.org/web/20050324000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/default.asp?first=1&last=100&term=myojin&Field_Name=on&Field_Rules=on&Field_Type=on&setfilter=ChampionsofKamigawa&colorfilter=All&output=summary&sort=name) -- in the set.

But there are correspondences in the art as well. Compare Kev Walker's art of the Myojin to the Honden below. Note how the shrines are mostly ruined; that's an indication of the abandonment the shrines faced during the Kami War, when the spirit and mortal worlds turned on one another.

  
*[Honden of Life's Web](https://web.archive.org/web/20050324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Honden+of+Life%27s+Web) art by Rob Alexander*

  
*[Myojin of Life's Web](https://web.archive.org/web/20050324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myojin+of+Life%27s+Web) art by Kev Walker*

  
*[Honden of Night's Reach](https://web.archive.org/web/20050324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Honden+of+Night%27s+Reach) art by Jim Nelson*

![Myojin of Night](https://i.ormos.uk/de/b8/deb8444ddf0e28176eea5561e5eaa19fe69f5d078a4ac6265b11b7dbdabbdba9.jpg)  
*[Myojin of Night's Reach](https://web.archive.org/web/20050324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myojin+of+Night%27s+Reach) art by Kev Walker*
