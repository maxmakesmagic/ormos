This weekend, we see the first Standard tournament (Grand Prix Calgary) since the release of ***Magic** 2014*. This weekend is also the ***Magic** 2014* Prerelease weekend **Magic Online** (it's available there on Monday, July 29). Next week is Worlds Week, when both an individual World Champion and a World **Magic** Cup champion will be crowned. There's going to be a lot of exciting **Magic** to watch, and if you plan on going to a PTQ this season, you'll want to take note of the Standard decks there. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#theros). Good luck; have fun!
