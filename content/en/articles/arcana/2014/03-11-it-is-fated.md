It's Fated Week here at DailyMTG.com, so let's look at [the Fated cycle](https://web.archive.org/web/20140311000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=cn+&name=+[fated]) from *Born of the Gods*! Trick took [a close-up look at one](/en/articles/arcana/2014/01-30-fated-retribution-2) already, but since Mark Rosewater wrote this week about [the history behind the cycle's design](/en/articles/makingmagic/2014/03-10-twist-of-fate), today we're going to show off all five art pieces.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fa/83/fa834da11260f08980e9bc80b04e2a5aa78071d436e747fa4ea53314af37e69f.jpg) 

Fated Infatuation | Art by [Winona Nelson](https://web.archive.org/web/20140311000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22Winona+Nelson%22])
