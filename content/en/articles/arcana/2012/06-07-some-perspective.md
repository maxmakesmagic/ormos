[Malignus](https://web.archive.org/web/20120607000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Malignus) is one of the Mythic rares in *Avacyn Restored*. But what is it? Well, it's a giant elemental spirit that appears to be pretty unhappy with some villagers. If you check the art closer you'll see he's rolling through a village and laying waste to it.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/989_malignus.Jpg)  

As was noted in the set's FAQ, [Malignus](https://web.archive.org/web/20120607000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Malignus) is not hindered by Protection of any sort, including "Protection from everything" ala [Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20120607000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Progenitus).
