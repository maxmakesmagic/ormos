Everyone knows red decks can win, but did you know that in today's Standard, they can win on a theme? Just ask Douglas Scheinberg! He made the Top 16 of the StarCityGames.com Standard Open in Edison, New Jersey playing this modern take on a Goblins deck. Ember Hauler, Goblin Chieftain, Goblin Guide, and Spikeshot Elder are just some of the members of the red tribe that allowed him to damage the opponent enough for his smattering of burn spells to finish the deed.
