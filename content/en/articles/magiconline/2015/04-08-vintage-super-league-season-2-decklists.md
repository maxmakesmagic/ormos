Below are the decklists used by Eric Froehlich and Luis Scott-Vargas in their [tie-breaker match](http://www.twitch.tv/magic/b/647201483) for 2nd place. Read Randy Buehler's preview of the match-up [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/04-07-vintage-super-league-episode-10).

You can follow the links below to see the three different sets of decklists players used through the Vintage Super League season.

Watch the Vintage Super League live on [twitch.tv/magic](http://www.twitch.tv/magic) Tuesday nights at 6:00 p.m. Pacific (UTC-7).

View the past Vintage Super League decklists by following the below links.
