Although **Birds of Paradise** got benched for the *Ninth Edition* of **Magic**'s Core Set, it got a timely reprint in the coloriffic *Ravnica: City of Guilds* set.

Marcelo Vignali's art for the new Birds puts them in a fresh light, their plumage richly prismatic against drab Ravnican architecture:

![Birds of Paradise art by Marcelo Vignali](https://i.ormos.uk/f6/e9/f6e9d4366d655f1e442dc452fb1462c4a7706c7cda614ba841424861fd528629.jpg "Birds of Paradise art by Marcelo Vignali")  
*Birds of Paradise art by Marcelo Vignali*

> *Long ago, birds of paradise littered the skies. Thanks to the city’s sprawl, most now exist as pets of society’s elite.*

When it came time to commission art for *Dissension's* handy color-fixer **Utopia Sprawl**, the creative team found the perfect spot to show both the city in full bloom, and a picturesque place to show off the new Ravnican Birds. It captures the flavor of a guild-leader's prized pet in a tended garden, while giving a clue to the multicolor-mana capabilities of the card. Check them out here in Ron Spears' art of Utopia Sprawl:

![Utopia Sprawl art by Ron Spears](https://i.ormos.uk/39/e2/39e2c2511f082f6e3b02722bba0fac85e4b3e53d08a0a085c639f7e16306f1a2.jpg "Utopia Sprawl art by Ron Spears")  
*Utopia Sprawl art by Ron Spears*
