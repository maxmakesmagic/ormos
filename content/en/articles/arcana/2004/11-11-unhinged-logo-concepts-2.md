There's more to the artistic development of a **Magic** set than the art of all the individual cards. There's also the logo, expansion symbol and packaging art for the set -- important stuff, since it's art that has to express the tone for the entire set.

We've [already seen](https://web.archive.org/web/20041111000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/unhinged-logo-and-symbol-2004-08-24) the final logo and expansion symbol for *Unhinged*, but those were not the only ideas, by any means.

Here are some of the other ideas proposed for the logo design (thanks to **Magic** art director Jeremy Cranford for providing these):

![Unhinged logo final](https://i.ormos.uk/df/48/df48585b8ad62882a168f88850154e807cd306e6349ed56f0bc05e7658302130.jpg)

This last logo was pretty close in spirit to the final. For comparison, here's that final logo again in full color.
