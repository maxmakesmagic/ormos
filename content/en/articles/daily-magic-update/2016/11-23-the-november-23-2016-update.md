The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on November 23, 2016. Today's Update is brought to you by Standard Showdown, starting this weekend in local game stores around the world!

Tomorrow is Thanksgiving in the US, and Wizards HQ will be closed so that we can all spend some quality time with our friends and families. DailyMTG will be taking a break Thursday and Friday, but we'll be back on Monday with all your regularly scheduled *Magic* goodness. Happy Thanksgiving!

Now, back to the Update.

# Today's Must

1.  [The Best On-Camera Deck of All Time](http://www.starcitygames.com/article/34090_The-Best-On-Camera-Deck-Of-All-Time.html) | StarCityGames.com | Mark Nestico

[Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20161123000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel) is one of the most intriguing build-around cards currently in Standard. Every time it leaves the metagame as a contender, it seems to surge back in with a new game plan of vengeance. Mark breaks down his team's most recent iteration of the deck, which is now in Golgari instead of the usual Temur. I think we'll be seeing a lot more of this deck at Standard Showdown this weekend and in the months to come.

# After That, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [How to Play Planechase: A Multiplayer Variant of *Magic: The Gathering*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wC98RS2YvJk) | Tolarian Community College | The Professor

In case you missed [my recent article on *Planechase Anthology*](/en/articles/feature/2016/11-14-planechase-anthology-a-celebration-of-magics-history), the Professor has a video to help prime you on Planechase as a format as well as what to expect from the launch of *Planechase Anthology* this Friday. If you've never played a game of Planechase, don't miss this!

1.  [Commander VS: Community Outreach!](http://www.starcitygames.com/article/34088_Commander-VS-Community-Outreach.html) | StarCityGames.com | Justin Parnell and Stephen Green

The Commander VS. crew is piloting decks from some of the *Magic* community's most respected content producers, including a [Roon of the Hidden Realm](https://web.archive.org/web/20161123000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roon+of+the+Hidden+Realm) deck from our very own Blake Rasmussen. Blake's deck features [Cloudblazer](https://web.archive.org/web/20161123000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudblazer), [Mulldrifter](https://web.archive.org/web/20161123000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mulldrifter), and [Panharmonicon](https://web.archive.org/web/20161123000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panharmonicon), so you know it's authentic.

1.  [Power Rankings](http://www.channelfireball.com/power-rankings/) | Channel Fireball

Channel Fireball reveals their latest power rankings for cards in Standard, Modern, and Legacy. Personally, I'm glad to finally see my girl Emrakul making the charts in Standard. Between the return of Aetherworks and the rise of Black-Green Delirium, I'm sure she'll be disrupting some board states at Standard Showdown this weekend.

1.  [Millstone Mayhem](http://www.starcitygames.com/article/34086_Daily-Digest-Millstone-Mayhem.html) | StarCityGames.com | Ross Merriam

Ross really wants an answer for Black-Green Delirium in Standard, and I'm with him. His proposal this time is a traditional Blue-Black Control deck centered around milling your opponents out with [Startled Awake](https://web.archive.org/web/20161123000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Startled+Awake), [Minister of Inquiries](https://web.archive.org/web/20161123000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Minister+of+Inquiries), and [Manic Scribe](https://web.archive.org/web/20161123000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Manic+Scribe).

1.  [The 6 Most Misleading Uncommons in *Kaladesh* Draft, Part 1](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/the-6-most-misleading-uncommons-in-kaladesh-draft-part-1/) | Channel Fireball | Frank Lepore

If you're as guilty as I am of drafting [Whirlermaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20161123000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whirlermaker) and then never doing much with it, then Frank is ready to hit you with some hard truths about your *Kaladesh* drafting habits.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Bradley Hill's Jeskai Control](http://magic.tcgplayer.com/db/article.asp?ID=13638&writer=Seth+Manfield&articledate=11-23-2016)

In a Standard currently brimming with White-Blue Flash and Green-Black Delirium, Bradley Hill managed to take second place at the [StarCityGames.com Open Standard in Knoxville](http://www.starcitygames.com/events/191116_knoxville.html) last weekend with this Jeskai Control deck. With the addition of red mana for stronger board control from [Nahiri, the Harbinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20161123000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nahiri%2C+the+Harbinger) and [Harnessed Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20161123000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Harnessed+Lightning), the deck looks to overtake White-Blue Flash as the strongest control deck of the format.
