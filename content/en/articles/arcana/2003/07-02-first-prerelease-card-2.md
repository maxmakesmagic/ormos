We showed you the [promotional card](/en/articles/arcana/2003/06-27-promo-premium-rukh-egg) for the upcoming [Global Celebration tournaments](https://web.archive.org/web/20030702000000/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/resource/10astores) last week. So when did we start making promo cards for events?

Dirtcowl Wurm was the very first "prerelease card," given out at the *Tempest* prerelease tournaments in October of 1997.

![Dirtcowl Wurm – Prerelease](https://i.ormos.uk/3a/1b/3a1bf98e82fc579322c384ff4fc93f9cfdec7832b3ad6bab791e7972914cf705.jpg) 

The card was not "foil;" it was simply a normal version of the card with the word "Prerelease" and the **Magic** "M" stamped in gold leaf on the type line.
