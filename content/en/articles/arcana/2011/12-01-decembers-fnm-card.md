It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20111201000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=events/magic/fnm)! And since it's the first Friday in December, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

![Savage Lands](https://i.ormos.uk/57/57/5757af3209d747f2b2db1ada4210a5d99fa6c171f90fd9b241ffd300868579d4.jpg)  

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards often feature original flavor text and updated wordings.
