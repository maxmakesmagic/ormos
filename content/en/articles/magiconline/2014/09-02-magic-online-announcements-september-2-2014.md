With over fifty cards that have been updated, be sure to check out the full card list, as well as the full change list, included within Adam's [article](/en/articles/magiconline/2014/09-01-magic-2015-cube-update#changes).

Downtime for September 3, 2014

*   All tournaments will close at 5:00 a.m. Pacific (12:00 UTC)
*   Store and trade activity will be suspended at approximately 7:45 a.m. Pacific (14:45 UTC)
*   The system will be down from 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. Pacific (15:00 to 19:00 UTC)

**Build Notes**

**General:**

*   Fixed: Fixed the special refund restriction description link in store product information.
*   Updated: Free server has been updated to use M15 decks.

**Card and Gameplay Fixes:**

*   Fixed: If you concede while scrying, the cards are no longer revealed after the game.
*   Fixed: Corrected cases where mana is incorrectly consumed during cost payment steps.
*   Fixed: Corrected cases where effects like [Chained to the Rocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20140902000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chained+to+the+Rocks) would not return exiled permanents immediately.
*   Fixed: Corrected cases where [Daring Thief](https://web.archive.org/web/20140902000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daring+Thief) does not correctly exchange control of permanents.
*   Fixed: [Spirespine](https://web.archive.org/web/20140902000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spirespine) can now group block an attacking [Tromokratis](https://web.archive.org/web/20140902000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tromokratis).
*   Fixed: [Hunting Wilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20140902000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hunting+Wilds) now correctly untaps permanents when kicked.
