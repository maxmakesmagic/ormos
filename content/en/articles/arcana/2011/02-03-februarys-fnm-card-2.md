It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6)! Since we're now in February, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

![Spellstutter Sprite](https://i.ormos.uk/9e/34/9e345f57fa643464f6f6f6cdc41d6f6e6a6885ef12fb86c9c618d62e5aa4c264.jpg "Spellstutter Sprite") 

  

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards feature the FNM logo in the text box and many have original flavor text and updated wordings.
