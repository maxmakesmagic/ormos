Today marks the start of the Chinese New Year. 2012 is the year of the dragon, and we at Wizards of the Coast know a thing or two about dragons! Take, for example, this carnage-filled casual deck built by none other than R&D member Ken Nagle. Filled to the brim with dragon goodness, Ken has scorched many a planeswalker with this deck. And check out that on-theme Zodiac Dragon!

Ken Nagle's Dragons! 

Casual
