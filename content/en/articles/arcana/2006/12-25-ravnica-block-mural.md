Once upon a Ravnica Block, Greg Staples created a mural for Wizards to use to advertise the plane of Ravnica in print media. The mural depicted all ten guilds contained in said block, weaving guild leaders, champions, guildmages, figureheads and assorted beasties together into one enormous Ravnica family portrait.

For media purposes, the mural was broken into thirds along set lines; you may have seen pieces of it in an [Ask Wizards from April](https://web.archive.org/web/20061225000000/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/asksearchablearchive&term=advertisement_for_guildpact). Each piece tended to appear as a two-page magazine spread for its respective set. Here is the mural restored to its continuous origins:

*Art by Greg Staples*
