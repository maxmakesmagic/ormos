The long summer season of premier events is almost at an end. A record crowd of 1,371 players made their way to Portland, Oregon to take their last shots in a ***Magic** 2011* Limited Grand Prix, with Martin Juza emerging victorious over Thomas Kiene. The pre-*Scars of Mirrodin* event season comes to a close this weekend with the Brazil National Championship, with coverage beginning Saturday morning right here on DailyMTG.com. After that, all eyes will be on the [*Scars of Mirrodin* Prerelease](https://web.archive.org/web/20100916000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prerelease-facts) on September 25-26.
