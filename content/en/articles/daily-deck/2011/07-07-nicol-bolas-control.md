The next *Duel Decks* installment has been announced. The two factions represented? Powerful planeswalkers Nicol Bolas and Ajani. Here's a deck list Zac Hill (now a Wizards R&D member) used to make the Top 8 of [Pro Tour Honolulu](/en/events/2009/pthon09/mitamura-finds-gold-in-paradise). The deck prominently featured Nicol Bolas, Planeswalker. You can see the packaging of the next *Duel Decks* [here](/en/articles/arcana/2011/07-05-ajani-vs-nicol-bolas).
