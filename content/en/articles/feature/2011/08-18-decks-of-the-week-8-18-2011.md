Another week of Nationals, another wide range of Top 8 Standard decks. Helge Nelson [triumphed at Germany Nationals](/en/articles/event-coverage/2011/08-12-2011-germany-national-championship) with a mono-red build featuring a single [Sword of War and Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20110818000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+War+and+Peace), while long-time pro Joel Calafell [took home the trophy at Spain Nationals](https://web.archive.org/web/20220517134507/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/event-coverage/coverage-spain-national-championship-2011-08-13) with Splinter Twin. The Standard metagame gets another burst of energy this weekend with live coverage from National Championships in Great Britain and Canada.
