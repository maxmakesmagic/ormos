This quarter we bring you a **Magic** mana puzzle that will break your brain and interviews with R&D’s MaRo and BraDo. (That’s Mark Rosewater and Brady Dommermuth to the uninitiated.) There's also new short fiction “The Dreamer and the Fiend” and a big helping of amazing illustrations from Wizards.Community members.
