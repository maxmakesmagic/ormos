## *Modern Horizons 2* Leagues and Premier Play Events Return

*   The *Modern Horizons 2* Sealed League and Draft League are planned to return on Wednesday, July 21, and run until *Innistrad: Midnight Hunt* events launch on Thursday, September 9 (the Phantom Draft League will not return).
*   Those Leagues are returning to act as support for the four Limited Premier Events currently slated between now and *Innistrad: Midnight Hunt*—the plan is to use *Modern Horizons 2* for all of them instead of *Adventures in the Forgotten Realms*.
    *   Increased entry options of 50 Event Tickets or 500 Play Points will be used for the two *Modern Horizons 2* Super Qualifiers
    *   The QP entry option will remain at 40 for both Qualifiers and Super Qualifiers
*   *Modern Horizons 2* will be used as the Limited Preliminary format starting July 21 at 4 p.m. instead of *Adventures in the Forgotten Realms*.
    *   Entry options: 45 Event Tickets or 450 Play Points

* * *

## *Modern Horizons 2* Limited Preliminary Events Prize Increase

Starting with tomorrow's 4 p.m. Pacific *Modern Horizons 2* Limited Preliminary event and lasting until the season ends with *Innistrad: Midnight Hunt*, we've increased the prizes of 4–0 and 3–1 finishes in *Modern Horizons 2* Limited Preliminaries by 150 Play Points each.

**Entry**: 45 Event Tickets or 450 Play Points

**Prizes**:

*   4–0: **750** **Play Points** and 40 Qualifier Points
*   3–1: **450** **Play Points** and 20 Qualifier Points
*   2–2: 150 Play Points and 10 Qualifier Points

## August Is Spotlight Cube Series Month

For the next 3 weeks, *Magic Online* will be featuring a new cube from the community each week! And at the culmination, we'll bring *Legacy Cube* back. It's a good time to be a cube fan! The three Spotlight Cube Series cubes you'll have the pleasure of drafting with starting tomorrow are:

*   August 4–11: Autumn Burchett's *Bant Cube*
*   August 11–18: Ellie Rice's *Temur Cube*
*   August 18–25: Ryan Saxe's *Classic Cube*

* * *

## Spotlight Cube Series: *Bant Cube*

Autumn Burchett's *Bant Cube* runs for a week starting tomorrow, August 4, through August 11 and is our first community entry during Spotlight Cube Series Month. *Bant Cube* mixes synergy-focused archetypes with pockets of random interaction to create a sense of discovery that makes for some truly exciting decks, all in a color combination where death and chaos hold no sway. For more information on archetypes and the inspiration behind the cube, be sure to check out [Autumn's *Bant Cube* article](/en/articles/magiconline/2021/08-03-spotlight-cube-series-bant-cube).

## Showcase Challenge Weekend

Players looking to prove their mettle against the best players in *Magic Online* will want to use their QPs to enter *Magic Online* Champions Showcase (MOCS) events such as the returning Showcase Challenges this weekend. These MOCS events are a set of linked events designed to highlight the best players on *Magic Online* each season, culminating in the Champions Showcase and its prize pool of $70,000 and a Set Championship invitation.

On Saturday, August 7, we have the Vintage Showcase Challenge at 10 a.m. PT, and on Sunday, August 8, we finish things off with the Legacy Showcase Challenge at 8 a.m. PT. Each of these events requires 40 QPs to enter. The Top 8 finishers from each event will earn a qualification token to enter the appropriate Showcase Qualifier in August.

Following Showcase Challenge Weekend, your next opportunity to continue toward qualification for the Season 2 MOCS are the Last Chance Events.
