Last weekend was the ***Magic** 2011* Prerelease and if you went to a large regional Prerelease you probably saw a celebrity gunslinger there, playing cards all weekend! If you were in Minneapolis that meant playing against R&D member Bill Stark, though his red deck to the untrained eye didn't seem to have a lot of ***Magic** 2011* influence. "I played red simply because Lightning Bolt came back!" he explained. "Lightning Bolt was such a fundamental element of my early years in **Magic** that when given the gift of playing with it for two years in a row I *had* to take advantage. Plus, I wanted to try out Arc Runner!"
