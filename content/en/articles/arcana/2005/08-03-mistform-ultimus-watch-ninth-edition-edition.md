Welcome again to Mistform Ultimus Watch! In this recurring sub-feature of Magic Arcana we keep track of that eponymous Wiitigo Drill-Sergeant, Mistform Ultimus, and see what creature types it has become. 

We haven't checked in with Mistform Ultimus since the release of *Fifth Dawn*, but we did [make a note](/en/articles/arcana/2004/09-06-mistform-ultimus-watch-the-new-legend-rule) of how Legend moved off its list of creature types since the updated template for legendary cards. Since then *Unhinged* and the entire Kamigawa block have released; Mistform Ultimus is now a Child, a Waiter, a Gamer, a Ninja, and *is no longer* a Nekrataal. 

It is a Giant Cat Carrier, a Dinosaur Egg Eater, an Undead Dragon Child, and a Brother, Sister and an Uncle-Istvan. Mistform Ultimus is at once a Warrior, a Rogue, a Cleric and a Wizard, and many more...
