We've got two wallpapers this week, and one of them has two things on it. That's like *three* wallpapers!

First up, to remind you that *Duel Decks: Divine vs. Demonic* hits stores today, we've got a wallpaper that combines Chippy's new [Akroma, Angel of Wrath](https://web.archive.org/web/20090410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroma%2C+Angel+of+Wrath) and [Lord of the Pit](https://web.archive.org/web/20090410000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lord+of+the+Pit):
