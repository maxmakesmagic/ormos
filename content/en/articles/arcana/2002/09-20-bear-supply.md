After Bearscape was made [green instead of black](/en/articles/arcana/2002/01-22-bearscape-of-the-dead-2) and created Bears instead of Zombies, R&D had to come up with a name for it. Below is R&D's list of names that, once you read it, you'll be glad weren't used.

![Bearscape names](https://i.ormos.uk/81/0f/810f6bb053ad51ef4b1729aedabf2770b708eb32049376b0510215207fa204e6.jpg)

It is interesting to note that Grizzly Fate was used in *Judgment* and Animal Magnetism in *Onslaught*. Don't expect a card called "Bear Supply" anytime soon, however.
