## GRAND PRIX PRAGUE 2016

TOP 8 Coverage

It was great weekend of Magic here in the Czech Republic's capital of Prague. 1,477 players entered the tournament, 450 qualified for the second day, and finally eight worthy players gathered in the feature match area for the playoffs.

The biggest names in the Top 8 were (15) Alexander Hayne, (19) Lukas Blohon, and renowned Legacy expert Thomas Enevoldsen, but in the end it was Spain's Rodrigo Togores who defeated all comers, personally dispatching Blohon in the quarters and Enevoldsen in the semifinals.

Togores operated his Storm deck masterfully throughout the tournament, but especially so in the finals against Miracles player Nicolas Tholance, which culminated in what was likely the finest game of the Grand Prix. There, Togores had to overcome two copies of Force of Will, Surgical Extraction, and a total of three Flusterstorms. And he did, leaving his opponent and many spectators flustered indeed.

So congratulations to Rodrigo Togores, Storm master and champion of Grand Prix Prague 2016!

DAY 2 COVERAGE

Welcome back to Day 2 of Grand Prix Prague 2016! 1,477 players had been in the running yesterday morning, only 450 of them qualified to continue playing today.

The format of the weekend, both here and at Grand Prix Columbus, was Legacy and, as always, Legacy was a blast to play and watch and talk about. The big three decks in the current metagame appeared to be Eldrazi, Miracles, and Shardless Sultai, with various versions of Delver decks not far behind. At the same time, Legacy never disappoints with regards to the variety of viable decks, and many players could be found running their trademark or pet decks. Most notably, Death and Taxes master Thomas Enevoldsen went 9-0 yesterday.

Today, six more rounds of Legacy would be played, followed by the Top 8. Stay tuned for the Top 100 metagame breakdown, for decklists, and for all the latest straight from the battlefield of Grand Prix Prague 2016!

DAY 1 COVERAGE

Day 1 of Grand Prix Prague had begun with 1,477 players, but after nine grueling rounds of Legacy the field was whittled down to just 450.

450 would come back tomorrow for another six rounds of Legacy, for a shot at fame and fortune. Overnight, but four players sported a pristine record of 9-0: Niels Molle, Rodrigo Togores, Gianluca Gazzola, and Thomas Enevoldsen. They would have a head start tomorrow, but fierce competition wasn't far behind, including Grzegorz Kowalski, (19) Lukas Blohon, (16) Ondřej Stráský, Martin Jůza, Marc Tobiasch, and (15) Alexander Hayne, all at 8-1.

So far, the biggest decks appeared to be Miracles Control and Eldrazi Beatdown, with a healthy dose of Shardless Sultai providing a more midrange approach to the format. Check back tomorrow for a close look at the metagame, for lots of decklists, for live feature match coverage, and for the epic conclusion of Grand Prix Prague 2016!
