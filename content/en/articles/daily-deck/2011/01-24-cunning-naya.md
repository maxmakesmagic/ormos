K.I.S.S. is the acronym for "Keep It Simple, Stupid," and Misha Gurevich kept his Naya deck for Extended stripped down to the basics: mana, the best creatures red, green, and white have to offer, and a smattering of removal. Making his list standout from his fellow PTQers is the fact he has put four Cunning Sparkmages into the main deck. The hasty 0/1 allows him to keep his opponent's mana Elves, Faeries, and other small creatures *off* the battlefield.
