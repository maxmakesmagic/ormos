Learn more about Grand Prix Las Vegas Limited's 2017 Top 8 competitors—including who they are, what they’ve done in the game, and more—by taking a look through their profiles!

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gplv17/t8_lim_herr.jpg)Name:** Paul Herr

**Age:** 32

**Hometown:** Irvine, California

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** None

**What was your record on Day 1, and what did you play?**

9-0, Black-Green

**What were your records on Day 2, and what did you play?**

3-0 Red-White splash Black

1-2 White-Black Zombies

**Which cards performed well for you this weekend, and why?**

[Sandwurm Convergence](https://web.archive.org/web/20170617000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sandwurm+Convergence) – I never lost when I played it in the Sealed portion.

[Insult // Injury](https://web.archive.org/web/20170617000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Insult+%2F%2F+Injury) – It works great with trample.

[Unburden](https://web.archive.org/web/20170617000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unburden) – I cast it when my opponents have two cards left.

**What's been the best part of the Las Vegas weekend so far?**

Spending time with my friends John Balla and Greg Hatch

**What *Amonkhet* God best describes you?**

Oketra
