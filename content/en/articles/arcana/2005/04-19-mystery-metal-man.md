The answer is ![1 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/4c/6b4cf558eab279fb9414f146ebdd0e0705ec92984fec530597dd2a3bce43ce24.gif). 

Yup, just one colorless mana. All you have to do is have Acidic Sliver, Armor Sliver, Barbed Sliver, Blade Sliver, Brood Sliver, Clot Sliver, Crypt Sliver, Crystalline Sliver, Essence Sliver, Heart Sliver, Hibernation Sliver, Horned Sliver, Hunter Sliver, Magma Sliver, Mindwhip Sliver, Mistform Sliver, Mnemonic Sliver, Muscle Sliver, Plated Sliver, Quick Sliver, Root Sliver, Shifting Sliver, Spectral Sliver, Spined Sliver, Synapse Sliver, Talon Sliver, Toxin Sliver, Victual Sliver, Ward Sliver, and Winged Sliver already in play. It's Metallic Sliver, and it was actually printed in *Tempest* at just one colorless mana. Pretty good deal!
