Last month, we announced our [**Magic** Holiday Cookie Contest](/en/articles/arcana/2007/11-27-magic-holiday-cookie-contest), and now it's time for the results!

All entries were judged by a blue-ribbon committee of experts, with "**Magic** theme," "creativity," and "holiday cheer" each being worth 33% of the total score. First, let's look at the ten runners-up, each of whom won two randomly-chosen *Lorwyn* theme decks.

### Runner-up: Kim Janik ([Goblin Snowman](https://web.archive.org/web/20080114000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Snowman))

![Goblin Snowman](https://i.ormos.uk/a6/ae/a6ae9291bf64f23c124d5033c464e7af0078510819058cc53c44f0fbf8abf2fa.jpg)

Kim says: "I have to admit that I had a lot of fun creating this life-size version of the Goblin Snowman. The entire snowman cookie is edible and we donated the finished product to my children's school for the teachers to enjoy on their break."

### Runner-up: Angel Ducak

![Festive!](https://i.ormos.uk/f5/0f/f50f245eadb3a1abf3880e7c92cd89aeb35417817becd9030fc985229421469b.jpg)

### Runner-up: Sara Yarrington

The judges decided that there was a lot of holiday cheer in getting together with your friends to eat cookies at a **Magic** shop.

### Runner-up: Zane Barker

![Rawr!](https://i.ormos.uk/76/f1/76f1be5118a5778569ba6fb17cedc22aac6adb24f0bf2c21c447aa7af64a8b7d.jpg)

Zane's description of the cookie says: "A dragon celebrating the holidays and enjoying a treasure chest full of gold. Covered in frosting, sprinkle diamonds in the treasure, and mini-chocolate kisses for back spines."

### Runner-up: Sam Condon (Planeswalker Santa)

![Ho ho ho!](https://i.ormos.uk/37/c3/37c36ba565cd8c06b5b96b3d418013944ee36af8fdc853f25edce0ba972987e7.jpg)

Sam's version of Santa as a Planeswalker is all about giving gifts: 

Santa Claus  
 3RW  
 Planeswalker--Santa  
 +1: Each player puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature they don't control.  
 -3: Each player gives an opponent control of a permanent they own.  
 -7: Each player wins the game. 

As Sam says, "I may have made him just slightly overpowered, but he IS Santa Claus, after all."

### Runner-up: Matthew Greenblatt

![A heartwarming holiday story!](https://i.ormos.uk/d6/8e/d68e179605c8fd83c6df36690c210e4c348186b1c111a62541b2bd74ae7ccef6.jpg)

Sam Condon told a story with his cookes, thus combining two great things: stories and cookies. Oh, and holidays. And **Magic**. Also probably some butter, eggs, and sugar.

### Runner-up: Jesse D. (A Very Treefolk Christmas)

![A heartwarming holiday story!](https://i.ormos.uk/8c/9b/8c9bcf4c9b6a0cfe81dce9d42fc32911570adf05687820d4f0d45ac9492c0088.jpg)

Jesse writes: "My friends and I have been debating whether or not the card [Sheltering Ancient](https://web.archive.org/web/20080114000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sheltering+Ancient) is useful...well, I finally found a use for it in my entry! The cookie is entirely gingerbread with sugar glaze; it includes the Treefolk and figures from the card, all dressed up for the holidays, as well as all five Moxes as decorations! "

### Runner-up: Timothy Nyberg (I Saw Zombies Kissing Santa Claus)

![Oh no! Santa!](https://i.ormos.uk/be/26/be26a904d1b8118370bbf2437457eba3fcb5cf71a343ac106a5312887fe7e847.gif)

Timothy gives us another heartwarming -- oh no! Santa! Look out for those zombies! Noooooo! He writes, "I thought it best to preserve the traditional ginger-bread-man form for the zombies, as it seemed the best integration of your requirements that the content be both M:TG and holiday themed."

### Second Place: Robert Owen & Lenny Talbot (Santa Claus Planeswalker)

![Santa the Planeswalker](https://i.ormos.uk/84/05/840535ffca1a87d8d61e5104e0639cbb18c8ee4467ec7a2b5dd7494b5caabdcb.jpg)

The complete text of Robert and Lenny's rendition of Santa is: 

Santa Claus  
 1WRG  
 Planeswalker - Santa  
 +1 Put two 1/1 reindeer tokens with flying and bands with other reindeer into play  
 -1 Put a coal counter on target player.  
 -6 If there are 8 or more reindeer in play all players without coal counters search there libraries for a card and may play that card without paying its mana cost.

Of course, the true spirit of the holiday is declaring a winner. And that winner is...

### Champion: Paul Arsenault

![Wow!](https://i.ormos.uk/94/44/9444210abf014ff0934fb043f34adf9db2267059042047d2312adb62a78bd91b.jpg)

Paul was far and away the winner (and takes home a *foil* set of *Lorwyn* for his troubles!). He says: "The concept centered around all the MTG creatures putting away their differences during Christmas, enjoying the holiday, and all pitching in to decorate a tree. The goblins, of course, couldn't help starting a snowball fight with the elves and a few beasts gave Rudolph and the gang a day off."
