Do you play or draw in Sealed, and how many lands is correct?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Lee-Shi-Tian.jpg)

***Lee Shi Tian:** Play. 17.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Tiago-Chan.jpg)

***Tiago Chan:** Play. 18.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Gao-Tan.jpg)

***Gao Tan:** Draw. 17.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Soh-Weng-Heng.jpg)

***Soh Weng Heng:** Draw. 17 + 1 Banner.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Park-Jun-Young.jpg)

***Park Jun Young:** Play. 17 + 1 Banner.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Tomoharu-Saito.jpg)

***Tomoharu Saito:** It's very close, but usually you should play first and play 17 lands.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Raymond-Tan.jpg)

***Raymond Tan:** Play. 17 lands if you're fast like Mardu, or 18 if you're slower like Sultai.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Nam-Sung-Wook.jpg)

***Nam Sung Wook:** Draw. 17.*
