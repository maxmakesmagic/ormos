The first game started as Standard games often do. Creatures traded with removal spells and the game remained at parity.

The board quickly started to fill up with powerful threats as the game progressed. Eventually, Sperling had a [Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc) token with two +1/+1 counters, a pair of [Rakshasa Deathdealer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Deathdealer)s, and [Anafenza, the Foremost](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anafenza%2C+the+Foremost) to Pardee's pair of [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon)s, [Goblin Rabblemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Rabblemaster), and three Goblin tokens.

Both players were at precarious life totals and the combat math was complicated. Sperling made a pair of tight attacks and managed to squeak through exactly enough damage to take down the first game.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpden15/sfsperling.JPG)  
*Matt Sperling*

**Sperling 1 - 0 Pardee** 

The second game was an absolute blowout. Pardee's turn three [Goblin Rabblemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Rabblemaster) went unanswered and every defensive body that Sperling could muster met a removal spell. Rabblemaster was able to take over the game and chop away at Sperling's life total in a few short turns.

**Sperling 1 - 1 Pardee**

The third game was more interactive. Sperling's [Rakshasa Deathdealer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Deathdealer) met [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike) and [Hushwing Gryff](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hushwing+Gryff) came down in response to [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) to prevent the trigger.

Pardee had an answer for [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) in [Glare of Heresy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glare+of+Heresy) and Sperling had [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight) to deal with Pardee's flyer.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpden15/sfpardee.JPG)  
*Sam Pardee*

Both players tried to establish boards, but the other seemed to always have a removal spell.

Eventually, another [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) came down for Sperling and Pardee's [Chained to the Rocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chained+to+the+Rocks) met [Back to Nature](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Back+to+Nature), which gave Sperling another [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) trigger. Pardee drew more lands off the top while taking rhino stomps to the face. Unable to find an answer, Pardee soon found himself underfoot.

**Sperling 2 - 1 Pardee**

**Matt Sperling defeats Sam Pardee in three games to secure himself a spot in the finals!**
