There's no secret source of our ideas. We do what I imagine most creative professionals do: we read books, magazines, and comics, we watch movies and TV of all kinds, we play games. There's also a lot of staring into space and smacking our heads on the keyboard waiting for inspiration to come.</p>&#10;<p> &quot;It deserves mentioning, though, that the creative aspects of <b>Magic</b> are the result of a highly collaborative process. Card designers and developers, the art director, the concept illustrators, the card illustrators, the card name and flavor text writers ... all these folks shape the world as they flesh it out, and none of us know exactly what it's going to look like until very late in the process.&quot;</p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>
