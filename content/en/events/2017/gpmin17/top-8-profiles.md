The Top 8 is more than just a pile of cards. It's the players that piloted those decks and weathered the ebbs and flows of a two-day tournament. Here are the players who performed well enough this weekend to make the Top 8 of Grand Prix Minneapolis.

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpmin17/gpMinn17_CoreyBaumeister.jpg)Name:** Corey Baumeister 

**Age:** 26 

**Hometown:** Fargo, North Dakota 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Better Brother 

**What did you play this weekend, and why?** Black-Green Constrictor; Brad told me to. 

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Winding Constrictor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Winding+Constrictor), it's gas. 

**What was the best card in your sideboard, and why?** [Skysovereign, Consul Flagship](https://web.archive.org/web/20170806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skysovereign%2C+Consul+Flagship) (The boat). T-Pain agrees. 

**What was your plan to beat Ramunap Red this weekend?** Cast 2/3s when they play 2/1s. 

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** First Top 8 with my big bro!!! I couldn't be happier. 

* * *

  
 **![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpmin17/gpMinn17_BradNelson.jpg)Name:** Brad Nelson 

**Age:** 31 

**Hometown:** Roanoke, Virginia 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Better Brother. That's not true. 

**What did you play this weekend, and why?** Black-Green Constrictor, because Corey told me to. 

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** Whatever came off the top. 

**What was the best card in your sideboard, and why?** I'm on a boat! 

**What was your plan to beat Ramunap Red this weekend?** Have early draws to dodge it. 

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** My brother and I have dreamed of being in the same Top 8 since we started playing *Magic*. This means everything! 

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpmin17/gpMinn17_RayHuang.jpg)Name:** Ray Huang 

**Age:** 26 

**Hometown:** Madison, Wisconsin 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Three Pro Tour appearances 

**What did you play this weekend, and why?** Mardu Vehicles 

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Thraben Inspector](https://web.archive.org/web/20170806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thraben+Inspector). It does everything. 

**What was the best card in your sideboard, and why?** [Skysovereign, Consul Flagship](https://web.archive.org/web/20170806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skysovereign%2C+Consul+Flagship). Black decks can't kill it. 

**What was your plan to beat Ramunap Red this weekend?** Race with [Aethersphere Harvester](https://web.archive.org/web/20170806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aethersphere+Harvester), have good blockers. 

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** Back on the Pro Tour! 

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpmin17/gpMinn17_DanielWeiser.jpg)Name:** Daniel Weiser 

**Age:** 22 

**Hometown:** Minneapolis, Minnesota 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** One Grand Prix Top 16, one Top 32. 

**What did you play this weekend, and why?** Black-Green, because it's nice. 

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Blooming Marsh](https://web.archive.org/web/20170806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blooming+Marsh), because [Bayou](https://web.archive.org/web/20170806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bayou) is strong. 

**What was the best card in your sideboard, and why?** [Ishkanah, Grafwidow](https://web.archive.org/web/20170806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ishkanah%2C+Grafwidow). It's tutorable and has a variety of applications. 

**What was your plan to beat Ramunap Red this weekend?** Win. The matchup is good. 

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** The payoff of a lot of hard work, and a trip to the Pro Tour. 

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpmin17/gpMinn17_RobCastro.jpg)Name:** Rob Castro 

**Age:** 41 

**Hometown:** San Salvador, El Savador. I live in Brookfield, Wisconsin. 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 1997 Wisconsin Standard State Champion. 

**What did you play this weekend, and why?** Mardu Vehicles. I asked Matt Severa how he liked the deck at the Pro Tour. He loved it and sent me the latest version. I was the only deck I had played. 

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** There is not one card, the entire deck was great. 

**What was the best card in your sideboard, and why?** [Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20170806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar), because I didn't run four main. 

**What was your plan to beat Ramunap Red this weekend?** Play [Aethersphere Harvester](https://web.archive.org/web/20170806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aethersphere+Harvester). 

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** It means that Dan Bock was not there to stop me from making the Pro Tour. I believe I'm in the first Savadoran to make Top 8 of a Grand Prix.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpmin17/gpMinn17_SteveLocke.jpg)Name:** Steve Locke 

**Age:** 30 

**Hometown:** Ames, Iowa 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Top 32 at Pro Tour Kuala Lumpur 

**What did you play this weekend, and why?** Mono-Black Zombies; it seemed well-positioned. 

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** Liliana's Mastery, because it lets you turn the corner and go over the top. 

**What was the best card in your sideboard, and why?** [Murder](https://web.archive.org/web/20170806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Murder). It helps you beat Vehicles, which can be problematic. 

**What was your plan to beat Ramunap Red this weekend?** Make sure I could beat their midrange plan post-board. 

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** Fun to break through and qualify for a Pro Tour.
