*What's your favorite Limited combo of all time?* 

![Mike Turian](https://i.ormos.uk/2b/09/2b094d288b20f58968d51834eb02b2ed1381ae876700918a5c14f07004174e55.jpg "Mike Turian")

**Mike Turian:** My favorite Limited combo was during *Odyssey* / *Torment* Draft. I had always loved opening up a pack to see Wild Mongrel. It was such a powerful two-drop (my favorite!) in Limited.

 ![Wild Mongrel](https://i.ormos.uk/a4/66/a4662692f3afa80eeff7f490ee71b304fa3ed34e3ba37073f1d18a90fdcef4f8.jpg) 

  

When *Torment* was released, Wild Mongrel became even stronger with the madness mechanic. After casting a turn-two Mongrel, I loved madnessing out an Arrogant Wurm for only ![2 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/f9/cc/f9cc8a15b009997919d58ad79964384351495d1b6c9231f938316577453038a8.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif). One time I followed up a turn-two Wild Mongrel with a turn-three Arrogant Wurm, followed by a turn-four Violent Eruption. That is the type of combo I love!
