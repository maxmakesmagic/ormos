107 teams of three showed up in Seattle this weekend to play Magic at the Pro Tour. At the end of 14 rounds, Von Dutch, our champions, Younger, bigger, Small, Camille Cornelison and Sharon Remy demolished the competition. They were in first place at the end of Saturday. They were the favorites going into Sunday and they did not disappoint. In the first semifinal, Von Dutch played a nail biter against Pocket Rockets. the Rockets had never played on the pro tour before, but that didn't stop them from forcing it to come down to match three-game three. In the end, Camille Cornelison kept smashing his Tangle Golem into Matthew Woods Creatures and then he just pick it up again with Crystal Shard. Wood couldn't break out of the lock and Cornelius and sent his team through to the finals. The other semifinal was an all Japanese affair with SSI taking on shopfireball.com SA. I opened up all the bombs with Ichiro Shimura getting Pristine Angel, Luminous Angel and Megatog. Masami Yamamoto had locks it on Warhammer and Ryuji Arita had Vedalken shackles. However, all weekend long Jean Okimoto and Segobia Kada had been saying just trust Ataru and Itaru Ishida did not let them down just like he had all weekend. On the other side, most spectators thought the Dutch were a big favorite in the finals, but not the Dutch players themselves. They have a lot of respect for the Japanese Magic players, and this team in particular, Shop Fireball had them quite concerned. Shop Fireball showed once again in the finals why they've earned that respect. Itaru Ishida made several brilliant picks during the draft, but in the end, Camille Cornelison wound up with not just Glissa, not just grab the reins, but also in Oblivion stone and all that power was just too much. It all came down to the final game, but Camille Cornelison clinched it, his teammates embraced him, and the $60,000 first prize is headed to the Netherlands.
