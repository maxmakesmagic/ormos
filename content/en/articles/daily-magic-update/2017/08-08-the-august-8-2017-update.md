The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on August 8, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by Dragons Day (not to be confused with [Day of the Dragons](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Day+of+the+Dragons))! # What People Are Talking About

1.  > I don't like the idea of people telling me how to play a casual format. Don't like the Ring? House ban. Done. Argument over.
    >
    > — Wedge (@TheManaSource) [August 8, 2017](https://twitter.com/TheManaSource/status/894920094412918784)

There was a weird conversation that arose this morning with the startling revelation (read: rather obvious reveal) that [Sol Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sol+Ring) was being reprinted in *Commander (2017 Edition)*. Mostly a couple pros chimed in on [Sol Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sol+Ring) being the biggest turnoff from the format. But Wedge has got it. Most Commander players I've met (plus myself) enjoy the card, but if your playgroup doesn't, that's okay too!

# Deck of the Day

1.  Scion of the Ur-Dragon

Today is Dragon Day, so why not a Dragon Deck? Created by a Tappedout user [going by the name Kevinpetsca](http://tappedout.net/users/Kevinpetsca/), this [Scion of the Ur-Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scion+of+the+Ur-Dragon) deck looks to use its commander as a toolbox to Dragon all over people. It's also capable of some pretty quick kills, often using [Moltensteel Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moltensteel+Dragon) to pump it sky-high, and then [Skithiryx, the Blight Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skithiryx%2C+the+Blight+Dragon), or it can play controlling with any number of dangerous dragons. If Dragons get you going, this is a good place to start getting ideas for upgrading your Dragon *Commander 2017* deck.
