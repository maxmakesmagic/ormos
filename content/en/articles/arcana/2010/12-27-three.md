*We're taking a two-week holiday break and will be back with new stuff on Monday, January 3, 2011. Until then, please enjoy our favorite Arcanas of 2010. This article originally ran July 14, 2010.* 

Let's say you suddenly gain the ability to turn invisible. Naturally, the first thing you do is sneak into R&D so you can watch them playtest upcoming sets, right? It's every **Magic** player's dream to get behind the scenes and learn what's coming up!

Well, maybe. Because it turns out that R&D doesn't always use stickers for its playtests. When making constructed decks, some people like to take ... shortcuts. For example, check out these three ***Magic** 2011* playtest cards.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/493_cards.jpg) 

  

The first question this raises, of course, is "Where in the color pie is '3'?" Also, "Why does '3' cost both ![3 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/7f/5d/7f5d2f1e55eaa9d19a9a23027b63bfbb5b39dbf8642160dda1a477a03df23a6a.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) and ![1 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/4c/6b4cf558eab279fb9414f146ebdd0e0705ec92984fec530597dd2a3bce43ce24.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)?"

Okay, the question it actually raises is "What are each of those cards?" Because it turns out that the "3" in question means something else *each time*.

 ![Jace's Ingenuity](https://i.ormos.uk/56/6e/566e974113aa0614cba57c0d610a10c8a605f1f6ac3e889c8e04756f7615a944.jpg) 

  

Well, that makes sense to abbreviate as "3". Sure.

 ![Lightning Bolt](https://i.ormos.uk/8c/a8/8ca8d678d54ad0fa984afd93ad1ce4439d6a097f36050d57d38892e35add180d.jpg) 

  

Also logical. What *else* would a 3 that cost ![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif) mean?

 ![Mana Leak](https://i.ormos.uk/8d/0d/8d0d44ff91cf8131534b9205a8d74b9d47d4a3a1bfb76296f1968fb94a35a26b.jpg) 

  

And that would be our ![1 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/4c/6b4cf558eab279fb9414f146ebdd0e0705ec92984fec530597dd2a3bce43ce24.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif). Interestingly, if that card had had a 1/1 added to it, it could have stood in for Augury Owl.

So let this be a warning to you! Even if you *do* become invisible and sneak into R&D to watch playtests, you might still not know what's going on.
