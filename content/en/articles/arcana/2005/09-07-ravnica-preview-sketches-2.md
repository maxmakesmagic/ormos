You've only seen four *Ravnica: City of Guilds* preview cards this week, and already we're clamoring to show you their cool full-size art and the sketches that went into their creation.

On Monday you saw **[Agrus Kos, Wojek Veteran](https://web.archive.org/web/20050907000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/283)**, a heroic investigator from the red-white Boros Legion. Here's the art behind it, by Donato Giancola.

![Agrus Kos, Wojek Veteran sketch by Donato Giancola](https://i.ormos.uk/5c/dd/5cdd303965665a2b82dce7fd335f1ee1b8911311637e97f950d279ccb18f424d.jpg)  
*Agrus Kos, Wojek Veteran sketch by Donato Giancola*

![Agrus Kos, Wojek Veteran final art by Donato Giancola](https://i.ormos.uk/f7/3e/f73ee5993800a9bad4f56d1543814e179e6c1a3ee77644ec5d007d574a723d3a.jpg)  
*Agrus Kos, Wojek Veteran final art by Donato Giancola*

Also on Monday you saw **[Selesnya Guildmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20050907000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/mr192)**, a preview of the new "hybrid" mana costs from the green-white Selesnya Conclave guild. Here's the art behind it, by Mark Zug.

![Selesnya Guildmage sketch by Mark Zug](https://i.ormos.uk/f3/45/f345a74178cc2b94c2522e793354ab8d841017978e78bd6f6770fadbc8c14262.jpg)  
*Selesnya Guildmage sketch by Mark Zug*

![Selesnya Guildmage final art by Mark Zug](https://i.ormos.uk/79/8d/798df04415c16ace00ab8a2a290d069e6cdb648b6dd221eeffb5d69a9fac2a8e.jpg)  
*Selesnya Guildmage final art by Mark Zug*

On Tuesday you saw **[Carven Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20050907000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/aa192)**, a new, tougher [Wall of Blossoms](https://web.archive.org/web/20050907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Blossoms). Here's its art by Jim Nelson.

![Carven Caryatid sketch by Jim Nelson](https://i.ormos.uk/51/2e/512ecea2dec873e6c122e21e8299434f82e6d15661e118ab7a57bfdccce67d94.jpg)  
*Carven Caryatid sketch by Jim Nelson*

![Carven Caryatid final art by Jim Nelson](https://i.ormos.uk/32/dd/32ddbd20d861dda890f7704d4b040210f0696953e53f4b76aab2f261f47787dd.jpg)  
*Carven Caryatid final art by Jim Nelson*

Last but not least, today's preview is of **[Sunhome, Fortress of the Legion](https://web.archive.org/web/20050907000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/zm9)**, a mighty nonbasic land that blesses your Boros or any other troops with double strike. Here's its art by Martina Pilcerova.

![Sunhome, Fortress of the Legion sketch by Martina Pilcerova](https://i.ormos.uk/bf/74/bf745cdae620c49aaaa1a7e7bd43b9066aa690408e3372a984f0408b86a0c0b9.jpg)  
*Sunhome, Fortress of the Legion sketch by Martina Pilcerova*

![Sunhome, Fortress of the Legion final art by Martina Pilcerova](https://i.ormos.uk/94/61/94613bacf049fa6282a2cd3c871fad3e6bfdf38e839055a2910bf202ab58acd5.jpg)  
*Sunhome, Fortress of the Legion final art by Martina Pilcerova*

Expect more *Ravnica* card and art previews over the next two and a half weeks. Of course, you'll love the art of the special *Ravnica: City of Guilds* **[prerelease card](https://web.archive.org/web/20050907000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/prereleases)** -- but you'll have to be a bit patient for that.
