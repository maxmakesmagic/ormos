Ask Wizards is a weekly feature that allows you to ask us questions! If you'd like to submit your question please email it to AskWizards@wizards.com. We aren't able to answer every question we receive but if your question is good then it might show up in the coming weeks!

**Q: The [mechanics article](/en/articles/feature/2014/04-07-mechanics-of-journey-into-nyx) doesn't mention inspired or tribute. Are no inspired or tribute cards in JOU? [—su92, on Tumblr](http://markrosewater.tumblr.com/post/82017982919/the-mechanics-article-doesnt-mention-inspired-or)** 

**A: Mark Rosewater, head designer for Magic:**

There is inspired but not tribute.

 **Q: The [mechanics article](/en/articles/feature/2014/04-07-mechanics-of-journey-into-nyx) for today implies that we'll only be seeing devotion on the Gods for this set. Is this true? [—our-discontent, on Tumblr](http://markrosewater.tumblr.com/post/82017932177/the-mechanics-article-for-today-implies-that-well-only)** 

**A: Mark Rosewater, head designer for Magic:**

It is. The denizens of Theros have lost their devotion to the gods.

 **Q: Thank you! \*finally\* a one-drop Minotaur for the Minotaur deck. [—mdbryan84, on Tumblr](http://markrosewater.tumblr.com/post/81967529919/thank-you-finally-a-one-drop-minotaur-for-the)** 

**A: Mark Rosewater, head designer for Magic:**

The third set is about making those cards that players have been asking for.

 **Q: I can finally build that Starfish deck! Thank you so much! [—potatosteve, on Tumblr](http://markrosewater.tumblr.com/post/81974365564/i-can-finally-build-that-starfish-deck-thank-you-so)**

A lot of Starfish love tonight. : )
