*When a new set comes out, R&D will often bring decks to events to show off the potential of the new cards. These aren't our best FFL decks per se, but instead a glimpse into what the set has to offer. Often these decks are intentionally un-streamlined, both to keep opponents guessing and to make the deck more fun to play for 10 straight hours!*

*Mike Turian was gunslinging at the Los Angeles, CA Prerelease:*

![Vampire Nocturnus](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Vampire+Nocturnus)

There are many ways to make a Vampire deck, but this is the simplest approach: mono-black with four copies of ***Magic** 2010*'s [Vampire Nocturnus](https://web.archive.org/web/20091006000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vampire+Nocturnus). This deck is fast and can be unforgiving. The biggest surprise was how good the one [Mind Sludge](https://web.archive.org/web/20091006000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind+Sludge) was; it was constantly catching people by surprise and taking them completely out of the game.
