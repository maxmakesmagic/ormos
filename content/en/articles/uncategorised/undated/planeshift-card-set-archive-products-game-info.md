### FAT PACK

Includes a Top Deck magazine player’s guide pullout, six 15-card booster packs, two premium cards, and the *Planeshift* novel.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d6/7c/d67cd1794f06259dcb567a130d44cb5ddee8c0216ebb7d604a518e391119ec04.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d6/7c/d67cd1794f06259dcb567a130d44cb5ddee8c0216ebb7d604a518e391119ec04.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/95/a7/95a7760fdd1be2f7411294aa773c791ff6db3d9125e2b067415d617d33d231ab.png)

This 143-card, black-bordered set features the mechanics of the *Invasion* block and cycles of Familiars, Battlemages, and “Dragon Lairs.”

**Set Name:** *Planeshift*  
**Block:** Set 2 of 3 of the *Invasion* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 143

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044242/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/expert/planeshift/planeshift_faq.txt) 

**Game Design:** Mike Elliott (lead), Bill Rose, Mark Rosewater, and Henry Stern

**Game Development:** William Jockusch (lead), Randy Buehler, Henry Stern, Brian Tinsman, and Teeuwynn Woodruff, with contributions from Brady Dommermuth and Beth Moursund
