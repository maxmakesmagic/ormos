## PRO TOURS

Pro Tour *Battle for Zendikar* takes place October 16-18, 2015. The formats for this Pro Tour are Standard and Limited.

## HOW CAN I PLAY ON THE PRO TOUR?

To play in a Pro Tour, you must earn an invitation. You can earn a Pro Tour invitation by:

*   Being a top finisher at a [Regional Pro Tour Qualifier](https://web.archive.org/web/20210502063302/http://magic.wizards.com/en/protour/regionalqualifiers)
*   Winning a [Magic Online Championship Series](https://web.archive.org/web/20210502063302/http://magic.wizards.com/en/2015-magic-online-championship-series) event
*   Being a top finisher of a [Grand Prix](https://web.archive.org/web/20210502063302/https://media.magic.wizards.com/content/eventsschedulegrand-prix)
*   Having the appropriate [Pro Players Club](https://web.archive.org/web/20210502063302/https://media.magic.wizards.com/node/152291) status
*   Finishing with 33 match points or greater at the previously held Pro Tour
*   Being a member of the [Pro Tour Hall of Fame](https://web.archive.org/web/20210502063302/https://media.magic.wizards.com/node/142796)

## PRIZES

Pro Tours award cash prizes to the Top 75 finishers at each event. Here is a breakdown of cash prizes based on a player's finish:

| Place | Prize Money |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | $40,000 |
| 2 | $20,000 |
| 3-4 | $12,500 |
| 5-8 | $10,000 |
| 9-16 | $5,000 |
| 17-25 | $2,500 |
| 26-50 | $1,500 |
| 51-75 | $1,000 |
| 76+ | $0 |

Players are awarded Pro Points, which earn players benefits if they reach certain levels on the [Pro Players Club](https://web.archive.org/web/20210502063302/https://media.magic.wizards.com/node/152311). Benefits for reaching Silver, Gold, and Platinum levels in the Pro Players Club include invitations to future Pro Tours and other major events, appearance fees, and awards to attend upcoming Pro Tours.
