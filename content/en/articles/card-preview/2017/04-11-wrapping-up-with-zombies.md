One of the oldest archetypes for *Magic* decks is, still today, one of the most popular: tribal.

Not the tribal supertype—[we don't talk about that anymore](/en/articles/makingmagic/2015/10-19-concept-vs-execution)—but honest to earth tribes of creatures. Elf. Goblin. Merfolk. Soldier. Human. Vampire. Ally. Sliver. Treefolk.

Zombie. Oh yes, definitely Zombie.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&multiverseid=93)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=93)

Zombies have been a feature in many sets, getting plenty of tribal action in *Onslaught* block, both trips to the Innistrad plane, as well as benefiting from heavy use as a creature type over *Magic*'s decades.

*Amonkhet* is home to even more Zombie action. While [Mark Rosewater's introduction last week](/en/articles/makingmagic/2017/04-03-amonkhet-down-to-business-part-1) left it clear that Zombies were going to matter, I found [Matt Tabak's words](/en/articles/feature/2017/04-03-amonkhet-mechanics) about the embalm mechanic to be less cryptic: "You'll find lots of ways to take advantage of its new zombiehood, I'm sure."

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_zU0ywdXVb1.png)

Zombie tribal leadership has come a long way since 1993. Thanks, Nicol Bolas.

# When the Shambling Gets Tough

Zombies are fun in *Magic* right now, even if they're not leading the Standard pack. Both *Shadows over Innistrad* and *Eldritch Moon* dropped a boatload of shambling action into the format. Lord of the Accursed tells us quite a few things about our visit to *Amonkhet*:

*   As an uncommon, Zombies have tribal synergy available in Limited. Zombies are everywhere, doing Zombie things. Oh, and they're all bigger too.
*   A 2/3 for three mana, at an easy-to-cast single black mana requirement, lets this become playable in plenty of decks. It's an easy add for Zombie fun, Limited or beyond.
*   If the previous two points weren't enough, Lord of the Accursed gives your Zombies some evasion too. Menace may not be the end-all of Constructed—[Graf Harvest](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Graf+Harvest) has had plenty of time for a breakout—but it'll be the death of you on Amonkhet.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_Cus62c1PIT.png)

Oh. Whoops. I guess menace might be the end of you in Standard too. A cheap, evasive creature with a minimal drawback—killing a Zombie you're probably just going to get right back—is rounded out by dinging your opponents whenever one of your Zombies dies.

If you're playing a lot of Zombies, boy will a lot of Zombies die.

Plague Belcher is another step into the madness of Zombie tribal. No, *Shadows over Innistrad* and *Eldritch Moon* didn't have actual madness Zombies. (That was another popular undead tribe.) But it did have things like [Relentless Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Relentless+Dead), [Haunted Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Haunted+Dead), and [Prized Amalgam](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prized+Amalgam) with a tendency to get undead repeatedly.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Relentless+Dead)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Relentless+Dead)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Haunted+Dead)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Haunted+Dead)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Prized+Amalgam)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prized+Amalgam)

Even better, everyone's favorite one-drop Zombie, [Cryptbreaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptbreaker), is around to load the battlefield with Zombies and stock your graveyard with goodies—like all those sweet new creatures with embalm.

A month ago, Holy\_85 took Zombies all the way through to perfection in a Standard League. The deck doesn't dip into blue beyond the freebie [Prized Amalgam](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prized+Amalgam)–from-the-graveyard action, which gives us a great base to start thinking about tribal in *Amonkhet* Standard.

There is one thought that comes up often when tribal strategies are looked at: "But what if some or *all* our Zombies get killed? How do we keep fighting?" Did you think a world controlled by Nicol Bolas wouldn't have the appropriate answer?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_pskzGDdBWi.png)

Dread Wanderer is exactly the kind of card that makes all the Zombies we know and love in Standard happy:

*   It's a one-drop, arriving early and attacking fast.
*   It's recursive, getting to come back from the graveyard as often as needed (bringing [Prized Amalgam](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prized+Amalgam) along for the ride).
*   Its ability pairs well with the discard-for-value Zombies like [Cryptbreaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptbreaker) and [Haunted Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Haunted+Dead).

When you start to piece together all three of these new *Amonkhet* cards, you can paint a beautiful picture of tribal undeath:

*   Dread Wanderer and [Cryptbreaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptbreaker) fill the battlefield early, backed up by [Haunted Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Haunted+Dead) and [Prized Amalgam](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prized+Amalgam)s.
*   Lord of the Accursed turns 2/2s into 3/3s, and can even make them menace through an opponent's meager defense.
*   Plague Belcher arrives at some point, setting up a powerful and cheap attacker while punishing the opponent for killing any Zombies.

A full battlefield of Zombies and a Plague Belcher (or two!) makes an opponent's [Fumigate](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fumigate) give less life, making your Zombies' quick return from the graveyard even more dangerous.

Zombie tribal is gearing up for its return, so be sure to check out [the rest of the previews](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2017/04-14-amonkhet) and head to a store near you when [*Amonkhet* Prereleases](https://web.archive.org/web/20170411000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/event-types/prerelease) begin April 22!
