Trick asked me to write today's Arcana and I knew exactly what I wanted to do. [I've established before](/en/articles/arcana/2014/02-17-heading-to-the-temple-2) that I'm a big fan of the [Temples](https://web.archive.org/web/20140417000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&format=+[%22Theros+Block%22]&type=+[%22Land%22]&name=+[Temple]) of *Theros* block, and *Journey into Nyx* completes our cycle of ten. Huzzah! 

Since they went into the [Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424063343/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/journeyintonyx/cig) without much fanfare, I will give these two the attention you probably figured I eventually would, anyway.
