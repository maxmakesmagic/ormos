Everything is bigger in Texas, so this will be a super-sized Texas Grand Prix, with bigger than usual fun! Located in the heart of Texas, Grand Prix Dallas/Fort Worth will be an amazing event starting on Friday and ending late Sunday. An event that has something for everyone, the Grand Prix will include casual and competitive events at all times. If you cannot make it one day, there is certainly something for you the next day.

In addition to great **Magic** events (the Standard main event, Commander, Two-Headed Giant, drafts, Win-A-Box for all formats), there will be vendors from all over the country to buy, sell, and trade with. If you're a fan of **Magic** artists, this is a show not to miss. We have a very rare U.S. appearance by John Avon—the master of all **Magic** lands! John has created some of the most memorable lands in all of **Magic**, as well as the Grand Prix promo Maelstrom Pulse. Fan favorite Steve Argyle, the artist behind such cards as Chandra Ablaze, Slave of Bolas, Everflowing Chalice, and Admonition Angel, will also be in attendance. 

So join us for this amazing event—the fun is huge, the prizes are bountiful and the giveaways are great. Gather your friends, make your plans and we will see you in Texas!!

*The **Magic: The Gathering** Grand Prix tournament series is a great reason to get out of the house, catch up with old friends, meet some new ones, and play **Magic**. Grand Prix tournaments are world-wide open events that allow all **Magic** players to participate and experience the thrill of a large-scale competition. In addition to $30,000 in cash prizes, Pro Tour invitations for the top 16 finishers, and exclusive foil promo cards for participants, Grand Prix feature a host of other activities, including public events and artist signings (prizes subject to change). Find out more about the Grand Prix [tournament schedule here](/en/events/2002/welcome/grand-prix#1).*

**Format**: Standard  
**Participation Fee**: $40  
 Grand Prix Dallas/Fort Worth will feed Pro Tour Philadelphia.

**Registration and Schedule** 

*   Friday, April 8: Registration times Noon - 8 p.m.
*   Saturday, April 9: Registration times 8 a.m. - 9:30 a.m.
*   Saturday, April 9: Tournament begins at 10 a.m.
*   Sunday, April 10: Day Two begins at 9 a.m.

The main tournament at Grand Prix Dallas/Fort Worth is open to **Magic** players of all ages. 

Players must have a signed publicity release form to be included in Wizards of the Coast's official online event coverage. Forms can be [downloaded here](http://www.wizards.com/dci/downloads/publicityrelease_grandprix_2011.pdf) (15k PDF) and will be available onsite. Minors must have a parent or guardian’s signature on this form. Publicity releases will be collected during registration. 

**Tournament Format**  
 

*   [Standard](https://web.archive.org/web/20130708023701/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Resources.aspx?x=judge/resources/sfrstandard)
*   Deck lists are required
*   Players must use the same deck on Day 2 that they used on Day 1

**Tournament Structure** 

*   Modified Swiss-style (50-minute rounds).
*   Rules Enforcement Level (REL) is Competitive (Day 1) and Professional (Day 2)
*   If 799 or fewer players register for the event, all players with an X-2-0 or better record OR the top 64 players—whichever number is greater—will advance to the second day of the event.
*   If 800 or more players register for the event, all players with an X-2-0 or better record OR the top 128 players—whichever number is greater—will advance to the second day of the event.
*   The Top 8 players after the final Swiss round on Day 2 will advance to the Top 8 single-elimination finals.
*   Top 8 playoff matches will be best 2 of 3, and have no time limit. (Players are still expected to play at a normal pace and complete playoff matches in a reasonable amount of time.)
*   Where necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will still be used to determine final order in the standings.

**Judges**  
 The head judge for this event is Sheldon Menery. The judge staff for this event has been finalized. Questions should be directed to judge@pastimes.net.

**Notes**  
 

*   40K, Rules Enforcement Level (REL) is Competitive (Day 1) and Professional (Day 2)
*   The **Magic: The Gathering** Tournament Rules and the **Magic: The Gathering** Infraction Procedure Guide are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document. Rules documents can be found in the [**Magic** Document Center](https://web.archive.org/web/20110307000000/http://www.wizards.com/wpn/Events/Rules.aspx?category=magic:thegathering).
*   Players must bring pen, paper, card sleeves and appropriate counters.
*   In the event that a player who wins an invitation to a Pro Tour stop at a Grand Prix has already qualified for an invitation, the invitation does not pass down under any circumstances. Previous qualification may be through a Qualifier tournament, through performance at a professional tournament, or by being an invited DCI player (valid upon the date of the official rank announcement for the appropriate professional tournament).

Byes will be given to players in the Grand Prix based on the following criteria *(Byes are not cumulative throughout the season or year. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Players that accumulate multiple byes to a particular Grand Prix tournament receive only the bye for the greatest number of rounds)*:

**Third-Round Byes**  
 

*   Players with a [Players Club](/en/events/2006/playersclub/pro-players-club-guidelines-and-procedures) level of 4 or higher
*   Players with a DCI Total rating of 2050 or higher. Byes are based on DCI Total ratings published on April 6, 2011.
*   Winners of Grand Prix Dallas/Fort Worth Trial tournaments.

**Second-Round Byes**  
 

*   Players with a [Players Club](/en/events/2006/playersclub/pro-players-club-guidelines-and-procedures) level of 2 or 3
*   Players with a DCI Total rating of 1950 or higher. Byes are based on DCI Total ratings published on April 6, 2011.

**First-Round Byes**  
 

*   Players with a [Players Club](/en/events/2006/playersclub/pro-players-club-guidelines-and-procedures) level of 1
*   Players with a DCI Total rating of 1850 or higher. Byes are based on DCI Total ratings published on April 6, 2011.

**Trial winners**  
 People who win a Trial event are awarded 3 byes in the Grand Prix that it feeds. Trial byes are not cumulative with other byes and do not pass down.
