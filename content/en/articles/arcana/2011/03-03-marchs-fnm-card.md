It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6)! Since we're now in March, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

![Wall of Omens](https://i.ormos.uk/68/ef/68efd4dbf321a1e4d97bf6c8eba819900bd9964dd4d0d949b478504f3185bc5c.jpg "Wall of Omens") 

  

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards feature the FNM logo in the text box and many have original flavor text and updated wordings.
