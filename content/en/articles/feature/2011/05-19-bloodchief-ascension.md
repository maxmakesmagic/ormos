Looking for a fun new combo deck in Standard? Why not try Dean Kruse's innovative list featuring [Bloodchief Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20110519000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodchief+Ascension)? Dean played the deck at the recent StarCityGames.com Standard Open in Orlando. He explains the deck and its many intricacies fully in a [deck tech](http://www.starcitygames.com/magic/standard/21863_Deck_Tech_Bloodchief_Ascension_with_Dean_Kruse.html) from the StarCityGames.com coverage.
