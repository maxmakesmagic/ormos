 ![Crovax the Cursed](https://i.ormos.uk/bd/21/bd21d9dae05ce0e1586917d70255f9c604f8845b6462d40d142545d29113a3e9.jpg) 

The card **Crovax the Cursed** represents the cursed nobleman Crovax after he has become transformed into a vampire. And then there's Ascendant Evincar, which depicts Crovax after he's become the evincar of Rath. But that's not all the cards that depict this former *Weatherlight* crewman's bloody history -- not even close.

  

 ![](https://i.ormos.uk/4b/74/4b748405e9be6fb7ed26e2c9bfa6d9422e1846dee6276a763b1e27c374b7b8f3.jpg) 

Enfeeblement shows Crovax despairing over his betrayal by Selenia, Dark Angel.

  

 ![Slaughter](https://i.ormos.uk/0d/92/0d9266f0e4321ae5fc07b1ae45980179d01c8fe394a6ef6ffc12293ef698ca7b.jpg) 

Slaughter shows him killing Mirri, Cat Warrior soon after becoming a vampire.

  

 ![Death Stroke](https://i.ormos.uk/5f/32/5f3210d5044454df6e0872765c4e6da48996089f4b4284136e83fb88924d6018.jpg) 

Death Stroke shows Crovax slaying Selenia in revenge, sealing his doom and fulfilling his curse.

  

 ![Gerrard's Verdict](https://i.ormos.uk/49/da/49dae9b0ea15e90705e55bdbfa57bf72d30bd2cf5d0792a6eb5d12f5bbc7f497.jpg) 

And Gerrard's Verdict depicts Gerrard Capashen killing the now cruel and genocidal evincar Crovax.

  

There are plenty more. What other cards can you find that show off this tortured soul? Check out the [**Magic** Storyline Summaries](https://web.archive.org/web/20090604063521/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Article.aspx?x=magic/products/storylinesummaries) page for more stories of Crovax and the *Weatherlight*.
