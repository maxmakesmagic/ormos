In [today's Serious Fun article](/en/articles/seriousfun/2013/12-10-tis-the-season), Bruce Richard provides a buyer's guide for the **Magic** player on your list (or for you, if you hand the article to your loved ones!). One of the gift ideas he mentions are [mana hoodies](http://www.mtgmerch.com/catalogsearch/result/?q=hoodie) from [MTGMerch.com](http://www.mtgmerch.com/).

We wanted to bring these up because once they sell out they are gone for good. Also because they are quite awesome. Don't take my word for it, though; check it out: 

[![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/Araca_ManaHoodies_12-15.jpg)](http://www.mtgmerch.com/catalogsearch/result/?q=hoodie)  

Something else we wanted to mention: if you order $50 or more on [MTGMerch.com](http://www.mtgmerch.com/) before December 15, you will get free shipping!
