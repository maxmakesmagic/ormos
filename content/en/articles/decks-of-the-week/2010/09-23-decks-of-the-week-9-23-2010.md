The [prerelease event](https://web.archive.org/web/20100923000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prerelease-facts) is only two days away, and you can view the entire set in the [Visual Spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20100923000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/scarsofmirrodin/spoiler). As you prepare for how the new set will shake up Constructed environments, take a look at the deck lists from Brazil Nationals and a bushel of **Magic Online** deck lists below and get a head start on fine-tuning your favorite deck with the new cards.
