## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ed/25/ed25a98f25c326d9239e17568b3f475a82ddd4a2faabf12bd180273e1e3503ec.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, a card box with panoramic art, two deck boxes, nine 15-card booster packs, 80 land cards, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8b/b3/8bb3529c2a71d9d2acdb043f2ed8c1c1bd95ef84c806e913ac051b14fd0e7921.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c4/e8/c4e87cefc2a3280358a8c2e2cec73bceab0580fe6115ab026577bc926a4c3231.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 cards, a foil premium rare card, one 15-card booster pack, a strategy insert, and a **Magic** learn-to-play guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719045018/http://magic.wizards.com/node/960526)

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/82/9e/829e678a7861bdf4f4e7c842bebefd0d1fbc24192b18e01555c3119aa8dbfdc0.png) This 155-card black-bordered set includes randomly inserted premium versions of all cards in the set, new mechanics (Battle Cry and Living Weapon), and a new Planeswalker card.

**Set Name:** *Mirrodin Besieged*  
**Block:** Set 2 of 3 of the *Scars of Mirrodin* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 155

**Languages:** English, Chinese Traditional, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

**Official Three-Letter Code:** MBS  
**Twitter Hashtag:** #MTGMBS

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719045018/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/MBS_FAQ_EN.rtf)

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Mark L. Gottlieb (lead), Gregory Marques, Ken Nagle, Mark Rosewater, Mike Turian

**Final Game Design and Development:** Erik Lauer (lead), Ryan Dhuse, Zac Hill, Tom LaPille, Mike Turian
