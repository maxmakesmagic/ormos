Check out these Top 8 Standard decks from recent PTQs and WMCQs, as well as various decks from recent **Magic Online** events. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#theros). Good luck; have fun!
