I've been liking hybrid decks recently, and particularly ones that combine the [Life from the Loam](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Life+from+the+Loam) engine with other themes and/or colors. Maybe it's that I like the combination of two good things, or that I really like casting [Life from the Loam](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Life+from+the+Loam), or possibly both. Either way, today I'm taking a look at a deck that lands somewhere on the [Life from the Loam](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Life+from+the+Loam) to Maverick spectrum, with interesting elements of each.

The deck is certainly more Loam-based than not, with the full seven [Life from the Loam](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Life+from+the+Loam)s (thanks to [Burning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Wish)), but the presence of [Knight of the Reliquary](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+the+Reliquary), [Gaddock Teeg](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gaddock+Teeg), and [Green Sun's Zenith](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Green+Sun%27s+Zenith) adds some spice. You don't often see [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant), [Chalice of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chalice+of+the+Void), [Gaddock Teeg](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gaddock+Teeg), and [Burning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Wish) all fighting side by side, although some of these pairs do like teaming up.

Knights help drive the Loam engine, and are a solid replacement for [Seismic Assault](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seismic+Assault). After you've found a Loam, [Knight of the Reliquary](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+the+Reliquary) can help fetch all sorts of interesting lands, with a toolbox that includes [Wasteland](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland), [Karakas](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karakas), [Maze of Ith](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Maze+of+Ith), [Nantuko Monastery](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Monastery), [cycling lands](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler%1Aamp;method=visual%1Aamp;action=advanced%1Aamp;text=+[cycling]%1Aamp;type=+[%22Land%22]), [Bojuka Bog](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bojuka+Bog), [Grove of the Burnwillows](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grove+of+the+Burnwillows), and even [Dryad Arbor](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dryad+Arbor). Of course, fetching up a cycle land isn't as effective as the rest, so I'd try and avoid doing so.

The rest of the deck is an eclectic mix of powerful and disruptive cards. [Burning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Wish) of course pulls double duty here, fetching all sorts of answer card from your sideboard, but the deck also has a full four [Chalice of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chalice+of+the+Void)s, three [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay)s, three [Punishing Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Punishing+Fire)s, and the lone [Gaddock Teeg](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gaddock+Teeg) (that can be searched for with the one [Green Sun's Zenith](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Green+Sun%27s+Zenith)). Between Wish, Chalice, Decay, and Fire, there aren't many things that can't be dealt with, given enough time (and [Karakas](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karakas) helps cover a ton of bases as well).

It does look odd to me that the deck only plays one [Green Sun's Zenith](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Green+Sun%27s+Zenith), so I'd definitely try and fit more. Having a [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf), a [Gaddock Teeg](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gaddock+Teeg), and a [Dryad Arbor](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dryad+Arbor) seems a little odd when you only have one way to find them, so playing two or three Zeniths seems good. I also like more [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20131023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant)s, if only because Bob is just that good.
