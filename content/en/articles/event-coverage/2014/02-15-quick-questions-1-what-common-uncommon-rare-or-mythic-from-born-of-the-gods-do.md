![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmex14/fennell_qh.jpg)

***Chris Fennell:** "I think the best common is [Fall of the Hammer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fall+of+the+Hammer). For uncommon, probably [Ornitharch](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornitharch). And then for rare/mythic, I think [Forgestoker Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forgestoker+Dragon) is the best rare."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmex14/manfield_qh.jpg)

***Seth Manfield:** "For common, [Bolt of Keranos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bolt+of+Keranos). [Raised by Wolves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raised+by+Wolves) is the best uncommon. I also like WU Heroic so I think [Ephara, God of the Polis](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ephara%2C+God+of+the+Polis) is the best rare you can open."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmex14/freeman_qh.jpg)

***Grand Prix Detroit 2013 Quarterfinalist Marcelino Freeman:** "For common I think I'd really like to open [Bolt of Keranos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bolt+of+Keranos), or maybe [Akroan Skyguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroan+Skyguard). For rare/mythic, I would most want to open either [Xenagos, God of Revels](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xenagos%2C+God+of+Revels) or [Forgestoker Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forgestoker+Dragon)."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmex14/marlon_qh.jpg)

***Grand Prix Dallas 2013 Champion Marlon Avila Gutierrez:** "For rare, it is [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos). I think it is my top pick. For uncommon, it is [Glimpse the Sun God](https://web.archive.org/web/20140215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glimpse+the+Sun+God). For common, it is Snake of the Garden Grove."*
