A return to Ravnica isn't complete without some trusty tokens, and [*Ravnica Remastered*](https://web.archive.org/web/20231212000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/ravnica-remastered) has 19 beautiful full-art tokens and a Domri Rade emblem to collect. In Draft Boosters, these appear as single-faced non-foil tokens. In Collector Boosters, you can find these as double-faced tokens in traditional foil.

Experience beloved characters and mechanics again or for the first time with *Ravnica Remastered*! The set releases on January 12, 2024, and is available for preorder now at [your local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20231212000000/https://locator.wizards.com/), online retailers like [Amazon](https://gtly.to/omY1ePWS8), and elsewhere *Magic* products are sold.
