Halloween is a little over a month away, which means it's time to start thinking about your costume. Are you thinking about it? Good! Because this is another in our series of Arcanas featuring people in **Magic**-inspired costumes. We've shown [Wizards employees](/en/articles/arcana/2008/05-19-magic-convention-costumes) (from 1995 or so) and [fans](/en/articles/arcana/2009/01-14-efficient-costuming) (at PAX 2008), and now it's time to check out the state of **Magic** costume design!

At the [**Magic** party at PAX 2009](/en/articles/arcana/2009/09-16-zendikar-prerelease-promos), we had people roaming the crowd in Planeswalker costumes. But they weren't just *any* costumes, because they had helpful fold-down chest panels to allow the people in the costumes to give **Magic** demos while maintaining their strict roaming schedule. Check it out!
