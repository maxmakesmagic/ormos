Evaluating the power of individual cards against one another can be difficult when a new set is first released. The ability to establish which cards are most desirable at every rarity level puts a player well ahead of his or her opponents. I sat down with some of the most well-known players here at Grand Prix Mexico city to ask, “What’s your favorite uncommon in *Fate Reforged* Limited?”

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmex15/gpmexqq2huang-2.jpg)  
**Hao-Shan Huang:** “[Abzan Beastmaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20150131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Beastmaster).”

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmex15/gpmexqq2stern-2.jpg)  
**Jon Stern:** “[Elite Scaleguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20150131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elite+Scaleguard) is probably the best, but [Honor's Reward](https://web.archive.org/web/20150131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Honor%27s+Reward) is really fun... I’m gonna have to go with Frostwalker.”

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmex15/gpmexqq2juza-2.jpg)  
**Martin Jůza:** “[Abzan Beastmaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20150131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Beastmaster).”

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmex15/gpmexqq2yu-2.jpg)  
**Ben Yu:** “[Temur Sabertooth](https://web.archive.org/web/20150131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temur+Sabertooth) is probably the best with [Elite Scaleguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20150131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elite+Scaleguard) and [Pyrotechnics](https://web.archive.org/web/20150131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyrotechnics) close behind. Seth \[Manfield\] thinks [Arc Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20150131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Lightning) is better than [Pyrotechnics](https://web.archive.org/web/20150131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyrotechnics), but he’s wrong. Rawr!”

* * *

This is only our first week exploring the *Fate Reforged* Limited format and opinions are sure to change with time. What’s your favorite uncommon for Limited in *Fate Reforged*?
