**Announcement Date:** June 19, 2009  
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2009

**Vintage**  
[Thirst for Knowledge](https://web.archive.org/web/20090619000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thirst+for+Knowledge) is restricted

[Crop Rotation](https://web.archive.org/web/20090619000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crop+Rotation) is no longer restricted  
[Enlightened Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20090619000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enlightened+Tutor) is no longer restricted  
[Entomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20090619000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Entomb) is no longer restricted  
[Grim Monolith](https://web.archive.org/web/20090619000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Monolith) is no longer restricted

**Standard, Extended, Legacy, *Shards of Alara* Block Constructed**  
 No changes

Changes to **Magic Online**–only formats are now announced monthly in the [**Magic Online** Announcements forum.](http://forums.gleemax.com/forumdisplay.php?f=667)

For an explanation of this announcement, read the [Latest Developments](https://web.archive.org/web/20140425064701/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/Archive.aspx?tag=Latest%20Developments&description=Latest%20Developments) column next Friday, June 26. For the complete list of all banned and restricted cards by format, click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424103806/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=judge/resources/banned).
