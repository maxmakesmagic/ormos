## GRAND PRIX FLORENCE 2015

TOP 4 COVERAGE

432 teams entered the tournament on Saturday, 40 teams made it back on Sunday, but in the end only three players could hoist the trophies of Grand Prix Florence 2015! Those three were Frank Schäfer, Rosario Maij, and Adrian Rosada (pictured above, left to right), all from Berlin, who battled it out in the finals versus fellow Berlin players Robin Steinborn, Jasper Grimmer, and Amit Cohen. Before that both had defeated British teams in the semifinals, Eduardo Sajgalik/Daniel Royde/Fabrizio Anteri and Dan Gardner/Marco Orsini Jones/Matteo Orsini Jones respectively.

As always, the Team Limited format brought out some of the best players in the world, including the trio of (14) William Jensen, (21) Reid Duke, and Ben Stark as well as (11) Shahar Shenhar, (19) Alexander Hayne, and Jesse Hampton. It also demanded a number of difficult decisions during deck construction, in intense matches, and, of course, during the final two drafts.

Throughout all of that, three prevailed. Congratulations to Frank Schäfer, Rosario Maij, and Adrian Rosada, who took the team title and trophies for Berlin!

DAY 2 COVERAGE

Welcome back for the second day of the competition. Of an original 432 teams, merely 40 remain in the running, as players battle for their shot at the fame, the glory, and the title of champions of Grand Prix Florence 2015!

On this second stretch of the tournament, teams will again have to build three 40-card decks from a pool of eight boosters *Dragons of Tarkir* and four boosters *Fate Reforged*. Those decks will then be played through another five rounds before cutting to the Top 4. Following that, two teams will be eliminated through one Team Booster Draft, with another draft finally determining the winners of the Grand Prix.

In a particularly good position at the start of the day were the teams of Frank-Samuel Schäfer, Adrian Rosada, and Rosario Maij at 9-0 and Simon Nielsen/Martin Müller/Alexander Pasgaard as well as Dan Gardner/Matteo Orsini Jones/Marco Orsini Jones and perennial favorites Ben Stark/William Jensen/Reid Duke, all at 8-1.

Join us for another exciting day full of fun, action, and excitement, in short: a day full of *Magic* …

DAY 1 COVERAGE

After nine rounds of Team Sealed play the original number of 432 teams has been reduced to a mere 40.Those 40 teams and their 120 members will return in the morning for another Team Sealed deck construction and another five rounds before the Top 4.

Overnight, just one team leads the field with a perfect record of 9-0: Congratulations to Frank-Samuel Schäfer, Adrian Rosada, and Rosario Maij on this achievement! However, this can only be considered a stepping stone on the stony road toward the playoffs, and fierce competition like Simon Nielsen/Martin Müller/Alexander Pasgaard, perennial favorites Ben Stark/William Jensen/Reid Duke, or Fabrizio Anteri/Daniel Royde/EduardoSajgalik is in hot pursuit.

The players will return tomorrow, we will return tomorrow with more coverage, and you should return too! Until then, good night from Florence.
