One of the cool things about the otherwise rote job of revealing the set packaging to all you fine folks is that sometimes that means I get to also reveal preview cards to you that happen to be on the packaging.

And we've got some doozies on the *Eternal Masters* packaging.

First up, it's banned in Modern, incredibly powerful in Legacy, and one of the great Cube all-stars, let's hear it for *Deeeeeeaaaaattttthhhhhhh-riiiiiiiiiiite Shaaaaaaaaa-maaaaaan*.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/adhfianalodifdj2_EMA/en_Q1ty4hS9yB.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/XC6NgZgpGL.png)

On our second booster, weighing in at 3 power and 2 toughness, hailing from the plane of Alara, it's the banned-in-Modern *Bloooooooooood-braaaaaaaaaaid Elllllllllf*!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/adhfianalodifdj2_EMA/en_PbzylUZQJk.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/ASW37OK3Kr.png)

And finally, in our third booster of the set we have...

Well, I'm going to let [The Professor](https://youtu.be/k9jR4_23GTo) tell you all about it. (If that hint isn't strong enough, click that link for a preview video from Tolarian Community College).

The booster carrying the image of The Professor's preview card looks a little like this:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/gwLLJCmiSz.png)
