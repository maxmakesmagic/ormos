Echo Tracer dominated the vote, followed by Essence Sliver and Mistform Seaswift. With no illusions to enhance the potency of the sliver, most of you feel the blue-white deck needs the versatile bounce effect. I have always found Echo Tracer to be strictly better than Mistform Seaswift, but fifteen percent of you would rather have the flier. Let's see where the professionals stand on this issue.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5a/1f/5a1f7bb6ca78b1a91d234c2d05f8a6d75a2141b1f453c1291103233dc7e86d9d.jpg)

Kai Budde: I would definitely pick Essence Sliver here. While I do like Echo Tracer a lot, I learned to not like the morph trigger cards that much any more. Six mana for an Unsummon and a Gray Ogre is not exactly cheap. The sliver, on the other hand, provides a great body that is in some cases obscene if you happen to have one or more Mistforms in play. When I draft white-blue, I want a really aggressive deck and I don’t want to go turn three morph and then spend a turn to bounce something. That is obviously great if you go first, but that happens only every second game. And if your opponent played first, you don’t really get tempo advantage. My ideal draw is a two-power creature on turn two, a two-power flier on turn three and a three-power creature or a flier on turn four. Essence Sliver fits really well into that curve and that is why I’d pick it.

Ed Fear: In this booster pack, my pick would be Mistform Seaswift. First off, there is certainly no card powerful enough that we need to consider picking a card outside our colors. Of the five cards in our colors, Starlight Invoker and Covert Operative are weak enough that they do not merit consideration. Of the other three, each is likely worth consideration, especially depending upon the exact contents of your deck, but the best in this case is Mistform Seaswift for a couple of reasons. First, the absence of any Mistform creatures devalues the Essence Sliver. Secondly, it also increases the likelihood that there is a current drought of fliers in your deck, since you have no Mistform Dreamers or even Mistform Shriekers. While Essence Sliver is certainly a powerful card, I feel Seaswift is the best choice in this situation.

**William Jensen**: Echo Tracer is not only the best card in the pack by a far margin, but the fact you have no Mistforms at all makes an already easy choice even easier. The pick is Echo Tracer.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/66/e1/66e186af71ca20c0e8923861b3b5c4f5ccfeeb1c8dee5b722bded7dc902d5e97.jpg)

**Brian Kibler**: I'd pick Echo Tracer. Despite the efficiency of Essence Sliver at 3/3 for four with built-in Spirit Link, the bounce effect of Echo Tracer is extremely important to the aggressive flier-based plan of blue-white in O-O-L draft. The ability to break through flying defenses like Spitting Gourna by sending it back to your opponent's hand is essential to the archetype.

**Craig Krempals**: Although the Essence Sliver is a very powerful card for its mana cost, the Echo Tracer is just far and away a better card for the blue-white deck. The blue-white deck is generally aggressive, looking to win with its fast fliers before the opponent can stabilize. This is where the Tracer really shines, bouncing opposing fliers, Gournas, and other problem creatures such as a wizard enchanted with Lavamancer's Skill. Late in the game, Echo Tracer turns into the perfect blocker, chumping their fatty every turn while bouncing itself.

**Ken Krouner**: I don't actually see this pick as that tricky. Echo Tracer is simply the best card in the pack. It fills a void that white-blue needs filled. The only realistic argument for taking Essence Sliver is the possibility of getting the Seaswift, or at certain tables the Echo Tracer on the lap. Considering how weak the pack is though, if nothing else, these cards will likely be defensively drafted before you see them again. The short answer: Echo Tracer.

**Raphael Levy**: Echo Tracer is one of the cards you need to have in blue-white, If you are blue-white, you probably already have evasion creatures from *Onslaught* (if you're hesitating between Tracer and Seaswift). You will also most likely get some in *Legions*: Keeneye Aven, Wingbeat Warrior, Aven Redeemer or even a Seaswift. It is also more likely that you will get a Seaswift than a Tracer passed to you. I would only take Cromeshell Crab and Akroma, Angel of Wrath (it's even close on this one), which are both better than Essence Sliver, over Tracer.

**Sol Malka**: I would take Essence Sliver. While it's true that Echo Tracer is a key card in the archetype, the Sliver's size at that cost is a novelty in either blue or white and is especially good in the land of 2/2s for ![3 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/7f/5d/7f5d2f1e55eaa9d19a9a23027b63bfbb5b39dbf8642160dda1a477a03df23a6a.gif). Played on turn 4, the Sliver is probably going to get to swing unmolested once or twice and the Spirit Link ability, even when it isn't being granted to any of your other creatures (illusions), swings games by putting you well ahead in a race. Finally, since there are three playable blue cards in the pack and at least eight solidly playable cards left after you take your first pick, it's very likely you'll wheel the Operative, which usually makes the cut. Besides, Echo Tracer isn't really an answer to the Onslaught commons (Sparksmith, Lavamancer's Skill, and to a lesser extent Wellwisher) that give u-w nightmares. An early 'Smith (assuming one other goblin) or a Skilled Wizard is going to wreck you whether or not you have Echo Tracer in your hand, because you won't be able to play and use it until you have six mana and by then the 'Smith or Skill will have stunted your development enough to make the game virtually unwinnable.

**Gabriel Nassif**: Assuming the blue-white deck has a soldier theme, I would take Echo Tracer. I want my non-soldier creatures to add something to the deck, not just be another flyer or random guy. For other archetypes, it really depends on what I have, but I will either take Tracer or Sliver.

**Josh Ravitz**: I think for most blue-white decks the choice is between Echo Tracer and Essence Sliver. In most of my soldier decks, any non-soldier is certainly going to have to pull its weight and both of these cards do that much more so than Mistform Seaswift does. For me, the choice is Essence Sliver. It simultaneously provides a solid ground defense into which most green decks won't be able to attack while letting you race from the air. It also has the bonus of allowing any potential Mistform Seaswifts you do happen upon to become that much better. The Gourna issue is always something to be concerned with, but with Choking Tethers, Essence Fracture, various Gustcloaks, and any further Echo Tracers you may get a crack at, I think Essence Sliver is the correct choice. 

**Paul Sottosanti**: Given the knowledge that I currently have no Mistforms, it is fairly easy for me to discount the idea of taking the Essence Sliver. The card is good at a lot of things, but it doesn't excel at any one thing unless it has help, either from other slivers or from being able to occasionally impart its ability to your illusions. Given that the other white-blue slivers are rather sub-par, and the fact that it would even sometimes help opposing blue mages who do have Mistform Dreamers and the like, I'm not too excited about the pick. So it comes down to Mistform Seaswift versus Echo Tracer, which is ironically the dilemma that Ken Krouner and I fought over a couple months ago. In that article I went with the Seaswift, but I admit that I wasn't completely convinced then, and I'm still not now. It depends mainly on the variable of how much flying your deck already has, as a strong white-blue deck must start dropping good evasion creatures by turn four in this format. Without access to that information I'm going to have to go with the Echo Tracer due to his flexibility and uniqueness. However, if I hadn't managed to pick up one or two Ascending Avens in the *Onslaught* packs, I would have no qualms about taking the Seaswift instead.

**Ben Stark**: This week's question isn’t a drafting situation, it's simply which card is better in blue-white. While Essence Sliver is a solid creature and certainly a good card, I don't feel this decision is very close. Echo Tracer fills a spot in the blue-white deck that very few cards fill in this format. There is no other common bounce and you want and need him. I would take Echo Tracer every time.

**Mike Turian**: I would pick the Echo Tracer. I think the Tracer is needed in blue-white for its removal ability. There are cards like the Gournas that the Seaswift and the Essence Sliver don't deal with that well. When I am blue-white, I am often looking for a nice soldier build with blue for support and Echo Tracer provides that.

**Mark Zajdner**: It is clearly a toss up between two cards. There is the best blue common in the set, Echo Tracer, and a powerful white rare, Essence Sliver. I don't believe that anyone picks the Seaswift over the Tracer, so that is why I put it as a toss up between these two cards. I think that the pick here is the Sliver despite the lack of illusions. It fills up the four-drop slot which is usually lacking in that color combination other than the Stingers, and you will have a shot at getting another Echo Tracer in a later pack whereas you have no chance at another Essence Sliver.

Most people, be they the public or the professionals, take Echo Tracer out of this pack. While blue-white must have fliers and this situation was in the abstract, it is very difficult, if not impossible, to imagine a scenario where Mistform Seaswift is better than Echo Tracer or Essence Sliver. Blue-white craves fliers because it often needs to overcome cards it can not remove by pressing a temporal advantage. Cards such as Gustcloak Harrier and Ascending Aven are good for the deck because they are inexpensive and prove efficient when comparing stats, creature type, and ability to casting cost. On the other hand, Aven Fateshaper and Primoc Escapee, though they both fly, are not spectacular in the deck because you are trying to race. With this in mind, Essence Sliver is always going to be better than Mistform Seaswift. The extra two toughness and Spirit Link are superior to flying, morph, and the illusion abilities. The possibility of picking up Mistform creatures later in *Legions* (or if you had already drafted Imagecrafter in *Onslaught*) outweighs the concern that other people will use this tactic against you, and is really just another of the sliver's positive attributes. 

Echo Tracer, while not normally a potent tempo card as Kai pointed out, is better than Mistform Seaswift because it provides such a unique and important function. Provided that you have the time and resources, it can remove Lavamancer's Skill, Improvised Armor, or any creature stopping your efficient blue-white army, such as a Spitting or Needleshot Gourna. It can also be used on itself to stall a morph army or one giant beast if you have the mana but need the time to attack in the air. Finally, it can protect any of your creatures from removal if they are valuable enough to warrant leaving three open, or return a Pacified creature. Bounce is a rare and valuable effect in this format, and Echo Tracer's diverse set of applications makes it powerful in any game in which you have six mana to pay for it. Mistform Seaswift will only be better than Echo Tracer in a game where a four casting cost, three-power flier is better due to mana curve and tempo needs. Flipping over a Seaswift clearly pales in comparison to flipping over a Tracer, so Seaswift's flexibility doesn't matter when comparing the two. Mistform Seaswift does not excel in its role as a four casting cost three-power flier due to its vulnerability, therefore Echo Tracer, which does excel at what it does, is the more powerful card.

So the real question is: Echo Tracer or Essence Sliver? This debate between William Jensen (Echo Tracer) and Kai Budde (Essence Sliver) took place after John Larkin had to face the decision in a practice draft in Yokohama and took the Tracer. As you see here, their arguments are pretty straightforward, and characterize different views on what blue-white as an O-O-L archetype needs. William calls Echo Tracer, "the best card in the pack by a far margin", and this is true in a sense. In terms of power and versatility, Echo Tracer is better than Essence Sliver, but this assumes you can afford the necessary six mana. Josh, Kai, Mark, and Sol point out the value of efficient blue-white four drops, and Essence Sliver is a very efficient four drop indeed. O-O-L draft has taught us to devalue morphs because of the abundance of them in *Legions*, and morph-triggers drop in value as a result. One, two, and four casting-cost standout creatures get an extra boost in power just for fitting into the mana curve. Everyone agrees blue-white is an aggressive deck in this format, and the mana curve becomes more important as a result. Although Echo Tracer is often solid against a problem creature such as a Gourna, it is just usually too slow against a Skilled creature, or some threatening provoke engine like Deftblade Elite/Crown of Fury. A blue-red deck normally aims to stabilize the game and utilize cards such as Echo Tracer to their fullest, but a blue-white deck needs to choose efficiency when given a close option. I have become convinced that Essence Sliver is the best choice.
