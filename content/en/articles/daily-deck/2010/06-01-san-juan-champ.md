With five Pro Tour Top 8s headed into Pro Tour–San Juan, Brazilian superstar Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa had a resume many players can only dream of. But he had never managed to actually clinch the title. That all changed in Puerto Rico. With *Rise of the Eldrazi* Draft the format for the Top 8, Paulo managed to finally earn his trophy with this green-black deck. Read about Paulo's exploits in the [official coverage](/en/events/2010/ptsj10/paulo-celebrates-six-on-the-beach-in-san-juan) of the Pro Tour.
