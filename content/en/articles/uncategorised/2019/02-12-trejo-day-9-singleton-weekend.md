Join Danny Trejo and Day\[9\] for an unforgettable weekend event—with no entry fee!

Trejo & Day\[9\] Singleton Weekend starts with the *Magic: The Gathering Arena* 0.12 Release on February 14 and continues until February 18 at 8 a.m. PT (4 p.m. UTC).

Have you ever thought to yourself . . .

*"I wish I could play Singleton this weekend."* 

*"I wish I could get alternate-art copies of [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20190212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) and [Ghalta, Primal Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20190212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghalta%2C+Primal+Hunger)."*

*"I wish I could get a picture of Danny Trejo holding Garruk's axe and Day\[9\] making a heart symbol, and maybe make it my desktop background."*

*MTG Arena* will make all those dreams into a reality this weekend. Below are the event details. We'll see you in game!

![VS_Valentines](https://media.wizards.com/2019/images/daily/PgnOAYETiD.jpg)

# Trejo & Day\[9\] Singleton Weekend

**Entry Fee:** None  
**Event Ends After:** 5 wins or 2 losses (whichever comes first)  
**Event Prizes:**

*   **0 Wins:** None
*   **1 Win:** 1 alternate-art copy of [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20190212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress)
*   **2 Wins:** 1 alternate-art copy of [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20190212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress)
*   **3 Wins:** 1 alternate-art copy of [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20190212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress)
*   **4 Wins:** 1 alternate-art copy of [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20190212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress)
*   **5 Wins:** 1 alternate-art copy of [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20190212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress), 1 alternate-art copy of [Ghalta, Primal Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20190212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghalta%2C+Primal+Hunger)
