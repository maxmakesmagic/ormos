The Rakdos guildhall, Rix Maadi (if you can call the lava-dripping, skull-covered, palatial, undercity lair of a demonlord merely a "guildhall"), took its look from the Rakdos section of the *Dissension* style guide. You wouldn't want to live there -- and you wouldn't want to visit, either. A den of gleeful atrocities? Hellscape of torturous incomprehensibility? Bad vacation spot!

  
  

Here's a good shot of some of those streams of molten Ravnican slag, from the "Hit" half of Hit // Run, in which a crazed Rakdos zombie holds a knife to an Azorius mage's throat. You're a long way from Prahv, lawmage.... 

![Hit // Run art by Darrell Riche](https://i.ormos.uk/52/6e/526e133614a6dc8076f56474503a3096581a8c6d6458ecd592f46e1b9c5fd853.jpg "Hit // Run art by Darrell Riche")  
*Hit // Run art by Darrell Riche*

When it comes to inflicting pain, Cult members are remarkably inventive.... 

And below is Rix Maadi itself. Note the steam boiling up from the sewer-pool surrounding the Dungeon Palace. Note also the *scale* of the guildhall -- those are human-sized figures on the lower bridge on the right, and you can see another figure, either exulting in some sort of torture or screaming for escape, on a platform on the left side.
