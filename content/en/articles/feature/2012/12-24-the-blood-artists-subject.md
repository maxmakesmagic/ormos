*This Arcana originally appeared on May 10, 2012.*

This is [Blood Artist](https://web.archive.org/web/20121224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Artist) from *Avacyn Restored*.

![Blood Artist](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Blood+Artist)

 

Naturally, the first thing we're going to do is take a closer look at the art.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/35/9e/359e22c5c3ae8c1ebff80febe59326e6c573f631145b5fa2b302eab9a6ef3941.jpg)

[Blood Artist](https://web.archive.org/web/20121224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Artist) | Art by [Johannes Voss](https://web.archive.org/web/20121224000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Johannes+Voss%22%5D)

When it's that big, we can see the artist's painting pretty clearly. Does it look kind of familiar? 

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/968_curseofoblivion.jpg)

[Curse of Oblivion](https://web.archive.org/web/20121224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Curse+of+Oblivion) | Art by [Jana Schirmer & Johannes Voss](https://web.archive.org/web/20121224000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Voss%22%5D&artist=%5B%22Schirmer%22%5D)

Yes, the pose is flipped, but the [Blood Artist](https://web.archive.org/web/20121224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Artist) is clearly working on [Curse of Oblivion](https://web.archive.org/web/20121224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Curse+of+Oblivion). Except that it's all done in red because that's what happens when you paint with blood.

![Curse of Oblivion](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Curse+of+Oblivion)
