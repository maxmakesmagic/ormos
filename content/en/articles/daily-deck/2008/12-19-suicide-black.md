We wrap up Rerun Week on Daily Decks with a rerun from one of the game's most popular archetypes: Suicide Black. Michael Belfatto used the above list to earn a Top 8 at Grand Prix-Columbus, which featured the Legacy format. He did it with some innovative cards like Plague Sliver; because some of his opponents had opted to play Counter Slivers, the 5/5 was actually a powerful win condition! While Michael's success with the deck is certainly spectacular, it seems like just a matter of time before we see another take on Suicide Black rerunning amok at the top tables...
