# Deck of the Day

1.  [Draw-Go](/en/events/2017/competitive-standard-constructed-league-2017-04-27/competitive-standard-constructed-league)

This is not a joke. This is not a test. This is not a cheeky reference. This is a real, 5-0 Standard Competitive League deck from *Magic Online* user Nasty that is fully capable of playing the draw-go game we thought we left behind some time ago.

Thanks to the power of [Engulf the Shore](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engulf+the+Shore) and [Torrential Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrential+Gearhulk), this deck is actually capable of playing almost its entire game at instant speed. And thanks to the reintroduction of [Essence Scatter](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Essence+Scatter), the deck can actually play a whopping *sixteen* counterspells—four each of [Censor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Censor), [Essence Scatter](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Essence+Scatter), [Disallow](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disallow), and the new and funky [Commit // Memory](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Commit+%2F%2F+Memory). From there, [Glimmer of Genius](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glimmer+of+Genius) and [Pull from Tomorrow](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pull+from+Tomorrow) refill the hand.

[Commit // Memory](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Commit+%2F%2F+Memory) is actually a key piece of this plan, as it's an instant-speed way to actually remove permanents from the board. It also has a powerful interaction with [Torrential Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrential+Gearhulk), in that you can flash in the Gearhulk to actually play the Memory half to refill your hand full of, well, let's face it, probably more counterspells. But it also lets you keep grabbing more [Engulf the Shore](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engulf+the+Shore), [Pull from Tomorrow](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pull+from+Tomorrow), and [Glimmer of Genius](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glimmer+of+Genius).

Also worth noting? The deck gets to play the full eight cycling lands to avoid flooding because, and this is key, they count as Islands for [Engulf the Shore](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engulf+the+Shore).

The only sorcery-speed spells in the deck are a pair of [Confiscation Coup](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Confiscation+Coup)—aka, the deck's best way to deal with artifacts—and a single [Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulamog%2C+the+Ceaseless+Hunger) to finish out the game should [Torrential Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrential+Gearhulk) prove not to be enough.

Oh, and that sideboard? Twelve *more* counterspells, each a touch more conditional.

With this deck, the most terrifying thing you can do is say "draw," pause, play a land, look your opponent square in the eye and say "go."
