Last weekend, Kyle Boggemes beat out 1,700+ other players to win in Standard at [Grand Prix Cincinnati](https://web.archive.org/web/20140328000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/gpcin14/welcome). Across the Atlantic, Aniol Alcaraz bested No. 1 ranked Jérémy Dezani in the finals at [Grand Prix Vienna](https://web.archive.org/web/20140328000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/gpvie14/welcome). Congratulations, Kyle and Aniol, who will be going to Pro Tour ***Magic** 2015* in Portland this August. You can join them by winning a Pro Tour Qualifier! The M15 PTQ season is Sealed Deck. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](https://web.archive.org/web/20140328000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtg/event/protour/qualifierlist#m15). Good luck; have fun!
