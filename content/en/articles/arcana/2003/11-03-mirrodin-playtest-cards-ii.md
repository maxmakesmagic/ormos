You've seen the common [Replica cycle](/en/articles/uncategorised/2003/09-22-mirrodin) in *Mirrodin*; you've read Randy's [discussion](/en/articles/latestdevelopments/2003/10-03-artifacts-and-color) over their colored activations; you've seen them [in action](/en/events/2003/gpkc03/finals-antonio-de-rosa-vs-nate-heiss) at the top tables of *Mirrodin* limited tournaments. But each one of these useful artifact creatures went through major or minor tweaking during design and development. Take a look at these playtest versions of this cycle - we think you'll be pleased with the agressive ways they turned out. (Click each playtest card to see the finished *Mirrodin* version.)
