While most players are taking their aggro decks in red-black form, VAT21 has decided to forge a different path. Eschewing black for green, the above list was taken all the way to the Top 8 of a Magic Online Premier Event. Boartusk Liege makes cards like Rip-Clan Crasher, Tattermunge Maniac, and Boggart Ram-Gang go the extra mile!
