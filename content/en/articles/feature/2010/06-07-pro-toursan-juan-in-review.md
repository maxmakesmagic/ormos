The 2010 **Magic** Pro Tour season continued in [San Juan](/en/events/2010/ptsj10/paulo-celebrates-six-on-the-beach-in-san-juan), and now it's time to see how the dust has settled. The second Pro Tour of the season showcased the full *Zendikar* Block Constructed format, which is always a great place to look for seeds of the upcoming Standard rotationn to Standard. Was there a dominant archetype? How varied was the field? Paul Jordan crunched the numbers for all the major deck archetypes. If you're looking for the hard numbers on what beat what, you won't want to miss it.

### Winner Interview with Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa

Pro Tour Statistician Rich Hagon sat down with San Juan champion Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa just minutes after the trophy had been awarded. Paulo talks about his team's approach to the Block Constructed format, how he came back from losing his first two matches on Friday, his plan for drafting *Rise of the Eldrazi* in the Top 8, and much more. You can listen to the podcast via the player to the right, or go to the Podcast page to download it.
