A deck that saw some play in the *Zendikar* Block Constructed format is Big Green. It seeks to stall the early game, then explode with a giant Eldrazi or Summoning Trap. Paul Ernest Lazaro made the second day of competition at Grand Prix–Manila with this updated-for-Standard take on the deck. Splashing white for Wall of Omens and Path to Exile, Paul does an even better job stalling. Either cast Summoning Trap or hard-cast a giant Eldrazi and voila! Dead opponent.
