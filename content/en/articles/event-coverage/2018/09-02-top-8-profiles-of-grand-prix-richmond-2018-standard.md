After 8 rounds the 786 players in the Standard Grand Prix Richmond were cut down to 171. After another 7 rounds, we have a Top 8!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpric18-standard/GP_Richmond18_JoeStempo.JPG)

**Name:** Joe Stempo

**Age:** 31

**Occupation:** Server

**Hometown:** Catasauqua, California

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** StarCityGames Invitational Top 8, SCG Open Top 8

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?**  
 Blue-Black Midrange. Played a bunch of games with Bant [Nexus of Fate](https://web.archive.org/web/20180902000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nexus+of+Fate) and it felt terrible. Went with an old standby instead.

**What was the best card in your deck and why?**  
 One Witty Boi ([Champion of Wits](https://web.archive.org/web/20180902000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Champion+of+Wits))! It's [Faithless Looting](https://web.archive.org/web/20180902000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faithless+Looting) on a body.

**What, if anything, was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Grixis Dragos. It wasn't Flame of Keld, Black-Red Aggro, or Esper Control.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**  
 First Pro Tour!!!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpric18-standard/GP_Richmond18_Tiu.JPG)

**Name:** Oliver Tiu

**Age:** 20

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Cambridge, Massachusetts

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** This is my 6th Grand Prix Top 8 this year.

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?**  
 Blue-White Control. [Teferi, Hero of Dominaria](https://web.archive.org/web/20180902000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%2C+Hero+of+Dominaria) is still good.

**What was the best card in your deck and why?**  
 Teferi; it has cool abilities.

**What, if anything, was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Black-Red Aggro - wasn't expecting that.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**  
 Just another Sunday.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpric18-standard/GP_Richmond18_SethManfield.JPG)

**Name:** Seth Manfield

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Professional *Magic* Player

**Hometown:** Columbia, Maryland

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Pro Tour *Ixalan* winner, 2015 Worlds winner

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?**  
 Red-Black Midrange because Brad Nelson told me to.

**What was the best card in your deck and why?**  
[Karn, Scion of Urza](https://web.archive.org/web/20180902000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn%2C+Scion+of+Urza). It can provide insane card advantage.

**What, if anything, was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?**  
 No surprises.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**  
 At least one point towards the Player of the Year race.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpric18-standard/GP_Richmond18_JoelSodowski.JPG)

**Name:** Joel Sadowsky

**Age:** 24

**Occupation:** Whole Foods

**Hometown:** Columbia, Maryland

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** World *Magic* Cup competitor and one-time Pro Tour competitor.

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?**  
 Esper Control because [Torrential Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20180902000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrential+Gearhulk), [Teferi, Hero of Dominaria](https://web.archive.org/web/20180902000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%2C+Hero+of+Dominaria), and [Vraska's Contempt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180902000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vraska%27s+Contempt) are great.

**What was the best card in your deck and why?**  
[Search for Azcanta](https://web.archive.org/web/20180902000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Search+for+Azcanta) because it smoothed my draws.

**What, if anything, was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?**  
 None.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**  
 I get Bronze, play on the Pro Tour, and finally reach one of my goals!

**Hometown:** Atlanta, Georgia

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Dominican Republic
