Ixalan is a plane full of wonderous treasure—so much treasure that you may have trouble knowing what to do with it all! In the Treasured Festival: Historic Singleton event, you can collect Treasure card styles—four wins gets them all!

![Gold-Forged Thopteryx Treasure card style](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/cardstyle_treasure_gold-forged_thopteryx.png)

![Harnessed Snubhorn Treasure card style](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/cardstyle_treasure_harnessed_snubhorn.png)  
Harnessed Snubhorn Treasure card style 

Bring a [60-card Historic deck](https://web.archive.org/web/20230529000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/historic) with no duplicates besides basic lands and excluding [cards in the banned list (see below)](#historicbanned). At the start of each of your upkeep phases, you create an artifact Treasure token to sacrifice for a mana of any color, reaping the bounty of Ixalan!

## Event Details

*   **Dates**: June 2, 6 a.m. PT (15:00 UTC) until June 5, 6 a.m. PT (15:00 UTC)
*   **Entry**: 2,500 gold or 500 gems
*   **Format**: Historic Singleton Best-of-One
*   **Structure**: Play as much as you want until event ends.
