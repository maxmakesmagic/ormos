Although Avacyn is missing from Innistrad, her symbol is everywhere. We originally covered this in [September](/en/articles/arcana/2011/09-29-the-symbol-of-avacyn) by looking at several pieces of art from *Innistrad* that showed the symbol. Since then, *Dark Ascension* has come out, and that means it's time to take another look. Hooray!

The most prominent place to see the symbol of Avacyn is in the iconography of her church. For example, there's this collar:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8b/fb/8bfb4f60fad403c3edc9a65a5c0f76fa4ba2d126e1abe96d23270f4dadf97cb5.jpg) 

Avacyn's Collar | Art by [James Paick](https://web.archive.org/web/20120301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22james+paick%22])

You'd think those collars would be unique to the priesthood, but they've also collared their griffins:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/40/28/402874a6cd504ecc3bf8c78c2784409a57a10e1dc8a83b625b22e1b023960264.jpg) 

Silverclaw Griffin | Art by [Daniel Ljunggren](https://web.archive.org/web/20120301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22daniel+ljunggren%22])

Even Clerics who aren't working inside the church wield Avacyn's symbol. Thraben Doomsayer has a staff that should look very familiar at this point.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/03/eb/03eb53d0b6d5d90e47ead1291499b8d97425bfb1481fb5a59ee5107ddfca1083.jpg) 

Thraben Doomsayer | Art by [John Stanko](https://web.archive.org/web/20120301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22john+stanko%22])

The obvious symbol on Executioner's Hood is on the wall in the background. But a closer inspection of the hood itself reveals that the symbol has been stiched onto the face!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/40/30/40307bbe7926176b3a1041ff0a43cbe7318c5198009991c1baea9e92f28c61fd.jpg) 

Executioner's Hood | Art by [Anthony Palumbo](https://web.archive.org/web/20120301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22anthony+palumbo%22])

The symbol of Avacyn is so widespread, it's even in common use as a gravestone, as seen in Grafdigger's Cage

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9d/a1/9da13ae2d75cdb660aba058e804841d0abaa5dcae86ec4a4bcaa8f93c56766ce.jpg) 

Grafdigger's Cage | Art by [Daniel Ljunggren](https://web.archive.org/web/20120301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22daniel+ljunggren%22])

In fact, we've already shown you gravestones in that shape. Scroll back up to that Silverclaw Griffin and check out the graveyard. There are a couple of broken stones that clearly used to be in the shape of the symbol of Avacyn.
