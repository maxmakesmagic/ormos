### 

Dear Sideboard Readers,

We're pleased to announce that the popular trivia feature, Question Mark will be returning to Sideboard.com! 

Beginning on Monday, May 3 we'll be posting a new question every weekday on Sideboard.com. Questions are designed by Magic R&D member, Mark Rosewater.

After each question is posted, players will have until 11:00 AM the following weekday to submit their answer. That means that people have approximately 24 hours to answer a question on Monday through Thursday and approximately 72 hours to answer a Friday question, since answers will be due at 11:00 AM the following Monday.

Everyone who submits a correct answer will have an equal chance to win the daily prize. The daily prize will consist of a random promo foil card.

Because extra time is given on Fridays, the Friday questions will be "bonus" questions and will generally be more difficult than the other questions. The prize for the Friday questions will also be larger, consisting of a box of the latest Magic expansion in addition to a random promo foil card.

We hope everyone enjoys the contest and good luck!

*Sideboard Staff*
