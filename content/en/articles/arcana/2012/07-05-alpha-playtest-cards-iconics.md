Before **Magic** went on sale, it was playtested with cards made with clip art. We've shown them several times:

*   **[Alpha Playtest Cards](/en/articles/arcana/2003/05-22-alpha-playtest-cards-2)** (May 22, 2003)
*   **[More Alpha Playtest Cards](/en/articles/arcana/2008/06-09-more-alpha-playtest-cards-2)** (June 9, 2008)
*   **[Alpha Playtest Art](/en/articles/arcana/2008/08-28-alpha-playtest-art)** (August 28, 2008)
*   **[Alpha Cards That Didn't Make It](/en/articles/arcana/2009/06-24-alpha-cards-that-didnt-make-it)** (June 24, 2009)
*   **[Alpha Cards That Didn't Make It](/en/articles/arcana/2009/10-28-alpha-playtest-cards-more)** (October 28, 2009)
*   **[They Didn't Make It](/en/articles/arcana/2010/08-31-return-of-the-alpha-playtest-cards)** (May 19, 2010)
*   **[Return of the Alpha Playtest Cards](/en/articles/arcana/2010/08-31-return-of-the-alpha-playtest-cards)** (August 31, 2010)
*   **[Alpha: Setting the Record Straight](/en/articles/arcana/2010/09-21-alpha-setting-the-record-straight)** (September 21, 2010)
*   [**Alpha Playtest Cards: Flavorless Edition**](https://web.archive.org/web/20120705000000/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/807) (September 27, 2011)

That doesn't count the times [Mark Rosewater](/en/articles/feature/2010/03-08-raiders-of-the-lost-cabinet) has shown them. It's just Arcanas. And probably not all of the Arcanas, either.

Today, we're going to look at iconic **Magic** cards (a couple of which are in ***Magic** 2013*) and then show the original version. Don't be confused by the mana costs; during playtesting, "4RR" would mean that a card cost a total of four mana, of which two had to be red.
