![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/LamPhan.jpg)

### Lam Phan

**Age:** 32  
**Hometown:** Oshawa  
**Occupation:** Project Manager in construction 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
 Made a [Werebear](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Werebear) a thing at some GP Top 8. 

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 UR Landstill. I want to make [Sudden Shock](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sudden+Shock) a thing. 

**What was your record on Day 1 and Day 2?**  
 9-0, 4-2. 

**What, if anything, would you have done differently with your deck for this weekend, and why?**  
 I would play [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe). 

**Which card in your sideboard exceeded your expectations this weekend, and why?**  
[Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm). This metagame is full of x/2 creatures. 

**Were you cruisin' or are you bruisin' this weekend?**  
 Little bit of both. I played one [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) and one [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time).

### Philipp Schonegger

**Age:** 20  
**Hometown:** Germany  
**Occupation:** Student 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
 Top 8 GP Paris, Top 64 GP Strasbourg. 

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

 Deck is practically perfected!

 None, because I knew which place they had. Red/[Blue Elemental Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blue+Elemental+Blast) and Snapcaster are pretty amazing, though. 

**Were you cruisin' or are you bruisin' this weekend?**  
 Toppin'!

### Tom Ross

**Age:** 31  
**Hometown:** Baton Rouge, LA  
**Occupation:** StarCityGames Columnist 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
 PT Top 8, 3 Gp Top8s, 2 SCG Invitational wins 

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Infect. [Become Immense](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Become+Immense) is great. 

**What was your record on Day 1 and Day 2?**  
 8-1, 5-0-1

 Cut the Umezawa's Jitte from the sideboard. Play the 4th [Spell Pierce](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Pierce). 

**Which card in your sideboard exceeded your expectations this weekend, and why?**  
[Bojuka Bog](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bojuka+Bog). I played against Reanimator twice and Dredge once. 

**Were you cruisin' or are you bruisin' this weekend?**  
 Just one boat. It's all I need.

### Phillip Braverman

**Age:** 23  
**Hometown:** Hillsborogh, NC  
**Occupation:** Masters Accounting Student 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
 Two Top 8s StarCityGames Legacy Opens 

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Counterstone because I enjoy having card selection. Also, my friend Wilson helped me tune a lot of the sideboard slots. 

**What was your record on Day 1 and Day 2?**  
 7-1-1, 6-0. 

**What, if anything, would you have done differently with your deck for this weekend, and why?**  
 I would consider adding an additional [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time). The card overperformed for me all weekend. 

**Which card in your sideboard exceeded your expectations this weekend, and why?**  
 The blue blast - [Hydroblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hydroblast), [Blue Elemental Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blue+Elemental+Blast) - effect are very versatile. It took care of [Sneak Attack](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sneak+Attack)s and [Sulfuric Vortex](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sulfuric+Vortex)s for me. 

**Were you cruisin' or are you bruisin' this weekend?**  
 I am blasting!
