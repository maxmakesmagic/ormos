## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b2/e3/b2e3cfe38dddc863d98ebdda1a0927a6f415561e617e3d1741c8737ee04d2a09.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, six 15-card booster packs, one random premium card, one foil basic land card, the *Judgment* novel, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b2/e3/b2e3cfe38dddc863d98ebdda1a0927a6f415561e617e3d1741c8737ee04d2a09.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b2/e3/b2e3cfe38dddc863d98ebdda1a0927a6f415561e617e3d1741c8737ee04d2a09.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.
