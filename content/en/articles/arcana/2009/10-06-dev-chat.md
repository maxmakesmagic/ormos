Do you ever wish you could sit down and talk with the people who create **Magic: The Gathering**? Well, we've got the next best thing: a *Zendikar* Dev Chat!

  

**Who:** 

> **Magic** R&D director Aaron Forsythe (*Zendikar* Development Team) and **Magic** creative writer Doug Beyer (*Zendikar* Design Team)

**When:** 

> Thursday, October 8, 2:00 PM PDT (5:00 PM EDT)

**How:** 

> There are three ways to join the chat.  
>   
> 
> *   via widget: [http://tinyurl.com/devchat](https://web.archive.org/web/20091006000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://tinyurl.com/devchat&origin=) (Should take you directly to a login screen)
> *   via mibbit: [http://mibbit.com/chat](https://web.archive.org/web/20091006000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://mibbit.com/chat&origin=) (Takes you to a screen where you type in your desired username and your channel name (the channel will be #devchat )
> *   via IRC: If you have an IRC client, you can access it by going to the mibbit IRC and then the channel (#devchat).

See you there!
