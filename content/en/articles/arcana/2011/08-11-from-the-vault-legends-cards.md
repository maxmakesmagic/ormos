Hey! Let's quit fooling around here, okay? [*From the Vault: Legends*](https://web.archive.org/web/20140428185931/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/ftvlegends/productinfo) is coming out on August 26. It's going to have fifteen legendary creatures, all in foil. Its MSRP is $34.99. *You* want to know what's going to be in it. *We* have the answers. Let's just do this!

### New Cards!

That last card is Sun Quan, Lord of Wu from the extremely rare set *Portal Three Kingdoms*. This is not only Sun Quan's first appearance in the current card frame, it's his first appearance with black borders! And he's not the only *Portal Three Kingdoms* card to make it into *From the Vault: Legends*....
