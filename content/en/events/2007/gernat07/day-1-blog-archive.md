Thursday was meatgrinder day, with four full Standard grinders (64 players each) and one Draft grinder (58 players) paving the last-minute road to Nationals. The Draft grinder was the last to finish, but even winner Babak Mojtahedy had enough time to recover properly: He received his ticket to Nationals as early as 1 a.m.</p>&#10;      <p>In the Standard grinders, Christian Jäger (Angelfire), Andreas Hieninger (Angelfire), Alexander Jersch (Dredge) and Carsten Dannowski (Idiot Life) grabbed their seats for today's big show. But the attention-grabber someone else: <a>Stun</a>ning Josephine Rieth managed to captivate most player's attention like a magnet. Players flocked to watch the 22-year old play for a spot in the finals of the first meatgrinder, with R/G Beatdown.</p>&#10;      <table>&#10;        <tbody><tr>&#10;          <td>&#10;            <div>&#10;              <img src="http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/gernat07/josephine_rieth.jpg">&#10;            </div>&#10;            <div>&#10;              <i>Josephine Rieth, representing Germany's female Magic players.</i>&#10;            </div>&#10;          </td>&#10;        </tr>&#10;      </tbody></table>&#10;      <p>&quot;I started playing four years ago&quot;, recounts the gorgeous red-head. I got interested and then hooked.&quot; The assumption that she came to Aschaffenburg just as her friend's sidekick proved false; in fact, she says, &quot;I had to drag the others here.&quot; Josephine's goal is the Pro Tour - eventually: &quot;If it works out, that's fine. If not, I'll just have to keep working on it.&quot; She didn't qualify through the grinders, but not through lack of dedication: She has already signed in for today's side event PTQ.</p>&#10;      <p>For your enjoyment, here are the winning grinder lists.
