The key to this deck is that both [Undercity Informer](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Undercity+Informer) and [Balustrade Spy](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balustrade+Spy) mill the top card of your library until you hit a land, and if you don't have lands, well... it gets your whole deck. Under most circumstances, this would spell defeat, but not for this deck. It uses this opportunity to pull off the following combo: 

![Laboratory Maniac](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Laboratory+Maniac)

1.  Return four [Narcomoeba](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narcomoeba)s to play.
2.  Sacrifice three [Narcomoeba](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narcomoeba)s to flashback [Dread Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dread+Return), targeting [Angel of Glory's Rise](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel+of+Glory%27s+Rise).
3.  [Angel of Glory's Rise](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel+of+Glory%27s+Rise) returns your [Undercity Informer](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Undercity+Informer)s; [Wild Cantor](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Cantor); [Azami, Lady of Scrolls](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azami%2C+Lady+of+Scrolls); and (most importantly) [Laboratory Maniac](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Laboratory+Maniac) to the battlefield.
4.  Tap a Wizard with [Azami, Lady of Scrolls](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azami%2C+Lady+of+Scrolls) to draw a card, letting [Laboratory Maniac](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Laboratory+Maniac)'s ability replace that draw with winning the game.
5.  Profit

To get the mana needed to get [Undercity Informer](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Undercity+Informer) or [Balustrade Spy](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balustrade+Spy) to actually mill your deck, [Lotus Petal](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotus+Petal) and [Chrome Mox](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chrome+Mox) can get you started on black mana for [Dark Ritual](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Ritual) to ramp, and [Summoner's Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoner%27s+Pact) is able to get [Elvish Spirit Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Spirit+Guide) to add a mana as well. 

Finally, the sideboard. If your opponent is bringing in graveyard hate, this transformational sideboard lets you throw your opponent for a loop and turn into a subpar [Goblin Charbelcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Charbelcher) deck. Take out as many of the combo pieces as you can, and if your opponent plays a [Leyline of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leyline+of+the+Void), smile and rattle off a few Rituals into a [Goblin Charbelcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20130207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Charbelcher). If you activate it and reveal lands, but don't have any, it will simply deal damage equal to the number of cards in your library, which will be far more than twenty.
