### Hero's Path

Let's switch gears here and talk about the Hero's Path, which Dave Guskin explained [in yesterday's feature article](/en/articles/makingmagic/2013/07-24-the-path-of-the-hero). If you go to the [*Theros* Prerelease](https://web.archive.org/web/20130725000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/648591), September 21–22, 2013, you'll get one of these: 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2b/50/2b50449211489474e962a1f251ea92f42a69d2977be6d2f5b7fc68d3f55ca8bd.jpg)  

And here they are so you can read them all:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/21/fd/21fd93b61cf54326d103c4be5979c26b46904d4f78eab535e49369f32d90cf9c.jpg)  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/f8/58/f858847d83663d882cf0d889f468a6d125677da5b7d0c823482b8e29a5826d82.jpg)  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/39/e9/39e962c69196639ed713ffce2c2339b5ffbb44df06bcb177d238489f6d038287.jpg)  

And in case you didn't know, the backs of those cards are not normal **Magic**-card backs. Each one has a unique code you'll be able to redeem at [PlaneswalkerPoints.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20200422045834/http://www.wizards.com/magic/planeswalkerpoints) for an achievement. Those cards will then be used at Game Day when you go against the Face the Hydra Challenge Deck, which has cards that look like...
