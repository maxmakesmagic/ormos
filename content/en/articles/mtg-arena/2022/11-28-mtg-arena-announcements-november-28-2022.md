## Get Schematic Card Styles in The Brothers' Brawl Event!

Are you Team Urza or Team Mishra? On Friday, December 2, you'll have a chance to prove which is the mightier brother in [The Brothers' Brawl](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/11-21-battle-in-the-brothers-brawl-on-mtg-arena-december-29)! There are two events in one: join either Mishra's Forces or Urza's Army and go head-to-head with opposing players using preconstructed decks.

But don't limit yourself to just one side—try them both! Each event has its own set of schematic card styles that participants can claim, like these:

### Examples of Mishra's Forces Card Style Rewards

![Phyrexian Processor schematic card style](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/mishra_hqg42auxe8q.png) ![Ichor Wellspring schematic card style](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/mishra_y3rdbmpj9ut.png) ![Ramos, Dragon Engine schematic card style](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/mishra_ybkxyc5dm83.png)

### Examples of Urza's Army Card Style Rewards

![Key to the City schematic card style](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/urza_chcd6b49ew8.png) ![Self-Assembler schematic card style](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/urza_dxvsmnuq52z.png) ![Ornithopter schematic card style](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/urza_gfga4djykfm.png)

You'll find [complete details in The Brothers' Brawl event article](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/11-21-battle-in-the-brothers-brawl-on-mtg-arena-december-29).

## Premier Play Schedule

*All times listed are Pacific time (UTC-08:00).*

### Qualifier Play-Ins

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

#### December

*   **Best-of-One Qualifier Play-In**
     December 3, 6 a.m. PT–December 4, 6 a.m. PT (UTC-08:00)
    *   Format: *The Brothers' War* Sealed
*   **Best-of-Three Qualifier Play-In**
     December 9, 6 a.m. PT–December 10, 3 a.m. PT (UTC-08:00)
    *   Format: *The Brothers' War* Sealed

### Qualifier Weekends

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to the Arena Championship 1 held in the fourth quarter of 2022.

*Qualifier Weekend Day One and Day Two events open to entries at 6 a.m. PT and close to new entries at 8 a.m. PT (UTC-08:00) each day. You must enter within this two-hour window—late entries cannot be accommodated.*

#### December

*   **Qualifier Weekend**
     December 10, 6 a.m. PT–December 12, 4 p.m. PT
    *   Format: *The Brothers' War* Sealed (Best-of-Three)

### Arena Open

*The Arena Open Day 1 entry window begins at 6 a.m. PT and closes to new entries the following day at 3 a.m. PT (UTC-08:00).*

*Day 2 entry window is two hours only, from 6 a.m. PT until 8 a.m. PT (UTC-08:00).*

*   **Arena Open December 17–18**

*   Day 1, December 17: *[Arena Cube](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/11-28-the-arena-cube-draft-december-13january-1)* Player Draft Best-of-One and Best-of-Three
*   Day 2, December 18: *[Arena Cube](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/11-28-the-arena-cube-draft-december-13january-1)* Player Draft
     There are two Player Draft events on Day 2.
