When we say "the face of **Magic**, what do you think of? [Serra Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20080407000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serra+Angel)? [Shivan Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20080407000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Dragon)? [Maro](https://web.archive.org/web/20080407000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Maro)?

Well, what if we were to tell you (or, in the case of the old-time players, *remind* you) that for years and years, the face of the game was . . . [Hurloon Minotaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20080407000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hurloon+Minotaur):

![Hurloon Minotaur](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Hurloon+Minotaur)

This guy was *everywhere*, from denim jackets to the rulebook. When Wizards of the Coast opened a fancy Game Center in Seattle, guess who was there to greet people?

![Hurloon Minotaur welcomes you to the Game Center!](https://i.ormos.uk/13/bf/13bf01d32a0e69593ff33a7ac055546dc6a533c9e477fb8cd76fbc5b81269256.jpg)

In fact, the Hurloon Minotaur was actually the face of the game *before the game was even published.* Check out this ad for **Magic** (from *Primal Order: Chessboards*, an early Wizards of the Coast product), printed event before Hurloon had been added to the name:

![Anatomy of the Card](https://i.ormos.uk/3f/2e/3f2e701b8898bc2efbc217a90a480fd3830d51868d8858b9466c6e4393106d1f.jpg)

Incidentally, the Game Center Minotaur took a vacation when the Game Center was closed down. Now he lives very happily in a private backyard:

![Hurloon Minotaur welcomes you to a backyard!](https://i.ormos.uk/e3/59/e3597ea2426c120e4b5af0be4e92a2c6b527ae26cccf493d19341456528a81eb.jpg)

And here's an assortment of other places the Minotaur has turned up:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/11/da/11da69747a53f719a87996837047b24e706df595841ed88341f6117860c9a1bd.jpg)*In the original Alpha rulebook*  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/62/1a/621a48ce8b6a780fa26eeb08dd16d8b57045b1d20763fac5d520d78000103829.jpg)*In handsome pin form*  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/8a/89/8a89cb1d654c62965b9d4444ba7f7a93b0ad73f89f75e745fbfb1af85805c1bc.jpg)*A prototype Hurloon Minotaur tailcoat (only one was ever made!)*  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/ab/80/ab801239a201bf9a387d2b764014b6508474e80151d900dcd44027b84fff40b2.jpg)*The Hurloon Minotaur denim jacket (sold only to Wizards of the Coast employees back in 1995 or so)*

So, in honor of the former Face of the Game, here's a larger version of our old friend, the [Hurloon Minotaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20080407000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hurloon+Minotaur).
