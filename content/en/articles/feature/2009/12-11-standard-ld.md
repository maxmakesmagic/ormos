Many **Magic** players have long had a love/hate relationship with land destruction. At the [2009 World Championships](/en/articles/event-coverage/2009/11-12-coverage-of-2009-world-championships) in Rome, Italy, Bernhard Lehner took his turn at the strategy. [Goblin Ruinblaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20091211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Ruinblaster), [Ajani Vengeant](https://web.archive.org/web/20091211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ajani+Vengeant), and [Acidic Slime](https://web.archive.org/web/20091211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Acidic+Slime) were all accelerated to the battlefield with [Rampant Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20091211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rampant+Growth) and [Trace of Abundance](https://web.archive.org/web/20091211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trace+of+Abundance). Batting cleanup were [Garruk Wildspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20091211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Wildspeaker) and [Elspeth, Knight-Errant](https://web.archive.org/web/20091211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Knight-Errant), a powerful backup squad if there ever was one!
