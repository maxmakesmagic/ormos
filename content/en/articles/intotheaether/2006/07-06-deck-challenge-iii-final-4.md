### Tribal Wars - Standard 4x Open

Sunday June 25, 2006  
*78 players*

The end of June brought the first big Premier Event featuring the new Tribal Wars format – Standard. However, due to a mix-up, Umezawa's Jitte was not banned this time. Sometime in July we should be getting another Jitte-less Tribal Wars 4x, but in the meantime it's interesting to see what the new Tribal Wars looks like.

*Kamigawa's* Snake Tribe made a big splash with three entries in the Top 8, and Humans and Spirits nabbed two spots each. ineedabucket's Elves rounded out the top spots, employing a little spiritual help in the form of Seedborn Muse, which nicely turbo-charges Selesnya Guildmage, Vitu-Ghazi, and Glare of Subdual. The Spirit decks both made nice use of Greater Good for powerful card-drawing and the lock-down combo with Yosei. At least two of the Snakes decks also utilized Glare of Subdual to allow their small brethren to lock down larger foes until Seshiro could come out to lead the slithery troops to victory. Both of the Human decks were of the Orzhov variety, riding on the back of Dark Confidant and Black/White power cards.
