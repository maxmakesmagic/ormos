* * *

At midnight Pacific Standard Time, the <i>Onslaught<sup>TM</sup></i> set rotated into the <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> Constructed formats. What does that mean, exactly?</p>&#10;<p>With the <i>Onslaught</i> set rotating in, it means that the <i>Invasion<sup>TM</sup></i> block is leaving Standard Constructed format. Yes, it's time to say so long to cards like enemy-color pain lands, <a>Flametongue Kavu</a>, and <a>Meddling Mage</a>. Will <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> start seeing Standard decks like those played in the <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20021114000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=sideboard/info/champs2002decks&amp;origin=magic_magiconline_articleonsrotation">State and Provincial tournaments</a> at the beginning of this month? Only time will tell.</p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

<table><tbody><tr><td><p>It also means two new Constructed formats are available on <i><b>Magic</b> Online:</i> Online Extended and <i>Onslaught</i> Block Constructed. It's all about Otaria now, folks.
