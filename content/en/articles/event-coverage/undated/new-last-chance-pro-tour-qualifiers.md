### A new feature has been added to the Pro Tour!

![](http://www.wizards.com/protour/images/pt_sm.gif)![](http://www.wizards.com/protour/images/qt_sm.gif)Wizards of the Coast is pleased to announce a new addition to the Pro Tour. Beginning with the 2002-2003 tournament season, all Pro Tours will feature a last-chance qualifier tournament the day before the Pro Tour. 

All tournaments are run using the Swiss match structure, with a cut to Top 8. The Top 4 players from the tournament will earn invitations to the Pro Tour held the next day! For the team-format Pro Tour, the format will be slightly modified, with the Top 2 teams receiving Pro Tour invitations.

This new feature starts at Pro Tour-Boston and will be held in Houston, Chicago, Venice and Yokohama. Players who are already qualified may not participate in these last-chance qualifiers.

**Pro Tour-Boston Last Chance Qualifier**  
 The Bayside Expo Center  
 3-person Team Limited format using *Odyssey*-*Torment*-*Judgment*  
 2 team invitations will be awarded  
 4:00 PM registration, 5:00PM start time  
 $70 entry fee (per team)

More information will be available later this year at [http://www.wizards.com/protour.](https://web.archive.org/web/20050201021146/http://www.wizards.com/protour/)
