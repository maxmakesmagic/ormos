Two little-known players met in this semifinal pairing, two players still looking to make a name for themselves. One of them would have further opportunity by advancing to the finals of this 1,760-player event!

Magnus Lantto was playing [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod); Piotr Głogowski had brought an [Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor)/Primeval Titan/Hive Mind concoction with [Summer Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summer+Bloom), [Azusa, Lost but Seeking](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azusa%2C+Lost+but+Seeking), and lots of bouncelands like [Simic Growth Chamber](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simic+Growth+Chamber). This would certainly make for an interesting match!

# Game 1

Głogowski only cast his first spells on turn three: [Summoner's Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoner%27s+Pact) followed by [Azusa, Lost but Seeking](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azusa%2C+Lost+but+Seeking). He then spend turn four paying for the pact.

Meanwhile, Lantto had summoned some creatures, stuffed those creatures into [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) to get bigger creatures, but all of that proved immaterial when, on turn five, Głogowski cast [Hive Mind](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hive+Mind) and two [Summoner's Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoner%27s+Pact)s Lantto couldn't pay for.

Piotr Głogowski 1-0 Magnus Lantto

# Game 2

This time, Lantto started with some disruption. A turn-one [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) took [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan) out of Głogowski's hand, and basically that was enough already. Lantto assembled some creatures, pointed a timely [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay) at Głogowski's [Seal of Primordium](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seal+of+Primordium), and began using his [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod), while Głogowski had a lot of mana but nothing to do with it.

And that didn't change until [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino)s had decreased Głogowski life to 0 either.

Piotr Głogowski 1-1 Magnus Lantto

# Game 3

Again, Lantto had [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) and decided to pick Głogowski's only real business spell in [Summoner's Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoner%27s+Pact). By turn three, Głogowski had access to six mana and again nothing to spend it on.
