After many rounds of Swiss and some great games of Magic, the Top 8 of Grand Prix Portland is set! Learn more about Portland's Top 8 competitors, including their previous accomplishments and what a win today would mean to them, below.

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppor16/t8_botelho.jpg)Name:** Chris Botelho

**Age:** 23

**Hometown:** Vancouver, Washington

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Top 8'ed a Game Day.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Grixis Kitty Pact/Cat Pact, because it's what the world needed but didn't know it should want.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

Real Answer: [Demonic Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonic+Pact)

Fun Answer: [Dark Petition](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Petition), because it's always the best card

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

Whatever counterspell was appropriate for the deck at hand. Alternatively, [Infinite Obliteration](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Infinite+Obliteration). Pish posh on Emrakul.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you? And what would it mean if you won?**

I'm almost too shell-shocked to say. Getting onto the Pro Tour is already unbelievable. Winning a GP would be an utterly unreal dream come true. I'd never even considered it before right now.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppor16/t8_oliver.jpg)Name:** Oliver Tomajko

**Age:** 1738/115.867 \[15\]

**Hometown:** Croton-on-Hudson

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

-Won an SCG Open

-Was adopted by Mario Martinez after Evan Petre disowned me

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Temur Emerge because it's great and all the other decks suck, and my cheerleader, Mike Soles, enjoys value.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Elder Deep-Fiend](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elder+Deep-Fiend), because it won't eat my cookie dough ice cream, unlike Emrakul. #NoTrophiesTeam

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Reality Smasher](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reality+Smasher), it allows me to kill scary Planeswalkers. #TeamFYP

**What does this Top 8 mean to you? And what would it mean if you won?**

It means that I get to go to the next PT and also that my brother, Gabriel, doesn't think I suck.

Bant Company, trying to get back to just playing the best deck.

Helps wash the bad taste of Sydney out of my mouth. If I won it would be gone completely.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppor16/t8_mick.jpg)Name:** Max Mick

**Age:** 14

**Hometown:** San Diego, California

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Won 2 PPTQs???

Otherwise none. Only playing competitively for a year.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Bant Company, because it's the only deck I know how to play well and I felt comfortable with the mirrors, which is the deck I expected to see the most.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Tamiyo, Field Researcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tamiyo%2C+Field+Researcher), because it improves some of your worst match-ups and crushes the mirror.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Tragic Arrogance](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tragic+Arrogance), because again, it crushes the mirror and helps somewhat with [Ishkanah, Grafwidow](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ishkanah%2C+Grafwidow).

**What does this Top 8 mean to you? And what would it mean if you won?**

It means a ton because it's my first Pro Tour and my first large event win.

This Top 8 means the world. I have been grinding for about two years now and finally going to the Pro Tour feels amazing.
