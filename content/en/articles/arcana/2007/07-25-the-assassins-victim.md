Time *Spiral's* Assassinate card was obviously modeled and named after the ability of Royal Assassin—destroying a tapped creature.

Now with the two cards both in *Tenth Edition* together, you can see how close the connection was. The victim in the art of Assassinate was designed to represent a victim of Mark Zug's *Eighth Edition* Royal Assassin in particular.

Note how, in Assassinate's art, the black ichor dripping from the victim's mouth and leaking into his eye socket resembles Royal Assassin's identity-obscuring makeup. And the dagger stabbed into the victim's gut is the same as the one that Royal Assassin is drawing from under his cloak.
