Kuldotha Forgemaster is a surprising hit that has seen play in Standard and in Legacy. Edwin Garcia recently used the five-cost artifact at the StarCityGames.com Standard Open in Los Angeles. What can the Forgemaster forge up in Edwin's deck? All manner of good stuff. Blightsteel Colossus is a massive whooping stick that no doubt is the principal cause of victories for the deck. Mindslaver is a blast from the past that lets you take your opponent's turn. Finally, in a pinch Myr Battlesphere and Wurmcoil Engine will clutter the battlefield with blockers and gain you life. Read more on the StarCityGames.com Open in the [event coverage](http://www.starcitygames.com/php/news/expandnews.php?Article=21462).
