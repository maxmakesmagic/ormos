Join the *Avacyn Restored* League and experience the newest **Magic: The Gathering** set in an exciting and dynamic format!

The top finishers at the end of the league will get a cool poster!

### Where Can I Join on May 4, 2012?

*Avacyn Restored* Leagues are happening at local stores all over the world! Check out our [tournament locator](https://web.archive.org/web/20120625050822/http://ww2.wizards.com/storeandeventlocator/) to find an *Avacyn Restored* League near you. Contact your local store to confirm the event venue, start time, and date.

### What Happens There?

When you join the *Avacyn Restored* League, you will be given the opportunity to play with the newest cards in an ongoing, four-week league. In addition to prizes, players may choose to use special rules for the league that change weekly with the Avacyn's Angel Flights. 

The suggested format for the *Avacyn Restored* League is **Sealed Deck**. In Sealed Deck, each player receives six *Avacyn Restored* booster packs to use for building a deck. Please check with your local store to confirm the league format.

There are also optional rules players can use at the start of their matches. These rules are determined by the Avacyn's Angel Flights, and they change every week.

**Herons**, Week One: Each player may choose a basic land card from his or her deck and start the game with that card on the battlefield.

**Alabaster**, Week Two: Whenever a creature enters the battlefield, that creature's controller may put an Aura card from his or her hand that could enchant it onto the battlefield attached to that creature.

**Goldnight**, Week Three: If a creature would deal damage to a permanent or player, it deals double that damage to that permanent or player instead.

**Avacyn**, Week Four: Whenever a creature attacks, it becomes indestructible for the rest of the turn.

Over the course of the league, you will be able to add more boosters to your league deck.

Each participant should play five matches per week, for a total of twenty over the course of the league. Players joining the *Avacyn Restored* League late can play extra matches to catch up to the current match cap of five times the current total of weeks of the league.

At the end of the league, there will be cool prize posters for the top finishers. Your local store may offer other prizes as well. Be sure to contact it to confirm the details of its league.

### Are You a Store Owner?

For more information about running an *Avacyn Restored* League, visit the [information page](https://web.archive.org/web/20120625050822/http://www.wizards.com/wpn/events/article.aspx?x=magic_new_set_celebrations) on the WPN site.
