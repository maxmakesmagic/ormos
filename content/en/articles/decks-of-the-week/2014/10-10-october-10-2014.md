Pro Tour *Khans of Tarkir* begins today! Check out coverage here on DailyMTG and streaming live at [Twitch.tv/Magic](http://www.twitch.tv/magic).

Last weekend saw two Grand Prix playing *Khans of Tarkir* Limited on opposite sides of the world—[Shanghai](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-shanghai-2014-coverage-events) and [Orlando](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-orlando-2014-coverage-events). Congratulations to Yu Yin for his victory in Shanghai, and Eugene Hwang for his in Orlando!

If you'd like to join the Grand Prix winners at Pro Tour *Fate Reforged*, be sure [to find a PTQ near you](https://web.archive.org/web/20141010000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/pro-tour-fate-reforged-qualifiers)!

Good luck; have fun!
