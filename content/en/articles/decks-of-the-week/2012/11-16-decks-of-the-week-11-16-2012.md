Last weekend, Jacob Wilson won [Grand Prix Chicago](/en/events/2012/gpchi12/jacob-wilson-is-first-in-the-second-city) in a Modern Jund mirror match. Tracking what happens in recent Grand Prix is a lot of fun, but if you want to prepare for Pro Tour *Gatecrash* you will need to practice both Standard and *Return to Ravnica* Booster Draft. Fortunately for you, the current PTQ season is all about *Return to Ravnica* Limited, so you have plenty of opportunities to practice! Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#gtc). In the meantime, see what other people are putting together in this season's Limited environments with the decklists here. Enjoy!
