Red decks are a constant part of Standard, but Chris Cannon's take on the deck from the StarCityGames.com Standard Open in Charlotte, North Carolina is a bit unique. His "All-In" concept sacrifices many of its own permanents in order to turn them into creatures. Its best opening play is a Mountain, an artifact that can be cast for zero mana, like Mox Opal, then Kuldotha Rebirth. The red *Scars of Mirrodin* sorcery allows him to blow up his Mox for three attackers. Goblin Bushwhacker and Goblin Chieftain allow him to then turn those creatures into a formidably sized army!
