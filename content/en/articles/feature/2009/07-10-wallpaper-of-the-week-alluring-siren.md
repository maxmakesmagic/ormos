### Core Set Survivor!

There are exactly sixteen cards that have been in every core set from *Alpha* to *Tenth Edition*. When ***Magic** 2010* comes out, only *eight* cards will have been in every core set. Over the weeks before the ***Magic** 2010* prerelease, we've been counting them down in this special Arcana sub-feature, giving you an "IN" or "OUT" on one card a day. And it all comes down to this. Two cards remain. [Rod of Ruin](https://web.archive.org/web/20090710000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rod+of+Ruin) and [Scathe Zombies](https://web.archive.org/web/20090710000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scathe+Zombies). One will be in, and one will be out.

Reveal the fate of the final two!
