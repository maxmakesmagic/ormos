The Extended season is winding down, but it's still got a few surprises up its sleeves! Barrett Neigel used this innovative Zoo-like list to earn a spot in the Top 8 of a Pro Tour Qualifier in Calgary. One thing that sets it apart from other Extended decks? Barrett's utilization of Stoneforge Mystic. The tiny Equipment tutor was able to find Basilisk Collar, Sword of Light and Shadow, and Umezawa's Jitte. All are powerful cards in the mirror match and against other specific decks in Extended. And check out a whopping EIGHT Leylines in Barrett's sideboard!
