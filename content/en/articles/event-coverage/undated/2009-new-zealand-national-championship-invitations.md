The **Magic: The Gathering** New Zealand National Championship is an invitation-only event, open to citizens or foreign players resident in New Zealand from Jan 1st 2009. Invitations are awarded based on the following criteria:

*   2008 New Zealand Worlds Team

*   Daniel Bretherton (New Zealand Champion)
*   Matthew Ogier
*   Digby Carter

*   The Alternate member of the 2008 New Zealand National Team

*   Gene Brumby

*   Top eight (8) finishers from the Regional Qualifying Tournaments in Auckland, Christchurch, Hamilton and Wellington. Regional tournaments take place on May 16 or 17, 2009 as follows:
