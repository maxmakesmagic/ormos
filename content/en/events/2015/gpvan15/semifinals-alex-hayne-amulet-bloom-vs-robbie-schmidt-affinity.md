A classic showdown between two of the format’s most explosive decks. On one side, Robbie Schmidt with Affinity, who cruised to a 9-0 Day 1 and never looked back. On the other, Canadian national treasure Alex Hayne with Amulet Bloom.

**The Games**

Hayne shipped back his first hand, but was happy with his second. Schmidt led off with [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter), [Inkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inkmoth+Nexus) and [Signal Pest](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Signal+Pest). Hayne had the all-important [Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor) off [Gemstone Mine](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gemstone+Mine). Schmidt played a second Nexus, animated his first and hit with his team for a mixed bag of two poison and one damage.

Hayne untapped and played [Boros Garrison](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Garrison), getting green from his [Gemstone Mine](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gemstone+Mine) before bouncing it, and cast Asuza, Lost But Seeking. He replayed his Mine and played a Gemstone Cavern on Giant. Schmidt played [Darksteel Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Citadel) and hit for two more poison. After combat he added a second Pest to his board. One more mana meant he could bring a lethal attack on the following turn.

Hayne drew [Tolaria West](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tolaria+West) and went into the tank. He played it and tapped it for blue, then played [Vesuva](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vesuva) as a second [Boros Garrison](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Garrison) and returned it to his hand to transmute it for [Summoner's Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoner%27s+Pact). The problem was that doing so meant he could have at most one green left, so the Titan would have to wait a turn. Without a green bounce land he couldn’t [Summer Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summer+Bloom) into additional green either. The downside of this line is that with one more mana, Schmidt kills him the following turn.

Which is exactly what happened.

Schmidt 1 – Hayne 0

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpvan15/gpvan_sfschmidt.jpg)

This time Schmidt had to send back his seven. He stayed on six, and they were off.

Hayne led with [Ancient Stirrings](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Stirrings), netting himself a [Simic Growth Chamber](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simic+Growth+Chamber). Naturally, Schmidt’s turn was more explosive. [Memnit](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memnit)e, [Blinkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinkmoth+Nexus), and two [Springleaf Drum](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Springleaf+Drum)s spilled onto the board. Hayne untapped and played a second Stirrings. This one served up [Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor). [Radiant Fountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Radiant+Fountain) got it into play immediately.

Schmidt speeded up his clock with [Ensoul Artifact](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ensoul+Artifact) on one of his Drums and bashed in for five, leaving Hayne at 17. There was more good news for Schmidt on Hayne’s turn: He didn’t have a way to get extra land plays. No Asuza, no [Summer Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summer+Bloom). He simply played [Gruul Turf](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gruul+Turf) to pick up his Fountain and passed. Schmidt animated his Blinkmoth and hit for six more.

Hayne found himself a third Stirrings. He replayed his Fountain and cast it. This one got him [Tolaria West](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tolaria+West), but without blue mana he would have to wait to transmute it. Schmidt tapped out to get himself a [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge) and a [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating) and knocked Hayne down to eight.

Hayne’s deck served up some hope in the form of [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan), and Hayne went through the possibilities. He settled on playing [Simic Growth Chamber](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simic+Growth+Chamber) and the Titan, then getting [Boros Garrison](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Garrison) and [Slayers' Stronghold](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slayers%27+Stronghold) to attack immediately, which got him two more lands: [Khalni Garden](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Khalni+Garden) and [Ghost Quarter](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Quarter).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpvan15/gpvan_sfhayne.jpg)

The problem with these defenses is that any one-mana artifact would let Schmidt present a flying lethal attacker with [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge). Hayne did his best to keep a stone face when Schmidt played a pre-combat [Signal Pest](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Signal+Pest). They went through the motions, Schmidt activating one of his Nexuses for the seventh artifact, Hayne [Ghost Quarter](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Quarter)ing, and Schmidt activating the other. Hayne extended the hand and wished him good luck in the finals.

Robbie Schmidt defeats Alex Hayne 2-0
