Grand Prix Singapore has come and gone! Brazilian superstar Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa took the title going back-to-back in making two straight Grand Prix Top 8s. The feat is an impressive one considering it was done in two separate formats, but Owen Turtenwald also managed to achive the same feat with Paulo. Read full coverage of the event from the official coverage archives [here](/en/events/2011/gpsin11/da-rosa-reigns-supreme-in-singapore).
