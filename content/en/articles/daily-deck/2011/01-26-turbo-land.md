The first professional event of 2011 was held this past weekend in Atlanta, Georgia and Jason Ford was crowned champion! He used an innovative take on the Prismatic Omen / Scapeshift deck that has recently taken the state of Minnesota by storm. After his friend won the local Pro Tour Qualifier with the deck, Jason and his teammates tweaked it for the Grand Prix. In addition to taking the title, the group also finished Day 1 with an astounding combined record of 33-3-0! Read full coverage of Grand Prix Atlanta [here](/en/events/2011/gpatl11/jason-is-built-ford-tough-in-atlanta).
