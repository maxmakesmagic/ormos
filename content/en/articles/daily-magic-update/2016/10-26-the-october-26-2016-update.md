# Deck of the Day

1.  [Julian Wildes' Four-Color Aggro—Grand Prix Providence 10th Place](https://web.archive.org/web/20170414222341/http://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/gppro16/9-64-decklists-2016-10-23)

What better time to highlight this deck than when we're talking about four-color *Commander* decks?

Julian Wildes nearly crashed the Top 8 of Grand Prix Providence with this four-color aggressive deck that decided it *could* have it all. Where else can you find [Voltaic Brawler](https://web.archive.org/web/20161026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voltaic+Brawler) and [Spell Queller](https://web.archive.org/web/20161026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Queller) fighting side-by-side? Where else will [Woodland Wanderer](https://web.archive.org/web/20161026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Woodland+Wanderer) finally get its due? And how in the world do you make the mana work?

It turns out the key is another resource—energy. [Attune with Aether](https://web.archive.org/web/20161026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Attune+with+Aether), [Servant of the Conduit](https://web.archive.org/web/20161026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Servant+of+the+Conduit), and [Aether Hub](https://web.archive.org/web/20161026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Hub) are key in letting you cast all of your spells, and when your mana is all set up, you can then use the excess energy on [Longtusk Cub](https://web.archive.org/web/20161026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Longtusk+Cub), [Voltaic Brawler](https://web.archive.org/web/20161026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voltaic+Brawler), [Whirler Virtuoso](https://web.archive.org/web/20161026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whirler+Virtuoso), and [Harnessed Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20161026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Harnessed+Lightning)! It's as if he was playing Red-Green Energy and noticed all of the extra, untapped sources of other colors of mana he had available, and decided to just run with it. And he ran all the way to 10th place.
