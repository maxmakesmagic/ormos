Today, I’m super excited to announce a new project from Wizards of the Coast’s digital studio: **Magic Online** Buddy featuring everybody's new **Magic** friend: Planey!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1d/82/1d825968dacdf1e1795217593f705ef45de13f0b953d6daad6ec2545b805992f.jpg)

With the work being done on the **Magic Online** Wide Beta, it was suggested that as a way to help players get better at **Magic** we could utilize groundbreaking technology creating a helpful avatar.

Let’s say you’re drafting *Gatecrash* and aren’t sure what to pick when faced with a pack:

Click the new **Magic Online** Buddy icon and up pops “Timmy” the helpful **Magic** helper.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/92/8c/928c2809998879629533ce1f6b5ddbfb6eaa2139794022e47734c27159be8b0c.jpg)

Or depending on what the our advanced scenario analyzer turns up, it might see an opportunity for a new adventure in **Magic** and thus offer up:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1e/37/1e37a4dae279d344d60c2122a1c01295846611c019099832fd41a9f0559a24dc.jpg)

If perhaps the helpful persona doesn’t actually help you, we’re working on programming three other personalities into **Magic Online** Buddy:

“Spike” will be keyed toward helping players become more competitive. We've trained its AI engine using millions of lines of **Magic Online** chat logs, it will speak using the gamer jargon they are already familiar with:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/26/8b/268b834d0435f5eb3b0cb34e1356a23a47083a063d9f6fcd056677e1375e7a18.jpg)

Or after a particular trouncing it can turn to you and present the obvious thing that went wrong during that game:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ef/8e/ef8edbbaef8510e7b7999b769e7dcb6db8e77d248bcc848248f8a78ab7c3763c.jpg)

We have a lot more planned for **Magic Online** Buddy including more personas such as: Johnny, Vorthos, Melvin, MaRo, Dr. Richard Garfield, Garvas Elscott, Fblthp and more!

We hope you're as excited as we are about this new technology for the **Magic Online** client!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1c/f1/1cf1d082c82b5962951b3aeb0410b0bc86315bdb44774ef79bf1cb06c0296514.gif)
