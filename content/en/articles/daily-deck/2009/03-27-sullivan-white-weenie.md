Adrian Sullivan is a well known deck innovator and **Magic** writer. His latest creation? This innovative White-Green aggro deck, just in time for the Pro Tour Qualifier-Honolulu season! It features some standard players like Wild Nacatl and Tarmogoyf alongside some more surprising choices. O-Naginata? Lightning Greaves? The Steelshaper's Gift to fetch up the equipment on a whim? If you're looking for something truly innovative this Extended season, this deck is for you.
