**Grand Prix Yokohama 2010 Top 8 Profiles**

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4e/9f/4e9f536cfba4b99ca2ebceb63a40646906a8c161bf77e3578df64ef9be3648fa.jpg)

 Company employee

**Byes**  
 2

 5-1  
**Where do you usually play *Magic*?**  
 I play **Magic** Online a lot, and I also practice at the Amenity Dream store in Nanba.  
**Tell us about the deck you used this weekend.**  
**Deck Name/Type**  
[Hypergenesis](https://web.archive.org/web/20100321000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hypergenesis)  
**Designer**  
 Me  
**Key points**  
 I don’t like [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20100321000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) so I have two [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20100321000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest) s. I also put in an Akroma’s Memorial.  
**Tell us about a memorable match you played this weekend.**  
 I have three stand-out memories. Once, I was going to lose a game if I didn’t draw a land; I got a gorilla instead. Another time, I had to burn a gorilla with Borgardan Hellkite to remove a [Bridge from Below](https://web.archive.org/web/20100321000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bridge+from+Below) . Finally, I was going to lose a game when I didn’t have any blockers, and I top-decked a gorilla. It’s all about the gorillas.  
**What other *Magic* achievements do you have?**  
 PT Kobe 2004 champion, Masters Venice champion, The Finals champion  
**How do you see yourself playing *Magic* after this?**  
 As long as I can keep using angels, dragons, and gorillas, I’ll keep playing.  
**Do you have a Twitter account? How do you use it?**  
 今後登録します。  
 I don’t have one now, but I’m planning on signing up.
