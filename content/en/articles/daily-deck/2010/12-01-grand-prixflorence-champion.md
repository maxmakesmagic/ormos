The last Grand Prix of the 2010 professional season has come and gone! Pierluigi Aceto is its champion as he managed to take down Grand Prix–Florence drafting black-green infect. It is the final event heading into the World Championships in Chiba, Japan, December 9-12. For more coverage of Grand Prix–Florence, visit [here](/en/events/2010/gpflo10/pierluigi-poisons-them-all).
