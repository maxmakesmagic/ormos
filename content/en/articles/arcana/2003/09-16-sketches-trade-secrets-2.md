This week's "Sketches" takes a look at Ron Spears' illustration of Trade Secrets from *Onslaught*. 

### 1\. Art Description

These were the instructions given to the artist:

"Location: Breaker Bay city  
 Action: A refugee wizard and a cephalid sitting across from each other at a table, haggling over various magic items, scrolls, etc." 

### 2\. Sketches

Here is Ron's initial sketch: 

![Trade Secrets sketch](https://i.ormos.uk/35/75/3575dd4a00a215b79540be6a95629f902368e81133f5fa936c8053a6436069e6.jpg)

There is one major problem with the sketch. We can see the requisite magic items strewn about, and haggling is clearly going on. The problem is that one of these guys was supposed to be a cephalid. The art team had few opportunities to represent cephalids in *Onslaught* cards (Callous Oppressor and Cephalid Pathmage were the only Cephalid creatures in all of *Onslaught* block) so they wanted to be sure to include one here. 

### 3\. Final Art

Ron got the message. Behold, the shrugging human on the right becomes a big, slimy cephalid.

![Trade Secrets final art](https://i.ormos.uk/ff/ed/ffedd7608a0ce27b6e0e6fa14ad0fe6581035b15161547c720c3a2a8ff08801c.jpg)

"I'll trade you that blue glass container for your headdress. No? Then how about two cards for you and four for me?"
