### *Legions* Release Event Schedule

* * *

![](https://i.ormos.uk/99/bf/99bfe841bdeecddc79f28656b9af555af734365db361b76283a09da02812049a.jpg)

On March 5, 2003, beginning at 12 midnight PST, *LegionsTM* product will be available at the [***Magic** Online* Store](https://web.archive.org/web/20030206000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com&origin=magic_magiconline_newslgnevents). One hour later, at 1 AM PST, the fun begins...

It's time for the ***Magic** Online Legions* Release Events! These include both Release Tournaments and new draft queues, and run from March 5 at 1 AM PST through March 6 at 10 PM PST.

An avatar.

To be specific, the first avatar digital object available on ***Magic** Online:* Phage the Untouchable.

As a digital object, it will show in your collection and you can trade it to other players. While an avatar digital object is in your collection, you can use it to represent you while playing ***Magic** Online.*

You get one copy for each of the Release Tournaments you play in, but you have to play through at least one round. If you drop before the tournament starts, you won't get an avatar for it.

After the *Legions* Release Tournaments are over, you won't be able to get a Phage avatar unless you trade someone for it.

### How Many Release Tournaments Will There Be?

The *Legions* Release Tournaments run in batches. The first event launches at 1 AM PST. From then on, here's how they work:

Each tournament requires 24 players to launch. As long as the 24-player minimum isn't hit, the tournament continues waiting for other players to join. (After a while, if the tournament doesn't reach 24 players, it may be cleared. *Keep reading for more on this.*) Once 24 players have joined, the system waits to let other players register. As long as players keep registering, the tournament waits to launch.

Once 48 players join a tournament, the system launches that tournament immediately and spawns a new one.

### Eat 'em All, We'll Make More

Whether or not an earlier queue is still spawning tournaments, a new tournament queue will be created every three hours after 1 AM. So, if the earlier tournament doesn't fill (which means that tournament may be cleared), you just have to wait until the next batch starts up. If earlier queues continue to fill, new tournament queues will still spawn every three hours.

The last Release Tournament queue will launch on March 6 at 10 PM PST. We'll leave the queue up and spawning new ones for a while unless players stop joining.

### Side Events

Also starting up at 1 AM on March 5th will be two new draft queues.

The first will be the changeover from *Onslaught-Onslaught-Onslaught* draft to *Onslaught-Onslaught-Legions* draft. This is how the draft queue will run from now on, and prizes come from both sets.

During the Release (March 5 at 1 AM to March 6 at 10 PM), there will be a second new queue available: *Legions-Legions-Legions* draft. This queue is only available until 10 PM on March 6th, and the prizes will be all *Legions* boosters.

Both draft queues will be standard cost: 2 Event Tickets plus product used.

### When *Legions* Comes Marching In

Here are the dates to remember:  
*March 7, 12 midnight PST*  
 All *Onslaught* Block Limited events in the **Premier Events** room will include the *Legions* set.  
 New *Onslaught-Legions-Legions* leagues will be available.

*March 19, 12 midnight PST*  
 Standard, Extended, and *Onslaught* Block Constructed events in the **Premier Events** room will include the *Legions* set.

As soon as the *Legions* set becomes available on ***Magic** Online,* you can play both Constructed and Limited events using *Legions* cards in any of the **Casual Play** rooms.

### For Those Of You Checking Your Calendars...

Yes, March 5 falls on a Wednesday. So, why are we holding the *Legions* Release Events in the middle of the week? Well, there are a couple reasons.

The first is that we don't want to delay these events when *Legions* product becomes available after the March 4 update. There were actually quite a few complaints about the *Onslaught* Release Events getting in the way of other weekend activities. We know you want to start playing sanctioned events with *Legions* cards as soon as possible, and we're not going to make you wait any longer.

The second is that there are more players on ***Magic** Online* in the middle of the week than any other time. With Release Tournaments launching on a rolling basis and open draft queues, you should be able to play any time of the day or night during the entire period of the Release Events.

### Summary

So, join the rest of the ***Magic** Online* community in round-the-clock *Legions* Release Events from March 5 at 1 AM PST through March 6 at 10 PM PST. Play in the Release Tournaments and get the limited-release Phage the Untouchable avatar. Join triple-*Legions* draft queues that are only available during the Release Events. Find out how one *Legions* booster changes *Onslaught* Block drafting.

But most of all, have fun!
