![](https://i.ormos.uk/87/71/8771cf1a153b09bef0796dcf88f22a21750d97d3e82ce1a6c92d35ef589783dd.jpg) The Fat Pack, that chunky little miracle of a **Magic** product, is always chock full of goodies. On top of all the **Magic** cards and storage, the fat pack contains a piece of original art that captures some of the highlights of the set.

Every Fat Pack since *Ninth Edition* has had a mural that helps you understand some of the themes and mechanics of the set—HA. No, who are we kidding? They're pictures of giant monsters... who (in general) just FIGHT! Today we take a look at a few of these murals.

  
  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/68/9a/689aac02d219326002a93152650a11d2775e4b2f84a61e90900bebb629bd68dc.jpg) *Ninth Edition fat pack mural: Serra Angel vs. Hypnotic Specter*  
 *Art by Greg Staples* 

  
  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/15/00/150042b76a0da9eca85e65457dffdc2d56bb652f49680a4658fa77eb94584f86.jpg) *Ravnica: City of Guilds fat pack mural: Savra vs. Razia vs. Szadek vs. Tolsimir*  
 *Art by* *Zoltan Boros & Gabor Szikszai* 

  
  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a2/1c/a21c83d5cf6cafa062815655c3baf1400a22be05d4811847ec68908cfbae8100.jpg) *Guildpact fat pack mural: Niv-Mizzet, Angel of Despair, and Borborygmos*  
 *Art by Parente* 

  
  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/42/bd/42bdb16a55d5cb5a29003ed9bce7ba5307a9b86a4c10c3d9a40d8a9a276c9456.jpg) *Dissension fat pack mural: Rakdos, Augustin IV, and Momir Vig*  
 *Art by* *Zoltan Boros & Gabor Szikszai*
