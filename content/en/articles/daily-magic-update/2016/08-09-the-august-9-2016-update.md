The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on August 9, 2016. Today's Update is brought to you by jet lag. So much jet lag. So if today's Daily *Magic* Update is actually jibberish scrawled on a wall somewhere by a man stammering about "[Distended Mindbender](https://web.archive.org/web/20160809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Distended+Mindbender)," and not a recap of today's best *Magic* content, then you know why.

# Today's Must(s)

1.  [Jund, the Promised End](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/jund-the-promised-end/) | ChannelFireball | Joel Larsson

You're going to see a lot of Pro Tour analysis and reports this week, but ChannelFireball's slate is always worth noting. This is the most recent article about a deck played by one of their writers (and it's an excellent article and deck—Larsson placed in the Top 16), but they have a number of them. Reid Duke wrote about [Red-Green Delirium](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/pantheon-deck-tech-gr-delirium/), Daniel Unwin wrote about [Standard Dredge](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/standard-dredge/), Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa wrote about [Black-Green Delirium](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/bg-delirium-deck-tech/), and Ben Stark provided a [comprehensive Draft guide](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/the-eldritch-moon-draft-guide/). Really, all of them are must reads for anyone who cares about the Pro Tour or the decks you'll be playing against at FNM for the next three months.

# After That, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [MtA Episode 185: Gotta Gotta Get Up to Get Down Under](http://www.magictheamateuring.com/mta-episode-185-gotta-gotta-get-up-to-get-down-under/) | Magic the Amateuring | Maria Bartholdi and Meghan Wolff

Meghan and Maria recap the Pro Tour, talk about the future of Standard'mrakul, talk lore, and also answer reader's questions. That's a lot in one podcast that's just over an hour. How do they do it? My bet is on performance-enhancing cheese.

1.  [Story Time—*EMN* Edition](http://www.gatheringmagic.com/snacktimewithmikeandant-08092016-snack-time-37-story-time-emn-edition/) | Gathering Magic | Mike Linnemann and Ant Tessitore

With story on everyone's mind after [Liliana and Gideon teamed up to take down Emrakul in the finals of a Pro Tour](/en/events/2016/ptemn/finals-19-lukas-blohon-white-black-control-vs-2-owen-turtenwald-temur-emerge), Mike and Ant are right on time with the latest edition of Story Time to talk about *Eldritch Moon* and a certain tentacle monster.

1.  [Podcast 081: Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*, Limited Coverage](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/articles/podcast-081-pro-tour-eldritch-moon-limited-coverage) | MTGGoldfish | SaffronOlive and Chas V

Chas V and SaffronOlive talk, of course, [Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-eldritch-moon). But what's that bit about Limited coverage? Over the weekend, Brian Kibler [started a discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/magicTCG/comments/4wd9z9/brian_kibler_if_wotcs_goal_is_a_compelling/), as he does, about whether or not Limited should be featured on the Pro Tour. Opinions were mixed, but it's likely a discussion you'll see pop up now and again.

1.  [Grumpy Old Men in Australia](http://www.starcitygames.com/article/33439_Grumpy-Old-Men-in-Australia.html) | StarCityGames.com | Shaheen Soorani

This one was intriguing, as Soorani spends the first part of the article singing the praises of a deck his team *didn't* play at the Pro Tour. Considering the team ended up on Black-Green Delirium, one of the best-performing decks of the weekend, that's some high praise worth paying attention to.

1.  [Modern PPTQ Season](http://magic.tcgplayer.com/db/article.asp?ID=13438&writer=Ali+Aintrazi&articledate=8-9-2016) | TCGPlayer | Ali Aintrazi

Lost in all of the Pro Tour hype (and it is some pretty justifiable hype) is the fact that it's Modern PPTQ season. While pros were showing off just how awesome a Pro Tour can be, a ton of people are trying to get there. Aintrazi is here to help show you how you can qualify for an RPTQ this season.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Blue-Red Fevered Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20200805231316/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/ptemn/24-28-point-standard-decklists-2016-08-06)

Pedro Carvalho wins for the most unique deck to have success at Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*, as he took this Blue-Red [Fevered Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20160809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fevered+Visions) deck to a 9-1 record in Standard. The archetype has been around since [Fevered Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20160809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fevered+Visions) was printed, but it got a nice kick in the pants with *Eldritch Moon*. [Collective Defiance](https://web.archive.org/web/20160809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collective+Defiance) and [Incendiary Flow](https://web.archive.org/web/20160809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Incendiary+Flow) were easy additions, improving the burn suite. [Unsubstantiate](https://web.archive.org/web/20160809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unsubstantiate) is a nice tempo play that becomes positively [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20160809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand)-like with [Fevered Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20160809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fevered+Visions) in play, and [Thermo-Alchemist](https://web.archive.org/web/20160809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thermo-Alchemist), that Limited standout, actually does a reasonable job blocking a lot of the format's 2-power creatures while throwing quite a bit of burn the opponent's way.

In just the past few days I've played against this deck a ton on *Magic Online*, so don't be surprised if this under-the radar-winner is the real sleeper hit from the Pro Tour.
