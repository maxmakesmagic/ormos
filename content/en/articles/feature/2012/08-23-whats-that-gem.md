Think about [Gem of Becoming](https://web.archive.org/web/20120823000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gem+of+Becoming).

![Gem of Becoming](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Gem+of+Becoming)

  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/c2/9e/c29ec1cf00bbf8d44c87f907478256bb2577d3e64599bd19f0b07f8821735171.jpg)

[Gem of Becoming](https://web.archive.org/web/20120823000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gem+of+Becoming) | Art by [Jack Wang](https://web.archive.org/web/20120823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Jack+Wang%22%5D)

It's not exactly the thing hanging between the horns of [Nicol Bolas, Planeswalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20120823000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nicol+Bolas%2C+Planeswalker), but it's certainly the same sort of thing. So here's the question: why is it a Gem of *Becoming* exactly? 

![Pools of Becoming](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Pools+of+Becoming)

  

Because it's from the [Pools of Becoming](https://web.archive.org/web/20120823000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pools+of+Becoming) on Nicol Bolas's private meditation plane! The pools have changed a little since we last saw them, but that decaying tower in the lower left of the gem's art looks awfully familiar....
