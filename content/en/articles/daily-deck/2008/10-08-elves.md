Looking for a jumping off point for the upcoming State Championships using the new Standard format? ImACloud offers up an innovative concept straight from the digital realm of **Magic Online**. His concoction, which is almost entirely new Standard ready, powers out a horde of Elf tokens, pumped by cards like Bramblewood Paragon, Imperious Perfect, and Elvish Champion. How does Cloud manage to do it so fast? By giving all Elves a mana ability thanks to Heritage Druid! And if the opponent has a mass removal spell? No worries; a pre-emptive Regal Force will make sure Cloud's hand stays full for post-Wrath good times. It's up to innovative players all over the world to determine which new *Shards of Alara* cards best replace the few rotations from ImACloud's ELVES! list.
