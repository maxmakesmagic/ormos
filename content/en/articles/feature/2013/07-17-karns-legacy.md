I've always liked the idea of colorless Commander decks, and while browsing Commander lists on TCGplayer, I found a [Karn, Silver Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20130717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn%2C+Silver+Golem)[list made by a user named terry\_suave](http://magic.tcgplayer.com/db/deck.asp?deck_id=1139269). In order to harness the power of [Karn, Silver Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20130717000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Karn%252C%2BSilver%2BGolem) as your Commander, you do have to give up colored spells of any kind, which is a big cost. Luckily, that cost is more than made up for by sweetness, as [Karn, Silver Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20130717000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Karn%252C%2BSilver%2BGolem) opens the door for all sorts of cool interactions. 

![Karn, Silver Golem](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Karn%2C+Silver+Golem)

There are clearly too many combinations to list, but here are a few of my favorite ones:

[Rings of Brighthearth](https://web.archive.org/web/20130717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rings+of+Brighthearth) + almost anything = value. There are a ton of ways to go infinite, but my favorite combos are the ones that are explicitly not infinite. It's so much more fun to just make a bunch of [Lux Cannon](https://web.archive.org/web/20130717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lux+Cannon) counters or [Phyrexian Processor](https://web.archive.org/web/20130717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Processor) tokens than to go off and win the game. 

[Temporal Aperture](https://web.archive.org/web/20130717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temporal+Aperture) + Eldrazi ([Ulamog, the Infinite Gyre](https://web.archive.org/web/20130717000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Ulamog%252C%2Bthe%2BInfinite%2BGyre), [Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20130717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=193632), etc.). If you are lucky enough to flip an Eldrazi, you get the full benefit, which is awesome. There are also plenty of other very expensive spells, so even a "miss" might not be. 

[Darksteel Forge](https://web.archive.org/web/20130717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Forge) + [Nevinyrral's Disk](https://web.archive.org/web/20130717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nevinyrral%27s+Disk). This may not be the most amusing for your opponents, but getting to blow up the world every turn is powerful indeed. 

[Karn Liberated](https://web.archive.org/web/20130717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn+Liberated) + seven mana. You may not know this, but [Karn Liberated](https://web.archive.org/web/20130717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn+Liberated) is one of my favorite cards, and just casting it is enough to make me happy. 

Past the combos, this deck has enough huge monsters and powerful cards that I know I'd have a blast playing it. Accelerating into [Myr Battlesphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20130717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Battlesphere) or [Steel Hellkite](https://web.archive.org/web/20130717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steel+Hellkite) sounds awesome, and as long as you stay away from hitting the infinite combo space too hard, your opponents shouldn't feel it too necessary to kill you first.
