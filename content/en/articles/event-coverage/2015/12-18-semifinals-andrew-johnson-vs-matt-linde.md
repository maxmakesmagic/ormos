![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpatl01/866.jpg)*Andrew Johnson vs. Matt Linde*Both Andrew Johnson and Matt Linde had gotten through their quarterfinal match in the same way. They both bashed their opponents with fast red/green decks. Andrew Johnson had just dispatched Alex Borteh and Linde had just beaten Antonio DeRosa. 

### Game One

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpatl01/867.jpg)*Andrew Johnson*Johnson won the roll and opted to go first. He started out with a turn two [Mad Dog](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mad+Dog). His third turn he missed a land dropped, but [Reckless Charge](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reckless+Charge)d his Mad Dog to get in some quick damage. Linde dropped a [Barbarian Lunatic](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barbarian+Lunatic) and traded with the Dog. When he tried the trick again though with a second Lunatic and Johnson's [Krosan Avenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Krosan+Avenger), Johnson [Sylvan Might](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Might)ed and kept his Avenger around. Linde tried to stop the beats with a [Rabid Elephant](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rabid+Elephant). Johnson offered up a trade with his Reckless Charged Chatter token, but Linde refused. Linde played more creatures and stalled up the ground, but Johson summoned a forestwalking [Leaf Dancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leaf+Dancer). Johnson played the Might again on his Dancer knocking Linde to three and showed Linde he was holding a [Scorching Missile](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scorching+Missile) which caused Linde to concede.

### Game Two

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpatl01/868.jpg)*Matt Linde*Johnson went second, but he still had the first play with a turn two [Werebear](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Werebear). Linde played a Lunatic and a [Chainflinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chainflinger) while Johnson got out an [Ember Beast](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ember+Beast). Linde tried calling Johnson's bluff by blocking the Werebear with his Chainflinger, but it was no bluff and Johnson played Sylvan Might. Linde sacked his Lunatic to kill the Werebear and keep the Ember Beast from attacking, but this only succeeded temporarily because Johnson play [Chatter of the Squirrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chatter+of+the+Squirrel)s twice and got two 1/1s. Linde played a [Bomb Squad](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bomb+Squad) hoping to deal with the Ember Beast in a turn or two, but he didn't realize there was a loophole to put an extra fuse counter on creatures during the upkeep. The Beast survived to do the extra damage Johnson needed to set up a game winning Demoralize.

Johnson wins 2-0
