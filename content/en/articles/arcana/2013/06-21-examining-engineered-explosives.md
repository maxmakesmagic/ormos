There were a number of new art pieces on cards in *Modern Masters*, among them was a rare from *Fifth Dawn* called Engineered Explosives.

Even though the new card is in *Modern Masters*, the art remained set on the plane of *Mirrodin*. If you look carefully in the art you can see the hexagonal metalic plates which make up the ground on *Mirrodin*.

 ![Engineered Explosives](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Engineered%20Explosives&options=) 

  

Additionally, while it is obvious you are observing some sort of magical explosion, it may be less obvious that the things being thrown by the explosion are [Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20130621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?name=+[myr]), who are now mid-flight as they flung by the explosive force.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/arc1260_engineered.jpg)
