**Magic Online** continues to welcome *Innistrad* to Standard with some innovative takes on what the new cards can do. BDeCandio7 used a blue-red-green shell with [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20111019000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) to rebuy spells and the team-up of [Daybreak Ranger](https://web.archive.org/web/20111019000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daybreak+Ranger) / [Nightfall Predator](https://web.archive.org/web/20111019000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightfall+Predator) and [Mayor of Avabruck](https://web.archive.org/web/20111019000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mayor+of+Avabruck) / [Howlpack Alpha](https://web.archive.org/web/20111019000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Howlpack+Alpha) to punish slow opponents.
