![](https://i.ormos.uk/42/b1/42b14d367bc45a7f01fc5f255f937bab4aa3825bcdb01613e67a3e06aa1f29b1.jpg)  

Welcome to beautiful Nagoya, Japan. ***Magic** 2012* all-stars Sorin, Jace, Gideon, Garruk and Chandra are watching over you!

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/D1Photo_02.jpg)  

Even in the early morning, downtown Nagoya is equal parts hustle and bustle.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/17/8d/178d90576a8885e62206fb1e59821284d2fba3df14ccf467f5f7854eb7471a66.jpg)  

Last minute memorization of important phrases, such as "All glory to the Father of Machines!"

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d2/d5/d2d599f5f2a0ef03b8a1cf116afdaff0960c15b1904cbb5b6bebfdc6bdee6775.jpg)  

The countryside is steeped in beauty, history, and stairs.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/D1Photo_05.jpg)  

Pointing the way to Pro Tour greatness, and deliciousness!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0e/dd/0edd081460f36d9b8f69a54e2f274777c81f3094d4f635fc8d46ee896bb560dc.jpg)  

Your diligent judges, who keep things running smoothly.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/33/e3/33e3ce3fe3fbc7a599297fccc3ae544baa8ce8f2dacd0c89e12a3cf18aaca266.jpg)  

Veteran Nate Price takes cub reporter Steve Sadin under his wing.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/44/47/4447b50a5e626b679e3709e3de8919a76eb50965e4b2b09fc4f7f1f671e61ed8.jpg)  

Spellslinging this weekend: R&D's Ken Nagle, Tom LaPille, and Dave Guskin, as well as jack-of-all-trades event staffer Matt Danner. Three parts wacky sitcom, one part slasher flick.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6d/2c/6d2c07226f63565b3fbb17f9fc6c9d13488187f2fb6fa8e98e9c401275cabe8b.jpg)  

This guy locked Tom LaPille up good with the retro combo of [Sands of Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20110610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sands+of+Time) and [Equipoise](https://web.archive.org/web/20110610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Equipoise). 

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/D1Photo_10.jpg)  

Guest Artist Steve Belledin shows off [Ichor Wellspring](https://web.archive.org/web/20110610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichor+Wellspring), a popular card this weekend.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5a/f4/5af49578746f28b32bbf99783a486198a64f95ec8ec7eaff35fdcd3efea1abbd.jpg)  

Also in attendance, Daarken, posed here with [Kulrath Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20110610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kulrath+Knight).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f7/4a/f74a7aa4c3015b9f01e14735400621ef696a4a37fe7c18eddc54b20bc9f93778.jpg)  

The great Ookubo, master of three-dimensional card art.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6c/91/6c91fc28b402c33d6f877adb9f6c77a7a4036350d1affa93750eb596f28f18a1.jpg)  

Just a few of his many masterpieces.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/D1Photo_14.jpg)  

[Chandra Nalaar](https://web.archive.org/web/20110610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra+Nalaar) never looked so good.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/63/94/63941ae949a5fd3a99ed0964523386017c275d2f48216403443a4f8f5dafa565.jpg)  

For Round 6, things switch to draft, and the 5-0's duke it out.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a7/cc/a7ccabea77b15c33744530b2bbc1715bf739fddd0be8fe3eb7e5d51d6fb046c3.jpg)  

Sometimes judge, sometimes competitor, but always stylish.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1e/ee/1eeea4bc0e9ad5e1f2ab206b59226fde9cfb6b08b26adf8b5a69d8a05dbd6a14.jpg)  

The Pro Tour is full of highs...

![](https://i.ormos.uk/48/64/486438141f6d91bde3f0dc436c52ec474dcf346e3edd6c0941658d2268817b29.jpg)  

...lows...

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1c/30/1c3035ab417a1c58b4f3d814a20fd53c31ff4d021365f8809c1ebc200be451d9.jpg)  

...and, of course, pancake soda.
