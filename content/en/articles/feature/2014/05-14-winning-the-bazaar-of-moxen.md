Recently, Eternal players in all of Europe flocked to Annecy, France, for a weekend full of Modern, Legacy, and Vintage tournaments called the [Bazaar of Moxen](http://www.bazaar-of-moxen.com/en/bazaar-of-moxen-coverage-bom9,24.html). The prizes for these tournaments are some of the most sought-after cards in the game. Coming out on top of a field of 214 players at the latest edition of the Bazaar of Moxen, Gwen De Schamphlaere took home one of each of the five original Moxes! 

![331](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&multiverseid=331)

Gwen's deck is one of the classic Vintage decks—a [Time Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Time+Vault) deck featuring [Tezzeret the Seeker](https://web.archive.org/web/20140514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tezzeret+the+Seeker) to both find [Time Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Time+Vault) and untap it to take all of the turns. [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) seems incredible with [Time Walk](https://web.archive.org/web/20140514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Time+Walk) and [Ancestral Recall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Recall), while [Mental Misstep](https://web.archive.org/web/20140514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mental+Misstep) makes an appearance in one of the only formats where it is legal. The sideboard features an [Oath of Druids](https://web.archive.org/web/20140514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oath+of+Druids) + [Forbidden Orchard](https://web.archive.org/web/20140514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forbidden+Orchard) package. The typical game plan with Oath involves hitting [Rune-Scarred Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rune-Scarred+Demon) and finding [Yawgmoth's Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20140514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yawgmoth%27s+Will) with the Demon, then recasting all of the cards in your graveyard because of the [Oath of Druids](https://web.archive.org/web/20140514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oath+of+Druids). 

Starting June 16, you can play Vintage and open pieces of the [Power Nine](/en/articles/arcana/2003/10-15-the-power-nine-2) on [**Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20140514000000/https://accounts.onlinegaming.wizards.com/)! *Vintage Masters* will bring the Vintage format to **Magic Online** for the very first time! Having worked on the set personally, I am really excited to see what happens with online Vintage.
