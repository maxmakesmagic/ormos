![](https://i.ormos.uk/01/21/012192627746e10e208dd7f611c1352c767bf012d1bf34a8ddf1bae8d1071267.jpg)

He did it the hard way.

From Grinder entrant to National Champion, that's the story of Guillaume Cardin. On Thursday, Cardin could be found scrabbling for a spot in a frantic attempt to earn one of the final places in the tournament come Friday morning. He made it, and once there, he also made it count. In fact, Cardin was nothing short of masterful when playing Constructed on the weekend, losing only a single match with his [Heartbeat of Spring](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heartbeat+of+Spring) deck from Thursday through Sunday. Combined with his solid draft performance, that was enough to see him through to a Sunday appearance, where he beat Zoo, Hand in Hand, and then Snakes to secure the honor of carrying the Canadian flag at the Louvre.

Joining Cardin on the National Team is relative unknown Mike Vasovski, and 2003 Canadian Nats runner-up Nathon Braymore, who lost a single contested match in any format on the weekend. The Top 8 was diverse in archetypes, featuring two Black/White aggro decks plus, Tron, Vore, White Weenie/red, Snakes, Zoo, but in the end it was Cardin and [Heartbeat of Spring](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heartbeat+of+Spring) that took home the title. Congratulations to Guillaume Cardin, the 2006 Canadian National Champion!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c5/fb/c5fb3b073eb501115d77123b9fd140940aa0af7bdb71ea5a8be4fd8f134e9bc0.jpg)*Nick Page*

If you haven't been following this weekend, let me bring you up to speed. Nathon Braymore has attained a record of *10-0* in played matches. He's hoping to make his second national team and so far he's on track with nary a loss to his name. Nick Page hasn't had quite the same success, but still accomplished an impressive 9-3 record in the swiss and even 3-0'd his *Coldsnap* pod with a triple [Icefall](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Icefall) deck. He'll need to be fortunate, though, as both players had come to the conclusion that this was the worst matchup he could hope for. We'll see how it plays out and who gets to go to Paris to fight for Canada on the worldwide stage.

### Game 1

Nathon won the die roll and started off with a mulligan, but kept his next hand quickly and Page followed suit. An [Orzhov Basilica](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Orzhov+Basilica) from Braymore on turn 2 was not met by an [Eye of Nowhere](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eye+of+Nowhere), but rather a [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) was played to stop a [Phyrexian Arena](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Arena). [Stone Rain](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stone+Rain) hit the Basilica but Nathon untapped and played a [Plains](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plains) and a [Castigate](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Castigate), showing [Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm), [Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm), [Compulsive Research](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsive+Research), [Magnivore](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magnivore), [Shivan Reef](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Reef). All Braymore had at this point was an Isamaru, and one of the [Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm)s took it down. However, his draw step was kind to him, as Paladin En-Vec emerged and met with the [Umezawa's Jitte](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Umezawa%27s+Jitte) that lurked in his hand. Page could only grimace and compliment his opponent on his topdeck while land after land came off the top of his deck. The Paladin with the knife would not be denied and charged in twice to pile on the damage, dropping Page to 7. A [Sleight of Hand](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sleight+of+Hand) into a [Demolish](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demolish) offered a short reprieve for the [Magnivore](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magnivore) player, but Braymore's luck had not run out. Another Jitte came off the top, and even though Page had a [Keiga, the Tide Star](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Keiga%2C+the+Tide+Star) to stop the beatings, [Eight-and-a-Half-Tails](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eight-and-a-Half-Tails) made an appearance and set Paladin in for lethal.

A snake token picked up the Jitte now, forcing Samms to reveal his third [Condemn](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Condemn) of the game, letting [Court Hussar](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Court+Hussar) eat [Coiling Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Coiling+Oracle), as Samms dropped to 19. Duncan once again put his hand on top of his deck without delay. His second hand was much more acceptable to him, and Guillaume once again was pleased with his hand. The [Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top) and [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) once again entered play on the first two turns, and all McGregor could muster was a turn 3 [Umezawa's Jitte](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Umezawa%27s+Jitte). [Carven Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Carven+Caryatid) came down for Cardin, but Duncan's rejoinder was the powerful [Mindslicer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindslicer).

Guillaume was dismayed at the creature but had another Elder to get some lands and stop some damage. He blocked the [Mindslicer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindslicer) bearing the Jitte, worked his Top, and [Kodama's Reach](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kodama%27s+Reach)ed on his own turn, bringing him up to eight lands in play. Cardin drew his card and entered into deep thought. He played out a [Heartbeat of Spring](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heartbeat+of+Spring) and looked with his Top. All he could muster was another Top and another Elder, though, and Duncan untapped quickly as he calculated what all his newfound mana would enable him to do. The Elder died to a poke from the Jitte, and when [Mindslicer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindslicer) entered the attack step, an [Okiba-Gang Shinobi](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Okiba-Gang+Shinobi) jumped out and assaulted Guillaume's hand, which at the moment held only two cards. A [Mortify](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mortify) tried to hit a Heartbeat, but it was Muddled, leaving Guillaume with no cards in hand but with the Heartbeat in play. [Weird Harvest](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Weird+Harvest).

Guillaume Cardin wins the match 3-1 and advances to the finals. Duncan drops down to the playoff to see who gets to be on the team in an Orzhovan mirror match.

Duncan McGregor is on a tear this season, having played in every Pro Tour thus far, and finishing 9th at Grand Prix-Toronto just a short while ago. With his improbable victory over Jason Patterson in the quarterfinals, he also guaranteed himself a trip to Worlds, though he'll need to win one more match to play on the Canadian National Team. Sitting across from him is the guy who has steamrolled the tournament, Nathan Braymore. A 2003 National Team member, Braymore was undefeated in contested matches this weekend until he fell to Mike Zakovski's deluge of Snakes in the semifinals. The deck matchup here is a Black/White aggro mirror match, though the archetypes differ slightly. Braymore will be relying on his Hand in Hand deck to pull him through, while McGregor picked Ghost Husk as his weapon of choice this weekend.

McGregor mulliganned to six in game 1 and then kept a one-[Plains](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plains), all-Black hand for his second set. He drew a [Shizo, Death's Storehouse](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shizo%2C+Death%27s+Storehouse) on turn 3, but it already happened to be in play on Braymore's side of the board. That is what we in the business like to call a "bad beat." Death followed hard upon.

Braymore 1 - McGregor 0

McGregor kept his seven (for once - he'd mulliganned in his last 3 games prior to this) and cast a turn 1 [Isamaru, Hound of Konda](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Isamaru%2C+Hound+of+Konda) followed by [Umezawa's Jitte](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Umezawa%27s+Jitte). A second Confidant from Braymore died to a Jitte counter, but Nathon then played a [Paladin en-Vec](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Paladin+en-Vec) to stymie McGregor's fresh [Nantuko Husk](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Husk), and tossed a Jitte down to get rid of Duncan's. [Promise of Bunrei](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Promise+of+Bunrei) from Duncan and [Descendant of Kiyomaro](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Descendant+of+Kiyomaro) for Braymore were the next two plays, though the creatures on the board were forced to do nothing more than sit and stare at each other menacingly.

[Shining Shoal](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shining+Shoal) made sure that plot failed, redirecting the damage to McGregor's new [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant). [Eight-and-a-Half-Tails](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eight-and-a-Half-Tails) provided Duncan an opening to attack, but [Last Gasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Gasp) made certain that only worked once. Braymore had taken care of McGregor's first [Umezawa's Jitte](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Umezawa%27s+Jitte), but the second one plus colorless tokens did him in, evening the match at a game a piece.

Braymore 1 - McGregor 1

Game 3 got off to a fast start for both players, with [Last Gasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Gasp) and Mortifies flying everywhere, making certain to keep cards like 8.5 Tails from having a major impact on the game. Another [Last Gasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Gasp) from Braymore killed [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant) while [Manriki-Gusari](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Manriki-Gusari) prevented any Jitte shenanigans on McGregor's part. Braymore 2 - McGregor 1

Braymore opened game 4 with Isamaru, which McGregor answered with [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant), only to see it die to [Last Gasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Gasp). It was quickly turn 4, and the board was already filled with [Hand of Honor](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hand+of+Honor), a pair of Paladins en-Vec, [Nantuko Husk](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Husk) and the puppy. [Promise of Bunrei](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Promise+of+Bunrei) gave McGregor some blockers, but not before he lost both his Rusalka and his Husk. Braymore 3 - McGregor 1

Guillaume wins the die roll and chose to play. Both players kept their hands, and Cardin had the iconic turn one [Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top) to match the similarly iconic turn one [Sakura-Tribe Scout](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sakura-Tribe+Scout) from Vasovski. As the Heartbeat player has been known to do, he worked the Top once again, and played a [Kodama's Reach](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kodama%27s+Reach) on untap, bringing him up to six lands. The remaining two of those lands tapped to cast a [Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm), and all Vasovski could manage was an Elder on the following turn and a "Nice rip."

An [Early Harvest](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Early+Harvest) resolved, Top was cashed in to draw a card, and a [Weird Harvest](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Weird+Harvest) fetched two Drifts, a [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sakura-Tribe+Elder), and [Maga, Traitor to Mortals](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Maga%2C+Traitor+to+Mortals). A Drift transmuted for this very purpose and Maga once again came down for lethal.

His next turn was a [Sakura-Tribe Scout](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sakura-Tribe+Scout), and it looked as though the Jitte would finish this game off. Cardin could only muster a hardcast [Drift of Phantasms](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drift+of+Phantasms), and after another draw step he packed up the cards.

Mike Vasovski takes the lead 2-1 in the finals. He declined to resideboard, while Cardin brought in 1 [Weird Harvest](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Weird+Harvest), 1 [Heartbeat of Spring](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heartbeat+of+Spring), 1 [Invoke the Firemind](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Invoke+the+Firemind), and 1 [Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top) for 4 [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand)s.

### Game 4

Once again Cardin had to mulligan, though somehow both players kept their carefree attitudes. Guillaume claimed that he would win on turn three, but one could say that Mike (as well as myself) did not believe him. Guillaume started with a turn one Top and even though it was Needled, a [Naturalize](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Naturalize) was fired out in his mainphase. Vasovski managed a turn 2 [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sakura-Tribe+Elder), and on the following turn he brought in a Ninja. A lighter moment surfaced when he tried to sacrifice Elder in response to the Ninja trigger (which doesn't work as Elder is returned as part of the cost) and Guillaume grinned as he exclaimed "So that's how you made it to the finals!", eliciting a laugh from the bystanders.

His next turn had a [Kodama's Reach](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kodama%27s+Reach), which led into a [Savage Twister](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Twister), clearing the board.

![pithing Needle](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=pithing+Needle)

Cardin: "Tap out! Tap out!"  
 Vasovski: "Only for you."

Guillaume tapped out to draw 4 cards with [Invoke the Firemind](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Invoke+the+Firemind), and after Vasovski's [Coat of Arms](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Coat+of+Arms) left him low on mana once again, though with lethal damage next turn. Cardin untapped and decided he had to go for it.

Cardin: "What do I have to lose?"  
 Spectators: "Canadian Nationals."  
 Cardin: "Very funny."

He entered deep into thought, though, and played a Heartbeat, a Harvest, and then transmuted [Muddle the Mixture](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Muddle+the+Mixture). With the search on the stack, Mike picked up his cards.

The match advances to the fifth and final game-the last game that will be played in the entirety of Canadian Nationals. Neither player resideboarded.

### Game 5

Mike scans his hand and keeps, while Guillaume was finally pleased with his seven as well. Mike's first turn [Breeding Pool](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Breeding+Pool) met a [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest) and a Top from the other side. [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) entered play for the Snakes player, but the following turn simply had an attack and a Growth Chamber-not the start he wanted. Cardin got his turn 3 [Kodama's Reach](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kodama%27s+Reach), to which Vasovski responded with "My hand's bad." The Reach resolved and the following turn had a land drop (a [Ghost Quarter](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Quarter)) and nothing else. Another Reach resolved and it would take some serious work for Mike to catch up.

An end-of-turn [Chord of Calling](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chord+of+Calling) for two fetched a [Coiling Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Coiling+Oracle), which revealed a [Sakura-Tribe Scout](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sakura-Tribe+Scout). A second [Ghost Quarter](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Quarter) came down, complicating the Heartbeat Player's plans, but the pressure of the three 1/1s was still not enough for Vasovski to feel confident in his position. Guillaume lapsed deep into thought and decided to play a [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain) and [Savage Twister](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Twister) for one. A [Ghost Quarter](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Quarter) was used during the end step to take out the [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain), and Vasovski tapped down to the second Quarter to lay a [Coat of Arms](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Coat+of+Arms) with no creatures in play. Cardin untapped, played an [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island), and once again lapsed into thought.

Vasovski: "[Kodama's Reach](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kodama%27s+Reach)-you know you want to."  
 Cardin: "You read my mind."

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ef/95/ef9552f05c69be62ee853eeeaefe77d6a571c0b93c47bc1d3799259eb1c8cf5b.jpg)*Guillaume Cardin is the Canada Nationals Champion!*

A Heartbeat entered play and a [Weird Harvest](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Weird+Harvest) for four was cast. Cardin fetched three Drifts and a Maga while Mike got three [Coiling Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Coiling+Oracle)s and a [Sakura-Tribe Scout](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sakura-Tribe+Scout). . All that was done after the Harvest was a look with the Top, though, and Mike played out all three Oracles (revealing Summons, [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak), [Coat of Arms](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Coat+of+Arms)). He had seven cards left in hand, two [Breeding Pool](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Breeding+Pool)s up, and the [Ghost Quarter](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Quarter) ready to act.

Vasovski: "Wouldn't it be amazing if my top card was [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle)?"

Cardin untapped and prepared to take the last turn he would for the entire tournament. He transmuted a Drift for a second Heartbeat and played it, leaving both players with ample amounts of mana. Guillaume played his [Swamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swamp) and tapped it for three black mana, giving him what he would need to win. All it would take is a resolved [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand), though, and Cardin would not be able to win. He tutored for a Harvest and started his procedure of adding tons of mana to his pool as he tried to puzzle out a way to win past two counters and or if he should just move all-in. Top was used, another Drift transmuted… for a Drift, Top was used again, and finally it was cashed in. The last Drift went to get another Harvest, and Guillaume was ready to cast the lethal Maga. Vasovski looked at his hand… tapped a land… and conceded, making Guillaume the Canadian national champion!

The kicker? Vasovski flipped over the top card… [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20160921100129/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle).
