It's all hands on deck to defend against Bolas's incursion into Ravnica, which can only mean one thing—loads of tokens in *War of the Spark*!

If you haven't seen the cards that make these eighteen tokens (and one emblem), head to the [Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20190418000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/card-image-gallery/war-spark) and prepare to be amazed.

Behold, the stalwart defenders and ruthless invaders turning the City of Guilds into a Plane-size battlefield.
