* * *

Blue has a theme of shapeshifting in *Planar Chaos*, as you can see from cards like Erratic Mutation, Aquamorph Entity, and Pongify. Another example is Shaper Parasite. Although it’s actually an Illusion, its power/toughness-modifying abilities are often used to “shapeshift” a creature right into the graveyard.

Like many blue morph creatures, the art of Shaper Parasite depicts a bizarre creature bursting from its spiderlike [morph shell](/en/articles/arcana/2006/10-24-hidden-morph-spiders-2). You may not have noticed, however, that the body of Shaper Parasite is actually *composed* of morph crawler legs. Near its head, the legs become clawlike appendages. Creepy!
