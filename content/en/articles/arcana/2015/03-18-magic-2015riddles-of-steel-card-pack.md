Do you play [*Magic 2015—Duels of the Planeswalkers*](https://web.archive.org/web/20150318000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/game-info/products/duels-of-the-planeswalkers)? Do you like new things? Do you like answering rhetorical questions? Do you like bonus content? Of course you do!

And just for you, starting March 18—today!—you'll receive, as a bonus, the Riddles of Steel card pack. Different systems update at different times, but look for this bonus content in your account soon!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/F15B_swrve.jpg)

So what is this Riddles of Steel card pack? Keep reading!

*Magic 2015* Riddles of Steel Card Pack includes three new ten-card all-foil sets for use with your collection (30 cards in all):

*   "Might and Majesty" Set—Bolster your ranks with this collection of powerful white cards. Raise an army with [Captain of the Watch](https://web.archive.org/web/20150318000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Captain+of+the+Watch) or win in majestic style with [Felidar Sovereign](https://web.archive.org/web/20150318000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Felidar+Sovereign).
*   "Brain Power" Set—Bend the rules to your advantage with this collection of blue cards. Command the seas to rise with [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20150318000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) or use [Laboratory Maniac](https://web.archive.org/web/20150318000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Laboratory+Maniac) to invent a new way to win.
*   "Doomsday Machine" Set—Unleash your unstoppable mechanical creations with this collection of cards that empower your artifacts. [Darksteel Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20150318000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Colossus) towers over other creatures while [Master of Etherium](https://web.archive.org/web/20150318000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Etherium) makes your artifact creatures into an overwhelming clockwork army.

A client update is required for content to appear in your collection.
