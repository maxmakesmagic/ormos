Unified Will and Shared Discovery are cards players are familiar with as remaining in the pack during the latter picks of a draft. But Jonathan Mark Te figured out a better use for the cards. The Grand Prix–Manila Day 2 competitor uses them in a green tokens deck! That means you get Ancestral Recall and Counterspell in Standard! Visit the [official Grand Prix–Manila coverage](/en/events/2010/gpman10/nakada-emerges-unscathed-in-manila) for more.
