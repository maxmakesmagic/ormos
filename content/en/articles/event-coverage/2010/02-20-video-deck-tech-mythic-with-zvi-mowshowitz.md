Pro Tour Hall of Famer and master deck designer Zvi Mowshowitz shows off his creation, a [Finest Hour](https://web.archive.org/web/20100220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Finest+Hour)/[Rafiq of the Many](https://web.archive.org/web/20100220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rafiq+of+the+Many)/[Knight of the Reliquary](https://web.archive.org/web/20100220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+the+Reliquary+) build with tons of mana acceleration topping out with [Baneslayer Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20100220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baneslayer+Angel)s, [Rampaging Baloths](https://web.archive.org/web/20100220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rampaging+Baloths), and a [Thornling](https://web.archive.org/web/20100220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thornling).
