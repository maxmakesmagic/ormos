**How can I earn an invitation to Pro Tour Charleson?** 

The Invitation Policy is under Tournament Rules at [www.thedci.com](http://www.thedci.com/) - section Pro Tour, sub-section Team.

Each team-format Pro Tour invitation list comprises the following:

*   Any team of three players whose member's Pro Player Club levels equal 7 or greater. Teams formed in this manner must be announced at player registration before the tournament.
*   Any team of three players whose member’s DCI Constructed rating average is 2000 or higher (i.e. total 6000+).
*   Any team of three players whose member’s **average** DCI Individual Team Constructed rating is 1700 or higher (i.e. total 5100+).
*   The Top 8 finishing teams of each Grand Prix tournament that feeds the appropriate Pro Tour event.
*   Teams (who were previously uninvited) who earn invitations through appropriate Qualifier tournaments.

Rating date is 3-May-06

The winning team of the qualifier earns the travel award (Please note that travel awards are a regional matter and Hasbro offices outside North America may have different details and policies regarding travel awards than the US office has.) - regardless of who ends up with the invitation after rescinsions. The Travel Award cannot be rescinded, passed-down, optioned for another event, replaced with cash, etc...

Each member of the winning team earns the travel award as an individual. They forfeit the award if they do not book as instructed before 30-days prior, or if they fail to register and play in Pro Tour Charleston.

The team with the invitation from the qualifier earns a team-invite as that team.

**Q: Does this mean that Team 1 comprising of Player A, B and C wins a Team PTQ, all 3 players qualify and receive individual travel awards. Can Player A now play on Team 2 at the Pro Tour because Team 2 made up of Player A, D and E who qualify on Individual Team Rating average?** 

A: Yes

**Q: What then happens to Player B and C from Team 1?** 

A: The team they qualified with (Team 1 - A, B, & C) holds the invitation cannot play. They cannot play unless they qualify by another method with another player.

**Q: Can they add Player F who hasn't qualified because the Player B and Player C have an invite?** 

A: Players B, C, & F do not constitute Team 1 (A, B, & C) that won the invite. If the team meets a different qualification (i.e. rating, pro points, etc...) they could qualify. Even if Player F was a member of Team 3 (with player G & H) he cannot transfer his invite from Team 3 to Team 1.

**A substantial number of the PTQs for this event are occuring *after* the 6week cutoff-this seems unfair.** 

The 6 weeks prior date for ratings invites to a worldwide event was picked to allow for players to have time to find out they are qualified on rating, communicate with others going, and still be able to make travel and hotel arrangements at a reasonable price (which is commonly 28 days from departure date - after which prices tend to rise quickly).

We realize this means not all qualifiers are finished. Qualifier seasons generally run 8 or 10 weeks and generally end 3 weeks prior to the event. If we wait until the end of the QT season then rating players would have 15 days from the Wednesday of the ratings-run before the event started.

A point missed is that ratings invitations are not about the qualifiers entirely (qualifiers have a major effect though as they are 32K), but actually to support all the other sanctioned play that exists. This is why Charleston's ratings date has raised this issue I believe. Most other ratings runs include huge numbers of other, regularly sanctioned event of the type inviting to the Pro Tour (Standard, Extended, Legacy, etc...for a Constructed PT like Honolulu). Team-Constructed has existed for years but few players played this format casually and so few have a rating established. 

We feel the time to arrange travel at a reasonable rate, and the general acknowledgment of inviting the best players in a format from any sanctioned event (not just that qualifier season) is more of a priority when involving a worldwide event such as a Pro Tour.
