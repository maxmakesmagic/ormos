The *Dissension* set showcases **Magic**'s innovation and flexibility in the double cycle of gold split cards. During each split card's creation, it represented a special double challenge for its artist, since each half had its own art description and mood to illustrate. 

How well can you recognize the art of these split cards? To make this exercise more challenging, we're only showing either the right half or left half of five of *Dissension's* split cards. Here's a hint: the guild shown in the art will give you a clue to the colors in the card's mana cost; from there you may be able to guess the split card. *Click each art image to reveal the answer.*
