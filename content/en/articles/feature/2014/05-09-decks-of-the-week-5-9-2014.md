Grand Prix return with a vengeance this weekend, with a Modern tournament at [Grand Prix Minneapolis](https://web.archive.org/web/20140509000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=mtg/event/grandprix/minneapolis14_2) and a *Theros* Block Limited one at [Grand Prix Warsaw](https://web.archive.org/web/20140509000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=mtg/event/grandprix/warsaw14). Speaking of Limited, this season's Pro Tour Qualifiers (PTQs) for Pro Tour ***Magic** 2015* are using *Theros* Block Sealed as the format. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](https://web.archive.org/web/20140509000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtg/event/protour/qualifierlist#m15). Good luck; have fun!
