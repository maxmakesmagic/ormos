Your 2009 **Magic** Game Day has come and gone! Matthew Cerino had a very successful run in Doylestown, Pennsylvania with a new interpretation on Bant. The blue-green-white deck played powerful spells like Rhox War Monk and Baneslayer Angel. Bant Charm and Path to Exile keep creatures off the board, and when you need to refill your hand? Mind Spring is all you need to rebuild!
