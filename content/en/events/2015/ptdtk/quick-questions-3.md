# What's your favorite draft archetype in this format, and what's the best card to open for that?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_wiegersma.jpg)  
**Jelger Wiegersma, Five Pro Tour Top 8s:** Blue-black is the best color combination, and I'm always looking to draft more of a control deck. The best first pick is [Dragonlord Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20150411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar), I guess.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_levy.jpg)  
**Raphaël Lévy, Hall of Famer:** Black-red, with lots of small deathtouch creatures like [Hand of Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20150411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hand+of+Silumgar) and [Typhoid Rats](https://web.archive.org/web/20150411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Typhoid+Rats), followed by [Gravepurge](https://web.archive.org/web/20150411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravepurge). The best card to open is [Dragonlord Kolaghan](https://web.archive.org/web/20150411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Kolaghan), which I did yesterday. As for none-rares, [Rakshasa Gravecaller](https://web.archive.org/web/20150411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Gravecaller) is also great.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_juza.jpg)  
**Martin Jůza, 22 Grand Prix Top 8s:** Blue-black, because it just does the most powerful stuff with exploit, flying creatures, and removal. The best card to open is [Sidisi, Undead Vizier](https://web.archive.org/web/20150411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sidisi%2C+Undead+Vizier), but leaving rares aside, I like [Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20150411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price). It's just so flexible.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_shenhar.jpg)  
**Shahar Shenhar, Two-Time World Champion:** Definitely blue-black, with lots of exploit synergies, [Gurmag Drowner](https://web.archive.org/web/20150411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gurmag+Drowner), [Rakshada Gravecaller](https://web.archive.org/web/20150411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshada+Gravecaller), and so on. [Dragonlord Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20150411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar) is the card to open for that.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_sullivan.jpg)  
**Adrian Sullivan, *Magic* Theorist/Writer/Veteran:** Definitely *not* blue-black. I guess I would say red-green, based on [Sabertooth Outrider](https://web.archive.org/web/20150411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sabertooth+Outrider). The card is so strong that I'm then picking [Ainok Artillerist](https://web.archive.org/web/20150411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ainok+Artillerist), [Gore Swine](https://web.archive.org/web/20150411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gore+Swine), and basically anything that helps turn on formidable. The best first pick for the deck might just be [Sabertooth Outrider](https://web.archive.org/web/20150411000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sabertooth+Outrider) itself, actually.
