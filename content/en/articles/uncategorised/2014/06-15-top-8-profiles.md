Featuring players spread out amongst three different continents as well as newly-minted Platinum Pro Lee Shi Tian all playing a wide spread of decks, the Grand Prix Moscow Top 8 is set. Check out the profiles of these players below!

  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/48/54/4854d3c93bca01c09b59bfe7ab8d7817c9a3d9243f05366c56abd84aef173839.jpg)

### Alexey Rogov

**Age:** 26  
**Hometown:** Kazan, Russia  
**Occupation:** Scientist (Physics)

 

**Previous **Magic** Accomplishments:**  
 Top 8 at the World **Magic** Cup Qualifier in 2013, Top 8 at the Grand Prix Trial Izhevsk in 2012

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?**  
 Jund Monsters - it has a great match-up against Mono-Black Devotion and a high card quality, which can result in unbeatable draws.

**What changes would you apply to your deck and why?**  
 I would change 1 copy of [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) for a Forest or a Mountain.

**What are the most important cards in your deck?**  
 An [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic) on turn 1, followed by [Sylvan Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Caryatid) on turn 2.

  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/fa/28/fa2845d900204976f90b4a5fe024fe841665b414a597ea497dcfedc7d2acee54.jpg)

**Occupation:** Sales Manager and a IESE Student

 

**Previous **Magic** Accomplishments:**

### Igor Gorbunov

**Age:** 32  
**Hometown:** Moscow, Russia  
**Occupation:** Owner of a restaurant chain

 

**Previous **Magic** Accomplishments:**  
 I advanced to the finals of my National Championship. I have participated in the **Magic** World Championship in both Kyoto and San Francisco and I made it to the Pro Tour Valencia and attended 2 Grand Prix prior to this one.

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?**  
 Boros Burn - I'm a huge fan of red decks. Whenever I can make a burn deck work, I will play it in the respective tournament.

**What changes would you apply to your deck and why?**  
 I would swap out [Eidolon of the Great Revel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eidolon+of+the+Great+Revel) for [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer) in my main deck, because I think it would have worked better in the current environment.

**What are the most important cards in your deck?**  
[Chandra's Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Phoenix)!

### Lee Shi Tian

**Age:** 26  
**Hometown:** Hong Kong  
**Occupation:** Accountant

 

**Previous **Magic** Accomplishments:**  
 Top 4 Pro Tour *Return to Ravnica*, Top 8 Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*, Grand Prix Birmingham 2008 Champion, Hong Kong National Champion 2008, APAC Representative for the Player's Championship 2013

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?**  
 Mono-Black Devotion with a splash of Blue. Nam Sung Wook's combined record in 4 consecutive events was an impressive 37 - 3, so I decided to follow the master!

**What changes would you apply to your deck and why?**  
 I would cut [Notion Thief](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Notion+Thief) for an additional copy of [Dark Betrayal](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Betrayal), but it's Nam's signature card and I don't dare to make the change!

**What are the most important cards in your deck?**  
[Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault). They have been following me since 2008 where I won the Grand Prix!

  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/98/8c/988cd6cc6dd08ffb47683c1f4aeabf19289cd7ffd341664a5e9156603469c598.jpg)
