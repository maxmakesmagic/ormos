### Help on getting your new account.

* * *

<table><tbody><tr><td><p>Welcome to the <i><b>Magic: The Gathering®</b> Online</i> website. With the launch of <i><b>Magic®</b> Online</i> on Monday, June 24th, we've had a lot of new players ask questions about getting their accounts set up. This article will step you through the process and hopefully answer some questions you might have.</p>&#10;<h3>Installing the Software</h3>&#10;<p>The first thing you need to do is load the software onto your PC. </p>&#10;<ul><li>Make sure you're connected to the Internet.</li>&#10;<li>If you <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20020530000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/downloadlinks&amp;origin=magic_magiconline_articleprelaunch">downloaded </a> the software, double click the file to install.</li>&#10;<li>If you have a CD, just put it into your CD-ROM drive. If it doesn't start the installation automatically, click <b>Start</b> on your taskbar, then click <b>Run</b>. Type the letter of your CD-ROM drive followed by <b>:\setup.exe</b>. For example, if your CD-ROM drive is drive D, you would type <b>d:\setup.exe</b> here. Then click <b>OK</b>.</li>&#10;<li>A dialog box will appear showing where on your computer <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> will install. To select a different location, click <b>Browse</b> and select a new folder. When you're finished, click <b>OK</b>.</li>&#10;<li>Click <b>Install</b> when the installation screen appears.</li>&#10;<li><i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> will automatically start and download the latest update.</li>&#10;</ul>&#10;<h3>Free Trial</h3><img src="https://i.ormos.uk/5a/17/5a17c05f352979370906ea916485de5bface10c3f9d12451633caf215ad52de8.jpg"><p>If you aren't sure you're ready to buy an account, or you just want to see what <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> is all about, check out the <b>Free Trial</b>. When the <b>Sign In</b> screen appears, click on the <b>Free Trial</b> tab. Click <b>Try it!</b> and you'll go to a room much like the <b>Practice Games</b> room in the full game, where you can play games against other players using <i>Seventh Edition</i> theme decks.</p>&#10;<p>You can also click the <b>Deck Editor</b> and <b>Collection</b> tabs to see what those screens are like in the full game. Once you're done, click <b>Log Off</b> to go back to the <b>Sign In</b> screen.</p>&#10;<h3>Getting a New Account</h3>&#10;<p>Whether you just finished installing <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> or are done playing the <b>Free Trial</b>, you'll want to be on the <b>Sign In</b> screen. From here you can start setting up your new account.</p>&#10;<table><tbody><tr><td><ul><li>Click <b>New Account</b>.</li>&#10;<li>Your Internet browser will open and go to the <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20020530000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com&amp;origin=magic_magiconline_articleprelaunch"><i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> Store</a> web site.</li>&#10;<li>Click the link to set up a new account. The site will walk you through the process of getting a user name.</li>&#10;<li>Once you've filled out all the required information, your password will be emailed to the address you supplied.</li>&#10;</ul>&#10;</td>&#10;<td><img src="https://i.ormos.uk/2d/b9/2db91a6d419a2bb4ffc8017e68bf85c3900436368dd801bed7a0587e02d3db86.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;<p>Keep in mind, you need to be at least 18 years old (13 with a parent's or guardian's consent) to get a <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> account. You also need a valid email address and credit card.</p>&#10;<p>A new <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> account costs $9.99 U.S. However, along with your password you'll get another email with a $9.99 coupon for use at the <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> Store.</p>&#10;<h3>Getting Started</h3>&#10;<p>Once you've got an account, it's time to play! If you're not sure what to do, try the following: </p>&#10;<table><tbody><tr><td><img src="https://i.ormos.uk/3e/8b/3e8bb8e59fbcf25e727b7ded5e2bd4679cc74b8705030c57a24be266a9055b60.jpg"></td>&#10;<td><ul><li>Log on to <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> with your user name and password.</li>&#10;<li>Go to the <b>Training Room</b> and play through the tutorials.</li>&#10;<li>Play some games with <i>Seventh Edition</i> theme decks in the <b>Practice Games</b> room.</li>&#10;</ul>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;<p>If you have any problems, make sure to check out the <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> help files. You also might want to check out the articles in <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20020530000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/mtgou&amp;origin=magic_magiconline_articleprelaunch"><b>The Academy</b></a> for more information about starting out in <i><b>Magic</b> Online.</i></p>&#10;<p>Once you're comfortable playing <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> games, go back to the <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> Store and buy some cards and decks to play games with.</p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>
