Do you want one of *these*?

  

![Treva, the Renewer](https://i.ormos.uk/2d/63/2d63c10ead1967e16227a6932fa02638a1e7abd9786daa748539714d59b636d7.jpg "Treva, the Renewer") 

  

Well, *do ya?*

You can get your copy of Treva simply by coming to the [2008 World Championships in Memphis](/en/articles/event-coverage/2011/10-10-magic-weekend-san-francisco-and-2011-worlds-event-information) or to any of the 2009 Pro Tours (Kyoto February 27-March 1, Honolulu June 5-7, Austin October 16-19). Participation in the main event isn't required—anyone who comes to the event hall can get a free promotional card (while supplies last). For more information on next year's Pro Tour season, click [here](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour). Qualifiers for [Pro Tour–Kyoto](https://web.archive.org/web/20081201000000/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/protour/kyoto08-qualifiers) are going on now.

![Chrome Mox](https://i.ormos.uk/3c/64/3c6437957451181204127e1edb6164c1491ab203e3ac3653931c016e9ff5df4c.jpg "Chrome Mox") 

  

This shiny Chrome Mox can be yours for attending any 2009 Grand Prix (while supplies last). You can see the complete schedule for next season [here](/en/articles/feature/2008/11-20-2009-grand-prix-schedule-announced).
