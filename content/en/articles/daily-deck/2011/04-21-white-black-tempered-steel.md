The National Qualifier season is underway, and the very first events were held in Japan. Tempered Steel is a card that has drawn a lot of attention since it was originally printed in the *Scars of Mirrodin* set, and has already seen play in Extended. Now Tomoaki Oomori has innovated this white-black build for Standard that centers around LOTS of free artifact creatures that can be pumped up by the enchantment or any of the cards in the deck with the battle cry mechanic. Neat! [National Qualifiers](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2011-national-qualifiers) are going on now.
