Last week, [I wrote about populating](/en/articles/daily-deck/2013/01-10-trostanis-angels) Geist of Saint Traft's Angels with Trostani, Selesnya's Voice. Well, since then, PillPallPoll on **Magic Online** has turned populate up to eleven by throwing Cackling Counterpart into the mix. 

 ![Drogskol Reaver](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Drogskol%20Reaver&options=) 

PillPallPoll's deck replaces Geist of Saint Traft and Planeswalkers with a robust, synergistic lifegain theme, pushing it much further than we usually see. This deck combines Trostani, Selesnya's Voice; Rhox Faithmender; Chalice of Life; and Drogskol Reaver. Any two of those work together spectacularly, and each of them buys time so you can get them all together. With Cackling Counterpart, you can make a token of Drogskol Reaver, which you can then populate with Trostani, at which point I quickly lose track of how much life you gain and how many cards you draw. 

If you're looking to go over the top with a deck that gives you plenty of time to rub it in, this is an outstanding choice for Friday Night **Magic** to really win spectacularly. 

  
  

PillPallPoll's Lifegain Populate
