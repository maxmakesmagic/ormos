A little while back, Wizards of the Coast offered a promotion to encourage people to teach their friends to play **Magic**: the Guru program. Included as giveaways were five basic lands illustrated by [Terese Nielsen](/en/articles/behind-the-canvas/2002/04-24-behind-the-canvas-terese-nielsen). The lands -- called Guru lands -- show five stages of a "double eclipse."

![Guru lands 'double eclipse'](http://www.wizards.com/global/images/mtgcom_arcana_92_pic1_en.jpg)  

The expansion symbol of the cards was someone wearing a mortarboard, stylized to look like an eye. The symbol was stamped in bronze.![Guru symbol](http://www.wizards.com/global/images/mtgcom_arcana_92_pic2_en.jpg)
