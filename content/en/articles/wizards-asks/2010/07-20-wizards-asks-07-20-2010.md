*What's your favorite multiplayer format, and why?* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9e/d4/9ed42fbf034eb908091f251c60232531dd0631477510baaa68bb9a668c2cc083.jpg) **Zac Hill:** Archenemy! I like going into a game knowing whom I need to beat up. Having *worked* in politics for the better part of five years, the last thing I want to do when I'm having fun is haggle over interpersonal power dynamics and juggle even *more* politics. Archenemy gets rid of that step and just says, "Hey you over there—kill those guys!" Plus, the moment of flipping a scheme card is one of the most fun, high-variance moments in **Magic**.
