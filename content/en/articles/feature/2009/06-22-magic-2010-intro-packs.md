**Core Set Survivor!**

There are exactly sixteen cards that have been in every core set from *Alpha* to *Tenth Edition*. When ***Magic** 2010* comes out, that number will be eight. Over the next three weeks, we'll be counting them down in this special Arcana sub-feature, giving you an "IN" or "OUT" on one card a day. Step forward, [Fear](https://web.archive.org/web/20090622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fear).
