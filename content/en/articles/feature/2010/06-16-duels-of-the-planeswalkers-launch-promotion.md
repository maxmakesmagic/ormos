We have good news for PC users: [*Duels of the Planeswalkers*](https://web.archive.org/web/20100616000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx) is now available for PC Users via Steam! All you have to do to play *Duels of the Planeswalkers* on your PC is to go to [Steam](http://www.steampowered.com/) and set up an account.

Not enough of an enticement? Well, what if we were to throw in a foil promo card?

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/arcana/473_nissapromo.jpg)

 

Yes, that's a foil Nissa Revane. And you want one.

Here's how to get one. If you are eligible to receive the promo card (see [Terms & Conditions](https://web.archive.org/web/20100616000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx?x=mtg/digital/dotp/terms)) then select **View Games Library** off of the **Games** tab on your Steam window, right click on Magic: The Gathering – Duels of the Planeswalkers and click on **Register your game** to register. Grab yours now before they are gone! (while supplies last – restrictions apply)

Duels of the Planeswalkers players will enjoy the benefits of the Steamworks's features included in all PC versions, such as auto-updating, Steam Achievements, Leaderboards, downloadable content, ability to access from any computer, multiplayer matchmaking and more. More information is available in the [official press release](https://web.archive.org/web/20100616000000/http://archive.wizards.com/company/press.aspx?x=wz/press/44)
