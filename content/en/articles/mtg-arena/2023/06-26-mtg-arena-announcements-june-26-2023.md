## *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth™* Is Here!

![Play The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth now on MTG Arena](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/LTR-NPA_AN_1920x1080_01.jpg) 

The epic set of heroes and adventures is now on *MTG Arena*! You'll find packs, avatars, card style bundles, a full-art map lands bundle, and [more to collect](https://web.archive.org/web/20230626000000/https://stage.magic.wizards.com/en/news/mtg-arena/collecting-the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earth-on-mtg-arena) in the Store—and don't forget to check your in-game mail for three packs to get your started.

Midweek *Magic* Phantom Sealed Deck

Want to play the set to get a feel for it first? Don't miss the [Midweek Magic *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth* Phantom Sealed event](/en/articles/announcements/2022/03-16-this-week-in-midweek-magic#schedule) this week, June 27–29. This is a casual and fun way to play *Magic* without entry costs while earning individual card rewards and cosmetics!

Download Story Decks

Not sure where to start building a deck? Import narrative-inspired decks detailed in [*The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth™* Story Decks article](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2023/06-26-the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earthtm-story-decks) to start playing in Alchemy and Historic with the new set quickly—there's even a 100-card Historic Brawl deck!

Dive into the Details

Ready to build your own decks? Take a [look at all the cards in the set](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/05-30-the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earthtm-card-image-gallery), and then [deep dive into *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth* Mechanics](/en/articles/feature/2023/05-30-the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earthtm-mechanics) to build your own decks and strategies.

## June 30–July 11: *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth* Quick Draft

![Shortcut to Mushrooms card art showing Hobbits eating mushrooms](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/10nitethadas.jpg) 

Have fun, build your collection, and earn prizes in Quick Drafts. Join the first *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth* Quick Draft beginning this Friday, June 30.

In Quick Drafts, you are drafting against a bot rather than players, which is great if you're still learning the cards and need time to consider your options—take all the time you need, because the bot doesn't mind!

Check out more events in [the Event Schedule below](#schedule).

## Arena Open This Weekend

[![Aragorn holding his magic sword with the phrase, reforge your position, across from him](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/zatiqoz03rlz.jpg)](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2023/06-19-arena-open-the-lord-or-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earth-limited-july-12) 

Take step toward the big leagues of *Magic* competitive play in [the next Arena Open happening this weekend, July 1–2](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2023/06-19-arena-open-the-lord-or-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earth-limited-july-12). Plus, everyone who enters gets this Dragon Hoard sleeve!

## July 7–9 Celebrate *Magic* at Your Local WPN Store

![Gandalf with his fireworks at the Shire celebration](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/0a0res1uyitr.jpg) 

Join us for the *Magic* Celebration event at [participating WPN and WPN Premium stores worldwide](https://web.archive.org/web/20230626000000/https://locator.wizards.com/) July 7–9! It takes just three easy steps:

1.  Register and play *Jumpstart*! Purchase two *Jumpstart* packets and play three casual rounds at your store. The first 30 players to register and play *Jumpstart* at the *Magic* Celebration event will receive a limited-edition, collectible dice set that includes three six-sided (d6) dice and a burgundy bag with a golden Planeswalker symbol!
     ![Example set of green dice and burgundy dice bag with gold Planeswalker symbol](https://media.wizards.com/2023/wpn/w19/iy2clann68.jpg)
2.  Form your Fellowship! Bring up to eight friends, and you'll all get a special Thought Vessel promo card!
3.  Claim a Precious! Use your registered *MTG Arena* [Wizards account](https://web.archive.org/web/20230626000000/https://myaccounts.wizards.com/) in the [*Magic* Companion app](https://web.archive.org/web/20230626000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/companion-app) to get a special, ready-to-play deck in your *MTG Arena* inbox, beginning July 23—just be sure to log in with the account you use to play *MTG Arena*! (This promotion only applies in select regions where *MTG Arena* codes are valid.)

Don't miss it!

### QUALIFIER EVENTS

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in)are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend)are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events. Check out the standings to see [who's qualified for Arena Championship 4](https://magic.gg/standings/arena-championship-4).

Qualifier tokens are delivered in your *MTG Arena* inbox. Remember to claim them before the event starts!
