We kick off our second Quick Questions with a look at six pro player's favorite *Magic 2015* Booster Draft "pet cards." These are the cards that may be good, but not the best, that a player can't help but play when able. 

 **Do you have any pet cards in *Magic 2015*?** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/maher_feature.jpg)

 ***Bob Maher, Hall of Famer** - [Ensoul Artifact](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ensoul+Artifact). The Blue-Red artifact deck is insanely powerful, but almost impossible to draft because you need a very specific set of cards. I chickened out of drafting it here, and I would've wound up with an unbeatable deck if I'd pulled the trigger.* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/jensen_draft.jpg)

 ***William "Huey" Jensen, Hall of Famer** - [The Chain Veil](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Chain+Veil). Make sure you put that down.* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/sperling_feature.jpg)

 ***Matt Sperling** - [Brood Keeper](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brood+Keeper). The Red-White [Brood Keeper](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brood+Keeper) / Auras deck is one of the best you can have in this format.* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/levy_QQ.jpg)

 ***Raphael Levy, Hall of Famer** - [Soulmender](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soulmender). It does so much. It trades with x/1 creatures, but unlike those it does something in a standoff. The life is actually really relevant in this format. It's still the MVP since *Magic* 2014.* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/dezani_wc.jpg)

 ***Jeremy Dezani, Player of the Year Hopeful** - Probably [Darksteel Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Citadel) and [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter). I love the artifact deck.* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/finkel_draft.jpg)

 ***Jon Finkel, Hall of Famer** - [Sanctified Charge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sanctified+Charge). I mean, I really like [Sanctified Charge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sanctified+Charge). A lot of people undervalued it early. I don't know if they've come around or what, but it's a really powerful spell.*
