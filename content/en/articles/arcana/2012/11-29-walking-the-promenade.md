This is Transguild Promenade.

  

 ![Transguild Promenade](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Transguild%20Promenade&options=) 

It's a walkway in Ravnica that's used by people of all guilds. It even has different guild symbols in the pillars!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fb/82/fb822518602104cbd0075de3d9bb47e70ec86c9ecb2eb2514689d81cd933554d.jpg) 

Transguild Promenade | Art by [Noah Bradley](https://web.archive.org/web/20121129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22noah%20bradley%22])

In fact, if you look closely, you can see that people from multiple guilds are actually walking on the promenade right now! This person is definitely from the Izzet League.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4f/0c/4f0ce07b8180e3cd597358524c621c4877ff36e6e9c3782dcf84577d9385367f.jpg) 

  

And over on the left, there's someone from the Rakdos Cult talking to someone from the Selesnya Conclave!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9f/09/9f09341be1ddf36bb8dd7b2df6d923fd001545953d7575b85358266171a9cf11.jpg)
