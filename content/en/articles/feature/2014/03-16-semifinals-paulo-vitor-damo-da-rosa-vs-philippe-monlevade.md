Brazil has always been the dominant force in South American **Magic**, and it has always had a particular stranglehold on events in Buenos Aires. This Semifinal match between Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa, from Porto Allegre, and Phillipe Monlevade. from Rio de Janeiro, ensures that, once again, there will be a Brazilian player vying for the title of Grand Prix Buenos Aires Champion. 

**The Decks** 

Damo da Rosa opted to play his trusty standby, Esper Control, and has had a very impressive run with the deck. He had praised the strength of [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) prior to beginning this Semifinal match, and it was very likely that it would come into play in a major way. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpba14/sf_monlevade.jpg)

  

One reason for this is the power of Monlevade's Planeswalkers. His Jund Monsters deck is running more than the standard number of prime targets for [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere), with [Vraska the Unseen](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vraska+the+Unseen) joining [Domri Rade](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Domri+Rade) and both versions of Xenagos. In addition to these permanent threats, Monlevade also has access to the very powerful [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon), providing a hasty threat that can be difficult for the Esper Control deck to deal with. 

**The Games** 

Damo da Rosa held the first game in a vice grip from the word go. He had answers to the first three threats that Monlevade tried to play, keeping the way more or less clear for a sixth-turn [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion). The one threat that Monlevade was able to keep in play actually did more harm than good. His [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix) revealed five non-land cards in a row, providing himself no benefit, and giving Damo da Rosa more free information. 

Elspeth did her job, protecting herself with tokens and even dispatching of a [Reaper of the Wilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reaper+of+the+Wilds) before Damo da Rosa cleared the board with [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict). Now denied any threats at all, Monlevade watched helplessly as Damo da Rosa filled his hand up with [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) and took over the game with Elspeth. It only took a few turns for the number of tokens to exceed Monlevade's patience, and he picked up his cards. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpba14/sf_pv.jpg)

  

The second game provided an example of the power of haste creatures in this matchup. Damo da Rosa locked things up nicely in the early stages of the game, using [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle) to shut down [Domri Rade](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Domri+Rade) and [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) to keep the board clear. Onto an empty board, Monlevade managed to throw two [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon)s and two copies of [Xenagos, the Reveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xenagos%2C+the+Reveler). Each of the first three met an untimely death in turn, but not before doing their damage. With Damo da Rosa at 4 life and his own board clear, Monlevade made a Satyr token with his Xenagos before playing [Vraska the Unseen](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vraska+the+Unseen), using her ability to destroy the [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) locking down the other Xenagos. It came into play, giving him another attacker, and making a very elegant lethal board position. Damo da Rosa shrugged and packed things up for the final game. 

In that final game, it looked like Damo da Rosa had the huge lead early. His second-turn [Blind Obedience](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blind+Obedience) would have done wonders in the previous game, preventing the massive amount of hasty damage he took. It managed to do that in this game, preventing Xenagos, [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon), [Mistcutter Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistcutter+Hydra), and the like from ever reaching his life total. Damo da Rosa even had all four copies of his important [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) to destroy the threats before they could hurt him. Unfortunately for Damo da Rosa, he also drew only four of his lands, keeping him from being able to do anything but react. He took a massive hit to [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) away a [Ruric Thar, the Unbowed](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ruric+Thar%2C+the+Unbowed), and Monlevade was able to capitalize. He got another [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon) into play, this time surviving the torrent of removal. When it went monstrous on the next turn, it did a massive amount of damage to the mana-screwed Damo da Rosa. That, plus the increase in power, was enough to make the Dragon lethal, sending Phillipe Monlevade to the Finals!
