There was a time when Urza was the most important unseen figure in **Magic**. He had towers, hats, and cool shades. But no one knew what he looked like.

Eventually, of course, he made an appearance on a card: Mind Over Matter, from *Exodus*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2a/ce/2ace0d12407a667a4d6a778ad016f33e7b365715e2f6e71499fbf2e6ebd651b4.jpg) 

Mind Over Matter | Art by [Keith Parkinson](https://web.archive.org/web/20100325000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22keith+parkinson%22])

The next block was *Urza's Saga*, so it's no surprise that Urza's appearances ramped up a bit. Since then, he's appeared on many cards. Sometimes he's been in disguise, and one time he was a severed head. Since his story spans thousands of years, it's not surprising that he sometimes looks a little different. Here's some of our favorite Urza cameos.
