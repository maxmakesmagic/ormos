One of the card cycles most talked about from *Shards of Alara* for the upcoming States and Provincial tournaments has been the Ultimatum cycle. Of the five, perhaps Cruel Ultimatum is the one most talked about, and for good reason. Worlds finalist Patrick Chapin has referred to the card as an "11-for-1," a sizable amount of card advantage rarely ever seen in a single card! Podlos has put the Ultimatum to good use in this Premier Event Top 8 list of Quick and Toast. The deck controls the early game with removal, Kitchen Finks, and a small modicum of counters, then wallops the late game with Cruel Ultimatum. The sorcery can even be returned to hand in a loop with Nucklavee and Cryptic Command! To find out more information about States (including how to get special unreleased promo cards for attending as well as the nearest location to you) visit [www.mtgchamps.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20081029000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://mtgchamps.com/&origin=). As an added bonus, you can check out some insider info on the Ultimatums [here](/en/articles/arcana/2008/10-27-whose-ultimatum).
