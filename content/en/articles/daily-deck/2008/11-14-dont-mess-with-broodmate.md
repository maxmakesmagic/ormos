When a group of European players introduced the five color "Quick 'n Toast" deck at [Pro Tour–Hollywood](/en/events/2008/pthol08/gindy-puts-name-in-lights), the Standard format was irrevocably changed. The impact of that deck can still be felt today, and this list used by Chris Poff to win the Texas State Championship is a great example. Chris added platinum hits from *Shards of Alara* like Violent and Cruel Ultimatum, Bant Charm, Esper Charm, and Broodmate Dragon to beat opponents into submission.
