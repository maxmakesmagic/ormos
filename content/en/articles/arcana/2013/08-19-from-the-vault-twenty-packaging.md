Releasing this Friday is this year's new *From the Vault: Twenty*. In case you missed it, we revealed the full contents of this box set in Gavin Verhey's ReConstructed [two weeks ago](/en/articles/reconstructed/2013/08-06-from-the-vault-twenty), where he talked about each of the twenty cards inside. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/19/d8/19d8a974fb97703b9ea80b2128282c1cac47f9ace9770176212d42f5afd0ca15.jpg)  

Gavin went card by card and shared the deck it was from and the story around the card. From new [Fyndhorn Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20130819000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fyndhorn+Elves) to [Tangle Wire](https://web.archive.org/web/20130819000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tangle+Wire) to, well, [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20130819000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor)—this From the Vault, is awesome.
