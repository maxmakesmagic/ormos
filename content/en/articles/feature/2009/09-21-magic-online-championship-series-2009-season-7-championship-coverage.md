The [Magic Online Championship Series](/en/articles/feature/2009/05-07-magic-online-championship-series) awards invites to the [Magic World Championships](https://web.archive.org/web/20090921000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/642286) as well as a cash tournament for players who qualify through the series. The Standard format 2009 Season 7 Championship was held on October 10.
