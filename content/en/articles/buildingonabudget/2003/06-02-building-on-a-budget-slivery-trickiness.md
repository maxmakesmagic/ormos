### Slivery Trickiness

* * *

Did you see all of the mad trickiness? Let's go through the list of cool things you can do.

*   Provoke from Hunter Sliver with attackers that grow bigger from Spectral Sliver or regenerate from Crypt Sliver.  
  

*   Attack with regenerating poisonous creatures from Toxin Sliver and Crypt Sliver.  
  

*   Make your slivers unblockable with pro-color Ward Sliver and swing for massive damage with Magma Sliver.  
  

*   Attack for practically infinite life with Magma Sliver and Essence Sliver.  
  

*   Confuse the heck out of your opponent by only attacking with one sliver every turn while your army hangs back. If your opponent blocks, the blocker will probably die. If your opponent doesn't block, Magma Sliver will make it an expensive decision.

But beware -- the slivers can trick you too! Be careful about what color you name with Ward Sliver, because it can turn off some of your effects as well.

<table><tbody><tr><td><p></p><h3>Playing The Deck</h3> Slivers may be powerful, but they aren't the most efficient beating machines in the world until you have a couple out. It feels kind of stupid playing a <a>Hunter Sliver</a> on turn two, but then you notice how important it is to the deck three turns later. Overall, I think <a>Crypt Sliver</a> may be the most vital, because it lets you do the trickiest of tricks.<p></p>&#10;<p>You have to be sure to watch out for <a>Wrath of God</a> effects. Don't over commit, because you have no way to come back from a big Wrath. It's fine to put out slivers with new abilities, but try to hold back the duplicate slivers unless you need bodies for pumping with <a>Magma Sliver</a>.</p>&#10;<p>Overall, your strategy should be to stall the board and pick things off with provoke until you're able to swing for the win. </p>&#10;</td>&#10;<td><table><tbody><tr><td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/65/b4/65b44cec90bda938bbb278a79acba1f455a38a19b714807bea68c253b77de30d.jpg"></td><td></td><td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/dd/0a/dd0a617788694ee0e82eed58b0746ab9dbdbdb6e709ee5390dcfb32556a6990e.jpg"></td></tr>&#10;<tr><td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/96/f5/96f5d53e3ef958055c4fdf5bf835be99797bd18449a68a882c96ab3801bae612.jpg"></td><td><a><img alt="Crypt Sliver" src="https://i.ormos.uk/bd/6c/bd6c63ca5a65c8b6248fa4e7943484cecfe8c776a8bac444b71f08b606fedfbb.jpg"></a></td><td></td></tr>&#10;<tr><td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/a0/a9/a0a91e69ef087e17e689f758224146196f0a29fad934eb2639d2fb73d4cf3c75.jpg"></td><td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/c0/e2/c0e24bff2fa206011911cb4b5cb20b4559cb82301a77e757b6e86de3c1ae659a.jpg"></td><td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/fa/ab/faab219b94a5d6a8405ff77aefe6f815ba2bba52fc447652a633eec85e227836.jpg"></td></tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

### Sidebording Tips

**Patriarch's Bidding**  
 I always like putting Patriarch's Bidding in my Tribal decks, and I think it might be okay to sideboard in against non-Zombie decks, because you can get Ward Sliver back and it's also really hard to attack into a horde of toxic regenerators. Sideboarding more Ward Slivers may also be a good idea against slower decks -- it works especially well with the Bidding.

If you don't want to deal with the Tribal headache of Bidding, try Balthor the Defiled against non-Red-Black decks.

**Smother**  
Smothers are always nice to have against those people playing Wild Mongrel or Psychatog. You actually might want to run these maindeck if you plan on playing in 8-player tournaments.

**Worship**  
Worship is good against many decks since your slivers become rather hard to kill between Crypt Sliver and Ward Sliver. This is especially true against decks that don't run Wrath effects.

**Visara the Dreadful**  
Visara the Dreadful is great versus creature decks -- just bog down the early game and follow up big in the late game with a Gorgon Legend. Decks that plan on winning with creatures aren't going to be happy seeing Visara on the other side of the table.

**Persecute**  
Persecute will help you beat any monocolor deck, but is especially useful against blue.

<table><tbody><tr><td><p></p><h3>Adding More Money To The Deck</h3> Well, a three-color deck will never have a lack of expensive lands that can be added, but besides that I think <a>Coat of Arms</a> is particularly useful in this deck. Now, Coat of Arms is one of those cards that's scary to play in Tribal Wars, but you can play it when you have creature advantage and then provoke down all your opponent's creatures at once. Played well, it can turn into a one-sided <a>Wrath of God</a>.
