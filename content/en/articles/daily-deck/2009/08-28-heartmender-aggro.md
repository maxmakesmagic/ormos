Heartmender was custom-made for persist creatures, and Nathan Gelderloos combined his copies with Kitchen Finks, a powerful pairing against any beatdown deck. Another combo in the deck? Wilt-Leaf Liege with almost all green-white creatures! Nathan played the deck to a local Top 8 at an event in Michigan.
