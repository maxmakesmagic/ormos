Back when we showed off the [*Dark Ascension* Art Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20120130000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/dark-ascension-art-gallery-2012-01-04), there was a mysterious piece of art that kind of stood out.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/896_jarofeyeballsfinal.jpg)

[Jar of Eyeballs](https://web.archive.org/web/20120130000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jar+of+Eyeballs) | Art by [Jaime Jones](https://web.archive.org/web/20120130000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Jaime+Jones%22%5D)

"What is this mysterious jar of eyeballs?" asked people around the Internet. And soon, the answer was made clear: it was [Jar of Eyeballs](https://web.archive.org/web/20120130000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jar+of+Eyeballs).

![Jar of Eyeballs](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Jar+of+Eyeballs)

  

Now, we're not mentioning this just to show off a jar of eyeballs. That's the main reason, obviously. But we also thought it would be fun to look at the original sketch, before the eyeballs got finished.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/896_jarofeyeballssketch.jpg)

Jar of Eyeballs Sketch | Art by [Jaime Jones](https://web.archive.org/web/20120130000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Jaime+Jones%22%5D)
