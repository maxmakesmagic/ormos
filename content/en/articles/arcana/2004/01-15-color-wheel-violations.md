As **Magic** senior designer Mark Rosewater has often [discussed](https://web.archive.org/web/20040115000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/mr85) on MagicTheGathering.com, the color wheel, also known as the color pie, keeps the five colors in balance while adding spice to the game.
