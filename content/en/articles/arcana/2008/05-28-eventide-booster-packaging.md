We were going to dance around the subject a little and maybe build things up a bit, but then we noticed that the headline for today's arcana clearly reads "*Eventide* Booster Packaging". So there's probably not much mystery about what we're going to be looking at. So let's get it on!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1b/0a/1b0ae04337a3ee9b9b98ba44c2f08069bd2aaaad6f865c8859bf12006f435e01.jpg)

Hmm. That lady has a glowing chest! And she's covered with owls. There's something you don't see every day. How about another one?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/22/16/221635ca5589955ac456e6e2b5365e959abe92785cca2fa8cf75d80499581aa6.jpg)

Ooh, creepy! Some kind of greasy underwater... thing! Hit it again.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0d/a9/0da98cc6340d551fc0a58e7855bfb3074b7d2fd0dd29066613044ee08f2b96d2.jpg)

That guy's busting all the way out of the pack! Look out world, here comes this guy! Finally, let's take a look at the display box.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c8/db/c8db669f066dd3c88868bb9489f13ff095f7496a1ac21b6cea3b46b975906e33.jpg)

Very handsome. Some kind of shaggy thing with a spear riding a gigantic insect. Unless it's a really tiny shaggy thing. It's hard to tell the scale.

*Eventide* previews start on June 30, although the Minisite will come in the previous week. We're not admitting anything, but there *might* be something else before then too...
