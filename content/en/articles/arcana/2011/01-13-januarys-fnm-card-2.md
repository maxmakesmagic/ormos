It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6)! Since we're now in January, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

![Everflowing Chalice](https://i.ormos.uk/63/76/6376743000ccee776b53b7ae2687bed389795d2100b531952da5ff3fec72058f.jpg "Everflowing Chalice") 

  

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards feature the FNM logo in the text box and many have original flavor text and updated wordings.
