Looking for a fun deck to try out at your Friday Night **Magic**? Then look no further than Michael Rooks's creation! Based around the Scars of Mirrodin enchantment Furnace Celebration, Michael's deck sacrifices as many permanents as it can to trigger the Celebration over and over. Kozilek's Predator, Nest Invader, and Awakening Zone give him Spawn tokens to sacrifice on a whim and, as a bonus, they even produce mana to help him pay for the Celebration! In a pinch, Mortarpod also lets him toss creatures at his opponents. Michael played the deck at the StarCityGames.com Standard Open in Washington DC.
