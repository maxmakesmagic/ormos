The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know for today, January 13, 2016. Today's Update is brought to you by [Intro Packs](#number1) and sneaky-good Reddit comments.

# What People Are Talking About, Like, A Lot

1.  > [@SvanteLandgraf](https://twitter.com/SvanteLandgraf) This is entirely about stores running tournaments for profit (not about playtesting)
    >
    > — Helene Bergeot (@HeleneBergeot) [January 13, 2016](https://twitter.com/HeleneBergeot/status/687072898545102848)

If we're being honest, this is pretty much all people have been talking about in *Magic*'s corner of the internet—the store proxy policy that has been inundating the Reddit forums for the past 36 or so hours.

Look for more on this soon on DailyMTG.

# Today's Must See

1.  [*Oath of the Gatewatch*—Set Visualization](http://www.visualizingmagic.com/oath-of-the-gatewatch-set-visualization/) | VisualizingMagic.com

Well if this isn't ridiculously cool, I—I don't even have a way to finish that sentence. It's just ridiculously cool. Visualizing Magic created, well, a visualization of *Oath of the Gatewatch*. Check it out if you like cool things and *Magic*. Which is pretty much our demographic here.

# After That, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [*Oath of the Gatewatch* review: *Magic* has a new colour of mana. Kinda.](http://arstechnica.co.uk/the-multiverse/2016/01/oath-of-the-gatewatch-review-magic-has-a-new-colour-of-mana-kinda/) | Ars Technica UK | Kyle Chapman

1.  Duke Ascends
