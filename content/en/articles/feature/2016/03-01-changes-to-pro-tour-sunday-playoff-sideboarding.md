After discussion following the success of the return of best-of-five matches for Sunday playoffs at Pro Tours, we have decided to make a change to how sideboarding functions in best-of-five matches played on Sunday at Pro Tours and the *Magic: The Gathering* World Championship.

Starting with [Pro Tour *Shadows over Innistrad*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-shadows-over-innistrad-event-information) in Madrid, Spain, the first two games played in a best-of-five playoff match on Sunday will be *pre-sideboard* games. After Game 2 is completed, players may change cards in and out from their sideboards and main decks in between games.
