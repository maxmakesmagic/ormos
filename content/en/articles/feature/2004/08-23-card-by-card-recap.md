The year: 1994. The event: the first **Magic** World Championships. Both decks from the finals, and card-by-card recap of the entire finals.

Transcribed by Mark Rosewater and Chris Page compiled by Mark Rosewater retranscribed in 1999 by Monty Ashley

Finalists: 

Zak Dolan (U.S.) \[beat Domic Symens (Belgium) 2-0 in semi-finals\] 

Bertrand Lestreé (France) \[beat Cyrille DeFoucand (France) 2-1 in semi-finals\] 

Zak Dolan 1994 World Championships Bertrand Lestreé 1994 World Championships
