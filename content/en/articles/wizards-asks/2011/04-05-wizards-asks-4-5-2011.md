*What's your best poison counter story?* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e0/0c/e00c2900e4532fed44efdbc0223f486ee1518c0cf594bb289867319eeda0f043.jpg)

**Kelly Digges:** On the surface, all poison counter stories are the same: "...Then my opponent had ten (or more) poison counters and died." But c'mon—that's like saying all the games that end with regular damage are the same—"...Then my opponent had 0 (or less) life and died."—and that's clearly not true. If it were, why would anybody tell **Magic** stories at all?

Two of my favorite poison counter stories are not, strictly speaking, my stories, and you can read about them elsewhere anyway: Daily MTG correspondent Monty Ashley's [successful bid](/en/articles/wizards-asks/2011/02-01-wizards-asks-2-1-2011) to get a Prerelease achievement by intentionally going to nine poison counters, and Chris Lachmann and Jake Van Lunen's end run around the Two-Headed Giant Draft format at [Pro Tour San Diego](/en/events/2007/ptsdg07/sliver-kids-stun-san-diego), powered in part by Virulent Sliver. (It's worth noting that it [now takes fifteen poison counters](/en/articles/feature/2011/03-18-march-18-2011-dci-banned-restricted-list-announcement) to kill a Two-Headed Giant team.)

Leaving those aside, my favorite poison counter story (that actually happened to me) was in a two-person Winston draft. I had a rather shaky green-blue-black infect deck. This was, sadly, before *Mirrodin Besieged* gave us Corrupted Conscience, so the blue was mainly there for Inexorable Tide and... well, we'll get there.

I'd stalled out pretty badly, and it looked my lone Plague Stinger was not going to be able to count to ten before my opponent's creatures delivered 20 points of plain old pain to my delicate face.

 ![Plague Stinger](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Plague%20Stinger&options=) 

  

Then I drew my other big splashy blue card: Quicksilver Gargantuan. The Gargantuan copied the Plague Stinger, giving me a monstrous 7/7 flier with infect and eliciting a jaw-drop on the other side of the table. 

 ![Quicksilver Gargantuan](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Quicksilver%20Gargantuan&options=) 

  

...Then my opponent had like fifteen poison counters and died. That's what they're there for, after all!
