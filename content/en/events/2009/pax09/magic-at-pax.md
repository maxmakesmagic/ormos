**Magic: The Gathering** will have a truly impressive digital presence at this year's *Penny Arcade Expo* in Seattle on September 4-6, with the hit XBLA title *Duels of the Planeswalkers* and a **Magic Online** tournament leading to the **Magic** *World Championships* in Rome just the tip of the iceberg!

This year **Magic** will be hosting a party that is sure to be the highlight of the weekend. On Saturday, September 5 from 7-9 p.m., hundreds of lucky players will behold "The Sights of *Zendikar*" at Trinity Nightclub in downtown Seattle. We'll be showing a ton of exclusive spoiler cards and new art from the upcoming set, as well as previewing the new *Duels of the Planeswalkers* downloadable content. We'll also have an interactive puzzle to test your wits, with the savvy winner taking home a big prize (along with smaller door prizes for everyone). Free food and drinks will be served while guests mingle with artists Jeremy Jarvis, Richard Whitters, Mark Tedin and Todd Lockwood, along with R&D designers and developers who make the game what it is. 

To get invited to the party, you'll need to overcome the challenges of five distinct planes located throughout the Washington State Convention & Trade Center. Successfully solve the puzzles at each of the five stations and earn the right to attend this way-out sneak preview of **Magic** goodness.\* For more information, email zendikarparty@starfish-events.com or visit the Wizards booth onsite.

Featuring cool foil promo cards, a video contest, a *Duels of the Planeswalkers* live "Game with Fame" event, **Magic** tournaments, a **Magic** party, an "Art of Wizards of the Coast" panel, introduction to *Planechase* sessions, and many other activities, **Magic** fans are sure to walk away from PAX with great memories.

### Magic Tournament Highlights

*   [**Magic Online** Live Series](/en/articles/uncategorised/2009/04-02-2009-magic-online-live-series) event with $5000 in prizes
*   Intro Pack League
*   Standard tournaments with a **Magic**-skinned Xbox 360 as first prize
*   ***Magic** 2010* Two-Headed Giant Sealed Deck tournament with *From the Vault: Exiled* as first prize (one per player)
*   Mini-Master single-elimination tournaments for a ***Magic** 2010* foil set
*   Grand Prix Trial
*   Super **FNM**
*   Grand Melee
*   **Magic Online** Meet and Greet – Come talk with the people that make **Magic Online** (Friday 7 p.m., Room 615)

No matter how you like to experience **Magic**, PAX will have something for you! For more information about PAX, visit [www.paxsite.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20090819000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.paxsite.com/&origin=). For a full listing of **Magic** tournaments at PAX, visit [www.cascadegames.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20090819000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.cascadegames.com/events/785&origin=).

\*Must be 21 to attend. Invitations limited on a first come, first served basis.
