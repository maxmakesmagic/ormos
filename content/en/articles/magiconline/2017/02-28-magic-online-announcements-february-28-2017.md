**The *Magic Online* Championship starts Friday, March 3!**

It's time to start getting hyped! From Friday, March 3, through Sunday, March 5, the *Magic Online* Championship will be streaming live from Wizards HQ. Watch 16 of the world's top *Magic* players battle it out for online supremacy. [Find out more details here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/02-07-the-magic-online-championship-approaches)!

**Full *Invasion* Block Flashback Drafts begin tomorrow, March 1**

The Flashback Favorites Draft series continues tomorrow with *Invasion* block drafts starting Wednesday, March 1! Draft *Invasion*-*Planeshift*-*Apocalypse* with seven other players through March 8. For more information, [click here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/08-02-flashback-favorites-and-throwback-standard-gauntlets).

***Aether Revolt* Standard Championship on Sunday, March 5**

The *Magic Online* *Aether Revolt* Standard Championship begins Sunday, March 5! Qualifying players will be able to complete for awesome prizes like premium *Aether Revolt* sets! [Find out more details here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/02-07-magic-online-aether-revolt-championship-event-details)!

[Top](#top)

***Amonkhet* Regional Pro Tour Qualifier on Saturday, March 4**

A Standard Regional Pro Tour Qualifier (RPTQ) is coming up on March 4. Please note, these events are only open to players who have qualified through an in-store qualifier event.

Learn more about *Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers [here](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/regional-pro-tour-qualifiers-rptqs-for-pro-tour-amonkhet-2017).

[Top](#top)

***Hour of Devastation* Sealed Pro Tour Qualifer on Sunday, March 5**

Attention all Pro Tour hopefuls: a Pro Tour Qualifier (PTQ) is approaching! We hope to see you in the PTQ Finals on Sunday, March 5!

Remember, the Pro Tour Qualifier Preliminaries now follow a set weekly schedule, thanks to the redesigned PTQ system that *Magic Online* Events Manager Lee Sharpe announced [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/02-14-pro-tour-qualifier-preliminary-schedule-changes).

Learn more about *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20170228000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers#HOU_schedule).

**Vintage Super League airs tonight**

Season 6 of Vintage Super League continues tonight! Tune it at 5 p.m. PT on [twitch.tv/magic](https://web.archive.org/web/20170601050947/http://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/magic-online/twitch.tv/magic) to catch the action live! For more information on Vintage Super League, [check out their website here](https://vintagesuperleague.com/about/).
