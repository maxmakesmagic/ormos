This is a White Weenie deck featuring the Kor tribe and new artifact creatures and equipment from *Scars of Mirrodin*. The dream scenario is to activate Quest for the Holy Relic using Memnites and Kor Skyfishers to cheat out Argentum Armor. Alternatively, use Stoneforge Mystic to find and plop down Argentum Armor, then use Kor Outfitter to equip it for free! Plan C is to just attack your opponent to death with creatures equipped with Sword of Body and Mind. The deck was designed by Ken Nagle and used for gunslinging at a *Scars of Mirrodin* Prerelease this past weekend.
