### Welcome!

This mini-site provides information to approved Wizards Play Network Organizers on how to schedule and report DCI- and RPGA-sanctioned events.

**Join the WPN!**

**To become a WPN Organizer, please click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20090602085403/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dci/wpn).** 

Running WPN events is a great service you can offer your friends and customers, keeping them involved in organized play at your location for a long time to come.

For more information about the new **Wizards Play Network**, please visit [www.wizards.com/wpn](https://web.archive.org/web/20090604003758/http://www.wizards.com/wpn).
