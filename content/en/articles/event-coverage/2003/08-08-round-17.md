![](https://i.ormos.uk/9c/e9/9ce97e28c5386b2e0cc7788de628491570aa1cbc851f9ddcd16716f66903935e.jpg)This was essentially an elimination round for these two players. They were both sitting on 12-4 records and all a victory here would earn is the right to wage another do or die battle during the final round. Peer was the old time player with a couple of Pro Tour Top 8s on his resume while his opponent was a young player from Malaysia—and their National Champion. 

The hometown crowd was pulling for Peer to return to the Top 8 after a lengthy absence but the match-ups were working against him. Peer was playing a White Weenie deck with blue for [Meddling Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meddling+Mage). His opponent's deck was a poor match-up for him—Rock. He was also having a bad week of feature matches. Three of his four losses were waiting for him in the wings of center stage.

### Game 1

Peer opened with [Savannah Lions](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savannah+Lions) and his opponent came back with Bird of Paradise. Peer's draw was pretty fast but Han had the turn two [Wall of Blossoms](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Blossoms) as well as a [Treetop Village](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treetop+Village). A [Yavimaya Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yavimaya+Elder) came down and things were looking up for Han. Unfortunately for him, he led off the fifth turn with a [Swamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swamp). When he announced an attack and Peer had no responses he chose no attackers. He then played a [Wall of Blossoms](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Blossoms) and followed it up with a [Dust Bowl](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dust+Bowl)—HIS SECOND LAND FOR THE TURN.

A judge was called over and a game loss was issued. Han blamed nerves for his error. He had never played in a big tournament like this before much less in a feature match. His friends urged him to be more careful as they prepared for the next game.

Peer – 1 Han – 0

### Game 2

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e9/4b/e94b8615dffa8d15c83dfc0d0c052147540e77ed46a3d91daed93c1a7fe50512.jpg)Peer had the turn two [Whipcorder](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whipcorder) but it came after Han played a [Wall of Blossoms](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Blossoms). [Yavimaya Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yavimaya+Elder) was next and after [Smother](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smother)ing the [Whipcorder](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whipcorder)—Peer's only play—he played a [Spiritmonger](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spiritmonger). A [Benevolent Bodyguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Benevolent+Bodyguard) offered no permanent solutions and Han played a [Deranged Hermit](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deranged+Hermit) a turn later to close the game out.

Peer – 1 Han – 1

### Game 3

Both players kept what would be the final relevant hand of the weekend for one of them. Peer led off with [Ramosian Sergeant](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ramosian+Sergeant) off of a pain land and Han played a Village. [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm) caused more pain and found another pain land to play [Savannah Lions](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savannah+Lions). Han slammed the gate shut with a [Wall of Blossoms](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Blossoms). 

Peer could not afford to sit back on the Sarge—he was mindful of a Deed and he played a [Meddling Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meddling+Mage) naming the enchantment. A [Benevolent Bodyguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Benevolent+Bodyguard) joined the team and Peer fell to fifteen form his lands. Dustbowl was Han's third land and it helped him play an [Engineered Plague](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engineered+Plague) on Rebels.

[Parallax Wave](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Parallax+Wave) was painful for Peer—he fell to thirteen—but it was more painful for Han with his [Wall of Blossoms](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Blossoms) out of the game. Han missed land drop number four but had another Plague and infected the [Savannah Lions](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savannah+Lions).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e2/df/e2df19be60a7d11ce72d7cbc2595d6377e5fa2aa2ed8142b7ac7ec40981a0bfc.jpg)Peer got in for another three and played a face-down [Whipcorder](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whipcorder). Han missed another land drop but played a Bird of Paradise. Peer attacked with everyone and Han did not block and he dropped to five. Peer had only one card in hand while Han was choking on a full grip of seven. Not wanting to give Han the mana from the Bird he tucked it under his Wave. Han [Vampiric Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vampiric+Tutor)ed down to three to find a [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest). He used it to play a [Spike Feeder](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spike+Feeder).

Peer continued to send the team and the Bodyguard died to save the Mage from dying to the Feeder. After the dust settled Han had gained a point of life and was teetering on four. Peer added a [Silver Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20030808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silver+Knight) and passed the turn back. On his next turn the Wave went away and the Bird and Wall came back. The game was slowly slipping away from Peer and he had nothing in hand.

His draw?

Another Wave for the win.

**Final Result:** Peer – 2 Han - 1
