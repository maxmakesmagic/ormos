The guilds of *Return to Ravnica* cards are Azorius, Izzet, Rakdos, Golgari, and Selesnya. But because all ten guilds exist on Ravnica, you can sometimes see the influence of the other five guilds.

For example, take [Dispel](https://web.archive.org/web/20121105000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=253566).

![253566](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&multiverseid=253566)

 

Because it's a blue card, [Dispel](https://web.archive.org/web/20121105000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=253566) could technically be Azorius, Dimir, Izzet, or Simic. But if you examine the art carefully, you'll figure out which one it *really* is.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/1096_dispel.jpg)

[Dispel](https://web.archive.org/web/20121105000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=253566) | Art by [Chase Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20121105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Chase%20Stone%22%5D)

It's Simic! The mage is clothed in the Simic garb and he's got the characteristic facial spirals. And there's something else you might not have noticed. Click here to see something really neat!
