[Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial) has been a Constructed powerhouse ever since it was released in Darksteel. Since then, it has been reprinted not only in Modern Masters, but also in From the Vault: Relics. Despite Merfolk being the predominant deck in Modern to utilize [Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial), we're here to explore other ways to use the amazing artifact in other shells, as several other players have done at Grand Prix Guangzhou!

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=%C3%86ther+Vial)](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial)

What properties of [Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial) can we appreciate from Yaso's masterpiece which he piloted to a second place finish at the 2012 Player's Championship? In a nutshell, here are the fundamental benefits.

*   [Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial) provides mana advantage, allowing you to summon creatures for free while keeping your mana available for other business.
*   [Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial) provides tempo advantage, allowing you to recruit multiple creatures ahead of schedule to overwhelm your opponents with.
*   [Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial) offers mana fixing, allowing to put creatures onto the battle even if you don't have the correct amount of mana or the correct colored mana to cast it.
*   [Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial) breaks the rules that creatures can only be cast at sorcery speed and effectively grants all of your creatures flash.
*   [Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial) allows you to evade opponent's countermagic and sorcery speed removal. Since you're putting a creature into play directly, your opponents cannot use cards such as [Spell Snare](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Snare) or [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) to counter it and neither will they be able to kill your creature with [Maelstrom Pulse](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Maelstrom+Pulse) or [Wrath of God](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wrath+of+God) if you "vial out" your creature at end of your opponent's turn.
*   [Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial) allows you to disrupt your opponent's hand after the draw step. [Vendilion Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vendilion+Clique) can pluck away a card which your opponent freshly-drew, and if you use (spoiler alert!) [Thought-Knot Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thought-Knot+Seer) he won't even get to replace it immediately.

The White-Blue Spirits deck has a very similar goal as Merfolk, to quickly whittle away at your opponent's life total while countering key spells and keeping your team alive. The idea is to use [Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial) to "gain" mana such that you're able to play two or more creatures on a single turn. For example, once you get two counters on [Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial), you'll be able to summon both [Leonin Arbiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Arbiter) and [Kitchen Finks](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kitchen+Finks) on turn three. Such a line of play forces your opponent to deal with multiple threats, but they may be unable to because they might be bottlenecked on mana during the early turns.

Free [Spell Queller](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Queller)s? Sign me up!

"Vialing out" [Flickerwisp](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flickerwisp) is one of the most annoying plays you can imagine. Conveniently, it is also a Spirit which pumps [Mausoleum Wanderer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mausoleum+Wanderer). Not only can it remove an attacker or blocker temporarily (a lifesaver against [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Aeons+Torn)), it can also save your creatures from spot removal, preserving your army in the process. Han throws in [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel) for more blinking goodness plus additional attackers in the sky. In conjunction with [Reflector Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reflector+Mage), [Spell Queller](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Queller) and [Kitchen Finks](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kitchen+Finks), Han has a lot of tools in his arsenal against various decks out there.

I bet many of you would have LOVED to see [Rattlechains](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rattlechains) in action, huh? As we speak, I'm figuring out how to incorporate a couple of copies into Han's list.

[Sinew Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sinew+Sliver) and [Predatory Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Predatory+Sliver) turn harmless looking 1/1s into monsters of elephantine proportions. [Sedge Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sedge+Sliver) is basically "Muscle Sliver" numbers 9 through 12 and [Leeching Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leeching+Sliver) might as well be one of them, considering that it helps all your creatures "deal additional damage" when attacking.

The caveat of playing a 4-Color deck is usually a painful manabase, since you'll usually take a lot of damage from Fetch lands and [Shock](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shock) lands. However, [Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial) mitigates a huge part of that because it happens to be an excellent mana fixer itself! [Cavern of Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cavern+of+Souls) and [Sliver Hive](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sliver+Hive) are also perfect since they are essentially [Mana Confluence](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Confluence) without a drawback.

Last but not least, Cai's W/B Eldrazi has the same concept Legacy's infamous Death and Taxes archetype. However, he added black mana for a generous portion of hand disruption and removal. Functioning like a traditional Hatebear deck, it aims to disrupt with an assortment of annoying weenies. [Thalia, Guardian of Thraben](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thalia%2C+Guardian+of+Thraben) is very upsetting to a combo or control player, as are [Thought-Knot Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thought-Knot+Seer) and [Tidehollow Sculler](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidehollow+Sculler).

The synergy between [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile) and [Wasteland Strangler](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland+Strangler) isn't new tech, but for it to be housed alongside [Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial) makes it a rather fresh prospect. To make things even crazier, [Flickerwisp](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flickerwisp) and [Eldrazi Displacer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Displacer) help you re-trigger [Wasteland Strangler](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland+Strangler) as well as [Blade Splicer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blade+Splicer). In conjunction with the capabilities of your mighty bottle, you'll be able to present a ton of mischievous pranks at instant speed.
