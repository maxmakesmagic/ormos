S*aviors of Kamigawa* is finally in stores -- so today we take a moment to look back. We [introduced](https://web.archive.org/web/20050607000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/606676) Kamigawa as "a world that waged a brutal war against its own gods." Three sets and over a year later, we've seen twenty short stories set in the context of that struggle between Kamigawa's mortals and kami. 

Did you enjoy the "Meet the *Champions/Betrayers/Saviors*" vignettes on **magicthegathering.com**? Ever wish you could see one big list of all those legend-riffic vignettes in one place?

Well, [now you can](https://web.archive.org/web/20050607000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/kamigawa/vignettes).
