At the end of the first day, only nine of the original 1,169 players remained with a perfect record of 8-0 still intact: Mario Rodenas Lopez, Ashley Baden, Klaas Gruber, Sean Lynch, Alexander Hugill, Marco Del Pivo, Mark Mc Govern (pictured below, left to right) as well as Davide Cappiello and Goncalo Pinto (not pictured).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-legacy/bs_8-0.jpg)

The decks these nine had chosen included White-Blue God-Pharaoh's Gift, White-Black Vehicles, and even Mono-Black Control. They also gave us a first idea of the top decks of the new format, as Black-Green Winding Constrictor was featured twice here, while four copies of Black-Red Vehicles supported [Goblin Chainwhirler](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Chainwhirler) and [Heart of Kiran](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heart+of+Kiran)'s claim for the crown.
