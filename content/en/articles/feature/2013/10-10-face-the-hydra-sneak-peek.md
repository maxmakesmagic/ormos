Continue your quest down the Hero's Path at *Theros* Game Day on October 19–20. Gather the Hero Cards that you collected from the *Theros* Prerelease and Launch Weekend, and get ready for a brand-new play experience—Face the Hydra! Here, a few players from the **Magic Online** [Community Cup team](/en/events/2013/mtgoccup13/2013-magic-online-community-cup) got a sneak peek at the experience and survived to tell the tale!

Nathan Holt and Shawn Kornhauser are "hardcore casual" **Magic** players exploring the rich tapestry of the ever-expanding Multiverse. As a team, they've co-produced several experimental independent films, and Walking the Planes is their biggest, baddest effort to date.
