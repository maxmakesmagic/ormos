*What's your favorite fantasy creature?* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e0/0c/e00c2900e4532fed44efdbc0223f486ee1518c0cf594bb289867319eeda0f043.jpg) **Kelly Digges:** I've been playing **Magic** for so long that it's hard to untangle my favorite **Magic** creatures from my favorite fantasy creatures. I don't think I cared about Goblins very much before **Magic**, and I know I had absolutely no opinions about fungus-monsters.

In fact, I can hardly remember what sort of fantasy stuff I liked before I played **Magic** or **D&D**. I was just about to give up and go with my favorite **Magic** creature type when I remembered that there was one kind of fantasy monster I loved way before **Magic**, and it's one of my favorite creature types in **Magic**.

I am all about the dragons. I was raised on *The Hobbit* and Anne McCaffrey's Pern books, and I know I was doodling sketches of dragons on things long before I had ever tapped a Mountain to summon one.
