R&D designs the Core Set (currently in its *[Ninth Edition](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/expansion/9e)*) both as a learning tool for new **Magic** players and as a source of staple cards for both Constructed formats and casual play. But since it's packed with spells hailing from Alpha through (in the case of *Ninth*) *Betrayers of Kamigawa*, it's also a 350-card tour through the game's past. Take a look below at the art of some *Ninth Edition* cards (in no particular order), and enjoy a planeswalkers'-eye view of events in **Magic**'s history.

### [Furnace of Rath](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Furnace+of+Rath)

The airship *Weatherlight* navigates the deadly [Furnace of Rath](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Furnace+of+Rath)...

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/c4/52/c452509940bd1a4ce426c4c67fb751cecf83720c24bdf8c5f6284605c754096f.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Furnace%2Bof%2BRath) *[Furnace of Rath](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Furnace+of+Rath) art by John Matson*

### [Lumengrid Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lumengrid+Warden)

A [Lumengrid Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Lumengrid%2BWarden) overlooks the spires rising from Mirrodin's Quicksilver Sea...

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/82/f1/82f1da2494238b2eda073461aa982444c0d4d88080aaff6dd30010ec72cd4412.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Lumengrid%2BWarden) *[Lumengrid Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lumengrid+Warden) art by Francis Tsai*

### [Mending Hands](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mending+Hands)

A kitsune healer puts her [Mending Hands](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mending+Hands) to two samurai in Kamigawa...

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/c5/40/c540a6256c1db2b3123df1d3694a1bbf669b22da3bd1a13ee25d4f1d061ab0c1.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Mending%2BHands) *[Mending Hands](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mending+Hands) art by Douglas Schuler*

### [Slay](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slay)

Uncaring Phyrexian minions [Slay](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slay) a host of Llanowar elves...

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/07/7e/077e605364408361e0849b3ca237726a816ec66fc1c56de6ea00d02064802b0a.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Slay) *[Slay](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slay) art by Ben Thompson*

### [Biorhythm](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Biorhythm)

In Otaria, Kamahl realizes his destiny lies with the [Biorhythm](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Biorhythm) of Nature...

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/4d/aa/4daa73780c50e53614d3a1d1fb3b7816167fb28abba7f9ad06f817cd03652144.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Biorhythm) *[Biorhythm](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Biorhythm) art by Ron Spears*

The Core Set isn't just a source of [Grizzly Bears](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grizzly+Bears) and [Flight](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flight)s for teaching your little brother a **Magic** lesson, and isn't just a source of [Hypnotic Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hypnotic+Specter)s and [Greater Good](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Greater+Good)s for *teaching your older brother a lesson*; it's also a feast of storyline content drawn from all points in the timeline. No doubt the Core Set's *Tenth Edition* will showcase even more of the history of the **Magic** multiverse. After all, we've visited an entirely new [plane](https://web.archive.org/web/20060308000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/expansion/rav) since *Ninth*...
