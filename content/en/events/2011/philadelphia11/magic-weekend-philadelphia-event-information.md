A centerpiece of early American history, Philadelphia is the fifth-largest city in the United States. The city's central location in the original 13 colonies meant that it was a focal point for America's revolutionaries. The Declaration of Independence was signed here in 1776 and many national historic sites are popular tourist attractions. Home to the Liberty Bell and the cheesesteak, Philadelphia offers a wide range of activities for visitors.

### Ground Transportation from Philadelphia International Airport

Philadelphia International Airport is located just 7.2 miles southwest of Central Philadelphia. The Ground Transportation System at the airport is an extensive network of taxicab, car rental, shuttle-bus, limousine, and van operations working to get you where you want to go in a safe, courteous and efficient manner. In addition, the Airport also offers SEPTA bus and regional rail service to Downtown Philadelphia and points in between. For more information, visit [www.phl.org](http://www.phl.org/ground_transportation.html) or [www.septa.org](http://www.septa.org/).

### Driving Directions

The Pennsylvania Convention Center is located in the heart of Center City Philadelphia at 1101 Arch Street, Philadelphia, PA 19107. For more information about driving directions and parking options, visit [www.paconvention.com](http://www.paconvention.com/explore-philadelphia/directions-and-parking).

**From Route 76 (Schuylkill Expressway)**  
 Take Route 76 to Exit 344/I-676 East. Take I-676 East and exit at Broad Street/Route 611 (second exit). This will place you on Vine Street. Follow signs for Vine Street/PA Convention Center to 12th Street (four traffic lights). Make a right onto 12th Street. The entrances to the Convention Center are located two blocks ahead at the NE and NW corners of 12th & Arch Streets. 

**From Interstate 95 North**  
 Take I-95 North to Exit 22 Central Philadelphia/I-676. Stay in the left lane of this exit. Follow signs for I-676 West to the first exit (Broad Street/Route 611). This exit brings you up to 15th Street. Get into the left lane and follow the sign for 611/Broad Street and make a left turn on to Vine Street. Follow signs for Vine Street/PA Convention Center. Make a right on to 12th Street. The entrances to the Convention Center are located two blocks ahead at the NE and NW corners of 12th & Arch Streets. 

**From Interstate 95 South**  
 Take I-95 South to Exit 22 Central Philadelphia/I-676. Stay in the left lane of this exit. Follow signs for 676 West to the first exit (Broad Street/Route 611). This exit brings you up to 15th Street. Get into the left lane, follow the sign for Broad Street/Route 611 and make a left turn onto Vine Street. Follow signs for Vine Street/PA Convention Center. Make a right on to 12th Street. The entrances to the Convention Center are located two blocks ahead at the NE and NW corners of 12th & Arch Streets. 

**From the Pennsylvania Turnpike**  
 Follow the Pennsylvania Turnpike to Exit 20/I-476. Take I-476 South to Exit 57/I-76 Philadelphia. Stay on I-76 East for approximately 12 miles to Exit 344/I-676 East. Take I-676 East and exit at Broad Street/Route 611 (second exit). This will place you on Vine Street. Follow signs for Vine Street/PA Convention Center to 12th Street (four traffic lights). Make a right onto 12th Street. The entrances to the Convention Center are located two blocks ahead at the NE and NW corners of 12th & Arch Streets. 

**From the New Jersey Turnpike**  
 Take NJ Turnpike to exit 4 (Philadelphia/Camden Exit). Take Route 73 North and follow it to Route 38 West. Take 38 West to the Benjamin Franklin Bridge, crossing into Philadelphia. Follow local traffic and the signs for Vine Street/PA Convention Center. Go approximately six blocks and make a left turn onto 12th Street. The entrances to the Convention Center are located two blocks ahead at the NE and NW corners of 12th & Arch Streets. 

**Arriving via Philadelphia International Airport**  
 Take I-95 North to Exit 22 Central Philadelphia/I-676. Stay in the left lane of this exit. Follow signs for I-676 West to the first exit (Broad Street). This exit brings you up to 15th Street. Get into the left lane and follow the sign for Broad Street/Route 611 and make a left turn onto Vine Street. Follow signs for Vine Street/PA Convention Center. Make a right onto 12th Street. The entrances to the Convention Center are located two blocks ahead at the NE and NW corners of 12th & Arch Streets.

### Site Attractions

 

**PUBLIC EVENTS**  
 Come one, come all to participate in four days of events open to the public. Starting Thursday with the Last-Chance Qualifier, there are tournaments, drafts, ***Magic: The Gathering** - Commander*, and other events scheduled for the entire all weekend. Public event prizes include invitations and airfare to the first Pro Tour of 2012, foil card sets, iPads, xBox gaming systems, and much more! 

*   [Complete **Magic** Weekend Philadelphia Public Events Information](/en/events/2011/philadelphia11-public/magic-weekend-philadelphia-2011-public-event-information)

**SPELLSLINGERS**  
 Test your **Magic** skills against Wizards of the Coast R&D members Aaron Forsythe, Ryan Spain, and Matt Tabak. Win and you can walk away with cool **Magic** prizes! 

**ARTISTS ON SITE**  
 Prominent **Magic** artists Eric Deschamps and Steve Belledin will be on hand for free signings of your favorite cards, plus you’ll have the opportunity to buy prints and even original artwork. Artist booth times to be announced. 

*   [**Magic** card art by Eric Deschamps](https://web.archive.org/web/20110728000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Eric+Deschamps%22%5d)
*   [**Magic** card art by Steve Belledin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110728000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Steven+Belledin%22%5d)

**WATCH THE PRO TOUR TOP 8**  
 Sunday's Pro Tour Top 8 can be viewed on screens in the hall starting at 10:45 a.m Sunday morning, with Pro Tour Statistician Rich Hagon and Pro Tour Historian Brian David-Marshall providing the commentary. The matches are also [available as a webcast](/en/events/2004/webcast/magic-the-gathering-webcast-schedule) from the comfort of your own home.
