A sliver’s anatomy often reflects the adaptation that it transfers to the other members of its species.

Check out the brainy lobes on [Telekinetic Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20061212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Telekinetic+Sliver) for example:

![Telekinetic_Sliver_640](https://i.ormos.uk/21/38/213837832948164af81824db6b45441251551cd9c5454ba02cf14daff6d29fe0.jpg)  
*[Telekinetic Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20061212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Telekinetic+Sliver) art by Randy Elliott* 

To reflect its added wiliness in battle, [Watcher Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20061212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Watcher+Sliver) has evolved rows of eyes along its head, where most slivers have none:

![Watcher_Sliver_640](https://i.ormos.uk/c5/73/c57378aed56a0ee2b42299a2ecd2c2038641ece143216a7157cda51fa171a380.jpg)  
*[Watcher Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20061212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Watcher+Sliver) art by Liz Danforth* 

It’s subtle, but [Bonesplitter Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20061212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bonesplitter+Sliver)’s adaptation is shown right in the art. It doesn’t carry around Bonesplitters; it has a special adaptation to its blood. Its blood rages with *bioluminescent blood*. You can see it flowing through its veins right through the skin:

![Bonesplitter_Sliver_640](https://i.ormos.uk/33/dc/33dc8453c9870f720aaba95ad198cd154a25f6f7124d7d2eb29b7094fedf9053.jpg)  
*[Bonesplitter Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20061212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bonesplitter+Sliver) art by Dany Orizio* 

[Spinneret Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20061212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spinneret+Sliver) sports organs referred to in its name—organs that let it spin webs like a spider. Imagine the arachnid-like clicking sound this guy would make:

![Spinneret_Sliver_640](https://i.ormos.uk/d3/4c/d34c89ebe5eba12768a39a7dd2a9f0d54c796559be953def02caca39b905defa.jpg)  
*[Spinneret Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20061212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spinneret+Sliver) art by Michael Sutfin*
