This week's wallpaper features a card from an artist who's only illustrated one **Magic** card: Mark Rosewater's [Look at Me, I'm the DCI](https://web.archive.org/web/20110401000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Look+at+Me%2C+I%27m+the+DCI) artwork from *Unglued*. It's like having Mark Rosewater himself drawing on your monitor with crayons! Enjoy. If you can.
