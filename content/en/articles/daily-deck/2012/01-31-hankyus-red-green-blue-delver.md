The *Innistrad* Block Constructed format is quickly turning out to be one of the most interesting block formats in recent memory! Amid beatdown and control decks alike, **Magic Online** player Hankyu ran this interesting red-green-blue aggro-control deck all the way to first place in a recent premier event. With *Dark Ascension* being added to the mix soon, what new cards will make the cut? Check out the [visual spoiler in Gatherer](https://web.archive.org/web/20120131000000/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&set=+[%22Dark%20Ascension%22]) and start preparing—the ascent into darkness has begun!
