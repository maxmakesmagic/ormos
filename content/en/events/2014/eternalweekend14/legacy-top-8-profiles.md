# Kevin Jones

**Age:** 26  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** SCG New Jersey Standard Open Champion 2014. Won two PTQs. Some SCG Open Top 8s. 

**What did you play this weekend? Why?**  
 Blue/Red Delver. It's the best [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) deck. The deck has high velocity and [Pyroblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroblast) in the main to fight [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) mirrors. Can come back from behind with powerful card advantage. 

**What would you change about your deck if you played it next weekend?**  
 I might add another [Hydroblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hydroblast) for the Burn matchup. [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer) was very mediocre for me. 

**What has been the most important card in your deck this weekend? Why?**  
[Pyroblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroblast) fights enemy [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise)s. [Monastery Swiftspear](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Swiftspear) presents a fast clock and is deceptively hard to kill. 

**What would winning the Legacy Championship mean to you?**  
 I've never Top 8ed or won an event with Wizards coverage. It's awesome to be featured on the mothership. I would be very happy with my range as a *Magic* player. I mostly play Standard.
