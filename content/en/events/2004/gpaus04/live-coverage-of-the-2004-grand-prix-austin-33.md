Finally he decided to tap out for a Kami of Lunacy. Gerry untapped and scorched it with Yamabushi's Flame. Brock eyeballed the Burr Grafter in his bin and frowned, &quot;Remove from the game, huh?&quot;</p>&#10;      <p>He had to think about whether or not their was going to be land on top of Gerry's deck for the Feral Deceiver and reluctantly put his Graverobber in the way.
