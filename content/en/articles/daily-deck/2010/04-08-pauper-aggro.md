Looking for a fun format to try on **Magic Online**? Look no further than Pauper! The crazy commons-only format allows players to build decks using all the cards in their collections, provided those cards are commons. Monkeywrenched85 stepped up to the challenge, doing very well recently with a classic strategy: red-green aggro. A mini-madness theme in Fiery Temper and Basking Rootwalla supported by Wild Mongrel and Gathan Raiders definitely helped!
