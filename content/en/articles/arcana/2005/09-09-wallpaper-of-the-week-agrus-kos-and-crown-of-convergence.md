This week's desktop wallpaper showcases **[Agrus Kos, Wojek Veteran](/en/articles/feature/2005/09-05-life-in-the-big-city)** from this week's *Ravnica: City of Guilds* previews. 

As a bonus, we bring you a wallpaper of **Crown of Convergence**, a *Ravnica* card we've not yet previewed, which happens to be the **Magic** art debut of the web team's own [Jen Page](/en/articles/arcana/2005/06-23-magic-dossier-jen-page). Enjoy.
