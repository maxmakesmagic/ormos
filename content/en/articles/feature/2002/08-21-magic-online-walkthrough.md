### Day 1: Accounts and Cards

* * *

As I read all the questions I get through Ask ***Magic** Online,* the most common questions seem to be about getting started. There's some confusion about how much the game costs, some about the free trial, and some general questions about getting started. Instead of trying to answer each in the Ask ***Magic** Online* list, I'm going to try to answer these questions in a single article.

### Show Me the Money

One of the most frequent questions (in various forms) is "How much does ***Magic** Online* cost to play?" The answer comes in several parts.

First, there is no monthly fee for ***Magic** Online*. I'd boldface that, put it in all capitals, and make it flash in twelve inch tall letters if it weren't just too obnoxious. However, this is one of the most frequent questions, so I'll say it again. There is NO monthly fee for ***Magic** Online*.

You pay for the game by purchasing an account and online **Magic** cards. An account costs $9.99 (everything's in US dollars, by the way) and never expires. The online cards come in the same packages as physical cards: 75-card tournament packs, 15-card boosters, and 40- or 60-card theme decks.

Many questions are about why players have to buy online cards. "Why can't we play for free?" What can I say? How about this: 

*   Wizards spent some serious dough developing ***Magic** Online.* This is a robust piece of software, folks. It took years for the Wizards and Lizards to make this happen.
*   There's no monthly fee (I think I mentioned that), yet there's plenty of upkeep to this game. Bandwidth, maintenance, continued development, hardware, customer support, etc. ***Magic** Online* isn't something that Wizards could put out then ignore.

**How Much Is That Booster In The Window?**

Which brings us to the next question: "How much do online cards cost?" The answer is: "The same as physical **Magic** cards." If you don't know what those prices are, here's a rundown.

*   75-card tournament packs: $9.99 + tax
*   60-card theme decks: $9.99 + tax
*   40-card *Seventh EditionTM* theme decks: $7.99 + tax
*   15-card booster packs: $3.29 + tax

There are also Event Tickets ($1.00 + tax) which are used to enter in sanctioned events and some leagues.

**Try Before You Buy**

But what if you don't know if you like the game or not? Well, that's why we have the free trial. On the free trial server, you can play against other online opponents using *Seventh Edition* theme decks. You get two hours to play and poke around a bit before you're disconnected from the server.

### Present and Accounted For

Let's move on to purchasing your ***Magic** Online* account. Now, while you can go to the [***Magic** Online* Store](https://web.archive.org/web/20020821000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com&origin=magic_magiconline_walkthrough1) and get your account without having the client software installed, where's the fun in that? After all, you're getting the account so you can play.

**Lock 'n' Load**

The first thing you need to do is load the software onto your PC.

*   Make sure you're connected to the Internet.
*   If you [downloaded the software](https://web.archive.org/web/20020821000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/downloadlinks&origin=magic_magiconline_walkthrough1), double click the file to install.
*   If you have a CD, just put it into your CD-ROM drive. If it doesn't start the installation automatically, click **Start** on your taskbar, then click **Run**. Type the letter of your CD-ROM drive followed by **:\\setup.exe**. For example, if your CD-ROM drive is drive D, you would type **d:\\setup.exe** here. Then click **OK**.
*   A dialog box will appear showing where on your computer ***Magic** Online* will install. To select a different location, click **Browse** and select a new folder. When you're finished, click **OK**.
*   Click **Install** when the installation screen appears.
*   ***Magic** Online* will automatically start and download the latest update.

If you have any problems with downloading or installing the software, email the Technical Support Group at techsupport@wizards.com.

After you've installed the software, you'll see the **Sign In** screen. As I said above, if you want to try the game first, click the **Free Trial** tab and connect to the free server. If you're ready to really dig in, you'll need to buy an account.

**Papa's Got a Brand New Account**

Before you can play the full version of ***Magic** Online*, you'll need to get an account. Either click **New Account** from the **Sign In** screen, or go to the ***Magic** Online* Store ([magiconlinestore.wizards.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20020821000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com&origin=magic_magiconline_walkthrough1)) to set up your account.

At the ***Magic** Online* Store, you click **click here** under **Create A New Account**. From there, the store web site will take you through the process. A few things to remember: 

*   Unless you purchased the box set and are using your coupon to cover the cost of the account, you will need a credit or debit card. Make sure your billing address matches the address assigned to the card you use.
*   You must also have a valid email address. Wizards will send all communication for the account to that email address. This includes your password, your $9.99 coupon code, and receipt confirmations for online product purchases.
*   If you're under 18 years old, you must have a parent's or guardian's consent to complete the purchase of an account. That person is responsible for anything done using the new account.
*   Have an account name in mind. This is both your store log on name and the name you'll have inside the ***Magic** Online* game, so choose wisely. Once you've created the account, you can't change the name.

Wizards is working on ways to purchase online cards without needing a credit or debit card. While I don't have an estimate for when this might happen, I'll post the information on the ***Magic** Online* web site when I have more information.

Another item that I can't stress enough: make sure you read and understand the [**Terms of Service**](https://web.archive.org/web/20020821000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com/help/tos.htm&origin=magic_magiconline_walkthrough1) and [**Code of Conduct**](https://web.archive.org/web/20020821000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=/default.asp?x=community/coc&origin=magic_magiconline_walkthrough1) before you purchase your account. Your account is subject to both and you can be suspended from ***Magic** Online* for violating either. They keep our community healthy, as everyone must adhere to them. The **Terms of Service** and **Code of Conduct** not only protect your account, but also protect you from players who might ruin your ***Magic** Online* experience.

As I mentioned above, a new account costs $9.99, which, conveniently enough, is exactly how much the coupon in the box set is worth. If you bought the box set, put in the code number when asked (it's on the back of the CD case) and you're on your way.

Once the account is created, you'll get an email with your username, password, and a coupon code worth $9.99 at the ***Magic** Online* Store. So, when you purchase your account, you get $9.99 to spend on cards.

If you have any problems creating your account, send an email to magiconlinestore@wizards.com for help.

### Where Do We Go From Here?

Now that you have your account, it's time to play. If you haven't already, launch the ***Magic** Online* program. At the **Sign In** screen, type in your username and password and click **Log On**.

Once inside, you'll see the **Main Room**, which has nine buttons in it along with some more down the side of the screen. If you've never played ***Magic** Online* before, you might want to take a gander at the articles in [**The Academy**](https://web.archive.org/web/20020821000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/mtgou&origin=magic_magiconline_walkthrough1) before you start.

However, I know some of you will be keen to get playing, so I'll give you a few pointers.

When you're comfortable with the game mechanics, go challenge some other new players in the **Practice Room**. This room is like the **Free Trial**, as you can play games using *Seventh Edition* theme decks without having to buy them. Unlike the free trial, you can play games until you're ready to move into the real world of ***Magic** Online*.

**Ready for Anything**

If you know the **Magic** game, but you've never played ***Magic** Online*, spend some time with the tutorials or playing in the **Practice Games** room. Learning the interface is key to not making mistakes in your games.

Once you're good to go, you have a veritable cornucopia of choices before you. If you want to play in any sanctioned event, you'll need some Event Tickets (**Sanctioned Tournaments**, **Premier Events**, and most events in the **Leagues** room require them) along with the product required for the event. Don't worry, though, the game will tell you what you need to purchase and give you the opportunity to do so before you begin.
