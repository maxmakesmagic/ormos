* * *

A game of **Magic** is a collection of stories waiting to happen -- and not just stories about game zones, card advantage and damage going on the stack. It's also about the flavor of the game: the imagery of dueling wizards duking it out on a mystic battlefield, which is evoked by the cards' names, art, flavor text, mechanics and rules.

In honor of Equipment Week we again investigate the [flavor of equipment](/en/articles/arcana/2004/02-18-the-flavor-of-equipment-2), this time focusing on the rich tradition of weaponry of the Kamigawa Block and all the top-down flavor it brings to your **Magic** games. 

*   ![Godo, Bandit Warlord equipped with General's Kabuto and a Hankyu](https://i.ormos.uk/6f/d5/6fd5e1d0ee5f57f8b6d767d37c73de4f852cf0234c70c08c118165c76d219bcf.jpg)Certain notoriously flavorful **bandit-lords** are never without their trademark weapons, or at least a handy battlefield-salvaged helmet or a trusty, well-strung bow.

*   **Neko-Te** conjures the image of a ninja wearing poisoned claws, his venom paralyzing any guardian who stands in his way as he steals a set of ancestral daisho.

*   **O-Naginata** is a hefty polearm with long reach -- suitable for doing battle even with dragons -- but with an iron blade so heavy that only a powerful warrior can wield it.

*   The **Manriki-Gusari** is a chain weapon specially designed to break other weapons. Many an overconfident foe wielding a powerful artifact has been brought down with a snap of the manriki, even without the interference of the spirits.

*   The **banner of a champion** can inspire the hearts of samurai troops who see it wave, or fuel the rage of one spirit and his dedicated minions in their battle for justice.

*   **Ninja** are masters of flavor, pulling out every trick in the book to make life hard on well-meaning defenders while sneaking in to cause mayhem clawmark by clawmark.
