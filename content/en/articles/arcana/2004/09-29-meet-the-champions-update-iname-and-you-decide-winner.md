Ready for another story about the heroes of Kamigawa? Check out the [*Champions of Kamigawa* site](https://web.archive.org/web/20040929000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/displayexpansion.asp?set=chk&page=1) for an update in the "Meet the Champions" area.

This week's story is "**[Thankless Child](https://web.archive.org/web/20040929000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/chk/iname)**" by Rei Nakazawa. It's the story of Iname, a legendary kami with two opposing halves: one side representing [Iname, Life Aspect](https://web.archive.org/web/20040929000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Iname%252C%2BLife%2BAspect), the other [Iname, Death Aspect](https://web.archive.org/web/20040929000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Iname%252C%2BDeath%2BAspect). Enjoy!

![You Decide!](https://i.ormos.uk/f2/7d/f27de86376451d5760c7f0bd73f7af5b729272f95aa0804bd5ea182598270732.jpg)
