Guillaume Matignon of France narrowly missed the 2010 Player of the Year title at Pro Tour Paris, part of **Magic** Weekend Paris. The Standard deck he opted to bring to battle is a Tezzeret, Agent of Bolas very similar to the list that took American Patrick Chapin to the Top 8 of the event. Though it wasn't good enough to fend off Brad Nelson, who dispatched Matignon to lock up the title, it is a fascinating new addition to the world of Standard. Read full coverage of the event [here](/en/events/2011/ptpar11/stark-flies-high-with-planeswalkers-in-paris).
