*Do some rares or mythic rares seem to follow you around?* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e0/0c/e00c2900e4532fed44efdbc0223f486ee1518c0cf594bb289867319eeda0f043.jpg) **Kelly Digges:** I think this has happened to everybody, hasn't it? Sometimes it feels lucky, other times unlucky, but if you open a lot of boosters, odds are you'll notice some weird patterns in the rares. That's why they call it random!

In *Lorwyn* it was Jace Beleren. In *Shards of Alara* it was Mirror of Fate and Tezzeret the Seeker. In *Zendikar*, I couldn't stop opening Archive Traps, but I have barely any Hellkite Chargers. In *Worldwake*, I seem to have a bizarre affinity for Bazaar Traders—I already have four, and I've opened maybe a box. And on the mythic rare front, so far Novablast Wurm and Eye of Ugin are tied at two each. I've been rooting for Novablast Wurm, which is my kind of fatty, but after [yesterday's Arcana](/en/articles/arcana/2010/03-01-the-secrets-of-the-eye) I'm not so sure .....
