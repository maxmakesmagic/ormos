Tomorrow (or even today, depending on your part of the world and when you read this) is that glorious day of the week we like to call Friday (or *Freitag*, *Vendreidi*, or *Viernes*, if you're feeling international). And with Friday comes [Friday Night *Magic*.](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-event-types-events-2)

And with Friday Night *Magic* comes FNM Promos! Let's take a look at the promos you can pick up at FNM over the next few months.

**October**

Pay tribute to the [Fanatic of Xenagos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140925000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fanatic+of+Xenagos) in October.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/arc20140725_fnmprm.jpg)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/cardart/cardart_fanaticofxenagos_prm.jpg)

[Fanatic of Xenagos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140925000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fanatic+of+Xenagos) | Art by [Zoltan Boros](https://web.archive.org/web/20140925000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5BZoltan%5D+%5BBoros%5D)

**November**

Seriously, how gross is this [Brain Maggot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140925000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brain+Maggot) art? The same art you can pick up in November at FNM, of course.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/tcg/products/ktk/8arg43u984398yf/en_sqh95x8zuu.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/cardart/cardart_brainmaggot_prm.jpg)

[Brain Maggot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140925000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brain+Maggot) | Art by [Dave Kendall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140925000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5BDave%5D+%5BKendall%5D)

**December**

December might be winter for the northern hemisphere, but [Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20140925000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames) is sure to keep many people nice and toasty. Or just plain dead.
