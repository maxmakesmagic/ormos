Only two types of tokens are produced by cards in *Legions*. We already showed off the Sliver token, and the other—a Goblin token—exists only in **Magic** Online. Here is the art.

The only card that uses this token is [Warbreak Trumpeter](https://web.archive.org/web/20030328000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warbreak+Trumpeter).
