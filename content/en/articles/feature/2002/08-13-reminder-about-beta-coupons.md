### Beta Coupons Expire Friday

* * *

Just a reminder to players who haven't redeemed their Beta Test coupons, you only have this week to use them. As stated in the initial email, these coupons expire on Friday, August 16, 2002. At 12 midnight (PDT) Saturday, August 17, the Beta Test coupons will no longer be valid.
