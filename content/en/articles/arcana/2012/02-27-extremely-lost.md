Here is the art for the *Dark Ascension* card Lost in the Woods.

  

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/916_lostinthewoods.jpg) 

Lost in the Woods | Art by [Matt Stewart](https://web.archive.org/web/20120227000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22matt+stewart%22])

Does something look odd here? Consider the way the flames of the torch look a little strange. Look at the top of the picture. Do those look like the *tops* of trees? Wait a minute... these people aren't just lost! They're *upside-down*! No wonder that guy on the right looks so disturbed!
