After many rounds of Swiss and some great games of *Magic*, the Top 8 for Grand Prix Pittsburgh is set! Learn more about this event’s Top 8 competitors, their history at the top tables, and more by taking a look through the Top 8 profiles.

* * *

  
  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppit15/t8_long.jpg)Name:** Rob Long

**Age:** 28

**Hometown:** Washington DC

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Top 8 appearance at a StarCityGames Open

**What did you play this weekend and why?** [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin). I like being reactive but having a quick win in Modern.

**What was the best card in your main deck and why?** [Twisted Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Twisted+Image) was surprisingly strong. I liked only having one [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard and why?** [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) and [Keranos, God of Storms](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Keranos%2C+God+of+Storms). They help make the matches where you can't combo better.

**What was the most unexpected matchup you faced this weekend? How did that go for you?** [Doran, the Siege Tower](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doran%2C+the+Siege+Tower) Abzan. It's where I was able to cast [Twisted Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Twisted+Image).

**You are [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy) and just transformed into a Planeswalker. What are you flashing back?** [Swords to Plowshares](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swords+to+Plowshares).

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppit15/t8_cucunato.jpg)Name:** Rob Cucunato

**Age:** 29

**Hometown:** Toledo, OH

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Five StarCityGames or TCGPlayer Top 8 appearances; competitor at Pro Tour *Magic Origins* in Vancouver

**What did you play this weekend and why?** Affinity, because I've played it since it was in Standard.

**What was the best card in your main deck and why?** [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating), [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager) and [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite). Two mana wins are the best wins.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard and why?** [Ghirapur Æther Grid](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghirapur+%C3%86ther+Grid) slew everything.

**What was the most unexpected matchup you faced this weekend?** **How did that go for you?** I lost on Turn 2 on the draw to [Protean Hulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Protean+Hulk) combo, but [Grafdigger's Cage](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grafdigger%27s+Cage) stole games for me.

**You are [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy) and just transformed into a Planeswalker. What are you flashing back?** [Reanimate](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reanimate) on [Griselbrand](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Griselbrand).

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppit15/t8_burkhart.jpg)Name:** Corey Burkhart

**Age:** 23

**Hometown:** Alta Loma, CA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Top 8 Grand Prix San Diego; Top 25 at Pro Tour *Battle for Zendikar;* member of team Ultra Pro.

**What did you play this weekend and why?** Grixis Control. I couldn't find another deck I truly loved. [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy) and [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) are truly powerful spells.

**What was the best card in your main deck and why?** [Kolaghan's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kolaghan%27s+Command) and Jace. The looping and interaction between those cards is so good.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard and why?** [Fulminator Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fulminator+Mage). Locking out opponents on Amulet Bloom, [Scapeshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scapeshift) and even Jund from killing you is really important.

**What was the most unexpected matchup you faced this weekend? How did that go for you?** Red-Green [Scapeshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scapeshift) with [Commune with Lava](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Commune+with+Lava). I luckily watched as he drew mostly lands, and died to [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) and [Fulminator Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fulminator+Mage) beatdown.

**You are [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy) and just transformed into a Planeswalker. What are you flashing back?** With five counters, a removal spell (or in a dream world, [Enter the Infinite](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enter+the+Infinite)). With three counters, a [Kolaghan's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20151122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kolaghan%27s+Command), getting Jace back.
