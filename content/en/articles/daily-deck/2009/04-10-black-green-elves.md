Black-Green Elves was a popular deck over the course of the past two years, but has cooled off in Standard of late. Now, Mrine brings us a new version of the deck straight from **Magic Online**. This version recently made the Top 8 of a Premier Event there, running unique inclusions like Leaf Gilder and Platinum Angel. Could Elves be the new tribe back on the block?
