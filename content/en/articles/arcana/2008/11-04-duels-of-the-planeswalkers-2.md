Pro Tour–Berlin featured the public debut of a much-anticipated product: the *Duels of the Planeswalkers* game for XBOX and PC!

It's going to be available on XBOX Live in Spring 2009, and it will feature a Campaign Mode (single-player or co-op) as well as online dueling and 2HG. The PC game will be single-player Campaign only. There will be challenges and puzzles galore.
