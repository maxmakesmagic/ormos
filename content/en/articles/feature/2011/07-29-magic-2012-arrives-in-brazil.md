Wizards of the Coast is excited to announce that ***Magic** 2012* has reached Brazil and is now available at local retail stores. To celebrate the release, WPN locations in Brazil are holding [prerelease events](/en/events/2008/prerelease-facts/worldwide-prerelease-tournaments) throughout the country this weekend (July 30-31), and Devir will be providing additional boosters to the prize pool for these events. Gather your allies and head to your local retail store this weekend!
