This week's Sketches looks at the *Judgment* card [Spelljack](https://web.archive.org/web/20020514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spelljack). [Spelljack](https://web.archive.org/web/20020514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spelljack) was illustrated by Pete Venters, who has been doing **Magic** cards since *Antiquities*.

### 1\. Art Description

These are the instructions given to the artist:

"This is a blue card and should show a blue wizard catching a green spell out of the air. It trails down into his open spellbook creating some sort of diagram in the book." The card was originally called "Super Desertion," playing off the *Visions* card [Desertion](https://web.archive.org/web/20020514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desertion).
