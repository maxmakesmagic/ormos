Whether you want to focus on a single format or dabble in a variety of event types, whether you want to qualify for the Set Championship or the end-of-season *Magic Online* Champions Showcase and a share of its $70,000 prize pool and Set Championships invitation, now more than ever you can play your way with 2021 *Magic Online* Premier Play Program.

## MTGO Premier Play Seasons

The new MTGO Premier Play Program splits the year into seasons of roughly seventeen weeks each.During each season, you'll earn Qualifier Points which can be used to join Qualifiers, Super Qualifiers, and Champions Showcase events, all of which provide a link to a specific Set Championship on *MTG Arena*. At the end of the season, we'll reset the Qualifier Points in players' accounts and then start the next season.

*   Season 1 of the 2021 MTGO Premier Play Program begins Wednesday, December 9, 2020 and ends with the maintenance on Wednesday, April 14, 2021 with the maintenance downtime.
*   Season 2 begins immediately following the finish of Season 1 and ends Wednesday, August 11, 2021 at 10:00 a.m. Pacific time (17:00 UTC).
*   Season 3 begins immediately following the finish of Season 2 and will end at a date to be announced later.
*   When seasons coincide with a maintenance downtime, the season will end at the start of that maintenance and the new season will begin when the maintenance is over. For season transitions that happen without a maintenance downtime, the transition will take place at 10:00 a.m. Pacific.

## Earning MTGO Premier Play Qualifier Points

During each Premier Play season, players will earn Qualifier Points (QPs) by performing well in various *Magic Online* events. The number of QPs earned will vary by event and finish as indicated by the various event types described below.

Players can use accumulated QPs to enter various Premier Play events over the course of the Premier Play Season as described in the Using Qualifier Points section below. At the start of each MTGO Premier Play Season, all unused QPs will be removed from players accounts and a new season will begin with its own Champions Showcase to qualify for!

# Leagues and Queues

The most common way players will earn QPs is through Leagues and Queues. Performing well in these events will provide players with QPs that they can accumulate over the course of the season. Once a player has earned enough QPs, they can use them to join any upcoming Premier Play event in the current season.

For more information on these *Magic Online* event types, check the [Events & Formats](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-events-formats) section of our online [Game Guide](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-game-guide).

# Preliminary Events

These 4-round scheduled events are designed for players who prefer a more traditional tournament style and want to earn their QPs in in larger quantities at once. Players who finish a Preliminary Event with either 4 wins earn 40 QPs, enough to enter an upcoming Premier Event.

Preliminary Events are scattered across the week on a set schedule and include a variety of formats, including most major constructed formats and current sealed deck limited. The full schedule can be found below. Please note, however, that this schedule may be adjusted as needed. Updates will be announced the weekly announcements on [https://mtgo.com/](https://mtgo.com/).

**Size**: 12-256 players

# Format Challenges

Held exclusively on the weekends, Format Challenges are Swiss-style scheduled events that feature a Top 8 playoff and provide a big tournament feel. These are great places to practice for Qualifiers since the structures are similar with the added benefit of earning a good number of QPs if you do well.

## Using Qualifier Points for MTGO Premier Play

After collecting at least 40 QPs over the course of the season, the next step is to find the Premier Play event that is right for you. There are a few options to choose from.

Please note:

*   In Season 1, players who hold an invitation to the Set Championship that is fed by a Qualifier or Super Qualifier are ineligible to compete in that tournament.
    *   This also includes top finishers from the previous Set Championship, players with 7 wins in an *MTG Arena* Qualifier Weekend feeding that Set Championship, and members of the *Magic* Pro League and Rivals League.
*   In Season 2, players already invited to the next Set Championship are eligible to compete in Qualifiers, Super Qualifiers, and Champions Showcase events unless otherwise noted below. This change takes effect April 14, 2021.
*   Each event in *Magic Online* has a maximum number of players (as indicated below) and events at the end of each season are likely to have more players. Once an event fills, no further entries will be allowed, so plan your QP usage accordingly.

Starting with Season 2, the Top 8 finishers of events that include playoff rounds will earn Leaderboard Points. *   April 14-August 11: Qualifiers, Super Qualifiers, Showcase Challenges
*   August 14-29: Showcase Qualifiers and Showcase Opens

Events that count for the Season 3 Leaderboard are:

*   August 14-TBD: Qualifiers, Super Qualifiers, Showcase Challenges
*   TBD: Showcase Qualifiers and Showcase Opens

Following the last Showcase Open of a season, the two players with the most Leaderboard Points who are not already qualified will earn invitations to the Champions Showcase as well as an upcoming Set Championship event on *MTG Arena*. Ties for Leaderboard invitations will be broken as follows, in order:

*   Most Top 8 finishes among events counting toward this Leaderboard
*   Most Leaderboard Points earned in a single eligible event
*   Most Qualifier Points earned during the Leaderboard's season *(e.g. for Season 2: April 14-August 11)*

## Other Rules

# Scheduling

Wizards of the Coast reserves the right to add, remove, modify, reschedule, and/or delete any tournament for any reason, including without limitation, if in its sole judgment, that tournament's integrity has been compromised by an unforeseen technical issue and/or error in that tournament's administration.

# Entry and Invitations

For all events that require Qualifier Points or an invitation to enter: if a player believes they should be able to enter the event and cannot, it is their responsibility to contact Customer Service before the event begins (see the Connectivity and Other Event Problems section below). Once the event begins, players cannot be added to the event.

Players who earn a Set Championship Series invitation or *Magic Online* Champions Showcase invitation from a *Magic Online* event will be contacted via the email account associated with their *Magic Online* account by a Wizards of the Coast representative within 72 hours of the qualifying event to verify account information and coordinate the invitation. If you plan on playing in the MTGO Premier Play Program, please ensure that your account information is correct.

For Season 1, players who have already earned a Set Championship Series invitation (through either tabletop or digital play) are not eligible to participate in further qualifying events (tabletop or digital) that lead to that same Set Championship. The only exception is a player can participate in a *Magic Online* event that includes both a Set Championship Series invitation and a *Magic Online* Champions Showcase invitation as part of the prizes. In this case, the player cannot earn two invitations to a Set Championship Series in the same tabletop season and any Set Championship Series invitation earned this way will pass down as indicated in the Prizes section below.

For Seasons 2-3, players who are already invited to the Set Championship fed by a Qualifier or Super Qualifier are eligible to compete in further events feeding that Set Championship. If a player would earn a redundant invitation to a Set Championship via a *Magic Online* event, that invitation will pass down as indicated in the Prizes section below.

For all Seasons, players who have already earned a *Magic Online* Champions Showcase Invitation are not eligible to participate in further qualification events for that *Magic Online* Premier Play Season's Champions Showcase.

# Connectivity and Other Event Problems

Players who experience issues during a premier play event should contact Customer Service as quickly as possible. Some events include coverage by Digital Event Coordinators (DECs) who can be contacted by joining the #DEC chat channel in the game client.

Wizards of the Coast is not responsible for the results of any disconnection or network problems experienced by players. Please refer to [these guidelines](/en/articles/magic-online-event-info/2014/07-30-guidelines-for-ptq-finals-and-mocs-finals) for more information on stability and connection issues in MTGO premier play.
