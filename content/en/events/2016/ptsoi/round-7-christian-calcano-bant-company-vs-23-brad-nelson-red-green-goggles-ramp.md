It must be hard not to get ahead of yourself when you kick off a Pro Tour with a perfect 3-0 Draft and then three straight wins in Constructed. You're locked for Day 2 and fighting for pole position. Of course, these two are old hands. They know to take it one match at a time.

Christian Calcano is best known as a fixture on the North American Grand Prix circuit. His resume boasts seven Grand Prix Top 8s, including two wins. Still, he has yet to play on a Pro Tour Sunday. This weekend, he's playing the Pro Tour's most popular deck, Bant Company.

Former Player of the Year Brad Nelson has a reputation as one of the winningest players in Standard Constructed. He's played with a variety of teams over the years, but most recently he ventured across the Atlantic to join up with Team EUreka. He's playing their Red-Green Goggles Ramp deck, which has made quite a stir.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/ptsoi/r7_calcano_nelson.jpg)  
*Two famous American players, globetrotter Christian Calcano and former Player of the Year Brad Nelson, showed how they got as far as they did unscathed in Round 7.*

# The Games

Game 1 kicked off with Nelson playing the control role, burning away Calcano's first two plays. Calcano managed to stick a [Tireless Tracker](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tracker), but unfortunately had no fourth land. Nelson played [Nissa's Pilgrimage](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%27s+Pilgrimage), netting three lands thanks to having Spell Mastery, and then pitched one to [Magmatic Insight](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magmatic+Insight). All Calcano could do was hit for three and pass the turn.

Nelson dropped his sixth mana and brought out [Chandra, Flamecaller](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Flamecaller), who immediately cleared out the Tracker. Nothing was coming from Calcano, and now the door was slamming shut. Chandra dealt 6 with a pair of elemental tokens, and [World Breaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=World+Breaker) set Calcano even further back. Unable to mount a defense, Calcano scooped.

Nelson sent back his opening seven in the second game, but stayed on six. Both players doled out their lands, and Nelson was first on the board with [Tireless Tracker](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tracker). [Declaration in Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Declaration+in+Stone) sent it packing before it could get out of hand. Nelson spent his fourth turn on a [Hedron Archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hedron+Archive) and cracked the clue after Calcano played [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy).

Nelson blasted the Planeswalker-in-training with [Dragonlord Atarka](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Atarka). Calcano could only buy time with [Reflector Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reflector+Mage). Nelson spent a turn on two [Nissa's Pilgrimage](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%27s+Pilgrimage)s and passed. Calcano passed it back. Again, Atarka made her appearance, burning the Mage. Calcano tapped the Dragonlord down with [Bounding Krasis](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bounding+Krasis), but that fell to [Fiery Impulse](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Impulse). He replaced it with yet another [Reflector Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reflector+Mage).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/ptsoi/r7_nelson.jpg)  
*Nelson's Red-Green Goggles Ramp deck is simply monstrous.*

Nelson brought out [Pyromancer's Goggles](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyromancer%27s+Goggles). Calcano hit for 2 and passed the turn back. Once again, [Dragonlord Atarka](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Atarka) hit play, but Calcano was ready with yet another Krasis and got in for t3 damage on his turn. For a moment, it looked like he might manage to tempo Nelson out. Nelson hit for 8 in the air, but another Krasis attack took him down to 8 life.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/ptsoi/r7_calcano.jpg)  
*Calcano sleeved up the deck with a target on its head—Bant Company—this weekend.*

Unfortunately for Calcano, Nelson had found additional action to force things along. He brought out [World Breaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=World+Breaker). Calcano risked tapping out for [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company), but it did not go well. Nelson surged out a [Fall of the Titans](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fall+of+the+Titans) for 8, doubled through the [Pyromancer's Goggles](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyromancer%27s+Goggles). Calcano nodded and extended the hand in defeat.

**Calcano 0 – Nelson 2**

"I think I messed up with the [Declaration in Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Declaration+in+Stone) on your Tracker," said Calcano. "I should've just bounced it with Mage."

"Yeah, I think that would've given you a better chance of winning that game," agreed Nelson.

I asked Calcano about his decision to play Bant Company this weekend. "You know, I looked at tournament results, my own testing, and it just seemed like Company was the best deck," he said. Was he concerned about coming into a tournament playing a deck with a target on its head? "Sure, people are going to try to metagame against it, but that's what I was trying to do and I couldn't figure it out, so hopefully neither did they." He shook his head and gestured where Nelson had been sitting. "That deck is just beautiful."
