**Hometown:**

**Profession:**

 Reanimator. It’s lit.

**What was the coolest deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Eldrazi since I never played it before.

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpcol16/t8_walton.jpg)**Christopher Walton

**Hometown:** Sort of Detroit (Milford, MI)

**Profession:**

**What deck did you play this weekend? Why?**  
 White-Blue Miracles, it has reasonable matches and allowed me to play maindeck [Back to Basics](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Back+to+Basics) as a haymaker that allows me game in some tight matches.

**What was the coolest deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Green-Blue Twelve Post. Easily my worst Matchup. I won, somehow without seeing a copy of [Back to Basics](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Back+to+Basics). Deck is powerful and interesting.

**What’s the most underrated card in Legacy right now?**  
 Likely [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic). I should have tested a Blade deck for this event.

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpcol16/t8_aaron.jpg)Aaron Kasprzak

**Hometown:** Toronto, ON

**Profession:** Investments

**What deck did you play this weekend? Why?**  
[Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder) Miracles because I’ve had a serious amount of practice with it in the last year and even in a [Chalice of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chalice+of+the+Void) meta the deck is still extremely powerful and consistent.

**What was the coolest deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Goblins on Day 1. I like that people are still playing that deck.

**What’s the most underrated card in Legacy right now?**  
[Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance).

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpcol16/t8_joe.jpg)Joe Lossett

**Hometown:** Newport Beach, CA

**Profession:** Streamer/Investor

**What deck did you play this weekend? Why?**  
 Legend Miracles, because the Year of Cavern never ends and I’m kind of in love with Venser.

**What was the coolest deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Bryant Cook shot me for a ton with [Grapeshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grapeshot) while simultaneously clearing my creatures. So I guess his TES deck was the coolest.

**What’s the most underrated card in Legacy right now?**  
[Null Rod](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Null+Rod). It is good against several totally different decks so it can be used often as a very high impact card.

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpcol16/t8_wilson.jpg)Wilson Hunter

**Hometown:** Johnson City, TN

**Profession:** Sales Representative

**What deck did you play this weekend? Why?**  
 Mentor Miracles. The [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm) show cast helped brew a list that we thought would be great in the current metagame.

**What was the coolest deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Storm. It’s the coolest deck in Legacy.

**What’s the most underrated card in Legacy right now?**  
[Monastery Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Mentor). People know the card is pretty good
