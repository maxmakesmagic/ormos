### Once More, With Feeling

* * *

After fixing the tournament bug that cancelled the [2,500-player tournament on March 22](/en/articles/feature/2003/03-12-another-tournament-announcement), the development team dug a little deeper and found some more bugs to quash. This past Wednesday, we ran a 4,096-player tournament simulation to prove that those bugs were fixed. (TestBot1601 won the tournament, but refused to comment on its deck.)

What does this mean to the average ***Magic** Online* player? Yep, you guessed it…

It's time for another free Constructed tournament!

### Oh No, Not Again

As we said in the [March 22 Server Crash Report](/en/articles/feature/2003/03-27-march-22-server-crash-report), we appreciate that many of you gave up your Saturday to join that tournament. We also understand that many of you might be reluctant to do so again. What we can guarantee is that we fixed the bug that forced us to cancel that tournament, and we've tested and proved that we can run large tournaments on the test server.

We've proven to the best of our ability that this tournament will run as planned. But sometimes a closed test doesn't tell you everything about how things work in the real world. We're asking the ***Magic** Online* community to help us test the system again so we can continue to improve the game.

This test will prove conclusively that the live ***Magic** Online* server can handle large tournaments. Why is that important? We want to make sure large tournaments are ready for set release events, championships, and other special events.

### Without Further Ado

On Saturday, April 12, 2003, the normal 9:00 AM (Pacific Standard Time) tournament will be replaced with a free Standard (with *LegionsTM*) Constructed tournament. Like the last one, there's no fee to enter, it has a maximum of 4,096 players, and the top 512 players will receive prizes.

And, because we know this issue is going to be raised, the success or failure of this stress test has no bearing on whether or not we run the [Worlds Qualifier tournaments](/en/articles/feature/2003/03-24-press-release) on ***Magic** Online.* We figured that, with so many of you interested in the qualifiers, you'd join a free tournament just for the playtesting opportunity. So we scheduled it before the weekend of April 25, instead of after. We want to test, you want to practice--it's a pretty good combination all around.

### What Could Go Wrong?

As we said above, our testing has shown that this tournament should go off as planned. However, there's always the chance we'll come across a bug that we couldn't find until we tested under real world conditions.

Our technical and customer service staff will be online, ready to catch any problems as they occur. Here's the lowdown on what'll happen if there are any issues.

If the tournament hiccups and won't run, we'll try restarting the tournament and, if that fails, we'll create a smaller version of the test tournament. If neither option works, we'll cancel the event, but won't be running the small tournaments we did last time. Instead, the development team will go squish the bugs we find and decide if we'll run another live stress test in the future.

If the ***Magic** Online* servers go down, the Adepts will post updates to the [message boards](https://web.archive.org/web/20030401000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://boards.wizards.com/tcg-bin/ultimatebb.cgi?category=14&origin=magic_magiconline&origin=magic_magiconline_news033103) so you all know what's happening. Any tournaments running when the servers go down have a 30-minute window for the servers to come back up. Within that window, all events will resume at the point they left off. Outside that time limit, all events that were running before the shutdown will be cancelled and all tickets and sealed product used for entry fees will be refunded.

For players not participating in the event, be aware that it's taking place on April 12 and that problems with the event may interrupt your games.

As always, if you have any difficulties due to the server going down, contact our [Technical Support group](https://web.archive.org/web/20041211074516/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/tspage).

### Thanks Again

Thank you for helping us make ***Magic** Online* a better game, we appreciate your time and support. Hope to see you on April 12 in the **Premier Events** room.
