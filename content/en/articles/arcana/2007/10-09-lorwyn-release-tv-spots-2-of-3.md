Another day, another batch of TV spots for the [*Lorwyn* release events](https://web.archive.org/web/20071009000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/releases). Check them out on Comedy Central and Adult Swim, and then this weekend, get your mitts on as many *Lorwyn* cards as you can carry. In fact, bring a backpack or something—it’s a really fun set to draft while watching *The Daily Show* or *Aqua Teen Hunger Force* off the TiVo. Trust us.

  
*[Adder-staff Boggart](https://web.archive.org/web/20071009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Adder-staff+Boggart)*

*[Goldmeadow Stalwart](https://web.archive.org/web/20071009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goldmeadow+Stalwart)*

In conclusion, *[Lorwyn release events](https://web.archive.org/web/20071009000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/releases) are this weekend at a store near you!*
