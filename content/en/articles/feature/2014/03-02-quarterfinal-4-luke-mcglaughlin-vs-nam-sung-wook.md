**Game One** 

In this classic matchup between Mono-Blue Devotion and Mono-Black Devotion, Nam swiftly clinched Game One before I could even get seated. After going down to five cards, McGlaughlin found himself facing one of the most dreaded openings his opponent could present in Standard.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Thoughtseize)](https://web.archive.org/web/20140302000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize)

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Pack+Rat)](https://web.archive.org/web/20140302000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)

 

 

After duplicating some rodents, McGlaughlin reached for his sideboard and Nam was up one Game just like that.

 

 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmel14/qf_mcglaughlin.jpg)  
*McGlaughlin tried to fight back after a double mulligan to no avail.* 

**Game Two**

McGlaughlin retaliated in Game two, opening aggressively with triple [Cloudfin Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140302000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudfin+Raptor) and double [Judge's Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140302000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge%27s+Familiar). However, Nam did what the Mono-Black Devotion deck ought to be doing and led with [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140302000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) and [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140302000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) once again.

With two [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140302000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault)s on his side, there was a good chance Nam could outrace McGlaughlin's flyers. Nam made a copy of [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140302000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat), and then another. Suddenly, McGlaughlin was facing a horde of 5/5s when turn 5 rolled around, and offered the handshake soon after.

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmel14/qf_nam.jpg)  
*Nam Sung-Wook* 

**Nam Sung-Wook defeats Luke, McGlaughlin and advances to the semifinals.**
