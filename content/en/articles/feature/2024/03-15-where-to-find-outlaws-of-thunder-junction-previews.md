[*Outlaws of Thunder Junction*](https://web.archive.org/web/20240315000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/outlaws-of-thunder-junction) card previews are just on the horizon, and the dust has yet to settle for Oko's rag-tag crew and our familiar *Magic* friends [in the *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* story](https://web.archive.org/web/20240315000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/story). So, saddle up. Prep your posse. Cue tumbleweed. This handy guide to preview season has you covered.

On March 26, 2024, beginning at 9 a.m. PT (12 p.m. ET), debut for *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* kicks off on the [official *Magic: The Gathering* YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC8ZGymAvfP97qJabgqUkz4A) and [twitch.tv/Magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic), where you'll get a rip-roarin' look at the mechanics, artwork, and designs from this new set.

For the most up-to-date list of preview outlets, bookmark this page and check back each day during *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* preview season! The set releases on April 19, 2024, and you can preorder *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* products now through your [local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20240315000000/https://locator.wizards.com/), online retailers like [Amazon](https://gtly.to/Q0IT0FBOU), and elsewhere *Magic* is sold.

[![](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/button_local_game_store_e83411_2.png)](https://web.archive.org/web/20240315000000/https://locator.wizards.com/) [![](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/button_amazon_e83411.png)](https://gtly.to/Q0IT0FBOU) 

And if you want to see all the cards in one place, you can check out the [*Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20240315000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/outlaws-of-thunder-junction/card-image-gallery)! We'll update the gallery every morning once previews begin, Monday through Friday, so it'll contain all the cards previewed the previous day (and earlier) but not the day of. We'll also be revealing some commons in the card image gallery throughout preview season, so keep an eye out!
