## *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* on *MTG Arena*

![Outlaws of Thunder Junction logo](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/splash_otj_3_packs.webp) 

The latest set is out—have you logged in to get your three *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* packs? Check your player inbox!

There's a lot happening with the new set, so don't miss these events:

*   [Ten new packets](https://web.archive.org/web/20240422000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/jump-in-packets-update-for-outlaws-of-thunder-junction) were recently added to ***Jump In!***
*   This is the last week for the **Best-of-Three *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Sealed** event. It ends April 29, so don't miss it!
*   The ***Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Quick Draft** event begins this Friday, April 26. Earn gems, packs, and build your season ranking.

You can see more of what's coming up in the [*MTG Arena* event calendar](https://web.archive.org/web/20240422000000/https://magic.wizards.com/mtgarena/events). 

Pick up new *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* phrases to use during matches, plus bundles of full-art landscape lands, stickers, and [loads more from the *MTG Arena* Store](https://pex8.adj.st/store/?adj_t=nr03woc&adj_deep_link=mtga%3A%2F%2Fplay.mtgarena.com%2Fstore%2F%3Fmtg_campaign%3DArena_MTGArena_OTJ_Daily%26mtg_adgroup%3Dwizards-web-otj&mtg_campaign=Arena_MTGArena_OTJ_Daily&mtg_adgroup=wizards-web-otj&adj_fallback=https%3A%2F%2Fmagic.wizards.com%2Fen%2Fmtgarena%3Futm_source%3Dwizards%26utm_medium%3Dweb%26utm_campaign%3Dotj-weekly-announcements-mtgarena&adj_redirect_macos=https%3A%2F%2Fmagic.wizards.com%2Fen%2Fmtgarena%3Futm_source%3Dwizards%26utm_medium%3Dweb%26utm_campaign%3Dotj-weekly-announcements-mtgarena&adj_redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fmagic.wizards.com%2Fen%2Fmtgarena%3Futm_source%3Dwizards%26utm_medium%3Dweb%26utm_campaign%3Dotj-weekly-announcements-mtgarena)!

There's even more to come, so don't let your mount go barn sour—saddle up and strike out into the new frontier!

## Bonny Pall Standard Festival April 23–30

![Festival: Bonny Pall Standard, April 23–30](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/OTJ%20Bonny%20Pall%20Festival%201920x1080.webp)

## Pro Tour Thunder Junction This Weekend in Seattle!

![](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/PTOTJ-1080x1080-Schedule.webp) 

The Pro Tour returns to the Emerald City when [Pro Tour Thunder Junction](https://magic.gg/events/pro-tour-thunder-junction) kicks off in Seattle, Washington, this Friday, April 26 with a trophy and $500,000 in prizes. There's history under that Space Needle, and you can take a nostalgic trip back in *Magic* time with Meghan Wolff's article, [Settle in for Seattle Tales](https://magic.gg/news/settle-in-for-seattle-tales).

Also, [Frank Karsten is back with his Metagame Mentor](https://magic.gg/news/metagame-mentor-predicting-standard-at-pro-tour-thunder-junction) guide to the top decks and more that'll carry you into the big event with analysis of the metagame now that *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* has arrived.

You can watch Pro Tour Thunder Junction streaming online at [twitch.tv/magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic)—for complete schedule and details, [check out the Pro Tour Thunder Junction viewers guide](https://magic.gg/news/pro-tour-thunder-junction-viewers-guide).

## Competitive Play Schedule

All times listed are Pacific time (UTC-07:00).

### Premier Play

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events.

Qualifier tokens are delivered to your *MTG Arena* inbox. Remember to claim them before the event starts!

#### May

*   **Qualifier Play-In (Best-of-One)**
     May 4, 6 a.m. PT

*   Format: *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Limited

*   **Qualifier Play-In (Best-of-Three)**
     May 10, 6 a.m. PT–May 11, 3 a.m. PT

*   Format: *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Limited

*   **Qualifier Weekend**
     May 11, 6 a.m. PT–May 12, 4 p.m. PT

*   Format: *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Limited

### Arena Open

The Arena Open Day One entry window begins at 6 a.m. PT and closes to new entries the following day at 3 a.m. PT (UTC-07:00). The Day Two entry window is 2 hours only, from 6 a.m. PT until 8 a.m. PT (UTC-07:00).

*   [**Arena Open: *Outlaws of Thunder Junction***](https://web.archive.org/web/20240422000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/arena-open-outlaws-of-thunder-junction-may-4-5)

    *   May 5: Day Two, *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Draft (Best-of-Three)
