Tomorrow, the new [*Theros* Holiday Gift Box](https://web.archive.org/web/20131116165742/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/holiday2013giftbox/productinfo) finally arrives on shelves! Pick it up and you can get four *Theros* booster packs and a special premium Karametra's Acolyte.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/bb/f1/bbf1a980f0237cb86490167e90665b891cbf14e0819419faf6dc8c327e586e99.jpg) 

  

This box is great for holding collections and storing miscellaneous cards from your collection!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a6/dd/a6dd4d3d72ad7dceb259d9caddb14fd03b835052506aefe1de00134496412a81.jpg) 

  

Note! You do not get the loose cards pictured above. Here is what is inside the box:

*   Four *Theros* booster packs
*   Beautiful storage box that can hold more than 2,000 **Magic** cards
*   Twenty *Theros* basic lands
*   Six illustrated plastic dividers
*   Sticker sheet for customizing the dividers
*   An alternate-art Karametra's Acolyte from the *Theros* set
