This past weekend the Grand Prix circuit stopped off at Kobe, Japan for its latest event, but that wasn't the first time the city has hosted major international play. Kobe has hosted multiple Grand Prix and Pro Tour tournaments over the years. This deck comes from [the Pro Tour](/en/events/2006/ptkob06/merkels-time-is-now) that took place there in 2006. It was won by relative unknown Jan Moritz-Merkel, who ripped through a loaded Top 8 to take down the title in his first appearance on the Sunday stage! The format for that tournament was *Time Spiral* Block Limited.
