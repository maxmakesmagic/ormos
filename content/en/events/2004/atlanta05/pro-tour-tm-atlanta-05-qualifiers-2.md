1.  Any team of three players with a combination of at least 50 pro points as of the most recent pro standing preceding the Pro Tour event. Teams formed in this manner must be announced at player registration before the tournament.
2.  The Top 4 finishing teams (who were previously uninvited) of each Grand Prix tournament that feeds the appropriate Pro Tour event.
3.  Teams (who were previously uninvited) who earn invitations through appropriate Qualifier tournaments.
4.  Any team with a DCI three-person Limited rating of 1750 or more as of the publish January 26th, 2005
