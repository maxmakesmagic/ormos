It's time for *Jumpstart 2022* previews! To help our readers and preview seekers, we've created this handy guide to preview season.

![Jumpstart 2022 hero image](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/j22_hero.jpg) 

This page will help you follow along with everything coming by giving you the outlets where upcoming *Jumpstart 2022* previews are planned on each day. The goal is to help you find what you're looking for every day and avoid engaging in previews if you choose.

Below, you'll find a list of dates as well as any outlets previewing a card on that date. Once the previews are live, we'll update this article so that you can easily find them. If the link simply goes to the front page of a website or social media site, that means they probably haven't previewed the card yet.

And if you just want to see all the cards that have been officially previewed in one place, you can always check out our [*Jumpstart 2022* Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2022/11-21-jumpstart-2022-card-image-gallery). Note that these galleries are updated every morning, Monday through Friday, so they contain all cards previewed the previous day (and earlier), but not the day of.

For the most up-to-date listings, bookmark this page and check back regularly for all the latest previews from *Jumpstart 2022*!

Ready for fun themes, wild combos, and fast *Magic* for your next play night? Jump into *Jumpstart 2022* with preorders available now at your [local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20221121000000/https://locator.wizards.com/), online at [Amazon](https://gtly.to/hKdH21k7l), and elsewhere *Magic* is sold!
