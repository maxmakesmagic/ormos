After 15 hard-fought rounds of Modern, with a total of 1,545 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running. Their Top 8 performance came with invitations and airfare to Pro Tour *Ravnica Allegiance* ... and the opportunity to fight for the title and the trophy.

Learn more about Grand Prix Atlanta's Top 8 competitors—including who they are, why they chose their Modern deck, and more—by taking a look through the Top 8 profiles!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpatl18/GP_ATL18_Juza.JPG)

**Martin Jůza**

**Age**: 31

**Occupation**: *Magic* pro player

**Hometown**: Plzen, Czech Republic

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments**: 4 Pro Tour Top 8s, Hall of Fame class of 2017.

**What Modern deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?** Hollow Boys. It's great and it can easily win through the sideboard hate.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20181104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt)! Just ask my opponents.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Leyline of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20181104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leyline+of+the+Void). It just wins games on its own.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** 4-8 pro points, 1,500-10,000 USD.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpatl18/GP_ATL18_PeiyuanZheng.JPG)

**Peiyuan Zheng**

**Age**: 18

**Occupation**: Student

**Hometown**: Beijing, China

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments**: Lost two consecutive RPTQ win-and-PT. Several GP Top 32s.

**What Modern deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?** Bant Spirits. I like this [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20181104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company) deck.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20181104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company). I feel excited when using it because you don't know what you will get.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Knight of Autumn](https://web.archive.org/web/20181104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+Autumn), a creature that can handle multitasks.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** Going to the PT, and earning money for a plane ticket to GP Portland.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpatl18/GP_ATL18_Larsen.JPG)

**Christoffer Larsen**

**Age**: 30

**Occupation**: Navy/school

**Hometown**: Copenhagen, Denmark

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments**: Got to my plane last weekend :) Playing Nassif in the semis \[of Grand Prix Lille\].

**What Modern deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?** Played [Hardened Scales](https://web.archive.org/web/20181104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hardened+Scales).

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Mishra's Bauble](https://web.archive.org/web/20181104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mishra%27s+Bauble). Best card to side out.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** Not sure what to Claim.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** Steak Dinner!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpatl18/GP_Atl18_Negri.JPG)

**Kazu Negri**

**Occupation**: Professional turn-3 killer

**Hometown**: Morgantown, WV

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments**: Won an Open, handful of 12-3s, that's about it.

**What Modern deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?** Infect. After nearly a year of playing Jeskai Control, I didn't feel like working as hard for wins.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Distortion Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20181104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Distortion+Strike). It makes [Glistener Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20181104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glistener+Elf) suck a bit less.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Distortion Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20181104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Distortion+Strike)! I wish I had 4.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** I'm excited as hell.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpatl18/GP_ATL18_JoaoChoca.JPG)

**Joao Choca**

**Age**: 27

**Occupation**: Marketer

**Hometown**: London, UK

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments**: Grand Prix Turin 2018 champion.

**What Modern deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?** KCI. Rob Catton gave me a 75 with sideboard plans, and I didn't want to waste time thinking about Modern.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Sai, Master Thopterist](https://web.archive.org/web/20181104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sai%2C+Master+Thopterist)—literally won every game I resolved it.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** Same as above, except also Game 2/3s—Sai won every game she appeared in.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** Almost Silver!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpatl18/GP_ATL18_Utsunomiya.JPG)

**Yoshihiko Ikawa** 

**Age**: 32

**Occupation**: Fleeter

**Hometown**: Tokyo, Japan

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments**: Pro Tour San Diego 2010 Top 8

**What Modern deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?** Tron. I love this deck.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20181104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulamog%2C+the+Ceaseless+Hunger). Three Ulamogs are the best!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpatl18/GP_ATL18_Glogowski.JPG)
