While Jund has been grabbing a lot of Standard headlines, Pro Tour–Hollywood champion Charles Gindy is firmly in the Naya camp when it comes to winning. He took this list to the top spot at a recent StarCityGames.com Open event. He bested over 400 players to win the first live tournament he had attended since the 2009 World Championships in Rome. With powerful spells like Bloodbraid Elf, Ranger of Eos, and Wild Nacatl, it's no surprise Gindy managed to take the title!
