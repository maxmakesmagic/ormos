*Sideboard Magazine* Online is a central hub for information. Here you will find statistics, rosters, lists, data and results from many **Magic**-related areas.

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/ENinformation_for_players.jpg)

[Banned/Restricted Lists](https://web.archive.org/web/20090302124521/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=judge/resources/banned) - The complete list of all cards banned or restricted in any format  
[Oracle Card Reference](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603133503/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=dci/oracle) - The official wordings of all **Magic** cards  
[Constructed Decklist](http://www.wizards.com/DCI/downloads/DecklistConstructed.pdf) - Decklist for Constructed Tournaments  
[Official Tournament Rules](/en/articles/event-coverage/2002/12-29-2003-2004-dci-rules) - Official rules for all DCI-Sanctioned tournaments  
[Amateur Player Status](https://web.archive.org/web/20090306224225/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=sideboard/facts/amateurs) - All players on this list have earned 1 or more pro points and have therefore lost their amateur status. (last updated 11/10/2003)  
[Rulings and Errata](https://web.archive.org/web/20090306224225/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.crystalkeep.com/magic/rules/summaries.html&origin=) - Stephen D'Angelo's compilation of all clarifications ever issued for **Magic: The Gathering**

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/ENtournament_results.jpg)

[Pro Player of the Year Race](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2003-2004-player-of-the-year-race) - Rickard Osterberg has the early lead in the 2003-2004 Player of the Year race. *(Updated 11/10/2003)*  
[Rookie of the Year](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2003-2004-rookie-of-the-year-race) - Tomohiro Kaji has the lead for Rookie of the Year. *(Updated 11/10/2003)*  
[Lifetime Winnings Leaders](https://web.archive.org/web/20090604115608/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=sideboard/facts/winnings) - Everyone who's won more than $25,000 on the Pro Tour. *(Updated 11/10/2003)*  
[Grand Prix Top 8 Lists](https://web.archive.org/web/20090604163026/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=sideboard/facts/top8gp) - Top 8 lists from all Grand Prix events. *(Updated 10/6/2003)*  
[Pro Tour Top 8 Listing](https://web.archive.org/web/20090604163034/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=sideboard/facts/top8pt) - A complete list of all Top 8 lists from Pro Tour stops and World Championships. *(Updated 10/6/2003)*  
[Continental Championship Top 8 Lists](https://web.archive.org/web/20090306224225/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=sideboard/facts/top8cc) - Top 8 lists from all Continental Championships. *(Updated 7/21/03)*
