Each guildmage embodies the core values of its guild, and the [Izzet](https://web.archive.org/web/20060116000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/guildpact/izzet) representative is no exception -- in this case, creativity, passion, brilliance, and just a touch of mania. Part of the art description for *Guildpact's* **Izzet Guildmage** ([previewed today](https://web.archive.org/web/20060116000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/mr211) by Mark Rosewater) summarizes the wizard as looking "smart and a little crazy," which Jim Murray nailed in the art.

*Izzet Guildmage sketch by Jim Murray*

![Izzet Guildmage final art](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/fd/e1fd0688d852b7393b53c608f38883543e3685f2a4c4716dfc1ab1c032df0fca.jpg)  
*Izzet Guildmage final art by Jim Murray*

The *mechanics* of the [card](https://web.archive.org/web/20060116000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/making/mr211_czwsbv4s2spnv4vw.jpg), of course, leave you thinking, "What two-mana instants and sorceries can I copy with this?" You may already be familiar with the usual *instant* suspects at converted mana cost two and under, due to [Isochron Scepter](https://web.archive.org/web/20060116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Isochron+Scepter), but you might want to take a stroll through Gatherer for another look at the sorceries...

Of course, at this writing, Gatherer doesn't show off the goods from *Guildpact*. Stay tuned until after the [prerelease](https://web.archive.org/web/20060116000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/prerelease/guildpact) for that.

Are there other examples of "smart and a little crazy" in Izzet art? Do you think we'd put that question there if the answer weren't "oh heck yes"?
