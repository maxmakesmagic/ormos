Dreadful. 

  
  

Full of Dread. 

That's how the ancient Greeks felt about gorgons. So much so that the name they gave them actually translates as "dreadful." In **Magic**, Gorgons are just as dreadful because they either have deathtouch or a very deathtouchy ability. 

  

While in myth there were only three gorgons, sisters actually, **Magic** makes [Gorgon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;subtype=+%5B%22Gorgon%22%5D) a creature subtype, just like [Minotaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;subtype=+%5B%22Minotaur%22%5D) and [Cyclops](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;subtype=+%5B%22Cyclops%22%5D) (there are also multiple [Cerberuses](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;name=%7C%5B%22hound+of+Griselbrand%22%5D%7C%5Bcerberus%5D)). While we aren't limited to just three gorgons like the Greeks, we still have some pretty unique Gorgons. 

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/0c/d0/0cd0820a6be5bb18f1a532db7ab70ca0a951be7fa4b099b5244ea18edb80f1a3.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;subtype=+%5b%22Gorgon%22%5damp;type=+%5b%22Legendary%22%5damp;name=+!%5bcruel%5d)  

And of course, the powerful planeswalking Gorgon:

  

On Theros, folks better keep their eyes down. Seems to me like there are gorgons everywhere! And there's something different about them; Theros gorgons have the lower body of a snake! That certainly would make it easier to tell them apart from your average *femme fatale*. 

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/2a/60/2a6068539d09575f94e3f7cf7dff458eeef2b7bf36639bd38b9f86ab45a58d5b.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;subtype=+%5b%22Gorgon%22%5damp;set=%7c%5b%22Theros%22%5damp;type=+!%5b%22Legendary%22%5d)  

Before you get upset, I didn't forget about the beautifully dangerous [Hythonia the Cruel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hythonia+the+Cruel)—I just wanted to make sure you knew this was available: 

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/8a/61/8a61c2d0a520f3d19032c957df530142809b8ac5c58db3f22057755a40325109.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/activity/1353)  

This is all ancient history, though. What we really want to see is the new *Born of the Gods* Gorgon, right? Well, here she is. Don't be fooled by her pretty face, she is pretty powerful!
