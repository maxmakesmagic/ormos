When the [**Magic** Pro Tour Hall of Fame](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/tournaments-events-trading-card-game-magic-the-gathering-2) inducted its first class in 2005, the inductees were Jon Finkel, Darwin Kastle, Tommi Hovi, Olle Råde, and Alan Comer. Alan Comer's [Hall of Fame profile](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/alan-comer-magic-pro-tour-hall-of-fame) includes his five Top 8s and numerous innovative decks. But it doesn't mention his heart.
