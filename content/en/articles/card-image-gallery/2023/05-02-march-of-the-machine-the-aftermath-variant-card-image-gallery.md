Check out all the Booster Fun variants and promo cards from [*March of the Machine: The Aftermath*](https://web.archive.org/web/20230502000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/march-of-the-machine-the-aftermath) below. You can see all the regular cards in our [*March of the Machine: The Aftermath* Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/05-02-march-of-the-machine-the-aftermath-card-image-gallery).

Note that cards with collector numbers 101 through 150 feature the foil-etched treatment and collector numbers 186 through 228 feature the Halo foil treatment.

*March of the Machine: The Aftermath* releases May 12 as a follow-up to [*March of the Machine*](https://web.archive.org/web/20230502000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/march-of-the-machine) and will be available at your [local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20230502000000/https://locator.wizards.com/), online through [Amazon](https://gtly.to/God2e8cdO), and elsewhere *Magic* is sold.
