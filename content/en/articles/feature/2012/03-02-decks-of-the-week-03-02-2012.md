At Grand Prix Madrid, eventual winner Ivo Grossholz began his Top 8 draft with a [Huntmaster of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20120302000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Huntmaster+of+the+Fells) and later picked up a [Mayor of Avabruck](https://web.archive.org/web/20120302000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mayor+of+Avabruck) for some undeniable Werewolf power. Ultimately, though, it was the relatively unassuming [Fling](https://web.archive.org/web/20120302000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fling) that won him the tournament. On the other side of the Atlantic, Pro Tour *Dark Ascension* Top 8er Matt Costa steered the only Delver deck in the Grand Prix Baltimore Top 8 to ultimate victory with the help of a constant flow of information via [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20120302000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe).
