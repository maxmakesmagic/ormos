Robert Cash is a player who loves him some tokens! His Sacramento, California PTQ Top 8 deck featured a whopping *five* different token generators in the maindeck! What did he *do* with his tokens? Why, he used his Furystoke Giants and Torrents of Souls to annihilate his opponents post haste. Find out which cards from the exciting new set *Shards of Alara* you could add to Robert’s deck with the [*Shards of Alara* Visual Spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20090601212653/https://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/shardsofalara/spoiler).
