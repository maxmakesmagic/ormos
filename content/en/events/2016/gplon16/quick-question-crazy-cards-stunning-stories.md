For a little fun, we asked around for cool stories about some of the more creative cards doing something spectacular in Limited. Did anyone see [Aetherflux Reservoir](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherflux+Reservoir) in action, have a massive [Electrostatic Pummeler](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrostatic+Pummeler) do a bit of pummeling, or go deep with [Madcap Experiment](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Madcap+Experiment) or [Dubious Challenge](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dubious+Challenge)?

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gplon16/gplon16_qq_snepvangers.jpg)

**Hall of Famer Bram Snepvangers:** I try not to play these cards. I guess, [Dubious Challenge](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dubious+Challenge) might work if you have a lot of bounce ...

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gplon16/gplon16_qq_martin.jpg)

**Pro Tour Prague 2006 quarterfinalist Quentin Martin:** I hope I get the chance! I've always loved making some wacky, underrated rare work. Like when you used to get [Mindmoil](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindmoil) as a fifteenth pick. In this format, so far, the best I've done was to draw my own [Chief of the Foundry](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chief+of+the+Foundry), steal my opponent's with [Confiscation Coup](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Confiscation+Coup), and copy both with [Saheeli's Artistry](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Saheeli%27s+Artistry) ...

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gplon16/gplon16_qq_carvalho.jpg)

**2016 World Championship finalist Márcio Carvalho:** Doesn't involve any weird card, but my first loss \[yesterday\] was against turn one [Mana Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Vault), turn two [Verdurous Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Verdurous+Gearhulk). How do you beat an 8/8 with trample on turn two?

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gplon16/gplon16_qq_huschenbeth.jpg)

**Grand Prix Rimini 2016 champion: Arne Huschenbeth:** I once lost against a Draft deck with two [Panharmonicon](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panharmonicon)s, three [Cloudblazer](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudblazer)s, two [Visionary Augmenter](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Visionary+Augmenter)s, [Angel of Invention](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel+of+Invention), and [Wispweaver Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wispweaver+Angel). I am not making this up.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gplon16/gplon16_qq_juza.jpg)

**24-time Grand Prix Top 8er Martin Jůza:** I was gonna build an [Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel) deck in Sealed! I had so many great enablers and cross synergies, but my pool simply had nothing big to marvel into. The largest creature was a 4/5. If I'd had anything, any Gearhulk basically or better yet [Metalwork Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metalwork+Colossus), my deck would have looked something like this:
