Angels have been part of the **Magic** Multiverse since the earliest of days with the well-known Serra Angel. While she might be the most famous Angel in **Magic**, she’s far from the only one. Today’s quiz is all about Angels.

  

* * *

  
 ![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/arc1300_warning.jpg)

**The Mistform Ultimus Disclaimer** – Every time I do one of these quizzes I inevitably get emails and tweets saying how I forget about the changelings and Mistform Ultimus.

This is my official disclaimer and recognition that I am purposefully ignoring them for the purposes of this quiz, and as such you should too.

  

* * *

  

Q**1.** What Angel is the mythic rare Angel in ***Magic** 2014* Core Set?

*(Difficulty: Easy)* 

[50/50 Hint](#) - Click this to eliminate two wrong answers.

*   [Archangel of Thune](#) - RIGHT - Archangel of Thune made quite the splash when it was revealed and continues to carry on the tradition of combat-ready Angels in **Magic**.
*   [Baneslayer Angel](#) - WRONG - First printed in ***Magic** 2010* Baneslayer Angel proved to be quite the powerhouse in competition, where Brian Kibler famously raced Progenitus with it.
*   [Seraph of the Sword](#) - WRONG - Seraph of the Sword is indeed in ***Magic** 2014* Core Set, but as a rare, not a mythic rare.
*   [Adakar Valkyrie](#) - WRONG - Adarkar Valkyrie was just rereleased in *Modern Masters*, but it did not make an appearance in ***Magic** 2014* Core Set.

  

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?multiverseid=370627&type=card)

Q**2.** Which color is the only color NOT to have a monocolored Angel?

*(Difficulty: Easy)* 

[50/50 Hint](#) - Click this to eliminate two wrong answers.

*   [White](#) - WRONG - Really? That's what you're going with?
*   [Blue](#) - WRONG - Nope sorry. Up until 2012 this was one of the correct answers, but with *Planechase (2012 edition)* it gets its own Angel in Illusory Angel.
*   [Green](#) - RIGHT - While there are a handful of Angels who have green in their casting costs, there are no mono-green Angels.
*   [Colorless](#) - WRONG - There are only two colorless Angels, but interestingly there are three artifact Angels.

Q**4.** How many artists have illustrated Serra Angel on tournament-playable Serra Angel cards?

*(Difficulty: Vorthos)* 

[50/50 Hint](#) - Click this to eliminate two wrong answers.

*   [2](#) - WRONG - Try again!
*   [3](#) - RIGHT - Read more below!
*   [4](#) - WRONG - Keep guessing!
*   [5](#) - WRONG - Nope, not this one.

The correct answer is three versions are tournament legal. Douglas Shuler's original painting was the face of Serra Angel from Alpha to *Fourth Edition* and remains one of the most well known pieces of art in **Magic**. After sitting out *Fifth* and *Sixth Editions*, Serra Angel returned in *Seventh* and *Eighth Edition* with card art by Mark Zug. Then with *Ninth Edition* Greg Staples's painting became the current face of Serra Angel we know.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/arc1300_serraangels.jpg) 

  

Rebecca Guay illustrated a comic book about Serra Angel which included an *oversized* (and thus not tournament-legal) Serra Angel which she illustrated. The comic book was distributed in 1996 and that oversized Serra Angel has become one of the game's most sought-after oversized cards.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/arc1300_guay.jpg)
