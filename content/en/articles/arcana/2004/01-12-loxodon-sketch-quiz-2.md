Elephants are said to have long memories. Today we test *your* memory of cards featuring Loxodons, the race of humanoid elephants from the plane of Mirrodin. Guess all these Loxodon cards from their art, and win a date with [Iron Tusk Elephant](https://web.archive.org/web/20040112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Iron+Tusk+Elephant)!

But to make things a little more challenging, we'll only provide you with the sketches that were initially submitted for each card. Happy guessing!
