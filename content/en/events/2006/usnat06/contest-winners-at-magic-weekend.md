Magic Weekend 2006 was a smashing success. Congratulations to all the winners, including those in the Contest of the Year and the Create Your Own Magic contests.

### Contest of the Year

**Richard Garfield's socks:** Tony Petrocillo  
**Framed original of Adarkar Valkyrie by Jeremy Jarvis:** Robert Mangum  
**4x Ice Age Block:** David Joshua Pruitt

### Create Your Own Magic!

Each category awarded a $1,000 scholarship to first prize, and the top three finalists received one-on-one discussion with the judges of their respective categories.

**Game Design:**  
 1. Jed Schmidt  
 2. Dylan Fay  
 3. Justin McDonald

**Fiction:**  
 1. [Dylan Fay](/en/articles/arcana/2006/08-10-create-your-own-magic-fiction-winner-dylan-fay)  
 2. Justin McDonald  
 3. Tom Emmons

**Art:**  
 1. Danielle Mansur  
 2. John Garman  
 3. Daniel Garman

**Online Writing:**  
 1. [Anthony Izzo](/en/events/2006/usnat06/create-your-own-magic-internet-writing)  
 2. Matt Nickolai  
 3. Justin McDonald

Full rules for the Create Your Own Magic contest can be found [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20090604030417/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/jss/06contestrules).
