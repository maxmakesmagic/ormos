The celebrated and wise planeswalker Teferi made his initial appearance in card names and flavor text way back in *Mirage*, in cards like [Teferi's Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20041215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%27s+Imp), [Teferi's Curse](https://web.archive.org/web/20041215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%27s+Curse) and [Reality Ripple](https://web.archive.org/web/20041215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reality+Ripple). He was referenced lightly during Urza block ([Stern Proctor](https://web.archive.org/web/20041215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stern+Proctor), [Disruptive Student](https://web.archive.org/web/20041215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disruptive+Student)) and once in Masques block (just [Excavation](https://web.archive.org/web/20041215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Excavation) as far as we can tell), but he didn't show up in earnest in **Magic** *art* until *Invasion*.

In that set, Teferi was depicted with a characteristic robe, headdress and staff. Check it out:

[Exclude](https://web.archive.org/web/20041215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Exclude)  
[Teferi's Care](https://web.archive.org/web/20041215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Teferi%2527s%2BCare)  
[Teferi's Moat](https://web.archive.org/web/20041215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Teferi%2527s%2BMoat)

Note how Teferi holds back those bad guys above. Can you tell what group that's supposed to be across the moat? Here's a [hint](https://web.archive.org/web/20041215000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/symbol-phyrexia-2004-02-16).

You can also see Teferi on [Disrupt](https://web.archive.org/web/20041215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disrupt) and [Teferi's Response](https://web.archive.org/web/20041215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%27s+Response), among others. (Teferi also made a [Teferi's Puzzle Box](https://web.archive.org/web/20041215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Teferi%2527s%2BPuzzle%2BBox) once. Look, a Puzzle Week tie-in!)
