**North America & Latin America – November 11-13, 2005  
 Europe & Asia Pacific – December 9-11, 2005**  
  

* * *

### ![](https://i.ormos.uk/30/72/3072e56621e4413b0b7e108b7a2ef5520f7b79ae5ceb612e2434d9358c89bbaf.jpg)When

**North America and Latin America:** Tournaments will be held Friday, November 11th or Saturday, November 12th or Sunday, November 13th. Call the location nearest you for exact date and time information ([schedule posted below](#1)).

**Europe and Asia Pacific:** Tournaments will be held Friday, December 9th or Saturday, December 10th or Sunday, December 11th. *(Specific locations coming soon)*

### Entry Fee & Product Mix

*   The entry fee should include product use and any additional product use for prize support. Call the location nearest you for exact Entry Fee information.
*   Cost of entry will provide Teams with, one (1) Tournament pack and four (4) boosters. (Or 7 boosters per team)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9e/23/9e2390d340631292fd5a81d711cc3207688006986f3bf0f16df099c58393cae4.jpg)

### Tournament Structure/Format

*   The structure of this tournament is modified Swiss-style.
*   The format for this tournament is Sealed Deck.
*   Teams build two 40-card minimum decks *(1 Tournament Pack & 4 Boosters)*

### Prizes

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f0/e0/f0e05d422eca9dee388952ed30798134e3ff26cee913839e58fd7a6abebef5a4.jpg)
*   First place Team wins an exclusive Two-Headed Dragon Deck box. (1 each)
*   The first 16 players receive an “Underworld Dreams” foil promo card (while supplies last).
*   New Player – If one or both members of a team are new players to the store they both receive an exclusive **Magic**® Beanie. *(Limited to the first four teams with a new player.)*

### Registration Information

*   Players who want more specific local tournament or registration information should call their local tournament location.
*   Players who have general questions about the **Magic: The Gathering**® TCG, Wizards of the Coast® tournaments, the **Magic: The Gathering Pro Tour**TM circuit, the ArenaTM league, the DCITM players’ organization, Qualifier tournaments, the **Magic** World Championships, National Championships, State Championships, or Regionals, should call Wizards of the Coast Customer Service at (800) 324-6496, or go to, [http://wizards.custhelp.com](http://wizards.custhelp.com/).
