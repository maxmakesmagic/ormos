Yesterday [we revealed the full contents](/en/articles/feature/2014/08-18-duel-decks-speed-vs-cunning) of [*Duel Decks: Speed vs. Cunning*](https://web.archive.org/web/20140819000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/game-info/products/card-set-archive/duel-decks-speed-vs-cunning), including a number of preview cards planned for *Khans of Tarkir*. But what might pass by people's new-card radar is the new art for two popular cards: [Repeal](https://web.archive.org/web/20140819000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Repeal) and [Goblin Bombardment](https://web.archive.org/web/20140819000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Bombardment).

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/features/aasdjb_DDN/en_lxxyp1rzpj.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/features/aasdjb_DDN/en_kwujyg17hf.png)

But let's take a closer look at the new art and figure out what's going on.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/cardart/cardart_repeal_ddn.jpg)

[Repeal](https://web.archive.org/web/20140819000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Repeal) | [Anthony Palumbo](https://web.archive.org/web/20140819000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5b%22Anthony+Palumbo%22%5d)

According to the art description, what we're seeing here is a demon being sent back to wherever it is demons come from. The monks inside are the ones [Repeal](https://web.archive.org/web/20140819000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Repeal)ing the demon with blue light coming out of his chest. Neat.

Meanwhile, the art on [Goblin Bombardment](https://web.archive.org/web/20140819000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Bombardment) from *Tempest* is an old-school favorite, but the new version is a pretty cool update.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/cardart/cardart_goblinbombardment_ddn.jpg)

[Goblin Bombardment](https://web.archive.org/web/20140819000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Bombardment) | [Dave Kendall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140819000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5b%22Dave%20Kendall%22%5d)

Here, instead of seeing goblins loading themselves into siege weaponry just before being fired, we see a flaming goblin being hurled off a cliff, seemingly at a distant enemy.

Can't wait to get your hands on these new versions of classic cards? [*Duel Decks: Speed vs. Cunning*](https://web.archive.org/web/20140819000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/game-info/products/card-set-archive/duel-decks-speed-vs-cunning) releases September 5.
