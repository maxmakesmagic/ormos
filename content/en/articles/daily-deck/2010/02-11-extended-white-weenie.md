White Weenie is an archetype as old as the game itself, and its representatives have graced this page more than once. But Patrizio Golia did something on **Magic Online** that few players would have thought possible at the beginning of the Extended season: Top 8ed with the archetype at the Pro Tour Qualifier level. The deck features powerful two-drop creatures like Samurai of the Pale Curtain and Ethersworn Canonist, which can significantly impact some of the more popular archetypes in the format. Patrizio also had Mana Tithe for the occasional blowout. The counter could frequently catch an unassuming opponent off guard, punishing them for tapping low against a white deck and assuming the coast was clear.
