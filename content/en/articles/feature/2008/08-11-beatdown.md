### Collector's Highlights

*   A premium creature card featuring never-before-seen art included in each deck.
*   Two 2-sided SpindownTM life counters.
*   A two-sided locker poster.

### Game Features

*   Two 61-card prebuilt decks containing five rare cards each.
*   Two 2-sided SpindownTM life counters.
*   A full-color booklet containing play tips and creature histories.

G**et ready for a beating!**

The Beatdown Gift Box for **Magic: The Gathering** is here, and it is ONE BAD-ASS SET. Don't believe us? Take it up with creatures like [Mahamoti Djinn](https://web.archive.org/web/20080811000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mahamoti+Djinn), [Erhnam Djinn](https://web.archive.org/web/20080811000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Erhnam+Djinn), and [Sengir Vampire](https://web.archive.org/web/20080811000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sengir+Vampire).

It's BIGGER, too. Bigger creatures. Bigger combat. Bigger hurt! Grab a friend and tear them apart. ("Get well" card not included.)
