### Top 5 - What You Think Is The Best Card and Why

**1st prize**  
*Brandon Mitchell, North Carolina, US*

In my opinion the best Magic Card of all time would have to be a fairly easy card to get. Like Birds of Paradise (BOPs) its a one forest mana costing 0/1 bird. But the fact that you can tap it to produce any one color of mana of your choice. That's great in its own. But what better decoy could you possible use. As soon as someone sees a 0/1 bird come down, bang! Shock! Or if it comes down with pumps on it smother. No one likes it when a Birds of Paradise comes into play and there is nothing you can do to stop it. I play with Birds of Paradise in almost every deck I own. If they cant target it because of some steely Resolve or some other effect that makes it unable to be targeted then they can watch as the almighty taps for mana. How scary can that be? Scary enough if the Birds of Paradise has like a "Might of Oaks" or a "Pemmin's Aura" or just maybe an Alpha Status with Illusions floating around. Either way you look at it.

Birds of Paradise is great and by far my Top Pick for Bearclaw's Power 9. If BOPs were not a great card, then how come they continue to this day, reprint the Birds of Paradise if wasn't as great of a card as I said it was. In the future I see the Birds of Paradise being reprinted in a base set somewhere along the lines. In conclusion, my cry for the Bird's Voice to be heard as one of the original Power 9's as it should have been must become one. I'm not talking about someone's Power 9 list. I'm talking about Magic the Gathering's Power 9 listing should and one day hope to include the God of birds, "Birds of Paradise". Thank You!

**2nd prize**  
*Aaron Porath, British Columbia, Canada*

My favourite card. No other card embodies more aspects of the game, in both flavour and history. With a cartoonish image, many might overlook the card, but Phelddagrif has more going for it than most cards can say. It has R&D's love of anagrams, in homage to the game's creator \[Garfield, PHD\]. It is a "gold" bordered card, signifying how nothing in magic, or even in life, is simple, it is often many things working together for a greater purpose.

With a legendary status, it brings to front another unique aspect of our game, the legend status. What better way to make Magic's "god" \[creator\] than to incarnate him as a legend?

At a converted mana cost of only 4, it is a bargain for a 4/4, but offset by the making of it a gold creature. While a simple concept now, it was one of the pioneers of this now-commonplace costing. it even helps reflect aspects of each of it's colours \[twice!\] in it's 3 abilities. With white, it shows us that white has flying, and that white is the colour of lifegain, giving the opponent life for it's activation. Blue brings in it's trickery aspect, with it's card drawing, and it's "don't touch me" attitude of returning it to owners hand. With green, you have green's common ability of trample, along with it's ability to generate hordes of mini creatures. How many other cards can boast this many different aspects, all loaded in to a single card? And that's not all! It's a multiplayer's dream!

Politicing is easy - just stay on people's bright side by lending them some hippos, or maybe they need that extra life..... make some friends, and stay in the game! And we don't even stop there! While it stayed in a the shadows for a while, you couldn't keep this guy caged. When Planeshift came out, this guy begged for another chance at stardom! He was "reborn" as the Questing Phelddagrif, so that a new generation could come to appreciate the beauty of this creature. Leaner and meaner, it still had the original phelddagrif's feel. I think that all generations of players should at one time or another be able to play with a Phelddagrif. It's just a real cool, well, phelddagrif.

Phelddagrif pioneered so many things that we now use in everyday Magic. What card advantage was to "the Deck", and the mana curve is to "sligh" Pheldagriff is to the players and history of magic. Obvious now, but groundbreaking in it's day. 

These, and other things make Phelddagriff my favourite card in the game. And, to sum it up with one of my favourite sayings..... Come on, it's a purple flying hippo! What's not to like?!

So, sit back, grab some phelddagrifs, and get playing this fabulous game of ours!

**3rd prize**  
*Nick Bedo, California, US*

You are playing against the snobby rich kid at Friday Night Magic at your local card store. He is bragging about how he has the "Power 9" in his deck and how he can kill anyone first turn with his combo. So you decide to challenge him, to see what he can really do. First turn he lays down a Black Lotus, a Mox Ruby, a Mox Emerald, and a Mountain. He laughs at you and calls everyone over to watch him kill you with a Ball Lightning/Blood Lust/Berserk combo. He sacrifices the Lotus to cast the Ball Lightning, and he is smiling the whole time. Then he stops smiling because he sees you put a Counterspell off to the side and move your life counter down to 19. Force of Will hits the board. "Ohhhhh!!!" exclaims the crowd of onlookers. He looks up and you look him in his watering teary eyes and yell, "Owned!" Yes, that is the power of Force of Will, the best card ever made. Not only can you cast it without mana, but it can be a big surprise when your opponent thinks that your mana supply is tapped. All that rolled into one, with the only exceptions being that you lose one card and one life point. The benefits definitely outweigh the drawbacks. That is why Force of Will is the best card ever.

**4th prize**  
*Rich Hinds, Massachusetts, US*

In my opinion, the best Magic card ever printed is Black Lotus. Though it may not be the most powerful card in the eyes of many players, it is by far the most memorable card in Magic. No other TCG card in history is its equal in value. Just mentioning it on eBay will make your auction for hundreds, even thousands of dollars! In terms of playability, think of it as a free Dark Ritual for any color. Black Lotus is the centerpiece for many 'broken' decks and one-turn kills. Few other cards are banned/restricted across the board. If I had to sell all of my Magic cards, the one card I would keep would be my Black Lotus; my baby. :) Thus, Wizards of the Coast should be proud that, after 10 great years, one of the original cards is still the best!

* * *

**5th prize**  
*Jake LeClair, Massachusetts, US*

I started Magic just when Ice Age was coming out, and was totally addicted to the strategy, competitivness, and all out can of whipass. My brother,which started playing Magic when Homelands came out, being my motivation on playing Magic constantly. Recently, we always argue on what card we think is the best of all time.

For me taking the view and zooming out, and try to just keep in mind all those amazing combinations of cards, is thrown out the window! We are just looking at one single card. I think the best card in Magic: the Gathering in my opinion, would have to be... Time Walk. For 1 Blue mana and one colorless, one extra turn? WOW! All other cards have really cool abilities or are strong creatures and so forth. But taking another turn is like another chance or another world to get into the zone and do what you gotta do. Taking extra turns is freaking amazingly strong and I really don't think people will disagree. Playing Magic is like you're in a different world with skeletal muscular figures, standing with in the distance of stonehenge.
