If you've scoured through the [*Innistrad* site](https://web.archive.org/web/20110923135643/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/products.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/products/innistrad), you might have seen some small pictures of the booster packs. And if you haven't, good news! We're going to look at *big* pictures of the booster packs right now!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7c/a3/7ca3a7084bc1a3211a3764a4f5249b997994571cb1582e9fa5ac837b8bac74bd.jpg) 

  

In these Arcanas, we normally we like to avoid being too specific about what's shown. But we'll be honest: this is Liliana. Hi, Liliana!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0b/05/0b05ff87336952845726c60ef286ccac5864574a485195dcf009063126352599.jpg) 

  

This, too, is a Planeswalker. Okay, fine: it's Garruk. It's really hard to get over the instinct not to be specific!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/98/c1/98c14156ccfa0798fa9702a5f0db77a44e105c3b74e0610a4e03de51adb45eff.jpg) 

  

Ah! Now that we're out of the Instant Recognition Zone, we can return to the traditional vague descriptions. So here we go: this is a huge, monstrous creature of some variety! It's probably a werewolf, right? It's certainly somewhat werewolf-y.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/da/a8/daa8601301a6810ad7b06aa00b8c381817f721d758fe4c04d204b86e621a39a5.jpg) 

  

Oh, hello! This is a vampire. You'd know that if you'd gone to the *Innistrad* site like the first paragraph suggested, because the card Olivia Voldaren is already in the [*Innistrad* Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20110916135340/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/innistrad/cig).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/03/31/033125491246ba88e803ecc74c3697f6e77ec76e548a8e1464d0be43be26104c.jpg) 

  

And finally, we have a demon! Specifically, a Bloodgift Demon. This time, you have no excuse for not knowing that. It's your own fault!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7c/24/7c247eab7a198cbc879c84021134fc2b91c07a7d0ed4c1d35c6380f331cc00ab.jpg)
