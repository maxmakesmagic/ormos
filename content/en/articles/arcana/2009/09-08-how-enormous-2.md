Consider, for a moment, the *Legions* card Enormous Baloth.

  

 ![Enormous Baloth](https://i.ormos.uk/7d/66/7d667ef11eb8748bb8c627e71544ad21d2974c46a5fe11c10de70796522f3ef2.jpg) 

  

Fairly straightforward, right? Well, you wouldn't *believe* how much work went into deciding on its flavor text. We bring this up because we've just found a memo from 2002 (our archives are extensive, but not particularly well organized) listing six options for Enormous Baloth's flavor text:

![Enormous Baloth Flavor Memo](http://www.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/arcana/272_memo.jpg "Enormous Baloth Flavor Memo") 

  

The really surprising thing? The final version wasn't even one of these options!
