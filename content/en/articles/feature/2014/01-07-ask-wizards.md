Ask Wizards is a weekly feature that allows you to ask us questions! If you'd like to submit your question please email it to AskWizards@wizards.com. We aren't able to answer every question we receive but if your question is good then it might show up in the coming weeks! 

  

* * *

  

 **Q: : I was checking out the art on the [Moxes for Magic Online](https://web.archive.org/web/20140107000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/1387) and noticed they are all placed in different planes. [Mox Sapphire](https://web.archive.org/web/20140107000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Sapphire) and Zendikar were easy enough to recognize and the [Mox Pearl](https://web.archive.org/web/20140107000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Pearl) looked like it was on Ravnica. What are the other locations? —Adam D.**

 **A: Mike McArtor, copy editor, DailyMTG:** 

A keen eye, Adam! Your question gave me an excuse to delve into our Multiverse database (always a joy, let me tell you!) to fully answer it. Here is the relevant part of each art description handed off to [Volkan Baga](https://web.archive.org/web/20140107000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;artist=%5B%22volkan+baga%22%5D): 

[Mox Pearl](https://web.archive.org/web/20140107000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Pearl): 

...an iconic artifact of good, re-imagined to fit in the Ravnica setting. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/arc1387_pearl.png)  

[Mox Sapphire](https://web.archive.org/web/20140107000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Sapphire): 

...please imply that it's set on Zendikar. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/arc1387_sapphire.png)  

[Mox Jet](https://web.archive.org/web/20140107000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Jet): 

...an iconic artifact of evil, re-imagined to fit in the *Time Spiral* setting. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/arc1387_jet.png)  

[Mox Ruby](https://web.archive.org/web/20140107000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Ruby): 

...an iconic artifact of passion and power, re-imagined to fit in the *Lorwyn* setting. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/arc1387_ruby.png)  

[Mox Emerald](https://web.archive.org/web/20140107000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Emerald): 

...set in Naya, the shard of Alara... 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/arc1387_emerald.png)  
  

So there you go!
