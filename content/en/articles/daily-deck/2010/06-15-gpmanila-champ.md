With 1,071 people in attendance, Grand Prix–Manila was one for the record books! But it was Japan's Naoki Nakada who managed to take the final slot, winning the title. He had to fend off stiff competition like Player of the Year Yuuya Watanabe, who was competing in back-to-back Top 8s. Read more about the Grand Prix in the [official coverage](/en/events/2010/gpman10/nakada-emerges-unscathed-in-manila).
