![](https://i.ormos.uk/5a/aa/5aaa49fa22f343785862479afc00c816190b5d840ad6b8cae668132986edd22c.jpg)This round feature two European players that are generally well known for their eclectic and surprising deck construction. Bram Snepvangers complained that he was unable to find a suitable combo deck – his [Confessor](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Confessor)-Lich deck has too many problems. So he decided to got for a variant of the most popular deck in the format, Mono Black control. Labarre has also opted for a lesser played mainstay, a Blue Black [Pyschatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyschatog)/[Upheaval](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Upheaval) deck.

Game 1 sees the Dutchman mulligan a one swamp hand, but his second hand didn't seem that much better, being forced to keep a one swamp, [Cabal Coffers](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Coffers) hand with a multitude of expensive spells. He didn't draw a swamp till turn 3, but it did allow him to cast a [Nantuko Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Shade) on that turn. Labarre responded with a [Pyschatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyschatog), with a blue mana open, trying to represent he had a [Circular Logic](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Circular+Logic). This was confirmed when Bram attempted to [Chainer's Edict](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chainer%27s+Edict) him, and he responded with the expected Logic. He proceeded to [Concentrate](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Concentrate) in an attempt to improve his position, but this allowed for more Snepvangers to Edict away the [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog) and begin attacking with his [Nantuko Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Shade). Labarre triedto come back in the match by casting his another ![](https://i.ormos.uk/0b/ba/0bba6a868ab5ecb4d36ca45cb3c804553e64595d98613ac011ed94be1dda8336.jpg)[Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog) and a [Shadowmage Infiltrator](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shadowmage+Infiltrator), but the defensive line of the Dutchman was bolstered by a [Shambling Swarm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shambling+Swarm). The Shade kept coming in, for another 6 damage as the Frenchman declined to block, and was soon joined by a twin. Finally the Frenchman decided to block the twin shades with his Infiltrator and Tog, but it became clear that something was afoot when the Frenchman decided to [Compulsion](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsion) perfectly good cards into his graveyard. His strategy became clear when he [Upheaval](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Upheaval)ed the next turn as well as casting a [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog). But Bram saw through that plan and had the [Innocent Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Innocent+Blood) at the ready. After that plan failed the match deteriorated with multiple Shade's ending the first game.

Snepvangers 1-0

Bram brings in [Mindslicer](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindslicer)s, [Mesmeric Fiend](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mesmeric+Fiend) and Echoes, with Nicolas bringing in [Divert](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divert)s, Fiends and more [Compulsion](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsion)s.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/99/50/99509ea83a0c85ba6bd62c5eb037bb654f2914473b55e35a83f49f8df7f8cf62.jpg)Game 2 sees Labarre a little more active with a second turn [Zombie Infestation](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zombie+Infestation), with Snepvangers replying with a [Mesmeric Fiend](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mesmeric+Fiend), taking a [Shadowmage Infiltrator](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shadowmage+Infiltrator). The fiend was short lived with an Edict burying it before it held Finkel for any longer. Shadowmage Infiltrator hit he board the next turn, but was held off by a [Nantuko Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Shade), but a combination of [Aether Burst](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Burst)s made sure that the Invitational card got in for a few hits. Snepvangers attempt to kill the Mage were continually thwarted by the creation of Zombies from the Infestation, as well as the occasional [Circular Logic](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Circular+Logic). Finally Snepvangers stabilized the board with a [Mindslicer](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindslicer) which held off a ton of Infiltrators, and when a [Laquatus' Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Laquatus%27+Champion) hit the table there were not many turs remaining in the match. Snepvangers added insult to injury by casting [Hypnox](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hypnox), which elicited a chuckle and concession from the beaten Frenchman.

**Final Result:** Snepvangers wins 2-0
