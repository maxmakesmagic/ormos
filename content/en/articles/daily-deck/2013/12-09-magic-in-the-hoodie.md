In [today's Serious Fun article](https://web.archive.org/web/20131209000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/sf/277), Bruce Richard provides a buyer's guide for the **Magic** player on your list (or for you, if you hand the article to your loved ones!). One of the gift ideas he mentions are [mana hoodies](http://www.mtgmerch.com/catalogsearch/result/?q=hoodie) from [MTGMerch.com](http://www.mtgmerch.com/).

We wanted to bring these up because once they sell out they are gone for good. Also because they are quite awesome. Don't take my word for it, though; check it out: 

[![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/Araca_ManaHoodies_12-15.jpg)](http://www.mtgmerch.com/catalogsearch/result/?q=hoodie)  

Something else we wanted to mention: if you order $50 or more on [MTGMerch.com](http://www.mtgmerch.com/) before December 15, you will get free shipping!
