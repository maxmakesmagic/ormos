The previews for *Shadowmoor* are going pretty well so far, don't you think? But so far we've only previewed the *cards.* How about some tokens?

  
 ![](https://i.ormos.uk/48/da/48da068d7eabce841404f8a0e00c86d389c7ce6999d6975fb1c8339739fd4d30.jpg)

Ah, an Elf Warrior. Note how different he looks from the [*Lorwyn* Elf Warrior token](https://i.ormos.uk/a0/32/a0324329a9f806c1ea66f0a71c1e927282dd8a55916a6ecd3c9d2787b97314ea.jpg). 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a3/b4/a3b4adfef8221e5b992135cf56736f42f7d2557858a2941e6adc6cbcbfed9818.jpg)

A 5/5 Elemental token! And it appears to be both black and red, too. Interesting.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/05/ed/05ed7a31151a037cb3811f3f5c3e22088588bf1980d20ca0762da8538fecef15.jpg)

Well, that seems logical enough. A 2/2 green Wolf token could certainly come in handy at times.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1d/e0/1de0e34bc99fc5cfe0750bb1c327f62e43f29898071ea517bd622f57681ad4ff.jpg)

Yes, a 1/1 red and green Goblin token, that makes sense.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/93/11/9311e428c00b40a68937dcd383b62b747bb7c005946297b4ed8c87a01bd62b9a.jpg)

Well, here's something we didn't see much of in *Lorwyn*: our old friend, the rat!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/56/ea/56ead1febca74ea6847aec08b3defa4374d89c4119cbb5a760c8876ee31685eb.jpg)

And we close things out this time with a 1/1 white Kithkin Soldier. There will be another round of *Shadowmoor* token previews, though, so stay tuned!
