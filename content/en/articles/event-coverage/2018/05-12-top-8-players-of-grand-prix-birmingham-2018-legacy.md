After fifteen rounds of Legacy, with a total of 1,192 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-legacy/gpbir18_t8_vanderham.jpg)

**Name:** Peter van der Ham

**Age:** 30

**Occupation:** Storage tank integrity engineer

**Hometown:** Dordrecht, the Netherlands

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Dutch Legacy champion 2011, 5-0 with Rebels on *Magic Online*.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Grixis Delver—couldn't find a good deck to beat it.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Deathrite Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20180512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathrite+Shaman)—it makes mana, it gains life, it wins the game.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Badlands](https://web.archive.org/web/20180512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Badlands), because you can't cast all the BB spells without it, at least not consistently.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-legacy/gpbir18_t8_santos.jpg)

**Name:** Bernardo Santos

**Age:** 24

**Hometown:** Lison, Portugal

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Grand Prix Liverpool Top 4, qualified for MOCS twice.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Grixis Delver. I like how it plays well against everything and I think it's very good.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Deathrite Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20180512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathrite+Shaman). The card is a Legacy staple for a reason; so good all around.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20180512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy). It overperformed and I sideboarded it a lot.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-legacy/gpbir18_t8_degruyter.jpg)

**Name:** Johan De Gruyter

**Age:** 28

**Occupation:** Ph.D. student in biology

**Hometown:** Lier, Belgium

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Making four consecutive Top 8s in Belgian Legacy Cup Trials with Esper Stoneblade, and some other Top 8s in local events.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Steel Stompy. I chose this deck because it's tons of fun and it has a good matchup against a lot of the field.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20180512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager), because people forgot how to play against it.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Karn, Scion of Urza](https://web.archive.org/web/20180512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn%2C+Scion+of+Urza). I only played the card a few times, but when I did it always won.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-legacy/gpbir18_t8_andersen.jpg)

**Name:** Janus Andersen

**Age:** 31

**Occupation:** Brand strategist

**Hometown:** Frederiksberg, Denmark

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Two-time FNM winner.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Grixis Kess Control. I love Kess and I feel favored against Four-Color and Miracles. Also, Leovold is a miserable card.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Kess, Dissident Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20180512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kess%2C+Dissident+Mage). The card gives you and edge in the "mirror" against Czech Pile and can pull you back from horrible positions.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Liliana, the Last Hope](https://web.archive.org/web/20180512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana%2C+the+Last+Hope). She is incredible in all fair matchups.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-legacy/gpbir18_t8_takahashi.jpg)

**Name:** Yuta Takahashi

**Age:** 32

**Occupation:** Hareruya employee

**Hometown:** Narita, Japan

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Win a Legacy GP with Miracles.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Four-Color Leovold. It's the best.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

Beta duals. I complete!

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Flusterstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20180512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flusterstorm). Good against combo and Delver.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-legacy/gpbir18_t8_kowalski.jpg)

**Name:** Grzegorz Kowalski

**Age:** 26

**Occupation:** Wannabe *Magic* pro

**Hometown:** Wrocław, Poland

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Grand Prix Lyon champion.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Grixis Delver. I don't play enough Legacy to understand the whole format, so I just picked what's considered the best deck and played it online as much as I could.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

The [Forked Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forked+Bolt) which already won Grand Prix Lyon for me.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20180512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy)—never knew what I should name though.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-legacy/gpbir18_t8_mertins.jpg)

**Name:** Alexander Mertins

**Age:** 26

**Occupation:** FNM grinding

**Hometown:** Cologne, Germany

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Top 8 Grand Prix Amsterdam 2017, 8-8 record at Pro Tour *Ixalan*.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Dredge, because it was the only affordable Legacy deck for me. I borrowed the [Lion's Eye Diamond](https://web.archive.org/web/20180512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lion%27s+Eye+Diamond).

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Ichorid](https://web.archive.org/web/20180512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichorid)! It returns from the grave.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Silent Gravestone](https://web.archive.org/web/20180512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silent+Gravestone). Shuts down most graveyard hate.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-legacy/gpbir18_t8_campbell.jpg)

**Name:** Gary Campbell

**Age:** 52

**Occupation:** Coffin dodger

**Hometown:** Dundee, Scotland

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

First *Magic* player in Scotland. Played Pro Tour 22 years ago, ten in total.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Red Prison. I played it because I'm confident I'm capable of casting "Chalice for one" first turn.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Chalice of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20180512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chalice+of+the+Void)—win on turn one.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

None—most cards remained unused.
