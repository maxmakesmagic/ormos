You've already seen some [secrets of the Azorius](https://web.archive.org/web/20060412000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/dissension/azorius) in the *Dissension* minisite. Now it's time to breach the bio-wombs of Novijen — and if you don't know what we're talking about, you will soon.

[![The Simic](https://i.ormos.uk/19/f2/19f227052971e90319d6048b479d156161ed044bbfe8462d4bb10f3489b4515b.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20060412000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/dissension/simic) The Blue-Green guild, the [**Simic Combine**](https://web.archive.org/web/20060412000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/dissension/simic), hits the *Dissension* minisite today. There you'll find:

*   Simic guild lore and exclusive concept art from the style guide

*   Simic short fiction by Rei Nakazawa

*   Special Simic art preview with bonus wallpaper

Tune in again next Wednesday for the final update — the Cult of Rakdos.
