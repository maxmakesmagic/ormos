Well it’s been over a year since our last Mistform Ultimus check-in, so I think it’s about time we see how *Return to Ravnica*, ***Magic** 2014*, and *Theros* have been treating it. 

For those of you who might not know what I’m talking about, here’s the deal. Before Changeling was an ability in *Lorwyn* block, Mistform Ultimus was the only odd creature to have every creature type. Over the years, we’ve been keeping tabs on Mistform Ultimus to see how its type line has changed.

The [last time we checked in](/en/articles/arcana/2012/08-15-mistform-ultimus-watch-magic-2013-edition) on it was in August, 2013, just after ***Magic** 2013*, when it had 224 types.

The *Return to Ravnica* block didn’t create any new creature types (despite the Simic’s best efforts), same for ***Magic** 2014*, so it is actually here in *Theros* that we get our new additions. 

Obviously, becoming a God is sort of a big deal. But there are two other new types from *Theros*: Sable and Nymph.
