In the *Mirage* card Preferred Selection, we see a frenzied horned hound chasing after some rats. Which Enchant Creature card from a later set shows what happens next?

Answer below.

  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
 ![Mortal Wound](http://www.wizards.com/global/images/mtgcom_arcana_75_pic2_en.jpg)

The answer is Mortal Wound from *Visions*. Kev Walker turned in both pieces at once, and they ended up on different cards in different sets.

Mmm… tasty rats.
