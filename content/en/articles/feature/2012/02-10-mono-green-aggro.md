The StarCityGames.com Open Series in Richmond this past weekend saw several players packing decks backed by brand-new *Dark Ascension* cards. Former US National team member Todd Anderson brought an especially exciting deck to the table: Mono Green Aggro!

Mono Green Aggro hasn't been seen in Standard for a while, but the power of *Dark Ascension*'s [Strangleroot Geist](https://web.archive.org/web/20120210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strangleroot+Geist) has tempted some players down the forested path. Using the Geist as the exciting lynchpin for the deck, other hard-hitting green creatures like [Dungrove Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20120210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dungrove+Elder) and even [Bellowing Tanglewurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20120210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bellowing+Tanglewurm) also help provide a reason to stick with a twenty-four-[Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20120210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest) manabase. [Phyrexian Metamorph](https://web.archive.org/web/20120210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Metamorph) and [Sword of War and Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20120210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+War+and+Peace) add a dash of non-green, giving the one-color deck a wide array of effects.

With tempo-based [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20120210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets) decks at a high point in popularity, Todd's green deck is poised to take them down. The hexproof on [Dungrove Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20120210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dungrove+Elder) and [Thrun, the Last Troll](https://web.archive.org/web/20120210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thrun%2C+the+Last+Troll) prevent cards like [Vapor Snag](https://web.archive.org/web/20120210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vapor+Snag) from setting you back, and [Sword of War and Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20120210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+War+and+Peace) helps the creatures break past a wall of white tokens. After sideboarding, the addition of [Ratchet Bomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20120210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ratchet+Bomb) helps push back against the white-blue deck even further. Todd's careful preparations for that popular archetype helped propel him through the field all the way to the Top 8!

With Pro Tour *Dark Ascension* starting today, this could be a deck to keep an eye on! Stay tuned to the [expanded coverage all weekend](/en/articles/feature/2012/02-06-knowing-where-to-look-a-pro-tour-dark-ascension-preview) for up-to-the-minute text and video updates. With plenty of new room to innovate in Standard, this Pro Tour is one you won't want to miss!
