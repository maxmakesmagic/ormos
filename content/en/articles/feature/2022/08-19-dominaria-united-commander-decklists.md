*[Dominaria United](https://web.archive.org/web/20220819000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/dominaria-united)* hits stores worldwide on September 9, and for Commander fans, we have two new Commander decks featuring powerful legends, reprints, and all-new cards.

![Legends' Legacy](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/en_28NBweyydd.png)![Painbow](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/en_m2usdmweiy.png)

You can check out the cards in the [*Dominaria United* Commander Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2022/07-21-dominaria-united-commander-card-image-gallery), as well as the regular cards in the [*Dominaria United* Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2022/08-26-dominaria-united-card-image-gallery) and the [Booster Fun variants of *Dominaria United* cards](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2022/07-21-dominaria-united-variant-card-image-gallery).

Plus, for a rundown of all the cards across the set and where you can find them, check out the [Collecting *Dominaria United* article](/en/articles/feature/2022/08-18-collecting-dominaria-united), which includes details on the [Lost *Legends* cards](/en/articles/feature/2022/07-21-lost-legends)!

Look for the *Dominaria United* Commander decks at [your local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20220819000000/https://locator.wizards.com/), online through [Amazon](https://gtly.to/HZV8BGWl_), and everywhere you find *Magic: The Gathering* products sold.
