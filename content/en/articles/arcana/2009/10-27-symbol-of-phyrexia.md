In the distant past ([2004](/en/articles/arcana/2004/02-16-the-symbol-of-phyrexia) and [2005](/en/articles/arcana/2005/07-26-yawgmoths-demon-2), anyway), we showed the Symbol of Phyrexia and where it appeared on Order of Yawgmoth, the *Seventh Edition* Yawgmoth's Edict, and the *Ninth Edition* Yawgmoth's Demon.

And since that symbol has [shown up again](/en/articles/arcana/2009/10-14-announcing-duel-decks-phyrexia-vs-the-coalition), we thought it would be fun to spotlight a couple of cards that have the symbol even though they're not directly Yawgmoth-related. So return with us now to those thrilling days of yesteryear!
