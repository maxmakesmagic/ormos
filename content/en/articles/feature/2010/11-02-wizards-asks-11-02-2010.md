*What's your favorite [Timmy](/en/articles/makingmagic/2009/03-09-designing-for-timmy) card, and why?*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b4/9a/b49a97d847733a73985c5d85979f9c0df999dfc0380369be66282a7035d6c565.jpg)

**Mons Johnson:** My favorite Timmy card is probably the *USS Iowa*. At 68 points, this monster battleship can hit hard and move fast, and is very difficult to kill!

Oh, you meant **Magic** card. My bad ....

The Timmy card that I had the most fun with was definitely [Gurzigost](https://web.archive.org/web/20101102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gurzigost). With a 6/8 body, 'Gost was the phattest guy in the time of madness. It came at a reasonable cost of a mere ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless3.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/green.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/green.gif) and could do a lot of damage directly to the opponent, particularly if you had a [Fiery Temper](https://web.archive.org/web/20101102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Temper) in hand. Its upkeep was an actual advantage in some circumstances, most notably while you had a [Wild Research](https://web.archive.org/web/20101102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Research) in play.
