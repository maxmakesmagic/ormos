It was the largest **Magic** tournament in the history of the game. Nearly 2,000 players at one event, all looking to earn their share of Grand Prix fame and glory. But only one sealed the deal and his name was Arjan van Leeuwen. His Jund deck lasted him through the Top 8 on the backs of Kresh the Bloodbraided and Spearbreaker Behemoth. Of course, some early weenies by way of Dragon Fodder, Blister Beetle, and Cylian Elf probably didn't hurt. You can read more about Arjan's win [in the Grand Prix–Paris coverage](/en/events/2008/gppar08/arjan-van-leeuwen-devours-paris). Better yet, you can start playing *Shards of Alara* Limited right now on **Magic Online**! Find out how you can get involved with casual drafts, Release Events, and more [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2008/10-13-shards-of-alara-release-events).
