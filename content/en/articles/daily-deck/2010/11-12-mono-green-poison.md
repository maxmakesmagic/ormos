*Scars of Mirrodin* Game Day brought a special promotion not offered at other such events before: players who used [aligned decks](/en/articles/arcana/2010/10-06-game-day-excitement) could qualify for special prizes. For Kine Thorvaldsen that meant setting up camp with the Phyrexians at Outland Oslo in Norway. The above poison deck took first place amongst Phyrexian-aligned decks using only Phyrexian cards and lots of pump spells to infect opponents to death!
