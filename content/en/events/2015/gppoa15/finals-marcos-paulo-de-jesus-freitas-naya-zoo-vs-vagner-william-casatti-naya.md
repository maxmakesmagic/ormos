The elimination rounds proceeded at blistering speed, largely due to the fact that there were 16 copies of [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl) in the Top 8. In under an hour and a half, Marcos Paulo De Jesus Freitas and Vagner William Casatti had secured their seats the finals, where they will compete in the final match of Grand Prix Porto Alegre.

This matchup isn't unfamiliar to De Jesus Freitas at all. In fact, he only just experienced it ten minutes ago, when he dispatched Alejandro Cesa, who was also piloting Naya Burn just like Casatti was.

To boil it down, the onus was on De Jesus Freitas to fend off Casatti's early threats. He had the stronger late game anyway, and will have no problems dominating the board as long as he preserved his life totals. Exchanging blows was the worst idea for De Jesus Freitas, a notion that Casatti would have loved.

Fat creatures like [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf), [Loxodon Smiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Smiter) and [Knight of the Reliquary](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+the+Reliquary) will stymie Casatti's attempts to cross the red zone, but with so much burn in his deck all he really needs is to sneak in for 3 or 5 damage with [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) and [Monastery Swiftspear](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Swiftspear) and land [Eidolon of the Great Revel](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eidolon+of+the+Great+Revel) to force a couple more. [Lava Spike](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lava+Spike), [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm) and [Atarka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Atarka%27s+Command) were all part of that grand plan.

In addition, De Jesus Freitas' mana base will also play a part in accelerating his doom, owing to the high number of fetchlands and shocklands that fuel Casatti's cause.

However, Casatti had a confession.

“I'm afraid of the [Worship](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Worship), and perhaps the [Unflinching Courage](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unflinching+Courage)” undoubtedly referring to the nasty cards in Marcos Paulo De Jesus' sideboard.

Could these cards be his downfall?

Regardless, both friends wished each other good luck, as they compete for the coveted honor of being a Grand Prix Champion.

# Game One

De Jesus Freitas cracked [Wooded Foothills](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wooded+Foothills) for [Stomping Ground](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stomping+Ground) to cast [Noble Hierarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noble+Hierarch). Casatti wasted no time (over)killing it with [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt).

De Jesus Freitas dropped [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl) as the replacement. Casatti sacrificed [Arid Mesa](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arid+Mesa) for [Stomping Ground](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stomping+Ground), casting a 2/2 [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl) and [Monastery Swiftspear](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Swiftspear), unable to attack.

Laying [Kessig Wolf Run](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kessig+Wolf+Run), De Jesus Freitas added [Knight of the Reliquary](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+the+Reliquary) to the board, now a 4/4. Casatti untapped and laid [Sacred Foundry](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sacred+Foundry) to kill [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl) with [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt), and attacked with both his creatures. [Knight of the Reliquary](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+the+Reliquary) blocked, and [Atarka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Atarka%27s+Command) forced the trade, but he had a replacement copy.

After this crucial turn, De Jesus Freitas was at 9, Casatti at 15.

When [Scavenging Ooze](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scavenging+Ooze) joined the board, it was clear as day that victory was out of reach for Casatti. He watched as [Scavenging Ooze](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scavenging+Ooze) chomped away on both graveyards, restoring De Jesus Freitas' life total in the process. [Knight of the Reliquary](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+the+Reliquary) grew to 5/5 and [Monastery Swiftspear](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Swiftspear) was sent on a [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile).

“I can't win from this point on. Let's move on,” Casatti lamented as he revealed the 2 Mountains and [Lava Spike](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lava+Spike) from his hand, lightly flooded.

**Marcos Paulo De Jesus Freitas 1 - Vagner William Casatti 0**

# Game Two

Casatti opened with [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide), knocking De Jesus Freitas down to 18. He presented [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl) to block, but [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) zapped it away to pave the way for a second [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) attack.

Stuck on 1 land, De Jesus Freitas was forced to use [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile)s on the [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide), as well as a freshly-summoned [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl). After baiting out the removal, Casatti finally felt safe to drop [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer), which he had been sandbagging for a few turns.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppoa15/gppoa15_finals_casatti.jpg)**  
*Vagner William Casatti evens the score!*

When De Jesus Freitas drew [Noble Hierarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noble+Hierarch), Casatti killed it with [Rift Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rift+Bolt) to keep him stuck on one mana. [Eidolon of the Great Revel](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eidolon+of+the+Great+Revel) was the final nail in the coffin and De Jesus Freitas acknowledged that it was over.

**Marcos Paulo De Jesus Freitas 1 - Vagner William Casatti 1**

# Game Three

The tournament was in its final throes and both players were visibly nervous. After all, this was a pretty cutthroat matchup where every turn, every mana and every single point of damage mattered.

De Jesus Freitas prayed for a good hand, but was cursed with a mulligan. It was the final game of the day, and after claiming Game 1, he really didn't want to go down just like that.

Laying [Stomping Ground](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stomping+Ground) untapped and passing the turn was unorthodox, but it was something De Jesus Freitas opted to do, fearing hasty creatures in Casatti's deck. Unfortunately for him, Casatti's creatureless draw was laden with burn spells.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Lightning+Bolt)](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Searing+Blaze)](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Rift+Bolt)](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rift+Bolt)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Boros+Charm)](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm)

That's 13 damage in the bag. Drawing [Eidolon of the Great Revel](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eidolon+of+the+Great+Revel), Casatti declined cast it. After all, De Jesus Freitas had already represented [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) on the very first turn.

Counting the [Shock](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shock)s and Fetches, De Jesus Freitas was down to 16. When he tapped out for [Knight of the Reliquary](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+the+Reliquary), Casatti decided it was time to offload [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm).

De Jesus Freitas goes down to 12.

When [Voice of Resurgence](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voice+of+Resurgence) hit play, Casatti thought hard about whether to cast [Searing Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze). If he did, that would knock De Jesus Freitas down to 9. Accompanied with the [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) and two suspended [Rift Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rift+Bolt)s, that was lethal and even granting De Jesus Freitas two Elemental tokens didn't matter!

However, he failed to calculate the [Unflinching Courage](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unflinching+Courage), which joined forces with [Knight of the Reliquary](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+the+Reliquary) for the life cushion and the title of Grand Prix Porto Alegre Champion!

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppoa15/gppoa15_finals_de-jesus-freitas.jpg)**  
*[Unflinching Courage](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unflinching+Courage) for the win!*

**Marcos Paulo De Jesus Freitas 2 - Vagner William Casatti 1**

**Congratulations to Marco Paulo De Jesus Freitas!**
