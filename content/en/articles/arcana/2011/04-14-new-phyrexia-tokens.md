Usually, when an expansion set involves tokens that already exist in the block, we don't make new token cards for them. But *New Phyrexia* is different! Sure, there was a [Myr token in *Scars of Mirrodin*](https://web.archive.org/web/20110414000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/scars-mirrodin-tokens-2010-09-15). But that was a Mirran Myr. *New Phyrexia* brings something new to the party!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b6/2c/b62c07dd13f331d13cc4d6b5081668813134da8ce7a319d5b6b20c5c0ed7cb8c.jpg)  

Ooh! Phyrexian-y! Hey, do you think any other standard creature types might show up in compleated form?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2b/8d/2b8d7ca524b1277e87280584fcdedca71a24890e51385ab1eedab8076586ff43.jpg)  

I guess so! But what about something more in theme for the block? Maybe something that gets created fairly often?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ab/4b/ab4b61a4076d1e3e22ded38378d0ae7a89dbd1d5227426387ef1e5cbd2c0a471.jpg)  

Perfect! But how about a token that *doesn't* already exist in the block? Could we get that taken care of?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6c/8c/6c8c39ed26db13c9df61ffb82bb9047c7528e36722ea47e637398ef8eca04e1f.jpg)  

Very nice.
