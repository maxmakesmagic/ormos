**Magic** Weekend agrees with David Sharfman. The 21-year-old Floridian defeated Toshiyuki Kadooka 3-0 in the finals of Pro Tour Nagoya, following up his Grand Prix victory back at **Magic** Weekend Paris in February. Qualifying for the Pro Tour via the his Grand Prix finish in Paris, Sharfman swept all three of his opponents en route to earning the Pro Tour champion’s trophy. Sharfman joins Geoffrey Siron and Paul Rietzl as Pro Tour winners who didn’t lose a game on Sunday. 

Sunday began with thoughts of a Tsuyoshi Fujita-Luis Scott-Vargas final, but the new blood of the Top 8 showed the old guard a thing or two about *Scars of Mirrodin* drafting. Sharfman defeated Fujita in the quarterfinals and then dispatched Fabian Thiele in the semifinals.

Congratulations to David Sharfman and all the competitors at **Magic** Weekend Nagoya. We'll see you all again at **Magic** Weekend Philadelphia in September.
