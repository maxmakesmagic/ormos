*Updated November 23, 2011* 

![Magic: the Gathering Pro Tour](https://i.ormos.uk/72/33/7233580ab42233846709a253f2c477d01df8a3ce0e4a4de5f531a79594c6b5fb.jpg "Magic: the Gathering Pro Tour")

Congratulations to Matthias Hunt, the [2011 Pro Tour Rookie of the Year](/en/events/2011/worlds11/rookie-of-the-year-matthias-hunt)!

The player with the most Pro Points at the end of a tournament season who has not participated in a Pro Tour or World Championship tournament in any previous season is identified as the Rookie of the Year. In addition to the title, the Rookie of the Year receives travel and accommodations to his or her choice of one of the next season’s Pro Tour events (for which that player has an invitation) or to the World Championships.

This page shows all rookie players who have received at least 10 Pro Points in the 2011 season.
