Coverage veteran Brian David-Marshall was on hand for all of the Extended festivities at Grand Prix-Kobe this past weekend, and boy did he pick up a doozy! Hisaya Tanaka managed to take a deck he dubbed "Stretcherator" to some last second byes to start the weekend off undefeated. Featuring Time Stretch, Tezzeret, the Seeker, and more, the deck builds up its mana before taking a long string of turns either through its namesake Time Stretch, or by taking all of its opponent's turns with Mindslaver. For more on Grand Prix-Kobe, read the official event coverage [here](/en/events/2009/gpkob09/saitos-back-to-back) .
