The*Planar Chaos* Fat Pack (available everywhere that **Magic** cards are sold!) is chock full of goodies from the *Planar Chaos* novel, the Player's Guide, to a twenty-sided life counter, to extra basic lands, to extremely cool card storage boxes with custom art and color dividers.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/03/43/0343cb419e519d2fca231429e4c3ad8a3c4309135f0891138783ae18fa1c3553.jpg)

  
*This photo contains an outdated shot of the Player's Guide with Akroma instead of Serra Sphinx*

Here are a couple of detail views of the design wireframes of the card boxes, featuring art by Daren Bader of Akroma, Angel of Fury and the dragon Oros, the Avenger. If you put the card boxes side-by-side, the art lines up across the two boxes. Check out those design wireframes for a stretched-out view of that art:
