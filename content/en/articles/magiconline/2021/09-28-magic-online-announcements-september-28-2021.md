## Cube Spotlight Series: *Alpha Core Set Cube*

In 2018, we unveiled[Core Set CubeonMagic Online](/en/articles/magiconline/2018/10-30-cube-spotlight-series-core-set-cube)—well, Harper O'Neill has done it again with *Alpha Core Set Cube*. *Alpha Core Set Cube* grants access to all core sets from*Magic*'s history instead of limiting players to *SeventhEdition*and onwards. In fact, you'll find over 50 cards in this list that were originally printed in*Limited Edition Alpha*, and the total card list is remarkably different.

*Alpha Core Set Cube* comes to *Magic Online* tomorrow, September 29, and will run for a week. Check out all the archetypes and the full card list in [Harper's article here](/en/articles/magiconline/2021/09-28-spotlight-cube-series-alpha-core-set-cube).

## Multiplayer Commander Constructed League

Since Wednesday, September 22, we've been running a Constructed Commander League! It adheres to the official [Commander ban list](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/game-info/gameplay/rules-and-formats/banned-restricted) and runs for two weeks. Each League course allows you to play three four-player games of Commander. Much like how we ran *Commander Legends* draft, each player votes for a player at the culmination of the event, with the game winner automatically receiving "two" votes just for winning! So, if you win in a cool way that everybody likes, then the votes should come pouring in!

We are always trying to find new ways for players to play Commander on *Magic Online*, so please let us know your thoughts by emailing MagicOnlineFeedback@wizards.com or tweeting [@MagicOnline](https://twitter.com/MagicOnline).

## MTGO Convention Series – All Access Returns September 29

Want to join in on the fun of PAX, Gen Con, or Origins while playing from home? Want to try out almost all the cards available on *Magic Online* for up to a week at a time? Just like last year, MTGO is running a special series of tournaments hosted by the team at Pastimes Events.

Starting tomorrow, September 29, at 10 a.m. Pacific (17:00 UTC), purchase a Gen Con entry package at [www.pastimesevents.com/origins-online-events](https://pastimesevents.com/origins-online-events/) for $20 and receive a code for redeeming inside *Magic Online*. When you redeem your code, you receive:

*   A **Mythic Event Token** usable for entry into one of the 22 Constructed events held during the four days of the convention.
*   **All Access** to cards in *Magic Online* until Wednesday at 10 a.m. Pacific following the convention—build a deck and enter games without having the cards in your collection!
    *   *All Access excludes* Battlebond*,* Secret Lair*, and old-frame Power Nine.*
    *   Enter any number of casual games at no cost.
    *   Leagues, queues, and scheduled events still require paying an entry option to join.

Play three rounds in a Convention Event and earn up to nine booster packs plus a pair of Convention Series-exclusive promos—a **Xanathar** avatar and a non-foil **Demilich**.

*NOTE: Each event has a cap of 56 players, and unused Mythic Event Tokens will be removed from accounts once All Access for that convention ends.*

![Xanathar Avatar](https://media.wizards.com/2021/images/daily/Xanathar.JPG)![Demelich promo](https://media.wizards.com/2021/images/daily/Demelich%20promo.JPG)

If you win three matches in a single Convention Event, you will earn a Wizards Ticket good for entry into the **Convention Series Championship** on Saturday, January 1.
