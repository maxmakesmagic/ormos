### System Down Beginnning At 8:00 AM PDT

* * *

There will be scheduled downtime beginning at 8:00 AM PDT on Wednesday, August 20. This downtime is to post bug fixes and get ready for *Eighth Edition* redemption to come online.

Also, the downtime will be used to fix the premium Elvish Champion problem we had just after *Eighth Edition* cards became available. Those players who got a normal Elvish Champion instead of the premium one will find a shiny elf in their collections when we come back up.

The servers will be brought back online after we've taken care of the Elvish Champion issue.
