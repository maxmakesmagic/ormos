The Pro Tour is headed to Amsterdam in just a few short weeks, but it is not the first time the tournament series has headed there! In 2004 the first Pro Tour-Amsterdam was held, and it featured the Rochester Draft format. Back then the Pro Tour featured only one format, not the split format it features today. Eventual Hall of Famer Nicolai Herzog took down that event with this deck list! Find information about the upcoming Pro Tour–Amsterdam [online](/en/events/2010/amsterdam10/pro-touramsterdam-event-information).
