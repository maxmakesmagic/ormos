### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or mythic rare. 

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

**Set Name:** *Modern Masters 2015 Edition*  
**Number of Cards:** 249  
**Languages:** English, Chinese Simplified, Japanese

**Twitter Hashtag:** [#MTGMM2015](https://twitter.com/search?q=%23MTGMM2015)

**Release Date:** May 22, 2015  
***Magic Online* Release Date:** May 28, 2015

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Erik Lauer (lead), Ben Hayes, and Ken Nagle

**Final Game Design and Development:** Tom LaPille (lead), Ben Hayes, Max McCall, and Adam Prosak
