**Previous Accomplishments:** none

**Where do you normally play *Magic*?** Table Game Square “Draw”

**What deck did you play?** Counter Top Mentor

**Why did you choose it?** Because I wanted to win with an original deck.

**What was your record?**

Byes: 2

Day 1: 8-1

Day 2: 5-0-1

**What was your best sideboard card and what sideboard card did you wish you had?** [Flusterstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flusterstorm); a fourth [Meddling Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meddling+Mage)

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkyo15/gpkyo15_t8_takahashi.jpg)Yūta Takahashi
