What's rarer than a *Battle for Zendikar* expedition? A Grand Prix stamped Expedition!

When players draft on Day 2 of a Grand Prix, the packs have all been pre-opened and stamped by the judge staff to help preserve the integrity of the draft. For a long time, Grand Prix stamped cards have been desirable among a niche audience, but now they have an even sweeter collectable to search for.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsyd15/GPSydney_D2_Exp_Ball.jpg)  
*Alex Ball opened a [Hallowed Fountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20151011000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallowed+Fountain) in his draft.*

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsyd15/GPSydney_D2_Exp_Li.jpg)  
*William Li got a [Windswept Heath](https://web.archive.org/web/20151011000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Windswept+Heath) in his, and he got it 5th pick!* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsyd15/GPSydney_D2_Exp_Neagle.jpg)  
*Justin Neagle was thrilled to open a [Bloodstained Mire](https://web.archive.org/web/20151011000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodstained+Mire) for himself.*
