*Updated 2008-07-12* 

 **[Saturday, July 12](#saturday) | [Sunday, July 13](#sunday)** 

**When**  
 All Tournaments will be held on the weekend of July 12 - 13, 2008

**Entry Fee & Product Mix**  
 

*   Check with your local tournament organizer for specific entry fee information.
*   Cost of entry will provide you one *ShadowmoorTM* tournament pack and three *EventideTM* boosters.
*   In addition, some locations may offer 3-person Team & Two Headed Giant formats.
    *   Each 3-person team will receive two *Shadowmoor* tournament packs & six *Eventide* boosters to build 3 decks.
    *   Each Two-Headed Giant team will receive one *Shadowmoor* tournament pack & five *Eventide* boosters to build two decks. For further information about Two-Headed Giant format, please go to the [Two Headed Giant FAQ](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/welcome-to-the-dci-document-center).

**Tournament Structure**  
 

*   The structure of the main *Eventide* Prerelease tournament is modified Swiss-style.
*   *Eventide* Prerelease side tournaments will be offered throughout the day and will consist of single-elimination rounds.

**Tournament Formats**  
 The format for each Prerelease tournament is:  

*   DCITM sanctioned, *Eventide* Sealed Deck.
*   Select locations, DCITM Theme Deck Open Duelling using *Eventide,* Theme Decks.
*   Select locations, DCITM sanctioned *Eventide*, Two Headed Giant Sealed Deck.
*   Select locations, DCITM sanctioned *Eventide,* Team Sealed Deck.

**Prizes**  
 

*   Select locations, check with organizer, will offer Door Prizes of three *Eventide* Booster Packs to be given out at random each hour.
*   Special commemorative *Eventide* prerelease foil card for each tournament participant, ***while supplies last.***
*   Additional packs of *Eventide* cards to top players, (as determined by your local prerelease Tournament Organizer).
*   Other items for winners of the main tournament and side tournaments may be awarded, (as determined by your local prerelease Tournament Organizer).

**Registration Information**  
 

*   Players who want more specific local tournament or registration information should call their local contact phone number.
*   Players who have general questions about the **Magic: The Gathering®** TCG, Wizards of the Coast® tournaments, the **Magic: The Gathering Pro TourTM**, the DCI players' organization, Qualifier tournaments, the **Magic®** World Championships, National Championships, Champs, or Regionals, should call Wizards of the Coast Customer Service at (800) 324-6496, or go to [wizards.custhelp.com](http://wizards.custhelp.com/).
