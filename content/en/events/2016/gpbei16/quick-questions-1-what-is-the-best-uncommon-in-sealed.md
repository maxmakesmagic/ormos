With 9 rounds of Sealed Deck play in store for players today at GP Beijing, I asked pros what they thought the best uncommon in *Shadows over Innistrad* sealed deck was.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbei16/gpBeijing16_QQYuuyaWatanabe2.jpg)  
**No. 19 Yuuya Watanabe:** I think [Duskwatch Recruiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20160416000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duskwatch+Recruiter) is the strongest. It's pretty unbeatable.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Duskwatch+Recruiter)](https://web.archive.org/web/20160416000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duskwatch+Recruiter)[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Krallenhorde+Howler)](https://web.archive.org/web/20160416000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Krallenhorde+Howler)

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbei16/gpBeijing16_QQShotaYasooka2.jpg)  
**Platinum Pro Shota Yasooka:** \[thinks for a while\] [Ulvenwald Mysteries](https://web.archive.org/web/20160416000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulvenwald+Mysteries).

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ulvenwald+Mysteries)](https://web.archive.org/web/20160416000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulvenwald+Mysteries)

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbei16/gpBeijing16_QQChapmanSim2.jpg)  
**Four-time GP Top 8 competitor Chapman Sim:** Man, I really want to say [Magnifying Glass](https://web.archive.org/web/20160416000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magnifying+Glass), is there something that is better? I suppose [Duskwatch Recruiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20160416000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duskwatch+Recruiter) is better, but I really like [Magnifying Glass](https://web.archive.org/web/20160416000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magnifying+Glass).

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Magnifying+Glass)](https://web.archive.org/web/20160416000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magnifying+Glass)

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbei16/gpBeijing16_QQHuanHaoShan2.jpg)  
**Grand Prix Taipei Champion Huang Hao-Shan:** I think I'm going to have to go with [Bound by Moonsilver](https://web.archive.org/web/20160416000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bound+by+Moonsilver).

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Bound+By+Moonsilver)](https://web.archive.org/web/20160416000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bound+By+Moonsilver)
