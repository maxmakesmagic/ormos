*Scars of Mirrodin* Game Day is fast approaching, and Brian Frye's Koth Red deck from the [StarCityGames.com](http://starcitygames.com/) Open–Nashville would only need a little tweaking to be an affiliated deck. What's that, you ask? It's simply a way to represent which side of the Mirrodin battle you're on, the Phyrexian or the Mirran. And, by playing an affiliated deck at Game Day you can win exclusive sneak peeks at the upcoming *Mirrodin Besieged* set! To find out how to build your affiliated deck for Game Day, visit the official [facts page](/en/events/2009/gameday-facts/world-wide-game-day).
