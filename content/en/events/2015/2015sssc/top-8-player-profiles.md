# Chas Hinkle

 ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/2015SSSC/top8-hinkle.jpg)

**Hometown:** Philadelphia, PA 

**Which Grand Prix did you win your Super Sunday Series at?**  
 Cincinnati, OH 

**What was your record in Sealed Draft (and what did you play)?**  
 3-0, Abzan (Siege Rhino J) 

**What was your record in Standard Constructed (and what did you play)?**  
 2-1, Abzan Aggro (Siege Rhino J) 

**What was your record in Booster Draft (and what did you play)?**  
 2-0-1, Temur ([Torrent Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20150125000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrent+Elemental) & [Arc Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20150125000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Lightning) J) 

**What has been your favorite part about this trip?**  
 I was able to meet some friends face-to-face for the first time. Everyone’s support online has been amazing as well.

# Jérémie Ross-Latour

 ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/2015SSSC/top8-rosslatour.jpg)

**Hometown:** Montréal 

**Which Grand Prix did you win your Super Sunday Series at?**  
 Montréal! 

**What was your record in Sealed Draft (and what did you play)?**  
 2-1. Temur. I played 4/1 guys and removal. It worked well! 

**What was your record in Standard Constructed (and what did you play)?**  
 3-0. [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150125000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy). The version that plays green creatures and in which you combo out by drawing your deck. On MTGO, I call it [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150125000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy) Clicks. 

**What was your record in Booster Draft (and what did you play)?**  
 2-1. Jeskai. I won thanks to [End Hostilities](https://web.archive.org/web/20150125000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=End+Hostilities) and [Crater's Claws](https://web.archive.org/web/20150125000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crater%27s+Claws) to the dome. 

**What has been your favorite part about this trip?**  
 The social part was great! Getting to casually talk over a meal with the very people who make the game was something unique.

**Which Grand Prix did you win your Super Sunday Series at?**  
 Melbourne

 2-1, I played a Jeskai tempo deck with lots of Prowess and tap/bounce effects. 

**What was your record in Standard Constructed (and what did you play)?**  
 2-1, I played Abzan Aggro with the all new [Valorous Stance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150125000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valorous+Stance).
