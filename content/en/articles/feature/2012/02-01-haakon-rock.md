In a recent **Magic Online** PTQ, Wirecat brought a crazy new Modern deck to the (virtual) table that has stirred up a lot of discussion. While [Burning Vengeance](https://web.archive.org/web/20120201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Vengeance) was made to work with flashback, Wirecat discovered it *also* works with the *Eventide* retrace mechanic and the *Coldsnap* rare [Haakon, Stromgald Scourge](https://web.archive.org/web/20120201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Haakon%2C+Stromgald+Scourge)! This unique innovation has drawn the attention of several noted players, and both [Mike Flores](/en/articles/top-decks/2012/01-26-five-things-about-modern) and [Brian David-Marshall](/en/articles/theweekthatwas/2012/01-27-dark-comprehension) wrote about it last week. Wirecat made the Top 8 of his PTQ with this deck—try it for yourself and [see if you can do the same](https://web.archive.org/web/20120201000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/node/640181)!
