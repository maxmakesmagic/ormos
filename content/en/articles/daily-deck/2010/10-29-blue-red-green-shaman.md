Canada recently played host to a Grand Prix, but before that this deck was tearing up the Champs tournament held in Alberta. Ryan Durand managed to make the Top 8 using a deck that happens to be almost Mirran aligned. What does that mean? It means if Ryan takes his deck to the *Scars of Mirrodin* Game Day held October 30-31 with a few minor tweaks, he could win an exclusive preview card from *Mirrodin Besieged*! To find out how you can get one, visit the Game Day [facts page](/en/events/2009/gameday-facts/world-wide-game-day).
