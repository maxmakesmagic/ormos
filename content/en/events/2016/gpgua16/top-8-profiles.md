After many great games of *Magic*, the Top 8 of Grand Prix Guangzhou is set! Learn more about Guangzhou's Top 8 competitors, including their previous accomplishments, below.

* * *

### Jia Hao

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpgua16/Top-8-Jia.jpg)

**Age:** 32  
**Occupation:** Businessman  
**Hometown:** Beijing, China

**Previous Accomplishments:** Two Super Sunday Series Top 8s

**Score on Day 1:** 9-0 (2 byes)  
**Score on Day 2:** 4-2

**What deck are you playing and why?**  
 Naya Burn without [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl), because I am the most familiar with this deck.

**What is the most interesting deck you saw all weekend?**  
 None. Burn is the best!

* * *

### Ryoichi Tamada

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpgua16/Top-8-Tamada.jpg)

**Age:** 31  
**Occupation:** Shop Owner  
**Hometown:** Osaka, Japan

**Previous Accomplishments:** Pro Tour Battle for Zendikar finalist and 3 Grand Prix Top 8s

**Score on Day 1:** 9-0 (3 byes)  
**Score on Day 2:** 4-2

**What deck are you playing and why?**  
 Jeskai [Pyromancer Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyromancer+Ascension). I wanted to play with [Lightning Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Helix) and that's why I splashed white.

**What is the most interesting deck you saw all weekend?**  
 In Round 13, my opponent was playing a White-Blue Spirits “flash” deck with [Spell Queller](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Queller) and [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel).

* * *

### Wu Kon Fai

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpgua16/Top-8-Wu.jpg)

**Age:** 27  
**Occupation:** Bank Employee  
**Hometown:** Hong Kong

**Previous Accomplishments:** World Magic Cup 2012 participant

**Score on Day 1:** 8-1 (2 byes)  
**Score on Day 2:** 5-1

**What deck are you playing and why?**  
 Naya Burn (with [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl)). It is good in the metagame now and doesn't have a lot of bad matchups.

**What is the most interesting deck you saw all weekend?**  
 The Sliver deck running around in Day 2 was pretty fun!
