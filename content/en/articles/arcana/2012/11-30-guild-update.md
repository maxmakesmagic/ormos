And that's from the standpoint of "people who have registered with their guild on [Planeswalker Points](https://web.archive.org/web/20121205140645/http://www.wizards.com/magic/planeswalkerpoints/guild), not "which guild has the best aesthetics" or something fuzzy like that. We're measuring success here.

Well... Izzet got the most. There's not a lot of drama there. But Azorius is right behind! Part of that is because Izzet didn't have any Grand Prix winners in the last two weeks, while Azorius had one:

**Grand Prix Bochum**: Martin Juza (Simic)  
**Grand Prix Taipei**: Makihito Mihara (Azorius) 

That's one win for Simic and one win for Azorius, which matches up well with their excellent points-per-member numbers. If you're thinking about winning a Grand Prix in the near future, make sure you've chosen your guild at [Planeswalker Points](https://web.archive.org/web/20121205140645/http://www.wizards.com/magic/planeswalkerpoints/guild)!
