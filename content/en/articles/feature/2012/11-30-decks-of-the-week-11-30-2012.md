Last weekend, Tyler Lytle piloted Black-Red Zombies to a win at [Grand Prix San Antonio](https://web.archive.org/web/20121130000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/lytles-zombies-big-texas). If you are interested in qualifying for Pro Tour *Gatecrash*, though, you will be more interested to check out the decklists and strategies from [Grand Prix Taipei](https://web.archive.org/web/20121130000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/mihara-triumphant-taipei), won by Japan's Makihito Mihara. Why? Because PTQs are also *Return to Ravnica* Limited! Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#gtc). In the meantime, see what other people are putting together in this season's PTQs with the decklists here. Enjoy!
