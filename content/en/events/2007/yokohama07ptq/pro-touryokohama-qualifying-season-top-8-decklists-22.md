![](https://www.wizards.com/protour/images/qt_lg.jpg)Refine your Extended skills to earn a ticket to Yokohama, Japan, by winning a Pro Tour Qualifier! 

You can test each decklist thanks to our [Sample Hand Generator](https://web.archive.org/web/20130710150318/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/294). Just click on the card fan in the upper right of each decklist and you can see what your opening hand could be, along with mulligans and the first three cards you'd draw.

Decklists will be updated weekly during the PTQ season. The season runs until March 11. To find a PTQ near you, [click here](/en/events/2007/yokohama07-qualifiers/pro-touryokohama-07-qualifiers). Pro Tour-Yokohama is scheduled for April 20-22, 2007, and the format is *Time Spiral* Block Constructed.
