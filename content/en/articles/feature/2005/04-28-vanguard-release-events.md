*Vanguard* Release events begin Thursday, May 5. For complete information on Vanguard, including information on how to build a Vanguard format deck, please refer to the [Vanguard Fact Sheet](/en/articles/faq/2005/04-28-magic-online-vanguard).

### Special Note regarding Invitations to the Championship and System Crashes:

If during a crash, an event is "finished" (prematurely ended) by the system or by a WotC representative, the system should award prizes and/or invites based on the standings at the time they were finished. We are going accept the prize payouts and invites "as is". No changes will be made to these events from what the system automatically pays out as prizes or awards invitations. If the tournament is still in the Swiss portion at the time it is "finished", Product and tickets will be refunded (use the link below to request a refund). If the event is in the Top 8 playoffs at the time it is "finished", no product or tickets will be refunded

If an event is cancelled by the system, tickets should be refunded automatically. We will not recreate these events, award prizes or invites for these events. The records of cancelled events are erased and it is not possible to reconstruct such events. In the case of a cancelled event, if you did not receive the system ticket refund or if you need to request a product refund, please [contact Customer Service](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=161).
