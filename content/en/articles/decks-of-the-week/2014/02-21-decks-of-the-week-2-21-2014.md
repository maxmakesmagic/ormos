Pro Tour *Born of the Gods* happens this weekend! Catch all the highlights—including every game in the Top 8—with our [top-notch coverage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=mtg/event/protour/bornofthegods14#viewer). Last weekend, a country away, Javier Dominguez won [Grand Prix Paris](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/gppar14/welcome) while, across the Atlantic, Marc Lalague won his second Grand Prix, in [Mexico City](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/gpmex14/welcome). Congratulations to Javier and Marc! They will be heading to Atlanta in May for Pro Tour *Journey into Nyx* and if you'd like to join them, you still have time. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtg/event/protour/qualifierlist#jou). Good luck; have fun!
