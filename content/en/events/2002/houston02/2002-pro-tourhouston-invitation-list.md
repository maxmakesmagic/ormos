Please note that "Threshold" invites are contingent upon play at intervening Pro Tours, but do prevent these individuals from playing in Qualifiers. Questions? Contact your Organizer or Andy Heckt at heckt@wizards.com

The top-50 previously uninvited ratings-based invitations for Pro Tour Houston will be taken from the September 25th publish of ratings. The DCI will also simultaneosly invite the top 50 previously invited from the Extended Ratings as of August 31, 2000, Frozen Extended Ratings. Anyone who would have been invited under the old system will receive an invitation, whether they received an invitation under the new system or not.
