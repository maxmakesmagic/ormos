Welcome back to Building on a Budget. This week I'd like to explore the possibility of a Standard Enchantress deck. [Kor Spiritdancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kor+Spiritdancer) makes a whole new archetype possible. [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas), an aura, is arguably the most well positioned card in Standard these days. Decks packing a full count of [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) get a huge percentage of wins off the [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) alone. I've found it to be very difficult to lose games to Jund where I win the die roll and have a [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) in my opening hand. That may seem like a rare occurrence, but it comes up surprisingly often. [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) also does an excellent job at turning off manlands in the late-game.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8d/1b/8d1bc528b792ad522988aa545c43d19af4118368a9efd5e4efb4450922f1c349.jpg)

I've recieved well over fifty emails from you guys with various lists for the archetype. Some lists have been straight white-blue, some green-white, but most lists have played with all three Bant colors.

The biggest choice, it seems, when designing a Standard Enchantress deck is whether or not to include [Sigil of the Empty Throne](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sigil+of+the+Empty+Throne). There are sound arguments on both sides of the debate. Personally, I like having a big win condition. [Kor Spiritdancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kor+Spiritdancer) can be big and bad, but having an aerial assault of 4/4s seems like a much more reasonable way to win the game. I'd love to hear your thoughts on this in the forums.

Lets take a look at the card choices:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/eb/aa/ebaa3cc082a01cc7503e8e24ede5a6f3f25a373bdd36045c323b59b23c695f6a.jpg)

[Kor Spiritdancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kor+Spiritdancer): The inspiration for the deck! [Kor Spiritdancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kor+Spiritdancer) provides us with an excellent defense, an absurd amount of card advantage, and a formidable win condition. Truly scary things become possible when you have multiple copies of this in play.

[Mesa Enchantress](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mesa+Enchantress): The extra mana here is a hefty price, but having more enchantress effects is crucial to the inner workings of the deck. [Mesa Enchantress](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mesa+Enchantress) lets us draw cards off our [Oblivion Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Ring)s and [Sigil of the Empty Throne](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sigil+of+the+Empty+Throne)s, so the extra mana isn't all for nought.

[Canopy Cover](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Canopy+Cover): [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile) seems like your worst enemy. [Canopy Cover](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Canopy+Cover) lets you abuse any opening your opponent might give you. If they tap out for a turn four [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) you can follow with a [Kor Spiritdancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kor+Spiritdancer) and a [Canopy Cover](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Canopy+Cover). [Canopy Cover](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Canopy+Cover) really shines against the planeswalker decks, once your [Kor Spiritdancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kor+Spiritdancer) has an Umbra and a [Canopy Cover](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Canopy+Cover) the game is just about over.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b4/5a/b45ac50b9ae2fc275b31527abea59a2643cd858a97c32f3d19fe3c21f49d0c80.jpg)

[Hyena Umbra](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hyena+Umbra): One mana for the totem armor effect is what's most important here. The first strike is very nice against decks like Jund. Making your [Kor Spiritdancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kor+Spiritdancer) immune to [Day of Judgment](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Day+of+Judgment) or [Martial Coup](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Martial+Coup) is always good.

[Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas): I've already talked at length about this one. [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) may be the best card in the current Standard. It happens to be an Aura, making it fit perfectly in this type of strategy.

[Sigil of the Empty Throne](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sigil+of+the+Empty+Throne): I wanted a good late-game finisher. [Sigil of the Empty Throne](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sigil+of+the+Empty+Throne) seems like my best option.

[Bear Umbra](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bear+Umbra): I just couldn't help myself. Giving my [Kor Spiritdancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kor+Spiritdancer) +4/+4 and totem armor, getting to untap all my lands to draw some more cards with the goodies I've found. Sign me up!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/71/97/7197e90ee0fdf0a4d9bafa35abda85abc83b1e989eca86dfad96554106669e7b.jpg)

[Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder): I'm worried that this deck won't perform very well if we cannot find a [Kor Spiritdancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kor+Spiritdancer) in the early stages of the game. I want to play [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder) to ensure we draw the right pieces at the right times.

[Oblivion Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Ring): I'm a strong believer in [Oblivion Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Ring) these days. Planeswalkers, [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Emrakul%252C%2Bthe%2BAeons%2BTorn)s, [Vengevine](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vengevine)s, you name it. [Oblivion Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Ring) gets the job done. We may not draw a card off our [Kor Spiritdancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kor+Spiritdancer), but we deal with whatever problem we need to.

[Crystallization](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crystallization): I thought about playing [Pacifism](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pacifism), but I did a quick Gatherer search and found this gem. I used to first pick it in drafts all the time, so I figure it will probably be good enough.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f3/d6/f3d60d7a50ff727815bd1dbe6fce6a4f03acac8add4c94173ddf48d4bd1ec4ea.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f0/ff/f0ffcd68a768f6a45008e4de1fe205132eea0b5c4a346a4289a502a51a28f432.jpg)

Sometimes [Kor Spiritdancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kor+Spiritdancer) can get really out of hand. The deck is not as consistent as I would like it to be, but once the cogs get going, this deck becomes very difficult to beat. Argothian Enchantress was one of my favorite cards of all time and I really wanted to build a new Enchantress deck. If I were going to add more money to this deck I would probably do so in the manabase. If you're lucky enough to have [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor)s or [Gideon Jura](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon+Jura)s then those may actually have a place in the deck. They don't necessarily work toward our engine, but their absurd power levels make them very tempting to play. [Lifelink](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifelink) seems like it could be a great sideboard option. I can't see a lot of decks beating an umbra'ed lifelinking [Kor Spiritdancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kor+Spiritdancer). There may be a build of this that has true competitive potential. Perhaps it will be revealed at the Grand Prix in Washington D.C. this weekend.
