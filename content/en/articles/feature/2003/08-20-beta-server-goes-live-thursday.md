### Now You Know Why I Said "Tomorrow"

* * *

You see, if I say "tomorrow" then it can always mean the day after you read the article. I planned it that way.

Okay, you're not buying it. On to the announcement:

First, I’d like to say that we found the bug that caused a majority of the recent crashes. While there are many of you who claimed that the server crashed every time you logged on, some of you were actually correct. We found a few accounts that were crashing the system, figured out what was happening, slapped our foreheads and said, “You’ve *got* to be kidding me.” The developers fixed those accounts and will be putting a patch in later so this never happens again.

Second, the beta server will be coming up Thursday morning at 7:00 AM PDT and will remain up until 5:00 PM PDT. Those are the hours the beta server will be up on business days for as long as we have it open. I’ll be posting instructions on accessing the beta server early tomorrow morning.

A few things about playing on the beta server:

The beta server **isn’t** attached to the live server in any way. Any cards, avatars, or ratings changes on the beta server can be wiped out at any time and **won’t** transfer over to the live server.

Even though cards are free on the beta server, please trade while you’re there. We need you to act as much like you would on the live server as possible.

Since we also want you to play in tournaments, we’ll have some special prizes. Things like, say, an Akroma avatar or maybe even Birds of Paradise.

The developers will be doing some extreme things to the code on the beta server. Chances are downtimes will happen and probably won’t be announced. (Yeah, I know, so what else is new?)

Lastly, since this is a test server, the normal moderation rules don’t apply. In other words, if someone on the ***Magic** Online* team decides you’re behaving inappropriately, they can kick you from the beta server indefinitely. For example, this probably isn’t the best place to voice your complaints to the developers about the downtime…they’re all pretty grouchy at this point.

Overall, we just want to come on board and have some fun while helping us clean up our mess. Check here or on the message boards Thursday morning for information regarding getting access to the beta server.

Oh, by the way, Wizards of the Coast employees aren’t restricted from tournaments on the beta server.

Just sayin’...

-Dan
