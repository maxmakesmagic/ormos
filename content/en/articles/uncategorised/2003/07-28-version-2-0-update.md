### Answers To Your Questions And Concerns

* * *

As most of you know by now, we're having performance issues with the planned ***Magic** Online* 2.0 update. I'm here to explain what's going on and clear up some issues that you're obviously concerned about.

### What's Happening

We're taking ***Magic** Online* down as the development team works on a solution. They will be bringing it back up sometime around 5:00 PM Pacific Daylight Time today to continue checking their work.

**Note:** When the development team is confident that they've resolved the performance issue, there will be an announcement made on the [Message Boards](https://web.archive.org/web/20030728000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/forumdisplay.php?forumid=217) and in the ***Magic** Online* game. Until that announcement, the team will be taking the game offline and restarting it as necessary. During this time there won't be any sanctioned events available on ***Magic** Online.*

### Player Concerns

The master server is **not** unstable--every instance of the server going down over the past 24 hours was because the development team took it offline to put in a fix. The update hasn't crashed the master server a single time, but it is causing serious performance issues. We haven't isolated the problem yet and are continuing to work on it.

Your card collections are also completely safe. Even if the problem is within the database, all of your information has been backed up and can be restored. Once we have Version 2.0 working at acceptable performance levels, all of your cards will still be there.

### *Eighth Edition* Cards And New Accounts

The ***Magic** Online* Store won't be selling either the *Eighth Edition* Core Set or new accounts until this problem is resolved. Once our solution is in place, we will announce when both will be available.

The *Eighth Edition* Release Events will also be rescheduled. They will take place two days after the set is available at the ***Magic** Online* Store.

### Conclusion

The development team will be working on the game until the performance issue is resolved. After spending the past night with the development team as they wrestled with the problem, I can assure you we understand your frustration and appreciate your patience.

Sincerely,  
 Daniel Myers  
*on behalf of the* **Magic** Online *Team*
