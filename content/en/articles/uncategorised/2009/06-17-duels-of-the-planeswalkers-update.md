As of 6 p.m Wednesday here at Wizards of the Coast, an unexpected system-wide issue has prevented Microsoft from pushing games live on Xbox LIVE® Arcade today. Unfortunately that means that Duels of the Planeswalkers is not yet available for download. The people at Microsoft are diligently working to resolve the problem, and we’ll let you know as soon as the game becomes available. Thank you for your patience.

**Update**: Microsoft has dealt with the issue and Duels of the Planeswalkers is [available now for download](http://marketplace.xbox.com/en-us/games/media/66acd000-77fe-1000-9115-d80258410860). For product information, tips, tricks, articles and more, visit [www.DuelsofthePlaneswalkers.com](http://www.duelsoftheplaneswalkers.com/).
