The first Grand Prix starring *Scars of Mirrodin* has come and gone, and Jeremy Neeman is its champion! The format was *Scars of Mirrodin* Sealed Deck on the first day of competition, followed by booster draft on the second. Jeremy's strategy drafting in the Top 8? An aggressive red and white one, and it served him well! Read full coverage of the event [here](/en/events/2010/gpsyd10/neeman-shatters-the-opposition-in-sydney).
