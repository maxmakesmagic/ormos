After 15 rounds, with a total of 1,944 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gplyo15/t8_neirynck.jpg)

### Branco Neirynck

**Age:** 25  
**Occupation:** *Magic Online* grinder  
**Hometown:** Bovekerke, Belgium  
**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** Top 8 at Grand Prix Utrecht 2015 and Grand Prix Copenhagen 2015. 2009 national champion, 2015 World *Magic* Cup team captain.

**What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
[Murk Strider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Murk+Strider), blue-red, 9-0.

**What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 Black/Green Tokens with two [Swarm Surge](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swarm+Surge)s and four [Eyeless Watcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eyeless+Watcher)s. 2-1.

**What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
[Akoum Firebird](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akoum+Firebird), blue-red, 2-0-1.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gplyo15/t8_seibold.jpg)

### Christian Seibold

**Age:** 26  
**Occupation:** Student  
**Hometown:** Nuremberg, Germany  
**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** Pro Tour *Born of the Gods* Top 8, Grand Prix Barcelona winner, Grand Prix Utrecht semifinalist, Grand Prix Stockholm Top 8.

**What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 8-1, blue-black. [Clutch of Currents](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clutch+of+Currents) was actually the best card in my deck.

**What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
[Rolling Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rolling+Thunder), red-black, 3-0.

**What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
[Ob Nixilis Reignited](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ob+Nixilis+Reignited), red-black, 2-0-1.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gplyo15/t8_saito.jpg)

### Tomoharu Saito

**Age:** 31  
**Occupation:** Hareruya CEO and pro player  
**Hometown:** Narita, Chiba, Japan  
**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** Pro Tour winner, four-time Grand Prix winner, and more.

**What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
[Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulamog%2C+the+Ceaseless+Hunger) and [Valakut Invoker](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut+Invoker). Blue/Black/Red Control and Red/White Beatdown, switch style! 8-1.

**What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
[Grip of Desolation](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grip+of+Desolation), white-black, 3-0.

**What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
[Ondu Rising](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ondu+Rising) and [Drowner of Hope](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drowner+of+Hope), green-white-blue, 2-1.

### Florian Reiter

**Age:** 28  
**Occupation:** Writer, editor, PR dude  
**Hometown:** Munich, Germany  
**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** Member of Team #GPWhatever. Not getting a haircut before this.

**What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 I played blue-white to an 8-1 finish. Best cards were Quarantine field and [Oblivion Sower](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Sower), shockingly.

**What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 I played Blue/Red Devoid, went 2-1. Best card was the splashed [Dominator Drone](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dominator+Drone), though everyone disagrees with me on this.

**What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 Black/Red Devoid Aggro. Best cards were my two copies of [Forerunner of Slaughter](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forerunner+of+Slaughter), although quadruple [Nettle Drone](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nettle+Drone) was helpful too.

**Age:** 37  
**Occupation:** Travel agent  
**Hometown:** Brussels, Belgium
