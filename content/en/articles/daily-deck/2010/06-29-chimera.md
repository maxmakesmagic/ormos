Nicholas Labarre is one of France's greatest deck building minds. At Worlds 2000 he took this deck, called "Chimera," to the Top 8. So how did it work? He used Saproling Cluster, Ashnod's Altar, , and Fecundity, to generate creatures, mana, and to draw cards. Serra Avatar allowed him to ensure he never decked himself while Whetstone served as a win condition. It was a wacky combo deck even by turn of the century standards and remains one of Labarre's best known decks.
