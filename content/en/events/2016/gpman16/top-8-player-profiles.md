After 15 rounds of Standard, with a total of 1,689 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpman16/gpman16_t8_hunt.jpg)Matthew Hunt

**Age:** 28

**Occupation:** Engineer

**Hometown:** Bath, United Kingdom

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Won the PTQ in Cardiff which was the final PTQ ever, I think.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Blue-Red Ulamog. It's fun, plays [Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulamog%2C+the+Ceaseless+Hunger) and a ton of counterspells. Why play anything else? Also, Chandra's pretty good.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

No idea. Maybe a few cards in the sideboard need switching up.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

Ulamog not really. [Drowner of Hope](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drowner+of+Hope) is the best.

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpman16/gpman16_t8_vuorela.jpg)Lauri Vuorela

**Age:** 30

**Occupation:** Supply chain coordinator

**Hometown:** Stockholm, Sweden

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

I lost the semifinals of two Game Days.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Bant Eldrazi, basically a [Cryptolith Rite](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptolith+Rite) deck with main deck [Reality Smasher](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reality+Smasher)s. Magnus Lantto and Oliver Tiu played it at the MOCS and I liked the way it played.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I'm not sure, maybe play something else instead of the Nissa, because she didn't really perform during the weekend.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Eldrazi Displacer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Displacer). As long as [Reflector Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reflector+Mage) hangs around at least.

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpman16/gpman16_t8_christensen.jpg)Oscar Christensen

**Age:** 20

**Occupation:** Substitute teacher

**Hometown:** Copenhagen, Denmark

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Grand Prix Gothenburg 2013 winner, Super Sunday Series finalist 2015, positive record against Christoffer Larsen.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Green-White Tokens, the best deck of the format. I haven't really played this Standard format before so the choice was obvious to me.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I would put a Plains into the sideboard so I could bring it in whenever I cut some copies of [Oath of Nissa](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oath+of+Nissa).

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

All of the four-ofs.

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpman16/gpman16_t8_friberg.jpg)Jonas Friberg

**Age:** 21

**Occupation:** Chef

**Hometown:** Stockholm, Sweden

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Top 8 at Grand Prix Barcelona 2016.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Grixis Control. It fits my play style and it has room for one-ofs to tune.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I would add a [Radiant Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Radiant+Flames) to the sideboard, not sure what to take out.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Chandra, Flamecaller](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Flamecaller).

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpman16/gpman16_t8_henry.jpg)Julien Henry

**Age:** 23

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Reims, France

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Played Pro Tour Oath of the Gatewatch, came in tenth at Grand Prix London 2015.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Bant Company. Very high power level and has game against everything.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I would not play [Clip Wings](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clip+Wings).

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Evolving Wilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Evolving+Wilds).

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpman16/gpman16_t8_larsen.jpg)Christoffer Larsen

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Navy

**Hometown:** Copenhagen, Denmark

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Three Grand Prix Top 8s, Top 8 at the World Magic Cup 2015, two-time Two-Headed Giant champion with Oscar Christensen.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Green-White. Should have played Martin Müller's Mono-Blue.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Nothing performed very well.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Oath of Nissa](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oath+of+Nissa).

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpman16/gpman16_t8_bajerowicz.jpg)Adam Bajerowicz

**Age:** 31

**Occupation:** Purchasing manager

**Hometown:** Gdańsk, Poland

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

—

**What deck did you play and why?**

Bant Company because it is a good deck against Green-White Tokens and against control.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I would not change anything.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Declaration in Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Declaration+in+Stone). It won many games for me.

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpman16/gpman16_t8_levy.jpg)Raphaël Lévy

**Age:** 34

**Occupation:** Publisher at Jits Magazine

**Hometown:** Toulouse, France

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Magic Pro Tour Hall of Fame. Thanks a lot to my sponsors: TCGPlayer.com, Fantasy Sphere, and Cartapapa.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Green-White with [Chandra, Flamecaller](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Flamecaller) in the main deck. It's the only deck I managed to win games with prior to the tournament.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Maybe cut the [Lambholt Pacifist](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lambholt+Pacifist)s, they were pretty underwhelming.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Chandra, Flamecaller](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Flamecaller)!
