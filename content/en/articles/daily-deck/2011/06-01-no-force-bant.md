The latest Legacy Grand Prix is in the books! Grand Prix Providence was a smashing success with over 1,000 players in attendance. Its champion? Legacy stalwart James Rynkiewicz, who took the title without a single Force of Will in his deck, even though he was playing blue! His amazing performance forced him to go through Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa to earn the dubya. Read more in the [official coverage](/en/events/2011/gppro11/a-legacy-champion-descends-from-jupiter)!
