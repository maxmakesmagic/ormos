## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6d/34/6d34de214c91c54a46c5d113f53ebc630106d4f6a7cebe47eaf450656774dd2d.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide, two card boxes, six plastic dividers, six 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, one random Pro Tour player card, a special-edition life counter, and the first book in the *Lorwyn* cycle.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8b/cd/8bcd61b304cbca46c5dd493d89513b1673d280ae987f552299a53f5be31c90f7.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f3/73/f3736e356448cc082f89c7b35baa3fd2ef5ec2aa9677c283cbc06f49860c3c21.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160712041236/http://magic.wizards.com/node/961826)

(\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a3/04/a304c6b0bf8fe2d5781f2aa6b8ca001a4ca9c209ba879333fc96f9bc7178ea54.png)

### TOURNAMENT PACKS

The foundation for exciting Sealed Deck play. Includes 45 randomly assorted cards and 30 basic land cards.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ed/51/ed51ad868e5b1b49c643f072dfbc5d946aaaf6949f3000563ab4559201a8528a.png)

The first set in the *Lorwyn-Shadowmoor* block, Lorwyn features 301 cards with randomly inserted premium cards.

**Set Name:** *Lorwyn*  
**Block:** Set 1 of 4 of the *Lorwyn-Shadowmoor* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 301

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160712041236/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/MTGLRW%20FAQ%20092507.doc)

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Aaron Forsythe (lead), Brady Dommermuth, Nathan Heiss, Paul Sottosanti, and Mark Rosewater, with contributions from Andrew Finch

**Final Game Design and Development:** Devin Low (lead), Doug Beyer, Matt Place, Bill Rose, Henry Stern, and Michael Turian, with contributions from Mark L. Gottlieb
