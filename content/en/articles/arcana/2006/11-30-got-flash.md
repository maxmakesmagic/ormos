![Mystical Teachings](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Mystical+Teachings)

So you're a time-bending chronomancer, consulting with a being from some dimension outside of time, and this being is about to let you in on one of its chronomantic secrets. In other words, you have [Mystical Teachings](https://web.archive.org/web/20061130000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystical+Teachings) on the stack—what kinds of cards can you tutor up?

Since *Time Spiral*, the flash keyword has shortened the Oracle text of several cards. Which ones now say “flash” on them matters a lot for [Mystical Teachings](https://web.archive.org/web/20061130000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystical+Teachings), so let's take a trip through the pre-*Time Spiral* cards that were affected by the new flash keyword.
