The month is nearly over, so once again it's time to take a trip in our Friday Night *Magic* time machine and see what promos we have in store (and in stores!) for the coming months.

**October**

The spookiest month deserves a spooky [Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20150922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price) promo. Get your own head in a bag with new art by Scott Murphy—and get it soon, because the colorless Eldrazi are about to have the upper hand.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/EN_td6tixlmi4.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_i436uu3dc2.jpg)

[Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20150922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price) Promo | Art by [Scott Murphy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Scott%20Murphy%22%5d)

**November**

Is it a coincidence that our hottest promos come during some of the coldest months in North America? Stay warm with this Zoltan Boros take on [Roast](https://web.archive.org/web/20150922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roast).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/c4rd_09b1vGHdZs.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/c4rd4r7_pAtmwPHvaG.jpg)

[Roast](https://web.archive.org/web/20150922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roast) Promo | Art by [Zoltan Boros](https://web.archive.org/web/20150922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Zoltan+Boros%22%5d)

**December**

For many, December is a highly anticipated month full of holidays, presents, the end of one year, and the start of a brand-new one. To celebrate, we're giving you a new tool to see what's ahead with an updated version of [Anticipate](https://web.archive.org/web/20150922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anticipate) from Volkan Baga. What? You didn't see that coming?
