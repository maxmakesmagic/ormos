They are a tribe as old as the game itself, and at Pro Tour–Amsterdam the Netherlands's own Robert Van Medevoort managed to use Goblins to make his mark on the tournament. Thanks to Simian Spirit Guide he can start with Warren Instigator on his very first turn, and Punishing Fire / Grove of the Burnwillows turns his late land flood into tons of extra damage. Powerful! Read more from the official coverage [here](/en/events/2010/ptams10/rietzl-sweeps-to-victory-in-amsterdam).
