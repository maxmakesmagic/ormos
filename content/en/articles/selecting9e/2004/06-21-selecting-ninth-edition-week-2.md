Ready for more?

This week your job is to decide between two powerful white flying creatures, [Blinding Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20040621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinding+Angel) and [Dawn Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20040621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dawn+Elemental). [Blinding Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20040621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinding+Angel) is a beatdown control device and a win condition rolled into one. [Dawn Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20040621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dawn+Elemental) is an efficient combat powerhouse unaffected by damage. Which will you choose to put into *Ninth Edition*?

![Blinding Angel vs. Dawn Elemental](https://i.ormos.uk/d8/4d/d84d99e42ca098c9db62ba0ef2cc180bfc03929dcf96d8465ae710f25d4f198d.jpg)

Thanks for making your voice heard. But this is just the beginning. Tune in next Monday -- and every Monday through September 6th -- to make even more important changes to your next Core Set.
