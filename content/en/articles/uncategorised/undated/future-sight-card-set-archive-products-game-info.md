### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide, two card boxes, six 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, one random Pro Tour player card, a special-edition life counter, and the *Future Sight* novel.

### BOOSTER PACKS

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/cd/dd/cddd874e85e13ad5dc59463e568be42ae77b9ccf67cf4d0042439204f8b5d29c.png)

The third and final set in the *Time Spiral* block, *Future Sight* features 180 cards, including 81 with a frame treatment from an alternative future timeline, and randomly inserted premium cards. Some “timeshifted” cards from this set have appeared in later expansions.

**Set Name:** *Future Sight*  
**Block:** Set 3 of 3 of the *Time Spiral* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 180

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718094827/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/MTGFUT%20FAQ%20032507.doc)

**Game Design:** Mark Rosewater (lead), Matt Cavotta, Mark L. Gottlieb, Devin Low, Ryan Miller, and Zvi Mowshowitz

**Game Development:** Michael Turian (lead), Matt Cavotta, Matt Place, and Brian Schneider, with contributions from Mark L. Gottlieb
