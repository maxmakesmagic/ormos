With the online release of *New Phyrexia*, *Scars of Mirrodin* Block Constructed is now fully playable on [**Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20110925172955/http://www.wizards.com/magic/Digital/MagicOnline.aspx?)! This version of White Weenie went undefeated in a recent event. Featuring a strong assortment of white creatures, it also uses artifact creatures backed by Tempered Steel to pack additional punch. You don't want to be on the wrong end of a Memnite when it's a 3/3—or larger!
