Officially sanctioned events offer a variety of opportunities to play *Magic*. Even better, they create a place to meet new friends who enjoy the game just as much as you.

You can find small gatherings of players who just want to play against others who love the game. Or join large-scale multi-day tournaments and see how you rank against those who take their passion for *Magic* to the competitive stage.

Whether you’re trying to break onto the Pro Tour or are new to *Magic*, Organized Play has something for you. Read on to learn about your options.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d8/e2/d8e2457ae8afc7f10c2d04c93941a6bca7ec00203bbab8cfe73910b3a923762f.png)

### GRAND PRIX

Grand Prix are the ultimate Magic experience. These three-day events, held all around the globe, feature a wide array of side events, card dealers, artists, and a two-day main event with thousands of dollars on the line. It’s a festival of Magic unlike anything else.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20190113232117/https://magic.wizards.com/content/eventsschedulegrand-prix)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/53/0b/530b0f8551254206ffc663d8b6e49598c2c46a13d0817076fc4daeb57515ece8.png)

### PRO TOUR

Compete against the greatest Magic players around the globe for your share of a $250,000 prize purse.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20190113232117/https://magic.wizards.com/content/eventsscheduleprotours)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/18/eb/18ebdce40970eed59a0c947659db78b5b7dfdac24687b0d0b08a6b9f9732a4c0.png)

### PRELIMINARY PRO TOUR QUALIFIERS

Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifiers (PPTQs) are the first step for new players on their path to the *Magic: The Gathering* Pro Tour.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20190113232117/https://magic.wizards.com/en/protour/preliminaryqualifiers)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/21/ec/21ec801904456a805bdd19908046d0d92ee057b4a092420bd8906959d710fef5.png)

### REGIONAL PRO TOUR QUALIFIERS

Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers (RPTQs) are your chance to play on the *Magic: The Gathering* Pro Tour! Top finishers at a Regional PTQ will receive an invitation – and airfare – to the Pro Tour that it qualifies for!

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20190113232117/https://magic.wizards.com/en/protour/regionalqualifiers)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a5/a2/a5a291c911047c88a647a6d6eb1fcd630df9e05fdcb0a77c644ab7b4e3bbb447.png)

### WORLD MAGIC CUP

With teams of four representing more than 70 countries, the World Magic Cup is like no other tournament on the planet! Fame, glory, and a year’s worth of the ultimate bragging rights await the team that can navigate multiple formats over several days to claim the World Magic Cup.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20190113232117/https://magic.wizards.com/content/eventsscheduleworldmagiccup)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d3/48/d348f3a919e9b3e1bbae8c667952e45f803e5009d83b2a13fa6ea245cefc97af.png)

### NATIONALS

Are you ready to take on the world? Do you dream of representing your country and hoisting your flag at the World *Magic* Cup? Challenge the best players in your country at Nationals and play your way onto your National team for the World *Magic* Cup.

[LEARN MORE](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/nationals)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/48/a2/48a268eaad24848da4402fc27dc29103f171ddd4ad41fc4952ef239b9bc92410.png)

### WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP

The world’s top twenty-four players will chase after the same life-changing prize, but only one can be the Magic World Champion. Will it be you?

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20190113232117/https://magic.wizards.com/node/116773)
