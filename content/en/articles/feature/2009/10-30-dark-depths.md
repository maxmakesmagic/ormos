Upon the release of *Zendikar*, players were abuzz with the possibilities of combining [Dark Depths](https://web.archive.org/web/20091030000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Depths) with [Vampire Hexmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20091030000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vampire+Hexmage). Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa demonstrated the deck is for real by riding it to the Top 8 at Pro Tour–Austin.
