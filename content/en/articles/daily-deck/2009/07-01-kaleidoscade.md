Kaleidoscope is a fun Magic alternative available on Magic Online in which players must use decks where all nonland cards are multicolored! TheUsualSuspect took this list to first place during a recent Premier Event featuring the format. A key to the deck’s success? Cascade! Bloodbraid Elf and Enlisted Wurm make sure the best spells are always hitting the table. End result? A Kaleidoscope championship!
