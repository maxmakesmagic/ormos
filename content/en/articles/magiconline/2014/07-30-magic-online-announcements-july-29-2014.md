What Is the *Magic Online* Weekly Announcements Blog?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6e/4a/6e4a7a020ca50c43233f51a0b066f9f7456faed9cbb7fb16e2fad2f3d308ec0b.jpg)

Every Tuesday, we round up all of the biggest *Magic Online* news and post it right here in the Weekly Announcements Blog. Check in every Tuesday for all of the latest news!

Magic Pro Tour Qualifier Announcements Today and Tomorrow

Check out [Helene Bergeot's article](/en/articles/feature/2014/07-29-20142015-premier-play-updates) to read about upcoming changes to the structure of face-to-face Pro Tour Qualifiers. Once you're done with that, check out [MTGO.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20140730000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/magic-online) and [DailyMTG.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207194842/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles) tomorrow for information around Pro Tour Qualifiers on *Magic Online*.

Wizards of the Coast is Looking for New Team Members

Ever wanted to work at Wizards of the Coast? Now's a good time to apply! The *Magic* Digital Team is currently seeking to fill two positions.

[***Duels of The Planeswalkers*, Technology Project Manager**](https://web.archive.org/web/20140730000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/technology-project-manager-duels-planeswalkers-job-renton-wa-us)

If you love *Duels of the Planeswalkers* and have a passion for delivering games on-time, on-budget, and of the highest quality – then this might be the career for you!

[***Magic Online* – Card-Set Project Manager**](https://web.archive.org/web/20140730000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/technology-project-manager-temporary-job-renton-wa-us)

Fundamental to everything we do – the Project Manager for *Magic Online* card-sets will have a passion working with multiple teams to deliver amazing experiences to our players – on-time, on-budget, and of the highest quality!

July MOCS and MOPR Seasons End July 30, 2014

As a reminder, the July seasons for MOCS and MOPR both end tomorrow, July 30. After the downtime tomorrow, the new August MOCS season begins! August's promo is [Show and Tell](https://web.archive.org/web/20140730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Show+and+Tell)! The July MOCS promo, [Geist of Saint Traft](https://web.archive.org/web/20140730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geist+of+Saint+Traft), as well as the July MOPR promos, [Dissolve](https://web.archive.org/web/20140730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dissolve) and [Magma Spray](https://web.archive.org/web/20140730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Spray), will be granted during the downtime.
