Do you remember [Copperhoof Vorrac](https://web.archive.org/web/20100927000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Copperhoof+Vorrac)? It was one of the three [Vorrac-related cards](https://web.archive.org/web/20100927000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?name=+%5B%22vorrac%22%5D) in *Mirrodin*.
