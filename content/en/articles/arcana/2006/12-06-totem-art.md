No, not *token* art—today’s Magic Arcana is about the art of *Time Spiral’s totems*.

Each of the five totems is an artifact that generates colored mana, and turns into a facsimile of a past **Magic** creature when activated. The art of each totem was designed to conjure the referenced creature as well; in fact, in most cases, the same artist as the original creature card was brought back to illustrate the corresponding totem.

Check it out—mouse over the *Time Spiral* art below to see the art of the original creature the totem becomes.
