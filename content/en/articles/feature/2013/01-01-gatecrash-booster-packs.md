It's the first day of the year and we're already deep into *Gatecrash* previews. Let's keep the momentum going with a look at the booster packs!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d6/b8/d6b84efe46fb56b3a04da9f46b01abab80ece11eea59fefb3b6dc80cdb4277da.jpg)

There are five *Gatecrash* booster packs, so it seems like a safe guess that the five guilds of *Gatecrash* will be represented. This one is Dimir!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c4/5c/c45c670ca778d7e297707b4cf63ac228a272863a9d940dbef3cab0cb7f6a3a3d.jpg)

Let's see... this is a big goon of a bruiser, so this is probably Gruul.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/61/c1/61c1e0dbdb5d7b31d30a6a94982c6d6529296ca2e27a306a8119a2c803db257d.jpg)

It's not immediately clear which guild this image is attached to. So we'll do you a favor: it's Simic!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/30/13/301365c2d782fe3609cd47a79fe68fdbe4dd14bbf45702c9ed003086fa18e5a7.jpg)

This image shows an Orzhov mage of some sort.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/fa/d9face4bbe09183d5444169908684990b9903c1d439b7e22c5ca51ade733c4a9.jpg)
