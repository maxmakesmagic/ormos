## Encore Halloween *Innistrad* Draft

It wouldn't be Halloween without a trip back to the original Draft format that introduced us to the spoopiest plane in the Multiverse!

**Dates**: October 27–November 3  
**Format**: A League of up to three Best-of-Three matches of Triple *Innistrad* (INN) draft.  
**Phantom**: No  
**Entry Options**:

*   12 Event Tickets
*   3 *Innistrad* boosters + 2 Event Tickets
*   120 Play Points

**Prizes**:

*   3 Wins: 6 *Innistrad* boosters, 2 QPs, and a Competitive Trophy
*   2 Wins: 2 *Innistrad* boosters

## *Vintage Cube* October 2021 Update

Just in time for the spooky season, *Innistrad: Midnight Hunt* is here, bringing with it a host of changes to *Vintage Cube*. This time, we're looking at expanding the parts of the sandbox that include build-arounds and reactive cards. Read about the changes in [Emma Handy's article](/en/articles/magiconline/2021/10-19-vintage-cube-october-2021-update), check out the change list below, and see [the full card list on the updated *Vintage Cube* page](/en/articles/uncategorised/2016/11-30-vintage-cube-cardlist).

## 2021 *Magic Online* Champions Showcase Season 2

The second *Magic Online* Champions Showcase of 2021 is almost here, featuring eight players battling for their share of $70,000 and the title of the 2021 Season 2 Showcase Champion!

The 2021 *Magic Online* Champions Showcase Season 2 will be streamed live beginning at 10 a.m. PT on October 30 at [twitch.tv/magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic).

You will find decklists for the event at the [2021 *Magic Online* Champions Showcase Season 2 event page](https://magic.gg/events/2021-magic-online-champions-showcase-season-2), with round-by-round standings and updates provided through the live broadcast.

For the more info on the event, make sure to check out the [viewers guide here](https://magic.gg/news/2021-magic-online-champions-showcase-season-2-viewers-guide).

## Eternal Weekend Dates

Get ready for Eternal Weekend 2021, taking place through *Magic Online* next month:

*   Legacy events will be held on *Magic Online* on the weekend of November 19–21, 2021.
*   Vintage events will be held on *Magic Online* on the following weekend of November 26–28, 2021.

The All-Access pass is back for these events, with six new artworks featuring some of the most iconic cards in both formats up for grabs as prizes. [Check out all the details over on Magic.gg](https://magic.gg/news/get-ready-for-eternal-weekend-2021) and mark your calendar for Eternal Weekend 2021!

## Limited Qualifiers Saturday and Sunday

On Saturday, October 30, there will be an *Innistrad: Midnight Hunt* Sealed Qualifier with a special start time of 1 a.m. PT (08:00 UTC). On Sunday, October 31, there will be another *Innistrad: Midnight Hunt* Sealed Qualifier at 7 a.m. PT (14:00 UTC). The events cost 40 Season 3 QPs, and the winner will earn an invitation to the *Innistrad* Championship event on *MTG Arena*.

Learn more about Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021#ptq).

* * *

## Limited Super Qualifier Saturday

On Saturday, October 30, there will be an *Innistrad: Midnight Hunt* Sealed Super Qualifier with a special start time of 2 p.m. PT (21:00 UTC). The entry fee is 40 MOCS Qualifier Points (Season 3), 40 Event Tickets, or 400 Play Points. The top two finishers qualify for the *Innistrad* Championship on *MTG Arena*.

The normally scheduled Saturday Limited Preliminaries are cancelled. Learn more about Super Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021#sq).

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format Challenges are the first step toward qualifying for Premier Play through your favorite *Magic* formats and provide excellent prizes for those looking to test their mettle in their favorite formats. [Learn more about Premier Play qualification](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021) to get started today.

*Magic Online*'s Format Challenges run once on Saturdays and once on Sundays for each Constructed format! On weekends featuring Showcase Challenge events, the Format Challenge that starts at either 6 a.m., 8 a.m., or 10 a.m. Pacific time is pre-empted by the Showcase Challenge—Format Challenges scheduled at other times remain in place. *NOTE: Pauper, Vintage, and Standard Challenges are never pre-empted.*

*New for MOCS Season 3: Pauper Showcase Challenges are held at 8 a.m. Saturday and do not pre-empt the Modern Challenge.*
