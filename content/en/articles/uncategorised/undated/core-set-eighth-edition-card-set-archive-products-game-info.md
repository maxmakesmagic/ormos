## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/08/49/084975385572f6a69671e24472eddc08d516dfd408e1fc037e5e2b0737ada71b.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/08/49/084975385572f6a69671e24472eddc08d516dfd408e1fc037e5e2b0737ada71b.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 40 cards and a strategy guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718093745/http://magic.wizards.com/node/976541)  
 (\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/08/49/084975385572f6a69671e24472eddc08d516dfd408e1fc037e5e2b0737ada71b.png)

### TWO-PLAYER STARTER GAME

Includes two 30-card decks, one 14-card booster, a collectable premium card, two play guides, rulebook, two play mats with scorekeeping counters, and a CD-ROM with access to Magic Online.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/46/bf/46bf8530bb27c8a13b54b639bb89725d093068af3ed1c4502e896b65b51d7131.png)*Core Set Eighth Edition* features 350 cards, including at least one from every set since *Alpha*! For the first time, the core set also features randomly inserted premium black-bordered versions of all cards in the set.

**Set Name:** *Core Set Eighth Edition*  
**Number of Cards:** 350

**Languages:** English, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, German, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional
