*This Arcana originally appeared on October 18, 2012.*

Two weeks ago, we [showed you the winners](/en/articles/arcana/2012/10-05-coloring-contest-winners) of the PAX Prime Coloring Contest. But we didn't show you the whole coloring book!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8d/a4/8da472ec897fa10cc65bfa046509e7c75779b76e793ac714bc5fda897acd3f23.jpg)

The art was created by Mike "Gabriel" Krahulik from [Penny Arcade](http://www.penny-arcade.com/). And it came with these neat **Magic**-themed crayons!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/36/de/36de2aee472293641cba9314252965ea813da6886da7c1f3b9a25e189e617bcc.jpg)

Incidentally, the crayon "Violet Ultimatum" was named by Monty Ashley, who is also the person who's writing this Arcana. That isn't important; I just wanted to mention it because it's a pretty good pun and I'm proud of it.

Anyway! Mike Krahulik did eight Planeswalkers, and now we're going to show them to you! Click on each thumbnail to get a life-size page, suitable for printing and coloring in at home.
