![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsto14/Metagame.png)

  

Overall, [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) is still the card to beat, but all of the five clans from **Khans of Tarkir** are included in the top of the archetype list. The rise of Mardu in particular is noteworthy; many players followed Brad Nelson's lead. Besides the five clans, there's a whole slew of mono-color and two-color decks being played today as well, so Standard appears to be quite diverse.
