The blue-black transmute spell **[Perplex](https://web.archive.org/web/20051110000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Perplex)** represents House Dimir's harsh idea of countermagic. Since Dimir guildmages are dedicated to secrecy, the choice they provide to their victims is simple: stop your activities, or lose your mind.

The art for Perplex underwent quite a progression from initial sketch to final art. Follow artist Tsutomu Kawade through three artistic stages in this interactive look at [Perplex](https://web.archive.org/web/20051110000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Perplex)'s journey to illustration.
