The color pair is Red/Blue and our theme is "Two Elements: Fire and Air".

You will now be voting on creatures to include in the theme deck.

Listed for vote are the red and blue creatures that *could* be included. Some that just won't fit have been left out. Note that many will be more appropriate than others. See if you can stay on theme!

They've been grouped by commonality. Vote for 6 out of 11 commons and 6 out of 12 uncommons.
