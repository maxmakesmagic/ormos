Yesterday, [I wrote about](/en/articles/daily-deck/2013/03-26-jund-in-all-formats) a strategy's successful port from Standard to Block Constructed. Today, I'm going to examine a deck that has recently exploded on **Magic Online** despite not yet having physical results that can be seen as an updated port of a Block deck. 

 ![Unburial Rites](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Unburial%20Rites&options=) 

The Block deck is the WBR deck [I've previously written about](/en/articles/daily-deck/2013/03-20-spark-trooper-control) — what I consider Block's answer to midrange Jund strategies. 

It's unusual to see several copies of a deck 4–0 daily events on **Magic Online** without previous tournament success, so I asked Prolepsis9 where this deck came from. This deck was reportedly designed by Good Gamery's NicotineJones, who's been responsible for a number of other innovative decks recently, and was reportedly popularized by cjlack92's MOCS [Top 4 finish](https://web.archive.org/web/20130601013758/http://www.wizards.com/magic/digital/magiconlinetourn.aspx?x=mtg/digital/magiconline/tourn/5088522) with the deck. 

Like its Block predecessor, this deck uses Boros Reckoner; Aurelia, the Warleader; and Obzedat, Ghost Council as its core creatures, and supports them with removal and Rakdos's Return. 

Unlike the Block version, this deck gets to take advantage of the *Innistrad* Block trio of flashback spells Lingering Souls, Unburial Rites, and Faithless Looting, which all play marvelously together. 

This is cjlack92's more recent version from a later Standard Daily Event.

cjlack92's Dega Midrange 

Standard – 4–0, Magic Online Daily #5193045
