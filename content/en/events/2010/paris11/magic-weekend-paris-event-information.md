### Grand Prix Tournament Format and Schedule

Grand Prix Paris is a two-day event that is open to the public. Day One is *Scars of Mirrodin / Mirrodin Besieged* Sealed Deck and will be held on Saturday, February 12. Day Two is *Scars of Mirrodin / Mirrodin Besieged* Booster Draft and will be held on Sunday, February 13, with a *Scars of Mirrodin / Mirrodin Besieged* Booster Draft Top 8. Registration for the Grand Prix will be on Thursday, February 10 and Friday, February 12 from 9 a.m. to 11 p.m. each day. Note: There will be no Saturday morning registration for the Grand Prix. Be sure to register on Thursday or Friday to ensure a spot in the Grand Prix.

### Pro Tour Tournament Format and Schedule

Pro Tour Paris is a three-day invitation-only Standard and *Scars of Mirrodin / Mirrodin Besieged* Booster Draft format event with an Standard Top 8. Day One will be held on Thursday, February 10, Day Two on Friday, February 11, and then the Top 8 on Sunday, February 13. Note: The Last-Chance Qualifier for Pro Tour Paris will take place at **Espace Charenton** approximately 20 minutes from the **Magic** Weekend venue. Complete information about the Last-Chance Qualifier can be found on the Public Events page.

 1. Get the shuttle bus «Paris par le train» from Paris-Orly to the RER C station «Pont de Rungis Aéroport d’Orly».

*   If you arrive from Paris-Orly South, the shuttle bus stops at entrance F
*   If you arrive from Paris-Orly West, the shuttle bus stops at entrance G Arrival level.

### Site Attractions

 

**PUBLIC EVENTS**  
 Come one, come all to participate in five days of events open to the public. Starting Wednesday with the Last-Chance Qualifier, there are tournaments, drafts, Magic Commander, and open dueling all weekend. Magic Weekend Paris will also play host to a few extra-special events, highlighted by Grand Prix Paris on Saturday and Sunday. Public event prizes include invitations and airfare to Pro Tour Nagoya, foil card sets, an Xbox gaming system, iPods, Power 9 cards, and much more! 

Please be advised that there the Last-Chance Qualifier for Pro Tour Paris will be held at [**Espace Charenton** approximately 20 minutes](http://maps.google.com/maps?f=d&source=s_d&saddr=Espace+Charenton+323bis+rue+de+Charenton+Paris&daddr=Rue+Jean+Oestreicher,+75017+Paris,+France+\(Paris+Expo+Espace+de+Champerret\)&geocode=FRMh6QIdeZEkACFyWzpGxID55w%3BFXbx6QIdGukiACG79JW9CM1j-g&hl=en&mra=ls&sll=48.903561,2.3112&sspn=0.312785,0.801315&ie=UTF8&z=13) from the **Magic** Weekend venue. 

*   [Complete **Magic** Weekend Paris Public Events Information](/en/events/2011/paris11-public/magic-weekend-paris-2011-public-event-information)

**PLAYER OF THE YEAR SHOWDOWN**  
 Brad Nelson and Guillaume Matignon will battle at **Magic** Weekend Paris in a best-of-seven match to determine the winner of the 2010 Player of the Year award. The two players finished in a tie for the title at the 2010 World Championships. The showdown will take place Saturday, February 12 starting at 9 a.m., during the three-round bye period of Grand Prix Paris. Here's how the schedule breaks down:

*   Games 1-2: Standard
*   Games 3-4: Double Sealed Deck
*   Game 5\*: Double Sealed Deck
*   Games 6-7\*: Standard

\* if necessary

Both players will use the same decks they played in the Standard portion of Pro Tour Paris. The Double Sealed Deck portion will consist of six *Scars of Mirrodin* booster packs and six *Mirrodin Besieged* booster packs.

**SPELLSLINGERS**  
 Test your **Magic** skills against Wizards of the Coast R&D members Mark Gottlieb, Dave Humpherys, and Ken Troop. Win and you can walk away with cool **Magic** prizes! 

*   [More Information about **Magic** Weekend Paris Spellslingers](/en/events/2011/paris11-spellslingers/magic-weekend-paris-spellslingers)

**ARTISTS ON SITE**  
 Prominent **Magic** artists Aleksi Briclot and Igor Kieryluk will be on hand for free signings of your favorite cards, plus you’ll have the opportunity to buy prints and even original artwork. Artist booth times to be announced. 

*   [More Information about **Magic** Weekend Paris Artists](/en/events/2011/paris11-artists/magic-weekend-paris-2011-artists)

**WATCH THE PRO TOUR SEMIFINALS AND FINALS**  
 Sunday's Pro Tour Top 8 semifinals and finals can be viewed on screens in the hall, with Pro Tour Statistician Rich Hagon and Pro Tour Historian Brian David-Marshall providing the commentary. The matches are also available as a webcast from the comfort of your own home.
