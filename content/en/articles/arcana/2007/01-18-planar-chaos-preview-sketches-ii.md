It's time again to take a look at the sketches and art of some of the mind-blowing *Planar Chaos* preview cards. Read the italicized text to see the description handed over to the artist, check out the sketch he or she turned in thereafter, and then mouse over the sketch to see the final art!

### Torchling

> Art description provided to the artist: *Show a pair of red-aligned human warriors (styleguide look/feel) in roughly the same composition as art ID 8849 (Morphling) uses. But whereas that piece shows one of the researchers with a little tail and leathery wings, this piece should show one of the warriors with hair and eyes of flame. (The idea here is that one is the real warrior and the other is a fire-changeling copying it.)*

*Torchling sketch and art by rk post*  
  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/b1/b4/b1b45eba4bdad7686337132dcd722e613f16040f6f2d540c9f1d92569af7f81e.jpg)

### Null Profusion

Art description provided to the artist: *Show the mage Ixidor in an alternate timeline in which he turns to black-aligned magic (instead of blue magic). He is on his knees, and his mind is directly connected to a plane of darkness and void. How to represent this is up to you, but it should be clear that he is directly channeling the darkest of magic.*

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/04/1b/041bb1bd69b294111f1294d02819d1ffd667aad83fb5c5edaf1fc244b073074a.jpg)](#)  
*Null Profusion sketch and art by Kev Walker*  
  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/97/fb/97fbeac3f56e7494f8fa5076d1a25f7a37b39729a46ea2c95634a3d92dc1d604.jpg)

### Harmonize

Art description provided to the artist: *Show an old human druid sitting in a small clearing in the forest. The clearing is demarcated somehow, as with some mossed-over rocks in a rough circle. The druid is in deep meditation. Maybe moths or leaves or fireflies circle the druid in a loose, lazy orbit.* 

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/3a/3f/3a3fe2cf77e131c3c015a3b6217159a1d7ccc14fe01635699028b9c43c7c7356.jpg)](#)  
*Harmonize sketch and art by Rob Alexander*
