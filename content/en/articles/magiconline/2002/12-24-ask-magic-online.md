### Becoming A *Magic Online* Adept

* * *

<table><tbody><tr><td><p>So, I've gotten a few emails since I offered to reopen Ask <i><b>Magic </b>Online,</i> although not as many as I'd expected. I've taken the questions that have come up most frequently and started gathering answers from the folks here at Wizards. As I get answers, I'll post them here (hopefully to the tune of once a week). So keep sending your questions in to <a>askmagiconline@wizards.com</a> and I'll keep hunting for the answers.</p>&#10;<p>Remember, if you have support questions, check the <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20021224000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/mofaq&amp;origin=magic_magiconline_askmagiconline"><i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> FAQ</a> or contact one of the <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20041214061621/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/csmain"><i><b>Magic </b>Online </i>support groups</a> for your answer.</p>&#10;<table><tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td> <b>January 9, 2003</b></td>&#10;<td> </td>&#10;<td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/50/6a/506a81655d6f8e73a751a1459805e125bbc0f9a665920c3a74b8f7a7a16a7928.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr>&#10;<td colspan="3">&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td><p><b>Q:</b> How can I become a <i><b>Magic </b>Online </i>Adept?</p>&#10;<p><i>From Paul Quinn, <b>Magic </b> Online Community Manager:</i></p>&#10;<p><b>A: </b> The <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> Adept team is made up of both volunteers and hired staff who share the awesome responsibility of helping out with Community Support. New Adepts must start out as volunteers in order to learn the ropes and see if this is really something that they want to do (it might seem like all fun, but it is not for everyone!) and if things go well they may have an opportunity to be part of the hired staff. While they essentially do the same job, most people prefer to stick with the volunteer system rather than commit to the rigors of the hired staff.</p>&#10;<p>For more information on being a <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> Adept, go to the website and click on <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20041214060043/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/moadepts">Meet the <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> Adept team</a>. </p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table><br><table><tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td> <b>December 25, 2002</b></td>&#10;<td> </td>&#10;<td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/c7/c1/c7c1fe5cf49f58117fbd931561f1b60bd3183ad6276cf758a8fd3c7d6702fe27.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr>&#10;<td colspan="3">&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td><p><b>Q:</b> The <b>Trading Post </b>rooms are full of bad information and players fill the rooms with spam. Does Wizards perceive this as a problem? Are there plans to fix it?</p>&#10;<p><i>From Paul Quinn, <b>Magic </b> Online Community Manager:</i></p>&#10;<p><b>A: </b><br> a) Yes we see this as a problem.</p>&#10;<p>b) We are working on a solution.</p>&#10;<p>The problem is that trading, in general, is chaotic. Before the new setup it was a chat room and was absolutely unusable. The new setup is much better, but has it's drawbacks including no way to regulate what kind of trade actually goes into which room nor any way to change the posts without kicking the player offline. We don't have the resources to patrol the trade rooms and make sure everyone is posting in the correct category. We also decided to allow people to be in multiple rooms so they can have multiple trade categories. We have the ability to only allow people in one trade room at a time and it's up for consideration since some players don't seem to be able to exercise any semblance of restraint.</p>&#10;<p>The underlying issue with the <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> secondary market is that the nature of our system has allowed the market to explode exponentially. With physical cards people have to collect the cards and sell them to a shop, or shops had to buy, sort, and sell cards during business hours to their local players. <i><b>Magic</b> Online</i> is a 24/7, global forum for the secondary market and savvy people have taken advantage of that. It has become so big that casual players are being affected. This is a direct result of our policy not to become involved in the secondary market. Since traders must use the same system that the players use (unlike with physical cards) the two cultures collide and we have no system of segregation.</p>&#10;<p><i>From Daniel Myers, <b>Magic </b>Online Web Site Producer:</i></p>&#10;<p>The word from the development team is that they're working on additional functionality that should alleviate a lot of the issues players are having with the current <b>Trading Post </b>rooms. It's still in the planning phase, so there's no timeline on it currently. I'll give more details once they've got things hammered out.</p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table><br><table><tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td> <b>December 17, 2002</b></td>&#10;<td> </td>&#10;<td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/f0/9f/f09f4d2a172231ed02bcfadb60e0264389ebd0a5d2bbaaf40ad6a927154a8f0e.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr>&#10;<td colspan="3">&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td><p><b>Q:</b> The redemption feature has been talked about since before <i><b>Magic </b>Online </i>launched. What's causing the delay?</p>&#10;<p><i>From John Doyle, Director: Digital Object Technology</i><br><b>A: </b> The path to redemption has been long and arduous, with many twists and pitfalls. The rush towards our July launch required us to make some hard decisions about which features we launched with and we chose to hold off on redemption.</p>&#10;<p>The original code, both game and store, for the feature was completed in September and hit test in October. We ran into a host of issues, including which international locations we could support, warehousing problems, and the actual card packaging, all of which put us well behind schedule. All of those issues have been solved and QA has signed off on the feature so I expect you’ll be seeing redemption in the very near future. </p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table><br></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>
