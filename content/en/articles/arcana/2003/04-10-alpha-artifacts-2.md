Below are four artifacts from Richard Garfield's Alpha playtesting. You can click on each card to see the version that was printed.

The two numbers in the top corner are mana cost and activation cost, respectively. Yes, early versions of Gauntlet of Might cost ![1 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/4c/6b4cf558eab279fb9414f146ebdd0e0705ec92984fec530597dd2a3bce43ce24.gif) to play, whereas the earliest Black Vise cost ![4 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/36/29/3629288966daa042decf01d3752789ab6676fddd67c3679fa580cd3baecae158.gif).
