After many rounds of Swiss and some great games of *Magic*, the Top 8 for Grand Prix Detroit is set! Learn more about this event's Top 8 competitors, their history at the top tables, and more by taking a look through the Top 8 profiles.

* * *

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpdet16/gpdet16_t8_batesh.jpg)**Ralph Betesh

**Age:** 30

**Occupation:** Wi-Fi Tech

**Hometown:** Philadelphia, PA

**Previous Magic accomplishments:** Top 8 Grand Prix Miami

**What deck did you play and why?** Abzan Company. I didn't lose a match to White-Blue Eldrazi on MTGO in the past week of testing.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?** Nothing as of now - if Eldrazi get banned you don't need [Intrepid Hero](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Intrepid+Hero), and possibly the [Orzhov Pontiff](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Orzhov+Pontiff) in the main.

**Are the Eldrazi inherently evil? Would that mean Eldrazi players are inherently evil too?** 100%.

**What's your favorite *Shadows over Innistrad* preview so far, or, what's your favorite *Innistrad* memory?** I haven't looked at any previews, but I liked [Charmbreaker Devils](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Charmbreaker+Devils) and [Into the Maw of Hell](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Into+the+Maw+of+Hell) in Limited in *Innistrad.*

* * *

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpdet16/gpdet16_t8_english.jpg)**Eric English

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Lab Technician

**Hometown:** Rochester, NY.

**Previous Magic accomplishments:** Won a StarCityGames.com Open and have one Pro Tour appearance.

**What deck did you play and why?** Red-Green Eldrazi. The lands are busted and [Kozilek's Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kozilek%27s+Return) plus [World Breaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=World+Breaker) is incredible.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?** Nothing unless I could figure out a better plan against White-Blue Eldrazi.

**Are the Eldrazi inherently evil? Would that mean Eldrazi players are inherently evil too?** Of course not, squiggly monsters are great!

**What's your favorite *Shadows Over Innistrad* preview so far, or, what's your favorite *Innistrad* memory?** [Spider Spawning](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spider+Spawning) was easily one of my most favorite Limited archetypes ever.

* * *

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpdet16/gpdet16_t8_thompson.jpg)**Gerry Thompson

**Age:** 31

**Occupation:** Writer for StarCityGames.com.

**Hometown:** Roanoke, VA

**Previous Magic accomplishments:** I'm making a run for the Player's Championship.

**What deck did you play and why?** White-Blue Eldrazi. It's the best deck and I'm trying to win more.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?** [Shriekmaw](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shriekmaw), [Calciform Pools](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Calciform+Pools) and [Training Grounds](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Training+Grounds) all seemed sweet.

**Are the Eldrazi inherently evil? Would that mean Eldrazi players are inherently evil too?** No, we're the good guys who are cleaning up Modern.

**What's your favorite *Shadows Over Innistrad* preview so far, or, what's your favorite *Innistrad* memory?** Archangel Avacyn, Relentless Dead, Heir of Falkenrath and Incorrigible Youths. They all look great, and this set should be fun.

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpdet16/gpdet16_t8_huang.jpg)**Hao-Shan Huang

**Age:** 33

**Occupation:** MTGMintCard Pro Player

**Hometown:** Chinese Taipei

**Previous Magic accomplishments:** Five Grand Prix Top 8s, including a win at Grand Prix Taipei in 2014. Two Nationals Top 8 appearances, two World *Magic* Cup appearances, and the 2015 Taiwan national champion.

**What deck did you play and why?** White-Blue Eldrazi. I had worked on several decks I didn't want to regret not playing a broken deck when it's available do so. I played the same 75 as Kelvin Chew who lost his win-and-in in Melbourne.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?** None for now but definitely need to tune a bit to adapt to the upcoming metagame.

**Are the Eldrazi inherently evil? Would that mean Eldrazi players are inherently evil too?** They are. But I believe the justice will come in two months. I feel a little bit guilty about being on the guilty side for once.

**What's your favorite *Shadows Over Innistrad* preview so far, or, what's your favorite *Innistrad* memory?** Archangel Avacyn. I believe the common answer, and I am not special and I like whatever people like.

* * *

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpdet16/gpdet16_t8_sajgalik.jpg)**Eduardo Sajgalik

**Age:** 28

**Occupation:** Searching for tomorrow

**Hometown:** [Unknown Shores](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unknown+Shores)

**Previous Magic accomplishments:** Three Grand Prix Top 8s, Top 8 at Pro Tour *Return to Ravnica* in 2012, four Nationals Top 8s, and three National Team appearances.

**What deck did you play and why?** White-Blue Eldrazi; it's the best deck by far.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?** Find another deck, it's unlikely it sticks. I liked the list. [Cyclonic Rift](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cyclonic+Rift) is the flex, and one more [Grafdigger's Cage](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grafdigger%27s+Cage) would be nice.

**Are the Eldrazi inherently evil? Would that mean Eldrazi players are inherently evil too?** What is evil? What is good? You cannot reason with it; you cannot comprehend it. You just need to destroy them.

**What's your favorite *Shadows Over Innistrad* preview so far, or, what's your favorite *Innistrad* memory?** Archangel Avacyn is sweet. I like that Avacyn, the Purifier is red (passionate), not black (selfish). In original *Innistrad* I liked the [Altar of the Lost](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Altar+of+the+Lost)/[Burning Vengeance](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Vengeance)/[Secrets of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Secrets+of+the+Dead) deck. Also turn 2 [Geist of Saint Traft](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geist+of+Saint+Traft), turn 3 [Increasing Savagery](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Increasing+Savagery).

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpdet16/gpdet16_t8_buchholz.jpg)**Evan Buchholz

**Age:** 23

**Occupation:** Salon supervisor/streamer (JustevSopriv)

**Hometown:** Lawrenceville, NJ.

**Previous Magic accomplishments:** None.

**What deck did you play and why?** White-Blue Eldrazi. it's the best deck and has a shot against everything.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?** I would cut one [Hurkyl's Recall](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hurkyl%27s+Recall) for a [Meddling Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meddling+Mage) and have a better plan for the mirror.

**Are the Eldrazi inherently evil? Would that mean Eldrazi players are inherently evil too?** No. They just eat and everything is in the way. No comment on the second.

**What's your favorite *Shadows Over Innistrad* preview so far, or, what's your favorite *Innistrad* memory?** Heir of Falkenrath. It looks like a sweet enabler.
