**In addition to the main Nationals event, NZ Nationals will host a great Public events program with something for everyone. So even if you are not qualified – come along and join in the fun** 

### Special promotion

 *On the Friday and the Sunday, one lucky player playing in a **public event** will win a From the Vault Dragons Set!* 

### Friday July 3

**Last Chance Qualifiers** aka Grinders from 10am (registration opens 9am)  
 The last grinder will start at 7PM  
 16-player single elimination

**Sealed Deck** $45  
 2 Shards of Alara boosters, 2 conflux boosters, 2 Alara Reborn boosters  
 Prizes 1st - 1 Display of boosters + Invitation to Nationals, 2nd - 1 Display of boosters.

**Standard** $20  
 Format Standard Constructed  
 Legal sets *Xth Edition, Lorwyn, Morningtide, Shadowmoor, Eventide, Shards of Alara, Conflux, Alara Reborn*  
 Prizes 1st - 1 Display of boosters + Invitation to Nationals, 2nd - 1 Display of boosters.

### Saturday July 4

4pm **Ironman! – Who will survive!**  
 1 starter of Champions of Kamigawa, 2 Boosters Saviours of Kamigawa  
 Entry $30  
 Prizes Winner gets whatever has not been "destroyed" + boosters for top 4

8pm **Vintage Champs 2009**  
 Entry $10  
 Prizes – Italian Legends Boosters

### Sunday July 5

10am **PTQ Austin – Get an invite and flight to the big show**  
 Format Standard  
 Winner gets invite and return airfare to Pro Tour Austin Texas, USA. 

4pm **2 Headed Giant Sealed – bring friend, smash face**  
 Entry $50 per team($25ea!)  
 Format: Shards of Alara Block 2 headed giant sealed. Boosters 4 SoA 2 CON, 2 ARB  
 Booster Prizes

6pm **Elder Dragon Highlander – have you tried your hand at this great new format?**  
 format EDH constructed  
 open generals (there can be more than 1 of the same general)  
 entry $10  
 Prize Large Mounted Shivan Dragon Print + boosters

### All Weekend

8 man Queues on demand all weekend (fri 10am - sun 6pm)  
 Standard $10, Extended $10, SCA Booster Draft $25  
 Prizes, boosters and other MtG giveaways

For more information contact Wellington Premier T/O Gene Brumby genis@paradise.net.nz
