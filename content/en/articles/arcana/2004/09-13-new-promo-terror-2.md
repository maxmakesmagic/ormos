Now *that* is a promo card.

![DCI Promo Terror](https://i.ormos.uk/d0/ea/d0ea9e7251194eb06252b230c1efaeae87bc21426a55c1ecefba60e352c73ae6.jpg)

This promotional [Terror](https://web.archive.org/web/20040913000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terror) card is one of a number of new DCI promo cards given away as future prizes to [Magic Player Rewards](https://web.archive.org/web/20040913000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/rewards/welcome) members. Watch this space to see similar promo cards in the future.

Note that there will still be premium foil promo cards in the future; these art-intensive promo cards do not represent the end of the foil promo cards era.

You may now pick your jaws up off the floor.
