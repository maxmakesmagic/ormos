 ![Bazaar of Baghdad](https://i.ormos.uk/74/e8/74e87019c2a419e93e43c6f754f1e0c254ca4b98cdce798f8b73006d51a05ccf.jpg) 

Dredge is a powerful draw engine that allows a player to mill through their own library almost completely in a single turn. Why would one want to do such a thing, you ask? To take advantage of graveyard-matters cards like Dread Return and Bridge from Below. Bazaar of Baghdad turbo-charges the combo in **Magic Online** Classic, and the land is so important Dredge players in that format are willing to use Serum Powder to make sure they start with it in their opening hand.
