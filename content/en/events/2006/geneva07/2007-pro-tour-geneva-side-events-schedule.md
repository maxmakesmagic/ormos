Unless otherwise noted, scheduled events will have a prize pool of two boosters per player and Qualifier Tournaments will have a prize pool of three boosters per player. Prizes awarded will be based on match points and will be determined once the Final Standings have been displayed following the last Swiss round. The minimum number of points required to guarantee a prize is shown in the table to the right.

### Pro Tour Qualifiers

Qualifiers for individual and Two-Headed Giant format Pro Tour tournaments award an invitation and a travel award to a specific Pro Tour tournament for the top-finishing competitor.

### Chilled Events

All scheduled Constructed events (including PTQs and GP Trials) that allow *Coldsnap* cards are designated Chilled. The top 8 players of these events will receive an invitation for the special "Chilling Out" Booster Draft.

**Daily Drawings (Not Thursday)**
