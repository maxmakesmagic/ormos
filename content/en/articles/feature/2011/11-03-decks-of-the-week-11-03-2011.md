Congratulations to Martin Juza on his victory at Grand Prix Hiroshima! Juza won the Standard tournament with a Green-white Tokens deck armed with the key metagame choice of [Mirran Crusader](https://web.archive.org/web/20111103000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirran+Crusader) against [Kessig Wolf Run](https://web.archive.org/web/20111103000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kessig+Wolf+Run) decks, which had been one of the dominant archetypes heading into the tournament.
