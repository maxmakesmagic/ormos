With ***Magic** 2012* in the mix, the world of Standard is shaping up for an entirely new Standard season. On display this past weekend at the StarCityGames.com Open in Seattle, Washington were a host of archetypes, including the popular White Weenie strategy known as Puresteel. Chris Schafer played this version to a Top 16, relying on a plethora of Equipment to fuel ridiculous draws once [Puresteel Paladin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110728000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Puresteel+Paladin) is on the battlefield.
