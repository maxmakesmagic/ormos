As you know, [*Morningtide* Launch Parties](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-launch-parties-4) are this weekend, starting on Friday. In some locations, they're starting midnight Thursday night. That's February 1-3. [*Morningtide* Launch Parties](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-launch-parties-4). Hang on, I lost my place.

Sorry about that. My *point* is that the Launch Parties are your first opportunity to get a keen *Morningtide* Fat Pack, which looks like this:

![Morningtide Fat Pack](https://i.ormos.uk/39/06/3906bdf25975a4dfc1a67e9bb9b6000b237c7ef13dd7a779101c397c7314cd70.jpg)

Ooh, doesn't it look enticing? Let's take a peek inside!

![Morningtide Fat Pack Contents](https://i.ormos.uk/0c/6c/0c6c8b6653f4e2fd62a8d586f77f332dbf6a62f92b1194167367fc7c7c1a2108.jpg)

Say! That Pack really *was* Fat! As you can see, you get:

*   A player's guide with a complete visual encyclopedia of the set,
*   Two card boxes with panoramic art (see below) by Steve Prescott, and six color-coded card dividers,
*   Six *Morningtide* boosters,
*   A pack of 40 *Lorwyn* basic lands,
*   A *Morningtide* spindown life counter (only available in this fatpack)
*   The second book of the Lorwyn cycle, by Cory J. Herndon and Scott McGough (the first chapter is [here](http://ww2.wizards.com/Books/Downloads/products_magicnovel_216337400.zip) as a 129KB ZIP file), and
*   One random Pro Tour Player card.

Looks good, doesn't it? And do you know how when your first opportunity to get one will be? Correct: your local [*Morningtide* Launch Party](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-launch-parties-4). And remember, if you're in North America, select stores are having Midnight Madness events ([USA Schedule](/en/events/2008/releases-na/eventide-launch-parties-north-america#usa1); [Canada Schedule](/en/events/2008/releases-na/eventide-launch-parties-north-america#canada1)) so you can get your *Morningtide* Fat Pack as soon as possible!
