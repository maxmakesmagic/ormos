Remember [a couple of weeks ago](https://web.archive.org/web/20100809000000/http://staging.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/503) when we showed you that ***Magic** 2011*'s Dryad's Favor contained a reference to ***Magic** 2010*'s Borderland Ranger? Well, today we've got another refugee from the previous core set.

Let's take a look at Chandra's Outrage (from a safe distance):

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/511_chandrasoutrage.jpg) 

Chandra's Outrage | Art by [Christopher Moeller](https://web.archive.org/web/20100809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22christopher+moeller%22])

Something seems to have incurred her anger. And that something is ...

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/511_snappingdrake.jpg) 

Snapping Drake | Art by [Todd Lockwood](https://web.archive.org/web/20100809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22todd+lockwood%22])

Aha! So *that's* why Snapping Drake isn't in ***Magic** 2011*! Clearly Chandra burned it to the ground before it could take a card slot.
