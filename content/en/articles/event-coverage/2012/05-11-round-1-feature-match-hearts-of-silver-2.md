The last time Antonino De Rosa and Luis Scott-Vargas (LSV) played against each other was in the quarterfinal of U.S. Nationals in 2006. At the time, De Rosa was one of the top American Pros and a heavy favorite to win the event, while LSV was virtually unknown outside of the *Magic* Online community.

LSV managed to pull off (what was then considered) a stunning upset, defeating De Rosa, and ultimately earning himself a spot on the U.S. National Team.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptavr12/r1_derosa_vs_lsv.jpg)

*The last time Antonino De Rosa and Luis Scott-Vargas played was in the 2006 U.S. Nationals, where LSV claimed victory. Will history repeat itself?*

Seven years after their last match, their roles have been reversed, with De Rosa now playing the role of the underdog against the utterly dominant LSV.

### Game 1

LSV opened on [Avacyn's Pilgrim](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avacyn%27s+Pilgrim), [Silverblade Paladin](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silverblade+Paladin), and [Mayor of Avabruck](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mayor+of+Avabruck), while De Rosa found himself stuck on only two lands, dying before he got the chance to play a single spell.

Antonino De Rosa 0, Luis Scott-Vargas 1

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptavr12/r1_derosa.jpg)

*De Rosa: "Sorry I couldn't make that game more exciting."*

### Game 2

De Rosa began the second game with a [Scorned Villager](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scorned+Villager), while LSV got off to another fast start with his White-Green Humans deck, playing a [Champion of the Parish](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Champion+of+the+Parish) and a [Avacyn's Pilgrim](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avacyn%27s+Pilgrim).

A miraculous [Bonfire of the Damned](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bonfire+of+the+Damned) wiped LSV's board, and [Huntmaster of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Huntmaster+of+the+Fells) plus [Wolfir Silverheart](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wolfir+Silverheart) allowed De Rosa to even the match up at one game apiece.

Antonino De Rosa 1, Luis Scott-Vargas 1

### Game 3

LSV took a mulligan, but he was able to get off to quick lead with two copies of [Avacyn's Pilgrim](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avacyn%27s+Pilgrim), [Champion of the Parish](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Champion+of+the+Parish), and a [Slayer of the Wicked](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slayer+of+the+Wicked) (which took out De Rosa's [Scorned Villager](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scorned+Villager)).

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptavr12/r1_lsv.jpg)

*"LSV starts the game out with a lead..."*

However, that lead would prove to be short-lived as a [Fiend Hunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiend+Hunter), a [Huntmaster of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Huntmaster+of+the+Fells), and a [Garruk Relentless](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Relentless) allowed De Rosa to take over the game.

A [Fiend Hunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiend+Hunter) of his own bought LSV a bit of time, but a [Wolfir Silverheart](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wolfir+Silverheart) and a [Huntmaster of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20120511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Huntmaster+of+the+Fells) were more than enough for De Rosa to pull off the upset against the captain of team ChannelFireball.

Antonino De Rosa 2, Luis Scott-Vargas 1
