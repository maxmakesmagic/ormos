Consider Zombie Goliath.

  

 ![Zombie Goliath](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Zombie%20Goliath&options=) 

  

At first glance, it's just a zombie. So what makes it a Zombie Giant? Well, take a closer look.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/1013_zombiegoliath.jpg) 

Zombie Goliath | Art by [E.M. Gist](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22e.+m.gist%22])

See its head? See how there are a couple of things in there? *Those* are regular-size zombies. They're riding in the Zombie Goliath's cranium! Now the flavor text makes more sense:

>  *"Removing the encumbrance of useless brain tissue grants several advantages: a slight increase in mobility, a response of revulsion and horror from the enemy, and, in the case of large specimens, room for passengers."  
>  —Zul Ashur, lich lord*
