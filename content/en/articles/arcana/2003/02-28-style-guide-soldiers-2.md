Here is a selection from the *Onslaught* Style Guide, which is a series of reference illustrations given to the artists that worked on the set. This particular image is of a pit-fighting Soldier.

![Pit-Fighter Soldier](https://i.ormos.uk/da/d0/dad09178c71b4e3395875741b58e73e7a7acac48ed21b96d5c1d91401c18974f.jpg)

Some of the cards that feature Soldiers fighting in the Coliseum include Whipcorder, Daru Cavalier, and Crowd Favorites. As you can see when looking at these cards, some artists chose to stay very true to the Style Guide, and others chose to follow it only loosely.
