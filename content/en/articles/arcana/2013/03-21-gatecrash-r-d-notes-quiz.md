Quiz time! Let's look at some developer notes and try to figure out what *Gatecrash* card they're talking about. If you need a hint, select the text next to the word "Hint!"

> DH: Did the more complicated but hopefully very flavorful change to its unblockability. Spy vs Spy!  
>  TML: This is hilarious.  
>  DH: 3HH to 2HH  
>  ZH: Highly support this change.  
>  DH: 2HH 2/3 to 1HH 2/2.

Hint: Dimir  
[Reveal](#)

 ![Deathcult Rogue](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Deathcult%20Rogue&options=) 

> Mago: Callback to RAV Viashino Slasher. Gives common blue a 2-drop, and plays nicely with +1/+1 counter-granting cards.

Hint: Unguilded  
[Reveal](#)

 ![Metropolis Sprite](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Metropolis%20Sprite&options=) 

> GSV: Making him a 1/2 gives Johnny players a really interesting build around that maybe has fringe constructed playability.  
>  DH: Isn't this more appealing to Johnny as a 1/1? Also, not sure we want it to be 'easy' to make this start going off.  
>  GTH: I believe it is more appealing as a 1/1. Cool card.

Hint: Orzhov
