# Any Surprises in the New Standard?

We went to a few of the more well-known players here at the Grand Prix this weekend and asked them whether anything in the format had really surprised them since the introduction of *Fate Reforged*. A deck, a card maybe? Here's what they had to say...

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsev15/qq-juza.jpg)  
**Martin Jůza:** [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon)! I thought it was overcosted but I guess I've been proven wrong. Personally, though, I like to be more aggressive than that.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsev15/qq-anteri.jpg)  
**Fabrizio Anteri:** Meh. Nothing yet.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsev15/qq-lemoine.jpg)  
**Vincent Lemoine:** No. Turns out the *Fate Reforged* cards that look good really are good.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsev15/qq-hatto.jpg)  
**Steve Hatto:** I stumbled over an interesting deck idea which used convoke—mainly [Triplicate Spirits](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triplicate+Spirits)—to turn [Soulfire Grand Master](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soulfire+Grand+Master) into an actual engine card of sorts.
