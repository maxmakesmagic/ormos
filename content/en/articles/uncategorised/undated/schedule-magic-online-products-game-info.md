## MAGIC ONLINE EVENTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c8/72/c872a1bd85f385d229e674d1edbc5ef3f96706e39bd18bc040722771251fc532.png)

### MAGIC ONLINE EVENTS SCHEDULE

*Magic Online* runs events around the clock. No matter when the urge to play strikes, you can log on and find events to join in any *Magic* format, from eight person drafts to giant events with hundreds of other players!

[VIEW SCHEDULE](https://web.archive.org/web/20160619021105/http://magic.wizards.com/node/229976)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/88/c7/88c7005bbd568d62042d9f6b428777d6d0288f2b4f40e946369bb02b685d89af.png)

### MAGIC ONLINE CHAMPIONSHIP SERIES

The Magic: The Gathering Online Championship Series, also known as MOCS, is a monthly series of events, broken into twelve seasons, in which players can [earn promo cards](https://web.archive.org/web/20160619021105/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/other/110413/mocs2014#promos). Top players can also earn an invitation to the annual in-person Magic Online Championship event, as well as a Pro Tour event.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20160619021105/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2016-magic-online-championship-series)

### MAGIC ONLINE PRO TOUR QUALIFIERS

*Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifiers (PTQs) consist of a set of Preliminaries that grant invitations to the PTQ Finals. Winners of PTQ Finals receive invitation to the associated Pro Tour as well as the associated Pro Tour Challenge.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20160619021105/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers-pro-tour-shadows-over-innistrad)

![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/images/featured/EN_PersistentNightmare_Schedules_Page_Point-To_InlineR_B.png)

### MAGIC ONLINE LEAGUES

Play as much *Magic* as you want, when you want, with *Magic Online* Leagues! Each League consists of five matches. Knock all five matches out in an afternoon, or stretch them over the full course of the League!

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20160619021105/http://magic.wizards.com/node/1015606)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a7/10/a7107307a6db45690fc809e0b67fd7f00a0c575ac97651457b9f31c33054cad5.png)

### MAGIC ONLINE FORMAT CHALLENGES

To create exciting, high-level play experiences across a variety of formats, the *Magic Online* team hosts a variety of regularly scheduled Challenge Events!

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20160619021105/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-format-challenges)

### MAGIC ONLINE PLAYER REWARDS

The Magic: The Gathering Online Player Rewards program (MOPR) offers players the chance to earn digital promo cards for their monthly Magic Online activity. Qualifying activity includes playing in tournaments of a sufficient size, as well as making purchases in the Magic Online Store.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20160619021105/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2015-magic-online-player-rewards)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/76/7e/767edbe580ebd404e2d2d685a9cb87f12c375c0ef57e9a7a470632147ec4d567.png)

### MAGIC ONLINE REGIONAL PRO TOUR QUALIFIERS

Qualified for a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier but aren't able to make the trek to the closest event? Now, you can participate in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier on *Magic Online*!

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20160619021105/http://magic.wizards.com/node/820956)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/23/21/2321f877e4979b9095ea283da8fe331bee3806e90eb3976ebdd0b96bfa3ff51e.png)

### REDEMPTION

Turn your digital cards into paper cards! Magic Online players are able to redeem complete sets of most card sets we release online.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20160619021105/http://magic.wizards.com/en/gameinfo/products/magiconline/redemption)
