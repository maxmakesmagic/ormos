## Don't Miss the May WPN Promo Happening Now

[![](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/otj_wpn_arena_cross_promo_social_1920x1080_en_yemgw38xg3h1.webp)](https://web.archive.org/web/20240513000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/announcements/draft-at-a-wpn-store-in-may-get-rewarded-in-june-on-mtg-arena) 

We're about halfway through May—have you joined a draft at your local game store yet to earn *MTG Arena* rewards? There are three rewards you can get, one for each time you [play in a Draft event at your local WPN store](https://web.archive.org/web/20240513000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/announcements/draft-at-a-wpn-store-in-may-get-rewarded-in-june-on-mtg-arena), up to three times: draft once for a High Noon card style, draft twice for a Goblin card sleeve, and draft three times for a Draft token!

This promo only lasts until June 2, so don't miss it!

## Arena Open: *Outlaws of Thunder Junction*, May 25–26

[![Ornery Tumblewagg card art](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/hero_event_arena_open_otj_may_25.webp)](https://web.archive.org/web/20240513000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/arena-open-outlaws-of-thunder-junction-may-25-26) 

You've tussled with the lawless frontier of *Outlaws of Thunder Junction*, and now it's time to test your skills against other outlaws vying for the big winnings in the [Arena Open, May 25–26](https://web.archive.org/web/20240513000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/arena-open-outlaws-of-thunder-junction-may-25-26)! There are up to $2,000 in prizes and June Qualifier Weekend invitations up for grabs!

Plus, everyone who enters will receive the delightfully disoriented Fblthp, Lost on the Range avatar!

![Fblthp, Lost on the Range avatar](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/avatar_fblthp_lost_range.webp)

Avatar: Fblthp, Lost on the Range

Get all the [*Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Arena Open details](https://web.archive.org/web/20240513000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/arena-open-outlaws-of-thunder-junction-may-25-26), and then get ready for a showdown starting May 25.

## Play the Brawl Builder Challenge This Week

[![Fully armored humanoid reaching toward the camera with a clawed hand, wearing a metal mask with glowing blue eyes, a red-trimmed cowboy hat, and is surrounded by black trailing smoke while his red, tattered cape floats raggedly behind him like smoke](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/hero_mwm_brawl_builder_may_2024.webp)](https://web.archive.org/web/20240513000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/announcements/midweek-magic) 

Bring your wildest [Brawl deck](https://web.archive.org/web/20240513000000/https://magic.wizards.com/formats/brawl) dreams to the [Midweek *Magic* Brawl Builder Challenge](https://web.archive.org/web/20240513000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/announcements/midweek-magic) and play to your heart's content this week, from Tuesday, May 14, starting at 2 p.m. PT, until Thursday, May 16, ending at 2 p.m. PT.

## Historic Metagame Challenge, May 17–20

![A kneeling older samurai ratfolk with the text, Historic Metagame Challenge, May 17–19](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/hero_historic_metagame_may_2024.webp) 

[*Modern Horizons 3*](https://web.archive.org/web/20240513000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/modern-horizons-3) is coming, and it promises to shake up the [Historic](https://web.archive.org/web/20240513000000/https://magic.wizards.com/formats/historic) metagame. But before it does, here's an opportunity to enjoy the current metagame and flex with those finely tuned Historic decks in Best-of-Three matches for packs and prizes!

The Historic Metagame Challenge runs from 8 a.m. PT on May 17 until 8 a.m. on May 20.

## Competitive Play Schedule

All times listed are Pacific time (UTC-07:00).

### Premier Play

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events.

Qualifier tokens are delivered to your *MTG Arena* inbox. Remember to claim them before the event starts!

#### June

*   **Qualifier Play-In (Best-of-One)**
     June 8, 6 a.m. PT–June 9, 6 a.m. PT
    *   Format: Explorer
*   **Qualifier Play-In (Best-of-Three)**
     June 14, 6 a.m. PT–May 15, 3 a.m. PT
    *   Format: Explorer
*   **Qualifier Weekend**
     June 15, 6 a.m. PT–June 16, 4 p.m. PT

*   Format: Explorer

### Arena Open

The Arena Open Day One entry window begins at 6 a.m. PT and closes to new entries the following day at 3 a.m. PT (UTC-07:00). The Day Two entry window is 2 hours only, from 6 a.m. PT until 8 a.m. PT (UTC-07:00).

*   **Arena Open: *Outlaws of Thunder Junction***
    *   May 25: Day One, *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Sealed (Best-of-One and Best-of-Three)
    *   May 26: Day Two, *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Draft (Best-of-Three)
