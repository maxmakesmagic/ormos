### Card Vote #6

In another twist, this vote offers you three choices to pick from. In honor of Red Week you’ll be choosing between [Balduvian Horde](https://web.archive.org/web/20040719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balduvian+Horde) from *Alliances*, [Goblin Goon](https://web.archive.org/web/20040719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Goon) from *Legions*, and [Rathi Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20040719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rathi+Dragon) from *Tempest*. All of these cards offer enormous bodies for their casting cost but each comes with a different drawback to make up for it. [Balduvian Horde](https://web.archive.org/web/20040719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balduvian+Horde) only loses you 1 card from the hand to [Rathi Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20040719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rathi+Dragon)’s two cards already in play, but it’s a random card. Meanwhile [Goblin Goon](https://web.archive.org/web/20040719000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Goon) offers the largest body of all three, but comes with the requirement to gain creature superiority.

Three cards enter, one leaves! Which will it be?

### Sketch Vote #2

This week’s vote pits Paolo Parente against Greg Staples.
