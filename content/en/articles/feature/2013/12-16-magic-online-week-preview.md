It’s [**Magic Online** Week on DailyMTG.com](http://www.dailymtg.com/)! Every day this week, you’ll find new articles from key members of the **Magic Online** business and R&D teams that discuss all aspects of the game. You’re also going to find daily deck lists, downloadable art, and more.

Here’s a preview of just a few items you’ll find this week:

Magic Online Week Preview

**Monday, December 16**

*   Get to know **Magic Online**'s Executive Producer Worth Wollpert as he kicks off the week with a [**Magic Online** Executive Summary](/en/articles/makingmagic/2013/12-16-magic-onlinethe-executive-summary) detailing goals and plans. You definitely don't want to miss this!
*   New to **Magic Online**? [“**Magic Online** 101”](/en/articles/makingmagic/2013/12-16-magic-online-101) gathers tutorial videos that will help introduce you to the game, the client user interface, finding matches, tournament events, and more.

**Tuesday, December 17**

*   Digital Product Manager Mike Turian, a **Magic** veteran with years of experience throughout Wizards of the Coast, stops by on Tuesday to talk about [**Magic Online**'s many event offerings](/en/articles/feature/2013/12-17-balancing-act), what each entails, and the importance of balance in each.
*   ORCs—the Online Response Crew—are live, in-game support reps available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. You don’t find that kind of support very often in online games nowadays. [Find out more about the team](/en/articles/makingmagic/2013/12-17-highlight-on-game-support), their passion for **Magic Online**, and their dedication to solving problems fast.

**Wednesday, December 18**

*   The **Magic Online** update baton is passed to Chris Kiritz on Wednesday as he goes in-depth into [the plans for the future](/en/articles/makingmagic/2013/12-18-seeing-the-future).
*   The **Magic Online** Holiday Cube kicks off on Wednesday, and R&D steps up to the plate with Max McCall, who [explores the Holiday Cube](/en/articles/feature/2013/12-18-merry-wubrg-and-happy-new-cube). Whether you’re a Cube enthusiast or you have yet to try the format, you’ll find great information about this extremely popular yearly event.

**Thursday, December 19**

*   Jon Loucks, a regular contributor on [MTGO.com](http://www.mtgo.com/) from the R&D team, stops by on Thursday to talk about [the importance of card text style](/en/articles/makingmagic/2013/12-19-text-me-maybe) in **Magic Online**. What does blue text mean? What does it mean when text is in black italics? Jon explores these and a lot more on this simple but important aspect of the game.
*   [James Sooy takes up the user interface topic](https://web.archive.org/web/20131216000000/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/feature/278h&date=12/19/2013) to discuss in more detail the design strategy of the **Magic Online** client.

**Friday, December 20**

*   Earlier in the week, Max McCall whetted our appetites for the Holiday Cube. On Friday, [Ryan Spain visits to share strategies](/en/articles/makingmagic/2013/12-20-the-unfairest-of-them-all) on how to master this format.
*   Lauren Beauregard provides an overview of the [upcoming MOCS Championship event](/en/articles/makingmagic/2013/12-20-2013-magic-online-championship) to be held in San Francisco with a prize pool of $116,000!
*   Clayton Kroh wraps up **Magic Online** week with a timely article on the [Holiday Cube and Commander](/en/articles/makingmagic/2013/12-20-holiday-cube-and-commander-2013-edition)—on the day ***Magic: The Gathering**—Commander* (2013 Edition) is released in **Magic Online**!

That's just a taste—there’s a lot more to come this week, so be sure to visit [DailyMTG.com](http://www.dailymtg.com/) each day!
