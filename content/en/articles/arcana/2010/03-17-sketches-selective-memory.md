Today, we're going to look at Jace Beleren, as seen in the *Worldwake* card Selective Memory. The original sketch came from a different angle than the final version.

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3b/98/3b989ce9f56ec523a779d817571a229f5040a5f4b59162c375cb85ccc9751cc3.jpg) 

Selective Memory | Sketch by [Chippy](https://web.archive.org/web/20100317000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22chippy%22])

The first thing to do was to lose Jace's face in the glowing blue magic. The idea is that he's purging himself of useless knowledge, so the smoke should be more of a focus.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c9/0d/c90dd549eddd5bbb09b89700c71773b88f5da916affe083e556958abbf08c636.jpg) 

Selective Memory | Sketch by [Chippy](https://web.archive.org/web/20100317000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22chippy%22])

That hit the basic idea, but it didn't feel as dramatic as everyone had hoped. So Chippy went back to the beginning, zoomed in, and gave it a different angle. Presto!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0c/4a/0c4a5bca08425381206ba541640f1799397615b248b93fb8c4c1525f617748f7.jpg) 

Selective Memory | Sketch by [Chippy](https://web.archive.org/web/20100317000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22chippy%22])

This version, incidentally, is a great example of how a sketch can use simplified colors and lines to really convey the emotion of the final. And here's that final!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b3/89/b38903938e52d39bde64b3deda33dad5346ee9395beffedebb95404bda4f6f7a.jpg) 

Selective Memory | Art by [Chippy](https://web.archive.org/web/20100317000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22chippy%22])

Note that you can just barely make out Jace's eyes in this. Now that you've seen that, maybe you can convince yourself you can see them at card size, too!
