**Magic** designer and Great Designer Search 2 champion [Ethan Fleischer](https://web.archive.org/web/20130521180529/http://wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Archive.aspx?author=Ethan%20Fleischer) has been having a lot of fun with his newest Commander deck! This deck features cards like Shanodin Dryads, Thought Nibbler, and Zodiac Rabbit. Yes, you read that right—Zodiac Rabbit!

Best of all yet, the deck has been competitive! It's made almost everyone in R&D laugh by now—and it also tends to silence each person in turn as he or she gets smashed by it.

How is this possible? Edric, Spymaster of Trest!

Inspired by a match at Grand Prix Seattle-Tacoma and by a popular Commander thread on a **Magic** forum, Ethan took care in acquiring every last one-drop he could find for this deck. You set up with a bunch of innocent 1/1s, and then on turn three Edric turns them all into a card-drawing machine! By using the card-drawing 1/1s in conjunction with Beastmaster Ascension and Time Warp effects, the deck can get out of hand quickly.

It's rare you find an aggressive deck in Commander, and even rarer you find one sporting such silly cards. And yet, it looks like one has been found. Ethan would like to thank all of the people on forums who developed this hysterically fun Commander deck. If you're interested in something completely different, check out this Commander decklist!
