You are, of course, familiar with the Thrinax. In *Shards of Alara*, it looked like this:

 ![Sprouting Thrinax](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Sprouting%20Thrinax&options=) 

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/17/a0/17a0c568854e6af1682ec6125de8331bc4201687cc274ad93f79ec270c7192c7.jpg) 

  

In *Conflux*, it looked like this:

 ![Scarland Thrinax](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Scarland%20Thrinax&options=) 

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a5/6f/a56f9f858716defb590140bb81015f20cc069024405b2f3e76422dab78705100.jpg) 

  

And in the Triassic Period, it looked something like this:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/22/36/22363d5cea8166b367fcc8f92e4922a97c206b3194e8e2f64bb0ce10aca0ba98.jpg) 

  

That's right! Thrinaxes are actually based on a real creature, who lived somewhere around 245 million years ago. According to [Wikipedia](https://web.archive.org/web/20090528000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thrinaxodon&origin=), it was probably fur-covered and cat-sized but still laid eggs. Freaky!
