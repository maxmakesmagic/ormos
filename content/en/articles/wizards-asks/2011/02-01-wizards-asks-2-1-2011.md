*Got any fun stories from this weekend's Prerelease events?* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fa/fe/fafe439c8cd14e179675a1e7a1b7baa0a63f2c6f3dc2c816785a52fff5e4d843.jpg)

**Monty Ashley:** At the employee Prerelease, as with *Mirrodin Besieged* Prereleases in the real world, we were told to pick between Mirran and Phyrexian. But because we had an unexpectedly high turnout (120 people!), by the time I got there, the prepared Prerelease sets had all been taken and new ones were being frantically assembled. The first one that was ready was Mirran, so that's what I went with. Within ten minutes, I was hanging out at the Mirran table, making fun of the people playing for Phyrexia.

My deck was white-blue metalcraft, which I'm pretty sure was a mistake. The only useful thing the blue did for me was let me play Riddlesmith and give my Gust-Skimmer flying. My wins were largely due to any of Gust-Skimmer, Leonin Skyhunter, or Thopter Assembly giving me more air power than my opponent, with Tempered Steel pushing me over the top. But I think I would have been better off cutting most of the blue for some red.

My final record was 3-3, but I still consider the evening a huge success, due to this:

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/activity/da637_achievements.jpg) 

  

That's a list of goals for the Prerelease. The Mirran side ranges from things like "High-five three other Mirrans" to "Win at nine poison counters." Apparently, I find achievement lists to be a powerful motivating factor, which isn't really a surprise after I played *Dragon Age* for an extra twenty hours for the last achievement.

After I lost the first two rounds, I decided I wasn't trying hard enough. Not "hard enough to win," you understand. "Hard enough to get the achievements." I put in cards that aren't all that competitive, like Golden Urn, because it has the ability to give itself counters, which would help me get the "*Charged Up* – Control a permanent with five or more counters on it" achievement. I also loaded up with Equipment so I'd up my chances of getting "*Suit Up* – Control a creature with three Equipment attached to it." Here's the game state the turn before I hit both of those:

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/activity/da637_cards.jpg) 

  

That's also the game where I won at nine poison counters. I had to choose my blocking on the last turn so I could let a Phyrexian Juggernaut through for the last five, and hope my opponent didn't have any proliferate to kill me.

So in the end, I didn't have a stellar win-loss record (although one of my losses came against R&D's David Humpherys, who used to be the Pro Tour's David Humpherys, so I don't think it should count against me), but I think I might be the only person with a perfect set of achievements. That's gotta count for something, right?
