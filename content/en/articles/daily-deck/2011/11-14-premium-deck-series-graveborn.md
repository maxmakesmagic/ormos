The *Premium Deck Series: Graveborn* deck challenges opponents to handle huge threats when they're least prepared to do so: sometimes as early as the second turn. And for opponents who always seem to have the answers, this deck is packed with spells that can make your opponents discard whatever creature removal spell they were banking on to save them. For more about *Graveborn*, check out deck designer Dave Humpherys's [feature article](/en/articles/feature/2011/11-14-premium-deck-series-graveborn).

*Premium Deck Series: Graveborn* releases on November 18.
