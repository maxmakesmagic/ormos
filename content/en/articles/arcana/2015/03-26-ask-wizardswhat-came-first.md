**Q: Which came first, the chicken or the egg?** 

**A: Mike McArtor, copy editor of DailyMTG.com:**

It depends on what you mean by "first."

The first card with [Egg in the name](https://web.archive.org/web/20150326000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&name=+%5Begg%5D) is [Dingus Egg](https://web.archive.org/web/20150326000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=12), from Alpha. The first card with [Chicken in the name](https://web.archive.org/web/20150326000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&name=+%5Bchicken%5D) appeared in *Unglued*.

Alphabetically, of course, a Chicken card comes first. In this case, [Chicken à la King](https://web.archive.org/web/20150326000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chicken+%C3%A0+la+King).

And if you're looking for "eggs" for your deck, I recommend the winning decklists from [Pro Tour *Return to Ravnica*](/en/articles/uncategorised/2012/10-20-top-8-decklists-modern) (Cifka's) and [Grand Prix San Diego](https://web.archive.org/web/20150326000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/gpsd13/welcome) (Holiday's).
