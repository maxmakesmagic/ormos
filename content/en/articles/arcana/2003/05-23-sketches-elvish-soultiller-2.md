This week's "Sketches" takes an interactive look at Ron Spears' Elvish Soultiller from *Legions*. 

### 1\. Art Description

These are the instructions given to the artist:

"Color: Green  
 Location: Wirewood forest  
 Action: Show an elf who is much larger and stronger than other elves of his kind. Other elves can be shown but it's not necessary.  
Mood: Strength"

The card was called "Elvish Reviver" in playtest.

### 2\. Sketches

Here is one of two preliminary sketches of the "Elvish Reviver" submitted by Ron Spears. The art team agreed that the sketch did capture the strong elf of the art description, but didn't capture the spirit of the 5/4 mutant elf they were really looking for.
