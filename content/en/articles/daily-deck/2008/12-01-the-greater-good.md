Top 8ing the World Championships are a recurring theme for Japanese star Katsuhiro Mori. He used this Greater Good control deck to win the title in 2005, before Top 8ing again in 2006 and 2007. Will he be able to make it a fourth time in 2008? Find out during the 2008 World Championships, coming to you live from magicthegathering.com with [the webcast of the Top 8](/en/events/2004/webcast/magic-the-gathering-webcast-schedule) set to kick off from Memphis at 9:45 a.m. ET on December 14th.
