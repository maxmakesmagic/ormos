* * *

### Rodrigo Goncalves Dos Santos

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmex16/t8_santos.jpg)

**Hometown:** Brazil  
**Age:** 29  
**Occupation:** Commercial Manager  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 2nd place Grand Prix São Paulo in 2012.

**What colors was your Sealed Deck, and what was your record?**  
 Blue-Black Edlrazi. I went 9-0.

**What was the best card in your Sealed deck?**  
[Ruins of Oran-Rief](https://web.archive.org/web/20160201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ruins+of+Oran-Rief).

**What were your Draft decks, and what was your record with each?**  
 White-Black Allies: 2-1; White-Black Allies: 1-0-2.

**What card do you most want to see in *Oath of the Gatewatch* Draft, and why?**  
[Linvala, the Preserver](https://web.archive.org/web/20160201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Linvala%2C+the+Preserver) because I opened it in the first pack of the first draft!

### Martin Dang

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmex16/t8_dang.jpg)

**Hometown:** Aarhus, Denmark  
**Age:** 34  
**Occupation:** Platinum Pro Player  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Pro Tour *Dragons of Tarkir* winner; Grand Prix Liverpool winner; World *Magic* Cup Top 8 appearance and member of Team EUreka

**What colors was your Sealed Deck, and what was your record?**  
 Black-Green and I went 9-0 obviously.

**What was the best card in your Sealed deck?**  
[Thought-Knot Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thought-Knot+Seer) and lands that make colorless mana.

**What were your Draft decks, and what was your record with each?**  
 Blue-Black and went 2-1, and Black-Green in the second where I went 1-0-2.

**What card do you most want to see in *Oath of the Gatewatch* Draft, and why?**  
 Chandra seems like the best card. Also Expeditions because they're so shiny.
