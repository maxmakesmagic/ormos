# What are you playing today and why?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Ivan-Floch.jpg)  
**Ivan Floch:** Both Martin Jůza and I are playing Abzan Whip, as the maindeck creatures are bigger and better and doesn't rely all-in on the graveyard.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Lee-Shi-Tian2.jpg)  
**Lee Shi Tian:** I'm running the same four colour Jeskai Combo deck I ran at Pro Tour *Khans of Tarkir*. There's been a metagame shift recently, and besides I don't like playing the lifegain mirror matches, which go on too long.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Raymond-Tan.jpg)  
**Raymond Tan:** Mardu, I've been playing it ever since *Khans of Tarkir* was released and I'm familiar with the deck.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Makhihito-Mihara.jpg)  
**Makihito Mihara:** Black Green Devotion. I like combo decks.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Andrew-Cantillana.jpg)  
**Andrew Cantillana:** White Blue Heroic. It's good as long as I dodge all the Mardu decks.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Huang-Hao-Shan2.jpg)  
**Huang Hao-Shan:** Today I'm playing Abzan Whip, as I prefer casting [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150103000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) to [Sidisi, Brood Tyrant](https://web.archive.org/web/20150103000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sidisi%2C+Brood+Tyrant).
