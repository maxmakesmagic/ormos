## Join Your Local Community

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3d/98/3d98debaa831a565dd1a59c870bc2c49baf195cedf9431984c23285f10597592.png)

New friends and rivals await! Head to your local game store, where you can pick up a deck and find other players to challenge. There, you can also participate in Friday Night Magic, a casual event that happens every Friday around the world.

Your local game store is the best place to meet your local community of fans. No matter you experience level, there are events, games and tournaments designed for you. Bring your friends and make new ones!

[FIND A LOCAL GAME STORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20160613041927/http://locator.wizards.com/)  
[FRIDAY NIGHT MAGIC](https://web.archive.org/web/20160613041927/http://magic.wizards.com/events/event-types/friday-night-magic)
