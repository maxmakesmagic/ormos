As Nationals season winds down, the world is finally getting a glimpse of who will represent which nations at the 2011 World Championships in San Francisco. This year's captain from Brazil is one Marcus Camargo. He will be joined by Igor Sousa, Guilherme Medeiros Merjan, and Marcus Pacheco. For more information on the event, make sure to check out the [official coverage](/en/events/2011/branat11/camargo-no-1-brazilian-pod-caster).
