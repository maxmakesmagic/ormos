This week's desktop wallpaper showcases **[Agrus Kos, Wojek Veteran](https://web.archive.org/web/20050909000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/283)** from this week's *Ravnica: City of Guilds* previews.

As a bonus, we bring you a wallpaper of **Crown of Convergence**, a *Ravnica* card we've not yet previewed, which happens to be the **Magic** art debut of the web team's own [Jen Page](https://web.archive.org/web/20050909000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/magic-dossier-jen-page-2005-06-23). Enjoy.
