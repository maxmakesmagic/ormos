The newest set, *Born of the Gods*, goes on sale today! It will almost certainly have an effect on Standard, which is the format for this season's Pro Tour Qualifier (PTQ) season. Although it's still entirely too early to know how the set will change the metagame, many brewers are already putting out ideas, including Hall of Famer Luis Scott-Vargas in this week's [Top Decks](/en/articles/top-decks/2014/02-07-return-of-the-king). Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#jou). Good luck; have fun!
