There are tokens in ***Magic** 2013*. That can't come as a surprise. But who knew they'd look this good? Us! We did! That's who! So it's time to look at some tokens and also marvel at how nice the art looks without the card frame around it. Hooray!

The first token on the list is "Cat." But it's not an adorable little kitty; it's some kind of vicious 2/2 humanoid cat. Not that there's anything wrong with adorable kitties. After all, *Dark Ascension* has two of them. ![](https://i.ormos.uk/e4/31/e431dfc9edc2b50ef3c01411603f4c835762142552579ba94c9b4093994948fe.jpg)
