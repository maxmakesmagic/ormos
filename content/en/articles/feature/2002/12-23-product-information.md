**Booster packs**  
 15 randomly assorted cards

**Theme decks**  
 4 decks of 60 pre-set cards  
**The theme decks’ names are:**  
 "Elvish Rage" deck  
 "Zombies Unleashed" deck  
 "Morph Mayhem" deck  
 "Sliver Shivers" deck 

**Fat packs**  
 Containing 6 boosters, a premium land card and random common, a *Legions* novel and the *Legions* Players Guide.

**Deck boxes**  
 1 polypropylene deck boxes with tab closure  
 75 tournament legal, black textured sleeves
