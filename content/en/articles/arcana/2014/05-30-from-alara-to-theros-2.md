The Modern Event Deck goes on sale today! [It includes](/en/articles/feature/2014/05-13-decklist-for-modern-event-deck) 1 Elspeth, Knight-Errant, a card first introduced in *Shards of Alara*. All this week, [the Card of the Day theme](/en/articles/cardoftheday/2014/05-01-card-of-the-day-may-2014) has been about the Elspeth story on Theros (spoilers!), which tied in with Monday's feature, "[Journey's End](/en/articles/makingmagic/2014/05-26-journeys-end)." So it makes sense to bookend the week with an Arcana about Elspeth, knight-errant and sun's champion.
