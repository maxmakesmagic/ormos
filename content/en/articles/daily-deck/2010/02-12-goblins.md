Red decks of all kinds are mainstays in Standard, but DustyStorm has a different take for **Magic Online**. A return to the days of Goblins was responsible for Dusty's 3-1 performance in a recent daily event. Featuring five representatives from the most chaotic of tribes, DustyStorm takes advantage of Warren Instigator's abilities. The deck also benefits from a healthy smattering of burn and heavy hitters like Ball Lightning and Hellspark Elemental. A great choice for heading off to Friday Night **Magic** this week!
