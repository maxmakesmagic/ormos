Congratulations to Yuuya Watanabe for his amazing sixth Grand Prix victory this past weekend in Manila! Watanabe-*san* will be leading his country at the [World **Magic** Cup](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2012-world-magic-cup) in Indianapolis, IN, this August, during Gen Con! Not invited to the WMC? You can still get into high-level play by entering a [Pro Tour *Return to Ravnica*](/en/events/2012/seattle12-qualifiers/qualifiers-for-pro-tour-return-to-ravnica-in-seattle) Qualifier [near you](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/event-coverage/pro-tour%E2%80%93amsterdam-standard-qualifier-season-top-8-decklists-2015-07). For ideas of the kinds of decks people are playing competitively, check out the Top 8 decklists from some of the recent PTQs (including those on **Magic Online**) and WMC Qualifiers!
