           </div>&#10;            <div>&#10;              <i>Who are Faddy Josh?</i>&#10;            </div>&#10;          </td>&#10;        </tr>&#10;      </tbody></table>&#10;      <p>This is just a guess, but I'm certain there are more pleasant things to wake up to than the smiling face of Antonino De Rosa. Unfortunately for Tim Bulger and his Faddy Josh teammates, that's exactly what they were faced with this morning</p>&#10;      <p>So who are Faddy Josh? Fans of the late, great Tim Aten are probably already aware of names like TBulge, Taka, and Ziegler, but the short answer are that they are a group of players from the University of Minnesota who have been playing in the same Magic circle for years. Tim Bulger is described as &quot;the best player in his group&quot; and can be found in the Top 8 of most local PTQs when he's not playing with Kobolds at the kitchen table. Brian Ziegler is a twin who abandoned his sometimes-Magic-playing brother for actual good players this weekend. Everybody loves a Ziegler. As for Taka Sato, he's the Japanese translator for StarCityGames.com, and happens to be about as crazy as they come (but mostly in a good way). He was part of the Worlds karaoke party that nearly submarined the Japanese National Team's chances at the team title.</p>&#10;      <p>As for the match itself, De Rosa lost to <a>Shining Shoal</a> action with Wildfire on the stack, while Josh Ravitz dispatched Sato's Gruul deck with little trouble. This left the entire match riding on the outcome of Lieberman vs. Ziegler in a tough Heartbeat mirror. Another Grotesque a turn later saw Sato scoop up his cards as they headed into game 2.</p>&#10;      <p>Sonne 1 - Sato 0</p>&#10;      <p>Incredibly enough, Chris McDaniel managed to lose game 1 to Bulger even faster than Sato, keeping a one-land + <a>Sensei's Divining Top</a> hand post-mulligan and then failing to draw a second land. Ziegler managed to post a game 1 win right after the rest, raking in game 1 of the Heartbeat vs. Zoo matchup.</p>&#10;      <p>Sato again took a mulligan to start game 2, and had no plays before a turn 3 <a>Burning-Tree Shaman</a>. A second Burning-Tree followed a turn later, but this was surely not the start Sato was hoping for. Sonne blew him out a turn later, attacking with a <a>Descendant of Kiyomaro</a> and then <a>Mortify</a>ing a Shaman when Sato chose to double-block. The rest of the match was purely perfunctory, as Sonne put the first match win on the board for the bikeless wonders.</p>&#10;      <p>Sonne 2 - Sato 0</p>&#10;      <p>Sonne's victory ended up not mattering, as the rest of Faddy Josh was busy demolishing their opponents in seats A and B, giving Faddy Josh the win and a probably berth in the Top 4.
