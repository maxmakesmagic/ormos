### Invitation List

**2009 Chinese Taipei National Championship Invitations**  
 The **Magic: The Gathering** Hong Kong/ Macau National Championship is an invitation-only event, open to citizens or foreign players resident in Hong Kong/ Macau from Jan 1st 2009 Hong Kong/ Macau. Invitations are awarded based on the following criteria:

*   2008 Hong Kong/ Macau Worlds Team
    *   Tsz Yeung Lam (Hong Kong/ Macau Champion)
    *   Plato Chan
    *   Kai Yip Lee
*   The Alternate member of the 2008 Hong Kong/ Macau National Team
    *   Hong Fei Yueng
*   Top 4 Winners from the Regional and National Qualifier Tournaments.

Regional( June 7) and National Qualifier( July 5) tournaments take place as follows:
