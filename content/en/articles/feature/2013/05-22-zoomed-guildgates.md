*This arcana was originally ran just over a month ago, but given I am on vacation and it is Gate Week here on DailyMTG, I felt you all might enjoy seeing it run again.*

* * *

As we showed during our Comic Con panel, the *Dragon's Maze* guildgates feature a zoomed out view of each of them. We did this during the panel but for a lot of you this will be your first change to see a good look at these gorgeous pieces of art.

Click on the images to zoom out or zoom back in.
