This week we have a special **double batch** of *Saviors of Kamigawa* desktop wallpaper (thanks to Jen Page, magicthegathering.com's graphic designer whiz)!

First, in honor of our final [legend vignette](https://web.archive.org/web/20050603000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/meet-saviors-ayumi-last-visitor-2005-06-01), there's **[Ayumi, the Last Visitor](https://web.archive.org/web/20050603000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ayumi%2C+the+Last+Visitor)** by rk post.

And second, in honor of Fire Week, it's **[Skyfire Kirin](https://web.archive.org/web/20050603000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skyfire+Kirin)** by Tsutomu Kawade. Enjoy.
