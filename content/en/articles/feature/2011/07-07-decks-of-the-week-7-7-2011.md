The first results from the post-Jace/Stoneforge Standard metagame are in! Congratulations to Chen Zhuang, who devised a Blue-black Control deck heavy on hand disruption, counterspells, and removal to capture the China National Championship. You can also check out two Pro Tour Qualifiers that featured multiple fresh archetypes in the Top 8. With this new metagame evolving quickly, don't miss out on the [***Magic** 2012* prereleases](https://web.archive.org/web/20110707000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prerelease-facts) this weekend for your first chance at the new cards!
