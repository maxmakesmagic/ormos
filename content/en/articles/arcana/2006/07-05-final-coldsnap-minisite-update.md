![](https://i.ormos.uk/c7/a9/c7a90c330b05d98a18f8a99688c48af7b0a0ccac75e6dc2cd0c650f5a82852e2.jpg)This week, Adam Lee, *Coldsnap* flavor text writer extraordinaire, brings you a tale of icy drama to complete the offerings of the *Coldsnap* minisite.

It answers the question, “When dying is not an option, what does a slain soldier do?” In other words, what are the advantages—and drawbacks—of fighting on the same battlefield as [Adarkar Valkyrie](/en/articles/seriousfun/2006/06-27-flight-of-the-undying-bombs-2)? You’ll see other *Coldsnap* cards and creatures make cameos in Adam’s story as well.

Additionally, this week the *Coldsnap* minisite reveals four Adult Swim ads that never made it to to air. You'll find them on the "[Lore From Terisiare](https://web.archive.org/web/20060707033717/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/coldsnaplore/home)" page for your viewing pleasure, along with an introduction from Jake Theis of Magic Brand fame. 

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/e6/45/e6454c3f71d6f3fb88cc98802987b1c62707aa5c3bc1d5eeebb0a4b39ea8c511.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20080422233619/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/coldsnap/home)

That does it for the [*Coldsnap* minisite](https://web.archive.org/web/20080422233619/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/coldsnap/home), but the *Coldsnap* fun is just beginning. See you at the [prerelease](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-prereleases-3)!
