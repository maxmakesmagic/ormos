Luis Scott-Vargas is on a tear! After winning Pro Tour-Berlin, then Grand Prix-Atlanta, Luis Scott-Vargas has won back-to-back North American Grand Prixs with a title in Los Angeles! The deck that took him there? An innovative Blue-Red Storm concoction that had just enough black to swap Grapeshot for Tendrils of Agony. You can read all about Grand Prix-Los Angeles in [the official event coverage](/en/events/2009/gpla09/luis-scott-vargas-conquers-in-la). To find out how to get to an Extended Pro Tour Qualifier for Honolulu near you, visit the [official PTQ page](/en/events/2009/honolulu09-qualifiers/pro-tourhonolulu-2009-qualifiers).
