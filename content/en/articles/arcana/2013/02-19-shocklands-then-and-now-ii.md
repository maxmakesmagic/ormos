Last year, we took a look at [the way the shocklands changed](/en/articles/arcana/2012/09-24-shocklands-then-and-now) between the original *Ravnica* block and *Return to Ravnica*. But we could only look at five of them, which seemed a little incomplete. So now that *Gatecrash* is out, let's do some side-by-side comparisons of Godless Shrine, Watery Grave, Stomping Ground, Sacred Foundry, and Breeding Pool!
