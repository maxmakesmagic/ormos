The ["cog" theme](/en/articles/makingmagic/2004/05-24-cog-wild) of *Fifth Dawn* pertains to zero- and one-mana artifacts. Trinket Mage and Artificer's Evolution can search them up and Auriok Salvagers, Leonin Squire and Salvaging Station can re-use them.

But keep in mind that if a card has ![X Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/b1/6bb1152b0a3305d0a7c2e2eb725b8ebd18620607efc62f6f38b0642b97c8501f.gif) in its mana cost, the amount is treated as zero for the purposes of determining converted mana cost. So cards with only ![X Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/b1/6bb1152b0a3305d0a7c2e2eb725b8ebd18620607efc62f6f38b0642b97c8501f.gif)s in their mana costs are, you guessed it, cogs.

Here's a list of cards that you might have missed for your cog decks because of that ![X Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/b1/6bb1152b0a3305d0a7c2e2eb725b8ebd18620607efc62f6f38b0642b97c8501f.gif).

**Phyrexian Marauder** - It's an artifact creature from *Visions* that you can make as big as you please. 

**Shifting Wall** - This artifact wall from *Stronghold* can be a useful thing to tutor for when you're facing down a large attacker.

**Engineered Explosives** - Although you play it with lots of colors, its converted mana cost is still ![0 Mana](http://www.wizards.com/images/Symbols/Symbol_0_mana.gif) -- and it's a great way to rid yourself of problematic permanents.

**Chalice of the Void** - Remember, *all* ![X Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/b1/6bb1152b0a3305d0a7c2e2eb725b8ebd18620607efc62f6f38b0642b97c8501f.gif)s are treated as zero! So you can search up a Chalice when you know your opponent has spells of a certain cost that you want to stop.
