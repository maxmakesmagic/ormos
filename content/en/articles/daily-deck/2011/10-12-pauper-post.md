Big-mana Post decks have posted fine finishes in a variety of formats. The deck's key cards—Cloudpost and Glimmerpost—are both commons, which let the.zzz pilot this EightPost deck to a 3-1 finish in a Pauper Daily Event on **Magic Online**.
