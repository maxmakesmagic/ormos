[Lost in the Woods](https://web.archive.org/web/20120227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lost+in+the+Woods) | Art by [Matt Stewart](https://web.archive.org/web/20120227000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Matt+Stewart%22%5D)

Does something look odd here? Consider the way the flames of the torch look a little strange. Look at the top of the picture. Do those look like the *tops* of trees? Wait a minute... these people aren't just lost! They're *upside-down*! No wonder that guy on the right looks so disturbed!
