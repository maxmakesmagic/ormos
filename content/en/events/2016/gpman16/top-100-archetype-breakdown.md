At the top tables at the beginning of Day 2, the most popular options were Green-White Tokens and Naya Midrange. This would imply that [Sylvan Advocate](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Advocate) and [Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar) are the key cards to beat in the current Standard format.

So Green-White Tokens and Naya Midrange are the most popular decks, followed by a variety of other archetypes. Looking a bit more closely, we can find a number of commonalities among those other decks.

First, [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company) and [Eldrazi Displacer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Displacer) had big followings, and they were often seen in the same deck. Both are key pieces of most [Cryptolith Rite](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptolith+Rite) decks, and [Eldrazi Displacer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Displacer) has become a frequent inclusion in Bant Company decks as well.

Second, [Languish](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Languish) is the go-to card for control players. It doesn't matter whether it's white-black, Sultai, Abzan, black-green, or Esper—[Languish](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Languish) appears to be the sweeper of choice, frequently flanked by [Transgress the Mind](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transgress+the+Mind) and [Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price).

Finally, [Thalia's Lieutenant](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thalia%27s+Lieutenant) is the card that aggro players flock to in the current Standard. Bant Humans may not be as hyper-aggressive as White Weenie Humans, but for both decks, [Thalia's Lieutenant](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thalia%27s+Lieutenant) enables the fastest starts in Standard.
