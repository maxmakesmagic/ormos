### Gateway Tournaments

Gateway Tournaments are open to any player, and can be organized by any WPN Gateway level organizer. (For more information about the WPN and organizer levels, please refer to the [WPN page](https://web.archive.org/web/20100116031101/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dci/wpn/main).)

There will be three seasons of Gateway Tournaments in 2010, held during the following date ranges: February 13-March 7, April 29-May 30, and July 24-August 15. Tournament organizers may run one Tournament during each round. Gateway Qualifiers will be run using Standard format, are Rules Enforcement Level: Regular, and are 16K. (For more information about formats, rules enforcement levels and K Value, please refer to the [DCI Tournament Rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20101005174249/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dci/doccenter/home).) Organizers and other event staff are permitted to play in Gateway Tournaments. Please refer to the above links for event information for each qualifier round.

Winners of a Gateway Tournament will earn a 1-round bye at the Regional Tournaments (see below). Byes are not cumulative; if a player wins more than one Gateway Tournament, he or she will receive only one 1-round bye. However, byes are not "used up"—a player with a bye from a Gateway Tournament may play in more than one Regional Tournament and have a 1-round bye at each. Byes do not pass down under any circumstance; if the winner of a Gateway Tournament already has a bye, the second-place finisher does not then receive a bye. Additional prizes such as product or promotional cards may be offered; check with your local organizer for details.

### Regional Tournaments

There are two types of Regional Tournaments: Regular and Special. All Regional Tournaments will take place between October 9 and October 24. Regional Tournaments are run using Standard format, are Rules Enforcement Level: Competitive, and are 24K.

### The Finals Championship

The Championship for The Finals will be held at the 2010 **Magic** World Championships in Chiba the week of December 6 (exact date will be announced later). The event is open to any player who meets one of the following criteria:

*   Regional Qualifier top finishers
*   The Finals 2009 Champion

After an equal number of Swiss rounds of Standard and Extended Constructed, the Top 8 players will advance to the single elimination round, which will be played using the Standard Constructed format (using the same decks as the Swiss rounds). The Championship is Rules Enforcement Level: Competitive and 32K.
