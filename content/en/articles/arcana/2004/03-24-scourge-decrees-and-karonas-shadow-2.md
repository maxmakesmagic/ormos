![Karona, False God](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Karona%2C+False+God)

The card **[Karona, False God](https://web.archive.org/web/20040324000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Karona%252C%2BFalse%2BGod)** depicts the *Onslaught* block's "Big Bad" herself, that five-color fusion of [Akroma](https://web.archive.org/web/20040324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroma) and [Phage](https://web.archive.org/web/20040324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phage), who simultaneously led every mutated tribe on Otaria to war with every other. (See Rei Nakazawa's article, "[Scourge of Otaria](https://web.archive.org/web/20040324000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/150)," for more storyline info on [Karona](https://web.archive.org/web/20040324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karona).)

In *card art* terms, [Karona](https://web.archive.org/web/20040324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karona)'s influence spreads beyond the five-color Legend itself. In fact, it spills over into Cycling Week territory, because [Karona](https://web.archive.org/web/20040324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karona) also appears in the art of the cycle of five rare "supercyclers" from *Scourge*: **[Decree of Justice](https://web.archive.org/web/20040324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Decree+of+Justice)**, **[Decree of Silence](https://web.archive.org/web/20040324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Decree+of+Silence)**, [Decree of Pain](https://web.archive.org/web/20040324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Decree+of+Pain), **[Decree of Annihilation](https://web.archive.org/web/20040324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Decree+of+Annihilation)**, and **[Decree of Savagery](https://web.archive.org/web/20040324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Decree+of+Savagery)**.

But only as an ominous shadow.

Er... except on the blue one.

[Karona](https://web.archive.org/web/20040324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karona)'s shadow, in its characteristic cross-legged, long-robed pose, appears in the four non-blue Decrees, as seen in the art close-ups below. The blue Decree, [Decree of Silence](https://web.archive.org/web/20040324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Decree+of+Silence), depicts [Karona](https://web.archive.org/web/20040324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karona) herself surrounded by glowing energy, as if to cast long shadows on the other four cards...
