After many rounds of Swiss and some great games of *Magic*, the Top 8 for Grand Prix Minneapolis is set! Learn more about this event’s Top 8 competitors, their history at the top tables, and more by taking a look through the Top 8 profiles.  
  

* * *

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmin16/gpmin16_t8_turtenwald.jpg)

### Owen Turtenwald

**Age:** 27

**Hometown:** Las Vegas  
**Occupation:** Channelfireball.com

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

3 Pro Tour Top 8, Player of the Year 2011

**What Deck are you playing and why?**

White-Black Control

**What card are you most excited about playing with in Eternal Masters?**

[Volunteer Reserves](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Volunteer+Reserves)

**What's the most underrated card in Standard?**

[Read the Bones](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Read+the+Bones)

**Age:** 22

**Hometown:** Ann Arbor, MI  
**Occupation:** Mechanical Engineer

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

2 Pro Tour Top 50s, Columbus SCG Invitational Winner, 4-1ed a competitive Standard league with Humans

Humans. I have won every tournament I played it in.

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmin16/gpmin16_t8_hartman.jpg)Reed Hartman

**Age:** 28

**Hometown:** Dekalb, IL  
**Occupation:** Electrician

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmin16/gpmin16_t8_elenbogen.jpg)Andrew Elenbogen

**Age:** 22

**Hometown:** Ann Arbor, MI  
**Occupation:** Software Engineer

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

GP Omaha Top 8

13-2 GP Boston

Many GP Top 16s and Top 32s

**What Deck are you playing and why?**

Bant Humans with [Eldrazi Displacer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Displacer). I wanted to play Bant Company, but it wasn't proactive enough to beat rites and Green-White Tokens. This deck really ups the pressure to have mostly favorable matchups. Displacer is great against everything.

**What card are you most excited about playing with in Eternal Masters?**

[Winter Orb](https://web.archive.org/web/20160529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Winter+Orb)
