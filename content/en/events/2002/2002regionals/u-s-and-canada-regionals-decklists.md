The 2002 North American Regionals were a huge success. 6145 people competed in Regionals events in North America with two reports still out. Ohio Valley Regionals had the highest attendance with 688 people, making it the largest tournament ever held in North America. 

Here is a nearly complete archetype breakdown. With only 2 regions missing it seems clear that Green is the best color in a pretty diverse field. 

This is the Top 8 archetype breakdown with 23 of the 25 Regions reporting. 

*   **Red-Green Beatdown**: 39 (13 splashing black and 5 splashing white mostly for Mystic Enforcer)
*   **Psychatog**: 34
*   **Blue-Green-Red**: 26
*   **Blue-Green**: 25
*   **Braids** (including Black-Green, Black-White, and Black-Green-Red variants): 19
*   **Red-White-Blue** (Star-Spangled Slaughter): 7
*   **Black Control**: 6
*   **Mystic Enforcer**: 5
*   **Wild Research**: 2
*   **Bridge Decks**: 2
*   **Other**: 18
