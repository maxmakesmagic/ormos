## 2001 Grand Prix Valencia Coverage

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fd/df/fddfee672c07d16fbb35b69d1c892b2453f3a711d8f9fb6f318517601453edca.gif)

After thirteen rounds of Swiss, Spain's Ricard Tuduri was the number one seed going into the Top 8. Then he proved it was no fluke,losing only one game on his way to becoming 2001 Grand Prix Valencia Champion.
