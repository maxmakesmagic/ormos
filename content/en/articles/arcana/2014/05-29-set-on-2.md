Although *Conspiracy* takes place on Fiora, not all of the art in the *Conspiracy* set was limited to that plane. An obvious example would be the new art for [Rout](https://web.archive.org/web/20140529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rout).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c3/84/c384506c8ee52a8b450d9a15305c85a7adae8b883e007d502c110dc32d7acaf5.jpg)

[Rout](https://web.archive.org/web/20140529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rout) | Art by [Igor Kieryluk](https://web.archive.org/web/20140529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22igor%20kieryluk%22%5D)

**Q:** What plane does this art take place on?

*   Mirrodin - Right! This is during the war before Mirrodin succumbs to become New Phyrexia.
*   New Phyrexia - Wrong!
*   Innistrad - Wrong!

All right, I got a little sneaky with the "easy" one, sorry about that. Now onto a few other arts to show:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ae/0a/ae0a5842459c48fbefbee26df2fa5a5cb016383605d79379c87ba6ea4b94a9ba.jpg)

[Decimate](https://web.archive.org/web/20140529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Decimate) | Art by [Zoltan Boros](https://web.archive.org/web/20140529000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22zoltan%20boros%22%5D)

*   Innistrad - Wrong!
