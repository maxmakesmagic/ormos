Hey everyone, come see how good these lands look!

Seriously, the lands in *Commander (2016 Edition)* are home runs. With three versions of each basic land—one of each basic from [Rebecca Guay](https://web.archive.org/web/20161025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Rebecca%20Guay%22%5d), [Mark Poole](https://web.archive.org/web/20161025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Mark%20Poole%22%5d), and [Kev Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20161025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Kev+Walker%22%5d)—you're sure to find one you love.

Personally, I'm a fan of Rebecca Guay, but my individual favorite is the Kev Walker Plains, which manages to be haunting and serene at the same time.
