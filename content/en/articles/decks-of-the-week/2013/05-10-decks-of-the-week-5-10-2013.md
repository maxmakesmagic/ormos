The *Dragon's Maze* Prerelease on **Magic Online** is going on *right now*! New sets always change the Standard metagame, and *Dragon's Maze* is no exception. Get an early feel for where Standard is going with the following decks. Once you have a handle on the new metagame, try your hand at a nearby PTQ, which you can find with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#theros)! Good luck; have fun!
