Round 14 featured trios of familiar faces squaring off against each other for the fabled win-and-in match. In the last round of Swiss, only four teams would proceed to the Top 4 Team Draft.

On one side, No. 13 Ranked Player Samuel Black, Matt Severa, and Gaudenis Vidugiris. On the other side, the No. 3, 4, and 5 Ranked Players of the world respectively, Owen Turtenwald, William Jensen, Reid Duke.

Only one of these teams will advance. Which will it be?

**Severa vs. Jensen**

With both Severa and Jensen armed with aggressive red decks – Severa sporting some black and Jensen splashing green for [Kird Chieftain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kird+Chieftain)'s ability – it was no surprise to see these two finish up with ample time on the clock in three games.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gppor14/r14_black.jpg)

*Left to right: No. 13 Ranked Player Samuel Black, Matt Severa, Gaudenis Vidugiris*

The two traded blows in record time in the match, but the third game quickly became a lopsided affair when Severa's [Chandra, Pyromaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Pyromaster) was outclassed by the hard-hitting [Scuttling Doom Engine](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scuttling+Doom+Engine) from Jensen. Aside from having one of the coolest names to shout in a crowded room full of *Magic* players, the Doom Engine made quick work of Severa's Planeswalker, and then Severa himself over a couple of turns.

**Black vs. Turtenwald**

Black's [Soul of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soul+of+Zendikar) after both players cleared through each others' cards in hands gave him the upper hand, and Turtenwald was unable to catch up in the first game.

The second game was a particularly interesting affair, as Black had a slew of high-powered threats. [Soul of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soul+of+Zendikar), [Genesis Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genesis+Hydra), multiple [Illusory Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Illusory+Angel)s, along with some [Void Snare](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Void+Snare)s to keep Turtenwald off of [Avacyn, Guardian Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avacyn%2C+Guardian+Angel) looked like it would be enough. However, Black was unable to push through on those key turns, and Turtenwald's Avacyn did a fine job of holding back all of Black's forces.

However, before their match was able to finish...

**Vidugiris vs. Duke**

Vidugiris and Duke were into their third game while Black and Turtenwald were neck-deep into the second game. While the two were 1-1 going into the round, it was Duke who pulled out ahead in the third game. While [Paragon of New Dawns](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Paragon+of+New+Dawns) bolstered Vidugiris's [Raise the Alarm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raise+the+Alarm) tokens, [Triplicate Spirits](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triplicate+Spirits) proved to be too much of a commitment, as Duke aimed [Stab Wound](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stab+Wound) at the Paragon so that [Festergloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Festergloom) could leave Vidugiris with an empty board.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gppor14/r14_duke.jpg)

*Left to right: No. 3 Ranked Player Reid Duke, No. 4 Ranked Player William Jensen, No. 2 Ranked Player Owen Turtenwald*

After that, Vidugiris had little chance to get back into the game, as Duke's Nightfire Ghoul and grip full of spells made things elementary.

**Turtenwald, Jensen, and Duke defeat Black, Severa, and Vidugiris**
