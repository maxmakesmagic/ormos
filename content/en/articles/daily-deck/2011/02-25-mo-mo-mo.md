A little confused? The deck list you're looking at is from the Momir Basic format on [**Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20110526173949/http://www.wizards.com/magic/Digital/MagicOnline.aspx). A world of fun, Momir uses a Momir Vig, Simic Visionary Avatar. Rather than play spells, your deck is entirely basic lands. Your avatar allows you to "cast" your lands as creatures; you simply pay an amount of mana and discard a land and receive a random creature of that converted mana cost, pulling from all of **Magic Online**. That means that for seven mana, you can get something awesome like Platinum Angel, or lose instantly from something like Phage the Untouchable! It's a great break and an easy format to get into.
