One of the most famous misprints in Alpha (the very first edition of Magic) is the poor [Elvish Archers](https://web.archive.org/web/20021004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Archers).

The first printing of the card had the power and toughness reversed, making it a 1/2 first striker for ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless1.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/green.gif)! I'm sure there was some consternation among players once they figured out that it was a rare, as even the correct version doesn't knock anyone's socks off.

Like most of the Alpha errors, the Archers were printed the way Richard Garfield intended -- as 2/1 -- in Beta.
