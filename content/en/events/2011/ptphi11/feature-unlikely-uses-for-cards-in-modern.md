While most of the decks that are performing well in the Modern portion of Pro Tour Philadelphia are souped-up descendants of decks that have performed well in previous Standard, or Extended formats, there are still plenty of cards that have never before seen play in competitive Constructed decks which now serve oh-so-crucial roles in Modern.

For example, who would have thought that [Blighted Agent](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blighted+Agent) would be a key component in the fastest combo deck in Modern? Then there are other cards, like [Dragonstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonstorm), that are being used in ways that few people ever could have imagined they would be a few weeks ago.

![Blighted Agent](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Blighted+Agent)

Think you're ready to take a look at some of the strangest sights that Modern has to offer? Then read on!

### When You Absolutely Can't Wait

If you've been keeping up with Modern for the past few weeks, then you probably already know that [Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor) is a staple card in most [Cloudpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudpost) decks.

![Amulet of Vigor](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Amulet+of+Vigor)

[Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor) speeds these decks, which are full of enters-the-battlefield-tapped lands like [Cloudpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudpost) and [Vesuva](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vesuva), up by a turn or more. Getting your mana just one turn earlier can be the difference between casting an [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Emrakul%252C%2Bthe%2BAeons%2BTorn), the Aeon's Torn that you use to ravage your opponent or losing to a barrage of attacks from [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl)s and [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf)s or a million copies of [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch).

[Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor) works well in multiples allowing you to tap your brand new lands for mana while the other [Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor) triggers are still on the stack, and they even combine with [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan)s to give you your mana immediately.

If you feel like your [Cloudpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudpost) deck is a bit too slow to compete, then [Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor) might be just the card you're looking for.

### Two Creatures Enter

Gerry Thompson and Michael Jacob have been seen searching up [Arena](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arena) with [Knight of the Reliquary](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+the+Reliquary) this weekend. Yes, that [Arena](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arena).

![Arena](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Arena)

The land has been used to kill off [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant)s that were threatening to draw players into all sorts of good cards, [Gaddock Teeg](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gaddock+Teeg)s that were locking out key spells, and even [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch)s while [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin)s are on the stack.

### Turn Two [Wall of Roots](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Roots), Turn Three [Nekrataal](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nekrataal), Turn Four You're Dead

Have you ever seen someone play a turn-two [Wall of Roots](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Roots) then follow it up with a turn-three [Nekrataal](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nekrataal)? If you've been playing long enough you almost certainly have as that was once a common sequence of plays. But have you ever seen someone then use both of those creatures to set up a turn four kill with the aid of only one additional spell? Even if you've been playing religiously since 1994, I'm going to go out on a limb and guess that you probably haven't.

How does it work you ask? Pretty easily actually.

The player simply plays a fourth land, adds a mana with [Wall of Roots](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Roots), and casts [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod). The player then sacrifices [Wall of Roots](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Roots) to fetch a [Pestermite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pestermite) (or a [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch)) untapping [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) – which is then activated again to upgrade [Nekrataal](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nekrataal) into a [Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kiki-Jiki%2C+Mirror+Breaker) (which is in turn used to make a copy of [Pestermite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pestermite) that untaps [Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kiki-Jiki%2C+Mirror+Breaker), which makes another copy of [Pestermite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pestermite)...).

### Alfred, I've Been Poisoned

[Dragonstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonstorm) has been a key component in combo decks for years; cast a couple of [Seething Song](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seething+Song)s and [Rite of Flame](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rite+of+Flame)s, then once you're up to 9 mana cast [Dragonstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonstorm) and fetch out four copies of [Bogardan Hellkite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bogardan+Hellkite) to burn your opponent for 20. This weekend, players have been seen running [Dragonstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonstorm) in decks that don't even have any Dragons in them!

Wait, what?

John Stolzmann, Sam Black, and Zaiem Beg were seen pitching [Dragonstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonstorm) to [Blazing Shoal](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blazing+Shoal) to set up turn-two and turn-three kills with infect creatures like [Inkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inkmoth+Nexus) and [Blighted Agent](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blighted+Agent). Why are they playing [Dragonstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonstorm) as an expensive red spell instead of something like [Greater Gargadon](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Greater+Gargadon) that might have utility even when it isn't being used as a part of the combo?

No, as Zaiem explained, it's not because they're trying to be cute; it's because they can find [Dragonstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonstorm) with [Peer Through Depths](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peer+Through+Depths) while they would need to naturally draw [Greater Gargadon](https://web.archive.org/web/20110903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Greater+Gargadon).
