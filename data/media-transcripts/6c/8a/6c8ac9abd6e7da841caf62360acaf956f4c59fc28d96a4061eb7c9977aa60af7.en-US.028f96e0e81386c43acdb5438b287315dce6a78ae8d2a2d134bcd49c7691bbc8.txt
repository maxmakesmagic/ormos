Is Jonathan Becker reporting to you from Pro Tour Philadelphia 2005? Now, one of the coolest things about being here on the Pro Tour is the ability to travel a lot of cool different places. And one of the best things about traveling a lot of different places is ability to eat a lot of different kinds of great food. At the Pro Tour the days are so jam packed for the players do they often have to make do with a quick bite or a bag of chips? The Rain Tournel Markets been operating in Philadelphia since the mid 1880s and since in the building which is the Philadelphia historical landmark.
