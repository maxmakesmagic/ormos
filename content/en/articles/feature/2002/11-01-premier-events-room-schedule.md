***All times shown are Pacific Standard Time.***  
**Notes:**  
All qualifier events have double the normal prizes. The first and second place finishers in a qualifier event both receive invites to the Monthly Championship.  
 Entry Fees: $10 for Constructed events; $5 for Limited events.  
 All non-qualifier events have the normal prizes. Any unscheduled non-qualifier events will be announced in game, and will appear in the **Premier Events** room list.  
 Entry Fees: $5 for Constructed events; $2 for Limited events.
