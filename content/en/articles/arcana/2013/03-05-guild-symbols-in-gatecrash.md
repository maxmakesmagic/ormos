The city-plane of Ravnica is full of guilds. And it's also full of guild symbols. And from that it follows that *Gatecrash*, being set on Ravnica, has guild symbols in card art.

Sometimes, the symbol is prominent, in the fashion of the Boros Truefire Paladin.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f4/64/f464acb3b123f5a385c3b9486dfd8a038913961e54366fc51a715179b28248a8.jpg) 

Truefire Paladin | Art by [Michael C. Hayes](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22michael%20c.%20hayes%22])

Sometimes, they're being formed out of *pure magic*, like the Simic symbol on Gyre Sage.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/78/ee/78ee524a2b0678fbf14cc7dfaf7337d73cba6d868627590c31fa2d0697418218.jpg) 

Gyre Sage | Art by [Tyler Jacobson](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22tyler%20jacobson%22])

Sometimes they're hidden, like the Gruul graffiti on the wall next to Wasteland Viper.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/cd/3d/cd3d2a22dbac3aab7727a2a68aa8b6adeb24fdb6eb76f3d84acd88bbdb9c55f0.jpg) 

Wasteland Viper | Art by [Lucas Graciano](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22lucas%20graciano%22])

Sometimes it's chiseled on the wall of an Orzhov mausoleum, as on Immortal Servitude.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/dc/27/dc27be58d18184b198efff2fc1021e19fc6f725ca54adfc29f2c7fc41fcef3a9.jpg) 

Immortal Servitude | Art by [Seb McKinnon](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22seb%20mckinnon%22])

Sometimes they're graffiti on a person! Wait, that's just a Dimir tattoo. Anyway, here's Coerced Confession.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0c/05/0c05ecd3c2fc5d82ebc8285d4473d9acece23ca2d79c64027762047a120a5c01.jpg) 

Coerced Confession | Art by [Mathias Kollros](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22mathias%20kollros%22])
