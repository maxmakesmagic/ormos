After 15 rounds of Standard, with a total of 1,502 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppar16/gppar16_t8_magnani.jpg)Paolo Magnani

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Bomberteam pro

**Hometown:** Rome, Italy

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

I sometimes beat my buddy Marco Cammilluzzi.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Mardu Green. Marco shipped me his list and it kind of reminded me of Modern Jund.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Probably put some [Cranial Extraction](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Extraction) effect into the sideboard.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino).

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppar16/gppar16_t8_reale.jpg)Giuseppe Reale

**Age:** 31

**Occupation:** Cammilluzzi's best friend

**Hometown:** Rome, Italy

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Back to back Top 8s. Here and at Grand Prix Bologna.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Four-Color Rally. It's the best one.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Ask Owen Turtenwald.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Reflector Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reflector+Mage).

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppar16/gppar16_t8_tamada.jpg)Ryoichi Tamada

**Age:** 31

**Occupation:** Shop owner

**Hometown:** Osaka, Japan

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

One Pro Tour Top 8, two Grand Prix Top 8s.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Bant Company. [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company) and [Reflector Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reflector+Mage) are very nice. But Four-Color Rally is weak against [Dispel](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dispel) and [Hallowed Moonlight](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallowed+Moonlight).

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

None.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc).

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppar16/gppar16_t8_juza.jpg)Martin Jůza

**Age:** 28

**Occupation:** Professional tourist

**Hometown:** Plzen, Czech Republich

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Some Top 8s here and there.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Abzan, because this is the last Standard Grand Prix where [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) is legal. Also, because it's great.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I'd replace one [Arashin Cleric](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arashin+Cleric) in the sideboard with [Kalitas, Traitor of Ghet](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kalitas%2C+Traitor+of+Ghet). I just forgot to do that before the tournament.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino), duh.

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppar16/gppar16_t8_sochurek.jpg)Petr Sochůrek

**Age:** 22

**Occupation:**

**Hometown:** Prague, Czech Republic

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Pro Tour Top 16, Grand Prix Top 8, gold-level pro.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Grixis. I never considered not playing [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy) and [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) as long as they are legal, and Grixis seemed like the best shell for them.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

The deck is the best.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time).

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppar16/gppar16_t8_stark.jpg)Ben Stark

**Age:** 32

**Occupation:** None

**Hometown:** Cord Springs, Florida, USA

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Won Pro Tour Paris in 2011, won Grand Prix Indy in 2012, four Pro Tour Top 8s, 17 Grand Prix Top 8s, Magic Hall of Fame.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Dark Jeskai. Plays all close games, every one is a sweat, never know if you will win or lose.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Take the [Arashin Cleric](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arashin+Cleric)s out of the sideboard. No one played Atarka Red.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy). Best card in Standard.

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppar16/gppar16_t8_huschenbeth.jpg)Arne Huschenbeth

**Age:** 18

**Occupation:** None

**Hometown:** Hamburg, Germany

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

None.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Esper Dragons, because [Dragonlord Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai) is the best.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Take out [Dragonlord's Prerogative](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord%27s+Prerogative), put in [Painful Truths](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Painful+Truths). Not play [Infinite Obliteration](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Infinite+Obliteration).

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

Island.

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppar16/gppar16_t8_friedrich.jpg)Fabian Friedrich

**Age:** 30

**Occupation:** Mechanical engineer

**Hometown:** Berlin, Germany

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Berlin City Champion 2014.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Blue Abzan. If you're on curve it's nearly unbeatable. And you can counter [Rally the Ancestors](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rally+the+Ancestors)!

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Change [Flaying Tendrils](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flaying+Tendrils) to [Languish](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Languish).

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Stubborn Denial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160320000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stubborn+Denial).
