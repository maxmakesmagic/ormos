### Go To A Paper Event, Get A Royal Assassin

* * *

***Magic** Online* may have just celebrated its first birthday last month, but the **Magic** game itself is about to celebrate its tenth! There's a pretty cool party going on too and all of you are invited.

<table><tbody><tr><td><p>Whether you're a grizzled veteran who already plays lots of tournaments using the paper-version of the game, or someone who came back to <b>Magic</b> because of the convenience and ease of finding opponents via <i><b>Magic </b>Online, </i>or someone who is brand new to the game and has never ventured out into a local gaming store, we want you to come celebrate with us. We're even going to give you a bribe.</p>&#10;<p>One of the coolest cards from early days of <b>Magic </b>was <a>Royal Assassin</a>. It was in the first four iterations of the core set, but it hasn't been reprinted since 1995. Well not only is Royal Assassin back, but he's also being turned into a digital object avatar within <i><b>Magic </b>Online.</i></p>&#10;<p>Here's the catch: The only way to get a Royal Assassin avatar in <i><b>Magic </b>Online </i>is to venture out into the world of paper <b>Magic </b>and participate in one of the Global Celebration tournaments that are being offered around the world to celebrate the <b>Magic</b> game's 10th Anniversary. (Of course, you could also trade for one, but that doesn't sound like as much fun as participating in a big birthday party, does it?)</p>&#10;</td>&#10;<td><img src="https://i.ormos.uk/1d/2f/1d2faab9b9c63acce6b65cc23cf019eb84d8acd56b8712ba6e2fb91a6bd65e9e.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

  
  

<table><tbody><tr><td><p><a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20030711000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=/default.asp?x=mtgcom/resource/10aStores&amp;origin=magic_magiconline_news071103"><b>You can click here for a list of retail locations that are hosting the events</b></a>, which takes place on Saturday July 26 and Sunday July 27. It's a Sealed Deck tournament, so you don't need to bring any cards or prepare anything in advance. Just show up at one of those stores and pay the entry fee and you'll get five <i>Eighth Edition </i>boosters plus all the land you want to build your deck. You get to keep all those cards afterwards plus you get to keep the foil promotional <a>Rukh Egg</a> that is being handed out to everyone who plays, plus if you do well you may win other prizes.</p>&#10;<p>It doesn't matter if you've never played a tournament before. You will be given a DCI number when you sign up (and you'll need that DCI number later when it comes time to claim your Royal Assassin avatar). Of course, if you already have a DCI number you should use that one -- if you can't remember it they can look it up for you on site. On August 1 we'll put up a web page on the <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20030711000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com&amp;origin=magic_magiconline_news071103"><i><b>Magic </b>Online </i>Store</a> where you can input your DCI number plus your <i><b>Magic </b>Online </i>user name and request your Royal Assassin. Then on September 1 we'll run all that information through our database and hand out avatars to everyone who played in the Global Celebration events.</p>&#10;</td>&#10;<td><img src="https://i.ormos.uk/71/27/7127345575ed5524aed1c9f7ce6351cce7df3f4399384af6e4c9c4b40008853e.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

Pretty simple, eh? Just go have fun and play in a Global Celebration event on July 26 or 27 -- that should be a good time all on its own -- and then sometime in August you fill out a simple web form. Come September you'll be tapping to kill stuff.
