Kuldotha Forgemaster in Standard? If you're Martin Juza, you don't have any hesitation about casting the five-drop to tutor up powerful artifacts like Blightsteel Colossus! The top pro used the deck to post a respectable Standard record at the recent Pro Tour Paris, part of **Magic** Weekend Paris. For full coverage of the event click [here](/en/events/2011/ptpar11/stark-flies-high-with-planeswalkers-in-paris).
