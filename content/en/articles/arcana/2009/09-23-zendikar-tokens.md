If you have read [today's Savor the Flavor](/en/articles/savortheflavor/2009/09-23-the-master-at-arms), you will have noticed a Kor Soldier token from *Zendikar*. If you haven't yet, well, there's the link right there in the sentence before this one. We'd reprint the image here, but we've got *ten other token cards* to get through first. Mixmaster *Zendikar*, whatcha got for us?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/33/1e/331ea6947acfb99c6293f37af2ca6219e9fd669576cfb19a698d77d5623f535a.jpg) 

  

Sure, a 4/4 Angel token. I believe that goes well with [Luminarch Ascension](https://i.ormos.uk/7b/2e/7b2ece54c9d59b79a37c16facd5506dcb7a1887e065544832ff47d29f7c23892.jpg).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4c/67/4c67cf702c035dc13464a1913899d194a5219489cc39508e590d4865d6afcf81.jpg) 

  

A 1/1 flying bird. Very nice. I wonder what *Zendikar* card makes those!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9f/17/9f175d72be15922cfd7697b331bde8d7642f519431fd33b786761934a6b1707b.jpg) 

  

Next we have a 2/2 Illusion—or do we? (Yes. We do.)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9d/14/9d14e959e46fd34117695af51fb3cddf2036b96571aa76e6209997d8b3f22376.jpg) 

  

You never know when you'll meet a 1/1 Merfolk. Although you're more likely to when you're already working with [Lullmage Mentor](https://i.ormos.uk/99/7d/997d61a6ba667ed34513852745c4e88b326e1907c1f96647292a5b0315674a27.jpg).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/60/09/60094ff669b5c1ebc91398d51cd2f3b69349fa1bbfaab24885d24432bd560de2.jpg) 

  

Who is this mysterious vampire with the variable power and toughness? Only [Kalitas, Bloodchief of Ghet](https://i.ormos.uk/38/2a/382ac95b008e55dde68627b3d462e609cfb91ac22009b9c908e368c2084d1f24.jpg) knows the answer!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c6/f9/c6f9a4e06bc2890c306210557346ab509104bf03b8bb7acbf9622495abf97449.jpg) 

  

Ooh! A Zombie Giant! And he's 5/5, which is certainly big for a zombie. So *that's* what happens when you go on a [Quest for the Gravelord](https://i.ormos.uk/3f/53/3f53d89e6e02851d954bc6fa5971ed1ebfd85e8b50a2de212816967d72fcbeff.jpg)!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a9/dc/a9dc1f8845ea8390e65e75ca2f9917cfa11bfb5758311263e08264b8cdff5301.jpg) 

  

Our seventh token (go back and count!) is, fittingly enough, a 7/1 Elemental. It's really got that [Elemental Appeal](https://i.ormos.uk/91/7c/917cc5781f103a798dab193785927142cd5bb9d24180b42c2dd333cb7f781085.jpg).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9c/28/9c2884eb4d4103e7f10ccc10ad771b4b55a867eaad55e1c093e37e351afd56bd.jpg) 

  

What would **Magic** be without 4/4 Beasts? Thanks to [Rampaging Baloths](https://i.ormos.uk/a0/cc/a0cc40f1662b3f3beaf671d4b63779c472ffb2891e418c9ce3a5d203f0e8c12d.jpg), we don't have to consider the terrifying possibility!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/76/14/7614b9615fd9fb0f8b04e79c00a4fd256750a07ac7caae2791f9ed1de9c4aec8.jpg) 

  

Snake. Snake? SNAAAAAAAAKE!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6a/54/6a540b873cd18f80bdd8e107c200c2910d741b9a73ae0e41568f01c50d60b9e7.jpg) 

  

And finally, thanks to [Turntimber Ranger](https://i.ormos.uk/be/d2/bed2e048a25273483d200ab3a0f78ad410efda8ec0c6e69a08eae5912faec87d.jpg), we've got a 2/2 Wolf. Nice doggy!
