Check out the coverage for [Grand Prix–Kansas City](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/heroics-in-the-heartland) and [Grand Prix–Paris](/fr/articles/event-coverage/2008/10-18-grand-prixparis-2008), and of course scope out the Top 8 lists below. 

After that we've got your weekly PTQ update, the Worlds Qualifier on **Magic Online**, and your Premier Event lists too. Enjoy! (And don't forget, *Shards of Alara* has been released on **Magic Online**.
