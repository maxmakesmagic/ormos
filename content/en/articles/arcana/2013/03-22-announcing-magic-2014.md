Today, PAX East begins in Boston, and with that comes your first look at **Magic 2014—Duels of the Planeswalkers**, our follow up from last year's [Duels of the Planeswalkers 2013](https://web.archive.org/web/20130620085249/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Digital/DuelsOfThePlaneswalkers.aspx). This year join the Planeswalker Chandra Nalaar in a quest to track down a manipulative and dangerous Planeswalker foe.

![Magic 2014—Duels of the Planeswalkers](https://i.ormos.uk/cd/10/cd10ac6f1a0de3ae97b7c7d56f72bf47983779389276280d77fb2966a7e029df.jpg "Magic 2014—Duels of the Planeswalkers") 

There are a number of cool new things in **Magic 2014** this time around, including:

1.  **Android**: It will be available on Android tablet for the first time ever via Amazon App Store and Google Play. It will feature ad-hoc multiplayer at launch and we will continue to examine online multiplayer options for a future update. **Magic 2014** will continue supporting XBox, Playstation, PC, and iPad as well.

2.  **New Play Mode**: We're not revealing what it is yet, but we've added another great way to play **Magic**!

3.  **Ten New Decks**: More decks to unlock and battle online with!

4.  **More Encounters** and **More Challenges**

If you are at PAX East come by the **Magic** booth (#562) and play a demo of the new game before it is released.
