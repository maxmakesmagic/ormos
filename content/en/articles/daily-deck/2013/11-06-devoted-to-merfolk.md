Merfolk has hovered around the fringes of Modern for quite some time, never making as many waves as it does in Legacy. The lack of [Force of Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+of+Will) and [Wasteland](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland) hurts the squad of fighting fish, as disruption is what makes tempo decks like this truly great. However, with a few new additions from *Theros* and a suite of the disruption that is available, Hall of Famer Raph Lévy took Merfolk to battle at [Grand Prix Antwerp](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/gpant13/welcome).

The combination of [Thassa, God of the Sea](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thassa%2C+God+of+the+Sea); [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves); and [Kira, Great Glass-Spinner](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kira%2C+Great+Glass-Spinner) gives this deck plenty of legs (something it was certainly lacking before). Spot removal and cheap sweepers have long been Merfolk's bane, and the three cards I just named are not only resistant to them, they tend to make your other cards more resistant as well. Kira straight-up protects your team, and Thassa and Master both let you commit less to the board while still pressuring your opponent. Thassa and Master also sometimes just provide 5+ power in a single card, something Merfolk has also not had before.

[Lord of Atlantis](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lord+of+Atlantis) is still the king, though, as you can see by the amount of [Lord of Atlantis](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lord+of+Atlantis)es, [Master of the Pearl Trident](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+the+Pearl+Trident)s, and [Coralhelm Commander](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Coralhelm+Commander)s present. Interestingly enough, [Merrow Reejerey](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merrow+Reejerey) didn't make the cut, likely because Thassa and Kira took all the three-drop spots. Getting multiple lords into play is the classic Merfolk win condition, and this deck is still very capable of doing so. Besides the lords, [Cursecatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cursecatcher) and [Silvergill Adept](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silvergill+Adept) round out the actual Merfolk component, and both are mainstays in any Merfolk deck.

[Spell Pierce](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Pierce), [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas), [Vapor Snag](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vapor+Snag), and [Tectonic Edge](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tectonic+Edge) may not have quite the power of [Force of Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+of+Will), [Wasteland](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland), and [Daze](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daze), but they are all very cheap and can certainly stop the various combo decks in the format (with [Vapor Snag](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vapor+Snag) being a catch-all against creature decks). With a fast enough clock, a timely [Spell Pierce](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Pierce) is enough to buy the extra turn the deck needs, and the same is true of [Tectonic Edge](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tectonic+Edge). [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) plays double duty, not only disrupting colors and Urza lands, but also giving the opponent Islands for your Merfolk to walk all over.
