You may know Jedit Ojanen as a 7-mana blue-and-white legend from *Legends*: 

 ![Jedit Ojanen](https://i.ormos.uk/55/39/5539950fdd9cbd28c89c5688abe52b054a953b69253bb7ef3d34f20f4ad29c38.jpg) 

But he also starred in a 1996 comic book from Armada / Acclaim Comics written to tell his story:

![Legend of Jedit Ojanen](https://i.ormos.uk/80/a3/80a37aad0bdf5df8b992ede9d1af03ac390aed1aa3c66703f4d6cdf6a16323f8.jpg)  
 

During issue 1 of 2 of Jedit's story, he encounters other creatures seen on **Magic** cards. He spots a Fire Drake and gets a Healing Salve from passing Desert Nomads. At the cliffhanger climax on the last page of the issue, he encounters this recognizable fellow:

![Johan and his... dogs?](https://i.ormos.uk/ea/c9/eac90d17ebceb997268eecbf117c60031793cb0bf1a2612f403edf21339ba3d2.jpg)  
 

Next week: Can Jedit overcome fellow legend Johan and his summoned Beasts of Bogardan?
