### Roland Chang

**Age:** 31  
**Occupation:** Sales Engineer @ Yahoo!  
  

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
2005 Vintage World Champion, 2006 Legacy World Champion 

**What did you play this weekend? Why?**  
I played Martello Shops because to me it was a coinflip between running either this deck or Terranova. The decision came at the eleventh hour and I went with something I spent the last year competing with in local east coast tournaments. 

**What would you change about your deck if you played it next weekend?**  
Not one card. I'll leave that up to the Forino brothers. 

**What has been the most important card in your deck this weekend? Why?**  
[Trinisphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trinisphere). It made life very easy when it resolved. 

**What would winning the Vintage Championship mean to you?**  
It would make me incredibly happy to see things come full circle after taking a hiatus from the game from 2006 to 2012.

### Harry Corvese

**Age:** 26  
**Occupation:** Professional Vintager  
  

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
Four Grand Prix Top 8s, 32,171 lifetime planeswalker points. 

**What did you play this weekend? Why?**  
[Oath of Druids](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oath+of+Druids) because it seemed like Blue/Red Delver would be very popular. So said Kurt Speiss. 

**What would you change about your deck if you played it next weekend?**  
My sleeves. 

**What has been the most important card in your deck this weekend? Why?**  
All the broken cards have been spectacular, but [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) really shined in round nine. 

**What would winning the Vintage Championship mean to you?**  
I have always wanted to break into Vintage and this would be a great start! I see that there is an awesome Vintage community and I hope to do them proud! 

  
  

  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/eternalweekend14/t8glackin1.jpg)

### Ryan Glackin

**Age:** 34  
**Occupation:** Online Store Manager, Alternate Universes  
  

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
Top 4 2011 Vintage Champs, Top 8 2010 Vintage Champs, Top 9 2012 Legacy Champs, A few NYSE Vintage tournament wins, A random PTQ Top 8. 

**What did you play this weekend? Why?**  
Blue/Red Delver. I took Rich Shay's Vintage Super League list and made a couple adjustments to suit my style. (More bolts!) I honestly didn't know what I was playing until last night after running well in Legacy Champs with a similar deck. 

**What would you change about your deck if you played it next weekend?**  
I might try to find room for one more [Gush](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gush). Switch out the [Izzet Staticaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Izzet+Staticaster) for either [Electrickery](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrickery) or CIRCLE OF FLAME. 

**What has been the most important card in your deck this weekend? Why?**  
[Time Walk](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Time+Walk), not close. Watch my feature match against Joel. It's way more than a Blue [Explore](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Explore). Sometimes, I cast it twice in a single game. 

**What would winning the Vintage Championship mean to you?**  
Keeping this beautiful painting in the state of Pennsylvania. Promotion to "full member" on TMD. (Maybe "Vintage Adept" if I'm lucky)
