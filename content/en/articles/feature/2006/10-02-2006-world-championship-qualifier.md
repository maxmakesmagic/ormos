The 2006 Magic: The Gathering World Championship will take place November 29 – December 3, 2006 in Paris, France. The World Championship is the highest-level competition that Magic™ has to offer.

The Magic Online World Championship qualifier is a one-time opportunity which gives Magic Online players a chance to qualify and compete in the most exciting Magic: The Gathering Tournament of the year. Not only will the winner of the qualifier receive an invitation to compete, airfare and hotel in France is included in the prize for those who qualify!\*

### Magic Online World Championship Qualifier Preliminary Tournaments

Dates: Thursday, October 5, 2006 through Wednesday October 11, 2006.

Thirty-two (32) Premier Events will be designated "Worlds QT Prelim" events. Each Worlds QT Prelim will invite the Top 8 finishers to play in the 2006 World Championship Qualifier Tournament on Saturday October 14, 2006 at 9:00 AM PST. See the [Magic Online Event Schedule](https://web.archive.org/web/20070514063412/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/eventsmain) for a complete schedule of Worlds QT Prelims.

Formats: Standard, RAV Sealed, and CSP Sealed

Cost: Constructed Formats – 10 Tix  
 Limited Formats – 5 Tix, plus Product

Tournament Size: All Worlds QT Prelim events will have a maximum attendance of 128 players.

Prizes: 3x Prizes, manual pay to Top 16:

In addition, the Top 8 finishers of each Worlds QT Prelim are invited to the 2006 World Championship Qualifier on October 14, 2006.

### 2006 Magic Online World Championship Qualifier

START TIME: Saturday October 14, 9:00 am PST 

FORMAT: Standard

ADMISSION: FREE – (No Tix, Invitation only)

PRIZE SUPPORT: 6x, Manual pay to Top 32:

<table><tbody><tr><td><b>Place</b></td>&#10;<td><b>Boosters</b></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr><td>1</td>&#10;<td>72</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr><td>2</td>&#10;<td>54</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr><td>3-4</td>&#10;<td>36</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr><td>5-8</td>&#10;<td>18</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr><td>9-16</td>&#10;<td>9</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr><td>17-32</td>&#10;<td>6</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

The 9-32 place prizes will be manually granted within 72 hours of event completion.

In addition, the winner of the World Championship Qualifier receives an invitation to the 2006 World Championships in Paris, France, including two round-trip economy-class tickets from the airport nearest the winner's location to Paris and five nights hotel accommodation during Worlds\*.

Although players may have multiple accounts qualified for the World Championship Qualifier event, players are not permitted to have more than 1 of their accounts entered in the event. Players are not permitted to have secondary accounts played by another person. If a player is discovered to have more than 1 account playing in the World Championship Qualifier event, either before, during, or after the event, appropriate penalties will be applied (including without limitation disqualification from the event with no refund of product or event tickets).

If a player wins who has previously qualified for Worlds in Paris, the airfare and accommodations will be awarded to that player while the invitation to 2006 World Championship will pass down to the player with the best finish who is not already invited to Worlds. The final Swiss standings will determine the difference between third and fourth place and the difference between fifth through eighth place, should that be necessary.

If a player wins that is ineligible to play in DCI™-sanctioned tournaments (i.e. DCI suspended and/or a paid contractor), then the Worlds invitation and the travel and accommodation prizes will pass down to the player with the best finish who is not already invited to Worlds. The final Swiss standings will determine the difference between third and fourth place and the difference between fifth through eighth place, should that be necessary.

In either of the above cases, the booster pack prizes will not pass down.

In order to remain eligible for Prizes, all qualified players **must** email molworldsqt@wizards.com by Thursday, October 12, 2006 at 5:00 PM PDT with the following information: Full Legal Name, Full Address, Telephone number, and DCI Number. **Players must put their MTG:O Username as the subject of the email**. (Account passwords will not be requested--never give your password to anyone.) *Please do not email unless you have already qualified for the Championship.* Do not send an email for another player or account, and do not have another person send the email for you.

In addition, during the tournament, Wizards of the Coast staff (marked with the Wizards logo beside their online name) may ask players for their name and/or contact information. Do not give out your information to anyone who is not a Wizards of the Coast Staff (marked with the Wizards logo beside their online name).

Failure to provide accurate information may result in significant penalties, including suspension from online **Magic**tournaments.

**\*** The **Magic Online™ travel prize package** includes round-trip, coach airfare for the winner and a guest from the major airport nearest the winner's home to Paris, France to attend the **Magic the Gathering** 2006 World Championship held on November 29 through December 3, 2006, and double occupancy accommodations at a hotel chosen by Wizards of the Coast, Inc. or its designate. Meals, gratuities, ground transportation and all other fees, costs, and expenses not specified herein are the winner's sole responsibility. All taxes are the sole responsibility of the winner. A representative of Wizards of the Coast, Inc. will contact the winner regarding this prize package. This prize is non-negotiable, non-transferable, and not redeemable for an alternate award except in Wizards' sole discretion. Trip must be taken on the dates designated by Wizards, or prize will be forfeited and will not be re-awarded. Specific travel dates and arrangements are subject to availability. Winner and guest must travel together on same itinerary. The winner and his/her guest will be required to sign and return prior to travel (i) a signed Consent and Release Agreement, (ii) the appropriate IRS tax form, (iii) proof of identification, and (iv) and other documentation designated by Wizards; failure to do so will result in disqualification. Minors (i.e., those under 18 years of age) who win the Magic Online World Championship Qualifier are not eligible to be awarded the plane ticket and hotel travel award. Instead, minors will be awarded a "Cash Equivalent Award" equal to the cost of a round trip ticket to the World Championship, as determined by Wizards in its sole discretion. This award will be issued only if the player actually shows up to the World Championship in Paris on his or her own, and registers to play. Wizards will pay out this "Minor Qualifier Travel – Cash Equivalent Award" after the World Championship.

### Important information regarding system crashes and refunds for Premier Events

If during your event the system crashes, 1 of 3 things will happen:

1.  The system will come right back up. In this case:

    *   Events will continue and additional time will be given to allow players to finish the event
    *   No Compensation will be given

2.  The system cannot recover the event and "finishes" (prematurely ends) the event, or a WOTC Tournament Coordinator must "finish" (prematurely end) the event.

    *   We will accept the prize payouts "as is" - This means that whatever prize payouts and invites the system awards, we will not change what the system does.
    *   No changes will be made to these events from what the system automatically pays out as prizes or invitations

    If the event is in TOP 8:

    *   No product or tickets will be refunded
    *   All players keep the product used to enter the event as well as the drafted cards from the TOP 8 draft.

    If the event is still in Swiss Rounds:

    *   Players can [request a refund through Customer Service](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=161).
    *   We will refund your Product and tickets.

3.  The event is cleared or cancelled by the system. If an event is cancelled this means the event can no longer be viewed in the premier event listings.

    *   The system will automatically refund your tickets.
    *   Players can [request a refund through Customer Service](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=161).
    *   We will refund your Product.
