This match pits the Dutch Schietkoe team against the French NPC All Stars. Tom Van De Logt heads the team of two Cornelissen brothers, while the French team consists of Alexandre peset, Sylvain Lehoux and Loic Degrou, the former fresh off a PT Top 8 in Kobe.

The Dutch team won the coin flip and chose to kick off the draft. 

The first booster had three good black cards in [Pewter Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pewter+Golem), Betrayal Of Flesh and [Nightmare Lash](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightmare+Lash). Tom took the Betrayal and Jesse the [Pewter Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pewter+Golem). This left [Soldier Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soldier+Replica) for Lehoux, [Nightmare Lash](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightmare+Lash) for Peset and [Vulshok Berserker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulshok+Berserker) for Degrou. Stijn wheeled [Slith Predator](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slith+Predator) and [Clockwork Condor](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clockwork+Condor), leaving Talisman Of Dominance for Degrou. 

The second booster gave Jesse his first foray into affinity - a possible bomb in [Broodstar](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Broodstar). Lehoux took [Auriok Bladewarden](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auriok+Bladewarden), Peset [Banshee's Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banshee%27s+Blade) and Degrou [Viridian Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viridian+Shaman). Stijn took the remaining good green card in [Fangren Hunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fangren+Hunter), leaving [Pewter Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pewter+Golem) and [Soldier Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soldier+Replica) for Tom. Seems like the Frenchmen made a mistake here taking the Blade over [Pewter Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pewter+Golem) in Peset's obviously black deck. 

Lehoux took an [Auriok Transfixer](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auriok+Transfixer) for his first pick, Peset continuing his heavy black commitment with [Consume Spirit](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Consume+Spirit). [Tel-Jilad Chosen](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tel-Jilad+Chosen) went into Degrou's deck. Stijn then picked a [Vulshok Berserker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulshok+Berserker), Tom a [Relic Bane](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Relic+Bane), and Jesse got an off-color spellbomb for his affinity deck. 

Peset again didn't need to commit to a second color when he opened an [Icy Manipulator](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Icy+Manipulator). Degrou got a [Tel-Jilad Archers](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tel-Jilad+Archers), a strong card against the affinity deck. However, this left [Myr Enforcer](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Enforcer) and [Nim Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nim+Replica) for Jesse's deck. Tom got a [Leonin Skyhunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Skyhunter), Stijn took a [Copper Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Copper+Myr), and Lehoux wheeled a Raise The Alarm and [Irradiate](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Irradiate). 

Degrou was happy to get another Archers against the Jesse's affinity deck when he opened his first booster. Stijn got a [Krark-Clan Grunt](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Krark-Clan+Grunt), Tom took a Raise The Alarm, and Jesse got a [Somber Hoverguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Somber+Hoverguard). Lehoux wheeled a [Pyrite Spellbomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyrite+Spellbomb) and a red myr, and Peset got another of his beloved [Banshee's Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banshee%27s+Blade)s. This left [Rustmouth Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rustmouth+Ogre) for Stijn. 

The last booster of the first set was pretty weak, Stijn taking a [Tel-Jilad Chosen](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tel-Jilad+Chosen), Tom taking the slow [Vulshok Battlegear](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulshok+Battlegear), and Jesse getting a [Consume Spirit](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Consume+Spirit) - not the best affinity card, but probably playable. Lehoux got [Hematite Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hematite+Golem) and [Auriok Transfixer](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auriok+Transfixer), Peset a [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite), and Degrou hate drafted a [Cobalt Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cobalt+Golem). 

After the first round of boosters, all of the players had decent starts to their decks. Jesse's deck was looking like a really good B/U affinity deck, while his opponent had some protection from artifacts guys that should be good in the matchup. Lehoux has a decent start to a R/W deck, while Stijn has some good R/G cards. In the middle matchup, van de Logt has a good start to a W/b deck, while Peset took some questionable picks and is looking like a disaster.

The second round of boosters started with Stijn picking an [Electrostatic Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrostatic+Bolt). Unfortunately, this left a [Leonin Sun Standard](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Sun+Standard) for his opponent. Degrou got a [Viridian Longbow](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viridian+Longbow), and Peset took a [Consume Spirit](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Consume+Spirit). This left a huge wheel for Jesse with [Myr Enforcer](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Enforcer) and [Wizard Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wizard+Replica), both amazing cards in the affinity deck. Van de Logt got a talisman and a [Nim Shambler](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nim+Shambler). The French clearly had no affinity player, and suffered because of it. Peset could possibly have taken the Enforcer over the drain, but it's unclear how good it would be in his deck.

Degrou opened the next booster, taking [Vulshok Battlegear](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulshok+Battlegear) over [Vulshok Berserker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulshok+Berserker), probably a mistake, as the Battlegear is usually a bit slow. Peset took a [Moriok Scavenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moriok+Scavenger), and Lehoux got a [Blinding Beam](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinding+Beam). Jesse took [Neurok Spy](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Neurok+Spy), and Tom [Soldier Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soldier+Replica). Stijn then got a decent wheel in [Predator's Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Predator%27s+Strike) and [Trolls of Tel-Jilad](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trolls+of+Tel-Jilad). Tom then hated [Ogre Leadfoot](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ogre+Leadfoot), and Jesse hated [Vulshok Berserker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulshok+Berserker), leaving a Spellbomb for Lehoux. This was probably a mistake, as the Leadfoot isn't all that good, and spellbombs are always a wanted commodity in the affinity deck. Instead, Tom could have hated the Berserker and left the Spellbomb for Jesse. However, the French are getting into trouble here, having no blue mage to pick up the [Neurok Spy](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Neurok+Spy). Perhaps Peset should have moved into B/U affinity earlier, or maybe Lehoux should have abandoned his red in favor of blue.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpboc04/sf1_3.jpg)

The third booster was a disaster for Peset, only yielding him a red Myr. Again, he could have taken a [Somber Hoverguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Somber+Hoverguard) if he were blue. Lehoux got a much-needed [Bonesplitter](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bonesplitter) for his white deck, and the flier went to Jesse. Tom, took an [Ancient Den](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Den), and Stijn took a [Deconstruct](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deconstruct). Degrou got a nice wheel of [Rustmouth Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rustmouth+Ogre) and [Slith Firewalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slith+Firewalker). Stijn got a Forger on the way back, and Jesse got a nice pick in Seat Of The Synod for his affinity deck.

Lehoux was next to open, finally getting something worthwhile in a Grab The Reins. The game-winning instant was picked with little deliberation, while Jesse got a [Silver Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silver+Myr) and Tom got a [Mask of Memory](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mask+of+Memory). Stijn got another [Electrostatic Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrostatic+Bolt). On the wheel, Degrou got [Pyrite Spellbomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyrite+Spellbomb) and another [Rustmouth Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rustmouth+Ogre), the third in the draft. Peset wheeled [Nim Lasher](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nim+Lasher) and [Alpha Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Alpha+Myr), staying in mono-black. 

Jesse's booster contained the powerful [Lodestone Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lodestone+Myr), adding more insanity to his affinity deck. Perhaps he can use the same deck for his Nationals this year ? Tom got a [Terror](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terror), and Stijn a [Tel-Jilad Archers](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tel-Jilad+Archers). Degrou wheeled [Pyrite Spellbomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyrite+Spellbomb) and [Altar's Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Altar%27s+Light), Peset got [Goblin War Wagon](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+War+Wagon) and a Talisman, and Lehoux got [Vulshok Gauntlets](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulshok+Gauntlets) and [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite). 

The final *Mirrodin* booster was opened by van de Logt, who took a [Culling Scales](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Culling+Scales). Stijn then took a [Tel-Jilad Archers](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tel-Jilad+Archers), leaving [Crystal Shard](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crystal+Shard) and [Somber Hoverguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Somber+Hoverguard) in the pack. The Dutch had probably realized that there were no blue drafters on the other team. Degrou took another [Pyrite Spellbomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyrite+Spellbomb), and Peset had to take the [Crystal Shard](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crystal+Shard). Lehoux took the generally poor [Soul Nova](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soul+Nova), leaving [Somber Hoverguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Somber+Hoverguard) to Jesse. 

After the second set of boosters, the French were hurting even more. The boosters were full of good blue cards, and they didn't have a blue drafter. This made Jesse's pile absolutely ridiculous, although Degrou had some good cards in the matchup, like multiple [Tel-Jilad Archers](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tel-Jilad+Archers) and [Rustmouth Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rustmouth+Ogre)s. Peset really wanted to draft mono-black it seems, as he has a couple of [Consume Spirit](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Consume+Spirit)s and the early Nighmare Lash. Tom has what's shaping up to be a powerful B/W deck, but is perhaps a bit shy on cards. Stijn's deck is really good as well, and Lehoux has really nothing that can deal with Stijn's fat creatures, as he had no good fliers at this point. 

The Darksteel packs needed to be nice to the French for them to have any chance at this point. Tom opened the first pack, and the French team frowned when seeing Sword Of Light And Shadow and [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp) in the pack. The sword went to Tom, and Jesse got the clamp, a nice card in the affinity deck as most type 2 players know by now. Lehoux had to contend himself with Drill Skimmer, while Peset finally got something good in [Murderous Spoils](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Murderous+Spoils), a possible bomb against Tom's white equipment deck. Degrou took [Unforge](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unforge) over a [Karstoderm](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karstoderm) to be able to deal with the clamp. The [Karstoderm](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karstoderm) went to Stijn, a fine card in his matchup. He also hated an [Essence Drain](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Essence+Drain). Lehoux finally hated a [Spire Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spire+Golem) that noone on his team could play.

The second booster gave Jesse another [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp), putting some oomph in his otherwise mediocre deck (!). Lehoux got a [Razor Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Razor+Golem), and Peset had to take an [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker) as there were no black cards of note in the pack. Degrou got [Specter's Shroud](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Specter%27s+Shroud) and Stijn got another [Karstoderm](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karstoderm). Van de Logt wheeled [Loxodon Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Mystic) and [Drill-Skimmer](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drill-Skimmer), and Stijn hated a [Neurok Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Neurok+Prodigy).

Lehoux booster had no good white cards, and after lots of waving of arms and facial gestures, he randomly took a [Whispersilk Cloak](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whispersilk+Cloak). Peset couldn't have been happy to have to pick another [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker). The Darksteel boosters seem to have forget their supposed to be good in black. Degrou got a bomb for his matchup, a [Furnace Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Furnace+Dragon) that should be devastating against Jesse. All in all, the Dutch had a much better draft than the French. All of the matchups are seemingly in their favor. Jesse's amazing affinity deck is facing off against the artifact-hating R/G deck in Degrou's hands. This should be a slaughter, although Degrou does have some good cards for the matchup - his [Tel-Jilad Archers](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tel-Jilad+Archers) might be able to stall the game to a point where his [Rustmouth Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rustmouth+Ogre)s can become relevant. He also has plenty of removal for Jesse's [Somber Hoverguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Somber+Hoverguard)s. In the end though, he will probably have to mise with [Furnace Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Furnace+Dragon) to win. Alexandre Peset seemingly has no deck, with some small, fast Arcbound beaters and the very powerful [Nightmare Lash](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightmare+Lash). Tom has a good black/white deck with double [Leonin Battlemage](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Battlemage) and [Sword of Light and Shadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+Light+and+Shadow). The final matchup is pretty lopsided, with Stijn's solid R/G deck facing off against Lehoux's red/white deck that never really came together. Lehoux has very few ways to deal with the many fat creatures in Stijn's deck and should crumble quickly. Prediction: Shietkoe wins 2-1 or 3-0.
