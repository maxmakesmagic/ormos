### Changes To The ***Magic** Online* Page

* * *

As part of the [**magicthegathering.com**](https://web.archive.org/web/20080725102347/http://wizards.com/magic) redesign, the ***Magic** Online* home page is becoming part of the main **Magic** site. Soon you'll be seeing changes to this page as the redesign reaches it's content.

In the meantime, you can find my weekly column, "[Inside ***Magic** Online*](https://web.archive.org/web/20041031134824/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/columnarchive&column=InsideMagicOnline)", along with Nate Heiss' "[Building On A Budget](https://web.archive.org/web/20041230082941/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/columnarchive&column=BuildingonaBudget)" column on the [**magicthegathering.com**](https://web.archive.org/web/20080725102347/http://wizards.com/magic) site.

Thanks to everyone for your interest in this website. Producing it for the past year-and-a-half has been a great experience, and I hope you like the redesign as the ***Magic** Online* page gets folded into **magicthegathering.com**.

Sincerely,  
 Daniel Myers
