"Cycle [Deadshot Minotaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20140618000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deadshot+Minotaur). Cycle [Street Wraith](https://web.archive.org/web/20140618000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Street+Wraith)."

You settle in for a game against [Living End](https://web.archive.org/web/20140618000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+End). You prepare to put your own creatures in the graveyard to make the [Living End](https://web.archive.org/web/20140618000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+End) not so one-sided. You try not to overcommit creatures to the battlefield.

"Cast [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140618000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon)."

Wait, what? You didn't expect this. [Living End](https://web.archive.org/web/20140618000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+End) doesn't PLAY [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140618000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon)! Oh well, this basic land will help you cast some spells, and hopefully they will be enough.

"[Avalanche Riders](https://web.archive.org/web/20140618000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avalanche+Riders), target your basic land"

You've been had.

Modern is a format where the unexpected can be a potent advantage. Putting [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140618000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) into a place where it is not expected is a perfect example of catching opponents by surprise. Land cyclers such as [Pale Recluse](https://web.archive.org/web/20140618000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pale+Recluse) and [Twisted Abomination](https://web.archive.org/web/20140618000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Twisted+Abomination) can help get the Forest and a Swamp so the pilot can operate under [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140618000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon).
