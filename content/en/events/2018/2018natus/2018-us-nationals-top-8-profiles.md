Congratulations to the Top 8 players from US Nationals 2018!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/2018natus/natgroup.jpg)

* * *

**Name:** Kevin Jones

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/2018natus/t8_jones.jpg)

**Age:** 30

**Hometown:** Kingston, NY

Occupation: content producer @ The Bearded Dragon Games

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

GP Montreal Champion 2017

US Nationals Team 2016

2 SCG wins!

PT Dominaria Top 17!

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Jeskai Control - good against UB and Red Decks. Ryoichi Tamada is the best.

**What was the best card in your main deck and why?**

[Glimmer of Genius](https://web.archive.org/web/20180701000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glimmer+of+Genius) - helps hit land drops

[Teferi, Hero of Dominaria](https://web.archive.org/web/20180701000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%2C+Hero+of+Dominaria) - does everything

**What card from Magic 2019 are you most excited for?**

Nicol Bolas!

**What would winning US Nationals mean to you?**

It would be fulfilling one of my Magic career goals!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/2018natus/t8_andrus.jpg)

**Name:** Justin Andrus

**Age:** 29

**Hometown:** St. Louis

Occupation: Financial analyst

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

two for two on GPs for Day 2

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Esper Control. I enjoy value decks with toolbox styles. There's a lot of cheap card filter.

**What was the best card in your main deck and why?**

[Yahenni's Expertise](https://web.archive.org/web/20180701000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yahenni%27s+Expertise). 4 mana board wipe combos with [Doomfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20180701000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doomfall) for a strong tempo play

**What card from Magic 2019 are you most excited for?**

Bolas. Value town.

**What would winning US Nationals mean to you?**

I'd love to win with my brew. I've been brewing for years and this is my best deck.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/2018natus/t8_brown.jpg)

**Name:** Dylan Brown

**Age:** 25

**Hometown:** Port Huron, MI

Occupation: Server

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Small time stuff. This is my best finish so far.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Blue-Black Midrange. Casting Scarab God was the place to be this weekend.

**What was the best card in your main deck and why?**

Scarab God for obvious reasons

**What card from Magic 2019 are you most excited for?**

Nicol Bolas the Ravager

I've been chasing the big stage for a long time. It would be fantastic to represent the US at the World Cup.

**Hometown:** Indianapolis, IN

Occupation: Student

WU Gift, Jack Kiefer told me to.

Goblin Trashmaster, the art and name are funny

It would be an honor to represent my country, but my studies come first.
