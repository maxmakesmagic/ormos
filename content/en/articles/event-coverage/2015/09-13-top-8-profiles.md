* * *

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpokc15/t8_jasper.jpg)**  
 Jasper Johnson-Epstein

**Age:** 25

**Hometown:** Madison, WI

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Two Pro Tour Top 50 appearances, five Pro Tour appearances, too many Grand Prix close calls.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?** Burn. It has served me well for many years, so I saw no reason to give it up.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer). My very favorite *Magic* card, it provided some very useful angles of attack by mowing down opposing creatures and players indiscriminately and despite blockers.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Electrickery](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrickery). It’s the best creature control you can find against Affinity, Bogles and Infect.

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?** [Footsteps of the Goryo](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Footsteps+of+the+Goryo)/[Protean Hulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Protean+Hulk) combo. A blast from the blast.

**Are you and Ally against the Eldrazi, or resigned to be consumed by them?** Who am I to stand against Emrakul?

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpokc15/t8_reiter.jpg)**  
 Joseph Reiter

**Age:** 23

**Hometown:** Omaha, NE

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** 42nd at Grand Prix Omaha

**What did you play this weekend, and why?** Affinity. I couldn’t build a Grixis Control or Grixis Twin list that I liked, so I went to Affinity as a fallback.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating). There’s something amazing about top decking five or more points of damage when you need it.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Ghirapur Æther Grid](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghirapur+%C3%86ther+Grid). The card was amazing in creature matchups and really sealed the deal for some of my games.

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?** A Naya Zoo deck similar to the Naya Blitz back in *Return to Ravnica-Innistrad* Standard. I haven’t seen [Burning-Tree Emissary](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning-Tree+Emissary) or [Ghor-Clan Rampager](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghor-Clan+Rampager) in a while.

**Are you and Ally against the Eldrazi, or resigned to be consumed by them?** Against the Eldrazi. Maybe I ignite my spark!

* * *

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpokc15/t8_duin.jpg)**  
 Brian Braun-Duin

**Age:** 29

**Hometown:** Roanoke, VA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Won Grand Prix New Jersey and Louisville.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?** Blue-Red Twin. Was lost in Modern and Sam Pardee said it was great so I blindly followed him.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage). Easily one of the top five cards in Modern, maybe the best.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm). It killed a lot of 1/1 spirits and other assorted small things.

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?** Elves. It’s not too weird but most of my matches were against known quantities.

**Are you and Ally against the Eldrazi, or resigned to be consumed by them?** Resigned to be consumed.

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpokc15/t8_rietzl.jpg)**  
 Paul Rietzl

**Age:** 30

**Hometown:** Scottsdale, AZ

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Pro Tour Hall of Fame member; four Pro Tour Top 8s and 13 Grand Prix Top 8s.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?** Merfolk. I haven’t dusted off Revised Islands in a while.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial)! It’s very hard to play against.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Spell Pierce](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Pierce). It’s to be able to interact with my linear aggro deck.

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?** Elves!

**Are you and Ally against the Eldrazi, or resigned to be consumed by them?** Truly resigned.

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpokc15/t8_sullano.jpg)**  
 Andrew Sullano

**Age:** 23

**Hometown:** Dallas, TX

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Day 2 at Pro Tour *Return to Ravnica* and *Avacyn Restored.* I also rode in the same car as Zac Elsik.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?** Elves, because I own foil [Heritage Druid](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heritage+Druid)s.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Heritage Druid](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heritage+Druid), because I got to play my entire hand.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Chameleon Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chameleon+Colossus). It’s got protection from black and is uncounterable.

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?** Merfolk, because [Hibernation](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hibernation) is a thing.

**Are you and Ally against the Eldrazi, or resigned to be consumed by them?** Ally.

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpokc15/t8_elsik.jpg)**  
 Zac Elsik

**Age:** 28

**Hometown:** Dallas, TX

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** 17th at Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored*

**What did you play this weekend, and why?** Lantern Control because it has the highest win-rate against all non-Burn Modern decks.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Lantern of Insight](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lantern+of+Insight). It removes variance from my opponent’s and my draws.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Welding Jar](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Welding+Jar). It’s a one-for-one answer to sideboard cards from my opponent.

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?** Gerry Thompson’s Grixis Jace value deck with [Rise & Fall](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rise+%2F%2F+Fall).

**Are you and Ally against the Eldrazi, or resigned to be consumed by them?** Ally.

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpokc15/t8_duggan.jpg)**  
 Matthew Duggan

**Age:** 28

**Hometown:** Albuquerque, NM

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Two State Championships.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?** [Scapeshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scapeshift), because it’s a one-card combo.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Repeal](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Repeal) and/or [Scapeshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scapeshift).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Dispel](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dispel). It’s great against all kinds of different decks.

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?** Nothing special.

**Are you and Ally against the Eldrazi, or resigned to be consumed by them?** Well, I win games with a *Zendikar* land ([Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut%2C+the+Molten+Pinnacle)) so…. kill all the Eldrazi.

### **![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpokc15/t8_miller.jpg)**  
 Benjamin Thomas Miller

**Age:** 18

**Hometown**: Ankeny, IA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** I’ve won Friday Night *Magic,* and went 0-3 at Grand Prix Omaha.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?** Modern, because it’s my favorite format.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor), because it enables combo kills with [Summer Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summer+Bloom) and [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Leyline of Sanctity](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leyline+of+Sanctity) to prevent discard and [Liliana of the Veil](https://web.archive.org/web/20150913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+of+the+Veil).

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?** I played against Jund six times.

**Are you and Ally against the Eldrazi, or resigned to be consumed by them?** Eldrazi are awesome. You have to love their power.
