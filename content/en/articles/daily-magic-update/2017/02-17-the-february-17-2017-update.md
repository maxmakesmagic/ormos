The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on February 17, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by [Positive Feedback for *Magic*](https://www.sylvanmtg.com/2017/02/16/positive-feedback-for-magic/). (Thanks, Ryan D. at SylvanMTG!)

# What to Watch This Weekend

1.  [Grand Prix Vancouver](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-vancouver-2017) | Saturday and Sunday | [twitch.tv/magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic)

The Grand Prix circuit turns to Modern, just across the border with our Canadian friends in Vancouver. Coverage starts at noon ET/9 a.m. PT. This is the first high-level play for Modern since [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe) and [Golgari Grave-Troll](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golgari+Grave-Troll) were banned, not to mention the influx of powerful *Aether Revolt* cards, making for an interesting, potentially surprising weekend.

1.  [StarCityGames.com Baltimore Open Weekend](http://www.starcitygames.com/events/180217_baltimore_fact_sheet.html) | Saturday and Sunday | [twitch.tv/scgtour](https://www.twitch.tv/scgtour)

It's three, *three*, formats for the price of one as the SCG Tour hosts a Team Constructed weekend in Baltimore that features one teammate playing Standard, one on Modern, and one on Legacy. Coverage begins at 10:30 a.m. ET/7:30 a.m. PT each day.

1.  > Today at 2:30pm EST [@AndrewCuneo](https://twitter.com/AndrewCuneo) and [@HueyJensen](https://twitter.com/HueyJensen) will be discussing the state of standard and doing some drafting! [https://t.co/HGthUrDpIs](https://t.co/HGthUrDpIs)
    >
    > — PantheonMagic (@PantheonMagic) [February 17, 2017](https://twitter.com/PantheonMagic/status/832608828961148930)

This is more of a what to watch today. Pantheon is amping up their stream game as of late, and this seems like the perfect way to get through a Friday afternoon and on to the weekend (where, of course, you can watch more *Magic* streaming).

# After Those, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [Breaking Kari Zev's Expertise](http://www.toplevelpodcast.com/kari-zevs-expertise/) | Top Level Podcast | Mike Flores and Patrick Chapin

With all the Modern action happening this weekend, now's a good time to talk about some of the Modern-ready cards *Aether Revolt* has bestowed upon us—particularly the [Expertise cycle](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&set=+%5b%22Aether%20Revolt%22%5d&name=+%5b%22Expertise%22%5d), particularly particularly [Kari Zev's Expertise](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kari+Zev%27s+Expertise) (aka, the cheapest one) and [Breaking // Entering](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Breaking+%2F%2F+Entering), which, yes, can be cast fused off of an Expertise. If you didn't already know that, take a moment to pick your jaw up off the floor, then listen to this podcast.

1.  [Channel Pardee Time—Modern Blue-Black Control](http://www.channelfireball.com/videos/channel-pardee-time-modern-ub-control/) | ChannelFireball | Sam Pardee

Speaking of [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push) and Expertises, Sam Pardee pilots a blue-black control deck that seeks to use [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push) and [Yahenni's Expertise](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yahenni%27s+Expertise) to leverage an advantage over his opponents. See how these *Aether Revolt* cards fare in a tougher, more Modern environment.

1.  [Top 5 *Amonkhet* Predictions for *Magic: The Gathering*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CpsG_4ZdR4Q) | Tolarian Community College | The Professor

Previews are still a ways away for *Amonkhet* (the start in earnest April 3, but we'll have a couple things to show off the week prior), but that doesn't stop people from speculating now that we're through the entire *Aether Revolt* story. Here, the Professor offers five predictions for what happens in *Amonkhet*. And, no, I won't say how many he got right or wrong.

1.  [Art in Focus—Sundering Titan *Masterpiece* by Lius Lasahido](https://www.originalmagicart.store/blogs/oma-blog/art-in-focus-sundering-titan-masterpiece-by-lius-lasahido) | Original Magic Art | Josh Krause and Lius Lasahido

The [Sundering Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sundering+Titan) *Masterpiece* is imposing, terrifying, and one of my favorite pieces of art in recent years. Now Lasahido gives a peek behind his thoughts and his process when creating this terrifying mechanical monster.

1.  [Unique hobby helps student pay for school, see the world](http://www.theadvertiser.com/story/news/local/education/2017/02/16/magic-the-gathering-donald-smith-jr/97850848/) | The Advertiser | Leigh Guidry

I love local papers. I've written for and been an editor at a number of them. There's something pure and refreshing about how personal the news gets. # Deck of the Day

1.  [Modern Snake Scales](/en/events/2017/competitive-modern-constructed-league-2017-02-16/competitive-modern-constructed-league)

While we've got several Modern events this weekend ([Grand Prix Brisbane](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-brisbane-2017) is also Modern, it's just not streamed), so it seems like a good time to check in on the format. And this 5-0 *Magic Online* League deck from Themata is certainly something to behold as a completely new creation spurred on by everyone's favorite Standard-legal Snake, [Winding Constrictor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Winding+Constrictor). The deck piles on the +1/+1 counters thanks to both [Hardened Scales](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hardened+Scales) and [Winding Constrictor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Winding+Constrictor) tossing them in for free.

Check out this scenario. You have a [Hardened Scales](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hardened+Scales) or [Winding Constrictor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Winding+Constrictor) in play and you cast [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker). It comes into play as a 2/2. You then cast [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager), also as a 2/2. If you then sacrifice the Worker to the Ravager, the Ravager gets two +1/+1 counters from its ability and then three from the modular trigger, resulting in a 7/7 [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager). And that's just with one [Hardened Scales](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hardened+Scales) in play. Throw in [Walking Ballista](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Walking+Ballista) and [Hangarback Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hangarback+Walker), and things get out of control quickly.

And heaven help your opponent if you ever get to activate [Gavony Township](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gavony+Township).

The rest of the deck is filled out with more +1/+1 counter generators that do other things as well. [Scavenging Ooze](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scavenging+Ooze) patrols graveyards and [Dromoka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dromoka%27s+Command) serves as removal. Speaking of, we also have *Aether Revolt*'s best card for Modern (sorry, Snake) in [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20170217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push), which is reshaping the format in about 20 different ways.
