## Wizards Account FAQ

### **What is a Wizards Account? How do I get one?**

A Wizards Account is an account system that simplifies your access to many of the Wizards of the Coast websites and services. A Wizards Account gives you the ability to get your DCI number online, track your Planeswalker Points, contact customer support, and participate in the community forums. You can sign up for a Wizards Account at [accounts.wizards.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20160613041531/https://accounts.wizards.com/).

 

### **Why should I get a Wizards Account?**

The Wizards Account makes it easy for you to access our services and manage your account with a single login.

### **Why are we making this change?**

We're always looking for ways to improve our customer service and support. Before this change, each Wizards service, like Planeswalker Points or community forums, required a separate account with a separate login ID and password. Over time, this became [burdensome for many customers](https://web.archive.org/web/20160613041531/http://magic.wizards.com/node/290601).

### **I already have other existing Wizards of the Coast accounts, like multiple DCI numbers. What should I do?**

When you first login with your existing account, you'll be prompted to migrate that account to the Wizards Account system. This process should take less than a minute. Once your account is migrated, you have the option to combine more accounts (for example, if you have multiple DCI numbers) from your account information page at [accounts.wizards.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20160613041531/https://accounts.wizards.com/).

### **What is the difference between my Wizards Account and my DCI number?**

Your Wizards Account provides access to Wizards of the Coast sites, your DCI number, management of your personal information, and more. Your DCI number is your personal identification number at events like Friday Night Magic and D&D Encounters. For Magic: The Gathering events, the DCI number is also used to track your wins on [Planeswalker Points](https://web.archive.org/web/20160613223741/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/PlaneswalkerPoints).

### **What Wizards of the Coast services use the Wizards Account?**

You must use your Wizards Account to access the following services:

*   DCI number and associated information
*   Planeswalker Points
*   Community forums
*   Help site

 

### **What Wizards of the Coast services DO NOT use the Wizards Account?**

The following services do not use the Wizards Account. You should continue to use your existing login information to access them:

*   Magic Online
*   Judge Center
*   Wizards Event Reporter (WER)

 

### **How is D&D Insider affected by these changes?**

D&D Insider is handled a little differently than our other products. If you have an existing D&D Insider account, you can continue to log in with your current credentials (your email address and password). If you have a Wizards Account, or if you have migrated your existing D&D Insider account to a Wizards Account, you can access D&D Insider using your new login credentials (login ID and password).

### **Is Magic Online affected by these changes?**

Not at this time. Use your existing login information.

### **Can I see my current Planeswalker Points and standings?**

Yes, you will still be able to view your standings and play history on the [Planeswalker Points](https://web.archive.org/web/20160613223741/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/PlaneswalkerPoints).

### **Will I lose my Planeswalker Points if I combine my accounts?**

No. You will not lose Planeswalker Points, earned achievements, or play history from any combined accounts.

### **Why does my progress to the next Planeswalker Points achievement look incorrect after merging?**

Unfortunately, the Planeswalker Points system doesn't allow for matches won in other accounts to be applied toward unearned achievements in the primary account. Example: your primary account has 50 matches won and your secondary account has 25 matches won. After merging, you will see 75 matches won but only 50 of these would count toward the Artful Tactician achievement (win 100 Magic event matches).

### **Will Magic Online, WER, or Judges Center be included as part of my Wizards Account in the future? If so, when?**'

Wizards of the Coast works hard to create the best possible experience for all of our customers without disrupting the quality of the experience they have today. We're currently evaluating options for adding other services but, do not have additional information at this time.

### **I have multiple DCI accounts or I activated a DCI number given to me at an event. Can I merge these separate DCI accounts into one account?**

Yes. Once you create a Wizards Account, you have the option to merge other DCI accounts into it. This includes older DCI accounts you may have activated in the past, as well as new DCI numbers you may have received at a recent event. Login to [accounts.wizards.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20160613041531/https://accounts.wizards.com/) and click "Combine Accounts". The process takes less than a minute to complete.

### **I have one of the older paper DCI cards that I have not yet activated. Can I use this?**

Yes. Go to [http://dci.wizards.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20160613041531/http://dci.wizards.com/) and enter the DCI number and activation code from your DCI card.

### **How do I customize my DCI card?**

Log in to [accounts.wizards.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20160613041531/https://accounts.wizards.com/) and then click "Customize."

  
**How will Wizards use my information?**

Wizards of the Coast does not share your information with outside vendors. For a full explanation of our privacy policy statement, [check here](https://web.archive.org/web/20160613041531/http://company.wizards.com/policies/web/privacy).

  
**Will your websites and forums allow anonymous posting?**

No. Wizards of the Coast websites will only allow posting by registered users.

  
**Can I connect my player username on *Duels of the Planeswalkers* platforms to my Wizards Account?** 

No, not at this time.

  
**Can I log in using my Facebook or Twitter account?**

No. We currently only support logging in with your Wizards Account login ID and password.

  
**Who do I contact if I need help with my Wizards Account or with other questions?**

For further assistance, please contact Wizards of the Coast game support via our online [Help System](http://wizards.custhelp.com/). Alternatively, if unable to access or email us through the Help System, please visit our [Contact Us](https://web.archive.org/web/20160613041531/http://company.wizards.com/contact/customer-support) page for information on how to reach game support by phone.
