Hey! How about some more *Morningtide* posters?

As you remember from [yesterday](https://web.archive.org/web/20080108000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/603031), the tribes in *Morningtide* are more class-based than race-based. And do you know what makes a neat class? Soldiers!
