Looking for a fun and exciting way to beat Jund at your upcoming Friday Night **Magic**? Pro Tour superstar Luis Scott-Vargas has exactly what you're looking for! He dispatched more than a few copies of the black-red-green deck on his way to the top slot at a recent [StarCityGames.com Open tournament](http://www.starcitygames.com/php/news/article/18542.html) in Los Angeles. His strategy was to control the game with removal and counters, wipe the board with Earthquake, and beat opponents up with powerful planeswalkers Ajani Vengeant and Jace Beleren. And to make sure he never ran out of cards? How about massive Mind Springs! Make sure to head to your Wizards Play Network location for [Friday Night **Magic**](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6), where you can earn a premium Tidehollow Sculler throughout January.
