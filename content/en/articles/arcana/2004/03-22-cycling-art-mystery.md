Scott M. Fischer illustrated a cycle of cycling cards (that's right, a group of related cards, each of which bears the cycling ability) that all featured a similar woman in the art.

  

Do you know on what cards this art appears? *(Give up? Scroll down to see the answer.)*

![Cycling art mystery](https://i.ormos.uk/a8/dc/a8dc4a086cec44c438c9cee5153c258d3bae8ca65c1dfb7c11edf64e106c7267.jpg)

  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  

Answer: They're the cycling of Runes of Protection from *Urza's Saga* (see below).

Note that **Rune of Protection: Artifacts** and **Rune of Protection: Lands** were also illustrated by Scott M. Fischer, and may also feature this same woman in their art! (Well, it's debatable whether those hands on ROP: Lands belong to the same woman. But hey, let's call it a 7-card mono-white art cycle, pending any countermanding evidence. Scott, if you're reading, write in and let us know the mystery!)
