Katsuhiro Mori wrapped up his <a href="/en/events/2005/worlds05/final-convoluted-chaos-crowns-champion">Finals match</a> against Frank Karsten at Worlds 2005 by playing an end-of-turn <a>Congregation at Dawn</a> for three punishing copies of <a>Yosei, the Morning Star</a>. Makihito Morita's deck at Worlds 2006 won by playing <a>Lotus Bloom</a>, <a>Seething Song</a>, and <a>Rite of Flame</a> in some combination to power out a backbreaking <a>Dragonstorm</a>, fetching enough <a>Bogardan Hellkite</a>s and <a>Hunted Dragon</a>s to end the game. It was <a>Gigadrowse</a>, however, that won him his <a href="/en/events/2006/worlds06/finals-mihara-rides-storm-to-victory">Finals match</a> against Ryo Ogura through a <a>Circle of Protection: Red</a>.
