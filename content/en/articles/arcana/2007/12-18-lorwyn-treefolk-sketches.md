* * *

In honor of Treefolk Week, today we take a look at the sketch stages of the art of a few Treefolk cards. *(Mouse over each sketch to see its final art.)*

We start with the honorable Doran, a demonstration of how the Treefolk creatures in *Lorwyn* are unusual in that they span across three colors instead of just two (or five). Doran was commissioned for booster packaging (seen at the end of [this Arcana](/en/articles/arcana/2007/08-14-lorwyn-booster-packaging))—he is definitely a face of the set.

[![](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/1484_TreefolkSketch_Doran.jpg)](#image)  
*Doran, the Siege Tower sketch and art by Mark Zug*

After seeing William O'Connor's first sketch of Thorntooth Witch, the creative team wanted the treefolk witch to look a little more sinister while hovering over her witch's brew. The final art satisfied that request.

[![](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/1484_TreefolkSketch_Thorntooth.jpg)](#image)  
*Thorntooth Witch sketch and art by William O'Connor*

Oakgnarl Warrior was going to be called Oakgnarl Champion, until the champion mechanic got its keyword. Jim Nelson's straight-and-tall treefolk got broader and more irregular at final at the creative team's suggestion.

[![](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/1484_TreefolkSketch_Oakgnarl.jpg)](#image)  
*Oakgnarl Warrior sketch and art by Jim Nelson*

Jim Murray's Oaken Brawler is full of activity. It's not just the squirrels scampering around the treefolk's trunk in the foreground, but also the kithkin looking on to see who will win in the clash of tree vs. giant.

[![](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/1484_TreefolkSketch_Brawler.jpg)](#image)  
*Oaken Brawler sketch and art by Jim Murray*
