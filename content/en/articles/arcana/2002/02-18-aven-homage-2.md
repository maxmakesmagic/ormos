Sometimes cards are reprinted without being actual "reprints;" the new cards have the same functionality as the old ones, but different names. (Meaning you could use four of each in a deck.)

Two of *Odyssey*'s Aven are just such cards. Aven Cloudchaser and Aven Fisher are descended from *Tempest*'s Cloudchaser Eagle and *Urza's Destiny*'s Kingfisher, respectively. Both new cards pay homage to the old ones not only in name, but also subtly in their art (the speared fish being the most obvious link).
