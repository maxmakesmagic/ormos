### Xbox LIVE® Arcade – Game with Developers – June 27, 2009

Team members from Stainless Games, Ltd., Wizards of the Coast, Microsoft, and other **Magic** developers behind the new [**Magic: The Gathering Duels of the Planeswalkers**](https://web.archive.org/web/20140409211056/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Digital/DuelsOfThePlaneswalkers.aspx) will play Xbox LIVE® Gold Members on Saturday, June 27. 

Join us [Saturday, June 27 from 2–5 p.m. ET](http://www.xbox.com/en-US/community/calendar/) for a classic gaming session. Plus, this is your chance to get the XBLA **Spellslinger Achievement** directly from the source by defeating **Duels of the Planeswalkers** developers!
