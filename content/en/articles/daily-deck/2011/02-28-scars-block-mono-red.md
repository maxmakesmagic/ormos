*Scars of Mirrodin* Block Constructed tournaments are now available on [**Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20110925172955/http://www.wizards.com/magic/Digital/MagicOnline.aspx?)! Watchwolf recently took this build to the top of the standings in one Premier Event, and check out the key combo: Liquimetal Coating and Hoard-Smelter Dragon. With those two online you can turn an opponent's permanent into an artifact, then munch it with your monster for a bonus. Cool!
