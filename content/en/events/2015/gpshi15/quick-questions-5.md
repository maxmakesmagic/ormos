# ***Which Khans of Tarkir card has changed the most in your evaluation for Limited?***

By now, the set has been out for several months, and after getting more experience with the set, card evaluations have changed. I was curious to learn what card has changed the most in people’s evaluation.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/QQ-Martin-Juza2.jpg)  
**Platinum pro Martin Jůza:** The pump spells, like [Awaken the Bear](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Awaken+the+Bear) and [Dragonscale Boon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonscale+Boon), are much better than people give them credit for. But [Bring Low](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bring+Low) has gone down in my evaluation because it doesn’t kill the big creatures you want to kill.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/QQ-Kenji-Tsumura2.jpg)  
**Hall of Famer Kenji Tsumura:** The common dual lands. I used to think that you could draft two-color decks and that they wouldn’t be necessary, but it’s tough to get a two-color deck.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/QQ-Ivan-Floch.jpg)  
**No. 2 Ivan Floch:** [Sultai Scavenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sultai+Scavenger) and [Mardu Warshrieker](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Warshrieker) are both way better than I initially thought. Scavenger is the best common delve card; you can build decks around it. Warshrieker is an insane [Black Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Lotus).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/QQ-Christian-Calcano.jpg)  
**Gold pro Christian Calcano:** I used to think [Debilitating Injury](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Debilitating+Injury) was insane, but it often falls short in the late game. [Disowned Ancestor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disowned+Ancestor), on the other hand, is a card that I’ve grown to like more and more. When my opponents play it on turn one, I’m not happy.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/QQ-Lee-Shi-Tian.jpg)  
**No. 9 Lee Shi Tian:** [Tormenting Voice](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tormenting+Voice) is much better than I had expected. You want to play 18 lands in this format, and [Tormenting Voice](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tormenting+Voice) mitigates mana flood while providing enough gas for the game.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/QQ-Yuuki-Ichikawa.jpg)  
**No. 23 Yuuki Ichikawa:** [Monastery Flock](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Flock) because the draft metagame has changed over time. In the beginning, W/B Warriors was best, then R/G Ferocious, and now I like defensive decks. [Monastery Flock](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Flock) is great in a control-style draw-first deck with 18 lands.
