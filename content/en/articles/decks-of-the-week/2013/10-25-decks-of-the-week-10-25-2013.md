Pro Tour Hall of Famer Shuhei Nakamura defeated fellow world traveler and [Team ChannelFireball](https://web.archive.org/web/20131025000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=%20http://www.channelfireball.com/) teammate Martin Juza in the finals of [Grand Prix Hong Kong](https://web.archive.org/web/20131025000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/nakamura-masters-hong-kong) last weekend, bringing his GP victory total to six. Meanwhile, across the Pacific, Brian Braun-Duin proved his devotion to black was up to the task by winning [Grand Prix Louisville](https://web.archive.org/web/20131025000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/braun-duin-leader-pack-louisville). Congratulations Shuhei and Brian! Want to play against them at Pro Tour *Born of the Gods* in Valencia, Spain? One way is to win a PTQ! This season's format is *Theros* Limited, just like GP Hong Kong! Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#bog)! Good luck; have fun!
