It’s filled to the brim with brand new *Rise* cards like [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20100422000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Emrakul%252C%2Bthe%2BAeons%2BTorn), [Ulamog, the Infinite Gyre](https://web.archive.org/web/20100422000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Ulamog%252C%2Bthe%2BInfinite%2BGyre), [Pelakka Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20100422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pelakka+Wurm), and [Kozilek's Predator](https://web.archive.org/web/20100422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kozilek%27s+Predator). And you can keep going by using [Momentous Fall](https://web.archive.org/web/20100422000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Momentous+Fall) to draw even more of the powerful new spells. Awesome!
