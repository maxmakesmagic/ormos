Ray Daniel figured out how to train his Dragon in time for a Top 16 finish at a large cash tournament in California. His Dragons of choice? Bogardan Hellkite, Hellkite Charger, Spellbound Dragon, and honorary "Dragon" Sphinx of Jwar Isle. Spreading Seas served to cantrip him into the Dragons while keeping his opponents off their colors. A smattering of counters and burn allowed him to survive long enough to begin dropping Dragon-sized haymakers. Nice work Ray!
