Last weekend, Wizards of the Coast hosted the first [Super Sunday Series Finals](https://web.archive.org/web/20140214000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/owen-smashes-first-ever-super-sunday-series) here in our building in Renton, which featured a variety of Limited formats, plus decks in the new Standard. It also featured another win by familiar victor, Owen Turtenwald. Congratulations, Owen! How much *Born of the Gods* is going to change Standard remains uncertain, but it's something you'll want to watch if you're interested in attending a Pro Tour Qualifier (PTQ), as the current season's format is Standard. Also! Starting today, *Born of the Gods* Prerelease Events are active all weekend on **Magic Online**. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#jou). Good luck; have fun!
