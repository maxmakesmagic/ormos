### *Magic Online* Visits Origins In Columbus

* * *

The first part of the 2003 ***Magic** Online* Midwest Tour has come and gone -- and I'm still recovering from it. However, even after I came down with a cold on the first day of Origins, the convention went well for both ***Magic** Online* and Wizards of the Coast as well.

<table><tbody><tr><td><p>The new Wizards booth is pretty darn cool -- lots of metal, glass, and oversized graphics from all the games. The new setup leaves a lot of open floor space, which is good as the booth was packed all four days from opening to closing. Part of that was due to the Wizard's Quest giveaway we were running, and part was because there was a lot of cool stuff to demo -- from TCGs to board games.</p>&#10;<p><i><b>Magic </b>Online </i>got a nice spot front-and-center in our area, with four computer kiosks for people to play on. (Frankly, we could've used a few more, as there were sometimes three or four people to a screen.) We were also nicely framed by new art on both sides -- <a>Royal Assassin</a> on one and <a>Savannah Lions</a> on the other.</p>&#10;<p>We decided to get all the stress out of the way on the first day, with the computer kiosks overheating before the show opened. Fortunately, between the outside doors finally getting shut and a quick tweak to the temperature settings, everything was up and running by the time everyone was allowed in.</p>&#10;</td>&#10;<td><img alt="Savannah Lions" src="https://i.ormos.uk/76/bf/76bf876e4b4663043af6e0dde74bf5538b626fbe5a2e220274e937a1d0dcbfff.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

A lot of interesting people came by to take a look at ***Magic** Online.* I got a chance to talk with Eric Tam (former Pro Tour player) for a while. And, I hate to admit, one of the guys from [Star City Games](https://web.archive.org/web/20030710000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.starcitygames.com&origin=magic_magiconline_origins2003) came by while I was too busy to talk. (If you happen to read this, I didn't mean to blow you off, I was just trying to survive sickness and demoing at the same time. My apologies for being rude.) Even one of the stormtrooper security guards stopped by for a look.

Yes, the security team was wearing full stormtrooper uniforms. Well, at least the ones not in the death squad commando or biker scout uniforms. What can I say? It's a gaming convention.

<table><tbody><tr><td><img src="https://i.ormos.uk/d0/9b/d09bc685e5c552e5352a6b37521282f281925401e2ed7899bd93ca4c840d74c7.jpg"></td>&#10;<td><p>One of the more amusing parts was watching the transformation that occurred as people started playing <b>Magic</b> games at the kiosks. The change from mild-mannered convention-goer to hardcore player only took moments. It didn't take long for players to start talking about how they got mana screwed or how they were laying down the beats on their opponent.</p>&#10;<p>And, typical of <i><b>Magic </b>Online, </i> age and gender didn't make a difference when it came to being drawn into the game. I taught players who were eight years old and others in their fifties. Moms and dads were learning from their kids and people were bringing over friends to see what was going on. It was like seeing a slice of the community we've already got in <i><b>Magic </b>Online.</i></p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

By the time we closed shop on Sunday, I think I could've done my speech about ***Magic** Online* in my sleep. Come to think of it, I might have once or twice.

<table><tbody><tr><td><p>I'd love to give everyone a sense of the rest of Origins, but I was too busy at the booth to get around much. Over four days, we ran over five hundred people through the demo. There were also a lot of folks who came by one day to do the tutorials, then came back to play a real game on another. It was great to have so many new players interested and to have folks who already had accounts take time to show newbies around. My thanks to everyone who helped out.</p>&#10;<p>I hope everyone who came by had as much fun as I did at Origins this year. If you missed it, don't forget that the tour continues at GenCon in Indianapolis. I'll be out there showing off <i><b>Magic </b>Online </i>again from July 24 through July 27 -- stop by and say hello.</p>&#10;</td>&#10;<td><img src="https://i.ormos.uk/7a/63/7a63b89572670e9c2734b0d8e54983a077da00ca753394d5dd95bf3d7d0f9fac.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>
