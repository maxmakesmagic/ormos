After a long, grinding day of *Khans of Tarkir* Sealed matches, 133 players found themselves on the bubble going into the final round of Day 1, needing one more win to reach the 21-point threshold needed to come back and draft on Sunday.

At Table 80, No. 1 Owen Turtenwald faced down Michael Rothkopf for a spot in Day 2. Turtenwald was quick to the board with a pair of outlasters in [Ainok Bond-Kin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ainok+Bond-Kin) and [Salt Road Patrol](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Salt+Road+Patrol), along with a vigilant flyer in [Alabaster Kirin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Alabaster+Kirin). His opponent fought back with a [Mardu Hordechief](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Hordechief), which brought along its raiding Warrior friend. An attack from Rothkopf triggered raid once again, allowing a [Mardu Heart-Piercer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Heart-Piercer) to shoot down Turtenwald's Bond-Kin.

A [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) from Turtenwald then swung the life totals to 24-14 in his favor, and [Rakshasa's Secret](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa%27s+Secret) took care of the [Jeskai Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Charm) and [Force Away](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+Away) in Rothkopf's hand, leaving him hellbent. Turtenwald's [Smite the Monstrous](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smite+the+Monstrous) smote a [Glacial Stalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glacial+Stalker), and a few attacks later his 3/5 [Salt Road Patrol](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Salt+Road+Patrol) had won him Game 1.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpbal15/round-9-turtenwald.jpg)

 *Owen Turtenwald's Abzan outlast creatures endured an onslaught of raiding Jeskai Warriors to win Game 1.* 

  

Meanwhile, in a bubble match between Brian Braun-Duin and Mitchell Garrant, Braun-Duin's [Dead Drop](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dead+Drop) opened a trapdoor under his opponent's [Rattleclaw Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rattleclaw+Mystic) and [Pine Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pine+Walker), leaving behind an empty board. However, Garrant followed up with a pair of flying threats in [Swarm of Bloodflies](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swarm+of+Bloodflies) and [Sultai Scavenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sultai+Scavenger).

Braun-Duin's [Throttle](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Throttle) took down the Swarm before it could grow out of range, but Garrant was able to follow up with an [Armament Corps](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Armament+Corps), putting two counters on the Scavenger, which took down Game 1 for Garrant.

Back in the Turtenwald-Rothkopf showdown, Turtenwald's [Herald of Anafenza](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Herald+of+Anafenza) and [Ainok Bond-Kin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ainok+Bond-Kin) were in the graveyard, the apparent victims of a timely [Arc Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Lightning). Rothkopf was applying pressure with a [Horde Ambusher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Horde+Ambusher), a [Mardu Heart-Piercer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Heart-Piercer) and an [Ainok Bond-Kin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ainok+Bond-Kin).

The deathtouch [Ruthless Ripper](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ruthless+Ripper) and [Shambling Attendants](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shambling+Attendants) temporarily stabilized the board for Turtenwald, but [Narset, Enlightened Master](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narset%2C+Enlightened+Master) appeared for Rothkopf, threatening to break the uneasy truce. An attack from the legendary Monk flipped a [Bring Low](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bring+Low), allowing Rothkopf to eat away at Owen's board, and Game 2 went to Rothkopf soon after.

Across the event hall, Pro Tour *Khans of Tarkir* semifinalist Mike Sigrist was squaring off in a bubble match against Ben Friedman, who looked to have his back against the wall in Game 3 as he faced down Sigrist's Temur army. Friedman had attempted to outlast the onslaught, with a [Disowned Ancestor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disowned+Ancestor) and an [Ainok Bond-Kin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ainok+Bond-Kin), but an attack from Sigrist's [Rattleclaw Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rattleclaw+Mystic), [Longshot Squad](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Longshot+Squad) and [Savage Knuckleblade](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Knuckleblade) were too much to handle, and Friedman extended his hand, putting Sigrist into Day 2.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpbal15/round-9-bbd.jpg)

 *Mitchell Garrant's (right) [Sorin, Solemn Visitor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorin%2C+Solemn+Visitor) made several appearances, but never managed to stay long enough to defeat Brian Braun-Duin's morph army.* 

  

After winning Game 2 thanks to a timely [Disdainful Stroke](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disdainful+Stroke) to prevent the appearance of his opponent's [Sorin, Solemn Visitor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorin%2C+Solemn+Visitor), Braun-Duin found himself facing down the planeswalking vampire once again in Game 3. Garrant got Sorin to stick this time, but the pressure applied by Braun-Duin's [Abzan Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Guide) meant the planeswalker could manage only to make two tokens before leaving for greener planes. With two morphs facing down Garrant's lone Vampire token, Braun-Duin unmorphed [Thousand Winds](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thousand+Winds), and the flying Elemental won him the match soon after. After Garrant had scooped up his cards, Braun-Duin diligently revealed his final face-down morph, a [Kheru Spellsnatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kheru+Spellsnatcher), just in case.

Over at Table 80, Turtenwald had led off Game 3 with a third-turn [Herald of Anafenza](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Herald+of+Anafenza), but was only able to generate a single Warrior token before an [Arc Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Lightning) from Ropthkopf burned it away. After eliminating Turtenwald's Herald, Rothkopf landed one of his own, to go along with a [War-Name Aspirant](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=War-Name+Aspirant) and multiple raiding [Mardu Hordechief](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Hordechief)s.

A [Murderous Cut](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Murderous+Cut) from Turtenwald returned the favor on the Herald, and an [Ainok Bond-Kin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ainok+Bond-Kin), [Salt Road Patrol](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Salt+Road+Patrol) and [Alabaster Kirin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Alabaster+Kirin) began chipping away at his opponent's forces, and then his life total. A few turns later Turtenwald cast a face-down [Ruthless Ripper](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ruthless+Ripper), only to immediately flip it face up, draining Rothkopf for two, and wining the match.

While all this was going on, Grand Prix Salt Lake City 2014 Top 8 quarterfinalist Jamie Parke was slogging through two long, drawn-out games against Josh Davis. After Parke took Game 1, he was forced into the tank after Davis cast [Rush of Battle](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rush+of+Battle), threatening tons of damage and tons of lifegain with his [Ponyback Brigade](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponyback+Brigade), three Goblin tokens, [Timely Hordemate](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Timely+Hordemate), [Chief of the Scale](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chief+of+the+Scale), and [Kin-Tree Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kin-Tree+Warden).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpbal15/round-9-parke.jpg)

 *Jamie Parke, survives a frightening [Rush of Battle](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rush+of+Battle) before his [High Sentinels of Arashin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=High+Sentinels+of+Arashin) carry him to victoroy.* 

  

After the dust settled, Parke was left with a [High Sentinels of Arashin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=High+Sentinels+of+Arashin), [Glacial Stalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glacial+Stalker), [Archers' Parapet](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archers%27+Parapet) and 9 life, versus Davis's team of several creatures, but no fliers. A few attacks later the High [Sentinel](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sentinel)s were able to whittle Davis's life down from 34 to zero, winning the match for Parke in two games just as time was called in the round.
