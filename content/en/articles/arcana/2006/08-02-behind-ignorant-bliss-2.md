The *Dissension* spell **Ignorant Bliss** was [designed](/en/articles/makingmagic/2006/04-24-in-the-cards) as a hellbent enabler, with the twist that it actually fills up your hand after its effect goes off. It doesn’t give a lot of impact even in a hellbent deck, but small and narrow effects like this can often make narrow cards worth playing if the cantrip “draw a card” effect is added.

But how do you illustrate an abstract concept like “you temporarily lose access to your spells”? The creative team’s art description left it up to artist Jeff Miracola, giving him only the abstract idea of what the card did. What Jeff came up with is this mage floating out of the normal world into weirdo-space:
