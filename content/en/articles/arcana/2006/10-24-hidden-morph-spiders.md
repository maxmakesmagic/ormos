The morph mechanic came back in a big way in *Time Spiral*. Many morph creatures have a visual cue in their art that represents the mysteriously uniform “morph shell” from which the morph creature emerges. It’s that spider-shaped creature shell represented in *Onslaught’s* expansion symbol (![](https://i.ormos.uk/a8/4a/a84a50bb06c8db6280a931bb651b3c3820d648c7050b234b118ecae8c171256d.gif)).

  
*[Break Open](https://web.archive.org/web/20061024000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Break+Open) art by Alex Horley-Orlandelli – [Fatal Mutation](https://web.archive.org/web/20061024000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Mutation) art by Heather Hudson* 

You can find lots of these “morph spiders” throughout the art of *Time Spiral* morph cards:

![Fortune_Thief_640](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/cardart/TSP/Fortune_Thief_640.jpg%0A%09%09%09)  
*Check out the discarded “morph spider” on the left – [Fortune Thief](https://web.archive.org/web/20061024000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fortune+Thief) art by Christopher Moeller* 

 

![Fathom_Seer_640](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/cardart/TSP/Fathom_Seer_640.jpg%0A%09%09%09)  
*See it way down below, on the sea floor? – [Fathom Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20061024000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fathom+Seer) art by Ralph Horsley* 

 

![Weathered_Bodyguards_640](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/cardart/TSP/Weathered_Bodyguards_640.jpg%0A%09%09%09)  
*The morph spider looms behind these cool plains survivors – [Weathered Bodyguards](https://web.archive.org/web/20061024000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Weathered+Bodyguards) art by Wayne Reynolds* 

 

![Vesuvan_Shapeshifter_640](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/cardart/TSP/Vesuvan_Shapeshifter_640.jpg%0A%09%09%09)  
*Bet you can tell which one’s the Shapeshifter! – [Vesuvan Shapeshifter](https://web.archive.org/web/20061024000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vesuvan+Shapeshifter) art by Quinton Hoover* 

How many others can you find in *Time Spiral* – or in *Onslaught* Block?
