Incinerate has been getting a lot of attention thanks to [Selecting Tenth Edition](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603133326/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/selecting10e/wrapup) announcing its return to Standard next year. In the final sketch vote from that promotion, Zoltan Boros & Gabor Szikszai dominated the poll, taking over 80% of the vote. The final version of their Incinerate is now here in the office, so today we're able to give you a look at the piece's journey from sketch to completed illustration.
