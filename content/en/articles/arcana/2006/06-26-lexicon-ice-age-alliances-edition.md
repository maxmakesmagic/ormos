Welcome to a special pre-*Coldsnap* edition of the [Lexicon](https://web.archive.org/web/20060626000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/fullarchive&tablefilter=lexicon), in which we revisit the *Ice Age* and *Alliances* sets and explore the meanings of some of the more obscure words we use in the names of those cards. While many offbeat names in **Magic** (like "[Shyft](https://web.archive.org/web/20060626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shyft)") are simply made up, we often dig around dictionaries for real little-used and archaic words for inspiration. 

![Aurochs](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Aurochs)

**aur•ochs**  
*noun, both singular and plural* 

1.  European bison having a smaller and higher head than the North American bison.
2.  A large, recently extinct long-horned European wild ox; considered one of the ancestors of domestic cattle.

![Ashnod's Cylix](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ashnod%27s+Cylix)

**cy •lix**  
*noun* 

1.  A shallow drinking cup with two handles; used in ancient Greece.

![Foxfire](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Foxfire)

**fox•fire**  
*noun* 

1.  A phosphorescent glow, especially that produced by certain fungi found on rotting wood.

![Fumarole](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Fumarole)

**fu•ma•role**  
*noun* 

1.  An opening in the Earth’s crust, often near a volcano, that emits steam and gases.

![Fylgja](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Fylgja)

**fyl•gja**  
*noun* 

1.  In Scandinavian mythology, a supernatural creature, usually in the form of an animal, who accompanies a person.

![Hipparion](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Hipparion)

**hip•pa•ri•on**  
*noun* 

1.  An extinct genus of Tertiary mammals allied to the horse, but three-toed, having on each foot a small lateral hoof on each side of the main central one.

![Jokulhaups](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Jokulhaups)

**jo•kul•haups**  
*noun* 

1.  A type of mudslide that occurs when a volcano erupts under a glacier, sending a mixture of glacial water, mud, and volcanic debris flowing downhill.

![Thermokarst](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Thermokarst)

**ther•mo•karst**  

1.  A land surface that forms as ice-rich permafrost melts.

*Sources: [dictionary.com](http://www.dictionary.com/), [wikipedia.com](http://en.wikipedia.org/)*
