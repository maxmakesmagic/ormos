### Who's Squishing The Bugs?

* * *

One of the jobs I've been given is to keep an eye on questions that come up repeatedly on the message boards. I keep the team updated on what issues are hot on the boards and try to find answers to what you want to know. I can't get answers for everything, but when I do get one, I'll get it out to you as quickly as possible.

While the beta server hasn't been up for a long period of time, it has already helped us fix some problems. And I want to express our thanks for all your help testing the code. So far we've had over 2000 members of the ***Magic** Online* community join us on the beta server, which proves that we all have the same goal: A stable live server for you to play on. (Or was that we all just wanted to play for free? Nah, couldn't be.)

The big success over the past week is the performance of the live server. It's been up for over a week with the only downtime from a scheduled maintenance this morning. And, while we're happy with this progress, we're still doing everything we can to get full functionality back on the live server.

Along those lines, some of you have noticed old, familiar faces poking 'round in Bugzilla. The crew at Leaping Lizard Software has always kept in touch with what's going on in ***Magic** Online,* and are just as interested in its success as we all are. They offered to help us in any way they could and have been fixing and clearing out various bugs. That's given our in-house team more time to investigate remaining bugs on the beta server. The Lizards have also been talking with the in-house team about restoring Version 2.0 functionality.

It's our hope that, between all of us, we'll have everything back to normal in the not-so-distant future. Then we can have that party that Chuck mentioned. (More details on *that* soon.)

Thanks again for all your help.

-Dan
