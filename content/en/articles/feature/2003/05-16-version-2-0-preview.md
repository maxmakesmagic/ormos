### A Little About What's Coming

* * *

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d1/45/d145a36bdcc834117f051a3e9a551d4f363545b99b346136ef3a1ed90a2e8396.jpg)  
For all you folks who, like me, aren't there to see what's going on at E3, I've got a little detail on what's coming in Version 2.0.

First, if you didn't read the [press release](/en/articles/pressreleases/2003/05-09-press-release), current users don't have anything to worry about. For you, Version 2.0 is going to be just another update -- albeit somewhat large. Since that seemed to be a big question everyone had, I figured it couldn't hurt to reiterate it.

And, no, you're not going to lose any cards in your collections.

Next, I'll talk about the most significant change to the ***Magic** Online* interface -- **New Player** and **Expert Player** views.

### New Player View

The **New Player** view eliminates all but the most basic rooms in ***Magic** Online.* Players using the **New Player** setting will have access to only the **New Players**, **Q & A** (formerly **Moderators & Support Staff**), and **Tutorials** rooms.

### Expert Player View

The **Expert Player** view eliminates all the rooms designed solely with new players in mind. So this is the view you'll use for trading, leagues, casual games, and tournaments.

This separation of views should help new players ease in to ***Magic** Online* for the first time, without overwhelming them with the options available to more advanced players.

### Trophy Case

![](https://i.ormos.uk/56/2c/562c4df0f5c6effa6baac6b81e6f706e73279f2d8e4a92d5a8d07758023c3b3c.jpg)  
For those of you who like to keep track of your tournament accomplishments, the **Trophy Case** is for you. Every prize, league, or sanctioned event you win will be kept track of for your viewing pleasure.

Finally, let's take a look at the changes coming to the **Trading Post**.

### Bulletin Board

This first part of the **Trading Post** is going to be very familiar to all of you. That's because it's exactly like the current rooms. You post a short message advertising what you have up for trade. Other players can do a text search for the cards they're looking for, then select a player to trade with.

The main difference is that we're eliminating all the various trading rooms. It has become obvious that labeling the rooms for different types of trading hasn't been effective, so why have them?

### Market

There will be a dedicated auction room called the **Market**. It's currently formatted as a fairly standard chat room with two extra buttons: **Pause** and **Play**. Players will be able to stop the scrolling of the text so they don't miss what they're looking for.

### Emporium

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8a/cb/8acb85610bb91a2c5bdba9d5dc1db9d21101e70307d4187dc126dc2f6c847590.jpg)  
The **Emporium** is the largest change to the **Trading Post**. This is a room much like those you find in the **Casual Play** area. If you have cards to trade, you "sit down" at a table, which will display just like a two-player game. Other players can initiate a trade with you by clicking on the empty chair at your table. This room allows traders to open up a table and walk away until another player decides he or she wants to trade.

On the table in front of each trader is a stack of binders -- the more binders on the table, the more rares that player has to trade. Also, above each trader's avatar is a number that tells you how many other players that trader has successfully traded with.

### Other Changes

These are just a few of the changes slated for ***Magic** Online 2.0*. Once I have more details about what's going in, I'll make sure to pass them on. Of course, I wouldn't doubt that the team will want to keep a *few* items back to surprise you when you see them.
