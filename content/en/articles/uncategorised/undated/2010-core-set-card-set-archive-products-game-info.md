## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/83/cd/83cd11f0038858ce2b5170acde3bf514692bfca695c2b0d01f8b6c1b62638810.png)

### 

Includes eight 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, a player’s guide, a learn-to-play guide, a card box, an excerpt from *The Purifying Fire* novel, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/95/ec/95ecd98ebdfc05bc16fdcb8866a43d55746c6c4b74cdb0ad91a2a8abb72d859a.png)

### 

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/64/e8/64e896a03419151e22f240dfc88162d1b47766275a32c98e02dd272d7a4f770c.png)

### 

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 41 cards, a foil premium rare card, a sealed 15-card booster pack, a strategy insert, and **Magic** learn-to-play guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718094335/http://magic.wizards.com/node/957476)

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7d/59/7d59afe676922434eccaf0f38d18180dec1dc8b45eb7295cc4b98f1794962ef4.png)The *2010 Core Set* features 249 black-bordered cards, including randomly inserted premium versions of all cards in the set. It is available in booster packs, intro packs, and fat packs.  
  
**Set Name:** *2010 Core Set*  
**Number of Cards:** 249

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

**Official Three-Letter Code:** M10  
**Twitter Hashtag:** #MTGM10

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718094335/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/MTGM10FAQ20090702.rtf)

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Aaron Forsythe (lead), Brady Dommermuth, Devin Low, Bill Rose, Mark Rosewater, and Brian Tinsman

**Final Game Design and Development:** Erik Lauer (lead), Tom LaPille, Gregory Marques, Mike Turian, Brandon Bozzi, and Devin Low, with contributions from Mark L. Gottlieb
