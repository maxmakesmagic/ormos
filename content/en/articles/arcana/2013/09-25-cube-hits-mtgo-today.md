When **Magic Online** comes back up from its downtime today players will be able to join Cube queues with prizes for the original *Ravnica* block! Cube will run until the *Theros* Prerelease begins on **Magic Online** which is 10 A.M. Pacific, Friday, October 4, 2013.

You might recall we ran a vote a few weeks ago asking what format you want paired with this Cube and 31.2% of the voters demanded the original *Ravnica* block! Second, with 26.9% of the vote, was triple *Innistrad*.

So what's changed in the cube since the last time you played it on **Magic Online**? Here's the breakdown:

*   [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic) replaces [Edge of Autumn](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Edge+of+Autumn)
*   [Celestial Flare](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Celestial+Flare) replaces [Cataclysm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cataclysm)
*   [Banisher Priest](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banisher+Priest) replaces [Fiend Hunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiend+Hunter)
*   [Archangel of Thune](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archangel+of+Thune) replaces [Tithe](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tithe)
*   [Chandra, Pyromaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Pyromaster) replaces [Banefire](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banefire)
*   [Ogre Battledriver](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ogre+Battledriver) replaces [Staggershock](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Staggershock)
*   [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie) replaces [Skeletal Vampire](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skeletal+Vampire)
*   [Liliana's Reaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana%27s+Reaver) replaces [Korlash, Heir to Blackblade](https://web.archive.org/web/20130925000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Korlash%2C+Heir+to+Blackblade)
