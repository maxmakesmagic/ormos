It is one of the world's oldest games, and even the people behind **Magic: The Gathering**, like Richard Garfield, have been impacted by it. The game? Chess, of course! Daily Decks pays homage to the legendary creation with two mini-decks reimagining it through the **Magic** lens. For black you have a front row filled with [Pawn of Ulamog](https://web.archive.org/web/20101004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Pawn%2Bof%2BUlamog), two [Castle Sengir](https://web.archive.org/web/20101004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Castle%2BSengir) appearing as rooks, (Black) Knights, and [Priest of Gix](https://web.archive.org/web/20101004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Priest+of+Gix) serving as bishop (in time the Priest will be bishop, right?). Of course, [Coffin Queen](https://web.archive.org/web/20101004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Coffin+Queen) and [Lord of the Undead](https://web.archive.org/web/20101004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lord+of+the+Undead) are serving as queen and king, respectively.

Now on to the white half of the chess set! While white in **Magic** doesn't have pawns, per se, [Eager Cadet](https://web.archive.org/web/20101004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eager+Cadet)s will no doubt be happy to en passant themselves to grand position. [Castle](https://web.archive.org/web/20101004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Castle) is, of course, a solid stand in for rooks, the bishop is represented by [Preacher](https://web.archive.org/web/20101004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preacher), and you have your [White Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20101004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=White%2BKnight). [King Suleiman](https://web.archive.org/web/20101004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=King+Suleiman) and [Jhovall Queen](https://web.archive.org/web/20101004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jhovall+Queen) represent, well, that should be obvious.
