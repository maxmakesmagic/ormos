Watch players compete for their last shot at the Super League Championship in the Last Chance Qualifier episode this Tuesday, October 20, at 6:00 p.m. Pacific (UTC-7) on [twitch.tv/magic](http://www.twitch.tv/magic)!

If you missed it the first time around, Randy Buehler wrote an [article](/en/articles/feature/2015/10-06-super-league-last-chance-qualifier) that recaps the Super League season so far and details the schedule leading up to the Super League Championship later this year. Who will emerge as the Super League grand champion? Find out by watching along!

Follow the links below to take you to each player's Last Chance Qualifier decklist!
