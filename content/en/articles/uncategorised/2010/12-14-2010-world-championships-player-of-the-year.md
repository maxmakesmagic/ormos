It's a tie!

  

Before [Worlds](/en/events/2010/worlds10/theres-no-jace-like-guillaume) this weekend, Brad Nelson was the frontrunner in the Player of the Year race. Combine a rough patch of playing on Brad's part and an amazing run for Guillaume Matignon (he did win the whole thing, after all), and the result? A tie!

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/activity/da602_players.jpg) 

  

What to do? Let's have them play more **Magic**!

The Player of the Year will be decided in [Pro Tour–Paris](/en/events/2010/paris11ptq/pro-tourparis-qualifier-season-top-8-booster-draft-deck-lists-34), February 10-13, 2010, after a tie-breaker playoff!

Read the [Player of the Year feature](/en/events/2010/worlds10/2010-magic-the-gathering-world-championships-feature-player-of-the-year) to get the whole story!
