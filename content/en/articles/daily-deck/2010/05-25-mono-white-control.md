Looking for a deck that puts some serious hurt on players using black or red? Joel Allen's qualifying list for U.S. Nationals is right up your alley! It plays all of white's best spells, like Day of Judgment, Oblivion Ring, and Path to Exile, as well as a creature engine powered by Emeria, the Sky Ruin. Build up to seven Plains with Pilgrim's Eye and Kor Cartographer, then start recurring Wall of Omens to draw extra cards or Lone Missionary to gain life. Opponent cast Spreading Seas on your Emeria? Bounce it with Kor Skyfisher! See more National Qualifier deck lists [here](/en/events/2010/natqual10/2010-national-qualifiers-top-8-standard-deck-lists).
