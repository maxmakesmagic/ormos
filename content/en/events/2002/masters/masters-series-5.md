The Masters Series is an invitation-only tournament series that showcases the top players from the Pro Tour circuit in single-elimination competition with large cash prizes. Masters Series tournaments are held approximately four times a year in both individual and team formats. They are run at REL 5, require a level 4 head judge, and are DCI sanctioned with a K-value of 48. They are invitation-only tournaments.

The 2002-2003 **team-format** Masters Series invitation list comprises the following:

*   The winner and finalist team of the previous team-format Masters Series event (Osaka).
*   The top finishing previously uninvited team from Grand Prix-New Jersey.
*   The top finishing previously uninvited team from Grand Prix-Nagoya.
*   The Top 2 finishing, previously uninvited team from Pro Tour Boston 2002.
*   The Top 2 teams of the Gateway tournament immediately preceding the team-format Masters Series event.

**MASTERS SERIES GATEWAY TOURNAMENTS**  
 Each Masters Series event is preceded by a Gateway tournament. Gateway tournaments offer invitations to specific Masters Series events. Gateway tournaments use the single-elimination format. They are run at REL 4, require a level 3 head judge, and are DCI sanctioned with a K-value of 40. They are invitation-only tournaments.

Each **team-format** Gateway tournament invitation list comprises the following:

*   Any team with a DCI team-format Limited rating of 1700 or higher as of the February 5th Ratings-publish.
*   Any team that placed in the Top 8 at Grand Prix New Jersey or Nagoya.
*   Any team that played on Day 2 at Pro Tour Boston.
*   Any team that played in the previous year's team-format Masters Series (Osaka) event.
