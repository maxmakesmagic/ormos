It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6)! Since we're now in April, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

![Artisan of Kozilek](https://i.ormos.uk/27/b2/27b2e9d76932d3df4820f96af5a1b6a8bbb429a49c36c3a528798a65d346f9b8.jpg "Artisan of Kozilek") 

  

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards feature the FNM logo and many have original flavor text and updated wordings.
