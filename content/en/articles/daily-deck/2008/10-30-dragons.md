Fresh from the **magicthegathering.com** forums comes this fiery creation from user TechOverride. A Jund creation, the deck centers around Dragons, both "real" in the form of Broodmate, and "fake" in the form of changeling creatures like Chameleon Colossus, Changeling Berserker, and Taurean Mauler. Holding all the creatures together? The powerful Crucible of Fire, capable of giving all the Dragons, changeling or no, a hefty +3/+3 boost. It's the perfect amount of creativity for the upcoming States and Provincial championships. To find out more information about States (including how to get special unreleased promo cards for attending as well as the nearest location to you) visit [www.mtgchamps.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20081030000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://mtgchamps.com/&origin=).
