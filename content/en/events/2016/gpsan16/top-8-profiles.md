Learn more about Grand Prix Santiago's Top 8 competitors—including who they are, what they’ve done in the game, and more—by taking a look through their profiles!

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpsan16/t8_chavarria.jpg)Name:** John Chavarría  
  
**Age:** 40  
  
**Hometown:** Santiago, Chile.  
  
**What deck did you play?:** White-Blue Flash.  
  
**Why did you choose this deck?:** Because it's fun and I enjoy playing it.  
  
**What's the best card in your sideboard?:** [Gisela, the Broken Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20161030000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gisela%2C+the+Broken+Blade)  
  
**What's your favorite moment of the tournament so far?:** The gift my wife gave me when I learned that I made top 8.

* * *

  

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpsan16/t8_santos.jpg)Name:** Eduardo dos Santos Vieira  
  
**Age:** 27  
  
**Hometown:** Londrina, Brazil.  
  
**What deck did you play?:** Black-Green Delirium  
  
**Why did you choose this deck?:** I wanted to play Temur Energy, but after a lot of insistence from my friends Edel and Babones, I decided against it and brought a tier 1 deck instead.  
  
**What's the best card in your sideboard?** [Lost Legacy](https://web.archive.org/web/20161030000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lost+Legacy), because PV said so.  
  
**What's your favorite moment of the tournament so far?:** Getting the concession from Jonathan Melamed in round 14, as he was already locked for top 8.

* * *

  

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpsan16/t8_melamed.jpg)Name:** Jonathan Lobo Melamed  
  
**Age:** 30  
  
**Hometown:** Brasilia, Brazil.  
  
**What deck did you play?:** Blue-Red [Fevered Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20161030000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fevered+Visions).  
  
**Why did you choose this deck?:** Because I feel very comfortable with it.  
  
**What's the best card in your sideboard?:** [Bedlam Reveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20161030000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bedlam+Reveler)  
  
**What's your favorite moment of the tournament so far?:** Registering [Fevered Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20161030000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fevered+Visions) in the maindeck as a metagame call, because William Bossanelli Araujo told me to.

* * *

  

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpsan16/t8_silva.jpg)Name:** Victor Fernando Silva  
  
**Age:** 23  
  
**Hometown:** São Paulo, Brazil,  
  
**What deck did you play?:** Black-Green Delirium  
  
**Why did you choose this deck?:** Because it's well positioned, it's very consistent and it has a lot of lines of play to it.  
  
**What's the best card in your sideboard?:** [Blossoming Defense](https://web.archive.org/web/20161030000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blossoming+Defense), because nobody expects it and it leads to huge blowouts.  
  
**What's your favorite moment of the tournament so far?:** Locking Top 8 after winning a very intense round 14, it was exciting!

* * *

  

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpsan16/t8_salvatto.jpg)Name:** Luis Salvatto  
  
**Age:** 27  
  
**Hometown:** Luján, Buenos Aires, Argentina.  
  
**What deck did you play?:** Black-Green Delirium  
  
**Why did you choose this deck?:** It seemed like the most well-positioned deck in the current metagame. A mix of advice from Javier Luna and the list that Seth Manfield played last weekend in [Providence](https://web.archive.org/web/20161030000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Providence).  
  
**What's the best card in your sideboard?:** [Transgress the Mind](https://web.archive.org/web/20161030000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transgress+the+Mind)  
  
**What's your favorite moment of the tournament so far?:** The first round of day two, I lost to Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa after making a mistake that probably cost me game one, and after that I learned a great play from him that I later used to my advantage in another Black-Green mirror in the tournament.

* * *

  

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpsan16/t8_cespedes.jpg)Name:** Cristian Cespedes  
  
**Age:** 31  
  
**Hometown:** Viña del Mar, Chile.  
  
**What deck did you play?:** Black-Green Delirium  
  
**Why did you choose this deck?:** It's the one that suits me best, and resolving an [Ishkanah, Grafwidow](https://web.archive.org/web/20161030000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ishkanah%2C+Grafwidow) with Delirium wins a ton of games.  
  
**What's the best card in your sideboard?:** [Emrakul, the Promised End](https://web.archive.org/web/20161030000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Promised+End)  
  
**What's your favorite moment of the tournament so far?:** Winning round 12, which basically locked me for top 8.

* * *

  

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpsan16/t8_garcia.jpg)Name:** Ignacio García Sepúlveda  
  
**Age:** 27  
  
**Hometown:** Viña del Mar, Chile.  
  
**What deck did you play?:** Mardu Vehicles.  
  
**Why did you choose this deck?:** I'm an aggro player and Mardu is my favorite color combination.  
  
**What's the best card in your sideboard?:** [Galvanic Bombardment](https://web.archive.org/web/20161030000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Bombardment)  
  
**What's your favorite moment of the tournament so far?:** When I used [Ceremonious Rejection](https://web.archive.org/web/20161030000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ceremonious+Rejection) to counter [Flaying Tendrils](https://web.archive.org/web/20161030000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flaying+Tendrils).

* * *

  

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpsan16/t8_freitas.jpg)Name:** Marcos Paulo de Jesus Freitas  
  
**Age:** 28  
  
**Hometown:** Rio de Janeiro, Brazil  
  
**What deck did you play?:** Blue-Red Colossus.  
  
**Why did you play this deck?:** I think this deck is under the radar and [Ceremonious Rejection](https://web.archive.org/web/20161030000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ceremonious+Rejection) is seeing less play. Also, I'm always looking to play a ramp deck.  
  
**What's the best card in your sideboard?:** [Elder Deep-Fiend](https://web.archive.org/web/20161030000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elder+Deep-Fiend)  
  
**What's your favorite moment of the tournament so far?:** The top 8 announcement messing with my emotions, because I was the last one to be named. I also want to thank Willy Edel for always supporting me. #EdelGenius
