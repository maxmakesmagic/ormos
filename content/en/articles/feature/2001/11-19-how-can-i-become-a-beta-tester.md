The beta test will be executed in waves, enabling us to control the number of players as we stress-test our server. The first wave starts on November 16th when we will be installing it on Wizards of the Coast Retail LAN, creating a limited number of accounts. Soon after, the second wave will offer a limited number of CDs to Premier Stores to distribute to customers. The third wave will offer the beta as a download on select web sites.

[Download the *Magic Online* Beta Client now](https://web.archive.org/web/20051201090047/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/modownload) and claim your turf (and a cool username).

Starting February 14th we will be opening the Beta to the masses through Gamespot.com and their speedy Download Express service.

Once you have installed the program, you will be able to register for testing through the new account button on the main page. This, and the read-me file will walk you through all the steps to get playing.
