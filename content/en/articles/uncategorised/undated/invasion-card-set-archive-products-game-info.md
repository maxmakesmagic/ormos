### 

Includes one 75-card tournament pack, three 15-card booster packs, one foil basic land card, one foil common card, the *Invasion* novel, and a *Top Deck* magazine player’s guide pullout.

### 

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0e/25/0e2513d29da9cf510039c9b7bb3e23182fb2744339687d586adea371d4603a3e.png)

### 

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

## INVASION PRODUCT INFORMATION

This 350-card set includes new Kicker cards, Split cards, and Multicolored cards with stronger allied color strategies.

**Set Name:** *Invasion*  
**Block:** Set 1 of 3 of the *Invasion* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 350

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, Spanish
