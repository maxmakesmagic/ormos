While his countryman Marijn Lybaert was busy making his fourth Pro Tour Top 8, Jan Van Nieuwenhove managed to get to 18th place at his first Pro Tour playing an innovative cascade deck. But rather than fetching up Hypergenesis, which is banned in Extended, or Living End as many players did last year, Jan was far more clever. He used Borderposts to bounce his lands to his hand then fired off Restore Balance. That let him wipe his opponent's board of creatures and lands, while he waited patiently for Greater Gargadon to enter the battlefield from suspend or even follow up his cascade with a planeswalker! Read the official coverage of the event [here](/en/events/2010/ptams10/rietzl-sweeps-to-victory-in-amsterdam)
