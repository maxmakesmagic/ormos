It’s been quite a while since my last installment of DCI News and Notes, but we have a few important things to communicate, so let’s get right to it!

### Global Celebration

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9a/36/9a36305cfa8ca6b66c24e5050aed5434a8d6348a3f96fc8b4ece7d435c8360a0.gif)We’re just a few weeks away from the 10th Anniversary Global Celebration. This amazing event will take place the weekend of July 26th and 27th in over fifty countries around the world. All **Magic** players are welcome to attend this event. 

Event organizers have been provided with twenty or more copies of the special 10th Anniversary Rukh Egg card. The card uses the new card face with a black border. It has the new improved foil treatment and has a foil 10th Anniversary Logo in the text box and a 10th Anniversary stamp in the corner of the art box. If more players attend an event than an organizer has Rukh Eggs, then the top finishing players in the tournament will receive the cards. If an organizer gets fewer players than cards, then the top finishing players will get two cards so that all cards are distributed. The DCI will also be sure to send a card to the Head Judge of each event that is reported using DCI Reporter. 

 ![rukh egg](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=rukh%20egg&options=) 

I’ve seen organizers refer to this event as the “*8th Edition* Prerelease” because organizers are allowed to use the new *8th Edition* set before the official release date. Each player will receive 5 booster packs of *8th Edition* to complete their deck and we’re sending organizers *8th Edition* basic lands that players can add to their decks. Organizers will decide the entry fee.

We’re maintaining a list of the locations that will be hosting an event. So far we have most of North America, but more stores will be added from around the world in the coming days. You can find the list [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20030702000000/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/resource/10astores). Be sure to contact your local organizer for more information. 

### Sets Legal Earlier

As announced in *Sideboard* Magazine, we’ve made a minor change to the date sets are legal for use in Constructed-format tournaments.

Starting with the *Mirrodin* set, **Magic** sets will be legal for Constructed on the 20th of the month in which they are released. For example, *Mirrodin* releases in early October and will be legal for Constructed play on October 20th. The *Darksteel* expansion will be legal in Constructed on February 20th, 2004.

Note that this policy does NOT apply to *8th Edition*, which will be legal for Constructed play on September 1st, as normal.

### And Now the Exceptions

For most tournaments, *8th Edition* will be legal on September 1st, 2003. However, two tournaments that take place in August will follow the format rules as if they were played after September 1st. In other words, for these two tournaments, *8th Edition* will be legal and *7th Edition* will NOT be legal.

The two tournaments with the special exemption are:

**2003 World Championships** – *8th Edition* will be legal for both the Extended and Standard portions. *7th Edition* will not be legal for the Standard portion but will continue to be one of the legal sets for the Extended portion.

**Grand Prix-Atlanta** – Held a few days before September, it will be played as if it were in September and *7th Edition* will not be legal in favor of *8th Edition*.
