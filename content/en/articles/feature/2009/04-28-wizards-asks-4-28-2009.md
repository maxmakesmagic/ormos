*What's your favorite creature type and why?*

![Jenna Helland](https://i.ormos.uk/9d/95/9d95a2cb2142a94ab2e5013f672af829ce9fc6cfc661b07ac099eea62305818b.jpg)

**Jenna Helland:** Elementals! In particular, elementals from Lorwyn.

If I were a character in a fantasy book, I'd be an alchemist tinkering with raw nature to create something powerful. Classic cards like [Earth Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20090428000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Earth+Elemental) and [Air Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20090428000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Air+Elemental) have always appealed to me. It's no wonder that so many stories adapt the archetypal elements into their world building. Earth. Air. Fire. Water. It's a pattern that still resonates centuries after it ceased to be accepted as a fundamental truth. 

Some creature types are well defined—a specter is as a specter does. A fantasy tiger must have some cat-like tendencies. Elementals enjoy more flexibility, which provides interesting creative opportunities. *Lorwyn* introduced a new breed of elemental. These aren't just manifestations of pure energy, they're amalgamations of matter and thought. It's like a tussle between chemistry and psychology—and may the best [Shriekmaw](https://web.archive.org/web/20090428000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shriekmaw) win.

Here's art description for [Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20090428000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vigor) that sent to Jim Murray:

> Keep in mind that Lorwyn's elementals have elements of animal anatomy, surreality, and physical implausibility . . . show a creature that resembles an enormous bull or bison whose back looks like a tortoise's shell was fused onto it. The creature's face resembles a rock that's been shattered from a central point. Its top side is covered with a layer of moss. Flailing tentacles of young vine grow along its back and sides. It has lowered its head as though about to charge.

That's just fun.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9a/6f/9a6fadfdfc4af1c4fd1394617954410f46b2b322a311097dddc3f9751ce741b9.jpg)  

Elementals are fun to name, too: [Flickerwisp](https://web.archive.org/web/20090428000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flickerwisp), [Horde of Notions](https://web.archive.org/web/20090428000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Horde+of+Notions), [Wispmare](https://web.archive.org/web/20090428000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wispmare), [Primalcrux](https://web.archive.org/web/20090428000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primalcrux). Those names are a pleasing mix of dream and substance. Especially to an aspiring alchemist.
