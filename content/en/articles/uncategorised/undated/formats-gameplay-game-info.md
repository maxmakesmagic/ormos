There are countless different formats for playing Magic, all of which dictate what cards you can play in a given game. In Constructed Formats, for instance, you bring a deck made of carefully selected cards you already own. By contrast, limited formats require everyone to start out with a certain number of unopened packs from which they build their decks on the spot.

## CONSTRUCTED

**BLOCK**

Each block of expansions has its own constructed format which only allows cards released in that block’s expansions. Additionally, each deck must contain at least sixty cards.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/https://magic.wizards.com/content/gameinfoformatsblock)

**STANDARD**

The Standard format uses the most recently released Magic sets. The following card sets are currently permitted in the Standard format:

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/https://magic.wizards.com/content/gameinfoformatsstandard)

**MODERN**

Cards from *Core Set Eighth Edition* and *Mirrodin* through today are legal in this format. Again, decks require a minimum of sixty cards and may have a sideboard of up to fifteen cards.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/https://magic.wizards.com/content/gameinfoformatsmodern)

**COMMANDER**

Each player chooses a legendary creature to be their “commander” and makes a 99-card deck around that creature.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/https://magic.wizards.com/content/gameinfoformatscommander)

## ETERNAL

Eternal formats are unique in that they contain every black-bordered card set Wizards has ever printed, each new card set is added to these formats.

**LEGACY**

Allows cards from all legal sets, but bans certain cards for power level reasons.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/https://magic.wizards.com/content/gameinfoformatslegacy)

**VINTAGE**

The most powerful of constructed formats, this format allows the “Power Nine” to be played.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/https://magic.wizards.com/content/gameinfoformatsvintage)

## LIMITED FORMATS

**BOOSTER DRAFT**

Players open booster packs and take turns picking cards from boosters to then build into 40-card decks.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/https://magic.wizards.com/content/gameinfoformatsboosterdraft)

**SEALED DECK**

Each player opens six booster packs and competes with a 40-card deck made from those cards.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/https://magic.wizards.com/content/gameinfoformatssealeddeck)

## TEAM FORMATS

Team formats let you and one or two friends work together to achieve victory.

* * *

 

**TEAM UNIFIED CONSTRUCTED**

Build three 60-card decks with a four-of limit across all three decks in this fun and challenging format.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/https://magic.wizards.com/node/374041)

 

* * *

**Team Limited**

**Team Booster Draft**

Draft your 40-card deck with an eye not just the strength of your own deck, but also your teammates’ and opponents’ decks.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/https://magic.wizards.com/node/373501)

 

**Team Sealed Deck**

Team Sealed combines the fun of Sealed Deck with two of your friends, letting you build powerful 40-card decks to be used in an exciting team format!

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/https://magic.wizards.com/node/265026)

 

## *MAGIC ONLINE*-SPECIFIC FORMATS

The following formats are only sanctioned for play on *Magic: The Gathering Online*.

[LEARN MORE ABOUT *MAGIC:THE GATHERING ONLINE*](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-products-game-info)

#### MAGIC ONLINE COMMANDER

Build a 100-card singleton deck around a Commander, typically a single legendary creature.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/https://magic.wizards.com/game-info/gameplay/rules-and-formats/banned-restricted/magic-online-commander)

#### FREEFORM

All sets and cards are allowed in this 40-card minimum format.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/https://magic.wizards.com/content/gameinfoformatsfreeform)

#### MOMIR BASIC

Decks must have exactly 60 basic lands and one “Momir Vig” Vanguard card.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/https://magic.wizards.com/content/gameinfoformatsmomirbasic)

#### PLANESWALKER

Limited to gold-bordered cards, available in Magic Online Planeswalker deck packs.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20210501065528/https://magic.wizards.com/content/gameinfoformatsplaneswalker)
