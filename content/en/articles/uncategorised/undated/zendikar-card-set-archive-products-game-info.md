## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/45/b8/45b8a62d267461d2aa8f88d33fbcf8030a06db2251998d79861ae668d2868dc7.png)

Zendikar, a new world for **Magic: The Gathering**, is the first set in the Zendikar block. It features 249 black-bordered cards, including randomly inserted premium versions of all cards in the set.

**Set Name:** *Zendikar*  
**Block:** Set 1 of 3 of the *Zendikar* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 249

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

**Official Three-Letter Code:** ZEN  
**Twitter Hashtag:** #MTGZEN

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160716110736/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/EN_MTGZENFAQ20090924.rtf)

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Mark Rosewater (lead), Doug Beyer, Graeme Hopkins, Kenneth Nagle, and Matt Place

**Final Game Design and Development:** Henry Stern (lead), Aaron Forsythe, Mark Globus, Tom LaPille, Erik Lauer, Devin Low, Matt Place, Mike Turian, and Steve Warner, with contributions from Mark L. Gottlieb
