TToday's deck is a clever hybrid of Junk Rites and Act 2. Those aren't really decks I'd think to put together, because the mana involved in ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/black.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/green.gif) (for [Grisly Salvage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grisly+Salvage)) into ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red-white.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red-white.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red-white.gif) (for [Boros Reckoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Reckoner)) is a little tricky, but usokui3 found a clever way to make it work and managed to find a number of clever synergies between the two shells. 

![Boros Reckoner](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Boros+Reckoner)

Every land in this deck taps for either green or black and either red or white, which means every land other than [Cavern of Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cavern+of+Souls) can help cast both [Boros Reckoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Reckoner) and [Grisly Salvage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grisly+Salvage). 

This deck has the standard-issue [Grisly Salvage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grisly+Salvage)s and [Mulch](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mulch)es to find [Unburial Rites](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unburial+Rites) and targets for them, but it supplements them with [Faithless Looting](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faithless+Looting)—a card that has awesome synergies with [Mulch](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mulch) on every level, but which Junk Rite's mana usually can't support. All this graveyard action obviously fuels the [Unburial Rites](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unburial+Rites) package and finds [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls), but it also sets up giant [Harvest Pyre](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Harvest+Pyre)s to use in combination with the [Boros Reckoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Reckoner)s this deck digs for. 

[Angel of Serenity](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel+of+Serenity) and [Thragtusk](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thragtusk) offer the standard Reanimator game plan, with [Sire of Insanity](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sire+of+Insanity) and [Olivia Voldaren](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Olivia+Voldaren) as additional bullets, but the major twist is that this deck can use [Boros Reckoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Reckoner) over the much weaker [Centaur Healer](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Centaur+Healer) and potentially combo-kill the opponent with it. It may not be all that easy to get the opponent down to 13 to make [Blasphemous Act](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blasphemous+Act) lethal with this deck, but with [Unburial Rites](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unburial+Rites), it can be trivial to get a second [Boros Reckoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Reckoner) into play to set up a 26-point [Blasphemous Act](https://web.archive.org/web/20130523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blasphemous+Act).
