Fate Reforged preview season is in full swing. A lot of people are excited about cards like Ugin, the Spirit Dragon, but I wanted to get a deeper feel of what types of interactions better deck builders were starting to recognize. 

I found a few familiar faces and asked them, "Based on what you've seen thus far from Fate Reforged, what card currently in Standard are you most excited about playing alongside the new cards?"

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpden15/seck.JPG)

 ***The Ben Seck:** "[Eidolon of Blossoms](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eidolon+of+Blossoms), Kruphix, and [Prophet of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prophet+of+Kruphix) all seem very fun with the Frontier Siege."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpden15/kibler.JPG)

 ***Brian Kibler:** "[Savage Knuckleblade](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Knuckleblade) and [Boon Satyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boon+Satyr) seem like great partners for Flamewake Phoenix."* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpden15/martell.JPG)

 ***Tom Martell:** "[Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy) and [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider) will be a lot better with all the new Jeskai cards."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpden15/black.JPG)

 ***Sam Black:** "[Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames) plays well with all the Jeskai cards we've seen thus far. Those seem like the best cards besides Ugin, the Spirit Dragon."*
