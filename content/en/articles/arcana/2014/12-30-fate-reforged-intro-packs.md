If you happen to follow Trick Jarrett on Twitter ([@TrickMTG](https://twitter.com/TrickMTG)) and you happened to be on Twitter yesterday, you were likely greeted by this Jeskai Djinn Monk.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/ujehvsbfrf153/en_9br2elg7l6.png)

What you may not know is that Sage-Eye Avengers will be looking back at you from shelves over the next few months as the face of the Jeskai Intro Pack.

Now, most preview seasons we tease you a bit with the Intro Pack packaging, blocking out the card image and making you wait a few days to see what they contain. But the holidays have put me in a giving mood, so I figured, why wait?
