* * *

In flavor terms, changelings are chameleons, instinctual shapechangers who effortlessly and involuntarily take on the forms of those around them. However, they don't always do that great a job of it—and this can get them into trouble.

The art of Woodland Changeling shows a changeling in mid-transformation into the form of an elf—a sensible form for a 2/2 green changeling, right?

![Woodland_Changeling_640](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/cardart/LRW/Woodland_Changeling_640.jpg)  
*Woodland Changeling art by Franz Vohwinkel*

But of course, the elves of Lorwyn prize their beauty above all things. They see a changeling doing a poor imitation of an elf, and they only see mockery—and it's dangerous business to mock Lorwyn elves. You can see the elves in the background, chasing after and firing arrows at our poor changeling.

Of course, in mechanical terms, changelings are all creature types all the time—which means it really is an Elf creature. So despite the art, Woodland Changeling is completely at home in an elf deck!
