## SELECTION COMMITTEE

Listed below are the members of the 2015 Hall of Fame Selection Committee. Each was chosen for his or her place in the history of the game. For the 2015 committee, players with 150 or more lifetime Pro Points are assigned to that category unless they are also a current Hall of Fame member.
