Last week I introduced you all [to Marath](https://web.archive.org/web/20131014000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/650231), one of the new Commanders in the upcoming *Commander* (2013 Edition.) Now it's time to take a closer look at its art. I won't dive into the art description, because while it provides a few interesting tidbits, it leaves a lot up to [Tyler Jacobson](https://web.archive.org/web/20131014000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Tyler%20Jacobson%22%5D). But I do love the art here and there are a few things you might not notice in the scaled down version.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/13/cc/13cc0e1ac0b72622a3d36e0f614cb486c54b367d8cfe02f5a8bfa642a0ba2179.jpg)  

Here's a blown up version of the art, check out his feet where vines grow up from the earth. I also love the variety in the antlers on its head; it's a very unique looking beast.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b9/9a/b99a76ef93b760813e1333715d58877870168181bbddebec0a02ec912d930b8a.jpg)
