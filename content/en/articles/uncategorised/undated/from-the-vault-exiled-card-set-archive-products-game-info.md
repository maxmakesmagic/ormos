## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/14/b7143ed0ac41f44df8f8105b5f13ab783f88ceec83207f0589a874a5601f8a38.png)*From the Vault: Exiled* features 15 premium foil cards—each one previously banned or restricted from organized play at some point in Magic history—as well as a full-color collector's insert with information about each card, and a special-edition life counter. All cards are tournament-legal, and eight feature new artwork.

**Set Name:** *From the Vault: Exiled*  
**Number of Cards:** 15

**Languages:** English

**Product Concept and Development Team:** Tom LaPille, Jeremy Jarvis, Mark Purvis, and Mark Rosewater
