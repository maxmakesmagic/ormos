For our final Pro Tour *Battle for Zendikar* deck this week, we've got a pretty sweet brew that kept Christian Calcano in contention deep into Day Two—Blue-Black Aristocrats.

Named after an old sacrifice-based deck that utilized creatures with the title "Aristocrat" in their card names, this deck borrows both the sacrificing theme and the name. Check out the video where Calcano and Oliver Tomajko discuss this very tricky deck.
