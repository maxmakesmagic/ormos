This Quarterfinals match featured two Californians facing off in their first Grand Prix Top 8. Adam Mancuso hailed from Santa Monica, while Eric Pei was just a short drive away from San Francisco.

**The Decks**

Eric Pei's blue-white heroic deck was capable of some very aggressive starts, thanks in part to some solid two-mana creatures like [Battlewise Hoplite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battlewise+Hoplite) and [Phalanx Leader](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phalanx+Leader). However, it also had access to some large blockers with multiple copies of [Coastline Chimera](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Coastline+Chimera), a solid flying blocker that could hold back creatures while his heroic creatures stayed on offense.

Mancuso's deck, on the other hand, was much more about big ground creatures. His green-red concoction was capable of some brutal starts thanks to Two-Headed Chimera and his bestow creatures, but the deck also had access to a nasty mythic in the form of [Xenagos, the Reveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xenagos%2C+the+Reveler).

**The Games**

Pei was first onto the table with [Battlewise Hoplite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battlewise+Hoplite), which promptly ate a [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike) from Mancuso, who followed up that turn with [Agent of Horizons](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Agent+of+Horizons). However, Pei's next creatures were [Phalanx Leader](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phalanx+Leader) and [Heliod's Emissary](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heliod%27s+Emissary), which backed Mancuso against a wall early on. [Traveling Philosopher](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Traveling+Philosopher) and [Chosen by Heliod](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chosen+by+Heliod) soon came down, growing Pei's army.

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpsac14/qf_mancuso.jpg)

*Adam Mancuso*

Unable to stop the board, Mancuso did what he could, trading when available and deploying a very large [Vulpine Goliath](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulpine+Goliath) onto the table. However, the [Heliod's Emissary](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heliod%27s+Emissary) made blocking effectively moot, and the [Phalanx Leader](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phalanx+Leader) duo powered through for a first game win.

Mancuso led off the second game with [Leafcrown Dryad](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leafcrown+Dryad) into [Nessian Courser](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nessian+Courser), the Dryad held back by Pei's second-turn [Bronze Sable](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bronze+Sable). The artifact creature was joined by [Wingsteed Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingsteed+Rider) on the next turn. Mancuso sent in the [Nessian Courser](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nessian+Courser) for 3, then aimed [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike) at [Wingsteed Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingsteed+Rider). It was replaced with [Battlewise Hoplite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battlewise+Hoplite) after an attack with the Sable on the next turn. Mancuso cast [Nylea's Disciple](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nylea%27s+Disciple) pre-combat, then sent in his creatures, attacking for 5. Pei had [Fate Foretold](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fate+Foretold) for his [Battlewise Hoplite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battlewise+Hoplite), growing it and drawing a card. [Traveling Philosopher](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Traveling+Philosopher) was the only follow-up after that.

The Hoplite traded with the [Nylea's Disciple](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nylea%27s+Disciple) on the next attack from Mancuso, and it was replaced with heliod's Emissary on the next turn. Mancuso, however, had [Fade into Antiquity](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fade+into+Antiquity) lying in wait for the enchantment creature, and [Destructive Revelry](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Destructive+Revelry) on the Sable put Pei in a bind.

Pei, however, was not done yet, as he cast [Evangel of Heliod](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Evangel+of+Heliod) to buy himself some time. It worked, as Mancuso no longer had any good attacks with his opponent stuck at 2. [Hopeful Eidolon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hopeful+Eidolon) gave him a glimmer of a chance when it was bestowed on the [Evangel of Heliod](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Evangel+of+Heliod), but Mancuso continued adding creatures to his board, with [Satyr Rambler](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Satyr+Rambler), [Spearpoint Oread](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spearpoint+Oread), and [Centaur Battlemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Centaur+Battlemaster) coming on subsequent turns.

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpsac14/qf_pei.jpg)

*Eric Pei*

However, Pei had a bestowed [Thassa's Emissary](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thassa%27s+Emissary) on the already bestowed Evangel, giving him a 5/7 lifelink attacker that would draw him cards if Mancuso simply let the attacks go through. When Pei sent in his massive creature, Mancuso opted to triple block it with the [Nessian Courser](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nessian+Courser), [Leafcrown Dryad](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leafcrown+Dryad), and [Spearpoint Oread](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spearpoint+Oread). [Battlewise Valor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battlewise+Valor) punished Mancuso for that block, as he lost all three of his creatures, and Pei's life total jumped from 2 to 9.

Mancuso continued playing for a few turns, but the game appeared to be over. When a land awaited him on top after another attack from Pei's Evangel, he offered the handshake.

**Mancuso 0 – Pei 2**
