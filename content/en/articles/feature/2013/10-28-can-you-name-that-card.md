Magic players use the art to identify cards, many do so more than the actual written name. Looking across the battlefield you can see what you're facing just by seeing the renderings of Magic's amazing artists. But, can you identify the card just from the descriptions? I'm betting you might have some trouble even though, once you see the card, it becomes obvious.

Location: Waterfall  
  
 Action: In Greek mythology, many aspects of nature were infused with life. Design an elemental creature created from the mist of a waterfall that feels at home in a Greek myth. It has flying so show it hovering in the air. Maybe it resembles an ethereal bird with a long, coiling mid-section.
