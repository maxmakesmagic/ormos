## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6c/de/6cdead46b762a320db03a0613222e29d9e8d872118880ead8a9ef84f2264dc53.png)

This set includes 140 silver-bordered cards and randomly inserted premium foil cards. *Unhinged* introduces three new Magic mechanics: Gotcha, Fractions, and Donkeys.

**Set Name:** *Unhinged*  
**Number of Cards:** 141

**Languages:** English

**Official Three-Letter Code:** UNH

Read [“The Un Starts Now”](https://web.archive.org/web/20160715102130/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/mr149) by Mark Rosewater  
 Read [“Bottled Up“](https://web.archive.org/web/20160715102130/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/aa149) by Anthony Alongi

**Original Game Design:** Richard Garfield 

**Game Design and Development:** Mark Rosewater (lead), Brandon Bozzi (copper), Randy Buehler Jr. (gold), and Brady Dommermuth (silver), with contributions from Paul Barclay, Tyler Bielman, Mike Elliott, Aaron Forsythe, Mark L. Gottlieb, Devin Low, and Brian Schneider
