Learn more about Grand Prix Providence's Top 8 competitors—including who they are, what they’ve done in the game, and more—by taking a look through their profiles!

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppro16/t8-lebedowicz.jpg)Name:** Osyp Lebedowicz  
  
**Age:** 36 (old)  
  
**Occupation:** Marketing Director  
  
**Hometown:** Jersey City, New Jersey  
  
**Previous Magic accomplishments:** Pro Tour Venice 2003 Champion. Kai Budde stayed at my apartment once. 1996 Latin Dance Champion, Northeast Division.  
  
**What deck did you play and why?** Red-White Vehicles. I haven't played the format at all and figured I'd make less mistakes with an aggressive deck. And Reid Duke played it.  
  
**What sideboard card was the most important against the field? Why?** [Skysovereign, Consul Flagship](https://web.archive.org/web/20161023000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skysovereign%2C+Consul+Flagship) was solid for me but everything else was worse than my main deck.  
  
**After this weekend would you play this deck again? What would you change?** Yes, it has game against any deck.  
  
**If you were competing in the Inventor's Fair, what would you bring?** [Smuggler's Copter](https://web.archive.org/web/20161023000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smuggler%27s+Copter) or Lan D. Ho's glasses.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppro16/t8-shiels.jpg)Name:** Dave Shiels  
  
**Age:** 28  
  
**Occupation:** IT Security Engineer  
  
**Hometown:** Medway, Maryland  
  
**Previous Magic accomplishments:** Four Grand Prix Top 8s, Grand Prix Dallas champion.  
  
**What deck did you play and why?** Black-Green Delirium; I didn't like any of the blue decks.  
  
**What sideboard card was the most important against the field? Why?** [Transgress the Mind](https://web.archive.org/web/20161023000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transgress+the+Mind). Everyone goes bigger after sideboarding.  
  
**After this weekend would you play this deck again? What would you change?** Yes. I'd cut the Nissas for literally anything else.  
  
**If you were competing in the Inventor's Fair, what would you bring?** Mr. Potato Head

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppro16/t8-maxim.jpg)Name:** Maxim Belanger

**Age:** 26  
  
**Occupation:** Music teacher  
  
**Hometown:** Sherbrooke, Canada  
  
**Previous Magic accomplishments:** Played in Pro Tour Dragon's Maze. Beat Ricky Chin a couple of times.  
  
**What deck did you play and why?** Black-Green Delirium. I'm really a Golgari player, and I liked the way I can interact with everything. Also, Liliana is love.  
  
**What sideboard card was the most important against the field? Why?** The third [Ishkanah, Grafwidow](https://web.archive.org/web/20161023000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ishkanah%2C+Grafwidow) saved me against White-Blue and vehicles.  
  
**After this weekend would you play this deck again? What would you change?** I'd definitely play the exact same 75 again.  
  
**If you were competing in the Inventor's Fair, what would you bring?** Some spicy time traveling machine.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppro16/t8-kiihne.jpg)Name:** Zachary Kiihne  
  
**Age:** 19  
  
**Occupation:** Student at UMass Amherst  
  
**Hometown:** Concord, MA  
  
**Previous Magic accomplishments:**  
  
**What deck did you play and why?** White-Blue Flash. Four lists went 9-1 at the Pro Tour and it is similar to Green-White Tokens and Bant Company from past Standard seasons, both of which were decks I enjoyed playing.  
  
**What sideboard card was the most important against the field? Why?** [Declaration in Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20161023000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Declaration+in+Stone). It is great in the Black-Green Delirium matchup and against Zombies, both of which are tough matchups.  
  
**After this weekend would you play this deck again? What would you change?** I would definitely play this deck going forward, but I would cut the [Ceremonious Rejection](https://web.archive.org/web/20161023000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ceremonious+Rejection)s for a [Fumigate](https://web.archive.org/web/20161023000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fumigate) and another [Declaration in Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20161023000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Declaration+in+Stone). I'd cut the [Rattlechains](https://web.archive.org/web/20161023000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rattlechains) for two Thalia.  
  
**If you were competing in the Inventor's Fair, what would you bring?** A computer. They're pretty amazing.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppro16/t8-manfield.jpg)Name:** Seth Manfield  
  
**Age:** 26

**Hometown:** Laurel, MD

**What sideboard card was the most important against the field? Why?** [Natural State](https://web.archive.org/web/20161023000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Natural+State); blowing up a Copter or [Stasis Snare](https://web.archive.org/web/20161023000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stasis+Snare) is great.  
  
**After this weekend would you play this deck again? What would you change?** I would play the same 75 moving forward.  
  
**If you were competing in the Inventor's Fair, what would you bring?** Lots of artifacts! And a cow.
