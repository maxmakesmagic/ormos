### ...At Gen Con SoCal 2004

That's right, at [Gen Con SoCal](https://web.archive.org/web/20041201000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.gencon.com/socalhome.aspx?file=socal&origin=mtgcom_arcana_703), Mark Rosewater will be donning a donkey suit and presiding over a massive *Unhinged* Tournament! If you're in Southern California or think you can get there in the next few days, you won't want to miss it!

<table><tbody><tr><td><img alt="Mark Rosewater in a chicken suit at Gen Con 1998" src="https://i.ormos.uk/3c/b4/3cb4c806fe345fe60b8743a4f2cdd01a20bd940bd716f1e455b073cd2d9c51d0.jpg" title="Mark Rosewater in a chicken suit at Gen Con 1998"><br><i>Mark Rosewater in a chicken suit, Gen Con '98</i></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

People are still talking about Mark's epic chicken suit from the *Unglued* release. Do you want to be left out of the stories and anecdotes this time around? Of course you don't!

And Gen Con SoCal isn't just *Unhinged*! It's the best four days in gaming, featuring qualifiers for Pro Tour Atlanta *and* Pro Tour Nagoya, a **Star Wars TCG** Tournament where you could win your very own lightsaber, and [much, much more](https://web.archive.org/web/20041201000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.gencon.com/displaysocal.aspx?file=socal-event-highlights&origin=mtgcom_arcana_703)!

So what are you waiting for?
