The Orzhov love their stained glass; it can be seen on a number of cards from *Gatecrash*, such as the most obvious example:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/87/a9/87a93bd1161b1b2de57b511f34cbba38eafc3e429448c50ca03a08e80424a0bb.jpg)

It also inspires the magic of the Orzhov, such as is visible on [Gift of Orzhova](https://web.archive.org/web/20130403000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gift+of+Orzhova).

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/mtg/images/daily/arcana/1203_gifts.jpg)

But obviously stained glass has appeared in **Magic** cards for many years. Take for example the *Portal Second Age* card [Temple Acolyte](https://web.archive.org/web/20130403000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temple+Acolyte).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/dd/be/ddbe3cfc875af587e00ccef769dbb21bd45a1b914c38f8f5bf231a4c0300d56e.jpg)

Perhaps the currently most famous piece of stained glass in **Magic** is the terrifying view on [Endless Ranks of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20130403000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Endless+Ranks+of+the+Dead).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1d/54/1d54573ed63fc1b1d843ff92fc2fdbc3e6447bb1b724db3970ec984e390cec72.jpg)

Or the more abstract [Halcyon Glaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20130403000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Halcyon+Glaze). Halcyon is a term that refers to a period in the past which was idyllic and happy. Not that that necessarily explains this beautiful art.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5f/36/5f36a716610ca31cadd3fba0712c65a64dbb923998c84eab58b5f3fbae960915.jpg)

To the much more recent example [Fires of Undeath](https://web.archive.org/web/20130403000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fires+of+Undeath), which features a more monochromatic stained glass example.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e9/87/e987915d4694b5f0da4f7254f97d0f90ba91c807abcff14a8a267f7b58c418a5.jpg)

There are a number of other examples of cards featuring stained glass. Have a favorite I don't include here? [Tweet me](http://twitter.com/trickmtg) or send me a feedback email below!
