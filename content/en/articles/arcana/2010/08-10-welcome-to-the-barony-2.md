Say, have you been wondering about [Barony Vampire](https://web.archive.org/web/20100810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barony+Vampire)? Wondering where she could be, who she is with, and all that sort of thing? Wondering, specifically, what this Barony is?

![Barony Vampire](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Barony+Vampire)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/28/60/2860c7ff14cafe48beeebc18c72efe436cd2c2627f1c1da1bf60f82b9ec310e2.jpg)

[Barony Vampire](https://web.archive.org/web/20100810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barony+Vampire) | Art by [Daarken](https://web.archive.org/web/20100810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Daarken%22%5D)

Well, if you played *Planechase*, you're probably way ahead of us. But don't tell anybody the answer. We wouldn't want to spoil the surprise that's coming up just after the end of ... this ... sentence.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ed/bc/edbc3073ae71a4dd4bb317eaf51de6690dca06378e6bcef8dbd358e970ef4519.jpg)

Of course, the story doesn't start with *Planechase*, because the barony in question is under the thrall of none other than [Baron Sengir](https://web.archive.org/web/20100810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baron+Sengir)!
