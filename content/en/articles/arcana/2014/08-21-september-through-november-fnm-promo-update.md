Tomorrow (or even today, depending on your part of the world and when you read this) is that glorious day of the week we like to call Friday (or Freitag, Vendreidi, or Viernes, if you're feeling international). And with Friday comes [Friday Night *Magic*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-event-types-events-2).

And with Friday Night *Magic* comes FNM Promos! Let's take a look at the promos you can pick up at FNM over the next few months.

# September

Add white staple [Banishing Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banishing+Light) to your collection in September.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/kjsdhfucn0_arc20140627_p.jpg)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/cardart/cardart_banishinglight_prm.jpg)

Banishing Light | Art by [Wesley Burt](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5bWesley%5d+%5bBurt%5d)

# October

Go a little bit crazy in October with [Fanatic of Xenagos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fanatic+of+Xenagos).

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/arc20140725_fnmprm.jpg)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/cardart/cardart_fanaticofxenagos_prm.jpg)

Fanatic of Xenagos | Art by [Zoltan Boros](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5bZoltan%5d+%5bBoros%5d)

# November

And if [Brain Maggot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brain+Maggot) made you squirm before, wait until you see the new art from Dave Kendall on November's promo card.
