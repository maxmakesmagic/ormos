As you might have read in Savor the Flavor, each of the three Eldrazi titans has its own "brood lineage" of foul spawn, and the [three lineages](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/stf/88) have different physical characteristics. And as you might have noticed, there are three different Eldrazi Spawn tokens in *Rise of the Eldrazi*—one for each lineage—and many of the cards that create Eldrazi Spawn reference one of the titans or its lineage, either explicitly or obliquely.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/02/d3/02d3607136ed9c6e052760a3b8ff8213026e63f2ea86e6d4146f3b13724b52ef.jpg)*From left: Eldrazi Spawn of the Ulamog, Emrakul, and Kozilek lineages*

 

When you're actually playing, you probably just grab whatever tokens are handy—and for most people, that's fine. If you're a flavor goob, though, some part of you longs to root around for the *right* Eldrazi Spawn tokens for the lineage in question. In some cases it's obvious which lineage that is; in others, it's less obvious. Fortunately, between the card names, the art descriptions, and the *Rise of the Eldrazi* style guide, **Magic** Arcana can help!

The mighty [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Emrakul%252C%2Bthe%2BAeons%2BTorn) gets the short end of the stick by a long shot, with only two cards that create tokens of its lineage: the obvious [Emrakul's Hatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%27s+Hatcher), plus [Nest Invader](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nest+Invader), which clearly shows the baleen-like fleshy lattice of the [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Emrakul%252C%2Bthe%2BAeons%2BTorn)-lineage spawn. (As a consolation prize to the lineage, [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Emrakul%252C%2Bthe%2BAeons%2BTorn) itself appears in art [Gravitational Shift](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Gravitational%2BShift) [Time of Heroes](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Time%2Bof%2BHeroes) [Eldrazi Conscription](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Eldrazi%2BConscription) [Consume the Meek](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Consume%2Bthe%2BMeek).)

The [Ulamog, the Infinite Gyre](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Ulamog%252C%2Bthe%2BInfinite%2BGyre) lineage, with their fleshy tentacles and featureless, skull-like heads, appear not only on the eponymous [Pawn of Ulamog](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pawn+of+Ulamog) and [Spawnsire of Ulamog](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spawnsire+of+Ulamog), but also on [Brood Birthing](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brood+Birthing) and [Growth Spasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Growth+Spasm). The spawn in the art of [Awakening Zone](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Awakening+Zone) also look a lot like Ulamog-lineage spawn—note the tentacles and featureless heads—although the art description doesn't specify.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/24/0c/240c4b5bcae7cf0a54f728796e49eeae4f2af5a71571b5cf2b8885f7843e4c01.jpg)

[Awakening Zone](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Awakening+Zone) | Art by [Johann Bodin](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Johann+Bodin%22%5D)

The winner of the bunch is the [Kozilek, Butcher of Truth](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Kozilek%252C%2BButcher%2Bof%2BTruth) lineage, whose spawn appear in the art of [Kozilek's Predator](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kozilek%27s+Predator), [Dread Drone](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dread+Drone), [Corpsehatch](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Corpsehatch), [Rapacious One](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rapacious+One), and [Spawning Breath](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spawning+Breath). Although the Kozilek-lineage Eldrazi Spawn token shows off the characteristic branching limbs and strangely placed eyes of its lineage, the spawn in the art of [Essence Feed](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Essence+Feed) have the winglike obsidian structures that are also an identifying mark of the Kozilek lineage.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/40/83/40831f7aff6eb4b1359966817ed2226957f5af65ee7134b53be4fe0a9b9518c2.jpg)

[Essence Feed](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Essence+Feed) | Art by [Daarken](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Daarken%22%5D)

That leaves one more card that makes Eldrazi Spawn, which at first we weren't sure how to classify: [Skittering Invasion](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skittering+Invasion). The art description doesn't specify the lineage, and the spawn in the art don't look like they belong to particular lineages at first glance. But then we took a closer look:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fe/b1/feb1170d933ab893a1b483ed482d02db9eaee31d05e01aae5019fb85e08e170a.jpg)

[Skittering Invasion](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skittering+Invasion) | Art by [Eric Deschamps](https://web.archive.org/web/20100615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Eric+Deschamps%22%5D)

It's actually a *mix* of lineages, pouring out of the sea in a horrifying tide of multihued flesh. We count three Ulamog-lineage spawn (the one in the foreground on the left, plus the two on the shoreline), one Emrakul-lineage spawn (out in the water), and one Kozilek-lineage spawn (in the foreground on the right).
