Alexander Hayne looked to be a long shot when Sunday dawned at Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored*. But when your deck features 16 miracles, anything is possible. A thrilling run through the Top 8 with victories over all-time great Jon Finkel, Joshua Cho, and Gaudenis Vidugiris secured the title of Pro Tour champion, the lead seat on the Canadian national team at this summer’s World **Magic** Cup, a spot in the **Magic** Players Championship, and Rookie of the Year honors for Hayne.
