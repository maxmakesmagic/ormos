If you're on the lookout for interesting and slightly offbeat decks, here's one that should tickle your fancy.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Tarmogoyf)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf)

[Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant)...[Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf)...[Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic)...These are all cards everyone is familiar with, and it's not unusual to see any of them in a Legacy decklist, it's not every day that one sees them in the *same* decklist.

This 3rd-place deck of Kikuchi's could be called "Abzan Good Stuff." It has some of the best cards from each of those colors, including [Swords to Plowshares](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swords+to+Plowshares) and [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay); two of the best removal spells in the format; and spices the mix with [Mirri's Guile](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirri%27s+Guile) and some Planeswalkers.

The [Spirit of the Labyrinth](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spirit+of+the+Labyrinth) casually sitting in the main deck is a great foil to the strategy currently popular in Legacy of using cheap draw spells to build up to [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise). The single [Enlightened Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enlightened+Tutor) in the sideboard is also quite clever, as it essentially acts as a third copy of [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle), [Grafdigger's Cage](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grafdigger%27s+Cage), and [Engineered Plague](https://web.archive.org/web/20141112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engineered+Plague), saving space for additional cards.
