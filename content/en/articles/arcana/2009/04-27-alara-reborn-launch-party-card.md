Did you know that [*Alara Reborn* Launch Parties](https://web.archive.org/web/20090427000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prereleases) start on *Thursday*, not Friday? It's true! And you can thank Labor Day for that. Not the US Labor Day. The other one. Ask a European.

Anyway, because the Launch Parties are a day earlier than usual, we're showing you the Launch Party promo card... *right now!*
