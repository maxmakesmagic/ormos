![](http://www.wizards.com/tournaments/images/nationals.gif)Magic Online Roundtable with Justin Ziran, Brand Manager, & Michael “elf” Feuell, Senior Programmer.

When: 2:00 pm – 4:00 pm Saturday, July 29th 2006 at the Magic the Gathering™ Nationals event in Atlanta Georgia

**Cobb Galleria Center**  
 Concourse C & D  
 Room 102  
 Two Galleria Parkway  
 Atlanta, Georgia 30339  
 (770) 955-8000  
[www.cobbgalleria.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20060720000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.cobbgalleria.com&origin=magic_magiconline_article072006)

Do you play Magic Online? Want to put a face to all your buddy list entries? Set up a clan? Trade? Then join us for the first Magic Online™ Roundtable Discussion at Nationals in Atlanta, Georgia.

I'll be talking about the current state of the game – How is Magic Online doing? What are our short term plans for Magic Online? What are the long term plans for Magic Online? How does that affect you, the player?

I'll also be showing Magic Online 3 and discussing what our goals and objectives are for the project. 

**Pre-register and then show up at the Roundtable event and you'll get a Magic Online coupon worth $4.00 (NOTE: You must pre-register via the online form below)**. Hang around until the end and you'll be entered in to a raffle for 1 of 10 $100.00 Magic Online coupons and a 30 GB Apple IPOD Video. Contest rules can be found [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20060720000000/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/article072006rules).
