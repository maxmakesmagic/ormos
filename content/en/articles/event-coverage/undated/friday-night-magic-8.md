To make it easier to participate, Friday Night **Magic** events are open to anyone and feature the Standard Constructed, Sealed Deck or Booster Draft formats. Stores may also choose to run one Two-Headed Giant tournament (Standard or Sealed) each month. Running events in these formats makes it easier for new players to participate, and you only have to remember three things: Pack your Standard deck; remember where the tournament is happening, and what time it starts. You can find out more about each format [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20100619105103/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Resources.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/resources/formats-sanctioned).

### Tournament Information

The DCI is giving players in a growing number of countries and stores the opportunity to play in a fun weekly event and have a shot at cool FOIL prize cards in the process. FNM is designed to keep the play style casual: 

*   FNM events have significantly less impact on players' ratings than most tournaments
*   Prize cards are available to participants in other ways than just to the winners
*   For rules enforcement, the emphasis is on teaching players the correct ways to play

Stores around the world are running Friday Night **Magic** nearly every week. Use the Event Locator at the top of this page to find a location near you. If you have questions about Friday Night **Magic** in Europe, send us an e-mail at dcieurope@hasbro.co.uk. In the rest of the world, please email us through the [Customer Service Help Center](https://web.archive.org/web/20100603033805/http://www.wizards.com/customerservice).

### Friday Night Magic Rules

*   1st and 2nd place receive one prize card each.
*   Door prize: The tournament organizer will randomly award the remaining two prize cards to two players that did not place first or second.
*   FNM events are 8k tournaments and have a Regular Rules Enforcement Level (REL).
*   Stores do not have to run FNM tournaments each week, so contact the store in advance be sure they are running FNM on the date you're planning on visiting.
*   All 4 prize cards associated with each Friday Night **Magic** event must be awarded to the players.
*   Friday Night **Magic** events can only be run on Fridays.
