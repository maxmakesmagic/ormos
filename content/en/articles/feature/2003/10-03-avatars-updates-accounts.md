### Multiple Accounts

A question about sharing accounts came up on the Message Boards this week, and there seems to be some confusion about the "official" stance on this issue.

Wizards of the Coast frowns on players sharing accounts -- mostly because the original owner is the one who, in the end, is responsible for what that account is used for. Will we actively hunt you down and peck out your eyes for letting someone else play on your account? Probably not. But if you allow someone else to use your account, realize that you're responsible for that person's behavior.

This also ties into the subject of buying someone's account. You see, the history of the account goes with it -- no matter who uses it. If an account is associated with abusive or illegal behavior, the new "owner" of an account might make a simple mistake and lose the account. Since we don't want problem accounts being passed off on unsuspecting customers, we do try to stop this behavior.

Also, because an account is always associated with the person who purchased it, the account may be penalized for what happens on another of the original owner's accounts. If behavior on one account is bad enough to get that account terminated, any other accounts owned by the person who purchased the account may also be terminated.

### Chuck's Virtual Party

Man, was it nice to announce this. I'm excited that we're able to do something big for everyone to show our appreciation for your support. Make sure you take the time to vote for the three pre-*Invasion* cards you want available in ***Magic** Online*. We'll be taking votes through Wednesday, October 8 -- I'll announce the results sometime after that.

There was a bit of confusion about logging on to the website to participate in the survey. The user name and password aren't associated with ***Magic** Online* -- they're specifically for the website. If you've never taken a survey before or logged on at [MagicTheGathering.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20041216090459/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/welcome), you can register for a user name [**here**](https://web.archive.org/web/20041204211828/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=company/person/register).

If you're one of those wondering why we didn't choose some of the wackier, cool old **Magic** cards, it has to do with programming. Because of the way cards are added to ***Magic** Online,* each time new mechanics and rules are programmed, some older cards may start working. Hence, all twelve of the possible cards already "work" on ***Magic** Online,* and require little or no additional programming.
