The *Lorwyn* and *Shadowmoor* blocks' Kithkin tribe has brought a resurgence of a popular **Magic** archetype: White Weenie. But long before the Kithkin hordes came 1996 World Champion Tom Chanpheng. He used his White Weenie deck to cut through a field of Necropotence players to win the championship title. And he did it after a deck registration error forced him to play with a Sleight of Mind in his sideboard he couldn't actually use!
