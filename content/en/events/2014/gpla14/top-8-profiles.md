### Brad Nelson

**Age:** 28  
**Hometown:** Roanoke, Virginia  
**Occupation:** Content Producer for StarCityGames.com  
  

**Favorite clan (Temur, Abzan, Jeskai, Sultai, Mardu)**  
Temur!!! 

 **(Other) previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
2 Pro Tour Top 8s, 9 Grand Prix, Player of the Year 

**What Standard deck are you playing, and why did you choose it?**  
Mardu Midrange. [Butcher of the Horde](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Butcher+of+the+Horde) does some pretty absurd things. 

**What are your best and worst matchups?**  
Best: Jeskai, Worst: Sultai Delve \[Sidisi Whip\]things. 

**What card won you the most matches this weekend?**  
[Hordeling Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hordeling+Outburst) probably won them all. 

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**  
I don't know yet. I will be changing strategies once people realize just how busted three goblin tokens can be. 

**If you could meet any person from history, who would it be?**  
Arcanis, the Omnipotent.

### Chris Goldsmith

**Age:** 33  
**Hometown:** San Diego, California  
**Occupation:** Systems Engineer  
  

**Favorite clan (Temur, Abzan, Jeskai, Sultai, Mardu)**  
Abzan 

 **(Other) previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
N/A 

**What Standard deck are you playing, and why did you choose it?**  
Abzan, The deck had the most success in the recent Pro Tour and Abzan performed well at my local store since the release. 

**What are your best and worst matchups?**  
Best: Temur Tempo, Worst: Big Burn/Hasty Flyers 

**What card won you the most matches this weekend?**  
 Tied between [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino), [Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc), and Elspeth Sun's Champion, of course. 

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**  
Sideboard, mostly. Felt the [Duneblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duneblast) wasn't what I wanted, maybe too greedy. Games needing rests could be done at a lower cost. Few other changes. 

**If you could meet any person from history, who would it be?**  
George Washington

### Denis Ulanov

**Age:** 27  
**Hometown:** Samara, Russia  
**Occupation:** Software Engineer  
  

**Favorite clan (Temur, Abzan, Jeskai, Sultai, Mardu)**  
Abzan 

 **(Other) previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
a few StarCityGames Open Top 8s 

**What Standard deck are you playing, and why did you choose it?**  
Mono-Red Sligh, a.k.a., "Red Army." The deck was outstanding during testing and this is the kind of deck I like to play. 

**What are your best and worst matchups?**  
Most of the popular matches are favorable, including Abzan and Jeskai. The worst is probably Mardu Midrange. 

**What card won you the most matches this weekend?**  
[Hammerhand](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hammerhand), [Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames), and [Goblin Rabblemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Rabblemaster) 

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**  
I would not play [Blinding Flare](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinding+Flare). The rest of the deck is pretty insane. 

**If you could meet any person from history, who would it be?**  
My wife is the person I would like to meet, others are not even close. Even the ones from history.
