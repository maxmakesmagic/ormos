Here's an offer sure to appeal to every player: The first 20 (30 for Advanced locations) people who preorder or purchase a Magic: The Gathering® Journey into Nyx™ booster box in your retail store, will get an Alternate Art Premium promo card! But hurry, this offer is limited to **ONLY** the first 20 (or 30) people per store who have either preordered or purchase a full booster box!
