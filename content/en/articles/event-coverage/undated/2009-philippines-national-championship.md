### Invitation List

PHILIPPINES Nationals Invitation list comprises the following:

*   The current PHILIPPINES National Champion and members of the current PHILIPPINES national team (including alternate).
*   Players from the PHILIPPINES with Pro Tour Players Club level 2 or higher.
*   Winners of invitations through Regional Championships.
*   Winners of invitations through PHILIPPINES National Qualifiers.
*   The Top 75 DCI Composite–ranked players from the PHILIPPINES on June 10, 2009.
*   The Top 75 DCI Total–ranked players from the PHILIPPINES on June 10, 2009.

All players on the list below are already qualified for PHILIPPINES Nationals and are not permitted to play in PHILIPPINES Regional Championships and/or PHILIPPINES Nationals Qualifier.
