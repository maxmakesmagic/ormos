The 2001 World Championships in Toronto, Canada saw Jan Tomcani make the Top 8 playing Dark Fires. Based on [Fires of Yavimaya](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fires+of+Yavimaya) and adding a splash of black, Jan's deck was the same archetype that had dominated Standard for months.
