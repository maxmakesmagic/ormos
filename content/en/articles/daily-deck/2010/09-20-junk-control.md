*Scars of Mirrodin* is almost here! That means a host of new cards to build new decks with. This week's Daily Decks will examine some of the top decks in Standard and ask you how you would change them with the upcoming cards from *Scars of Mirrodin*. Like this black, green, and white Fauna Shaman deck. What cards from the new set do you think will make this deck better? Check out the previews we've seen so far on the [Visual Spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20101122090603/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/scarsofmirrodin/spoiler).
