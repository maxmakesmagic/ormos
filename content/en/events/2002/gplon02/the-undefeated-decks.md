After 8 rounds of play four decks remained unbeaten. While the format saw a switch to new design types like the blue-black Upheaval/Infestation decks preferred by some of the pro’s it was mono-black control that once again delivered the goods. Three of the four unbeaten decks chose this strategy while the fourth was a black-blue Braids beatdown deck.

Gérard Garcia
