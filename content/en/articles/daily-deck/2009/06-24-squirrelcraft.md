Elfball is an age-old strategy for **Magic**, and it saw a resurgence in Extended this past PTQ season. Now it’s seeing new life in the world of **Magic Online** Classic, finishing second in a recent Premier Event. An added twist unique to the format? The Earthcraft/Squirrel Nest combo. Simply play Earthcraft, then play Squirrel Nest. Each Squirrel you create with your land will be able to untap a land. Untap the land you used to make the first Squirrel, and make another! Rinse, wash, and repeat to Squirrel your opponents to death.
