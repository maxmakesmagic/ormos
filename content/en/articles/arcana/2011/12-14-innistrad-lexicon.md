Welcome to the *Innistrad* edition of the Lexicon, in which we explore the meanings of some of the more unusual words we use in the names of those cards. While many offbeat names in **Magic** (like "moldgraf") are simply made up, we often dig around dictionaries for real little-used and archaic words for inspiration.

* * *

 ![Abattoir Ghoul](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Abattoir%20Ghoul&options=) 

**ab•at•toir**  
 *noun* 

a slaughterhouse.

  

* * *

 ![Ulvenwald Primordials](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Ulvenwald%20Primordials&options=) 

**pri•mor•di•al**  
 *noun* 

1.  constituting a beginning; giving origin to something derived or developed; original; elementary: *primordial forms of life*.
2.  *Embryology.* first formed.
3.  pertaining to or existing at or from the very beginning: *primordial matter.*

  

* * *

 ![Bloodcrazed Neonate](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Bloodcrazed%20Neonate&options=) 

**ne•o•nate**  
 *noun* 

a newborn child, or one in its first 28 days.

  

* * *

 ![Paraselene](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Paraselene&options=) 

**par•a•se•le•ne**  
 *noun* 

a bright moonlike spot on a lunar halo; a mock moon. 

  

* * *

 ![Trepanation Blade](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Trepanation%20Blade&options=) 

**tre•pan**  
 *noun* 

1.  a tool for cutting shallow holes by removing a core.
2.  *Surgery.* an obsolete form of the trephine resembling a carpenter's bit and brace.

  

* * *

 ![Morkrut Banshee](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Morkrut%20Banshee&options=) 

**ban•shee**  
 *noun* 

(in Irish folklore) a spirit in the form of a wailing woman who appears to or is heard by members of a family as a sign that one of them is about to die.
