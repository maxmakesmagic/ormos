Joekewwl, a.k.a. tireless beta tester Joe Dillard, piloted this aggressive mono-red deck to a 3-0 finish in the Unified Modern portion of last week's **Magic Online** Community Cup. Modern is a format created specifically for the Cup that uses cards in expansions from *Mirrodin* forward and core sets from *Eighth Edition* forward, and the decks from this event give an interesting glimpse into how such a format might shape up. You can read more about Modern [here](/en/articles/latestdevelopments/2011/05-27-a-modern-proposal), or check out the Community Cup coverage, including both teams' Modern deck lists, [here](/en/events/2011/mtgoccup11/community-team-makes-it-three-in-a-row).
