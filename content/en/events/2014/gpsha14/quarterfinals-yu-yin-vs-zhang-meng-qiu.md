Both players are playing in their first Grand Prix Top 8 and were clearly very excited to qualify for Pro Tour Fate Reforged. Zhang Meng Qiu is one of the best from Hong Kong, while Yu Yin has a National Champion title under his belt. After a little friendly banter, both players commenced their match.

# Game 1

Zhang opened with [Heir of the Wilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heir+of+the+Wilds) and used [Debilitating Injury](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Debilitating+Injury) to kill an opposing [Mardu Hateblade](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Hateblade). He refilled his hand and filled his graveyard with [Bitter Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitter+Revelation). [Scout the Borders](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scout+the+Borders) added five additional cards to his graveyard, allowing him to cast [Hooting Mandrills](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hooting+Mandrills) (for the bargain price of 2 mana) on Turn 5. 

Yu could only manage [Disowned Ancestor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disowned+Ancestor) and a morph, but he buffed his army with [Abzan Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Ascendancy). It looked like he was trailing behind, until he added [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) to the board. 

Zhang tried to find a solution with a second [Bitter Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitter+Revelation), and it seemed like he was in trouble. [Deprive](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deprive)d of any blue mana, it seemed like he was unable to deploy some of the powerful spells in his hand. He found [Murderous Cut](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Murderous+Cut) to deal with the rhino, but [Ivorytusk Fortress](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ivorytusk+Fortress) was too much for Zhang to handle and he bowed down to the mighty elephant. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QF-Yu-Yin.jpg)

 *Yu Yin wields the power of [Abzan Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Ascendancy) and [Ivorytusk Fortress](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ivorytusk+Fortress).* 

  

**Yu Yin 1 – Zhang Meng Qiu 0** 

# Game 2

Zhang opted to play once again and tried to apply pressure quickly. [Heir of the Wilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heir+of+the+Wilds) and [Rakshasa Deathdealer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Deathdealer) were held back by Yu's [Kheru Bloodsucker](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kheru+Bloodsucker), [Ainok Bond-Kin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ainok+Bond-Kin) and [Salt Road Patrol](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Salt+Road+Patrol). 

But that didn't prevent [Abomination of Gudul](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abomination+of+Gudul) from flying in. Zhang triggered its ability and agonized over whether to discard [Throttle](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Throttle), [Murderous Cut](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Murderous+Cut) or [Dead Drop](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dead+Drop). He eventually pitched the most expensive spell, using [Throttle](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Throttle) to kill [Abzan Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Guide), leaving up a single mana just in case he needed to cast [Murderous Cut](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Murderous+Cut). 

It turns out that he didn't need it at all. With the biggest threat removed and the board gummed up, it was the mighty [Abomination of Gudul](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abomination+of+Gudul) that went all the way. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QF-Zhang-Meng-Qiu.jpg)

 *Zhang equalizes the score with [Abomination of Gudul](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abomination+of+Gudul) and his powerful Delve cards.* 

  

# Game 3

Yu offered to let Zhang play first despite losing the previous game. It turned out to be a good decision, since Zhang was forced to mulligan down to 5.

Yu was relentless, curving out nicely with [Highland Game](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Highland+Game), [Mardu Skullhunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Skullhunter) and [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino). Fortunately, Zhang had [Disowned Ancestor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disowned+Ancestor) to buy him a little time while he Scouted the Borders twice. 

He found [Rakshasa Deathdealer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Deathdealer) and [Hooting Mandrills](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hooting+Mandrills) but Yu was beating him down with [Gurmag Swiftwing](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gurmag+Swiftwing) and [Sultai Scavenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sultai+Scavenger). 

The duo dealt 4 damage.

And then 4 more.

Another 4.

A topdecked [Awaken the Bear](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Awaken+the+Bear) shortened the clock and Zhang extended his hand. 

**Yu Yin 2 – Zhang Meng Qiu 1** 

**Yu Yin defeats Zhang Meng Qiu and advances to the semifinals!**
