*Where do you usually play **Magic**?* 

![](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/authorpics/authorpic_stevewarner.jpg) **Steve Warner:** Typically the times I get to play **Magic** outside of work, it's down in the café with friends that visit, or on **Magic Online**. There is a store nearby, Uncle's Games in Southcenter mall, that is trying to fire Tuesday unsanctioned events so Wizards employees can play. If that happens I will probably play more there than I do now.
