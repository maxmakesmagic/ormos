**Mike Sutfin** has been working as a professional Illustrator since he graduated with a B.F.A. in 1997. His work has appeared on several **Magic: The Gathering** trading cards and products, covers and interiors of roleplaying games such as **Dungeons & Dragons** and Shadowrun, as wellas, covers for Dragon and Duelist magazines. In 2001, Lucasfilm Ltd. and Randomhouse Books chose Mike to illustrate 138 color pieces for *Star Wars: New Essential Guide to Characters.* This publication was the first in the series of guidebooks to appear on the New York Times Bestseller list. Mike's recent projects have focused on satisfying his desire to pursue personal interests. He has designed skateboard decks, wheels, and t-shirts for companies like Black Label, Foundation, Hollywood, Legacy, and Pig. Being a long-time musician, Mike has illustrated record covers for various punk and hardcore bands in addition to his own bands (Charles Bronson, The Killers). Currently, Mike is living and painting in San Diego with his girlfriend and their four cats.
