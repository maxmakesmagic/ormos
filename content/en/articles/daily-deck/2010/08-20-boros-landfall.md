[China has a new champion](/en/events/2010/chnat10/lu-crowned-champ-in-china)—however, Lu Chao's deck archetype is anything but. He piloted Boros Landfall, earning a triumphant victory amidst a slew of post-M11 newcomers like Naya Fauna Shaman and Titan Force. While the world scrambled to meet ***Magic** 2011*'s impact on the environment, Lu Chao honed last year's red-white aggro list without using a single M11 card!
