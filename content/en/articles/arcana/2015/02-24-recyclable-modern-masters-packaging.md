We're doing something new and pretty cool for the upcoming *Modern Masters* *2015 Edition*, and I don't just mean reprinting Modern-format staples in an awesome draft environment. Although we're doing that too.

What I do mean is that we're exploring a recyclable packaging material for our booster products with this year's *Modern Masters* release.

The packaging, the back of which you can see below (we’re not ready to show you the front just yet), is 100% Forest Stewardship Council-certified paperboard, which is a very official sounding way of saying the booster packaging can be recycled just like any other piece of cardboard.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/ARC20150224_BoosterDeck.jpg)

We'll only be rolling out this recyclable product in English version of the product. Chinese (Simplified) and Japanese product will be sold in the traditional flow wrap.
