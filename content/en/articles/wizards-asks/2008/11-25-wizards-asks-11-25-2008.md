*What's your favorite piece of **Magic** art?* 

![Tom Jenkot](https://i.ormos.uk/e7/ec/e7ec95d2a4e03683ae8a3a1fefb8126f5743dd3fbd0a4666048b357bff99db4f.jpg "Tom Jenkot")

**Tom Jenkot:** My earliest creative memories are of trying to sell a most definitely hurried rendition of a King Kong movie poster for a quarter when I was five. (I don't talk to my Uncle Terry anymore.) My mother helped me learn to draw, I went to art school, and I have a job in a creative field, but I never managed to keep up with the drawing / painting aspect so I'm left feeling jealous every time I see a new set.

Seeing the tiny art on the cards is cool, seeing it blown up in Arcana inspires envy, and peering at the originals from mere inches induces envy-laden drool. While it's almost impossible to pick one card, I don't have days to mull over my favorite. So here is one of them:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/76/2a/762a1aabbcd4b69561f8d3014a0172d0ee7d87df7cf034d757873b765ce59cc9.jpg) 

*Serra Angel art by Rebecca Guay* 

It might not be a commonly known piece because it never appeared on a card you could play. The only time this has seen print was on a 6"x9" oversized card, but that doesn't mean it's not cool. In fact, it might make it even cooler.
