The *Planeshift* set featured a cycle of rare global enchantments with "Planeswalker" in their name. They're capable of generating repeating effects based on the contents of another player's (another planeswalker's) hand. Each card in the cycle featured a different planeswalker from the **Magic** storyline in its art.

### White: Planeswalker's Mirth

The white enchantment in the cycle shows Commodore Guff chuckling his way through a lifegain effect.

[![Planeswalker's Mirth](http://archive.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/PlaneswalkersMirth.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071022000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Planeswalker%2527s%2BMirth) *Planeswalker's Mirth art by John Matson*

### Blue: Planeswalker's Mischief

The blue one shows Bo Levar firing off a mischievous blue effect.

[![Planeswalker's Mischief](http://archive.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/PlaneswalkersMischief.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071022000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Planeswalker%2527s%2BMischief) *Planeswalker's Mischief art by Pete Venters*

### Black: Planeswalker's Scorn

You can see Tevesh Szat performing some anti-personnel necromantic magic on the black one.

[![Planeswalker's Scorn](http://archive.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/PlaneswalkersScorn.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071022000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Planeswalker%2527s%2BScorn) *Planeswalker's Scorn art by Glen Angus*

### Red: Planeswalker's Fury

The red one shows the planeswalker Lord Windgrace doing fiery battle with a Phyrexian minion.

[![Planeswalker's Fury](http://archive.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/PlaneswalkersFury.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071022000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Planeswalker%2527s%2BFury) *Planeswalker's Fury art by Christopher Moeller*

### Green: Planeswalker's Favor

Finally, the green member of the cycle shows Freyalise conjuring some creature-enhancing magic.

[![Planeswalker's Favor](http://archive.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/PlaneswalkersFavor.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071022000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Planeswalker%2527s%2BFavor) *Planeswalker's Favor art by Rebecca Guay*
