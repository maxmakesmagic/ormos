## Products

![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/images/featured/EN_BFZ_Magic_Booster_1.png)

# BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or mythic rare. Some packs even contain a premium foil card!

![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/images/featured/EN_BFZ_Magic_FatPack_2.png)

# FAT PACK

Includes a Player's Guide with pictures of all cards in the set as well as two full deck boxes, nine 15-card booster packs, 80 full-art basic land cards, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ae/a1/aea1e1c239275af4614b24930e6f7fc18cb788fef7bbd23197fe86b75e215882.png)

# INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 cards, a foil premium rare card, two sealed 15-card booster packs, a strategy insert, and a Magic learn-to-play guide.

![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/images/featured/EN_BFZ_Magic_EventDeck.png)

# EVENT DECK

Battle like the best, right out of the box. Includes a tournament-ready 60-card deck plus a 15-card "sideboard" to swap in as needed, depending on what you expect your competitor will play.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/bc/1e/bc1e49d311756b905f4f73daf4680f20f3f53d837d94a987430ffd65e4fbe0c2.png)

# BUY-A-BOX

Purchase a *Battle for Zendikar* booster box in-store and receive an alternate art premium promotional card, limit one per customer, while supplies last.
