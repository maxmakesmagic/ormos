As [mentioned before](/en/articles/arcana/2004/09-07-kami-energy-objects) on **magicthegathering.com**, kami creatures in the Kamigawa block all have floating "energy objects" in their art, as a visual cue that they belong to the spirit world.

What's the origin of that visual cue? Early in Kamigawa world-building, concept illustrator [Ittoku](https://web.archive.org/web/20050315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/default.asp?term=ittoku&fields=%7Cartist&format=Allsets&color=All&output=summary&sort=name&first=1) produced this sketch below. Note the floating heads along the right-hand side.

As Brady Dommermuth [reports](/en/articles/askwizards/2005/03-01-ask-wizards-march-2005) in today's Ask Wizards, that sketch became the inspiration for Myojin of Cleansing Fire *and* the energy objects in general.
