Joel Calafell started the 2009 World Championships off strong with a 6-0 start using a Turbo Fog deck designed to take advantage of a field filled with Jund. Early card draw through things like [Howling Mine](https://web.archive.org/web/20091123000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Howling+Mine) and [Jace Beleren](https://web.archive.org/web/20091123000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace+Beleren) keep his hand stocked with [Fog](https://web.archive.org/web/20091123000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fog)s in the form of [Safe Passage](https://web.archive.org/web/20091123000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Safe+Passage) and [Angelsong](https://web.archive.org/web/20091123000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angelsong). Before long his opponent is low on cards, and a timely [Archive Trap](https://web.archive.org/web/20091123000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archive+Trap) is all it takes to send the challenger packing! For more on the 2009 World Championships, [visit the official event coverage](/en/articles/event-coverage/2009/11-12-coverage-of-2009-world-championships).
