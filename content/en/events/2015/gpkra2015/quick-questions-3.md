### Which deck are you playing this weekend and why?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkra15/gpkra_qq_anteri.jpg)  
**Fabrizio Anteri:** "Abzan Control. I need to make Top 8 to reach Gold status, so I can't afford to play a deck that has bad match-ups. My other options were Esper or Green/Red devotion, but they both have some tough match-ups, so I went with the deck that has around 50/50 against everything."

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkra15/gpkra_qq_dickmann.jpg)  
**Patrick Dickmann:** "Bant midrange, like the one Craig Wescoe played at Pro Tour Dragons of Tarkir. It has the tools to beat all the decks I expect in the field."

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkra15/gpkra_qq_manfield.jpg)  
**Seth Manfield:** "Esper control. It's a really good deck and is especially strong against the Green midrange decks in the format."

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkra15/gpkra_qq_muller.jpg)  
**Martin Müller:** "Blue/Black dragons with a splash of White for [Dragonlord Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20150418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai). I think it was the best deck at the Pro Tour last weekend."
