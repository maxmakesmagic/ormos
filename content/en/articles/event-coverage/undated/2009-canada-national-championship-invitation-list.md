The Canadian Nationals Invitation list comprises the following:

*   The current Canadian National Champion and members of the current Canadian national team (including alternate).
*   Players from Canada with Pro Tour Players Club level 2 or higher.
*   Winners of invitations through Regional Championships.
*   Winners of invitations through Canadian National Open tournaments ("Grinders").
*   The Top 75 DCI Composite–ranked players from Canada on June 10, 2009.
*   The Top 75 DCI Total–ranked players from Canada on June 10, 2009.

All players on the list below are already qualified for Canadian Nationals and are not permitted to play in Canadian Regional Championships and/or Canadian Nationals Open Events ("Grinders").
