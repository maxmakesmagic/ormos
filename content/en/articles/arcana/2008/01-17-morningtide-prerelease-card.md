If you stopped by the [*Morningtide* Minisite](https://web.archive.org/web/20080117000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/morningtide/home) yesterday, you might have enjoyed Mark Gottlieb's *Morningtide*-themed crossword. If you did, you got to see a preview of Door of Destinies:

![Door of Destinies](https://i.ormos.uk/e2/00/e200f7b8b6a11e5df15dc743bc68933878dadd65bbe2e8185cfc158331649a07.jpg)  
![Regular](https://i.ormos.uk/40/4b/404bcde2b3aa9367664fc2529e8c5642aa4a444bb48ff6fcbda6568bcec0c6c4.jpg)

That looks pretty cool. But Door of Destinies is also going to be the [*Morningtide* prerelease](https://web.archive.org/web/20080117000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prereleases) promo card, which means you can pick one up this weekend simply by playing in the event.
