*What's your favorite Constructed deck of all time?* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2b/09/2b094d288b20f58968d51834eb02b2ed1381ae876700918a5c14f07004174e55.jpg)

**Mike Turian:** Pick just one! What a difficult question! Should I pick Angry Hermit? Slugageddon? The Car Deck? Green-White Maro? Squirrel-Opposition? They all have a special place in my heart, but I will pick [Red-Green Madness](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/mike-turian-magic-pro-tour-hall-of-fame). I loved the feel of drawing from the Red-Green Madness deck. 

I played hundreds of games with the deck. Wild Mongrel, Call of the Herd, Violent Eruption, and Basking Rootwalla gave the deck explosive starts.

Starting with a Wild Mongrel in your hand would unlock the sheer power of the deck. I loved the deck so much that when a reporter asked to take a picture of me with an opening hand, I took the green-red deck and constructed the perfect hand. Even though you could only see the backs of the cards in the photograph, I will always know how amazing that draw was.
