We do love announcing our announcements, but you should pay special attention to this one.

All fall, we've been offering [small glimpses](/en/articles/competitive-gaming/2018/10-04-2019-schedules-pro-tour-qualifying-and-magicfest) into the [future of competitive *Magic* gaming](/en/articles/competitive-gaming/2018/09-06-2019-to-feature-six-pro-tours), and we know you have questions. On December 6 at The Game Awards (and on this website), you will get answers.

So, we ask that you're just a *little* bit more patient with us. It will be worth the wait.
