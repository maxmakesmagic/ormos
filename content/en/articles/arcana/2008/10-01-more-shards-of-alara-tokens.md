It's time to show more *Shards of Alara* tokens. You saw [yesterday's set](https://web.archive.org/web/20081001000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/shards-alara-tokens-2008-09-30), right? If you didn't, you should go look at them. And be amazed!

Okay, hit it!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9a/eb/9aeb50e6cc79bd61d4a215161f77af6bd65380dbe682978ecbedb4b581e87df9.jpg)  

This Thopter token indicates that a [Sharding Sphinx](https://web.archive.org/web/20081001000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sharding+Sphinx) has recently dealt combat damage to someone.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/93/52/93522dcd38217b3f7d367aca32b1afa1f5b86f52c7d031f501c5479b4fcaea75.jpg)  

The [Archdemon of Unx](https://web.archive.org/web/20081001000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archdemon+of+Unx) is well known for producing two things: delicious baked goods and 2/2 Zombie tokens.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6f/07/6f0748f348a1a06a84adb734fae48daf1221ad444db870d4e8908f1987ff95d0.jpg)  

The Goblin token does not have haste, so it must come from [Dragon Fodder](https://web.archive.org/web/20081001000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragon+Fodder). However, you could probably use it for [Goblin Assault](https://web.archive.org/web/20081001000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Assault) in a pinch.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4a/6d/4a6d07df7d6bd1ea9265e628fb9c62b7b6c8b4c601f14d499f7df2ad8010a74a.jpg)  

Ah, the [Ooze Garden](https://web.archive.org/web/20081001000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ooze+Garden) in action is a beautiful thing, isn't it? Gross, but beautiful.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d1/4e/d14eec2e70b45d4281ada894884b41f9667907d7fde7f9a727be236f698eaed4.jpg)  

Saprolings are a common feature of Jund. They're often seen is association with [Jund Battlemage](https://web.archive.org/web/20081001000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jund+Battlemage), [Mycoloth](https://web.archive.org/web/20081001000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mycoloth), [Necrogenesis](https://web.archive.org/web/20081001000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Necrogenesis), and [Sprouting Thrinax](https://web.archive.org/web/20081001000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sprouting+Thrinax).

But tokens aren't the only extras that come in packs. You might also get this *Conflux* promotion:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/79/2a/792a9c38bd0d3740349b00c64b1b0a6ae87a62b52d1405ebb58fc3b389858519.jpg)  

Nice, huh? Jason Chan did a great job on that art. Let's look at it a little larger!
