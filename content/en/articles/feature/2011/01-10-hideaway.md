Looking for a fun way to sneak [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20110110000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Aeons+Torn) onto the battlefield? Why not consider the hideaway lands from the *Lorwyn* block? With [Windbrisk Heights](https://web.archive.org/web/20110110000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Windbrisk+Heights) and [Mosswort Bridge](https://web.archive.org/web/20110110000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mosswort+Bridge), you'll have yourself a 15/15 in no time! As a backup, just in case you don't find one of your lands, you can try to hit with [Summoning Trap](https://web.archive.org/web/20110110000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoning+Trap). Of course, [Knight of the Reliquary](https://web.archive.org/web/20110110000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+the+Reliquary) helps to make sure you've always got the land you want, so it shouldn't be that difficult!
