## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a0/33/a033c8b011d670310dfe773ff4e5e30897a8e368f19c05191304fd96b7824e2d.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, a card box with panoramic art, two deck boxes, nine 15-card booster packs, 80 land cards, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/14/63/14636fa3868056dd5cea541aca8a3be37537876da48bf696004b6577099e3257.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6f/5f/6f5f5f64a4189d45a597bc053ab09c79335d027ee7278fd41133ac3df3f21664.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 cards, a foil premium rare card, one 15-card booster pack, a strategy insert, and a **Magic** learn-to-play guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044006/http://magic.wizards.com/node/287276)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0d/b1/0db12e76c6724985ae188164f5efc56f05cbeedbdff1eac1953e7d7a9af9a8cb.png)

### EVENT DECKS

Battle like the best, right out of the box. Includes a tournament-ready 60-card deck plus a 15-card “sideboard” to swap in as needed, depending on what you expect your opponent will play.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044006/http://magic.wizards.com/node/287281)

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1d/b2/1db2dafdd4fc084a59bb00960e9506de91a958d70110f868fc0da52c43bfa0aa.png) The third in the Scars of Mirrodin block, this set features 175 black-bordered cards, randomly inserted premium versions of all cards in the set, new mechanics (Phyrexian Mana), and a new Planeswalker card.

**Set Name:** *New Phyrexia*  
**Block:** Set 3 of 3 of the *Scars of Mirrodin* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 175

**Languages:** English, Chinese Traditional, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

**Official Three-Letter Code:** NPH  
**Twitter Hashtag:** #MTGNPH

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044006/https://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/703) (or download as [RTF](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044006/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/MTGNPH_FAQ_EN_20110425.rtf))

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Ken Nagle (lead), Dave Guskin, K. Joseph Huber, Matt Place, Mark Rosewater

**Final Game Design and Development:** Aaron Forsythe (lead), Dave Guskin, Zac Hill, Tom LaPille, Erik Lauer
