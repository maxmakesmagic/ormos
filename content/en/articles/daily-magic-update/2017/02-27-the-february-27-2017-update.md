The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on February 27, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by [Damnation](https://web.archive.org/web/20170227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Damnation). We did it. We finally did it. You know it's quite the day when reprinting the *Zendikar* fetch lands is the second coolest reprint.

# Today's Previews Shown and Yet to Come

1.  Damnation and Fetch Lands | Wizards of the Coast

I don't usually list our own articles here, especially since you can just click on them from the same page you probably found this article, but given the community desire for these cards to be reprinted, it's hard not to include them.

[Get Ready To Dual](/en/articles/makingmagic/2017/02-28-get-ready-to-dual) | Mark Rosewater (fetch lands)

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Samuel Vuillot's Mardu Ballista](https://web.archive.org/web/20211114083514/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/gputr17/event-coverage/top-8-decklists-2017-02-26)

Add possibly the best card in the format to possibly the best deck in the format, and the result is a Grand Prix victory for Samuel Vuillot, who, like a lot of players, found a way to put [Walking Ballista](https://web.archive.org/web/20170227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Walking+Ballista) into Mardu Vehicles. Vuillot, however, was the last player standing at Grand Prix Utrecht.

But there was much more to the Grand Prix than robots driving air ships—and you can read all about it [on the coverage page](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-utrecht-2017), check out the [Top Moments from Frank Karsten](/en/events/2017/gputr17/top-5-moments), and/or check out our Utrecht video coverage on our [Twitch archive page.](https://www.twitch.tv/magic/videos/all)
