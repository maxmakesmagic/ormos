# What kind of metagame were you expecting this weekend?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkyo15/QQ-Yuuta-Takahashi.jpg)  
**Pro Tour San Diego 2007 Finalist, Yuuta Takahashi:** I expected a lot of creature and combo decks and that's why I'm playing cards like [Terminus](https://web.archive.org/web/20150418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terminus) and [Ethersworn Canonist](https://web.archive.org/web/20150418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ethersworn+Canonist).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkyo15/QQ-Chikara-Nakajima.jpg)  
**Two-Time Pro Tour Top 8 Competitor, Chikara Nakajima:** I expected a lot of Blue Red Delver and my sideboard is filled with cards to beat it.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkyo15/QQ-Kentaro-Yamamoto.jpg)  
**Two-Time Top 8 Competitor and Platinum Pro, Kentarou Yamamoto:** Delver is probably the most popular, especially Temur.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkyo15/QQ-Shouta-Yasooka.jpg)  
**Pro Tour Champion and Player of the Year, Shouta Yasooka:** I have no idea. There are so many decks!
