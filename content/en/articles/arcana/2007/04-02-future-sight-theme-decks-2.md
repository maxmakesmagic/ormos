*Future Sight* is just around the corner. The *Future Sight* minisite launches this Wednesday, April 4.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/66/ee/66ee6a1b9e2e56d05f0806d85bd096fbe8d77c0281578ba8a854c3ede581ef41.jpg)

*Future Sight* previews begin on magicthegathering.com on Monday, April 9th.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d1/65/d1655e1a1933e89e578883ce4041606f9e7e79ab7fce3f52fa85670ab41442a1.jpg)

You'll get your first crack at *Future Sight* at the [prerelease events](/en/events/2007/prereleases-fut/future-sight-worldwide-prerelease-fact-sheet), April 21-22.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/56/e4/56e4f4ad3a88626b0fcc5b6ddf3924e1b8e31de930a754bff1316997b6646c7d.jpg)

For now, we thought you'd enjoy seeing those preconstructed little bundles of joy, the *Future Sight* theme decks. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5f/eb/5feb87117ad1e65da9a553178b04f5d9d777b332a40f31d8edafe29c16db4284.jpg)
