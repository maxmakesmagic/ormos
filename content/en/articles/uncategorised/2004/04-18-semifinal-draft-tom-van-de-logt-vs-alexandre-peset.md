![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpboc04/sf3_1.jpg)Tom, member of Schietkoe, is a former World Champion. Peset is coming fresh of a top 8 in Pro Tour Kobe, and is playing on NPC All Stars. Tom drafted an amazing black/white deck featuring 2 [Leonin Battlemage](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Battlemage), [Sword of Light and Shadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+Light+and+Shadow), and other assorted goodies. Alexandre got a seemingly suboptimal mono-black deck with a very low manacurve.

### Game 1

Tom won the die roll and chose to play first. His opening hand was [Sword of Light and Shadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+Light+and+Shadow), [Vulshok Battlegear](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulshok+Battlegear), [Mask of Memory](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mask+of+Memory), Loxodon Mxstic, [Leonin Battlemage](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Battlemage) and 2 [Plains](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plains). After thinking it over, Tom decided that it was worth the gamble and kept it. His gamble didn´t pay off however as his first two draw steps yielded [Darksteel Gargoyle](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Gargoyle) and Betreyal of Flesh. Alexandre in the meanwhile had an blazing fast start to punish Tom´s manascrew: 2 [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker) and [Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault) were out by turn 2. Alexandre however only attacked with one Worker on turn 3, fearing [Raise the Alarm](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raise+the+Alarm) which Tom didn´t have. He improved his board with Banshee´s Blade and [Grimclaw Bats](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grimclaw+Bats). Tom found his 3rd land and made Sword of Light&Shadow. It seemed he might make it into this game if he´d draw a land, but he needed one right now. His next draw yielded [Darksteel Gargoyle](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Gargoyle) and Tom could only shake his head. Alexandre added a [Scavenging Scarab](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scavenging+Scarab) to his force, and after not drawing a land again Tom scooped.

Alexandre 1 - Tom 0

### Game 2

Tom played first again and this time his opening hand was better: turn 3 [Razor Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Razor+Golem) is a great start! Alexandre´s opening was fast as well with turn 1 [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker), turn 2 [Grimclaw Bats](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grimclaw+Bats) and turn 3 [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite), but all those little guys lacked the quality of [Razor Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Razor+Golem). Tom played an unspectacular Drill Skimmer on turn 4, while Alexandre´s turn 4 was spectacular. He cast [Nightmare Lash](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightmare+Lash), equipped it to his Bats and swung for 5! Unfazed, Tom took his turn and attacked with both his creatures, making the life totals 14-9 in his favour. He then improved his board with [Leonin Skyhunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Skyhunter) and [Soldier Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soldier+Replica). What an incredibly fast race; and this was just turn 5!  
 Next up, Alexandre improved his board with [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker) and [Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault) and hit with his Bats for 5, neglecting to use the pump ability with his remaining mana. On his turn, Tom attacked with [Leonin Skyhunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Skyhunter) and laid [Stalking Stones](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stalking+Stones) and [Soldier Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soldier+Replica), leaving mana open for its ability and Drill Skimmer on defence.

Alexandre then thought for a while on his turn, trying to figure out the optimal play in this difficult situation. His board now consisted of [Grimclaw Bats](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grimclaw+Bats) equipped with [Nightmare Lash](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightmare+Lash), 2 [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker), [Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault), [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite) and 5 [Swamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swamp). Tom´s board was [Leonin Skyhunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Skyhunter), 2 [Soldier Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soldier+Replica), [Razor Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Razor+Golem) and 6 lands including [Stalking Stones](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stalking+Stones). Tom was at 9 life and Alexandre at 7.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpboc04/sf3_2.jpg)

Alexandre finally settled on an attack with his Bats. Tom chumped and took 1 from the Disciple since his Drill Skimmer hit the grave. After the attack, Alexandre played [Consume Spirit](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Consume+Spirit) for 5 on Tom´s [Leonin Skyhunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Skyhunter). Tom untapped, drew a land and slammer [Darksteel Gargoyle](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Gargoyle), bringing the game to a stall. Were it not for the [Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault), I´d say Tom was in a good position to win the game. But with lots of artefacts on the board and Tom only at 8 life, it actually seemed like the opposite was true. Alexandre drew [Goblin War Wagon](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+War+Wagon) and attacked with [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker). Tom blocked, lost a life to the Disciple and the War Wagon grew to 4/4. Tom´s draw steps yielded no answers in the meanwhile, only 2 1/1s: [Leonin Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Elder) and [Auriok Glaivemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auriok+Glaivemaster). Tom traded [Soldier Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soldier+Replica) against the 4/4 War Wagon in the next attack phase, losing 2 life in the process. Alexandre topdecked [Arcbound Stinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Stinger) and next turn he equipped his [Nightmare Lash](https://web.archive.org/web/20040418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightmare+Lash) to it. Since Tom had only one flyer and now the Stinger and the Bats were lethal, Alexandre´s next attack finished Tom and gave him the match win.

End result: Alexandre Peset beats Tom van de Logt 2-0
