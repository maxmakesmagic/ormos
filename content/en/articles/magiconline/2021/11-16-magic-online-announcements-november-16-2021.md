## Eternal Weekend Dates

Get ready for Eternal Weekend 2021, taking place through *Magic Online* starting this weekend!

*   Legacy Events
    *   Wasteland Event: Friday, November 19, at 5:00 p.m. PT (01:00 Saturday UTC)
    *   Bayou Event: Saturday, November 20, at 2:00 a.m. PT (10:00 UTC)
    *   Sylvan Library Event: Sunday, November 21, at 10:00 a.m. PT (18:00 UTC)
*   Vintage events will be held on *Magic Online* on the following weekend of November 26–28, 2021.

The All-Access pass is back for these events, with six new artworks featuring some of the most iconic cards in both formats up for grabs as prizes. Entry Tokens with All-Access are now on sale in the *Magic Online* Store. [Check out all the details over on Magic.gg](https://magic.gg/news/get-ready-for-eternal-weekend-2021) and mark your calendar for Eternal Weekend 2021!

## *Modern Horizons 2* Returns

While we currently have *Vintage Cube* up and running, it's time to bring back another fan favorite: *Modern Horizons 2* Draft! We're bringing back the non-Phantom Draft League now through January 5 with the same prize structure and chance to open a Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer you know and love!

*   Entry Options:
    *   25 Event Tickets
    *   3 *Modern Horizons 2* boosters and 4 Event Tickets
    *   250 Play Points
*   Prizes:
    *   3 Wins – 6 *Modern Horizons 2* boosters, 2 MOCS Season 3 QPs, and a Competitive Trophy
    *   2 Wins – 2 *Modern Horizons 2* boosters

## Innistrad Chaos Draft Starts Wednesday

From November 17–24, you will be able to find Innistrad Chaos Draft in the Limited Play Lobby. In this three-round Draft format, each of the eight players will open a random booster pack from among all seven Innistrad sets. The Spooky Season may be over for now, but Innistrad horrors (including opening a booster pack of Avacyn Restored) live on forever!

## Pioneer Qualifier Saturday

On Saturday, November 20, there will be a Pioneer Qualifier with a start time of 9 a.m. PT (17:00 UTC). The event costs 40 Season 3 QPs, and the winner will earn an invitation to the *Neon Dynasty* Championship event on *MTG Arena*.

Learn more about Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021#ptq).

* * *

## Modern Qualifier Sunday

On Sunday, November 21, there will be a Modern Qualifier with a special start time of 1 a.m. PT (09:00 UTC). The event costs 40 Season 3 QPs, and the winner will earn an invitation to the *Neon Dynasty* Championship event on *MTG Arena*.

Learn more about Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021#ptq).

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format Challenges are the first step toward qualifying for Premier Play through your favorite *Magic* formats and provide excellent prizes for those looking to test their mettle in their favorite formats. [Learn more about Premier Play qualification](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021) to get started today.

*Magic Online*'s Format Challenges run once on Saturdays and once on Sundays for each Constructed format! On weekends featuring Showcase Challenge events, the Format Challenge that starts at either 6 a.m., 8 a.m., or 10 a.m. Pacific time is pre-empted by the Showcase Challenge—Format Challenges scheduled at other times remain in place. *NOTE: Pauper, Vintage, and Standard Challenges are never pre-empted.*

*New for MOCS Season 3: Pauper Showcase Challenges are held at 8 a.m. Saturday and do not pre-empt the Modern Challenge.*
