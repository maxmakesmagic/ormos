With six rounds in the books, the metagame of the top tables is starting to shape up. I checked in on the top tables during round seven to see what's been winning. Let's take a look! 

Blue/Red Delver continues to be a dominant force here in New Jersey. The deck applies quick pressure with [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer), [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets), and [Monastery Swiftspear](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Swiftspear) before controlling the game with countermagic and burn spells while keeping its hand filled with [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise). There are significantly more copies of the deck than any other archetype at the top tables. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/Gerrytop.JPG)

 *Gerry Thompson is cruising through his opponents in the Garden State.* 

  

Miracles has long been an established powerhouse in Legacy. The deck has only become stronger with the inclusion of Khan's of Tarkir. [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) gives the deck tutoring power and longevity that were previously unavailable. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/Reidtop.JPG)

 *It's no miracle that Reid Duke finds himself at the top of the standings coming into round seven.*
