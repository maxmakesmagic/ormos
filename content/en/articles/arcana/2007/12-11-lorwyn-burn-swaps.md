An art swap, such as the swap that happened to some of [the *Lorwyn* elementals](/en/articles/arcana/2007/11-28-lorwyn-elemental-swaps), happens when final art ends up not fitting the mechanical feel of the card. Sometimes it's an elaborate swap involving multiple cards—Shriekmaw gets Æthersnipe's art, Æthersnipe gets Ingot Chewer's, and Ingot Chewer gets Shriekmaw's. 

But more often it's a straight one-for-one swap. An example is two burn spells in *Lorwyn*.

Originally, Needle Drop and Lash Out had one another's art. Lash Out lost a mana in development, and became a premier removal spell in the set—and besides, it just plain did more damage. So it got the art of a fierce flamekin lashing out with his flame. The more whimsical art of the giant dropping an enormous, giant-sized needle—which, admittedly, would hurt a lot, but looked less vicious in the art—got put onto the more narrow cantrip spell.
