But that's not the end of the story…National Qualifiers in WPN locations are happening all over North America this weekend and next, so check out Standard lists below, scout the metagame, and build your deck to battle for your invitation to Nationals. If you have eyes on Pro Tour Philadelphia, you can also find worldwide Standard qualifiers for that by using the event locator to the right.
