Welcome to another edition of [Sketches](https://web.archive.org/web/20040630000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/fullarchive&tablefilter=sketches:)! This week we take an interactive look behind the scenes of the art of *Fifth Dawn*'s **[Grafted Wargear](https://web.archive.org/web/20040630000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grafted+Wargear)** by Alex Horley-Orlandelli.

### 1\. Art Description

Once a card is designed, an art description is written to tell the artist what the card will do, what its flavor is, and what the mood of the illustration should be. Here were the instructions given to Alex for the project:

> *This is gunmetal-colored Vulshok armor (see style guide) that magically grafts to the wearer's skeleton. Once someone equips this armor, they would have to rip themselves apart to remove it.*

Alex Horley-Orlandelli has over forty **Magic** card credits to date since *Apocalypse*, including [Quicksilver Dagger](https://web.archive.org/web/20040630000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quicksilver+Dagger), [Gravestorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20040630000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravestorm), [Nantuko Vigilante](https://web.archive.org/web/20040630000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Vigilante), and [Myr Incubator](https://web.archive.org/web/20040630000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Incubator), among others.
