It's time for the Eighth Annual **Magic** Invitational. Twelve spots have already been awarded (see below), but four slots remain. This is where all of you come in. You see, the last four slots are voted in by the public. You will have the ability to vote for up to sixteen invitees for the Invitational you'd like to see. The top four vote getters from the ballot will be invited to this year's Invitational.

The current invitees are:

Slot 1 - Last Year's Invitational Winner - **Jens Thoren**  
 Slot 2 - 2003 World Champion - **Daniel Zink**  
 Slot 3 - Top Rated DCI Player - **Jordan Berkowitz**  
 Slot 4 - Top Ranked Player, Pro Points 2002-2003 Season - **Kai Budde**  
 Slot 5 - 2nd Ranked Player, Pro Points 2002-2003 Season - **Justin Gary**  
 Slot 6 - 3rd Ranked Player, Pro Points 2002-2003 Season - **Mattias Jorstedt**  
 Slot 7 - 4th Ranked Player, Pro Points 2002-2003 Season - **Dirk Baberowski**  
 Slot 8 - 5th Ranked Player, Pro Points 2002-2003 Season - **Bob Maher**  
 Slot 9 - Top Ranked Player, Pro Points, North American Region - **Osyp Lebedowicz**  
 Slot 10 - Top Ranked Player, Pro Points, Latin American Region - **Carlos Romao**  
 Slot 11 - Top Ranked Player, Pro Points, European Region - **Nicolai Herzog**  
 Slot 12 - Top Ranked Player, Pro Points, APAC Region - **Masashi Ooiso**

The deadline for the ballot is Friday, August 29th, 3:00PM PDT. Only DCI members may vote and only once per person.
