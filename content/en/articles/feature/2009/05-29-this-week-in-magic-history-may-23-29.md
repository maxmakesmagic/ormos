### May 23-25, 2008: Gindy Puts Name in Lights

![](https://i.ormos.uk/29/8c/298c7950293e48174ae5365082f1a6f22c07a4ca7c7bceb4395a64b41059598e.jpg)

Charles Gindy, welcome to Hollywood...a land where dreams come true and stars are made. The 22-year-old American, who has been playing Magic for half his life, became Tinseltown’s latest break-out performer when he swept Jan Ruess in the [Pro Tour–Hollywood](https://web.archive.org/web/20090529000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/gindy-puts-name-lights) finals.

Gindy, who said he had been dreaming of playing in a Top 8 ever since he started playing Magic, received a fortune cookie the Saturday of the Pro Tour, saying "You’ll make a name for yourself." Behind a stellar supporting cast of [Garruk Wildspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20090529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Wildspeaker), [Chameleon Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20090529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chameleon+Colossus), [Imperious Perfect](https://web.archive.org/web/20090529000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Imperious+Perfect), and a host of Elves, Gindy overwhelmed Ruess’s Merfolk to take home the champion’s trophy and the $40,000 first prize.
