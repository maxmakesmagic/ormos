## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1d/fe/1dfe00c0d53afc2df9243384d57125d01d322f4c3b426e55749d4a621fd2483e.png)

### 

Includes a player’s guide, a card box, eight 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, an excerpt from the *Test of Metal* novel, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/40/b5/40b5041e0e5cd0216d8b0921aa8d3f18f5cb1244ed14e17b064f39226ba561ef.png)

### 

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/00/fd/00fd39c8ee6a28e882557cda5fedeabf02e598c3f608f21909a614c25ef1a7d0.png)

### 

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 cards, a foil premium rare card, one sealed 15-card booster pack, a strategy insert, and a **Magic** learn-to-play guide.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d5/71/d571af7e9af673c1dea36369152a5ef43a300515dbe336d1ed9e3278edef0b38.png)

**Magic: The Gathering** *2011 Core Set* features 249 black-bordered cards, including randomly inserted premium versions of all cards in the set.

**Set Name:** *2011 Core Set*  
**Number of Cards:** 249

**Languages:** English, Spanish, Chinese Simplified, Russian, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Italian, French, German, Chinese Traditional

**Official Three-Letter Code:** M11  
**Twitter Hashtag:** #MTGM11

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718094831/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/EN_MTGM11_FAQ_20100702.doc)

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Aaron Forsythe (lead), Doug Beyer, Mark Globus, Tom LaPille, and Gregory Marques

**Final Game Design and Development:** Erik Lauer (lead), Dave Guskin, Tom LaPille, and Kenneth Nagle, with contributions from Mark L. Gottlieb
