With *Rise of the Eldrazi* now available in stores, Daily Decks will spend this week examining popular archetypes from *Zendikar* Block Constructed events held on **Magic Online**. That's a format that will really be shaken up by the coming of the monstrous Eldrazi, and we're here to give you a jump start on the competition. Today we start with Green-White Allies. Are there cards from *Rise* you think can make this deck better? Take a look through the [searchable spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20100724202522/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/riseoftheeldrazi/spoiler) to decide for yourself.
