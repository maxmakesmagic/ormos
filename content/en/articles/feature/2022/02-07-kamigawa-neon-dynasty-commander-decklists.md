On February 18, [*Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty*](https://web.archive.org/web/20220207000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/kamigawa-neon-dynasty) releases in stores worldwide, and with it, two new Commander decks that feature awesome reprints and powerful new cards.

![NEO Commander decks](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/en_n238sdnwDT.png)

Check out all the new and returning cards in the [*Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty* Commander Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2022/02-08-kamigawa-neon-dynasty-commander), extended-art versions of *Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty* Commander cards available in Collector Boosters in the [*Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty* Variant Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2021/12-17-kamigawa-neon-dynasty-variants)—including cards not found in these decks [exclusive to *Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty* Set and Collector Boosters](/en/articles/feature/2022/01-27-collecting-kamigawa-neon-dynasty)!

***(Editor's Note: The decklists below pull the newest printing of each card automatically from*** [**Gatherer**](https://web.archive.org/web/20220207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/)***, including printings which are not in this product. These decklists are not card-for-card product displays but rather interactive lists of the cards included in each deck. Additionally, card images for new Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty Commander cards will appear in decklists at a later time.)***
