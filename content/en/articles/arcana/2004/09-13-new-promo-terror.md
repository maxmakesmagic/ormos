Now *that* is a promo card.

![DCI Promo Terror](https://i.ormos.uk/d0/ea/d0ea9e7251194eb06252b230c1efaeae87bc21426a55c1ecefba60e352c73ae6.jpg) 

This promotional Terror card is one of a number of new DCI promo cards given away as future prizes to [Magic Player Rewards](https://web.archive.org/web/20090601195738/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Article.aspx?x=magic/rewards/welcome) members. Watch this space to see similar promo cards in the future.

Note that there will still be premium foil promo cards in the future; these art-intensive promo cards do not represent the end of the foil promo cards era.
