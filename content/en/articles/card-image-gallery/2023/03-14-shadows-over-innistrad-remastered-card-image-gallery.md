Cards from the sets *Shadows over Innistrad* and *Eldritch Moon* come to *MTG Arena* in this new set, *Shadows over Innistrad Remastered*. See all the cards in the set below, plus [check out the Shadows of the Past cards](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2023/03-13-shadows-of-the-past-card-list-and-schedule) that can appear in *Shadows over Innistrad Remastered* packs!

*Shadows over Innistrad Remastered* launches on *MTG Arena* on Tuesday, March 21.
