Ravnica is like *Magic*'s second home, and going back to it means revisiting some of the plane's greatest hits. Each of the guilds has their own Guild Kit filled with cards from Ravnica's past and present. The decks below release on November 2. These decks are not Standard legal. Cards from *Guilds of Ravnica* are playable in Standard. Note that cards from *Guilds of Ravnica* won't have been added to the system yet and reprints are going to show up as the previous versions below.
