A number of players took a Naya Zoo deck powered by Stoneforge Mystic and created by Tom "The Boss" Ross to a dominating performance at [Pro Tour–San Diego](/en/events/2010/ptsd10/gortzen-goes-all-the-way-in-san-diego). Following the event, the deck has been a big factor in tournaments held on **Magic Online**. The above list was used by Brad Nelson, one of the players who played it in California, to go undefeated in a digital event. It makes a great choice for playing at your local [Friday Night **Magic**](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6)!
