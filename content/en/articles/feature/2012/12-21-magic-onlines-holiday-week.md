Next week, many of you will be home with your families, staring longingly out the window as the snow gently falls. Perhaps you'll imagine the hours of uproarious fun you could be having at your local game shop as the snow mounts ever higher outside your window. What's a **Magic** gamer to do when he or she is trapped at home? 

Open that laptop and log onto **Magic Online**, obviously. Just because you're home with your family doesn't mean you can't shuffle up and battle! 

Check out these six awesome events scheduled between now and the ringing in of the new year!

That's four opportunities to [qualify for Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze*](https://web.archive.org/web/20130522122848/http://wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/events/ptqmtgo/dragonsmaze/13) (happening in San Diego, May 17-19) and two chances to qualify for the MOCS in just a week's time! 

Along with that we are running the [**Magic Online** Cube: Holiday 2012](/en/articles/feature/2012/11-27-magic-online-cube-holiday-2012), allowing you a chance to play with [Power](/en/articles/arcana/2003/10-15-the-power-nine) for the first time in these Phantom Draft events! 

And lastly, of course, we have the normal operating schedule for **Magic Online** you can check out by visiting [this page](https://web.archive.org/web/20130622061210/http://www.wizards.com/magic/digital/magiconline.aspx?x=mtg/digital/magiconline/calendar)!
