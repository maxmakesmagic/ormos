In the [Selecting *Ninth Edition* Wrap-up article](/en/articles/selecting9e/2004/09-20-selecting-ninth-edition-wrap-up) you saw the final art for Dancing Scimitar and Mahamoti Djinn. As you can see, they turned out terrific; if you voted on the Selecting *Ninth Edition* sketches over the last few months, they just prove how good an art director you were.
