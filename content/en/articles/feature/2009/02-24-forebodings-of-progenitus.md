Hey, wanna see something cool?

As you know, [Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20090224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Progenitus) did not appear in *Shards of Alara*, but it did appear in *Conflux*. Just so you remember it clearly, and because it looks cool blown up, here's the art for [Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20090224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Progenitus):

![](https://i.ormos.uk/17/7a/177a5f14c356e2e9a89fa7046460cb7e413c073dcc407de5fb65cf9baed8c29b.jpg)

Dramatic and iconic, yes? But it seems like we've seen that silhouette somewhere before. Almost as if a pre-*Conflux* card hinted at what [Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20090224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Progenitus) would look like.

![Relic of Progenitus](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Relic+of+Progenitus)

If we blow up the art for [Relic of Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20090224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Relic+of+Progenitus), you can even see what its body looks like!
