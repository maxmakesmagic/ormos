![](http://www.wizards.com/protour/images/qt_lg.jpg)Get your Extended skills warmed up, because tickets to San Juan, Puerto Rico are up for grabs this Pro Tour Qualifier season! 

You can test each decklist thanks to our [Sample Hand Generator](https://web.archive.org/web/20130710150318/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/294). Just click on the card fan in the upper right of each decklist and you can see what your opening hand could be, along with mulligans and the first three cards you'd draw.

Deck lists will be updated weekly during the PTQ season. The season runs from January 2 to April 11. For more information about the PTQ season, [click here](/en/events/2009/sanjuan10-qualifiers/pro-toursan-juan-2010-qualifiers). You can also [qualify on **Magic Online**](/en/articles/event-coverage/2009/12-21-magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers-san-juan-2010)!
