![Ajani's Chosen](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ajani%27s+Chosen)

In [this Latest Developments article](/en/articles/latestdevelopments/2014/01-03-core-developments-for-standard), Sam Stoddard talked about how core sets contain cards that hint at what is coming in future blocks. One such "plant" card in Magic 2014 is [Ajani's Chosen](https://web.archive.org/web/20140612000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ajani%27s+Chosen), who can be quite powerful once the full *Theros* block is unleashed upon it. 

The core synergy with [Ajani's Chosen](https://web.archive.org/web/20140612000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ajani%27s+Chosen) comes from the removal suite, [Banishing Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20140612000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banishing+Light) and [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140612000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere). These two enchantments are some of Standard's most powerful removal spells, and getting a 2/2 on top of that is powerful. Outside of this core synergy, there are many aggressive, disruptive creatures like [Keening Apparition](https://web.archive.org/web/20140612000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Keening+Apparition) and [Lyev Skyknight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140612000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lyev+Skyknight) to help keep an opponent off balance.
