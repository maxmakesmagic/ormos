Ready for another story about the heroes of Kamigawa? Check out the [*Champions of Kamigawa* site](https://web.archive.org/web/20080422233625/http://www.wizards.com/magic/displayexpansion.asp?set=chk&page=1) for an update in the "Meet the Champions" area.

This week's story is "**[Eight and a Half Tales](https://web.archive.org/web/20080415035700/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/chk/eighthalf)**" by Rei Nakazawa. It explores the life and memories of legendary kitsune Eight-and-a-Half *Tails*, venerated foxfolk elder and sensei, including the story of how he lost that one-half of a tail. Enjoy.
