Thanks for the introductions, Iroas. Congratulations to the winners of the *Journey into Nyx* Championship! UglyCat was victorious in the Constructed Championship, while FilippoK claimed the title of Limited Champion!

UglyCat conquered the top eight in Constructed:

*   UglyCat
*   Dethvidehul
*   Hrymfaxe
*   diagon
*   krom321
*   FreshGaming
*   ability170146
*   itsnotme

FilippoK emerged as the victor from these eight Limited competitors:

*   FilippoK
*   SOLTYS
*   Dethvidehul
*   Entropei
*   BennyBoy2525
*   KO\_mak
*   esquilo
*   cortex982

Check out all of the details for the [Limited](#limited) and [Block Constructed](#blockconstructed) matches below!

Block Constructed Format Recap

[Top](#top)

**Block Constructed Game One**

The finals saw UglyCat's Red/Green/White ramp deck face off versus krom321's Black/Green/White [Eidolon of Blossoms](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eidolon+of+Blossoms) deck.

UglyCat started the first game with a [Sylvan Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Caryatid) into [Xenagos, the Reveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xenagos%2C+the+Reveler). The 2/2 Satyr was all that remained of [Xenagos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xenagos) as krom321 cast a [Banishing Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banishing+Light).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3a/23/3a23c9b8108c6c8b9d63b5d08d32850768101815de9d225564f6fbaea7778506.png)  
 A well-timed [Banishing Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banishing+Light) saved krom321 from dealing with [Xenagos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xenagos) early in the game. After dispatching the Planeswalker, krom321 was still overrun by a horde of non-Planeswalking Satyrs later in the game.

UglyCat built his mana with a second [Sylvan Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Caryatid) and a [Voyaging Satyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyaging+Satyr) on the following turn, while krom321 brought out the powerful [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix). The mana ramp of UglyCat allowed him to cast a turn 5 [Revel of the Fallen God](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Revel+of+the+Fallen+God), a turn 6 [Elspeth](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth) (killing krom321's just cast) [Doomwake Giant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doomwake+Giant), and a second [Revel of the Fallen God](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Revel+of+the+Fallen+God) on turn 7. The band of Satyrs frolicked all over krom321's life total to allow UglyCat to take game one.

**Block Constructed Game Two**

In the second game, Krom321 started off with a turn 3 [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) taking a [Banishing Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banishing+Light). UglyCat again ramped with a [Voyaging Satyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyaging+Satyr) into a turn 3 [Xenagos, the Reveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xenagos%2C+the+Reveler). A [Brain Maggot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brain+Maggot) took a second [Banishing Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banishing+Light) from UglyCat. With only a second [Sylvan Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Caryatid), UglyCat attacked with two Satyrs and a [Voyaging Satyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyaging+Satyr) taking krom321 down to 11.

With Krom321 missing a land play on turn 4, krom321 conceded when UglyCat cast a [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon) and made a third Satyr.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e7/a3/e7a3d031c2b2329aebb0f66a47f38243cb15079e1b6050c8debdaab963e7d43d.png)  
 UglyCat's mana ramp went off without a hitch, leaving him with [Xenagos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xenagos), [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon), [Voyaging Satyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyaging+Satyr), [Sylvan Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Caryatid), three Satyrs, and the title of *Journey into Nyx* Block Constructed Champion by turn 5.

Congrats to UglyCat for winning the *Journey into Nyx* Block Constructed Championship!

You can view the brackets and the event details for the Constructed Championship [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Digital/MagicOnlineTourn.aspx?x=mtg/digital/magiconline/tourn/7195038).

Limited Format Recap

[Top](#top)

The finals of the *Journey into Nyx* Limited Championship saw FilippoK's white-red aggro deck facing off versus BennyBoy2525's green-black Constellation deck.

**Limited Game One**

FilippoK came out fast in the first game with his first picks from *Journey into Nyx* and *Born of the Gods* in the first three turns of the game. The [Stonewise Fortifier](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stonewise+Fortifier) followed by [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos) were joined by an [Akroan Mastiff](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroan+Mastiff) before BennyBoy2525 conceded game one of the finals without casting a single spell.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/31/21/3121bc2c903ec905198cdf27430114830feff908d62e02659a0c11d5d39d7b12.png)  
 FilippoK raised a sizeable army too quickly for BennyBoy2525 to have a chance at a comeback.

  
**Limited Game Two**

![](https://i.ormos.uk/65/d4/65d4129adaef85937b3659161b9c6c00e066d50cbc473a8b6d9545b22048bb08.png)  
 With [Grim Guardian](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Guardian+) [Chained to the Rocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chained+to+the+Rocks),  
 FilippoK had too strong of a lead, forcing BennyBoy2525 to concede.

While game two did not go much better for BennyBoy2525, he did manage to cast a few spells. A turn 2 [Golden Hind](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golden+Hind) traded with a [Oreskos Swiftclaw](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oreskos+Swiftclaw). FilippoK was missing land drops but did manage to cast a [Traveling Philosopher](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Traveling+Philosopher) and [Akroan Line Breaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroan+Line+Breaker). BennyBoy2525 got to six mana and played a [Archetype of Finality](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archetype+of+Finality). The [Archetype](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archetype) blocked and killed the [Akroan Line Breaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroan+Line+Breaker) before dying to a final point of damage from [Forgeborn Oreads](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forgeborn+Oreads). The final blow of the match came when BennyBoy2525 cast a [Grim Guardian](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Guardian) and enchanted it with [Nyx Infusion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nyx+Infusion). FilippoK saw fit to [Chained to the Rocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chained+to+the+Rocks) the [Grim Guardian](https://web.archive.org/web/20140616000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Guardian) at which point BennyBoy2525 conceded the match.

 

Congratulations to FilippoK for winning the title of *Journey into Nyx* Champion!
