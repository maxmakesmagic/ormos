Janos Csomos is the 2009 Slovakian National Champion! He used this take on the popular Cascade Swans deck to take the top slot. Interesting changes? Three Primal Commands, which allow the deck to recycle all its discarded lands and, in effect, "go infinite" by continuously recycling the Primal Commands as well. He'll head to Rome later this year to defend his nation's pride at the World Championships.
