Qualifiers for Pro Tour *Born of the Gods* begin this weekend! The format for this season will be Limited (Sealed Deck Swiss and Draft in the Top 8). Until September 27, PTQs will be run with ***Magic** 2014* boosters; starting on September 27, they will switch to *Theros* boosters. [This page](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/qualifiers-for-pro-tour-born-of-the-gods-in-valencia) has a lot more information for you. If you plan on going to a PTQ this season, find one near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#bog). In the meantime, we still have some Standard decklists rolling in from last season to share with you. Good luck; have fun!
