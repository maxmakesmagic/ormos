## Addressing Pauper Card Availability

To help cards flow to our most engaged *Magic Online* Pauper fans, we are granting two copies of each of 38 commons to a group of players as part of our weekly patch between 9–10 a.m. Pacific time tomorrow.

*   A Pauper League course anytime in 2022 until last Sunday, or

## *Double Masters 2022* Limited Preliminaries – Now with QP Entry Option

Limited Preliminary events now feature *Double Masters 2022*. For the first time, players will be able to enter these events with Qualifier Points!

This is the Limited offering we mentioned back in April for players to use their excess QPs (especially all of those earned during the 20th Anniversary Series) due to our Premier Play gap in May.

Like other *Masters* offerings, the entry options are 45 Event Tickets and 450 Play Points. The third option is 45 MOCS Season 2 QPs.

Prizes are the same as regular-set Preliminaries—this is due to the ability to reinvest QPs into extra Preliminaries in addition to the normal Qualifier, Super Qualifier, and Showcase Challenge options.

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format Challenges are the first step toward qualifying for Premier Play through your favorite *Magic* formats and provide excellent prizes for those looking to test their mettle in their favorite formats. [Learn more about Premier Play qualification](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021) to get started today.

*Magic Online*'s Format Challenges run once on Saturdays and once on Sundays for each Constructed format! On weekends featuring Showcase Challenge events, the Format Challenge that starts at either 6 a.m., 8 a.m., or 10 a.m. Pacific time is pre-empted by the Showcase Challenge—Format Challenges scheduled at other times remain in place.

*NOTES:* 

*   *Pauper, Vintage, and Standard Challenges are never pre-empted.*
*   *Saturday Legacy Challenges require 32 players and give prizes to Top 32.*
*   *Sunday Legacy Challenges require 64 players and give prizes to Top 64.*

## Change Log

*   [Deranged Assistant](https://web.archive.org/web/20220712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deranged+Assistant) and [Millikin](https://web.archive.org/web/20220712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Millikin)'s mana abilities have been changed to work only at instant speed. This change is to prevent players from undoing the ability and then being unable to take further game actions.
*   [Greater Gargadon](https://web.archive.org/web/20220712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Greater+Gargadon) and other suspended cards will properly be able to be cast when the last time counter is removed.
    *   [Greater Gargadon](https://web.archive.org/web/20220712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Greater+Gargadon) will be returned to *Double Masters 2022* packs once the weekly patch is applied.
*   [Clockwork Fox](https://web.archive.org/web/20220712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clockwork+Fox) will properly draw its controller two cards with its dies trigger.
*   [Sacred Ground](https://web.archive.org/web/20220712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sacred+Ground) will properly only trigger off opponent-controlled land destruction.
    *   [Sacred Ground](https://web.archive.org/web/20220712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sacred+Ground) will be unbanned in all formats once the weekly patch is applied.
*   [Grafdigger's Cage](https://web.archive.org/web/20220712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grafdigger%27s+Cage) and [Weathered Runestone](https://web.archive.org/web/20220712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Weathered+Runestone) will interact correctly with [Muxus, Goblin Grandee](https://web.archive.org/web/20220712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Muxus%2C+Goblin+Grandee); [Animist's Awakening](https://web.archive.org/web/20220712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Animist%27s+Awakening); [Depala, Pilot Exemplar](https://web.archive.org/web/20220712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Depala%2C+Pilot+Exemplar); and [Animal Magnetism](https://web.archive.org/web/20220712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Animal+Magnetism).
