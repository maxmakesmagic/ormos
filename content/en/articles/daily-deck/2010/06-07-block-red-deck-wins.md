Red Deck Wins always seems to pop up, and for Italian Andrea Giarola it was his ticket to the Top 8 at Pro Tour–San Juan. Using powerful spells like Kargan Dragonlord and Devastating Summons, the young player celebrated his 18th birthday on Sunday with his first Top 8. Read the official coverage of Pro Tour-San Juan [here](/en/events/2010/ptsj10/paulo-celebrates-six-on-the-beach-in-san-juan).
