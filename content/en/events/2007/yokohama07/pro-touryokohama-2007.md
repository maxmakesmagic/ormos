April 20-22, 2007

Yokohama, Japan is the second stop in the 2007 Pro Tour season. But not only the Pros will be enjoying themselves: the Pro Tour is above all a great weekend of **Magic** that brings you fantastic opportunities. Try out the new Two-Headed Giant team format with your friends, win great prizes, and much more! 

Pro Tour–Yokohama is a *Time Spiral* Block Constructed event that runs April 20-22, 2007. The Last-Chance Qualifier will be run on Thursday, April 19 at the tournament site (registration begins at 11 a.m.).

### Tournament Format and Schedule

Pro Tour–Yokohama is a three-day Block Constructed format event with a prize purse of $240,245. For more information about the format, schedule and prize payout, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20130630082743/https://www.wizards.com/Default.asp?x=mtgcom/protour/yokohama07-format).

### Site Attractions

**SIDE EVENTS**  
Play **Magic** all weekend long, even if you're not qualified for the Pro Tour! Information on all the events being held at Pro Tour-Yokohama can be found in the [Side Event Schedule](/en/events/2007/yokohama07-sides/2007-pro-tour-yokohama-side-events-schedule)

**ARTISTS ON SITE**  
 Pro Tour–Yokohama 2007 will feature appearances by **Magic** artists **Adam Rex**, **Dan Frazier**, **Ittoku**, and **Hideaki Takamura**! [Click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20130630082743/https://www.wizards.com/Default.asp?x=mtgcom/protour/yokohama07-artists) to read more about these artists. 

  

**WATCH THE TOP 8**  
 All of Sunday's Top 8 action can be viewed on a projection screen in the hall, with Randy Buehler and Brian David-Marshall providing the play-by-play and color commentary. Sunday's action begins at 10:45 a.m. local time, which is 9:45 p.m. ET Saturday, and is also available as a [webcast](/en/events/2004/webcast/magic-the-gathering-webcast-schedule) from the comfort of your own home.
