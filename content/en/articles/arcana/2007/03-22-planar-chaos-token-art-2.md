[Last week](https://web.archive.org/web/20070322000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/planar-chaos-token-art-1-2007-03-15) we showed three of the five unique pieces commissioned for *Planar Chaos* token art. Today we'll look at the remaining two, plus a "little" something to whet your appetite for later. 

### Soldier token

With the help of [Stormfront Riders](https://web.archive.org/web/20070322000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormfront+Riders) and/or [Benalish Commander](https://web.archive.org/web/20070322000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Benalish+Commander), you'll have the Salt Plains swamped with these guys in no time. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8d/ac/8dac639eba6d646d54eb5847219ed6f6401c211bbc100c80ae586bbc5c20a87d.jpg)  
*Soldier token art by Alex Horley-Orlandelli*

  

### Knight token

What happens when your [Riftmarked Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20070322000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Riftmarked+Knight) joins the party after some time in suspendsville? 

![Riftmarked Knight](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Riftmarked+Knight)

Say hello to his evil twin. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/48/5b/485b3a19213752eaea2bbcef896ddb858f2e972faa80556611a4e5aeb89ae650.jpg)  
*Knight token art by William O'Connor*

  

That's it for *Planar Chaos* token art, but don't worry, there's more to come in the future, where this guy's already waiting for you. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/85/3f/853f04a7b6c7c44f64df6855a0a6dc1461ebffce5cab226722dfdc1c2b39fe0b.jpg)  
*Token art by Raymond Swanland*
