### Invitation List

MALAYSIA Nationals Invitation list comprises the following:

*   The current MALAYSIA National Champion and members of the current MALAYSIA national team (including alternate).
*   Players from the MALAYSIA with Pro Tour Players Club level 2 or higher.
*   Winners of invitations through Regional Championships.
*   Winners of invitations through MALAYSIA National Qualifiers.
*   The Top 75 DCI Composite–ranked players from the MALAYSIA on June 10, 2009.
*   The Top 75 DCI Total–ranked players from the MALAYSIA on June 10, 2009.

All players on the list below are already qualified for MALAYSIA Nationals and are not permitted to play in MALAYSIA Regional Championships and/or MALAYSIA Nationals Qualifier.
