Check out the *Scars of Mirrodin* Booster Draft Top 8 decks from late December, which saw four players snag an early Christmas present—a trip to Paris! And what's the holiday season without a few leftovers? We've included the top Extended deck lists from 2010 Worlds again as a primer for the [Extended PTQ season](https://web.archive.org/web/20110106000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/protour/nagoya11-qualifiers) under way now! Be sure to tune in next week for the latest Extended decks feeding Pro Tour Nagoya.
