Get to know the players that made the Top 8 of Grand Prix Portland 2017!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gppor17/gppor17_Top-8.jpg)

* * *

### Brandon Goe

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gppor17/gppor17_Top-8-Goe.jpg)

**Twitter Handle:** @NightFury301

**Hometown:** Spokane, WA

**Occupation:** Aspiring Pro Gamer :)

**Day 1 Record:** 7-2

**Day 2 Record:** 6-0

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**

2nd at States, Montana 2012

Consistent 2nd place PPTQ finishes

**What deck are you playing and why?**

Mono-Red, I have several games with the deck. I know the match-ups, and I love casting Hazoret!

**What was your best card this weekend and why?**

Three-way tie between Ferocidon, [Harsh Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20171120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Harsh+Mentor), Hazoret! They make your opponents play so differently and synergies well.

**What was your favorite memory from this weekend?**

I cam to play a GP with all my friends/test partners, made Day 2, made Top 8, and it's my birthday weekend

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**

Everything. I've played my entire life for a moment like this and it's the first step to making it closer to the dream!
