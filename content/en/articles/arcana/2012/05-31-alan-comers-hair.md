When the [**Magic** Pro Tour Hall of Fame](https://web.archive.org/web/20120531000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/574031) inducted its first class in 2005, the inductees were Jon Finkel, Darwin Kastle, Tommi Hovi, Olle Råde, and Alan Comer. Alan Comer's [Hall of Fame profile](/en/articles/event-coverage/2005/11-22-magic-the-gathering-2) includes his five Top 8s and numerous innovative decks. But it doesn't mention his heart.

Here's what Alan Comer looked like a few days ago:

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/984_long.gif)  

He's been growing his hair long, intending to donate it to Locks of Love. So here's what he looks like now:

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/984_short.gif)  

Nice work, Alan!
