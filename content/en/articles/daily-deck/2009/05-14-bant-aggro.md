Preparing your Standard deck for the upcoming Regionals tournaments? Check out Michal Swierczynski's aggressive Bant list from a recent Nationals Qualifier in Poland. Jhessian Infiltrator, Rhox War Monk, and Rafiq of the Many are some of the most powerful creatures in their colors. Snakeform and Behemoth Sledge provide spell support, and the list was good enough to finish in the Top 8. For more information on Regionals, [visit the Regionals page](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/regional-championships-2008).
