Last weekend, Andres Martinez won [Grand Prix Guadalajara](/en/events/2013/gpgua13/martinez-salvages-victory-in-guadalajara), keeping the trophy in Mexico, with a Golgari Reanimator deck (which you can see at the link below). *Dragon's Maze* Standard is now in effect, and if you want to do well at a PTQ, it behooves you to keep up on the metagame. Check out these decks from recent PTQs and GP Guadalajara and find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#theros)! Good luck; have fun!
