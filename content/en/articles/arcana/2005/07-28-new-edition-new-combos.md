> ### Other New Friendships
> 
> Hell's Caretaker will become fast friends with Solemn Simulacrum. Form of the Dragon has been seen on its cell phone with a certain recent Epic spell on the other end. Slate of Ancestry and Verdant Force met in a coffee shop recently and are already pen-pals. And Storage Matrix and Icy Manipulator have been planning mischief since the spoiler went up!
