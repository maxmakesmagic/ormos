## Are You Ready for *Khans of Tarkir*?

[![Three Khans of Tarkir packs, set release on 12/12](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/ktk_release.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20231212000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/khans-of-tarkir-card-image-gallery) 

This is it! Get ready, because tomorrow, December 12, *Khans of Tarkir* will be unleashed in *MTG Arena*, bringing more than 200 cards and fun Sealed and Draft events in a set that will excite veteran and new players alike. Stop by the [*Khans of Tarkir* Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20231212000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/khans-of-tarkir-card-image-gallery) to see all the cards.

Check your inboxes, too, because you'll find three *Khans of Tarkir* packs to get your collection started!

## It's Time for Timeless

[![Same sorceress image as above, but with the text Timeless, What Is It? and bullet points: Nonrotating, Constructed format with 60-card decks; Any card you can collect on MTG Arena; and Rebalanced digital cards & original tabletop printings](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/wanifrudosw0.png)](https://web.archive.org/web/20231212000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/introducing-timeless-a-new-mtg-arena-format) 

Are your decks *fetching?* They can be starting tomorrow with [the debut of the new Timeless format](https://web.archive.org/web/20231212000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/introducing-timeless-a-new-mtg-arena-format). Just in time for the release of *Khans of Tarkir*, you can build Timeless decks using any card in *MTG Arena*, including those sweet fetch lands.

Celebrate the launch of Timeless with new events, including this week's [Midweek *Magic* Introducing Timeless event](https://web.archive.org/web/20231212000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/announcements/midweek-magic) where you can try out the Timeless format and earn a *Khans of Tarkir* pack for playing!

Plus, don't forget to claim your Timeless Dragon card that you'll find in your inbox after the release!

![Timeless Dragon](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/phi8ld4upr2t.png)

## Play Commander, Get Four Legendary Creature Cards

[![](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/lci_commander_party.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20231212000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/get-commander-cards-on-mtg-arena-by-playing-at-your-local-wpn-store) 

Buff your Brawl decks! December 15–17—that's this Friday through Sunday—if you play in [*The Lost Caverns of Ixalan* Commander Party event](https://web.archive.org/web/20231212000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/get-commander-cards-on-mtg-arena-by-playing-at-your-local-wpn-store) at your local Wizards Play Network (WPN) game store, you'll receive four legendary creature cards in *MTG Arena* !

To be eligible, you will need to register for *The Lost Caverns of Ixalan* Commander Party event at [a WPN game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20231212000000/https://locator.wizards.com/) **using your Wizards account connected with your *MTG Arena* account** and play between December 15–17. Then, look for your four legendary creature cards later in the month in your *MTG Arena* inbox!

## *Jump Into Middle-earth* Returns, Plus Store Bundles!

[![Samwise and Golum with a lightning bolt dividing the image diagonally, upper left to lower right, with Samwise on the right holding Galadriel's glowing phial gifted to Frodo, and Gollum in the left leering](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/ltr_jump_into_middle-earth.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20231212000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/jump-into-middle-earth-on-mtg-arena) 

*Jump Into Middle-earth* returns December 12! There are ten *Jump Into Middle-earth* packets that expand possibilities for strategies and synergies as you mix and match packets for fast and exciting *Jump Into Middle-earth* games.

Check out the [event details and all ten packets](https://web.archive.org/web/20231212000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/jump-into-middle-earth-on-mtg-arena), and get ready to return to Middle-earth!

Also, be sure to check the *MTG Arena* Store for *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth™* bundles!

## Premier Play Schedule

All times listed are Pacific time (UTC-08:00).

### Qualifier Events

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events.

Qualifier tokens are delivered to your *MTG Arena* inbox. Remember to claim them before the event starts!

#### January

*   **Qualifier Play-In (Best-of-One)**
     January 6, 6 a.m. PT

*   Format: Explorer

*   **Qualifier Play-In (Best-of-Three)**
     January 12, 6 a.m. PT–January 13, 3 a.m. PT

*   Format: Traditional Explorer

*   **Qualifier Weekend**
     January 13, 6 a.m. PT–January 14, 4 p.m. PT

*   Format: Traditional Explorer

### Arena Open

The Arena Open Day One entry window begins at 6 a.m. PT and closes to new entries the following day at 3 a.m. PT (UTC-08:00). The Day Two entry window is 2 hours only, from 6 a.m. PT until 8 a.m. PT (UTC-08:00).

*   **Arena Open: *Khans of Tarkir* Limited**
    *   December 16: Day One, *Khans of Tarkir* Sealed (Best-of-One and Best-of-Three)
    *   December 17: Day Two, *Khans of Tarkir* Draft (Best-of-Three)

### Arena Championship

The Arena Championship is an invitation-only, two-day virtual event for players who earn invitations through Qualifier Weekend events.
