Round 13 has just ended and my, oh, my! Dragons, dragons, everywhere!

Ever since the release of *Dragons of Tarkir*, everyone's learning how to train their dragons! With the introduction so many cards pushing the dragon theme, there's bound to be something for everyone whether you're an aggro, midrange or control player!

Here are some of the Dragon-themed decks from Day 2 of Grand Prix São Paulo, along with their respective pilot's current score after Round 13! Just two more rounds and we will find out which dragon is the mightiest of them all!

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Haven+of+the+Spirit+Dragon)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Haven+of+the+Spirit+Dragon)

Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa has gone the distance and decided to play with the full set of [Dragonlord Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai) today, shaving a copy of [Silumgar, the Drifting Death](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silumgar%2C+the+Drifting+Death). He has also found a spot for [Dragonlord Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar) in the maindeck, kicking [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon) to the curb.

That brings his "dragon count" up to six, making [Silumgar's Scorn](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silumgar%27s+Scorn) and [Foul-Tongue Invocation](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foul-Tongue+Invocation) considerably better! [Dragonlord's Prerogative](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord%27s+Prerogative), another Dragon-themed card, will prove crucial in the mirror match.

Despite being the same colors as the previous list, Guido Barlaro has a very different approach. Barlaro is possibly the player playing with the most "Dragon Matters" cards in the entire tournament. Twelve in total, even without counting [Crux of Fate](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crux+of+Fate) and [Dragonlord's Prerogative](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord%27s+Prerogative)!
