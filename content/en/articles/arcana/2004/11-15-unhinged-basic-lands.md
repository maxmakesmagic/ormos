This is either a preview for you, or it isn't! 

*Unhinged* hasn't released yet, but if you're a CCoAL (Compulsive Clicker of Autocard Links) you may already have seen the cool look of the basic lands in the set. You see, we hid *Unhinged* basic lands in last week's non-*Unhinged*-preview **magicthegathering.com** articles. 

But in case you missed them, here they are in all their black-bordered (tournament-legal!) glory. All are illustrated by landscape maestro John Avon, and you can get one in each booster pack of *Unhinged* when it releases this Friday.
