Today's card is [Insidious Dreams](https://web.archive.org/web/20020409000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Insidious+Dreams), a black rare from *Torment*. The Dreams is illustrated by John Avon, an artist popular with **Magic** fans for his breathtaking landscapes.

### 1\. Art Description

The card was known in playtesting as "Dark Knowledge." These are the instructions given to the artist:

"This card should show a black wizard casting a spell which causes knowledge to erupt all around her. This could be shown by ghostly books and scrolls spinning about the wizard. The main focus of the card is the huge size of the spell. The main characters and elements can be shown smaller, but clearly visible."
