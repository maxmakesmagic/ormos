### *[Starter](https://web.archive.org/web/20050403223234/http://wizards.com/magic/starter/starter/welcomeold2.asp)* Information:

*Starter* was created to help segue new players into the game. Unlike *Portal*, *Starter* used "real" **Magic** card types and terminology, but it still contained some cards that were not tournament legal, a sticking point with players that moved on to play in events. The *Starter* idea lasted two years, and has since been replaced by the "Core Game" – a two-player learning tool that contains only the simplest Core Set cards.

### Promotional Card Information:

Wizards of the Coast has released several promotional cards, but the most well-known group are those given away to players that redeemed coupons clipped from a series of **Magic** novels printed in 1994 and 1995. Although these cards were never part of any official expansion sets, they are all legal in Type 1 tournaments (and all but Mana Crypt are legal in Type 1.5). The best place to see these cards is on "Flaminio's **Magic** Rarities" page **[here](https://web.archive.org/web/20030616000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.flaminio.com/magic/rarities-harper-us.html&origin=mtgcom_8erollout_starter99)**.
