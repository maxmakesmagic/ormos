Faeries is definitely one of the breakout archetypes to come from the *Lorwyn* block, and saw plenty of Top 8 action throughout the Nationals season. One of the most intriguing Top 8 finishes came out of the Italy National Championships. What was so interesting? Not just the Murderous Redcaps in the maindeck or the Bottle Gnomes in the sideboard, but the player piloting the list: Filippo Kratter. Fans of X-sports will recognize Filippo’s name from the world of professional snowboarding. In fact, you can check him out in action on the slopes [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20081222000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6zt6bbfGtcw&origin=) and slinging spells at Nationals [here](/it/events/2008/itnat08/2008-italy-national-championship-2).
