There are 22 Kithkin creatures and spells in *Lorwyn* (plus the changelings). But as the plane is full of those fiercely community-oriented humanoids, kithkin show up on many more cards than that. Look for their squat, pumpkinlike faces on these and other *Lorwyn* cards.

Sometimes kithkin are shown taking the brunt of spells or effects. Here’s a pair of kithkin experiencing the wrong end of a point of Tremor-like damage.

![Hurly-Burly_640](https://i.ormos.uk/f5/c5/f5c5b16bd05b479fbeb79ab6e87ed7494069870736042c6e0f31691fc9f20f42.jpg)  
*Hurly-Burly art by Steve Prescott*

Here’s a kithkin about to make the unpleasant acquaintance of a marauding boggart with a hedgehog-loaded slingshot.

![Quill-Slinger_Boggart_640](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/cardart/LRW/Quill-Slinger_Boggart_640.jpg)  
*Quill-Slinger Boggart art by Warren Mahy*

Take a look at the full art of Cloudgoat Ranger to see the three little kithkin grooming the giant’s cloudgoat. The giant is grooming her nails with a kithkin longsword.

![Cloudgoat_Ranger_640](https://i.ormos.uk/08/3c/083caf013812d7c8a7f52fe9e7c09f3b8b11f5519e88a40cb56c936bb1baa5e8.jpg)  
*Cloudgoat Ranger art by Adam Rex*

Here are just a few more of the *Lorwyn* cards that feature kithkin cameos—some subtle, some pretty obvious. Can you find more?
