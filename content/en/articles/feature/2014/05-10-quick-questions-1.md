**Which Color Gained the Most from the Introduction of Journey into Nyx into the Limited Mix?** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpwar14/qqlevy.jpg)

***(24) Raphaël Lévy**: In Sealed it's definitely green. I've played a couple and I was green every time.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpwar14/qqkrautmann.jpg)

***Wenzel Krautmann**: Green. There's more mana ramp now. [Golden Hind](https://web.archive.org/web/20140510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golden+Hind) is particularly good.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpwar14/qqrachid.jpg)

***Denniz Rachid**: Black. It was good in *Theros*, then terrible in *Born of the Gods*, and now it's good again.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpwar14/qqdezani.jpg)

***(1) Jérémy Dezani**: Green.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpwar14/qqjuza.jpg)

***(25) Martin Jůza**: There's a Pro Tour next week, so I'll have to say, no comment. Sorry.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpwar14/qqseverin.jpg)

***Thoralf Severin**: People seem to like black more, and I don't like that because I like black myself. But what actually gained the most is probably white.*
