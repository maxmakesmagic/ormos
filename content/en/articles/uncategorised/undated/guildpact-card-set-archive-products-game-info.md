## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/42/2a/422a4dd92e0321837c03cd7c3cad6617de8bbf46aff9395208dc104836f974f6.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide, two card boxes, six 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, one random Pro Tour player card, a special-edition life counter, and the *Guildpact* novel.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0b/11/0b11d8f299efbbda3f9b318fcde74d50d8f9f899088eaccfe7b74d40bf17339d.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0a/dc/0adc72d52c9b62b7e16610fee40a6bebfc93273045123a295b4258856f99c897.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/cf/20/cf20c5bbdd032c020b09152ba3af3843abcc42b5295c5186af58f66cee7364da.png)

This 165-card deck introduces three new Ravnica guilds: Orzhov Syndicate (white-black), Gruul Clans (red-green), and Izzet League (blue-red).

**Set Name:** *Guildpact*  
**Block:** Set 2 of 3 of the *Ravnica* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 165  
**Guilds:** Orzhov Syndicate, Gruul Clans, Izzet League

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160707025625/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/GPTFAQ122205_EN.doc)

**Game Design:** Mike Elliott (lead), Aaron Forsythe, Devin Low, and Brian Schneider

**Game Development:** Henry Stern (lead), Aaron Forsythe, Cormac Russell, Brian Schneider, and Mike Turian, with contributions from Paul Barclay, John Carter, and Mark L. Gottlieb
