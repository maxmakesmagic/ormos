### Black

![](https://i.ormos.uk/27/f2/27f2b99e0f4ee88c645ae85b77c585deac5dd3ae20f62ff2b9e8a65226385867.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/93/6d/936d15291cd7c19fa82d299bf5a7c4dd50b443f0e9120f70bbf681cdf6072d1b.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/aa/9a/aa9a7608883a0d6c50459d98fdeea4fe13cb3568877e3e5d75255570fffb6663.jpg)

The Dark Side is stroooooong in me, I fear. I sunk my teeth into black like a shark into so much winded surfer. This was our perfect opportunity to hit the Phyrexian continuity, and hard! I pitched the idea that swamps themselves were bio-organic graveyards of Phyrexian wreckage. As Phyrex ships and Dragon Engines and Spore Sowers rotted away, the slues from their decaying necro-tech would puddle into pools and lakes of sickening black ichor. The areas would be so tainted by the remains of the Phyrexians that even the sky would hold the cast of the roiling Rathi environment. That last point didn’t really play out on the cards, but damn it, I still like the idea.

You can see what I was going for in the concept painting. Yawgmoth’s last cruel joke of the war would be his mask’s visage worn on the faces of those too close to his defeated armies. Spend much time in these ichor swamps, and the eyes blacken, and spittle overflows and runs dark. Then the real horror starts. After becoming mad with fever, a finger falls off here, a plug of flesh releases there, and the powerful cancer of black literally consumes whatever lives in it. Whatever race or creature needed to be shown on a black card could be spattered with ichor, stained with black tears, and missing an important bit or two. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ef/98/ef9865251c4f4258c1b97d29de5834b98d856e95a877ff374a44fc910d01a602.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/cb/68/cb689a5fd9c1eb2d0dab3de491039f142293c89f789a9cad0f08379916265715.jpg)

Weapons and armor cued from these choices… the weapons are makeshift constructs of vicious serrated blades set into foraged Phyrexian metal, and shields are slotted and edged, meant to be strapped as a weapon breaker to compensate for a missing limb.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/93/79/9379a7078067aa450bde91852ac93b19d0abba09b1ff8c273a27e8063d40da08.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e9/d9/e9d9999c6752cada81be60bb76cd467b7359baa4cbca38a8e1c9eba9d53c2537.jpg)

![Dark_Withering_640](https://i.ormos.uk/d1/5a/d15a18092ddb410614315f41bc907c28be3e3240eb1bfffba06d412fdfc55637.jpg)  
*You can see several of these elements come together in Wayne Reynolds’ illustration for Dark Withering. The corruption of the Phyrexian Ichor is just too much for this poor elf. He succumbs, eyes black and flesh rotting.*

![Assassinate_640](https://i.ormos.uk/23/06/23069c9670a884e4d76b29d8cdcb06b7e3ba8ea21bb7f3ce2c68c7eb25d9fb25.jpg)  
*Kev Walker’s Assassinate is one of the most interesting uses of the black ‘skin’. We not only see the Time Spiral elf weeping the black ichor from contact with the Assassin’s blade, but the way it coats his lips and runs from his eye ties the image to the card that Assassinate homages, the Royal Assassin.*
