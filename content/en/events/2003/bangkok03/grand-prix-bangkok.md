The **Magic: The Gathering**® Grand Prix tournament series is an international circuit of large-scale Qualifier tournaments featuring cash prizes and multiple invitations to Pro Tour™ events.

**TOURNAMENT FORMAT AND SCHEDULE**  
 The format for Grand Prix–Bangkok is Standard Constructed. All DCI™ Constructed-format rules, **Magic**® Floor Rules, and all subsequent updates will be enforced.

**Friday, July 11:** Onsite registration is from 4:00 – 9:00 P.M.  
**Saturday, July 12:** Late registration from 10:00 – 11:00 A.M. Tournament begins at 11:30 A.M. The Top 64 players advance to competition on Sunday.  
**Sunday, July 13:** The Top 64 players begin play at 10:00 A.M.

**TOURNAMENT** 

**Divisions**  
 The main tournament at Grand Prix–Bangkok is open to **Magic** players of all ages.

*   **Head Judge**
     The Head Judge for Grand Prix–Singapore will be Jacky Yang, Level 3 from Taiwan.

*   **Tournament Structure**
     The structure for Grand Prix-Bangkok will be modified Swiss-style (50-minute rounds), with the Top 64 players advancing to the second day of competition for prizes and invitations.

*   **Pro Tour Invitations**
    The Top 8 previously uninvited players will each win an invitation to Pro Tour-New Orleans. *In the event that a player who wins an invitation to a Pro Tour stop at a Grand Prix has already qualified for an invitation, the invitation shall be extended to the next-best finisher in that Grand Prix. Previous qualification may be through a Qualifier tournament, through performance at a professional tournament, or by being an invited DCI player (valid upon the date of the official rank announcement for the appropriate professional tournament).*

*   **Tournament Divisions and Prizes**
     Grand Prix–Singapore will feature the standard main tournament prize schedule, as well as a special Amateur division prize schedule. Players who have 0 lifetime Pro Points will be eligible for the Amateur prizes. The Amateur prizes will be paid to the highest-finishing amateur players *in addition to any winnings those players receive from the main tournament prize schedule*. All prizes are in U.S. dollars.

*   **Amateur Status**
     A player is considered to have amateur status if he or she has 0 lifetime pro points. If a player has ever been awarded any points from a Grand Prix, Pro Tour, or World Championship event, that player no longer has amateur status, and is not eligible for amateur awards at Grand Prix or other tournaments.

*   **Tournament Byes** *(Byes are not cumulative throughout the season or year)*
     Byes will be given to players in the Grand Prix based on the following criteria:

    > ***Third-Round Byes***
    >
    > *   Players with 28 or more professional points in the 2003 Chicago Pro Standings.
    > *   DCI players with a Constructed rating of 1950 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI Constructed ratings published on March 19, 2003.
    >
    > ***Second-Round Byes***
    >
    > *   Players with 14 or more professional points in the 2003 Chicago Pro Standings.
    > *   DCI players with a Constructed rating of 1850 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI Constructed ratings published on March 19, 2003.
    >
    > ***First-Round Byes***
    >
    > *   Players with 7 or more professional points in the 2003 Chicago Pro Standings.
    > *   DCI players with a Constructed rating of 1750 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI Constructed ratings published on March 19, 2003.

**SIDE EVENTS** 

*   **Grand Prix–Bangkok Trials** ([Full List of Trials](/en/events/2003/bangkok03/grand-prix-bangkok-trial-schedule))
    **Friday, July 11:** Trials start at 1:00 p.m., 3:00 p.m., and 5:00 p.m. Registration closes 15 minutes prior to each Trial. Maximum 32 players per Trial, single-elimination. The top finisher receives a third-round bye at Grand Prix-Bangkok and one display of *Scourge*™ boosters. The format is Standard Constructed. Cost: THB250

*   **Pro Tour–Boston Qualifier**
     **Sunday, July 13:** Registration closes at 10:15 a.m.; play begins at 10:30 a.m. The top finishing team receives US$750 and an invitation to Pro Tour-Boston. The format is 3-person *Onslaught™-Legions™-Scourge* team Sealed Deck with team Rochester Draft finals. Cost: THB1,200 per 3-person team

*   **On Demand Tournaments**
     Eight-person single-elimination, on-demand tournaments will be offered throughout the weekend. Costs: Constructed THB150, Draft THB350, Sealed THB650
