Starting on April 1st and coinciding with the [2009 Magic Online Championship Series](/en/articles/magiconline/2009/03-10-magic-online-2009-championship-series) and the announced [changes in constructed prize support](http://forums.gleemax.com/showthread.php?t=1164559) we will select a different MTGO expansion or block on which to offer Nix Tix drafts.

Each week we will offer these drafts (no event tickets required) on different product in order to make it easier to try and earn qualifier points for the season championships and to coincide with our [Weekend Premier Events](/en/articles/uncategorised/2008/09-18-magic-online-weekend-premier-events). The announced events will start after each week's downtime and run until the next one begins.

We will offer 4-3-2-2, 8-4, and Swiss booster drafts for the product(s) selected each week.
