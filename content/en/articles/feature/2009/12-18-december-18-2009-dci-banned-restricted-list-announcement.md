**Effective Date:** January 1, 2010

 ***Zendikar* Block Constructed**, **Standard, Extended, Legacy, Vintage**  
 No changes

Changes to **Magic Online**–only formats are now announced monthly on the [**Magic Online** group page in Wizards Community](https://web.archive.org/web/20091218000000/http://community.wizards.com/magiconline).

For the complete list of all banned and restricted cards by format, click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20120303155950/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=judge/resources/banned).
