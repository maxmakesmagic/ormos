While Cruel Ultimatum is generally the tournament Ultimatum staple, James Nguyen and Sean Collins recently Top 8ed the StarCityGames.com Standard Open in Seattle with similar lists playing Brilliant Ultimatum. When you consider the fact they use Jace, the Mind Sculptor to make sure the top of their deck has an Emrakul, the Aeons Torn in it, it doesn't take much to see why such a deck might be successful!
