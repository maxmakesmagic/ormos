Here is a page from the *Shards of Alara* Style Guide, showing the creatures of Jund:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/06/81/068184d406e208763c53e18abb3d7f34569827d4ef7e15a28a22edd5d1441474.jpg)  

These guys have shown up all through the block. Consider [Savage Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20090521000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Hunger) from *Shards of Alara*:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/17/6f/176fd075c0c10834fcfedc0c316f2da9f8ceaf36bc34aca2d6fa0220f8a6775d.jpg)*[Savage Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20090521000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Hunger) art by Trevor Claxton*  
  

By the time the shards have fully converged in *Alara Reborn*, the creatures of Alara are showing that they belong to multiple shards. Specifically, check out [Marrow Chomper](https://web.archive.org/web/20090521000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Marrow+Chomper), which would be a generic Jund monster...if it weren't for the fact that it's also *undead!*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/28/86/2886f084d5563920806e8cd4023fdbd1f5cb11259fdef689412dac3525d8ef66.jpg)*[Marrow Chomper](https://web.archive.org/web/20090521000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Marrow+Chomper) art by Lars Grant-West*
