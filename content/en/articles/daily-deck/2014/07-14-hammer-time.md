This deck has a little bit of everything. Plan A is to accelerate out a [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) effect, preferably on the second turn via [Simian Spirit Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simian+Spirit+Guide) or [Desperate Ritual](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desperate+Ritual). [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) has a huge effect on most games, stifling opposing mana bases often filled with dual lands and utility lands.

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Koth%20of%20the%20Hammer)

Plan B is to play an efficient threat, many of which are capable of winning the game on their own. [Figure of Destiny](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Figure+of+Destiny), [Boros Reckoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Reckoner), [Koth of the Hammer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Koth+of+the+Hammer), and [Thundermaw Hellkite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thundermaw+Hellkite) are each big threats that can close the game quickly when unanswered. Given the dedication to [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) and acceleration, each threat needs to be capable of winning the game on its own. These creatures have been selected to do so.

Finally, there is a suite of disruption. [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt), [Skred](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skred), and [Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm) are all excellent removal spells, and [Skred](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skred) and [Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm) in particular have a great interaction with [Boros Reckoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Reckoner). As for noncreature disruption, [Relic of Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Relic+of+Progenitus) makes its way into the main deck so the sideboard can contain many different ways to interact with opposing strategies. [Leyline of Sanctity](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leyline+of+Sanctity), [Shatterstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shatterstorm), [Torpor Orb](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torpor+Orb), and [Combust](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Combust) provide some very hateful cards for popular strategies in Modern. Besides, you haven't lived until you've cast [Combust](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Combust) on your own [Boros Reckoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20140714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Reckoner) for lethal damage!
