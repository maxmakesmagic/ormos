### Collector's Highlights

The *Exodus* expert expansion for **Magic: The Gathering** offers 143 new cards and concluded the *Rath Cycle* block.

### New Features

Collect the [answers to your rules questions](https://web.archive.org/web/20080811000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/expert/Exodus/Exodus_FAQ.asp).

See the [changes to **Magic** cards](https://web.archive.org/web/20080811000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/expert/Exodus/Exodus_Changes.asp), starting with Exodus.

### Story Line

Join the crew of the Weatherlight as we explore the last journey of the *Rath Cycle* in [The Exodus Storybook](https://web.archive.org/web/20080811000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/expert/Exodus/Exodus_Backstory.asp).
