Through fifteen rounds, the original field of 1,426 players had been reduced to a mere eight who would now duke it out in the Top 8—for the fame, the glory, and potentially the trophy. Time to learn a bit more about each of those eight!

* * *

# Christian Hauck

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppar15/t8_hauck.jpg)

**Age:** 30  
**Hometown:** Mutterstadt, Germany  
**Occupation:** Controller  
**Favorite dinosaur:** Stegosaurus which kinda looks like [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino)  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Top 8 at Grand Prix Barcelona, Top 8 at Nationals 2010.

**What deck did you play and why did you choose it?**  
 Abzan Aggro, the most solid choice in a wide-open field.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**  
 Cut one [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall) for the fourth [Abzan Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Charm) perhaps.

**What is the most important card in your deck?**  
[Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino), and after sideboarding, [Den Protector](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Den+Protector).

* * *

# Antonio Del Moral León

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppar15/t8_delmoralleon.jpg)

**Age:** 20  
**Hometown:** Córdoba, Spain  
**Occupation:** *Magic* player  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Won a Pro Tour, won a MOCS, cash at various Grand Prix.

**What deck did you play and why did you choose it?**  
 Abzan Control. I think it's good in the metagame.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**  
 None.

**What is the most important card in your deck?**  
[Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion)

* * *

# Hugo Diniz

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppar15/t8_diniz.jpg)

**Age:** 25  
**Hometown:** Funchal, Madeira, Portugal  
**Occupation:** Nephrology Resident at Hospital in São João  
**Favorite dinosaur:** [Deathmist Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathmist+Raptor)  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 23rd at the 2014 World *Magic* Cup with Team Portugal. 0-6 at one WMCQ before qualifying.

**What deck did you play and why did you choose it?**  
 Green Devotion with a splash of red. I was going to play Abzan Control until Thursday, when I lost six matches in a row and decided to switch to this because of its good matchups against green-white decks like Abzan, Bant, et cetera.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**  
 I like the main deck, maybe play a third [Den Protector](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Den+Protector) or [Arc Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Lightning) in the sideboard.

**What is the most important card in your deck?**  
 Vespa Rainha! Or, in English, [Hornet Queen](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hornet+Queen)

* * *

# Tristan Pölzl

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppar15/t8_polzl.jpg)

**Age:** 27  
**Hometown:** Toulouse, France  
**Occupation:** Chemistry technician  
**Favorite dinosaur:** Old Fossile  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Won MKM Rome Standard, won Ovinospring Legacy.

**What deck did you play and why did you choose it?**  
 Mono-Red Goblins. It's the most stable deck in the format.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**  
 None.

**What is the most important card in your deck?**  
 All the 1-drops.

# Jasper Grimmer

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppar15/t8_grimmer.jpg)

**Age:** Jasper Grimmer  
**Hometown:** 26  
**Occupation:** Berlin, Germany  
**Favorite dinosaur:** Educator  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Top 8 at GP Malmö 2012.

**What deck did you play and why did you choose it?**  
 Abzan Aggro Morphs. I've been listening to Standard Master Brad Nelson on the SSL.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**  
 Cut the [Warden of the First Tree](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warden+of+the+First+Tree) in the sideboard for probably another [Drown in Sorrow](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drown+in+Sorrow). Otherwise it's great.

**What is the most important card in your deck?**  
 Still [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino). Although every hand with [Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion) is great.

# Amand Dosimont

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppar15/t8_dosimont.jpg)

**Age:** 32  
**Hometown:** La Louviere, Belgium  
**Favorite dinosaur:** Raptors if they are in my opponent's deck  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Grand Prix Prague Top 16.

**What deck did you play and why did you choose it?**  
 Abzan Aggro because I had not finished my Atarka Red's decklist during sitting.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**  
 I would add more Anafenzas and [Abzan Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Charm)s but that will make my deck illegal.

**What is the most important card in your deck?**  
[Abzan Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Charm).

# Kyoungsoo Kim

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppar15/t8_kyoungsoo.jpg)

**Age:** 33  
**Hometown:** Pusan, South Korea  
**Occupation:** Financial planner  
**Favorite dinosaur:** What?  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Korea National Team finalist in 2002 and 2009. Took part in the last World Championship in San Francisco. Top 32 at Grand Prix Taipei 2013.

**What deck did you play and why did you choose it?**  
 Mono-Red. I'm just sightseeing with my wife. So I have to sightsee with my wife at night. I never expected my Top 8!

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**  
 I want to have one more [Searing Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blood) in my sideboard. It is very good against morphs.

**What is the most important card in your deck?**  
[Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames).
