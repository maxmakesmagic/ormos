At the StarCityGames.com Invitational this past weekend, Jonathan Job showcased an exciting new Standard deck featuring [Parallel Lives](https://web.archive.org/web/20111216000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Parallel+Lives)! Duplicating everything from Golem tokens to [Mimic Vat](https://web.archive.org/web/20111216000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mimic+Vat) tokens, the enchantment works overtime in this strategy. Check it out, and bring it to your Friday Night **Magic** tournament tonight!
