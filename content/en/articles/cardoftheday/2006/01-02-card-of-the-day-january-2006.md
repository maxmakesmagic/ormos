Many gold cards are created by adding one ability for each color in the mana cost (such as <a>Selesnya Sagittars</a>). However, another way to create gold cards is by identifying an area where both colors overlap, and then giving them something better than normal. Because red and white overlap on the first strike ability, the Boros Swiftblade was given double strike and costed more aggressively since both colors contribute to this one ability.
