After many rounds of Swiss and some great games of *Magic*, the Top 8 for Grand Prix Chicago is set! Learn more about this event’s Top 8 competitors, their history at the top tables, and more by taking a look through the Top 8 profiles.

  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpchi14/t8_sullivan.jpg)

 

### Adrian Sullivan

**Age:** 37  
**Hometown:** Madison, WI  
**Occupation:** Gaming Industry

 

 

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
 Top 8 GP Memphis and a million lesser accomplishments.

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?**  
 WU Control. It's so resilient and powerful and I know it VERY well.

**What would you change about your deck after this weekend?**  
 Nothing.

**What has been the most important card in your deck this weekend and why?**  
[Elixir of Immortality](https://web.archive.org/web/20140622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elixir+of+Immortality). It gave me inevitability.

**What will getting to play at the Pro Tour in Hawaii mean to you?**  
 Being back for my twentieth Pro Tour!

 

 

 

 

  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpchi14/t8_klomparens.jpg)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpchi14/t8_boettcher.jpg)

 

### Jared Boettcher

**Age:** 22  
**Hometown:** Rensselaer, NY  
**Occupation:** MTG Pro

 

 

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
 PT *Born of the Gods* 9th, PT *Journey into Nyx* 10th, GP DC 2013 2nd, GP Cincinnati 2014 3rd

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?**  
 Black Devotion splashing red. It's a consistent deck and has a lot of powerful cards.

**What would you change about your deck after this weekend?**  
 Would have to think about it more. Maybe change [Silence the Believers](https://web.archive.org/web/20140622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silence+the+Believers) to a second [Erebos, God of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20140622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Erebos%2C+God+of+the+Dead).

**What has been the most important card in your deck this weekend and why?**  
[Whip of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos) was there when I needed to stabilize against the red decks I played this weekend.

**What will getting to play at the Pro Tour in Hawaii mean to you?**  
 I was already qualified, but, either way, it means a new place to add to my list of travels and the start of a fresh Pro Tour season.

 

 

 

 

  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpchi14/t8_rubin.jpg)

 

### Steve Rubin

**Age:** 22  
**Hometown:** Pittsburgh, PA  
**Occupation:** Student

 

 

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
 None

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?**  
 Black Devotion splashing green. It's the deck I'm the most comfortable playing.

**What would you change about your deck after this weekend?**  
 I would find room for an additional [Brindle Boar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brindle+Boar) or [Staff of the Death Magus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Staff+of+the+Death+Magus) in the sideboard.

**What has been the most important card in your deck this weekend and why?**  
[Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat). It helped me race against fast decks and win mirrors.

**What will getting to play at the Pro Tour in Hawaii mean to you?**  
 It will be nice to be qualified for the first Pro Tour of the season for the first time.

 

 

 

 

  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpchi14/t8_wise.jpg)

**Occupation:** Transportation

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpchi14/t8_blum.jpg)

 

### Tyler Blum

**Age:** 24  
**Hometown:** Calgary, Alberta Canada  
**Occupation:** Student

 

 

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
 3rd Canadian Nationals 2005, played in 3 Pro Tours

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?**  
 Black Devotion. At 11:45 p.m. Friday night I was losing a lot with Blue/White Control. Neal Oliver had a backup Black Devotion deck! Thanks to Jon Stern for the list and Francis Toussaint and Alexander Hayne for coaching me.

**What would you change about your deck after this weekend?**  
 -2 [Temple of Malady](https://web.archive.org/web/20140622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temple+of+Malady) +2 [Temple of Deceit](https://web.archive.org/web/20140622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temple+of+Deceit)

**What has been the most important card in your deck this weekend and why?**  
[Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat). So many free wins. Very vital in going 6-0 vs. Blue Devotion.

**What will getting to play at the Pro Tour in Hawaii mean to you?**  
 Everything. I play this game to play on the Pro Tour!
