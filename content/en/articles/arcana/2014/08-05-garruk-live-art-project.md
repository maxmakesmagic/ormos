One of the cool side programs at [Pro Tour Magic 2015](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-magic-2015-coverage-events) this past weekend was to have artists [Eric Deschamps](https://web.archive.org/web/20140805000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22Eric+Deschamps%22]) and [Cynthia Sheppard](https://web.archive.org/web/20140805000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22Cynthia+Sheppard%22]) create an exclusive piece of art featuring M15-focal character Garruk Wildspeaker! Between rounds, players and spectators alike could spin a wheel to randomly determine a new visual element to include in the piece, from Fblthp to a rainbow.

Here is a look at the piece as it progressed from initial sketch to final piece:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/Garruk_PROCESS.gif)
