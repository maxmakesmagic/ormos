## *Legacy Cube* Ends Tomorrow

For those of you who love the power level of *Vintage Cube* without the huge spikes that come with actual power, you have one more day to enjoy *Legacy Cube* before it ends!

Check out the full card list and most up-to-date changes [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/04-05-legacy-cube-cardlist).

## *Alt-Vintage Cube*: A New Take on a Classic

MTGO's *Vintage Cube* returns this Wednesday, reinterpreted by Cube aficionados Gaby Spartz and Luis Scott-Vargas. To find out what this means for you, be sure to check out [the article](/en/articles/magiconline/2021/05-26-spotlight-cube-series-alt-vintage-cube) that walks through how they approached the process, what changes they made, and available archetypes . . . and, no, it's not just a mono-storm cube.

## Showcase Challenge Weekend

Players looking to prove their mettle against the best players in *Magic Online* will want to use their QPs to enter *Magic Online* Champions Showcase (MOCS) events such as the returning Showcase Challenges this weekend. These MOCS events are a set of linked events designed to highlight the best players on *Magic Online* each season, culminating in the Champions Showcase and its prize pool of $70,000 and a Set Championship invitation. Each of these events requires 40 QPs to enter. The Top 8 finishers from each event will earn a qualification token to enter the appropriate Showcase Qualifier in August.

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format Challenges are the first step toward qualifying for Premier Play through your favorite *Magic* formats and provide excellent prizes for those looking to test their mettle in their favorite formats. To learn more about Premier Play qualification, click [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021).

*Magic Online*'s Format Challenges run once on Saturdays and once on Sundays for each Constructed format! On weekends featuring Showcase Challenge events, the Format Challenge that starts at either 6 a.m., 8 a.m., or 10 a.m. Pacific time is pre-empted by the Showcase Challenge—Format Challenges scheduled at other times remain in place. *NOTE: Pauper and Standard Challenges are never pre-empted.*
