Want to know what Aaron’s favorite *Dissension* card is? Will Jonny Magic be returning to the spotlight at Pro Tour-Prague? Download the podcast now to find the answers to all your questions!

If you’re already a subscriber, the audio file should be in your virtual hands already – and if you get your podcast via iTunes, you’ll be able to see the card Randy and Aaron preview!
