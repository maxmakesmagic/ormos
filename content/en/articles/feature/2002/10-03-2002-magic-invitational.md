### Pros Go Online October 18-20

* * *

You might have noticed a subtle addition to the **Main Room** after the October 1 update. For those of you who missed it, it looks like this:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ce/ee/ceee2366bc57174e43e2d12f94839b446d7fd2eb2ef2d0978e287aeb5361ccee.jpg)

What's the **Magic: The Gathering** Invitational about? It's about sixteen of the top Pro Tour players getting together to battle it out for the chance to make his own **Magic** card. This year, from October 18 through October 20, they'll be doing it all right here on ***Magic** Online.*

Not only does the winner get to design the card, but he gets his face on it as well. This being the Seventh Annual **Magic** Invitational, there are already six of these cards in existence, which you get a taste of on the buttons in the **Invitational** room.

### Coverage

While [*SideboardTM* Online](https://web.archive.org/web/20021003000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=sideboard&origin=magic_magiconline_invit02-intro) will be doing their own exceptional coverage as always, you'll also be able to keep track of the Invitational on the [***Magic** Online* web site](https://web.archive.org/web/20050323090832/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline). In the coming weeks, we'll provide more information about the players, the formats, and other interesting stuff that's going on.

### Replays

All the games played during the Invitational will be available for replay after they're finished. Just go to the room you want and click on the table the match took place. A menu will appear where you can choose the game you want to watch. In addition, you'll be able to replay the draft portion of Rochester Drafts.

One of the tweaks we've added for the Invitational is you'll be able to see both players' hands during the replay. So get out that comfy chair, because it's time to do some armchair quarterbacking online.

### Gunslinging

Think you've got what it takes to be on the Pro Tour? Have you built the killer Standard deck? Bring it to the **Gunslinging** area on October 20 and see if you're right.

Each Invitational player has his own **Gunslinging** room, where he'll pick opponents to play against in Standard Constructed Format (sorry kids, no *OnslaughtTM* cards allowed). Open a table up in a room and get a taste of what the Pro Tour players are packing.

Keep an eye on the [*Sideboard* Online web site](https://web.archive.org/web/20021003000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=sideboard&origin=magic_magiconline_invit02-intro), [MagicTheGathering.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20021003000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/welcome&origin=magic_magiconline_invit02-intro), and [this site](https://web.archive.org/web/20050323090832/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline) for more information on the Invitational in the coming weeks.
