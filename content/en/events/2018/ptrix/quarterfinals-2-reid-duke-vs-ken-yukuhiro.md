One of the first matches to get underway on Sunday's Pro Tour *Rivals of Ixalan* Top 8 matches featured a Modern showcase of "classic midrange archetype" versus "classic shenanigans".

Reid Duke, Ultimate Guard Pro Team member and No. 2 ranked player, a little under a month ago noted that many in the Modern *Magic* community felt that the days of [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) and [Liliana of the Veil](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+of+the+Veil) were long gone.

> All 20 articles that I read this morning tell me that Tarmogoyf and Liliana are bad in Modern. Should I take it as a lesson or as a challenge?
> 
> — Reid Duke (@ReidDuke) [January 10, 2018](https://twitter.com/ReidDuke/status/951123053525823490?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw)

It was of little surprise that Duke sleeved up a variation of the popular black-green midrange deck. Archetypes like Jund and Abzan have been his go-to options in Modern for nearly the entire time Modern has even been a format people could play, and he wasn't about to let naysayers slow him down.

Challenge accepted, and conquered, as Duke showcases just how good he is with the specific archetype.

Ken Yukuhiro, in his fourth Pro Tour Top 8 and from the Musashi super-team, however, is known to be a little more varied in his deck choices. The Japanese pro—a regular streamer in Japan—chose to go with something a little fresher to the format. His Black-Red [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One) deck was capable of some of the format's most frightening starts, as a first-turn [Burning Inquiry](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Inquiry) can enable any [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One)s that remain in his hand to be free to cast. Multiple 4/4s on turn one can be close to unbeatable, especially when backed with any sort of disruption.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/ptrix/qf_duke_yukuhiro.jpg)  
*Both Reid Duke and Ken Yukuhiro have been in the same Top 8 before. Now, they have the opportunity to play against each other on Sunday to see which of these two seasoned competitors would keep Pro Tour champion dreams alive.*

As the two shuffled, both players reminisced on one of their last times seeing each other in the prep area for Sunday's Pro Tour matches.

"We made Top 8 together in my last one too, at Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*," Duke noted, as Yukuhiro nodded and smiled. It had been about a year and a half since the last time both were in the same Sunday playoff. "Yes, good combination," he replied.

The two were always professionals in the feature match area, courteous to opponents and everyone around them, but both had multiple early exists on Pro Tour Sundays, and both had a fire to make this the Pro Tour that they finally win.

# The Games

Duke started the first game at six cards, his opener getting more interesting once Yukuhiro led off the match with [Burning Inquiry](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Inquiry) following a [Street Wraith](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Street+Wraith) cycle. After a bunch of random discards, Yukuhiro was left without [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt), [Goblin Lore](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Lore), and [Faithless Looting](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faithless+Looting), while Duke was left without a [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), [Blooming Marsh](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blooming+Marsh), and [Nihil Spellbomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nihil+Spellbomb).

Of course, that wasn't what drew cries of excitement. That was attributed to Yukuhiro's two follow-up [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One)s. It made Duke's [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek) feel fairly innocuous, especially when it was only left taking away a [Flamewake Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flamewake+Phoenix). Once the phoenix was recurred, Duke's [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) was formality of never conceding until lethal was dealt.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/ptrix/qf_yukuhiro.jpg)  
*Yukuhiro is known for some off-the-wall deck choices, and Black-Red [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One) fits nicely with his typical selections.*

In the second game, Duke's [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek) was far more effective on the play, robbing Yukuhiro of a [Burning Inquiry](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Inquiry) and preventing a potentially disastrous first-turn recreation of Game 1, seeing two [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One)s at the ready. A sigh of relief came from Duke when Yukuhiro only had a land on the first turn, as he got the first creature of the game on the battlefield with [Scavenging Ooze](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scavenging+Ooze). Yukuhiro went a step larger than that with Tasigur, the Golden King.

The legendary Sultai creature did not last long, as Duke untapped and pointed a [Maelstrom Pulse](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Maelstrom+Pulse) at it. Yukuhiro, upon drawing [Goblin Lore](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Lore) in the third turn, quickly cast it, and lost a [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One), [Street Wraith](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Street+Wraith), and a land. Despite losing one of his two namesake artifact creatures, he still deployed the other 4/4, then pointed a [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) at Duke's Scavenger Ooze.

The 4/4 quickly looked small in the face of Duke's follow-up [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf). Another [Goblin Lore](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Lore) from Yukuhiro showcased the power of his deck, with the possibility to chain multiple massive draw spells. The second one had him bin another land, a [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt), and a [Flamewake Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flamewake+Phoenix), which he immediately brought back from the graveyard. [Gurmag Angler](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gurmag+Angler) also gave him some ability to fight the size of Duke's [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf), exiling his already-binned [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One) and some instants.

However, when Yukuhiro went for an attack, Duke had a [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push) to destroy Yukuhiro's [Flamewake Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flamewake+Phoenix) (revolt on thanks to [Marsh Flats](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Marsh+Flats)), powering up his [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) to a 4/5 to profitably block the attacking [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One). With the failed attack, Yukuhiro fell on the back foot. His third [Goblin Lore](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Lore) let him dig further, but he was quickly running out of fuel. A desperation flashback [Faithless Looting](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faithless+Looting) was enough for him to move to sideboarding.

Yukuhiro led the third game off the same as the first, with [Burning Inquiry](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Inquiry) causing some nervous smiles all around. Duke was left without a [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) and two lands, while Duke binned three basics. Unfortunately, none of the discards were as important as the two [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One)s, and the [Gurmag Angler](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gurmag+Angler) revealed by Duke's [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek) did not make matters better. Duke, in searching for his second land, opted against his formality from Game 1, conceding when he saw there was no chain of plays that could get him out of the situation.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/ptrix/qf_duke.jpg)  
*Duke's natural poise rarely—if ever—lets off any tell of what he may be thinking or what he may have.*

Duke led off Game 4 with [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress), ridding Yukuhiro of [Burning Inquiry](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Inquiry) and leaving him with nothing more than a bunch of cards that weren't going to enter play anytime soon. Duke bought enough time to deploy [Liliana, the Last Hope](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana%2C+the+Last+Hope), but by that time, Yukuhiro found a second [Burning Inquiry](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Inquiry), leaving Yukuhiro without a [Flameblade Adept](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flameblade+Adept), [Flamewake Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flamewake+Phoenix), and [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One) and Duke without a [Grim Flayer](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Flayer), Lililana, the Last Hope, and [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push). The resulting discards left Yukuhiro with few additional lands and no real follow-up.

[Collective Brutality](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collective+Brutality) left Yukuhiro without a [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt), and [Nihil Spellbomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nihil+Spellbomb) ensured Yukuhiro would have no food for his in-hand [Gurmag Angler](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gurmag+Angler). Without any real action, Yukuhiro was left watching as [Liliana, the Last Hope](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana%2C+the+Last+Hope) continued to tick up in loyalty. A [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls) army came down to protect her, and while a third [Burning Inquiry](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Inquiry) found a [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One) and enabled [Flamewake Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flamewake+Phoenix), it was not nearly enough to come back from the looming [Liliana, the Last Hope](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana%2C+the+Last+Hope) emblem that was going to come in the next turn.

Yukuhiro led off the final game not with a [Burning Inquiry](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Inquiry), but instead a [Faithless Looting](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faithless+Looting), discarding [Flamewake Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flamewake+Phoenix). Duke, meanwhile, opened not with an [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek), but instead a [Grafdigger's Cage](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grafdigger%27s+Cage). Yukuhiro followed with a [Gurmag Angler](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gurmag+Angler), exiling his graveyard, fodder all coming from [Faithless Looting](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faithless+Looting) and two fetch lands. Duke had little in terms of action, and the Angler presented a problem. After two attacks from the Angler, it was clear what was up when Duke chose cycle through a [Nihil Spellbomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nihil+Spellbomb) with little to exile from Yukuhiro's graveyard.

He was drowning in lands, and needed any sort of action.

A [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) revealed [Big Game Hunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Big+Game+Hunter) lying in wait, as Duke was pressed to find removal for the 5/5 Zombie Fish.

[Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf), however, was all that he could find. Yukuhiro, weary of a [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push) being disastrous for him, used [Goblin Lore](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Lore) to try and find a [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One) that could be a backup target for his [Big Game Hunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Big+Game+Hunter). He did, and a [Faithless Looting](https://web.archive.org/web/20180204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faithless+Looting) to enabler the madness on his Hunter was enough to earn the concession from Duke.

**Ken Yukuhiro defeats Reid Duke 3-2 and advances to the semifinals!**
