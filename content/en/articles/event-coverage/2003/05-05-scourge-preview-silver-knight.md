Dragons, dragons and more dragons. You can turn into a dragon! We have undead dragons that bring back other dragons! There’s a new Wheel of Fortune dragon coming up!

That’s what the *Scourge* previews have been all about up until this point. Huge flying beaters. It made me wonder whether we were really going back to the Stone Age of **Magic** again. Everyone was running Craw Wurms and Shivan Dragons because they were simply huge. Lord of the Pit ruled the skies, until Nightmare came along.

Today’s card, however, isn’t all about huge fliers. Back when *Tempest* was released, two of my favorite cards from the set where white weenies. I loved the elegance of Soltari Monk and Soltari Priest. They were solid beaters for ![White Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/55/78/55785924f8cda9ab2a85364f807ba172c7530e43d94b14aad222ec4bbf2589ca.gif)![White Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/55/78/55785924f8cda9ab2a85364f807ba172c7530e43d94b14aad222ec4bbf2589ca.gif), one with protection from red, and the other with protection from black. Unfortunately, the Monk proved to be rather unimportant, but Soltari Priest saw a lot of play back then. It wouldn’t die to red removal and there was no blocking it with artifact creatures either. Those two Soltari made sense from a color perspective as well; after all, it was only logical for white to hate black and red equally.

Soltari Monk however, always had some resemblance to White Knight, being a ![White Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/55/78/55785924f8cda9ab2a85364f807ba172c7530e43d94b14aad222ec4bbf2589ca.gif)![White Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/55/78/55785924f8cda9ab2a85364f807ba172c7530e43d94b14aad222ec4bbf2589ca.gif) bear. The Monk outclassed the Knight though, being better at its weenie role than the Knight. Soltari Priest never had a friend just like that. *Scourge* finally brings the missing piece in this puzzle with Silver Knight popping up. It’s just like White Knight except it has protection from red instead of black and that’s quite a difference. After all, Tom Champheng tried to play Sleight of Mind in his white weenie deck in the 1996 World Championships. One of the reasons for that was to change the Protection from Black into Protection from Red. It was just too bad he forgot to include the Adarkar Wastes on his deck list to actually cast it! *Scourge* is going to provide us with the Sleighted White Knight: Silver Knight.

Elegant and simple, Silver Knight is going to see play in each of the formats. It’s not just a solid creature on its own; it’s also an extremely good answer to one of the most consistent decks throughout time: goblins. While there have been better answers to the oncoming hordes of little red men, Silver Knight will not be a pretty sight for the goblin player. Add to that the difficulty red has of finishing the Knight off and you’ve got a great two drop. Take, for example, the decks from Pro Tour-Venice. White Knight was crappy as it died to Shock, Lightning Rift, Sparksmith, Starstorm and it couldn’t do a thing about a Goblin Goon on the other side of the table. Silver Knight, on the other hand, will just shrug those off. If the Slide decks in Venice would’ve had access to Silver Knight, the goblin decks would’ve had very little chance at beating Slide. Silver Knight is definitely going to be a big nuisance if goblins would prove to still be viable when the *Onslaught* Block Constructed PTQ season rolls around.

In Standard however, Silver Knight isn’t going to be all that spectacular. At the moment, red is only being used in r-g and while Silver Knight will survive all the removal that deck can throw at it, it won’t be able to block all that many creatures. All of r-g’s creatures are potentially non-red 3/3’s, so protection from red isn’t going to accomplish all that much. Instead in Standard, people remove creatures with Smother and Chainer’s Edict and neither of those has any problem finishing off Silver Knight.

The last relevant Constructed format will notice the addition of Silver Knight. In Extended, people love to run the little red men. While removing Silver Knight is far from impossible in Extended, they do need Cursed Scroll or Barbarian Ring to finish one off. With White Weenie living up in the past Extended season, Silver Knight should find a spot in at least some of those decks. Where Soltari Priest was unable to block red’s creatures, Silver Knight will stop the hordes of hounds, goblins and cats coming your way. With Warrior En-Kor, you’ll effectively get a second protection from red creature on the board. All of this will make Sligh vs. White Weenie in Extended a whole different game than it was before.

Finally, Silver Knight is also very important in Limited. While it isn’t a soldier for the soldier tribe, it does fill the 2-drop slot for white. Between Glory Seeker and Whipcorder in *Onslaught*, White Knight in *Legions* and Silver Knight in *Scourge*, a white drafter should be able to find enough two-drops for his draft deck. With *Legions* added to the format, having early pressure has become much more important. Of course, *Scourge* might deliver more cheap creatures, but it’s going to be hard to be more efficient than a Silver Knight. Also, from what has been shown so far, the casting costs in *Scourge* are going through the roof. That would make cheap weenies even more valuable. If people don’t watch out with the high casting cost cards, one Silver Knight could beat down for 14 or more life points before it is checked… and of course you could’ve added more creatures to your army as well.

I think anyone who has played **Magic** for some time will recognize the resemblance between the two Soltari and the two Knights. If you play a turn two Silver Knight when your opponent only has a mountain in play, the look on their face should be anything but happy. While weenies never really leave a real impression on the history of the game, there is certain elegance about them. They’re not as splashy as Rith, the Awakener used to be, but they are extremely efficient and have a lot of power packed for very little mana. Silver Knight is another one of those packages… as a result, I expect Silver Knight to end up as one of white’s trademark weenies. Efficient and clean. 2/2 for 2. Hard to remove.

That’s what Silver Knight is.
