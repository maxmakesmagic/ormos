***Hour of Devastation*/*Amonkhet* Sealed PTQs for Pro Tour *Ixalan* Friday andSunday, August 4 and 6**

Attention all Pro Tour hopefuls: Two Pro Tour Qualifier (PTQ) are approaching! We hope to see you in the PTQ Finals on Friday, August 4 and Sunday, August 6 at 7 a.m. PT. Remember to plan ahead and earn enough tokens for both events!

Learn more about *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20170801000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers).

**Upcoming Standard MOCS Playoff on Saturday, August 5**

The third quarter of competition in the *Magic Online* Championship Series (MOCS) culminates on August 5 for the third [Playoff Event](https://web.archive.org/web/20170801000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2017-magic-online-championship-series#PlayoffEvents) of the series! Big prizes, the chance to move up the [MOCS Leaderboard](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/magic-online-leaderboard-points), plus invitations to the *Magic Online* Championship and *Pro Tour Ixalan* are all on the line in this event.

Learn more about the 2017 *Magic Online* Championship Series and how to qualify for future Playoff Events [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20170801000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2017-magic-online-championship-series).

***Magic Online* Format Challenges back this weekend**

The *Magic Online* Format Challenges introduced a new prize structure and recurring weekend events for Modern, Vintage, 1v1 Commander, Legacy, and Pauper formats! Check out Lee Sharpe's [article](https://web.archive.org/web/20170801000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-format-challenges) for specific details.

Events begin this Saturday, August 5 at 8:00 a.m. PT.
