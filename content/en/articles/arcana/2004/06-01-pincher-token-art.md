Y[esterday](/en/articles/arcana/2004/05-31-the-stations-mural) you read the story of how the four *Fifth Dawn* Stations got their art. The interchangeable mural by Greg Staples and Steve Tappin was a great success.

But the story doesn't end there. The art team liked so much what Steve Tappin did with the little pincher in the art of **Summoning Station**:

![Summoning Station art by Steve Tappin](https://i.ormos.uk/23/a4/23a452f64dea5c3698d8d6be8c68ec6c62da32488482021ab7abe1106e886701.jpg)  
 *Summoning Station art by Steve Tappin* 

...that they commissioned him to do the art for the upcoming Pincher creature token card.

![Pincher token art by Steve Tappin](https://i.ormos.uk/d4/a8/d4a8bec0fa909bb279cffdc56219ff75653136140dc0e3a581475b8838d578b2.jpg)  
 *Pincher token art by Steve Tappin* 

The Pincher token card will be available from the **Magic** Player Rewards program.
