### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, six 15-card booster packs, one foil basic land card, one other random foil card, the *Legions* novel, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/36/6a/366aa0c485d9b370ed7fa29e47970d1b4fbedc49620e307292191a5440da1fe2.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/36/6a/366aa0c485d9b370ed7fa29e47970d1b4fbedc49620e307292191a5440da1fe2.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0c/44/0c44e0749e0260eef5f76dbc0492c33d2429a6673b5cc72d698cf04a3ad79a53.png)*Legions* is a 145-card, creature-only set with randomly inserted foil-finished premium cards. But with creatures that have abilities like Provoke, Amplify, Morph, and Slivers, who needs spells?

**Set Name:** *Legions*  
**Block:** Set 2 of 3 of the *Onslaught* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 145

**Languages:** English, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719045013/https://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/faq/legions) (or download as [RTF](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719045013/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/Legions_FAQ.rtf))

**Game Design:** Mike Elliott (lead) and Michael Donais

**Game Development:** William Jockusch (lead), Elaine Chase, Michael Donais, and Worth Wollpert
