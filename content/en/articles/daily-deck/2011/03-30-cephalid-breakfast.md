A blast from the past, Cephalid Breakfast, has returned to the world of Legacy. Using the powerful Cephalid Illusionist, the Breakfast deck the blue creature with *en*-Kor creatures, redirecting damage over and over again (whether there is any on the stack or not). When the graveyard is stocked, Narcomoeba allow the deck to Dread Return a juicy target back. In this case, Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker works nicely with Sky Hussar to generate infinite 4/3 flyers. Read more from the [event coverage](http://www.starcitygames.com/php/news/expandnews.php?Article=21462) of the StarCityGames.com Legacy Open in Los Angeles.
