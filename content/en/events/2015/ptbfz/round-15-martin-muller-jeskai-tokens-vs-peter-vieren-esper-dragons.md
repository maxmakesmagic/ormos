Martin Müller was among the youngest competitors to participate in the World Championship this year. Balancing his school and friends at home with the world travel of competitive *Magic* has been a whirlwind of successes. A team Denmark victory at the 2014 World *Magic* Cup, a Grand Prix Top 8, and an appearance at the 2015 World Championship put Müller firmly on the competitive map.

And he wasn't alone in reaching even higher at Pro Tour *Battle for Zendikar*.

Peter Vieren had a similar background to Müller's but one that came over the years. With a 2012 World *Magic* Cup appearance as part of Belgium's team and two Grand Prix Top 8s half a decade apart, Vieren was after the same thing as Müller: A breakthrough to Pro Tour Top 8.

Both players entered the round with a record of 11-3, putting each into position to draw into Top 8 if they took the win here.

# The Decks

While there were plenty of Jeskai decks in the tournament, Müller's Jeskai Tokens was among the oldest and less popular. Using [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy) and the new [Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar) to pump up creatures—like Goblins from [Hordeling Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hordeling+Outburst) and [Dragon Fodder](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragon+Fodder)—the ability for the deck to chain spells and explode with damage was high. With [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) to help keep the hand full of pressure, it had been enough to keep Müller in the line of battles for a potential Top 8 appearance.

Vieren's Esper Dragons was another returning face from the previous Standard. Swapping in new options, like [Scatter to the Winds](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scatter+to+the+Winds), [Complete Disregard](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Complete+Disregard), the faces of [Dragonlord Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai) and [Dragonlord Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar) were completed by the potent [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy). With a suite of removal and countermagic for every situation, Esper Dragons looked to take hold of the game and never let it go.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptbfz/ptbfz_r15_muller_vieren.jpg)  
*The last rounds of Swiss at a Pro Tour tend to be tense, as Martin Müller and Peter Vieren's Top 8 dreams were hanging in the balance.*

# The Games

Vieren led the way in the first game with [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy) in the second turn but Müller had none of that thanks to [Fiery Impulse](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Impulse). [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy) was Müller's reply thereafter, which Vieren hit with [Utter End](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Utter+End) after untapping and taking away any chance for Müller to use it.

The [Hordeling Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hordeling+Outburst) that followed and Müller was poised to attack the following turn. Vieren had a follow up [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy) that Müller hit with a [Silkwrap](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silkwrap), beginning his Goblin assault. [Crux of Fate](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crux+of+Fate) finally cleared the rabble away for Vieren.

[Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar) was Müller's next call, but Vieren went big: [Dragonlord Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar) to steal Gideon and pop him for an emblem was a powerful way to answer the Planeswalker. Müller's follow-up [Hordeling Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hordeling+Outburst) seemed small compared to Vieren's [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time).

But looks could be deceiving.

[Silumgar's Scorn](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silumgar%27s+Scorn) countered Müller's second Gideon and a [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) stripped [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) as well. Another [Hordeling Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hordeling+Outburst) gave Müller a massive Goblin army as Vieren kept passing back to Müller with all his mana up.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptbfz/ptbfz_r15_muller.jpg)  
*Müller's deck can quickly turn a board of critters into a frightful assault if given the opportunity.*

But when Müller cast [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy), it resolved, and let him cast [Wild Slash](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Slash) to attack for a ton of damage. [Foul-Tongue Invocation](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foul-Tongue+Invocation) kept Vieren alive at 4 life, but a second [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy) on the next turn was enough for the tokens to take the lead.

Vieren opened the second game with a first turn [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress), taking out [Crackling Doom](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crackling+Doom) and leaving behind [Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar). A second turn [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy) forced the [Wild Slash](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Slash) Müller had into action. On the third turn [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) took out [Hordeling Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hordeling+Outburst). A fourth turn [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) cleared [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy).

While Vieren worked to keep Müller off of any action, neither player had hit four lands. The one Müller finally hit was a [Smoldering Marsh](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smoldering+Marsh) that didn't help him cast his two Gideons in waiting.

[Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) from Vieren let him begin to pull away with cards. [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy) appeared again for Vieren but Müller made three 1/1 Warrior tokens with [Secure the Wastes](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Secure+the+Wastes) in response. [Silumgar's Scorn](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silumgar%27s+Scorn) was waiting for the follow up [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy)—revealing [Dragonlord Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar). A second Scorn hit the [Narset Transcendent](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narset+Transcendent) Müller tried next.

[Virulent Plague](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Virulent+Plague) was the sideboard card for Vieren, annihilating Müller's meager forces.

With a second [Battlefield Forge](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battlefield+Forge), Müller could make Gideon, but at the cost of 2 life. [Dragonlord Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar) took the Gideon and made an emblem, but this time it was enough to give Vieren the tying game.

The third game began with [Dragon Fodder](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragon+Fodder) on the second turn from Müller but no matching [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy) for Vieren. A second [Dragon Fodder](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragon+Fodder) found the first [Silumgar's Scorn](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silumgar%27s+Scorn) in Vieren's hand. [Hordeling Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hordeling+Outburst) hit [Negate](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Negate) and Vieren kept the opposing forces from growing.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptbfz/ptbfz_r15_vieren.jpg)  
*Vieren's Dragonlords were very good at giving him an emblem collection.*

When Müller tried [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) next the wall of counters ceased. Out of reactive options, Vieren went for [Dragonlord Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai) but Müller revealed his own counter to stop it before reloading with [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) again. Vieren was down to 10 life from the repeated Goblin attacks. [Disdainful Stroke](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disdainful+Stroke) hit the second [Dragonlord Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai) from Vieren, and [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy) into [Hordeling Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hordeling+Outburst) for Müller's next turn put presumably lethal pressure on Vieren.

[Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) was the first step for Vieren on his turn, with [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy) as his follow. With the third [Dragon Fodder](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragon+Fodder), Müller's [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy) triggered and Vieren extended his hand.

"Good luck in the Top 8," Vieren said, shaking Müller's hand.

**Müller 2 – Vieren 1**
