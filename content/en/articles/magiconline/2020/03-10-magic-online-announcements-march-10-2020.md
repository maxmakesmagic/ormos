## Cube Spotlight Series: *Pauper Cube* March 11–18

Welcome back to the Cube Spotlight Series! After over 10 years maintaining the cube, Adam "Stybs" Styborski, member of the [Commander Advisory Group](https://mtgcommander.net/index.php/about-us/) (CAG) and *Magic* esports content and coverage teams, is ready to share *Pauper Cube* on MTGO. Entry options include both League and Single-Elimination Queue, and events cost 10 Event Tickets or 100 Play Points. *Pauper Cube* runs from March 11–18.

All undefeated players get their trophy *and* a copy of Pauper staple [Thorn of the Black Rose](https://web.archive.org/web/20200310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thorn+of+the+Black+Rose)! And because this is a bit of a special occasion, don't forget to login to *Magic Online* by March 18 to redeem the code "PauperCube2020" for your copy of [Standard Bearer](https://web.archive.org/web/20200310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Standard+Bearer) from *Apocalypse*. Even free accounts can get in on this promotion!

For more *Pauper Cube* information, check out Tolarian Community College's [announcement video](https://youtu.be/oOSAdOyMfaw) and go behind the scenes with Adam's [*Pauper Cube* article](/en/articles/uncategorised/2020/03-10-spotlight-cube-series-pauper-cube).

## Pioneer PTQ Saturday

On Sunday, March 14, there will be a Pioneer PTQ at a special start time of 4 a.m. PT (11:00 UTC)! The entry fee is 1 Red PTQ Finals Token or 40 [MOCS Qualifier Points](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020). The winner qualifies for their choice of events in Series 2 of the [2020 Players Tour](https://magic.gg/players-tour) in May.

Learn more about Players Tour Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020#ptq).

## Modern PTQ Sunday

On Sunday, March 15, there will be a Modern PTQ at 9 a.m. PT (16:00 UTC)!
