With the [announcement](/en/articles/feature/2011/06-20-june-20-2011-dci-banned-restricted-list-announcement-2) that Jace, the Mind Sculptor has been banned in Standard, deck builders around the world are trying to determine the best way to create new decks. Take this example of Exarch Twin, the popular combo deck. It centers around Deceiver Exarch and Splinter Twin, allowing you to create a an "infinite" supply of hasty Deceiver Exarchs. Jace, the Mind Sculptor provides consistency, "hides" key cards from discard like Despise or Duress, and bounces opposing Spellskites. Can you imagine a way to rebuild the deck without Jace?
