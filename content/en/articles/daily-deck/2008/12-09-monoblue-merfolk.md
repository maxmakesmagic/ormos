Merfolk have made a resurgence in popularity as of late, even earning a berth in the finals of Pro Tour-Hollywood. The tribe has a long history of success, and American player Alex Borteh took the above list all the way to the finals at the 2001 World Championships before losing to Tom Van de Logt of the Netherlands. Will the small blue creatures be able to surge back onto the main stage at the World Championships this year? We won't know til' the smoke has cleared Sunday, but you can tune in to catch all of the action as it happens on **magicthegathering.com**. The big show kicks off Thursday and [the live webcast](/en/events/2004/webcast/magic-the-gathering-webcast-schedule) is set to kick off from Memphis at 9:45 a.m. on December 14th.
