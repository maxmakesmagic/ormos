The goal of [The Lexicon](https://web.archive.org/web/20031215000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/columnarchive&column=thelexicon) is to teach players the meanings of some of the more obscure words we use in card names. While many of the offbeat names (like "[Groffskithur](https://web.archive.org/web/20031215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Groffskithur)") are simply made up, we often dig around dictionaries for real little-used and archaic words for inspiration. Check out these Lexicon entries drawn from *Mirrodin* cards.

![Lumengrid Augur](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Lumengrid+Augur)

**au·gur**   
*noun*

1.  One of a group of ancient Roman religious officials who foretold events by observing and interpreting signs and omens.
2.  A seer or prophet; a soothsayer.

![Empyrial Plate](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Empyrial+Plate)

**em·py·ri·al** *variant of **empyreal***   
*adjective*

1.  Of the sky; celestial.
2.  Elevated; sublime.

![Galvanic Key](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Galvanic+Key)

**gal·van·ic**  
*adjective*

1.  Of or relating to direct-current electricity, especially when produced chemically.
2.  *   Having the effect of an electric shock: *a galvanic revelation.*
    *   Produced as if by an electric shock: *The new leader had a galvanic effect on our morale.*

![Hematite Golem](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Hematite+Golem)

**he·ma·tite**   
*noun*

1.  A black or blackish-red to brick-red mineral, essentially Fe2O3, the chief ore of iron.

![Mesmeric Orb](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Mesmeric+Orb)

**mes·mer·ic**   
*adjective*

1.  attracting and holding interest as if by a spell: *she had a warm mesmeric charm*.

![Auriok Transfixer](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Auriok+Transfixer)

**trans·fix**   
*transitive verb*

1.  To pierce with or as if with a pointed weapon.
2.  To fix fast; impale.
3.  To render motionless, as with terror, amazement, or awe.

*Source: dictionary.com*
