Here's Into the Void from *Avacyn Restored*.

  

 ![Into the Void](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Into%20the%20Void&options=) 

  

There's something interesting about this card. Which is a good thing, because otherwise this Arcana would have a problem. Let's blow the art up and take a closer look.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/992_intothevoid.jpg) 

Into the Void | Art by [Daarken](https://web.archive.org/web/20120612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22daarken%22])

See those two things being swooped up into what is presumably the void? You've seen them before. Click here to see where!

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/992_hellrider.jpg) 

Hellrider | Art by [Svetlin Velinov](https://web.archive.org/web/20120612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22svetlin+velinov%22])

It's the devil and its mount from Hellrider, as seen in *Dark Ascension*!
