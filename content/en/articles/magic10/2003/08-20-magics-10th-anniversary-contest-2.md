### The Winning Stories

Over the summer we asked Magic players all across the globe to send us their own personal stories to celebrate the 10th Anniversary of **Magic: The Gathering**.

Our judges have finished selecting the winning stories and we're posting them here for all to read. Select a link to jump to the top five winners in that category.

**Categories**:  

*   [The best trip you've taken to go play Magic](/en/articles/magic10/2003/08-20-magics-10th-anniversary-contest-5)
*   [Most memorable place you've played Magic](/en/articles/magic10/2003/08-20-magics-10th-anniversary-contest-4)
*   [What you think is the best Magic card and why](/en/articles/magic10/2003/08-20-magics-10th-anniversary-contest-3)
*   [The best game of Magic you ever played and why it was great](/en/articles/magic10/2003/08-20-magics-10th-anniversary-contest)

Many thanks to everyone that sent us stories. It tooks us QUITE a while to read all of them, and we had some laughs and some giggles reading them. Congratulations to the winners.
