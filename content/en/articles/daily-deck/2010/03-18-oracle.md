For Konrad Sokolowski, *Worldwake* Game Day was about one creature: Oracle of Mul Daya. The player used the diminutive 2/2 as a card draw engine in a Bant-style control deck that made the Top 8 at GameLord in Lódz, Poland. No doubt it was an exciting time to be mana "flooded" as Oracle allowed Konrad to play additional lands off the top of his deck for free! For more Standard decks, check out [all the *Worldwake* Game Day deck lists](/en/events/2010/worldwakegameday/worldwake-game-day).
