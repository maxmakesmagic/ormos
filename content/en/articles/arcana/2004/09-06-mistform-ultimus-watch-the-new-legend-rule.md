Welcome to a supplemental edition of **Mistform Ultimus Watch**. In this recurring sub-feature of Magic Arcana, we keep track of what creature types that auto-updating *Legions* creature has recently become, and in effect, what creature types are part of **Magic**.

In today's case, we can report that Mistform Ultimus will actually *lose* one creature type in the near future -- but the card will still be functionally the same.

As Mark Rosewater's article [last week](/en/articles/makingmagic/2004/08-30-now-with-added-flavor) described, once *Champions of Kamigawa* becomes legal in tournaments, the Legend subtype will be replaced by the Legendary supertype. A "Creature - Bird Soldier Legend" (like Major Teroh) will become a "Legendary Creature - Bird Soldier" instead, bringing it in line with legendary artifacts and lands. 

So what happens to our pal Mistform Ultimus? The same thing. He will still be a legend, but he will be so in virtue of his new Legendary supertype as opposed to his Legend subtype. His new Oracle text type line will be "Legendary Creature - Illusion." (Of course, the "Illusion" part is still there just for flavor -- he's all the other creature types too.) 

The next edition of Mistform Ultimus Watch will be in October, after the release of *Champions of Kamigawa*, when the Ultimus takes up his katana and becomes a Samurai, among other things...
