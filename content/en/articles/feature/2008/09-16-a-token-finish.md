Fear. That’s the word that best describes a player facing down a playing field of token creatures when [Nantuko Husk](https://web.archive.org/web/20080916000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Husk), [Greater Gargadon](https://web.archive.org/web/20080916000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Greater+Gargadon), or [Furystoke Giant](https://web.archive.org/web/20080916000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Furystoke+Giant) hit the board. Brandon Lau, 2008 Australian Nationals finalist, knows all about that feeling. He used a half dozen token generators, including the easy-to-overlook [Kher Keep](https://web.archive.org/web/20080916000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kher+Keep), to flood the field with innocuous 1/1s. Shortly thereafter said 1/1s would turn into +2/+2 bonuses for the [Nantuko Husk](https://web.archive.org/web/20080916000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Husk), hasten a suspended Gargadon into play, or start [Shock](https://web.archive.org/web/20080916000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shock)ing everything in sight after having their fury stoked. For coverage of the 2008 Australian National Championship, check [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20080916000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/nicastri-conquers-australia-nats).
