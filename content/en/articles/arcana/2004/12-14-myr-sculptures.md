You know that the five [mana myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20041214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/default.asp?term=_myr&fields=%7Cname&format=Mirrodin&color=All&output=summary&sort=power&first=4) from *Mirrodin* all have a distinctive artifact-race look.

You may not know that they were recently immortalized in three dimensions by 3-D artist [Henry Higgenbotham](https://web.archive.org/web/20041214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/default.asp?term=Henry_G._Higgenbotham&fields=%7Cartist&format=Allsets&color=All&output=summary&sort=name&first=1) (visit his site at [www.henryh.com](http://www.henryh.com/)). Here are some photos of the statues he created and showed off at [2004's World Championships](https://web.archive.org/web/20041214000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/nuijten-nassif-dominate-worlds) in San Francisco.

![Myr sculpture](https://i.ormos.uk/bb/b8/bbb80ae813d737f3cb8af895e37fe009c721d69b39ec6035a17e80d55a756478.jpg) ![Myr sculpture](https://i.ormos.uk/a7/c9/a7c9eb129cda925d66218d4b31039cc6e531efef67f79c718d3618211b7ed293.jpg) ![Myr sculpture](https://i.ormos.uk/27/6f/276fa0eba4f023b110f9847a5e25da2072009510f4b02582dd4ea0d911930210.jpg)

Mark Rosewater has [mentioned](https://web.archive.org/web/20041214000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/mr90) in the [past](https://web.archive.org/web/20041214000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/mr127) how the Gnome creature type became Myr in *Mirrodin* block (except for [Bottle Gnomes](https://web.archive.org/web/20041214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bottle+Gnomes)) -- how fitting that these Myr sculptures are set up like garden gnomes in this photo below.... Maybe that's [plains4](https://web.archive.org/web/20041214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=plains4)!
