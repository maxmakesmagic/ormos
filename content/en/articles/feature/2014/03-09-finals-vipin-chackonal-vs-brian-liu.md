Could the birthday boy unwrap one of the sweetest presents in **Magic**? It's a question Vipin Chackonal wanted to answer in the affirmative. With his first deep Grand Prix run, on his birthday no less, Chackonal was one match away from an achievement perhaps no one else has claimed: Find a Grand Prix victory on his or her birthday.

Brian Liu would not be an easy opponent. After slaying the monstrous Melira Pod deck twice in back-to-back matches, the last remaining contender was one that Liu surely saw over the event: Affinity. The most popular deck in the room coming into Day 2, Liu was determined to break down the last machinations standing in his way.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Linvala%2C+Keeper+of+Silence)](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Linvala%2C+Keeper+of+Silence)

Chackonal's artifacts flooded the battlefield in Game 1, with [Signal Pest](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Signal+Pest), [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating), [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter), [Springleaf Drum](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Springleaf+Drum), [Inkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inkmoth+Nexus), and [Blinkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinkmoth+Nexus) all by his second turn. Liu's was equally impressive with a [Noble Hierarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noble+Hierarch) into second turn [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod).

Chackonal then activiated his [Inkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inkmoth+Nexus) to equip [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating), dealing 8 poison damage. Three turns down in the game. Liu thought for a moment before summoning [Linvala, Keeper of Silence](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Linvala%2C+Keeper+of+Silence) – a creature with flying to ostensibly block.

Vipin planned his attack carefully, summoning [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge) before using [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter) with [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating) to attack for 8 damage. That put Liu at 4 life and 8 poison counters. Four turns of uncontested Affinity, and Liu could only consider his options and pass the turn back. When Chackonal attacked, Liu used [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel) to play a second flying blocker, but there was just enough mana to move [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating) to the unblocked Vault *Scourge*.

Of course, Liu missed that using Pod on Linvala for [Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kiki-Jiki%2C+Mirror+Breaker) to copy [Noble Hierarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noble+Hierarch) then cast [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel) would have let him win on his turn.

Five turns. Affinity doesn't turn down a gift like that.

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gprich14/f_chackonal.jpg)  
*Chackonal was joyous to make it as far as the finals.* 

The second game started similarly, with [Inkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inkmoth+Nexus), [Blinkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinkmoth+Nexus), [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager), [Springleaf Drum](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Springleaf+Drum), and [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge) all in play on Chackonal's second turn. This time, Liu had [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) to curb any modular options, and hitting four mana on turn four meant he also had the threat of [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel) active. Chackonal found himself without a clear attack and only another [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge) to add.

At the end of Chackonal's turn, Liu went for [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel) but the opportunity was open for [Galvanic Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Blast) to handle [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite). A [Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kiki-Jiki%2C+Mirror+Breaker) off the top for Liu would end the game, but [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) was a close second as [Wall of Roots](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Roots) transformed into [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch), which untapped the Pod and transformed Linvala into Kiki-Jiki.

"I had to go for the combo. I couldn't play around another [Galvanic Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Blast) – which could have been the last card in \[Jones'\] hand. [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) was holding it together," Liu said before looking to Chackonal. "So you had a pretty poor hand?"

"Yeah." Chackonal just nodded and shrugged.

"All the marbles for the match are in my sideboard," Liu said.

"So are mine," Chackonal agreed.

"But mine are pretty powerful." Liu was unwittingly prescient with his boast.

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gprich14/f_liu.jpg)  
*Liu laid our the future before anyone knew what happened.* 

The third, final game put Chackonal on the play again but with a mulligan. [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge) off an Island, then another [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge), [Mox Opal](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Opal), and [Signal Pest](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Signal+Pest) was a step slower than his blistering first game. This time Liu played the "fill the battlefield" style of Affinity, chaining [Wall of Roots](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Roots) into another [Wall of Roots](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Roots) and [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise) all from a starting three mana.

Despite falling to 14 on the next attack Liu seemed unphased. [Shatterstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shatterstorm) on his turn was the reason why, and Chackonal was left with just an Island. Unknown to Chackonal, Liu had two [Chord of Calling](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chord+of+Calling) in his hand. At the end of the turn, Liu used on to grand Restoration Angle, then untapped with the other for [Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kiki-Jiki%2C+Mirror+Breaker).

Chackonal smiled and extended his hand, unable to open the ultimate birthday gift. "Congratulations," he said.

Liu smiled back, and the cheer from his friends hanging above was all you needed to hear to know it was over.

"I saw two red sources and [Shatterstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140309000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shatterstorm). I couldn't not keep." Liu beamed.

**Brian Liu defeated Vipin Chackonal, 2-0**
