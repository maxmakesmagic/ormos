The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on March 16, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by Draft tips for *Modern Masters 2017 Edition*. So many Draft tips.

# Today's Must

1.  [*Modern Masters 2017* Instant-Speed Tricks](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/articles/modern-masters-2017-instant-speed-tricks) | MTGGoldfish | Sameer Merchant

With *Modern Masters 2017 Edition* releasing tomorrow, there will be drafts happening. Oh, so many drafts. If you're going to draft, be sure to make the most of it with this handy reference to all the instant-speed tricks in the format, complete with a handy PDF!

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Aetherworks Temur Tower](/en/events/2017/competitive-standard-constructed-league-2017-03-15/competitive-standard-constructed-league)

Temur Tower—an energy-based [Dynavolt Tower](https://web.archive.org/web/20170316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dynavolt+Tower) deck—has gained some popularity over the past few weeks as people uncovered its power. Likewise, people are rediscovering [Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel) as players look for ways to break through the Copy Cat/Mardu Vehicles paradigm.

Why not both?

Diego\_Mediano recently went 5-0 in a *Magic Online* Competitive Standard League with a deck that utilizes about 60% of a Temur Tower deck and combines it with part of an [Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel) deck, effectively dropping counterspells for the [Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel) package.

This wasn't a fluke. How do I know? Because SEN\_ also went 5-0 with [the exact same decklist](/en/events/2017/competitive-standard-constructed-league-2017-03-15/competitive-standard-constructed-league) at about the same time.

And this is what Standard is right now—evolution. People finding new ways to upgrade or shift existing strategies to find an edge. And, sometimes, smashing two sweet decks together.
