*Updated November 23, 2011* 

![Magic: the Gathering Pro Tour](https://i.ormos.uk/72/33/7233580ab42233846709a253f2c477d01df8a3ce0e4a4de5f531a79594c6b5fb.jpg "Magic: the Gathering Pro Tour") Congratulations to Owen Turtenwald, the [2011 Pro Tour Player of the Year](/en/events/2011/worlds11/player-of-the-year-owen-turtenwald)!

The player with the most Pro Points over the course of a season is identified as the Pro Player of the Year. In addition to the title, the Pro Player of the Year receives invitations to certain tournaments throughout the next season.
