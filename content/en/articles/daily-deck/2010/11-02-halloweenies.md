Daily Decks is a fan of theme decks of all varieties, but just because a deck is on theme doesn't mean it can't be played in a tournament setting! Check out this deck Mike Huttman played at the StarCityGames.com Standard Open in Charlotte, North Carolina this past weekend. It follows a Halloween theme, as Mike explained to Daily Decks: "This is a Halloween-themed deck that I built to take advantage of Demon of Death's Gate. The deck is packed with all sorts of things that go bump in the night! The deck presents a 'scary' threat in the form of a turn two or three Demon."
