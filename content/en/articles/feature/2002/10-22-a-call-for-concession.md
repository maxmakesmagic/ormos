### My Invitational Weekend

* * *

Wow! What a weekend! I'd like to start of with congratulations to Jens Thoren for winning the 2002 **Magic: The Gathering** Invitational. Jens and Tomi Walamies put on quite a show during the final three matches, but when Thoren opened up with the "V" deck, it was all over. If you're wondering what I'm talking about, take a look at [*Sideboard* Online coverage](https://web.archive.org/web/20021022000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=sideboard/events/mi02&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm102202) of the event, or log on to ***Magic: The Gathering** Online* and [watch the replays for yourself](https://web.archive.org/web/20021022000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/inv02replay&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm102202). (They're in the **Invitational** Finals room.)

I'd like to talk a bit about my experience this weekend. First, while I live all of five minutes from the Wizards of the Coast office, I decided to spend the weekend at home to see the Invitational from a player's perspective. Yeah, I could've been down there, hobnobbing with the Pros, getting cards signed, schmoozing for deck tech; but I wanted to see how the whole thing went off from the outside.

I now pause for everyone to finish calling me an idiot.

Anyway, in general, I liked what I saw. There were many good moments during the weekend, and it was great to see the Pros spending time chatting with folks in the room. It really did feel like I was there. A nice part of being outside the office were the surprises I got along the way.

For example, I wasn't aware how Mark Rosewater and Paul Barclay were going to pull off the auction until it happened. Originally, it was going to be all offline and [*Sideboard*](https://web.archive.org/web/20021022000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=sideboard&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm102202) was going to post the [results](https://web.archive.org/web/20021022000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=/default.asp?x=sideboard/mi02/auction&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm102202). Imagine my surprise when I logged into ***Magic** Online* Saturday morning at the beginning of the auction. Using the **away** function like that was brilliant. (If you missed it, [*Sideboard* Online](https://web.archive.org/web/20021022000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=sideboard&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm102202) has a transcript of the auction available [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20021022000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=sideboard/mi02/auctionlog&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm102202).)

It was also amusing to watch the chat come to a screeching halt when the first replay came up on Friday. A couple hundred players, chatting away merrily, then WHAM! Gotta watch the replay. I'm happy to see everyone enjoyed that feature.

Then there were the Finals. Oh my. Yes, I was there in the room watching so many people saying, "Why doesn't Jens just concede?" Tomi was at 16 life, Jens was at 1; Tomi had Phyrexian Arena in play, Jens wasn't drawing fast enough; Tomi was hitting with two Spiritmongers each turn, Jens was chump-blocking with Birds of Paradise. Most players thought Thoren was better off getting on to the third game.

Then, all Phyrexia broke loose. Literally.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/d6/ef/d6ef39053483b2c11a5138478b20fe2d9380de700a80930b2a7e8b84382ba01e.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20021022000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/thorensvwalamies&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm102202)  

At 16 life, Walamies played Phyrexian Arena, using a Llanowar Wastes which put him at 15, a fairly safe bet with Thoren at 1 life. He also had two Spiritmongers in play along with a couple Birds of Paradise and a Phantom Centaur. Thoren had his own Spiritmonger in play, two Phantom Centaurs with only one +1/+1 counter on each, and a Bird.  
 On Thoren's next turn, he played a Llanowar Elves and passed play back to Walamies. Walamies (now at 14 from the Arena) attacked all out and Thoren chump blocked with the Elves, Bird, and 'monger. This left Thoren with two 3/1 Centaurs and a 7/7 Spiritmonger. Walamies had two tapped 'mongers and a Centaur, plus two untapped Birds of Paradise.

Then Thoren played Pernicious Deed, activated it for 1, and sent both Walamies's Birds to the graveyard. Suddenly, the writing was on the wall. Thoren swung for 13 and got to watch as Walamies's own Phyrexian Arena took his last point of life the next turn. *(Game 50660185-Replay Game 2)*

Randy Buehler summed it up nicely in his live commentary: "Holy \_\_\_\_!" Fortunately, the Adepts didn't boot Randy for attempting to use inappropriate language.

In the end, Walamies won the next round and it was all up to the [Auction of the People decks](https://web.archive.org/web/20021022000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=sideboard/feature/20021009a&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm102202)--as Randy mentioned, it was only appropriate. And, after all the talk about the ugly mana base, the "V" deck came out swinging.

### Other High Points

**Brian Kibler's Standard With *OnslaughtTM* Deck**  
 You *have* to see this deck go off! Check out his final game against Eivind Nitter, where he empties his library and swings for 156 points of damage. Meow indeed! *(Game 50533401-Replay Game 2)* Thanks Eivind, for not conceding and letting Brian run with it.

**Two-Headed Giant**  
 As a casual player, I loved seeing Pros play multiplayer. It looked like they were having some fun, too. Although, I'm not sure if it's because they enjoyed the format or just because they could talk smack to three other players all at once.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/47/1d/471dba7b08f323ffabdae8d943dfbc45087df71e4286ee6c28ba5515ec6e23ff.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20021022000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/draftingons&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm102202)

***Onslaught* Cards Online**  
 For the first time we all got to see the latest set live on ***Magic** Online.*[Only a couple weeks until we get our hands on the beasties.](https://web.archive.org/web/20021022000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/onsannounce&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm102202)

**Online Extended**  
 We also got a first look at what's going to happen when the Online Extended format becomes available. For the next three years, ***Magic** Online* will have a format that doesn't exist in the physical game. If you don't understand why, check out [this article](https://web.archive.org/web/20021022000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=sideboard/news/20020517a&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm102202) about Extended format block rotation.

### Conclusion And Kudos

To wrap up, I had a great time watching with everyone this weekend. I laughed, I cried, it became a part of me. I hope you all enjoyed it as much as I did.

Now, a round of thanks to everyone who made it possible. (Please forgive me if I forget anyone, it's an awful big list.):

Mark Rosewater: For continuing my favorite Pro event ever. Cheers and hopes for many more Invitationals to come.
