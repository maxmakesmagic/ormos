Rogue deck builder Conley Woods is at it again! At Grand Prix Salt Lake City this past weekend, the renowned decksmith decided it was time to pull together Titans, Praetors, and Planeswalkers alike to come over the top of his opponents.

The engine the deck uses is the core ramp strategy seen elsewhere in decks like Red-Green Ramp by utilizing cards like Rampant Growth, Solemn Simulacrum, and Sphere of the Suns to quickly accelerate its mana. Once the deck has reached six mana, it's time for the show to begin! Six Titans alongside two copies of Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite are enough to wheel show-stopping haymaker after haymaker onto the table.

Make no mistake, though: Conley's deck isn't just a single-minded ramp deck. He *also* developed a plan for the middle of the game when his ramp didn't come together. Spot removal alongside Day of Judgment helps keep the board clear to develop the ramp plan. If that doesn't work, the combination of Lingering Souls and token-generating planeswalkers alongside Gavony Township and Vault of the Archangel gives the deck plenty of ways to distract opponents until it can bury them in Titans.

Standard is full of cheap, powerful creatures, but this deck serves as a reminder to everybody that plenty of large creatures still stomp around the top end of the format. If the newest Conley brew featuring some of Standard's most powerful spells sounds like something you're interested in, take a look at the decklist below!

Conley Woods's Junk Titans
