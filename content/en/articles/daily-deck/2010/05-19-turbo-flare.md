Matt Rader has managed to pull off an accomplishment that has been waiting in the wings in Standard for some time. He uses Runeflare Trap and Howling Mine effects to deal huge amounts of damage to his opponent because of the amount of cards the opponent has in hand! The trick was strong enough to net him a Top 8 at a Lincoln, Nebraska PTQ. In addition to Runeflare Trap, Matt also uses burn like Lightning Bolt to get more damage in. He can even cast Twincast on his Trap to double its effect, or on Time Warp to take two additional turns. Awesome!
