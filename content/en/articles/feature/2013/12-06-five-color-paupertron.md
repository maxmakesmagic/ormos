Today's list combines Pauper, Tron lands, and five colors of spells, which neatly explains why I chose to feature it. As much as I enjoyed playing [Cloudpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudpost), the format is better off without it, and assembling the set of Urza lands is hard enough that it won't be quite as oppressive to the format as a whole. 

![Urza's Tower](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Urza%27s+Tower)

That being said, I like casting ridiculous things, and this list certainly delivers on that front. Despite being a five-color deck, there are only seventeen lands here, and none of them tap for colored mana save the lone [Shimmering Grotto](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shimmering+Grotto). That can be explained by the full sixteen color-filtering artifacts, with four [Chromatic Star](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chromatic+Star)s, [Chromatic Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chromatic+Sphere)s, [Prismatic Lens](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prismatic+Lens)es, and [Prophetic Prism](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prophetic+Prism)s in attendance. By using all artifacts, the deck doesn't waste its time with more non-Tron lands than necessary, and many of its mana sources draw a card, smoothing out the games. 

[Expedition Map](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Expedition+Map) and [Ancient Stirrings](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Stirrings) look for Tron pieces, and do so in a timely fashion, as we've seen in RG Tron in Modern. Once Tron is assembled, the fun starts, as [Mulldrifter](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mulldrifter)s and [Sea Gate Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sea+Gate+Oracle)s let you tear through your deck. [Fangren Marauder](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fangren+Marauder) is an awesome way to stay alive, and works well with the twelve artifacts that sacrifice themselves (plus, sometimes you get to play against Affinity). Tron also flashes back [Firebolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firebolt) easily, letting the deck play a one-drop that has utility even when the mana is flowing. 

The actual winning comes from two copies of [Rolling Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rolling+Thunder) and one [Ulamog's Crusher](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulamog%27s+Crusher), although winning is a formality in many of the games. 

The sideboard is a work of art as well, and I can barely imagine a more eclectic mix of cards. How often do [Doom Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doom+Blade), [Circle of Protection: Green](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Circle+of+Protection%3A+Green), and [Earth Rift](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Earth+Rift) fight side by side, much less with [Pyroblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroblast) and [Rest for the Weary](https://web.archive.org/web/20131206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rest+for+the+Weary)?
