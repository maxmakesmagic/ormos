Standard is full of powerful control elements—and it turns out you don't even need access to blue to use them all! **Magic Online** player AliEnWaRe\_ built up a black control deck that touched a little white for some extra board sweepers, took it into a Daily Event, and finished with a 3–1 record. The primary kill condition? [Sorin's Vengeance](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorin%27s+Vengeance)! Let's take a look.

It turns out that when you have a strong supply of board sweepers and powerful removal spells in the format, each card is so powerful that you don't necessarily need blue card drawing to glue it all together! [Terminus](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terminus), [Black Sun's Zenith](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Sun%27s+Zenith), [Ratchet Bomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ratchet+Bomb), [Curse of Death's Hold](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=227075), [Oblivion Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Ring), and [Go for the Throat](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Go+for+the+Throat) all put a quick end to your opponent's attackers.

Once the coast is clear, it's time to deploy your win conditions. [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls) can get in for some damage—or chump block to buy time if necessary—but the main way this deck seals things up is with a Sorin package. Running two [Sorin Markov](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorin+Markov) and a whopping four [Sorin's Vengeance](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorin%27s+Vengeance), this deck rules the long game. Back-to-back [Sorin's Vengeance](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorin%27s+Vengeance) will slay most opponents, and even if their life totals are higher than 20 the one-two punch of [Sorin Markov](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorin+Markov)'s -3 ability and his [Vengeance](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vengeance) equals game over.

The deck also has a neat artifact package as well. [Solemn Simulacrum](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Solemn+Simulacrum) and [Ichor Wellspring](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichor+Wellspring) alongside [Phyrexia's Core](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexia%27s+Core) and [Buried Ruin](https://web.archive.org/web/20120622000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Buried+Ruin) help give you some extra cards and provide some extra gas if the game goes on.

Black-based control is always a lot of fun to play and can consistently answer creature after creature. You won't want to miss this decklist—give it a try at Friday Night **Magic** tonight!
