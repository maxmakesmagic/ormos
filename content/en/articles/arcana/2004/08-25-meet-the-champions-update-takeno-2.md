Ready for another story about the heroes of Kamigawa? Check out the [*Champions of Kamigawa* site](https://web.archive.org/web/20080422233625/http://www.wizards.com/magic/displayexpansion.asp?set=chk&page=1) for an update in the "Meet the Champions" area.

This week's story is "**[Duty Bound](https://web.archive.org/web/20080415035721/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/chk/takeno)**" by Ed Stark. It's the story of a samurai attack on the nezumi rat-men, and of the harsh rules of honor in the daimyo's lands. Enjoy.
