**Magic Online** Championship winner and StarCityGames.com writer Reid Duke worked hard to come up with an exciting deck for the Legacy portion of the StarCityGames.com Invitational a few weeks ago... and he found it! This blast from the past features several cards that haven't seen play in ages, topped off by Pox! Take a look for yourself!

Reid Duke's Mono-Black Pox 

Legacy – StarCityGames.com Invitational
