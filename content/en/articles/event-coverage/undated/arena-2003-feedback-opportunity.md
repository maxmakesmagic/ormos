### 

The **Magic** Arena League program is being redesigned and upgraded for 2003 in an effort to make it as cool as possible.

![](http://www.wizards.com/tournaments/images/ArenaLogo_288.jpg)The goal of Arena League is to help retailers get people playing in their stores and to get players used to playing competitive **Magic** before they take the next important step into sanctioned tournaments.

We're giving the public a chance to share their ideas of what they would like to see. Please email your suggestions to dci@wizards.com with the subject line "Arena 2003 Suggestions". 

Here are the constraints:

*   Nothing going into an Arena kit can be heavier than 1 pound or be too large to fit into an Arena folder. For example, a deck box would be too large and a metal plaque would probably be too heavy.
*   The contents of an Arena kit shouldn't cost us much more than $10.00 to produce.
*   The target audience for Arena league is not Pro Tour caliber players. Therefore, the prizes should not be something that would attract lots of high-level players to play in Arena.

Please be creative but realistic with your ideas and follow the constraints above. Feel free to think "outside of the box".

Some additional questions that you can answer in your email are:

1\. Should we give Arena organizers the choice of running any format or should there be some kind of limitation?

2\. Should Arena organizers be able to sanction a "season-end" tournament for their league?

3\. Are non-foil alternate-art Arena basic lands cool?

4\. Should Arena organizers be able to run their season any length they want? Could they run an Arena season as a one-day activity and give the prizes out at the end of the day?

5\. What should we do to make sure people don't just order Arena kits and sell the prizes on Ebay or through some other dishonest means?

I will personally read every suggestion that is sent. Please remember to send your suggestions to dci@wizards.com. Suggestions must be sent before August 23, 2002. 

Take Care,

Jeff Donais  
 DCI Tournament Manager
