This is the alternate-art foil prerelease version of Moonsilver Spear. You can get one just for playing in an [*Avacyn Restored* Prerelease](/en/events/2008/prerelease-facts/worldwide-prerelease-tournaments)! The Angel tokens are also created by Entreat the Angels, as seen in Tuesday's [Serious Fun](/en/articles/seriousfun/2012/04-17-hearts-aflutter), where Adam Styborski used it to good effect in a couple of fun decks.
