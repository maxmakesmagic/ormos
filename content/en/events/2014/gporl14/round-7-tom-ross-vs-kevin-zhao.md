While the *Magic Online* Community Cup put him onto our [Twitch](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Twitch).tv screens everywhere recently, it hasn't been since his runner-up finish at Grand Prix Houston last year Tom Ross has been in the premier *Magic* spotlight. With a Pro Tour Top 8 from Honolulu in 2009 and another Grand Prix Top 8 shortly before that, is might seem as though Ross hasn't been playing competitive *Magic* recently.

Looking along the scoreboards of StarCityGames' Open Series tells a different tale.

Ross has been playing plenty of *Magic*, and doing quite well holding his own among the top performers on the Open Series. Thanks to near-weekly tournaments all across the US, Ross has only cemented his skills practicing Constructed formats. Grand Prix Houston, if you check back, was a Limited event.

Skill is like a blade, and Ross was keeping his sharp in all directions.

Of course, repeating a deep run this weekend was a long way off. Kevin Zhao, one of the many trying their hands at *Khans of Tarkir* Sealed this weekend was standing in the way. Coming into the round each player had a draw, which is often as bad as a lass: Only players with 21 or more points can return for Day 2, and earning just one through a draw doesn't help one get there.

For both players losing this match would be just like losing a second game, setting them into the path where no more losses were possible.

**The Decks** 

Both players brought four-color decks to bear. Ross was firmly Abzan, with powerful monsters such as Ivorytust Fortress, [High Sentinels of Arashin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=High+Sentinels+of+Arashin), and [Rakshasa Deathdealer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Deathdealer) to work with, backed up by a splash of blue for [Sagu Mauler](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sagu+Mauler). Two copies each of [Savage Punch](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Punch) and [Debilitating Injury](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Debilitating+Injury) rounded out a redundant removal package for Ross.

Zhao, too, packed a copy of [High Sentinels of Arashin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=High+Sentinels+of+Arashin), but white was his splash color. With the dash of white to support [Flying Crane Technique](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flying+Crane+Technique) as well, Zhao brought the ferocity of the Temur with him: Bear's Companion, [Winterflame](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Winterflame), and some Jeskai converts in [Riverwheel Aerialists](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Riverwheel+Aerialists) and two copies of [Mystic of the Hidden Way](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystic+of+the+Hidden+Way). With the ability to punch damage through and overrun through the air, Zhao's removal was fewer in number but mattered much more for him.

**The Games** 

Zhao led with an early [Temur Charger](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temur+Charger), clocking Ross repeatedly as the Community Cup champion assembled a morph to pair with [Ghostfire Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghostfire+Blade). [Mystic of the Hidden Way](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystic+of+the+Hidden+Way) was Zhao's mystery revealed, putting Ross to just 10 life.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gporl14/gporlando_ross.jpg)

*Tom Ross battled from behind to take the first game.* 

  

Sidisi'd Pet was Ross's morph, netting him three life thanks to the Equipment buff, but Zhao kept ahead with a [Sultai Flayer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sultai+Flayer) to up his race.

[Sagu Mauler](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sagu+Mauler) was Ross's other morph, eating Zhao's [Temur Charger](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temur+Charger) on the next attack and stabilizing his blocking situation. While Zhao had a [Leaping Master](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leaping+Master) at the ready next to his [Mystic of the Hidden Way](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystic+of+the+Hidden+Way), he didn't have the white mana needed to make it jump. [Murderous Cut](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Murderous+Cut) took away Zhao's attacks altogether, forcing him to face an incoming [Sagu Mauler](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sagu+Mauler) next turn.

[High Sentinels of Arashin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=High+Sentinels+of+Arashin), followed by a [Savage Punch](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Punch) from his beefy Sidisi's Pet, was enough for Ross to finish the game.

In the second game, Zhao led the way again but off of a mulligan to five cards. While [Trail of Mystery](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trail+of+Mystery) promised to supply Zhao with the lands he wanted, Ross had the time to set up an [Abzan Falconer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Falconer) using its outlast ability, and a morph. While Ross's morph paired off in a trade with Zhao's, Ross followed up with [Ivorytusk Fortress](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ivorytusk+Fortress).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gporl14/gporlando_zhao.jpg)

*Starting with just five cards put Kevin Zhao at a distinct disadvantage in the second game.* 

  

[High Sentinels of Arashin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=High+Sentinels+of+Arashin) took a [Savage Punch](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Punch) from the Fortress, opening the door for Ross to take Zhao down to just 6 life. [Sultai Flayer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sultai+Flayer) looked to buy another turn, but [Throttle](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Throttle) took care of the last blocker against Ross.

It was over before either player could blink.

"Are you five colors?" Ross asked.

"No I'm just four." Zhao said. "Not a Swamp in sight."

There was a moment of quiet as Ross flicked two cards out of his deck – a foil and non-foil [Ghostfire Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghostfire+Blade) each – and waited for Zhao to look up.

"Oh wow. Two of them! That's tough to beat."

Two games. Two Blades. Two games cut through. Ross didn't miss the mark.
