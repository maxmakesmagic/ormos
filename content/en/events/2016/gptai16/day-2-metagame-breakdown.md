After 9 rounds, the field of 793 was boiled down to 266. That's still a healthy representation of the mature Standard metagame and we're here to share the Top 12 Archetypes with you.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gptai16/Day-2-Metagame-Breakdown.jpg)

Most of the numbers here correspond to the [numbers from yesterday](/en/events/2016/gptai16/day-1-metagame-breakdown), but what we'd like to delve into a little more is the conversion rate.

For example, 171 players showed up with Green-White Tokens yesterday, putting 71 players through to Day 2. This represents a 40% conversion rate, which is an indication of the deck's performance yesterday.

Looking at the data above, Humans has got a poor conversion rate of only 30%. Despite it being potentially very explosive, it doesn't quite stack up in the mid-to-late game once things spiral out of control and the board gets gummed up. A few of the lists lurking around the top tables are playing as many as 20 one-mana creatures, increasing the effectiveness of [Thalia's Lieutenant](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thalia%27s+Lieutenant) significantly, but this "all-in" approach is literally make or break.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Collected+Company)](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company)

Bant Coco and Bant Humans lend strength from [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company), a superbly powerful card that allows creature-loving players to cheat on mana costs, dig for a specific utility creature, populate the board or rebuild it after suffering heavy losses from [Languish](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Languish) or [Tragic Arrogance](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tragic+Arrogance). In particular, Bant Company spots a 60% conversion rate, which indicates that it is a force to be reckoned with.

Nearly a quarter of all players in Day 2 are sporting this format-defining instant, and the trend will likely stay this way until *Dragons of Tarkir* rotates.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Dragonlord+Silumgar)](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar)

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Seasons+Past)](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seasons+Past)

Of note are also the Sultai Control decks, which have been serving their pilots very well. Some of the [Seasons Past](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seasons+Past) Control players are also splashing Blue to include [Dragonlord Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar) as a tool to beat the Planeswalkers found in Green-White Tokens, which means that almost 20 players have adopted this strategy.

Black typically gives the control access to quality spot removal ([Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price), [Grasp of Darkness](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grasp+of+Darkness) and [Dead Weight](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dead+Weight)) and mass removal ([Languish](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Languish)), but one crucial part of the puzzle is the ability to run [Dark Petition](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Petition). This enables the control deck to play silver bullets and a toolbox sideboard, opening up the doors to usually narrow options such as [Virulent Plague](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Virulent+Plague) for instance. You'll also be able to find [Seasons Past](https://web.archive.org/web/20160626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seasons+Past) to regrow your entire graveyard, or grab the best card you need if you find yourself in a fix.
