As you may have read, **Magic** art is sometimes illustrated longer or taller than the dimensions of a card's art box, so it must be cropped for the purposes of fitting on the card. Take, for example, *Darksteel's* Pristine Angel by Scott M. Fischer:

![Pristine Angel sketch](https://i.ormos.uk/29/2b/292ba13aaf9dd12b07422d973a7b437f7cddc4e7979af9af15b1b1b04d6655a3.jpg)

Many of you enjoyed our look at the full-sized art of some of **Magic**'s [Tall Angels](/en/articles/arcana/2004/02-26-tall-angels-2). So here are two more pieces from *Darksteel* that had to be cropped, but are shown here in their full glory:

![Sundering Titan](https://i.ormos.uk/c4/53/c45348430369df96421c4ef8032bcbf25498c492b5c73d3a36b60eb4b021755e.jpg)  
**Sundering Titan** by Jim Murray 

![Flamebreak](https://i.ormos.uk/8b/de/8bde7ecf2cd266b7b6afc5dcb5d83e4bd940eca8732a0543919112fbaeb85c18.jpg)  
**Flamebreak** by Jim Murray

(Flamebreak's art is even wider than we can fit here! Click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20040414000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=/magic/images/mtgcom/arcana300/bigart_flamebreak.jpg&origin=mtgcom_arcana_545) to see an even wider version.)
