## *Magic Online* Premier Play Returns!

If you missed it, the *Magic* Esports team has announced the [*Zendikar Rising* Split](https://magic.gg/news/announcing-the-2020-2021-magic-esports-season) and has outlined Premier Play for the next few months. For *Magic Online*, the qualifiers start this weekend with a *Core Set 2021* Sealed event Saturday, July 18, at 7 a.m. PT and a Modern Super Qualifier on Sunday, July 19, at 7 a.m. PT.

The rest of the MTGO Premier Play schedule can be found on the [MTGO Premier Play page](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020). Have fun!

## *Vintage Cube* July 1–29

That's right! *Vintage Cube* is back, and it's nothing like any other cube out there. It's simply overflowing with iconic cards from *Magic*'s almost 27-year history, and you can draft the super-deck of your dreams when it arrives tomorrow, July 1, and runs for nearly the whole month, ending July 29.

You can view the change list below or, for a full card list, click [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/2016/11-30-vintage-cube-cardlist).

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format Challenges are the first step toward qualifying for Premier Play through your favorite *Magic* formats. To learn more about Premier Play qualification, click [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020).

*Magic Online*'s Format Challenges now run once on Saturdays and once on Sundays for each Constructed format! On weekends featuring Showcase Challenge events, the Format Challenge that starts at either 6 a.m., 8 a.m., or 10 a.m. Pacific time is pre-empted by the Showcase Challenge—Format Challenges scheduled at other times remain in place. *NOTE: The Pauper Challenge is never pre-empted.*
