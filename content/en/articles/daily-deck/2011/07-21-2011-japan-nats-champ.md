Ryuuichiro Ishida is the 2011 Japan National Champion, and the deck he used to get there was Mono-White Tempered Steel. His powerful and aggressive artifact creatures teamed up with the the white enchantment to put a serious hurt on his opponent in short order. Ryuuichiro will be joined on the National team by Tomoyo Fujimoto and former World Champion Makihito Mihara. Read full coverage of the 2011 Japan National Championships [here](/en/events/2011/jpnat11/ishida-the-man-of-steel-at-japan-nationals-2011).
