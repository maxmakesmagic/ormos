As you can see from the cover of the novel, the art is almost the same; Scott Fischer’s art was cropped and the background figure was de-emphasized to provide the card art.

The figure is Radha (RAHD-ah), a half-Keldon, half-elf butt-kicker born in Skyshroud. Her stony gray skin tone from her Keldon parentage, mixed with her natural coppery highlights, gives her camouflage in the trees, which helps her hunt slivers and other dangers in Skyshroud Forest. Her appearance on a card called Undying Rage is no stretch—she’s famous for her snarling temper, as the card’s flavor text attests.

As you can see below, it is Teferi, on a quest to halt the collapse of Dominaria’s fractured timeline, who lurks behind Radha in the illustration.
