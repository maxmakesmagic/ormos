Hello folks!

  
  

I've been involved with the roll out and work on achievements for [PlaneswalkerPoints.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20130522151937/http://www.wizards.com/magic/planeswalkerpoints/guild), and with *Gatecrash* we're adding in a few new achievements that you can earn. 

For those of you who aren't aware, achievements on [PlaneswalkerPoints.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20130522151937/http://www.wizards.com/magic/planeswalkerpoints/guild) are similar to those you might have seen on social media websites or for other games. By doing specific things, playing in certain events, or beating certain opponents, you unlock a new achievement for [PlaneswalkerPoints.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20130522151937/http://www.wizards.com/magic/planeswalkerpoints/guild). These are badges you can display on your page or in the widget that you put on forums or blogs.

Remember, for most of these achievements, you need to have chosen your guild on [PlaneswalkerPoints.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20130522151937/http://www.wizards.com/magic/planeswalkerpoints/guild). If you haven't, you are going to be missing out!

So, with *Gatecrash*,we're adding the following achievements: 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4d/b8/4db8a14c69fd1e8e269a0df20ae72beb86c88d086e25727b3f5ff0723cb7449e.jpg) 

Tablet of the Guilds | Art by [Nic Klein](https://web.archive.org/web/20130117000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22nic%20klein%22])

### "Team Ups"

There is one of these achievements for each of Ravnica's ten guilds, so you'll see "Azorius Team Up," and this achievement is earned by entering a sanctioned Two-Headed Giant event with a member of your same guild.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/39/e1/39e1c73e405ede617e85987d40743e3d73eca39eacf14230bb91b7939ab9f23a.jpg) 

Art by [Michael Komarck](https://web.archive.org/web/20130117000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22michael%20komarck%22])

### Proponent /Vanquisher/Conqueror

This is a multi-tier achievement, again unique for your guild. You earn this one by beating three, four, or five different guilds in the same event. So if you play a member of Selesnya in Round 1 of your local [FNM](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6), Simic in Round 2, and Rakdos in Round 3, you've got it! 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a9/c9/a9c92301216d8257b76fbfccff459774d6df6530976e716e22fc12c2744c3ac9.jpg) 

Boros Keyrune | Art by [Daniel Ljunggren](https://web.archive.org/web/20130117000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22daniel%20ljunggren%22])

### Tourcrasher & Trialcrasher

Both of these achievements reward players who are earnest in their desires. Earn Tourcrasher achievements by playing in three [Pro Tour Qualifiers](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/qualifiers-for-pro-tour-dragons-maze) for Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze*. The Trialcrasher achievement is earned by playing in five Grand Prix Trials during the Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze* qualifier season. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/04/b9/04b9138ffd2784a5cf0a167d7194d1d7a76817e937e87e360a8806b3a1d481b4.jpg) 

Art by [Raoul Vitale](https://web.archive.org/web/20130117000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22raoul%20vitale%22])

### "At the Gates"

This achievement is earned by sharing the guild of the winner of your Friday Night **Magic**. The window for this one is February through May, 2013. 

And lastly...

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c0/94/c094eb74b7c5ef038c96c44931d17ba3acf66562681bc1e1912151172e4a385c.jpg) 

Sorin, Lord of Innistrad | Art by [Michael Komarck](https://web.archive.org/web/20130117000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22michael%20komarck%22])

### Duel of *Innistrad*

Although *Innistrad* is behind us, with the approaching release of *Duel Decks: Sorin vs. Tibalt*, we briefly return there. You can earn this achievement simply by winning three matches during the weekend that follows the release of these Duel Decks. 

You can expect us to continue to introduce new achievements regularly. These are just the few for this time around.

Also, we've updated the Planeswalker Points' Leaderboard tab interface. Look under the Yearly section on the Leaderboard to see how many points you have already received. Players with enough points are able to compete at the World **Magic** Cup Qualifiers to represent your country in Amsterdam this year! You can go [here](/en/events/2012/thresholds2013/2013-world-magic-cup-qualifier-thresholds) and see how many points you need to be qualified to play in the World **Magic** Cup Qualifiers! 

See you on [PlaneswalkerPoints.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20130522151937/http://www.wizards.com/magic/planeswalkerpoints/guild)!
