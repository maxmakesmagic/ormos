*Scars of Mirrodin* has hit shelves, and the **Magic** world is busily brewing with the latest set. One slightly unlikely card that's attracted a lot of attention is the ![0 Mana](http://www.wizards.com/images/Symbols/Symbol_0_mana.gif)-cost Memnite. In this deck, piloted by Axel Jensen to a Top 16 finish at last weekend's TCGPlayer Wizard World Series in New York, Memnite and fellow ![0 Mana](http://www.wizards.com/images/Symbols/Symbol_0_mana.gif)-cost creature Ornithopter team up to power out Glint Hawks, carry Adventuring Gear, and trigger Quest for the Holy Relic to search up deadly Equipment like Argentum Armor and Sword of Body and Mind.
