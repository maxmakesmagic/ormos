* * *

The winner of the Fantasy Pro Tour for Osaka was Per Karrman, whose team earned $66,565. Led by Pro Tour Champion Ken Ho's $30,000 and third-place finisher Jens Thoren's $15,000, Karrman's team also benefited from Sylvain Lauriol, who got $9,500 for finishing fifth, as well as Geoffrey Siron (9th place, $6,000), Tsuyoshi Ikeda (13th place, $4,100), Takayuki Nagaoka (30th place, $1,350), and Andrew Mitchell (53rd, $615). The only person on Karrman's team who did not finish in the money was Akitsugo Ito, who finished 93rd.

In second place, Tom Martell also had Ho, Lauriol, Siron, Ikeda, and Nagaoka. The rest of his team was made up of Eric Froehlich (15th place, $3,500), Peter Guevin (27th place, $1,600), and David Price (28th place, $1,500). Everybody on Martell's team finished in the Top 32, and the total score was $57,550.

Shannon Rabong's third-place team earned $55,550, the highest finish for a team that didn't include Ken Ho. Aside from Lauriol, Siron, Ikeda, Guevin, and Nagaoka, Rabong had finalist Olivier Ruel ($20,000) and fourth-place finisher Rob Dougherty ($13,000). Palle Poulsen rounded the team out, finishing 75th.

The fourth-place team earned $55,500, only $50 less than the third-place finisher. Picked by Paula Coghlan, the team started with Ruel and Thoren, added Lauriol, Guevin, and Siron, and added Jonathan Pechon (32nd place, $1,250), Anthony Menzer (33rd place, $1,200), and Florent Lefranc (88th place, $950).

The most popular player was Tsuyoshi Fujita, with 273 picks. All of the ten most-picked players finished in the money.
