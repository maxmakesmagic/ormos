Did you know that for the past several years, Wizards of the Coast has provided 30-card sample decks to stores and organizations as an easy way for players to learn **Magic**? 

We provide five decks, one for each color, updating them each year with cards from the latest core set. Stores have begun to get these decks featuring cards from ***Magic** 2015* , but with a bit of a twist this year. Each sample deck has several cards numbered 270 and higher that do not appear in ***Magic** 2015* booster packs. These cards are legal for tournament play in any format that includes the ***Magic** 2015* core set. These cards are available in the sample decks as well as ***Magic** 2015* Deck Builder's Toolkits.

Those cards are pictured beneath each sample deck.

Previews for ***Magic** 2015* start June 23!
