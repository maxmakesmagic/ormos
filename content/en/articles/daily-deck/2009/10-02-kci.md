Artifacts were the word of the day for Standard at the World Championships in 2004; Affinity was all the rage. Manuel Bevand opted for a different plan of attack, eschewing the red zone entirely for a combo-based artifact deck. His build centered around generating fast artifact mana, funneling it all into Krark-Clan Ironworks, then generating even more mana with Myr Incubator and ending the game with a fiery Fireball or Goblin Charbelcher activation. For more on his performance, read the official coverage from [the event archive](/en/events/2004/worlds04/nuijten-nassif-dominate-worlds).
