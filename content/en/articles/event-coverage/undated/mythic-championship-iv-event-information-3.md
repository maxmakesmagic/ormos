## Overview

**Date:** July 26-28, 2019  
**Format:** Modern and *Modern Horizons* Booster Draft  
**Official Hashtag:** [#MythicChampionshipIV](https://twitter.com/hashtag/MythicChampionshipIV?src=hash)

Mythic Championship IV will take place at:

[Fira de Barcelona - Gran Via](https://www.firabarcelona.com/en/gran-via)  
 Av. Joan Carles I, 64  
 08908 L'Hospitalet de Llobregat  
 Barcelona, Spain

*(Note: this is a different venue from previous Pro Tour, World *Magic* Cup, and Grand Prix events)*

The formats for this Mythic Championship are Modern Constructed and *Modern Horizons* Booster Draft.

Three packs of *Modern Horizons* will be used for the Booster Draft portions of the event.

Decklists are required.

The closest airport to this Mythic Championship is El Prat Barcelona International Airport.

## Player Information

#### Hall Hours

*   Thursday (Registration): Hall opens at 4 p.m. and closes at 8 p.m.
*   Friday: Hall opens at 8 a.m. and closes at the end of Round 8 of the Mythic Championship
*   Saturday: Hall opens at 8 a.m. and closes at the end of the live Top 8 announcement
*   Sunday: Hall opens at 8 a.m. and closes at the end of the Mythic Championship awards ceremony

#### Registration

Decklists must be submitted using the Online Decklist Submission tool that is provided to players via email no later than Wednesday, July 24 at 11:59 p.m. local time (ET).

Registration takes place on Thursday, July 25 from 4 p.m. - 8 p.m. at the venue.

If you are unable to attend the official player registration that day, please email esports@wizards.com as soon as possible so that we know that you will be attending and can plan accordingly.

Players must have a signed waiver and consent form to compete at Mythic Championship IV. Forms are available onsite or can be obtained by contacting esports@wizards.com. Minors must have a parent or guardian's signature on these forms. Minors will not be able to register or play unless we receive a waiver signed by a parent or guardian. No exceptions.

To register, all participants must present one of the following forms of identification:

*   a driver's license, or
*   a passport, or
*   a copy of a birth certificate and photo IDs (school IDs are acceptable).

#### On-Site Vendor

Mythic Championship IV is taking place at MagicFest+ Barcelona. For information on vendors hosting at this event, please refer to the MagicFest+ Barcelona event information page.

## Tournament Information

*The* [Magic: The Gathering Tournament Rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20201205232315/http://wpn.wizards.com/en/document/magic-gathering-tournament-rules) *and* [Infraction & Penalty Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20201205232315/http://wpn.wizards.com/en/document/magic-infraction-procedure-guide) *are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information on this page.*

*   Day One will consist of 3 rounds of Booster Draft followed by 5 rounds of Modern
*   Day Two will consist of 3 rounds of Booster Draft followed by 5 rounds of Modern
*   16 Swiss Rounds total, 50 minutes per round
*   When time is called, the active player will finish the turn and five additional turns will be taken
*   Stalling and slow play rules will be strictly enforced during the tournament
*   All players may participate in all 8 Swiss rounds of Friday's competition
*   Players with 12 or more match points after Round 8 will advance to Saturday's competition
*   The Top 8 players after Round 16 will advance to Sunday's single-elimination playoff rounds

#### Playoffs

The Top 8 will use a traditional single-elimination bracket.

*   Playoff rounds will be Modern Constructed format
*   All playoff matches will be best 3 out of 5
*   The first two games played in a 3 out of 5 playoff-match on Sunday are pre-sideboard games. After Game 2 is completed, players may exchange cards from their deck for cards in their sideboard
*   Standings after the Swiss rounds will be used to determine final order in the standings for losing players in the quarterfinal and semifinal playoff rounds
*   While there are no time limits in the playoff rounds, players are expected to play at a reasonable pace and finish their matches within 60 minutes (for quarterfinal and semifinal matches) and 90 minutes (for the finals match)
*   For the first game of each match in the playoff rounds, the player that finished higher in the Swiss rounds chooses either to play first or to play second. For subsequent games in each playoff match, the usual Play/Draw rule applies (loser of the previous game decides whether to play first in the next game.)

## How Can I Play In A Mythic Championship?

Earn and invitation by:

*   Being a top finisher at a Mythic Championship Qualifier
*   Winning a Magic Online Championship Series event
*   Being a top finisher at a Grand Prix
*   Having the appropriate Pro Players Club status
*   Finishing with 33 match points or greater at the previously held Mythic Championship
