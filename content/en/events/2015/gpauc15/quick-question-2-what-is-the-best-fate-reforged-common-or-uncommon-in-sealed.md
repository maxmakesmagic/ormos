# What is the best *Fate Reforged* common or uncommon in sealed deck?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ_Wilfy_Horig.jpg)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ_Anthony_Hallett.jpg)  
**Anthony “Rangor” Hallett:** [Sultai Emissary](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sultai+Emissary).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ_Don_Van_Ravenzwaaij.jpg)  
**Don van Ravenswaaij:** [Mistfire Adept](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistfire+Adept).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ_Hao-Shan_Huang.jpg)  
**Huang Hao-shan:** Common: [Goblin Heelcutter](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Heelcutter), uncommon: [Elite Scaleguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elite+Scaleguard).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ_Nam_Sung_Wook.jpg)  
**Nam Sung Wook:** Common: [Rakshasa's Secret](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa%27s+Secret), uncommon: [Arc Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Lightning).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ_Luke_Mulcahy.jpg)  
**Luke Mulcahy:** [Temur Sabertooth](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temur+Sabertooth).
