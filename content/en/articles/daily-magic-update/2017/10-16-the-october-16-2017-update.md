The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on October 16, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by Gerry and Oliver T—the *T* stands for "Totally going to represent the US at the World *Magic* Cup."

Okay, that was a stretch.

# What People Are Talking About

1.  > Team Brazil is me, Carlos Romao and Lucas Berthoud! Not bad :)
    >
    > — Paulo Vitor (@PVDDR) [October 15, 2017](https://twitter.com/PVDDR/status/919660454246518784?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw)

Not bad at all! Brazil is looking like [one of the teams to beat](https://twitter.com/Top8Games/status/919666404147761152) come the World *Magic* Cup. It would be fitting, too—is any country having as good a year on the Pro Tour as Brazil?

And the USA is unreasonably excited to have Gerry T representing it alongside National Champion and professional 17-year-old Oliver Tomajko. The good old US of A looks to have a strong squad this year as well.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Modern Green-Blue Merfolk](http://sales.starcitygames.com/deckdatabase/displaydeck.php?DeckID=116674)

R&D doesn't believe Green-Blue Merfolk's time in Standard is here yet with just one set under their—belt? I don't know what Merfolk wear to secure their pants.

But in Modern? Apparently the time is now. Or at least this past weekend.

Alan Cummings finished in exactly 8th place with a Green-Blue Merfolk list that played eight green *Ixalan* Merfolk, plus a copy of [Kopala, Warden of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20171016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kopala%2C+Warden+of+Waves). [Kumena's Speaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20171016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kumena%27s+Speaker) is basically always a 2/2 for G in this deck (and often attacks as a 3/3 on turn two thanks to various lords), and [Merfolk Branchwalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20171016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merfolk+Branchwalker) is kind of, almost, sort of like a [Silvergill Adept](https://web.archive.org/web/20171016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silvergill+Adept)—one of the deck's best cards.

The deck is real, but questions will still remain about it. For one, is it better than just Mono-Blue Merfolk? [Natural State](https://web.archive.org/web/20171016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Natural+State) is a nice upgrade in the sideboard, certainly. Second, does the deck want [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20171016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company) as well, or is four mana just too much?

Third, how sad is [Sygg, River Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20171016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sygg%2C+River+Guide) right now?
