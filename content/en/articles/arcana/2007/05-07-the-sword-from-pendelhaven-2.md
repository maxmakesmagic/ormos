[Sword of the Meek](https://web.archive.org/web/20070507000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+the+Meek) might look familiar.

As you may have read in [Magic Arcana #1294](https://web.archive.org/web/20070507000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/arcana/1294), the same sword is depicted in the art of both [Krosan Grip](https://web.archive.org/web/20070507000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Krosan+Grip) and [Return to Dust](https://web.archive.org/web/20070507000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Return+to+Dust) from *Time Spiral*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/bc/60/bc6001c1b60ed01908afe58a943c283b862ec6d07aaa915f91e8c9cf9d24c533.jpg)

*Future Sight's* [Sword of the Meek](https://web.archive.org/web/20070507000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+the+Meek) solves the puzzle of how the same sword can be "disenchanted" multiple times.

Check out the art—you'll see that Franz Vohwinkel illustrated that same sword yet again for a piece of equipment that has the power to come back from the graveyard.

[![Sword of the Meek](https://i.ormos.uk/06/81/0681fc2dcf30aa07362bbcd30b85f9fe1f4788b84691f830813f93b3f600b07e.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20070507000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Sword%2Bof%2Bthe%2BMeek) *[Sword of the Meek](https://web.archive.org/web/20070507000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+the+Meek) art by Franz Vohwinkel*

The weapon grants a [Pendelhaven](https://web.archive.org/web/20070507000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pendelhaven)-like +1/+2 enhancement and has a [Pendelhaven](https://web.archive.org/web/20070507000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pendelhaven)-like affinity for 1/1 creatures. Who first bore this sword? Why, it's [Pendelhaven](https://web.archive.org/web/20070507000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pendelhaven) protector Jacques Le Vert.

![Jacques Le Vert](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Jacques+Le+Vert)

*Abandoning his sword to return to the lush forest of Pendelhaven, Jacques le Vert devoted his life to protecting the creatures of his homeland.*
