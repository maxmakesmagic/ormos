## Overview

Bring your top strategies with you to a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier, and you may be one of the few to emerge from the event with an invitation and airfare to Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation*!

Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers (RPTQs) for Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation* take place June 3-4, 2017 and June 10-11, 2017.

The Top 4 finishers of a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier receive airfare and invitations to Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation*, while 1st- through 8th-place finishers receive invitations to the next round of Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers.

*Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers will take place on June 10, 2017 and June 17, 2017. If you are interested in competing in a *Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifier, you will need to let us know before Wednesday, the weekend of the Regional Pro Tour Qualifier that you'd like to compete. Details can be found further down on this page.

**The format for this Regional Pro Tour Qualifier season is Sealed Deck with Booster Draft Top 8.**

**As a reminder, players are only allowed to compete in *one* Regional Pro Tour Qualifier if they are qualified to play in one.**

[INVITED PLAYERS](https://web.archive.org/web/20211202074813/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/instoreplay/rptqhou/invitations)

## Qualification

**Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers are an invitation-only event.** An invitation to participate in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier for Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation* is offered to the following:

*   Players with a [Pro Tour Players Club](https://web.archive.org/web/20220930134731/http://magic.wizards.com/en/protour/playersclub/standings) Silver level (as of the date of the Regional Pro Tour Qualifier).
*   Players that won a Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifier from January 21, 2017 to April 16, 2017.
*   Players that finished 1st- through 8th-place at a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier for Pro Tour *Amonkhet*.
*   Players that won a Regional Last Chance Qualifier that ties to that Regional Pro Tour Qualifier.

## Eligibility

The following players may ***not*** compete in Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers for Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation*:

*   Players that previously participated in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier which awards an invitation to Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation*.
*   Players that won a *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifier Final which awards an invitation to Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation*.
*   Players that finished in the Top 2 of a *Magic Online* Championship Series (MOCS) Playoff tournament which awards invitations to Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation*.
*   Players that finished in the Top 2 of a *Magic Online* Championship Series (MOCS) Open tournament which awards invitations to Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation*.
*   Players that finished in the Top 8 or finished with 39 or more match points in an individual-format Grand Prix which awards invitations to Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation*.
*   Players that finished in the Top 4 or finished with 36 or more match points in a team-format Grand Prix which awards invitations to Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation*.
*   Players that finished with 33 or more match points at Pro Tour *Amonkhet*.
*   Players that won a designated "Series-Style" event that awards invitations to Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation*.
*   Players with a Pro Tour Players Club Gold or Platinum level (as of the date of the Regional Pro Tour Qualifier).
*   Players that are members of the Pro Tour Hall of Fame.
*   Players that are otherwise already qualified for Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation*.

If you have any questions as to your eligibility to play in a particular Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifier, please email your inquiry to PremierPlay@wizards.com.

Complete information about invitations to Pro Tours can be found in the [*Magic: The Gathering* Invitation Policy](https://web.archive.org/web/20220930134731/http://wpn.wizards.com/en/document/magic-premier-event-invitation-policy).

## General Information

The structure for Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers is modified Swiss-style.

The top finishing players after the Swiss rounds play in one single-elimination playoff match to determine a Top 4.

*   *Amonkhet* Sealed Deck/Booster Draft
*   6 *Amonkhet* booster packs per player will be used for the Sealed Deck portion of the tournament
*   The Top 8 Booster Draft will use three *Amonkhet* booster packs
*   Decklists are required

*   Swiss rounds will be 50 minutes in length.
*   Single-elimination playoff matches will have no time limit.
*   Number of Swiss rounds based on attendance according to the following chart:

| Attendance | Swiss rounds | Playoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | 3 Rounds (Single-Elimination only, No Swiss) | None |
| 9—16 | 4 Rounds | Top 8 |
| 17—32 | 5 Rounds | Top 8 |
| 33—64 | 6 Rounds | Top 8 |
| 65—128 | 7 Rounds | Top 8 |
| 129—226 | 8 Rounds | Top 8 |
| 227—409 | 9 Rounds | Top 8 |
| 410 or higher | 10 Rounds | Top 8 |

*   Playoff matches will be best two of three.
*   Playoff matches will be single-elimination.
*   While there are no time limits in the playoff rounds, players are expected to play at a reasonable pace and finish their matches within 60 minutes.
*   Standings after the Swiss rounds will be used to determine final order in the standings for losing players in the quarterfinal and semifinal playoff rounds.
*   For the first game of each match in the playoff, the player who finished higher in the Swiss rounds chooses either to play first or to play second. For subsequent games in each playoff match, the usual Play/Draw rule applies (loser of the previous game decides whether to play first in the next game).
*   The [*Magic: The Gathering* Tournament Rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20220930134731/http://wpn.wizards.com/en/document/magic-gathering-tournament-rules) and the [*Magic: The Gathering* Infraction Procedure Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220930134731/http://wpn.wizards.com/en/document/magic-infraction-procedure-guide) are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Fact Sheet.
*   Players are responsible for bringing their own method of tracking life totals and appropriate counters or tokens.

There is no entry fee to compete in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier.

The Top 16 players in each Regional PTQ will receive a Regional PTQ playmat.

Other prizes are determined by the organizer of each event. Contact the organizer for additional details.

\*Travel

The players eligible to receive a travel award will receive instructions from Wizards of the Coast regarding whom they must contact and the deadline by which they must arrange their travel awards. Wizards of the Coast will not refund travel made outside this service. Wizards of the Coast reserves the right to award a cash equivalent award in lieu of any travel award in its sole discretion.

All travel itineraries must be from the international airport nearest the player's home and to the host city of the Pro Tour, and must include all the dates of that event. No award will be substituted if member is unable to participate. All other expenses are the responsibility of the member and may include, without limitation, accommodations, ground transportation, taxes, itinerary change fees, passport and visa fees.

This award must be redeemed through member's arrangement of travel itinerary no later than (30) days before departure. Failure to complete travel arrangements as directed by Wizards of the Coast prior to this deadline will result in forfeiture of the award. Every member receiving a travel award must provide to Wizards of the Coast an executed liability release, the appropriate US taxation form (W9 or W8) (or have one on file), [up-to-date contact information on your DCI membership](https://web.archive.org/web/20220930134731/http://accounts.wizards.com/), and proof of ID and passport (when required by international destination). In all cases, all such documentation must be provided to Wizards of the Coast, or must have been previously on file, at least thirty (30) days before departure. Failure to timely provide such documentation will result in forfeiture of the award.

Players under the age of eighteen (18) at the time of the Pro Tour will receive a cash equivalent award in lieu of expenses, paid air ticket, and/or hotel accommodations. Such player must still attend, compete, and be listed in the finish of the tournament or forfeit this award.

Players who have general questions about the *Magic: The Gathering* trading card game, Wizards of the Coast LLC, the Wizards Play Network, or any *Magic: The Gathering* Organized Play program should contact Wizards of the Coast as follows:

*   In North and South America, access the [Knowledge Base](http://wizards.custhelp.com/).
*   In all other areas, go to the [Global Contact Information Page](https://web.archive.org/web/20220930134731/http://company.wizards.com/contact-us).

## Magic Online Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers

The round of Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers taking place between June 10, 2017 and June 17, 2017 will have two *Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers in which qualified players may participate. The first will take place on June 10at 2 p.m. PT, and the second one will take place June 17 at 5 a.m. PT.

To register for a *Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifier, a player who is qualified to compete in that round of Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers must send an email to PremierPlay@wizards.com with the following elements:

*   The title of the email should be "*Magic Online* RPTQ."

The email should include your DCI number, *Magic Online* username, and the date of the *Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifier that you wish to participate in.

The deadlines to register are:

*   Wednesday, June 7 at 11:59 PM PT (for the June 10 tournament)
*   Wednesday, June 14 at 11:59 PM PT (for the June 17 tournament)

*Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers will be capped at 212 players, so sign up early if you intend to play.

The Top 8 players will receive invitations to compete in Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation*, as well as an invitation to play in the Pro Tour Challenge event at that Pro Tour.

The format for these Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers is *Amonkhet* Sealed Deck.

As a reminder, if you play in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier during a qualifying round, **you cannot play in any other Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers during that qualifying round, including *Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers**. Your invitation to compete in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier only lets you compete in one Regional Pro Tour Qualifier.

The prize structures for *Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers differ from the prize structures for in-person events. Please see the event details for this round of Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers in the *Magic Online* client for more information.
