What he was not prepared for was to have <a>Castigate</a> wipe the engine right out of his hand, allowing Herzog's white and black weenies to kill Kai before he could recover. Game 2 went the other way, with Herzog stalling on three land for the entirety of the game. A lack of <a>Castigate</a> this time opened the door for multiple <a>Seething Song</a>s to dump out four <a>Bogardan Hellkite</a>s and a <a>Hunted Dragon</a>, letting Budde attack for the win a turn later despite heavy interference from multiple Martyrs of Sands. This was the result of a suspended <a>Lotus Bloom</a> on turn 1 and then <a>Rite of Flame</a>, <a>Rite of Flame</a>, <a>Rite of Flame</a>, <a>Dragonstorm</a>. He's recently transitioned to an internship as well, and his schedule has kept him away from competitive <b>Magic</b>. Since he was clearly playing an original deck, I asked him where it came from, especially if he hasn't played much lately. He said that he found it &quot;somewhere on the internet&quot; and thought it looked fun, so he tested it a bit and brought it with him to Paris. In theory, it looks somewhat like normal <a>Dragonstorm</a> decks in that it has a red mana engine and <a>Lotus Bloom</a> to kick up an early storm count, but Olle's deck also has <a>Ancestral Vision</a>s, <a>Clockspinning</a>, <a>Grapeshot</a>, and <a>Ignite Memories</a>. He said that he chose this version because it's a little bit faster than the <a>Dragonstorm</a> decks, and it's a bit more fun as well. Judging by his record it's not only fun but is also competitive, since Råde is currently 3-2 with <a href="/en/events/2006/worlds06/round-5-memories-ignited-visions-burst">his last loss</a> coming to Gabriel Nassif in the feature match area.</p>&#10;      <p>Råde said that he got a third-turn kill earlier today via <a>Lotus Bloom</a>, some Rites of Flame, <a>Grapeshot</a> for six copies, <a>Infernal Tutor</a>, and then <a>Ignite Memories</a> for seven copies, but the play that first turned me on to Råde's clever deck came from Josh Ravitz. Ravitz was playing control against Råde and thought himself plenty safe at 21 life. He was wrong. <a>Lotus Bloom</a>, <a>Clockspinning</a> with buyback on two more Lotuses, another <a>Clockspinning</a> on Ancestral Memories, <a>Rite of Flame</a>, <a>Rite of Flame</a>, <a>Grapeshot</a> and a final <a>Grapeshot</a> killed Ravitz and left him grumbling about the loss rounds later.</p>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <a name="6"> </a>&#10;      <li>&#10;        <b>Wednesday, November 29: 8:22 pm - News and Notes</b>&#10;        <div>&#10;          <i>by Ted Knutson</i>&#10;        </div>&#10;      </li>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <p>I'm running a little late here because of a Max Bracht judge ruling during the last round, so I'll wrap up with an avalanche of end-of-day notes and start Thursday with pretty pictures.
