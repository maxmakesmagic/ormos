Before we turn toward *Magic Origins* when previews start next week, we wanted to take one last look back at Modern Masters Weekend—the biggest celebration of *Magic* ever, held around the world.

From Chiba to Las Vegas to Utrecht, the weekend produced memorable stories, fun moments, exciting games and drafts, and memories that more than 15,000 players will take with them for a long time.

Let's start by taking a short look back at the weekend as a whole.

But the weekend produced so many great moments, they couldn't all be contained in just one video. To relive every *Magic* moment we recorded, head on over to [our playlist for Modern Masters Weekend 2015](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL3rP64NRtmbgZZeOEk9sFW0Jr0aYUZ39k). There, among others, you'll find:

*   [Features](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-zFkrI17OL0&list=PL3rP64NRtmbgZZeOEk9sFW0Jr0aYUZ39k&index=1) on cosplay, including the [Chiba cosplayer draft](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z-rMeEvgWtM&list=PL3rP64NRtmbgZZeOEk9sFW0Jr0aYUZ39k&index=3)
*   Interviews with artists, such as [Mark Tedin](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZFkgTfR44M&list=PL3rP64NRtmbgZZeOEk9sFW0Jr0aYUZ39k&index=9)
*   The famous [Chiba Sushi Draft](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zDh7FwVp3HM&list=PL3rP64NRtmbgZZeOEk9sFW0Jr0aYUZ39k&index=6)
*   And, of course, plenty of match coverage!

So take one last look back at the weekend when we all made *Magic* history. Who knows when we'll see another weekend quite like it.
