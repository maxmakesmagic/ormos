One of the surprise hits to come from Grand Prix-Sao Paulo was Diego Crucius’s Cascade list. Foregoing the usual control elements or the Seismic Assault/Swans of Bryn Argoll combo, Diego ramped all the way up to Enlisted Wurm and was all too happy to cascade into either Primal or Incendiary Command. After board, he could even morph into a Land Destruction deck thanks to Fulminator Mage and Rain of Tears. On top of that, due to a miscommunication with the friend who gave him the deck, Diego played an Adarkar Wastes instead of a Llanowar Wastes with no blue cards in his deck! You can read more about Grand Prix-Sao Paulo in the [official event coverage](/en/events/2009/gpsao09/daniel-dominates-defending-brazil).
