## *Double Masters* on *Magic Online*

[As announced previously](/en/articles/magiconline/2020/07-23-double-masters-magic-online-edition-and-gen-con-online), *Double Masters* events are ongoing with Sealed Deck, Draft, and Phantom Draft Leagues available. If you haven't checked out the updated *Magic* Premier Play schedule yet and what Super Qualifiers qualify you for, go [read that announcement](https://magic.gg/news/announcing-the-2020-2021-magic-esports-season).

## Supreme Draft: RAVNIXTRAVAGANZA

[As announced yesterday](/en/articles/magiconline/2020/08-24-introducing-supreme-draft), we're running a series of Draft events in a new format dubbed Supreme Draft, the first of which begins tomorrow, August 26, and ends September 2. We're kicking off this first week with RAVNIXTRAVAGANZA, featuring a cluster of the eight sets from Ravnica that feature the guilds (so that's *Ravnica: City of Guilds*, *Guildpact*, *Dissension*, *Return to Ravnica*, *Gatecrash*, *Dragon's Maze*, *Guilds of Ravnica*, and *Ravnica Allegiance*).

You'll get two or three packs from each of those guild sets, then draft your very Ravnica deck. Entry options include 10 Event Tickets or 100 Play Points, and events are Phantom.

For more information on Supreme Draft and how it works, please check out the announcement article [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2020/08-24-introducing-supreme-draft).

## Showcase Qualifier Weekend

Players who earned entry into the Showcase Qualifier will be squaring off for an invitation to the Champions Showcase. The entry fee for Showcase Qualifiers is a format token that matches the format of the Showcase. As with QPs, after the season's Showcase Qualifiers are finished, all unused Format Tokens will be removed from players' accounts. Each Showcase Qualifier is a scheduled Swiss-style event with a Top 8 playoff. The winner of each Showcase Qualifier earns a spot in the final Showcase event and a chance to earn their share of $70,000 and an upcoming Premier Event invitation.

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format Challenges are the first step toward qualifying for Premier Play through your favorite *Magic* formats. To learn more about Premier Play qualification, click [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020).

*Magic Online*'s Format Challenges now run once on Saturdays and once on Sundays for each Constructed format! On weekends featuring Showcase Challenge events, the Format Challenge that starts at either 6 a.m., 8 a.m., or 10 a.m. Pacific time is pre-empted by the Showcase Challenge—Format Challenges scheduled at other times remain in place. *NOTE: The Pauper Challenge is never pre-empted.*

## Next Downtime August 26

The next downtime will be August 26, beginning at 9 a.m. and continuing until noon. All times listed are Pacific time (UTC-7).

## Build Notes

*   The default size of both cards and text have been increased in the Draft Scene.
*   Unused Season 2 QPs have been removed from players' accounts.
*   Casual games will no longer be forced to end during updates (except full downtimes).
*   A bug has been fixed that could cause an Aura enchanting another player's permanent to occasionally stop displaying in multiplayer games if the board state was crowded.
