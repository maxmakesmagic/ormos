Harry Corvese is well-known on the Pro circuit. A familiar resident of the top tables late on day two, he has three Grand Prix Top 8s to his name, and a reputation as a very tough opponent to out-think. He came into draft 2 today well in contention to add to his Top 8 collection. Here's how the draft panned out:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gporl14/gporlando_corvese.jpg)

  

**Pack 1** 

[Pine Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pine+Walker) set things off, followed by a pair of decent black cards in [Bellowing Saddlebrute](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bellowing+Saddlebrute) and [Debilitating Injury](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Debilitating+Injury). Archer's [Parapet](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Parapet) and [Woolly Loxodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Woolly+Loxodon) cemented the green theme, with a fair-to-middling [Kin-Tree Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kin-Tree+Warden) coming at a fair-to-middling sixth pick. Things got a little bumpy after that, with a very late [Woolly Loxodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Woolly+Loxodon) the highlight. In the meantime, though, there were meagre pickings. No black, minimal green. He spent his mid-to-late picks on [Wind-Scarred Crag](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wind-Scarred+Crag), [Tranquil Cove](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tranquil+Cove), and [Dismal Backwater](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismal+Backwater). It's also worth noting that, alongside that 11th pick [Woolly Loxodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Woolly+Loxodon), there was a [Mardu Roughrider](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Roughrider). From the outside looking in, this looked like making Mardu all kinds of open. 

**Pack 2** 

For the longest time (ie 42 seconds), Corvese looked at [Zurgo Helmsmasher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zurgo+Helmsmasher) in his first pick of pack 2. In the event, he didn't move in, taking an unexceptional, but totally serviceable, [Throttle](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Throttle). [Ivorytusk Fortress](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ivorytusk+Fortress) was up next, which should, you would think, have cemented him in Abzan. 

Then came [Surrak Dragonclaw](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Surrak+Dragonclaw), which he just couldn't pass up, and that took us into full-on 'take the land' mode, with [Scoured Barrens](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scoured+Barrens), [Jungle Hollow](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jungle+Hollow), [Wooded Foothills](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wooded+Foothills), and [Blossoming Sands](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blossoming+Sands) his next four picks. Two of the next three picks definitively proved that Mardu couldn't have been more open – [Mardu Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Charm) pick 8, and [Ponyback Brigade](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponyback+Brigade) pick 10. That still wasn't late enough for all of the lands, with another [Blossoming Sands](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blossoming+Sands) joining the ever-growing pile of non-basics. 

So, let's take a moment to review. We started out black and green, but found both of those colors problematic. We've taken a pile of lands, so right now, at the end of pack two, we have access from our eight non-basics to the following sources of mana:

At this point, I assumed that Corvese would be full steam ahead on the 'best card in the pack' plan, with his mana essentially sorted. It was payoff time. Wasn't it?

**Pack 3** 

Boom! A second [Surrak Dragonclaw](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Surrak+Dragonclaw). That's exactly why you go five color, to destroy people with golden power, right? Wrong. [Abzan Falconer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Falconer) was the pick here. Pick 2 wasn't a strong pack, but [Sandsteppe Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sandsteppe+Citadel) wasn't exactly a windmill slam. Then he took [Swiftwater Cliffs](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swiftwater+Cliffs) over [Ponyback Brigade](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponyback+Brigade), and then [Bloodfell Caves](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodfell+Caves) over both [Rite of the Serpent](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rite+of+the+Serpent) and [Mardu Roughrider](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Roughrider). [Smite the Monstrous](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smite+the+Monstrous) and [Dragonscale Boon](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonscale+Boon) would clearly both make the deck, but then it was back to the lands with [Mystic Monastery](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystic+Monastery). The back end of the pack provided some useful Abzan synergies, featuring two [Salt Road Patrol](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Salt+Road+Patrol), plus an [Archers' Parapet](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archers%27+Parapet), and [Mardu Hateblade](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Hateblade). 

OK, so when all was said and done, Corvese had access to the following sources of mana, just from the cards he'd drafted:

OK, so, terrific, you get to cast your spells. But what are the spells you're trying to cast? At this point, looking through the complete pick order, I was truly struggling to find even close to 22/23 cards that I'd actually want to play in a draft, and obviously the goal with the five color decks is to out-power your opponents with the devastating card quality of your picks. I don't see this here, so it's probably time for us to show you the entire pool, and see what you would play!

All of the lands – but not necessarily all of the spells to go with them. This format is just incredible.
