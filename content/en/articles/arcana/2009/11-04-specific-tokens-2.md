Sometimes, tokens are generic. The *Zendikar* Bird Token is, frankly, just a bird:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4c/67/4c67cf702c035dc13464a1913899d194a5219489cc39508e590d4865d6afcf81.jpg) 

  

Other times, however, a token moves from the general to the particular. Take, for example, this *Zendikar* Wolf token:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6a/54/6a540b873cd18f80bdd8e107c200c2910d741b9a73ae0e41568f01c50d60b9e7.jpg) 

  

You *could* use it with your Fable of Wolf and Owl or Master of the Wild Hunt. But isn't it especially handsome next to Turntimber Ranger?

 ![Turntimber Ranger](https://i.ormos.uk/13/d5/13d50c49b5104648ec226323ec558ffd1f63c48d3b9fa381a502d8fee22005a6.jpg) 

Yep. Same wolf.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/35/f1/35f1c4c6fe760740cdea426a50fc6bcdf8c9a239638bdd5c6e6eb6777eca873c.jpg) 

Turntimber Ranger art by Wayne Reynolds
