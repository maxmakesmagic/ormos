## Pioneer on *Magic Online*

Pioneer, *Magic*'s new nonrotating format featuring cards from *Return to Ravnica* forward, has arrived on *Magic Online*. Pioneer Leagues and Format Challenges are well underway, so now's the time to build something new!

For more information about Pioneer, check out [Blake's announcement article](/en/articles/feature/2019/10-21-announcing-the-pioneer-format).

* * *

## *Innistrad* Flashback Draft October 30

To celebrate Halloween, players can enjoy frightful fights in the upcoming *Innistrad* Flashback Draft! Get in on all the spooky action starting Wednesday, October 30. *Innistrad* Flashback Draft runs through November 6, so you'll have plenty of time to enjoy the creepiest of creatures.

*Innistrad* Flashback Draft is Phantom—cards you open will not be added to your collection.

## Legacy Premier League

[Legacy Premier League](https://itsjulian.com/tag/legacy-premier-league/), Legacy's popular community league, makes its return on twitch.tv/itsJulian. Don't miss the first week of playoffs on Wednesday at 6 p.m. ET (3 p.m. PT). Head over to the [Legacy Premier League](https://itsjulian.com/tag/legacy-premier-league/) page to check out previous results, past videos, decklists, and more.

## Pioneer PTQ Finals Friday

Friday, November 1, is our first-ever Pioneer PTQ Finals at 9 a.m. PT! The event will cost 30 Event Tickets or 300 Play Points. The winner receives an invitation to their choice of the first set of Players Tour [regional events in February](https://magic.gg/news/regional-players-tour-events-coming-in-february-2020).

Learn more about Players Tour Qualifier events [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20191029000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-players-tour-qualifiers#constructedprelims).

* * *

## Standard MOCS Preliminary Saturday

On Saturday, November 2, there will be a *Magic Online* Championship Series Preliminary event for the 2019 MOCS! The format will be Standard, and things will kick off at 7 a.m. PT. MOCS Preliminaries are eight rounds of Swiss and require 35 Qualifier Points to enter. Winners will receive (among other prizes) an invitation to the MOCS Playoff event on December 28.

Learn more about MOCS events [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20191029000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2019-magic-online-championship-series#MOCSPrelims2019).

* * *

## Standard PTQ Finals Sunday

On Sunday, November 3, there will be a Standard PTQ Finals at 9 a.m. PT (17:00 UTC—one hour later due to North America's Daylight Savings). The event will cost 30 Event Tickets or 300 Play Points. The winner receives an invitation to their choice of the first set of Players Tour [regional events in February](https://magic.gg/news/regional-players-tour-events-coming-in-february-2020).

Learn more about Players Tour Qualifier events [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20191029000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-players-tour-qualifiers#constructedprelims).
