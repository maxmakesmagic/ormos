Narrowly missing out on the Top 8 of a Pro Tour can be a mixed blessing. While you miss out on the Sunday Stage, which can be disappointing, a finish just outside the Top 8 still provides a Pro Tour invitation, a handful of Pro Points, and a nice prize payout as a consolation.

![Hero of Iroas](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Hero%20of%20Iroas&options=)Jared Boettcher has experienced this near miss two Pro Tours in a row. After a 9th-place finish at [Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*](https://web.archive.org/web/20140526000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/ptbng14/welcome), Jared nearly duplicated the feat, finishing in 10th place at [Pro Tour *Journey into Nyx*](https://web.archive.org/web/20140526000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/ptjou14/welcome). With two excellent Pro Tours, Jared finds himself comfortably in the lead of the [Rookie of the Year race](https://web.archive.org/web/20140526000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Events.aspx?x=protour/standings/roy201314). If Jared's lead holds, he will be invited to the World Championship later this year.

His Constructed deck of choice was a WU Heroic deck with some unconventional card choices. [Aqueous Form](https://web.archive.org/web/20140526000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aqueous+Form), [Battlewise Hoplite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140526000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battlewise+Hoplite), and [Ordeal of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140526000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ordeal+of+Thassa) are not cards that were expected to make the leap to Constructed, but Jared found a way to make a high-synergy deck using unexpected cards. Making a large creature quickly is easy for this deck, and there are cards like [Gods Willing](https://web.archive.org/web/20140526000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gods+Willing) and [Ajani's Presence](https://web.archive.org/web/20140526000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ajani%27s+Presence) to protect them.
