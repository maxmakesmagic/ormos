**Magic: The Gathering Pro Tour - Venice Qualifier  
 January 25-26, 2003**

[](/ja/events/2002/hiroshima02/grand-prix-hiroshima)  
[Trial Schedule](https://web.archive.org/web/20041226175818/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=grandprix/hiroshima02/trials)

The **Magic: The Gathering** Grand Prix tournament series is an international circuit of large-scale Qualifier tournaments featuring cash prizes and multiple invitations to Pro Tour events.

### TOURNAMENT FORMAT AND SCHEDULE

The format for Grand Prix - Hiroshima is Extended Constructed format. All DCI™ Constructed rules and **Magic**® Floor Rules announced on October 1, 2002, along with all subsequent updates, will be enforced.

*   **Friday, January 24:** Registration from 15:00 - 22:00.
*   **Saturday, January 25:** Tournament begins at 9:00. The Top 64 players advance to the finals on Sunday.
*   **Sunday, January 26:** Finals begin at 9:00.

### TOURNAMENT DETAILS

**Divisions**  
 The main tournament at Grand Prix - Hiroshima is open to **Magic** players of all ages.

*If the player who wins an invitation to Pro Tour – Venice at Grand Prix – Hiroshima has already qualified for an invitation, the invitation shall be extended to the next-best finisher. Previous qualification may occur through a Qualifier tournament, through performance at a professional tournament, or by being an invited DCI player (valid on the date of the official rank announcement for the appropriate professional tournament).*

**Tournament Structure**  
 The structure for Grand Prix – Hiroshima is modified Swiss-style (50-minute rounds, with the number of rounds determined by the number of participants), with the Top 64 players advancing to the Sunday finals for prizes and invitations.

**Tournament Byes** *(Byes are not cumulative through the season or year.)*  
 Byes for Grand Prix - Hiroshima are based on the 2002 Pro Tour Boston professional standings and DCI ratings as of October 23, 2002. Please refer to the [Grand Prix - Hiroshima Bye List](/en/events/2002/hiroshima02/grand-prix-hiroshima-byes) for details. 

**HEAD JUDGE**  
 Kazuhiro Wakatsuki (Level 3/Japan)
