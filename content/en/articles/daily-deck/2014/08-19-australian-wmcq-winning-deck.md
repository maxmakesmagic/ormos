World *Magic* Cup Qualifiers happened all over the world this past weekend. Justin Robb took one of the breakout decks of Pro Tour *Magic 2015*, Jund Planeswalkers, and won the Australian WMCQ with his take on the archetype. Congratulations to Justin! See the full schedule of World *Magic* Cup Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20140819000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/wmcq/schedule).
