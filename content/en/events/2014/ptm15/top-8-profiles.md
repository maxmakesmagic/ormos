### Matt Sperling

**Age:** 31  
**Hometown:** San Francisco, California USA  
**Limited Record:** 4-2  
**Constructed Record:** 8-1-1

 

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**  
 GP Champion, PT Top 16, PT Top 32

**Did you know?**  
 Matt Sperling had a staggering 82.9% win rate at Grand Prix events this season!

**What colors were the decks you drafted? What did you think of each deck?**  
 Red-Green, good aura synergy. Blue-Black, control. Both decks were 2-1/3-0 caliber.

**What Constructed deck are you playing, and why?**  
 Red-White Burn. It's under the radar, [Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames) is a nice *Magic 2015* addition, as is [Battlefield Forge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battlefield+Forge).

**What *Magic 2015* cards do you think had the greatest impact on the weekend?**  
 Pain-lands

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?**  
 A dream come true. 20 years in the making.

**Who did you prepare with?**  
 Whoever responded to my Facebook status where I asked for input/help: Patrick Sullivan, Adrian Sullivan, Anand Khare, Frank Gilson, Dustin Cristos, Adam Barnello, Jason Ford, Jarvis Yu, and Mark Herberholz.

### Ivan Floch

**Age:** 28  
**Hometown:** Bratislava, Slovakia  
**Limited Record:** 5-1  
**Constructed Record:** 7-2-1

 

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**  
 Team World Champion, Chiba 2010

**Did you know?**  
 Ivan won more than 70% of his Grand Prix matches this season.

**What colors were the decks you drafted? What did you think of each deck?**  
 Red splashing Blue; very aggressive with 7 one-drops, good deck. 2-1. Red-Green with very high curve and 2 rates; not that great, but it was good in the Red-Green mirror, which I played twice. 3-0.

**What Constructed deck are you playing, and why?**  
 White-Blue Cleansing Control, because I like the style and because of Stanislav Cifka — he worked on the deck list.

**What *Magic 2015* cards do you think had the greatest impact on the weekend?**  
 Maybe [Goblin Rabblemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Rabblemaster).

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?**  
 A LOT!

**Who did you prepare with?**  
 Stanislav Cifka, Martin Juza, Frank Karsten, Matej Zatlkaj, Robert Jurkovič, Robin Dollar, Adam Koska, Nikola Vavra, and Petr Sochurek.
