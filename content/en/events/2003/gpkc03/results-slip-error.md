The Round two feature match pitting Laura Mills against Cameron Heize was reported here on www.sideboard.com as a victory for Cameron, on the final turn of the round.

One sharp observer already has noted that if you look at the points following round two you see that Laura Mills is listed with six points.

Laura and Cameron incorrectly filled out their results slip, awarding a two to one victory to Laura instead of to Cameron. Both players then signed the slip and turned it into the judges at the event.

Once match slips are filled out, signed and turned in, they become the final result for that match. Laura was awarded with a round two win and Cameron was recorded as suffering the defeat.

Let this be a lesson to you children. Be sure to double check your results slips before you sign them and turn them in!
