The Japanese coverage team here this weekend have undertaken the gargantuan task of typing all three hundred and fifty eight decklists.

That's a lot of [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20110717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain)s. However, sometimes enquiring minds want answers on the spot, and that means turning to one of the many 'excellent' free translation software packages available courtesy of the internet.

Let's give you a neat example. Here's a Standard decklist:

OK, so let's start with the easy ones, **the lands.**

The Mountain is, er, [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20110717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain).  
 The Dry Plateau is [Arid Mesa](https://web.archive.org/web/20110717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arid+Mesa).  
 The Small Lake Which Boils is [Scalding Tarn](https://web.archive.org/web/20110717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scalding+Tarn).  
 The Peak Which Is Shaken is, of course, [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20110717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks).

**Now the creatures.** Some of these are great names.

 The Patriarch of the Toge Shooting is [Spikeshot Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20110717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spikeshot+Elder).  
 The Lava of the Tannin Surface To Use is [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer).

**The spells:**

 The Grenade of Goburin is [Goblin Grenade](https://web.archive.org/web/20110717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Grenade) (surprise again!) [Devastating Summons](https://web.archive.org/web/20110717000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devastating+Summons) is...wait...
