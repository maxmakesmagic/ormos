The [Super Sunday Series Finals](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/owen-smashes-first-ever-super-sunday-series) showcased *Born of the Gods*, and once the Standard rounds started, all eyes were on two people—Makihito Mihara and Aaron Lewis. I'm looking at Mihara's deck today, but Aaron's will show up later this week. 

![Ephara, God of the Polis](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ephara%2C+God+of+the+Polis)

Mihara, Hall of Fame hopeful and former world champion, has been doing incredible work as of late. He made Top 4 of [Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze*](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/voice-raises-wescoe-above-all-pro-tour) with Esper Control then followed it up by making Top 8 at [Pro Tour *Theros*](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/dezani-proclaims-allez-les-bleus-pro-tour) with RG Devotion. He demonstrated his range even further at the Super Sunday Series by playing a difficult-to-categorize Esper deck. 

His deck, similar to the deck that won [Grand Prix Shizuoka](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/nakadas-orzhov-army-marches-victory), is a white-based deck with some aggressive elements like [Soldier of the Pantheon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soldier+of+the+Pantheon), [Precinct Captain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Precinct+Captain), and [Imposing Sovereign](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Imposing+Sovereign). However, Mihara's deck also features the [Obzedat, Ghost Council](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Obzedat%2C+Ghost+Council)/[Whip of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos) combo, plus Ephara and Heliod for even more late-game power. 

Is it an aggro Human deck? Is it an aggro-control deck? I'm guessing neither or both, depending on your draw. When you curve out from [Soldier of the Pantheon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soldier+of+the+Pantheon) into [Obzedat, Ghost Council](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Obzedat%2C+Ghost+Council), it won't matter what you classify your deck as—you're going to win.
