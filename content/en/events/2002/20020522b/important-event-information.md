### **Magic: The Gathering**® Nationals Event Update!

**The Magic: The Gathering**® Nationals mailer that went out to all East Coast DCI players incorrectly listed the dates for the event. The actual dates of the event are as follows:

Nationals Open: **Thursday, May 30, 2002**  
 Nationals Friday: **May 31 through June 2, 2002**

For more information on this event visit [wizards.com/tournaments](https://web.archive.org/web/20050201021134/http://www.wizards.com/tournaments), or call **Disney Sports Travel** at (407) 939-7810 to make your travel arrangements today.
