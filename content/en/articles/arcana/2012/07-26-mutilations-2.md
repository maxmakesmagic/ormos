The card Mutilate made its appearance in *Torment*, where it looked like this.

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0f/2e/0f2ef955aab28ef860dab2d361555b0f07d453bfa3425c508bc7f282bc5bd0f7.jpg) 

Mutilate | Art by [Eric Peterson](https://web.archive.org/web/20120726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22eric+peterson%22])

Then it went away for a long time, because it was very powerful. It made a reappearance in *Duel Decks: Garruk vs. Liliana*, where it looked like this.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/1024_mutilate_dd.jpg) 

Mutilate | Art by [Zoltan Boros & Gabor Szikszai](https://web.archive.org/web/20120726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?action=advanced&artist=+[zoltan]+[gabor])

But since its first apperance in *Torment* back in 2002, Mutilate hasn't been in a regular set. Until ***Magic** 2013*! And now it looks like this.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/1024_mutilate_m13.jpg) 

Mutilate | Art by [Tyler Jacobson](https://web.archive.org/web/20120726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22tyler+jacobson%22])
