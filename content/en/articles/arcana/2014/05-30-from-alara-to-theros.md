The Modern Event Deck goes on sale today! [It includes](/en/articles/feature/2014/05-13-decklist-for-modern-event-deck) 1 [Elspeth, Knight-Errant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140530000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Knight-Errant), a card first introduced in *Shards of Alara*. All this week, [the Card of the Day theme](/en/articles/cardoftheday/2014/05-01-card-of-the-day-may-2014) has been about the Elspeth story on Theros (spoilers!), which tied in with Monday's feature, "[Journey's End](/en/articles/makingmagic/2014/05-26-journeys-end)." So it makes sense to bookend the week with an Arcana about [Elspeth](https://web.archive.org/web/20140530000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=212241), [knight-errant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140530000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=174859) and [sun's champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140530000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=373649).
