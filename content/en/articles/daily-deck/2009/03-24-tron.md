The Urzatron (Urza's Mine, Power Plant, and Tower) is a powerful strategy that has taken many players to great successes. It has fallen in popularity of late in the world of Extended, but Masaya Honda has put an end to that! He used the lands to power out heavy hitters like Grim Poppet, Platinum Angel, and Mindslaver on his way to a PTQ Top 8 in Hiroshima, Japan.
