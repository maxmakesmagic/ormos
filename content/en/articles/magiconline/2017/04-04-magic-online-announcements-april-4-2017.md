**Throwback Standard Gauntlet begins tomorrow, April 5**

The Throwback Standard Gauntlet is back on the schedule! Starting tomorrow, you'll be able to enjoy the Standard environment of *Mirage*-*Tempest* with preconstructed decks on *Magic Online*. For more information, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20170819053137/http://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/magic-online/flashback-standard-gauntlets-mirage-tempest).

***Aether Revolt*-*Kaladesh* Sealed MOCS Open on Saturday, April 8**

A Sealed *Magic Online* Championship Series (MOCS) Open Event is coming up on Saturday, April 8! No Qualifier Points are needed; players use Event Tickets or Play Points to enter instead! The winner of the tournament will receive an invitation to Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation* in Kyoto and an invitation to the *Magic Online* Championship!

We look forward to seeing you on April 8 for the latest event! Learn more about the 2017 *Magic Online* Championship Series here.

**Legacy Challenge on Saturday, April 8**

The next Legacy Challenge is coming up on April 8! Big prizes are on the line for the Top 8, like the ten original dual lands, [Rishadan Port](https://web.archive.org/web/20170404000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rishadan+Port), [Wasteland](https://web.archive.org/web/20170404000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland), and more. Get the full event details [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20170404000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-format-challenges#Legacy).

***Hour of Devastation* Modern Pro Tour Qualifier on Sunday, April 9**

Attention all Pro Tour hopefuls: a Pro Tour Qualifier (PTQ) is approaching! We hope to see you in the PTQ Finals on Sunday, April 9!

Remember, the Pro Tour Qualifier Preliminaries now follow a set weekly schedule, thanks to the redesigned PTQ system that *Magic Online* Events Manager Lee Sharpe announced [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/02-14-pro-tour-qualifier-preliminary-schedule-changes).

Learn more about *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20170404000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers#HOU_schedule).

After Monday, April 10, Constructed Pro Tour Qualifier Preliminaries will revert to Standard format.
