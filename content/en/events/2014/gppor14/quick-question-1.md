 **What's the most under or overrated card in *Magic 2015* Sealed?** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gppor14/Paulo.JPG)

 ***Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa:** "[Xathrid Slyblade](https://web.archive.org/web/20140809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xathrid+Slyblade) is overrated. He looks like he's invincible, but every time you attack you hope they don't block because you have to spend so much mana on it. And there are so many cards, like [Raise the Alarm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raise+the+Alarm), that trade back the two damage or block it."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gppor14/Neal%20Oliver.JPG)

 ***Neal Oliver:** "[Festergloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20140809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Festergloom) is underrated. It's a really good card and I'm main-decking one today. In my last round I used it to kill my [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic), his Mystic and [Netcaster Spider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Netcaster+Spider). Then I played a 10/10 [Undergrowth Scavenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Undergrowth+Scavenger)."* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gppor14/Josh%20Utter-Leyton.JPG)

 ***(8) Josh Utter-Leyton:** "Underrated is [Pillar of Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20140809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pillar+of+Light). This format is really about the good cards, and it kills so many of the bombs. It deals with the things that will beat you. It's not as good as [Triplicate Spirits](https://web.archive.org/web/20140809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triplicate+Spirits), but it's up there."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gppor14/David%20Ochoa.JPG)

 ***David Ochoa:** "One-toughness creatures are overrated. [Child of Night](https://web.archive.org/web/20140809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Child+of+Night) or Oreskos Swift-Claw. There are so many things that trade with them and [Forge Devil](https://web.archive.org/web/20140809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forge+Devil)."*
