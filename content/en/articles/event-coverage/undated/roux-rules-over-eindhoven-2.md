Sebastien Roux wins Grand Prix Eindhoven!

This was the largest Grand Prix in Holland with 1011 competitors. An incredibly diverse Extended field saw 8 different decks in the Top 8. Sebastien Roux made tough work of it with his [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog) deck. His quarterfinal match was the last to finish as he overcame Michael Leicht's Madness deck. Then it was a marathon two-hour match of control on control as he struggled to overcome Xuan-Phi Nguyen's [Solitary Confinement](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Solitary+Confinement) deck. With barely a pause it was onto the final against former Dutch champion Rogier Maaten. Maaten was thought to have a clear advantage with his Affinity deck but it never really fired. Repeated [Rebuild](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rebuild)s kept knocking the Dutch player back to square one until Roux could go lethal with his 'tog. Eventually Mr. Teeth prevailed over Mr. Ravager as Roux picked up the title.

## Sunday, Feb. 27: 6:22 pm - Quarterfinals - Kamiel Cornelissen vs. Tobias Radloff

In the rush to cover red deck I managed to lose out, but don't be too disheartened as there'll be giant monsters galore smashing into the red zone this match. Kamiel Cornelissen is running the Cephalid [Life](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Life) deck. This bizarre concoction either makes itself almost invulnerable by gaining a billion life or milling its entire library away and smashing face with a [Reanimate](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reanimate)d [Sutured Ghoul](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sutured+Ghoul). Tobias Radloff is running straight Reanimator. He'll get his monsters out quicker but they probably won't kill Cornelissen before he gets to pull off one of the combos. On paper this match should be the quickest but there is the Kamiel factor as the Dutch player is known to be a very careful...Oh, the first game is over.

That was brutal. Radloff imprinted a [Chrome Mox](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chrome+Mox), dropped a [Putrid Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrid+Imp) and then [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm)ed into a monster at the end of turn. The second turn whipped Akroma into play. [Rorix](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rorix) followed on turn 3 to end a lighting fast game.

Radloff 1-0 Cornelissen

Cornelissen opened Game 2 with a flimsy [Starlit Sanctum](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Starlit+Sanctum). Radloff had a less explosive start this time, merely dropping Akroma off into the graveyard with [Careful Study](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Careful+Study).

Cornelissen dropped a [City of Brass](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=City+of+Brass) and passed the turn. Radloff [Exhume](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exhume)d Akroma, Angel of many stupid abilities and slapped Cornelissen in the face for 6.

The critical turn three approached, but Cornelissen had a cheekier play. He cast [Worldly Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Worldly+Tutor) to put a [Gilded Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gilded+Drake) on top of his library and then cast it. A [Gilded Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gilded+Drake) for an Akroma is a fair trade, right. Cornelissen kept the Angel at home, not even blocking when Radloff swung at him with the Drake. If the Drake died it would be too easy for Radloff to steal it out of a graveyard and get Akroma back.

Radloff was getting his girl back anyway, as [Echoing Truth](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Echoing+Truth) returned her to hand. He then imprinted a Tutor on a Mox to summon a [Putrid Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrid+Imp) Cornelissen [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm)ed into his third land and managed a [Nomads en-Kor](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nomads+en-Kor). He wasn't pulling this one out though as Radloff discard Akroma to the Imp and then [Reanimate](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reanimate)d her for a final brutal attack.

In all the match took maybe 14 turns.

Tobias Radloff beats Kamiel Cornelissen 2-0.

  

## Sunday, Feb. 27: 6:37 pm - Quarterfinals - Bas Postema vs. Xuan-Phi Nguyen

Bas is a well-respected player in the Netherlands since he wins a lot of PTQs and performs well in other tournaments, but he's never posted a very good result in international tournaments until now. This is Xuan-Phi's second GP top 8 result, his first being 2003 GP Brussels.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a2/5a/a25ada5534e166fb24757a5c78415ebc330b0f500ebe4eae0c3e26e37625fbe9.jpg)*Bas Postema*

As soon as I sat down to cover the matchup, Bas commented that it would be boring because of the silliness of it. It's all about speed: Can Xuan-Phi set up his Confinement lock which Bas has no answer for before he gets beaten down by the little green men?

A big part of the matchup is the die roll. Bas wins it and elects to play first. He opens a hand of double [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain), [Wasteland](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland), Port, [Gempalm Incinerator](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gempalm+Incinerator), [Goblin Warchief](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Warchief) and [Goblin Matron](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Matron). He thinks about it for a short while, but sends it back. A lot of people would keep this hand immediately since they see lands and spells, but the cards Bas opened are just not what he's looking for. If he can't kill Xuan-Phi quickly, he's going to get hardlocked. Bas' second hand looks a bit better as it has the key card of his deck which can also get around a Scepter/Chant lock: AEther Vial. Along that he's got a [mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=mountain), Port, [Wasteland](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland), [Mogg Fanatic](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mogg+Fanatic) and [Gempalm Incinerator](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gempalm+Incinerator). Xuan-Phi keeps his hand.

Maaten went first and managed a modest start: a second turn Ravager. The third turn was more exciting as he played Worker, [Welding Jar](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Welding+Jar) and then a 1 mana [Myr Enforcer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Enforcer). [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) stopped the Enforcer, but Maaten wasn't about to waste any time as the Ravager fed itself up to a 4/4.

Roux [Boomerang](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boomerang)ed the Ravager on the following turn when Maaten went for a second Enforcer. The Enforcer got in to knock Roux down to 11 and then Maaten emptied his hand.

A [Cunning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cunning+Wish) to fetch a [Rebuild](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rebuild) made the Dutchman pick it all up again. Would it give Roux enough time to get into the game. He took a few nibbles from an [Atog](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Atog) and then cast [Fact or Fiction](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fact+or+Fiction) in response to the Ravager coming back.

Maaten split [Intuition](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Intuition), [Engineered Explosives](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engineered+Explosives), [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island) to [Masticore](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Masticore) and [Accumulated Knowledge](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Accumulated+Knowledge). Roux took the larger pile and then [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm)ed. He [Intuition](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Intuition)ed in his upkeep to fetch the [Swamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swamp) he needed and then dropped an [Engineered Explosives](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engineered+Explosives) for two. That picked off both an [Atog](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Atog) and Ravager and it was starting to look like Maaten might have run out of steam. He dropped a [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite) only to see Mr Teeth finally make an appearance on Roux's side of the board.

Could Maaten retrieve this game? He paid two mana for a [Myr Enforcer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Enforcer) and followed with [Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault). End of turn Roux used the Tog to send [Rebuild](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rebuild) into the removed from play. From here he could retrieve it with [Cunning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cunning+Wish). Maaten picked up virtually all of his permanents for the second time in the game. From here it was all about counting cards as [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog) went lethal.

Maybe this wouldn't be the bloodbath anticipated.

Roux 1-0 Maaten

"This is the first time I've lost Game 1 this weekend," Maaten mentioned as he looked through his sideboard.

Game 2 started much better for the Dutchman. His opponent mulliganed and he found turn one [Aether Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Vial). Maaten didn't have a second land but a second [Aether Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Vial) felt just as good. He Vialed out a Worker and started swinging.

Two [Chromatic Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chromatic+Sphere)s took the artifact count to 7. Except Roux didn't like the second Sphere and countered it with [Miscalculation](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Miscalculation).

Both players were sitting on only two land, but it was not even remotely balanced as Maaten had six other permanents to Roux's zero.

Roux drew another non-land card, and was forced to discard. Maaten hadn't really exploded but as Roux hadn't even got started, a hand of minor threats was enough to level the match.

Roux 1-1 Maaten

And so it came down to a deciding game with Roux to lead off. This time it Maaten with the mulligan. His affinity deck hadn't really fired yet this match and was in danger of missing the opportunity. Another mulligan and predictions of a one-sided bloodbath were feeling rather premature.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/15/d8/15d87a90063eef39bb856e0f4dde6b513b187e1e0ae68bccd057d1783209a214.jpg)*Rogier Maaten*

Maaten went with the five cards, finding [Chromatic Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chromatic+Sphere) and then [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite). The [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite) met a [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak), but this allowed a Ravager to sneak in on the next turn. Roux calmly Wished for [Rebuild](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rebuild). He held onto it as Maaten didn't look to be going anywhere in a hurry.

Mr Teeth entered the board as Maaten looked for juice with a [Thoughtcast](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtcast). He had an [Aether Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Vial) but desperately needed some form of offence as the game was slipping away from him.

A second [Accumulated Knowledge](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Accumulated+Knowledge) pumped up Roux's hand. He thought for a while and then cast the [Rebuild](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rebuild). Maaten Vialed a Disciple into play and thought. Could he do enough damage if the Ravager ate everything and was Flung at Roux's head.

Maybe not, he only chose to ate a excess land for one damage before picking up his cards. Roux took total control on his next turn. [Engineered Explosives](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engineered+Explosives) blew up Maaten's [Aether Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808200528/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Vial) and Disciple and a Dustbowl left the Dutchman permanentless. There was no coming back from that.

Sebastien Roux beats Rogier Maaten 2-1 and is champion of GP Eindhoven.
