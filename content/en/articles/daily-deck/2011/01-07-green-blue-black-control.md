Looking for something fun to try in the world of Standard? Why not give Green-Blue-Black Control a chance? Lennyo did during a Standard queue on [**Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530152419/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Digital/MagicOnline.aspx) and managed to go 4-0 as a result. The deck solves the debate over which is better, Frost or Grave Titan, by simply playing both! Jace, the Mind Sculptor and Oracle of Mul Daya ensure a steady stream of card advantage while Doom Blade and Spreading Seas help make sure the opponent is kept off balance.
