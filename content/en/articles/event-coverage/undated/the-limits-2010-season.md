### Gateway Tournaments

Gateway Tournaments are open to any player, and can be organized by any WPN Gateway level organizer. (For more information about the WPN and organizer levels, please refer to the [WPN page](https://web.archive.org/web/20100116031101/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dci/wpn/main).)

There will be three seasons of Gateway Tournaments in 2010, held during the following date ranges: March 13 – April 4, June 5 – July 4, and August 21 – September 12. Each organizer may run one Tournament per round. Gateway Tournaments will be run using Sealed Deck format, are Rules Enforcement Level: Regular, and are 16K. (For more information about formats, rules enforcement levels and K Value, please refer to the [DCI Tournament Rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20101005174249/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dci/doccenter/home).) Organizers and other event staff are permitted to play in Gateway Tournaments. Please refer to the above links for event information for each Tournament round.

Winners of a Gateway Tournament will earn a 1-round bye at the Regional Tournaments (see below). Byes are not cumulative; if a player wins more than one Gateway Tournament, he or she will receive only one 1-round bye. However, byes are not "used up"—a player with a bye from a Gateway Tournament may play in more than one Regional Tournament and would have a 1-round bye at each. Additional prizes such as product or promotional cards may be offered; check with your local organizer for details.

### Regional Tournaments

There are two types of Regional Tournaments: Regular and Special. All Regional Tournaments will take place between October 30 and November 14. Regional Tournaments are run using Sealed Deck format, are Rules Enforcement Level: Competitive, and are 24K.

### The Limits Championship

The Championship for The Limits will be held at the 2010 **Magic** World Championships in Chiba the week of December 6 (exact date will be announced later). The event is open to any player who meets one of the following criteria:

*   Regional Tournament top finishers
*   The Limits 2009 Champion

The Limits Championship is run using three rounds of Draft format, three rounds of Sealed Deck format, followed by a cut to a Top 8 single elimination round using Rotisserie Draft, a format in which all cards except basic lands in the current block are drafted using Rochester Draft rules. The Championship is Rules Enforcement Level: Competitive and 32K. (Note: Due to the format, the Top 8 portion of the Championship is non-sanctioned.
