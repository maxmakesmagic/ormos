The shocklands are back! Well, five of them are, anyway. *Return to Ravnica* contains [Hallowed Fountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20120924000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallowed+Fountain), [Steam Vents](https://web.archive.org/web/20120924000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steam+Vents), [Blood Crypt](https://web.archive.org/web/20120924000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Crypt), [Overgrown Tomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20120924000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overgrown+Tomb), and [Temple Garden](https://web.archive.org/web/20120924000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temple+Garden), each of which were first seen in the original *Ravnica* Block. Let's see how they've changed!
