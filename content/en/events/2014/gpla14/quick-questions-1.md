**What Standard Card Has the most Unrealized Potential?**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpla14/QH%20-%20Wily.jpg)

***Willy Edel** – [Purphoros, God of the Forge](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Purphoros%2C+God+of+the+Forge). Red has a new [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) \[Monastery Swiftblade\], and we are seeing a new resurgence of control. Purphoros is positioned well to give red decks reach against control. And gives the midrange match-up inevitability.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpla14/QH%20-%20Huey.jpg)

***(9) William Jensen** – [Perilous Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Perilous+Vault). It's very powerful against Green. Nobody's playing it yet.* 

 

 

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpla14/QH%20-%20Stern.jpg)

***Jon Stern** – [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves). It's just a great card, especially against things like [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino). All the pieces aren't quite there, but it could become very good in the meta.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpla14/QH%20-%20Sperling.jpg)

***Matt Sperling** – [Nessian Game Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nessian+Game+Warden). Green decks need a threat that also develops your hand. There is a lot of removal going around now.* 

 

 

 

 

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpla14/QH%20-%20EFro.jpg)

***(11) Eric Froehlich** – [Nullify](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nullify). Can I say that? People need to play more of that.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpla14/QH%20-%20Comer.jpg)

***Alan Comer** – [Howl of the Horde](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Howl+of+the+Horde). I tried to make an [Ensoul Artifact](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ensoul+Artifact) deck with [Shrapnel Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrapnel+Blast) and [Howl of the Horde](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Howl+of+the+Horde) for twenty damage all at once. It didn't work out, but something's there.* 

 

 

 

 

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpla14/QH%20-%20Wescoe.jpg)

***Craig Wescoe** – [Clever Impersonator](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clever+Impersonator). He can beat a [Sarkhan, the Dragonspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sarkhan%2C+the+Dragonspeaker) by being a Sarkhan; he can negate [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino); and a whole horde of other uses people aren't using yet.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpla14/QH%20-%20Wilson%20and%20Pardee.jpg)

****(22) Jacob Wilson and Sam Pardee –**** 

 

 

 

 

 

*Sam: "[Savage Knuckleblade](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Knuckleblade). I worked on a few decks with it, and just didn't get there. I didn't get to test in between the Pro Tour and here, but if I did, I would've worked on that deck."* 

 

 

*Jacob: "I would say [Stubborn Denial](https://web.archive.org/web/20141018000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stubborn+Denial) for the same reasons. That card goes right into that Knuckleblade deck."* 

*Sam: "Oh, and Crater's Claws. That card is real good. Wait, it goes in that deck too doesn't it?"*
