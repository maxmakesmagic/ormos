Do you play *Magic* at an awesome local game store? It may soon have something extra for players like you!

![Serra Angel (Borderless Promo)](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/GYpCLTq62Q5G.png)

**Serra Angel (Borderless Promo)**

![Lord of Atlantis (Borderless Promo)](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/mitQfBMXYBZc.png)

**Lord of Atlantis (Borderless Promo)**

![Zombie Master (Borderless Promo)](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/sbuDD2bLhCgh.png)

**Zombie Master (Borderless Promo)**

![Goblin King (Borderless Promo)](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/wFQRDhSySYEU.png)

**Goblin King (Borderless Promo)**

![Gaea's Liege (Borderless Promo)](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/R2XMwYgN0VSN.png)

**Gaea's Liege (Borderless Promo)**

Coming later this year to [WPN Premium stores](https://web.archive.org/web/20231002000000/https://locator.wizards.com/), these promos will arrive quarterly and aren't tied to any specific event or promotion: stores can make these promos available however it best suits their needs. The schedule for when promos begin to arrive follows *Magic* color order:

*   Fourth Quarter 2023: Serra Angel
*   First Quarter 2024: Lord of Atlantis
*   Second Quarter 2024: Zombie Master
*   Third Quarter 2024: Goblin King
*   Fourth Quarter 2024: Gaea's Liege

Plus, there's an annual promo for WPN Premium stores we've already shown you:

![Cavern of Souls (Cosmium Neon Ink)](https://media.wizards.com/2023/lci/en_29f786bb6a.png)

**Cavern of Souls (Cosmium Neon Ink)**

Vibrant neon ink returns with *The Lost Caverns of Ixalan®* ([alongside new treatments, *Special Guests*, and more](/en/articles/announcements/2023/09-22-a-first-look-at-the-lost-caverns-of-ixalan)), and you'll only find the yellow cosmium neon ink color at WPN Premium stores. These promo versions of Cavern of Souls arrive throughout the next year, like the other promos above.

As always, check with [your local WPN Premium store](https://web.archive.org/web/20231002000000/https://locator.wizards.com/) to learn about their events and opportunities to collect these upcoming promos!
