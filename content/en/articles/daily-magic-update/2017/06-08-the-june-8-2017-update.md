# Deck of the Day

1.  [Benjamin Luft's Blue-Red Control](https://web.archive.org/web/20170814030300/http://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/gpams17/top-8-decklists-grand-prix-amsterdam-2017-06-04)

Luft came *thiiiiiiis* close to winning a Grand Prix last weekend with four [Torrential Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170608000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrential+Gearhulk)s in his deck, but was halted by Lukas Blohon's Black-Green Delirium deck. Still, it's an impressive performance for this deck that has been on the rise as of late.

There were a few spicy bits that might have contributed to Luft's success. One is the main deck [Sphinx of the Final Word](https://web.archive.org/web/20170608000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx+of+the+Final+Word), which gives the deck a leg up in other control matchups. The other is a single [Summary Dismissal](https://web.archive.org/web/20170608000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summary+Dismissal), which is, of course, excellent against [Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170608000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulamog%2C+the+Ceaseless+Hunger).

The rest of the deck is prototypical control deck stuff—cheap removal, counterspells, and card draw. Basically, all the good stuff.
