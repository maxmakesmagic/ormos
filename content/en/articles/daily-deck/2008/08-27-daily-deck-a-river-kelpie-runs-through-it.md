Looking to get some extra mileage from the cards in your graveyard? Conley Woods' 34th place deck from Grand Prix–Denver lets you do just that! He used retrace from Raven's Crime and reanimation spells like Profane Command, Makeshift Mannequin, and Puppeteer Clique to get multiple uses out of his spells. Plus, with one of his River Kelpies in play he even gets to draw a card for doing so!
