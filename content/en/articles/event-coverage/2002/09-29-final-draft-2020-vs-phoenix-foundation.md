![](https://i.ormos.uk/13/b2/13b215f39ad7a57e2e69ea43ac659fa6538208f222ad2d2b111b62a3665d4284.jpg)

I seem to remember this from last year: Kai Budde, Dirk Baberowski, and Marco Blume against a team of relative unknowns in the team finals. Last year, Kai's team won, but the losing Les Plus Class ended up becoming quite dominant on the PT the course of the following year. 2020, the surprise Canadian team of David Rood, Elijah Pollock, and Steve Wolfman would like to have it all – the PT win and the impressive follow-up year. They beat Courtney's Boys last round; can they put an end to Phoenix Foundation's run here?

Phoenix Foundation has a strategy, they stick to the strategy, and that's all there is to it. They've found that the most consistent and powerful decks come when you put white/red in seat C, blue/green in B, and black in A, and they almost never deviate. It's worked so far; they're 6-for-6 in drafts in this tournament.

2020 has a different plan; they like to move colors around to try and generate good matches. Phoenix Foundation feels that strategy is inherently flawed, and never reacts. Slay Pillage Gerard tried to make good matchups for themselves and still lost. Phoenix Foundation knows 2020 likes to react, and let them draft first, hoping to get them to switch and draft weaker decks.

Rood, in seat B, opened the first pack and took [Mystic Zealot](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystic+Zealot), showing a willingness to be blue/white. Pollock took [Rabid Elephant](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rabid+Elephant), setting up green/red or green/blue, and then the Germans took [Gravedigger](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravedigger) for Dirk, [Caustic Tar](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Caustic+Tar) for Kai (probably a D-draft), and [Mystic Visionary](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystic+Visionary) and [Rites of Initiation](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rites+of+Initiation) for Marco. Wolfman, in 2020's A seat, wheeled a [Whispering Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whispering+Shade) and an [Innocent Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Innocent+Blood).

Pollock took [Firebolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firebolt) in pack 2, showing green/red, and after that things fell into a pattern. Kai drafted green/blue in B and Marco red/white in C, right according to plan. Rood was blue/white in B and Pollock was red/green in C. Both A-set players were drafting black and trying to determine good choices for their second colors.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/58/a6/58a613157a11ab328691edc47cef6d3864ccc2fd36198da5504818f884912a55.jpg)

Phoenix Foundation looked to come out ahead by the end of *Odyssey*. Marco picked up a [Kamahl, Pit Fighter](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kamahl%2C+Pit+Fighter) and a [Divine Sacrament](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divine+Sacrament) which will be great because Pollock isn't white. Kai grabbed a [Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel) and lots of good aggressive cards, whereas his opponent Rood took card drawing and counters. Only Dirk looked to be lagging behind Wolfman, who had [Ghastly Demise](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghastly+Demise), [Afflict](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Afflict), and [Dirty Wererat](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dirty+Wererat) in his pile.

In *Torment*, Wolfman started off by taking [Chainer, Dementia Master](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chainer%2C+Dementia+Master) over [Soul Scourge](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soul+Scourge), a pick many people would disagree with. Then Phoenix Foundation went on a run of drafting great cards: [Breakthrough](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Breakthrough) and [Fiery Temper](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Temper) for Marco, [Arrogant Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrogant+Wurm), [Basking Rootwalla](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basking+Rootwalla), and [Narcissism](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narcissism) for Kai, and [Balshan Collaborator](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balshan+Collaborator), [Faceless Butcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faceless+Butcher), and [Crippling Fatigue](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crippling+Fatigue) for Dirk.

As the packs started coming back the other way, 2020 got their share as well: a [Pardic Collaborator](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pardic+Collaborator), a [Faceless Butcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faceless+Butcher), and a [Crippling Fatigue](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crippling+Fatigue) for Wolfman, [Teroh's Faithful](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teroh%27s+Faithful) and [Skywing Aven](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skywing+Aven) for Rood, and [Flash of Defiance](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flash+of+Defiance) and [Possessed Barbarian](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Possessed+Barbarian) for Pollock.

The interesting this was that Wolfman was stockpiling [Krosan Constrictor](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Krosan+Constrictor)s, and was probably going to be black/green.

Wolfman was in fact planning to draft green, and did so in *Judgment*, taking many [Phantom Tiger](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phantom+Tiger)s, which should be bad times for Dirk's red/black deck.

At one point Pollock had to D-draft a black card, and his teammates opted for him to take [Masked Gorgon](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Masked+Gorgon) instead of [Filth](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Filth), handing Dirk a [Filth](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Filth) for the second draft in a row.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/ptbos02/a755.jpg)

As the dust settled, Marco didn't get any decent damage removal from *Judgment*, making his deck very vulnerable to the one [Flash of Defiance](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flash+of+Defiance) in Pollock's deck. Pollock also got a seventh-pick [Worldgorger Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Worldgorger+Dragon) that could be trouble for Blume.

Budde has way too much offense for Rood to handle. Mongrel/Rootwalla/[Arrogant Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrogant+Wurm) is nutty, and while Rood's deck can certainly draw cards, there aren't many answers for him to draw into.

Dirk is once again the underdog; his decks don't come together well against enemy black/green decks. But he beat Sonne in the semis, so anything is possible.

The Canadians are sure they're going to win going in. They figure Wolfman is a lock and Pollock should win with [Flash of Defiance](https://web.archive.org/web/20020929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flash+of+Defiance).

Phoenix Foundation, however, are as cool as the proverbial cucumbers. Nothing worries them. They haven't lost a match yet, and if they win this one, they'll be 1-2-3 lifetime in number of Pro Tours won. I expect that to come true.

#### Steven Wolfman, Seat A
