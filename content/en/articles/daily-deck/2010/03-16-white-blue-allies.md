*Worldwake* Game Day was just two weekends ago, and we'll be highlighting decks from the event on Daily Decks! Featuring the Standard format, the event was a huge success. Alex Fann used a blue-white Allies strategy to make the Top 8 at the event hosted by Pat's Games in Austin, Texas. Hits included Clarion Ultimatum, Rite of Replication, and Marshal's Anthem to get back any ally that didn't manage to survive! For more Standard decks, check out [all the *Worldwake* Game Day deck lists](/en/events/2010/worldwakegameday/worldwake-game-day).
