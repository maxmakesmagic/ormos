The captain of the U.S. National Team, Josh Utter-Leyton had quite the World Championships for 2010. In the Extended portion of the event, Josh, who is nicknamed "Wrapter" for his **Magic Online** name, decided to run Five-Color Cruel Control. Named for its powerful Cruel Ultimatums and somewhat neurotic mana base, the deck has proved powerful in previous formats. Read more Worlds in the archive [here](/en/events/2010/worlds10/theres-no-jace-like-guillaume).
