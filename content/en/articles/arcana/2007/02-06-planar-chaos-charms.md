The cycle of [*Planar Chaos* charms](https://web.archive.org/web/20070206000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/gatherer/index.aspx?term=charm&Field_Name=on&setfilter=Planar%20Chaos) represent fragments of pure colored energy—refracted through the slightly twisted lens of *Planar Chaos*. Take a look at John Avon’s art of each charm (presented here in classic “WUBRG” order) as you contemplate each spell’s abilities. Which abilities seem in-flavor to you? Which make sense in the light of *Planar Chaos’s* expanded definitions of the “color pie”? How do the illustrations evoke the flavor of each color? 

### [Dawn Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20070206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dawn+Charm)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/de/6f/de6fec2e917e301dc867a97f69cb4ba0876f12e66760029584a7cc6d31d2580b.jpg)  
  
*[Dawn Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20070206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dawn+Charm) art by John Avon*  
  
 The design of [Dawn Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20070206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dawn+Charm) evokes the feel of an achievement medal. The charm encases raw white spell magic, and seems to hang in the air, radiating a holy glow.

### [Piracy Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20070206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Piracy+Charm)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9c/e9/9ce9d738301ab3c00085122148bd2947013188584ec943c7d73eb6025e1317c8.jpg)  
  
*Piracy Charm art by John Avon*  
  
[Piracy Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20070206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Piracy+Charm) is sleek, burnished metal swirling with mists of blue energy. The blue material under it evokes the cloth of a formal wizard’s gown.

### [Midnight Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20070206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Midnight+Charm)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b8/1a/b81a55136027aed75b16ec8d00ed3404f0598ad615082903e4376ede55cdd5e5.jpg)  
  
*[Midnight Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20070206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Midnight+Charm) art by John Avon*  
  
 Part of a bone necklace, [Midnight Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20070206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Midnight+Charm) is held here by the withered hands of a necromancer or other black-aligned mage. Note that he's missing part of a thumb, no doubt a symptom of past deals with sinister forces.

### [Fury Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20070206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fury+Charm)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/11/8f/118f74ae3458396fda4391c762f58a338c4667f68680e667604c49cb6e411b3b.jpg)  
  
*[Fury Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20070206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fury+Charm) art by John Avon*  
  
[Fury Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20070206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fury+Charm) depicts dynamic red magic inside a heavy, worn-metal bauble, resting inside weather-beaten hands. It evokes the feel of a worldly shaman keeping his trusty wild magics close at hand.

### [Evolution Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20070206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Evolution+Charm)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/da/1b/da1bb64a13472238dff3517a92579e182d6a19b5aa1c784c7337f7236c14fe61.jpg)  
  
*[Evolution Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20070206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Evolution+Charm) art by John Avon*  
  
 Gentle hands cradle [Evolution Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20070206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Evolution+Charm). Note the use of natural materials in this charm—feathers, leaves, and bone.
