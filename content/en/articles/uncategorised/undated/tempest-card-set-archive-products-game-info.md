### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to help make your deck better, whatever way you play.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/74/06/7406bdaa91c79ea7afa796632709a8b136991ad7f8ebff50dd7e2a7a4cbba23f.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. The 60-card deck includes a strategic mix of creatures and spells, land cards, and a strategy insert.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160707012321/http://magic.wizards.com/node/976771)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/74/06/7406bdaa91c79ea7afa796632709a8b136991ad7f8ebff50dd7e2a7a4cbba23f.png)

### TOURNAMENT PACKS

This 60-card deck includes a random mix of basic land cards, creature cards, and spell cards for sealed deck play.
