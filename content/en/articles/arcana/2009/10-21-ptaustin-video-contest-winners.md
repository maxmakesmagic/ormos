**PT–Austin Video Contest Winners!**

You might recall that a little over a week ago, the Arcana slot hosted an [announcement](/en/articles/arcana/2009/10-12-pro-touraustin-video-contest) about a video contest at Pro Tour–Austin. Well, the Pro Tour is over, the [coverage](https://web.archive.org/web/20091021000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/kibler-completes-comeback-austin-victory) is up, and Brian Kibler has walked away with his giant novelty check. What about the video contest? How good of you to ask!

Our crack team of judges selected three finalists from the pool of videos submitted at Austin. Here's **Magic** head designer and **magicthegathering.com** columnist Mark Rosewater—a former television writer, by the way—reviewing the top three videos:

**3rd Place:** [Never Give Up](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QPFapMhjGKM), by Marijn Lybaert  
**2nd Place:** [Adventures at the PTA](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11cl42RW5eo), by Cameron Davis  
**1st Place:** [Glimpses](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CTHYoXDF3zM), by Kasandra Bessey

Congratulations to Kasandra Bessey on winning the Pro Tour–Austin Video Contest and to all three entrants, who each won a Flip Mino camcorder!
