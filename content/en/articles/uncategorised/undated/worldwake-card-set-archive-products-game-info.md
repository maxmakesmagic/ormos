### FAT PACK

Includes player’s guide, card box with panoramic art, eight 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, special-edition life counter, and an excerpt from the novel In the *Teeth of Akoum*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7a/8a/7a8a06fb68c993bb57023e07dda61592f57fe29880d232affbd54b08cc625c82.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/df/23/df23bf4f936a60bae13a2fb76ed7f05c62330018a44a91c8e15a4f2ec4aba81c.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes a 41-card preconstructed deck, a rare foil card, a strategy insert, a learn-to-play insert, and one 15-card *Worldwake* booster pack.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/default/files/EN_GAMEINFO_PRODUCTS_SetIcon_T_WWK_131025_0.png)Worldwake features 145 black-bordered cards, including randomly inserted premium versions of all cards in the set.

**Set Name:** *Worldwake*  
**Block:** Set 2 of 3 of the *Zendikar* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 145

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

**Official Three-Letter Code:** WWK  
**Twitter Hashtag:** #MTGWWK

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718095412/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/EN_MTGWWKFAQ_20100114.rtf)

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Kenneth Nagle (lead), Kelly Digges, Mark Globus, Matt Place, and Mark Rosewater

**Final Game Design and Development:** Mike Turian (lead), Tom LaPille, Scott Johns, Erik Lauer, and Mons Johnson, with contributions from Mark L. Gottlieb
