Normally at this point, we'd show you some previous 3/4 Artifact Creature that Soliton references. And today is no exception! It turns out that Soliton is a descendant of a card from *Darksteel*. In the distant past, Soliton was part of something that had four arms and four legs. But when it split in two, half of it got lost and half of it became Soliton. Click here to see what it used to be!
