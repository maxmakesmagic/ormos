**Is there any color comination you will not draft in *Theros*?**

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/ochoa_qh.jpg)

David Ochoa, Silent Arbiter — *“Black-Green and Green-Red, they're both too clunky.”* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/hayne_qh.jpg)

Alexander Hayne, Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* Champion — *“I think they've all got potential. If your combination is open, you're going to get a good deck.”* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/parke_qh.jpg)

Jamie Parke, [The Man in This Photo](https://i.ormos.uk/03/3b/033baf9b4dfb682ed6b45113b5c579a353edfdfd1177f8980c9d3da7fe21d724.jpg) — *“I hesitate to say 'Never' but I dislike red-blue.”* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/wolfman_qh.jpg)

Steve Wolfman, Semifinalist Pro Tour Philadelphia — *“Black-Red. I just never win with it. I just first picked [Underworld Cerberus](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Cerberus) and it ruined my whole draft. I should've taken [Hopeful Eidolon](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hopeful+Eidolon).”* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/turtenwald_qh.jpg)

Owen Turtenwald, Dreamcrusher — *“Blue-Red is SO BAD. All its cards are underpowered and inefficient. Giving the 0/3 flyer trample? THAT was ambitious.”* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/jensen_qh.jpg)

William Jensen, Master — *“I don't like to play white or red. They're overdrafted and high-variance.”*
