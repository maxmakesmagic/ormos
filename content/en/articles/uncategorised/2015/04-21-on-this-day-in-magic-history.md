On this day exactly eight years ago, we saw greatness achieve its peak.

Though I have a habit of overstating how awesome something is, I assure you that, if anything, I'm underselling how great a day in *Magic* history April 21, 2007 was.

You see, April 21, 2007, was the day Guillaume Wafo-Tapa made the Top 8 of [Pro Tour Yokohama](https://web.archive.org/web/20150421000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Events.aspx?x=mtgevent/ptyok07/welcome) with the Wafo-Tapaiest of Wafo-Tapa decks—Mystical Teachings Control.
