![](https://i.ormos.uk/4f/5c/4f5ccf556e03be2dcea1957d7efa4fcb81009a8749b49ad391b6934fc0ac7b51.jpg)Planned for an on-sale date of September 10th, 2007, *Masters Edition* is a 195 card, **Magic Online** only set of some of the greatest cards from **Magic's** past. All cards in the set have appeared in pre-*Mirage***Magic** sets and will appear with their original artwork and old-style frames.

Offered as a booster-only product, *Masters Edition* will be on sale for a limited time and then removed from our product offering. At least one month of notice with announcements here on the website and in forums will precede removing *Masters Edition* from the **Magic Online** store. Leagues, Premier Events and draft queues will begin offering *Masters Edition* product in events when release events begin on September 14th. 

There will be a special *Masters Edition*-related Oracle update shortly before the product goes on sale.

Booster packs will be sold for $3.99 each and available in the **Magic Online** store starting September 10th.

Check [www.magicthegathering.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20080518032212/http://www.wizards.com/magic/welcome.asp?) for card previews starting Monday, July 23rd. 

*Masters Edition* cards will be legal in the Classic format and any format that uses Classic to define card legality.

Breakdowns for rarities include 60/60/60 C/U/R, and the 15 basic lands from Alpha/Beta, with standard 15 card boosters. Collation will be 1(basic land) 10c 3u 1r per pack, similar to *Tenth Edition* collation.

Premium cards will be inserted at roughly the same rate as they are in *Tenth Edition* as well, at a 1:56 ratio.

This is an online only non-redeemable product.
