After fifteen rounds of Standard, with a total of 1,238 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

  
**![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpwar17/t8_vuillot.jpg)Name:** Samuel Vuillot

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Web developer

**Hometown:** Tulle, France

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Champion Grand Prix Utrecht 2017, Top 48 Pro Tour *Aether Revolt*, Top 24 Pro Tour *Ixalan*.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Four-Color Temur, really strong deck.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[The Scarab God](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Scarab+God), that card just wins them all!

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Confiscation Coup](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Confiscation+Coup). I'm beating you with your own cards!

* * *

  
**![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpwar17/t8_bachaud.jpg)Name:** Louis Bachaud

**Age:** 21

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Lyon, France

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Won packs at Rhich Hagon's Trivia Game Show. Also, Bronze pro.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Sultai Energy. No time to test, and this was closest to what I was used to playing.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Blossoming Defense](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blossoming+Defense). The deck is all about protecting key threats.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Vraska's Contempt](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vraska%27s+Contempt). Came in in every single matchup.

* * *

  
**![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpwar17/t8_depraz.jpg)Name:** Jean-Emmanuel Depraz

**Age:** 22

**Occupation:** This year ... *Magic*

**Hometown:** Paris, France

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Two Grand Prix Top 16 finishes and two Pro Tours played.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Four-Color Energy. I'm a midrange guy.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer). It puts the opponent off-balance and it less expected now that most lists only have two.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

No card really stood out, it's more like a package of decent cards. I guess maybe [Negate](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Negate), in the matchup against [God-Pharaoh's Gift](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=God-Pharaoh%27s+Gift).

* * *

  
**![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpwar17/t8_luminati.jpg)Name:** Valerio Luminati

**Age:** 21

**Occupation:** Employee

**Hometown:** Rovereto, Italy

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Two Grand Prix Top 64 finishes and some mediocre results.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Temur Energy. Less clunky than Four-Color Energy and the sideboard is great. Best deck in Standard.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

Promo version of [Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer). You always draw it at the right moment.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Negate](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Negate), allows you to win against anti-Temur decks.

* * *

  
**![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpwar17/t8_privalov.jpg)Name:** Alexander Privalov

**Age:** 29

**Occupation:** IT manager

**Hometown:** Moscow, Russia

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

2008 national champion, many Pro Tour starts, but without any good finishes.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Mardu Vehicles, also known as "Mashinki!" I played this deck for a long time, since last season.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Heart of Kiran](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heart+of+Kiran) on turn two, can easily make a game.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Dusk // Dawn](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dusk+%2F%2F+Dawn) leads to interesting plays and can completely turn a game.

* * *

  
**![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpwar17/t8_szmyd.jpg)Name:** Jedrek Szmyd

**Age:** 23

**Occupation:** Student/working at my local game store

**Hometown:** Poznan, Poland

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

A few 12-3 finishes at GPs.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

White-Blue [God-Pharaoh's Gift](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=God-Pharaoh%27s+Gift), because it has three of the best features a *Magic* deck can have—blue card selection, lots of value, and a good matchup versus "kanister" \[otherwise known as Piotr Głogowski\].

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Hostile Desert](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hostile+Desert) and [Angel of Sanctions](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel+of+Sanctions). Both hit hard and are great versus energy decks.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

The dream team of [Angel of Sanctions](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel+of+Sanctions) and [Fairgrounds Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fairgrounds+Warden), once again great versus energy.

* * *

  
**![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpwar17/t8_kowalski.jpg)Name:** Grzegorz Kowalski

**Age:** 26

**Occupation:** Wannabe *Magic* pro

**Hometown:** Wroclaw, Poland

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Three Grand Prix Top 8s, a few Pro Tour Top 16s.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Four-Color Energy. The black cards are powerful enough to make the splash worthwile. The matchup against Temur Energy is great.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Skysovereign, Consul Flagship](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skysovereign%2C+Consul+Flagship). It's just a better version of [Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer). I regret that I didn't realize this on time before Pro Tour *Ixalan*.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Cartouche of Ambition](https://web.archive.org/web/20171112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cartouche+of+Ambition). Never played this card before, but my teammates told me to on Friday evening. I trusted them without any testing and it won three matches today. Thanks guys!
