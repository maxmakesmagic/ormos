It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20110602000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=events/magic/fnm)! Since we're now in June, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

![Rhox War Monk](https://i.ormos.uk/68/9b/689b14f30ff0ed21dc8949b23756676ac01ea62ec5d2d267d8ad7f64ee3bd025.jpg)  

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards feature the FNM logo and many have original flavor text and updated wordings.
