Instructions for using the **[Deck Entry Form](https://web.archive.org/web/20120309233318/http://www.wizards.com/magic/deckentry/deckentry.asp)** for Pro Tour Qualifiers:

1\. Enter Player's Name.

2\. Enter Tournament Organizer's name.

3\. Enter Tournament Organizer's email.

4\. Select Qualifier from Location dropdown box.

5\. Select Place from dropdown box.

6\. Enter Top 8 Modern decklist by typing the card names into the Decklist field. Cards need to be typed in the following format: (number of card) (card name). Cards may be left off if you do not include the quantity. Please be sure you are submitting the draft deck list from the Top 8 and not the sealed deck list used in the Swiss rounds.

For the sideboard, add a row that says Sideboard: and then every card listed after that will be automatically put into the deck's sideboard. The deckbuilder will also insert an asterisk at the end of each sideboard line as a reminder. 

The cards do not need to be in any order. The deckbuilder automatically sorts them among Land, Creatures and Spells.

A sample decklist, as it would appear in the Decklist field:

> <table>&#10;    <tbody><tr>&#10;      <td>&#10;        <p>1 Island <br> 7 Swamp <br> 14 Forest <br> 4 Birds of Paradise <br> 4 Sakura-Tribe Elder <br> 4 Eternal Witness <br> 4 Viridian Shaman <br> 3 Molder Slug <br> 1 Lose Hope <br> 2 Eerie Procession <br> 4 Night's Whisper <br> 4 Echoing Decay <br> 4 Rend Flesh <br> 4 Cranial Extraction <br> Sideboard: <br> 1 Eerie Procession* <br> 1 Lose Hope* <br> 2 Plow Under* <br> 2 Hana Kami* <br> 2 Barter in Blood* <br> 2 Hideous Laughter* <br> 2 March of the Machines* <br> 3 Naturalize* </p>&#10;      </td>&#10;    </tr>&#10;  </tbody></table>

7\. After completing the decklist, hit the "Update & Preview Deck" button. This will automatically check the card names and display the deck in the center of the page. When a deck of at least 40 legal cards (Limited) or 60 legal cards (Constructed) has been updated and previewed, a button will appear at the bottom of the page to email the deck to Wizards of the Coast.

Note: The card name must be typed exactly as it shows on the card, in regard to punctuation. For example, if you typed in "Kiki-Jiki Mirror Breaker" the deckbuilder would not recognize the card. It would need to be Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker. The tool is not case-sensitive. 

If a card is not recognized, it will show up like so: // ? 1 Kiki-Jiki Mirror Breaker. When fixing the name, be sure to remove the // ? before updating the deck. If a card in the sideboard is not recognized, you must re-type the card name after the "Sideboard:" line for it to show up in the sideboard section.

If you need to check official card names, consult our card database Gatherer at [http://gatherer.wizards.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20080102000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Default.aspx).
