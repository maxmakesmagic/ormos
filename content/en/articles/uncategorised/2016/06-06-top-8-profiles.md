After many rounds of Swiss and some great games of *Magic*, the Top 8 for Grand Prix Costa Rica is set! Learn more about this event’s Top 8 competitors, their history at the top tables, and more by taking a look through the Top 8 profiles.  
  

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpcos16/t8_lopez.jpg)Name/Nombre:** Erick Manuel Lopez Basulto

**Age/Edad:** 23

**Occupation/Ocupación**: Merchant

**Hometown/Residencia:** Merida, Yucatan, México

**Previous Magic accomplishments/Logros Previos en Magic:**  
 Winning a PTQ, unfortunately I could not attend because my Visa was denied.

  
**What deck did you play, and why?/Que deck jugaste y porque?**  
 Green-White Tokens because I think it’s the best Standard deck at the moment.

  
**What changes would you make to your deck, and why?/Que cambios harias a tu deck, y porque?**  
[Angelic Purge](https://web.archive.org/web/20160606000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angelic+Purge) to sideboard for any other removal, like [Declaration in Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20160606000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Declaration+in+Stone) or [Silkwrap](https://web.archive.org/web/20160606000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silkwrap).

  
**What's the most important card in your deck and sideboard?/Cual es la carta mas importante en tu deck, y sideboard?**  
 In main deck, without a doubt, [Archangel Avacyn](https://web.archive.org/web/20160606000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archangel+Avacyn), and then [Chandra, Flamecaller](https://web.archive.org/web/20160606000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Flamecaller). In the sideboard, [Tragic Arrogance](https://web.archive.org/web/20160606000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tragic+Arrogance) and [Planar Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20160606000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Planar+Outburst). Gave me a lot of games.

**Are you in Costa Rica for business? Pleasure? Or is business pleasure?/Estas en Costa Rica por trabajo? Placer? O trabjando por placer?**  
 I don’t know if playing Magic is considered a job, for me it’s more like a sport, so then I would say pleasure.

*\[Thanks to translation by Scorekeeper McCoy\]*

**Age/Edad:** 29

**Occupation/Ocupación:** Magic Grinder

**Hometown/Residencia:** Barcelona, Spain

**Previous Magic accomplishments/Logros Previos en Magic:**

**What changes would you make to your deck, and why?/Que cambios harias a tu deck, y porque?**  
 Would change [Tragic Arrogance](https://web.archive.org/web/20160606000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tragic+Arrogance) for [Lambholt Pacifist](https://web.archive.org/web/20160606000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lambholt+Pacifist) — I love that card!

  
**What's the most important card in your deck and sideboard?/Cual es la carta mas importante en tu deck, y sideboard?**  
[Archangel Avacyn](https://web.archive.org/web/20160606000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archangel+Avacyn). She’s your defender and your finisher in one card. And the Pacifist lets you play Aggro more.

  
**Are you in Costa Rica for business? Pleasure? Or is business pleasure?/Estas en Costa Rica por trabajo? Placer? O trabjando por placer?**  
 Pleasure. The business is casting Gideon!
