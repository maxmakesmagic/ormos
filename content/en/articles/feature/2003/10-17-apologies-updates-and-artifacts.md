### Blocking Users, Pt. II -- An Apology

Well, there was some quick reaction to my piece last Friday about blocking a user. I figured there were going to be some strong feelings about what I did (or didn't) do, and y'all didn't prove me wrong.

However, I owe an apology to both parties.

I've learned that the person at the receiving end of the comment wasn't actually offended by it. In the meantime, the "offender" has disappeared from the game. In my attempt to start a community discussion about how we feel about online behavior, I seem to have offended two regular members.

Since I brought it up in my weekly editorial, I'm making my apology here, too. I'm sorry that I used that situation as an example -- I obviously misinterpreted how serious it was. Since I was the person actually upset by the comment, I should've taken my own advice -- block or leave and keep my big mouth shut.

My apologies to both of you.

While I could write up the lessons learned from this incident, I'd rather avoid inserting my other foot in my mouth. I'll trust you to draw your own conclusions.

### Downtime Posting?

In the continuing saga of my screwups, I forgot to modify the date on the downtime last week. Unfortunately, as things are set up currently, I can't set up the date change automatically at a time that makes sense. So, I do a manual date change each week after the update finishes.

Seems I forgot about that last week.

As you can see, it's now changed to show the downtime that'll occur next week. In general, expect a downtime every Thursday morning. While this Thursday's update only took a quick bounce, the scheduled four hours will be closer to normal.

### Bits And Pieces

There have been a couple other quick fixes thrown in over the past week, as well.

First, if you haven't already discovered this for yourself, you can join more than one queue at a time. I know this has been a thorn in many players' sides, so I'm glad we've fixed it. Yes, believe it or not, this wasn't some kind of evil plan -- it was a bug.

Second, you can play Entomb in casual formats based on the Extended card pool. Due to the way formats work in ***Magic** Online,* formats like Prismatic and Singleton are subsets of the Online Extended format. So, when Entomb got banned in the tournament format it went across to the casual formats as well.
