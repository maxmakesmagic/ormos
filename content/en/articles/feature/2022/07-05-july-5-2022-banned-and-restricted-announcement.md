**Announcement Date**: July 5, 2022

**Alchemy**:

[Grinning Ignus](https://web.archive.org/web/20220705000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grinning+Ignus) is banned.

**Effective Date**: July 7, 2022

The list of all banned and restricted cards, by format, is [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20220705000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/game-info/gameplay/rules-and-formats/banned-restricted).

**Alchemy Changes**

For an overview of the latest Alchemy changes, including the ban of [Grinning Ignus](https://web.archive.org/web/20220705000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grinning+Ignus), [check out Donald Smith's breakdown](https://web.archive.org/web/20220705151831/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/magic-digital/alchemy-rebalancing-july-07-2022) of the July 7 updates.
