Grand Prix–Bochum was held this past weekend and the Czech Republic's Martin Juza took the top slot at the tournament. The format was *Scars of Mirrodin* Draft, and Martin was all too happy to draft the poison strategy. It served him well, allowing him to claim the title of champion! Check out full coverage of the event [here](/en/events/2010/gpboch10/juzas-safe-pair-of-hands).
