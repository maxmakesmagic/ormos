Learn more about the Top 8 competitors of Pro Tour *Battle for Zendikar* by reading their player profiles below.

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptbfz/t8_turtenwald.jpg)Owen Turtenwald

**Age:** 26 

**Hometown:** Milwaukee, WI 

**Limited Record:** 5-1 

**Constructed Record:** 8-1-1 

 **Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** Two Pro Tour Top 8 appearances, 2011 Player of the Year. 

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?** Dark Jeskai. It was designed by Ben Rubin and the Pantheon. I tried everything I could to build a deck that beats it and I couldn't, so I had no choice but to play it. 

 **What *Battle for Zendikar* card impressed you the most this weekend?** 

[Radiant Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Radiant+Flames).

**Who did you prepare for the tournament with?** 

ChannelFireball, the Pantheon.

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptbfz/t8_chin2.jpg)Ricky Chin

**Age:** 27 

**Hometown:** Drummondville, Quebec Canada 

**Occupation:** Financial Advisor 

**Limited Record:** 4-2 

**Constructed Record:** 8-0-2 

 **Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 

Top 24 at Grand Prix Montreal 2015

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?** 

Green-White Megamorph. This is the most consistent deck out there. Every card in the deck can be good on its own. Also, I've practiced a lot with this deck and I know the matchups.

 **What *Battle for Zendikar* card impressed you the most this weekend?** 

[Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar).

**Who did you prepare for the tournament with?** 

A friend in my hometown and I practiced drafting on *Magic Online*. 

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?** 

It would be the best thing that's ever happened in my *Magic* career. All the time and hard work that I put in would pay off. 

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptbfz/t8_damodarosa.jpg)Paulo Vitor Damo Da Rosa

**Age:** 28 

**Hometown:** Porto Alegre, Brazil 

**Occupation:** *Magic* player and writer for ChannelFireball.com 

**Limited Record:** 6-0 

**Constructed Record:** 6-3-1 

 **Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** Ten Pro Tour Top 8s! 

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?** 

Atarka Red. It's so much faster than anything else, and the format is kind of clunky with people playing four colors.

 **What *Battle for Zendikar* card impressed you the most this weekend?** 

[Radiant Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Radiant+Flames), I guess. I'm not playing many *Battle for Zendikar* cards myself, but that one was good every time someone cast it versus me. 

**Who did you prepare for the tournament with?** 

The [Face to Face](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Face+to+Face) guys: Alexander Hayne, Jon Stern, Nathan Holiday, Josh McClain, Shuhei Nakamura, Sam Pardee, Mike Sigrist, Neal Oliver, and Tyler Bloom.

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?** 

It'd show me I can still compete at the highest level.

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptbfz/t8_muller.jpg)Martin Müller

**Age:** 18 

**Hometown:** Jyllinge, Denmark 

**Occupation:** Student 

**Limited Record:** 4-2 

**Constructed Record:** 8-1-1 

 **Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 2014 World *Magic* Cup winner; Top 8 at Grand Prix Krakow; 6th at 2015 World Championship. 

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?** Jeskai Tokens, because Martin Dang and Matej Zatlkaj told me to. 

 **What *Battle for Zendikar* card impressed you the most this weekend?** 

[Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar).

**Who did you prepare for the tournament with?** Team EUreka. 

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?** It would just be life-changing. 

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptbfz/t8_takimura.jpg)Kazuyuki Takimura

**Age:** 31 

**Hometown:** Tokyo, Japan 

**Occupation:** Office Worker 

**Limited Record:** 4-2 

**Constructed Record:** 8-1-1 

 **Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** Grand Prix Taipei Top 8 competitor 

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?** Abzan. It's the highest win percentage, and it is a steady deck. I thought it was a little bit ahead of the other decks. 

 **What *Battle for Zendikar* card impressed you the most this weekend?** 

[Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar).

**Who did you prepare for the tournament with?** Yuuki Ichikawa, Yuuya Watanabe, and Yuki Matsumoto. 

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?** I am getting married at the end of this year. I always told her that I would make it to a Top 8 someday, and I'm glad I could give this win to my future wife. 

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptbfz/t8_finkel.jpg)Jon Finkel

**Age:** 37 

**Hometown:** Fanwood, New Jersey 

**Occupation:** Hedge Fund Manager 

**Limited Record:** 4-2 

**Constructed Record:** 8-1-1 

 **Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 

Heh.

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?** 

Jeskai Black, because the battle lands from *Battle for Zendikar* shored up its greatest weakness, big ground creatures. It let's the deck play [Crackling Doom](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crackling+Doom) and Tasigur. 

 **What *Battle for Zendikar* card impressed you the most this weekend?** 

Battle lands.

**Who did you prepare for the tournament with?** 

The Pantheon.

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?** 

I'd have more than half the Pro Tour wins Kai Budde has.
