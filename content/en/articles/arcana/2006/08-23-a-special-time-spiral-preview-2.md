![](https://i.ormos.uk/14/5d/145d425e59dc756495e869591e98dfe5fb6589003fb359673bee789ab8cd2e03.jpg)*Time Spiral* releases in stores October 6, 2006, and in ***Magic** Online* on October 30.

Tune in to **magicthegathering.com** on Monday, August 28 for the premiere of the *Time Spiral* Orb of Insight. Two days later, on Wednesday, August 30, the *Time Spiral* minisite launches with exclusive previews. Card previews begin Monday, September 4.

Get your first *Time Spiral* packs at [Worldwide Prerelease Events](https://web.archive.org/web/20060823000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prereleases) starting September 23, 2006.
