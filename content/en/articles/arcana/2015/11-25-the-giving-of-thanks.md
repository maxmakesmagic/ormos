Here in the United States, tomorrow is Thanksgiving, and with Thanksgiving comes turkey, family, friends, football, and awkward conversations with your uncle about what you do for a living that invariably involve the phrase "no, not like magic tricks, it's a game."

It means all of those things for those of us here at DailyMTG, which is why we'll be taking the next two days off to eat, relax, and, yes, play some *Magic*.

So from everyone on the DailyMTG team here at Wizards of the Coast, happy Thanksgiving!
