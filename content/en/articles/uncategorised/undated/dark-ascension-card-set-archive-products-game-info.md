## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/18/78/187815f3ab8552f2b30f0d7a9409d405b33760734dedf90c47cbddcc81d52285.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set as well as two deck boxes, nine 15-card booster packs, 70 basic land cards, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4d/8e/4d8e150671918938d197b06a913930532350ef4f1c5429c114ac587f46701d40.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f1/71/f171e437cbc2e79be2c6d5326ab744014eb4a756c10094b4d1abc991f0e23266.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 cards, a foil premium rare card, one sealed 15-card booster pack, a strategy insert, and a **Magic** learn-to-play guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719043916/http://magic.wizards.com/node/283786)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/43/cb/43cb0a9487cc6627d09840e39a7cc13a579bc5681cdcb394088356b3cd16c7ac.png)

### EVENT DECKS

Battle like the best, right out of the box. Includes a tournament-ready 60-card deck plus a 15-card “sideboard” to swap in as needed, depending on what you expect your opponent will play.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719043916/http://magic.wizards.com/node/283791)

**Set Name:** *Dark Ascension*  
**Block:** Set 2 of 3 in the *Innistrad* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 158

**Languages:** English, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish
