**Flashback Favorites: Triple *Odyssey* begins tomorrow, August 9**

Tomorrow Flashback Favorites: Triple *Odyssey* begins! You will be able to enter this event at 10 a.m. PT on August 9 and the event will run through August 16. Please note that the following cards will not be in the booster collation: [Charmed Pendant](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Charmed+Pendant), [Holistic Wisdom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Holistic+Wisdom), and [Painbringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Painbringer).  
  
 More details about Flashback Favorites can be found [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/08-02-flashback-favorites-and-throwback-standard-gauntlets).

**The August MOPR Promo period is underway**

The August MOPR period runs from July 26 to August 30. On August 31, the [MOPR changes](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/07-31-magic-online-player-rewards-changes) announced last week will be implemented. Here is this month’s promo card:  
  

Event and Store Promo

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/images/hero/Fatal_Push_Promo.png)

[Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push)

  
 Yes, thats right! [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push) is the August promo for BOTH the Event and Store!  
  
 Play in one or more events during the August MOPR period and you'll get a non-premium [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push). Play in five or more and you'll get an additional premium [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push). Spend at least $20 total in the *Magic Online* Store during this MOPR period and you'll get a non-premium [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push). By spending $40 or more, you'll get an additional premium [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push).

**Standard PTQ for Pro Tour *Ixalan* Saturday, August 12**

Attention all Pro Tour hopefuls: A Pro Tour Qualifier (PTQ) is approaching! We hope to see you in the PTQ Finals on Saturday, August 12 at 9 a.m. PT. There is no need to qualify through a Preliminary for Constructed, simply join this event with 30 Event Tickets, 300 Play Points or a spare Blue Constructed PTQ Finals Entry Token!

Learn more about *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers).

***Magic Online* Format Challenges back this weekend**

The *Magic Online* Format Challenges introduced a new prize structure and recurring weekend events for Modern, Vintage, 1v1 Commander, Legacy, and Pauper formats!

Events begin every Saturday at 8:00 a.m. PT, and run through Sunday. Check out [this page](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-format-challenges) for further event details as well as the weekend schedule.

**Bug Blog updates**

Store | [Gideon, Martial Paragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Martial+Paragon) *Amonkhet* Planeswalker deck has 1 [Gideon's Intervention](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%27s+Intervention) instead of 1 [Glory-Bound Initiate](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glory-Bound+Initiate)

Vintage | With [Solemnity](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Solemnity) in play, you are still prompted to pay one mana for cards with Cumulative Upkeep that have zero Age counters.

Collection | A warning message is missing when adding cards that are in a deck in a League to a new Trade Binder.

Standard | [Diregraf Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Diregraf+Colossus) will in rare cases reset the game.

Duel Scene | If a creature is blocked by two creatures with combined toughness equal to the blocked creature’s power, you are unable to divide combat damage.

Standard | [Sunscourge Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sunscourge+Champion) will cause you to lose life if it has negative power when its trigger resolves.

Limited | If [Trueheart Duelist](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trueheart+Duelist) blocks multiple attackers, as well as another creature as part of a group, damage assignment errors can arise.

Multiplayer | If a commander is chosen for Riches in [Rags // Riches](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rags+%2F%2F+Riches), no other opponents need to choose a creature.

General | *Eventide* Mimics trigger even when a card of only one of its colors is cast.

Standard | [Vizier of Many Faces](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vizier+of+Many+Faces) keeps its abilities when an opponent controls [Overwhelming Splendor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170808000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overwhelming+Splendor).
