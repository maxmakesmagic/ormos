Welcome to Limited Information. Each week, I will post a poll based on the choices available in a draft situation. Sometimes, it will be the first pick of the draft. Other times, it will be an interesting moment during the draft or deckbuilding stage. Vote for the card you would take and feel free to discuss it on the [message boards](https://web.archive.org/web/20030306000000/http://boards.wizards.com/tcg-bin/ultimatebb.cgi?ubb=get_topic;f=55;t=000204). 

The week after each poll, I will post the results as well as some opinions and justification from Sideboard writers. I will then post a new draft situation and repeat the process. Sometimes, the choice may be easy; sometimes, it may be quite the puzzler.
