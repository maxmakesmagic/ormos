Looking for something heavy to play at your next Pro Tour Qualifier? Scott Bielick used Primalcrux to power his way into the Top 8, with no less than Oversoul of Dusk, Spellbreaker Behemoth, and Cloudthresher along for the ride! The cards combined quite well with the Phosphorescent Feasts in his sideboard, any red mage's worst nightmare!
