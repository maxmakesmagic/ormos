### Everyone Gets Something

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c5/c3/c5c3fe8100e476fe9bd771cf6a94805f63a5bbb46fe7a77e944ff49f2eee29f8.jpg)Like physical **Magic** Prerelease Tournaments, every player who joins one of the ***Magic** Online Eighth Edition* Release Tournaments gets something.

Following the tradition begun at the online *Legions* Release Tournaments, each player will get an Elvish Champion tradeable avatar. As a digital object, it will show in your collection and you can trade it to other players. While an avatar digital object is in your collection, you can use it to represent you while playing ***Magic** Online.*

You get one copy for each of the Release Tournaments you play in, but you have to play through at least one round. If you drop before the tournament starts, you won't get an avatar for it.

After the *Eighth Edition* Release Tournaments are over, you won't be able to get an Elvish Champion avatar unless you trade someone for it.

### How Many Release Tournaments Will There Be?

The *Eighth Edition* Release Tournaments will all run off the same queue. With the queue opening at 12:01 AM on August 1, here's how it works:

Each tournament requires 24 players to launch. As long as the 24-player minimum isn't hit, the tournament continues waiting for other players to join. Once 24 players have joined, the system waits to let other players register. As long as players keep registering, the tournament waits to launch. If players stop registering, the tournament will launch after a short wait with the number of players already joined.

Once 48 players join a tournament, the system launches that tournament immediately and spawns a new one.

The last Release Tournament will launch on August 2 at 11:59 PM PDT. After that, the Release Tournaments still running will finish and the rest will be history.

### Side Events

Also starting up at 12:01 AM on August 1 will be a new draft queue.

The current *Seventh Edition* draft queue will be replaced by an *Eighth Edition* draft queue. Unlike other single-set drafts that run only during the Release Events, this will be a permanent queue on ***Magic** Online.*

The draft queue will be standard cost: 2 Event Tickets plus product used.

### *Eighth Edition* Changes The Online World

Here are the dates to remember:  
*August 3, 12:01 AM PDT*  
 New all-*Eighth Edition* leagues will be available.

*September 1, 12 midnight PDT*  
 Standard and Extended Constructed events in the **Premier Events** room will include the *Eighth Edition* core set.

As soon as the *Eighth Edition* core set becomes available on ***Magic** Online,* you can play both Constructed and Limited events using *Eighth Edition* cards in any of the **Casual Play** rooms.

### Summary

So, join the rest of the ***Magic** Online* community in round-the-clock *Eighth Edition* Release Events from August 1 at 12:01 AM PDT through August 2 at 11:59 PM PDT. Play in the Release Tournaments and get the limited-release Elvish Champion avatar. Start competing in the new triple-*Eighth Edition* draft queues and see how the new core set changes the way you draft.

But most of all, have fun!
