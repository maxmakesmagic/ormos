Way back at the beginning of the summer we showed you that [token cards](/en/articles/arcana/2007/06-18-tenth-editions-tips-tricks-tokens) would be in *Tenth Edition*. Today we take a look at the art created for of each of these tokens, and the *Tenth* cards that would generate them. If it turns out that there are token-generating cards in *Lorwyn* (and there just might be), you'll find all-new token cards in packs of *Lorwyn*.

![Dragon](https://i.ormos.uk/f0/d7/f0d7761abc04d02de50c38033136a84bbd562f4f64b798324434c387dfd57151.jpg)  
*Dragon token art by Jim Pavelec – For use with [Dragon Roost](https://web.archive.org/web/20070913000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragon+Roost)*

![Goblin](https://i.ormos.uk/44/8c/448c766165a6bbe1643adf0e6c32c79e9649045fc67c196b13139c59a923b534.jpg)  
*Goblin token art by Dave Kendall – For use with [Siege-Gang Commander](https://web.archive.org/web/20070913000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege-Gang+Commander)*

![Saproling](https://i.ormos.uk/b3/72/b3727db4d8b0618b0b26bf1e7c85f5d3740e8ad17fb7d0b8909a9ffaeab29678.jpg)  
*Saproling token art by Cyril Van Der Haegen – For use with [Verdant Force](https://web.archive.org/web/20070913000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Verdant+Force)*

![Soldier](https://i.ormos.uk/87/a0/87a09fb79e597df1cc7c5d85c555dbf7a2887e842698ab58fd14d03ed5dd93fe.jpg)  
*Soldier token art by Parente – For use with [Mobilization](https://web.archive.org/web/20070913000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mobilization)*

![Wasp](https://i.ormos.uk/62/71/62713dca5ef5ffb4450b6779f8f9fa325769c9d7a211eb73db0f90007761b656.jpg)
