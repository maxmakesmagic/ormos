In our PAX East panel one of the things we announced was the designer cards that will appear in ***Magic** 2015*. We did it with the help of our friend George Fan, a game designer behind *Plants vs. Zombies*!

  

Here's a better look at his card:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b3/de/b3dee789d93dcfed952880a246cec9e28d7edf6290fc8f81b2cc9058b3399fb9.png) 

  

As you can see, we are crediting each of these designers on the ***Magic** 2015* printing of their card right in the text box!

Who else helped us? Here are a few in their own words on Twitter:

> It's official: I designed a Magic card that will be in the M15 set this summer! (I can't show it until June though.) [http://t.co/unKzTY4YfX](http://t.co/unkzty4yfx)
> 
> &mdash; Stone Librande (@StoneLibrande) [April 13, 2014](https://twitter.com/stonelibrande/statuses/455155342016409600)

Stone Librande is a lead designer at Riot Games, designer of *Diablo 3*, creative director of *SimCity*

> OH! And this is out now too. Uh. I designed a Magic card in M15. For real. COMMENCE FREAK OUT [http://t.co/tya4p6t2d3](http://t.co/tya4p6t2d3)
> 
> &mdash; Mikey Neumann (@mikeyface) [April 13, 2014](https://twitter.com/mikeyface/statuses/455174990389071872)

Mike Neumann works at Gearbox Software and worked on *Borderlands*

> its a HUGE honor to announce that i got to design a MTG card in the M15 set along side George Fan, [@notch](https://twitter.com/notch), Brian Fargo, Garriot &amp; many more
> 
> &mdash; Edmund McMillen (@EdmundMcMillenn) [April 12, 2014](https://twitter.com/edmundmcmillenn/statuses/455094048563290112)

Edmund McMillen was the Indie developer behind *Super Meat Boy*.

> You guys I got to design a Magic card with [@shawnmain](https://twitter.com/shawnmain) from Wizards of the Coast! It'll be out in this summer's M15 core set! :D! [#bucketlist](https://twitter.com/search?q=%23bucketlist&amp;src=hash)
> 
> &mdash; Brad Muir (@MrMooEar) [April 12, 2014](https://twitter.com/mrmooear/statuses/455043580277428224)

And Brad Muir works at Double Fine Productions on games like *Massive Chalice* and *Iron Brigade*.

So, for completion's sake here is the full list:

*   George Fan: designer of *Plants vs Zombies*
*   Mike Krahulik and Jerry Holkins: Gabe and Tycho, creators of Penny Arcade
*   Markus Persson: "Notch," creator of *Minecraft* and founder of Mojang
*   Richard Garriot: "Lord British," creator of the *Ultima* series
*   David Sirlin: designer on *Super Street Figher II Turbo HD Remix*, *Yomi*
*   Rob Pardo: chief creative officer at Blizzard, lead designer of *World of Warcraft*
*   Isaiah Cartwright: lead game designer for *Guild Wars 2*
*   Justin Gary: designer of *Ascension: Chronicle of the Godslayer* and *Solforge*
*   Stone Librande: lead designer at Riot Games, designer of *Diablo 3*, creative director of *SimCity*
*   Brian Fargo: founder and CEO of inXile Entertainment, director of *Bard’s Tale*, *Wasteland*, executive producer of *Fallout*
*   Mike Neumann: Gearbox Software, creative director on *Borderlands*
*   James Ernest: owner and lead designer for Cheapass Games, designer of *Kill Doctor Lucky*
*   Edmund McMillen: indie designer of *Super Meat Boy*, featured in *Indie Game: The Movie*
*   Brad Muir: game designer at Double Fine Productions leading *Massive Chalice*, project lead on *Iron Brigade*

Wait! There's one missing! You guys!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/83/51/83515d43a8427109b36630e8edb803fa933bbd1619c0c984482769e0b98e2a24.png) 

  

**Magic** is a unique game which, in many ways, makes everyone who plays it the designer of their own game. Since **Magic** is the greatest strategy trading card game on the planet... Ipso facto... you are all awesome game designers!
