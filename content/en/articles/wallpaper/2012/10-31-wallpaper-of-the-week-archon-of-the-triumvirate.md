Welcome to **Wallpaper of the Week**! It's Azorius Week, and we've got an Azorius wallpaper for you! And if that's not enough, [the Azorius page](https://web.archive.org/web/20140216235755/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/guilds/azorius) has a fan kit that includes wallpapers for [Isperia, Supreme Judge](https://web.archive.org/web/20121031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Isperia%2C+Supreme+Judge), [New Prahv Guildmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20121031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=New+Prahv+Guildmage), and [Hallowed Fountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20121031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallowed+Fountain)! But first, we've got David Rapoza's majestic [Archon of the Triumvirate](https://web.archive.org/web/20121031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archon+of+the+Triumvirate). Enjoy.

Check out the [Azorius Fan Kit](https://web.archive.org/web/20140216235755/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/guilds/azorius) for the other wallpapers!
