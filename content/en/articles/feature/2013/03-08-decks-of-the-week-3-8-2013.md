Congratulations to Masaya Kitayama for his victory at [Grand Prix Yokohama](https://web.archive.org/web/20130308000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/kitayama-makes-opponents-pay-yokohama), which broke an attendance record in Japan and became the second-largest **Magic** tournament (ever!) with 2,281 players! For his victory, Kitayama-*san* will be going to Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze* in San Diego. If you'd like to compete against him there, one way to do so is to win a PTQ, which for this season is using the Modern format. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#dgm) and check out recent PTQ results to see what you might face! Good luck; have fun!
