**This promotion ended on December 15, 2011.**

Looking for the alternate-art Liliana Vess or alternate-art Garruk Wildspeaker? We're sorry, but the original promotion has ended. See the terms and conditions for the PlayStation® 3 [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20110606000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx?x=mtg/digital/dotpps3/terms), for the XBox [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20110606000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx?x=mtg/digital/dotpxbox/terms), and for Steam [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20110606000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx?x=mtg/digital/dotpsteam/terms).

However, there will be a new opportunity to get a foil **Magic: The Gathering** card! **Duels of the Planeswalkers** 2012 will be available on June 15\*. And with it, a new crop of promo cards!

If you purchase [**Duels of the Planeswalkers** 2012](https://web.archive.org/web/20110606000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx?x=mtg/digital/dotp/d12) when it comes out this summer (and we're hoping you do!), if eligible, you'll get an in-game code for a voucher you can redeem at your local participating game store for a physical promo card. There are three different cards, depending on which platform you're using. \*

\* Release date may vary by platform and region. Cards are available while supplies last. Void where prohibited. See the [official terms](https://web.archive.org/web/20110606000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx?x=mtg/digital/dotp/terms).

People who buy the game via Steam for their PC will get Frost Titan, people on Xbox LIVE® Arcade will get Grave Titan, and PlayStation® 3 users will get Inferno Titan!
