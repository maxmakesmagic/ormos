Only two more wins to walk away with a Grand Prix title in Utrecht. First, the four remaining players had to overcome their respective opponents in the semi finals, though.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gputr14/polak-rottmann-vs-borneloev.jpg)

 *The first semi finals pitted Oliver Polak-Rottmann against Susanne Bornelöv!* 

  

It would be Oliver Polak-Rottmann against Susanne Bornelöv in a Mono-Blue Devotion vs. Black Devotion match on the first table while Richard Webels and his Blue-White Control went up against Eliott Boussaud and his take on Mono-Blue Devotion on the second table. 

Oliver Polak-Rottmann had the most explosive of starts with a [Cloudfin Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudfin+Raptor) and Frostborn Weird. Bornelöv tried to fight back with a [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat), but when Polak-Rottmann cast two copies of [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) with the first getting him four and the second one five tokens, Bornelöv was staring down a huge problem! 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gputr14/master-of-waves-game-1.jpg)

 *[Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) turned this game into a very one-sided affair!* 

  

An [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay) held [Cloudfin Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudfin+Raptor) at bay, but when Polak-Rottmann turned all of his cards sideways the following turn, Bornelöv started accessing her sideboard. 

Oliver Polak-Rottmann claims the lead!

Meanwhile, on the other semi finals table, little was happening. "2 cards were countered so far" was Frank Karsten's minimalistic and matter-of-factly analysis of the situation. While the German was holding on to all of his copies of [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) that were apparently great in the match-up, Boussaud had the perfect counters: [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault), [Thassa, God of the Sea](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thassa%2C+God+of+the+Sea) and [Bident of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bident+of+Thassa). 

Webels wiped Boussaud's field time and time again, but the French player simply rebuilt it twice with [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves), all the while sneaking in a few points of damage here and there and drawing new cards thanks to [Bident of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bident+of+Thassa). 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gputr14/game-1-webels-boussaud.jpg)

 *Boussaud had exactly what he needed against Richard Webels!* 

  

In the end, Boussaud remained victorious when Webels also started running low on removal. 

Eliott Boussaud leads 1 - 0 against Richard Webels! 

In the second game on the first table, Polak-Rottmann once again started with a [Cloudfin Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudfin+Raptor) while Bornelöv's first play was a [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) on her second turn that cost the Austrian a [Bident of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bident+of+Thassa). 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gputr14/thoughtseize-game-2b.jpg)

 *Bornelöv had an early [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) in the second game!* 

  

The game turned into a race where Polak-Rottmann added a second [Cloudfin Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudfin+Raptor) and two copies of [Tidebinder Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidebinder+Mage) while Bornelöv cast not one but two copies of [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon)! 

Polak-Rottmann simply added new creatures that he sacked right away to make sure Bornelöv's Demons couldn't block and the Swede was quickly down to just 2 life.

Bornelöv passed and Rottmann declared an attack with his [Cloudfin Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudfin+Raptor). Did Bornelöv have an answer? 

Yes, she did: [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay). Polak-Rottmann then shuffled up, staring down two huge [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon)s with no immediate answers. 

Susanne Bornelöv ties the score!

Polak-Rottmann had a slower start in the deciding game, but he was still a little ahead with two [Frostburn Weird](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frostburn+Weird), [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter) and Tidebinder Mage on the field. Bornelöv had an answer for the flying creature with [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight), though, and when she added a second [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon), the game took a huge swing in her direction. 

That changed once again when Polak-Rottmann cast a [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) that provided him with 8 (!) tokens. He now had more than enough "fodder" to shut down Bornelöv's Demons for turns to come. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gputr14/game-3-borneloev-polak-rottmann.jpg)

 *A huge [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) decided the third game between Polak-Rottmann and Bornelöv!* 

  

While a [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel) caused a minor swing once again, it wasn't enough; Polak-Rottmann simply turned all of his creatures the following turn and even though Bornelöv had a blocker in [Scavenging Ooze](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scavenging+Ooze), it wasn't enough. 

Oliver Polak-Rottmann advances to the finals!

Richard Webels and Eliot Boussaud just started their second game since the judges had to interfere to ensure a fair environment that caused a longer delay. 

Few things happened during the first few turns; Boussaud cast threats ([Bident of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bident+of+Thassa), [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves)) and Webels countered them ([Gainsay](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gainsay), [Dissolve](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dissolve)). Webels went looking for some new answers with [Jace, Architect of Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Architect+of+Thought), but two copies of [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) dealt with it. 

The tables got turned when Boussaud started countering - he attempted to [Negate](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Negate) an [Elixir of Immortality](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elixir+of+Immortality), but [Syncopate](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syncopate) foiled that plan. That was a close one for Webels, but he wasn't out of the danger zone just yet. 

He had to resolve a big [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) to re-stock, but Boussaud had once again two mana open. 

Did the French have the answer?

He did, [Gainsay](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gainsay)! 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gputr14/boussaud-webels.jpg)

 *Boussaud had enough threats to keep Webels busy throughout the game!* 

  

After that crushing blow, Webels couldn't quite recover and just a few turns later, he extended the hand. 

Eliott Boussaud advances to the finals!
