While the Gods in *Theros* block are at their most potent when they become indestructible creatures, many players forget that the Gods provide abilities not seen on other *Magic* cards. [Purphoros, God of the Forge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140729000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Purphoros%2C+God+of+the+Forge) can become a potent attacker with enough devotion, but this deck capitalizes on the non-devotion aspects of the card. In fact, this deck is not capable of providing the requisite five devotion in order to make Purphoros a creature!

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Purphoros,%20God%20of%20the%20Forge)

This deck takes advantage of [Purphoros, God of the Forge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140729000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Purphoros%2C+God+of+the+Forge) by making lots of tokens and using Purphoros to supply extra damage with those tokens. While dealing 6 damage each time you activate [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140729000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion)'s +1 ability is nice, don't forget that Purphoros can likely give those creatures +1/+0 a few times.

This deck seems well positioned against the other top decks in Standard while maintaining a high power level. Built-in two-for-one cards like [Voice of Resurgence](https://web.archive.org/web/20140729000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voice+of+Resurgence) and [Scion of Vitu-Ghazi](https://web.archive.org/web/20140729000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scion+of+Vitu-Ghazi) seem like a great place to win the attrition game against Mono-Black Devotion. [Mizzium Mortars](https://web.archive.org/web/20140729000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzium+Mortars), [Deicide](https://web.archive.org/web/20140729000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deicide), and [Mistcutter Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20140729000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistcutter+Hydra) are some of the strongest cards in the format against Mono-Blue Devotion. Finally, the tag team of [Sylvan Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20140729000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Caryatid) and [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140729000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix) will make life difficult for the low-to-the-ground aggressive decks.
