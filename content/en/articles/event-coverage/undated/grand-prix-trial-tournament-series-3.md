### Grand Prix Trial Tournament Series

The information on this page applies to all Grand Prix Trials. For information on Grand Prix Trials for a specific Grand Prix, please visit the [Grand Prix Event Schedule](/en/events/2002/welcome/grand-prix#1) (click "Info" in the "Trials" column)

### Entry Fee

Entry fee for each Grand Prix Trial is determined by the store running the tournament. Check with the store for specific information.

### Formats

The format for each Grand Prix Trial is chosen by the individual store running the event. Possible formats include Sealed Deck, Standard, Extended, Modern, or Block Constructed (If the format of a Grand Prix is Legacy, then the format for the Grand Prix Trials for that Grand Prix may also be Legacy). Contact the store or check the event locator for the format of each individual Grand Prix Trial.

If the chosen format is Sealed Deck, the playoffs for the event will be Boosters Draft or Sealed Deck (at the discretion of the store running that Grand Prix Trial). If a Sealed Deck playoff is run instead of a draft, players will play with the same sealed deck they played in the Swiss rounds.

### Prizes

*   Winner receives a three-round bye at the appropriate individual-format Grand Prix (two-round bye for his or her team at the appropriate team-format Grand Prix)
*   Other prizes are determined by the organizer of each event. Contact the organizer for additional details.

### Notes

*   Planeswalker Points Multiplier - 3x
*   REL - Competitive
*   All the **Magic: The Gathering** Tournament Rules and the **Magic: The Gathering** Infraction Procedure Guide are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document. Rules documents can be found in the [**Magic** Document Center](https://web.archive.org/web/20130530020928/http://www.wizards.com/WPN/Events/Rules.aspx?category=magic:thegathering).
*   Grand Prix Trials are premier Magic tournaments. The store owner, organizer, judge(s), and/or scorekeeper(s) of an individual Grand Prix Trial are not permitted to play in the tournament.
