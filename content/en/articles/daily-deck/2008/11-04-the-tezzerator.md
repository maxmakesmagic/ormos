One of the most fascinating new decks to come from Pro Tour-Berlin, Kenny Öberg's innovative control list took him all the way to the Top 8 before losing in the quarterfinals. He focuses on controlling the game with artifacts like Engineered Explosives, Vedalken Shackles, and Ensnaring Bridge. There's also a host of flexible tutor options, but what does he use to search up the individual artifacts? None other than Tezzeret the Seeker himself, who conveniently doubles as a win condition! The deck even received its name from the iconic planeswalker. Read more about Kenny, Tezzeret, and all the other exciting developments from Pro Tour-Berlin on the [official coverage page](/en/events/2008/ptber08/lsv-elves-equals-champion).
