Sanity Grinding made a brief blip on the radar screen of the tournament circuit just a few months back, but one John Jackson has pulled it back into the mainstream with a win at a PTQ in Lincoln, Nebraska. The deck uses Sanity Grinding, combined with cards like Cryptic Command and Twincast, to deck an opponent. The engine is fueled by Howling Mine, Jace Beleren, and Time Warp.
