Looking for a fun deck for Standard? Ken Yukuhiro took this interesting list to a solid finish at Pro Tour-Kyoto. He sought to abuse Mistmeadow Witch with numerous comes-into-play creatures like Reveillark, Sower of Temptation, and Mulldrifter. Once you get the Witch going, your creatures are virtually unkillable, providing you with bonus after bonus as they flip in and out of play!
