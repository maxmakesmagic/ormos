### 

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsd15/T8---Loren-Eakins.jpg)Loren Eakins

**Age:** 25

**Hometown:** Sacramento, CA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

30th at Grand Prix Sacramento

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Green-White Megamorph. I felt it was the most well-positioned deck in the format.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Den Protector](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Den+Protector). The card lets you go long with any deck and can force through a lot of damage.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Hangarback Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hangarback+Walker). The card is so difficult for opponents to deal with. It sets up so many situations where the opponent has no visible plays because the Thopters are coming.

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsd15/T8---Corey-Burkhart.jpg)Corey Burkhart

**Age:** 23

**Hometown:** Alta Loma, CA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Losing playing for SCG Top 8s, a MOCS finals, and one Pro Tour appearance

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Abzan Aggro. The deck applies heavy pressure to the linear decks while having game against other control strategies. Cutting all the [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall)s was a big part of the deck.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Herald of Torment](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Herald+of+Torment). The card steals so many games. After that, [Sandsteppe Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sandsteppe+Citadel).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Self-Inflicted Wound](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Self-Inflicted+Wound)s in the sideboard are fantastic. I had four this week on Magic Online up until Friday night where I added a [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress).

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsd15/T8---Daniel-Ward.jpg)Dan Ward

**Age:** 30

**Hometown:** Jefferson Height, NY

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Grand Prix Top 8 Vancouver, phoenix  
 RPTQ Winner (Season 1)

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Black-Red (Over-the-Top) Dragons. Well-positioned for the metagame. [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon); need I say more?

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

Strombreath Dragon! It's hard to kill and it kills quick. A lot of removal played currently doesn't line up well against it.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress). It made the matchups with less creatures better. Basically, takes the card I need to prevent my Dragons/[Goblin Rabblemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Rabblemaster)s from dying.

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsd15/T8---Soohwang-Yeem.jpg)Paul (Soohwang) Yeem

**Age:** 24

**Hometown:** San Diego and Berkeley, CA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

PTQ win for Pro Tour *Journey into Nyx*

11th at Grand Prix Atlanta

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Esper Dragons. I won a Grand Prix Trial with it (and I expected more Green-red Devotion).

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

Draognlord Ojutai. It finds [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy). It can basically flashback [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time).

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsd15/T8---Ben-Weitz.jpg)Ben Weitz

**Age:** 25

**Hometown:** St. Paul, MN

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Grand Prix Toronto Top 8; finalist Grand Prix Portland

35th Pro Tour Khans of Tarkir

54th Pro Tour Magic Origins

Silver in Pro Players Club

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Jeskai Midrange because Blue-Red [Ensoul Artifact](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ensoul+Artifact) and Red Aggro can be soft to unanswered [Soulfire Grand Master](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soulfire+Grand+Master) or [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider), and Jace and [Dragonlord Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai) are very powerful cards.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy). Fantastic powerful card with applications early and late game. Jeskai has a lot of conditional removal like [Wild Slash](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Slash) or [Valorous Stance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valorous+Stance), and Jace lets you find the right one and even cast it again.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Tragic Arrogance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tragic+Arrogance). It destroys Enchantress (Constellation) and Blue-Red [Ensoul Artifact](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ensoul+Artifact)s, and I played each of those decks twice.

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsd15/T8---Michael-Majors.jpg)Michael Majors

**Age:** 24

**Hometown:** Augusta, Georgia

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

2 Grand Prix Top 8s  
 2 Pro Tour Top 16s

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Blue-Red Tutelage; I trusted Seth Manfield's extensive testing—five matches against it on MTGO.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

Sphinx's Tutelage—can't actually win without it.  
 Honorable Mention to [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise), allowing me to play a bad eternal deck.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Fiery Impulse](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Impulse) — Functional [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) against [Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion) and [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider)

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsd15/T8---Arthur-Villela.jpg)Artur Villela

**Age:** 33

**Hometown:**

Juiz de Fora, Brazil

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

A couple of Grand Prix Top 8s (Rio 2013; Orlando 2014)

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Abzan Constellation, Jonathan “Jonais” Lobo told me to, and I trust him blindly.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Brain Maggot](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brain+Maggot). It's an incredibly powerful card in a field with a limited amount of removal spells. It also helped me a lot to win games where I didn't play my best.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall). It's not spectacular, but it sees a lot of play after Game 1.

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsd15/T8---Paul-Rietzl.jpg)Paul Rietzl

**Age:** 30

**Hometown:** Scottsdale, AZ by way of Boston, MA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Pro Tour Hall of Fame

11th Grand Prix San Diego 2011

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Abzan because it plays really good cards, gives you lots of decision points, and is very customizable after sideboarding.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang). Doubles by allowing you to cast two spells in a turn versus aggro while winning you long games with his activated ability.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon). Great way to win unwinnable games and use all those extra lands from [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix)/[Nissa, Vastwood Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150809000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%2C+Vastwood+Seer) and card draw.
