*Eternal Masters* Limited is not for the faint of heart. With complex components from throughout *Magic*'s history, it can be tough to figure out the best way to take advantage of synergies in the format.

But fret not, as Diego Fumagalli has put together five examples of some powerful things you can do in *Eternal Masters* to send your opponents running for their sideboards.
