It's time to see another finished piece of *Ninth Edition* art. You chose Pete Venters's sketch for [Threaten](https://web.archive.org/web/20041103000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Threaten) during the [Selecting *Ninth Edition*](https://web.archive.org/web/20041103000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/selecting9e/wrapup) promotion. Let's see the final piece!

First, a look back at that sketch:

  
*[Threaten](https://web.archive.org/web/20041103000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Threaten) sketch by Pete Venters* 

And here's the final art you'll see in packs of *Ninth*:

  
*Ninth Edition [Threaten](https://web.archive.org/web/20041103000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Threaten) art by Pete Venters*

4.  Kind of a tricky one: [Lavaborn Muse](https://web.archive.org/web/20041103000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lavaborn+Muse). Technically those aren't "energy objects" -- it's just some volcanic spray near the Muse. But the Muses *are* Spirits...
5.  [Innocence Kami](https://web.archive.org/web/20041103000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Innocence+Kami)
