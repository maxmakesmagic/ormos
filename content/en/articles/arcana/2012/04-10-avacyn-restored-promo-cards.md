The website has been taken over by *Avacyn Restored* previews! You've probably noticed. So this seems like a good time to look at some of the alternate-art promotional cards that will be available at special events!

  

### Prerelease

The [*Avacyn Restored* Prerelease](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-prereleases-3) is April 28-29. If you remember nothing else, please remember that. But if you'd like to remember a few extra things, perhaps you'd like to know what promotional card will be available to everyone who plays in one, while supplies last.

![Moonsilver Spear](https://i.ormos.uk/70/a5/70a511f987ffab36a0b67c2e450b89a0107580e889d04107c80b819d89d27f7f.jpg "Moonsilver Spear") 

  

And remember that select *Avacyn Restored* Prereleases will feature [the Helvault](/en/articles/arcana/2012/03-21-open-the-helvault), where participants get to open the Helvault and share a mysterious prize. Check with your local store about preregistration!

### Launch Weekend

*Avacyn Restored* will go on sale Friday, May 4, kicking off Launch Weekend. And if you go to the May 4th [Friday Night Magic](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6), there will be a special card available, while supplies last.

![Restoration Angel](https://i.ormos.uk/b6/6d/b66da450578cd7ad303dee43a4f5c6e8be7e10204693bbc628c9cc833ca6b3fa.jpg "Restoration Angel") 

  

### Game Day

Once *Avacyn Restored* is on sale, the big event is the [*Avacyn Restored* Game Day](/en/events/2010/gameday/magic-the-gathering-game-day). While supplies last, everyone who participates will get an extended-art Latch Seeker:

![Latch Seeker](https://i.ormos.uk/50/bb/50bb48c42281495a0814147eba6901b8b7a91da68c528223d6f87e979e6c6fe5.jpg "Latch Seeker") 

  

And if you get Top 8, you'll get a Killing Wave!

![Killing Wave](https://i.ormos.uk/1c/e7/1ce7daafe3cf9ff98a7a6c0c5f1e9307a682f7c5af4e73d9d91114689f0cd178.jpg "Killing Wave") 

  

### Buy-a-Box Card

And finally, there's the "Buy-a-Box card." You get it for being one of the first twenty people to buy a Booster Box of *Avacyn Restored* at a participating store. It's a foil alternate-art Silverblade Paladin!

![Silverblade Paladin](https://i.ormos.uk/eb/98/eb9801c4cf3518f19753e5d723266e118b8d84f550e2862ca66a77960c55cd85.jpg "Silverblade Paladin")
