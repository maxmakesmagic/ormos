Okay, yes, they're both blue. They're both snow creatures. They're both in *Coldsnap*. Something deeper than that! They both illustrate key themes from the set. Keep going. They're both painted by Lars Grant-West.

Now you're getting warmer.

Lars Grant-West painted these two cards as a single mural!
