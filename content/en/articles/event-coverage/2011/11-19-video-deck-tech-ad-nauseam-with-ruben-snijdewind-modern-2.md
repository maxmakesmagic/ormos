Rich Hagon catches up with Ruben Snijdewind, the Dutch National Champion, on Day Three at the World Championships. Snijdewind's [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20111119000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam+) deck has him on the cusp of the Top 8 and a spot for his team in the National Team final. A deck list will be posted after the event.
