Elementals have been an outside force in Standard since *Lorwyn* Block hit the world of Standard. Brandden "McDoogle" Mcdonough took this build to the Finals of a recent PTQ in Seattle, Washington. Fast mana on the backs of Smokebraider and Bloom Tender allow powerful Elementals to hit the battlefield early. Flamekin Harbinger ties the deck together, ensuring a player can always tutor for the Elemental they need!
