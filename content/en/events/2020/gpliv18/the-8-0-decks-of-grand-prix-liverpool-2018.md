Out of 504 teams, only two trios managed to win all of their rounds on Saturday: Francisco Sanchez, Mattia Rizzi, and Cristian Ortiz, as well as Niels Molle, Piotr Głogowski, and Joao Choca (pictured below, left to right).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpliv18/LIV_10.jpg)

They ran six completely different decks. There was Death's Shadow and Humans and Tron and White-Blue Control and Ironworks and Dredge. Such variety!
