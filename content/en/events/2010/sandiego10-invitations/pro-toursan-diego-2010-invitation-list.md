Invitations to Pro Tour-San Diego are awarded in the following manner:

*   The 2009 World Champion.
*   The 2009 Pro Tour Player of the Year.
*   The Top 50 players from Pro Tour-Austin.
*   The Top 50 players from the 2009 World Championship
*   The members of the Top 4 teams (including alternate) from the 2009 World Championship.
*   Players with 2010 Pro Tour Players Club level 6 or higher.
*   Players with 2010 Pro Tour Players Club level 4\* & 5\* (invitation for level 4 & 5 players are not awarded until February 19, 2010).
*   Players with 2010 Pro Tour Players Club level 3\* are invited to 1 Pro Tour or World Championship of their choice in the 2010 season (invitation for level 3 players are not awarded until February 19, 2010).
*   The Top 16 finishers of each Grand Prix tournament that feeds Pro Tour-San Diego.
*   Players who earn invitations through Pro Tour-San Diego Qualifier tournaments.
*   The Top 100 DCI-ranked players in the DCI Total format on January 6, 2010.

\* Players with Pro Tour Players Club levels 3, 4 and 5 are allowed to play in Pro Tour qualifiers if their *only* invitation to the Pro Tour is due to their level in the Players Club (see below)

If you believe that your rating or finish at a Pro Tour Qualifier or Grand Prix is in error, please Visit [https://webapp.wizards.com/login.aspx?target=personal](https://web.archive.org/web/20100325062709/https://webapp.wizards.com/login.aspx?target=personal). After logging in, click the "Appeals" link and fill out the form.
