Each team-format Pro Tour invitation list comprises the following:

*   Any team of three players with a combination of at least 50 pro points as of the most recent pro standing preceding the Pro Tour event (Nagoya Standing [www.wizards.com/protour](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603133729/http://www.wizards.com/protour)). Teams formed in this manner must be announced at player registration before the tournament.
*   The Top 4 finishing teams (who were previously uninvited) of each Grand Prix tournament that feeds the appropriate Pro Tour event.
*   Teams (who were previously uninvited) who earn invitations through appropriate Qualifier tournaments.
*   Any team with a DCI three-person Limited rating of 1750 or more as of the January 26th ratings publish.
