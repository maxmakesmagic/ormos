When we [last discussed Planeswalker Decks](/en/articles/makingmagic/2016/05-16-ramp-of-approval), we noted that there would be some cards in the decks that did not appear in the set, but were fully new cards. Last week [we showed you the versions of Chandra and Nissa](https://web.archive.org/web/20160913000000/https://magic.wizards.com/node/1061151) that would come with the decks. Here they are again, in case you've forgotten.

![Chandra, Pyrogenius](http://media.wizards.com/2016/bVvMNuiu2i_KLD/en_CO4tkc9sLe.png)![Nissa, Nature's Artisan](http://media.wizards.com/2016/bVvMNuiu2i_KLD/en_YXvBDsDjHq.png)

If you pick up the Chandra deck, here are the particular cards Mark Rosewater mentioned in his article that you'll be receiving:

![Flame Lash](http://media.wizards.com/2016/bVvMNuiu2i_KLD/en_FlbIqaqeQi.png)![Liberating Combustion](http://media.wizards.com/2016/bVvMNuiu2i_KLD/en_OhojOzc1Dy.png)

![Renegade Firebrand](http://media.wizards.com/2016/bVvMNuiu2i_KLD/en_Tobx7E8U8L.png)![Stone Quarry](http://media.wizards.com/2016/bVvMNuiu2i_KLD/en_qkWdicjSwy.png)

And if you pick up the Nissa deck, then you'll receive, among other cards, these four:

![Guardian of the Great Conduit](http://media.wizards.com/2016/bVvMNuiu2i_KLD/en_KQM8W25NWY.png)![Terrain Elemental](http://media.wizards.com/2016/bVvMNuiu2i_KLD/en_BkX9yguOSV.png)

![Verdant Crescendo](http://media.wizards.com/2016/bVvMNuiu2i_KLD/en_mq0P6WU7bz.png)![Woodland Stream](http://media.wizards.com/2016/bVvMNuiu2i_KLD/en_BN3HdwyCyO.png)

Planeswalker Decks will be available just like Intro Packs were at [local stores near you](https://web.archive.org/web/20160913000000/http://locator.wizards.com/).
