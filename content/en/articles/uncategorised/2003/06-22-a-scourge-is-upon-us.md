### The *Scourge* Release For Newbies

* * *

*Scourge* cards become available at the [***Magic** Online* Store](https://web.archive.org/web/20030622000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com&origin=magic_magiconline_articleak062203) on June 23, but the fun begins two days later! Events including all-*Scourge* drafts and Swiss Sealed Deck will be held on both Wednesday and Thursday.

Many of you may already have seen and played with *Scourge* cards during the physical Prerelease, but for those of you who haven't, or for those of you who are new to the **Magic** game, here are the important details you should know regarding the online *Scourge* release.

### Sealed Deck Events

The *Scourge* release tournaments will seat between 24 and 48 players, use Swiss-format pairing, and will be offered throughout the two-day period. Swiss-format events with 24 to 48 players allow you to play 5 to 6 rounds and, if you are one of the top 8 at the end of the final round, you'll qualify for a 3-round single-elimination booster draft to determine your final ranking. (Booster draft information can be found in great detail in the ***Magic** Online* help files and a basic description can be found below.)

To participate in a *Scourge* release tournament, you'll need to purchase or acquire 1 *Onslaught* tournament pack, 3 *Scourge* booster packs, and 2 event tickets.

### *Scourge-Scourge-Scourge* Booster Drafts

For two days only, special booster drafts using 3 *Scourge* boosters will be offered. For those of you who have never drafted, it's a fun and competitive way to open your product.

Booster drafts consist of eight players seated around a table. Each player opens one pack and examines the cards within. Each player then chooses the one card out of the assortment they want the most. This card is placed to the side, and the remainder of the cards are "passed" to the player next to him or her. 

As you pass your extra cards, so does everybody else, and you'll soon have another stack of cards to look through. Once again, you'll select one card, and pass the remainder along. This continues until all fifteen cards in each player's first pack are selected. The second and third packs are distributed in the same way.

Once you have 45 cards (meaning all the cards in the boosters have been drafted), you'll move to the **Deck Building** screen. Land is available by clicking on the **Add Land** button on the bottom of the screen. You assemble your deck of no less than 40 cards including land, then click the check button to save your deck. All other cards are your sideboard and can be swapped into your deck during games two or three in the match.

Booster drafts are single elimination, so if you lose a match, you're out. Prizes will consist of *Scourge* boosters.

To participate in one of the triple-*Scourge* booster drafts, you'll need to purchase or acquire 3 *Scourge* boosters and 2 event tickets. As always, these items are available at the [***Magic** Online* Store](https://web.archive.org/web/20030622000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com&origin=magic_magiconline_articleak062203).

### New Mechanics

There are two new mechanics to watch for on *Scourge* cards: Landcycling and Storm.
