Pro Tour Kobe 2004 starred the original *Mirrodin* Block Constructed format. The big deck at the event? Affinity. The powerful combination of Skullclamp, Disciple of the Vault, and Arcbound Ravager was very tough to beat. Ben Stark played this version of the archetype into the Top 8 long before the Phyrexians invaded. Read more about the event from the coverage archives [here](/en/events/2004/ptkob04/live-coverage-of-2004-pro-tour-kobe).
