Extended is a brand new format, and it was the environment over 400 professional **Magic** players found themselves battling with at Pro Tour-Amsterdam this past weekend. But before they took a crack at the format, the denizens of **Magic Online** carved up what things would be by battling in endless Premier Events in the digital world. Clyde the Glide, who one presumes must be a Trailblazers fan, took Merfolk to a 4-0 record on a recent event there. One of the more popular decks in Legacy, the little blue fish know how to get it done in the brave new world of Extended too.
