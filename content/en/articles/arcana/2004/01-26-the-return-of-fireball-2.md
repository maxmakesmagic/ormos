Born in the ancient days of **Magic**'s *Alpha* set, Fireball has been scorching armies, finishing off blockers, and of course, directly roasting opponents for a decade. 

Now, thanks to *[Darksteel](https://web.archive.org/web/20080506160149/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/expansion/darksteel)*, Fireball is back.

### Change of mana cost

Back in 1993, Fireball cost ![X Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/b1/6bb1152b0a3305d0a7c2e2eb725b8ebd18620607efc62f6f38b0642b97c8501f.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif), with an additional cost specified in its text box to pay for any additional char-broil targets beyond the first. Since the *Beatdown Box Set*, Fireball's mana cost had been ![X Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/b1/6bb1152b0a3305d0a7c2e2eb725b8ebd18620607efc62f6f38b0642b97c8501f.gif)![Y Mana](http://www.wizards.com/images/Symbols/Symbol_Y_mana.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif): the only ![Y Mana](http://www.wizards.com/images/Symbols/Symbol_Y_mana.gif) in **Magic** outside of *Unglued*. The ![Y Mana](http://www.wizards.com/images/Symbols/Symbol_Y_mana.gif) indicated the number of additional targets among which to distribute the damage. 

![Fireball, from Beatdown Box Set and from Darksteel](https://i.ormos.uk/65/c5/65c5232e2b886ff67ed7ad8dea148cbf9ada5c148d7a30051b220b6a5655b879.jpg)

Once *Darksteel* becomes legal in tournaments on February 20, 2004, Fireball's official [Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20080507180949/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dci/oracle) mana cost will change back, and its rules text will change. It has returned to the traditional ![X Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/b1/6bb1152b0a3305d0a7c2e2eb725b8ebd18620607efc62f6f38b0642b97c8501f.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif) mana cost, and the additional cost for targets is again specified in the card's rules text -- but it cooks, bakes and broils just the same as it always has. 

### Change of rarity

In addition, Fireball is uncommon in *Darksteel*, so that it doesn't ruin draft and sealed deck play for *Mirrodin* block. Remember, despite being an enormous rolling boulder of flame, Fireball is a remarkably efficient and flexible tool for its cost. R&D considered it wise if draft and sealed decks didn't contain too many of them!

### Change of art

Lastly, as you can see above (or maybe you've seen it already, if you were lucky enough to open it in your packs at the *Darksteel* prerelease), Fireball's art has changed for the first time in history. Check it out. 

![Fireball art from Darksteel](https://i.ormos.uk/da/97/da9769387d3455252c7e18dc14730a6f4f1e5d1d0d724ede44584c16b3e459a6.jpg)

Have fun throwing balls of fire with the release of *Darksteel!*
