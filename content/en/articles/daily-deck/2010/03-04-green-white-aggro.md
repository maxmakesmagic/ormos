The Pro Tour Qualifier season for Pro Tour–San Juan is winding down, but Kyle Eck managed to find a hole in the metagame. Using a Green-White Aggro deck, Kyle took the Top 8 at the Extended Qualifier at Pro Tour–San Diego. Even if you're not qualified for the Pro Tour, its larger-than-life environment is a great place to just show up and play **Magic**. For the full story of the event, visit the [official coverage](/en/events/2010/ptsd10/gortzen-goes-all-the-way-in-san-diego).
