Consider the [Panopticon](https://web.archive.org/web/20091202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panopticon). Here, we'll show you the card so you can consider it more easily.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ca/f0/caf0e1444d832f5886b85321351090d66596427cf52074edbc3caa255a18708c.jpg)  

Here's what the art for [Panopticon](https://web.archive.org/web/20091202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panopticon) looks like, shorn of its *Planechase* trappings.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2d/42/2d425c2ed7e6fb9b16a1a48860f1dd03201488d65594fa68b1aad101de03f0bb.jpg)*[Panopticon](https://web.archive.org/web/20091202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panopticon) art by John Avon*  
  

On Earth, a "panopticon" is a structure for a prison in which the guards stay at the center and can see into any cell at any time. On the plane of *Mirrodin*, it's a massive tower (occupied by [Memnarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20091202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memnarch)) from which the entire plane can be viewed. You might know it better as the [Darksteel Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20091202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Citadel).

![Darksteel Citadel](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Darksteel+Citadel)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/46/9c/469c556f3b171df2d0b8b5df59722e7c959a18625d3564f6a2d3189a7e52b9d2.jpg)*[Darksteel Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20091202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Citadel) art by John Avon*  
  

Ah, but what about those growths that are all around the tower in the [Panopticon](https://web.archive.org/web/20091202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panopticon) art? You can see a couple of them in the distant background of [Darksteel Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20091202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Citadel), but there's a better view of them on [Mirrodin's Core](https://web.archive.org/web/20091202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirrodin%27s+Core).

![Mirrodin's Core](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Mirrodin%27s+Core)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9c/4c/9c4c0f68ed2b04d8d1cb26c6a7b8357e2546312bf7e4a95097d7917a99bb209e.jpg)*[Darksteel Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20091202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Citadel) art by Greg Staples*
