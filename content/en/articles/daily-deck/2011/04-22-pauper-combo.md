No Common Week would be complete without featuring a deck from the Pauper format! That's right, an entire format devoid of rares and uncommons. You can play Pauper with your friends or nearly any time of the day on [**Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920224555/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Digital/MagicOnline.aspx). Check out this list as a starter: white-blue combo that allows you to generate tons of free spells and Temporal Fissure your opponents out of the game!
