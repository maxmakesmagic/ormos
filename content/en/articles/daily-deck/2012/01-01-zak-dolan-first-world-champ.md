By modern deckbuilding standards, Zak Dolan's World Championship winning list is "interesting" to say the least. He packed it full of some of the game's most powerful cards (look at how many members of the Power 9 are present!) and locked his opponent out of the match with his Stasis/Time Elemental combo and good ol' Serra Angel for a win condition. You can read more about the first World Champions in [this](https://web.archive.org/web/20120101000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/226) nifty article by **Magic** web manager Monty Ashley.
