## PRODUCT INFORMATION

These ready-to-play decks showcase cards from across Magic history, including six cards from the new *Khans of Tarkir* ™ set and four cards with new artwork.

**Set Name:** *Duel Decks: Speed vs. Cunning*  
**Number of Cards:** Two 60-card decks  
**Includes:** Two deck boxes  
 Two creature tokens  
 Strategy insert  
*Magic* ™ rules reference card  
**Languages:** English, Japanese  
**Official Three-Letter Code:** DDN  
**Twitter Hashtag:** #MTGSVC

**Deck Design and Development:** Chris Millar and Sam Stoddard
