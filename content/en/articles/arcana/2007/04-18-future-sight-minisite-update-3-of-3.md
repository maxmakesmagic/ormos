What's that, you say? [*Future Sight* prerelease events](/en/events/2007/prereleases-fut/future-sight-worldwide-prerelease-fact-sheet) are this weekend? That must mean that we're in the final week of **magicthegathering.com** card previews, which must mean it's time for the final content update for the [*Future Sight* minisite](https://web.archive.org/web/20080425024243/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/futuresight/home).

[![The Planeswalker's Path](https://i.ormos.uk/91/39/91397af790320ae4887a34256dcd7a40dcda59147dd1c126023c912717c94e69.jpg "The Planeswalker's Path")](https://web.archive.org/web/20080425024243/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/futuresight/home)

This week's content includes:

*   The "Lexicon of the Future," an interactive guide to some of the strange words and terms that *Future Sight* imports from future realms of **Magic: The Gathering**,
*   Exclusive *Future Sight* card art,
*   A final set of insights into the flavor and storyline of *Future Sight*, and
*   An online jigsaw puzzle featuring *Future Sight* desktop wallpaper.

[Off you go!](https://web.archive.org/web/20080425024243/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/futuresight/home)
