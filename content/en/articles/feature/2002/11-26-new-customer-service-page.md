### All The Whos And Whats

* * *

A lot of questions that come through the Ask ***Magic** Online* email address which are best handled by our Customer Service team. We've added a new [Customer Service contact page](https://web.archive.org/web/20050403180103/http://wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/csmain) so you can get your questions to the right people and get your answers quickly.

Each page lists the information needed to resolve your questions. Please read the page carefully before sending your email and, as always, please **do not** include your password information.
