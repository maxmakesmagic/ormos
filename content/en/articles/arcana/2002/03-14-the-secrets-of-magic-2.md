The *[Secrets of Magic](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/http://www.wizards.com/catalog/product.asp?88552)* is a new anthology of short stories edited by Jess Lebow. Each story reveals some previously undisclosed aspect of the **Magic** world.

Our own Rei Nakazawa wrote up a quick blurb for each story and tied them all to specific **Magic** cards, like Plague Rats, Kukemssa Pirates, and Balthor the Stout. You can read Rei's synopsis [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20050205051954/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/products/secretsofmagic)… and then go read the book!
