# What *Fate Reforged* card have you enjoyed playing with the most?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ_Patty_Robertson.jpg)  
**Patty Robertson:** [Goblin Heelcutter](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Heelcutter) or [Aven Surveyor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Surveyor).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ_John-Paul_Kelly.jpg)  
**John-Paul Kelly:** [Rite of Undoing](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rite+of+Undoing).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ_Chester_Swords.jpg)  
**Chester Swords:** [Citadel Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Citadel+Siege).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ_Don_Van_Ravenzwaaij.jpg)  
**Don van Ravenswaaij:** [Citadel Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Citadel+Siege), the card is bonkers.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ_Hao-Shan_Huang.jpg)  
**Huang Hao-shan:** [Soul Summons](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soul+Summons).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ_Shuhei_Nakamura.jpg)  
**Shuhei Nakamura:** [Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang).
