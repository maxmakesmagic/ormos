Even though Jace, the Mind Sculptor is gone from Standard, there are plenty of planeswalkers still looking for the right deck. Could Tezzeret, Agent of Bolas finally have found the 60 he was looking for? Xu Qian certainly thinks so, playing this combo/control Tezzeret deck into the China National Championships via a Last Chance Qualifier. Read more about China Nationals [here](/en/events/2011/chnat11/a-champion-is-born).
