Back when we showed off the [*Dark Ascension* Art Gallery](/en/articles/arcana/2012/01-04-dark-ascension-art-gallery-2), there was a mysterious piece of art that kind of stood out.

  

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/896_jarofeyeballsfinal.jpg) 

Jar of Eyeballs | Art by [Jaime Jones](https://web.archive.org/web/20120130000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22jaime+jones%22])

"What is this mysterious jar of eyeballs?" asked people around the Internet. And soon, the answer was made clear: it was Jar of Eyeballs.

 ![Jar of Eyeballs](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Jar%20of%20Eyeballs&options=) 

  

Now, we're not mentioning this just to show off a jar of eyeballs. That's the main reason, obviously. But we also thought it would be fun to look at the original sketch, before the eyeballs got finished.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/896_jarofeyeballssketch.jpg) 

Jar of Eyeballs Sketch | Art by [Jaime Jones](https://web.archive.org/web/20120130000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22jaime+jones%22])
