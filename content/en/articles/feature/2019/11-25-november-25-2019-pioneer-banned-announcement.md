**Announcement Date**: November 25, 2019

**Pioneer**:

No changes

The list of all banned cards in Pioneer is [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20191125000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/game-info/gameplay/formats/pioneer).

**Next Pioneer B&R Announcement**: December 2, 2019

* * *

We're choosing to make no changes to Pioneer this week. The data and results generated from [MTGO Pioneer week](/en/articles/magiconline/2019/11-19-magic-online-announcements-november-19-2019) tournaments will help inform our next steps for the format. Whether or not changes are needed, we'll provide another update next Monday, Dec 2.
