Sometimes you just want to look at a cute little gnome, you know? But we like to stay current around here and there aren't any gnomes in *Rise of the Eldrazi*. What to do? Well, luckily for us, *Urza's Saga* hit **Magic Online** a little over a month ago. And we've been shockingly negligent in showing you some of the cool tidbits from *that* set!

We shall start with a cute gnome. Let's say you wanted to show something that a mage would use to keep track of time. You'd probably end up with [Metrognome](https://web.archive.org/web/20100517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metrognome)!

![Metrognome](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Metrognome)

  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/05/b4/05b47db6036ed360a36a8c9dfe68323d8e308f925c8fe0802fd9cf27bf3f6f00.jpg)

[Metrognome](https://web.archive.org/web/20100517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metrognome) | Art by [Jeff Laubenstein](https://web.archive.org/web/20100517000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Jeff+Laubenstein%22%5D)

Now, back in 1998, you had to bring out beads or face-down lands or something to represent your gnomes. But now we have tokens! And we have a computer that is happy to put them out for you. So we commissioned art to go on the gnome tokens. And you know what that means: *new* adorable gnomes!

This piece was assigned to Ron Spencer with the note that it should look like a gnome from the same workbench that [Metrognome](https://web.archive.org/web/20100517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metrognome) is sitting on. Behold!
