White Weenie gets some extra help from some big friends in Brett Anderon’s take on the deck. Shoring up the early hitters of *Shards of Alara* creatures like Akrasan Squire and Sigiled Paladin comes planeswalkers Ajani Vengeant and Elspeth, Knight-Errant. Ajani clears the way of blockers or keeps a control opponent’s Vivid lands tapped down while Elspeth creates an army, sends said army to the air, and ultimately makes the indestructible. Brett took the list all the way to 6th place at the Alabama State Championships.
