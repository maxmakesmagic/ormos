T minus one work- or school-week and counting until this weekend's [*Dissension* release tournaments](https://web.archive.org/web/20060501000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/dissension-release-tournament-fact-sheet-2006-04-25). Ready to play? [Find a local shop](https://web.archive.org/web/20060501000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/dissension-release-tournament-fact-sheet-2006-04-25) where you can bust open some *Dissension* packs and finally fight with all ten guilds. It's the release tournament -- all the fun of the prerelease, but with a different promo card.
