Jun'ya Iyanaga is the 2011 **Magic** World Champion! Iyanaga's Standard deck, Wolf Run Ramp, is a well-established archetype that served him well in the Top 8, dispatching Josh Utter-Leyton, Conley Woods, and Richard Bland on his way to the title. Check out the [full coverage](https://web.archive.org/web/20160506045303/http://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/japan-stands-tall-worlds-again) to see how!
