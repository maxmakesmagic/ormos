Magicthegathering.com isn't the only site that gets [redesigned](/en/articles/feature/2004/02-09-the-new-magic-the-gathering-com) from time to time. The Wizards.com home page ([www.wizards.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20040303000000/http://www.wizards.com/)), home to news, events, promotions and announcements from all games and departments of Wizards of the Coast, has gotten its fair share of facelifts as well. In fact, the look of the Wizards.com front page has changed just about every year since its shaky beginnings in 1996. Let's take a walk down the digital memory lane, shall we?
