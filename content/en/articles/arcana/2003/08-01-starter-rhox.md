The great monster [Rhox](https://web.archive.org/web/20030801000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rhox) from the *Nemesis* expansion has been reprinted in the Core Set with its original art.

A rarer premium version of [Rhox](https://web.archive.org/web/20030801000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rhox)—with art based on how he was presented in television ads—was included in the [2000 Starter set](https://web.archive.org/web/20030801000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/starter/starter/welcome.asp).
