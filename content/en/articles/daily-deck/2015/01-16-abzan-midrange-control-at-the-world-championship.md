It was the third game of the last round. There was a continuous exchange of sharp plays, a seesaw game swaying back and forth between tempo and card advantage, game plans reconsidered over and over as the initiative switched. It finally ended with Chapin unleashing [Duneblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20150116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duneblast) to end the game in his favor, only to be blown out by [Nissa, Worldwaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20150116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%2C+Worldwaker) off the top.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Nissa%2C+Worldwaker)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%2C+Worldwaker)

Personally, this is my favorite bout from [Worlds Week](/en/events/2014/legacy-daily-2014-11-22/legacy-daily). Although falling slightly short of Sunday after such a dramatic victory, Shaun McLaren's Abzan Midrange deck is my final deck of this week.

Actually, I would call this deck Abzan Control, rather than Midrange.

The Jeskai deck he brought to [Pro Tour *Khans of Tarkir*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-khans-of-tarkir-coverage-events) was controlish, but this time there is no doubt.

For example, [Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20150116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc) is replaced by [Read the Bones](https://web.archive.org/web/20150116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Read+the+Bones), almost shedding all "attackers" from the deck.

The mana base also reflects this with additional scry lands over [Sandsteppe Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20150116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sandsteppe+Citadel). The title "midrange" seems incorrect since card quantity is valued over early mana.

This deck reminds me of Jund Control [two seasons ago](https://web.archive.org/web/20150116000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/pr/218).

Change [Farseek](https://web.archive.org/web/20150116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Farseek) to [Sylvan Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20150116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Caryatid), [Huntmaster of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20150116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Huntmaster+of+the+Fells) to [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) and you might see what I mean.
