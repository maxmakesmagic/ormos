> *Taking a sip of coffee, you log on to your computer and begin reading your email. You see a couple of messages from Scott Johns and Monty Ashley about updates to **magicthegathering.com** that need to be made. As you begin to work on those, Mark Rosewater swings by your desk with an idea he would like to try out in his next article and wants to know if you can program it for him. Later, Aaron Forsythe calls you into his office to talk about Gatherer and to thank you for the latest Super Cool Web Widget that you deployed last week...*

Does this sound like your normal work day? It could.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/67/93/679321782974b14314123e33a23cbca838f238a69b5047d3043ac666eed71d1b.jpg)My name is Don Frazier, and I'm the web development manager for Wizards of the Coast. As part of the Digital Gaming Initiative that [Randy Buehler announced](/en/articles/feature/2007/01-26-wizards-of-the-coasts-digital-initiative), we are looking for someone to join us as a web developer to work on **magicthegathering.com** and the Gatherer search application. This is a full time position located in Renton, Washington. The qualifications for the position are:

*   You must be passionate about **Magic: The Gathering**. No posers here! While you don't have to have won, or even played in, a Pro Tour, knowledge of the game and its history is essential.
*   You must show innovation. We want to provide the players who come to our site with lots of really cool tools, and at least half of this job will be coming up with and implementing those tools.
*   You must be a fun person to work with. We believe in surrounding ourselves with the best people in the world to work with. Just makes life better.
*   You must enjoy having fun. Duh!

So, those are the non-technical requirements, but, this is a technical position. There are some technical requirements as well. They are:

*   Experience creating dynamic web applications utilizing JavaScript, XML, XSLT and server side controls.
*   Experience programming ASP using VBScript and ASP.Net using C#
*   Experience accessing data from a SQL database using ADO.Net and writing SQL stored procedures.
*   Strong knowledge of web technologies and the latest trends in development and innovation.

A minimum of two years in the above areas is required, and four or more years is preferred. You also must be willing to relocate to Washington State (we'll help with that) and be eligible to work in the U.S for any employer.

If you think you've got what it takes and are interested, head over to our jobs page and post your resume for [job #2104](http://hasbro.recruitmax.com/MAIN/careerportal/Job_Profile.cfm?szOrderID=2104&szReturnToSearch=1&szWordsToHighlight=).
