The upcoming *[Guildpact](https://web.archive.org/web/20051207000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/expansion/guildpact)* set features three pre-constructed theme decks, each themed around one of *Guildpact's* guilds.

The first one is called **Izzet Gizmometry**, and it looks a little something like this:

![Izzet Gizmometry](https://i.ormos.uk/74/05/74059c66fc6e4ac649d5ca6d656c6c578facb105d01d790b2992a20d056646ac.jpg)  
*Guildpact* theme deck 1 of 3

*Guildpact* releases February 4, 2006. Get your first chance to crack open packs at the [*Guildpact* prerelease tournaments](https://web.archive.org/web/20051207000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prereleases), coming to a venue near you January 21-22, 2006. Then don't miss the [*Guildpact* release tournaments](https://web.archive.org/web/20051207000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/releases) on the weekend the set hits shelves, February 4-5, 2006.
