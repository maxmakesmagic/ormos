### September 2002: Alliterative Names

**Monday, September 2:**  
 What alliterative creature was the same cost as a more common card in the same set yet had two additional abilities?  
**Answer:**Granite Gargoyle (in Alpha). Like the common Gray Ogre, the rare Granite Gargoyle was a 2/2 for ![2 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/f9/cc/f9cc8a15b009997919d58ad79964384351495d1b6c9231f938316577453038a8.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif). Except the Gargoyle also had flying and toughness pumping.

**Tuesday, September 3:**  
Hanna, Ship's Navigator (in *Invasion*) was inspired by what alliterative creature?  
**Answer:**Argivian Archaeologist (from *Antiquities*)

**Wednesday, September 4:**  
 What alliterative sorcery does twice the effect of a sorcery a third its cost?  
**Answer:**Death's Duet (from *Exodus*). It returns two creature cards to your hand, twice Raise Dead (which cost only ![Black Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/6b/e16bcf75cffdd1079b6ba1f9f43c9e6e7253bb89521acdf6b3e93d3c2e63dbb5.gif)).

**Thursday, September 5:**  
 What card was called Star Burst when it was in design in a set then codenamed Menagerie?  
 Answer: Final Fortune (from *Mirage*).

**Friday, September 6:**  
 What alliterative sorcery has the name "MIKE" hidden in its art?  
**Answer:**Goblin Grenade, illustrated by Ron Spencer - in the spear (from *Fallen Empires*)

**Monday, September 9:**  
 What multi-colored alliterative creature was a redone version of a mono-colored creature that had the same converted mana cost, same creature type, same abilities, same power and same toughness?  
**Answer:**Sky Spirit (from *Tempest*). It was exactly the same as *Legends'*Thunder Spirit except one of the white mana in its mana cost was turned to blue.

**Tuesday, September 10:**  
 What alliterative instant is also known as the "green boon"?  
**Answer:**Giant Growth. The other "boons" in Alpha were Healing Salve, Ancestral Recall, Dark Ritual and Lightning Bolt. Each gave you three of something for one mana.

**Wednesday, September 11:**  
 What alliterative creature was thought merely a legend but seen as a sign of good luck?  
**Answer:**Glade Gnarr (from *Apocalypse*). The full flavor text: "Long thought merely a legend, the appearance of the gnarr was seen by the defenders as a sign of good luck."

**Thursday, September 12:**  
 What alliterative creature is a repeat of an older card except it has a new creature type that is a word found in its new name?  
**Answer:**Headless Horseman (from *Legends*) with creature type Horseman. It is a repeat of Alpha's Scathe Zombies.

**Friday, September 13:**  
 What alliterative creature has a creature type that has appeared on over twenty different cards yet is the only one of its type in the block it appears?  
**Answer:**Drifting Djinn (from *Urza's Saga*). It is the only djinn in the *Urza's Saga* block.

**Monday, September 16:**  
 If morph was inspired by Illusionary Mask, what alliterative card inspired the divvy mechanic (i.e. Fact or Fiction)?  
**Answer:**Raging River (from Alpha).

**Tuesday, September 17:**  
 What alliterative card pictures the close-up of a side of a head with braided hair and an earring?  
**Answer:**Mind Maggots (from *Exodus*).

**Wednesday, September 18:**  
 What alliterative enchantment was reprinted under a different name but both names had a possessive proper noun (i.e. Somebody's Something)?  
**Answer:**Arenson's Aura (from *Ice Age*). It was later reprinted in *Invasion* as Teferi's Care.

**Thursday, September 19:**  
 What alliterative creature was followed a set later by a creature with the same power, toughness, creature type, and abilities except the word red was changed to black? (The name was, of course, changed.)  
**Answer:**Freewind Falcon (from *Visions*). Duskrider Falcon, the protection from black version, was then printed in *Weatherlight*.

**Friday, September 20:**  
 What alliterative card has the same name as a solitaire variant printed in the *Duelist*? (Hint: I did numerous puzzles using this solitaire variant.)  
**Answer:**Mana Maze (from *Invasion*).

**Monday, September 23:**  
 What alliterative card's name is defined in the dictionary as "an artificial or little man obscured or made dim or cloudy"?  
**Answer:**Hazy Homunculus (from *Prophecy*)

**Tuesday, September 24:**  
 What alliterative creature has the following objects pictured in its artwork: mace, halberd, knife and rake?  
**Answer:**Mogg Maniac (from *Stronghold*)

**Wednesday, September 25:**  
 What alliterative creature was the first to have an enemy color activation?  
**Answer:**Electric Eel (from *The Dark*)

**Thursday, September 26:**  
 What alliterative creature has the strength of stone and the endurance of mountains?  
**Answer:**Earth Elemental (from Alpha)

**Friday, September 27:**  
 What alliterative card was almost printed with the name Rapture?  
**Answer:**Celestial Convergence (from *Prophecy*)
