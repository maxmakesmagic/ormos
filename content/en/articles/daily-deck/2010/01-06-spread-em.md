Spread 'Em is a new archetype that has been popularized by the 2009s and the StarCityGames.com Open series. Tim Hunt took it to the Top 8 of the first StarCityGames.com Standard Open. the deck's strategy focuses on turning its opponent's lands into unusable Islands (or otherwise). to do that, it plays a host of cascade spells, each of which must end in a two-mana enchantment, either Spreading Seas or Convincing Mirage. From there, you simply win with any creature as your opponent struggles through being unable to cast any spells! You can read StarCity's coverage of the event [here](http://www.starcitygames.com/magic/standard/18414_live_coverage_of_the_starcitygamescom_10k_weekend_in_st_louis.html).
