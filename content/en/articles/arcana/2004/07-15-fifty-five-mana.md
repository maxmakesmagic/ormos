Back when [Aladdin's Lamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040715000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aladdin%27s+Lamp) was originally printed in *Arabian Nights*, "10" could not be typeset into one mana symbol. Instead, it was printed as ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless5.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless5.gif).

 

![Aladdins Lamp](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Aladdins+Lamp)

When the Lamp was later reprinted in *Revised* and *Fourth Edition*, the newer ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless1.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless0.gif) mana symbol was used.

![Aladdins Lamp](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Aladdins+Lamp)

Since then, many cards have been printed with two-digit colorless mana costs:
