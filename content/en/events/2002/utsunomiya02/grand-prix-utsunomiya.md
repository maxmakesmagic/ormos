**Magic: The Gathering Pro Tour - Chicago Qualifier  
 October 12-13, 2002**

The **Magic: The Gathering** Grand Prix tournament series is an international circuit of large-scale Qualifier tournaments featuring cash prizes and multiple invitations to Pro Tour events.

### TOURNAMENT FORMAT AND SCHEDULE

The format for Grand Prix - Utsunomiya is *Onslaught* Block Sealed Deck, with Day 2 Rochester Draft. All DCI™ floor rules and Magic® Floor Rules announced on October 1, 2002, along with all subsequent updates, will be enforced. For the latest edition of the DCI and Magic Floor Rules, please refer to the [DCI Rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20130702061645/https://www.wizards.com/dci/main.asp?x=UTR_Intro) web site.

*   **Friday, October 11:** Registration from 15:00 - 22:00. All players must register by 22:00 Friday night. LATE REGISTRATION WILL NOT BE ALLOWED. Players that cannot meet this deadline should contact the organizer at the mail address listed below no later than October 9.
*   **Saturday, October 12:** Tournament begins at 09:00. The Top 64 players advance to the finals on Sunday.
*   **Sunday, October 13:** Finals begin at 09:00.

### TOURNAMENT

*   **Divisions**
     The main tournament at Grand Prix - Utsunomiya is open to **Magic** players of all ages.

    If a player that wins an invitation to Pro Tour - Chicago at Grand Prix - Utsunomiya is already qualified for an invitation, the invitation shall be extended to the next-best finisher. Previous qualification may occur through a Qualifier tournament, through performance at a professional tournament, or by being an invited DCI player (valid on the date of the official rank announcement for the appropriate professional tournament).

*   **Tournament Structure**
     The structure for Grand Prix - Utsunomiya is modified Swiss-style (50-minute rounds, with the number of rounds determined by the number of participants), with the Top 64 players advancing to the Sunday finals for prizes and invitations.

*   **Tournament Byes** *(Byes are not cumulative through the season or year.)*
     Byes for Grand Prix - Utsunomiya are based on the 2002 World Championship professional standings and DCI ratings as of August 21, 2002. Please refer to the [Grand Prix - Utsunomiya Bye List](/en/events/2002/utsunomiya02/grand-prix-utsunomiya-byes) for details.
