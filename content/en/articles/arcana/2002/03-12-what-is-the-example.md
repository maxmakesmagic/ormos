The rare *Odyssey* artifact [Charmed Pendant](https://web.archive.org/web/20020312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Charmed+Pendant) is one of a handful of cards that uses an example in its reminder text. The Pendant states that if you reveal a card that costs ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless3.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/black.gif), you add ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/black.gif) to your mana pool.

So what card is referred to in the example? Editing wanted to use a real, unique card when they were devising the example, so they chose the only card with a mana cost listed as ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless3.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/black.gif) -- [Spinal Embrace](https://web.archive.org/web/20020312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spinal+Embrace).

One other old card has a functionally identical mana cost: *Legends'* [Princess Lucrezia](https://web.archive.org/web/20020312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Princess+Lucrezia)'s cost is written as ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless3.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/black.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif). Back then, costs didn't follow the same rules they do now. Currently, all multi-colored mana costs are ordered clockwise around the color wheel. In the [Oracle reference](https://web.archive.org/web/20020312000000/http://archive.wizards.com/dci/main.asp?x=oracle), [Princess Lucrezia](https://web.archive.org/web/20020312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Princess+Lucrezia)'s mana cost appears in the current correct form -- identical to [Spinal Embrace](https://web.archive.org/web/20020312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spinal+Embrace)'s ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless3.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/black.gif).
