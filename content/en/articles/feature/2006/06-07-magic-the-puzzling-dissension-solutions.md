Here are the solutions to [Monday's Magic: The Puzzling puzzles](/en/articles/feature/2006/06-05-magic-the-puzzling-dissension-edition).

  
  

Puzzle 1 Solution:

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/a7/ef/a7ef0e8c49175c87d3dddbb727fadb71c503f715ad1731d6e5937179bb5c6bfc.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/bc/64/bc64e737b7c4da6403189fdd01d937191efc676c0443d4b8be0ce91a9a489dff.jpg)
1.   Play Commune with Nature, copying it with Twincast. Copy Twincast with Izzet Guildmage, which copies Commune with Nature.
2.   Allow the 1st Commune to resolve, put all 5 cards on bottom as x, x, x, x, Naturalize.
3.   Allow the 2nd Commune to resolve, put all 5 cards on bottom any order. Cytoshape on top of library now, 3rd Commune (original) still on stack.
4.   Using Novijen Sages' ability, remove 2 counters from Novijen Sages to draw a card (Cytoshape).
5.   Allow the 3rd Commune with Nature to resolve, put all 5 cards on bottom any order. Naturalize is on top of library now.
6.   Using Helium Squirter's ability, give Helium Squirter, Novijen Sages, and Vigean Graftmage flying.
7.   Remove a Jitte counter to give Helium Squirter +2/+2.
8.   Equip Vigean Graftmage with Jitte.
9.   Remove Jitte counter to give Vigean Graftmage +2/+2.
10.  Using Novijen Sages' ability, remove a counter from Helium Squirter and a counter from Vigean Graftmage to draw a card (Naturalize).
11.  Play Naturalize, targeting Privileged Position.
12.  Play Cytoshape, choosing Novijen Sages and targeting Stoic Ephemera (it will die, being a 0/0).
13.  Remove last Jitte counter to give Lantern Kami -1/-1 (it dies).
14.  Attack with Helium Squirter, Novijen Sages, and Vigean Graftmage (all 3 are flying). Opponent only has 2 flying blockers left (Isperia and Windreaver). Whichever attacker she does not block, tap Omnibian to make that attacker a 3/3 Frog. Rosa started at 3 life, so she dies.

Puzzle 2 Solution: (for 11 life)

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/90/43/90432076943ed4e55abf3adbbf8980d3872fc7cd631adbd56fb6b46e2acb16fd.jpg)](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/features/337_Diagram2_lg.jpg)
1.  Play Crypt Champion as a land. Attack with Sky Hussar for 4 (6 life). Play Valor Made Real targeting Sky Hussar (just to get rid of the card).
2.  On Barbara’s turn, her optimal play is make a 3rd token, then attack for 2.
3.  On your turn, tap ![2 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/f9/cc/f9cc8a15b009997919d58ad79964384351495d1b6c9231f938316577453038a8.gif)![Black Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/6b/e16bcf75cffdd1079b6ba1f9f43c9e6e7253bb89521acdf6b3e93d3c2e63dbb5.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif). Play Simic Growth Chamber, bouncing Crypt Champion.
4.  Play Crypt Champion (enhanced). In response to the spell, tap hellbent Ragamuffyn to sacrifice Rakdos Augermage/Land (the draw does nothing).
5.  Crypt Champion's CITP trigger reanimates Rakdos Augermage into play.
6.  Attack with Sky Hussar.
    1.  If opponent lets it through, she will be at 2 life. On her turn she makes a 4th token and 6/6 Pride of the Clouds. She cannot attack for the win. Your next attack of Crypt Champion, Rakdos Augermage, and Sky Hussar vs. 4 tokens and 6/6 Pride of the Clouds will leave your board position superior. You win.
    2.  Alternatively, opponent can chump the Sky Hussar. On her turn, she makes a 3rd token and 5/5 Pride of the Clouds. She attacks with two tokens. Your optimal play is to attack with all three creatures. She blocks Rakdos Augermage with Pride and chumps either Sky Hussar or Crypt Champion, letting the other 4 power creature through (2 life). She can only attack back for 6 with her remaining tokens and 4/4 Pride of the Clouds. Since this is not enough to kill you, you can keep attacking while she is forced to Chump, eventually running out of blockers.

Puzzle 3 Solution:

**Control Group:**  
 You want as many creatures alive as possible when Cytoplast Root-Kin comes into play. Therefore, don't move any +1/+1 counters via Graft. As the last creature, Cytoplast Root-Kin gives all 11 other creatures an extra counter each. Then use Cytoplast Root-Kin's ability to move everything to itself.

Total = \[Initial graft total\] + \[Number of other creatures besides Cytoplast Root-Kin\]  
 = 37 + 11  
 = 48

**Main Experiment:**  
 The only trick is to play creatures largest to smallest, so that none run out of counters. Play the Cytoplast Root-Kin last. You will end up with 294 counters. 

**Future Study:**  
 Arbitrarily large number. 

1.  Experiment Kraj has Cytoplast Root-Kin's activated ability to move a counter onto itself. Experiment Kraj moves a counter from Cytoplast Root-Kin onto itself (getting 2 counters).
2.  Cytoplast Root-Kin moves a counter to itself from Kraj (getting 2 counters).
3.  Repeat as many times as desired.

**Extra Challenge:**

Control Group: (SUM of Gi for i = 1 to n) + n + 4  
 Main Experiment: 4 \* (SUM of Gi for i = 1 to n) + n^2 + 3\*n + 8  
 Future Study: Still arbitrarily large.

Puzzle 4 Solution:

Solution for 9 lands (1 Plains, 1 Swamp, 1 Mountain, 3 Forest, 3 Island).  
 You start with 15 cards in your deck (Breeding Pool, Seal of Fire x3, Ghost Quarter, Utopia Sprawl, Loaming Shaman, Vision Skeins x2, Skyscribing x3, and Research // Development x3).

1.   Draw Vision Skeins.
2.   Tap your lands for 9 mana.
3.   Cast Vision Skeins, drawing Vision Skeins and Research // Development.
4.   Cast Research // Development fetching Early Harvest x4.
5.   Cast Vision Skeins, drawing Early Harvest and Skyscribing.
6.   Cast Early Harvest, untapping all of your lands.
7.   Cast Skyscribing for 4, drawing Research // Development, 2 Early Harvest, and Skyscribing.
8.   Cast Early Harvest and tap your lands for 9 mana.
9.   Cast Research // Development, fetching Samurai of the Pale Curtain, Ley Druid, Freed from the Real, and Surge of Zeal.
10.  Cast Early Harvest and tap your lands for a total of 13 mana.
11.  Cast Skyscribing for 8, drawing Ley Druid, Freed from the Real, Surge of Zeal, Breeding Pit, Utopia Sprawl, Early Harvest, Research // Development, and Skyscribing.
12.  Cast Early Harvest and tap your lands for 9 mana.
13.  Cast Ley Druid.
14.  Cast Freed from the Real, targeting Ley Druid.
15.  Play Breeding Pool (tapped).
16.  Enchant Breeding Pool with Utopia Sprawl, choosing Blue.
17.  Cast Surge of Zeal, targeting Ley Druid.
18.  At this point, you can use the Ley Druid to untap Breeding Pool. The Breeding Pool will generate ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) each time you tap it. You can untap Ley Druid for ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif), generating an unlimited amount of blue mana. Using this mana, you can untap Ley Druid to untap any other land, generating an unlimited amount of mana of any color.
19.  Cast Research // Development, fetching Nantuko Husk, Zombify, and Quicken.
20.  Cast Skyscribing, drawing Nantuko Husk, Zombify, Quicken, Seal of Doom, 2 Seal of Fire, Ghost Quarter, and Samurai of the Pale Curtain.
