Want to see what the time rifts delivered in terms of tournament playables? Here’s a chart of the numbers of *Time Spiral* timeshifted cards played in main boards and sideboards at [Standard Champs](https://web.archive.org/web/20061106000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/champs) 2006. This only counts [decklists](/en/articles/event-coverage/2006/10-31-2006-champs-standard-map) from North America Champs locations. At last count, Akroma tops the list in appearing in the most decks, but [Call of the Herd](https://web.archive.org/web/20061106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Call+of+the+Herd) gets the nod for most-played card!
