### **Name**: Pascal Maynard

**Age**: 21

**Occupation**: Game Store Manager at Botique Donjon

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**: Five GP 8

**What colors did you play on Day 1? What were your most important cards?**  
 Sultai splashing red. [Sandsteppe Mastodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sandsteppe+Mastodon).

**What colors did you play in the first draft? What were your most important cards?**  
 Blue/Red Tempo. [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise).

**What colors did you play in the second draft? What were your most important cards?**  
 Red/White Aggro. [Ghostfire Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghostfire+Blade).

**What card do you most hope to open from Fate Reforged in the Top 8 draft?**  
[Mistfire Adept](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistfire+Adept).

### **Name**: Martin Jůza

**Age**: 27

**Occupation**: Tourist

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**: Won a Constructed GP with [Shimmering Grotto](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shimmering+Grotto) in my deck. I also have more than twenty GP Top 8s.

**What colors did you play on Day 1? What were your most important cards?**  
 Green/Black. [Sandsteppe Mastodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sandsteppe+Mastodon).

 Black/White/Green. [Sandsteppe Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sandsteppe+Citadel).

**What colors did you play in the second draft? What were your most important cards?**  
 Blue/White/Red. [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider).

 One of the two color dragons.

### **Name**: Chapman Sim

**Age**: 29

**Occupation**: Event coverage writer and tourist! (Like Martin Jůza)

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**: Top 8 at Grand Prix Shizouka 2014. Finalist at Grand Prix Singapore 2013. Being on the best team ever, MTG Mint Card!

**What colors did you play on Day 1? What were your most important cards?**  
 Abzan. [Crux of Fate](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crux+of+Fate). Maindeck [Return to the Earth](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Return+to+the+Earth).

**What colors did you play in the first draft? What were your most important cards?**  
 Sultai. Double [Icy Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Icy+Blast) and [Sandsteppe Mastodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sandsteppe+Mastodon)!

**What colors did you play in the second draft? What were your most important cards?**  
 Abzan. [Gurmag Angler](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gurmag+Angler), a card that Lee Shi Tian, Huang-Hao Shan, and Zhang Meng Qui tipped me onto.

**What card do you most hope to open from Fate Reforged in the Top 8 draft?**  
[Abzan Beastmaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Beastmaster). The card is insane, and I wouldn’t have made Top 8 without it.

### **Name**: Juan Carlo Botis

**Age**: 29

**Occupation**: Psychologist

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**: None

**What colors did you play on Day 1? What were your most important cards?**  
 Abzan. [Palace Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Palace+Siege) was a key card, enabling comebacks from difficult situations.

**What colors did you play in the first draft? What were your most important cards?**  
 Jeskai. [Will of the Naga](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Will+of+the+Naga), [Force Away](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+Away), [Alabaster Kirin](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Alabaster+Kirin), and [Lotus Path Djinn](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotus+Path+Djinn).

**What colors did you play in the second draft? What were your most important cards?**  
 Blue/White. [Crippling Chill](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crippling+Chill), Slinging Bell Strike, and [Channel Harm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Channel+Harm).

**What card do you most hope to open from Fate Reforged in the Top 8 draft?**  
 Any card that will get the job done!
