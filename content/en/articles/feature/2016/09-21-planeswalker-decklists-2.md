Starting this weekend at your *Kaladesh* Prerelease, you can also start picking up the new Planewswalker Decks featuring unique versions of Chandra and Nissa. The decks are meant for newer players, but can be fun for anyone to battle them against one another.

You can check out the [exclusive cards found in these decks here](/en/articles/card-preview/2016/09-13-exclusive-cards-in-the-planeswalker-decks). Enjoy!
