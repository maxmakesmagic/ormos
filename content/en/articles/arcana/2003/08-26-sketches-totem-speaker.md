This week's "Sketches" takes a look at the illustration of Totem Speaker from *Legions*. Darrell Riche was the man at the helm for the project.

### 1\. Art Description

These were the instructions given to the artist:

"Location: The Wirewood Forest  
 Action: Show an elf emerging from the forest alongside a couple of mammal-like, fantasy forest beasts. They are clearly traveling together.  
 Notes: the elf is a cleric and so should be carring a staff and no weapons."

### 2\. Sketches

Here is Darrell's original sketch. The two creatures shown alongside the Elf Druid are odd critters -- beasts, but not quite *Legions* beasts.

![Totem Speaker sketch](https://i.ormos.uk/3c/6d/3c6d6cf23ceb03c2a9f269a79a7c178283b1f602873109ab62ef4040fbfffb06.jpg)

First, the art team wanted to see bonespurs -- thick spikes made of bone -- coming out of the Speaker's companions, since they had to be recognizably beasts for the art to make sense; see also Enormous Baloth and Feral Throwback. Second, they wanted the elf himself to correspond to the *Legions* look for elves -- see also Wirewood Channeler and Elvish Soultiller. That meant a change to his chin (spikier) and hair (made of vines), and to be sure his skin ended up greenish, since by *Legions*, elves had acquired many characteristics of their buddies, the trees. 

### 3\. Final Art

Here is a large version of the final piece. The elf's look is much more in line with other elves in the set, and his creature friends have acquired some token bone spurs.

![Totem Speaker final art](https://i.ormos.uk/ca/47/ca47de0411fb507b61995ddac76025e9343e4eeab3af899770673013d9b9958d.jpg)
