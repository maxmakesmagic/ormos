### Some Silliness From The Producer

* * *

Twas the day before Christmas,  
 And all through Wizards,  
 Not a creature was stirring,  
 Not even Leaping Lizards.

In my cube I sat  
 Reading the Message Board,  
 While players entered tournaments  
 Like a Balduvian Hoarde.

The DCI finished it’s list  
 Of players naughty and nice.  
 R&D was still arguing  
 About replacing squirrels with mice.

I was about to go home  
 When I heard a strange sound.  
 So I went to the mana pool  
 To see who was around.

A sled drawn by antelopes  
 Plowed in through the rain.  
 When they landed by the pool  
 It turned into a plain.

From the sled hopped a man  
 Dressed in boots and red hat.  
 Looking at the ground he said,  
 “I hate when they do that.”

He stood looking about  
 In his red rain slicker.  
 He looked just like Urza,  
 Only just a bit thicker.

Seeing me there  
 He said not a word.  
 I knew he was thinking,  
 "Here’s a **Magic** nerd."

He handed me a binder  
 Much worn at the spine.  
 "Don’t get too excited,  
 I’m keeping my Power Nine."

As I stood there in awe,  
 He climbed into his sleigh,  
 Then picked up the reins  
 And started to say:

"On Jet! On Ruby!  
 On Emerald and Sapphire!  
 Pearl, you silly Mox,  
 You run like a flat tire!"

"On 'twister! On Time Walk!  
 On Lotus and Recall!  
 Now dash away, dash away,  
 Dash away all!"

They cut through the fog  
 And rose through the sky.  
 All I could think was,  
 "What a strange guy."

As they flew to the west  
 He never looked back.  
 I suppose he was too busy  
 Dodging planes from SeaTac.

He called once more  
 While avoiding a wreck:  
 "Merry Christmas to all,  
 and to all a sweet topdeck!"

Happy holidays to all the ***Magic** Online* players. You make this job worth doing.

Sincerely,  
 Daniel Myers
