*What's your favorite instant in **Magic**, and why?*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/bc/df/bcdf4b16b64014da6e91f4589e9d89fc4a883b0d88977709b0ce75e68e8ef0a3.jpg)

**Tom Lapille:** My favorite instant in Magic is [Mystical Teachings](https://web.archive.org/web/20101026000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystical+Teachings). *Time Spiral* Block Constructed is my favorite Block format that I played when I was playing in the real world, and the deck that [Mystical Teachings](https://web.archive.org/web/20101026000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystical+Teachings) enabled is one of my favorite decks I've ever played. Having all the right answers, winning long games by grinding out marginal advantages, and playing control decks are among the things in **Magic** that make me feel the smartest, and I like feeling smart a lot, and the [Mystical Teachings](https://web.archive.org/web/20101026000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystical+Teachings) deck had all those things in some of the highest quantities I'd ever seen.

![Mystical Teachings](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Mystical+Teachings)

  

I also have good memories of the Pro Tour Qualifier I won for Pro Tour–Valencia with the deck. The version I had at that time was one that I had gotten from Gerry Thompson. I don't remember all the details, but it looked a lot like this:
