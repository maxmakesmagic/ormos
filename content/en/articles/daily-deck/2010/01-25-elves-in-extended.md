Elves took the top title during last year's Extended season at [Pro Tour–Berlin](/en/events/2008/ptber08/lsv-elves-equals-champion). This season it's making a comeback! In the hands of Pro Tour champion and Daily MTG columnist Jake Van Lunen, this take on Elves was good enough to earn the blue envelope at a [Pro Tour Qualifier](/en/events/2009/sanjuan10-qualifiers/pro-toursan-juan-2010-qualifiers) in Connecticut. Joining Jake in the Top 8? Chris Lachmann, his Two-Headed Giant teammate and co-champion at 2007's [Pro Tour–San Diego](/en/events/2007/ptsdg07/sliver-kids-stun-san-diego).
