### Game 1

Mono Red Control vs. Affinity

石田先攻で始まり、そこから立て続けに《静電気の稲妻/[Electrostatic Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrostatic+Bolt)》《酸化/[Oxidize](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oxidize)》《ヴィリジアンのシャーマン/[Viridian Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viridian+Shaman)》 \* 2 とキャストし続けて、ラストの 6 ターン目には《炉のドラゴン/[Furnace Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Furnace+Dragon)》キャスト。（ タリスマン 2 枚と《大焼炉/[Great Furnace](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Great+Furnace)》 ）

Caumes に 3 枚目のランドを置かせない猛攻で 1 本目を圧勝する。

**石田 1 – 0 Caumes**

### Game 2

Game 1 のうさを晴らすかの様に 1 ターン目から《大霊堂の信奉者/[Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault)》、 2 ターン目に《大霊堂の信奉者/[Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault)》《頭蓋骨絞め/[Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp)》と展開して一気に攻める Caumes。

石田も《静電気の稲妻/[Electrostatic Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrostatic+Bolt)》で 1 体の《大霊堂の信奉者/[Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault)》を除去するものの後続が続かず、思ったよりマナが伸びない。《減衰のマトリックス/[Damping Matrix](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Damping+Matrix)》で《電結の荒廃者/[Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager)》の能力そのものは封じるが、置いたときに 3 / 3 になっているためそれのダメージ自体がとても辛い。

《酸化/[Oxidize](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oxidize)》や《ヴィリジアンのシャーマン/[Viridian Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viridian+Shaman)》でゆっくりと体勢を立て直そうとするのだがその度に《大霊堂の信奉者/[Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault)》からの 1 点が響き最後まで生き残った 1 体の《大霊堂の信奉者/[Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault)》がどうしようもなく Caumes に押し切られる形となる。

**石田 1 – 1 Caumes**

### Game 3

勢いに乗ったのか Caumes は 1 ターン目から《羽ばたき飛行機械/[Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter)》《溶接の壺/[Welding Jar](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Welding+Jar)》《金属ガエル/[Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite)》と一気に展開。次のターンには《霊気の薬瓶/AEther Vial》《マイアの処罰者/[Myr Enforcer](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Enforcer)》と追加していわゆるぶん回りの状態になる。

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/ptkob04/r8fm1_2.jpg)

対して石田側の手札を見ると、《弧炎撒き/[Arc-Slogger](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc-Slogger)》が 2 体に《静電気の稲妻/[Electrostatic Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrostatic+Bolt)》が 1 枚で後は土地･･････。

Caumes は 3 ターン目に《電結の働き手/[Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker)》と手は止まらずに、石田がようやく《弧炎撒き/[Arc-Slogger](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc-Slogger)》を出すと、返しに《大霊堂の信奉者/[Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault)》を出してゲームセット。

石田格、無念の初日落ち。

**Final Results : Benjamin Caumes Win**
