We're still a couple of weeks away from the release of *Duel Decks: Sorin vs. Tibalt*. To be precise, it comes out on March 15. That's the Ides of March, for those of you still on the Roman calendar. So we're not quite ready to show you everything in it. But we can show you what it looks like!

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/68/e5/68e5fb29d2337c4002b6bae2359e2d205344e24f87fdbdd0ed17186e5bfa8075.jpg)
