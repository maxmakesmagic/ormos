Sometimes when cards are reprinted, there are little snafus in the files that no one catches. In the case of the *Chronicles* Wall of Shadows, the victim was its expansion symbol.

![Wall of Shadows](http://www.wizards.com/global/images/mtgcom_arcana_142_pic1_en.jpg)

Wall of Shadows originally appeared in the *Legends* expansion, but when it was reprinted, the symbol was accidentally changed to that of *Antiquities*.
