Welcome to **Wallpaper of the Week**! It's Azorius Week, and we've got an Azorius wallpaper for you! And if that's not enough, [the Azorius page](https://web.archive.org/web/20130919162341/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/guilds/azorius) has a fan kit that includes wallpapers for Isperia, Supreme Judge, New Prahv Guildmage, and Hallowed Fountain! But first, we've got David Rapoza's majestic Archon of the Triumvirate. Enjoy. 

Check out the [Azorius Fan Kit](https://web.archive.org/web/20130919162341/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/guilds/azorius) for the other wallpapers!
