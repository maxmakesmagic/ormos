Luis Scott-Vargas is one of the game's very best players, and he recently earned a spot in the Finals of the StarCityGames.com Standard Open with a green-blue Turboland deck that has begun seeing increased success in Standard. How does the deck work? You play out lots of extra lands with Oracle of Mul Daya, Explore, and Rampant Growth. Those lands let you abuse Avenger of Zendikar, and Time Warp lets you take extra turns to do that! Fun.
