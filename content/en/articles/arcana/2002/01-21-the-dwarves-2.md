If you've been paying attention, you should have noticed that dwarves have replaced goblins as the red race of choice in the *Odyssey* block, evident in cards like Dwarven Strike Force and Whipkeeper.

Below are two images from the *Odyssey* styleguide that show how our artists conceptualized the Otarian dwarves. Click on each for a larger image.
