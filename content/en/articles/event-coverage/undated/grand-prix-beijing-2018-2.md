## ARTICLES COVERAGE

Day 1 Coverage

Dominaria is a historic plane with a deep and fascinating history, where primordial dragons clashed and planeswalkers fought cataclysmic duels. Many Dominarians sought to save their world from utter obliteration, while other tried to further their own agendas.Here in Beijing, over 900 aspirants gathered to do the same. However, only one could be crowned champion.  
  
 In the tournament hall today were numerous legends in their own right, including Hall of Famers (5) Yuuya Watanabe and (5) Shota Yasooka (both tied as the highest ranked players in the room), defending champion (14) Kelvin Chew, (18) Ken Yukuhiro as well as numerous other Grand Prix Champions, National Champions, grizzled veterans, and so on.

As we speak, players are excitedly ripping open six fresh *Dominaria* booster packs, attempting to build the most powerful Sealed Deck they possibly can. Check back later as we bring you more updates on this legendary weekend!
