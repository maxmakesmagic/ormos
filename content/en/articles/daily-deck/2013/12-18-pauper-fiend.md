Pauper is **Magic Online**'s format that uses only commons, but that doesn't mean it isn't a format capable of quick wins. \_Shaper\_ finished 2nd in a Pauper Premier Event with a Pauper version of a deck that current **Magic** R&D intern [Gerry Thompson played at Pro Tour *Return to Ravnica*](/en/events/2012/ptrtr12/video-deck-tech-elemental-combo-with-gerry-thompson). This deck's plan is to get a Kiln Fiend or Nivix Cyclops on the battlefield and then play a lot of cheap instants and sorceries to quickly make its power huge.

 Sr. Business Intelligence Analyst—**Magic Online**
