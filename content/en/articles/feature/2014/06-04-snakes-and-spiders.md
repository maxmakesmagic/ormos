*Theros* Block is sprinkled with cards that interact with your graveyard. Cards like [Commune with the Gods](https://web.archive.org/web/20140604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Commune+with+the+Gods) and [Nyx Weaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20140604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nyx+Weaver) help fill your graveyard. [Nighthowler](https://web.archive.org/web/20140604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nighthowler) and [Pharika, God of Affliction](https://web.archive.org/web/20140604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pharika%2C+God+of+Affliction) are more powerful as your graveyard grows in size.

Once you take advantage of the cross-block synergy provided by the Golgari guild in *Return to Ravnica*, a black-green self-mill deck becomes very appealing. [Grisly Salvage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grisly+Salvage) and [Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord](https://web.archive.org/web/20140604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jarad%2C+Golgari+Lich+Lord) combine well with the strategies enabled by *Theros* Block. The end result is a synergy-driven Standard deck that is both fun and powerful.
