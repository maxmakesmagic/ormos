### **Name**: Robbie Schmidt

**Age:** 25

**Occupation:** Cook

**Hometown:** Calgary, AL

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Couple PTQ Top 8s; Day 2 Grand Prix Calgary; Provincial Champ 2014

**What deck are you playing? Why did you choose it? What is its worst matchup?**

Robots. Couple years’ experience with the deck. But it’s sadly not [Scapeshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scapeshift). I wanted to play a proactive deck that I had a bunch of reps with. Worst Matchup: Stock Abzan, maybe Infect.

**What was your Day 1 Record? How many byes did you have? How did you earn them?**

9-0, baby! I had 1 bye from Planeswalker points.

**What was the best moment from your games so far this weekend?**

Turn two [Etched Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Etched+Champion); turn three [Etched Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Etched+Champion); Turn four [Etched Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Etched+Champion)

**Knowing the decks you’ve faced, would you have made any changes to your deck?**

My best card against Abzan is [Etched Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Etched+Champion) so if I could play a fifth, I guess I would have.

### **Name**: Alexander Hayne

**Age:** 26

**Occupation:** Pro *Magic* player

**Hometown:** Montreal, Quebec, Canada

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* champion. Three Grand Prix wins and two other Top 8s. 2011–12 Rookie of the Year; 2012 & 2014 Canadian National team member. Just passed $100,000 USD lifetime winnings.

**What deck are you playing? Why did you choose it? What is its worst matchup?**

Amulet Bloom. I chose it because I was winning a lot in testing it as the enemy for Team F2F+CFB for Pro Tour *Fate Reforged*, and it is a lot of fun to play. The worst match-up is Mono-White Disruption, followed by [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) (or any deck that supports [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon).

**What was your Day 1 Record? How many byes did you have? How did you earn them?**

I was 8-1. I had three byes from Platinum Pro level.

**What was the best moment from your games so far this weekend?**

Killing my opponent turn two with two Amulets + [Summer Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summer+Bloom) + [Summoner's Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoner%27s+Pact), finding two [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan)s and giving them both haste, +2/+0, and double strike. My opponent had played one land.

**Knowing the decks you’ve faced, would you have made any changes to your deck?**

I would have played more Infect and Twin hate, probably some more Nature’s Claims.

### **Name**: Dan Lanthier

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Powerful Wizard

**Hometown:** Ottawa

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Canadian National champion; several Grand Prix Top 8 near misses.

**What deck are you playing? Why did you choose it? What is its worst matchup?**

[Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin). I’m very familiar with the archetype and felt it was well positioned. Abzan is my worst match-up by far.

**What was your Day 1 Record? How many byes did you have? How did you earn them?**

8-0-1. I had two byes from Planeswalker Points.

**What was the best moment from your games so far this weekend?**

Dying on turn one to Amulet Bloom.

**Knowing the decks you’ve faced, would you have made any changes to your deck?**

[Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember) was not very good. I would consider a [Combust](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Combust) or another [Flame Slash](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flame+Slash) in its place.

### **Name**: Tom Martell

**Age:** 32  
**Occupation:** Attorney/Entrepreneur

**Hometown:** Mountain View, California

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 Runner-Up 2013 Player of the Year; Pro Tour Gatecrash champion; Grand Prix Sacramento and Indianapolis champion

**What deck are you playing? Why did you choose it? What is its worst matchup?**  
 Infect—it’s the most consistent & powerful of the unfair decks. Worst matchup is burn.

**What was your Day 1 Record? How many byes did you have? How did you earn them?**  
 7-2 with three byes. Platinum level pro.

**What was the best moment from your games so far this weekend?**  
 Did 15 damage with an unblockable [Noble Hierarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noble+Hierarch) when my opponent was at 15 with lethal on the board next turn.

 Sideboard: -1 [Piracy Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Piracy+Charm), -1 [Carrion Call](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Carrion+Call); +2 [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember).
