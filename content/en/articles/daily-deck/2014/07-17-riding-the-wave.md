While devotion is primarily a Standard mechanic, the vast card pool of Modern has many more cards that would enable devotion cards.

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Master%20of%20Waves)

If you wanted to build a [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140717000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) deck for Modern, naturally you would look for cards that have mostly blue mana symbols in their cost. Looking through the Modern card pool shows a theme—Merfolk! [Lord of Atlantis](https://web.archive.org/web/20140717000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lord+of+Atlantis) and [Master of the Pearl Trident](https://web.archive.org/web/20140717000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+the+Pearl+Trident) are both efficient "lords" that boost all other Merfolk. [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140717000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) is even a Merfolk!

Once you have this core, you want to fill your deck with the right balance of additional Merfolk and efficient interaction. Dismember, [Relic of Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140717000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Relic+of+Progenitus), and [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20140717000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) are capable of keeping an opponent off balance enough for your Merfolk to swim through the red zone. [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20140717000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) serves double duty, disabling opposing mana bases and providing an Island to make your Islandwalking Merfolk unblockable.

You have many options to build a Merfolk deck, even in Mono-Blue.
