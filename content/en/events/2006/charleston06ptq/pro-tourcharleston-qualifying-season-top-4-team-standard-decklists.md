![](https://www.wizards.com/protour/images/qt_lg.jpg) Gather up your friends and earn your tickets to Charleston by winning a Team Pro Tour Qualifier! 

Team Standard makes its debut on the Qualifier circuit, coming on the heels of [Pro Tour-Honolulu](https://web.archive.org/web/20130701070904/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=)'s format-defining weekend. This is a unified constructed format, meaning no more than four versions of any non-basic land card can be in an entire team's set of decks.

You can test each decklist thanks to our [Sample Hand Generator](https://web.archive.org/web/20130624094147/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/294). Just click on the card fan in the upper right of each decklist and you can see what your opening hand could be, along with mulligans and the first three cards you'd draw.

Decklists will be updated weekly during the PTQ season. To find a PTQ near you, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20130701094827/https://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/events/protour?x=protour/charleston06/qualifiers). [Pro Tour-Charleston](https://web.archive.org/web/20130701094827/https://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/events/protour?x=protour/charleston06/facts) is scheduled for June 16-18, 2006.
