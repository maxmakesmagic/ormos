## Twisted Visions

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/e8/0e/e80e011467a8372e443d9bb72ee904e10cb8c901706ea512c9fbac8fe4b7f6f5.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/e8/0e/e80e011467a8372e443d9bb72ee904e10cb8c901706ea512c9fbac8fe4b7f6f5.jpg "The plane of Innistrad has always been treacherous for humanity. ### Traverse the Ulvenwald

#### The plane of Innistrad has always been treacherous for humanity. From the werewolves of the winding Ulvenwald forests to the vampires of mountainous Stensia, danger abounds.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/47/7a/477ade3c380463d25662f5c95bd67ec4ef3ae7d426a9d8a42137918558a281c8.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/47/7a/477ade3c380463d25662f5c95bd67ec4ef3ae7d426a9d8a42137918558a281c8.jpg "In these dangerous conditions, the Archangel Avacyn was created by Sorin Markov to maintain the balance between the monstrous predators of Innistrad and their prey by protecting humanity...")

### Archangel Avacyn

#### In these dangerous conditions, the Archangel Avacyn was created by Sorin Markov to maintain the balance between the monstrous predators of Innistrad and their prey by protecting humanity...

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/83/09/83091802ee144218898eaec0f0d0aa1c5208163ab0699b767ee7c594ee2a64e8.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/83/09/83091802ee144218898eaec0f0d0aa1c5208163ab0699b767ee7c594ee2a64e8.jpg "Incarnate forms of white mana, Avacyn and her angels were driven mad when Nahiri warped the plane's ley lines to summon forth Emrakul. Unable to identify the source of their madness, the angels lashed out against all living things.")

### Avacyn, the Purifier

#### Incarnate forms of white mana, Avacyn and her angels were driven mad when Nahiri warped the plane's ley lines to summon forth Emrakul. Unable to identify the source of their madness, the angels lashed out against all living things.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/9c/5b/9c5b76f45580ae3ce0019df355a230dc0ca28bb07d9ee06165dc39b7306c84f0.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/9c/5b/9c5b76f45580ae3ce0019df355a230dc0ca28bb07d9ee06165dc39b7306c84f0.jpg "With the madness of the angels and other strange happenings across Innistrad, this tendency toward dangerous insanity has risen to a fever pitch.")

### Avacyn, stained glass window

#### With the madness of the angels and other strange happenings across Innistrad, this tendency toward dangerous insanity has risen to a fever pitch.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/66/f0/66f0092287dfe76907219c4d9cd77b5ee3947b5662bd69bd214c2bda9a0baba3.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/66/f0/66f0092287dfe76907219c4d9cd77b5ee3947b5662bd69bd214c2bda9a0baba3.jpg "Well-meaning scholars who begin to pry into the reasons behind recent events grow obsessed with their search, unable to stop even as the knowledge they gain begins to twist their minds.")

### Manic Scribe

#### Well-meaning scholars who begin to pry into the reasons behind recent events grow obsessed with their search, unable to stop even as the knowledge they gain begins to twist their minds.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/44/81/44814c3e34d4daf773866cada8dbbaf516d3895659955ba8fff4b58fffa68ede.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/44/81/44814c3e34d4daf773866cada8dbbaf516d3895659955ba8fff4b58fffa68ede.jpg "Perhaps these people also happened upon forbidden lore, or maybe they practiced secret rituals at twisted stones that shattered their minds.")

### Westvale Cult Leader

#### Perhaps these people also happened upon forbidden lore, or maybe they practiced secret rituals at twisted stones that shattered their minds.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/8c/6b/8c6becd6380619c9eaa251195aa61e3372fc315ebc945e6f7c69662d7e3864ae.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/8c/6b/8c6becd6380619c9eaa251195aa61e3372fc315ebc945e6f7c69662d7e3864ae.jpg "Ordinary townsfolk can be equally stricken, often growing violent. Some seem to be innocent bystanders, unable to cope with the terrors of a world where the protectors of humanity have become destroyers.")

### Paranoid Parish-Blade

#### Ordinary townsfolk can be equally stricken, often growing violent. Some seem to be innocent bystanders, unable to cope with the terrors of a world where the protectors of humanity have become destroyers.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/42/10/42109c26e973459a9db80d6435696a28c088d86370782181c046632d4c000613.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/42/10/42109c26e973459a9db80d6435696a28c088d86370782181c046632d4c000613.jpg "Nahiri used her mastery of stone and earth to construct cryptoliths that channeled the flow of mana through the plane. In Sorin Markov's ancestral estate, she warped existing buildings into elaborate, gravity-defying patterns.")

### Foreboding Ruins

#### Nahiri used her mastery of stone and earth to construct cryptoliths that channeled the flow of mana through the plane. In Sorin Markov's ancestral estate, she warped existing buildings into elaborate, gravity-defying patterns.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/3f/59/3f59df7d9bb40d7c9d74930a486f57b58f41ca67c2b34759917dd9c4db807519.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/3f/59/3f59df7d9bb40d7c9d74930a486f57b58f41ca67c2b34759917dd9c4db807519.jpg "Sorin arrived to find not only his home destroyed, but members of his vampiric bloodline encased in stone -- a declaration of Nahiri's vengeful intent.")

### Declaration in Stone

#### Sorin arrived to find not only his home destroyed, but members of his vampiric bloodline encased in stone -- a declaration of Nahiri's vengeful intent.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/fd/c4/fdc46faeef1fe93b0e6b57dd1cd9022ecfad27e972085dffe6ca51d1b780411e.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/fd/c4/fdc46faeef1fe93b0e6b57dd1cd9022ecfad27e972085dffe6ca51d1b780411e.jpg "Jace Beleren tried to unravel the causes for these events with the help of the moonfolk scholar, Tamiyo. Their investigations lead them to Thraben, where they were attacked by the mad archangel Avacyn herself.")

#### Jace Beleren tried to unravel the causes for these events with the help of the moonfolk scholar, Tamiyo. Their investigations lead them to Thraben, where they were attacked by the mad archangel Avacyn herself.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/73/e1/73e15db6e2d34440b84b2e3611fc83b31e5a4b5886498b01973cc0b6e9a0d3dc.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/73/e1/73e15db6e2d34440b84b2e3611fc83b31e5a4b5886498b01973cc0b6e9a0d3dc.jpg "As Avacyn attacked, Sorin Markov intervened. Convinced there was no alternative, Sorin unmade Avacyn, thus dissolving the last of the plane's protections and unwittingly allowing Emrakul to fully enter Innistrad.")

### Anguished Unmaking

#### As Avacyn attacked, Sorin Markov intervened. Convinced there was no alternative, Sorin unmade Avacyn, thus dissolving the last of the plane's protections and unwittingly allowing Emrakul to fully enter Innistrad.
