Check out the latest cards revealed from the [*March of the Machine*](https://web.archive.org/web/20230329000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/march-of-the-machine) Multiverse Legends bonus sheet below. To see even more cards from *March of the Machine*, you can visit the following galleries:

*   [*March of the Machine* Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/03-29-march-of-the-machine-card-image-gallery)
*   [*March of the Machine* Variant Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/03-29-march-of-the-machine-variant-card-image-gallery)
*   [*March of the Machine* Commander Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/03-29-march-of-the-machine-commander-card-image-gallery)
*   [*March of the Machine* Planechase Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/03-29-march-of-the-machine-planechase-card-image-gallery), with Planechase cards exclusive to *March of the Machine* Commander decks

You can learn more about collecting extended-art and other Collector Booster–exclusive cards with the breakdown over in our [Collecting *March of the Machine* article](/en/articles/feature/2023/03-29-collecting-march-of-the-machine).

Note that Multiverse Legends collector numbers 66 through 130 feature the foil-etched treatment and collector numbers 131 through 195 feature the Halo foil treatment.

*March of the Machine* releases April 21 and will be available at your [local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20230329000000/https://locator.wizards.com/), online through [Amazon](https://gtly.to/XkWuLY3ab), and elsewhere *Magic* is sold.
