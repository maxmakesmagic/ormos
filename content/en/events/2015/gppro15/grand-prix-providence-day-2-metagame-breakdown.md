Some features of Standard have held stable since *Dragons of Tarkir* joined the format. Here, Abzan-focused decks account for 28% of the field. It’s not quite dominating, but it makes something packing [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) also unavoidable for players.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Siege+Rhino)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino)

Midrange *is* dominating the weekend. Over a third of the field is playing decks like Abzan Midrange, Red-Green Devotion, and newer flavors like Ojutai Jeskai. Megamorph decks – that is, those using the dynamic duo of [Deathmist Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathmist+Raptor) and [Den Protector](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Den+Protector) – range across the aggressive to more controlling spectrum, meaning seeing the two together doesn’t necessarily make the rest of the deck follow.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Deathmist+Raptor)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathmist+Raptor)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Den+Protector)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Den+Protector)

However, [Den Protector](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Den+Protector) is more universally played than [Deathmist Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathmist+Raptor), making its way into everything from aggressive [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company) builds to Abzan and Bant-flavored control decks.

Another card that’s made a move this weekend is Dragonlord Ojutai. While it’s been a mainstay of the fallen-out-of-favor Esper Dragons control deck, others have taken it up as a new angle to going over the top. The Ojutai Jeskai decks use it over something like [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion) as its top end; Bant decks generally include a copy or three to cap out its offensive potential, and seeing the white-blue Dragonlord shoehorned into Abzan builds means the Five-Color Dragons technology has trickled down into decks looking for an extra angle of attack.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Dragonlord+Ojutai)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Atarka%27s+Command)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Atarka%27s+Command)

On the aggressive side, Atarka Red – the mono-red deck sliding in [Atarka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Atarka%27s+Command)s and a copy or two of [Become Immense](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Become+Immense) – is in the same boat as the Heroic decks: outshined by the Abzan approach. [Rakshasa Deathdealer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Deathdealer) and [Heir of the Wilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heir+of+the+Wilds) alongside [Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150621000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion) is the core troops Abzan Aggro leads with, those the overlap with the megamorph package makes it a fuzzy math to tease out any exact numbers.

Standard is an interesting spot, providing a wide range of threats and plans for players to mix and match from. While that’s put a lot of pressure on control decks –often more things that need to be answered than what’s possible – it’s made the midrange bucket burst with potential. Whether that remained the case as we close in on Top 8 is the question every player is asking.
