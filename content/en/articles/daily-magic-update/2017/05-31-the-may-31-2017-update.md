# Deck of the Day

1.  [BSWeitz's Electrostatic Pummeler](/en/events/2017/competitive-standard-constructed-league-2017-05-30/competitive-standard-constructed-league)

We've seen an uptick lately in [Electrostatic Pummeler](https://web.archive.org/web/20170531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrostatic+Pummeler) decks doing well in Competitive Standard Leagues, and, for once, we can trace exactly the reasons why.

First, the deck is just explosive. It can kill by turn four with some regularity, and even if it's not that fast, it puts players under pretty serious clocks pretty quickly.

But, second, it has to deal with fewer predators these days—namely the Copy Cat combo (which was just as fast, but more disruptive) or [Walking Ballista](https://web.archive.org/web/20170531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Walking+Ballista) (which is seen far less frequently). Ballista was bad news for the namesake [Electrostatic Pummeler](https://web.archive.org/web/20170531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrostatic+Pummeler) specifically, and often for pump spells generally. But with those two hurdles out of the way, racing [Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel) has become a viable solution. Add in the fact that the deck is very, very fast and punishing, and you've got a winning formula.
