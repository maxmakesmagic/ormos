So what decks did the pro players pick for this Grand Prix? What did some of the game's biggest stars chose to run in Modern? Yesterday, [we had promised](/en/events/2014/gpmad14/the-pro-metagame) full decklists for all of the Platinum- and Gold-level players as well as all of the Hall of Famers in attendance, and here they are. 

Frank Karsten, playing Affinity, and Timothée Simonot, playing Burn, both missed Day 2, but all of the others made it. Check out their decks!
