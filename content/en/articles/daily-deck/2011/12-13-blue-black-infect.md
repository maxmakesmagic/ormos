At the StarCityGames.com Invitational this past weekend, Brian Kibler brought a deck he formerly championed out of the woodwork after setting it aside for nearly a year: Blue-Black Infect! Kibler's newest take on the deck features an infect engine backed up by blue countermagic and card selection. .. and is that Viral Drake!? Give this new iteration of the deck a try for yourself!

Brian Kibler's Blue-Black Infect 

Standard – StarCityGames.com Invitational
