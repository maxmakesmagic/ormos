With *Dragon's Maze* we introduce a new cycle of guild mana fixing: the Cluestones. A Ravnican might think of the Cluestones as an "in case of emergency break glass," providing useful magic to the members of their guild.

In the first *Ravnica* block, we had [the Signets](https://web.archive.org/web/20130416000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&name=+%5bsignet%5d&block=%7c%5b%22Ravnica+Block%22%5d):

 ![Boros Signet](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Boros%20Signet&options=) 

  

Then in *Return to Ravnica* and *Gatecrash* we were given [the Keyrunes](https://web.archive.org/web/20130416000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&name=+%5bkeyrune%5d):

 ![Boros Keyrune](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Boros%20Keyrune&options=) 

  

And now we have the Cluestones:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5b/5e/5b5ed0ad6753131cc977729b590639505f8fec77d304a02be6fd5bf1911cfbd9.jpg) 

  

All ten are in [the Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218013900/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/dragonsmaze/cig), but this is the Arcana, so let's take a good look at their art, all done by [Raoul Vitale](https://web.archive.org/web/20130416000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Raoul+Vitale%22%5d)!
