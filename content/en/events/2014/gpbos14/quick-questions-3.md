**What's the strangest deck you've faced this weekend?**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpbos14/Frank_KarstenQQ.jpg)

***Frank Karsten**: Zoo. It's not so strange, but I thought it had become unpopular since the Pro Tour.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpbos14/Matej_ZatlkajQQ.jpg)

***Matej "Big Z" Zatlkaj**: A crazy discard deck with 4 [The Rack](https://web.archive.org/web/20140727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Rack) and 4 [Shrieking Affliction](https://web.archive.org/web/20140727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrieking+Affliction).* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpbos14/Nathan_HolidayQQ.jpg)

***Nathan Holiday**: Soul Sisters. I know it's not strange- well, I know that people sometimes play it, but still.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpbos14/Denniz_RachidQQ.jpg)

***Denniz Rachid**: Blue-Black Teachings. I'm playing Infect and kept what I thought was a good hand. Then he played turn-one [Darkblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20140727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darkblast). I could've conceded right there.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpbos14/Guillaume_Wafo-TapaQQ.jpg)

***Guillaume Wafo-Tapa**: Mono-Blue Control, with [Scrying Sheets](https://web.archive.org/web/20140727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scrying+Sheets) and snow-covered Islands.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpbos14/Alexander_HayneQQ.jpg)

***Alexander Hayne** : Burn with [Keldon Megaliths](https://web.archive.org/web/20140727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Keldon+Megaliths).*
