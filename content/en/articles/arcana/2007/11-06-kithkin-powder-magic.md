As Jeremy Jarvis [described](https://web.archive.org/web/20071106000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/423) earlier on this site, part of the look and feel of kithkin was established through the look of their magic. Kithkin use a lot of powders and dusts to perform their spellcraft, which in turn necessitates a lot of containers—urns, jars, lockets, and pouches. Check out this image from the *Lorwyn* style guide for a look at some of the equipment kithkin might use to carry their magical materials.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2d/6a/2d6a871eb9accf17aa7f03aca3b53194efff36dd2346e79cd0ea493e79f3b718.jpg)

Check out the alchemical flying steed constructed by this advanced kithkin mage. It's part bird, part pottery! The mage's wind-defying power comes from the powders he has stoppered up in all those jars and urns.

[![Galepowder Mage](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/cardart/LRW/Galepowder_Mage_640.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071106000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Galepowder%2BMage) *[Galepowder Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20071106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galepowder+Mage) art by Jeremy Jarvis*

The versatile removal power of [Oblivion Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20071106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Ring) all comes down to a circle of fine particles, shown here trapping a frustrated flamekin in its center.

[![Oblivion Ring](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/cardart/LRW/Oblivion_Ring_640.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071106000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Oblivion%2BRing) *[Oblivion Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20071106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Ring) art by Wayne England*

A circle of powder can have healing properties, too. Below, a [Kithkin Healer](https://web.archive.org/web/20071106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kithkin+Healer) nurses a young kithkin back to health with an inscribed pattern made from specially-prepared dust.

[![Kithkin Healer](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/cardart/LRW/Kithkin_Healer_640.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071106000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Kithkin%2BHealer) *[Kithkin Healer](https://web.archive.org/web/20071106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kithkin+Healer) art by Rebecca Guay*
