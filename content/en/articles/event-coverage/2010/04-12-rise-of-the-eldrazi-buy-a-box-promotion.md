Here's an offer that is sure to appeal to every **Magic: The Gathering** player!

The first 20 people to purchase a *Rise of the Eldrazi* Booster Box at participating locations will get a free alternate-art premium Guul Draz Assassin promo card!\*

But hurry, this offer is limited to the first 20 people per store!
