We showed off the old *Vanguard* cards in a [previous Magic Arcana](/en/articles/arcana/2002/09-23-vanguard-series-1).

Well, just like everything else we make, the *Vanguard* cards had to go through playtesting. At the bottom of a desk drawer in R&D we found these two little gems—playtest versions of two cards from the first *Vanguard* series.

![Vanguard playtest](https://i.ormos.uk/e5/7d/e57d9f7bf665a69790bd470a193b37a7460118bf4bc40ebd1f23f0252e42c188.jpg)
