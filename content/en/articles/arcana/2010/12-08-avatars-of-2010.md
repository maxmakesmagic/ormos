If you buy the new [Momir Basic Event Deck](https://web.archive.org/web/20101208000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/mtgomomirvig/productinfo) on **Magic Online**, you'll receive a deck with twelve of each basic land (including a five-card set of the promotional Guru lands) and an alternate-art Momir Vig avatar. That avatar looks like this:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8d/ae/8dae57c137523236e443f73355b375de13b5b6deff31fe797720207968498bbd.jpg)

[Momir Vig, Simic Visionary](https://web.archive.org/web/20101208000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Momir+Vig%2C+Simic+Visionary) Alternate Art **Magic Online** Avatar
