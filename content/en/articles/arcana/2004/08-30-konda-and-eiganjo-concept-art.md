The Kamigawa block is suffused with setting, story, and flavor - and Magic Arcana will be one key way to discover the world behind the cards. Watch this space for behind-the-scenes concept art, rich storyline info, a glossary of Japanese terms relevant to Kamigawa, a look behind the flavor text of Kamigawa block, and more. 

Today we focus on a single man whose destiny was central to the set's main tale. Rei Nakazawa's [feature article this week](/en/articles/feature/2004/08-30-we-are-the-champions-my-friend) previews **Konda, Lord of Eiganjo**, the card representing the patriarch of a powerful ruling family in the world of Kamigawa. Haven't read Rei's article yet? [Go read it!](/en/articles/feature/2004/08-30-we-are-the-champions-my-friend)

![Konda, Lord of Eiganjo](https://i.ormos.uk/6e/02/6e022f2a5eecbd9c290a441a5f68a71ae5f827f14303083ee4dc7a1372954f4b.jpg)

<table><tbody><tr><td>&#10;<p>Lord Konda was a <i>daimyo</i> - a feudal lord and landowner who commanded a host of samurai soldiers. He ruled from Eiganjo Castle - hence &quot;Konda, Lord of Eiganjo.&quot; </p>&#10;<p> To the left you can see a concept sketch for Konda by Ittoku, an artist responsible for a lot of the look of the Kamigawa block. Ittoku is the illustrator of <a>Rude Awakening</a>, <a>Arcbound Fiend</a> and a few other cards in <i>Mirrodin</i> block. </p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>
