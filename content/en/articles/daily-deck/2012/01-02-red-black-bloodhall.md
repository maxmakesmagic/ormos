The *Innistrad* lands like Kessig Wolf Run, Gavony Township, Moorland Haunt, and Nephalia Drownyard have all seen extensive Standard play – but where's the love for Stensia Bloodhall? Rogue deck designer Ken Adams stepped up to give the Bloodhall some love, building this deck which he took to the StarCityGames.com Invitational a few weeks ago. If you've been looking for a midrange take on red strategies, give this deck a spin!

Ken Adams's Red-Black Bloodhall 

Standard — StarCityGames.com Invitational
