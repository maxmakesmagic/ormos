R*ise of the Eldrazi* Game Day is on May 22 and 23. Worldwide! And why should you go? Because playing **Magic** is fun, that's why!

Game Days are Standard Constructed format. The use of deck lists is recommended. The tournament will be Swiss-style, with the number of rounds determined by attendance. And they're the first opportunity to play Standard format on a global scale with the cards from *Rise of the Eldrazi*!

But in the unlikely event that a fun Standard tournament isn't enough to entice you out to your local **Magic** store, we've also got promo cards. While supplies last, all participants will get this handsome [Staggershock](https://web.archive.org/web/20100504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Staggershock)!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/83/fc/83fc206a305693493b561dab9f94a986e24ebfe1f491ea7b7f0672260e02265c.jpg)  

The Top 8 players each receive an exclusive [Deathless Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20100504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathless+Angel) full-art promo card. The cards are awarded to the top eight players even if the tournament cuts only to the Top 4. Two [Deathless Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20100504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathless+Angel) promo cards will also be awarded as door prizes at each location.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a1/b7/a1b7603eae4a2b61f9ce32f598280611491cbd585d9f657a7c702c4bfb12bc3e.jpg)  

Mark your calendars! May 22-23! [*Rise of the Eldrazi* Game Day](https://web.archive.org/web/20100504000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/gameday-facts)!
