What's that you say? You say you can't decide if you want to go to the [*Conflux* Prerelease](https://web.archive.org/web/20090115000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prereleases)? Even though there are not only [events all over the world](https://web.archive.org/web/20090115000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prerelease-facts) but also [thirty large Prereleases](https://web.archive.org/web/20090115000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prerelease-regional) for people who want to experience a big event?

Well, it's your decision, of course. We wouldn't want to *influence* you in any way. We just thought we’d mention (in a casual, offhand manner) that everyone who plays in a Prerelease will get one of these: 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f6/55/f655c8244d03ca47f5bb56db6e799e0e08e39ea6511ca8610bb23d3c64547cdd.jpg)  

You know. If that's the kind of thing you're into.

Oh! And it comes in six different languages. For *collecting*.
