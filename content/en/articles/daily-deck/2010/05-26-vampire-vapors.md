Think Vampires is dead? John Moore didn't, and his hard work paid off! He took this take on the archetype to first place at the Arizona National Qualifier. Check out the powerful Consuming Vapors. That card has seen increasing play in Standard as a solution to Baneslayer Angels, Thornlings, and all manner of fatties. See more National Qualifier deck lists [here](/en/events/2010/natqual10/2010-national-qualifiers-top-8-standard-deck-lists).
