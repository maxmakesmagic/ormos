Below is a snippet from issue 17 of *The Duelist* magazine, June 1997. For reference, this was right before *Weatherlight* and *Fifth Edition* were released.

The blurb refers to Olle Råde winning the Duelist Invitational and getting to design a card. Well, five years later, the card actually came out in *Judgment* - [Sylvan Safekeeper](https://web.archive.org/web/20020610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Safekeeper).

So what happened? Why five years? Had he turned in a reasonable idea, the card would have probably appeared in Stronghold. But Olle was young and flippant, and he had no real interest in making a serious card. His first submission was World of Bums, ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif), Enchant World. The card had no text. Its only purpose was to destroy other Enchant Worlds, which R&D stopped supporting after *Visions*.

His second attempt was some sort of permission spell that went to the top of its owners library upon resolution, instead of in the graveyard. Kind of like a reverse *Memory Lapse*. Needless to say, neither card made it very far.

After Olle saw how cool [Avalanche Riders](https://web.archive.org/web/20020610000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Avalanche%2BRiders), [Rootwater Thief](https://web.archive.org/web/20020610000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Rootwater%2BThief), and [Meddling Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20020610000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Meddling%2BMage) looked on their Invitational cards, he had a change of heart, and submitted something very close to Sylvan Safekeeper: a 1/1 for ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/green.gif) that could make a creature untargetable at the cost of returning a forest to your hand. The card was tweaked to fit in the Odyssey block by having it interact with threshold better, and was put into *Judgment*. Quite a wait!

In the art for [Sylvan Safekeeper](https://web.archive.org/web/20020610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Safekeeper), Olle is shown riding a spider. This is an homage to his winning deck at Pro Tour - Columbus (*Ice Age/Alliances* constructed), which became his signature deck.
