The *Unhinged* card **Johnny, Combo Player** depicts the innovating mage flinging a whirlwind of spells. Each of the **Magic** cards in the art is a spell with hefty "[Johnny](/en/articles/makingmagic/2002/03-11-timmy-johnny-and-spike-3)-style" combo potential.

Ready to test your squinting powers? Identify all the cards in the art of Johnny, Combo Player.
