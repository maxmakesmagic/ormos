An attentive reader named Jay pointed me to Kevin Florio's Golgari deck that won a Standard Grand Prix Trial at [Grand Prix Pittsburgh](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/parker-makes-proud-return-pittsburgh). I'm always on the lookout for something new and exciting, and this is definitely the kind of deck I can get into. 

![Sign in Blood](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Sign+in+Blood)

Several months ago, before *Return to Ravnica* had even come out, I was excited for the future of Standard, where I imagined a world dominated by sacrificing [Thragtusk](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thragtusk) to [Disciple of Bolas](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+Bolas) to down the opponent in life and card advantage. 

Then [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) was printed, and I learned that we wouldn't have to work anywhere near that hard for our life and card advantage, and I forgot all about my old dream. 

Well, Kevin didn't, and he earned three byes for his efforts with this spicy midrange concoction.

[Sign in Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sign+in+Blood) provides the bulk of the card draw, but it's supported by [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) and [Disciple of Bolas](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+Bolas), who's amazing with the four [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon)s in this deck. Speaking of amazing with [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon)—how about [Mutilate](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutilate)? This one-sided wrath doesn't even care about cards like [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm) that some aggressive decks use to fight [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict). 

The sideboard has all the best hate—[Deathrite Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathrite+Shaman) for Reanimator, [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) for control decks, [Gloom Surgeon](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gloom+Surgeon) for aggro, and a good mix of other support cards to tune the deck. 

My compliments to Kevin, as I like seeing people win with less-common decks, especially if those decks contain [Disciple of Bolas](https://web.archive.org/web/20130405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+Bolas).
