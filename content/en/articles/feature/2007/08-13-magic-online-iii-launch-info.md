This is a demonstration of how the cards stay as large as possible.

From [scene 1](https://web.archive.org/web/20070813000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=magiconline/images/SS_sel1.JPG&origin=magic_magiconline_IIIlaunch) to [scene 2](https://web.archive.org/web/20070813000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=magiconline/images/SS_sel2.JPG&origin=magic_magiconline_IIIlaunch) we see that our card shrink slightly to fit in our play zone.  
 From [scene 2](https://web.archive.org/web/20070813000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=magiconline/images/SS_sel2.JPG&origin=magic_magiconline_IIIlaunch) to [scene 3](https://web.archive.org/web/20070813000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=magiconline/images/SS_sel3.JPG&origin=magic_magiconline_IIIlaunch), our cards get a bit smaller again, to fit in a third row.  
[Scene 3](https://web.archive.org/web/20070813000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=magiconline/images/SS_sel3.JPG&origin=magic_magiconline_IIIlaunch) shows 3 rows and a full top row, but the cards shrink a bit in [scene 4](https://web.archive.org/web/20070813000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=magiconline/images/SS_sel4.JPG&origin=magic_magiconline_IIIlaunch) staying at 3 rows.  
[Scene 5](https://web.archive.org/web/20070813000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=magiconline/images/SS_sel5.JPG&origin=magic_magiconline_IIIlaunch) shows that cards only get a bit smaller before scrolling occurs.
