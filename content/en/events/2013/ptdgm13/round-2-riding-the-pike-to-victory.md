## Gabriel Nassif (Rakdos) vs. Martin Jůza (Boros)

[Ben Stark (Boros Blitz) vs. Gaudenis Vidugiris (Bant Control)](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/ptdgm13/fm4)

* * *

How does it feel to cast [Rakdos's Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos%27s+Return) to discard your opponent's entire hand after they've missed several land drops? Hall of Famer Gabriel Nassif humbly summed it as "pretty good" on his way to a split win over three fast games against Martin Jůza.

Both veteran players of the Pro Tour, Jůza and Nassif know the value in winning early rounds of a Pro Tour. Better tiebreakers come through victories on Day One, and each had set out to do that with aggressive decks.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/mtg/images/daily/events/ptdgm13/r2_juza_nassif.jpg)  
*While Martin Jůza's Boros deck had some aggressive creatures, Nassif's Rakdos deck packed a real punch with [Goblin Piker](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Piker)s, auras, and some powerful removal.* 

Jůza, renowned for his Limited prowess from years of success on the Grand Prix circuit, drafted a white-heavy Boros deck that brought two-drops to bear. While a small suite of tricks were there, like [Martial Glory](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Martial+Glory) and [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm), his deck lacked removal. It also included creatures that couldn't attack, with two copies of [Basilica Guards](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basilica+Guards).

Removal and efficient attackers were easier to come by for Nassif, the two-time Pro Tour winner and Hall of Fame member who had pulled together a red-filled Rakdos deck. Homing Lighting and [Launch Party](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Launch+Party) highlighted the lethal spell suite to match multiple copies each of [Bomber Corps](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bomber+Corps), [Riot Piker](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Riot+Piker), and [Deviant Glee](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deviant+Glee).

"I felt like the matchup was okay," Nassif said, "but then in Game 2 I saw [Concordia Pegasus](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Concordia+Pegasus) and my deck is all [Riot Piker](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Riot+Piker)s. I have a lot of ways to get them through, but coming in, I thought I was a big underdog. I felt pretty good after Game 1."

Jůza was tight on mana across all three games, but particularly in Game 1. "I couldn't cast anything," he admitted, and with a slow start to boot, Jůza was forced into blocks throughout. Nassif used [Weapon Surge](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Weapon+Surge) to help [Ember Beast](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ember+Beast), [Bomber Corps](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bomber+Corps), and [Ash Zealot](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ash+Zealot) punch through [Syndic of Tithes](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syndic+of+Tithes) and [Basilica Guards](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basilica+Guards), then [Homing Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Homing+Lightning) negated the worst of [Martial Glory](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Martial+Glory) when Jůza tried to save the Guards while blocking a turn later.

[Rakdos's Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos%27s+Return) for 4 damage and four cards when Nassif hit his sixth mana in as many turns left Jůza without anything to hold onto. While Jůza's notepad was covered with the powerful spells he saw from Nassif's deck, losing to two copies of [Bomber Corps](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bomber+Corps), one with [Deviant Glee](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deviant+Glee), was a high price to pay for the knowledge.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/mtg/images/daily/events/ptdgm13/r2_nassif.jpg)  
*Nassif closed out a first game off of a deadly [Rakdos's Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos%27s+Return) while his opponent was struggling to find lands.* 

The second game showed Jůza's resilience. He wanted to curve out even after a mulligan, and picked his cards up in order to see if it would happen. As he explained, "I always look at four then three, or three then three. I'll see two-drop, three-drop, four-drop and hope for land, land, land."

He got his wish on six cards, leading [Boros Mastiff](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Mastiff) into [Pyrewild Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyrewild+Shaman), then [Basilica Guards](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basilica+Guards). Nassif's [Bellows Lizard](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bellows+Lizard) and [Spike Jester](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spike+Jester) sat at home a turn before trading away favorably for Jůza, who followed up with [Syndic of Tithes](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syndic+of+Tithes) and two copies of [Concordia Pegasus](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Concordia+Pegasus), plenty of extort getting paid for along the way. By the time Nassif hit a [Launch Party](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Launch+Party) to clear some of the incoming damage, he had run out of creatures to cast.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/mtg/images/daily/events/ptdgm13/r2_juza.jpg)  
*Jůza may have been short on removal, but the quality of his creatures was high.* 

Game 3 was Nassif's opportunity to play first, and Jůza adjusted by sideboarding in [Zarichi Tiger](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zarichi+Tiger). However, it was the lack of removal that derailed Jůza's chances. Even with [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm) and [Martial Glory](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Martial+Glory) in hand, and creatures in play to work with, it took Nassif just three cards and six turns to win: [Riot Piker](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Riot+Piker) came in on turn two and was promptly enchanted by [Madcap Skills](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Madcap+Skills) on turn three. It attacked, and then attacked again on turn four. The Piker was finally made unblockable by [Rogue's Passage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rogue%27s+Passage) on turns five and six for the final points of damage.
