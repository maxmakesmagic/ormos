Standard events are being held all around the world for [the chance to compete in Seattle this October](/en/articles/feature/2012/03-15-qualifiers-for-pro-tour-return-to-ravnica-in-seattle). Check out the Top 8 decklists from some of the recent PTQs (including those on **Magic Online**) to see what you're likely face if you want to battle for a Pro Tour invitation!
