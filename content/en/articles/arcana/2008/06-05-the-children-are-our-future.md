The other week, Wizards of the Coast participated in Take Your Children to Work Day. The children seemed to enjoy spending the day at a game company, which isn't much of a surprise, really.

One of their activities was to design a **Magic** card, and we think the cards they came up with are pretty interesting. Mark Rosewater took each group (8-10 years old and 11-17) and showed them twenty potential pieces of art. Once the art was picked by the group, they named the card. From there, they selected a creature type, a power and toughness, a mechanic, and flavor text. Finally, they came up with the card's mana cost, which is where Mark provided the most guidance.

The 8-10s picked [Sickle Ripper](https://web.archive.org/web/20080605000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sickle+Ripper)'s art right away and came up with the Reaper of the Dark Flame:

![Reaper of the Dark Flame](https://i.ormos.uk/5d/a5/5da5997928507f9ba07e27f23918712cb14d03ebd0fa95ced54e8e2b5b482a64.jpg)

Meanwhile, the 11-17s picked [Jace Beleren](https://web.archive.org/web/20080605000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace+Beleren)'s art and renamed him "Kardoth the Impaler". Their theory was that he could turn his arms into knives and then throw the knives at people, resulting in this mechanic:

![Kardoth the Impaler](https://i.ormos.uk/61/bb/61bb90276cbe516c78f00e8bb10988b55c08b0151166fca016ab8c26a0b82fd5.jpg)

The smaller notes are the result of a quickie "Development" phase which cleaned up the wording on the cards before mocking them up. Each child that came to Wizards of the Coast got to take home a sheet of paper with shockingly realistic card images on it:

![Final mock-ups](https://i.ormos.uk/a9/f8/a9f85eb875f46e47c2a1c2329bf3dce5bd3b162e884fa077addc2d3af30c546b.jpg)

Not a bad souvenir for a day at your parent's job!
