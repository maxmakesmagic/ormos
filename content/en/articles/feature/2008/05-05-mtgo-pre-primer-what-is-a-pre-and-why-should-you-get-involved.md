*Alpha\_Centauri1*

*What is a PRE?*

PRE stands for player run event. Typically on MTGO, any "event" (or tournament) is run through the system, with the system acting as the tournament organizer; this is true for drafts, leagues, constructed queues and premier events. The system executes registration, sets up pairings and awards prizes, just like a tournament organizer in real life would do. In player run events, a player, or "host" as they are called, runs everything. This includes registration, pairings, rules enforcement and prize awarding. All of this must be done manually, since the system is only set up to handle certain events.

*Why do we need PREs?*

With events and tournaments automatically run by the system, why do we need PREs? There are two primary reasons that PREs benefit the community:

1\. WOTC supports only the most popular formats via these automated events (e.g., Standard, Extended, Block constructed). Many other formats have been coded into the system, but are not widely supported in tournament play on Magic Online (e.g., Commander/EDH, 2-headed giant, free-for-all). Still other formats have not even been coded into the system, but are played by manually enforcing the rules (e.g., Pauper format).

2\. PREs are free to enter, and are generally open to everyone. Tournaments run by the system cost money (via event tickets or booster packs) to enter. The ability to play competitive events (or just fun events) without having to buy in provides a valuable resource to all players.

*What types of PREs exist?*

Generally, the available PREs can be lumped into three broad categories: PDC, SREs and PREs.

**PDC**: PDC, or Pauper Deck Challenge, is a format played using only commons. Limiting the card pool to commons allows players to compete without spending hundreds of tickets to pick up the tier 1 uncommon and rare cards that are often necessary to be successful in constructed formats. The PDC community is entirely player run and supported, but it has flourished on the coattails of its passionate members. PDC PREs are generally available every day of the week, and often more than once per day.

**SREs**: SRE stands for sponsor run events. Recently these events have been differentiated from PREs, since a sponsor, typically an MTGO dealer, offers prizes (typically in the form of a gift certificate/credit at his or her online store) to the winners and/or participants in these events. These events are still run by hosts; however, high level decisions (such as the format of the event, the prizes for the event, start time, etc.) are typically determined by the sponsor, often with input from the host(s).

**PREs**: PREs are exactly what they sound like – player run events. These events are generally created and run by players, and are often created and run by specific MTGO clans. If you have an idea for an event you'd like to run – this would be a PRE. Typically the prizes offered for these events are those donated by other players (there is no obligation to donate prizes in order to play).

*What formats are available?*

In PDC (commons only), many different formats exist, including standard, extended, classic and prismatic singleton. SREs offer events including classic tribal, 10th theme deck wars, momir basic, 2hg, commander/EDH, extended ffa and lorwyn tribal standard. PREs offer events including commander/EDH, ffa and 2hg.

*Where can I find out about the available PREs?*

Most PREs are advertised on the player run events board on the [Gleemax forums](http://forums.gleemax.com/forumdisplay.php?f=590&order=desc). A comprehensive, up-to-date calendar of all existing PREs is not currently available, but the PRE community has discussed putting one in place. There is at least one PRE available every day of the week, so stop by and see if any of them interest you.

*How can I join a PRE?*

By looking at the description of the PRE, it will indicate a time and a place (i.e., a room on the MTGO client in which the event will take place). Simply log into your MTGO account, and type /join \[that room name\] at the indicated time. You will see a host in that room posting messages about the event, including how to register. Typically, registering requires typing "reg" in a private message to the host, and then following the instructions given by the host thereafter.

*How can I create my own PRE?*

PREs are easy to make. Once you come up with an idea for a new event, all you need to do is pick a time and a place, and then make a post to the gleemax forums telling everyone about your event. If you are looking to be a host for an existing event, or you would like a sponsor for your event, you should contact the necessary person(s) to discuss such a possibility.

*What if I am in Europe, or another non-U.S. timezone?*

A majority of the current PRE player base is in the U.S. As such, most of the events have start times that cater to that demographic. If you are in a different time zone and are interested in playing in a PRE, but can't find one to meet your schedule – you should create one! Alternatively, you could gather together some friends and approach some of the existing sponsors and/or hosts that run events to try to set up a new event. Typically the only thing needed is players, and the rest will sort itself out.

*What kind of prizes can I win?*

In non-sponsored PDC and PRE events, the prizes range from a few extra commons to event tickets, depending on what is donated to the prize pool. In sponsored events, typically the prizes are credits on that sponsor's bot or a gift certificate in that sponsor's store – ranging from 4-20 credits (or dollars) for a first place finish. Sponsored events often provide other unique prizes, including booster packs, invitations to special sponsor run events and unique prizes, such as a 3-hour magic tutorial with a pro tour player. The prizes vary from event to event, so be sure to read the description to find out what you could be playing for. In addition, all events offer bragging rights, to those who win, and entertainment/fun, to those who play.

*Do I have to win to win a prize?*

Not always. Some events offer "door prizes" or "random prizes" that can be awarded to any person who plays in the event, regardless of whether they win or lose. Be sure to check the event description to find out who is eligible to win prizes.

*Are there community resources available on PREs?*

Your most important resource is the Gleemax [player run event forum](http://forums.gleemax.com/forumdisplay.php?f=590&order=desc). For PDC events and format information, check out [www.pdcmagic.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20080505000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.pdcmagic.com&origin=magic_magiconline_article050508a). More information on available SREs can be found at [www.mtgocasual.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20080505000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.mtgocasual.com&origin=magic_magiconline_article050508a) and [www.puremtgo.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20080505000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.puremtgo.com&origin=magic_magiconline_article050508a). PRE information for specific events can be found at [www.onlyhell.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20080505000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.onlyhell.com&origin=magic_magiconline_article050508a) and [cerealkillaz.clangrid.com/main.asp](https://web.archive.org/web/20080505000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://cerealkillaz.clangrid.com/main.asp&origin=magic_magiconline_article050508a).

*Are PREs supported by WOTC?*

PREs are not supported by WOTC, and therefore any prizes you may be entitled to via a PRE is not guaranteed. However, most of the PRE community is reasonable, so if you have an issue, you should speak with the relevant person to resolve it. WOTC does not allow *advertising* for any PRE on MTGO. Advertising for PREs may only be done in the gleemax forum dedicated to PREs.

*I have more questions and/or suggestions on PREs – where should I go?*

The best place to go to get your questions answered or your suggestions considered is the gleemax forums. There are many helpful individuals in the PRE community that would be happy to assist and discuss.

### To sum it up...

You should get involved in the PRE scene because:

1.  PREs offer tournament support for unique and interesting events.
2.  PREs are free to enter.
3.  Many PREs offer cool prizes.
4.  Some PREs don't require you to win any games to be eligible for prizes.
5.  PREs are FUN – you can meet great people and enjoy the game at the same time.
