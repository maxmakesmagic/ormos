Aggressive green decks that finish with [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) have been a part of casual and competitive **Magic** since the release of *Tempest* back in late 1997. [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) has always been one of the most powerful Limited cards available in any given set that it has been printed in. The new core set introduces the latest, perhaps greatest, [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) effect. [Overwhelming Stampede](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overwhelming+Stampede) is a beautifully designed card. The flavor is spot on, the effect is powerful enough for Constructed play, and the artwork makes every old-school player remember that they used to play 8/4 trample creatures for eight mana and be happy about it. (Remember *Mirage*?)

![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/boab/boab104_over.jpg)

I knew [Overwhelming Stampede](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overwhelming+Stampede) was good, but I didn't realize just how unfair the card could be until I had it in a draft deck on **Magic Online** earlier this week. Literally 100% of games where I drew [Overwhelming Stampede](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overwhelming+Stampede) were won very easily. It's pretty easy to engineer a turn where that type of effect will win you the game in a Limited match. As a side note, I've noticed that winning matches in the new core set Draft format is very dependent on the number of creatures you play. I haven't had any trouble winning games with draft decks that feature seventeen or more creatures. Getting a chance to sling some [Overwhelming Stampede](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overwhelming+Stampede) was enough to get me excited about including it in a Standard deck.

I had forgotten about [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) Green strategies until Zvi Mowshowitz introduced me to an exciting [Beastmaster Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Beastmaster+Ascension) deck for the last Block Constructed Pro Tour. Looking through [Gatherer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Default.aspx), I was pleasantly surprised to see how many awesome tricks are available for this type of strategy. [Garruk Wildspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Wildspeaker) is a far more attainable card now that it has been printed in a few sets and been given out as a promotional card for Xbox LIVE **Magic** players. Even classic [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) is still legal until *Scars of Mirrodin* hits the shelves in October.

[Llanowar Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Elves) and [Arbor Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arbor+Elf) both accomplish the same goal here. An [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) Green deck wants to present a lot of creatures very quickly and force its opponent to make unfavorable blocks. Once the board is reasonably clogged the [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) green deck can easily break a stalemate with its [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) effects. [Llanowar Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Elves) and [Arbor Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arbor+Elf) allow the [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) Green deck to accelerate into threats in the early stages of the game. As the game progresses, [Llanowar Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Elves) and [Arbor Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arbor+Elf) both act as reasonable threats when paired with the [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) effects.

[Leatherback Baloth](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leatherback+Baloth) is a very efficient green fatty. It works very well with our [Overwhelming Stampede](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overwhelming+Stampede). Playing eight one-mana accelerants makes [Leatherback Baloth](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leatherback+Baloth) even more impressive. He enters the battlefield on the second turn in a remarkable percentage of games.

[Nest Invader](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nest+Invader) is one of the greatest unsung heroes from *Rise of Eldrazi*. It presents two bodies for a single card, which only increases in value as you play more [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) effects. It also allows its controller to accelerate into turn four [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) effects that can be followed up with turn five [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) effects. Remember, we're going to play with a lot of [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun)s.

[Kozilek's Predator](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kozilek%27s+Predator) is [Nest Invader](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nest+Invader)'s big brother. It accomplishes the same goals and occupies a different spot on the curve. A single [Kozilek's Predator](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kozilek%27s+Predator) can often present a lethal board when combined with [Garruk Wildspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Wildspeaker), [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun), or [Overwhelming Stampede](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overwhelming+Stampede).

[Garruk's Companion](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk%27s+Companion) is another gem that hasn't seen its day in the sun yet. Deals don't get much better than a 3/2 trampler for two-mana. Cards like [Garruk's Companion](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk%27s+Companion) ensure that our [Overwhelming Stampede](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overwhelming+Stampede) isn't a dead card.

[Wolfbriar Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wolfbriar+Elemental) lets us get back on the table after [Day of Judgment](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Day+of+Judgment). The sheer number of guys that this card can produce makes this card an automatic inclusion in the deck. I never liked drawing [Wolfbriar Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wolfbriar+Elemental) in my opening hand, though, so I decided to only play two copies of the top-end finisher.

[Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun), [Overwhelming Stampede](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overwhelming+Stampede), and [Garruk Wildspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Wildspeaker) let us finish off opponents out of nowhere. It's important to note that [Overwhelming Stampede](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overwhelming+Stampede) combos very well with Garruk's ultimate ability. I had many games where I was able to activate Garruk's ultimate with a few creatures on the table and follow it up with an [Overwhelming Stampede](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overwhelming+Stampede) for +6/+6 or +7/+7 to my whole team.

[Khalni Garden](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Khalni+Garden) makes it in as our non-basic land of choice because it gives us an extra body to crash into the red zone with our [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) effects.

![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/boab/boab104_Khalni_Garden.jpg)

Here's the deck once it's completed. The only card that may be difficult to acquire is [Garruk Wildspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Wildspeaker). You can replace the [Garruk Wildspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Wildspeaker)s with more [Wolfbriar Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wolfbriar+Elemental)s and a fourth [Overwhelming Stampede](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overwhelming+Stampede) if you are unable to get your hands on all three copies of the card.

Boom!

Jund is probably a very good match-up with this deck, especially if they don't [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) your mana creatures. Jund is forced to tap out often enough that an [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) effect is bound to close the game at some point.

The [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) Green deck seems very well positioned if people at your local store tend to play with Jund and [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan) decks more than White-Blue Control and red decks. The deck seems to race Mythic Conscription at about even pace. Our [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun) effects tend to be more devastating than their [Sovereigns of Lost Alara](https://web.archive.org/web/20100818000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sovereigns+of+Lost+Alara), but their ability to stick the Conscription on a lifelink creature gives them a nice bonus. I think the match-up should be about even, perhaps a little in favor of the Green Stompy deck.
