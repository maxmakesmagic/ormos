### *What is the most underrated card in Scars of Mirrodin Block Constructed? What is the most overrated?*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/19/83/198318ba974d5713761429cd1683cccd15a6ac08e82c8525439aea59e649f636.jpg)

**Yuuya Watanabe:** Underrated? Hmm... [Entomber Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Entomber+Exarch). Overrated is [Tempered Steel](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tempered+Steel).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/67/82/6782e983ece03da062cb8e3fa10401e11e0dd49b10cfa34a53be2d4a0df8d3f1.jpg)**Reid Duke:** I think the most overrated card in Block is [Elspeth Tirel](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth+Tirel). A five-drop in the control decks just needs to turn the game right there, and she doesn't quite do it. The most underrated card is [Darkslick Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darkslick+Drake) for the same reason why Elspeth is bad. It fits exactly what the control decks want to do at its slot.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3e/b7/3eb741ec8553bedc355821b5da6743983d0fad6d951e4ae3fc5df58b842af3ee.jpg)**Alex West:** Underrated is [Thrun, the Last Troll](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thrun%2C+the+Last+Troll) and overrated is... [Puresteel Paladin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Puresteel+Paladin).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f8/b4/f8b4f1a6761f9c47221434cd2fa49623bace3c72901f196f9ef550e1a391d95c.jpg)

**Sam Black:** For underrated, I should definitely say [Piston Sledge](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Piston+Sledge), but for general application, I'll say [Into the Core](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Into+the+Core). I think the most overrated is probably [Stoic Rebuttal](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoic+Rebuttal).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4f/c6/4fc61c53a105b1f3d16c62df35f1c6db5d03ef37a5a65d8fa6302b4e262cc264.jpg)

**Simon Görtzen:** The most overrated is definitely [Consecrated Sphinx](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Consecrated+Sphinx). I think the most underrated is probably [Karn Liberated](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn+Liberated). People think he's good, but he's even better than that.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2d/55/2d55753466ed66a72d2895e6798f954db2ea9f69913bc3351b8f8c660d53b57d.jpg)

**David Ochoa:** Heh, for overrated I would say [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island). Underrated though? Hmm... yeah, I think I'm going to say [Pristine Talisman](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pristine+Talisman).

### *What's the best planeswalker in Scars of Mirrodin Block Constructed?*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a7/c8/a7c8c57dc32d2c30c65dd976b68ca17a0b5501a2f4ff1d6274e32b1ccac18912.jpg)**Gaudenis Vidugiris:** [Koth of the Hammer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Koth+of+the+Hammer)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3e/b7/3eb741ec8553bedc355821b5da6743983d0fad6d951e4ae3fc5df58b842af3ee.jpg)**Alex West:** [Karn Liberated](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn+Liberated); he's the end boss in control mirrors.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7c/a9/7ca903623639f4b2f42c07579e36a097464f2a5eccc2788995d4538e64af088c.jpg)**Josh Utter-Leyton:** [Koth of the Hammer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Koth+of+the+Hammer).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/67/82/6782e983ece03da062cb8e3fa10401e11e0dd49b10cfa34a53be2d4a0df8d3f1.jpg)**Reid Duke:** Koth. [Tezzeret, Agent of Bolas](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tezzeret%2C+Agent+of+Bolas) and [Koth of the Hammer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Koth+of+the+Hammer) are the only planeswalkers that come down fast enough to impact the game, but Tezzeret forces you to play with too many artifacts for your deck to stay competitive against [Tempered Steel](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tempered+Steel).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4e/d5/4ed50e8b15ff7fe94e0f7f2480e94a62a61aca8dcf38336209d0177fb8a250a5.jpg)

**Conrad Kolos:** [Karn Liberated](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn+Liberated). The planeswalkers in this block are generally aggressive rather than defensive, so you can't really protect Koth.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4a/3f/4a3fa2a9f39a9f473e1946ba82c8f6080b3b95e04af9655703795af15399c7ad.jpg)

**Conley Woods:** [Elspeth Tirel](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth+Tirel). Koth puts too much of a constraint on how you build your deck. You can build a two-color Elspeth deck, but you can't build a two-color Koth deck.
