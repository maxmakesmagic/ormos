*What's your favorite multiplayer format, and why?*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/98/ff/98ff84c36158a8091193fe55e8d3028e6e766bb343fd8e5051e08a78fcb62548.jpg)**Zac Hill:** Archenemy! I like going into a game knowing whom I need to beat up. Having *worked* in politics for the better part of five years, the last thing I want to do when I'm having fun is haggle over interpersonal power dynamics and juggle even *more* politics. Archenemy gets rid of that step and just says, "Hey you over there—kill those guys!" Plus, the moment of flipping a scheme card is one of the most fun, high-variance moments in **Magic**.
