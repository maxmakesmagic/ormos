## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/45/48/45487025a21d99054258455079584b1daae67f16880093a783ac6865a7337287.png)The original *Limited Edition Alpha* set featured 295 cards. On its heels that same year were the 302 black-bordered *Limited Edition Beta* set, quickly followed by the 302 *Unlimited Edition* set—white-bordered versions of *Limited Edition Beta* cards.

**Set Name:** *Alpha*  
**Number of Cards:** 295

**Set Name:** *Beta*  
**Number of Cards:** 302

**Set Name:***Unlimited Edition*  
**Number of Cards:** 302

**Languages:** English

**Design and Development:** Richard Garfield, with contributions from Charlie Cateeno, Skaff Elias, Don Felice, Tom Fontaine, Jim Lin, Joel Mick, Chris Page, Dave Pettey, Barry "Bit" Reich, Bill Rose, and Elliott Segal
