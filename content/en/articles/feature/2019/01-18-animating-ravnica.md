With *Ravnica Allegiance* now live on [*Magic: The Gathering Arena*](https://web.archive.org/web/20190118000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/mtgarena?DailyMTG), a whole host of new creatures are springing to life on the battlefield! This seems like a great time to showcase a few of these creatures with the animations that play each time you summon one of them to fight by your side.

You may have already seen these animations during the *MTG Arena* Early Access Streamer Event, which previewed the changes coming with the [latest patch](https://forums.mtgarena.com/forums/threads/45109) before they went live globally. Seeing them a second (or third, or fourth . . .) time doesn't make them any less cool, though!

* * *

[Angel of Grace](https://web.archive.org/web/20190118000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel+of+Grace) swoops into action to protect you in the direst situations.

![Angel of Grace](https://media.wizards.com/2019/images/daily/uDyuqWiKHI.gif)

* * *

[Rakdos, the Showstopper](https://web.archive.org/web/20190118000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos%2C+the+Showstopper) lives to be entertained—and what's more entertaining than random death?

![Rakdos, the Showstopper](https://media.wizards.com/2019/images/daily/9LLFoaGAcn.gif)

* * *

[Ravager Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20190118000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ravager+Wurm) erupts from underground, eating a tasty creature or maybe just leaving a crater in the earth.

![Ravager Wurm](https://media.wizards.com/2019/images/daily/gPW9hs5GKc.gif)

* * *

[Skarrgan Hellkite](https://web.archive.org/web/20190118000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skarrgan+Hellkite) looms over the battlefield, rushing headlong into battle or patiently keeping your opponents at bay.

![Skarrgan Hellkite](https://media.wizards.com/2019/images/daily/cqMi21b3kQ.gif)

* * *

And if you're looking to keep the damage party going long term, it doesn't get much better than a turn-three [Spawn of Mayhem](https://web.archive.org/web/20190118000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spawn+of+Mayhem)!

![Spawn of Mayhem](https://media.wizards.com/2019/images/daily/g391TYmVme.gif)

* * *

These are just a handful of the cards you can see spring into action for you in *MTG Arena*—most rares and mythic rares in *Ravnica Allegiance* have their own unique visual effects, and each of the new guild mechanics brings something special as well! To see them all, [download *MTG Arena*](https://web.archive.org/web/20190118000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/mtgarena?DailyMTG) today and join us on the battlefield!
