Many times, a card from a previous block becomes much more powerful once a future block's cards become Standard-legal. Sometimes, Magic R&D [does this intentionally](/en/articles/latestdevelopments/2014/01-03-core-developments-for-standard). [Mana Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20140620000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Bloom) is one such card. Prior to the release of *Journey into Nyx*, [Mana Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20140620000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Bloom) was not a very powerful card. When used only to accelerate your mana, [Mana Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20140620000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Bloom) is relatively poor. However, with the constellation mechanic, [Mana Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20140620000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Bloom) becomes one of the most powerful enablers, by returning to your hand as often as you want.

This deck takes advantage of two of the most powerful constellation cards—[Eidolon of Blossoms](https://web.archive.org/web/20140620000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eidolon+of+Blossoms) and [Doomwake Giant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140620000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doomwake+Giant). One common play is to play [Mana Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20140620000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Bloom) for X=1 on your second turn, then play [Eidolon of Blossoms](https://web.archive.org/web/20140620000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eidolon+of+Blossoms) on your third turn, returning [Mana Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20140620000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Bloom) on your fourth turn to get an extra enchantment to play each turn. If you want, you can set [Mana Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20140620000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Bloom) to X=0 each turn so you have a constant source of constellation triggers.

The rest of the deck contains some powerful enchantments that help provide board control. [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140620000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) and [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140620000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix) provide some extra synergy and card advantage. [Banishing Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20140620000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banishing+Light) and [Brain Maggot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140620000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brain+Maggot) provide some disruption for your opponent. These cards do this all while being enchantments, further strengthening your constellation strategy.
