**This promotion ended on December 15, 2011.** 

If you purchase [**Duels of the Planeswalkers** 2012](https://web.archive.org/web/20131207055117/http://www.wizards.com/magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx?x=mtg/digital/dotp/d12) when it comes out this summer (and we're hoping you do!), if eligible, you'll get an in-game code for a voucher you can redeem at your local participating game store for a physical promo card. There are three different cards, depending on which platform you're using. \*

\* Cards are available while supplies last. Void where prohibited. Check back for more information and official terms in the coming weeks.
