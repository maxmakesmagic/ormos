Contact a Summer of **Magic** location near you to find out the specific day they will be hosting their next Summer of **Magic** Tournament. Also, start time, format, product mix, and entry fee. 

### When

If you enjoyed [**Magic** Game Day](/en/events/2007/07gameday/magic-the-gathering-worldwide-game-day), take the next step and stretch out the fun throughout the Summer of **Magic**. Tournaments begin July 21 (the week after **Magic** Game Day) and run until September 22. Organizers may run up to 10 Summer of **Magic** tournaments throughout the summer.

### Tournament Structure/Formats

*   All Summer of **Magic** Tournaments will be sanctioned as Limited. (Sealed or Booster Draft only)
*   All tournaments should be run using Swiss rounds. (Minimum of 3 rounds of play)
*   *Tenth Edition* Sealed Deck: Each participant receive 5 boosters.
*   Booster Draft: Each participant receive 3 boosters from any **Magic** expansion or Core Set.
*   Sealed Deck: Each participant receive 1 Tournament Pack, and 2 boosters from any **Magic** expansion or Core Set.
*   All players must receive the same product mix.
*   Players build a 40-card minimum deck

### Notes

*   All Summer of **Magic** Tournaments are sanctioned by the DCI™
*   Summer of **Magic** Tournaments will not run on Fridays.
*   Entry Fees includes product used in tournament and any additional prize support.
