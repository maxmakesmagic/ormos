The*Lorwyn* artifact Twinning Glass is all about duality. Mechanically, if if a spell is played, the Glass lets you play a second copy of it. The flavor text tells a little story about the duality behind the construction of this kind of magical mirror. And Franz Vohwinkel’s art of the card bears some secret duality too.
