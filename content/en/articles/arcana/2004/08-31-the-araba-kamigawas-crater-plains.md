In the *[Champions of Kamigawa](https://web.archive.org/web/20040831000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/displayexpansion.asp?set=chk&page=1)* storyline, the Kami War has already devastated the plains surrounding Eiganjo Castle. Attacks by the kami have turned the fields surrounding the castle into a battered, pockmarked plain -- not unlike the surface of the moon -- called the Araba.

Here's a great look at the blasted Araba in Greg Staples' art of one of the basic Plains from *Champions of Kamigawa*:

  
*Plains illustration by Greg Staples* 

You can also see hints of the crater plains in art you've already seen on this site. Take a look at the lower left-hand corner of **Yosei, the Morning Star**, the 5/5 dragon kami previewed in [Mark Rosewater's column yesterday](https://web.archive.org/web/20040831000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/mr139):
