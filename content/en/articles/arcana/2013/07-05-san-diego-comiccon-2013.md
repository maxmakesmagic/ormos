San Diego ComicCon is fast approaching, taking place this year on July 18-21, 2013! **Magic** will be there doing a few things and we want to tell you about them!

### The Panel

Saturday, July 20, 2013 - 12 p.m. - 1 p.m. - Room 25ABC

Come to the **Magic** panel and get a preview of some of the exciting products and events that are coming up this summer and beyond for **Magic**! Join Mark Purvis (Brand Director), Mark Rosewater (**Magic**'s Head Designer), Jenna Helland (**Magic** Creative Designer), and Dave Guskin (**Magic** Experience Designer) for this discussion and Q&A.

The panel will not be streamed but it will be recorded and posted online after the event.

### Magic Gaming

The **Magic** gaming hall is in the Marriott Marquis Marina Ballrooms E&F. It will run from 10 a.m. to 11 p.m. from July 18 to July 20, and 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. on July 21.

Every day there is a free Beginners League where players are given a 30 card half deck and are able to win a promo card. More advanced players will love the All-day Intro Pack League. Pay $15 and receive a ***Magic** 2014* Intro Pack deck and play as much as you want for the entire weekend. Players match themselves in casual matches and earn a **Magic** card after every match, win or lose.

Daily tournaments include Legacy, Standard, Two-Headed Giant, 4-Pack Sealed Deck, and the two most popular convention formats Mini-Master and Sealed Deck from Standard packs.

To learn more visit [CascadeGames.com](http://cascadegames.com/events/941).

### SDCC Exclusive

There will be an exclusive box set for sale at the Hasbro Toy Shop booth (#3329) available for $65 (tax included). After the event a limited number will be put up for sale on the Hasbro Toy Shop website for $59.99 plus tax.
