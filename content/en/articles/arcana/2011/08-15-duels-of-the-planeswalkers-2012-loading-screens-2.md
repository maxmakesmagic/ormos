D**uels of the Planeswalkers** 2012 has some great loading screens. If you haven't played the game, you've missed some great shots of Planeswalkers. And if you *have* played it, you might not have allowed yourself to take the time to really soak in the awesomeness. So here's your chance to look at a few of the pieces commissioned specifically for [**Duels of the Planeswalkers** 2012](https://web.archive.org/web/20110815000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=Magic/Digital/DuelsOfThePlaneswalkers.aspx)!
