Each of the ten guilds of Ravnica (which you can learn about on the logically titled [GuildsofRavnica.com](http://guildsofravnica.com/)) has its [own fan kit](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218115654/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/guilds/fankits) containing high-res art, wallpapers, textures, logos, and everything you'd need to make a website themed to your favorite guild.

This, you might be thinking, is not news. Indeed, there was an Arcana [back in November](/en/articles/arcana/2012/11-15-the-izzet-fan-site-kit) that went over this and showed the coolness that was available on [the guild Fan Kit page](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218115654/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/guilds/fankits).

However! Something new has been added, because we're now talking about *Gatecrash*! Here's what's new, in each guild's kit:

*   The *Gatecrash* logo in eleven different languages
*   High-res art of Gideon, Champion of Justice and Domri Rade, with and without backgrounds
*   200x200 avatars of Gideon and Domri Rade for social media use
*   The *Gatecrash* textures and streaks
