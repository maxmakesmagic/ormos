# Standard, Historic, and Alchemy Changes

If you missed the news yesterday, there were many changes announced for formats on *MTG Arena*.

Here's the [latest Banned and Restricted announcement](/en/articles/feature/2022/01-25-january-25-2022-banned-and-restricted-announcement):

**Standard**:

[Alrund's Epiphany](https://web.archive.org/web/20220126000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Alrund%27s+Epiphany) is banned.

[Divide by Zero](https://web.archive.org/web/20220126000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divide+by+Zero) is banned.

[Faceless Haven](https://web.archive.org/web/20220126000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faceless+Haven) is banned.

**Historic**:

[Memory Lapse](https://web.archive.org/web/20220126000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memory+Lapse) is banned (from suspended).

[Teferi, Time Raveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220126000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%2C+Time+Raveler) [has been rebalanced and unbanned](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/01-25-alchemy-rebalancing-for-january-27-2022).

Plus, [the following cards were rebalanced for Alchemy and Historic](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/01-25-alchemy-rebalancing-for-january-27-2022):

[Check out these updates in the dedicated article breaking them down card by card](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/01-25-alchemy-rebalancing-for-january-27-2022) before they're available to play this Thursday, January 27!

# Mad Science Is Happening Now!

Don't miss your chance to fill out your collection of *Innistrad: Crimson Vow* Eternal Night card styles by playing in the Mad Science event going on right now until February 1!

This Alchemy event features the Zombies! special emblem with the text: "Whenever you cast an instant or sorcery spell, amass X, where X is that spell's mana value."

Check out the [complete details on the Mad Science event page](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/01-12-mtg-arena-mad-science-event).
