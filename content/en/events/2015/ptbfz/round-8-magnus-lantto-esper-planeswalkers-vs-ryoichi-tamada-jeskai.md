2014 *Magic Online* Champion Magnus Lantto had been on a tear. After missing the 2015 World Championship Top 8 on tiebreakers, he fought his way to the finals of Grand Prix Madison the weekend leading up to Pro Tour *Battle for Zendikar*. Not too shabby coming into the opening Pro Tour of the competitive year.

Ryoichi Tamada, known for a pair each of Japan Nationals and Grand Prix Top 8s, was looking to finally break out at a Pro Tour. His success—and respect as a player—back home in Japan hadn't yet been matched by performance around the world. Now, sitting at 7-1 like his opponent Lantto, it was a chance to set up a Top 8 run on Day Two.

His heat might be just was he needed to put the chill on Lantto.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptbfz/r8_lantto_tamada.jpg)  
*Sweden's Magnus Lantto has recently seen success in the last year, while Japan's Ryoichi Tamada is looking for his breakthrough on the global stage.*

# The Decks

Lantto's take on Esper Planeswalkers was the direct descendant of the Esper Dragons of Standard past. Now with [Shambling Vent](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shambling+Vent) and the new dual lands to keep the mana solid, the main decks featured reflected the new tools of *Battle for Zendikar*: [Painful Truths](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Painful+Truths), [Ruinous Path](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ruinous+Path), [Ob Nixilis Reignited](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ob+Nixilis+Reignited) and [Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar). In lieu of any Dragons was [Sorin, Solemn Visitor](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorin%2C+Solemn+Visitor) rounding out the ways to end games and stay ahead on the battlefield thanks to the suite of countermagic and discard around.

Tamada had a straightforward Jeskai deck. Unlike those adding [Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang) and other powerful black cards, Tamada doubled down on the classic Jeskai shell with full suites of [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider), [Seeker of the Way](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seeker+of+the+Way), and [Jeskai Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Charm). [Hangarback Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hangarback+Walker) and Jace, Vryn's Prodigy—the standouts from *Magic Origins*—were joined by the four-of [Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar). Packed full of threats and removal, Tamada looked to apply pressure nonstop.

# The Games

Both players took the early turns of the first game to set up, Lantto playing lands that didn't produce blue mana before drawing just two cards off [Painful Truths](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Painful+Truths); Tamada opening up with [Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar).

Lantto's [Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang) was met by [Jeskai Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Charm) and Tamada went into beatdown mode. [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider) joined in, dropping Lantto to 8 life in the next attacks. Just [Hangarback Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hangarback+Walker) set to one blocked Gideon's next strike as Murderous Cut—cast at full rate—cleared the skies for Lantto.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptbfz/r8_lantto.jpg)  
*Lantto's Esper deck seeks advantage through Planeswalkers rather than dragons.*

Lantto dug again with [Painful Truths](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Painful+Truths) for two cards, and made a [Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang) appear again. [Fiery Impulse](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Impulse) cleared the Thopter token for Tamada before he attacked with another [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider), and [Jeskai Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Charm) burned through the last life Lantto had left.

In the second game blue mana appeared early for Lantto, leading to a second turn Jace, Prodigy of Vryn. [Silkwrap](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silkwrap) was Tamada's answer before making a Jace of his own. Gidion, Ally of Zendikar appeared for Lantto this time and the Knight Ally tokens began to appear.

[Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider) crashed in for Tamada, after digging with Jace, but Lantto kept the pressure on with [Sorin, Solemn Visitor](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorin%2C+Solemn+Visitor) making creatures hit harder. A second [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider) was enough for Tamada to take out the Gideon and drop Sorin to just two loyalty.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptbfz/r8_tamada.jpg)  
*Tamada's Jeskai spells can put most opponents on a very fast clock.*

Lantto went digging with [Painful Truths](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Painful+Truths) for three cards, falling to 18 but rising back to 21 on the next attack. Tamada's [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider)s cleared Sorin and started chipping away at Lantto next, being joined by a [Hangarback Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hangarback+Walker) with two +1/1 counters. [Hangarback Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hangarback+Walker) for three counters was hit by Tamada's [Silkwrap](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silkwrap) and things started to fall apart for Lantto.

[Utter End](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Utter+End) cleared one [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider) as Lantto tried to rally back with another [Hangarback Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hangarback+Walker). [Jeskai Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Charm) put it on top of the library and Tamada took a swing at the [Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar) Lantto tried as well.

After refilling off [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) and looting with [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy), Tamada had it all: [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider) finished off the opposing Gideon and adding his own was enough.

"Good game," Lantto said, the small army of Knight Ally tokens insignificant against the matching, and larger, [Hangarback Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20151017000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hangarback+Walker)s on Tamada's side.

**Lantto 0 – Tamada 2**
