Boston played host last weekend to both PAX East and the [**Magic Online** Championship](https://web.archive.org/web/20130329000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/butakov-tramples-his-way-past-opposition), won by Russia's Dmitriy "Butakov" Butakov! Up the road a ways, Brock Parker returned to the pro stage by winning [Grand Prix Pittsburgh](https://web.archive.org/web/20130329000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/parker-makes-proud-return-pittsburgh). Congratulations to both! The Modern Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze* qualifier season has wrapped up, but we still have some results coming in. The new Pro Tour *Theros* season is in Standard, so be sure to start paying attention to Standard decks (like the ones linked to below!) to get a grasp on the current metagame. And find one of those nifty Standard PTQs near you with a[this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#theros)! Good luck; have fun!
