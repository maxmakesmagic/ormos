Rei Nakazawa, a member of the *Onslaught* creative team, was kind enough to provide us with some concept notes on the Pit-Fighter Legends. Here's what Rei has to say about his part in the creation of these powerhouses...

<table><tbody><tr><td><b><a>Arcanis The Omnipotent</a>:</b> This wizard's name comes from the word &quot;arcane,&quot; meaning obscure. The character was concepted as something dark and mysterious—so mysterious, in fact, that no one knows whether &quot;he&quot; is male or female or even human! Justin Sweet did a great job with the artwork, communicating just how sinister the unknown can be.</td>&#10;<td><img alt="Arcanis the Omnipotent" src="https://i.ormos.uk/6c/31/6c31c58075a1a97f57567d03e5d3ee0524f9ddd60e101b0d146d26122f0d8635.jpg" title="Arcanis the Omnipotent"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr><td><b><a>Rorix Bladewing</a>:</b> This dragon's first name comes from the word &quot;roar.&quot; We wanted something different for this draconic legend than the tall, beefy dragons we normally do, so we asked for a serpentine dragon with knife-like wings (to make sense out of &quot;Bladewing&quot;) that could fly very fast (thus the haste). Note the spectators watching from the special platform at the Grand Coliseum in the art.</td>&#10;<td><img alt="Rorix Bladewing" src="https://i.ormos.uk/93/c6/93c6c9218187308cc004607622c4def89e6f5756d0044f8850f1e396476fc9ce.jpg" title="Rorix Bladewing"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr><td><b><a>Jareth, Leonine Titan</a>:</b> I chose the name &quot;Jareth&quot; because it sounded white, soldierly, and cool. The original concept was a powerful, heavily armored human soldier, but that seemed too mundane for such a powerful creature, so it was altered into a lion-like living stone statue. We asked that his only weapon be a nasty-looking shield, in keeping with his defensively oriented abilities. For those of you who haven't looked closely at the artwork, he's carrying the head of a lizard-like creature (maybe Viashino, maybe not). Its headless, twitching corpse—cropped from the card image—lies at Jareth's feet.</td>&#10;<td><img alt="Jareth Leonine TItan" src="https://i.ormos.uk/2f/ad/2fadfceffc1007de2d247d0cbe8fd0084cd9f34f7583ece607ebdbbfd2e15591.jpg" title="Jareth Leonine TItan"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr><td><b><a>Visara The Dreadful</a>:</b> This nasty creature's name comes from the word &quot;vision.&quot; My first attempt at a name for her was &quot;Gazera,&quot; from &quot;gaze.&quot; She was originally concepted as a Vampire, which was appropriate when she had the additional ability to gain +1/+1 counters for each creature she killed with her tap ability. But when R&amp;D removed that line (thank goodness!), being a Vampire seemed less appropriate. That's when we remembered <a>Masked Gorgon</a>. Since Gorgons turn people into stone, that seemed to be a more interesting creature type. (Note that <a>Masked Gorgon</a> can't destroy creatures because her eyes are, well, masked.) The only change we requested from the Matthew Wilson's <a>Masked Gorgon</a> art was that Visara have wings made of the same shell-like material the <a>Masked Gorgon</a>'s claw is made of.</td>&#10;<td><img alt="Visara the Dreadful" src="https://i.ormos.uk/3f/47/3f4749dad0a3172d5f1f1affb707c46da11f42cfc381b5a835aa46db6c2386ba.jpg" title="Visara the Dreadful"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr><td><b><a>Silvos, Rogue Elemental</a>:</b> This huge critter's name comes from &quot;sylvan,&quot; meaning &quot;characteristic of the woods or forest.&quot; His concept went through the most changes of the five, even as R&amp;D continued to increase his power. One of the more amusing ones was a giant, King Kong-like gorilla, but this struck some people as a little too silly. Finally, we settled on a fungal elemental along the lines of such creatures as <i>Fallen Empires' </i><a>Thallid Devourer</a>.</td>&#10;<td><img alt="Silvos Rogue Elemental" src="https://i.ormos.uk/fb/2c/fb2ca087f88af14ddf8f4f0677caf1a84c1cab7bffb63ff99bdf60d5d5ffa5f6.jpg" title="Silvos Rogue Elemental"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>
