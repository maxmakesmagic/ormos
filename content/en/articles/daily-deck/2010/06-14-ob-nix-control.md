Hall of Famer Ben Rubin used a card picked to be a key part of Standard when Zendikar was released in his Block deck at Pro Tour–San Juan: Ob Nixilis, the Fallen. He also used Rampaging Baloths and a host of mana acceleration like Explore, Growth Spasm, and Harrow. Could his deck, which netted him a Day 2 finish at the Pro Tour, be the jumping off point for the next big deck in Standard? Why not try your take on it at your FNM tournament today to find out!
