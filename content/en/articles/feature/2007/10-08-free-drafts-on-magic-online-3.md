Hey everyone, 

We're hoping that thousands of MOL players across the globe can help us out with the next phase of our beta testing for MOL V3. The latest build has just recently been deployed, and features many new features. If it's been a while since you've logged into the latest version of Magic's greatest online game, please consider updating your current beta client (no download needed from this page! Just log on!) or downloading the client from the link provided, and on THURSDAY evening October the 11th, between 4pm and 7pm Pacific time, help us to load test the current client. We're shooting for 1000 people, help us get there!

What's in it for you guys? Free drafts! For the entire time the beta is running, you will be allowed to draft for free, and provided with product to do so, which should be "purchasable" from the online store that is integrated within the client. Just open the client, poke around, and draft until you drop! No money is required, and the store will be set up to allow requests for product. Lots of formats will be available from years past, including IPA drafts! The first ever set released on MOL is back in the draft queues, for free, for a limited time, along with many other options like RAV block, TSP block, and more.

If you have an account for the current client it should be enabled with the same login/password you normally use. If you do not, or wish to set up a new account, our new account creation should be working so just follow the steps from the first screen. Downloading and installing should take 20-30 minutes total, so please plan accordingly. All 2.5 accounts that exist as of 3 weeks ago should work correctly. If you've changed your password in that time, please use the old one.

There will be lots of WotC staff on during peak times to help you and answer questions, as well as Adepts who can answer questions as well. 

Please visit our forums to discuss your experiences on the beta, or just chat about MOL in general, [here](http://forums.gleemax.com/forumdisplay.php?f=372).

Download the client now from here: [**MOL V3 INSTALLER**](https://web.archive.org/web/20071008000000/http://mtgoinstaller1.onlinegaming.wizards.com/mtgov3/MTGOIII_Helper.exe)

Thanks for your help everyone, we look forward to releasing MOL V3 when it's ready and giving our customers a better online Magic playing experience.
