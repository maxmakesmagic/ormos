The 2009 **Magic** Pro Tour showcased big names and big plays at [Pro Tour–Austin](https://web.archive.org/web/20091026000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/kibler-completes-comeback-austin-victory), and now it's time to see how the dust has settled. Austin showcased the best players in Extended and *Zendikar* Booster Draft. What decks emerged from the new Extended as the top dogs? Is combo the way to go, or is aggro a better choice? Paul Jordan crunched the numbers for all the major deck archetypes. If you're looking for the hard numbers on what beat what, you won't want to miss it.

*   **[Feature: Pro Tour–Austin Extended Metagame Analysis](https://web.archive.org/web/20091026000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/feature/62a)**

*   [**Recap and Winner Interview**](http://webcast2.wizards.com/podcasts/09austin/austin_highlights.mp3) *by PT Austin* (15.6MB)

Rich Hagon grabbed winner Brian Kibler for an in-depth interview moments after the Dragonmaster defeated Tsuyoshi Ikeda in the final. After a four-year absence from the game, Kibler has now made back-to-back Top 8s and put his name in the discussion for next year's Hall of Fame class. You can listen to the podcast via the player to the right, or go to the Podcast page to download it.

*   **[Podcast: Austin Highlights and Winner Interview](https://web.archive.org/web/20091026000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/podcast/33)**

And if you missed the Top 8 webcast, Evan Erwin has edited the finals down to highlight clips that you can watch right here. If you want the full story, sit back and check out the [complete Top 8 videos](https://web.archive.org/web/20091026000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/Magazine/Events.aspx?x=mtgevent/webcast/home).
