Here's something cool that went on sale on **Magic Online** yesterday.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/72/2c/722c66c4735b342ea70e8dcd2cdfc11674827273126ffd7e57e1291bc83f1f32.jpg)  

These **Magic Online**-only decks are designed to be competitive in Legacy out of the box. For $29.99, you get a sixty-card deck, a fifteen-card sideboard, and two Event Tickets to get you started.

Would you like to see what's in the decks? Well ... okay!
