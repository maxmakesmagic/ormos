Yesterday we highlighted an Extended take on blue-black-red control. Today it's time for the Standard version. Alex Muhich loves planeswalkers and wanted to play as many as he could at Grand Prix Dallas-Fort Worth. His control strategy enabled him to do exactly that, starring Koth of the Hammer; Tezzeret, Agent of Bolas; and Jace, the Mind Sculptor. The build was good enough to make the second day of competition! For more on Grand Prix Dallas-Fort Worth, check out the [official event coverage](/en/events/2011/gpdal11/shiels-stands-alone-in-dallas).
