One Dragon that doesn't fit the normal mold is the rarely-seen [Nalathni Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20030428000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nalathni+Dragon):

The card was created as a giveaway for the [Dragon\*Con](http://www.dragoncon.org/) convention in 1994 (hence the dragon expansion symbol). It was also distributed in *The Duelist* #3 (much to the annoyance of people that paid/traded heavily for them after Dragon\*Con).

Not only is [Nalathni Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20030428000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nalathni+Dragon) the smallest Dragon ever made, it also has the very out-of-flavor ability of banding (and is the only red creature with the ability).

Nalathni Dragon is legal in Type 1 tournaments. The card's Oracle text:

**Nalathni Dragon**  
![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless2.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif)  
 Creature – Dragon  
 Flying, banding  
![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif): Nalathni Dragon gets +1/+0 until end of turn. If this ability is played four or more times during this turn, sacrifice Nalathni Dragon at end of turn.
