I sat down with Jůza, whose current record is 8-3, to learn more about the deck.

**It looks similar to Mardu midrange deck, but without red. How did you arrive at the conclusion to cut the color?** 

"It's Brad Nelson's idea, and it makes sense," Jůza said. "[Butcher of the Horde](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Butcher+of+the+Horde) is not that great because it dies too easily, and [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike) is mediocre because it doesn't actually kill all that much in the format. [Goblin Rabblemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Rabblemaster) and [Hordeling Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hordeling+Outburst) were good in LA, but they die to [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight) and [Anger of the Gods](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anger+of+the+Gods), and many people were adding those cards since last weekend. If everyone has those removal spells, then you are better off running [Raise the Alarm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raise+the+Alarm) and [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos)." 

"Meanwhile, [Herald of Torment](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Herald+of+Torment) is very good against all of the ground creatures. I liked both Brimaz and Herald, but you can't play a double-black card and a double-white card in a deck with [Chained to the Rocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chained+to+the+Rocks). You need at least 6 basic Mountains for that, and then the mana base doesn't work. Cutting red was the solution." 

**So two colors it is. What are the good and bad matchups for your B/W deck?** 

"Mono-Red is the best matchup because you have all the life-gain cards. And the two-color mana base is great: instead of taking damage, you're actually gaining life with [Scour](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scour)ed Barren. Jeskai is a good matchup for similar reasons. Abzan is close, very much play-draw dependent, but if you can trigger [Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc), you should be fine. The bad matchups are the [Hornet Queen](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hornet+Queen) decks." 

**What is the ideal curve of the deck?** 

"[Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) on turn one, [Seeker of the Way](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seeker+of+the+Way) on turn two, Brimaz on turn three, Sorin on turn four, and [Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc) on turn five. I actually play 26 lands because you want to hit your curve and I have effectively eight five-drops in [Herald of Torment](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Herald+of+Torment) and [Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc)." 

**Can you explain some of your sideboard choices?** 

"[Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) and [Read the Bones](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Read+the+Bones) are meant against control. [Drown in Sorrow](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drown+in+Sorrow) comes in against Mono-Red. [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion) is good against Green Devotion and Mardu Mirange. [Soldier of the Pantheon](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soldier+of+the+Pantheon) is great against Abzan Aggro and also comes in against control decks so you can take out your removal spells. Finally, [Banishing Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banishing+Light) deals with problematic cards like [Whip of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos) and Planeswalkers, and [Silence the Believers](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silence+the+Believers) can take out Stormbreath, Sarkhan, or [Boon Satyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boon+Satyr). I generally take out [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) against attrition decks; [Raise the Alarm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raise+the+Alarm) is too slow on the draw against green decks; and [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight) is bad against Abzan Midrange." 

**Would you make any changes to the deck?** 

"I would add [Mass Calcify](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mass+Calcify) to the sideboard because Green Devotion is not a great matchup." 

**How big has the surprise factor been for you this weekend?** 

"People are not expecting [Raise the Alarm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raise+the+Alarm), so sometimes they attack their [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic) People are also not expecting cards like [Herald of Torment](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Herald+of+Torment). And while they may have sideboard plans against all of the top decks, they usually don't know how to board against me." 

Overall, the deck is filled with cards that are well-positioned right now, and as long as people don't know what to expect from it, the deck could be a strong contender in Standard.
