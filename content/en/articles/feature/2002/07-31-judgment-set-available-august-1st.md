### Joe Hauck Talks About The Online Set Release

* * *

Dear **Magic** Community,

Since ***Magic** Online* is taking us into uncharted waters, I felt it was best to hold back the *Judgment* online set until we could observe and monitor how a new platform for **Magic** would impact tournaments and the greater **Magic** community.

I am very excited to announce that beginning August 1, 2002, players will be able to purchase online *Judgment* cards for ***Magic: The Gathering** Online.* That day, the system will be down temporarily for about four hours while we incorporate system updates, as well as any other adjustments required for online *Judgment* purchasing, playing or tournament activities. As we get closer to August 1, our customer service folks will announce a more detailed downtime schedule.

I would like to take this opportunity to say that our decision to release *Judgment* at this time was in no way connected to the electronic viability of the set. Leaping Lizards is a first-class development house that delivered a quality *Judgment* online set, and delivered it on schedule. I have the utmost confidence that they will continue this level of performance for all future sets. 

I also want to emphasize that we are very committed to making online sets available closer to the release of paper sets in the future, and we are still working on the timing for these. After the online release of the *OnslaughtTM* set, we expect to have a policy in place for the timing of online releases in relation to the paper-based sets. An announcement will be made regarding the *Onslaught* online release some time in September, before the paper pre-release tournaments.

Our mission has always been to provide more opportunities and ways for people to play **Magic**. To that end, ***Magic** Online* was developed to supplement and augment the **Magic** paper experience - not replace it - and provide more connectivity for the **Magic** community. 

Thank you to all who have embraced ***Magic** Online,* making the launch of a revolution in **Magic** play such a success! 

Sincerely Yours,  
 Joe Hauck  
 Vice President  
**Magic: The Gathering** and  
***Magic: The Gathering** Online*
