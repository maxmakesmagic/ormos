Wizards of the Coast R&D member Steve Warner designed this deck for gunslinging at last weekend's *Rise of the Eldrazi* Prelease. How does it work? You simply build up your mana early with Explore while stalling with Wall of Omens. Then, when your opponent least suspects it, hit them with Summoning Trap. The instant can plop a Pelakka Wurm, Baneslayer Angel, even a gigantic Eldrazi onto the battlefield! It shouldn't take too much to figure out what you should do from there ....
