Battle of Wits leads to the types of decks we here at Daily Decks just love: innovative, goofy, and really wild! But when a player like Conley Woods tell you to play Battle of Wits, as he did recently in his weekly column for ChannelFireball.com, the deck steps up to the next level. Conley has a Pro Tour Top 8 under his belt and is well regarded as one of the Pro Tour's top American players. He'll kick off the 2010 season at Pro Tour–San Diego this weekend. That event won't feature Extended, but you can play Conley's deck at a [Pro Tour Qualifier](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-pro-tour-qualifiers-2) near you.
