T*heros* releases today! So before we dive into the upcoming Friday Night **Magic** promos, let's first go over everything you can get at tonight's FNM.

First up, you can still get the September FNM Promo: [Experiment One](https://web.archive.org/web/20130927000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Experiment+One).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1d/c3/1dc3298a6ce129319b01477773a8f6eb4768ec2324c5342cb92d0f95d70f4386.jpg)  

Then you also can get the *Theros* release promo: [Bident of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20130927000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bident+of+Thassa).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6e/0c/6e0c71cead8eb61307810b86fdd29ca6e6f182f0e9f8868c7f558ea4a400529d.jpg)  

If you seek the aid of the gods and [solve the puzzle](https://web.archive.org/web/20130927000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/checking-out-nykthos-2013-09-24) you'll get the new Hero card to help you on the Hero's Path:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/45/7a/457af0eb3934dc363ca830ce308b8f6dfbd16cd919d1b5f985ab9bdaf13d2cf5.jpg)  

Not a bad haul for a night of playing **Magic** with your friends!
