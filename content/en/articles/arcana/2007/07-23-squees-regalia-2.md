As a brains-challenged cabin hand, a lover of bug cuisine, a fierce protector of one part of the Legacy, and arguably the worst cook that ever served aboard a skyship, Squee is an irrepressible character of **Magic** continuity.

As Squee, Goblin Nabob is reprinted in *Tenth Edition*, he's getting back in the limelight for his "immortality" mechanic. But he had quite a history in the storyline before becoming so famously hard to kill.

Squee's political career was launched when he encountered the Kyren, a species of intelligent and unusually politically-minded goblins in Mercadia. They made Squee a general, and outfitted him with General's Regalia.
