Go to the ***Magic** 2014* Core Set Prerelease and experience the newest **Magic: The Gathering** set a week before it goes on sale! 

You will also receive an exclusive promo card (Megantic Sliver) while supplies last!

### Where Can I Play on July 13–14, 2013?

Prereleases are happening at local stores all over the world! Check out our [tournament locator](https://web.archive.org/web/20130826122408/http://locator.wizards.com/) to find a local store Prerelease event near you. Contact your local store to confirm the event venue location. 

### What Happens There?

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/tcg/products/m14/azsxdcfv/asdfasdf_PRM_Prerelease_EN_FF.jpg)

When you show up at a Prerelease, you will be given the opportunity to play with the newest cards in the following formats:

**Sealed Deck:** Each player receives six ***Magic** 2014* Core Set booster packs to use for building a deck. 

**Two-Headed Giant Sealed:** Each team of two players receives eight ***Magic** 2014* Core Set booster packs to use for building a deck. 

### What Do I Get?

In addition to the booster packs, each player receives (while supplies last):

One promo card (Megantic Sliver)  
 One achievement card 

  

There are also optional achievements that players can complete to earn a Planeswalker Points badge and achievement.

You can complete the achievement card you start at the ***Magic** 2014* Core Set Prerelease later, so be sure to bring it with you when you go to play League matches. 

Players who complete all of a Planeswalker's achievements on the achievement card will earn a code that can be redeemed on [PlaneswalkerPoints.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20130825063029/http://www.wizards.com/magic/planeswalkerpoints). 

For the prize pool, Wizards of the Coast provides two ***Magic** 2014* Core Set booster packs per player in the tournament. Contact your local Prerelease tournament organizer for specific entry fee, prize payout, and event information. 

### What Else Can I Do?

In addition to the main Prerelease Tournament event, some locations may offer open dueling.

**Open Dueling:** Open Dueling players each receive: 

One ***Magic** 2014* Core Set Intro Pack  
 One promo card (Megantic Sliver, while supplies last) 

  

Open Dueling players use their Intro Packs to play against each other as well as Prerelease tournament players who are between matches. All players should be encouraged to help teach Open Dueling participants how to play **Magic**. 

### Have Questions?

Players and tournament organizers who have general questions about Prereleases or any other Wizards of the Coast offerings should contact Customer Service or the WPN Support Team at [Wizards.com/CustomerService](http://wizards.custhelp.com/), or call 800-324-6496 in North America, or check the global contact list for their local Customer Service office at [Wizards.com/GlobalCS](https://web.archive.org/web/20130826122408/http://company.wizards.com/contactus).
