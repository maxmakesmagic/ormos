**Legacy Cube Begins On Wednesday**

Beginning Wednesday, January 14, following the downtime, the Legacy Cube will make its triumphant return to *Magic Online*. Accompanying the Legacy Cube will be triple *Odyssey* Flashback Drafts. Both *Odyssey* and Legacy Cube Events run in Swiss and Single Elimination Draft. Both sets of queues conclude with the Wednesday, January 28, downtime.

Check out the [article](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/01-05-resolve-to-legacy-cube) for the full details.

* * *

***Magic Online* is Hiring!**

Do you know *Magic Online* inside-out? Do you have previous judge experience? If so, you should apply to be a [Digital Events Coordinator](https://web.archive.org/web/20150113000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/digital-events-coordinator-temporary-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1) for *Magic Online*!

We're also on the search for a savvy [Assistant Digital Product Manager](https://web.archive.org/web/20150113000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/assistant-digital-product-manager-mtgo-cardsets-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1) to work on *Magic Online* Cardsets. Read about these jobs, as well as many others, on the Wizards of the Coast [careers page](https://web.archive.org/web/20150113000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers).

* * *

**Downtime for January 14, 2015**

*   All tournaments will close Wednesday, January 14, at 5:00 a.m. Pacific (13:00 UTC)
*   Store and trade activity will be suspended at approximately 7:45 a.m. Pacific(15:45 UTC)
*   The system will be down from 8:00 a.m. Pacific (16:00 UTC) until 12:00 p.m. Pacific (20:00 UTC)
