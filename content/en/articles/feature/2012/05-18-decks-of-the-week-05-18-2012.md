Congratulations to Alexander Hayne, winner of [Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored*](https://web.archive.org/web/20120518000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/angels-carry-hayne-miraculous-victory)! Hayne was miraculously able to [Entreat the Angels](https://web.archive.org/web/20120518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Entreat+the+Angels) to fly over Gaudenis Vidugiris in the fifth game of the finals for the victory. To get that chance, he had to overcome a 1–3 start to the tournament and face Hall of Famer Jon Finkel in the Top 8. You can find the Top 8 decks, along with all decks that recorded at least 18 points in the ten *Innistrad* Block Constructed rounds during Swiss competition, below.
