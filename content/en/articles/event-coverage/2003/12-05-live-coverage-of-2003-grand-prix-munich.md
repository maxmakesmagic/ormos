Hot on the heels of his [victory in Lyon](/en/articles/event-coverage/2003/10-25-live-coverage-of-2003-grand-prix-lyon), Yann Hamon proves the critics wrong with his second Grand Prix tournament victory in as many months. Could this be the evolution of a new French power? Hamon mercilessly destroyed his opponents and proved that he couldn't be defeated as he crushed those who would challenge him with his aggressive Blue/Green tempo deck. In the end, it was a cunning strategy, excellent drafting, and a fearless attitude that proved Hamon had what it takes to be a two-time champion.
