A fan of White Weenie? At the Nashville $5k, hosted by StarCityGames.com, Shane Giachetti took this build to a top performance. Abusing Emeria, the Sky Ruin by playing both Kor Cartographer and Knight of the White Orchid, the deck almost always has enough Plains on the battlefield to trigger the *Zendikar* rare. Add powerful spells like Baneslayer Angel, Path to Exile, and Ajani Goldmane and you have a competitive masterpiece!
