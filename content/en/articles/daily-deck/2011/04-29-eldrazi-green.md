The deck of choice for green mages at heart, Eldrazi Green allows you to quickly cast powerful Eldrazi while most opponents are still trying to summon Squadron Hawks and Goblin Guides. Eldrazi Temple and Eye of Ugin provide free mana for the colorless monsters while Overgrown Battlement and Joraga Treespeaker give you an additional boost. Opponent playing countermagic? Use Summoning Trap to sneak a creature to the battlefield, or cast Emrakul, the Aeons Torn to avoid the hassle of getting your big threat countered!
