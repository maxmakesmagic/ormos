When it came time for the first *Zendikar* style guide images to be made, Richard Whitters knew he had to assemble a crack squad of artists.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4d/38/4d38b4c632f317c6de11a545659779978109be9f75586a3055befb1a6aa2493d.jpg)  

Mark Tedin! A **Magic** artist from way back.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/47/22/4722cea88c2193636d637c1db0c2a6bb4100bdc10be97c757e49ea606c3236bf.jpg)  

Vincent Proce! The new guy on the block.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/bd/10/bd10209a81fcad2a045e9e252d45cfaa8b19666aee825c1b3b312fc72a5f9d51.jpg)  

Matt Cavotta! He's done it before and he'll do it again.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c1/7f/c17f47ccbdc5b6c02794f7236c7ed88b7efb4ac5cec8e255d8ad6f2c38297832.jpg)  

Here they are, along with Richard, after having covered the whiteboard behind them with sketches and ideas. Ideas like this:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/71/76/7176a3d2db8c11c6b6ece14cb32b9b8ea9933d65ac88a88593547dff3dca2126.jpg)  

And this:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e0/29/e02942811b4c305385f8824813718d7f7f4c6b4877c1ab90c1b501edcd93182a.jpg)  

Of course, you don't get ideas out of nowhere. Mark, Vincent, Matt, and Richard spent much of their time in consultation with key people. Like Mark Rosewater!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ae/c9/aec9298f4675b6065b38e90a4da96bc3211cc498c8422f1a4049b64c3dbfd9a0.jpg)  

And Brady Dommermuth (with special guest Mark Rosewater)!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/af/36/af360b34237b8ac71b3389de614dfdb3a70758869df3f63d0982ddf12dbe2291.jpg)  

The best thing about Style Guide Time is that the area around the Creative Team is covered with fascinating things.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a7/ea/a7ea6afc409fa1978e69d22f0f18034145f2d9f58b1f70c9a024384169ab01fb.jpg)  

The tricky part is that we always have to hold onto these cool pictures for months before it's okay to show them off!
