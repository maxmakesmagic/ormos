# What is the best common in *Battle for Zendikar* Draft, and why is it Blisterpod?

Sometimes opinions can be divided on the best common or uncommon card in any given format, but not for *Battle for Zendikar*, this time it's absolutely obvious. I asked some of the best players here this weekend why they felt [Blisterpod](https://web.archive.org/web/20151011000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blisterpod) is hands down the best card ever printed.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsyd15/GPSydney_D2_QQ_Takahashi_LG.jpg)  
**Zen Takahashi:** Because Black-Green sacrifice is the best deck, according to Jason Chung, even though he's only drafted it once. The best common is probably [Eldrazi Skyspawner](https://web.archive.org/web/20151011000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Skyspawner), though.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsyd15/GPSydney_D2_QQ_Jackson_LG.jpg)  
**Paul Jackson:** [Outnumber](https://web.archive.org/web/20151011000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Outnumber) is second only to [Blisterpod](https://web.archive.org/web/20151011000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blisterpod). [Outnumber](https://web.archive.org/web/20151011000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Outnumber) is so good because it allows you to be the first person to play more than 1 spell a turn as early as turn 3. It gives you so much tempo.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsyd15/GPSydney_D2_QQ_Swords_LG.jpg)  
**Chester Swords:** [Complete Disregard](https://web.archive.org/web/20151011000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Complete+Disregard).  
 (Editor: to my question? Or the card?)  
 Chester: Both.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsyd15/GPSydney_D2_QQ_Cameron_LG.jpg)  
**Maitland Cameron:** Incorrect! It's [Outnumber](https://web.archive.org/web/20151011000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Outnumber).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsyd15/GPSydney_D2_QQ_Loy_LG.jpg)  
**Sam Loy:** The best common is [Clutch of Currents](https://web.archive.org/web/20151011000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clutch+of+Currents).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsyd15/GPSydney_D2_QQ_Chew_LG.jpg)  
**Kelvin Chew:** Because... it's actually [Eldrazi Skyspawner](https://web.archive.org/web/20151011000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Skyspawner)?
