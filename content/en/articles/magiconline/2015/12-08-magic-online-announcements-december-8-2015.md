**No Downtime on December 30, 2015**

There will be no *Magic Online* downtime on Wednesday, December 30. This causes a few oddities with regular *Magic Online* services.

*   Set redemptions purchased after the December 23 downtime will not be processed until the January 6 downtime.

***Legendary Cube* and *Commander (2015 Edition)* Known Issues**

**Gameplay**

*   Cardset | [Caged Sun](https://web.archive.org/web/20151208000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Caged+Sun)’s mana ability incorrectly doesn't trigger when a land is tapped for an ability that produces more than one mana.
*   Cardset | Abilities that generate replacement effects preventing specific cards from being put into graveyards (such as those found on [Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20151208000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Progenitus) or [Blightsteel Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20151208000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blightsteel+Colossus)) do not function properly while resolving [Grenzo, Dungeon Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20151208000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grenzo%2C+Dungeon+Warden)'s activated ability.
*   Cardset | All legendary creature cards in all zones owned by a player who controls a [Heroes' Podium](https://web.archive.org/web/20151208000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heroes%27+Podium) incorrectly receive the power/toughness bonus from its first ability.
*   Cardset | [Kumano, Master Yamabushi](https://web.archive.org/web/20151208000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kumano%2C+Master+Yamabushi)'s ability does not cause creature cards to be exiled instead of being put into the graveyard.
*   Cardset | [Myriad Landscape](https://web.archive.org/web/20151208000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myriad+Landscape)’s activated ability incorrectly allows a player to put two basic lands that do not share a land type onto the battlefield.
*   Cardset | [Kurkesh, Onakke Ancient](https://web.archive.org/web/20151208000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kurkesh%2C+Onakke+Ancient) does not copy activated abilities of artifacts that sacrifice as part of their activation cost.
*   Cardset | Cards cast with conspire that require a player to sacrifice a creature as an additional cost to cast, such as [Life's Legacy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151208000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Life%27s+Legacy) and [Momentous Fall](https://web.archive.org/web/20151208000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Momentous+Fall), incorrectly look at the power and toughness of the last creature clicked for conspire.
*   Cardset | [Villainous Wealth](https://web.archive.org/web/20151208000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Villainous+Wealth)'s prompt box text incorrectly suggests that the caster must cast all appropriate exiled spells. This is a display issue only. The Cancel button allows the player to continue without casting remaining spells.
*   Cardset | Copying [All Suns' Dawn](https://web.archive.org/web/20151208000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=All+Suns%27+Dawn) sometimes causes a crash.
*   Cardset | Casting [Ray of Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20151208000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ray+of+Command) without paying its mana cost, via a spell or ability such as cascade or [Mizzix's Mastery](https://web.archive.org/web/20151208000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzix%27s+Mastery), incorrectly requires the player to pay the mana cost.
