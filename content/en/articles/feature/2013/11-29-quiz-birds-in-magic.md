### The Mistform Ultimus Disclaimer

Every time I do one of these quizzes I inevitably get emails and tweets saying how I forget about the Changelings and [Mistform Ultimus](https://web.archive.org/web/20131129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistform+Ultimus).

Let this serve as an official disclaimer that I am purposefully and willfully ignoring them for the purposes of this quiz.

* * *

 

Q**1.** Birds appear in every color of **Magic**, with blue having the most. Which color has the fewest? (Note: We are talking mono-color birds.)

 

*(Difficulty: Easy)*

[50/50 Hint](#) Click this to eliminate two wrong answers.

*   [White](#) WRONG White is the second-most prevalent, owing largely to the propensity for Aven creatures to be white.
*   [Black](#) WRONG While low, the Vultures of **Magic** buoy this count.
*   [Red](#) WRONG Red also struggles in the Bird department; however, the presence of Rocs in **Magic** lift them.
*   [Green](#) RIGHT! Despite having [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20131129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise), one of the most famous Birds in **Magic**, green is largely bereft of Birds, with only five!

Q**5.** *Commander* (2013 Edition) gave us [Derevi, Empyrial Tactician](https://web.archive.org/web/20131129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Derevi%2C+Empyrial+Tactician), who is a legendary Bird Wizard. A fact remarked upon in [the latest "Friday Nights" episode](/en/articles/feature/2013/11-06-friday-nights-politics). But, Derevi is far from our first legendary Bird. Which of these other legendary Birds was the first to see print?

*(Difficulty: Hard)*

[50/50 Hint](#) Click this to eliminate two wrong answers.

*   [Commander Eesha](#) WRONG *Judgment* hit the shelves in 2002.
*   [Kangee, Aerie Keeper](#) RIGHT! Kangee was printed in *Invasion*, which released in October of 2000.
*   [Lieutenant Kirtar](#) WRONG The Lieutenant was released in *Odyssey*, which hit the shelves in October of 2001.
*   [Major Teroh](#) WRONG Major Teroh was in *Torment*, which released in February of 2002.
