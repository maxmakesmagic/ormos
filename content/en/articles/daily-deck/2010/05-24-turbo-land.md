National Qualifiers recently took place all across North America, leading to a TON of fun new decks! One of the neatest comes from Arizona, and was piloted by Pro Tour star Adam Prosak. Judging by the list, it looks like he was loosely inspired by Hall of Famer Zvi Mowshowitz's Turbo Land from years gone by. Adam's plan was to play out lots of extra lands, use Time Warp and Fog to take extra turns and stay alive, and beat his opponents down with tons of tokens from Rampaging Baloths. Unfortunately for Adam, the deck was only good enough for 3rd place ... and he needed to finish 1st or 2nd for an invite to U.S. Nationals! See more National Qualifier deck lists [here](/en/events/2010/natqual10/2010-national-qualifiers-top-8-standard-deck-lists).
