Can't get enough of *Torment?* Want more advance information? Then check out the current issue of *Sideboard* Magazine... Included are *Torment* previews of [Basking Rootwalla](https://web.archive.org/web/20020108000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basking+Rootwalla) by Mike Turian and [Nantuko Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20020108000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Shade) by David Price. Plus, our own Mark Rosewater gives a few more hints (including [Mind Sludge](https://web.archive.org/web/20020108000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind+Sludge)) in his "Insider Trading" column.

And, as usual, you get top-notch coverage of major events, profiles of your favorite players, and strategy articles straight from the Pros! The *Sideboard* Magazine is available at all premier stores and premier events.
