*Magic Online* Player of the Year Standings Return

 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6e/4a/6e4a7a020ca50c43233f51a0b066f9f7456faed9cbb7fb16e2fad2f3d308ec0b.jpg)

We're tracking Player of the Year standings once again on [MTGO.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20140828000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/magic-online)! Both [Limited](https://web.archive.org/web/20140828000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/player-year-limited-standings) and [Constructed](https://web.archive.org/web/20140828000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/player-year-constructed-standings) Player of the Year standings are updated every Thursday. You can access these pages from the *Magic Online* [Resources page](https://web.archive.org/web/20140828000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/resources-magic-online-products-game-info)!
