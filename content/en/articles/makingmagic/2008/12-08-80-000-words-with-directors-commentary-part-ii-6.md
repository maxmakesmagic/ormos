You might think the "Research" line is a joke, but it's not. The Creative Team has to double check what some novel said a lot more than you would think.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/72/0a/720a874a03b1afee2cfc9dc0eca936b9b3378de9ae779b2ff27abfda1f45cf82.jpg) 

This proved to be one of the most popular photos on the tour. While Jeremy did pose for this shot, I feel he was only trying to recreate how he looks when he's had four or more Red Bulls.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d0/09/d00965256dab0f0327235f69620828bcd48624ba15288fce125976c4e97f0a23.jpg) 

This was another pick-up photo. CAPS (which stands for Creative And Production Services) is a huge and important part of the **Magic**-making process. They take up half of the third floor so once I realized I had forgotten them I went back to get this photo. The room has a lot of technical equipment that the photo did a poor job of capturing. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7e/dc/7edc8e470bd7b53ded3bf63a5d13f5a006a890ea0af69d7bbc01b55147092843.jpg) 

I do so love referencing old columns. If I could have figured out how to put a link to [Elegance](/en/articles/makingmagic/2004/10-18-elegance-66) in the photo I would. The reason I link old columns so much, by the way, is that the majority of readers have not read all 350+ of my columns, and this is a way to expose readers to columns they might have missed. (Warning: Elegance is a love it or hate it type of column.)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e0/ae/e0aedf09ff4e456e9741edbf75e81dd6b493e03ebf62eafaf20fe2f0530547db.jpg) 

To try and create closure for the tour, you'll notice that I'm moving back out through the lobby to exit the way I came in.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/54/16/5416d89dc3ba3d7447cffe867c68a0e8f3b7342651d2c1b1352be40872c2b54d.jpg)
