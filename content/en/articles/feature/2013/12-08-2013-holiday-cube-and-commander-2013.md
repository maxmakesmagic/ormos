The end of the year is rapidly approaching, and 'tis the season for the **Magic Online** Holiday Cube! The fun starts on Wednesday, December 18, 2013, and runs through Wednesday, January 8, 2014.

The "Cube" refers to the stack of cards selected for the format—in face-to-face **Magic** the stack resembles a cube composed of as many as 540 cards with no duplicates. The **Magic Online** Cube is made up of digital cards, and these are randomly divided into fifteen-card Cube booster packs used for drafting. Cube Draft encompasses many of the most enjoyable aspects of **Magic: The Gathering**, combining elements of both Constructed and Limited play, trading, and playing the game with friends.

Paired with this year's Holiday Cube will be the Flashback Draft [*Mercadian Masques*](https://web.archive.org/web/20131208000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/mercadianmasques/productinfo) until December 24 at 9:00 AM PST. Then, the Flashback Draft will change to a new format, *Urza's Saga* block, and then again on January 3. So check in throughout the holidays to see what's new.

[![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/tcg/products/cmd13/c13expsym.jpg)](#c13)

Also coming this week is [***Magic: The Gathering—**Commander* (2013 Edition)](https://web.archive.org/web/20131208000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/commander13/productinfo), which goes on sale in **Magic Online** December 20, 2013. These five Commander decks offer great Commander-format gameplay, bringing together some amazing cards in each.

There's a virtual sleigh-full of play coming in December to **Magic Online**!

[***Magic: The Gathering—**Commander* (2013 Edition)](https://web.archive.org/web/20131208000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/commander13/productinfo) goes on sale in **Magic Online** on Friday, December 20, 2013!

***Magic: The Gathering—**Commander* (2013 Edition) will consist of five different 100-card singleton decks, and each deck includes three premium legendary commander cards. These five decks each follow a central color of mana plus its two allied colors, which we refer to as shards of the color pie.
