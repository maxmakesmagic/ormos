### 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/13/e3/13e3243b5b3c710f3af29501fd155a37193ad85728114cceb82fd042f4112698.jpg)Sideboard.com is pleased to announce that *Sideboard* #40 is now available in North America. 

*Sideboard* is the official source of **Magic: The Gathering** strategy and tournament news. You won't find a magazine with more **Magic** content anywhere in the world.

*Sideboard* #40 contains the following good stuff:

  

*   A look at the current Standard tournament environment by two time Masters winner Ben Rubin.
*   A detailed analysis of Pro Tour Osaka Top 8 decks by Zvi Mowshowitz.
*   An awesome two-page Judgment preview.
*   Mark Rosewater's exclusive "Insider Trading" column, followed up by Alex Shvartsman's analysis of what's hot in singles: "Outsider Trading".
*   <table><tbody><tr><td><img src="http://www.wizards.com/bios/images/aaron_forsythe_lg.jpg"><br><i>An insider peek at R&amp;D from the dynamic Aaron Forsythe.</i></td></tr></tbody></table>

    A special behind-the-scenes feature, "A Day in the Life of Magic R&D" by Aaron Forsythe.
*   In-depth analysis of Odyssey-block drafting.
*   Erhnam Djinn's return to Standard.
*   Profiles of pro players Brian Hegstad and Alex Shvartsman.
*   A detailed look at **Magic: The Gathering** in Japan.
*   Coverage of 12 Grand Prix events, including Hong Kong, Biarritz, Curitiba, Las Vegas, Sendai, Houston, Lisbon, Heidelberg, Fukuoka, Tampa and Antwerp.

  

<table><tbody><tr><td><img src="http://www.wizards.com/protour/images/pt_lg.gif"></td>&#10;<td><ul><li>Detailed coverage of Pro Tour-Osaka and Pro Tour-San Diego, including coverage of both Masters events and decklists.</li>&#10;<li>DCI Rankings around the world.</li>&#10;<li>The most complete <b>Magic</b> event calendar you will find in publication.</li>&#10;</ul>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

All this and more for the low suggested retail price of $2.99.
