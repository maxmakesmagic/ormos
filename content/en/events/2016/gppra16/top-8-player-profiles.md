After 15 rounds of Legacy, with a total of 1,469 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppra16/gppra16_t8_molle.jpg)Niels Molle

**Age:** 29

**Occupation:** Project manager

**Hometown:** Dublin, Ireland

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Multiple cash finishes at Grand Prix.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Omni Sneak. I never play Legacy and Niels Noorlander said the deck was sweet.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Play a [Daze](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daze) in the sideboard because nobody expects it anymore.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Sneak Attack](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sneak+Attack).

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppra16/gppra16_t8_hayne.jpg)Alexander Hayne

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Traveling wizard

**Hometown:** Montreal, Canada

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Pro Tour Avacyn Restored champion, four Grand Prix wins. Co-founder of Team Face to Face. Once beat Seth Manfield in a match of Magic.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Grixis Delver. Ben Friedman told me to.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I would cut the main deck [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy) for some blue spells. Maybe add [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay) to the sideboard for all of these [Chalice of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chalice+of+the+Void) running around.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm).

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppra16/gppra16_t8_togores.jpg)Rodrigo Togores

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Video editor

**Hometown:** Madrid, Spain

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Won MKM Prague, won MKM Rome, won MKM Madrid, and won Ovino 2015.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Storm. Because counting to ten is easy.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I tested the deck a lot and would make no changes.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Infernal Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Infernal+Tutor).

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppra16/gppra16_t8_blohon.jpg)Lukas Blohon

**Age:** 26

**Occupation:** Tourist

**Hometown:** Prague, Czech Republic

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Pro Tour Top 8, Grand Prix win.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Shardless Sultai. My friend Zuzy had all the cards. I also didn't have much time to test and the deck plays all the good cards.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Probably play another deck. Or play this more and see if some matchups are solvable. Lands is a very bad matchup, Miracles isn't great. You actually don't have any great matchups.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Deathrite Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathrite+Shaman).

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppra16/gppra16_t8_thollance.jpg)Nicolas Tholance

**Age:** 24

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Lyon, France

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Top 32 at Grand Prix Lille and Grand Prix Kyoto.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Miracles. To get [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance) banned.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Mountain in the sideboard wasn't very effective; Shardless Sultai plays more [Creeping Tar Pit](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Creeping+Tar+Pit)s and less Wastelands.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top)/[Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm). But the second Mentor got me the most wins, courtesy of Maxime Gilles.

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppra16/gppra16_t8_gazzola.jpg)Gianluca Gazzola

**Age:** 24

**Occupation:** Factory worker

**Hometown:** Bassano del Grappa, Italy

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

First at Vintage Italian League, first at Legacy Italian League.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Temur Delver, because I always play those colors.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I wouldn't make any change.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Hooting Mandrills](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hooting+Mandrills).

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppra16/gppra16_t8_enevoldsen.jpg)Thomas Enevoldsen

**Age:** 28

**Occupation:** Associate attorney

**Hometown:** Frederiksberg, Denmark

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

2014 World Magic Cup win, 2013 Grand Prix Strasbourg win, two-time Danish national champion.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Mono-White Control \[actually Death and Taxes\]. Good deck, interesting to play, have had lots of practice.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

None.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

Plains.

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gppra16/gppra16_t8_baglin.jpg)Arnaud Baglin

**Age:** 33

**Occupation:** Advertising

**Hometown:** Reims, France

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Meeting Claire Dambron. Ninth at Grand Prix Brussels, 40th at Pro Tour Berlin.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Grixis Delver. I didn't playtest that much and it's a deck I'm used to. Also, I love the [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe)/[Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy)/[Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer) synergy.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

None.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160612000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer).
