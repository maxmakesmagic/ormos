[Last time](/en/articles/arcana/2007/08-16-fat-pack-murals-1) on As the Fat Pack Murals Turn, the *Ninth Edition* mural had just proposed marriage to the *Ravnica* mural, despite already being married to his evil twin, the *Guildpact* mural, and pregnant with the *Dissension* mural's baby.

As the curtains open on today's episode, the *Coldsnap* mural has vowed revenge, but the *Time Spiral* block murals might have a little something to say about it...
