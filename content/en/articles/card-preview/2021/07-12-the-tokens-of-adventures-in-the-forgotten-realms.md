Before you venture into the dungeon, make sure to secure these trusty tokens from *Dungeons & Dragons: Adventures in the Forgotten Realms*. [The set](https://web.archive.org/web/20210712000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/forgotten-realms) and [its associated Commander decks](/en/articles/feature/2021/07-09-adventures-in-the-forgotten-realms-commander-decklists) contain a combined 28 full-art tokens, four emblems, and three dungeons. In Draft and Set Boosters, you can find four emblems and fifteen full-art tokens, three of which have a dungeon on one side. In Collector Boosters, you can find many of these tokens as double-sided tokens.

Commander decks each come with ten double-sided tokens with a combined total of sixteen double-sided variations. Thirteen full-art tokens are unique to *Adventures in the Forgotten Realms* Commander products.

If you haven't yet seen the cards that make these tokens, go check out the [Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2021/07-07-d-d-adventures-in-the-forgotten-realms-2) and [Variant Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2021/07-12-d-d-adventures-in-the-forgotten-realms-variants)—plus the [*Adventures in the Forgotten Realms* Commander Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2021/07-12-d-d-adventures-in-the-forgotten-realms-commander) .
