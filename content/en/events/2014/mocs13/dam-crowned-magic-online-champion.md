Lars Dam finished off an amazing weekend by defeating Cory Lack in the finals of the **Magic Online** Championship. Dam's Black Devotion deck put the pressure on early and Lack and his Blue Devotion deck never had a chance to recover, done in by lethal Gray Merchant of Asphodel plays in both games of the finals. 

Sitting atop the standings all weekend, Dam not only earns the title of **Magic Online** Championship but also a seat at the 2014 World Championship, an invitation to next year's **Magic Online** Championship, and the $25,000 first prize. 

Dam defeated the defending **Magic Online** Champion Dmitriy Butakov in the semifinals in a tense three-game match. Lack earned his spot in the finals by getting past Tamas Glied in the other semifinal.
