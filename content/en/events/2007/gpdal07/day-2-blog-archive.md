He mulliganned and played the first spell of this match for top 8: <a>Silver Knight</a>. It got in for two and Tim surprisingly passed the turn with a land and a <a>Pillage</a> in hand, against Alex' board of <a>Mountain</a> and <a>Hallowed Fountain</a>. Tim <a>Lightning Helix</a>ed Alex when he played a <a>Sensei's Divining Top</a>, and Helixed him again after he tapped out for Ice on the <a>Silver Knight</a>, again choosing not to play the <a>Pillage</a>. <a>Orim's Chant</a> with kicker bought Alex some time and he used that to lay down <a>Counterbalance</a>, and now things weren't looking so good for Tim although he put his opponent down to nine in the next attack step. Tim tried to lure Alex to put the Top on top of the deck by playing <a>Firebolt</a>, but he just spinned it first and found another Top on top of the deck. Tim didn't try the <a>Silver Knight</a> he had in his hand and just passed the turn. <a>Meddling Mage</a> came down for Alex, naming <a>Firebolt</a>, and it seemed like Alex was ready to race Tim when he end-of-turn <a>Lightning Helix</a>ed him. While Alex had all of Tim's spells under control with Top/<a>Counterbalance</a>, he didn't have a solution to the <a>Silver Knight</a> yet, but surely he would be able to find something for it in one of the next four turns he had. He did, as he <a>Cunning Wish</a>ed for a <a>Condemn</a> which took care of Tim's last threat on the board, but somehow <a>Pillage</a> on the Top, putting the Top on top, was able to force through another <a>Silver Knight</a> as Alex still hadn't found anything. <a>Lightning Angel</a> for Alex came down soon after that though, putting Tim at 8 while Alex was still as 6. A <a>Lightning Helix</a> from Alex put him even further ahead, and the lone <a>Silver Knight</a> couldn't get the job done.</p>&#10;      <h3>Game 2</h3>&#10;      <p>Tim played first again and both players kept their seven. Tim had a rather weak start for a Boros deck: no turn one drop and just a Jitte on the second turn. A third-turn <a>Jotun Grunt</a> seemed like a good creature but when Alex played <a>Orim's Chant</a> without kicker in Tim's next upkeep, Tim put his <a>Jotun Grunt</a> in the graveyard for some reason, despite having two fetch lands in his own graveyard. In the meanwhile, Alex was setting up Top/<a>Counterbalance</a> again, but couldn't find a two mana spell on top when Tim <a>Disenchant</a>ed the Balance. Another <a>Jotun Grunt</a> came down to carry the Jitte, but it was Tim's only creature so Alex was able to solve it with <a>Porphyry Nodes</a>. <a>Thirst for Knowledge</a> refilled Alex' hand and he played another <a>Counterbalance</a> while still being at a very healthy 15. Things got even worse for Tim when Alex played <a>Isochron Scepter</a> and imprinted <a>Lightning Helix</a>, and at this point there was simply nothing Tim could do. </p>&#10;      <p>Alex Ledbetter won 2-0 and takes his rogue reroll deck to the Top 8!</p>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <a name="10"> </a>&#10;      <li>&#10;        <b>Sunday, Feb 25: 4:57 p.m. - Round 14: Analynn Bustamante vs. Jesse Hawkins</b>&#10;        <div>&#10;          <i>by Nate Price</i>&#10;        </div>&#10;      </li>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <table>&#10;        <tbody><tr>&#10;          <td>&#10;            <div>&#10;              <img src="https://i.ormos.uk/fc/e7/fce7abb51ea5eceb570db2f449a31c05fe946cfe8413e468f55c6ad9ca4c3ce3.jpg">&#10;            </div>&#10;            <div>&#10;              <i>Jesse Hawkins</i>&#10;            </div>&#10;          </td>&#10;        </tr>&#10;      </tbody></table>&#10;      <p>Jesse Hawkins is an up and coming <b>Magic</b> player who, after his performance yesterday, was able to shrug off the amateur label despite being only 15.
