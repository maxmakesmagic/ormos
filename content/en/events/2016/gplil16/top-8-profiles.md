After fifteen rounds of Modern, with a total of 1,810 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gplil16/t8_obraz.jpg)Name:** Jiří Obraz

**Age:** 21

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Prague, Czech Republic

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

None. This is my first.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Jund. Because it is good.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I would not change anything.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

It's Jund, so all the cards are important.

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gplil16/t8_hendriks.jpg)Name:** Thomas Hendriks

**Age:** 24

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Leiden, Netherlands

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

One Grand Prix Top 8 (Antwerp), one Pro Tour Top 8 (Dragons of Tarkir), permanent WMCQ winner, Gold level.

**What deck did you play and why?**

[Death's Shadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death%27s+Shadow) Zoo. It's the deck I know best and it's good against all non-Jund decks with few exceptions.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Add one extra [Faith's Shield](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faith%27s+Shield), it's very good. I would also like to cut [Steppe Lynx](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steppe+Lynx) since it's pretty bad but there's no attractive alternative.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Death's Shadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death%27s+Shadow). No contest.

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gplil16/t8_ballestin.jpg)Name:** Daniel Ballestin

**Age:** 22

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Madrid, Spain

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Top 10 at Grand Prix Bilbao, Top 16 at Grand Prix Antwerp, and more good finishes.

**What deck did you play and why?**

White-Blue Control because it's the best deck.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

None.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command).

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gplil16/t8_berger.jpg)Name:** Meciek Berger

**Age:** 32

**Occupation:** Account manager

**Hometown:** Łódź, Poland

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

A few Day 2s, won an FNM. BOM Top 8, a few PTQ Top 8s.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Infect, because my friend Grzegorz Marciniak told me to.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Maybe add [Relic of Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Relic+of+Progenitus) to the sideboard and [Sylvan Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Scrying) to the main deck.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Glistener Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glistener+Elf)!

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gplil16/t8_boulinguiez.jpg)Name:** Mike Boulinguiez

**Age:** 23

**Occupation:** Artisan

**Hometown:** Paris, France

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Nothing.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Red-Green [Through the Breach](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Through+the+Breach). The deck is well positioned in the metagame. It reminds me a lot of the time I was playing [Summer Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summer+Bloom), it's just a bit slower.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Nothing, it's awesome!

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan). And my aubergine ahah!

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gplil16/t8_metzger.jpg)Name:** André Metzger

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Logistics

**Hometown:** Ochsenfurt, Germany

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Beat Thomas Müller in a draft.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Bant Eldrazi, because I had no time to test for the Grand Prix.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Add two more [Eldrazi Skyspawner](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Skyspawner)s. Beat turn-two [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) twice with it, and it's good in the mirror.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Eldrazi Temple](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Temple).
