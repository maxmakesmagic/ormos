Take a look at Sylvan Echoes. It's a card that enables green to get extra benefit from the clash mechanic. Green might see clashing in terms of the hunt: a duel between predator and prey.

The art and flavor text of Sylvan Echoes refer to a hunt for mythical prey. Check out the high-resolution art below. Artist Rebecca Guay conjures up a mythical horse creature using the negative space between the branches and birds in the scene. See it?

[![](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/1438_SylvanEchoes.jpg)](#image)  
*Sylvan Echoes art by Rebecca Guay*

If you find yourself in a clash, maybe you'll get the benefit of some these mystical echoes from Lorwyn's sylvan glades.
