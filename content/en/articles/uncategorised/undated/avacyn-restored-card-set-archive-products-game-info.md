## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/89/10/8910c38208684a6fa9609b70b098cd95caceae0acf80e89eb45839987449c405.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set as well as two deck boxes, nine 15-card booster packs, 80 basic land cards, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e9/72/e972674ed270a6355ad8713d4037e2bfb54fb336579230c4586724078f9f9aa4.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/56/a5/56a5dd0ba210b0d893902a74f30e01f3849b76def4e04944c93a5c75d2264c21.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 cards, a foil premium rare card, one sealed 15-card booster pack, a strategy insert, and a **Magic** learn-to-play guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160712040214/http://magic.wizards.com/node/283556)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f3/d8/f3d8163f5e9110d9454b32d0c21e8d8566040513b8fa4aa24db08193d16a9bec.png)

### EVENT DECKS

Battle like the best, right out of the box. Includes a tournament-ready 60-card deck plus a 15-card “sideboard” to swap in as needed, depending on what you expect your opponent will play.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160712040214/http://magic.wizards.com/node/283691)

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/be/07/be071171d1945b898a773218e0a2c55df3e48308569b212f3e9c558b7b9ebdfe.png)

The *Avacyn Restored* set features 244 black-bordered cards, including randomly inserted premium versions of all cards in the set.

**Set Name:** *Avacyn Restored*  
**Block:** Set 3 of 3 in the Innistrad Block  
**Number of Cards:** 244

**Languages:** English, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish

**Official Three-Letter Code:** AVR  
**Twitter Hashtag:** #MTGAVR

[Avacyn Restored FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160712040214/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/faq/avr) (or download as [DOC](https://web.archive.org/web/20160712040214/http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/tcg/products/avr/EN_MTGAVR_FAQ_20120327.doc))  
  

**Design Team:** Brian Tinsman (lead), Mark L. Gottlieb, Dave Guskin, Kenneth Nagle, Bill Rose, Mark Rosewater

**Development Team:** David Humpherys (lead), Mark Globus, Dave Guskin, Erik Lauer, Billy Moreno, Matt Sernett
