Fans of "old" formats can enjoy them on **Magic Online** just as they do in the real world! This list managed a recent Premier Event Top 8 on the back of powerful black creatures like Nantuko Shade and Tombstalker. Joining the team is anti-aggro artifact creature Epochrasite. Of course, creatures alone aren't enough as Crucible of Worlds, Hymn to Tourach, Duress, and a host of other powerful spells pitch in to keep opponents from fighting fair.
