## What is Magic?

[### **Magic is Strategy**

Create your own decks of cards and devise your own strategies. Will you summon hordes of zombies, unleash a flurry of fireballs, or mind control your opponents? With thousands of cards to choose from, you can forge your path to victory.](http://magic.wizards.com/en/node/new-to-magic/strategy)

 

[### **Magic is Lore**

Explore awe-inspiring worlds of Magic, where you'll find yourself caught between battling guilds, running from vampires and werewolves, or cavorting with satyrs and nymphs. Discover the tales of intrigue and legend that await you.](http://magic.wizards.com/en/node/new-to-magic/lore)

 

[### **Magic is Community**

Magic is more than just a card game. It's an opportunity to make new friends, challenge old rivals, and have fun with people from around the world. With over 20 million players, you probably already know someone who plays!](http://magic.wizards.com/new-to-magic/community)
