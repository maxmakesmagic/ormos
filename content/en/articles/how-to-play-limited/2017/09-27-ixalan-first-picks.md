***Ixalan* releases September 30!**

*Ixalan* drafts will begin all over the globe this weekend when the set is finally released! Chart a course for [a store near you](https://web.archive.org/web/20170927000000/http://locator.wizards.com/) to join in the exploration of a brand-new world!

While we don't know exactly how *Ixalan* will play out as a Limited format, we can get started by figuring out which cards you want to start with. Identifying a range of cards you'd be happy to first-pick helps guide your drafts, and there are some nice ones here. I'm going to break them down into tiers and talk about what each tier means. Let's jump in!

# Windmill Slams

These are the cards you will undoubtedly take if you open them. The only one I'm not quite sure about is [Gishath, Sun's Avatar](https://web.archive.org/web/20170927000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gishath%2C+Sun%27s+Avatar) because it's three colors. Still, it looks worth building around, and rewards you mightily if you do. The rest of the cards are just awesome on their own and don't require much of you besides being able to cast them. If you open one of these cards, go forth and conquer!
