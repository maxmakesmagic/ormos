It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20110804000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=events/magic/fnm)! And since it's the first week of August, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

![Cultivate](https://i.ormos.uk/b8/1b/b81bdf1cc4f1f15025a5b4f2b59c151b52ccdc8756c414ff0db940bc6213b40b.jpg)  

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards often feature original flavor text and updated wordings.
