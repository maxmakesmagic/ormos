Every week, we round up all of the biggest *Magic Online* news for the Weekly Announcements Blog.  
 Check in weekly for the latest updates!

**Legacy Cube and Flashback *Odyssey* Queues**

Start the new year off right with some [Legacy Cube](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/01-05-resolve-to-legacy-cube)!

Beginning Wednesday, January 14, following the downtime, the Legacy Cube will make its triumphant return to *Magic Online*. Accompanying the Legacy Cube will be triple *Odyssey* Flashback Drafts. Both *Odyssey* and Legacy Cube Events run in Swiss and Single Elimination Draft. Both sets of queues conclude with the Wednesday, January 28, downtime.

Check out the [article](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/01-05-resolve-to-legacy-cube) for the full details.

***Magic Online* is Hiring!**

Do you know *Magic Online* inside-out? Do you have previous judge experience? If so, you should apply to be a [Digital Events Coordinator](https://web.archive.org/web/20150107000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/digital-events-coordinator-temporary-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1) for *Magic Online*!

We're also on the search for a savvy [Assistant Digital Product Manager](https://web.archive.org/web/20150107000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/assistant-digital-product-manager-mtgo-cardsets-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1) to work on *Magic Online* Cardsets. Read about these jobs, as well as many others, on the Wizards of the Coast [careers page](https://web.archive.org/web/20150107000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers).

**Downtime for January 5, 2015**

This week, our normal Wednesday downtime occurred on Monday, January 5, as noted [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20150107000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/MTGO/articles/archive/magic-online-announcements-december-23-2014#item5). We will return to our regularly scheduled downtimes on Wednesday, January 14.
