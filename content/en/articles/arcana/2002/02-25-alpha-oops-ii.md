When *Alpha* came out, Orcs must've thought they were kings of the world. All their spells cost ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless1.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif)! Now, obviously [Ironclaw Orcs](https://web.archive.org/web/20020225000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ironclaw+Orcs) is supposed to cost two mana, but what about [Orcish Artillery](https://web.archive.org/web/20020225000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Orcish+Artillery) and [Orcish Oriflamme](https://web.archive.org/web/20020225000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Orcish+Oriflamme)?

Those two cards inadvertently received casting costs of ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless1.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif), later changed to ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless1.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif) and ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless3.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif) respectively in *Beta*. The switch is not to be seen as a "changing" of their costs – they were never intended to cost two. It was simply a pair of misprints.
