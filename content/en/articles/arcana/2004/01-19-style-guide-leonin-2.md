Below are a few sketches from the *Mirrodin* Style Guide, which is a document given to artists that worked on the set for reference. This first sketch shows a Leonin, the race of cat humanoids associated with the color White (see also "[Mirrodin Environments: White](/en/articles/arcana/2003/10-01-mirrodin-environments-white)"). Note how the Leonin, like most races on the plane of Mirrodin, has its flesh fused and intermingled with metal. 

![Leonin concept sketch](https://i.ormos.uk/51/7c/517cd16430694db8533b6591db6732e1cfac7ff0aa7756885b7a38c70fe4bca9.jpg) 

![Leonin concept sketch](https://i.ormos.uk/67/c0/67c04a3674a233b3ed82ea9bf2d748cb58fbe826ac6aaadc04242075900c8ca2.jpg) These sketches inspired the illustrations in such creature cards as Leonin Abunas, Leonin Den-Guard, Leonin Elder, and Leonin Skyhunter, and the artifact cards Leonin Scimitar (left) and Leonin Sun Standard. 

Leonin claws (below) are nasty metallic structures!
