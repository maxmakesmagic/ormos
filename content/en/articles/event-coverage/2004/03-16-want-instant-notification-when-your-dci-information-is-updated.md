[Magic Player Rewards](https://web.archive.org/web/20040316000000/https://www.wizards.com/dci/rewards.asp) members have a great new benefit available to them: The ability to receive their ratings and rankings via text messaging as soon as they're updated. Every week, between Wednesday and Friday, from 2pm to 6pm EST, if any of your **Magic** ratings change in any individual format, you will receive a text message with your new rating as well as the number of points you gained (or may have lost). This alert is not yet available for team format. Other text-message-based services will be available soon, so get signed up and receive your latest information via your cell phone no matter where you are in the United States or Canada. (This service will be available in other markets in the coming months.)

If you are not yet part of the **Magic** Player Rewards Program, you can sign online [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20040316000000/https://www.wizards.com/dci/rewards.asp?x=rewards). You will need to receive your final confirmation email before being able to sign for the cell phone alert service.

 **[Click here for more information, or to sign up!](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603132007/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Article.aspx?x=magic/dci/dcialert)**
