The **Magic: The Gathering** Australian National Championship is an invitation-only event, open to citizens or foreign players resident in Australia from Jan 1st 2009 Australia. Invitations are awarded based on the following criteria:

*   2008 Australian Worlds Team

*   Aaron Nicastri (Australian Champion)
*   Brandon Lau
*   Justin Cheong

*   The Alternate member of the 2008 Australian National Team

*   Glenn Shanley

*   Top eight (8) finishers from the Regional Qualifying Tournaments in Adelaide, Brisbane, Canberra, Hobart, Melbourne, Perth, & Sydney.
*   Top four (4) finishers from the Regional Qualifying Tournament in Townsville and Dubbo.

Regional tournaments take place on May 16 or 17, 2009 as follows:
