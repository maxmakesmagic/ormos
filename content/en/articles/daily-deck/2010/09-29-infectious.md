Zac Hill is the third contributor of a **Wizards** R&D gunslinging list from last weekend's *Scars of Mirrodin* Prereleases. His deck puts the new mechanic "infect" to good use. With the exception of Overgrown Battlement, all of Zac's creatures have the ability. Add some pump, like Groundswell, Primal Bellows, and Vines of Vastwood, and you've got your opponent poisoned to death in short order! To read more about Zac, follow him on Twitter: @[zdch](http://www.twitter.com/zdch).
