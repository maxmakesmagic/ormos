Pro Tour–San Diego saw a lot of different archetypes make their mark in Standard. One you may not have heard much about was Tim Sussino's take on red-white-blue control. Using Dragon Fodder and Goblin Assault, Tim was free to cast Polymorph knowing that the blue sorcery would inevitably reveal the only creature in his deck: Iona, Shield of Emeria. You can read about all the decklists from Pro Tour–San Diego in last week's installment of [Decks of the Week](/en/articles/decks-of-the-week/2010/03-04-decks-of-the-week-3-4-2010).
