Looking for a new twist on **Magic**? Dr. Green won a recent 100 Card Singleton event on **Magic Online** using this concoction. The format restricts players from playing more than one copy of a card in their deck (excluding basic lands, of course); such restrictions create a fun format where nearly anything goes. Always wanted to try that legend in your deck but afraid it wouldn't be powerful enough? Singleton says go ahead, give it a shot! Dr. Green did and took down the top spot for the effort.
