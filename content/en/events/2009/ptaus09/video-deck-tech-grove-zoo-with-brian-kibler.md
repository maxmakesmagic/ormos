Fresh off his Pro Tour-Honolulu Top 8, Brian Kibler showcases an Extended Naya Zoo deck created by Hall of Famer Ben Rubin that was designed to combat the expected field. The deck features Grove of the Burnwillows plus Punishing Fire alongside Baneslayer Angel. Both Kibler and Rubin went 5-0 with the deck to kick off Pro Tour-Austin.

  

Brian Kibler - Grove Zoo 

Pro Tour-Ausitn, Extended
