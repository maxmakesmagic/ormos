From their early days when they were known as "The Rock and His Millions," black-green control strategies have popped up again and again as successful avenues of play. Paul Ewenstein's Top 8 at the Pro Tour Philadelphia Qualifier in Charlestown, Massachusetts is proof positive that the concept is alive and very well. Plus, he has some AWESOME combos. Fauna Shaman and Vengevine is one every Standard player knows, but Immolating Souleater and Death's Shadow? Or how about Death's Shadow and Momentous Fall? That's a good way to load up on a LOT of cards!
