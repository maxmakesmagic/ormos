After 15 rounds of Modern, with a total of 2,173 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbol16/gpbol16_t8_patel.jpg)

### Kayure Patel

**Age:** 28

**Occupation:** Lawyer

**Hometown:** Cheltenham, England

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Member of England's World Magic Cup team in 2015.

**What deck did you play and why?**

White-Blue Eldrazi. Our team said it was the best deck.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I would want my friend, Pro Tour competitor Ed Hughes, to have been here. He passed away on Tuesday and I dedicate this to him.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

The eight [Ancient Tomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Tomb)s.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbol16/gpbol16_t8_reale.jpg)

### Giuseppe Reale

**Age:** 31

**Occupation:** Cammilluzzi's best friend

**Hometown:** Rome, Italy

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Top 8 at the Rochester Draft Grand Prix in Paris.

**What deck did you play and why?**

[Living End](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+End), because it's well-positioned in the metagame.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Add one [Shriekmaw](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shriekmaw) to the main deck, remove [Ricochet Trap](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ricochet+Trap) from the sideboard.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Fulminator Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fulminator+Mage).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbol16/gpbol16_t8_lippi.jpg)

### Alessandro Lippi

**Age:** 33

**Occupation:** Magic shop

**Hometown:** Massa, Italy

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Top 4 at Grand Prix Turin, Top 4 at Grand Prix Utrecht.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Bant Company. Cammilluzzi gave me a good list, and with some changes it became the greatest deck!

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Nothing. Or maybe add another [Vendilion Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vendilion+Clique).

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Meddling Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meddling+Mage).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbol16/gpbol16_t8_mattioli.jpg)

### Alberto Mattioli

**Age:** 22

**Occupation:** Mechanic

**Hometown:** Piacenza, Italy

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Some minor Grand Prix finishes.

**What deck did you play and why?**

I chose White-Blue Eldrazi because the Eldrazi are really unfair.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I would change nothing.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Thought-Knot Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thought-Knot+Seer) by far.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbol16/gpbol16_t8_boucek.jpg)

### Jaroslav Boucek

**Age:** 28

**Occupation:** Magic player/traveler

**Hometown:** Samotišky, Czech Republic

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Top 8 at Grand Prix Lille, Top 32 at Grand Prix Rimini, some Top 64s.

**What deck did you play and why?**

White-Blue Eldrazi. It's busted, way too good and consistent to be Modern legal.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Definitely add something to the sideboard. Probably Ulamog and maybe [Stubborn Denial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stubborn+Denial). I saw way more [Living End](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+End)s floating around than I expected.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Eldrazi Temple](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Temple)/[Eye of Ugin](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eye+of+Ugin).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbol16/gpbol16_t8_perez.jpg)

### Ruben Perez Rodriguez

**Age:** 22

**Occupation:** Owner of Barcelona Magic store

**Hometown:** Cornellà de Llobregat, Spain

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Top 16 at Grand Prix Miami 2015, Top 18 at Grand Prix Charlotte 2015, fourteen Day 2 finishes at Grand Prix, and two PTQ wins.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Affinity. I don't trust the Eldrazi.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

None.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

All of them.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbol16/gpbol16_t8_rizzi.jpg)

### Mattia Rizzi

**Age:** 22

**Occupation:** Magic player

**Hometown:** Lecco, Italy

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

This season, Top 8 at Grand Prix Prague and ninth at Grand Prix Lyon. Top 8 at Grand Prix Portland 2013. Silver level pro status.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Red-Green Eldrazi, because Fabrizio Anteri told me that it's the best deck in this metagame.

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

I don't like [Matter Reshaper](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Matter+Reshaper). I would like to replace it with another card.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[Eye of Ugin](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eye+of+Ugin), for sure!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbol16/gpbol16_t8_anteri.jpg)

### Fabrizio Anteri

**Age:** 25

**Occupation:** Soon-to-be Platinum pro player

**Hometown:** London, England

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Won back to back GPs in 2014 and just won the last Grand Prix I played. I'm just saying.

**What deck did you play and why?**

Red-Green Eldrazi. Good friend and giocatore fortissimo \[MVP\] Humberto Patarca gave me his list and convinced me to work together to improve it. Great choice! Thanks, bro!

**What changes would you make to your deck and why?**

Maybe cut the [Crumble to Dust](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crumble+to+Dust) for a [Warping Wail](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warping+Wail) to beat [Living End](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+End), which seems to be one of the best non-Eldrazi options.

**What's the most important card in your deck?**

[World Breaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20160306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=World+Breaker).
