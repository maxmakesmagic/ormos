Aquí tenéis lo que se está jugando en el Nacional de España a día de hoy, aunque muchas de estas barajas no volverán a tocar la mesa mañana. Veamos de qué manera ha influido la prohibición de [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) y [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20110813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic) en la elección de los mejores jugadores del país.

Como era de esperar los primeros puestos están ocupados por las mejores barajas: [Caw](https://web.archive.org/web/20110813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=208279)[Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20110813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=382200) – [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) – [Valakut](https://web.archive.org/web/20110813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=190400). Rascando el podio con las uñas tenemos a UB Control y Monored Aggro en todas sus formas posibles, mientras que algo más alejadas quedan Steel Artifact y [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20110813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod). Ciertamente no esperaba contar con tantas de estas últimas, incluso hubo quien se atrevió con un híbridos como Pod-[Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=193474) o [Caw](https://web.archive.org/web/20110813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=208279)[Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=193474).Para gustos los colores.
