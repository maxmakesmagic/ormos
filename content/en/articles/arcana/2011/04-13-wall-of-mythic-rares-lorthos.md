Return with us now to the [Wall of Mythic Rares](https://web.archive.org/web/20110413000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/wall-mythic-rares-2011-03-10), where we watch what happens when the mythic rares of *Zendikar* got thrown up on the wall for all of R&D to see. To see, and to improve!

Today's entrant is [Lorthos, the Tidemaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20110413000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lorthos%2C+the+Tidemaker), or, as it was known at the time, "Loruthos, the Deeplord."

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f7/26/f726c097b36619e9d8f97299b23c0454166e14059170b28a9be511a6cb66ea31.jpg)  

Loruthos had two things that needed to stay unchanged: it needed to be a Legendary Octopus, and it needed as many eights on it as possible.

### The Notes

As always, the authors of these comments are anonymous. This is partly to protect them and partly because nobody signed their notes anyway.

> NAME = LORTHOS, THE TIDEMAKER

* * *

> Mana Cost: 4UUUU

* * *

> Up to 8?

* * *

> Also, if you choose Loruthos as one of the 8, he can never untap?

* * *

> Change "8T:" to "5UUU T:" and make it Tap "up to" eight permanents.

* * *

> Take the "8" off the activation.  
> agree!

* * *

> ATTACK TRIGGER  
> Now that's appealing!

And here's how the card ended up!
