### Booster Packs

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare card.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

*Unglued* is the first silver-bordered Magic set. It includes 94 cards, of which only the land cards are tournament-legal.

**Set Name:** *Unglued*  
**Number of Cards:** 94

**Languages:** English

**Official Three-Letter Code:** UNG

**Game Design and Development:** Mark Rosewater (lead), Brady Dommermuth, Mike Elliott, Richard Garfield, and Bill Rose, with contributions from Jeff Donais, William Jockusch, Mons Johnson, Beth Moursund, Michael G. Ryan, Henry Stern, John Tocher, and Jennifer Clarke Wilkes

**Art Direction:** Jesper Myrfors

**Graphic Design:** Daniel Gelon (lead), Chaz Elliott, and Jesper Myrfors

**Card Naming and Flavor Text:** Brady Dommermuth, Mark Rosewater, Michael G. Ryan, and Jennifer Clarke Wilkes, with contributions from Tyler Bielman, Rob Dalton, Mark Jessup, Darla Kennerud, Will McDermott, Lora Montrose, Brian Tinsman, and John Tocher

**Chicken Wrangler:** Gleemax

**Editing:** Jennifer Clarke Wilkes and Darla Kennerud

**Production:** Hans Reifenrath and Daniel Gelon

**Product Management:** Mark Gwyther and Tina Trenkler
