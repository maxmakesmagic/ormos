In corsa per i Top8 l'esperto Yanez Busi e il giovane Enrico, in un mirror di UB control.

### Game 1

Enrico vince il dado e parte, con Yanez che risponde subito con [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) portandosi via una [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek), poi a parte un paio di [Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain) di Enrico non succede nulla fino al turno 4 dove Yanez prova a forzare un [Jace Beleren](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace+Beleren) trovando però un Mana Leak. Parte di conseguenza un [Despise](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Despise) sullo scoperto romano che si vede scartare il [Solemn Simulacrum](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Solemn+Simulacrum), e che poi droppa un [Vampire Nighthawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vampire+Nighthawk) per iniziare a mettere pressione, "strippando” via poi a Yanez la [Creeping Tar Pit](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Creeping+Tar+Pit) da poco calata. Il vampiro prosegue il beatdown mentre una seconda [Tectonic Edge](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tectonic+Edge) leva un'altra terra doppia al suo avversario, fino a far calare i punti vita del piemontese a 10, che prova a forzare anche [Liliana Vess](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+Vess), inchiodata però da [Stoic Rebuttal](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoic+Rebuttal). Enrico vuole chiudere il game giocando [Grave Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grave+Titan) andando un po' all-in. Niente risposte per Yanez che pesca e concede.

### Game 1

Parte Yanez che fa [Ratchet Bomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ratchet+Bomb) di secondo e [Jace Beleren](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace+Beleren) di terzo, a cui Enrico risponde con [Vampire Nighthawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vampire+Nighthawk), steso da [Go for the Throat](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Go+for+the+Throat), ma subito sostituito da [Solemn Simulacrum](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Solemn+Simulacrum) topdeckato dal romano. Simulacro anche per Yanez che fa da tappo contro quello del suo avversario, lanciato verso Jace, ed ora cominciano le vere scintille: Consacrated Sphinx per Enrico che cambia sponda grazie a [Volition Reins](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Volition+Reins), [Liliana Vess](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+Vess) per Enrico e [Wurmcoil Engine](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wurmcoil+Engine) per Yanez che sembra voler mettere un pelino appena di pressione al suo avversario, che pesca la carta tutorata con Liliana e si riprende la sfinge con [Into the Roil](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Into+the+Roil), per poi calare un [Vampire Nighthawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vampire+Nighthawk) per difendersi dalla mucca artefattosa di Yanez. Ma l'alessandrino spacca a 3 con bomba e picchia con il wurm, spedendo il suo avversario a 14 e togliendogli un'altra [Creeping Tar Pit](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Creeping+Tar+Pit) con [Tectonic Edge](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tectonic+Edge), e fare un nuovo [Jace Beleren](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace+Beleren) per il vantaggio carte. Enrico fa nuovamente Nighthawk, e paga 3 in più per il [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) avversario, ma con 9 carte a tre dopo la pescata di Jace la questione sembra piuttosto in via di chiusura e la [Liliana Vess](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+Vess) di Yanez scende facendo scartare l'opponent e minacciando di arrivare a breve alla ultimate. [Grave Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grave+Titan) viene fermato da [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) e Yanez stappa e fa [Despise](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Despise): non c'è più margine di discussione ed il romano concede.

by Andrea "Pikappa" Panebianco

### Game 2

[Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain) di primo per Enrico e [Despise](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Despise) per Yanez che rivela 3 terre, una doom blade, Consacrated Sphinx e il [Solemn Simulacrum](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Solemn+Simulacrum) che va dirtto nel cimitero, ed un secondo [Despise](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Despise) fa la stessa cosa col secondo Simulacro. Yanez è fermo a due terre e cala [Ratchet Bomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ratchet+Bomb), vedendosi scartato un Jace Beleen da [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek), e poi non trovare la terza terra. Terzo [Despise](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Despise) che parte però, e la Sfinge ci lascia, sostiutia però dalla pescata e dal drop di [Jace Beleren](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace+Beleren) per Enrico. Ancora niente terre per il piemontese, che detona bomba e leva Jace, ma una [Liliana Vess](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+Vess) protetta da [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) fa capolino. Situazione disperata per Yanez che vede la terza terra (tappata) e prova a fermare un Jace con [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20110730000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak), ma non riuscendovi, e giocando il proprio planeswalker blu per uccidere quello opposto, ma di fronte all'ennesima pescata di spell, tende la mano al suo avversario e finisce la sua corsa al Top8 con parecchia amarezza.

**Busi 1-2 Santinelli**
