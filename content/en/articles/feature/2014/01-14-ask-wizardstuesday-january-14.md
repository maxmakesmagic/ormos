Ask Wizards is a weekly feature that allows you to ask us questions! If you'd like to submit your question please email it to AskWizards@wizards.com. We aren't able to answer every question we receive but if your question is good then it might show up in the coming weeks!

<table><tbody><tr><td> <b> Q: Can you please elaborate on how hybrid works with devotion? And what about the new God cards that are two colors? </b></td>&#10;			<td></td>&#10;			<td><img alt="" src="http://archive.wizards.com/magic/images/magicboxicon_nocolor.jpg"></td>&#10;		</tr><tr><td colspan="3">&#10;			<table><tbody><tr><td>&#10;						<p><b>A: Matt Tabak, Magic rules manager: </b></p>&#10;&#10;						<p>When counting your devotion to either a single color or a pair of colors, each mana symbol counts only once. A hybrid mana symbol is the color(s) of its component parts. So, for example, if you control just an <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20140114000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azor%27s+Elocutors">Azor's Elocutors</a>, your devotion to white is two, your devotion to blue is two, and your devotion to white and blue is two. If you control just a <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20140114000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reaper+King">Reaper King</a>, your devotion to any one color is one and your devotion to any pair of colors is two. If you control <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20140114000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azor%27s+Elocutors">Azor's Elocutors</a> and Ephara, God of the Polis, your devotion to white and blue is four (and Ephara isn't a creature… yet!).</p>&#10;&#10;						<p>Another way to think about it is if you're calculating your devotion to white and blue, for each mana symbol among the mana costs of permanents you control, ask yourself, &quot;Is this mana symbol white and/or blue?&quot; If the answer is yes, add one to your devotion.</p>&#10;						</td>&#10;					</tr></tbody></table></td>&#10;		</tr></tbody></table>
