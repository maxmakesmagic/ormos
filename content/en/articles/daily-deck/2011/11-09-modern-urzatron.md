With Cloudpost out of the Modern picture, some players are turning to another way to achieve fast mana: the Urzatron! Francis Toussaint of Manadeprived.com [wrote about](http://manadeprived.com/2011/10/fran-tic-search-ub-urzatron-in-modern.html) his take on the archetype. Could this be a major player in Modern going forward? With the Modern segment of the [World Championships](/en/articles/event-coverage/2011/10-10-magic-weekend-san-francisco-and-2011-worlds-event-information) only two weeks away, stay tuned to **DailyMTG.com** to find out!
