Gerry Thompson has returned to the champion's ring! The long-time pro won Grand Prix–Nashville this past weekend. He bested some 1,500 players at Draft and Sealed. His deck in the Top 8 featured cool combos like Soliton and Grand Architect, which allowed him to turn each blue mana he had into two colorless mana. Or Kuldotha Forgemaster to tutor up Mindslaver! Read the full coverage [here](/en/events/2010/gpnas10/xmas-comes-early-for-gerry-t).
