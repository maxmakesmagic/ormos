The 2011 **Magic** Pro Tour season continued in [Nagoya](/en/events/2011/ptnag11/sharfman-masters-magic-weekend-nagoya), and now it's time to see how things shook out. The second Pro Tour of the season showcased the full *Scars of Mirrodin* block in both Block Constructed and Booster Draft formats. The pre-event talk was all about Tempered Steel, but the deck ended up as a healthy 21% of the field—far from the boogeyman people expected. But did it turn out to be the dominant winning archetype? Was there a breakthrough rogue deck? Paul Jordan crunched the numbers for all the major deck archetypes. If you're looking for the hard numbers on what beat what, you won't want to miss it.

While you may not be familiar with David Sharfman as a Pro Tour mainstay, perhaps he should be known as Mr. **Magic** Weekend. Sharfman's victory in Nagoya came thanks to the invite he earned by winning the Grand Prix held at **Magic** Weekend Paris in February. Rich Hagon went in-depth with Sharfman in the minutes after he swept Toshiyuki Kadooka in the Finals of Nagoya for this revealing interview.
