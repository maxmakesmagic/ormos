Looking for more ways to use your Titans? Kamiya Shogo used this blue, red, and green deck at a recent Standard tournament in Japan. The game plan was to control the flow on the ground by accelerating mana using [Cultivate](https://web.archive.org/web/20100727000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cultivate) and [Rampant Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20100727000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rampant+Growth).
