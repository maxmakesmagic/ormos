Announcing the *Betrayers of Kamigawa* site!

[![Betrayers of Kamigawa site](https://i.ormos.uk/47/9d/479de3646e1e4e22c3639c18ed436383f9fabfe7a1834e185a21fe948c1fcd61.jpg)](http://www.magicthegathering.com/bok)

Conveniently located at [www.magicthegathering.com/bok](http://www.magicthegathering.com/bok), the site will expand to contain stories about the heroes of the Kamigawa world, wallpapers and screensavers, and an archive of cards previewed on magicthegathering.com.

### The First *Betrayers* Vignette

Ready for the first legend vignette from the *Betrayers* set? Because it's already out. This week's story is "[A Servant's Mission](https://web.archive.org/web/20080415035640/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/bok/inkeyes)" by Jay Moldenhauer-Salazar. Enjoy!
