Congratulations to Brian Kibler, the Pro Tour *Dark Ascension* champion. Kibler piloted a Wolf Run Ramp deck featuring [Huntmaster of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20120217000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Huntmaster+of+the+Fells) to victory over Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa in the Finals, after defeating Jon Finkel in a legendary semifinal showdown. You can find the Top 8 decks, along with all decks that recorded at least 18 points in the ten Standard rounds during Swiss competition, below.
