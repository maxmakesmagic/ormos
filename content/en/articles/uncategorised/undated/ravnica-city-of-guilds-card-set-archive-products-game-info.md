## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7e/59/7e59ff69dc73de31206a054070130ecfeae6fcd28ab19b7dc5221338d79f83b9.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide, two card boxes, six 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, one random Pro Tour player card, a special-edition life counter, and the *Ravnica: City of Guilds* novel.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/52/eb/52ebfb62dc94592f4a42d89d10dd07e6936b792d8b2e07c8582f6c724abc78d9.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a4/1c/a41c9ff2cbebaa9eaf40a205263271cc595d84004fc5f297a2619da2babf229f.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160707021040/http://magic.wizards.com/node/976346)

### TOURNAMENT PACKS

The foundation for exciting Sealed Deck play. Includes 45 randomly assorted cards and 30 basic land cards.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/16/97/169752e3cc36445ee0be6bd0de6caa90840df66d552ccbcdb9ab394968dccea6.png)This 306-card set features four of the Ravnica guilds: Boros Legion (red-white), House Dimir (black-blue), Golgari Swarm (black-green), and Selesnya Conclave (green-white).

**Set Name:** *Ravnica: City of Guilds*  
**Block:** Set 1 of 3 of the *Ravnica* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 306  
**Guilds:** Boros Legion, House Dimir, Golgari Swarm, Selesnya Conclave

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish
