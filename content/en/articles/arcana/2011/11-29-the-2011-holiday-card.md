Since 2006, Wizards of the Coast has made special holiday-themed cards. The first four (Fruitcake Elemental, Gifts Given, Evil Presents, and Season's Beatings) are [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20111129000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/wallpapers-week-holiday-cards-2009-12-11), and last year's card (Snow Mercy) is [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20111129000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/wallpaper-week-snow-mercy-2010-12-17). And this year's is, well, *here*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/19/f0/19f0202307d8d2c2e444fd4a3bbc5e21a2751fca007168c0560664b7fdc5dacc.jpg)  

Shiny! But not all that readable. Let's try that again. Say hello to the 2011 **Magic: The Gathering** holiday card: Yule Ooze!
