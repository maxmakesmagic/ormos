Z*endikar* wasn't the first appearance of traps in **Magic**. A quick Gatherer search will reveal the existence of Traproot Kamis and Trapjaw Kelpies, and Trappers of the Ballynock, Benalish, Dauthi, and Rathi varieties. There are even Giant Trap Door Spiders and, if you squint, Kongming's Con**trap**tions and Ex**trap**lanar Lenses to look out for.

But there's one pre-*Zendikar* trap that has a striking similarity to the deadly peril that awaits adventurers in the latest set. Can you think of what it is? Click here to see.
