### Entry Fee & Product Mix

*   The entry fee should include product use and any additional product use for prize support. Call the location nearest you for exact Entry Fee information.
*   Cost of entry will provide Teams with, one (1) Tournament pack and four (4) boosters. (Or 7 boosters per team)

### Tournament Structure/Format

*   The structure of this tournament is modified Swiss-style.
*   The format for this tournament is Sealed Deck.
*   Teams build two 40-card minimum decks *(1 Tournament Pack & 4 Boosters)*

### Prizes

*   First place Team wins an exclusive Two-Headed Dragon Deck box. (1 each)
*   The first 16 players receive an “Underworld Dreams” foil promo card (while supplies last).
*   New Player – If one or both members of a team are new players to the store they both receive an exclusive **Magic** Beanie. *(Limited to the first four teams with a new player.)*

### Registration Information

*   Players who want more specific local tournament or registration information should call their local tournament location.
*   Players who have general questions about the **Magic: The Gathering**® TCG, Wizards of the Coast tournaments, the **Magic: The Gathering Pro Tour** circuit, the Arena league, the DCI players’ organization, Qualifier tournaments, the **Magic** World Championships, National Championships, State Championships, or Regionals, should call Wizards of the Coast Customer Service at (800) 324-6496, or go to, [http://wizards.custhelp.com](http://wizards.custhelp.com/).
