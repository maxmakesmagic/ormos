Now that you know [all the cards in *Tenth Edition*](https://web.archive.org/web/20071222192610/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/cardlist/tenthedition), and you know that the lack of reminder text on *Tenth Edition* foils makes room for [alternate flavor text](/en/articles/arcana/2007/07-12-the-premium-foils-of-tenth-edition) in certain cases, you might wonder how to find out what flavor text appears on the foil versions of your favorite *Tenth* cards. 

You can find the answer in [Gatherer](https://web.archive.org/web/20070717000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/gatherer/).

When you click on an autocard link for a *Tenth Edition* card in a **magicthegathering.com** article, or click on a *Tenth Edition* card in your Gatherer search results, you'll see all the information you need. If the card has flavor text that only appears on the card's foil version, that flavor text will appear in brackets.

![](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/1377_GathererWindow10EFlavor.jpg)

  
*The brackets mean: "This flavor text only appears on the card's foil version."*

### Legacy Weapon Answers

[Last week](/en/articles/arcana/2007/07-10-the-legacy-weapon) we looked at the new Tenth Edition art of Legacy Weapon, in which Terese Nielsen embedded many, many of the Legacy artifacts into one piece of art. Today we reveal the answers.

There were seventeen Legacy artifacts shown in the piece. How many did you catch? Mouse over the image to see the answers.
