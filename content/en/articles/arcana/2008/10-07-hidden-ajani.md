If you look carefully at *Shards of Alara* cards, you can sometimes see planeswalkers strolling through, interacting with the scenery. Or, in the case of the three "Soul's" cards, busting things up. Observe!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/92/00/92003bcd97be66192ca66d950c82f993b8d4a170d3deb3dcf4d39b9d8eaa9e78.jpg) 

  

In Soul's Fire, Ajani is seen creating a fiery avatar of himself, an incarnation of his passion and fury.

 ![Soul's Fire](https://i.ormos.uk/5f/a5/5fa576c68a2dc93e06eba238e5eb3a537cd63f4c6be6f636b8f2e26811201ec3.jpg) 

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8f/e0/8fe0ce8b2adaa8ab405a60ff74479a37a01901c3152bbc8bf266d25fff3c9d33.jpg) 

  

In Soul's Grace, Ajani is again working from the background, bestowing his pridemate with a ghostly avatar of himself.

 ![Soul's Grace](https://i.ormos.uk/b9/6a/b96a3ad5d5b3682f79158b10e11e4f14c7473a03639a30e5b5aa6dc40e2ddbc7.jpg) 

  

Finally, in Soul's Might, Ajani uses his otherworldly power and wisdom to imbue a cat warrior with more inner strength.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9d/5a/9d5a4760ba10bdbe2141163f8aea6ecb5f3069ffa52820bcb530da9734e74b9c.jpg) 

  

 ![Soul's Might](https://i.ormos.uk/29/9e/299e939cf8cdcb4c5baedd79a086007f7a8394f25d7be9ac30be4d457e38f866.jpg)
