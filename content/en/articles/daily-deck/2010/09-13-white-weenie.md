Pro Tour–Amsterdam is in the books, and what a historic event it was! Paul Rietzl, playing in his second Pro Tour Top 8, took the entire event down despite the fact he was in one of the most star-studded Top 8s of all time! The legendary Kai Budde, a Hall of Famer, returned to the Top 8 playing an almost identical deck list as Rietzl, his teammate for the event. Their White Weenie deck beat down the opposition to take the top slot. Read more in the official coverage [here](/en/events/2010/ptams10/rietzl-sweeps-to-victory-in-amsterdam).
