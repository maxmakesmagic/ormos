## *Battlebond* Cards Live!

It is finally time for MTGO players to experience *Battlebond*, where competitors battle in pairs. Recruit a trusted partner and fight shoulder-to-shoulder, hand-in-hand, or back-to-back for glory and the roar of the crowd!

* * *

## PTQ Saturday

There is a Standard PTQ Finals on Saturday, that starts at 9 a.m. PT. The entry options are 30 Event Tickets or 300 Play Points.

Learn more about *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20181009000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers).

* * *

## PTQ Sunday

The Sealed PTQ Finals are on Sunday. The event starts at 7 a.m. PT, and costs one Chandra or Jace Limited Token to enter.

Learn more about *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20181009000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers).

## Downtimes October 24 and December 5

The next two downtimes will be October 24, 3 a.m.–noon PT/-7 from UTC and December 5, 3 a.m.–noon PT/-7 from UTC. Please note that there are no more 'extended' or 'normal' downtimes; in the new world with fewer downtimes, they're all the same length of time.

## Build Notes

Standard | [Demanding Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20181009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demanding+Dragon) now correctly allows a player to take damage instead of sacrificing a creature.

General | Snow mana now appears separate from other mana in the mana pool.

General | Triggers from [Aether Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20181009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=438778), [Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20181009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor), and the mentor ability will no longer be automatically placed on the stack to allow more precise control.

The following issues were implemented last Wednesday:

Modern, Cube | [Kalitas, Traitor of Ghet](https://web.archive.org/web/20181009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kalitas%2C+Traitor+of+Ghet) will no longer cause the game to restart.

Commander, Legacy | [Grindstone](https://web.archive.org/web/20181009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grindstone) and [Sphinx's Tutelage](https://web.archive.org/web/20181009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Tutelage) will no longer cause the game to restart.

Collection | You are now able to immediately update an imported deck with versions of cards from your collection.

Collection | Untradeable objects are no longer able to be added to trade binders.
