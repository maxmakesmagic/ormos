If you've never played [**Duels of the Planeswalkers 2012**](https://web.archive.org/web/20121221094006/http://www.wizards.com/magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx?), now would be a great time to start. That's because Steam and PS3 now have the Gold Bundle. For $19.99, that includes:

*   **Duels of the Planeswalkers 2012**
*   The [**Duels of the Planeswalkers 2012** Expansion](https://web.archive.org/web/20130706091135/http://www.wizards.com/magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx?x=mtg/digital/d12/exp1)
*   All three Deck Packs, giving you access to [every playable deck in the game](https://web.archive.org/web/20121220190909/http://www.wizards.com/magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx?x=mtg/digital/d12/decks)

Did you purchase the game when it first came out? We still have something to offer you! The Gold Deck Bundle costs $9.99 and it gives you all nineteen [playable decks](https://web.archive.org/web/20121220190909/http://www.wizards.com/magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx?x=mtg/digital/d12/decks) and the foil conversion for each one. Play in style!
