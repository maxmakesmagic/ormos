It was not an easy path for Ben Ge but he should be rejoicing in the knowledge that he was now qualified for the next Pro Tour in Portland. After dispatching fellow countryman Zhong Jian in the Boros Burn mirror, he hoped that his winning streak would continue

However, a Japanese [Juggernaut](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Juggernaut) was in his way. Playing in his TWENTIETH Grand Prix Top 8, Watanabe would most certainly like to win this one, because a victory here would put him at 7 Grand Prix victories, tying him with German [Juggernaut](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Juggernaut) Kai Budde. 

**Game One** 

Ge answered [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie) with [Searing Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blood), but didn't have [Chained to the Rocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chained+to+the+Rocks) for [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon). He was forced to ram [Chandra's Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Phoenix) into it, before finishing of the 6/6 flyer with [Warleader's Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warleader%27s+Helix). 

Now the he was relieved of pressure, Ge was able to slowly unleash an assortment of burn spells at the Japanese Player. [Magma Jet](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Jet) was first, followed by [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm), and then a second copy. 

Watanabe was down to one life in the blink of an eye and [Chandra's Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Phoenix) threatened to end things there and then. Watanabe prayed for the best as he tried to win with [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat), killing [Chandra's Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Phoenix) with [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall). 

A brief glimpse of hope shimmered in the distance for Watanabe, when he buffed his life total with [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel), putting him a comfortable 6 life. 

Well, not for very long though. Ge was ready with [Searing Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blood) to kill one of the [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s and a third [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm) prompted Watanabe to reach for his sideboard. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/sf_ge.JPG)

 *Triple [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm)s lead Ben Ge to victory.* 

  

**Ben Ge 1 – Yuuya Watanabe 0** 

**Game Two** 

Already estimating Boros Burn to be popular this weekend, Watanabe was fully prepared for his matchup and swiftly brought in the full set of [Staff of the Death Magus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Staff+of+the+Death+Magus). 

The Chinese audience gasped when it made an appearance on Turn 3, because it was obvious that the artifact would spell eventual doom for Ge.

[Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) resolved, gaining Watanabe one life. Swamp entered play, gaining Watanabe one life. [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) stripped away [Assemble the Legion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Assemble+the+Legion), gaining Watanabe one life. [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight) killed an opposing [Ash Zealot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ash+Zealot), gaining Watanabe one life. 

Well, you get the idea. Essentially, every card, other than his non-basics, would trigger the Staff. 

[Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel) was next, restoring Watanabe to a healthy 20. A second [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel) later and the audience were getting bored. When Watanabe cast a second [Staff of the Death Magus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Staff+of+the+Death+Magus) and then a third one, I could have sworn some of them even fell asleep. 

With every black spell and Swamp now upgraded to a [Healing Salve](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Healing+Salve), there was no way that Ge would be winning this one. If Watanabe doesn't clinch this game, I promise to gobble up my Mac Book Air, seasoned with any condiment of Riccardo's (Head Judge of Grand Prix Beijing) choice. 

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Staff+of+the+Death+Magus)](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Staff+of+the+Death+Magus)

  

**Ben Ge 1 – Yuuya Watanabe 1** 

**Game Three** 

Game three was almost as brutal as the last, except that it was much quicker. Watanabe proceeded to cast THREE [Staff of the Death Magus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Staff+of+the+Death+Magus) on turns three through five. 

A pair of [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress)es put him at a positive life total, while removing [Assemble the Legion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Assemble+the+Legion), [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike) and any of Ge's winning chances. A trio of [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s completed the match, sending Watanabe to the finals. 

**Ben Ge 1 – Yuuya Watanabe 2**

Triple [Staff of the Death Magus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Staff+of+the+Death+Magus) sends Yuuya Watanabe to the finals of Grand Prix Beijing!
