**The Setup**

Late in Day 1, two players with a loss apiece. On one side, Robert Smith, Grand Prix Sea-Tac Champion. On the other, Marc Angers, the hopeful amateur. The winner locks up Day 2 and a chance at a Top 8 run. The loser will have their back against the wall for Round 9.

**The Match**

The first game wasn't much of one, as Smith mulliganed into a one-land hand on the draw that failed to develop. Angers came out quickly with [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix), [Nyxborn Wolf](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nyxborn+Wolf) and [Nessian Courser](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nessian+Courser), all before Smith found his second land.

Angers 1 - Smith 0

For game two, both players went to six. On the third turn, Angers went for an unorthodox play. Here's the situation: Smith had a tapped [Oreskos Sun Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oreskos+Sun+Guide) and an untapped [Satyr Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Satyr+Wayfinder), freshly played. Angers had a [Swordwise Centaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swordwise+Centaur) and three mana available. Without hesitating, Angers went for the big lifeswing. He played [Mortal's Ardor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mortal%27s+Ardor) on his creature, then [Hunt the Hunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hunt+the+Hunter), killing Smith's Wayfinder. Combine that with the subsequent attack, and Angers was up twelve life.

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmon14/Smith.jpg)  
*Robert Smith*

 

The problem was that Smith wasn't far enough on the back foot. Sun Guide was keeping his life total high. [Hunter's Prowess](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hunter%27s+Prowess) drew him a mittful of cards, while [Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anger)s was operating on nearly an empty hand. They traded hits, but [Acolyte's Reward](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Acolyte%27s+Reward) from Smith ruined a crucial combat and sewed the game up nicely.

 

Angers 1 - Smith 1

Smith agonized over his opening seven. With a grimace, he shipped them back. He stayed on six, and they were off. [Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anger)s was first on the board with [Loyal Pegasus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loyal+Pegasus) and then the backbreaking [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix). Smith's first play of [Setessan Oathsworn](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Setessan+Oathsworn) was promptly dispatched by [Last Breath](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Breath), and then he passed a blank turn, leaving four mana open for [Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anger)s's combat step.

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmon14/SmithvAngers.jpg)

 

Heedless of the threat of [Divine Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divine+Verdict), [Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anger)s attacked, but not before tapping out for voyaging Satyr and [Ordeal of Heliod](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ordeal+of+Heliod). His read was good: Smith had no play. [Revoke Existence](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Revoke+Existence) rolled off the top for Smith to get rid of the Courser, but he had no answer to the growing threat of the Pegasus. [Nyxborn Wolf](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nyxborn+Wolf) bolstered the [Voyaging Satyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyaging+Satyr), and Smith was forced to play [Raised by Wolves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raised+by+Wolves) on his opponent's Satyr, just to get the pair of 2/2s. [Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anger)s saw victory ahead and got overeager, attacking with both his creatures and casting [Gods Willing](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gods+Willing) on his Satyr for [Protect](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Protect)ion from Green. The bestowed Wolf and Smith's enchantment fell off. The blunder didn't matter, however. Smith was too far behind, and the match went to [Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anger)s shortly thereafter, to the cheers of his friends along the rail.

 

Marc Angers defeats Robert Smith 2-1

**The Aftermath**

Both players brought their A-game to Round 9 to finish the day strong, Smith succeeding in his win-and-in, and [Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anger)s defeating Frank Skarren to finish at 8-1.
