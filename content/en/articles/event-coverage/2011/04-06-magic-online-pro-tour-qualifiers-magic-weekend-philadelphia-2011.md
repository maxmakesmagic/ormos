**PLAY THE GAME, SEE THE WORLD!** 

Think you have what it takes to beat the best? Now you can find out for sure. Pro Tour Qualifiers return to **Magic: The Gathering Online**. Open to everyone who has yet to qualify, players can earn an invitation to the [Pro Tour at **Magic Weekend** Philadelphia 2011](/en/events/2011/philadelphia11-qualifiers/qualifiers-for-pro-tour-philadelphia-2011) and an invitation to compete in the Philadelphia 2011 **Magic Online** Superdraft…all from the comfort of your own home!

**Magic Online** will host 16 Pro Tour Qualifier events between April 10, 2011 and June 26, 2011. See the complete schedule and event rules below.

### Magic Online Pro Tour Qualifier (Magic Weekend Philadelphia 2011)

*START TIME:* See above schedule.  
*LOCATION:* Premier Events room.  
*ENTRY FEE:* 30 Event Tickets for sealed deck events, 25 Event Tickets for constructed events.  
*PRODUCT:* **Magic Online** will provide 6 booster packs of the appropriate type (*Scars of Mirrodin*Block) for each sealed deck event (see schedule above). **Magic Online** will provide 3 booster packs of the same type as the event for the "Top 8" Draft for each sealed deck event. Players must bring a format legal constructed deck for each constructed event (see schedule above).  
*SIZE:* 33 players minimum, 768 players maximum.  
*DURATION:* 

*   For sealed deck events, deck building time plus a number of 50-minute [Swiss Pairing rounds determined by attendance](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=465). The "Top 8" will consist of 3 Rounds, Single Elimination.
*   For constructed events, a number of 50-minute [Swiss Pairing rounds determined by attendance](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=465) plus a 3-round, single elimination "Top 8" bracket.

  
*PRIZES:* Prizes are based on final standings at the end of the tournament.

 **NOTE:** The booster pack prizes for all **Magic Online** Pro Tour at **Magic Weekend** Philadelphia Qualifier events through 5/30/11 will be a combination of *Scars of Mirrodin* and *Mirrodin Besieged*. **Pro Tour Qualifier Eligibility**  
Invitations to the Pro Tour (including those awarded on **Magic Online**) are governed by the **Magic: The Gathering** Premier Event Invitation Policy. The latest version can be viewed [here](http://www.thedci.com/docs). Invitations to the Pro Tour awarded by **Magic Online** are invitations only and do not include compensation for travel or lodging. 

Players who already hold invitations to the Pro Tour at **Magic Weekend** Philadelphia 2011 may not compete in individual-format Qualifier tournaments (including **Magic Online** Qualifiers for the Pro Tour at **Magic Weekend** Philadelphia 2011) that feed into the Pro Tour at **Magic Weekend** Philadelphia 2011.

Players that compete in Qualifier tournaments (including **Magic Online** Qualifier tournaments) for which they already hold an invitation will be subject to appropriate DCI penalties, up to and including suspension.

**Important information regarding event crashes for the Pro Tour Qualifiers:** If, in the sole judgment of **Wizards of the Coast** and its representatives, a system error, tournament or rules bug, or other issue has significantly compromised the integrity of the event, **Wizards of the Coast** retains the right to modify the tournament structure as needed to maintain as fair and competitive an experience as possible for all players.

### Philadelphia 2011 Superdraft

*START TIME:* To be determined. The Philadelphia 2011 Superdraft will be scheduled around the other events at the Pro Tour at **Magic Weekend** Philadelphia 2011 so that registered players will be able to participate in both events.  
*LOCATION:* Live event at **Magic Weekend** Philadelphia 2011.  
*ENTRY FEE:* Free – Invitation only.  
*PRODUCT:* To be determined.  
*SIZE:* 2 flights; 8 players per flight.  
*DURATION:* 3 rounds, single elimination.  
*PRIZES:* Prizes are based on final standings at the end of the tournament.

**NOTE: *Superdraft qualification does not guarantee prizes. Players must participate in the Superdraft event at **Magic Weekend** Philadelphia in order to be eligible for the Superdraft prizes.***

Players must have a signed waiver and consent form to compete in the Philadelphia 2011 Superdraft. Forms can be downloaded [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20110406000000/http://www.wizards.com/protour) and will be available onsite. **Minors must have a parent or guardian's signature on these forms. Minors will not be able to register or play unless we receive a waiver signed by a parent or guardian. No exceptions!**

Bring these forms with you to registration. We will make forms available for competitors at registration, if necessary.  
 To register, all participants must present one of the following forms of identification:  

*   a driver's license, or
*   a passport, or
*   a copy of a birth certificate and photo IDs (school IDs are acceptable).
