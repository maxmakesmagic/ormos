Grand Prix Trials (GPTs) are smaller, regional **Magic** Tournaments that award at least one 2-round bye to the appropriate individual-format Grand Prix. Players with a 2-round bye at a Grand Prix get a "leg up" in the event – they don't have to play in the first two rounds of the tournament and are considered to have won those rounds!

If you’re interested in participating in a Grand Prix Trial, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20020423000000/http://locator.wizards.com/) to find one in your city.

To find out more about the format, rules, and product mix for the Grand Prix Trial you would like to participate in, [visit this page](/en/events/2002/welcome/grand-prix) and click "Info" in the Trials column on the seasonal calendar at the bottom of the page.

Note: as of September 5, 2013 there are no Grand Prix Trials for team-format Grand Prix.
