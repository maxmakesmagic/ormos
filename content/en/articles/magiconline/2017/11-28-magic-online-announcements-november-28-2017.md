**Vintage Super League tonight**

Vintage Super League (VSL) is live tonight at 5 p.m. PT on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/magic)!

Interested in the decklists being played on VSL? Decklists are updated weekly and can be found [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/12-06-vintage-super-league-season-7-decklists).

***Iconic Masters* now available on *Magic Online*!**

*Iconic Masters* is now available on *Magic Online*! Available in the *Magic Online* store for $6.99 per booster pack, *Iconic Masters* released simultaneously with tabletop *Magic* on Friday, November 17, and will be available until December 6.

*Magic Online* will feature two Draft leagues for this release, regular and phantom.

More information about the *Magic Online* release of *Iconic Masters* can be found [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/11-09-iconic-masters-comes-to-magic-online-on-november-17).

**Standard PTQ this Saturday, December 2**

Attention all Pro Tour hopefuls: A Pro Tour Qualifier (PTQ) is approaching! We hope to see you in the PTQ Finals on Saturday, December 2 at 9 a.m. PT. There is no need to qualify through a Preliminary—simply join this event with 30 Event Tickets or 300 Play Points.

Learn more about *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20171128000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers).

***Iconic Masters* Sealed PTQ Open this Sunday, December 3**

Attention all Pro Tour hopefuls: A Pro Tour Qualifier (PTQ) is approaching! We hope to see you in this PTQ Open on Sunday, December 3 at 7 a.m. PT.

Join this event with 50 Tix or 500 Play Points.

Learn more about *Iconic Masters* on *Magic Online* [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/11-09-iconic-masters-comes-to-magic-online-on-november-17)*.*

**Throwback Standard Gauntlet 10: Gauntlet of Greatness begins Wednesday, December 6**

Throwback Standard Gauntlet 10: Gauntlet of Greatness begins December 6 and will run until December 13. Expect Randy Buehler's article to be published tomorrow on [MTGO.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20171128000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-products-game-info), including the Gauntlet's decklists.

More information about Throwback Standard Gauntlets can be found [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/08-02-flashback-favorites-and-throwback-standard-gauntlets).
