**Adam Rex** has created art for **Magic: The Gathering** for about ten years, starting with *Mirage*. He received a BFA in Illustration from the University of Arizona in Tucson, and moved to West Philadelphia in 2001. His influences include the Brandywine School, John Singer Sargent, Phil Hale, and Maurice Sendak. When he's not painting for any of Wizards of the Coast's many divisions, he writes and illustrated books for children. His second picture book, STE-E-E-E-EAMBOAT A-COMIN'! by Jill Esbaum, was published in April 2005. His first book as both author and illustrator, TREE RING CIRCUS, will be available next year. While in Philadelphia, he strongly suggests you visit the Art Museum.
