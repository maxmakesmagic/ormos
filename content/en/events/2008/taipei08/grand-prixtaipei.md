Magic: The Gathering® Pro Tour™–Kyoto 2008 Qualifier  
 November 29-30, 2008

The **Magic: The Gathering®** Grand Prix tournament series is an international circuit of large-scale Qualifier tournaments featuring cash prizes and multiple invitations to Pro Tour™ events.

**TOURNAMENT FORMAT AND SCHEDULE** 

The format for Grand Prix–Taipei is *Shards of Alara*TM Block Limited, with Day 2 Booster Draft. All DCITM Limited -format rules, Magic® Floor Rules, and all subsequent updates will be enforced.

**Friday, November 28:** Onsite registration is from 03:00 –08:00 P.M.  
**Saturday, November 29:** Late registration from 08:00 – 09:00 A.M. Tournament begins at 09:30 A.M. If less than 800 players participate in the event, the Top 64 players advance to competition on Sunday If 800 or more players participate, the top 128 advance to Sunday.  
**Sunday, November 30:** The Top 64 (or 128) players begin play at 9:00 A.M.

**TOURNAMENT LOCATION**  
 Jian Tam Youth Center  
 Main Hall( 2F)  
 No.16, Chung Shan North Road, Section 4, Taipei 231

**ENTRY FEE**  
 NTD 1000

**GUEST ARTIST**  
 Rob Alexander

**TOURNAMENT** 

**Divisions**  
 The main tournament at Grand Prix–Taipei is open to **Magic** players of all ages.

*   **Head Judge**
     The Head Judge for Grand Prix–Taipei will be Jacky Yang, Level 3 from Taiwan.
*   **Tournament Structure**
     The structure for Grand Prix–Taipei will be modified Swiss-style (50-minute rounds), with the Top 64 players (or 128 if 800 or more players registered for the main event) advancing to the second day of competition for prizes and invitations.
*   **Tournament Divisions and Prizes**
     All participants in the Grand Prix will receive a Call of the Herd exclusive Grand Prix foil card, while supplies last

**Pro Tour Invitations**  
 The Top 16 previously uninvited players will each win an invitation to Pro Tour–Kyoto. Grand Prix invitations no longer pass down to previously uninvited players. The top 16 finishers will receive invitations even if one or more of those players already possesses an invitation.

**Tournament Byes** (Byes are not cumulative throughout the season or year)

Byes will be given to players in the Grand Prix based on the following criteria:

>  ***Third-Round Byes***  
>  
> 
> *   Pro Player club level 4 and above.
> *   DCI players with a Limited rating of 2000 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on the player's DCI Limited rating on the Wednesday before the Grand Prix.
> 
>  ***Second-Round Byes***  
>  
> 
> *   Pro Player club level 2 or 3
> *   DCI players with a Limited rating of 1900 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on the player's DCI Limited rating on the Wednesday before the Grand Prix.
> 
>  ***First-Round Byes***  
>  
> 
> *   Players with a [Players Club](/en/articles/feature/2005/05-02-welcome-to-the-pro-players-club) level of 1
> *   DCI players with a Limited rating of 1800 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on the player's DCI Limited rating on the Wednesday before the Grand Prix.

**Notes**
