### Agents of Artifice Fan Art Contest

![](https://i.ormos.uk/47/dc/47dc2cede0cc0176345518aef63217aaf5354e271ef12ec2846260191d79e1b9.jpg) They say a picture’s worth a thousand words. Give us your story and we could end up giving you $2,000!

Simply create a color work, either digitally or traditionally, depicting your favorite scene from the book [*Agents of Artifice*](https://web.archive.org/web/20120802044013/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Novels/Product.aspx?x=mtg/novels/product/agentsofartifice) by Ari Marmell. It can be a comic, a drawing, a painting ... use your imagination – and have fun with it! Plus, there are prizes for second and third place, too! 

Here is the [entry form](https://web.archive.org/web/20090608000000/http://www.wizards.com/mtg/images/novels/AofA_contestentryform.doc) (173k Word doc). You can email your entry to us at bookpromos@wizards.com, send it to us on a CD or DVD, or mail the actual piece to us. The deadline for submission is May 6, 2009.

For complete contest rules and eligibility, [click here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/agents-of-artifice-art-contest-official-rules-wizards-of-the-coast).
