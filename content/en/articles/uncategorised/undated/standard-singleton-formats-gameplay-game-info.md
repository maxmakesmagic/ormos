This specialty *Magic Online* format has most of the rules of a standard constructed deck:

*   Minimum of sixty cards.
*   No maximum deck size.
*   Up to fifteen cards in your sideboard, if used.
*   Only cards currently legal in the Standard format are permitted.

However, there’s one key exception: Instead of your main deck and sideboard being limited to *four* of any card, in standard singleton, you can’t have more than *one* of any card (other than basic lands).
