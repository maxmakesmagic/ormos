Grand Prix return with a vengeance this weekend, with a Modern tournament at [Grand Prix Minneapolis](/en/events/2014/minneapolis14-2/grand-prix-minneapolis-2014-event-information) and a *Theros* Block Limited one at [Grand Prix Warsaw](/en/events/2014/warsaw14/grand-prix-warsaw-event-information). Speaking of Limited, this season's Pro Tour Qualifiers (PTQs) for Pro Tour ***Magic** 2015* are using *Theros* Block Sealed as the format. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#m15). Good luck; have fun!
