A new event schedule will come into effect immediately following the December 10 update. These changes affect both when and how events will be run on ***Magic** Online.*

The first change is that events will no longer be scheduled in any of the following tournament rooms: **Draft Tournaments**, **Constructed Tournaments**, and **Sealed Deck Tournaments**. Also, the queues in the **Sealed Deck 8-player** room will be turned off. This is a precursor to removing these rooms entirely in the January update.

Instead, there will be four additional, non-qualifier events held in the **Premier Events** room each day. The formats vary, so check the [posted schedule](/en/articles/feature/2002/11-01-premier-events-room-schedule) and the list in the **Premier Events** room to see what's being played each day. These events cost the same as tournaments in the **Sanctioned Events** room: $5 for Constructed and $2 for Limited. 

Depending on the popularity of these non-qualifier events, additional unscheduled events will be added. These events will be posted in the **Premier Events** room and will be announced within the game.

Another change to the **Premier Events** room is that all qualifier events will have double the normal prizes. In addition, the top two finishers will receive invites to the Monthly Championship event. Players with invitations not only get to compete in the monthly championships, but also don't pay anything to play in the event.

Decisions were made based on analysis of event participation since the launch of ***Magic** Online,* including the drop in events starting after the November changes. With so many events not launching, we've decided to remove the least popular events and put all scheduled tournaments in the **Premier Events** room.

Previous to the launch of ***Magic** Online,* we decided to put in as much event support as possible. As time goes on, we'll continue to modify the schedules and rooms to accommodate what players want to play most. At the moment, we're putting more events in a central location (**Premier Events**) to give you a "one-stop shop" for what's going on in ***Magic** Online.*

***Magic** Online* is a living work: as the community grows and changes, so will the game. And while analysis of game statistics plays a role in the decision-making, so does your input. How do you feel about the changes to the schedule? Let us know on the ***Magic** Online* Message Boards.
