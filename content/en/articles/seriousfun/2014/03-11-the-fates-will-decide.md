The Fates have been particularly cruel to you. 

You played four games that night with your new deck and never once drew [Phyrexian Metamorph](https://web.archive.org/web/20140311000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Metamorph). Your choice was irrelevant. The Fates despise you and your belief that your choices are relevant.
