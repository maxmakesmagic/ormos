We asked some of the best players in the room about the most overrated card in *Khans of Tarkir* Limited. Here's what they had to say:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpstr14/qq_dickmann.jpg)

***Patrick Dickmann:** In draft, it's [Throttle](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Throttle). The card has never ever impressed me. I'd always rather cast another creature.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpstr14/qq_edel.jpg)

***Willy Edel:** [Ainok Bond-Kin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ainok+Bond-Kin). On turn three, you cast a morph, maybe another on turn four, then on turn five you really want to unmorph, and you basically never get to use outlast. Most people think [Ainok Bond-Kin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ainok+Bond-Kin) is better than [Jeskai Student](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Student). I don't.* 

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpstr14/qq_damodarosa.jpg)

***Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa:** [Weave Fate](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Weave+Fate). Or [Tormenting Voice](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tormenting+Voice).* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpstr14/qq_shenhar.jpg)

***Shahar Shenhar:** [Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc). It's the best card in the set, but it's still overrated. If that makes any sense.* 

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpstr14/qq_anteri.jpg)

***Fabrizio Anteri:** [Barrage of Boulders](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barrage+of+Boulders).* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpstr14/qq_juza.jpg)

***Martin Jůza:** [Valley Dasher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valley+Dasher). Maybe [Arc Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Lightning).*
