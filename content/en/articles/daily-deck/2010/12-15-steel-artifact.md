Looking for a fun new deck for Extended? Check out this version of an artifact-themed deck called "Steel Artifact." Pascal Maynard played this version to a flawless 6-0 record at the 2010 World Championships in Japan. It features tons of cheap artifacts powered by Tempered Steel. Jonathan Smithers, who also played the deck, was even featured in the above video deck tech discussing how the deck plays. Read more Worlds in the archive [here](/en/events/2010/worlds10/theres-no-jace-like-guillaume).
