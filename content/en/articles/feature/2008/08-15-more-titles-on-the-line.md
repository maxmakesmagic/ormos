The event coverage spotlight shines on two locations this weekend: Great Britain and the Netherlands. Who will be crowned as National Champions? Follow along all weekend for feature matches, podcasts, deck techs, draft analysis, and much more!

### [Great Britain National Championship](/en/events/2008/gbnat08/2008-great-britain-national-championship)

Coming to you from M-Fest in Birmingham, Tim Willoughby and Richard Hagon cover all the action.

### [Netherlands National Championship](/en/events/2008/nethnat08/2008-netherlands-national-championship)

The top Dutch players have gathered in Utrecht, with Eelco van Ruth, Henry van den Brink, and Jasper Bongaards handling coverage duties.
