### You Decide! -- The Legends Behind the Legends

The *Champions of Kamigawa* prerelease has come and everybody's had a chance to see the [spoiler](/en/articles/uncategorised/2004/09-20-champions-of-kamigawa). And by now, you've probably noticed that the [story on October 20](https://web.archive.org/web/20080516114245/http://www.wizards.com/magic/displayexpansion.asp?set=chk&page=2&lang=en) is marked by a question mark -- that's because we've been waiting for *you* to tell *us* whose backstory you want to know. Yes, it's time for you to pick which Legend's legend you want told.

We've got a list of ten legends (including two pre-legends, in the form of the new flip cards) for you to choose from. This vote will be up for a week, and then we'll commission an all-new story.

Have fun and choose carefully!
