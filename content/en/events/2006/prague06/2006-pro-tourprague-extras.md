### Oliver Cox, a famous actor in the Czech Republic has announced to attend PT Prague. Read more about him [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603151235/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=protour/prague06/cox)

### In many ways, Pro Tour-Prague is a **Magic** event you simply can’t afford to miss!

Besides watching the best Pro players battle you can participate in lots of side events, get artist signatures, meet the people behind **Magic**, but there’s more…

### Gunsling against the best!

Every day, Pro players and other **Magic** celebrities will be present on site to play games of **Magic** against whoever wants to challenge them! So, what are you waiting for… you might even beat him!  
*Names of gunslingers will be announced during the event*.

  
 

### Win great **Magic** prizes!… 3 days in a row!

Simply fill in the voucher\* that you will find at the Pro Tour welcome desk and deposit it in the contest box. A drawing will be held at the end of each day. Contest vouchers will be removed each day, so you need to enter a new voucher if you want to participate again.

### Friday contest

  
 1st prize: A trip to Grand Prix-Torino\*\*!  
 2nd – 11th prize: A Pro Tour souvenir T-shirt  
 12th – 21st prize: A free side event voucher (to be used at Pro Tour-Prague)  
  
  

### Saturday contest

1st prize: A trip to Grand Prix-Malmo\*\*\*!  
 2nd – 11th prize: A Pro Tour souvenir T-shirt  
 12th – 21st prize: A free side event voucher (to be used at Pro Tour-Prague)  
  

### Sunday contest

1st prize: A trip to Grand Prix-Athens\*\*\*!  
 2nd – 11th prize: A Pro Tour souvenir T-shirt  
 12th – 21st prize: A free side event voucher (to be used at Pro Tour-Prague)  
  
 \* 1 entry per person per day.  
 \*\* Transport + hotel (4 nights) + 3 free side event vouchers  
 \*\*\* Transport + hotel (2 nights) + 2 free side event vouchers  
  
  

### Learn the game!

If you’re still new to the game or have a friend who would love to learn to play **Magic**, then don’t worry. Throughout the event, there will be people on site to demo the game. So, don’t wait any longer… go to Pro Tour-Prague and learn to play **Magic**…!
