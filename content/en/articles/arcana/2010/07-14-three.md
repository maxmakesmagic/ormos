Let's say you suddenly gain the ability to turn invisible. Naturally, the first thing you do is sneak into R&D so you can watch them playtest upcoming sets, right? It's every **Magic** player's dream to get behind the scenes and learn what's coming up!

Well, maybe. Because it turns out that R&D doesn't always use stickers for its playtests. When making constructed decks, some people like to take ... shortcuts. For example, check out these three ***Magic** 2011* playtest cards.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/493_cards.jpg)  

The first question this raises, of course, is "Where in the color pie is '3'?" Also, "Why does '3' cost both ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless3.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif) and ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless1.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)?"

Okay, the question it actually raises is "What are each of those cards?" Because it turns out that the "3" in question means something else *each time*.

![Jace's Ingenuity](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Jace%27s+Ingenuity)

  

Well, that makes sense to abbreviate as "3". Sure.

![Lightning Bolt](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Lightning+Bolt)

  

Also logical. What *else* would a 3 that cost ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif) mean?

![Mana Leak](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Mana+Leak)

  

And that would be our ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless1.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif). Interestingly, if that card had had a 1/1 added to it, it could have stood in for [Augury Owl](https://web.archive.org/web/20100714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Augury+Owl).

So let this be a warning to you! Even if you *do* become invisible and sneak into R&D to watch playtests, you might still not know what's going on.
