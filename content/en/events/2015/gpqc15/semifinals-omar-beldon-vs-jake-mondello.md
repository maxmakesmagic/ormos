Jake Mondello had a narrow loss in his first Grand Prix Top 8, a finalist appearance at Grand Prix Cleveland earlier this year. Now, back in his second Top 8 Mondello was looking to earn the title that slipped away.

Standing in the way was Omar Beldon, who rallied to the Top 8 after going undefeated on Day 1. It was his first Top 8 at a Grand Prix, and his first notable *Magic* moment yet. Like Mondello, this was Beldon's chance to break through to a finals showdown and shot at a trophy.

**The Decks**

Beldon wielded a deck similar to what two other semifinal competitors had found success with as well: Dark Jeskai. One of the premier decks of Pro Tour *Battle for Zendikar*, the deck could attack across multiple modes. With [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider), it was a fast and efficient rempo deck that backed up threats with removal and countermagic. With [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) and [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy) it could grind out a controlling match, incrementally pulling ahead with card advantage and reusing its best options in the graveyard. Thanks to the free black mana off the combination of *Khans of Tarkir*'s fetch lands and *Battle for Zendikar*'s dual lands, [Crackling Doom](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crackling+Doom), [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) and [Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang) gave it both the “Dark” moniker and more potent tools for its arsenal.

Mondello however was the lone remaining that had taken a different path. Red-Green Eldrazi wasn't a deck on the map before Quebec City, and while others tried to make it work for the weekend it was Mondello that found the greatest success. With a slew of ramp spells from [Explosive Vegetation](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Explosive+Vegetation) and [Map the Wastes](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Map+the+Wastes) to [Nissa's Pilgrimage](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%27s+Pilgrimage) and [Shrine of the Forsaken Gods](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrine+of+the+Forsaken+Gods), Mondello was playing mosters like [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon) and [Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulamog%2C+the+Ceaseless+Hunger) to clear out the competition. With a smart suite of removal like [Rending Volley](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rending+Volley) and [Seismic Rupture](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seismic+Rupture) in the sideboard, Mondello's deck was built to bash in first with protection coming in one when needed.

**The Games**

Mondello led off the first game making plans with mana, finding [Shrine of the Forsaken Gods](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrine+of+the+Forsaken+Gods) with [Sylvan Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Scrying) on his second turn. Beldon missed his second land drop, and played a tapped [Prairie Stream](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prairie+Stream) on his third.

It left Mondello to  explode with mana off an unopposed [Explosive Vegetation](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Explosive+Vegetation).

[Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy) looked to turn things around but Mondello's fifth turn [Dragonlord Atarka](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Atarka) annihilated the puny Human Wizard. [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) revealed just [Jaddi Offshoot](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jaddi+Offshoot) in hand for Mondello but it didn't matter if the Dragon stuck around. Beldon made a [Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang) before falling to 4 life on Mondello's next attack.

Beldon didn't find anything to kill the Dragon in the end.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpqc15/gpqc15_SemiFinal-Beldon.jpg)  
*Omar Beldon was coming for the finals, but Jake Mondello's monsters started off eating everything.*

The second game featured Beldon leading off with Jace, Vyrn's Prodigy on the second turn. [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider) and Jace's transformation quickly followed as Mondello just kept playing lands.

[Explosive Vegetation](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Explosive+Vegetation) resolved, putting him up to an impressive eight in a hurry. [Rending Volley](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rending+Volley) handled the [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider) – saving Mondello some life – as Beldon kept ticking his Jace up.

[Nissa's Pilgrimage](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%27s+Pilgrimage) increased the mana even more for Mondello before Ugin, the Spirit resolved, removing Jace for “free” zero loyalty. More importantly, [Sanctum of Ugin](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sanctum+of+Ugin) was sacrificed, and Mondello pulled [Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulamog%2C+the+Ceaseless+Hunger) out from his deck.

The monsters were coming, but Beldon was prepared this time.

[Utter End](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Utter+End) was the end of Ugin, and Beldon dug in with [Painful Truths](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Painful+Truths) for three. Beldon passed and Mondello played his tenth land to cast [Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulamog%2C+the+Ceaseless+Hunger). It exiled two sources of red mana from Beldon, but [Ojutai's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ojutai%27s+Command) countered the Eldrazi and returned Jace to the battlefield.

Jace transformed again, and [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider) number two put the pressure on Mondello to find an answer. [Explosive Vegetation](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Explosive+Vegetation) was his first step, followed by [Map the Wastes](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Map+the+Wastes). Mondello fell to 8 life on Beldon's next attack, then had his [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon) countered by [Disdainful Stroke](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disdainful+Stroke).

5 life was next as Mondello drew into his second [Sanctum of Ugin](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sanctum+of+Ugin). Playing it then casting [Hangarback Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hangarback+Walker) for eight mana the Sanctum triggered, finding [Ruin Processor](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ruin+Processor) – which Mondello thought he could pay for by playing a Forest that was actually his second land that turn.

The judge paused and backed things up, which left Mondello to pass the turn into [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider) and [Crackling Doom](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crackling+Doom). Beldon had evened the score.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpqc15/gpqc15_SemiFinal-Mondello.jpg)  
*Jake Mondello needed to find a way over the flying doom Omar Beldon kept throwing his way.*

[Shrine of the Forsaken Gods](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrine+of+the+Forsaken+Gods) into [Map the Wastes](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Map+the+Wastes) began Mondello's usual ramping path in the third game, but [Soulfire Grand Master](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soulfire+Grand+Master) and [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider) put the pressure on for Beldon. [Explosive Vegetation](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Explosive+Vegetation) pushed the land count higher after Mondello played [Sanctum of Ugin](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sanctum+of+Ugin). [Rending Volley](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rending+Volley) cleared away the [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider), but Beldon kept up four mana to represent any number of counterspells.

Mondello went for [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon) anyway – which hit Beldon's [Disdainful Stroke](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disdainful+Stroke) – but it brought an [Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulamog%2C+the+Ceaseless+Hunger) to Mondello's hand off [Sanctum of Ugin](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sanctum+of+Ugin). Another [Rending Volley](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rending+Volley) killed the Grand Master and Mondello went for a second Ugin first. Using the Ghostfire loyalty ability, Mondello began to burn away at Beldon's tall life total.

[Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) at the end of Mondello's turn was a start, but Beldon kept his mana up. He dropped to 16 life from the next blast of Ugin and Mondello cast his final card in hand: Ulamog.

[Ojutai's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ojutai%27s+Command) hit the Eldrazi again, and [Utter End](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Utter+End) ended Ugin once more. [Jaddi Offshoot](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jaddi+Offshoot) and [Map the Wastes](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Map+the+Wastes) led into a 1/4 to blocker with and a [Haven of the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Haven+of+the+Spirit+Dragon) sacrifice to get back the namesake Planeswalker, but Mondello was in trouble: [Kolaghan's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kolaghan%27s+Command) into [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider) destroyed the Ugin and soon Mondello was facing down double [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider).

After drawing for one last turn all Mondello could do was shake Beldon's hand.

**Omar Beldon defeated Jake Mondello, 2-1.**
