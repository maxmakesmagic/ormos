Unfortunately for you, humble reader, that flood is not coming today because all we have for you are decks from **Magic Online**, which won't be seeing *Worldwake* until February 22. To see how *Worldwake* impacts Extended, be sure to check out live coverage of Grand Prix–Oakland this weekend, and then come back next week for Pro Tour–San Diego, featuring both Standard deck lists and *Zendikar/Zendikar/Worldwake* Booster Drafts!
