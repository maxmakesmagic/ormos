If you haven't heard by now, the [Game in the Gulf Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20081031000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.legionevents.com/cruise/&origin=) from Legion Events is a fantastic opportunity to enjoy your favorite game (**Magic**) while also enjoying a fantastic cruise! Plus, there are [tournaments all over the country offering a free cruise to winners](https://web.archive.org/web/20081031000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.legionevents.com/cruise/CruiseQualifier.htm&origin=)! One such event was recently held in Roanoke, Virginia. Joshua Owens finished in a respectable Top 16 with his blue-white-green Bant deck. It featured such *Shards of Alara* hits as Elspeth, Knight-Errant and Stoic Angel. No doubt we'll be hearing more from Bant at the upcoming State and Provincial championships. For more information on those tournaments, visit [www.mtgchamps.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20081031000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://mtgchamps.com/&origin=).
