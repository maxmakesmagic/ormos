Goblins have been a standby of **Magic** since the beginning, and so it's no surprise they have begun to show up in the new Modern format! In a recent **Magic Online** Daily Event, Adherant played this explosive Goblins decklist to a 3–1 record. Featuring a bevy of Goblins and Goblin Grenade to deal the last few points, this deck comes out of the gates quickly and threatens to finish off your opponent in no time. Take a look!

Adherant's Goblins 

Modern – Magic Online Daily Event #3314256
