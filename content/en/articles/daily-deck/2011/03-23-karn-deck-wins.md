Were it not for Karn, the world of Mirrodin wouldn't have come to exist in the first place. The Silver Golem has even seen his share of high level tournament play. He proved a valuable resource in Kai Budde's 1999 World Championship–winning artifact red deck, despite only taking one slot in the deck. Karn allowed Kai to turn his mana ramp into powerful attacking creatures, ultimately earning him the World Championship title.
