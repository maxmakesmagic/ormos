For Robert Berni, his appearance in the final match of Grand Prix Phoenix 2014 was a chance to claim what was lost in Grand Prix Kansas City 2013, where his chances at a trophy were dashed by eventual winner Seth Manfield in their Semifinal match. 

For Nathan Holiday, Grand Prix Phoenix 2014 was a chance for him to add another trophy to his collection. The Grand Prix San Diego 2013 Champion had already earned one Constructed title, and a win in Phoenix would cement his place among the United States' best Grand Prix Constructed competitors. 

The two faced off with relatively classic takes on Black and Blue Devotion, respectively. In a tournament where many were ready with splashes and new tech, it came down to a match between two decks that are synonymous with the post-*Theros* Standard metagame. Which one would win in this classic showdown? 

**The Games** 

Holiday spent his second and third turn deploying [Cloudfin Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudfin+Raptor), then growing it with a [Judge's Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge%27s+Familiar) with [Tidebinder Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidebinder+Mage). A [Bident of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bident+of+Thassa) threatened to over-take the game, despite Berni's best efforts at controlling the board with a hand of multiple [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight)s. [Domestication](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Domestication) on [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter) cleared the path for another swing with his only remaining creature, a [Judge's Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge%27s+Familiar). 

While Berni had spot-removal to answer most of Holiday's creatures, including for his own stolen [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter), it was too slow and not enough, as Holiday continued to fuel his hand with cards courtesy of his [Bident of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bident+of+Thassa). A [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) made things elementary, and Berni was more than willing to move to a second game. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpphx14/finals_berni.jpg)

*Robert Berni* 

  

When Berni's Thoughseize on the second turn of Game 2 revealed [Bident of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bident+of+Thassa), it almost immediately hit the graveyard. Holiday's [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter) met a [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall), and [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon) gave Berni an optimal start and a speedy, flying clock. That clock was downgraded to a frog lizard courtesy of [Rapid Hybridization](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rapid+Hybridization) on the next turn, though the 3/3 creature was still larger than Holiday's [Frostburn Weird](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frostburn+Weird) and [Tidebinder Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidebinder+Mage). [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) unsurprisingly was disposed of by Berni almost immediately upon entering play, and [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) gave Berni a way to mount an offense over the next couple of turns. 

[Domestication](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Domestication), however, cleared the frog lizard path and allowed Holiday to get an attack in with his [Tidebinder Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidebinder+Mage) and [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault). Berni's rats began to increase in number. One traded with an attacking [Frostburn Weird](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frostburn+Weird) on the next turn, and another Rat traded with the domesticated frog lizard a turn after that. When Holiday was out of threats, Berni took the opportunity to start attacking with his [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpphx14/finals_holiday.jpg)

*Nathan Holiday* 

  

One of Standard's lessons: when an army of giant rats start going after you, the game doesn't last long. When [Cyclonic Rift](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cyclonic+Rift) was not awaiting Holiday before his demise, he conceded. As added insult to injury, Holiday flipped the top card as he died to the rats, revealing the [Cyclonic Rift](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cyclonic+Rift) that eluded him. The final moved to its third and final game. 

Holiday started a card down due to a mulligan, and [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) made that hand smaller, stripping Holiday of [Rapid Hybridization](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rapid+Hybridization) and leaving him with two [Tidebinder Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidebinder+Mage)s, [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves), and an Island. While Holiday burned a [Cyclonic Rift](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cyclonic+Rift) to hold back Berni's [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter), he couldn't stop it on the next turn. When it attacked, it exiled a [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140406000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter) of Holiday's, to the chuckle of both players, as Berni cast it. 

When the Specter duo attacked on the next turn, Berni found another one of Holiday's Specters and immediately cast it from exile. 

Sure enough, a turn later, and Berni had a fourth Specter in play: three owned by Holiday. The game was all but over, and when Holiday found nothing waiting, he offered the handshake.

**Congratulations to Robert Berni, Grand Prix Phoenix 2014 Champion!**
