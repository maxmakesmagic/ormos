Last week, the *Mirrodin Besieged* [Visual Spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20110125000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/mirrodinbesieged/spoiler) (which is constantly being updated, incidentally) added a card called Into the Core. Dig it:

![Into the Core](https://i.ormos.uk/49/eb/49ebcda6d109b53b2566709eb93761bced2a62764fdd13d3455df3a015cc6c54.jpg)

What's happening in this card is that the Phyrexians have created a gigantic hole in Mirrodin's outer crust, causing whatever's on the surface to plummet into the fiery furnace beneath.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9c/d0/9cd0783cb9fcae1585f4c21d3bfae3e199670010ffe847c9bd8a63bec58b84a5.jpg)

Into the Core | Art by [Whit Brachna](https://web.archive.org/web/20110125000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Whit+Brachna%22%5D)

Now, do you see anything familiar in this piece? Maybe something that the Phyrexians might consider enough of a threat that they'd need to resort to this kind of overkill?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/be/79/be7935a151eaab6bc363780e7b82cd281e503e01cf9ca47fbcd544eddfd073a1.jpg)
