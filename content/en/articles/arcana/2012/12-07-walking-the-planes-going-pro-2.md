It's time for another Walking the Planes! In "Going Pro," Nate Holt and Shawn Kornhauser continue their journey through the **Magic** tournament Multiverse at San Antonio for that city's [first Grand Prix](/en/events/2012/gpsan12/lytles-zombies-big-in-texas). Get the Grand Prix perspective from Pro Tour legend Luis Scott-Vargas plus newcomers Grayson Maloch and Will Schendel.
