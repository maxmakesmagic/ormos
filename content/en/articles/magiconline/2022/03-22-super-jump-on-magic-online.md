Welcome to *Magic Online*'s take on *Jumpstart*: Super Jump!

![Super Jump! Logo](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/superjump-logo.png)

![Super Jump! Packet](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/superjumpbooster.png)

We've got 75 new 20-card packets exclusive to *Magic Online* ready for you to pick, put together, and play! From Kiki Copy and Stuffy Doll to Sacrifice VI, there is surely a combination of packets that'll tickle your fancy!

![Super Jump! Example](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/J2nwyeer.jpg)

Even though the packets are different in Super Jump!, the general *Jumpstart* experience remains the same. You are presented with three 20-card packets. After choosing one, you are presented with another three packets. After you pick another one, your two packets are combined, and you instantly see your 40-card deck, lands and all, and are ready to battle in up to three Best-of-One games with your Super Jump! deck.

*Note: You can double down, as the same packet might appear to you during both rounds of the "draft," so choose wisely!*

Much like in cube, these cards are not added to your collection, but unlike cube you only need to make 2 picks, and then you're off to battling!

## EVENT DETAILS

*   **Dates**: Wednesday, March 23–April 6, 2022
*   **Entry**: 5 Event Tickets or 50 Play Points
*   **Format**: Super Jump! Phantom League — Pick one Packet out of three, repeat, and play a combined deck.
*   **Location:** Limited Play Lobby
*   **Structure**: Three-round League featuring Best-of-One games and a 15-minute round timer
*   **Rewards**:
    *   3 Wins: 80 Play Points and 1 Treasure Chest
    *   2 Wins: 50 Play Points
    *   1 Win: 20 Play Points
    *   0 Wins: 10 Play Points

Below, you'll find a full card list and all 75 Super Jump! packets, searchable by packet or card name.

We hope you enjoy your time playing Super Jump! and how *Magic Online* translated this fan favorite format. Well, what are you waiting for? It's time to Super Jump!
