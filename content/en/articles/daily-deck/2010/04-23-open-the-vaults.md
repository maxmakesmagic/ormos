*Rise of the Eldrazi* playtester Bill Stark was the Wizards gunslinger at the Prerelease in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. He opted to bring an Open the Vaults deck to battle starring one of *Rise of the Eldrazi's* most interesting new cards: Wall of Omens. The Wall, which is a take on Wall of Blossoms in a different color, allowed him to set block while trying to set up a massive Open the Vaults. With Sphinx of Lost Truths allowing him to discard Filigree Angels, the Open the Vaults would lead to a massive upswing in life and a game win shortly after!
