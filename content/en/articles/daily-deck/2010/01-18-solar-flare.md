Solar Flare was a popular control/reanimator strategy during the days of *Ravnica* Standard. Now, thanks to Sphinx of Lost Truths and Rise from the Grave, the Esper-colored deck is making a return! SorryMsJackson recently took this list to the top of the standings in a Standard event on **Magic Online**!
