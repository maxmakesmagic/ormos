### October 14, 1997: *Tempest* Hits

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8b/1c/8b1cb057447b6db015cfa1c2156a862d88aac114091768c064a1868af9e81352.jpg)

Twelve years ago, Gerrard Capashen and the crew of the *Weatherlight* kicked off the Rath Cycle, picking up where *Weatherlight* left off. This set introduced **Magic** players to Slivers, Cursed Scroll, Living Death, and Tradewind Rider. And also Phyrexian Grimoire, Phyrexian Hulk, and Phyrexian Splicer, which is all the excuse we need to point out that two days ago, we announced [Duel Decks: Phyrexia vs. The Coalition](/en/articles/arcana/2009/10-14-announcing-duel-decks-phyrexia-vs-the-coalition) on the anniversary of *Tempest*'s release!
