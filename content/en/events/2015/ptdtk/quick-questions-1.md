# Which *Dragons of Tarkir* card has surprised you most with its performance in Limited?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_lax.jpg)  
**Ari Lax, No. 5 Ranked Player:** [Illusory Gains](https://web.archive.org/web/20150410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Illusory+Gains). I thought it wouldn't be good, but it is very powerful. If the dash mechanic didn't exist, it would be a Top 10 card in the set. As it stands, it's either the best card in your deck, or one you have to side out.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_turtenwald.jpg)  
**Owen Turtenwald, No. 1 Ranked Player:** [Berserkers' Onslaught](https://web.archive.org/web/20150410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Berserkers%27+Onslaught). In the past, cards like it haven't been that good, but in this format, it is excellent.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_floch.jpg)  
**Ivan Floch, No. 4 Ranked Player:** Two red rares: [Volcanic Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20150410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Volcanic+Vision) and [Descent of the Dragons](https://web.archive.org/web/20150410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Descent+of+the+Dragons). Both are splashy, powerful effects, but neither one has a home.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_Nakamura.jpg)  
**Shuhei Nakamura, Hall of Famer:** [Sidisi's Faithful](https://web.archive.org/web/20150410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sidisi%27s+Faithful). It looks bad, but it's great in all three of its modes, either as an early blocker, a bounce spell, or both. It also combos with [Lose Calm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lose+Calm) as a cheap exploit creature.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_wilson.jpg)  
**Jacob Wilson, No. 12 Ranked Player:** [Atarka Beastbreaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20150410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Atarka+Beastbreaker). This looked like a great card for beatdown strategies, and an early drop that could be powerful later, but it turns out that it's nothing exciting.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/ptdtk/qq_shenhar.jpg)  
**Shahar Shenhar, No. 7 Ranked Player:** All of the dragonlords. They look fantastic, and they are somehow so much better. Each one of them feels like it's game over.
