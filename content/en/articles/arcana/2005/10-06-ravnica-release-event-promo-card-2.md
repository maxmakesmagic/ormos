If you attend this weekend's [*Ravnica* Release Events](https://web.archive.org/web/20121112094004/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/release), you'll get a special, promotional, premium **Dimir Guildmage**:

We took photos from a couple angles to show you how it's foiled-out. *Behold the shininess.*

How do you get one? Simple.

1.  Click [these words right here](https://web.archive.org/web/20121112094004/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/release) to view the Release Event fact sheet.

2.  Find a release event near you at the top of that page.

3.  Go to your chosen event(s) and play some *Ravnica* sealed deck. You'll get your uber-foily Dimir Guildmage there, while supplies last.
