The art for *Fifth Dawn's* artifact-destruction spell **Granulate**, by Brian Snoddy, was originally a lot greener. The goblins in the art were losing their weapons to a green mold-like effect (see smaller image below), similar in concept to Oxidize or Creeping Mold.

![Granulate - in green?](https://i.ormos.uk/55/4c/554cd1816ddd0dde7ae8b70119a5edea49eed8fec340a2c468e417b2f0f2d9b3.jpg)  
*Brian Snoddy's Granulate gets a color touchup*

When the art was moved to the card that would become Granulate, the green-colored melting effect became inappropriate; the artifact-destruction effect in the art needed to change to red. So the image was re-colored to become the card you'll see in *Fifth Dawn* booster packs.
