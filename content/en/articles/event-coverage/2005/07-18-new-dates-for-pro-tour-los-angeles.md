Pro Tour-Los Angeles has been moved to October 28-30, 2005. 

This shift in dates also changes the format from "Rotated" Extended to normal Extended Constructed.

The following card sets will be legal for Pro Tour-Los Angeles: *Seventh Edition, Eighth Edition, Ninth Edition, Invasion, Planeshift, Apocalypse, Odyssey, Torment, Judgment, Onslaught, Legions, Scourge, Mirrodin, Darksteel, Fifth Dawn, Champions of Kamigawa, Betrayers of Kamigawa, Saviors of Kamigawa, Ravnica: City of Guilds*. 

Complete information about Pro Tour-Los Angeles can be found [here](/en/events/2005/la05/2005-pro-tourlos-angeles).

Wizards of the Coast apologizes for any inconvenience this might cause.
