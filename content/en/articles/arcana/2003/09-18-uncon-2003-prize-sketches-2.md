[Wizards.COMmunity's](https://web.archive.org/web/20030918000000/http://www.wizards.com/community) online convention, [Uncon](https://web.archive.org/web/20030918000000/http://wizopages.wizards.com/uncon/), featured several **Magic** tournaments and contests. Pete Venters, **Magic** artist since the time of *Antiquities*, was so kind as to autograph and sketch on the back of some artist's proofs for some of the winners. (An artist's proof is like a regular **Magic** card, but has a plain white back instead of a **Magic** card back.) Today's Arcana shows off some samples of those coveted prizes.

![Spelljack and Skirk Marauder](https://i.ormos.uk/9e/e3/9ee39d6372d352df764333116fcdb721b6dfd1c992b40b0baf90f2b470341348.jpg)

 ***Spelljack** sketch won by "Stud Bracelet," first place in the "Tog Block" tourney  
**Skirk Marauder** sketch won by "Sim User," first place in the Type 1.5 Highlander tourney* 

![Defiant Elf and Baron Sengir](https://i.ormos.uk/32/41/32416255774449a12381ca3cfbd9659ab5acc9051bbb3fbfa1f9c98f7bcaf1fc.jpg)

 ***Defiant Elf** sketch won by "Wulfgar911," first place in the Type 2 Peasant Magic tourney  
**Baron Sengir** sketch won by "Armadillo King," first place in the Jeopardy Tournament of Champions* 

Congratulations to all the winners at [Uncon 2003](https://web.archive.org/web/20030918000000/http://wizopages.wizards.com/uncon/)!
