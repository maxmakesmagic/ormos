Vengevine is the hottest card in Standard right now, but Yoel42 didn't even bother to put it main deck in a recent Pro Tour Qualifier Top 8 on **Magic Online**. Instead the goal of Yoel's deck was generating as many creatures as possible, preferably via Eldrazi Spawn tokens or Wolves from Wolfbriar Elemental, then overwhelming the opponent using Beastmaster Ascension, Eldrazi Monument, and Overrun. It's the perfect deck to test at your Friday Night Magic!
