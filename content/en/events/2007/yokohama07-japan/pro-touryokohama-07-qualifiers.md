### Entry Fee

The entry fee for all Pro Tour—Yokohama Qualifier tournaments in Japan is ¥2,000.

### Format

*   The format for all Pro Tour—Yokohama Qualifier tournaments is Extended Constructed. For information on this format, please refer to the DCI Universal Tournament Rule and **Magic** Floor Rules documents on the [DCI Document Center](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/welcome-to-the-dci-document-center).
*   The expansion *Planar Chaos* will become available for use in Extended Constructed on February 20, 2007. Players participating in Qualifier tournaments before that date cannot use *Planar Chaos* in their decks.
*   Deck lists are required.

### Tournament Structure

*   The structure for all Pro Tour—Yokohama Qualifier tournaments will be modified Swiss-style, with the Top 8 players playing in a single-elimination round for prizes and invitations.
*   Each round in the Swiss portion of the tournament will be 50 minutes.
*   The number of Swiss rounds will be determined based on attendance according to the following chart:

### Advancement

*   All players may participate in all Swiss rounds.
*   The top 8 players after the final Swiss round will advance to the Top 8 single-elimination finals.

### Top 8 Finals

*   All Top 8 matches at Pro Tour—Yokohama Qualifier tournaments will be best 2 of 3.
*   Top 8 matches will have no time limit.
*   When necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will be used to determine final order in the standings.

### Notes

*   All Pro Tour—Yokohama Qualifier tournaments are run at Rules Enforcement Level 3, and are 32K.
*   All Universal Tournament Rules and **Magic: The Gathering** Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this format document.
*   Players are responsible for bringing their own pen, paper, card sleeves, and appropriate counters.

### Invitations and Eligibility

Players already holding an invitation to Pro Tour—Yokohama are prohibited from participating in any Pro Tour—Yokohama Qualifier tournaments, with the exception of Grand Prix tournaments. Players may have earned invitations through performance at a previous Pro Tour, Player's Club membership level, winning a previous Qualifier or Grand Prix tournament, or by being an invited DCI player (valid upon the date of the official ratings invitation announcement for the appropriate Pro Tour). Please refer to the [Pro Tour—Yokohama Fact Sheet](/it/events/2009/yokohama07-invitations/2007-pro-touryokohama) for a list of qualified players.

### Prizes

*   The winning player of each Pro Tour—Yokohama Qualifier tournament in Japan will receive an invitation to the Pro Tour and a round-trip train ticket from his/her closest station to Sakuragi-chou station in Yokohama. (Residents of Hokkaido, Shikoku, and Kyuushuu will instead receive a round-trip airplane ticket from their nearest airport to Haneda Airport in Tokyo and a ticket from there to Yokohama.)
*   Product prizes to be determined by the organizer.
*   The Pro Tour—Yokohama Qualifiers will also feature a special Amateur prize schedule. The Amateur prizes will be awarded to the highest-finishing amateur players *in addition to any other prizes those players receive from the main tournament prize schedule*. The highest-finishing amateur players will receive additional booster packs as follows.
