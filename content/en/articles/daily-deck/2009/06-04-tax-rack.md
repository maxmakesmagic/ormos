A blast from the past, Tax Rack White Weenie was a deck bent on abusing the powerful draw engine Land Tax. By playing an aggressive weenie deck, Pro Tour Champion Randy Buehler was able to keep a low in-play land count so that he could best abuse Land Tax, drawing three cards a turn! He turned that advantage by abusing the additional cards with Firestorm, Mox Diamond, and Scroll Rack. Being able to re-shuffle his deck each turn as well as drawing three additional cards was powerful enough to net him an Extended Championship title!
