The noble masticore goes back to [Urza's Destiny](https://web.archive.org/web/20101101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Urza%27s+Destiny), when Paolo Parente provided this art for the Famous Original [Masticore](https://web.archive.org/web/20101101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Masticore):

![](https://i.ormos.uk/21/33/2133377463eb9723d859266743e882e5c12bc95009368b97163a3cf7b765b933.jpg)

[Masticore](https://web.archive.org/web/20101101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Masticore) | Art by [Paolo Parente](https://web.archive.org/web/20101101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Paolo+Parente%22%5D)

After a brief stop in the art for [Deep Analysis](https://web.archive.org/web/20101101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deep+Analysis), the [Masticore](https://web.archive.org/web/20101101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Masticore) got a revamp in *From the Vault: Relics*, when Steven Belledin gave us a leaner version.

The next masticore was *Fifth Dawn*'s [Razormane Masticore](https://web.archive.org/web/20101101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Razormane+Masticore), from the plane of Mirrodin. Jim Murray provided a more sharp-edged example of the species.

![Razormane Masticore](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Razormane+Masticore)

  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/25/e0/25e00411adf02227de022d4e975647063a360892e2d977deabf962910596730e.jpg)

[Razormane Masticore](https://web.archive.org/web/20101101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Razormane+Masticore) | Art by [Jim Murray](https://web.archive.org/web/20101101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Jim+Murray%22%5D)

And that brings us up to *Scars of Mirrodin*, and the ominous [Molten-Tail Masticore](https://web.archive.org/web/20101101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Molten-Tail+Masticore). Whit Brachna illustrated this mythic monster!
