It's the season of shamrocks and shillelaghs, annoying pinches and pots of leprechaun-guarded gold. To celebrate, **Magic Online** has put together an event full of gold—gold cards that is.

Beginning Sunday, March 16, at 12:00 PM PDT, and continuing until downtime begins on Wednesday, March 19, join in for fun Phantom Sealed events featuring booster packs from these sets:

*   Invasion
*   Planeshift
*   Apocalypse
*   Ravnica: City of Guilds
*   Guildpact
*   Dissension
*   Shards of Alara
*   Conflux
*   Alara Reborn
