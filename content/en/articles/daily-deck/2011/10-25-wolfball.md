Off-the-wall deck designer Travis Woo wasn't merely content finding Kessig Wolf Run with his Primeval Titans. Taking a note out of his Pro Tour Philadelphia playbook, he sleeved up some Glimmerposts and created the quickest Primeval Titan deck in Standard! Check out his unique and fun take on the Wolf Run Green strategy, which carried him all the way to a Top 8 finish at the Washington [2011's](http://www.the2011s.com/The2011s_Decklists.html).

Travis Woo's Wolfball 

Standard – The 2011's in Seattle, WA
