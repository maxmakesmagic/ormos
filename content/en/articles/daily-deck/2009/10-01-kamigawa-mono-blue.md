While the rest of the world's pros were battling with Gifts Ungiven style control decks, Antonino De Rosa was following the path of a different drummer. His innovative Mono-Blue Control deck was the perfect foil to the Hana Kami engine from Gifts Ungiven, and he managed to take his deck all the way to the title slot at Grand Prix–Salt Lake City. For more on that event, read the coverage in the [official event archive](/en/events/2005/gpslc05/the-big-man-is-blessed#6).
