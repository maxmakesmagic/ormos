![](https://i.ormos.uk/c7/38/c7383da62fd8d637de0a8bd04495e1e39849f77ece2487c3ac7c6a6a8eb8d18e.jpg)Pro Tour L.A. 2001 Champion Mike Pustilnik is not doing so hot this weekend. Already the mild mannered Doctor P has found his way to two losses. Sharing his misery is Grand Prix - Hong Kong Champion and Magic Colonist Jeff Fung. Unsurprisingly both have their titles in Limited formats.

The game started slow. Pustilnik found [Mind Sludge](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind+Sludge) with [Tainted Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tainted+Pact), then busted out [Nantuko Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Shade). Fung [Aether Burst](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Burst)ed it, but it came back the next turn. Fung's only defence was a meager [Aquamoeba](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aquamoeba). [Cabal Coffers](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Coffers) gave Pustilnik fuel for [Morbid Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morbid+Hunger) on the [Aquamoeba](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aquamoeba), but Fung had a second [Aether Burst](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Burst) to save.

Next turn, Pustilnik made [Mind Sludge](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind+Sludge) against Fung's two-card hand. Fung pitched forest and tried [Circular Logic](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Circular+Logic).

"So I have to pay 3 more? However, you have no hand." - Mike Pustilnik  
 "Dammit, Mikey P, why are you so smart?" - Jeff Fung

The Shade came back down. Fung's attacked with his [Aquamoeba](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aquamoeba), pushing it towards the Shade, but Pustilnik refused to block. Fung then tried asking for a money-split. When Pustilnik declined the offer, Fung scooped.

Pustilnik 1 - Fung 0

"This should be called the Battle of the Constructed All-Stars." - Mike Pustilnik  
 "Yeah, I mean, at least at San Diego I could handle the format. Here, I lose to a fifteen year-old kid, and have to say 'Yeah, sounds about right'." - Jeff Fung

Fung was amazed to find himself with a turn-two creature in the second game, but Pustilnik was quick to dispatch it with Innocent Blood. He had no answer for [Call of the Herd](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Call+of+the+Herd). He had to spend his mana finding a swamp with Tainted Pact.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/b5/e1b5fdc1acde8f26f1eb71651f32e9aaecdd8413f72cfe88b4922f4e4cdb2b23.jpg)Fung compounded his difficulties with a [Careful Study](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Careful+Study) that put Roar of the Wurm in the graveyard, and a [Standstill](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Standstill). Pustilnik looked at the board and said "Well, that went badly for me."

He had to give Fung the cards Mutilating the token. Fung's next play was to flashback the Roar, and play a sacrificial [Basking Rootwalla](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basking+Rootwalla). However, Pustilnik was ready with [Innocent Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Innocent+Blood) and another Edict. [Nantuko Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Shade) showed up ready to fight.

Fung decided it was time to act. He busted out [Squirrels Nest](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squirrels+Nest) and [Standstill](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Standstill). Then he let Pustilnik hit him once with the Shade (for ten!) so that he could have a Squirrel on the offensive. They played the waiting game. Pustilnik fell to twelve when Fung's hand filled.

Pustilnik used [Tainted Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tainted+Pact) to break the [Standstill](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Standstill), following it up with a [Skeletal Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skeletal+Scrying) for five. Fung discared [Arrogant Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrogant+Wurm) and Rootwalla into play, and pitched [Roar of the Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roar+of+the+Wurm). For all that power, things were not going Fung's way. Double-Coffers meant Pustilnik could [Mutilate](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutilate) and save his shade, with enough mana left over to pay for [Circular Logic](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Circular+Logic).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3b/1f/3b1f64715909ce227d498e1145584c8a282f2b6660024422ac77a7ca8374ef1d.jpg)However, Jeff Fung plays for keeps. Down came the second [Squirrels Nest](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squirrels+Nest), an endless supply of food for Pustilnik's Edict-based removal. Pustilnik had the gall to [Mind Sludge](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind+Sludge).

"[Mind Sludge](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind+Sludge), targetting you." - Mike Pustilnik  
 "Targetting Me? Of all people!" - Jeff Fung  
 "I'd target Ryan Fuller, but he's not in the game." - Mike Pustilnik

Once Fung's hand was gone, Pustilnik was free to send [Morbid Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morbid+Hunger) to the dome. The following turn he flashed it back, and played a second one. Fung extended the hand. Afterwards, Pustilnik confided that he had sided out both his [Caustic Tar](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Caustic+Tar)s and [Rancid Earth](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rancid+Earth)s, so his [Morbid Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20020314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morbid+Hunger)s were his only path to victory. Fung could only roll his eyes.

**Final Result:** Mike Pustilnik defeats Jeff Fung 2-0
