## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/33/4c/334c4ec2e7469c3db6cdccc7a61fad303122dde0d6e740c9ea8e102d45c5aaad.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, a card box with panoramic art, eight 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a6/5c/a65ca8fb01089e2ec462ee4d6a84dd02ab448452751fcabb76acdec0c5d0ee85.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/22/46/224659c55dee6ec50c56f6e3b81bb2acc8dfdf74e4513ad877e31f4882fb5e0e.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 cards, a foil premium rare card, one 15-card booster pack, a strategy insert, and a **Magic** learn-to-play guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044619/http://magic.wizards.com/node/960726)

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6a/41/6a417488d8debe99c65b8f8e869bbcbe346b7a7760c751b26708d1b4391399da.png)This 249-card, black-bordered expansion includes randomly inserted premium versions of all cards in the set, new mechanics (Metalcraft, Infect, and Proliferate), and new Planeswalker cards.

**Set Name:** *Scars of Mirrodin*  
**Block:** Set 1 of 3 of the *Scars of Mirrodin* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 249

**Languages:** English, Chinese Traditional, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

**Official Three-Letter Code:** SOM  
**Twitter Hashtag:** #MTGSOM

**Final Game Design and Development:** Mike Turian (lead), Aaron Forsythe, Erik Lauer, Mark Purvis, Matt Place
