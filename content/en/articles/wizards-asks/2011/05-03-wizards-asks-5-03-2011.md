I've found it a little too easy to win using Iona, Shield of Emeria and Akroma, Angel of Wrath, so I'm changing my deck to only use fatties I've designed. *New Phyrexia* has a slew of them for this deck, including:

> 1.  Chancellor of the Annex
> 2.  Chancellor of the Spires
> 3.  Chancellor of the Dross
> 4.  Chancellor of the Forge
> 5.  Chancellor of the Tangle
> 6.  Urabrask the Hidden
> 7.  Vorinclex, Voice of Hunger
> 8.  Jor Kadeen, the Prevailer (a maybe)

I promise I don't name the cards, but when I design cycles of cards, some are bound to make it into my pet decks! :D
