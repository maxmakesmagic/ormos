Today, we hand over Arcana to **Magic** art director Jeremy Jarvis. Take it away, JJ!

It's fairly well known that I vary the group of illustrators working on a new setting from the last to help visually set the tone for the new plane. Here are some names new to **Magic** with *Zendikar* to keep an eye out for:

**Adi Granov**: Two words. *Iron. Man.* I actually contacted Adi in hopes that he would paint a [webcomic](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/638086) for us. He couldn't, but he was game to paint cards.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/69/ba/69baac9ad09254e5a748499ab7056ce390c3dcf59474987e353e5c15f545191d.jpg)*[Bloodchief Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodchief+Ascension) by Adi Granov*  
  

**Goran Josic**: A video game guru.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/bb/29/bb2999dbc1dcbcfe0dfb4d91cc6d2bfd68f3f808a6c35edf207fe6702ae8ec5f.jpg)*[Terra Stomper](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terra+Stomper) by Goran Josic*  
  

**Igor Kieryluk**: Igor is a fun story .... He posted four of his new paintings in our **Magic** Art and Artists forum, and they were really good! I offered to buy three of them as "slush" (art that we will find a home for as soon as we can) and then continued to commission him as a **Magic** artist!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d7/a2/d7a2226ffaa930263bed4eb8e3dc9a4283eccdb7d83504f9b981fd0f0bfc5ea0.jpg)*[Cosi's Trickster](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cosi%27s+Trickster) by Igor Kieryluk*  
  

**James Paick**: Another video game guru. You've seen some of James's work by now in *Planechase*, but *Zendikar* was still his first (*Planechase* simply had an earlier release date).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/78/62/786224a2f605fe68b5d05202b9c96e1069c1282b4eda5cb93ae22c9b4b4216ff.jpg)*[Carnage Altar](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Carnage+Altar) by James Paick*  
  

**Jason Felix**: Jason and I have known each other from conventions for a while now. He was a great choice for helping us concept *Zendikar* ... as well as some ... other ... upcoming ... things .... and has contributed greatly to the setting!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2d/fe/2dfebd29a161bcd31358634be5681e6097f37a479d721b1174ebcc570c5e74b7.jpg)*[Feast of Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Feast+of+Blood) by Jason Felix*  
  

**Jung Park**: Video game guru.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/89/99/8999bb8f86a822ff316f07c4f04d723d5f9cdeaab2e0f3a23b9017691cc519e0.jpg)*Zendikar [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain) by Jung Park*  
  

**Kekai Kotaki**: Another super-talented artist from digital gaming.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2c/75/2c7588d654c359e25f6684ff05e2f851efaec1591699272e865504dc59502abb.jpg)*[Shepherd of the Lost](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shepherd+of+the+Lost) by Kekai Kotaki*  
  

**Kieran Yanner**: I stole Kieran from **D&D** :)

No, but seriously, it was keeping up with the work he was doing for **Dungeons & Dragons** that caught my eye.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ae/75/ae75871d2b8ffe8386f060619b60922a5c4ebcd26c9e2e2eba0ebf8cf922d443.jpg)*[Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut%2C+the+Molten+Pinnacle) by Kieran Yanner*  
  

**Mike Bierek**: Okay, I'm fudging again, Mike has cards in M10, but he's new enough. I met Mike at Massive Blacks workshop here in Seattle. I offered him cards on the spot.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ec/dd/ecdd343981e3b37cb6b9b00d58fe87dde60d0e7b0c2fddb439e17df5ccc5a8a0.jpg)*[Quest for Ancient Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quest+for+Ancient+Secrets) by Mike Bierek*  
  

**Nic Klein**: Nic's first card was actually [The Rack](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Rack) in [**Duels of the Planeswalkers**](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=Magic/Digital/DuelsOfThePlaneswalkers.aspx) for Xbox. *Zendikar* will feature his first cards in physical print.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9a/71/9a710db00f9ada5fcf1cbb3be5f4f0a97f42ec238629699e11179cfd337aa103.jpg)*[Unstable Footing](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unstable+Footing) by Nic Klein*  
  

**Ryan Pancoast**: Ryan submitted samples through our open-submission email address, mostly Western imagery if I remember correctly. The talent under the subject matter was clear, and he's been a **Magic** artist since M10.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ce/2e/ce2e6c68a21741f106206bafcc0db9aee1435c32a2e6787b882f72078e33be9a.jpg)*[Kor Cartographer](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kor+Cartographer) by Ryan Pancoast*  
  

**Scott Chou and Austin Hsu**: James Zhang, who's name you make recognize from D&D products, introduced me to these guys. Austin actually started in M10, and Scott painted the incredible [Demonic Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonic+Tutor) featuring Liliana and Kothophed that printed in *Duel Decks: Divine vs. Demonic.*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6a/73/6a731e1c5d2bf3f2fe694765310c3b22b5dacc7cab7de77fddac1c9f0021f726.jpg)*[Marsh Casualties](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Marsh+Casualties) by Scott Chou*  
  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9f/0c/9f0c66368a2706f4c01d0ac31f910f42e2fc7936f648f7eabb9286a7b70c5c10.jpg)*[Baloth Cage Trap](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baloth+Cage+Trap) by Austin Hsu*  
  

**Veronique Meignaud**: I started to communicate with Veronique more than a year ago. I saw samples somehow; I think someone referred her my way.

Her first card was actually the *Divine vs. Demonic* Thrull token, which is awesome. *Zendikar* is a new level of exposure for her, with her cycle of extended-art basic lands.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b5/2a/b52a0757d10a3b3fc78bad5a9db559ac1cf69b0f00a1870331808ac61deb4d91.jpg)*Zendikar [Plains](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plains) by Veronique Meignaud*  
  

**Vincent Proce**: Yet another artist/art director from video games. Vince was part of our in-house concept push for *Zendikar* and continued to paint cards for us, among them his striking cycle of extended-art basic lands.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c7/e4/c7e46b3e650640f92c377d2f8bd21ccdfb4c03e393fcfa9309c44f38148de803.jpg)*Zendikar [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20090928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island) by Vincent Proce*  
  

Look for these names in *Worldwake* and beyond:  
 Johann Bodin  
 Tomasz Jedruszek  
 Svetlin Velinov  
 Erica Yang

So long story short, your trusty **Magic** senior art director and illustrator of ill repute is always keeping his eye open for new talent. For that matter, so is Jon Schindehette (he's kinda Bizzaro-JJ for **D&D**). Everywhere from artdrop to conventions to our own forums, if you've got the chops, we'll find a project.

Have what it takes? Send a few small jpegs or a web link to your site to: artdrop@wizards.com

See you there,  
 JJ
