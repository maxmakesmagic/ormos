Remember [Braincat](/en/articles/arcana/2008/03-04-braincat)? It was a playtest card that looked like this:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/39/b6/39b6d34f575580b443f1e37a0f6a9457e077f84a85e85962e5a37686fc9db17c.jpg)  

That card ended up as [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20090507000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) (it actually says "Brain*cut*", but everyone agrees that it looks more like a cat than a cut).

Now, we return to Braincat in a new feature called...

![Why R&D Isn't Responsible for the Art](https://i.ormos.uk/bd/6e/bd6e416ebc019d5f1ae976590e1a18d1982f05069d4c6121804420a6bf3ac474.jpg)  

R&D member Mons Johnsons recently needed four [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20090507000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize)s for a playtest deck, and kept the colloquial name "Braincat". And he drew charming pictures for the four cards, which tell a fascinating story:

![Braincat 2](https://i.ormos.uk/3b/63/3b63825e81c4bc3bb49c2e4e11f6149efeab0d028b400fe5f427fc5fcbcb552b.jpg)  
![Braincat 3](https://i.ormos.uk/3e/b2/3eb2fbe3ddb95f3b1c9dc7ff0bd7ce53ace4a0d5a0526fa07270c21fdc95694a.jpg)
