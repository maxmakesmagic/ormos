*What's your favorite Standard deck from [Pro Tour Dark Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20160628235750/http://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/kibler-triumphs-epic-pro-tour)?*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e0/0c/e00c2900e4532fed44efdbc0223f486ee1518c0cf594bb289867319eeda0f043.jpg)

**Kelly Digges:** It's always fun to see what the pros brew up in a new Constructed format, especially in the innovation-friendly Standard format. The finals featured two Wolf Run Ramp decks that look pretty similar to last season's (with the hugely important addition of [Huntmaster of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20120214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Huntmaster+of+the+Fells)), but the rest of the field took a lot of fresh looks at the format: white-based weenie, various flavors of control, mono-green, mono-black, white-black token decks, and all kinds of other stuff.

My favorite decks from this Pro Tour are the [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20120214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) decks. Pod decks comes in a variety of flavors, with black-green and green-white-blue being the usual flavors. This tournament, though, saw the emergence of red-green-white Pod decks, exemplified by Lukas Blohon's Top 8 deck:

[Strangleroot Geist](https://web.archive.org/web/20120214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strangleroot+Geist), [Blade Splicer](https://web.archive.org/web/20120214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blade+Splicer), and [Huntmaster of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20120214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Huntmaster+of+the+Fells) make up the bottom of the Pod ladder, and they all have something in common: when you sacrifice them to [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20120214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod), they all leave something behind (the Geist comes back thanks to undying, and the other two leave a token).

Much of the rest of the creature base is a tricksy toolbox of powerful creatures, topping out at the mighty [Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite](https://web.archive.org/web/20120214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elesh+Norn%2C+Grand+Cenobite). Intricate interactions, silver-bullet answers, a token subtheme, and the chance to play my favorite Praetor in Standard? Sign me up!
