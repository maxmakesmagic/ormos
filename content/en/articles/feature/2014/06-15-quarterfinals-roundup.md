Even though the Grand Prix here in Moscow has been a smoothly organized tournament, you could tell that the players were starting to get exhausted after two long days of **Magic** Some had more at stakes than others, whereas Lee Shi Tian had already achieved his goal for the weekend. He locked up Platinum Pro Level by making Top 8 and that way already secured his flight ticket to Grand Prix **Magic** 2015. For everyone else, a slot there was on stake, as well as the infinite glory of taking home a Grand Prix title on home turf.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/55/55/5555b7e7d6309f61ad29b517e711caba270a0d9c94c4e3e958700c47e9d72554.jpg)

**Igor Gorbunov (Red/White Burn) vs Dimitriy Butakov (Bant Control)**

There was no doubt about which match had the most viewers when the games began. Players were tightly packed on the sideline to watch Siberian **Magic Online** pro Dimitriy Butakov and his Green White and Blue control deck with Planeswalkers from here and beyond. He might however have run into his one bad match-up in Igor Gorbunov and his Red/White Burn deck.

Game one was a quick affair, and not even an [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion), accelerated out with a Kiora, the [Crash](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crash)ing wave could stop Gorbunov's haste creatures and burn spells. A [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer) was handled with [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere), but [Chandra's Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Phoenix) along with a fistful of red instants gave Gorbunov Game One.

In the second game it was clear that Butakov wouldn't give in without a struggle when a turn five [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos) took over the battlefield. When Butakov had [Negate](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Negate) for Gorbunov's [Banishing Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banishing+Light), and could follow up with a [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) Gorbunov saved them some time and packed up his cards.  
 In game three Butakov had a turn three [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos). But Gorbunov wasted no time, exiling it with [Glare of Heresy](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glare+of+Heresy), getting an Elemental Token in the process from the [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer) he started off with.

Butakov had a [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) to clear the board, but had to damage to play a [Breeding Pool](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Breeding+Pool) untapped and was suddenly low in life and in a precarious position.  
 It didn't get any better when Eidolon of Great Revel came to Gorbunov's Aid, and two burn spells later, after Butakov partly tapped out for [Jace, Architect of Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Architect+of+Thought) and the game was over. Using [Negate](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Negate) on one of the burn spells only meant that Butakov took two damage rather than three, and his bad match-up did become his nemesis.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/55/55/5555b7e7d6309f61ad29b517e711caba270a0d9c94c4e3e958700c47e9d72554.jpg)

**Igor Gorbunov beats Dimitriy Butakov 2 - 1**

![](https://i.ormos.uk/82/b0/82b0570506bdd47ce2116a847a10e6e57a8a10376663d4ca98d6cd932543a3ba.jpg)

**Sergiy Sushalskyy (Jund Monsters) vs. Lee Shi Tian (Black Devotion, splashing Blue)**

In the classic battle of [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) against [Polukranos, World Eater](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Polukranos%2C+World+Eater) and his friend [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon) it was the Red/Green soul mates that ran away with the match. In the first game they came on turn three and four to race Lee's attempts of Ratlings. When the first [Polukranos, World Eater](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Polukranos%2C+World+Eater) met [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall) he was soon joined by his identical twin – [Polukranos, World Eater](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Polukranos%2C+World+Eater).

Game two was a swift affair, as Lee found himself with a [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) in play, but stuck on three lands, unable to cast the [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon) in his hand, and unable to interact with the giant Green monster, who once again hit the board early for Sushalskyy.

**Sergiy Sushalskyy defeats Lee Shi Tian 2-0**

![](https://i.ormos.uk/12/9a/129a89408351810f7350670e65c3306efd94d70671a9d7418808bc80c0180adb.jpg)

**Keraan Chetty (Black Devotion, splashing Red) vs. Alexey Rugov (Jund Monsters)**

The third quarterfinal saw South African Keraan Chetty, with the quite unusual splash of red for four [Dreadbore](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dreadbore). His opponent, Russian Alexey Rugov was with Polukranos and friends.  
  
 The match was a fine display of how Red, Green and Black deck is built to overcome Black Devotion. In game one a [Scavenging Ooze](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scavenging+Ooze) was the first play, which backed up by removal, all while making it bigger serves as a potent threat against the Black deck. It has to be dealt with, and if it is, [Polukranos, World Eater](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Polukranos%2C+World+Eater) doesn't need to come until he can be cast naturally on turn four and threaten lethal damage fast. Chetty did have a Nightveil Spectre going and kept knocking on his opponents deck for [Dreadbore](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dreadbore), but none was to be found.  
  
 Game two was Mono Black by the books. [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) your [Domri Rade](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Domri+Rade), [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) your [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix). Cast [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) on my Swamp. Draw two cards every turn and use my removal spells on all your creatures. Finally winning by casting either [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) or multiple [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel). Or like in this game – both!

The last game once again was about [Scavenging Ooze](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scavenging+Ooze) and removal. And the lack thereof from Keraan Chetty. He had [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie), but seeing no targets in Rugov's hand he could only display a fine example of good sportsmanship when falling to a [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon), that joined the Ooze for once, rather than [Polukranos, World Eater](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Polukranos%2C+World+Eater).  
  
 "Good games. Well done," the South African congratulated his opponent and shook his hand in defeat.  
  
**Alexey Rugov defeats Keraan Chetty 2-1**

![](https://i.ormos.uk/97/9a/979aa97a2e42ba64aa523d040671cdaa86710fe5f9d75ba94774189ff93316c7.jpg)

**Efim Kashapov (Four Color Midrange) vs Sergey Zheleznov (Mono Black Devotion)**

The last quarterfinal to finish was a grindy one. [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s took down the first two games, but for different players. Zheleznov didn't even activate his in the first game, but rather cast a second copy while stuck on two lands, one of them being a [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault). Extra style points however goes to Efim Kashapov for curving turn two [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s into turn four [Kiora, the Crashing Wave](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kiora%2C+the+Crashing+Wave).

The third game took some time. Even though Zheleznov had all the cards and threats he could hope for Kashapov was doing his best to live for as many turns as possible. The most interesting choice being Zheleznov's choice with a turn two [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize). When he saw [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion), [Blood Baron of Vizkopa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Baron+of+Vizkopa), [Erebos, God of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Erebos%2C+God+of+the+Dead), [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) and [Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price) he knew the game would take a while. He grinded through with [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress), [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie), [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie), [Erebos, God of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Erebos%2C+God+of+the+Dead), and after a pretty lopsided game that took surprisingly long Zheleznov secured a spot in the semi-finals.
