Get your Standard deck together and head down to your local game store this weekend for *Mirrodin Besieged* Game Day! The **Magic Online** metagame has been churning with the new card set for almost two weeks, so check out the slew of deck lists below for deck ideas. All Game Day participants receive a Treasure Mage promo card (while supplies last), and the Top 8 competitors get an alternate-art Black Sun's Zenith. But the big prizes may well be the preview cards from *Mirrodin Pure* (Pristine Talisman) and *New Phyrexia* (Suture Priest) given to the Top 2 finishes with faction-affiliated decks. For more information on Game Day, [go here](/en/events/2009/gameday-facts/world-wide-game-day).
