Welcome back to "On the Mirrodin Site," where we show off some of the recent highlights from the [Mirrodin Site](https://web.archive.org/web/20110424233051/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/).

This week, we'd like to draw your attention to the fact that *Mirrodin Besieged* is having its [Prerelease](/en/events/2008/prerelease-facts/worldwide-prerelease-tournaments) next week. How does that relate to the Mirrodin Site? Simple: The Mirrodin Site is previewing cards from *Mirrodin Besieged*!

Already, the Mirrodin Site has previewed Tezzeret, Agent of Bolas; Distant Memories; Scourge Servant; and Black Sun's Zenith. Other cards will be appearing today and next week. You don't want to to be left out of the loop, do you? Head to the [Mirrodin Site](https://web.archive.org/web/20110424233051/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/) for the latest cards!
