![](https://www.wizards.com/protour/images/qt_lg.jpg)Refine your Two-Headed Giant skills to earn a ticket to San Diego, California, by winning a Pro Tour Qualifier! 

You can test each decklist thanks to our [Sample Hand Generator](https://web.archive.org/web/20090602023708/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/294). Just click on the card fan in the upper right of each decklist and you can see what your opening hand could be, along with mulligans and the first three cards you'd draw.

Decklists will be updated weekly during the PTQ season. The season runs until May 20. To find a PTQ near you, [click here](/en/events/2007/sandiego07-qualifiers/pro-toursan-diego-07-qualifiers). Pro Tour-San Diego is scheduled for June 29-July 1, 2007, and the format is Two-Headed Giant Booster Draft.
