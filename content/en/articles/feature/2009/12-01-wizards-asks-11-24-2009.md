*You sit down to [Rotisserie Draft](https://web.archive.org/web/20091201000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/433) all the cards in **Magic**, and you're picking first. What do you take, and why?*

![Mike Turian](https://i.ormos.uk/4d/df/4ddf17870927d1fa29f7c99e3b74f59ad7b39456776c837fd321939967d75c7b.jpg)

**Mike Turian:** There is only one pick in this situation: [Black Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20091201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Lotus). Not only is [Black Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20091201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Lotus) the collector's dream card, it is an incredibly powerful card for playing Limited. There are few cards that offer either the color fixing or the mana explosion that [Black Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20091201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Lotus) provides.

![Black Lotus](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Black+Lotus)

  

I think the only cards that are close to [Black Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20091201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Lotus) in this über-Rotisserie draft are [Ancestral Recall](https://web.archive.org/web/20091201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Recall) or [Sol Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20091201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sol+Ring). The three cards Ancestral provides (plus it is another collector's gem) always is a huge bonus. [Sol Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20091201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sol+Ring) provides the same mana boost that [Black Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20091201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Lotus) does, but at the end of the day I'd love to walk away from any draft with a [Black Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20091201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Lotus).

You sit down to [Rotisserie Draft](https://web.archive.org/web/20091201000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/433) all the cards in **Magic**, and you're picking first.
