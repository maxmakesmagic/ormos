## Magic Online Leaderboard Points

Monthly Events and Playoff Events include Leaderboard Points as prizes. These are not physical objects tracked in your collection but are instead tracked on this page.

You can follow the Leaderboard for the 2018 *Magic Online* Championship Series on this page. It will be updated on the Wednesday following each Monthly or Playoff Event. You can learn more about the 2018 MOCS [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20190305130724/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2018-magic-online-championship-series).

Updated: January 2, 2019

*Players in bold are qualified for the 2018 Magic Online Championship.*
