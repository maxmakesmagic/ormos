What effect will today's release of *Gatecrash* and [the recent update to the banned list](/en/articles/feature/2013/01-28-january-28-2013-dci-banned-restricted-list-announcement) have on Modern in the coming months? It's hard to tell, but if you plan on attending a PTQ this season you'll want to stay abreast of the metagame's developments. You can start here with Top 8 decklists from recent (pre-*Gatecrash*) PTQs. And find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#dgm)! Good luck; have fun!
