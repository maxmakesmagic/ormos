Hall of Fame eligible Guillaume Wafo-Tapa made the Top 8 of the France National Championship with a new take on White-Blue Control starring Sun Titan. The deck put *three* total copies into the Top 8 and even won the tournament! For more on France's National Championship, read the [official coverage](/fr/events/2010/frnat10/parez-prevails-at-france-nationals) (much of which is in French).
