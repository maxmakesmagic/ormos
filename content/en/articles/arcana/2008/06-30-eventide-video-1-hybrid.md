Welcome to the beginning of official *Eventide* previews! Over the next two weeks we’ll be previewing **Magic**’s newest set in articles, more cards on the daily front page previews, and even *more* cards in the *Eventide* minisite’s [Visual Spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20100918072347/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Article.aspx?x=magic/eventide/spoiler). 

Today we’ve also got the first of three videos to get you all set for your upcoming *Eventide* battles at the July 12 and 13 Prerelease events.
