Did you know that the Merfolk of the Pearl Trident was the only merfolk in **Magic** for over a year? The second merfolk, Merfolk Assassin, did not show up until [*The Dark*](https://web.archive.org/web/20040209000000/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/daily/mr110-dark). (Lord of Atlantis is a lord and not a merfolk.)
