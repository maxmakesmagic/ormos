>  Whenever CARDNAME attacks, three target creatures can’t block this turn.

**#1) What is the most appropriate color for the above card?**

a) white  
 b) blue  
 c) black  
 d) red  
 e) green

**#2) What is the most appropriate rarity for the above card?**

a) common  
 b) uncommon  
 c) rare

**#3) What change would R&D most likely make to this card?**

a) Change “Whenever CARDNAME attacks” to “Whenever CARDNAME becomes tapped”.  
 b) Add “up to” before “three target creatures”.  
 c) Change “three target creatures” to “three target creatures another player controls”.

 e) Remove “this turn”.
