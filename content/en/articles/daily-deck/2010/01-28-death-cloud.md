Earlier this week we brought you news of a resurgence in the Elves strategy for Extended from ages past. Adding to the list of refurbished hits shaking the dust off and making the final tables of [Pro Tour Qualifiers](/en/events/2009/sanjuan10-qualifiers/pro-toursan-juan-2010-qualifiers) is Death Cloud. Coomaster took this version to the Top 8 of a [Magic Online](https://web.archive.org/web/20100328053643/http://www.wizards.com/magic/digital/magiconline.aspx?) PTQ, where another copy of the deck also managed to make the single-elimination rounds.
