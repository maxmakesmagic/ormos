In **Magic** "shorthand," letters often refer to mana symbols. For example, if a person says "R," he often means ![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif). Similarly, "XWB" really means ![X Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/b1/6bb1152b0a3305d0a7c2e2eb725b8ebd18620607efc62f6f38b0642b97c8501f.gif)![White Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/55/78/55785924f8cda9ab2a85364f807ba172c7530e43d94b14aad222ec4bbf2589ca.gif)![Black Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/6b/e16bcf75cffdd1079b6ba1f9f43c9e6e7253bb89521acdf6b3e93d3c2e63dbb5.gif).

I guess someone forgot to tell that to the people that typeset *Alpha*. Look at the two cards below – Phantasmal Forces and Force of Nature. See anything weird in the text boxes?

I know Forests tap for ![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif), but what taps for "G"? As with most errors in *Alpha*, these were fixed in *Beta*.

![](http://www.wizards.com/global/images/mtgcom_arcana_22_pic2_en.jpg)

Oh, and artist Douglas Shuler's name is misspelled on the Force.
