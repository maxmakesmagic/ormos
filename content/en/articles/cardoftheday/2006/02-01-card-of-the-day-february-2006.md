This card created quite a debate during the design and development of <i>Guildpact</i>. How many cards that could be used with these abilities would a set need to justify a card like Izzet Guildmage? (<a>Djinn Illuminatus</a> had a similar debate.) In the end, it was decided that while most cards are designed with their specific set in mind, it's also important to keep the larger picture available as context for some cards. With over 8000 cards to play with, sometimes cards should be printed that, though they may not interact as much with their own set, can create fun and new interactions with the cards in their block, larger formats, or even all cards ever created. </td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

One of the interesting things about the way the color wheel works out is that for each guild made of enemy colors, there is one guild that is their opposite. How do you find the opposing guild? Using the Izzet as an example, list all the enemies of the colors in the guild. Since Red and Blue are enemies of each other, that leaves White (enemy of red) and Green (enemy of blue). So, the guild diametrically opposed to the Izzet is Selesnya. Now take a closer look at <a href="https://i.ormos.uk/ec/e9/ece9a68d21eb9a8bed97eec6aad372878ca705d58932449daa33c722c2fa44fd.jpg">the art</a> from <a>Electrolyze</a>. See that guy having the bad day?
