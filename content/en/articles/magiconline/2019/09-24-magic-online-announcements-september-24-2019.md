## *Throne of Eldraine*: *Magic Online* Edition

Join Knights and Nobles to forge your path toward victory with the release of *Throne of Eldraine* on *Magic Online* this Thursday, September 26! Constructed events start following the downtime on Wednesday, September 25, and Limited events start Thursday at 10 a.m. PT. Make sure to also mark down the following dates for redemption:

*   Redemption begins October 23.
*   The *Throne of Eldraine* redemption guarantee period ends February 5, 2020.
*   *Throne of Eldraine* redemption will end on March 11, 2020.

For more information, check out Alli's release article [here](/en/articles/mtgo/2019/09-18-throne-of-eldraine-magic-online-edition).

## Modern Cube Ends Thursday

The Modern Cube is a great way to try out all the iconic cards and synergies that shape one of *Magic*'s most dynamic formats. Modern Cube can be found in the Limited Leagues section of the Play Lobby and will run through September 26, with the start of *Throne of Eldraine* events. Here are some words from Reggie Valk of *Magic* R&D about this year's Modern Cube:

*"The guiding principle behind this last batch of changes was to introduce relevant cards from the sets that came out since \[Modern Cube\] was last updated (November 2018), which ended up including RNA, WAR, MH1, and M20. Aside from including a lot of the more obvious inclusions due to iconic status and Modern playability (like Urza, Wrenn and Six, etc.), I also tried to update the Cube to have some cards and strategies that align with popular Modern strategies currently, which is why things like Arclight Phoenix and Duskwatch Recruiter were introduced. I also was cognizant of the amount of planeswalkers in this cube as that has been its largest criticism by far, so despite WAR adding a lot of cool new PWs, the number stayed the same and, if anything, should only go down from here."*

For event details, and to see the complete card list and the latest changes to it, click [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2020/09-14-modern-cube-event-and-cardlist#Cubelist). Happy Cubing!

## Build Notes

Client | The Login and Home scenes have been entirely rewritten. Further iteration is expected.

Account | *Magic Online* accounts are now available without paying a fee. Please visit [here](/en/articles/mtgo/2019/09-18-throne-of-eldraine-magic-online-edition) for more information.

C19 | Bugs with [Elsha of the Infinite](https://web.archive.org/web/20190924000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elsha+of+the+Infinite) have been resolved, and she has been released. You can find her on the Curated sheet in Treasure Chests.

Duel Scene | [Nissa, Sage Animist](https://web.archive.org/web/20190924000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%2C+Sage+Animist)'s third ability will now correctly animate lands on resolution.

Duel Scene | Players will now automatically concede matches after eight minutes of inactivity, up from five.
