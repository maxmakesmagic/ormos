Destructive Force turned a lot of heads when it was released in ***Magic** 2011*, and Tim Berg used it to great effect to make the Top 8 of a recent Pro Tour Qualifier for Amsterdam. The event, held by Professional Event Services in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, was one of the first to feature the brand-new Standard format. Tim's list plays a bit like the Ponza decks from years gone by, using Destructive Force as a massive sweeper while fatties, Walls, and planeswalkers hold the ground.
