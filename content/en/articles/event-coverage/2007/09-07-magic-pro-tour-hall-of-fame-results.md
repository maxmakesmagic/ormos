Listed below are the results from the Selection Committee and Players Committee for the 2007 Hall of Fame class. Voters were asked to vote for up to five candidates, and the top five earned spots in the Hall.

The results were weighted using the following formula:  
 (SC%\*.667) + (PC%\*.333)

There were 92 ballots cast by Selection Committee members and 82 ballots cast by Players Committee members. Individual ballots will not be made public.
