KwaaartzZ utilized the interaction between suspend spells from *Time Spiral* Block and the new cascade spells from *Alara Reborn* to put together a Premier Event Top 8–worthy Extended list on **Magic Online**. The combo works like so: by playing a three mana cascade card like [Violent Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20090714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Violent+Outburst) or [Ardent Plea](https://web.archive.org/web/20090714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ardent+Plea), KwaaartzZ can only reveal [Hypergenesis](https://web.archive.org/web/20090714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hypergenesis), the only card in his deck with a converted mana cost of less than three! From there, he and his opponent take turns putting gigantic monsters in play. Unfortunately for said opponents, KwaaartzZ came prepared, dropping [Akroma, Angel of Wrath](https://web.archive.org/web/20090714000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Akroma%252C%2BAngel%2Bof%2BWrath)s, [Magister Sphinx](https://web.archive.org/web/20090714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magister+Sphinx)es, and [Bogardan Hellkite](https://web.archive.org/web/20090714000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bogardan+Hellkite)s onto the board!
