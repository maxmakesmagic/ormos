Last night, after the Top 8 had been decided, the giant play mat in the middle of the Pro Tour–Yokohama tournament venue played host to a different kind of big event. The Colossal Game, an exhibition match using three-foot-tall cards, appeared in Japan for the first time. On one side of the gigantic Red Zone was ubiquitous Pro Tour veteran and commentator (and Wizards of the Coast VP of Digital Games) Randy Buehler; on the other, reigning World Champion Makihito Mihara of Japan. Buehler was assisted by New Zealand's Carter Digby, while Mihara paired with a Japanese player whose name the coverage staff didn't catch.

The decks were designed by R&D member Alan Comer, with input from Japan Organized Play Manager Ron Foster, to be *Planar Chaos*-style timeshifts of one another. One was red-black, the other white-blue, with each card—some in Japanese, some in English—chosen to have a counterpart in the other deck.
