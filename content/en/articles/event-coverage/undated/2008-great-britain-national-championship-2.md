Jonathan Randle, Too Hot To Handle!

When I asked Jon Randle if he had any great suggestions for captions for pictures of him in the coverage, only the title above came to mind. As it turned out it was quite prophetic. His Faeries deck was one of only a handful in the tournament, but it proved to be the equal of everything that it faced all weekend. He bested a pair of Reveillark decks along with Mono-Red to become the champion - a fair reflection of the most played decks in the tournament.

Joining him on the Great Britain National Team are Stephen Murray of Scotland, who has previously been a champion himself, and Ioannis Kyriazis, who has been close to top 8 of nationals for years, and this time filled an ambition that had been some time in coming to fruition.

Now these three will be frantically testing for Worlds in Memphis, Tenessee, where they compete for their nation, looking to go even further, becoming the best in the world.

## Quarterfinals: Just Deserts - Jonathan Randle vs Michail Dimakos

*by Tim Willoughby*

While there were barely a handful of Faeries decks in the entire tournament, there is still one in the top 8 of Great Britain’s National Championship, and being piloted by no less than Jonathan Randle, top 8 competitor from GP Birmingham. His opponent, Michail Dimakos with [Reveillark](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reveillark), on the face of things had a pretty bad matchup in the top eight, but some variations to his list meant that there was definitely game there.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/79/aa/79aafe8630b63e0117165ae4e33c12c48785ed841299f51e8dd9cc3745965bdf.jpg)

Jonathan won the roll, and playing first, took a mulligan. He had a turn 1 [Ancestral Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Vision)s, and a turn two [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom) though, so this didn’t seem to be too great a concern for the placid Englishman. A [Desert](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desert) from Michail was a great answer to that [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom) though. This was one of the cards that Jonathan had highlighted as making the matchup not as favourable as the average Faeries/[Reveillark](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reveillark) match might seem.

Jonathan attacked in with his tokens, only to lose one a turn to [Desert](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desert). He played a second [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom), hoping to successfully swarm. This plan was stymied slightly as well, as Michail had an [Aven Riftwatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Riftwatcher) – a great creature in a race.

[Ancestral Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Vision)s ticked down, giving Jonathan a whole host of options. He eventually went with a [Scion of Oona](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scion+of+Oona), but was hesitant to attack. Michail was not about to let the first game be easy for Jonathan, with a second [Aven Riftwatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Riftwatcher). At the end of turn, [Slaughter Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slaughter+Pact) killed this fresher Riftwatcher off. In his upkeep, Jonathan paid for the pact, and got two more Faeries, going to 14. His attacks took Michail to 21.

Dimakos thought for a while, and played a [Body Double](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Body+Double) targeting [Aven Riftwatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Riftwatcher). This too resolved, further increasing the mountain that Randle’s Faerie’s had to climb. Michail had a [Riftwing Cloudskate](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Riftwing+Cloudskate) to bounce [Scion of Oona](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scion+of+Oona), which was soon replayed by Randle. On just 10 life to his opponent’s 21 though, he would have to get beating.

[Nameless Inversion](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nameless+Inversion) got [Body Double](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Body+Double) out of the way, and with swings Jonathan finally got Michail sub 20 life, on 15. An evoked [Reveillark](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reveillark) made things very tricky though, as while Randle had a Remove Soul, Michail had a Rune Snag, which was good enough to stop the counter. [Body Double](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Body+Double) came back as [Reveillark](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reveillark), and [Aven Riftwatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Riftwatcher) again joined the team. This would be close.

Jonathan attacked in, and when Michail blocked three, Jonathan had a second [Scion of Oona](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scion+of+Oona). Much trading went on, and [Body Double](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Body+Double) (as [Reveillark](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reveillark)) along with [Aven Riftwatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Riftwatcher), came back. Michail was now on 12 to his opponent’s 6. However, those tokens that had been adding up coulld now reasonably swarm around, ans as 3/3s, they were too much for Michail to handle.

Jonathan Randle 1 – 0 Michail Dimakos

The tone was serious in this quarter-final match, and each player was businesslike in their sideboarding and shuffling. For Game 2, Michail was on the play, but just had to look on as a turn one [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) from Jonathan saw [Desert](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desert), [Riftwing Cloudskate](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Riftwing+Cloudskate), [Wispmare](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wispmare), [Reveillark](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reveillark), Wrath of God and [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command). The [Wispmare](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wispmare) got taken away, leaving the Cloudskate to be suspended on turn two, and [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom) to be a safe play from Randle. That turn two [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom) was followed by an [Ancestral Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Vision)s suspend from Jonathan, whose draws again seemed powerful in what was already a matchup where he had to feel quite confident. When [Riftwing Cloudskate](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Riftwing+Cloudskate) ticked down, it took out [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom), and before Randle replayed it, he did a little probing with [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize). He saw that Wrath of God again, [Reveillark](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reveillark) and [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command), along with what was the *third* [Desert](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desert) in Michail’s hand. He took [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command), and thought better of playing his [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom) – instead going for a [Mistbind Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistbind+Clique) in Michail’s upkeep.

The following turn [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom) came down again, but when the *fourth* [Desert](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desert) came down from Michail, Randle sighed. Now none of his team was safe from those lands, not even his 4/4!

Randle dropped to 12 of [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom) in his upkeep. He attacked with [Mistbind Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistbind+Clique) and two Faeries. Michail thought for a little. [Ancestral Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Vision)s had just ticked down for Randle, so there was every chance that a [Scion of Oona](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scion+of+Oona) would be coming down. He blocked [Mistbind Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistbind+Clique), and shot the Clique twice, and the two tokens. The Clique got bounced by a [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command) though, meaning there would be another effective Time Walk coming from Randle.

At the end of turn, Michail played a [Venser, Shaper Savant](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Venser%2C+Shaper+Savant), which targeted [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom), but was thwarted by [Scion of Oona](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scion+of+Oona)‘s shroud provision. In upkeep, [Mistbind Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistbind+Clique) again tried to tap out Michail, but a [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command) countered it and drew Dimakos a card.

The life totals at this point were 10 to Jonathan, while Michail was on 14. All those [Desert](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desert)s were now a little redundant, as [Scion of Oona](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scion+of+Oona) was never going to be attacking. Another [Mistbind Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistbind+Clique) came from Randle when an [Ancestral Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Vision)s tried to resolve for Michail. This allowed Rune Snag to stop the card drawing spell.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/01/d6/01d66b4ebd77d34aedc0cb437753080084ce153dc62bb761e3181027b926dbde.jpg)

Randle’s attacks also took Michail to 4. He was in a race that seemed very tough for him to get back into. When Wrath of God was countered by [Spellstutter Sprite](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellstutter+Sprite), Michail scooped them up.

Jonathan Randle 2 – 0 Michail Dimakos

Randle’s match had so far gone more or less to plan. He continued in similar vein with a turn one [Ancestral Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Vision)s for the third game. Michail successfully evoked [Mulldrifter](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mulldrifter) on turn three, but Randle had to be happy to see nothing but [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island)s from his opponent. Those [Desert](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desert)s had proven troublesome.

A [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) revealed 2 [Momentary Blink](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Momentary+Blink), [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command), Rune Snag, [Reveillark](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reveillark), [Aven Riftwatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Riftwatcher) and Wrath of God. ## Semi-Finals: Jonathan Randle vs Ioannis Kyriazis

*by David Sutcliffe*

Of all the red decks that kicked off the 2008 British nationals two days ago, on a sunny Friday morning, only one remained - that of Ioannis Kyriazis. The red decks have been fighting uphill for almost the entire weekend but as Kyriazis proved by overcoming yet another unfavourable matchup against Richard Moore's Seismic Swans deck in his quarter final, "The Force is strong in this one". Across the table, the accepted wisdom had it that Jonathan Randle's Faeries would need to harness some of that mystical energy field if they were to stop the red deck's remarkable march into the history books.

Could the evil Faerie Empire withstand the attacks from Red Squadron?

"Get set up for your attack run"

It didn't start well for Randle, losing the dice roll then being forced to mulligan down to 5 cards, his only consolation being that Kyriazis would be starting from 6. The red deck rolled out of the gates with a Magus Of The Scroll and a [Blood Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Knight), while Randle managed a second turn [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom). Kyriazis hadn't found a third mountain unfortunately, which slowed his offense - The Force didn't appear to be with either man. Randle [Slaughter Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slaughter+Pact)ed the [Blood Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Knight) as it was a threat his Faerie tokens would struggle to deal with, but paying that upkeep cost left him open and Kyriazis took advantage to play a game breaking Magus Of The Moon. His attempt to follow up with a [Flame Javelin](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flame+Javelin) was countered by a [Spellstutter Sprite](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellstutter+Sprite) from Randle's lone [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island), but it also left Kyriazis with the one card in hand that would make his Magus Of The Scroll a threat for the Faerie player's lifetotal despite the [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom) tokens.

Randle frantically fought back, swinging as many of his Faerie tokens as he dared at his opponent in an attempt to race, but as his lifetotal whittled away to the unstoppable Magus and [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom) damage he resigned himself to defeat - his only hope was that with two cards in hand Kyriazis might miss the Demigod Of Revenge he had been naming for Magus Of The Scroll but Kyriazis sheepishly revealed that he had drawn a second Demigod, and Randle scooped.

Jonathan Randle 0 - 1 Ioannis Kyriazis

Game 2 started much better for Jonathan Randle, and he played his dream opening of [Ancestral Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Vision)s and [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom), while Kyriazis fought back with a [Blood Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Knight) and [Ashenmoor Gouger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashenmoor+Gouger). Randle suspended a second [Ancestral Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Vision)s - if he could hang in for just a few turns he would be looking good, and when he was able to drop a [Mistbind Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistbind+Clique) in Ioannis' attack step to destroy the [Blood Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Knight) without any response it seemed he had taken a big step towards that goal. Sucking up cards from his first Visions Randle had a [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command) ready for a Demigod Of Revenge, sending the Gouger back to hand as well. Kyriazis immediately replayed his elemental beatstick, but a copy was [Rune Snag](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rune+Snag)ged and Randle gladly scooped up another handful of cards from [Ancestral Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Vision)s.

Randle still couldn't attack really, but had slightly better blockers now. He had to chump block again the following turn, thanks to [Mirror Entity](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirror+Entity). Even when left with virtually nothing, Randle didn't seem to flinch, calmly playing an [Ancestral Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Vision)s as if not in trouble at all.

Murray looked rattled by this calm demeanor, but as it turned out, there was no reason to worry. Attacks the following turn made the game 1 - 1, evoking a sigh of relief from the Scotsman.

Murray tried for a [Careful Consideration](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Careful+Consideration), only for [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command) to stop it and bounce a card back to his hand. A second [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) from Randle saw [Reveillark](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reveillark), [Prismatic Lens](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prismatic+Lens) and [Pact of Negation](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721042326/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pact+of+Negation).
