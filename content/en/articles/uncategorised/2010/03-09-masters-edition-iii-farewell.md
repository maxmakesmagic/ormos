As previously announced, **Magic Online** is saying goodbye to *Masters Edition III*. *Masters Edition III* product goes off sale March 22 at 9:00 AM PST / 17:00 UTC. 

*Masters Edition III* farewell events begin after the downtime Wednesday, March 17 through March 23 and will consist of two parts: 

*   Nix Tix *Masters Edition III* Daily Events
*   Nix Tix *Masters Edition III* Premier Events

  
 In addition, some events will award a textless alternate art Psionic Blast promo card.

***MASTERS EDITION III* DAILY EVENT**

*START TIME:* Scheduled start times beginning after the March 17 downtime.  
*LOCATION:* Daily Event room.  
*ENTRY FEE:* Product only.  
*PRODUCT:* 6 *Masters Edition III* booster packs.  
*SIZE:* Minimum 16 players, maximum 128 players.  
*DURATION:* 4 Rounds, Swiss Pairings.  
*PRIZES:* Prizes are based on the total match points at the end of the tournament. Each match win awards 3 points and each match loss awards 0 points.

*ADDITIONAL PRIZES:* Each Classic Daily Event player with at least 9 points will also receive a textless full-art Psionic Blast promo card. 

***MASTERS EDITION III* "TOP 8" PREMIER EVENT**

*START TIME:* Scheduled start times beginning after the March 17 downtime.  
*LOCATION:* Premier Event room.  
*ENTRY FEE:* Product only.  
*PRODUCT:* 6 *Masters Edition III* booster packs.  
*SIZE:* Minimum 33 players, maximum 256 players.  
*DURATION:* Number of [Swiss Pairing rounds determined by attendance](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=465) plus 3-round single elimination "Top 8" bracket.
