After many rounds of Swiss and some great games of *Magic*, the Top 8 for Grand Prix Atlanta is set! Learn more about this event’s Top 8 competitors, their history at the top tables, and more by taking a look through the Top 8 profiles.

* * *

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpatl15/t8_wiznitzer.jpg)Name:** Aryeh Wiznitzer

**Age:** 32

**Occupation:** Teacher

**Hometown:** Houston, Texas

**Previous Magic accomplishments:** Top 8 Grand Prix Dallas 2015; some other money finishes.

**What color combination was your sealed deck, and what was your record?**  
 Red-white removal. It had [Planar Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20151116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Planar+Outburst) and other good ones. I went 9-0.

**What was your best card, and why?**  
[Quarantine Field](https://web.archive.org/web/20151116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quarantine+Field). in Sealed it’s always a 2-for-1 or a 3-for-1.

**What color combination was your first draft deck, and what was your record?**  
 Black-Red Devoid, going 2-1.

**What was your best card, and why?**  
[Touch of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20151116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Touch+of+the+Void). It always overperforms. Sometimes it goes to the dome!

**What color combination was your second draft deck, and what was your record?**  
 Red-white midrange, going 2-0-1.

**What was your best card, and why?**  
[Turn Against](https://web.archive.org/web/20151116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Turn+Against). It found its way into my hand at opportune moments.
