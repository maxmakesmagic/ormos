Looking at the decks drafted by the six players who went undefeated in the draft portions, we see that the trend was to be aggressive. Six of the twelve undefeated decks were blue based. These decks each had their fair share of flying and unblockable creatures like [Spined Thopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spined+Thopter), [Trespassing Souleater](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trespassing+Souleater), and Gust Skimmer. Conveniently, these creatures being artifacts means that they fit perfectly in metalcraft builds as well as the traditional evasion decks.

Infect proved to still have what it takes to win a draft pod. All three of the undefeated Infect decks were green based. Not only did green provide quality infect creatures like [Cystbearer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cystbearer) and [Rot Wolf](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rot+Wolf), but each of these decks took advantage of the pump spells green had to offer. [Untamed Might](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Untamed+Might) and [Mutagenic Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutagenic+Growth) lead the charge, but the occasional [Mirran Mettle](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirran+Mettle) made the cut.

As with most trends, there are exceptions. Two undefeated decks managed to take a slower more controlling route to victory. Red-White removal had been a powerful archetype in the earlier draft formats. [Shatter](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shatter) and [Arrest](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrest) still prove to be valuable spells that can efficiently answer your opponent's threats. Dinosaurs like [Thundering Tanadon](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thundering+Tanadon) and [Alpha Tyrranax](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Alpha+Tyrranax) often saw play in many archetypes, but one Green-White deck managed to get to 3-0 by combining the two.
