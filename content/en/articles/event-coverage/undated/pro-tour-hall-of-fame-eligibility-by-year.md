## ELIGIBILITY BY YEAR

Listed below are players who meet the eligibility requirements for the *Magic* Pro Tour Hall of Fame and the year they will first appear on the ballot. Please note that the eligibility rules changes for the 2017 ballot removed names that had appeared here previously but now don’t meet the “two final days” eligibility requirement.
