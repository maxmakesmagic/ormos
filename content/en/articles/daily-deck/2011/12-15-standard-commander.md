TCGplayer.com Commander columnist Cassidy Silver tried out something new in [his most recent article](http://magic.tcgplayer.com/db/article.asp?ID=10184): a Standard-legal Commander deck! This budget-friendly alternative is something fun to try in your playgroup and helps open up the format to newer players. Take a look at Cassidy's first take on the format and try it for yourself!

Cassidy Silver's Standard Glissa
