It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6)! And since it's the first Friday in November, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

![Go For the Throat](https://i.ormos.uk/80/c0/80c0848ac4c4ddf5c15016d0e5eee5e202aae3721a4c1120d94b4b9914b9999c.jpg "Go For the Throat") 

  

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards often feature original flavor text and updated wordings.
