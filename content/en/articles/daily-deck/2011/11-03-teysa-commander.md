Ben McDole has a new column on GatheringMagic.com all about modifying Commander decks on a budget. What is his second entry into the series? A deck built around Teysa, Orzhov Scion! You can check out [his article](http://www.gatheringmagic.com/commander-chop-shop-2-%e2%80%93-teysa-the-silent-killer/) for more about the deck, and you can find the decklist below.
