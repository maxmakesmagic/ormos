Vintage is that unique format that allows players to play with nearly every card that's ever been fit to print in the Multiverse. A control deck based on [Tezzeret the Seeker](https://web.archive.org/web/20090811000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tezzeret+the+Seeker) that powered him all the way to the finals.
