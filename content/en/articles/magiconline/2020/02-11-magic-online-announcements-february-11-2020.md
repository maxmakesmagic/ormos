## CHAOS DRAFT! February 5–19

Flex your deck-building skills during February's CHAOS DRAFT!, a format that requires players to contemplate meta-archetypes over the curated archetypes typically found in a cube. CHAOS DRAFT! runs through February 19—plenty of time to smash face amidst the [Pandemonium](https://web.archive.org/web/20200211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pandemonium).

Sets that can be opened in this CHAOS DRAFT! include:

*Visions*, *Weatherlight*, *Stronghold*, *Exodus*, *Urza's Legacy*, *Urza's Destiny*, *Planeshift*, *Apocalypse*, *Torment*, *Judgment*, *Legions*, *Scourge*, *Darksteel*, *Fifth Dawn*, *Betrayers of Kamigawa*, *Saviors of Kamigawa*, *Guildpact*, *Dissension*, *Coldsnap*, *Planar Chaos*, *Future Sight*, *Morningtide*, *Eventide*, *Conflux*, *Alara Reborn*, *Worldwake*, *Mirrodin Besieged*, *New Phyrexia*, *Dark Ascension*, *Dragon's Maze*, *Born of the Gods*, *Journey Into Nyx*, *Fate Reforged*, *Oath of the Gatewatch*, *Eldritch* *Moon*, *Aether Revolt*, *Hour of Devastation*, and *Rivals of Ixalan*

## Pioneer PTQ Saturday

On Saturday, February 15, there will be a Pioneer PTQ at 9 a.m. PT! The entry fee is 1 Red PTQ Finals Token or 40 [MOCS Qualifier Points](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020). The winner qualifies for their choice of events in Series 2 of the [2020 Players Tour](https://magic.gg/players-tour) in May.

Learn more about Players Tour Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020#ptq).

## Build Notes

*   Players can no longer incorrectly play additional lands if their [Dryad of the Ilysian Grove](https://web.archive.org/web/20200211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dryad+of+the+Ilysian+Grove) loses all abilities.
*   A display bug was fixed that caused [Shadowspear](https://web.archive.org/web/20200211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shadowspear)’s ability to not appear to remove color-specific hexproof abilities even after removing them.
*   [Veil of Summer](https://web.archive.org/web/20200211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Veil+of+Summer) no longer provides hexproof from blue or black to activated abilities.
*   [Primitive Justice](https://web.archive.org/web/20200211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primitive+Justice) no longer causes a game reset.
*   Cards chosen with [Niv-Mizzet Reborn](https://web.archive.org/web/20200211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Niv-Mizzet+Reborn)'s ability now appear in the game log.
