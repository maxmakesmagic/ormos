## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b2/ce/b2ce87aa262cd7ca59d510124dbca17e12f635fa1160bd55baea216b9ba29e17.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide, two card boxes, six plastic dividers, six 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, one random Pro Tour player card, a special-edition life counter, and the second book in the *Lorwyn* cycle.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/83/64/83646e849eea1d4415ea489f4daeeb4c19a4e6f9b100c448f1b21b6cddbd9e74.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/36/3b/363bc62abbc18bd0f69bd145ca1362cff64fc7da31c54b3546225bc04913b366.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719050314/http://magic.wizards.com/node/961796)

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f3/d9/f3d9b93c72ed5ea9982801b63ad8935f4c0cc4daac79b606fd329b1b98de9463.png)

This second set in the *Lorwyn-Shadowmoor* block features 150 cards with randomly inserted premium cards. *Morningtide* expands on the themes introduced in Lorwyn and unveils three new mechanics: Kinship, Reinforce, and Prowl.

**Set Name:** *Morningtide*  
**Block:** Set 2 of 4 of the *Lorwyn-Shadowmoor* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 150

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719050314/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/MTGMOR%20FAQ%2001152008.doc)

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Paul Sottosanti (lead), Aaron Forsythe, Erik Lauer, and Mark Rosewater

**Final Game Design and Development:** Mike Turian (lead), Erik Lauer, Bill Rose, Henry Stern, Noah Weil, Alexis Janson, Kenneth Nagle, Mark Rosewater, and Jake Theis, with contributions from Mark L. Gottlieb
