You've seen *Dominaria* previews. You've seen some amazing art, astounding Sagas, and awe-inspiring lore dripping from the set.

And today, we're going to highlight all the playmats you'll see featuring *Dominaria* for the next couple of weeks at Grand Prix and the Pro Tour!

But first . . .

# Dominaria GP T-Shirt

As with all new set releases, the Grand Prix prize walls will feature a new T-shirt themed to *Dominaria*. This one will feature some sweet new art on a heather gray shirt. Here's a little sample of what that design is!

![](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/kBRKjOdEbZ.png)

![](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/st7yabWDWe.png)

Neat, huh? If you're looking to pick up a shirt, hit up one of the Grand Prix listed on that design and redeem tickets for the shirt on the prize wall!

# Bologna and Columbus

The first Grand Prix weekend to feature *Dominaria* doesn't showcase one playmat, but three! Grand Prix Bologna and Columbus are Team Limited events during *Dominaria* Release Weekend (April 27–29), and as such, both feature three different playmats teams can acquire. The playmats feature [Slimefoot, the Stowaway](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slimefoot%2C+the+Stowaway) (art by [Alex Konstad](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Alex%20Konstad%22%5d)); [Tiana, Ship's Caretaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tiana%2C+Ship%27s+Caretaker) (art by [Eric Deschamps](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Eric%20Deschamps%22%5d)); and [Weatherlight](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Weatherlight) (art by [Jaime Jones](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Jaime%20Jones%22%5d)).

# Dallas–Fort Worth and Beijing

Following the Team Limited weekend are individual Limited GPs in Beijing and Dallas–Fort Worth. The legendary playmat theme continues into the weekend of May 4–6 with [Muldrotha, the Gravetide](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Muldrotha%2C+the+Gravetide) as the flagship creature on this weekend's swag (art by [Jason Rainville](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Jason%20Rainville%22%5d)).

# Birmingham Double

The weekend of May 10–13 is the second of five double-main-event Grand Prix weekends. Birmingham's Legacy main event features the beautiful callback to the power of 1993 with [Mox Amber](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Amber) (art by [Steven Belledin](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Steven%20Belledin%22%5d)), while the Standard main event showcases [Blackblade Reforged](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blackblade+Reforged) (art by [Chris Rahn](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Chris%20Rahn%22%5d)).

![](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/OP20180419_Birmingham01.png)

![](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/OP20180419_Birmingham02.png)

Playmats aside, this is sure to be an exciting weekend, with three days of video coverage and a *lot* to do in Birmingham for four days!

# Toronto

The weekend of May 18–20 is Grand Prix Toronto, a Team Trios Constructed event featuring three playmats that showcase Esper colors in [Danitha Capashen, Paragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Danitha+Capashen%2C+Paragon) (art by [Chris Rallis](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Chris%20Rallis%22%5d)), [Tetsuko Umezawa, Fugitive](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tetsuko+Umezawa%2C+Fugitive) (art by [Randy Vargas](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Randy%20Vargas%22%5d)), and [Josu Vess, Lich Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Josu+Vess%2C+Lich+Knight) (art by [Tyler Jacobson](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Tyler%20Jacobson%22%5d)).

# Washington, DC

The Grand Prix in Washington, DC, the weekend of May 25–27 will be the last team Grand Prix this year that qualifies players for Pro Tour 25th Anniversary in August (which, if you haven't seen, is [kind of a big deal](/en/articles/feature/2017/09-26-the-1-million-weekend-pro-club-updates-and-more)). This Team Limited main event will, in teams fashion, feature three distinct playmats. The trio of mats teams will find at DC feature [Niambi, Faithful Healer](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Niambi%2C+Faithful+Healer) (art by [Greg Opalinski](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Greg%20Opalinski%22%5d)), [Arvad the Cursed](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arvad+the+Cursed) (art by [Lius Lasahido](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Lius%20Lasahido%22%5d)), and [Shalai, Voice of Plenty](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shalai%2C+Voice+of+Plenty) (art by [Victor Adame Minguez](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Victor%20Adame%20Minguez%22%5d)).

# Pro Tour Dominaria

While the GPs take a week off for the Pro Tour, folks traveling to Richmond, Virginia, to compete or check out the Pro Tour will be able to pick up this event-exclusive playmat while supplies last at the merch booth. As always, these playmats are unique, extremely limited, and a cool pickup as a memento of your Pro Tour experience!

![](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/OP20180419_PTDAR.png)

# Copenhagen

The Grand Prix circuit makes a Standard stop in Copenhagen, Denmark, June 8–10, and folks coming to this one will have a chance to pick up a playmat featuring the Angel of Angels, [Lyra Dawnbringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lyra+Dawnbringer) (art by Chris Rahn). Just look at all that stained-glass glory!

![](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/OP20180419_Copenhagen01.png)

# Vegas Double

Grand Prix Las Vegas will be home to more than just some [sweet birthday parties](/en/articles/feature/2018/03-05-announcing-the-25th-anniversary-birthday-celebrations) the weekend of June 14–17. It'll also be the home to two main events featuring Modern June 15–16 and Limited June 16–17.

The Modern and Limited GPs bring with them two distinct playmats that attendees can get, featuring [Shanna, Sisay's Legacy](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shanna%2C+Sisay%27s+Legacy) (art by [Magali Villeneuve](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Magali%20Villeneuve%22%5d)) and [In Bolas's Clutches](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=In+Bolas%27s+Clutches) (art by [Zack Stella](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Zack%20Stella%22%5d)).

![](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/OP20180419_LasVegas01.png)

![](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/OP20180419_LasVegas02.png)

# Singapore and Pittsburgh

The Grand Prix taking place June 22–24 in Singapore and Pittsburgh will feature a Merfolk-focused playmat, with a centerpiece of [Tatyova, Benthic Druid](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tatyova%2C+Benthic+Druid) (art by [Mathias Kollros](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Mathias%20Kollros%22%5d)).

# Barcelona

And finally, before we head into *Core Set 2019* land, Grand Prix Barcelona will take place June 29–July 1 and will wrap up our *Dominaria* playmat bonanza with [Grand Warlord Radha](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grand+Warlord+Radha) (art by [Anna Steinbauer](https://web.archive.org/web/20180419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5b%22Anna%20Steinbauer%22%5d)).

![](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/OP20180419_Barcelona.png)

We've got a lot of opportunities for you to play *Magic* with hundreds—if not thousands—of your past, present, and future friends, so check out a Grand Prix near you, pick up some playmat souvenirs to commemorate your trip, and maybe we'll see you at a Grand Prix or Pro Tour very soon!
