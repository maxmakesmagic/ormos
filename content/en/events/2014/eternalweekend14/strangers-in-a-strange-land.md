"So you'll draw three and I'll put two back?" Coming into The Legacy Championship, people expected a lot of [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise), White Weenie variants, Elves, and control strategies, but Legacy is a crazy format where virtually anything is possible. Let's talk about some of the strangest cards and interactions we've seen today. 

Time and time again we've seen [Notion Thief](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Notion+Thief) create veritable blowouts against [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm), [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise), and [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor). 

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Notion%20Thief)

  

In round one, (7) Ari Lax, last year's winner, found himself quickly in the losers' bracket after facing off against [Thragtusk](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thragtusk), [Consecrated Sphinx](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Consecrated+Sphinx), and [Frost Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frost+Titan). 

In round two, (20) Shahar Shenhar, playing a Death and Taxes White Weenie variant, was paired against a Reanimator deck. Shahar felt good about the game with Grafdigger's Cage in play, but he decided to turn the screws a bit more with [Rest in Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rest+in+Peace). Unfortunately for the reigning World Champion, his opponent had [Helm of Obedience](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Helm+of+Obedience). Shenhar's [Rest in Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rest+in+Peace) turned his opponent's [Helm of Obedience](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Helm+of+Obedience) into a lethal play! 

In round four, Ashok Chitturi found himself facing down a turn one [Sarkhan, the Dragonspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sarkhan%2C+the+Dragonspeaker) off of a combination of rituals including [Seething Song](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seething+Song). 

Right now, there's a [Veteran Explorer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Veteran+Explorer)/[Scapeshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scapeshift) deck that's still undefeated. 

The discard laden decks of the pre-*Khans of Tarkir* Legacy kept everyone honest, but those decks have become much worse with [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) in the format. Now, it's looking like players will have the opportunity to experiment with new cards and take their curves much high than in previous Legacy formats. 

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Treasure%20Cruise)

Has [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) ushered in a new era of Legacy? Will we start seeing more expensive spells and more unique strategies now that targeted discard is much weaker? Only time will tell.
