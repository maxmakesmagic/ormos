Last week in [Rei Nakazawa’s storyline article](https://web.archive.org/web/20040510000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/210) we previewed the Legendary cat soldier Raksha Golden Cub. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1b/47/1b471d3b4aaf9b5f670c5c17c92aed1ca85542826809cac893e4a1d71cc58254.jpg)

If you look closely, you can see that Raksha wears a mask that covers only the top half of his face. 

This is actually the Mask of Suns, a Leonin battle artifact fashioned by Dakan and said to contain his strength and cunning. Though the mask didn’t end up getting its own card in *Fifth Dawn* it does find a home in this dramatic piece by Pete Venters.
