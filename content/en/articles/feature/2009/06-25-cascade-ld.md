One of the surprise hits to come from Grand Prix-Sao Paulo was Diego Crucius’s Cascade list. Foregoing the usual control elements or the [Seismic Assault](https://web.archive.org/web/20090625000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seismic+Assault)/[Swans of Bryn Argoll](https://web.archive.org/web/20090625000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swans+of+Bryn+Argoll+) combo, Diego ramped all the way up to [Enlisted Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20090625000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enlisted+Wurm+) and was all too happy to cascade into either [Primal Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20090625000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Primal%2BCommand) or [Incendiary Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20090625000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Incendiary+Command). After board, he could even morph into a Land Destruction deck thanks to [Fulminator Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20090625000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fulminator+Mage+) and [Rain of Tears](https://web.archive.org/web/20090625000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rain+of+Tears). On top of that, due to a miscommunication with the friend who gave him the deck, Diego played an [Adarkar Wastes](https://web.archive.org/web/20090625000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Adarkar+Wastes+) instead of a [Llanowar Wastes](https://web.archive.org/web/20090625000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Wastes+) with no blue cards in his deck! You can read more about Grand Prix-Sao Paulo in the [official event coverage](https://web.archive.org/web/20090625000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/daniel-dominates-defending-brazil).
