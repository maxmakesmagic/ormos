![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gppra14/qq_zatlkaj.jpg)

***Matej Zatlkaj**: No significant changes due to GP Antwerp. I knew Mono-Green Devotion was a deck and I expected more Merfolk here after its good showing in Antwerp.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gppra14/qq_shenhar.jpg)

***Shahar Shenhar**: Metagame predictions are weird anyway. I played against Merfolk, Mono-Red, and Red-White [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter%C2%A0Twin). I could use almost nothing of the detailed sideboarding guide Frank Karsten gave me!* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gppra14/qq_dickmann.jpg)

***Patrick Dickmann**: I expected more of (my) [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter%C2%A0Twin) deck and more people to be prepared for the match-up. Unfortunately, true.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gppra14/qq_juza.jpg)

***Martin Jůza**: Not a big impact. I guess the blue-red decks, either the all-out combo versions of [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter%C2%A0Twin) or the ones like Antwerp's winning deck, are more popular now.*
