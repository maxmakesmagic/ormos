*Do you have any fun stories from this weekend's Prerelease events?* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9e/d4/9ed42fbf034eb908091f251c60232531dd0631477510baaa68bb9a668c2cc083.jpg)

**Zac Hill:** Two stories:

A) One guy was asking whether infect was at all a Constructed mechanic. I said, "Well, I have a sweet infect deck—want to battle?" I did nothing for the first three turns, then on his turn-four end step (falling to 3 from an attack with NOTHING on the board) I Carrion Called. He was dead on my next turn. Him: "Well, that was powerful."

B) In a game of *Elspeth vs. Tezzeret*, I was proliferating three times a turn with Tezzeret the Seeker / Contagion Clasp / Everflowing Chalice / Esperzoa. My opponent had zillions of guys, though, so I had to win by tutoring up a Pentavus.
