Hey, wanna see something cool?

As you know, Progenitus did not appear in *Shards of Alara*, but it did appear in *Conflux*. Just so you remember it clearly, and because it looks cool blown up, here's the art for Progenitus:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/17/7a/177a5f14c356e2e9a89fa7046460cb7e413c073dcc407de5fb65cf9baed8c29b.jpg) 

  

Dramatic and iconic, yes? But it seems like we've seen that silhouette somewhere before. Almost as if a pre-*Conflux* card hinted at what Progenitus would look like. See the earlier card!

 ![Relic of Progenitus](https://i.ormos.uk/05/1e/051ef5863efe6993d0878397c1d5b821675d66da4966a474c4a3d83cb9042343.jpg)
