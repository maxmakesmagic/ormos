The public choice was overwhelmingly Centaur Glade. I expected this, but the pack was interesting enough to generate some spirited discussion on the message boards. Let’s see what the pros had to say about the pack:

**Brian Kibler**: The clear pick out of this pack is Centaur Glade. None of the other cards rival the power of the green enchantment and although the commons Daru Sanctifier and Nantuko Vigilante in *Legions* make it harder to keep around, the game-swinging power of the card cannot be discounted. Additionally, it's the only solid green card in the pack, effectively cutting off the color to your left early on.

**Gary Wise**: I think Centaur Glade is hands down the strongest card in this pack, but it comes with an added bonus: going green now gives you a shot at Timberwatch Elves and the other goodies in *Legions* green, the strongest color in that set. People are still picking red cards to start their drafts like they're gold, so any excuse to go in a different direction is a good one.

**Joe Crosby**: Ascending Aven is the correct pick based on pure power level as well as the opportunity for smooth cooperation. Centaur Glade is the type of card I want to be opening and passing. While it is clearly very powerful as a late-game finisher, you have spent ![5 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/33/3c/333cbd82f68b5e306003b2955e517a94ffa7a3ea03087365c10afad540856dce.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif) before you get your first 3/3 and the result is an overwhelming setback in temporal advantage in a format where said advantage is paramount. The Aven is also a first-pick caliber card that will be top-notch in any blue deck, as well as being a great addition to your team at any point in the game. 

By passing the Centaur Glade, you ensure that your neighbor will be playing green (and therefore almost assuredly not blue) at a low cost to yourself, as the Glade is good, but overrated. The Skirk Commando is not an acceptable pick because the Glade and Aven are just much better cards for r-g or r-u respectively. For players wanting to force white at all costs, I'd suggest taking Piety Charm over Dive Bomber. The white deck you should be looking to obtain is based around soldiers and tempo-oriented beatdown and Piety Charm is the perfect spell for this type of deck. There are plenty of good soldiers to be obtained in *Legions* and the deck has to have combat tricks.
