### When

All Tournaments will be held on the weekend of January 20, 2007.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/08/b7/08b7aa7d9735b950662a434ccfe2d4526d927ddaca07f9aef9ddd69b9f9be750.jpg)*Yours for playing, while supplies last*

### Entry Fee & Product Mix

*   Check with your local tournament organizer for specific entry fee information.
*   Cost of entry will provide you, one (1) *Time Spiral* tournament pack and three (3) *Planar Chaos* boosters.
*   In addition, some locations may offer 3-person Team & 2-person Two Headed Giant formats.
    *   Each 3-person team will receive two (2) *Time Spiral* tournament packs & six (6) *Planar Chaos* boosters to build 3 decks.
    *   Each 2-person Two Headed Giant team will receive one (1) *Time Spiral* tournament pack & five (5) *Planar Chaos* boosters to build two decks. For further information about Two Headed Giant format, please go to the [Two Headed Giant FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20061220000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=dci/announce/dci20050805a).

### Tournament Structure

*   The structure of the main *Planar Chaos* Prerelease tournament is modified Swiss-style.
*   *Planar Chaos* Prerelease side tournaments will be offered throughout the day and will consist of single-elimination rounds.

### Tournament Formats

The format for each Prerelease tournament is:

*   DCI sanctioned, *Planar Chaos*, Sealed Deck.
*   Select locations, DCI sanctioned, *Planar Chaos*, Team Sealed Deck.
*   Select locations, DCI sanctioned, *Planar Chaos*, Two Headed Giant Sealed Deck.

### Prizes

*   Select locations, check with organizer, will offer Door Prizes of 3 *Planar Chaos* Booster Packs to be given out at random each hour.
*   Special commemorative *Planar Chaos* prerelease foil card for each tournament participant, ***while supplies last***.
*   Additional packs of *Planar Chaos* cards to top players (as determined by your local prerelease Tournament Organizer).
*   Other items for winners of the main tournament and side tournaments may be awarded (as determined by your local prerelease Tournament Organizer).

### Registration Information

*   Players who want more specific local tournament or registration information should call their local contact phone number.
*   Players who have general questions about the **Magic: The Gathering** TCG, Wizards of the Coast tournaments, the **Magic: The Gathering** Pro Tour, the Arena league, the DCI players’ organization, Qualifier tournaments, the **Magic** World Championships, National Championships, Champs, or Regionals, should call Wizards of the Coast Customer Service at (800) 324-6496, or go to, [http://wizards.custhelp.com](http://wizards.custhelp.com/).
