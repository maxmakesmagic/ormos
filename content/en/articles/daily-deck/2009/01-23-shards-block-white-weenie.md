It may be some time before the world of paper **Magic** adopts *Shards of Alara* Block Constructed, but players are already enjoying the exciting format on **Magic Online**. Bös managed to win a recent Premier Event with a very straightforward White Weenie list. Akrasan Squire? Knight-Captain of Eos? Even Antoine Ruel's Invitational winning card Ranger of Eos manages to find its way into the list. For more exciting formats you can play around the clock, including *Shards of Alara* Block Constructed, check out [**Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20100114131302/http://www.wizards.com/magic/digital/default.aspx).
