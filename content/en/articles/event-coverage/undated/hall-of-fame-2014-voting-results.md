Listed below are the results from the Selection Committee for the 2014 Hall of Fame class. Voters were asked to vote for up to five candidates, and all players who appeared on 40% or more of the ballots earned spots in the Hall.

There were 197 ballots cast by Selection Committee members. Individual ballots will not be made public.
