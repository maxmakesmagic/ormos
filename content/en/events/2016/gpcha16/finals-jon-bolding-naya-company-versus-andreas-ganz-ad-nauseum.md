Their meeting in the finals of Grand Prix Charlotte wasn't the first time Jon Bolding and Andreas Ganz battled one another. The two players have faced off twice before.

At the same Pro Tour.

In back-to-back rounds.

"You smashed me in Limited," Bolding told Ganz, "but I was able to return the favor."

Bolding took the play in Game 1, and both players kept their opening hands. Though Bolding was playing the more aggressive deck of the two, it was Ganz who made two plays on turn one, suspending a [Lotus Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotus+Bloom) and casting a [Serum Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serum+Visions). Bolding made no plays until turn three, when he cast a [Tireless Tracker](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tracker).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpcha16/f_Andreas-Ganz-Semifinals.jpg)  
*Andreas Ganz* 

Bolding's [Tireless Tracker](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tracker) went unchecked, picking up Clues for Bolding and +1/+1 counters for itself. A [Knight of the Reliquary](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+the+Reliquary) and a [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) joined it, though Ganz had a buffer between himself and defeat in the form of two copies of [Phyrexian Unlife](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Unlife).

With two poison counters and an opposing army of massive creatures, Ganz cast a [Serum Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serum+Visions) with only one other card in hand.

"I'm crossing my fingers," Bolding told him.

But Ganz cast a [Spoils of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spoils+of+the+Vault), found his [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam) four cards down, and showed Bolding a stack of [Simian Spirit Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simian+Spirit+Guide)s, [Lightning Storm](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Storm), and enough lands to kill him.

"I just needed one more creature earlier for pressure," Bolding said as he shuffled up for Game 2.

"In Game 1?" Ganz replied. "Yeah, I was too slow."

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpcha16/f_Andreas-Ganz-Finals.jpg)

Things looked like they might be off to a rough start for Bolding in Game 2 as he mulliganed to five, but on turn two he played a [Thalia, Guardian of Thraben](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thalia%2C+Guardian+of+Thraben), one of his deck's few ways of interacting with Ganz's combo.

Ganz made lemons back out of Bolding's lemonade, however, by casting a [Pentad Prism](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pentad+Prism) with three charge counters on it thanks to Thalia. He didn't have a follow-up, however, and Bolding continued to press his slim advantage by adding a [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) to the board.

On the following turn, however, Ganz took out Bolding's Thalia with a [Slaughter Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slaughter+Pact), and a follow-up [Angel's Grace](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel%27s+Grace) and [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20160523000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam) earned a handshake from Bolding. 

**Andreas Ganz defeats Jon Bolding 2-0 to become your Grand Prix Charlotte Champion!**
