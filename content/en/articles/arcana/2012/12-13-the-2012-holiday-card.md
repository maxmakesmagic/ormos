Every year since 2006, Wizards of the Coast has made special holiday-themed cards as a gift for employees and associates. Last year's was [Yule Ooze](/en/articles/arcana/2011/11-29-the-2011-holiday-card-2), 2010's was [Snow Mercy](/en/articles/arcana/2010/12-17-wallpaper-of-the-week-snow-mercy), and the [four before that](/en/articles/arcana/2009/12-11-wallpapers-of-the-week-holiday-cards) were Fruitcake Elemental, Gifts Given, Evil Presents, and Season's Beatings.

So it will come as no surprise that there's a new one this year. To see it, click here!
