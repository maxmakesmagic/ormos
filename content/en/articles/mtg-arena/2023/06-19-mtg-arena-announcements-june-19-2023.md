## *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth™* Arrives Tomorrow on *MTG Arena*!

![Sauron with the One Ring floating before him above his palm](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/bra2iyacrlho.jpg) 

Tomorrow, the world of Middle-earth comes to *MTG Arena* with the official digital release of *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth.* To prepare for this monumental launch, check out the [Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/05-30-the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earthtm-card-image-gallery) and [read up on the mechanics](/en/articles/feature/2023/05-30-the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earthtm-mechanics) found in this set!

## Release Notes for *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth*

![](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/giw4cre10che.jpg) 

With the release of a new set comes new release notes. *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth* will be an exciting launch for many players, both old and new, so we want to make sure all your questions are answered. Read more in [*The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth* Release Notes](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2023/06-19-mtg-arena-release-notes-the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earthtm).

## Get Ready for the Next Arena Open

[![Arena Open logo with the Planeswalker symbol](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/thi87phikuka.jpg)](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2023/06-19-arena-open-the-lord-or-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earth-limited-july-12) 

Celebrate the new set by competing in [the latest Arena Open](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2023/06-19-arena-open-the-lord-or-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earth-limited-july-12), a tournament where you can win up to $2,000 in addition to an invitation to the next Qualifier Weekend. The formats will include both Sealed and Draft matches of *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth.*

In addition, all participants will receive the Dragon Horde sleeve:

![Dragon Hoard sleeve entry reward in Arena Open](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/sleeve_dragon_hoard.png)

### QUALIFIER EVENTS

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events. Check out the standings to see [who's qualified for Arena Championship 4](https://magic.gg/standings/arena-championship-4).

Qualifier tokens are delivered in your *MTG Arena* inbox. Remember to claim them before the event starts!
