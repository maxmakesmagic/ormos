While mono-white decks featuring Tempered Steel were the talk of the town headed into [Pro Tour Nagoya](/en/events/2011/ptnag11/sharfman-masters-magic-weekend-nagoya), a group of Belgian pros took a different route. Their mono-white deck was a control build that eschewed aggressive artifact creatures for planeswalkers and board-sweepers.
