### Japan

[日本語の案内](/ja/events/2004/seattle04/2004-pro-tourseattle-qualifiers)

### Entry Fee

The entry fee for all Pro Tour—Seattle Qualifier tournaments in Japan is ¥7,500.

### Tournament Format

The format for Pro Tour—Seattle Qualifiers tournaments is *Mirrodin*TM Block 3-person Team Limited. For information on this format, please refer to the DCITM[Tournament Rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20051218140009/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dci/utr/intro) page.

### Tournament Structure

The structure for Pro Tour—Seattle Qualifier tournaments will be modified Swiss-style, with the Top 2 teams playing in a single-elimination Team Rochester format for prizes and invitations. 

### Invitations and Eligibility

Players already holding an invitation to Pro Tour—Seattle are prohibited from participating in any Pro Tour—Seattle qualifiers, with the exception of Grand Prix tournaments. Player may have earned invitations through cumulative past performance in the Pro Tour, winning a previous Qualifier or Grand Prix tournament, or by being an invited DCITM player (valid upon the date of the official ratings invitation announcement for the appropriate professional tournament.) Please refer to the [Pro Tour—Seattle Invitation List](/en/events/2004/seattle04/2004-pro-tourseattle#invites) for a list of qualified players.

### Prizes

*   Invitation to Pro Tour—Seattle
*   The winning team of each Pro Tour—Seattle Qualifier tournament will receive a cash prize of US $1500. (Cash prize awards will be distributed to all winners at the end of June.)
*   Product prizes will be awarded to the Top 4 teams of each Pro Tour—Seattle Qualifier tournament as follows: The winning team of each tournament will receive 96 booster packs of *Darksteel*TM. The finalist team will receive 48 booster packs of *Darksteel*. The 3rd- and 4th-place team will each receive 18 booster packs. (Tournaments held after the release of *Fifth Dawn*TM will award *Fifth Dawn* boosters instead of *Darksteel*.)

### Registration Information

Preregistration is required for all Pro Tour—Seattle Qualifier tournaments. Players must register no later than 10 days before the event they wish to participate in. Players may register by contacting Ron Foster, Wizards of the Coast Japan Organized Play Manager, at [ron.foster@wizards.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20051205154109/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=&origin=protour_seattle04_qualifiers-ja)
