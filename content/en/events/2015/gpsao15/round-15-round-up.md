As the Swiss Rounds draw close, the Top 8 is starting to shape up. At the time of print, it has been determined that the Top 2 Tables are most likely locked for the Top 8, but the next few tables need to play out their win-and-in match.

Here's a quick recap on four of the most important matches of the final round of Grand Prix São Paulo.

# Table 3: Claudio Barrientos (Abzan Aggro) VS. Luiz Ferreira da Silva (Atarka Red)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsao15/T3.jpg)  
*Claudio Barrientos (Abzan Aggro) vs. Luiz Ferreira da Silva (Atarka Red)*

Da Silva struck the opposing [Warden of the First Tree](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warden+of+the+First+Tree) with lightning and generated three tokens with [Hordeling Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hordeling+Outburst). That was not quite fast enough and Barrientos was able to take game one easily with the help of double [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) and [Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc). I heard those cards are pretty good against little red men.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Siege+Rhino)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Whip+of+Erebos)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos)

Game two did not go well for Da Silva either, since Barrientos was able to quickly assemble [Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion) and [Anafenza, the Foremost](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anafenza%2C+the+Foremost). A timely [Dromoka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dromoka%27s+Command) followed by [Whip of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos) secured Barrientos' spot in the Top 8.

**Claudio Barrientos 2 - Luiz Ferreira da Silva 0**

# Table 4: Maximiliano Ramiro Sanchez Pinilla (RG Dragons) VS. Fabio Barroso (Abzan Control)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsao15/T4.jpg)  
*Maximiliano Ramiro Sanchez Pinilla (RG Aggro) vs. Fabio Barroso (Abzan Control)*

Despite Pinilla putting up a speedy beatdown of [Boon Satyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boon+Satyr) and Surrak, the Hunt Caller, Barroso was able to stabilize the board with the help of a pair of [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall), [Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang) and [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon).

However, it was Pinilla who had the last laugh as he climbed back from that loss to win the match.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Thunderbreak+Regent)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thunderbreak+Regent)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Stormbreath+Dragon)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon)

He accelerated into a pair of [Thunderbreak Regent](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thunderbreak+Regent) with the aid of [Rattleclaw Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rattleclaw+Mystic) and Barroso was unable to find any elegant solution to both dragons without dying to their triggers. In the rubber game, Barroso gummed up the ground with [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) and Tasigur, the Golden Fang, but did [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall) to deal with Pinilla's [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon)

**Maximiliano Ramiro Sanchez Pinilla 2 - Fabio Barroso 1**

# Table 5: Pablo Ioppolo (WG Aggro) VS. Mateus Batista de Melo (Esper Dragons)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsao15/T5.jpg)  
*Pablo Ioppolo (WG Aggro) vs. Mateus Batista de Melo (Esper Dragons)*

De Melo quickly nabbed the first game with the help of an early [Dragonlord Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai) while Ioppolo was stuck on two land and could only manage [Warden of the First Tree](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warden+of+the+First+Tree) and [Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion).

In Game Two, Ioppolo retaliated with [Den Protector](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Den+Protector), [Hidden Dragonslayer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hidden+Dragonslayer), [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos) and [Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion). However, those threats were dealt with respectively by double [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight), [Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price) and [Foul-Tongue Invocation](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foul-Tongue+Invocation). The Esper Control deck is well-known for having that much removal.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Dragonlord+Ojutai)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Silumgar%2C+the+Drifting+Death)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silumgar%2C+the+Drifting+Death)

[Ashiok, Nightmare Weaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashiok%2C+Nightmare+Weaver) and [Silumgar, the Drifting Death](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silumgar%2C+the+Drifting+Death) eventually convinced Ioppolo to extend the hand as he wished de Melo good luck in the Top 8.  
  
**Pablo Ioppolo 1 - Mateus Batista de Melo 2**

# Table 6: Ricardo Nunes Martins (Esper Dragons) VS. Douglas Romani (Esper Dragons)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsao15/T6.jpg)  
*Ricardo Nunes Martins (Esper Dragons) vs. Douglas Romani (Esper Dragons)*

After taking the first game, Martins was the one with the trump for the sideboarded game.

Romani thought he was doing fine with his sideboard tech of [Risen Executioner](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Risen+Executioner), but unfortunately that was no match for Martins' [Monastery Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Mentor). A lonely 4/3 zombie was no match for a horde of monk tokens!

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Monastery+Mentor)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Mentor)

**Ricardo Nunes Martins 2 - Douglas Romani 0**
