Welcome to the first **Mistform Ultimus Watch**. In this to-be-recurring sub-feature of Magic Arcana, we keep track of what creature types that auto-updating *Legions* legend has recently become, and in effect, what new creature types have recently been added to **Magic**.

But since this is the first occasion of Mistform Ultimus Watch, you may want to do a little background reading on Mr. Ultimus.

*   Want to read Aaron Forsythe's [development stories](/en/articles/latestdevelopments/2004/03-12-the-ultimate-sliver) from when Mistform Ultimus was being created?
*   Want to read all of Rune Horvik's [rulings](https://web.archive.org/web/20030904220901/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/satschoolarchive&term=mistform_ultimus) about the Ultimus?
*   Want to read Jay Moldenhauer-Salazar's [deck ideas](/en/articles/houseofcards/2003/01-16-the-ultimate-octopus) for Mistform Ultimus?
*   Want to read [player-submitted poetry and stories](/en/articles/latestdevelopments/2004/04-30-a-mixed-bag) about the day the Ultimus became Human?

Okay, enough with the old; you're ready for the new. Today we focus on what the Ultimus has become as the result of *Mirrodin* block: the *Mirrodin*, *Darksteel* and *Fifth Dawn* sets.

*   *Mirrodin* adds **Archer** with Tel-Jilad Archers. It adds **Drone** with cards like Looming Hoverguard. It adds the high-profile **Human** with eleven cards like Disciple of the Vault. The **Myr** creature type of course also busts onto the scene as of *Mirrodin*. Also new are **Rogue** from cards like Neurok Spy and **Shaman** from cards like Viridian Shaman. The creature types **Pentavite** and **Pest** arrive due to the token creature generators Pentavus and Nuisance Engine. **Slith** shows up on cards like Slith Bloodletter. Lastly, *Mirrodin* adds **Vedalken** from cards like Vedalken Archmage.

*   *Darksteel* adds **Artificer** with cards like Slobad, Goblin Tinkerer. It adds **Blinkmoth** from Blinkmoth Nexus and **Twin** from Gemini Engine. Lastly, *Darksteel* brings us **Wirefly** from Wirefly Hive.

*   *Fifth Dawn* adds **Bringer** to the Ultimus's virtual type line with cards like Bringer of the White Dawn, and **Pincher** due to Summoning Station.

That's all for this block. The next time we'll catch up with Mistform Ultimus Watch is in October, after the release of *Champions of Kamigawa*!
