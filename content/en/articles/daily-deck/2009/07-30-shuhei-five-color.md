Japanese Nationals fielded an incredible Top 8, featuring such big names as Ren Ishikawa, Kenji Tsumura, Yuuya Watanabe, and reigning Player of the Year Shuhei Nakamura. It was the latter who managed to take down the entire event with his take on Five-Color Control. Read more about the event on the [official event coverage](/en/events/2009/jpnat09/shuhei-rocks-japan).
