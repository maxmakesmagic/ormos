The leader of the Azorius guild is the sphinx Isperia, currently appearing in *Return to Ravnica* as [Isperia, Supreme Judge](https://web.archive.org/web/20121029000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Isperia%2C+Supreme+Judge). But Isperia first appeared in *Ravnica: City of Guilds* as [Isperia the Inscrutable](https://web.archive.org/web/20121029000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Isperia+the+Inscrutable).

![Isperia the Inscrutable](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Isperia+the+Inscrutable)

  

The art for [Isperia the Inscrutable](https://web.archive.org/web/20121029000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Isperia+the+Inscrutable) was drawn with more background than you see on the card. In fact, it looks like this!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0a/19/0a19b9899f84410cfa43e41c9946c78baa3eff5908cfdb6dd6ae57a726d6c791.jpg)

[Isperia the Inscrutable](https://web.archive.org/web/20121029000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Isperia+the+Inscrutable) | Art by [Greg Staples](https://web.archive.org/web/20121029000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Greg%20Staples%22%5D)

Back then, the guildmaster was [Grand Arbiter Augustin IV](https://web.archive.org/web/20121029000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grand+Arbiter+Augustin+IV). But by the time Isperia came back in *Return to Ravnica*, the sphinx had taken the position and got a new card: [Isperia, Supreme Judge](https://web.archive.org/web/20121029000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Isperia%2C+Supreme+Judge).

There's another place to get a good look at Isperia, but it's not on a card.
