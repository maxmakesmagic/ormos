For months we've been teasing out bits of information about [*Arena of the Planeswalkers*](https://web.archive.org/web/20150701000000/http://wpn.wizards.com/en/products/arena-planeswalkers), the *Magic: The Gathering* strategy board game featuring the likes of Gideon, Jace, Liliana, Chandra, and Nissa. After [announcing the game](/en/articles/feature/2014/10-16-announcing-the-magic-the-gathering-strategy-board-game) in October of last year, [we showed off a few bits from the game](/en/articles/arcana/2015/02-25-arena-of-the-planeswalkers) in February, including some of Chandra's game pieces.

And now, the game has arrived.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/Arena_PW_logo.png)

That's right, *Arena of the Planeswalkers* is now available from select retailers, with expanded product availability increasing throughout the month.

Pick up your copy today and ensure you're the last Planeswalker standing!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/MTG_BoardGame.png)
