Last weekend's StarCityGames.com Open in Las Vegas saw some old favorites appear in the Top 8… like Aggro Loam! This powerful midrange deck has a deceptively strong land-based engine revolving around Life from the Loam. The *Ravnica* sorcery combines with cycling lands like Forgotten Cave to help Countryside Crusher get big quickly and let you kill out of nowhere with the one-two punch of Seismic Assault. Check out this deck for your next Legacy tournament!

Tony DeVeyra's Aggro Loam 

Legacy – StarCityGames.com Open in Las Vegas, NV
