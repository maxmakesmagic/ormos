### Redeem Your Cards Today!

* * *

Just an update to the [previous announcement](/en/articles/feature/2002/12-10-card-redemption-announcement) about redemption: The redemption feature will go live today, December 18, 2002 around noon Pacific Standard Time.

For those of you ready to redeem online sets, just click the **Redeem** button on your **Collection** screen to get the process rolling. If you're not sure about the details of redeeming a set, the [previous announcement](/en/articles/feature/2002/12-10-card-redemption-announcement) covers the basics.

Thanks again for your patience.

-*The **Magic** Online Team*
