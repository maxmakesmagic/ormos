# What Is the *Magic Online* Weekly Announcements Blog?

Every Tuesday, we round up all of the biggest *Magic Online* news for the Weekly Announcements Blog.  
 Check in weekly for the latest updates!

***Fate Reforged* R&D Feature**

The latest R&D Feature by Allison Medwin takes an inside look at bringing *Fate Reforged* to *Magic Online*!

Check out the full article [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/02-02-reforging-fate-reforged).

* * *

**Ugin's Fate Promo Cards**

*Fate Reforged* [Prerelease Events](/en/articles/magiconline/2014/01-12-fate-reforged-prerelease-and-release-events#prerelease) (which ran last week) and [Release Events](/en/articles/magiconline/2014/01-12-fate-reforged-prerelease-and-release-events#release) (available now through February 18) gave each participant five cards from the Ugin’s Fate boosters. Different events are awarding different cards. We’re saving Ugin himself for the future, but the rest of the alternate art promos are available through these events!

* * *

**New Episode of Vintage Super League Next Week**

No episode of [Vintage Super League](http://vintagesuperleague.com/) tonight—the league is taking a week off. Tune in next Tuesday at 6:00 p.m. Pacific (UTC-8) on [twitch.tv/magic](http://www.twitch.tv/magic) for the next episode of the Vintage Super League!

* * *

***Fate Reforged* Release Events**

*Fate Reforged* Release Events are going on now on *Magic Online*! For full event details, refer to the Release Events [article](/en/articles/magiconline/2014/01-12-fate-reforged-prerelease-and-release-events).

* * *

**Wizards of the Coast Employee Play Policy Update**

We've made some changes to our Wizards Play Policy that will allow Wizards folks to play in certain *Magic* Events that do no not feed into premier play.

Have questions? Check out the full article [here](/en/articles/feature/2015/01-30-wizards-announces-wizards-play-policy).

**Downtime for February 4, 2015**

*   All tournaments will close Wednesday, February 4, at 5:00 a.m. Pacific (13:00 UTC)
*   Store and trade activity will be suspended at approximately 7:45 a.m. Pacific(15:45 UTC)
*   The system will be down from 8:00 a.m. Pacific (16:00 UTC) until 12:00 p.m. Pacific (20:00 UTC)
