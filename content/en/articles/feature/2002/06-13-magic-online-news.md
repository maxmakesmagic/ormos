Dear ***Magic: The Gathering** Online* Beta Testers:

Thanks to each of you, the product has made tremendous strides, and we are confident that the official launch date of June 24, 2002 will be an historic day for **Magic: The Gathering.**

Here are some important final details:

*   June 16th will be the final day of open beta. During the evening of the 16th all card collections and accounts will be removed.
*   After launch on June 24th, you will not need to re-install the client software. All updates will happen automatically. If you no longer have the software installed, just click go to the store at [http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20020613000000/http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com/) and click the help link for instructions on where to download it.

In addition, we want to give a special thanks to our most dedicated testers. We will be offering qualified testers (those who had played 25+ games, as of June 12th at 2:00 PDT), the opportunity to pre-register. Notification emails will be sent out in waves between June 17th and June 23rd. If you register for a permanent account by July 31st, you will be able to retain your login name.

Those who qualify will receive an email awarding them a promotional coupon for the ***Magic** Online* Store. This means you can start playing with a theme deck or booster draft for FREE!

To participate in this offer it is critical that we have your accurate email address. If your email address is out of date, please call our Technical Support Team at (425) 687-2156 or email them with the following information at techsupport@wizards.com by 6/18 PDT 6:00 p.m.

*   Username
*   Billing Address
*   Updated current e-mail address

Thank you again for all of your support. We look forward to seeing each of you online!

\- ***Magic: The Gathering** Online* Team
