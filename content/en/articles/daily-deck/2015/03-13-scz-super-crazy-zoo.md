This week's theme was reversions of "old fashion" decks. However, today I would like to introduce a brand-new deck. Do you think "brand-new" is exaggerating? Nope, I'm pretty sure however long *Magic*'s history may be, no one's ever done this before. I mean, who in their right mind starts turn one with "[Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) myself"? It's against every rule in the strategy book.

Japan's crazy deck builder [Atsushi Ito and his new deck](http://www.happymtg.com/column/ito_tunelog/24645/) SCZ are responsible for this abomination against *Magic* strategic principal. This crazy deck takes the form of a Zoo deck; half the deck is devoted to attacking the opponent with cheap creatures. I suppose there are games where it doesn't ever reveal its hidden identity and attacks the opponent with [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl)s and [Steppe Lynx](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=171012)es. However, its true form is as a deadly one-shot-kill deck, much like that of Infect, with cards like [Become Immense](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Become+Immense) and [Temur Battle Rage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temur+Battle+Rage). Don't lower your guard, or you'll be assaulted by a huge double-striking trampler!

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Lightning+Bolt)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt)

This evil combo deck hides it fangs until the last moment as much as possible by using zero-mana cantrips like [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe) and [Street Wraith](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Street+Wraith), which also fill the graveyard. These two and other zero-mana draw spells make up almost half of the deck. Using these to put five cards in the graveyard is stage one…is what I would say if this was a normal *Magic* deck working under common reason.

However, this deck plays [Death's Shadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death%27s+Shadow).

These cantrips cost life and the Zoo deck already pays for its fetch lands and shock lands, often starting the game at 14 life. This [Death's Shadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death%27s+Shadow) has created a ruthless, mad deck that doesn't hesitate to [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20150313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) itself.

I mean, I guess that makes sense.
