After two days of competition, only eight players remained. Let's get to know them a bit!

* * *

# Claudio Barriento Ochoa

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsao15/Top-8-Claudio-Barriento.jpg)

**Hometown:** Temuco, Calama, Chile  
**Age:** 29  
**Occupation:** Civil Industrial Engineer  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Day 2, Grand Prix Buenos Aires 2014; Day 2, Grand Prix Santiago 2014 

**What Standard deck are you playing, and why did you choose it?**  
 Abzan Aggro, because my brother and my teammates convinced me to play it! I always play “Rock” decks! 

**What card won you the most matches this weekend and how?**  
[Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino). 

**What are your best and worst matchups?**  
 My best matchups are the combat-based ones, and the worst matchups are Control in general. 

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**  
 I will move [Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price) to the sideboard and add something to the maindeck for the Control matchups. 

* * *

# Mateus Batista de Melo

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsao15/Top-8-Mateus-Batista.jpg)

**Hometown:** Belo Horinzont, Brazil  
**Age:** 23  
**Occupation:** Entrepreneur  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 7-2 at São Paulo PTQ; Grand Prix Trial Winner 

**What Standard deck are you playing, and why did you choose it?**  
 Esper Dragons. It's the best deck of the metagame and I am used to playing Control. 

**What card won you the most matches this weekend and how?**  
[Dragonlord Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai) from the maindeck and [Dragonlord Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar) from the sideboard. 

**What are your best and worst matchups?**  
 GW Company 

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**  
 One more [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight), one less [Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price).

# Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsao15/Top-8-Paulo-Vitor-Damo.jpg)

**Hometown:** Porto Alegre, Brazil  
**Age:** 27  
**Occupation:** *Magic* Player and Writer at ChannelFireball.com  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 9 Pro Tour Top 8, three-time National Champion, Hall of Fame! 

**What Standard deck are you playing, and why did you choose it?**  
 Esper Dragons. I think it's very good and I've played a lot with it. 

**What card won you the most matches this weekend and how?**  
 Ojutai, attacking! Ashiok was pretty good too. 

**What are your best and worst matchups?**  
 The best matchups are the slow non-blue decks, like Abzan Control and bees. The worst matchups are the super-fast aggro decks, like Green/White and Mono-Red (though I'm 8-0 versus Mono Red with Esper!) 

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**  
 Right now, nothing.

# Maximiliano Sanchez Pinnilo

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsao15/Top-8-Maximiliano-Ramir.jpg)

**Hometown:** Chile  
**Age:** 32  
**Occupation:** Right end in Club Football, Lawyer  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** World *Magic* Cup 2012 

**What Standard deck are you playing, and why did you choose it?**  
 RG Dragons. I like Dragons because they are very massive and red. 

**What card won you the most matches this weekend and how?**  
[Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon) and [Crater's Claws](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crater%27s+Claws). [Crater's Claws](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crater%27s+Claws) is my best card for winning games in the Grand Prix. 

**What are your best and worst matchups?**  
 I think the best matchup is Esper Dragons and the bad matchup is Sidisi-Whip. 

**What changes would you make to your deck and why would you make them?**  
 I would cut 1 [Boon Satyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20150503000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boon+Satyr) for 1 more Mountain.
