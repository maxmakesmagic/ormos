[A while back](/en/articles/arcana/2014/08-27-wedges-by-the-numbers), we looked at the (small) assortment of wedge cards available in *Magic* before the release of *Khans of Tarkir*. To say the choices were meager would be a bit of an understatement, as some wedges had as few as four cards to their name. Heck, a "robust" number was five.

But with the actual release of *Khans of Tarkir* this past week, those numbers have swelled considerably. So while we won't be showing off every single card in every wedge this time around, we can look at the constitution of the three color cards for each.
