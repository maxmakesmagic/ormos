*What's your most prized **Magic** possession?* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e0/0c/e00c2900e4532fed44efdbc0223f486ee1518c0cf594bb289867319eeda0f043.jpg)

**Kelly Digges:** My most prized **Magic** possession is not an especially rare or noteworthy card. It's not signed, or foil, or 3-D, or even in especially good condition.

See, I'm an old-timer. In 1995, after playing **Magic** for the first time at a friend's house, I went out (and, um, begged my mom for some money) and bought two boosters and a "starter deck" of *Revised Edition*. Somewhere among those 90 cards lurked this gem:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/44/f0/44f06d3661b82a23b687f23c24afc8ff84b1d493818383b02e434fac47db65f1.jpg) 

  

I couldn't tell you for certain any of the other cards I got that day—well, except the basics—and I have no idea where any of them might be. Some of them are probably still in one of my card boxes, some I'm sure I traded off or gave away. But I know right where that Shivan Dragon is, and I plan to keep it that way for a long, long time.
