[![](https://i.ormos.uk/21/0f/210fda7cc49f3f16943a308941073389d54fc512773da2534eea7ae4218f811e.jpg)](http://www.childsplaycharity.org/)

Every year, Penny Arcade runs [Child's Play](http://www.childsplaycharity.org/), a charity devoted to rounding up toys, games, and money for children's hospitals. And one of the key events is the annual [Charity Auction](http://www.childsplaycharity.org/cpdinner.php), at which various cool gaming-related things are auctioned off.

We bring this up because this year, the "tour of Wizards of the Coast and **Magic** R&D" went for $1,900 (which, the **Magic** brand team would like us to point out, is $300 more than the D&D tour went for), and the tour happened on Monday, conducted personally by Mark Rosewater. Congratulations to Aaron Doukas:

  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/9f/2c/9f2c9970c6d86cd8151ab4d21d32d730cd2ab0e08f974249a277bed3da6b5eef.jpg)  

Aaron also brought his friend, Andrew Kaiser. Say hello, Andrew!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e2/39/e239e66d02aee66485be610d2bd4710ba9a051241d57a151213d9d5e1e8f7e86.jpg)  

It turns out that they timed their visit well, because there just happened to be some *Conflux* around. And even though the prerelease was still a few days away (as it still is, if you [haven't made your plans yet](https://web.archive.org/web/20090128000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prereleases)), Mark Rosewater rounded up some R&D and **Magic** brand members for an impromptu *Conflux* sealed deck tournament.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/94/6f/946f293a16982927a3b0029ccd32b63eea9f977bb4c19c4ca664845291413157.jpg)  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/4f/7d/4f7d1ddc3bdc13eac1ea568121a8829681f8f2578ce01535f5a359a46ad0a854.jpg)  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/70/e0/70e0a76fd9fc1fb780df5dcfe4302c17c5e459755bf79a5eb8fc2bcdc724d5ae.jpg)  

The official winners of the tournament were Mark Gottlieb and Mark Globus, who were both undefeated. Auction Winner Aaron beat Magic R&D Director Aaron Forsythe *and* R&D VP Bill Rose, but lost to Mark Rosewater.

Aaron and Andrew left happy (and loaded down with *Conflux* and other **Magic** product), so we hope they got their $1,900 worth. And Wizards of the Coast and Child's Play look forward to doing this again next year!
