The [Innistrad Championship](http://magic.gg/events/innistrad-championship) has its Top 8 players! Congratulations to Christian Hauck, Toru Saito, Yuuki Ichikawa, Zachary Kiihne, Simon Görtzen, Yuta Takahashi, Riku Kumagai, and Yo Akaike!

![](https://images.ctfassets.net/ryplwhabvmmk/2Wv0zYyvQouT0mc8zhedym/3a550bfc91dd05d902a9f9694b5782cf/1920x1080-Innistrad-Championship-Top-8-Social.jpg)

[Learn more about the Top 8 players](https://magic.gg/news/innistrad-championship-top-8-player-profiles), then watch to see who wins in the Historic format Top 8 double-elimination playoff December 5, beginning at 9 a.m. PST at [twitch.tv/magic](http://www.twitch.tv/magic).
