## GENERAL INFORMATION

The *Magic* Grand Prix circuit returns to Providence, Rhode Island the weekend of June 19-21!  The Rhode Island Convention Center provides a first class facility to play in. Our side event schedule is huge, featuring all formats during the weekend.

You can choose the prizes that *you* want to win thanks to our Prize Wall! Our Prize Wall has tons of choices ranging from single cards, booster packs, booster boxes, playmats, and cool *Magic* Collectibles.

Every player who competes in the main event will receive the official Grand Prix playmat as well! We'll also have an exclusive show playmat, Dragon on the River, which is being drawn by Christopher Rush, one of our Grand Prix artists in attendance who will be on-hand to sign your playmat! Dragon on the River will also be given to players for playing in select side events as well.

Join us for the biggest *Magic* experience in the USA's smallest state!

Entry into the Grand Prix event venue is free of charge.

**Main Event Registration Fee:** $50 Earlybird Preregister, $60 online PreRegister, $70 on site  
**Main Event Maximum Capacity is 1,950** **Players (changed from previously announced number; see [tjcollectibles.com](http://tjcollectibles.wpengine.com/category/tom-shea/) for details)**  
[Registration online](http://www.tjcollectibles.com/) until June 19th  
 Registration will be held on-site Friday, June 19th from 12:00 p.m. – 9:00 p.m.  
 There will be no on-site registration on Saturday.

Many Grand Prix offer additional services to participants, such as Sleep-In Specials and VIP packages. Sleep-In Specials allow players with byes to skip the player meeting on Saturday and arrive shortly before their first round begins. VIP packages provide various perks such as complimentary bottled water, special seating, and exclusive artist signings. Details of these services vary from event to event; please check the organizer's web site for specifics. 

The format for Grand Prix Providence is [Standard Constructed](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/standard-formats-magic-gathering). For more information on the format and schedule, [click here](#formats).

Players can earn tournament byes for the Grand Prix Providence main event on Saturday by meeting the appropriate Planeswalker Point thresholds by June 17 or by winning a Grand Prix Trial that awards byes to this event.

For more information on the number of byes you can earn for this event and how to earn them, [click here](#BYES).

Along with the main event, there are additional side-event tournaments to play in at a Grand Prix. Side events are offered at all Grand Prix on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday. For details on side events that you can find at every Grand Prix, [click here](#sideevents).

A list of additional events and a full schedule for when side events take place can be found on the [Grand Prix Providence website](http://tjcollect.com/). All side events (except casual events) will have a Planeswalker Points multiplier of 3x.

**Last Chance Trials**

Both Standard and Sealed Deck Trials will be offered on-demand on Friday, June 19.  
 Standard Trials: $25 entry, 11:00am – 5:00pm, 32-person single-elimination tournament  
 Sealed Deck Trials: $30 entry, 11:00am – 5:00pm, 32-person single-elimination tournament

Prizes:

*   1st place – 360 Prize Wall tix; 2-round bye for the Grand Prix; Full VIP upgrade which includes Sleep-in Special
*   2nd place – 360 Prize Wall tix
*   3rd/4th place – 180 Prize Wall tix
*   5th-8th place – 90 Prize Wall tix
*   9th-16th place – 30 Prize Wall tix

Some of the hottest and most famous artists in *Magic* will be joining us at Grand Prix Providence. All artists will be available to sign cards, playmats, and more. In addition, all will be selling prints, artist proofs, original art/sketches, and doing custom alterations of mats and cards!

Artists scheduled to appear at Grand Prix Providence include Christopher Rush, Eric Deschamps, Scott M. Fischer, and rk post. For more information on these artists, [click here](#artists).

## FORMAT AND SCHEDULE

**Format:** [Standard Constructed](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/standard-formats-magic-gathering)  
**Main Event Registration Fee:** $50 Earlybird Preregister, $60 online PreRegister, $70 on site  
**Main Event Maximum Capacity is 1,950 Players (changed from previously-announced number; see [tjcollectibles.com](http://tjcollectibles.wpengine.com/category/tom-shea/) for details)**

*   [Online registration](http://www.tjcollectibles.com/) until June 19
*   On-site registration on June 19 from 12:00 p.m. – 9:00 p.m.
*   Saturday, June 20: Grand Prix Providence begins at 9:00 a.m.
*   Sunday, June 21: Day Two begins at 9:00 a.m.

Registration for the Grand Prix main event closes Friday night. There will be no Saturday registration.

The main tournament at Grand Prix Providence is open to *Magic* players of all ages.

Players must have a signed waiver and consent form to compete at Grand Prix Providence. Forms can be [downloaded here](http://media.wizards.com/2014/docs/MTG_GP_Waiver_2015.pdf) and will be available on-site. Minors must have a parent of guardian’s signature on these forms. Minors will not be able to register or play unless we receive a waiver signed by a parent or guardian. No exceptions. 

*   [Standard Constructed](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/standard-formats-magic-gathering)
*   Deck lists are required
*   Players must use the same deck on Day Two that they used on Day One

*   Modified Swiss-style (50-minute rounds).
*   Day One will consist of 9 Swiss rounds. Day Two will consist of 6 Swiss rounds and the Top 8 single-elimination finals.
*   All players with an X-2-0 record (or better) or the top 64 players – whichever is greater – advance to the second day of competition for prizes and invitations.
*   The Top 8 players after the final Swiss round on Day Two will advance to the Top 8 single-elimination playoff.
*   Top 8 playoff matches will be best 2 of 3.
*   Top 8 playoff matches will have no time limit. (Players are still expected to play at a normal pace and complete playoff matches in a reasonable amount of time.)
*   Standings after the Swiss rounds will be used to determine final order in the standings for losing players in the quarterfinal and semifinal playoff rounds.
*   For the first game of each match in the Top 8, the player that finished higher in the Swiss rounds chooses either to play first or to play second. For subsequent games in each Top 8 match, the usual Play/Draw rule applies (loser of the previous game decides whether to play first in the next game).

## TOURNAMENT BYES

Grand Prix tournament byes can be earned either through a player's yearly [Planeswalker Points](https://web.archive.org/web/20160620192037/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/PlaneswalkerPoints/Information) total or by winning a [Grand Prix Trial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/grand-prix-trials-event-types-events).

Byes earned through Planeswalker Points are calculated using the Planeswalker Point totals from all reported events as of the Wednesday immediately before the Grand Prix. Points earned in tournaments held at the Grand Prix event site do not count toward earned byes at that Grand Prix.

Winners of a Grand Prix Trial tournament are awarded two byes in the Grand Prix that it feeds. Trial byes are not cumulative with other byes and do not pass down. Byes are not cumulative throughout the season or year. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Players who accumulate multiple byes to a particular Grand Prix tournament receive only the bye for the greatest number of rounds. For complete information about Grand Prix byes and their corresponding competitive season, please check the [*Magic: The Gathering* Premier Event Invitation Policy](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://wpn.wizards.com/en/resources/rules-documents/244).

*   Players with a [Pro Players Club](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://magic.wizards.com/node/152291) level of Gold or Platinum
*   Members of the [Pro Tour Hall of Fame](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://magic.wizards.com/node/142796)

 

*   Winners of this event's [Grand Prix Trial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/grand-prix-trials-event-types-events) tournaments.
*   Players with a [Pro Players Club](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://magic.wizards.com/node/152291) level of Silver
*   Players who have accumulated at least 2,250 Planeswalker Points in the 2015 Yearly Award Season (June 2, 2014 – May 31, 2015)
*   Players who have accumulated at least 2,250 Planeswalker Points in the 2016 Yearly Award Season (June 1, 2015 – May 29, 2016)

*   Players who have accumulated at least 1,300 Planeswalker Points in the 2015 Yearly Award Season (June 2, 2014 – May 31, 2015)
*   Players who have accumulated at least 1,300 Planeswalker Points in the 2016 Yearly Award Season (June 1, 2015 – May 29, 2016)

## SIDE EVENTS

Along with the main event, there are additional side-event tournaments to play in at a Grand Prix. The Side Events listed in this section are on the schedule of every Grand Prix.

**Last-Chance Trials**  
 Earn a valuable Two-Round bye by winning one of the Last-Chance Trials. [Last-Chance Trials](#LCT) are 32-person single-elimination events, starting as soon as 32 players register.

**"Foiled Again"**  
 A straight Swiss tournament lasting three or four rounds. Participants get one past promo card for playing, then one additional promo card per match win. Format varies by event.

**Value Drafts**  
 Enjoy a special discount on pick-up drafts on Saturday. Actual times and prices vary by event.

**"Rebound" Event**  
 Format at organizer's discretion. The winner(s) earn free entry into the "Super Sunday Series" (see Sunday Side Events). Players who also played in the main Grand Prix tournament will get a discount for entry.

**Super Sunday Series**  
 Qualifier tournaments for the Super Sunday Series will be held at each Grand Prix. The Top 8 players in the Super Sunday Series qualifier will each be awarded a commemorative Grand Prix water bottle. The winner of the Super Sunday Series qualifier will earn an invitation and travel award for the Super Sunday Series Championship 2016, expected to be held in January 2016 in Seattle. The Super Sunday Series Championship experience also includes a tour of Wizards of the Coast, dinner with Wizards employees, and more.

### Christopher Rush

One of the original 25 illustrators for the first Magic set, Christopher Rush created [Black Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=+298), the most powerful card in the game’s history, and [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=+209). Other famous cards first printed with Christopher Rush illustrations include [Brainstorm (Ice Age)](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=+2497) and [Wellwisher](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=+39497).

### Eric Deschamps

Eric Deschamps is a freelance illustrator and concept artist who has created the iconic art for some of *Magic*'s iconic Planeswalker cards, including [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=373649) and [Dack Fayden](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dack+Fayden).

### Scott M. Fischer

Scott Fischer graduted with honors from the Savannah College of Art and Design, and has spent years illustrating for a wide variety of clients ranging from role-playing game companies to major video game publishers. Some of his notable works in *Magic* include [Kiora, the Crashing Wave](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=378521) and [Bogardan Hellkite](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bogardan+Hellkite).

### rk post

A farmboy from Illinois with a gift for sketching, rk post has become one of *Magic*'s iconic artists. Some of his most beloved creations include [Morphling](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morphling) as well as [Lightning Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20160616190125/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Angel).
