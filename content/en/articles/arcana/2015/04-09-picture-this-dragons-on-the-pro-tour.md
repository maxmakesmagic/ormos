*   That one red mana symbol was from one [Ryusei, the Falling Star](https://web.archive.org/web/20150409000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ryusei%2C+the+Falling+Star) in Osyp's sideboard from the first Pro Tour Hawaii.
*   Shuhei Nakamura got 9th at Pro Tour Philadelphia 2005, on tiebreakers. Shuhei played 3 copies of [Jugan, the Rising Star](https://web.archive.org/web/20150409000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jugan%2C+the+Rising+Star); had Shuhei been luckier, there would have been nine green mana symbols up there.
*   To add insult to injury, Philly 2005 used the "skins game" elimination format... It was theoretically impossible to miss Top 8 on breakers! poor Shuhei (and poor Jugan)
