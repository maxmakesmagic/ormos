### Gateway welcomes you to the world of Magic: The Gathering !

Gateway is the brand new in-store Organized Play program by Wizards of the Coast for all over Europe.  
  
Gateway is the ideal way to meet new friends and improve your game in a friendly environment at your local games shop. The nice thing is, the more you play, the more points you get, and the more **Magic: The Gathering** promo cards you can earn.

### Seize the following opportunities that Gateway offers you:

*   To play against other new players in a **casual league** environment.  

*   To challenge other enthusiasts in **a real tournament**.

  
 Ask the **Gatekeeper** in your local store to fill you in on all the details and get your first taste of **Magic: The Gathering**.  
  
**Play the game** and become part of the international gaming community!  
  
 Check out **[where](https://web.archive.org/web/20080504094945/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/gatewaystores)** you can you play in your neighborhood:

### Fill up your Gateway reward card with 30 stamps = 30 points = get your promo card\*!

**How?**  
  

*   **By playing your very first game** in the store = **10 points**  
  

*   By participating in the **Gateway league**:
     You win = **4 points**
     You lose = **3 points**  
  

*   By participating in a **Gateway tournament**:
     You win = **6 points**
     You lose = **4 points**
     You participate = **5 points**  
  
 Ask your local shop for your reward card.  

![The reward card](https://i.ormos.uk/09/20/0920b1ce3686fdb8a5872e36e49665b71a1db4e2ef28e1480b4613947fa37fea.jpg "The reward card") 

 ***Note**: Gateway tournaments are official DCI sanctioned tournaments with a K value 8 in which you can earn rating points for the worldwide ranking system. Find out about the ranking system on: [www.theDCI.com](http://www.thedci.com/)*

\**Subject to promo card availability – valid until stock lasts*
