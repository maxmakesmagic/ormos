It's not easy to make a giant creature look giant. **Magic** artists have a variety of techniques to show scale. A huge monster can be shown from below or off in the foggy distance. And another technique, the one we're talking about today, is called "scale birds."

![](https://i.ormos.uk/34/8b/348b7568c59e7a2d20918728c1cc6a191a13fb85eead3ac1620bc05fb8a03a4e.jpg) 

Sphinx of Uthuun | Art by [Kekai Kotaki](https://web.archive.org/web/20110810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22kekai+kotaki%22])

See those little birds out there in the sky? They're there to show that Sphinx of Uthuun is bigger than them. The presence of tiny "scale birds" shows that the main creature is giant.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/67/7d/677db0a571ce9cf6fda2a024d1d0bc9f4f2d08713d7e251c6b5374ee6712d80f.jpg) 

Gaea's Revenge | Art by [Kekai Kotaki](https://web.archive.org/web/20110810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22kekai+kotaki%22])

Gaea's Revenge is so big that those birds probably just think it's a mountain or something.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/50/d0/50d06a2fc3b1e894cc5af6d5fad9ae3ca9bf6a2f5fdd77e7965ac34ad5a91c15.jpg) 

Sun Titan | Art by [Todd Lockwood](https://web.archive.org/web/20110810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22todd+lockwood%22])

Without birds: a guy with a sword. With birds: a *titan* with a sword.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/49/6b49babdcab873e281ccb2a70b4141587ec1e4a44a2bb88f6f6d43d0cea5fe4d.jpg) 

Primalcrux | Art by [Wayne Reynolds](https://web.archive.org/web/20110810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22wayne+reynolds%22])

Technically, the art for Primalcrux has scale *bats*, not birds. But that's close enough, right? Wayne Reynolds is a master; he can do that.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c6/6c/c66cf5c28c51cb432fe79ecda2b598ac484b69ff5842c0fb4771f5cce93a0917.jpg) 

Hedron Rover | Art by [Jason Felix](https://web.archive.org/web/20110810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22jason+felix%22])

That's actually not the original version of Hedron Rover. During development, the creature was a bit larger, so the first illustration looked like this:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/91/22/9122a90b1a94197298412e6abbfa6a71087d935a29fa679300309b6104aec112.jpg) 

Hedron Rover | Sketch by [Jason Felix](https://web.archive.org/web/20110810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22jason+felix%22])

See? Change the scale birds and you change the size of the creature!
