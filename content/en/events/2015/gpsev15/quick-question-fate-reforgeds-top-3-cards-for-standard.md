# Fate Reforged's Top 3 Cards for Standard

We found a couple of well-known players and asked them to name the top three cards from *Fate Reforged* with regards to Standard. Here's what they had to say... 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsev15/qq-juza.jpg)  
**Martin Jůza:** [Valorous Stance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valorous+Stance) because Abzan was so dominant before. Also [Wild Slash](https://web.archive.org/web/20150214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Slash). It's really nice to have these cheap interactive cards that fuel stuff like [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20150214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) or [Monastery Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Mentor) or what have you. But my favorite is [Outpost Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20150214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Outpost+Siege), definitely one of the sleeper cards of the set. 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsev15/qq-anteri.jpg)  
**Fabrizio Anteri:** Definitely [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon). Also [Shaman of the Great Hunt](https://web.archive.org/web/20150214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shaman+of+the+Great+Hunt) and [Outpost Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20150214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Outpost+Siege). 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsev15/qq-lemoine.jpg)  
**Vincent Lemoine:** [Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20150214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang) is number one for sure. Such a good and cool card. Then there's [Valorous Stance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valorous+Stance) and [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon). 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsev15/qq-hatto.jpg)  
**Steve Hatto:** Definitely [Wild Slash](https://web.archive.org/web/20150214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Slash). Definitely not Ugin. I'd also pick [Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20150214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang) and [Shaman of the Great Hunt](https://web.archive.org/web/20150214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shaman+of+the+Great+Hunt).
