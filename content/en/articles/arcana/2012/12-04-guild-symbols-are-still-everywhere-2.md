A few weeks ago, we saw [guild symbols in *Return to Ravnica* artwork](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/guild-symbols-are-everywhere-2012-10-23). But we only scratched the surface!

For example, the birds get into the act! Check out the armor on the chest of [Judge's Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge%27s+Familiar).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6f/f8/6ff856e583b317b84a9b640c8c80a5fa667d5338dee9b398293e63dc736c38fb.jpg)

[Judge's Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge%27s+Familiar) | Art by [Jack Wang](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Jack%20Wang%22%5D)

The [Loxodon Smiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Smiter) is covered in the Selesnya symbol, including on the hammer it smites with.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/cd/e6/cde618f3d7954d41200f1054d660e7270acf1aeaefb9cdf6ca5853f425876ed6.jpg)

[Loxodon Smiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Smiter) | Art by [Ryan Barger](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Ryan%20Barger%22%5D)

The [Splatter Thug](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splatter+Thug) is going to war with a tattered banner that used to have the Rakdos symbol on it.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a9/31/a9313a5decd6ac2d0a0bb310572efef6d5d0c4967de94897ccbb349b510b02b5.jpg)

[Splatter Thug](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splatter+Thug) | Art by [Kev Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Kev%20Walker%22%5D)

Even the arrowhead on [Avenging Arrow](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avenging+Arrow) clearly shows the guild of the archer:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/88/fb/88fb2e36e513913170bbd6579c96a348cb9e2d61cb3ff2e19777c74cb56ef4f0.jpg)

[Avenging Arrow](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avenging+Arrow) | Art by [James Ryman](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22James%20Ryman%22%5D)
