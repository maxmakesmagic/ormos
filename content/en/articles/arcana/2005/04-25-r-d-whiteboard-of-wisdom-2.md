Inside the Wizards of the Coast offices, in a hallway near the department known as **Magic** R&D, there's a whiteboard. On this whiteboard, deep questions are posed and then answered by passersby and their multicolored, dry-erase wisdom. In the past, for example, this whiteboard sought answers to the question, "[Is gum food?](/en/articles/faq/2004/11-15-unhinged-faqtiwdawcc-frequently-asked-questions-that-if-we-didnt-answer-would)" 

Here's a photo of the R&D whiteboard's current question: "What is the lamest superpower you can think of?"

![R&D whiteboard](https://i.ormos.uk/ba/fe/bafe099bd9b43499eb7a301a7d89bc3fc82fe34abaf2729e15c488d266a47c53.jpg)
