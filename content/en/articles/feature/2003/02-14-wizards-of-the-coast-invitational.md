Of course, listening to Aaron talk about the decks he's building for Tribal Wars is a bit disheartening. In fact, you'll notice a slight change in the format: it's gone from 20 cards with the same creature type to 1/3 of the deck being cards of the same creature type. As some of you might guess, Aaron told Randy that he'd built a 222-card deck with 20 wizards and 4 Battle of Wits.

And this is just some of the competition I face.
