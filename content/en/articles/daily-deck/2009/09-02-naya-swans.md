Saint-Raphael, France brings us our second **Magic** Game Day deck of the week! Adrien Peudecoeur used this innovative, mostly Naya build to make the Top 8. Magma Phoenix and a smattering of spot-removal burn spells held the ground while Swans of Bryn Argoll attacked in the air. And if Adrien needed more cards to get by? A timely Intimidation Bolt on the 4/3 flyer on the opponent's turn not only stopped the attack, but drew three cards!
