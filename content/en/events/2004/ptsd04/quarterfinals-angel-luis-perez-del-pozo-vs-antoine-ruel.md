![Antoine Ruel](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/ptsd04/qf3_1.jpg)Antoine Ruel is an old hand at Pro Tour events with an impressive resume that includes Top 4 at Worlds in 2001, two Masters series Top 4's, and a pair of Grand Prix trophies for his mantle. He had never won a Pro Tour and this was his first Top 8 other than the 2001 World Championships. He was considered by the experts to have drafted the best deck at the table. His deck is a nearly ideal red-white equipment deck with Spikeshot Goblin, Auriok Steelshaper, and Leonin Shikari. His arsenal of equipment included two Scimitars, Bonesplitter, and Sword of Fire and Ice.

If the veteran was finally going to win the Pro Tour he would have to get past this tournament's five-named Cinderella story, Angel Luis Perez Del Pozo. Prior to this tournament the biggest thing he had ever won was the PTQ in Madrid that earned him the invitation. He had acquitted himself well all weekend long facing tough draft table after tough draft table. He has gotten by with a couple of unlikely creatures--one deck sported a pair of Malachite Golems--and this draft was no different as he was running two Dross Prowlers. He did have two Spikeshots and his Prowlers could actually be good against Ruel who had few creatures that could block them. 

Draft observers noted that Del Pozo's deck was the one best suited to derailing a draft many felt was Ruel's to lose. In the Swiss portion of the event Angel had faced both Nicolai Herzog and his quarterfinal opponent. He lost to Nicolai but defeated Antoine. He was not encouraged by his victory though, "He was very unlucky against me. He had to mulligan Game 2 and was mana-screwed in Game 3…"

"I hope he has that luck again."

### Game 1

Antoine opened up the game with Alpha Myr and Angel played Grimclaw Bats. The Myr attacked for two and the Bats cracked back for one. Neither player had a third turn play. Angel exhaled dejectedly when his opponent's fourth turn ended in a Skyhunter Patrol. 

His Dross Golem on the fourth turn was no happier to see that Antoine's Alpha Myr was still 'round. He took four on the next turn and Ruel kicked up a Taj-Nar Swordsmith to deposit a Bonesplitter into play. Angel quickly fell to twelve.

Del Pozo's Grimclaw Bats put on Mask of Memory and attacked for one and a pair of cards. Angel played a Krark-Clan Shaman. Ruel played an Auriok Steelshaper and moved his Bonespitter over to the freshly cast man. In response Angel sacrificed his Dross Golem and killed the Steelshaper and the Alpha Myr. Ruel moved the Bonesplitter onto his Swordsmith and attacked for six.

Angel cast Chittering Rats and attacked for two more cards with his Bats but had no play. Ruel put the Bonesplitter on his flier and when he attacked the Spaniard shot it down with Barbed Lightning. He confirmed his life total was six and elected to chump black the Swordsmith.

Ruel looked sullen as Angel clawed back into the game with his card advantageous Bats. Angel thought for a while before killing the Swordsmith with Betrayal of Flesh . He entwined it to bring back his Chittering Rats. Antoine began to scoop up his cards in disbelief. "I played four cards against him. Yesterday I beat him in Game 1 and then played four cards in Game 2 and three."

Antoine - 0 Angel - 1

### Game 2

Antoine chose to play Pteron Ghost over Gold Myr on turn two due to Del Pozo's turn one Krark-Clan Shaman. Del Pozo had a Vulshok Morningstar for it on the second turn and attacked for three past the freshly cast Krark-Clan Grunt. Del Pozo's Scavenging Scarab was dispatched with a Purge end of turn and Ruel swung in with Gold Myr, Grunt, and Ghost. He played a Spikeshot Goblin but Del Pozo killed it with Barbed Lightning and attacked in for three more and played Grimclaw Bats.

Ruel's draw earned hoots and hollers from the Frenchman as he played Sword of Fire and Ice. He equipped it on his flier and Del Pozo had to chump block with the Bats. Del Pozo had an Oxidda Golem and attacked Ruel down to five. The Frenchman winced, "Shrapnel Blast?" 

Angel smiled at him but it quickly turned to a laugh and he shook his head, "No that would be good." He moved his equipment over to his Golem to get it out of range of the Sword. Ruel cracked back with everyone but the Myr. He shot the Shaman and drew a card. Del Pozo announced his next attack and the Frenchman flipped Blinding Beam onto the table.

Antoine - 1 Angel - 1

### Game 3

Ruel considered bringing in some additional artifact creatures in Drill Skimmer and Bottle Gnomes. Finally he decided to only bring in the Gnomes and took out a Plains to accommodate it. Once again Del Pozo led off with his Shaman. He followed with Leaden Myr. Antoine played Gold Myr. Del Pozo debated his options and decided to play Vulshok Morningstar and equip it onto his Shaman. He beat in for three. He could have sacrificed his Myr to kill the Frenchman's but he chose not to and Antoine cracked back for one and played a Soldier Replica.

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/ptsd04/qf3_2.jpg)

Angel set him back a turn with Chittering Rats and took him to thirteen. Antoine had Sword of Fire and Ice and was able to equip it onto his Replica. Angel put his Myr in the way. Angel was able to get in for three more and then he moved his Morningstar to the Rats. Antoine broke his heart with Blinding Beam and he attacked for four and shot the Shaman. Angel passed the turn and when Antoine played Vulshok Berserkers he entwined Barbed Lightning to kill it. Antoine had a Krark-Clan Grunts to play for defense and he chumped the 4/4 Rats.

Angel attacked for four on his turn and played a Spikeshot Goblin. Antoine attacked and shot the Goblin but Angel surprised him with Scale of Chiss-Goria. Antoine grumbled but looked to be in good shape when he cast Skyhunter Patrol and Bottle Gnomes. When the turn passed back to Angel Antoine had another Blinding Beam. Angel killed the Gold Myr in response. He played a Grimclaw Bats and moved the Morningstar over. 

Antoine attacked with his sword wielding Skyhunter Patrol and the Soldier. Angel put the Grimclaw Bats in the way and pumped its toughness with the Scale and paid one life to make it a 4/5. Antoine had to sacrifice the Soldier Replica to kill it and Angel only dipped to five from the activation of the Bats. His creatures did not untap though and the top of his deck only yielded a Dross Prowler.

Antoine - 2 Angel - 1

### Game 4

Antoine had an exceptional draw in the fourth game. He opened on Auriok Glavemaster and Leaden Myr and could have played a third turn Razor Golem but chose to play Skyhunter Patrol instead. Angel's draw during this time was Leaden Myr and Spikeshot Goblin. 

He killed the Myr with his Goblin on the fourth turn and used his own Myr to power out a Scavenging Scarab as well. Antoine had more than his share of spells this game and he summoned Vulshok Berserkers and attacked for six. Angel attacked for three and then played a sluggish Oxidda Golem which traded with the Berserkers. He took two from the flier and Antoine finally played the Razor Golem. Angel killed the Glavemaster at the end of the turn.

The Golem did not block Scavenging Scarab and the life totals were fourteen to eleven in Ruel's favor. "Are you going to draw the Sword?"

"I already have it." But the Frenchman was kidding. Angel killed the attacking flier with an entwined Barbed Lightning but took three from the Golem and the life totals changed to eleven to eight. Antoine's Swordsmith forged a Leonin Scimitar and picked it up. Angel attacked with his Scarab and it traded with the Swordsmith with a little help from the Goblin.

Angel had a Dross Prowler to chump block the Scimtared Golem on the next turn. Angel had no attack and played a Nim Shrieker--he was plinking with the Goblin though. Antoine played a Leonin Shikari and a second Scimitar. It looked impossible for Del Pozo to win but he had drawn a second Spikeshot and was able to chump with two creatures--he was at four and any unblocked two power creature would kill him--and shoot Antoine down to four. Angel drew and played a Grimclaw Bats on the next turn and passed with Bats, Goblin and Shrieker on guard duty.

Antoine drew the Sword of Fire and Ice which excited the crowd but not the French player. He explained in French that it was the worst card in his deck that he could draw in that situation. Angel was able to block both creatures and shot Ruel down to three. He could kill him with the Bats alone but he drew a Morningstar to salt the wound. The Spanish player had chump blocked to victory.

Antoine - 2 Angel - 2

### Game 5

Antoine kept a questionable hand with five lands, Blinding Beam, and Leonin Shikari. Angel mulliganed so it began to look better. "Two more," he encouraged but Angel kept his next six which featured Bats, Prowler, and Betrayal of Flesh. With the mulligan out of the way the two players had to wait for the Jonsson Oiso match to conclude before they could move to their deciding game under the feature match cameras. They had played the first four games with only a few friends along the rail to observe the action.

They had to wait almost half an hour for the other match to play out which is a long time to think about your mulligan decisions. If Anton had and doubts he did not show them and was walking around the feature match area and craning his neck over judges and reporters to get a glimpse of the match that was ongoing. Angel on the other hand remained seated at the table and looked decidedly less calm. This was easily the most important game of his Magic career and every decision could mean a difference of anywhere from $4,000 to $24,000. The enormity of the game showed in his posture and expression.

Angel shook his head at the second turn Shikari and Ruel did the same at the Spaniard's Bats. Ruel had no equipment and merely attacked for two. Angel had Specter's Shroud for his third turn. He attacked and Ruel went down a card. Ruel got in for another two and played Bottle Gnomes.

Del Pozo took another card from the Frenchman and played a Hematite Golem. Ruel attacked with both his creatures assuming he would get past for one point but he did not want to risk a combat trick and had no blocks and he took three instead of one. Ruel hoped to hold off the Bats with a Skyhunter Patrol. 

Angel had one Swamp and three Mountains so he could not get in with his Bats. He moved the equipment to his Golem and did two. Antoine was down to two cards in hand and had to choose between Swordsmith and Beam obviously pitching the former. Angel had a Dross Prowler as a third creature.

Ruel drew his card for the turn and attacked the Spaniard down to ten--he was at fourteen. He played a land and when it was Angel's turn he asked, "One card in hand?" He moved the Shroud to the Prowler and attacked with only that creature. Ruel used Blinding Beam on the other two and entwined it. The life totals were now eleven to ten in Ruel's favor. Angel played a Chittering Rats after combat. There were a number of other ways that turn could have played out for the Spaniard the most obvious being to cast Chittering Rats before the attack step. His other option was to attack with all his guys but now all of his men were tapped and he would not untap them next turn.

Ruel knocked and his deck and put a Vulshok Berserkers into play and attacked with everyone. Angel blocked the Berserkers and fell to five. He meekly played his sixth land and passed the turn. When Ruel attacked he cast Betrayal of Flesh with entwine to kill the flier and putting the Chittering Rats back into play. Ruel had topdecked another Blinding Beam and cast it with entwine in response to tap down the Rats. 

Angel was sitting on three lands and had a Clockwork Condor in hand. He could either topdeck a land or any three casting cost creature in his deck and he would probably win but it was not to be and he congratulated the French player for such an exciting match.

![Angel Perez del Pozo](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/ptsd04/qf3_3.jpg)

Ruel commented that he probably should have used the Betrayal during the Frenchman's draw step. It did not matter since Ruel drew the instant speed Blinding Beam but if he had drawn another haste creature or a piece of equipment he would have been forced to put it back on top of his library. Angel was hoping to devastate Ruel though by killing the flier and blocking the Shikari and killing it as well. The two damage that Del Pozo took when he chose not to block the Shikari with is Golem was much more critical and represented the turn he needed to untap and win the match.

**Final result:** Antoine Ruel - 3 Angel Luis Perez Del Pozo - 2

Antoine advanced to face off against Anton Jonsson in the semi-finals. With his worst match-up behind him the French player was the favorite to run the remainder of the table and win his first Pro Tour Championship.
