[日本語の案内](/ja/events/2004/nagoya05/pro-tournagoya-05-qualifiersoctober-november-2004)

### Entry Fee

The entry fee for all Pro Tour—Nagoya Qualifier tournaments in Japan is ¥3,500.

### Tournament Structure

The structure for Pro Tour—Nagoya Qualifier tournaments will be modified Swiss-style, with the Top 8 players playing in a single-elimination format for prizes and invitations. 

### Tournament Format

The format for Pro Tour—Nagoya Qualifiers tournaments is *Kamigawa*TM Block Limited (*Champions of Kamigawa*TM Sealed Deck + Rochester Draft finals). For information on this format, please refer to the DCITM[Tournament Rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20050406081141/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dci/utr/intro) page.

### Invitations and Eligibility

Players already holding an invitation to Pro Tour—Nagoya are prohibited from participating in any Pro Tour—Nagoya qualifiers, with the exception of Grand Prix tournaments. Players may have earned invitations through cumulative past performance in the Pro Tour, winning a previous Qualifier or Grand Prix tournament, or by being an invited DCITM player (valid upon the date of the official ratings invitation announcement for the appropriate professional tournament.) Please refer to the Pro Tour—Nagoya Invitation List for a list of qualified players.

### Prizes

*   Invitation(s) to Pro Tour—Nagoya (the exact number of invitations is determined by tournament location; see below)
*   The winner of each Pro Tour—Nagoya Qualifier tournament will receive a cash prize of US $250. (Cash prize awards will be distributed to all winners at the completion of the qualifier round.)
*   Product prizes will be awarded to the Top 8 players of each Pro Tour—Nagoya Qualifier tournament as follows: The winner of each tournament will receive 32 Japanese language-edition booster packs of *Champions of Kamigawa*TM . The finalist will receive 20 booster packs of *Champions of Kamigawa*. The 3rd- and 4th-place players will each receive 15 booster packs, the 5th- and 6th-place players will each receive 8 booster packs, and the 7th- and 8th-place players will receive 5 booster packs each.

### Registration Information

Preregistration is required for all Pro Tour—Nagoya Qualifier tournaments. Players must register no later than 10 days before the event they wish to participate in.
