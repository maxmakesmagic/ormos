Looking for a new deck to try out in the brave new world of Standard with *Mirrodin Besieged*? Check out this list! Used by a Wizards of the Coast spellslinger, this take on Elves features the powerful new Lead the Stampede. With only creatures and lands in the deck (besides the Leads, of course), you're sure to be leading quite the stampede into the red zone!
