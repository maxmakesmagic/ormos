When the *Onslaught* development process began, there were only three tribal lands in the set: Goblin Burrows, Riptide Laboratory, and Wirewood Lodge. All three were rare. After another cycle of lands were cut from the set, R&D decided to create five more tribal lands to reinforce the importance of creature types in the set.

Now each major race has a corresponding land, some of which are rare and some of which are uncommon. Some are more powerful than others, so try them out and see which ones you like.

*   Goblin Burrows - Goblins
*   Contested Cliffs - Beasts
*   Wirewood Lodge - Elves
*   Starlit Sanctum - Clerics
*   Daru Encampment - Soldiers
*   Unholy Grotto - Zombies
*   Seaside Haven - Birds
*   Riptide Laboratory - Wizards
