They are kind of the equivalent of a PTQ except that if you win them you get $500, 2nd gets $300, and 3rd/4th gets $100. </p>&#10;      <p>&#10;        <i>&#10;          <b>BDM: </b>And those qualify for you for the Championship?</i>&#10;      </p>&#10;      <p>&#10;        <b>Scott: </b>Kind of… We are not using an ELO rating system like we use for <b>Magic</b>. We are using a point accumulation system. Every match you play of Dreamblade will earn you points. If you get a win or a bye you get two points. If you get a loss or a draw you get one point. These things just stack up every year. It takes 1000 points to get to the end of year Championship - coincidentally you get 1000 points for winning a 1K but it is not technically a qualifier. If you finish in the top 10 of a 10K event you get at least 1000 points. </p>&#10;      <p>The incentive to keep people playing once they are qualified for this event is our rewards program which is participation based in that you get your swag when you show up for events. For example you would go to a 1K and they would look up how many points you have, and depending on what level you have you will get stuff right then. You don't have to wait for it to come in the mail. When you show up at a 10K you will get better stuff. The same for the Championship. The stuff has yet to be announced but we have some pretty cool items.</p>&#10;      <p>&#10;        <i>&#10;          <b>BDM: </b>What is the game like?</i>&#10;      </p>&#10;      <p>&#10;        <b>Scott:</b> It is not your typical miniatures game. There is no line of sight, there is no measuring off distances, and it's played on a grid that looks a little like a chess board. When you move you just go from one square to the other so the movement and combat are very easy. It is a very tactical game in that you are more concerned with trying to control areas of the board than killing your opponent's creatures. </p>&#10;      <img src="https://i.ormos.uk/e1/84/e1849a014c08bc91b92bcf0c7058bb2d2b9512545a8e44559e6fd06dd198b6e1.jpg">&#10;      <p>It is definitely collectible and it is sold in starters and boosters. Starters have 16 figures and boosters have seven. The interesting thing is that when you build your warband - kind of like your deck - you can only use 16 figures, no more and no less. There is no point cost to each one, just any 16 you want. There are no faction rules - you don't have to worry about having all the figures of the same race.</p>&#10;      <p>&#10;        <i>&#10;          <b>BDM: </b>Who<b>are the people responsible for the design of the game?</b></i><b>&#10;      </b></p><b>&#10;      <p>&#10;        <b>Scott:</b> The original game design is done by Rob Heinsoo and Jonathan Tweet and the current design team is Henry Stern, Mike Donais, and Andrew Finch; all names that should be familiar to <b>Magic</b> fans.</p>&#10;      <p>&#10;        <i>&#10;          <b>BDM: </b>When is the game going to launch and how soon until there are cash tournaments?</i>&#10;      </p>&#10;      <p>&#10;        <b>Scott: </b>We are launching the game at GenCon with a $20,000 Sealed Deck release event. You will get a starter and a booster and play a bunch of rounds over two days and we will give away $20,000 over the first two days it is released. You can go to <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20061116030116/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dbm/welcome">dreamblademinis.com</a> and download a virtual starter, booster, and a board and start playing right now. You print them out and fold them up and they look just like flat versions of the actual minis. The rules are right up there too so you can learn the game now and be prepared for when you are going to GenCon. </p>&#10;      <a name="9"> </a>&#10;      <li>&#10;        <b>Saturday, June 17: 9:09 pm - Parting Thought</b>&#10;        <div>&#10;          <i>by Brian David-Marshall</i>&#10;        </div>&#10;      </li>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <p>As the day ends, we leave you with the best beards from this, the largest Pro Tour in <b>Magic</b> history. </p>&#10;      <div>&#10;        <img src="https://i.ormos.uk/c1/8d/c18d8b4f3628dcab5b6eb09a5ac5be87858e588e8b42a58c2a57df0512553df2.jpg">&#10;      </div>&#10;      <br>&#10;    </b></td>&#10;  </tr>&#10;</tbody></table>
