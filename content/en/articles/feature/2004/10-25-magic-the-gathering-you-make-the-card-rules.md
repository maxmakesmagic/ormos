**Magic: The Gathering You Make The Card Contest** (the “Contest”) is open to legal residents of the United States subject to eligibility guidelines below. Void where prohibited by law. The submission period begins at 12:01AM PST on October 25, 2004 and ends at 11:59 p.m. PST on November 15, 2004. This Contest is sponsored by Wizards of the Coast, Inc. (“Wizards”). 

**2\. Eligibility:** The Contest is open to all legal residents of the United States.

**3\. How to enter:** Create an original work of art that is compatible to be shown on a card 2 1/6” wide by 1 9/16” tall (“Submission”). Scan or photograph the Submission and submit it by e-mail in a .JPEG format no greater than 1MB in size, along with your name, address, e-mail address, and telephone number, by clicking **\[here\]**.

**4\. Judging:** Wizards employees serving as judges of the Contest will review all Submissions and determine the finalists using the following criteria: originality and creativity (40%), artistic merit (40%) compatibility with gameplay (20%). By no later than January 15, 2005, Wizards will select at least three (3) finalists. The finalists’ Submissions will be displayed on the MagicTheGathering.com website by no later than January 30, 2005, and website visitors will vote on their favorite selection. The finalist with the most votes at the end of a week will be the Winner. All decisions made by Wizards on all matters relating to this Contest are binding. Winner will be notified by telephone. If Winner is unable to be contacted at the information provided within three (3) days after attempts by telephone, Winner will be disqualified and another winner may be chosen.
