## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4b/a8/4ba8fb135fa8a39e54fdefe02efe106b1db3f660c0f66c66445b65600257a45e.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set as well as two card boxes, six 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, one random Pro Tour player card, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/75/08/7508a8a7fbeb799b804b0f82fd8ce3ab101573c447a9eb9d860fe01793b7fa6e.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6f/70/6f7025ade97a2dea6f458ce4409172d500318eac265029ea2feaf23a4b9b122e.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 40 cards and a strategy guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718094822/http://magic.wizards.com/node/961856)

(\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7e/d5/7ed5164c7ed58eae13df365d4cd89f57731c1a634b9dfe4eab8086f220bb3ad1.png)

### TWO-PLAYER STARTER GAMES

Each of the five Core Set Tenth Edition starter games includes two 15-card boosters, a premium foil card, a pack of 20 land cards, and a **Magic** learn-to-play guide.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c9/85/c985d6b312252d618f6b599c5c4925d8aedaf847d950df12b176f68650fb8377.png)

Tenth Edition features 383 cards, including randomly inserted premium versions of all cards in the set.

**Number of Cards:** 383

**Languages:** English, Chinese Traditional, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish
