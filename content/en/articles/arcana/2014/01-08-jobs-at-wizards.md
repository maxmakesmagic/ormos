Welcome to 2014! Today's Arcana is a reminder that we are always looking to hire people. I do not know of a single day where we did not have open positions listed on our website.

As of this writing there were fifteen listings on the Wizards careers page, ranging from a [content manager](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/content-manager-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1) (working for me!) to a [Japanese support member](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/japanese-support-csr-remote-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1) to art director roles for both **[Magic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/art-director-magic-rd-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)** and **[D&D](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/sr-marketing-art-director-dungeons-dragons-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)**.

Here's a listing of positions available to work on **Magic** (note, positions may be filled by the time you're reading this).

*   **[Associate Brand Manager](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/associate-brand-manager-magic-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)** - The brand team is looking to hire a new associate brand manager to join the team. You'd be part of the decision-making process and help drive the vision for **Magic**.
*   **[Sr. Business Manager, Magic Online](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/sr-business-manager-magic-online-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)** - Help drive the business for **Magic Online**—working with Worth Wollpert and his team on the popular platform.
*   **[Digital Art Director, Magic Online](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/digital-art-director-magic-online-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)** - You would be responsible for guiding the aesthetic quality of **Magic Online**.
*   **[Sr. Marketing Art Director](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/sr-marketing-art-director-magic-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)** - You would be developing, executing, and maintaining the marketing campaign visuals and style guides for **Magic**!
*   **[Art Director](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/art-director-magic-rd-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)** - You would be working with Jarvis and the Creative team to world-build and commission illustrations for **Magic**.
*   **[Software Developer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/software-developer-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)** - Work on the **Magic Online** game and help us continue to improve it!
*   **[Sr. Product Manager](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/sr-product-manager-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)** - You would determine the strategic direction, business requirements, and critical feature sets of **Magic** digital products.
*   **[Localization Manager](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/localization-project-manager-analog-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)** - While not directly working on **Magic**, the localization team is a vital part of our system that enables **Magic** to be printed in eleven languages!
*   **[Content Manager](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/content-manager-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)** - This position is working for me, helping drive **Magic**'s content forward. You'd take on responsibilities over DailyMTG and other projects.
*   **[Content Manager, Trade Marketing](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/content-manager-trade-marketing-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)** - Like the role above, this one focuses on the content we use to communicate with our trade partners, such as stores in the WPN network.
*   **[Japanese Support CSR, Remote Job](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/japanese-support-csr-remote-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)** - Work remotely as a member of our game support team.
*   **[Technical Producer, MTGO Live](https://web.archive.org/web/20140108000000/http://company.wizards.com/about/careers/technical-producer-mtgo-live-job-renton-wa-us?careers-view=1)** - Work on **Magic Online** development setting clear production and technology direction, leading cross-functional teams to deliver an excellent customer experience on the **Magic Online** Game Service.
