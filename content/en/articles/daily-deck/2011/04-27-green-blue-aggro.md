Looking for something fun to try during this National Qualifier season? Why not give Laurence Swasey's Green-Blue Aggro deck a shot? Swasey managed to make the Top 8 of the recent StarCityGames.com Standard Open in Boston with the deck. Fauna Shaman, Nest Invader, and Vengevine provide the beatdown while Garruk Wildspeaker and Jace, the Mind Sculptor provide a heavy-hitting Plan B. Read more about the deck in the [official coverage](http://www.starcitygames.com/magic/standard/21683_Live_Coverage_of_StarCityGamescom_Open_Series_Boston.html) from StarCityGames.com.
