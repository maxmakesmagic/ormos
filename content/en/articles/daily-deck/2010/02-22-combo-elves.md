Matt Nass kicked off the 2010 Pro Tour season in style by winning Grand Prix–Oakland! The inaugural event of the year featured the Extended format, and Matt had no problem tearing through it with but a single loss. His deck? Combo Elves. Read the full coverage from the event [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20100429082142/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=judge/article/20090817a).
