### ![](https://i.ormos.uk/5d/dd/5ddd358678faf60227b48fe5b04575fe952f8a1a416641fd95b64500c653fbe5.jpg)Elvish Predation

The Elves are coming out of the woodwork! When you pilot the “Elvish Predation” deck, you’re in control of a massive Elf-producing force. Not only are all twenty-eight creatures in the deck Elves, but many of them, such as [Lys Alana Huntmaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20080814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lys+Alana+Huntmaster) and [Nath of the Gilt-Leaf](https://web.archive.org/web/20080814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nath+of+the+Gilt-Leaf), put Elf creature tokens into play. Some of your spells make tokens as well—in fact, [Elvish Promenade](https://web.archive.org/web/20080814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Promenade)*doubles* your number of Elves!

What to do with so many Elves? The easy answer is to pump them up with [Imperious Perfect](https://web.archive.org/web/20080814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Imperious+Perfect) and attack! Alternately, you can use [Lys Alana Scarblade](https://web.archive.org/web/20080814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lys+Alana+Scarblade) to make opposing creatures smaller based on how many Elves you’ve got, or use [Immaculate Magistrate](https://web.archive.org/web/20080814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Immaculate+Magistrate) to make your own creatures bigger based on how many Elves you’ve got. Then attack!
