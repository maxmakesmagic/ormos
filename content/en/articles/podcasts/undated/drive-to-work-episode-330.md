**Episode 330** Drive to Work #330—Storm Scale (17.6 MB)

In this podcast I explain what the Storm Scale is and how it will help you have a better sense of which mechanics might return in a Standard-legal set.

*Magic: The Gathering* Head Designer Mark Rosewater shares stories, insights, and more while driving to work. Listen in and learn more about Magic!
