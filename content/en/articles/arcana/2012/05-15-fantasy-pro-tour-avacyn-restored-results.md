That's a great ballot. The best one anyone came up with. But it's not the best one possible. For the theoretical maximum points, you'd want to swap out Curse of Death's Hold and Witchbane Orb for Triumph of Ferocity (worth 100 points) and Grafdigger's Cage (worth 190 points). And you'd probably also want to pick Alexander Hayne as your pro player, given that he [won the Pro Tour](/en/events/2012/ptavr12/angels-carry-hayne-to-miraculous-victory).
