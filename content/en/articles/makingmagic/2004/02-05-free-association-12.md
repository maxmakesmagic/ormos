The philosopher Descartes walks into a bar. As he sits at the bar, the bartender notices that Descartes is in a [bad mood](/en/articles/makingmagic/2004/02-05-free-association-61). Trying to cheer up his friend, he says, “Descartes, would you like a beer?”

Descartes looks up and says, “I think not.”

And then, he disappears!

  
  
  
  

If this joke doesn't make any sense to you, find a friend who took [philosophy](/en/articles/makingmagic/2004/02-05-free-association-51) in college.
