Like a draft deck full of Nim creatures, **Magic** artist Adam Rex is full of tricks.

When you line up the art from [Nim Lasher](https://web.archive.org/web/20040323000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nim+Lasher), [Nim Shrieker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040323000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nim+Shrieker), [Nim Devourer](https://web.archive.org/web/20040323000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nim+Devourer), and [Nim Shambler](https://web.archive.org/web/20040323000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nim+Shambler), you can see that they form one large mural.
