As mentioned earlier, a victory for Yuuya Watanabe here will cement him as the greatest Grand Prix master to have ever existed in the game's history. A win here will mark Watanabe's 7th victory, tying him with the legendary Kai Budde.

Sherwin Pu was not about to go down without a fight and seems to be in a favorable position. After all, his Boros Burn deck has been wrecking havoc in Beijing all weekend and Mono Black Devotion should have been within his metagame projection.

**Game One**

Pu was ready with [Magma Jet](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Jet) to deal with Watanabe's [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) before we could see it multiply. [Searing Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blood) seemed like the perfect answer to [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie).

[Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon) would prove to be problematic since it was difficult to remove without [Chained to the Rocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chained+to+the+Rocks). Pu took one hit, going to 14, but an end of turn [Warleader's Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warleader%27s+Helix) restored his life to 18.

Watanabe presented [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress), causing Pu to exclaim with mid amusement, "wow, maindeck!" It removed [Skullcrack](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullcrack), paving the way for any future Gray Merchants of Asphodel.

Watanabe had no intent of winning that way though, as he activated both his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) and charged into the red zone along with the demon, reducing Pu from 18 to 8 in one fell swoop. When Pu tried to tap down [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon) by sacrificing [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault), Watanabe put an end to that plan using [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall).

Since Watanabe was at 9 life himself, he declined to attack with [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault), because his opponent's [Searing Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blood) would potentially put him in danger zone. Playing it safe, he decided to spend two turns attacking with [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon) instead.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/f_watanabe.JPG)

*Yuuya Watanabe does the math and plays it safe to win Game One.*

 

**Sherwin Pu 0 – Yuuya Watanabe 1**

 

**Game Two**

Pu opened with [Firedrinker Satyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firedrinker+Satyr) and delivered a single punch before [Doom Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doom+Blade) sent it to the gallows. [Chandra's Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Phoenix) seemed like a good replacement and Watanabe's life was dripping away, two by two.

Watanabe wasted no time using double [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) to relieve him of [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm) and [Chained to the Rocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chained+to+the+Rocks), paving the way for Watanabe's [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon). However, Pu savagely ripped a replacement off the top, locking away the 6/6 flyer.

Watanabe cushioned his life total with a pair of [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel), but without other permanents in play, that only gained him a measly 6 life. Twelve life was not a lot when the duo of [Chandra's Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Phoenix) was dealing four a turn. The final kick was delivered by [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm).

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/f_pu.JPG)

 

Sherwin Pu rises from the ashes and wins Game Two, with the help of a pair of [Chandra's Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Phoenix)es.

 

**Sherwin Pu 1 – Yuuya Watanabe 1**

**Game Three**

With the tournament in its final throes, both players looked considerably tense. A Grand Prix title was at stake and every decision was crucial now. But sometimes, you don't get to to make many decisions.

Watanabe opened with Swamp, [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) and [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat).

Not having drawn any creature removal, Pu could only activate his own [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault), dealing two damage.

Pu watched in horror as Watanabe made a second copy, followed by the third. [Toil // Trouble](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Toil+%2F%2F+Trouble) and [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm) were not exactly useful in this situation.

A second [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) joined the horde and Watanabe could taste victory.

  
 "Gee, I came all the way here to lose to [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s." With that, Sherwin Pu extended his hand. Throwing his fists in the air, Watanabe cheered with joy. He had just won his seventh Grand Prix.

**Sherwin Pu 1 – Yuuya Watanabe 2**

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Pack+Rat)](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)

 

**Yuuya Watanabe defeats Sherwin Pu 2-1 and wins Grand Prix Beijing! Seventh time's the charm!**
