It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20111006000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=events/magic/fnm)! And since it's the first Friday in October, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

![Contagion Clasp](https://i.ormos.uk/94/45/944575d6b5bcec7b9672feecc02e5e09d7f82ab8db54b747f60722cd21bb63fa.jpg)  

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards often feature original flavor text and updated wordings.
