## Grand Prix Prague 2011

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e0/73/e073e72b0fa3e39784cb7ce8b42818dea6c7802c676ac5a4c987dbea4490084d.jpg)*Ondrej Baudys, your Grand Prix-Prague 2011 Champion!*

New Phyrexia came to Prague this weekend, and a highly-talented top 8 showed that this is a Limited format that is going to test the skills of the best players in the world at Pro Tour Nagoya next month.

Headlining the final eight was former Player of the Year Shuhei Nakamura, but he fell in the semi final to old-school Czech player Ondrej Baudys, a multiple National title-holder, with a pedigree at the highest level dating back to 1996.

Emerging from the other bracket was Lukas Blohon, with a Black-White deck headlined by [Enslave](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enslave) , [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember) , and [Mimic Vat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mimic+Vat) . Blohon took the opener, but the powerful Red-Black deck of Baudys made it a fight to the finish. In the decider, it was Baudys with his [Flesh-Eater Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flesh-Eater+Imp) who prevailed, poisoning in Prague for one devastating final time.

Fifteen years after his first high-level finish, the veteran had claimed a Grand Prix title.

Congratulations to Ondrej Baudys, Champion of Grand Prix Prague 2011!

## Top 8 – Drafting Feature: Lucas Blohon

*by David Sutcliffe* 

Despite the absence of Martin Juza, the strength of Czech **Magic** ensured that there were no less than three players flying the flag for the Czech Republic in the Top 8. With one of the three 'B's to choose from – Baudys, Brozek and Blohon – I settled down behind the Pro Tour regular Lucas Blohon to watch his draft. The Czech star had already made the Semi-Finals of the fictional Top 8 of 'Tap: Max's Game' but could he draft the deck that would add an actual Grand Prix Semi-Final?

 

### Pack One

The first pick proved to be a comfortable one for Blohon, and he took the Black 'Control Magic' – [Enslave](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enslave) , which proved an easy choice over a [Blind Zealot](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blind+Zealot) or [Razor Swine](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Razor+Swine) . Up next was more powerful removal, with [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember) , and then a trio of white cards; [Remember the Fallen](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remember+the+Fallen) and a pair of [Sensor Splicer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensor+Splicer) s. [Impaler Shrike](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Impaler+Shrike) was a brief distraction to blue, then it was back into artifacts that he knew could play, with [Pristine Talisman](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pristine+Talisman) , [Gremlin Mine](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gremlin+Mine) and [Hovermyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hovermyr) . An [Isolation Cell](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Isolation+Cell) came round late in a booster that still held a [Death-Hood Cobra](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death-Hood+Cobra) – Joel Larsson would collect that Cobra as his 14th pick!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3a/44/3a44296b7e4af06ec2be47c7eec602f6aa78474bd97fe269be69495870a4b2ec.jpg)*Lucas Blohon*

 

### Pack Two

After a first booster that had delivered some solid creatures and creature removal, the second pack began was less promisingly – a [Divine Offering](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divine+Offering) was fine, but [Inkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inkmoth+Nexus) was a pretty unexciting second pick that came from a booster that had a veritable bonanza of Red-Green riches. [Morbid Plunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morbid+Plunder) , [Ichor Wellspring](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichor+Wellspring) and [Virulent Wound](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Virulent+Wound) in his next few picks would all vie for a spot in his deck they would hardly strike terror into opponents. The packs finally came good as they started to wheel, with Blohon picking up a Copper Carapce, [Nested Ghoul](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nested+Ghoul) and [Dross Ripper](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dross+Ripper) . That meant the pack hadn't been a total misfire, and he rounded off his picks with a pair of [Flensermite](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flensermite) s and a Razorhead. Mirrodin Besieged hadn't been a total loss, but it could certainly have gone a lot better for his Black-White deck.

 

### Pack Three

Into the Scars of Mirrodin booster, then, but there was no [Carnifex Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Carnifex+Demon) in the rare slot and instead Blohon had to settle for a [Mimic Vat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mimic+Vat) . [Kemba's Skyguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kemba%27s+Skyguard) was preferred to a [Kuldotha Forgemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuldotha+Forgemaster) in his next pick, and then Blohon settled down to picking a succession of solid but uninspirational creatures – [Moriok Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moriok+Replica) , [Moriok Reaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moriok+Reaver) , [Corpse Cur](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Corpse+Cur) , [Soliton](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soliton) , [Loxodon Wayfarer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Wayfarer) . From the promising beginning of [Enslave](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enslave) and [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember) , Blohon's draft had gradually unravelled and he couldn't have been happy with the final result.

## Top 8: Pairings – Runners and Riders

*by David Sutcliffe* 

**A) Lucas Blohon (CZE) vs Lukasz Cichecki (POL)**

In the Lucas vs Lukasz deathmatch, Blohon was not happy about his chances.

"My deck is horrible!" he told me, as soon as I sat down to watch him build, "I don't know if I could have done anything differently thought, I can't remember a time when I could have switched to Green. Probably I could have done something differently, because it's been a long time since a draft went this badly. I'll have to ask somebody to see what I could have changed".

Blohon's Verdict: 4/10. Maybe a 5/10

Lukasz Cichecki was much happier with his White-Red deck, though, "It's not the best but it's ok. It's fine," he said with some understatement, "I had to take a Tezzeret, even though I'm not playing him – I just knew I couldn't beat him if I played against it. I do have Jor Kadeen the Prevailer though, that guy is awesome – I won so many games with him yesterday!"

Cichecki's Verdict: 7/10

**B) Anders Melin (SWE) vs Joel Larsson (SWE)**

The semi finals were only big enough for one Swede, and the two Scandinavians would have to battle it out between them, and both of them liked their decks – Larsson went first:

"I'm happy- I've got a good Blue-Green deck, and maybe a red splash for the Hellkite. Probably not though. I got a lot of good cards late, like I got a [Death-Hood Cobra](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death-Hood+Cobra) 14th pick, and then a [Halt Order](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Halt+Order) 14th pick. They're both in my deck, and I actually nearly took the [Halt Order](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Halt+Order) when it first came around – I think that card has got better in New Phyrexia because people are playing so many artifacts. I've got a lot of good creatures, so I like my chances".

Larsson's Verdict: 7/10

At first Anders Melin was not happy with his deck, managing only a curt "It may be good enough" but he seemed to have changed his mind once he'd finished building it. I watching him playing with the cards in his deck, and he seemed to have a fairly typical Green-Red deck of monsters and burn spells, although the curve seemed a little off, with a lot of four-drops. I left him to worry over the merits of including [Furnace Celebration](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Furnace+Celebration) and a [Gnathosaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gnathosaur) .

Melin's Verdict: 7/10

**C) Shuuhei Nakamura (JPN) vs Robert Jurkovic (SVK)**

"Shuuhei! Black White?"

"Yes"

"Happy?"

"No!"

The one thing I've learnt about Shuuhei is that he's a terrible judge of Draft decks. He always says they are bad, and they turn out to be really good. Or maybe he's just a very convincing liar. His deck featured what looked to me like some solid White and Black creatures, with bombs in the form of [Black Sun's Zenith](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Sun%27s+Zenith) and a [Chancellor of the Annex](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chancellor+of+the+Annex) .

Shuuhei's Verdict: 3/10 (you should probably just pretend he said 8 instead of 3, though)

Robert Jurkovic is, like Shuuhei, a man of few words. He had the classic Green-Black Infect deck, but unlike Shuuhei he was happy to offer that he thought it was good, "yeah I'm happy. It has the right cards in".

Jurkovic's Verdict: 7/10

**D) Petr Brozek (CZE) vs Ondrej Baudys (CZE)**

Czech on Czech, only one of these two could make it to the Semi-Finals.

Brozek didn't like his Blue-White deck, decreeing it "About four playables short" and it was easy to see why. His deck featured [True Conviction](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=True+Conviction) and the [Sword of War and Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+War+and+Peace) , but it had a distinct lack of flyers – trying to pilot a Blue-White deck along the ground would take some work from Brozek.

Brozek's Verdict: 6/10

Baudys felt similarly – he liked his Red-Black deck, but saw a problem; "I think my cards are really good, but from what I saw there were really good cards in the pool, so I think everyone else will have good cards too. My opponents will be the same as me". Baudys isn't a name we hear much of around Europe because he doesn't travel, but the three-time National Champion is one of the best players in the Czech Republic and would be a Top 8 contender who was used to the feature match pressure.

Baudys' Verdict: 6/10

That was how the players lined up, let's see how they got on!

## Top 5 Cards

Grand Prix Prague is in the books, and as is coverage tradition, we once again take a look at the Top 5 cards that helped to shape this weekend's storylines.

![Moltensteel Dragon](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Moltensteel+Dragon)

### 1\. [Moltensteel Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moltensteel+Dragon)

The first two times I sat down in the feature match arena yesterday, matches were decided largely by this card. The real strength of the Dragon is not that it's unbeatable, because clearly it isn't. Any number of removal spells exist which take care of the Dragon good and proper. Most of the creature removal and all of the artifact removal works just fine. The power of [Moltensteel Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moltensteel+Dragon) instead lies in its speed. Have an answer now, or die a fast, fiery death, the Dragon demands.

![Blinding Souleater](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Blinding+Souleater)

### 2\. [Blinding Souleater](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinding+Souleater)

Creatures with the ability to tap others have traditionally been powerhouses in all Limited formats. Always. They do have one minor flaw though. Their activation cost, at times, will be at odds with the reasonable wish to continue casting spells. [Blinding Souleater](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinding+Souleater) , with the help of New Phyrexia's brand-new mana innovation, avoids this issue elegantly.

![Corrupted Conscience](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Corrupted+Conscience)

### 3\. [Corrupted Conscience](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Corrupted+Conscience)

What's better than them having a monster? Them having no monster? Close, but not quite there yet. Indeed, it's *you* having *their* monster. No matter what creature, from [Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elesh+Norn%2C+Grand+Cenobite) to [Fangren Marauder](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fangren+Marauder) to [Blinding Souleater](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinding+Souleater) , when you play this (or one of its brethren [Volition Reins](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Volition+Reins) and [Enslave](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enslave) ) they will most likely join the winning side a.k.a. yours.

![Act of Aggression](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Act+of+Aggression)

### 4\. [Act of Aggression](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Act+of+Aggression)

[Blinding Souleater](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinding+Souleater) may need white mana, at least a little—this one really doesn't. [Act of Aggression](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Act+of+Aggression) has shown up throughout the weekend in so many decks which had a grand total of zero red mana sources ... in fact, I might have seen more copies in nonred decks than in actual red decks.

![Mycosynth Wellspring](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Mycosynth+Wellspring)

### 5\. [Mycosynth Wellspring](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mycosynth+Wellspring)

The influx of New Phyrexia led to more players going beyond the usual two colors. We've seen three, four, and even five colors in decks this weekend. [Mycosynth Wellspring](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mycosynth+Wellspring) is partly responsible. One thing to keep in mind: being able to sacrifice it is obviously preferable, but optional. The card provides fine mana fixing even without.

## Quarterfinals – Petr Brozek vs. Ondrej Baudys

*by Rich Hagon* 

In the one quarter final that's guaranteed to see a Czech player advance to the final four, Petr Brozek - representing the New - takes on Ondrej Baudys, representing the Old. Baudys has multiple National titles to his name, and was on the National Team at Worlds as long ago as 1996. Nowadays he rarely plays more than one high-level event each year, but he's certainly making his 2011 foray into global competition count.

### Game 1

Both players kept their opening hands, with Baudys showing Black-Red early, using [Mycosynth Wellspring](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mycosynth+Wellspring) to fetch a second [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain) on turn two. Brozek also had a popular turn two play, paying two life to cast [Porcelain Legionnaire](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Porcelain+Legionnaire) . That was promptly shattered by Baudys, leaving Brozek to start again with [Kemba's Skyguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kemba%27s+Skyguard) .

[Vulshok Refugee](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulshok+Refugee) was next for Baudys, while Brozek revealed his second color to be Blue when he laid an [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island) . [Pith Driller](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pith+Driller) is a card that frequently kills outright, but Baudys used it to halve the damage from [Kemba's Skyguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kemba%27s+Skyguard) . [Spire Serpent](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spire+Serpent) ensured a powerful ground defense for Brozek.

[Grim Affliction](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Affliction) changed the math, proliferating to kill the [Kemba's Skyguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kemba%27s+Skyguard) , and reducing the [Spire Serpent](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spire+Serpent) dramatically. Brozek had a riposte, however, using [Mutagenic Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutagenic+Growth) to save his Serpent, and kill [Vulshok Refugee](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulshok+Refugee) . Brozek improved his board position with [Soliton](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soliton) .

Baudys was struggling to get aggressive, and his next offering, [Trigon of Corruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trigon+of+Corruption) , looked to mean more ground stalemate. [Ichor Wellspring](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichor+Wellspring) drew him a card, but that was his only action, in a game that had yet to catch fire. However, [Gnathosaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gnathosaur) became a legitimate threat, especially as Baudys had the [Trigon of Corruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trigon+of+Corruption) to spread counters around.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4b/5e/4b5e08e31a42359691a07507973f6fb27b2be7761cf05f0ee717515ec9603967.jpg)*Petr Brozek*

Brozek cast [Tezzeret's Gambit](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tezzeret%27s+Gambit) before adding [Kuldotha Forgemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuldotha+Forgemaster) to the board, currently with no way to activate it. Gnathasaur gained trample when Baudys sacrificed his [Ichor Wellspring](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichor+Wellspring) for maximum value, then sent his 5/4 into the red zone. Five points of damage dropped Brozek to six, before [Invader Parasite](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Invader+Parasite) stole the lone [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island) from Brozek, who was suddenly looking in trouble.

A second [Porcelain Legionnaire](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Porcelain+Legionnaire) arrived, supported by [Gust-Skimmer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gust-Skimmer) , and now [Kuldotha Forgemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuldotha+Forgemaster) was active, if needed. [Moriok Reaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moriok+Reaver) and [Flayer Husk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flayer+Husk) joined the team for Baudys, and now the creatures in play were up to double figures. With no play from Brozek, Baudys took the chance to destroy [Porcelain Legionnaire](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Porcelain+Legionnaire) with his second counter from [Trigon of Corruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trigon+of+Corruption) , and with plenty of Black mana available, the long game would ensure he would never run out of counters.

[Mycosynth Wellspring](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mycosynth+Wellspring) sacrificed to [Gnathosaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gnathosaur) , with the final counter coming of [Trigon of Corruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trigon+of+Corruption) to kill Brozek's [Gust-Skimmer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gust-Skimmer) . S triple block from Brozek saw his [Kuldotha Forgemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuldotha+Forgemaster) die in exchange for the [Gnathosaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gnathosaur) , before Baudys landed [Batterskull](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Batterskull) . With Brozek passing the turn once more, it was time for an alpha strike from Baudys, who piled into the red zone with his team. Brozek's inactivity wasn't masking tricks of awesomeness, and it was time for game two.

Brozek 0 - 1 Baudys

### Game 2

Brozek opened game two with [Gust-Skimmer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gust-Skimmer) , while [Vulshok Refugee](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulshok+Refugee) answered for Baudys. The air assault began, with a backup [Kemba's Skyguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kemba%27s+Skyguard) ready to lend support. Not for long, though, as Baudys had [Slagstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slagstorm) to wipe the board, leaving his [Vulshok Refugee](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulshok+Refugee) to attack for three. Looking to reclaim some of the lost ground, Brozek cast Trasure Mage, searching up [Lumengrid Gargoyle](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lumengrid+Gargoyle) , the 4/4 flyer. With the job done, Brozek was happy to trade his [Treasure Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Mage) for the [Vulshok Refugee](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulshok+Refugee) .

Baudys cast [Kuldotha Ringleader](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuldotha+Ringleader) , facing off against [Kuldotha Forgemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuldotha+Forgemaster) for Brozek. The Ringleader had to attack, of course, but the Forgemaster and Ringleader merely bounced off each other, with no tricks on either side. Baudys added [Razor Swine](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Razor+Swine) , while Brozek had a first strike man of his own in the form of [Porcelain Legionnaire](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Porcelain+Legionnaire) .

[Shrine of Piercing Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrine+of+Piercing+Vision) completed the turn from Brozek.

First strike met first strike when Baudys attacked, [Razor Swine](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Razor+Swine) and [Porcelain Legionnaire](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Porcelain+Legionnaire) both heading for the graveyard. That left Ringleader and Forgemaster once again bumping heads to no meaningful effect. Brozek cast the [Lumengrid Gargoyle](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lumengrid+Gargoyle) he had found with his [Treasure Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Mage) , while Baudys flashed out a [Darksteel Sentinel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Sentinel) .

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e2/2e/e22ef8ce3be1b3d5dae08087df89b44693b04c8bab2d469db84a1a7323ea6e4e.jpg)*Ondrej Baudys*

The war of attrition continued, and it wasn't long before Lumegrid Gargoyle and [Kuldotha Forgemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuldotha+Forgemaster) for Brozek, and [Kuldotha Ringleader](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuldotha+Ringleader) for Baudys, were gone from play, leaving the [Darksteel Sentinel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Sentinel) alone, save for that troublesome [Trigon of Corruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trigon+of+Corruption) , which Baudys had just cast. Brozek began again with Spire Servant, his [Shrine of Piercing Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrine+of+Piercing+Vision) now up to four counters. Baudys wasted no time in casting [Flayer Husk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flayer+Husk) and Equipping it to a [Moriok Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moriok+Replica) .

Then came a game-changer - [Blue Sun's Zenith](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blue+Sun%27s+Zenith) for Brozek. He drew four cards, and now Baudys knew there was Inevitability on the other side of the table. ## Finals – Lucas Blohon vs. Ondrej Baudys

*by David Sutcliffe* 

We had an English champion in Grand Prix London, when Daniel Royde took the crown, and here in Prague we are going to have a Czech champion – all that remains to be decided is who that is; Lucas Blohon or Ondrej Baudys. The smart money was on Baudys – after watching his draft, Martin Juza had tipped Baudys for the title, and my fellow coverage guy Richard Hagon came away from having watched the three-time national champion's semi-final against Shuuhei Nakamura proclaiming "unless Blohon has a block constructed deck, he has no chance!"

If you remember my appraisal of Blohon's draft it wasn't too positive. Proving me wrong, Blohon had blown past the Grand Prix novice Lukasz Chicheski in his quarter final, before grinding Swede Joel Larsson into the ground with the raw hitting power of his Black-White deck. Finding himself in the final, could Blohon stand up to what Baudys was bringing?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3e/ba/3eba4d7cd7e96ef6786712d17ad3987af22ca3f0cd3879549454618e67e4d54e.jpg)

### Game 1

Baudys certainly made a rapid start to the final, with a [Flayer Husk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flayer+Husk) and [Ichor Wellspring](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichor+Wellspring) that he fed into his [Ferrovore](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ferrovore) , although Blohon had a [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember) waiting for the [Ferrovore](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ferrovore) once Baudys had finished drawing cards. Blohon deployed a [Soliton](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soliton) , which Baudys trumped with [Kuldotha Ringleader](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuldotha+Ringleader) , then Blohon played [Mimic Vat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mimic+Vat) . Taking time to read exactly what [Mimic Vat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mimic+Vat) did, Baudys shrugged and attacked with his [Kuldotha Ringleader](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuldotha+Ringleader) anyway, then added a [Invader Parasite](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Invader+Parasite) , exhiling Blohon's [Swamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swamp) .

On Baudys's next attack Blohon joined the forces of his [Soliton](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soliton) and a [Virulent Wound](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Virulent+Wound) to kill the Ringleader, Imprinting it to his [Mimic Vat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mimic+Vat) , then immediately generated a copy of the Ringleader and attacked back at Baudys. Ondrej Baudys countered the attack with a [Darksteel Sentinel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Sentinel) , but Blohon stole the Sentinel with [Enslave](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enslave) ! Between the [Mimic Vat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mimic+Vat) and the [Enslave](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enslave) , Blohon was beating his countryman up with his own creatures!

The pair played a [Moriok Reaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moriok+Reaver) each, and Baudys used a [Pith Driller](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pith+Driller) to shrink the treacherous [Darksteel Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Colossus) to a 2/2, but that barely slowed Blohon down – he produced another Ringleader copy from his [Mimic Vat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mimic+Vat) and the end of Baudys' turn, then untapped and created a second Ringleader! Sending his forces into the red zone, Blohon smashed Baudys down to 6 life!

Baudys needed to find a good response to this, because the [Mimic Vat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mimic+Vat) was threatening to steal the game, and the three-time champion went deep into the tank. Surfacing at last, Baudys attacked, trading his [Invader Parasite](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Invader+Parasite) for Blohon's [Moriok Reaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moriok+Reaver) , and Blohon used the opportunity to overwite the [Kuldotha Ringleader](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuldotha+Ringleader) on his [Mimic Vat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mimic+Vat) with the [Invader Parasite](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Invader+Parasite) . Baudys was clinging on as Blohon attacked again, but he could find no answer to either his own [Enslave](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enslave) d [Darksteel Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Colossus) , or Blohon's copies of his [Invader Parasite](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Invader+Parasite) .

Undone by his own creatures, Baudys conceded the first game.

Lucas Blohon 1 – 0 Ondrej Baudys

### Game 2

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c7/fd/c7fd04be0d8d1f7dfed5ec4cfa339af6f79e24170fdc0d1e0fe8ea20066c4d17.jpg)*Lucas Blohon*

Baudys was certainly the busiest of the two players at the start of the second game, playing a [Sphere of the Suns](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphere+of+the+Suns) , [Flayer Husk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flayer+Husk) , [Ichor Wellspring](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichor+Wellspring) and [Mycosynth Wellspring](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mycosynth+Wellspring) . While Baudys was busy playing out his artifacts and pulling up cards, Blohon was taking the more direct approach, playing a [Moriok Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moriok+Replica) and Reaper of Sheodred. Baudys played a [Kuldotha Ringleader](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuldotha+Ringleader) , but Blohon was in no mood to mess around and immediately took control of the Ringleader with [Enslave](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enslave) , then sent his team onto the attack, bashing Baudys down to 14 life, and up to 2 poison counters.

Baudys played [Invader Parasite](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Invader+Parasite) , but Blohon followed up with a [Mimic Vat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mimic+Vat) before attacking with everything. The Ringleader traded with both Baudys' [Flayer Husk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flayer+Husk) and [Invader Parasite](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Invader+Parasite) , meaning that [Enslave](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enslave) had accomplished an impressive 3-for-1 trade, even better for Blohon - the Replica and Reaper slipped past, putting Baudys down to 10 life and up to 5 poison!

Baudys produced a [Razor Swine](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Razor+Swine) , and seeing the ground path blocked Blohon abandoned his [Moriok Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moriok+Replica) to draw cards, playing a [Kemba's Skyguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kemba%27s+Skyguard) . A [Trigon of Corruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trigon+of+Corruption) began to whittle the Skyguard down, and then Baudys' next play handed the three-time champ a lifeline, or more accurately it handed him [Lifelink](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifelink) – a [Batterskull](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Batterskull) !

But Baudys was faced with a thorny problem – Lucas Blohon had the Reaper of Sheodred, and anything that dealt lethal damage to the Reaper would also mean Baudys picked up a lethal number of poison counters. The ground war was at a stalemate – Ondrej Baudys couldn't attack while the Reaper was ready to block, and Blohon couldn't attack with the Reaper because the [Razor Swine](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Razor+Swine) would Infect it down to a 0/3 that Baudys could ignore. The two players sat down to wait it out.

After a couple of turns of stalemate the stall was finally broken by Ondrej Baudys, who used a Gross Affliction to create a 3-for-1 trade of his own from Blohon's ground forces, amplifying the effect of the [Trigon of Corruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trigon+of+Corruption) to clear the way. With the [Reaper of Sheoldred](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reaper+of+Sheoldred) finished, Baudys could begin to exert his authority and began attacking with his [Batterskull](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Batterskull) - Baudys's health spiralled upwards as rapidly as Blohon's headed in the opposite direction and within a few turns the match was levelled.

Lucas Blohon 1 – 1 Ondrej Baudys

![](https://i.ormos.uk/75/13/7513df887300e0d9855f05c4d63f0668b7bedfeb75163ad566ee80fc5e4f3abb.jpg)*Ondrej Baudys*

### Game 3

The early game was once again dictated by Ondrej Baudys' creatures, as he was fastest to get out of the blocks with a [Moriok Reaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moriok+Reaver) , [Flesh-Eater Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flesh-Eater+Imp) and [Ferrovore](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ferrovore) . Blohon bought some time with a [Loxodon Wayfarer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Wayfarer) then played his [Razorfield Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Razorfield+Rhino) as a 6/6 with Metalcraft, adding a [Moriok Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moriok+Replica) alongside his hefty Rhino.

Baudys' early offense had made little impact on Blohon's lifetotal, and indeed Blohon's [Pristine Talisman](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pristine+Talisman) had put him up to 23 life, although he hadn't found an answer to the [Flesh-Eater Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flesh-Eater+Imp) , and had 4 poison counters. Despite Baudys playing an [Invader Parasite](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Invader+Parasite) , Blohon's [Razorfield Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Razorfield+Rhino) was now the dominant force on the table, and after one attack had dropped Baudys to 14, the three-time champ went back to the tank to find an answer.

Deciding that offense was the best form of defense, Baudys attacked with everything. Blohon blocked the ground creatures down but could do nothing about the [Flesh-Eater Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flesh-Eater+Imp) , which went unblocked. Sacrificing all his creatures to the [Flesh-Eater Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flesh-Eater+Imp) to make it a 5/5, Baudys put Blohon on 9 poison counters, then played a [Virulent Wound](https://web.archive.org/web/20170721051937/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Virulent+Wound) – the Proliferate added a final, lethal poison counter, and Baudys had won the final of Grand Prix Prague in a swift and decisive fashion!

**Lucas Blohon 1 – 2 Ondrej Baudys**

Ondrej Baudys is the champion of Grand Prix Prague 2011!
