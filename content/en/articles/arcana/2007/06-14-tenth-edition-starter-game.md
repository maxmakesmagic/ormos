[**Magic** Game Day](https://web.archive.org/web/20070614000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/07gameday), if you haven't heard, is coming July 14th. It's a great day to kick back, take in some *Tenth Edition*, smash face with black-bordered versions of your old favorites, and celebrate the Big X.

It's also an awesome time to introduce a friend or two to **Magic**. This year we've created these two-player starter products. Here, choose one. Go ahead, click on whichever one appeals to you.
