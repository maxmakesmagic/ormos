The upcoming *Ravnica: City of Guilds* set features four pre-constructed theme decks, each themed around one of Ravnica's guilds.

The second one is called **Dimir Intrigues**, and it looks a little something like this:

![Ravnica Theme Deck 2 of 4](https://i.ormos.uk/e8/6c/e86c44bdecb7fb09cb2a8bf675db437dd2830753d5948726efa62e84dbc9b833.jpg "Ravnica Theme Deck 2 of 4")  
*Ravnica: City of Guilds theme deck 2 of 4*

*Ravnica: City of Guilds* releases October 7, 2005. To get your hands on *Ravnica* any sooner than that, you'll have to attend the [prerelease](https://web.archive.org/web/20080416125625/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/prereleases), coming to a venue near you September 24-25.
