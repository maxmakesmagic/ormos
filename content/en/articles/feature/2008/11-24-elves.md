Elves were all the rage coming out of [Pro Tour-Berlin](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207083212/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/502416) but headed to North Carolina States, Nathaniel Muesing had the tribe in mind for Standard. His build, running a shy 16 lands, overwhelmed opponents with efficient green creatures like [Wolf-Skull Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20081124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wolf-Skull+Shaman), [Nettle Sentinel](https://web.archive.org/web/20081124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nettle+Sentinel), and [Imperious Perfect](https://web.archive.org/web/20081124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Imperious+Perfect). His late game was shored up with [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20081124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun), huge amounts of Elf tokens via [Elvish Promenade](https://web.archive.org/web/20081124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Promenade), and even drawing a ton of cards with [Regal Force](https://web.archive.org/web/20081124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Regal+Force). You can find Nathaniel’s list and more in the [2008 State Championship archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20081124000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/event/champs08/all).
