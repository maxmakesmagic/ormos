"You Make The Card" in which our readers get to vote on all the aspects of a card that will appear in a real expansion set in 2002, is in full swing.

The color has been determined (green), and voting for the card's type has been going on for a week. As of Tuesday afternoon, "creature" had a wide lead over the field. The totals (in order):

Voting will run through midnight Sunday night. If you haven't voted yet, do so by clicking the You Make The Card icon at the top of our [front page](https://web.archive.org/web/20080503034350/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/welcome). Tell your friends to vote as well! Or, [discuss it on our boards](https://web.archive.org/web/20020213000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://boards.wizards.com/tcg-bin/ultimatebb.cgi?ubb=get_topic&f=28&t=007843&origin=mtgcom_arcana_30)!
