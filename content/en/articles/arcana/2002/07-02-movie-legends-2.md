Anthony's [column](/en/articles/seriousfun/2002/07-02-limiting-your-bad-format) today is about the "Backdraft" format, which reminded us of the *Legends* card Backdraft, which reminded us of the movie *Backdraft*, which reminded us that *Legends* has a ton of cards that share names with movies. Among them: Field of Dreams, Cocoon, The Abyss, Raging Bull, and, of course, Backdraft.

![Field of Dreams](http://www.wizards.com/global/images/mtgcom_arcana_129_pic1_en.jpg)

We figured the number of card/movie pairs to be around 30, but Bob Flaminio did much more research than we did -- he has the number at 45 for just *Legends* alone! You can see Bob's complete list of **Magic** movie names here.
