### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, six 15-card booster packs, two premium cards, the *Heretic: Betrayers of Kamigawa* novel, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e4/51/e4513be10068a9c2bd6b70d39aaa8f355d2ed0a4e62e7b52926513b64e7348b3.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e4/51/e4513be10068a9c2bd6b70d39aaa8f355d2ed0a4e62e7b52926513b64e7348b3.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.
