The [2014 World *Magic* Cup](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2014-world-magic-cup) concluded with what the community has dubbed Daneblast—an auspicious [Duneblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141209000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duneblast) topdeck that let Simon Nielsen snatch victory from the jaws of defeat. While I've looked at [Duneblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141209000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duneblast)'s art in [an Arcana](/en/articles/arcana/2014/10-15-the-art-of-making-friends-in-multiplayer) already, this gave me the opportunity to not only give it another look but to bring back a series started by Trick last year: the artist focus.

Today, let's look at the eight pieces of Tarkir art done by [Ryan Alexander Lee](https://web.archive.org/web/20141209000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+[%22Ryan+Alexander+Lee%22]).
