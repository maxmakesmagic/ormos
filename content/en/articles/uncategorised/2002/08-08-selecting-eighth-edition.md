So, what did the public consider more important in a red mass destruction spell? A reasonable mana cost, or assurance that it would resolve? The nostalgia of an old-school favorite, or the excitement of introducing a new mechanic into the base set? Whatever the reason, "cannot be countered" seems to have won the hearts and minds of **Magic** fans everywhere, and Obliterate will be reprinted in the next base set. 

In the art department, the *Seventh Edition* "running down the hall" Steal Artifact beat out the *Fifth Edition* "pilfering the shriveled imp" Steal Artifact in a very close decision.

This week's card vote is a little more lighthearted. If you wanted make all your lands into 2/2 creatures, how would you do it? With the instant Natural Affinity, for a surprise attack (or block), or with the enchantment Nature's Revolt, for a more lasting effect?

![Nature's Revolt vs. Natural Affinity](https://i.ormos.uk/53/08/530817c91d2559f14ae3f339d15163b7ecc0c4bffd4aa64cdab0f151d42107bc.jpg)

This week's art vote is a three-pronged race between three of the more recent versions of the red instant Shatter, one from *Fifth Edition*, one from *Tempest*, and one from *Seventh Edition*.

![Shatter](https://i.ormos.uk/63/de/63de7a9899389e4c6b74185a4e33faad1ae655cbe87a365b0c7f32b5cdcffb96.jpg)

For this week's flavor text submission, we'll be focusing on the blue enchantment Confiscate, which will appear in![Confiscate](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/features/79_confiscate.jpg)*Eighth Edition* with the old *Urza's Saga* art. And just like Persecute before it, we want *real-world literary quotes.* Remember that the sourcematerial must be in the public domain, which generally means 100 years or more old. Please, attribute the quote so that we can fact-check all submissions. You can submit here.

And that's that for this week… Remember that your vote counts! Be sure to cast it [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20050312043028/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/vote8e/home), and tune in next week for another exciting set of choices!
