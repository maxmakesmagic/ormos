## GRAND PRIX SEVILLE 2015

TOP 8 COVERAGE

It's been an amazing weekend of *Magic* here in sunny Seville, Spain. 799 players had entered the tournament Saturday morning, full of hopes for fame and glory, but by nightfall all but 90 of them had already been eliminated. Sunday's rounds further reduced that number to eight. The eight players who battled it out in the playoffs included (19) Martin Jůza in his 22nd career Top 8, Marcio Carvalho in his eighth, and Immanuel Gerschenson in his second.

The Austrian had previously won Grand Prix Madrid just three months ago, and he claimed his second trophy here. The format then had been Modern, but Gerschenson proved just as strong in Standard, piloting his Blue/Black Control past all contenders, including old favorites like Abzan and Jeskai, but also decks like Red/White Beatdown and Green Devotion, which have been revitalized with the new toys from Fate Reforged.

Congratulations to Immanuel Gerschenson, champion of Grand Prix Seville 2015!

DAY 2 COVERAGE

Welcome back to Day 2 of Grand Prix Seville 2015! After yesterday's nine rounds of Standard play, the original field of 799 has been whittled down to just 90 players, now returning for six more rounds of Standard. Afterwards, we're off to the Top 8 to witness the brutal battles over elimination or advancement, for fame, glory, Pro Points, money, and the title of Grand Prix champion!

So far throughout the tournament, we've seen all kinds of interesting cards and decks. The biggest stories to come out of Day 1 were the rise of Red/White Beatdown and the revival of Green Devotion and blue-black control decks. As far as *Fate Reforged* cards are concerned, we’ve mainly seen Crux of Fate, Outpost Siege, Tasigur, the Golden Fang, Valorous Stance, Whisperwood Elemental, and Wild Slash dominate in the feature match area.

Join us now as we delve deeper into the format, as we bring you all the news, all the insights, all the action, and all the excitement straight from the Spanish battlefield!

DAY 1 COVERAGE

Last weekend, Spain just crowned its first Pro Tour champion with Antonio Del Moral León. This weekend marks Spain’s victory lap tournament, and after nine rounds of Standard play, Day One is in the books. Out of the original 799 players, 90 have earned the right to come back tomorrow and compete for the title of Grand Prix Seville Champion.

We’ve seen all kinds of interesting cards and decks competing today. The biggest stories to come out of Day 1 were the rise of R/W Aggro and the revitalization of Green Devotion and U/B Control decks. As far as Fate Reforged cards are concerned, we’ve mainly seen Crux of Fate, Outpost Siege, Tasigur, Valorous Stance, Whisperwood Elemental, and Wild Slash dominate in the feature match area.

Leading the field overnight with perfect records of 9-0 are Aitor Cabezas Blanco from Spain and Matt Light from England. But despite their great starts, these players will need to prove themselves tomorrow as we have freshly crowned Pro Tour winner Antonio Del Moral León and plenty of other top players lurking behind them with 8-1 records.

Join us here tomorrow for more text and video coverage as we see who of the 90 remaining competitors can successfully navigate through six more rounds of Standard to make it to the Top 8! The livestream will start at approximately 9:00 am CET.
