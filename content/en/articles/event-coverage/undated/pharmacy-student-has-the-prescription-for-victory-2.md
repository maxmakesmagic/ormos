Fighting his way through a field of emerging talent and longtime **Magic** greats, Toronto's own Andrew Ting-A-Kee took home the gold. His testing focused far more on constructed than limited, his decision to go with Blue-White-Red Blink paid dividends. He heads up a National Team that hails from all across the country, and this may prove to be the incentive that finally gets Ting-A-Kee onto **Magic** *Online*. Congratulations to Andrew Ting-A-Kee, 2007 Canadian National Chamion!

## BLOG

  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/87/0b/870bbf716ebaf501ad331d802c72606384ab0d7e29506f61bbb48f0a3e697a36.jpg)*Nick Page*

"I brought my wax, in case I make top 8." And then he gave his moustache a twirl. I'm not exaggerating. He gathered the corner of his moustache, and twisted it, impishly.

Page sent back his first seven, and Rider did the same. His six were no better.

"And there goes the crowd." - Josh Rider

After all those mulligans it was slow going. Page played a third-turn [Chronatog Totem](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chronatog+Totem). Rider had a [Spiketail Drakeling](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spiketail+Drakeling), and allowed Page to have his [Primal Plasma](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primal+Plasma). It became a 2/2 Flier. Rider swung in and Page attempted a trade, but Rider had [Brute Force](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brute+Force) for the save. Page upgraded to Whipspine Drake, but Rider had [Stingscourger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stingscourger) to send it home.

Page played a plains and a morph, pretending like it wasn't the Drake. Rider paid to keep his [Stingscourger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stingscourger) and passed the turn. Page flipped his Drake over, and tapped six for [Reality Strobe](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reality+Strobe) on the Drakeling. Rider shrugged and countered it. He replaced it with [Prodigal Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prodigal+Pyromancer).

And then began a parade of enormous monsters from Page. First there was a kicked up [Kavu Primarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kavu+Primarch). Then, [Imperiosaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Imperiosaur). A [Havenwood Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Havenwood+Wurm) was enough to convince Rider to move to Game 2.

Page 1 - Rider 0

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ae/c7/aec7c99d1eebd11a2bbfb948812c8e10a15e54c5166ed9fd9b9d2b1c755eafbe.jpg)*Josh Rider*

Again the players started with Drakeling and [Chronatog Totem](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chronatog+Totem). Rider added a morph and hit for two, then [Lightning Axe](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Axe)d Page's Whipspine Drake. He untapped, swung in, and unmorphed a 5/1 [Aquamorph Entity](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aquamorph+Entity), hoping to make a quick game of it.

Page untapped and showed him [Piracy Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Piracy+Charm), cutting down his hopes. The Drakeling was sacrificed to stop an incoming [Havenwood Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Havenwood+Wurm). When Page passed again on seven mana, Rider said "I was told the second one was the real surprise." Page obliged him by playing it, then followed up with a kicked [Kavu Primarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kavu+Primarch).

Rider had meanwhile dug with [Fathom Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fathom+Seer) and set up a [Flamecore Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flamecore+Elemental) as fall guy. His only hope was that he'd peel a plains to go with his emergency sideboard plan of [Teferi's Moat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%27s+Moat), but Page removed even that hope with [Tromp the Domains](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tromp+the+Domains).

"Such is my life." - Josh Rider

Nick Page defeats Josh Rider 2-0

  

## Sunday, Sept. 9: 1:29 p.m. - Round 9: Yi Cheng vs. Matt Vienneau

*by Josh Bennett*

 

Cheng started with a Thornwield Archer and a [Poultice Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Poultice+Sliver). Vienneau suspended a [Giant Dustwasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Giant+Dustwasp) and then put out [Grinning Ignus](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grinning+Ignus), trading for the Sliver. Cheng replaced it with [Pallid Mycoderm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pallid+Mycoderm). Vienneau played [Llanowar Empath](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Empath), fetching another Ignus. Cheng hit with the Mycoderm, but made no play on his five mana.

Vienneau attacked for two and played his Ignus. This time Cheng sent both his men, using [Dawn Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dawn+Charm) to save his Archer from the Ignus, but he still had no more men to add to the board. Vienneau's Dustwasp came into play, joined by [Wormwood Dryad](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wormwood+Dryad) and a 3/3 [Storm Entity](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Storm+Entity), and he crashed in for eight. Cheng had [Judge Unworthy](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge+Unworthy) for the Wasp. Luck was not with him. His top three were misses, and the fourth was a land.

[Arc Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Blade) took care of the Archer, and by extension, Cheng.

Vienneau 1 - Cheng 0

[Terramorphic Expanse](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terramorphic+Expanse) enabled a second-turn [Deathspore Thallid](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathspore+Thallid) for Cheng, but despite all three colours of mana he made no third-turn play. Vienneau suspended Dustwasp and then [Arc Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Blade). Cheng played his fourth land hit for one, and then passed it back.

Vienneau made a [Flowstone Channeler](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flowstone+Channeler). Cheng answered with Spoorloth Ancient. Vienneau played an Ignus and held his ground. Cheng swung in with both, and Vienneau put Channeler in front of the [Thallid](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thallid), but [Strength in Numbers](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strength+in+Numbers) bowled it over. The Ancient's ability allowed the [Thallid](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thallid) to generate two Saprolings and kill of the Ignus.

The Wasp and Blade came off suspension, killing the [Deathspore Thallid](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathspore+Thallid), and [Empty the Warrens](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Empty+the+Warrens) provided six goblins. Cheng played [Pallid Mycoderm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pallid+Mycoderm), but Vienneau kept up the pressure, a [Shivan Meteor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Meteor) allowing his crew to strike. Cheng's last two mana meant [Judge Unworthy](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge+Unworthy) on the Wasp, and this time he found Lucent Luminid to kill it off.

He untapped and put out Teneb. Vienneau was ready with [Utopia Vow](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Utopia+Vow), and swung in again, but Cheng put the brakes on him with [Deadwood Treefolk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deadwood+Treefolk) getting back the Ancient.

Now the game was swinging away from Vienneau. He suspended a [Nantuko Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Shaman) while Cheng put out his Ancient. He made a Goblin SKycutter, but his board was way outclassed. Cheng sent his men, and foiled a Vienneau double-block on the ancient by creating a Saproling and sacrificing it to the Mycoderm.

"Well, I screwed that up." - Matt Vienneau

Cheng played a morph and passed it back. [Evolution Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Evolution+Charm) regrew Dust wasp, and Vienneau played it. Unfortunately for him, Cheng's morph was [Thelonite Hermit](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thelonite+Hermit), overwhelming him.

Vienneau 1 - Cheng 1

Cheng had a turn-one [Essence Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Essence+Warden) and a [Scryb Ranger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scryb+Ranger) he traded immediately for Vienneau's [Goblin Skycutter](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Skycutter), but no third turn play. Nor Vienneau, who spend his mana making a kobold with [Kher Keep](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kher+Keep). Cheng made a [Goldmeadow Lookout](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goldmeadow+Lookout) that fell to Dead, and replaced it with [Pallid Mycoderm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pallid+Mycoderm).

Vienneau played a [Grinning Ignus](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grinning+Ignus), stuck on three land. When it survived the turn, he used it to bring out [Stonebrow, Krosan Hero](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stonebrow%2C+Krosan+Hero). Cheng's Warden had put a buffer on his life total, but the big centaur started taking bites out of it. Vienneau replayed the Ignus and at last a fourth land. Cheng had no play, and suffered another six for it.

Finally he hit six mana, which meant that it was Teneb time, and Cheng made a warding gesture, hoping potential asnwers were stuck at the bottom of Vienneau's deck. Unfortunately, he had the Vow in hand. He also used [Evolution Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Evolution+Charm) to fetch a second forest.

[Deadwood Treefolk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deadwood+Treefolk) recurred the Lookout and provided the necessary incentive for Vienneau to keep Stonebrow at home. He made [Fomori Nomad](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fomori+Nomad)e and passed the turn. Cheng tried the Lookout again, but this time [Arc Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Blade) sent it packing.

Cheng untapped and dropped [Magus of the Abyss](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magus+of+the+Abyss). Together with his fading [Deadwood Treefolk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deadwood+Treefolk) it meant another shot at an active Teneb. Vienneau watched him get back the Dragon. [Shivan Meteor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Meteor) was waiting in his hand, but Cheng had [Dawn Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dawn+Charm), and now things began to get very ugly indeed.

Teneb swung and resurrected the Treefolk, which in turn brought back the Lookout. To his credit, Vienneau didn't so much as slump.

And then, Cheng decided to get greedy.

He played a [Whitemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whitemane+Lion) at the end of Vienneau's turn, bouncing itself for the one life from the Warden, leaving him tapped out. Vienneau seized the opportunity with [Riddle of Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Riddle+of+Lightning) on Teneb. No big deal, right? Even if he killed it off, the Treefolk would start the whole dance over again. Vienneau checked his top three, no help there. He flipped over the fourth card.

[Scourge of Kher Ridges](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scourge+of+Kher+Ridges).

The clock was ticking, very little time remained in the match. Cheng sacrificed the Treefolk to the Abyss to get back Teneb and put it into play. Vienneau untapped and cashed in his two Ignuses for the Scourge, with mana left over to [Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm). Cheng untapped and got rid of his Magus. He hit with Teneb and brought back the Treefolk, and then replayed the Magus.

Now Vienneau had all his mana. He dropped an Ignus and blew Teneb out of the sky on Cheng's turn, forcing [Whitemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whitemane+Lion) and a replayed Dragon. He untapped untapped, and used all his mana to blow up the sky and the ground, clearing the board except for his Scourge and hitting for six. As he sped through the turn, time was called. The extra turns would start with Cheng.

This was a problem, because Vienneau's victory in the air needed three attacks. He called for the head judge to ask for an extension. While the table judge fetched him, Vienneau explained "I really don't want a draw, and I don't think you can win here." Cheng begged to differ, showing him the [Magus of the Abyss](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magus+of+the+Abyss) he had gotten back when his Treefolk died. Vienneau looked down at his tapped out lands, including the [Kher Keep](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kher+Keep) that could have made the save and said "Oh."

The Head Judge concluded that because the pace of play had been acceptable, there would be no extension. Cheng played his Magus and a Lucent Luminid and handed it back to Vienneau. Vienneau's outbound Scourge sent them both home. On turn three of five, Cheng played a morph. Vienneau's deck gave him [Empty the Warrens](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Empty+the+Warrens), which with his Ignus meant ten goblins ready to stand in the way of the attackers. His life total was two. Cheng swung in on the final turn and after Vienneau blocked, showed [Strength in Numbers](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strength+in+Numbers) to trample over for the win.

Yi Cheng defeats Matt Vienneau 2-1

  

## Sunday, Sept. 9: 2:41 p.m. - Round 12: Alex Kenny vs. Miles Smith

*by Josh Bennett*

 

It was make-or-break time for Alex Kenny and Miles Smith. Their tiebreakers meant that the winner of their match would have a spot in the Top 8. Kenny was with Dredge, and Smith was with blue-white-red Blink.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c9/f2/c9f27db10cafb4873972692c50861fe24cd58417b79959c2aa56ad78a4249da8.jpg)*Miles Smith*

Kenny mulled to five, and stalled on two lands, with just a [Narcomoeba](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narcomoeba) to his name. Smith played an end-of-turn [Sulfur Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sulfur+Elemental), untapped and dropped [Lightning Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Angel), swinging for six. Kenny drew his third land and played [Bonded Fetch](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bonded+Fetch). Smith had a Helix for it on his turn, but it still allowed Kenny to get a Troll into the graveyard. Kenny again declined to sacrifice the [Narcomoeba](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narcomoeba) and fell to 8.

Kenny dredged the Troll, scoring a second [Narcomoeba](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narcomoeba), as well as two [Bridge from Below](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bridge+from+Below). A [Bonded Fetch](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bonded+Fetch) let him dredge a second time, this time putting [Dread Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dread+Return) into the yard. He flashed it back for a 7/7 Troll and six zombie tokens. His mull to five had turned around.

Any hope was immediately extinguished as Smith showed him Demonfire to go with his Angel, dealing the final eight.

Smith 1 - Kenny 0

Kenny led with Drowned Rusalka, but stalled on one land. He sacrificed it, putting Grave-Troll in the yard and snagging a second land. He dredged back the Troll, getting [Narcomoeba](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narcomoeba) and a [Bridge from Below](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bridge+from+Below). He played Looter and passed it back. Smith meanwhile had a pair of Signets and a Court Hussar.

Kenny continued to loot and dredge, getting up to all four [Narcomoeba](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narcomoeba)s in play eventually. Smith's draw was not very busy. He had a Blink for Hussar to get rid of the Bridge, and a [Sulfur Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sulfur+Elemental). Kenny's library was dwindling. He dredged a second Bridge into his 'yard, as well as a [Dread Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dread+Return). He flashed back [Dread Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dread+Return), getting three tokens, but Smith had Venser to stop it.

The three tokens provided the fuel for a second [Dread Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dread+Return) which got him Drowned Rusalka. He sacrificed [Narcomoeba](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narcomoeba) to Rusalka and dredged, hitting two more Bridges. His three creatures in play was enough for a third Return, getting six zombies and a Flame-Kin Zealot, dealing enough to take the game.

Smith 1 - Kenny 1

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b0/6f/b06fda741a90cccd05dd2abe34349473f074bcccf7ffb9ab6779bd72b76e3f64.jpg)*Alex Kenny*

After a mulligan from Kenny, he led out with Merfolk Looter, which fell to Lightning Helix. His next play was a Stinkweed Imp. Smith stalled on three mana for a turn, taking two before he found a blue mana to get [Lightning Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Angel) online. Kenny held his Imp back and played [Magus of the Bazaar](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magus+of+the+Bazaar). Another Helix pushed it off the board.

Smith made a second Angel and hit with both, trading with Stinkweed Imp. Kenny dredged the Imp, but got no action, so he replayed the Imp and passed the turn. Cloudskate sent it back to hand and Smith got in for another three. Kenny had nothing more than to try the Imp again, but Blink sent it home and Smith got in for five.

By now Kenny had resigned himself to the futility of the Imp. Smith showed him a second Cloudskate to speed up his inevitable demise.

Miles Smith defeats Alex Kenny 2-1

## Sunday, Sept. 9: 5:33 p.m. - Photo Essay

*by Josh Bennett*

"I'd say 'May the best player win,' but I want to win." - Andrew Ting-A-Kee

![](https://i.ormos.uk/51/83/5183f3bcd0794d6dc4e2161f00f7242a22fdcaf6bc160c45ee73cbddf91998fb.jpg)*Shaun Mclaren*

So began the last match of the 2007 Canadian National Championships.

Ting-A-Kee started with a suspended Cloudskate which counted down while both players built mana with Signets. [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand) on McLaren's second slowed him down a little. Ting-A-Kee dug with [Court Hussar](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Court+Hussar), but McLaren showed him how it was done, firing off a pair of [Compulsive Research](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsive+Research)es, discarding land, Signet, and [Firemane Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firemane+Angel).

The Cloudskate returned a Boilerworks, further cutting into McLaren's mana production, and Ting-A-Kee hit for three. He [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand)ed a [Wrath of God](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wrath+of+God), but had to let it through on the following turn. He passed his turn on an empty board. McLaren suspended a Chronicler for three.

Ting-A-Kee continued to draw a wealth of land and a shortness of threats. He [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand)ed a [Lightning Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Angel). McLaren played a third [Compulsive Research](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsive+Research). His hand was overstuffed. Again, Ting-A-Kee found a blank, and this time the Angel had a friend. Ting-A-Kee decided his best course of action was to go to the dome. Helix, and then [Demonfire](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonfire) for eleven, put McLaren to seven.

The problem with this plan was twofold. One, the plan hinged on a second [Demonfire](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonfire) that he had yet to draw. Secondly, Ting-A-Kee was at sixteen after the Helix, and the incoming Chronicler and Angels would put him to two. So he would need McLaren to be holding eight blanks.

McLaren was not, in fact, holding eight blanks.

McLaren 1 - Ting-A-Kee 0

In the second game, Ting-A-Kee's deck remembered that it was supposed to do something. He had [Avalanche Riders](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avalanche+Riders) for McLaren's [Izzet Boilerworks](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Izzet+Boilerworks), and then a [Cryoclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryoclasm) on his island. This was just overture for his [Lightning Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Angel). McLaren had a Hussar and then had to pay two life for an untapped [Hallowed Fountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallowed+Fountain) in order to Wrath.

Ting-A-Kee another Angel in the wings. McLaren was at seven. When he tried a second [Wrath of God](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wrath+of+God), Ting-A-Kee flipped over his last card, a Venser. He called for a topdeck. "Something with Fire or Lightning."

He drew his card.

"Or Avalanche."

The Riders made it seven exactly.

McLaren 1 - Ting-A-Kee 1

![](https://i.ormos.uk/16/aa/16aa78ae60119390fa8ca66d09fc283790c9e6ce15c87b87298415b457815088.jpg)*Andrew Ting-A-Kee*

"Six lands and Blink," said Ting-A-Kee, shipping his hand back.

"I heard you kept six lands and [Dodecapod](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dodecapod)." - Shaun McLaren

"And won. To clinch Top 8." - Andrew Ting-A-Kee

"You're the worst." - Shaun McLaren

"Hey, he played turn two [Ravenous Rats](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ravenous+Rats)!" - Andrew Ting-A-Kee

It was mulligans all around, two for McLaren, one for Ting-A-Kee. McLaren undid some of the damage with an opening of Signet, karoo and [Compulsive Research](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsive+Research). Ting-A-Kee suspended a Cloudskate and made a Hussar. McLaren summoned [Lightning Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Angel) and swung in.

Ting-A-Kee wasn't fazed. [Cryoclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryoclasm) busted up [Steam Vents](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steam+Vents), McLaren's only source of red. The Angel got one more hit before Cloudskate sent it home. The Hussar and Cloudskate evened the scores at ten apiece. He added another Hussar and after a moment's hesitation Blinked the Cloudskate to bounce McLaren's karoo. McLaren was left with a board of two [Azorius Signet](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azorius+Signet)s and a [Hallowed Fountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallowed+Fountain).

McLaren held on for a turn, but there was no hope.

McLaren 1 - Ting-A-Kee 2

McLaren made the standard opening of Signet and Hussar, and lost a land to [Cryoclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryoclasm). He didn't have [Lightning Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Angel) for the followup, however, and had to spend two idle turns. Ting-A-Kee stumbled on one of then when he needed white mana and found only [Flagstones of Trokair](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flagstones+of+Trokair), which turned into a tapped land thanks the the copy on McLaren's side of the board.

The following turn he finally got out a [Lightning Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Angel). McLaren tried to outclass it with [Firemane Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firemane+Angel), but [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand) stopped that. Ting-A-Kee untapped and Vensered the Signet back to McLaren's hand, buying more turns without the Angel in play. McLaren replayed the Signet and Wrathed the board.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2d/a3/2da3d98d3cf93b609fb74d187f1c7a0f2a9756f54744c2fb7a37f00750983db9.jpg)*The 2007 Canada National Team*

Ting-A-Kee's hand was all gas, however. He had another [Lightning Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Angel) and a [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand) for the next [Wrath of God](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wrath+of+God). [Avalanche Riders](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avalanche+Riders) came down to crack an untapped land. McLaren pooled mana and thought for a moment before aiming Helix at Ting-A-Kee rather than his Riders. Ting-A-Kee hit. The life totals stood fourteen to ten in his favor.

McLaren untapped and stuck out an Angel of his own, getting past for three on the threat of a second [Lightning Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Helix). Ting-A-Kee paid for his Riders and tapped out for Cloudskate, clearing the path again. McLaren obligingly showed the second Helix, this time on the Riders, a painland putting him at nine. He played a Signet and Wrathed.

Ting-A-Kee swept his creatures off the board, untapped, and hurriedly counted his mana. After playing a land, it was ten, exactly enough for the lethal [Demonfire](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033323/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonfire).

Andrew Ting-A-Kee defeats Shaun McLaren 3-1
