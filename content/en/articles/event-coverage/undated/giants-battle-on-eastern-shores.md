       <b>Monday, April 2: 1:12 a.m. - Semifinals: Matt Wang/Steve OMS vs. Matt Rubin/Rob DiPalma</b>&#10;      </li>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <p>Seriously, this match could have easily been a team draft after a PTQ circa the late nineties at Neutral Ground. All four players in this match are long time players from the East Coast region. Matt Rubin - known to all East Coasters as MattR - and his teammate Rob DiPalma were still bickering over their decks as the no longer reclusive Matt Wang and the no longer retired Steve O'Mahoney-Schwartz made their way to the table. </p>&#10;      <div>&#10;        <div>&#10;          <a>&#10;            <img alt="damnation" src="https://i.ormos.uk/08/8d/088d5b345d9733384d8a905759a5ccb37ad5c482ac122eec38e7a1c010bac470.jpg">&#10;          </a>&#10;        </div>&#10;      </div>&#10;      <p>&quot;He got the consistent deck but I got the two bombs,&quot; explained DiPalma as he showed off <a>Damnation</a> and <a>Disintegrate</a>. They were fighting about whether or not Rubin should be mono-colored. In the end he was mono-white with a couple of weaker white cards over a splash of blue that all required double blue in the casting cost. </p>&#10;      <p>Matt Rubin did not feel like he could adequately shuffle his deck and asked the judge to do it for him after he took his free mulligan. He was not happy with the next set and handed it back to the judge to go down to six. He realized that it was the same judge who shuffled the hand he had just shipped back and thought better of it calling out for someone else, &quot;Judge!&quot;</p>&#10;      <p>The game finally got underway and neither Matt nor Steve did anything on the first few turns which seemed to surprise Matt: &quot;I really expected two suspends from you guys.&quot; Not that his team was doing much either - basically just Rob summoning an <a>Aetherflame Wall</a>.</p>&#10;      <p>Matt made a Morph and Steve played totems for the next two turns along with a <a>Flowstone Channeler</a>. MattR's team mustered a handful of guys but Steve sent them back from whence they came with a <a>Conflagrate</a>.</p>&#10;      <p>Rubin was stalling on three lands and the high end of his curve in his hand. <a>Premature Burial</a> killed the <a>Clockwork Hydra</a> that Matt had played. <a>Stingscourger</a> bounced the morph and MattR's team saw <a>Aquamorph Entity</a>. When Matt played <a>Prismatic Lens</a> and <a>Malach of the Dawn</a> but left mana up without playing the morph again it set off a flurry of discussion on MattR's team about a <a>Cancel</a> they assumed had to be in Wang's hand. </p>&#10;      <p>Steve's <a>Torchling</a> raised the level of the discussion to a din as MattR urged Rob to cast what was obviously <a>Damnation</a>. </p>&#10;      <p>&quot;Cast your spell please,&quot; asked Steve when Rob did cast the board sweeper. </p>&#10;      <div>&#10;        <div>&#10;          <a>&#10;            <img alt="cancel" src="https://i.ormos.uk/d3/91/d39134d1e8da2a66ad7b65d62822101672c6f741c74ef4cc1ba031e501d32798.jpg">&#10;          </a>&#10;        </div>&#10;      </div>&#10;      <p>&quot;I knew you had it,&quot; groaned Rob who hadn't wanted to play the spell but the reality was that Wang had just drawn it. </p>&#10;      <p>&#10;        <a>Essence Sliver</a> and Sidewinder from MattR drew <a>Tendrils of Corruption</a> at the end of the turn on the 3/3. Steve untapped and shot the Sidewinder. Then he activated the black Totem and the team got in for 11. Matt played another morph. </p>&#10;      <p>&quot;We really need that second <a>Damnation</a>.&quot;</p>&#10;      <p>But they didn't have one. Matt eventually extended the hand. He couldn't even eat the pizza that Gerard - whose team had dispatched Aten and Lundquist - had brought for him. </p>&#10;      <p>&quot;I am kind of nauseous about that <a>Damnation</a>. You had the <a>Cancel</a> the whole time, right?&quot;</p>&#10;      <p>Wang tried let them feel better by saying he had it the whole time but Steve let the cat of the bag - prompting a whole new round of bickering. </p>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <a name="5">&#10;        &#10;      </a>&#10;      <li>&#10;        <b>Monday, April 2: 1:16 a.m. - Finals: Matt Wang/Steve OMS vs. Gerard Fabiano/Eric Ziegler</b>&#10;      </li>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <table>&#10;        <tbody><tr>&#10;          <td>&#10;            <img src="https://i.ormos.uk/b0/ac/b0aca99e7b9c757f02acda604baad1e4287674e5a00c2619694fa6c991d78117.jpg">&#10;            <i>Gerard Fabiano &amp; Eric Ziegler</i>&#10;          </td>&#10;        </tr>&#10;      </tbody></table>&#10;      <p>Both of the Jersey players took mulligans.
