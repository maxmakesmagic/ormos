Due to a logistical issue, *Murders at Karlov Manor* Bundles will have limited availability for Prerelease events (beginning February 2) at some North America retail locations. Please check with [your local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20240125000000/https://locator.wizards.com/) for details.

Thank you for your understanding as we work to supply fans and stores as soon as possible.
