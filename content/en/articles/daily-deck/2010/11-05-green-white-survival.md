Survival of the Fittest is all the rage in Legacy. Vengevine allows the deck to use the enchantment to quickly put an army of 4/3 creatures onto the battlefield. Using Survival with a creature in hand, a player discards to find Vengevine, then discards each Vengevine to find another. You end the loop by hunting up Basking Rootwallas, which you can cast for free using their madness trigger. Once you've cast both you've satisfied the Vengevine triggers in the graveyard and have an instant army! Adam Cai's version foregoes the blue cards of most traditional builds for a core green-white build that is almost Zooish in nature.
