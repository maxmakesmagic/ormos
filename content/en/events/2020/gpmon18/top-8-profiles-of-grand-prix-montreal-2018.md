These eight players stood a head above the rest here in Montreal, as they proved their mastery with *Guilds of Ravnica* in its Grand Prix debut. Congrats to all the members of the Top 8!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmon18/gp-montreal-2018-Top8-Isaak-Krut.jpg)

**Name:** Isaak Krut  
**Hometown:** Kazan, Russia  
**Occupation:** Student  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Made Day 2 of the Pro Tour twice.

**What did you play in Sealed? What were your most critical cards?**  
 Abzan (green-white-black). [Find // Finality](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Find+%2F%2F+Finality) and [Izoni, Thousand-Eyed](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Izoni%2C+Thousand-Eyed) plus [Underrealm Lich](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underrealm+Lich) plus good removal. I went 7-2.

**What did you draft? What were your records, and which cards were key to winning?**  
 Golgari. Almost the same as above, and I went 3-0. Then I had Boros and went 2-0-1 with [Dawn of Hope](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dawn+of+Hope) of the best card.

**What would winning Grand Prix Montreal mean to you?** The greatest achievement of in life.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmon18/gp-montreal-2018-Top8-Christopher-Leonard-Huu-Nguyen.jpg)

**Name:** Christopher Leonard Huu Nguyen  
**Hometown:** Montreal  
**Occupation:** Inventory technician in a warehouse.  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** This is my first Day 2 and my first Top 8.

**What did you play in Sealed? What were your most critical cards?**  
 Izzet. My best card was [Gravitic Punch](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravitic+Punch) and [Invert // Invent](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Invert+%2F%2F+Invent) and [Erratic Cyclops](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Erratic+Cyclops). It steals games.

**What did you draft? What were your records, and which cards were key to winning?**  
 3-0 with Dimir and 2-1 with Golgari.

**What would winning Grand Prix Montreal mean to you?**  
 A lot!!!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmon18/gp-montreal-2018-Top8-Andrew-Reesor.jpg)

**Name:** Andrew Reesor  
**Hometown:** Boston, MA  
**Occupation:** Actuarial consultant  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Top 4 a RPTQ.

**What did you play in Sealed? What were your most critical cards?**  
 Boros. Tajic and [Legion Warboss](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Legion+Warboss).

**What did you draft? What were your records, and which cards were key to winning?**  
 Dimir splashing green, with a lot of surveil cards with two [Vigorspore Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vigorspore+Wurm) and [Lotleth Giant](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotleth+Giant) as a top-end, going 3-0. Then 2-01 with Boros with [Experimental Frenzy](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Experimental+Frenzy) and [Skyknight Legionnaire](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skyknight+Legionnaire).

**What would winning Grand Prix Montreal mean to you?**  
 It would be my most meaningful accomplishment in Magic and I have been playing off and on since *Mirrodin*, so it would be nice to finally hold a trophy.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmon18/gp-montreal-2018-Top8-Chris-Ha.jpg)

**Name:** Chris Ha  
**Hometown:** Toronto  
**Occupation:** Clinical Research Assistant  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Driving to GPs and having someone in the car win or Top 8 - it's happened four times.

**What did you play in Sealed? What were your most critical cards?**  
 Sultai (blue-black-green) with [Artful Takedown](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Artful+Takedown) and [Nightveil Predator](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Predator).

**What did you draft? What were your records, and which cards were key to winning?**  
 Grixis with four Mountains and two [Artful Takedown](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Artful+Takedown)s (2-1), then Grixis with five gates, two [Artful Takedown](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Artful+Takedown) and [Etrata, the Silencer](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Etrata%2C+the+Silencer).

**What would winning Grand Prix Montreal mean to you?**  
 It would be a crazy finish to this cinderella story. Also the money would let me buy more avocados.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmon18/gp-montreal-2018-Top8-Alex-Watt.jpg)

**Name:** Alex Watt  
**Hometown:** Brantford, Ontario  
**Occupation:** Student  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Won a PTQ on *Magic Online.* 

**What did you play in Sealed? What were your most critical cards?**  
 Boros. My most critical cards were [Inescapable Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inescapable+Blaze) and [Boros Challenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Challenger).

**What did you draft? What were your records, and which cards were key to winning?**  
 3-0 with Izzet where [Murmuring Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Murmuring+Mystic) was my best card, along with Ral and [League Guildmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=League+Guildmage). Then I went 0-2-1 with Boros.

**What would winning Grand Prix Montreal mean to you?**  
 I could finally justify the amount of hours spent on *Magic Online.*

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmon18/gp-montreal-2018-Top8-Martin-Eric%20Gauthier.jpg)

**Name:** Martin Eric Gauthier  
**Hometown:** Longueuil, Quebec  
**Occupation:** Pastry chef  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Top 8 at GP Montreal in 2015.

**What did you play in Sealed? What were your most critical cards?**  
 Izzet. [Niv-Mizzet, Parun](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Niv-Mizzet%2C+Parun) and that awesome [Quasiduplicate](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quasiduplicate) card.

**What did you draft? What were your records, and which cards were key to winning?**  
 I went 5-0-1, drafting Dimir twice with good removal and big flyers.

**What would winning Grand Prix Montreal mean to you?**  
 Bringing the title home to Montreal.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmon18/gp-montreal-2018-Top8-Michael-Van-Vaals.jpg)

**Name:** Michael Von Vaals  
**Hometown:** Oshawa, Ontarioa  
**Occupation:** Software developer  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Several GP Top 16s and Top 32s.

**What did you play in Sealed? What were your most critical cards?**  
 Sultai with two [Status // Statue](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Status+%2F%2F+Statue).

**What did you draft? What were your records, and which cards were key to winning?**  
 3-0 with Dimir with two [Disinformation Campaign](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disinformation+Campaign)s, and then Dimir splashing two [Status // Statue](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Status+%2F%2F+Statue) and two [Underrealm Lich](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underrealm+Lich).

**What would winning Grand Prix Montreal mean to you?**  
 Helps me get to my first Pro Tour and hopefully get to Silver. It also helps to pay off some of my student loans.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmon18/gp-montreal-2018-Top8-Andrew-Abela.jpg)

**Name:** Andrew Abela  
**Hometown:** Toronto, Ontario  
**Occupation:** Neuroscientist  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Top 16 Nationals in 2017.

**What did you play in Sealed? What were your most critical cards?**  
 Selesnya splashing Aurelia and Tajic.

**What did you draft? What were your records, and which cards were key to winning?**  
 Izzet splashing white. In one game I cast [Expansion // Explosion](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Expansion+%2F%2F+Explosion) to deck my opponent. Then I drafted Golgari with two Findbroker, a [Find // Finality](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Find+%2F%2F+Finality) and Izoni.

**What would winning Grand Prix Montreal mean to you?**  
 Deep down, I’m still the 12-year-old kid drafting *Apocalypse at* Dollar’s in Scarborough. I’m here to have a good time.
