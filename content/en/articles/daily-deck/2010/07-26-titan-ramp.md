We've already brought you a few examples of **Magic** R&D members' take on green and red ramp decks featuring the Titans from ***Magic** 2011*. Now Japanese player Tamada Ryouichi brings us another version, which he used to win a recent tournament held in Japan. Rather than be content with simply casting Titans, however, Ryouichi uses Primeval Titan as a combo finisher. By accelerating out land drops using Harrow, Cultivate, and Khalni Heart Expedition, Tamada set up Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle for lethal damage. By the time Primeval Titan is tutoring up extra copies of the *Zendikar* land as well as free Mountains, the opponent is already crispy.
