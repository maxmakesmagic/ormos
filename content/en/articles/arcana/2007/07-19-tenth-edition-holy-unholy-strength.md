For the *Tenth Edition* art for Holy Strength and Unholy Strength, art director Jeremy Jarvis had the idea of commissioning the two pieces as bookends to each other—similar in structure and composition—but with one depicting strength drawn from good, and the other drawn from evil.

Terese Nielsen was tasked with creating the linked illustrations. Mouse over each sketch below to see the finished piece.

Art description for *Tenth Edition's* Holy Strength:

> Color: White  
>  Location: Not important  
>  Action: Show the meager but armored torso (chin to belt maybe) of a male warrior (costume should feel "white-aligned"). He is holding his sword straight up in front of his body, both hands on the hilt. A stronger, bare torso is being built around his own by angel feathers, which are circling into the picture in a stylish way. He is strongly lit with holy light from above. This should be a bookend illo with Unholy Strength.  
>  Focus: The torso and its indications of holy strength  
>  Mood: Heaven-sent muscle

*Holy Strength sketch and art by Terese Nielsen*

Art description for *Tenth Edition's* Unholy Strength:

> Color: Black  
>  Location: Not important  
>  Action: Show the meager but armored torso (chin to belt maybe) of a male warrior (costume should feel "black-aligned"). He is holding his sword straight down in front of his body, both hands on the hilt. A stronger, more horrible, bare torso is being built around his own by monstrous teeth and jawbones, which are circling into the picture in a stylish way. He is strongly up-lit with infernal light from below. This should be a bookend illo with Holy Strength.  
>  Focus: The torso and its indications of unholy strength  
>  Mood: Strength granted from below
