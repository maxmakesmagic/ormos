Pro Tour Kobe 2004 starred the original *Mirrodin* Block Constructed format. The big deck at the event? Affinity. The powerful combination of [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20110322000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp), [Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20110322000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault), and [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20110322000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager) was very tough to beat. Ben Stark played this version of the archetype into the Top 8 long before the Phyrexians invaded.
