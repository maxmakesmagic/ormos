Wizards of the Coast is teaming up with [First 4 Figures](https://web.archive.org/web/20050207000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.first4figures.com/magic.htm&origin=mtgcom_arcana_751) to bring you high-quality, collectible **Magic: The Gathering** figurines based on creatures from the world of Kamigawa. 

Here's a behind-the-scenes look at the goods. You'll recognize **Kodama of the North Tree** and **Ink-Eyes, Servant of Oni**. 

![Kodama of the North Tree](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/cc/d9cccb09e31cb2a6d77aab8925e78eba83f8b621d63cd8bd2e238dc498dbe5dc.jpg)  
*Kodama of the North Tree*

![Ink-Eyes, Servant of Oni](https://i.ormos.uk/1a/66/1a660f3407ea59eff06f516b5b7024747d90df62b5c7de3c8e084bcdf1033f5e.jpg)  
*Ink-Eyes, Servant of Oni*

Head to the [First 4 Figures website](https://web.archive.org/web/20050207000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.first4figures.com/magic.htm&origin=mtgcom_arcana_751) to see the other three creatures that will be available. Stay tuned to **magicthegathering.com** in the coming weeks for release date and other product information.
