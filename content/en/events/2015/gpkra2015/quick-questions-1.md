### Which deck do you wish that you had played at the Pro Tour last weekend?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkra15/gpkra_qq_anteri.jpg)  
**Fabrizio Anteri:** "I was very happy with my deck choice last weekend (Green/Red devotion). It put both Thomas Hendriks and Ondřej Stráský in the top 8. I played the same deck to a 7-3 record."

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkra15/gpkra_qq_dickmann.jpg)  
**Patrick Dickmann:** "I liked my deck (Jeskai Dragons), but if I could have played something else, I would have gone with Craig Wescoe's Bant deck."

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkra15/gpkra_qq_manfield.jpg)  
**Seth Manfield:** "My deck was good (Abzan, splashing Atarka), but I didn't realize how good control decks are in the format. We mostly tested Blue/Black, but never tried adding white for Ojutai, which seems really good. So if I had to switch to something else I would go for the Esper Dragon deck."

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkra15/gpkra_qq_muller.jpg)  
**Martin Müller:** "I was happy playing the Red deck. (Mono Red, splashing [Atarka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Atarka%27s+Command)), after all it did end up winning the tournament. But if I had the Esper list that Channelfireball built, I might have gone for that instead."
