### Last Minute Changes

* * *

I'm sorry to announce that, due to personal reasons, I won't be able to participate in the Wizards of the Coast Invitational. Fortunately, my replacement will be a former Pro Tour player--Alan Comer. Since someone has to replace me, it's nice that it's someone who'll be able to make a showing and is a member of the development team. He'll be playing to have Quirion Dryad made into a ***Magic** Online* avatar.

Another change to the Invitational will be that events will be starting at 9am PST on Saturday and 8am PST on Sunday. That means gunslinging will begin earlier on Sunday than originally planned, so make sure you show up early.

Thanks again to everyone who voted me in. I'm sorry to have to drop, but sometimes life just happens.
