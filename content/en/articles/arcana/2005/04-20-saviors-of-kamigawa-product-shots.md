S*aviors of Kamigawa*, the third and final set of the Kamigawa block, releases June 3, 2005. 

Watch for *Saviors* legend vignettes on **magicthegathering.com** starting the week of May 2nd, and then *Saviors* card previews starting the following week, May 9th. 

You can already check out the [*Saviors of Kamigawa* prerelease schedule](https://web.archive.org/web/20050420000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/prereleases) (May 21-22 -- locations being added each day). Find a venue near you to see all the new cards.
