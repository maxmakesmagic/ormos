Quick Questions starts off with a look at awkward or retrospectively rough first picks in the first pack for select pro players here this weekend. What does it say about their drafting tendencies in this format?

 **Looking back at all the *Magic 2015* drafts you've done, what's the worst card you've taken Pick 1, Pack 1?** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/kuo_draft.jpg)

 ***Tzu-Ching Kuo, 6-2 on Day 1** - Some green card probably. I hate drafting green in this set. I'll do anything I can to avoid it.* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/jensen_draft.jpg)

 ***William Jensen (20), 8-0 on Day 1** - I think I once took [Aeronaut Tinkerer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aeronaut+Tinkerer), which might be worse than [Bronze Sable](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bronze+Sable).* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/martell_draft.jpg)

 ***Tom Martell (7), 5-3 on Day 1** - [Oreskos Swiftclaw](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oreskos+Swiftclaw). One-toughness guys are so bad in this format.* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/detora_draft.jpg)

 ***Melissa de Tora, 6-2 on Day 1** - [Liliana Vess](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+Vess). She's good, but it's a risky pick. You'd much rather take her later once you know what your deck looks like.* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/froehlich_draft.jpg)

 ***Eric Froehlich (21), 5-3 on Day 1** - I've first-picked [Midnight Guard](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Midnight+Guard) at least three times.* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/finkel_draft.jpg)

 ***Jon Finkel, 6-2 on Day 1** - I've first-picked some pretty bad cards, a lot worse than [Welkin Tern](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Welkin+Tern), I'll tell you that. Probably [Runeclaw Bear](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Runeclaw+Bear).*
