Starting with [Pro Tour Philadelphia](/en/events/2005/philadelphia05/2005-pro-tourphiladelphia), Wizards of the Coast will offer Calling All Cards events at all domestic Pro Tours and at Nationals in 2005. What is Calling All Cards you ask? Calling All Cards is an invitation to you, the **Magic** player, to bring out your prized **Magic** cards and collectibles and share them with the **Magic** community! 

Do you have a playset of foil, Japanese *Mercadian Masques* Brainstorms? How about 246 copies of Spitting Slug? Have a Questing Phelddagrif with a genuine Matt Cavotta signature? Want to know if there any keepers in your brother's old binder of cards? Or do you want deck building ideas from a Wizards employee? If you have any interest in one or any of these topics, then Calling All Cards is the event for you. 

Calling All Cards is a rallying point for the **Magic** community. You can share your **Magic** stories and flaunt your prized **Magic** memorabilia with players from around the globe. 

And that's not even the best part. Decksperts from Beckett Media and Wizards of the Coast will be available to answer your questions and listen to your stories. And we didn't even mention the giveaways… 

### Meet the Decksperts!

![Jake Theis, Wizards of the Coast Deckspert](https://i.ormos.uk/1b/73/1b73ab4337a7ab5de3029273c74f80280cf8c0a0837bb2f9ab6573d736c625ad.jpg)

### Jake Theis

The Wizards of the Coast Deckspert will be Jake Theis, Assistant Brand Manager for **Magic: The Gathering**.

Jake will comment on why cards are historically important, dissect card choices in players' decks, and discuss intermediate **Magic** strategy with casual and competitive **Magic** players alike. In addition, he will have giveaways to players who visit the Calling All Cards Booth.

The newest hire to the **Magic** Brand Team, Jake has been an avid **Magic** player and card collector for more than 10 years. A native of Joliet, Ill., (yes that literally makes him Joliet Jake), he has degrees in marketing and international business from Lewis University in Romeoville, Ill. 

Currently, Jake spends most of his free time "letting other **Magic** fans win" in the casual rooms of ***Magic** Online*. 

![Doug Kale, Beckett Deckspert](https://i.ormos.uk/0f/70/0f7060452982c60f692b0507efae398cd62f6db6c70412e0326a4fd02eb4847c.jpg)

### Doug Kale

The Beckett Deckspert will be Doug Kale, Editorial Director for Beckett Media. 

Doug will be grading **Magic** cards according to official Beckett standards. If you're curious if your Black Lotus is really in mint condition, Doug will be able to tell you on site. He also will let you know if your cards are keepers or clunkers. 

With more than 15 years of professional experience in the hobby and collectibles industry, Doug has tracked the prices and popularity of sports cards, TCGs, toys, animation cels, comic books and a variety of other collectible items. He also will be the price guide editor for the upcoming magazine *Beckett: Magic The Gathering*.

A native of Long Island, New York, Doug earned his Bachelor of Arts degree in communications and Associate of Arts degree in business marketing from Adelphi University. He resides in Dallas with his wife, Stacy, and four-year-old son, Daniel.

For more than 20 years, Beckett has been one of the premier names in trading card pricing and grading. Now with titles focusing on anime, TCGs, and sports collectibles, they are one of the most trusted names among all collectors.
