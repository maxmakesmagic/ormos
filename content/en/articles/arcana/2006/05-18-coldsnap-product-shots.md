It's going to be a frigid July.

  

![Coldsnap](https://i.ormos.uk/be/04/be0476ddf921cfcb63b0e780dbf87b561d2243f88f61ed0eaf2a366ff6237282.jpg "Coldsnap")

![Coldsnap Booster Display Box](https://i.ormos.uk/08/fa/08faa29d1fdbc23c1a86c0c510a52de3574dd897180c5c633fa406bada638734.jpg "Coldsnap Booster Display Box")

Above is *Coldsnap's* booster display box. Below are the three booster pack designs you'll see at your local hobby shop.
