## What is the Magic Online Championship Series?

The *Magic: The Gathering Online* Championship Series, also known as MOCS, is a series of events in which top players can earn an invitation to the annual in-person *Magic Online* Championship event, as well as a Pro Tour event.

Throughout the qualifying period from the conclusion of the November 25, 2015 downtime until the September 28, 2016 downtime, players earn Qualifier Points (QPs) by playing in events that award QPs as part of their prize structure. The series will contain 12 Monthly Events, each of which qualify top players for one of the four Playoff Events. These Playoff Events qualify top players for the *Magic Online* Championship, a Pro Tour, and the Pro Tour Challenge event at that Pro Tour.

Monthly and Playoff Events also award top players MOCS Leaderboard Points for the current quarter and for the year as a whole. Players of sufficient standing on the MOCS Leaderboard(s) will also qualify for Playoff Events later in the 2016 *Magic Online* Championship Series as well as the *Magic Online* Championship.

Wizards of the Coast reserves the right to add or remove qualifying formats, alter events that award Qualifier Points (QPs), and/or adjust the number of QPs offered by any event with a minimum of 24 hours' notice.

Qualifier Points for the 2016 *Magic Online* Championship Series may be earned from after the November 25, 2015 downtime until the September 28, 2016 downtime. If there is no downtime, 8 a.m. Pacific will be used instead.

## Pro Club and Hall of Fame Benefits

The *Magic Online* Championship series gives benefits to players with active Pro Player Club and/or Pro Tour Hall of Fame membership whose DCI membership and *Magic Online* accounts are in good standing as follows:

 

*   Platinum pros will be invited to each Playoff Event.
*   Platinum, Gold, and Hall of Fame pros will be granted 35 Qualifier Points on the Wednesday prior to each Monthly Event.
*   Silver pros who are not members of the Hall of Fame will receive 15 Qualifier Points the Wednesday prior to each Monthly Event.

 

Note that each Monthly Event requires 35 Qualifier Points to join. These Qualifier Points can be combined across grants as well as with Qualifier Points earned in other *Magic Online* events to join Monthly Events.

## MOCS LEADERBOARD POINTS

Monthly Events and Playoff Events include MOCS Leaderboard Points as prizes. These are not physical objects tracked in your collection, but can instead be viewed on the Wizards of the Coast website at [MTGO.com/leaderboard](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/magic-online-leaderboard-points).

Two leaderboards will be posted. The MOCS Yearly Leaderboard will include all MOCS Leaderboard Points awarded in all Monthly and Playoff events in the 2016 *Magic Online* Championship Series. The MOCS Quarterly Leaderboard will include only MOCS Leaderboard Points awarded in Monthly Events after the most recent Playoff Event.

These leaderboards will be updated no later than the Wednesday following a Monthly or Playoff event, and will be used to determine qualifications for Playoff Events and the *Magic Online* Championship.

## PLAYOFF EVENTS

A player qualifies for the first Playoff Event if they meet any of the following criteria (Updated November 19, 2015):

*   Finish in 1st–8th place in any of the three immediately prior MOCS Monthly Events
*   Are in the Top 50 of the MOCS Quarterly Leaderboard
*   Are a Platinum Pro Club member

A player qualifies for the second through fourth Playoff Events if they meet any of the following criteria (Updated November 19, 2015):

*   Finish in 1st–8th place in any of the three immediately prior MOCS Monthly Events
*   Are in the Top 25 of the MOCS Quarterly Leaderboard
*   Are in the Top 25 of the MOCS Yearly Leaderboard
*   Are a Platinum Pro Club member

In the event of a tie on a leaderboard that would determine a qualification for a Playoff Event, all tied players with at least one MOCS Leaderboard Point during the appropriate time are qualified for the Playoff Event.

## 2016 MAGIC ONLINE CHAMPIONSHIP

The *Magic Online* Championship will be a sixteen-player *Magic Online* tournament held in person and will have a total prize pool of $116,000.00 USD. Full event details, including the location and formats, will be released by Wizards of the Coast later in 2016. The following players are qualified for the *Magic Online* Championship:

*   The defending *Magic Online* Champion
*   The current *Magic* World Champion
*   The top two finishers from each of the four Playoff Events
*   The top six players from the MOCS Yearly Leaderboard

If a qualified player becomes ineligible or is otherwise unable to attend the *Magic Online* Championship, Wizards of the Coast will continue to invite players from the MOCS Yearly Leaderboard until sixteen players have been invited.

In the event of a tie in the MOCS Yearly Leaderboard to qualify for the *Magic Online* Championship, the following tiebreakers will be used:

1.  Most Playoff Events in which the player finished in 1st–8th place
2.  Most Monthly Events in which the player finished in 1st–8th place
3.  Highest number of match wins in the Swiss rounds of any Playoff Event
4.  Highest number of match wins in the Swiss rounds of any Monthly Event
5.  Most Qualifier Points earned between the November 25, 2015 and the September 28, 2016 downtime

The winner of the *Magic Online* Championship becomes the *Magic Online* Champion and receives the following prizes in addition to a portion of the $116,000.00 USD prize pool:

*   Invite to next year's *Magic Online* Championship
*   Invite to the next *Magic* World Championship
*   Platinum status in the Pro Player Club (as if that player had reached the minimum number of Professional Points at the *Magic Online* Championship to be promoted to Platinum level)

Here is a breakdown of prize pool distribution:

*   Copy of a birth certificate and a photo ID (school IDs are acceptable)

## PARTICIPATION IN MONTHLY AND PLAYOFF EVENTS

For all Monthly Events and Playoff Events in the 2016 *Magic Online* Championship Series, it is the player's responsibility to verify that they met the entry requirements and join the event prior to its start time. If a player believes he or she should be qualified and is unable to join an event, they will need to [contact customer service](http://wizards.custhelp.com/) prior to the start of the event. For assistance during an event, players can contact a Digital Event Coordinator (DEC) in the #MOCS chat room.

## CONNECTIVITY

Due to the nature of computers and the Internet, Wizards of the Coast is not responsible for the results of any disconnection or network problems experienced by players.

For more information on stability and connection issues in *Magic Online* Premier Play, refer to the [Guidelines for PTQ Finals and MOCS Finals](/en/articles/magic-online-event-info/2014/07-30-guidelines-for-ptq-finals-and-mocs-finals) article.
