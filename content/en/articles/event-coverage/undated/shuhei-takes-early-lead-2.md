![](https://i.ormos.uk/f8/44/f8448d35d44d71ebf8e40e492c9ee93ddb69e7b66d56be5e6dcac1a584110565.jpg)GP Stuttgart is over, and we have a new leader for the Player of the Year race: Shuhei Nakamura, Japanese level five pro, is the winner of the first big batch of points this year. His unorthodox draft strategy took him to a win, with a deck that met very mixed reactions among the spectators. However, it’s hard to argue with success. His runner-up, Robert van Medevoort, failed to produce his Profane Command in the finals, but he had a pretty good deck (which you can of course find here in the coverage).

The host country Germany brought about half of all competitors to the trade fair in Sindelfingen, and two of them made Top 8. Marc Vogt had a remarkable run with a clean 12-0 and just one bye, and Raul Porojan made his second GP Top 8 after GP Strasbourg. We saw a number of old favorites, as well as some rising stars here in Strasbourg. Marijn Lybaert was among them, missing Top 8 by a hair. The Belgian kid was trumped only by his National champion Fried Meulders, who notched a Top 8 appearance on his virtual achievemets badge.

GP Stuttgart was the biggest individual Grand Prix this year, and just as big as GP Amsterdam. Lorwyn Limited is over as a format, because the next event in the Magic year will be the Morningtide prereleases. Stay tuned to magicthegathering.com, where we will pick up with the Player of the Year race after that, and see if Shuhei Nakamura defends his early lead across the many formats of Pro Tours and Grand Prix in 2008!

## BLOG

## December 16th, 6:10 p.m.: Quarterfinal: Fried Meulders (BEL) vs Raul Porojan (DEU)

*by Hanno Terbuyken*

**Game 1**

With [Pestermite](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pestermite) and [Silvergill Douser](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silvergill+Douser), Fried was off to a better start than Raul. And the Belgian knew how to press his advantage further, with a timely [Faerie Trickery](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faerie+Trickery) taking out Raul's [Neck Snap](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Neck+Snap). Fried filled the air with Faeries, Raul sought his luck on the ground: [Nath's Elite](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nath%27s+Elite) and [Thorntooth Witch](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thorntooth+Witch) manned his fort.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/05/7d/057d6f90ed0c8ce1ea8914cb23fb4311d2ecab422c55457e789e5cb398510adc.jpg)*Fried Meulders of Belgium.*Fried's air force took Raul to 11 without giving the German a chance to strike back. Raul merely had [Lignify](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lignify) for [Pestermite](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pestermite). That met [Familiar's Ruse](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Familiar%27s+Ruse). A successful attack from Raul forced three measly damage through, but the counterstrike brought the German to six, facing mostly flying damage on the board with few blockers, because his [Thorntooth Witch](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thorntooth+Witch) had been [Aethersnipe](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aethersnipe)d.

Even though Raul replayed his Witch, he lost [Nath's Elite](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nath%27s+Elite) and [Woodland Changeling](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Woodland+Changeling) in combat, thanks to [Peppersmoke](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peppersmoke). Raul, now on 4, needed an out and found [Elvish Harbinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Harbinger) for [Nameless Inversion](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nameless+Inversion). [Nameless Inversion](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nameless+Inversion) took out a Faerie and [Ringskipper](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ringskipper), with the help of [Thorntooth Witch](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thorntooth+Witch), and the ensuing clash revealed [Final Revels](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Final+Revels) on Fried's library. He didn't even need it: Raul Porojan crumbled under flying pressure.

Fried Meulders 1, Raul Porojan 0

**Game 2:** 

Changeling faced Changeling in the first turns of the game: One of the Aquamoeboid variety fought for Fried, one of the Woodland sort was on Raul's side. But where Fried had [Moonglove Extract](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moonglove+Extract), Raul presented [Briarhorn](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Briarhorn), and killed [Sentinels of Glen Elendra](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sentinels+of+Glen+Elendra) with [Nameless Inversion](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nameless+Inversion). Porojan could deal with it the first time around, but when Medevoort targeted it with [Footbottom Feast](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Footbottom+Feast), Porojan started shuffling for Game 2, without even drawing another card.

Robert van Medevoort 1, Raul Porojan 0

**Game 2:**

 

![Shapesharer](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Shapesharer)

Again, Porojan had a quite slow start, with only [Woodland Changeling](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Woodland+Changeling) and [Fertile Ground](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fertile+Ground) in the first four rounds. Medevoort used that time to play and attack with [Sygg, River Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sygg%2C+River+Guide), Amoebid Changeling, and [Dreamspoiler Witches](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dreamspoiler+Witches). But Porojan really got ahead when he played [Nath of the Gilt-Leaf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nath+of+the+Gilt-Leaf) and [Changeling Hero](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Changeling+Hero) on consecutive turns. Medevoort started discarding, and even though he was ahead in life, the [Changeling Hero](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Changeling+Hero) meant that he would not be able to race Porojan. But there was one card in the deck that could deal with all problems. If you read about Medevoort's quarterfinals, you might know which one it is. Here is a hint: [Changeling Hero](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Changeling+Hero) is a Shapeshifter. [Nath of the Gilt-Leaf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nath+of+the+Gilt-Leaf) is legendary. Medevoort topdecked his [Shapesharer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shapesharer), invoked the Legend rule, and Porojan just shook his head and picked up his cards. Robert van Medevoort advanced to the finals of GP Stuttgart, to battle Shuuhei Nakamura for the title and the lead in the 2008 Player of the Year race!

Robert van Medevoort 2, Raul Porojan 0

## December 16th, 8:13 p.m.: GP Stuttgart Finals: Robert van Meedevort (NLD) vs Shuuhei Nakamura (JPN)

*by Hanno Terbuyken*

Both of these players have two consecutive vowels in their names, and both of them have plenty of experience in the limelight: Robert as a member of the 2006 Team World Champions, and Shuuhei Nakamura as a former level 6 mage. It's a worthy cast to determine the first leader of the Player of the Year race 2008! The two Magic heavyweights shook hands and off they were.

**Game 1**

Robert van Meedevort quickly announced to keep his hand, and Shuuhei followed suit. The Japanese also had the first play of the match with [Stonybrook Angler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stonybrook+Angler), a good protective measure against the bomb-laden 40 cards Robert had. [Judge of Currents](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge+of+Currents) and [Fallowsage](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fallowsage) came into play for Robert. Curiously, Robert van Meedevort is the only player I've seen all weekend who places his lands above his other permanents, tapping them almost in the red zone.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/dc/fd/dcfd2aefd775cd16b903624ab8780769ecb2c0102df69e84eefc712e26c49ab3.jpg)*Shuuhei Nakamura was locked in an intense battle for the GP win and PoY lead.* Shuuhei had [Inkfathom Divers](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inkfathom+Divers) and [Sentinels of Glen Elendra](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sentinels+of+Glen+Elendra), while Robert bolstered his board with Dreamspoiler Witch. [Weed Strangle](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Weed+Strangle) took out Shuuhei's Divers. Robert pondered and dropped the first bomb of his deck: [Sygg, River Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sygg%2C+River+Guide). Shuuhei answered with [Cloudcrown Oak](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudcrown+Oak) and [Merrow Harbinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merrow+Harbinger), fetching [Fallowsage](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fallowsage).

Robert added an Island to his already colorful manabase and declared an attack with Sygg. Shuuhei took it, and lost his first life of the match: 18 to 14 were the scores in his favor. [Shapesharer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shapesharer) and [Ghostly Changeling](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghostly+Changeling) joined the party powered by Robert's disco manabase, [Judge of Currents](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge+of+Currents) brought him back up to 13 (from a previous 11), and Shuuhei needed to find a way to break through Robert's defensive lines. The long game clearly favored the Dutchman, who was gaining life and copying creatures left and right.

Shuuhei had to let [Shapesharer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shapesharer) shenanigans happen, as the Sharer and [Ghostly Changeling](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghostly+Changeling) made quite a deadly combination. Shuuhei fell to 10 life and sought revenge with an offense. A joint strike from half his forces brought Robert to 11. Shuuhei had another ace up his metaphorical sleeve: He tapped two blue and two more to play Sower of Tempation.

Stealing Robert's [Shapesharer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shapesharer), the Japanese turned the board in his favor despite Robert gaining life. The [Shapesharer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shapesharer) became a [Sygg, River Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sygg%2C+River+Guide), to invoke the Legend rule and send both cards to Robert's graveyard. The Dutchman refilled with [Mournwhelk](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mournwhelk) and [Oona's Prowler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oona%27s+Prowler), but he had no clue about the [Changeling Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Changeling+Titan) Shuuhei had kept safely in his hand.

The Titan championed [Sower of Temptation](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sower+of+Temptation), quite a clever move. It didn't come to fruition, though. Shuuhei's fliers and Islandwalkers – [Pestermite](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pestermite), [Sentinels of Glen Elendra](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sentinels+of+Glen+Elendra), and [Merrow Harbinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merrow+Harbinger) – had dropped Robert to 5 already, and the [Oblivion Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Ring) Shuuhei drew wasn't even needed to take home Game 1 of the finals.

Robert van Meedevort 0, Shuuhei Nakamura 1

**Game 2:**

![](https://i.ormos.uk/86/29/8629634c3b4a44164fb2004b42a8d8b85b4affabfe99d1b9b8be81ff7c306a3f.jpg)*The two players looking at their opponent's deck before the final match.*Again, Robert led with [Judge of Currents](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge+of+Currents). Shuuhei's hand was juicy, holding not just the [Bog-Strider Ash](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bog-Strider+Ash) he played, but also [Sentinels of Glen Elendra](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sentinels+of+Glen+Elendra), a [Merrow Reejerey](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merrow+Reejerey) and a [Merrow Harbinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merrow+Harbinger), putting everything Shuuhei needed in his almost immediate reach.

Robert didn't need to reach, though. [Ghostly Changeling](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghostly+Changeling), and another [Judge of Currents](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge+of+Currents), followed by [Sygg, River Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sygg%2C+River+Guide), wanted to make Shuuhei's road to the title as stony as possible. The Japanese made [Thorntooth Witch](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thorntooth+Witch). Any Changeling he'd get with the Harbinger now would serve double duty as removal spell.

Robert was at a steadily climbing life total, already up to 24 against Shuuhei's 15. [Bog-Strider Ash](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bog-Strider+Ash) and [Sentinels of Glen Elendra](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sentinels+of+Glen+Elendra) brought Robert back to his starting life total. Shuuhei played the Reejerey from his hand and the Harbinger, getting not a Changeling but [Inkfathom Divers](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inkfathom+Divers) instead. He needed a surefire way to keep up against Robert's steady stream of life, and the Islandwalker looked like a fitting tool to do it.

Shuuhei sucked air through his front teeth in a thoughtful gesture as Robert added [Cloudgoat Ranger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudgoat+Ranger) and his three Goats to the board. An attack sent Robert to 17, but Shuuhei fought an uphill battle that he was unlikely to win. Already at 13, Shuuhei now had [Oona's Prowler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oona%27s+Prowler) to deal with in addition to everything else.

Attacking with [Inkfathom Divers](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inkfathom+Divers), [Bog-Strider Ash](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bog-Strider+Ash) and [Merrow Harbinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merrow+Harbinger) dealt nine evasion-fueled damage to Robert. Shuuhei played [Cloudcrown Oak](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudcrown+Oak), triggered his [Thorntooth Witch](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thorntooth+Witch) and removed [Cloudgoat Ranger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudgoat+Ranger). Robert was gaining 4 life a turn, but Shuuhei was subtracting 9. As long as he kept his flank protected, he could eventually drag this game to the safe zone.

However, it was Robert's turn to be the aggressor. Sygg and [Ghostly Changeling](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghostly+Changeling) attacked. Shuuhei blicked with [Cloudcrown Oak](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudcrown+Oak), [Merrow Reejerey](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merrow+Reejerey), and Thornthooth Witch, making it unfeasible for Meedevort to give his Changeling protection of any sorts with Sygg?. The Changeling traded with Reejerey, and Shuuhei cracked back for 7. Robert was now at 11, Shuuhei at 9.

A convoluted head-to-head, that's what this second game was. Robert crashed with [Judge of Currents](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge+of+Currents), [Judge of Currents](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge+of+Currents), and Sygg. Shuuhei added up: He had to block, and at best kill the Judges. But Sygg protected his brethren from the blockers. The stakes rose as Shuuhei attacked for a huge 9 points of damage. Without the lifegain, Robert would already have been dead, The Dutchman went to eight, now facing lethal damage on the board.

[Stonybrook Angler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stonybrook+Angler) bolstered Shuuhei's defense against a sudden death from 7 life. Neither player gave an inch if they could avoid it. The inches Shuuhei measured seemed slightly longer, though, as the Japanese was seemingly in control of the situation – and making Robert think.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e7/19/e719c58c13ac85bc957fd432f1b17740ae71f1bd013341e54eedd7808c405d45.jpg) *The Dutchman had Profane Command in his deck, but did not see it in the final match.*Robert attacked with double [Judge of Currents](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge+of+Currents) and Sygg, gaining six life, back up to 14. Shuuhei took the Sygg, down to 5. Shuuhei blocked both Judges, and the one that was stopped by a green and a blue creature should have been safe through Sygg. Robert had been ready to let the other one go, but the table judge intervened: Robert had announced Protection from Blue first, and thus the Sygg couldn't target that [Judge of Currents](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge+of+Currents) anymore to give it Protection from Green, too!

The ruling stood, as you have to clearly announce when you put respond to your own abilities. Robert could have stacked his protection activations correctly, but he didn't announce it that way. o both Judges died, Robert lost his protective wall of lifegain, and Shuuhei Nakamura was the first leader of the 2008 Player of the Year race!

Robert van Meedevort 0, Shuuhei Nakamura 2

After the match, Shuuhei received high fives from his fellow pro players: Ervin Tormos, Jelger Wiegersma, Raphael Levy and Richard Hoaen. The level five pro from Japan took a well-deserved lead in the 2008 Player of the Year race. Will the two-point lead be good until the very end? We do not know, but we know one thing: Shuuhei Nakamura was happy about his win at GP Stuttgart 2007!

## December 16th, 10:50 p.m.: Tying up the lose ends

*by Hanno Terbuyken*

Another GP has gone by, the last one of the year, and the first one of the new season. And again, I did not have enough time during the day to bring you all the stories. For example, I did not tell you anything about the two artists on site, Wayne England and Mark Tedin. Especially Mark Tedin had endless lines of people queuing up, to get their old classics signed. [Sol Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sol+Ring), [Mana Drain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Drain), [City of Brass](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=City+of+Brass), [Mana Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220928234536/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Vault), the list is impressive.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/63/9c/639cda13eed0009482d05544313e8e1f44da87f22c1e159fda76c0e1316d8c0b.jpg)*Mark Tedin and Wayne England*

Also, you didn’t read anything about the numerous side events that happened. The side event team ran 93 8-man draft queues on Saturday, and 109 8-man drafts on Sunday. The second day of the GP also saw a Grand Prix Trial happen, with 48 players grabbing their Extended decks. A 2HG event with 93 teams. A Legacy tournament with 86 players. A Vintage tourney with 28 players. And a special draft event: Germany’s biggest Magic website, [www.planetmtg.de](http://www.planetmtg.de/), had given away 16 seats in a draft event extraordinaire. Wizards Germany had sponsored 16 draft sets made of 10th Edition cards, each with one booster of Commons only, one booster of Uncommons only, and one booster pack consisting of Rares only. Yes, that’s right: Each player had the guarantee of 15 rares, and the decks were pretty ridiculous.

I think that pretty much covers it. Join the magicthegathering.com coverage team next year, when the 2008 season gets into full swing, and merry christmas and a happy new year to all of you!
