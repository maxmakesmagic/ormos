N*ew Phyrexia* has 175 cards, which is more than usual for a small expansion. And today, we're going to show you ten of them. You've probably figured this out from the headline, or from your peripheral vision reporting the images immediately below this paragraph, but here it is anyway: *New Phyrexia* contains ten basic lands to show just how Phyrexian-ized the plane of Mirrodin has gotten. Let's take a look!
