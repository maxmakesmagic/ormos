This weekend is Grand Prix Dallas, but it will not be the first time Dallas has played host to Premier-level **Magic**. The last event there featured the Extended format, and Hall of Famer Raphael Levy used this powerful Domain Zoo deck to take the top slot. Using dual lands like Breeding Pool with two basic land types and fetch lands like Flooded Strand to go get them, Levy was able to get maximum value out of his Gaea's Mights, and with the double strike of Boros Swiftblade he could swing for a whopping 12 damage on the third turn. Read full coverage of the event [here](/en/events/2007/gpdal07/hall-of-famer-lone-star-in-dallas).
