## Grand Prix Lille 2012

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ee/e6/eee667d69261ccaedd34211b0bdbab90343eac4d5eae3741872654a5c1ad5d51.jpg)

Sometime on Saturday lunchtime, round two of GP Lille began. 1505 players could still win. Hundreds hadn't begun. Following his one bye, Richard Parker of England shuffled up his UB Zombies deck for the first time. He won, and won again. Now he was level with the best in the world, the Pros with their three byes. He kept on winning, and by the end of round 7 had locked up a spot on day two. Six players went undefeated on day one. Parker was one of them.

He came back on day two. He carried on winning, and winning, and winning. By the time thirty or so players were desperately scrabbling for places in the top 8, Parker was sitting comfortably ahead of the field at 14-0. Two Intentional Draws saw him into the top 8. Historically, trailblazers fare badly in top 8s. Whether it's simple misfortune, or the pressure of pacesetting, or just heaping expectations on themselves, history is littered with undefeated records that mean nothing when it really matters.

Parker beat RG Aggro in the quarter final. He did it again in the semi final. Only the excellent Simon Diaz with his [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets) deck stood in the path of the unbeaten Parker. In the end, he wasn't just unbeaten, he was unbeatable.

There were other stories too, of course. There always are. Standard proved to be a ton of fun and ultra-versatile, with enough archetypes running around the top tables to keep the most ardent deck tinkerer happy for weeks. RG Aggro bared its claws significantly, while the French Frites deck and UB Control mostly flattered to deceive down the stretch. In the end, though, it turned out that copying [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) with [Phantasmal Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phantasmal+Image) , and smashing face with [Phyrexian Obliterator](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Obliterator) , was something nobody had an answer to - at least when it was Parker Perfect asking the questions.

Congratulations to Richard Parker, Grand Prix Lille Champion 2012!

## Quarterfinals - Richard Parker vs. Martin Zimmermann

*by Frank Karsten* 

This Quarterfinals threw together Richard Parker from England, playing Blue-Black Zombies, and Martin Zimmermann from Germany, playing Red-Green Aggro. It is the first Grand Prix Top 8 for both of them. Parker is the sole undefeated player remaining in the tournament, as he racked up 13 straight match wins after his bye and subsequently ID'd into the Top 8. He is now turning his attention towards the trophy, but Zimmermann is standing in his way.

### Game 1

Zimmerman had a fast start with double [Llanowar Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Elves) and [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise) to get ahead on mana. Parker tried to slow things down by playing [Tragic Slip](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tragic+Slip) on one a [Llanowar Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Elves) and subsequently added [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) , [Diregraf Captain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Diregraf+Captain) , and [Gravecrawler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravecrawler) to his board. In the meantime, Zimmerman played [Thrun, the Last Troll](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thrun%2C+the+Last+Troll) .

Now it was time for the two players to kill each other's creatures. [Garruk Relentless](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Relentless) dealt with [Diregraf Captain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Diregraf+Captain) , while [Phantasmal Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phantasmal+Image) legend-ruled [Thrun, the Last Troll](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thrun%2C+the+Last+Troll) away.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1d/2c/1d2c0f6c692c60871ae22856f6aeca12e187a1ceb0c3b01170aa3848d88eecb3.jpg)

Zimmerman, now facing a Zombie team of [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) and [Gravecrawler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravecrawler) nibbling away his life total, had to come up with a way to stop the beatdown. He sacrificed his [Llanowar Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Elves) to [Garruk, the Veil-Cursed](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk%2C+the+Veil-Cursed) (using the last loyalty counter, resulting in a dead Garruk) to search up [Strangleroot Geist](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strangleroot+Geist) . The Geist immediately hit the battlefield, together with a fresh [Garruk Relentless](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Relentless) plus 2/2 Wolf friend.

Parker replied by summoning a new [Diregraf Captain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Diregraf+Captain) and sent in his [Gravecrawler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravecrawler) and [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) . Zimmerman, with a board of [Strangleroot Geist](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strangleroot+Geist) , Wolf token, and [Garruk Relentless](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Relentless) (three loyalty counters) chose to preserve his life total by chumpblocking the 4/3 [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) with Stangleroot Geist and by putting his Wolf token in front of [Gravecrawler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravecrawler) . The 2/1 Zombie immediately came back from the grave however, and Zimmerman wasn't gaining any position.

A [Phantasmal Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phantasmal+Image) from Parker was next. Zimmermann, smelling that Parker was planning to copy his [Diregraf Captain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Diregraf+Captain) , used Galvanic Blast to kill it in response. Parker, unperturbed, copied [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) instead.

Although Zimmermann kept making Wolf tokens with [Garruk Relentless](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Relentless) , they did not provide a permanent answer to the recurring Gravecralwer. Zimmermann's next draw steps only provided him with lands that did not help him protect his life total against Zimmerman's onslaught of [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) s. A second [Phantasmal Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phantasmal+Image) from Zimmermann copied another [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) , and it was enough to convince Zimmermann to move on to the next game

Richard Parker 1 – Martin Zimmermann 0

### Game 2

Both players started by taking a mulligan down to 6. Zimmerman, now on the play, led off with [Llanowar Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Elves) . It quickly fell to [Geth's Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geth%27s+Verdict) , before [Green Sun's Zenith](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Green+Sun%27s+Zenith) provided an attacker in [Strangleroot Geist](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strangleroot+Geist) . Parker had an undying creature of his own in [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) .

[Huntmaster of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Huntmaster+of+the+Fells) from Zimmerman forced Parker to seriously think things over. Eventually, he chose to attack with his lone creature ( [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) ), play [Mortarpod](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mortarpod) and then [Phantasmal Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phantasmal+Image) copying Strangeroot Geist. Parker copying [Strangleroot Geist](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strangleroot+Geist) rather than [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) clearly revealed that he felt pressurized by Zimmermann's array of 2-power creatures and felt that he needed an untapped blocker to stay in the game.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7e/17/7e1729e340527ced0812cc9567aabab81a3cc0077da118bd8e14bec587f3c89d.jpg)

*Richard Parker*

It was now time to go for the bigger creatures. Zimmermann passed the turn without a play and flipped his Werewolf, turning it into a 4/4 (and turning [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) into a 4/3 while at it). Parker next ran out a 5/5 [Phyrexian Obliterator](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Obliterator) .

Parker next played [Phantasmal Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phantasmal+Image) and exclaimed "So many options!" Indeed, what to copy? [Phyrexian Obliterator](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Obliterator) ? [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) ? [Ravager of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ravager+of+the+Fells) ? Parker eventually went for [Ravager of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ravager+of+the+Fells) , thinking the trigger read "when this creature enters the battlefield or transforms" on *both* sides (which would allow him to ping for 2 damage), but that turned out not to be true. In the end, it didn't matter, as the Image was targeted by Galvanic Blast.

As the game progressed, Zimmermann added [Llanowar Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Elves) to his board and flipped his [Huntmaster of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Huntmaster+of+the+Fells) for an additional Wolf, while [Liliana of the Veil](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+of+the+Veil) and [Fume Spitter](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fume+Spitter) from Parker only served to make the board state more complicated. His mulligans had left Valkeneers without cards in hand by now, and when his opponent's [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) picked up a [Sword of War and Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+War+and+Peace) it was going downhill fast.

Valkeneers's [Honor of the Pure](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Honor+of+the+Pure) at least gave him another four power, but the damage race still favored Diaz. His [Timely Reinforcements](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Timely+Reinforcements) and [Revoke Existence](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Revoke+Existence) for [Honor of the Pure](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Honor+of+the+Pure) didn't exactly change that. One more turn, one more attack, one more trigger from [Sword of War and Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+War+and+Peace) , and it was all over.

Simon Diaz 2 – Tom Valkeneers 0

## Finals - Richard Parker vs. Simon Diaz

*by Rich Hagon* 

You finish higher than 1503, or 1504. Those are the only possibilities left after 18 rounds of Grand Prix action here in Lille. 1505 players began, but there's only room at the summit for one. Will it be Simon Diaz, the Frenchman who has battled quietly and impressively with Delver all weekend? Or will it be Richard Parker, who hasn't lost a single match so far? 1503 or 1504 - there's a big difference.

[Gravecrawler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravecrawler) began for Parker opposite the eponymous (and ubiquitous) [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets) for Diaz. With that information fresh in his mind, he cast [Invisible Stalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Invisible+Stalker) to end the turn.

[Invisible Stalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Invisible+Stalker) dealt the first damage for Diaz, who was looking to race via a new [Sword of War and Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+War+and+Peace) . Diaz cast [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder) , and eventually elected to shuffle the cards away, drawing blind. The Sword equipped his [Invisible Stalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Invisible+Stalker) and attacked. Parker fell to 13, with Diaz gaining 5 life back, taking him to 10.

Parker laid a second [Diregraf Captain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Diregraf+Captain) and turned everything sideways. "I'm dead, yeah?" asked Diaz. "I think so" replied Parker. They were right.

## The Top 5 cards of Grand Prix Lille 2012

*by Frank Karsten* 

Here are five cards that sum up some of the biggest stories of the weekend.

 

### 1\. [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger)

![Geralf's Messenger](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Geralf%27s+Messenger)

The big story for me this weekend was Richard Parker going undefeated with his Blue-Black Zombie deck. Richard remarked that his deck played extremely smoothly and felt a lot like a classic Red Deck Wins deck. [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) was largely responsible for that. It is not only a cheap, aggressive creature, but it also functions as a burn spell that can sap away the opponent's last life points. And that's exactly what Black Deck Wins needs.

With the finals being decided by [Phantasmal Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phantasmal+Image) copying [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) , the Undying Zombie is here to stay. (And with a +1/+1 counter to boot!)

 

### 2\. [Phyrexian Obliterator](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Obliterator)

![Phyrexian Obliterator](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Phyrexian+Obliterator)

When *New Phyrexia* gave us [Phyrexian Obliterator](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Obliterator) , many players rejoiced at the opportunity of playing Mono Black again, but it has taken quite a while for the card to find a viable home. With *Dark Ascension* making the Zombie deck a reality, it was time for [Phyrexian Obliterator](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Obliterator) to shine.

Richard Parker has Obliteraring throughout the Top 8 on the back of his 5/5s, and the quadruple-black creature also formed a big part of Gabriel Nassif's Mono Black Zombies list. The existence of [Phyrexian Obliterator](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Obliterator) as a card to be reckoned with also prompted various players to find creative and exiting answers: anyone who managed to cast [Wrack with Madness](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wrack+with+Madness) on [Phyrexian Obliterator](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Obliterator) this weekend will know what I am talking about.

 

### 3\. [Huntmaster of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Huntmaster+of+the+Fells)

![Huntmaster of the Fells](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Huntmaster+of+the+Fells)

A couple of weeks ago in Honolulu, the ChannelFireball team showed with their Wolf Run Ramp deck that the mythic werewolf would be a powerful force in Standard. While Grzegorz Kowalski made Top 8 here in Lille with a Wolf Run Ramp deck very similar to ChannelFireball's Pro Tour wining list, Lille also saw the rise of another Red/Green deck utilizing [Huntmaster of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Huntmaster+of+the+Fells) .

Jonas Köstler, Martin Zimmermann, and Kristof Benaets all piloted Red/Green Aggro to the Top 8, and it will absolutely be a force to be reckoned with in the future. The fast pressure provided by [Strangleroot Geist](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strangleroot+Geist) is one side of the deck, but Red/Green Aggro is not only about fast starts – the transforming mayhem provided by [Huntmaster of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Huntmaster+of+the+Fells) allows the deck to dominate the board in the late game. I can't put it better than Semifinalist Kristof Benaets' answer to the question of what card won him most matches this weekend: " [Huntmaster of the Fells](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Huntmaster+of+the+Fells) , not close!"

 

### 4\. [Sword of Feast and Famine](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+Feast+and+Famine)

![Sword of Feast and Famine](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Sword+of+Feast+and+Famine)

[Sword of Feast and Famine](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+Feast+and+Famine) was a crucial element of the Caw-Blade decks from Standard a year ago, but it had fallen out of favor since; many players were favoring [Sword of War and Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+War+and+Peace) instead in recent months. But with the metagame switching to Blue-Black control, Zombies, and Red-Green Aggro, protection from Black and Green has become a pivotal ability again.

At the beginning of the weekend, dealers were talking about how [Sword of War and Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+War+and+Peace) was in high demand; and it did not come as a surprise that [Sword of War and Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+War+and+Peace) found its way into many player's maindecks and sideboard. For instance, Kristof Benaets made Top 8 with a 2 [Sword of Feast and Famine](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+Feast+and+Famine) / 2 [Sword of War and Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+War+and+Peace) split rather than the more traditional 4 [Sword of War and Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+War+and+Peace) in his Red/Green Aggro deck. And pretty much everyone I talked to during the tournament mentioned that they would have liked to have additional copies going forward.

 

### 5\. [Champion of the Parish](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Champion+of+the+Parish)

![Champion of the Parish](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Champion+of+the+Parish)

Although no U/W Human deck ended up making the Top 8, U/W Humans was the most popular archetype here on Sunday in Lille, with [Champion of the Parish](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Champion+of+the+Parish) being the signature card of the deck. In the current Standard format, it's hard to think of a more efficient one-drop than [Champion of the Parish](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Champion+of+the+Parish) .

Semifinalist Tom Valkeneers also used the card in his Black-White token deck, hoping for the dream start of turn 1 [Champion of the Parish](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Champion+of+the+Parish) , turn 2 [Gather the Townsfolk](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gather+the+Townsfolk) . Tom credited [Champion of the Parish](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Champion+of+the+Parish) as being responsible for winning most of his matches this weekend, and it's hard to doubt any card that can parallel [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808205130/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl) .
