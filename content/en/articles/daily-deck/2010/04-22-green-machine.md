 Just in time for Earth Day: an all-green *Rise of the Eldrazi* deck! Wizards R&D member Matt Tabak used the above list for gunslinging in Seattle. It’s filled to the brim with brand new *Rise* cards like Emrakul, Ulamog, Pelakka Wurm, and Kozilek's Predator. And you can keep going by using Momentous Fall to draw even more of the powerful new spells. Awesome!
