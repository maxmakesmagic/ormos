After fifteen rounds of Modern, only eight competitors could continue on. Meet your GP Portland Top 8 players!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gppor18/gpPortland18_day2-top8-riecken.jpg)

**Name:** Steven Riecken

**Age:** 28

**Occupation:** Software Developer

**Hometown:** Vancouver, Canada

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**

Top 4 Grand Prix Vancouver (with *Avacyn Restored*)

**What deck did you play today, and why?**

Turbo Druid: The format is skimping on interaction and I'm comfortable with the deck.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

None of them were especially amazing. I just beat 'em.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

First Grand Prix Top 8 wasn't entirely luck! And a sweet trip and competition to look forward to.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gppor18/gpPortland18_day2-top8-geiter.jpg)

**Name:** Daniel Geiter

**Age:** 25

**Occupation:** Community Engagement Specialist

**Hometown:** Spokane, WA

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**

**What deck did you play today, and why?**

I played Golgari Midrange because it was a deck with close matchups that allowed me to leverage decision making.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Surgical Extraction](https://web.archive.org/web/20181210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Surgical+Extraction). I played against many comp decks where the ability to destroy or discard one of their key cards and remove it from the game was very important.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

This TOp 8 validates the thousands of dollars and hours I have spent on this game. Getting to here, my friends and wife show up to watch is moving.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gppor18/gpPortland18_day2-top8-putnam.jpg)

**Name:** Tyler Putnam

**Age:** 24

**Occupation:** A dude

**Hometown:** Spokane, WA (but living in Las Vegas, NV)

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**

I've done some things but this is the best.

**What deck did you play today, and why?**

I played Grixis Death's Shadow. It's always been the best. One mana 10/10s are too hard to resist.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20181210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer) has been MVP all weekend. It killed a lot of Spirits.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

It means I have to catch an early flight in the morning.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gppor18/gpPortland18_day2-top8-kasner.jpg)

**Name:** Aren Kasner

**Age:** 26

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Bellevue, WA

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**

2-3 in the Legacy Grand Prix Seattle

**What deck did you play today, and why?**

Black-Green Elves. I picked it because I know it and like the deck.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Phyrexian Revoker](https://web.archive.org/web/20181210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Revoker) is an answer to many different cards and he paid me well.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

It is great. I did not expect it.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gppor18/gpPortland18_day2-top8-courtney.jpg)

**Name:** Adam Courtney

**Age:** 28

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Reno, NV

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**

PPTQ Top 8s

**What deck did you play today, and why?**

Izzet Phoenix.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Spell Pierce](https://web.archive.org/web/20181210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Pierce). It is a cheap answer to either protect my threat or counter anything that I care about.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

This Top 8 means everything to me. I've been trying to do it for a long time, and having all the work and effort and playtesting with my buddy Gunnar (the person who really helped me become the player I am today) be worth it just means the world.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gppor18/gpPortland18_day2-top8-takahashi.jpg)

**Name:** Yuta Takahashi

**Age:** 32

**Occupation:** Nothing (I live freely!)

**Hometown:** Chiba, Japan

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**

Two Pro Tour Top 8s; 12 Grand Prix Top 8s

**What deck did you play today, and why?**

Blue-Black Faeries. My history of *Magic*.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Nihil Spellbomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20181210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nihil+Spellbomb). I played against three graveyard decks.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

My third win with [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20181210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom)s.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gppor18/gpPortland18_day2-top8-corzo.jpg)

**Name:** Jonathan Corzo

**Age:** 25

**Occupation:** Software Engineer

**Hometown:** Boston, MA

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**

Three PPTQ wins

**What deck did you play today, and why?**

Storm, because it's always the best deck! Been playing it two years and don't plan on stopping anytime soon. Plus, turn two [Empty the Warrens](https://web.archive.org/web/20181210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Empty+the+Warrens) for six-to-ten Goblins is great.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Pieces of the Puzzle](https://web.archive.org/web/20181210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pieces+of+the+Puzzle). It's by far the best way to combat gratuitous amounts of graveyard hate in the metagame.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

This is my first Grand Prix Top 8, and my first Pro Tour invite! It means everything for my *Magic* career and hopefully I can Top 8 in the future.
