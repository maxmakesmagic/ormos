Time to do some hanging out with people from Wizards! Next week we'll have not one, but two awesome opportunities for you to tune in and talk to people here!

### Magic Online Q&A

![](https://i.ormos.uk/77/1b/771b5b7b7779cd1faf43473cdaccf6fc1311e6b32c5e0c4f0fdc234b9788fd5a.jpg)

**When:** Wednesday, April 24, 2013 - 2:30-3:30pm PT  
**Who:** Ryan Spain, Chris Kiritz, and Sean Gibbons (host)

Ryan and Chris will be answering your questions about **Magic Online** questions, specifically focusing on the latest version of the **Magic Online** Wide Beta client.

  
  

### *Dragon's Maze* R&D Chat

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a0/dd/a0dd8f43e090bacdb5272564b0dd26ef6a6413cf4ac36b409398718c71140e29.jpg)

**When:** Thursday, April 25, 2013 - 3:00-4:00pm PT  
**Who:** Mark Globus, Alexis Janson, and Sean Gibbons (host)

Mark and Alexis will talk about their experience making and playing with *Dragon's Maze*.

  
  

Both of these will be taking place on our [Twitch.tv](http://www.twitch.tv/magicprotour) channel during the times above.
