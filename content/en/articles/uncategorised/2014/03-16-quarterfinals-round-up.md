While Nate Price has the full story on the match between Hall of Famer Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa and Sebastian Martinez, here is a recap of what went on in the other Quarterfinal matches. 

**Fernando Pietragallo vs. Demian Tejo** 

Pietragallo's removal stifled any early offerings from Tejo, leaving the Mono-Blue Devotion player with two [Cloudfin Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudfin+Raptor)s at 0/1 while Pietragallo mounted an offense. Out of desperation, Tejo woke up [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) and aimed [Rapid Hybridization](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rapid+Hybridization) at it to try and evolve his creatures. However, [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight) shut that plan down, cementing Pietragallo's lead. Tejo found a reprieve in a freshly drawn [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves), which helped turn the tides, buying Tejo a chance and a much needed breather. When Pietragallo was unable to force through any damage with his opponent at 1, Tejo was able to surmount a big enough offense to steal away the first game. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpba14/qf_pietregallo_tejo.jpg)

*Fernando Pietragallo and Demian Tejo* 

  

The second game was all about [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) on Pietragallo's side, allowing him to overpower anything Tejo had when it was also backed with a little removal. 

While Pietragallo once again had [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) in the third game, it did not come until the fourth turn. It was too late, as Tejo's draw gave him some two power [Cloudfin Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudfin+Raptor)s, and a [Bident of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bident+of+Thassa) threatened to close the door on Pietragallo's chance at the Semifinals. [Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nykthos%2C+Shrine+to+Nyx) also ensured Tejo would be casting the majority of those cards he was drawing, leaving Pietragallo out of options. After a few turns, Pietragallo congratulated Tejo on his win. 

**Demian Tejo defeats Fernando Pietragallo 2-1 and advances to the Semifinals!** 

  

**Eduardo Castro vs. Jose Echeverria** 

[Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) allowed Echeverria to overrun Castro in the first game despite early pressure, including removal and a [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter), fueled by [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections). The life swing from a [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel) made the game close however, and Echeverria was left to send in his whole team, including two 3/2 [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault)s courtesy of [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves). While a second [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel) ensured he was not dead, a second [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) from Echeverria and a flying creature too big for Castro to surpass locked Game 1 up for Echeverria. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpba14/qf_castro_echerverria.jpg)

*Eduardo Castro and Jose Echeverria* 

  

The second game was all about the [Bident of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bident+of+Thassa) for Echeverria, who was able to get his flying creatures through to draw a ton of cards. The avalance of card draw to come left Castro without any options, giving the game and match to Echeverria. 

**Jose Echeverria defeats Eduardo Castro 2-1 and advances to the Semifinals!** 

  

**Maetus Dos Anjos vs. Philippe Monlevade** 

Monlevade had the upper hand with an active [Domri Rade](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Domri+Rade) and [Xenagos, the Reveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xenagos%2C+the+Reveler) in Game 1, while Dos Anjos had a [Thassa, God of the Sea](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thassa%2C+God+of+the+Sea) and the [Bident of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bident+of+Thassa). Unfortunately for Dos Anjos, that was about all that was going on for him for most of the game. [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) turned things around in a big way for Dos Anjos though, giving him a form of card draw with his tokens. However, when [Domri Rade](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Domri+Rade) finally found Polukranos, the World Eather for Monlevade, the game turned back to grim for Dos Anjos. What comeback Dos Anjos mounted disappeared as the World Eater ate the [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves). While an unfortunate attack from Monlevade and a second [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) off the top bought Dos Anjos some time, it was not enough to defeat Monlevade's overwhelming board. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpba14/qf_dosanjos_monlevade.jpg)

*Mateus Dos Anjos and Philippe Monlevade* 

  

The second game went to Dos Anjos, who was able to use [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter) to control the spells that Monlevade would be drawing, courtesy of information provided by [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix). This let him deny a Mizzium Motars for Monlevade, and when nothing further was coming in time, Monlevade moved to a third game. 

Monlevade had his early lead in the third game with a first-turn [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic). [Scavenging Ooze](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scavenging+Ooze) followed, and then a third turn [Polukranos, World Eater](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Polukranos%2C+World+Eater) hit the table. However, Dos Anjos had [Cyclonic Rift](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cyclonic+Rift) to buy him a reprieve, and [Dissolve](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dissolve) to shut down the legendary hydra on its attempted re-appearance. [Doom Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doom+Blade) from Monlevade let him dispose of [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves), and the [Scavenging Ooze](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scavenging+Ooze) suddenly became a threat as it feasted on all of the creatures that had ended up in the graveyard during the previous turns. While [Tidebinder Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidebinder+Mage) locked down the Ooze, giving Dos Anjos a chance to drop [Bident of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bident+of+Thassa) in play, [Vraska the Unseen](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vraska+the+Unseen) off the top gave Monlevade the upper hand again, destroying the Tidebinder and freeing the [Scavenging Ooze](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scavenging+Ooze). While a second [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) from Don Anjos ensured he was not dead, he was unable to deal with Vraska, who ticked up to three loyalty and then dispensed of the second [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves)... 

...right after he cast [Reaper of the Wilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reaper+of+the+Wilds). The scry triggers found Monlevade something he actually wanted, and when another [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) did not come to buy Dos Anjos some time, he offered the handshake. 

**Philippe Monlevade defeats Mateus Dos Anjos 2-1 and advances to the Semifinals!**
