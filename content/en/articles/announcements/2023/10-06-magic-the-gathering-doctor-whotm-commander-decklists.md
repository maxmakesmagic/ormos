Ready to travel across time and space? You can join the Doctor, their companions, or even their enemies in adventures throughout the universe with [*Magic: The Gathering*® – *Doctor Who*™](https://web.archive.org/web/20231006000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/doctor-who) Commander decks. This latest addition to our *Universes Beyond* line-up features four Commander decks themed around different aspects of *Doctor Who*™. Each deck will include a 100-card Commander deck featuring all-new artwork themes from *Doctor Who*™. And with each deck containing 50 new-to-*Magic* cards, there's plenty of fun to be had.

You can browse the cards in these decks with the [*Magic: The Gathering*® – *Doctor Who™*](https://web.archive.org/web/20231006000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/magic-the-gathering-doctor-who/card-image-gallery) Card Image Gallery, including their showcase treatments available in Collector Boosters! Check out [Collecting *Magic: The Gathering*® – *Doctor Who*™](/en/articles/feature/2023/10-03-collecting-magic-the-gathering-doctor-whotm) for everything there is to know about the exciting Booster Fun cards from the set that you won't want to miss.

If you're just looking for the contents of the decks, you've come to the right place! We've got the decklists for all four of the Commander decks right here. With reprints, Planechase cards, new-to-*Magic* cards, and more, there's plenty to see here.

*Magic: The Gathering*® – *Doctor Who*™ releases on October 13, 2023. We hope you'll join us in this celebration of 60 years of Time Lord adventures! You can preorder these decks and more now from your [local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20231006000000/https://locator.wizards.com/), online retailers like [Amazon](https://gtly.to/9UijY2mlD), and elsewhere *Magic* products are sold.
