If you're looking for more data on the state of Standard, Decks of the Week delivers the goods. Along with the addition of 13 more States and Champs Top 8s this week, Magic Online provided 11 Standard premier events for your analysis. If you can't find a deck to take to Friday Night Magic, you're not looking hard enough!

PTQs got back into the swing of things for Kyoto this week as well. If your decklist eyes are glazing over looking at Constructed lists, take a gander at these *Shards of Alara* Booster Draft lists and see what strategies are bubbling up to [win that coveted invite](/en/events/2008/kyoto08-qualifiers/pro-tourkyoto-2009-qualifiers) (and plane ticket) to the first Pro Tour of 2009.

Aside from the bevy of Standard lists from Magic Online, we have a sampling of other formats. If you've been thinking about dipping a toe into *Shards of Alara* Block, Extended, Tribal Wars Standard, Classic, or *Ravnica* Sealed, here are the top lists from this week.
