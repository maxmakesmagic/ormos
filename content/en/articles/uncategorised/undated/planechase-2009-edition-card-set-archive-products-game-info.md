## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/14/b7143ed0ac41f44df8f8105b5f13ab783f88ceec83207f0589a874a5601f8a38.png)Each of the four *Planechase* game packs contains one ready-to-play 60-card deck, one all-new 10-card plane pack, one 6-sided planar die, and a Planechase strategy insert with multiplayer rules.

**Set Name:** *Planechase* (2009 Edition)  
**Number of Cards:** 60 per deck, 240 total  
**Number of Oversized Cards:** 40

**Languages:** English

**OfficialThree-Letter Code:** HOP

[Read “Planechase Rules Revealed”](https://web.archive.org/web/20160619003414/http://magic.wizards.com/node/683856)  
[Read “The Planes of Planechase”](https://web.archive.org/web/20160619003414/http://magic.wizards.com/node/692686)

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Brian Tinsman (lead), Aaron Forsythe, Peter Knudson, and Kenneth Nagle

**Final Game and Deck Development:** Mike Turian (lead), Dave Guskin, Peter Knudson, Scott Larabee, and Mark Purvis, with contributions from Mark L. Gottlieb
