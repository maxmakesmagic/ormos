The **Magic: the Gathering®** Scholarship Series Open will be on July 27th at Magic Weekend in the [Baltimore Convention Center](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603230329/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.bccenter.org/). The 2007 Scholarship Series Championship on July 28-29 is by invitation only, and the Open is your last chance to win an invitation!

**Tournament Location**  
 Baltimore Convention Center  
 Halls A and B  
 One West Pratt Street  
 Baltimore, MD 21201  
 (401) 649-7000

**Eligibility**  
 The **Magic: the Gathering®** Scholarship Series Open is open to DCI members in good standing who are age 18 or younger and do not currently hold an invitation to the Scholarship Series Championship. To see the current invite list, please [click here](/en/events/2007/mss-2007invitations/scholarship-series-championship-2007).

**Format** 

*   Standard Constructed
*   Deck lists are required and will be collected at the 9:30 AM player meeting on Friday
*   Check [www.thedci.com](http://www.thedci.com/) for Standard-format rules, including possible banned cards

**Tournament Length** 

*   7 Swiss Rounds total
*   50 minutes per round

**Advancement** 

*   All players may participate in all Swiss rounds
*   After round 7, all players with 15 or more match points will advance to the Scholarship Series Championship

**Prizes** 

*   All participants will receive a Scholarship Series Premium Card
*   All players with 15 or more match points will receive a second Scholarship Series Premium Card and 9 boosters.

**Notes** 

*   24K, REL 2
*   All Universal Tournament Rules and Magic: the Gathering Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document
*   Players must bring pen, paper, card sleeves and appropriate counters
*   Players already qualified for the Scholarship Series Championship may not participate
