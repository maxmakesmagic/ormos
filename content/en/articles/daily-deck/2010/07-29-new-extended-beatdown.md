Curious what the world of New Extended looks like? Check out this result from a recent event starring the format on **Magic Online**. Naemen managed to go 3-1 with this white and red beatdown deck. It features some powerful attackers in Figure of Destiny, Goblin Guide, and Keldon Marauders. And check out that volley of burn: Burst Lightning, Lightning Bolt, Incinerate, Searing Blaze, and more! While the format may have changed, the beatdown remains!
