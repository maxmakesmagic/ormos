In this round, rising Thai star Veerapat Sirilertvorakul squared off against Singapore's Bernard Mok. The disparity in the size of their last names only added to the rivalry. Sirilertvorakul tore up Day 1 with the sickest sealed deck ever, and has since stayed at the top of the standings. Mok, on the other hand, has had to fight his way up from twenty-seventh.

Mok parised to start. His grip of six was far from great. Sirilertvorakul got things going with [Dwarven Recruiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dwarven+Recruiter). Mok tried [Cabal Torturer](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Torturer). Sirilertvorakul trumped with [Chainflinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chainflinger). Mok's frown reached the floor. [Barbarian Outcast](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barbarian+Outcast) failed to impress.

The [Chainflinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chainflinger) did its job and got [Thermal Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thermal+Blast)ed in return. Sirilertvorakul was holding the brutal [Last Rites](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Rites) - [Violent Eruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Violent+Eruption) combo, but didn't want to wait on Mok's terrible draw. He used the Rites for one, stripping [Gorilla Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gorilla+Titan). His [Pardic Lancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pardic+Lancer) was answered by an emergency [Crippling Fatigue](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crippling+Fatigue) on Mok's side.

Still, Sirilertvorakul was way out in front. He Butchered the Barbarian and made a [Frightcrawler](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frightcrawler). Mok could only manage [Rotting Giant](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rotting+Giant), and it was far too late. [Violent Eruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Violent+Eruption) finished his blocker, and thus, him.

Sirilertvorakul 1 - Mok 0

Mok chose to draw in the second game, and he answered [Barbarian Lunatic](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barbarian+Lunatic) with [Flame Burst](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flame+Burst). However, his three-land hand failed to find a fourth, so Sirilertvorakul was free to get in with [Whispering Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whispering+Shade) and [Pardic Lancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pardic+Lancer).

Mok tried a [Halberdier](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Halberdier). Sirilertvorakul was kind enough not to laugh. He Demised it, and brought in [Hell-Bent Raider](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hell-Bent+Raider), hitting for seven. Mok had another [Flame Burst](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flame+Burst) for the Shade, and [Werebear](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Werebear), but Sirilertvorakul had [Firebolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firebolt) and a replacement Shade. It only took one more attack.

Final Result: Veerapat Sirilertvorakul defeats Bernard Mok 2-0
