There have been some weird tokens created over the years, and with a set like *Eternal Masters*, you're going to catch a lot of strange ones.

Many of the cards that produce these tokens have been previewed during this week. For the rest, check out the [Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2016/05-30-eternal-masters), which has been fully updated as of today!

You can collect all of these when *Eternal Masters* releases June 10!
