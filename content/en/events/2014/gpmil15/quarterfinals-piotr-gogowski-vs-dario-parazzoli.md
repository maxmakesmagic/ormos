One match, nine turns in total. One quarterfinal was somewhat more explosive than the others when Polish Piotr Głogowski and his [Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor) deck took on Italian Dario Parazzoli, who turned up in Milan with the most explosive of Affinity Decks. It even has Contested Warzone, for the full all in attack strategy!  
  
 Głogowski was on the play for the first game, and thought for a long time before keeping an opener of [Khalni Garden](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Khalni+Garden), [Cavern of Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cavern+of+Souls), [Azusa, Lost but Seeking](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azusa%2C+Lost+but+Seeking), [Hive Mind](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hive+Mind), 2 [Tolaria West](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tolaria+West) and a [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan). A decent hand, enabling a turn four [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan), but not fast enough, since the game was over in four turns when Parazzoli's Affinity deck played out as follows:

**Turn one:**[Blinkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinkmoth+Nexus), [Memnit](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memnit)e, [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge)

**Turn two:** Attack for two, drop Głogowski on 18. Play [Mox Opal](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Opal), [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite) and [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating)

**Turn three:** Cast [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter) and [Signal Pest](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Signal+Pest), equip [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating) to [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge), attack for 9, dropping  Głogowski to 9.  
  
**Turn four:** Attack for lethal.

  
  
 As it turns out Affinity with fast draws is one of the hardest decks to fight for Głogowski's deck. He did however come prepared, and sideboarded in 3 [Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm), 3 Seal of Primordial and 1 [Engineered Explosives](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engineered+Explosives), taking out 2 [Hive Mind](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hive+Mind), 2 [Pact of Negation](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pact+of+Negation) 2 [Sleight of Hand](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sleight+of+Hand) and 1 [Azusa, Lost but Seeking](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azusa%2C+Lost+but+Seeking).  
  
 For game two, his opening hand was “slightly” less explosive, with 2 [Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor), [Radiant Fountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Radiant+Fountain), [Cavern of Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cavern+of+Souls), [Simic Growth Chamber](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simic+Growth+Chamber), [Serum Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serum+Visions) and [Azusa, Lost but Seeking](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azusa%2C+Lost+but+Seeking).  
  
 He couldn't have asked for much more, and the second game he returned the favor of Parazzoli's fast start in the first game with the following turns:

**Turn one:** [Radiant Fountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Radiant+Fountain), [Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor)

**Turn two:** Play [Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor), play [Simic Growth Chamber](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simic+Growth+Chamber), tap it for mana between triggers from Amulets, cast [Serum Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serum+Visions) and Azusa, lost but Seeking, and play two more lands.  
  
**Turn three:** Cast [Summoner's Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoner%27s+Pact), find a [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan). Replay [Simic Growth Chamber](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simic+Growth+Chamber), tap it for mana between triggers, cast [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan), search out another [Simic Growth Chamber](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simic+Growth+Chamber) and [Tolaria West](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tolaria+West), return the [Tolaria West](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tolaria+West) to hand, transmute it to find a second [Summoner's Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoner%27s+Pact), find and play a second [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan). Put [Boros Garrison](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Garrison) and [Slay](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slay)er's Stronghold into play, give both Titans haste and attack.  
  
 “Nice turn,” Parazzoli commented as he scooped to the Titan's attacking.  
  
 Two games down, seven turns. Things couldn't get more explosive than this? Could they? Surely not when both players took mulligans to six cards for the decider?

Parazzoli was first out with [Memnit](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memnit)e, [Welding Jar](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Welding+Jar) and a [Mox Opal](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Opal), lacking a land to cast the [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager) in his hand or enough artifacts to fuel out a [Myr Enforcer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Enforcer).

  
  
 Głogowski however, had the most explosive start possible:  
  
**Turn one:** [Radiant Fountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Radiant+Fountain), [Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor)

**Turn two:** [Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor), [Selesnya Sanctuary](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Selesnya+Sanctuary), [Summer Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summer+Bloom), replay the Sanctuary three times, tap it for mana in between triggers and cast [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan). Titan searched for [Boros Garrison](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Garrison) and [Slay](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slay)er's Stronghold, which gave it haste and it attacked for 8, fetching [Ghost Quarter](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Quarter) and a [Gemstone Mine](https://web.archive.org/web/20141214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gemstone+Mine).  
  
 Parazzoli, clearly flabbergasted could only smile and pack up his cards. And the fastest quarterfinal in Modern Grand Prix history was already over.

**Result: Piotr Głogowski defeats Dario Parazzoli 2-1**
