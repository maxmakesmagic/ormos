The noble masticore goes back to Urza's Destiny, when Paolo Parente provided this art for the Famous Original Masticore:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/21/33/2133377463eb9723d859266743e882e5c12bc95009368b97163a3cf7b765b933.jpg) 

Masticore | Art by [Paolo Parente](https://web.archive.org/web/20101101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22paolo+parente%22])

After a brief stop in the art for Deep Analysis, the Masticore got a revamp in *From the Vault: Relics*, when Steven Belledin gave us a leaner version.

The next masticore was *Fifth Dawn*'s Razormane Masticore, from the plane of Mirrodin. Jim Murray provided a more sharp-edged example of the species.

 ![](http://www.wizards.com/global/images/magic/5dn/Razormane_Masticore.jpg) 

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/25/e0/25e00411adf02227de022d4e975647063a360892e2d977deabf962910596730e.jpg)

And that brings us up to *Scars of Mirrodin*, and the ominous Molten-Tail Masticore. Whit Brachna illustrated this mythic monster!
