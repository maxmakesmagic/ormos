Elves have always been a contender in Standard, but *Scars of Mirrodin* gives the tribe a new spell perfect for their big mana ways: Genesis Wave. The new sorcery allows an Elf player to ramp up their mana, then turn that mana into tons of permanents. It could be as good as one for each mana you can pump into the value of ![X Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/b1/6bb1152b0a3305d0a7c2e2eb725b8ebd18620607efc62f6f38b0642b97c8501f.gif). For Mark Miller, the strategy was powerful enough to take him to the top spot of the West Virginia [State Championships](http://www.the2009s.com/), where he defeated Sam Swisher for the title.
