Continue your quest down the Hero's Path at *Theros* Game Day on October 19–20. Gather the Hero Cards that you collected from the *Theros* Prerelease and Launch Weekend, and get ready for a brand-new play experience—Face the Hydra! Here, a few players from the **Magic Online** [Community Cup team](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2013-magic-online-community-cup) got a sneak peek at the experience and survived to tell the tale!
