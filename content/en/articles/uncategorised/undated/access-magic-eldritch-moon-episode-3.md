## Twisted Visions

### Gideon, Knight of Bant

#### Gideon has devoted his life to protecting those in need, a quest that has driven him since losing his fellow soldiers when his Planeswalker spark ignited.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/c2/45/c245c6421fb033da283e9df3b6862ae9a720c28dd02b09c8c050b98f477ec4b3.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/c2/45/c245c6421fb033da283e9df3b6862ae9a720c28dd02b09c8c050b98f477ec4b3.jpg "On Zendikar, Gideon first arrived to find the inhabitants under siege as the plane was overrun with the spawn of the Eldrazi titans who had once laid dormant deep beneath the land.")

### Inspired Charge

#### On Zendikar, Gideon first arrived to find the inhabitants under siege as the plane was overrun with the spawn of the Eldrazi titans who had once laid dormant deep beneath the land.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/ec/98/ec98d1784f9be1cfa6514096fb7e305549f000389896bb0ad7a1d4038e178f4f.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/ec/98/ec98d1784f9be1cfa6514096fb7e305549f000389896bb0ad7a1d4038e178f4f.jpg "Gideon sought help from other powerful Planeswalkers and joined forces with Chandra, Jace, and Nissa. Together, the four managed an epic victory over two Eldrazi titans.")

#### Gideon sought help from other powerful Planeswalkers and joined forces with Chandra, Jace, and Nissa. Together, the four managed an epic victory over two Eldrazi titans.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/90/35/903518b35077d4f37c1a5b40a259efada49f7f89adff78d2607b1b3632d9f0f3.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/90/35/903518b35077d4f37c1a5b40a259efada49f7f89adff78d2607b1b3632d9f0f3.jpg "Jace Beleren left Zendikar alone to seek out Sorin Markov, one of the Planeswalkers who had originally bound the Eldrazi titans thousands of years ago. He began his search for Sorin on Innistrad, at the manor of his old flame, Liliana Vess.")

#### Jace Beleren left Zendikar alone to seek out Sorin Markov, one of the Planeswalkers who had originally bound the Eldrazi titans thousands of years ago. He began his search for Sorin on Innistrad, at the manor of his old flame, Liliana Vess.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/ed/82/ed820c971578194c3561a6a762f4bf5e778cb4dbe840451eda271219aa75c2f2.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/ed/82/ed820c971578194c3561a6a762f4bf5e778cb4dbe840451eda271219aa75c2f2.jpg "With Avacyn's fall, the final Eldrazi titan Emrakul was finally able to enter Innistrad. The titan filled Thraben with her spawn, which soon overwhelmed the Gatewatch.")

#### With Avacyn's fall, the final Eldrazi titan Emrakul was finally able to enter Innistrad. The titan filled Thraben with her spawn, which soon overwhelmed the Gatewatch.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/36/df/36dfcf107b04587aaaa9cbb6877f566f13ab0ea3e0982a0230aff0fd55d5e4ec.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/36/df/36dfcf107b04587aaaa9cbb6877f566f13ab0ea3e0982a0230aff0fd55d5e4ec.jpg "Even in her remote Stensian manor, Liliana Vess was not oblivious to the arrival of Emrakul and the corruption spreading through Innistrad.")

### Liliana, the Last Hope

#### Even in her remote Stensian manor, Liliana Vess was not oblivious to the arrival of Emrakul and the corruption spreading through Innistrad.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/14/8d/148dbc70f452c666fad02d3548b40968d318b393b19b89a402344972d0f481d1.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/14/8d/148dbc70f452c666fad02d3548b40968d318b393b19b89a402344972d0f481d1.jpg "Though she's never been one to volunteer for teamwork, Liliana is well aware of the debt that the Gatewatch would owe for her assistance in their hour of need. What's more, she still has demons of her own that they could help her take care of...")

### Oath of Liliana

#### Though she's never been one to volunteer for teamwork, Liliana is well aware of the debt that the Gatewatch would owe for her assistance in their hour of need. What's more, she still has demons of her own that they could help her take care of...

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/c0/07/c007061a368489b691d3533e03997528ba40d27d37ebc8c5dd24b8418a4d667c.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/c0/07/c007061a368489b691d3533e03997528ba40d27d37ebc8c5dd24b8418a4d667c.jpg "As the Gatewatch teetered on the brink of defeat, an army of zombies poured into Thraben. Immune to Emrakul's effects and to their own losses, the zombies helped to turn the tide of battle.")

### Deploy the Gatewatch

#### As the Gatewatch teetered on the brink of defeat, an army of zombies poured into Thraben. Immune to Emrakul's effects and to their own losses, the zombies helped to turn the tide of battle.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/5b/c5/5bc5091feb3e4ff40b1977b6ef0c91603b4a93653017c3e8c768684e8e2f2d67.jpg)](https://i.ormos.uk/5b/c5/5bc5091feb3e4ff40b1977b6ef0c91603b4a93653017c3e8c768684e8e2f2d67.jpg "Desperate situations called for unlikely allies, and soon the soldier Gideon Jura found himself fighting back to back with an unlikely ally: the necromancer Liliana Vess.")

### Collective Effort

#### Desperate situations called for unlikely allies, and soon the soldier Gideon Jura found himself fighting back to back with an unlikely ally: the necromancer Liliana Vess.
