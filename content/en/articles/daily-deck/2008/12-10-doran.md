In the hands of Israeli standout Uri Peleg, Doran control was powerful enough to win the title of World Champion last year. Has the deck gained anything from the sets that have been released since then? Andrew Olson seems to think so, piloting the above version to the Top 8 of the New Brunswick Champs tournament. Tidehollow Sculler, Figure of Destiny, and Unmake are just a few of the new tools available to players looking to get an edge at this year's World Championships. Will Doran return to the final table? You don't have to wait to find out! **Magicthegathering.com** will be bringing you live coverage all weekend long as the 2008 World Championships commence Thursday, December 11. Tune in to [the live webcast](/en/events/2004/webcast/magic-the-gathering-webcast-schedule) of the Top 8 from Memphis at 9:45 a.m. ET on December 14th.
