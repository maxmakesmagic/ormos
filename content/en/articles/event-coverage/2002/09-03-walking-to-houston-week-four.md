My quest to qualify for Pro Tour-Houston approached its fourth weekend with two PTQ's, two decks, one Grand Prix Trial and one Top 8 finish under my belt. Last week's disaster with the Solitary Confinement had driven me back into the waiting arms of Wild Mongrels and Player Reward Tokens. I still think that the Confinement deck is viable - heck, it won a PTQ - but I still haven't found a *sideboard strategy* that seems viable.

Level 3 judge Eric Smith rarely gets a chance to play **Magic** outside of the occasional Rare Draft (if you have never played in one of these you may get a chance to during Pro Tour Boston - are you listening Scott Larabee?). In addition to being the head judge for the majority of Premiere Events in our neck of the woods, Eric possesses one of the worst mutant abilities I have ever encountered. Bit by a radioactive **Magic** player, he now has the uncanny ability to show up at Neutral Ground exactly one minute after a draft starts. But an event in Philadelphia meant two things... 

One - Eric would get a chance to play, since Dorian Anders is the sheriff in those there parts which leads into...

Two - I would have a reliable ride to Philadelphia.

Adam Rubens ended up joining our party and the three of us left from my house early Saturday morning. We finished up Eric's deck as we drove - a u-g hybrid that relied on Living Wish to pre-sideboard against what seemed to be a broader field of decks than originally suspected.

Members of the freshly dubbed *Young and the Restless* - Chunk, Jeremy Nash, David Chung, Matt Boccio and Sam Greenblatt - favored Psionic Gift as anti-u-w technology but I liked the fact that I could use one card against two decks.
