* * *

The dev team found the bug that was crashing ***Magic** Online* this afternoon and keeping it from automatically restarting. Since then, things seem fairly stable. We haven't gotten as far as we wanted, but the game is up and trading is turned back on. The game will be up overnight and we'll decide in the morning how we'll progress.

In the meantime, I've got an announcement for everyone whose draft or league (*Darksteel* release event or otherwise) was interrupted by the crashes on Thursday, March 4. We won't be able to continue the leagues that were still running when the crashes began. So we'll be compensating all players in the drafts and leagues that were lost during that time.

***Drafts***  
 If you weren't automatically refunded the product for your crashed draft, we'll be refunding you all product required for you to join the draft you were in.

***Regular Leagues***  
 If you were in a regular (non-*Darksteel*) league:
*   We'll refund all product you paid to join the league,  
  

*   You'll get one booster per week the league was in (1 booster for week 1, etc.), and  
  

*   You keep the product you opened for that league.  
  
**Darksteel *Release Leagues***  
 If you were in a *Darksteel* release league:
*   We'll refund all product you paid to join the league,  
  

*   You'll get a copy of the Veridian Zealot avatar for participating, and  
  

*   You keep the product you opened for that league.  
  

**Note:** No matter what *Darksteel* release league you joined, you'll get *exactly* what you paid to get in. For example, if you were in one of the first (4 ticket) leagues that launched, you'll get 4 tickets, 1 *Mirrodin* tournament pack, and 3 *Darksteel* boosters.

In all three cases, while we have information on who was in each event or league and when you joined, we don't have any statistics for games played in those events. That means we can't give out prizes for rankings, nor can we see if anyone had achieved a 5-0 record in the release leagues. I'm sorry that we can't award those prizes, but we just don't have the information required.

We'll start granting product as soon as we can and will have all the grants done by March 31. It's going to take some time to complete this, and some players will receive their product before others do. If you haven't gotten your refund by the end of the day on March 31, please email Customer Service.

I hope this helps make up for the interruption in your normal ***Magic** Online* play. We know the continuing problems affect more than these players; however, we're not out of the woods yet. Thank you for your continued patience. I'll get back with you all tomorrow morning with what's going on.

-Dan
