## PRO TOUR ELDRITCH MOON

SUNDAY COVERAGE

Lukas Blohon was no stranger to the Sunday stage, having been there before back in 2012 at Pro Tour *Dark Ascension*. Fittingly, again on the plane of Innistrad here at Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*, Blohon returned to the playoffs this weekend. This time, he brought White-Black Control with him, overcoming difficult odds and multiple rough match-ups. Many of those gamesended in flavorful fashion as Liliana, the Last Hope and her emblem overran multiple decks with Emrakul, the Promised End, and Blohon didn't stop until he secured the Pro Tour title and the $40,000 check that comes with it.

For Blohon, 2015-16 has been his big year, with his first Grand Prix victory and multiple successful Pro Tours which locked him in Platinum for the next season. His win here in Sydney, Australia cements 2015-16 as the best season of his career. Congratulations to Lukas Blohon, the winner of Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*.
