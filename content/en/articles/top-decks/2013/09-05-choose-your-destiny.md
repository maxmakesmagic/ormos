*Theros* is coming.

  

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/ct/ct263_xmztnyilad.jpg)

From the early teases seen [*Theros* Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20140609081522/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/theros/cig) to today's preview, everything about *Theros* is exciting me. And when I [asked you two weeks ago](/en/articles/top-decks/2013/08-22-old-faithfuls) to share your pass at creating Commander decks around the two legendary creatures from *Theros* available this Friday in [*Duel Decks: Heroes vs. Monsters*](/en/articles/feature/2013/08-19-heroes-vs-monsters) , it's clear many of you feel the same way.

It's time to choose your path on the next step through *Theros*.
