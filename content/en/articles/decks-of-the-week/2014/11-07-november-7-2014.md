Two Grand Prix took place on two different hemispheres last weekend. While [Grand Prix Santiago](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-santiago-2014-coverage-events) was all about Standard, [Grand Prix Nashville](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-nashville-2014-coverage-events) was a first look into the *Khans of Tarkir* Team Sealed format.

In Santiago, many regular archetypes made their appearance, as expected, but decks based on [Whip of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20141107000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos) were the ones that made their big show. Eduardo dos Santos Vieira of Brazil battled his way through 642 other players with his Abzan/graveyard-themed deck, highlighting powerful creatures like [Hornet Queen](https://web.archive.org/web/20141107000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hornet+Queen) recurring with [Whip of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20141107000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos) alongside *Magic 2015* Limited powerhouse [Soul of Theros](https://web.archive.org/web/20141107000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soul+of+Theros).

Speaking of powerhouses, Nashville was a hotbed of pro player activity, with the Top 4 teams featuring twelve players who had at least one thing in common: a minimum of one Grand Prix Top 8 each. When the dust settled, it was the west-coast contingent of Matt Nass, Jacob Wilson, and Jesse Hampton that hoisted the trophies in Tennessee.

We're nearing the final weeks of the Pro Tour Qualifier season for Pro Tour *Fate Reforged* as well. There are still plenty of invitations to the Pro Tour to win. [Be sure to find a PTQ near you](https://web.archive.org/web/20141107000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/protour/qualifierlist) and we hope to see you in Washington DC in February!
