It's been a long time since the UK has had any Magic achievements but this weekend they finally managed to defend a Grand Prix on their home turf for the first time in around 7 years. Now, with a Grand Prix title in Hasselt earlier this year, a second place finish at PT Honolulu and now a second Grand Prix title in Cardiff, UK Magic could be said to be enjoying a renaissance.

It was looking good as Quentin Martin, Martin Dingler and Julian Jardine all drafted their way into a top 8 that also included battle-hardened pro's such as Raphael Levy, Antoine Ruel and Bram Snepvangers. Dingler, also the highest placed amateur, had been drafting green fatties all day and stuck to the same plan on the final table. In the end it paid dividends as he ended up with a very solid green-white-black concoction.

Dingler's fat green men and reasonably consistent mana base were enough to overcome more exotic builds from first Quentin Martin, then Roel Heeswijk before Germany's Wesimo Al-Bacha's four color deck crucially failed to give him a green source of mana in the deciding game of the final.

Congratulations to Martin Dingler, champion of Grand Prix Cardiff.

## Sunday, March 26: 10:43 pm - Top 8 Draft Report

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9c/29/9c29221b85b3f0f628fd5f6c2b944e3c7d3c7a96114a77739480beb2b4147126.jpg)*The Final Table*

In this top 8 draft we decided to follow Antoine Ruel and Roel van Heeswijk who were seated next to each other just like in the last draft. Maybe this way we could discover some patterns. This time Antoine would be passing to Roel though.

Antoine first booster offered him the a very interesting choice between [Dream Leash](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dream+Leash), [Mark of Eviction](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mark+of+Eviction) and [Viashino Fangtail](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viashino+Fangtail). This was mainly interesting as Roel opened a [Mark of Eviction](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mark+of+Eviction) in the last draft and passed it to Antoine. Antoine took that as a signal since he thinks Mark is a first pick. Since Roel knew about this it the Fangtail seemed like a reasonable pick as Antoine would avoid giving mixed signals this way. However Antoine decided that [Dream Leash](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dream+Leash) was just much more powerful and picked the rare.  
 Roel also had a hard pick for his first booster as he himself was going to send some mixed signals. He opened a booster with [Disembowel](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disembowel) and [Dimir Guildmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dimir+Guildmage) and eventually opted for the removal spell.

Roel then picked logically picked the Mark for his second pick.  
 Antoine had to choose between [Belltower Sphinx](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Belltower+Sphinx), [Last Gasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Gasp) and [Master Warcraft](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+Warcraft). He didn't have to think long and took the [Last Gasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Gasp). Antoine then got passed a booster with [Moroii](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moroii) and [Peel from Reality](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peel+from+Reality). Dimir seemed to be wide open on the right so Antoine happily took [Moroii](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moroii).

However, this meant that he kept on sending mixed signals as Roel took [Belltower Sphinx](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Belltower+Sphinx) for his third pick and a [Peel from Reality](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peel+from+Reality) afterwards.

Antoine's fourth pick was Dimir Houseguard so at that point both players had 4 Dimir cards.

In the next couple of boosters Antoine took of color Signets over playable Dimir cards ([Roofstalker Wight](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roofstalker+Wight) and [Perplex](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Perplex)) indicating he likes to keep his options open and values mana acceleration highly.

Roel rounded out the booster with some green cards like [Shambling Shell](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shambling+Shell), [Siege Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Wurm) and [Elvish Skysweeper](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Skysweeper) sensing that Antoine might also be drafting Dimir.

Roel opened his second booster featuring [Vinelasher Kudzu](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vinelasher+Kudzu), [Shambling Shell](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shambling+Shell) and [Tattered Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tattered+Drake). He didn't think long as the Kudzu is clearly the best pick.  
 Roel second picked a Vedalkan Dismisser, passing a [Dimir House Guard](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dimir+House+Guard). He then picked a [Golgari Signet](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golgari+Signet) over [Dimir Infiltrator](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dimir+Infiltrator).

In the meantime Antoine opened [Gleancrawler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gleancrawler) and [Last Gasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Gasp). Although the rare fattie was very appealing he settled for his second [Last Gasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Gasp).  
 Then he made a very interesting pick as he took a off color [Shambling Shell](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shambling+Shell) over a [Tattered Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tattered+Drake), which this Fanatic can't agree with.  
 Dimit House Guard followed and Antoine rounded out pack 2 with [Dimir Infiltrator](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dimir+Infiltrator), [Woebringer Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Woebringer+Demon), [Boros Garrison](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Garrison) and some more Signets.  
 A fairly bad booster for Antoine since Roel picked all the Dimir cards in front of him.

Roel added [Compulsive Research](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsive+Research), [Civic Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Civic+Wayfinder), [Lurking Informant](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lurking+Informant) and some mana fixing (Dimir Aquaduct and [Farseek](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Farseek)).

Antoine wasn't sure which colors he was going to be although Dimir was a certainty with a random [Shambling Shell](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shambling+Shell) tossed in for value.  
 Roel was u/g for sure with a splash of black but he probably had to dip into red in order to get some picks in Guildpact.

## Sunday, March 26: 10:51 pm - Quarterfinals: Quentin Martin vs. Martin Dingler

![](https://i.ormos.uk/be/3f/be3fdf19c87b70119a4fd42fe3a5e13da8a24db9d7def859bb06f495fceaf6bb.jpg)*Quentin Martin*

Sorry Raph, but I'm covering the two English guys. Quentin Martin is the more experienced with two previous Grand Prix top 8's. Martin Dingler is technically an amateur and as the only amateur in the top 8 is already guaranteed a healthy payout. I say technically an amateur. This marks one of the few remaining occasions he has to cash in on his amateur status as he has already qualified for Prague.

This is actually the third time these players have met this weekend. Martin won in the battle of the Sealed Decks, Dingler got his revenge in the draft and then obligingly scooped to let Martin join him in the top 8.

Martin's deck features a lot of power and the risky mana base that marks a lot of the powerful Ravnica draft decks. Dingler has been drafting fat green men all weekend and saw no reason to change now, adding black and white.

Martin won the die roll and elected to kick off. He managed a second turn [Dimir Guildmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dimir+Guildmage), but then the fourth land didn't appear. Dingler capitalized with the fat green men he's been drafting all weekend. A [Greater Mossdog](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Greater+Mossdog) was used to convoke out [Root-Kin Ally](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Root-Kin+Ally) and then he followed with a [Transluminant](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transluminant).

Martin found a slow land, a [Dimir Signet](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dimir+Signet), but then the flow of his deck improved and he found a [Conclave Phalanx](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Conclave+Phalanx) to stem the bleeding a little. Dingler sent in the boys regardless. Martin was tapped out and facing probably any number of combat tricks from the black, white and green mana.

Martin thought for a while and then walked straight into [Seeds of Strength](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seeds+of+Strength).

Ouch.

He dropped [Followed Footsteps](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Followed+Footsteps) on the [Root-Kin Ally](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Root-Kin+Ally). It didn't help now, but would start to count if Martin stabilized. An attack against his empty board put him down to 4 life. Dingler cranked up the pressure with [Siege Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Wurm).

Martin again had to think. He had a copy of the Ally, courtesy of the [Followed Footsteps](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Followed+Footsteps), but needed to kill a creature and find another blocker.

A [Belfry Spirit](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Belfry+Spirit) seemed like the perfect answer. Maybe the ghost and bats could hold off Dingler and give the Footsteps time to bite. Dingler thought again and then sent with everything. Martin could probably block to stay alive, but it was difficult to see how it could be anything other than chump blocks to delay the inevitable.

His copy of an Ally went in the way of the [Siege Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Wurm). Between bats and spirit he could pump the Root-Kin to 7/7. This was smaller than the 8/8 [Siege Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Wurm) a second [Seeds of Strength](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seeds+of+Strength) turned it into. Again Martin was left with an empty board. Dingler added a [Screeching Griffin](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Screeching+Griffin) to his.

Martin made a [Terraformer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terraformer) and picked up another copy of [Root-Kin Ally](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Root-Kin+Ally). Surely he was dead now.

Nope. The [Terraformer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terraformer) gave Martin a mountain. And that enabled him to cast [Lightning Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Helix) on a haunted Mossdog. The Mossdog dying gave Martin two more bats to block with and now he could actually inflict some damage on Dingler's forces. The [Siege Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Wurm), Griffin and [Transluminant](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transluminant) toppled and Martin held on at 3 life. He followed with two more [Transluminant](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transluminant)s and was reduced to chipping through the air for one.

Could Martin actually turn this round? His army of Root-Kin Allies was growing. He added a [Torch Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torch+Drake). Dingler sent everything into the red zone and popped the [Transluminant](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transluminant)s for 1/1 flying spirits.

Martin now finally had a dominant board position, but precious little life. He had only one life point left and was facing three 1/1 fliers with no flying defence of his own. Could he, Houdini-like, summon another card from his library to pull off yet another escape.

Nope, his library of tricks had finally run dry and Dingler finally got to cement a win he must have thought was in the bag turns ago.

Dingler 1-0 Martin.

Martin chose to go first and this time Dingler mulliganed. Martin appeared to have found all the mana fixing in the world as he made two Signets and a Karoo land. Martin Dingler has gone green in all of his drafts and ended up with a fairly monstrous green-white-black deck for the final top 8 draft. His opponent Wesimo Al-Bacha of Germany has a Boros concoction that might not be in the same power level, but it's managed to drag him this far.

I joined the final a few turns in. Al-Bacha had stripped a [Boros Guildmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Guildmage) from Dingler's hand with [Shrieking Grotesque](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrieking+Grotesque) and the German player was slowly winning the damage race. Dingler had a [Selesnya Evangel](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Selesnya+Evangel), but hadn't really had a chance to get the Saproling forces rolling. He convoked out a [Root-Kin Ally](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Root-Kin+Ally) with the help of a [Ghost Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Warden) and [Transluminant](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transluminant).

Al-Bacha had a Trumpeter to keep the Root-Kin occupied. Rather more crucially he had a [Bottled Cloister](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bottled+Cloister) and was slowly drawing Dingler out of the match. His fliers gave him a head start in the race but Dingler was coming back hard with the green fatties. A [Gleancrawler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gleancrawler) gave the Trumpeter a more pressing target to keep at bay.

The [Gleancrawler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gleancrawler) also finally gave Dingler and aerial option as he could sacrifice two [Transluminant](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transluminant)s a turn repeatedly.

This left the game rather interestingly poised with both players now down to 10 life. Al-Bacha had a grotesque card drawing engine in the [Bottled Cloister](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bottled+Cloister), but Dingler was also pulling off some fairly nice tricks with his [Gleancrawler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gleancrawler).

Al-Bacha added a [Conclave Equenaut](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Conclave+Equenaut) and this time passed the turn with no attacks.

Back to his turn Dingler brought back the amazing repeating [Transluminant](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transluminant)s and then sent a [Root-Kin Ally](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Root-Kin+Ally) into the red zone. With the amount of tokens on Dingler's side of the board that could hit for a lot. It was chumping time and the [Mourning Thrull](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mourning+Thrull) was first to go.

Al-Bacha drew a couple of cards and then scooped in the face of the brokenness happening on the other side of the table.

Dingler 1-0 Wesimo Al-Bacha.

Al-Bacha made Dingler go first for the second and immediately found himself under [Caregiver](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Caregiver) beats. Yep, [Caregiver](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Caregiver). After a brief pause to drop a Rot Farm Dingler resumed the beats with [Transluminant](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transluminant). Al-Bacha attempted to keep pace with [Ghost Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Warden) and [Shrieking Grotesque](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrieking+Grotesque), but then fell foul of a [Seeds of Strength](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seeds+of+Strength).

The game was playing out very much the same as the last one. Al-Bacha had found his [Bottled Cloister](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bottled+Cloister) to sort out his long term card advantage, but in the mean time found himself behind on the board as a [Root-Kin Ally](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Root-Kin+Ally) entered play.

Dingler had only one card in hand. A second [Shrieking Grotesque](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrieking+Grotesque), this time enhanced with an [Orzhov Signet](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Orzhov+Signet), left him down to the top of his library. The board still looked good though for the Englishman. The Root-Kin came in again and Al-Bacha had no safe block so he was forced to drop down to 7.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4d/53/4d538850cdb4e819768bc33665e705a7709b13b2c5a1e8c9a4a3424c1ecdfb8a.jpg)*Martin Dingler*

Al-Bacha swung back with two Grotesques, but it only left Dingler at a healthy 14. He untapped them with To Arms and added a [Thundersong Trumpeter](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thundersong+Trumpeter).

Dingler swung with his team and Al-Bacha walked straight into a brutal second [Seeds of Strength](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seeds+of+Strength) that sent both the Grotesques to the 'yard and left Al-Bacha on the critical list with just three life.

His Cloister found him just a Spelunker and [Mourning Thrull](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mourning+Thrull). That might be enough as the Trumpeter was available to tie up the Root-Kin and he was able to survive the next attack (at a terminal 1 life) and keep his Thrull.

Now it seemed like the Cloister was starting to bite. Dingler had finally run out of gas and a [Last Gasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Gasp) was enough to take down the Root-Kin.

For a moment it seemed like Dingler had a window. Al-Bacha attacked with the Spelunker and [Mourning Thrull](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mourning+Thrull). This left just the [Thundersong Trumpeter](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thundersong+Trumpeter) on defense and Dingler was waiting with [Mortify](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mortify). The [Voyager Staff](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyager+Staff) saved it, but the Trumpeter was still out of the game when the turn went to Dingler.

But, before the cards went under the Cloister, Al-Bacha cast [Withstand](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Withstand). The next three points of damage Dingler could inflict would be negated.

Then the window slammed shut with a crash. A [Seeds of Strength](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seeds+of+Strength) on the [Mourning Thrull](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mourning+Thrull) followed by [Blind Hunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blind+Hunter) put the German's life total up to safe levels and eventually the rampant card advantage from the Cloister was enough to level the match.

Dingler 1-1 Al-Bacha.

For the decider Dingler chose to go first and kept his hand with a determined "Keep".

And then my computer battery died again.

Okay so here we are from my notes.

Al-Bacha had to mulligan but again had a first turn [Voyager Staff](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyager+Staff). How many of those things was he running?

I should say the same about Dingler's [Root-Kin Ally](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Root-Kin+Ally). Personally I always like to bash that card but it's been golden for Dingler throughout the top 8. A Grotesque netted a [Pillory of the Sleepless](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pillory+of+the+Sleepless) I was surprised at this but then remembered the [Voyager Staff](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyager+Staff).

Al-Bacha was scuffing about for land. He found a Signet but missed having four mana for a turn. When he found it he failed to make [Bottled Cloister](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bottled+Cloister).

Dingler was biffing with the [Root-Kin Ally](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Root-Kin+Ally) and as he had a [Caregiver](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Caregiver) and [Ghost Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Warden) in support Al-Bacha had no choice except take 5 a turn.

A 2nd Grotesque only hit land but still gave Al-Bacha no way to block the Ally as it cracked in for another five. An [Ostiary Thrull](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ostiary+Thrull) added to Dingler's commanding position.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/70/f7/70f78c9e31395063df1593880d35a55c364c66bf2ea76e33e5d6bebf38b134bc.jpg)*Wesimo Al-Bacha*

The German finally found a [Clinging Darkness](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clinging+Darkness) to stop the walking tree, or at least slow it down. Dingler's next turn was very odd as he attacked with all four of his team: a [Caregiver](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Caregiver), [Ghost Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Warden), [Transluminant](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transluminant) and [Ostiary Thrull](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ostiary+Thrull). Then it all made sense as a [Seeds of Strength](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seeds+of+Strength) wrecked Al-Bacha. He blew the Staff to save his Grotesque. Al-Bacha went for four.

[Last Gasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Gasp) took out the Thrull and gave the German a glimmer of hope. It looked desperate for Al-Bacha as a Warden supported Tranluminant dropped him to 1 life.

Like the last game he refused to lie down as [Blind Hunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blind+Hunter) clawed some life back both through coming into play and its haunt effect.

Al-Bacha was getting more and more frustrated with his draws as the game progressed. He threw a [Boros Signet](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Signet) onto the ground in disgust. He was forced into another chump block. The next card was a useless mountain and Al-Bacha finally extended his hand.

He'd been desperate for green mana, completely unable to cast the [Loxodon Hierarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Hierarch), [Streetbreaker Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Streetbreaker+Wurm) and [Seeds of Strength](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120072059/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seeds+of+Strength) in his hand.

No matter, Dingler had won. After waiting for over seven years the UK finally got to defend a Grand Prix on their home turf.

Martin Dingler beat Wesimo Al-Bacha 2-1 and is the winner of Grand Prix Cardiff.
