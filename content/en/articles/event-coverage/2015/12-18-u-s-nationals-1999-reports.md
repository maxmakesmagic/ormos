**![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/USNAT99/images/AwardsTeam.jpg)**Welcome to Sideboard Online's ongoing coverage of the 1999 U.S. Nationals. We will be posting round-by-round pairings, standings and match reports. Keep checking back for updates. 

**Congratulation to Kyle Rose - U.S. National Champion! Congratulations also to John Hunka and Christopher Tobler and Zvi Mowshowitz who have won spots on the U.S. National Team.** 

*Last update: Sunday, July 4, 1999 - 4:00 P.M. PST* 

### Finals

[Kyle Rose (3) def John Hunka (8), 3-2](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/http://archive.wizards.com/article.asp?USNAT99/d3feature4)  
 Christopher Tobler (5) def Charles Kornblith (2), 3-0  
 Zvi Mowshowitz (7)def Stephen McArthur (4), 3-2  

### Semifinals

John Hunka (8) def Stephen McArthur (4), 3-0  
[Kyle Rose (3) def Charles Kornblith (2), 3-2](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/http://archive.wizards.com/article.asp?USNAT99/d3feature3)  
 Christopher Tobler (5) def Brian Selden (1), 3-1  
 Zvi Mowshowitz (7) def David Humpherys (6), 3-1
