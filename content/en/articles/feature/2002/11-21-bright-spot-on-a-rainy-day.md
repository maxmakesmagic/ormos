### Some Message Board Humor From The Community

* * *

So, it's been a long couple weeks working on updating and editing help files (yes, I have that immensely fun job, too) and I go in to check out the message boards. I just have to send props out to Megan for starting the "You know you've been playing too much ***Magic** Online* when..." thread. I agree that there is an awful lot of gloominess on the boards and it's nice to come upon a bright spot in the midst.

For those of you who don't pay much attention to the boards, here's wrathofmegs' original posting:  
  
 You know you've been playing too much ***Magic** Online* when:  
  
 ...you tap your mouse sideways when browsing the Internet.  
  
 ...your clothes closet is organized into new/casual/serious.  
  
 ...you wonder why there are so many political activists against drafting.  
  
 ...the online cardshop knows you by name.  
  
 ...you get kicked out from an auction because you constantly bid "0" on things you don't like.  
  
 ...you excel at your stock broker's job b/c you're always in all 5 trade rooms.  
  
 ...you have 128 different ways of hitting F2.  
  
 ...you buy nothing but muscle t-shirts since your cards don't need sleeves either.  
  
 ...you argue with the cop that ONE speeding ticket should not be worth $100.  
  
 ...you're on chrisg's buddy list.  
  
 ...European players forget that you're not in their timezone.  
  
 ...you go to the Library of Congress an expect an Alpha Air Elemental.  
  
 ...you refer to stopping at a stop light as "auto-yielding."  
  
 ...you forget how to shuffle real cards.  
  
 And finally...  
  
 ...you post something like this on the ***Magic** Online* boards. 

As of my writing this article, the thread had hit four pages. Want to read the rest? Want to add your own? Check out: [http://boards.wizards.com/tcg/ultimatebb.php?ubb=get\_topic;f=114;t=001779;p=](https://web.archive.org/web/20021121000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://boards.wizards.com/tcg/ultimatebb.php?ubb=get_topic;f=114;t=001779;p=&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm112102)

And just to add my own .02:  
  
 You know you work on ***Magic** Online* too much when you rarely get to play an actual game.
