The leader of the Rakdos Cult is a demon named Rakdos, which is easy to remember. When Rakdos first appeared in the *Dissension* set, it was as [Rakdos the Defiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20121212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos+the+Defiler):

![Rakdos the Defiler](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Rakdos+the+Defiler)

  

And here's a closer look!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b0/b7/b0b7e971cf616cbd7af51360ab546830c0a686ebeb16bc9befa8fc31b986a6e1.jpg)

[Rakdos the Defiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20121212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos+the+Defiler) | Art by [Zoltan Boros & Gabor Szikszai](https://web.archive.org/web/20121212000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Zoltan%20Boros%20&%20Gabor%20Szikszai%22%5D)

In the time since the original *Ravnica: City of Guilds* block, many things have changed in the world of Ravnica. But Rakdos is still around! This time, he's [Rakdos, Lord of Riots](https://web.archive.org/web/20121212000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos%2C+Lord+of+Riots).
