This week's desktop wallpaper is *two* wallpapers! As a special preview of *Duel Decks: Divine vs. Demonic* (which is going to hit the stores on April 10, incidentally), we're going to show you two angels: [Akroma, Angel of Wrath](https://web.archive.org/web/20090227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroma%2C+Angel+of+Wrath) (by Chippy) and [Luminous Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20090227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Luminous+Angel) (by Jason Chan)! Not demonic enough for you? Come back next week!
