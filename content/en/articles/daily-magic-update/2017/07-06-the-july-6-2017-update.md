# Deck of the Day

1.  Second Sun Fevered Fog

For some reason, I keep running into this deck on *Magic Online*—and losing.

Now, to be fair, I've been doing a lot of losing lately in Standard as I try out new things, but this one was especially eye-opening as I went through my "attack with creatures" phase (ew), because I could just never kill them unless the deck faltered.

This four-color deck is reminiscent of the Turbo Fog decks of old, which used [Howling Mine](https://web.archive.org/web/20170706000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Howling+Mine) (cards that have everyone drawing more cards every turn) effects alongside [Fog](https://web.archive.org/web/20170706000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fog) effects (cards that say "Prevent all combat damage" or some variation thereof). In this case, the [Howling Mine](https://web.archive.org/web/20170706000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Howling+Mine) is [Fevered Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20170706000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fevered+Visions) and the Fog effects are legion—[Haze of Pollen](https://web.archive.org/web/20170706000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Haze+of+Pollen), [Commencement of Festivities](https://web.archive.org/web/20170706000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Commencement+of+Festivities), [Encircling Fissure](https://web.archive.org/web/20170706000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Encircling+Fissure), and even [Repel the Abominable](https://web.archive.org/web/20170706000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Repel+the+Abominable). The rest of the deck is made up of sweepers and card draw and the deck's main win condition, [Approach of the Second Sun](https://web.archive.org/web/20170706000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Approach+of+the+Second+Sun). Note that having [Fevered Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20170706000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fevered+Visions) in play speeds up the time by which you'll re-draw your second Sun. It seems Nicol Bolas has a fever, and the only cure is more cowbell [Approach of the Second Sun](https://web.archive.org/web/20170706000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Approach+of+the+Second+Sun).

Actually, the cure is [Negate](https://web.archive.org/web/20170706000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Negate). And [Fragmentize](https://web.archive.org/web/20170706000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fragmentize). And [Cast Out](https://web.archive.org/web/20170706000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cast+Out). Etc., etc. The deck is a bit of a glass cannon, but boy is it effective if all your opponent is trying to do is attack you with creatures. I would actually recommend looking into a transformational sideboard against control decks, or at least playing some number of [Sphinx of the Final Word](https://web.archive.org/web/20170706000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx+of+the+Final+Word).

This list, by the way, came courtesy [@jakejoelmtg](https://twitter.com/jakejoelmtg), who were kind enough to respond to my call for lists yesterday. And for their generosity, I'll mention that they have a YouTube channel called Jake and Joel are Magic. They publish pretty regularly, and [you can check out their stuff here](https://www.youtube.com/jakeandjoelaremagic).
