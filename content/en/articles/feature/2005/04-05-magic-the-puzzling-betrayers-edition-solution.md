### Puzzle 1 Solution

Tap all of your land for ![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Black Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/6b/e16bcf75cffdd1079b6ba1f9f43c9e6e7253bb89521acdf6b3e93d3c2e63dbb5.gif)![Black Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/6b/e16bcf75cffdd1079b6ba1f9f43c9e6e7253bb89521acdf6b3e93d3c2e63dbb5.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif).

Use ![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif)![Black Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/6b/e16bcf75cffdd1079b6ba1f9f43c9e6e7253bb89521acdf6b3e93d3c2e63dbb5.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif) and tap That Which Was Taken to put a divinity counter onto your Myojin.

3.  Remove the divinity counter to destroy all lands.

4.  Cast Crack the Earth. You will sacrifice your Myojin, and Nina will sacrifice one of her creatures.

5.  Use ![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif) to cast Eternal Witness, returning Crack the Earth to your hand.

6.  Cast Crack the Earth. You will sacrifice your Witness, and Nina will sacrifice her other creature.

7.  Cast Goryo's Vengeance to put your Myojin back into play.

8.  Attack with your Myojin for 7 damage. It will not be prevented by the Shoal, since the game considers this a different creature than the one whose damage was prevented.

**Non-land Cards Used:** 

*   That Which Was Taken
*   Myojin of Ininite Rage
*   Eternal Witness
*   Crack the Earth
*   Goryo's Vengeance

### Puzzle 2 Solution

1.   Tap Forests for 7 green mana.

2.   Cast Sakiko, Mother of Summer.

3.   ![Patron of the Orochi](https://i.ormos.uk/ba/c6/bac6ce28cd33c87fd344d54f2627a06de7f1a75a51ebfb55b45f36ed77cd3a19.jpg)

     Tap Patron of the Orochi to untap your Forests and itself.

4.   Tap 3 Forest and cast Unchecked Growth on Patron, splicing on Kodama's Might (targeting Patron).

5.   Attack with Patron and Moss Kami. Your opponent is forced to block Moss Kami, but may choose not to block Patron.

6.   Tap your Forests for 4 green mana.

7.   If he blocks:

     1.  Cast Kodama's Might (targeting Patron), splicing on Roar of Jukai. (opponent gains 5, going to 47)

     2.  Cast Roar of Jukai.

     3.  Assign all the possible trample damage to your opponent. Your 9/9 Moss Kami will deal 8 damage, and your 19/19 Patron will deal 16, for 24 total damage.

     4.  24 Green mana will be added to your mana pool, and Benedict is at 23.
8.   If he does not block:

     1.  Cast Kodama's Might (targeting Patron).

     2.  Cast Roar of Jukai.

     3.  Your 17/17 Patron and 5/5 Moss Kami will deal a total of 21 damage to your opponent (1 trample damage will be absorbed by the Screen).

     4.  21 Green mana will be added to your mana pool, and Benedict is at 21.
9.   Play and tap your Mountain.

10.  Fireball your opponent for the remainder of his life.

**Non-land Cards Used:** 

*   Moss Kami
*   Patron of the Orochi
*   Neurok Hoversail
*   Kodama's Might
*   Unchecked Growth
*   Sakiko, Mother of Summer
*   Fireball
*   Roar of Jukai

### Puzzle 3 Solution

1.  Attack with each Mistblade Shinobi and Loam Dweller. None can be blocked.

2.  Tap 2 Forests, 2 Islands, and both Birds for ![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Black Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/6b/e16bcf75cffdd1079b6ba1f9f43c9e6e7253bb89521acdf6b3e93d3c2e63dbb5.gif).

3.  ![Clash of Realities](https://i.ormos.uk/39/a2/39a25929b516d830b00c6ee1355c64fc1e4da304437c7bef239dceabd2552c33.jpg)

    Before assigning combat damage, use ![Black Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/6b/e16bcf75cffdd1079b6ba1f9f43c9e6e7253bb89521acdf6b3e93d3c2e63dbb5.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif) and return a Shinobi #1 to your hand to Ninjutsu Throat Slitter into play, targeting a spirit with the Clash of Realities trigger.

4.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) and return the Shinobi #2 to your hand to put Shinobi #1 into play, targeting another spirit with the Clash of Realities trigger.

5.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) and return the Shinobi #1 to your hand to put Shinobi #2 into play, targeting a third spirit with the Clash of Realities trigger.

6.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) and return the Loam Dweller to your hand to put the Shinobi #1 back into play, targeting the final spirit with the Clash of Realities trigger.

7.  Assign combat damage and let it resolve, triggering each Ninja's special ability. Choose three different targets to get rid of 3 more creatures. Lando falls to 2 life.

8.  After combat, tap 2 Forests and cast Loam Dweller. Use the Clash of Realities trigger to kill Lando's final creature.

9.  Tap your Mountain for ![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif) and cast Lava Spike, targeting Lando, to finish him off.

**Permanents Used:** 

*   2 Mistblade Shinobi
*   Loam Dweller
*   2 Birds of Paradise
*   Clash of Realities
*   Throat Slitter
