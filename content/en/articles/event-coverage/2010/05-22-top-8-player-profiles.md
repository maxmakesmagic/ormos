**Would you change anything about your deck in light of the weekend?**

 Nope, testing with friends whom are going

[Gideon Jura](https://web.archive.org/web/20100522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon+Jura), that guy is the real deal!
