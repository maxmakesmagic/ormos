This guy has been popping out of boosters since the inception of **Magic**: 

![Llanowar Elves](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Llanowar+Elves)

So it's only right that, for this year's return of [Llanowar Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20050816000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Elves) to the [Core Set](https://web.archive.org/web/20050816000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/expansion/9e), their look be consistent with Anson Maddocks's original piece.

Here's Kev Walker's sketch and final art for the updated [Llanowar Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20050816000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Elves).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5d/e0/5de04b02192f7859997427a83dde3b1494f095694aa28a6db5ba7c59f17a6260.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/49/4f/494fecf494debc0198cd6bb05c16bbc3f94c72475da281c437e8b4b77b3a2ef7.jpg)

Compare that with the look of other elves from the forests of Llanowar. Here are [Nomadic Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20050816000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nomadic+Elf), [Llanowar Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20050816000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Knight) and [Llanowar Elite](https://web.archive.org/web/20050816000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Elite).
