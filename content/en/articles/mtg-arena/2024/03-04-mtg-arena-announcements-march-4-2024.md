## *Alchemy: Karlov Manor* Arrives Tomorrow!

![](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/MTGMKM_ALC.jpg) 

We have 30 new cards coming to *MTG Arena* tomorrow, March 5, with the release of *Alchemy: Karlov Manor*. These cards are legal in Alchemy, Historic, Timeless, and Brawl formats, and the set includes the new mechanic incorporate.

Check out all 30 of the cards in the [*Alchemy: Karlov Manor* Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20240304000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/card-image-gallery/alchemy-karlov-manor).

## Alchemy Rebalances

We have two rebalances to announce.

Fragment Reality has created an unfun early game combo with multiple Leyline cards that works even when taking a mulligan to four or three cards. This has resulted in a powerful and consistent deck that is taking up too much space in Historic and Timeless metagames.

Geological Appraiser has proven it's a bit too aggressive for our current Alchemy and Historic formats. By slowing down this card, discover-based decks will be better balanced across the formats.

![Fragment Reality rebalanced Alchemy card](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/81083_Printed_FragmentReality_kl5xqg8u6zr3.png) 

**Fragment Reality** 

*   *Changed*: "Exile target nontoken artifact, creature, or enchantment."
     *To*: "Exile target nontoken artifact, creature, or enchantment **an opponent controls**."

## *Murders at Karlov Manor* Store Championship

[*Murders at Karlov Manor* Store Championship](https://web.archive.org/web/20240304000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/play-events/store-championship) is a chance for you to show your *Magic* skill and earn promo cards while having fun at [a WPN game store near you](https://web.archive.org/web/20240304000000/https://locator.wizards.com/). All rounds of this event feature [Standard Constructed](https://web.archive.org/web/20240304000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/standard).

One way to prepare your Standard deck is by checking out [Frank Karsten's latest Metagame Mentor article](https://magic.gg/news/metagame-mentor-murders-at-karlov-manor-standard-at-magiccon-chicago) that dives into the Standard metagame from the $75K Standard Open and the Standard Cup events held during the recent MagicCon: Chicago.

Then, once you've assembled your Standard deck, put it through its paces on *MTG Arena* for some fine tuning before heading out to [your local WPN game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20240304000000/https://locator.wizards.com/) for the *Murders at Karlov Manor* Store Championship event! While supplies last, everyone who participates gets a Mortify promo card. Make it to the Top 8, and you can get an Angel of Despair. Take the top winner spot, and a textless Dauthi Voidwalker is the prize!

![Midnight Clock, art by Alexander Forssberg](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/cardart_midnight_clock_alexander_forssberg.jpg)

## Competitive Play Schedule

All times listed are Pacific time (UTC-08:00). Note that daylight saving time in the US begins March 10 and UTC shift changes to -07:00.

### Premier Play

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events.

Qualifier tokens are delivered to your *MTG Arena* inbox. Remember to claim them before the event starts!
