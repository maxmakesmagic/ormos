If you were watching the coverage of Pro Tour–San Juan, you know that *[Archenemy](https://web.archive.org/web/20130721202513/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/archenemy/productinfo)* was played all weekend in the Champion Challenge. And we've already covered [how to play *Archenemy*](/en/articles/feature/2010/05-17-archenemy-rules-revealed). Which means that you might have learned what some cards do. So why should we beat around the bush? Say hello to one of the *Archenemy* decks!
