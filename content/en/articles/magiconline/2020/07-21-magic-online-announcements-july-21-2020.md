## *Double Masters* Events Start Next Week!

Surprise! We've moved the release of *Double Masters* events up to Friday, July 31! Leagues will start at 10 a.m. PT with Sealed Deck, Draft, and Phantom Draft available. In addition, we're kicking off the *Double Masters* season with a bang and have a Super Qualifier on Sunday, August 2! If you haven't checked out the updated *Magic* Premier Play schedule yet and what Super Qualifiers qualify you for, go [read that announcement](https://magic.gg/news/announcing-the-2020-2021-magic-esports-season).

* * *

## *Magic Online* Meets Gen Con Online!

We are happy to announce that *Magic Online* is joining forces with Gen Con and Pastimes this year to provide some unique *Magic* experiences, though, due to the pandemic, we're bringing the fun to our house! The *Magic* at Gen Con Online events start on July 30 and run through August 2. We'll be hosting the events, but Pastimes will be taking care of the entry and the prizes for these special events.

The event formats include Vintage, Legacy, *Double Masters*, and a special Gen Con Cube. In addition, we're testing a new feature with Gen Con Online that will let players build decks for the Vintage and Legacy events without having the cards in their collection! Check out the article later this week to see all the details, or head over to [Gen Con's website](https://www.gencon.com/event_finder?search=Magic+Online) to sign up.

## Limited Split Championship Qualifier Saturday

On Saturday, July 25, there will be a *Core Set 2021* Sealed Split Championship Qualifier at 7 a.m. PT! The entry fee is 40 MOCS Qualifier Points. The winner qualifies for the *Zendikar Rising* Split Championship on *MTG Arena*.

Learn more about Split Championship Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020#ptq).

* * *

## Modern Split Championship Qualifier Sunday

On Sunday, July 26, there will be a Modern Split Championship Qualifier at 7 a.m. PT! The entry fee is 40 MOCS Qualifier Points. The winner qualifies for the *Zendikar Rising* Split Championship on *MTG Arena*.

Learn more about Split Championship Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020#ptq).

It's simply overflowing with iconic cards from *Magic*'s almost 27-year history, and you can draft the super-deck of your dreams when it arrives tomorrow, July 1, and runs for nearly the whole month, ending July 29.

You can view the change list below or, for a full card list, click [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/2016/11-30-vintage-cube-cardlist).

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format Challenges are the first step toward qualifying for Premier Play through your favorite *Magic* formats. To learn more about Premier Play qualification, click [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020).

Beginning this weekend, July 25, Pioneer Challenges will have a minimum of 32 players, down from 64. Please note that the prize structure will be updated accordingly, as listed on the [Format Challenges page](https://web.archive.org/web/20200721000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-format-challenges).

*Magic Online*'s Format Challenges now run once on Saturdays and once on Sundays for each Constructed format! On weekends featuring Showcase Challenge events, the Format Challenge that starts at either 6 a.m., 8 a.m., or 10 a.m. Pacific time is pre-empted by the Showcase Challenge—Format Challenges scheduled at other times remain in place. *NOTE: The Pauper Challenge is never pre-empted.*
