It's Nationals week on Daily Decks, and we'll be highlighting some of the best decks the 2009 Nationals tournaments have to offer! Finland's Mikko Airaksinen, for example, took a strategy popularized by Jon Loucks to the top slot. How does it work? Play a ton of cantripping artifacts like [Kaleidostone](https://web.archive.org/web/20090727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kaleidostone) and [Howling Mine](https://web.archive.org/web/20090727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Howling+Mine) early, then use [Time Sieve](https://web.archive.org/web/20090727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Time+Sieve) to sacrifice them and draw an extra card. From there, [Open the Vaults](https://web.archive.org/web/20090727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Open+the+Vaults) allows you to get them all back and rinse, wash, and repeat!
