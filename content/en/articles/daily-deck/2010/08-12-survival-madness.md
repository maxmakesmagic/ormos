With a lot of buzz behind it, Caleb Durward's Survival of the Fittest Madness deck served him well on the weekend. Like a traditional green-blue Madness deck from the days of *Odyssey* Block Constructed and 2002-2003 Standard, Caleb can beat down with the best of them while holding back some countermagic. But Survival of the Fittest adds some extra oomph; by cycling through his creatures he can fill his graveyard with Vengevines, then "discard" two Basking Rootwallas. Thanks to their madness triggers, they hit the stack instead of the graveyard, in turn bringing back the Vengevines, and voila! You have a very lethal attack. Read more at the [official coverage](/en/events/2010/gpcol10/saitos-legacy-continues).
