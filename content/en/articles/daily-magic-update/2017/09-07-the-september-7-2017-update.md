# Deck of the Day

1.  [Four-Color Treasure for the New *Ixalan* Standard](https://www.channelfireball.com/articles/4-color-treasure-for-the-new-ixalan-standard/) | ChannelFireball | Frank Karsten

Leave it to Frank Karsten to create something that makes you multiply. The key to the deck is [Skyship Plunderer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skyship+Plunderer), which is, yes, a Pirate. It also just so happens to work really well with Jace, Cunning Castaway, which in turn works pretty well with [Anointed Procession](https://web.archive.org/web/20170907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anointed+Procession). Here's the key interaction, straight from Frank:

*The idea is as follows. You start on turn two with [Skyship Plunderer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skyship+Plunderer). Then, on turn three, you cast Jace, use his +1 ability, attack with [Skyship Plunderer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skyship+Plunderer), loot, and use the trigger to bring Jace up to 5 loyalty. (Meanwhile, your Temur Energy opponent hasn't affected the board yet because they kept [Aether Hub](https://web.archive.org/web/20170907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Hub), [Botanical Sanctum](https://web.archive.org/web/20170907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Botanical+Sanctum), Mountain, [Attune with Aether](https://web.archive.org/web/20170907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Attune+with+Aether), [Whirler Virtuoso](https://web.archive.org/web/20170907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whirler+Virtuoso), [Bristling Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20170907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bristling+Hydra), and [Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer) on the draw.)*

*On turn four, you cast [Anointed Procession](https://web.archive.org/web/20170907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anointed+Procession), and this is where the fun begins. You immediately ultimate Jace, giving you four copies of the no-longer-legendary planeswalker. You activate each copy's -2 ability, which again produces double the tokens, yielding a grand total of 16 power on turn four. Oh, and you still have four planeswalkers lying around in case that somehow isn't enough to win the game.*

The rest of the deck is filled with all kinds of synergy helped out by Treasure tokens and Treasure Map. It's the first fully unique deck I've seen on the internets using *Ixalan*, so definitely check it out to get your brewer's brain going.
