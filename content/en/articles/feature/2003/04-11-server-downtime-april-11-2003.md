### List Of Changes

* * *

### Changes/New Features

*   **Hide reminder text** available on the **Display Preferences** screen. (Replaces **Show English card text**.)  
  

*   Players can draw cards and reveal their hands at the end of games. These features are available before the OK button is clicked to dismiss the game.  
  

*   The **Draft 8-player** and **Constructed 8-player** rooms now have blind queues. The chat area notifies players in the queues when players join or leave. Chatting in the window *will* display that player's name next to each comment.

### Bug Fixes

*   Fixed some card interaction bugs.  
  

*   Fixed some problems regarding the **Adjourn** and **Replay** functions along with reconnecting.  
  

*   Fixed **/info** bug that made it impossible to use on the same player more than once.  
  

*   Fixed **Comment** field word wrapping/clipping on the **Player profile** window.
