# Who is the best Standard player in the room today, and why?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_QQYuuyaTakahashi.jpg)  
**Three-time Grand Prix Champion Yuuta Takahashi:** Me... (or anyone playing Abzan Aggro)... or Makihito Mihara!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_QQHuangHaoshan.jpg)  
**5 Grand Prix Top 8s, Huang Hao-Shan:** Yuuya Watanabe. I don't really need to defend my answer very much.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_QQLeeShiTian.jpg)  
**Four-Time Pro Tour Top 8 Competitor, Lee Shi Tian:** Kuo Tzu Ching. He has 11 Grand Prix Top 8s, mostly in Constructed Formats. I'm surprised he hasn't won a Grand Prix yet! (Just kidding! Hee hee!)

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_QQTeruyaKakumae.jpg)  
**Two-time Grand Prix Champion, Teruya Kakumae:** Nam Sung Wook. He is nsw1074 on *Magic Online* and is leading the MOL POY Standings!
