The [*Magic: The Gathering* Online Championship Series](https://web.archive.org/web/20160505000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2016-magic-online-championship-series), also known as MOCS, is a series of events in which sixteen of the top *Magic Online* players can earn an invitation to the annual in-person *Magic Online* Championship event, where they compete for a prize pool of $116,000. Aside from the defending *Magic Online* Champion and the current *Magic* World Champion, other players can qualify for the *Magic Online* Championship by climbing their way to the Top 6 positions on the [MOCS leaderboard](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/magic-online-leaderboard-points) or being one of the Top 2 players in a Monthly Playoff Event.

In his latest video, streamer extraordinaire Kenji Egashira gives a play-by-play of the April 30 MOCS Playoff Event. Kenji analyzes the matchups and the deck tech, and tells the tale of how a Top 8 event between the best players on *Magic Online*—and the world—turned out. Enjoy!
