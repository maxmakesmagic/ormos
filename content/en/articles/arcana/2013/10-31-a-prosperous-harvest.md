M**agic** and I go back a long ways. I have very fond memories of casting Prosperity when it was printed in *Visions*. That's one of the pieces of art from **Magic** which is indellibly inked into my mind.

 ![3647](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&MultiverseId=3647&options=) 

  

To me, Prosperity is perhaps the first card I can remember where I realized that I could "mill" my opponents out by making them draw cards as well as simply using Millstone like effects. Those sort of revelations are quite powerful as they open a whole new aspect of the game up to you,

After being printed in both *Visions* and *Sixth Edition*, Prosperity returned in the introductory product *Portal* with new art, this time by Phil Foglio, showing two people living "the good life."

 ![4273](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&MultiverseId=4273&options=) 

  

And now it is back again in *Commander* (2013 Edition) with a beautiful piece of new art by [Nic Klein](https://web.archive.org/web/20131031000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22nic%20klein%22]).

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/arc1354_art.jpg) 

Prosperity | Art by [Nic Klein](https://web.archive.org/web/20131031000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22nic%20klein%22])

If you look at that art as it is blown up you can realize that the mage is strolling down a path of solid silver, and those trees aren't in some autumnal season—they are actually gold bearing gold fruit! Now that's a prosperous harvest!

 ![376460](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&MultiverseId=376460&options=)
