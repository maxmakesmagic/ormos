How far will you go to do what you must? Go on a journey with Walking the Planes to [Grand Prix Strasbourg](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/gpstr13/welcome), where we meet a Hall of Famer, a Goblin gamer, a star of the stream, and a wayward American chasing a dream. Like moose in mating season, there's some awkward and silly encounters that you won't soon forget. It's Episode #12: "The Stranger!" 

Follow Walking the Planes on Twitter ([@WalkThePlanes](https://twitter.com/walktheplanes)).
