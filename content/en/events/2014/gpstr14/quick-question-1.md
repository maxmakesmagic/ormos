We asked some of the best players in the room about the most underrated card in *Khans of Tarkir* Limited. Here's what they had to say:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpstr14/qq_dickmann.jpg)

***Patrick Dickmann:** [Secret Plans](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Secret+Plans) and [Trail of Mystery](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trail+of+Mystery) used to be underrated but, I guess, not anymore. [Tormenting Voice](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tormenting+Voice) still is much better than it gets credit for.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpstr14/qq_edel.jpg)

***Willy Edel:** [Jeskai Student](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Student). [Seeker of the Way](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seeker+of+the+Way) and [Heir of the Wilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heir+of+the+Wilds) are better, but [Jeskai Student](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Student) is good too. I always see it wheeling in drafts and that just shouldn't happen.* 

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpstr14/qq_damodarosa.jpg)

***Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa:** [Rite of the Serpent](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rite+of+the+Serpent).* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpstr14/qq_shenhar.jpg)

***Shahar Shenhar:** [Valley Dasher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valley+Dasher).* 

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpstr14/qq_anteri.jpg)

***Fabrizio Anteri:** I really like [Leaping Master](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leaping+Master). Like, really.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpstr14/qq_juza.jpg)

***Martin Jůza:** [Rotting Mastodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rotting+Mastodon). Very stable.*
