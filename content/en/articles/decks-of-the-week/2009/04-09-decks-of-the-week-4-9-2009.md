One of the most fascinating stories to come from this season's Pro Tour-Honolulu Qualifier season has been the resurgence in old pros returning to the Pro Tour by way of winning a PTQ. Count Billy Moreno and Ben Stark amongst their numbers alongside the likes of Brian Kibler and Osyp Lebedowicz. Ben had to get there through none other than Antonino de Rosa! Check out their Top 8 lists below. Of course, there's still a little bit of time left to earn your plane ticket to Pro Tour–Honolulu, so check out [the official PTQ schedule](https://web.archive.org/web/20090409000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/protour/honolulu09-qualifiers).

The world of Premier Events on **Magic Online** continues to march forward as well! Classic, Standard, Extended, and *Shards of Alara* Block Constructed; we've got all the Top 8 lists for you right here! With *Alara Reborn* right around the corner, now is the time to scope out all the formats to get a feel for which decks are best. The brand new set promises to shake things up in exciting new ways, and you don't want to be left in the dust!
