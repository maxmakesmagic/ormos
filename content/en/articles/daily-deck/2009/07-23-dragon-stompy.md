Dragon Stompy is a tried and true strategy from the world of Legacy, but Suczi is taking it to the next level using this build to dominate a recent Classic Premier Event. The premise of the deck is simple: use accelerated mana in the form of Ancient Tomb, City of Traitors, and temporary mana effects like Seething Song to speed out a super-fatty or super-disruptive spell as early as the first turn. Many a mage's blood has turned cold at the sight of a first turn Magus of the Moon shutting down their deck's manabase.
