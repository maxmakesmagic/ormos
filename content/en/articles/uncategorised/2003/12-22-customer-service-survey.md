### Take A Few Minutes To Give Us Some Feedback

* * *

Have you had contact with the ***Magic** Online* Customer Service Team in the last six months? (The CS Team includes the online Adepts, message board moderators, and the folks who answer your email and phone calls.) If you have, please take a a few minutes to fill out [**this survey**](https://web.archive.org/web/20031222000000/http://www.wizards.com/survey/molcustserv1203.htm) and give us feedback on your experience. Please be sure to rate based on your interaction with these people, and not your overall experience with ***Magic** Online.*
