The floodgates have opened for new Standard decks. No fewer than three major national championships and 10 North American Pro Tour Qualifiers showcased the format last weekend with ***Magic** 2011* cards greatly impacting play. From the Titan-fueled Blue-white Control decks in France, [Fauna Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20100729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fauna+Shaman)s and [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20100729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan)s in Australia, and mana ramp decks in Canada to more than 1,800 players battling in PTQs, this week is bursting with deck ideas. And don't forget, [***Magic** 2011* Prerelease events begin Friday on **Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20100729000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/other/07142010e), with the set going on sale Monday, August 2. For those with a mind toward Pro Tour–Amsterdam and the new Extended format, you can delve into the daily Extended decks from **Magic Online**.
