We're nearing the next month, which means it's time for the latest update to the FNM promos list. Behold, the wonders that await you in the coming months at a Friday Night *Magic* near you…

# APRIL

April is a time of suspending disbelief, and this year it's also time to suspend some…fields? I don't know, just go with it. In April, we have this eerie rendition of [Suspension Field](https://web.archive.org/web/20150330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Suspension+Field) by Seb McKinnon. Here, a shadowy dragon is now the one doing the suspending; although, if I were the person trapped in the field, I might actually be thanking my lucky stars I'm not just being eaten by the dragon.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/card_EN_SuspensionField_FRF-FNM-Promo.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_SuspensionField_FRF-Promo.jpg)

Suspension Field Promo | Art by [Seb McKinnon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5B%22Seb%20McKinnon%22%5D)

# MAY

After April comes [Abzan Beastmaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20150330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Beastmaster), a peculiar Hound Shaman who wants to grant all your card-drawing wishes…so long as he can bring his bigger buddies along. If you like big creatures and you like drawing cards, [Abzan Beastmaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20150330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Beastmaster) will be your friend, too—especially if you do well in a May FNM.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/EN_card_EcGW7POkyr.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_promo_AbzanBeastmaster.jpg)

Abzan Beastmaster Promo | Art by [Scott Murphy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5B%22Scott%20Murphy%22%5D)

# June

It's warm outside, but it's getting frosty at Friday Night *Magic* as [Frost Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20150330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frost+Walker), a tragically terrifying 4/1 Elemental, comes bounding to a June FNM near you.
