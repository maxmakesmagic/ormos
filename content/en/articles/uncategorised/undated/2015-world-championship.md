## 2015 WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP

SUNDAY COVERAGE

For Seth Manfield, 2015 has been a year of firsts. In February, he earned his very first Sunday showing at Pro Tour *Fate Reforged*. This year marks the first time that Manfield achieved Platinum status in the Pro Tour Players Club, and also the first time that the young Maryland resident has competed in a *Magic: The Gathering* World Championship tournament. This is also the year where Seth Manfield became a father. His daughter was born two months ago.

Now, Manfield can add one more first to a momentous year. The 2015 *Magic: The Gathering World* Championship showcased 24 of the 2014-15 Premier Play season’s very best players. Twelve of those players were competing in the World Championship for the very first time, including Manfield. However, that did not stop the new competitor from making an incredible run through a field featuring the world’s very best. He was the first player to lock his seat in Sunday’s Top 4 playoffs with an unfathomable 13-1 record, and he didn’t stop there. He blasted past Samuel Black in the semifinals on his way to an edge-of-your-seat final match against one of the very best *Magic* players in the United States: Owen Turtenwald.

In a match that went to five games, Manfield was able to overcome Ugin, the Spirit Dragon, and a clash of Siege Rhinos gave Manfield the win over Turtenwald in the fifth and final game of the tournament.

The $50,000 first place prize is a big deal for the new dad, but this win is also the culmination of many years of success on the Grand Prix circuit, hard work, teamwork, and perseverance. Congratulations to Seth Manfield, the 2015 *Magic: The Gathering* World Champion. He played amongst the very best, conquered the best, and can now claim the title of being the world’s best.
