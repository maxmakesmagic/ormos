No, we're not talking about the film by James Cameron. Gunslinger Dave Guskin, who took this beauty to the Prerelease in Seattle, WA, was all about Titans. He played both Primeval Titan and Inferno Titan as well as the mana acceleration and stall to make sure he could cast them. Once he did? He had a dead opponent in no time, sometimes by combo'ing Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle with Primeval Titan!
