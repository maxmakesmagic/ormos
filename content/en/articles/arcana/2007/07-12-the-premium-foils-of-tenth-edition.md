Packs of *Tenth Edition* contain premium "foil" cards just like any other modern **Magic** set. Foils in *Tenth* have a new twist for the dedicated **Magic** player, however.

![No reminder text.](https://i.ormos.uk/a7/0b/a70bb153d6afb5eea906152b05915f5012c9978af49daf92e273342ab45fcf78.jpg)

Regular, nonfoil editions of *Tenth Edition* cards help new players learn to play by spelling out the meanings of abilities like flying, trample and shroud. But if you know these terms already, you can escape all that reminder text by collecting foil versions of those cards. Foil cards in *Tenth* have *no reminder text*.

In some cases, this allows more room for flavor text. Here are some samples of cards that gained significant real estate for some juicy new flavor text—flavor that wouldn't have fit on the nonfoil versions, what with all the reminding going on.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3c/fa/3cfa126f2fc4bc0e124645afa1c62a7139e19e8f449e48163bf5e7d1b50c978a.jpg)*Tenth Edition [Skyhunter Skirmisher](https://web.archive.org/web/20070712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skyhunter+Skirmisher), regular version*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/33/80/33809629e065cf6adefc6a50944a9ef246a1a39e29572d0852674dee6d24fcdc.jpg)*Tenth Edition [Skyhunter Skirmisher](https://web.archive.org/web/20070712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skyhunter+Skirmisher), foil version with no reminder text and all-new flavor text*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2a/a8/2aa808cc3ebfcb768577f1807fc7c0f505d804ce689576e1d255a4b9ce515430.jpg)*Tenth Edition [Uncontrollable Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20070712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Uncontrollable+Anger), regular version*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/71/bf/71bf2f1ea51db130bca3cf1978ca6b613328530a7b6c81445826c8700e3f46b6.jpg)*Tenth Edition [Uncontrollable Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20070712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Uncontrollable+Anger), foil version with no reminder text and all-new flavor text*

In some cases, the lack of reminder text just served to give more room for *awesome*.
