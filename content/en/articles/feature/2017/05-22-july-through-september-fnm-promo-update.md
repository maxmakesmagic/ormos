It's that time again: time to update you on what Friday Night *Magic* promos will be gracing (hopefully) your trade binders in just a few short months. We're smack in the middle of the *Kaladesh* run of FNM cards, so let's review those first before moving on to the *Aether Revolt* batch.

So, currently and in the near future, here are the cards you can pick up at Friday Night *Magic*—the format's best removal spell and its most-played card.

**May**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/images/daily/0nOLfWFk3w.png)

**June**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/images/daily/4jfEhASU2A.png)

So for what's left of May, you can grab an [Unlicensed Disintegration](https://web.archive.org/web/20170522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unlicensed+Disintegration), and for the entire month of June you'll have a chance at snagging that gorgeous [Aether Hub](https://web.archive.org/web/20170522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Hub). I know I want four.

It's hard to top those, but we're going to try. The next batch of cards are all from *Aether Revolt*, and they're all strong options.

**July**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/images/daily/Q1AGjBkcu2.png)

Improvise players will find this promo intriguing, as [Jason Kang](https://web.archive.org/web/20170522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Jason+Kang%22%5d) updates the card that fuels those fun [Paradox Engine](https://web.archive.org/web/20170522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Paradox+Engine) decks in Standard.

**August**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/images/daily/JCFfXa7K0P.png)

While it hasn't taken off in Standard, [Renegade Rallier](https://web.archive.org/web/20170522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Renegade+Rallier) actually has a strong spot in the Modern metagame as fetch lands enable the powerful ability basically at will. The updated art from [David Palumbo](https://web.archive.org/web/20170522000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22David+Palumbo%22%5d) isn't too shabby either.

*(Editor's Note: The collector numbers on the cards for August and September do not match the months due to a late scheduling swap. The months listed in this article are correct.)*

**September**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/images/daily/rxUBnnOsOK.png)

This is the one everyone will be pushing their way to FNM to snag. Alex Konstad shows us just what happens when a Kaladesh resident meets the wrong end of a hard shove.
