In the penultimate round of the day, two national team captains clashed: Nico Bohny and Bing Han, fighting for Switzerland and China, respectively. Neither was a stranger to the feature match area: Bing had been a semifinalist at Grand Prix Shanghai last month, Bohny, among other things, had been a finalist in Milan earlier this year.

Both players had just one loss in the tournament so far, so neither was in danger of being eliminated from Day 2 contention just yet. However, both would need at least one more match win to secure a spot in the Booster Draft portion of the event.

Bohny had built an Abzan deck with a slight Warrior theme, Bing was simply playing the best of all five colors.

# Game 1

Bohny led with [Seeker of the Way](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seeker+of+the+Way) and [Ainok Bond-Kin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ainok+Bond-Kin) but didn't get very far before Bing caught up to him with [Sultai Flayer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sultai+Flayer) and [Highspire Mantis](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Highspire+Mantis). Bohny's [Anafenza, the Foremost](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anafenza%2C+the+Foremost) was matched perfectly by Bing [Suspension Field](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Suspension+Field).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpstr14/fm8_bohny.jpg)

*Nico Bohny*

Bing further cemented his stranglehold on the game with Archer's [Parapet](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Parapet) and [Abzan Falconer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Falconer). In fact, Bohny was only able to mount another offense when he cast [Raiders' Spoils](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raiders%27+Spoils), and even then he only managed to get in another 4 damage. And since Bing traded away [Sultai Flayer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sultai+Flayer) in the process, even that was for naught.

[Highspire Mantis](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Highspire+Mantis) and [Abzan Falconer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Falconer) flew to victory with Archer's [Parapet](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Parapet) taking the final life.

**Bing Han 1-0 Nico Bohny**

# Game 2

Again, Bohny deployed the first creatures, with [Ainok Bond-Kin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ainok+Bond-Kin) and a morph, but lost both to [Arc Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Lightning).

Both players had further morphs. However, while Bing encountered some difficulties and basically never found his fourth land, Bohny's deck was operating on full power and gave him [Mardu Skullhunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Skullhunter), [Seeker of the Way](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seeker+of+the+Way), [Herald of Anafenza](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Herald+of+Anafenza), and [Armament Corps](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Armament+Corps).

Bing fought valiantly with [Kill Shot](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kill+Shot), but when he finally drew a fourth land to cast [Highspire Mantis](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Highspire+Mantis), he promptly lost it to [Throttle](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Throttle), and subsequently lost the game as well.

**Bing Han 1-1 Nico Bohny**

# Game 3

This was a quick and easy affair and easy to recount too. After a mulligan, Bohny only cast one [Mardu Skullhunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Skullhunter) during the first five turns of the game, followed by an Unyielding Kumar which immediately yielded to [Arc Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Lightning) and one [Woolly Loxodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Woolly+Loxodon) which immediately met its [Crackling Doom](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crackling+Doom).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpstr14/fm8_han.jpg)

*Bing Han*

Meanwhile, Bing had summoned [Abzan Falconer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Falconer) and one, two, three morphs, taught the Falconer to fly, and well ... Bohny soon conceded and revealed the [Rush of Battle](https://web.archive.org/web/20141129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rush+of+Battle) that had been one of two spells in his opening hand and not one of the three spells he had cast that game ...

**Nico Bohny 1-2 Bing Han**
