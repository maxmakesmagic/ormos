Nam began with a [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), taking Watanabe's [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections). He followed up with a [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s which was promptly killed with a [Devour Flesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devour+Flesh). Watanabe tried a [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress), nabbing himself a [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall), then followed up with a [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s. Nam tried a Nightveil Spectre, but Watanabe was having none of that nonsense and offed it in short order. 

Nam pushed on with the disruption with a [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), then both players summoned [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon)s, who dutifully glared at each other through the skies. Watanabe started using Rat Pack tokens to tap down Nam's [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon), knocking the GP:Melbourne champion down to dangerously low life. Nam pondered his options. With a [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) and 3 other lands on the board, he could stave off the Demon for a turn if necessary. He attacked, knocking Watanabe to 13, then summoned a second Demon and passed. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/qf_nam.jpg)

*Nam considers some triggers* 

  

Watanabe drew his card for the turn and settled in for a good consideration. He only needed to punch through for 6 damage, but Nam had a lethal amount of damage on the board and had mana open to activate a [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) if necessary. 

Watanabe moved to combat and set the Demon triggers on the stack. Nam sacrificed his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) to tap down Watanabe's Demon, and before attackers, Watanabe cast a [Devour Flesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devour+Flesh) to take out one of Nam's Demons, leaving them with one apiece, Watanabe still up a [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat). 

Nam attacked through the air for 7, then summoned a [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel), putting the life totals at 15-2, his favour. Watanabe went to combat on his turn and Nam declined to do anything with Watanabe's Demon trigger, letting him crash through the air for 7 (8-2). Nam knocked the top of his library and drew an [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections), which he cast before moving to combat. Watanabe created a [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) token, but fed his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) to the Demon trigger, leaving him with just enough cards and mana to spawn more [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s. Nam looked to the top of his deck but couldn't draw anything in time, as both players headed to their sideboards after a tense first game. 

**Yuuya Watanabe 1 - Sung-Wook Nam 0** 

**Game 2** 

A [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) from Watanabe stripped Nam of an underworld connections, and a follow-up [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) on the second turn took another one, but Watanabe failed to play a second land. Intrigued, Nam tried a [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) of his own and Watanabe spread a hand full of [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections), a [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight), and another pair of [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize)s. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/qf_watanabe.jpg)

*Packing Rats like there's no tomorrow.* 

  

Watanabe hissed out some long-held tension as he managed to draw his second and third consecutive lands, having fairly completely stripped Nam of plays in the interim. Nam threw a Nightveil Spectre onto the battlefield before Watanabe killed it with a [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight). Nam had a second Spectre, which Watanabe offed with his [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall). Nam, bemused, shook his head as he started to fall behind on lands, unusual against an opponent who had kept a 1-lander. 

The players went back and forth with threats and answers for a few cycles, Watanabe relentlessly drawing ahead as he loaded up his second [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) and cast an Erebos, putting Nam on a fiendish clock. Nam couldn't keep pace, and extended his hand a turn later. 

After the game, Watanabe reconstructed his opener - triple [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight), Swamp, double [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections). The players back-and-forthed over whether the hand was keepable, but after confirming Watanabe was running 25 land, Nam agreed the keep was reasonable. 

**Yuuya Watanabe 2 - Sung-Wook Nam 0**
