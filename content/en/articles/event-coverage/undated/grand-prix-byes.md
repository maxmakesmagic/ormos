Byes will be given to players in the Grand Prix based on the following criteria:

 ***Third-Round Byes*** 

*   Players with a Players Club level of 3 or higher
*   DCI players with a Limited rating of 2000 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI Limited ratings published on April 19, 2006.

 ***Second-Round Byes*** 

*   Players with a Players Club level of 2
*   DCI players with a Limited rating of 1900 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI Limited ratings published on April 19, 2006.

 ***First-Round Byes*** 

*   Players with a Players Club level of 1
*   DCI players with a Limited rating of 1800 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI Limited ratings published on April 19, 2006.
