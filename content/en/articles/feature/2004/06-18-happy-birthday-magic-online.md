Come join us on June 24th for Magic Online’s 2nd birthday celebration. The party will start at 12:01 am PST and run til midnight. Everyone that logs into their account on the 24th will receive a commemorative birthday avatar and we’ll also be releasing a new background for everyone. As an added bonus, all limited events will require ZERO tix to enter. 

We do need your help. We couldn’t decide which avatar to award so we are going to leave that you.

Vote for your favorite avatar below. Your vote will only be counted once so select carefully. At 9:00 am PDT on the 25th we will close voting and I will announce the winner later that day. Avatars will be distributed within 2 weeks.
