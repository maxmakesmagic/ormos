A few cards in Alpha were printed with what turned out to be incorrect casting costs (see this old [Magic Arcana](/en/articles/arcana/2002/02-25-alpha-oops-ii-2)), but one card was printed without a casting cost at all!

Check out the Alpha Cyclopean Tomb below. Nothing in the corner, not even a ![0 Mana](http://www.wizards.com/images/Symbols/Symbol_0_mana.gif) like Black Lotus had.

The cost -- ![4 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/36/29/3629288966daa042decf01d3752789ab6676fddd67c3679fa580cd3baecae158.gif) -- was added in the Beta printing.
