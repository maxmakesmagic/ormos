After the release of *New Phyrexia*, sharp-eyed players spotted the combo between Deceiver Exarch and Splinter Twin almost immediately: put Splinter Twin on Deceiver Exarch, and you can tap it to create a new one, untap the original, and make as many hasty copies as you want. Part of the reason they found it so quickly was its close resemblance to the Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker / Pestermite combo that met success in Extended a few years previous. Well, in the new Modern format, you can play all four of those cards! **Magic Online** user Numdiar did exactly that, piloting a "Pester Twin" combo deck to a 4-0 finish in a recent daily event on **Magic Online**. You can find more Modern deck lists on the [**Magic Online** page](https://web.archive.org/web/20110818023522/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Digital/magiconline.aspx?x=mtg/digital/magiconline/whatshappening).
