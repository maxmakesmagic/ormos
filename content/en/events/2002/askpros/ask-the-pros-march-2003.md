Ever wonder what it's like playing on camera in Top 8 of a Pro Tour? Curious about the origins of a word like "mise"? Interested in learning more about how a major team prepares for a tournament, or what the pro players think about the metagame? [Just ask them!](/en/articles/event-coverage/2002/11-14-ask-the-pros)

<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;                 <b>March 31, 2003</b></td>&#10;<td> </td>&#10;<td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/24/ed/24ed5be5578a04d4bd33e38ab1834ea7e497fbcee8f490f096ac18a1ccfd9f54.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr>&#10;<td colspan="3">&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<p><b>Question</b>: If you could have your own theme music, what would it be?</p>&#10;<p><b>Answers:</b></p>&#10;<p><b>Antonino de Rosa</b>: &quot;I’m Too Sexy...&quot;. That’s one of my favorites for sure.<br><b>Mike Pustilnik</b>: &quot;Pressure&quot; by Billy Joel.<br><b>Alex Shvartsman</b>: &quot;The Final Countdown&quot; by Europe.<br><b>Dirk Baberowski</b>: Something from Rocky - not Eye of the Tiger though. Too common. Probably Burning Heart, also by Survivor. Or the Miami Vice Theme.<br><b>Kai Budde</b>: The “Fall Guy” Soundtrack by Lee Majors.<br><b>Eric Taylor</b>: &quot;Dancing Queen&quot; by ABBA</p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

  

<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;                 <b>March 28, 2003</b></td>&#10;<td> </td>&#10;<td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/d5/77/d577174465a6216c4498875eafb3da2b3211ea99f3f114f05b0965ac4275f693.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr>&#10;<td colspan="3">&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<p><b>Question</b>: What is your least favorite color in <b>Magic</b>, and why?</p>&#10;<p><b>Answers:</b></p>&#10;<p><b>Dirk Baberowski</b>: White, clearly. White hasn't had anything going for it since Savannah Lions and Swords to Plowshares rotated out. Whenever you put together a white deck, it's just a bunch of guys. No Giant Growths, no Bolts, no Counterspells, just guys. Not even particularly good ones. Although it's a degenerate card, white really needs Plow back.<br><b>Diego Ostrovich</b>: Green. I like to play control decks most of the time and green doesn't really contribute very much to them.<br><b>Kai Budde</b>: Green. It implies that you have to cast creatures and turn them sideways. Boring.<br><b>Mike Pustilnik</b>: White, because of its holier-than-thou attitude.<br><b>Zvi Mowshowitz</b>: My least favorite color in <b>Magic</b> is purple. They don't print enough good cards.</p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

  

<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;                 <b>March 27, 2003</b></td>&#10;<td> </td>&#10;<td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/50/6a/506a81655d6f8e73a751a1459805e125bbc0f9a665920c3a74b8f7a7a16a7928.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr>&#10;<td colspan="3">&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<p><b>Question</b>: What rares and uncommons would you pick over Sparksmith? Does this change in packs two or three?</p>&#10;<p><b>Answers:</b></p>&#10;<p><b>Dirk Baberowski</b>: Visara, Rorix, Silvos, Starstorm, Exalted Angel, Menacing Ogre, Quicksilver Dragon, Slice and Dice, Ravenous Baloth, Silent Specter, Infest, Goblin Sharpshooter, Butcher Orgg and Lightning Rift. In u-r, I take Skill over Sparksmith and in g-r, I have taken Shock over it. I guess I really don't like ‘Smith as much as some do.<br><b>Gary Wise</b>: Angel, Jareth, Quicksilver Dragon, Arcanis, Visara, Specter, Silvos, Slice and Dice, Rorix, Sharpshooter and Starstorm.<br><b>Mike Pustilnik</b>: Visara, Slivos, Rorix, Exalted Angel, Slice and Dice, Infest and Quicksilver Dragon. In packs 2 and 3, I devalue Sparksmith if I don't have many goblins.<br><b>Eric Taylor</b>: I leave that sort of deep thinking to Gary Wise.</p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

  

<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;                 <b>March 26, 2003</b></td>&#10;<td> </td>&#10;<td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/c7/c1/c7c1fe5cf49f58117fbd931561f1b60bd3183ad6276cf758a8fd3c7d6702fe27.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr>&#10;<td colspan="3">&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<p>Question: In an <i>Onslaught-Onslaught-Legions</i> draft game, your opening hand includes one swamp, two forests, Festering Goblin and Elvish Warrior. Which land do you play first?</p>&#10;<p><b>Answers:</b></p>&#10;<p><b>Kai Budde</b>: When going first, definitely forest. When going second, it depends a bit on my opponents deck, but in 95% of the games, it's forest.<br><b>Gary Wise</b>: How is that even close? Forest lets you play the goblin turn 4 along with a morph. You can keep your 1-2 points of damage.<br><b>Diego Ostrovich</b>: Swamp.<br><b>Antonino de Rosa</b>: Forest. I think a turn 2 2/3 will impact the game more than turn 1 1/1. When you attack on turn 3 with the elf, it’s very unlikely that your opponent will be able to block, so the damage you would have done its still the same. And it is easier to cast the Festering Goblin later on the game like turn 4 with a morph and so on.<br><b>Mike Pustilnik</b>: Forest</p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

  

<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;                 <b>March 19, 2003</b></td>&#10;<td> </td>&#10;<td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/f0/9f/f09f4d2a172231ed02bcfadb60e0264389ebd0a5d2bbaaf40ad6a927154a8f0e.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr>&#10;<td colspan="3">&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<p><b>Question</b>: What is the most skill-intensive Constructed deck in the history of the game?</p>&#10;<p><b>Answers:</b></p>&#10;<p><b>Diego Ostrovich</b>: Probably the Bargain combo deck.<br><b>Antonino de Rosa</b>: Oath of Druids. The deck is so good and it has so many decisions every turn. I remember playing the deck and as early as turns 1 and 2, I started to mess up.<br><b>Ken Krouner</b>: My perspective is a little off, but I would have to say Prosbloom. Back when it was a deck, I would lose many games goldfishing because it was so hard to go off. To be fair, I was terrible back then.<br><b>Adrian Sullivan</b>: Almost certainly Zvi-Bargain. You could very much puzzle out wins from the deck, but you had to be very prepared to understand exactly what possibilities you might be cutting off with almost every decision.<br><b>Mike Pustilnik</b>: Turboland.</p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

Ramosian Sergeant isn't even close.<br><b>Mike Pustilnik</b>: Ramosian Sergeant.<br><b>Dirk Baberowski</b>: Savannah Lions.<br><b>Brian Kibler</b>: Ramosian Sergeant. <table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;                 <b>March 17, 2003</b></td>&#10;<td> </td>&#10;<td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/d5/77/d577174465a6216c4498875eafb3da2b3211ea99f3f114f05b0965ac4275f693.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr>&#10;<td colspan="3">&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<p><b>Question</b>: The Masters Series has been cancelled and the prize money will be distributed through the Player of the Year race. What is your reaction? </p>&#10;<p><b>Answers:</b></p>&#10;<p><b>Kai Budde</b>: Very good. That means that we don't have to practice two formats. We can actually leave the tournament site at 7-9 PM, instead of playing until very late. Plus, we can get to the site one day later. I suggested something like this and I'm very glad that they dropped the old system.<br><b>Brian Kibler</b>: I pumped the fist. The Masters is far too luck-oriented. Single elimination tournaments for tens of thousands of dollars are a bad idea. The new payout structure rewards consistency and makes professional <b>Magic</b> much more legitimate. That's good for me, because once I graduate, I'm a gamer for life...<br><b>Ken Krouner</b>: I think that it is great for the pro players, and great for Wizards, however the fans miss out on following these tournaments and getting new tech.<br><b>Gary Wise</b>: Yearly paychecks? Who complains?<br><b>Mike Pustilnik</b>: The change might mean that I get more money (since I was often just a few points short of Masters qualification), but I think that a lot of players will miss the Gateway.<br><b>Olivier Ruel</b>: It's cool - it gives me one extra day of sightseeing before the Pro Tour.</p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

He has the most interesting stories and he tells them very, very well.<br><b>Raphael Levy</b>: Mike Sochon will be angry at me if I don’t mention him. <br><b>Mike Pustilnik</b>: Scott Johns.<br><b>Dirk Baberowski</b>: Stephan Valkyser and Christian Luehrs (obviously I would be naming Germans), because I always appreciate the company of “adults” at Magic events. Besides those two, there are many personalities that made events more interesting: Donnie Gallitz, Mike Long, Erik Lauer, etc.<br><b>Gary Wise</b>: Chris Pikula. Best story teller I've ever seen.<br><b>Zvi Mowshowitz</b>: We need to bring back Scott Johns</p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

  

<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;                 <b>March 4, 2003</b></td>&#10;<td> </td>&#10;<td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/c7/c1/c7c1fe5cf49f58117fbd931561f1b60bd3183ad6276cf758a8fd3c7d6702fe27.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr>&#10;<td colspan="3">&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<p><b>Question</b>: Who is the best <i>Dance Dance Revolution</i> player on the Pro Tour?</p>&#10;<p><b>Answers:</b></p>&#10;<p><b>John Larkin</b>: Obviously Osyp Lebedowicz has to be the favorite, with his “dance skills”. I certainly would not be caught dead on one of those machines or, come to think of it, on an actual dance floor. You are more likely to find me propping up the bar.<br><b>Raphael Levy</b>: I know Marc Hernandez planned to train a lot to become the ultimate Dance Dance Revolution champion. When I last saw him dancing in Sydney, he was eager to learn more! He's probably very close to being the best dancer in the world right at the moment. <br><b>Zvi Mowshowitz</b>: He’ll probably never admit it, but it’s Carlos Romao. <br><b>Ken Krouner</b>: Brian Hegstad is the best that I have witnessed. Bryan Hubble and Brian Kibler can cut a mean pad too.<br><b>Ben Bleiweiss</b>: Either Josh Bennett or Colin Jackson.</p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

  

<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;                 <b>March 3, 2003</b></td>&#10;<td> </td>&#10;<td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/f0/9f/f09f4d2a172231ed02bcfadb60e0264389ebd0a5d2bbaaf40ad6a927154a8f0e.jpg"></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr>&#10;<td colspan="3">&#10;<table>&#10;<tbody><tr>&#10;<td>&#10;<p><b>Question</b>: Who is better, Jon Finkel or Kai Budde? </p>&#10;<p><b>Answers:</b></p>&#10;<p><b>Dirk Baberowski</b>: Finkel may be more talented and I think Finkel has an edge on Kai in certain aspects of his game, but overall, it is not even close. Kai is better.<br><b>Antonino de Rosa</b>: Budde has won three times the Pro Tours that Finkel has. Finkel keeps doing well on raw talent alone so, for now, Budde is superior, but I believe that if Finkel cared more, he would be just as good as Kai. Budde is the perfect example of the fact that the most talented individual who puts in the most work will win the most.<br><b>Matt Vienneau</b>: Budde - for now! Finkel is like the unfit Rocky from the first half of all the Rocky movies facing the young up-and-comer. If Rocky bears down, does a whole bunch of training and some inspiring music is played, he'll come out punching and manage to take it in the end.<br><b>Ken Krouner</b>: Kai, without a doubt. Even when Finkel was at he best, he didn't dominate like Kai.<br><b>Diego Ostrovich</b>: I guess not even Finkel can compete with Kai nowadays, can he?.</p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>
