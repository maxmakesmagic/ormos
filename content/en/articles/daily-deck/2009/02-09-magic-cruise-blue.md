In honor of the Magic Cruise, which set sail Saturday from Texas for southern Mexico, Daily Decks presents a Magic Cruise themed deck! Designed for the 60 card Highlander/Singleton format, every card in the deck is centered around an oceanic voyage. Be it the boats (Ghost Ship, Pirate Ship, Skeleton Ship...), the scenery (Underground Sea, Volcanic Island, Coral Atoll...), or the supporting cast (Spiny Starfish, Segovian Leviathan, Electric Eel), every card contributes to some element of the theme. That's one of the great things about **Magic**! Not only can you have a great time building the most broken decks for the upcoming tournament, but you can relax with a more light hearted, less competitive exercise like building a theme deck to commemorate a cool **Magic** experience like the Game in the Gulf cruise!
