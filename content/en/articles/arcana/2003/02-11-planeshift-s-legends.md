The three Legendary rares in *Planeshift* were unique in that the premium (foil) versions have different art than the regular versions. Here are both versions of each card, side-by-side.

 ![Ertai the Corrupted](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Ertai%20the%20Corrupted&options=) 

 ![ertai the corrupted alt](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=ertai%20the%20corrupted%20alt&options=) 

  

Regular version by Mark Tedin, premium version by Kev Walker.

 ![Tahngarth, Talruum Hero](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Tahngarth,%20Talruum%20Hero&options=)

Regular version by Dave Dorman, premium version by Kev Walker.
