What are the best cards in both your Booster Drafts today? 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Gao-Tan.jpg)

 ***Gao Tan:** Pod 1: [Sagu Mauler](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sagu+Mauler)  
 Pod 2: [Savage Knuckleblade](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Knuckleblade)* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Han-Bing2.jpg)

 ***Han Bing:** Pod 1: [Crater's Claws](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crater%27s+Claws)  
 Pod 2: [Utter End](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Utter+End)* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Charles-Lai.jpg)

 ***Charles Lai:** Pod 1: [Winterflame](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Winterflame). It won me a crucial game.  
 Pod 2: Either [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) or [High Sentinels of Arashin](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=High+Sentinels+of+Arashin).* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Yuuki-Ichikawa.jpg)

 ***Yuuki Ichikawa:** Pod 1: [Empty the Pits](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Empty+the+Pits). That card is crazy.  
 Pod 2: Double [See the Unwritten](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=See+the+Unwritten)!* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Zhang-Mengqiu.jpg)

 ***Zhang Mengqiu:** Pod 1: [Butcher of the Horde](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Butcher+of+the+Horde)  
 Pod 2: My deck is terrible, I think the best card is [Warden of the Eye](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warden+of+the+Eye).* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Albertus-Law.jpg)

 ***Albertus Law:** Pod 1: [Ashcloud Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashcloud+Phoenix)  
 Pod 2: [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider)* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Yuuya-Watanabe.jpg)

 ***Yuuya Watanabe:** Pod 1: [Incremental Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Incremental+Growth). I had two copies!  
 Pod 2: [Necropolis Fiend](https://web.archive.org/web/20141005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Necropolis+Fiend)*
