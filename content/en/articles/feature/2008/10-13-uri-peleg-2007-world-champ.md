Uri Peleg was a well respected Israeli pro who had worked with some of the game's biggest and brightest. Prior to the [2007 World Championships](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/historic-wins-for-peleg-swiss-at-worlds-2), however, he hadn't managed a breakthrough performance. That all changed as he bested three separate formats on the weekend before hoisting the title of 2007 **Magic** World Champion. The deck that took him to the title through the Top 8? The above Doran list!

You can live the dream too, by qualifying for the [2008 World Championships](https://web.archive.org/web/20081013000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=events/magic/worlds), to be held in Memphis, Tennessee December 11-14. How? By participating in the Last Chance Qualifier tournaments being held on [**Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20081013000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/digital/magiconline.aspx)! The events feature Standard and Limited formats and run from Wednesday, October 15 through Thursday, October 16.
