Owl? Crow? Seagull? Not funny.

Chicken? Instant funny.

One of the running themes in *Unglued* is chickens. There are more of the traditionally funny fowl in the set than you might think, however: they appear not just in the type line, but also in *Unglued* art (especially that of Kaja Foglio), flavor text, and rules text. Here's a list of *Unglued* cards that support the chicken theme. Also, click the pictures at right for high-resolution images of some finger-lickin' chicken art.

*   **[Charm School](https://web.archive.org/web/20040817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Charm+School)** - Features a chicken pattern on the borders of the robe.

*   **[Chicken a la King](https://web.archive.org/web/20040817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chicken+a+la+King)** - Is a chicken.

*   **[Chicken Egg](https://web.archive.org/web/20040817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chicken+Egg)** - Makes a chicken creature token.

*   **[Double Play](https://web.archive.org/web/20040817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Double+Play)** - Mentions "chicken" in its flavor text.

*   **[Fowl Play](https://web.archive.org/web/20040817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fowl+Play)** - Makes the enchanted creature into a chicken, and mentions "chicken" in its flavor text.

*   **[Free-Range Chicken](https://web.archive.org/web/20040817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Free-Range+Chicken)** - Is a chicken.

*   **[Hurloon Wrangler](https://web.archive.org/web/20040817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hurloon+Wrangler)** - Features a chicken pattern on the back pockets of the minotaur's jeans.

*   **[Lexivore](https://web.archive.org/web/20040817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lexivore)** - Mentions "chicken" in its flavor text.

*   **[Mesa Chicken](https://web.archive.org/web/20040817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mesa+Chicken)** - Is a chicken.

*   **[Mirror Mirror](https://web.archive.org/web/20040817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirror+Mirror)** - Features a chicken on the back of the mirror.

*   **[Poultrygeist](https://web.archive.org/web/20040817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Poultrygeist)** - Is a chicken.

*   **[Sorry](https://web.archive.org/web/20040817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorry)** - Features chickens on the wizards' robe clasps.

*   **[Spatula of the Ages](https://web.archive.org/web/20040817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spatula+of+the+Ages)** - Features (chicken) eggs in the hieroglyphics to the left of the spatula.

*   **[Strategy, Schmategy](https://web.archive.org/web/20040817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strategy%2C+Schmategy)** - Features chicken icons on the wheel.

*   **[Team Spirit](https://web.archive.org/web/20040817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Team+Spirit)** - Mentions "chicken" in its flavor text.
