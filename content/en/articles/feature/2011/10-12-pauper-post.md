Big-mana Post decks have posted fine finishes in a variety of formats. The deck's key cards—[Cloudpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20111012000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudpost) and [Glimmerpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20111012000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glimmerpost)—are both commons, which let the.zzz pilot this EightPost deck to a 3-1 finish in a Pauper Daily Event on **Magic Online**.
