### Collector's Highlights

*Urza's Saga* contains 350 new cards for **Magic: The Gathering** and is the beginning of the *Urza's Block* of Expansions.

### New Features

Learn the answers to your new rules questions in the [Urza's Saga FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20080811000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=dci/downloads/EN_MTGUZFAQ_20100324.rtf). (**Updated March 2010**)

Explore the [new rules and card abilities](https://web.archive.org/web/20080811000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/expert/Urzas_Saga/UZ_Rules.asp) appearing in *Urza's Saga*.

### Story Line
