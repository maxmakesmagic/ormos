## Grand Prix Santiago 2017

Day 2 Coverage

Mauro Sasso, piloting the Temur Aetherworks Marvel deck that dominated the tournament, took the trophy at Grand Prix Santiago 2017!

Earlier on Saturday morning, 718 players arrived at the Gran Palace in Santiago to play in the main event, and we've had a great weekend all around. After 15 rounds of Swiss competition, the Top 8 contained a variety of decks (3 Temur Aetherworks Marvel, 2 Mardu Vehicles, R/G Energy, Mono-Black Zombies, and U/R Control) and a mix between newcomers to the Top 8 and veteran pros.

In the finals, Mauro Sasso may not have had as much premier event experience as his opponent, but he decisively beat European Gold level pro Grzegorz Kowalski nevertheless. In the Marvel mirror, he cast an early Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger to win the first game and attacked for lethal with a swarm of seven Thopter tokens to get the trophy.

Congratulations to Mauro Sasso, champion of Grand Prix Santiago 2017!
