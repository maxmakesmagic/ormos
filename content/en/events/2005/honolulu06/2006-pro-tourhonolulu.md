Situated on the island of Oahu, Honolulu is the capital of Hawaii. The most populated of Hawaii's six major islands, Oahu offers amazing beaches for surfing and sunbathing. The volcanic Diamond Head provides a majestic backdrop to Waikiki a few miles outside of downtown Honolulu. The Pro Tour visits Honolulu on March 3-5, when the average temperature is 74° F (23° C).

Pro Tour-Honolulu is a Standard format event with a last-chance qualifier run the day before the event starts. But you don't have to be a Pro to enjoy the Pro Tour -- there will be wall-to-wall Magic to play in Side Events all weekend, with prizes including an iPod, a helicopter tour of Oahu, and qualification plus an airplane ticket to Pro Tour-Prague (prizes may vary).

### Hall Attractions

**SIDE EVENTS:**  
 Play **Magic** all weekend long, even if you're not qualified for the Pro Tour! Prizes include an iPod, a helicopter tour of Honolulu for two, and qualification for Pro Tour-Prague. Click [here](/en/events/2005/honolulu06/2006-pro-tourhonolulu-side-events) to view the Pro Tour-Honolulu Side Events Schedule.

**ARTISTS:**  
 Meet your favorite artists and have them sign your cards. Click [here](/en/events/2005/honolulu06/2006-pro-tourhonolulu-artists) to find out which **Magic: The Gathering** artists will be featured at Pro Tour-Honolulu.

**WATCH THE TOP 8:**  
 All of Sunday's Top 8 action can be viewed on a projection screen in the hall, with Randy Buehler and Mike Flores providing the play-by-play and color commentary. Sunday's action begins at 10:45 a.m. local time, which is 3:45 p.m. ET, and is also available as a [webcast](/en/events/2004/webcast/magic-the-gathering-webcast-schedule) from the comfort of your own home.
