# Synergy of the Day

1.  Discarding for Fun and Profit

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Bomat+Courier)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bomat+Courier)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Bedlam+Reveler)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bedlam+Reveler)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_D2PjMt6hhU.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_6Oog5GhkuJ.png)

[Bomat Courier](https://web.archive.org/web/20170410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bomat+Courier) and [Bedlam Reveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20170410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bedlam+Reveler) are already strong cards that see some amount of Standard play (and, in Reveler's case, some amount of Modern play, even). But the benefit has always been on the "draw" side of the ability, with the discard part mostly as a cost. But what if you could make both sides work for you? What if discarding your hand meant pumping your Flameblade Adept sky-high? Or, possibly more frightening, what if it meant giving -1/-1 to each of your opponent's creatures for each card you formerly had in hand?

The three red cards could definitely form the backbone of an aggressive red deck, possibly paired up with madness cards like [Fiery Temper](https://web.archive.org/web/20170410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Temper). The Archfiend looks like it could go particularly well with [Bedlam Reveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20170410000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bedlam+Reveler) in a more midrange or controlling black-red deck that utilizes a ton of spells to keep opponents in check.

Whichever direction you choose, the synergy is very real.
