Commander is a fun format for friends, family, and your average gaming group. Part of the reason it is so fun? All the wacky situations that can arise! Take, for example, this deck list created by Pro Tour scorekeeper Nick Fang. What's so interesting about Nick's deck? His combo of Grip of Chaos, Mycosynth Lattice, and Confusion in the Ranks. When he sticks that triumvirate, every targeted decision in the game is determined at random, and every permanent players put onto the battlefield winds up swapping places with another randomly determined permanent controlled by another player!
