As you know, Zendikar is a dangerous place, what with all the deadly perils and traps and whatnot. There are a few cards which demonstrate how the land itself provides dangers to the unwary adventurer.

Consider [Rumbling Aftershocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20100215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rumbling+Aftershocks). Apparently it's set in a part of Zendikar where the slightest tremor can cause giant boulders to plummet down onto helpless passersby.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/ad/d9ad8b257f7cf5b0338acc956a548219358051d2daa96de65792a99cc01970f0.jpg)

[Rumbling Aftershocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20100215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rumbling+Aftershocks) | Art by [Tomasz Jedruszek](https://web.archive.org/web/20100215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Tomasz+Jedruszek%22%5D)

Can you identify the place these two goblins are standing?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/86/b3/86b38b345c821b60c1307adf1c653a6f82fa0705b537bb73178d3971d883e0c9.jpg)

[Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20100215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks) | Art by [Fred Fields](https://web.archive.org/web/20100215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Fred+Fields%22%5D)

They're journeying through the teetering peaks! Too much magic in this area could easily dislodge one of those boulders. Better step carefully!
