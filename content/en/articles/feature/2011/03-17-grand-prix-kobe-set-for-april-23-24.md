Grand Prix Kobe has been rescheduled for April 23-24, 2011. The event was postponed from its original date following the damage caused by the magnitude 9.0 earthquake and resulting tsunami on March 11.

The format for the Grand Prix is Extended and the Top 16 finishers will earn invitations to Pro Tour Nagoya, as previously announced. All byes earned at Grand Prix Kobe Trial tournaments will apply. For more information about Grand Prix Kobe, visit the [event information page](/en/events/2011/kobe11/grand-prix-kobe-event-information).

**Magic** Weekend Nagoya remains scheduled for June 11-13, 2011 in Nagoya, Japan. At this time Wizards of the Coast has no plans to postpone or cancel **Magic** Weekend Nagoya. If this changes, we will communicate this through our normal announcement channels.

For information about how you can help those affected by the disaster, visit [www.redcross.org](http://www.redcross.org/en/) and donate to Japan Earthquake and Pacific Tsunami.
