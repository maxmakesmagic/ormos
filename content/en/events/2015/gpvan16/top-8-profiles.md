### Gabe Carleton-Barnes

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpvan16/t8_barnes.jpg)

**Hometown:** Portland, Oregon  
**Age:** 36  
**Occupation:** Software Developer  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 12 PT Day 2s. Many PTQ wins. Once beat a [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20160201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) in Limited. 

**What color combination was your Sealed Deck, and what was your record?**  
 W/R/b/g- 6-1 

**What was your best card in Sealed and why?**  
 Goblin Dark Dwellers because I had two [Devour in Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20160201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devour+in+Flames). 

**What color combinations were your draft decks, and what was your record?**  
 B/<>/r: 3-0 W/B/r: 2-1 

**What was your best card in each draft and why?**  
[Transgress the Mind](https://web.archive.org/web/20160201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transgress+the+Mind) because it hit bombs and triggered the three [Sky Scourer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sky+Scourer)s, and [Resolute Blademaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20160201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Resolute+Blademaster) with fifteen allies. 

**What card surprised you the most this weekend?**  
[Visions of Brutality](https://web.archive.org/web/20160201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Visions+of+Brutality). It was surprisingly effective.

### Allen Sun

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpvan16/t8_sun.jpg)

**Hometown:** Alexandria, Virginia  
**Age:** 37  
**Occupation:** Software Engineer  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Attended Pro Tour Vancouver and was one match win away from Top 8 at GP Salt Lake City. 

**What color combination was your Sealed Deck, and what was your record?**  
 U/B splash W- 9-0 

**What was your best card in Sealed and why?**  
 Seer’s Lantern. It won me many games when the game went long. 

**What color combinations were your draft decks, and what was your record?**  
 W/R 2-1, U/G 2-0-1 

**What was your best card in each draft and why?**  
 In first draft it was [Fall of the Titans](https://web.archive.org/web/20160201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fall+of+the+Titans) because it just kills your opponent.  
 In second draft it was Seer’s Lantern because with it I was always drawing gas. 

**What card surprised you the most this weekend?**  
 Seer’s Lantern for the better.
