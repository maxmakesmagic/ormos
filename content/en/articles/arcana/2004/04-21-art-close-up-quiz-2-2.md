We hope you enjoyed the [first](/en/articles/arcana/2004/04-12-art-close-up-quiz-1) Close-Up Art Quiz. In fact, we hope you enjoyed how easy it was, because the vacation is over: today's quiz is a good bit harder. 

Take a look at these snippets of zoomed-in card art from the *Mirrodin* block. Can you identify the card to which each belongs? Try checking in at the [Magic Arcana boards thread](https://web.archive.org/web/20040421000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/showthread.php?s=&threadid=211646) to discuss your guesses!

![Quit looking for clues by reading the mouseover text! You'll get no help and like it!](https://i.ormos.uk/1b/ca/1bcae4e32b78a07dc7322874843bd4e96f89e857c2d7de6c7c4ffc3fc8f84d69.jpg)

If you need a resource for looking up some art, try the *[Mirrodin](https://web.archive.org/web/20080501125902/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/expansion/mirrodin)* and *[Darksteel](https://web.archive.org/web/20080506160149/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/expansion/darksteel)* product pages. They contain links to sortable spoiler lists chock full of "autocard" links. Or load up ***Magic** Online* and use its card search abilities to find art.

Tune in to Magic Arcana tomorrow for the answers!
