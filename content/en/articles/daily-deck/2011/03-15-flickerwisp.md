No matter how long a [Pro Tour Qualifier](/en/events/2010/nagoya11-qualifiers/qualifiers-for-pro-tour-nagoya-2011) season goes on, there are always new, exciting strategies that pop up at the top tables. Take, for example, the card Flickerwisp. When it was legal in Standard, it saw little if any play. But fast forward a few years to the Extended format, and Jeremiah Haney used the 3/1 flyer (along with its 3/3 cousin, Glimmerpoint Stag) to take the top slot at a PTQ for Nagoya in Seattle, Washington. His deck abuses Flickerwisp's "enters the battlefield" trigger with cards like Lone Missionary for life, Mortarpod to shoot creatures with a new Germ each time it gets "flickered," Pilgrim's Eye to hunt up lands, and Wall of Omens to draw cards. For winning the long game? Mr. Haney had Sun Titan to keep the card advantage engine going. Awesome!
