How are you enjoying *Innistrad* so far? Do you have any questions about it? What if you could ask your question directly to **Mark Rosewater** (the design lead), **Erik Lauer** (the development lead), and **Brady Dommermuth** (the creative lead)?

The chat is on October 5 at 2:00 PM PDT. That's 5:00 PM on the East Coast, 9:00 PM GMT, and you'll have to figure it out for yourself for the rest of the world.

You can submit questions ahead of time [in this thread](https://web.archive.org/web/20111005000000/http://community.wizards.com/go/thread/view/75842/28409153/innistrad_live_chat_with_mark_rosewater,_erik_lauer,__brady_dommermuth_105_2:00pm_pdt?liveview=0), and the chat will be [right here](https://web.archive.org/web/20111005000000/http://community.wizards.com/go/chat/3).

Actually, that's probably important enough to give it its own paragraph: the chat will be [right here](https://web.archive.org/web/20111005000000/http://community.wizards.com/go/chat/3).

If you miss the chat, a complete transcript is [right here](https://web.archive.org/web/20111005000000/http://community.wizards.com/magicthegathering/blog/2011/10/05/innistrad_chat_transcript)!
