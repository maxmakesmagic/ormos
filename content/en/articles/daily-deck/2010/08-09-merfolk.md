The final Legacy Grand Prix of 2010 was held last weekend in Columbus, Ohio, and legendary Japanese pro Tomoharu Saito took the top spot! Saito, who is eligible for the 2010 Hall of Fame, got there on the back of a controlling Merfolk strategy. Many predicted Merfolk couldn't survive a field that included aggro decks like Zoo and Goblins, but Saito proved them wrong! Read more at the [official coverage](/en/events/2010/gpcol10/saitos-legacy-continues).
