On Monday, March 3rd at 9:00 am PST, *Morningtide* goes on sale in the Magic Online store.

NOTE: *Morningtide* becomes legal in ALL Constructed formats the day it goes on sale (March 3). All casual rooms, constructed queues, and Premier Events will use *Morningtide* starting on March 3.

*Morningtide* Release events begin Friday, March 7 and will have 3 components:

Release Leagues  
 Draft Queues  
 Premier Event "No Top 8" Flights

### *Morningtide* Release Leagues

SIGNUP: March 7, 9:00am – March 20, 9:00pm (the final 1 week, 2x league will start no later than 9:00pm on March 20). Release Leagues will have a maximum of 256 players each and will auto-spawn (as soon as a league reaches 256 players, another will begin registration).

LEAGUE DURATION: 1 week

PRODUCT: 1 *Lorwyn* Tournament Pack and 3 *Morningtide* Booster Packs.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 2x prizes, Participation Avatar: Stonehewer Giant; Records of 5-0 get Prize Avatar: Maralen of the Mornsong .

Avatars will be distributed Approx. 3 weeks after the last league concludes

*NOTE: The regularly schedule downtime will still take place on Wednesday, March 12 & 19 at 8:00 am PST. Plan your league games accordingly as no compensation will be issued if you lose a game due to the servers going down for maintenance.*

### *Morningtide* Draft Queues

QUEUES BEGIN: Draft Queues will run for a 24-hour period (except for scheduled maintenance downtimes) every other day starting on Saturday, March 8 at 10:00 AM PST. A complete schedule of when draft queues will be in operation (all times PST):

March 8 10:00 AM – March 9 10:00 AM  
 March 10 10:00 AM – March 11 10:00 AM  
 March 12 10:00 AM – March 13 10:00 AM  
 March 14 10:00 AM – March 15 10:00 AM  
 March 16 10:00 AM – March 17 10:00 AM  
 March 18 10:00 AM – March 18 10:00 AM

FORMATS: Booster Draft

PRODUCT: 2 *Lorwyn* booster and 1 *Morningtide* Booster, or 3 *Morningtide* Boosters. (Note: Only *Lorwyn/Morningtide* and *10th Edition* drafts will run for the duration of the *Morningtide* Release Events)

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 4-3-2-2 and 8-4

*NOTE: The regularly schedule downtime will still take place on Wednesday, March 12 & 19 at 8:00 am PST. Queues will be down during the downtime.*

### *Morningtide* "No Top 8" Premier Events

DURATION: Premier Events will run for a 24-hour period every other day starting on Friday March 7 at 12:00 PM PST. "No Top 8" Flights will have a minimum of 24 players, maximum of 32 players per flight and will start every 15 minutes. A complete schedule of when Premier Events will be in operation (all times PST):

March 7 10:00 AM – March 8 9:45 AM  
 March 9 10:00 AM – March 10 9:45 AM  
 March 11 10:00 AM – March 11 10:00 PM  
 March 13 10:00 AM – March 14 9:45 AM  
 March 15 10:00 AM – March 16 9:45 AM  
 March 17 10:00 AM – March 18 9:45 AM  
 March 19 10:00 AM – March 20 9:45 AM

PRODUCT: 1 *Lorwyn* Tournament Pack and 3 *Morningtide* Booster Packs.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product 

PRIZE SUPPORT: As there is no top 8, prizes are based on total match points at the end of the tournament. Each match win awards 3 points, each draw awards 1 point, and each loss awards 0 points.

*NOTE: The regularly schedule downtime will still take place on Wednesday, March 12 & 19 at 8:00 am PST. There will be no Premier Events starting from 10:00 pm on March 11 through 10:00 am on March 12, or starting from 10:00 pm on March 18 through 10:00 am on March 19.*

### Important information regarding system crashes and refunds for Premier Events

If during your event the system crashes, 1 of 3 things will happen:

1.  The system will come right back up. In this case:

    *   Events will continue and additional time will be given to allow players to finish the event
    *   No Compensation will be given

2.  The system cannot recover the event and "finishes" (prematurely ends) the event, or a WOTC Tournament Coordinator must "finish" (prematurely end) the event.

    *   We will accept the prize payouts "as is" - This means that whatever prize payouts and invites the system awards, we will not change what the system does.
    *   No changes will be made to these events from what the system automatically pays out as prizes or invitations

    If the event is in TOP 8:

    *   No product or tickets will be refunded
    *   All players keep the product used to enter the event as well as the drafted cards from the TOP 8 draft.

    If the event is still in Swiss Rounds:

    *   Players can [request a refund through Customer Service](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=161).
    *   We will refund your Product and tickets.

3.  The event is cleared or cancelled by the system. If an event is cancelled this means the event can no longer be viewed in the premier event listings.

    *   The system will automatically refund your tickets.
    *   Players can [request a refund through Customer Service](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=161).
    *   We will refund your Product.
