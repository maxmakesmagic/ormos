If you thought Nicol Bolas, Planeswalker was to be found dominating only the world of Standard, Jesse Speers would like to have a word with you. Packing the ancient evil into a Grixis-colored control deck, Speers made the Top 8 of a Pro Tour Qualifier in Calgary. Helping Nicol Bolas out was Jace, the Mind Sculptor, Night of Souls' Betrayal, counters, bounce, and removal. One has to imagine that those weenies in the Zoo deck start quaking in their boots when they see Nicol Bolas, Planeswalker hit the battlefield!
