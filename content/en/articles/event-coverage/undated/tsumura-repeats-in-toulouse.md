Kenji Tsumura is the 2006 Grand Prix Toulouse Champion. 

A mere three weeks after his last title at Grand Prix Kuala Lumpur, Tsumura defeated Marijn Lybaert from Belgium for his second victory this month. Not only his own performance was impressive, the overall Japanese performance was as well: Of the four Japanese at the event, three made it to the top 8, and no European could beat them there - they only lost to each other. 

This was the last Grand Prix using *Ravnica* block draft - while *Ravnica* still will be used for the Sealed Deck portion of the events, from Malmö on the second day will feature *Coldsnap*. So join us for new challenges in Malmö or St. Louis on July 22nd, or follow the coverage here on magicthegathering.com

     <p>The draft started with a hard choice, <a>Lightning Helix</a>, <a>Compulsive Research</a>, or <a>Belltower Sphinx</a>. After thinking long, Shuuhei opted for the flyer.The next booster contained another Research and after some thinking he took it over <a>Viashino Fangtail</a>.</p>&#10;      <p>He went for white in the next booster with <a>Veteran Armorer</a> over <a>Strands of Undeath</a> and the safer but worse <a>Flight of Fancy</a>. A 4th pick <a>Civic Wayfinder</a> taken over another <a>Flight of Fancy</a> made him think about green though. I know Shuuhei dislikes Karoo lands, so I was only slightly surprised when he took <a>Terraformer</a> over <a>Selesnya Sanctuary</a>. <a>Selesnya Evangel</a> and <a>Carven Caryatid</a> seemed to pull him further into green. A very late <a>Twilight Drover</a> surprised him, and he took it over <a>Conclave Phalanx</a> to keep graft madness open.</p>&#10;      <p>Shuuhei wasn't very happy with his deck so far, and after the draft he told me he made a mistake with taking any green but the Wayfinder (there was a <a>Watery Grave</a> in the same booster as the Evangel)</p>&#10;      <div>&#10;        <div>&#10;          <a>&#10;            <img alt="Pillory of the Sleepless" src="https://i.ormos.uk/23/51/235162930be3f05838b8d12fb31af262fb57c7da86f36277880347d02bbc07a6.jpg">&#10;          </a>&#10;        </div>&#10;      </div>&#10;      <p>The first <i>Guildpact</i> booster offered one of the best rares in the format, Debtor's Knell, and Shuuhei happily took it. With a second pick <a>Pillory of the Sleepless</a> and a <a>Blind Hunter</a> next Shuuhei moved away from green into his favourite guild Orzhov. The switch got rewarded with a <a>Shrieking Grotesque</a> and a <a>Ostiary Thrull</a> next (over another Grotesque). The 6th booster offered nothing less than what is considered by some the best common in <i>Guildpact</i>: another Pillory. <a>Ostiary Thrull</a> (over <a>Orzhov Basilica</a>), <a>Ghost Warden</a>, <a>Vacuumelt</a> and <a>To Arms!</a> made sure he had more than enough playables.</p>&#10;      <p>His deck seemed to be getting very good, but it all depended on the Azorius cards in the last booster. He gets a <a>Minister of Impediments</a> first, followed by <a>Grand Arbiter Augustin IV</a>, <a>Ocular Halo</a>, <a>Silkwing Scout</a> (over <a>Macabre Waltz</a>) and a <a>Freewind Equenaut</a> . After that, the good cards stopped and Shuuhei had to be content with Enemy of the <i>Guildpact</i>, <a>Spell Snare</a>, Riot Spike and <a>Overrule</a>.</p>&#10;      <p>The deck didn't turn out as good as it looked after the <i>Guildpact</i> booster, but it still might be good enough to win or at least reach the finals.</p>&#10;      <p>After the first match, Shuuhei explained his Orzhov common pick order to me. He likes <a>Pillory of the Sleepless</a> best, followed by <a>Blind Hunter</a>. So far everyone I know agrees, but he continues the list with <a>Ostiary Thrull</a> over <a>Shrieking Grotesque</a>, then <a>Ghost Warden</a> followed by <a>Mourning Thrull</a>. <a>Withstand</a> and <a>Absolver Thrull</a> share the next spot, but Shuuhei says he takes Guardian's Magemark over either sometimes as well.</p>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <a name="7">&#10;        &#10;      </a>&#10;      <li>&#10;        <b>Sunday, June 25: 8:41 p.m. - Semifinals: Adrian Olivera (ESP) vs. Marijn Lybaert (BEL)</b>&#10;      </li>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <img src="https://i.ormos.uk/63/d3/63d38aa0bcb6b46db9617df6609bc24a686747b4fb53e2ed4278c5ed2fa735be.jpg">&#10;      <h3>Game 1</h3>&#10;      <p>Marijn chose to play first. Both players took some damage, when Marijn forgot to untap his <a>Beacon Hawk</a> and took two damage for it. Next turn, he was now able to get another <a>Aquastrand Spider</a> into play with only 1 life left. Marijn had a <a>Faith's Fetters</a> for the Spider, and <a>Torch Drake</a> dealt the final point of damage.</p>&#10;      <p>Marijn Lybaert - Adrian Olivera 2-0</p>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <a name="8">&#10;        &#10;      </a>&#10;      <li>&#10;        <b>Sunday, June 25: 9:22 p.m. - Semifinals: Shuuhei Nakamura (JPN) vs. Kenji Tsumura (JPN)</b>&#10;      </li>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <br>&#10;      <table>&#10;        <tbody><tr>&#10;          <td>&#10;            <img src="https://i.ormos.uk/9c/08/9c081895746d49bac61af1cac127720bb5011affdc356c7221f3c4edd28cf66e.jpg">&#10;            <i>Kenji Tsumura</i>&#10;          </td>&#10;        </tr>&#10;      </tbody></table>&#10;      <p>Before the match, both players got to see each others decks.
