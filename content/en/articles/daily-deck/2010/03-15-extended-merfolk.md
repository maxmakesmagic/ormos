Merfolk decks have been a long-time inclusion in the world of **Magic**, but so far this season in the Extended PTQs for San Juan, no Merfolk deck has made the Top 8. That all changed recently when Marshall Arthurs took the top slot using this take on the fishy tribe! Cryptic Command and Mana Leak keep the opponent's worst spells from resolving, while a bevy of Merfolk, many of which pump other Merfolk, do the deed on the ground. It's an awesome deck innovation from Arthurs!
