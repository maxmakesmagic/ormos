If you are a fan of the Knight class, John Thorson’s Vermont States Top 4 list is exactly the deck you’re looking for. He played, as the deck’s name implies, twenty Knights maindeck. He also managed to squeeze in a whole boatload of planeswalkers with Elspeth, Ajani Vengeant, and Sarkhan Vol all helping to push the Knights over the top. You can find John’s list and more in the [2008 State Championship archive](/en/events/2008/all/state-championships-2008-top-8-decklists).
