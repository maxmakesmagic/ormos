![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpoka04/r6fm1ja1.jpg)This round we have a battle of reanimation decks, as Hirabayashi's Angry Hermit inspired Dancing Ghoul deck goes up against Ishida's more traditional blue-black reanimator. Thus far both players have fared well, coming into this round at 5-0. This match is a contrast in reputations, as Ishida is one of Japan's most experienced Magic veterans, while Hirabayashi is extremely well-respected as a writer and deck builder.

### Game 1

Hirabayashi played [Mox Diamond](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Diamond) and [City of Brass](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=City+of+Brass), and Ishida played [Putrid Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrid+Imp). [Mystical Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystical+Tutor) searched up [Buried Alive](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Buried+Alive), which put two [Krosan Cloudscraper](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Krosan+Cloudscraper)s and [Sutured Ghoul](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sutured+Ghoul) into Hirabayashi's graveyard. It seemed that Hirabayashi was set for a quick kill, but Ishida cast [Careful Study](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Careful+Study), put Akroma into his graveyard, and [Reanimate](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reanimate)d Hirabayashi's Ghoul to get it out of the way and provide a 6/6 body (Akroma was removed from the game for the Ghoul). Hirabayashi cast [Burning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Wish) to get [Exhume](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exhume) and brought back a Cloudscraper, which was representing lethal damage if Hirabayashi had [Fling](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fling), due to Ishida taking a point from a sac-land. Even this plan was foiled, as Ishida used [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) to strip the [Fling](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fling) away from Hirabayashi's hand, and then [Reanimate](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reanimate)d a [Phantom Nishoba](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phantom+Nishoba).

Ishida- 1 Hirabayashi- 0

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpoka04/r6fm1ja2.jpg)

### Game 2

Hirabayashi mulliganed his opening hand, and played a first turn [Mox Diamond](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Diamond), digging for a land with [Overmaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overmaster). It wasn't waiting on top of the deck, and this was not the time to get a bad draw. Ishida played [Careful Study](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Careful+Study), discarded two Akromas, played [Chrome Mox](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chrome+Mox), and put the powerhouse legend into play all on turn one. Hirabayashi just laughed, and conceded a turn later.

Ishida- 2 Hirabayashi- 0
