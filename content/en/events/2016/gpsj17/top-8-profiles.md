Learn more about Grand Prix San Jose's Top 8 competitors—including who they are, what they’ve done in the game, and more—by taking a look through their profiles!

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpsj17/t8_hausman.jpg)Name:** Ari Hausman-Cohen

**Age:** 23

**Hometown:** Sunnyvale, California

**Occupation:** Software Engineer

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** 4-0’d an *Aether Revolt* pre-release; Day 1’d 2 Grand Prix (Sane Jose & Vegas 2015)

**What colors did you play in Sealed Deck on Day 1? What cards most impressed you?**

Grixis. [Aether Chaser](https://web.archive.org/web/20170129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Chaser)/[Aether Poisoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20170129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Poisoner) did serious work enabling +3/+4 improvise plays. I underestimated them; it took me until round 6 to realize I was an aggro deck.

**What colors did you play in the first Draft of Day 2? What cards most impressed you?**

Black-Green. Two [Ridgescale Tusker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ridgescale+Tusker) and a [Verdurous Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Verdurous+Gearhulk) is pretty great.

**What colors did you play in the second Draft of Day 2? What cards most impressed you?**

Green-Blue Energy. Larger than Life was pivotal in winning at least one game each round. [Untethered Express](https://web.archive.org/web/20170129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Untethered+Express) was good—surprising no one.

**What is your favorite Limited rare, uncommon, and common in *Aether Revolt*? Why?**

Rare: Aetherspeher Harvester

Uncommon: [Untethered Express](https://web.archive.org/web/20170129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Untethered+Express)

Common: [Aether Poisoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20170129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Poisoner)

*Aether Revolt*'s Uncommon-and-up vehicles do work!

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

Guess I’m better/luckier than I thought.
