* * *

The card Bribery was originally slated to be in *Urza's Destiny*, and had the playtest name "Kick-Ass Control Magic." But the *Destiny* team wanted to continue the "free mechanic" into that set, and created Treachery, which is basically a five mana "free" Control Magic.

Bribery was moved up a set into *Mercadian Masques*, where it found a happy home, and both cards made significant impacts on their respective Block Constructed -- and Standard -- metagames.

Later, *Odyssey* debuted Persuasion, which is Treachery without the free mechanic. The development teams believe the original Control Magic was undercosted at 4 mana, and Treachery was undercosted at 5, but both Bribery and Persuasion are costed correctly.
