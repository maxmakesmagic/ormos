### Redemption Feature Available Soon

* * *

Wizards of the Coast is happy to announce the ***Magic** Online* redemption feature is in the final testing stages, and will be available by the end of this year.

Here are the basics of what redemption is all about.

### What is "Redemption"?

In the ***Magic** Online* world, redemption means that players can trade in a full set of online cards for a matching set of physical cards. And, since there still seems to be some confusion on this point, you can't turn in a set of physical cards for a set of online cards.

### What Do You Mean by "Turn In"?

If you decide to redeem a set of cards, those cards are removed from your collection. So the online cards go away, and the physical cards are sent to your home. Once you've decided to redeem a set, you can't put the cards back in your online collection.

### What Can Players Redeem?

Players can redeem full standard or premium sets of cards, not mix-and-match. For example, if you turn in a full set of *InvasionTM* standard cards, you'll get a full set of standard physical cards. If you turn in a full set of *Invasion* premium cards, you'll get a full set of premium physical cards in all their foily glory.

### What Makes Up a Full Set?

Well, that depends on the set. There are 350 individual cards in a large set, and 143 cards in a small set. The *Seventh EditionTM, Invasion, OdysseyTM,* and *OnslaughtTM* sets are large, while the *PlaneshiftTM, ApocalypseTM, TormentTM,* and *JudgmentTM* sets are small.

A lot of players have been waiting for the redemption feature to be implemented, and we appreciate your patience. Once we have it up and running, all the nitty-gritty details will be available at the [***Magic** Online* Store](https://web.archive.org/web/20021210000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com&origin=magic_magiconline_redemption).
