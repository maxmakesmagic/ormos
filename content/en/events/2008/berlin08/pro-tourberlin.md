Berlin, Germany is the second stop in the 2008 Pro Tour season. But the pros won't be the only ones enjoying themselves: the Pro Tour is above all a great weekend of **Magic** that brings fantastic opportunities for all players, whether you're qualified for the main event, participating in public events, playing against the people who make **Magic**, or just hanging around with your friends.

Pro Tour–Berlin is an Extended format event that runs October 31-November 2, 2008. The Last-Chance Qualifier will be held Thursday at the event hall (registration starts at 3 p.m.). For more information, visit the complete [Public Events schedule](/en/events/2008/berlin08-public/pro-tourberlin-public-events).

### Site Attractions

**PUBLIC EVENTS** 

Come one, come all to participate in four days of events open to the public. Starting Thursday with the Last-Chance Qualifier, there are tournaments, drafts, and leagues all weekend. Prizes include invitations and airfare to Pro Tour–Kyoto and Pro Tour–Honolulu, iPods, Digital Cameras, a set of 30 Revised Dual Lands, and a trip to the 2008 World Championships in Memphis, USA! Pro Tour–Berlin also features Super Friday Night **Magic** and the €2,000 Draft Challenge.

*   **[Complete Public Events Schedule](/en/events/2008/berlin08-public/pro-tourberlin-public-events)**
*   **[A Planeswalker's Journey through the Pro Tour](/en/events/2008/berlin08-journey/a-planeswalkers-journey-through-the-pro-tour)**

**GUNSLINGING**  
 Test your **Magic** skills against the guys who make the game Pro Tour–Berlin 2008 will feature these members of **Magic** R&D: Mark Gottlieb and Scott Johns. Win and you get to walk away with cool **Magic** prizes! 

**ARTISTS ON SITE**  
 Top **Magic** artists John Avon, Zoltan Boros, and Gabor Szikszai will be at Pro Tour–Berlin. The artists booth will be open Friday-Sunday from 10 a.m. - 5 p.m. For more information about each artist, [click here](/en/events/2008/berlin08-artists/pro-tourberlin-artists).

**WATCH THE TOP 8**  
 All of Sunday's Top 8 action can be viewed screens in the hall, with Randy Buehler and Brian David-Marshall providing the play-by-play and color commentary. Sunday's action begins at 10:45 a.m. local time, which is 4:45 a.m. ET, and is available as a [webcast](/en/events/2004/webcast/magic-the-gathering-webcast-schedule) from the comfort of your own home.
