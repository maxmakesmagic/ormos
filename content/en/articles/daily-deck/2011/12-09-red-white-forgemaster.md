At the StarCityGames.com Open in St. Louis this past weekend, Anthony Molinari unveiled an exciting brand new archetype: Red-White Forgemaster! Using the card's namesake Kuldotha Forgemaster, Anthony was powering out Blightsteel Colossus and Mindslaver as early as turn four. Check out this fun deck yourself, and consider it for a Friday Night **Magic** event tonight!

Anthony Molinari's Red-White Forgemaster 

Standard – StarCityGames.com Open in St. Louis
