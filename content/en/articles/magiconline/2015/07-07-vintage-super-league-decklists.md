Every Tuesday through July 24, at 6:00 p.m. Pacific (UTC-7), the newest episode of the Vintage Super League airs live on [twitch.tv/magic](http://www.twitch.tv/magic).

If you haven't already, check out Randy Buehler's [article](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/06-30-standard-super-league-finals-and-the-return-of-vintage) that gives an overview of what the Super League schedule is going to look like over the next few months as it builds towards the Super League Championships!

Follow the links below to take you to each player's decklist—they'll be using these decks throughout the duration of the the upcoming Vintage Super League season.
