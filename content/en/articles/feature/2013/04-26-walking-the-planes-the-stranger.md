How far will you go to do what you must? Go on a journey with Walking the Planes to [Grand Prix Strasbourg](https://web.archive.org/web/20130426000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/danes-make-their-mark-strasbourg), where we meet a Hall of Famer, a Goblin gamer, a star of the stream, and a wayward American chasing a dream. Like moose in mating season, there's some awkward and silly encounters that you won't soon forget. It's Episode #12: "The Stranger!"
