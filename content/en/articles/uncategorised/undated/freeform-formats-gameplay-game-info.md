The rules of this casual *Magic Online* format are rather relaxed:

1.  Play with any set or card.
2.  Nothing is banned.
3.  Build your deck with as many copies of a card as you’d like.
4.  Throw in Vanguard cards and even the promotional Gleemox card, if you’d like.
5.  All you need is at least 40 cards in your deck.
