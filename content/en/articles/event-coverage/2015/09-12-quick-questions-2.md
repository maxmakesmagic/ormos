**What is your favorite spoiled card from *Battle for Zendikar,* and why?**

The *Battle for Zendikar* spoilers are in full spring, and *Magic’s* return to a treasured (if beleaguered) plane is fast approaching. I talked to a few of the players competing at Grand Prix Oklahoma City about what they’re most looking forward to when the battle for Zendikar begins.

**Pascal Maynard**

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpokc15/qq2_GP_OKC_MAYNARD.jpg)**  
*Pascal Maynard*

“Ob Nixilis Reignited. Black lost [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20150912000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) but Abzan it’s still going to be a strong choice, and Ob Nixilis is probably the best follow-up to [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150912000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino).”

**William “Huey” Jensen**

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpokc15/qq2_GP_OKC_JENSEN.jpg)**  
*William “Huey” Jensen*

“Gideon, Ally of Zendikar. I lost to [Xenagos, the Reveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20150912000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xenagos%2C+the+Reveler) a lot with control decks. It’s nice to have a similar planeswalker who can also fit in control decks.

**Corey Burkhart**

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpokc15/qq2_GP_OKC_BURKHART.jpg)**  
*Corey Burkhart*

“From Beyond. [Awakening Zone](https://web.archive.org/web/20150912000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Awakening+Zone) was a good card that people liked, and this one is just as good. From a design standpoint, I actually really like Kiora, Master of the Depths. I think it’s one of the best-designed planeswalkers in a while; it’s exactly what blue and green want to be doing.”

**Eric Centauri**

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpokc15/qq2_GP_OKC_CENTAURI.jpg)**  
*Eric Centauri*

“It may not be the most exciting answer, but the full-art lands are gorgeous. It’s sweet that anyone can get them for their decks, and it’s really exciting to know you might be able to pull an Expedition.”
