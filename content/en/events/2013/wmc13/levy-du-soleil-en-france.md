With a thrilling comeback rarely seen at this level of competition, France returned to the forefront of **Magic**'s international stage by defeating Hungary in the finals of the World **Magic** Cup. 

While France has never claimed any form of international team trophy, it has long been recognized as one of **Magic**'s powerhouse nations. Led by Hall of Famer Raphaël Lévy, the French contingent came to the World **Magic** Cup with a dream of returning home with the trophy for the first time. Over the course of three days, thirteen rounds of Swiss, and three elimination rounds, they have fulfilled that dream and now head back to a proud nation with the World **Magic** Cup trophy. 

In the Unified Standard finals, each team held their collective breath on every draw step, waiting to see what the top of their deck held. The final game of the final match of the World **Magic** Cup hinged on the advantage that the miracle cards from *Avacyn Restored* could provide. Though they faced down a lethal Olivia Voldaren from the Hungarian team, France had maneuvered themselves into position to return home victorious thanks to a top-decked Rakdos's Return. 

Congratulations to the French team: Raphaël Lévy, Yann Guthmann, Timothée Simonot, and Stephane Soubrier!
