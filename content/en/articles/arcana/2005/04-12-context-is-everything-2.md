M**agic** is a game of context.

![Dusk Imp is pretty darn close to Feral Shadow](https://i.ormos.uk/71/69/7169fd8826bfd24245b2f2d3a794f6b49a2a07262e0d4f370370b0385a70df52.jpg)

Over the years Magic Arcana has touched on the concepts of "[functionally identical](/en/articles/arcana/2003/04-18-something-old-something-new)" (a card that, while not a strict reprint of another card, differs from it only in irrelevant characteristics) and "[strictly better](/en/articles/arcana/2003/11-13-mirrodin-playtest-cards-iii-strictly-better)" (a card that does more than another card for the exact same cost). But as **Magic** grows, context changes, and so do these concepts.

Emphasis on creature type in *Onslaught* block, and spiritcraft in Kamigawa block, is one way that **Magic** has expanded its context. During the *Odyssey* days, the difference between Dusk Imp (Creature—Imp) and *Mirage's* Feral Shadow (Creature—Nightstalker) was almost nil. 

However, some recent near-reprints have made quite a bit of difference. Consider:

*   **Uktabi Orangutan -> Viridian Shaman:** Both Elf *and* Shaman are useful creature types nowadays

*   **Merfolk Looter -> Thought Courier:** The Courier has the more useful Wizard creature type nowadays, since Merfolk are in decline

*   **Suntail Hawk -> Lantern Kami:** The kami works with spiritcraft; and interestingly, these cards are both in Standard right now, allowing you to play 8 1-mana flyers in White Weenie decks

*   **Horned Turtle -> River Kaijin:** Only one of these triggers Sire of the Storm

*   **Merfolk of the Pearl Trident -> Fugitive Wizard -> Wandering Ones:** Blue always seems to have a 1/1 vanilla creature for ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) hanging around, but it matters a lot which you play, depending on whether you have Lord of Atlantis, Voidmage Prodigy, or Soul of Magma already on the board!

*Unhinged* further expanded that context when it introduced "name matters," "flavor text matters" and "artist matters" cards. Ekundu Griffin and Razorfoot Griffin may be functionally identical to a Callous Oppressor, but only one gets +2/+2 and trample after you cast Graphic Violence naming David A. Cherry!
