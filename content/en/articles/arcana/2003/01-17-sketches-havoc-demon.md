This week's "Sketches" looks at *Legions* new Demon, [Havoc Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20030117000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Havoc+Demon) by Thomas Baxa. Thomas is a relatively new artist, and specializes in black cards.

### 1\. Art Description

These are the instructions given to the artist:

"Color: black  
 Action: Show a demon flying at night."

Quite simple!

### 2\. Sketch

This is the preliminary sketch submitted by Thomas.

The creative team was not thrilled with the idea of a composite piece (think senior picture), and asked Thomas to choose one or the other—the head shot or the body shot. The "X" shows the decision was made to just use the head shot.

Here is a large version of the final piece.
