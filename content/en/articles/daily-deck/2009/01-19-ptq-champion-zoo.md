The first PTQs for Honolulu have started rolling in, and Bradley Carpenter earned a ticket to the tropical Hawaii playing the tried and true Zoo. His build used Tidehollow Sculler instead of the sometimes popular Shadow Guildmage, but the truly innovative card he added to the mix was a full playset of Lightning Angel! Read Mike Flores' [discuss this innovation in the most recent Top Decks](/en/articles/top-decks/2009/01-15-two-for-the-rogues).
