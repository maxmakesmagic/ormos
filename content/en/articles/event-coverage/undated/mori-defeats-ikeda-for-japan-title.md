## 2010 Japan National Championship

[日本語の取材へ](http://mtg-jp.com/eventc/jpnats10/) 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/26/55/2655cc71e109c4688c2c7d8adb471a178c169eed7768532b7f4216b4bc395d02.jpg)

The 2010 Japan National Championship has drawn to a close! The Top 8 was set with four seasoned veterans of the Pro Tour and four newcomers to the premier level stage. The vets? Former Player of the Year Shouta Yasooka, Pro Tour Top 8er Naoki Shimizu, the legendary Tsuyoshi Ikeda, and former world champion Katsuhiro Mori.

Across the bracket from those four were a host of names little was known about. For Ryouichi Tamada, Shou Tagomori, Shou Ishikawa, and Jinpei Hassaku it was their chance to make their mark on the Japanese scene. Tagomori and Tamada escaped the Quarterfinals but were felled in the Semifinals by the more seasoned of their compatriots. That set up a playoff between the two in order to determine which would join his teammates to represent Team Japan at the 2010 World Championships. The winner? Shou Tagomori.

All that set up a Finals between legends Katsuhiro Mori and Tsuyoshi Ikeda. Tsuyoshi had beaten one red deck already on the day but Mori was no average red mage. Throughout the Top 8 he had managed to open on a first turn [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide), one of the most powerful plays in Standard, nearly every game. He continued that streak through the Finals allowing him to sweep his opponent to repeat and become a two-time Japanese National Champion.

Congratulations to Katsuhiro Mori, the 2010 Japanese National Champion!

## Quarterfinals: Katsuhiro Mori vs. Naoki Shimizu

*by Bill Stark*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d1/46/d146fcb48a279feb9e8dc6e6ccf1f415c386e09b2b95a5415df68b85245ca470.jpg)*Naoki Shimizu*

Being the rare breed of ***Magic*** player with a Pro Tour Top 8 on your resume puts you into an elite class of player few will ever know the graces of. Unfortunately for Naoki Shimizu, who earned the accolade after making the Top 8 of Pro Tour-Austin in 2009, he found his resume paled in comparison to his Quarterfinals opponent at the 2010 Japan National Championship. That man? Katsuhiro Mori who was a former Japan national champion and a former world champion. Both were talented players, but Naoki would have his work cut out for him to take the superstar down in order to advance to the Semifinals.

That uphill battle became decidedly more pronounced as Naoki found himself mulliganing to five to kick off the first game. A [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide), his opponent's most aggressive start, hit on the first turn and immediately went on the warpath. Shimizu found a [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) to help him play defense but Katsuhiro cast [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) targeting Naoki; when a red mage starts sending burn to the face a player knows they're in trouble.

Shimizu gained a slight reprieve as Mori found himself stuck on just two lands and unable to attack through the 0/4 Wall. When he finally found a third land he was able to cast [Hell's Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hell%27s+Thunder) but had the 4/4 permanently answered via [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile) when it moved to the red zone; at least he knew he'd hit his fourth land drop.

But the creature path wasn't Mori's plan any longer and he switched gears to just burn. The spells continued going straight to Naoki's face with a second [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) taking him to 5. Mori revealed a [Quenchable Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quenchable+Fire) maindeck to make it 2 and though Shimizu had access to blue mana to pay for the extra damage from the spell, Katsuhiro had one final burn spell to finish the injured Shimizu off and they headed to the second game.

Katsuhiro Mori 1, Naoki Shimizu 0

The mulligan troubles continued for Naoki in the second game as he had to ship back his hand of seven for a hand of six. He was able to keep those but took 2 as Katsuhiro Mori had a first-turn [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) yet again. Naoki was ready with [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) to help with blocking duty on his turn two, but [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks) pre-combat from Mori made the Guide a 4/2 and begged the question of whether Naoki was willing to sacrifice his Wall to block in order to save himself 4 life. He was, and the 0/4 hit the bin.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fd/eb/fdebb90ab78e313ad53e82acc6e363e5eab01f2d165626558f1957f4933e1ff3.jpg)*Katsuhiro Mori*

[Sea Gate Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sea+Gate+Oracle) gave Shimizu a strong follow-up and another means of blocking his opponent's Goblin. [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) from Mori put a damper on those plans, however, and [Hellspark Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellspark+Elemental) joined the Guide to drop Naoki to 13 life. A second [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) from Naoki was killed with [Flame Slash](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flame+Slash) and Mori unearthed his [Hellspark Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellspark+Elemental) and sent his team back in to drop Shimizu to just 8 life.

With a hand full of [Sovereigns of Lost Alara](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sovereigns+of+Lost+Alara) and [Eldrazi Conscription](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Conscription), Naoki Shimizu found himself in the wrong position: having drawn his expensive spells while sitting on five mana. Fortunately for him his opponent started to flood and found himself in the exact opposite position. With Naoki on 6 life Mori drew card after card, passing calmly. He wasn't going to pull the trigger on his burn spells until his opponent tapped out, or he was sitting on a lot of lands in hand on a bluff.

A [Cunning Sparkmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cunning+Sparkmage) came off the top for the red mage, and he cast it. That forced Naoki's hand and he used [Bant Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bant+Charm) in an attempt to put it to the bottom of his opponent's library. In response the Sparkmage turned sideways to blow up a [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise).

Naoki finally found himself with enough mana to cast [Sovereigns of Lost Alara](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sovereigns+of+Lost+Alara) with two left over to bluff [Negate](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Negate). He didn't have the counter, however, and Katsuhiro called, revealing his hand to be not land but spells and sending a host of red instants and sorceries at his opponent's head on his turn. Naoki nodded solemnly and found himself behind by two games.

Katsuhiro Mori 2, Naoki Shimizu 0

The mulligan woes continued yet again for Naoki Shimizu's Bant Conscription deck and he sent back not one but two hands to his library for the third game of the match. He got a small reprieve in the fact his opponent didn't have a [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) on the first turn for the first time in their games, but Mori pressed in with a second-turn [Hellspark Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellspark+Elemental). Shimizu, stuck on an [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island) and a [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest) for lands, missed his third land drop and passed with a grip full of white cards.

Shimizu found a third land but it did not make white mana. He used it to cast [Sea Gate Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sea+Gate+Oracle), but his 1/3 failed to find him a source of white too. [Hell's Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hell%27s+Thunder) pressed in for Mori, who followed up with a second the next turn and even had [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks) to pump it. The 4/4s proved to be far too much for poor Naoki's mana screwed board position and in lackluster fashion he found himself out of the 2010 Japan National Championship Top 8 just one round after he had started it.

Katsuhiro Mori 3, Naoki Shimizu 0

## Quarterfinals: Tsuyoshi Ikeda vs. Shouta Yasooka

*by Bill Stark*

For many years the Pro Tour failed to respect pros from the island nation of Japan, but with his Player of the Year title in 2006 Shouta Yasooka helped shake that assumption to the core and effectively proclaimed Japanese players to be the best in the game. His opponent in the Quarterfinals of the 2010 Japan National Championship, Tsuyoshi Ikeda, was one of the men responsible for paving the way for Shouta's success. After toiling on the early Pro Tour for years, Ikeda's trailblazing there gave younger players like Yasooka the confidence they needed to compete on the highest levels.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/70/81/708194485608480c7dae024a17ad7733b0870dc0fcf72024ab7ba24d7aea38cd.jpg)*Tsuyoshi Ikeda*

Ikeda won the roll and cast [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) on his second turn to knock out Shouta's first-turn land drop. Yasooka's Jund deck was notoriously shaky on its manabase, and a second copy of [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) further added to his woes a turn later. On four mana Shouta had enough colored mana to cast [Bloodbraid Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodbraid+Elf) and used the 3/2 to make up some ground by cascading a free [Sprouting Thrinax](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sprouting+Thrinax) to the table. [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) for Tsuyoshi kept the Elf at bay and he cast a third [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) to leave his opponent with three [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island)s and a [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest).

Shouta began attacking through the Wall with both his Elf and the Thrinax but had no more lands and could only watch as Tsuyoshi cast a [Baneslayer Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baneslayer+Angel) and began going on the offensive. [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) came down to help Tsuyoshi stay ahead and he used the fateseal ability to prevent it from dying to an attack out of Shouta.

Feeling quite confident in his hold on the game Ikeda began activating [Celestial Colonnade](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Celestial+Colonnade) to join in attacking Yasooka. It took one turn of 9-point beats for Yasooka to realize he was too color screwed from [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) to continue and Ikeda had taken the first game.

Tsuyoshi Ikeda 1, Shouta Yasooka 0

On the play in the second game Shouta had at least one turn to make full use of his mana before Tsuyoshi Ikeda's [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) could begin mucking with it. He took full advantage casting [Putrid Leech](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrid+Leech) after opening on [Raging Ravine](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raging+Ravine) and [Swamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swamp). The 2/2 was a powerfully aggressive threat against Ikeda's slower blue-white-red control strategy in part because it could attack through [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) and represented a five turn clock.

Sure enough Ikeda cast [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) on his second turn but opted not to block when Shouta crashed in with the Leech. Post-combat the Jund player cast [Blightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blightning) and nabbed [Day of Judgment](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Day+of+Judgment) and [Baneslayer Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baneslayer+Angel). Tsuyoshi simply played a land and passed on his turn.

The Leech went back to the red zone and Ikeda blocked it with his Wall. Before it could die, however, he used [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile) on his own creature to ramp himself up a land. Post-combat Shouta cast a second [Putrid Leech](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrid+Leech) and passed and thanks to his extra mana Ikeda was able to cast [Gideon Jura](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon+Jura) and use it to blow up his opponent's tapped Leech. Shouta fired back with [Bloodbraid Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodbraid+Elf) cascading into [Maelstrom Pulse](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Maelstrom+Pulse) for the planeswalker and sent his team sideways for 7.

Without his Gideon Tsuyoshi found himself scrambling for a second plan and Shouta's 3/2 Elf and [Putrid Leech](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrid+Leech) quickly proved too much to handle, evening the match at one game a piece.

Tsuyoshi Ikeda 1, Shouta Yasooka 1

[Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) was the first spell cast of the third game, just as it had been in the first game and it bought a surprising amount of time for Ikeda. He watched as his Jund opponent passed for three straight turns without a spell. Meanwhile the control player exploded with [Everflowing Chalice](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Everflowing+Chalice) accelerating him into [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) and [Elspeth, Knight-Errant](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Knight-Errant). The planeswalker began pumping out Soldier tokens to serve as blockers as the game went on.

[Goblin Ruinblaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Ruinblaster) with kicker was Yasooka's first spell, and it knocked out a [Glacial Fortress](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glacial+Fortress). His second was [Bloodbraid Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodbraid+Elf) cascading into [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress). The discard spell revealed [Oblivion Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Ring), [Journey to Nowhere](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Journey+to+Nowhere), and [Celestial Purge](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Celestial+Purge) which was sent to the graveyard. He couldn't attack his Elf through the Wall and it looked like he was going to need to come up with an alternative to creatures if he wanted to win due to all the removal in his opponent's hand.

Feeling comfortable with having the pace of the game in hand, Ikeda began activating a [Celestial Colonnade](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Celestial+Colonnade) in order to work on Shouta's life total. His Elspeth pumped out Soldiers to continue blocking on the ground and Yasooka's chances started looking very grim. He did find a second [Bloodbraid Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodbraid+Elf) that cascaded him into [Doom Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doom+Blade) and cleared the table of Soldiers by attacking, though it cost him one of the Bloodbraids and a Ruinblaster to do so.

A second [Goblin Ruinblaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Ruinblaster) for Shouta allowed him to knock the [Celestial Colonnade](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Celestial+Colonnade) out of the sky, but he found himself in need of a solution to [Baneslayer Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baneslayer+Angel) as Ikeda plopped a copy of the 5/5 onto the battlefield. He found it in [Maelstrom Pulse](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Maelstrom+Pulse) but that still left Elspeth pumping Soldiers out; no matter what he did, Shouta couldn't catch a break.

He cast [Sarkhan the Mad](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sarkhan+the+Mad) but lost it a turn later to an attack from a Soldier token given an [Angelic Blessing](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angelic+Blessing) by Elspeth. He managed to clear his opponent's hand with a [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress), forcing Ikeda to respond by using [Celestial Purge](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Celestial+Purge) on [Goblin Ruinblaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Ruinblaster), and began pressuring with [Lavaclaw Reaches](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lavaclaw+Reaches). The creature-land's attack was simply sucked up by a Soldier token, which was replaced by Elspeth the following turn, and a second [Celestial Colonnade](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Celestial+Colonnade) made its way to the battlefield for Tsuyoshi. Shouta was still spinning his wheels, trying to dig his way out of the hole he was in.

He cast [Blightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blightning) to knock three loyalty counters off Elspeth and cast [Goblin Ruinblaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Ruinblaster) with kicker to take out the Colonnade. The 2/1 and his [Lavaclaw Reaches](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lavaclaw+Reaches) traded for three Soldier tokens, clearing the field of all creatures, and Shouta followed up with a planeswalker of his own in [Garruk Wildspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Wildspeaker). The green mythic pumped out a Beast and Ikeda had no play except to make a Soldier on his turn.

Yet another [Goblin Ruinblaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Ruinblaster) showed up for Shouta who sent it and his Beast to the red zone at Elspeth. A Soldier took out the Goblin and Elspeth fell to four loyalty. With no cards in his hand Tsuyoshi needed help from the top of his library but luckily he found it: [Baneslayer Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baneslayer+Angel). He cast the 5/5 and watched as his opponent used the last loyalty counter on Garruk to make a third Beast, passing with no other action.

The Angel went to the red zone to drop Shouta to 10 and Ikeda continued making Soldiers. Shouta attacked his Beasts into the planeswalker and then cast [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle) to shut off Elspeth for good. He fell to 5 on his opponent's next attack and had one turn to draw an answer to the Angel. He didn't find it and found himself down two games.

Tsuyoshi Ikeda 2, Shouta Yasooka 1

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ec/b2/ecb2683f7eb24ea070f5987313bd3a1b53406371189a1530b44677670e69bac0.jpg)*Shouta Yasooka*

Back on the play Shouta again had his second-turn [Putrid Leech](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrid+Leech) and immediately sent it to the red zone on his second turn. When he pumped his opponent responded with [Celestial Purge](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Celestial+Purge) to take it out. Ikeda cast [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) to stall the ground and put himself up a card but lost a [Celestial Colonnade](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Celestial+Colonnade) to [Goblin Ruinblaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Ruinblaster) with kicker from his opponent.

A second copy of [Goblin Ruinblaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Ruinblaster) took out a second non-basic from Ikeda and allowed Shouta to start attacking through the [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) for 2 each turn. With no spells to play on six mana, Yasooka activated a [Raging Ravine](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raging+Ravine) and made his attack worth 6 as the Wall blocked a Ruinblaster. Ikeda's control deck worked on building up its manabase again, finally getting to four for [Elspeth, Knight-Errant](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Knight-Errant). She hopped a Soldier token to the battlefield and the 1/1 was ready and able to take out any [Goblin Ruinblaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Ruinblaster)s headed towards its owner.

With not a lot of time left, Shouta had no choice but to send his team sideways attacking with [Bloodbraid Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodbraid+Elf), [Putrid Leech](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrid+Leech), and two Beast tokens. Ikeda blocked a Bloodbraid with his [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) and fell to 13. On what had the potential to be the final turn of the match, Tsuyoshi Ikeda untapped and considered his options. Did he risk attacking all in and losing to some type of trick from his opponent? With no evidence either way which play was correct, Ikeda activated his Colonnade and sent the team to the red zone. Shouta exhaled and nodded his head, scooping his cards up.

Ikeda had done it!

Tsuyoshi Ikeda 3, Shouta Yasooka 2

When the 2010 Japan National Championship Top 8 was called it split exactly in half: four seasoned veterans of the Pro Tour stage, four relative newcomers. The third turn brought a [Ball Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ball+Lightning) for Mori who sent both the 6/1 and his Guide to the red zone. The Leech traded for the [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) and Tagomori fell to 6. The Jund player cast [Sprouting Thrinax](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sprouting+Thrinax) to keep up with his opponent, but fell to 2 on an attack from [Hell's Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hell%27s+Thunder). [Blightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blightning) emptied Mori's hand of all but one card but he revealed that card to be [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) for the final points of damage and the players headed to the second game.

Katsuhiro Mori 1, Shou Tagomori 0

Though he was on the play, Katsuhiro Mori started the second game of his Semifinals match aggressively just the same with a [Hellspark Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellspark+Elemental). He unearthed it a turn later and played [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks) to attack for 5, and had knocked his opponent to 12 on the third turn. Shou Tagomori fired back with a [Bloodbraid Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodbraid+Elf) that netted him a free [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt). A turn later he activated a [Raging Ravine](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raging+Ravine) and attempted to send it to the red zone but his opponent had a trump: activating [Arid Mesa](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arid+Mesa) for a [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain) then casting two copies of [Searing Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze). Because of the fetch-land Mori was able to trigger landfall and dropped his opponent to 6.

Tagomori's total Semifinals time had taken under ten minutes and the second game was looking as poor as the first. He tried to get some action going, but his opponent revealed a [Burst Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burst+Lightning) with kicker and [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) to finish him off and Shou was down two games.

Katsuhiro Mori 2, Shou Tagomori 0

[Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) hit on the first turn for Katsuhiro Mori, then was followed by a twin on his second turn. He also had [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks) to pump that copy, but Shou was ready with [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) to kill the 4/2 and fell to 16. The Jund player then cast [Sprouting Thrinax](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sprouting+Thrinax) to help stabilize and his opponent was clearly not pleased about the 3/3 hitting the battlefield.

Considering his options Mori cast [Hellspark Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellspark+Elemental), then sent his team sideways. The Thrinax traded for the Hellspark but the Guide managed to connect. Tagomori replaced his first Thrinax with a second and that traded with the unearth from Hellspark. He then cast [Putrid Leech](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrid+Leech) and with a [Doom Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doom+Blade) in hand and a [Raging Ravine](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raging+Ravine) able to block felt confident enough to start attacking for the first time in the match. His Saprolings and [Putrid Leech](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrid+Leech) turned sideways and Mori had to adjust his life total from his opponent's actions for the first time in the Semifinals.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/19/d5/19d5493e8bfea2df6751169279e6b5939042b0fe6ea8b97236a2a00ff080b997.jpg)*Shou Tagomori*

A [Siege-Gang Commander](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege-Gang+Commander) spelled almost certain doom for Mori and when Tagomori was able to begin attacking with his Goblins, Saprolings, Leech, and had mana up for Commander sacrifices Katsuhiro scooped.

Katsuhiro Mori 2, Shou Tagomori 1

The fourth game of the match kicked into high gear with Katsuhiro on the play. He opened on [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) and attacked for 2 but gave his opponent a free land in doing so. That meant Tagomori had to discard a [Putrid Leech](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrid+Leech) on his turn for having eight cards in hand but he got a pretty solid reprieve as Mori had no land drops for his second turn. [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) from the Jund deck took out [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) and Katsuhiro began sending burn spells at his opponent's face.

A [Scalding Tarn](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scalding+Tarn) gave Mori some hope, giving him his second land, and he used it on his opponent's turn to [Searing Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze) a [Sprouting Thrinax](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sprouting+Thrinax) Shou had cast as a blocker. That put Tagomori to 10 but gave him three 1/1 Saprolings with which to potentially go aggro with. A [Blightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blightning) from the Jund player knocked out [Hellspark Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellspark+Elemental) and a second [Searing Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze) from Mori's hand.

Katsuhiro drew, but didn't find a third land. He considered things for a moment, but then opted to concede. Just like that the match was headed to the fifth and deciding game!

Katsuhiro Mori 2, Shou Tagomori 2

The final game of the match started off in relatively slow fashion compared to the opening games with Mori's red deck failing to play any spells over the first three turns. That opened an opportunity for his opponent to cast [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek), seeing three copies of [Searing Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze) and opting to force one to his opponent's graveyard. Mori topdecked a [Hellspark Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellspark+Elemental) and cast it, but it died to [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) before being able to connect.

[Sprouting Thrinax](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sprouting+Thrinax) hit for Shou, but he took 3 from a [Quenchable Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quenchable+Fire), then 3 more for lack of access to blue mana. A [Verdant Catacombs](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Verdant+Catacombs) dropped him to 13 life and he needed to find a pretty aggressive clock to beat his opponent before he died to burn. He tried for a second [Sprouting Thrinax](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sprouting+Thrinax) and a [Putrid Leech](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrid+Leech) and the duo stuck, putting the onus on Katsuhiro to come up with 13 more points of burn to stay in things.

Mori unearthed his [Hellspark Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellspark+Elemental) and played [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks) to make it 5/1. He sent the creature sideways and Tagomori blocked with both a Thrinax and [Putrid Leech](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrid+Leech). Pre-combat Mori cast [Searing Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze) to take out the Leech and managed to drop his opponent to 7 with the attack. Unfortunately for the former champ, his opponent had [Siege-Gang Commander](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege-Gang+Commander) to help stabilize and kill him in one turn. It was all down to one final draw step for Katsuhiro Mori.

Mori untapped and played a [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain). He used [Searing Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze) to take out the Siege-Gang, then revealed [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) and [Burst Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burst+Lightning). Tagomori checked the life totals but knew it was too late; his opponent had done it. Katsuhiro Mori was on to the Semifinals.

## Playoff: Ryouichi Tamada vs. Shou Tagomori

*by Bill Stark*

When the Top 8 for the 2010 Japan National Championship was announced it was split into two halves, seasoned pros and relative newcomers. The seasoned pros sent half their number to the Finals but it was down to Ryouichi Tamada and Shou Tagomori to determine which amongst the newcomers would join their veteran countrymen on behalf of Japan at the 2010 World Championships in Chiba. Tagomori's Jund deck was definitely not the favorite as it had already lost to a red deck on the day, which was exactly what Ryouichi Tamada was playing.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9d/b6/9db6efabea2ccb047dffda15b7da13f97f428dcf61e4352dcc0d372ab77fd4a6.jpg)*Ryouichi Tamada*

Right on schedule [Hellspark Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellspark+Elemental) hit for Ryouichi on the second turn, but when he unearthed it and targeted it with [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks) on the third turn Shou cast [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) to blow it up. The Jund player then cast a [Sprouting Thrinax](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sprouting+Thrinax) to help him stabilize, sacrificing it to block a second Hellspark. Tamada winced as he discarded two [Earthquake](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Earthquake)s to a [Blightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blightning) while taking 3 damage from Saprolings. He sent a [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) straight to Tagomori's head at the end of his turn.

A third copy of [Earthquake](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Earthquake) cleared the table of Saprolings, but a second [Blightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blightning) cleared Tamada's hand of spells. He unearthed a [Hellspark Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellspark+Elemental) to attack for 3 but lost the creature to a [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt). The game was tied at 9 life each, and [Bloodbraid Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodbraid+Elf) hit for Tagomori though the cascade whiffed. The 3/2 rumbled in to make it 9-6 in favor of Jund. A turn later [Raging Ravine](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raging+Ravine) joined the party and the players headed to the second game with Shou Tagomori's Jund deck in the lead.

Shou Tagomori 1, Ryouichi Tamada 0

A [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) on the first turn for Ryouichi Tamada was killed by [Forked Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forked+Bolt) from Shou Tagomori. A second copy of the 2/2 stuck, and was soon joined by [Hellspark Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellspark+Elemental). That left Ryouichi feeling confident enough to begin sending burn at his opponent's head which he did in the form of an unkicked [Burst Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burst+Lightning). A [Sprouting Thrinax](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sprouting+Thrinax) from Shou sacrificed itself to absorb an attack from the [Hellspark Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellspark+Elemental) unearthed but [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks) made sure some of the trampler's damage made it through.

Tagomori tried to stabilize but it was too little too late. His opponent unloaded with burn spells at the end of his turn, then untapped and revealed [Earthquake](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Earthquake) for enough to even the match count; the game had whizzed by breathtakingly fast.

Shou Tagomori 1, Ryouichi Tamada 1

The third game of the match started very well for Ryouichi Tamada's red deck as he came right out of the gates with [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide). Shou had the [Forked Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forked+Bolt) to kill it for the second game in a row, but a second copy hit for Tamada a turn later. Tagomori answered right back with a second [Forked Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forked+Bolt), but both missed a land drop.

Shou cast [Sprouting Thrinax](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sprouting+Thrinax) but lost the 3/3 to a [Searing Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze) a turn later and fell to 13 life. That became 12 as he used a [Verdant Catacombs](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Verdant+Catacombs) to find a [Swamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swamp) but doing so enabled him to cast [Sprouting Thrinax](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sprouting+Thrinax) number two. Tagomori was ready for that play though, using a second [Searing Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze) to take out the Thrinax then having exactly enough mana left over to [Earthquake](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Earthquake) for 1 to clear the board of Saprolings and leave Tagomori on 8. [Blightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blightning) wiped out the red player's hand, but he ripped [Hellspark Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellspark+Elemental) and crashed in for 3.

After Shou had simply drawn and said go, Ryouichi topdecked a [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain), which he played, then opted to pass rather than attack with an unearthed [Hellspark Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellspark+Elemental). When he decided to make exactly that play the following turn, he only got 1 damage in due to the fact Tagomori had cast [Siege-Gang Commander](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege-Gang+Commander) to fetch up a team of blockers. A [Putrid Leech](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrid+Leech) soon joined his team and he left his opponent with just one turn to live.

Unfortunately for Shou Tagomori that turn was all his opponent needed as Ryouichi had [Burst Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burst+Lightning) for exactly lethal damage to send the match to the fourth game with the red mage in the lead.

Ryouichi Tamada 2, Shou Tagomori 1

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d3/9a/d39ad2c2fc64fb5bfff7a82643c469674aba049d5ae862ed8280e4f1fa15e170.jpg)*Shou Tagomori*

With his tournament life on the line, Shou Tagomori agonized over his opening hand, carefully examining every card in it to determine if he could afford to keep it or not. The verdict? No he could not and the grip went back to his library for six. Ryouichi's red deck was happy to stay on seven, and they got underway with Tagomori keeping after his mulligan.

[Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek) for Shou revealed Ryouichi to be holding all lands except for a [Hellspark Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellspark+Elemental) and a [Flame Slash](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flame+Slash). It was a weak hand, made weaker as the Hellspark went to the graveyard. A second [Inquisition](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition) from Tagomori nabbed the [Flame Slash](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flame+Slash) too and left his opponent in a very poor position indeed.

[Sprouting Thrinax](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sprouting+Thrinax) hit the table for Shou while Tamada's red deck delivered up land after land to his draw steps. Before long [Lavaclaw Reaches](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lavaclaw+Reaches) and the Thrinax were pressuring Ryouchi's life, swinging for 6 while he was devoid of any action. A [Burst Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burst+Lightning) gave him a removal spell with which he could target the creature-land to buy himself some time but a red deck burning an opponent's creatures to stay alive was rarely a good sign for the red player.

[Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) pumped by [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks) attempted to attack for 4, but was handled by [Terminate](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terminate). The [Sprouting Thrinax](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sprouting+Thrinax) continued crashing in, and got some help from a [Blightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blightning) that emptied much of Ryouchi's hand. When the red player didn't draw a solution to [Sprouting Thrinax](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sprouting+Thrinax) on the top of his deck the players headed to the last game of the match.

Ryouichi Tamada 2, Shou Tagomori 2

It was all down to one final game, and Ryouichi Tamada's deck made the most of it giving him a [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) on his first turn and pumping it with a [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks) on the second. Shou Tagomori worked to come up with a solution to the 2/2 but took some time to cast [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek). The discard spell binned a [Staggershock](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Staggershock) from his opponent's hand but revealed two copies of [Searing Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze) waiting in the wings; [Forked Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forked+Bolt) then took out the Guide.

A [Blightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blightning) nabbed one [Searing Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze) and a land from Tamada's hand, but Shou found himself stuck on three lands. A second [Blightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blightning) got most of the rest of the cards his opponent was holding and though he was slightly mana screwed Shou Tagomori looked to be in good shape against what was supposed to be a bad matchup. He ripped a [Rootbound Crag](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rootbound+Crag) and cast [Sprouting Thrinax](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sprouting+Thrinax). Ryouichi played a [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks) with nothing to pump, then cast [Searing Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze) to blow up the 3/3 Lizard.

A [Bloodbraid Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodbraid+Elf) managed to cascade into another discard spell, [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek), and Ryouichi was under intense pressure to topdeck like a fiend. His opponent had attackers, his life total was unstable as a result of the [Blightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blightning)s, and he had an empty hand. When Tagomori sent his team sideways, Ryouichi extended his hand in defeat.

Shou Tagomori will join the 2010 Japan National Team!

Shou Tagomori 3, Ryouchi Tamada 2

## Finals: Tsuyoshi Ikeda vs. Katsuhiro Mori

*by Bill Stark*

The title of 2010 Japanese National Champion was down to one final match. The players battling? Masters Katsuhiro Mori and Tsuyoshi Ikeda. Both were well documented successes at the game playing in just one of many Top 8s over the course of their careers, but there's something extra special about playing to represent the champion of your home country. For Mori it would be his second turn with the title, but he would have a tough road to pull that off with his red deck. After all, Ikeda had already beaten one red opponent on his way to the final match of the day.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/99/b5/99b56e2d4a9d7935d12c61858426380b0e5325a74380ebbefae6947cbd6c2365.jpg)*Tsuyoshi Ikeda*

Tsuyoshi's blue-red-white deck didn't offer up the goods for his first hand and he opted to mulligan to six. He took 2 damage on his first turn as Katsuhiro cast [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide), but he undid half the attack by playing [Sejiri Refuge](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sejiri+Refuge) as his first land of the match. A turn later a second [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) allowed Mori to smash for 4, a very promising start for the former champion.

Ikeda worked to dig himself out of the early hole he was in and cast [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens). The 0/4 absorbed one of the Guides in the following combat step and Katsuhiro cast a [Kargan Dragonlord](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kargan+Dragonlord) after attacking. Unfortunately for Tsuyoshi, his deck failed to give him a third land and he cast [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) in an attempt to find one. The play didn't work, and he passed with only two mana on the battlefield. Katsuhiro showed no mercy sending his team to the red zone and leveling his Dragonlord once post-combat. The red mage was slightly land screwed himself, also with only two on the table and one of those had been transformed into a relatively useless [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island).

A third land appeared for Tsuyoshi who used it to cast [Oblivion Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Ring) on his opponent's Dragonlord. He was still taking hits from the [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide)s but had managed to snap out of his mana screw. Mori, unfortunately, was still struggling. He finally found a third land and began doing calculations in his head. He cast [Hell's Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hell%27s+Thunder) and sent it to the red zone. The 4/4 connected and dropped Ikeda to 2. That was low enough to make any of a dozen cards from his opponent lethal, and when Mori revealed a land and [Searing Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze) targeting his opponent's [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) Ikeda conceded the first game.

Katsuhiro Mori 1, Tsuyoshi Ikeda 0

[Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) made an appearance on the first turn of the game yet again for Katsuhiro Mori but the little 2/2 was stopped in its tracks by [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) from Tsuyoshi Ikeda. That forced Mori to use [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) after blockers to clear the path for his Goblin. Ikeda missed his third land drop and shipped the turn.

A [Ball Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ball+Lightning) from Mori met its end by way of [Celestial Purge](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Celestial+Purge), but Katsuhiro got a small victory when his [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) failed to reveal a third land waiting for Ikeda on top of his deck. Instead the control player drew [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens), casting it and cantripping into his third land. Mori drew and pumped his fist, clearly excited by his draw. He played a [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain), cast [Flame Slash](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flame+Slash) to take out the Wall, then cast a second [Ball Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ball+Lightning) and crashed in for 8. It halved his opponent's life total putting the scores 19-8 in Katsuhiro's favor.

[Hell's Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hell%27s+Thunder) met [Flashfreeze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flashfreeze) and the [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) bit the dust to [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile), but still there was no fourth land for Tsuyoshi. He found a fake version in [Everflowing Chalice](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Everflowing+Chalice) but fell to 4 after an attack from the unearthed [Hell's Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hell%27s+Thunder). The two settled in to a game of draw, go with Katsuhiro building up a hand full of instant burn spells while Tsuyoshi bluffed a strong hand of counters and spot removal. Mori cast a [Kargan Dragonlord](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kargan+Dragonlord) and leveled it twice but lost it on Ikeda's turn to [Journey to Nowhere](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Journey+to+Nowhere). Ikeda tapped out to cast [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) but topdecked a land and played it.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/89/ef/89efdefe8ef278c469c39b8e2ed34b095baa86a47cda54b5821e6965d2764c1a.jpg)*Katsuhiro Mori*

Mori smelled blood in the water and went for it. He cast [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) at the end of Tsuyoshi's turn and it resolved, dropping Ikeda to 1. He untapped and drew, then revealed [Searing Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze). Even without a land drop for the turn it was enough for lethal and the players headed to the third game.

Katsuhiro Mori 2, Tsuyoshi Ikeda 0

For the third game of the match Katsuhiro Mori had a [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) on his first turn of the game while his opponent had but a land. Ikeda accelerated on turn two with an [Everflowing Chalice](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Everflowing+Chalice) but Mori absolutely unloaded. He played [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks) to pump his [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide), then cast a second copy of the 2/2 allowing him to drop Tsuyoshi to just 12 life on the second turn. Was the last game of the Finals going to be over by turn four?

Ikeda went into the tank on his turn to come up with the best plan of action. He was in very hot water and needed to execute on some damage control quickly but what was the best course of action? He only had access to a single white mana meaning he couldn't cast [Oblivion Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Ring) and have mana up for [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile), nor could he cast the [Elspeth, Knight-Errant](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Knight-Errant) in his hand. He tapped three mana, leaving [Glacial Fortress](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glacial+Fortress) up, and cast [Mind Spring](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind+Spring) to draw a card. He then passed the turn, ready to pull the trigger on his removal spell if he needed to.

Mori could taste victory, but he didn't want to risk letting it slip from his grasp by making a small mistake. He did some math, then turned his [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide)s sideways. One was exiled via his opponent's pseudo [Swords to Plowshares](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swords+to+Plowshares) and post-combat he played an [Arid Mesa](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arid+Mesa) before passing the turn. Tsuyoshi cast [Oblivion Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Ring) to take out the second Guide and used a [Tectonic Edge](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tectonic+Edge) to knock out a [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks) from his opponent. Mori untapped and cast [Hell's Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hell%27s+Thunder), playing a [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks) to pump it to 6/4. That dropped Ikeda to 4, and he had one foot in the grave.

He drew for his turn and failed, yet again, to find a second source of white. Mori unearthed [Hell's Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hell%27s+Thunder) and moved it to attack but Ikeda revealed [Flashfreeze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flashfreeze) to counter. Mori passed the turn, but the judges swooped in to notify the players unearth is an activated ability, not a casting of the spell in the graveyard. That meant [Flashfreeze](https://web.archive.org/web/20220706092948/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flashfreeze) couldn't stop it and Mori had actually been able to attack for lethal; Tsuyoshi extended his hand in defeat.

Katsuhiro Mori is the 2010 Japanese National Champion!

Katsuhiro 3, Tsuyoshi Ikeda 0
