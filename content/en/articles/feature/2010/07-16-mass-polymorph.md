Looking for a fun way to use [Mass Polymorph](https://web.archive.org/web/20100716000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mass+Polymorph)? The powerful new Magic 2011 card was the perfect win condition for Zac Hill's white-blue deck at the Champion Challenge in Atlanta, Georgia. Using [Martial Coup](https://web.archive.org/web/20100716000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Martial+Coup) and [Elspeth, Knight-Errant](https://web.archive.org/web/20100716000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Knight-Errant), he could generate a few Soldier tokens. That meant he could then cast the [Mass Polymorph](https://web.archive.org/web/20100716000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mass+Polymorph) to hunt up [Iona, Shield of Emeria](https://web.archive.org/web/20100716000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Iona%2C+Shield+of+Emeria), [Stormtide Leviathan](https://web.archive.org/web/20100716000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormtide+Leviathan), and [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20100716000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Aeons+Torn). From there, winning was no doubt easy!
