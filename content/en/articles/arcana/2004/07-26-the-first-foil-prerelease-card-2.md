[Lightning Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20040726000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Dragon) was the first widely-available foil premium card. It was the card given away at the *Urza's Saga* prerelease. Foil premium cards began showing up in regular booster backs in the next set, *Urza's Legacy*, and have been a part of **Magic** ever since.
