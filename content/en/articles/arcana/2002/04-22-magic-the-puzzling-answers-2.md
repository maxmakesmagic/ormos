Here are the answers to "Build Me Up, Buttercup," Mark's **Magic**: The Puzzling from last Thursday. You can go back and look at the puzzle [here](/en/articles/feature/2002/04-18-magic-the-puzzling).

Here are the answers written out:

*   Willow Elf + Lightning Reflexes = Elvish Archers
*   Merfolk of the Pearl Trident + Armor of Faith + Flight + Unholy Strength + Enfeeblement = Tower Drake
*   Mons's Goblin Raiders + Divine Transformation + Giant Strength = Trained Orgg
*   Muck Rats + Maniacal Rage + Sicken = Spineless Thug
*   Eager Cadet + Firebreathing + Holy Strength = Angelfire Crusader
