### Japan

[プロツアー・ロンドン予選　日本語の案内](/ja/events/2005/london05/pro-tourlondon-05-qualifiers)

### Entry Fee

The entry fee for all Pro Tour—London Qualifier tournaments in Japan is ¥3,500.

### Format

*   The format for all Pro Tour—London Qualifiers tournaments is *Kamigawa* Block Sealed Deck, with a Top 8 Booster Draft. For information on this format, please refer to the DCITM Universal Tournament Rule and **Magic** Floor Rules documents on the [DCI Document Center](http://www.thedci.com/docs).
*   1 *Champions of Kamigawa* Tournament Pack and 2 *Betrayers of Kamigawa* Booster Packs will be used for Sealed Deck. 2 *Champions of Kamigawa* and 1 *Betrayers of Kamigawa* Booster Packs will be used for drafting.
*   Deck lists are required.
*   If product is not stamped or preregistered, deck registration and deck swap will be performed for the Sealed Deck portion of the tournament.

### Tournament Structure

*   The structure for all Pro Tour—London Qualifier tournaments will be modified Swiss-style, with the Top 8 players playing in a single-elimination Booster Draft format for prizes and invitations.
*   Each round in the Swiss portion of the tournament will be 50 minutes.
*   The number of Swiss rounds will be determined based on attendance according to the following chart:

### Advancement

*   All players may participate in all Swiss rounds.
*   The top 8 players after the final Swiss round will advance to the Top 8 single-elimination finals.

### Top 8 Finals

*   The Top 8 matches at Pro Tour—London Qualifier tournaments will take place after a Booster Draft by the Top 8.
*   All Top 8 matches at Pro Tour—London Qualifier tournaments will be best 2 of 3.
*   Top 8 matches will have no time limit.
*   The Top 8 players will be seated randomly at the draft table immediately propr to drafting. The player in seat 1 will open the first booster.
*   The Top 8 bracket will be determined using this random seating.
*   When necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will be used to determine final order in the standings.

### Notes

*   All Pro Tour—London Qualifier tournaments are run at Rules Enforcement Level 3, and are 32K.
*   All Universal Tournament Rules and **Magic: The Gathering** Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this format document.
*   Players are responsible for bringing their own pen, paper, card sleeves, and appropriate counters.

### Invitations and Eligibility

Players already holding an invitation to Pro Tour—London are prohibited from participating in any Pro Tour—London Qualifier tournaments, with the exception of Grand Prix tournaments. Players may have earned invitations through cumulative past performance in the Pro Tour, winning a previous Qualifier or Grand Prix tournament, or by being an invited DCI player (valid upon the date of the official ratings invitation announcement for the appropriate Pro Tour). Please refer to the [Pro Tour—London Invitation List](/en/events/2005/london05/2005-pro-tourlondon#invites) for a list of qualified players.

### Prizes

*   Invitations to Pro Tour—London (exact number of invitations determined by tournament location).
*   The winning player of each Pro Tour—London Qualifier tournament will receive a cash prize of US $500. (Cash prize awards will be distributed to all winners at the end of the round.)
*   Product prizes to be determined by the organizer.

### Registration Information

Players who want more specific local tournament or registration information should contact their local organizer.
