# Which is the best deck without Dragonlord Ojutai or Deathmist Raptor?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_QQShuheiNakamura.jpg)  
**Hall of Famer, Shuhei Nakamura:** Mono-green Devotion without [Deathmist Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathmist+Raptor).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_QQ-ShotaYasooka.jpg)  
**Pro Tour *Dragons of Tarkir* finalist, Shouta Yasooka:** Atarka Red, the deck that Martin Dang played to beat me.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_QQNamSungWook.jpg)  
**Platinum Pro & Pro Tour *Journey into Nyx* finalist, Nam Sung Wook:** Abzan Aggro. It is difficult to metagame against it and every matchup is 50/50 at worst.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_QQKuoTzuChing.jpg)  
**11 Grand Prix Top 8s, Tzu Ching Kuo:** Abzan Control. The decks with [Dragonlord Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai) and [Deathmist Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathmist+Raptor) are not super powerful in the first place.
