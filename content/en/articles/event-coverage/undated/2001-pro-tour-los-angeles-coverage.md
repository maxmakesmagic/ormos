## 2001 Pro Tour Los Angeles

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c9/6c/c96c69dfac717ce405d52b47b9cb11c56c6027d087fa01cc07a13effa8fd0699.gif)![](https://i.ormos.uk/7c/c9/7cc9e2a4d806aed2bbb0f03c9390073ae77a668d1338c391a605e08cb8319987.jpg)

Mike Pustilnik has won Pro Tour Los Angeles, defeating Kamiel Cornelissen, 3-2 in the finals. This is Pustilnik's first Pro Tour victory. Cornelissen has made the finals in the last two Pro Tours (one constructed, one limited).
