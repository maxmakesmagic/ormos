### Give us your feedback

Congratulations MagicTheGathering.com on your first month on the web! Since the site launched at the beginning of 2002 we've seen the Orb of Insight receive close to a quarter of a million requests in a single day, as well as discovered that Ask Wizards is one of the most read sections of the new site. In our first month alone we posted over 60 new tidbits and articles to help you understand and enjoy what makes **Magic: The Gathering** such a great game. And guess what? Due to your support for all of this great content, the powers at be have asked us to do even more!!

So before we start scrambling around for more goodies to show you - and before we start coding the next great activity for you to hammer on - we'd like to get your feedback on what you would like to see from this website. Our goal is to create a site where you have a voice in what you would like to see. That's why we've created a new monthly survey to let you tell us what we are doing right and what we are doing wrong. 

This monthly survey will ask you to rate the various sections of MagicTheGathering.com and tell us which types of content you would like to see more of... and perhaps which types of content we should reconsider. Again, this is your chance to tell us what you want.

Seeing a theme yet?

We want to offer many thanks to all the people who have written in to tell us how much they like the new site, and please, keep the letters coming. It is only through your help and feedback that we can make this site have all the features and content you desire.

Please take a few moments and give us your feedback and let us know what you think of MagicTheGathering.com!
