Monday, June 27 9:00 am PDT, *Saviors of Kamigawa* goes on sale in the MTG:O store.

NOTE: *Saviors of Kamigawa* becomes legal in ALL Constructed formats the day it goes on sale (June 27). All casual rooms and constructed queues will use *Saviors of Kamigawa* starting on June 27. Constructed Premier events will not run until July 5 and will use *Saviors of Kamigawa* starting on that date.

*Saviors of Kamigawa* Release events begin Thursday, June 30 and will have 4 components:

Release Leagues  
 Draft Queues  
 Premier Event Flights (2x and 4x Prizes)  
 Release Championship

Note that these are release events. Per the [*Unhinged* basic lands promotion](/en/articles/feature/2005/04-02-unhingedtm-basic-land-promotion-in-magic-online) fact sheet, no *Unhinged* basic lands will be given away during these events.

***Saviors of Kamigawa* Release Leagues**

SIGNUP: June 30, 9:00am (or immediately following the scheduled downtime, whichever is later) – July 5, 9:00am (the final 1 week, 2x league will start no later than 9AM on July 5). Release Leagues will have a maximum of 256 players each and will auto-spawn (as soon as a league reaches 256 players, another will begin registration).

LEAGUE DURATION: 1 week

PRODUCT: CHK Tournament Pack and 3 SOK Booster packs.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 2x prizes, Participation Avatar: Oni of Wild Places; Records of 5-0 get Prize Avatar: Sakashima, the Impostor. 

Avatars will be distributed Approx. 3 weeks after the last league concludes

***Saviors of Kamigawa* Draft Queues**

QUEUES BEGIN: June 30, 11:00 am (or 2 hours after the scheduled downtime, whichever is later). SOK SOK SOK queues will end on Tuesday, July 5 at 9:00 am

FORMATS: Booster Draft

PRODUCT: 3 SOK Boosters or 1 CHK, 1 BOK, and 1 SOK Boosters.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 4-3-2-2, 8-4

***Saviors of Kamigawa* Premier Events (2x Flights)**

DURATION: June 30 3:00 pm (or 6 hours after the scheduled downtime, whichever is later) – July 5 9:00 am (last starts at 9am). 2x Flights will have 32 players per flight and will start every hour, on the hour. Each flight will have a minimum of 24 players, maximum of 32 players.

PRODUCT: CHK Tournament Pack + 3 SOK Boosters Sealed deck, top 8 booster draft. The booster draft format will be 1 CHK, 1 BOK, and 1 SOK Boosters.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 2x Prizes, Top 8

### Important information regarding system crashes and refunds for Premier Events

If during your event the system crashes, 1 of 3 things will happen:

1.  The system will come right back up. In this case:

    *   Events will continue and additional time will be given to allow players to finish the event
    *   No Compensation will be given

2.  The system cannot recover the event and "finishes" (prematurely ends) the event, or a WOTC Tournament Coordinator must "finish" (prematurely end) the event.

    *   We will accept the prize payouts "as is" - This means that whatever prize payouts and invites the system awards, we will not change what the system does.
    *   No changes will be made to these events from what the system automatically pays out as prizes or invitations

    If the event is in TOP 8:

    *   No product or tickets will be refunded
    *   All players keep the product used to enter the event as well as the drafted cards from the TOP 8 draft.

    If the event is still in Swiss Rounds:

    *   Players can [request a refund through Customer Service](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=161).
    *   We will refund your Product and tickets.

3.  The event is cleared or cancelled by the system. If an event is cancelled this means the event can no longer be viewed in the premier event listings.

    *   The system will automatically refund your tickets.
    *   Players can [request a refund through Customer Service](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=161).
    *   We will refund your Product.
