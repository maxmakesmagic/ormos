To celebrate Halloween, this week we have three spooky desktop wallpapers. Alan Pollack's **[Urborg Skeleton](https://web.archive.org/web/20031031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Urborg+Skeleton)** from *Invasion*, Larry Elmore's **[Childhood Horror](https://web.archive.org/web/20031031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Childhood+Horror)** from *Odyssey*, and Puddnhead's **[Nightmare Lash](https://web.archive.org/web/20031031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightmare+Lash)** from *Mirrodin*. Happy Halloween!
