Very rarely, a submitted piece of art is "vast" enough that it provides enough material for *two* cards!

When *Odyssey* was being laid out, liberal art-shuffling left Gallantry "out in the cold" with no acceptable art at all. To solve the problem, the painting for Repentant Vampire was split in half, and the woman was used for Gallantry.

You can see the entire original painting below.
