Pyromancer Ascension–based combo decks are back in Standard! User mGerken took this list to the Top 4 of a recent Pro Tour Qualifier on [**Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20110816030145/http://www.wizards.com/magic/Digital/MagicOnline.aspx?). The deck works to search up Pyromancer Ascension, then build up counters on the enchantment. From there, a bevy of burn spells are copied by the Ascension to finish off opponents. Mmmmm... crispy.
