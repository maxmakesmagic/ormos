Cards like Clockspinning, Paradox Haze, Dust of Moments, and Rift Elemental are great cards in the *Time Spiral* Block for manipulating time counters. And there are plenty of ways to abuse them: speed up your suspend spells, slow down the vanishing of your permanents or speed it up, keep bad things out of play or hasten their appearance.
