Thought Equipment decks were gone with the banning of Stoneforge Mystic? You'll need to think again, friends, as Caleb Durward proves quite handily. He made the Top 8 of a Pro Tour Philadelphia Qualifier in Columbus, Ohio with a whopping FIFTEEN Equipment! Puresteel Paladin is his engine, and Kor Duelist and Trinket Mage provide plentiful support in putting the hurt on his opponents. Living weapons like Flayer Husk give Caleb's deck more creatures with which to maneuver, a clever application of the ability that allows him to cheat his creature count in favor of more Equipment. Neat!
