Welcome to the *Time Spiral* edition of the [Lexicon](https://web.archive.org/web/20081002181222/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/fullarchive), in which we explore the meanings of some of the more obscure words we use in the names of those cards. While many offbeat names in **Magic** (like “gnarr”) are simply made up, we often dig around dictionaries for real little-used and archaic words for inspiration.

* * *

 ![Blazing Blade Askari](https://i.ormos.uk/95/b6/95b669c3459812340d69e4b06110c10fccf987b109b0d0594020dea30dbf6dba.jpg) 

**as•ka•ri**  
 *noun* 

1.  Arabic, Persian and Swahili word meaning “soldier.”
2.  A native African police officer or soldier, esp. one serving a colonial administration.

  

* * *

 ![Basal Sliver](https://i.ormos.uk/0d/ae/0daeb02021f24ab962c055d170e3be3fdc03ad4511d279086d7a1b4504dd1655.jpg) 

**ba•sal**  
 *adjective* 

1.  Of, at, or forming the base.
2.  Forming a basis; fundamental; basic.
3.  *Physiology*.

    1.  Indicating a standard low level of activity of an organism, as during total rest.
    2.  Of an amount required to maintain this level.

  

* * *

 ![Conflagrate](https://i.ormos.uk/83/c2/83c2aaaaf28e308c807b5c72b9d7277592f31a42af1b5335ec8148d1956f5d43.jpg) 

**con•fla•grate**  
 *verb* 

1.  Cause to start burning.
2.  Start to burn or burst into flames.

  

* * *

 ![Errant Doomsayers](https://i.ormos.uk/ac/4a/ac4a7a40d14bb70f17ca4ca417cefe1a1747c1f4152ccd0f3aa6b64fea095532.jpg) 

**doom•say•er**  
 *noun* 

1.  A person who predicts impending misfortune or disaster.

  

* * *

 ![Sengir Nosferatu](https://i.ormos.uk/36/47/364796425de61730756450358eb7367f9bae000e44fbd9690c502568c523817b.jpg) 

**nos•fer•a•tu**  
 *noun* 

1.  A hideously ugly vampire.

  

* * *

 ![Pentarch Paladin](https://i.ormos.uk/82/61/82618e1e15b0d137deb252822e22c70942e0c792b02525f8f55a237726f288d9.jpg) 

**pen•tarch**  
 *noun* 

1.  Member of a pentarchy.

pentarchy  
*noun*

1.  a government by five persons.
2.  a governing body of five persons.
3.  a federation of five nations, each under its own government or ruler.

  

* * *

 ![Penumbra Spider](https://i.ormos.uk/a7/66/a766a7aab635bbe8dda3c477979b3e7b18ed5554e2887202a08f5c361b114287.jpg) 

**pe•num•bra**  
 *noun* 

1.  *Astronomy*.
    1.  The partial or imperfect shadow outside the complete shadow of an opaque body, as a planet, where the light from the source of illumination is only partly cut off.
    2.  The grayish marginal portion of a sunspot.
2.  A shadowy, indefinite, or marginal area.

  

* * *

 ![Phthisis](https://i.ormos.uk/cb/f5/cbf5679309974c7f28b024e5a4c5ac35b1ba33542e88d12689315428ed1a7e5d.jpg) 

**phthi•sis**  
 *noun* 

1.  A wasting away.
2.  Pulmonary tuberculosis; consumption.

  

* * *

 ![Scion of the Ur-Dragon](https://i.ormos.uk/77/9b/779ba258087b2d0a767844e55c188efe09f46b135c3bc06702c872598befa9e9.jpg) 

**sci•on**  
 *noun* 

1.  A descendant or heir.
2.  A detached shoot or twig containing buds from a woody plant, used in grafting.

  

* * *

 ![Spinneret Sliver](http://www.wizards.com/global/images/magic/general/Spinneret_Sliver.jpg) 

**spin•ner•et**  
 *noun* 

1.  An organ or part by means of which a spider, insect larva, or the like spins a silky thread for its web or cocoon.
2.  A metal plate or cup with tiny holes through which a chemical solution is extruded to form continuous filaments, as of rayon, nylon, or polyester.

  

* * *

 ![Viscid Lemures](https://i.ormos.uk/41/82/4182ac392edf58221cc29d2591efe0631feca6a041e25a5dc4f731fc68a67e90.jpg) 

**vis•cid**  
 *adjective* 

1.  Having a glutinous consistency; sticky; adhesive; viscous.
2.  *Botany*. Covered by a sticky substance.
