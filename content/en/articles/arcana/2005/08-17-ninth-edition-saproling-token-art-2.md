As *Ninth Edition* appears in ***Magic** Online* on the 29th, you'll see brand new art for the tokens generated by a few of those new cards.

As you know, the amazing token-generating machine **[Verdant Force](https://web.archive.org/web/20050817000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Verdant+Force)** makes its Core Set debut in *Ninth*. 

Here's a sneak peek at the art of the new token art for the Saprolings it pumps out.
