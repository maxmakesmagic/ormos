Remember [Braincat](/en/articles/arcana/2008/03-04-braincat)? It was a playtest card that looked like this:

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/39/b6/39b6d34f575580b443f1e37a0f6a9457e077f84a85e85962e5a37686fc9db17c.jpg) 

  

That card ended up as Thoughtseize (it actually says "Brain*cut*", but everyone agrees that it looks more like a cat than a cut).

Now, we return to Braincat in a new feature called...

![Why R&D Isn't Responsible for the Art](https://i.ormos.uk/bd/6e/bd6e416ebc019d5f1ae976590e1a18d1982f05069d4c6121804420a6bf3ac474.jpg "Why R&D Isn't Responsible for the Art") 

  

R&D member Mons Johnsons recently needed four Thoughtseizes for a playtest deck, and kept the colloquial name "Braincat". And he drew charming pictures for the four cards, which tell a fascinating story:

![Braincat 1](https://i.ormos.uk/ad/1f/ad1fc4935ae95ff039fc9164d0f9bc7fcd1bd8ad6fc0a29f3f1bfd6d9241e53e.jpg "Braincat 1")
