It's late December, and here in the non-tropical portions of the Northern Hemisphere, that means winter! And that, in turn, means snow, holiday festivities, and gift giving! This year Zur the Enchanter is ready to play Santa Claus with this [Commander deck](/en/articles/feature/2010/12-02-magic-the-gathering-commander), bestowing presents of Steel of the Godhead and Cover of Winter on you and yours, while showering your opponents with the gifts of Pacifism and Recumbent Bliss. A snow theme, a Cleric tribal theme, and a few nods to holiday traditions round out the deck.

We'll be back with all-new articles on January 3 and *Mirrodin Besieged* previews soon after that. See you then!
