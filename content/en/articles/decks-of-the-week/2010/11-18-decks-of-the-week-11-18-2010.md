We're heading down the home stretch of the 2010 season, with just two Grand Prix and the 2010 World Championships left on the schedule. The final North America Grand Prix of the season hits [Nashville this weekend](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=mtg/event/grandprix/nashville10), and there's no better way to get a head start on your draft preparation than looking at the Top 8 *Scars of Mirrodin* Booster Draft deck lists from the current Pro Tour Qualifier season. For those looking farther down the road to Worlds, don't forget about the slew of **Magic Online** Standard decks to get a handle on that format!
