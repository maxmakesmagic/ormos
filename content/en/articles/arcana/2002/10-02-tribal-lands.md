When the *Onslaught* development process began, there were only three tribal lands in the set: [Goblin Burrows](https://web.archive.org/web/20021002000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Burrows), [Riptide Laboratory](https://web.archive.org/web/20021002000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Riptide+Laboratory), and [Wirewood Lodge](https://web.archive.org/web/20021002000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wirewood+Lodge). All three were rare. After another cycle of lands were cut from the set, R&D decided to create five more tribal lands to reinforce the importance of creature types in the set.

Now each major race has a corresponding land, some of which are rare and some of which are uncommon. Some are more powerful than others, so try them out and see which ones you like.

*   [Goblin Burrows](https://web.archive.org/web/20021002000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Burrows) - Goblins
*   [Contested Cliffs](https://web.archive.org/web/20021002000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Contested+Cliffs) - Beasts
*   [Wirewood Lodge](https://web.archive.org/web/20021002000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wirewood+Lodge) - Elves
*   [Starlit Sanctum](https://web.archive.org/web/20021002000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Starlit+Sanctum) - Clerics
*   [Daru Encampment](https://web.archive.org/web/20021002000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daru+Encampment) - Soldiers
*   [Unholy Grotto](https://web.archive.org/web/20021002000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unholy+Grotto) - Zombies
*   [Seaside Haven](https://web.archive.org/web/20021002000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seaside+Haven) - Birds
*   [Riptide Laboratory](https://web.archive.org/web/20021002000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Riptide+Laboratory) - Wizards
