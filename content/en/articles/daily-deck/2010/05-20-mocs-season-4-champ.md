The fourth season of the **Magic Online** Championship Series has concluded, and Jabs was its champion! The digital player drafted this black-red deck, the same color combination used by Florian Koch to win Grand Prix–Lyon. Both tournaments featured the *Rise of the Eldrazi* booster draft format. For more information on the series and how you can become involved, visit the [FAQ](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/magic-online-championship-series).
