E*ventide* previews continue today with an update to the [*Eventide* minisite](https://web.archive.org/web/20080628092201/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/eventide/home). We've got a new puzzle and wallpaper, a story about Rhys's Reunion, and of coure the constantly updated Visual Spoiler with all the officially previewed cards so far. That includes cards that have been previewed in magazines, so there might be cards in there *right now* that you haven't seen. And it all still exists in *nine different languages*! Exciting!
