It's the eve of a new year and, just as enticing for *Magic* fans, the eve of the eve of the first [Friday Night *Magic*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-event-types-events-2) of 2015. That means a new set of FNM promos and, starting in February, a cool twist on how alternative art for the FNM promos is handled for a few months.

But we're getting ahead of ourselves. First, the known quantities.

# January

A new year makes a good time to add new art to your…Goblin deck? Kev Walker updates the most frenzied of Goblins. I wouldn't expect your opponents to do much blocking once you get your hands on a few of these Frenzied Goblins.

Frenzied Goblin Promo | Art by [Kev Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20141231000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5b%22Kev%20Walker%22%5d)

# February

We already previewed the Disdainful Stroke for February, complete with sweet alternative art. But what we didn't point out was that this was the start of a mini-pattern of alternative *Khans of Tarkir* art that shows a timeline where dragons rule the roost. This promo shows off a dragon in place of the Naga casting the spell. The flavor text takes on a whole new meaning when you imagine a dragon being quoted in an alternative timeline.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/0002_EN_FNM.jpg)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/cardart_disdainfulstroke_prm.jpg)

Disdainful Stroke Promo | Art by [Svetlin Velinov](https://web.archive.org/web/20141231000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5b%22Svetlin%20Velinov%22%5d)

# March

Which brings us to Hordeling Outburst, the March FNM promo card. This one even more closely mirrors the original (with art still by Zoltan Boros!), although now, instead of a trio of goblins emerging from shelter to rush into battle, they're fleeing a dragon who looks very, very hungry.
