### Gather Your Allies

![](https://www.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/promo/m12_logo_300.jpg)

Here’s an offer sure to appeal to every player!

The first 20 people that preorder or purchase a ***Magic: The Gathering** 2012 Core Set* booster box in your retail store, will get an Alternate Art Premium Chandra’s Phoenix promo card!\*

 ***Magic** 2012* is already available in your local store, so hurry, as this offer is limited to only the first 20 people per store who either preorder or purchase a full booster box! And when the cards are all gone, they’re gone.

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/11/fe/11fe0f7e6db0cc61266fd8b03af6126c956a8e4797bfe269286700dd98edd657.jpg) 

*   [List of participating stores (North America)](https://web.archive.org/web/20110727000000/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=mtg/daily/events/m12buyaboxpromolocations)

If you're in Europe, [click here](/en/articles/event-coverage/2011/07-27-magic-2012-buy-a-box-europe) for promotion information in your area.

\*While Supplies Last. Online orders not eligible.
