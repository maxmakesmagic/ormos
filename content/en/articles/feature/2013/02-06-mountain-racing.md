Another deck came out of the first week of *Gatecrash* Standard, this time from Japan's own Tomoharu Saito. Mono-Red decks have been popular over the last few weeks for their blazing fast speed and ability to race the decks with [Thragtusk](https://web.archive.org/web/20130206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thragtusk). Tomoharu has added [Flinthoof Boar](https://web.archive.org/web/20130206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flinthoof+Boar) (who gets a huge bonus from the newly released [Stomping Ground](https://web.archive.org/web/20130206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stomping+Ground)), and [Burning-Tree Emissary](https://web.archive.org/web/20130206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning-Tree+Emissary) for amazingly explosive starts, especially if you can chain multiple Emissarys into each other. 

![Boros Reckoner](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Boros+Reckoner)

This deck also features [Boros Reckoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20130206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Reckoner), a card that has been getting a lot more attention in the last week. While three-mana 3/3s don't do much by themselves, Reckoner's ability to punish people who damage him make him an ideal blocker or a nearly unstoppable attacker. This deck knows how to start the damage, and how to keep the pressure on. 

The sideboard comes with [Volcanic Strength](https://web.archive.org/web/20130206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Volcanic+Strength) for the mirror, [Flames of the Firebrand](https://web.archive.org/web/20130206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flames+of+the+Firebrand) for the popular Elf-heavy acceleration decks, [Reckless Waif](https://web.archive.org/web/20130206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reckless+Waif) for control strategies, and [Traitorous Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20130206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Traitorous+Blood) as an answer for would-be blockers like [Thragtusk](https://web.archive.org/web/20130206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thragtusk).
