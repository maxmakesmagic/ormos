Standard is the format for this season's PTQs and WMCQs. Now that *Dragon's Maze* is here, it will undoubtedly change up the Standard metagame… but the real question is how. Find out what Standard looks like before *Dragon's Maze* shakes it up (it releases today!) with the decklists linked to below! Once you have a handle on the new metagame, try your hand at a nearby PTQ, which you can find with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#theros)! Good luck; have fun!
