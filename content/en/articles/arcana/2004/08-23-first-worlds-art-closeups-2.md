In this week's feature article, Monty Ashley celebrates the ten-year anniversary of the [first World Championships](https://web.archive.org/web/20040823000000/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=). The finals match between Zak Dolan and Bertrand Lestree involved many cards familiar to modern players (Birds of Paradise, Icy Manipulator, Wrath of God) and some you may not have heard of -- at least in a long time.

Today's Magic Arcana tests your knowledge of old-timer **Magic** art. Each of these closeups are from cards used in the final match of the first World Championships. Click "See answer" to reveal each card.
