Starting this weekend at your *Hour of Devastation* Prerelease, you can pick up the new Planeswalker Decks featuring unique versions of Nicol Bolas and Nissa. The decks are meant for newer players, but they are fun for anyone to battle against one another.

You can check out the [exclusive cards found in these decks here](/en/articles/feature/2017/06-21-exclusive-cards-in-hour-of-devastation-planeswalker-decks). Enjoy!
