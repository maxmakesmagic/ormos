## August Path to Premier Play Prestige Begins Saturday

A new month means another chance for you to quickly jump into the upper echelons of competitive *Magic* players through the Premier Play path; it can lead you all the way to Arena Championship 4 and beyond.

This month's events feature the [Explorer format](https://web.archive.org/web/20230731000000/https://magic.wizards.com/formats/explorer)!

[![Qualifier Play-In logo](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/logo_play-in_left.png)](https://www.magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) 

**August 5, Best-of-One Qualifier Play-In**: Your first opportunity to earn an entry to the August Qualifier Weekend. Compete in Explorer matches, perform well, and get an invitation.

**August 11, Best-of-Three Qualifier Play-In**: Another way to get into the competitive track. Compete in Explorer matches for a crack at claiming an invitation to the Qualifier Weekend that happens this same weekend!

[![Qualifier Weekend logo](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/logo_qw_400.png)](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) 

**August 12, Qualifier Weekend**: The main event this August, you'll need an invitation to take part in this two-day competition in the Explorer format. Top players can earn invitations to join the very select club of [Arena Championship 4 invitees](https://magic.gg/standings/arena-championship-4) and a Pro Tour invitation.

It all begins this Saturday, August 5!

## What Is Rotation and Renewal?

![Graphical representation of sets that will be rotating in Standard and Alchemy](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/67ljephudutr.jpg) 

 [*Wilds of Eldraine* arrives September 8](https://web.archive.org/web/20230731000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/wilds-of-eldraine), and with it comes the yearly set rotation where older sets move out of the Standard format to make room new sets.

There are changes coming to Standard rotation starting this year—Standard will not rotate this year as we shift to rotating sets out every three years instead of every two, but Alchemy *will* rotate this year as *MTG Arena* enters its yearly Renewal.

Find out everything you need to know about Renewal—including gifts!—in the article [Renewal for *MTG Arena*'s 2023 Standard Rotation](https://web.archive.org/web/20230731000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/mtg-arena-renewal-rotation).

## Premier Play Schedule

All times listed are Pacific time (UTC-07:00).

### Qualifier Events

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events. Check out the standings to see [who's qualified for Arena Championship 4](https://magic.gg/standings/arena-championship-4).

Qualifier tokens are delivered in your *MTG Arena* inbox. Remember to claim them before the event starts!
