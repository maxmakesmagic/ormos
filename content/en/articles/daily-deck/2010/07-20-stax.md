Legacy is one of the most popular formats in **Magic**. In fact, a Grand Prix featuring the format was the largest **Magic** tournament ever held, with over 2,000 competitors showing up to play! STAX is an archetype from the format designed to lock a player out of lands while still having its own thanks to Crucible of Worlds. Check out this version by FiatLux featuring Mana Vortex and Smokestack to keep its opponent under the gun!
