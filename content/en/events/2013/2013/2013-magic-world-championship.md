The 2013 **Magic** World Championship showcases the best players in the world battling against one another for the title of World Champion. Sixteen players will be invited to compete across three formats for the title and $40,000 first prize, with a total cash purse exceeding $100,000. 

Follow all the World Championship talk on Twitter with the hashtag [#mtgchamp](http://twitter.com/#!/search/realtime/%23mtgchamp) and mark your calendars for three days of can't-miss live coverage of this unique event. 

**Note**: The players with the most Pro Points in Japan (Yuuya Watanabe), North America (Josh Utter-Leyton), and Europe (Stanislav Cifka) earned invitations in other categories. By rule, these regional invitations become at-large invitations given to players with the most Pro Points who are not otherwise invited.
