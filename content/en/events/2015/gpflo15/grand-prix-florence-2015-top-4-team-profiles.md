After fourteen rounds the original field of 1,296 players had been reduced to a mere twelve. They defeated all comers and sat down in the feature match area to finally do some drafting. Time to learn more about them!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpflo15/t4_anteri_royde_sajgalik.jpg)  
*Left to right: Fabrizio A*nteri, *Daniel Royde and Eduardo Sajgalik*

**Name:** Daniel Royde

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Software developer

**Hometown:** London, UK

**Your team's record on Day 1:** 7-1-1

**Your personal record on Day 1:** Don't know.

**Your team's record on Day 2:** 4-1

**Your personal record on Day 2:** Don't know.

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

I won Grand Prix London in 2011 as well as the the UK Nationals in 2011; I came in 17th at Pro Tour Return to Ravnica in Seattle in 2012.

**Who are your teammates, and why did you play with them?**

Eduardo—we always team up together. Fabrizio—the best player we could convince to team with us.

**What color combination did you play in your first Sealed Deck, and what was your most important card?**

Green-white. Anafenza, Kin-Tree Spirit—a 2/2 for WW that I never played.

**What color combination did you play in your second Sealed Deck, and what was your most important card?**

Black-white. [Citadel Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Citadel+Siege).

**Hometown:** Reading, UK

**Who are your teammates, and why did you play with them?**

Daniel Royde—he's a good old friend and a long-time teammate. Fabrizio Anteri—he currently is the best UK player.

Red-black. [Temur Battle Rage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temur+Battle+Rage) and [Butcher's Glee](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Butcher%27s+Glee).

**Name:** Fabrizio Anteri

**Age:** 25

**Occupation:** Magic player (kind of)

**Hometown:** London, UK

**Your team's record on Day 1:** 7-1-1

**Your personal record on Day 1:** 9-0

**Your team's record on Day 2:** 4-1

**Your personal record on Day 2:** 2-3

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

I won some Grand Prix (Grand Prix Manchester 2014 and Grand Prix Warsaw 2014) last May.

**Who are your teammates, and why did you play with them?**

Ed and Dan. They pointed out we would be three English National champions after we had already teamed up. I didn't have time to change my mind at that point.

**What color combination did you play in your first Sealed Deck, and what was your most important card?**

Blue-black. [Silumgar, the Drifting Death](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silumgar%2C+the+Drifting+Death), [Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang), [Crux of Fate](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crux+of+Fate), [Silumgar Assassin](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silumgar+Assassin), and [Foul Renewal](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foul+Renewal). Rares are good!

**What color combination did you play in your second Sealed Deck, and what was your most important card?**

Green/Black Rock. [Den Protector](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Den+Protector) and [Deathmist Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathmist+Raptor). I played the deck in Standard and liked it, so I decided to run it in Limited as well.
