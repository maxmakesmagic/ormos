The Pro Player of the Month is awarded by Pro Tour Historian Brian David-Marshall in his The Week That Was column. A list of candidates is posted in the first column of each month for public discussion and the winner is announced in the second column of the month. Follow the discussion on Twitter with the hasthag [**#MTGPoM**](https://twitter.com/search?q=%23mtgpom).
