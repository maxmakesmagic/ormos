## PRODUCT INFORMATION

*Portal Three Kingdoms* is a 180-card, starter-level set based on the famous Chinese epic of the Three Kingdoms. Renowned Chinese artists illustrated the entire set.

**Set Name:** *Portal Three Kingdoms*  
**Number of Cards:** 180

**Languages:** English, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, Japanese

**Design and Development:** Henry Stern
