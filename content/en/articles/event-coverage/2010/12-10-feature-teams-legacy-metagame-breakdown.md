Here's a quick look at the most-played Legacy decks during the National Team rounds of Worlds competition. 

**Survival of the Fittest - 12**  
 A green based deck that uses [Survival of the Fittest](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Survival+of+the+Fittest+) to search out silver bullets as well as power out multiple [Vengevine](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vengevine)s in a single turn thanks to [Basking Rootwalla](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basking+Rootwalla).

**Merfolk - 8**  
 The quartet of [Lord of Atlantis](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lord+of+Atlantis), [Merrow Reejerey](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merrow+Reejerey), [Merfolk Sovereign](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merfolk+Sovereign+) and [Coralhelm Commander](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Coralhelm+Commander+) backed by free counterspells like [Force of Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+of+Will+) and [Daze](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daze+) are swinging games in its favor. 

**Tendrils Combo - 6**  
 Cheap mana effects are plentiful in Legacy and when combined with the storm spell, [Tendrils of Agony](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tendrils+of+Agony), games could end in a single turn.

**Junk - 5**  
 When you combine green, white and black into one deck, you get the best creatures like [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf), [Knight of the Reliquary](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+the+Reliquary+) and [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant), paired with the best removal in the form of [Swords to Plowshares](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swords+to+Plowshares), [Vindicate](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vindicate+) and [Pernicious Deed](https://web.archive.org/web/20101210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pernicious+Deed).
