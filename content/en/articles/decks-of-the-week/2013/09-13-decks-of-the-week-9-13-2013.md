While players wait with bated breath for the release of *Theros*, the PTQ season for Pro Tour *Born of the Gods* is ramping up both on [**Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20140217125411/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Digital/MagicOnline.aspx?x=mtg/digital/magiconline/download) and in stores across the world! This PTQ season uses the Sealed format (switching to Draft for the Top 8) with ***Magic** 2014* boosters for a couple more weeks; then, beginning on September 27, PTQs will use boosters from *Theros* instead. Make sure to check out the [*Theros* Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20140609081522/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/theros/cig) for all the previewed cards and find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#bog). Good luck; have fun!
