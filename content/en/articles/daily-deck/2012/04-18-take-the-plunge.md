Modern is a format with plenty of highly aggressive decks, but this deck takes that moniker to a whole new level. **Magic Online** player Pureincognito has been playing a deck in Daily Events that can only be described as suicidal. Harkening back to the [Hatred-fueled Suicide Black days](/en/articles/feature/2002/03-12-deconstructing-suicide-black), Pureincognito has built a deck that goes all-in and purposely reduces its life total to a dangerously low amount to win the game.

Death's Shadow and Immolating Souleater are this deck's primary routes to victory. How do you make these two creatures win Modern games? By using an odd collection of effects! Tainted Strike and Assault Strobe let you kill your opponent in one shot provided you can pump either creature high enough. Removal spells, Apostle's Blessing, and Smoldering Spires help ensure your lethal creature gets through.

The Hatred-style spell that pulls the deck together? Plunge into Darkness! By paying plenty of life you make your Death's Shadow huge—while simultaneously finding whichever card you're missing in the process!

If you can't find Plunge, Hex Parasite and Immolating Souleater also fit the bill, allowing you to pay life in increments of 2 over and over. Phyrexian mana cards can also reduce your own life total to work toward that goal.

If you've been looking for a crazy Modern beatdown deck, this might just be exactly what you're looking for. An unblocked 1/1 Death's Shadow has never looked so dangerous!

Pureincognito's Suicide Rakdos
