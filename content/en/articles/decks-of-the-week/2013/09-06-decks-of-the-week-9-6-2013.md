Russian player Anatoly Chuhwichov navigated a tough Top 8 that included Hall of Famer Raphaël Lévy and GP powerhouse Martin Jůza to win [Grand Prix Prague](https://web.archive.org/web/20130906000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/anatoly-beats-them-black-and-blue-prague) last weekend. Congratulations, Anatoly! Meanwhile, here in Washington State, the **Magic Online** Community team [won back the Community Cup](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2013-magic-online-community-cup) etc. etc. congratulations Community team, moving on. Like GP Prague, this PTQ season uses the Limited formats of Sealed and Draft. For now, tournaments are using ***Magic** 2014* boosters; beginning on September 27, they will use boosters from *Theros*, so make sure to check in with the *Theros* [Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20140609081522/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/theros/cig) to know what to expect! Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#bog). Good luck; have fun!
