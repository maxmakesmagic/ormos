### FAT PACK

Includes a guide with pictures of all cards in the set, a card box with panoramic art, eight 15-card booster packs, 40 basic lands, a special-edition life counter, and excerpts from *Agents of Artifice*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f3/81/f381e3edb8ea55a826a3377c951b83200c51b295e0c85e9cbee7e8561c6cb9ec.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f1/fd/f1fd00c2319eeff176eb1a88b8210415549ec07b0297100f2a5f2aca12801d5b.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes a 41-card preconstructed deck, a rare foil card, a strategy insert, a learn-to-play insert, and one 15-card *Conflux* booster pack.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719045023/http://magic.wizards.com/node/957481)

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ac/dd/acdd04f333dfd1630ba4347b662cb7c2cb9c63ac07a412743ed8a21f9cd30060.png)*Conflux* is the second set in the Shards of Alara block. It’s a 145-card expansion set with randomly inserted premium cards.

**Set Name:** *Conflux*  
**Block:** Set 2 of 3 of the *Shards of Alara* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 145

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719045023/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/EN_MTGCON%20FAQ%20121608.doc)

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Bill Rose (lead), Mark Globus, Mark L. Gottlieb, and Kenneth Nagle

**Final Game Design and Development:** Mike Turian (lead), Graeme Hopkins, Erik Lauer, and Devin Low, with contributions from Mark L. Gottlieb
