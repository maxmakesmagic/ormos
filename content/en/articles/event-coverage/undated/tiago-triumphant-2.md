Behind a few beefy attackers in Winston Draft and the power of Hulk Flash in Vintage, Portugal's Tiago Chan defeated Canada's Rich Hoaen 2-1 to win the 2007 **Magic** Invitational at Spiel Essen in Germany.

Elected to attend as Europe's representative, Chan powered through 15 round-robin matches to make the finals, acing both the Auction of the People and Winston Draft formats. Hoaen, who got the North America slot when Mark Herberholz was unable to attend, matched Chan's record in the Auction and 3-0'd Choose Your Own Standard to make the finals.

Chan now gets to create his own **Magic** card, to be included in an upcoming set. Congratulations to Tiago Chan, the 2007 **Magic** Invitational champion!

## Winston Draft Coverage: Hoaen Hoping for Big Spells

*by Tim Willoughby*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6e/6b/6e6be69168b79365506451ecafe5191bb78b37c979e057857f2a5fccff8d0e9c.jpg)*The titans squared off to Winston Draft off the Cube.*The first format of the Invitational finals is the Winston Draft. While in the round robin portion of the event this was done with *Lorwyn*, for the finals we have something a little special. The Cube has again been rolled out, but trimmed from its original 720-card goodness: 100 cards, split evenly by colour, were randomly chosen to be shuffled together for this final Winston Draft of the tournament. From those 100, 10 were removed, so that there were less guarantees about the number of cards of any one colour. Following that it was just up to each play to draft their best, as they looked to get ahead in Sunday's play.

Prior to the match, Rich Hoaen suggested in an open interview with himself and Tiago that Cube Draft is all about big spells, while creatures were very much second-class citizens. Of course in Winston it is important to take whatever chances you can get, so upon sitting down he had to remain open to the possibility of having a creature deck.

The very first option for Rich in the draft, going second, was [Chandra Nalaar](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra+Nalaar) and [Gaddock Teeg](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gaddock+Teeg). Two of the most powerful *Lorwyn* cards were not good enough to make Rich's pile; a testament to the overall power level of the Cube. Hoaens first picks were in pile B—[Swords to Plowshares](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swords+to+Plowshares) and [White Shield Crusader](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=White+Shield+Crusader).

He got a second option on the *Lorwyn* legends, and took them along with [Powder Keg](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Powder+Keg) and [Spike Weaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spike+Weaver). It seemed that Hoaen still really wanted big spells, as he ignored [White Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=White+Knight), [Elves of Deep Shadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elves+of+Deep+Shadow) and [Knight of Meadowgrain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+Meadowgrain), instead going through all three piles before getting an unexciting [Awakening](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Awakening) off the top. When Tiago took pile A, Rich got a second look at these weenies, and when [Gaea's Cradle](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gaea%27s+Cradle) had joined them, he was lured in.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c2/8f/c28f83ed8ee37c78911bcecbc85ee5f35b45deeeab4c722987998574aca7dd65.jpg)*Tiago contemplates a one-card stack.*[Academy Rector](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Academy+Rector) and [Opposition](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Opposition) were a tempting proposition that Hoaen gladly took, and after letting a pile get juiced up for a while, Hoaen also got a fair pick in [Kris Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kris+Mage), [Stinkweed Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stinkweed+Imp), [Dauthi Horror](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dauthi+Horror) and [Demonic Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonic+Tutor). The final spell there was likely what drew him in—it can never hurt to have two copies of the best card in your deck.

[Loxodon Warhammer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Warhammer) came with [Palinchron](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Palinchron), [Persecute](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Persecute) and [Soltari Monk](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soltari+Monk) next, and was soon joined by [Vendetta](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vendetta) off the top of the pile, a nice rip. Against his preferences, Hoaen's deck was turning into a beatdown deck, as [Watchwolf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Watchwolf), [Soltari Priest](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soltari+Priest), [Goblin Trenches](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Trenches) and [Arcane Denial](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcane+Denial) got added to the pile. [Deranged Hermit](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deranged+Hermit) and [Visara the Dreadful](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Visara+the+Dreadful) soon followed.

When [Cataclysm](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cataclysm) showed up along with [Rogue Elephant](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rogue+Elephant) and [Vedalken Shackles](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vedalken+Shackles), Hoaen was quick to snap it up. It seemed that he wasn't getting a great many of the "big spells," so having a board sweeper looked pretty important for him. [Condemn](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Condemn) and [Smokestack](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smokestack) got the nod from Richie, who finished things off with [Kavu Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kavu+Titan).

Hoaen's draft had given him a reasonable enough beatdown deck, but the Canadian player known for his mastery of Limited formats was pretty down on his deck, which was heavy on two-power beaters, but low on power in general.

  

## Winston Draft Finals: Use the Force, Tiago

*by Tim Willoughby*

"How's your deck?"

"Terrible."

I could pretty much assign those quotes either way around in this Winston Draft portion of the Invitational, as neither player was overly happy with their deck. Tiago felt that he was at a disadvantage given less experience with playing Cube than Rich, but Hoaen was fairly down on his deck, which didn't have the caliber of spells that he wanted.

Having watched Hoaen's side of the draft, his deck looked pretty good—but it was a beatdown deck, which according to Hoaen is not exactly the ideal plan for Cube.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f7/10/f71084e052ed601b1f839f611850dacb391db80dd77c5b03d4b37605d870be71.jpg)*Hoaen's Warhammer wasn't enough to stop Tiago's invading army.*

### Game 1

On the play Rich kept a hand with [White Shield Crusader](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=White+Shield+Crusader), [Gaddock Teeg](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gaddock+Teeg), [Demonic Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonic+Tutor), [Loxodon Warhammer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Warhammer), [Deranged Hermit](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deranged+Hermit) and land. He played out the Crusader first, while Tiago had a [Spiketail Hatchling](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spiketail+Hatchling) to hold back Hoaen on some spells.

Tiago hit three land types early while Rich was stuck with just two Plains and a Forest. He did play [Gaddock Teeg](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gaddock+Teeg) though, locking Tiago out from playing any "big" spells. Chan had a morph, which looked likely to be [Akroma, Angel of Fury](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroma%2C+Angel+of+Fury) based on what Rich had seen in the draft, but was well away from flipping it.

Chan played [Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anger) and bashed with his team. Hoaen blocked the morph, and saw that in face it was [Whipcorder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whipcorder). He was ahead in the race though, and had a great racing card in the form of [Loxodon Warhammer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Warhammer), which if equipped to [Soltari Monk](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soltari+Monk) would be really tough to fight.

"So was that an off-colour morph?" asked an incredulous Hoaen looking at the [Whipcorder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whipcorder) in Tiago's graveyard.

Chan just gave a bashful smile... "Maybe he's not off-colour..."

Chan played [Spectral Force](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectral+Force), and blocked [White Shield Crusader](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=White+Shield+Crusader), though he had to take 5 from the shadow priest with a hammer. [Spectral Force](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectral+Force) from Tiago didn't look so hot in the face of this clock. What did was [Triskelion](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triskelion), who came next. Hoaen played a kickered [Kavu Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kavu+Titan) and passed the turn.

All Tiago had for his turn was a [Fyndhorn Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fyndhorn+Elves), which left Rich to equip the hammer and swing. ## Vintage Finals: Done in a Flash

*by Tim Willoughby*

And so it all comes down to this. Vintage will decide the finals of the 2007 Invitational here at Spiel Essen in Germany. Tiago Chan dominated the Winston Draft, while Hoaen took the Choose Your Own Standard match 2-0. Each player had gone 3-0 in the respective formats in the round robin, but equally each player had gone 2-1 in Vintage.

### Game 1

The die roll would be critical. Each player rolled six dice, and Tiago got to play first. This was very bad new for Richard Hoaen. His Stax deck has plenty of disruption for the Flash-Hulk combo deck, but there is literally nothing he can do against the turn-one play of Chan.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c1/9d/c19d0409a9fb75090c5ae66f36b72cba04f2d4db32adb7b131239f6173766888.jpg)*Welcome to the turn-one kill.*Hoaen had lost the early game. In Vintage, the mid-game is the mulligan decision.

Tiago won the mid-game too, with no mulligans. As it turns out, it didn't really matter what Hoaen did. His hand of [Sphere of Resistance](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphere+of+Resistance), Mox Jet, Wasteland, Barbarian Ring, [Chalice of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chalice+of+the+Void), [Ancestral Recall](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Recall), [Sundering Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sundering+Titan) would have been an utter beating on the play, with turn-one Sphere and Chalice for zero. On the draw, though, it was way too slow.

Tiago dumped his hand on the table. Mox, Mox, [Summoner's Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoner%27s+Pact) and [Flash](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808111148/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flash) were enough for the turn-one win.
