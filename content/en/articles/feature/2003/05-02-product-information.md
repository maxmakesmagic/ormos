**Booster packs**  
 15 randomly assorted cards

**Theme decks**  
 4 decks of 60 pre-set cards  
  
***The theme decks’ names are:***  
 “Storm Surge” deck  
 “Max Attax” deck  
 “Pulverize” deck  
 “Goblin Mob” deck

**Fat packs**  
 Containing 6 boosters, a *Scourge* novel, a spindown life counter, the *Scourge* Players Guide, 1 premium land card and 1 random premium card.

**Deck boxes**  
 1 polypropylene deck boxes with tab closure 80 tournament legal, black textured sleeves
