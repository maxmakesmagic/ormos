Let the Sealed Deck building and drafting begin! The latest Pro Tour Qualifier Season started last week with everyone's eyes on what the *Scars of Mirrodin* Limited environment is like. Is infect the best way to punch your ticket to Paris? What about metalcraft? Take a look at the first results and then [find a Pro Tour Qualifier](https://web.archive.org/web/20101007000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/protour/paris11-qualifiers) for yourself. As for **Magic Online**, we're still a week away from [*Scars* prerelease events online](https://web.archive.org/web/20101007000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/other/09292010e), so mark your calendar for October 14!
