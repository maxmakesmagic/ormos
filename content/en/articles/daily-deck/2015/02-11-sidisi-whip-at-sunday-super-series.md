I know I talked a lot about [Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20150211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang) last week. But he isn't only good in Modern. A 4/5 that can be played on turn three is a huge threat in Modern, but even more so in Standard. Of course, it does need a little help to do so.

Sidisi Whip is the perfect home for Tasigur with the combination of fetch lands and discard spells, similar to that of a Modern deck. He can also utilize unnecessary cards in the graveyard from [Satyr Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20150211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Satyr+Wayfinder) and support the deck by putting cards into the graveyard with his own ability.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang)

Another fascinating card is the single [Torrent Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20150211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrent+Elemental).

I love delving it to cast Tasigur after milling it from an early Wayfinder, and then later summoning it from the exile zone or returning it from [Whip of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20150211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos).

This deck can really abuse this rare ability.

It's also a good size and can open a path for an attacking [Sidisi, Brood Tyrant](https://web.archive.org/web/20150211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sidisi%2C+Brood+Tyrant) with its tapping ability.
