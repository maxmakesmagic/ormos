### Magic: The Gathering Pro Tour - Boston Qualifier  
 February 16-17, 2002

**Magic: The Gathering Pro Tour - Boston Qualifier  
 February 16-17, 2002**

[![Japanese Fact Sheet](http://www.wizards.com/grandprix/images/JapaneseLink.jpg)](/ja/events/2002/nagoya02/grand-prix-nagoya)  
[Trial Schedule](https://web.archive.org/web/20020623131019/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=grandprix/nagoya02/trials)

The **Magic: The Gathering** Grand Prix tournament series is an international circuit of large-scale Qualifier tournaments featuring cash prizes and multiple invitations to Pro Tour events.

### TOURNAMENT FORMAT AND SCHEDULE

The format for Grand Prix - Nagoya is *Odyssey* block Team Limited, with Day 2 Team Rochester Draft. All DCI Limited rules and **Magic** Floor Rules announced on October 1, 2001, along with all subsequent updates, will be enforced.

*   **Friday, May 10:** Registration from 15:00 - 21:00. All teams must register by 21:00 Friday night. LATE REGISTRATION WILL NOT BE ALLOWED. Teams that cannot meet this deadline should contact the organizer at the mail address listed below no later than May 9.
*   **Saturday, May 11:** Tournament begins at 09:00. The Top 20 teams advance to the finals on Sunday.
*   **Sunday, May 12:** Finals begin at 08:30.

### TOURNAMENT

*   **Divisions**
     The main tournament at Grand Prix - Nagoya is open to **Magic** players of all ages.

    If a team that wins an invitation to Pro Tour - Boston at Grand Prix - Fukuoka is already qualified for an invitation, the invitation shall be extended to the next-best finisher. Previous qualification may occur through a Qualifier tournament, through performance at a professional tournament, or by being an invited DCI team (valid on the date of the official rank announcement for the appropriate professional tournament).

*   **Tournament Format**
     The format for Grand Prix - Nagoya is *Odyssey* Block Team Limited, with Day 2 Team Rochester Draft. All DCI Limited rules and **Magic** Floor Rules announced on October 1, 2001, along with all subsequent updates, will be enforced.

    For the latest edition of the DCI and **Magic** Floor Rules, please refer to the [DCI Rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20020623131019/http://www.wizards.com/dci/main.asp?x=UTR_Intro) web site.

*   **Tournament Structure**
     The structure for Grand Prix - Nagoya is modified Swiss-style (50-minute rounds, with the number of rounds determined by the number of participants), with the Top 20 teams advancing to the Sunday finals for prizes and invitations.

*   **Tournament Byes** *(Byes are not cumulative through the season or year.)*
     Byes for Grand Prix - Nagoya are based on the Pro Tour - Osaka 2002 professional standings and DCI ratings as of March 20, 2002. Please refer to the [Grand Prix - Nagoya Bye List](https://web.archive.org/web/20020623131019/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=grandprix/nagoya02/byes) for details.
