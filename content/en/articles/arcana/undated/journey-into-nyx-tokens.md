Renowned Weaver is a reference to the greek myth of Arachne. The name Arachne is derived from the same Greek root that the word "arachnid" comes from.

While Pharika's Chosen has a snake in the art, it does not produce tokens. Only Pharika herself makes snake tokens in *Journey into Nyx*.
