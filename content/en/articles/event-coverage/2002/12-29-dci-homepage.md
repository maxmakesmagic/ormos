 **[Ratings & Rankings](https://web.archive.org/web/20021229000000/http://webapp.wizards.com/ratings.aspx)**  
 Find out your personal ranking information or see who's on top in your region, country or the world.

 **[Magic Player Rewards](https://web.archive.org/web/20090204082405/http://wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Article.aspx?x=magic/rewards/welcome)**  
To support the thousands of players around the world playing in DCI-sanctioned **Magic** tournaments, Wizards of the Coast provides Magic Player Rewards. We're dishing out never-before-seen textless spells and copies of a special premium card based on a player's tournament play in the DCI database.

 **[Tournament Locator](https://web.archive.org/web/20021229000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Magic/EventLocator.aspx)**  
 Find DCI sanctioned events in your area.

 **[Personal Information Center](https://web.archive.org/web/20021229000000/http://webapp.wizards.com/dcimember/)**  
 Get information regarding your ratings and rankings. 

 **[Magic Rules, Card Text, and Tournament Rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20090218110712/http://wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Article.aspx?x=magic/rules/tourneyplayer)**  
 DCI tournament rules are a set of official rules and regulations for sanctioned tournaments. They cover deck construction, player conduct, and floor rules. Gatherer is the official searchable card text database.

 **[DCI Document Center](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/welcome-to-the-dci-document-center)**  
From this page, you can access the rules for playing in DCI-sanctioned tournaments. Players are advised to review the DCI Universal Tournament Rules and the DCI Floor Rules. Also included are penalty guidelines and premier-event invitation policies.

 **[Tournament Organizer Information](https://web.archive.org/web/20090216154701/http://wizards.com/default.asp?x=dci/sanction/welcome)**  
 Running DCI-sanctioned tournaments is a great service you can offer your players, keeping them involved in organized play at your location for a long time to come.

 **[Judge Certification Program](https://web.archive.org/web/20021229000000/http://wizards.com/dci/judge)**  
 The DCI Judge Certification Program is designed to ensure consistency in all rulings and penalties at DCI-sanctioned tournaments, and to identify and recognize experienced and knowledgeable judges.

 **[Rules Advisor Program](https://web.archive.org/web/20090215181559/http://wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=judge/resources/rulesadvisorprogram)**  
 You may be one already and simply not be recognized. Are you the person at your local events that everyone asks rules questions of? Are you the person who figures out the cards and what great combinations can be made from them and share these with others? Rules Advisors seek to be at the top of the ‘rules-game’ and assist others who have questions in casual games or store events that a certified judge is understaffed or unavailable for.

 **[Tournament Appeals](/en/articles/event-coverage/2002/12-29-dci-match-history-appeals)**  
 Click here for more information about how to appeal match results.
