Do you like *Magic*?

Of course you do, that's why you're reading this!

Do you like watching things on your screen?

Of course you do, you're reading something on a screen right now!

Do you like watching *Magic*?

Probably! Watching *Magic* is the next best thing to playing *Magic*.

How about watching some of the most popular *Magic* streamers playing the Holiday Cube on *Magic Online* today on Twitch.tv? Is that something you might be interested in?

Of course it is!

This month marks the first episode of a monthly streaming program we're calling Streamer Showdowns that features two eight-player Draft pods boasting a number of popular streamers battling with one of the latest *Magic Online* sets—in this case, the Holiday Cube.

Don't know what the [Holiday Cube](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/06-22-holiday-cube-updates) is? It's a Cube featuring the most powerful cards in *Magic*'s history, including [Black Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Lotus), [Ancestral Recall](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Recall), and, a personal favorite, [Vedalken Shackles](https://web.archive.org/web/20150716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vedalken+Shackles) (which is basically unbeatable).

This month, the streamers showing down include a bevy of popular streamers, Pro Tour competitors, Grand Prix winners, the melodious voice of Pro Tour coverage, professional funny people, several bastions of the community, and also Frank Lepore!
