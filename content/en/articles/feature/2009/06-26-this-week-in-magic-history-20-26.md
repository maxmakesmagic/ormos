### June 18-20, 2004: Craig Krempels Wins US National Championship

![Rankko Bongo Wheshiwheshi](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/db/e1db765901746641999e1edb509bedb0120bd356d5a89f8417f66eac740deb0b.jpg)

In what would prove to be [Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20090626000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp)'s swan song in Standard, Krempels ran the CMU/Togit Elf and Nail deck through Alex Melnikow and William Postlethewait, before defeating Bill Stead in the finals. Krempels left Kansas City with the National Championship, a spot on the US team at 2004's World Championships, and a sizeable $25,000 prize for his performance this weekend.
