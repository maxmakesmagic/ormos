Welcome to another edition of [Sketches](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/fullarchive&tablefilter=sketches:). This week we take a look behind the scenes at the art of *Darksteel's* **[Panoptic Mirror](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panoptic+Mirror)**, created by Glen Angus. During the design of the *Mirrodin* block, the Mirror was one of the main inspirations for the Imprint mechanic (read more about the history of [Panoptic Mirror](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panoptic+Mirror) in [Aaron Forsythe's article](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/af1).) As it is, the Mirror is a powerful way to hit your opponent with the same instant or sorcery each turn.

The Mirror also has important flavor implications in this set. The Panopticon, represented as the card [Darksteel Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Citadel), is the abode of the creator of the plane of Mirrodin, [Memnarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memnarch) (as seen in [last week's Sketches](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/sketches-memnarch-2004-02-23)). In the top of his tower, [Memnarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memnarch) can peer over all of Mirrodin using that Mirror. So Mr. Angus has his work cut out for him.

### 1\. Art Description

The art description establishes the parameters of the artist's task. It provides details of what the card will do, what its flavor role is, and what the mood of the illustration should be. Here were the instructions given to Mr. Angus for the project:

> *"An arcane mirror. Spells reflected in it can emerge from its surface. Suggestion: Show the mirror's surface bending outward, as if the spell being reflected (a lightning bolt, for example) were trying to push its way out."*

You'll recognize Glen Angus's work from cards like [Tangle Wire](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tangle+Wire), [Gigapede](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gigapede), [Longhorn Firebeast](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Longhorn+Firebeast), [Broodstar](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Broodstar), [Ivory Mask](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ivory+Mask) and [Kaboom!](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kaboom%21).

Note how, in the final sketch, Glen Angus's initials are now upside-down! (You heard it here at **Magic** Arcana first.) [Memnarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memnarch) now has his [Darksteel Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20040301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Darksteel%2BCitadel) powerful artifact centerpiece.
