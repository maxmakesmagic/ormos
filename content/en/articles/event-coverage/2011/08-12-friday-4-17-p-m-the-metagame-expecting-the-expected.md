As always, German Nationals happen late in the season. Japan, France, USA and Italy have all found their champions and teams for Worlds already. The 195 players who came to Iserlohn to grab their piece of glory took a good look at those decks and decided that this would be their metagame, too. With four rounds of Standard done, we've asked a couple of players what they had expected to face here. The results? Not really surprising. (We'll have the metagame breakdown for you tomorrow, so you can see how the expectations of the players compared to the facts.)

### Florian Pils (U/B Control):

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gernat11/meta1_florian_pils.JPG)

  
*"I expected [Caw](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=208279)-[Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=382200), [Valakut](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut), [Tempered Steel](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tempered+Steel), Mono-Red and U/B Control. I did play against [Valakut](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut), an Eldrazi deck, Mono-Red and U/B Control – but I wouldn't have minded not playing against the last two."*

  
  

### Tobias Gräfensteiner ([Valakut](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut)):

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gernat11/meta2_tobias_graefensteiner.JPG)

  
*"Mostly Control, that was my expectation, especially [Caw](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=208279)-[Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=382200) and U/B. If people would go aggro, I'd expected them to choose [Tempered Steel](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tempered+Steel)."*

  
  

### Simon Görtzen ([Caw](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=208279)-[Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=382200)):

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gernat11/meta4_simon_goertzen.jpg)

  
*"Do you want percentages? 20 % [Caw](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=208279)-[Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=382200), 10 % each for [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) decks, [Valakut](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut), U/B, Mono-R including [Goblin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin)s, and 7% [Tempered Steel](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tempered+Steel). But really, the metagame is [Caw](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=208279)-[Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=382200) and decks that try to beat it. That's why I am playing [Caw](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=208279)-[Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=382200)."*

  
  

### Klaus Jöns ([Valakut](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut)):

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gernat11/meta3_klaus_joens.JPG)

  
*"I can beat those numbers! 21 % [Caw](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=208279)-[Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=382200), 12 % U/B Control, 10 % [Valakut](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut) and [Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pod)-Decks, then [Steel](https://web.archive.org/web/20110812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steel), Mono-Red and various combo decks. Really, though, percentages don't really mean much beforehand. I expected those decks, in that order of popularity."*
