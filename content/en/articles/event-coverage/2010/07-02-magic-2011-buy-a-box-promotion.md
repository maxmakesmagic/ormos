### Buy a box ... Spread your wings ... Dive into *Magic 2011*!

Buy one full ***Magic** 2011* Core Set booster display and receive the exclusive Birds of Paradise promo card\*.

*   [List of Participating Stores](http://www.playmagic.eu/en/stores/m11)

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/74/bf/74bfd23429c1b38576cec6fc335c52a5a511044aa620eac35a093572697b8d0d.jpg) 

  

\*Offer valid only in [participating stores](http://www.playmagic.eu/en/stores/m11) until 17th September 2010 and while stock lasts. If you buy 1 full booster display in the participating stores, you can collect the special promo card from this store (if stock is available). The promotion is for purchases in store only. Online purchases are not eligible for this promotion. Please ask your retailer for further information.
