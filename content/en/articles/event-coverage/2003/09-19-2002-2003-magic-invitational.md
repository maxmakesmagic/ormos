After tallying all the votes, I’m happy to announce this year’s invitees to the 2002/2003 **Magic** Invitational. Like last year, this year’s Invitational will be held on ***Magic** Online*. The date for the event hasn’t been finalized yet but expect it to occur in late winter or early spring 2004. Here are the sixteen invitees:
