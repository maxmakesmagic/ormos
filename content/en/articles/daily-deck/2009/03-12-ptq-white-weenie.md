What's that? You didn't think White Weenie could be a viable Extended deck for the Pro Tour Qualifier-Honolulu season? Mr. Andrew Wagner would ask you to guess again as he took his list of the age-old deck to a Top 8 at a recent PTQ. With plenty of maindeck hate for the format in the likes of Ethersworn Canonist, Kataki, War's Wage, and Burrenton Forge-Tender, Andrew charged right through the naysayers all day long.
