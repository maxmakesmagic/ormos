Announcement Date: December 1, 2003  
 Effective Date: January 1, 2004

### Standard

> No changes

### Extended

*   [Ancient Tomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Tomb) is banned
*   [Goblin Recruiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Recruiter) is banned
*   [Grim Monolith](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Monolith) is banned
*   [Hermit Druid](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hermit+Druid) is banned
*   [Oath of Druids](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oath+of+Druids) is banned
*   [Tinker](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tinker) is banned

### Type 1

*   [Burning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Wish) is restricted
*   [Chrome Mox](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chrome+Mox) is restricted
*   [Lion's Eye Diamond](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lion%27s+Eye+Diamond) is restricted

(All cards on the Type 1 Banned and Restricted Lists are banned in Type 1.5.)

*   [Burning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Wish) is banned

*   [Lion's Eye Diamond](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lion%27s+Eye+Diamond) is banned

### Explanations in Brief

Here are explanations of the changes from **Magic** R&D:

### Extended Bannings

**[Ancient Tomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Tomb)**  
 Years of watching constructed metagames evolve has taught us that efficient ways to generate fast mana are always a problem. [Ancient Tomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Tomb) simply adds too much speed to the environment.

**[Goblin Recruiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Recruiter)**  
[Goblin Recruiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Recruiter)'s ability to stack an arbitrarily large portion of your deck allows goblin-based combo decks to kill as early as turn 2. That's too fast for a healthy environment and the Recruiter is one of several casualties of our effort to eliminate realistic turn 2 kills from the environment.

**[Grim Monolith](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Monolith)**  
[Grim Monolith](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Monolith) has to go for essentially the same reasons as [Ancient Tomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Tomb). Things happen too quickly when the available mana acceleration cards are this good.

**[Hermit Druid](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hermit+Druid)**  
 The current crop of reanimator decks are winning the game by turn 2 and they all depend on dumping their entire library into the graveyard with [Hermit Druid](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hermit+Druid). The Druid simply does too much, too fast.

**[Oath of Druids](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oath+of+Druids)**  
 In our effort to make sure games last more than two or three turns, we felt we needed to get rid of [Oath of Druids](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oath+of+Druids) in addition to fast mana and combo enabling cards. [Oath of Druids](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oath+of+Druids) effectively ends the game on turn 2 against almost all creature decks, making it essentially impossible to have a healthy environment as long as this card is legal. 

**[Tinker](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tinker)**  
[Tinker](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tinker) is both a tutor and a mana accelerator, so we've been worried about it for years. The recent release of *Mirrodin* added many powerful artifacts to the environment and put [Tinker](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tinker) decks over the top (as evidenced by [Pro Tour New Orleans](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=sideboard/events/ptno03)). 

### Type 1 Restrictions

**[Burning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Wish)**  
 Most “tutors” are restricted in Type 1 because it defeats the purpose of restricting a card if you can play four copies of an efficient way to go get it. Since the most powerful cards in Type 1 are sorceries ([Yawgmoth's Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yawgmoth%27s+Will), [Timetwister](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Timetwister), [Balance](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balance), [Mind Twist](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind+Twist), etc.) it was possible to play the equivalent of four copies of any one of these simple by moving it into your sideboard. Interestingly, [Cunning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cunning+Wish) was left off the restricted list because the one extra mana combined with the fact that Type 1 has less powerful instants than sorceries means it does not appear to be a problem.

**[Chrome Mox](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chrome+Mox)**  
 Time has shown that fast mana is even more powerful in Type 1 than in other formats.

**[Lion's Eye Diamond](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lion%27s+Eye+Diamond)**  
 Some fast mana cards are easier to abuse than others, but recent advances in deck design have shown that even the drawback on [Lion's Eye Diamond](https://web.archive.org/web/20031201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lion%27s+Eye+Diamond) isn't severe enough to balance a zero-mana artifact that gives you three mana.
