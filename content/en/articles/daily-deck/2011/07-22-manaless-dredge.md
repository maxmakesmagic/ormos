Looking for a Legacy deck that doesn't require Force of Will or Wasteland? Nicholas Rausch has you covered, and his deck is a proven winner! Manaless Dredge, a Dredge deck for Legacy that doesn't need mana to operate, allows Nicholas to fill up his graveyard without ever having to tap mana! From there he can beatdown with Ichorid or use Bridge from Below to swarm his opponents to submission. Read more about Nicholas's winning deck in the [official coverage](http://www.starcitygames.com/magic/standard/22243_The_StarCityGamescom_Open_Series_is_Coming_to_Cincinnati_on_July_1617.html) of the StarCityGames.com Open in Cincinnati.
