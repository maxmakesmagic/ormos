### October 28-30, 2006: A Starring Role for Antoine Ruel

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fe/36/fe36de5543c261b64457b114bdec78e62d84291aa5713b6643adb5a5d8247234.jpg)  

Packing an old-school Psychatog deck, Pro Tour veteran Antoine Ruel fought his way through the 340-player field to become champion of Pro Tour-Los Angeles 2005.

Ruel eschewed the just-rotated new Extended format's popular [Dredge](https://web.archive.org/web/20091030000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dredge)/[Life from the Loam](https://web.archive.org/web/20091030000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Life+from+the+Loam) addition to the classic 'Tog build and it proved to be a smart decision. He started his run through the Top 8 by defeating Tsuyoshi Fujita and his Boros Deck Wins 3-1 and never looked back.
