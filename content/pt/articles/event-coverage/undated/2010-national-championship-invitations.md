Mexico Nationals Invitation list comprises the following:

*   The current Mexico National Champion and members of the current Mexico national team (including alternate).
*   Players from Mexico with Pro Tour Players Club level 2 or higher.
*   Winners of invitations through National Qualifiers.
*   Winners of invitations through Mexico National Open tournaments (“grinders”).
*   The Top 75 DCI Total–ranked players from Mexico on June 2, 2010.

All players on the list below are already qualified for Mexico Nationals and are not permitted to play in Mexico National Qualifiers and/or Mexico Nationals Open Events (“Grinders”).
