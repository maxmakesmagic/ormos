If you've never played [**Duels of the Planeswalkers 2012**](https://web.archive.org/web/20120327000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx), now would be a great time to start. That's because Steam and PS3 now have the Gold Bundle. For $19.99, that includes:

*   **Duels of the Planeswalkers 2012**
*   The [**Duels of the Planeswalkers 2012** Expansion](https://web.archive.org/web/20120327000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/duels-planeswalkers-2012-expansion-1-2011-09-13)
*   All three Deck Packs, giving you access to [every playable deck in the game](https://web.archive.org/web/20120327000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/631066)

Did you purchase the game when it first came out? We still have something to offer you! The Gold Deck Bundle costs $9.99 and it gives you all nineteen [playable decks](https://web.archive.org/web/20120327000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/631066) and the foil conversion for each one. Play in style!
