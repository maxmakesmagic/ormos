Before we get into today's Arcana, here's some news: **all of *Scars of Mirrodin*** is now visible on [the Visual Spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20100922000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/scarsofmirrodin/spoiler). Every card! Go look at it! Prepare for the Prerelease this weekend!
