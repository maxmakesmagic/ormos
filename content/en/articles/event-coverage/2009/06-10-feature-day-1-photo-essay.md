![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_01.jpg)

 

Welcome to beautiful Nagoya, Japan. *Magic 2012* all-stars Sorin, Jace, Gideon, Garruk and Chandra are watching over you!

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_02.jpg)

 

Even in the early morning, downtown Nagoya is equal parts hustle and bustle.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_03.jpg)

 

Last minute memorization of important phrases, such as "All glory to the Father of Machines!"

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_04.jpg)

 

The countryside is steeped in beauty, history, and stairs.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_05.jpg)

 

Pointing the way to Pro Tour greatness, and deliciousness!

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_06.jpg)

 

Your diligent judges, who keep things running smoothly.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_07.jpg)

 

Veteran Nate Price takes cub reporter Steve Sadin under his wing.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_08.jpg)

 

Spellslinging this weekend: R&D's Ken Nagle, Tom LaPille, and Dave Guskin, as well as jack-of-all-trades event staffer Matt Danner. Three parts wacky sitcom, one part slasher flick.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_09.jpg)

 

This guy locked Tom LaPille up good with the retro combo of [Sands of Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20090610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sands+of+Time) and [Equipoise](https://web.archive.org/web/20090610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Equipoise).

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_10.jpg)

 

Guest Artist Steve Belledin shows off [Ichor Wellspring](https://web.archive.org/web/20090610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichor+Wellspring), a popular card this weekend.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_11.jpg)

 

Also in attendance, Daarken, posed here with [Kulrath Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20090610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kulrath+Knight).

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_12.jpg)

 

The great Ookubo, master of three-dimensional card art.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_13.jpg)

 

Just a few of his many masterpieces.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_14.jpg)

 

[Chandra Nalaar](https://web.archive.org/web/20090610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra+Nalaar) never looked so good.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_15.jpg)

 

For Round 6, things switch to draft, and the 5-0's duke it out.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_16.jpg)

 

Sometimes judge, sometimes competitor, but always stylish.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_18.jpg)

 

The Pro Tour is full of highs...

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_19.jpg)

 

...lows...

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptnag11/d1photo_20.jpg)

 

...and, of course, pancake soda.
