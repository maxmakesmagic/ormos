Thanks to everyone who entered the *Agents of Artifice* Fan Art Contest. (See the bottom of this page for the original announcement.) We received loads of entries and each one exceeded our expectations, making judging all the more difficult. But the following entries stood out due to their adherence to the *Agents of Artifice* story, creativity, and originality.

Congratulations to all of the winners and honorable mentions.

### Agents of Artifice Fan Art Contest

![](https://i.ormos.uk/47/dc/47dc2cede0cc0176345518aef63217aaf5354e271ef12ec2846260191d79e1b9.jpg)They say a picture’s worth a thousand words. Give us your story and we could end up giving you $2,000!

Simply create a color work, either digitally or traditionally, depicting your favorite scene from the book [*Agents of Artifice*](/en/articles/feature/2009/01-15-agents-of-artifice) by Ari Marmell. It can be a comic, a drawing, a painting ... use your imagination – and have fun with it! Plus, there are prizes for second and third place, too!

Here is the [entry form](https://web.archive.org/web/20090608000000/http://www.wizards.com/mtg/images/novels/AofA_contestentryform.doc) (173k Word doc). You can email your entry to us at bookpromos@wizards.com, send it to us on a CD or DVD, or mail the actual piece to us. The deadline for submission is May 6, 2009.

For complete contest rules and eligibility, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20090608000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/agents-artifice-art-contest-official-rules-2009-03-02).
