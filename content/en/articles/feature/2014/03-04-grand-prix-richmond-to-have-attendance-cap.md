As preregistration numbers approach record-setting levels, StarCityGames has announced its intent to cap attendance at [Grand Prix Richmond](/en/events/2014/richmond14/grand-prix-richmond-event-information) at 5,000 players. Details regarding this decision are available on [starcitygames.com](http://www.starcitygames.com/article/28025_Countdown-to-Grand-Prix-Richmond-ndash-3-Days-Out-Capping-Attendance-at-5000-Guaranteed-Rewards.html). Players interested in attending this event are encouraged to read the article on starcitygames.com and preregister as soon as possible.
