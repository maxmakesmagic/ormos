As we celebrate the holidays, we're bringing you some of our favorite decks from throughout the year.

Enjoy these fun flashbacks into last year’s favorites.

Wizards of the Coast Staff

  

* * *

  

Gerry Thompson's really been helping me with my homework this week, and this is another sweet deck he directed my attention to (something he's always good for, really).

![Experiment One](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Experiment+One)

  

This deck was piloted to a 4–0 finish by excellent deck builder Shouta Yasooka, yaya3 on **Magic Online**, so I assume he is at least largely responsible for the list, which takes Bant Flash in a much more aggressive direction than we'd seen so far. 

[Experiment One](https://web.archive.org/web/20131224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Experiment+One) is great here, because despite the relatively high spell count, the creatures (and some of the spells) are particularly great at triggering evolve. 

I like his decision not to play [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20131224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets) or [Augur of Bolas](https://web.archive.org/web/20131224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Augur+of+Bolas), which allows him to just play the cards he wants rather than worrying about spell count. Instead, he prioritizes cards he can play at instant speed. 

[Spell Rupture](https://web.archive.org/web/20131224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Rupture) is a great counterspell in this deck, but the choice to play exclusively [Spell Rupture](https://web.archive.org/web/20131224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Rupture) and no [Syncopate](https://web.archive.org/web/20131224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syncopate)s is significant just because it shows that he's not really concerned with exiling spells, which can be very significant against Reanimator. Still, with so many large, cheap creatures, [Spell Rupture](https://web.archive.org/web/20131224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Rupture) is likely to be much more consistent as a counterspell and plays far better with [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20131224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage). 

His sideboard offers Block Constructed powerhouse [Rootborn Defenses](https://web.archive.org/web/20131224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rootborn+Defenses), which I'm surprised he only has two of, as well as an assortment of lifegain, counterspells, and removal.
