Lots of pros have started making Highlander decks to play for fun.<br><b>Victor van den Broek</b>: Right now, I enjoy playing Collector’s Edition draft a lot. It's fun to play with old cards and it's casual play with friends.<br><b>Osyp Lebedowicz </b> (<a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20050113012112/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.togit.com/writers/osyp.html&amp;origin=sideboard_askpros_0603">http://www.togit.com/writers/osyp.html</a>): My favorite non-competitive format has to be Fallen Empires Block Constructed. Nothing is more fun than simply accumulating spore tokens.<br><b>Eric Taylor</b>: I love identical draft, a format invented by Pat Chapin, where you take 4 people with sets of three identical 15 card boosters, and draft normally, somehow it's a cross between Constructed and Limited.<br><b>Brian Kibler</b>: Five Color - It's always interesting to see bizarre games with such swing potential from cards like Contract from Below.<br><b>Eric Froehlich</b>: 250 (Five Color), especially on Magic Online, because it does all the shuffling for you.<br></p>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;</td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>
