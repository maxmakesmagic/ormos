It's almost November, so it's time to announce the promos that Friday Night *Magic* players can look forward to over the next three months. They are shiny, and soon they can all be yours.

**November**

Well that's just—just horrifying. I know *Eldritch Moon* and *Shadows over Innistrad* were all about horror but, this is madness...

...oh, I get it. Yeah, this one features an Angel doing something wholly terrifying. But that was the point, wasn't it, Eric Deschamps?

**December**

If you happened to pick up one of those terrifying [Fiery Temper](https://web.archive.org/web/20161020000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Temper)s, this is a pretty effective way to madness it out. And 1/1 lifelink Vampires, to boot!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/3gU2ll5zKi.png)

![Call the Bloodline Promo](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/c4rd4r7_3gU2ll5zKi.jpg)

[Call the Bloodline](https://web.archive.org/web/20161020000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Call+the+Bloodline) Promo | Art by [Magali Villeneuve](https://web.archive.org/web/20161020000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Magali+Villeneuve%22%5d)

**January**

Speaking of effective ways to utilize madness, [Noose Constrictor](https://web.archive.org/web/20161020000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noose+Constrictor) is one of the better ones around. This [Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20161020000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel) update has been used in a number of powerful tournament decks to block fliers, put cards in the graveyard, and just generally serve as a menacing Snake.
