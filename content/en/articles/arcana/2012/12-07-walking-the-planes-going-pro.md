It's time for another Walking the Planes! In "Going Pro," Nate Holt and Shawn Kornhauser continue their journey through the **Magic** tournament Multiverse at San Antonio for that city's [first Grand Prix](https://web.archive.org/web/20121207000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/lytles-zombies-big-texas). Get the Grand Prix perspective from Pro Tour legend Luis Scott-Vargas plus newcomers Grayson Maloch and Will Schendel.
