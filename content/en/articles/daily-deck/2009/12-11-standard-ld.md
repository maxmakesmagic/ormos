Many **Magic** players have long had a love/hate relationship with land destruction. At the [2009 World Championships](/en/events/2009/worlds09/all-roads-lead-to-victory-for-coimbra-china) in Rome, Italy, Bernhard Lehner took his turn at the strategy. Goblin Ruinblaster, Ajani Vengeant, and Acidic Slime were all accelerated to the battlefield with Rampant Growth and Trace of Abundance. Batting cleanup were Garruk Wildspeaker and Elspeth, Knight-Errant, a powerful backup squad if there ever was one!
