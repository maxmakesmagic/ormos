This matchup of very similar decks was determined by [Wingsteed Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingsteed+Rider)s, [Hopeful Eidolon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hopeful+Eidolon)s and [Wavecrash Triton](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wavecrash+Triton).

By turn four of game one, both players had [Wingsteed Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingsteed+Rider) bestowed with [Hopeful Eidolon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hopeful+Eidolon), while Fabien had the slight lead with [Traveling Philosopher](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Traveling+Philosopher).

Park then stole the lead for a brief moment with [Blood-Toll Harpy](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood-Toll+Harpy) and [Cavern Lampad](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cavern+Lampad) on his Rider, but Fabien had [Voyage's End](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyage%27s+End), [Wavecrash Triton](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wavecrash+Triton) and [Favored Hoplite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Favored+Hoplite) to get back in the game.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gpkl14/s_park.jpg)

*Park Jun Young*

Park tried to rebuild with [Heliod's Emissary](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heliod%27s+Emissary) on the Eidolon, but Fabien's alpha strike with [Dauntless Onslaught](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dauntless+Onslaught) and [Triton Tactics](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triton+Tactics) on his Rider and Hoplite was just enough to seal the game.

Game two was more drawn out.

[Hopeful Eidolon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hopeful+Eidolon) on [Wingsteed Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingsteed+Rider) gave Park an early lead, right before [Voyage's End](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyage%27s+End) copied by [Meletis Charlatan](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meletis+Charlatan) negated it.

Park also had an answer for [Hundred-Handed One](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hundred-Handed+One) in [Glare of Heresy](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glare+of+Heresy), right before he summoned his own copy of the giant. He secured his lead with a pair of [Wingsteed Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingsteed+Rider) and ways to target them, though Fabien was able to put up quite a struggle by tapping down with [Wavecrash Triton](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wavecrash+Triton) and some bounce spells.

In the end though, the Park's sheer board presence was sufficient to win out.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gpkl14/s_li.jpg)

*Fabien Li*

Fabien never lost control of the rubber game.

Park's [Blood-Toll Harpy](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood-Toll+Harpy) was one-upped by [Wingsteed Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingsteed+Rider) and [Hopeful Eidolon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hopeful+Eidolon). Then [Ordeal of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ordeal+of+Erebos) met [Celestial Archon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140125000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Celestial+Archon) and Emissary of Heliod.

Park found a way to deal with the Archon in Devine Verdict, but by then he was too far behind in board position to get back in the game.

Park beats Park 2-1 and is a Grand Prix Kuala Lumpur finalist.
