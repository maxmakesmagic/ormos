Last weekend, seventy-one countries across the globe ran their first of three [World **Magic** Cup Qualifiers](/en/events/2012/qualifiers/2012-world-magic-cup-qualifiers). If you missed last weekend's event in your country, don't worry! Two more qualifiers will run, on the weekends of April 20–21 and June 1–2. We're going to take a look at some of the Top 8 decks from those WMCQs, which is relevant for those of you hoping to win a Pro Tour *Theros* Qualifier… because just like the PTQ season, every WMCQ uses Standard! Find the nearest Standard PTQ to you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#theros)! Good luck; have fun!
