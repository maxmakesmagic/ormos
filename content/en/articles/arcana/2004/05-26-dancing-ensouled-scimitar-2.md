Originally printed in *Arabian Nights*, **Dancing Scimitar** hasn't been in print since *Sixth Edition*. So you may not recognize the homage represented by *Fifth Dawn's* **Ensouled Scimitar**.

The flavor of Ensouled Scimitar is that it's an equippable weapon like Leonin Scimitar, but with a little mana it becomes animated, flying around and fighting for you like Dancing Scimitar! The flavor is reinforced by the fact that the equipped creature gets +1/+5, "adding" Dancing Scimitar's power and toughness to the creature.
