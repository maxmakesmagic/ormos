While the potency of Miracles – the Jeskai version now defining all the Miracles archetype overall – was the talk of the town in a post-[Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) Legacy world, it was [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets) and [Shardless Agent](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shardless+Agent) that have returned in spades.

While the Delver decks covered a variety of angles – from [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer) meets [Gurmag Angler](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gurmag+Angler) Grixis to [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) meets [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) Temur and [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay) meets [Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang) Sultai – the common thread connecting them all was a stockpile of Legacy's best one- and two-mana instants and sorceries to back up a turn one [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets). Death by [Insectile Aberration](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Insectile+Aberration) was a common theme throughout Day 1.

Shardless Sultai was similar to many Delver builds – [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm)s and removal – but layers in things like [Ancestral Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Vision)s and [Baleful Strix](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baleful+Strix) to provide resources and removal while setting up their endgame, often leading up to a [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) set to ultimate.

Miracles of course had made a splash, presenting [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance)-meets-[Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top) throughout the day as well. Close behind included Death and Taxes, Burn, Elves, Lands and [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=174915) [Tendrils](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=383125), all staples that never seemed to truly dwindle at large Legacy events.

Buried deep in the Day 2 breakdown were some fascinating gems as well. Patrick Chapin and Matt Spering, together in collaboration with Tom Martell, both made Day 2 with a unique twist on Esper built around [Monastery Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Mentor). [Aluren](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aluren) and Goblin Prison – [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) and [Trinisphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trinisphere) meets [Goblin Settler](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Settler)s into [Siege-Gang Commander](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege-Gang+Commander) – went undefeated alongside a 12 Post deck that can cast impressive and expensive threats like [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Aeons+Torn) and [Karn Liberated](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn+Liberated).

With staples and off-the-radar choices leading the way it was still any deck's tournament to take.
