Check out the full art of *Future Sight's* wind-controlling legend, Linessa, Zephyr Mage.

![Linessa_Zephyr_Mage_640](https://i.ormos.uk/a5/c0/a5c093504b664a3c690f44856683482f83df5979467954e97130187cf50802fa.jpg)  
*Linessa, Zephyr Mage art by Jim Murray*

Linessa is a descendant of Alexi, Zephyr Mage (or will be, when that possible future comes to pass—only time will tell when that occurs). Note that she carries the same staff that Alexi held. It's held up well, but shows its age in a bit more character and texture.

Also note the building in the background of Linessa's art. That's part of [Tolaria West](/en/articles/arcana/2007/05-30-tolaria-west), a new mystical academy built among the storm-swept Spice Islands of Dominaria (since the old [Academy was destroyed](https://i.ormos.uk/5d/e9/5de9e52d0ebecc231256b30473c6e80aa639d80517c758740754ea98a2289922.jpg)). Linessa could be very useful in helping the fledgling academy face initial obstacles. Seriously, someone with some mastery of wind magic needs to do something about those [acid rainstorms](/en/articles/arcana/2007/03-12-blues-slag-islands-2)...
