[Thermopod](https://web.archive.org/web/20060803000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Thermopod) is a snow creature, but it super-heats its underside to glide over the ice. [Surging Flame](https://web.archive.org/web/20060803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Surging+Flame) depicts an orc shaman surrounded by snow and freezing rain, yet he summons a gout of spiraling flame on his enemies. There is definitely a tension between hot and cold in *Coldsnap*-era Terisiare, with Heidar and his Rimewind wizards at the center of it.

> *“There are deep, deep places in this world that have long burned hot with little regard for the whitening of the earth's thin skin." —[Sek'Kuar, Deathkeeper](https://web.archive.org/web/20060803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sek%27Kuar%2C+Deathkeeper)*  
>  ([Deepfire Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20060803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deepfire+Elemental))

[Braid of Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20060803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Braid+of+Fire) depicts a shaman so focused on his meditation that he has melted the snow around him.

![Braid_of_Fire_640](https://i.ormos.uk/4a/af/4aafdc138342373dbd447a91b7d75758377c0fc1631eb58858cd8d900fb0e9fe.jpg)  
*[Braid of Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20060803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Braid+of+Fire) art by Cyril Van Der Haegen*

[Thermal Flux](https://web.archive.org/web/20060803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thermal+Flux) shows a rocky outcropping draped in a chilly shawl of snow, but also thawing from the sun's heat. (Hi, Cantrip Week!)

![Thermal_Flux_640](https://i.ormos.uk/7e/7f/7e7fee8022c40efa61f08d8149f8d7c4e9cb09a572b3ca44e1aa06662ff3a247.jpg)  
*[Thermal Flux](https://web.archive.org/web/20060803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thermal+Flux) art by Jeff Miracola*

There's quite the thermal jumble going on in the art of [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20060803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance), in which a spell has annihiliated with its illusionary counterpart, sort of like a matter-antimatter reaction.

![Counterbalance_640](https://i.ormos.uk/f9/5a/f95af95426fd331583eac6c4df3c300eccfc2bd26fc2fb0fa0b733b688966033.jpg)  
[Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20060803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance) art by John Zeleznik

> *Deep beneath the ice, beneath the soil and the rock, Dominaria's fire still burned hot.*  
>  ([Rite of Flame](https://web.archive.org/web/20060803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rite+of+Flame))
