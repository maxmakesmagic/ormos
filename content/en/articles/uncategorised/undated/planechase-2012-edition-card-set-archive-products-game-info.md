## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/09/a9/09a9615e2ac15d0276d951d6ff15ebb241082d3220aaca610b552eb14e0a34d8.png)Each of the four *Planechase* game packs contains one 60-card deck, one 10-card planar deck, one planar die, and a Planechase strategy insert with multiplayer rules. Each deck has eight new plane cards and two new phenomenon cards, for a total of 32 new planes and eight new phenomena.

 

**Set Name:** *Planechase* (2012 Edition)

**Number of Cards:** 60 per deck, 240 total

**Number of Oversized Cards:** 40

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, and Japanese

**Official Three-Letter Code:** PC2

[Read “Planechase Rule Revealed”](https://web.archive.org/web/20160709214626/http://magic.wizards.com/node/683856)

[Read “The Planes of Planechase”](https://web.archive.org/web/20160709214626/http://magic.wizards.com/node/692686)

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Mark Globus (lead), Kelly Digges, Dave Guskin, and Ryan Miller

**Final Game Design and Development:** Mark L. Gottlieb (lead), Ryan Miller, Lee Sharpe, and Ken Troop, with contributions from Matt Tabak
