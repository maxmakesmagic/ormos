It's the first Tuesday in preview week! Around here, that means it's time to unveil the *Magic 2015* packaging and all of the veiled secrets those images contain. Hints abound as we look at the wrappers, boxes, and containers that hold all of those sweet, sweet *Magic 2015* cards.

Let's start with images on the front of the booster packs you'll be ripping open.

Boosters

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/EN_MTGM15_Boo_01_01.png)

Didn't we just see Ajani on Theros? Either there's a leonin running around that looks just like him, or Ajani's up to something in *Magic 2015*.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/EN_MTGM15_Boo_02_01.png)

Hm, a blue wizard-looking fellow with a cloak and a prime spot on the packaging. I wonder what that means?

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/EN_MTGM15_Boo_03_01.png)

Ghostly flame-looking skeleton guy? Check.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/EN_MTGM15_Boo_04_01.png)

We've actually seen her before. Soul of Shandalar was previewed in [Aaron Forsythe's article](/en/articles/feature/2014/06-23-magic-2015its-in-there) yesterday.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/EN_MTGM15_Boo_05_01.png)

Just an elf, nothing more to see here...right? This elf feels somehow...familiar.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/EN_MTGM15_BstrDsp_03.png)

Sample Decks

A bit ago, we gave you the decklists for the [Sample Decks](https://web.archive.org/web/20140624000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/1515) that will be going out with M15. Now you can see them all in package form!

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/EN_MTGM15_SmplDkTkBx_01_03.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/EN_MTGM15_SmplDkTkBx_02_03.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/EN_MTGM15_SmplDkTkBx_03_03.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/EN_MTGM15_SmplDkTkBx_04_03.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/EN_MTGM15_SmplDkTkBx_05_03.png)

Finally, when you pick up a Fat Pack, you'll see all of this, punctuated by Garruk's intimidating visage staring back at you, pretty much no matter where you look.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/EN_MTGM15_FtPkFmly.png)

So what about the Intro Packs, you might ask? Well, you’ll just have to wait a little longer, as we have a special medium for showing off the cards headlining those coming Monday.
