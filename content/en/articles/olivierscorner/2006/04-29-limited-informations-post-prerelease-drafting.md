![](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/ads/Be%20Oc%20Drafting.jpg)After the prerelease week end comes the time for drafting. After a couple of two on two drafts at home, I left for Amsterdam where I am currently staying, at Julien Nuijten’s place. We’re forming a little colony there, as Julien’s parents are away until Prague. I arrived in the early afternoon and met Wessel Oomens and Julien. The next to arrive were Geoffrey Siron and Bernardo Da Costa Cabral, followed soon after by Frank Karsten and Jelger Wiegersma. It was 8 PM, practicing could begin. We went for a six man draft as Wessel had to leave early. 

  
  
  
  

### Ravnica

![](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/ads/Be%20Oc%20Mark.jpg)On my first pack I opened two of the top Ravnica’s uncommons: Mark of Eviction and Moldervine Cloak. After a long thinking, I went for Mark, which is a little less good, but I like blue a lot more than green and I thought cutting blue could make my left neighbors feed me with Izzet cards on pack 2.  
 The best two card of the next pack were Stinkweed Imp and Golgari Rotwurm. The thing was that I wanted to try and end up UW, so I went for Conclave Equenaut. My aim was to cut the blue and white cards in *Ravnica*, in order to be fed Orzhov or Izzet, the best two guilds in Guildpact, by my right neighbors. Then, as Simic and Rakdos as the two more popular guilds in *Dissension*, I would probably be passed many blue/white flyers in the last pack. Pick three was pretty easy as Auratouched Mage was by far the best card. I then had to choose between Plague Boiler and Farseek as there were no good cards in the other colors. Once again I went for the second best card in Farseek, as Boiler requires green and black to be a really good card. The most notable cards I received then were Nullmage Shepherd and Copy Enchantment. Unfortunately I didn’t get a third white card, so my strategy seemed a little hopeless at this point.

Guildpact![](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/ads/Be%20Oc%20Savage.jpg)Shall I give up White? I haven’t got a lot of white cards so far. The thing is I am Green Blue for sure, so if I want to go White, I need to pick Orzhov cards in pack two. After one pack, my only mana fixer is a Farseek. Decent but a little light, I’ll take a decision after my first 3 picks.  
 I open Blind Hunter, but also Savage Twister, which I pick considering that (1) the card itself is better (2) I have a better chance to receive Izzet than Orzhov after what I passed in *Ravnica*. Pick two is difficult as I receive Douse in Gloom, Blind Hunter, and Silhana Starfletcher as only UGR card. I confirm my decision to give up white and go for UGR by picking the mana fixer.  
 Pick three isn’t easy either as I am passed Repeal, Izzet Chronarch and Izzet Signet. I won’t pick the creature as I don’t have many instants and sorceries yet, and I eventually opt for the fixer, which I will need at some point. Not much to say about my following picks, Repeal and Ogre Savant, nor about my final picks in the pack, not interesting enough to be shared except for an Izzet Boilerworks.

### Dissension

![](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/ads/Be%20Oc%20Helium.jpg)Helium Squirter and Simic Guildmage are solid picks one and two. Having them, I decide to try and pick as much graft as possible. Minister of Impediment is good third choice, but it is almost my last good card in the draft. I also select 2 Vigean Hydropon I probably pick a little too high, but I want to see what’s they’re worth.  
 I also receive a pretty late Kindle the Carnage. It’s not Twister, but I now have two mass destruction removals.  
  
 I end up with my weakest deck in the format, as my creatures aren’t consistent, and my removals almost invisible. I’ve two good points: a solid mana, and Savage Twister, but that’s about it.  
  
 I end up 1-2, losing to Geoffrey and Frank 2-0 and beating Bernardo with the same score.  
 What went wrong exactly? When did my deck start being crap? My main mistake was my first pick. I love Mark, it has been pretty good on the matches I’ve played tonight, but it’s not as good as it was in RRR or in RRG. There are way less combos with the one mana aura in the last pack. Therefore, I should definitely have taken Cloak, followed by Golgari Rotwurm, Plague Boiler and Elves of Deep Shadow (over Fists). When you pick the second or the third best card in every pack, it is no longer a bet or any kind of a risk, it is just a mistake.  
 Then, another important thing, it is crucial to playtest new cards or draft strategies in practicing, better than in a tournament, but you shall not test everything at once. Forcing UW, forcing Graft, trying 2 Vigean Hydropon is good, you have to see if these things work, but don’t do everything at the same time, it usually ends up with a crappy deck.  
 For now I’m going to stop forcing archetypes, I’ll just take the drafts as they come, and I’ll wait until I know the format better to try and decide what to draft before seeing the cards.
