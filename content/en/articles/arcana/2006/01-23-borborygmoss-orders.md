Borborygmos (sound it out -- it's quite pronounceable, and actually rather fun to say), a cyclops the size of some of Ravnica's tallest towers, is the Gruul guild leader. He holds court over Gruul's motley clans and smashes foes with a maul made from a slab of masonry tangled in the roots of its tree-trunk handle.

![Borborygmos art by Todd Lockwood](https://i.ormos.uk/84/a1/84a15e58021d3e2bcd8e0ed8da9450b813755bd2ff3aec176637a90491106509.jpg)  
*Borborygmos art by Todd Lockwood*

Check out his improvised metal bracer on his left arm: it's constructed from dozens of scavenged shields, some marked with the signets of other guilds.

In Borborygmos's flavor text, fellow legend Teysa, Orzhov Scion weighs in with a snarky comment about the Gruul clanlord:

<table><tbody><tr><td><i>“It’s easy to see why those Gruul dirtbags follow him—the only orders he gives are ‘Crush them!’ and ‘We eat!’”<br> —Teysa</i></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>
