After nine rounds of Sealed and two drafts, eight players topped the standings, ready to battle for the trophy in the GP Top 8. Congratulations to the GP DFW Top 8 competitors!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpdfw18/gpdfw18_t8_group.jpg)

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpdfw18/gpdfw18_t8_rubin.jpg)

**Name:** Aaron Rubin

**Hometown:** Pasadena, CA

**Occupation:** Accountant

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

4th place SCG Open

**What color combination did you play on Day 1, and what was your best card?**

WB – [Demonlord Belzenlok](https://web.archive.org/web/20180506000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonlord+Belzenlok)

**What color combinations did you draft on Day 2, and what were your best cards?**

Draft 1 - WB, [Lyra Dawnbringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20180506000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lyra+Dawnbringer)

Draft 2 - RW, [Goblin Barrage](https://web.archive.org/web/20180506000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Barrage)

**What's the most exciting play you've made in Dominaria Limited?**

[Fungal Infection](https://web.archive.org/web/20180506000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fungal+Infection) into [Vicious Offering](https://web.archive.org/web/20180506000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vicious+Offering) on camera in Round 12

**What would winning GP DFW mean to you?**

I really just wanted to qualify for the PT. This is gravy. But it would be a fantastic accomplishment.

Draft 2 - Red-White, Keldon Marauder? The Pegasus? [Run Amok](https://web.archive.org/web/20180506000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Run+Amok)?

Hard to parse at this point, but it would mean a lot more toward our first house.
