Here are two related cards in ***Magic** 2013*.

<table><tbody><tr><td><div><div><img alt="Healer of the Pride" src="http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&amp;name=Healer+of+the+Pride"></div></div></td><td><div><div><img alt="Ajani, Caller of the Pride" src="http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&amp;name=Ajani%2C+Caller+of+the+Pride"></div></div></td></tr></tbody></table>

So there are both feline characters associated with a pride. But is it the *same* pride? The answer is yes! First, look at the circlet on [Healer of the Pride](https://web.archive.org/web/20120806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Healer+of+the+Pride)'s chest.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/52/86/52868235fcdf6f5ebc703413f2b9e61f33d8e759f3a56d70f9cabb4f9b4c0569.jpg)

[Healer of the Pride](https://web.archive.org/web/20120806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Healer+of+the+Pride) | Art by [Christopher Moeller](https://web.archive.org/web/20120806000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Christopher+Moeller%22%5D)

Now, look at the circlet on [Ajani, Caller of the Pride](https://web.archive.org/web/20120806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ajani%2C+Caller+of+the+Pride)'s shoulder.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/cc/7e/cc7e0cd59a5ba488453c53249df0ebbe4b2919ba958fc1623163bb3db2426fbf.jpg)

[Ajani, Caller of the Pride](https://web.archive.org/web/20120806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ajani%2C+Caller+of+the+Pride) | Art by [D. Alexander Gregory](https://web.archive.org/web/20120806000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22D.+Alexander+Gregory%22%5D)

Same circlet? Same pride!
