After fifteen rounds of Swiss, the field of 1082 was down to just eight! Let's get to know them a little, and have them tell us about their amazing weekend.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmem18/gpmem18_miller.jpg)

**Name:** Blake Miller

**Hometown:** Hartselle, Alabama

**Age:** 27

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Top 32 at Grand Prix Atlanta

**Day 1 Record:** 7-1 (1 bye)

**Day 2 Record:** 6-0-1

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Mardu Vehicles. I know the deck better than any other in the format. [Walking Ballista](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Walking+Ballista) was the best card all weekend.

**Did any particular card or deck surprise you this weekend?**

[Profane Procession](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Profane+Procession).

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

This is only my second Grand Prix, so it still hasn’t sunk in yet!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmem18/gpmem18_tenjum.jpg)

**Name:** Andrew Tenjum

**Hometown:** Madison, Wisconsin

**Age:** 24

**Occupation:** Student

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** 23rd at Pro Tour Dragons of Tarkir and 2nd at SCG Invitational.

**Day 1 Record:** 7-1 (2 byes)

**Day 2 Record:** 6-1

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Grixis Energy. It is the best [The Scarab God](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Scarab+God) deck.

**Did any particular card or deck surprise you this weekend?**

[Confiscation Coup](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Confiscation+Coup) stealing my [The Scarab God](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Scarab+God) in the mirror match.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

A chance to play on the Pro Tour again.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmem18/gpmem18_schroeder.jpg)

**Name:** Tyler Schroeder

**Hometown:** Iowa City, Iowa

**Age:** 26

**Occupation:** Mathematician

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** [Stubborn Denial](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stubborn+Denial) targeting [Violent Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Violent+Outburst)

**Day 1 Record:** 7-1 (2 byes)

**Day 2 Record:** 6-1

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Red-Green Monsters. It’s resilient and abrasive.

**Did any particular card or deck surprise you this weekend?**

Some crazy five-color ramp deck.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmem18/gpmem18_heath.jpg)

**Name:** Heath Vance

**Hometown:** Clarksville, Tennessee

**Age:** 30

**Occupation:** Between jobs, just got out of the Army.

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** PPTQ win!

**Day 1 Record:** 6-2 (2 byes)

**Day 2 Record:** 7-0

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Blue-Black Control, because I feel it is the strongest deck and it is also able to beat Mono-Red. [The Scarab God](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Scarab+God) and Vraska’s [Contempt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Contempt) were my best cards this weekend.

**Did any particular card or deck surprise you this weekend?**

Mastermind Ramp with main deck [Carnage Tyrant](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Carnage+Tyrant).

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

I qualified for my first Pro Tour.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmem18/gpmem18_barich.jpg)

**Name:** Aaron Barich

**Hometown:** Gulfport, Mississippi

**Age:** 21

**Occupation:** Bum

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** 5 SCG Open Top 8s

**Day 1 Record:** 8-0 (2 byes)

**Day 2 Record:** 4-1-2

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Sultai Constrictor. Overall, the matchups are good and it is great against midrange decks with [The Scarab God](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Scarab+God). Hadana’s Climb and [Blossoming Defense](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blossoming+Defense) were great!

**Did any particular card or deck surprise you this weekend?**

Mardu with [Snubhorn Sentry](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snubhorn+Sentry)

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

Another chance to win a Pro Tour.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmem18/gpmem18_froehlich.jpg)

**Name:** Eric Froehlich

**Hometown:** Las Vegas, Nevada, USA

**Age:** 34

**Occupation:** I don’t believe in jobs.

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Pro Tour Hall of Fame, 5 Pro Tour Top 8s, 16 previous Grand Prix Top 8s.

**Day 1 Record:** 7-1 (3 byes)

**Day 2 Record:** 5-0-2

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Blue-Black Control. In a world where most things are even, a better mana base and card drawing is king. [Torrential Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrential+Gearhulk) was my best card!

**Did any particular card or deck surprise you this weekend?**

[Scrapheap Scrounger](https://web.archive.org/web/20180226000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scrapheap+Scrounger) was tough to deal with.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

I’m going to the Pro Tour!
