Consider the case of the humble [Ballynock Trapper](https://web.archive.org/web/20080716000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ballynock+Trapper):

![Ballynock Trapper](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ballynock+Trapper)

Clearly, she's setting a trap for something. But what kind of trap? Let's take a closer look!

[![Ballynock Trapper](https://i.ormos.uk/ab/e6/abe6f7e650c0a45c5585ae1beb3074326adbb7b753d3f89c5eaa3578d04ccb5c.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20080716000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Ballynock%2BTrapper)

Do you recognize the trap? 

It's our old friend [Trip Noose](https://web.archive.org/web/20080716000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trip+Noose)!
