At the StarCityGames.com Standard Open in Boston, Anthony Patronick used Mountains to take the top spot. Now that *Innistrad* is entering the world of Standard and *Zendikar* block is leaving, Anthony's deck will take some major retooling to make it in the new environment. What changes? Can Stromkirk Noble replace Goblin Guide? Will new burn make the cut over the departing Lightning Bolt? How would you change Anthony's deck for the new world of Standard?
