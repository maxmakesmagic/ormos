![Magic's 10th Anniversary](https://i.ormos.uk/ac/c6/acc6f7c7b2067f206b8f95bd792d984f8d8ad1627c2a1ffeb0129250c6637a43.jpg)*This year, **Magic: The Gathering®** celebrates its 10th Anniversary. Tell us about your experiences playing **Magic** — and maybe win cool prizes at the same time!*

If you’ve been playing **Magic®** for a while, you probably have several exciting or funny stories about the games that you've played, places you've visited and the people you’ve met. We want these stories.

  

### What do you need to do?

Tell us a story that fits into one of the following categories: 

*   Tell us about the best game of **Magic** you ever played and why it was so great.
*   Tell us about the best place you have played **Magic** (i.e. on the top of the Eiffel Tower, in the Rain Forest, on an airplane flying to Japan
*   What is the best trip you took to go play **Magic**
*   What do you think is the best **Magic** card and why

You can make it up to 500 words -- try to be as detailed as possible.

### What will we do with the stories?

For the 10th Anniversary, we’re organizing a large Public Relations campaign. Your stories will be used in that campaign. We will send them to journalists so they can use them in stories about **Magic®** around the world.

### What can you win?

Next to the possibility of being featured in leading magazines and newspapers the world over, we will also post a selection at [www.magicthegathering.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20030609000000/http://www.wizards.com/www.magicthegathering.com) on a regular basis.

As if the fame wasn’t enough… 

The best story out of each category will win an exciting trip to the **Magic: The Gathering®** World Championships in Berlin (August 6th – 10th). You’ll get a round-trip coach airfare for you and a guest and stay in a hotel chosen by us during the time of the event (4 nights, breakfast included). During your stay you’ll not only experience the excitement of a top-level **Magic®** tournament, but you’ll be able to compete in the many on-site side events. But there’s more! You’ll also have a free slot to play in the on-site Vintage draft side event! 

The 4 runner-up stories in each category will receive an exclusive **Magic®** Pocket Album and a display (36 boosters) of random **Magic: The Gathering®** booster packs.

### What next?

Interested? Click over to the Online Entry Form and start typing us your story. Don’t forget to include links to your pictures (If you have them) in your story!

### [ONLINE ENTRY FORM](https://web.archive.org/web/20040910220237/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/promo/10annivstory)

For instructions on how to enter via snail mail, go to the [MAIL IN ENTRY](https://web.archive.org/web/20041108101509/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/promo/10annivstorychild) page.

**Important: all entries need to be submitted in English.**

Make sure we receive your entry by Sunday **July 6th, 2003** before midnight (12am), Pacific Standard Time.

We will review all entries and the Grand Prize winners will be contacted before **July 25th, 2003**. Other prizes will be sent before the end of August. Stories will be posted on the site around this time as well.

*This promotion is only open to residents of Belgium, Canada, Denmark, France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Norway, Spain, Sweden, United Kingdom, and United States. Due to legal restrictions, persons under the age of 13 may participate, but are ineligible for contest prizes.*
