**Masayasu Tanahashi**

**Hometown:** Niigata  
**Age:** 24  
**Profession:** Company employee

**What deck did you play, who designed it, and what was the best card in it?**  
 I played a Kithkin deck designed by Aikora. My best card was Windbrisk Heights.

**Which card do you think is currently the most undervalued in *Eventide*?**  
 Drain the Well.

**In your opinion, which *Eventide* card has had the most influence on Constructed?**  
 Figure of Destiny.

**What interests do you have other than *Magic*?**  
 I like the Japanese comedy duo “Sekai no Nabeatsu”.

**What is your most lasting impression from Grand Prix Kobe?**  
 Not getting Mirrorweave off even once in the Swiss.

**What are your other major *Magic* achievements?**  
 None.

**What are your other major non-*Magic* achievements?**  
 None.

**Shou Yoshimori**

**Hometown:** Chiba  
**Age:** 24  
**Profession:** Company employee

**What deck did you play, who designed it, and what was the best card in it?**  
 I played a traditional Doran, the Siege Tower I designed myself. My best combo was Leaf-Crowned Elder with Doran, the Siege Tower.

**Which card do you think is currently the most undervalued in *Eventide*?**  
 I don’t know yet. 

**In your opinion, which *Eventide* card has had the most influence on Constructed?**  
 Figure of Destiny and Unmake.

**What interests do you have other than *Magic*?**  
 Acapella and games in general.

**What is your most lasting impression from Grand Prix Kobe?**  
 In the 8th round, I was playing against Quick and Toast, and managed to pull out a win after getting hit by Profane Command.

**What are your other major *Magic* achievements?**  
 I made Top 8 at three different GP Kobe Trials. I’ve also played in the Finals.

**What are your other major non-*Magic* achievements?**  
 Performing acapella throughout university.

 For Block Constructed, Stillmoon Cavalier. For Standard, Figure of Destiny.

 Jazz, the works of Yukio Mishima.

 I was playing Shintarou Ishimura. He went first and played Devoted Druid followed by Chameleon Colossus, then Cloudthresher, but I still won.

 I’ve lost a lot.

 None...says a friend of mine.
