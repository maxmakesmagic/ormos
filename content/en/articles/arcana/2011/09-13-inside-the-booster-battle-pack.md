Now that it's on shelves (for $9.99), let's talk in a little more detail about what's inside the [Booster Battle Packs](https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/announcing-booster-battle-packs-2011-06-23).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ce/93/ce93c4964acedbdcc48a1a4110665c4ea1d335c853fd5a5bdc29847370149dd4.jpg)  

That package contains everything you need for a two-player **Magic** game. When you rip it open, you get two boxes:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/85/a4/85a45eef5e10032c48aa42b915e6b0b069435aefac7429b2e83b68d5865d9a4d.jpg)  

In each box, there's a ***Magic** 2012* Booster Pack and two color-specific groups of cards. At this point, we're going to move from the world of theory to the world of acutality. Which is a fancy (and possibly inaccurate) way of saying I played a Booster Battle Pack game against Daily MTG editor Kelly Digges. So from here on out, you'll be seeing what I opened.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2d/77/2d77ce91b48278464c888bad3cbcbad41f880088ea555d6a2de205a1a0a56e11.jpg)  

My two colors were red and black while Kelly had blue and white. So I had five red cards, five black cards, five mountains, and five swamps. The next step was to open up my booster pack and select up to five cards.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2b/2a/2b2ac6e2c0653f615eadced3cfe813e2922915bccdc28d5e38b783d6b78c5332.jpg)  

That will do nicely!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b1/73/b173af4d8a674f06b9e62edb18fe3ba5b5d01f2fe4b2d507744c284aed6777ba.jpg)  

I started the game with a fairly unexciting [Goblin Tunneler](https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Tunneler).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/30/94/3094d8f159ee3aa0b00c35ac598c59d351e0fafb1a94ca960c9adebc6fdf7d48.jpg)  

The addition of [Fiery Hellhound](https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Hellhound) gave me an unblockable four damage per turn, if that's what I wanted to spend my time doing. And it was!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/43/f8/43f8a1001cfb817c8dc2ef010efe2f1c8dec6e75e7e2139a7cce0bf7feb7197f.jpg)  

Meanwhile, Kelly had played [Merfolk Mesmerist](https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merfolk+Mesmerist) and was using it to whittle away at my library. And considering that my library was only 25 cards to begin with, it was a pretty good plan. And then he got his white cards working together.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5f/6c/5f6cf54cfbf3edca94086390963ad534fa91d9d8e7d7a75337940dba8f01f286.jpg)  

The griffin plan was okay in principle, but I was way ahead on damage, so they were mostly ornamental. And then my bomb appeared, putting Kelly on a deadline.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7d/77/7d779812c9a3993c8b4677f9140cd8932e802c8f5ebbba3570054a617406f19c.jpg)  

Boom! [Inferno Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inferno+Titan)! Kelly was knocked down to five life and had to scramble a bit to stay alive. He played [Aether Adept](https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Adept) to bounce my [Fiery Hellhound](https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Hellhound), so the only damage he couldn't stop was a [Tormented Soul](https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tormented+Soul) (for one damage) and the [Inferno Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inferno+Titan)'s three, should I choose to throw all the damage at Kelly's face. He'd be at one life, but he'd still be alive until his turn.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2c/07/2c07fa1498a077057a58d9c9502146c74c41a4e3c4fd50d4acb331f22ca9356e.jpg)  

...or so he thought. This picture shows Kelly thinking through the options, never dreaming that the [Taste of Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Taste+of+Blood) in my hand was about to kill him.

Sorry! That turned into a game recap, didn't it? Hopefully, it has given you a feel for what to do with the Booster Battle Pack.
