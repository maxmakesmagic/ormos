This was the Mono-Black Control deck that Hall of Famer Zvi Mowshowitz played at the US National Championship in 2003. While it features a host of cards that are no longer legal in Standard, its core strengths—killing creatures and gaining card advantage—are still around. Now that you've had the chance to pick up some [shiny new *Innistrad* cards](https://web.archive.org/web/20110927000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&set=+[%22Innistrad%22]) at the Prerelease this past weekend, how would you reimagine Zvi's classic take on this archetype with the new set for Standard?
