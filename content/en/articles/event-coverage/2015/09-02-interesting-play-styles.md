Everyone knows that different people have different styles of play. In an effort to find out more about what constitutes a "Play Style", players who made Day 2 at Grand Prix - Kobe were given a questionnaire and asked to describe the way they play the game. Below is a list of some of the more unexpected responses. We here at Sideboard.com hope they encourage you, the reader, to be just as introspective.
