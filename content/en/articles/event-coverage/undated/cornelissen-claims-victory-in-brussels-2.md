![](https://i.ormos.uk/91/7c/917c1a333aa906ca0717cb19ad02d44a9fc3f6565d48a73c1ce5dda8ad7b696e.jpg) Kamiel Cornelissen fought his way through a Top 8 full of **Magic** celebrities, first beating Alexandre Peset in the quarterfinals, then Raphael Levy in the semis, and finally besting Gabriel Nassif in an epic three-game match.

Whereas today most players had opted for aggressive strategies in the brand new *Shadowmoor* Booster Draft format, the two finalists were prepared to take the fight to the lategame. There, Cornelissen's blue-black construction, featuring [Isleback Spawn](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Isleback+Spawn) and [Ghastlord of Fugue](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghastlord+of+Fugue), triumphed over Nassif's red-green ramp-style deck.

Congratulations to Kamiel Cornelissen, the champion of Grand Prix Brussels 2008!

## 9:00 a.m. - Drafting with Antoine

*by André Coimbra*

From time to time the argument comes up that Sealed Deck is a format where only luck plays a major roll and that even the good players can't do anything with a bad card pool. However, at the end of nine rounds of Sealed Deck play, there were four French top players ready to draft at the first table of the Grand Prix: Antoine Ruel, Amiel Tenenbaum, Gabriel Nassif, and Raphael Levy. So... is it all about luck?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/82/f0/82f0ffd706acf0cf8303aa54034bbafe72b139b439e67f53cd6fc6d593cf939c.jpg)Antoine Ruel is one of the most popular players on **Magic: the Gathering**'s competitive scene, having under his belt wins at every kind of major tournaments, so following his draft is always a path for learning and improving.

Yesterday we asked some quick questions about the pros' choice for the top rare, uncommon and common in the set. Antoine's pick for top uncommon was [Incremental Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Incremental+Blight) and he was quite happy when his first booster offered exactly that. After picking this great black card, Antoine decided to to stay on the "dark side", taking as much black as possible. When later asked why, his answer was: "Black is the worst color." Most players just consider their cards and boosters to make their decisions while drafting, but here Antoine was thinking one level ahead and he was aware that the other pros at the table would probably stay out of black, because in their opinion it's the worst color.

Out of the rest of the first booster, he took some hybrid cards that could be played in a black and red deck, as well as some artifacts, but the picks were not very interesting overall.

In the second booster, however, Antoine got a second pick [Burn Trail](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burn+Trail), which is his favorite common in the set and he kept to his plan of going deep into black. He chose red as his second color and tried to get as much color synergy as possible with cards like: [Emberstrike Duo](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emberstrike+Duo), [Sootwalkers](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sootwalkers) and [Sootstoke Kindler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sootstoke+Kindler).

The third booster started quite bad for him, as he was really unhappy to take [Emberstrike Duo](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emberstrike+Duo) as his first pick. He continued with [Spiteflame Witch](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spiteflame+Witch) and a mid-pack [Power of Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Power+of+Fire). At this point the knew that something had gone wrong, as after a few picks the boosters were already "dry".

After deck construction he was feeling that the deck was okay and that he liked his chances in the draft. Keep following the coverage to see how that turned out!

## Feature Match: Round 10 - Olivier Ruel vs. Jan Doise

*by Tobias Henke*

Both players are currently at 7-2, with one more loss equalling certain elimination from Top 8 contention.

**Game 1**

 

![Helm of the Ghastlord](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Helm+of+the+Ghastlord)

Olivier won the die-roll, chose to play first and kept his opening seven. Jan kept as well and both players were off to completely mirrored first two turns: Both had [Swamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swamp)s and [Inkfathom Witch](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inkfathom+Witch). But while Ruel had to pass his third turn without any play at all, Doise had [Scuttlemutt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scuttlemutt). Next up, Ruel played [Wanderbrine Rootcutters](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wanderbrine+Rootcutters), while Doise enchanted his Witch with [Helm of the Ghastlord](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Helm+of+the+Ghastlord) and attacked for some cardadvantage.

Ruel returned the favor and played [Gloomlance](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gloomlance) on the ghastly Witch, but Doise was not out of tricks yet. He placed a -1/-1 counter on his [Scuttlemutt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scuttlemutt) via [Scarscale Ritual](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scarscale+Ritual) and then had [Fate Transfer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fate+Transfer) to finish off his opponent's witch.

Jan followed it up with [Gravelgill Axeshark](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravelgill+Axeshark) and yet another [Helm of the Ghastlord](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Helm+of+the+Ghastlord), while Olivier was totally out of gas. He could do nothing but shake his head in disgust and - a few turns later - concede.

Olivier Ruel 0 - 1 Jan Doise

Olivier had to do some massive sideboarding. (After the match he explained, that he really likes his deck - but not in the mirror match-up.)

**Game 2**

Ruel elected to play first again and had [Spiteflame Witch](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spiteflame+Witch) for turn two. Jan meanwhile had [Chainbreaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chainbreaker), a lowly 1/1 at first, but [Fate Transfer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fate+Transfer) quickly changed that at the expense of the Witch.

Things began looking interesting when [Puca's Mischief](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Puca%27s+Mischief) came into play on the French side, but in fact, for the rest of the game it did nothing but lock down the [Chainbreaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chainbreaker), that changed sides every upkeep without ever being around in any player's untap step.

In the end the game was decided by mana problems. While Olivier first had seven, then eight lands, Jan stopped exactly at five and drew spell after spell. Two of his [Gloomlance](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gloomlance)s later this game and match was over...

Jan Doise defeats Olivier Ruel 2- 0.

## 12:30 p.m. - 'Moor Combos!

*by Tobias Henke*

 

![Wort, the Raidmother](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Wort%2C+the+Raidmother)

Evil things abound on the nightmarish plane of *Shadowmoor*...

Take, for example, [Wort, the Raidmother](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wort%2C+the+Raidmother) followed by [Din of the Fireherd](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Din+of+the+Fireherd) and - thanks to Conspire - a copy of [Din of the Fireherd](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Din+of+the+Fireherd) as well. Yes, that's right, two 5/5 monsters enter play on one side, while nine (!) lands and three creatures have to be sacrificed on the other, as witnessed in round five.

Right now the crowd had to watch Tomoharu Saito's downfall in the Feature Match area, brought on by [Steel of the Godhead](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steel+of+the+Godhead), which his opponent, Simon Görtzen, had expertly placed on his [Augury Adept](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Augury+Adept). The attack went uncontested and the Augury's ability revealed... [Armored Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Armored+Ascension)! 8/7 fliers with lifelink, card-drawing capability and even more life-gain are pretty good, after all.

But there's not only the big splashy ones. [Pili-Pala](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pili-Pala) enchanted with [Power of Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Power+of+Fire) has been running rampage today, both cards being common and so easily splashed. Multiple player are commiserating and stories of this two-card Masticore (a better one at that) can be heard all throughout the hall.

## 1:00 p.m. - Drafting with Nassif

*by André Coimbra*

Gabriel Nassif from France is one of the most consistent European players on the professional circuit, from Player of the Year to Pro Tour champion, he has mastered both constructed and limited formats. Currently he is in need of one more win and one draw out of the three rounds of today's second draft.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/04/4a/044afbbb2d479243d44911551f1979f038488ffc1bb7142a7a3c23203679059f.jpg)When Nassif opened his first booster and saw [Plumeveil](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plumeveil) in it, he considered every option available, as well as possible paths that he could take from that point and when the judge told the players to pick a card, it was [Plumeveil](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plumeveil) that got into his card pool.

## Feature Match: Round 14 - Fabio Reinhardt vs. Rogier Kleij

*by Gottlieb Yeh*

 

![Tattermunge Witch](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Tattermunge%20Witch&options=)

Both players have a 12-2 record, so they still are in contention for Top 8. Rogier won the die-roll and chose to go first. After taking a mulligan down to six he starts out with a turn-two [Trip Noose](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trip+Noose) . Fabio answers with a [Tattermunge Witch](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tattermunge+Witch) . Turn three sees a [Wasp Lancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasp+Lancer) on Rogier's side. Fabio's witch attacked and dealt three damage with the help of Viridiscent Wisps, bringing Rogier down to 17.

A [Rustrazor Butcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rustrazor+Butcher) joined Fabio's side. Rogier kept attaking with his flier while tapping Fabio's witch, which got enchanted with [Shield of the Oversoul](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shield+of+the+Oversoul) , to leave the life totals at 14 (Fabio) - 16 (Rogier). Rogier then tapped out to cast [Ghastlord of Fugue](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghastlord+of+Fugue) .

Fabio attacked with both his creatures, getting no block from Rogier's Ghastlord, bringing him down to 12, and then played [Deus of Calamity](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deus+of+Calamity) . Rogier himself attacked with both his creatures, getting Fabio down to seven and letting him discard [Crabapple Cohort](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crabapple+Cohort) . Rogier's flier died to a [Puncture Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Puncture+Bolt) and Fabio attacked for four (Deus getting tapped by [Trip Noose](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trip+Noose) ), bringing Rogier down to eight.

Rogier again attacked with the Ghastlord, bringing Fabio down to three and discarding a [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest) , and added a [Kulrath Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kulrath+Knight) to his team. Fabio didn't draw a solution for any of Rogier's creatures and we are off to game 2.

## Quarterfinals - Gabriel Nassif vs. Gaetan Lefebvre

*By André Coimbra*

After about 15 hours of Swiss rounds, it was time for single elimination mode in order determine who would become the champion of GP Brussels 2008. On this first round of single elimination, two players from France got to play to find out who would play for the championship. Most readers probably know who is Gabriel Nassif, his opponent on the other hand, Gaetan Lefebvre had as his best score a Top 32 at Pro Tour Valencia, but a win at this GP would change everything.

**Game 1**

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ca/b7/cab7b171757da874680c54915a3a4bb2af1881ca6c7d907c349bf6191e716abd.jpg)Gabriel Nassif won the die-roll and [Farhaven Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Farhaven+Elf) was the first creature to come into play, accelerating Nassif's mana. Gaetan played [Leech Bonder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leech+Bonder) and Nassif had [Rosheen Meanderer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rosheen+Meanderer) after attacking with the 1/1 elf. [Rosheen Meanderer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rosheen+Meanderer) got into the red-zone, dealing four damage in a single attack.

[Farhaven Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Farhaven+Elf) got killed on the next turn by [Murderous Redcap](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Murderous+Redcap) . Nassif played [Kulrath Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kulrath+Knight) , which made [Leech Bonder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leech+Bonder) useless while it was in play. Nassif attacked with both creatures, but [Consign to Dream](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Consign+to+Dream) slowed his offense for two turns. Now that [Kulrath Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kulrath+Knight) was not nullifying [Leech Bonder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leech+Bonder) , it was the time for Gaetan to attack and transform his opponent's 4/4 into a 3/3 - this way it was not a good attacker anymore, as a double block from Gaetan would just trade one for one.

To finish the turn he played [Watchwing Scarecrow](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Watchwing+Scarecrow) . Nassif replayed [Kulrath Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kulrath+Knight) and also played his second [Farhaven Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Farhaven+Elf) , reducing the chances of drawing lands in the next turns. Gaetan Lefebvre had unfinished businesses with [Kulrath Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kulrath+Knight) and with [Gloomlance](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gloomlance) he was able to not only kill it, but also to get his opponent to discard a card in the process. Gaetan attacked with Watching [Scar](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scar) ecrow a few times and when he got [Ashenmoor Gouger](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashenmoor+Gouger) to join it, Nassif put all his hope into an alpha strike, but it left his opponent at four life... With no creatures left for defense he got killed on the next turn.

**Gabriel Nassif 0 Gaetan Lefebvre 1**

**Game 2**

Once again, Nassif chose to start the game, but the first creature to come into play was on the other side of the table: Gaetan put [Manaforge Cinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Manaforge+Cinder) into play. Nassif played [Boggart Ram-Gang](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boggart+Ram-Gang) and it was sent into the Red Zone right away. Haste can do such things. Gaetan played [Faerie Macabre](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faerie+Macabre) and attacked with both the Faerie Rogue and the [Manaforge Cinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Manaforge+Cinder) , which got an [Aphotic Wisps](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aphotic+Wisps) .

![](https://i.ormos.uk/78/1c/781cf660f99e491b964dbb2439929a55783d145c465a5b5fa284b24d378c74b9.jpg)In an attempt to not miss a land drop, Gaetan drew two cards off of a [Scarscale Ritual](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scarscale+Ritual) , which killed his [Manaforge Cinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Manaforge+Cinder) , but there were no lands to be found. Nassif kept attacking and played [Kulrath Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kulrath+Knight) . A [Puncture Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Puncture+Bolt) on Gaetan's [Watchwing Scarecrow](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Watchwing+Scarecrow) one turn later was enough to make him concede.

**Gabriel Nassif 1 Gaetan Lefebvre 1**

**Game 3**

Nassif didn't like his first seven cards and got to mulligan, but the next six cards were good enough to keep. This game was a good example of how to win a war of attrition, for both players used their resources to deal with opposing threats. But in the end, Gabriel Nassif prevailed because of [Illuminated Folio](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Illuminated+Folio) , which let him draw more resources until he was ready for a lethal attack.

**Gabriel Nassif 2 Gaetan Lefebvre 1**

## Semifinals - Antoine Ruel vs. Gabriel Nassif

*by Mathias Passin*

It’s seven p.m. local time and while most players still in the building compete in an eight-round Legacy side event, four pros are left in the main event. Apparently, three of them are French.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/37/4a/374ad97cf3e875ac31cd065bec2e79d861318e28159b8735d2353ef8c796e793.jpg)Gabriel had drafted a straight green-red deck featuring two [Kulrath Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kulrath+Knight) , while Antoine’s deck is a blue-red build with two (!) splashed Incremental Growth. Fixing is plenty: Two [Elsewhere Flask](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elsewhere+Flask) , two [Manamorphose](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Manamorphose) and a [Morselhoarder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morselhoarder) can easily provide enough black mana for the gamebreaking sorcery.

After some smalltalk, Antoine won the die-roll and decided to play first.

Neither player had a play for the first turns, besides Antoine cycling through his deck with an [Elsewhere Flask](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elsewhere+Flask) . Gabriel didn’t even have a third land and could only keep himself from discarding by destroying the Flask with [Gleeful Sabotage](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gleeful+Sabotage) .

While Antoine started playing threatening creatures after that, Gabriel could only find a [Devoted Druid](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devoted+Druid) – which was [Puncture Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Puncture+Bolt) ed right away. Finally, a [Firespout](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firespout) cleared the board and gave Gabriel (now on precarious two life) the opportunity to slowly draw himself out of the screw. However, Antoine was able to sneak in two more damage by simply being able to cast more creatures in less turns.

Antoine Ruel 1 – 0 Gabriel Nassif

Both players sideboarded some cards. (Antoine revealed the two [Gloomwidow’s Feast](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gloomwidow%E2%80%99s+Feast) he’s boarding out, as they’re just useless against a red-green deck like Gabriel’s.)

Both players kept their opening seven for the second game. Gabriel went first, but Antoine could play a [Puresight Merrow](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Puresight+Merrow) on turn two with the help of [Manamorphose](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Manamorphose) , while Gab had nothing.

However, this time Gabriel had found lands and two [Farhaven Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Farhaven+Elf) s got him even more. Quite surprisingly, he couldn’t play any creature the following turn, despite his seven lands. Apparently, he’s flooded this time.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/44/c3/44c368e92633d78693c0f86b4a0a46ce556cd9f6544e27be28e1bc3888ee6ba2.jpg)Antoine took to the air with two [Briarberry Cohort](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Briarberry+Cohort) s, that the Elfs couldn’t block. They brought Nassif down to 16, then 12. [Firespout](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firespout) cleared the board and Nassif’s [Mudbrawler Raiders](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mudbrawler+Raiders) entered play and – subsequently – the red-zone. Those kept on ripping lifepoints out of Antoine until he, at nine life, found [Burn Trail](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burn+Trail) (that attempt at the Raiders’ life was thwarted by [Barkshell Blessing](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barkshell+Blessing) ) and [Morselhoarder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morselhoarder) .

Gabriel played [Kulrath Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kulrath+Knight) . Now it was time for [Incremental Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Incremental+Blight) and [Cultbrand Cinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cultbrand+Cinder) to take care of the two victims on Gabriel’s side of the table. But this was not the end: With the help of [Illuminated Folio](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Illuminated+Folio) , Nassif could draw enough cards to keep himself alive at one life. Antoine was even forced to trade his [Morselhoarder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morselhoarder) against [Foxfire Oak](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foxfire+Oak) and eventually, [Gnarled Effigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gnarled+Effigy) took down the last blockers to turn this exciting game to Gabriel’s favor.

Antoine Ruel 1 – 1 Gabriel Nassif

Antoine went first, but with [Boggart Ram-Gang](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boggart+Ram-Gang) , Gabriel had the first play on turn three. He lost his first creature to [Torpor Dust](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torpor+Dust) , so his [Mudbrawler Raiders](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mudbrawler+Raiders) was to replace it. Antoine still hadn’t played a creature spell yet, but as soon as he had found a sixth land, [Morselhoarder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morselhoarder) came down on his side. That very same [Morselhoarder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morselhoarder) had to block the [Mudbrawler Raiders](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mudbrawler+Raiders) , but with another Raiders and the Ram-Gang in play, Gabriel could save the attacking Raiders with a conspired [Barkshell Blessing](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barkshell+Blessing) .

Antoine’s [Wanderbrine Rootcutters](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wanderbrine+Rootcutters) wanted to do better than the [Morselhoarder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morselhoarder) , but they failed doing so, because – as Gabriel had to remind him – the Raiders could not be blocked by blue creatures. Apart from chumping with [Rustrazor Butcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rustrazor+Butcher) , Antoine could not do anything else and eventually succumbed to the unblockable Raiders.

Antoine Ruel 1 – 2 Gabriel Nassif

## Semifinals – Kamiel Cornelissen vs. Raphael Levy

*By André Coimbra*

There are some Grand Prix top 8s where the combined profiles of all the players are less impressive than Kamiel Cornelissen or Raphael Levy profile alone. Luckily we got those two **Magic** super stars to compete against each other for the chance to play for the title in the final.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c7/cf/c7cfd9c21cd9e733534e08e7569e927a9b20f8851a6138cfcdf746ced1fdede7.jpg)They wished good luck to each other, Raphael Levy won the die-roll and, as usually, showed a very aggressive approach to the game, playing [Zealous Guardian](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zealous+Guardian) at the end of his opponent’s first turn and [Somnomancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Somnomancer) on his own second. Kamiel Cornelissen played [Silkbind Faerie](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silkbind+Faerie) . Raphael Levy attacked with both creatures and, as Kamiel chose to not block any of them, he played [Raven’s Run Dragoon](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raven%E2%80%99s+Run+Dragoon) .

Kamiel played [Sootwalkers](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sootwalkers) and Levy played [Steel of the Godhead](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steel+of+the+Godhead) on [Somnomancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Somnomancer) , attacking with it and the 3/3 creature. At this point, things seemed quite good for the French pro, but Kamiel retaliated with [Torture](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torture) on [Raven’s Run Dragoon](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raven%E2%80%99s+Run+Dragoon) and after a few attack steps where Levy tried to trade some resources on the table for damage, things didn’t look that good for the French anymore, as Kamiel was now in control.

Some turns later the Dutch pro played [Isleback Spawn](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Isleback+Spawn) and [Gravelgill Axeshark](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravelgill+Axeshark) . After a couple of turns where Levy was losing creatures just to survive, he ran out of resources and his deck didn’t show any “reach” when needed.

**Kamiel Cornelissen 1 Raphael Levy 0**

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3f/b9/3fb93e59b5c16d1794004ff04aa7f72052746ab1e6cd663cc090a904bee51452.jpg)Levy chose to start, but it was Kamiel to play the first creature on his second turn. Raphael Levy, knowing his opponent’s deck, realized: “Your *one* 2 drop... nice."

Kamiel played [Cemetery Puca](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cemetery+Puca) and Levy played his second [Ballynock Cohort](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ballynock+Cohort) .

The Dutch pro had [Ghastlord of Fugue](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghastlord+of+Fugue) to try to get back into the game, but an [Inquisitor’s Snare](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisitor%E2%80%99s+Snare) destroyed it when Levy attacked with all his creatures.

At this point Raphael Levy that couldn’t see a way to lose the game said: “I’m wondering what I will do if I lose this game..." Kamiel played [Gravelgill Axeshark](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravelgill+Axeshark) , but in the last turn of the game, the French player had [Niveous Wisps](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Niveous+Wisps) to tap his opponents creature and Steel of the Goodhead to allow a successful alpha strike.

Levy could only kill it after a lot of turns getting damage from it and losing creatures simply to save some of that.

## Finals - Gabriel Nassif vs. Kamiel Cornelissen

*by Tobias Henke*

Mostly we've seen aggressive decks throughout the whole weekend, especially today in the draft rounds, but on the third turn of the first game of the finals neither Frenchman Gabriel Nassif nor Kamiel Cornelissen of the Netherlands had any creatures on the board.

But first things first: Nassif had won the die-roll and - more importantly - had taken two mulligans.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/29/e6/29e62bbae049bff9975d87d5b3119c8b185a020c536e7bf7835f979d6ffcc66a.jpg)On turn five his only nonland permanent was [Illuminated Folio](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Illuminated+Folio) , while Cornelissen had [Lockjaw Snapper](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lockjaw+Snapper) and just now a freshly cast [Ghastlord of Fugue](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghastlord+of+Fugue) . [Mercy Killing](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mercy+Killing) got rid of that, but not without leaving four tokens in its wake.

However, these and the Snapper were removed by Gabriel's [Firespout](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firespout) , who had all the while drawn two cards per turn with the help of the [Illuminated Folio](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Illuminated+Folio) . It seemed that Nassif took control of the game. He deployed [Kulrath Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kulrath+Knight) and saved it in a fight with Kamiel's [Gravelgill Axeshark](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravelgill+Axeshark) via [Barkshell Blessing](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barkshell+Blessing) .

Kamiel recovered with [Sootwalkers](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sootwalkers) , [Silkbind Faerie](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silkbind+Faerie) , and [Scuttlemutt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scuttlemutt) , when Gab stumbled upon a serious clump of land on the top of his deck. [Hungry Spriggan](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hungry+Spriggan) and [Crabapple Cohort](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crabapple+Cohort) came to his aid, but the board position certainly was approaching a serious stand-off. A stand-off that favored Nassif (with him activating the Folio first thing each turn).

Well ... if it weren't for Kamiel's topdecked [Isleback Spawn](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Isleback+Spawn) !

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ac/cc/accc2dd828e40955d80752db95b33f38cc1a357de068f89d0da114b970d5aa3d.jpg)Kamiel was feeling safe with the monstrous (and shrouded) blocker, but when he attacked with all of his other creatures, had to learn that, in fact, Nassif had only been waiting for such an opening. [Boggart Ram-Gang](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boggart+Ram-Gang) came down and yet another [Barkshell Blessing](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barkshell+Blessing) forced the last two points of damage.

Gabriel Nassif 1 - 0 Kamiel Cornelissen

**Game 2**

Both players started slow again, but this time Nassif did have [Farhaven Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Farhaven+Elf) to speed things up. For example, Kamiel's first play, [Sootwalkers](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sootwalkers) , was instantly out-classed by an early [Rosheen Meanderer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rosheen+Meanderer) . The 4/4 was taken care of by [Torture](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torture) , but not without getting in at least a little damage. When Nassif attacked with the giant (2/2 by now thanks to some -1/-1 torture marks) and the elf, the [Sootwalkers](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sootwalkers) didn't dare to block for fear of [Barkshell Blessing](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barkshell+Blessing) .

Up next, Nurturer Intiate came down for Nassif, while [Helm of the Ghastlord](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Helm+of+the+Ghastlord) turned [Sootwalkers](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sootwalkers) into a respectably sized 4/4. However, now that the Initiate was around, it still traded with the 1/1, when Gab played [Barkshell Blessing](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barkshell+Blessing) .

Some very happy draw steps for the Dutch followed: First he got [Isleback Spawn](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Isleback+Spawn) , then [Loch Korrigan](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loch+Korrigan) . In this slow match-up both of them were at a premium and went about their business of taking big chunks out of Gabriel Nassif's lifetotal. When he tapped out for some more chump blockers, Cornelissen revealed [Aphotic Wisps](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aphotic+Wisps) , and Nassif dutifully conceded.

Gabriel Nassif 1 - 1 Kamiel Cornelissen

**Game 3**

Nassif opened with [Nurturer Initiate](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nurturer+Initiate) , which immediately got tortured to death... [Faerie Macabre](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faerie+Macabre) was played on the Dutch side, [Mudbrawler Raiders](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mudbrawler+Raiders) on the French, followed by [Crabapple Cohort](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crabapple+Cohort) . Kamiel put a stop to that one with his [Cinderbones](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cinderbones) , while [Faerie Macabre](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faerie+Macabre) was happily beating down for two.

[Scuttlemutt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scuttlemutt) joined Levy's team but Kamiel easily trumped that with [Gravelgill Axeshark](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravelgill+Axeshark) , [Silkbind Faerie](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silkbind+Faerie) , and [Cemetery Puca](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cemetery+Puca) . While he finally lost his [Cinderbones](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cinderbones) , which had to chump at one point without mana up for regeneration, [Silkbind Faerie](https://web.archive.org/web/20220808202327/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silkbind+Faerie) took over the job of keeping Gabriel's best fatty at bay. All the while the little fliers relentlessly worked on Nassif's lifetotal. In the end they succeded.

Kamiel Cornelissen defeats Gabriel Nassif 2 - 1 in the finals of GP Brussels 2008 and now both players advance to the award ceremony!
