![](https://www.wizards.com/protour/images/qt_lg.jpg)Refine your *Lorwyn* Draft skills to earn a ticket to Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, by winning a Pro Tour Qualifier! 

You can test each decklist thanks to our [Sample Hand Generator](https://web.archive.org/web/20090602023708/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/294). Just click on the card fan in the upper right of each decklist and you can see what your opening hand could be, along with mulligans and the first three cards you'd draw.

Decklists will be updated weekly during the PTQ season. The season runs from October 13 through December 30. To find a PTQ near you, [click here](/en/events/2007/kualalumpur08-qualifiers/fyywenzk). Pro Tour–Kuala Lumpur is scheduled for February 15-17, 2008, and the format is *Lorwyn-Morningtide* Booster Draft.
