### List of Changes

* * *

*   Casual Format 1 is available in the Constructed and Multiplayer rooms of the **Casual Play** area. In this format, decks may only contain one copy of any card except for basic lands.
*   Chat messages are now visibly time stamped. This can be turned off on the Settings tab.
*   Spell range in multiplayer as a new setting: **Effect Range**. When **Effect Range** is set equal to **Spell Range**, global effects from cards are limited.
*   Significant server-side optimizations.
*   Fixed mousewheel support.
*   Puppet Master/Twiddle interface changed to add **Tap** and **Untap** buttons.
*   Improved interface on Chain cycle and other target changing cards and effects. There is now a **Same Targets** button.
*   Menus support mousewheel, arrow keys, and enter to select.
*   Fixed Wish cycle targeting split cards in your collection.
*   Future Sight now correctly reveals cards when multiple cards are drawn at once.
*   Fixed issues with cards that have triggered abilities affected by other cards coming into play at the same time.
*   Fixed Stag Beetle getting no counters when brought into play in ways other than casting it.
*   Fixed Read The Runes causing a card to end up in the wrong graveyard.
*   Fixed bringing Clone into play from another player's graveyard with Chainer, Dementia Master resulting in the wrong player controlling Clone.
*   Fixed using Words cycle cards to replace the card draw when a player's library is empty.
*   Help updates.
