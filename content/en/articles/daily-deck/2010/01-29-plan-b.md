What starts off as a simple Bant deck quickly becomes anything but! Krazy Kirby is happy to use a mill strategy on opponents if the efficient beatdown of green, white, and blue's best creatures doesn't work. Archive Trap and Hedron Crab will get the job down. Or turn them on yourself to make Knight of the Reliquary lethally huge out of nowhere. It's a great plan for this week's [Friday Night Magic](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6)!
