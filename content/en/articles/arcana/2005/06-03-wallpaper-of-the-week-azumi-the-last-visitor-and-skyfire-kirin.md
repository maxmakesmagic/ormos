This week we have a special **double batch** of *Saviors of Kamigawa* desktop wallpaper (thanks to Jen Page, magicthegathering.com's graphic designer whiz)!

First, in honor of our final [legend vignette](/en/articles/arcana/2005/06-01-meet-the-saviors-ayumi-the-last-visitor-2), there's **Ayumi, the Last Visitor** by rk post. 

And second, in honor of Fire Week, it's **Skyfire Kirin** by Tsutomu Kawade. Enjoy.
