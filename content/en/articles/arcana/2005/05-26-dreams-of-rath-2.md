S*aviors of Kamigawa* hits shelves in nine days, on June 3rd. But did you see this little number at the prerelease or in the [spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20050526000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/266)? 

![Dreamcatcher](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Dreamcatcher)

[Dreamcatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20050526000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dreamcatcher), a 1/1 kami of daydreams, is shown feeding off of the thoughts of a dozing Kamigawa peasant. In the misty shapes that orbit the kami, you can see a bird, a dragon, and... 

...check this out:

[Dreamcatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20050526000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Dreamcatcher)

The [Dreamcatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20050526000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dreamcatcher) has leeched a *sliver* -- a creature not native to the plane of Kamigawa -- out of his head. That peasant must have quite the imagination -- and clearly artist Mark Tedin does!
