If the sleeping golem on which *Darksteel's* Slobad, Goblin Tinkerer (right) is perching looks familiar, it should...

  

It's everybody's favorite enormous, lumbering iron golem from *Mirrodin*, **Bosh**. In the storyline for *Mirrodin* block, Slobad, an extremely clever artificer (particularly for his race), reactivates the dormant Bosh.
