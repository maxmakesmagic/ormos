It was the boogeyman of Standard for years, but Jund has, for the most part, appeared to die off in regards to making Top 8 at events around the world. ***Magic** 2011* helped with the printing of Obstinate Baloth, a poor card to find in an opponent's hand when cascading into Blightning. But Matthew Macmullin opted to simply *play* the card in Jund and keep Blightnings anyway! The trick was good enough to earn a Top 8 at the Canada National Championship, which you can read more about [here](/en/events/2010/canat10/elarar-repeats-as-canadian-champ).
