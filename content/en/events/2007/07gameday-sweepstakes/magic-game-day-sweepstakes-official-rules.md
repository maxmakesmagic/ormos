VOID WHERE PROHIBITED BY LAW. The Sweepstakes takes place on July 14, 2007. This Sweepstakes is sponsored by Wizards of the Coast, Inc. ("Wizards"). YOU ARE NOT REQUIRED TO MAKE A PURCHASE TO ENTER OR WIN. 

**2\. How to enter:** To enter, go to a participating **Magic** Game Day retailer on July 14, 2007 and complete an entry form. This Sweepstakes is open only to the first fifty (50) participants who go to a participating **Magic** Game Day retailer. Such retailers will then mail the entry forms to Wizards. You may be asked to enter your complete name, address, e-mail address, birth date, daytime and evening phone numbers and the entry form. All entries must be received by participating retailers on July 14, 2007 no later than such retailer's close of business for that day. Limit one entry per person.

**3\. Random Drawing:** One winner will be selected in a random drawing by Wizards on or about August 14, 2007, from all eligible entries received. All decisions made by Wizards on all matters relating to this Sweepstakes are binding. The winner will be notified by mail, e-mail or telephone on or about August 14, 2007.

**6\. Prize and Approximate Retail Value:** One winner will be awarded a prize consisting of two (2) roundtrip coach air-tickets from the major commercial airport nearest winner's home to New York City, New York to attend the **Magic** World Championships 2007. Both winner and his/her guest must be at least eighteen (18) years of age at the time of entry. Travel arrangements must be made at least two (2) weeks in advance of travel date. Prize includes four (4) night's accommodation (double occupancy, room, and tax only). All travel and hotel arrangements must be made through Wizards and are subject to availability.
