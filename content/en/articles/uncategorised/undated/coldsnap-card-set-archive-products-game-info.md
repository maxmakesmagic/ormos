## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/aa/b6/aab6a1add3ca2d521c67e53f4dcc65b3f3b0a6871a657b24456260a17e32e0d8.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, two card boxes with panoramic art, six 15-card booster packs, 40 snow-covered basic land cards, one random Pro Tour player card, a special-edition life counter, and *The Gathering Dark* novel.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/72/5a/725a6d72858e2dd532cc4c49e50ebf82619f3bfda97c93c6fa00102f709acedb.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ca/f4/caf46c13acefa952a9f1c11f6c20931e443df8bc5783b12c0bb4e1df0e763d98.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718094225/http://magic.wizards.com/node/975921)

(\* Not available in all languages)

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2b/61/2b6160cd20b1749f1322451eb60ddb136984b74a5958d49df479f5a2b88b3c33.png)Released 10 years after its predecessor, *Alliances*, this final set of the Ice Age block contains 155 cards with randomly inserted premium cards.

**Set Name:** *Coldsnap*  
**Block:** Set 3 of 3 of the *Ice Age* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 155

**Languages:** English, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Chinese Simplified, Spanish, German

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718094225/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/CSPFAQ061406_EN.doc)

**Game Development:** Randy Buehler Jr. (lead), Devin Low, Zvi Mowshowitz, and Michael Turian, with contributions from Mark L. Gottlieb
