## GRAND PRIX MELBOURNE 2014

![](https://i.ormos.uk/72/b1/72b1baf4162412b734188eb729836ca2c4db85df8cf21ce1f7f0e58aac7eae77.jpg)

The Gods have spoken and Nam Sung-Wook is your Grand Prix Melbourne 2014 Champion!Defeating local hero Patty Robertson in the finals, Nam also makes history by becoming the first South Korean player to win a Grand Prix. To make this moment even more special, his victory comes from ousting over 900 other players in Australia's largest ever tournament.

Semifinalists Wei Yifan and Ash Webster have also tried their best to defend the trophy and they should not be disappointed to return home with a hefty prize check, along with airfare and a much-coveted invitation to Pro Tour Journey to Nyx.

Rounding off the rest of the top 8 include four-time Grand Prix Top 8 competitor Huang Hao-Shan from Taiwan, Pro Tour *Born of the Gods* Top 16 competitor Zheng Jingwei as well as Luke McGlaughlin and Craig Chapman. Congratulations to all, and may the Gods be with you!

## Quarterfinals - Patty Robertson vs. Jingwei Zheng

*by Pip Foweraker*

 

The Robertson-Zheng matchup promised to be that classic test of skill, the U/W control mirror. Both decks feature the same core set of cards and become even closer mirrors post-sideboard. A great deal of the matchup will come down to resource and tempo management. Traditionally, players focus on hitting their early land drops so that they can fight more effectively over the powerful late-game spells like Planeswalkers and [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation). This careful, studious matchup often comes down to a single game in Swiss rounds. The finals are untimed, meaning the players will have to beat each other the 'fair' way.

The first clash of the game came over [Jace, Architect of Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Architect+of+Thought). Zheng blinked first and had his Jace [Syncopate](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syncopate)d, Robertson resolving his a turn later. The players dug through spells for a few more turns, focussing on hitting their land drops and moving into the later game as rapidly and fluently as possible. Zheng fired off a [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation), but wasn't able to stop Robertson from resolving an Ætherling. Zheng settled down to glare at the Ætherling and see what he could get done. A [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) showed Robertson with a hand full of power, with another Jace, a [Doom Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doom+Blade), a pair of Revelations and some [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere)s.

Zheng went for an [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion), and generated some tokens with her after it resolved. Robertson dropped a [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) to stop Elspeth from getting too out of hand, but Zheng had another to keep the pressure on. Zheng started generating more tokens, but the unblockable Ætherling was enough for Robertson to take the first game.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/dc/e9/dce946136e709ec35ccd7a40b1145fbaedb4c8a3635f865a5c06655685428256.jpg)  
*Zheng: You want to kill which dude?* 

### Patty Robertson 1 - Jingwei Zheng

Both players started off with early [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize)s, but then didn't do much but play lands for quite a few turns. Robertson tried another [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), but it got hit with a [Dissolve](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dissolve). Zheng started sneaking in some points of damage with his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) while both players accumulated cards and lands.

A few turns later, the [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) was still going.

The same thing kept happening. And then the unusual, if not unthinkable, happened: Zheng's [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) did a combined total of 18 damage (Plus the earlier [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize)) to take the second game, drawing an appreciative wave of applause from the gathered crowd. All those other, massively powerful spells? Not so relevant in this game.

### Jingwei Zheng 1 - Patty Robertson 1

A pair of early [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize)s from Robertson cleared out Zheng's Jace's. Zheng tried for a freshly topdecked third, but Robertson had a [Syncopate](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syncopate) to keep it out of contention. Zheng chuckled when Robertson activated his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) and swung. "Ah, the bitter taste of my own medicine', Zheng grinned as he adjusted his life total.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/31/90/31907b21e785a64e56ecbd18db05613f600b2a83a1431cb28960aed5c6cf4bff.jpg)  
*Robertson: THIS ONE. I KILL THIS ONE.* 

Zheng summoned an [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion) to slow down the [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault), but Robertson had a [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) to keep it out of play. Robertson cast a Supreme *Judgment* to clear out the Elspeth tokens, holding a copy of the Planeswalker in hand but needing an extra mana to cast it and keep [Negate](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Negate) as a backup.

Zheng tried a [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), which gave Robertson pause before he went to [Negate](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Negate) it. Zheng had a [Dissolve](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dissolve) to get the discard spell through, and nabbed the Elspeth, stymieing Robertson's schemes.

Robertson switched back to his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) plan, steadily dropping Zheng's life total downwards. He tried to accelerate it with a [Blood Baron of Vizkopa](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Baron+of+Vizkopa), but it got [Dissolve](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dissolve)d as well. Zheng summoned his own, which Robertson cleared out with his Verdict. Both players threw a few more haymakers, but nothing stuck on either side before the inimitable [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) sealed the match for Robertson.

### Game One

After throwing back his initial seven, Wei found himself stuck on two Islands but had just enough mana to stop Robertson's [Jace, Architect of Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Architect+of+Thought) with [Syncopate](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syncopate). With Wei holding two utterly useless [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict)s, he watched as Robertson's horde of Soldier tokens and [Ætherling](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86therling) flood the board. Returning the attack, Robertson swung with all his Soldier tokens and a [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault), keen to push his offence. Sung-Wook ate a Soldier with his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) and took the rest, falling to 10. Another attack, and Robertson took the epic first game and sent both players to their sideboards.

### Patty Robertson 1 - Nam Sung-Wook 0

A [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) from Sung-Wook revealed Jace, Elspeth, and four lands, Sung-Wook taking the Jace. Robertson played (obviously) lands for a few turns, while Sung-Wook summoned a [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter) and began attacking with it.

![Erebos, God of the Dead](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Erebos%2C+God+of+the+Dead)

Sung-Wook followed up with an [Erebos, God of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Erebos%2C+God+of+the+Dead). Robertson had a [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault), while Sung-Wook used [Erebos, God of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Erebos%252C%2BGod%2Bof%2Bthe%2BDead)'s [Greed](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Greed) ability to dig for more threats. While Sung-Wook was tapped out, Robertson fired off a [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) for 2.

Robertson cast an [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion), which Sung-Wook countered with a [Dissolve](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dissolve) from his [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter). Sung-Wook then summoned a [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon) and attacked with his Specter and [Erebos, God of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Erebos%252C%2BGod%2Bof%2Bthe%2BDead). Robertson had a [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), knocking himself to 5 life before casting a [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) to clear out the [Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter) and Demon.

A [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) took Robertson's [Fated Retribution](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fated+Retribution) and another [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter) made an appearance. With his hand stripped and his life total dropping, Robertson scooped up his cards and headed for the decider.

### Nam Sung-Wook 1 - Patty Robertson 1

Sung-Wook's opening [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) saw a pair of Jace's and a [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere), the latter heading straight to the graveyard. Robertson cast some discard of his own, his [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) seeing [Erebos, God of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Erebos%252C%2BGod%2Bof%2Bthe%2BDead), [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter), [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie) and triple [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault). He took the Zombie, but Sung-Wook drew and summoned another. Robertson cast one of his Jace's and started creeping his loyalty count upwards.

Sung-Wook attacked the Jace with his Zombie, then summoned an [Erebos, God of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Erebos%252C%2BGod%2Bof%2Bthe%2BDead). Robertson dug furiously with his Jace and scored a [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) and a Plains. Sung-Wook summoned another [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie), revealing a [Last Breath](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Breath) and Robertson's remaining Jace. With the fresh information, Sung-Wook attacked Robertson with his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) and [Erebos, God of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Erebos%252C%2BGod%2Bof%2Bthe%2BDead) and went to take out Jace with one of his Zombies.

Robertson cast a [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) in the nick of time to take out both of Sung-Wook's Zombies and de-activate [Erebos, God of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Erebos%252C%2BGod%2Bof%2Bthe%2BDead), buying himself some valuable breathing time. Sung-Wook attacked with both of his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault)s and Robertson threw one of his own in as a blocker, happy to trade lands for life and time.

Robertson summoned his second [Jace, Architect of Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Architect+of+Thought), then took a [Revoke Existence](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Revoke+Existence) over a [Dissolve](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dissolve) and a [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault), then exiled Sung-Wook's [Erebos, God of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Erebos%252C%2BGod%2Bof%2Bthe%2BDead). A [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) revealed Robertson holding a pair of lands, leading Sung-Wook's [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault)s to take out the Planeswalker in short order. [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter) joined the battlefield, the [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault)s cracked in, Robertson scooped up his cards, and just like that, the tournament was over.

## Top 5 Cards

*by Chapman Sim*

 

 

![Fated Retribution](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Fated+Retribution)

 

### 5\. [Fated Retribution](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fated+Retribution)

It's been a while since an instant speed "[Wrath of God](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wrath+of+God)" effect has been printed and control players are happy to supplement their removal suite with a board wiper that did not require them to tap out. Planeswalkers have always been the bane of control decks and [Fated Retribution](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fated+Retribution) conveniently removes them while keeping friendly [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere)s safe from harm. Wei Yifan went to the extent of playing two copies in the maindeck, a testament of how good the card is in the Esper control archetype.

 

![Xenagos, God of Revels](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Xenagos%2C+God+of+Revels)

 

### 4\. [Xenagos, God of Revels](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xenagos%2C+God+of+Revels) &  
[Xenagos, the Reveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xenagos%2C+the+Reveler)

The combination of both his incarnations is more than sufficient for Red Green based decks to make a resurgence, providing resilience against control decks by being so difficult to remove. Red Green decks will continue to be a menace in the metagame, especially while partner-in-crime [Domri Rade](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Domri+Rade) is in the mix.

 

![Courser of Kruphix](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Courser+of+Kruphix)

 

### 3\. [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix)

One of the best cards introduced in *Born of the Gods*, this nifty Centaur is tough enough to deter incoming attackers and conveniently gains the owner some life while generating card advantage. The "Jack of all Trades" found homes in Ash Webster's Jund deck, as well as Huang Hao-Shan's Red Green Monsters archetype and some Simic brews utilizing [Kiora, the Crashing Wave](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kiora%2C+the+Crashing+Wave).

 

![Sphinx's Revelation](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation)

 

### 2\. [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation)

Old habits die hard. Players love to draw cards and its difficult to kick the habit. Nowadays, either you go really fast and kill the control player before this card gets out of hand, or you simply get buried by the insane card advantage. Good enough to send Patty Robertson to the finals and two other players into the Top 8, [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) will continue to be force to be reckoned with, until the next Standard rotation. [Temple of Enlightenment](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temple+of+Enlightenment) deserves an honorable mention too!

 

![Pack Rat](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Pack+Rat)

 

### 1\. [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)

Nope, we're not quite done yet. Ever since its dominance in the world *Return to Ravnica* Limited, these pesky rodents continue to dominate in Standard. Exponential growth is often unfair, and when paired with [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), it can mean a swift death for the unprepared. Champion Nam Sung-Wook was always first to finish his Top 8 matches, thanks to the raw brutality of [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20220702215906/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat).
