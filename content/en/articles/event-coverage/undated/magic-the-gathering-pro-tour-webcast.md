### [Pro Tour-Honolulu: June 5-7, 2009](/en/events/2009/honolulu09/pro-tourhonolulu-event-information)

Who will be crowned the second Pro Tour champion of 2009? The top players in the world are headed to Honolulu to answer that very question. You can watch the Top 8 Booster Draft and matches right here starting at 4:45 p.m. ET on Sunday, June 7.

We also want to know what you think about Pro Tour webcasts. [**Click here**](http://deploy.ztelligence.com/start/survey/survey_taking.jsp?PIN=13B8JFZBEXUYR) to take a short survey (5-10 minutes).

Here's a handy time zone guide to figure out when the webcast starts in your neighborhood:

*   Honolulu: 10:45 a.m.
*   Los Angeles: 1:45 p.m.
*   Chicago: 3:45 p.m.
*   New York: 4:45 p.m.
*   Rio de Janeiro: 5:45 p.m.
*   London: 9:45 p.m.
*   Berlin: 10:45 p.m.
*   Moscow: 12:45 a.m. Monday
*   Tokyo: 5:45 a.m. Monday
*   Sydney: 6:45 p.m. Monday

Be sure to check out the Magic: The Gathering Pro Tour Videos and Podcast as well. With multiple daily updates during the tournament, they're your best way to stay on top of the movers and shakers at the event. For video updates, visit **Magic** on YouTube.com by [clicking here](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603175435/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.youtube.com/profile?user=wizardsmtg&origin=). For podcasts, click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20090602043528/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/podcast/home) to subscribe today via iTunes.

To view past Top 8 webcasts, visit the [Video Archive](/en/events/2004/webcast/webcast-video-archive).
