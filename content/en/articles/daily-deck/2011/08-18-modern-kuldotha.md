Last week it was [announced](/en/articles/latestdevelopments/2011/08-12-welcome-to-the-modern-world) that the upcoming [Pro Tour Philadelphia](/en/events/2011/philadelphia11/magic-weekend-philadelphia-event-information) will feature the Modern format. So what might a Modern deck look like? Here's one from Wizards of the Coast's own Lee Sharpe, based around Kuldotha Rebirth. Lee's goal is to plunk as many creatures onto the table as quickly as possible, then use Haze of Rage or Goblin Bushwhacker to hit the opponent for lethal! The deck list comes from the 2011 **Magic Online** Community Cup, where Lee was a competitor on behalf of the Wizards team.
