Dark Jeskai was one of the decks to beat in Standard this weekend. I sought out the advice of experienced players regarding the best card, best deck, or best game plan for beating it.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpbru15/gpbru15_qq_strasky.jpg)  
*Ondřej Stráský*

**No. 16-ranked Ondřej Stráský:** "Practice and practice, because the deck can be tough to play against at first. If you want a specific card, then play [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20151114000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon)—the ramp deck has a good matchup against Jeskai, for instance."

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpbru15/gpbru15_qq_anteri.jpg)  
*Fabrizio Anteri*

**No. 22-ranked Fabrizio Anteri:** "Play Esper tokens. It's the best Gideon deck, and [Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151114000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar) is the best way to get around [Ojutai's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20151114000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ojutai%27s+Command). Esper Tokens also has [Silkwrap](https://web.archive.org/web/20151114000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silkwrap) to exile both [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151114000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy) and [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151114000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider), so it's the best removal spell against them."

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpbru15/gpbru15_qq_lantto.jpg)  
*Magnus Lantto*

**Magic Online Champion Magnus Lantto:** "I like going wide. Token decks are pretty good against Dark Jeskai because they go around all their card advantage spells. Also, [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20151114000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon) is a good plan."

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpbru15/gpbru15_qq_bonde.jpg)  
*Michael Bonde*

**Grand Prix Madrid 2015 semifinalist Michael Bonde:** "You need cards that are not vulnerable to [Ojutai's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20151114000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ojutai%27s+Command) or their spot removal. [Mastery of the Unseen](https://web.archive.org/web/20151114000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mastery+of+the+Unseen) and [Exert Influence](https://web.archive.org/web/20151114000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exert+Influence) are good examples."

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpbru15/gpbru15_qq_zatlkay.jpg)  
*Matej Zatlkaj*

**Gold level pro Matej Zatlkaj:** "Play around [Ojutai's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20151114000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ojutai%27s+Command) and [Kolaghan's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20151114000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kolaghan%27s+Command). Try not to give them an opportunity for a two-for-one."

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpbru15/gpbru15_qq_hendriks.jpg)  
*Thomas Hendriks*

**WMC competitor badge collector Thomas Hendriks:** "Have the right threats against their mix of removal spells, or disrupt them with [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20151114000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress). The ramp deck is great against them too when you start chaining Ulamogs."

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpbru15/gpbru15_qq_muller.jpg)  
*Martin Müller*

**Pro Tour Battle for Zendikar quarterfinalist Martin Müller:** "If you're playing Jeskai yourself, then add more late-game cards. The combination of removal spells to ensure you don't die in the early game and cards like [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20151114000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon) or [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20151114000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) to go over the top when the game goes long, is where you want to be."
