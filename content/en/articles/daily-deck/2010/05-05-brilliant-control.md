Cruel Ultimatum and Violent Ultimatum are both cards that have seen play in Standard since they saw print during *Shards of Alara*. But Brilliant Ultimatum? Not so much. Or at least, no so much until Alex Viksnins managed to pull the feat off at the TCGPlayer $5K in Boston. It turns out the card is very good at letting you cast Emrakul, the Aeons Torn for free. And if you don't flip an Emrakul? Perhaps any of your four different types of planeswalker, or even a second copy of the Ultimatum!
