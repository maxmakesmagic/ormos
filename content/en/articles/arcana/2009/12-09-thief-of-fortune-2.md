Come with us now to the long-ago days of *Time Spiral*! Remember [Fortune Thief](https://web.archive.org/web/20091209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fortune+Thief)?

![Fortune Thief](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Fortune+Thief)

  

The card, of course, is a reference to [Ali from Cairo](https://web.archive.org/web/20091209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ali+from+Cairo).

![Ali from Cairo](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ali+from+Cairo)

  

You might even recognize the turban. But there's something else. First, let's blow up the [Ali from Cairo](https://web.archive.org/web/20091209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ali+from+Cairo) art a bit.
