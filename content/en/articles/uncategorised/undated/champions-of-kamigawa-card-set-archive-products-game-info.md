## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fc/61/fc6137345e1cf8b4daafc9757cc14ecdbf93f888b166d4aa1310c89446322a6a.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, one 75-card tournament pack, three 15-card booster packs, two premium cards, the *Outlaw: Champions of Kamigawa* novel, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fc/61/fc6137345e1cf8b4daafc9757cc14ecdbf93f888b166d4aa1310c89446322a6a.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fc/61/fc6137345e1cf8b4daafc9757cc14ecdbf93f888b166d4aa1310c89446322a6a.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717021701/http://magic.wizards.com/node/976411)  
 (\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fc/61/fc6137345e1cf8b4daafc9757cc14ecdbf93f888b166d4aa1310c89446322a6a.png)

### TOURNAMENT PACKS

The foundation for exciting Sealed Deck play. Includes 45 randomly assorted cards and 30 basic land cards.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/00/90/0090196fbc04be1a238939e5ede13a8b85b697763661f69440995387d9eb237a.png)*Champions of Kamigawa* introduces several new mechanics (Bushido, Flip Cards, Soulshift, and Splice onto Arcane) as well as the first Legendary Enchantments and more Legendary creatures than any previous set.

**Set Name:** *Champions of Kamigawa*  
**Block:** Set 1 of 3 of the *Kamigawa* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 306 (plus 1 art variant)

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Chinese Simplified, Spanish
