So there are both feline characters associated with a pride. But is it the *same* pride? The answer is yes! First, look at the circlet on Healer of the Pride's chest.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/52/86/52868235fcdf6f5ebc703413f2b9e61f33d8e759f3a56d70f9cabb4f9b4c0569.jpg) 

Healer of the Pride | Art by [Christopher Moeller](https://web.archive.org/web/20120806000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22christopher+moeller%22])

Now, look at the circlet on Ajani, Caller of the Pride's shoulder.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/cc/7e/cc7e0cd59a5ba488453c53249df0ebbe4b2919ba958fc1623163bb3db2426fbf.jpg) 

Ajani, Caller of the Pride | Art by [D. Alexander Gregory](https://web.archive.org/web/20120806000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22d.+alexander+gregory%22])

Same circlet? Same pride!
