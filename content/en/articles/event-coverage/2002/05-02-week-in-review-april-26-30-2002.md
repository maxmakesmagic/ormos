### Coming Up: Pro Tour-Nice and Nice Master Series

<table><tbody><tr><td><img src="https://i.ormos.uk/8d/96/8d96b3bfdf6b0eb6f3ecdf3d7da73b059182c0663c82595a8bb82668ff3af9ef.jpg"><br><i>Can it dominate Extended again?</i>&#10;</td></tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

Pro players are converging on Nice, France for the last Pro Tour of the season, not counting Worlds. It is hard to come up with a better location for it than the French Rivera in the spring. After having some fun in the sun, players will compete in either Gateway or Masters using the Extended format. Last time there was a major Extended tournament, Oath of Druids decks were just reasserting their dominance after several weeks of Miracle Grow and Super Grow variants running rampant. *Torment* has become legal in tournament play since then, and some of the top players in the world spent their time trying to break the format, so rest assured we will see some good decks on Thursday.

On Friday, the Pro Tour itself shall begin. There is a very good chance that Kai's third Player of the Year title will be locked in after this one - all it would take is an average performance from him and several of the other players at the top of the standings. The year is winding down with few Grand Prix tournaments left, so after Nice we can really see who has a shot at attending the **Magic** Invitational in South America this autumn. 

Zvi Mowshowitz will be sporting his "I am going to draft white" t-shirt. Will the gambit work out for this always unorthodox player? It will certainly be worth checking out, so make sure to tune into our live coverage this weekend. And with any luck, I won't be writing any of it.

### Magic Trivia

Last week's question:

*How is it possible to have Faceless Butcher remove itself from the game (physically the same card, not technically)? Which cards do you need?*

The complicated answer from Gis and Rune: 

<table><tbody><tr><td><img src="https://i.ormos.uk/72/63/726381088c4894c23d4496a17f774f9e1a2eb97780d600576ef79fa1ba4ea48c.jpg"><br><i>The answer to many complex trivia questions...</i>&#10;</td></tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

Play a Butcher, when it comes into play target a Silver Wyvern with its ability. In response bounce the Butcher to your hand (multiple ways to do this) and resolve that. Then still in response to the Butcher's CIP ability, you play the Butcher again as an instant via Winding Canyons or Flash. Resolve it and when it is in play you can deflect the Butcher's ability, which is still targeting the Wyvern to the Butcher, thereby removing its physical self from the game.
