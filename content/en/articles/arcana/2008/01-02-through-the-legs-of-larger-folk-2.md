*This article originally ran November 7, 2007*

The first kithkin in **Magic**, [Amrou Kithkin](https://web.archive.org/web/20080102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amrou+Kithkin) from *Legends*, is flavored as being so short and agile that it's able to "run between the legs" of creatures much larger than it. Its *Weatherlight* homage, [Kithkin Armor](https://web.archive.org/web/20080102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kithkin+Armor), continued this flavor trend, granting the sneaky nimbleness of a kithkin to another creature.

This established a pattern of kithkin being unaffected by "big stuff." *Future Sight's* [Mistmeadow Skulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20080102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistmeadow+Skulk) has a new look at that same flavor, with its protection from high converted mana costs. 

![Mistmeadow Skulk](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Mistmeadow+Skulk)

In *Lorwyn*, the kithkin legend Gaddock Teeg also captures some of that flavor, and puts an exclamation point on it—he makes it impossible to cast noncreature spells with high mana costs at all!

![Gaddock Teeg](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Gaddock+Teeg)
