1844 players rocked and rolled their way to Cleveland on Saturday morning with dreams of being crowned champion of Grand Prix Cleveland. Only 220 had strong enough records to continue on to the draft portion of the tournament this morning. The dust settled, and now only 8 remain. These are your Top 8 competitors for Grand Prix Cleveland!

  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpcle15/gpcleveland-2015-T8-calcano.jpg)

**Name:** Christian Calcano

**Age:** 26

**Hometown:** New York City, New York

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Four Grand Prix Top 8s (1 win)

**What did you play in Sealed, and why? What was your record?**

Black/White splashing Red and Green. Red for [Arc Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Lightning), [Mardu Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Charm), and a rare dragon. Green for [Abzan Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Charm). Had five dual lands to support the splashes. Also had [Mastery of the Unseen](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mastery+of+the+Unseen) and [Master of Pearls](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Pearls). 8-1

**What did you play during your drafts, and why? What were your records?**

Red/White Aggro (3-0) and Green/White (2-1). Red/White Aggro is my favorite archetype and the cards were there. Second draft I opened [Warden of the First Tree](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warden+of+the+First+Tree) and two [High Sentinels of Arashin](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=High+Sentinels+of+Arashin).

**What was the best play you saw all weekend?**

I didn't see this, but one player told me his opponent cast [Villainous Wealth](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Villainous+Wealth) for six hitting [Ponyback Brigade](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponyback+Brigade) and three other creatures with three or less power. He untapped and cast [Mob Rule](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mob+Rule) for a lethal attack.

**What are you looking forward to most in *Dragons of Tarkir*, and why?**

Ojutai's Command because #value.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpcle15/gpcleveland-2015-T8-merriam.jpg)

**Name:** Ross Merriam

**Age:** 26

**Hometown:** Middletown, Connecticutt

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Over ten SCG Open Top 8s, 13-2 GP NJ 2014

**What did you play in Sealed, and why? What was your record?**

8-1 with Green/White splashing Blue and Black. My bombs were [Mastery of the Unseen](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mastery+of+the+Unseen) and Dromoka so I built around them. I sideboarded into Green/Blue splashing White and Black several times and it was likely a better build because of Cancel and [Disdainful Stroke](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disdainful+Stroke)'s strength against other bombs.

**What did you play during your drafts, and why? What were your records?**

3-0 with Green/Blue splashing White. I opened Dromoka and Blue was open in pack one. Green was open in pack two. 2-1 with Green/Red Aggro. I took Red cards like an idiot. Never take Red cards.

**What was the best play you saw all weekend?**

Most exciting play was in round 9 when I had to block the right one of two morphs with no information at all. I won the coin flip and here I am.

**What are you looking forward to most in *Dragons of Tarkir*, and why?**

Hopefully finding a tempo deck to break the midrange monotony of Standard.
