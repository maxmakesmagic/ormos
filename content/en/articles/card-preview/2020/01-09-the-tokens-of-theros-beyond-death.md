The full-art tokens from *Theros Beyond Death* look astounding—like age-old myths waiting to be told. Soldiers, Satyrs, and . . . Tentacles? Fabled odysseys abound when you play your games with these truly epic depictions.

If you haven't yet seen the cards that make these fourteen tokens, swiftly voyage to the [Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2019/09-20-throne-of-eldraine) to unearth their creation.

Thwart the wrath of the Gods, or aid in the destruction of their foes, with these full-art tokens from *Theros Beyond Death*!
