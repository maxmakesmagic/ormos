### Tournaments Down Until Further Notice

* * *

As most of you know, ***Magic** Online* crashed around 10:00 AM Pacific Standard Time yesterday. We think the crash was connected to the 300+ Release Event tournaments we had just added to the system, but to be perfectly honest we aren't sure. To get the system back up again, we removed all tournaments and leagues from the system. Then last night around 9:00 PM PST we crashed again. This last crash was particularly puzzling, and as of this morning we still don't know what caused it.

So here's where we are now: We aren't comfortable having you pay money for prize events when we aren't confident that the system will stay stable. Therefore, we're going to turn off all tournament play until we've identified the new problem. In addition, consider yourself warned that we're running a bigger crash risk right now than we are happy with. *Mirrodin* cards are working fine and we're doing everything we can to allow you all to play with them in casual games, but treat this as an apology in advance for any crashes that might happen over the upcoming holiday weekend.

We're committed to having *Mirrodin* Release Events, but we can't determine their schedule until we understand more about what caused us to crash yesterday. We hope to have a plan in place by the end of next week and we'll post a follow-up then one way or the other.

We take leagues very seriously and spent a good amount of time over the last day trying to figure out if there was any way to restore the leagues that we took down and let them play out to their natural conclusion. Unfortunately, there isn't. Everyone who was in one of those leagues will get to keep the product they opened and will also be compensated for their inconvenience (and tickets). The details of this compensation are still under review and we expect to have an announcement ready sometime next week.

I wish I had better news (or even just more news), but I don't. We aren't sure exactly what caused yesterday's crashes so we're putting leagues and tournaments on hold for a while. Meanwhile, Release Events will almost certainly be postponed or modified in some way. Our next follow-up on all these issues will be posted sometime next week.
