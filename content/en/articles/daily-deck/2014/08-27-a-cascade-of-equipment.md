[Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic), [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm), and [Force of Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20140827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+of+Will) have been seen together for many years, but today's deck puts a heavy spin on the classic archetype. Paul Lynch was able to take his take on the archetype to a 4th-place finish at the [StarCityGames Legacy Open](http://www.starcitygames.com/events/230814_washingtondc.html) in Washington DC.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Stoneforge%20Mystic)](https://web.archive.org/web/20140827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge%20Mystic)

The first major difference was incorporating [Shardless Agent](https://web.archive.org/web/20140827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shardless+Agent) and [Ancestral Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20140827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Vision) into the deck. The cards in this deck are selected to be powerful at all stages of the game, so [Shardless Agent](https://web.archive.org/web/20140827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shardless+Agent) can be cast liberally. The countermagic in the main deck is only [Force of Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20140827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+of+Will) and a [Misdirection](https://web.archive.org/web/20140827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Misdirection), both of which cannot be cascaded into by [Shardless Agent](https://web.archive.org/web/20140827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shardless+Agent).

The other major change is the inclusion of the [Sword of the Meek](https://web.archive.org/web/20140827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+the+Meek) and [Thopter Foundry](https://web.archive.org/web/20140827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thopter+Foundry) combo. When combined, a single mana turns into gaining 1 life and making a 1/1 flier, and this can be done repeatedly. What makes this function well in the deck is that [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic) can search up [Sword of the Meek](https://web.archive.org/web/20140827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+the+Meek)—being an Equipment comes in handy!
