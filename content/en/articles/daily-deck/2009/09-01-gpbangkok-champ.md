A well respected Japanese pro, Shingou Kurihara recently padded his Pro Tour resume with a victory in the Limited format at Grand Prix–Bangkok. His white-blue deck was just powerful enough to get him there, with double Pacifism and Excommunicate containing his opponent's creatures while heavy hitters like Serra Angel got there in the air. For more about Shingou's win and the rest of the details from the event, visit the [official event coverage](https://web.archive.org/web/20090901000000/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/gpban09/welcom).
