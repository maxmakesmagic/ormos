 March 28-30, 2014

The **Magic Online** Championship features sixteen players battling for a share of more than $100,000 in cash prizes in the Standard, Modern, Cube Draft, and *Theros/Born of the Gods* Booster Draft formats. Tune in for the live webcast starting Friday morning for complete access to the **Magic Online** Championship: live match coverage, player interviews, and much more! 

The sixteen 2013 **Magic Online** Championship finalists include: 

*   The defending 2012 **Magic Online** champion, Dmitriy Butakov
*   Twelve individual **Magic Online** Championship Series season winners
*   Two MOCS Player-of-the-Year Championship winners
*   One MOCS Last-Chance Qualifier winner

Think you have the skills to make it to the 2014 **Magic Online** Championship? The 2014 **Magic Online** Championship Series is under way now. Visit [this page](/en/articles/feature/2013/11-04-2014-magic-online-championship-series-2) for more information and a full schedule of season championships.

**More Event Coverage**  
 In addition to the live video stream, text coverage by Mike Rosenberg will be available at [DailyMTG.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20140326193344/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Default.aspx) starting Friday morning. 

The official Twitter hashtag for the **Magic Online** Championship is [#mtgochamp](http://twitter.com/#!/search/realtime/%23mtgochamp). 

**Media Requests**  
 Please send an email to press@wizards.com if you wish to set up a media credential for the **Magic Online** Championship. Wizards of the Coast asks that all media requests be submitted by March 14 (two weeks prior to the event). Requests made after that date, including requests made on-site, may be denied. 

Media check-in will take place Thursday, March 27 from 4 p.m. - 7 p.m. If you cannot attend the check-in period, please make arrangements via the press@wizards.com email address prior to the event. 

The **Magic Online** Championship is not open to the public.

The 2013 **Magic Online** Championship is a three-day invitation-only [Standard](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219083410/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Resources.aspx?x=judge/resources/sfrstandard), [Modern](https://web.archive.org/web/20140404163859/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Resources.aspx?x=judge/resources/sfrmodern), Cube Draft, and *Born of the Gods/Theros* Booster Draft format event with Standard Top 4 playoff. 

**Format**  
 

*   [Standard](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219083410/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Resources.aspx?x=judge/resources/sfrstandard), [Modern](https://web.archive.org/web/20140404163859/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Resources.aspx?x=judge/resources/sfrmodern), Cube Draft, and *Theros/Born of the Gods* Booster Draft
*   [Cube Draft card list](https://web.archive.org/web/20140323104733/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg_daily_other_03032014_MOCS2013ChampandCube#cardlist)

**Tournament Length**  
 

*   Day One will consist of three rounds of Cube Draft followed by four rounds of Modern
*   Day Two will consist of three rounds of *Theros/Born of the Gods* Booster Draft, followed by four rounds of Standard, followed by a cut to the Top 4 playoff
*   The Top 4 playoff will be Standard format. Players are required to use the same Standard deck as on Day Two.
*   All matches will be best two of three.

**Schedule** 

**Friday**  
 9 a.m. – 9:45 a.m.: Player registration at event site  
 10 a.m.: Day One play begins 

**Saturday**  
 10 a.m.: Day Two play begins 

**Sunday**  
 Noon: Top 4 playoff play begins
