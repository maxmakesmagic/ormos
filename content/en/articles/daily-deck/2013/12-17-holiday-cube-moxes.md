You have perhaps heard that the **Magic Online**[Holiday Cube](https://web.archive.org/web/20131217000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/other/01222013D) returns [the Power](https://web.archive.org/web/20131217000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/feature/223b) introduced last year? Well, with great Power comes great... uh... new art. For the online Cube, we commissioned [Volkan Baga](https://web.archive.org/web/20131217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;artist=%5B%22Volkan+Baga%22%5D) to create all-new images of the iconic and beloved Moxes. Let's take a look!
