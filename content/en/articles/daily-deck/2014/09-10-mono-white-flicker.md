[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Flickerwisp)](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flickerwisp)

In a format as diverse as Modern, there is room for many different decks. Even among the relatively small niche that is mono-white creature decks, there is room for multiple distinct archetypes. One popular archetype in Modern is a [Soul Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soul+Warden)–based deck, using [Martyr of Sands](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Martyr+of+Sands), [Ajani's Pridemate](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ajani%27s+Pridemate), and [Serra Ascendant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serra+Ascendant) to turn lifegain into huge undercosted creatures. Today's deck is very, very different from that, despite also being a mono-white creature deck.

This deck is based around the redundancy of [Flickerwisp](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flickerwisp) and [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel). Both cards are efficient flying creatures that allow you to get second uses from your creatures. [Blade Splicer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blade+Splicer) is particularly effective with these creatures, providing a second Golem token on top of your efficient fliers. There is even a hilarious copy of [Akroma, Angel of Fury](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroma%2C+Angel+of+Fury), which can only be cast via morph—[Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial) with eight counters seems unlikely—but is one of the best things to target with [Flickerwisp](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flickerwisp) or [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel). Like all morphs, Akroma will come back face-up when temporarily exiled.

The synergies in this deck are not Limited to [Flickerwisp](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flickerwisp) and [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel). Many of the creatures in this deck make life more difficult on the opponent's mana. [Thalia, Guardian of Thraben](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thalia%2C+Guardian+of+Thraben) and [Judge's Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge%27s+Familiar) make it difficult for opponents to cast their spells. [Leonin Arbiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Arbiter) and [Aven Mindcensor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Mindcensor) provide a similar function against the [fetch land](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&type=+%5b%22Land%22%5d&text=+%5bsearch%5d+%5byour%5d+%5blibrary%5d&set=%7C%5b%22Zendikar%22%5d) mana bases while working with both [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile) and [Ghost Quarter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140910000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Quarter) to potentially prevent an opponent from finding a basic land.

(Deck provided with fourteen-card sideboard.)
