### 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/51/64/5164c201f789cb60de5089c1873129e882d72277b42c899b973614a34f622947.jpg)

And so it all comes down to this. Olivier Ruel in his loud yet stylish long-sleeved Jersey, Ken Ho in a subdued beige sweater. Ever since his name was announced in the Top 8, players have simply assumed that this was Ruel's Tour. His dead-last finish at the Magic Invitational came with the promise that he would win a Pro Tour this year, just as Steve OMS, Gary Wise and Zvi Mowshowitz did before him. Ruel was doing his fair share to fuel that fire, defeating fellow Frenchman Nicolas Olivieri in the quarters before stomping all over Rob Dougherty in a quick and brutal semifinal. Throughout it all he's worn his trademark bemused expression, and answered adversity and success with the same sly smile. Ruel is running the tournament's most successful deck, mono-black control.

Ken Ho is a member of the much-disparaged Magic Colony, a month-long conglomeration of Magic players that featured such names a Brian Hegstad, Chris Benafel, and San Diego Top 8'er Jeff Cunningham. Ho himself is not running the Colony's "A" deck, a blue-red-black control deck that put co-designers Brian Kibler and Ben Rubin into Day 2. Instead he is playing a blue-green deck that aims to optimize Madness spells by accelerating them through Wild Mongrel and Aquamoeba.

Ruel won the right to play first. His opening grip was strong: Four lands, Innocent Blood, Shambling Swarm and Diabolic Tutor. Ho's was so bad he had to send it back. His six cards were acceptable. He started with a Werebear that Ruel Blooded away.

Ho tried again with Aquamoeba. Ruel was stuffed with removal, but chose to let it live and Diabolic Tutor for Mind Sludge. That gave Ho a window to bust out Arrogant Wurm. Still, Ruel Mind Twisted him the following turn, watching Roar of the Wurm, Upheaval, Wild Mongrel and Squirrel Nest hit the graveyard.

Ho topdecked Wild Mongrel. Ruel was unfazed. He Blooded away the Aquamoeba and dropped the Swarm. Ho thought hard. He chose to pass it back. For some reason, Ruel attacked before playing his second Swarm. That let Ho quash it with Logic and gave him a window to swing back. Ruel Edicted to lessen the pain.

Ho's next draw was a land to flash the Roar back. Ruel didn't like the look of it. He played Skeletal Scrying for three, leaving two mana up and going to five. He drew up another Edict and the seventh swamp! Now Ruel had plenty of kill at his disposal. Ho sacrificed the Roar token and threw his Wurm into the Swarm for one more damage.

Ruel Tutored for Nantuko Shade and Edicted away Ho's next two plays. Ho drew Basking Rootwalla just as Ruel hit nine mana, enough to kill him if he didn't chump. The next attack did him in.

Ruel 1 - Ho 0

Ho mulliganed his opening hand. He didn't have any Basking Rootwallas in hand to protect his two Mongrels from Ruel's Chainer's Edicts, but his Nantuko Blightcutter survived. Ruel Tutored up a Mind Sludge. Ho tried Standstill, but Ruel had enough swamps to Sludge him right out.

Ho had some fight left in him. Another Blightcutter came to his aid. Ruel Mutilated his team and threw down two Shades. Ho topdecked Squirrel Nest. Ruel just laughed as he showed him Rancid Earth, taking the second game

Ruel 2 - Ho 0

![](https://i.ormos.uk/19/78/1978a8ad86e938271b567f41b67f464bb8e8024896894d8fc1c9af7e1a1658eb.jpg)

Ruel paused, looking at his opening hand in Game 3. It held just two swamps. However, he was drawing. He couldn't mulligan that. Besides, he had Nantuko Shade.

Ho kicked off with Basking Rootwalla, then moved on to Blightcutter. Ruel Edicted and worked his mojo to find a third swamp for Rancid Earth. Ho simply played a second Blightcutter. Suddenly, Ruel was in dire straits.

Not even looking, he just dumped the top card of his library into play. It was a swamp. The Mutilate leapt from his hand onto the table. Ho calmly played Circular Logic. The Blightcutters brought Ruel to three.

Ruel slammed down the second Mutilate, and this time, it succeeded. Ho shrugged as he sent them to the graveyard. He had Squirrel Nest at the ready. Ruel played two Nantuko Shades, buying time to Tutor up Rancid Earth. Ho simply added Aquamoeba and Wild Mongrel to his team.

Ruel was forced to trade his shades for those last two, and fell to one in the process. Would the squirrels be his undoing? No! Another Mutilate was waiting for him! Ho answered with yet another Nest! Ruel had Swarm, Tutor and Edict, but couldn't go get Rancid Earth, because he was at threshold and it would kill him. It turned out not to matter, as Ho had the Logic anyways.

Ruel 2 - Ho 1

Again Ho had to throw back his first seven cards. He winced at his second set, but chose to keep. He started with Basking Rootwalla, Edicted away by Ruel, then followed up with Wild Mongrel. When Ruel missed his third land drop, Ho didn't even smile. He had troubles of his own. It was Nantuko Shade against Wild Mongrel.

Ho swung in and pitched Still Life, Aquamoeba and Upheaval to save, then played Standstill. Ruel drew his third land and Rancid Earthed the Island. Ho drew three. Down came Tarnished Citadel and Werebear. Ruel had Innocent Blood, but the dead Mongrel put Ho to threshold. Ho smashed him to twelve.

Ho wasn't done, not by a long shot. He added Nantuko Blightcutter to the mix. Ruel ripped his fourth swamp and chose to Tutor for Mutilate rather than play Shambling Swarm. He fell to six and tried to Mutilate Ho's board. Ho shook his head, tapping his Tarnished Citadel for blue, fuelling Circular Logic for the win.

Ruel 2 - Ho 2

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d4/57/d4573e280c9ebeb94ef247ec82655cf9924fa8801f14aaf47e74420183ebf6cc.jpg)

Ruel kept yet another sketchy two-lander for the final game. However, Ho had to mulligan yet again, not liking the looks of his six. Ruel started with Nantuko Shade while Ho showed only island. Next came the oft-mocked Tarnished Citadel. This time, however, it would prove its worth.

For three life, Ho got green mana to make a Werebear. Ruel had no removal, just Rancid Earth. He destroyed the island hoping to force Ho to use the Citadel for blue. Ho just played another. Ruel tried another Shade. Ho trumped with Blightcutter.

Ruel continued to draw six-mana spells instead of swamps. He smashed in for three past the Blightcutter, but it was clear things were quickly going from bad to second-place. Ho held back and made a Mongrel. Ruel sent his Shades in again, trading one for two pitches from Ho. Unfortunately, one of them was Roar of the Wurm.

Ho made the Roar token, and Ruel slumped in his chair. He tried another Shade, but it fell to Circular Logic. His last draw phase was, inevitably, the fourth swamp.

**Final Result:** Ken Ho defeats Olivier Ruel 3-2
