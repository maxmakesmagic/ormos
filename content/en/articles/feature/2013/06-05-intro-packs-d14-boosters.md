Another day, another set of awesome stuff to show you all. Yesterday you saw a lot of the ***Magic** 2014 Core Set* packaging from boosters to fat packs to *Deck Builder's Toolkit*. Today let's take a look at the special boosters you can get by playing ***Magic** 2014—Duels of the Planeswalkers*.

First up are the boosters you get by playing on the Android, iPad and Steam platforms. Each of these contain the new [Scavenging Ooze](https://web.archive.org/web/20130605000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scavenging+Ooze) promo.
