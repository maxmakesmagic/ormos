## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fa/86/fa86a13100fcce01f88d5adf32e563b594b46412367ecee890b3c564bfd2b669.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes three 15-card booster packs, one tournament pack, two premium cards, the *Mercadian Masques* novel, and a player’s guide magazine.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fa/86/fa86a13100fcce01f88d5adf32e563b594b46412367ecee890b3c564bfd2b669.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fa/86/fa86a13100fcce01f88d5adf32e563b594b46412367ecee890b3c564bfd2b669.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044252/http://magic.wizards.com/node/977026)  
 (\* Not available in all languages)  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fa/86/fa86a13100fcce01f88d5adf32e563b594b46412367ecee890b3c564bfd2b669.png)

### TOURNAMENT PACKS

The foundation for exciting Sealed Deck play. Includes 45 randomly assorted cards and 30 basic land cards.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/42/2f/422fe899582fdce561813ffc4e54fc764540fdef3b80c9e3b37517aef1095dbe.png)*Mercadian Masques* is the first set to include premium basic lands. It also features mechanics like Mercenaries, Rebels, and Spellshapers.

**Set Name:** *Mercadian Masques*  
**Block:** Set 1 of 3 of the *Masques* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 350

**Languages:** English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, Chinese Traditional

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044252/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/expert/mercadianmasques/features.asp)

**Design Team:** Mike Elliott (lead), Bill Rose, Mark Rosewater

**Development Team:** Henry Stern (lead), Mike Elliott, Robert Gutschera, William Jockusch, Mark Rosewater with contributions from Charlie Tin Canno and Beth Moursund
