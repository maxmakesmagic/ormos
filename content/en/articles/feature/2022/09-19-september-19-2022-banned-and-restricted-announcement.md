**Announcement Date**: September 19, 2022

**Pauper**:

[Aarakocra Sneak](https://web.archive.org/web/20220919000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aarakocra+Sneak) is banned.

[Stirring Bard](https://web.archive.org/web/20220919000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stirring+Bard) is banned.

[Underdark Explorer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220919000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underdark+Explorer) is banned.

[Vicious Battlerager](https://web.archive.org/web/20220919000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vicious+Battlerager) is banned.

***Magic Online* Effective Date**: September 19, 2022

**Tabletop Effective Date**: September 19, 2022

View the [list of all banned and restricted cards by format](https://web.archive.org/web/20220919000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/game-info/gameplay/rules-and-formats/banned-restricted).
