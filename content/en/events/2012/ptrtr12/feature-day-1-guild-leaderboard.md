Based on the top competitors after Day 1 here at Pro Tour *Return to Ravnica*, here's how the guilds are represented among the top ranked players! 

Be sure to choose your guild on [PlaneswalkerPoints.com](http://www.planeswalkerpoints.com/). If the winner of the Pro Tour is a member of your guild, you'll receive an exclusive achievement! Not sure what guild you are? Take the quiz at [GuildsofRavnica.com](http://www.guildsofravnica.com/) and find out! 

These rankings are based on the number of players identifying themselves as a member of this guild amongst the top players at the event.
