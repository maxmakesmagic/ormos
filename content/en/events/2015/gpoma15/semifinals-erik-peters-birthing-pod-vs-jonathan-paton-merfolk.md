Erik Peters, the Nebraska native rising as one of the last local heroes vying for a win, had defeated a dominant Blue-Red Delver deck in the quarterfinals and was now facing another aggressive deck. This time, it was Jonathan Paton with a slippery Merfolk deck. Paton had overcome the beasts and burn of a Four-Color Zoo deck, though that was nothing like the [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) deck Peters was packing.

Would it be the hometown hero with the public enemy number one, or the surprising stroke of a favorite tribe? This was the match to settle it.

**The Games**

Paton’s early plays were defensive, getting Aether Vial online and slowing down Peters’ assault. Paton even used [Phantasmal Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phantasmal+Image) to copy Peters’ Kicthen Finks. Peters used his removal to keep Paton on the defense, and set up an impressive array of creatures including two copies of [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise), [Wall of Roots](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Roots), and [Scavenging Ooze](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scavenging+Ooze).

However, Peters’ hand was emptied out.

Paton began to go wide with two copies of [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves), adding three 3/2 Elemental tokens to his side of the battlefield. Peters coolly played [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) on his turn to begin his chain of value.

“That was a top deck-and-a-half,” Paton said.

[Kitchen Finks](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kitchen+Finks) became [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino), and the tables had turned in Peters’ favor. Paton made a poor attack, and lost all of the army his had built.

“I attacked and thought ‘Oh. Wait.’ I shouldn’t have done that,” Paton said. “I should not have done that.”

From there, Peters marched virtually uncontested to complete control of the game.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpoma15/SF_peters.jpg)

“Let’s just go to Game 2,” Paton said as me gathered up his cards in play when he was left without any defenses.

[Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise) into [Kitchen Finks](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kitchen+Finks) was Peters’ aggressive play in the second game, though [Silvergill Adept](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silvergill+Adept) and [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) promised to hold the fort for Paton.

Then Peters played the indomitable [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino).

“When in doubt, just play [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino),” he said.

Paton fought back, copying the Rhino with [Phantasmal Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phantasmal+Image) and locking Peters’ down with [Tidebinder Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidebinder+Mage). It let Paton take the initiative to attack, but [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel) “untapped” Peters’ Rhino to block. It also let Peters pile back an attack that dropped Paton to 7 life.

Despite playing a few more creatures, Paton didn’t quite have the opportunity to attack for lethal: Peters sat at 22 life and enough blockers. Getting in with [Merrow Reejerey](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merrow+Reejerey), Paton had to leave back the team to soak up all the damage Peters would be throwing his way.

After falling to 1 life, the crack back was enough for Paton to strike through for lethal: Peters had miscalculated his padding against Paton’s potential attacks, and the games were now tied at one a piece.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpoma15/SF_paton.jpg)

The third game was a brisk start for Paton: [Tidebinder Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidebinder+Mage) to slow down Peters’ mana as a quick [Kira, Great Glass-Spinner](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kira%2C+Great+Glass-Spinner) joined the fray. It looked great for Paton until [Choke](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Choke) resolved.

“That’s why you kept that hand!” Paton said as he set aside his locked-away lands.

Without mana to keep advancing his battlefield, Paton quickly fell behind as [](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Orzhov+Pontiff)Orzhov Pontiff and more ate away his forces.

“What to use my two mana for?” Paton pondered aloud.

“I wasn’t joking when I said I’d rather be lucky than good,” Peters said. While both were in fair spirits, joking all the while, Peters deck didn’t slowing down in the least. [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) and more attacks followed. It was much more than Paton could recover from.

“[Choke](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Choke)!” Paton said in a dramatic flourish and laugh-pierced groan, after shaking Peters’ hand.

“I know man! That’s why it’s there!” Peters said. Sideboard cards can do some incredible things.

**Erik Peters defeated Jonathan Paton, 2-1, to advance to the finals.**
