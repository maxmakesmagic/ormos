It's almost Game Night! Releasing November 16, *Game Night* is an out-of-the-box multiplayer experience. The set has five decks playable out of the box that include a mix of reprints and five new cards.

Those new cards?

![Highcliff Felidar](https://media.wizards.com/2019/images/daily/Cah5bSXDdR.png)![Sphinx of Enlightenment](https://media.wizards.com/2019/images/daily/jucQwxzzoJ.png)![Calculating Lich](https://media.wizards.com/2019/images/daily/9T9yBBEZes.png)

![Fiendish Duo](https://media.wizards.com/2019/images/daily/mzMwR5GwnN.png)![Earthshaker Giant](https://media.wizards.com/2019/images/daily/fDTLk7vkIm.png)

Below, you'll find the deck list for each of the five decks. So, grab four friends, a table, and enjoy a Game Night in November.
