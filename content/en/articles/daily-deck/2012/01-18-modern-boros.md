Modern PTQ season is in full swing around the world—and across **Magic Online** as well! In a recent **Magic Online** PTQ, Kbr3 took advantage of the recent banning of Wild Nacatl by taking a build of Boros all the way to the top! Could this become a staple beatdown deck in the still-settling landscape of Modern? Check out the decklist, then take a look at the [schedule of **Magic Online** PTQs](https://web.archive.org/web/20130701233059/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/events/ptqmtgo/barcelona/12) to try and follow in Kbr3's footsteps!
