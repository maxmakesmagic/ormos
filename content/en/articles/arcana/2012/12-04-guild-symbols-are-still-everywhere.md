A few weeks ago, we saw [guild symbols in *Return to Ravnica* artwork](/en/articles/arcana/2012/10-23-guild-symbols-are-everywhere). But we only scratched the surface!

For example, the birds get into the act! Check out the armor on the chest of Judge's Familiar.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6f/f8/6ff856e583b317b84a9b640c8c80a5fa667d5338dee9b398293e63dc736c38fb.jpg) 

Judge's Familiar | Art by [Jack Wang](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22jack%20wang%22])

The Loxodon Smiter is covered in the Selesnya symbol, including on the hammer it smites with.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/cd/e6/cde618f3d7954d41200f1054d660e7270acf1aeaefb9cdf6ca5853f425876ed6.jpg) 

Loxodon Smiter | Art by [Ryan Barger](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22ryan%20barger%22])

The Splatter Thug is going to war with a tattered banner that used to have the Rakdos symbol on it.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a9/31/a9313a5decd6ac2d0a0bb310572efef6d5d0c4967de94897ccbb349b510b02b5.jpg) 

Splatter Thug | Art by [Kev Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22kev%20walker%22])

Even the arrowhead on Avenging Arrow clearly shows the guild of the archer:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/88/fb/88fb2e36e513913170bbd6579c96a348cb9e2d61cb3ff2e19777c74cb56ef4f0.jpg) 

Avenging Arrow | Art by [James Ryman](https://web.archive.org/web/20121204000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22james%20ryman%22])
