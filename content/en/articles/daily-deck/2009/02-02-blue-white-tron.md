Looking for something new and exciting to take to your next Extended tournament? Nicholas Gulledge took this 75 all the way to the blue envelope at a Pro Tour Qualifier in Mobile, Alabama. With powerful spells like Mindslaver accelerated onto the field of play by the Urzatron, Nicholas whomped through a field of Faeries, Zoo, Affinity, and more! You can find a PTQ near you [here](/en/events/2009/honolulu09-qualifiers/pro-tourhonolulu-2009-qualifiers). To find out how to get a chance to play with the brand new set this weekend, the first it becomes available for sale, visit [this page](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-launch-parties-4).
