Due to unexpected circumstances we aren't able to bring you Quentin Martin's Limited Information article this week. While we fully expect to have him back in time for next week, in the meantime we've included the links to the two draft viewers from the recent [U.S. Nationals](/en/events/2007/usnat07/all-hail-scott-vargas-the-omnipotent) for your Limited reading pleasure. How did Osyp Lebedowicz manage to pick up a fifth pick Riftwing Cloudskate and a seventh pick Careful Consideration at a table of undefeated players? How about Ichor Slick as an eighth pick for Michael Jacob in *Future Sight*? Thanks to the Draft Viewer, you can follow the cards around yourself by watching the draft from any player's perspective, any pick of the draft. Take a look into these two drafts, then drop into the thread to let everyone know where you think the drafters went right (or wrong). 

[Click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20070807000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/magic/draftools/draftviewer.asp?draftid=7_27_2007) to open the Draft Viewer for U.S. Nationals Draft 1, Pod 1. Players include 2007 U.S. National Team members Thomas Drake and Michael Jacob, 2007 U.S. Nationals Top 8 member Thomas Huteson, Pro Tour champion Osyp Lebedowicz, Pro Tour champion Chris Lachmann, Derek Thompson, Steve Collett, and Jimmy Young.

[Click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20070807000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/magic/draftools/draftviewer.asp?draftid=7_28_2007) to open the Draft Viewer for U.S. Nationals Draft 2, Pod 1. Players include 2007 U.S. National Team members Thomas Drake and Michael Jacob, 2004 U.S. National champion and 2007 U.S. Nationals Top 8 member Craig Krempels, Andy Ewen, Adam Yurchick, David Ochoa, Alex Majlaton, and Frederick Chang.
