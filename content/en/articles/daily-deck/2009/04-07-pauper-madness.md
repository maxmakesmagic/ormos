Looking for a chance to use some of those commons you haven't played with in a while? The Pauper format is just for you! It allows players only cards of the common variety for building their decks. In fact, **Magic Online** recently held a Premier Event for the format. BryTheFryGuy managed to make the Top 8 with this take on the popular Martyr of Sands strategy. Using Grim Harvest and Disturbed Burial, Bry could set up a loop with Martyr of Sands that would continually generate massive amounts of life gain!
