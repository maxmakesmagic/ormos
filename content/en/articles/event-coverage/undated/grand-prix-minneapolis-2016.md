## GRAND PRIX MINNEAPOLIS 2016

TOP 8 COVERAGE

Yesterday, 1530 players arrived at the Minneapolis Convention Center with dreams of being crowned Grand Prix Minneapolis Champion. Players fought their way through nine rounds of Standard swiss on Day 1 and only the players with the best records returned today for another six rounds of swiss before we cut to the Top 8 Elimination rounds.

Standard seemed incredibly diverse as we entered the second day of competition with over twenty unique archetypes present in the top 100 players. However, when the dust settled and the Top 8 was set, various aggressive Human decks proved their worth in the new Standard metagame as they accounted for five of the Top 8 slots.

Alexander Johnson and his Green-White Tokens deck thrashed through a Top 8 filled with opponents who were Human in more way than one. Max McVety fell in the quarterfinals, then Matt Severa suffered a similar fate in the semis, finally, Johnson defeated Shota Takao to earn himself a trophy and the title of Grand Prix Minneapolis 2016 Champion!

The players battled their way through nine rounds of Standard and only those with the best records earned the right to return this morning for another six rounds of Standard before we cut to the Top 8 elimination rounds. Four players emerged from Day 1 with undefeated records: (18) Yuuya Watanabe, Max McVety, Paul Dean, and Matt Severa lead the pack as we enter the second day of competition.

Standard continues to evolve and new decks continue to rise up from obscurity to the very top of the standings. Innovative versions of Red-Green Ramp and Bant Humans seem to be the breakout decks of this weekend thus far. Will these decks continue their meteoric rise to dominance?
