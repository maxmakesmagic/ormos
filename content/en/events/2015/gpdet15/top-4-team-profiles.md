### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpdet15/gpdet15_t4_parker-costa-shenhar.jpg)  
 Brock Parker, Matt Costa, and Shahar Shenhar

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpdet15/t4_costa.jpg)**Name:** Matt Costa

**Age:** 22

**Hometown:** Eastham, MA

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** Top 8 Pro Tour *Dark Ascension*; winner of Grand Prix Baltimore.

**What colors were your first Sealed deck? What was your record and best card?** I went 7-2 with red-green. [Leaf Gilder](https://web.archive.org/web/20150816000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leaf+Gilder) was my best card.

**What about your second?** 5-0 with black-red, [Molten Vortex](https://web.archive.org/web/20150816000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Molten+Vortex) was my best card.

**What was a card you wanted for a deck, that your teammate needed to play?**[Lightning Javelin](https://web.archive.org/web/20150816000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Javelin).

**What was the most helpful thing your teammates did for you in the tournament?** They mulliganned bad hands.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpdet15/t4_parker.jpg)**Name:** Brock Parker

**Age:** 34

**Hometown:** Las Vegas, NV

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** Team wins at a Pro Tour and Grand Prix; winner at Grand Prix Pittsburgh.

**What colors were your first Sealed deck? What was your record and best card?** Blue-black, I went 7-2 and my best card was Hangy b. ([Hangarback Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20150816000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hangarback+Walker)).

**What about your second?** Green-blue, going 5-0. [Willbreaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20150816000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Willbreaker) was my best card.

**What was a card you wanted for a deck, that your teammate needed to play?** [Evolving Wilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20150816000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Evolving+Wilds).

**What was the most helpful thing your teammates did for you in the tournament?** Won their matches.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpdet15/t4_shenhar.jpg)**Name:** Shahar Shenhar

**Age:** 21

**Hometown:** Jerusalem, Israel

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** Two-time (defending) World Champion; three Grand Prix titles; lost to Matt Nass, Jesse Hampton and Jacob Wilson in the finals of Team Limited at Grand Prix Nashville last year.

**What colors were your first Sealed deck? What was your record and best card?** I went 7-2 with red-white, and [Molten Vortex](https://web.archive.org/web/20150816000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Molten+Vortex) was the best card.

**What about your second?** I was mono-white, and [Celestial Flare](https://web.archive.org/web/20150816000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Celestial+Flare) was the best card.

**What was a card you wanted for a deck, that your teammate needed to play?** [Chandra's Fury](https://web.archive.org/web/20150816000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Fury).

**What was the most helpful thing your teammates did for you in the tournament?** Brock never watched my matches!
