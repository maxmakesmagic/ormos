With *Scars of Mirrodin*, there is finally a zero-cost 1/1 in **Magic**. And it's been a long time coming. Come with us now as we take a look at the rich history of ![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless0.gif)!

There are also cards [Shifting Wall](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shifting+Wall) and [Phyrexian Marauder](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Marauder), which could technically cost zero mana but go straight to the graveyard. They don't count. And neither do creatures with alternate ways of getting onto the battlefield, like [Allosaurus Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Allosaurus+Rider) or [Spirit of the Night](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spirit+of+the+Night). Or [Cheatyface](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cheatyface). Looks, let's just do this, okay? Any creatures that aren't here were skipped on purpose.

### *Antiquities*

The first free creature was the humble [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter), which made its appearance in 1994's *Antiquities*:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d7/b9/d7b9217256882f06696b6ab79ed090990df94ece9c2351bf1916c57fb49691a8.jpg)

[Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Ornithopter) | Art by [Amy Weber](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Amy+Weber%22%5D)

![Ornithopter](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ornithopter)

  

[Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter) went through a number of different looks, but we [already covered that](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/ornithopters-2010-05-06). The important thing today is that ![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless0.gif) could get you a 0/2 flyer.

### *Legends*

*Legends* introduced the first non-artifact creatures that cost zero mana. And there were three of them! In exchange for not being artifacts, they lost a point of toughness and the ability to fly. Frankly, even for ![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless0.gif), the Kobolds aren't a very good deal.

These days, it's unlikely that we'd print three functionally identical creatures in a single set, even if one of them did get a bonus from [Rohgahh of Kher Keep](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rohgahh+of+Kher+Keep). It's also worth noting that all the card names are plural, which means that even when you get a bunch of Kobolds in one place, they still don't add up to enough to get above zero power.

### *Alliances*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a0/43/a043fca86862c84ebfa67f011eacfdc5f0aea8b3356c2affa192f079b5ce9c73.jpg)

[Shield Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Shield%2BSphere) | Art by [Alan Rabinowitz](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Alan+Rabinowitz%22%5D)

![Shield Sphere](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Shield+Sphere)

  

[Shield Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shield+Sphere) is larger than [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter), but it also exchanges the ability to fly with the inability to attack.

### *Visions*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/57/72/57727bfa0ed196d6053dacad192a13df94cd5f355f196760f7dc251089357068.jpg)

[Phyrexian Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Phyrexian%2BWalker) | Art by [Bryan Talbot](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Bryan+Talbot%22%5D)

![Phyrexian Walker](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Phyrexian+Walker)

  

With [Phyrexian Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Walker), we got closer to the [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter) of old. Unlike [Shield Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shield+Sphere), it's able to attack. If that seems like a good idea.

### *Future Sight*

Does [Dryad Arbor](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dryad+Arbor) count? It doesn't have a mana cost of ![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless0.gif), although it certainly costs zero mana to play. Just in case, here it is. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d8/9c/d89c40ee8b3949b1cbfa1a5735a9f4b298c5575807a8c56f60139d89edb5c3f1.jpg)

[Dryad Arbor](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Dryad%2BArbor) | Art by [Eric Fortune](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Eric+Fortune%22%5D)

![Dryad Arbor](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Dryad+Arbor)

  

Hey, it's the first time you could get a 1/1 for no mana! But it still comes with strings attached, since it takes up a land drop. Still, [Dryad Arbor](https://web.archive.org/web/20101118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dryad+Arbor) didn't seem to break anything when it came out, so the stage was set (a few years later) for...
