### What’s the most underrated card in *Magic Origins* Limited formats?

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/gpsan15_qq_edel.jpg)  
*Willy Edel*

**Willy Edel:** [Aspiring Aeronaut](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aspiring+Aeronaut) - it's two flying bodies, and there’s also the artifact synergy to consider.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/gpsan15_qq_saito.jpg)  
*Tomoharu Saito*

**Tomoharu Saito:** [Volcanic Rambler](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Volcanic+Rambler). It’s good at closing out both short games and long games.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/gpsan15_qq_denicola.jpg)  
*Nicolás de Nicola*

**Nicolás de Nicola:** [Fiery Impulse](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Impulse).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/gpsan15_qq_sanchez.jpg)  
*Maximiliano Sanchez*

**Maximiliano Sanchez:** [Cobblebrute](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cobblebrute).
