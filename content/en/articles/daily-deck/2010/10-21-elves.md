Elves are back baby! Jacob Baugh was the champion of the recent [StarCityGames.com](http://www.starcitygames.com/magic/standard/20274_Live_Coverage_of_StarCityGamescom_Open_Series_Nashville.html) Standard Open in Nashville, Tennessee and he did it on the back of the game's greenest tribe. Ezuri, Renegade Leader allows Jacob to Overrun at will, and ![2 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/f9/cc/f9cc8a15b009997919d58ad79964384351495d1b6c9231f938316577453038a8.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif) is not a lot of mana for his deck to come up with. After all, Llanowar Elves, Arbor Elf, and Elvish Archdruid all contribute to the lands he's playing. If Ezuri isn't willing to play ball, Jacob can also rely on Joraga Warcaller and Eldrazi Monument.
