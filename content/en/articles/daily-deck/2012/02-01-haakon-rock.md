In a recent **Magic Online** PTQ, Wirecat brought a crazy new Modern deck to the (virtual) table that has stirred up a lot of discussion. While Burning Vengeance was made to work with flashback, Wirecat discovered it *also* works with the *Eventide* retrace mechanic and the *Coldsnap* rare Haakon, Stromgald Scourge! This unique innovation has drawn the attention of several noted players, and both [Mike Flores](/en/articles/top-decks/2012/01-26-five-things-about-modern) and [Brian David-Marshall](/en/articles/theweekthatwas/2012/01-27-dark-comprehension) wrote about it last week. Wirecat made the Top 8 of his PTQ with this deck—try it for yourself and [see if you can do the same](/en/events/2012/barcelona12-qualifiers/qualifiers-for-pro-tour-avacyn-restored-in-barcelona)! 

Wirecat's Burning Vengeance
