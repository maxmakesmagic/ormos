Yesterday, there was an 80-person Modern Confrontation event for people who are ready for the Pro Tour to be here. There were some good decks in Top 8, and it's a great way to scan just a sample of the expected field for Pro Tour *Oath of the Gatewatch*. Spoiler Alert: Very few of the expected winners in the Twin ban were to be found. But there were a couple more controlling decks that have emerged already.

Henry Lam won the whole event with a White-Blue deck with [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20160131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict)s, [Dragonlord Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20160131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai), and the recursion, enter-the-battlefield strategy with [Kitchen Finks](https://web.archive.org/web/20160131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kitchen+Finks), [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20160131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) and [Vendilion Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20160131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vendilion+Clique) to be reset with [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20160131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel) and a [Sun Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20160131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sun+Titan).

Additionally, I'd like to make special mention of Scott Peters' Abzan Wish deck. It's your normal Abzan, but with three copies of [Glittering Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20160131000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glittering+Wish) to gain access to your powerful sideboard silver bullets in the first game.
