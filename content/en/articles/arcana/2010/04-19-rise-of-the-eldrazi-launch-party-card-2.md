R*ise of the Eldrazi* had its prerelease last weekend. That means that it's going be officially on sale this Friday. And *that* means that it's [Launch Party](https://web.archive.org/web/20100419000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/releases) time! And if you play in a *Rise of the Eldrazi* Launch Party (and supplies hold out), you'll get one of these!
