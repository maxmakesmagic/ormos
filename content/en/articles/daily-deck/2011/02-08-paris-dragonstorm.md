The 2006 **Magic: The Gathering** World Championships were held in Paris, France. The winner? Japan's Makihito Mihara, who took the top slot against native son Gabriel Nassif in the Finals. Mihara's innovative Dragonstorm list used *Time Spiral* charge lands to power Gigadrowse, giving the pro an almost unbeatable advantage against control decks of the era. The Pro Tour is returning to Paris! Tune in to [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20110418111736/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Events.aspx?) this weekend for the Pro Tour and all the other festivities as **Magic** Weekend kicks of the 2011 season in Paris!
