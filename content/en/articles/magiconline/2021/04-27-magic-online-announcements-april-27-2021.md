## *Pioneer Cube* Debuts Tomorrow

Created by veteran *Magic* and *Magic Online* designer Tom LaPille, *Pioneer Cube* takes the popular Constructed format and distills it into an incredible Cube experience. Read all about it tomorrow morning on [MTGO.com](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-home), then you'll have a week to play it on *Magic Online*!

* * *

## *Strixhaven* Season Alternative Play Calendar

Classes are now in session, with guest-lecture alternative play events available weekly. Each of these events begins on Wednesday at 10 a.m. Pacific time and continues for one week unless otherwise specified. *Strixhaven* season ends with the MTGO launch of *Modern Horizons 2* in early June.

*   April 28: *Pioneer Cube*
*   May 5: Emma Handy's *Proliferate Cube* returns – One of the most popular spotlight cubes from 2020 is back with an update. You can *counter* on having a good time for a week in early May!
*   May 12: *Legacy Cube* returns – For two weeks, we're reviving a cube that's been dormant for almost a full year. For those of us who love the power level of *Vintage Cube* without the huge spikes that come with actual power, this is the cube to watch for.
*   May 26: New to the Spotlight Cube Series – As usual for new spotlight cubes, we'll have more information as we get closer.

## Limited Super Qualifier Friday

On Friday, April 30, there will be a *Strixhaven* Sealed Super Qualifier with a start time of 2 p.m. PT. The entry fee is 40 MOCS Qualifier Points, 40 Event Tickets, or 400 Play Points. The top two finishers qualify for the *Strixhaven* Championship on *MTG Arena*.

Learn more about Super Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021#sq).

* * *

## Pioneer Super Qualifier Saturday

On Saturday, May 1, there will be a Pioneer Super Qualifier with a special start time of 4 a.m. PT. The entry fee is 40 MOCS Qualifier Points, 40 Event Tickets, or 400 Play Points. The top two finishers qualify for the *Strixhaven* Championship on *MTG Arena*.

Learn more about Super Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021#sq).

* * *

## Modern Qualifier Monday

On Monday, May 3, there will be a Modern Qualifier with a special start time of 2 a.m. PT *(yes, early morning in the Americas)*. The event costs 40 QPs, and the winner will earn an invitation to the *Strixhaven* Championship event on *MTG Arena*.

Learn more about Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021#ptq).

* * *

## Showcase Qualifiers

Players who earned entry into the Showcase Qualifier will be squaring off for an invitation to the Champions Showcase. The entry fee for Showcase Qualifiers is a format token that matches the format of the Showcase. As with QPs, after the season's Showcase Qualifiers are finished, all unused Format Tokens will be removed from players' accounts. Each Showcase Qualifier is a scheduled Swiss-style event with a Top 8 playoff. The winner of each Showcase Qualifier earns a spot in the final Showcase event and a chance to earn their share of $70,000 and an upcoming Premier Event invitation.

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format Challenges are the first step toward qualifying for Premier Play through your favorite *Magic* formats and provide excellent prizes for those looking to test their mettle in their favorite formats. To learn more about Premier Play qualification, click [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021).

*Magic Online*'s Format Challenges run once on Saturdays and once on Sundays for each Constructed format! On weekends featuring Showcase Challenge events, the Format Challenge that starts at either 6 a.m., 8 a.m., or 10 a.m. Pacific time is pre-empted by the Showcase Challenge—Format Challenges scheduled at other times remain in place. *NOTE: Pauper and Standard Challenges are never pre-empted.*
