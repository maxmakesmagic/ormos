[*The Brothers' War*](https://web.archive.org/web/20221028000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/the-brothers-war) hits stores worldwide on November 18, and for Commander fans, we have two new Commander decks featuring epic legends, reprints, and all-new cards.

![Isperia](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/en_82nUWbewyd.png)

Urza's Iron Alliance

 

![Starter](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/en_snwun3DrwE.png)

Mishra's Burnished Banner

You can check out the cards in [*The Brothers' War* Commander Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2022/10-28-the-brothers-war-commander-card-image-gallery), as well as the regular cards in [*The Brothers' War* Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2022/09-29-the-brothers-war-card-image-gallery) and [Booster Fun variants of *The Brothers' War* cards](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2022/10-03-the-brothers-war-variant-card-image-gallery).

Plus, for a rundown of all the cards across the set and where you can find them, check out our [Collecting *The Brothers' War* article](/en/articles/feature/2022/10-27-whats-inside-the-brothers-war-boosters)!

Look for *The Brothers' War* Commander decks at your [local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20221028000000/https://locator.wizards.com/), online through [Amazon](https://gtly.to/SrLywky5i), and everywhere you find *Magic: The Gathering* products sold.

***(Editor's Note: The decklists below pull the newest printing of each card automatically from*** [***Gatherer***](https://web.archive.org/web/20221028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Default.aspx)***, including printings which are not in this product. Decklists are not card-for-card product displays but rather interactive lists of the cards included in each deck.)***
