Pro Tour coverage reporter and The Week That Was columnist Brian David-Marshall has been brewing all sorts of decks for the world of new Standard. The above list is his take on a Warp World deck that features Ob Nixilis, the Fallen. When the legendary black creature enters the battlefield after Warp World resolves, he forces the opponent to lose 3 life for each land entering with him. That generally leads to a very dead opponent! Brian posted the list on his **Magic** blog, Top8Magic.com.
