It's been a while since I've played anything in my own draw step, but I heard a great story from Gold level pro Fabrizio Anteri, who is attending Grand Prix Los Angeles on the way back from the Pro Tour in Honolulu and it currently sitting at a comfortable 12-2 record with his controlling take on Jeskai Wins.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpla14/Fabrizio%20Anteri.JPG)

  

"I was playing a match with my Jeskai deck against a Temur deck, and at some point during the game, my opponent passed without attacking with his [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic) or [Rattleclaw Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rattleclaw+Mystic)," Fabrizio began. "I didn't have a blocker on the table, so that was pretty suspicious. Since had [Yavimaya Coast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yavimaya+Coast), [Rattleclaw Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rattleclaw+Mystic), and [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic) untapped, I expected that he would have [Temur Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temur+Charm) in hand. If he had [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike), he would've attaked with [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic), but since he didn't, I was fairly sure he had [Temur Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temur+Charm). This was my fifth turn and I was planning to cast [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider), so [Temur Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temur+Charm) would be able to counter it. I then drew my card for the turn, and it was a [Magma Jet](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Jet)." 

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Temur%20Charm)

Anteri could get around [Temur Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temur+Charm) by burning an Elf in his main phase, but then his opponent would float a mana in response. So, if Anteri would do that, then he would not be able to resolve [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider) in his first main phase and attack with it. Instead, he informed his opponent that he was going to [Magma Jet](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Jet) the [Rattleclaw Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rattleclaw+Mystic) in his draw step. Mana you tap in the draw step doesn't float over to the main phase, so this guaranteed him a window to resolve [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider) before his combat phase. 

The draw step is kind of an obscure place in the world of *Magic*: the Gathering timings. Sometimes you play [Jeskai Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Charm) on [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix) in your opponent's draw step. Sometimes you cast [Mardu Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Charm) in their draw step in the hope of snagging [End Hostilities](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=End+Hostilities) before they can cast it. But playing a card in your own draw step has got to be the pinnacle of timing.
