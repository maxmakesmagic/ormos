*What's your favorite [Timmy](/en/articles/makingmagic/2009/03-09-designing-for-timmy) card, and why?* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b4/9a/b49a97d847733a73985c5d85979f9c0df999dfc0380369be66282a7035d6c565.jpg)

**Mons Johnson:** My favorite Timmy card is probably the *USS Iowa*. At 68 points, this monster battleship can hit hard and move fast, and is very difficult to kill!

Oh, you meant **Magic** card. My bad ....

The Timmy card that I had the most fun with was definitely Gurzigost. With a 6/8 body, 'Gost was the phattest guy in the time of madness. It came at a reasonable cost of a mere ![3 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/7f/5d/7f5d2f1e55eaa9d19a9a23027b63bfbb5b39dbf8642160dda1a477a03df23a6a.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif) and could do a lot of damage directly to the opponent, particularly if you had a Fiery Temper in hand. Its upkeep was an actual advantage in some circumstances, most notably while you had a Wild Research in play.
