Kai Budde is often heralded as the worlds' best **Magic** player, and one of his most celebrated victories came at Pro Tour–New Orleans in 2001. A series of bannings had removed cards like [Necropotence](https://web.archive.org/web/20090819000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Necropotence) from the format, rendering the Trix combo deck, which used the infamous enchantment to draw many cards in order to force through [Illusions of Grandeur](https://web.archive.org/web/20090819000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Illusions+of+Grandeur) only to [Donate](https://web.archive.org/web/20090819000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Donate) it to a hapless opponent, obsolete. Or was it? Budde proved otherwise using a blue-red version of the deck to get there anyway. Read more from the event in [the official event coverage](https://web.archive.org/web/20090819000000/http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/event.asp?event=PTNOR01).
