**Date** 

*   May 15 and May 16, 2010

**Format** 

*   Standard-constructed format
*   Deck lists are required

**Invitations** 

*   Dubbo and Townsville National Qualifiers both award 4 invitations to Australia Nationals
*   All other Australia National Qualifiers award 8 invitations to Australia Nationals.
*   All New Zealand National Qualifiers award 8 invitations to New Zealand Nationals

**Tournament Length** 

*   50 minutes per round
     Number of Swiss rounds based on attendance according to the following chart:

**Advancement** 

*   All players may participate in all Swiss rounds
*   If a National Qualifier offers 4 invitations to Nationals, the top 8 players after the final Swiss round will advance to the top 8 single-elimination finals.
*   If a National Qualifier offers 8 invitations to Nationals, no top 8 single-elimination will be run. Prizes and invitations will be awarded on Swiss standings only.

**Top 8** 

*   Top 8 matches will be best 2 of 3
*   Top 8 matches will have no time limit. Players are expected to play at a reasonable pace and finish their matches within 50 minutes.
*   National Qualifiers that offer 4 invitations to Nationals will have 1 single-elimination round to determine invitation and prizewinners.
*   Where necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will still be used to determine final order in the standings

**Notes** 

*   32K, REL Competitive
*   Players are responsible for knowing all DCI rules
*   Players must bring pen, paper, card sleeves and appropriate counters
