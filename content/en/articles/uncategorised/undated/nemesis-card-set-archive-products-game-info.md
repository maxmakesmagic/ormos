![](https://i.ormos.uk/67/80/67806a7979bdc0b3eb8fffecf2bfcf73f95ab250656ddf9de05ba8b84252b1fd.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes six 15-card booster packs, two premium cards, the *Nemesis* novel, and a special *Top Deck* magazine pullout with pictures of all cards in the set.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/67/80/67806a7979bdc0b3eb8fffecf2bfcf73f95ab250656ddf9de05ba8b84252b1fd.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/67/80/67806a7979bdc0b3eb8fffecf2bfcf73f95ab250656ddf9de05ba8b84252b1fd.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/56/ca/56caefa6b977797f827014eaa3bf4a8d4405b2287d1d05a111984237d3675153.png)

This 143-card, black-bordered set includes randomly inserted foil premium cards, as well as new mechanics like Laccoliths and Fading.

**Set Name:** *Nemesis*  
**Block:** Set 2 of 3 of the *Masques* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 143

**Languages:** English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, Chinese Traditional

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044758/http://archive.wizards.com/Sideboard/mtg_article.asp?sb20000201e)

**Design Team:** Mike Elliott (lead), Bill Rose, Mark Rosewater

**Development Team:** Mike Elliott (lead), Charlie Catena, Paul Peterson, Teeuwynn Woodruff
