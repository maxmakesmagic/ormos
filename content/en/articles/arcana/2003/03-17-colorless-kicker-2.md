In the *Invasion* block, cards with the kicker ability usually contained a part of the [Coalition Symbol](https://web.archive.org/web/20030317000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/arcana/31) in their artwork—usually the part associated with the color of the kicker.

Cards with colorless kickers, however, usually contain the entire symbol in their art. Below are close-ups of four cards from Invasion that have colorless kickers. The Coalition Symbol in the art has been pointed out, but do you know what the cards are? Answers below.
