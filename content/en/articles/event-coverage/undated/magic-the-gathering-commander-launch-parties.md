 ***Magic: The Gathering** - Commander* Launch Parties are a great way to celebrate the release of the latest **Magic** multiplayer set. Play, trade, spend time with friends and get a cool promo card! All participants receive one of five different premium oversized Legendary Creature promo card exclusive to the Launch Party (while supplies last).

### Where Can I Play on June 17-20, 2011?

Local Stores — Launch Parties are happening worldwide the weekend of June 17-20, 2011! Check out our [tournament locator](https://web.archive.org/web/20130628212820/http://ww2.wizards.com/StoreAndEventLocator/) to find an event near you. Contact your local store to confirm the event venue location.

### What Happens There?

Try out the newest multiplayer product, ***Magic: The Gathering** – Commander* in a fun, casual setting!

 ***Magic: The Gathering** – Commander* Launch Parties will use an open play format. Players will be placed into game groups of 3-5 players. When a game finishes, players can play again or be regrouped into another game with new players.

### What Do I Get?

Each player receives:  

*   1 of 5 premium oversized promo cards (while supplies last).

*   Edric, Spymaster of Trest
*   Basandra, Battle Seraph
*   Skullbriar, the Walking Grave
*   Vish Kal, Blood Arbiter
*   Nin, the Pain Artist

*   1 ***Magic: The Gathering** – Commander* deck
*   1 Achievement Card

The Achievement card is a fun activity for players to complete. Earn the satisfaction of completing all the tasks over the course of the Launch Party!
