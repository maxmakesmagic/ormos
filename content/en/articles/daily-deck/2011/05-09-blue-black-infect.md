Looking for something fun to try at your upcoming Standard event? Why not give Blue-Black Infect a shot? The deck foregoes the usual Blue-Black Control strategy of killing an opponent via decking or damage to instead focus on a poisonous end game. The poison plan paid off at the recent TCGPlayer.com Wizard World Series in Anaheim in the hands of Thomas Scharwark, who managed to win the entire tournament with this particular build!
