Learn more about Pro Tour *Kaladesh's* Top 8 competitors—including who they are, what they’ve done in the game, and more—by taking a look through their profiles!

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/ptkld/t8hull.jpg)Name:** Ben Hull

**Age:** 33

**Hometown:** Vancouver, Canada

**Occupation:** Research Engineer

**Limited Record:** 5-1

**Constructed Record:** 7-2

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 

Does a 2K *Magic Online* Limited rating count?

**Did you team up with anyone to practice for this Pro Tour? Who?**

I drafted with random locals. I did not practice Standard.

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?**

Green-Blue no removal and red-green planeswalkers. The red-green deck was my favorite, because planeswalkers.

**What Standard deck are you playing and why? What card from *Kaladesh* made it possible?**

Red-White Vehicles. I wanted something reasonably aggressive, since I had very little time to prepare.

**What Standard deck, other than your own, impressed you the most this weekend?**

The [Metalwork Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20161015000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metalwork+Colossus) deck was sweet!

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?**

I'd have to take time off work for the rest of the year's Pro Tours.

I can take it easy for the rest of the season.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/ptkld/t8_matsoukas.jpg)Name:** Makis Matsoukas

**Age:** 24

**Hometown:** Athens, Greece

**Occupation:** Student

**Limited Record:** 5-1

**Constructed Record:** 8-0-2

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 

Was unbeaten against Socrates Rozakeas, but recently lost the streak.

**Did you team up with anyone to practice for this Pro Tour? Who?**

My friends back in Greece, Panagiotis Savvidis, George Volakis, Socrates Rozakeas, Bill Tsorbatzidis, Bill Chronopoulos, and many more that helped me get here. Without them this would not have been possible.

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?**

U/W Fliers, W/B Artifacts. No favorites really.

**What Standard deck are you playing and why? What card from *Kaladesh* made it possible?**

R/W “Tokens.” Both aggressive 1-drops and [Smuggler's Copter](https://web.archive.org/web/20161015000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smuggler%27s+Copter) are the deck's core.

**What Standard deck, other than your own, impressed you the most this weekend?**

The decks that utilize [Torrential Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20161015000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrential+Gearhulk). The card seemed fine, but it is much stronger than I initially thought.

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?**

Getting to play in the World Championship would be pretty special.

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/ptkld/t8_dagen.jpg)Name:** Pierre Dagen

**Age:** 29

**Hometown:** Paris, France

**Occupation:** Healthcare Consultant

**Limited Record:** 5-1

**Constructed Record:** 8-2

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 

2nd PT Theros, 3 GP Top 8s, 3rd at WMC 2015

**Did you team up with anyone to practice for this Pro Tour? Who?**

Team Renaissance, a gathering of the top French players like PT Champ Remi Fortier, Silver Pros

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?**

Draft 1: B/G, 3-0. It is by far my favorite, flexible, deep, with the ability to splash for removals.

Draft 2: U/W, 2-1. I do not like it, but [Smuggler's Copter](https://web.archive.org/web/20161015000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smuggler%27s+Copter) was good.

**What Standard deck are you playing and why? What card from *Kaladesh* made it possible?**

Blue-Red [Dynavolt Tower](https://web.archive.org/web/20161015000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dynavolt+Tower), because that is the best way we found to beat “Level 1” (B/G Delirium and R/W Vehicles). Kevin Chiche had the original idea. Obviously it depends on [Dynavolt Tower](https://web.archive.org/web/20161015000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dynavolt+Tower)!

**What Standard deck, other than your own, impressed you the most this weekend?**

[Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20161015000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel), obviously. It is more consistent than I thought, and much better at beating control than one would think.

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?**

Proof that I still progress as a *Magic* player even though I cannot grind as much as before, and a spot in my dream tournament: The World Championship. But more importantly, it would show how well my team worked.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/ptkld/t8_manner.jpg)Name:** Joey Manner

**Age:** 21

**Hometown:** Somerville, New Jersey

**Occupation:** Owner and Operator of The Only Game in Town

**Limited Record:** 3-2-1

**Constructed Record:** 9-1

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 

Medium Pro Tour/Grand Prix Performances

**Did you team up with anyone to practice for this Pro Tour? Who?**

Teamed up with a group of North East grinders. We played every single game on *Magic Online*.

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?**

U/G Energy and R/W Control (14 removal spells)

**What Standard deck are you playing and why? What card from *Kaladesh* made it possible?**

U/W [Flash](https://web.archive.org/web/20161015000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flash). It performed really well against almost everything, has game against both aggro and control. [Smuggler's Copter](https://web.archive.org/web/20161015000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smuggler%27s+Copter) was the best card in the deck.

**What Standard deck, other than your own, impressed you the most this weekend?**

Grixis Control. I was working on a similar list but locked in U/W because I wanted to focus on limited preparation.

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?**

It would be a dream come true. I've imagined being on the Sunday Stage ever since I learned what the Pro Tour was.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/ptkld/t8_lee.jpg)Name:** Lee Shi Tian

**Age:** 29

**Hometown:** Hong Kong

**Occupation:** Accountant

**Limited Record:** 5-1

**Constructed Record:** 7-2-1

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 

Four, now five, Pro Tour Top 8s. Seven Grand Prix Top 8s. Four Hong Kong National Championship titles.

**Did you team up with anyone to practice for this Pro Tour? Who?**

Team MTG Mint Card and Huang Hao-Shan, Jason Chung, Nam Sung Wook, Yam Wing Chun, Eduardo Sajgalik, Tomoharu Saito, Katsuhiro Mori, Andrea Mengucci, Christian Calcano, and Javier Dominguez.

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?**

Red-Green Creatures. They are just too big. [Peema Outrider](https://web.archive.org/web/20161015000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peema+Outrider) is just great.

**What Standard deck are you playing and why? What card from *Kaladesh* made it possible?**

Red-White Vehicles with [Cultivator's Caravan](https://web.archive.org/web/20161015000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cultivator%27s+Caravan). It's a two-cost 5/5 if you use it properly. Big shout out to Chan Sju Wing and Wu Kon Fai. Two years ago we build [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20161015000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy) on the street and now put me into another Top 8. We will never forget that.

**What Standard deck, other than your own, impressed you the most this weekend?**

Grixis Control from Shota Yasooka. True Masterpiece.

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?**

I'm really not sure now. I've lost one semifinal and three quarterfinals before. Will this be my one time?
