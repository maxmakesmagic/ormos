On first read, many players thought Hatred was a junk rare. It didn't take long for people to realize that a first-turn Carnophage followed by two Dark Rituals into Hatred meant a second-turn kill! Suicide Black, already a popular aggro deck of the day, quickly incorporated the windfall of a strategy into its main deck. In no time, players all over the world were trying to put together a playset of the powerful rare.
