Of the eight players that sat down for the quarterfinals at Grand Prix Memphis, fully five were sleeving up Abzan Control decks. The pairings provided one Abzan-on-Abzan mirror match, while the rest of the Abzan builds would have to best one of the Sultai Control or White-Red Aggro strategies that had also battled their way to the Top 8.

# Brad Nelson vs. Eric Rath

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmem15/quarters-rath.jpg)  
*Rath gets out of the gates quickly in Game 1.*

Nelson fell behind quickly in the Game 1, when he didn't have a [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight) to answer an early [Hordeling Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hordeling+Outburst) from Rath's White-Red Aggro deck. In the blink of an eye Nelson was overrun and the quarterfinalists were off to Game 2.

[Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion)s came out of the sideboard for Nelson, a strategy that had worked wonders for Abzan Control mages all weekend. Nelson had one on his second turn, halting the tokens from Rath's [Goblin Rabblemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Rabblemaster). Rath was stuck with only three lands and forced to cast his [Ashcloud Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashcloud+Phoenix) as a morph—a sure sign that he was in dire straits and they were soon on to the decider.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmem15/quarters-nelson.jpg)  
*Nelson leaned on his [Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion)s to turn the tide after he lost Game 1.*

Rath got off to another lightning-fast start in Game 3, with two copies of [Seeker of the Way](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seeker+of+the+Way) and a [Chained to the Rocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chained+to+the+Rocks) on Nelson's Lion threatening to put the game away quickly. But a third-turn [Drown in Sorrow](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drown+in+Sorrow) engulfed the Seekers, earning Nelson a scry in the process. From there, Rath had difficulty rebuilding a board against Nelson's ample removal. When Nelson stuck an [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion), Rath was forced to extend his hand.

**Nelson 2 – Rath 1**

# Patrick Cowe vs. Steve Rubin

Game 1 of this Abzan Control mirror match was, predictably, a slugfest with Planeswalkers flying back and forth. Cowe landed an early [Liliana Vess](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+Vess), though it soon met a heroic downfall. Rubin fired back with an [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion), minusing to take out Cowe's [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino). Rubin followed up his Elspeth with [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon) and the pair of Planeswalkers soon buried Cowe in card and token advantage.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmem15/quarters-cowe-rubin.jpg)  
*The graveyards are well-stocked as Cowe and Rubin lob threats and removal spells back and forth.*

Much as in the first game, both players spent turn after turn removing each other's threats and trying to eke out an advantage. [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) after [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) and Planeswalker after Planeswalker entered the battlefield, only to be quickly hustled along to the graveyard. The removal soon dried up however, and Cowe and Rubin found themselves in a good old-fashioned board stall. Both plays searched for a way to break it open, but it was Rubin who found the answers, with multiple copies of [Read the Bones](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Read+the+Bones) digging him to just the cards he needed.

**Rubin 2 – Cowe 0**

# Chris Fennell vs. (22) Ben Stark

Fennell took a mulligan in the first game and was quickly under assault from Stark's [Goblin Rabblemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Rabblemaster). Fennell used an [End Hostilities](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=End+Hostilities) to clear away the Rabblemaster and one of his token friends, but promptly found himself facing the same Goblin-y predicament, this time with one fewer answer in hand. The quick start proved too much for Fennell and he scooped them up and looked to his sideboard.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmem15/quarters-fennell.jpg)  
*Fennell tries to stabilize in Game 2, with [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall) on [Seeker of the Way](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seeker+of+the+Way) and [Erase](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Erase) for [Outpost Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Outpost+Siege).*

Just as Brad Nelson did against his quarterfinals opponent, Fennell brought in his [Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion)s from the sideboard. He looked to be taking control when a [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall) eliminated Stark's [Seeker of the Way](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seeker+of+the+Way), while an [Erase](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Erase) took care of a freshly cast [Outpost Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Outpost+Siege). But Stark had a pair of [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon)s in hand, and while Fennell was able to answer the first hasty flier, he was no match for the second.

**Stark 2 – Fennell 0**

# Jack Fogle v. Alex Majlaton

In a match-up of Fogle's Sultai Control against Majlaton's Abzan Control both players, predictably, spent their early turns drawing cards with the charms of their respective clans. Fogle began to tentatively develop a board presence with a pair of [Satyr Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Satyr+Wayfinder)s chipping in for damage while providing fodder for his deck's playset of [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time). As each player gradually cast more and more expensive spells, the match culminated the only way it could: an Ugin-on-Ugin battle. Fogle stuck the first copy of the colorless Planeswalker, and after it was destroyed, Majlaton stuck the second. After that one met a [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall), Fogle followed up with yet another copy of his own, and when *that* one went unanswered he won Game 1.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmem15/quarters-majlaton.jpg)  
*Majlaton manages to sneak a Tasigur underneath Fogle's wall of countermagic.*

In Game 2, Fogle used [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) to sculpt a wall of counterspells like [Disdainful Stroke](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disdainful+Stroke) and [Negate](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Negate) that would be invincible to any assault Majlaton could through at him. Invincible, that is, if Majlaton had not already developed a threatening board of multiple Soldier tokens and [Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang). With a grip full of countermagic, Fogle found himself too far behind to recover and they were off to a deciding Game 3.

Fogle once against took a patient approach in the third game, sitting with multiple removal spells and two copies of Ugin in hand, while Majlaton pecked away with [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix). When Majlaton cast [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino), Fogle finally began to take action, using a kicked [Silence](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silence) the Believers to dispatch both creatures. Still holding back his Ugins, he led with a [Satyr Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Satyr+Wayfinder) and a one-mana Tasigur. The banana aficianado began netting him more cards—a [Sultai Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sultai+Charm) here, a [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) there—and before long Majlaton stuck out his hand.

**Fogle 2 – Majlaton 1**
