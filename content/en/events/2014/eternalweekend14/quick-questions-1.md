**How has [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) changed Legacy? How did the existence of [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) affect your deck choice?**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/eternalweekend14/QQDuke1.jpg)

***Reid Duke:** "[Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) makes it very difficult to play a fair deck and it makes discard spells much worse. I chose the deck I would've played anyway, but I have two [Red Elemental Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Red+Elemental+Blast) in the main and I'm playing two copies of [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) myself."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/eternalweekend14/QQShenhar1.JPG)

***Shahar Shenhar:** "Blue/Red Delver wasn't quite a tier one deck and now it definitely is. [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) upgrades Delver decks and makes them mulligan more efficiently. I'm actually surprised that it wasn't banned. It's extremely good. The existence of [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) encouraged me to play Death and Taxes with [Kor Firewalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kor+Firewalker) in the sideboard."* 

 

 

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/eternalweekend14/QQShiels1.jpg)

***Dave Shiels:** "[Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) invalidates grindy fair decks. [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) stock goes up and [Swords to Plowshares](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swords+to+Plowshares) stock goes down. It affected my deck choice by necessitating that I play a deck with four copies."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/eternalweekend14/QQFabiano1.JPG)

***Gerard Fabiano:** "[Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) diminishes the value of [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) and discard. I cut one [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) from my deck as a reaction to the amount of [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) I expected, but I should've cut all four."*
