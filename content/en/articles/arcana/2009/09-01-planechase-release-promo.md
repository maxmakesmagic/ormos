The [*Planechase* Release Events](/en/events/2009/planechase-facts/magic-the-gathering-planechase-release-events) are this weekend, September 4-6. Whee!

There's more information about *Planechase* [on the *Planechase* page](https://web.archive.org/web/20130308082242/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/planechase/productinfo), including full decklists. But there's one more card you haven't seen yet!

Just for showing up and playing, each player will receive a Tazeem promo plane card (while supplies last). What's Tazeem? Oh, it's just a plane not included in any of the four *Planechase* decks, that's all. And it looks ... like *this*!

![Tazeem](https://i.ormos.uk/a7/ff/a7ff6170db3165d1e5775466c6dfa5cb870f04a970b7b54b3055bbfd28ea555b.jpg "Tazeem") 

  

Tazeem is not included in any of the four *Planechase* decks. If you want one, the [*Planechase* Release Events](/en/events/2009/planechase-facts/magic-the-gathering-planechase-release-events) are the place to be!
