Congratulations to Tom Martell for leading Aristocrats to victory at [Pro Tour *Gatecrash*](https://web.archive.org/web/20130222000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/martell-calls-down-reckoning-pro-tour) this past weekend! He will be back for Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze* and you could face him! One way is to win at a Grand Prix—and tomorrow brings us back to Canada and Standard with [Grand Prix Québec City](/en/events/2013/quebeccity13/grand-prix-quebec-city-event-information). If you plan to compete there, it behooves you to study Tom's Aristocrats deck as well as the decklists of all the Top 8 finishers. The other way to get to the Pro Tour is to win a PTQ. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#dgm) and check out recent PTQ results to see what you might face! Good luck; have fun!
