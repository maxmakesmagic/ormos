### Manual Update Available Here

* * *

For players who have trouble getting automatic ***Magic** Online* updates, we now have a manual patch available. Each time a new version goes up, we'll make sure to give you a link to download the manual patch.

Make sure you check the **Tech Updates** section of the sidebar to see when an update is going to happen. As soon as possible afterward, we'll make the manual update available on the [**Tech Updates**](/en/articles/feature/2003/10-09-magic-online-tech-updates) page.

For the September 3, 2002 update, you can [click here](http://mtgodownloads.wizards.com/mtgo/V968-To-V970-Patch.zip) to download the new update. Once you've downloaded the file, unzip it into your ***Magic** Online* directory and you'll be ready to play.

**Note:** The manual patch currently only updates from version 1.968 to the latest version. If your ***Magic** Online* software isn't updating correctly, and you have a version older than 1.968 (the August update), please send an email to the Technical Support group at techsupport@wizards.com to get help.
