Welcome back to "On the Mirrodin Site," where we show off some of the recent highlights from the [Mirrodin Site](https://web.archive.org/web/20110104134724/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/).

The biggest development of the week was not actually on the site itself, but on the Facebook pages that represent the Mirran and Phyrexian factions. Each faction got a recruitment video designed to lure you (yes, *you!*) to its side. Go to the [Mirran Faction Page](http://www.facebook.com/pages/mirrodin-faction/111175072274346) and the [Phyrexian Faction Page](http://www.facebook.com/pages/phyrexian-faction/144426112262398) to see them.

Now that that's out of the way, let's get down to it.

On the Mirran side, Farris of the Anvil [continues his story](https://web.archive.org/web/20110104134724/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/#!blog,626) in a new field report. There's concept art for [goblin equipment](https://web.archive.org/web/20110104134724/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/#mountains,hotspots;r7;0;) and [red creatures](https://web.archive.org/web/20110104134724/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/#mountains,hotspots;r11;0;). And, of course, there are short flavoriffic snippets scattered throughout the site.

The Phyrexians didn't have a field report this week, but their concept art kept pace with the Mirrans, giving a glimpse of [Auriok equipment](https://web.archive.org/web/20110104134724/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/#plains,hotspots;w5;0;) and [creatures of the Mephidross](https://web.archive.org/web/20110104134724/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/#swamps,hotspots;b5;0;).
