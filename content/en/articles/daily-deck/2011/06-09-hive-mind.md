Looking for something fun and exciting to try in Legacy? Pro Tour Top 8 alumni Thomas Ma took this doozy for a spin at the recent StarCityGames.com Invitational in Indianapolis. The deck uses Hive Mind in hilarious fashion: after accelerating it onto the battlefield early, Thomas casts any number of Pacts. His opponents get free copies of the cards, but unfortunately for them, they have to pay the costs of the spells on their next upkeep or immediately lose the game! In a pinch, Thomas can also use Show and Tell to plop an Emrakul, the Aeons Torn onto the battlefield.
