**Magic: The Gathering Pro Tour - Yokohama Qualifier  
 March 29-30, 2003**

[](/ja/events/2003/kyoto03/grand-prix-kyoto)  
[Trial Schedule](https://web.archive.org/web/20041229021621/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=grandprix/kyoto03/trials&amp)

The **Magic: The Gathering** Grand Prix tournament series is an international circuit of large-scale Qualifier tournaments featuring cash prizes and multiple invitations to Pro Tour events.

### TOURNAMENT FORMAT AND SCHEDULE

The format for Grand Prix – Kyoto is Limited (Sealed Deck + Booster Draft). All DCI™ Limited rules and **Magic**® Floor Rules announced on October 1, 2002, along with all subsequent updates, will be enforced.

*   **Friday, March 28:** Registration from 15:00 - 22:00. LATE REGISTRATION IS NOT PERMITTED. If you have preregistered for the event and cannot register by Friday night, contact Ron Foster via email (information below) no later than March 27.
*   **Saturday, March 29:** Tournament begins at 9:00. The Top 64 players advance to the finals on Sunday. (In the event Grand Prix Kyoto has more than 1,000 players, the Top 128 players will advance to Day 2.)
*   **Sunday, March 30:** Play begins at 8:30.

### TOURNAMENT DETAILS

**Divisions**  
 The main tournament at Grand Prix - Kyoto is open to **Magic** players of all ages.

*If the player who wins an invitation to Pro Tour – Yokohama at Grand Prix – Kyoto has already qualified for an invitation, the invitation shall be extended to the next-best finisher. Previous qualification may occur through a Qualifier tournament, through performance at a professional tournament, or by being an invited DCI player (valid on the date of the official rank announcement for the appropriate professional tournament).*

**Tournament Format**  
 The format for Grand Prix – Kyoto is Limited, with both Sealed Deck and Booster Draft. All DCI Limited rules and **Magic** Floor Rules announced on October 1, 2002, along with all subsequent updates, will be enforced.

For the latest information on Limited formats, refer to the 2002 Floor Rules or [www.wizards.com/dci/judge/main.asp?x=judge/resources/sfr\_extended](https://web.archive.org/web/20130702164223/http://www.wizards.com/dci/judge/main.asp?x=judge/resources/sfr_extended)

**Tournament Structure**  
 The structure for Grand Prix – Kyoto is modified Swiss-style (50-minute rounds, with the number of rounds determined by the number of participants), with the Top 64 players advancing to the Sunday finals for prizes and invitations. (In the event Grand Prix – Kyoto has more than 1,000 participants, the Top 128 players will advance to Sunday.) The first day of competition will consist of Sealed Deck. On Day 2, there will be a booster draft and 3 Swiss rounds of competition, followed by another draft with 3 addition Swiss round, to determine the Top 8 players, who will compete in single elimination after another draft.

**Tournament Byes** *(Byes are not cumulative through the season or year.)*  
Byes for Grand Prix—Kyoto are based on the Pro Tour—Houston 2002 professional standings and DCI ratings as of December 18, 2002. Please refer to the Grand Prix Kyoto [Bye List](/en/events/2003/kyoto03/grand-prix-kyoto-byes) for details.
