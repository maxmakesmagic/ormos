So we barely advanced to Day 2. Our tiebreakers were the second worst of all teams that advanced. Even a 4-0-1 wouldn't put us into the Top 4, so we were in single elimination mode. We were quite happy with our draft strategy and didn't really want to modify at all if circumstances didn't make that necessary.

In Round 1 we were up against The Flaming Gambit - Patrick Johnson, Morgan Douglass and Tim Wu.  
 I knew Morgan from **Magic** Online, as I was in a clan with him almost all the way through the beta test. He was also their B seat, so we would have to play. The draft didn't work out all that great for us. Marco opened Aboshan in an otherwise strong pack. He had to take it and we passed them the goods anyway. Fortunately, Marco's opponent figured that the way to beat w-u was to go g-r. He ended up getting only one Flash of Defiance which was basically his only road to victory. Dirk's matchup looked very good. He had a strong g-b deck with two Faceless Butchers while his opponent seemed to have a black-blue-green pile with horrible mana. As a result of Marco drafting blue, I had to go into red. My matchup looked good by mid-Judgment. Morgan had the higher card quality but I had really good cards for the matchup with double Ember Shot, Fledgling Dragon, Anurid Swarmsnapper and a splashed Looter he had no way to deal with. I prepared for long games that my card advantage would win for me. And then he opened Glory in his last pack. And right there my whole plan was blown apart. I couldn't really do anything about it apart from hoping that he wouldn't draw it.
