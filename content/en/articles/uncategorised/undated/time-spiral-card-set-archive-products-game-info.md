## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/91/19/911931a3d58ca822efc20d7133bcf0b2e6e02f72bb30cd25ff3ba29999306b9a.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide, two card boxes, six 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, one random Pro Tour player card, a special-edition life counter, and the *Time Spiral* novel.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/aa/7c/aa7c2d5225697aa5e3ef88dbc747f6fd1a4177fe9ac90bcae4b96c91add91e3e.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b6/78/b678e033be320a0448e0e57501a5673f277649be85241ede12303367b678a2d0.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160709213006/http://magic.wizards.com/node/975801)

(\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3f/26/3f263a2a14da313e0f360bab27cdccb70d4f0f3fec535c32b33c12595f825566.png)

### TOURNAMENT PACKS

The foundation for exciting Sealed Deck play. Includes 45 randomly assorted cards and 30 basic land cards.
