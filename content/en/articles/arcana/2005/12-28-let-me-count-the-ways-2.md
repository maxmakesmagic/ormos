H**ow many legal ways are there to win or lose a game of Magic, as of right now?** 

*(Thanks to Devin Low for today's Arcana, and to Steve from Chicago for the question that inspired it.)*

![Battle of Wits](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Battle+of+Wits)

What's your guess?

.  
.  
.

The answer is 28.

.  
.  
.

Oh, you actually wanted to know what they were? Well just remember it the way **Magic** R&D's Devin Low does, with this... er, *simple* mnemonic poem he devised:

> *5 Tormented *Odysseys*,  
>  5 Paths to Lose by Rules,  
>  7 Scattered Crazy Wins,  
>  3 Fortunes' Final Fools,  
>  3 Liches to Live in Death,  
>  5 Risks for Those Who Find Them,  
>  One Comp Rules to Rule them All, and in the Darkness Bind Them.*

5+5+3+7+3+3+5 = 28. Simple. If you didn't catch them all from the poem, just sound it out.

.  
.  
.

More detail?? Really. Okay. So the comprehensive rules say you win when your opponents all lose, or when a card effect says you win.

### "5 Tormented *Odysseys*"

*Odyssey* Block has a cycle of 5 alternate win conditions:

1.  [Battle of Wits](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battle+of+Wits)
2.  [Chance Encounter](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chance+Encounter)
3.  [Epic Struggle](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Epic+Struggle)
4.  [Mortal Combat](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mortal+Combat)
5.  [Test of Endurance](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Test+of+Endurance)

### "5 Paths to Lose by Rules"

There are 5 ways your opponent can lose according to the [comprehensive rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/comprules):

1.  He or she has zero life.
2.  He or she tries to draw a card with no cards left in his or her library.
3.  He or she has ten poison counters.
4.  He or she loses and wins at the same time. (HINT: You lose.)
5.  He or she concedes.

### "7 Scattered Crazy Wins"

There are 7 other alternate win conditions across various sets:

1.  [The Cheese Stands Alone](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Cheese+Stands+Alone)
2.  [Now I Know My ABC's](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Now+I+Know+My+ABC%27s)
3.  [Celestial Convergence](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Celestial+Convergence)
4.  [Coalition Victory](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Coalition+Victory)
5.  [Darksteel Reactor](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Reactor)
6.  [Door to Nothingness](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Door+to+Nothingness)
7.  [Phage the Untouchable](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phage+the+Untouchable)

(These last two are wins disguised as losses.)

### "3 Fortunes' Final Fools"

There are 3 Final Fortunes:

1.  [Final Fortune](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Final+Fortune)
2.  [Last Chance](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Chance)
3.  [Warrior's Oath](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warrior%27s+Oath)

### "3 Liches to Live in Death"

There are 3 Liches that can make your opponent lose the game:

1.  [Lich](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lich)
2.  [Lich's Tomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lich%27s+Tomb)
3.  [Nefarious Lich](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nefarious+Lich)

### "5 Risks for Those Who Find Them"

And there are 5 other risky cards your opponent might use that can make him or her lose the game:

1.  [Amulet of Quoz](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Quoz)
2.  [Forbidden Crypt](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forbidden+Crypt)
3.  [Form of the Squirrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Form+of+the+Squirrel)
4.  [Rocket-Powered Turbo Slug](https://web.archive.org/web/20051228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rocket-Powered+Turbo+Slug)

Yup, 28.
