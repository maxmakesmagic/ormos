Due to Hurricane Isabel, Star City Events was forced to cancel it's *Mirrodin* Prerelease in Virginia Beach, VA. Although, they were granted permission to reschedule this event for the following weekend, they were unable to secure a site in the Virginia Beach or Richmond areas on such short notice. With that being the case, the decision was made to reschedule the event in Roanoke, Virginia. Below, you will find full information on this special event! 

**Sunday, September 28, 2003**  
*Mirrodin* Prerelease - Roanoke, VA  
 Clarion Hotel  
 I-581 exit 3W  
 Take a right at the first light to 3315 Ordway Drive  
 Roanoke, VA 24019-2905  
 Questions? Please call 1.540.767.GAME(4263) or check [www.starcitygames.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20030922000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.starcitygames.com&origin=sideboard_news_20030918a) for daily updates!
