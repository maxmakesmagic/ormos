The time has come once again to usher in the newest class of **Magic** Pro Tour Hall of Famers. Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa, Kenji Tsumura, Masashi Oiso, and Patrick Chapin comprise the Class of 2012, adding their names to the list of **Magic** immortals already enshrined.

The players were inducted at a special ceremony at Pro Tour *Return to Ravnica* in Seattle. Along with personalized commemorative rings, the inductees also earn qualification to each Pro Tour event.

Take a trip into **Magic**'s history by reading the player profiles of each classes, including biographies, career highlights and statistics, famous decklists and video from past Pro Tours and Worlds.
