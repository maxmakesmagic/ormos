In honor of the start of the Pro Tour Qualifier-Honolulu season, Daily Decks is bringing you some of the hottest Extended decks all week long! Sebastian Thaler, who earned a Top 8 playing Extended at the recent Pro Tour-Berlin, took this innovative twist on Zoo to a strong finish in Memphis at Worlds. He teched his build with Shadow Guildmage over Tidehollow Sculler, a solid maneuver against a field filled with 1/1s be they Elves, Faeries, or Goblins. To find an Extended PTQ for Honolulu near you, visit [this link](/en/events/2009/honolulu09-qualifiers/pro-tourhonolulu-2009-qualifiers).
