### August 2002: Art Descriptions

**Thursday, August 1:**  
 Black, enchantment  
 The focus of the art is an army of artifact creatures trying to invade the Phyrexian zone. Unfortunately for them, the Phyrexians have had weeks to prepare since the outside world of Tolaria moves so slowly in relation to the fast time of the Phyrexian zone. In the art, the Phyrexian forces are dissembling the invading artifact creatures as they step from the slower world of Tolaria to the quicker world of the Phyrexian zone. See Style Guide for Tolaria, Tolarian artifacts, Phyrexians and the Phyrexian zone.  
**Answer:**No Mercy (from *Urza's Legacy*)  
**Winner:** Dennis Park.

**Friday, August 2:**  
 White, enchantment  
 This scene takes place in the tunnels leading to the main entrance in the stronghold. Two Kor, in the foreground, have sneaked into a vantage point to watch what a squad of Mogg soldiers and stronghold guards are up to.  
**Answer:**Reconnaissance (from *Exodus*)  
**Winner:** Katrina Bolasco.

**Monday, August 5:**  
 Multi-color, sorcery  
 Tsabo's (Style Guide P 79) long-taloned fingers are just about to dig into Thaddeus' head, sucking the information out of it. Show the moment just before puncture.  
**Answer:**Lobotomy (from *Invasion*)  
**Winner:** Brandon Clor.

**Tuesday, August 6:**  
 White Enchantment  
 A shot of Karn, the silver golem, surrounded by enemy Mogg. He is on the Weatherlight's deck as it is being boarded by the Predator high in the sky. He refuses to fight the Mogg Goblins as they close in to do him harm. The Mogg see him as a treasure like any other artifact and intend to take him back to the Predator.  
**Answer:** Pacifism (from *Tempest*)  
**Winner:** Chris Hartman.

**Wednesday, August 7:**  
 Black sorcery  
 This is a black card and should show an artist with his head bandaged and has gone mad and is locked in an asylum; he draws the exact same picture over and over on his walls.  
**Answer:**Hint of Insanity, (from *Odyssey*)  
**Winner:** Matthew Okrentowich.

**Thursday, August 8:**  
 Blue creature  
 This is a small (under 3' ) mischevious winged creature. Though not evil it is certainly troublesome. It should be carrying something valuable to its owner, such as a wand or a spell tome, something magical that would be missed. It might be creeping along, it might also be vanishing into thin air.  
**Answer:**Teferi's Imp (from *Mirage*)  
**Winner:** Nathan Brewer

**Friday, August 9:**  
 Artifact  
 Belbe lies on a black operating table made of twisted metal; everything but her neck and head are hidden by a surgical scrim; her expression is blank and catatonic. A vicious clawed servo-hand is lowering a smooth, black glass discus towards her chest for implantation. Focus on the disk. See Style Guide for Belbe reference.  
**Answer:**Eye of Yawgmoth, (from *Nemesis*)  
**Winner:** Andy Gifford.

**Monday, August 12:**  
 Blue enchantment  
 Barrin head shot with brightly glowing energy eyes/halo, fierce, attitude.  
**Answer:**Heightened Awareness (from *Prophecy*)  
**Winner:** Tom Fowler

**Tuesday, August 13**  
 White sorcery  
 Show four images of the exact same Serran soldier in the exact same pose. From left to right, the images progress symmetrically from complete solidity to nothingness, then back again. The first image of the soldier should be harried and disheveled; the final image should be pristine and refreshed.  
**Answer:**Flicker (from *Urza's Destiny*)  
**Winner:** Alex Leader-Smith

**Wednesday, August 14:**  
 Black enchantment  
 Show a troubled sleeping figure. Looking into the figures dream you can see a slain evil creature, but behind it appears a larger and more monstrous beast, and dimly seen behind that is an even worse one, on and on, as it looking into the abyss.  
**Answer:**Recurring Nightmare (from *Exodus*)  
**Winner:** Andrej Awgustow

**Thursday, August 15:**  
 Red creature  
 A burly male humanoid courier holds a scroll in his clenched fist. He is focusing a beam of black energy through the scroll that brutally slams into a flying angel above him. Black coalition symbol visible; focus on the courier.  
**Answer:**Shivan Emissary (from *Invasion*)  
**Winner:** Chi-Hay Tong

**Friday, August 16:**  
 Green enchantment  
 In a forest clearing, a circle of green-robed druids casts a spell. Energy cascades between them and toward the center of the circle, where a human-shaped silhouette rises.  
**Answer:**Regeneration (from *Seventh Edition*)  
**Winner:** Mike MacDonald
