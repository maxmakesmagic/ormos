Earlier this week we looked at a White Weenie deck that played planeswalkers as a backup to an early game plan of beating down. Jason Roach, 6th place finisher at the Nova Scotian Champs tournament in Canada, took a different path. His White Weenie list plays a seemingly odd 1/3 in the form of Painter’s Servant. The artifact creature is only odd until the end game when Jason unloads with his coup de grace: Chaotic Backlash! Using the Servant to whitewash his opponent’s board (pun intended!) the red spell will deal a damage for every single permanent on the other side of the board. Now *that’s* a backlash!
