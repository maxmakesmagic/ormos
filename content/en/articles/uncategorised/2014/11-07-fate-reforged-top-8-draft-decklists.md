The Pro Tour Qualifier season for Pro Tour *Fate Reforged* is under way. This is your chance to test your Limited skills, because invitations and tickets to Pro Tour *Fate Reforged* go to PTQ winners!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptq_400.jpg)

Look through the events below for Top 8 draft decklists. Decklists will be updated weekly during the PTQ season. The new season runs August 30, 2014 – November 30, 2014. For more information about the PTQ season, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20141107000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/pro-tour-fate-reforged-qualifiers).
