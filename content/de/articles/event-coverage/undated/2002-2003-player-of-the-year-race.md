### 

The Pro Player of the Year title is awarded to the player who receives the most Pro Points in a season. Pro Points are awarded for Grand Prix, Pro Tours and the World Championships. The Pro Player of the Year receives invitations to all major events next season and free travel to all Pro Tours and World Championships. Previous Pro Player of the Year winners have included Olle Rade (1996), Paul McCabe (1997), Jon Finkel (1998), Bob Maher (2000), and Kai Budde (1999, 2001, 2002).
