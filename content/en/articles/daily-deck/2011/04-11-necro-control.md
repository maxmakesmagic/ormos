Necropotence has long been one of the game's most powerful spells, and future Player of the Year Paul McCabe proved why at Pro Tour Dallas during the 1996-1997 Pro Tour season. By trading for his opponent's spells with cards like Contagion and Hymn to Tourach, Paul was able to refill his hand using Necropotence. And if he ran low on life? Drain Life was there to help him draw more cards! Necropotence won many a match back in its day, and Paul was just one of the players who benefited from the powerful card.
