## Counters Are Getting a Revamp

Do you run a deck that uses counters? If so, we have a treat for you! We're updating the look of the current counter system, so you can more easily identify what counters are being used. Egg counters, Despair counters, Pain counters, you name it, it is getting a visual update. To learn more about it and see what the counters will look like, check out [this article](/en/articles/magiconline/2018/09-05-counter-development) by our client developer Christopher Bellach.

## PTQ Saturday and Sunday

There will be a Standard PTQ this Saturday at 9 a.m. PT. Then on Sunday, there will be a Modern PTQ at 9 a.m. PT. The winner of each event qualifies for Pro Tour Cleveland and its associated Pro Tour Challenge.

Learn more about *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20180911000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers).

## Downtimes September 26 and October 24

The next two downtimes will be September 26, 3 a.m.–noon PT/-7 from UTC and October 24, 3 a.m.–noon PT/-7 from UTC. Please note that there are no more 'extended' or 'normal' downtimes; in the new world with fewer downtimes, they're all the same length of time.
