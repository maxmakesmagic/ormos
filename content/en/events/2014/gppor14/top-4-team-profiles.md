After many rounds of Swiss and some great games of *Magic*, the Top 8 for Grand Prix Portland is set! Learn more about this event’s Top 8 competitors, their history at the top tables, and more by taking a look through the Top 8 profiles.

  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gppor14/scottvargas_froehlich_cheon.jpg)

# Luis Scott-Vargas

**Age:** 31  
**Hometown:** Oakland, CA

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
 2013 Hall of Fame inducteee; five Pro Tour Top 8s; three National Team appearances; 11 Grand Prix Top 8s and a 2014 Silver-level pro.

**What colors was your first Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 I was blue-black splash red, and my best card was [Plague Wind](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plague+Wind), also known as [Polymorph](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Polymorph)ist's Jest plus [Festergloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Festergloom). I went 6-3.

**What colors was your second Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 I was blue-green, and my best card was [Ætherspouts](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86therspouts). Nobody even considered not attacking me and I went 5-0.

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**  
 In the abstract, a sense of pride and accomplishment. In the not-abstract, that I missed my flight home.

# Eric Froehlich

**Age:** 30  
**Hometown:** Las Vegas, NV

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
 Three Pro Tour Top 8s, six Pro Tour Top 16s, six Team Grand Prix Top 4s.

**What colors was your first Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 I went 8-1 with mono-white. [Triplicate Spirits](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triplicate+Spirits) was the best card.

**What colors was your second Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 I went 4-0 (the team went 5-0), and [Triplicate Spirits](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triplicate+Spirits) was the best card.

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**  
 It would mean a lead in the Player of the Year race but missing my flight.

# Paul Cheon

**Age:** 29  
**Hometown:** Las Angeles, CA

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
 Six Grand Prix Top 8s. 2006 US Nationals champion.

**What colors was your first Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 It was red-green splashing black. I went 4-5; my teammates are great. My best cards were [Garruk, Apex Predator](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk%2C+Apex+Predator) and [Cone of Flame](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cone+of+Flame). #LuckyCheon

**What colors was your second Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 I played black-red and went 2-2 (team went 5-0). My teammates are great... seriously. [Scuttling Doom Engine](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scuttling+Doom+Engine) is sweet and LSV and Efro did not lose a match today.

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**  
 Everything. It is an absolutely amazing feeling being qualified for the Pro Tour again. I can't stress this enough, but I've been fortunate enough to be teamed with two all-time greats. I still can't believe that I'm qualified!

# Owen Turtenwald

**Age:** 25  
**Hometown:** Milwaukee, WI

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
 Two Pro Tour Top 8s, 16 GP Top 8s

**What colors was your first Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 Mono-White, going 9-0, losing one total game. It had three [Triplicate Spirits](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triplicate+Spirits).

**What colors was your second Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 Green-White Convoke, going 2-3. It had [Avacyn, Guardian Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avacyn%2C+Guardian+Angel), Yisan, Wanderer Bard, and [Chord of Calling](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chord+of+Calling).

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**  
 Winning a Grand Prix would be nice but winning a Grand Prix with two great friends would truly be something special.

# William Jensen

**Age:** 32  
**Hometown:** Boston, MA

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
 Top 8 of Pro Tour *Magic 2015*, 2013 *Magic* Hall of Fame member.

**What colors was your first Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 Blue-Red with [Soul of Shandalar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soul+of+Shandalar). We went 8-1.

**What colors was your second Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 Red splashing green with [Cone of Flame](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cone+of+Flame). We went 4-1.

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**  
 Winning team tournaments is among the most fun things to do in *Magic*. I really hope I'm able to win one with Owen and Reid. It has been a goal of mine for a while, and I hope to achieve that goal this afternoon.

# Reid Duke

**Age:** 24  
**Hometown:** Sugar Loaf, NY

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
 2011 *Magic Online* champion, Top 8 Pro Tour *Journey into Nyx*, ten previous GP Top 8s, two wins

**What colors was your first Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 Mono-Black with [Nightfire Giant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightfire+Giant) and gren sideboard cards. We went 8-1 on Day 1.

**What colors was your second Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 Mono-Black with [Nightfire Giant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightfire+Giant)s and blue sideboard cards. We went 4-1.

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**  
 It would mean a great start to the new Pro Tour season.

# Eric Severson

**Age:** 22  
**Hometown:** Berkeley, CA

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
 Day 2 Pro Tour *Theros*, two Grand Prix Top 64s

**What colors was your first Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 Blue-black. Our team was 8-0-1 and the combo of [Polymorph](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Polymorph)ist's Jest and [Festergloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Festergloom) was good.

**What colors was your second Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 White-blue. Our team was 3-2 and [Dauntless River Marshal](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dauntless+River+Marshal) was the best card.

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**  
 It's a great start to the pro season and a big step toward my pro *Magic* dreams.

# Benjamin Weitz

**Age:** 24  
**Hometown:** St. Paul, MN

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
 I won a local "Standard for Power" tournament once, and I placed 13th at Grand Prix Oakland.

**What colors was your first Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 Red-green. My team went 8-0-1 and my deck was good enough to not weight my teammates down too much. [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike) was slightly better than [Kalonian Twingrove](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kalonian+Twingrove).

**What colors was your second Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 Blue-green, and bad enough to weigh my teammates down significantly. We went 3-2 and [Siege Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Wurm) was my best card.

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**  
 Winning the Grand Prix would be one of my life's greatest accomplishments. I've been playing *Magic* for a LONG time but haven't had much top-tier success. I love this game so much and I'm going to enjoy battling in Honolulu!

# Josiah Skallerup

**Age:** 32  
**Hometown:** Oakland, CA

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
 32nd place at Grand Prix Columbus in *Mirrodin* Limited.

**What colors was your first Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 White-black. Our team went 8-0-1. My best cards were my two [Stab Wound](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stab+Wound). Honorable mention goes to the two Heliod's Pilgrim I had to fetch them up. Bleeding my opponents out all day was incredibly satisfying.

**What colors was your second Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 Black-red. Our team went 3-2. [Accursed Spirit](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Accursed+Spirit) was my biggest workhorse, but a top-decked [Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames) (sometimes facilitated by [Liliana Vess](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+Vess), sometimes not) was also rather good.

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**  
 Winning the Grand Prix would mean so much to me, both by affirming the love I have for the game, and the bond I share with my teammates.

# Matt Katz

**Age:** 29  
**Hometown:** Westborough, MA

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
 I placed 11th at Grand Prix Indianapolis.

**What colors was your first Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 I went 5-4 with blue-black-red. [Ob Nixilis, Unshackled](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ob+Nixilis%2C+Unshackled) and [Indulgent Tormentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Indulgent+Tormentor) were my best cards.

**What colors was your second Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**

[Xathrid Slyblade](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xathrid+Slyblade) + [Burning Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Anger) = [Visara the Dreadful](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Visara+the+Dreadful).

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**  
 Better hardware for my cubicle.

# Ben Yu

**Age:** 28  
**Hometown:** Las Vegas, NV

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments**  
 I finished in the Top 4 at Grand Prix Warsaw. I also forgot to play [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic) and Turn 1 today and just passed the turn and won the game, obviously.

**What colors was your first Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 I played Green-White Convoke. I used [Chord of Calling](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chord+of+Calling) for [Hornet Queen](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hornet+Queen), [Constricting Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Constricting+Sliver) when I had [Sliver Hive](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sliver+Hive), or [Yisan, the Wanderer Bard](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yisan%2C+the+Wanderer+Bard), which I then used to fetch the [Soulmender](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soulmender)/Ajani's Pridemate combo.

**What colors was your second Sealed deck, what was your record with it, and what was your best card?**  
 I played mono-white with [Avacyn, Guardian Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140810000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avacyn%2C+Guardian+Angel) splashing green and blue.

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**  
 I finished last year with 16 Pro Points and missed Silver. I wasn't qualified for Pro Tour Khans of Tarkir in Honolulu and I was going to stop going to Grand Prix and everything else. Now I'm going to go to everything again. ALOHA!
