As we continue our series looking forward to the new Standard with *Innistrad*, we're going to take a look at a deck that doesn't actually lose much. That deck? Mono-Green Infect, like this version played recently on **Magic Online** by Zemox. The deck's plan is to play a creature with infect, then beat down as quickly as possible. The archetype will lose a few cards, including [Vines of Vastwood](https://web.archive.org/web/20110930000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vines+of+Vastwood) and [Ancient Stirrings](https://web.archive.org/web/20110930000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Stirrings). So what cards would you include from [*Innistrad*](https://web.archive.org/web/20110930000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&set=+%5B%22Innistrad%22%5D) to replace them?
