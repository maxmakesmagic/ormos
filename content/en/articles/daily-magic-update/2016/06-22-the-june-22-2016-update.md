The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on June 22, 2016. Today's Update is brought to you by the one coworker who I'm pretty sure has realized I'm not Blake. She keeps looking at me funny, and my costume is falling apart. I think the jig is up, guys.

# Today's Must

1.  [Against the Odds (Bonus): Worldgorger Combo Draft](http://www.mtggoldfish.com/articles/against-the-odds-bonus-worldgorger-combo-draft) | MTGGoldfish | SaffronOlive

I'm pretty sure this is the apotheosis of drafting, and we should all be glad to witness it. Off the back of a first-pick [Worldgorger Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20160622000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Worldgorger+Dragon) and second-pick [Animate Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20160622000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Animate+Dead), SaffronOlive managed to assemble an infinite combo so powerful it was once banned in legacy...in *Eternal Masters* Draft! Just watch it. It's...it's beautiful.

# After That, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [How to Play a *Magic* Prerelease: *Eldritch Moon* Edition](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-d8w--gGJdc) | Wizards of the Coast

Curious what a Prerelease is and how to do it? Or just wondering whether to be concerned about hordes of Zombies showing up? We've got you covered with this handy video! Yichao breaks down what to expect and how to maximize your fun.

1.  [Vintage Super League, Week 4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W7pcsrJQWmA)

Have you been watching the Vintage Super League? Personally, I find it fun to watch what I know is a game of *Magic*, but one wherein I have little to no idea what is actually going on. I'm not even kidding! This is a whole different level of gameplay, and it involves cards and rules interactions that you aren't likely to see anywhere else.

1.  [Legacy Lands](http://www.crcast.com/podcasts/constructed-resources-50-legacy-lands/) | Constructed Resources | Marshall Sutcliffe and Eric Froehlich

Speaking of Eternal formats, Marshall and Eric talk Legacy, including the emergence of Miracles as a Legacy juggernaut and the surprising power of the Lands deck. They also touch on the current state of Standard and check in on World *Magic* Cup lineups.

1.  [The Perfect Crime](http://imgur.com/KA1su7M)

A perceptive Imgur user has noticed something rather, well, *alarming* about the sequence of events depicted on these two cards. We commend this anonymous citizen for their astute observational skills, and we will be launching a full investigation into the young woman in question and the extent of her alleged misdeeds.

# What People Are Talking About

1.  [#HanweirChronicle concludes](https://twitter.com/hashtag/HanweirChronicle?src=hash)

For months, the Hanweir Chronicle has been issuing cryptic dispatches from the slowly warping Innistrad, giving fans and players around the world a firsthand glimpse into the madness overtaking the plane. At long last, the Chronicle has concluded, as the now-collective consciousness of the town's citizens has no need for such communications. If you've missed any parts of the Hanweir Chronicle, or just want to revisit your favorites, they're collected at [HanweirChronicle.com](http://hanweirchronicle.com/).

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Modern Dredge](/en/events/2016/competitive-modern-constructed-league-2016-06-21/competitive-modern-constructed-league)

This looks pretty different from a typical Legacy Dredge deck, but what really caught my eye is the full set of four [Prized Amalgam](https://web.archive.org/web/20160622000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prized+Amalgam)s. There's a card that I literally do not think I've seen played in a Limited environment once—but in this particular Constructed strategy, it's an obvious all-star. If you play a lot of Limited, like me, you can easily start to disregard cards like [Prized Amalgam](https://web.archive.org/web/20160622000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prized+Amalgam) that don't do anything notable in their particular Limited environment, and it's always fun to see those cards find a home.

Congratulations to *Magic Online* user MTGMulligan for taking this list to a 5-0 finish in a Competitive Modern Constructed League. I'm looking forward to seeing what other sleeper cards from *Shadows over Innistrad* and *Eldritch Moon* will make their way into older formats!
