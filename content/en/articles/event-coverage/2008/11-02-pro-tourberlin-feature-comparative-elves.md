The sideboards are a mixture of overlapping cards (everyone has extra [Viridian Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20081102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viridian+Shaman)s) and individual choices (Saito is the only one with [Blasting Station](https://web.archive.org/web/20081102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blasting+Station) or [Gleeful Sabotage](https://web.archive.org/web/20081102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gleeful+Sabotage), Juza is the only one with [Naturalize](https://web.archive.org/web/20081102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Naturalize) or [Voidstone Gargoyle](https://web.archive.org/web/20081102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voidstone+Gargoyle), Doise has [Seal of Primordium](https://web.archive.org/web/20081102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seal+of+Primordium) and an extra [Chord of Calling](https://web.archive.org/web/20081102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chord+of+Calling), Thaler has [Choke](https://web.archive.org/web/20081102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Choke) and [Sundering Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20081102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sundering+Titan), and Scott-Vargas has [Mycoloth](https://web.archive.org/web/20081102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mycoloth), [Nullmage Shepherd](https://web.archive.org/web/20081102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nullmage+Shepherd), and a spare [Pendelhaven](https://web.archive.org/web/20081102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pendelhaven)).
