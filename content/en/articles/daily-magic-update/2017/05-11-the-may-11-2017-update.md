# Deck of the Day

1.  [Try Hard Humans (White-Red Humans)](/en/events/2017/competitive-standard-constructed-league-2017-05-10/competitive-standard-constructed-league)

Ah, [Bloodlust Inciter](https://web.archive.org/web/20170511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodlust+Inciter)—somehow the most controversial card in *Amonkhet* Limited has made its way to Standard at the hands of BigBoss5150 on *Magic Online*.

Hm. Big "Boss." White-Red Humans, a style Tom "The Boss" Ross is known for. Probably nothing.

But the deck certainly isn't nothing. It's a bit innovative for the White-Red Humans archetype in a few ways. First, it pushes hard on [Always Watching](https://web.archive.org/web/20170511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Always+Watching), with exert creatures going up to [Devoted Crop-Mate](https://web.archive.org/web/20170511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devoted+Crop-Mate) as well as [Town Gossipmonger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Town+Gossipmonger) having its longstanding synergy with the enchantment.

Second is that [Bloodlust Inciter](https://web.archive.org/web/20170511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodlust+Inciter), who happens to be a Human, and two copies of [Hazoret the Fervent](https://web.archive.org/web/20170511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hazoret+the+Fervent) to provide some reach the deck was often missing.

I also like the use of [Declaration in Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20170511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Declaration+in+Stone) as the removal spell of choice. It's always been better in Humans, who are often fast enough not to care about the clue, but also because there are a ton of Ulamogs running around right now that need to be turned into stone.

Someone, somewhere will bring a deck similar to this to the Pro Tour tomorrow. Will [Bloodlust Inciter](https://web.archive.org/web/20170511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodlust+Inciter) be the key? I hope so, if only to listen to the commenters blow a gasket.
