Since it's printing in *Innistrad*, [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets) has been a Legacy staple, fueling many different blue tempo decks. [Force of Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+of+Will), [Daze](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daze), and [Wasteland](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland) are Legacy staples that are commonly seen with everyone's favorite [Insectile Aberration](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Insectile+Aberration). RUG. RWU. UR. BUG. Many different color combinations have been successful in combination with [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets), but Legacy aficionado Eric Rill has taken it up a notch. He built a four-color deck featuring [Deathrite Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathrite+Shaman); [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer); and a singleton copy of [Edric, Spymaster of Trest](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Edric%2C+Spymaster+of+Trest). I can only imagine how incredible [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer) + [Edric, Spymaster of Trest](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Edric%2C+Spymaster+of+Trest) are in combination. 

![Young Pyromancer](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Young+Pyromancer)

One of my favorite interactions in this deck has to do with the [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer) and [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy) from the sideboard. If you cast [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer), you can cast [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy), generating a token from [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer), which is perfect fodder for sacrificing to [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy). Not only that, but playing the flashback is essentially free, since the Elemental token you sacrifice to pay the cost on the flashback of [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy) is replaced by the [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer)! [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe) is also powerful with [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy), allowing you to know what to name. 

Eric Rill took his new take on [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Delver%2Bof%2BSecrets) to a 1st-place finish at [SCG Milwaukee](http://www.starcitygames.com/events/050414_milwaukee.html), and just to prove it was no fluke, Michael Ferrante finished 7th two weeks later, at [SCG Detroit](http://www.starcitygames.com/events/190414_detroit.html). There might be a new [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20140502000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Delver%2Bof%2BSecrets) deck in the Legacy metagame!
