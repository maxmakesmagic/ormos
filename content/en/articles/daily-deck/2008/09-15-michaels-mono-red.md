In 2007 Michael Jacob joined the U.S. Nationals team as a member at the World Championships. In 2008, Jacob will lead the team after repeating his Top 8 performance and finishing the tournament with the championship trophy. His Mono-red deck utilized many cards pundits considered bad. Where most players had Ghitu Encampment, Mike had Keldon Megaliths. Where other players had Keldon Marauders, Mike opted to play Blood Knight. Ultimately his ingenuity paid off as Michael Jacob became the 2008 U.S. National Champion. For coverage of the 2008 U.S. Nationals Championship, including all the Standard decklists, go [here](/en/events/2008/usnat08/jacob-re-ignites-great-chicago-fire).
