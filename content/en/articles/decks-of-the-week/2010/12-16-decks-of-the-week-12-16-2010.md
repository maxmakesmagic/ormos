If you're a fan of deck lists (and we're guessing you are, since you clicked on the link), get ready for a holiday feast of metagame information. The [2010 Magic World Championships](https://web.archive.org/web/20160325102524/http://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/theres-no-jace-guillaume) were held last weekend in Chiba, Japan, featuring multiple tournaments across formats. From the top Standard and Extended decks in the main event to a all-foil *Scars of Mirrodin* Rotisserie Draft, there's sure to be something to catch your eye. Congratulations to Magic's newest world champions Guillaume Matignon, Carlos Romão, and Team Slovak Republic (Robert Jurkovic, Ivan Floch, and Patrik Surab)!
