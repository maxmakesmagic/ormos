*Shards of Alara* Block Constructed is firing events around the clock on **Magic Online**. Here's a look at a recent Premier Event Top 8 deck featuring the best the plane of Esper has to offer. Sanctum Gargoyle combos with both Courier's and Executioner's Capsules. Master of Etherium pumps the team and grows large itself. Oblivion Ring and Path to Exile take care of opposing creatures; the deck is truly an artifact work of wonder!
