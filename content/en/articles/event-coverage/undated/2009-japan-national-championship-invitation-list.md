### ２００９年度日本選手権招待選手一覧

The following players have invitations to the 2009 Japan National Championship. This list will be updated as Regional Championship results are reported. In addition, all players who Level 2 or higher in the Pro Players Club (having 10 or more pro points in the 2009 Player of the Year Race) also have an invitation. (Current Pro Club standings can be viewed at the [Pro Players Club](https://web.archive.org/web/20090602112555/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=protour/standings/proplayersclub09&tablesort=4) page.

２００９年度日本選手権の出場資格のあるプレイヤーは、下記の通りです。この一覧は、地区予選の結果が報告され次第、随時更新されます。他に、プロプレイヤークラブ　レベル２以上（２００９年度プロプレイヤー年間順位でプロポイント１０点以上）のプレイヤーも出場資格を所有します。（プロプレイヤー年間順位は [Pro Players Club](https://web.archive.org/web/20090602112555/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=protour/standings/proplayersclub09&tablesort=4) のホームページにてご覧になれます。）
