Welcome to the first week of [Selecting *Ninth Edition*](https://web.archive.org/web/20061112003030/http://wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/columnarchive&column=SelectingNinthEdition), where you get to put on your R&D hat and help build **Magic**'s next Core Set. For more information, check out the [Selecting *Ninth Edition* home page](/en/articles/selecting9e/2004/06-13-selecting-ninth-edition).

To kick off the voting this week, we present two green monsters. Both weighing in at a hefty 5/5 and costed at four, they're bargain fatties any way you cut it. Emperor Crocodile is a splashable beatstick for creature-heavy decks. The resume of Jade Leech includes tournament play in Fires of Yavimaya decks. Which will you choose to put into *Ninth Edition*?

![Emperor Crocodile vs. Jade Leech](https://i.ormos.uk/24/87/248712cd75a8d5dc31904fbea1365609c3c8238e96bc9f1ba04c9b53ae90f070.jpg)

To vote, you'll need to [register](https://web.archive.org/web/20050314021057/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=company/person/register) first. 

Once you've registered, **click here to vote**! 

Thanks for making your voice heard. But this is just the beginning. Tune in next Monday -- and every Monday through September 6th -- to make even more important changes to your next Core Set.
