Blinkmoth Infusion untaps all your artifacts at great expense -- unless you have a ton of them. The affinity for artifacts ability is actually depicted in Alan Pollack's art of Blinkmoth Infusion, in the form of small artifacts shown on pedestals behind the magical light shining down on the main artifact in the center.

And what is that main artifact in the center? Gilded Lotus -- a very sensible artifact to untap.
