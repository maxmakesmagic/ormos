### WEREWOLVES

#### Werewolves are created in moments of intense despair, when a human's soul becomes intertwined with the wild essence of nature—after which, both sides are locked in a struggle for control.

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&multiverseid=409896)

### VAMPIRES

#### Though their abilities may differ, vampires all have enhanced strength, eternal youth, the ability to dampen noise, and prominent canines—often the last terrifying image seen by many an unwary traveler.

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&multiverseid=401934)
