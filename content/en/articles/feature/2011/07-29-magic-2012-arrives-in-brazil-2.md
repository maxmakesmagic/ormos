Wizards of the Coast is excited to announce that ***Magic** 2012* has reached Brazil and is now available at local retail stores. To celebrate the release, WPN locations in Brazil are holding [prerelease events](https://web.archive.org/web/20110729000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prerelease-facts) throughout the country this weekend (July 30-31), and Devir will be providing additional boosters to the prize pool for these events. Gather your allies and head to your local retail store this weekend!
