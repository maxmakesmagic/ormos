After 15 rounds, with a total of 2,076 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

* * *

### Fabrizio Anteri

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbar16/t8_anteri.jpg)

**Age:** 26 

**Occupation:** 

**Hometown:** London, England 

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 

Nine Grand Prix Top 8s, four wins.

**What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?** 

[Wolf of Devil's Breach](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wolf+of+Devil%27s+Breach). Red-white. 8-1.

**What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?** 

[Town Gossipmonger](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Town+Gossipmonger). Red-white. 3-0.

**What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?** 

[Call the Bloodline](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Call+the+Bloodline). Black-green splash white. 2-1.

### Mehdi Saadi

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbar16/t8_saadi.jpg)

**Age:** 24 

**Occupation:** Pharmacy student 

**Hometown:** Paris, France 

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 

Went 10-4-1 at Grand Prix Madrid in September.

**What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?** 

[Tireless Tracker](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tracker). I was playing a green-white Clue deck. 8-1.

**What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?** 

[Accursed Witch](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Accursed+Witch). Black-Green Delirium. 2-0-1.

**What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?** 

[Pack Guardian](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Guardian) and [Tireless Tracker](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tracker). Green-Blue Clue Value. 3-0.

### Viktors Kazanskis

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbar16/t8_kazanskis.jpg)

**Age:** 26 

**Occupation:** Project manager 

**Hometown:** Riga, Latvia 

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 

Attended two Pro Tours.

**What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?** 

White-Black No Rares. Every removal spell was good.

**What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?** 

Black-red splash green for [Arlinn Kord](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arlinn+Kord). She won a couple of games. 3-0.

**What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?** 

White-red. [Fiery Temper](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Temper). 2-0-1.

### Brian Braun-Duin

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbar16/t8_bbd.jpg)

**Age:** 29 

**Occupation:** Writer at ChannelFireball.com 

**Hometown:** Roanoke, VA, USA 

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 

Four Grand Prix Top 8s. Two Grand Prix wins.

**What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?** 

[Accursed Witch](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Accursed+Witch). I played blue-black and went 9-0. 

**What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?** 

[Thing in the Ice](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thing+in+the+Ice). I drafted a really bad blue-green deck and barely squeaked out a 1-2.

**What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?** 

Three copies of [Topplegeist](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Topplegeist). I played Green-White Aggro/Delirium and went 3-0.

### Shaun McLaren

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpbar16/t8_mclaren.jpg)

**Age:** 28 

**Occupation:** Professional *Magic* player and content creator for StarCityGames.com 

**Hometown:** Edmonton, Alberta, Canada 

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 

Winner of Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*. Second at Pro Tour *Khans of Tarkir*. Three Grand Prix Top 8s. 

**What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?** 

Red-Green Werewolves with two [Pack Guardian](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Guardian)s. 8-1.

**What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?** 

[Arlinn Kord](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arlinn+Kord). Red-Green Werewolves. 2-1. Had a bunch of 2-drops.

**What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?** 

Green-Blue Clues with [Jace, Unraveler of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Unraveler+of+Secrets), [Tamiyo's Journal](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tamiyo%27s+Journal), and two [Fleeting Memories](https://web.archive.org/web/20160417000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleeting+Memories).
