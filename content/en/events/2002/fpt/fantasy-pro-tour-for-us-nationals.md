It's time for the Fantasy Pro Tour for US Nationals!

Each owner will select an eight-player team worth 100 Pro Tour Points, based on the Professional Standings that led to that event. Players are free to select a team consisting of only two people worth a total of 100 points. There is no requirement that all eight slots be filled.

If more than one entry is received from the same person, the last entry will be used.

The person whose team wins the most money at the event will be the winner. The winning owner will receive a box of any Standard-legal expansion. The second-through-fourth place owners will split a box.
