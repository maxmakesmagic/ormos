Pro Tour champion Luis Scott-Vargas has another title for his mantle! After making the Top 8 at Pro Tour Nagoya two weeks earlier and making a layover to help win the **Magic Online** Community Cup, the American superstar has captured the title of Grand Prix Kansas City champion! The format was *Scars of Mirrodin* Block Sealed Deck on Day 1 followed by *Scars of Mirrodin* Block Draft on Day 2. Luis had to best some solid competition in the Top 8, but his blue-black deck proved up to the task. Read more about the event in the [official coverage](/en/events/2011/gpkan11/lsv-vapor-snags-a-trophy-in-kansas-city).
