Once again, we have two Planeswalker Decks being released with *Amonkhet*, this time featuring the visages of Gideon, Martial Paragon and Liliana, Death Wielder. Should you go looking for one of these decks, these are the two cards you'll see staring back at you from the front.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_dZGIK04peP.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_D5IulOvOh1.png)

If you pick up the package featuring Liliana gracing its cover, you'll get a full deck of Standard-legal cards, including these four cards that are found only in the Planeswalker Deck:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_56ySiCdtoi.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_fEaY9jHDFz.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_mbVorMDziL.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_olEf5iXy9L.png)

If you pick up the Gideon, Martial Beefslab Paragon deck, you'll receive, among other cards, these four:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_LRAdMU66VH.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_6oZ3HyFmjc.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_2N7Yi2uqi1.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/dw466ytu5_akh/en_UF2RIm3huj.png)

Planeswalker Decks will be available with *Amonkhet* at [a local store near you](https://web.archive.org/web/20170406000000/http://locator.wizards.com/). We'll release the full decklists the week leading up to the [*Amonkhet* Prerelease](https://web.archive.org/web/20170406000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/event-types/prerelease) (which is April 22!).
