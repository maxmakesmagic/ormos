## *March of the Machine* Out Tomorrow!

![March of the Machine logo](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/en_XgPR556ttyOM.png) 

The end is nigh!

Realmbreaker, Elesh Norn's twisted version of Kaldheim's World Tree, has breached the planes, and Phyrexian forces are swarming. Tomorrow, its loathsome limbs deposit *March of the Machine* in *MTG Arena*, bringing [new cards](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/03-29-march-of-the-machine-card-image-gallery), [new mechanics](/en/articles/feature/2023/03-29-march-of-the-machine-mechanics), [new Store offerings](#store), and new *March of the Machine* Sealed and Draft events—including the [Standard Metagame Challenge](#smc) happening this weekend!

## New Store Offerings

We have new goodies coming to the Store tomorrow alongside the release of *March of the Machine*:

*   Full-art lands
*   New Praetor card styles
*   Sticker bundle, featuring Chandra, Borborygmos & Fblthp, and Yargle & Multani stickers

![Chandra sticker, with Chandra, hair and finger aflame, blowing out her finger and her hair goes out at the same time](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/sw7cu3hodr5g.gif) ![Borborygmos & Fblthmp sticker, with Fblthp popping up on Borborygmos's head, waving](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/ya9po2ospito.gif) ![Yargle & Multani sticker, with Multani clinging to Yargle while Yargle slaps away a spiky metallic Phyrexian sphere](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/si2rebifraph.gif) 

Visit the *MTG Arena* Store in game tomorrow to pick up these and more!

## Arena Open: *March of the Machine* April 29–30

![Arena Open: March of the Machine happening April 29–30](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/wrodrinez1ju.jpg) 

Get your Sealed and Draft skills limbered up, because [the next Arena Open](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2023/04-17-arena-open-march-of-the-machine-april-2930) is coming up fast! April 29–30, compete over two days in *March of the Machine* Sealed (Best-of-One and Best-of-Three) and *March of the Machine* Traditional Draft matches for up to $2,000 and an invitation to the May 13 Qualifier Weekend event.

Every participant in this Arena Open will receive the Atraxa avatar!

![Atraxa avatar entry reward for Arena Open: March of the Machine](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/avatar_full_atraxa.png)

## PREMIER PLAY SCHEDULE

All times listed are Pacific time (UTC-07:00).

### QUALIFIER EVENTS

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events.

Qualifier tokens are delivered in your *MTG Arena* inbox. Remember to claim them before the event starts!

#### May

*   **Qualifier Play-In**– **Best-of-One**
     May 6, 6 a.m. PT
    *   Format: *March of the Machine* Limited
*   **Qualifier Play-In**– **Best-of-Three**
     May 12, 6 a.m. PT–May 13, 3 a.m. PT
    *   Format: *March of the Machine* Limited
*   **Qualifier Weekend**
     May 13, 6 a.m. PT–May 14, 4 p.m. PT
    *   Format: *March of the Machine* Limited (Best-of-Three)

### Arena Championship 3

The Arena Championship is an invitation-only, two-day virtual event for players who earn invitations through Qualifier Weekend events. 

*   **Arena Championship 3**
     May 27–28
     More details to come!

The Arena Open Day One entry window begins at 6 a.m. PT and closes to new entries the following day at 3 a.m. PT (UTC-07:00). The Day Two entry window is 2 hours only, from 6 a.m. PT until 8 a.m. PT (UTC-07:00).
