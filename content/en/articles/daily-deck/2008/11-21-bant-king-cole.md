It doesn’t get more Bant than Cole Griffith’s States Top 8 list from Nebraska. It features Bant Charm, Rhox War Monk, and a full set of Stoic Angels to control the opponent’s forces on the ground. Broken Ambitions, Negate, and Cryptic Command help control any unexpected plays, and Jace Beleren keeps Cole’s hand filled to the brim with Banty goodness. No doubt we’ll see more such decks in the future as the brand new Standard format feels its way out.
