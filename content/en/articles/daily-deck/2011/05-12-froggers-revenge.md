This is the deck former Pro Tour player and current Wizards R&D member Zac Hill used for spellslinging at the *New Phyrexia* Prerelease. Zac called the deck "Frogger's Revenge" after the playtest name of the card we know as Fresh Meat. You can follow Zac on his Twitter feed, @[ZDCH](https://web.archive.org/web/20110512000000/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Twitter.com/ZDCH). Can't wait to play the set yourself? You'll get your chance [this weekend](/en/events/2008/release-facts/worldwide-launch-parties) when the set goes on sale!
