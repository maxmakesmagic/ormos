![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gplon02/963.jpg)

Jelger has had a breakout year on the Pro Tour, with third place in New Orleans and three Grand Prix top eights enough to place him in the top ten in the player of the year race. Sam is one of England's top players and he represented them in the World Championships earlier this year. He is also playing a very interesting deck type, with [Mirari's Wake](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirari%27s+Wake) powering a control deck with [Fervent Denial](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fervent+Denial) and [Krosan Reclamation](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Krosan+Reclamation)s and the like, which is capable of recursing and countering every single one of an opponent's spells in the long game.

### Game One

The game started out slowly with only lands for the first three turns. Sam's land drops of Forest, Island, and Plains probably served to confuse Jelger a little. The Dutchman finally dropped a [Nantuko Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Shade) on his fourth turn, which was [Aether Burst](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Burst)ed and since Sam didn't have a counter in hand he decided to tap out for [Concentrate](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Concentrate), hoping that he wasn't about to get [Mind Sludge](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind+Sludge)d. Instead he was Haunting Echoed, which removed all his [Aether Burst](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Burst)s, [Concentrate](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Concentrate)s, and [Moment's Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moment%27s+Peace)s, although Sam did have another Peace in his hand.

![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gplon02/962.jpg)

Sam dropped a [Mirari's Wake](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirari%27s+Wake), while Jelger replayed his [Nantuko Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Shade) and added a second one for good measure. Gomersall played [Compulsion](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsion), and fogged Jelger's attack with the Peace. When Sam played a [Mirari](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirari) it looked as though he was getting ready to do something fairly impressive. Once again Wiegersma's large Shades failed to do damage as Sam flashed back his [Moment's Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moment%27s+Peace). On his next turn Sam cast [Kirtar's Wrath](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kirtar%27s+Wrath) which put two 1/1s into play. They were promptly Edicted but it was pretty obvious that Sam was in a great position as he [Mirari](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirari)ed [Cunning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cunning+Wish) to get [Fervent Denial](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fervent+Denial) and [Flash of Insight](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flash+of+Insight), then forked his Insight to look twice for ten cards. Another double [Cunning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cunning+Wish) fetched [Elephant Ambush](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elephant+Ambush) and [Moment's Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moment%27s+Peace). Jelger had had enough and conceded when Sam cast the Ambush.

### Game Two

Sam had to take a mulligan at the start of game two which at least gave Jelger hope as the match up is very bad for him. Jelger attempted to cast [Braids](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Braids) which was immediately [Aether Burst](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Burst)ed. When Gomersall main phase Compulsed, the Dutchman was happy to take the chance to [Mind Sludge](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind+Sludge) his entire hand. Wiegersma played [Braids](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Braids) again, and in a hopeless position, Sam conceded to save a little time.

### Game Three

![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gplon02/961.jpg)

Once again Sam had to mulligan, this time after a long think. His six card draw also had no blue mana sources, so it was down to five cards. He kept this and played a second turn [Compulsion](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsion). Jelger had two [Nantuko Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Shade)s which threatened to end the game fairly quickly. Gomersall forgot to activate [Compulsion](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsion) and this might have cost him as he was one mana short of playing [Kirtar's Wrath](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kirtar%27s+Wrath) when he died. The last two games were very unfortunate for Sam, as this match-up is usually heavily in favour of the [Mirari](https://web.archive.org/web/20020831000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirari) deck.

Jelger Wiegersma 2 - Sam Gomersall 1
