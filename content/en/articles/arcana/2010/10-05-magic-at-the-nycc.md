Are you going to the New York Comic Con this weekend? Well, we are! In fact, there's a [list of our activities](/en/articles/feature/2010/09-29-magic-at-new-york-comic-con-2010) already. Here, we'll reprint the highlights so you don't even need to click that link.

### Schedule Highlights

*   **Magic: The Gathering** Intro Pack League – daily from opening to close
*   **Magic** Booster Drafts – daily from opening to close
*   Mini Master: *From the Vault: Relics* – Friday 5 p.m. Top 2 players will each receive a *From the Vault: Relics*, additional boosters given out based on record
*   Super Friday Night **Magic** – Friday 7 p.m.
*   EDH Tournament – Friday 7:30 p.m.
*   Team Mini Master – Saturday 10 a.m. and 7 p.m.
*   Mini Master: *From the Vault: Relics* – Sunday, 2:30 p.m. Top 2 players will each receive a *From the Vault: Relics*, additional boosters given out based on record

But we'd like to focus in a bit on a special event. You can also challenge the Frag Dolls on Duels for a chance to win an Astro A30 gaming headset! That's a headset that looks very much like this:

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/552_headset.jpg)  

And here's what it looks like on a Frag Doll!

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/552_headsetworn.jpg)  

That's a picture from a very similar Frag Doll Challenge from PAX. Everyone seemed to have a good time at that one, so we're confident it will be fun again. And here are some details!

Date:Friday 10/8/10  
 Time: 3:00 – 5:00 PM  
 Place: Wizards of the Coast Booth 1624  
 Challenge/Platform: Duels of the Planeswalkers on Xbox or PC

Date: Saturday 10/9/10  
 Time: 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM & 4:00 – 6:00 PM  
 Place: Wizards of the Coast Booth 1624  
 Challenge/Platform: Duels of the Planeswalkers on Xbox or PC

Date: Sunday 10/10/10  
 Time: 1:00 – 3:00 PM  
 Place: Wizards of the Coast Booth 1624  
 Challenge/Platform: Duels of the Planeswalkers on Xbox or PC

1.  The Frag Doll Challenge on Xbox, PC and MTGO is open to all ticketed New York Comic Con attendees.
2.  Winners will each receive one (1) A30 Astro Gaming Headset valued at $129.95.
3.  Runners up will receive one (1) Duels of the Planeswalkers PC version coupon code.

You can see a full list of **Magic** events at New York Comic Con [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20101005000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=mtg/daily/events/nycc10-schedule), or you can visit [www.tjcollect.com](http://www.tjcollect.com/).

For complete information about New York Comic Con, visit [www.nycomiccon.com](http://www.nycomiccon.com/).
