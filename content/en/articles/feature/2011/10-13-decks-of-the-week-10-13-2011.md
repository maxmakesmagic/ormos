Last weekend threw open the flood gates on *Innistrad* in the Limited environment. Grand Prix Milan saw almost 1,800 players take their shot at glory, with Italy's Marco Ricci emerging out of a strong Top 8 to [keep the title on home soil](https://web.archive.org/web/20111013000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/innistrad-italy-sees-ricci-run-rampant). The qualifier season for Pro Tour *Dark Ascension* got into full swing as well, with three players already earning their tickets to the first Pro Tour of 2012 in sunny Honolulu. Makes you wonder... do zombies surf?
