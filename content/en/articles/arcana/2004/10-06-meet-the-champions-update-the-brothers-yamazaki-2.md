Ready for another story about the heroes of Kamigawa? Check out the [*Champions of Kamigawa* site](https://web.archive.org/web/20041006000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/displayexpansion.asp?set=chk&page=1) for an update in the "Meet the Champions" area.

[](https://web.archive.org/web/20041006000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/chk/yamazaki)

This week's story is "**[Bonds of Ice and Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20041006000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/chk/yamazaki)**" by Jay Moldenhauer-Salazar. It's the story of how the legendary warriors, the [Brothers Yamazaki](https://web.archive.org/web/20041006000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Brothers%2BYamazaki) [Brothers Yamazaki2](https://web.archive.org/web/20041006000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Brothers%2BYamazaki2), became revered in their own time, and yet why their accomplishments were never recorded in Kamigawa's written history. Enjoy!
