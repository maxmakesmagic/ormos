## GRAND PRIX HONG KONG 2015

TOP 8 COVERAGE

851 players arrived in Hong Kong to immerse in a weekend of *Magic Origins*. Fifteen rounds later, the Top 8 consisted of four local players, two from the Philippines and the remaining two from China and Malaysia.

Perhaps what was most surprising was the lack of Japanese players in the Top 8, for they have traditionally posed a huge challenge at Asian Grand Prix. The most experienced players in the elimination rounds were Liu Yuchen, a three-time Top 8 competitor, Ang Jan (former Philippines National Champion) and MTG Mint Card’s Gold Pro Yam Wing Chun.

However, it was Chye Yian Hsiang who emerged victorious with an extremely streamlined and brutal White Red Aggro deck, harnessing the power of Pia and Kiran Nalaar!

Rounding off the rest of the Top 8 are Wu Songwei, Cheung Lap Chung, Lim Victoriano and Kwok Suen Wang, all very happy to earn invitations to Pro Tour Return to Zendikar in Milwaukee.

As we prepare to vacate the tournament hall, the doors are being opened at San Diego, where a second Grand Prix Champion will be crowned for the week.

Congratulations to Chye once again! You’ve done Malaysia proud and you're currently leading the Pro Player Race for the new season!
