[*Commander Masters*](https://web.archive.org/web/20230726000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/commander-masters) Set Boosters, available for preorder from [your local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20230726000000/https://locator.wizards.com/) and online retailers like [Amazon](https://gtly.to/pE5bySDfY), include a special art card depicting 1 of 81 pieces of artwork from the set. In addition, 1 in 10 art cards opened may be gold stamped with the artist's signature or a Planeswalker symbol.

You can learn more about Set Boosters and the other amazing products from *Commander Masters* in our [Collecting *Commander Masters*](/en/articles/feature/2023/07-11-collecting-commander-masters) article and check out the art cards themselves below (non-stamped versions have the same art but without the artist's signature or Planeswalker symbol).
