Round 2 of the World Championships sees France's Gabriel Nassif versus Mike Hron of Madison, Wisconsin, USA. Gab is known for his mastery of Constructed play, including a Top 8 at last year's World Championships, while Mike's big move on the Pro Tour came this year in Geneva at the start of the season, where he became the first American in years to win a Pro Tour.

To determine who was to go first, Gab got two cards from a Legacy deck and shuffled them up.

"Find the high casting cost!"

When Hron found a land compared to Nassif's Force of Will, he joked that they both cost zero. Nassif smiled, and chose to play.

### Game 1

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration//sideboard/images/worlds07/r2_hron_nassif.jpg)*Gabriel Nassif and Mike Hron, both Pro Tour winners*A turn-one [Lotus Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotus+Bloom) was soon joined by an array of charge lands from Nassif, while Hron had a [Wall of Roots](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Roots) to accelerate out [Siege-Gang Commander](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege-Gang+Commander) from his red-green big mana deck.

Nassif played a [Spinerock Knoll](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spinerock+Knoll), hiding something away for later, and passed his first turn with the Bloom in play without playing a spell. The big spells were coming from Hron at this point. He followed up on his Siege-Gang with [Garruk Wildspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Wildspeaker), untapped two lands, and used [Into the North](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Into+the+North) to find a [Scrying Sheets](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scrying+Sheets).

With four creatures on the board that could attack and a [Garruk Wildspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Wildspeaker) on four counters, Hron could move in for the kill the very next turn. Nassif had other plans, though, as he unleashed his deck's brutal finish. Two copies of [Pyromancer's Swath](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyromancer%27s+Swath) came down, followed by a [Grapeshot](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grapeshot) for 15 damage. This turned on the ability of [Spinerock Knoll](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spinerock+Knoll). When it revealed a second [Grapeshot](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grapeshot), Hron scooped up his cards.

Nassif 1, Hron 0

### Game 2

On the draw in Game 2, Nassif faced a slightly unusual draw including just one land, but three copies of [Rite of Flame](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rite+of+Flame). He kept it, but missed his second land drop. A suspended [Rift Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rift+Bolt) came from Nassif on turn two, and on turn three, he went for it.

First the [Rift Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rift+Bolt) took Hron to 17. Then three copies of [Rite of Flame](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rite+of+Flame) gave Nassif seven mana. [Pyromancer's Swath](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyromancer%27s+Swath) came down, followed by an [Incinerate](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Incinerate) for 5 damage. To finish things, [Grapeshot](https://web.archive.org/web/19691231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grapeshot) with seven storm copies left Hron dead before playing a single spell.

**Final Result:** Nassif 2, Hron 0
