**Magic** Game Day whetted your appetite for *Tenth Edition*. Now that you have all these *Tenth* cards lying around—or you didn't make it to Game Day and you need to kick off your *Tenth Edition* collection with a bang—find yourself a *Tenth Edition* fat pack.

![Tenth Edition Fat Pack](https://i.ormos.uk/a0/2e/a02e1f6c19aa0688de0c5b97aa06ff78b7bc55b9e8915bfdba370e4cba67c5ea.jpg)  
*Tenth Edition Fat Pack contents*

As you can see above, the fat pack comes with:

*   A player's guide with a complete visual encyclopedia of the set,
*   Two card boxes with a panoramic art (see below) by Kev Walker, and six color-coded card dividers,
*   Six *Tenth Edition* boosters,
*   A pack of 40 *Tenth Edition* basic lands,
*   A Tenth Edition Spindown life counter (only available in this fat pack), and
*   One random Pro Tour Player card.

![Tenth Edition Fat Pack](https://i.ormos.uk/55/b5/55b591860943de18db375912d202aa230299986de986e5a99bf2cadbb10ac265.jpg)  
*Tenth Edition Fat Pack*

Kev Walker's panorama depicts [Reya Dawnbringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20070716000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reya+Dawnbringer) and two [Serra Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20070716000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serra+Angel)s taking on the [Lord of the Pit](https://web.archive.org/web/20070716000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lord+of+the+Pit)—a classic battle of the forces of good versus a singular master of evil. The panorama art is tiled across the two card boxes in such a way that they line up when placed next to each other.

![Tenth Edition Fat Pack](https://i.ormos.uk/76/ba/76ba3073636ec7822c8e73df8ef5c132dce79f4dcde73093a1d2f26fc166c0e4.jpg)  
*Tenth Edition fat pack panorama by Kev Walker*

Check the hobby store nearest you for the *Tenth Edition* fat pack!
