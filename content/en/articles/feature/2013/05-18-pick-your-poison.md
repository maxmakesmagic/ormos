One certain creature in particular is casting a very long shadow over Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze*.

Well, two shadows.

[Ætherling](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86therling), the super upgrade of the former "best creature in the game" [Morphling](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morphling), has certainly lived up to its lineage. Effectively untouchable, quite unblockable, and almost literally unbeatable, [Ætherling](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86therling) has been the finisher of choice for control decks sporting blue mana.

But it hasn't been the only one.

While everyone with blue mana and a plan to extend the game past turn 6 or so is rocking several copies of [Ætherling](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86therling), control decks of all kinds are also leaning on a number of cards to close out games, some with more success than others.

Inspired by Jace, Architect of thought, we played a little game of Pro and Con with Reid Duke and Martin Juza on the possible finishers in *Return to Ravnica* Block Constructed to see what you want and why you want it when trying to end a long game in a hurry.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Aetherling)](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherling)

 

"There are just very few ways to get it off the board once it sticks. You would have to really go out of your way, like playing something like [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle). Plus, it's difficult to race."  
 –Reid Duke

"[Ætherling](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86therling) is the best, by far. The fact is that nothing interacts with it and it races really well. I mean, you can [Fog](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fog) it for a turn with something like [Far // Away](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Far+%2F%2F+Away), but that's about it."  
 –Martin Juza

**Pros:** Virtually unkillable with enough blue mana, fast clock once in play, plays offense and defense, no vulnerability to [Renounce the Guilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Renounce+the+Guilds), [Mizzium Mortars](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzium+Mortars), [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict), or pretty much anything

**Cons:** Expensive to play and protect in one turn, more expensive than other options, somewhat vulnerable to [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle) (which isn't seeing much play this weekend)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Blood%20Baron%20of%20Vizkopa)](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood%20Baron%20of%20Vizkopa)

 

"Very good against creatures, not very good against [Ætherling](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86therling)."  
 –Reid Duke

"We thought there would be more Junk, which it's good against, but I don't like it at all. I wish it was just more Obzedats."  
 –Martin Juza (his team is playing four [Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood) Baron's in their Esper deck)

**Pros:** [Lifelink](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifelink) destroys aggressive decks, protection from two highly-played colors

**Cons:** Vulnerable to [Mizzium Mortars](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzium+Mortars) and [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict), restricted to Orzhov-based decks, smaller than [Advent of the Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Advent+of+the+Wurm) token

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Obzedat,%20Ghost%20Council)](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Obzedat,%20Ghost%20Council)

 

"Ghost Council is a very good card, but it's not really the best tool for any single job. It's good in RWB decks that need to beat [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict)."  
 –Reid Duke

"There's a lot of Esper here, and it's very good in the mirror. If you expect a lot of Esper, you play this."  
 –Martin Juza

**Pros:** Difficult for control decks to kill, not vulnerable to [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict), helps race or hold off aggressive decks

**Cons:** [Renounce the Guilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Renounce+the+Guilds), very restrictive casting cost, legendary, vulnerable to [Selesnya Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Selesnya+Charm)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Maze%27s%20End)](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Maze%27s%20End)

 

"It's a really, really good card, but it causes a lot of problems with deck construction. You can play it, but then if you get a Guildgate milled by [Psychic Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychic+Strike), you lose unless you play two of each. But then all of your lands come into play tapped."  
 –Reid Duke

"We tried to prevent Luis from seeing the list, so he wouldn't say 'sweet' and try to play it,"  
 –Martin Juza, laughing and adding that it restricts deck construction a lot.

**Pros:** Virtually indestructible and uncounterable, uses up land slots instead of spell slots, provides inevitability, fixes mana along the way

**Cons:** Extremely restrictive on deck building choices, lands come into play tapped, very slow

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Angel%20of%20Serenity)](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel%20of%20Serenity)

 

"Angel is the type of finisher that I'm used to: all I have to do is survive until I can cast it. It's particularly strong against the token decks."  
 –Reid Duke

"I think it's like one mana too slow. It's great against Green-White, but [Merciless Eviction](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merciless+Eviction) does the same thing for one less mana."  
 –Martin Juza

**Pros:** Plays defense and offense, can turn around disadvantageous board positions, can rebuild wrecked board positions, offers [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) protection

**Cons:** Expensive, vulnerable to [Selesnya Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Selesnya+Charm), doesn't race [Obzedat, Ghost Council](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Obzedat%2C+Ghost+Council), [Ætherling](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86therling), or [Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood) Baron well

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Aurelia,%20the%20Warleader)](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aurelia,%20the%20Warleader)

 

"It's a nice one-of or two-of in decks that can pressure life totals."  
 –Reid Duke

"There really isn't a good deck for it. The UWR deck is fine, but it doesn't have [Far // Away](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Far+%2F%2F+Away). That deck has a big problem with regenerating creatures like [Lotleth Troll](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotleth+Troll) and [Varolz, the Scar-Striped](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Varolz%2C+the+Scar-Striped)."  
 –Martin Juza

**Pros:** Kills quickly, one of the few that can race [Ætherling](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86therling)

**Cons:** Restrictive casting cost, vulnerable to [Mizzium Mortars](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzium+Mortars), [Renounce the Guilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Renounce+the+Guilds)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Assemble%20the%20Legion)](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Assemble%20the%20Legion)

 

"It's good when you need to diversify."  
 –Reid Duke

"It's bad because of this card \[flashes [Jace, Architect of Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Architect+of+Thought)\]. It might be fine with [Legion's Initiative](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Legion%27s+Initiative), but there isn't really a deck that wants those two."  
 –Martin Juza

**Pros:** Difficult to kill, plays some defense, not vulnerable to [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict)

**Cons:** [Jace, Architect of Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Architect+of+Thought), doesn't race [Ætherling](https://web.archive.org/web/20130518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86therling) well
