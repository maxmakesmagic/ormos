Despite Modern being a relatively powerful format, it's often the fair-looking decks that perform well. [Ghor-Clan Rampager](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghor-Clan+Rampager), [Flinthoof Boar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flinthoof+Boar), and [Thundermaw Hellkite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thundermaw+Hellkite) have made appearances in various decks, but so far not in the same deck.

![Domri Rade](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Domri%20Rade&options=)Presenting a steady stream of large threats has done well against the grindy BG decks of the format, especially with [Chandra, Pyromaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Pyromaster) and [Domri Rade](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Domri+Rade) giving the deck a nice late game. [Thundermaw Hellkite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thundermaw+Hellkite) is a particularly strong threat right now considering how good it is against the recently unbanned [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom). [Strangleroot Geist](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strangleroot+Geist) is also pretty good against both [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom) and [Liliana of the Veil](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+of+the+Veil).

I really like this deck's sideboard also. Combo decks are always an issue, but [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon), [Combust](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Combust), and [Torpor Orb](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torpor+Orb) team up in the sideboard to fight the various combo decks in Modern. [Ancient Grudge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Grudge) and [Bonfire of the Damned](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bonfire+of+the+Damned) are great hosers for other popular strategies, too.

Is grebyn90 Brian Kibler's new Magic Online screen name? I doubt it, but I wouldn't be surprised to see Kibler rocking something similar at Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*, except with more [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20140221000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl)s.
