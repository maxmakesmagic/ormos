Adrian Sullivan is a name that has appeared in this space more than a time or two, and the commencement of the Pro Tour Qualifier season for San Juan sees him return in the first weekend of competition! His Bloodchief Ascension deck for Extended is truly one of a kind and took him all the way to 3rd place in Minneapolis, Minnesota. This black-red burn deck has powerful backup plans in the form of Countryside Crusher, Bloodchief Ascension, and Isochron Scepter.
