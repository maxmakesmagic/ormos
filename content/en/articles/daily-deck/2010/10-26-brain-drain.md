*Scars of Mirrodin* Game Day is fast approaching! The event, scheduled for October 30-31, is a global opportunity to play Standard with the new set! You get cool promos just for playing, and the tournament will even debut the very first cards to be seen from the upcoming release *Mirrodin Besieged*! How do you earn those cool sneak peeks? By playing an affiliated deck! Gavin Verhey published the following deck in a recent article for [StarCityGames.com](http://starcitygames.com/), and it happens to follow the rules for affiliated decks. What are those?
