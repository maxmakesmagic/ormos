## *Live the Dream Cube* Season 3

The Spotlight Cube Series returns with Season 3 updates to David McDarby's *Live the Dream Cube*. Since its last showing, David has added cards from *Strixhaven: School of Mages*, *Dungeons & Dragons: Adventures in the Forgotten Realms*, *Modern Horizons 2*, *Innistrad: Midnight Hunt*, and *Innistrad: Crimson Vow*! The cube runs from November 24 through December 1, and you can check out the full card list and read all about the archetypes in [today's update article](/en/articles/magiconline/2021/11-23-spotlight-cube-series-live-the-dream-cube-season-3).

## *Modern Horizons 2* Returns

While we currently have *Vintage Cube* up and running, it's time to bring back another fan favorite: *Modern Horizons 2* Draft! We're bringing back the non-Phantom Draft League now through January 5 with the same prize structure and chance to open a Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer you know and love!

*   Entry Options:
    *   25 Event Tickets
    *   3 *Modern Horizons 2* boosters and 4 Event Tickets
    *   250 Play Points
*   Prizes:
    *   3 Wins – 6 *Modern Horizons 2* boosters, 2 MOCS Season 3 QPs, and a Competitive Trophy
    *   2 Wins – 2 *Modern Horizons 2* boosters

## Innistrad Chaos Draft Ends Tomorrow

From November 17–24, you will be able to find Innistrad Chaos Draft in the Limited Play Lobby. In this three-round Draft format, each of the eight players will open a random booster pack from among all seven Innistrad sets. In addition, as there are only seven sets to choose from but eight different players opening eight packs each round, each set will be opened at least once each round, with only a single set being duplicated. The Spooky Season may be over for now, but Innistrad horrors (including opening a booster pack of Avacyn Restored) live on forever!

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format Challenges are the first step toward qualifying for Premier Play through your favorite *Magic* formats and provide excellent prizes for those looking to test their mettle in their favorite formats. [Learn more about Premier Play qualification](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021) to get started today.

*Magic Online*'s Format Challenges run once on Saturdays and once on Sundays for each Constructed format! On weekends featuring Showcase Challenge events, the Format Challenge that starts at either 6 a.m., 8 a.m., or 10 a.m. Pacific time is pre-empted by the Showcase Challenge—Format Challenges scheduled at other times remain in place. *NOTE: Pauper, Vintage, and Standard Challenges are never pre-empted.*

*New for MOCS Season 3: Pauper Showcase Challenges are held at 8 a.m. Saturday and do not pre-empt the Modern Challenge.*
