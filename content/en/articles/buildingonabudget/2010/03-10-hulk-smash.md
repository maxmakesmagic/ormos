It's Adventure Week here on **magicthegathering.com**, so strap on your [Adventuring Gear](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Adventuring+Gear) and get ready for a wild ride. This week I am proud to introduce a new Extended archetype that has the ability to win games on the first turn!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5f/59/5f593c032d368301af540252a35b53b4a97551b6b81a6248cca076110b3c1521.jpg)  

I was shuffling a random pile of cards at a friend's house last week when I saw a copy of [Through the Breach](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Through+the+Breach). I started asking around about [Through the Breach](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Through+the+Breach) with [Protean Hulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Protean+Hulk). [Carrion Feeder](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Carrion+Feeder) is not legal in extended anymore so the [Reveillark](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reveillark) / [Body Double](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Body+Double) loop just doesn't work. A few people suggested that I could use the [Protean Hulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Protean+Hulk) trigger to fetch up a playset of [Hedron Crab](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hedron+Crab)s along with a [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) and a playset of [Dryad Arbor](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dryad+Arbor). The idea sounded a bit silly, but I decided to throw together a list and see how things panned out.

I will start by explaining the combo:

Once you have assembled [Protean Hulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Protean+Hulk) and [Through the Breach](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Through+the+Breach) in your hand you cast the [Through the Breach](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Through+the+Breach) and put the [Protean Hulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Protean+Hulk) onto the battlefield. At the end of the turn you sacrifice the [Protean Hulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Protean+Hulk). You search your library for four copies of [Hedron Crab](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hedron+Crab), four copies of [Dryad Arbor](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dryad+Arbor), and one copy of [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sakura-Tribe+Elder). Each crab mills for three per land. The math works like this: 3 x 4 x 5 = 60. If you already drew combo pieces, it usually doesn't matter. If you draw [Hedron Crab](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hedron+Crab) you can usually get a few mills out of it before your opponent decides to kill it. You also have an army of blockers post-combo that can usually buy you an extra turn to play a land and fully mill out an opponent if you had an awkward draw.

**[Seething Song](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seething+Song) and [Simian Spirit Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simian+Spirit+Guide):** Mana acceleration is very important. Turn four is the norm nowadays in Extended. We need cards like this to break serve. I won one of my first test games on turn two with a [Simian Spirit Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simian+Spirit+Guide) and [Seething Song](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seething+Song).

**[Through the Breach](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Through+the+Breach):** A key combo piece.

**[Protean Hulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Protean+Hulk):** I chose to play only two copies of the [Protean Hulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Protean+Hulk). [Summoner's Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoner%27s+Pact) seemed strictly superior to me because I could grab it with [Peer Through Depths](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peer+Through+Depths).

**[Peer Through Depths](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peer+Through+Depths):** This is the best tutor available for a deck like this. [Peer Through Depths](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peer+Through+Depths) finds our mana acceleration, [Summoner's Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoner%27s+Pact)s, and [Through the Breach](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Through+the+Breach)es.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/76/9e/769eca685e1583502d0aae81a6c29a21bd3810ac3be1a0f271405b5a805404cb.jpg)  

**[Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder):** I have a hard time not playing [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder) in my blue combo decks.

**[Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sakura-Tribe+Elder):** The most impressive Snake in the history of the game serves two purposes here: [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) accelerates our mana and acts as a combo piece.

**[Hedron Crab](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hedron+Crab) and [Dryad Arbor](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dryad+Arbor):** The fruition of the combo.

The deck performed decently for me in further testing. It lacks the consistency to be a top-tier tournament deck, but it is certainly a lot of fun to play. The deck sometimes gets unbeatable draws. (I don't mean [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom) into [Mistbind Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistbind+Clique) unbeatable, I mean [Black Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Lotus) into [Ancestral Recall](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Recall) into [Yawgmoth's Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yawgmoth%27s+Will) unbeatable.) If you're the type who likes to play mill decks, then I strongly recommend trying out this combo. It also seems like a good alternative for people who have been playing with [Hive Mind](https://web.archive.org/web/20100310000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hive+Mind) this Extended season.
