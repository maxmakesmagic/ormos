P*lanechase* is coming on September 4! Refresh yourself on the rules [here](/en/articles/feature/2009/08-10-planechase-rules-revealed), then prepare to purchase! Each deck will have an MSRP of $19.99. Today, we're going to tell you what's in the Metallic dreams deck. We'll show you the plane deck first, then move on to the sixty-card **Magic** deck. And watch for the *Zendikar* preview card!

### Metallic Dreams

The "Metallic Dreams" deck uses the power of artifacts to quickly produce the mana needed to cast powerful, mana-hungry creatures. Planeswalk to the Hippodrome to bog down combat while you amass your forces, then demolish the opposition.
