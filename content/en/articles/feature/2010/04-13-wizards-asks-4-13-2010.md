*What are you hoping to open at the Rise of the Eldrazi Prerelease?*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e0/0c/e00c2900e4532fed44efdbc0223f486ee1518c0cf594bb289867319eeda0f043.jpg)**Kelly Digges:** Unlike my answer about board sweepers a couple of weeks ago (I was talking about All Is Dust, by the way, as many of you guessed), I'll keep this fair and stick to [cards that have been previewed](https://web.archive.org/web/20130702132920/https://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/riseoftheeldrazi/spoiler).

Tom LaPille has [already talked about](/en/articles/latestdevelopments/2010/04-09-the-hand-that-feeds) my quest to cast Hand of Emrakul on turn three (which there are multiple ways to do, two that use only commons!). But that's not *one* card exactly.

I'm tempted to pick Mark Rosewater's [preview from yesterday](/en/articles/feature/2010/04-12-on-the-rise-part-iii), Broodwarden, which gives all my Eldrazi Spawn +2/+1. But smashing with a swarm of 2/2s will always be Plan B.

Ultimately, I've got to go with the biggest, craziest creature in *Rise of the Eldrazi*: Emrakul, the Aeons Torn.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c4/0d/c40d61f9ad1c25d67faa401c67ecc4556b50e27e230615d2fe4b06f2c58c40bd.jpg)  

Sure, it's the Prerelease card, so I'll walk away with at least one copy. Sure, it costs fifteen mana, so I might not even be able to cast it. But when I do, it is going to be unforgettable.

Have fun at the Prerelease!
