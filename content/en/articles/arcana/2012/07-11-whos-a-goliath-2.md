Consider [Zombie Goliath](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zombie+Goliath).

![Zombie Goliath](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Zombie+Goliath)

  

At first glance, it's just a zombie. So what makes it a Zombie Giant? Well, take a closer look.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/1013_zombiegoliath.jpg)

[Zombie Goliath](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zombie+Goliath) | Art by [E.M. Gist](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22E.+M.Gist%22%5D)

See its head? See how there are a couple of things in there? *Those* are regular-size zombies. They're riding in the [Zombie Goliath](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zombie+Goliath)'s cranium! Now the flavor text makes more sense:

>  *"Removing the encumbrance of useless brain tissue grants several advantages: a slight increase in mobility, a response of revulsion and horror from the enemy, and, in the case of large specimens, room for passengers."  
>  —Zul Ashur, lich lord*
