**Throwback Standard Gauntlet 8: *Innistrad* era ends tomorrow, November 8**

Throwback Standard Gauntlet 8: *Innistrad* era will close at 7 a.m. PT tomorrow. Join this event in the Play Lobby -> Limited Tournaments -> Leagues. Entry options include 10 Tix, or 100 Play Points.

Decklists and additional information about Throwback Standard Gauntlet 8: *Innistrad* era can be found [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/10-25-throwback-standard-gauntlet-8-innistrad-standard).

**Throwback Standard Gauntlet 9: Recent Past begins tomorrow, November 8**

Throwback Standard Gauntlet 9: Recent Past begins tomorrow, November 8 and will run until November 17. That's right, this Gauntlet will run an extra two days! Join this event in the Play Lobby -> Limited Tournaments -> Leagues. Entry options include 10 Tix, or 100 Play Points.

Decklists and additional information about Throwback Standard Gauntlet 9: Recent Past can be found [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/11-01-throwback-standard-gauntlet-9-recent-past).

**Standard MOCS Playoff this Saturday, November 11**

The fourth quarter of competition in the *Magic Online* Championship Series (MOCS) culminates on November 11 for the final [Playoff Event](https://web.archive.org/web/20171107000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2017-magic-online-championship-series#PlayoffEvents) of the 2017 series! Big prizes, the chance to move up the [MOCS Leaderboard](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/magic-online-leaderboard-points), plus invitations to the *Magic Online* Championship and *Pro Tour Rivals of Ixalan* are all on the line in this event.

Learn more about the 2017 *Magic Online* Championship Series and how to qualify for future Playoff Events [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20171107000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2017-magic-online-championship-series).

**Modern PTQ this Sunday, November 12**

Attention all Pro Tour hopefuls: A Pro Tour Qualifier (PTQ) is approaching! There is no need to qualify through a Preliminary– simply join this event with 30 Event Tickets or 300 Play Points. We hope to see you in the PTQ Finals on Sunday, November 12 at 9 a.m. PT.

Learn more about *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20171107000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers).

**No downtime tomorrow, November 8**
