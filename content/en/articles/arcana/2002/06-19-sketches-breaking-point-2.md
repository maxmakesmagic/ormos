This week's Sketches looks at the *Judgment* rare Breaking Point from Matthew Wilson, the artist just featured in [Behind the Canvas](/en/articles/behind-the-canvas/2002/06-14-behind-the-canvas-matt-wilson).

### 1\. Art Description

These are the instructions given to the artist:

"This is a red card and should show Kamahl slamming his sword, with the Mirari attached to the pommel, into the ground. This causes the ground to erupt and sends lava streaming towards a battlefield. See reference for Kamahl."

### 2\. Sketch

This is the preliminary sketch submitted by Matt. He was kind enough to label the lava for ease of viewing.
