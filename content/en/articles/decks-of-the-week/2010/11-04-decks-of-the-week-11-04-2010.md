How's this for a full menu of **Magic** decks? Let's start with *Scars of Mirrodin* Booster Draft at Grand Prix–Bochum and the current Pro Tour qualifier season as an appetizer. Then dig in to the new Standard metagame with 35 *Scars of Mirrodin* Game Day Top 8s plus a slew of **Magic Online** events featuring the new set. If you still have room for dessert, treat yourself to the rest of the formats that **Magic Online** brings to Decks of the Week every week. Dig in!
