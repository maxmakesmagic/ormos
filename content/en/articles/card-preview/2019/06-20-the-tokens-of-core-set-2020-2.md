The tokens and emblems you'll find inside *Core Set 2020* look a little different from tokens of the past—they're all full art!

If you haven't seen the cards that create these ten tokens (and two emblems), head over to the [Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2019/06-25-core-set-2020) and check them out for yourself.

Now, without further ado, the full-art tokens (and emblems) of *Core Set 2020*!
