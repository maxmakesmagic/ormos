## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/74/59/745950c3309212f13752e4f5d44bae4d0f34be80eff3df6d946302fde0a3b904.png)*Conspiracy* features 210 black-bordered cards, including randomly inserted premium versions of all cards in the set. There are sixty-five new cards in Conspiracy. Fifty-two of the new cards will be legal in Legacy and Vintage; thirteen cards will not.

**Set Name:** *Conspiracy*  
**Number of Cards:** 210

**Twitter Hashtag:** [#MTGCNS](https://twitter.com/#!/search/realtime/%23MTGCNS) 

**Languages:** English, Japanese, Chinese Simplified

**Official Three-Letter Code:** CNS

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Shawn Main (lead), Dan Helland, David Humpherys, Kenneth Nagle, and Matt Tabak

**Final Game Design and Development:** David Humpherys (lead), Dan Emmons, K. Joseph Huber, Sam Stoddard, and Gavin Verhey, with contributions from Matt Tabak
