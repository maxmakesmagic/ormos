The *Magic* *Story* Podcast goes behind the scenes with the people who make the worlds, create the characters, and infuse this game with imagination. This week, in a special episode of the *Magic* Story Podcast, Alison Luhrs and Kelly Digges, two important members of the creative team, will be answering a slew of fan-submitted questions.

Next week we'll have a special *Magic Story* episode featuring a familiar character, and then the *Hour of Devastation* stories start up the following week!

In the meantime, I do hope you enjoy Alison and Kelly answering questions on the mending, Tamiyo's kids, and what's made Nissa so blue.
