Last week we held a live Q&A about the new **Magic Online** client via our [Twitch.tv](http://www.twitch.tv/magicprotour) channel. On the panel were:

 

*   Worth Wollpert - Executive Producer for **Magic Online** and **Duels of the Planeswalkers**
*   Chris Kiritz - Business Manager for **Magic Online**
*   Ryan Spain - R&D Digital Designer
*   Sean Gibbons - Community Coordinator for **Magic Online**

They showed the client, highlighting some of the newest changes and talking about where the client is going in the future. Check it out below!
