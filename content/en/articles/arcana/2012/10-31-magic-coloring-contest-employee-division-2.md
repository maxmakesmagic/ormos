It's time to revisit the [**Magic** Coloring Book](/en/articles/arcana/2012/10-18-the-magic-the-gathering-coloring-book)! After the [coloring contest at PAX](/en/articles/arcana/2012/10-05-coloring-contest-winners-2) was such a success, we decided to have one internally here at Wizards of the Coast!

  

There were three categories: Best Jace, Best Nicol Bolas, and Most Creative. Because there are a number of people here with actual artistic talent, I tried for the "Most Creative" prize. See what you think:

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/1093_monty.jpg) 

  

Instead of using crayons, I scanned in the sheet and then did some nonsense with Photoshop. No, I did not win. But here's what did!
