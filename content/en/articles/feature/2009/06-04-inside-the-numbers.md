Yes! [Karrthus, Tyrant of Jund](https://web.archive.org/web/20090604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karrthus%2C+Tyrant+of+Jund)!

As you can see from the Top Tens, the most popular land was [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20090604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest) and the most popular *nonbasic* land was [Savage Lands](https://web.archive.org/web/20090604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Lands). The most popular creature, as you might have heard [from Mike Flores](https://web.archive.org/web/20090604000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/td/41), was [Bloodbraid Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20090604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodbraid+Elf).
