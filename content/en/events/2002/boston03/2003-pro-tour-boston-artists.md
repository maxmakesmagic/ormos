![John Avon](http://www.wizards.comhttp//www.wizards.com/protour/images/Artist_JohnAvon.jpg)John Avon was born in Cardiff \[Wales\]-UK, in 1961 and studied art at Brighton. His style was always realistic, which meant he never fit in with anything Fashionable -- his Cliff Richard haircut stayed the same, as did the record collection.

John's first paid work were brochures for local advertising. He then managed to start painting book covers by the ton, almost all them in the Horror/Crime/SI-FI markets and was on a roll, until the quantity, took a toll on his dwindling sanity. The painstaking realism and craft seemed very much at odds with the pace of the commercial world. John worked on covers for Steven King's *The Dark Tower* vol 2 & 3, Arthur C. Clarke's *Hammer of God*, and Terry Pratchett's *Johnny and the Dead* amongst others, but it was only till he got an Apple Mac did a variation on a theme appear. As soon as he was computerised his whole orientation towards the fun of image making returned, and could experiment again, taking on a healthy variety of commissions from greetings cards to CD covers and corporate advertising to packaging. Also the world of illustrating for publishing had changed, with many covers now been produced 'in house'.

He got into the world of **Magic: The Gathering** on the *Mirage* expansion and has worked on many sets since. **Magic** is the perfect foil to the computer work-- it largely has to be hand-painted, which means 3 or 4 times a year the brushes and paint come out again. The only problem is that there is no un-do button! He finds the subjects very rewarding and is always keen to paint images where atmosphere and lighting are the prime movers.

John is currently working on many up-coming MTG expansions amongst other commissions, and lives in Sussex, England, with his illustrator wife and two sparky little sons.
