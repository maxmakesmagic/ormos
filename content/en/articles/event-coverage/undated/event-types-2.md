### MagicFest

MagicFests are the ultimate *Magic* experience. These three-day events, held all around the globe, feature a wide array of side events, card dealers, artists, and a two-day main event with thousands of dollars on the line. It’s a festival of *Magic* unlike anything else.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20200625214656/https://magic.wizards.com/content/eventsschedulegrand-prix)

![](https://magic.wizards.com/)

### Mythic Championship Qualifiers

Earn a seat at a Mythic Championship by winning Mythic Championship Qualifier tournaments. There are more avenues than ever to play your way to the top in both tabletop and digital *Magic*.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20200625214656/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/esports/mythicchampionshipqualifiers)

![](https://magic.wizards.com/)

### Mythic Championship

Compete against the *Magic Pro League* and other Challengers from around the globe for your share of a $500,000 prize pool.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20200625214656/https://magic.wizards.com/content/eventsscheduleprotours)

![](https://magic.wizards.com/)

### Magic World Championship

The 2019 *Magic* World Championship will feature 16 of the year’s top players competing for a $1 million prize pool. The winner of will be crowned the best *Magic* player in the world!

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20200625214656/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/esports/magicworldchampionship)
