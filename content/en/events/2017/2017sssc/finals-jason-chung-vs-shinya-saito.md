The finals of the Super Sunday Series Championship represented the dynamics of this event: A place where new players, veterans, and pros collide, become friends, and compete, with a mixture of all groups oftentimes meeting the finals. In this case, Gold-level pro Jason Chung faced off against relative premier play newcomer Shin'ya Saito to determine who would win this tournament.

Both Chung and Saito were on blue-black reanimation strategies, though Chung's deck was dipped into red for [Firebolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firebolt), as well as the haste effect for his two copies of [Torrent of Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrent+of+Souls). The red gave Chung an advantage in this matchup, as Saito's reanimation plan would need to be a full two turns ahead of Chung's to properly race. [Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Void) also provided an answer to an opposing [Inkwell Leviathan](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inkwell+Leviathan), which would prove to be powerful. That said, having an advantage did not mean having a guarantee, and Saito's tight play all weekend was likely to make up for a lot.

# The Games

Chung led off with [Night's Whisper](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Night%27s+Whisper) as the first play, discarding [Nekrataal](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nekrataal) after his end step. Saito's first play, [Ticking Gnomes](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ticking+Gnomes), met an [Eyeblight's Ending](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eyeblight%27s+Ending), as Chung took 1 to fall to 17. Saito switched gears with [Jayemdae Tome](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jayemdae+Tome), to which Chung retorted with the powerful [Fact or Fiction](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fact+or+Fiction). The piles flipped an unexciting four lands with a [Twisted Abomination](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Twisted+Abomination), with the creature being placed in a pile with a Mountain as three lands sat in the other. Chung quickly took the two cards.

With Saito still struggling to do anything big, Chung began to execute his game plan, using [Mystical Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystical+Tutor) for [Entomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Entomb), and then [Entomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Entomb) to bin an [Inkwell Leviathan](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inkwell+Leviathan). This set up a [Torrent of Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrent+of+Souls) to bring the giant artifact creature back, which crunched in for 9. Saito had no way to deal with the [Leviathan](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leviathan) in time, and quickly nodded before picking up his cards.

"All that cube drafting paid off," Chung said.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/2017sssc/f_chung.jpg)  
*Jason Chung* 

Saito, after some deliberation and sideboard, opted to draw first for the second game. He opened the game with [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress), taking [Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Void) and leaving [Gravedigger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravedigger), [Eyeblight's Ending](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eyeblight%27s+Ending), [Torrent of Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrent+of+Souls), a Mountain, and a Swamp. [Prismatic Lens](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prismatic+Lens) was next, providing some acceleration or—in the case of Saito's third turn—some fixing to cast [Hymn to Tourach](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hymn+to+Tourach). The discard spell robbed Chung of [Eyeblight's Ending](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eyeblight%27s+Ending) and a Swamp.

[Night's Whisper](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Night%27s+Whisper) began to refill Saito's hand, and was followed up by [Carrion Feeder](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Carrion+Feeder). Chung, who found an Island after missing a color and a land drop on the fourth turn, let him get on to the board with [Gravedigger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravedigger). That proved to stop nothing, as [Sengir Autocrat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sengir+Autocrat) produced enough tokens to ensure an unimpeded swing from the zombie creature.

[Peregrine Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peregrine+Drake) gave Chung another blocker for any non-[Carrion Feeder](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Carrion+Feeder) creatures, but the zombie had eaten the [Sengir Autocrat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sengir+Autocrat)'s army, including the autocrat itself, and Chung found himself at 13 after playing his land—a [Swiftwater Cliffs](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swiftwater+Cliffs)—for the turn.

[Animate Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Animate+Dead) brought the [Sengir Autocrat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sengir+Autocrat) back for Saito, which gave him more [Carrion Feeder](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Carrion+Feeder) fodder. [Tragic Slip](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tragic+Slip) cleared away one blocker, and after one turn of chump-blocking the 1-mana zombie, Chung picked up his cards in the face of a massive 1-mana titan.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/2017sssc/f_saito.jpg)  
*Shin'ya Saito* 

Chung chose to draw for the third and final game, and he showcased an excellent start, with [Night's Whisper](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Night%27s+Whisper) to dig deeper, discarding [Wonder](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wonder) after his end step. Saito was slow to get on the board, with [Wakedancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wakedancer) being his first form of pressure, followed up with [Wake of Vultures](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wake+of+Vultures). [Gravedigger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravedigger) brought back Chung's discarded [Wonder](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wonder), then traded with the [Wakedancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wakedancer) on Saito's next attack.

While Saito had a good follow-up in a second [Wakedancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wakedancer), this time with morbid active, Chung went for an upkeep [Mystical Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystical+Tutor), finding [Toxic Deluge](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Toxic+Deluge) for 3 life to clear Saito's army away.

Saito, as a follow-up, had nothing, and a [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) from Chung let him see why as his opponent fanned out [Inkwell Leviathan](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inkwell+Leviathan), [Sengir Autocrat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sengir+Autocrat), and a land. With no defense lying in wait, Chung deployed [Wonder](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wonder) to begin attacking, as while the hand wasn't capable of anything at that point, it would be if Saito drew more lands.

With Saito needing to deploy something, [Sengir Autocrat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sengir+Autocrat) finally hit the table. Chung, after thinking for a moment, decided to [Entomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Entomb), putting [Duplicant](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duplicant) into the graveyard. [Torrent of Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrent+of+Souls) for [Gravedigger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravedigger) returned the artifact creature to Chung's hand, and [Eyeblight's Ending](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eyeblight%27s+Ending) got rid of the Autocrat and its serfs.

Of course, while this has been going on, Saito's draw steps—lands—weren't worthless. He eventually reached the mana necessary to simply cast the 7/11 out of his hand. Chung had an answer in his hand with [Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Void)... but unfortunately, lacked the red mana to cast it. [Hymn to Tourach](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hymn+to+Tourach) from Saito hit both that as well as [Torrent of Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20170108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrent+of+Souls), and the sigh from Chung signaled the end approaching.

With one more attack, Chung offered the handshake, giving Saito the match, the championship title, and the $6,000 prize that goes with it.
