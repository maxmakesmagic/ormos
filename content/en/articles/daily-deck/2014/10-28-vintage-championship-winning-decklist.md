Congratulations to Mark Tocco on winning the 2014 Vintage Championship!

For his victory, Mark receives a unique, framed, oversized version of [Mox Pearl](https://web.archive.org/web/20141028000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Pearl) with a new piece of art by [Raoul Vitale](https://web.archive.org/web/20141028000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5b%22Raoul%20Vitale%22%5d).
