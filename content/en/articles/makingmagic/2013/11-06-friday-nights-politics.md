The Alex Initiative seeks support as the Loading Ready Run crew do battle with Commander. Alex wields [Purphoros, God of the Forge](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Purphoros%2C+God+of+the+Forge); Kathleen battles with [Oona, Queen of the Fae](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oona%2C+Queen+of+the+Fae); Jeremy uses [Diaochan, Artful Beauty](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Diaochan%2C+Artful+Beauty); while both Graham and Paul use the new *Commander* (2013 Edition) [Derevi, Empyrial Tactician](https://web.archive.org/web/20131106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Derevi%2C+Empyrial+Tactician).
