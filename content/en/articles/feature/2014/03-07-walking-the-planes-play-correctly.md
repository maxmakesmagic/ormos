Before you make a play, how much do you think about it? How do you know it's the right move? **Magic** is a deeply strategic game, and playing correctly is harder than you think. It might even be impossible. How much does playing correctly matter to you? Join Walking the Planes at [Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*](/en/events/2014/ptbng14/mclaren-weaves-victory-in-valencia).

Follow Walking the Planes on Twitter! - [@WalkThePlanes](http://www.twitter.com/walktheplanes)

Nathan Holt and Shawn Kornhauser are "hardcore casual" **Magic** players exploring the rich tapestry of the ever-expanding Multiverse. As a team, they've co-produced several experimental independent films, and Walking the Planes is their biggest, baddest effort to date.
