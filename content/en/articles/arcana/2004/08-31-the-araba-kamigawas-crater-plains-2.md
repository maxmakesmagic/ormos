In the *[Champions of Kamigawa](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603191203/http://www.wizards.com/magic/displayexpansion.asp?set=chk&page=1)* storyline, the Kami War has already devastated the plains surrounding Eiganjo Castle. Attacks by the kami have turned the fields surrounding the castle into a battered, pockmarked plain -- not unlike the surface of the moon -- called the Araba.

Here's a great look at the blasted Araba in Greg Staples' art of one of the basic Plains from *Champions of Kamigawa*:

![Plains illustration by Greg Staples](https://i.ormos.uk/55/67/5567f654dd35e0a9123fb6932b58ab1e7b6cc6aec6f96c74be5d965909e5abd5.jpg)  
 *Plains illustration by Greg Staples* 

You can also see hints of the crater plains in art you've already seen on this site. Take a look at the lower left-hand corner of **Yosei, the Morning Star**, the 5/5 dragon kami previewed in [Mark Rosewater's column yesterday](/en/articles/makingmagic/2004/08-30-now-with-added-flavor): 

![Yosei, the Morning Star illustration by Hiro Izawa](https://i.ormos.uk/77/61/7761eeae7098728da7d68655a5b9a1f32d2a4183b1684dc89399ad4874b7a6e3.jpg)  
 *Yosei, the Morning Star illustration by Hiro Izawa*
