Done? Good. *Now* let's look at promo cards!

The *Innistrad* Prerelease Events will be September 24-25, 2011. The full information [is here](/en/events/2008/prerelease-facts/worldwide-prerelease-tournaments), and the foil promo card is right here:

You'll get that card just for participating (while supplies last)! Just like the other one!
