Josh Utter-Leyton is a member of a team that includes the likes of Luis Scott-Vargas, a Pro Tour champion. So when he made the Top 8 of Pro Tour–San Juan with a Comet Storm control deck playing blue-red-green, people took note. The man they call "Wrapter" played in his first Pro Tour Top 8 on that Sunday, though using the Rise of the Eldrazi Draft format. Could his deck be a few revamps away from becoming the next big thing in Standard? Read the official coverage of Pro Tour–San Juan [here](/en/events/2010/ptsj10/paulo-celebrates-six-on-the-beach-in-san-juan).
