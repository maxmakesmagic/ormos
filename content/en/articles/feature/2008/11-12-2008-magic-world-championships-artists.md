Looking for something to do in between games at the World Championships? Take some time to meet actual **Magic** artists, have them sign your cards, and check out cool prints and other artwork! 

![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/image_legacy_migration/magic/images/tournamentcenter/2008/worlds_tedin1.jpg)

### Mark Tedin

Mark Tedin has worked in the game industry for fifteen years on everything from **Magic: The Gathering** to roleplaying games. He’s illustrated such famous cards as [Necropotence](https://web.archive.org/web/20081112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Necropotence), [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20081112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder), and [Forgotten Ancient](https://web.archive.org/web/20081112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forgotten+Ancient). 

*   [Complete list of Mark Tedin's Magic card artwork](https://web.archive.org/web/20081112000000/http://beta.gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Spoiler.aspx?method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+tedin)

  
![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/image_legacy_migration/magic/images/tournamentcenter/2008/worlds_pollack1.jpg)

### Alan Pollack

Alan Pollack hails from New Jersey and got his start with *Dungeon* magazine. After deciding to make fantasy illustration his life’s work, he has gone on to illustrate numerous TCGs, RPGs, book covers, and more. His famous **Magic** cards include [Story Circle](https://web.archive.org/web/20081112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Story+Circle), [Plague Wind](https://web.archive.org/web/20081112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plague+Wind), and [Recoil](https://web.archive.org/web/20081112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Recoil).

*   [Complete list of Alan Pollack's **Magic** card artwork](https://web.archive.org/web/20081112000000/http://beta.gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Spoiler.aspx?method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+pollack)

  
![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/image_legacy_migration/magic/images/tournamentcenter/2008/worlds_cavotta1.jpg)

### Matt Cavotta

Matt Cavotta is best known to players as the man behind [Anavolver](https://web.archive.org/web/20081112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anavolver), [Questing Phelddagrif](https://web.archive.org/web/20081112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Questing+Phelddagrif), [Noble Panther](https://web.archive.org/web/20081112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noble+Panther), and [Iridescent Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20081112000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Iridescent+Angel). As you can tell from most of his work, Matt has a unique style that strays a bit from conventional fantasy art.

*   [Complete list of Matt Cavotta's **Magic** card artwork](https://web.archive.org/web/20081112000000/http://beta.gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Spoiler.aspx?method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+cavotta)
