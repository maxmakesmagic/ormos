As you know, we like to print tokens that match up to the card that creates them. It just makes us feel all warm and cozy inside. For example, [Summoner's Bane](https://web.archive.org/web/20100120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoner%27s+Bane) and the Illusion Token from *Zendikar*:
