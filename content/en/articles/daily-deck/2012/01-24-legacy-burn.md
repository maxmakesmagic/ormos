In a format full of Brainstorms and Dark Rituals, a deck sporting seventeen Mountains is not something you prepare for. Austin Yost took advantage of this fact, rising to the top of last weekend's StarCityGames.com Open Series by systematically burning out each of his opponents with an array of cheap red spells! If you've been looking for a powerful, budget entry into Legacy, this might be just what you're looking for!

Austin Yost's Burn 

Legacy – StarCityGames.com Open Series Washington DC
