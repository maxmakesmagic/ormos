Last week, the *Mirrodin Besieged* [Visual Spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20110510072723/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/mirrodinbesieged/spoiler) (which is constantly being updated, incidentally) added a card called Into the Core. Dig it:

![Into the Core](https://i.ormos.uk/49/eb/49ebcda6d109b53b2566709eb93761bced2a62764fdd13d3455df3a015cc6c54.jpg "Into the Core") 

  

What's happening in this card is that the Phyrexians have created a gigantic hole in Mirrodin's outer crust, causing whatever's on the surface to plummet into the fiery furnace beneath.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9c/d0/9cd0783cb9fcae1585f4c21d3bfae3e199670010ffe847c9bd8a63bec58b84a5.jpg) 

Into the Core | Art by [Whit Brachna](https://web.archive.org/web/20110125000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22whit+brachna%22])
