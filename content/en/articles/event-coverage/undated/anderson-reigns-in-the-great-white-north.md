![](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/76/b77635f7a4e461c8f6d33333461700bc71dfa24602be76def2fc873e5ef68414.jpg)

Marc Anderson is your 2011 Canadian National Champion! After sweeping the draft portion with a perfect 6-0 record, Anderson put up the 1-1 necessary to draw into the Top 8 in the final round. Unfortunately for him, he was paired down against veteran and former National Team member Jon Boutin, who could guarantee Top 8 with a win. They split the first two games, but with everything on the line, Anderson pulled out a win. In the Top 8, his Twin-Pod deck was the bane of his opponents, giving him quick 2-0 wins over first Paul MacKinnon and then Dan Lanthier.

In the finals he faced Noah Long, who had Top 8'd last year's Nationals. Long was playing Valakut, and it had served him well all weekend. They started off 1-1, and then Anderson mulliganed to five, and things were looking grim. However, it proved to be a golden grip, giving him everything he needed to steal the third game. In the fourth, he went for a gutsy unprotected Deceiver-Twin kill, and caught Long without Dismember.

Anderson and Long will be joined by teammates Dan Lanthier and Marcel Zafra. Lanthier, the 2008 National Champion, edged out Zafra for third place in a grueling five-game series.

Congratulations to Marc Anderson and the Canadian National Team!

## Top 8 Player profiles

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b4/e1/b4e101a302f80cf3ab9655b106cae17b4790166073fb20b3a91d74237ec3a367.jpg)

**Marc Anderson**  
  

 

**Hometown (and local shop, if applicable):**  
 Toronto, Heavy Support Games.

**Notable Finishes:**  
 Hahahaha, yeah right...

**What did you play in Standard, and why?**  
 Twin-Pod, because I'm terrible with control, and aggro loses to [Timely Reinforcements](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Timely+Reinforcements).

**Did you do any tweaks on the archetype you played. If so, why? If not, why not?**  
 I started with Aaron Nicall's list, made a ton of changes, then slowly gravitated back to close to what he built.

**Having played the swiss, is there anything you would change in your standard deck?**  
 -1 Urabrask, the Hidden  
 +1 [Kazuul, Tyrant of the Cliffs](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kazuul%2C+Tyrant+of+the+Cliffs)

**Did you have any plans for m12 Limited? What's your preferred archetype? Is there anything you won't draft?**  
 I prefer aggro, obviously, but I'm open to anything. Being open is better because people don't switch out of their first color as much in M12, as there are lots of fringe playables.

**What decks did you draft and what were your records with them?**  
 Blue-Red first, 3-0, a sick deck with Chandra, Lavamancer, and [Mind Control](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind+Control)  
 White-Black second, 3-0, a pile with two [Day of Judgment](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Day+of+Judgment) and lots of filler.

**What's the worst card you played in your draft decks?**  
[Zombie Goliath](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zombie+Goliath)

**Dan Lanthier**  
  

 

**Hometown (and local shop, if applicable):**  
 Ottawa

**Notable Finishes:**  
 2008 National Champion, a PT Top 50, a GP Top 16 and a Top 32

**What did you play in Standard, and why?**  
[Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin). It's good against most of the field, and gives your opponents a lot of chances to play poorly.

**Did you do any tweaks on the archetype you played. If so, why? If not, why not?**  
 Started playing [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite)s to shore up the weak matchups (Blue-Black is playing more [Doom Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doom+Blade)s right now). [Dragonmaster Outcast](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonmaster+Outcast) in the sideboard, probably the best alternate win condition right now.

**Having played the swiss, is there anything you would change in your standard deck?**  
 I would probably cut the [Twisted Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Twisted+Image) for a [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember) or a fourth [Into the Roil](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Into+the+Roil).

**Did you have any plans for m12 Limited? What's your preferred archetype? Is there anything you won't draft?**  
 Force red and get as many 2-drops as possible. Stay open to a second color and avoid green like the plague.

**What decks did you draft and what were your records with them?**  
 Red-white (3-0)  
 Red-White (1-2)

**What's the worst card you played in your draft decks?**  
[Goblin Bangchuckers](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Bangchuckers), but I did have two [Goblin Chieftain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Chieftain)s.

## Quarterfinals - Ronald Be vs Dan Lanthier

*by Josh Bennett* 

Be had to ship his opening hand, and Lanthier did the same. Their sixes were acceptable, and the match began.

Be started by fetching a mountain. Lanthier fetched island, and began sculpting his hand with [Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain), denying both cards. Be fetched again on turn two and played [Immolating Souleater](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Immolating+Souleater). Lanthier played [Halimar Depths](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Halimar+Depths) and passed. Be hit with Souleater, pumping thrice with his mana, and then spending a further twelve life. Lanthier calmly showed him a backbreaking [Twisted Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Twisted+Image).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/06/cc/06cce2711f907f4943974c332dfd2cc5b67dd57d35c0923d09497e72152dc13f.jpg)*Dan Lanthier*

Lanthier untapped and laid [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) off a mountain. Be played his fourth land and summoned [Hero of Oxid Ridge](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero+of+Oxid+Ridge). The attack brought Lanthier down to twelve. Lanthier [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder)ed and shuffled. Next came [Halimar Depths](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Halimar+Depths). He looked at the three cards with a frown, set them, and passed the turn back to Be. Be went to attack, and Lanthier sent the Hero home with an unkicked [Into the Roil](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Into+the+Roil). He blocked with [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite). Be spent four life and a mana to get rid of it, then replayed his Hero.

Lanthier was in a pickle. He played [Arid Mesa](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arid+Mesa) for a mountain, then [Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain)ed. Be sent his squad and tapped out, trying for the kill. Lanthier played [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch), untapped an island, and played [Into the Roil](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Into+the+Roil) on the Souleater. He showed Be the [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin), and they were on to Game 2.

Lanthier 1 - Be 0

This game Be started off right, with [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide). Unfortunately for him, Lanthier had [Mental Misstep](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mental+Misstep) at the ready. He played island and [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder)ed, choosing to shuffle. Be got in for a quick four with a second Guide and [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks). Lanthier put out a [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite).

Be swung in, and after Lanthier blocked, he cast [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt). Lanthier tried to save with [Mutagenic Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutagenic+Growth), but Be had a second Bolt, killing the [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) and saving his Guide. Lanthier played out a [Shrine of Piercing Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrine+of+Piercing+Vision). Be dropped another Peaks, and though the Guide gave Lanthier an [Arid Mesa](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arid+Mesa), he was already down to eight.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c4/c0/c4c0b852aa987c30cf4c3abff441efe2439a8b9377c0515c7311e07b9ee32cfd.jpg)*Ronald Be*

Lanthier played his fourth land and passed. Be was stalled on three land, and hit again with the Guide. A kicked [Into the Roil](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Into+the+Roil) sent it packing. Be replaced it with [Chandra's Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Phoenix). Lanthier [Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm)ed it away and played a second Shrine, leaving an island untapped. Be threw down two Guides and charged in. Lanthier was down two four.

He dug with [Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain) and [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder), shuffling everything away. Be moved to attack with his Guides, and Lanthier tapped one with [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch). Be tried to [Combust](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Combust) it, but [Mutagenic Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutagenic+Growth) made the save, but left Lanthier at two. Be was happy to trade his Guide for the Exarch, and did so. Lanthier drew his next card, then conceded.

Lanthier 1 - Be 1

Lanthier kicked off with [Halimar Depths](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Halimar+Depths). Be fetched a mountain and got to stick his [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide), knocking Lantheir to eighteen. Lanthier untapped and [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder)ed, choosing not to shuffle. Be played [Teetering Peaks](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teetering+Peaks) and hit again, giving Lanthier a free land. He fetched away a dud card from the [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder), and was down to thirteen.

He untapped and [Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm)ed. Be replaced the Guide with [Kargan Dragonlord](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kargan+Dragonlord) and levelled up once. Lanthier passed on four lands and took two from the incoming Dragonlord. At end of turn, he played [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch), tapping a land and leaving Be with three mana free. He tapped four for [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin), and breathed a sigh of relief when Be tapped out for [Act of Aggression](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Act+of+Aggression). He tapped his last mana for [Dispel](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dispel), and he was on to the semifinals.

Dan Lanthier defeats Ronald Be 2-1

## Semifinals - Dan Lanthier vs Marc Anderson

*by Josh Bennett* 

"You know, you, Marcel and Noah would make a great National Team," said Anderson

"What about you?" asked Lanthier

"Me? I'm terrible."

Terrible or not, Marc Anderson is having a great weekend. His Twin-Pod deck ran over Paul MacKinnon in the quarterfinals. Now he just needed to beat Dan Lanthier, former National Champion, who had torn up the competition all weekend with his [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) deck.

Anderson led out with [Raging Ravine](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raging+Ravine) after an island from Lanthier. Lanthier paid two life for [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe), seeing two [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod), three land, [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember) and [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch). He played a mountain and [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d3/f8/d3f86848cc993baeee7365a98e07e356d3bfede129a3948105f8528ca2c6172f.jpg)*Dan Lanthier*

## Finals - Marc Anderson vs Noah Long

*by Josh Bennett* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/55/e8/55e884787d97a48900d74840dd229762f790433217cebe63be2e5e97daa4d2cf.jpg)

Noah Long offered a handshake to Marc Anderson as they sat down to play. "Congratulations on making the team!"

"Same to you."

Both these players were all smiles having locked up their place on the Canadian National team. While they shuffled up they talked about the logistics of the testing they had ahead of them for Worlds. All that was left was to play the one best-of-five match to determine which of them would be National Champion - Anderson playing Twin-Pod, Long with [Valakut](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut).

In response Anderson cast [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch), untapping his [Copperline Gorge](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Copperline+Gorge). Anderson [Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain)ed and kept both. Down came the [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin). Marc Anderson defeats Noah Long 3-1 and is the 2011 Canadian National Champion!

**[Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin)**

The combo kill of champions, literally. Marc Anderson's Twin-Pod deck was undeniable in the Top 8, giving up only a single loss. Asked about his deck choice, he joked "I wanted to play [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod), but it requires so much thinking. I decided to play the combo so I could get some easy wins." From where I was standing he had to work pretty hard for them, even with the combo. Ditto for Dan Lanthier's streamlined Blue-Red Twin Combo deck. It ends with an attack for a million, but it's a rough road to get there. One mistake and your game goes up in smoke.

 

![Phantasmal Dragon](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Phantasmal+Dragon)

**[Phantasmal Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phantasmal+Dragon)**

Opinions were split wildly about the value of this card, even among the Top 8. Some called it unplayable, others a solid first pick. Marc Anderson certainly had no problem playing a pair of them in his first draft deck, and posted a comfortable 3-0 with it. [Chandra, the Firebrand](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+the+Firebrand) and [Mind Control](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind+Control) may have helped out, too. Anderson was one of only two players to post a perfect 6-0 in limited, along with Will Bax, who finished 16th.

 

![Squadron Hawk](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Squadron+Hawk)

**[Squadron Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squadron+Hawk)**

The most numerous deck in the field for Standard Constructed was the blue-white menace, Caw-Blade. Both Marcel Zafra and Mani Davoudi rode the flying [Ancestral Recall](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Recall) to the Top 8, though they did it with competing versions. Zafra had an [Emeria Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emeria+Angel) build (which he credits to LSV) and Davoudi played a full four [Hero of Bladehold](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero+of+Bladehold) instead.

 

![Primeval Titan](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Primeval+Titan)

**[Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan)**

Noah Long showed just how strong [Valakut](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut) can be, plowing through to the finals, but it was fellow Top 8'er Jeremie Ross-Latour who really stands out as the [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan) poster child. His homebrew "Turn 4 Titan" showed that [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan) is more than just the lynchpin of [Valakut](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut) decks, and also that, yes, Titans on turn four are very good.

 

![Dismember](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Dismember)

**[Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember)**

Get used to seeing this card. Among the Top 8 decks, only Dan Lanthier's [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) deck eschewed it entirely. The exclamation point on Marc Anderson's finals victory over came courtesy of [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember), or rather, its absence. In the face of untapped mana from Noah Long's [Valakut](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120070810/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut) deck, Anderson gambled that he didn't hold the deadly instant, and it paid off with the Champion's Trophy.
