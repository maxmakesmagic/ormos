Welcome back to "On the Mirrodin Site," where we show off some of the recent highlights from the [Mirrodin Site](https://web.archive.org/web/20110228151411/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/).

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/activity/da655_image1.jpg)  

On the Mirran side, a new blog entry has appeared in the Mountains. Farris of the Anvil, a Vulshok leader, tells the tale of a clash with Phyrexian forces.

  

And over on the Phyrexian side, in the Forest, there's a new wallpaper: Vorinclex, Voice of Hunger.
