You never know what type of deck professional card player David Williams will bring with him to the tournament tables. For the 2010 World Championships in Chiba, Japan, he brought a green-blue Genesis Wave deck to battle. The deck's goal? Build up into giant Genesis Waves over and over to garner a huge permanent and card advantage over one's opponent. Dave took some time out of his busy Worlds schedule to do a video deck tech on his creation. Read more Worlds in the archive [here](/en/events/2010/worlds10/theres-no-jace-like-guillaume).
