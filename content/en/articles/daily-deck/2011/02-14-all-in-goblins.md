Christoffer Andersen is a respected American deck-builder, and his latest brew took him to the Top 8 of the recent StarCityGames.com Indianapolis Open. His deck? All-In Goblins, which uses the new battle cry mechanic from *Mirrodin Besieged* to great effect. Goblin Wardriver and Contested War Zone allowed Christoffer to put some serious hurt on his opponents with Kuldotha Rebirth. Even Memnite and Ornithopter can become sizable threats with some pumps from their battle-crying compatriots!
