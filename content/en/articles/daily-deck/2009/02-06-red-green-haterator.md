Originally created by writer Richard Feldman, Red-Green Haterator features some of the strongest "hate" cards in the format to out-metagame an expected field. Goblin Sharpshooter mows down Faeries and Elves, Iwamori of the Open Fist is a cheap 5/5 in a format devoid of legends, and Magus of the Moon punishes players relying too heavily on non-basic lands. Put it together and you have a successful strategy for **Magic Online** user newkidsontheblock who Top 8ed a recent Premier Event using the above list. But are there new hate options on the horizon? As *Conflux* joins the world of **Magic**, the world waits with bated breath.
