Includes a player’s guide, card box, eight 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, one random Pro Tour player card, a special-edition life counter, and the Eventide novel.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ea/f8/eaf804430e55cdad0791020e065bebe79c64fdbc8480b2a123faf386e020856f.png)

The fourth set in the Lorwyn-Shadowmoor block, this 180-card expansion set has randomly inserted premium cards and two new mechanics: Chroma and Retrace.

**Set Name:** *Eventide*  
**Block:** Set 4 of 4 of the *Lorwyn-Shadowmoor* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 180

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719045008/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/EN_MTGEVE%20FAQ%20070808.doc)

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Mark Rosewater (lead), Alexis Janson, Kenneth Nagle, Jake Theis, and Brian Tinsman

**Final Game Design and Development:** Matt Place (lead), Nate Heiss, Erik Lauer, Kenneth Nagle, and Mike Turian, with contributions from Mark L. Gottlieb
