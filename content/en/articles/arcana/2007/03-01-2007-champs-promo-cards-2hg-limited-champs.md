> **turf**  
> *noun*  
>  1. A layer of matted earth formed by grass and plant roots.  
>  2. The neighborhood around **Magic** players' homes over which they assert their authority.

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/9d/1d/9d1d2f01792388d01f6b14237392136e3c271e131d0f268998cd258444b609df.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20080514054502/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/states)

It's all-out war this weekend, and you and your buddy's home turf is at stake. It's [Champs](https://web.archive.org/web/20080505102450/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/champs) time (also known as [States](https://web.archive.org/web/20080514054502/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/states) in the US), and it's Two-Headed Giant Limited. Find another head and [read up](https://web.archive.org/web/20080514054502/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/states) on the details before some, um, shall we say, *lesser minds* from across town manage to claim bragging rights over *your* layer of matted earth.

As for promo cards this season, it's all Matt Cavotta, all the time. Just for showing up, you get a full-art Blood Knight, while supplies last:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/72/e9/72e91fbddf2ceb28c7cf585c8d0c7a384617bb5ece2bad2394420d4926bfd7e2.jpg)

If you make Top 4, you'll get a premium foil full-art Groundbreaker -- but we're keeping that one a secret for now. If you want to see it, you'd better find that perfect second head to win the card.

In addition, Top 4 teams get playmats (one per head), and the winning team gets a pair of victory plaques (one per head).

Two-Headed Giant Limited [States](https://web.archive.org/web/20080514054502/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/states) / [Champs](https://web.archive.org/web/20080505102450/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/champs) 2007. Heads will roll.
