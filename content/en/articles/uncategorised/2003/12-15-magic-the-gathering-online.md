***Magic** Online* is a place where all should feel welcome. As with other online communities, this means establishing a few basic rules of courtesy. All site members and guests are responsible for knowing and understanding the information contained in this Code Of Conduct. We have made the following rules as simple and straightforward as possible. All chat areas, account names, clans, profiles, store information, e-mail and all other areas of ***Magic** Online* are subject to the following rules.

**VIOLATION OF ANY OF THESE RULES IS GROUNDS FOR TERMINATION OF YOUR *MAGIC ONLINE* ACCOUNT(S). TERMINATION MEANS THAT YOU FORFEIT ALL PRODUCT CONTAINED IN YOUR ACCOUNT(S) AND ARE NOT ALLOWED TO ACCESS *MAGIC ONLINE* IN ANY WAY. SEE THE [TERMS OF SERVICE](https://web.archive.org/web/20031215000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com/help/tos.htm&origin=magic_magiconline_coc) FOR SPECIFIC DETAILS.**

1.   Do not use vulgar, discriminatory, or otherwise inappropriate language or content.  
  
2.   Do not circumvent language filters or in any way portray inappropriate language through masking, alternate spellings, or implication.  
  
3.   Do not advertise or otherwise approach people for trades outside of the designated trading areas. This includes, but is not limited to:
     *   Using account names that reference trading (TIX4CARDS, PaulsKardHaus etc.), or
     *   Using account names referring to any business or commercial venture.
     These accounts may only appear in designated trading areas.  
  
4.   Do not advertise "ante" matches or player-run tournaments or events.  
  
5.   Do not advertise any business or commercial venture.  
  
6.   Do not post any non-Wizards of the Coast/Hasbro URLs, links, or websites.  
  
7.   Do not harass, bully, threaten, or cause discomfort for other players.  
  
8.   Do not impersonate or otherwise imply that you are an Adept or other Wizards of the Coast staff member.  
  
9.   Do not create or use an account name that in any way violates the Code of Conduct including, but not limited to:
     *   Names or titles of, or references to any ***Magic** Online* staff member or position.
     *   References to offensive or notorious persons, controversial political figures, or cultural tragedies.


     **NOTE: The name you choose will be permanent and will not be changed.** Do not risk your account by selecting a controversial or marginal account name.  
  
10.  Do not disrupt the chat rooms by posting multiple messages within a short time span, spamming, prolonging off topic posts, using ALL CAPS or any other means of disruption.  
  
11.  Do not promote or engage in any illegal activity.  
  
12.  Do not scam, rip-off, or otherwise attempt to take people's accounts or product under false pretenses.
     *   Any person caught scamming will have their account(s) terminated.
     *   Any person posting or promoting a ***Magic** Online* hack, mod, virus, or password mining site will have their accounts terminated and may face legal action.
     *   Ultimately, the responsibility for all trades is yours. We are not responsible for and will not support any deals that work outside the ***Magic** Online* trading system.
  
  
13.  Do not attempt to artificially alter the outcome of a ***Magic** Online* league, sanctioned event, or game. This includes, but is not limited to:
     *   Bribing or offering any compensation in order to change the outcome.
     *   Stalling, spamming, harassing, or behaving in any other unsportsmanlike manner that affects the game.
  
  
14.  Do not behave in any manner that is inappropriate for ***Magic** Online* and the ***Magic** Online* community. Wizards of the Coast and the ***Magic** Online* community representatives are the sole judges of what is appropriate.

**Wizards of the Coast and the *Magic Online* staff reserve the right to change these rules at any time without notice.**

**Violators may not receive a warning prior to termination.**
