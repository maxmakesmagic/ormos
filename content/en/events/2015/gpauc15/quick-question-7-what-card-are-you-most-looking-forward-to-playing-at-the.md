# What card are you most looking forward to playing at the Prerelease next weekend, and where will you be playing?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ2_Hau-Shan_Huang.jpg)  
**Huang Hao-shan:** [Dragonlord Atarka](https://web.archive.org/web/20150314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Atarka), at Fight for Fun in Taichung, Taiwan. 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ2_Patty_Robertson.jpg)  
**Patty Robertson:** [Dragon Fodder](https://web.archive.org/web/20150314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragon+Fodder), but I haven’t decided which store I’ll be playing at yet. 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ2_Joseph_Sclauzero.jpg)  
**Joseph Sclauzero:** [Dragonlord Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20150314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar), at Infinity Games in Adelaide, South Australia. 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ2_Paul_Jackson.jpg)  
**Paul Jackson:** [Pacifism](https://web.archive.org/web/20150314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pacifism), at Good Games Cannington, Western Australia. 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ2_Isaac_Egan.jpg)  
**Isaac Egan:** Anything that’s good in 2-Headed Giant, and at Good Games Russell Alphey (officially known as Good Games Melbourne), in Melbourne, Victoria. 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ2_Don_van_Ravenzwaiij.jpg)  
**Don van Ravenswaaij:** I’m flying out to The Netherlands to visit family before Pro Tour Brussels, so I suspect I won’t have time to attend a Prerelease, but I will play it on *Magic Online* before the Pro Tour.
