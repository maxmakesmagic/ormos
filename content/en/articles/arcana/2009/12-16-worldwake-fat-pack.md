As 2009 winds down, we look back at what we've achieved and what we hope to achieve in the coming year. And then we show pictures of the *Worldwake* Fat Pack!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/77/25/77254310e83eefcd2a574d31d02bd4748b0d04773284d038f2e3428558fa6da5.jpg)  

Well, hello there, *Worldwake* Fat Pack! What treasures do you contain?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d0/c9/d0c9dc6d6d7d0b83ff822a6f613f136153ef1bac6595f8a7601413b4921b68db.jpg)  

Reading from left to right, there's a handsome storage box, the *Worldwake* Player's Guide (featuring a [familiar Planeswalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20091216000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/ProductArticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/worldwake/planeswalkerchase) on the cover), forty *Zendikar* basic lands (the kind without the text box), the wrapper, a sample chapter from Robert B. Wintermute's *In the Teeth of Akoum*, and eight *Worldwake* booster packs.

We'll give you a closer look at those boosters tomorrow, but right now we're more interested in the art by Eric Deschamps, featuring Jace dealing with some unusual Zendikar weather.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e5/25/e5256bcc8e0909ffec2d83ff1d37d35199a7fd0f3e0c59d4b32e4ba75f67bf7d.jpg)

*Worldwake* Fat Pack | Illustration by [Eric Deschamps](https://web.archive.org/web/20091216000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Eric+Deschamps%22%5D)

  

*(Note: The thing we originally identified as a second storage box is actually the wrapper, which can be unfolded to show the great art. We apologize for the error.)*
