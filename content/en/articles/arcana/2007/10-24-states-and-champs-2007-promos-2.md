The time: This Saturday, October 27th. The format: Standard with *Lorwyn*. The place: game stores and convention centers across the US ([States](https://web.archive.org/web/20071024000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/states)) and the world ([Champs](https://web.archive.org/web/20071024000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/champs)). Get ready to smash your Standard deck through the gauntlet and defend your home turf. To be eligible, all you need is a deck and residency in the region where you're playing.

Just for showing up, you'll get this (while supplies last): a full-art promo of *Lorwyn's* elf lord, [Imperious Perfect](https://web.archive.org/web/20071024000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Imperious+Perfect).

![Imperious Perfect](https://i.ormos.uk/ce/8a/ce8ad2e01d2947dd6c8b31a75a5df3fd96bff1d5e90dbb0eb4f14f526e02c6a5.jpg)

If you crush your way all the way to the Top 8, you'll take home a *foil* full-art promo of the *Lorwyn* treefolk legend, [Doran, the Siege Tower](https://web.archive.org/web/20071024000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doran%2C+the+Siege+Tower).

![Doran, the Siege Tower](https://i.ormos.uk/88/26/882632bd9630a5075af01e737bcb8b6e697c2587bc7ffbb0946aebfa76060c96.jpg)

Need to know more? Get the details on [States here](https://web.archive.org/web/20071024000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/states) and [Champs here](https://web.archive.org/web/20071024000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/champs). And prepare to knock some heads.
