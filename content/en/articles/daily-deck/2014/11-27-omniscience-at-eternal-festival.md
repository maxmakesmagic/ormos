There was one other player in the Eternal Festival beside [Kageyama](/en/articles/daily-deck/2014/11-13-jund-at-eternal-festival) and [Kikuchi](/en/articles/daily-deck/2014/11-12-abzan-at-eternal-festival) who posted a 23-point top finish in the Swiss: Hisayuki Hōseki, with his [Omniscience](https://web.archive.org/web/20141127000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Omniscience) deck.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Omniscience)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141127000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Omniscience)

I don't think much needs to be said about [Omniscience](https://web.archive.org/web/20141127000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Omniscience). Basically, if it hits the table, you basically win. The trick, then, is getting it out.

How does one support a ten-mana card and build in a win condition? It's truly a head-scratcher that makes you sit down and think. At least, if one is in regular *Magic* thinking mode, as I was when I first saw this card.

But in Legacy thinking mode, the answer to the first part of the question is easy: you can ignore the mana cost with [Show and Tell](https://web.archive.org/web/20141127000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Show+and+Tell). So now it's just about the win condition. Ahhh, Legacy.

Looking closer, we can see this deck is built around [Burning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20141127000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Wish) as the axis. Since the Wish is used to get [Show and Tell](https://web.archive.org/web/20141127000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Show+and+Tell), there are effectively seven copies of that card. Then, with [Omniscience](https://web.archive.org/web/20141127000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Omniscience), the Wish can be used to pull in [Enter the Infinite](https://web.archive.org/web/20141127000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enter+the+Infinite), which lets you go into super-combo mode with infinite [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20141127000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Aeons+Torn). Free Eldrazi? Sure, why not?
