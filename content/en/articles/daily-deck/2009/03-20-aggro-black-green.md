Dark Ritual. Hypnotic Specter. Sinkhole. Some of the most powerful spells in the game, and they all came together in Paul Rietzl's Black-Green Aggro deck at Grand Prix-Chicago. He competed against 1,230 other competitors, and made it all the way to the semifinals. To find out more about Paul, Grand Prix-Chicago, and the Legacy format, [visit the official web coverage](/en/events/2009/gpchi09/two-victories-in-a-row-for-gabriel-nassif).
