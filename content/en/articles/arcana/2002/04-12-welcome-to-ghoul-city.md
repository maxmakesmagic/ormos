Graveyard interactions! Discard! Madness! We must be talking about the *Odyssey* block, right? Wrong!

Below are some red and black cards from *Mirage* playtesting. Besides looking like they could have been in *Torment*, they're all quite wordy. Can you figure out what they ended up as in *Mirage*? Click each one to find out.

[Tombstone Stairwell](https://web.archive.org/web/20020412000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Tombstone%2BStairwell)  
[Hammer of Bogardan](https://web.archive.org/web/20020412000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Hammer%2Bof%2BBogardan)

 

[Delirium](https://web.archive.org/web/20020412000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Delirium)  
[Tainted Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20020412000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Tainted%2BSpecter)

 

Notice that the Hammer had "If this is countered, remove it from the game." It's a good thing that line was removed from the game!
