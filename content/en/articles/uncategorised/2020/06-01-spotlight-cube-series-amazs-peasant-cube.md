*Magic Online*'s Spotlight Cube Series returns with yet another Cube offering. Our last Cube addition to MTGO's *Ikoria* alternative play season, *Peasant Cube*, created by Amaz, starting June 3 and ending June 10, features powerful commons and uncommons for a multitude of play options. Drafting is dynamic, and gameplay is crisp and interactive, with mechanics that prioritize the new-player experience while providing enough flexibility for seasoned veterans. Check out the full card list below to see what's in store.
