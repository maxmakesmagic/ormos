The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on August 25, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by *Commander (2017 Edition)*, which releases today!

We've got one last look at *Hour of Devastation* Limited this weekend, as scores of people decend on Indianapolis with fingers crossed a [Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer) can, well, bring them glory.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [New Perspectives](/en/events/2017/competitive-standard-constructed-league-2017-08-23/competitive-standard-constructed-league)

After failing spectacularly at Pro Tour *Amonkhet*, [New Perspectives](https://web.archive.org/web/20170825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=New+Perspectives) combo pretty much fell off the radar. But as more decks become midrange focused and worry more about destroying artifacts with the rise of [God-Pharaoh's Gift](https://web.archive.org/web/20170825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=God-Pharaoh%27s+Gift) and the return of [Oketra's Monument](https://web.archive.org/web/20170825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oketra%27s+Monument), it's never been better to base your deck around an enchantment. Or in Melv.P's case, two enchantments. Unlike previous versions of this deck that killed with [Approach of the Second Sun](https://web.archive.org/web/20170825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Approach+of+the+Second+Sun), people are experimenting with other win conditions. I've seen [Vile Manifestation](https://web.archive.org/web/20170825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vile+Manifestation) plus [Fling](https://web.archive.org/web/20170825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fling), for example. Melv.P went with a single copy of [Faith of the Devoted](https://web.archive.org/web/20170825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faith+of+the+Devoted), which, all-told, requires only ten to thirteen mana (depending on if it was already in play), where [Approach of the Second Sun](https://web.archive.org/web/20170825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Approach+of+the+Second+Sun) required fourteen. It also has the advantage of simply being played for value.

The other big upgrade was [Nimble Obstructionist](https://web.archive.org/web/20170825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nimble+Obstructionist), which actually does things in Standard right now. Shutting off [Gate to the Afterlife](https://web.archive.org/web/20170825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gate+to+the+Afterlife), for example, is no small feat. Also, funnily enough, [Nimble Obstructionist](https://web.archive.org/web/20170825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nimble+Obstructionist) can be used to break up other [New Perspectives](https://web.archive.org/web/20170825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=New+Perspectives) decks if they have exactly seven cards in hand when they cycle. [Nimble Obstructionist](https://web.archive.org/web/20170825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nimble+Obstructionist) can counter the card drawing ability of cycling, since it's activated, keeping your opponent off seven cards and shutting off [New Perspectives](https://web.archive.org/web/20170825000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=New+Perspectives)' cycling discount.
