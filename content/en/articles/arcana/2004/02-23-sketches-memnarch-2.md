Welcome to another edition of [Sketches](https://web.archive.org/web/20040223000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/fullarchive&tablefilter=sketches:). This week we take an look at the creation of *Darksteel's* **[Memnarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20040223000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memnarch)** by Carl Critchlow. 

### 1\. Art Description

Here were the instructions given to Mr. Critchlow for the project:

> *"Money shot of Memnarch (p. 13 of style guide). Remember that he's an artifact creature who's slowly developing flesh. Line between metal and flesh should be very blurry."*

The task of illustrating an iconic character of a **Magic** setting presents a special challenge to an artist. Memnarch was sketched out in the *Mirrodin* Style Guide already: his look has already been defined. So the artist must put a creative spin on this vision while maintaining the prescribed look -- or, putting it another way, he must illustrate the preexisting character while still expressing his own artistry. It's a tricky balance.

Critchlow has been steadily illustrating **Magic** cards since the time of *Weatherlight* (in which he has illustrated the legends [Morinfen](https://web.archive.org/web/20040223000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morinfen) and [Gallowbraid](https://web.archive.org/web/20040223000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gallowbraid)). Recently he has been seen on *Mirrodin's* entire [Replica cycle](/en/articles/arcana/2003/11-03-mirrodin-playtest-cards-ii-2) and, in *Darksteel*, a couple of [Arcbound Overseer](https://web.archive.org/web/20040223000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Arcbound%2BOverseer) [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040223000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Arcbound%2BRavager) and the splashy [Darksteel Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20040223000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Colossus).

### 2\. Sketches

Mr. Critchlow came up with this initial sketch for [Memnarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20040223000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memnarch). 

It's interesting to note that many **Magic** artists turn over full-color sketches. Memnarch's blue influence is definitely seen in the rough sketch above. For the final art, Critchlow's attention to detail went into overdrive, and the rough edges became clean, gleaming lines.
