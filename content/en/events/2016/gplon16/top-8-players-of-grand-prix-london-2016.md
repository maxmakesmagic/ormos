After 15 rounds, with a total of 2,558 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

* * *

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gplon16/t8_griffiths.jpg)**

****Name:**** Pip Griffiths

****Age:**** 29

****Occupation:**** Psychometrician

****Hometown:**** Folkestone, Wales

****Previous Magic accomplishments:****

Former three-time national champion of Wales, by default.

****What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?****

I played red-green to an 8-1 finish. Although [Smuggler's Copter](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smuggler%27s+Copter) and [Chandra, Torch of Defiance](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Torch+of+Defiance) were great, [Bomat Courier](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bomat+Courier) was the MVP.

****What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?****

The Dwarf lord [Depala, Pilot Exemplar](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Depala%2C+Pilot+Exemplar) was very good in my red-white deck.

****What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?****

I drafted a mediocre red-white deck and put in [Giant Spectacle](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Giant+Spectacle). It did the business.

********![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gplon16/t8_hensing.jpg)********

**********Name:********** Marcus Hensing

**********Age:********** 26

**********Occupation:********** Student

**********Hometown:********** Copenhagen, Denmark

**********Previous Magic accomplishments:**********

Cashed a few Limited GPs.

**********What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**********

Green-white, 8-1 with one bye. The best card was [Aerial Responder](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aerial+Responder), followed by two [Renegade Freighter](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Renegade+Freighter)s.

Black-green, 2-1. Probably the two copies of [Hunt the Weak](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hunt+the+Weak). The deck only had one rare, [Syndicate Trafficker](https://web.archive.org/web/20161009000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syndicate+Trafficker).
