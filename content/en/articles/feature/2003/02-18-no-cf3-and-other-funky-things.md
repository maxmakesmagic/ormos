### February Update Notes

* * *

Since there are some significant changes to the system on this update, I thought I'd take a moment to go over them in detail.

### No CF3

Yep, that's right. There isn't a Casual Format 3 in this update. This time R&D came up with the name *before* we released the format. Now you'll find Tribal Wars available in your favorite **Casual Play** rooms. (Okay, you won't if your favorite rooms don't involve Constructed formats.) This is the same format that'll be used in the [Wizards of the Coast Invitational](https://web.archive.org/web/20030218000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=/default.asp?x=mtgcom/daily/rb58&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm021703). This is an Extended Constructed format with the following restrictions:

*   ***Deck Construction:*** 1/3 of the cards in a deck must share a creature type.
*   ***Banned Cards:*** Engineered Plague, Endemic Plague, Tsabo's Decree, Peer Pressure, Unnatural Selection, and Circle of Solace

And while they were at it, the team went ahead and okayed new names for CF1 and CF2. From now on, Casual Format 1 is called "Singleton", while Casual Format 2 is called "Prismatic". So, if you want to play these formats, make sure you select the new names.

### League Catch-Up Games

In any league starting after the update today, you won't have to worry about not playing all five of your league matches during a week. If you don't get all five matches in, the remaining matches roll over into the next week--including games missed because you joined after the first week. Now you can join in the fourth week of a league and still get all twenty league matches in. (Not that I'd recommend this approach unless you have a *lot* of time on your hands.)

### The New Limited Games Room

The **Draft Games** room in the **Casual Play** area has been replaced by the **Limited Games** room. Along with the name change come some significant changes.

The first change is that you'll be able to set up your own Sealed Deck tournaments. These are single-elimination events that can have up to eight players in them.

The second change is that you can now set up any of these events (Booster Draft, Rochester Draft, or Sealed Deck) as team events. Each team consists of three players, and each player plays all the members of the opposing team. In the draft events, players supply their own boosters, then draft and build their own decks. In Sealed Deck events, one player (the captain) supplies all the product for his or her team. Then each team builds their decks from a combined pool of cards. (The captain keeps all the cards after the event is over.)

### Prize Splits

Along with these casual tournament changes comes an addition to the 8-player rooms (**Draft 8-player** and **Constructed 8-player**). The top two players in any of the 8-player events can choose to split the prizes. If they do, ***Magic** Online* will split the product evenly for them.

### Trade Interface

There are a few modifications to the trading interface, as well. One having to do with viewing the cards, the second having to do with some underhandedness that goes on during trading on ***Magic** Online.*

On the **Display Settings** window, there's a new option: **Stack cards in trading window**. With this option selected, you won't see any changes on the **Trade** screen. With this option cleared, however, you'll see the entirety of every card in the **You Give** and **You Get** windows. To avoid taking up too much real estate, duplicate cards will "stacked" on top of each other, with a number showing how many of that card is in the window.

The second change is a bit more significant. From now on, every trade will require two confirmations. So, as normally happens, each person selects **Confirmed** when they're done adding cards to the trade. Then a confirmation window pops up with a summary of the trade. Each person must click OK for the trade to go through--if a person clicks cancel, both people are taken back to the **Trade** window. Also, if there are *any* changes to the cards involved in the trade (including cards being switched from tradeable to non-tradeable status) the trade is cancelled automatically.

### And One Other Thing...

Last, but not least, *LegionsTM* cards are now visible on the **Collection** screen. For those of you who haven't done this yet (I'm sure there's one or two who haven't), click the **Collection** tab, select **Legions** from the **Sets** drop-down box, then set **Number owned** to **\>=** and **0**. Voila! Instant spoiler list.
