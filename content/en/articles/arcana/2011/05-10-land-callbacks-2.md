As you might remember from last weekend's *New Phyrexia* Prerelease (or from [this Arcana](/en/articles/arcana/2011/04-18-new-phyrexia-basic-lands)), *New Phyrexia* has basic lands in it. What you might *not* have noticed is that half of them depict the scene inside the Phyrexianized core of Mirrodin, while half of them contain references to lands from *Mirrodin*. Observe!
