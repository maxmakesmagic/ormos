Martina Pilcerova finished the Master Degree at the Academy of Fine Arts and Design in Bratislava, Slovak Republic. She has won various awards including the "Jack Gaughan Award" for the Best Emerging Artist in 2003 (Boston, USA). Her paintings were published on more than 70 covers of books, magazines and games and on many collectible cards all around the world, including publishers like: Paper Tiger, Berkley Books, FoxAcre Press, Prime Books, BeWrite Books (UK), Argument Verlag (Germany), magazines: SF Chronicle, Talebones, Asimov's, games: Magic the Gathering, Dungeons & Dragons, A Game of Thrones, Tales of Gaea. She worked on two movie projects: When the Music's Over, Red Beat Pictures (Berlin) and the next project of Volker Engel and Marc Weigert (the creators of special effects on Independence Day) of Uncharted Territory (Los Angeles).

Official web site: http://www.martina.sk
