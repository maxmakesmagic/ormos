While Vintage remains the spotlight on *Magic Online*, Standard is the format *du jour* right now for Grand Prix, and Modern decks are getting a workout in this season's Pro Tour Qualifiers (PTQs). Speaking of Standard Grand Prix, hometown here Igor Gorbunov became the [Grand Prix Moscow](https://web.archive.org/web/20140620000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/grand-prix-moscow-2014-coverage-events) champion last weekend, while Chicago plays host this weekend. Congratulations Igor! Find a Pro Tour Qualifier near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#huey). Good luck; have fun!
