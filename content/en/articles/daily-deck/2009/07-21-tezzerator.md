Looking for something outside the box to give a spin in the world of *Shards of Alara* Block Constructed? Goliat2 managed a Premier Event Top 8 with a take on Tezzerator. Tezzeret the Seeker is the engine that turns solid cards like Wildfield Borderpost and Scepter of Dominance into an attacking force that can quickly put opponents under. Use Vedalken Outlander and Bant Charm to hold the fort until it's time to go ultimate.
