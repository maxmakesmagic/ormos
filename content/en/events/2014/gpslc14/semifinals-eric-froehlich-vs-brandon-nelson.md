Both players began the semifinals period resolving other spells on the stack: The logistics of flights and hotels in the face of Grand Prix success. Earning his second Grand Prix Top 8, Brandon Nelson had already dispatched one professional *Magic* veteran, Jamie Parke, in his quarterfinal match. Taking aim with his powerful [Spectra Ward](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectra+Ward) and burn deck, he was confident facing down another longtime player. 

17th-ranked Eric Froehlich had overcome his quarterfinal opponent through similar means. Also packing the powerful Pilgrim-Ward duo, Froehlich instead added blue to his impressive arsenal. With [Illusory Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Illusory+Angel), [Quickling](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quickling), and a few bounce spells, what Froehlich lacked in reach he made up for in evasion and tricks. The three copies of [Sanctified Charge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sanctified+Charge) alongside it all didn't hurt either. 

**The Games** 

Froehlich started the match attacking, using [Frost Lynx](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frost+Lynx) and [Kinsbaile Skirmisher](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kinsbaile+Skirmisher) to slip past Nelson's [Midnight Guard](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Midnight+Guard). While Froehlich jumped ahead in the life race, Nelson's Heliod's Pilgrim pulled the powerful [Spectra Ward](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectra+Ward) out of his deck. 

However, Nelson was missing both Mountains and a fifth land, stranding his powerful red cards and leaving room for Froehlich to run unimpeded. Attacking aggressively, Froehlich used one [Sanctified Charge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sanctified+Charge) to win a round of combat, then another to demolish what Nelson had left. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpslc14/nelson_sf.jpg)

 *Brandon Nelson fell in the first game, forcing him to aim at winning two back-to-back against his own combo.* 

  

The rising star decided the sun had set on that game

**Froehlich 1 – Nelson 0** 

Dueling [Soulmender](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soulmender)s faced off in the second game, though Nelson's third turn Heliod's Pilgrim instead grabbed [Inferno Fist](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inferno+Fist) over [Spectra Ward](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectra+Ward): Nelson already had it in hand, something Froehlich likely deduced. Froehlich cast his own Pilgrim and revealed to Nelson that he, too, had the indomitable rare Aura in his deck. 

The race to five mana was off.

Nelson used his [Inferno Fist](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inferno+Fist) to keep Froehlich off of the opportunity to play [Spectra Ward](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectra+Ward), at least until he hit his fifth mana to cast it himself. Froehlich paused before allowing it to resolve, and Nelson wasted to time clocking in with the nigh invincible Pilgrim. 

Froehlich flashed [Quickling](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quickling) in, gamely moving to race by adding [Amphin Pathmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amphin+Pathmage) to his team. Nelson's [Triplicate Spirits](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triplicate+Spirits) complicated things for the longtime pro, forcing Froehlich to settle for [Spectra Ward](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectra+Ward) on the Pathmage as his only attack. 

Nelson moved all in on his next attack, sacrificing [Selfless Cathar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Selfless+Cathar) to defeat all of Froehlich blockers. Too far behind to outrace and without an answer in sight, Froehlich fell. 

**Froehlich 1 – Nelson 1** 

Froehlich began the third game as Nelson typically had so far, with Heliod's Pilgrim for [Spectra Ward](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectra+Ward). Nelson, meanwhile, moved slower with Ajani's Pridemate – sans life gain – and [Midnight Guard](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Midnight+Guard). [Triplicate Spirits](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triplicate+Spirits) was one way for Nelson to apply pressure, which he followed the next turn with [Spectra Ward](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectra+Ward) on [Midnight Guard](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Midnight+Guard). 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpslc14/froehlich_sf.jpg)

 *With a hand flourish and wave, 17th-ranked Eric Froehlich found an answer to [Spectra Ward](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectra+Ward). His missing Plains was never found, however.* 

  

Froehlich was ready with [Peel from Reality](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peel+from+Reality) though, putting Nelson's Ward from the stack into the graveyard and forcing him into racing through the air. Missing the second Plains to cast his own [Spectra Ward](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectra+Ward), Froehlich gamely kept a steady stream of creatures coming, including [Frost Lynx](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frost+Lynx), to diminish Nelson's attacks. 

Reloaded with [Midnight Guard](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Midnight+Guard) and [Borderland Marauder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Borderland+Marauder), Nelson was able to untap into his army when Froehlich ran out of plays. Using mana kept one of Nelson's creatures out of combat, Froehlich found a little more time with [Quickling](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quickling) to rebuy [Frost Lynx](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frost+Lynx). 

However, Nelson's [Resolute Archangel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Resolute+Archangel) undid Froehlich's previous work, and buffed Ajani's Pridemate in the process. Still without a second source of white mana, Nelson began to pull away. [Divine Favor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divine+Favor) fattened up [Borderland Marauder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Borderland+Marauder), giving Pridemate another counter and unleashing a large attack. With five mana up and [Sanctified Charge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sanctified+Charge) ready, Froehlich's blocks resulted in more than enough damage for Nelson to move on to the finals. 

**Brandon Nelson defeated Eric Froehlich, 2-1.**
