At last weekend's StarCityGames.com Open Series, one unique deck stood out amid a sea of white-blue decks: Michael Rooks's metalcraft deck! Rooks cruised all the way to second with his off-the-radar concoction, defeating several white-blue decks in his path. If you've been looking for a new deck to play in Standard, give this a try!

Michael Rooks's Red-Black Metalcraft 

Standard – StarCityGames.com Open Series Washington DC
