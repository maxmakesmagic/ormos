We have some news! Our new [Learn to Play](https://web.archive.org/web/20100315000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/NewtoMagic.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/newtomagic/learntoplay) application is now online! So if you've got someone who wants to know how to play **Magic**, send 'em [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20100315000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/NewtoMagic.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/newtomagic/learntoplay).

And in addition to the interactive application, we've also got the "Planeswalker Primer" videos to help the process along. And here they are!
