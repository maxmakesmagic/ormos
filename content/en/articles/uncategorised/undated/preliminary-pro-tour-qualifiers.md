Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifiers (PPTQs) are the first step for new players on their path to the *Magic: The Gathering* Pro Tour. By winning a Preliminary PTQ, you will qualify for that season’s [Regional Pro Tour Qualifier (RPTQ)](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624033721/http://magic.wizards.com/en/protour/regionalqualifiers).

Over 3,000 Wizards Play Network locations worldwide have the opportunity to host one Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifier each season. With so many stores running these events each season, finding one near you will be easy!

[PRELIMINARY PTQS FOR PRO TOUR KALADESH](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/preliminary-pro-tour-qualifiers-pptqs-for-pro-tour-honolulu)

## WHAT IS THE PRO TOUR?

The *Magic: The Gathering* Pro Tour is a tournament series where legends are born. With four annual worldwide stops, the *Magic* Pro Tour is the premier circuit to play competitive *Magic*. Each event showcases a prize pool of $250,000.

Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifiers are your first step on your Path to the Pro Tour.

## FORMATS

Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifier formats vary from store to store between the following Constructed and Limited formats:

*   [Standard](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624033721/http://magic.wizards.com/content/gameinfoformatsstandard)
*   [Modern](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624033721/http://magic.wizards.com/content/gameinfoformatsmodern)
*   [Sealed Deck](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624033721/http://magic.wizards.com/content/gameinfoformatssealeddeck) and [Booster Draft](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624033721/http://magic.wizards.com/content/gameinfoformatsboosterdraft) featuring the latest *Magic* sets

To find out which format a store is running for its PPTQ, contact them.

[FIND A STORE NEAR YOU](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624033721/http://locator.wizards.com/)

## PRIZES

The winner of a Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifier will receive an invitation to compete in the [Regional Pro Tour Qualifier](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624033721/http://magic.wizards.com/en/protour/regionalqualifiers) of that season.

Additional prizes may also be awarded to top finishes at your PPTQ. Check with your PPTQ store for more information.

## RULES

Tournaments are run at Competitive REL ([Rules Enforcement Level](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624033721/http://wpn.wizards.com/en/document/magic-infraction-procedure-guide)).

## READY TO PLAY?

Check out our [Store Locator](https://web.archive.org/web/20160624033721/http://locator.wizards.com/) to find a store near you that’s hosting a Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifier. Have fun, and good luck!
