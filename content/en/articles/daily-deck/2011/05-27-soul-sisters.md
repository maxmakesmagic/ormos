Soul Sisters, so named because of the tandem effects of Soul Warden and Soul's Attendant, has reared up in the world of Standard before. Now that the deck also has Suture Priest, brewmeister Conley Woods has taken a stab at resurrecting the archetype. Here's his list from the StarCityGames.com Louisville Open, which you can read more about in StarCity's [coverage](http://www.starcitygames.com/php/news/expandnews.php?Article=21896).
