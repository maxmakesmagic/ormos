### 

**From Issue #46:**

![Magic The Puzzling](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/MagicThePuzzLOGO.jpg)1) Pass your turn without doing anything at all. 

2) Kevin begins the last turn of the game and draws Aphetto Vulture. 

3) During Kevin's beginning of combat step, tap a swamp for B and activate Walking Desecration's ability. Force Beasts to attack. 

4) Kevin attacks with Thrashing Mudspawn, Tephraderm, and Battering Craghorn. It's irrelevant whether or not he attacks with his other creatures. (In fact, if he does, it just makes it easier for you to win.) 

5) Block the Mudspawn with your Glarecaster and the Craghorn with your Butcher Orgg. 

6) Tap a Plains and two Mountains for mana and play Inspirit targeting the Mudspawn. It untaps and gets +2/+4, becoming a 6/8 creature. 

7) First strike damage resolves. The Orgg takes 3 damage and the Mudspawn takes 4 damage. Thanks to Inspirit, the Mudspawn lives. The Mudspawn's ability triggers and Kevin loses 4 life. He's at 16. 

8) Regular damage is assigned. Assign all of the Butcher Orgg's damage to the defending player: you. 

9) While regular combat damage is on the stack, tap the rest of your lands for mana and activate Glarecaster's ability targeting the Mudspawn. 

10) When combat damage resolves, the 6 damage the Mudspawn would deal to the Glarecaster, the 4 damage the Tephraderm would deal to you, and the 6 damage the Orgg would deal to you is all redirected to the Mudspawn. (If Kevin attacked with his Clerics, even more damage would be redirected to it.) The Mudspawn takes 16 damage. Its ability triggers and Kevin loses 16 life. (You have to filter the damage through the Mudspawn; if you target Kevin directly, he could use his Beacon of Destiny to avoid taking the damage.) Kevin goes to 0, and you win!
