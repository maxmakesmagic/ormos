The Hall of Fame class of 2011 has been announced, and one of its inductees is New York star Steven O'Mahoney-Schwartz. Steve was one of the game's first international stars, traveling around the globe exclusively to play in professional **Magic** tournaments. His big breakthrough? Winning Pro Tour Los Angeles 1999 with this powerful mono-black draft deck. The format at that event was *Urza's Saga* Limited, and Steve knew Pestilence, Corrupt, and Befoul were powerful Limited spells that could propel him to the title. Read the [announcement](/en/articles/theweekthatwas/2011/08-19-introducing-the-2011-hall-of-fame-class) for more on this year's Hall of Fame class.
