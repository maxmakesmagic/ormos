Riddle time: 

This goblin (pictured on Goblin Grenade from *Fallen Empires*) has named his spear. Can you tell from the art what his spear's name is? 

![Goblin Grenade art by Ron Spencer](https://i.ormos.uk/ae/13/ae1351aebeac4439299a5adf34ad73068b126a85b3837b83d00f45c5d9b4e6e0.jpg)  
 *Goblin Grenade art by Ron Spencer*

Yep, it's Mike, as you can see spelled out on the spikes. Fine name for a spear, don't you think? (It's actually the name of a buddy of the artist, Ron Spencer. Ron is fond of hiding names in his art.)
