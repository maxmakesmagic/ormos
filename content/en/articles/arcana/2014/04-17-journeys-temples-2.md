Trick asked me to write today's Arcana and I knew exactly what I wanted to do. [I've established before](https://web.archive.org/web/20140417000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/1432) that I'm a big fan of the [Temples](https://web.archive.org/web/20140417000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&format=+%5B%22Theros+Block%22%5D&type=+%5B%22Land%22%5D&name=+%5BTemple%5D) of *Theros* block, and *Journey into Nyx* completes our cycle of ten. Huzzah!
