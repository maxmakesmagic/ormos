Learn more about Grand Prix Toronto's Top 8 competitors—including who they are, what they've done in the game, and more—by taking a look through their profiles!

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gptor17/t8_potter.jpg)Name:** Doug Potter

**Age:** 30

**Hometown:** Edmonton, Alberta

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Top of Grand Prix Toronto 2016 while also making Top 8 of the Sunday Super Series at the same time.

**What colors was your Sealed Deck? Record?** Green-white splashing red. 9-0.

**What was the best card in your Sealed deck?** [Oasis Ritualist](https://web.archive.org/web/20170724000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oasis+Ritualist)

**What were your draft decks, and records with them?** Blue-Red, 1-2; Blue-Red 3-0.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** My podcast cohosts on First Strike did a lot of prep, and I feel this Top 8 validates their findings. Without them I wouldn’t be here so I am very thankful.  
  

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gptor17/t8_rashid.jpg)Name:** Taimur Rashid

**Age:** 21

**Hometown:** Toronto, Ontario

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** None

**What colors was your Sealed Deck? Record?** The Three-Color Jund Special. 8-1

**What was the best card in your Sealed deck?** Oasist Ritualist; it lets you put anything into your deck.

**What were your draft decks, and records with them?** Green-White Exert, 3-0. Then 1-1-1 with white-black.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** It’s been a goal for a while. And being better than MTGO grinder Proltilary.  
  

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gptor17/t8_nickolai.jpg)Name:** Matthew Nickolai

**Age:** 25

**Hometown:** Waterford, MI

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** JSS super star.

**What colors was your Sealed Deck? Record?** White-blue splashing green for [River Hoopoe](https://web.archive.org/web/20170724000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=River+Hoopoe). It teamed up with [Crested Sunmare](https://web.archive.org/web/20170724000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crested+Sunmare) to make a LOT of Horses. I went 8-1.

**What was the best card in your Sealed deck?** Despite what I just said, the best card was definitely Royal Caracal.

**What were your draft decks, and records with them?** 2-1 with blue-red aggro with a lot of reach, and then 3-0 with black-green.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** It means I get to go win a Pro Tour :) Shoutouts to Kyle, Jennifer, Jarod, Tiny Rick, the Cranberry Boiz and the Haolophiles.  
  

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gptor17/t8_an.jpg)Name:** You Cheng An

**Age:** 30

**Hometown:** Beijing, China

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** FNM Top 8

**What colors was your Sealed Deck? Record?** Red-white, 7-1-1.

**What was the best card in your Sealed deck?** [Gust Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170724000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gust+Walker).

**What were your draft decks, and records with them?** Went 5-1 with white-red and then white-black.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** I can’t get back home on time.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gptor17/t8_cuneo.jpg)Name:** Andrew Cuneo

**Age:** 42

**Hometown:** Philadelphia

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Top 8 at GP Las Vegas.

**What colors was your Sealed Deck? Record?** White, blue and black.

**What was the best card in your Sealed deck?** Sideboarded in [Shatter](https://web.archive.org/web/20170724000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shatter)storm to destroy [Edifice of Authority](https://web.archive.org/web/20170724000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Edifice+of+Authority) in one round. Also got Rhonis’s Monument and [Sunset Pyramid](https://web.archive.org/web/20170724000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sunset+Pyramid) in another round.

**What were your draft decks, and records with them?** Esper, 3-0. Black-red 2-0-1.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** Locked Gold.  
  

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gptor17/t8_sun.jpg)Name:** Allen Sun

**Age:** 39

**Hometown:** Alexandria, Virginia

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Top 8 at Grand Prix Vancouver

**What colors was your Sealed Deck? Record?** 8-1 with Blue-White splash black.

**What was the best card in your Sealed deck?** [Hour of Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20170724000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hour+of+Revelation) because it won my every game I cast it. The second best was [Cartouche of Ambition](https://web.archive.org/web/20170724000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cartouche+of+Ambition) because I played against five red-white decks.

**What were your draft decks, and records with them?** White-black splash green, 3-0. Four-Color Ramp, 1-1-1.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** It means the world to me because I’ve been trying for the past year to get back on the Pro Tour. Shoutout to all the great *Magic* players in the north DC metro area.  
  

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gptor17/t8_anderson.jpg)Name:** Robert Anderson

**Age:** 32

**Hometown:** Montreal, Quebec

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Top 64 at Pro Tour Philadelphia at the first Modern Pro Tour. I am old.

**What colors was your Sealed Deck? Record?** Blue-black with one [Trial of Zeal](https://web.archive.org/web/20170724000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trial+of+Zeal) that I never drew. I went 8-1.

**What was the best card in your Sealed deck?** [Torment of Hailfire](https://web.archive.org/web/20170724000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torment+of+Hailfire).

**What were your draft decks, and records with them?** Red-white terrible, went 2-1. White-blue great, went 3-0.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** So happy to finally get there and share this with this great community.  
  

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gptor17/t8_weitz.jpg)Name:** Benjamin Weitz

**Age:** 27

**Hometown:** St. Paul, MN

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** four Grand Prix Top 8, 2017 MOCS competitor. Gold pro.

**What colors was your Sealed Deck? Record?** White-black, went 9-0.

**What was the best card in your Sealed deck?** [The Scarab God](https://web.archive.org/web/20170724000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Scarab+God). Actually unbeatable.

**What were your draft decks, and records with them?** Black-red with sideboarded [Nicol Bolas, God-Pharaoh](https://web.archive.org/web/20170724000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nicol+Bolas%2C+God-Pharaoh), went 3-0. Then drafted White-Blue [Anointed Procession](https://web.archive.org/web/20170724000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anointed+Procession) and went 1-1-1.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** Winning the tournament would get me Gold for next year in the Pro Players Club and mean my first trophy. That’s what I’ve got my sights set on.
