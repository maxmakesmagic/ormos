Ol' Rosewater has been out of the office for the past few days, but we must press on! Mr. Babycakes stops for no man!

**The result of the art vote:** Mark Tedin, he of Lord of the Pit fame, edged out Matt "Scandalmonger" Cavotta by only 50 votes. Mark is one of "last men standing" from the days of Alpha card art, and he should do a sweet looking Babycakes. We hope to have some sketches from Mark to show you soon.

**The next step:** "Mr. Babycakes" was funny a few months ago, but the name has gotten old. I'm ready to give him a "real" name, and you all should be too. This week we'll be accepting names for the card.

Some quick rules on naming gleaned from Brady Dommermuth, our creative director:

*   No anachronisms (guns, computers, etc.)
*   No double-entendres
*   No foreign words
*   Names cannot start with "The" (The Rack, The Abyss, etc. Bad.)

Good enough. The creative guys can sort out the rest.

**Refresher course:** Mr. Babycakes is a ![1 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/4c/6b4cf558eab279fb9414f146ebdd0e0705ec92984fec530597dd2a3bce43ce24.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif) 0/3 Elemental with the following text: "Whenever a player plays a spell, you may put a +1/+1 counter on CARDNAME. At the beginning of your upkeep, you may remove all +1/+1 counters from CARDNAME and distribute them among any number of creatures." His art description reads: "A humanoid elemental of moss supports itself with a hand against a dead tree. From where the elemental touches, it spreads wild regrowth." Name him appropriately.

You must be registered to submit a name, and you get to submit one per person. Submissions end midnight Sunday night. The top handful, as determined by our creative team, will be presented for a vote. I can't wait to see what you come up with.
