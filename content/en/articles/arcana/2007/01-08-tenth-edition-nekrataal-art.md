During [Selecting *Tenth Edition*](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603133326/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/selecting10e/wrapup), you selected Christopher Moeller's sketch of Nekrataal for the card's *Tenth Edition* printing. The final art is done, and we thought you'd like to see it way, way in advance of *Tenth's* release this summer.

First, here was Mr. Moeller's original sketch:
