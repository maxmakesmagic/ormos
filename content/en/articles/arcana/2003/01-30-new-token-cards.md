![Player Reward](http://www.wizards.com/dci/images/Magic_PlayerRewards_Logo.jpg "Player Reward")

Two new token cards are already in the mail to Player Reward members.

  
![Insect token and Sliver token](https://i.ormos.uk/d0/a6/d0a6a2864168e1466071acb9e34f8dd294610664c3c8cee1076979d014f8de24.jpg)

The first is a 1/1 green Insect token that can be used in conjunction with lots of cards, from Saber Ants to the *Onslaught* Symbiotic crew to Wirewood Hivemaster.

The second is a 1/1 colorless Sliver token, which works perfectly with not only Brood Sliver, but the Sliver Queen as well!

Interested in joining Player Reward and getting your own tokens in the mail? Click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20030130000000/http://www.wizards.com/dci/rewards.asp?x=rewards).
