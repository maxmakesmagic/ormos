Last year, we took a look at [the way the shocklands changed](https://web.archive.org/web/20130219000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/shocklands-then-and-now-2012-09-24) between the original *Ravnica* block and *Return to Ravnica*. But we could only look at five of them, which seemed a little incomplete. So now that *Gatecrash* is out, let's do some side-by-side comparisons of [Godless Shrine](https://web.archive.org/web/20130219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Godless+Shrine), [Watery Grave](https://web.archive.org/web/20130219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Watery+Grave), [Stomping Ground](https://web.archive.org/web/20130219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stomping+Ground), [Sacred Foundry](https://web.archive.org/web/20130219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sacred+Foundry), and [Breeding Pool](https://web.archive.org/web/20130219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Breeding+Pool)!
