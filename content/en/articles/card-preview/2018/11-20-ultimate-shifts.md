Not too long ago, I [wrote about one of my favorite decks](/en/articles/feature/2018/10-09-the-decks-of-wotcstaff), my (nearly) all foil [Azami, Lady of Scrolls](https://web.archive.org/web/20181120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azami%2C+Lady+of+Scrolls) Commander deck. The story I didn't share there was the difficulty I had finding two key foils—[Laboratory Maniac](https://web.archive.org/web/20181120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Laboratory+Maniac) and, somewhat ironically, [Foil](https://web.archive.org/web/20181120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foil).

[Laboratory Maniac](https://web.archive.org/web/20181120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Laboratory+Maniac) I ended up finding in a pile of old *Innistrad* cards I had forgotten existed, but [Foil](https://web.archive.org/web/20181120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foil) was printed during the multi-year period where I took my one and only break from *Magic*, and it had not ever been reprinted in foil. Uncommon foils from *Prophecy* just weren't all that, well, common.

I did, eventually, find one, but that job would have been much easier if, say, it had been printed as a common in a *Masters* set. Like, say, *Ultimate Masters*.

![Foil](https://media.wizards.com/2018/uma/en_wo1wBJOnIQ.png)

That's right, foil [Foil](https://web.archive.org/web/20181120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foil) lovers and Pauper players, [Foil](https://web.archive.org/web/20181120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foil) is back, *and it's common*.

In fact, *Ultimate Masters* is just lousy with cards downshifted for people like me. In fact, for people *exactly* like me.

![Laboratory Maniac](https://media.wizards.com/2018/uma/en_NgDN6uuBO2.png)

Yup, that's an uncommon. But why stop there?

[Living Lore](https://web.archive.org/web/20181120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+Lore) lacked the love of players, but maybe they'll understand it as an uncommon?

![Living Lore](https://media.wizards.com/2018/uma/en_1Ce9ECjHyN.png)

[Firewing Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20181120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firewing+Phoenix) and [Magmaw](https://web.archive.org/web/20181120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magmaw) both served their purposes in Limited, but when the set is juiced up, some Limited rare bombs can stand to become Limited uncommon bombs.

![Firewing Phoenix](https://media.wizards.com/2018/uma/en_LI02lTMFOQ.png)![Magmaw](https://media.wizards.com/2018/uma/en_G3WGqYWqLo.png)

Similarly, [Hero of Leina Tower](https://web.archive.org/web/20181120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero+of+Leina+Tower) was a payoff card for heroic decks back in *Theros* block, but can you imagine getting more than one of these? I'll do you one better, can you imagine having more than one of these and then casting *Travel Preparations*?

![Hero of Leina Tower](https://media.wizards.com/2018/uma/en_2AYr9wu20s.png)![Travel Preparations](https://media.wizards.com/2018/uma/en_bqWTdJIH5w.png)

"Sure," you say, "those are cool and all, but [Foil](https://web.archive.org/web/20181120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foil) whetted my appetite for some Pauper additions. That card seems absurd in the format. Surely that's not all you've got."

Surely not.

![Tethmos High Priest](https://media.wizards.com/2018/uma/en_3shCbJPguT.png)![Miming Slime](https://media.wizards.com/2018/uma/en_v7gWKxjscT.png)![Slum Reaper](https://media.wizards.com/2018/uma/en_uE7MJuLvtO.png)

![Dimir Guildmage](https://media.wizards.com/2018/uma/en_ZzuLPZypIn.png)

They probably won't bend the format on their ear like [Foil](https://web.archive.org/web/20181120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foil) will (or foil [Foil](https://web.archive.org/web/20181120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foil)s, if you're fancy), but getting this many new toys all at once has surely got to shake things up.

And even if it somehow doesn't, it will be enjoyable giggling a little bit every time you crack a foil [Foil](https://web.archive.org/web/20181120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foil), now very much easier to find.
