Terastodon is a fascinating new rare from *Worldwake* that players have initially been excited about, but have struggled to find a place for in their decks. Former Player of the Year Tomoharu Saito had no problems, swapping the fatty into his Hypergenesis combo deck, then taking the deck to the Top 8 of Grand Prix–Oakland. When he plopped the 9/9 onto the battlefield, he usually blew up his own lands, giving him a whopping 18 power worth of creatures! After all, once he had gone off, he no longer needed his lands. Read the full coverage from the event [here](/en/events/2010/ptsd10/gortzen-goes-all-the-way-in-san-diego).
