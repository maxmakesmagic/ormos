For sequel week, we're going to take another look at a ***Magic** 2010* card that makes a cameo in a different ***Magic** 2011* card. We start with the story of [Siege Mastodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20100901000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Mastodon).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9f/cd/9fcd16141bd88277b8484ab74c8ae539ea84ff30f76a2a6e5c9725da2b371526.jpg)

[Siege Mastodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20100901000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Mastodon) | Art by [Matt Cavotta](https://web.archive.org/web/20100901000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Matt+Cavotta%22%5D)

This armored elephant appears in both ***Magic** 2010* and ***Magic** 2011*, with different flavor text:

<table><tbody><tr><td><div><div><img alt="Siege Mastodon" src="http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&amp;name=Siege+Mastodon"></div></div></td><td><div><div><img alt="Siege Mastodon" src="http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&amp;name=Siege+Mastodon"></div></div></td></tr></tbody></table>

In its original printing, it appears to have been engaged in the defense of a city, but once it's in the hands of General Avitora (instead of some nameless Mastodon keeper) it's attacking someone defended by walls. Which might remind you of a different piece of flavor text.

![145](https://i.ormos.uk/7e/e0/7ee03606576e3f48eaff4fa7234a5917ca201f71b1234eaed828c14d8af20717.jpg)  

So ... what card in ***Magic** 2011* shows a flying armored elephant?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9d/9b/9d9b79159d18f82218aeab2b210eccc80b1969c320406c83cc86b7439ee0a210.jpg)

[Mighty Leap](https://web.archive.org/web/20100901000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mighty+Leap) | Art by [rk post](https://web.archive.org/web/20100901000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22rk+post%22%5D)

![Mighty Leap](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Mighty+Leap)

  

(Okay, so the armored elephant on [Mighty Leap](https://web.archive.org/web/20100901000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mighty+Leap) doesn't have its tusks cut off. Close enough!)
