**Magic: The Gathering® Pro Tour™–Berlin 2008 Qualifier  
August 30-31, 2008** 

The **Magic: The Gathering**® Grand Prix tournament series is an international circuit of large-scale Qualifier tournaments featuring cash prizes and multiple invitations to Pro TourTM events.

### TOURNAMENT FORMAT AND SCHEDULE

The format for Grand Prix–Manila is *Lorywn-Shadowmoor*TM Block Constrcuted. The sets used will be *Lorwyn, Morningtide, Shadowmoor*, and *Eventide*. All DCITM constructed-format rules, Magic® Floor Rules, and all subsequent updates will be enforced.  
**Friday, August 29:** Onsite registration is from 3:00 –8:00 P.M.  
**Saturday, August 30:** Late registration from 9:00 – 10:00 A.M. Tournament begins at 10:15 A.M. If less than 800 players participate in the event, the Top 64 players advance to competition on Sunday If 800 or more players participate, the top 128 advance to Sunday.  
**Sunday, August 31:** The Top 64 (or 128) players begin play at 9:00 A.M.

### TOURNAMENT

**Divisions**  
 The main tournament at Grand Prix–Manila is open to **Magic** players of all ages. 

*   **Head Judge**
     The Head Judge for Grand Prix–Manila will be Lindsay Heming, Level 3 from Australia.

*   **Tournament Structure**
     The structure for Grand Prix–Manila will be modified Swiss-style (50-minute rounds), with the Top 64 players (or 128 if 800 or more players registered for the main event) advancing to the second day of competition for prizes and invitations.

*   **Tournament Divisions and Prizes**
     All participants in the Grand Prix will receive a Call of the Herd exclusive Grand Prix foil card, while supplies last

**Pro Tour Invitations**  
 The Top 16 previously uninvited players will each win an invitation to Pro Tour–Berlin. Grand Prix invitations no longer pass down to previously uninvited players. The top 16 finishers will receive invitations even if one or more of those players already possesses an invitation.

**Tournament Byes** (Byes are not cumulative throughout the season or year)

Byes will be given to players in the Grand Prix based on the following criteria:

>  ***Third-Round Byes***  
>  
> 
> *   Pro Player club level 4 and above.
> *   DCI players with a Constructed rating of 2000 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on the player's DCI Constructed rating on the Wednesday before the Grand Prix.
> *   Winners of Grand Prix–Manila Trials
> 
>  ***Second-Round Byes***  
>  
> 
> *   Pro Player club level 2 or 3
> *   DCI players with a Constructed rating of 1900 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on the player's DCI Constructed rating on the Wednesday before the Grand Prix.
> 
>  ***First-Round Byes***  
>  
> 
> *   Pro Player club level 1
> *   DCI players with a Constructed rating of 1800 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on the player's DCI Constructed rating on the Wednesday before the Grand Prix.
