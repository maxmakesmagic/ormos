It is time to look at some of the promo cards that will be associated with *Return to Ravnica*. For example, *Return to Ravnica* Game Day is October 27-28, 2012. And while supplies last, everyone who plays gets this!

![Dryad Militant](https://i.ormos.uk/94/71/9471c77fedf84a1b4930e8aceaa01f381a83c56817d802fadd273ed3830cc8f1.jpg)

And if you reach the Top 8, you'll take this home!

![Cryptborn Horror](https://i.ormos.uk/87/d8/87d82cd95ef4439f5519b49088b943b9e895820a022c7f25e43be11ea3d930ce.jpg)

Moving backwards slightly, *Return to Ravnica* goes on sale on Friday, October 5, 2012. And because that's a Friday, there's a special card at Friday Night Magic!

![Deadbridge Goliath](https://i.ormos.uk/31/da/31daf74d3b20acde715e166f918af2b786b34a869ab982ca5bcbd87e3812007c.jpg)

And on October 5, the *Return to Ravnica* League starts in your local store. When it ends, the prizes include this Knight token!

![Return to Ravnica Knight Token](https://i.ormos.uk/6f/50/6f500f2123dbd6dd77c93903d03f5edda21581f9267ab95254821729ffa5becd.jpg)

Of course, there's also a Buy-a-Box card. To get this, simply find a participating store and reserve a box of boosters. But do it quickly, because you have to be one of the first 20 or 30 (depending on the store).

![Supreme Verdict](https://i.ormos.uk/39/64/3964f596af1d692bb62261a5d4ec6f268a96f5088f65f73613401fc50f4f49ea.jpg)

And finally, we have the alternate-art Prerelease promo cards. As with previous Prereleases, you get these at the Prerelease (which is September 29-30, 2012). But there are some changes this time! First, there are five different ones, one for each guild in *Return to Ravnica*. And second, you get to play your Prerelease card *in your deck at the Prerelease*!

And here are the Prerelease cards. You might notice some similarity to the cards in the cards in the [*Return to Ravnica* Intro Packs](https://web.archive.org/web/20120911000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/return-ravnica-intro-packs-2012-09-05).
