### The Announcement You've Been Waiting For

* * *

**Note:** [As posted by Linda Cox](https://web.archive.org/web/20030729000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/showthread.php?threadid=70795), Brand Manager for ***Magic** Online,* there was an issue with the *Eighth Edition* Core Game SKU that caused downtime after this article was posted. While players could recieve the packs of digital cards that match those that come in the Core Game, attempts to open them were causing serious problems within the ***Magic** Online* game. Until this issue is resolved, players who purchase the *Eighth Edition* Core Game will be able to recieve the packs of cards, but not open them within the game.

*Daniel Myers  
 Producer*  
**Magic** Online Website

* * *

After a couple of days of frustration, we're ready to go ahead with ***Magic** Online* Version 2.0. I know you've all been waiting for details on a number of issues and this should answer most of them.

### Magic Online Store

The [***Magic** Online* Store](https://web.archive.org/web/20030729000000/http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com/) is already open for business. At 12:00 Noon PDT today, both *Eighth Edition* product and new accounts will be available for purchase.

### Sanctioned Events

We'll be keeping sanctioned events turned off until 12:00 Noon PDT tomorrow, July 30, as we keep an eye out for any game-critical issues. This includes Premier Events and the Draft and Constructed queues. We're doing this to prevent any possible event interruptions if we need to restart the servers.

### Eighth Edition Release Events

We're planning on beginning the *Eighth Edition* Release Events beginning at 12:01 AM on Thursday, July 31 and running them until 11:59 PM on Friday, August 1. If any game-critical issues arise during the next few days, we may have to reschedule the events. Please keep an eye on the [***Magic** Online* Message Boards](https://web.archive.org/web/20030729000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/forumdisplay.php?forumid=142) and watch for any in-game announcements for information.

### Version 2.0 Features

As some of you have already noted, not all of the planned elements of Version 2.0 have been added to ***Magic** Online.* While a majority of features are already in place, those that aren't already in the game will be added as we get a chance.

Our primary concern has always been making sure that nothing affects your gameplay on ***Magic** Online.* As we have reached that point after an extended period of downtime, we wanted to get you back in the game. We're sorry for the delay and thank you for your patience and support as we got ***Magic** Online* up and running.

### On A Personal Note

I want to thank everyone who used the message boards to voice their opinions, concerns, frustrations, support, and even anger during this period. By keeping everything on this public forum, we were able to understand what was going on within the ***Magic** Online* community while keeping our development team free to get the game back to you.

It has been an exhausting few days for all of us and it's great to have good news for you this morning. Thank you for being part of the ***Magic** Online* community.

Sincerely,  
 Daniel Myers  
*On behalf of the* **Magic** Online *Team*
