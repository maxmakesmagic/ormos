The **PLAY THE GAME, SEE THE WORLD** Qualifier tournaments are a series of qualifiers run throughout Europe. The top two finishers at each qualifier earn invitations to the **PLAY THE GAME, SEE THE WORLD** Championship tournament, to be held at the 2009 **Magic: The Gathering** World Championship in Rome, Italy on November 22, 2009. Additional qualifiers will be held at the World Championship for the **PLAY THE GAME, SEE THE WORLD** Championship tournament, where the winner will receive paid airfare and hotel at the 3 2010 Pro Tour Events and the 2010 World Championships, subject to the [official rules](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2009-play-the-game-see-the-world-championship-tournament-official-rules).

*   [Complete Rules of the **PLAY THE GAME, SEE THE WORLD** Tournaments](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2009-play-the-game-see-the-world-championship-tournament-official-rules)
*   [Locations of **PLAY THE GAME, SEE THE WORLD** Qualifier tournaments](#locations)
*   [More Information on Public Events at the 2009 Magic: The Gathering World Championships](/en/articles/event-coverage/2011/10-10-magic-weekend-san-francisco-and-2011-worlds-event-information#publicevents)

*All Universal Tournament Rules and Magic: the Gathering Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document.* 

**Entry Fee**  

*   Contact your local organizer for entry fee.**Format**  

*   Format determined by organizer (Standard, Extended, Legacy, or Sealed Deck)
*   Deck lists are required
*   Tournaments with less than 8 players will be cancelled.

**Tournament Length**  

*   Swiss rounds - 50 minutes per round
*   Number of Swiss rounds based on attendance according to the following chart:

**Advancement**  

*   All players may participate in all Swiss rounds.
*   The top 8 players after the final Swiss round will advance to the top 8 single-elimination finals.**Top 8**  

*   Top 8 matches will be best 2 of 3
*   Top 8 matches will have no time limit.
*   Where necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will still be used to determine final order in the standings.

**Prizes**  

*   The top 2 players will win an invitation to the **PLAY THE GAME, SEE THE WORLD** Championship tournament held at the 2009 World Championship on Sunday, November 22, 2009 at 9:00 AM, which is subject to the [official rules](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2009-play-the-game-see-the-world-championship-tournament-official-rules).
*   Product Prizes as determined by the organizer.

**Notes**  

*   16K, REL Regular
*   All Universal Tournament Rules and Magic: the Gathering Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document
*   Players must bring pen, paper, card sleeves and appropriate counters.
