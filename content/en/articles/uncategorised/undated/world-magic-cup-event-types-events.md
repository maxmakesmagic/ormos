The World Magic Cup is an annual Magic event, gathering representatives from nations all over the world to compete in a unique team format for the ultimate national bragging rights. Teams of four, representing more than 70 countries, face off every year over a multi-day, multi-format event to crown the World Magic Cup Champion.

[2015 World Magic Cup Information](https://web.archive.org/web/20180409162055/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2015-world-magic-cup-event-information)

[LIST OF 2015 WORLD MAGIC CUP COMPETITORS](https://web.archive.org/web/20180409162055/http://magic.wizards.com/en/2015wmc/invitationlist)

## WORLD MAGIC CUP QUALIFIERS

The World Magic Cup is an invitation-only event—there is no open entry. To get there, you must either be the top ranked pro in your country or a winner from a World Magic Cup Qualifier.

[LEARN MORE](https://web.archive.org/web/20180409162055/https://magic.wizards.com/node/116774)

 

## PRIZES

Players receive Pro Points and prize money based on their finish. All members of the Top 4 receive invitations and airfare to the very next Pro Tour and the captain of that team wins an invitation to participate in the next years [Magic World Championship](https://web.archive.org/web/20180409162055/https://magic.wizards.com/content/eventsscheduleworldchampionship). All prizes are subject to change.

## RULES

Players qualify for the World Magic Cup either by winning a [World Magic Cup Qualifier](https://web.archive.org/web/20180409162055/https://magic.wizards.com/content/eventsscheduleworldmagiccupqualifiers) or by finishing the season as the National Champion—the nation’s top Pro Points earner who then becomes the team captain. For more information please refer to the [Premier Event Invitation Policy](http://archive.wizards.com/ContentResources/Wizards/WPN/Main/Documents/Magic_The_Gathering_Premier_Event_Invitation_Policy_PDF1.pdf).

Players are responsible for knowing all high-level [tournament rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20180409162055/http://wpn.wizards.com/en/node/58).
