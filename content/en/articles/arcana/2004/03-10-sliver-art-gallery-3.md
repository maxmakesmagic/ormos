M[onday](/en/articles/arcana/2004/03-08-sliver-art-gallery-2) we looked at *Stronghold's* multicolored Slivers, and [yesterday](/en/articles/arcana/2004/03-09-sliver-art-gallery-2-2) we focused on *Tempest's* 2/2 uncommon Slivers. Today we were going to finish out *Tempest* with a look at the art of the 1/1 commons; unfortunately, not all the commons' art is currently in the art database (no worries; the couple missing ones are around here somewhere, they're just temporarily misplaced).

So in place of [Muscle Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20040310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Muscle%2BSliver), [Clot Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20040310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Clot%2BSliver) and [Metallic Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20040310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Metallic%2BSliver) Slivers' art, instead please enjoy the bonus art of [Sliver Overlord](https://web.archive.org/web/20040310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sliver+Overlord) and of the *Legions* Sliver token, both by the illustrious Tony Szczudlo.
