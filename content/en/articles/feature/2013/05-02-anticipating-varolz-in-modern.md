Recently, osmanozguney finished 3–1 in a Modern Daily event with a black-green aggro deck with [Death's Shadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death%27s+Shadow) that used [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant), [Overgrown Tomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overgrown+Tomb), fetch lands, [Putrid Leech](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrid+Leech), [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), and [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember) to pay life to make it big early in the game. 

![Varolz, the Scar-Striped](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Varolz%2C+the+Scar-Striped)

While this is a relatively interesting strategy in Modern, in that it isn't all over, a 3–1 finish with a [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) and [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant) deck might not ordinarily warrant closer investigation. However, *Dragon's Maze* is about to come out, and [Varolz, the Scar-Striped](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Varolz%2C+the+Scar-Striped) has been hyped as part of a two-card combo with [Death's Shadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death%27s+Shadow) to turn [Death's Shadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death%27s+Shadow) into a one-mana "Aura" that gives +13/+13, which can be, as we say in the business, "big game." 

The question has been finding a shell for this combo, and there are some who think it's all hype because the cards aren't independently good enough. I'm optimistic about this combo specifically because I think each card is good on its own, and they fit so well in the shell osmanozguney has built that I wonder if it was being tested specifically in anticipation of [Varolz, the Scar-Striped](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Varolz%2C+the+Scar-Striped). 

[Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) and [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek) are perfect for making sure your opponent can't kill one of your creatures before you invest your +13/+13 and, incidentally, [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) doesn't make a bad Aura after it's dead, either. 

The question is, what can be cut to make room for [Varolz, the Scar-Striped](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Varolz%2C+the+Scar-Striped)? Unfortunately, I don't have an easy answer for that. All the cards in this deck look both good and important, and I expect it to be more a question of trimming than a direct replacement. Maybe I'd try three [Varolz, the Scar-Striped](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Varolz%2C+the+Scar-Striped)s over a [Liliana](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+of+the+Veil), an [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek), and a [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember) to start.
