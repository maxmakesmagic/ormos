You may have seen **Oyobi, Who Split the Heavens** spitting out 3/3 flying spirits in ***Magic** Online*. There's original art for those spirit tokens that you can see in the game.

![Oyobi and spirit tokens](https://i.ormos.uk/db/a8/dba8d375395b1ecf0ec2c8614bac4e36d0145547fd116b06aa51291c70fba5f4.jpg) 

The art for those 3/3 spirits was created by Matt Thompson. Would you like to see the full art in its hi-res glory?

![Betrayers of Kamigawa spirit token art by Matt Thompson](https://i.ormos.uk/b9/db/b9dbc1a6aa50ed1b6331d7805073faac1604e4b7ffdb248849c750f4302c68a5.jpg) 

Thought you might.

If you look closely at the art of Oyobi, Who Split the Heavens by Chris Moeller, you can see the soaring, birdlike spirits that served as the basis for Matt's design.

![Oyobi, Who Split the Heavens art by Christopher Moeller](https://i.ormos.uk/7b/84/7b84212c9d2d441700d7b61fb87252cff93805efdf199a0f6d98c55c6decef35.jpg)
