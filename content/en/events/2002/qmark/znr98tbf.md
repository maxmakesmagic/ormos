### July 2002: Cards with a Converted Mana Cost of 4

**Monday, July 1:** What flier with a converted mana cost of four has an anagram in its name that makes fun of how bad the card is?  
**Answer:**Kyscu Drake (*Visions*). "Kyscu" is an anagram of "sucky". On a satirical whim, the R&D team came up with the Kyscu Mountains where all green and red's weak fliers live.  
**Winner:** Rory Johnston

**Tuesday, July 2:** In what artifact with a converted mana cost of four was Mangara held captive?  
**Answer:**Amber Prison. Kaervek and Jolrael ambushed their fellow wizard and imprisoned him. This all happen during *Mirage*.  
**Winner:** Elly Chen

**Wednesday, July 3:** What enchantment with a converted mana cost of four is key to the "Fruity Pebbles" deck?  
**Answer:**Enduring Renewal. Popular a number of years ago in Extended, the deck revolved around the combo or Enduring Renewal, Goblin Bombardment, and a 0-cost artifact creature such as Phyrexian Walker or Shield Sphere.  
**Winner:** Eric Martin

**Thursday, July 4:** What red creature with a converted mana cost of 4 has appeared in an expansion, in the base set, in two gift box sets, and as a promotional oversized card?  
**Answer:**Balduvian Horde. It appeared first in *Alliances*. Then it was reprinted in Sixth Edition. It was turned into a promotional oversized card. And it appeared in two gift box sets: Beatdown Box and Deckmaster 2001.  
**Winner:** Matthew Hall

**Friday, July 5:** What creature with a converted mana cost of four appeared on the cover of the alpha rule book?  
**Answer:**Bog Wraith. The beta rulebook pictured Shivan Dragon.  
**Winner:** Alex Hutton

**Monday, July 8:** What creature with a converted mana cost of four was the first creature to ever be banned or restricted?  
**Answer:**Ali from Cairo (from *Arabian Nights*).  
**Winner:** Robert Hilliard

**Tuesday, July 9:** There is a creature with a converted mana cost of four that shares a word in its name with four other creatures. All four of these other creatures share a mechanic while this one does not. What creature is it?  
**Answer:**Clockwork Gnomes. It shares its name with Clockwork Beast (alpha), Clockwork Avian (*Antiquities*), Clockwork Steed (*Homelands*) and Clockwork Swarm (*Homelands*).  
**Winner:** Nate Price

**Wednesday, July 10:** Blue, black, red and green got a mechanic that didn't appear in white until over a year later on an enchantment with a converted mana cost of four. What is the white enchantment?  
**Answer:**Serra Aviary. The mechanic was enchant worlds. The other four colors had their first enchant worlds in *Legends*. White didn't get its first enchant world until *Homelands*.  
**Winner:** Chris Lawrence

**Thursday, July 11:** What spell with a converted mana cost of four was called "Barry's Propaganda" in design?  
**Answer:**Collective Restraint. The domain mechanic was called the "barry" mechanic after its creator Barry Reich.  
**Winner:** Richard Mott

**Friday, July 12:** You have an enchantment with a converted mana cost of four. You have no other permanents in play, no cards in hand and no cards in your graveyard. Your opponent attacks you with a creature. Without sacrificing the enchantment or spending any mana, you use the enchantment to destroy the attacking creature. What enchantment do you have in play?  
**Answer:**Spirit Mirror. You used it to kill an attacking reflection token creature.  
**Winner:** Steve Gasior

**Monday, July 15:** What spell with a converted mana cost of four exists in an identical form in a non-tournament legal set except missing a proper name in its title?  
**Answer:**Unyaro Bee Sting (from *Mirage*). It exists in *Portal* and *Portal: Second Age* as Bee Sting.  
**Winner:** Andrew Levine

**Tuesday, July 16:** What two cards with a converted mana cost of four can produce tokens with power greater than toughness?  
**Answer:**Balduvian Dead (from *Alliances*) and Tooth and Claw (from *Tempest*). The first makes a 3/1 black and red Graveborn token and the latter a 3/1 red Carnivore tokens.  
**Winner:** Toby Elliott

**Wednesday, July 17:** What black card with a converted mana cost of four has all five vowels appear in its title only once and in alphabetical order?  
**Answer:**Scavenging Ghoul (from Alpha).  
**Winner:** Juan Antonio Romero Lara

**Thursday, July 18:** What wall with a converted mana cost of four pictures the stuffy doll? (The stuffy doll is the creature that first appeared on the Black Vise.)  
**Answer:**Wall of Wonder. Richard Thomas illustrated both cards.  
**Winner: Peter Williams**

**Friday, July 19:** What card with a converted mana cost of four was printed in the first six basic sets each time with a different rules text? (Alpha, Beta, and Unlimited obviously are all the same.)  
**Answer:**Castle. Alpha/Beta/Unlimited - "Your untapped creatures gain +0/+2. Attacking creatures lose this bonus." Revised - "Your untapped creatures gain +0/+2. Attacking creatures do not get this bonus." Fourth - Untapped creatures you control get +0/+2 when not attacking. Fifth - "Each untapped creature you control gets +0/+2 unless it is attacking." Sixth - "Untapped creatures you control get +0/+2."  
**Winner:** Nick McAvoy

**Monday, July 22:** What enchantment with a converted mana cost of four has an effect that continues for a duration after its destroyed?  
**Answer:**Titania's Song (from *Antiquities*). "If Titania's Song leaves play, artifacts return to normal just before the untap phase of the next turn."  
**Winner:** Miles Hulsey

**Tuesday, July 23:** What artifact with a converted mana cost of four is named after the man who connected Richard Garfield with Peter Adkinson (the founder and former CEO of Wizards of the Coast)?  
**Answer:**Jayemdae Tome (from Alpha). It was named after J. Michael Davis, a good friend of Richard's and one-time head of R&D. The meeting between the two came about after Mike tried to sell Richard's game Roborally to Wizards of the Coast.  
**Winner:** Andrew Wolf

**Wednesday, July 24:** What *Tempest* block card with a converted mana cost of four was known as Sudden Death Overtime in design?  
**Answer:**Furnace of Rath (from *Tempest*).  
**Winner:** Christopher Richer

**Thursday, July 25:** What card with a converted mana cost of four was originally printed without a mana cost?  
**Answer:**Cyclopean Tomb. In alpha, the Tomb's mana cost was mistakenly left off.  
**Winner:** Kevin Austin

**Friday, July 26:** In the art of what card with a converted mana cost of four does the following quote appear: This demonstrates the law of wobble. "If you push something hard enough, it will fall down" - F.T. ?  
**Answer:**Browse (from *Alliances*)  
**Winner:** Ben McIntyre

**Monday, July 29:** What enchantment with a converted mana cost of four has a creature type mentioned in its name that has never appeared in that color?  
**Answer:**Feast of the Unicorn (from *Homelands*). Black has never had a unicorn.  
**Winner:** Alex Hazlewood

**Tuesday, July 30:** What card with a converted mana cost of 4 was inspired by the short story "The Beginning of the End"?  
**Answer:**Nevinyrral's Disk (from Alpha). The story is about an all powerful artifact capable of destroying the world. Nevinyrral is Larry Niven backwards, the author of the story.  
**Winner:** Kevin Doyle

**Wednesday, July 31:** On what card with a converted mana cost of four did the goblin Squee's name first appear?  
**Answer:**Relentless Assault (from *Visions*). He appeared in the flavor text: "Flog and Squee / Up the tree / See the army / Flee, flee, flee." -Goblin nursery rhyme/war cry. As the author of this lovely piece of flavor text, I can let you know that Squee got his name because it rhymed with tree.  
**Winner:** John Danaher
