## Overview

Bring your top strategies with you to a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier, and you may be one of the few to emerge from the event with an invitation and airfare to Pro Tour *Kaladesh*!

Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers (RPTQs) for Pro Tour *Kaladesh* take place August 20-21, 2016 and August 27-28, 2016.

The Top 4 finishers of a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier receive airfare and invitations to Pro Tour *Kaladesh*, while 1st- through 8th-place finishers receive invitations to the next round of Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers.

**The format for this Regional Pro Tour Qualifier season is Standard Constructed.**

**As a reminder, players are only allowed to compete in *one* Regional Pro Tour Qualifier if they are qualified to play in one.**

[TENTATIVE LIST OF QUALIFIED PLAYERS](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/regional-ptqs-for-pro-tour-kaladesh-rptq-player-invitation-list)

## Qualification

**Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers are an invitation-only event.** An invitation to participate in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier for Pro Tour Honolulu 2016 is offered to the following:

*   Players with a [Pro Tour Players Club](https://web.archive.org/web/20210923235846/http://magic.wizards.com/en/protour/playersclub/standings) Silver level (as of the date of the Regional Pro Tour Qualifier).
*   Players that won a Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifier from April 23, 2016 to July 10, 2016.
*   Players that finished -1st through 8th-place at a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier for Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*.
*   Players that won a Regional Last Chance Qualifier that ties to that Regional Pro Tour Qualifier.

## Eligibility

The following players may ***not*** compete in Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers for Pro Tour *Kaladesh*:

*   Players that previously participated in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier which awards an invitation to Pro Tour *Kaladesh*.
*   Players that won a *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifier Final which awards an invitation to Pro Tour *Kaladesh*.
*   Players that won a *Magic Online* Championship Series (MOCS) Playoff tournament which awards invitations to Pro Tour *Kaladesh*.
*   Players that finished in the Top 8 or finished with 39 or more match points in an individual-format Grand Prix which awards invitations to Pro Tour *Kaladesh*.
*   Players that finished in the Top 4 or finished with 36 or more match points in a team-format Grand Prix which awards invitations to Pro Tour *Kaladesh*.
*   Players that finished with 33 or more match points at Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon.*
*   Players with a Pro Tour Players Club Gold or Platinum level (as of the date of the Regional Pro Tour Qualifier).
*   Players that are members of the Pro Tour Hall of Fame.
*   Players that are otherwise already qualified for Pro Tour *Kaladesh*.

If you have any questions as to you eligibility to play in a particular Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifier, please email your inquiry to PremierPlay@wizards.com.

Complete information about invitations to Pro Tours can be found in the [*Magic: The Gathering* Invitation Policy](https://web.archive.org/web/20210923235846/http://wpn.wizards.com/en/document/magic-premier-event-invitation-policy).

## General Information

The structure for Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers is modified Swiss-style.

The top finishing players after the Swiss rounds play in one single-elimination playoff match to determine a Top 4.

*   Standard Constructed.
*   Decklists are required.

*   Swiss rounds will be 50 minutes in length.
*   Single-elimination playoff matches will have no time limit.
*   Number of Swiss rounds based on attendance according to the following chart:

*   Playoff matches will be best two of three.
*   Playoff matches will be single-elimination.
*   While there are no time limits in the playoff rounds, players are expected to play at a reasonable pace and finish their matches within 60 minutes.
*   Standings after the Swiss rounds will be used to determine final order in the standings for losing players in the quarterfinal and semifinal playoff rounds.
*   For the first game of each match in the playoff, the player who finished higher in the Swiss rounds chooses either to play first or to play second. For subsequent games in each playoff match, the usual Play/Draw rule applies (loser of the previous game decides whether to play first in the next game).
*   The [*Magic: The Gathering* Tournament Rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20210923235846/http://wpn.wizards.com/en/document/magic-gathering-tournament-rules) and the [*Magic: The Gathering* Infraction Procedure Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20210923235846/http://wpn.wizards.com/en/document/magic-infraction-procedure-guide) are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Fact Sheet.
*   Players are responsible for bringing their own method of tracking life totals and appropriate counters or tokens.

There is no entry fee to compete in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier.

## Magic Online Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers

The round of Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers taking place between August 20 and August 27, 2016 will have two *Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers in which qualified players may participate. The first will take place on August 20 at 2 p.m. PT, and the second one will take place August 27 at 5 a.m. PT.

To register for a *Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifier, a player who is qualified to compete in that round of Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers must send an email to PremierPlay@wizards.com with the following elements:

*   The title of the email should be "*Magic Online* RPTQ."

The email should include your DCI number, *Magic Online* username, and the date of the *Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifier that you wish to participate in.

The deadlines to register are:

*   Wednesday, August 17 at 11:59 PM PT (for the August 20 tournament)
*   Wednesday, August 24 at 11:59 PM PT (for the August 27 tournament)

*Magic Online* Regional ProTour Qualifiers will be capped at 212 players, so sign up early if you intend to play.

The Top 8 players will receive invitations to compete in Pro Tour *Kaladesh*, as well as an invitation to play in the [Pro Tour Challenge](https://web.archive.org/web/20210923235846/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers#challenge) event at that Pro Tour.

The format for these Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers is Standard Constructed.

As a reminder, if you play in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier during a qualifying round, **you cannot play in any other Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers during that qualifying round, including *Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers.** Your invitation to compete in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier only lets you compete in one Regional Pro Tour Qualifier.
