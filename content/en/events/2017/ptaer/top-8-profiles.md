Learn more about Pro Tour *Aether Revolt*'s Top 8 competitors—including who they are, what they’ve done in the game, and more—by taking a look through their profiles! 

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/ptaer/t8_carvalho.jpg)Name:** Marcio Carvalho 

**Age:** 31 

**Team:** DEX Army 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 2 Pro Tour Top 8s. 13 Grand Prix Top 8s, with 3 wins. Finals of the 2016 World Championship. 

**Limited Record:** 4-2 

**Standard Record:** 9-0-1 

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?** 

Blue-Red Improvise and Black-Red Aggro. I preferred the Black-Red deck.

 **What's the key to drafting *Aether Revolt*/*Kaladesh*?** 

Curve them out. Don't draft white.

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?** 

Mardu Vehicles! I was going to play Black-red, but Thiago Saporito told me that Mardu was awesome. I played some games and I was sold!

**What card was your MVP for the weekend?** 

[Release the Gremlins](https://web.archive.org/web/20170204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Release+the+Gremlins)

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?** 

A dream come true!

 **![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/ptaer/t8_smith.jpg)Name:** Donald Smith 

**Age:** 21 

**Hometown:** Cut Off, Louisiana 

**Team:** [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20170204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls) 

**Twitter Handle:** @donaldwsjr 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 

Top 25 Pro Tour *Battle for Zendikar*, Top 25 *Pro Tour Kaladesh*, Silver pro. 

**Limited Record:** 4-2 

**Standard Record:** 8-0-2 

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?** 

Green-blue with [Baral's Expertise](https://web.archive.org/web/20170204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baral%27s+Expertise), then black-red with two [Weldfast Engineer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Weldfast+Engineer), my favorite.

 **What's the key to drafting *Aether Revolt*/*Kaladesh*?** 

Read signals carefully in Pack 1 to get a payoff in *Kaladesh*. 

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?** 

Mardu Vehicles. Amazing versus [Saheeli Rai](https://web.archive.org/web/20170204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Saheeli+Rai) and its "bad" matchup (Black-Green) is close.

**What card was your MVP for the weekend?** 

[Depala, Pilot Exemplar](https://web.archive.org/web/20170204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Depala%2C+Pilot+Exemplar).

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?** 

I would be able to start a career playing *Magic*—my dream.

 **![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/ptaer/t8_ksandr.jpg)Name:** Jan Ksandr 

**Age:** 24 

**Hometown:** Prague, Czech Republic 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 

Finals of Grand Prix Sydney.

**Limited Record:** 5-1 

**Standard Record**: 7-2-1 

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?** 

First draft, I played a four-color deck, green-blue splashing white and black. In the first two packs I took four [Unbridled Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20170204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unbridled+Growth). Then I got two [Torrential Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrential+Gearhulk) in the third pack. Second draft was Blue-Black Improvise.

 **What's the key to drafting *Aether Revolt*/*Kaladesh*?** 

Stay open, focus on archetypes.

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?** 

Black-Green. My teammates Niels Molle and Kayure Patel built the deck and told me to play it.

**What card was your MVP for the weekend?** 

[Scrapheap Scrounger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scrapheap+Scrounger).

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?** 

It would mean a lot to me. I think I would focus on *Magic* even more, travel around the world, doing what I really like.

 **![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/ptaer/t8_sagalik2.jpg)Name:** Eduardo Sajgalik 

**Age:** 29 

**Hometown:** Montreal, Canada 

**Team:** MTG Mint Card 

**Twitter Handle:** @Walaoumpa 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Top 8 at Pro Tour *Return to Ravnica* (2012). Four Grand Prix Top 8, four National Team appearances, including twice as captain. 

**Limited Record:** 5-1 

**Standard Record:** 7-2-1 

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?** 

Black-Red Midrange—Super solid, removal and aggressive creatures.

Blue-Green Anything Energy—Combine bad cards and hope. ([Shipwreck Moray](https://web.archive.org/web/20170204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shipwreck+Moray) and Larger than Life)

 **What's the key to drafting *Aether Revolt*/*Kaladesh*?** 

Stay open, pick the late archetype cards. Also, Moray and Larger than Life.

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?** 

Mardu Vehicles—The team believe the deck was good. I went with it.

**What card was your MVP for the weekend?** 

[Unlicensed Disintegration](https://web.archive.org/web/20170204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unlicensed+Disintegration)

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?** 

No idea. It sounds insane to me! I'm just very happy at the moment.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/ptaer/t8_damo.jpg)Name:** Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa 

**Age:** 29 

**Hometown:** Porto Alegre, Brazil 

**Team:** Channel Fireball Ice 

**Twitter Handle:** @PVDDR 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 

Second most Pro Tour Top 8s of all time.

**Limited Record:** 4-2 

**Standard Record:** 8-2 

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?** 

Green-blue, red-white. I liked green-blue more.

 **What's the key to drafting *Aether Revolt*/*Kaladesh*?** 

I think all the colors are good, but I like to take enablers before payoff.

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?** 

Mardu Vehicles, the only strategy that doesn't need to sacrifice much to beat Jeskai Copy Cat.

**What card was your MVP for the weekend?** 

[Toolcraft Exemplar](https://web.archive.org/web/20170204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Toolcraft+Exemplar), it's so much better than any other one-drop.

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?** 

I'd be able to have a wedding party!
