Looking for a fun Extended deck with a wacky combo? How about trying Chester Li's Kiki-Jiki deck from the Top 8 of a Pro Tour Qualifier held in Boston? The combo is simple: cast Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker and use it to copy Pestermite. The token untaps a permanent—Kiki-Jiki—and the 2/2 Goblin is free to make another copy of Pestermite. Repeat ad nauseam to create infinite Pestermite tokens with haste and attack!
