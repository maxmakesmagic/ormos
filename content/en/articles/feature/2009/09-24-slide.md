Hall of Famer Bob Maher was semi-retired from **Magic** when Grand Prix–Detroit rolled around in 2003. When his friend and noted deck builder Brian Kowal handed him a powerful red-white Astral Slide deck for the event, Bob's gamer instincts took over and he powered his way to the championship trophy. Using his cyclers in conjunction with [Astral Slide](https://web.archive.org/web/20090924000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Astral+Slide), he held off all comers. [Read more about the event in the official event coverage archives](/en/articles/event-coverage/2003/07-12-live-coverage-of-2003-grand-prix-detroit).
