StarCityGames.com columnist Sean McKeown writes a column in which he helps critique Commander decks that readers send him. Recently, he took a look at a Commander deck featuring the *Champions of Kamigawa* legendary creature [Konda, Lord of Eiganjo](https://web.archive.org/web/20111201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Konda%2C+Lord+of+Eiganjo). This oddball legend is one you don't see played often, but he works well with all kinds of battlefield-wiping effects like [Planar Cleansing](https://web.archive.org/web/20111201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Planar+Cleansing) and [Worldslayer](https://web.archive.org/web/20111201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Worldslayer). It's hard enough to deal with an indestructible creature once. How about over and over again? Use this deck for some inspiration, and check out [Sean's article](http://www.starcitygames.com/magic/commander/23171_Dear_Azami_Storming_Eiganjo_Castle.html) for more about the deck!
