Since the release of Alpha, when the first player cast Dark Ritual into Hypnotic Specter, the discard tribe has been a staple member of the Multiverse. Here at Daily Decks, we thought it'd be fun to put together a deck that answered the question "What happens if you put the best discard Specters in to one deck?" Hyppie is joined by Hollow, Abyssal, Shimian, and Needle Specter amongst others with Dark Ritual to speed your flying, hand ravaging horde to the table and Doom Blade to clean up anything that hits the battlefield first.
