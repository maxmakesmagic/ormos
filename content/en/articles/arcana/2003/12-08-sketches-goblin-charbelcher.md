Welcome to another edition of [Sketches](https://web.archive.org/web/20031208000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/fullarchive&tablefilter=sketches:). This week we take an interactive look at **[Goblin Charbelcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20031208000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Charbelcher)**, the damage-chucking artifact from *Mirrodin*. The piece was painted by **Stephen Tappin**, who also illustrated [Auriok Transfixer](https://web.archive.org/web/20031208000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auriok+Transfixer) and [Nuisance Engine](https://web.archive.org/web/20031208000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nuisance+Engine) in the *Mirrodin* set.

### 1\. Art Description

Here are the instructions that were given to Stephen for the piece:

> "Color: Red-aligned Artifact  
>  Location: Generic  
>  Action: Show a sort of magical mortar tube. The weapon fires spell energy that appears as multicolored balls of fire and spark.  
>  Focus: The spell-mortar weapon  
>  Mood: FOOM!"

### 2\. Sketch

Mr. Tappin came up with this initial sketch:

FOOM indeed. We have the mortar-like artifact poised on metal legs, standing against a generic background of spiky cone structures. This is immediately recognizable as close to the final art of [Goblin Charbelcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20031208000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Charbelcher), but the twist comes when Stephen adds color to the illustration.

### 3\. Final Art

Below is a color sketch subsequently submitted by Mr. Tappin for the project. The art team didn't like the bluish tones of the piece, since the Charbelcher's ability associates it with Mountains and therefore red. Blue was therefore right out. But since it will turn out that the final art is so close in composition to this color sketch, we thought it'd be a good time to whip out the Sketches Flip-Book technology again to compare it with the final art.
