What Is the *Magic Online* Bug Blog?

While the [Known Issues List](https://web.archive.org/web/20150129000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-known-issues) is simply that—a running list of issues we expect players to encounter most often—this accompanying “Bug Blog” is the list’s companion. Here you’ll find updates regarding emergent new issues, details around issue statuses when applicable, known issue workarounds, and a running tally of Known Issues List updates. The Bug Blog will be updated each Thursday, in conjunction with the [Known Issues List](https://web.archive.org/web/20150129000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-known-issues) on the same day.

**Known Issues List Updates**

Lots of updates this week in preparation for the *Magic Online* *Fate Reforged* Prerelease, beginning Friday, January 30. Check out the list below for a break-down of what's new since last week's Bug Blog. Refer to the [Known Issues List](https://web.archive.org/web/20150129000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-known-issues) for the most current *Magic Online* Known Issues.

* * *

**Fixed During the Wednesday, January 28 *Magic Online* Client Update**

**Game Play**

*   Exodus | Fixed a crash that was caused when Fade Away was cast while there were multiple creatures on the battlefield
*   General | Fixed an issue that caused cards that have multiple targets to select, such as [Arc Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20150129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Lightning) or [Inferno Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20150129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inferno+Titan), have the 'OK' improperly grayed out preventing users from being able to proceed
*   General | Fixed an issue that caused choices such as shuffling with Ponder to incorrectly register the incorrect response from the player
*   KTK | You can now select opponent’s creatures while casting [Duneblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20150129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duneblast) even if you don’t control a creature
*   MMA | [Deepcavern Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20150129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deepcavern+Imp) no longer forces the player to pay Echo

**Client**

*   Duel Scene | We have redone the prompt that player’s receive after a game ends to accurately reflect who has won the game and/or match

* * *

**Newly Added Known Issues as of Wednesday, January 28**

**Game Play**

*   FRF | Face-down manifested cards that change controllers have incorrect art assets when viewed by the new controller
*   FRF | If a manifested creature has been granted a keyword ability that the face up creature naturally has, it will not properly display blue text to the controller indicating that the manifested creature also has that ability. For example, if you have a face-down manifested Flying Men then Lightform will not give blue rules text indicating 'Flying' on a the face-down side of the card. Even lacking the blue text, the creature will have flying properly.
*   FRF | Two-colored land textbox backgrounds only display one color
