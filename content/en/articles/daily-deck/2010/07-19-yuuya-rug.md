While Katsuhiro Mori was the eventual champion, Pro Tour Player of the Year Yuuya Watanabe cooked up an innovative deck for the 2010 [Japan National Championship](/en/events/2010/jpnat10/mori-defeats-ikeda-for-japan-title). The star opted to hybridize a blue-red-green deck to utilize Siege-Gang Commander and Jace, the Mind Sculptor. With the addition of ***Magic** 2011* to the format, heralding the exit of ***Magic** 2010* in the near future, what changes do you think the deck would benefit from for the new world of Standard?
