*How many decks do you have on hand at any given time, and what sorts?* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e0/0c/e00c2900e4532fed44efdbc0223f486ee1518c0cf594bb289867319eeda0f043.jpg)

**Kelly Digges:** Would "lots" cover it? No? Okay, let's see...

There are usually one or two Winston drafts still kicking around the house because my wife and I think there might still be a few drops of fun to be squeezed out of them, but that doesn't really count as decks "on hand." More like "not yet disassembled."

Moving on to the 60-card decks, we keep a huge number of them around. We don't play a ton of casual multiplayer with 60-card decks, but when we do, it's often with out-of-town friends who don't maintain decks of their own or don't have them handy. That means we're often loaning out all the decks, and over a weekend of casual **Magic**, seeing the same decks over and over gets old. That's why we've got one of each *Planechase* deck, one of each *Archenemy* deck, some (most?) of the *Duel Decks*, and something like a dozen of our own 60-card concoctions, mostly sleeved up, all ready to go. So let's say... twenty-five decks in the 60-card category, give or take.

Then there are the Commander decks. These take up a bit more space at 100 cards, tend to be heavy on rares, and are all home brews by necessity (at least until June's [***Magic: The Gathering** Commander*](/en/articles/arcana/2011/04-06-commander-decks-2) release). None of that has stopped us from amassing an enormous pile of Commander decks. My wife has at least four, her crown jewel being a frequently updated, carefully pruned Mayael the Anima deck with *extremely* strict standards for inclusion. I have seven in active rotation (although I'm prohibited from playing at least two of those, for very good reasons), one that I stripped some spare parts out of but could restore pretty easily, and another six that are finished and playable but not quite ready for primetime without more work.

So that's just shy of forty-five decks in the house between the two of us. But I don't say this to brag; on the contrary, I think I might have some sort of problem. We're outnumbered. Surrounded. Running out of shelf space. Send help!
