2471 players arrived at Grand Prix Chiba to partake in eight rounds of Core Set 2013 Sealed Deck. All players who scored 6-2 or better advanced to Round 9, where they played one additional round of Sealed Deck. When all was said and done, a total of 461 would return tomorrow for six rounds of Booster Draft, while there were a total of eight 9-0 players.

These players were Naoya Nanba, Noriyuki Hagiwara, Kazuyuki Takimura, Yusuke Arai, Kazushige Shimamura, Hiroaki Mukai, Jun Takahashi, and Shozoburo Notake! Let’s take a look at their 9-0 decklists!
