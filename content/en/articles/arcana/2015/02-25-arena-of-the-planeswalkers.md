Psst, hey, want to see something cool? I mean, really cool? And also adorably tiny and fierce?

Check this out:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/MTG_GamePices.jpg)

Some of you may know what we're getting at already. Some of you may have even been among the lucky few to see early versions of, well, of this:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/Arena_PW_logo.png)

*Magic: The Gathering Arena of the Planeswalkers* is the *Magic: The Gathering* strategy board game we told you about [way back when](/en/articles/feature/2014/10-16-announcing-the-magic-the-gathering-strategy-board-game). It's a game where you play as one of Gideon, Jace, Liliana, Chandra, or Nissa, summoning armies and casting game-changing spells to outmaneuver your opponents. The goal is to be the last Planeswalker standing.

Created for two to five players, *Magic: The Gathering Arena of the Planeswalkers* is a standalone game set in the world of *Magic*, but with a whole different gaming experience for you to explore. The art, themes, and characters will all feel very familiar as you try to lead one of the Planeswalkers to victory.

*Magic: The Gathering Arena of the Planeswalkers* is scheduled for release in Summer 2015, and we'll be sure to keep you updated as we get closer to the release.
