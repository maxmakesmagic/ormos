Merfolk of the Pearl Trident was the very first member of the Merfolk family, all the way back in Alpha. Since then the combination of aggressive, cheap blue creatures backed by counterspells and card draw has been a powerful strategy, dubbed "Fish" in the game's early days. It proved successful once again for Alex Bertoncini during the Legacy portion of the StarCityGames.com Open in Memphis, Tennessee this past weekend.
