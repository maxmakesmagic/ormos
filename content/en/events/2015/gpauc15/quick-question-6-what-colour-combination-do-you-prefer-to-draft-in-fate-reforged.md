# What colour combination do you prefer to draft in *Fate Reforged*?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ2_Wilfy_Horig.jpg)  
**Wilfy Horig:** Black-X Control.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ2_Hau-Shan_Huang.jpg)  
**Huang Hao-shan:** White-Black or Red-White.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ2_Joseph_Sclauzero.jpg)  
**Joseph Sclauzero:** Blue-Red.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ2_Shuhei_Nakamura.jpg)  
**Shuhei Nakamura:** Blue-Red or White-Black.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ2_Jason_Chung.jpg)  
**Jason Chung:** Red-X.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpauc15/QQ2_Isaac_Egan.jpg)  
**Isaac Egan:** All five colours!
