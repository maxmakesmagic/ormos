### BOOSTER PACKS

Each *Alliances* booster pack includes 12 cards to help customize and improve your deck, whatever way you play.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0f/ac/0facb6068747ba610a4a0481a39bcb63fb1d6b45db7cdd655480a26f2f76313f.png)*Alliances* continues several themes introduced by *Ice Age*. With Cantrips, Cumulative Upkeep, and Pitch Spells, this 199-card set was (and remains) a popular expansion. The common cards in this set were each printed in two different versions, each with its own unique art.

**Se**t** Name:** *Alliances*  
**Block:** Set 2 of 3 of the *Ice Age* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 144 (plus 55 art variants)

**Languages:** English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish

**Design Team:** Skaff Elias (lead), Jim Lin, Chris Page, Dave Petty

**Development Team:** Charlie Catripino (lead), Skaff Elias, William Jockusch, Joel Mick, Bill Rose, Paul Peterson, Mark Rosewater
