Hey! Let's talk [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20100506000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter)s! They've been around almost as long as **Magic**, having appeared originally in *Antiquities*, when Amy Weber provided this basic blueprint.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d7/b9/d7b9217256882f06696b6ab79ed090990df94ece9c2351bf1916c57fb49691a8.jpg)

[Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20100506000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Ornithopter) | Art by [Amy Weber](https://web.archive.org/web/20100506000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Amy+Weber%22%5D)

That remained the standard look for [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20100506000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter)s from *Fourth Edition* through *Classic Sixth Edition*. Then **Magic** had to live without [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20100506000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter)s until *Mirrodin* brought Dana Knutson's version to life.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ad/50/ad50e00958be5adbe4a05f06eb8304a743bbd3a8e30a7b37bf29ad81ee8808b6.jpg)

[Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20100506000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Ornithopter) | Art by [Dana Knutson](https://web.archive.org/web/20100506000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Dana+Knutson%22%5D)

With *Ninth Edition*, Dana Knutson's [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20100506000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter) was in the core set, staying there through *Tenth Edition*. And then ***Magic** 2010* brought the most recent look. Franz Vohwinkel, take it away!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/05/f5/05f572f3a611a3a4a8a3c7a98b2e97261878c2a14bb2ca9b64bf5056888d967e.jpg)

[Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20100506000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Ornithopter) | Art by [Franz Vohwinkel](https://web.archive.org/web/20100506000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Franz+Vohwinkel%22%5D)

Who knows what [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20100506000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter)s the future of **Magic** might hold?
