L[ast Friday](/en/articles/latestdevelopments/2010/06-04-core-samples), Tom LaPille asked three questions at the end of Latest Developments. Well, he technically asked four, but the last three are the ones we're talking about today:

*   Precisely eight cards have appeared in every core set from *Alpha* through *Magic 2010*. How many of these eight do you think will be in *Magic 2011*?
*   Which of the eight cards are you most confident will be IN *Magic 2011*?
*   Which of the eight cards are you most confident will be OUT of *Magic 2011*?

Before we get into the answers (by which I mean both the answers you gave and the real answers, let's talk about the history of these eight cards, much of which is borrowed from [this Mark Rosewater article](/en/articles/makingmagic/2005/07-25-outwit-outplay-outlast-11) from 2005.

There were 283 cards in *Alpha*. Only 250 of those cards made it into *Revised*. We're down to eight cards that have been in every core set. Before we list them, here's the round-by-round breakdown.

>  ***Alpha*** : 283  
> ***Revised***: 250  
> ***Fourth Edition***: 211  
> ***Fifth Edition***: 151  
> ***Sixth Edition***: 79  
> ***Seventh Edition***: 60  
> ***Eighth Edition***: 40  
> ***Ninth Edition***: 24  
> ***Tenth Edition***: 16  
> ***Magic 2010***: 8

And here are the eight cards:

> Air Elemental  
>  Bog Wraith  
>  Drudge Skeletons  
>  Giant Growth  
>  Giant Spider  
>  Howling Mine  
>  Nightmare  
>  Rod of Ruin

So! Let's get some results!
