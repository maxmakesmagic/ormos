Looking for a use for Nyxathid? Look no further! Alongside Rotting Rats and Blightning the 7/7 shines in this *Shards of Alara* Block Constructed deck from Yliajo. It recently managed a Top 8 in a Premier Event on **Magic Online**, beating down opponents by keeping their hand size reduced and pounding through the ground with the aforementioned Nyxathid. What does *Alara Reborn* add to the deck? Find out by picking up the latest set from your favorite TCG!
