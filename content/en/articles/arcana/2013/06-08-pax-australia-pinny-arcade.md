At PAX East this year, the folks over at Penny Arcade introduced "Pinny Arcade" which is their pin trading program at their conventions. We were proud to be [among the first batch](/en/articles/arcana/2013/03-14-magic-at-pax-east) of pins created, with a Mike Krahulik drawn Chandra head.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/93/58/9358fdb9e3c77795efff1b6f34901d67a3706c510d03b52514f78b1bf4b5f034.jpg) 

  

Part of Pinny Arcade is that the pins are limited edition and only produced for the specific show they were released at. So the above pin was only available at the 2013 PAX East in Boston.

But this year Penny Arcade is also launching [PAX Australia in Melbourne](http://aus.paxsite.com/) from July 19-21, 2013. And guess what, we've got another awesome Pinny Arcade pin!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7f/aa/7faac9ab34d6041de2db240fee675f41d00f72fe1ea5ed478a9cb19c57e38d73.jpg) 

  

To see what other pins will be available at PAX Australia check out [this blog post over on Penny Arcade](http://www.penny-arcade.com/2013/06/05/pinny-arcade2). We'll be releasing more information about our plans at PAX Australia in the coming weeks, if you're going to be there be sure to check back!
