# Fate Reforged's Impact on Standard

We found a couple of well-known players and asked them to describe the impact of *Fate Reforged* on the Standard format. Here's what they had to say... 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsev15/qq-juza.jpg)  
**Martin Jůza:** There's a bunch of cool new cards. I really like where Standard is at right now. You can basically come up with a new deck every week. You can brew, you can innovate on sideboard cards, metagame... It's awesome! 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsev15/qq-anteri.jpg)  
**Fabrizio Anteri:** I think it's more midrange-dominated now. 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsev15/qq-lemoine.jpg)  
**Vincent Lemoine:** *Fate Reforged* gave us some really good cards. But they're more like good additions for existing archetypes than sparking any new ones. Except for the revival of Blue/Black Control maybe. 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsev15/qq-hatto.jpg)  
**Steve Hatto:** It's easier now for aggressive decks to fill holes in their mana curve, so we get to see a lot more two-color decks.
