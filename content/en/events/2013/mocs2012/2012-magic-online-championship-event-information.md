The **Magic Online** Championship features sixteen players battling for a share of more than $100,000 in cash prizes in the Standard Constructed, Cube Draft, and *Gatecrash* Sealed Deck formats. Tune in for the live webcast starting Friday morning for complete access to the **Magic Online** Championship: live match coverage, player interviews, and much more!

Increased to a field of sixteen finalists for 2012, the **Magic Online** Championship Series (MOCS) provided more chances than ever for players to earn an invitation to the **Magic Online** Championship. The sixteen 2012 **Magic Online** Championship finalists include: 

*   The defending 2011 **Magic Online** champion, Reid Duke
*   Twelve individual **Magic Online** Championship Series season winners
*   The MOCS Player-of-the-Year Championship winner
*   Two MOCS Last-Chance Qualifier winners

The 2012 **Magic Online** Championship is a two-day invitation-only [Standard](https://web.archive.org/web/20130708023701/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Resources.aspx?x=judge/resources/sfrstandard), Cube Draft, and *Gatecrash* Sealed Deck format event with Standard Top 2. 

**Venue Information**  
 PAX East  
 Boston Convention and Exhibition Center  
 Hall C  
 415 Summer Street  
 Boston, MA 02210  

**Format**  
 

*   [Standard](https://web.archive.org/web/20130708023701/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Resources.aspx?x=judge/resources/sfrstandard), Cube Draft, and *Gatecrash* Sealed Deck
*   [Cube Draft card list](/en/events/2013/mocs2012cubelist/2012-magic-online-championship-cube-draft-card-list)

**Tournament Length**  
 

*   Day One will consist of three rounds of Cube Draft followed by four rounds of Sealed Deck
*   Day Two will consist of four rounds of Standard, followed by a cut to the Top 2
*   The Top 2 Finals will be Standard format. Players are required to use the same Standard deck as on Day One.
*   All matches will be best two of three.

**Tournament Scoring** 

The **Magic Online** Championship features three formats, each of which award points based on the total number of match wins for that format. At the end of the third format, the finalists' points for each format will be totaled and the two players with the highest totals will square off in the Finals to determine the 2012 **Magic Online** champion. DCI tie-breakers will be aggregated across all three tournaments and used if needed.
