It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6)! Since we're now in June, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

![Rhox War Monk](https://i.ormos.uk/68/9b/689b14f30ff0ed21dc8949b23756676ac01ea62ec5d2d267d8ad7f64ee3bd025.jpg "Rhox War Monk") 

  

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards feature the FNM logo and many have original flavor text and updated wordings.
