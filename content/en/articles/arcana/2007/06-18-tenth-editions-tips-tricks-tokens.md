At [**Magic** Game Day](/en/events/2007/07gameday/magic-the-gathering-worldwide-game-day) you'll see that there are sixteen thin, rectangular objects inside every *Tenth Edition* booster pack: fifteen **Magic** cards, and a bonus card.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0d/6c/0d6c4e05ba5d38c9fafa5d3cfd360148cfbf0ac61e110bff5682e54915c8fa6e.jpg)

Just like the usual fifteen, these bonus cards are random; you might get any one of them every time you rip a pack. Some of the bonus cards are informational "tips & tricks" cards that explain more about **Magic.** Some of them spill the beans on some of the combos available in the *Tenth Edition* set.

And still others are all-new token cards with original art by **Magic**'s top artists. Each of these tokens is usable with one or more token-generating cards in the *Tenth* set.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8a/a6/8aa68017768a8ff134f22ddebcb2b4b67eac1236336680f6aa3cef8d3572c145.jpg)  
 *Three of the Tenth Edition token cards* 

Rip open a booster of *Tenth*, get some black-bordered goodness. Maybe open a tips & tricks card for your buddy who's just learning. And maybe crack a token card for yourself. Will you and your friend have a great time at [**Magic** Game Day](/en/events/2007/07gameday/magic-the-gathering-worldwide-game-day)? The odds are excellent.
