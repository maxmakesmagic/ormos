# What is the best way of dealing with Deathmist Raptor?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_QQ-ShotaYasooka.jpg)  
**Pro Tour *Dragons of Tarkir* finalist, Shouta Yasooka:** [Perilous Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Perilous+Vault), it's a colorless way to handle them.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_QQYuuyaTakahashi.jpg)  
**Three-time Grand Prix Champion Yuuta Takahashi:** [Anafenza, the Foremost](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anafenza%2C+the+Foremost) and [Abzan Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Charm).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_QQTeruyaKakumae.jpg)  
**Two-time Grand Prix Champion, Teruya Kakumae:** My plan against them is [Hornet Nest](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hornet+Nest).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_QQShuheiNakamura.jpg)  
**Hall of Famer, Shuhei Nakamura:** [Ashiok, Nightmare Weaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashiok%2C+Nightmare+Weaver).
