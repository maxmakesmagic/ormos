**The Year of Modern Flashbacks Add *Guildpact***

It's 2016: The Year of Modern Flashbacks! If you want to build up a collection for Modern, these Flashback Drafts add everything you draft to your collection once the event is over. Even if Modern isn't your thing, you can enjoy a leisurely draft through the planes of the past with rotating formats each week!

*Ravnica*-*Ravnica*-*Guildpact* Flashback Drafts begin after the Wednesday, March 23 downtime and run until Wednesday, March 30, at 5:00 a.m. PT (UTC-7), and are followed up by full *Ravnica* Block Flashbacks. Get the full schedule [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/12-14-year-of-modern-flashbacks) and mark your calendar for the formats you're most excited about!

**Build Notes**

**General**

*   Duel Scene | Players should no longer experience lag when tapping cards.
*   Cardset | The mana abilities of [Commander's Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Commander%27s+Sphere) and [Command Tower](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Command+Tower) once again work as expected.
*   Cardset | [Myr Superion](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Superion) can again no longer be cast with mana from noncreature sources.
*   Cardset | [Lightning Storm](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Storm)’s activated ability once again works as expected.
*   Duel Scene | Creatures tapped to help cast a spell with convoke no longer retain a green highlight after the spell is cast.
*   Cardset | [Containment Priest](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Containment+Priest) now works as expected. Notably, it will no longer exile itself if it enters the battlefield without being cast, nor will it exile a double-faced *Magic Origins* creature/planeswalker attempting to return to the battlefield as a planeswalker.
*   Duel Scene | Activated abilities that require players to discard cards at random, such as those found on [Ogre Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ogre+Shaman) and [Barbarian Bully](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barbarian+Bully), now actually require players to discard cards at random.
*   Cardset | The targeting comments on [Rolling Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rolling+Thunder) and [Electrolyze](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrolyze) are now more clear.
*   Cardset | [Patrician’s Scorn](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Patrician%E2%80%99s+Scorn) can once again be cast for its alternative cost.
*   Cardset | Players can once again use exiled split cards with [Jester’s Scepter](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jester%E2%80%99s+Scepter) to counter an opponent’s split card.
*   Cardset | [Gaze of the Gorgon](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gaze+of+the+Gorgon) now properly destroys creatures again.
*   Cardset | Players whose opening hand contains [Gemstone Caverns](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gemstone+Caverns) and who are not playing first can once again choose to have [Gemstone Caverns](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gemstone+Caverns) start the game on the battlefield with a luck counter.
*   Cardset | [Mizzix of the Izmagnus](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzix+of+the+Izmagnus) no longer causes spells with a variable in their mana cost to sometimes cost an incorrect amount of mana.
*   Cardset | Players must once again pay the proper amount of mana to activate the abilities of [Candelabra of Tawnos](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Candelabra+of+Tawnos) and [Magus of the Candelabra](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magus+of+the+Candelabra).
*   Cardset | [Elemental Resonance](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elemental+Resonance) now allows players to properly choose a color of mana for each hybrid symbol in the enchanted creature's cost.
*   Cardset | [Balduvian Fallen](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balduvian+Fallen)’s triggered ability now correctly increases its power when its controller pays its cumulative upkeep cost with either black or red mana.
*   Cardset | Spells cast for a bestow cost are no longer incorrectly affected by spells or abilities that reduce the cost of creature spells.
*   Cardset | When paying a cost that involves the snow mana symbol, mana from snow permanents is no longer removed from the player's mana pool without being applied to pay the cost.
*   Cardset | Players who control [Wort, the Raidmother](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wort%2C+the+Raidmother) and cast [Burn at the Stake](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burn+at+the+Stake) or [Firecat Blitz](https://web.archive.org/web/20160322000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firecat+Blitz) may no longer pay the spell's conspire cost without tapping creatures.
*   Duel Scene | Several bugs not listed elsewhere that caused crashes have been resolved, including a bug involving a player conceding while another player is making a mulligan decision.
