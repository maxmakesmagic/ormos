The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know for today, January 20, 2016. Today's Update is brought to you by [Frank Karsten's super math](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/magic-math-oath-of-the-gatewatch/) and [the year 2004](#2004).

# Today's Must See

1.  [MTG Lore: Nissa's Fate](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8TdkaF1qTQ) | SeibenMTG

Behind on the *Battle for Zendikar* and *Oath of the Gatewatch* story? SeibenMTG catches you up on Nissa's story so far as told in [Official *Magic* Fiction](/en/articles/columns/undated/magic-story-archive). Not behind on the story? Still worth it for the recap and his storytelling voice.

# After That, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [MtA Episode 156: Let's Get Wasted](http://www.magictheamateuring.com/mta-episode-156-lets-get-wasted/)| *Magic* the Amateuring | Maria Bartholdi and Meghan Wolff

The *Magic* the Amateuring crew podcasts *live* from their Prerelease over the weekend. There's also something about koalas. I don't know. Just listen.

1.  [Modern Flashback Series: *Fifth Dawn*](http://puremtgo.com/articles/modern-flashback-series-fifth-dawn) | PureMTGO | Vincent Borchardt

PureMTGO has been all over the [Year of Modern Flashbacks](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/12-14-year-of-modern-flashbacks) on *Magic Online*, and their [latest batch of content](http://puremtgo.com/) parties like it's 2004 with multiple articles on *Mirrodin*-*Darksteel*-*Fifth Dawn* drafting.

1.  [*Oath of the Gatewatch* Set Review: Red and Multicolor](http://magic.tcgplayer.com/db/article.asp?ID=13074&writer=Conley+Woods) | TCGPlayer | Conley Woods

[Luis Scott-Vargas](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/oath-of-the-gatewatch-constructed-set-review-blue/) doesn't get to have all of the set-review glory. Conley Woods is running through the cards in *Oath of the Gatewatch* as well, and today he's on red and multicolor.
