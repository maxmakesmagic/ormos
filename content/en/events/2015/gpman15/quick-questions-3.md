# Did you have a card you wanted to include in your maindeck but didn't?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Christian-Calcano.jpg)  
**Christian Calcano:** I've been talking to Raymond Price Jr. and Oliver Tomajko, a fourteen-year-old in New York who helped build my decklist for the last Pro Tour. We cut [Soul of Innistrad](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soul+of+Innistrad) and moved [Eidolon of Blossoms](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eidolon+of+Blossoms) to the sideboard.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Terry-Soh.jpg)  
**Terry Soh:** I am playing [Read the Bones](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Read+the+Bones), one maindeck and three in the sideboard. If I could have or wanted a 61st card, it would be a second [Read the Bones](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Read+the+Bones).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Jelger.jpg)  
**Jelger Wiegersma:** I wanted a third [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall) but cut it for a [Sorin, Solemn Visitor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorin%2C+Solemn+Visitor).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Raymond-Tan.jpg)  
**Raymond Tan:**[Barrage of Boulders](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barrage+of+Boulders), but I didn't have enough time to test it out.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Martin-Juza.jpg)![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Ivan-Floch.jpg)  
**Martin Jůza and Ivan Floch:** We didn't have a 61st card. We hit 74 cards and felt we didn't need number 75.
