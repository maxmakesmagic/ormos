S*aviors of Kamigawa* is finally in stores -- so today we take a moment to look back. We [introduced](/en/articles/arcana/2004/04-08-announcement-the-kamigawa-block-2) Kamigawa as "a world that waged a brutal war against its own gods." Three sets and over a year later, we've seen twenty short stories set in the context of that struggle between Kamigawa's mortals and kami. 

Did you enjoy the "Meet the *Champions/Betrayers/Saviors*" vignettes on **magicthegathering.com**? Ever wish you could see one big list of all those legend-riffic vignettes in one place?

Well, [now you can](https://web.archive.org/web/20080509092726/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/kamigawa/vignettes).
