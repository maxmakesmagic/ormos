The following cards are banned or restricted in the listed **Magic Online** formats as of the August 26 downtime.

### 100 Card Singleton

*   [Balance](https://web.archive.org/web/20090820000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balance) is banned.
*   [Strip Mine](https://web.archive.org/web/20090820000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strip+Mine) is banned.
*   [Tinker](https://web.archive.org/web/20090820000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tinker) is banned.

### Classic

*   [Balance](https://web.archive.org/web/20090820000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balance) is restricted.
*   [Channel](https://web.archive.org/web/20090820000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Channel) is restricted.
*   [Strip Mine](https://web.archive.org/web/20090820000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strip+Mine) is restricted.
*   [Tinker](https://web.archive.org/web/20090820000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tinker) is restricted.

### Commander

*   [Balance](https://web.archive.org/web/20090820000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balance) is banned.
*   [Karakas](https://web.archive.org/web/20090820000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karakas) is banned.
*   [Tinker](https://web.archive.org/web/20090820000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tinker) is banned.

### Prismatic

*   [Balance](https://web.archive.org/web/20090820000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balance) is banned.
*   [Grim Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20090820000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Tutor) is banned.
*   [Strip Mine](https://web.archive.org/web/20090820000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strip+Mine) is banned.
*   [Tinker](https://web.archive.org/web/20090820000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tinker) is banned.
