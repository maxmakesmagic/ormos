Last weekend saw two Grand Prix in North America: [Mexico City](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-mexico-city-2015) and [San Jose](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-san-jose-2015). The California event featured Team Limited and saw Paul Rietzl, Matt Sperling, and David Williams fighting to repeat their Grand Prix San Jose Team Limited victory. It was not to be, though, as they fell in the finals to Luis Scott-Vargas, Paul Cheon, and Eric Froehlich, who dubbed themselves "the Pork Bun Oath." In Mexico, GP Top 8 regular Pascal Maynard fought his way to victory in an exciting finals match that gathered quite the crowd. Congratulations to Luis, Paul (Paul Paul Paul), Eric, and Pascal!

Today (if you're reading this the day the article goes live), more than 400 of the world's best players are assembled in Washington, DC, to do battle at Pro Tour *Fate Reforged*. Make sure to check out [the full coverage, here](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-fate-reforged)!

If you dream of Pro Tour glory yourself, one way to get there is with Pro Tour Qualifiers (PTQs). The season for Pro Tour *Dragons of Tarkir* feature Standard and is happening now! So [find a PTQ near you](https://web.archive.org/web/20150206000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/protour/qualifierlist)!
