With Modern PTQ season underway, plenty of players are innovating new ways to combine the thousands of Modern legal cards at their disposal. *Magic Online* user coughl has found a deck that is capable of doing something few other Modern decks are capable of.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Geist%20of%20Saint%20Traft)](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geist%20of%20Saint%20Traft)

Win on the third turn.

[Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch) + [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) is one of the defining combinations in Modern, but is often thought about as a turn-four combo. After all, [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) costs four mana. However, this deck features [Noble Hierarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noble+Hierarch) and [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise) to accelerate the combo by a full turn. Like many great Modern combo decks, this deck comes with a potent backup plan—[Geist of Saint Traft](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geist+of+Saint+Traft). With the mana accelerants in this deck, a turn-two [Geist of Saint Traft](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geist+of+Saint+Traft) provides a potent threat, and often a flurry of [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt)s, [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand)s, and [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage)s is enough for [Geist of Saint Traft](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geist+of+Saint+Traft) to attack for 20 damage quickly. Of course, when the opponent commits heavily to stopping [Geist of Saint Traft](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geist+of+Saint+Traft), a [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch) or [Pestermite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pestermite) comes down and picks up a [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140702000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin), ending the game in a hurry.
