Our target is New Phyrexia, formerly known as Mirrodin. An entire plane rebuilt from the inside out, filled with predators, hazards, and traps. Everything there will kill you, compleat you—or both—given half a chance. This should be a walk in the park, right? You'd better do your homework, or you may find yourself Compleatly Outmatched!

![Compleatly Outmatched Historic Brawl event festival, February 7–13](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/one_brawl_9p5efrjxdphk.jpg) 

Learn where the real power lies with five different Historic Brawl decks, each helmed by a dangerous foe who can be found defending (or also attempting to conquer) New Phyrexia. As you win, you'll earn borderless planeswalker manga card styles from *Phyrexia: All Will Be One*, and there are ten card styles to collect:
