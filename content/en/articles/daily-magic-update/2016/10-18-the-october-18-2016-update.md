Because I'm thrilled to be back writing on the internet in your general direction. You complete me. You had me at hello. You've got something on your nose.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Temur Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20161019090623/http://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/ptkld/21-23-point-standard-decklists-2016-10-16)

Want to go big? Like, really big? Oliver Polak-Rottmann did so to the tune of a 7-3 record in Standard at Pro Tour *Kaladesh* last weekend with [Metalwork Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20161018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metalwork+Colossus), a giant creature that Polak-Rottmann could, with some frequency, cast for free or very close to it.

The deck uses a variety of useful artifacts to gum up the board and, just as importantly, reduce the cost of [Metalwork Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20161018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metalwork+Colossus). From there, [Sanctum of Ugin](https://web.archive.org/web/20161018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sanctum+of+Ugin) can be sacrificed to find another colossus or an [Elder Deep-Fiend](https://web.archive.org/web/20161018000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elder+Deep-Fiend), as the situation calls for it.

The deck is a *Kaladesh* invention through and through, and that inventiveness will likely be coming to a Friday Night *Magic* near you very soon.
