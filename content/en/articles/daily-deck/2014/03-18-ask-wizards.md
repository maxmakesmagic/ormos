Ask Wizards is a weekly feature that allows you to ask us questions! If you'd like to submit your question please email it to AskWizards@wizards.com. We aren't able to answer every question we receive but if your question is good then it might show up in the coming weeks! 

  

* * *

  

 **Q: What interesting, infinite combos are there in Standard today? While they might not be competitive I think that they are the most fun to construct and play with. —Shiftrex**

 **A: Mike McArtor, copy editor for DailyMTG:** 

Here are a few to chew on:

Shortly after *Theros* released, Gavin Verhey [presented one in ReConstructed](https://web.archive.org/web/20140318000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/rc/266). 

Later, he wrote about [another one](https://web.archive.org/web/20140318000000/https://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/rc/274)! That same week, Mike Cannon presented two different takes [on that idea as well](https://web.archive.org/web/20140318000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/ftl/274). It's apparently a popular combo. 

Jacob Van Lunen got in on the fun as well, [once *Born of the Gods* released](https://web.archive.org/web/20140318000000/https://archive.wizards.com/Magic/magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/pr/284). 

Enjoy!
