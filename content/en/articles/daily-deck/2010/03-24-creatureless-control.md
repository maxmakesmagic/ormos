Tired of all those pesky Elves and Thopter tokens in Extended? Zack Shaffner came up with an elegant solution: Night of Souls' Betrayal. The enchantment shuts down any and all 1/1s automatically. Zack packed the rest of his deck with counters and spot removal. To win? Pick your choice of two of the biggest and/or baddest planeswalkers who've ever roamed the planes: Nicol Bolas, Planeswalker or Jace, the Mind Sculptor. The creative concoction was good enough to earn Zack a Top 8 at a recent PTQ!
