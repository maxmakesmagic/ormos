What Is the *Magic Online* Bug Blog?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/mtgo/icon_bugblog.jpg)

While the [Known Issues List](https://web.archive.org/web/20141204000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-known-issues) is simply that—a running list of issues we expect players to encounter most often—this accompanying “Bug Blog” is the list’s companion. Here you’ll find updates regarding emergent new issues, details around issue statuses when applicable, known issue workarounds, and a running tally of Known Issues List updates. The Bug Blog will be updated each Thursday, in conjunction with the [Known Issues List](https://web.archive.org/web/20141204000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-known-issues) on the same day.

Known Issues List Updates

This week, many issues that were scheduled previously (as well as a few that weren't scheduled) had fixes deployed in the *Magic Online* update on Wednesday, December 3. Tune into the Bug Blog next week for an update on some of the issues we're targeting for the next *Magic Online* deployment.

**Newly Fixed during the Wednesday, December 3 *Magic Online* Client Update**

**Game Play**

*   C14 | Artifact creature tokens created by C14 cards show no art when zoomed or in the preview pane
*   C14 | [Nahiri, the Lithomancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nahiri%2C+the+Lithomancer)'s first ability attaches the equipment to previous tokens if they exist
*   C14 | Offerings display blue text identifying a chosen opponent before it is chosen. The cards function correctly.
*   C14 | [Song of the Dryad](https://web.archive.org/web/20141204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Song+of+the+Dryad) enchanting another Aura appears incorrectly. The card functions correctly.
*   General | A copy of a spell that divides damage among multiple targets doesn't display the distribution among the copy's targets
*   General | [Cruel Ultimatum](https://web.archive.org/web/20141204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cruel+Ultimatum)'s resolution can crash the game, causing it to restart
*   General | Lands in M15 frames that produce mana of two differnet colors have the wrong background colors in the rules text box
*   General | [Trinisphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20141204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trinisphere)'s effect does not apply to spells with X in the mana cost
*   General | Various library searching cards hang the game if there is nothing legal to find in the library

**Client**

*   Collection | Rarity filter works inconsistently
*   Collection | Selecting different versions of a card and using the context menu to "Add 1 to Deck" only adds one card, not one of each version
*   Collection | Sorting columns in list view prevents quantity from being displayed correctly. Changing to card view will show correct quantities.
*   Duel Scene | The window for [Thoughtsieze](https://web.archive.org/web/20141204000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtsieze) (and other spells or effects that allow selection from a subset of revealed cards) is not sized correctly to show all cards. Scrolling, resizing the window, or checking the game log will allow players to see all the cards revealed.
*   Event Details | Row color does not always update to show completed matches correctly
*   Events | Decks saved during limited deck building occasionally fail to load and deliver a "No decks available matching your pool" message
*   Events | In limited deckbuilding, decks sometimes fail to show all cards which can prevent deck submission
*   Events | Occasionally cards can become visually duplicated in your sideboard and prevent deck submission
*   Events | Scheduled events that are not four rounds show up as Daily (4-round) in the Play Lobby

**Server**

*   Limited Deckbuilding | Submit button is greyed out even though deck is legal
*   Play Lobby | Legacy Cube is just named "Cube"
