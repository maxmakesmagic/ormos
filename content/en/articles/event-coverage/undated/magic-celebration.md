**On Sept. 10, 2011, go to a participating game store near you, where, depending on your gaming platform you can reap one of the following rewards: †** 

> *   **Either Steam® or Xbox LIVE® Arcade:** You’ll get a special code for an exclusive Duels of the Planeswalkers 2012 digital item – a Dragon picture pack!

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/promotional/MagicCelebration/MagicCelebration_InsidePage_DragonPack.jpg) 

  

> *   **PlayStation® Network:** You’ll receive Garruk’s axe as a Sony Home avatar item!

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/promotional/MagicCelebration/MagicCelebration_InsidePage_GarrukAxe.jpg) 

  

And no matter what platform you're on you'll receive:

> *   The chance to bust open some booster packs and play Magic at no charge.\*

* * *

  

Play now at a Friday Night Magic event near you! 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/promotional/MagicCelebration/MagicCelebration_InsidePage_FNMLogo.jpg)
