We showed off the old *Vanguard* cards in a [previous Magic Arcana](https://web.archive.org/web/20030213000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/vanguard-series-1-2002-09-23).

Well, just like everything else we make, the *Vanguard* cards had to go through playtesting. At the bottom of a desk drawer in R&D we found these two little gems—playtest versions of two cards from the first *Vanguard* series.

See the actual cards—and learn more about *Vanguard*—[here](https://web.archive.org/web/20030213000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/vanguard-series-1-2002-09-23).
