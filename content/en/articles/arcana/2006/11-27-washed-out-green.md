The look of the color green in post-apocalyptic Dominaria is… not that green. You’ll see vegetation, but it’s clinging fungus. You’ll see ground cover, but it’s low, sickly moss. You’ll be hard-pressed to find even a single tree-leaf in art of *Time Spiral*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/22/3b/223bbaacccab6caa8ec5f9c6e497a00b119ae18ce6732f84d4483067a802b14f.jpg)  
*Yes, these are green cards!  
 (Wurmcalling, Wormwood Dryad, Havenwood Wurm)*

In some cases, the art director had to *remove* color from full-color art to produce the bleak, washed-out look of Dominaria’s devastated woodlands. 

Here’s Greg Hildebrandt’s initial art for the artifact-destruction spell, Molder.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d1/47/d147e0802ae7f3687090688750c8e82bd97970d6c3f61939618e50fdbabbf8bd.jpg)  
*Molder art by Greg Hildebrandt*

The final piece has color leeched from it to produce the washed-out look you see on the card.

![Molder_640](https://i.ormos.uk/92/6d/926de9b73e2d61e147916081af716e54152429d46800aa0aa8a6838d7cc28b60.jpg)  
*Molder art by Greg Hildebrandt*

For another example, check out Tom Wänerstrand’s Unyaro Bees, before color-correction and after.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a8/7e/a87e154015f12791b75cbb2259f1ee585b9fe1557129fd6672daa84f3f951581.jpg)  
*Unyaro Bees art by Tom Wänerstrand*
