Throwback Week isn't all about Feral Throwback, but here's an interesting factoid about that beast and its provoking brethren anyhow.

The *Legions* creatures with the provoke ability, including Feral Throwback, are tied together with a common visual cue. [All the provoke creatures](https://web.archive.org/web/20041129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/default.asp?term=provoke&fields=%7Crules&format=Legions&color=All&output=summary&sort=name&first=1) have a hook element in their art, whether it be a hooked tail, curving hook-horns, or a hooked weapon. The flavor is that the hooks allow the creatures to grapple others and draw them into combat with them. 

[![Krosan Vorine, Crested Craghorn, Lowland Tracker](https://i.ormos.uk/97/77/97770a66905f7004f0878476e80855762f261aefe5f9129ac17325db9f801479.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20041129000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/default.asp?term=provoke&fields=%7Crules&format=Legions&color=All&output=summary&sort=name&first=1)
