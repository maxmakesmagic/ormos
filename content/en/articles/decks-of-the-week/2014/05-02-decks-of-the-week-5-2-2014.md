Starting today, *Journey into Nyx* is available for purchase. It's release weekend! The set promises to shift Standard, but it's much too early to know how. What we do know, though, is that Pro Tour Qualifiers (PTQs) for Pro Tour ***Magic** 2015* begin again in a big way May 10. This season's format is Sealed Deck. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#m15). Good luck; have fun!
