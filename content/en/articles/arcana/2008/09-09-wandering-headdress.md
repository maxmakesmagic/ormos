Come with us now to the bygone days of *Mirrodin* block and the nearly forgotten Healer's Headdress from *Fifth Dawn*.

 ![Healer's Headdress](https://i.ormos.uk/2c/7d/2c7d53922ccba4058c0c3f8ecb9c30108bcc1b09ae709c8bf8afa05f86c511ff.jpg) 

Here's what that art looks like close-up.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1f/cf/1fcfe5cb2b3ae2b934c8060f0717c86f93c720e172b5513ce38f90df87f31f88.jpg) 

  

That's pretty straightforward. A metal turban of some sort with inset jewels. But it looks somehow familiar...

 ![Samite Healer](https://i.ormos.uk/db/07/db075fd4028147e9f4b58ff3be3a45dbb4f97e3d786a33185b409dbff27ff895.jpg) 

What? Is this somehow a Samite Healer's Headdress that found its way onto an all-metal plane and got, um, metallized? Let's look closer!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ac/65/ac6563e6b2ed0aa3a1945421bbcdc2eb0c65a77ba67a98ff24d62f400c29ac6b.jpg) 

  

Yup. No question, that's the same headdress. Neat!
