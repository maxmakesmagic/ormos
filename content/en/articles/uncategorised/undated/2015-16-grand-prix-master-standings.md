## 2015-16 Grand Prix Master Standings

#### Updated June 7, 2016

The player with the most Pro Points earned for Grand Prix in a Premier Play season is given the title of 2015-16 Grand Prix Master and will be awarded an invitation to the 2016 World Championship.

*Note: Pro Points earned at Grand Prix for this title count for all of a player's finishes at Grand Prix, and not just the best six finishes.*
