For those of you who didn't see it on the **Magic Online** website yesterday, we announced the arrival of Vintage as a format for **Magic Online** through the news of a new **Magic Online**-exclusive set called *Vintage Masters*.

You can read [the full announcement](/en/articles/feature/2013/10-21-introducing-vintage-masters) about *Vintage Masters* on the **Magic Online** website, where Hall of Famer and Wizards employee Mike Turian dives into it and explains what the set will be, what it will cost, and when to expect it!
