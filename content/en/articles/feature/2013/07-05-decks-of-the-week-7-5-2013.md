Congratulations to Reid Duke, who won his second Grand Prix last weekend in [Miami](https://web.archive.org/web/20130705000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/duke-draws-blood-miami). The Top 8 decklists from that GP give us a glimpse into what Standard looks like right now, so if you're getting ready to head to a PTQ this weekend, you might want to give them a look. Check out those and other Top 8 decklists from recent events below, and find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#theros). Good luck; have fun!
