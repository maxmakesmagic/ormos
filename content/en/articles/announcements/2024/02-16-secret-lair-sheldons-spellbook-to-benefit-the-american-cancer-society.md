Sheldon Menery, one of the beloved friends of the *Magic: The Gathering* community, partnered with Wizards of the Coast to design *Sheldon's Spellbook* to benefit the American Cancer Society®. Known as "the Godfather of Commander," Sheldon had an enormous impact on the Commander format, competitive play, and the broader *Magic* community.

![Sheldon's Spellbook Inkshield, art by Donato Giancola](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/sld_inkshield_donato_giancola_j3s5ndmwlnzm.jpg) 

Inkshield | Art by: [Donato Giancola](https://web.archive.org/web/20240216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Donato+Giancola%22%5d) 

Sheldon was actively involved in choosing the cards and the art in this very special collection. It showcases some of his favorite cards and those that reflect his style of play in non-foil and foil. Select cards even feature artwork with his likeness.

In collaboration with Sheldon, the American Cancer Society was chosen as the beneficiary of this philanthropic *Secret Lair* drop, and 50% of the product price will be donated to the organization to help fight cancer.

*Sheldon's Spellbook* is a time-boxed, to-demand product and will be produced to order after the preorder window has ended. You will be able to [preorder through MagicSecretLair.com beginning February 26 at 9 a.m. PT until March 24 at 11:59 p.m. PT](https://web.archive.org/web/20240216000000/https://secretlair.wizards.com/?utm_source=daily_mtg&utm_medium=article_announcement&utm_campaign=sheldon-s-spellbook-2024#sheldons_spellbook_event).
