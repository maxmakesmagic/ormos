Welcome to Paris, city of lights! They’ve turned on the bat-signal to call the world’s greatest for **Magic** Weekend Paris.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3c/4a/3c4af43a14c885238bcf9dd21c6958e78d6086eba12846d5263f2a9fbeeeb817.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/95/98/9598f16ed4f5a116e0315684c0a7d78786fd70abd6fc823abadc08f8aac0cf3f.jpg) 

Until the site opens, the players make do as best they can.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/da/4b/da4bb9406d8f680289d2421cb26abdbcf0aa88b345e371c4068dd6cc3acba032.jpg) 

They may look laid back now, but come tournament time, the Judges are all business.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/06/f9/06f944bc3306006edaad33aa979d89ff94463b83ac60515c8bff61d1e69aed1c.jpg) 

Everything tastes sweeter when drunk from a Champion’s Cup.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9d/9f/9d9f8436c66fc2fbe0fd645f4a64e17c8d08dace1d1d0cc0f5c96e2335c46e87.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fc/1f/fc1f5c33857cada222a3439347e6bbbe48d471613d4645a71714005e476b94f8.jpg) 

Soon replaced by the orderly business of spell combat.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/aa/80/aa80505972472abe7bda707d3b6be72e7885aa0e81ab6cd1b10baec88d72a026.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1f/d7/1fd786770ddfc9ef0656a1303bd5e3be0fc594c29c0c9fda3340b6996468c1b6.jpg)
