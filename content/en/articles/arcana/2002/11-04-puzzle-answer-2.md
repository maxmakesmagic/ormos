Here is the solution to yesterday's [Magic: The Puzzling](https://web.archive.org/web/20021104000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/magic-puzzling-2002-11-01), "Double Duty." The steps can be performed in a slightly different order, but the general idea is as follows:

1.  Tap the plains and two islands to add ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/white.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif) to your mana pool. One island is destroyed by [Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20021104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blight), but that doesn't matter.
2.  Play [Green Ward](https://web.archive.org/web/20021104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Green+Ward) on Paul's [Bog Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20021104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bog+Imp). Both [Web](https://web.archive.org/web/20021104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Web)s fall off the Imp.
3.  Play [Enchantment Alteration](https://web.archive.org/web/20021104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enchantment+Alteration) on the [Green Ward](https://web.archive.org/web/20021104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Green+Ward). Move it to your [War Mammoth](https://web.archive.org/web/20021104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=War+Mammoth).
4.  Play [Unstable Mutation](https://web.archive.org/web/20021104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unstable+Mutation) on the Mammoth.
5.  Attack with the 6/6 [War Mammoth](https://web.archive.org/web/20021104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=War+Mammoth). Because it has protection from green, it can not be blocked by the [Grizzly Bears](https://web.archive.org/web/20021104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grizzly+Bears) or the [Giant Spider](https://web.archive.org/web/20021104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Giant+Spider). Paul can block with the [Bog Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20021104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bog+Imp), but even if he does, you can assign five trample damage to Paul, killing him.
