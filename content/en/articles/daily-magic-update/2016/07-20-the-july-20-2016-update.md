The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on July 20, 2016. Today's Update is brought to you by [Deploy the Gatewatch](https://web.archive.org/web/20160720000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deploy+the+Gatewatch). That's [Deploy the Gatewatch](https://web.archive.org/web/20160720000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deploy+the+Gatewatch), for all of your Eldrazi hunting needs!

# Today's Musts

1.  [Magic Math: Deploy the Gatewatch](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/magic-math-deploy-the-gatewatch/) | ChannelFireball | Frank Karsten

I love a good Frank Karsten math article. If you aren't aware, the Hall of Fame pro literally has a Ph.D. in this kind of thing, so when he throws weird looking equations up on the screen, just trust that they're correct. Today he tackles Superfriends and just how likely you are to get the most out of [Deploy the Gatewatch](https://web.archive.org/web/20160720000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deploy+the+Gatewatch).

1.  [Superfriends—EDH Archetypes](https://rocketjump.com/listen/superfriends-edh-archetypes-114) | The Command Zone | Jimmy Wong and Josh Lee Kwai

[Deploy the Gatewatch](https://web.archive.org/web/20160720000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deploy+the+Gatewatch) gets printed, and suddenly The Command Zone looks at the Superfriends archetype? *And* it's on the same day Frank Karsten does the math for it? Coincidence? Probably, but it does make it a banner day for [Deploy the Gatewatch](https://web.archive.org/web/20160720000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deploy+the+Gatewatch).

Superfriends is an archetype that uses as many planeswalkers as possible, so Jimmy and Josh walk through building a deck jam-packed with planeswalkers for Commander.

But busted? Guess we'll see.

# Decks of the Day

1.  [A Match to Remember](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpVVsso-oSM)

Last night Eric Froehlich and Luis Scott-Vargas, two of the best players on the planet, played one of the [best matches in the history of the Vintage Super League](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpVVsso-oSM). Both players were playing similar Jeskai Mentor decks ([Monastery Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20160720000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Mentor) and [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160720000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer) serving as the key win conditions), and the matches were ones worth playing again and again.

For one, both players put on a clinic in both taking strong lines of play, and just playing fast. But that wasn't the highlight—and there were several highlights. There was an insane comeback in the first game where the announcers basically were calling the game before fortunes reversed. Then there was the absurd second game where there were approximately a million tokens on the board and a billion triggers any time anyone played a spell.

It was an epic match, and certainly one worth reliving.
