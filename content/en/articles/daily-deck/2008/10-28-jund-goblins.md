Fan of playing Goblins? Then **magicthegathering.com** forum user HKDizzy has the deck for you! Combining some of *Shards of Alara*'s coolest new cards with some that already existed in Standard, this black-red-green concoction is a great starting point for the upcoming States and Provincial championships. Sarkhan Vol, Goblin Assault, and Blightning team with Siege-Gang Commander, Chameleon Colossus, and Murderous Redcap to dominate the combat step. To find out more information about States (including how to get special unreleased promo cards for attending as well as the nearest location to you) visit [www.mtgchamps.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20081028000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://mtgchamps.com/&origin=).
