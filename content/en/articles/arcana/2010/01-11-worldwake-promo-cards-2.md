Here, for example, is the Prerelease card, which will be available at the [Prereleases](https://web.archive.org/web/20100111000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prerelease-facts) on January 30-31.

Say hello to "Multikicker"! Now, let's move on to the [Launch Parties](https://web.archive.org/web/20100111000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/release-facts), which will be February 5-7.

![Joraga Warcaller](https://i.ormos.uk/7b/36/7b367d7b34b299995b2553de162a956b784283ba80fc8f989c797c0319e14ce6.jpg)  

Say hello to "Multikicker" ... again! And stick around for Wednesday's *Worldwake* product section, which will reveal all sorts of exciting new things!
