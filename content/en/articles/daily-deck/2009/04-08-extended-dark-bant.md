After watching a similar deck Top 8 the recent Standard Pro Tour in [Kyoto](/en/events/2009/ptkyo09/nassif-the-greater-power), Wataru Hosaka has adapted the Dark Bant strategy for Extended. Instead of the usual Blue-White-Green configuration, Wataru has touched just enough black to play things like Countersquall and Doran, the Siege Tower. The powerful strategy was good enough for a PTQ Top 8 at a recent event held in Japan.
