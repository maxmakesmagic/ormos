**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/vintagechamp15/eternalweekend15-Mastriano-Top8_sml.jpg)Name:** Paul Mastriano

**Age:** 33

**Hometown:** Pittsburgh, PA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 2008 Vintage Champion, NYSE I Champion, 2012 Vintage Championship Finalist

**What did you play today, and how did you do?**  
 Hangarback Workshop. Great, obviously.

**Why did you choose your deck?**  
 It’s an evolution of the deck I played to win the byes at the GenCon Vintage trial. It’s very good in the Workshop mirror.

**What were your best cards in your main deck? Why?**  
 Mishra’s Workshop. It’s a [Black Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Lotus) that doesn’t go away.

**What were the best cards in your sideboard? Why?**  
[Ghost Quarter](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Quarter). It’s in there for the Workshop Mirror, and I played several of them.

**What is your favorite card in Vintage?**  
[Time Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Time+Vault). I love a [Time Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Time+Vault) deck, but today was not a great day for it.

* * *

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/vintagechamp15/eternalweekend15-Brophy-Top8_sml.jpg)Name:** Sullivan Brophy

**Age:** 19

**Hometown:** Philadelphia, PA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 Winner NYSE Open III, Several game Day Top 8s

**What did you play today, and how did you do?**  
[Dredge](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dredge), 8-1-1 (unintentional draw).

**Why did you choose your deck?**  
 It’s the best fast combo deck that doesn’t lose to Workshop and also the only one I own.

**What were your best cards in your main deck? Why?**  
[Bazaar of Baghdad](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bazaar+of+Baghdad). It is the reason the deck exists. [Ichorid](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichorid) because it just keeps happening and generating Zombies. [Petrified Field](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Petrified+Field) because [Wasteland](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland) is the most played non-blue land in the format.

**What were the best cards in your sideboard? Why?**  
[Ingot Chewer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ingot+Chewer) because it kills a thing and makes a Zombie. [Barbarian Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barbarian+Ring) because it kills [Containment Priest](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Containment+Priest), goes to the face and makes mana.

**What is your favorite card in Vintage?**  
 Currently [Narcomoeba](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narcomoeba), the creature without haste that is somehow the fastest dude in the deck.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/vintagechamp15/eternalweekend15-Herbig-Top8_sml.jpg)Name:** Michael Herbig

**Age:** 31

**Hometown:** Auburn, NY

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 Winner, Kadilak’s Dual Land draft 2006, 8th place SCG Power Nine Rochester 2006

**What did you play today, and how did you do?**  
 Midrange Mentor designed by Li Xu. The deck performed great. No complaints.

**Why did you choose your deck?**  
 I feel it is well positioned and is a ton of fun to play.

**What were your best cards in your main deck? Why?**  
[Dack Fayden](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dack+Fayden). It sigs, fueling delve and is great against Workshop decks.

**What were the best cards in your sideboard? Why?**  
[Pulverize](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pulverize) is insane. I faced four Workshop matchups.

**What is your favorite card in Vintage?**  
[Demonic Consultation](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonic+Consultation). Please unrestrict it.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/vintagechamp15/eternalweekend15-Shay-Top8_sml.jpg)Name:** Rich Shay

**Age:** 34

**Hometown:** Norwood, MA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 Two-time Watersbury Champion. SCG Power 9 winner. Former Rhode Island State Champion. Member of the Vintage community.

**What did you play today, and how did you do?**  
 I played Ravager Workshop. I did well enough to be filling \[a Top 8 profile\] out.

**Why did you choose your deck?**  
 Because Mishra’s Workshop is overly powerful. I wanted to play blue but felt that would be an irresponsible decision.

**What were your best cards in your main deck? Why?**  
 Mishra’s Workshop. It’s an uncounterable three-for-one.

**What were the best cards in your sideboard? Why?**  
 My [Dredge](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dredge) hate. because of the Zombie horde.

**What is your favorite card in Vintage?**  
 Mind Slaver! Please unrestrict [Thirst](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thirst) for Knowledge. Of course, [Atog](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Atog) is pretty good too.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/vintagechamp15/eternalweekend15_Kelly-Top8_sml.jpg)Name:** Brian Kelly

**Age:** 35

**Hometown:** Wilkes-Barre, PA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 Split first place at BMG’s Eternal Extravaganza. Had an entire Vintage archetype named after me.

**What did you play today, and how did you do?**  
 Dragonlord Salvagers Oath. I did well.

**Why did you choose your deck?**  
 It’s powerful and intricate, had personality, is not easily hated out and should be well positioned in a [Gush](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gush)/Mishra’s Workshop metagame.

**What were your best cards in your main deck? Why?**  
 Dragonord Dromoka. [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor). [Ancient Grudge](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Grudge). [Oath of Druids](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oath+of+Druids). [Ancestral Recall](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Recall). Honorable mention to [Polluted Delta](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Polluted+Delta): needs no elaboration.

**What were the best cards in your sideboard? Why?**  
[Magus of the Moat](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magus+of+the+Moat). It shuts down so many attack angles so efficiently.

**What is your favorite card in Vintage?**  
[Library of Alexandria](https://web.archive.org/web/20150823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Library+of+Alexandria).
