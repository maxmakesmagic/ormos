As Mattias Leveratto approached the feature match area, Grand Prix Santiago 2009 Finalist Andres Monsalve shook his head.

"We always play against each other in every event," said Monsalve about this match-up. It does not matter how large or small the event is; these two happen to get paired against each other a lot. 

The two gave each other a heartfelt handshake before the match quickly got underway.

**The Decks** 

This match-up is not one that you will be unfamiliar with if you've been following Standard prior to *Born of the Gods*. Leveratto was sporting a traditional White-Blue Control deck, with all of the recognizable cards such as [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) and [Jace, Architect of Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Architect+of+Thought). Monsalve's Blue Devotion deck was more the white-blue variety, sporting [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere)s as a catch-all for anything that troubles him. 

**The Games** 

Monsalve led off early with [Judge's Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge%27s+Familiar), while Leveratto started slow with his [Temple of Enlightenment](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temple+of+Enlightenment). Monsalve matched scry lands with his own [Temple of Enlightenment](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temple+of+Enlightenment) before attacking in for 1. An attack before any lands or action on the next turn though elicited a decision for Monsalve: before damage was dealt, Leveratto attempted to draw a card with [Azorius Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azorius+Charm). Monsalve thought for a moment before sacrificing his bird to counter Leveratto's draw, following this up with [Thassa, God of the Sea](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thassa%2C+God+of+the+Sea). Another [Judge's Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge%27s+Familiar) came down on the next turn, and Monsalve kept the pressure up with an attack from [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault). 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpba14/monsalve_rd4.jpg)

*Andres Monsalve* 

  

This was enough pressure to warrant [Jace, Architect of Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Architect+of+Thought) from Leveratto, but the planeswalker was quickly dispatched with [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere). Leveratto had no follow up on the next turn, while Monsalve added a third [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) to his board. He spent his turn waking two of them up, sending them in along with [Judge's Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge%27s+Familiar). This prompted Leveratto to trade one of his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault)s away, replacing it with another on the next turn in a game that emphasized the lands more than the actual spells. The same thing happened on the next turn, with Leveratto now out of [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault)s compared to Monsalve's one. 

That second [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) was also when Leveratto stopped playing hands, as he was now stuck on four mana. When [Rapid Hybridization](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rapid+Hybridization) from Monsalve on his own [Judge's Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge%27s+Familiar) prompted [Dissolve](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dissolve) from Leveratto, who was struggling to stay afloat, a [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter) on the next turn sealed the deck, as Monsalve attacked in with [Judge's Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge%27s+Familiar) as well as his now-active Thassa. 

[Tidebinder Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidebinder+Mage) was the first attempted play of the second game from Monsalve, and it was met with [Essence Scatter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Essence+Scatter). [Thassa, God of the Sea](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thassa%2C+God+of+the+Sea) ran into Leveratto's [Gainsay](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gainsay) on the next turn, leaving Monsalve to maintain offense with only a [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) attack on the turn following that. However, Leveratto stopped drawing lands, giving Monsalve some breathing room. The two [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault)s crashed in while Leveratto was missing land drops, forcing him to pop a [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) for a card. [Jace, Architect of Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Architect+of+Thought) came after he played his fifth land, opening up a window for Monsalve to resolve [Jace, Memory Adept](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Memory+Adept), which quickly got to work on Leveratto's deck. 

While Leveratto had [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) to keep from having his deck plunged into his graveyard, Monsalve had a trump with [Revoke Existence](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Revoke+Existence) to free his five-mana Jace from detention, and it got to work once again. Leveratto dug for an answer with his Jace, but only found three lands as he took the two-card pile. He had [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) at the ready regardless, and also had [Gainsay](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gainsay) ready to stop Monsalve's copy of the counterspell. [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle) came down naming [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault), and suddenly Monsalve went from a dominating position to struggling. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpba14/leveratto_rd4.jpg)

*Mattias Leveratto* 

  

[Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) shut down Monsalve's only mustered offense in the form of [Frostburn Weird](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frostburn+Weird). A Thassa from Monsalve ran straight into an [Essence Scatter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Essence+Scatter), and with eight lands in play and enough cards in his deck, Leveratto's chances continued to improve. [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion) met its fate to a [Dissolve](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dissolve) from Monsalve, and play passed back, who took the opportunity to resolve [Judge's Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge%27s+Familiar) and [Bident of Thassa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bident+of+Thassa) with his opponent's mana tapped too low. Leveratto reloaded with [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation), while Monsalve attempted to mount more offense with [Rapid Hybridization](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rapid+Hybridization) on his [Judge's Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge%27s+Familiar), giving him a creature large enough to punch through Jace's plus one activation. 

When Monsalve drew after his attack, [Glare of Heresy](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glare+of+Heresy) was found and quickly used to dispose of the [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) locking away his [Jace, Memory Adept](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Memory+Adept). An activation sent Leveratto down to only four cards left in his deck. 

Despite Leveratto having another [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) for Jace, Leveratto tapping out for [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion) cleared the path for Monsalve to show the Memory Adept waiting in his hand, giving him the game and the match. 

**Leveratto 0 – Monsalve 2**
