As you might remember, the wallpaper during Selesnya week was actually <a href="/en/articles/wallpaper/2012/10-03-wallpaper-of-the-week-sphinx-of-the-chimes">Sphinx of the Chimes</a>, which is a great piece, but not particularly Selesnyan.</p>&#10;                  <p>There are really two questions to answer here. That's a 33MB ZIP file with 220 images. It's got <i>extremely</i> high resolution art files. It's got PNGs of each aspect of the Selesnya texture. It's got logos. And it's got wallpapers for <a>Common Bond</a>; <a>Vitu-Ghazi Guildmage</a>; and, yes, <a>Trostani, Selesnya's Voice</a>. And the other nine guilds also have fan kits! So if you're just looking for wallpapers (especially if you're interested in Guildmages and Guild Leaders), you should know that there are thirty of them waiting for you in <a href="http://www.guildsofravnica.com/">GuildsofRavnica.com</a>.</p>&#10;                  <p>And yes, some of those wallpapers are from <i>Gatecrash</i>.</p>&#10;                </td>&#10;              </tr>&#10;            </tbody></table>&#10;          </td>&#10;        </tr>&#10;      </tbody></table>&#10;    </td>&#10;  </tr>&#10;</tbody></table>
