### Invitation List

**2009 Chinese Taipei National Championship Invitations**  
 The **Magic: The Gathering** Chinese Taipei National Championship is an invitation-only event, open to citizens or foreign players resident in Chinese Taipei from Jan 1st 2009 Chinese Taipei. Invitations are awarded based on the following criteria:

*   2008 Chinese Taipei Champion

    *   Wang, Ming Chee (Chinese Taipei Champion)
*   Winner from the Regional Qualifying Tournaments.

Regional( 1 invites) and National Qualifier on July 3rd( 2 invites) tournaments take place as follows:
