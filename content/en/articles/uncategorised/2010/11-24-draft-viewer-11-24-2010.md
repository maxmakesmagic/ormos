Welcome back to the Draft Viewer! Today we've got a classic viewer from the 2009 World Championships. This was the second of two triple-*Zendikar* Booster Drafts from the second day of Worlds and featured not one but two 2010 Pro Tour Hall of Fame inductees: Tomoharu Saito and Bram Snepvangers. The [2010 World Championships](/en/articles/event-coverage/2011/10-10-magic-weekend-san-francisco-and-2011-worlds-event-information) begin December 9 in Chiba, Japan.
