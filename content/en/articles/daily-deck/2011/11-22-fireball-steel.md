Team Channelfireball.com spent days working on Standard for the World Championships, and their hard work paid off when they rediscovered an underlooked archetype – Tempered Steel! A whopping four players from the team made the Top 8 on the back of the archetype. Could this become the dominant force in Standard, or is its success just a flash in the plan that will quickly be undermined by a slew of sideboard cards? Whether you're looking to play or beat the artifact menace, head on over to the [full coverage page](/en/events/2011/worlds11/japan-stands-tall-at-worlds-again) to read up on how the best players in the game approached it!

Conley Woods 

2011 Worlds Top 8, Standard
