> **turf**  
> *noun*  
>  1. A layer of matted earth formed by grass and plant roots.  
>  2. The neighborhood around **Magic** players' homes over which they assert their authority.

  
As for promo cards this season, it's all Matt Cavotta, all the time. Just for showing up, you get a full-art [Blood Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20070301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Knight), while supplies last:It's all-out war this weekend, and you and your buddy's home turf is at stake. It's [Champs](https://web.archive.org/web/20070301000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/champs) time (also known as [States](https://web.archive.org/web/20070301000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/states) in the US), and it's Two-Headed Giant Limited. Find another head and [read up](https://web.archive.org/web/20070301000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/states) on the details before some, um, shall we say, *lesser minds* from across town manage to claim bragging rights over *your* layer of matted earth.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/72/e9/72e91fbddf2ceb28c7cf585c8d0c7a384617bb5ece2bad2394420d4926bfd7e2.jpg)

If you make Top 4, you'll get a premium foil full-art [Groundbreaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20070301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Groundbreaker) -- but we're keeping that one a secret for now. If you want to see it, you'd better find that perfect second head to win the card.

In addition, Top 4 teams get playmats (one per head), and the winning team gets a pair of victory plaques (one per head).

Two-Headed Giant Limited [States](https://web.archive.org/web/20070301000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/states) / [Champs](https://web.archive.org/web/20070301000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/champs) 2007. Heads will roll.
