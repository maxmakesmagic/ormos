Zoo has been one of the most popular decks during the Extended PTQ season for San Juan. Patrick Cox took his version to the Top 8 of Grand Prix–Oakland. Loam Lion, known as "Kird Kitty" while being tested in R&D, was one of the new inclusions to the deck from the new *Worldwake* set. Perhaps most intriguing about Patrick's deck was the use of Damping Matrix in his sideboard. Read the full coverage from the event [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20100429082142/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=judge/article/20090817a).
