* * *

A game of **Magic** is a collection of stories waiting to happen -- and not just stories about game zones, card advantage and "damage going on the stack." It's also about the *flavor* of the game: the imagery of dueling wizards duking it out on a mystic battlefield, which is evoked by the cards' names, art, flavor text, mechanics and rules.

The design of *Darksteel* cranks up **Magic**'s flavor aspect with even more equipment and equipment-focused spells. Today's **Magic** Arcana asks you to take a moment to pause and reflect on the "flava" of equipment.

![An Ogre slips on a Cloak](https://i.ormos.uk/fe/ac/feac1048978a5f284daaa5ced0ce8430c4c60e470d402cdaf2d86528869dfdd1.jpg)

*   **Whispersilk Cloak** brings to mind a gauche and noisy ogre who slips on the Cloak, fades from view and slips past enemy defenses.

*   **Heartseeker** conjures visions of a soldier picking up the deadly mystical blade, battling goblins in combat with it, then at a crucial moment throwing it at a hapless distant champion and striking it dead in the chest.

*   If you've ever had **Murderous Spoils** played against you, you already know the card's brutal flavor. Did your flyer, stricken with the spell, lose its grip on a powerful sword, only for it to drop into the hands of an enemy minion?

*   Has the **heat of the forge** ever melted your creature's boots right around her ankles, so hot that she perished while still wearing them?

*   Has a master of the **Leonin Bola** ever tied up your troops long enough for an infiltrator wielding a specially-prepared **Spellbinder** staff to sneak by?

Got a flavorful story from a **Magic** game you've played, especially one involving equipment? Share it on our [message boards](https://web.archive.org/web/20040218000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/showthread.php?s=&threadid=184151)!
