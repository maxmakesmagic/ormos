Look at these boosters.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2e/1e/2e1e4df71b00458fba4c2dde90097a647fbc8af424aaff8fb206c3e039b07bad.jpg)  

They're what await players of **Duels of the Planeswalkers** 2013. When a player first launches the game (or goes into the Extras tab), they'll get their code. And when they enter that code on our website, they'll get a voucher to print out. Then they'll take that voucher to a participating store and get a booster. Here's a better look at them!

Players on PlayStation 3 will get this booster:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b1/9e/b19eee003d42b21e42135c75e5e5e548606aa669cef967a3193412eedead2002.jpg)  

Players on XBOX 360 will get this booster:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/15/d3/15d3edc0eeca417d75e331235a0a4ef3bcab8a9ff486564ca7a091bc7c71f0d8.jpg)  

And people playing on their PCs (via Steam) or on their iPads will get this one:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/05/26/052670e04bb5336adac981f48843da57f74e238c506b9cfad3b98460cb6f5638.jpg)  

Each booster contains six **Magic** cards. Five of them are random, but the sixth is a ***Magic** 2013* mythic rare cunningly tied to the art on the booster. So, for example, that PlayStation 3 booster up there will contain this:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8a/a7/8aa7d31cfa2b3fc52e6a04866f97b877cd16ce780bd40d634c4ebe0fd59ca215.jpg)  

Neat, right? And here are the other two cards, which are for XBOX and Steam/iPad users respectively.
