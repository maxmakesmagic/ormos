Welcome to the new Standard metagame! *Rise of the Eldrazi* saw wide use throughout the world as new deck types emerged showing off the new cards. Want to turn a humble 0/1 plant token into Emrakul, the Aeons Torn? Check out the winning deck from Montreal's PTQ. How about dropping an Eldrazi Conscription on a Baneslayer Angel? Then Salt Lake City and San Diego have the deck for you. With the [Pro Tour–Amsterdam qualifier season](/en/events/2010/amsterdam10-qualifiers/pro-touramsterdam-2010-qualifiers) in full swing plus [National Qualifiers](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2011-national-qualifiers) being run around the world, there are lots of opportunities to put the new tech to work.
