Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* in Barcelona is May 11-13, 2012.

Qualifiers for Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* start December 26 and run until April 1, 2012. Check the Event Locator to find an event near you that could earn you an invitation to Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored*. Pro Tour Qualifiers offer a [Planeswalker Points](https://web.archive.org/web/20200522091854/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/PlaneswalkerPoints/Information) multiplier of 5x, making them prime opportunities to add to your Planeswalker Point totals.

**Magic Online** Pro Tour Qualifiers will be run as well, so if you can't make it to an event near you, there's still a chance for you to qualify! Complete information on **Magic Online** can be found [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20111207000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/events/ptqmtgo/barcelona/12).

*   [Invitation List for Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored*](https://web.archive.org/web/20111207000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/invitation-list-pro-tour-avacyn-restored-2012-2012-02-03)
*   [Deck Lists from Pro Tour Qualifier Season](https://web.archive.org/web/20111207000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/pro-tour-avacyn-restored-qualifier-season-top-8-modern-decklists)

### Entry Fee

Entry fee varies from location to location. Check with local organizer for specific information.

### Tournament Structure

The structure for qualifier tournaments feeding Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* will be modified Swiss-style, with the Top 8 players playing in a single-elimination format for prizes and invitation(s).

### Tournament Format

*   [Modern Constructed](https://web.archive.org/web/20111207000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Resources.aspx?x=judge/resources/sfrmodern)
*   The *Dark Ascension* expansion is not legal for use in the Modern format until February 4, 2012. Events held prior to this date will not use the Dark Ascension expansion
*   Deck lists are required

### Tournament Length

*   Swiss rounds - 50 minutes per round
*   Top 8 matches will have no time limit.
*   Number of Swiss rounds based on attendance according to the following chart:

### Advancement

*   All players may participate in all Swiss rounds.
*   The Top 8 players after the final Swiss round will advance to the Top 8 single-elimination finals.

### Top 8

*   Top 8 matches will be best 2 of 3
*   Top 8 matches will have no time limit
*   Where necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will still be used to determine final order in the standings.

### Notes

*   [Planeswalker Points](https://web.archive.org/web/20200522091854/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/PlaneswalkerPoints/Information) multiplier 5x, REL: Competitive
*   The [**Magic: The Gathering** Tournament Rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20111207000000/http://www.wizards.com/wpn/Document.aspx?x=Magic_The_Gathering_Tournament_Rules) and [**Magic: The Gathering** Infraction Procedure Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20111207000000/http://www.wizards.com/wpn/Document.aspx?x=Magic_The_Gathering_Infraction_Procedure_Guide) are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document
*   Players must bring pen, paper, card sleeves and appropriate counters.

### Eligibility

*   Players who have won a Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* Qualifier may not compete in further Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* Qualifiers (including **Magic Online** Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* Qualifiers)
*   Players with [2012 Pro Tour Players Club](https://web.archive.org/web/20111207000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=protour/standings/proplayersclub11) level 7 or higher may not compete in Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* Qualifiers
*   All other players may participate in Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* Qualifiers

Complete information about invitations to Pro Tours can be found in the [2012 **Magic: The Gathering** Premier Invitation Policy](https://web.archive.org/web/20111207000000/http://www.wizards.com/wpn/Document.aspx?x=Magic_The_Gathering_Premier_Event_Invitation_Policy).

### Prizes

*   The winner of each event will receive an invitation and airfare to Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* in Barcelona.
*   Product prizes to be determined by the organizer.

### Registration Information

*   Players who want more specific local tournament or registration information should call their local contact phone number.
*   Players who have general questions about the **Magic: The Gathering**® trading card game, Wizards of the Coast LLC, the **Magic: The Gathering** Pro Tour, DCI players’ organization, Qualifier tournaments, the **Magic**® World Championships, National Championships, or Regionals should call Wizards of the Coast at (800) 324-6496, or access [the Knowledge Base](http://wizards.custhelp.com/).
