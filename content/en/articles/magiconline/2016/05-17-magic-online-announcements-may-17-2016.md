**A Champion Has Been Crowned!**

The *Magic: The Gathering Online* Championship Series, also known as MOCS, is a series of events in which the top *Magic Online* players can earn an invitation to the annual in-person *Magic Online* Championship event, where they compete for a prize pool of $116,000.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/mtgo/MTGO_Championship_Black_v2.png)

After a week of fierce competition, Niels Noorlander emerged triumphant with the title of *Magic Online* Champion! If you missed the live coverage, check out a recap of the events of last weekend on the [coverage page](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2015-magic-online-championship).

Interested in making it to the *Magic Online* Championship one day? Learn more [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20160517000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2016-magic-online-championship-series).

**The Year of Modern Flashbacks Resumes**

It's 2016: the Year of Modern Flashbacks! If you want to build up a collection for Modern, these Flashback Drafts add everything you draft to your collection once the event is over. Even if Modern isn't your thing, you can enjoy a leisurely draft through the planes of the past with rotating formats each week!

Full *Time Spiral* block Flashback Drafts begin Wednesday, May 18, after the downtime, and are followed up with a triple *Tenth Edition* Flashback Draft. The [full Flashback Draft schedule](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/12-14-year-of-modern-flashbacks) has been updated with formats throughout the rest of the year, so mark your calendar for the formats you're most excited about!

**Upcoming *Shadows over Innistrad* Sealed MOCS Monthly Event on May 21**

A *Magic Online* Championship Series (MOCS) *Shadows over Innistrad* Monthly Event is coming up on May 21! Players who have at least 35 Qualifier Points (QPs) in their account are eligible to participate. The series will contain twelve Monthly Events, each of which qualify top players for one of the four Playoff Events. These Playoff Events qualify top players for the *Magic Online* Championship, a Pro Tour, and the Pro Tour Challenge event at that Pro Tour.

Monthly and Playoff Events also award top players MOCS Leaderboard Points for the current quarter and for the year as a whole. Players of sufficient standing on the MOCS Leaderboard(s) will also qualify for Playoff Events later in the 2016 *Magic Online* Championship Series as well as the *Magic Online* Championship.

We look forward to seeing you on May 21 for the latest Monthly Event! Learn more about the 2016 *Magic Online* Championship Series [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20160517000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2016-magic-online-championship-series).

**Upcoming Extended Downtime on Wednesday, May 25**

There will be an extended downtime on Wednesday, May 25, for *Magic Online*. Events close at 12:00 a.m. PT/7 a.m. UTC (that's midnight Pacific), and store activity and trades end at 2:45 a.m. leading up to the system coming down at 3:00 a.m. The system is estimated to return from downtime at 12:00 p.m.
