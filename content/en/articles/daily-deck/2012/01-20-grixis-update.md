At Grand Prix Orlando this past weekend, Patrick Chapin brought an update on an archetype he has become known for: Grixis control! After his strong performance at Worlds with the deck, he made some tweaks and ran it all the way to the Grand Prix finals. To get in the mood for *Dark Ascension*, Chapin's deck even featured a singleton Sorin Markov! Check out his decklist and consider trying it out at FNM tonight! You can find the other [decks that made top 8 of Grand Prix Orlando here](/en/events/2012/gporl12/its-a-brewers-world-at-grand-prix-orlando). 

Patrick Chapin's Grixis Control 

Standard – 2nd place at Grand Prix Orlando
