Back when Aladdin's Lamp was originally printed in *Arabian Nights*, "10" could not be typeset into one mana symbol. Instead, it was printed as ![5 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/33/3c/333cbd82f68b5e306003b2955e517a94ffa7a3ea03087365c10afad540856dce.gif)![5 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/33/3c/333cbd82f68b5e306003b2955e517a94ffa7a3ea03087365c10afad540856dce.gif).

 ![](http://www.wizards.com/global/images/magic/an/Aladdins_Lamp.jpg) 

When the Lamp was later reprinted in *Revised* and *Fourth Edition*, the newer ![10 Mana](http://www.wizards.com/images/Symbols/Symbol_10_mana.gif) mana symbol was used.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6e/e6/6ee66dc44d25f3eacfe8386cad3a288b858c35dcdd535c40ca2bfbad5e581f0d.jpg)
