NimbleRooster teamed the best green and white had together to create this deck and ride it to the Top 8 of the **Magic Online** Premier Event circuit. Featuring a healthy dosage of early beaters that are both green and white, Rooster was able to capitalize with cards like Sigil Blessing to put his deck over the top. Check out those Shields of the Oversoul in the sideboard! They'll make nearly any target in the deck a hard to deal with monster!
