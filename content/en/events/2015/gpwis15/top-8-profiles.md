* * *

### Joe Lavrenz

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpwis15/t8_lavrenz2.jpg)

**Age:** 35

**Hometown:** Fond du Lac, WI

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** None (This is my first Grand Prix).

**What did you play Saturday, and how did you do?** Black-Red Devoid, went 8-1.

**What did you draft today, and how did that go?** White-Blue with Noyan Dar, went 3-0, then Red-White Allies and went 2-1.

**What were your best commons or uncommons this weekend? Why?** [Complete Disregard](https://web.archive.org/web/20151012000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Complete+Disregard) and [Vile Aggregate](https://web.archive.org/web/20151012000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vile+Aggregate). That card led to some crazy damage in Sealed.

**What do you want open for pack one, pick one? Why?** [Kiora, Master of the Depths](https://web.archive.org/web/20151012000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kiora%2C+Master+of+the+Depths) since it’s an excuse to play green-blue. Also I could use one for Commander.

**Which of the *Zendikar Expeditions* do you like the most?** [Misty Rainforest](https://web.archive.org/web/20151012000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Misty+Rainforest).

* * *

### Paul Rietzl

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpwis15/t8_rietzl.jpg)

**Age:** 30

**Hometown:** Scottsdale, AZ

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Member of the *Magic* Pro Tour Hall of Fame; Top 4 competitor at the 2015 World Championship; third Grand Prix Top 8 this season.

**What did you play Saturday, and how did you do?** Esper Ingest; went 8-1.

**What did you draft today, and how did that go?** Black-Red Devoid and went 3-0, then Blue-Red Devoid and went 2-1.

**What were your best commons or uncommons this weekend? Why?** I really liked [Molten Nursery](https://web.archive.org/web/20151012000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Molten+Nursery). It’s removal, inevitability, tricky, and fun.

**What do you want open for pack one, pick one? Why?** [Ob Nixilis Reignited](https://web.archive.org/web/20151012000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ob+Nixilis+Reignited), it seemed like everyone had one.

**Which of the *Zendikar Expeditions* do you like the most?** [Arid Mesa](https://web.archive.org/web/20151012000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arid+Mesa)!!!
