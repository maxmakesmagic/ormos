### Round 3: Luca Chiera vs. David Brucker

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/gprim04/fm3_1.jpg)They might be the two best-known two byes players and they already have to face each other in round 3. David Brucker was European Champion in 2002 and is the leader of the German delegation since Kai Budde has stayed at home. Luca was a member of the national team in San Francisco and was in very good position at Worlds before he lost the last 9 rounds.

The match-up seems to be quite in Luca's favor as his mono red deck featuring Molten Rain, Slith Firewalker and Furnace Whelp seems done to beat Tooth and Nail. Moreover, David plays the mono-green version, more consistent than the red green but without many ways to deal with red's fast starts.

Though, Game 1 is pretty unfair for Luca. He has Slith and Molten Rain in opening hand but can't draw a second red before turn 6 despite a Magma Jet.

On his side, David played two Witnesses, both regrowing each other between bolts and Jet.

On his seventh turn, Luca gets pressure on David for the first time. He plays Furnace Whelp, untaps, plays Seething Song, Flamebreak for the 2 Witnesses and Solemn, Slith with the two mana floating and pumps the dragon once to put David down to 11.

It's a bit late though and those early turns doing nothing give Brucker all the time to develop his mana. The German plays Tooth and Nails two turns in a row, for Abunas and Platinum Angel first, and then for Colossus and Witness.

Not having any Furnace Dragon in his deck, Luca concedes.

Luca Chiera 0-1 David Brucker

David takes in 3 Bauble, Reap and Sow, Mindslaver and 2 Duplicant for artifact removal while Luca boards in 3 Grab the Reins for as many Flamebreaks.

Once again, Luca elects to play first. He has no Slith, no Molten Rain in hand but two mountains, song and Arc Slogger.

David's opening hand is all mana sources, with Sylvan Scrying, Reap and Sow, Solemn Simulacrum and Eternal Witness but not much to optimise them.  
 As the hands seemed to indicate, Luca got the first strikes in this game as his third turn Slogger is followed by a fourth turn Whelp. David, thanks to Reap and Sow with entwine, untaps on turn five with two forest and three Cloud Post. The question is whether he drew Tooth, Mindslaver, or even Oblivion Stone out of his first five draws.

Luca-"Stop smiling!"

He went for Reap and Sow and took his fourth post, before playing Mindslaver and sac'ing it, just on time. The artifact reveals Shrapnel Blast and an artifact land, Brucker plays them both, targeting Slogger, which he activates in response targeting Luca two times and Whelp.

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/gprim04/fm3_2.jpg)

Luca-"Show me anything and I concede"  
 David-"No tooth but two witnesses"  
 Lucas-"I guess I'll concede then…"

In the end, Luca seemed as disappointed as David was happily surprised "I was pissed to see you were big red, I kept on loosing to that deck in PTQs…". Maybe this is a good time to qualify then.

Luca Chiera 0-2 David Brucker
