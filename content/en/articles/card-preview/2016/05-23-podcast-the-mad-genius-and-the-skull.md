"So you just win, right?"

That was the reaction of Erik "Mad Genius" Lauer when he first saw today's preview card:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/adhfianalodifdj2_EMA/en_w0ITbCBgMU.png)

Lauer—a member of the *Magic: The Gathering* Hall of Fame and current principle game designer in R&D—sat down with me to discuss the history of Necropotence. In this short podcast, he lets us know why "the skull" is one of the best cards ever printed.

[Podcast: The Mad Genius and the Skull](http://media.wizards.com/2016/podcasts/magic/lauer_on_necro.mp3)
