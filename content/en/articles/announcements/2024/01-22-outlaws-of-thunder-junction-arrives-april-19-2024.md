*Outlaws of Thunder Junction*, *Magic*'s first Western-themed set, rolls into participating Wizards Play Network stores on April 12 for Prerelease events. Then the showdown continues with the official release to follow on April 19.

![](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/1920x1080_OTJ_Key-Art.jpg)

Art by: [Lie Setiawan](https://web.archive.org/web/20240122000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+[%22Lie%20Setiawan%22])

We'll share a bit more about what exactly will be included in the set at [MagicCon: Chicago](https://mcchicago.mtgfestivals.com/?utm_source=dailymtg&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=magiccon-chicago) in just a few weeks, but for now, gather your friends and get excited to explore *Magic*'s newest frontier.
