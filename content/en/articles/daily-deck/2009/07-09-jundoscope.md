Jund decks have been popular in Standard for some time. But Dev used this list to win a recent Premier Event on Magic Online. The format? Kaleidoscope, which requires all players use cards that are multi-colored. Savage Twister and Terminate keep the board cleared, while spells like Anathemancer and Lavalanche punish opponents in the late game. As a backup? No less than Broodmate Dragon to sweep things up in the air!
