## *The Brothers' War* Launches Next Tuesday!

The latest *Magic* set, *The Brothers' War*, arrives in *MTG Arena* on November 15. As we approach next Tuesday, here are a few things to keep you occupied until then!

### Get Your Preorder Bundle

[![Mishra Pack Bundle with 50 packs and more](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/bro_mishra_bundle_420.jpg) ![Urza Play Bundle preorder that include Mastery Pass, Urzan Sentinel pet, and more](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/bro_urza_bundle_420.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20221109000000/https://stage.mtg.wizards.com/en/news/mtg-arena/mtg-arena-announcements-october-26-2022#bundles)

*The Brothers' War* preorder bundles are still available, but only until the set launches, so time is running out. These give you a great head start with the new set and cool perks like cards, card styles, a pet, sleeves, and more. [Check out what each bundle includes](https://web.archive.org/web/20221109000000/https://stage.mtg.wizards.com/en/news/mtg-arena/mtg-arena-announcements-october-26-2022#bundles), then jump into the *MTG Arena* Store to get yours.

### Watch *The Brothers' War* Early Access

![The Brothers' War Early Access Event, November 10 at 10 a.m. PT](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/bro_earlyaccess.jpg)

Don't miss the chance for a first look at the new set with your favorite *Magic* content creators and streamers! Tune in tomorrow, November 10, starting at 10 a.m. PT (18:00 UTC), for discussions and gameplay.

### Sign Up to Get Your Play Stats

![Sample of player James's stats from the MTG Arena newsletter](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/n8vx27bg8s2.jpg)

Curious about your gameplay patterns from *Dominaria United*, such as your favorite format, the color you play most, how many games you've played (and how that compares to other players), and more? [Sign up for the *MTG Arena* email newsletter by November 14](https://web.archive.org/web/20221109000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/mtgarena#arena-email), and you'll receive your *Dominaria United* details when those are sent out! Then, with each new set, you'll get an email with your latest stats when you're opted in to receive the newsletter.

[Join the *MTG Arena* newsletter list](https://web.archive.org/web/20221109000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/mtgarena#arena-email) by clicking the link and entering your email address.

## *MTG Arena* Announcements Move to Mondays on November 14

Starting this coming Monday, our weekly *MTG Arena* announcements will shift to Mondays. This is to better sync with the new Tuesday release schedule that begins with the launch of *The Brothers' War* on November 15.

See you next Monday!

## This Weekend: Best-of-Three Qualifier Play-In and Qualifier Weekend

[![Qualifier Play-In event November 11, 2022](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/qualifier_play-in_nov11.jpg)](https://www.magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in)

It's time for the big November Premier Play weekend! These back-to-back events, the Qualifier Play-In followed by the Qualifier Weekend, can propel you into the competitive *Magic* world and put you on the path toward the Arena Championship, and then the ultimate showdown, the *Magic* World Championship.

The [Best-of-Three Qualifier Play-In](https://www.magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) begins on Friday, November 11, at 6 a.m. PT (14:00 UTC) and wraps up at 3 a.m. PT on Saturday, November 12. Four wins will get you an invitation to the Qualifier Weekend event.

[![Qualifier Weekend event November 12–13, 2022](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/qualifier_weekend_nov12-13.jpg)](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend)

Then, just three short hours later, the November 12 [Qualifier Weekend two-day event](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) kicks off at 6 a.m. PT (14:00 UTC). This is where seven wins on Day Two can earn you entry to the Arena Championship!
