### Are Some Free Tournaments

* * *

Recently, I was enjoying Lunedust's thread on the Message Boards about possible **Magic** gifts for Christmas. (Why not make the reindeer work like Slivers? Of course, Rudolph might have something to say about being called "the new Sliver Queen.") The suggestions were pretty funny, but it did prompt the question: What is ***Magic** Online* giving players for Christmas this year?

How about free Premier tournaments? From Midnight to 11:59 pm Pacific Standard Time on December 25th, Premier tournaments will require no Event Tickets. Check the [Premier Events](/en/articles/feature/2002/11-01-premier-events-room-schedule) room schedule or in game to see what events are running and when.

So, when you need a break from trying to figure out how Tab A fits into Slot B and where exactly did that long bolt go anyway, jump on to ***Magic** Online* and play. Maybe you'll get to unwrap some boosters for the holidays.

  

Note: A previous version of this announcement was incorrect when it stated all tournaments were free. For obvious reasons 8-Player queues will still require event tickets. We are sorry for the confusion this error may have caused.
