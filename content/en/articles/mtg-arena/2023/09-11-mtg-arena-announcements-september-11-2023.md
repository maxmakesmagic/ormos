## Celebrate Five Years of *MTG Arena*

On September 27, 2018, *MTG Arena* began its open beta, offering *Magic* players a new way to enjoy Standard gameplay online. Our guiding principle was then, as it still is now, to bring fast, fun *Magic* to anyone, anywhere.

Five years have passed, five more formats have been added—including formats unique to the digital game—and millions of players have logged in to enjoy *Magic* in their own individual ways. We want to commemorate this journey with you all, so we're throwing a little celebration with cool stuff over the next couple of weeks!

![Celebrate the Harvest card art by Eelis Kyttanen](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/celebrate.jpg)

## Arena Open: *Wilds of Eldraine*

[![Arena Open: Wilds of Eldraine showing a withered hand touching a skull with pale mist rising out of it—Necropotence alt art](http://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/1200x628_arenaopen_woe.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20230911000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/arena-open-wilds-of-eldraine-september-16-17) 

*Wilds of Eldraine* is out, and now's the time to build your skills with the new set as we approach [Arena Open: *Wilds of Eldraine*](https://web.archive.org/web/20230911000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/arena-open-wilds-of-eldraine-september-16-17), happening this weekend, September 16–17. You won't even need a well-stocked collection to be competitive, because this tournament features Limited formats: Sealed Best-of-One and Best-of-Three on Day 1, and then Traditional Draft (Best-of-Three) on Day 2. All you need is your *Magic* skill to vie for the top prize of $2,000 and an invitation to the October Qualifier Weekend!

Plus, everyone who enters will receive the Talion, the Kindly Lord sleeve!

![Talion, the Kindly Lord sleeve](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/sleeve_woe_talion-kindly-lord.png)

## Claim Up to 4,000 Bonus XP During September

[![September XP WPN and MTG Arena promotion](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/wpn_september_xp_2.jpg)](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2023/08-21-earn-mtg-arena-xp-when-you-play-wpn-in-store-events-in-september) 

We're almost halfway through September now—have you played in *Magic* event at a local Wizards Play Network game store yet? If you haven't yet, you're leaving as much as 4,000 XP just sitting on the metaphoric gaming table!

How do you get your bonus XP? Just register and play in a *Magic* event at a WPN game store during September, and you'll score 1,000 XP. Play in more than one event, and you'll get an extra 1,000 XP, up to 4,000 total. Try Friday Night *Magic* events, check out Commander Nights, and more—[find your local WPN game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20230911000000/https://locator.wizards.com/) and see what they have to offer in September.

### QUALIFIER EVENTS

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events. Check out the standings to see [who's qualified for Arena Championship 4](https://magic.gg/standings/arena-championship-4).

Qualifier tokens are delivered to your *MTG Arena* inbox. Remember to claim them before the event starts!
