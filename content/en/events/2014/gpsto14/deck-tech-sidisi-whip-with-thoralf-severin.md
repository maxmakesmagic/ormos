If you if you want to learn more, you can find Seibold's deck tech [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m5j79EGUtS8), and Hwang's deck tech [here](/en/events/2014/gpla14/deck-tech-sidisi-with-eugene-hwang). However, Severin has made a few small tweaks for this weekend. 

First, Severin has 1 [Sagu Mauler](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sagu+Mauler) in the maindeck. "The U/B Control list from Ivan Floch cannot beat it, except for double-blocking with [Prognostic Sphinx](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prognostic+Sphinx)," Severin explained. 

Second, Severin has 4 [Drown in Sorrow](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drown+in+Sorrow) in the sideboard. When asked about why he included the sweeper, he said that he expected more mono-red this weekend and needed a way to beat it. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsto14/Thoralf-Severin.jpg)

*Thoralf Severin* 

  

Finally, Severin has a Hero's Downfall in the sideboard, and no [Empty the Pits](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Empty+the+Pits). "The deck is slow and needs answers to [Sorin, Solemn Visitor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorin%2C+Solemn+Visitor) and [Sarkhan, the Dragonspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sarkhan%2C+the+Dragonspeaker); that's why I have Hero's Downfall. [Empty the Pits](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Empty+the+Pits) is not really what you want against blue decks because they can so easily counter it." 

According to Severin, the deck is currently well-positioned because it has a good matchup against Jeskai and Abzan. Combining that with his small tweaks to adjust to the ever-changing metagame, Sidisi-Whip might just have its breakout performance this weekend.
