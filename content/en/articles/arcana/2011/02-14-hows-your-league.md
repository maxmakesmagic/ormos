Don't worry. I'm not going to tell you about the [War League](https://web.archive.org/web/20110214000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/its-war-league-time-2011-02-02) and how you should be doing it at your local store. I'm going to tell you about how *we're* doing it here at Wizards of the Coast. Look, we get to have fun and play **Magic** too, okay?

Because we've got a lot of **Magic** players, we overflowed the first War chart that got posted. And the second. So here's what we have at the moment.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/646_charts.jpg)  

We're playing with slightly different rules from the ones used in your local game store. Because one of the reasons we have a league is to help everyone be familiar with the current set, we have sealed decks made out of three *Mirrodin Besieged* packs and three faction packs of *Mirrodin Besieged*.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/646_packs.jpg)  

So that's no *Scars of Mirrodin* packs at all. No mana myr. No spellbombs. Just hyper-focused decks.My Phyrexian deck, for example, has four [Phyrexian Digester](https://web.archive.org/web/20110214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Digester)s, three [Core Prowlers](https://web.archive.org/web/20110214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Core+Prowlers), and three [Skinwing](https://web.archive.org/web/20110214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skinwing)s. And a [Tezzeret, Agent of Bolas](https://web.archive.org/web/20110214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tezzeret%2C+Agent+of+Bolas), so I feel like I have a pretty clear game plan. Unfortunately, I recently faced a Mirran deck that used four consecutive [Burn the Impure](https://web.archive.org/web/20110214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burn+the+Impure)s to wreck me. So it goes both ways.

On the plus side, we get cool pins!

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/646_phyrexianpins.jpg)  

The current standings (where "current" means "when this was written") have Phyrexia slightly ahead:

Phyrexian vs Mirran: 28 wins
