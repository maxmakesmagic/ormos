The art of the *Weatherlight* set has a few cards that tell a little story across two pieces of art. The art of Argivian Find shows a relic hunter digging up an idol made of jade, likely an artifact left over from the time of the ancient artificer civilization of the Thran.

![Argivian Find](https://i.ormos.uk/e2/f8/e2f889f9e86ee8e0b3c7e3d63e4cea8894bfb10de57527cddb7cb820feae545d.jpg)*Argivian Find art by Roger Raupp*

The same man can be seen later on, having the artifact restored on Argivian Restoration.

![Argivian Restoration](https://i.ormos.uk/e8/b9/e8b99a9ecdc1860c69aa8270d06ce11eee915f00671ec27be9d27a6a8e8071c0.jpg)

![Argivian Find](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Argivian+Find)

![Argivian Restoration](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Argivian+Restoration)

*Weatherlight* also has early signs of the storyline of the *Weatherlight* Saga. In the art of Desperate Gambit, Gerrard is seen dueling the fierce warlord Maraxus of Keld. In the background you can see [Starke of Rath](https://web.archive.org/web/20071205000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Starke+of+Rath) sneaking up behind him, about to stab him in the back.

![Desperate Gambit](https://i.ormos.uk/4d/db/4ddbe29c4d9d44b5147935629e04c7519ab69f2941935f92974d45de96fcf529.jpg)**Desperate Gambit art by Pete Venters**

*In Debt of Loyalty, artist Pete Venters continues the scene by showing Gerrard recovering from the fight. Gerrard agrees to let Starke come along on the *Weatherlight* (not yet realizing the depth of Starke's villainy).*

*![Debt of Loyalty](https://i.ormos.uk/93/fe/93fef7d0a0836467e19f8a945bc29aa7e93276d86b7fb40502446f98048e1193.jpg)*Debt of Loyalty art by Pete Venters**
