If you're looking for a great way to experience **Magic**, get yourself to one of these conventions. Not only does each feature a large schedule of public tournaments, but you can also find out the latest news on future sets, the state of the game, and much more at our panel discussions. 

### PAX East: Boston, March 22-24, 2013

The twin highlights of **Magic** at PAX East were the 2012 Magic Online Champinship and the Magic panel discussion, featuring senior creative designer Brady Dommermuth, developer Dave Guskin, organized play program manager Scott Larabee, and brand manager Adam Colby. The panel went into detail on *Dragon's Maze* and announced all three set names in the upcoming *Theros* block.
