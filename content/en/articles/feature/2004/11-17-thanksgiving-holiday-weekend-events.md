We are planning some special events for the U.S. Thanksgiving Holiday weekend. We will be introducing the Fallen Angel Avatar as prizes for these events. Events include "Nix Tix" Draft queues, a 6x Standard event, and the first ever Prismatic format Premier Event! The details of all the events are below.

I hope everyone enjoys these events during the holiday weekend. We are also planning a slate of events for the Christmas Holiday that will offer further opportunities to win a Fallen Angel Avatar.

### "Nix Tix" Draft Queues

QUEUES BEGIN: Thursday, November 25, 9:00 AM  
 QUEUES END: Monday, November 29, 9:00 AM  
 ADMISSION: 0 Tix, Product

All draft queues will require 0 event tickets from Thursday, November 25 at 9:00 AM through Monday, November 29 at 9:00 AM.

### Premier Events

DURATION: November 25, 9:00 AM – November 29, 9:00 AM (last starts at 9 AM).

The Top 8 players of all Premier Events will receive a Fallen Angel Avatar.

### Note about Premier Events and crashes:

As a general rule, tickets paid for an event will be automatically refunded, any product (in the case of Sealed Deck events) will not be *automatically* refunded by the system, but may be refunded by Tech Services. Make that you make note of the event number for the event in which you are playing so that any product refunds can be quickly resolved.

If a game crash cost you an entry fee and possibly prizes, visit [http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=company/feedbackform](https://web.archive.org/web/20050403130354/http://wizards.com/default.asp?x=company/feedbackform) and supply them with as many details as you can. These would include tournament number, date, game numbers, time and opponents.

As far as Premier Event prizes are concerned, if a crash causes an event to cancel, prizes are normally not paid. Sometimes the system will "finish" an event and declare it over, sometimes events get stuck and we have to manually finish the event. In these cases, prizes that are paid automatically by the system are final.
