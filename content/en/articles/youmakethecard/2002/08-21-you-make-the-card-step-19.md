Mark Tedin, the lucky artist selected to illustrate Mr. Babycakes for "You Make The Card," spent the last week in Sydney, Australia, at the Magic World Championships. But he wasn't there just slacking off! Oh, no. While in the land down under he produced three quality sketches… one of which will turn into the actual card art for Mr. Babycakes!

Have a look, then vote for your favorite. (You may need to register in order to vote.) Mark was even kind enough to write a few words on each one.
