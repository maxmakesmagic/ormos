In honor of Rerun Week, Daily Decks brings you Shuhei Nakamura's Black-Green Elves! Shuhei used his list to make the Top 8 of Pro Tour-Hollywood, on his way to a run at the 2008 Player of the Year title. Of course, that wasn't the first time he had played the list. Using a very similar copy, he made the single elimination rounds at Grand Prix-Copenhagen earlier in the 2008 season. Furthermore, a number of fellow Japanese countrymen had Top 8ed the 2007 World Championships with versions of the deck as well, making Black-Green Elves one of the most successful "reruns" of the 2008 season!
