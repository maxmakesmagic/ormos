“What card are you most hoping to open at the Dragons of Tarkir prerelease? Why?”

**(17) Will Edel**

“Dragonlord Kolaghan. I first got on the Pro Tour around Onslaught and I always loved playing with [Rorix Bladewing](https://web.archive.org/web/20150315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rorix+Bladewing). This is an easier to cast version of the card with a lot of upside.”

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpcle15/ClevelandQQEdel.jpg)  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/dftyuvbd564776rvf/en_Jqu8AEoXVr.png)

**Gerard Fabiano**

“Dragonlord Silumgar. It doesn’t matter how good the opponent’s rares are if I can just steal them. This is always a bomb and it also steals my opponent’s bomb.”

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpcle15/ClevelandQQFabiano.jpg)  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/dftyuvbd564776rvf/en_pwQd7C5LL8.png)

**(1)Owen Turtenwald**

“Sarkhan Unbroken seems like a very reasonable card.”

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpcle15/ClevelandQQTurtenwald.jpg)  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/dftyuvbd564776rvf/en_FNDOT7s8R3.png)

**Josh Mcclain**

“Narset Transcendent seems like it would be a lot of fun to play with. I’m not sure it’s the greatest Limited card, but I’m looking to have some fun at my prerelease.”

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpcle15/ClevelandQQMcclain.jpg)
