After fifteen rounds of Standard, with a total of 745 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpcop18/t8_dominguez.jpg)

**Name:** Javier Dominguez

**Age:** 31

**Occupation:** *Magic*

**Hometown:** Palau de Plemagans, Spain

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

One Pro Tour Top 8, 2nd at Worlds 2017, two Grand Prix wins.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

White-Blue Control. I was trying to beat [Goblin Chainwhirler](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Chainwhirler).

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Approach of the Second Sun](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Approach+of+the+Second+Sun). It's an easy way to win games.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Torrential Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrential+Gearhulk). [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress)-proof!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpcop18/t8_dang.jpg)

**Name:** Martin Dang

**Age:** 36

**Occupation:** Pro dad for Thea

**Hometown:** Aarhus, Denmark

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Sponsored by MTGMintCard and Dragon Shields.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

White-Blue. I tuned it for the Red-Black matchup. It's also what I played at the Pro Tour.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Teferi, Hero of Dominaria](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%2C+Hero+of+Dominaria). Because Teferi.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Ixalan's Binding](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ixalan%27s+Binding) was very good versus Red-Black.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpcop18/t8_blohon.jpg)

**Name:** Lukas Blohon

**Age:** 28

**Occupation:** Tourist and content producer for tcgplayer.com

**Hometown:** Prague, Czech Republic

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

PT *Eldritch Moon* champion.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Red-Black Aggro. It is too good not to play it and Thomas Hendriks gave me a great list and sideboard plans for every matchup.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Goblin Chainwhirler](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Chainwhirler). It's just too powerful.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Chandra's Defeat](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Defeat). It is one of the ways to get back into the game when you are on the draw in the red mirror.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpcop18/t8_aahlstroem.jpg)

**Name:** Christian Aahlstroem

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Teacher

**Hometown:** Järna, Sweden

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

0-5 two Pro Tours in a row!

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Reid Duke and Owen Turtenwald's Pro Tour deck, exact same 75. Got home from the Pro Tour jet-lagged and stole the tech. It's insanely well built.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Goblin Chainwhirler](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Chainwhirler), because reasons.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Chandra's Defeat](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Defeat) because of Chainwhirler.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpcop18/t8_maurer.jpg)

**Name:** Tobias Maurer

**Age:** 31

**Occupation:** Project coordinator

**Hometown:** Mannheim, Germany

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Played Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation*.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Mono-Red. I didn't have time to test, and I played the deck before. It's the best!

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Goblin Chainwhirler](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Chainwhirler) is insane.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer) won me a lot of games.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpcop18/t8_kernke.jpg)

**Name:** Tim Kernke

**Age:** 25

**Occupation:** Retail

**Hometown:** Magdeburg, Germany

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Nothing. This is my first GP. Started 3-2, then won the rest of the matches.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Mono-Red Aggro. I didn't expect much from the event and wanted to play a deck that follows a straight line.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Earthshaker Khenra](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Earthshaker+Khenra), early pressure with late-game potential!

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer), awesome value machine!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpcop18/t8_sanchez.jpg)

**Name:** Francisco Sanchez

**Age:** 25

**Occupation:** Chef

**Hometown:** Malaga, Spain, currently living in London, UK

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

A few 12-3 finishes at GPs and qualified for a couple of PTs.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Red-Black Aggro. It had the best results at the Pro Tour.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer). Despite only playing one, I was lucky enough to draw it many times.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

More [Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer)s. Card is just so good.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpcop18/t8_stahle-nilsson.jpg)

**Name:** Joakim Stahle-Nilsson

**Age:** 23

**Occupation:** Lecturer

**Hometown:** Karlshamn, Sweden

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

None.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

A very low-end mono-red deck. Because most removal in the current metagame costs 2+.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Bomat Courier](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bomat+Courier), because it hits turn one and can replenish my hand later.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

Probably [Abrade](https://web.archive.org/web/20180610000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrade). It works well against so many decks in the current metagame.
