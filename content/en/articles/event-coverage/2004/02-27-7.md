### Game 1

Affinity(Stark) vs. 《歯と爪/[Tooth and Nail](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tooth+and+Nail)》デッキ(Bernardo)

Stark は 1 マリガンするが 2 枚の《頭蓋骨絞め/[Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp)》を引き当ててカードアドバンテージ面での不安は取り除かれ、 3 ターン目から《厳粛な空護り/[Somber Hoverguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Somber+Hoverguard)》に装着となかなかに順調。

対して Bernardo はゆるやかな展開で《森の占術/[Sylvan Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Scrying)》から《雲上の座/[Cloudpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudpost)》を持ってきてはセット、持ってきてはセット。マナだけは潤沢な状態になるが、肝心の《歯と爪/[Tooth and Nail](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tooth+and+Nail)》がこないために、一度《忘却石/[Oblivion Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Stone)》でリセットして場を整理する。

その後流したはずの 2 枚の《頭蓋骨絞め/[Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp)》が手札から登場して Bernardo も顔をしかめる。しばらく《頭蓋骨絞め/[Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp)》をついたクリーチャーをブロックし続けるはめとなり、ライフは 3 そこでようやく《忘却石/[Oblivion Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Stone)》を引いて再び場を一掃。

後は《歯と爪/[Tooth and Nail](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tooth+and+Nail)》を引くだけとなるのだが･･････先に Stark の《大霊堂の信奉者/[Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault)》と《電結の荒廃者/[Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager)》が揃ってしまった。

**Stark 1 – 0 Bernardo**

### Game 2

Stark は《溶接の壺/[Welding Jar](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Welding+Jar)》《頭蓋骨絞め/[Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp)》 Bernardo は森と《雲上の座/[Cloudpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudpost)》から《森の占術/[Sylvan Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Scrying)》とお互いに好調な滑り出し。

そんな中で先手を打ったのは Bernardo で、 3 ターン目に《忘却石/[Oblivion Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Stone)》を置いて Stark の展開へ待ったをかける。

出てくるクリーチャーとの相打ちで《大霊堂の信奉者/[Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault)》からのダメージを食らい、ライフ 13 になった Bernardo は Stark に 2 体目の《大霊堂の信奉者/[Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault)》を出されてしまい、その時点で流さなかった為に（ 手札に 1 枚でもインスタントで出るアーティファクトが有ると負けるため）《忘却石/[Oblivion Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Stone)》を温存して、若干の不安は有るが《歯と爪/[Tooth and Nail](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tooth+and+Nail)》をキャストして、《レオニンの高僧/[Leonin Abunas](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Abunas)》 & 《白金の天使/[Platinum Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Platinum+Angel)》の負けない状態を作り上げる作戦に切り替える。

《歯と爪/[Tooth and Nail](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tooth+and+Nail)》はカウンターされる事も無くとおり負けない状態を作ると、その後に《ダークスティールの巨像/[Darksteel Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Colossus)》を追加。  
 引けども、引けども打開策を見つけられない Stark に《精神隷属器/[Mindslaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindslaver)》を使用すると Stark 投了。

**Stark 1 – 1 Bernardo**

### Game 3

《頭蓋骨絞め/[Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp)》《電結の荒廃者/[Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager)》と順調な Stark 。 Bernardo も 2 ターン目に《森の占術/[Sylvan Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Scrying)》、 Stark の場に《マイアの処罰者/[Myr Enforcer](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Enforcer)》が現れようとすればすかさず《酸化/[Oxidize](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oxidize)》と全く流れに澱みが無い。

更に Stark 唯一の黒マナ源であった《空僻地/[Glimmervoid](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glimmervoid)》も《刈り取りと種まき/[Reap and Sow](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reap+and+Sow)》の双呪で壊すと《雲上の座/[Cloudpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudpost)》を追加して Bernardo のマナは加速する一方だ。

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/ptkob04/r7fm2_2.jpg)

一度《厳粛な空護り/[Somber Hoverguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Somber+Hoverguard)》と《マイアの処罰者/[Myr Enforcer](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Enforcer)》に殴られて 13 になるが、豊富なマナ源から 11 マナだし、《ヴィリジアンのシャーマン/[Viridian Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viridian+Shaman)》で《頭蓋骨絞め/[Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp)》を壊して、同時に《忘却石/[Oblivion Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Stone)》キャスト起動と場を一気に掃除した。

Bernardo 優勢は続き、ついには 4 枚になった《雲上の座/[Cloudpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudpost)》からの《精神隷属器/[Mindslaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindslaver)》起動で Stark のターンを貰いハンドを《恐怖/[Terror](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terror)》 2 枚と土地だけにすると万を持して《白金の天使/[Platinum Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Platinum+Angel)》を登場させて、勝負を決めた。

**Final Results : Da Costa Cabral Bernardo Win**
