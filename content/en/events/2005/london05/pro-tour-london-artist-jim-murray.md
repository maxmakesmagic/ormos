Born in 1972, son of a retired marine turned artist, spending former years in England’s Westcountry, **Jim Murray** was tutored in fine arts at an early age. He began a career in comics at 21 with a story called ‘Harmony’ for the British comic ‘Judge Dredd Megazine’. The commissions kept coming, over the next few years Jim illustrated characters such as Holocaust 12, Slaine and Judge Dredd. He moved from Black and white artwork to a fully painted style resulting in two 48 page graphic novels. The first was a ‘Judge Dredd Batman’ crossover called ‘Die Laughing’ published in 1998, the follow-up an Elseworlds book for DC Comics, ‘Batman Demon’ in 1999. Other work in this period included props and promotional pieces for the TV series ‘Spaced and a 25 x 50 foot canvas backdrop for the Prodigy (completed in spray-paint with fellow artist Jason Brashill) 

Jim began working as a concept artist for video games in 2000, first with Virgin studios in London, then three years later for Artificial Mind and Movement in Montreal. During this time, Jim began freelancing in his spare time for Wizards of the Coast, painting **Magic** cards and continues to enjoy that privilege to this day.

Jim lives in Montreal with his lovely partner Annabel and his equally lovely daughters, Rebecca and Jaimie.
