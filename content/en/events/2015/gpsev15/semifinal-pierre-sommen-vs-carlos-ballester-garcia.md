In the first semifinal to finish, two-time Grand Prix Top 8 competitor and one-time winner, Pierre Sommen met newcomer Carlos Ballester García. Sommen had brought a version of Abzan Aggro, Ballester García was piloting a green devotion deck with a sizeable addition of red.

**Game 1**

Sommen was the aggressor right from the start. His [Rakshasa Deathdealer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Deathdealer) was itself dealt with via [Crater's Claws](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crater%27s+Claws), but his [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) stuck around and was later joined by another [Rakshasa Deathdealer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Deathdealer), while Ballester García's [Polukranos, World Eater](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Polukranos%2C+World+Eater) didn't, thanks to [Abzan Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Charm). Another Polukranos tried to stand in the way of the almighty Rhino but caught a bout of [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight).

Ballester García tried to claw his way back into the game by way of [Whisperwood Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whisperwood+Elemental). Alas, it was too late, as Sommen took to the air with [Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc) and its mate and took the game.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsev15/sf1_sommen.jpg)

Pierre Sommen 1-0 Carlos Ballester García

**Game 2**

Ballester García led with [Voyaging Satyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyaging+Satyr), then dismantled [Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion) with [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike), then summoned [Whisperwood Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whisperwood+Elemental), then killed [Anafenza, the Foremost](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anafenza%2C+the+Foremost) with [Crater's Claws](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crater%27s+Claws), then summoned [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix), and finally trumped all of Sommen's defenses with [Hornet Queen](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hornet+Queen). This game was all Ballester García and the victory was his as well.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsev15/sf1_ballestergarcia.jpg)

Pierre Sommen 1-1 Carlos Ballester García

**Game 3**

Sommen again led the charge, this time with [Anafenza, the Foremost](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anafenza%2C+the+Foremost) followed by [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino). After [Voyaging Satyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyaging+Satyr), Ballester García didn't have a fourth land. He was able to shoot down [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) with a pair of [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike)s, but found no answer to Sommen's [Sorin, Solemn Visitor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorin%2C+Solemn+Visitor) and that was that.

Pierre Sommen 2-1 Carlos Ballester García
