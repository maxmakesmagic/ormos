## 2012 Players Championship

[日本語の記事へ](/ja/articles/event-coverage/undated/2012-2) 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/cf/07/cf072e7b4ac9e50a72e8f3e5851f590712015cad4f739212f3c1cc14f58e8340.jpg)

Yuuya Watanabe found a way to defeat the nearly invincible Shouta Yasooka [in the finals of the Players Championship](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/08-31-finals-crushing-cascades) to capture his second Player of the Year title. Watanabe joins the immortal Kai Budde as the only multiple Player-of-the-Year winners.

Yasooka entered the Top 4 as the dominant favorite, having dropped just one match over the entire tournament prior to the finals. But Watanabe prevailed in a thrilling five-game match over his countryman to be crowned the 2011-2012 Player of the Year.
