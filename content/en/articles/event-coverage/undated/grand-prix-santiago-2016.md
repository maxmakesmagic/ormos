## GRAND PRIX SANTIAGO 2016

TOP 8 COVERAGE

754 players competed in Santiago, Chile, in a burgeoning Standard Format. Pro Tour *Kaldesh* had demonstrated the variety of the format, and GP Santiago sported a wide range of decks. Of the 754 players, 197 made it to Day 2, and eight of them played off against each other in the Feature Match area.

Many of the biggest names had fallen by that point, including several World Magic Cup Team Captains - Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa (12), Marcelino Freeman, Rodrigo Lopez. The only Captain that remained was Luis Salvatto, who made Top 8 alongside John Chavarria, Eduardo Dos Santos, Jonathan Melamed, Victor Silva, Cristian Cespedes, Ignacio Garcia and Marcos Paulo De Jesus Freitas.

Although the event was largely dominated by Green-Black Delirium and Blue-White Flash decks, the Top 8 also included two very different archetypes, Blue-Red Fevered Visions and Blue-Red Colossus. But it was John Chavarria, piloting Blue-White Flash, who won through the Top 8 to a first-place finish at GP Santiago, 2016. Congratulations, John Chavarria!

DAY 1 COVERAGE

Over 760 players came to battle at GP Santiago today; however, only 197 remain. Christian Cespedes and Jonathan Melamed were the two player to remain undefeated at the end of the 9th round. However, they are being harried by such players as Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa (12), currently at 3rd place, and Marcelino Freeman, currently at 4th spot.

The day was dominated by Black-Green Delirium and White-Blue Flash; however, that may be been heavily influenced by that fact it were the choice of many of the top players in the room. Three aggro decks followed behind - Red-White Vehicles, Black-Red Aggro and Red-Green Energy.

The surprise deck, however, is sitting in second place - Blue-Red Fevered Visions, piloted by the undefeated Jonathan Melamed. Will it truly be the breakout metagame choice? We will know by the end of Day 2 of GP Santiago tomorrow.
