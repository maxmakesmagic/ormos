Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle has fast become a fan favorite since it was first printed in *Zendikar*. At the 2009 Texas State Championship, Justin Corbett made it the centerpiece of his Top 8 deck. He accelerates out Mountains while tutoring for Valakut via Expedition Map. From there on out, virtually every land drop he makes sends a Lightning Bolt at something!
