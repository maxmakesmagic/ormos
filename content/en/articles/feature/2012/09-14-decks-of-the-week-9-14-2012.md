The qualifying season for Pro Tour *Gatecrash* is under way! Find out about the Limited formats used in the current season of Pro Tour Qualifiers, as well as a schedule of PTQs, [here](/en/events/2012/gatecrash13-qualifiers/qualifiers-for-pro-tour-gatecrash). And to give you an idea of what kinds of archetypes players are drafting these days, check out the Top 8 decklists from recent PTQs, including the one held last weekend on **Magic Online**. You might also be interested in looking at the [*Return to Ravnica* Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218192439/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/returntoravnica/cig), which shows off some of the cards that will undoubtedly be played at the Pro Tour in October! Enjoy!
