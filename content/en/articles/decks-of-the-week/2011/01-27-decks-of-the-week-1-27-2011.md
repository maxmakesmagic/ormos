T**he 2011 Magic: The Gathering** season kicked off with [Grand Prix Atlanta](/en/events/2011/gpatl11/jason-is-built-ford-tough-in-atlanta) last weekend, and congratulations to Jason Ford for capturing the trophy and taking an early lead in the 2011 Player of the Year race. His turbo-land version of the popular Valakut archetype proved to be the story of the tournament—the deckbuilding team from Minnesota that crafted the deck went an amazing 33-3-0 on Day One! Speaking of the Player of the Year race, we're only two weeks away from the showdown at [Magic Weekend Paris](/en/events/2010/paris11/magic-weekend-paris-event-information) between Brad Nelson and Guillaume Matignon to see who is crowned the 2010 Player of the Year. We'll see the new Standard format debuted in Paris thanks to the introduction of *Mirrodin Besieged*, but until then cruise through the latest lists from **Magic Online** for ideas to take to Friday Night **Magic**!
