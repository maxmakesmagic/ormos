One of the thrills of new *Commander* products is getting updated art on some often unexpected cards. For example, virtually no one was expecting the *Mirage* Diamonds to reappear, let alone with new art, but lo and behold, [Lindsey Look](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Lindsey+Look%22%5D) [knocked them out of the park](/en/articles/arcana/2014/10-29-a-wizards-best-friend).

There's also a cycle of Medallions by [Daniel Ljunggren](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Daniel+Ljunggren%22%5D), pushing the number of new art pieces up to ten just in those two artifact cycles.

In fact, there are a few too many to show all of the new art in this one Arcana, but I wanted to highlight some notable pieces.

# Sacred Mesa

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Sacred%20Mesa)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sacred%20Mesa)

[Sacred Mesa](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sacred+Mesa) was once a tournament powerhouse, and even part of an early [Earthcraft](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Earthcraft) combo with a Plains and [Wild Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Growth). It was reprinted once in [Time Spiral](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Time+Spiral), but has never had new art. Until now. [Robbie Trevino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Robbie+Trevino%22%5D)'s update paints a gorgeous landscape that makes us believe it's home to pegasi.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/cardart_sacredmesa_c14.jpg)

[Sacred Mesa](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sacred+Mesa) | Art by [Robbie Trevino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Robbie+Trevino%22%5D)

# Sylvan Safekeeper

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Sylvan%20Safekeeper)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan%20Safekeeper)

Originally designed by Olle Råde as a prize for winning the old *Magic* Invitational, this is actually [Sylvan Safekeeper](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Safekeeper)'s first reprinting ever. Along with gaining new wording (shroud and the Human Wizard type), [Magali Villeneuve](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Magali+Villeneuve%22%5D) also updates the look of the Safekeeper.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/cardart_sylvansafekeeper_c14.jpg)

[Sylvan Safekeeper](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Safekeeper) | Art by [Magali Villeneuve](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Magali+Villeneuve%22%5D)

# Desert Twister

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Desert%20Twister)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desert%20Twister)

Let's stay in green for a moment to revisit an old, old favorite. [Desert Twister](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desert+Twister) has actually been reprinted a number of times. Originally from *Arabian Nights*, one of the few green cards ever able to destroy a nonflying creature then jumped into the core set with *Revised* and popped up occasionally over the years, yet it only received new art once, in *Mercadian Masques*. Now, [Noah Bradley](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Noah+Bradley%22%5D) has provided a third take on the iconic card, one that certainly matches the flavor text: *Massive, mindless, and imbued with one terrible purpose.*

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/cardart_deserttwister_c14.jpg)

[Desert Twister](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desert+Twister) | Art by [Noah Bradley](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Noah+Bradley%22%5D)

# Exclude

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Exclude)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exclude)

Finally, at least for today, we jump over to an old *Invasion* favorite that has never seen new art. There's so much value here, how could we not show it off? Oh, and [Greg Staples](https://web.archive.org/web/20141105000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Greg+Staples%22%5D)'s new take is pretty spiffy too.
