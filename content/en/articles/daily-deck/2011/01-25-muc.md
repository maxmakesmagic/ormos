With *Mirrodin Besieged* hitting the Prerelease tables this weekend, *Scars* Block Constructed is going to get even more interesting. The exciting new format is available for play now on **Magic Online**. Looking for a starting point? Try Fatality101's take on the classic Mono-Blue Control. Tumble Magnet controls opposing creatures, Silver and Palladium Myr accelerate into Myr Battlesphere and Precursor Golem, and Stoic Rebuttal stops your opponent's biggest spells cold.
