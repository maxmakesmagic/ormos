National Qualifiers are underway in Japan, and as the world waits to see how the new Standard will shape up, Taiji Urasawa is helping to decide the matter. Taiji got 3rd at the Nagoya National Qualifier playing a modified Valakut deck list. Without any of the recently banned cards, Urasawa crushed the competition with his powerful deck. The goal? Combine Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle with a number of cards that put Mountains onto the battlefield. It worked!
