Ken Yukuhiro is a master deckbuilder. I've had the honor of working with him in Valencia. One of the great minds in the creation of Blue Moon, a deck good enough to put Lee Shi Tian into the Top 8 at Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*. 

Today, he is armed with an eye-raising concoction of his own and he has aptly named it the "Blue Black Heroic Draft Deck", to the amusement of anyone hearing about it.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/ken_yukuhiro.JPG)

*Always bubbly and always smiling!* 

  

Playing with [Mizzium Skin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzium+Skin)s, [Boon of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boon+of+Erebos) and the full set of [Triton Tactics](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triton+Tactics), he hopes to use these cards to trigger the Heroic abilities of maligned cards like [Agent of the Fates](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Agent+of+the+Fates), [Artisan of Forms](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Artisan+of+Forms) and [Tormented Hero](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tormented+Hero). 

"I was inspired by a Blue Red White Heroic deck which I saw on **Magic Online**, but I could not accept a shaky three-color manabase that also needs to support [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault). I experimented with Blue Red, Blue White but eventually settled on Blue Black." 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/ken_action.jpg)

*Yukuhiro, performing shenanigans with an assortment of odd cards never seen before in the Standard environment.* 

  

Correctly predicting the metagame dominated by Mono Black Devotion and Esper Control decks, he felt that a creature base comprising [Pain Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pain+Seer) and [Artisan of Forms](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Artisan+of+Forms) was probably good. In response to [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict)s or plain old spot removal, he could transform [Artisan of Forms](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Artisan+of+Forms) into a copy of Xantrid Necromancer to flood the board with a bunch of tokens. Additionally, in matchups against large creatures like [Polukranos, World Eater](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Polukranos%2C+World+Eater) and [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon), [Artisan of Forms](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Artisan+of+Forms) was able to help him achieve parity. 

At the moment, Yukuhiro is in contention for the Top 8 but he says he might end up either 8th, 9th or 10th even if he wins out. . Currently at 18 Pro Points, he is aiming to achieve Gold level again this year, and professes that Platinum was "really really hard".

Hopefully his trusty [Agent of the Fates](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Agent+of+the+Fates) will twist his fate in his favor!
