7勝2敗ゾーンまで上ってきたモリカツこと森勝洋が対するは、フランスの「同一フォーマットGP2勝」Yann Hamonである。  
 Affinity同士の対戦ながら、Hamonのそれがオーソドックスな組み合わせの黒赤であるのに対し、モリカツのそれは同系を意識した黒タッチ《酸化/[Oxidize](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oxidize)》である。  
 正統派に変化球。勝つのはどちらか。

### Game 1

モリカツ先手で《囁きの大霊堂/[Vault of Whispers](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+of+Whispers)》《羽ばたき飛行機械/[Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter)》《彩色の宝球/[Chromatic Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chromatic+Sphere)》と一気に並べてのスタート。Hamonは《電結の働き手/[Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker)》と、お馴染みの顔ぶれが並ぶ。

《チス＝ゴリアの歯/[Tooth of Chiss-Goria](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tooth+of+Chiss-Goria)》を用いてダメージを与えた後、モリカツは続いて《マイアの回収者/[Myr Retriever](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Retriever)》を展開。Hamonが《電結の荒廃者/[Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager)》をプレイすれば、《彩色の宝球/[Chromatic Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chromatic+Sphere)》を回した上で《頭蓋骨絞め/[Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp)》を使い、デッキを回転させる。

だが、Hamonが《電結のとげ刺し/[Arcbound Stinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Stinger)》《金属ガエル/[Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite)》2枚と展開するに至って、さすがのモリカツも一旦手を止めざるを得なくなる。彼には珍しく考慮に沈むが、出した答えは《頭蓋骨絞め/[Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp)》付き《羽ばたき飛行機械/[Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter)》で攻撃の後さらなる《頭蓋骨絞め/[Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp)》で4枚ドロー。だが、思わしいカードがなかったのか、《大霊堂の信奉者/[Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault)》を展開して《空僻地/[Glimmervoid](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glimmervoid)》を立てたまま終了する。

Hamonは返すターン怒涛の攻撃。土地が止まっているものの、7点のダメージを先行する。モリカツはターン終了時に《電結の荒廃者/[Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager)》に《酸化/[Oxidize](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oxidize)》。その力は《電結のとげ刺し/[Arcbound Stinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Stinger)》に乗り移る。

大量のドローで地上は止めてはいるものの、《電結のとげ刺し/[Arcbound Stinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Stinger)》への回答を用意できないモリカツ。《頭蓋骨絞め/[Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp)》で絞めて絞めて、デッキを掘り進む。だが、親和クリーチャーを一向に引かないのも劣勢を加速しているようだ。

なんとか《マイアの回収者/[Myr Retriever](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Retriever)》で回収した《羽ばたき飛行機械/[Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter)》を出せたのだが、直後Hamonは《爆片破/[Shrapnel Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrapnel+Blast)》を2枚公開し、モリカツは即座に投了した。

**森 –0　Hamon -1**

### Game 2

再びモリカツ先攻で、第1ターンは《ダークスティールの城塞/[Darksteel Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Citadel)》から《電結の働き手/[Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker)》の鏡打ち。モリカツは第2ターンの攻撃後にこれを即座に《頭蓋骨絞め/[Skullclamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullclamp)》してドローに換える。Hamonは《電結の荒廃者/[Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager)》と《金属ガエル/[Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite)》を追加。

土地が《ダークスティールの城塞/[Darksteel Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Citadel)》ばかりで満足行く展開ができないモリカツは、続く第3ターンにも《マイアの回収者/[Myr Retriever](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Retriever)》を即絞めてみるが、上手く行かない。《チス＝ゴリアの歯/[Tooth of Chiss-Goria](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tooth+of+Chiss-Goria)》を出すのみでターンを終了する。  
 勢いに乗ったHamonは《大霊堂の信奉者/[Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault)》《彩色の宝球/[Chromatic Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chromatic+Sphere)》《マイアの処罰者/[Myr Enforcer](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Enforcer)》と並べた上で《電結の荒廃者/[Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager)》にアーティファクトを投入し、《大霊堂の信奉者/[Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault)》とあいまって苦しいモリカツに強烈な王手を。

![](https://i.ormos.uk/60/30/603011e1c48afafac85ec3ea4a354b1280b1ac238d4c64fdbee69e74452751ec.jpg)

モリカツは必死に打開策を探す。《金属ガエル/[Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite)》《電結の働き手/[Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker)》《電結の荒廃者/[Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager)》と並べ、Hamonが手を止めてくれるのを祈る。  
 だが、Hamonの手に残された最後の手札は。  
 そう、《爆片破/[Shrapnel Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20040227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrapnel+Blast)》。

「相手のドローが完璧だもん。超強い」

**森 –0 Hamon -2**

**Yann Hamon defeats Katsuhiro Mori.**
