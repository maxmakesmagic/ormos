When the final *Duel Decks* releases April 6, it will mark the end of an era.

But why dwell on that when we can look at sweet previews of the decks? Yay previews!

First up, let's check out that packaging!

![](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/muX6hRkgi3.png)

Before we get to the weird black spot we've placed over the Elves side (no, that's not how it really looks), let's zoom in closer on the Inventors.

![Goblin Welder](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/c4rd4r7_ClBLcfRReI.jpg)

[Goblin Welder](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Welder) | Art by [Victor Adame Minguez](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Victor+Adame+Minguez%22%5d)

Yup, that's our old friend [Goblin Welder](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Welder)! What's it going to be welding? Well, we're not going to show you everything yet, but this particular Goblin will have some friends to help out its welding, like [Solemn Simulacrum](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Solemn+Simulacrum), [Thopter Assembly](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thopter+Assembly), and [Shivan Reef](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Reef). [Thopter Assembly](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thopter+Assembly) is even getting some new Kaladesh-themed art!

![Thopter Assembly](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/c4rd4r7_efw6et2EnN.jpg)

[Thopter Assembly](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thopter+Assembly) | Art by [Svetlin Velinov](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Svetlin+Velinov%22%5d)

As for the other side, we've got something a bit special planned for that card. We'll explain as we get closer to the release date (April 6, natch). But I can tell you that the card is [Ezuri, Renegade Leader](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ezuri%2C+Renegade+Leader), and he's getting new art that looks a bit like this:

![Ezuri, Renegade Leader](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/c4rd4r7_Nl7hXUlXtq.jpg)

[Ezuri, Renegade Leader](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ezuri%2C+Renegade+Leader) | Art by [Victor Adame Minguez](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Victor+Adame+Minguez%22%5d)

Obviously, Ezuri will be bringing some Elf friends (and friends of Elves) with him. [Elvish Archdruid](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Archdruid), [Regal Force](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Regal+Force), and [Sylvan Advocate](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Advocate) will all be making appearances. [Elvish Archdruid](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Archdruid) even gets updated art!

![Elvish Archdruid](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/c4rd4r7_0hYaRzDxop.jpg)

[Elvish Archdruid](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Archdruid) | Art by [Raymond Swanland](https://web.archive.org/web/20180206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Raymond+Swanland%22%5d)

It's a safe bet there will be some other Elves involved as well. You'll find out more as we get closer to that April 6 release date!
