With each new **Magic** set we get to show off a handful of new tokens that we're printing. Usually this is four or maybe five new tokens. For *Modern Masters* we are creating a slew of tokens to support the cards, some of which are token cards that are being made for the first time ever!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fe/48/fe4877ed2637e82c20cb263519b2bc2933f9d778454a738d8892b31f13ff1157.png)

Behold the mighty Giant Warrior! Originally printed for *Morningtide*, it is back again but with all new art!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/81/c7/81c7e4fb506a1cbb4a3cb342b088afae91797584812464dfdfc92215c456b1d4.png)

There have been two Faerie Rogue tokens made, one as mono-black in *Morningtide*, and the Blue-Black token in *Shadowmoor*. This is the latter brought up to *Modern Masters*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ef/1e/ef1ea9abdb54047a6f6868f74b557d942974e611465eb5d6a98f8cb4a6545e15.png)

Another *Lorwyn* token!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f5/93/f5933d14c13bd172c5c1257cf42039cb29af125d85f83b77abe918090b536ce4.png)

Here are our valiant white Soldier tokens from *Scars of Mirrodin*. I really love this art.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f6/9d/f69df68a9c38bad2bac60b17eb22ae1952cc60b569bcb7c7d036480fd9c45d5f.png)

We've made 2/2 Illusion tokens before during *Zendikar* but this is the first time we've done a 1/1.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/14/03/1403bcf155843bc4e90c865f521d862645267d174238461e2c2240d0c58d4b15.png)

And here is a completely new token! Bats have been around **Magic** for a while but they've never had a token card printed in any shape. Until now.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d1/1f/d11f47bf44a0061958ea938f6f92824aa2bcf7e8395ae9ca516dd8e898f01a33.png)

Here's a *Lorwyn* token with new art!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2f/fc/2ffcbcbfead1fdbc30cc62434e13a196060a8a64ce486be8374bbf2c512c6383.png)

Now here's a brand new token! We've done 1/2 green Spider tokens before but this is the first time we've done one for black and as a 2/4.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/33/81/3381cde14c41e959115e8311929ed7900db0d0bb273c5f230a8e4a793fc02636.png)

We've seen a lot of Zombie tokens over the years; this one comes from ***Magic** 2010* and ***Magic** 2011* and now *Modern Masters*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6a/c0/6ac07c53cb25c34f5ebfb5f5f38ff286b5915761bd060342417f7f8eef0919ac.png)

Who doesn't love the 4/4 Flying red Dragon? The 1/1 white Soldiers, that's who.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/64/80/64808b41dc346ebc118d2ce748c9e175a4275dbff04c8fd8b58594cd2253dff9.png)

Goblins, goblins, goblins. This Goblin token was introduced in *10th Edition* and has been used quite a few times since then.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/bd/49/bd496ae7ca2570c92fe1f375a0d630ab0c389f94157ae3aca77ca7d5523bcf6d.png)

There are Elemental tokens of many colors and sizes. This 4/4 green Elemental token comes from *Lorwyn*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8b/95/8b95970462cc547a82d5698370685288939ac8b5d4f8c3e2a19bcca8198e99ef.png)

There have been numerous Saproling tokens made over **Magic**'s history, most recently for *Return to Ravnica*. This is the Saproling token art from the *Duel Decks: Phyrexia vs. The Coalition*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9d/44/9d441fcda18a5488dc0f1f5d22457a117da8eca10120d9483c4d3f89e964df1e.png)

Originally we printed the Treefolk Shaman token in *Morningtide*, but here is some new art for the Treefolk Shaman token!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/30/11/3011a5f9e38aa858df94e961b597e058e5cf36c9de7908183c2e1a38ad3dc971.png)

This token art was originally released in *Eventide* for [Worm Harvest](https://web.archive.org/web/20130530000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Worm+Harvest). However, unlike *Eventide*, [Worm Harvest](https://web.archive.org/web/20130530000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Worm+Harvest) is now an uncommon, rather than being a rare.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/81/df/81df9b64c8fc16cd740de1f5ed092fda23011b31ade90fb1d7c84fd10535a225.png)

And we wrap this up with *Modern Masters*'s new [Elspeth](https://web.archive.org/web/20130530000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth) emblem card! While she was the first Planeswalker who gave players emblems, we did not make an emblem card until the *Duel Decks: Venser vs. Koth*, which had an emblem for [Venser](https://web.archive.org/web/20130530000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Venser).
