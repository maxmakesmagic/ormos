Looking for something fun to try at this week's Friday Night **Magic**? How about this spicy red-green number from the world of **Magic Online**! Featuring a smattering of acceleration, the deck hopes to quickly deploy four-drops like Hero of Oxid Ridge and Goblin Ruinblaster. It also controls the ground using removal like Arc Trail, Dismember, and Forked Bolt!
