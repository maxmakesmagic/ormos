From the [Kamigawa Glossary](/en/articles/arcana/2004/09-14-a-kamigawa-glossary-part-1):

> **nezumi** (nay-ZOO-mee) Rat. In Kamigawa, short for nezumi-bito, meaning “ratfolk.”

During Kamigawa's war with its own kami, the black-aligned nezumi mostly looked out for their own interests. They lived in Takenuma Swamp among the rotten bamboo, scavenging shabby equipment from the swamp or stealing goods from travelers. They favor chain weapons, such as this burly fellow.

![Nezumi Cutthroat art by Carl Critchlow](https://i.ormos.uk/02/74/02749e7279ed5931a8b36ae0527a8999f5fdd458b8e426d5f31891e3b61bd9e8.jpg)  
 *Nezumi Cutthroat art by Carl Critchlow* 

The nezumi were greedy, treacherous opportunists, mercenaries and thieves -- often involved in deals with other races who needed to get the nastiest sorts of jobs done.

![Night Dealings art by Darrell Riche](https://i.ormos.uk/0c/3e/0c3e9e72a0bf3e6d820e1bae74b7df33dc2f1ee870bc7b35c8e13bd3569bb63f.jpg)  
 *Night Dealings art by Darrell Riche* 

The nezumi were also talented shamans (check out the great shamanic details here -- the fish strapped to his thigh armor and the lizard hanging from his staff)...

![Nezumi Bone-Reader art by Dan Scott](https://i.ormos.uk/ec/63/ec63e6d6c701908dce7637bf3db16dabfa8c5e24e97b6551f7fcc0158a349b0c.jpg)  
 *Nezumi Bone-Reader art by Dan Scott* 

...and in some cases, worked their way up from common graverobbers to legendary necromancers.

![Nighteyes the Desecrator / Nezumi Graverobber art by Jim Nelson](https://i.ormos.uk/b8/7e/b87e4d5432014fb9b41b0279b7b03c2a018fdc97e1eb993f647951c7038e73ac.jpg)  
 *Nighteyes the Desecrator / Nezumi Graverobber art by Jim Nelson*
