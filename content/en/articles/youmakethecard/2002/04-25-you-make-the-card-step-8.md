I'm glad we did that vote, as it prompted a lot of discussion. But the masses have spoken, and Mr. Babycakes will be able to attack.

Time to move on to the size of the creature. This will be a multiple step process that begins with choosing a size range. Be aware that the mana cost is directly correlated to size. You have three choices:

*   **Small** -- The creature’s power and toughness when added together will be 4 or less. The projected mana cost will be from two to four mana.
*   **Medium** -- The creature’s power and toughness when added together will be from 5 to 9. The projected mana cost will be from four to six mana.
*   **Large** -- The creature’s power and toughness when added together will be 10 or greater. The projected mana cost will be six or more mana.

The vote will begin tomorrow (we’re trying to give you all a little chance to [discuss](https://web.archive.org/web/20020425000000/http://boards.wizards.com/tcg-bin/ultimatebb.cgi?ubb=get_topic;f=44;t=000504) the options before voting begins) and will end at midnight on Sunday, May 5th. Once the size range is selected, we will move on two the second part where we give you all the applicable power/toughness combinations along with what we feel are appropriate mana costs.
