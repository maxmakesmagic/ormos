**What was the best card in your *Magic 2015* sealed deck on Day 1?** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/02_QQ_HuangHaoShan.jpg)

***Huang Hao-Shan:** "[Soul of Theros](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soul+of+Theros) won so many games, it's just so good."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/02_QQ_SohWengHeng.jpg)

***Soh Weng Heng:** "I had 3 [Covenant of Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Covenant+of+Blood). They allowed me to buy back damage lost in the early game."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/02_QQ_AnthonyHallett.jpg)

***Anthony "Ranggor" Hallett:** "[Generator Servant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Generator+Servant). Won the day so many times with [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic) to bring out a turn four [Siege Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Dragon)."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/02_QQ_MaitlandCameron.jpg)

***Maitland Cameron:** "Triple [Triplicate Spirits](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triplicate+Spirits)."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/02_QQ_ParkJunYoung.jpg)

***Park Jun Young:** "[Spirit Bonds](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spirit+Bonds). Even though I also had [Avacyn, Guardian Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avacyn%2C+Guardian+Angel) and [Soul of Theros](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soul+of+Theros) in my deck, [Spirit Bonds](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spirit+Bonds) did most of the work with my 2-drops."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/02_QQ_MattGriffin.jpg)

***Matt Griffin:** "[Reclamation Sage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reclamation+Sage). Took down seven rares by itself, including [Spirit Bonds](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spirit+Bonds) and [Scuttling Doom Engine](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scuttling+Doom+Engine)."*
