The card [Mana Drain](https://web.archive.org/web/20130509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Drain) is absurd. At twice the mana it still seems extremely powerful. While it's possible to build a deck around exploiting eight+ mana spells with [Plasm Capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20130509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plasm+Capture) as a supplemental ramp spell, I think it's better to take advantage of the mana offered by [Plasm Capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20130509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plasm+Capture) to allow a deck to do something it wouldn't normally be capable of doing, to avoid flooding on redundant accelerators. 

![Plasm Capture](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Plasm+Capture)

To do this, I've build a deck that can cast all of its spells while playing a normal game of **Magic** but that can use [Plasm Capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20130509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plasm+Capture) to do something bigger than it could otherwise do or cast a threat while leaving mana up. It functions as a kind of strange, delayed-blast [Rewind](https://web.archive.org/web/20130509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rewind), if that makes sense. 

This deck is designed to play as much as possible at instant speed, and especially to make any turn when I pass with four mana particularly difficult for my opponent, as I could counter his or her spell, play a 5/5, or do both if I have [Rewind](https://web.archive.org/web/20130509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rewind). [Ætherling](https://web.archive.org/web/20130509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86therling) will often be the best thing to do with mana from [Plasm Capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20130509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plasm+Capture), which will allow me to put it into play with mana to protect it, and it should be able to win races pretty easily from there. 

The sideboard offers [Trostani](https://web.archive.org/web/20130509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trostani) against aggressive decks, which can populate tokens from [Advent of the Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Advent+of+the+Wurm), [Thragtusk](https://web.archive.org/web/20130509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thragtusk), or the [Voice of Resurgence](https://web.archive.org/web/20130509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voice+of+Resurgence) that I'll bring in to block early. [Voice of Resurgence](https://web.archive.org/web/20130509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voice+of+Resurgence) also makes things extremely difficult for my opponent if I play against another control deck.
