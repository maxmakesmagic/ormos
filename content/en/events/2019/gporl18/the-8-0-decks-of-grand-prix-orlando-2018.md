997 players turned up to play Standard at Grand Prix Orlando. After 8 rounds of play, 7 of them ended the day with perfect 8-0 records. They were Jacob Tilk, Luke Feeney, Patrick Tilsen, Andrew Calderon, Vincent Daniels, and Kyle Cooper. Mountains (and [Goblin Chainwhirler](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Chainwhirler)s) continued to be well represented, but we also had some variety in Mono-Blue Storm and Esper Control to round out the undefeated bracket.
