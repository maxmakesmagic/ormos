Rich Hagon and Brian David-Marshall run through the major lessons learned at [Pro Tour *Journey into Nyx*](https://web.archive.org/web/20140529000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/chapins-journey-takes-him-pro-tour-title). They spotlight the top *Theros* Block cards that you can expect to see making their way into Standard, run through the key changes to the full-block draft environment with Marshall Sutcliffe, and honor long-time pro Patrick Chapin on his first Pro Tour victory.
