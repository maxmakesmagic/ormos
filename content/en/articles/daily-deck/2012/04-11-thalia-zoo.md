Modern PTQs might have come to a halt for this season, but that doesn't mean the format is showing any sign of slowing down! **Magic Online** is still pushing the robust new metagame ahead with a constant stream of tournaments. One older deck that has appeared out of the woodwork recently? Zoo!

Even with Wild Nacatl out of the picture, **Magic Online** player Rous Ita has done well with his version that uses Thalia, Guardian of Thraben. Featuring a full four copies of the *Dark Ascension* legend, Rous Ita uses the Innistrad storyline's key character to slow down decks like Storm to a grinding halt. When all of your mana-producing spells and cantrips cost an extra mana to cast, generating a lethal storm count is nearly impossible.

In addition to Thalia, the Zoo deck still has all of the classic non-Nacatl pieces. Aggressively costed creatures fill up the one- and two-drop slots. This version also has a more burn-heavy bent, using eleven burn spells in addition to a full set of Grim Lavamancer to knock down creatures and players alike.

If you like attacking and sending fire to the opponent's face in Modern, this is going to be a deck to keep an eye on. Don't miss the decklist below!

Rous Ita's Zoo 

Modern – 3-1 in Magic Online Daily Event #3681526
