During its reign in Standard, [Squadron Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20140603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squadron+Hawk) was an integral part of one of the most feared Standard decks in the history of **Magic**, Caw-Blade. The reason for the deck name is simply the combination of equipping a [Squadron Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20140603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squadron+Hawk) with a Sword of some variety. **Magic Online** user Schutz2kill has brought this basic strategy to Modern, even if [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) and [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic) are left behind. 

![Squadron Hawk](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Squadron+Hawk)

The core of the Caw-Blade strategy is to play a controlling game while threatening to get in a hit with one of your Swords. In this case, [Sword of Fire and Ice](https://web.archive.org/web/20140603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+Fire+and+Ice) and [Sword of Feast and Famine](https://web.archive.org/web/20140603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+Feast+and+Famine) are the Swords of choice. The beauty of this strategy is that your creatures feel like they will never go away. 

> "[Squadron Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20140603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squadron+Hawk), search up two more. Equip Sword."  
>  "Kill it? Okay, I'll cast [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20140603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls). Equip Sword."  
>  "Kill it? Okay, [Elspeth, Knight-Errant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Knight-Errant). Make a token. Equip Sword."

At some point, opposing creature removal will run out, and getting to trigger any Sword is a great feeling and often leads to game wins. Meanwhile, this deck has cards like [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20140603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak), [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20140603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember), and [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) to help keep the game under control while poking down opposing life totals and threatening to hit with one of the Swords.
