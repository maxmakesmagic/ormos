## Columnists

*   ![Mark Rosewater](https://i.ormos.uk/91/20/91202e53a27ff33ec4d6b0b4c8cf4ab7c7b82f8f1c2e04db951dafec5491484a.jpg)

    ### Mark Rosewater

    MAKING MAGIC

    Working for Magic R&D since October, 1995, Mark Rosewater is currently the head designer. His hobbies include spending time with his family, talking about Magic on every known medium (including a daily blog and a weekly podcast), and writing about himself in the third person.

    [SEE HIS ARTICLES](https://web.archive.org/web/20190401013037/https://magic.wizards.com/articles/archive/116841)

*   ![Bruce Richard](https://i.ormos.uk/91/c7/91c77f806a552f569b3a54d92fa0da00a20022e32fe2881d12357421baec9b3e.jpg)

    ### Bruce Richard

    SERIOUS FUN

    Bruce's games invariably involve a kitchen table, several opponents, crazy plays, and many laughs. Bruce believes that if anyone at your table isn't having fun playing Magic, then you are doing it wrong.

    [SEE HIS ARTICLES](https://web.archive.org/web/20190401013037/https://magic.wizards.com/articles/archive/117916)

*   ![Gavin Verhey](https://i.ormos.uk/9d/61/9d61858fa0d3722d027f1a6256ab4433397afefd365cf57cde3d439262bb326c.jpg)

    ### Gavin Verhey

    ReCONSTRUCTED

    When Gavin Verhey was eleven, he wanted a job making Magic cards. Ten years later, his dream was realized as his combined success as a professional player, deck builder, and writer brought him into Wizards R&D during 2011. He's been writing Magic articles since 2005 and has no plans to stop.

    [SEE HIS ARTICLES](https://web.archive.org/web/20190401013037/https://magic.wizards.com/articles/archive/117690)

*   ![Masrshall Sutcliffe](https://i.ormos.uk/33/b0/33b00b5cd5f8d4165a2e0e2acf8bd5f606a904fcd78f66e4fe9ccf381634e059.jpg)

    ### Masrshall Sutcliffe

    LIMITED INFORMATION

    Marshall Sutcliffe hosts the Limited Resources podcast, does Pro Tour and Grand Prix video coverage, writes articles, and produces strategy videos. Marshall came back to Magic after discovering Limited following a long hiatus from the game, but he enjoys all forms of the game. He lives in Seattle, WA.

    [SEE HIS ARTICLES](https://web.archive.org/web/20190401013037/https://magic.wizards.com/articles/archive/152186)

*   ![Mike Rosenberg](https://i.ormos.uk/49/60/4960bd8e58301b0f4a5619075bdcebf29a26ffc9befa31b4d71fa3b18cf87ff1.jpg)

    ### Mike Rosenberg

    ORGANIZED PLAY

    Mike Rosenberg is a writer and gamer and has been part of the Magic text coverage team since 2011. These days, he spends less time globetrotting around the world for Grand Prix and more time in Renton, WA, working as the organized play content specialist for Wizards of the Coast.

    [SEE HIS ARTICLES](https://web.archive.org/web/20190401013037/https://magic.wizards.com/articles/archive/162121)

*   ![Adam Styborski](https://i.ormos.uk/59/3f/593f4a20fb21612e27fc0dd20dd471ef7de9d33ee56da72735677de5bf719266.jpg)

    ### Adam Styborski

    COMMAND TOWER

    Adam "Stybs" Styborski joined DailyMTG.com in 2009 to take over Serious Fun, before switching over to begin Command Tower in 2013. With his passion for Commander and community inclusion, you'll find plenty of opportunity each week to share your thoughts about everyone's favorite casual format.

    [SEE HIS ARTICLES](https://web.archive.org/web/20190401013037/https://magic.wizards.com/articles/archive/156066)

*   ![Jacob Van Lunen](https://i.ormos.uk/c5/d1/c5d14ac5294264e88ba2fe8736859feee50ced2c8d629060a4ef818b5cae047a.jpg)

    ### Jacob Van Lunen

    PERILOUS RESEARCH

    Jacob Van Lunen began playing Magic in 1995. He has participated in organized play at every level of competition and was a member of the winning team at Pro Tour San Diego in 2007, thanks to an innovative draft strategy. As a writer, Van Lunen has had more than three hundred Magic strategy pieces published.

    [SEE HIS ARTICLES](https://web.archive.org/web/20190401013037/https://magic.wizards.com/articles/archive/117123)

*   ![Luis Scott Vargas](https://i.ormos.uk/c6/c0/c6c03952af0d13cb961dfb56c85b217e3ca24733dba15f3df00c9f208fb394ba.jpg)

    ### Luis Scott Vargas

    TOP DECKS

    Luis Scott-Vargas plays, writes, and makes videos about Magic. He has played on the Pro Tour for almost a decade, and between that and producing content for ChannelFireball, often has his hands full (of cards).

    [SEE HIS ARTICLES](https://web.archive.org/web/20190401013037/https://magic.wizards.com/articles/archive/116769)

*   ![Brian David - Marshall](https://i.ormos.uk/3e/a7/3ea7d73546c98b2e6d24f9bcab40c596940ebf1c3b96a1022fedf2ce0efcfc42.jpg)

    ### Brian David - Marshall

    THE WEEK THAT WAS

    Brian David-Marshall is a New York–based game designer who has been involved with Magic since 1994, when he started organizing tournaments and ran a Manhattan game store. Since then, he has been a judge, a player, and one of the longest-tenured columnists on DailyMTG.com, as he enters his second decade writing for the site. He is also the Pro Tour Historian and one of the commentators for the Pro Tour.

    [SEE HIS ARTICLES](https://web.archive.org/web/20190401013037/https://magic.wizards.com/articles/archive/156291)

*   ![Sam Stoddard](https://i.ormos.uk/1b/dd/1bdd2bddc5d0335bc2a27cc91bafcdf0a3f7e9c9933424ebb78af4d76206b004.jpg)

    ### Sam Stoddard

    LATEST DEVELOPMENTS

    Sam Stoddard came to Wizards of the Coast as an intern in May 2012. He is currently a game designer working on final design and development for Magic: The Gathering.

    [SEE HIS ARTICLES](https://web.archive.org/web/20190401013037/https://magic.wizards.com/articles/archive/156276)

*   ![Blake Rasmussen](https://i.ormos.uk/ac/0e/ac0ec4664f570b6003fc83e5a66fa8719f6c9bc4067772772ee31ffdb5cca496.jpg)

    Blake is the content manager for DailyMTG.com, making him the one you should email if you have thoughts on the website, good or less good (or not good). He's a longtime coverage reporter and hasn't turned down a game of Magic in any format ever.

    [SEE HIS ARTICLES](https://web.archive.org/web/20190401013037/https://magic.wizards.com/articles/archive/116421)
