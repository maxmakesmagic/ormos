![Sarpadian Empires Vol VII](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Sarpadian+Empires+Vol+VII)

Until*Time Spiral*, the story of the Sarpadian Empires (also known as the *Fallen Empires* set) had only six volumes. But with the introduction of the long-awaited Volume VII, it seems like a good time to go back and look at the story of the previous volumes, as far as we can tell from the fragments that appear on flavor text:

### Volume I

Volume I of *Sarpadian Empires* sets the scene for the set: Orcs and Goblins are attacking the Dwarves and the Order of Leitbur while the Elves are dealing with climate changes by breeding Thallids.

### Volume VII

Of course, *Time Spiral* brings us the previously-unknown *[Sarpadian Empires, Vol. VII](https://web.archive.org/web/20061005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sarpadian+Empires%2C+Vol.+VII)*. And what does it do? By itself, it replicates five cards that are central to the story of the Sarpadian Empires: it lets you create a white Citizen (like the [Icatian Town](https://web.archive.org/web/20061005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Icatian+Town)!), blue Camarid ([Homarid Spawning Bed](https://web.archive.org/web/20061005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Homarid+Spawning+Bed)!), black Thrull ([Breeding Pit](https://web.archive.org/web/20061005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Breeding+Pit)), red Goblin ([Goblin Warrens](https://web.archive.org/web/20061005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Warrens)), or green Saproling ([Night Soil](https://web.archive.org/web/20061005000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Night+Soil)).
