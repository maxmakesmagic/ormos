Before **Magic** went on sale, it was playtested with cards made with clip art. We've shown them several times:

*   **[Alpha Playtest Cards](/en/articles/arcana/2003/05-22-alpha-playtest-cards)** (May 22, 2003)
*   **[More Alpha Playtest Cards](/en/articles/arcana/2008/06-09-more-alpha-playtest-cards)** (June 9, 2008)
*   **[Alpha Playtest Art](https://web.archive.org/web/20120705000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/1665)** (August 28, 2008)
*   **[Alpha Cards That Didn't Make It](https://web.archive.org/web/20120705000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/218)** (June 24, 2009)
*   **[Alpha Cards That Didn't Make It](https://web.archive.org/web/20120705000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/308)** (October 28, 2009)
*   **[They Didn't Make It](https://web.archive.org/web/20120705000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/527)** (May 19, 2010)
*   **[Return of the Alpha Playtest Cards](https://web.archive.org/web/20120705000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/527)** (August 31, 2010)
*   **[Alpha: Setting the Record Straight](https://web.archive.org/web/20120705000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/542)** (September 21, 2010)
*   [**Alpha Playtest Cards: Flavorless Edition**](https://web.archive.org/web/20120705000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/807) (September 27, 2011)

That doesn't count the times [Mark Rosewater](https://web.archive.org/web/20120705000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/mm/81) has shown them. It's just Arcanas. And probably not all of the Arcanas, either.

Today, we're going to look at iconic **Magic** cards (a couple of which are in ***Magic** 2013*) and then show the original version. Don't be confused by the mana costs; during playtesting, "4RR" would mean that a card cost a total of four mana, of which two had to be red.
