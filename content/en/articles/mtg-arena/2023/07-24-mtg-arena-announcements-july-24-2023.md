## Get Ready for 2023 Rotation and Renewal

![Graphical representation of sets that will be rotating in Standard and Alchemy](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/67ljephudutr.jpg) 

September is a big month in the world of *Magic*, and this September is especially big for a couple reasons. [*Wilds of Eldraine* arrives September 8](https://web.archive.org/web/20230724000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/wilds-of-eldraine), and with it comes the yearly set rotation where older sets move out of the Standard format to make room new sets.

This year introduces a change to Standard rotation—specifically, Standard will not rotate this year as we shift to rotating sets out every three years instead of every two. Alchemy, however, *will* rotate this year as *MTG Arena* enters its yearly Renewal.

Renewal is an exciting time in *MTG Arena*, not only because of set rotations, but also for all the special rewards you can get to help kick off the new *Magic* year! Get the full scoop on what's coming and what to expect in the article [Renewal for *MTG Arena*'s 2023 Standard Rotation](https://web.archive.org/web/20230724000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/mtg-arena-renewal-rotation).

## Available Now: Historic Anthology 7 and Explorer Anthology 3

![Deathrite Shaman artwork showing a dark-robed elf in a cowl with a skull hovering above his outstretched hand](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/haswesw5xifa.jpg) 

Two new card anthologies are now available in *MTG* Arena! Historic Anthology 7 and Explorer Anthology 3 together introduce 50 previously unavailable cards to the game that bring new dimensions to the Historic and Explorer formats:

*   [Historic Anthology 7 Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20230724000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/card-image-gallery/historic-anthology-7)
*   [Explorer Anthology 3 Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20230724000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/card-image-gallery/explorer-anthology-3)

Plus, for those who missed it the first time around, you can also pick up [Explorer Anthology 2](https://web.archive.org/web/20230724000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/explorer-anthology-2-arrives-december-13) with its 25 cards until September 5!

## Watch Pro Tour Barcelona This Weekend

![Tableau of the casters hosting the stream of Pro Tour The Lord of the Rings in Barcelona](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/protour_LTR_castergraphic.jpg) 

The last Pro Tour of the 2023 season is this weekend in beautiful Barcelona, July 28–30. [MagicCon: Barcelona](https://mcbarcelona.mtgfestivals.com/) will host 280 of *Magic*'s best players in Pro Tour *The Lord of the Rings* as they vie for a share of the $500,000 in prizes and the prestige of being the best of the best!

For those of us who can't make it to Barcelona, we can still keep up with the action. All three days of the event will be streamed at [twitch.tv/magic](https://twitch.tv/magic). Find details on how to watch, who's playing, who's casting, and the schedule in the [Pro Tour *The Lord of the Rings* Viewers Guide](https://magic.gg/news/pro-tour-the-lord-of-the-rings-viewers-guide)

## Buy the *Magic* Anniversary Sleeve Next Week!

![Happy Birthday Magic Bundle featuring the Magic Anniversary weathered card back sleeve](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/bundle_weathered_sleeve.jpg) 

The *MTG Arena* Summer Sale happens next week, and among the cool stuff you'll find will be the *Magic* Anniversary weathered card back sleeve from April Fool's! Players loved the sleeve, and this will be the first time you can purchase it! Look for the Happy Birthday *Magic* Bundle next week.

While you wait for the sale to kick off, check out Ian Adams's latest article that recounts [the story behind the April Fool's battlefield and weathered card back sleeve](https://web.archive.org/web/20230724000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/making-the-april-fools-battlefield-and-other-battlefields).

## Gen Con in Indianapolis Begins August 3

[![Gen Con logo](https://images.gencon.com/2020.gencon.logo.black.png)](/en/articles/announcements/2023/07-18-celebrate-30-years-and-beyond-of-magic-at-gen-con) 

Gen Con is one of the biggest game conventions around, and [Wizards of the Coast will be at Gen Con in Indianapolis, August 3–6](/en/articles/announcements/2023/07-18-celebrate-30-years-and-beyond-of-magic-at-gen-con)! *MTG Arena*'s own Ian Adams will be there, and we'll be talking about *Magic* over the past 30 years, where we are now, and what to look forward to in the future.

### Qualifier Events

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events. Check out the standings to see [who's qualified for Arena Championship 4](https://magic.gg/standings/arena-championship-4).

Qualifier tokens are delivered in your *MTG Arena* inbox. Remember to claim them before the event starts!
