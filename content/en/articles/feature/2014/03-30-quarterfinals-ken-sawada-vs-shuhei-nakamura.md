**Game One** 

Nakamura was on the play and promptly removed [Precinct Captain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Precinct+Captain) with [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight), and forced Sawada to discard [Ephara, God of the Polis](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ephara%2C+God+of+the+Polis) with [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize). 

However, he was on the receiving end of a blistering assault, fueled by [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos) as well as [Lyev Skyknight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lyev+Skyknight) and [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault). 

When Sawada used [Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price) to kill Nakamura's [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon), it felt like he was truly behind. Nakamura did have [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall) to deal with Brimaz but he was already down to a precarious 5 life. 

Lyev Skynight and [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) mopped things up and both players reached for their sideboards. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/qf_sawada.JPG)

  

Ken Sawada assembles a formidable army, championed by [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos). 

**Ken Sawada 1 - Shuhei Nakamura 0** 

**Game Two** 

Sawada was on the back-foot this time, facing Turn 2 [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) and Turn 4 [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon) from Nakamura. It seemed like Nakamura was ready to retaliate, using [Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price) to destroy [Precinct Captain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Precinct+Captain), and [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) to remove [Ephara, God of the Polis](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ephara%2C+God+of+the+Polis) once again. 

Despite the resistance, Sawada proceeded to bolster his army with [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos) and Keening Apparition. However, those were not effective solutions to a [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon), and Sawada was forced to sacrifice his smaller creatures just to keep the demon tapped, and himself alive. Bear in mind that the number of rats were also multiplying as we speak. 

With the life totals at 13 to 7 in Nakamura's favor, [Obzedat, Ghost Council](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Obzedat%2C+Ghost+Council) arrived too late to make a difference. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/qf_nakamura.JPG)

 *Shuhei Nakamura equalizes the score with [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) and [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon).* 

  

**Ken Sawada 1 - Shuhei Nakamura 1** 

**Game Three** 

Game Three was close to perfect for Sawada. He had [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) to deny Nakamura of his trusty [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) and proceeded to assemble an army of [Soldier of the Pantheon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soldier+of+the+Pantheon), [Lyev Skyknight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lyev+Skyknight) and [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos). 

Nakamura draw of 5 Swamp, [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) and [Devour Flesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devour+Flesh) was not sufficient to beat Sawada's nearly perfect draw and congratulated Sawada in making the Top 4. Leaving [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos) unanswered really did him in. 

**Ken Sawada 2 - Shuhei Nakamura 1** 

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos)](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos)

  

Ken Sawada advances to the semifinals, thanks to the help of [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos).
