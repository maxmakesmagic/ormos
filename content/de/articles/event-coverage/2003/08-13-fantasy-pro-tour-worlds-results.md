The most popular player was two-time PT Champ Tommi Hovi. Now that Kai Budde's Pro Point total has fallen low enough that he doesn't take up all the available points, he's actually a *less* popular Fantasy Pro Tour pick, falling all the way to 26th. Many players made money from the Team competition, which resulted in lesser-known competitors on national teams being very popular picks. Tuomo Nieminen and Gabe Walls demonstrated that they were capable of taking home large checks on their own, too.
