The blue-black transmute spell **Perplex** represents House Dimir's harsh idea of countermagic. Since Dimir guildmages are dedicated to secrecy, the choice they provide to their victims is simple: stop your activities, or lose your mind.

The art for Perplex underwent quite a progression from initial sketch to final art. Follow artist Tsutomu Kawade through three artistic stages in this interactive look at Perplex's journey to illustration.
