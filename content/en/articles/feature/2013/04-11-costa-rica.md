[![](https://i.ormos.uk/71/5d/715d6aeaf2100f25708e57122234f2cb76b132f901283f65ba2902725904e050.jpg)](/en/events/2012/qualifiers/2012-world-magic-cup-qualifiers)

Fernando Solorzano secured his spot on the Costa Rican national team by winning his [WMCQ](/en/events/2012/qualifiers/2012-world-magic-cup-qualifiers) with the Brad Nelson–designed update of The Aristocrats called Act 2. 

Act 2 turns the aggressive midrange strategy of The Aristocrats into much more of a combo/aggro deck. It often tries to win by killing the opponent with [Blasphemous Act](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blasphemous+Act) because of a combination of [Boros Reckoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Reckoner)s and [Blood Artist](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Artist)s that it controls. Sometimes the game never gets to that point, because [Skirsdag High Priest](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skirsdag+High+Priest) has already summoned an army of Demons that have won the game, or maybe it was something as simple as a [Falkenrath Aristocrat](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Falkenrath+Aristocrat). 

Act 2 keeps The Aristocrats shell of [Cartel Aristocrat](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cartel+Aristocrat) and [Falkenrath Aristocrat](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Falkenrath+Aristocrat) with [Doomed Traveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doomed+Traveler) and [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls) to feed them, and [Skirsdag High Priest](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skirsdag+High+Priest) to beat midrange decks, but it adds [Blood Artist](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Artist) to the mix to take advantage of all those creatures getting sacrificed. 

The core plan of the main deck is simple, focused, and trying to do something very unfair. But when opponents expect that and can sideboard their decks to remove [Blood Artist](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Artist) and [Boros Reckoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Reckoner) to prevent any such combos, or when they don't have their own creatures to fuel the [Blasphemous Act](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blasphemous+Act), Act 2 can shift gears completely during intermission and become an entirely different deck for the second game. 

[Intangible Virtue](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Intangible+Virtue) and [Sorin, Lord of Innistrad](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorin%2C+Lord+of+Innistrad) join [Doomed Traveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doomed+Traveler) and [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls) to play as a white-black token deck, which the spot removal the opponent might have to fight [Boros Reckoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20130411000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Reckoner) can be very bad against. 

Outside of Costa Rica, Brad Nelson himself had success with this deck this weekend in Atlanta, and Jasper Boelens won the WMCQ in the Netherlands with the same deck. So, while very new, this deck already has a substantial pedigree of tournament success, making it a serious deck to consider for the next [World **Magic** Cup Qualifiers on April 20–21](https://web.archive.org/web/20140610104018/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtg/event/worldmagiccup/qualifierlist) .
