* * *

*   *Scourge* cards have been added and are now viewable the **Collection** screen. (On the **Collection** screen, select **Scourge** from the **Sets** dropdown list, then select **\>=** and **0** under **Number owned** to see *Scourge* cards.)  
  

*   Legality formats for Constructed have been changed. All **(no Legions)** options have been changed to **(no Scourge)**. *Legions* cards are now legal by default in Constructed events.
