### Getting to Know You

![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/activity/da1130_cedric_phillips.jpg)

**Name:**  
 Cedric Phillips

**Age:**  
 26

**Occupation:**  
 Founder of The Stream Team, full-time SCGLive broadcaster, NBA addict, and happy to be your source of entertainment. =)

**Twitter:**  
[@CedricAPhillips](https://twitter.com/CedricAPhillips)

**Magic Online username:**  
 Celldweller

**Guild affiliation:**  
 \[unanswered\]

**Where do you live?**  
 Seattle, WA

### You, **Magic**, and **Magic Online**

**How long have you played Magic?**  
 14+ years

**What is the first set you remember from Magic?**  
*Ice Age*. [Woolly Mammoths](https://web.archive.org/web/20121221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Woolly+Mammoths) ftw!

**How did you learn to play Magic, or who taught you?**  
 My best friend in elementary school, John Cross, taught me how to play on the bus. We originally became friends because I couldn't beat Sigma's Dog in *Megaman X* and I caught wind that he could. Then he showed me what a [Polar Kraken](https://web.archive.org/web/20121221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Polar+Kraken) was…

**How long have you played Magic Online?**  
 8+ years.

**Do you stream? If so, what's your channel URL?**  
[www.twitch.tv/ceddyp](http://www.twitch.tv/ceddyp)

**How often do you draft on Magic Online?**  
 If a Limited event is coming up, four or five times a day for two to three weeks until the event begins.

**How often do you play Constructed on Magic Online?**  
 If a Constructed event is coming up, five to eight hours a day for two to three weeks until the event begins.

**Do you play any casual formats on Magic Online?**  
 Nah. I'm too competitive and don't know how to turn it off.

### Magic Online's Beta Client

**Have you tried the Wide Beta client?**  
 Some. I've mostly watched Michael Jacob play on it via his stream.

**What's your favorite change that has been made?**  
 Honestly, not a ton of things have been made that much better. =(

**What are you looking for us to do on it that we haven't done yet?**  
 Leagues were something that people loved with the old client. I also like checking ratings of my opponent and having clan affiliation mean something.

### Your Favorites

**Favorite formats to play on Magic Online:**  
 Legacy

**Favorite draft format:**  
 Three answers actually: MMD, RGD, and M13

**Favorite Magic Card:**  
[Figure of Destiny](https://web.archive.org/web/20121221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Figure+of+Destiny)

**Favorite piece of Magic art:**  
 Currently, I really like the new [Syncopate](https://web.archive.org/web/20121221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syncopate) artwork a lot

**Favorite Planeswalker card:**  
[Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20121221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) (surprised?)

### *Return to Ravnica*

**What do you think of *Return to Ravnica*?**  
 I like the idea of going back to Ravnica but I have fond memories of how things used to be back in my college days, and it would be impossible for anything to come that close again in my opinion.

**Did you play during the original Ravnica?**  
 Far too much.

**Favorite guild to draft in *Return to Ravnica*?**  
 Rakdos.

**What's your favorite non-rare first pack first pick in *Return to Ravnica* draft?**  
[Rix Maadi Guildmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20121221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rix+Maadi+Guildmage)

### Silly Questions

**If you were to get a **Magic** tattoo (or already have one) what would it be?**  
 The full artwork of [Cloudgoat Ranger](https://web.archive.org/web/20121221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudgoat+Ranger) ON MY BACK.

**If you could play Magic against anyone, who would it be and why?**  
 Gabe Walls, because he is impossible to read and is a ton of fun to play against.
