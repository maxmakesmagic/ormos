How far will you go to do what you must? Go on a journey with Walking the Planes to [Grand Prix Strasbourg](/en/events/2013/gpstr13/danes-make-their-mark-in-strasbourg), where we meet a Hall of Famer, a Goblin gamer, a star of the stream, and a wayward American chasing a dream. Like moose in mating season, there's some awkward and silly encounters that you won't soon forget. It's Episode #12: "The Stranger!" 

Follow Walking the Planes on Twitter ([@WalkThePlanes](https://twitter.com/walktheplanes)).

Nathan Holt and Shawn Kornhauser are "hardcore casual" **Magic** players exploring the rich tapestry of the ever-expanding Multiverse. As a team, they've co-produced several experimental independent films, and Walking the Planes is their biggest, baddest effort to date.
