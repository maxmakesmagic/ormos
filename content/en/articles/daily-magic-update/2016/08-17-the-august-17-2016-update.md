# Top 25

1.  Rietzl Roars Back

One week after a disappointing finish at [Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-eldritch-moon) stopped Rietzl from claiming the end-of-season rewards he was in line for, he started the new season off with a bang, making the Top 8 at [Grand Prix Portland](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-portland-2016) and setting himself up nicely for the 2016–2017 season.

Which begs the question for you newcomers—what exactly are you seeing here? A quick lesson: the Top 25 ranks players on their past performance over the last calendar year, so everything from August 16, 2015 until August 16, 2016. So what you see crosses over two Pro Seasons; the 2015–2016 season ended last week in Sydney. Thus, the rankings right now pretty closely match where everyone ended up at the end of last season.

Got it? Great.

Besides Rietzl, there wasn't a ton of movement. Steve Rubin slid up two spots, as did Shota Yasooka, who also did well on the Grand Prix circuit last weekend. Meanwhile, Petr Sochůrek finished [second at Grand Prix Rimini](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-rimini-2016), helping him enter the Top 25. Dropping off the rankings are Michael Majors and Andrew Brown. Trading two for one seems like an odd deal for a Top 25, but last week's went to 26 due to ties.

# Deck of the Day

1.  Paul Rietzl's Vintage Spirits

Paul Rietlz might just be the best thing to happen to the Vintage Super League since [Lodestone Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20160817000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lodestone+Golem) was restricted. Playing a white-blue deck that utilizes some of the most disruptive Spirits in the game—with help from a few Humans and Kor—Reitzl won his playoff match against Eric Froehlich in exciting fashion, using [Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160817000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial) to put [Spirit of the Labyrinth](https://web.archive.org/web/20160817000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spirit+of+the+Labyrinth) in play at instant speed against a [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20160817000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor).

Also, note the [Tradewind Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20160817000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tradewind+Rider) in the sideboard. Where else will you see that old-school gem?

The videos will be up on YouTube later, but you can watch the match in its entirety in the [archives for twitch.tv/magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic/v/84025619).
