The August 2008 [Friday Night Magic](https://web.archive.org/web/20080729000000/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=Link%20to:%20http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/fnm) promo card is an alternate-art Desert. The art featured in this promo was illustrated by Glen Angus who passed away in July 2007. This piece of art was actually commissioned for the *Odyssey* set, but it got bumped during an eleventh hour frenzy of art swapping. 

Our story starts with this piece, which needed a home:
