Christopher Schiber was all too excited to play a brand new *Conflux* card at his recent PTQ for Honolulu. The card? Nyxathid. The 7/7 is perfect for a discard deck, and between Augur of Skulls, Smallpox, Stupor, and Thoughtseize, you can bet the rare was a veritable under-costed fattie all weekend long for Christopher.
