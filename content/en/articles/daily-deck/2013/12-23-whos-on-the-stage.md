During the holiday rerun weeks, we are presenting some of the year's most popular Arcana articles. It was a challenge to narrow down to just ten, so we hope you enjoy this look back into 2013!

—DailyMTG Staff

  

* * *

  

Let's talk about [Thespian's Stage](https://web.archive.org/web/20131223000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thespian%27s+Stage). As you may already know, "thespian" is a highfalutin' word for "actor," and the card shows two actors, well, acting:

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Thespian%27s+Stage)](https://web.archive.org/web/20131223000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thespian%27s+Stage)

  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/72/6f/726f689a6c3a04fea0bc932408a70d720d1e432b225991f539584566bab315a6.jpg)

[Thespian's Stage](https://web.archive.org/web/20131223000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thespian%27s+Stage) | Art by [John Avon](https://web.archive.org/web/20131223000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;artist=%5B%22John%20Avon%22%5D)

Now, because this is Arcana, you may have already guessed that the actors are significant. In fact, they're acting out an important moment from the history of Ravnica. Let's look closer!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3e/8e/3e8eebb5c03a51d36ea8b1103db83be4a7865f8a346c02698a81852195ac6c76.jpg)  

Do you recognize them? Click here!

It's [Szadek, Lord of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20131223000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=89092) and [Agrus Kos, Wojek Veteran](https://web.archive.org/web/20131223000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Agrus+Kos%2C+Wojek+Veteran), from the original *Ravnica* block! And they're fighting, which means this is probably the moment when Agrus Kos arrests Szadek, breaking the Guildpact!

You can see what it's like to watch this play in [the Uncharted Realms article](https://web.archive.org/web/20131223000000/https://magic.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/ur/230) that previewed [Thespian's Stage](https://web.archive.org/web/20131223000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thespian%27s+Stage). And you can read the whole story of the original *Ravnica* Cycle in eBook form. More information is [right here](https://web.archive.org/web/20131223000000/https://magic.wizards.com/Magic/Merchandise/Product.aspx?x=mtg/merchandise/ebooks).
