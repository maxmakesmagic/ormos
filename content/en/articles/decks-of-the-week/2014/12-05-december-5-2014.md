Last weekend, San Antonio, Texas, played host to [a Standard Grand Prix](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-san-antonio-2014-coverage-events) won by Ryan Scullin and the Mardu Horde. Meanwhile, across the pond, Hungary's Tamás Nagy won [Grand Prix Strasbourg](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-strasbourg-2014-coverage-events), in France. Congratulations Ryan and Tamás!

But Tamás Nagy isn't in France just for the GP. He's also there for Worlds Week, as a member of Hungary's national team. That's right, it's Worlds Week! By the end of the French day on Sunday, December 7, we will have our newest World Champion and World *Magic* Cup Champions! Catch all the excitement at the [World Championship](/en/events/2014/legacy-daily-2014-11-22/legacy-daily) and [World *Magic* Cup](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2014-world-magic-cup) coverage!

And don't forget, PTQs begin for Pro Tour *Dragons of Tarkir* this weekend, so [be sure to find a PTQ near you](https://web.archive.org/web/20141205000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/protour/qualifierlist) and win your invitation and airfare to the Pro Tour!
