***Magic Online* fifteen-year anniversary article tomorrow, June 7**

*Magic Online* is celebrating its fifteen-year anniversary! Be sure to check out DailyMTG tomorrow for Lee Sharpe’s article celebrating fifteen years of *Magic Online*, as well as details surrounding the anniversary-focused events.

**Team Draft Super League tonight at 5 p.m. PT**

Another episode of Team Draft Super League begins tonight at 5 p.m. PT. Team Draft Super League is a live streamed event Tuesday nights on our Twitch [channel](https://www.twitch.tv/magic).

You can also watch past Team Draft Super Leagues on our YouTube [channel](https://www.youtube.com/user/wizardsmtg).

**Throwback Standard Gauntlet 4: *Mirrodin* Standard begins tomorrow, June 7**

Throwback Standard Gauntlet 4: *Mirrodin* Standard begins tomorrow! Randy Buehler outlined the event details in this [article](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/05-31-throwback-standard-gauntlet-4-mirrodin-standard), which included an in-depth analysis of the decks that will be available to play. The event will begin tomorrow at 10 a.m. PT and will be available until June 14.  
  
 More information about Throwback Standard Gauntlets can be found in Lee Sharpe’s article [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/08-02-flashback-favorites-and-throwback-standard-gauntlets).

***Magic Online Amonkhet* Championships begin tomorrow, June 7**

The *Magic Online Amonkhet* Championships begin tomorrow, June 7, at 12:30 p.m. PT. Refer to last week’s [article](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/05-31-magic-online-amonkhet-championships-and-constructed-ptq-changes) for *Amonkhet* Championship specifics as well as updated information regarding Constructed PTQs.

***Aether Revolt*/*Kaladesh* Redemption ends tomorrow, June 7**

Take advantage of *Magic Online*'s Redemption feature and trade in your *Aether Revolt*/*Kaladesh* card sets before 9:00 a.m. PT tomorrow, June 7.

Redemptions purchased after the downtime on May 24 will be processed on Wednesday, June 14.

Learn more about *Magic Online* Redemption [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20170606000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/gameinfo/products/magiconline/redemption).

***Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifier for Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation* on Saturday, June 10** 

*Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers (RPTQ) for Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation* will take place on Saturday, June 10, and Saturday, June 17. These events are only open to players who qualified via in-store Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifier events – pre-registration is also required.

Additional information about RPTQs can be found [here](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/regional-ptqs-for-pro-tour-hour-of-devastation).

***Magic Online* Format Challenges back this weekend**

The *Magic Online* Format Challenges introduced a new prize structure and recurring weekend events for formats Modern, Vintage, 1v1 Commander, Legacy, and Pauper! Check out Lee Sharpe’s article [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/05-10-magic-online-format-challenges) for specific details about the Format Challenges.

Events begin Saturday, June 10 at 8:00 a.m. PT.
