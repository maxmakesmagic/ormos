* * *

### Mirrodin Leagues, Draft Queues, And Rotation

I know many of you are also wondering what's up with normal *Mirrodin* leagues and draft queues. Draft queues will begin at the same time as the Release Leagues--tomorrow after our regularly scheduled downtime. Traditional four-week *Mirrodin* leagues will begin Monday, December 8 at 9:00 AM (0900 hours) Pacific Standard Time when we stop taking signups for Release Leagues. At the same time, *Mirrodin* cards will officially rotate into the Standard Constructed format (and the *Odyssey* block cards will rotate out).

I think that's everything. We don't know when Premier Events will be back, but everything else seems to be working. Go have fun with *Mirrodin!*
