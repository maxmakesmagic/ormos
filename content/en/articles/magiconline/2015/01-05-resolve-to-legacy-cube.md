Happy New Year, *Magic Online*! The new year is a time of looking forward, of plotting where we want to go in the fresh twelve months ahead. It's also a time of retrospection, though. How can we know where we want to go if we don't assess where we came from? You might say that it's a time to take a long look at one's...

*Legacy*.

That's right! The *Magic Online* Legacy Cube is returning for your gaming pleasure.

Beginning Wednesday, January 14, following the downtime, the [Legacy Cube](/en/articles/magiconline/2014/11-03-the-new-magic-online-legacy-cube) will make its triumphant return to *Magic Online*. Accompanying the Legacy Cube will be triple *Odyssey* Flashback Drafts. Both *Odyssey* and Legacy Cube Events run in Swiss and Single Elimination Draft. Both sets of queues conclude with the Wednesday, January 28, downtime.
