On February 18, *[Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty](https://web.archive.org/web/20220203000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/kamigawa-neon-dynasty)* hits store shelves, and with it comes an update to the 300 cards featured on *The List*! This is a selection of cards from across *Magic*'s history that can appear in the final card slot of Set Boosters 25% of the time. These cards are in addition to *Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty*'s awesome new cards. For a look at what else you can find in the new set, check out these articles:

*   Harless Snyder's [*Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty* Product Overview](/en/articles/feature/2022/01-27-kamigawa-neon-dynasty-product-overview)
*   Max McCall's [Collecting *Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty*](/en/articles/feature/2022/01-27-collecting-kamigawa-neon-dynasty)
*   Clayton Kroh's [*Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty* Booster Fun](/en/articles/feature/2022/01-27-kamigawa-neon-dynastys-booster-fun-lights-it-up)

Cards on *The List* fit well with the set's narrative and mechanical themes, and all rarities are represented, from commons to mythic rares. A card that is on *The List* does not make it legal in Standard, though; these cards are legal in whatever [formats](https://web.archive.org/web/20220203000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/game-info/gameplay/rules-and-formats/formats) they are already legal in.
