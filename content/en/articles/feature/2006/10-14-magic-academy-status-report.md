![Infinite_Hourglass](https://i.ormos.uk/1d/25/1d25b1e63fde081e73d144c2aff33e9fd73469e76cc48c93d59425c6556960b6.jpg)[Last month](/en/articles/feature/2006/09-13-change-on-the-horizon), I posted an article letting people know about the latest batch of changes coming to the site. Those included Kelly Digges taking over as copy editor for magicthegathering.com, The Ferrett taking over as the new columnist for Serious Fun, and that Ted's final Magic Academy article would be coming soon. Since then, Kelly has been doing outstanding, and it's clear from email and the forums that Ferrett has done a great job filling Anthony's giant shoes. As expected though, filling Magic Academy has been a tough job. As the actual target readers of the column will tell you (and have been telling us), Ted did a great job doing exactly what we asked him to do, which was writing a column that could help newer players and newer readers get up to speed and participate at the level the rest of the site (and other sites) are written at. Some people not in the target audience seem to relish quibbling over whether a given article was supposed to be 15th or 16th in the series and other "small picture" issues, but as far as I've been hearing and seeing from the audience this column was intended for, Academy has been a great success so far. 

The reason I'm going in to all this is that I want to make it clear that we're committed to continuing this column. I've said on repeated occasions that I regard it as one of the most important **Magic** columns on the entire Internet. I believed that back when I pitched it as part of my first interview for this job three years ago, and now that I've had a chance to see it in action I'm even more convinced. 

So, with all of that in mind, please know that, one way or the other, we *will* be continuing with this column as soon as possible. Given its importance, and just how difficult it is to write, it's important that we take the time to get it right. So, for now, we're going to put the column on temporary hiatus, but I'm hopeful that we may only be talking a couple weeks or so, and maybe even shorter than that. Though I can't go into details right now, we have a very promising prospect that I'm positive would do an incredible job, and I can pretty much guarantee that anyone that knows his work would be very happy to see him involved. However, for now he's still thinking it over and I want him to take the time he needs to make sure he knows what he's getting in to. He's far and away my first choice, so for now I'm going to wait and see how that works out. 

In the meantime, since I'm sure there will be plenty of speculation anyway; who would *you* actually want to see on this column? As I see it, there are a number of critical skills needed, given the nature of the column and the things we'd like to see it accomplish. 

*   Ability to write for less experienced readers and players without sounding condescending and without sounding watered down.
*   Ability to teach effectively.
*   Ability to see the big picture, and plan accordingly. (IE: setting up the column's plan)
*   Ability to write about complex concepts simply and clearly.
*   Knowledge of **Magic's** history: cards, decks, premier events, people, articles, pretty much everything.
*   Able to write with authority on theory, deckbuilding, general strategy, and more.
*   Ability to pull all that off with an article that's actually readable and engaging.
*   Oh, and actually wants a column like this! :)

And that's just the quick list. So, with all that in mind, take some time to think about who is out there, and who you think could pull it off. There are plenty of writers out there we know and love but that probably wouldn't handle this particular column well. It's a tough job, with a tough audience. If you were the producer, who would you go for?

I look forward to your responses. Hopefully as we move forward I'll have an announcement shortly on the new author. In the meantime, starting next week if needed, we'll start rerunning existing Academy articles in this slot until the new writer starts. If news comes up that's not big enough for a big announcement, but applies to this column, I'll post it to whatever the current message board is for that given week's Magic Academy. 

As I head out, I wanted to give a quick note of thanks for your patience. I know it's tough waiting, so I appreciate those that understand why it's important enough to make sure we get the right person. Hopefully I'll have news for you shortly, but if not, you have my word that we'll be doing everything we can to get the column back up and running with new content as quickly as possible. 

Thanks again,

\- Scott Johns, **magicthegathering.com** producer
