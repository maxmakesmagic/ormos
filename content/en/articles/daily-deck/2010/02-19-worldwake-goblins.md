It's everyone's favorite red tribe, Goblins! Michael Rose took this list to a Top 8 at a large Standard event held recently in Kansas. He won some brand-new packs of *Worldwake* with a new take on an old tribe. Quest for the Goblin Lord is almost assured to go active thanks to Dragon Fodder, Warren Instigator, and Siege-Gang Commander, not to mention a host of support Gobbos waiting in the wings. It's the perfect concoction to test out *Worldwake* at Friday Night **Magic** this weekend!
