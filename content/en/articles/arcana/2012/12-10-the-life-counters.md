Hello! My name's Monty, and I write the **Magic** Arcana column. You're reading it right now! I recently changed desks, and that meant that a lot of old **Magic** things bubbled up to the surface. And that makes for perfect Arcana fodder!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/77/41/7741c44ee61a17536d6f9fa24bde07ae2c59b5459508ca93c3a2b6b518fa1039.jpg)  

These are life counters that vary in age from "a few" to "several" years old. At one point, they were given away to champions of the Arena League in stores:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ce/be/cebea25907ab5657396a6a9976b07db8d03ea27ad5652f02aa065abcb6d15f2a.jpg)  

There was also a set made for the Super Series:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3d/5d/3d5dcacc58991ad5fc07b076dfdf7e70778bbbb4eb562118aa84b55773e72481.jpg)  

Even the Japan-only High School Championship got one:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2c/41/2c41e42cb8a650ada5dc09920613ca2b6f75fed2c792f70c2b7223eda9ca9b1e.jpg)  

But the ones that most people will be familiar with are the set-specific ones. I don't have a full collection, but for the original *Ravnica: City of Guilds* Block, there was one life counter for each guild:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a4/af/a4af4729371c136f512e3fa115c959d511d8302fe95f39ac35b3319247bebf09.jpg)  

There were a few that were made in odd shapes, like this one for the *Ninth Edition* core set:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9c/c4/9cc443f9a60d9c2f21d438f2435979933237abb1eaa8c5248668237c3c6e2b4d.jpg)  

My favorite is this one for *Unhinged*:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f5/4b/f54b3cdfc32a2fcb04732a4db2b0bb01206e8257f17154b1b2bf833c5ceb6e78.jpg)  

What makes that one cool (aside from just being associated with *Unhinged*) is that there were halves in the set (like [Sauté](https://web.archive.org/web/20121210000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Saut%C3%A9), which does 3½ points of damage), so the life counter had to have halves on it as well:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4a/f8/4af8eddb9120744da176ead83d03072596b51623b0f6c2df812121293c21a009.jpg)
