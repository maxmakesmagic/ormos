Interested in trying out the Legacy format? Now you can! Legacy will soon be available to play in tournaments and casually on **Magic Online**. The format, which allows cards from all of **Magic**'s expansions (minus a list of banned cards), allows for all sorts of innovative decks. Take Nick Montaquila's, for example. He recently made the Top 8 of a large Legacy tournament, the StarCityGames.com Open in Indianapolis. He relied on the Goblin tribe to take him to 2nd place, with hits like Goblin Piledriver, Goblin Warchief, Stingscourger, and Goblin Ringleader to reload.
