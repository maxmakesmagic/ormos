With the latest addition to the game now flying off the store shelves, it's time to look at potential decks for the new world of Standard. StarCityGames.com columnist Gavin Verhey recently published this deck list in an article about the powerful new Birthing Pod. Your early creatures combine with the Pod to power out more powerful late-game threats, an exciting new interaction for Standard that's sure to see some play!
