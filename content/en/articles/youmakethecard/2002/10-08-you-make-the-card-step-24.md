Well, the votes are in and no one flavor text managed to run off with the victory. This means that the top three vote-getters will have a runoff to see what [Forgotten Ancient](https://web.archive.org/web/20021008000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=/magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/features/MrBabyCakes8.jpg&origin=mtgcom_feature_97)'s flavor text will be. The options are: (in alphabetical order)

*   "If a tree falls in the forest, I hear it."
*   Its blood is life, its body is growth.
*   The grass is meaner on the other side.

The deadline for the run-off is midnight (EDT), Sunday, October 13th, and afterwards we'll do a wrap-up of the process.

We have preliminary plans to do another "[You Make the Card](https://web.archive.org/web/20041226203216/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/columnarchive&column=youmakethecard)" in the near future, so if you enjoyed this process, join us again for the encore! If you haven't participated yet in the creation of Forgotten Ancient, you still can! I highly encourage you to cast a vote.
