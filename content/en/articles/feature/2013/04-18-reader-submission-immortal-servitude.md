Craig Wescoe was the first person who seriously suggested building an [Immortal Servitude](https://web.archive.org/web/20130418000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Immortal+Servitude) deck in Standard to me, but he was mostly interested in returning a lot of one-mana Humans with [Champion of the Parish](https://web.archive.org/web/20130418000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Champion+of+the+Parish) to create a huge creature. Since then, many others have suggested [Immortal Servitude](https://web.archive.org/web/20130418000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Immortal+Servitude) for two as a combo engine with [Blood Artist](https://web.archive.org/web/20130418000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Artist) and [Cartel Aristocrat](https://web.archive.org/web/20130418000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cartel+Aristocrat) or [Bloodthrone Vampire](https://web.archive.org/web/20130418000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodthrone+Vampire). 

![Immortal Servitude](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Immortal+Servitude)

  

Reader "MrPhysics" suggested today's list, and I like what he's done with it. He has [Lotleth Troll](https://web.archive.org/web/20130418000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotleth+Troll) to potentially discard creatures to speed up his combo, with [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130418000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) to help him play a fair game, and he's minimized the cards that are designed to just fill his graveyard, because he doesn't want to fall behind or make himself too vulnerable. 

[Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130418000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) is a little narrow in Game 1, but that's not necessarily that big of a problem, because in Game 1, you usually want to just flash back your [Grisly Salvage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130418000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grisly+Salvage)s when you can anyway, and in Game 2, when that won't be as valuable, you have access to a lot more spells. 

Also, while it is a seven-mana play, [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130418000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Snapcaster%2BMage) means that [Grisly Salvage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130418000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grisly+Salvage) can potentially find [Immortal Servitude](https://web.archive.org/web/20130418000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Immortal+Servitude), which can be very important. 

If you're looking for a fringe combo deck or you just like playing small creatures, this might be a good deck for you to try out.
