Pro Tour Hall of Famer Brian Kibler finished Day 1 on top of the standings with his take on Temur Aggro. "It's been a while! Now that there are no more Lifebane Zombies, I can actually win!" he exclaimed after his final attack with [Savage Knuckleblade](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Knuckleblade) put him at a pristine 9-0 record. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpla14/Brian%20Kibler%20with%20some%20of%20his%20favorite%20cards.JPG)

*Brian Kibler with some of his favorite cards.* 

He unfortunately lost the first two rounds of Day 2, but I still looked to find out a little more, sitting down with Kibler to get the lowdown on the latest tweaks and card choices. For reference, here's his list.

**Could you tell me how you arrived at this deck?** 

"So I actually went through a number of iterations of all the green clans," Kibler said. "My original Temur decks were all more ramp into monsters, and they weren't good. [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon) in particular seemed pretty bad because it's so weak versus Jeskai, and spending all these slots in your deck to ramp into a flyer when your opponent can just [Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames) it is not worth it." 

"So after having my mana creatures and dragons killed and not winning, I made the deck more aggressive. I still kept the mana creatures that can actually attack and that can be turned into threats with [Boon Satyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boon+Satyr), but I scrapped [Sylvan Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Caryatid) and some copies of [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon) to add [Heir of the Wilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heir+of+the+Wilds). This is where my deck started turning good, and it also made [Temur Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temur+Charm) better, because that's a card that's only good when you're ahead." 

**How important is it to have mana-efficient plays in a deck like this?** 

"A lot of the format comes down to exchanging mana and having good ways to spend your mana on early on," Kibler explained. "Tempo is so important in many matches. [Heir of the Wilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heir+of+the+Wilds), for example, is great because it's good on turn two and never outclassed in the late game." 

**What is the role of Crater's Claws in the deck?** 

"Crater's Claws is one of the super-powerful cards in the deck. It can be either [Shock](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shock) or better than [Fireball](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fireball). Crater's Claws for 0 is actually the way I've cast it the most---being able to play [Savage Knuckleblade](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Knuckleblade) and kill their guy on turn three is a great way to get back tempo." 

**Did you change any cards from your Pro Tour decklist?** 

"I took out [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon) from the maindeck altogether because a five-cost spell that can easily die to [Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames) is too bad. I replaced them by [Ashcloud Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashcloud+Phoenix) and [Sarkhan, the Dragonspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sarkhan%2C+the+Dragonspeaker). Also, at the Pro Tour I was too afraid of [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy) combo so I had a fourth [Stubborn Denial](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stubborn+Denial) and a [Destructive Revelry](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Destructive+Revelry) that I don't have now—I'm playing [Disdainful Stroke](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disdainful+Stroke) instead." 

**How does an ideal draw for the deck look like?** 

"[Yavimaya Coast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yavimaya+Coast), Mountain, [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic), and 4 [Savage Knuckleblade](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Knuckleblade) is the best hand you can get, although you need to draw a third land so you can give it haste. Knuckleballer is the best card in the deck; it's so powerful, it trades up favorably, and it makes [Hunt the Hunter](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hunt+the+Hunter) in the sideboard much better." 

**What are some interesting plays that we might not see right away picking up this deck?** 

"If you don't have a turn-three play, then just morph [Ashcloud Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashcloud+Phoenix) or cast [Boon Satyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20141019000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boon+Satyr) without bestow. You'd rather play them on turn four or five, but it's important to make uses for your mana. You don't have a great long game, so you want to get ahead and get damage in early, so you can eventually kill people with Crater's Claws." 

If you've been working on a Temur deck yourself, then be sure to give this deck a spin!
