## ARTICLES COVERAGE

Sunday Coverage

Twelve Pro Tour Top 8s, Pro Tour Hall of Fame member, Pro Tour champion. It seemed difficult to believe that Brazil’s Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa, already in the conversation with Jon Finkel and Kai Budde as *Magic* history’s greatest player, could continue to make his already stellar resume even more impressive.

Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation* did just that.

Navigating a difficult field with the deck of the tournament—Ramunap Red—Damo da Rosa dodged precarious situations and ultimately ended this weekend in Kyoto, Japan with his second Pro Tour title. Not only that, but Damo da Rosa’s victory earned him the 2016-17 Player of the Year title in what seemed to be insurmountable for frontrunner Márcio Carvalho to lose. It was the first time in *Magic* history that the Player of the Year title has gone to South America.

Damo da Rosa has once again proven why he is one of the game’s all-time best, and that he’s far from slowing down.

Saturday Coverage

Pro Tour Hall of Famer Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa leads the charge into Sunday with his twelfth Pro Tour Top 8! He is joined by the Samuel Pardee in his second Pro Tour Top 8, Samuel Black in his third, and Seth Manfield with his third. Hong Kong’s Yam Wing Chun, Singapore’s Felix Leong, and Japan’s Shintaro Kurata and Yusuke Sasabe round out Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation*’s Sunday competitors with their first Pro Tour Top 8s.

Who will win the $50,000 first place prize and the invitation to 2017’s *Magic: The Gathering* World Championship? Tune in to twitch.tv/magic on Sunday at 9 a.m. local time (12 a.m. UTC) to find out. Remember, because of time zones, that’s Saturday at 5 p.m. PT/8 p.m. ET if you’re in North America.
