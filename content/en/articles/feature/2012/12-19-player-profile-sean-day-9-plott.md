### Getting to Know You

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d7/b2/d7b2edafbc777686c1138dd8d9861127d4e4dc1a1daacc590e342704012480f1.jpg)

**Name:**  
 Sean "Day\[9\]" Plott

**Age:**  
 26

**Occupation:**  
 gamer, shoutcaster, vlogger

**Twitter:**  
[@day9tv](https://twitter.com/day9tv)

**Magic Online username:**  
 I TELL NO ONE!

**Guild affiliation:**  
 Golgari! Yeah scavenge!

**Where do you live?**  
 Los Angeles, CA

### You, **Magic**, and **Magic Online**

**How long have you played Magic?**  
 I've played on and off for over twelve years, but only in extremely low-key kitchen table settings. In the last eight months, I've tried to truly learn the game: drafting, Standard, following tournaments, etc.

**What is the first set you remember from Magic?**  
*Ice Age*! I played before that set, but the imagery from the set has always stuck with me (particularly the art for the snow-covered lands).

**How did you learn to play Magic, or who taught you?**  
 When I was young, I learned the game via a mix of my 6th-grade buddies and the instruction manual. As a result, I had wildly incorrect understandings, each of which was slowly undone over my thirteen years of playing with new people, lol. That said, my friend Case Kiyonaga (of *Legends of the Five Rings* fame) has been teaching me how to play well. He watches most of my drafts and critiques my decisions and soon will be helping me learn Standard.

**How long have you played Magic Online?**  
 Seven months (started in June 2012!)

**Do you stream? If so, what's your channel URL?** [http://www.twitch.tv/day9tv](http://www.twitch.tv/day9tv) (Mostly *StarCraft*, but soon **Magic** drafts too!)

**How often do you draft on Magic Online?**  
 At least five times a week or so. :D I love drafting!

**How often do you play Constructed on Magic Online?**  
 I've never played Constructed. I'm drafting to build my collection and soon I'll be sparring away! :D

**Do you play any casual formats on Magic Online?**  
 I'm a total try hard. Despite my lack of skill, I will valiantly try to gobble up all the rating points!

### Your Favorites

**Favorite formats to play on Magic Online:**  
 Drafting!

**Favorite draft format:**  
 I love 3x RTR drafts, but triple–*New Phyrexia* was extremely fun too. Phyrexian mana made for very interesting splashes and drafting decisions.

**Favorite Magic Card:**  
[Wolfir Silverheart](https://web.archive.org/web/20121219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wolfir+Silverheart). I love the art and having an 8/8 on turn five feels amazing.

**Favorite piece of Magic art:**  
[Wurmcoil Engine](https://web.archive.org/web/20121219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wurmcoil+Engine).

**Favorite Planeswalker card:**  
[Gideon Jura](https://web.archive.org/web/20121219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon+Jura). He's has the coolest Planeswalker name to me. So fun to say. :D

### *Return to Ravnica*

**What do you think of *Return to Ravnica*?**  
 It's incredibly fun to draft! Also, the new cards have made the Standard format super interesting. There seems to be a huge variety of deck builds.

**Did you play during the original *Ravnica*?**  
 I tragically didn't. :\[

**Favorite guild to draft in *Return to Ravnica*?**  
 Golgari, although I don't draft it very much. If I can snatch up a few [Axebane Guardian](https://web.archive.org/web/20121219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Axebane+Guardian)s and [Trestle Troll](https://web.archive.org/web/20121219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trestle+Troll)s, there's no better feeling than casting some enormous seven-drops on turn five.

**What's your favorite non-rare first pack first pick in *Return to Ravnica* draft?**  
[Stab Wound](https://web.archive.org/web/20121219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stab+Wound). That card does SO much damage. There needs to be a ticker on **Magic Online** of the global amount of damage [Stab Wound](https://web.archive.org/web/20121219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stab+Wound) has done to players. It might exceed the national debt.

### Silly Questions

**If you were to get a **Magic** tattoo (or already have one) what would it be?**  
 I'd tattoo a [Wingcrafter](https://web.archive.org/web/20121219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingcrafter) on my arm. When I'm at the club, all the hotties will come up and say "Oooh what's the tattoo all about?" I'd reply, "I'm looking for a hunny to soulbond with. Join me, and you'll feel like you can fly."

**If you could play Magic against anyone, who would it be and why?**  
 I'd want to play my *StarCraft* idol Jaedong in a one-on-one. I'll never beat him in *StarCraft*, so hopefully we can spar in **Magic** and, afterwards, I can tell everyone how much awesomer I am. And, if I lose, I can just claim that I drew poorly. The important thing about my hypothetical fantasy is that I have excuses in advance.

**If you could play Magic Online against any villain AI from the movies, which would it be?**  
 I'm a nerd so I'm going to choose an AI from video games. I'd play against LINC from [*Beneath a Steel Sky*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beneath_a_Steel_Sky) . I really, really hated the character, so I'd like any opportunity to bash him down.
