Kaladesh has raised a multitude of questions, from which colors are the strongest in each Limited format, to the speed of games and matches, to, of course, the most important question of them all: if you lived on Kaladesh, what vehicle would you drive? We had pros and personalities from the floor of Grand Prix Atlanta put their formidable minds to these questions in today’s Quick Questions.
