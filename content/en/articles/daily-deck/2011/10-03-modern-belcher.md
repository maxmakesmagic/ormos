Modern is beginning to flex its muscle as a new format, and recently had a new set of bannings announced. While that will change many Modern decklists, that hasn't stopped players from testing the waters with new creations. Take, for example, this Goblin Charbelcher list used on **Magic Online** by 1nf0rm4luko. It features just 3 Mountains, but with a host of other mana sources it can cast its combo extremely quickly: Goblin Charbelcher, which is virtually guaranteed to be lethal with so few lands in the deck, or Grapeshot combined with Pyromancer's Swath. How will this deck change with [the bannings](/en/articles/feature/2011/09-20-september-20-2011-dci-banned-restricted-list-announcement) in effect?
