If you're just getting here, you should know that we've been showing off some of the *Zendikar* basic lands this week. We showed [Plains](https://web.archive.org/web/20090814000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/zendikar-plains-2009-08-10) on Monday, [Islands](https://web.archive.org/web/20090814000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/zendikar-islands-2009-08-11) on Tuesday, [Swamps](https://web.archive.org/web/20090814000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/zendikar-swamps-2009-08-12) on Wednesday, and [Mountains](https://web.archive.org/web/20090814000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/zendikar-mountains-2009-08-13) on Thursday. Today, of course, is Forests.

But it's *also* time for the Wallpaper of the Week! So be sure and scroll down past the forests to get to your **five *Zendikar* Basic Land wallpapers**.
