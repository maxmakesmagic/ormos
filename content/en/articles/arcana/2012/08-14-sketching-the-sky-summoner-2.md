Let's talk Talrand.

  

 ![Talrand, Sky Summoner](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Talrand,%20Sky%20Summoner&options=) 

  

Talrand, Sky Summoner is an important character, not to mention being a neat Legend. But forget about that for a minute, because today we're going to talk about the drakes behind him. The original sketch for Talrand naturally focused on the Sky Summoner himself:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fd/a0/fda0991b80780106b5e810898338a934308890e1414078eb34ad6ce4216a45ae.jpg) 

Talrand, Sky Summoner | Sketch by [Svetlin Velinov](https://web.archive.org/web/20120814000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22svetlin+velinov%22])

Neat, right? But in the final version, the drakes got more detailed and developed their distinctive coloration:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1f/6e/1f6ee879d7cc02cf37b829095abee8455a59923abbfb919b8402b9ea4383dac6.jpg) 

Talrand, Sky Summoner | Art by [Svetlin Velinov](https://web.archive.org/web/20120814000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22svetlin+velinov%22])

Now, let's go on a tour of ***Magic** 2013*. They're the same kind of drakes you get in Talrand's Invocation, unsurprisingly.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7a/76/7a76ea696c3a2ce34e7fba62bfcbb70c6d5a281df178f46ca4df014e493f0d75.jpg) 

Talrand's Invocation | Art by [Svetlin Velinov](https://web.archive.org/web/20120814000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22svetlin+velinov%22])

And you can also see very similar drakes on the ***Magic** 2013* Drake token card! Naturally, Talrand, Sky Summoner; Talrand's Invocation; and the Drake token were all illustrated by Svetlin Velinov.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/41/90/41904c400b4ce484da8d07816ec0003a2df78987e16f6ae7eadbce312a5e6a2e.jpg) 

 ***Magic** 2013* Drake Token | Art by [Svetlin Velinov](https://web.archive.org/web/20120814000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22svetlin+velinov%22])
