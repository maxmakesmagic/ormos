The second in our series of Wizards R&D members' gunslinging decks from this past weekend at the *Scars of Mirrodin* Prereleases nationwide comes from Mons Johnson. Says Mons, "The centerpiece of this deck is the powerful Grand Architect. The Grand Architect both increases the power of the beatdown as well as accelerating out such powerful artifact creatures as Molten-Tail Masticore or Wurmcoil Engine as early as turn three. And like the classic Fish decks, it can just win against any slower deck." Well put Mons!
