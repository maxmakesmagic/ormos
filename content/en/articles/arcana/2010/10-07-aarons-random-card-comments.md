Let's say you're Aaron Forsythe. Hi, Aaron! How's it going? You sure have a lot to do, what with being the Director of **Magic** R&D. But do you ever miss your days being Editor of **magicthegathering.com**? Sure, you get to write an article or two every year, but what if you want to talk about something that's not so high-pressure as [the state of **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20101007000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/feature/72) or [the new Core Set](https://web.archive.org/web/20101007000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/feature/97)?

Okay, stop pretending you're Aaron. Unless you actually are, in which case you already know what's about to happen. This is starting to resemble a hall of mirrors, so it's time to cut through the maze of illusions and impersonations: Aaron Forsythe is commenting on random cards in [Gatherer](https://web.archive.org/web/20101007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/)!

> 9/27/2010: [Planar Chaos](https://web.archive.org/web/20101007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Discussion.aspx?multiverseid=35088) (*Judgment*)  
>  9/28/2010: [Animate Artifact](https://web.archive.org/web/20101007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Discussion.aspx?multiverseid=1190) (*Revised Edition*)  
>  9/29/2010: [Merfolk of the Pearl Trident](https://web.archive.org/web/20101007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Discussion.aspx?multiverseid=2169) (*Fourth Edition*)  
>  9/30/2010: [Crazed Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20101007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Discussion.aspx?multiverseid=5555) (*Urza's Saga*)  
>  10/1/2010: [Kei Takahashi](https://web.archive.org/web/20101007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Discussion.aspx?multiverseid=1664) (*Legends*)  
>  10/4/2010: [Favorable Destiny](https://web.archive.org/web/20101007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Discussion.aspx?multiverseid=3491) (*Mirage*)  
>  10/6/2010: [Deity of Scars](https://web.archive.org/web/20101007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Discussion.aspx?multiverseid=153488) (*Eventide*)

Okay, now pretend you're *not* Aaron Forsythe. Do you wonder what the Director of **Magic** R&D thinks of random cards? Well, you could follow @[MTGAaron](http://www.twitter.com/mtgaaron) on Twitter, or even just the specific [#arccd](http://twitter.com/#search/%23arccd) hashtag. Or just follow [this handy link](https://web.archive.org/web/20101007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&user=+%5BAaron_Forsythe%5D), which collects all of them. Or there's a pretty good chance Aaron's just going to keep this page updated (the one you're on now. Remember, just because you've been pretending to be other people doesn't mean you've been reading a different page).
