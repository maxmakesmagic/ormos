How was your *New Phyrexia* Prerelease? We talked with our own R&D members about some of the decks they brought to spellsling with this past weekend, and here's what Mons Johnson used. Mons, who is famous as the namesake of Mons's Goblin Raiders and who has been helping to make **Magic** for many years, described his deck: "The key elements are a lot of support creatures for Myr Superion and a reasonable chain for Birthing Pod." You'll be able to play with the set [this weekend](/en/events/2008/release-facts/worldwide-launch-parties) when it goes on sale!
