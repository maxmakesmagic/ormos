The 2010 [State Championships](http://www.the2009s.com/), a tournament circuit organized by Glenn Godard of Sunmesa Events, was held this past weekend. It featured the new Standard format starring *Scars of Mirrodin*, and provided many players a first chance to try their new decks. In New York, David Russo's red deck managed to make the Top 8 using an innovative new card from the new set: Assault Strobe. Unlike other red mages, who also used Kargan Dragonlord, Kiln Fiend, and Plated Geopede, David designed his deck with the red sorcery in mind giving his high-powered threats a double punch!
