## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/cf/3f/cf3fd38c29ddd5ff81ffd81ba800c3cf40c0ce7c2b968ff476042c017fd0110c.png)*Fallen Empires* takes creature generation to a new level featuring Thrulls and Fungus alongside perennial favorites like Goblins, Elves, Merfolk, and Soldiers. The common cards in this set were each printed in three or four different versions, each with its own unique art.

**Set Name:** *Fallen Empires*  
**Number of Cards:** 102 (plus 85 art variants)

**Languages:** English

**Design and Development Team:** Skaff Elias, Jim Lin, Dave Pettey with design contributions from Chris Page
