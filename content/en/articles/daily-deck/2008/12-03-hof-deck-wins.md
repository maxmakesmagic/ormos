Mike Turian kicked the 2001 Pro Tour season off with a bang, winning the first event of the season alongside his Potato Nation teammates. He finished things off strongly too with a Top 8 at the World Championships, held that year in Toronto, Canada. His deck fit the famous Sligh curve, heavy on the low-casting cost end with far more one-drops than four-drops. Mike will return to the 2008 World Championships not to play, but to be inducted into the Pro Tour [Hall of Fame](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/tournaments-events-trading-card-game-magic-the-gathering-2). You can experience Mike's induction live from the 2008 World Championships in Memphis, or from the **magicthegathering.com** coverage with [the webcast of the Top 8](/en/events/2004/webcast/magic-the-gathering-webcast-schedule) set to kick off from Memphis at 9:45 a.m. ET on December 14th.
