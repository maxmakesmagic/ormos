*Scars of Mirrodin* Game Day has come and gone. Jos Morales took part in the festivities at Legends and Dragons in Guatemala. The end result was this Mono-Black Control deck, which uses hand disruption and creature removal to keep the opponent under wraps. From there, Jos uses powerful attackers like Abyssal Persecutor and Grave Titan to finish the hapless competition off!
