Not too long ago we announced that, starting with *Kaladesh*, [we would be introducing something called Planeswalker Decks](/en/articles/makingmagic/2016/05-16-ramp-of-approval). Planeswalker Decks will serve as ramps for new players to get into the game.

That means that *Eldritch Moon* will be your last opportunity to pick up Intro Packs with any release. If you enjoy Intro Packs, this will be your last chance to grab them, so let's take a look at the rares that will grace the cover of each Intro Pack.
