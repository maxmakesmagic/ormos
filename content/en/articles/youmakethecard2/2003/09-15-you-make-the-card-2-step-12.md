Our last vote really enforces the concept of "You Make The Card." I guarantee that our art team would have never commissioned this card with an "Earth" forest in the art, preferring instead to stick with the block theme of the metallic "Tangle"—but if the people want an "Earth" forest, that's what the people get.

Moving right along…
