This is Geralf's Mindcrusher, a blue rare from *Dark Ascension*.

  

 ![Geralf's Mindcrusher](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Geralf%27s%20Mindcrusher&options=) 

  

As you can see in the art, it's a large monster, which explains why it's a 5/5 Zombie Horror. But what about the mindcrushing? What does the art have that explains that? Well, what if you were to learn that Geralf made a creature that's covered with *dozens of tiny eyes*?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3c/e8/3ce8c69f887479e03cd678a6cf27b4c4436d585ad10a145440c99456b36028a2.jpg) 

Geralf's Mindcrusher | Art by [Steven Belledin](https://web.archive.org/web/20120214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22steven+belledin%22])

Ooh! Creepy! But not creepy *enough*. Let's get an even closer look at that head.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3f/2b/3f2bbda95ee1e9e29899c3ec3ca0f7aebd4b24d57b1e35d6c3073d3208d2382b.jpg) 

  

So many eyes! That's enough to crush even the strongest mind.
