Last year, one of the strongest decks involved [Cartel Aristocrat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cartel+Aristocrat) and sacrificing creatures, such as [Doomed Traveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20140508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doomed+Traveler), to gain value with [Blood Artist](https://web.archive.org/web/20140508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Artist). [Doomed Traveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20140508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doomed+Traveler) and [Blood Artist](https://web.archive.org/web/20140508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Artist) aren't Standard legal anymore, but there are still some quality creatures that require some sacrifices to be made.

[Cartel Aristocrat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cartel+Aristocrat) and [Varolz, the Scar-Striped](https://web.archive.org/web/20140508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Varolz%2C+the+Scar-Striped) are powerful cards if you have a stream of creatures available, while [Precinct Captain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Precinct+Captain) and [Xathrid Necromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xathrid+Necromancer) can provide those creatures. Mix in some powerful creatures such as [Obzedat, Ghost Council](https://web.archive.org/web/20140508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Obzedat%2C+Ghost+Council) and some efficient removal spells in [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20140508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay) and [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall) and you have a powerful, synergistic Standard deck.
