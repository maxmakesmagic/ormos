By far the breakout deck of the 2010 Extended season has been Dark Depths, centered around the eponymous card's interaction with Vampire Hexmage. The innovation of including the Thopter Foundry / Sword of the Meek combo has made it resilient against match-ups that were previously bad, and it has gone on to dominate Pro Tour Qualifiers. Its most recent success: winning Grand Prix–Houston in the hands of American Adam Yurchick. Read more about his win in the [official event coverage](/en/events/2010/gphou10/yurchick-first-at-last).
