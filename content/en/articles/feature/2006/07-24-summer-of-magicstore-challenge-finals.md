*Updated 2007-09-06*

### Store Challenge Final

### 

### Tournament Location

**Hilton Birmingham Metropole**  
National Exhibition Centre  
Birmingham  
B40 1PP  
[www.hilton.co.uk/birminghammet](http://www.hilton.co.uk/birminghammet)

### Tournament Schedule

**October 20, 2007**: Registration from 9:00 A.M – 10:00 A.M. Tournament begins at 10:45 A.M.  

### Entry Fee

FREE. Invitation only.

### Invitations

*   [Store Champions](https://web.archive.org/web/20060724000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/feature/summer-magicstore-challenge-champions-2006-10-02)
*   [Challenge Series Points](https://web.archive.org/web/20060724000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/feature/summer-magicstore-challenge-points-2006-10-02) (5+ points = invitation)

### Format

*   Swiss Rounds: Standard
*   Swiss Rounds: *Lorwyn* Booster Draft
*   Playoff: Standard
*   Decklists will be required.

### Tournament Length

*   Swiss rounds - minutes per round
*   Number of Swiss rounds based on attendance according to the following chart:

<table><tbody><tr><td><b>Attendance</b></td><td><b>Number of Swiss rounds</b></td></tr><tr><td>64 or less</td><td>6</td></tr><tr><td>65 - 128</td><td>7</td></tr><tr><td>129 - 256</td><td>8</td></tr><tr><td>257 or higher</td><td>9</td></tr></tbody></table>

### Notes

*   16K, REL 3
*   All Universal Tournament Rules and **Magic: the Gathering** Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document
*   Players must bring pen, paper, card sleeves and appropriate counters.

### Prizes

*   To be confirmed

### Additional Information

*   Please note that the invitation lists contains only those Store Champions and points awarded, from reported 8+ player tournaments only. If your name does not appear on the lists then please contact the tournament organiser to arrange for the tournament to be uploaded.
*   Players who have general questions about the **Magic: The Gathering** trading card game, Wizards of the Coast, Inc., the **Magic: The Gathering** Pro Tour, Arena-The League for **Magic: The Gathering**, DCI players' organization, Qualifier tournaments, the **Magic** World Championships, National Championships, State/Provincial Championships, or Regionals should call Wizards of the Coast at 08457 12 55 99, or send an email to us at wizards@hasbro.co.ukwizards@hasbro.co.uk
