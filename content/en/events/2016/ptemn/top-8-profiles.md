![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/ptemn/t8-blohon.jpg)

### Lukas Blohon

**Age**: 26

**Hometown**: Prague, Czech Republic

**Occupation**: *Magic* player

**Limited Record**: 5-1

**Constructed Record**: 7-2-1

**Previous Magic Accomplishments**: Pro Tour Top 8, Grand Prix Win

**Did you team up with anyone to practice for this Pro Tour? Who?**

Team Cabin Crew

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?**

Green-White Humans twice

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?**

White-Black Control with creatures. I thought it would be solid, but we misjudged the metagame and I don't have many good matchups.

**What breakout card from *Eldritch Moon* most impressed you this weekend?** 

[Emrakul, the Promised End](https://web.archive.org/web/20160806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Promised+End)

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?**

One step closer to the Hall of Fame

### Samuel Pardee

**Age**: 27

**Hometown**: Berkeley, CA

**Occupation**: *Magic* player

**Limited Record**: 6-0

**Constructed Record**: **6-2-2**

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 

2 Grand Prix wins, 9 Grand Prix Top 8s

**Did you team up with anyone to practice for this Pro Tour? Who?**

Team Face to Face Games! They are the reason the Pro Tour is fun for me.

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?**

Black-red in Draft 1, green-white in Draft 2. Black-red was my preference going into the event.

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?**

Black-Green [Grim Flayer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Flayer) Delirium. Lots of solid matchups and takes good advantage of a lot of the new cards.

**What breakout card from *Eldritch Moon* most impressed you this weekend?** 

[Liliana, the Last Hope](https://web.archive.org/web/20160806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana%2C+the+Last+Hope). She just offers so much card advantage as well as board control.

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?**

The fulfillment of a dream I've had since I found out there was a Pro Tour.

### Andrew Brown

**Age**: 24

**Hometown**: Los Angeles, CA

**Occupation**: Game Designer

**Limited Record**: 4-2

**Constructed Record**: 8-2

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 

Pro Tour *Oath of the Gatewatch*, Top 8

Grand Prix Denver Champion

**Did you team up with anyone to practice for this Pro Tour? Who?**

Team East-West Bowl!

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?**

Mono-Black, splashing Arlinn Cord

White-Black, love melding the Rats

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?**

Temurge; [Elder Deep-Fiend](https://web.archive.org/web/20160806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elder+Deep-Fiend) is a powerful *Magic* card

**What breakout card from *Eldritch Moon* most impressed you this weekend?** 

[Grapple with the Past](https://web.archive.org/web/20160806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grapple+with+the+Past). You get infinite Deep-Fiends, and [Sanctum of Ugin](https://web.archive.org/web/20160806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sanctum+of+Ugin) to [Time Walk](https://web.archive.org/web/20160806000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Time+Walk) your opponents ad nauseum.

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?**

That my love and dedication to the game will pay off.
