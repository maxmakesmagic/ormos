Ever wanted to just shuffle up the most powerful cards from all five colors? For *Worldwake* Game Day, Vilayphou Nicolas looks to have done just that! Vilayphou made it to the Top 8 at Trollune in Lyon, France with a deck featuring Maelstrom Archangel, Cruel Ultimatum, Nicol Bolas, and Obelisk of Alara amongst others. Vilayphou didn't have any trouble casting the spells thanks to a sturdy mana base and some support from Harrow! For more Standard decks, check out [all the *Worldwake* Game Day deck lists](/en/events/2010/worldwakegameday/worldwake-game-day).
