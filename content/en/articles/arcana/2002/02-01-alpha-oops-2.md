In **Magic** "shorthand," letters often refer to mana symbols. For example, if a person says "R," he often means ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif). Similarly, "XWB" really means ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/x.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/white.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/black.gif).

I guess someone forgot to tell that to the people that typeset *Alpha*. Look at the two cards below – [Phantasmal Forces](https://web.archive.org/web/20020201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phantasmal+Forces) and [Force of Nature](https://web.archive.org/web/20020201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+of+Nature). See anything weird in the text boxes?

I know [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20020201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest)s tap for ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/green.gif), but what taps for "G"? As with most errors in *Alpha*, these were fixed in *Beta*.

Oh, and artist Douglas Shuler's name is misspelled on the Force.
