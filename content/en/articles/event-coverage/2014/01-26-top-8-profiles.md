**Age:** 30  
**Hometown:** San Francisco, CA  
**Occupation:** Lawyer

 Mono-Black. Paul Rietzl told me to. (0 matches tested by me, several dozen by him.

**Day 1 Record:**  
 9-0

**Day 2 Record:**  
 4-1-1
