The *Alara Reborn* Borderposts are many things. They're multicolored cards, they're artifacts, they're manafixing, and so on. But have you taken a close look at them? Because it turns out their flavor is also central to the theme of the block.

Take [Fieldmist Borderpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20090511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fieldmist+Borderpost), which provides both white and blue mana. It stands literally on the border between Bant and Esper, as you can tell from the terrain on either side of the art:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fd/af/fdaf83f66bdc3cc5c3a5e2b56574129df9dbbfb7d9bb88c61100636a2fffbbc4.jpg)*[Fieldmist Borderpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20090511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fieldmist+Borderpost) art by Michael Bruinsma*  

The [Mistvein Borderport](https://web.archive.org/web/20090511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistvein+Borderport), then, stands between the lands of Esper and Grixis:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/cb/f9/cbf973a1caf753dd9e10081e790191d5b4e2ddeae71d50cdf1d0e7b237c6bfc3.jpg)*[Mistvein Borderpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20090511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistvein+Borderpost) art by Pete Venters*  

Next, we see the [Veinfire Borderpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20090511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Veinfire+Borderpost) between Grixis and Jund:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f2/1c/f21c36d449a38116e6d066707c8fdf4ff9c07192644761ac933839955a1c4784.jpg)*[Veinfire Borderpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20090511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Veinfire+Borderpost) art by Ralph Horsley*  

That takes us to the fourth borderpost, the [Firewild Borderpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20090511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firewild+Borderpost) between Jund and Naya:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/25/1f/251f2f698eb5012edec585d19cd20111338448a47bb245bba91f97067cb22532.jpg)*[Firewild Borderpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20090511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firewild+Borderpost) art by Jean-Sébastien Rossbach*  

Finally, we come full circle with the [Wildfield Borderpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20090511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wildfield+Borderpost) between Naya and Bant:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fc/da/fcda63ec792a82ff49c59d71eefc6cc78e26724c4a6033578608d7891cd0754b.jpg)*[Wildfield Borderpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20090511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wildfield+Borderpost) art by Zoltan Boros & Gabor Szikszai*
