In honor of Name Week on **magicthegathering.com**, today we look at playtest names. Most of the time, when an artist receives an art description for a card, only the playtest name of the card has been decided. Can you name these Kamigawa Block cards from their playtest names and sketches? *Click each picture for the answer.*
