Gen Con, one of the largest gaming conventions in the world, has concluded. But before doing so a number of **Magic** championship events played out. Each year the Vintage championships conclude there, and in 2010 it was Owen Turtenwald who emerged victorious. His deck was a 75-card mirror of that of his Finals opponent. That player? Hall of Famer Bob Maher! The match ended in spectacular fashion as Maher flipped up a lethal Force of Will to his Dark Confidant—a card *he* designed after winning the **Magic** Invitational and which bears his likeness!
