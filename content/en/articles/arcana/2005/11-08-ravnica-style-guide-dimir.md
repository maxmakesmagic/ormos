The Dimir are cunning, unseen manipulators, so the Dimir chapter of the *Ravnica* style guide is full of unpleasant spooks and the dreary places they haunt. Today we look at a few concept sketches from that very chapter, and some of the final *Ravnica* art that was inspired by it.

![Ravnica style guide: Dimir skeleton troops and Dimir House Guard](https://i.ormos.uk/e9/8d/e98da961b2ed369f60c2f013b9aa85d8ad9b21ebec48a88514a9a80a7cc6c8ef.jpg)  
*Ravnica style guide: Dimir skeleton troops and [Dimir House Guard](https://web.archive.org/web/20051108000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dimir+House+Guard)*

Combine Blue's mastery of metamagic and Black's ready access to undead troops, and you get the Dimir skeleton: a enlightened regenerator that aids its master find a more appropriate spell when necessary. Note how the cool hovering skull details suggests a more mystically empowered undead minion.

![Ravnica style guide: Thieving spirit sketch and Dimir Cutpurse](https://i.ormos.uk/51/9d/519d7b7dc79c5ce057a7b7321090b0096572628f456bd598f9458b90f97f6acb.jpg)  
*Ravnica style guide: Thieving spirit sketch and [Dimir Cutpurse](https://web.archive.org/web/20051108000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dimir+Cutpurse)*

The Dimir walk among us, unseen but detectable through what they manipulate and steal. It's tough to see in this screen-width ([click here for Kev Walker's full art](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/migration/DimirCutpurse.jpg)), but the [Dimir Cutpurse](https://web.archive.org/web/20051108000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dimir+Cutpurse) has its ghostly, rags-dressed arm right in an unsuspecting Ravnican's pocket. Creepy.

![Ravnica style guide: U/B guild warrior and zombie assassin; Dimir Infiltrator and Roofstalker Wight](https://i.ormos.uk/76/a9/76a9edacd1bfd6573ffe30ab1ebc98b54954827e080fbe21a78b0c84a1e110c3.jpg)  
*Ravnica style guide: U/B guild warrior and zombie assassin; [Dimir Infiltrator](https://web.archive.org/web/20051108000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dimir+Infiltrator) and [Roofstalker Wight](https://web.archive.org/web/20051108000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roofstalker+Wight)*

Exoskeletons. Ribcages. Assassin's blades made from sharpened bone. There's a lot of emphasis on exposed skeletal structure -- a dramatic twist on the secrecy of the guild. It suggests that, if you were ever to actually catch a [Glimpse the Unthinkable](https://web.archive.org/web/20051108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Glimpse%2Bthe%2BUnthinkable) of [Dimir Infiltrator](https://web.archive.org/web/20051108000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dimir+Infiltrator) or [Roofstalker Wight](https://web.archive.org/web/20051108000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roofstalker+Wight), you'd be out of your mind with horror. Right before you died.
