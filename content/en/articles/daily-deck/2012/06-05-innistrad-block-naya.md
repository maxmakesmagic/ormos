Pro Tour competitor Melissa DeTora, on [GatheringMagic.com](http://www.gatheringmagic.com/melissadetora-060112-standard-decks-of-tomorrowland-avr-block-metagame-breakdown), discusses decks from the *Innistrad* Block metagame as a possible starting point for this fall's Standard metagame. One of the decks she highlights is a Naya Midrange deck where she says the following:

> It's less powerful than Bant but way more consistent. It plays the best creatures of green, white and red and the most powerful removal spell, Bonfire of the Damned. It also has a lot of ways to obtain card advantage, thus making it one of the safest choices for a tournament.

Naya by Melissa DeTora
