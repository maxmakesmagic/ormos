Not everyone affected by the Reborning of Alara is unhappy about it. Take this woman:

 ![Vedalken Heretic](https://i.ormos.uk/d7/3b/d73b0482f9a50931e376c18f446726667241013936982e0881aa5019c1dab901.jpg) 

  

This Vedalken has thrown herself wholeheartedly into the world of Alara, instead of trying to keep everything Espery forever (as described in [yesterday's Savor the Flavor](/en/articles/savortheflavor/2009/04-29-choices-in-the-new-alara)). And she's found a snake to be her friend!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/30/dc/30dcb024862192043d865643ca19147f8ccf5f67b1155bc4becbfb49a7f8e283.jpg)
