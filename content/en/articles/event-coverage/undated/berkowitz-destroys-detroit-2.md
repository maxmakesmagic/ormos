## GRAND PRIX DETROIT 2005

![](https://i.ormos.uk/41/73/417306c4daa938131727615aab093770e0226621ca9fb827c0b2cd277b14d5ec.jpg)

Pro Tour Venice Top 8 finisher Jordan Berkowitz squared off with another Pro Tour Top 8 player in the finals of this Limited Grand Prix event. With a remarkable streak of strong money finishes at the last two years of Limited Pro Tours, Rich Hoaen was the favorite coming into this very experienced Top 8 field. Rich drafted a red-white deck and cruised past Sam Gomersall and Michael Krumb before falling to Jordan and his green-black, self-described "pile" in a grueling three-game match.

Jordan drubbed Osyp Lebedowicz in two fast games in the quarterfinals and proceeded to [Bile Urchin](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Urchin) Jeroen Remie out twice in the semis. Against Hoaen he split the first two before an exacting drawn-out rubber game. Rich was gaining massive amounts of life via [Genju of the Fields](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genju+of+the+Fields) and plinking away with [Frostwielder](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frostwielder) while Jordan attempted to reach critical mass. He built a Rube Goldbergian endgame that involved [Honden of Life's Web](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Honden+of+Life%27s+Web), [Long-Forgotten Gohei](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Long-Forgotten+Gohei), [Dance of Shadows](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dance+of+Shadows), [Devouring Greed](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devouring+Greed), and some very tricky math.

Jordan nearly skipped out on the main event to go see his beloved 76ers in playoff action against the Detroit Pistons in the NBA playoffs. Between the $2,400 cash prize and the thumping his Sixers took in Game 1, it seemed like he made the right decision.

## Sunday, April 24: 8:19 pm - Quarterfinals: Osyp Lebedowicz vs. Jordan Berkowitz

Osyp Lebedowicz was trying to reschedule his flight as the match got underway. He had his cell phone tucked between his shoulder and ear while he awkwardly pile-shuffled his cards. It was obvious that he was trapped in automated hell.

"Newark!"

"Today!"

"Newark!"

Finally he gave up and clicked the phone shut in frustration. "I need to reschedule and I got a robot."

Jordan was also dealing with phone issues as Jon Becker was calling him at the same time. Berkowitz offered a solution, "If you are going to miss your flight you should just concede to me."

Osyp laughed, "Let's just say I am not going to be happy either way."

The judge put out a handful of change and Osyp was able to get into his game, "You are never going to see that change again. I need lots of counters for this deck and when this is over I am going to get myself a nice cold soda."

The judge eyed him suspiciously and arranged the coins in a stack. Osyp shook his head. "Look how he puts those dimes on top. I'm going straight for the quarters."

### Game 1

Turn-two Tribe Elder for Osyp was parlayed into [Order of the Sacred Bell](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Order+of+the+Sacred+Bell) but Jordan was ready with [Rend Flesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rend+Flesh). Berkowitz untapped and played Sosuke. Osyp had nothing and the next turn yielded [Kami of the Hunt](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kami+of+the+Hunt) for Jordan. Osyp had [Cage of Hands](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cage+of+Hands) on the legend after it got its beat on for one turn.

Jordan eyed Osyp's board with suspicion and sniffed, "Green-white? You have to have a [Wrath](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wrath). Why else would you draft those colors?" [Burr Grafter](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burr+Grafter) powered up his Kami and he beat for three more. Osyp continued to play nothing. [Kami of the Waning Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kami+of+the+Waning+Moon) allowed Jordan to attack for five and he kept harping on the idea that Osyp might be sand-bagging him with a [Wrath](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wrath) effect.

Osyp was exasperated. "Yes, I am slow rolling a [Wrath](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wrath). You're an idiot." He laid his hand full of lands on the table and rolled his eyes.

The exchange between the two grew a little heated as there was some history there. "I'm not allowed to talk?" Jordan barked. "Coming from the king of never-shuts-up?"

### Game 2

Osyp looked through his sideboard and singled out [Harbinger of Spring](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Harbinger+of+Spring). "I got a sweet little piece of tech for you. I picked it up a couple of rounds ago from some kid I played. If it works I am going to buy him dinner." Osyp, who is notoriously…thrifty suddenly realized that he might be overheard and looked around the room. "That kid's not actually here is he?"

Jordan was still chaffing from the exchange a moment earlier and put the needle to Osyp, "You get so mad when you lose."

Osyp smiled, "I'm not mad."

"Every time I beat you, you call me some kind of names."

"I was nice to you when we played before."

"You beat me that round."

"Oh…besides I didn't call you any names."

![Devouring Greed](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Devouring+Greed)

"You just called me an idiot!"

"Oh…but I didn't mean it."

Berkowtiz led off Game 2 with [Bile Urchin](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Urchin) and neither player had a turn-two play. Osyp had a Decoy but Berkowitz used [Rend Flesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rend+Flesh) and beat for one with the Urchin. Osyp missed his fourth land drop and passed without a play. Jordan dropped [Blood Speaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Speaker). Osyp found his missing land and summoned a [Feral Deceiver](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Feral+Deceiver). He chose not to block which surprised Berkowitz, who was clearly offering a trade and Osyp went to 14. [Skullmane Baku](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullmane+Baku) came down after the attack.

Osyp cracked back with the Deceiver and played Order of Sacred Bell. There was still no blocking going on for Osyp's team and Jordan got in for three more. His team welcomed [Bloodthirsty Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodthirsty+Ogre). Osyp attacked with both his guys. Jordan considered a block and then decided to take seven. Osyp put down Forked Branch Garami. Jordan swung back in with everyone. The score was 10 to 11 in Osyp's favor. Osyp chose to block the Bloodspeaker and fell to 6. Jordan finished him off with [Devouring Greed](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devouring+Greed).

## Sunday, April 24: 8:40 pm - Richard Hoaen Top 8 Draft Report

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c3/d8/c3d8adc05dde64a991b362e9f55f5b9c044af7cca092942406942f49437b373d.jpg)*Richie Hoaen gets his draft on.*

Richard Hoaen is something of a phenomenon when it comes to Limited, with high finishes at all of the recent Limited Pro Tours. Even in a Top 8 draft as powerful as this one, he stands out as the best drafter at the table. His picks are listed below in bold, with other relevant cards from the pack also listed.

As the draft got under way, Rich found himself nestled in between Jeroen Remie to his left, and more importantly, Jordan Berkowitz to his right.

**[Eight-and-a-Half-Tails](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eight-and-a-Half-Tails)**, [Rend Spirit](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rend+Spirit), [Kami of the Hunt](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kami+of+the+Hunt), [Kodama's Might](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kodama%27s+Might), [Blessed Breath](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blessed+Breath), [Scuttling Death](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scuttling+Death), [Kami of Old Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20210925012353/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kami+of+Old+Stone)

## Sunday, April 24: 9:02 pm - Semifinals: Jeroen Remie vs. Jordan Berkowitz

Jordan asked after the quality of Jeroen's deck as they shuffled for their match. Jeroen shrugged, "My deck is alright. Not like your deck. I hear your deck is ridiculous."
