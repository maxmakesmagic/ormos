*The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth™* brings new cards and new treatments to collect in MTG Arena. Here's a rundown of what you'll find in the set when it launches on Tuesday, June 20!

## Full-art Map Basic Lands

If the old paths seemed too well trodden, the full-art map basic lands may leave you wondering what lay beyond their edges . . .

You'll be able to collect all ten versions of these cards by purchasing the full-art map lands bundle in the *MTG Arena* Store.

**Cost**

*   5,000 gems or 25,000 gold

## Borderless Legendary Lands

If you don't keep your feet, there is no knowing where you might be swept off to! You'll also be able to experience more full-art beauty of Middle-earth on *MTG Arena* with the borderless legendary lands showcase bundle.

![Mount Doom legendary land](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/84950_peph2phobrlr.png) ![The Shire legendary land](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/84952_peph2phobrlr.png) 

**Cost**

*   5,000 gems or 25,000 gold

## Showcase Ring Card Styles

The showcase Ring treatment highlights 30 legendary cards in epic moments of the story, and you'll be able to add them all to your collection!

![Frodo, Sauron's Bane with Ring showcase treatment](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/87613_febaspl0exop.png) ![Samwise the Stouthearted with Ring showcase treatment](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/87615_febaspl0exop.png) ![Gollum, Patient Plotter with Ring showcase treatment](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/87618_febaspl0exop.png) 

![Gandalf the Grey with Ring showcase treatment](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/87631_febaspl0exop.png) ![Tom Bombadil with Ring showcase treatment](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/87640_febaspl0exop.png) 

These showcase card styles will be available for purchase in the *MTG Arena* Store through a variety of bundles. Will you resist the temptation of the Ring and join the saviors of Middle-earth? Or will you succumb to the enemy and join the forces of darkness . . .

**Cost**

*   Varies by bundle (gems and gold)

## Nazgûl Showcase Card Styles

The Nine are abroad again, and they have taken the guise of riders in black . . .

![Nazgul on horseback with red glow emanating from the horse's eye and the Nazgul's cowl](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/84792_kuzusu6hisi0.png) ![Mounted Nazgul charging toward the left of the frame through a pass of stone, the horse and the Nazgul's eyes trailing magenta colored magical streams](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/87602_kuzusu6hisi0.png) ![A Nazgul with two red eyes within the shadows of its cowl on an armored horse charging toward the right frame](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/87603_kuzusu6hisi0.png) 

Is it not enough to know that they are servants of the enemy? In addition to the main set version available in packs, you'll be able to craft the eight alternate-art versions for your collection.

**Cost**

*   Alternate-art versions craftable with wildcards

## A Note on Borderless Scene Cards

Due to technical restrictions, *MTG Arena* will not support borderless scene cards at this time.

## Neither Stay nor Turn Back

*The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth* arrives June 20, 2023, on *MTG Arena*! Stay tuned for more information, because the journey has just begun!
