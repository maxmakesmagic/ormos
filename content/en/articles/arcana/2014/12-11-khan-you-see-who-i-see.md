So here's a bit of fun. Let's look at how many times (according to art descriptions) a khan appears in card art in *Khans of Tarkir*.

# Anafenza, the Foremost

The khan of Abzan is in exactly one card. Hers:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/cardart_anafenzatheforemost.jpg)

[Anafenza, the Foremost](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anafenza%2C+the+Foremost) | Art by [James Ryman](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5b%22James+Ryman%22%5d)

There are several art descriptions that refer *to* her, but none others that request her presence. Maybe she's camera shy.

# Narset, Enlightened Master

On the other hand, Narset appears in three cards. First, of course, is her own:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/cardart_narsetenlightenedmaster.jpg)

[Narset, Enlightened Master](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narset%2C+Enlightened+Master) | Art by [Magali Villeneuve](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5b%22Magali+Villeneuve%22%5d)

But Narset is also known for quietly contemplating the world around her…

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Quiet+Contemplation)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quiet+Contemplation)

…and for shattering the fists of Mardu warriors with her palm…that deflects…things.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Deflecting+Palm)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deflecting+Palm)

Ouch.

# Sidisi, Brood Tyrant

And just like Anafenza, Sidisi isn't one to photobomb. So she appears in her card and no others:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/cardart_sidisibroodtyrant.jpg)

[Sidisi, Brood Tyrant](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sidisi%2C+Brood+Tyrant) | Art by [Karl Kopinski](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5b%22Karl+Kopinski%22%5d)

…but…

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Jeering+Instigator)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeering+Instigator)

Her HAT appears in another card. Ha!

# Zurgo Helmsmasher

The Mardu khan appears on three cards. First, his own:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/cardart_zurgohelmsmasher_KTK.jpg)

[Zurgo Helmsmasher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zurgo+Helmsmasher) | Art by [Aleksi Briclot](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5b%22Aleksi+Briclot%22%5d)

He's known to utterly end opponents…

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Utter+End)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Utter+End)

…and to absolutely despise Sarkhan:

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Despise)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Despise)

# Surrak Dragonclaw

And finally, what of the Temur khan? Well, Surrak makes an appearance on two cards. First is his:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/cardart_surrakdragonclaw.jpg)

[Surrak Dragonclaw](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Surrak+Dragonclaw) | Art by [Jaime Jones](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5b%22Jaime+Jones%22%5d)

But that's NOT the card he's most famous for. Nay, indeed, Surrak features pretty prominently on what is perhaps the most famous and beloved piece of card art in *Khans of Tarkir*: Bear Punch.

Oop, I mean:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/cardart_savagepunch.jpg)

[Savage Punch](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Punch) | Art by [Wesley Burt](https://web.archive.org/web/20141211000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5b%22Wesley+Burt%22%5d)
