I sat down with some familiar faces to ask, "What's the most interesting play you've seen this weekend?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/MartellQQ.JPG)

 ***Tom Martell:** "I had [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) and two copies of [Daze](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daze) in my hand. My opponent was at three. I had exactly enough lands to cast [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise), but I was dead the next turn. I cast [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) and returned [Volcanic Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Volcanic+Island) to my hand to counter my own [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) with [Daze](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daze) unless I paid one. Then I returned another land to my hand and used my second copy of [Daze](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daze) to target the first. [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) resolved, I found [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt), and I was able to replay the [Volcanic Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Volcanic+Island) to kill my opponent."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/ChoQQ.JPG)

 ***Joshua Cho:** "I floated mana and sacrificed lands to cast [Fireblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fireblast). This left me with enough cards in graveyard and mana in pool to cast [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise), draw into another land and spell, and get my opponent for lethal damage with [Monastery Swiftspear](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Swiftspear)."* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/DukeQQ.JPG)

 ***Reid Duke:** "My opponent cast [Sudden Shock](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sudden+Shock) when I was at two. That was interesting."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/LaxQQ.JPG)

 ***Ari Lax:** "I cast [Chain Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chain+Lightning) so that my [Monastery Swiftspear](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Swiftspear) would be lethal. My opponent chained me back and used [Fireblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fireblast) before I got a combat step."* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/FloresQQ.JPG)

 ***Michael Flores:** "Tom Ross was playing against Dredge. His opponent had a stocked graveyard with a Zombie and [Ichorid](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichorid) ready to start attacking. Upon seeing the coast was clear with [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy), his opponent went all-in with a [Breakthrough](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Breakthrough) for zero. With his opponent's graveyard stocked, Ross cast Crop-Rotation at instant speed to find [Bojuka Bog](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bojuka+Bog)."*
