Welcome back to the Draft Viewer! How about something from last weekend's [World Championships](/en/events/2009/worlds09/all-roads-lead-to-victory-for-coimbra-china)? This is Table 1 of the second draft, featuring Bram Snepvangers, Shota Yasooka, Petr Brozek, Alexey Antonenko, Seraj Haroun, Marijn Lybaert, Tomoharu Saito, and Robert Jurkovic. Two of the eventual Top 8 would come out of this draft, which featured players who were all at either 8-1 or (in the case of Lybaert) 9-0. This is truly the big time when it comes to triple-*Zendikar* draft!

Complete coverage of the Worlds [is here](/en/events/2009/worlds09/all-roads-lead-to-victory-for-coimbra-china). And we've got detailed coverage of the [Round 10 Yasooka-Snepvangers](/en/events/2009/worlds09/worlds-2009-round-10-doing-the-math) match and the [Round 12 Lybaert-Snepvangers](/en/events/2009/worlds09/worlds-2009-round-12-between-a-maw-and-a-hallowed-place) match for your further edification. Enjoy!
