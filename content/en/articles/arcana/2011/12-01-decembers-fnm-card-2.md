It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6)! And since it's the first Friday in December, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards often feature original flavor text and updated wordings.
