Arjan van Leeuwen won Grand Prix Paris in 2008, at the time the largest **Magic** tournament ever with just under 2,000 competitors. In fact, whenever Paris, France has been the host of a professional **Magic** event, it has broken previous attendance records. If history is any indication, this weekend will be more of the same as the 2011 **Magic** Weekend comes to Paris featuring both a Grand Prix and a Pro Tour! Check out coverage [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20110418111736/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Events.aspx?).
