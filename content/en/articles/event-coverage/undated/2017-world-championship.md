## ARTICLES COVERAGE

Sunday Coverage

The curse of the Peach Garden Oath has been broken.

William "Huey" Jensen rose to the top of the 2017 *Magic: The Gathering* World Championship field and never surrendered his lead. While most of the 24 competitors traded blows, Jensen went on a tear, winning twelve matches in a row before finally taking his first loss near the end of Saturday. As Sunday's play began, Jensen had his friends and teammates—Reid Duke and Owen Turtenwald—along with his family from Boston cheering him on as he matched up against Singapore's Kelvin Chew in the semifinals, and then Spain's Javier Dominguez in the finals. 

After navigating some difficult games with Temur Energy against Dominguez's Ramunap Red deck, Jensen emerged triumphant in four games, breaking the curse that his close friends faced in the finals of previous World Championships. The Bostonian's performance earned him $100,000, the trophy, and the title of one of the game's all-time greats.

Jensen is joined by team Musashi—featuring Yuuya Watanabe, Shota Yasooka, Kentaro Yamamoto, Yuuki Ichikawa, Teruya Kakumae, and Ken Yukuhiro—as trophy winners this weekend. The Japanese all-star team took home trophies as winners of the inaugural Pro Tour Team Series Championship.

"I've been doing this for 20 years," Jensen said, when asked about what it means being the World Champion, and to do it in his hometown. "It's been a lot of time and a lot of hard work. I'm happy, and I'm proud, and I'm happy I could do it here."

It took a lot of hard work, and Jensen's time paid off. Congratulations once again to Jensen on his win and his title.
