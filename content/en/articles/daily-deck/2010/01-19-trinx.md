The Pro Tour Qualifier season for San Juan has kicked into high gear, but did you know you could play Qualifiers on **Magic Online**? It's true! This winning list came from just such a tournament. Sullivan managed to take the top slot with an aggro-control strategy that utilized Trinket Mage as a tutor. It also packed powerful counterspells, and won off the back of Tezzeret the Seeker or the Sword of the Meek / Thopter Foundry combo.
