*Updated July 18, 2007* 

Invitations to the 2007 Scholarship Series are awarded in the following manner:

*   The Top 2 finishers from each Scholarship Series Event.
*   The Top 25 DCI Constructed–ranked amateur players from North America ages eighteen and under (as of the date of the Scholarship Series Championship) who were previously uninvited. The rating date used is June 13, 2007
*   Players qualified from the Scholarship Series Open.

If you believe that your rating or finish at a Scholarship Series event is in error, please Visit [**https://webapp.wizards.com/login.aspx?target=personal**](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603230332/https://webapp.wizards.com/login.aspx?target=personal). After logging in, click the “Appeals” link and fill out the form.
