### Game 1

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8f/67/8f67231b5f2f004035b70ff31c29d5be85d34feaa34d0c33fdeada0bbdfea49f.jpg)*当然の黒：藤田憲一* 《アクアミーバ/[Aquamoeba](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aquamoeba)》《尊大なワーム/[Arrogant Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrogant+Wurm)》で速攻する中村に対して、唯一の《島/[Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island)》を《腐臭の地/[Rancid Earth](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rancid+Earth)》で破壊して足止めした後に《もぎとり/[Mutilate](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutilate)》でリセットをかける藤田。

その後は《野生の雑種犬/[Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel)》を《無垢の血/[Innocent Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Innocent+Blood)》で除去されたところで中村の手が止まってしまい、燃料補給手段である《占骨術/[Skeletal Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skeletal+Scrying)》を持つ藤田は《魔性の教示者/[Diabolic Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Diabolic+Tutor)》で《ミラーリ/[Mirari](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirari)》をセットして将来のアドバンテージを確保する。

マッドネスできずに《尊大なワーム/[Arrogant Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrogant+Wurm)》が登場したところで、X = 3 の《占骨術/[Skeletal Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skeletal+Scrying)》を《ミラーリ/[Mirari](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirari)》によってコピーして除去を探すも見つからず、残り 3 まで追い詰められるがさすがに次のドローは《チェイナーの布告/[Chainer's Edict](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chainer%27s+Edict)》。

![Krosan Reclamation](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Krosan+Reclamation)

その後は《汚れた契約/[Tainted Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tainted+Pact)》《魔性の教示者/[Diabolic Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Diabolic+Tutor)》（いずれも《ミラーリ/[Mirari](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirari)》によってコピー）によって《チェイナーの布告/[Chainer's Edict](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chainer%27s+Edict)》《精神ヘドロ/[Mind Sludge](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind+Sludge)》《もぎとり/[Mutilate](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutilate)》とつないで完全に藤田の場が出来上がる。

引いてきた除去を片っ端から乱打して出てきたクリーチャーを処理していくのだが、肝心の《ナントゥーコの影/[Nantuko Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Shade)》、もしくはそれを導く《魔性の教示者/[Diabolic Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Diabolic+Tutor)》を藤田は引くことができない。

出すたびにクリーチャーが除去され続けて暇を持て余した中村が、せめてもの抵抗と《クローサ流再利用/[Krosan Reclamation](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Krosan+Reclamation)》で《チェイナーの布告/[Chainer's Edict](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chainer%27s+Edict)》を戻し、まあついでにとライフが 1 の藤田に対して《占骨術/[Skeletal Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skeletal+Scrying)》をライブラリーに戻す。

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpsap02/a956.jpg)*お約束の青緑で参戦している中村聡* やっとの事で《汚れた契約/[Tainted Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tainted+Pact)》を引き当てた藤田（もちろん《ミラーリ/[Mirari](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirari)》でコピー）、しかし《腐臭の地/[Rancid Earth](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rancid+Earth)》《占骨術/[Skeletal Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skeletal+Scrying)》と 2 回とも失敗に終わってしまう・・・・

マジックは実に不思議だ。  
 《ミラーリ/[Mirari](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirari)》によって完全に場を掌握したはずの藤田が《物静かな思索/[Quiet Speculation](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quiet+Speculation)》からのワームトークンに屈することになるとは誰が予想しただろうか？

**藤田 0-1 中村**

### Game 2

藤田は完全に運気を失ってしまったのだろうか。

6 枚の《沼/[Swamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swamp)》《魔性の教示者/[Diabolic Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Diabolic+Tutor)》という初手をマリガン、続いての手札は《陰謀団の貴重品室/[Cabal Coffers](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Coffers)》のみ・・・・

こうなってはアドバンテージの泉である《ミラーリ/[Mirari](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirari)》《陰謀団の貴重品室/[Cabal Coffers](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Coffers)》も全く意味をなさない。

![Mirari](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Mirari)

中村が《入念な研究/[Careful Study](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Careful+Study)》で《日を浴びるルートワラ/[Basking Rootwalla](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basking+Rootwalla)》を出した返しでの《催眠の悪鬼/[Mesmeric Fiend](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mesmeric+Fiend)》で手札を見ると、

《尊大なワーム/[Arrogant Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrogant+Wurm)》と他土地。

ちょっとは期待してみたものの、《催眠の悪鬼/[Mesmeric Fiend](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mesmeric+Fiend)》でチャンプして《占骨術/[Skeletal Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20020824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skeletal+Scrying)》でドローを進めているような展開では勝てるわけも無く。

**藤田 0-2 中村**

**Final Result:藤田 0-2 中村**
