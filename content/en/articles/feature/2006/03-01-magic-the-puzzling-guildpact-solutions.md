Here are the solutions to [Monday's Magic: The Puzzling puzzles](/en/articles/feature/2006/02-27-magic-the-puzzlingguildpact).

  

Part I: Replication Aberration 

### Puzzle 1a:

[![Diagram 1a](https://i.ormos.uk/02/b4/02b49f1391ccb02e9b0e46e96ce9da259734e4e19687e9d33d7bd59e442fe519.jpg)](https://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/features/314_diagram1abig.jpg)

1.  Play the Island from your hand.
2.  Tap enough of your lands to add seven Blue and two Red mana to your mana pool. Leave Steam Vents untapped.
3.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) to animate Genju of the Spires.
4.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif) to cast Rain of Embers, killing your Mountain and returning Genju of the Spires to your hand.
5.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) to animate Genju of the Falls.
6.  Use ![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif) to cast Genju of the Spires on Steam Vents.
7.  Attack with your Genju.
8.  After no blockers are declared, use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) to animate your Steam Vents via Genju of the Spires. This makes it a 6/1 that will attack for lethal damage.

  

### Puzzle 1b:

[![Diagram 1b](https://i.ormos.uk/aa/47/aa472e47bce88b8a412aabb2db91b4410f9a19e7c3a33bb6c9249274b1d9cd74.jpg)](https://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/features/314_diagram1bbig.jpg)

1.  Play the Island from your hand.
2.  Tap all your lands for nine Blue and two Red mana.
3.  Cast Niv-Mizzet and Drowned Rusalka.
4.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) to animate Genju of the Spires.
5.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) to sacrifice your animated land via your Rusalka. Discard any card and draw a card. Target Gelectrode with Niv-Mizzet's trigger.
6.  With Gelectrode out of the way, sacrifice Wild Cantor for ![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif) and cast Surge of Zeal targeting Niv-Mizzet.
7.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) to sacrifice your Rusalka. Discard any card and draw a card. Target your opponent with Niv-Mizzet's trigger.
8.  Attack with Niv-Mizzet for the other four damage.

  

### Puzzle 1c:

[![Diagram 1c](https://i.ormos.uk/1b/8a/1b8ac80456e5c742dcba2a840309d368b4432d1a7ad93458ba69971836622edc.jpg)](https://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/features/314_diagram1cbig.jpg)

1.  Play an Island.
2.  Tap all of your lands except for one Island to add ten Blue and two Red mana to your mana pool. Make sure that the untapped Island is not the one you just played.
3.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) to animate each of your Genjus.
4.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif) to cast Rain of Embers, killing your Mountain and returning Genju of the Spires to your hand.
5.  Use ![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif) to cast Genju of the Spires on Steam Vents.
6.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) to animate your Steam Vents via Genju of the Spires. It still has one damage on it, and will go to your graveyard. Both Genjus will return to your hand.
7.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) to cast Genju of the Falls on an untapped Island.
8.  Use your remaining ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) to animate your Genju, and attack for the final three damage.

  

### Puzzle 1d:

[![Diagram 1d](https://i.ormos.uk/75/a1/75a16b1571c5becd6c8a9d7ed41e5f84a8aa7db77064b433f6d822d2a2d3b6be.jpg)](https://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/features/314_diagram1dbig.jpg)

1.  Play a Mountain.
2.  Tap enough of your lands to add five Blue and three Red mana to your mana pool. Leave your Genju of the Spires-enchanted land untapped.
3.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif) to cast Gelectrode.
4.  Use ![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif) to cast Surge of Zeal targeting Gelectrode.
5.  Tap Gelectrode targeting Dripping-Tongue Zubera.
6.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif) to cast Rain of Embers. With that on the stack, Gelectrode will untap. Tap it to destroy the Zubera and put the 1/1 token into play.
7.  Rain of Embers will resolve, kill all of your opponent's potential blockers.
8.  Use ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) to animate Genju of the Spires.
9.  Attack for the remaining six damage.

  

Part II: Ideas Unbound 

### Puzzle 2:

[![Diagram 2](https://i.ormos.uk/4f/5e/4f5eae59ec47cb3dfd3d94eca58cb2b7c5498af14f0a3ed9e78041f04c24721c.jpg)](https://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/features/314_diagram2big.jpg)

1.   End your turn.
2.   The rest of the solution occurs as a stack of instant effects during your opponent's upkeep.
3.   Tap all lands for 27 mana.
4.   Tap Niv-Mizzet to draw a card. The Underworld Dreams' trigger is stacked, then Niv-Mizzet's, targeting Mandark. Let the Mizzet trigger resolve dealing one damage to Mandark.
5.   Cast Gigadrowse, replicate five times, all targeting your Guildmage. (21 mana, six Gigadrowse on stack)
6.   Cast Rewind, replicate four times, each other targeting a unique Gigadrowse copy (one being the original).
7.   Upon each resolution of Rewind, untap three Izzet Boilerworks and an Island, tapping them for eleven mana.
8.   This puts Rewind and Gigadrowse into your graveyard and leaves you with 56 mana and a copy of Gigadrowse on the stack.
