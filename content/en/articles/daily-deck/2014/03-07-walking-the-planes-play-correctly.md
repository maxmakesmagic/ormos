Before you make a play, how much do you think about it? How do you know it's the right move? **Magic** is a deeply strategic game, and playing correctly is harder than you think. It might even be impossible. How much does playing correctly matter to you? Join Walking the Planes at [Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*](https://web.archive.org/web/20140307000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/ptbng14/welcome).
