Astral Slide is back! Zac Ricketts took this list to a free trip to Hawaii at his PTQ in Lincoln, Nebraska. Abusing the name-sake enchantment, Zac uses his cycling cards to gain advantage by flipping Cloudthreshers in and out of play (or Kitchen Finks, or Loxodon Hierarch...). When he gets tired of that, he can always turn the Astral Slide activations on attacking creatures to keep them from connecting!
