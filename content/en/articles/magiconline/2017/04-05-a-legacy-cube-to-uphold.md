*Legacy Cube* will return on April 12, but this update entails a bit of a shake-up. Back in September, we made a big change to the cube: cutting iconic broken cards like [Show and Tell](https://web.archive.org/web/20170405000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Show+and+Tell), [Sneak Attack](https://web.archive.org/web/20170405000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sneak+Attack), and [Natural Order](https://web.archive.org/web/20170405000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Natural+Order) in favor of cards that push toward playing "regular *Magic*." Many players prefer that baseline gameplay—that's why it's our baseline. With the *Vintage Cube* pushing so hard toward doing broken things, we wanted to ensure those players had a cube tailored to them. But with the addition of the *Modern Cube* Natalie Weizenbaum created, the *Legacy Cube* no longer has to pick up that slack. We also want to make things for those of you who prefer playing with the powerful and iconic cards of *Magic*'s history, and much like Legacy itself, the *Legacy Cube* is a prime spot to let you do just that.

The cube says Legacy right in the name, after all, so as of Wednesday you can [Sneak Attack](https://web.archive.org/web/20170405000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sneak+Attack) in [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20170405000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Aeons+Torn); search up [Batterskull](https://web.archive.org/web/20170405000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Batterskull) with [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20170405000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic); and [Natural Order](https://web.archive.org/web/20170405000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Natural+Order) out a [Craterhoof Behemoth](https://web.archive.org/web/20170405000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Craterhoof+Behemoth). And those aren't the only changes! The cube now features the Mirran Swords, the full cycle of fast lands, and a few newcomers from *Aether Revolt*.

Whether you're a longtime *Legacy Cube* veteran who's been itching to cast these powerful spells again or this is your first time playing [Show and Tell](https://web.archive.org/web/20170405000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Show+and+Tell), there's plenty of excitement to be had. And if you'd rather do battle with boards full of creatures and planeswalkers, we've got a few new toys for you as well. Check out the [complete card list](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/04-05-legacy-cube-cardlist), and get ready for some cubing!
