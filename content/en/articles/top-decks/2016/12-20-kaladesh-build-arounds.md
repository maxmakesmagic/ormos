*Kaladesh* is one of the most exciting sets in recent memory. There are countless powerful cards that promise to shake up Standard and Modern. Some cards may not seem particularly powerful in a vacuum, but have a tremendous amount of potential that can be unlocked if we're willing to put in the work. Today, we'll be looking at the cards that encourage us to build decks around them.

# Electrostatic Pummeler!

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Electrostatic+Pummeler)](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrostatic+Pummeler)

The first card I'd like to talk about is [Electrostatic Pummeler](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrostatic+Pummeler). It's super fragile, but the effect is tremendously strong if we use it alongside pump spells. There's a lot of good threats that use energy and it's not difficult to kill an opponent as early as the fourth turn by attacking with a 20/20 creature. Let's build an aggressive Standard deck that uses [Electrostatic Pummeler](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrostatic+Pummeler) as a potent finisher à la [Temur Battle Rage](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temur+Battle+Rage)/[Become Immense](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Become+Immense).

This deck is really good at making a lot of energy. The deck can use [Blossoming Defense](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blossoming+Defense) to protect [Electrostatic Pummeler](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrostatic+Pummeler) while also inching it closer to lethal proportions. [Voltaic Brawler](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voltaic+Brawler) is one of the best aggressive creatures I've ever seen and we'll likely be seeing it in a lot of different decks; here, it's even better than it might be elsewhere because the leftover energy can be put to incredible use. If you like attacking and you're looking to kill people out of nowhere with one of the more overlooked cards from Kaladesh then this is definitely the deck for you!

# Metalwork Colossus!

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Metalwork+Colossus)](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metalwork+Colossus)

There are a lot of ways in which we can find [Metalwork Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metalwork+Colossus) in the current Standard and the card does a very good job of closing games quickly if we're willing to push it to its limits. [Hedron Archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hedron+Archive) does a particularly good job of getting the Colossus into play and [Engulf the Shore](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engulf+the+Shore) is a great way to clear the board while leaving us with a massive attacker. Let's put together a deck that pushes [Metalwork Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metalwork+Colossus) to its limits.

# Aetherflux Reservoir!

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Aetherflux+Reservoir)](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherflux+Reservoir)

[Aetherflux Reservoir](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherflux+Reservoir) is a card with a lot of exciting applications. In Standard, we can probably use cards like [Paradoxical Outcome](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Paradoxical+Outcome) to cast a ton of inexpensive spells in a single turn to gain enough life to kill opponents out of nowhere. In a format like Modern, a card like [Aetherflux Reservoir](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherflux+Reservoir) has the ability to help us survive before being used at a late game win condition. Let's put together a Modern deck that uses [Aetherflux Reservoir](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherflux+Reservoir) to its fullest potential!

It's a lot easier to get to get a high enough storm count to kill someone with [Aetherflux Reservoir](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherflux+Reservoir) than it is with [Grapeshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grapeshot). [Aetherflux Reservoir](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherflux+Reservoir) also lets us play a pair of [Noxious Revival](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noxious+Revival)s in our sideboard to have the ability to gain infinite life every turn forever if the opponent has somehow prevented us from killing them. Eventually, the opponent will deck themselves in this scenario. We can also build [Aetherflux Reservoir](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherflux+Reservoir) decks using [Cloudstone Curio](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudstone+Curio) with artifacts that cost zero mana; this could be a nice angle to take with the strategy and may actually be the final form of the deck.

This deck can use [Cloudstone Curio](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudstone+Curio) to create a lot of infinite loops. For example, [Mox Opal](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Opal) along with any other "free" artifact creates infinite mana, which can then be used to loop prophetic prism as much as we want, which finds us our [Aetherflux Reservoir](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherflux+Reservoir). If we already have [Aetherflux Reservoir](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherflux+Reservoir), then the [Cloudstone Curio](https://web.archive.org/web/20161220000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudstone+Curio) has the ability to just win the game immediately by gaining us infinite life and blasting our opponent for some multiple of 50 damage until they're dead.
