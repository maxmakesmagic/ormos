Listed below are the votes cast by members of the 2005 **Magic** Pro Tour Hall of Fame Players Committee. Voters were asked to vote for a single candidate. All players with 100 or more lifetime Pro Points as of August 1, 2005, were eligible to vote.

As the top vote-getter, Olle Råde becomes the fifth member of the 2005 Hall of Fame class.
