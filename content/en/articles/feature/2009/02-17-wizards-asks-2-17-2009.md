From the R&D whiteboard: *What's the hungriest creature in **Magic**?*

Today we're trying something a little different. The above question first appeared on the infamous R&D whiteboard a few weeks ago. The resulting "discussion" looked like this:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b1/1d/b11dee58c50d3df56bb8fcc5c5ff3e72ed391e4646fd8c67a6b836db9e17a925.jpg)

The bottom of the continuum on the left, by the way, reads "do not feed another wafer-thin mint."

And no, we don't know what a "Goyfatog" is, either.

Below, a few R&D members argue in favor of their chosen creature:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fa/fe/fafe439c8cd14e179675a1e7a1b7baa0a63f2c6f3dc2c816785a52fff5e4d843.jpg)

**Monty Ashley:** After careful consideration, I believe that things that are [Ravenous Rats](https://web.archive.org/web/20090217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Ravenous%2BRats) are hungrier than things that are merely [Voracious Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20090217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Voracious%2BDragon) or even [Hungry Hungry Heifer](https://web.archive.org/web/20090217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Hungry%2BHungry%2BHeifer). And of the "ravenous" cards, only one is a creature that's also defined by its hunger. I give you [Ravenous Vampire](https://web.archive.org/web/20090217000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ravenous+Vampire), the hungriest creature in **Magic**.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b4/9a/b49a97d847733a73985c5d85979f9c0df999dfc0380369be66282a7035d6c565.jpg)

**Mons Johnson:** [Chronatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20090217000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chronatog). It eats time!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e0/0c/e00c2900e4532fed44efdbc0223f486ee1518c0cf594bb289867319eeda0f043.jpg)

**Kelly Digges:** [Worldgorger Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20090217000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Worldgorger+Dragon) almost got my vote, but it was pointed out that one can gorge without being hungry. In fact, once [Worldgorger Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20090217000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Worldgorger+Dragon) eats everything in sight, it's so stuffed it can't even keep it all down. So [Doomgape](https://web.archive.org/web/20090217000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doomgape) is my answer, for the reasons listed on the whiteboard.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/36/f7/36f721b7d86cd6baa42e539d43b8e75e0b0213a5a1b8570f547b0e9cfc889867.jpg)

**Mark Rosewater:** [Fat Ass](https://web.archive.org/web/20090217000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fat+Ass): Its hunger is so great it makes any mage summoning it eat.

What's the hungriest creature in **Magic**? Click the Discuss link below to make your case and then see what everybody else has to say!
