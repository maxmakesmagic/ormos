Congratulations to [Alexander Hayne, the 2011-2012 Rookie of the Year](/en/events/2012/ptavr12/feature-rookie-of-the-year-alexander-hayne)!

The player with the most Pro Points at the end of a tournament season who has not participated in a Pro Tour or World Championship tournament in any previous season is identified as the Rookie of the Year. 

This page shows all rookie players who have received at least 10 Pro Points in the 2011-2012 season (from June 13, 2011 to May 13, 2012).
