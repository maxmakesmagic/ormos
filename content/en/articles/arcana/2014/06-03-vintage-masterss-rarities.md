We're all excited about the opportunity to play with the [Power Nine](/en/articles/arcana/2003/10-15-the-power-nine-2) in **Magic Online**'s upcoming expansion *Vintage Masters*, but there are a ton of players eyeing rarities in the set well below the [special rarity](https://web.archive.org/web/20140603000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/vintage-masterss-special-rarity-2014-05-12-0) that will hold **Magic**'s most precious gems. 

The [Pauper](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218012756/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/resources.aspx?x=magic/rules/pauper) community, for example, is eagerly awaiting getting its hands on a number of commons that, prior to *Vintage Masters*, have never been available in the format before. Additionally, plenty of players are looking to get their hands on a number of other cards that will be more plentiful in *Vintage Masters* than at their previous rarities. 

So to get you started, here is a complete list of cards for *Vintage Masters*, along with the rarity you'll find them printed at when you start cracking packs on Friday, June 13!
