Have you wondered what the deal is with [Moon Heron](https://web.archive.org/web/20111219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moon+Heron)?

![Moon Heron](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Moon+Heron)

  

Well, the fact is, this is a ghostly heron that's associated with the legendary "Heron in the Moon" that some people say they can see on the moon of *Innistrad*. Have you ever noticed it? Let's look at one of the moon-heavy cards:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e0/2e/e02e60d11a0a5faed00557af700505299ede7de660d035e6bdeb406ee4fa5f95.jpg)

[Paraselene](https://web.archive.org/web/20111219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Paraselene) | Art by [Ryan Yee](https://web.archive.org/web/20111219000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Ryan+Yee%22%5D)

Still having trouble seeing it? Well, let's pull in an image from the *Innistrad* Style Guide!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/18/f8/18f8586d796c89c8adf15fbb8f228dd80f4c7a819deacdecc6c7c71ddd20fd15.jpg)
