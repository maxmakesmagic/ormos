###  Mandatory Registration

Player registration will occur at the tournament site **4pm-8pm on Thursday, January 27th**.

Players must have a signed Consent and Release form to compete at Pro Tour Nagoya. Forms will be available onsite or can be downloaded [here](http://www.wizards.com/dci/downloads/consent_form_nagoya_2005.pdf). Minors must have a parent or guardian’s signature on these forms.

Bring these forms with you to registration. 

**At registration, all participants must present one of the following forms of identification upon request:** 

*   a driver’s license, or
*   a passport, or
*   a copy of their birth certificates and photo IDs (school IDs are acceptable).

If you anticipate any difficulty registering during the official registration hours, please contact Andy Heckt at (425) 254-2941 or andy.heckt@wizards.com.

###  Format and Schedule

**Format** 

*   Rochester Draft
*   3 *Champions of Kamigawa*TM booster packs will be used for drafting.
*   Competition will consist of 5 Rochester Drafts, each followed by 3 rounds of competition.
*   Check [www.thedci.com](http://www.thedci.com/) for Rochester Draft rules.

**Round Length** 

*   15 Swiss Rounds total, 55 minutes per round
*   Top 8 single-elimination finals have no time limit
*   When time is called, the active player will finish the turn and five additional turns will be taken
*   Stalling and slow play rules will be strictly enforced during the tournament.

**Advancement** 

*   All players may participate in the first 2 drafts and Swiss rounds of Friday’s competition.
*   Players with 12 or more match points after round 6 will advance to draft 3. Draft 3 will take place on Friday immediately following round 6 (See schedule below).
*   The top 8 players after round 15 will advance to Sunday’s top 8 single-elimination finals.

**Top 8** 

*   Top 8 matches will be best 3 of 5
*   The Top 8 players will be seated randomly at the draft table immediately prior to drafting
*   The Top 8 bracket will be determined using this random seating.
*   Where necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will still be used to determine final order in the standings.

**Notes** 

*   48K, REL 5
*   Players are responsible for knowing all DCI rules and [high-level tournament rules](http://www.wizards.com/protour/images/highlevelrules.pdf).
*   Players must bring pen, paper, card sleeves and appropriate counters.

*   Prizes for each event will be determined by the number of participants, and will take the form of Champions of Kamigawa booster packs, with exceptions as noted below.

    *   The winner of the IT Media PSP Tournament will receive as an extra prize a Playstation Portable (Japanese OS model), courtesy of IT Media.
    *   The winner of the IT Media Nintendo DS Tournament will receive as an extra prize a Nintendo DS (Japanese OS model), courtesy of IT Media.
    *   The winner of the IT Media iPod Tournament will receive as an extra prize an Apple iPod, courtesy of IT Media.
*   Participation in Saturday's Amateur Tournament is limited to players who do not have a lifetime Pro Point.
*   Participation in Saturday's Japan Junior Tournament is limited to junior high school students and younger. Each participant will receive a special Japan Junior Tournament Slith Firewalker premium card, and the winner will receive a special Japan Junior Tournament Royal Assassin premium card. Furthermore, 12 booster packs will be awarded to the winner, 8 booster packs to 2nd place, 4 booster packs each to 3-4th place, and 2 booster packs each to 5-8th place.
*   Participation in Sunday's Femvitational Tournament is limited to female players.
*   Participation in Sunday's His-Her Sealed Tournament is limited to 2-person teams made up of one male and one female player. A special prize for the winning team will be announced on-site.
