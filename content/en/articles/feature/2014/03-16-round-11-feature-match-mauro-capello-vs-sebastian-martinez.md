With two losses, Mauro Capello and Sebastian Martinez found themselves in a tight spot heading into this Round 11 Feature Match. While a third loss is not a guarantee that they will fall out of Top 8 contention, it is likely that only one or two players at 12-3 will sneak in. In addition, a Top 64 performance will likely cement Sebastian Martinez's spot as the top Uruguayan player, something he will be able to defend at Pro Tour Journey to Nyx in a couple of months.

**The Decks** 

Capello's deck is one of the most common sights around the room and easily the most-played deck in the tournament: Esper Control. Still, there are a few interesting wrinkles in his build that provide him a bit of an advantage over the other Esper decks in the field. First, he has access to [Ashiok, Nightmare Weaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashiok%2C+Nightmare+Weaver), which provides a great threat against the mass of [Hallowed Fountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallowed+Fountain) decks in the field. Second, he has taken a cue from the WU Control decks that have been popular as of late, adding an [Elixir of Immortality](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elixir+of+Immortality) to his deck, giving his spells a little more life. He also has access to [Fated Retribution](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fated+Retribution), perfect for destroying opposing Planeswalkers. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpba14/r11_capello.jpg)

*Mauro Capello* 

  

As for Martinez, he is playing one of the more unique decks in the room: BR Control. Between [Dreadbore](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dreadbore), [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), [Rakdos's Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos%27s+Return), and [Chandra, Pyromaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Pyromaster), Martinez's deck seems like a nightmare for all of the [Hallowed Fountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallowed+Fountain) decks in the field, Capello's included. Unlike the Br Devotion decks, this deck opts out of playing the [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel)s and [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) of the Black Devotion decks, opting for a stronger red presence with [Anger of the Gods](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anger+of+the+Gods), [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon), and [Chandra, Pyromaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Pyromaster). Martinez found this deck while surfing Twitch, watching it crush a US store event. He then used it to demolish a PTQ and knew that it was going to be perfect for this weekend. 

**The Games** 

Martinez appeared to be in control from the outset of the first game, stripping a [Jace, Architect of Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Architect+of+Thought), from Capello's hand and keeping Ashiok at bay with [Dreadbore](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dreadbore). The first real haymaker came when he found a window to resolve a [Chandra, Pyromaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Pyromaster). Thanks to Chandra's +0 effect, Martinez was able to build up a fairly large advantage, hitting cards like [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie) and [Rakdos Keyrune](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos+Keyrune). Capello tried to equalize things with [Jace, Architect of Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Architect+of+Thought), but a massive [Rakdos's Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos%27s+Return) killed Jace and left Capello without a hand. It is rare to see Esper Control behind on cards, but Martinez's card advantage simply manhandled Capello. Eventually, [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon) finished off the defenseless Capello. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpba14/r11_martinez.jpg)

*Sebastian Martinez* 

  

Martinez set the tone once again for the second game, but this time it was Capello who wrested control. [Syncopate](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syncopate) and [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) took care of Martinez's early [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon)s, keeping the board clear for [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion). Capello had a [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) to once again clear out Martinez's attempts to mount an offense, keeping Elspeth nice and safe. Capello had the clear board advantage, but took complete control of the game when h cast [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) for five with an empty hand. At that point, Martinez was gassed, allowing Capello's Soldier tokens and [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) to easily attack for the win. 

The final game opened with a flurry of disruption from Martinez, setting the tone early. He tipped his plan by taking [Syncopate](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syncopate) and [Blood Baron of Vizkopa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Baron+of+Vizkopa), leaving a pair of Jaces in Capella's hand. Right after the first one came down, Martinez locked them up with a [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle). Still, Capella looked to be in good position with a pair of [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) in his hand. At least until Martinez dropped a sideboarded [Sire of Insanity](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sire+of+Insanity) onto the table. Capello had nothing to stop it, watching as his pair of [Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140316000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Revelation)s hit the graveyard. It didn't take much more than that for Martinez to attack and end the match, moving to a sturdy 9-2.
