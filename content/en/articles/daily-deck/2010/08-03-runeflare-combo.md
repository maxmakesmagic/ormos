Ryan Reynolds took the top slot of the Santa Clara, California Pro Tour Qualifier by abusing Howling Mine, Temple Bell, and Jace Beleren. How did he abuse them? With Runeflare Trap of course! With just a couple more months left until ***Magic** 2010* rotates out of the Standard format, Ryan made short work of his opponents with his blue-red combo deck.
