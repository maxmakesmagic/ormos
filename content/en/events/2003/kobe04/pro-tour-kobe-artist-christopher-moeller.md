Christopher Moeller has been working as a writer and painter in the comics industry since 1990. His best-known work includes **JLA: A League of One** (2000, DC Comics) and the **Iron Empires** graphic novels (2004, Dark Horse Comics). He is currently the regular cover artist for **Lucifer**, and is working on another painted JLA graphic novel entitled **JLA: Cold Steel**, due to be released in 2005. 

Christopher has contributed upwards of seventy illustrations to the collectible card game Magic: the Gathering, as well as award-winning artwork for posters, magazines, paperback and hardcover books, game illustrations, trading cards, and advertising. 

He lives in Pittsburgh, PA
