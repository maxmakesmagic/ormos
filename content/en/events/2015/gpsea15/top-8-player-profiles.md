### Andrejs Prost

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsea15/top8_prost.jpg)

**Hometown:** Anchorage, AK  
**Age:** 23  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Two Pro Tour Top 8s; one Grand Prix Top 8; Latvian National Champion.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Sharless Sultai. I didn't have enough time to get reacquainted with Miracles.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm) because it's [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Meddling Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meddling+Mage). It's very versatile.

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?**  
 AIR (All-in Red).

**When did you play your first Legacy Constructed event, and how did that go?**  
 Online. I won a Daily event.

### Jarvis Yu

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsea15/top8_yu.jpg)

**Hometown:** Rockville, MD  
**Age:** 30  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Six Pro Tour appearances; two PTQ wins at Pro Tours.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 35 Lands (Green-Red). It beats most fair decks and has excellent draws.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Gamble](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gamble). It's [Demonic Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonic+Tutor) in this deck.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Sphere of Resistance](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphere+of+Resistance). It's the best way to beat combo.

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Oops! (All Spells)

**When did you play your first Legacy Constructed event, and how did that go?**  
 2005 with [Æther Vial](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Vial) Goblins. Lost in the Top 4 to double [Engineered Plague](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engineered+Plague).

### Brian DeMars

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsea15/top8_demars.jpg)

**Hometown:** Detroit, MI  
**Age:** 31  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Grand Prix Boston champion; StarCityGames Indianapolis champion; StarCityGames Richmond Power 9 champion, etc.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Miracles because [Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top) is incredible.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon). It punishes greedy mana bases and provides free wins.

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?**  
 I didn't play against any "brews" - just real decks.

**When did you play your first Legacy Constructed event, and how did that go?**  
 I've played since 1995. I was casting [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm) since *Ice Age* came out!

### Chase Hansen

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsea15/top8_hansen.jpg)

**Hometown:** Snohomish, WA  
**Age:** 24  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 20th GP Denver; several Top 16s at StarCityGames Opens

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Reanimator because it's what I've played for over six years.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Archetype of Endurance](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archetype+of+Endurance) because Death and Taxes can't beat it.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay) because [Grafdigger's Cage](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grafdigger%27s+Cage) and [Rest in Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rest+in+Peace) exist.

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?**  
 There weren't any surprising decks that I played against.

**When did you play your first Legacy Constructed event, and how did that go?**  
 Many years ago, and it went poorly. I didn't have [Daze](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daze)s, dual lands, or [Force of Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+of+Will)s.

### Gary Wong

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsea15/top8_wong.jpg)

**Hometown:** Glendale, AZ  
**Age:** 27  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Top 4 Grand Prix Phoenix, Top 32 at Grand Prix San Diego and Los Angeles

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Four-Color Delver. [Deathrite Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathrite+Shaman), [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer), and [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets) are the best.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Gurmag Angler](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gurmag+Angler). It's a one-mana [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) that can't be [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay)ed.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay)

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?**  
 I didn't play a matchup I was surprised against.

**When did you play your first Legacy Constructed event, and how did that go?**  
 I played Legacy when it was called Type 1.5 and I lost because I was playing a Standard deck.

### Martin Goldman-Kirst

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsea15/top8_goldman-kirst.jpg)

**Hometown:** Seattle, WA  
**Age:** 25  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Played in six Pro Tours

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
[Aluren](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aluren) because nobody knows how to play against it and it's a great deck.

 Alurne because it's why the deck works.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Carpet of Flowers](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Carpet+of+Flowers) because I played against blue all day long.

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?**  
 A midrange [Life from the Loam](https://web.archive.org/web/20151108000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Life+from+the+Loam) deck but it wasn't that surprising.

**When did you play your first Legacy Constructed event, and how did that go?**  
 A StarCityGames Open with Merfolk. I went 5-3 I think.
