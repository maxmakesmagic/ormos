There are 13 cards in *Invasion* and *Planeshift* combined that create creature tokens. Most of their offspring are covered by the fine people in [Player Rewards](https://web.archive.org/web/20020815000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/arcana/103), from [Assault/Battery](https://web.archive.org/web/20020815000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Assault%252FBattery)'s Elephant to [Aura Mutation](https://web.archive.org/web/20020815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aura+Mutation)'s Saprolings to [March of Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20020815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=March+of+Souls)' Spirits.

But there are two token makers--one in each set--that produce tokens for which real physical versions do not exist: [Pure Reflection](https://web.archive.org/web/20020815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pure+Reflection) and [Questing Phelddagrif](https://web.archive.org/web/20020815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Questing+Phelddagrif).

Just as we [showed earlier](/en/articles/arcana/2002/07-30-unseen-tokens-2) for a few *Judgment* cards, these tokens do have art associated with them, art that is visible in [***Magic** Online*](https://web.archive.org/web/20020815000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline). If you create a Reflection or Hippo token online, here's what you'll see:
