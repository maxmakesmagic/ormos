*Editor’s Note: Shuhei submitted his Daily Decks before the [Banned and Restricted Announcement](/en/articles/feature/2015/01-19-january-19-2015-banned-and-restricted-announcement) and, as such, did not know the contents of the announcement. We still thought it was worth revisiting the format prior to the announcement.*

* * *

The World Championship Week wasn't only for Standard decks.

I would like to talk about the next Pro Tour's format, Modern, starting with the Temur deck the Japanese (Kentaro Yamamoto, Yuuya Watanabe, Yuuki Ichikawa) piloted.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Tarmogoyf)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150119000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf)

The question being, how to develop the blue-red [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20150119000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets) deck I introduced last week even further.

Their decision was to add green, or more specifically [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20150119000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf), the strongest two-mana creature in the format.

[Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20150119000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) is two mana but doesn't die to [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20150119000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt). It easily grows into a 4/5 green monster due to the numerous sorceries in the deck.

That's probably enough to explain how good this card is.

[Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20150119000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) and [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20150119000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) do not work well together. However, even if you have no graveyard, your opponent has one. I guess they decided it won't matter because the graveyard will probably contain three or so cards the next turn anyway.
