We've spent a few [recent](/en/articles/arcana/2010/09-20-know-your-myr) [Arcanas](/en/articles/arcana/2010/09-27-copperhorn) looking at how the world of Mirrodin has changed between the days of *Mirrodin* and *Scars of Mirrodin*. But how about the Phyrexians?

Well, let's take a look at some cards from [Phyrexia vs. the Coalition](https://web.archive.org/web/20100929000000/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/phyrexiavsthecoalition/productinfo) and contrast them with cards in *Scars of Mirrodin*. That sounds fun, right?
