T minus two days till the *Ravnica* prerelease! 

After three weeks of *Ravnica* previews, you're already familiar with the hybrid mana symbols on such cards as [Selesnya Guildmage](/en/articles/makingmagic/2005/09-05-city-planning-part-i).

![Selesnya Guildmage and its hybrid mana symbols](https://i.ormos.uk/60/93/6093c50c7cf5cbb8d1de6e72fdd9d50682eb1cda7e2da527ede1d4412f9b8069.jpg) 

The design for the hybrid mana symbols was chosen after careful consideration -- and the final versions weren't the only ideas. Here's a peek at some of the other designs considered.

![Hybrid mana symbol ideas](https://i.ormos.uk/f8/63/f863b6527cb164ae3ae60225b1398eba9dae65722b4c9cf099e8953ae46cb8d2.jpg)
