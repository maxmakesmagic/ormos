Tuesday we brought you the deck that won Grand Prix London, but there was another big event in old London town this past weekend: the royal wedding! To wish Prince William and Princess Middleton well on their journey into wedded bliss, Daily Decks presents to you the royal deck! It features the king and queen (technically they're not yet, but they will be someday), a clergyman, some royal guards, a palace for spending their days, some noble steeds to carry them off into the sunset, faithful servants, a sword, some rings, and the key to any good wedding: a bit of jester-inspired comic relief. Enjoy!
