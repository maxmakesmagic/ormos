Learn more about Grand Prix Montreal's Top 8 competitors—including who they are, what they’ve done in the game, and more—by taking a look through their profiles!

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmon16/t8_gareau.jpg)Name:** Philippe Gareau

**Age:** 28

**Hometown:** Quebec City

**Occupation:** Imaginaire.com

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** 2005 Provincial champion

**What was your Sealed deck record? What were your colors and best card?**  
 9-0 with White-Black Midrange Vampires. My best card was [Vampire Cutthroat](https://web.archive.org/web/20160801000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vampire+Cutthroat).

**What were your draft decks? What was your record with each?**  
 I went 2-1 with Blue-Red Tempo with 11 2-drops, [Startled Awake](https://web.archive.org/web/20160801000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Startled+Awake) and [Elder Deep-Fiend](https://web.archive.org/web/20160801000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elder+Deep-Fiend) in the first draft, and then 1-0-2 with White-Blue Tempo with two [Spell Queller](https://web.archive.org/web/20160801000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Queller)s in the second. Both decks had [Fortune's Favor](https://web.archive.org/web/20160801000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fortune%27s+Favor), which is the most fun ever.

**What card do you most want to open Pack 1, Pick 1 in the Top 8?**  
 Red.  
  
**Are you with Emrakul, or a part of the resistance?**  
 Emrakul.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmon16/t8_madill.jpg)Name:** Jamie Madill

**Age:** 37

**Hometown:** Montreal, Quebec, Canada

**Occupation:** Software engineer

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Nothing. 3-0'd a *Magic Online* 8-4? Won Friday Night *Magic?*

**What was your Sealed deck record?**  
 What were your colors and best card? I went 9-0 in Sealed with a Blue-Green Emerge deck splashing red for [Spreading Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20160801000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Flames). It and [Ishkanah, Grafwidow](https://web.archive.org/web/20160801000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ishkanah%2C+Grafwidow) were my best cards but [Ongoing Investigation](https://web.archive.org/web/20160801000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ongoing+Investigation) was my MVP.

**What were your draft decks? What was your record with each?**  
 I went 2-1 with an aggressive green-white deck with [Gryff's Boon](https://web.archive.org/web/20160801000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gryff%27s+Boon), two [Town Gossipmonger](https://web.archive.org/web/20160801000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Town+Gossipmonger) and two [Noose Constrictor](https://web.archive.org/web/20160801000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noose+Constrictor). Then I went 1-0-2 with black-red mono-removal deck to win the first round and draw into Top 8.

**What card do you most want to open Pack 1, Pick 1 in the Top 8?**  
 Not sure at the moment!  
  
**Are you with Emrakul, or a part of the resistance?**  
 I see myself as more of an Emrakul fan.
