A brand-new set of anthologies is coming to *MTG Arena*! With powerful and exciting cards for Historic, Explorer, Brawl, and many other formats, you won't want to miss these previews. In fact, they've already begun! We showcased some of these exciting additions in [our weekly announcement article](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2023/06-30-mtg-arena-announcements-july-3-2023#ea3previews).

To ensure you catch all the upcoming previews, we've created this handy guide to the many previews of Explorer Anthology 3 and Historic Anthology 7, going over all of the places you can see these cards revealed.

Below, you'll find a list of the outlets previewing the cards along with the date they'll be releasing them.

Don't worry, if you want to wait until all the cards have been previewed, you can find all of the previewed cards as they're revealed in the [Explorer Anthology 3 Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/06-30-explorer-anthology-3-arrives-on-mtg-arena) and the [Historic Anthology 7 Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/06-30-historic-anthology-7-comes-to-mtg-arena).

For the most up-to-date information, be sure to bookmark this page and check back each day as more exciting cards from Explorer Anthology 3 and Historic Anthology 7 are previewed! You won't want to miss it.
