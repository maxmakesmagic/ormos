*Time Spiral's* Ith, High Arcanist represents Lord Ith, powerful mage from the *Ice Age* trilogy of novels emerging from the blue curtain of time energy (the visual cue for the suspend mechanic, internally called the "[suspend barrier](/en/articles/arcana/2006/11-15-sketches-ith-high-arcanist-2)"). The secretive archmage kept a maze on his estate meant to keep out intruders—and keeping out intruders is exactly what the card Maze of Ith does (at least, for those who opened enough packs of *The Dark* back in 1994).
