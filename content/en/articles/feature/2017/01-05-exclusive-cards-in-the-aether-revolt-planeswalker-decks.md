Before we went on holiday break, we showed you the face cards of the upcoming *Aether Revolt* Planeswalker Decks. Here they are again, in case you've forgotten.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/c1lRLirbrl_AER/en_7xxmuTIsqj.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/c1lRLirbrl_AER/en_nZfbo6vo5j.png)

If you pick up the deck featuring Ajani gracing its cover, you'll get a full deck of Standard-legal cards, including these four cards that are found only in the Planeswalker Deck:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/c1lRLirbrl_AER/en_ZHnUtsPBjw.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/c1lRLirbrl_AER/en_xGmUaE08BY.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/c1lRLirbrl_AER/en_bDCS51WpIz.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/c1lRLirbrl_AER/en_2J0BLo2lxg.png)

And if you pick up the Tezzeret deck (which will totally rock your face off), you'll receive, among other cards, these four:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/c1lRLirbrl_AER/en_KxfQnkCGdN.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/c1lRLirbrl_AER/en_2JzHDFispt.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/c1lRLirbrl_AER/en_Vtdp3BnjUq.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/c1lRLirbrl_AER/en_mQbRHRwZgL.png)

Planeswalker Decks will be available with *Aether Revolt* at [a local store near you](https://web.archive.org/web/20170105000000/http://locator.wizards.com/).
