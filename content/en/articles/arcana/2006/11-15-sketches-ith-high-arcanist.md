![Ith, High Arcanist](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ith%2C+High+Arcanist)

The archmage Lord Ith, famous for his [Maze of Ith](https://web.archive.org/web/20061115000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Maze%2Bof%2BIth) and [Wand of Ith](https://web.archive.org/web/20061115000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Wand%2Bof%2BIth) cards and his exploits throughout the *Ice Age* storyline, finds himself embodied as a legend in *Time Spiral*. Today we take a look at the sketches that led to the final art by artist team Zoltan Boros and Gabor Szikszai.

Click the thumbnails below to see this humble initial sketch gradually change into the powerful archmage.
