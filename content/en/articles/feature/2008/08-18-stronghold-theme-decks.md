### ![](https://i.ormos.uk/cd/6c/cd6c8ae519b2e9d6304571f2923facbc9732125a90364925057daca071ecfb3b.jpg)Call of the Kor

The "Call of the Kor" deck introduces the Kor race and exploits its damage-redirection abilities. This unique white and black deck allows players to keep white Kor creatures in play by redirecting damage to other creatures.

### ![](https://i.ormos.uk/29/05/29054718841d3c22e634f4f6422aabdc50dc38c7eb317d190fa3816fad54e230.jpg)The Spikes

The Spikes deck introduces new creatures that sling +1/+1 counters from card to card, giving a player more control over the battlefield. As a starting point for more sophisticated decks using Spike creatures, this green and red deck is sure to be popular around gaming tables.
