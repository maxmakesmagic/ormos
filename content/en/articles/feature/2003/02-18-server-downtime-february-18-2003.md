### List Of Changes

* * *

*   **Leagues** -- The Leagues system has been changed to allow players to make up games they missed from previous weeks. For example, players who play 3 games one week may play 7 the next to make up for the missed games.

    **Note:** Leagues that started before the February 18 update will complete using the old system. Only new leagues will allow games to be made up.  
  

*   **Limited Games Room** -- The **Draft** room in the **Casual Play** area has been renamed the **Limited Games** room, and now also includes the following options:
    *   Sealed Deck - Players can play casual sealed deck tournaments in this room (rather than just matches in the **Sealed Deck** room).

    *   Teams - Sealed Deck, Booster draft, and Rochester draft can be played as teams in this room.  

*   ***Legions*** -- The *Legions* card set is included both as art and as formats in this build. Cards won't be on sale until Midnight PST on March 5, 2003. The following changes are included:
    *   Standard Format -- Now includes Standard (no *Legions*) and Standard (with *Legions*).

    *   Extended Format -- Now includes Extended (no *Legions*) and Extended (with *Legions*).

    *   Limited Formats -- Now contain selections to run tournaments in *Legions* formats (Prerelease / All *Legions* / *Onslaught* Block, etc.).  

*   **Trade Interface** -- The trade interface includes new features:
    *   Stack / Head to Foot -- The **You Give** / **You Get** windows have been altered to make cards display head to foot, with overlaid numbers indicating the quantity of each card included. Users who do not like this interface may change back to the old stacking display by using a check box in the **Display Settings** window.

    *   Double confirm -- Players are now presented with an extra confirmation screen after both users confirm a trade. This screen summarizes the trade and doesn't allow any changes to be made. Players must confirm from this screen for the trade to execute. Canceling from the second confirmation screen returns users to the trade interface.  

*   **Tribal Wars** - Casual Format 3, or Tribal Wars, is in and will be available in the **Casual Play** rooms. This is an Extended Constructed format with these additional restrictions:
    *   A minimum of 1/3 of the cards must be creatures that share a creature type.

    *   The following cards are banned: Engineered Plague, Endemic Plague, Tsabo's Decree, Peer Pressure, Unnatural Selection, and Circle of Solace.  

*   **Wizards of the Coast Invitational** -- The banner at the top of the **Main** room that used to lead to the 2002 **Magic** Invitational will now lead to the upcoming Wizards of the Coast Invitational.  
  

*   **Prize Splits** -- The top two players in any 8-player sanctioned event can now choose to split prizes automatically. The game is declared a draw and prizes are distributed appropriately.
