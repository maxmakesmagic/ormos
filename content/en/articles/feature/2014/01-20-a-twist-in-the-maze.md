Lots of people asked, so I'm here to present it in all its glory—Jon Louck's Maze's End deck from the R&D Challenge. Jon decided to spice it up and play his deck singleton style with the exception of [Maze's End](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Maze%27s+End) and [Guildgates](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&name=+%5BGuildgate%5D). 

![Maze's End](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Maze%27s+End)

There are some absurd situations that can come up when you're playing a singleton deck, as you never know what you're going to draw next. Will you be able to find that [Saruli Gatekeepers](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Saruli+Gatekeepers) to gain the life you need? Is [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay) going to show up to answer that [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections)? Will our opponent walk into our [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict)? Will we win with [Maze's End](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Maze%27s+End), or will Elspeth show up and kill us before it comes to that? 

You never know!

Jon's deck is perfect if you're looking for a good time, although I'd find room for a [Sylvan Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Caryatid) and maybe an [Axebane Guardian](https://web.archive.org/web/20140120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Axebane+Guardian). Even if you're only playing one of each spell, there can be some important stuff you might miss.
