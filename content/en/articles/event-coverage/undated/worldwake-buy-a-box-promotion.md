### Awaken Your World...

**...and get the special *Worldwake* promo card!** 

If you buy 36 boosters or 5 Intro Packs or 24 boosters and 2 Intro Packs of *Worldwake* in one of the [participating stores](http://www.wotc-direct.com/en/worldwake-storelist) in Europe, you can get the special promo card Celestial Colonnade from this store (if stock is available)\*.

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/87/16/8716049a76a0660d180c5c96b031d14eb1a57b3cb19ba219c1080601a16958e5.jpg) 

  

\*Offer valid only in participating stores in selected European countries until 2nd April 2010 and while stock lasts. Participating countries in Europe are: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Israel, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Russia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey and UK.
