Renowned deck builder Conley Woods is back at it! His green-blue Genesis Wave deck recently took 2nd place at the StarCityGames.com Open. The deck is based around generating large amounts of mana quickly which are then funneled into the ![X Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/b1/6bb1152b0a3305d0a7c2e2eb725b8ebd18620607efc62f6f38b0642b97c8501f.gif)-spell. When Conley gets going, he can flip up all manner of mana disruption in the form of Frost Titan, Acidic Slime, and Spreading Seas!
