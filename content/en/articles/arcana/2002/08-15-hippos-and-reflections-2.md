There are 13 cards in *Invasion* and *Planeshift* combined that create creature tokens. Most of their offspring are covered by the fine people in [Player Rewards](https://web.archive.org/web/20020815000000/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/arcana/103), from Battery's Elephant to Aura Mutation's Saprolings to March of Souls' Spirits.

But there are two token makers--one in each set--that produce tokens for which real physical versions do not exist: Pure Reflection and Questing Phelddagrif.

Just as we [showed earlier](/en/articles/arcana/2002/07-30-unseen-tokens) for a few *Judgment* cards, these tokens do have art associated with them, art that is visible in [***Magic** Online*](https://web.archive.org/web/20110208174103/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Article.aspx?x=magic/magiconline).
