Changelings' translucent, blue-green "skin" can stretch and contract to mimic just about any other form. As you can see in the art of some changeling cards, some of the more challenging creature-shapes put that magical skin to the test.

[](https://web.archive.org/web/20071120000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Turtleshell%2BChangeling)[![Turtleshell_Changeling](https://i.ormos.uk/81/43/814321655f72b2dface39523a813217323c72746018b71a5241b53e560ed03d1.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071120000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Turtleshell%2BChangeling)

Taking the form of a turtle is like Changeling 101. No problem.

[](https://web.archive.org/web/20071120000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Skeletal%2BChangeling)[![Skeletal_Changeling](https://i.ormos.uk/c8/29/c829913003059b6a91a5664dc304a04924e115d25824737fa020018743364fed.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071120000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Skeletal%2BChangeling)

The tiny shapes of a cat skeleton are more of a challenge, but this bemused fellow doesn't seem to notice. (Note that [Skeletal Changeling](https://web.archive.org/web/20071120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skeletal+Changeling) is both a Cat and a Skeleton, of course.)

[](https://web.archive.org/web/20071120000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Changeling%2BBerserker)[![Changeling_Berserker](https://i.ormos.uk/83/ab/83ab3fb7142e3e40ade0bed852d55a10cb7e5c91ef89db77e434adfd8f12ad37.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071120000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Changeling%2BBerserker)

Here's a true test of a changeling's flexibility: imitating the flames of a flamekin, as seen on [Changeling Berserker](https://web.archive.org/web/20071120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Changeling+Berserker)!

[](https://web.archive.org/web/20071120000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Shapesharer)[![Shapesharer](https://i.ormos.uk/51/08/510800efbe9884d976f008e83757e495fe7cec01aaae0fae3264a3f7d7a88409.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071120000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Shapesharer)

[Shapesharer](https://web.archive.org/web/20071120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shapesharer) might show the most impressive feat of changeling mimicry. This changeling is shown not only mimicking the stance and size of a giant, but also his fishing net. [Shapesharer](https://web.archive.org/web/20071120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shapesharer) isn't holding a net of its own—note the bluish tint of the structure holding its river fish. It's growing out of the changeling's thumb—it's actually part of his shapechanging body.
