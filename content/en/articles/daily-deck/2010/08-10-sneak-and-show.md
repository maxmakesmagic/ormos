One interesting strategy to come out of the recent Legacy Grand Prix held in Columbus, Ohio was Korey Age's Sneak and Show deck. The player's goal was to rush out a Sneak Attack or Show and Tell using cards like Lotus Petal and Ancient Tomb. That would allow him to plop a fatty like Emrakul, the Aeons Torn or Woodfall Primus onto the battlefield. From there it wasn't hard to mop up opponents! Read more at the [official coverage](/en/events/2010/gpcol10/saitos-legacy-continues).
