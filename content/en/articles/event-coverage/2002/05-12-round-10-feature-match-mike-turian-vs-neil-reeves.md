![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpmil02/919.jpg)The centerpieces of two respective **Magic** Online teams squared off this round (Mike from Team CMU and Neil from the board-leading Southeast Locals), each having fought to a 7-2-1 record. Both players were in joking moods, as evidenced by the first exchange after they sat down:

Neil: You hate me.  
 Mike: I don't hate you.  
 Neil: But you've said it four times!  
 Mike: But I didn't really mean it.

At the next table, the other feature match had gathered quite a crowd. Our table didn't seem to have as many spectators initially. Pro Tour player and spades afficianado Matt Linde wandered over just as the game began.

Matt: Your crowd is just me and Bleiweiss.  
 Neil: That's because we're a has-been and a never-will-be.  
 Mike: Last time someone called me a has-been, I won the Pro Tour.

### Game One

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpmil02/920.jpg)Mike won the die roll, and chose to play first. Mike also chose to mulligan first, keeping a slow hand with lands and high casting cost spells. Neil stayed with a risky hand, playing two pain lands and a [Crosis's Catacombs](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crosis%27s+Catacombs) by turn three. "This is going to be really embarrassing", Neil proclaimed, returning a land to his hand to play the dragon's lair. The Potato threw some steam in the pressure cooker with a [Call of the Herd](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Call+of+the+Herd) and [Squirrel Nest](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squirrel+Nest).

Finally, NeilR began drawing more consistent lands, sowing an Island, [Underground River](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underground+River), [Shivan Reef](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Reef) and [Shadowblood Ridge](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shadowblood+Ridge) in successive turns. These were used to bring out a [Nightscape Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightscape+Familiar), a [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog) and to Repulse the token elephant out of the game. Spurred on by the toothsome visage of everyone's favorite 1/2 creature, Neil sang this song:

"I love cookie.  
 Cookie is for me."  
 -Neil Reeves, GP Milwaukee 2002. Turn Five, Round Ten. All rights reserved. TM.

Although he tapped down to a single open mana in order to drop these spells, Mike failed to capitalize. His fifth turn [Opposition](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Opposition) met with a [Force Spike](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+Spike) (Mike stalled on four lands), and the game went downhill very quickly from there. Neil kept chanting his battle cry.

"I love cookie.  
 Cookie is for me!"  
 -Neil Reeves, GP Milwaukee 2002. Turns six, seven, eight, nine, and ten.

"I like cookies too! I'm all about the cookies!"  
 -Mike Turian, somewhere between the cookie chorus.

The CMU player kept trying to stay in the game, but each of his spells was dealt with, starting with a [Merfolk Looter](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merfolk+Looter) (counterspelled), then his hand ([Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Probe)), and lastly a second [Call of the Herd](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Call+of+the+Herd) (countered). Eventually Neil dropped a second [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog), and cleared away all the squirrels over the course of a few turns for the win.

Neil Reeves 1-Mike Turian 0.

### Game Two

Mike Turian again took a mulligan. Neil pumped his fist, and exclaimed:

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpmil02/921.jpg)Neil: You're lucky I'm not a judge, you'd get a warning for being so ugly.  
 Mike: You mean sexy.  
 Neil: That was the next thing I was going to say.

The game was even less close this time. No matter what Mike would play, Neil had an answer. An early [Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel) was [Repulse](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Repulse)d and then FTKed. A [Merfolk Looter](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merfolk+Looter) was FTKed. [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise) were FTKed. The only non-land permanent Mike could keep in play was a [Squirrel Nest](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squirrel+Nest), and Neil simply kept swinging with his army of 4/2 creatures backed with a [Nightscape Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightscape+Familiar). A [Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Probe) stripped Mike's hand of all spells, and the game ended quickly and brutally on turn nine, after Turian drew consecutive [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise) to lose the game.

Neil Reeves 2-Mike Turian 0.
