The color blue has the enviable position of hogging the ability to counter spells, but on the other hand, it then has the unenviable position of occasionally staring down a host of uncounterable creatures and other spells. Blue's enemy colors, green and red, have both led the charge in cards that can't be knocked off of the stack, but green is the only color to have had creatures attached to the property of uncounterability.

![Scragnoth](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Scragnoth)

[Scragnoth](https://web.archive.org/web/20040224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scragnoth), way back in *Tempest*, was the original "can't be countered" creature. On top of its uncounterability and large size, it has protection from blue -- so blue has trouble bouncing or blocking Scraggy after it waltzes through walls of counterspells. But [Scragnoth](https://web.archive.org/web/20040224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scragnoth) was hardly a blip on blue's powerful radar, until more recent times.... No uncounterable creatures (or other spells) were printed again until *Invasion*.

![Blurred Mongoose](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Blurred+Mongoose)

*Invasion's* [Blurred Mongoose](https://web.archive.org/web/20040224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blurred+Mongoose) originally had protection from blue as well as uncounterability, but this was seen as too obviously blue-hosing and was actually changed to protection from artifacts for a while (at which point it would have been an uncounterable version of *Mirrodin's* [Tel-Jilad Chosen](https://web.archive.org/web/20040224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tel-Jilad+Chosen))! Eventually it became untargetable instead of having a protection ability: it still hoses blue's targeted bounce spells (like [Repulse](https://web.archive.org/web/20040224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Repulse)) but can at least be blocked by the odd [Horned Turtle](https://web.archive.org/web/20040224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Horned+Turtle).

![Kavu Chameleon](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Kavu+Chameleon)

*Invasion* also brought [Kavu Chameleon](https://web.archive.org/web/20040224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kavu+Chameleon), who adds a beefy 4/4 frame and an *Invasion*-block-friendly color-changing ability to the uncounterable lineup.

![Gaea's Herald](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Gaea%27s+Herald)

*Planeshift's* (and now *Eighth Edition's*) [Gaea's Herald](https://web.archive.org/web/20040224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gaea%27s+Herald) continued the charge of bringing uncounterability to creatures, though it is not uncounterable itself. It originally cost a mere ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/green.gif) (!) but R&D worried that that hosed blue's counterspell ability *too* much, and its cost was increased to ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless1.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/green.gif).

![Root Sliver](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Root+Sliver)

Uncounterable creatures reared their head again in *Legions*. One was [Root Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20040224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Root+Sliver) -- whose art was originally commissioned for a sliver that donated an ability to block flyers -- brought uncounterability to sliverkind. The other, [Vexing Beetle](https://web.archive.org/web/20040224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vexing+Beetle), began life as a pricey 6/6 in design, shrank to a cheap 2/2, and inflated again to its present form: an uncounterable 3/3 with another control-deck-hosing growing ability.

![Vexing Beetle](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Vexing+Beetle)

No doubt **Magic** will see more uncounterable creatures in the future, and blue will continue take it on the chin. But as **Magic**'s pendulum swings, will blue have the [Last Word](https://web.archive.org/web/20040224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Word)?
