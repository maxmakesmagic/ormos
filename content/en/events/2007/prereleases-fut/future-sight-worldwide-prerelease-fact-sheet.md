**When**  
 All Tournaments will be held on the weekend of April 21, 2007

**Entry Fee & Product Mix**  

*   Check with your local tournament organizer for specific entry fee information.
*   Cost of entry will provide you, one (1) *Time Spiral*tournament pack and three (3) *Future Sight* boosters.
*   In addition, some locations may offer 3-person Team & 2-person Two Headed Giant formats.
    *   Each 3-person team will receive two (2) *Time Spiral* tournament packs & six (6) *Future Sight* boosters to build 3 decks.
    *   Each 2-person Two Headed Giant team will receive one (1) *Time Spiral* tournament pack & five (5) *Future Sight* boosters to build two decks. For further information about Two Headed Giant format, please go to the [Two Headed Giant FAQ](http://www.thedci.com/docs).

**Tournament Structure**  

*   The structure of the main *Future Sight* Prerelease tournament is modified Swiss-style.
*   *Future Sight* Prerelease side tournaments will be offered throughout the day and will consist of single-elimination rounds.

**Tournament Formats**  
 The format for each Prerelease tournament is:  

*   DCI™ sanctioned, *Future Sight* Sealed Deck.
*   Select locations, DCI sanctioned *Future Sight,* Team Sealed Deck.
*   Select locations, DCI™ sanctioned *Future Sight*, Two Headed Giant Sealed Deck.

**Prizes**  

*   Select locations, check with organizer, will offer Door Prizes of 3 *Future Sight* Booster Packs to be given out at random each hour.
*   Special commemorative *Future Sight* prerelease foil card for each tournament participant, ***while supplies last.***
*   Additional packs of *Future Sight* cards to top players, (as determined by your local prerelease Tournament Organizer).
*   Other items for winners of the main tournament and side tournaments may be awarded, (as determined by your local prerelease Tournament Organizer).

**Registration Information**  

*   Players who want more specific local tournament or registration information should call their local contact phone number.
*   Players who have general questions about the **Magic: The Gathering®** TCG, Wizards of the Coast® tournaments, the **Magic: The Gathering Pro Tour™**, the DCI players' organization, Qualifier tournaments, the **Magic®** World Championships, National Championships, Champs, or Regionals, should call Wizards of the Coast Customer Service at (800) 324-6496, or go to [http://wizards.custhelp.com](http://wizards.custhelp.com/).
