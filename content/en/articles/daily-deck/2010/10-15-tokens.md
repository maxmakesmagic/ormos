A staple of Standard during the days of *Lorwyn* and *Shadowmoor*, Tokens is back! Featuring lots of 1/1 creatures powered by pump effects, Douglas Bishop took this version to a Top 8 finish at the 2010 [State Championships](http://www.the2009s.com/) tournament in the Yukon Territory in Canada. Check out new *Scars of Mirrodin* cards Asceticism and Elspeth Tirel pulling no punches in Douglas's main deck!
