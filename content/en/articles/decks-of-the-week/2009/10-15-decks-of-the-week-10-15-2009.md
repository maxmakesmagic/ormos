The latest **Magic** set, *Zendikar*, is now legal for Constructed play! The first tournaments featuring the new Standard format are already in the record books. Meanwhile, in the world of **Magic Online** the first Pro Tour Qualifiers in the digital realm have taken place. We've got their Top 8 deck lists ready to go, as well as all the deck lists from **Magic Online**. As an added bonus, we have the Top 8 decks from Grand Prix–Melbourne, which was *Zendikar* Booster Draft.

Enjoy!
