U.S. Nationals Invitation list comprises the following:

*   The current U.S. National Champion and members of the current U.S. national team (including alternate).
*   Players from the U.S. with Pro Tour Players Club level 2 or higher.
*   Winners of invitations through Regional Championships.
*   Winners of invitations through US Open tournaments ("grinders").
*   The Top 100 DCI Composite–ranked players from the U.S. on June 10, 2009.
*   The Top 100 DCI Total–ranked players from the U.S. on June 10, 2009.

All players on the list below are already qualified for U.S. Nationals and are not permitted to play in U.S. Regional Championships and/or U.S. Nationals Open Events ("Grinders").
