The little white tribe that can is back! Kithkin were last seen during their heyday of *Lorwyn / Shadowmoor* Block Constructed play, but they returned in the hands of Daniel Vigil at last weekend's [Pro Tour Qualifier for Nagoya](/en/events/2010/nagoya11-qualifiers/qualifiers-for-pro-tour-nagoya-2011) in Denver, Colorado. The archetype saw two versions in the Top 8. The other copy was a more traditional mono-white build, but Daniel squeezed in just enough blue in to run 4 copies of Negate in his sideboard.
