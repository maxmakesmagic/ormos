# ***What is your favorite 2-card combo with commons or uncommons in Khans of Tarkir Limited?***

There are plenty of cool combos in *Khans of Tarkir* Limited. After playing with the set for several months, many of them have become well known, but I asked some of the pros in attendance today what stood out for them.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/QQ-Lee-Shi-Tian.jpg)  
**No. 9 Lee Shi Tian:** [Alpine Grizzly](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Alpine+Grizzly) plus [Dragonscale Boon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonscale+Boon). They always block the 4/2 with a morph, and then you have a 6/4 left standing.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/QQ-Shota-Yasooka.jpg)  
**Pro Tour Charleston 2006 winner Shota Yasooka:** [Armament Corps](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Armament+Corps) plus [Abzan Falconer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Falconer). You can suddenly fly over with all of your creatures.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/QQ-Ivan-Floch.jpg)  
**No. 2 Ivan Floch:** [Kin-Tree Invocation](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kin-Tree+Invocation) plus [Archers' Parapet](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archers%27+Parapet). It’s a cheap 5/5.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/QQ-Yuuki-Ichikawa.jpg)  
**No. 23 Yuuki Ichikawa:** [Secret Plans](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Secret+Plans) plus [Monastery Flock](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Flock). The morphs with a cheap unmorph cost are best with the enchantment.
