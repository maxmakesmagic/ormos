### *Magic Online* Worlds Qualifier Tournaments

* * *

### MAGIC: THE GATHERING® ONLINE PLAYERS GET CHANCE TO GO TO WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP IN BERLIN THIS SUMMER

Wizards of the Coast will host a special online tournament for ***Magic: The Gathering®** OnlineTM* players, offering a grand prize trip to Berlin, Germany, and a chance to compete in the 2003 **Magic: The Gathering** World Championship, which has an aggregate prize purse of $400,000[\*](#prize). The ***Magic®** Online* World Championship Qualifier will be held April 25-27 and is open to all ***Magic** Online* players.

**Sign-Up**  
 The open portion of the tournament will take place April 25 and 26. Players with valid accounts will be able to sign up through ***Magic: The Gathering** Online* for the open tournaments any time before the tournament start times on April 25 and 26. The cost to participate in the open tournament is $10. Players who qualify will go on to the World Championship Qualifier on April 27.

**Prizes**  
 Players will be eligible to win a limited number of booster packs of ***Magic** Online* cards in all the open tournaments, as well as the World Championship Qualifier. The top 8 players from each open tournament will earn an invitation to play in the World Championship Qualifier. The winner of the World Championship Qualifier will receive roundtrip airfare to and accommodations in Berlin during the **Magic: The Gathering** World Championship, and be invited to compete in the paper-based **Magic: The Gathering** World Championship, one of the most established events in the trading card game industry, for international prestige and cash prizes.

**Online Tournament Format**  
 The format of all the online events, including the open portion and the World Championship Qualifier, will be Standard Constructed. Players will be able to build their decks with cards from the *Seventh EditionTM, OdysseyTM, TormentTM, JudgmentTM, OnslaughtTM*, and the brand-new *LegionsTM* expansions.

Celebrating its 10th anniversary in 2003, the **Magic: The Gathering** game was created by award-winning game designer Richard Garfield and is published by Wizards of the Coast. The **Magic** game is available in 52 countries and nine languages, with more than 100,000 official tournaments sanctioned each year by the **DCI®** players' organization.

Wizards of the Coast, Inc., a subsidiary of Hasbro, Inc. (NYSE: HAS), is the worldwide market share leader in the trading card game and tabletop roleplaying game categories. A leading developer and publisher of game-based entertainment products, the company holds an exclusive patent on the play mechanic of trading card games.

For more information visit [www.magicthegathering.com](http://www.magicthegathering.com/) or [www.wizards.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20030324000000/http://www.wizards.com/).

\*For a breakdown of prize amounts, visit [www.wizards.com/worlds](https://web.archive.org/web/20030324000000/http://www.wizards.com/worlds).
