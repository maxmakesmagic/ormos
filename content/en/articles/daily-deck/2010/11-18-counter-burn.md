While it may seem counter-intuitive to die-hard burn fans, many a player has combined Lightning Bolt with Counterspell to powerful effect. Anthony Justice used just such a strategy at Grand Prix–Milwaukee. His Counter Burn deck used Prophetic Bolt, Urza's Rage, and Counterspell to stall the opponent, protect his own life total, and eventually take the opponent out. You can check out the brand new burn deck, *Premium Deck Series: Fire & Lightning*, [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20110127115910/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/ProductArticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/pdsfirelightning/productinfo).
