Looking for a cheap introduction to the world of [Magic Online](https://web.archive.org/web/20100127000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=Magic/Digital/MagicOnline.aspx)'s Classic format? Nothing beats burn."
