![](https://i.ormos.uk/35/0f/350ff7a25730cf6fdc0c3d64e1a828a5823178144811236adeb85335bdc5b921.jpg)Want to get hip-deep in *Coldsna*p immediately? Check out the *Coldsnap* theme decks, hitting shelves along with booster packs and Fat Packs on [July 21](https://web.archive.org/web/20060711000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/releases). 

### *Ice Age* and *Alliances* cards?!

Yep. Since *Coldsnap* is part of the *Ice Age* Block, its cards of course play nicely with cards from the *Ice Age* and *Alliances* sets. And just like other third sets' theme decks, *Coldsnap's* theme decks contain cards from its venerable predecessors, *Ice Age* and *Alliances*.

### Online *Ice Age* and *Alliances* cards?!

Yep. The contents of these theme decks is particularly big news for the ***Magic** Online* crowd. As soon as these theme decks hit ***Magic** Online*, *Ice Age* and *Alliances* cards that have never been available online will become available. That means your Online Classic decks can feature such true classics as [Gorilla Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20060711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gorilla+Shaman), [Bounty of the Hunt](https://web.archive.org/web/20060711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bounty+of+the+Hunt), [Giant Trap Door Spider](https://web.archive.org/web/20060711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Giant+Trap+Door+Spider), [Kjeldoran Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20060711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kjeldoran+Dead), [Death Spark](https://web.archive.org/web/20060711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death+Spark), [Ashen Ghoul](https://web.archive.org/web/20060711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashen+Ghoul), [Scars of the Veteran](https://web.archive.org/web/20060711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scars+of+the+Veteran), [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20060711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm), [Storm Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20060711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Storm+Elemental), [Browse](https://web.archive.org/web/20060711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Browse), and good old [Swords to Plowshares](https://web.archive.org/web/20060711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swords+to+Plowshares).
