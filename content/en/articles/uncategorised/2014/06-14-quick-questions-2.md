**What is the best deck in Standard?** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/QQ-Marijn-Lybaert.jpg)

*Marijn Lybaert: There isn't one. I would say that Boros Burn, Black Devotion variants, control like Esper or Blue White and Jund Monsters are the four top decks, and they are pretty evenly matched.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/QQ-Alessandro-Portaro.jpg)

 *Alessandro Portaro: The [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) decks! They have all the answers, and when the game goes long it's impossible to lose.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/QQ-Tomoharu-Saito.jpg)

 *Tomoharu Saito: That's a hard one. I like Blue/White, either with or without a splash. Maybe not in terms of power of the deck. But it has a lot of play to it, and many choices.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/QQ-Lee-Shi-Tian.jpg)

 *Lee Shi Tian: Mono black devotion, or Black based with a small splash. Cards like [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) and [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) makes it the best deck in the format.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/QQ-Patrick-Dickmann.jpg)

*Patrick Dickmann: If you have strong opening hands I think Mono Blue Devotion is the best deck. But the Black based decks are probably better on average, and can win even without a great opening hand.*
