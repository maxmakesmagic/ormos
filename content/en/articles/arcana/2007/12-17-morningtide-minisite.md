*Morningtide*, the second set in the two-set *Lorwyn* Block, hits shelves February 1st. *Morningtide* card preview articles come to **magicthegathering.com** beginning on January 7, 2007, but now you can see the *Morningtide* minisite, a resource for previews, art, flavor, and information about the set. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f6/78/f6789b415c98436a4fcc9a5aeaf085b9d108b11104d95299c7ceae25c8b21190.jpg)The *Morningtide* minisite contains: 

*   Product information about the set, including glimpses of the display packaging, boosters, and theme decks of *Morningtide,*
*   Exclusive previews of the art of *Morningtide,*
*   Background narrative on the characters and “class matters” themes of the set,
*   Online card-art jigsaw puzzle and bonus desktop wallpaper, and
*   Information about finding a *Morningtide* launch party near you, so you can find your first opportunity to play the set.

[Check it out now!](https://web.archive.org/web/20080503094145/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/morningtide/home)
