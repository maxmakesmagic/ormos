*Magic* is a game with both an always-new feature and a little history that comes with it. Players all over the world learn what's new together, and continually explore the every-growing game. Some have been doing that for longer than you might think. 

**The Players** 

Richard – better known by Neil – Reeves was once considered one of the best Limited players in the world. Jumping to the world's attention with two Pro Tour Top 8 finishes in 2002, Reeves fell into the background thereafter. With his solid performance this weekend, after winning his way back to the Pro Tour through a Pro Tour Qualifier this year, it was on him to find a way into the Top 25 for an invite to the next. 

Across from him sat another longtime player that had just qualified again through a PTQ: Neil Rigby. While his record isn't as flashy as Reeves's, Rigby was similarly carrying well over a decade of *Magic* experience. Fighting his way to at least Top 25 would ensure he'd have another chance to prove he still compete with the best. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/r11_reeves_rigby.jpg)

 *Both Neil Reeves and Neil Rigby have a long history with *Magic*, as the battle of the Neils quickly got underway.* 

  

**The Games** 

[Goblin Kaboomist](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Kaboomist) was Rigby's second-turn play, and Reeves took it as well as you'd expect. 

"Really? I can't beat that guy! This is absurd!" Reeves railed.

"Last game he made like four things." Said Rigby, playing it along – and up.

Mirroring Mountains and [Krenko's Enforcer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Krenko%27s+Enforcer)ers were a stalemate broken only by lucky rolls of Rigby's die. There were three Landmine tokens in play. 

Paragon of the Fierce Defiance added to Rigby's impressive battlefield, but a Forest from Reeves turned his [Kird Chieftain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kird+Chieftain) into a powerhouse of pumping. [Hunt the Weak](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hunt+the+Weak) bumped Reeves's Enforcer up but [Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames) from Rigby cleared the Chieftain. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/r11_reeves.jpg)

*Reeves fights through a field of goblin-made landmines.* 

  

"That's the only card that makes [Hunt the Weak](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hunt+the+Weak) good on this guy," Reeves said, pointing to his now deceased Chieftain. However, Chaining [Satyr Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Satyr+Wayfinder) into [Runeclaw Bear](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Runeclaw+Bear) meant a [Living Totem](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+Totem) notched Reeves's Enforcer up to a 4/4. 

"I have to use two of these guys to kill your 4/4," Rigby lamented with a grin, but two blew up [Living Totem](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+Totem) when it attacked the next turn. [Carnivorous Moss-Beast](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Carnivorous+Moss-Beast) after the attack put Reeves in command. When Reeves attacked with it the next turn, both of the remaining Landmines went off before Rigby when to block it. [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike) stopped that from happening. 

"That did not work out well for our hero," Rigby said.

While Rigby did reveal a [Heat Ray](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heat+Ray), killing just the Moss-Beast wasn't enough to save him from the rest of Reeves's army. 

"How do you beat the Ernham Djinn, bro?" Reeves asked, referring to the Moss-Beast with his nickname for it from another era.

"I don't know, but not like that."

Game 2 quickly got underway.

"Timewalk. Go." Reeves had played an [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic). 

Rigby passed back before Reeves continued. "Playing the beats now!" [Satyr Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Satyr+Wayfinder) joined Reeves's side. Rigby's third turn [Rummaging Goblin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rummaging+Goblin) didn't serve to filter cards. Instead, it joined in several attacks, led by [Thundering Giant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thundering+Giant). 

"We like to call this one 'First pick in my second pack.' Reeves said as he laid [Gargoyle Sentinel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gargoyle+Sentinel) on the table. He had opened two different enemy-colored lands in his draft. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptm15/r11_rigby.jpg)

*Rigby partakes in the all-Neil banter.* 

  

"All the most average of guys." Rigby agreed. [Into the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Into+the+Void) cleared the way for Thundering Gaint for another turn. After dropping Reeves to 11 life, [Amphin Pathmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amphin+Pathmage) promised a way through for Rigby. 

Then Reeves tapped all his lands and most of his creatures.

"Beasts!" Reeves called out as [Feral Incarnation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Feral+Incarnation) tipped the scales back. After a curious attack with just one Beast token, Reeved passed. It was enough mana for [Heat Ray](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heat+Ray) at the end of Rigby's turn, putting the Pathmage down to a "Bzzzt!" from Rigby. Satisfied, Reeves attacked with almost everything: three Beast tokens, [Gargoyle Sentinel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gargoyle+Sentinel), and [Satyr Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Satyr+Wayfinder). [Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames) helped stymie some damage but it wasn't enough. 

"Too little too late," Rigby lamented before drawing and playing a desperately needed sixth land. He revealed his remaining cards and extended his hand. "I was just desperately searching for land. I couldn't even draw it off the [Divination](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divination)," referring to when he cast it late in the game to no effect. 

"The only card I could think of the could beat me there was [Ætherspouts](https://web.archive.org/web/20140802000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86therspouts) and I wasn't sure you had it." Reeved said. Rigby had cleverly left up exactly the mana for it several turns. 

"If I played anything you'd just come crashing in. I left it up for several turns to see what I could do. If I had drawn a land earlier I could have taken care of the Beasts handily." Rigby just shrugged and smiled. "Good luck."

"You too."

**Neil Reeves 2 – Neil Rigby 0**
