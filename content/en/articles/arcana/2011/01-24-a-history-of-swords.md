One of the cards in *Mirrodin Besieged* is the Sword of Feast and Famine. Let's take a look!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d7/f6/d7f61c312da8d792341006d6b59716de83e1fcf7a90bad64d2c83ec4d35c12ea.jpg)  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/08/2c/082ca25ccb14fd6deef35600bbf7f31916aad26da6fc398903750aba87bab15c.jpg)

As you may already know, the Sword of Feast and Famine is part of an ongoing series of swords. This series started way back in *Darksteel* with swords featuring fire and light and ice and shadow. Not in that order.

After *Darksteel*, it wasn't clear if this was a cycle or just a pair of swords. And that's the way it stayed until *From the Vault: Relics*, which previewed the *Scars of Mirrodin* card [Sword of Body and Mind](https://web.archive.org/web/20110124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+Body+and+Mind).
