The flurry of Friday activity before a Grand Prix is both early arrivals and eager competitors ready to earn a few byes at the last minute. With Standard excitement running high there were plenty of players duking it out in the hours before Grand Prix Quebec City.

These first set of decks are those from traditional Grand Prix Trials, 32-player events where the last competitor standing took the two byes and other prizes up for grabs.
