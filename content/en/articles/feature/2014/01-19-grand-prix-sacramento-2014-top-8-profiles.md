**How long have you been playing Magic?** 15 years 

**Which of the preview cards from Born of the Gods are you most excited about?** Xenagos, God of Revels 

**What did you play on Day One?** Red/Green Aggro. [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20140119000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike). 8-1 

**What did you play in the first draft of Day Two?** Naya. [Sylvan Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20140119000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Caryatid). 3-0 

**What did you play in the second draft of Day Two?** Red/Green Aggro. [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20140119000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike). 2-0-1

Nathan Maliszewski **Age:** 33**Hometown:** Denver

**How long have you been playing Magic?** 10 Years 

**Which of the preview cards from Born of the Gods are you most excited about?** Pain Seer. More Black/White Humans. 

**What did you play on Day One?** Black/White Midrange. [Gods Willing](https://web.archive.org/web/20140119000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gods+Willing) always saved the card I cared about. 8-1. 

**What did you play in the first draft of Day Two?** Green/Black/White rares. [Ashen Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140119000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashen+Rider), he’s my boy! 3-0 

**What did you play in the second draft of Day Two?** Black/White Aggro. [Ephara's Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20140119000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ephara%27s+Warden). 2-0-1
