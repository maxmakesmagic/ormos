Woolly Thoctar was a card that drew many a pair of bugged out eyes when it was spoiled on the front page of **Magicthegathering.com**. Give Micah Rabin credit for being one of the first players to achieve mainstream success with the 5/4. It was the above list played by Rabin at Vermont States that took home the title. Other interesting *Shards of Alara* cards featured include Rip-Clan Crasher, Hell's Thunder, and Ranger of Eos. Find out about more neat States decks all week long on Daily Decks!
