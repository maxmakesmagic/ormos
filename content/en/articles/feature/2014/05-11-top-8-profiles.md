![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpwar14/t8_anteri.jpg)

 

**Sergiy Sushalskyy** **Age:** 30  
**Hometown:** Kiev, Ukraine  
**Occupation:** Sales manager

 

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**  
 Won a couple of FNMs.

 

**What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 I played Green-White Heroic with a lot of creatures and combat tricks. 7-2.

**What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 In the first draft I played a black-green midrange deck with [Reaper of the Wilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reaper+of+the+Wilds). 3-0.

**What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 An aggressive red-green deck with [Harness by Force](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Harness+by+Force) being the best card. 3-0.

 

  

 

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpwar14/t8_vanderhallen.jpg)

 

**Nicolas Vanderhallen** **Age:** 24  
**Hometown:** Antwerp, Belgium  
**Occupation:** Sales executive

 

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**  
 A PTQ win and a PTQ Top 8, and Day 2 at Pro Tour Born of the Gods.

 

**What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 Green-Blue Midrange. My best cards were [Noble Quarry](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noble+Quarry), [Fated Intervention](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fated+Intervention), and [Wavecrash Triton](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wavecrash+Triton), but [Noble Quarry](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noble+Quarry) won me most of my games.

**What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 Red-Black Aggro, 3-0. Lots of removal but my best card was [Spiteful Returned](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spiteful+Returned).

**What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 Blue-Black Tempo, 1-1-1. Best card was [Shipwreck Singer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shipwreck+Singer) which almost single-handedly won my second round.

 

  

 

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpwar14/t8_fior.jpg)

 

**Daniel Fior** **Age:** 28  
**Hometown:** Caracas, Venezuela  
**Occupation:** Wanderer

 

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**  
 Three Grand Prix Top 8s, never cashed at a Pro Tour.

 

**What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 Black-white, [Whip of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos), 8-1.

**What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 Red-white, [Lightning Diadem](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Diadem), 2-1. Deck was bad!

**What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 Black-red, two Ordeals, 3-0.

 

  

 

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpwar14/t8_lehner.jpg)

[Kiora's Follower](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kiora%27s+Follower), [Retraction Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Retraction+Helix). 9-0.

**What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 Same as in Sealed. 2-1.

**What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 Same as before. 1-0-2.

**Ben Yu** **Age:** 28  
**Hometown:** Las Vegas, Nevada  
**Occupation:** Poker player

 

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**  
 Qualified for my first Pro Tour (Born of the Gods) after only playing Magic for one year. It's the reason I'm still in Europe and here at this Grand Prix.

 

**What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 Red-Black Control, [Sigiled Skink](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sigiled+Skink), 7-1-1.

**What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 Green-White Aggro, [Nyx-Fleece Ram](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nyx-Fleece+Ram) (Baaaa!), 3-0.

**What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**  
 Green-black, [Eidolon of Blossoms](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eidolon+of+Blossoms), 2-1. Eidolon was so good Ivan Floch used a card to kill it, then [Fated Returned](https://web.archive.org/web/20140511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fated+Returned) it.

**Hometown:** Bratislava, Slovakia  
**Occupation:** Professional tourist

And he was insane in this deck … 1-1-1.
