According to this data, there are four big archetypes in Standard: Bant Company, U/R Fevered Burn, W/B Control, and Emrakul decks. (The class of Emrakul decks, good in total for 14.1% of the metagame, contains Temur Emerge, R/G Ramp, and Jund Delirium.) Together, these four make up almost 3/4th of the entire Day 2 field. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gprim16/Day-2-Pie-Chart.jpg)

While Bant Company, W/B Control, and Emrakul decks were seen in the Top 8 of [Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-eldritch-moon) last weekend, the breakout deck for this weekend is U/R Fevered Burn. 

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Thermo-Alchemist)](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thermo-Alchemist)

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Fevered+Visions)](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fevered+Visions)

Created by Pedro "\_megafone\_" Carvalho, [the deck](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/ur-fevered-visions-deck-guide/) was unveiled last weekend in Sydney, where he took it to a 9-1 record in Standard. It exploits a lot of new cards from *Eldritch Moon*, such as [Thermo-Alchemist](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thermo-Alchemist), [Collective Defiance](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collective+Defiance), and [Incendiary Flow](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Incendiary+Flow). [Thermo-Alchemist](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thermo-Alchemist) in particular blocks 2-power creatures, dodges [Kozilek's Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kozilek%27s+Return), and provides a repeated source of unblockable damage. It's what a burn deck needs in the current Standard. 

The best-known players playing the deck in Rimini are (3) Lukas Blohon and (23) Ivan Floch. Yesterday, Floch told me that he felt the deck was powerful against Emrakul strategies in particular. But after learning that the deck was a well-known commodity and that players were ready with cards like [Peace of Mind](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peace+of+Mind), Floch said that he would have chosen a different deck if he knew people would have adapted so quickly. His friend Lukas Blohon shared a similar sentiment.

> 8-0 so far with U/R Burn, deck is pretty good, surprised how many people are playing it and how many play [Orbs of Warding](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Orbs+of+Warding) :) [#GPRimini](https://twitter.com/hashtag/GPRimini?src=hash) 
> 
> — LukasBlohon (@LukasBlohon) [August 13, 2016](https://twitter.com/LukasBlohon/status/764512034079830016)

But how to call the deck? Yesterday, we put a poll on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/magicprotour/status/764373221445697536) where "U/R Thermo-Thing" and "U/R Alchemist" got the most votes. However, given that (i) some players have cut [Thing in the Ice](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thing+in+the+Ice) from their deck, (ii) [Fevered Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fevered+Visions) is arguably more important to the strategy than [Thermo-Alchemist](https://web.archive.org/web/20160814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thermo-Alchemist), and (iii) Blohon called it a burn deck, we will call it "U/R Fevered Burn" going forward. 

Will the name stick? Will the deck become a fixture of the new Standard? Or will its numbers dwindle in a sea of Bant Company? Stay tuned to find out.
