The recent [announcement](/en/articles/latestdevelopments/2011/08-12-welcome-to-the-modern-world) that [Pro Tour Philadelphia](/en/events/2011/philadelphia11/magic-weekend-philadelphia-event-information) will feature the Modern format means players the world over are looking for inspiration in building with the new format. One place to start? The 2011 **Magic Online** Community Cup, where Unified Modern was one of the formats on display. Here is Troy Reppas's White Weenie deck list from that very event!
