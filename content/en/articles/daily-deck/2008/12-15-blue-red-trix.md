It's Rerun week here on **Magicthegathering.com**, and Daily Decks is proud to do our part! This Blue-Red concoction, piloted by Kai Budde to the championship title at Pro Tour-New Orleans in 2001, was known as "Trix." It wasn't the first deck by that name, however. Trix was commonly applied to all combo decks that relied on playing Illusions of Grandeur, then using Donate to ship it to their opponent. The combo player netted a free 20 life, and their hapless opponent had to come up with the upkeep cost on the enchantment or lose 20 life when it left play! The original versions ran Necropotence, but by the time Pro Tour-New Orleans rolled around, Kai no longer had access to the infamous *Ice Age* power enchantment. Instead he used an Accumulated Knowledge engine to draw lots of cards, and Sapphire Medallion to allow him to play the combo cheaply. Read more about the event in the **Magicthegathering.com** archives [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20081215000000/http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/event.asp?event=PTNOR01).
