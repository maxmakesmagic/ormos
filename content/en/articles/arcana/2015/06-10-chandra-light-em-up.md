[Chandra Nalaar](https://web.archive.org/web/20150610000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=393821) | Art by [Kev Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20150610000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Kev%20Walker%22%5d)

Chandra has been lighting things on fire for some time now, but her [origin story is laid bare today for the first time](/en/articles/feature/2015/06-10-chandras-origin-fire-logic). First debuted in *Lorwyn* as part of the original five Planeswalkers, Chandra has been featured in numerous cards and flavor text since then. Check out some of our favorite pieces of Chandra art.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_SealofFire.jpg)

[Seal of Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20150610000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=393869) | Art by [Christopher Moeller](https://web.archive.org/web/20150610000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Christopher%20Moeller%22%5d)

Chandra's version of subtlety: fire that explodes LATER rather than right away.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_ActonImpulse.jpg)

[Act on Impulse](https://web.archive.org/web/20150610000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Act+on+Impulse) | Art by [Brad Rigney](https://web.archive.org/web/20150610000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Brad%20Rigney%22%5d)

She isn't just a pyromancer—she's a pyromancer with a sense of *style.*

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_ChandrasPhoenix.jpg)

[Chandra's Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20150610000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Phoenix) | Art by [Aleksi Briclot](https://web.archive.org/web/20150610000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Aleksi%20Briclot%22%5d)

Chandra doesn't just light things on fire. She also conjures creatures made of fire to light things on fire.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_Incinerate.jpg)

[Incinerate](https://web.archive.org/web/20150610000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Incinerate) | Art by [Steve Prescott](https://web.archive.org/web/20150610000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Steve%20Prescott%22%5d)

Chandra doing what Chandra does best. Also check out [Flames of the Firebrand](https://web.archive.org/web/20150610000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flames+of+the+Firebrand) and [Chandra's Fury](https://web.archive.org/web/20150610000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Fury).

* * *
