Pack 6 featured little of note other than Canu's questionable choice of [Famished Ghoul](https://web.archive.org/web/20000101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Famished+Ghoul) over the highly underrated [Sadistic Hypnotist](https://web.archive.org/web/20000101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sadistic+Hypnotist), while Pack 7 saw Fung choose [Mystic Visionary](https://web.archive.org/web/20000101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystic+Visionary) over [Kirtar's Desire](https://web.archive.org/web/20000101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kirtar%27s+Desire) (signaling an aggressive U/W strategy) and Canu sinking further into 3-color with [Chatter of the Squirrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20000101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chatter+of+the+Squirrel). The last of the first set of packs gave Noah Weil a 2nd pick [Cephalid Retainer](https://web.archive.org/web/20000101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cephalid+Retainer), solidifying his abandonment of Red in favor of heavy Blue.

As the draft direction switched, Nicolai Herzog opened his second consecutive [Ghastly Demise](https://web.archive.org/web/20000101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghastly+Demise), while Justin Polin got [Iridescent Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20000101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Iridescent+Angel), one of the best possible win conditions for slow Blue/White. Franck Canu took 6th pick [Careful Study](https://web.archive.org/web/20000101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Careful+Study) over [Still Life](https://web.archive.org/web/20000101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Still+Life), signaling he hadn't abandoned Blue and was still thinking 3-color. The next pack gave Polin a Broken Broker, Hovi a [Thermal Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20000101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thermal+Blast) (he would abandon Black entirely in favor of Red), and Dato a Looter. Franck Canu got a much-needed [Darkwater Egg](https://web.archive.org/web/20000101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darkwater+Egg) but had to pass a [Dreamwinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20000101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dreamwinder) to get it.

The third set of packs is where things got crazy. In four consecutive packs, rare bombs were opened. Jeff Fung scored an Aboshan, Canu snagged a [Mortivore](https://web.archive.org/web/20000101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mortivore), Froehlich busted a Kamahl, and Hovi opened a [Kirtar's Wrath](https://web.archive.org/web/20000101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kirtar%27s+Wrath) that he shipped to Polin.
