Welcome to *Mardu Cube*! This will be the *Magic Online* spotlight cube running for a week starting on April 6 and follows in the footsteps of Ryan Overturf's *Grixis Cube*, Ellie Rice's *Temur Cube*, and Autumn Burchett's *Bant Cube* in that it features only cards from three colors of *Magic*. This time, it's red, white, and black! So, if you're interested in attacking quickly, casting lots of removal spells, or otherwise not having to worry about green creatures or blue instants, then this is the cube for you!

# We *Mardu* what others Mardon't.

This cube is unique in that it features three colors prominently and has a whopping 600 cards! This is a classic cube number and borders on the "large" range. But since this experience is limited to only three colors, it was important to have diversity for repeat drafts, as you experience the full range of what the three most aggressive colors in *Magic* have to offer! That's not to say there aren't control or combo strategies, but I wouldn't expect to be playing a lot of counterspells or creatures that add mana this time around.

# Degavolving Themes

Since there are 600 cards that are mostly in these three colors, there are a lot of deep cuts that you might not see in most cubes, which allow for a cornucopia of experiences illustrated with only red, white, and black ink. For that reason, each color combination has two different themes: an "aggro" one for those who like to [Smile at Death](https://web.archive.org/web/20220405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Alesha%2C+Who+Smiles+at+Death), and a "control" archetype for those who like to [Crackle Doom](https://web.archive.org/web/20220405000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crackling+Doom). But since only 360 cards are present in each draft, they aren't quite as "thick" as they might be in other cubes. Many of the themes blend together, even across color combinations, to make your decisions while drafting that much harder and/or fun!

And to continue to set expectations, while all the cards featured in these themes are sweet build-arounds, there is a plethora of cheap creatures that attack and removal spells that stop them from attacking! Oh, and hybrid cards are fair game, as long as one of their colors is red, white, or black.
