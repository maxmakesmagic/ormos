### February 21-27: Eleven Grand Prix Events!

This was a great week to have a Grand Prix, with eleven events in the last ten years. Let's count them down!

![](http://www.wizards.com/grandprix/images/NEW_GP.jpg)

**February 26-27: 2000 Grand Prix–Cannes**: Team Black Ops (Florent Jeudon, Antoine Ruel, and Olivier Ruel) defeats Team New Wave (Thomas Preyer, Alex Shvartsman, and Bram Snepvangers)

**February 24-25: 2001 Grand Prix–Boston**: [Tom Swan defeats Scott Johns](https://web.archive.org/web/20090227000000/http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/event.asp?event=gpbos01)

**February 24-25: 2001 Grand Prix–Cologne**: [Jim Herold defeats Antoine Ruel](https://web.archive.org/web/20090227000000/http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/event.asp?event=gpcol01)

**February 23-24: 2002 Grand Prix–Tampa**: [Sol Malka defeats Jeff Cunningham](/en/articles/event-coverage/2002/02-23-grand-prix-tampa-coverage)

**February 22-23: 2003 Grand Prix–Boston**: [Brian Kibler defeats Matthew Cory](/en/articles/event-coverage/2003/02-22-live-coverage-of-2003-grand-prix-boston)

**February 22-23: 2003 Grand Prix–Sevilla**: [Anton Jonsson defeats Carlos Romão](/en/articles/event-coverage/2003/02-22-live-coverage-of-2003-grand-prix-sevilla)

**February 21-22: 2004 Grand Prix–Madrid**: [Kai Budde defeats Bernardo Da Costa Cabral](/en/articles/event-coverage/2004/02-21-live-coverage-of-2004-grand-prix-madrid)

**February 26-27: 2005 Grand Prix–Eindhoven**: [Sebastien Roux defeats Rogier Maaten](/en/events/2005/gpeind05/roux-rules-over-eindhoven)

**February 24-25: 2007 Grand Prix–Dallas**: [Raphael Levy defeats Paul Cheon](/en/events/2007/gpdal07/hall-of-famer-lone-star-in-dallas)

**February 23-24: 2008 Grand Prix–Vancouver**: [Paul Cheon defeats Ben Lundquist](/en/events/2008/gpvan08/cheon-conquers-canada)

**February 21-22: 2009 Grand Prix–Rotterdam**: [Arjan van Leeuwen defeats Robert van Medevoort](/en/events/2009/gprot09/arjan-van-leeuwen-smokes-rotterdam)
