The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on September 13, 2016. Today's Update is brought to you by Dovin Baan. Between him and Cloudblazer, I'm going to be gaining 2 life while drawing cards a lot in the new Standard.

# Today's Previews

For the previews not already found on DailyMTG, look no further than this section here. Well, click on the links below, but, no further than that. After that, you're good for the day.

1.  [Authority of the Consuls](http://weibo.com/2643025570/E83aIcwHP) | Weibo (account required)

This cheap enchantment, and far-too-late [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20160913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) hoser, is an interesting enchantment meant to slow down the opponent. Find out what it does (if you don't have an Weibo account and/or don't speak Chinese) on [this Reddit thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/magicTCG/comments/52ic9i/kldconsuls_power/).

1.  [Kaladesh Previews](http://www.gatheringmagic.com/snack-time-41-kaladesh-previews/) | Snack Time with Mike and Ant | Mike Linnemann and Ant Tessitore

Pretty art and fantastic flavor text. Seems right up their alley.

1.  More to come

We've got a few more previews still coming today. If you have a Wizards Account that is set up to receive emails, then you should receive three very Gremlin-y preview cards today. Beyond that, [The Command Zone](https://rocketjump.com/podcasts#thecommandzone) is scheduled to preview a card (watch [their Twitter](https://twitter.com/commandcast)), as is [Mark Rosewater](https://twitter.com/maro254).

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Jund Delirium](/en/events/2016/standard-mocs-2016-09-11/standard-mocs)

Continuing with this week's theme of decks that survive rotation relatively intact. Today's deck is Jund Delirium from Bone55, who finished in 3rd place at last weekend's *Magic Online* Championship Series tournament. The deck is, at its core, a [Traverse the Ulvenwald](https://web.archive.org/web/20160913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Traverse+the+Ulvenwald) Delirium deck that seeks to top things off with [Emrakul, the Promised End](https://web.archive.org/web/20160913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Promised+End) and [Distended Mindbender](https://web.archive.org/web/20160913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Distended+Mindbender) while holding firm with a ton of removal.

The deck does lose a few important cards. [Nissa, Vastwood Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%2C+Vastwood+Seer) is pretty perfect for the deck's value engine. The deck can function without it—it just doesn't want to. [Languish](https://web.archive.org/web/20160913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Languish) is more or less irreplaceable, but with only two copies main and one in the side, the deck be able to make due with more spot removal, depending on how the metagame shapes up. Finally, [Fiery Impulse](https://web.archive.org/web/20160913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Impulse) can easily be replaced by [Galvanic Bombardment](https://web.archive.org/web/20160913000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Bombardment). The rest of the main deck stays untouched.

Plus, you'll get some new toys. Chandra, Torch of Defiance is on the top of every red mage's list, and will certainly get a look.
