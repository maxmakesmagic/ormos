***Ixalan*** **Prerelease Sept. 23–24!**

There are plenty of wild and exciting new cards to discover in *Ixalan*! Chart a course for a [store near you](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/http://locator.wizards.com/) this weekend to experience the new set for yourself!

I'll be honest: *Ixalan* hooked me when I heard Pirates were going to be fighting with Dinosaurs. And with Vampires. And with Merfolk. And with other Pirates. And there was going to be *real Treasure*, in token form, hanging around too.

![Prosperous Pirates](https://media.wizards.com/2017/images/daily/cardart_XLN_Prosperous-Pirates.jpg)

[Prosperous Pirates](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prosperous+Pirates) | Art by [Joss Hass](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Josh+Hass%22%5d)

Getting into Pirates is something for another day as it's the *everything else* in *Ixalan* that I'm digging through. For example, both the [Growing Rites of Itlimoc](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Growing+Rites+of+Itlimoc) and [Legion's Landing](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Legion%27s+Landing) will be making it into my [Rhys the Redeemed](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rhys+the+Redeemed) Commander deck soon. Finding the obvious upgrades and additions is always a treat with a new set—as are the cards I'm not quite so sure about where they'll go but I'm positive they'll find a home.

Here's five that I'm still puzzling out.

# Tocatli Honor Guard

When *Amonkhet* arrived, it brought a humble little two-mana creature: [Vizier of Remedies](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vizier+of+Remedies). The tiny little text of reducing the -1/-1 countered placed on creatures opened up significant options for Modern Abzan [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company) decks.

[Tocatli Honor Guard](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tocatli+Honor+Guard) feels like it hits similar notes:

*   Hosing [enters-the-battlefield effects](/en/articles/play-design/2017/07-14-the-m-files-hour-of-devastation-part-1#enters-the-battlefield-effects), like [Torpor Orb](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torpor+Orb), turns off everything from [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) to [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan).
*   Unlike [Torpor Orb](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torpor+Orb), [Tocatli Honor Guard](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tocatli+Honor+Guard) is a creature that fits neatly into existing "[hate bears](/en/articles/play-design/2017/07-14-the-m-files-hour-of-devastation-part-1#hate-card)" and [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company) decks. Slipping this into play at the right moment can make, or more likely break, someone's plans.
*   It even specifically counters Abzan [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company) decks, forcing them to fight "fair" on the ground.

# Vanquisher's Banner

A long time ago in a *Magic* set far, far away, I previewed a card called [Adaptive Automaton](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Adaptive+Automaton). In [that article](/en/articles/seriousfun/2011/07-05-i-for-one-welcome-our-new-robot-overlord), I created a Jellyfish tribal deck that, surprisingly, mostly worked. (Umbra Mantle was involved. It was a very strange time.)

[Vanquisher's Banner](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vanquisher%27s+Banner) is another card I'm scratching my head over. While more limited than its Modern-banned forerunner, [Glimpse of Nature](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glimpse+of+Nature), casting creatures spells to draw cards is usually a good deal for tribal decks. Pumping an army, any army you choose, is also a good bet.

But where does it end up?

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Edgar+Markov)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Edgar+Markov)

A set filled with new Vampires is good for a Vampire deck of any stripe, but [Edgar Markov](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Edgar+Markov) wants to see plenty of Vampires get cast, and [Vanquisher's Banner](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vanquisher%27s+Banner) draws cards when you cast Vampires.

Elsewhere:

*   Kobolds have always been a fun little type of creature, and so are [Memnite](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memnite)s and [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter)s. Maybe [Arcane Adaptation](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcane+Adaptation) could help?
*   [Cloudstone Curio](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudstone+Curio) has been part of [Glimpse of Nature](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glimpse+of+Nature) Shenanigans in the past. Perhaps a colorless combo deck exists?
*   Elves have always been cheeky about cheating themselves into play. Slipping [Vanquisher's Banner](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vanquisher%27s+Banner) into Modern might be as easy as little green Forest friends.

# Dire Fleet Ravager

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Dire+Fleet+Ravager)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dire+Fleet+Ravager)

I have a confession: I'm a fan of [Pox](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pox) and [Smallpox](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smallpox). While [Dire Fleet Captain](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dire+Fleet+Captain) isn't either of those spells, the ability to rip away a third of opponents' life totals in one convenient package shouldn't be overlooked.

Figuring out where it ends up is a bit harder.

Standard has some options to make it easier to hit multiple times, such as [Acrobatic Maneuver](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Acrobatic+Maneuver) and [Fairgrounds Warden](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fairgrounds+Warden), but pairing it up with burn spells is the more obvious angle. Menace and deathtouch make it a threat all on its own too, but I can't shake trying to see how to make its enters the battlefield trigger work harder (maybe outside Standard):

*   [Exquisite Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exquisite+Blood) is one way to turn your frown upside down.
*   [Gonti's Machinations](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gonti%27s+Machinations) has been looking for a home since it arrived in *Aether Revolt*.
*   [Mindcrank](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindcrank) and [Bloodchief Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodchief+Ascension) have been buddies in life loss for a long time.

Of course, the format you play [Dire Fleet Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dire+Fleet+Ravager) in matters more than card legality—losing a third of your life in Commander hits much hard than in Standard. # Vance's Blasting Cannons

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Vance%27s+Blasting+Cannons)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vance%27s+Blasting+Cannons)[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Spitfire+Bastion)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spitfire+Bastion)

When *Fate Reforged* was in Standard, [Outpost Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Outpost+Siege) was a standout staple for red decks. Choosing the khans mode meant you got an extra, free card to use every turn. [Vance's Blasting Cannons](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vance%27s+Blasting+Cannons) brings that effect back (for the most part), but now asks if you can cast three spells in a turn.

If you do, you get [Open Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Open+Fire) repeatable on a land: [Spitfire Bastion](https://web.archive.org/web/20170922000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spitfire+Bastion).

The dream of playing a burn deck that just keeps on burning is amazing, but so is an enchantment that could not only help set up storm combo in Modern, but also gives your deck a little extra edge if you whiff.
