If you're a fan of white cards, you'll love the deck Eulaers Wannes took to **Magic** Game Day in Antwerp, Belgium. Using powerful creatures like Captain of the Watch and Baneslayer Angel, Eulaers was able to stall the ground against aggressive opponents. Emeria, the Sky Ruin helped ensure a solid long-game plan against control players, and making sure it all came together was none other than Kor Cartographer. The 2/2 could make sure the land drops were hit for the expensive creatures while also guaranteeing Emeria would be active and minimizing the amount of land draws Wannes experienced.
