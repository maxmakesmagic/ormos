We invited the former Grand Prix Champion Lee Shi Tian, who is currently ranked 16th in the Top 25, to our feature match table in round 11. He went with Mono-Black Devotion this weekend and so far it's serving him well, leaving him on a 8 - 1 record.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/fm11-lee-shi-tian.jpg)

*Lee Shi Tian is No. 16 in the World just now!* 

  

He would now have to go up against Russia's Alexey Shashov who is having quite a successful season as well, having already amassed 13 Pro Points, which leaves him only 5 points behind Dmitriy Butakov. He needed this victory to shorten the gap between him and his countryman and raise his hopes of leading the Russian team into the World **Magic** Cup. He went with an aggressive Naya deck. 

Shashov took a mulligan while Shi Tian thought a little longer before he decided to keep his opening 7.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/fm11-alexey-shashov.jpg)

*Alexey Shashov is playing the season of his life!* 

  

The first game was a furious exchange of blows with Shashov taking the gloves off first, inviting a [Dryad Militant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dryad+Militant) to his party. Shi Tian started fighting back with [Pharika's Cure](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pharika%27s+Cure) and he then shifted into high gear, casting Nightveil Spectre. 

Shashov was desperately looking for something to hold it at bay, but he couldn't find a solution while Shi Tian added a second copy after the first connected, exiling a [Loxodon Smiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Smiter). 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/fm11-double-nightveil.jpg)

*Nightveil Spectre was putting in serious work for Shi Tian in the first game!* 

  

Shashov drew, but he failed to find an out. Shi Tian added [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel) and that already concluded the first game since Shashov still couldn't find an answer to the flying attackers! 

**Lee Shi Tian claims the lead!** 

The second game started in much slower fashion; both players opted not to make any plays until turn 3, where Shi Tian cast Nightveil Spectre again. [Selesnya Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Selesnya+Charm) got Shashov a token and he added [Fabled Hero](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fabled+Hero) the turn after. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/fm11-fabled-hero.jpg)

 *Shashov was on to something, casting [Fabled Hero](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fabled+Hero)!* 

  

When [Ghor-Clan Rampager](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghor-Clan+Rampager) got sent to the graveyard for its effect to turn [Fabled Hero](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fabled+Hero) into a real threat, Shi Tian attempted to get rid of the attacker with [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall), however, [Brave the Elements](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brave+the+Elements) made sure Shashov's Hero wouldn't fall just yet. 

Shi Tian's second attempt to deal with the Hero was much more successful; [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight) made short use of it. Another [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall) the turn after left Shashov with no field. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/fm11-ghor-clan-rampager.jpg)

 *Shashov rebuilt with [Ghor-Clan Rampager](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghor-Clan+Rampager)!* 

  

The Russian came back with [Ghor-Clan Rampager](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghor-Clan+Rampager). 

[Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel) wasn't putting up too much of an opposition, Shi Tian then failed to find a target for [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) since Shashov used his only non-land card, [Selesnya Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Selesnya+Charm), in response and when the Russian also added [Voice of Resurgence](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voice+of+Resurgence) on his following turn, Shi Tian shuffled up. 

**Shashov ties the score!** 

Wow; there weren't even 15 minutes off the clock and we had already seen the conclusion of two games. Everything would now be on the line in the deciding game, where Shi Tian would go first.

He kicked things off with [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress), sending [Giant Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Giant+Growth) to the graveyard. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/fm11-duress-game-3.jpg)

 *[Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) marked Shi Tian's opening in the deciding game!* 

  

[Voice of Resurgence](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voice+of+Resurgence) marked Shashov's opening play, but he wouldn't stay ahead on board for long; a [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie) exiled [Ghor-Clan Rampager](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghor-Clan+Rampager). 

Shi Tian applied more pressure with Nightveil Spectre and all the Russian could muster up was another [Selesnya Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Selesnya+Charm) to add a token. He added [Dryad Militant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dryad+Militant), but that was still leaving him far behind. 

Shi Tian tapped his creatures, with Nightveil Spectre connecting for the first time this game. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/fm11-board-state-game-3.jpg)

 *Shi Tian was far ahead in the third game!* 

  

When Shi Tian added [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon), Shashov had to think long and hard how to put up any form of an offensive effort. He added a [Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion) and sacrificed it right away again to make sure [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon) wouldn't get to stop his attackers. 

It wasn't enough for the Russian, though; Shi Tian cast [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel), he pointed at the many Swamps among the creatures he controlled and that concluded the game! 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/fm11-gray-merchant-finish.jpg)

 *[Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel) wrapped things up for Lee Shi Tian!*
