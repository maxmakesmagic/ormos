**Which card in Theros Sealed has changed the most in value for you since the set was released?**

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/stern_qh.jpg)

No. 25 Ranked Player Jon Stern — *“I thought [Magma Jet](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Jet) was a premium uncommon when the set was first released. But now, I know that it doesn't kill what you need to kill. I also initially thought [Voyaging Satyr](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voyaging+Satyr) was very good, but now I think it is even better.“*

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/demestrio_qh.jpg)

Grand Prix Portland Finalist Joe Demestrio — *“[Hopeful Eidolon](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hopeful+Eidolon). I always thought it was okay, but now I think it may be in the top 5 commons of the set for Sealed.”* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/manfield_qh.jpg)

Two-Time Grand Prix Champion Seth Manfield — *“Probably [Sedge Scorpion](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sedge+Scorpion).”*

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/turtenwald_qh.jpg)

No. 12 Ranked Player Owen Turtenwald — *“[Dragon's Mantle](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragon%27s+Mantle). I thought it was medium, but it's actually quite good.”* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/hayne_qh.jpg)

No. 12 Ranked Player Alexander Hayne — *“[Aqueous Form](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aqueous+Form). At the start of a format, cards like that seem only like card disadvantage, but it turns out the format is more about building up a big guy and cards like that are key for breaking through.”*

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/gptor13/duke_qh.jpg)

No. 3 Ranked Player Reid Duke — *“[Opaline Unicorn](https://web.archive.org/web/20131201000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Opaline+Unicorn). It didn't really stand out to me at first, but I think both the ramping effect and the color fixing have both impressed me.”*
