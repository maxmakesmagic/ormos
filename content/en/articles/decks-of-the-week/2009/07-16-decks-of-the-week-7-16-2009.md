Looking for some deck lists to get your creative juices flowing for the new Constructed formats headed your way? Decks of the Week is here with your weekly dosage exactly that !

And if you missed your Prerelease? Never fear; there's more Release Event festivities headed your way this weekend! Find out where on the ***Magic** 2010* [product page](https://web.archive.org/web/20100118183352/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Products.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/products/magic2010).
