At 8:41 AM yesterday, Community Manager Mike Robles used the @wizards\_magic Twitter account to tweet [this](https://twitter.com/#!/wizards_magic/status/169461272826490881):

> 
> [@wizards\_magic](http://www.twitter.com/wizards_magic) (Mike) Hey guys. Let's have some fun today. Chandra: Valentine, you set fire to my heart. #mtgvalentines
