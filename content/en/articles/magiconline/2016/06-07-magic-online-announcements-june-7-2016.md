**The Legacy Festival Begins June 8!**

*Magic Online* has festivals to celebrate a format and its players, as well as to encourage others to join in on the fun. Interested in playing Legacy but haven't given it a try? Already play and want to get your friends to join you? This festival is the time! The Legacy Festival will walk players through the essential steps to discovering a format:

*   DISCOVER: A way to try out the format. Learn about the different decks, see what the metagame is like, and find a deck that you want to play.
*   ASSEMBLE: Play the new *Eternal Masters* set for an an opportunity to acquire cards you'll need for your deck.
*   CONQUER: Once you've gotten your deck together, a way to play for some sweet prizes when you jump in and take on the competition.

Interested in trying out Legacy? The Legacy Festival begins on June 8, at 10:00 a.m. PT/5:00 p.m. UTC, with Legacy Gauntlet Events! Get the full event details [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2016/05-13-magic-online-legacy-festival-discover-assemble-conquer#Discover) and peruse the available decklists [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/2016/06-01-the-legacy-gauntlet).

***Eternal Masters* Events Begin June 16**

The "Discover" phase of the Legacy Festival begins tomorrow, June 8, and is immediately followed up by the *Eternal Masters* "Assemble" phase on June 16! The Legacy Festival's second phase is where you can start assembling your Legacy deck of choice via *Eternal Masters* Events that kick off right after the Legacy Gauntlet's conclusion! Get the full *Eternal Masters* Event details [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2016/05-13-magic-online-legacy-festival-discover-assemble-conquer#Assemble) and get ready to play from June 16-July 6.

**The Year of Modern Flashbacks Adds *Morningtide***

It's 2016: the Year of Modern Flashbacks! If you want to build up a collection for Modern, these Flashback Drafts add everything you draft to your collection once the event is over. Even if Modern isn't your thing, you can enjoy a leisurely draft through the planes of the past with rotating formats each week!

*Lorwyn*-*Lorwyn*-*Morningtide* Flashback Drafts begin Wednesday, June 8, at 10:00 a.m. PT/5:00 p.m. UTC, and are followed up with a triple *Shadowmoor* Flashback Draft. The [full Flashback Draft schedule](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/12-14-year-of-modern-flashbacks) has been updated with formats throughout the rest of the year, so mark your calendar for the formats you're most excited about!

**Watch TWO Super Leagues This Week**

Hear ye, hear ye! Let it be known that there are *two* different opportunities to watch a Super League on [twitch.tv/magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic) each and every week through June 30! Tune in at 5:00 p.m. PT/12 a.m. UTC for Vintage Super League Tuesdays and Community Super League Thursdays!

This week, the Vintage Super League will be in Week 2 of Season 5 and the Community Super League will be seeing who the most flavorful deck chef is in Ironroot Chef!

**Double Pro Tour Qualifier Weekend**

Attention all Pro Tour hopefuls: there's a double Pro Tour Qualifier (PTQ) weekend coming up! Standard Pro Tour Qualifier Preliminaries and the *Shadows over Innistrad* Sealed Pro Tour Qualifier Preliminaries begin Thursday, June 9, and run through Sunday, June 12.

Learn more about *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20160607000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers#KLD_schedule).

**Reminder: No Downtime on June 8**

In an effort to make sure *Magic Online* is available when you want to play *Magic*, we’re working on ways to reduce the number of times we have to bring the system offline for maintenance. As a first step we’re simply going to eliminate at least one downtime each month.

Here are the dates of the exact downtimes we’ll be skipping through August of 2016:

*   June 8
*   June 22
*   July 13
*   August 3
