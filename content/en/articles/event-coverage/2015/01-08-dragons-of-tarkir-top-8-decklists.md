The Pro Tour Qualifier season for Pro Tour *Dragons of Tarkir* is under way. This is your chance to test your knowledge of Standard Constructed, because invitations and tickets to Pro Tour *Dragons of Tarkir* go to PTQ winners!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptq_400.jpg)

Look through the events below for Top 8 Standard decklists. Decklists will be updated during the PTQ season. The new season runs December 6, 2014 – February 22, 2015 . For more information about the PTQ season, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20150108000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/qualifiers-pro-tour-dragons-of-tarkir).
