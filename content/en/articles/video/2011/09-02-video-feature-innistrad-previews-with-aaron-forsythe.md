Senior Director of Magic R&D Aaron Forsythe previews four cards from Innistrad, the new set coming out at the end of the month. Check out Village Cannibals, Snapcaster Mage, Slayer of the Wicked, and a new double-faced card Instigator Gang / Wildblood Pack. You can see the complete card preview gallery for Innistrad [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20140217222036/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/innistrad/cig).
