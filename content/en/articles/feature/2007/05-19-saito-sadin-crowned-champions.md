Top **Magic** players around the world flocked to Strasbourg, France, and Columbus, Ohio, to take part in a huge Grand Prix weekend. More than 2,000 players shuffled up in the major events, but only two could be crowned champion. Dive in to complete coverage from Brian David-Marshall, Hanno Terbuyken, Richard Hagon, Ben Coleman, and Craig Jones. 

### [Grand Prix–Strasbourg: Saito Cruises to Block Constructed Victory](https://web.archive.org/web/20070519000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/saitou-seizes-strasbourg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/38/ae/38ae4731557ef4ec8d6d76ade1f79a8d9517cba33d3e954b51434368c0cdd732.jpg)Tomoharu Saito's mono-red Greater Gargadon-powered deck was enough to put him in the books for a cool $3,000 and 8 Pro Points to slingshot him into the lead in the Player of the Year race.

  

### [Grand Prix–Columbus: Sadin Smashes with Hulk Flash](https://web.archive.org/web/20070519000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/hulk-flash-smashes-columbus)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f0/c6/f0c614e507e2134a04a52e5d0c1d24a10a4b0e5b753c91e9c5b7b65339ef8b63.jpg)Nearly 900 players descended on Columbus with Goblins, Affinity, Threshold, mono-black, Slivers, and just about every Legacy deck you can name, but Steve Sadin piloted a Billy Moreno-designed version of Hulk Flash to victory.
