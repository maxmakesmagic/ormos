### Game 1

オリジナル《呪文織りのらせん/[Spellweaver Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellweaver+Helix)》デッキ vs. 噴出サイカトグ

先攻中村が 2 ターン目に《金属モックス/[Chrome Mox](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chrome+Mox)》を介しての《直観/[Intuition](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Intuition)》を通して《陰謀団式療法/[Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy)》を 3 枚指定。次のターン《陰謀団式療法/[Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy)》のキャストに対して八朔のレスポンスに注目が集まったが、中村が《対抗呪文/[Counterspell](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterspell)》と指定するのを読んでか、ノータイムでありませんと宣言。手札には《渦巻く知識/[Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm)》も有り唯一のカウンターであった《マナ漏出/[Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak)》を隠しても良かったのだが、絶対に外すと読んでのノータイム宣言は流石といえる。実際に中村が指定したのは《対抗呪文》だった。

その後は思うように動けない中村に対して《嘘か真か/[Fact or Fiction](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fact+or+Fiction)》、《蓄積した知識/[Accumulated Knowledge](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Accumulated+Knowledge)》と連続でキャストした八朔の《サイカトグ/[Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog)》がパワー 20 を超える状態であることを確認すると中村は投了を宣言した。

**中村 0 – 1 八朔**

### Game 2

コンボ要素である《呪文織りのらせん/[Spellweaver Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellweaver+Helix)》を引っこ抜いてマッドネスに黒を加えた様なデッキに変化した中村は 1 ターン目から《陰謀団式療法/[Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy)》（指定は《対抗呪文/[Counterspell](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterspell)》、手札に無し。）《野生の雑種犬/[Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel)》と連打、《野生の雑種犬/[Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel)》に対して予想していなかった様で驚いた表情を見せる八朔。

《蓄積した知識/[Accumulated Knowledge](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Accumulated+Knowledge)》でタップアウトした瞬間に《直観/[Intuition](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Intuition)》を通されてしまい、墓地に《ワームの咆哮/[Roar of the Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roar+of+the+Wurm)》 2 枚が送り込まれ苦しい戦いが続く。

《渦巻く知識/[Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm)》《蓄積した知識/[Accumulated Knowledge](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Accumulated+Knowledge)》と連打してなんとか 1 回は《ワームの咆哮/[Roar of the Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roar+of+the+Wurm)》をカウンター出来たが、その次のカウンターは中村の《堂々巡り/[Circular Logic](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Circular+Logic)》に阻まれてしまい、ライブラリーをシャッフルする手段が無い状態から《渦巻く知識/[Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm)》を撃たざるをえない状況に追い込まれてしまう。

しかし、ここで八朔は無事《燻し/[Smother](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smother)》を引き当てライフ 10ながらもなんとか盛り返せる体制が整ってきた。

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpoka04/r9fm2ja2.jpg)

中村は1 度目の《陰謀団式療法/[Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy)》で見えていた《噴出/[Gush](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gush)》を指定しての《陰謀団式療法/[Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy)》（八朔の手札は《嘘か真か/[Fact or Fiction](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fact+or+Fiction)》《サイカトグ/[Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog)》）をプレイするのだが、《渦巻く知識/[Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm)》によってかわされてしまい、泣く泣く唯一のクリーチャーである《野生の雑種犬/[Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel)》をサクリファイスしての《陰謀団式療法/[Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy)》フラッシュバック。指定は《嘘か真か/[Fact or Fiction](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fact+or+Fiction)》。勝つための道を模索し続ける中村。

その後中村は《直観/[Intuition](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Intuition)》《綿密な分析/[Deep Analysis](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deep+Analysis)》と引いて手札を増やすのだが･･･ 10 枚引いて土地が 7 枚という有様ではどうすることも出来ない。

最強リセット呪文《激動/[Upheaval](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Upheaval)》を通してからの《サイカトグ/[Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20040124000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog)》が出て中村敗北。

**Final Results : 八朔 人平 Win**
