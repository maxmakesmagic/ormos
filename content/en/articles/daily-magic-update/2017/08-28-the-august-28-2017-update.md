# Deck of the Day

1.  [Standard Blue-Black Zombie Gift Primer](https://mtg.one/standard-ub-zombie-gift-primer/) | MTG.one | Nick Peternell

Why stop innovating? The basic shell of the [God-Pharaoh's Gift](https://web.archive.org/web/20170828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=God-Pharaoh%27s+Gift) strategy is really only six cards—the gift itself, plus 4 [Gate to the Afterlife](https://web.archive.org/web/20170828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gate+to+the+Afterlife). After that, you really just need a bunch of creatures that find their way to the graveyard with some regularity, and the ways to do so are many. Peternell decided to have his cake and eat brains too, mixing Zombies—which are really good at dying—with the [Gate to the Afterlife](https://web.archive.org/web/20170828000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gate+to+the+Afterlife) engine. There are a ton of ways you can take that core, and this is certainly a good one for Zombie fans.

(Note: the thirteen-card sideboard is as the article was published.)
