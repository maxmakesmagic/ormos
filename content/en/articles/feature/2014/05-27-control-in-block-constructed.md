In a Block Constructed Pro Tour, many strategies that are powerful at the Pro Tour shape the future of Standard. With access to a smaller card pool, deck builders for Block Constructed are forced to find new ways to do solve problems. Much of the time, these restrictions can breed innovation.

![Prognostic Sphinx](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Prognostic+Sphinx)

Stu Somers's finished in 12th place at [Pro Tour *Journey into Nyx*](https://web.archive.org/web/20140527000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/chapins-journey-takes-him-pro-tour-title) with an Esper Control deck that is strategically similar to Esper Control in Standard. However, there are many cards in Stu's deck that might have a place in Standard. [Prognostic Sphinx](https://web.archive.org/web/20140527000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prognostic+Sphinx) was one of the big winners of the Pro Tour as a finisher that is extremely difficult to kill. It lines up especially well with [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140527000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion), another card that made big waves in Block Constructed. Stu used these cards as a one-two punch to help him in *Theros* Block Constructed. Can it make the transition to Standard?
