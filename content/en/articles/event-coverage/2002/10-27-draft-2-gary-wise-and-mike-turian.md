Gary Wise and Mike Turian—teammates on the Pro Tour winning Potato Nation—both need to go 3-0 in order to make the Top 8. Only one player form their pod can advance and the former teammates found themselves in seat one and eight as the draft got underway.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpphi02/a895.jpg)

*   Gary Wise
*   Dan Rodemann
*   Gerard Fabiano
*   Matt Severa
*   Pat Sullivan
*   John Sonmez
*   Chris Manning
*   Mike Turian

Gary went immediately into white, choosing [Pacifism](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pacifism) over [Snarling Undorak](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snarling+Undorak), [Swat](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swat), and [Chain of Plasma](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chain+of+Plasma). Mike took a [Mistform Dreamer](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistform+Dreamer) and [Crown of Suspicion](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crown+of+Suspicion)s on the wheel. In the second pack—which opened on a [Silklash Spider](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silklash+Spider)—Turian took a [Haunted Cadaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Haunted+Cadaver) and a [Misery Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Misery+Charm) on the bounce while Gary took a [Wave of Indifference](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wave+of+Indifference) and a cycling card on the wheel.

The third pack opened with a first pick [Nantuko Husk](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Husk) and then [Contested Cliffs](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Contested+Cliffs). Turian seemed content to be the only blue mage at the table—is that the lot of the eighth seat? —and took a [Screaming Seahawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Screaming+Seahawk). Gary took an [Akroma's Blessing](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroma%27s+Blessing) and got a [Daru Cavalier](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daru+Cavalier) on the way back—this would become a habit for Gary.

Matt Severa's started to emerge as the early front-runner in the pod when he opened [Centaur Glade](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Centaur+Glade) for his G/R deck. Turian remained the only blue mage and took a [Fleeting Aven](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleeting+Aven) while Gary nabbed a late [Goblin Sledder](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Sledder) and a [Goblin Piledriver](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Piledriver) on the flip side. Gary's nearest competitor for white cards? Pat Sullivan—four seats away!

Sullivan's B/W was coming together nicely and he opened a [Smother](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smother) that required little thought. Turian took a [Fallen Cleric](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fallen+Cleric) and Wise was able to pick up a fairly late [Gustcloak Harrier](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gustcloak+Harrier) with a [Gustcloak Runner](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gustcloak+Runner) on the flip side.

Sonmez opened a [Sparksmith](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sparksmith) that Gary looked at longingly but it was not to be as Sonmez nabbed it giving Manning [Voice of the Woods](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voice+of+the+Woods) and Turian a [Severed Legion](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Severed+Legion). Gary consoled himself with a [Dive Bomber](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dive+Bomber). The seventh pack plopped a second pick [Nantuko Husk](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Husk) into Turian's lap and Gary picked up [Crude Rampart](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crude+Rampart)s and [Renewed Faith](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Renewed+Faith). Turian first picked a [Voidmage Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voidmage+Prodigy) to close out the first eight packs and Gary nabbed a [Shock](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shock) over Avarix, which found its way into Severa's beastly pile of cards.

The next set of packs reversed directions and Turian scooped up a [Swat](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swat). Manning took a Jareth with the second pick over a [Wirewood Savage](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wirewood+Savage). With two Undoraks and a [Voice of the Woods](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voice+of+the+Woods) already under his belt the Savage seemed like a sure bet for the YMGer. [Foothill Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foothill+Guide) and a random [Complicate](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Complicate) comprised Gary's wheel. The Savage ended up going seventh. Severa chose a Mauler over it for his deck.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpphi02/a894.jpg)

The ninth pack yielded a [Unified Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unified+Strike) for Gary and a [Mistform Stalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistform+Stalker) and [Fallen Cleric](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fallen+Cleric) for Turian on the wheel. Gary took a [Pearlspear Courier](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pearlspear+Courier) in the next pack while Turian took [Read the Runes](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Read+the+Runes) with a bounced [Mistform Wall](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistform+Wall). Gary got another [Daru Cavalier](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daru+Cavalier) on the way back. Sullivan took a [Dive Bomber](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dive+Bomber) out of his pack but there was still an [Improvised Armor](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Improvised+Armor) for Gary. Turian took a [Festering Goblin](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Festering+Goblin) and got a [Frightshroud Courier](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frightshroud+Courier) on the bounce. Gary happily took a [Daru Healer](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daru+Healer) the long way back.

Matt Severa's deck took a turn for the frightening with a first pick [Gratuitous Violence](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gratuitous+Violence). Wise and Turian took a [Pacifism](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pacifism) and a [Festering Goblin](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Festering+Goblin) respectively. Wise found nothing better than a [Foothill Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foothill+Guide) in the next pack and Turian took a [Thrashing Mudspawn](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thrashing+Mudspawn). Wise woke up X-mas morning and found a second pick [Slice and Dice](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slice+and+Dice) under his tree. Turian nabbed a [Tribal Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tribal+Golem) to compliment his Mistform men. Wise closed out the second set of packs with a nigh unkillable [Tephraderm](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tephraderm) while Turian took a [Dirge of Dread](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dirge+of+Dread).

Christmas turned into an eight day Chanukah celebration for Wise when he opened up a Jareth to start the final round of packs. Turian wheeled a second Seahawk and a [Wretched Anurid](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wretched+Anurid). Somewhere in the last eight packs Dan Rodemann emerged as the other blue drafter a the table and picked up a pick one [Callous Oppressor](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Callous+Oppressor) that would surely have made it to Turian otherwise. Turian took another [Dirge of Dread](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dirge+of+Dread) and a [Mistform Wall](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistform+Wall) while Gary wheeled his third [Daru Cavalier](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daru+Cavalier) and a [Secluded Steppe](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Secluded+Steppe).

Fabiano first picked a [Lavamancer's Skill](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lavamancer%27s+Skill)s out of a very good pack. Severa finally got the [Wirewood Savage](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wirewood+Savage) his deck was pining for, Sully took a [Mobilization](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mobilization) and Sonmez took a Gourna. Turian was excited to grab a [Riptide Biologist](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Riptide+Biologist) and a [Barren Moor](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barren+Moor) while Gary grinned at Sully as he picked up a [Dawning Purist](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dawning+Purist).

Severa picked up gratuitous [Gratuitous Violence](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gratuitous+Violence)—his second! Turian snatched a [Screeching Buzzard](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Screeching+Buzzard) to go with his [Screaming Seahawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Screaming+Seahawk)s and Wise picked up the Avarix he missed earlier. Nothing else exciting happened until the final three packs when Wise picked up a twelfth pick Cavalier—his fourth!—and [Sparksmith](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sparksmith)s in the last two packs! His deck went form good to formidable in the final two packs.

I sat down with Turian as he spread out his cards.

Turian: "The only guy that could possibly open a [Callous Oppressor](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Callous+Oppressor) and have it not end up my deck. I was so mad, I wanted that guy!"

He seemed pretty happy with his [Read the Runes](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Read+the+Runes) as he examined the possibilities of using the card with his multiple [Festering Goblin](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Festering+Goblin)s. "This card is a bomb", he nodded seeing its potential more clearly with his cards out in front of him. Apparently he regretted one pick, which might come as a surprise to most readers. He tried to bounce an [Imagecrafter](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Imagecrafter) over a [Severed Legion](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Severed+Legion) but the 1/1 wizard did not come back to him. Had he to do it all over again, Turian claims he would pick the wizard.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpphi02/a893.jpg)

I asked him bout his chances to 3-0 the table and he seemed confident that he had the best deck. He conceded that Wise's deck was also quite good, "We cooperated really well together."

When I asked Wise how he felt about his deck he could only laugh maniacally and show off his pack 23 and pack 24 [Sparksmith](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sparksmith)s. "I think I got the most powerful deck at the table!" With the exception of his own he felt that Severa's deck had the best chance to run the table. "I really wanted a [Demystify](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demystify) but it just wasn't there." He is running the [Dawning Purist](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dawning+Purist) in case he has to face the [Gratuitous Violence](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gratuitous+Violence).dec. As I walked away he was still cackling, "I have a [Slice and Dice](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slice+and+Dice) with FOUR [Daru Cavalier](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daru+Cavalier)s!"

I also had the chance to ask Manning about his choice of Jareth over [Wirewood Savage](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wirewood+Savage). He could only shake his head and show me an Elvish Pathfinder he had to play in his deck alongside TWO [Voice of the Woods](https://web.archive.org/web/20021027000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voice+of+the+Woods)! "I think I overestimated Jareth and underestimated the Savage. I'm not even playing with him."

In the end I think that both Wise and Turian have a good shot at the Top 8 but if you are looking for a dark horse candidate keep an eye out for Matt Severa.
