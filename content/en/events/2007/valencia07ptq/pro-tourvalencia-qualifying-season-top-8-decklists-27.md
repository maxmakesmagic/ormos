![](https://www.wizards.com/protour/images/qt_lg.jpg)

Refine your *Time Spiral* Block Constructed skills to earn a ticket to Valencia, Spain, by winning a Pro Tour Qualifier! 

You can test each decklist thanks to our [Sample Hand Generator](https://web.archive.org/web/20130710150318/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/294). Just click on the card fan in the upper right of each decklist and you can see what your opening hand could be, along with mulligans and the first three cards you'd draw.

Decklists will be updated weekly during the PTQ season. The season runs until August 26. To find a PTQ near you, [click here](/en/events/2007/valencia07-qualifiers/pro-tourvalencia-07-qualifiers). Pro Tour-Valencia is scheduled for October 12-14, 2007, and the format is Extended.
