*This article originally ran February 1, 2007*

P*lanar Chaos* features timeshifted cards that resemble older cards, but twisted into a new color. The art of these cards often references the original in some way, or other past cards to flavor.

Check out the art of two graveyard-manipulation spells—one a spell from *Ice Age*, the other timeshifted in *Planar Chaos*.

It’s not just the art of *Planar Chaos*’s timeshifted cards that look back in time for inspiration. Note how Jim Nelson’s [Ana Battlemage](https://web.archive.org/web/20071224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ana+Battlemage) has the same kind of barklike skin covering as Matt Cavotta’s [Anavolver](https://web.archive.org/web/20071224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anavolver).

![Ana Battlemage](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ana+Battlemage)

![Anavolver](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Anavolver)

And Jim Nelson looked at an older piece for [Muck Drubb](https://web.archive.org/web/20071224000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Muck+Drubb), too—this time not for a direct mechanical link at all, but rather a similar composition and mood. Can you see the resemblance to a creature from **Magic**’s past?
