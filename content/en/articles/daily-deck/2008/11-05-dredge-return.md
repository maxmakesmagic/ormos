With the rotation of a number of sets from Extended, many thought the popular Dredge strategy was surely dead. A group of pros proved the world wrong at Pro Tour–Berlin. Their new take on the deck, exemplified by Armin Berner’s version above, tries to dredge up enough cards early to kill the opponent with a Flame-Kin Zealoted crowd of Zombie tokens from Bridge from Below. Barring that, it’s not afraid to simply use Dread Return to plunk a huge fattie into play. How does it manage all this? Discard/mill spells like Glimpse the Unthinkable, Goblin Lore, and Ideas Unbound alongside Magus of the Bazaar. Check out how well the 0/1 combos with Fatesticher from the graveyard, allowing you to pay a singe blue mana to untap the Magus and dredge two more times!

For more on Pro Tour – Berlin including blogs, feature matches, podcasts, and video coverage, check out the [official Pro Tour–Berlin event coverage](/en/events/2008/ptber08/lsv-elves-equals-champion)!
