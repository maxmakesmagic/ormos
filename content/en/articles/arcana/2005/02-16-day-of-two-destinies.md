Daren Bader illustrated *Betrayers of Kamigawa's* legendary enchantment, **Day of Destiny**. Not only does the art take a cool perspective from under one samurai's thundering horse, it depicts two -- count 'em, *two* -- legendary creatures from *Champions of Kamigawa*.

Can you recognize the two legends? Click around on the larger image below to find them.
