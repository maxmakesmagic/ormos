Eternal formats, even in the digital world of **Magic Online**, isn't always about the most broken cards in the format. Tallandskinnyman proves exactly that using this blue-white-green concoction to Top 8 a recent Classic Premier Event online. The creatures are straight out of the plane of Alara: Rhox War Monk, Jenara, even Meddling Mage. Proof positive that innovative deck design can be more important than powerful cards.
