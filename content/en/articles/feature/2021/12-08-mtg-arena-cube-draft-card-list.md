*Updated December 8, 2021*

[Alchemy](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2021/12-02-introducing-alchemy-a-new-way-to-play-mtg-arena) brings new digital-only cards and rebalanced older cards to *MTG Arena*, and in this iteration of the *Arena Cube* you'll find four rebalanced cards appearing in the lists:

*   A-Luminarch Aspirant
*   A-Alrund's Epiphany
*   A-Esika's Chariot
*   A-Cosmos Elixir

These cards include the "A-" prefix in their card names to identify them as *MTG Arena* rebalanced versions. You can see these cards and find out the reasoning behind their rebalancing by visiting Donald Smith's article [Alchemy Rebalancing Philosophy](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2021/12-02-alchemy-rebalancing-philosophy).

# Arena Cube Draft Event Details

## Best-of-One

**Dates**: January 6–20, 2021

**Entry**: 4,000 gold or 600 gems

**Structure**: 7 wins or 3 losses (whichever comes first)

## Best-of-Three (Traditional)

**Dates**: June 6–20, 2021

**Entry**: 4,000 gold or 600 gems

**Structure**: Three matches (regardless of wins/losses)
