The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on May 25, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by not acknowledging rumors either way. Which is our policy. You know, in case that was relevant.

# Today's Must

1.  [A Victory 14 Years in the Making](http://articles.mtgcardmarket.com/a-victory-14-years-in-the-making/) | MTG Card Market | Kevin Jones

This "wide-eyed little kid" (disclaimer: Kevin Jones is neither little nor actually a kid) recounts his experience growing up with *Magic* and just how much it means to him to have finally won a Grand Prix. The article is a heartfelt and touching look at what it means to finally get a trophy after living this game for so long.

Want more Kevin Jones? Who doesn't! StarCityGames.com [had an interview with him](http://www.starcitygames.com/article/35175_Interview-With-The-Editor-Kevin-Jones.html) today as well.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Brad Carpenter's Modern Abzan (Counters) Company](http://sales.starcitygames.com/deckdatabase/displaydeck.php?DeckID=113708)

As [Sam Black said in a (premium) article yesterday](http://www.starcitygames.com/article/35160_My-SCG-Baltimore-Recommendations.html), the "Counters" moniker probably won't stick as this becomes the de facto Abzan Company strategy going forward. And all thanks to a mid-level *Amonkhet* draft uncommon that doesn't even see Standard play. And it's thanks to this combination:

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Vizier+of+Remedies)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170525000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vizier+of+Remedies)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Devoted+Druid)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170525000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devoted+Druid)

Persist combos have long been a part of Modern. First [Kitchen Finks](https://web.archive.org/web/20170525000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kitchen+Finks)/[Murderous Redcap](https://web.archive.org/web/20170525000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Murderous+Redcap) and [Viscera Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170525000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viscera+Seer) combined with [Melira, Sylvok Outcast](https://web.archive.org/web/20170525000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Melira%2C+Sylvok+Outcast). Then [Anafenza, Kin-Tree Spirit](https://web.archive.org/web/20170525000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anafenza%2C+Kin-Tree+Spirit) arrived and replaced some number of Melira. But now they've both been overshadowed by the Vizier, who does the same thing those two do in the combo while also adding another infinite mana combo alongside it.

Vizier stops -1/-1 counters from being placed on creatures (well, one per effect, which is all we need). That means counters from creatures who persist, yes, but it also means [Devoted Druid](https://web.archive.org/web/20170525000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devoted+Druid) doesn't get a -1/-1 counter when it untaps itself. Which means it can tap and untap to its heart's content (it's devoted—it certainly has a heart, though I'm not up to date on my elvish biology).

With as much green mana as you'd like, you can do a few things. [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20170525000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company) and [Chord of Calling](https://web.archive.org/web/20170525000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chord+of+Calling) can search up [Duskwatch Recruiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20170525000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duskwatch+Recruiter), who can then find you every creature in your deck by activating its ability over and over again. Once you've drawn everything with legs, something like a [Walking Ballista](https://web.archive.org/web/20170525000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Walking+Ballista) can kill an opponent immediately.

The key part, and this has always been the attraction of these kinds of decks, from [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20170525000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) to Company, is that the deck has a reasonable backup plan of just attacking with effective, sometimes disrupting creatures. You don't *need* to combo to win. But it's nice to know you have the option.
