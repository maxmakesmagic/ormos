Here's a commercial for **Magic** from twelve years ago. We're not just showing it to you because it sells **Magic**, honest. It's funny, and we have something cool to show you about it when you're done watching it.

Okay. **Magic** Brand Director Elaine Chase was cleaning her office last week, and she ran across the kind of buried treasure that makes working here so much fun. So check this out: the original storyboards for this commercial!
