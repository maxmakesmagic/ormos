When [Claymore J. Flapdoodle](/en/articles/makingmagic/2004/02-05-free-association-35) first turned in the art for Elvish Impersonators, he chose to represent "young" Elvis. I made him change it to "old" Elvis. My rationale? Old Elvis was funnier.
