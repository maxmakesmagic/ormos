If you missed last week's announcement about Eternal Weekend, make sure to [read all about it here](https://magic.gg/news/eternal-weekend-2020-on-magic-online). ## Cube Spotlight: *Proliferate Cube*

Be the first to try out the latest addition to the Cube Spotlight Series: Emma Handy's *Proliferate Cube*! Emma's cube combines high-complexity synergies with an emphasis on combat math by using every kind of counter under the sun. There are even a few incidental combos thrown in for good measure. *Proliferate Cube* runs from October 14–21, and entry options are as follows:

*   Swiss League – 10 Event Tickets or 100 Play Points
*   Elimination Draft – 10 Event Tickets or 100 Play Points

Check out all the details and the full card list in [Emma's announcement article](https://web.archive.org/web/20201013000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/magic-online/cube-spotlight-series-emma-handys-proliferate-cube-2020-10-13).

## Limited Split Championship Qualifier Sunday

On Sunday, October 18, there will be a *Zendikar Rising* Sealed Split Championship Qualifier at 7 a.m. PT! The entry fee is 40 MOCS Qualifier Points. The winner qualifies for the *Zendikar Rising* Split Championship on *MTG Arena*.

Learn more about Split Championship Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020#ptq).

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format Challenges are the first step toward qualifying for Premier Play through your favorite *Magic* formats. To learn more about Premier Play qualification, click [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020).

*Magic Online*'s Format Challenges now run once on Saturdays and once on Sundays for each Constructed format! On weekends featuring Showcase Challenge events, the Format Challenge that starts at either 6 a.m., 8 a.m., or 10 a.m. Pacific time is pre-empted by the Showcase Challenge—Format Challenges scheduled at other times remain in place. *NOTE: The Pauper Challenge is never pre-empted.*

## Next Downtime November 18

The next downtime will be November 18, beginning at 9 a.m. and continuing until noon. This is for the release of *Commander Legends* and is a change from previous announcements. All times listed are Pacific time (UTC-7).

## Build Notes

*   [Robber of the Rich](https://web.archive.org/web/20201013000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Robber+of+the+Rich) no longer lets players play MDFCs exiled with its triggered ability as lands.
*   [Skyclave Shadowcat](https://web.archive.org/web/20201013000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skyclave+Shadowcat)'s triggered ability will now correctly see itself dying, as long as it has a +1/+1 counter when it dies.
*   [Akiri, Fearless Voyager](https://web.archive.org/web/20201013000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akiri%2C+Fearless+Voyager) can now correctly trigger multiple times per combat phases if its controller attacks multiple players.
*   Several cards that allow a player to reveal some cards from the top of their library and put one into their hand, notably including Tajuru Paragon, will now have a game log entry identifying the chosen card.
