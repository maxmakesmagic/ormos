*Future Sight's* Zoetic Cavern has a unique perspective among cards with morph. Although many morph creatures depict [the morph shell](/en/articles/arcana/2006/10-24-hidden-morph-spiders-2), Zoetic Cavern shows what it might be like on the *inside* of a morph crawler! When it's face down, it would look like just another 2/2 "morph spider," indistinguishable from an Exalted Angel or even a Whetwheel. Once you see it face-up, the morph shell has become a *location* instead of a creature—the hollowed-out shell, empty of life, forms an eerie, organic cave. Check out the full art below, in which you can see the organ-like structures of the cavern.
