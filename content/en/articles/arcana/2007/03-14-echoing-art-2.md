The *Darksteel* cycle of “echoing” spells each affect all permanents that share a name with their target. The “echoing” title, in this case, doesn’t relate to the *mechanic* echo (of Timbermare fame), but instead to a figurative repetition of the spell’s effect.

Greg Staples illustrated each of these spells, and each depicts beams of colored light falling down on its multiple targets. Perhaps you’ve felt similar beams of light emptying your board of Saprolings or blowing up all your Ancient Dens…

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9d/d8/9dd89751f317ad0b6c0703e1347309fb9ea72344e7f02d1daa2903ae251579b5.jpg)  
*Echoing Calm art by Greg Staples*

In [Echoing Calm](https://web.archive.org/web/20070314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Echoing+Calm), evil enchantments are being cleansed from three loxodons. Note how the two in the background have red-glowing eyes, indicating that the spell hasn’t eradicated their afflictions yet.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/92/1b/921b489fd0d3527222f9ad7341dac4da808d27a41c00333a51f82e32eabe04aa.jpg)  
*Echoing Truth art by Greg Staples*

[Echoing Truth](https://web.archive.org/web/20070314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Echoing+Truth) depicts a pair of dragons being washed away by waves from the Quicksilver Sea. (We’ve [seen before](https://web.archive.org/web/20070314000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/dragons-echoing-truth-2004-04-27) what type of dragons these are in the art.)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ba/26/ba26b6572c309e7eec4239d2236a624883698c8247c5b3cd62164ee348f98b22.jpg)  
*Echoing Decay art by Greg Staples*

In [Echoing Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20070314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Echoing+Decay) we see two leonin soldiers being wiped out by the force of the spell. In *Mirrodin* Block art, black magic is typically represented with pale green coloration, symbolizing the taint of necrogen gas.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f0/83/f083d3772f8d78588b03611f9a7d9ff6f2e0c06f869102bfa060e06c85c1ae83.jpg)  
*Echoing Ruin art by Greg Staples*

[Echoing Ruin](https://web.archive.org/web/20070314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Echoing+Ruin) shows a pair of artifacts getting targeted by a spell of rust and corrosion. These were war wagons constructed by goblins of the Oxidda Mountains.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/63/3e/633e54707b4b364fabd5d575af085f3fd6a8e685186463fe1af34a18bcc52262.jpg)  
*Echoing Courage art by Greg Staples*

[Echoing Courage](https://web.archive.org/web/20070314000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Echoing+Courage) depicts human warriors in the [Tangle](https://web.archive.org/web/20070314000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/mirrodin-environments-green-2003-11-19), Mirrodin’s metal forest, receiving the benefit of a spell of strength and growth.
