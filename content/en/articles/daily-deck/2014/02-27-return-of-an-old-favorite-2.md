Who doesn't love a good WU Control deck?

![Sun Titan](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Sun%20Titan&options=)It's not for everyone, especially when it's dominating a format. However, when WU Control is relatively tame compared to the other decks in a format, suddenly WU Control is the good guy. In Modern, that seems to be the case. In a format with [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant), [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf), [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan), and Kiki-Jiki, you're a hero if you're playing [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens).

(Re) Enter [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl).

Suddenly there's a good reason to play [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) again! [Kitchen Finks](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kitchen+Finks) also starts to look appealing. [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation)? No thanks, we've seen that before. How about [Sun Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sun+Titan) for a finisher instead? Maybe we could find room for a [Phantasmal Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phantasmal+Image) or two...

Another great reason to play WU Control in Modern is because white's sideboard options are phenomenal. [Rest in Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rest+in+Peace); [Stony Silence](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stony+Silence); [Kataki, War's Wage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kataki%2C+War%27s+Wage); [Aven Mindcensor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Mindcensor); [Linvala, Keeper of Silence](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Linvala%2C+Keeper+of+Silence); and [Mirran Crusader](https://web.archive.org/web/20140227000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirran+Crusader) are just some of the very hateful options you could play to even things up.
