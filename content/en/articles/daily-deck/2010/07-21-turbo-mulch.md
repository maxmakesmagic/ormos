Oath of Druids is one of the most powerful cards in the history of **Magic**—so powerful it is presently banned in Legacy. But it took future Wizards of the Coast R&D member Erik Lauer to discover the card's power. In the summer of 1998 he built this deck and gave it to his friend, and fellow future R&D member, Randy Buehler. Randy ripped off a perfect record with the deck at U.S. Nationals using the enchantment to fetch up Archangel, then using Mulch to fuel Scroll Rack draws and turning those extra cards into life with Gerrard's Wisdom. Gaea's Blessing made sure the Oath player couldn't be decked, and the rest is history!
