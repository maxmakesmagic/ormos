It's a flavor-fest today at **magicthegathering.com**. A flavor-stravaganza, if you will. Along with the debut of Matt Cavotta's flavor-focused column [Taste the Magic](/en/articles/savortheflavor/2005/08-24-the-flavor-of-magic), we present [Legendology](https://web.archive.org/web/20050824000000/http://www.wizards.com/legendology), a new area of Wizards.com dedicated to fiction set in the worlds of Wizards of the Coast games, including the many planes of **Magic: The Gathering**.

A second chapter of Scott McGough's **Magic** story "[Family Man](https://web.archive.org/web/20090601194020/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=legendology/magic/article1)" goes up today on Legendology.com. Drawn from the *The Secrets of Magic*, it chronicles the exploits of Virot Maglan, a mage not to be trifled with, much less threatened.
