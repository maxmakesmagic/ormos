Looking for someone to play? Eager for new challenges? **Magic: The Gathering**'s network of leagues and tournaments is just what you're searching for. From the casual venues of Friday Night Magic and Gateway to the best of the best on the Pro Tour, you're sure to find the right place to enjoy the best trading card game in the world.

[![Friday Night Magic](https://i.ormos.uk/97/43/9743337e50be87b84ce6538b444522c6ad68f3440630771dc0018a6bd11e240e.jpg "Friday Night Magic")](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6)

### [Friday Night Magic (FNM)](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6)

Friday Night Magic allows you to play tournament-level **Magic**, learn new strategies and win a variety of exclusive prize cards. FNM is the place to begin building DCI points and become a rated player. Click on the FNM image to the right to locate a store near you and find out why Fridays are the best night of the week. 

### [Gateway](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/tournaments-events-trading-card-game-magic-the-gathering) (Europe only)

Gateway is the ideal way to meet new friends and improve your game in a friendly environment at your local games shop. The nice thing is, the more you play, the more points you get, and the more **Magic: The Gathering** promo cards you can earn.

### [Prereleases](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-prereleases-3)

Can't wait for the newest set of **Magic** to be released? Head to your nearest Prerelease and play with the new cards before you can buy them -- and receive a special commemorative foil card. If you're looking to start your career in sanctioned **Magic** events in a relaxed, low-key environment, there isn't a easier or more fun way than playing at a Prerelease. Formats include Sealed Deck, Drafts, Team, and Two-Headed Giant.

### [Launch Parties](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-launch-parties-4)

Launch Parties are a perfect way to get an introduction to new **Magic** cards as soon as they become available to buy, all while meeting other players in your region at a local retail store. Participants receive a special commemorative foil card and can win prizes such as a rare Life Counter. Formats include Sealed Deck and Booster Drafts.

### [City Championships](https://web.archive.org/web/20080913053413/http://ww2.wizards.com/magic/citychamps/Default.aspx)

Take your first step down the Road to Worlds by participating in your City Championships. Multiple tournaments during the season feed the City Finals, which brings together the top players from all across your region for a final showdown for city bragging rights. Top prizes include invitations to Nationals and byes at Regionals. 

### [Regionals](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/regional-championships-2008)

If you dream of playing for a national or world championship, you'll want to start here. Your country's regional championships offer prizes and multiple invitations to your country's National Championships. Check the schedule for your area to find a Regionals near you! 

### [National Championships](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2011-national-championships-2)

Ever wonder what being No. 1 feels like? Find information about your national tournaments on the National Championships page and get ready to become your country's national champ. 

[![Qualifiers](http://www.wizards.com/protour/images/qt_lg.jpg "Qualifiers")](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-pro-tour-qualifiers-2)

### [Qualifier Tournaments](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-pro-tour-qualifiers-2)

Qualifier tournaments are where the game really gets serious. The excitement level is high and the stakes are even higher. Players the world over converge with a common goal in mind -- an invitation to the Pro Tour, which comes with an airplane ticket to the next Pro Tour stop. 

### [Grand Prix](/en/events/2002/welcome/grand-prix)

Grand Prix are easily the biggest open events the DCI offers. They're high-energy and held around the world. Plus, no invitation is necessary -- anyone can attend. No pro points? No problem. Playing a good game is all that matters, and with Pro Tour Invitations down to 16th place and cash prizes all the way down to 64th place, one thing is certain: a Grand Prix is sure-fire great time. Look for one near you. 

[![Pro Tour](http://www.wizards.com/protour/images/pt_lg.gif "Pro Tour")](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour)

### [Pro Tour](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour)

This is where legends are born. Four annual worldwide stops make up the Tour, with each event showcasing a prize pool of more than $230,000. Travel the world, face off against the best players the game has to offer, and enjoy the spoils available to those living the life of a professional **Magic** player. Along with the main event, Pro Tours are loaded with tournaments and events open to the public that turn the hall into a **Magic** convention when the Pro Tour comes to your town.

### [World Championships](/en/articles/event-coverage/2011/10-10-magic-weekend-san-francisco-and-2011-worlds-event-information)

The World Championships is the culmination of the **Magic** year. Each year, players from more than 50 countries come together for the pinnacle of international **Magic** competition. National Champions, Pro Tour winners, and the highest-ranked DCI players square off to earn the coveted title of "World Champion." Plus Worlds features the best public events of the season and a slew of attractions like artist signings, fan activities, trading, and much more for every type of **Magic** player.
