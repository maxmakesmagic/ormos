The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on October 4, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by [Admiral Beckett Brass](https://web.archive.org/web/20171004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Admiral+Beckett+Brass). Because she made me say so.

# Today's Must

1.  [Commander VS: *Ixalan* Season Kickoff!](http://www.starcitygames.com/article/35949_Commander-VS-Ixalan-Season-Kickoff.html) | StarCityGames.com | Justin Parnell and Stephen Green

It's the first episode of a new season of Commander VS, and, as they so often do, the crew is kicking things off with the legends of the latest set. It's [Gishath, Sun's Avatar](https://web.archive.org/web/20171004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gishath%2C+Sun%27s+Avatar) vs. [Tishana, Voice of Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20171004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tishana%2C+Voice+of+Thunder) vs. [Admiral Beckett Brass](https://web.archive.org/web/20171004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Admiral+Beckett+Brass) vs. [Vona, Butcher of Magan](https://web.archive.org/web/20171004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vona%2C+Butcher+of+Magan)—and each deck is tribal themed. Watch to see who comes out on top—and it better be Tishana or Admiral Brass.

# After That, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [Preparation, Expectation, and Reconciliation: Lucas Berthoud's Journey to Worlds](https://mtg.one/preparations-expectations-and-reconciliation/) | MTG.one | Lucas Berthoud

The [*Magic* World Championship](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2017-magic-world-championship-event-information) is *this weekend*. I know, right?! I'll be there watching Berthoud and 23 others vie for the title of World Champion and $100,000. Berthoud goes way back—like 1996 way back—to talk about his journey from someone who just loved coverage to someone on *Magic*'s biggest stage.

1.  [The Right Way to Netdeck](https://collected.company/2017/10/03/right-way-netdeck-181/) | The Command Zone | Josh Lee Kwai and Jimmy Wong

Look, we all do it. There's a wealth of information out there, it's hard not to look at at least some of it. Like when you think you've got a sweet [Nekusar, the Mindrazer](https://web.archive.org/web/20171004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nekusar%2C+the+Mindrazer) deck, but you want to check to see if you missed something. Or you really want to build around [Mairsil, the Pretender](https://web.archive.org/web/20171004000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mairsil%2C+the+Pretender), but aren't sure where to start. It's not a bad thing—and Josh and Jimmy show you how to do it right in Commander.

1.  [4 Lessons from GP Providence](https://www.channelfireball.com/articles/4-lessons-from-gp-providence/) | ChannelFireball | Riley Knight

What did we learn from Grand Prix Providence? Besides the fact that [Rich Hagon puns](https://www.reddit.com/r/magicTCG/comments/73nk6r/i_got_to_meet_mr_chair_himself_today/) are a national treasure, I mean.

1.  [Commander Review: *Ixalan* Part 2 (Red, Green, Multicolor, Colorless, Lands)](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/articles/commander-review-ixalan-part-2-red-green-multicolor-colorless-lands?src=wizards) | MTGGoldfish | Tomer Abramovici

There was a part 1, so clearly there had to be a part 2. This is that part 2. It's long, it's thorough, and it's worth your time.

1.  [Vampire Pirates Abound in This Gorgeous New *Magic: The Gathering* Art](https://io9.gizmodo.com/vampire-pirates-abound-in-this-gorgeous-new-magic-the-1819110033?rev=1507061791885) | io9 | James Whitbrook

In which io9 calls *Ixalan* "a beautiful mess of the internet's favorite things, blended together and pressed into a set of cards." I mean, all we really need are cats and memes and this is pretty spot on.

1.  [Voice](https://www.hipstersofthecoast.com/2017/10/dear-azami-voice-mtg-story/) | Hipsters of the Coast | Levi Byrne

Byrne uses a recent *Magic* Story as a jumping-off point to discuss the concept of voice in *Magic* Story—and comes away feeling good about how the characters are differentiated and fleshed out in *Ixalan*.
