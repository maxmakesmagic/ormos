The 1999 World Championships were a big year for **Magic**'s marquee tournament. The legendary Kai Budde won the event, but it is Jamie Parke's Sped Red deck we're talking about today. Using suppressive fire of a land destruction suite, Jamie kept his opponents off balance long enough to steal games with his powerful red cards. It was not the last time Jamie would taste the Worlds Top 8 stage, playing again on Sunday nearly one decade later. Coverage of the 2010 World Championships in Chiba, Japan begins this weekend, on [this very website](/en/articles/event-coverage/2011/10-10-magic-weekend-san-francisco-and-2011-worlds-event-information). Read more about Jamie's run in Tokyo ten years ago [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20101208000000/http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/article.asp?x=WORLDS99/welcome).
