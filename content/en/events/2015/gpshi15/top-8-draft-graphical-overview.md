The picture below described the draft seating, the direction in which the first and third packs were passed, and the colors that all players drafted.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/Top-8-draft-picture.jpg)

Although Akito Shinoda’s deck is listed as 5-color, in reality it was more of a base-Jeskai deck splashing for the two other colors. As the only Jeskai drafter at the table, he was able to snag up all of the good Jeskai rares, from [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider) to [Flying Crane Technique](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flying+Crane+Technique). As it turns out, finding the underdrafted color is a great strategy for winning a Grand Prix!
