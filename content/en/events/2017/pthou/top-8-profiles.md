Learn more about Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation* Top 8 competitors—including who they are, what they’ve done in the game, and more—by taking a look through their profiles!

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/pthou/t8_sasabe.jpg)Name:** Yusuke Sasabe

**Age:** 27

**Hometown:** Tokyo

**Team:** Dora-Kichi (which means “Draft Junky”)

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** GP Shizuoka 2017 Top 8

**Limited Record:** 5-1

**Standard Record:** 7-3

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?**

1.  Black-Green 3-0
2.  White-Black 2-1

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?**

Mono-Black Zombies with Kalitas in the main deck. It's pretty good against Mono Red \[Ramunap Red\].

**What card was your MVP for the weekend?**

[Kalitas, Traitor of Ghet](https://web.archive.org/web/20170729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kalitas%2C+Traitor+of+Ghet)

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?**

My Dream.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/pthou/t8_kurata.jpg)Name:** Shintaro Kurata

**Age:** 27

**Hometown:** London/Tokyo

**Team:** Waseda MTG

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Grand Prix Kobe 11th, BMI 8th

**Limited Record:** 4-2

**Standard Record:** 8-1-1

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?**

1st Green-white-red ([Overcome](https://web.archive.org/web/20170729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overcome) deck)

2nd Green-White

I liked the second one.

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?**

Mono Red (and Black), because of [Collective Brutality](https://web.archive.org/web/20170729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collective+Brutality) and [Ammit Eternal](https://web.archive.org/web/20170729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ammit+Eternal). They are strong against mono red. Other decks I made couldn’t beat mono red.

**What card was your MVP for the weekend?**

[Ammit Eternal](https://web.archive.org/web/20170729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ammit+Eternal)

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?**

FUN. Let’s go to the PT with me, friend!

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/pthou/t8_yam.jpg)Name:** Yam Wing Chun

**Age:** 25

**Hometown:** Hong Kong

**Team:** No team (but testing with MTG Mint Card and MTG Bent Card Squad)

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** PT Fate Reforged Top 16, 2 GP Top 8

**Limited Record:** 4-2

**Standard Record:** 9-1

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?**

1.  White-Black Zombies
2.  Blue-Black Aggro

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?**

Ramunap Red. Jason Chung was winning with it a lot on *Magic Online*. After going to the arcade with him the night before the Pro Tour, I was convinced to play his 75.

**What card was your MVP for the weekend?**

[Hazoret the Fervent](https://web.archive.org/web/20170729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hazoret+the+Fervent). It won me so many matches.

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?**

A dream come true. Also, I would get to play in the World Championship with the squad (Lee Shi Tian, Eduardo Sagjalik, and Kelvin Chew).
