## Grand Prix - Bochum 2010

![](https://i.ormos.uk/12/2e/122ea5538298ae7b28d2cab9d84a8f7aae294ce204ce635b4d8ea8e9ac31144a.jpg)

It's a spectacular two Grand Prix titles in six weeks for Czech star Martin Juza, whose Grand Prix Portland victory is still fresh in the memory.

Martin "I don't feel like drafting poison" Juza's magnificent Infect deck swept through the Top8 without losing a game, a pair of [Hand of the Praetors](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hand+of+the+Praetors) sending it over the top and ensuring that he didn't lose a single game as he closed in on victory. Yves Sele provided his greatest test in the final, coming close to actually milling Juza out with his Screeching Silclaw before the Phyrexian waves finally overwhelmed the Mirran defenses and the Swiss player was swept away.

From a long way out Juza had looked like a man marked for the win: locked onto his second trophy like a cruise missile through Sunday. A Top-8 packed with players who had already proven themselves adept at Scars of Mirrodin drafting found themselves helpless to prevent Juza hovering up all the Infect cards on the table and seizing the crown.

And that marks the end of a tremendous Grand Prix here in Bochum, which featured not only some terrific Grand Prix action but also a 512 player PTQ on Sunday that could well be the biggest PTQ ever held! From Bochum the Grand Prix train moves on to Nashville before steaming back to Europe for a stop in Florence at the end of November. Join us in there as the Pro season comes to a head - you won't regret it and we'll be glad to see you!

## Top 8 – Packs by Picks

*by David Sutcliffe*

We've all been in drafts where we see a good card swing the whole way around the table to us at seventh pick, and the temptation is always there to think "What the hell are you guys doing - this card is awesome! How can it still be in the pack!?!". We might also make a quick mental calculation that at least four of the other players on the table are idiots for allowing this card to get to you.

The truth, though, is often the exact opposite. The players on your table AREN'T idiots, and it's because they're not idiots that they left that card for you. Not because they think it's bad, but because they knew that their decks needed other cards more. The best way to show you this is to follow a booster pack around a table of good players… and it just so happens that we have a table of good players here in the Top8 of Grand Prix Bochum. These are players who have already proven their drafting skills twice today, and we'll leave it to them to justify why there was a 7th pick [Myrsmith](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myrsmith), or how [Darksteel Sentinel](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Sentinel) can be picked at 9th in one booster and 3rd in another.

Let's pause it there. In the first pack Kunzler had passed the [Darksteel Sentinel](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Sentinel) to take [Sky-Eel School](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sky-Eel+School) and yet here he was taking the [Sentinel](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sentinel) ahead of [Lumengrid Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lumengrid+Drake). The implication was obvious - Kunzler needed artifacts more than he needed yet another flyer. The reverse was true for Lee, though. He had taken the [Silver Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silver+Myr) on the first pass, and felt more comfortable taking the Metalcraft Drake than Kunzler this time around. It was a clear example of how a card's value for a player changes as the draft develops, and why 'pick orders' often don't make much sense when applied to a real draft.

Martin Juza - Juza's 7th pick Hand of Praetors was actually his second Hand! He had an Infect deck that was light on two-drops but packed enough Proliferate in the form of two [Contagion Clasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Contagion+Clasp) and [Throne of Geth](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Throne+of+Geth) to mean he could poison an opponent without actually needing to get his creatures across the table very often.

Yves Sele - his Blue/White deck looked solid but uninspirational.

Geertjan Woltjes - had a fighting Black/Red deck. It was light on removal but brought plenty of aggressive creatures, anybody who stumbled against Woltjes would be in trouble.

Julien Perez - had battled it out with Woltjes for the red cards, but paired it with Green metalcraft.

Sok-Yong Lee - Lee's deck looked crazily good. Lee's biggest problem was that he would face Juza's poison deck in the quarter finals.

Matthias Kunzler - trapped next to Lee, Kunzler had been forced to abandon the white flyers but picked up black as he had a clear run on that color. [Necrogen Scudder](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Necrogen+Scudder) and [Necrotic Ooze](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Necrotic+Ooze) supported blue flyers, but the legacy of beginning in white was his three-color mana base for a pair of [Arrest](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrest)s.

Manuel Mayer - did not seem happy, but had somehow wound up with a pair of [Genesis Wave](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genesis+Wave)s! He had been picking up all the green that Perez had left for him several picks earlier, and had yet another ground pounding heavy-hitting green deck.

  

## Quarter Final – Matthias Künzler (Switzerland) v Yves Sele (Switzerland)

*by Rich Hagon*

Swiss watch.

Thought I'd get the obvious one in early.

Sele got things underway with [Plains](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plains), opposite an [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island) and [Nihil Spellbomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nihil+Spellbomb) for Kunzler. ## Quarterfinals – Sok-Yong Lee vs Martin Juza

*by Tim Willoughby*

This matchup saw top Czech pro Martin Juza facing down against German local Sok-Yong Lee. Each had had a pretty reasonable draft, with some explosive potential from their decks.

Even [Plated Seastrider](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plated+Seastrider), normally an all-star against infect decks, did not look good enough.

Sele traded his replica for the now 3/3 [Contagious Nim](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Contagious+Nim), and took two poison from [Ichorclaw Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichorclaw+Myr).

Sele cast a [Neurok Invisimancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Neurok+Invisimancer), and a [Gold Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gold+Myr) before swinging with [Glint Hawk Idol](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint+Hawk+Idol) and passing. The Invisimancer died to [Instill Infection](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Instill+Infection), which allowed Juza to swing with his [Ichorclaw Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichorclaw+Myr) and [Hand of the Praetors](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hand+of+the+Praetors). [Gold Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gold+Myr) chump blocked the infect lord, and Sele was up to 5 poison. Halfway there.

Sele cycled a [Twisted Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Twisted+Image), looking for an answer, but then passed the turn with five mana up. Juza cast an [Ichor Rats](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichor+Rats), giving Sele two poison counters (one thanks to his lord) and attacked. He then cast a [Throne of Geth](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Throne+of+Geth), which prompted the concession.

Martin Juza 1 - 0 Yves Sele

All in all, the shuffling had taken longer than the game for game one, and Sele probably spent as long again sideboarding. He knew he was facing a monster, and had to do whatever he could to fight it.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8e/8e/8e8ece67781d584009c149e9ea7def2a10bc4ecbfc72c73d2ef4daf75aed4d27.jpg)*Juza surveys his finals victim.*

There is only so much that you can do against monsters on [Hallow](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallow)een though. That is when monsters are their most powerful. When asked what he would dress up as for [Hallow](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallow)een if it were a **Magic** card, Sele had said [Hand of the Praetors](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hand+of+the+Praetors). As it was it was the Hand in Juza's deck that was putting him on.

Both players kept in game two, and this time Sele had a turn one play, in [Origin Spellbomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Origin+Spellbomb). Juza's first turn brought a [Sylvok Lifestaff](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvok+Lifestaff). There was nothing but a spellbomb activation for Sele, who looked on as [Ichorclaw Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichorclaw+Myr) joined Juza's board. Juza's myr disappeared thanks to a [Revoke Existence](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Revoke+Existence) from Sele, who had caught a break inasmuch as Juza had not yet drawn a third land. Sele played a [Palladium Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Palladium+Myr) and passed after a small attack.

When Juza did hit a third [Swamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swamp), it brought with it a [Contagious Nim](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Contagious+Nim). Sele had made it to turn four without being poisoned, and when Juza elected to trade his Nim for [Palladium Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Palladium+Myr), it looked like he might make it even longer. Sele dropped [Auriok Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auriok+Replica) and [Screeching Silcaw](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Screeching+Silcaw) onto his side of the board to make up for the loss of his myr. Juza added [Contagious Nim](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Contagious+Nim) to his.

With a flyer, Sele was able to plink in for a little damage, but Juza seemed unconcerned. He watched as an [Ichorclaw Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichorclaw+Myr) came for his opponent (after combat, such that there was no metalcraft). He cast an [Infiltration Lens](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Infiltration+Lens), double equipped his Nim, and ran in. [Infiltration Lens](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Infiltration+Lens) meant that Sele was much less inclined to block, taking his first three points of poison of the game.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/13/18/13183c26ff004c5d71819b50630bffc59206846662405cf8669cf3ae428fef0d.jpg)*All black borders all the time.*

The swing back milled Juza for four, and dealt him four. The cards that hit the bin were two copies of [Hand of the Praetors](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hand+of+the+Praetors), [Contagion Clasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Contagion+Clasp) and [Cystbearer](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cystbearer). Given that only one of them would have been castable for Juza, he didn't seem to mind. Sele added a [Neurok Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Neurok+Replica) to his board and passed. With the replica and [Ichorclaw Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichorclaw+Myr) to block with, and mana up for replica activations if necessary, he didn't seem in bad shape.

Juza drew for his turn and swung in. Juza was on 13, while Sele had 3 poison. It was tricky to work out how the race was going, and Sele thought long and hard before blocks. Eventually he settled on a double block, which let Juza draw a whopping four cards. Now Juza had some thing to do, as he suddenly had a lot more options for his turn. He elected to put 3 counters on [Neurok Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Neurok+Replica), and letting his Nim die, gaining him 3 from [Sylvok Lifestaff](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvok+Lifestaff). He then used [Throne of Geth](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Throne+of+Geth) to ditch his [Infiltration Lens](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Infiltration+Lens) and finish off the replica while there wasn't a good target for Sele to bounce, and give Sele another poison counter, thanks to proliferate.

After a little thought, Juza cast a [Corpse Cur](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Corpse+Cur) post combat to fetch back [Hand of the Praetors](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hand+of+the+Praetors). At the end of turn he discarded due to his suddenly ample grip. A [Blackcleave Goblin](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blackcleave+Goblin) was deemed the worst card, and hit the grumper to take [Hand of the Praetors](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hand+of+the+Praetors) place. A reasonable trade.

Sele cast a [Golem Artisan](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golem+Artisan) for his turn and attacked with [Auriok Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auriok+Replica), [Screeching Silcaw](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Screeching+Silcaw) and his 1/1 Myr token. He this time milled four lands from Juza's deck, In the face of the possible pump from Sele, Juza blocked [Auriok Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auriok+Replica) for the trade, and took three as [Golem Artisan](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golem+Artisan) pumped the 1/1 Myr token to 2/2.

Juza was on 13, and had 13 cards left in his deck. That Silcaw was suddenly not looking too silly, and [Golem Artisan](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golem+Artisan) threatened quite a bit of damage the following turn from Sele, who had seven lands in play, and no cards in hand. Juza cast a [Contagion Clasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Contagion+Clasp) and used it to make [Golem Artisan](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golem+Artisan) a 2/2, before casting a [Plague Stinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plague+Stinger), and equipped his Lifestaff to it. Now Sele would have to think very hard about attacking in the air, as once his Golem succumbed, he would only have that Silcaw to defend the air.

Sele cast an [Auriok Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auriok+Replica) and pondered attacking. He eventually went in with [Auriok Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auriok+Replica), the Silcaw and his Myr. Juza blocked the Silcaw, stopping any milling for the turn, but taking a little damage from the other members of Sele's attack squad. He was on 12, thanks mainly to lifegain from [Sylvok Lifestaff](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvok+Lifestaff), which he finally sacrificed to [Throne of Geth](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Throne+of+Geth) to get [Golem Artisan](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golem+Artisan) down to 1/1 in size, and Sele up to five poison.

Juza cast [Instill Infection](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Instill+Infection) on [Auriok Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auriok+Replica), then cast a [Darksteel Axe](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Axe) and sacrificed it to [Throne of Geth](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Throne+of+Geth), to leave Sele on 6 poison, with just a Myr token and [Ichorclaw Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichorclaw+Myr) as his board.

From here it was up to Juza to finish things off. He cast [Hand of the Praetors](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hand+of+the+Praetors) and [Contagious Nim](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Contagious+Nim), to put another counter on Sele. That's seven.

A [Fume Spitter](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130163817/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fume+Spitter) to clear the path made the attack easily enough to finish the game. Sele extended his hand, and Martin Juza became Grand Prix Bochum champion!
