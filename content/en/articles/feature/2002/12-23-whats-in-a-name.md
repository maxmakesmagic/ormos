### Submit Your Ideas For CF1

* * *

So there's been a lot of talk on the Message Boards about the new format in the **Casual Play** rooms. Casual Format 1 is familiar to many **Magic** players who are used to a different name.

### The Basics

Casual Format 1 is a Constructed format where you may only have one of any card (except for basic land) in your deck and sideboard. You may use any cards available in the Extended format to build your deck.

### The Name

**Magic** players have a skill for finding correlations between the game and pop culture references. For example, Prodigal Sorcerer is often called "Tim" after a character in *Monty Python and the Holy Grail.* Hence, players refer to a format where "there can be only one" by the title of a movie.

While a great deal of time and effort went into delivering the name "Casual Format 1," we understand that players might like a somewhat more...original name for it. So, we're asking you, the ***Magic** Online* community, to give us your ideas for the final name of Casual Format 1.

Send your suggestions to Casualformat1@wizards.com. We'll collect the names, run them up the flagpole, and then have you vote on the most popular submissions.
