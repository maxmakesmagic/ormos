After 15 rounds, with a total of 1,236 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

* * *

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpsto16/t8_studer.jpg)  
 Name:** Yannick Studer

**Age:** 22

**Occupation:** Student at the Hotel Management School in Lucerne

**Hometown:** Bern, Switzerland

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

18th at Grand Prix Liverpool 2015.

**What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

[Odric, Lunarch Marshal](https://web.archive.org/web/20160731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Odric%2C+Lunarch+Marshal). I was playing White-Blue Creatures. I could give my team vigilance, first strike, lifelink, and flying. 8-1.

**What was the best card in your first draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

I was playing White-Blue Spirits. It was awesome. Best card [Nebelgast Herald](https://web.archive.org/web/20160731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nebelgast+Herald). It tapped almost fifteen creatures in six games. 3-0.

**What was the best card in your second draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

I was playing green-white. Best card probably was the [Angelic Purge](https://web.archive.org/web/20160731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angelic+Purge). 1-1-1.
