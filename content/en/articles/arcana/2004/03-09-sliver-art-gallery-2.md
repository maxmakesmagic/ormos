Y[esterday](/en/articles/arcana/2004/03-08-sliver-art-gallery) we looked at the art of *Stronghold's* cycle of multicolored Slivers. Get set for more: today we look at some of *Tempest's* adaptable nasties.

*Tempest* has two cycles of Slivers: a common cycle of 1/1 Slivers for converted mana cost of ![2 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/f9/cc/f9cc8a15b009997919d58ad79964384351495d1b6c9231f938316577453038a8.gif) (plus Metallic Sliver for ![1 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/4c/6b4cf558eab279fb9414f146ebdd0e0705ec92984fec530597dd2a3bce43ce24.gif)), and an uncommon cycle of 2/2 Slivers for ![3 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/7f/5d/7f5d2f1e55eaa9d19a9a23027b63bfbb5b39dbf8642160dda1a477a03df23a6a.gif). Today we present the art for the 2/2 uncommons. Enjoy.
