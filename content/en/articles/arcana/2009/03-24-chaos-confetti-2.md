Back when Tom Jenkot did [his dossier](https://web.archive.org/web/20090324000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/magic-dossier-tom-jenkot-2009-03-03), he included a clever image of a sealed, PSA-graded copy of [Chaos Confetti](https://web.archive.org/web/20090324000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chaos+Confetti):

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e7/14/e714d0ad2192a57e1c82c74453f0e4b13f39e2458fea36a4ef5782d08e70b437.jpg)  

What you probably didn't realize is that although some visual trickery was involved, that was a *real* PSA-graded [Chaos Confetti](https://web.archive.org/web/20090324000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chaos+Confetti)! Tom's a big fan of the card, and also has a Mark-Tedin-customized "goth" variant:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8e/09/8e091c78c7cdd99a97ab7649b042492616ac0693d1d8f6ea8ad8570c1ac06042.jpg)
