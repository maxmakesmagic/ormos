Can we now pay nothing to use &quot;bestow&quot; as long as the enchantment creature has a converted mana cost less than 4? —Kieran </b></td>&#10;						<td></td>&#10;						<td><img alt="" src="http://archive.wizards.com/magic/images/magicboxicon_nocolor.jpg"></td>&#10;					</tr><tr><td colspan="3">&#10;						<table><tbody><tr><td>&#10;									<p><b>A: Matt Tabak, Magic rules manager: </b></p>&#10;&#10;									<p>Greetings, citizen. Many spells in <b>Magic</b> have abilities that let you pay what's known as an alternative cost. That is, instead of paying a spell's mana cost, you pay some other cost, often with a bonus for doing so. Bestow is one such ability. Any effect that says you can cast a spell without paying its mana cost is also an alternative cost. Unfortunately, you can use a maximum of one alternative cost for each spell you cast.
