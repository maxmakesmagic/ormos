### The Hosts:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6f/bf/6fbf9271bf8d73ebeeda85a2d5e097b7105cb97d64942bfdd5aac57a4105d4c9.jpg)

Patrick "Trick" Jarrett is the Editor-in-Chief of DailyMTG.com. Born in Atlanta, GA, he moved his wife and their two dogs to Seattle to work for Wizards of the Coast. He's a big fan of the Commander format and loves his [Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20131119000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kiki-Jiki%2C+Mirror+Breaker) mono-red Commander deck. Follow him on Twitter: [@TrickMTG](https://twitter.com/TrickMTG).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ae/f8/aef82ea9cd2822a8265fdc4654d4715166da0ae6337255cc6831717c2b5635d3.jpg)

Jennifer "Tifa" Robles is the Assistant Brand Manager for **Magic: The Gathering** as well as the founder and organizer for [The Lady Planeswalker Society](http://www.facebook.com/pages/Lady-Planeswalkers-Society/206021052816060), a welcoming and friendly environment for women **Magic** players of all skill levels. Jennifer has been an avid gamer since birth, and **Magic** became her life source during ***Magic** 2011* . Follow her on Twitter: [@TifaMeyen](https://twitter.com/TifaMeyen) and [@MTGLadySociety](https://twitter.com/MTGLadySociety).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/90/32/903225d1bb2eadd9dc8297cfafdc8e0bd5b211edb9f0e03bc76329611e52575d.jpg)

When Gavin Verhey was eleven, he wanted a job making **Magic** cards. Ten years later, his dream was realized as his combined success as a professional player, deck builder, and writer brought him into Wizards R&D during 2011. He's been writing **Magic** articles since 2005 and has no plans to stop. Follow Gavin on Twitter: [@gavinverhey](https://twitter.com/gavinverhey).
