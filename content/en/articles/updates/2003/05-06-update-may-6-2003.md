### New Privacy Setting

* * *

The May 6, 2003 update included one feature that will hopefully eliminate the trade spamming problem some players were experiencing.

If you go to the **Privacy** tab on your **Settings** window, you'll see a new option at the bottom of the page: **Ignore all chat requests except from users on my buddy list**. If you select this option, players who aren't on your **Buddy list** won't be able to start private chats with you, send messages to you, challenge you, or attempt to begin a trade with you.

If you don't want to block all players all the time, but still want to use this while playing in events, just make sure you select this option before you begin the event.

We understand this may not be a perfect fix, but we felt it was important enough to remedy this problem as soon as possible.
