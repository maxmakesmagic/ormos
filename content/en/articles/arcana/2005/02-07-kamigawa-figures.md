Wizards of the Coast is teaming up with [First 4 Figures](http://www.first4figures.com/magic.htm) to bring you high-quality, collectible **Magic: The Gathering** figurines based on creatures from the world of Kamigawa. 

Here's a behind-the-scenes look at the goods. You'll recognize **[Kodama of the North Tree](https://web.archive.org/web/20050207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kodama+of+the+North+Tree)** and **[Ink-Eyes, Servant of Oni](https://web.archive.org/web/20050207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ink-Eyes%2C+Servant+of+Oni)**. 

  
*[Kodama of the North Tree](https://web.archive.org/web/20050207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kodama+of+the+North+Tree)* 

  
*[Ink-Eyes, Servant of Oni](https://web.archive.org/web/20050207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ink-Eyes%2C+Servant+of+Oni)* 

Head to the [First 4 Figures website](http://www.first4figures.com/magic.htm) to see the other three creatures that will be available. Stay tuned to **magicthegathering.com** in the coming weeks for release date and other product information.
