There is another Deck Pack for [**Duels of the Planeswalkers** 2013](https://web.archive.org/web/20130109000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx)! Pack One had [Boros and Simic](https://web.archive.org/web/20130109000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/duels-2013s-deck-pack-2012-11-07), and Pack Two had [Orzhov and Gruul](https://web.archive.org/web/20130109000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/duels-planeswalkers-2013-deck-pack-2-2012-12-05). The new Deck Pack has Dimir and a five-color deck. And the five-color deck has [Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20130109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Progenitus)!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ca/34/ca34e885bcdba4352bedaf11073dc33989e601b050eaa27c3552e3c408aa74d2.jpg)  

### Rogues' Gallery

Manipulate and infiltrate with the blue and black "Rogues’ Gallery" deck. Disrupt your enemies’ plans with spells, then summon stealthy mages and shadowy undead to deliver the killing blows.
