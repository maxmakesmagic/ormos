M**agic: The Gathering** comes to PAX East in full force March 26-28. With the **Magic Online** Live Series, Super Friday Night **Magic**, a Grand Melee, a packed schedule of public events, and deck building seminars with Mike Turian, there's something for every **Magic** player.

Held in Boston, Massachusetts, PAX East is a three-day game festival for tabletop, videogame, and PC gamers.

The 2010 **Magic Online** Live Series features a $5,000 cash prize pool. *Urza's Saga* Booster Draft qualifiers run all weekend, with the 8-person *Urza's Saga* Booster Draft final held Saturday night. In addition, all players who create a **Magic Online** account or fill out a survey on site will receive a foil Crystalline Sliver promo card and the opportunity to play in a free **Magic Online** draft! For a full rundown of **Magic Online** events, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20100303000000/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=mtg/daily/events/paxeast-mtgo).

Super Friday Night **Magic** highlights the three-day public event schedule. Starting at 6 p.m. on Friday, this *Zendikar* Block Sealed Deck event will show why Friday night is the best night of the week. All participants receive one Ancient Ziggurat, the March 2010 FNM promo card, with top finishers receiving their choice of other promo cards plus *Worldwake* booster packs.

![](http://www.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/paxeast10_promos.jpg) 

Get your Standard deck ready for Saturday's Grand Melee. Starting at 2 p.m., this huge event pits everyone against each other in one giant game. Attack to your left, defend to your right, and win one *Worldwake* booster pack for each player you eliminate. The last person standing takes home an 8gb iPod Touch!

There are tons of other public events available all weekend long—with *Zendikar* Sealed Deck, Minimaster, Standard, Booster Draft, and EDH events firing all the time, there's sure to be an event for you. Prizes include xBox 360 Elites, iPods, complete foil sets of *Worldwake*, plus other **Magic** products. For a full schedule of public events, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20100303000000/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=mtg/daily/events/paxeast-magic).

Whether you are new to **Magic** or just want to learn some tips and tricks, be sure to come to the Deck Building 101 seminars and discover the ins and outs of deck building from Wizards of the Coast R&D. These seminars will be hosted by Mike Turian, Advanced Developer and **Magic** Pro Tour Hall of Famer, and will be held in Room 208 on Friday (4:30 – 5:30 p.m.), Saturday (2:30 – 3:30 p.m.), and Sunday (12:30 – 1:30 p.m.).

If you're in the mood for **Magic**, get yourself to Boston and be a part of PAX East.
