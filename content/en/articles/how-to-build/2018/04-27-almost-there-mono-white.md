***Dominaria*** **Draft Weekend Starts Tomorrow!**

It's finally time for *Magic* to return home. *Dominaria* Draft events will begin firing tomorrow, April 28, so find a [store near you](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/http://locator.wizards.com/) and get ready to explore *Magic* history!

One of the things that *Dominaria* has brought us is the introduction of Knights as a competitive tribe. There isn't a Knight [lord](/en/articles/makingmagic/2017/08-07-cats#lord) like there are Merfolk or Vampire lords, but there is a lord who happens to be a Knight, and also a Saga that both creates and pumps Knights. Both those cards require heavy white commitment, so lists have been leaning toward mono-white or close to it.

Here are three almost-mono-white lists that recently went 5-0 in Standard Leagues:

This is the most Knights-focused of the lists, playing the full four [Dauntless Bodyguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dauntless+Bodyguard)s, [Benalish Marshal](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Benalish+Marshal)s, and [Knight of Grace](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+Grace)s. Let's take a look at the new cards.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=History+of+Benalia)](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=History+of+Benalia)

[History of Benalia](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=History+of+Benalia) is a powerhouse. It's a payoff for having Knights that also fuels itself, unlike cards such as [Pride of Conquerors](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pride+of+Conquerors) or [Trial of Solidarity](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trial+of+Solidarity). Even if you have no other Knights, it creates two 2/2s and attacks for 8 on turn five. The best part is that it stacks well with itself, which has historically not been true for pump effects—a hand of two [Radiant Destiny](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Radiant+Destiny)s does nothing, but two [History of Benalia](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=History+of+Benalia)s can win the game on their own.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Benalish+Marshal)](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Benalish+Marshal)

[Benalish Marshal](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Benalish+Marshal) is a solid 3/3 for three with a good ability. It's a Knight, which means it gets bonuses from [History of Benalia](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=History+of+Benalia), but it will pump anything, which makes it pretty good with token-makers and Vehicles.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Dauntless+Bodyguard)](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dauntless+Bodyguard)

[Dauntless Bodyguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dauntless+Bodyguard) is a good one-drop that has late-game applications. As a general rule, you should always play it on turn one if you have it and no other one-drop.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Knight+of+Grace)](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+Grace)

[Knight of Grace](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+Grace) is a 2/2 first striker for two with the subtype "Knight." It dodges [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push) and [Vraska's Contempt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vraska%27s+Contempt), but the deck will have plenty of targets anyway, so it's not like you're blanking their removal spells. Still, it's a plus. The third list is more token-based. It has an artifact subtheme but no Vehicles and no Karn, instead opting to go wider with [Pride of Conquerors](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pride+of+Conquerors) to abuse the token makers, including [Legion's Landing](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Legion%27s+Landing), which is absent in the other two lists. It has four copies of [History of Benalia](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=History+of+Benalia), but no other Knights apart from [Benalish Marshal](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Benalish+Marshal).

So, which one of the lists should you play? In my opinion, the best list is one that's a hybrid between lists two and three. I think if you're playing the artifact token–makers, then you want Karn, and if you have Karn you want the Vehicles. But I also think that [History of Benalia](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=History+of+Benalia) is too powerful not to play—it's an enabler and payoff in one card and it scales very well in multiples. (There are some lists with only two and I think that's weird; it's really a four-of kind of card.)

The ideal list to me is similar to JeanClaude11's, but with [History of Benalia](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=History+of+Benalia). Then, once you have [History of Benalia](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=History+of+Benalia), it actually makes sense to play [Dauntless Bodyguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20180427000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dauntless+Bodyguard) as your one-drop.
