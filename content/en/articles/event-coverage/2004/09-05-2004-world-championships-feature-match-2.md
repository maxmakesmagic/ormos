### Game 1

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7f/74/7f74a5d3430221413b81301201976fee6c321d392e6f775b3789f94a150a436c.jpg)

Murray Evans said that to win this match he would need to get early [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating) with one or two creatures to keep the pressure on Nuijten. Then he could use the card-drawing power of [Thoughtcast](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtcast) and [Night's Whisper](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Night%27s+Whisper) to keep the threats coming versus Julien Nuijten's removal.

Evans came out of the gate quickly just as Affinity was designed to do. Evans played down two [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker)s, a [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite) and [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating). Nuijten cleared the board with a third-turn Wrath of God while sitting at 14 life.

[Somber Hoverguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Somber+Hoverguard) came out for Evans while Nuijten played the namesake of his deck, Astral Slide. Evans sent in the Hoverguard for six and then Mana Leaked what would have been a devastating [Eternal Witness](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eternal+Witness). The [Eternal Witness](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eternal+Witness)-Astral Slide combo was what Nuijten's deck was designed to do but Evans didn't allow the combo to happen.

Nuijten was forced to continually find cycling cards to keep [Somber Hoverguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Somber+Hoverguard) from hitting him. He played a [Viridian Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viridian+Shaman) and destroyed a [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating). When Evans cast a second Plating, Nuijten simply cycled out the Shaman.

After Evans played a second Hoverguard, Nuijten played a second [Eternal Witness](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eternal+Witness) to fetch back Wrath of God. After casting the Wrath, Nuijten was at a precarious 1 life.

Evans tried one last push for the final damage but Nuijten had taken total control of the game with a Decree of Justice.

Julien Nuijten 1, Murray Evans 0

### Game 2

Evans started out game 2 with a mulligan. After the mulligan he played a first-turn [Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault). Nuijten tried to get an easy win by destroying Evans first land with an [Oxidize](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oxidize) but Evans had plenty of land even after the mulligan.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b5/a7/b5a7d3307ca00822cb57487fb1635534926113a95e184ee0b94878388f0e025b.jpg)

Evans kept sending in [Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault) for one damage a turn while countering two of Nuijten's Astral Slides with Mana Leaks. Nuijten was determined to get out an Astral Slide and played an [Eternal Witness](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eternal+Witness) to get one back. Evans used a [Thoughtcast](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtcast) and a [Night's Whisper](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Night%27s+Whisper) to refill his hand. Then he played out [Slobad, Goblin Tinkerer](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slobad%2C+Goblin+Tinkerer), [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager), and an [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker) but Nuijten had a Wrath of God and then a Akroma's Vengeance to first deal with Slobad and then kill the previously indestructible artifacts.

The Vengeance wiped out too much of Evans' board and conceded after Nuijten played a couple of creatures.

Julien Nuijten, 2 Murray Evans 0 

### Game 3

Evans found himself in a must-win situation going into game 3. He needed to win three straight games in order for him to advance to the semifinals.

Once again Evans, started off the game with a [Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault) but this time he also had an [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager) to join in the assault. While Nuijten set up his mana base with two Rampant Growths, Evans had sacrificed an [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker) to pump his [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager) up to a 3/3. Nuijten's [Viridian Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viridian+Shaman) hit the [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager) but Evans had played a second [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker) to modular the counters onto.

A [Slobad, Goblin Tinkerer](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slobad%2C+Goblin+Tinkerer) came out just in time to save a small piece of Evans' board from Akroma's Vengeance. A [Blinkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinkmoth+Nexus) knocked Nuijten to one life for the second time in the match. Nuijten played out a Eternal Dragon to hold of the Nexus but if Evans drew a land his second Nexus would deal lethal damage.

He knocked on his deck and drew . . . [Night's Whisper](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Night%27s+Whisper). The Whisper found Evans a [Shrapnel Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrapnel+Blast) that killed Nuijten the following turn.

Julien Nuijten 2, Murray Evans 1

### Game 4

Nuijten mulliganed to start game 4. Evans started off the game with a [Chromatic Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chromatic+Sphere). Once again, Nuijten went for the mana screw plan, using his [Oxidize](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oxidize) on Evans' first land. This time the strategy seemed to be successful, as Evans didn't have a second land.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0b/e1/0be1bd7c3de908f8603f0154c9fa462b76900683316e8f8dcb67ee5c6504e041.jpg)

On his third turn, Evans played out a [Seat of the Synod](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seat+of+the+Synod) and used his [Chromatic Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chromatic+Sphere) to make the mana for a [Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault). Nuijten used an [Eternal Witness](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eternal+Witness) to bring back the [Oxidize](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oxidize) to leave Evans landless once again.

Evans discarded on his next turn and then for the rest of the game whenever he found a land Nuijten would have another [Eternal Witness](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eternal+Witness) to retrieve the same [Oxidize](https://web.archive.org/web/20040905000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oxidize) over and over. After Nuijten had three Witnesses in play Evans had finally had enough and conceded with a lone City of Brass in play.

The 15-year-old Julien Nuijten was moving on to the Worlds semifinals.

**Final Score**  
 Julien Nuijten 3, Murray Evans 1
