Wizards of the Coast is committed to hosting **Magic** Weekend Nagoya in June. The Japanese players and fans are looking forward to the event, and we’re excited to bring them the highest level of **Magic** competition in the world.

We understand that each individual player needs to make the decision for themselves about whether or not they’re comfortable attending, and we always recommend checking with your local travel authority for more information. Official details about **Magic** Weekend Nagoya can be [viewed here](/en/events/2011/nagoya11/magic-weekend-nagoya-event-information). 

*Arron Goolsbey is the senior director of Organized Play for Wizards of the Coast.*
