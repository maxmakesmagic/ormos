Let's talk ***Magic** 2010* Intro Packs! As you remember, each Intro Pack contains a ready-to-play 41-card deck featuring a foil premium rare, a sealed 15-card booster pack, a strategy insert, "learn to play" guide. We're guessing you're most interested in the contents of the decks. So let's get to it!

### We Are Legion

![](https://i.ormos.uk/db/49/db493d0340f8243148e5f5a7c3e71463a20277c134ff433f58e9f114aa895de4.jpg)  

The forces of order attack with righteous fury! [Lightwielder Paladin](https://web.archive.org/web/20090713000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightwielder+Paladin) leads your charge, flanked by Soldiers, Angels, Griffins, and Pegasus, while your spells power up your creatures and protect you from harm.

### Firebomber

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1c/e2/1ce2a90d5ae55002029e1bb75f3d2040ea277af1364e8f84235ceb2b6bda1341.jpg)  

Fiery forces of destruction and frenzied creatures are the strengths of this deck. Spells like [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20090713000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt), [Fireball](https://web.archive.org/web/20090713000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fireball), and [Earthquake](https://web.archive.org/web/20090713000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Earthquake) shake your opponent up, allowing creatures like [Shivan Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20090713000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Dragon) to launch a fatal attack.
