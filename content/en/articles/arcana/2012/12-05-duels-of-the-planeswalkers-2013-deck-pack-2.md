There's another Deck Pack for [**Duels of the Planeswalkers** 2013](https://web.archive.org/web/20140508181032/http://www.wizards.com/magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx)! The first pack (which was released [last month](https://web.archive.org/web/20121205000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/duels-2013s-deck-pack-2012-11-07)) had a red-white deck and a green-blue deck. Or "Boros" and "Simic" if you prefer. And this month, we've got white-black (Orzhov) and red-green (Gruul). And both decks are usable in both the campaign and in multiplayer. Enjoy!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/68/28/6828a197c7a0b8faffc6fd4f3e29801b45d6da9a52e9bcca289a1bcae7a58938.jpg)  

### Grim Procession

The end is nigh! Wield the spirits, shades, and life-sapping magic in the white and black “Grim Procession” deck to show opponents that their defeat is inevitable.
