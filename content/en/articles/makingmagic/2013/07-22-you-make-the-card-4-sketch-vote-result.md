Welcome back to You Make the Card 4, the process in which readers of this site (i.e., you!) create a **Magic** card, together, to appear in a future set. Last week, you voted on which sketch of \[Waste Not\] you wanted artist [Matt Stewart](https://web.archive.org/web/20130722000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5B%22Matt+Stewart%22%5D) to turn into a final art piece.

The results are in!
