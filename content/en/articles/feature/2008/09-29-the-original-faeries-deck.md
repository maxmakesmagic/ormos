*Lorwyn* and *Shadowmoor* have brought about a resurgence in the popularity of the Faeries tribe, but did you know Faeries have been around **Magic** since the very beginning? All the way back at the very first World Championships in 1994, French player Bertrand Lestree managed to take this deck, featuring Fae representative [Argothian Pixies](https://web.archive.org/web/20080929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Argothian+Pixies), to the final table before losing to Zak Dolan. Why did Bertrand play the Pixies over more popular cards of the day like [Elvish Archers](https://web.archive.org/web/20080929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Archers)? Because it couldn't be killed by [Mishra's Factory](https://web.archive.org/web/20080929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mishra%27s+Factory)*or* [Juggernaut](https://web.archive.org/web/20080929000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Juggernaut), both common threats of the era!
