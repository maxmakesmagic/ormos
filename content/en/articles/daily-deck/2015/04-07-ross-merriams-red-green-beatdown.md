Control isn't the only deck that received new Dragons from *Dragons of Tarkir*. [Thunderbreak Regent](https://web.archive.org/web/20150407000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thunderbreak+Regent) is a great card that doesn't die without dealing damage to an opponent, and a 4/4 flyer is a fine attacker for an aggressive deck.

Red already had [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150407000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon) prior to *Dragons of Tarkir*. Eight strong Dragons plus [Draconic Roar](https://web.archive.org/web/20150407000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Draconic+Roar) is enough to build an aggressive red Dragon deck. The question is how to build around this.

Red-White or Jeskai were popular decks from the previous format. Mono-Red Devotion or splashing black for [Kolaghan, the Storm's Fury](https://web.archive.org/web/20150407000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kolaghan%2C+the+Storm%27s+Fury) sounds like an option too.

The pairing Ross Merriam made was green.

The combination of mana creatures and Dragons is an established strategy in *Magic* history. However, he added another interesting card from the new set. [Surrak, the Hunt Caller](https://web.archive.org/web/20150407000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Surrak%2C+the+Hunt+Caller)—the former khan—has returned as a mono-green creature.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Surrak%2C+the+Hunt+Caller)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150407000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Surrak%2C+the+Hunt+Caller)

He isn't as flashy compared to all the Dragons in the set, but that doesn't mean he's inferior to them.

In most cases he should have haste, unless he was quickly summoned with a mana creature. I'm very impressed by this card.
