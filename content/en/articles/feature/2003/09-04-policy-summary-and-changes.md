### Code of Conduct And Terms of Service: The Short Form

We understand that there's a lot to the Code of Conduct and Terms of Service, so here's a brief summary of some of the more important points. By following these general rules, you're pretty much assured of not violating either of these agreements.

**Note:** Keep in mind that this is only a summary of these documents and doesn't include everything. By creating and/or using a ***Magic** Online* account, you agree to be held to the policies set forth in the full [Terms of Service](https://web.archive.org/web/20030904000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com/help/tos.htm&origin=magic_magiconline_policies) and [Code of Conduct](/en/articles/uncategorised/2003/12-15-magic-the-gathering-online).

1.  Do not advertise or otherwise approach people for trades outside of the designated trading areas.
2.  Do not advertise "ante" matches or player-run prize tournaments.
3.  Do not use vulgar or discrimantory language.
4.  Do not circumvent the language filters.
5.  Do not harass, bully, or threaten other players.
6.  Do not impersonate or otherwise imply that you are an Adept or other Wizards of the Coast staff member.
7.  Do not disrupt the rooms by posting multiple messages within a short time span, whether repetitive or not.
8.  Don't scam, ripoff, or otherwise attempt to take people's stuff under false pretenses.

### The Code of Conduct Explained

After reading over the new version of the ***Magic** Online* [Code of Conduct](/en/articles/uncategorised/2003/12-15-magic-the-gathering-online), players have expressed concern over why we chose to add what we did to our statements. This is a brief explanation of why we felt we had to do things in this way.

### Trading Policy

Players may only engage in trade activity in the officially designated trading areas.

We found out early on that if we allow people to trade anywhere on ***Magic** Online* it resulted in mayhem in the chat rooms. We had no choice but to completely restrict trading to the **Trading Post**.

### Trade/Business Names

It took a long time to find a middle ground for this issue. While we don't want to discourage dealers, we also can't get involved in the secondary market nor have ***Magic** Online* turn into one big advertisement. We settled on allowing account names that depict trading--be it a dealer name or a name that advertises trading--but those accounts can only appear in designated trading areas. Otherwise it compromises our trading policy.

### No Non-official URLs

We tried for a long time to allow people to post personal **Magic**-related URLs, but due to abuses and workload it is just not feasible. We had to go with either all or nothing. Since we can't allow it all, we can't allow any.

### Ante/Prize Games

This is another one that took a long time to work out. Allowing player run prize tournaments opens us up to all kinds of problems from chat room disruption to people complaining about how an event is run. We did manage to get this one worked out.

*   A player may not advertise a private tournament anywhere on ***Magic** Online*--prize or no prize--period. Anyone trying to sneak around this will have his or her account(s) suspended or terminated.
*   Players may use private chat rooms to run or talk about their own tournaments, so long as those tournaments do not disrupt any other area of ***Magic** Online.*
*   We can't and won't support player-run tournaments in any way.

### Trade Scamming

This is pretty clear in the CoC, however it needs reinforcing. There are 2 main points: 

1.  There is only one way to trade safely on ***Magic** Online:* Through our trading system, one trade at a time. Treat every trade as a single event, not as part of a series. Once you click on the second confirmation the deal is done and can't be reversed--be sure, take your time, and don't let anyone rush you.

    We can and will only support single trades through our systems. Any arrangement outside of those systems (PayPal, multiple trades, borrowing cards, "I will pay you Tuesday," etc.) is completely out of our control and we can't and won't support it. If you wish to engage in that kind of deal you are on your own.

2.  With the above said, if we do catch someone engaging in any trade practice that we feel are inappropriate, we reserve the right to terminate that account and any accounts associated with it immediately and without warning whether the other party agreed to it or not. For a definition of "terminate" see section 14 of the [Terms of Service](https://web.archive.org/web/20030904000000/http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com/help/tos.htm).

We do what we can to protect players and their stuff, but nothing we do will help if players do not protect themselves.
