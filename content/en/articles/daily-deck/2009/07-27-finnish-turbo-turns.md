It's Nationals week on Daily Decks, and we'll be highlighting some of the best decks the 2009 Nationals tournaments have to offer! Finland's Mikko Airaksinen, for example, took a strategy popularized by Jon Loucks to the top slot. How does it work? Play a ton of cantripping artifacts like Kaleidostone and Howling Mine early, then use Time Sieve to sacrifice them and draw an extra card. From there, Open the Vaults allows you to get them all back and rinse, wash, and repeat!
