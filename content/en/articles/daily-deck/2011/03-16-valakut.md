Valakut is back! Joey Mispagel has resurrected the archetype in Standard by using it to win the recent StarCityGames.com Standard Open in Memphis this past weekend. He did it with some interesting card choices: Inferno Titan joins the staple Primeval Titan in the deck, and Koth of the Hammer gives him a great plan B that can also be used to accelerate out the Titans.
