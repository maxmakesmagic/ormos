## Ready to Get Competitive?

We have two Premier Play events coming up that will get you into competitive *Magic* while enjoying the latest set!

## Arena Open*: The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth* Limited, July 1–2

[![Arena Open, July 1–2, promotional graphic showing Aradune, thoughtful with eyes downcast, and his sword resting on his right shoulder](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/zatiqoz03rlz.jpg)](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2023/06-19-arena-open-the-lord-or-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earth-limited-july-12) 

Two days of exciting competitive play await you in the [next Arena Open, July 1–2](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2023/06-19-arena-open-the-lord-or-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earth-limited-july-12).

*   Day 1 features Best-of-One and Best-of-Three Sealed Deck matches, with max wins—seven in Best-of-One or four in Best-of-Three—earning you an invitation to Day 2 events.
*   Day 2 steps up the competition as invited players from Day 1 face each other in two Best-of-Three Player Draft showdowns. Earn three or four wins in Draft One to be invited to Draft Two, where the prizes include an invitation to the August Qualifier Weekend and up to $2,000!

All players who enter the Arena Open will receive the Dragon Hoard sleeve:

![Dragon Hoard sleeve](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/sleeve_dragon_hoard.png) 

## Best-of-One Qualifier Play-In, July 8

[![Qualifier Play-In, July 8. A white cloaked king wearing a crown kneels facing away with text, Now Come the Days of the King](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/hudl9tekospa.jpg)](https://www.magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) 

The Friday following the Arena Open will feature [the first of July's Qualifier Play-In tournaments](https://www.magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in). This Best-of-One event can earn you an invitation to the July Qualifier Weekend, July 15–16, that is the gateway to Arena Championship 4 later this year!

## Keep It Casual with *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth* Events

*MTG Arena* isn't just about high-stakes competitive play—we have lots of events for you to relax and play with the latest set!

## Quick Draft: *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth*

![Quick Draft: The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth happening now. Image of Shortcut to Mushrooms card art with Hobbits eating mushrooms. Text: Short Cuts Don't Always Make Long Delays](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/11ki2hirigaq.jpg) 

Right now, you can play *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth* Quick Draft. Take your time drafting against bots, and then enjoy single matches against other players with your carefully considered 40-card deck. Earn prizes and keep the cards you draft! Read more in the [Quick Draft Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20230630000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/mtgarena/draft) and check [the schedule below](#schedule) for even more events.

## The Two (Command) Towers Brawl July 7–14

Your favorite legendary creature from *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth* is ready to command your 60-card Alchemy-legal Brawl deck into battle from July 7–14! Legendary creatures and planeswalkers are banned except those from *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth*.

Play, win *Core Set 2021* showcase basic lands, and change decks as much as you want until the event ends!

## July 7–9 Celebrate *Magic* at Your Local WPN Store 

![People enjoying playing Magic in person at their local game store](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/hophich0sigl.jpg) 

Join us for the *Magic* Celebration Event at [your local WPN game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20230630000000/https://locator.wizards.com/) July 7–9! It takes just three easy steps: 

1.  Register and play *Jumpstart*! Purchase two *Jumpstart* packets and play three casual rounds at your store. The first 30 players to register and play *Jumpstart* at the *Magic* Celebration Event will receive a limited-edition, collectible dice set that includes three six-sided (d6) dice!
2.  Form your Fellowship! Bring up to eight friends, and you'll all get a special Thought Vessel promo card!
3.  Claim a Precious! Use your registered *MTG Arena* [Wizards account](https://web.archive.org/web/20230630000000/https://myaccounts.wizards.com/) in the [*Magic Companion* app](https://web.archive.org/web/20230630000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/companion-app) to get a special, ready-to-play deck in your *MTG Arena* inbox, beginning July 23—just be sure to log in with the account you use to play *MTG Arena*! (This promotion only applies in select regions where *MTG Arena* codes are valid.)

Don't miss it!

## Correction: June Ranked Season Pack Rewards

In previous *MTG Arena* Announcements, we incorrectly listed the June 2023 Ranked Seasons pack rewards as *March of the Machine* packs. In fact, the June 2023 Ranked Season pack rewards are *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth* packs. We've made note of this and corrected the packs listed in our previous posts. 

If you wish to swap your rewarded packs to the previously mentioned *March of the Machine* packs, please reach out to [Customer Support](https://web.archive.org/web/20230630000000/https://support.wizards.com/hc/en-us), and we will make this change to your rewards. We appreciate your understanding!

## Two New Anthologies Are Coming July 18!

July is hot in *MTG Arena*! Two new anthologies arrive on July 18, introducing a total of 50 *Magic* cards to digital play! To give you a taste, we're previewing three for each anthology right here, with [more previews to come on both July 8 and 9](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2023/06-30-where-to-find-explorer-anthology-3-and-historic-anthology-7-previews).

## Historic Anthology 7

[![Historic Anthology 7, coming to MTG Arena July 18](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/switrlvevut2.jpg)](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/06-30-historic-anthology-7-comes-to-mtg-arena) 

[Historic gets an injection of 25 cards](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/06-30-historic-anthology-7-comes-to-mtg-arena) to deepen the format even further. These are exciting cards from across *Magic*'s history that are coming to *MTG Arena* for the first time. Here are a few to whet your appetite!

![Tooth and Nail](https://media.wizards.com/2023/ha7/en_5J5Zks4EnGZg.png) ![Worn Powerstone](https://media.wizards.com/2023/ha7/en_jGCBfokdp0xm.png) ![Acidic Slime](https://media.wizards.com/2023/ha7/en_GaN4Z4Ivx3vA.png) 

## Explorer Anthology 3

[![Explorer Anthology 3, coming to MTG Arena July 18](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/klc91tro1ren.jpg)](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/06-30-explorer-anthology-3-arrives-on-mtg-arena) 

Also on July 18, [the Explorer format gets 25 new-to-digital cards](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/06-30-explorer-anthology-3-arrives-on-mtg-arena) that expand this exciting and growing format. It's not just Explorer that'll benefit from these additions—these cards will be legal in all Historic formats!

## August Qualifier Weekend Format Change

The format for the August Qualifier Weekend event on August 12–13 is changing. As we just announced above, [we're adding 25 cards in Explorer Anthology 3](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/06-30-explorer-anthology-3-arrives-on-mtg-arena) on July 18, and we want to give those a chance to shine in competitive play. So, the new format for the August Qualifier Weekend will be Explorer!

### Qualifier Events

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events. 

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events. Check out the standings to see [who's qualified for Arena Championship 4](https://magic.gg/standings/arena-championship-4). 

Qualifier tokens are delivered in your *MTG Arena* inbox. Remember to claim them before the event starts!
