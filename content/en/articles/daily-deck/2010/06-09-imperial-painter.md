The Pro Tour is home to top level competition, but even if you're not there to play the Pro Tour, there are plenty of Magic tournaments to be had. At a generic Grand Prix Trial at Pro Tour–San Juan, in which the winner would earn three byes to any Grand Prix, Michael Pozsgay managed to make the Top 4 with his Imperial Painter deck. The deck strives to cast Painter's Servant, set it to blue, then use Grindstone to mill the opponent's entire library in one swoop! You even have cards like Red Elemental Blast and Pyroblast to help force through your spells against blue decks (or, in a pinch, with Painter's Servant you can simply use them to blow up / counter any spell).
