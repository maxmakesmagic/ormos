Wrapping up a previous vote… Noble Purpose edged out Orim's Prayer in one of the closest votes we've had yet. Looks like gaining life on offense is more important that gaining life on defense.

On the art side, the original black Paladins by Carl Critchlow beat out the new versions quite handily. Chalk one more up for old art…

This week's vote will stick with the theme of white enchantments (and not be quite as controversial as the Birds/Elves vote). Which one of the following three enchantments should be in *Eighth Edition*: Worship, Pariah, or Kismet? All three have seen play in tournaments over the years, and each forms the backbone of a specific type of deck. So which will it be, one of the ones from *Seventh Edition* making an encore, or will Kismet, out of print since *Sixth Edition*, be brought back from the graveyard?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c4/2d/c42dc4bcb9661c9f0e023573806c49d0412fe9e2c818254ba0a6fe07a06bfa91.jpg)

The art vote this week is for a couple funny little goblins and pigs. Goblin Chariot debuted in *Starter* and was moved into *Seventh Edition*. It will live on in *Eighth*, but with which art?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3f/fe/3ffe6e5542e66c25ccb8abe43904b53f570df64ab4c2576db06b2899ce5ecb97.jpg)

And finally, the flavor text. This week's card for flavor text submission is the always-laughable Orcish Artillery. The art that will be used in *Eighth* is the Dan Frazier version ![](https://i.ormos.uk/57/30/5730e10913530997377923216480e906a247ca54798bb51b28f2d5660c4d24bc.jpg) from *Sixth Edition* (not the *Seventh Edition* art), so make sure your text doesn't refer to the wrong picture. You can read Rei's guidelines for flavor text in [last week's article](/en/articles/uncategorised/2002/07-12-selecting-eighth-edition), or just go to the [FlavOracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20041216034035/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/flavoracle/home) and [submit](#)!

All these votes will be up for two weeks, and don't forget, you can still vote on Birds of Paradise + Vine Trellis vs. Llanowar Elves + Utopia Tree, art for Spined Wurm, and submit flavor text for Aladdin's Ring[here](https://web.archive.org/web/20050206002559/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/vote8e/home).
