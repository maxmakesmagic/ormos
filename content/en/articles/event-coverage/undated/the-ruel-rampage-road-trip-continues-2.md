![](https://i.ormos.uk/d6/2e/d62e947bd389f34545fdd270fc7a9dda1ea7cf7aa3ff966cf25309c34b360305.jpg)*David Brucker, left, and champion Olivier Ruel.*

Can Olivier Ruel be stopped?

The Frenchman has done it again. Grand Prix-Bologna saw the debut of *Saviors of Kamigawa* on the GP circuit. More than 650 players came to test their mettle with the new format at the Palamalaguti basketball stadium on the outskirts of Bologna. In the end, experience counted as the two most accomplished players in the Top 8, David Brucker and Olivier Ruel, faced off against each other in the finals.

Ruel had drafted a juicy almost mono-black deck and swept both Marco Castellano and Giulio Barra in the quarters and semifinals. Brucker had also drafted black-green but was also splicing white for Oyobi and [Waxmane Baku](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Waxmane+Baku). He also overcame his quarterfinal and semifinal opponents, Tomas Klimes and Leonard Barbou, in quick matches.

The match was all about overlaps. Ruel had more creatures in the first game while Brucker came back in the second with a mighty [Strength of Cedars](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strength+of+Cedars). In the decider, a ninjitsu'd [Okiba-Gang Shinobi](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Okiba-Gang+Shinobi) put Brucker on the back foot and he could never quite relieve the pressure to make his bomb Oyobi turn the game.

The Ruel Rampage Road Trip continues!

## Sunday, June 12: 10:10 pm - Quarterfinal: Quentin Martin vs. Giulio Barra

Quentin Martin is a good English player who's come close a number of times but now finally gets his Top 8 appearance. Giulio Barra from Italy has an 11th place finish to his name back at PT San Diego a few years back.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0b/cf/0bcfe20997ea64b915c3ce7f8e8296069fe8b0cbdb51a3584ce888d2ebfcdbb0.jpg)*Giulio Barra*

Martin's draft could best be described as kamikaze. He likes the [Dampen Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dampen+Thought)/splice deck and tried to push it a little too hard. Most of the pro's watching felt it was a little premature to go all-in with a second pick [Psychic Puppetry](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychic+Puppetry). In a Top 8, when it is straight knockout, sometimes you have to gamble. This time the packs didn't pay off and Martin ended up with half a red-blue splice engine and a bunch of mediocre creatures. Barra has a fairly solid black-white deck and Martin will be hard-pressed not to get flattened.

Martin won the coin flip and opted to go first.

"I need a miracle," Quentin said.

"I want you to have a miracle," Olivier Ruel said from the next table.

It looked like Martin might get a sniff as Barra mulliganed down to 5. The Italian's five cards seemed pretty good as he busted out a [Sensei Golden-Tail](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei+Golden-Tail) on turn 2 and started swinging.

Martin had managed only a [Blademane Baku](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blademane+Baku). Ruel went to turn the screw as he bolstered his forces with a [Cruel Deceiver](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cruel+Deceiver) and [Lifespinner](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifespinner).

Brucker wasn't quite dead. A [Scaled Hulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scaled+Hulk) and [Shinen of Life's Roar](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shinen+of+Life%27s+Roar) meant Ruel could only overlap with the 1/1 [Kuro's Taken](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuro%27s+Taken). That was enough though as he was able to Ninjutsu the rat into a bigger rat, [Okiba-Gang Shinobi](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Okiba-Gang+Shinobi).

Ruel 1-0 Brucker

Brucker fetched a second swamp with a Tribe Elder that enabled him to cast a [Hand of Cruelty](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hand+of+Cruelty). Ruel also had the pro-white samurai as well as [Kuro's Taken](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuro%27s+Taken) and a [Hankyu](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hankyu). An [Okina Nightwatch](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Okina+Nightwatch) looked like it could possibly be scary, but a [Waking Nightmare](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Waking+Nightmare) ensured Olivier kept the hand size advantage. The Frenchman was still waiting for his fourth land. Kagemaro's clutch shrunk Ruel's [Hand of Cruelty](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hand+of+Cruelty), but didn't finish it off.

A [Okiba-Gang Shinobi](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Okiba-Gang+Shinobi) snuck through to finish off Brucker's hand and then the game got a little brutal as creatures traded in the red zone. The Nightwatch remained unscathed as creatures died around it. The board was a [Moonring Mirror](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moonring+Mirror) for Ruel and the Nightwatch for Brucker. The life totals were 16 to 10 in Brucker's favor.

The Mirror started accumulating cards as Ruel set himself up for the long game. He needed to deal with the immediate problem of Brucker's fatties. The German laid a seventh land and Ruel was pleased to see [Vine Kami](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vine+Kami) enter play and not Oyobi. A pair of [Kuro's Taken](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kuro%27s+Taken) took care of the Kami, although Ruel couldn't regenerate both. Another creature appeared for Brucker and with the overlap he was able to smash through for the game with a [Strength of Cedars](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strength+of+Cedars).

Ruel 1-1 Brucker

And now … the decider.

Brucker had to take a mulligan. Ruel looked the capitalize as he summoned a [Hand of Cruelty](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hand+of+Cruelty) and then equipped it with a [Manriki-Gusari](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Manriki-Gusari) before charging in.

Brucker wasn't doing to badly for his mulligan. On turn three he had all three colors out and was able to follow a [Shinen of Life's Roar](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shinen+of+Life%27s+Roar) with a [Waxmane Baku](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Waxmane+Baku). A fourth land gave him the mana to cast a Kagemaro's Clutch and deal with a [Cursed Ronin](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cursed+Ronin) before it got too out of hand.

Ruel was still getting the beats in with the Hand and then joined it with [Kami of Empty Graves](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kami+of+Empty+Graves). Brucker started whittling down his hand with a [Honden of Night's Reach](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Honden+of+Night%27s+Reach). He summoned an Ashen Skin Zubera.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/08/42/08426e6a050a578f311916181c7a6eb9027a55cef11bb4a6d452d7801dc0defa.jpg)*Olivier Ruel*

Then came perhaps pivotal moment in the game. Ruel went into his attack step. The [Waxmane Baku](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Waxmane+Baku) tapped the [Kami of Empty Graves](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kami+of+Empty+Graves) down before it could be declared as an attacker. The Hand came in and Brucker chose to let it through rather than chump with the Ashen Skin Zubera. An [Okiba-Gang Shinobi](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Okiba-Gang+Shinobi) leapt out of the unblocked samurai and ripped the remaining two cards out of Brucker's hand. They were a [Scaled Hulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scaled+Hulk) and Oyobi.

"Nice catch," Ruel said.

Brucker drew successive [Death Denied](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death+Denied) to repair the damage, but Ruel's deck had him so far on the defensive he had to resort to fetching back Zuberas just to stay alive. A [Waxmane Baku](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Waxmane+Baku) kept him alive long enough to cast Oyobi, but even that mighty legend couldn't save him as the [Moonring Mirror](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719033410/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moonring+Mirror) kept finding Olivier enough creatures to maintain enough of an overlap to crash in for the win on the following turn.

Olivier Ruel beats David Brucker 2-1 and is champion of GP Bologna!
