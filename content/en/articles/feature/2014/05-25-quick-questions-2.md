### What is your color preference in *Theros* block Sealed?

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpatl14/duke_qh2.jpg)

***No. 1 Ranked Player Reid Duke**: "I would say black, with my second choice being white. White is probably the best color in Draft, but Sealed Deck is a little slower, so having access to the black and removal and card advantage is definitely my preference.”* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/db/5c/db5c5dcdf5d778a06842102c2adfcf7585cbf24493d46ffcd5f7883c9a61c876.jpg)

***No. 2 Ranked Player Jeremy Dezani**: “In Sealed for sure it's green. It's deep and has a lot of good cards.”* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpatl14/cifka_qh2.jpg)

***No. 3 Ranked Player Stanislav Cifka**: “I think green is the best color in general. It was before as well.”* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpatl14/bruno_qh2.jpg)

***2013 Magic Online Championship Competitor Bruno Diaz**: “I really enjoy blue. Blue is the best color since it has a lot of tricks and other things you can do.”*
