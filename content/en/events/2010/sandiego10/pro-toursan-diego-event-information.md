*   [**Complete Coverage of Pro Tour–San Diego**](/en/events/2010/ptsd10/gortzen-goes-all-the-way-in-san-diego)

The **Magic: The Gathering** Pro Tour travels to sunny California for Pro Tour–San Diego! Truly the crown in the **Magic** tournament circuit, the Pro Tour is a four-day **Magic** festival offering something for everyone whether you’re new to the game or a seasoned World Champion. 

There will be a whole lot more than a $230,000 tournament taking place! **Magic** fans of all ages and experience levels will find something waiting for them at Pro Tour–San Diego. Try Public Events, tournaments open to all offering fantastic prizes throughout the weekend. Face off against **Magic** celebrities in the Champions Challenge, or mingle with some of the game’s most talented artists as they sign cards for fans all weekend long. Get a glimpse into the pro lifestyle while seeing some of the game’s best in action live and up close. Whether you come for one day or the entire weekend, Pro Tour–San Diego offers you the opportunity for an unforgettable **Magic** experience!

Pro Tour–San Diego is a Standard/Booster Draft format event with a Standard Top 8 that runs February 19-21, 2010. The Last-Chance Qualifier will be held Thursday, February 18 at the San Diego Convention Center starting at 5 p.m. The format for the LCQ is *Zendikar* Sealed Deck, with the top four finishers earning a spot in the main event. For more information, visit the complete [Public Events schedule](/en/events/2010/sandiego10-public/pro-toursan-diego-public-event-information).

### About San Diego

San Diego, California has the nicest weather in the United States of America. That's not just an opinion; it's scientific fact! The average temperature ranges from 72 degrees Fahrenheit (22 Celsius) in August all the way down to 57 (14 Celsius) in January. This remarkably pleasant weather has led to a wide variety of outdoor activities and entertainments, from the world-class San Diego Zoo to Balboa Park and many fine beaches. It's also home to bases for the U.S. Navy and Marine Corps. And if you're looking for a more Mexican flavor, Tijuana is only a trolley ride away. Just blocks from the Convention Center, the Gaslamp Quarter is San Diego's center for dining and nightlife. And every year, San Diego is host to Comic Con International, the largest comic book and popular arts convention in the world which regularly boasts over 100,000 attendees. San Diegans are generally relaxed, happy, and proud to live in "America's Finest City." 

This will be the fourth time the Pro Tour has come to San Diego. Previous stops provided such memorable events as [Farid Meraghni's crowning achievement](https://web.archive.org/web/20100113000000/http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/event.asp?event=PTSD02) in his 2002 Rookie of the Year season, [Nicolai Herzog's remarkable win in 2004](/en/events/2004/ptsd04/live-coverage-of-2004-pro-tour-san-diego) (in back-to-back Limited Pro Tours), and the [Sliver-fueled triumph of Chris Lachmann and Jacob Van Lunen](/en/events/2007/ptsdg07/sliver-kids-stun-san-diego) in 2007's Two-Headed Giant event.

### Tournament Format and Schedule

Pro Tour–San Diego is a three-day Standard and Booster Draft format event with a prize purse of $230,795, with a Standard Top 8. For more information about the format, schedule, and prize payout, [click here](/en/events/2010/sandiego10-format/pro-toursan-diego-2010-format-schedule-and-prizes).

### Site Attractions

**PUBLIC EVENTS**  
 Come one, come all to participate in four days of events open to the public. Starting Thursday with the Last-Chance Qualifier, there are tournaments, drafts, EDH, *Planechase* events, and open dueling all weekend. Pro Tour–San Diego will also play host to a few extra-special events, such as Massive **Magic**!, two Pro Tour Qualifiers, the $3,000 Draft Challenge, Super Friday Night **Magic**, and the **Magic Online** Live Series Championship. Prizes include invitations and airfare to Pro Tour–San Juan, Power 9 cards, iPods, an Xbox 360 Elite gaming system, a Kindle DX, digital cameras, and much more! 

*   **[Complete Public Events Schedule](/en/events/2010/sandiego10-public/pro-toursan-diego-public-event-information)**

**CHAMPIONS CHALLENGE**  
 Test your **Magic** skills against Wizards of the Coast R&D members Mike Turian, Erik Lauer, and Mark Gottlieb. Win and you can walk away with cool **Magic** prizes! 

*   [**Champions Challenge Profiles**](/en/events/2010/sandiego10-challenge/pro-toursan-diego-champions-challenge)

**ARTISTS ON SITE**  
 Prominent **Magic** artists rk post and Jason Felix will be on hand for free signings of your favorite cards, plus you’ll have the opportunity to buy prints and even original artwork. The artists booth will be open Friday-Sunday from 10 a.m. - 5 p.m. 

*   [**Artist Profiles**](/en/events/2010/sandiego10-artists/pro-toursan-diego-artists)

**WATCH THE TOP 8**  
 All of Sunday's Top 8 action can be viewed on screens in the hall, with Rich Hagon and Pro Tour Historian Brian David-Marshall providing the commentary. Sunday's action begins at 10:45 a.m. local time, which is 1:45 p.m. ET, and is available as a [webcast](/en/events/2004/webcast/magic-the-gathering-webcast-schedule) from the comfort of your own home.
