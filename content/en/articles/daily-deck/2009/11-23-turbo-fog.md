Joel Calafell started the 2009 World Championships off strong with a 6-0 start using a Turbo Fog deck designed to take advantage of a field filled with Jund. Early card draw through things like Howling Mine and Jace Beleren keep his hand stocked with Fogs in the form of Safe Passage and Angelsong. Before long his opponent is low on cards, and a timely Archive Trap is all it takes to send the challenger packing! For more on the 2009 World Championships, [visit the official event coverage](/en/events/2009/worlds09/all-roads-lead-to-victory-for-coimbra-china).
