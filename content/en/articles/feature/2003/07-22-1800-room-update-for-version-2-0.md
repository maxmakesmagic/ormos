### This just in...

* * *

We’re going to change the way the 1800 Room works. Yesterday we announced that entry into the room (and it’s tournament queues with 9-5 booster pack prize payouts) would be limited to players with an 1800 composite rating. We knew that there weren’t a huge number of players with ratings that high, but we believed that there were enough people who were at least close to this threshold (especially since the incentive of getting to draft in 9-5 queues would motivate players to work on their ratings). As I explained yesterday, one of the goals of the 1800 Room is to serve players who prefer to play in tournaments with top-heavy prize distributions.

However, when we re-analyzed the ratings data today we discovered that we had been looking at flawed data when we made our initial decisions about how to set up the room. We did the right analysis (looking at what percentage of accounts already had sufficiently high ratings, etc.), but the data we were looking at was incorrect. Basically, someone made the equivalent of a typo and so the committee that decided how to set up the room thought there were a lot more accounts above 1800 than there actually are.

Oops … we’re sorry.

The good news is that we caught our error in time to change things before the new queues go live next week. Instead of requiring an 1800 composite rating, we’re going to require an 1800 rating in either Constructed or Limited.

We believe this change will allow a sufficient number of accounts into the 1800 Room so that many players who don’t like the 4-3-2-2 payouts will continue to have another option. We will definitely be watching the results, of course, and we expect to reevaluate the entrance requirements in a couple of months. (Consider yourself warned, by the way, that we will strongly consider switching the room back to a composite rating threshold at that time.)
