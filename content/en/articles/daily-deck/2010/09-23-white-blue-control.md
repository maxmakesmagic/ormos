It is one of **Magic**'s oldest archetypes: White-Blue Control. With the printing of Wall of Omens and Sun Titan, the deck has experienced a resurgence in Standard. The question, particularly with a white-blue planeswalker joining the fray, is "How will *Scars of Mirrodin* change the deck?" You can get a sneak peek by looking at previews catalogued on the [Visual Spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20110102021805/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/scarsofmirrodin/spoiler).
