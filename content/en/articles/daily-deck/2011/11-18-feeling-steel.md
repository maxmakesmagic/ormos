A lot of people had written off Tempered Steel as a viable Standard archetype, but it's still putting up results. **Magic Online**'s Nunca added a twist to the archetype using Riddlesmith and the innocuous *Innistrad* common Feeling of Dread, riding this deck to a 4-0 finish in a recent Daily Event. Could this deck end up being a major player at the **Magic** World championships? Click on over to [the coverage](/en/events/2011/worlds11/japan-stands-tall-at-worlds-again) and see for yourself!
