Most players at Grand Prix Stockholm opted for more traditional strategies, but Jan van der Vegt brought a spicy one to the table: U/W Heroic! Or "Falcon Punch!" as he calls it, in reference to the singleton [Abzan Falconer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Falconer) that can push heroic creatures through a creature stall.

To learn more about the deck, I sat down with Jan, whose record overnight was 7-2, and asked him to explain what makes the deck tick.

**How was the deck conceived and built – who else was involved?** 

"Daan Pruijt saw a couple of lists on *Magic Online* and figured that it would be a good idea in a format where all the non-situational removal spells are three mana, so you can tempo them out with [Gods Willing](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gods+Willing) and [Feat of Resistance](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Feat+of+Resistance)," Jan explained. "He started testing it, did really well on *Magic Online*, and then I started testing together with him." 

**What is the game plan of this deck?** 

"Get a big guy out there; protect it; kill opponent," Jan answered. "The deck looks like an aggro deck, but it's really more of a combo deck. It's effectively a 3-card combo: a heroic creature, a way to pump it, and a protection spell."

 **Your deck looks like an update of a *Theros* Block Constructed deck. What are the additions from **Khans of Tarkir** ?** 

"The key additions are [Defiant Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Defiant+Strike), [Feat of Resistance](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Feat+of+Resistance), and [Flooded Strand](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flooded+Strand). [Defiant Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Defiant+Strike) is a good way to put the first +1/+1 counter on a heroic creature so that the Ordeals can trigger right away after that. [Feat of Resistance](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Feat+of+Resistance) trades for a removal spell that typically costs more mana, and it's better than [Ajani's Presence](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ajani%27s+Presence) because it can stop cards like [Abzan Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Charm) and [Jeskai Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Charm). [Flooded Strand](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flooded+Strand) may be the most important addition because it fixes the mana." 

 **Many Block Constructed versions had [Stratus Walk](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stratus+Walk) and [Fabled Hero](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fabled+Hero). Why did you cut those cards?** 

"[Stratus Walk](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stratus+Walk) is good if you have [Hero of Iroas](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero+of+Iroas), but if not, then it's very expensive for a card that you can get blown out with if they have a removal spell. Every mana that you can spare is really important, especially in the early turns of the game. [Fabled Hero](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fabled+Hero) was cut for the same reason: you don't want expensive cards that [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike) and [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight) can easily take out. I prefer [Lagonna-Band Trailblazer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lagonna-Band+Trailblazer) in that slot." 

**What are the good and bad matchups for the deck?** 

"Jeskai and Abzan are good matchups. Mono-red and Green Devotion are even better. The bad matchup is Mardu because [Crackling Doom](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crackling+Doom) is really good against me. [Stubborn Denial](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stubborn+Denial) from the sideboard helps a bit, but I'm still not favored." 

**Can you explain some of your sideboard choices and how you sideboard against the top decks?** 

"Against Abzan, it really depends on what cards you expect from them. [Lagonna-Band Trailblazer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lagonna-Band+Trailblazer) is good against [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight); [Stubborn Denial](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stubborn+Denial) is good versus [End Hostilities](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=End+Hostilities); and [Suspension Field](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Suspension+Field) is an answer to [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino). They generally board in a lot of extra removal, so I tend to take out a couple of [Aqueous Form](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aqueous+Form) and maybe [Abzan Falconer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Falconer)." 

"Against Jeskai, it also depends on the version. But most of the time, I put in [Lagonna-Band Trailblazer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lagonna-Band+Trailblazer); [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos); and [Glare of Heresy](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glare+of+Heresy). I cut 4 [Aqueous Form](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aqueous+Form) because after sideboard, it becomes less about blocking and more about removal." 

**Q: How many other people are playing this deck, and how are they faring?** 

"Three Dutchmen played the deck: Daan Pruijt, Cedric van Oene, and myself. Cedric went 3-6, Daan went 6-2-1, and I went 7-2."

**What are some interesting plays that we might not see right away picking up this deck?** 

"These may be relatively obvious, but you can use [Defiant Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Defiant+Strike) to counter [Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames) on [Favored Hoplite](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Favored+Hoplite), and you can use a protection spell as a [Falter](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Falter)." 

If you've been looking for a powerful aggro/combo deck in Standard, then give this deck a spin!
