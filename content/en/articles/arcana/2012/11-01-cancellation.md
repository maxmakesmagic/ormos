In *Return to Ravnica*, the standard way to counter a spell is Cancel.

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fe/7e/fe7ef8ba68e14c20b8a76afeabd47667081850144acf782c34b7a8914f3d04cd.jpg) 

Cancel | Art by [Karl Kopinski](https://web.archive.org/web/20121101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22karl%20kopinski%22])

Cancel has been in several sets since it was first introduced in *Time Spiral*:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/26/56/2656b634d65b5eba5377973a7e89fbc3d83a102c419362220b99e8edbdb68af6.jpg) 

Cancel | Art by [Mark Poole](https://web.archive.org/web/20121101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22mark%20poole%22])

When it first appeared, Cancel was naturally compared to the original Counterspell. This wasn't just because it was the same effect for ![1 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/4c/6b4cf558eab279fb9414f146ebdd0e0705ec92984fec530597dd2a3bce43ce24.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif) instead of ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif). The wizard in Mark Poole's artwork was making the same arcane gesture as in the very first Counterspell!

After that, it became clear that Cancel was the new standard "Counter target spell" option. It appeared in *Tenth Edition* and every core set since, and also made periodic appearances in expansion sets. Here it is in *Zendikar*:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9b/a9/9ba989625686b14ded2c1065c25061c2d971b93c44561536c0c65b62c602569d.jpg) 

Cancel | Art by [Scott Chou](https://web.archive.org/web/20121101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22scott%20chou%22])

When Cancel appears in an expansion, it usually takes on characteristics appropriate to the setting. The *Return to Ravnica* version is *Azorius*, for example. And the *Shards of Alara* version appears to be from the Bant shard:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3c/c5/3cc58562a1783ed5af6e2a2c0b09d684f6497f23abca416bb7a857d706a35686.jpg) 

Cancel | Art by [David Palumbo](https://web.archive.org/web/20121101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22david%20palumbo%22])
