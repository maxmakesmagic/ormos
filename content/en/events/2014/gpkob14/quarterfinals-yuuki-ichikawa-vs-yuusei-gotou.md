A massive crowd gathered as the headline quarterfinals began. On one side sat 14th ranked Yuuiki Ichikawa and his Blue-Red [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) deck. Unlike many [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) players this weekend, Ichikawa had opted not to taint his deck with [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf)s--keeping his two card combo deck clean and simple. Though Ichikawa already has two Pro Tour top eights on his resume, a Grand Prix top eight had eluded him until now. He earned it by winning a nail biting match in round fifteen and praying that his tiebreakers would carry him through.

His opponent, Yuusei Gotou, on the other hand, was able to intentionally draw his way into the top eight. Escaping the swiss rounds with only one loss, Gotou headed into battle with his innovative "No Affinity" deck. Gotou's deck was named as such because his artifact based deck included zero cards with the Affinity keyword. In their place, Gotou had access to serious heavy hitters: 4 of each [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf), [Ensoul Artifact](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ensoul+Artifact), [Shrapnel Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrapnel+Blast), and [Galvanic Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Blast). Throughout the weekend Gotou had been able to get through massive damage with his large creatures and finish opponents off with burn.

The Games

While Ichikawa begrudgingly kept 5 cards, he was happy to see that Gotou did not have the most explosive draw that his deck could muster: turn one [Memnite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memnite), turn two [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating).

Gotou pried into Ichikawa's hand on the third turn with a [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) revealing that Ichikawa had only lands.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpkob14/QF_ichikawa.jpg)

*Yuuki Ichikawa*

While Gotou's hand was not explosive, [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating) was able to tack on six points of damage when Gotou equipped it to his [Memnite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memnite). Noticing that his future was bleak, Ichikawa packed up his cards and headed for his sideboard.

**Gotou 1-0 Ichikawa**

Gotou's start in the second game consisted of a singleton [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge). When Ichikawa used [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) to remove the 1/1 flyer, Gotou was unable to attain Metalcraft, leaving him with a useless [Mox Opal](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Opal) and only a single mana source.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpkob14/QF_gotou.jpg)

*Yuusei Gotou*

Gotou was able to land another artifact--[Chromatic Star](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chromatic+Star)-- a few turns later, allowing him to landa [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf). By this time it had already grown to be a 5/6. While Ichikawa had the counter in the form of [Sower of Temptation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sower+of+Temptation), Gotou used a Path To [Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exile) to regain control of his [Lhurgoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lhurgoyf).

Ichikawa found a [Relic of Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Relic+of+Progenitus) on his next turn and used it in combination with a [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) to remove the [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf).

Gotou had an opening to land a pair of [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating)s and a [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge). Holding all the answers Ichikawa cast and sacrificed an [Engineered Explosives](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engineered+Explosives) on 2, destroying all three of Gotou's newly minted artifacts.

Gotou's final two cards were a third copy of [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating) and a [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize). He cast thoughtseize and saw what could only be described as a nightmare: [Ancient Grudge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Grudge), [Anger of the Gods](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anger+of+the+Gods) and [Spell Snare](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Snare). Gotou took the [Ancient Grudge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Grudge) and fired up his [Blinkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinkmoth+Nexus) and equipped it with Plating. Ichikawa immediately flashed back his [Ancient Grudge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Grudge) to destroy not the Plating but the Nexus.

Gotou peeled a second [Blinkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinkmoth+Nexus) and halved Ichikawa's life total dropping him to 6.

Ichikawa used [Sower of Temptation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sower+of+Temptation) to provide a blocker for the [Blinkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinkmoth+Nexus). Gotou used his following turn to bait out Ichikawa's [Spell Snare](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Snare) with a [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf), allowing for him to cast [Whipflare](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whipflare) on the following turn.

Gotou allowed his [Blinkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinkmoth+Nexus) to get [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt)ed and baited out Ichikawa's [Anger of the Gods](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anger+of+the+Gods) with a [Memnite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memnite) and resolved a [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge).

When Gotou equipped the [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge) with [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20140824000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating) and attacked, Ichikawa revealed his hand full of lands and wished his opponent luck in the semifinals.

**Yuusei Gotou defeats Yuuki Ichikawa 2-0**
