[![](https://i.ormos.uk/ae/c9/aec9e06e02808fa5e79e3be7be064f47929302635b0496616d9d047ef00435a8.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615225950/http://www.wizards.com/wpn/) So you've gotten started with a few games of **Magic: The Gathering** and now you're looking for some like-minded individuals to play? The Wizards Play Network (WPN) is designed to bring **Magic** players all over the world together to pursue their common interest. The WPN offers a number of programs that provide you with great opportunities to enjoy playing **Magic** (read more about our flagship tournament series Friday Night **Magic** below!), and the best part is that it's really easy to use! 

To find a retailer in your area who offers **Magic: The Gathering**, use the **Magic** Locator on the left hand navigational bar of this webpage, making sure to click on the "Stores" option. To find out about WPN events happening in your area, you can use the same **Magic** Locator with the button set to "Events." 

So what are you waiting for? Find a location near you and take your game to the next level!

### [Friday Night Magic](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/26/f0/26f05112c19c1d38fa8c9ef439b34195c26d1cd7081bf8b83f832c6985876886.jpg)Are you looking for a way to play **Magic** while meeting new friends and winning cool foil prize cards? Then the Friday Night **Magic** (FNM) program is exactly what you're looking for! 

To make it easier to participate, Friday Night***Magic*** events are open to anyone and feature the Standard Constructed, Sealed Deck or Booster formats. Stores may also choose to run one Two-Headed Giant tournament (Standard or Sealed) each month. Keeping these formats makes it easier for new players to participate, and you only have to remember three things: Pack your Standard deck; remember where the tournament is happening, and what time it starts. You can find out more about each format [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20140415053259/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Resources.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/resources/formats-sanctioned). 

To find a retailer near you that participates in *Friday Night **Magic***, visit the [**Magic** Locator](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615225950/http://ww2.wizards.com/StoreAndEventLocator/Default.aspx) . Contact the store for participation dates, starting times, and tournament format. For more information, check out the [Friday Night ***Magic*** page](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6) !

Want to find the biggest, highest-energy WPN events possible? Look no further than your nearest WPN Premium Tournament!

Only the best WPN stores with the strongest support can offer WPN Premium Tournaments. The WPN ensures that stores provide certified judges, clear tournament rules and great prize support in order to earn the WPN Premium Tournament badge. Premium Tournament can be any rated format and at any time of the year. The only constant is that they’re big, and they’re a blast! And the WPN makes sure that you can find the Premium Tournament you’re looking for with a special link on our event locator.

Stores can also offer WPN Premium Qualifiers that lead up to the Premium Tournament. These feed the WPN Premium Tournaments with invitations or byes. This means your nearest Premium Tournament may have a whole series of great events leading up to it, making it even bigger and better! Make sure to check out each Premium Tournament’s website for details on how the event works.

Play in your local WPN Premium Tournament today!

Are you a WPN Advanced-level store that would like to put together your own WPN Premium Tournament? Click [here](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/wizards-play-network-premium-tournaments) for more information! 

### [The Wizards Play Network (WPN) for Organizers](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615225950/http://www.wizards.com/wpn/)

We've been playing games for decades and we've got a lot of great ideas how to get people to join the fun, and how to keep 'em coming back for more. In fact, we can help you get your gaming program set up, off the ground, and going strong.

Our Wizards Play Network will provide you with constant information, materials, and support—everything you need to establish and maintain a successful gaming club. And, we can help you advertise your location/club to help you attract new gamers and ensure your program's long-term success.

The best part is that it's all *FREE*. To get started, head to the [WPN Registration Site](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615225950/http://www.wizards.com/wpn/).
