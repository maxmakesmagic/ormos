## CHRONICLES PRODUCT INFORMATION

The first Magic compilation set, *Chronicles* features 116 white-bordered cards from *Legends*, *Arabian Nights*, *Antiquities*, and *The Dark*. Many of the commons had 4 pieces of art, so for collectors this set has 125 cards.

**Set Name:** *Chronicles*  
**Number of Cards:** 125

**Languages:** English, Japanese

**Official Three-Letter Code:** CHR
