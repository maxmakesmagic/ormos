As we [may have mentioned](/en/articles/arcana/2011/02-22-announcing-duel-decks-ajani-vs-bolas), *Duel Decks: Ajani vs. Nicol Bolas* will be coming out on September 2. But what we have *not* mentioned is what that will look like! So let's remedy that right now: it looks cool!

Okay, that didn't take up as much space as an Arcana is normally supposed to. Maybe instead of just mentioning its appearance, we should let you actually see it. "Show," that is, as opposed to "tell."

![Ajani's deck and Nicol Bolas's deck](https://i.ormos.uk/64/ff/64fffc47842c86f0e0b0eadc114d830052375f2913085f706739afae60842ec8.jpg "Ajani's deck and Nicol Bolas's deck") 

  

Those are the two decks. But, of course, they come contained in a handsome package:

![Duel Decks: Ajani vs. Nicol Bolas](https://i.ormos.uk/b4/fd/b4fda6f8da48d4188ff7f0d42f6c35ba52deae2832fc41d1f30156876de3aa2c.jpg "Duel Decks: Ajani vs. Nicol Bolas") 

  

Very nice! Of course, keen-eyed viewers (that's you!) have probably noticed that they can see the titular cards in there. So here, technically, are a couple of preview cards!

![Ajani Vengeant and Nicol Bolas](https://i.ormos.uk/11/8f/118ffeacd69d32a2d4cd86e3cfa74f0832b75098ff861a32be59dd79a0935b40.jpg "Ajani Vengeant and Nicol Bolas") 

  

**Deck Design and Development:** Chris Millar and Mark Gottlieb  
**Twitter Hashtag:** #AvNB
