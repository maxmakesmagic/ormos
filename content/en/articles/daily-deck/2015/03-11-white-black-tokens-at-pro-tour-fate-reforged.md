This is another old deck. Luis Scott-Vargas once took it to 2nd place at the [Standard Pro Tour in Kyoto](https://web.archive.org/web/20150311000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/ptkyo09/welcome). The deck evolved into its present form in Modern with the printing of [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20150311000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls). I remember [Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze*](https://web.archive.org/web/20150311000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/pro-tour-dragons-maze-2013) Champion Craig Wescoe loving the deck. This deck also disappeared with the appearance of [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20150311000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod), but the Czech and Slovak [Team Well Played](https://twitter.com/TeamWellPlayed) brought it to success at [Pro Tour *Fate Reforged*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-fate-reforged).

White-Black Token's strongpoint is against beatdown. The tokens start out as a bunch of 1/1s but change into a strong army with enchantments like [Intangible Virtue](https://web.archive.org/web/20150311000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Intangible+Virtue). Give the deck one turn and what started out as three 1/1s become three 2/2s or 3/3s. You'll really feel the pressure facing a huge army of tokens with an almost pointless removal spell in hand. Your attacks are negated, and the game usually ends one-sidedly in your opponent's favor.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Intangible+Virtue)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150311000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Intangible+Virtue)

I love seeing games with a 5/6 [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20150311000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) helplessly facing down four 3/3 flying Sprit tokens with vigilance. The return of [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20150311000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom) is a boost to the deck. Also, having hand disruption gives it a chance against combo decks, so there aren't really any bad matchups.
