In the same weekend he was announced as a 2010 member of the Pro Tour Hall of Fame, Brian Kibler managed to rip off a powerful performance that saw him competing in the Top 8 of Pro Tour–Amsterdam! The deck that got him there? Doran Rock, which was also played by Top 8 member Brad Nelson. Like traditional Rock decks it controls the game with removal and hand-kill while beating down with powerful creatures like Doran, the Siege Tower. As an added benefit, Treefolk Harbinger not only calls forth the 0/5, but even tutors for the only "dual" land in Extended: Murmuring Bosk! Read full coverage at [here](/en/events/2010/ptams10/rietzl-sweeps-to-victory-in-amsterdam).
