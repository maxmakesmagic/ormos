At one time a popular Extended archetype, Aggro Loam has made a comeback in Legacy. Using the powerful combination of Life from the Loam and cycling lands from *Onslaught*, the deck is able to put the hurt on both control and aggro strategies. Just don't ask them about Extirpate!
