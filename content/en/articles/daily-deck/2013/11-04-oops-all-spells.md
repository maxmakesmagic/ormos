One of the reasons that Legacy is awesome is that there are multiple manaless or near-manaless decks that see play, with this being the newest one. Dredge is the most-played manaless deck, and one- or two-land [Goblin Charbelcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Charbelcher) makes the rounds as well, but Oops, All Spells! is definitely the coolest. With the release of *Gatecrash*, two specific cards provided enough incentive to play zero lands, and both are members of the Dimir guild.

[Undercity Informer](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Undercity+Informer) and [Balustrade Spy](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balustrade+Spy) will mill your entire deck, assuming you have declined to add any lands, which may not sound great, but is actually an instant win. You end up with four [Narcomoeba](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narcomoeba)s in play, which lets you then flashback [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy) to make sure the coast is clear, and then flashback [Dread Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dread+Return) on [Angel of Glory's Rise](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel+of+Glory%27s+Rise). The Angel brings back [Azami, Lady of Scrolls](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azami%2C+Lady+of+Scrolls) (of Commander fame) and [Laboratory Maniac](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Laboratory+Maniac), after which you tap Azami and win the game. You even have extra [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy)s in case you need to put a combo piece you've drawn into the graveyard, or if your opponent has multiple relevant spells.

The deck basically just needs two things to go right: draw one of the eight Informers or Spies, and a way to make four mana (one of which is black). To that end, just about every free-mana source in the format is in the deck, including such convoluted engines as [Summoner's Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoner%27s+Pact) for [Elvish Spirit Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Spirit+Guide). The deck also has four [Street Wraith](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Street+Wraith) and four [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe), making it a fifty-two-card deck.

I like the sideboard too, as it sides into [Goblin Charbelcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Charbelcher) and [Lion's Eye Diamond](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lion%27s+Eye+Diamond), along with [Spoils of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spoils+of+the+Vault). It may seem like the same sort of thing, but the idea is to dodge graveyard hate, which most opponents will have.

This deck kills turn one a good amount of the time and has to mulligan pretty aggressively to ensure that. It has a surprising amount of mana, so keeping hands that are just a mana short probably are decent, although hands without Informer or Spy and no [Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Probe)s or [Street Wraith](https://web.archive.org/web/20131104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Street+Wraith)s don't seem great. It's definitely a different type of **Magic**, but it finished well at a recent [StarCityGames.com](http://www.starcitygames.com/) Open, and will at least give you plenty of time in between rounds!
