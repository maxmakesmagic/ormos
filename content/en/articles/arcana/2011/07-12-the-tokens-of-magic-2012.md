Did you go to the ***Magic** 2012* prerelease? Was it fun? Great! You know, the [Launch Parties](/en/events/2008/release-facts/worldwide-launch-parties) are this weekend. Unless you're reading this more than a week after it was posted. In which case, did you go to the ***Magic** 2012* Launch Parties on July 15-18? Was it fun?

Okay, never mind all that. It was supposed to smoothly segue into showing off the ***Magic** 2012* token cards, but it kind of got away from me. Let's just look at tokens!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b1/f6/b1f603c47c8ce62b00238787187b4030477f3a334a6ac1e036b53c5167038f31.jpg) 

  

This Bird token is officially created by Roc Egg, but you can use it for any 3/3 bird. We won't stop you!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/74/88/74880a177adb1b11af1e87767e1713f2f03a8d0f441a315abedc8242323cdac4.jpg) 

  

If you're looking for a soldier, you probably want Throne of Empires or Timely Reinforcements.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/14/df/14dfefb0c54cbf05a13aa9d2c0b925c8c5ced8cca3839dcf7a070228417ad664.jpg) 

  

Zombies are well-represented among ***Magic** 2012* token generators. Cemetery Reaper, Grave Titan, and Zombie Infestation are all prepared to zombie it up for you.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f6/2c/f62c1357ef52a3bb70ea92bad5fa7200479d5465e7d533d089eb47b3f415a522.jpg) 

  

Beasts! They're created by Garruk, Primal Hunter! Yeah!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/af/b1/afb159a79ac3379c3e85cb40b93f4cd9dac05729a167f0138d9beab80e79092d.jpg) 

  

Saprolings can be found hanging out with Jade Mage or buried in a Druidic Satchel.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/cd/e7/cde729d5485542d31f749d29116e673b817748645e8f049bfe4b0ace025f1280.jpg) 

  

Wurms!! They're created by Garruk, Primal Hunter! Yeah!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/02/13/0213e9eb7be3f0e375b7dc659e409f7445cded87e1020883fbde524f6f4ac129.jpg) 

  

As far as I'm aware, the only way to get a Pentavite is to use Pentavus. Although technically Volrath's Laboratory can make a 2/2 Pentavite token, so there's probably something else out there.
