ChannelFireball.com Columnist Alexander Shearer recently [took a look](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/alexander-shearer/in-development-curses/) at the new *Dark Ascension* curses and came up with a new spin on a Standard favorite: Kessig Curse Run! Featuring all of the power of the Primeval Titan-fueled ramp strategy, Alexander's deck *also* sports a curse package to attack the opponent from an entirely different angle! Now that *Dark Ascension* is on shelves, you might consider giving this deck a try at FNM tonight!

Alexander Shearer's Kessig Curse Run 

Standard
