Open to U.S. residents who are above the age of majority in their state of residence. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED. 

The winner of the **WIN GOLD** Championship tournament will win one of the prizes described below. The championship tournament will take place Sunday, December 14, 2008 at the **Magic: The Gathering** World Championship Tournament at the Memphis Cook Convention Center in Memphis, TN, USA. The format of the tournament will be *Shards of Alara* Sealed Deck. The winner of the tournament will have the option of winning a $10,000 engraved 14K gold bar or the equivalent in cash. There will be no entry fee.

This championship tournament is sponsored by Wizards of the Coast, Inc., with a principal place of business at 1600 Lind Ave. SW, Suite 400, Renton, WA 98057 ("Sponsor" or "we" or "Wizards").

*   [Complete Worlds 2008 Public Events Schedule](/en/articles/event-coverage/2008/11-12-2008-magic-world-championships-public-events)

### HOW TO PLAY

Players will need to qualify to play in this championship tournament. Players may qualify in one of two ways:

Four public events at Worlds (run December 11-13, 2008) will be designated as events which will qualify players for the **WIN GOLD** Championship tournament. The top two finishers at each of these events (top two teams from the 2HG event) will qualify to play in the "Gold" event (10 players). 

In November 2008 we will run a number of tournaments in the United States in retail stores ([click here for locations](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2008-win-gold-challenges)). The Top two players from each of these events will be qualified to play in the **WIN GOLD** Championship tournament. (number of players TBD, dependant on number of events). Stores will be solicited by the Wizards Play Network. 

LIMIT: You may only play one (1) time during each of the above described tournaments. From these events, the 10 players from the Worlds qualifier tournaments and the top two finishing players from each of the retail store tournaments with the top overall scores will be selected to participate in the championship tournament.

### PRIZES:

The winner of the **Win GOLD** tournament may choose 1 of the following prizes for which he or she is eligible. 

**Option A:**  
 ****Magic** Gold estimated value $10,000 (at the date of purchase approximately November 1, 2008)**  
 

**1 bar of 14 carat gold with the Worlds logo embossed onto it. Prize will be on site, winner must take custody of it on day of winning tournament.**  
 In order to receive this prize package, winner must be U.S. resident, over the age of eighteen. Failure to accept possession of the Gold within thirty (30) days of notification of availability may result in prize forfeiture. 

**Option B**

**Prize: $10,000 USD**. 

Limit: One prize per person. Odds of winning prize depend on number of eligible entries received and the skill of the participants. Winner may select either prize so long as the selection is made immediately upon being named a potential winner in the Championship Tournament. Potential winner may be required to sign an affidavit of eligibility and publicity and liability release.
