Forty players came to the new Academy Games in Ayer, Massachusetts for the MagicSingles.com Invitational hoping to get some of the $2500 up for grabs. Your Move Games caused plenty of heads to turn with Rob Dougherty's Verdant Succession deck, and its potential to create infinite loops of life, damage and creatures among other impressive tricks. Alex Shvartsman carried the deck to the finals, where he met Andy Stokinger, who was running white weenie- a deck completely out of the current metagame's focus. His Exalted Angels flew over the insects and elves on the ground before Shvartsman could get his combo going, making the local hero and store manager $1000 richer.
