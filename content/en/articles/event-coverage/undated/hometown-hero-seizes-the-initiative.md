## Grand Prix - Toronto 2010

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a0/16/a016c9809fe854792d6f4983082e7dde1f630025f3cd123115e371b3d5caae27.jpg)

Ottawa's own Jon Smithers is the Grand Prix–Toronto champion!

Heading in to the Top 8, all eyes were on Brad Nelson. His performance to that point this weekend had gone a long way to locking up the Player of the Year title, and the man just doesn't do a lot of losing. Even if you bucked convention and rooted for someone else, you had a lot of talent to choose from. Pro Tour veterans Eric Froehlich and Ben Stark, and two **Magic Online** ringers, David Howard and Dustin Faeder. The story on Smithers was that he was the guy who 10-0'd Day 1 with a pool full of rares. Just another guy who got a lucky pool and wouldn't last in draft.

Except he didn't crash and burn come Day 2. He posted a respectable 2-1 in the first draft, and won his next round to lock up a berth in the Top 8. In the Top 8 draft he brewed a controlling black-white deck with powerful equipment, and defeated first Howard and then Faeder. That meant that he would face the FFfreak himself in the finals. Brad Nelson was already mentally polishing the trophy after he took game 1. But Smithers dug in and managed to beat the best when it counted most.

Congratulations to Jon Smithers, Grand Prix–Toronto champion!

## Sunday, 8:34 p.m. – Top 8 Drafting with Brad Nelson

*by Brian David-Marshall*

Brad Nelson needs little introduction to anyone who finds their way to the coverage page to read these words. As he was sitting down to draft for the Top 8 of GP Toronto it was quite possible that he could all but lock up the Player of the Year title if he could select the right 42 cards from the three booster packs in front of him.

Brad opened the first pack and agonized between taking [Oxidda Scrapmelter](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oxidda+Scrapmelter) and [Necropede](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Necropede) -- a pick that had vexed him earlier in the day as well. He ultimately took the red card all the players refer to as the new Flametongue. He followed that with a second pick [Leaden Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leaden+Myr) over a couple of viable infect cards and then took [Golem Artisan](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golem+Artisan) with his third pick. [Chrome Steed](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chrome+Steed) joined Team Nelson before Brad made a pick that might be considered to be at odds with traditional pick orders. He took [Sunspear Shikari](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sunspear+Shikari) -- a card that was very good for him in his Sealed Deck pool on Day One.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d4/b4/d4b4655af8e86f808185ef344720b11cc1e3e4cddcfe1accc8b70a9dad2369bb.jpg)*Brad Nelson's First Pack*

"I think that card is like [Squadron Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squadron+Hawk) in [M10](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=M10) Limited," explained Brad after the draft. "I think if you pick them early they get better because you can build your deck around them."

A late [Trigon of Corruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trigon+of+Corruption) was mildly surprising but not as much as when the [Necropede](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Necropede) came all the way back around the table.

"This card can go in all seven of the other decks at this table and there were not seven better cards in that opening pack," said a grateful Nelson.

The second set or packs led off with [Arc Trail](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Trail) -- a slam dunk pick for him -- and he followed it up with an even more aggressively drafted [Sunspear Shikari](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sunspear+Shikari). For his third pick he flicked back and forth between a [Gold Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gold+Myr) and [Barrage Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barrage+Ogre) before finally taking the artifact.

"I would rather have a [Gold Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gold+Myr) and no [Barrage Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barrage+Ogre) than the other way around," reasoned Nelson.

It didn't actually matter since he was rewarded with a fourth pick [Barrage Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barrage+Ogre) in the very next pack. Brad had shipped a couple of packs deep in infect cards early in the draft and got paid off for putting multiple players to his left into green-black with seventh and ninth pick [Glint Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint+Hawk)s.

Brad kicked off pack three with [Contagion Clasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Contagion+Clasp) and followed it up with Razor Hippogrif. He could have taken a third [Glint Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint+Hawk) but felt that 3/3 flier was better and that he might get the [Glint Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint+Hawk) to come back around to him -- it would not. [Silver Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silver+Myr) was his third pick -- over [Snapsail Glider](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapsail+Glider) -- and then he got some more late white cards in [Myrsmith](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myrsmith) and [Glimmerpoint Stag](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glimmerpoint+Stag). One pick that stood out late in the draft was when he passed Rachet Bomb 7th. I have been in many drafts where people have taken that card first and I was surprised to see it going around so late -- and to see Brad pass on it for something like a Spellbomb.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5b/3f/5b3f3dc0aeda62bdd895ce4f3862a9c81f1ec452a13505c8274d61ed1b5a68a3.jpg)*Brad Nelson's Third Pack*

"My deck does not have any trouble with the decks that have one or two drops. It has trouble with bomb rares and against expensive bombs that card is not going to help at all."

Brad thought his deck was fine but did not like that he had no way to deal with deck that had any of the premier cards of the format. He had no idea if it called for a happy face or a nervous frown and posed for both shots after deck construction.

## Quarterfinal Feature Match – Brad Nelson vs. Pat Cox

*by Brian David-Marshall*

Brad looked happy to be sitting in the Top 8 Feature Match area for Grand Prix Toronto. And why not? He was further solidifying his lead in the Player of the Year race, had a clear grasp of the Scars of Mirrodin Limited format, and he did not have the same feelings of anxiety he experienced at his last event.

"In GP Portland I felt so alone and scared," said Brad as he sat down to play.

"What happened there?" asked his opponent.

"I didn't Top 8," said Brad with a sly grin.

"Was that like the first in ten events?"

"In six," said Brad who had made the Top 8 of multiple Grand Prix, a Pro Tour, Nationals, and the **Magic** Online Championship Series before missing the cut in Portland. "When they were getting everything together for the Top 8 I went to the judge and asked where they wanted me, if they needed me to fill anything out. I did not know what to do."

Pat Cox has been having a nice season himself although not on the scale that Brad is enjoying. He previously made the Top 8 of GP Oakland this season and is guaranteed to reach Level 3 which will qualify him to play in Worlds in Chiba. It will also let him string multiple events together since he will be qualified for Paris as a result of his Top 16 finish.

### Game one

Patrick led off with two [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain)s into [Silver Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silver+Myr) and Brad mirrored him card for card. Patrick missed his third land but played an [Iron Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Iron+Myr). Brad did not miss his land drop and played Gold and [Leaden Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leaden+Myr). Patrick had to wait a turn to make his third mana creature. Brad used his extra mana to play [Golem Artisan](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golem+Artisan) and [Sunspear Shikari](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sunspear+Shikari) only to have Patrick [Galvanic Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Blast) the Golem at the end of the turn.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/43/e1438146d2fe4244ac65b97e3735cc1adccc0415d34bf5e2c09e5f685e83d0ca.jpg)*Pat Cox*

Patrick was still working with only two lands but was able to play [Spikeshot Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spikeshot+Elder) and kill a [Silver Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silver+Myr). Brad just untapped and attacked with his dwinding squad. Pat metalcrafted out Blade-tribe [Berserk](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Berserk)ers and took a chunk out of Brad's life total. Brad managed to play [Barrage Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barrage+Ogre) but Pat flashed out [Darksteel Sentinel](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Sentinel) and untapped to [Volition Reins](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Volition+Reins) the Ogre. That was enough for Brad to reach for the sideboard.

### Game two

Pat mulliganed to start things out but played [Perilous Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Perilous+Myr) after Brad's leadoff [Silver Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silver+Myr). They traded in combat and Brad took two before playing a [Gold Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gold+Myr). Pat played a [Lumengrid Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lumengrid+Drake) with no artifacts in sight. Brad had slow rolled [Oxidda Scrapmelter](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oxidda+Scrapmelter) for the Deathmantle and even let Jon equip the Myr. He drew it out a little longer playing the [Barrage Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barrage+Ogre) instead. Jon did manage to get at least one [Sunspear Shikari](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sunspear+Shikari) and moved the Deathmantle over.

Brad leaped into action and threw the [Necropede](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Necropede) at the equipped Shikari and used the ability from the artifact creature to put a -1/-1 counter on the [Perilous Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Perilous+Myr). Jon sent them Myr damage at Brad's [Silver Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silver+Myr) and he tapped it for mana and used it to play [Scrap](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scrap)melter to destroy the Deathmantle and wipe Jon's board clear.

They played a couple of additional cards but Brad was able to mop up from that point.

### Game two

Brad reached for his sideboard and pulled out two [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain)s and swapped them for two other [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain)s in his deck while Jon was doing a little sideboarding of his own. Jon kept his hand this game while Brad thought about his Myr-light hand but opted to keep and manage to play a [Silver Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silver+Myr) on turn two after Jon's [Sunspear Shikari](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sunspear+Shikari).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3d/42/3d421e6bc64d66d2ebfc349a3551ed3a2c071eca3c685594611800d41b85b827.jpg)*Brad Nelson*

[Accorder's Shield](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Accorder%27s+Shield) came down and swung the life totals in opposite directions when the equipped creature got in for two. Brad played [Chrome Steed](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chrome+Steed) but would have to play catch up this game.

"Your start is good."

Jon played [Nim Deathmantle](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nim+Deathmantle+) and [Perilous Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Perilous+Myr). Brad played [Trigon of Corruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trigon+of+Corruption) and [Panic Spellbomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panic+Spellbomb) and now had a big Steed. Jon had [Flesh Allergy](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flesh+Allergy) and was able to wipe Brad's creatures off the board by sacrificing the [Perilous Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Perilous+Myr). Brad played [Barrage Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barrage+Ogre) but was getting ready to take four a turn when the Sunspear picked up the Deathmantle.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c9/58/c9587095dfcc7c703ff686adf409cbc329576c85970e7321a4f3a0a5a83ed0c5.jpg)*Jonathan Smithers*

Brad cycled his spellbomb and played [Vulshok Replica](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vulshok+Replica) leaving mana up for the Trigon. Brad did not let Jon gain any life. He blocked the Sunspear and threw the Replica at him. Jon went to work post-combat and was able to play two [Glint Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint+Hawk)s and pick up and replay his [Accorder's Shield](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Accorder%27s+Shield) for free each time. Finally he reequiped it to the Sunspear. Brad began whittling it down to size with his Trigon and played Razor Hippogriff returning Steed.

Brad replayed the Steed and was able to block the Sunspear with his [Chrome Steed](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chrome+Steed) and only give Jon one life per turn. The score was 31 to 9 in Jon's favor. Jon played [Kemba's Skyguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kemba%27s+Skyguard) and passed the turn. Brad shot down a [Glint Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint+Hawk) at end of turn with [Barrage Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barrage+Ogre) and his empty Trigon while Jon was tapped below Deathmantle mana.

Brad added a [Leaden Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leaden+Myr) whose sole job was to get flung by [Barrage Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barrage+Ogre) at the first opportunity, like when Jon tried to move the Shield over to [Glint Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint+Hawk). Jon had four mana up but instead chose to move the Deathmantle over to his Skyguard. He was tapped out.

Brad glared at the board before finally sending his [Chrome Steed](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chrome+Steed) and flyer into battle. After combat he used [Arc Trail](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Trail) on the Skyguard and finished it off with a tossed [Chrome Steed](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chrome+Steed). [Painsmith](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Painsmith) and [Perilous Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Perilous+Myr) were enough for Brad to finally say, "You got it"

### Game three

Brad chose to play and kept his opening hand. Jon deliberated and finally -- and grudgingly -- mulliganed.

"I need to learn not to peek."

"I always peek, it is still right to mulligan," said Brad while Jon relayed the story of the missing color of mana waiting on top of his deck.

"Would you have mulliganed this hand -- " began Jon before Brad cut him off.

"Tell me about it after the game."

"I can tell you now," shrugged Jon.

"I don't want to think about it now," said Brad as he kicked things off with [Myrsmith](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myrsmith).

"Yeah that's a good one," shrugged Jon as he played [Leaden Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leaden+Myr).

Brad played [Panic Spellbomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panic+Spellbomb), made a guy, and cycled the spellbomb to get in for two past the [Leaden Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leaden+Myr) that was barred from blocking. Jon played [Glint Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint+Hawk) and replayed [Leaden Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leaden+Myr).

Brad tapped four for Trigon and Jon sighed in relief, "I thought you were gonna [Arc Trail](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Trail) me."

"That would have been sick," Brad said wistfully.

Little did Brad know the sickness that was lurking on the other side of the table. Jon untapped and played [Skithiryx, the Blight Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skithiryx%2C+the+Blight+Dragon).

"Are you for real?" slumped Brad.

"Yeah, that was passed to me in pack two. I am not even joking."

Brad soldiered on with a [Necropede](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Necropede) making another Myr. He asked the judge for a token.

"I will just give you some and you can use them at your leisure," offered the judge.

"I don't know how long I am gonna be using them," frowned Brad. He used the Trigon to make the dragon a 3/3 and took that many poison counters. Jon played [Revoke Existence](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Revoke+Existence) to make sure there was no additional dragon shrinkage. Brad passed the turn back to a wide-eyed Jon.

"Wow, I just drew it!" he declared as he played [Livewire Lash](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Livewire+Lash), equipped the dragon, and attacked. He had exactly one white mana left for [Seize the Initiative](https://web.archive.org/web/20220526090255/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seize+the+Initiative) to get poison overkilll. The ability on the Lash let him do two infect damage when it was targeted by the white instant, it got a +2/+0 bonus from the Lash in the first place, and then an additional +1/+1 for good measure from the spell.

Just like that the hometown player who had never earned a Pro Point before this weekend had defeated the player well on the way to being the fastest player to amass 100 career Pro Points.

Congratulations to Jonathan Smithers the 2010 Grand Prix Toronto champion!
