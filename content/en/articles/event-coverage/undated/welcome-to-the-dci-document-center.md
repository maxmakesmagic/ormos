From this page, you can access the rules for playing in DCI-sanctioned tournaments. All players should review two main documents:

*   **DCI Universal Tournament Rules:** includes rules that apply to **all** DCI-sanctioned games.
*   **DCI Floor Rules:** includes rules that apply to a specific game.

DCI tournament players and judges should also be familiar with the **DCI Penalty Guidelines** -- this document explains what players and judges should do when things go wrong during a tournament.

DCI members playing in higher-level **Magic** tournaments should review the **Premier Event Invitation Policy**.

 ***Magic** Online* players should follow the tournament rules found in the help files of the ***Magic** Online* software, which differ slightly from the tournament rules for physical **Magic** tournaments.
