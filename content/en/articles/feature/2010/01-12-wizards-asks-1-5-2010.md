*What's your favorite way to win a game of **Magic**?*

![Zac Hill](https://i.ormos.uk/98/ff/98ff84c36158a8091193fe55e8d3028e6e766bb343fd8e5051e08a78fcb62548.jpg)

**Zac Hill:** I like to build an aggro deck with ridiculous range. Some people like to just kill the opponent quickly and not ask too many questions, but I like games where I don't just die immediately if my opponent can stabilize.

![Beacon of Destruction](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Beacon+of+Destruction)

  

My favorite deck I've played in a tournament was Adrian Sullivan's Johnny Walker Red in [Worlds 2007](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/historic-wins-for-peleg-swiss-at-worlds-2), because you were a mono-red deck with 32 mana sources. Frequently you'd beat people by just throwing [Beacon of Destruction](https://web.archive.org/web/20100112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Beacon+of+Destruction) at their face four times—although the single greatest moment was playing turn-four [Hostility](https://web.archive.org/web/20100112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hostility), turn-five [Molten Disaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20100112000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Molten+Disaster) as my only two non-mana-producing spells for exactly lethal against Shuhei Nakamura.
