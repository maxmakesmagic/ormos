Behind the seven unique archetypes in the Top 8 of Grand Prix Hartford are the players, from newcomers to veterans. Here are the eight players who advanced to the elimination rounds with a shot at the title.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gphar18/gphar18_mcvety.jpg)

****Name:**** Max Mcvety

**Age:** 24

**Occupation:** Mechanical Engineer

**Hometown:** Ann Arbor, MI

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Two GP Top 8s; SCG Invitational Champion

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?**

Black-Red [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One). The deck is broken. My two losses were to the mirror match.

**What was the best card in your main deck and why?**

[Faithless Looting](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faithless+Looting). It helps win both your fair and broken draws.

**Did any cards or decks surprise you?**

[Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt). I started out the tournament boarding it our a lot, but I realized that was wrong and [Lava Spike](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lava+Spike) with upside isn't so bad in this deck.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

It locks me for Silver Pro Players Club status, and shows that Oliver Tomajko and Ethan Gaeski are great [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow%C2%A0One) deck builders. Also thanks to RIW Hobbies for being a fantastic sponsor.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gphar18/gphar18_mapson.jpg)

**Name:** Michael Mapson

**Age:** 26

**Occupation:** Customer Service

**Hometown:** Phoenixville, PA

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

1 SCG Open Top 8. Two-time Game Day Champion. L2 Judge.

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?**

Amulet Titan. The deck is secretly busted. It’s got good matchups versus basically everything once you figure them out.

**What was the best card in your main deck and why?**

[Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan)—have you read that card?

**Did any cards or decks surprise you?**

[Torpor Orb](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torpor+Orb). It got played against me in both win-and-ins.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

I’ve finally cashed a Grand Prix that I wasn’t judging.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gphar18/gphar18_friedman.jpg)

**Name:** Gene Friedman

**Age:** 29

**Occupation:** Employee for Children’s Services (ACS)

**Hometown:** Queens, New York City, NY

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Won a RPTQ a few weeks ago. Many PPTQ Top 8s and StarCityGames IQ Top 8s.

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?**

Burn. It’s my favorite deck and is what I am most comfortable with.

**What was the best card in your main deck and why?**

Skull Crack. It cancels out Burn’s worst enemy: life gain.

**Did any cards or decks surprise you?**

No.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

Every. I’m going to the Pro Tour for the first time regardless of how I do in the Top 8. All my playing and hard work in the game has paid off. It’s my greatest *Magic* accomplishment to date.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gphar18/gphar18_grahn.jpg)

**Name:** Matt Grahn

**Age:** 22

**Occupation:** Student at Quinnipiac University

**Hometown:** Hebron, CT

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 9th at a PPTQ

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?** Elves, I had the cards and I’ve worked on it since the GP was announecd.

**What was the best card in your main deck and why?** [Shaman of the Pack](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shaman+of+the+Pack). It’s a nice punch to the face that helps you win.

**Did any cards or decks surprise you?** The guy I lost to in the Swiss put [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide), [Ramunap Ruins](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ramunap+Ruins) and [Hazoret the Fervent](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hazoret+the+Fervent) into his Eldrazi aggro deck.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** Not sure yet. I would’ve been happy just making Day 2. Let’s see if my *Magic* friends from QU step up their game!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gphar18/gphar18_skarren.jpg)

**Name:** Frank Skarren

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Loan Officer Assistant

**Hometown:** Merrick, NY

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** Winner at GP Charlotte in 2013 and GP Philly in 2014. Also made the Top 8 of a SCG Invitational.

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?** Affinity - I used to play Affinity a lot and I feel like it is in a good place in the meta again.

**What was the best card in your main deck and why?** Either [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating) or [Etched Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Etched+Champion). Plating is busted and Champion is an ace against all the fair decks.

**Did any cards or decks surprise you?** Not really. A lot of different decks but nothing mindblowing.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** More than I can explain. It’s been a while since I’ve had a strong finish. Getting back on the Pro Tour is huge.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gphar18/gphar18_sharp.jpg)

**Name:** John Sharp

**Age:** 23

**Occupation:** Landscaper

**Hometown:** Preston, CT

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** Top 8 of Connecticut States in 2007, Day 2 of GP Boston.

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?** Bogles because the deck is consistent at closing games.

**What was the best card in your main deck and why?** [Ethereal Armor](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ethereal+Armor), because it is far better than [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) on a bad day.

**Did any cards or decks surprise you?** Elves. The tribe put up a good fight.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** This Top 8 is a leap from unknown amatuer to the MTG Hall of Fame - here I come!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gphar18/gphar18-nass.jpg)

**Name:** Matt Nass

**Age:** 26

**Occupation:** Game designer and writer at ChannelFireball.

**Hometown:** Stanford, CA

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 9 other GP Top 8s and one Pro Tour Top 8.

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?** [Krark-Clan Ironworks](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Krark-Clan+Ironworks). It fits my style and it’s the best deck.

**What was the best card in your main deck and why?** KCI, because I need it to win.

**Did any cards or decks surprise you?** [Engineered Explosives](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engineered+Explosives) because it answers everything.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** Hopefully making the Top 25 so Andy Cooperfauss will stop making fun of me. Also points toward Worlds and Platinum.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gphar18/gphar18_pastore.jpg)

**Name:** Kellen Pastore

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Phd Candidate

**Hometown:** Maple Glen, PA

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 9th at GP Richmond

**What deck did you play this weekend and why?** Bogles. [Gryff's Boon](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gryff%27s+Boon) is very good.

**What was the best card in your main deck and why?** [Gryff's Boon](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gryff%27s+Boon). It’s a recursive threat, I always win when I play it.

**Did any cards or decks surprise you?** Bogles - it was much better than I thought.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** I get to play more *Magic,* and Ralph Betesh was right about [Gryff's Boon](https://web.archive.org/web/20180415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gryff%27s+Boon).
