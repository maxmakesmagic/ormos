Profane Command? Doran, the Siege Tower? Wilt-Leaf Liege? No, it's not Standard but Extended we're talking about! Orie Guo managed to ride a number of Standard stalwarts all the way to the Top 8 of a recent Pro Tour Qualifier for Honolulu. Of course, there were plenty of Extended staples riding the pine too, but one has to imagine there were a few surprised opponents beaten down by some of the very best from the world of Standard.
