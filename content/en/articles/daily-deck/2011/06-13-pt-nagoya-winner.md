After starting off the Pro Tour Nagoya Top 8 Booster Draft with Shrine of Loyal Legions followed by Phyrexian Ingester, David Sharfman settled into blue, picked up a few good white cards, and ultimately moved into red for Kuldotha Flamefiend, with an Into the Core and other red goodies as his reward for reading the draft correctly. Well, that, and $40,000! You can read more in the [official coverage](/en/events/2011/ptnag11/sharfman-masters-magic-weekend-nagoya).
