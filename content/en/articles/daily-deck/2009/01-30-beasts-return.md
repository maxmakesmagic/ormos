Looking for the latest in Extended innovation? James Wise managed to make it all the way to Day 2 at Grand Prix-Los Angeles with this build of Beasts. The deck was created with Kyle Sanchez, and featured interactions between old cards and new. Take Woolly Thoctar and Contested Cliffs for example. What's better than a 5/4 for three mana? How about one that can blow up all your opponent's creatures for two mana?
