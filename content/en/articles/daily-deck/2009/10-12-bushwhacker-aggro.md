Looking for a fun, new aggressive strategy in the new Standard? Kelly Reid of QuietSpeculation.com recently posted this list, starring Goblin Bushwhacker, to his blog. It features the most aggressive threats red-white has to offer (check out Cerodon Yearling finally seeing some play!) and attempts to burn an opponent to dark and crispy in a New York minute.
