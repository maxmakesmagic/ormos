It was the Jund boogeyman that took six of the Top 8 slots a the recent Grand Prix–Kuala Lumpur featuring the Standard format. But the powerful deck couldn't stand up to Ding Yuan Leong's aggressive red deck. Leong touched just enough black for Blightning and Lavaclaw Reaches, as well as some creature removal in the sideboard, and powered through the Jund menace with ease. Read more about the event in the [official coverage](/en/events/2010/gpkl10/ding-dong-jund-is-dead-in-kuala-lumpur).
