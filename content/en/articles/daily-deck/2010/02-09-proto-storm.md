In Toronto, Charles Trottier managed to find himself in the Top 8 of his local Pro Tour Qualifier for San Juan with this take on a proto-Storm combo deck. Instead of Pyromancer's Swath, Trottier opted for Pyromancer Ascension, allowing him to create numerous copies of his spells for free. Is this an Extended one-hit wonder, or will Charles's deck begin tearing up the PTQ scene?
