**Announcement Date:** September 20, 2010  
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2010

**Vintage**  
[Frantic Search](https://web.archive.org/web/20100920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frantic+Search) is no longer restricted.  
[Gush](https://web.archive.org/web/20100920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gush) is no longer restricted.

***Zendikar* Block Constructed**, **Standard, Extended, Legacy**  
 No changes

Changes to **Magic Online**–only formats are now announced monthly in the [Magic Online Community Group blog](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-gatecrash-qualifier-season-top-8-standard-decklists-43).

For the complete list of all banned and restricted cards by format, click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424103806/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=judge/resources/banned).

For an explanation of the B&R changes from R&D member Erik Lauer, click [here](/en/articles/feature/2010/09-20-explanation-of-september-20-2010-b-r-changes).
