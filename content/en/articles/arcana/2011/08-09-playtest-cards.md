Hey there! We've got some playtest cards here, and we thought it might be fun to show them to you. It's an exciting look into the process of making **Magic** cards. It's also an exciting look into Monty's desk!

These four cards came from four different sets: *Champions of Kamigawa, Eventide, Ravnica: City of Guilds,* and *Time Spiral*. Can you guess which cards these playtest cards became? Click on each card to find out.
