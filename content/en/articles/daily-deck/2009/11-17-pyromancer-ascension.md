In Tucson, Arizona Karl Goronowski came to play for the [2009 *Zendikar* Game Day](/en/events/2009/zendikargameday/zendikar-game-day-2). The deck he called upon for the task? A blue-black-red control deck with a powerful endgame of Pyromancer Ascension. By controlling the field early with spells like Lightning Bolt, Double Negative, and Earthquake, Karl was able to build counters on the Ascension. That meant late-game spells like Time Warp netted *two* additional turns, while Worldly Counsel and Ponder drew double the cards.
