This week's wallpaper features Ryan Yee's [Command Tower](https://web.archive.org/web/20130628212303/https://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/commander/cig) from [***Magic: The Gathering** Commander*](https://web.archive.org/web/20130728050637/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/commander/productinfo). Command Tower is in all five of the decks being released on June 17, with [Launch Parties](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-commander-launch-parties) going on all weekend. All the way to Monday in some locations! Enjoy.
