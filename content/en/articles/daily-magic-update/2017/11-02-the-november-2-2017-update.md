The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on November 2, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by Albuquerque. Albuquerky? Albuquercey? Albaqwerky?

# What to Watch Tomorrow

1.  [Pro Tour *Ixalan*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-ixalan-event-information) | [Twitch.tv/magic](https://go.twitch.tv/magic) | 8 a.m. PT/11 a.m. ET/3 p.m. UTC

It's here! For the first time in maybe ever (I'm not a *Magic* historian scientist!), the Pro Tour is coming deep in the season, well after the initial metagame has been set. But with so many shifts happening—Blue-Black Control is almost nowhere to be seen on *Magic Online*, tokens are taking over, [God-Pharaoh's Gift](https://web.archive.org/web/20171102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=God-Pharaoh%27s+Gift) is a real thing—it’s quite possible the Pro Tour shakes things up even more. What can the best players in the world do with a running start? Start finding out tomorrow on [Twitch.tv/magic](https://go.twitch.tv/magic).

# Today’s Must

1.  [MTG – The Worst *Magic: The Gathering* Cards Ever Printed – Episode 13!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1c0TOUya1k) | The Mana Source | Wedge

Wedge is back after a bit of a hiatus, so you can see him again, as well as his card-laden background. He celebrates—sure, "celebrates" is the word I’m going for—with Worst Cards Ever, the popular segment where Wedge complains about terrible, terrible *Magic* cards. It’s a crowd favorite, so you should be sure to constantly ask Wedge to do more of these. Like, all the time.

# Also, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [\[MTG\] Cards to Watch at Pro Tour *Ixalan*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SG31lUzcSTM) | TCGplayer | Riley Knight

Mr. Riley Knight knows a thing or two about Pro Tours, and he offers his insight along with a heaping helping of side references, puns, and pop-culture call-outs in this short, entertaining video.

1.  [Countering Common Commander Strategies | Mainboarding for your Meta](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kGgnVVtPhuM) | Jumbo Commander

For anyone with a strong Commander play group, this video is super useful and fascinating—though I’ll admit what mostly drew me in was the callbacks to cards I had forgotten existed. Things like [Dystopia](https://web.archive.org/web/20171102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dystopia), [Omen of Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20171102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Omen+of+Fire), and [Anarchy](https://web.archive.org/web/20171102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anarchy). Back when Planeswalkers were basically gods and non-allied colors really, *really* hated one another.

1.  [An Actual Pirate’s Life for Me](http://www.gatheringmagic.com/jasonalt-11022017-an-actual-pirates-life-for-me/) | Gathering Magic | Jason Alt

Jason just wants to steal all of the things. Is that so wrong?

1.  [PV’s Plays: 5 What’s the Play Solutions](https://www.channelfireball.com/articles/pvs-plays-5-whats-the-play-solutions-2/) | ChannelFireball | Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa

Go inside the mind of a Hall of Famer as he shows you the solutions to his puzzles from the other day. Beware: there are a lot of Disney songs stuck in there.

1.  [Standard Green Approach](/en/events/2022/competitive-standard-constructed-league-2017-11-01/competitive-standard-constructed-league)

People keep finding different ways to cast [Approach of the Second Sun](https://web.archive.org/web/20171102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Approach+of+the+Second+Sun). The [Hour of Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20171102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hour+of+Revelation) is particularly interesting, and picking up steam as players look to beat token-based strategies. But [Sandwurm Convergence](https://web.archive.org/web/20171102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sandwurm+Convergence)?

Hey, if you’re going to go big, you might as well go all the way big.
