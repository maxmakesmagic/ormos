Grand Prix tournaments will feature the standard "main tournament" prize schedule as well as a special "Amateur Award" prize schedule. DCI members who have 0 lifetime pro points will be eligible for the Amateur Award.

Eligible players may receive prizes from both the Amateur Division and the main tournament prize. **For example:** If the player who places first in the Grand Prix has 0 lifetime pro points, they will receive $3,900 ($2,400 + $1,500).

**Individual Grand Prix Prizes:**

*   Eight (8) invitations to the Pro Tour for which the Grand Prix qualifies.
*   $25,000 in cash awards, distributed in the following manner:

*   $17,500 to be distributed to the top 32 players in the Grand Prix
*   $7,500 to be distributed to the top 16 players in the Grand Prix with 0 lifetime pro points.

**Team Grand Prix Prizes:**

*   Four (4) invitations to the Pro Tour for which the Grand Prix qualifies.
*   $25,000 in cash awards, distributed in the following manner:

*   $20,000 to be distributed to the top 12 players in the Grand Prix
*   $5,000 to be distributed to the top 5 teams in the Grand Prix with 0 lifetime pro points.
