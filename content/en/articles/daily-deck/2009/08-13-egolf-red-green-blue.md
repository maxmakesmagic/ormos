Marlon Egolf is the 2009 Grand Prix-Boston champion! He finished the job in the Top 8 M10 draft with a spicy red-green-blue deck that featured some pretty bomby cards in the form of Ant Queen, Earthquake, and Shivan Dragon. Of course, his talented play didn't hurt! To read more about Grand Prix-Boston, visit the [official event coverage](/en/events/2009/gpbos09/egolf-wins-big-in-boston).
