With Randy posing questions about Hibernation in the base set, the question of the art for the *Urza's Saga* Hibernation came up. Why are there armadillos in it?

![Hibernation](https://i.ormos.uk/5a/09/5a0956bb89991455db97f9aab735cf962ad6d1dab6bf2f37a59672af2bf93128.jpg)

*Urza's Saga*'s design codename was "Armadillo". The art is a tribute to that codename.
