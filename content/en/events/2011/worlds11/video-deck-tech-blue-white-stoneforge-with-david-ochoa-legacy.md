US National Team member David Ochoa shows off his Legacy deck used in the three-person team rounds at Worlds. With such a huge card selection to pick from, Ochoa focused on the best blue and white cards throughout the history of the game. 

  

David Ochoa - Legacy 

2011 Worlds, U.S. National Team
