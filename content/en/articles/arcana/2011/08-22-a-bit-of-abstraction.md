Abstract art has been a part of **Magic** all the way back to [Stasis](https://web.archive.org/web/20110822000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stasis). And all the way up to the present day! So here are a few of the more abstract pieces we've seen, and we'll end with a ***Magic** 2012* card.
