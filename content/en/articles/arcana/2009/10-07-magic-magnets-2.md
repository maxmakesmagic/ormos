Do you have something made out of metal? Do you wish you had something **Magic**-related to stick to it? Well, you should have thought of that twelve years ago, when we made this magnet:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fc/91/fc91a024200e7e58ac299e73236447654380aa9e2ef4ddf7f8348dfbe20f842d.jpg)  

Okay, that's pretty old. We've done other magnets, too. For example, in 2001 and 2002, the Junior Super Series came with special **Magic**-themed sets of words that could be used with Magnetic Poetry.
