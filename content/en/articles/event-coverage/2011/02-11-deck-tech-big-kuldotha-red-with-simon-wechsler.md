Simon Wechsler of Boston MA is here at his fourth Pro Tour, and has brought with him an interesting take on Kuldotha Red. The deck has received a real shot in the arm from Mirrodin Besieged, with [Signal Pest](https://web.archive.org/web/20110211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Signal+Pest) going straight into the deck, and [Goblin Wardriver](https://web.archive.org/web/20110211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Wardriver) and [Contested War Zone](https://web.archive.org/web/20110211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Contested+War+Zone) allowing for colossal attacks. [Goblin Warchief](https://web.archive.org/web/20110211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Warchief) has typically left the deck, allowing the land count of the deck to stay lower than just about anything else in Standard.

Where Wechsler's build varies a little is with the inclusion of [Hero of Oxid Ridge](https://web.archive.org/web/20110211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero+of+Oxid+Ridge), and [Koth of the Hammer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Koth+of+the+Hammer) in the sideboard. These four-drops are very powerful in the deck, and while they do mean that [Devastating Summons](https://web.archive.org/web/20110211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devastating+Summons) is no longer a safe inclusion, they can break open matches, making up for one of the weaknesses of the deck: that it can sometimes run out of steam after the initial flurry. Entertainingly, despite the fact that his list isn't troubled by [Devastating Summons](https://web.archive.org/web/20110211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devastating+Summons), Simon is still carrying Elemental tokens with him, just to throw people off the scent. Here's the full list.
