Let's take a look at Isolation Cell from *New Phyrexia*.

  

 ![Isolation Cell](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Isolation%20Cell&options=) 

  

Now, would you like to see something neat? Here's the art, somewhat larger.

![Isolation Cell](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/729_isolationcell.jpg "Isolation Cell") 

  

Those green pods are the titular isolation cells. And, in fact, if you look carefully at the pod in the upper right, you can see a Mirran imprisoned in one. Neat!
