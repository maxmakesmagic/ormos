Faeries was the scourge of Standard when Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa used the archetype to make the Top 8 of the 2008 World Championship in Memphis. Paulo is a favorite at this year's champs in Chiba, Japan, which take place this weekend. You'll find coverage of that event on the [front page](/en/articles/event-coverage/2011/10-10-magic-weekend-san-francisco-and-2011-worlds-event-information) of this very website, and the archive of Paulo's win is [here](/en/events/2008/worlds08/malin-team-usa-crowned-kings-of-magic).
