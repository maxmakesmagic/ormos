It's only when you look at *Onslaught* blue in one big grouping that you realize just how bad it is. I feel like the Dean of any University party flick, looking at the assembled mishmash of worldwide geekdom assembled in one screw up fraternity: we accept all misfits, as long as they let us stamp them on the head with the teardrop symbol.

Blue is not without its charms. There are a few nice quality commons at or near the top, including my personal favorites: Choking Tethers and Mage's Guile. It utilizes cycling better than its four more macho counterparts, provides you with flying and offers astonishing synergy with red in forming what was until recently the unanimous choice for best archetype in the format and it has the bombs: Quicksilver Dragon, Arcanis, Mistform Skyreaver and Future Sight. These four cards, unless immediately dealt with (hard to do in any of their cases), will almost definitely win you that game and do so at a cost that is respectable given their talents.

There is a problem, though. With so little quality blue, the color really doesn't support three drafters, meaning that in booster draft, you're taking a big chance by going down this road. Now that we have a pack without Lavamancer's Skill or Sparksmith (though granted, *Legions* blue goes far deeper than *Onslaught*), the two cards blue exploits like no other color, some of the luster is gone. In other words, going blue is a die roll: just pray you don't get snake eyes.

**Quicksilver Dragon**  
 What needs to be said? It's huge, it's cheap, it's splashable…there's nothing to complain about here.

**Arcanis, the Omnipotent**  ![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/cardback.jpg)  
 It's the Dragon's single blue morph cost that gives it the edge, but the distinction is moot. Last night, I had Arcanis and Aphetto Alchemist out together. Shockingly, I won.

**Future Sight**  
 I value creatures over non-creature spells for the simple fact that the creatures do the killing, but you won't find much better in the spell department than Future Sight. If you're already blue-red, take it over ‘Smith.

**Mistform Skyreaver**  ![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/cardback.jpg)  
 A massive flyer, too large to kill with burn, with an ability that protects it from Cruel Revival. Ridiculous. 

**Callous Oppressor**  ![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/cardback.jpg)  
 This is where the ‘Smith line is drawn. If you're blue-red, take Sparksmith before these cards. That's not to say the Oppressor isn't great. It being the only cephalid in your deck is a real bonus when you have a Shapeshifter.
