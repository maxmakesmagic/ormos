Voting for [the **Magic** Invitational](https://web.archive.org/web/20070718000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/580771) is going on right now! This year's Invitational will be October 18-21 at Spiel 2007 in Essen, Germany. A new ballot opens each week, and this week **Magic** fans are choosing their favorite player from the Asia/Pacific region. All Invitational voting is being done through the Wizards of the Coast message boards, so if you don't have an account there yet, you'll need to [go sign up](https://web.archive.org/web/20070718000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/register.php). If you haven't voted yet, this is your chance.

![Magic Invitational: Vote for Asia/Pacific](https://i.ormos.uk/9c/b3/9cb39a305aca6137054cde8bf87bae99f90c60f9432f53be68c4abd637f4b67b.jpg)  
 To see the full schedule of upcoming votes (and which players are already in), head to [the Magic Invitational voting page](https://web.archive.org/web/20070718000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/580771). For this week's Asia/Pacific vote, you can read [profiles of each candidate](https://web.archive.org/web/20070718000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/580381) and then cast your vote. Results will be posted in Brian David-Marshall's next "The Week That Was" column. Make sure to keep checking back for the results and to cast your vote for the remaining slots for the 2007 Magic Invitational!
