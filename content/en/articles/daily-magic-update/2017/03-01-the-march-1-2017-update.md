The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on March 1, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by Liliana. There was much fretting over whether she would return, but *Wish Fulfillment* *Modern Masters 2017* is trying pretty hard not to let you down.

# Top 25

1.  Strasky and Larsson move on up

Just last week, Joel Larsson was on the trapdoor of the Top 25 hoping to stave off falling off.

And so he did.

Moving up four spots, Larsson is now a far more secure No. 21 in the world, while Samuel Black slips down to No. 25.

The only other big mover this week was Ondrej Strasky, who took a mighty 6-place leap to No. 13 on the back of a strong Grand Prix performance. The Czech player may be young, but he has to be feeling pretty good about his ascension up the list of the top players in the world.
