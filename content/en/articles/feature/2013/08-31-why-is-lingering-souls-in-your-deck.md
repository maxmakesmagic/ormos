During the Ironroot Challenge, The Wizard wandered the floor and asked the competitors to justify some of their choices.

In between the 2nd, and 3rd round – The Wizard found Sam Stoddard and asked him to explain why [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20130831000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls) was included in his [Angelic Accord](https://web.archive.org/web/20130831000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angelic+Accord) + [Strionic Resonator](https://web.archive.org/web/20130831000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strionic+Resonator) deck.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/mtgoccup13/interrogation.jpg)  
*The Wizard Interrogating Sam Stoddard* 

> Sam Stoddard: "Well, do you know [Bogbrew Witch](https://web.archive.org/web/20130831000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bogbrew+Witch)?"  
>  TW: "Yes, I know her personally. We have tea sometimes. It's not very good tea, but it's still tea."  
>  SS: "Well, then you know about the time when she massacred her family... then resurrected them, and massacred them again."  
>  TW: "Ah yes, of course. It was a very difficult time in her life."  
>  SS: "Well, the souls of her family still linger and haunt her to this day."
