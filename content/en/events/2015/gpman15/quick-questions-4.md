# ***What are you playing today and what is your worst matchup?***

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Lee-Shi-Tian.jpg)  
**Lee Shi Tian (Jeskai Ascendancy):** My worst matchups are the [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike) decks, such as Mono-Red and Jeskai Tempo.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Christian-Calcano.jpg)  
**Christian Calcano (Abzan Whip):** Abzan Aggro with four [Anafenza, the Foremost](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anafenza%2C+the+Foremost). The card is as brutal against me as it is against my opponents.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Andrew-Cantillana.jpg)  
**Andrew Cantillana (White Blue Heroic):** Mardu. They have so much removal, especially [Crackling Doom](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crackling+Doom).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Raymond-Tan.jpg)  
**Raymond Tan (Mardu Midrange):** I don't think I have a worst matchup but I don't like to face [Hornet Queen](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hornet+Queen).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Kuo-Tzu-Ching.jpg)  
**Tzu Ching Kuo (Abzan Aggro):** I think I'm 55-45 or 45-55 against everything, except maybe Green Black Devotion.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Makhihito-Mihara.jpg)  
**Makihito Mihara (Green Black Devotion):** I don't want to face Mono Red.
