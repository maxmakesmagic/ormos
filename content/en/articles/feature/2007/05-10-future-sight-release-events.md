On Tuesday, May 29th at 9:00 am PST, *Future Sight* goes on sale in the Magic Online store.

NOTE: *Future Sight* becomes legal in ALL Constructed formats the day it goes on sale (May 29). All casual rooms and constructed queues will use *Future Sight* starting on May 29. Constructed Premier events will not run until June 12 and will use *Future Sight* starting on that date.

*Future Sight* Release events begin Thursday, May 31 and will have 4 components:

Release Leagues  
 Premier Event Flights (2x and 4x Prizes)  
 Draft Queues  
 Release Championships (2x and 4x)

***Future Sight* Release Leagues**

SIGNUP: May 31, 9:00am – June 11, 9:00pm (the final 1 week, 2x league will start no later than 9:00pm on June 11). Release Leagues will have a maximum of 256 players each and will auto-spawn (as soon as a league reaches 256 players, another will begin registration).

LEAGUE DURATION: 1 week

PRODUCT: 1 *Time Spiral* Tournament Pack and 3 *Future Sight* Booster Packs.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 2x prizes, Participation Avatar: Jhoira of the Ghitu; Records of 5-0 get Prize Avatar: Heartwood Storyteller.

Avatars will be distributed Approx. 3 weeks after the last league concludes

*NOTE: The regularly schedule downtime will still take place on Thursday, June 7 at 8:00 am PST. Plan your league games accordingly as no compensation will be issued if you lose a game due to the servers going down for maintenance.*

***Future Sight* Draft Queues**

QUEUES BEGIN: Tuesday, June 5, 10:00 am. 3 *Future Sight* Booster Drafts will end on Monday, June 11 at 9:00pm

FORMATS: Booster Draft

PRODUCT: 3 *Future Sight* Boosters, or 1 *Time Spiral* Booster, 1 *Planar Chaos* Booster, and 1 *Future Sight* Booster. (Note: Only *Time Spiral* Block*, Future Sight,* and *9th Edition* drafts will run for the duration of the *Future Sight* Release Events)

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 4-3-2-2 and 8-4

*NOTE: The regularly schedule downtime will still take place on Thursday, June 7 at 8:00 am PST. Queues will be down during the downtime.*

***Future Sight* Premier Events (2x Flights)**

DURATION: May 31, 2:00 pm – June 11 9:00 pm (last starts at 9:00 pm). 2x Flights will have 32 players per flight and will start every ½ hour. Each flight will have a minimum of 24 players, maximum of 32 players.

PRODUCT: 1 *Time Spiral* Tournament Pack and 3 *Future Sight* Booster Packs, top 8 booster draft. The booster draft format will be 1 *Time Spiral*, 1 *Planar Chaos* Booster, and 1 *Future Sight* Booster.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product (Events that start between the hours of 10 pm and 8 am will be "Nix Tix events and require 0 event tickets)

### Important information regarding system crashes and refunds for Premier Events

If during your event the system crashes, 1 of 3 things will happen:

1.  The system will come right back up. In this case:

    *   Events will continue and additional time will be given to allow players to finish the event
    *   No Compensation will be given

2.  The system cannot recover the event and "finishes" (prematurely ends) the event, or a WOTC Tournament Coordinator must "finish" (prematurely end) the event.

    *   We will accept the prize payouts "as is" - This means that whatever prize payouts and invites the system awards, we will not change what the system does.
    *   No changes will be made to these events from what the system automatically pays out as prizes or invitations

    If the event is in TOP 8:

    *   No product or tickets will be refunded
    *   All players keep the product used to enter the event as well as the drafted cards from the TOP 8 draft.

    If the event is still in Swiss Rounds:

    *   Players can [request a refund through Customer Service](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=161).
    *   We will refund your Product and tickets.

3.  The event is cleared or cancelled by the system. If an event is cancelled this means the event can no longer be viewed in the premier event listings.

    *   The system will automatically refund your tickets.
    *   Players can [request a refund through Customer Service](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=161).
    *   We will refund your Product.
