*When a new set comes out, R&D will often bring decks to events to show off the potential of the new cards. These aren't our best FFL decks per se, but instead a glimpse into what the set has to offer. Often these decks are intentionally un-streamlined, both to keep opponents guessing and to make the deck more fun to play for 10 straight hours!*

*Ken Nagle played in the Champion Challenge at the Roanoke, VA Prerelease:*

This is a control deck with heavy creature removal and a small [Trapmaker's Snare](https://web.archive.org/web/20091008000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trapmaker%27s+Snare) package. A surprising number of difficult problems can be solved via the exile clauses on [Mindbreak Trap](https://web.archive.org/web/20091008000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindbreak+Trap) ([Bloodbraid Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20091008000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodbraid+Elf), [Banefire](https://web.archive.org/web/20091008000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banefire)) and [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20091008000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile) ([Bloodghast](https://web.archive.org/web/20091008000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodghast), [Magma Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20091008000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Phoenix)). This deck particularly showcases the debilitating [Lavaball Trap](https://web.archive.org/web/20091008000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lavaball+Trap) as a tutorable instant-speed wrecking ball that leaves [Sphinx of Lost Truths](https://web.archive.org/web/20091008000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx+of+Lost+Truths), [Baneslayer Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20091008000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baneslayer+Angel), and [Jace Beleren](https://web.archive.org/web/20091008000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace+Beleren) unscathed. Players who lean on their [Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle](https://web.archive.org/web/20091008000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut%2C+the+Molten+Pinnacle) or [Emeria, the Sky Ruin](https://web.archive.org/web/20091008000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emeria%2C+the+Sky+Ruin) for a late-game trump against slow control decks like this are in for a rude awakening via [Lavaball Trap](https://web.archive.org/web/20091008000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lavaball+Trap).
