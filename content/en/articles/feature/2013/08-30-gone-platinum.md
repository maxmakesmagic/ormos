After the Community Team got off to an early 42 to 30 point lead in *Modern Masters*, the competitors moved on to "Wacky Draft". The Wacky Draft is done using the most recent iteration of the *Magic Online* Cube and each player is randomly assigned a draft stipulation that they must follow. During the draft, if a player can make a pick that fulfills the requirements of his or her draft rule, he or she must do so. If there are no cards that fulfill the draft rule, the player can draft any card in the pack.

A full list of the rules and restrictions that the players were subject to can be [found here](/en/events/2013/mtgoccup13/wacky-draft-rules-and-restrictions).

Jan van der Vegt got randomly assigned perhaps the wackiest of the stipulations. Gone Platinum.

As a result Jan was required to "Pick the card with the name that makes the best band name". In order to ensure that these picks were not made with a bias, Jan's draft was judged by Pro Tour Historian, and band name aficionado, Brian David-Marshall.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/mtgoccup13/plat.jpg)  
*From Left to Right Greg Collins, Nate Holt, Jan van der Vegt, Brian David-Marshall, Fblthp* 

Below are some of the highlights of his draft:

*   After taking [Rancor](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rancor) first (a pick which Jan had some reservations about – as he couldn't see himself ever listening to a band called [Rancor](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rancor)) Jan had no trouble making his second pick. "[Mind's Desire](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind%27s+Desire) is clearly the best band name here... and it goes really well with my 1st pick, so..."

*   "[Genesis Wave](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genesis+Wave)... they're a new wave [Genesis](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genesis) cover band." – Jan
     "That sounds terrible." – Greg Collins

*   "[Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation), that's a pretty big band name." - Jan
     "It's not better than [Search for Tomorrow](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Search+for+Tomorrow)." - BDM

*   "[Stunted Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stunted+Growth), they're a band that's had a lot of trouble..." - Jan

*   "I like [Hand of Cruelty](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hand+of+Cruelty) as a band name. I like [Tangle Wire](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tangle+Wire) too - but [Hand of Cruelty](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hand+of+Cruelty) is the clear pick." - BDM
*   "I'm sorry - but Man-o' War is an actual band name so..." - BDM

*   "[Bloodghast](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodghast) is the best band name." - BDM
*   "Not [Augur of Bolas](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Augur+of+Bolas)? I really like [Augur of Bolas](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Augur+of+Bolas)..." – (a clearly biased) Jan
     But a little while later, Jan started to see the value of adding [Bloodghast](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodghast) to his deck
     "I can Brainfreeze myself, and get back [Bloodghast](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodghast)! We're doing it! DING!"

*   "You're going to have a hard time getting anything past [Bone Shredder](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bone+Shredder)." - BDM

*   "I like [Ninja of the Deep Hours](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ninja+of+the+Deep+Hours) as a Hipster Brooklyn Band. – Nate Holt

*   "I think it's [Old Man of the Sea](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Old+Man+of+the+Sea) or [Tundra](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tundra)." –BDM
     "Fine, I'll take the [Old Man of the Sea](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Old+Man+of+the+Sea)... My mana base is not good..." – (a now unbiased) Jan

*   "[Hellrider](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellrider) is the best band name here. There's just no way around it." - BDM
     "Alright, straight to my sideboard." - Jan

*   "[Bane of the Living](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bane+of+the+Living)!" - BDM
     "Is that better than [Wolfir Silverheart](https://web.archive.org/web/20130830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wolfir+Silverheart)?" - Jan
     "Only by a lot" - BDM

In the end, Jan wound up with a (surprisingly) solid Blue-Green-Black deck that was able to make quick work of Brian Clinton's Backdrafted deck in the first round.
