As I write this, my room is a mess, cluttered with laundry, cards, books and whatnot. I’m packing, two weeks before departing for the Pro Tour, because as I did half a year ago, I’m heading to Wisconsin to stay with the Maher family, Bob, Courtney and Brinkley (the cat), meeting up with Neil Reeves so that Courtney’s Boys can get some preparation time in for the Venice Masters and hopefully a semifinal date with Kai, Dirk and Marco.

Once I get settled there, I’ll turn this column’s focus to the revoked Masters and other issues that have arisen over the last week, but for the moment, there are unresolved issues in Wise Words: namely the *Onslaught* card rankings for both black and blue. This week, we look at the swamp in all its dark glory, hopefully clearing up a few questions along the way.

While low on the bombs, black is high on the kill and with the introduction of *Legions* to the Limited formats, that always-valued commodity is more precious now than ever before. Black’s kill is not as coveted as red’s due to the latter’s strength in *Onslaught*, and before *Legions*’ introduction, that was black’s weakness: with everyone feeling they must have one or the other when the two colors split evenly, the red decks were just going to be better than black ones.

Now though, things have changed a bit. I’m still learning, but it feels like in *Legions*, black is better than red, while all those red first picks still exist in *Onslaught* and people are more open to drafting decks in the white-blue-green realm, which means fewer black drafters. If three people draft black instead of four, that means a third more of the kill and evasion coming to you. Seems like a good thing when you get the bonus of cards like Skinthinner, Aphetto Exterminator and Corpse Harvester.

Keep all that and the increased creature counts of *Onslaught-Legions* decks in mind when perusing this list. Hopefully, it will help you with your ***Magic** Online* Prereleases and the drafts you do afterwards.
