Out of all the 1,426 players who were in the running originally, only four managed to win all nine of Saturday's rounds. And in a testament to the diversity of the current Standard format, all four played very different decks.

Jochen Frenster chose Esper Dragons, Manuel Faber picked a green devotion deck with a splash of red, Amand Dosimont decided to run Abzan Aggro, and Yohan Dudognon build his very own deck: a four-colored monstrosity featuring [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company) and so many cool things you should probably just check out the decklist yourself!
