*From* Worldwake *lead designer Kenneth Nagle:*

This is a Kor Firewalker deck that I used to play in the FFL. The goal is to play a creature with protection from red on turn two, then an Earthquake or Chain Reaction on turn three or four. The Pilgrim's Eye helps fix colors and curve into turn-four plays, while the big Angels, Dragons, and Contructs bat cleanup. An alternative to any of these cards, if you're looking for one, would be Wall of Reverence.
