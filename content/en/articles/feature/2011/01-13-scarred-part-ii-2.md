It's time to return to the adventures of [Koth of the Hammer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110113000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Koth+of+the+Hammer); [Venser, the Sojourner](https://web.archive.org/web/20110113000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Venser%2C+the+Sojourner); and [Elspeth Tirel](https://web.archive.org/web/20110113000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth+Tirel)! Venser and Koth are trekking through the Mephidross, while Elspeth has an encounter with one of the Vulshok. Follow all the action in [Scarred, Part II](https://web.archive.org/web/20110113000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/626381)!
