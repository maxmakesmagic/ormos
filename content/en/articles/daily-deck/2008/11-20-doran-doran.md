Battlegrace Angel has turned many a head after being printed in *Shards of Alara*. John Wickham used the Angel to make good at the Maine State Championships, finishing in 6th place with this black-green-white Doran deck. Running only 17 lands, he was able to cheat by playing Safewright Quest and mana Elves. That allowed him to still play many expensive cards right on time, cards like Chameleon Colossus or the aforementioned Battlegrace Angel. His end game was also shored up by Elspeth, Knight-Errant and a host of other fatties.
