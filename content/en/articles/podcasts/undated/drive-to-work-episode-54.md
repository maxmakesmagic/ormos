**Episode 54** : *Flavor Text* (10.7 MB)

My first podcast today is another [Matt Cavotta](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112125922/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Archive.aspx?author=Matt%20Cavotta) carpool episode. He and I talk about the act of making flavor text, something we both have been in charge of at one point.

Magic: The Gathering Head Designer Mark Rosewater shares stories, insights, and more while driving to work. Listen in and learn more about Magic!
