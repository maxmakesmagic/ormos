![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpmil02/929.jpg)Long time pro player from Canada Gab Tsang showed up at GP Milwaukee with an exciting new deck called Zombies Online! On the surface it appears to be a typical BUG deck, but after further inspection you notice that it doesn't run cards typical of that deck such as [Pernicious Deed](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pernicious+Deed) and [Spiritmonger](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spiritmonger). Instead, Zombies Online takes massive advantage of the madness mechanic from *Torment*. I sat down to discuss his deck build with him between rounds nine and ten.

**Ben:** How'd you do this past round?

**Gab:** I lost, but I was really mana screwed. I just couldn't draw enough lands, but it's going to happen sometime during the tournament so oh well.

**B:** Where'd you come up with the deck idea?

**G:** I'm playing **Magic** Online a lot these days, and I saw someone running a similar deck in a premier tournament. He had the usual Green/Blue but was running Black for [Zombie Infestation](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zombie+Infestation), and it seemed really good to me. So I took the specs of the deck, and changed it around a bit. I kept testing it, and I assumed there would be a lot of [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog), Trenches and Green/Blue, so I metagamed against those decks.

![Zombie Infestation](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Zombie+Infestation)

**B:** What cards did you change for that?

**G:** I took out all the Red/Green hate, like Roar (of the Wurm) and [Call of the Herd](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Call+of+the+Herd). I mean, [Call of the Herd](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Call+of+the+Herd) is really good regardless, but it's not amazing in those matches. I put in [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) to combat the control decks, and played more black and pain lands. I have 10 damage lands now, but against the control decks damage doesn't usually matter-they either will kill you all at once or they won't kill you.

**B:** How does the deck play?

**G:** It's a pretty typical Blue/Green tempo deck, but it adds Infestation. If I drop a turn two Infestation, I can start dropping turn 3 [Arrogant Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrogant+Wurm) and [Circular Logic](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Circular+Logic), and add zombies on top of that. 

**B:** And what are your best and worst matchups?

**G:** It's really good against 'Tog and Trenches. Against Blue/Green playing red for [Flametongue Kavu](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flametongue+Kavu), it's bad. Flametongue Kavu is really good in that deck.

**B:** It's you and PTR (Peter Szigeti) in day two now with the deck?

**G:** Yeah.

**B:** Tell me about the sideboard.

**G:** It's a lot of 'Tog hate.

**B:** Yeah, the [Gainsay](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gainsay)s, [Disrupt](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disrupt)s and [Spellbane Centaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellbane+Centaur)s are really good against blue in general, and they [Slay](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slay)s are decent against green decks. What are the [Unnatural Selection](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unnatural+Selection)s for?

**G:** I bring them in for the mirror match, you know, to kill other Mongrels, Wurms, and things like that. And [Merfolk Looter](https://web.archive.org/web/20020512000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merfolk+Looter)s. My deck has a really hard time killing a Looter.

**B:** So you try to put a lot of legends in play.

**G:** (smiles) Yup.
