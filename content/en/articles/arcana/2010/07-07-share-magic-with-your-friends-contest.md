What if we told you that **Magic** was making a surprise appearance in a movie this summer? Well, you'd probably point out that it's not much of a surprise anymore. And you'd be right. But that doesn't change the fact that keen-eyed viewers of *[The Sorcerer's Apprentice](https://web.archive.org/web/20100707000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Resources.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/products/sorcerersapprentice)* will see **Magic: The Gathering** on the big screen. And that brings us around to the reason we're here today: a contest!

Five winners will each receive a draft set of product for 8 friends consisting of a display of ***Magic** 2011* product (36 boosters), 8 alternate art [Jace Beleren](https://web.archive.org/web/20100707000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace+Beleren) promo cards, and 16 movie tickets for them and their friends to go see *The Sorcerer's Apprentice*. And forty second-place winners each get an alternate-art [Jace Beleren](https://web.archive.org/web/20100707000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace+Beleren) and four tickets. Not bad! So what do you have to do to win? Read on! (And then follow the instructions. You don't win just for reading)

### Share **Magic** with Your Friends Contest

**Show us your best *M11* Planeswalker deck design and win **Magic** to share with your friends!** 

On July 14th the summer blockbuster *The Sorcerer's Apprentice* opens in theaters. On July 16th, ***Magic** 2011* goes on sale. Both of these products will give you the opportunity to experience Magical conflict and cutting edge fantasy graphics. The "Share **Magic** with your Friends" contest gives you the chance to win **Magic: The Gathering** cards for your collection and tickets to see the movie! 

In the ***Magic** 2011* base set, the 5 iconic Planeswalkers – Ajani Goldmane, Jace Beleren, Liliana Vess, Chandra Nalaar, and Garruk Wildspeaker all receive 2 new signature spells that play well with the strategies carved out by their namesake. We would like you to show us deck building skills by submitting a Planeswalker themed deck. 

To enter, design a Planeswalker themed deck for one of the 5 iconic Planeswalkers using only cards released in ***Magic** 2011* (the M11 visual spoiler will go live on July 7 and all of the cards in ***Magic** 2011* will be added to the [Gatherer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100707000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Default.aspx) on July 8). Your deck design needs to consist of 60 *M11* cards (including lands) following the normal constructed deck rules (no more than 4 copies of a non-basic land card) and can include a 15 card sideboard if you choose. 

Email your deck design and any needed explanation on how it works best to contest@wizards.com by 11:59 p.m. (PDT) July 12, 2010. 

Along with your deck design and signed waiver, please provide your contact information (name, email, phone) and the store where you attend Friday Night **Magic**.\*  
 \*FNM location is optional.

Winners will be notified by July 15, 2010. Entries will be judged on creativity, uniqueness, and relevancy.

READ THE [OFFICIAL RULES](https://web.archive.org/web/20100707000000/http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/SA/ShareMagic_Rules.doc) AND FILL OUT THE [PARTICIPANT AGREEMENT](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/SA/ShareMagic_Agreement.pdf) BEFORE YOU ENTER. 

By submitting this information, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to be subject to the Official Rules. You also agree to be contacted by Wizards of the Coast and/or its representatives throughout this contest.

ALL PARTICIPANTS MUST BE AT LEAST 18 YEARS OF AGE AND BE LEGAL U.S. RESIDENTS. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED.
