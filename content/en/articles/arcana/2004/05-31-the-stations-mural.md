![Grinding Station](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Grinding+Station)

The four *Fifth Dawn* Stations, as seen in [Aaron Forsythe's article](https://web.archive.org/web/20040531000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/af16) on Friday, have art that lines up to form one long mural.

An interesting story behind the "Stations mural" is how it came to be painted by two different artists. When it was decided that the art for the Stations should connect up, there was no one artist at the time who was available to do four paintings. Steve Tappin and Greg Staples were both promised two cards from *Fifth Dawn*, and as it happened, **Magic** art director Jeremy Cranford knew they were good friends. Jeremy asked if they were willing to work together to create a giant "artifact machine," and they were game.

The original order in which they were layed out was:  
[Grinding Station](https://web.archive.org/web/20040531000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grinding+Station), [Blasting Station](https://web.archive.org/web/20040531000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blasting+Station), [Salvaging Station](https://web.archive.org/web/20040531000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Salvaging+Station), [Summoning Station](https://web.archive.org/web/20040531000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoning+Station)

![The Great Machine](https://i.ormos.uk/ef/55/ef55a5c5d58c21636eb7b2b4f4b2e6637a474c28776ceb19b4899605de4772f3.jpg)

However, to reflect that the Stations can combine very flexibly in multiple ways, the pieces are interchangeable. The two artists made sure the art lines up so well, and they kept such a similar artistic style between them, that any of the Stations can link up with any of the others in any order.
