Ever since [you voted](/en/articles/selecting10e/2006/08-23-selecting-tenth-edition-wrap-up) for Aleksi Briclot’s Story Circle sketch in Selecting *Tenth Edition*, he’s been madly crafting new art for this powerful defensive enchantment. He was generous enough to share with us several work-in-progress stages of his piece, from his first sketches to **today’s premiere** of the final *Tenth Edition* art! Aleksi even provides some comments on each stage of the art. Check it out.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0d/fb/0dfb77954390650f7f5929285cc9408c0eabbf90a1d53caaf05563927580618c.jpg)  
 Stage 1: “It was the first doodle, just trying to find an interesting composition focused on a circle.”  
  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/0d/40/0d40ffe5401a7dd72b11fae00eb5ac916b86d9aebde8bc357fa576e12bbcaa6e.jpg)  
 Stage 2: “Complete change, I wasn’t happy with the first try. Just focusing on the body gesture.”  
  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/dd/16/dd161d0effeab16020e6ae1a14a3722e604ebc131ab9ddaba8dd2abc0c0047df.jpg)  
 Stage 3: “Adding the magic effect circle.”  
  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/a6/c6/a6c6257db46b460db4a9dbe3204adf37b32c170e96b5f11aa59f9af140b408fd.jpg)  
 Stage 4: “More details and more magic effects, adding some other circles.”  
  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/68/a2/68a244e1d3cdc9f5c670999e4e5f0ef1d5b345cfb926bfebaabc250b2bf2d5f6.jpg)  
 Stage 5: “Managing colors, the most important part of the card. This was the sketch I sent for the deathmatch, for the **Magic** site.”  
  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/1a/40/1a409e574348c6c738597fe1309bbbda219c2c05721052c99450289985993fc8.jpg)  
 Stage 6: “Then I read in the thread dedicated to the contest that the card must be white. I tried to keep my first feeling; I needed darker areas to keep the different colors well separated, so I tried to avoid flashy and too-vivid effects, but also to make the magic effects pop out.”  
  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/34/74/34743c3b82598023314549fe2164b91131d36ecca184d3556fc1cd1d76c9b6df.jpg) Stage 7: “And then the final, after a lot of small or bigger adjustments.” 

* * *

Thanks Aleksi! You’ll see Story Circle in its final form when *Tenth Edition* releases, Summer 2007.
