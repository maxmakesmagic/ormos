*The Lord of the Rings* world is rich with so many great characters and locations, and the goal of [*The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth*™](https://web.archive.org/web/20230531000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earth) is to express the story and setting in cards for *Magic: The Gathering*. *The List* follows this same guiding principle, gathering 300 cards chosen to complement and accentuate that richness of *The Lord of the Rings* through *Magic*'s own deep history of cards and mechanics.

You can find cards from *The List* when opening Set Boosters. They appear about 25% of the time in the final card slot, and all rarities are represented, from common all the way up to mythic rare. You'll know these cards by the Planeswalker symbol located in the lower-left corner:

![Example of the Planeswalker symbol in the lower left corner of cards from The List](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/list_symbol.png) 

As we covered in [our first look at the set](/en/articles/announcements/2023/03-14-a-first-look-at-the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earthtm), *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth* cards will be legal in Modern, Legacy, Vintage, and Commander. This does not change the legality of cards that appear in *The List*, however. All cards in *The List* retain the format legality they normally have.

## Cards on *The List*

Here are all the cards on *The List*, broken into groups: cards that have been added to *The List*, cards that have been removed since the previous set's release, and the complete list of all cards.

*(Note: Card images may not match the versions found in* The List*. See the set identifier in the right column for the version included in* The List*.)*
