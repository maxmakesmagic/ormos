Looking at the top decks in Standard at the moment, there could hardly be any doubt that planeswalkers played a central role in the format. But which planeswalkers are actually the best? We asked a few of the more notable players in attendance at Grand Prix Manchester for their Top 3 …

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpman16/man16_qq_dickmann.jpg)

**Patrick Dickmann, Pro Tour *Oath of the Gatewatch* semifinalist:** [Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20160528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar) in first place, that's for sure. Then either [Chandra, Flamecaller](https://web.archive.org/web/20160528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Flamecaller) or [Nissa, Voice of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20160528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%2C+Voice+of+Zendikar). Chandra is more powerful in general but has utility for fewer decks.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpman16/man16_qq_larsen.jpg)

**Christoffer Larsen, three-time Grand Prix Top 8 competitor:** [Nissa, Voice of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20160528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%2C+Voice+of+Zendikar), [Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20160528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar), and [Nahiri, the Harbinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20160528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nahiri%2C+the+Harbinger). Gideon is in first place if you're on the play, Nissa is better on the draw.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpman16/man16_qq_sochurek.jpg)

**Petr Sochůrek, champion of Grand Prix Paris 2016:** [Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20160528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar), [Chandra, Flamecaller](https://web.archive.org/web/20160528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Flamecaller), and [Nissa, Voice of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20160528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%2C+Voice+of+Zendikar).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpman16/man16_qq_larsson.jpg)

**Joel Larsson, champion of Pro Tour *Magic Origins*:** [Nahiri, the Harbinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20160528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nahiri%2C+the+Harbinger) in first place, then [Nissa, Voice of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20160528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%2C+Voice+of+Zendikar) and [Ob Nixilis Reignited](https://web.archive.org/web/20160528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ob+Nixilis+Reignited). I don't like Gideon at the moment.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpman16/man16_qq_jones.jpg)

**Craig Jones, finalist at Pro Tour Honolulu 2006:** Even if [Jace, Telepath Unbound](https://web.archive.org/web/20160528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Telepath+Unbound) did count, none of the Jaces are actually good right now. [Arlinn Kord](https://web.archive.org/web/20160528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arlinn+Kord) never took off and [Nahiri, the Harbinger](https://web.archive.org/web/20160528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nahiri%2C+the+Harbinger) is better in Modern. For Standard, it's Gideon, Nissa, and Chandra, in that order.
