This week we're celebrating **Magic Online** and bringing you a new wallpaper each day. Today's wallpaper is the legendary [Black Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20121217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Lotus) from Chris Rahn. Play in the [Holiday Cube draft](/en/articles/feature/2012/11-27-magic-online-cube-holiday-2012) in the upcoming weeks to get your chance to play with this card. Enjoy.
