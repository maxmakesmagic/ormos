Okay, this might be a little self-indulgent. On the other hand, people like it when we show pictures of the Wizards of the Coast offices, so it might be fine.

The **magicthegathering.com** team (Monty Ashley, Greg Collins, Kelly Digges, Tom Jenkot, and Scott Johns) recently got moved into The Pit, which is where **Magic** R&D works. That's pretty cool, right? Here, check out our new cubes:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ae/30/ae30e4ee0c5d4f515c455db8137505060b029eb64e5875bc93eabad98756c024.jpg)  

And here's our view of the rest of the Pit:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/81/e3/81e3d33e2d177eec1bf71f8576a2a4986205d3d9ffadd8e4f7594c62c9665a33.jpg)  

One of the odd things about these new cubes is that they've been occupied for some time, and there's a lot of detritus. For some reason, the ceiling is particularly full of cards, notes, and the occasional stuffed camel:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2d/4a/2d4af486a7ac2fc3c401a8ced5a487373c1ee94b032b506d037893379399938a.jpg)  

This also means that we have our own whiteboard, which means that we can bring back important questions like [Is Gum Food?](https://web.archive.org/web/20090126000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/features/mr147_gum.jpg). Until we get that going, though, have a challenge!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8b/c0/8bc0dca4fca56e2d05f84440f3d19289b37435b943c6626f142da0a4020fa3c9.jpg)
