A*vacyn Restored* has arrived, adding new weapons to your arsenal. New spells, creatures, and planeswalkers provide new ways to conquer your foes, while miracles can turn the tables when you are on the ropes. *Avacyn Restored* release events wrap up next week, so there is no better time to start thinking about updating your constructed decks. 

Just in-case you needed a little motivation, we are running a special 'Modern Spotlight' tournament on June 9! This Modern Constructed Premier Event starts at 9:00 AM PDT and only requires 5 Event Tickets to join – half the normal entry for a Premier Event! 

If you were looking for a reason to try Modern or an excuse to join your first Premier Event, now is your chance. Check out the full event details below. 

**Modern Spotlight Tournament**  
*START TIMES:* Saturday, June 9 at 9:00 AM PDT (16:00 UTC).  
*LOCATION:* Premier Event room.  
*ENTRY OPTION(S):* 5 Event Tickets.  
*PRODUCT:* Modern format legal deck.  
*SIZE:* Determined by number of qualified players.  
*DURATION:* Number of [Swiss pairing rounds determined by attendance](http://wizards.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/465/). The "Top 8" will be 3 rounds, single elimination.
