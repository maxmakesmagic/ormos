![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpnj18/t8_nelson.jpg)

**Name:** Brad Nelson

**Age:** 32

**Occupation:** SCG Content Producer

**Hometown:** Roanoke, Virginia

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

19 Grand Prix Top 8s

3 Pro Tour Top 8s

2010 Player of the Year

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

“Stone Quarry Angels”. It's very good against Golgari and Izzet Spells.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

All of them are great. Well, except [Shock](https://web.archive.org/web/20181028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shock).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Dire Fleet Daredevil](https://web.archive.org/web/20181028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dire+Fleet+Daredevil). You’ll have to wait for my article on the deck this week at Starcitygames.com

**Did any card, deck, or interaction surprise you in the tournament? Why?**

Not really. I only played against Golgari, Izzet and Jeskai

**Regardless of your deck choice and play style, what *Ravnica* guild do you belong to in real life? Why?**

I am Legion.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpnj18/t8_sirhan.jpg)

**Name:** Nizar Sarhan

**Age:** 24

**Occupation:**

**Hometown:** Paterson, New Jersey

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Topped a Toy Wiz local

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Boros because [Militia Bugler](https://web.archive.org/web/20181028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Militia+Bugler) into [Aurelia, Exemplar of Justice](https://web.archive.org/web/20181028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aurelia%2C+Exemplar+of+Justice) is nice.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

Aurelia, because it’s the highest quality creature card in Standard.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Bounty Agent](https://web.archive.org/web/20181028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bounty+Agent), because it kills [Lyra Dawnbringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20181028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lyra+Dawnbringer).

**Did any card, deck, or interaction surprise you in the tournament? Why?**

[Dive Down](https://web.archive.org/web/20181028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dive+Down) from Oliver Tomajko, got blown out when I tried to Lave Coil his [Crackling Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20181028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crackling+Drake)

**Regardless of your deck choice and play style, what *Ravnica* guild do you belong to in real life? Why?**

Boros, because I’m assertive and composed.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpnj18/t8_kulik.jpg)

**Name:** Lirek Kulik

**Age:** 23

**Occupation:** Quantitative Teacher

**Hometown:** New York

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Hmm... Top 8 PTQ?

17th at Grand Prix Atlanta 2015

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Jeskai — seemed like [Deafening Clarion](https://web.archive.org/web/20181028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deafening+Clarion) is really strong and [Teferi, Hero of Dominaria](https://web.archive.org/web/20181028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%2C+Hero+of+Dominaria) is still one of the most powerful things to do.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Deafening Clarion](https://web.archive.org/web/20181028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deafening+Clarion). Three mana [Wrath of God](https://web.archive.org/web/20181028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wrath+of+God). That gains 10 life if you have a Drake in play.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

My sideboard kind of sucks. [Lava Coil](https://web.archive.org/web/20181028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lava+Coil) is often relevant.

**Did any card, deck, or interaction surprise you in the tournament? Why?**

I was surprised Boros Angels is good.

**Regardless of your deck choice and play style, what *Ravnica* guild do you belong to in real life? Why?**

Dimir. And Azorius.
