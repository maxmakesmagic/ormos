The art description for the *Stronghold* uncommon [Flame Wave](https://web.archive.org/web/20020612000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flame+Wave) reads: "Show a literal wave of flame that travels down the exterior of Volrath’s mountain. The Vec soldiers advancing up the mountainside are being fried by the oncoming wave. They don’t have a chance to escape."

Artist Donato Giancola decided one lucky soldier had the means to escape: a surfboard. Hidden in the inferno is a lone surfer hanging 10 on the fire. Duuuuuude!
