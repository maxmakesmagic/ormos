### Get on the battlefield - Open to all !!

Grand Prix are the most exciting events the DCI offers. They're high-energy and held around the world.  
  
 The main event is all about competition, but there are also plenty of other reasons to come. Grand Prix’s in Europe are true **Magic** events. Besides the main tournaments, there’s plenty of excitement throughout the weekend that you can enjoy… Side events, **Magic** artists on site to sign your **Magic** cards, Gunslinging Game Play against the pro’s, and lots more…  
  

* * *

  

### Tournament Format And Schedule [![](https://i.ormos.uk/92/12/92123813650397c32cf6c9c091f43728edd511c37f19fee30bcfdbf932261304.gif)](<#GP Factsheet>)

*   **Tournament Format**: Sealed deck with Day 2 boosterdraft (Top 64/128)
*   **Participation Fee**: € 30  

*   **Registration**:
    **Friday 17 October**: from 5:30 to 8:00 P.M.
    **Saturday 18 October**: from 8:00 to 9:00 A.M.
*   **Tournament begins**: **09:00 A.M**
*   **Sunday 19 October**: The top 64 (or 128) players begin play at 8:00 A.M.

The main tournament at Grand Prix–Paris is open to **Magic** players of all ages.  

*   **Tournament Structure**

    *   Modified Swiss-style (50-minute rounds).
    *   Rules Enforcement Level (REL) is Competitive (day1) and Professional (day2)
    *   The Top 64 players (or 128 if 800 or more players registered for the main event) advance to the second day of competition for prizes and invitations.
    *   In the event that there are more than 2000 registered players, tie breaks will reset after day 1.
    *   The top 16 Players will receive invitations to the 1st Pro Tour in 2009
*   **Tournament Divisions and Prizes**
    All prizes are in U.S. Dollars.

**Tournament Byes** *(Byes are not cumulative throughout the season or year)*  
 Byes will be given to players in the Grand Prix based on the following criteria:

 ***Third-Round Byes*** 

*   Players with a [Players Club](/en/articles/feature/2005/05-02-welcome-to-the-pro-players-club) level of 4 or higher
*   DCI players with a Limited rating of 2000 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI Limited ratings published on 15/10/2008.

 ***Second-Round Byes*** 

*   Players with a [Players Club](/en/articles/feature/2005/05-02-welcome-to-the-pro-players-club) level of 2 or 3
*   DCI players with a Limited rating of 1900 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI Limited ratings published on 15/10/2008.

 ***First-Round Byes*** 

*   Players with a [Players Club](/en/articles/feature/2005/05-02-welcome-to-the-pro-players-club) level of 1
*   DCI players with a Limited rating of 1800 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI Limited ratings published on 15/10/2008.

 ***Trial winners:*** 

People who win a Trial event are awarded 3 byes in the Grand Prix that it feeds. Trial byes are not cumulative with other byes and do not pass down.

### Travel Information: [![](https://i.ormos.uk/92/12/92123813650397c32cf6c9c091f43728edd511c37f19fee30bcfdbf932261304.gif)](<#GP Factsheet>)

WotC has negotiated the highly reduced rate of 119€ per room + 1.10€ taxes per person. (Not per player) to guarantee all “Magic, the gathering” players the best, cheapest accommodation you can find in the park.  
  
 Download the [special form](https://media.ormos.uk/04/76/0476925075bc808e9e8fd8a743b9fdb0f5b5b78507c4ebbc43ac9a33c061cb8f.pdf) and fax it to the group reservations department at 00 33 1 60 45 73 98.  
 You will receive a letter in return from the reservations department confirming your booking. Calling the normal reservations department will only insure you higher prices than the ones negotiated by WotC.  
  
**[HOW TO REACH](https://web.archive.org/web/20130630064229/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.drpbusiness.com/uk/pdf/Acces_DLRP_an.pdf&origin=)**  
  
**TRAVEL BY CAR:**  
 Free parking is availible on the parking of the Newport Bay Club.  
  
**Public Transport:** Val d'Europe Shuttles ([VEA](https://web.archive.org/web/20130630064229/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.vea.fr&origin=)) insure connections between the 2 airports and Disneyland Resort Paris.  
 Free shuttle service is present between the TGV train station at Marne la Vallée / Chessy and the Disney parc hotels.  
  
  
  
**THINGS TO [SEE/DO](https://web.archive.org/web/20130630064229/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.drpbusiness.com/uk/map_park.php&origin=):**  
 The tournament location is located 5 minutes from the entrance of the park. Within walking distance you will find restaurants, bars, pubs and an numerous variety of fantastic entertainment possibilities.
