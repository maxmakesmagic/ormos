Welcome back once to again to Sketches! Today's card is ***Magic** 2011*'s [Greater Basilisk](https://web.archive.org/web/20100727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Greater+Basilisk), the latest in a line of [basilisks](https://web.archive.org/web/20100727000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?%20subtype=+%5Bbasilisk%5D) that stretches all the way back to [Thicket Basilisk](https://web.archive.org/web/20100727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thicket+Basilisk) at the dawn of time.

### Artist Portfolio

Artist [James Ryman](https://web.archive.org/web/20100727000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&artist=%5B%22James%20Ryman%22%5D) is a relative newcomer to **Magic** illustration, but he's not taking it slow: He started with [Lightkeeper of Emeria](https://web.archive.org/web/20100727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightkeeper+of+Emeria) in *Worldwake*, and as of ***Magic** 2011*, he's got nine cards to his credit.

### Art Description

The first step of the creation of a card's art is its art description. The art description tells the artist what the card will do, what its flavor is, and what the mood of the illustration should be. Here were the instructions James was given for this latest, greatest Basilisk:

> Color: Green creature  
>  Location: Forest  
>  Action: Show a basilisk, a six-legged lizard with a deadly gaze. I want the halo death screen here... we see the basalisk in a very dutched camera shot, as if we are laying dead on our side viewing it. the viewer's own petrified hand can be seen on the ground in frame.  
>  Focus: The basilisk  
>  Mood: One look, and you die.

### Sketch

With that art description to work with, James submitted a sketch to **Magic**'s creative team:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e2/07/e20738e024448e5873c5fc55328a7adb5b6bdb58c0163e37edea02aee4ca5f5f.jpg)

[Greater Basilisk](https://web.archive.org/web/20100727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Greater+Basilisk) | Sketch by [James Ryman](https://web.archive.org/web/20100727000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5B%22James%20Ryman%22%5D)

The creative team thought the composition was great, and suggested a little smoke around the basilisk and a stonier texture on the hand in the foreground. These details would help drive it home that you, the viewer, are now a piece in a basilisk's statuary.
