Izzvergg was the *Masters Edition 2* champion on **Magic Online** last week, piloting this particular draft special through a tough field to emerge victorious. *Masters Edition 2*, released exclusively for use on **Magic Online**, brings a number of the game's most famous and most cherished cards to the world of digital **Magic**, most for the first time ever. It also creates an interesting Limited format allowing Whirling Catapult, Combat Medic, and Ironclaw Orcs to do battle side-by-side for the first time in **Magic**'s history!

Adding to the excitement of new releases on **Magic Online** comes this week's release of *Shards of Alara* for digital play! Casual drafts are happening right now with Release Events just around the corner, and you can join in the fun. Visit [the **Magic Online** page](https://web.archive.org/web/20090511064658/http://www.wizards.com/magic/digital/magiconline.aspx) or try [this official announcement](/en/articles/magiconline/2008/10-13-shards-of-alara-release-events) for more details.
