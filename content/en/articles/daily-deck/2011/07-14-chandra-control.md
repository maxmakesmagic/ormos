With the new Chandra, the Firebrand available with the printing of ***Magic** 2012*, players around the world are trying to come up with a way to abuse the powerful new card. Elmo the Elk may have done it. This take on a Mono-Red Control deck uses Chandra to copy the powerful Geosurge. From there you have plenty of mana to cast your fatties much faster than you should be able to. Koth of the Hammer will let you double up your mana production as well.
