## *Ravnica Allegiance* MTGO Release

*Ravnica Allegiance* hits *Magic Online* this Thursday, January 17, before your local prerelease! You can start Sealed play the day it arrives, while Draft play starts Friday, January 18. Represent your guild proudly, and get ready for all sorts of exciting changes with the coming of the new set.

For more information, check out the latest updates in Alli's release article [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2019/01-11-ravnica-allegiance-magic-online-edition).

* * *

## Vintage Cube Ends Jan. 17

In the mood for some Vintage Cube? Now's your last chance to get in on the classic action before the release of *Ravnica Allegiance* on MTGO! It's nothing like any other cube out there, and it's overflowing with iconic cards from *Magic*'s entire 25-year history. Vintage Cube ends January 17, so try it out today!

See the full card list and the most recent changes [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/2016/11-30-vintage-cube-cardlist).

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format challenges are the first step toward qualifying for premier play through your favorite *Magic* formats. To learn more about Format Focus events and premier play qualification, click [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2018/11-12-2019-magic-online-championship).

The *Magic Online* Format Challenges continue every weekend for Modern, Vintage, Legacy, and Pauper formats.

## Downtime January 16

The next downtime will be January 16, 3 a.m.–noon PT/-8 from UTC. Please note that there are no more 'extended' or 'normal' downtimes; in the new world with fewer downtimes, they're all the same length of time.
