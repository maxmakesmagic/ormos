Monday, February 28 9:00 am PST, *Betrayers of Kamigawa* goes on sale in the MTG:O store.

NOTE: *Betrayers of Kamigawa* becomes legal in ALL Constructed formats the day it goes on sale (February 28). All casual rooms and constructed queues will use *Betrayers of Kamigawa* starting on February 28. Constructed Premier events will not run until March 8 and will use *Betrayers of Kamigawa* starting on that date.

*Betrayers of Kamigawa* Release events begin Thursday, March 3 and will have 4 components:

Release Leagues  
 Draft Queues  
 Premier Event Flights (2x and 4x Prizes)  
 Release Championship

Please note: *Betrayers of Kamigawa* Release Events are "Special" events. Per the BOT Policy, **no** automated trade BOTs will be allowed on the system for the duration of these events (March 3, 9 AM through March 8, 9 AM).

***Betrayers of Kamigawa* Release Leagues**

SIGNUP: March 3, 9:00am – March 8, 9:00am (the final 1 week, 2x league will start no later than 9AM on March 8). Release Leagues will have a maximum of 256 players each and will auto-spawn (as soon as a league reaches 256 players, another will begin registration).

LEAGUE DURATION: 1 week

PRODUCT: CHK Tournament Pack and 3 BOK Booster packs.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 2x prizes, Participation Avatar: Higure, the Still Wind; Records of 5-0 get Prize Avatar: Ink-Eyes, Servant of Oni. 

Avatars will be distributed Approx. 3 weeks after the last league concludes

***Betrayers of Kamigawa* Draft Queues**

QUEUES BEGIN: March 3, 3:00 pm

FORMATS: Booster Draft

PRODUCT: 3 BOK Boosters or 2 CHK Boosters and 1 BOK Booster.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 4-3-2-2, 8-4

***Betrayers of Kamigawa* Premier Events (2x Flights)**

DURATION: March 4 9:00 am – March 8 9:00 am (last starts at 9am). 2x Flights will have 32 players per flight and will auto-spawn (as soon as a 32 person flight begin, another will begin signing up and will start when full) 

PRODUCT: CHK Tournament Pack + 3 BOK Boosters Sealed deck, top 8 booster draft. The booster draft format will be 2 CHK Boosters and 1 BOK Booster.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 2x Prizes, Top 8

The winner of the Championship will also receive the framed, original artwork\* for the *Betrayers of Kamigawa* card Ink-Eyes, Servant of Oni by artist Wayne Reynolds.

In order to remain eligible for Prizes, all qualified players must email molworldsqt@wizards.com by Wedensday, March 17, 2005 at 12:00 PM PST with the following information: Full Legal Name, Full Address, and Telephone number. Player must put their MTG:O Username as the subject of the email. (Account passwords will not be requested--never give your password to anyone.) In addition, during the tournament, Wizards of the Coast staff (marked with the Wizards logo beside their online name) may ask players for their name and/or contact information. Do not give out your information to anyone who is not a Wizards of the Coast Staff (marked with the Wizards logo beside their online name).

Failure to provide accurate information may result in significant penalties, including suspension from online **Magic**tournaments.

**Special Note regarding Invitations to the Championship and System Crashes:**

If during a crash, an event is "finished" (prematurely ended) by the system or by a WotC representative, the system should award prizes and/or invites based on the standings at the time they were finished. We are going accept the prize payouts and invites "as is". No changes will be made to these events from what the system automatically pays out as prizes or awards invitations. If the tournament is still in the Swiss portion at the time it is "finished", Product and tickets will be refunded (use the link below to request a refund). If the event is in the Top 8 playoffs at the time it is "finished", no product or tickets will be refunded

If an event is cancelled by the system, tickets should be refunded auomatically. We will not recreate these event, award prizes or invites for these events. The records of cancelled events are erased and it is not possible to reconstruct such events. In the case of a cancelled event, if you did not receive the system ticket refund or if you need to request a product refund, please [contact Customer Service](https://web.archive.org/web/20050829023002/http://www.wizards.com/magiconline/servercrash.asp).
