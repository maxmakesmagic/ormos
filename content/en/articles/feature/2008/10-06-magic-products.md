Wizards of the Coast has released over 40 **Magic: The Gathering** expansion sets and 10 versions of the base game. On these card set pages you can find basic product information, card lists, strategies, and a variety of other useful facts.

Looking for checklists or spoilers? You can generate them for any set with [Gatherer](https://web.archive.org/web/20081006000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Gatherer/Index.aspx). For set FAQs, see the **[Magic Set FAQs page](https://web.archive.org/web/20081006000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/rules/faqs)**.
