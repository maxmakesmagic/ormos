*   [**Coverage of the 2013 World Championship**](/en/events/2013/wc13/youth-served-in-a-flash)

The 2013 **Magic** World Championship showcases the best players in the world battling against one another for the title of World Champion. Sixteen players will be invited to compete across three formats for the title and $40,000 first prize, with a total cash purse exceeding $100,000. In addition to the prize money, players earn one Pro Point for each match win during the first two days and two points per match win on the final day.

The 2013 **Magic** World Championship takes place on July 31, August 1, and August 4 in Amsterdam, with the 2013 [World **Magic** Cup](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2013-world-magic-cup) being run August 2-4. All five days will be live streamed.

*   [**2013 Magic World Championship Competitors**](/en/events/2013/2013/2013-magic-world-championship)

### Event Coverage

The live video webcast for the 2013 **Magic** World Championship starts Wednesday, July 31. The live video stream can be found at [wizards.com/webcast](/en/events/2004/webcast/magic-the-gathering-webcast-schedule) and [twitch.tv/magic](http://www.twitch.tv/magic). Check out the schedule below to find the start time in your area (start times approximate). Sunday's live stream features the Top 8 of the World **Magic** Cup, followed by the Top 4 of the World Championship.

**Day 1 (Wednesday, July 31)**  
 

*   *Modern Masters* Booster Draft – 3 Swiss rounds.
*   [Standard](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219083410/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Resources.aspx?x=judge/resources/sfrstandard) constructed – 3 Swiss rounds.

**Day 2 (Thursday, August 1)**

*   ***Magic** 2014* Booster Draft – 3 Swiss rounds.
*   [Modern](https://web.archive.org/web/20140410124645/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Resources.aspx?x=judge/resources/sfrmodern) constructed – 3 Swiss rounds.

**Advancement to Playoff**  
 

*   All players may participate in all 12 Swiss rounds of Wednesday and Thursday's competition.
*   The top 4 players after round 12 will advance to Sunday's Playoffs.

**Day 3 (Sunday, August 4) - Playoffs**  
 

*   Playoff matches will be Modern constructed format.
*   Playoff matches will be single-elimination.
*   Playoff matches will be best 3 of 5.
*   Where necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will still be used to determine final order in the standings.

**Round Times** 

*   All rounds during days 1 and 2 will be 55 minutes per round.
*   Day 3 single-elimination payoff matches have no time limit; however, players are expected to play at a reasonable pace and finish their matches within 90 minutes.

**Other Tournament Information**  
 

*   Planeswalker Points multiplier: 12x (Lifetime only), REL Professional.
*   Players are required to use the same Modern constructed deck for Day 1, Day 2, and Playoffs.
*   The [**Magic: The Gathering** Tournament Rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424172608/http://www.wizards.com/wpn/Document.aspx?x=Magic_The_Gathering_Tournament_Rules) and the [**Magic: The Gathering** Infraction Procedure Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424172608/http://www.wizards.com/wpn/Document.aspx?x=Magic_The_Gathering_Infraction_Procedure_Guide) are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Fact Sheet.
