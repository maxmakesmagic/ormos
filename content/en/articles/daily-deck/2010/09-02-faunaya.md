One of three national championships to conclude last weekend, the Great Britain National Championship determined Joseph Jackson would lead the nation to the 2010 World Championships. Jackson's deck of choice was the popular Naya strategy that benefits from the interaction between Fauna Shaman and Vengevine. Read more about Great Britain Nationals [here](/en/events/2010/gbnat10/great-scot-jackson-wins-great-britain-nationals).
