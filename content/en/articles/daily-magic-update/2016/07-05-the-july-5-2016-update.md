The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on July 5, 2016. Today's Update is brought to you by Zombies, Liliana, and the final week of *Eldritch Moon* previews. We're nearly to the end, but we've still got plenty of strong, flavorful, and just plain awesome cards left to show off. The full set will be shown off at 8 a.m. PT this Friday in the [Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2016/07-08-eldritch-moon).

# Today's Previews

Throughout preview season, I'll use this space to highlight the day's previews that can't be found on DailyMTG. Worried you might miss something? We'll make sure to have it here.

1.  [Exclusive *Eldritch Moon* Preview—Grim Flayer](http://manadeprived.com/exclusive-eldritch-moon-preview-grim-flayer/) | Mana Deprived | Kar Yung Tom and Jay Lansdaal

I'm going to be honest here—I had no idea Mana Deprived had previewed [Rakshasa Deathdealer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Deathdealer). Sometimes I'm really good at creating this kind of symmetry between preview seasons. This one was just a happy little accident.

1.  [Breaking the Crypt in Standard and Commander](http://www.mtggoldfish.com/articles/breaking-the-crypt-in-standard-and-commander) | MTGGoldfish | SaffronOlive

Zombies get a kick-start with this one-drop previewed at the drop of midnight last night.

1.  [Selfless Spirit](http://emn.bigmagic.net/) | BigMagic.net (Japanese)

Japan lives in the future, so if you've been paying attention, you've likely already seen this. But it's a pretty exciting Spirit, so it's certainly worth calling out. If you can't read Japanese (for shame), you can get a pretty accurate translation on the [Reddit thread discussing it](https://www.reddit.com/r/magicTCG/comments/4r7l1i/emn_translation_for_spoiler/).

1.  [Exclusive LR *Eldritch Moon* Preview Cards](http://lrcast.com/exclusive-lr-eldritch-moon-preview-cards/) | Limited Resources | Marshall Sutcliffe

Four new uncommons to set you on your way in Draft. I wonder if Marshall has opinions on them?

1.  One more to go today...

That's everything for today (well, everything you haven't already seen on [DailyMTG.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718094425/http://magic.wizards.com/en/articles)), but [Mark Rosewater](https://twitter.com/maro254) has a Twitter preview for you today. Chances are good he'll make you work for it. #whereismypreviewcard

# Decks of the Day

1.  [Mark Nestico's New Pet Card](http://www.starcitygames.com/article/33219_My-New-Pet-Card.html)

Today's card to brew with is Harmless Offering, which Mark Nestico has grabbed on to and started running with. The new red version of [Donate](https://web.archive.org/web/20160705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Donate) enables all kinds of wacky and not-so-wacky brews.
