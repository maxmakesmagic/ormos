Invitations to Pro Tour-Charleston are awarded in the following manner:

### Pro Players Club

*   *Any team of three players whose members' Pro Player Club levels equal 7 or greater. Teams formed in this manner must be announced at player registration before the tournament.*

Players can view their Pro Club level [here](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/2006-player-of-the-year-race). Add the Pro Players Club levels of the 3 players together. If the total is 7 or more, the team is invited to Pro Tour-Charleston.

**Example**: Player A has a Pro Players Club level of 4, Player B has a Pro Players Club level of 2, Player C has a Pro Players Club level of 1. The total of these levels is 7. This team is invited to Pro Tour-Charleston.

If you believe there is an error with your Pro Points or Pro Club Level, please contact Andy Heckt at andy.heckt@wizards.com

### Constructed Rating

*   *Any team of three players whose members' DCI Constructed rating average is 2000 or higher.*

Players can view their Constructed Rating [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20060828131430/http://webapp.wizards.com/ratings.aspx). Enter your DCI number in the box in the "Personal Rankings" section and click "Search Member." You will need to do this for each team member.

![](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/tournamentcenter/2006/constructedexample.jpg)  

The Constructed Rating that was published on May 3 is used. Add the ratings of the 3 players together and divide the total by 3 (round up). If the result is 2000 or higher, the team is invited to Pro Tour-Charleston.

**Example**: Player A has a Constructed Rating of 2174, Player B has a Constructed Rating of 1955, Player C has a Constructed Rating of 1999. The total of these ratings is 6128. This total divided by 3 (rounded up) is 2043. Since the result is 2000 or higher, this team is invited to Pro Tour Charleston.

### Individual Team Constructed Rating

*   *Any team of three players whose members' DCI Individual Team Constructed rating average is 1700 or higher.*

Players can view their Constructed Rating [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20060828131430/http://webapp.wizards.com/ratings.aspx). Enter your DCI number in the box in the "Personal Rankings" section and click "Search Member." You will need to do this for each team member.

![](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/tournamentcenter/2006/teamconstructedexample.jpg)  

The Individual Team Constructed Rating that was published on May 3 is used. Add the ratings of the 3 players together and divide the total by 3 (round up). If the result is 1700 or higher, the team is invited to Pro Tour-Charleston.

**Example**: Player A has an Individual Team Constructed Rating of 1755, Player B has an Individual Team Constructed Rating of 1675, Player C has an Individual Team Constructed Rating of 1702. The total of these ratings is 5132. This total divided by 3 (rounded up) is 1711. Since the result is 1700 or higher, this team is invited to Pro Tour Charleston.
