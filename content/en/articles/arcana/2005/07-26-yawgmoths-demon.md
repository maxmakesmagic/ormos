Remember the symbol of Phyrexia? We mentioned in an [earlier Arcana](https://web.archive.org/web/20050726000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/symbol-phyrexia-2004-02-16) how it crops up in the art of cards like [Order of Yawgmoth](https://web.archive.org/web/20050726000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Order+of+Yawgmoth) and [Yawgmoth's Edict](https://web.archive.org/web/20050726000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yawgmoth%27s+Edict), and looks a little something like this:

![The symbol of Phyrexia](https://i.ormos.uk/52/02/5202c099ba339c4b8c355e83bf63e36506a9e2dc7c6b6f8f0987a631b22c4941.jpg)

[Yawgmoth Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20050726000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yawgmoth+Demon) follows suit in its Pete Venters-illustrated *Ninth Edition* printing. Check it out.

[Yawgmoth Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20050726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Yawgmoth%2BDemon)

Its head bears the mark of Phyrexia, and its staff has the circle-and-bar design as well. But of course, you don't have to be Phyrexian to order this artifact-hungry demon around. Just open a pack of *Ninth*. 

![Yawgmoth Demon](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Yawgmoth+Demon)
