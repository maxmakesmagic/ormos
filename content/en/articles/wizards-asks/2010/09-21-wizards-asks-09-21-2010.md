*What card from the [Visual Spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20101129082738/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/scarsofmirrodin/spoiler) are you most hoping to open at the Prerelease?* 

**Ken Nagle:** I'm most excited to open Argentum Armor, mostly because it's my submitted design.

  

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/tcg/products/scarsofmirrodin/9j39nwg1ye_en.jpg) 

  

To me, nothing is more "Mirrodin" than Equipment, and I felt *Scars of Mirrodin* needed the "biggest Equipment ever," both for my Godo EDH deck and for shenanigans with Stoneforge Mystic / Stonehewer Giant (again, my designs).
