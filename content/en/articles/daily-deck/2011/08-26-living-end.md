The first results from the Modern format on **Magic Online** are in! User superchibi put in a 4-0 performance with a Modern take on the Living End deck. Cycle and sacrifice until your graveyard is full of creatures, then cascade into Living End, simultaneously getting rid of your opponent's creatures and flopping most of your graveyard onto the battlefield. For more Modern deck lists, check out [the **Magic Online** page](https://web.archive.org/web/20110818023522/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Digital/magiconline.aspx?x=mtg/digital/magiconline/whatshappening)!
