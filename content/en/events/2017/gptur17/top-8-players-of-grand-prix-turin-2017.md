After fifteen rounds of Standard, with a total of 918 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

* * *

### Simon Nielsen

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gptur17/t8_nielsen.jpg)

**Age:** 23

**Occupation:** Former Linguistics student

**Hometown:** Slagelse, Denmark

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Gold pro by exactsies two years in a row, three Grand Prix Top 8s, Pro Tour Sydney Top 16. Also, didn't sleep at all between Saturday and Sunday.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Jeskai Gift. I spent the week between GPs in lovely Annecy, France, without Internet. So with no tech to rely on, I just chose what I knew best. Deck is good, just not well positioned.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

Can I say [God-Pharaoh's Gift](https://web.archive.org/web/20170903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=God-Pharaoh%27s+Gift) bringing back [Angel of Invention](https://web.archive.org/web/20170903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel+of+Invention)? On turn four. Every game.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

I honestly didn't really use my sideboard. Nine of the cards I never brought in.

### Andrea Sonzogni

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gptur17/t8_sonzogni.jpg)

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Plumber

**Hometown:** Vercelli, Italy

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

\[This space intentionally left blank.\]

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

I chose White-Blue Approach because it has a great matchup versus Temur and other energy decks.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Approach of the Second Sun](https://web.archive.org/web/20170903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Approach+of+the+Second+Sun). Because it makes you win the game second time you cast it.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

The best card in my sideboard is [Regal Caracal](https://web.archive.org/web/20170903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Regal+Caracal) because it gains a lot of advantage versus aggro decks.

### Robin Dolar

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gptur17/t8_dolar.jpg)

**Age:** 28

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Ljubljana, Slovenia

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Four Grand Prix Top 8s.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Blue-Black Control. It is well positioned against decks which aren't mono-red.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[The Scarab God](https://web.archive.org/web/20170903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Scarab+God). It's the main reason to play this deck.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Kalitas, Traitor of Ghet](https://web.archive.org/web/20170903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kalitas%2C+Traitor+of+Ghet). It gives you a chance against mono-red.

### Alain Bardini

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gptur17/t8_bardini.jpg)

**Age:** 33

**Occupation:** *Magic*

**Hometown:** Toulouse, France

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Two Grand Prix Top 32s, one Pro Tour, semifinals of the last MOCS playoffs.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Red-White Vehicles, because I got some good results with it on *Magic Online* and I think it's well positioned right now.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Aethersphere Harvester](https://web.archive.org/web/20170903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aethersphere+Harvester), because it's really hard to kill, really good to have, and really easy to crew.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Archangel Avacyn](https://web.archive.org/web/20170903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archangel+Avacyn). Really impactful card in the mirror and against Temur.

### Leon van der Linden

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gptur17/t8_vanderlinden.jpg)

**Age:** 30

**Occupation:** Salesman

**Hometown:** Maassluis, the Netherlands

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Qualified for my first Pro Tour at the last RPTQ, played the World *Magic* Cup last year.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Mardu Vehicles, played it a lot and feel comfortable with it.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20170903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar). Still the best card in Standard.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20170903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar). Games slow down after sideboarding and Gideon is especially good then.

### Dario Parazzoli

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gptur17/t8_parazzoli.jpg)

**Age:** 39

**Occupation:** Programmer

**Hometown:** Milan, Italy

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Two Grand Prix Top 8s, nineteen Pro Tour participations.

Mono-Black Zombies, metagame choice.

[Triskaidekaphobia](https://web.archive.org/web/20170903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triskaidekaphobia), unexpected and funny card.

### Christian Hauck

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gptur17/t8_hauck.jpg)

**Age:** 32

**Occupation:** Controller

**Hometown:** Mutterstadt, Germany

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

One Nationals Top 8, two Grand Prix Top 8s.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Temur Energy with black. Fits my playstyle and it's hard to hate it out of the metagame.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

Rhonas the Indmoitable. Won a lot of games alone—when followed up by a [Bristling Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20170903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bristling+Hydra).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Cartouche of Ambition](https://web.archive.org/web/20170903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cartouche+of+Ambition), won me an unwinnable match against mono-red.
