We wanted to make sure you knew that [*Lorwyn* release events](https://web.archive.org/web/20071008000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/releases) are this *weekend* at a store near you. Furthermore, [*Lorwyn* release events](https://web.archive.org/web/20071008000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/releases) are *this* weekend at a *store* near you. In summary, [*Lorwyn release events*](https://web.archive.org/web/20071008000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/releases) are this weekend at a store *near you*. 

To celebrate, this week we're showing off some of the TV spots we've been running on Comedy Central and Adult Swim. Most of them are irreverent and funny jabs at the *Lorwyn* tribes combined with our usual "call to action" to go to the release events. (The video quality is the best we could get out of YouTube—if you want to see them in better resolution, you might set your TiVo to try to pull them down off the airwaves.) Anyway, check them out!
