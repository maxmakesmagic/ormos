Today marks my first appearance as the author of Daily Decks, so I decided to kick things off with a deck that is near and dear to my heart: BW Tokens. Granted, the deck may no longer feature [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom), but this is more an update to the Standard list I played at [PT Kyoto](https://web.archive.org/web/20160401123900/http://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/nassif-greater-power) than a new deck. 

![Lingering Souls](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Lingering+Souls)

The deck is based on the combination of [Honor of the Pure](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Honor+of+the+Pure) and [Intangible Virtue](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Intangible+Virtue), which make [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls), [Spectral Procession](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectral+Procession), [Midnight Haunting](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Midnight+Haunting), and [Raise the Alarm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raise+the+Alarm) into huge threats (and [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls) doesn't need much help). [Auriok Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auriok+Champion) and [Windbrisk Heights](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Windbrisk+Heights) offer more token synergy, gaining a ton of life and giving the deck free spells at a very low cost. 

Past the token part, this deck also gets to play a ton of cards that are awesome in their own right. [Tidehollow Sculler](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidehollow+Sculler) and [Deathrite Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathrite+Shaman) do plenty of work on their own, and [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile) and [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) disrupt the opponent's plans very effectively. I would be interested in adding [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant), as it's one of the best cards in the format, but besides that I like the look of this deck (and think it's especially sweet that it plays the underutilized [Patrician's Scorn](https://web.archive.org/web/20130709000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Patrician%27s+Scorn) in its sideboard!).
