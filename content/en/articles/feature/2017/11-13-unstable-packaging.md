Hey, you. Yeah, you, the one drawing monkey-spiders all over your notebook. I've got something for you.

![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/images/daily/I3TCVlfJCW.png)

Yup, that's a monkey-spider-scientist. I think. *Unstable* is a weird place. Full of crossbred animals, three-headed goblins . . .

![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/images/daily/BPtB1Ojax5.png)

 . . . and super spies with totally impractical hats. This guy is not undercover.

![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/images/daily/vLt7a68KhR.png)

We've also got a scientist with a massive brain—and a kitten? Plus some kind of bird squirrel?

![](https://media.wizards.com/2017/images/daily/T9w94kFJkm.png)

Yeah, things are going to get weird. *Unstable* previews start today and run through November 22. Prepare yourself.
