### Invitation List

**2009 S. Korea National Championship Invitations** 

The **Magic: The Gathering** S. Korea National Championship is an invitation-only event, open to citizens or foreign players resident in S. Korea from Jan 1st 2009 . Invitations are awarded based on the following criteria:

*   2008 S. Korea Worlds Team

    *   Jun Hee Kang (S. Korea Champion)
    *   Cynic Kim
    *   Ji-Hoon Lee
    *   Sung-Wook Nam
*   The Alternate member of the 2008 S. Korea National Team

    *   Hyun Woo Jeong
    *   Top 4 winners from the Seoul Regional on May 10
    *   Top 2 winners from Busan Regional on June 6
    *   Top 4 winners from National Qualifier Tournaments on July 4

Regional( May 10 & June 6) and National Qualifier( July 4th) tournaments take place as follows:
