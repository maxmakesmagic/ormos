The art for *Tenth Edition’s* Lord of the Pit is done! Michael Sutfin was generous enough to share some images of the process he underwent along the way.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/24/41/24414a435b9b3bd00ec07e7ac7fa24ff063664a6ebb91e42739759aaf94e59a9.jpg)  
 Initial sketch.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/14/1e/141eb38b9d88ace847f9e1ef4c260510f363853c871afbec0200e7061ab87a40.jpg)  
 The color study is done in Photoshop.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/94/1b/941b513917da7da394c87dbf6f686a43aa5251aef65c24d2167080d14f33b6d2.jpg)  
 The printout on archival paper is glued onto masonite with matte medium.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/24/9e/249ea3a9476e544908e743cae947b56a33a280f4488f74fbb3cd69ee01818540.jpg)  
 The application of acrylic paint begins, using toothbrush spray, rags and brush.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/18/d9/18d905a0b887ceaf5adc6eb78b49d9ae71de0f2817ac9ad372152c388cbd1c56.jpg)  
 Beginning of laying down oils on top.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b6/4e/b64ec4810f3471ef8b4639224cb81a1fc302069e811ee68c6c6a8bb9b203a519.jpg)  
 Finished pro scan.
