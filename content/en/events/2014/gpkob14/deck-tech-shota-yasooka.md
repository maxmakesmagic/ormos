![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpkob14/kao_yasooka.jpg)

 

Known as one of the best constructed players of all time, it is no surprise that Shota Yasooka brought his own creation to Grand Prix Kobe. Yasooka is familiar with playing Blue-Black decks in Modern, opting to play Faeries at Pro Tour Born Of The Gods earlier this year. Today, however, he played a U/B Tezzeret Control Deck.

 

"At Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*, Modern as a format had yet to solidify," Yasooka explained, "Therefore, I thought Faeries would be powerful for the metagame. Since then, the metagame has changed quite a bit, making Faeries not that powerful."

"I really love Tezzeret." Yasooka continues, "Two months ago I created \[this\] deck and was planning on playing in a PTQ with it, but I became busy. I thought the deck was strong and I get to play with all of my favorite cards, so I decided to play it this weekend"

Yasooka's deck takes advantage of Tezzeret and [Liliana of the Veil](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+of+the+Veil) to generate a recurring advantage for him. He has [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek) to disrupt his opponent early on, and has [Smother](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smother), [Damnation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Damnation), Go For the Throat, [Doom Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doom+Blade) and [Slaughter Pact](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slaughter+Pact) to deal with opposing creatures. He has powerful disruptive artifacts including [Torpor Orb](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torpor+Orb), [Relic of Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Relic+of+Progenitus), and [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle). Once he has bought enough time against the opposing deck he has powerful finishers. His namesake planeswalker, [Tezzeret, Agent of Bolas](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tezzeret%2C+Agent+of+Bolas), allows him to turn his utility artifacts into 5/5 creatures. Along with heavy hitters such as [Batterskull](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Batterskull) and [Wurmcoil Engine](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wurmcoil+Engine), Yasooka has [Creeping Tar Pit](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Creeping+Tar+Pit) and [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) to sneak in the final points of damage.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpkob14/r5_yasooka_board.jpg)

 

One of Yasooka's favorite main deck cards is a pair of Relic Of [Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Progenitus). "It's a one mana artifact that allows me to power on [Mox Opal](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Opal) while providing an artifact for Tezzeret. On top of that it is really powerful against [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf)."

 

Between his main deck and sideboard, Yasooka has a number of interesting choices. "In matchups that go long, I can board in [Ashiok, Nightmare Weaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashiok%2C+Nightmare+Weaver) and Meloku, The Clouded Mirror. Against [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon), I have Talisman of Dominance and [Dimir Signet](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dimir+Signet) to cast my colored spells. Playing against both [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) and [Ancient Grudge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Grudge) can be really tough,but I have [Negate](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Negate) and [Flashfreeze](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flashfreeze) to make it a little easier."

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpkob14/r5_yasooka.jpg)

 

"On day one I beat Jund three times, Black-Green once, and White-Blue-Red Burn once. My only loss was to [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod). Today I've played and beaten Ad Nauseum, Affinity, and White-Blue-Red Midrange." Yasooka mentioned. Along with [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod), "Burn and Tron are really difficult matchups"
