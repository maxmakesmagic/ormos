### Special Invitational Update

* * *

### General Changes

*   All users can replay Rochester Drafts in the **Invitational** area.
*   Changed automatic chat phrases available when players right click in the chat area of the **Duel** screen.
*   Fixed a bug where new cards were not highlighted for league **Deck Editor** screens when the last booster was added.
*   Having one player modify another player's league deck no longer causes an error. Now the deck is rejected for having the wrong serial numbers on the cards.
*   Players can replay games in an exhibition room.

### Invitational Changes

*   The Invitational **Rochester Draft** room allows both three *OnslaughtTM* boosters and 90-minute rounds.
*   The Invitational **Finals** room now supports both Standard with *Onslaught* and Online Extended formats.
*   The **Gunslinging** rooms support only Standard format.
*   The replay/watch menus allow clicking on tables and right clicking on spreadsheets.
*   Parallel Evolution copies names and multiple creature types correctly.
