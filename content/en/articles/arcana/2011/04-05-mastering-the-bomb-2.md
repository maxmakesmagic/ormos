Let's talk for a second about Metallic Mastery, which represents the Phyrexians' ability to use the Mirrans' own technology against them.

 ![Metallic Mastery](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Metallic%20Mastery&options=) 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/44/9f/449feb4b8806b9d7a6184ed54ebdf7d4fe283f949d46d7bbe0160504974df097.jpg) 

Metallic Mastery | Art by [Erica Yang](https://web.archive.org/web/20110405000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22erica+yang%22])

The art showcases a Phyrexian furnace worker holding a familiar-looking artifact from *Scars of Mirrodin*. If you don't recognize it, click here.

 ![Ratchet Bomb](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Ratchet%20Bomb&options=) 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0b/4b/0b4bed43cb1e8cc591a6dff1f22518e59c1ad87e65d5a180506c69c96d412c80.jpg) 

Ratchet Bomb | Art by [Austin Hsu](https://web.archive.org/web/20110405000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22austin+hsu%22])

Now that that's out of the way, let's look a little more closely at the rest of the card. The furnace worker, and even the furnace background, were taken from concepts in the *Mirrodin Besieged* style guide. Observe!
