Burn is a strategy that never seems to go out of style, and Patrick Sullivan is a man who loves red decks. He achieved a Top 32 finish at Grand Prix–Columbus playing the above take on the deck. Price of Progress, Lightning Bolt, and Fireblast are all powerful burn spells. But when they also pump your Kiln Fiend? You have a clock as fast as any in the format! Read more at the [official coverage](/en/events/2010/gpcol10/saitos-legacy-continues).
