![](https://i.ormos.uk/dc/6f/dc6fef200ae15758995f1ae45ac85d425bdad64eea378b62d461cbb3929ac432.jpg)

During the finals, a shockingly young-looking Mark Rosewater keeps careful track of everything Zak Dolan does. The person immediately behind Mark is Jim Lin, who would go on to become the Vice-President of R&D. Former editor of the *Duelist Companion* Rhias Hall is barely visible behind the video camera on the right.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/27/f8/27f88b6b7af4d0e8031196eae0b919a1d6c75b88542482f1166ad0e1c7fb81c7.jpg)

The Wizards booth. From left, Wade Racine, Logan Grey, Corey Smith, Steve Bishop, Chris Ferris, and Paul Peterson.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0b/c2/0bc214b6b83942473d9f16888b649c596a9fb99eec18eee3ad2a603b5ae63572.jpg)

Head of the Duelists' Convocation Steve Bishop presides over the finals between Bertrand Lestreé and Zak Dolan.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4e/0b/4e0b2af27662f85ed3bb6d5771e46be8fa92e9a0d2f5eca740394141ef21c697.jpg)

At the first World Championship, there was a lot of trading. Here, someone's trade binder has been left open to the page with two Black Lotuses and six Moxes.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/72/1e/721e31cd0674531a389fb84cd39ab88161b34154d56478e88965db65ca22f75e.jpg)

Semifinalist Cyrille DeFoucand, in the process of losing to Bertrand Lestreé. The woman on the left in the M.C. Escher shirt is eventual **Magic** Rules Manager Beth "bethmo" Moursund. The face on the right belongs to Jared Earle, oneo f the founders of Nightfall games, which later became part of Wizards of the Coast via the UK office.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d0/98/d098a335fd00cf94a97edd7bda984ee5725bffc0982635f00fa8917517f1885d.jpg)

Back then, nobody used sleeves. Note the condition of the table that both players have Moxes on.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7b/46/7b46e6181a29d56463fd975628755424bbe4a09e280c04f24c7c3fe5d98433d8.jpg)

Corey Smith (left) watches Zak Dolan play Dominic Symens in the finals.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/99/34/9934f3dbe49c6e6698e38401c059944af7402420e9152e6e4a5feee90b20e947.jpg)

Belgium's Dominic Symens wonders how he's going to get to the finals while staring down a [Black Vise](https://web.archive.org/web/20040823000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Vise) and a [Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20040823000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotus). The answer, it turns out, is that he wouldn't, losing the semifinals in two straight games.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d4/dd/d4dd66da3ecbf3ba475b32ba0498c83af474a4fac604f5aaa91e955aa2c250dd.jpg)

The French contingent: an unidentified French player, Cyrille De Foucand, and Bertrand Lestreé. Looking on is a Spanish player we don't recognize (in the glasses) and Dominic Symens.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7a/6c/7a6c22d8e96dffb4f9c5b28c99cb9bbe2dc19422857586a83b96629755adfe61.jpg)

Steve Bishop congratulates Zak Dolan on his victory. The awards ceremonies have gotten somewhat more formal over the years.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9c/e9/9ce95be12283fd4c9c1345438e71d83dcc4d9370b4b3df892cdbaab24f6425fc.jpg)

Bertrand Lestreé, possibly accepting applause. Incidentally, see that guy on his left, in the glasses? With the wolf on his shirt? That's Chris Page, one of the [designers of *Ice Age*](https://web.archive.org/web/20040823000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/dawn-magics-ice-age-2004-08-02). He and Mark Rosewater transcribed everything that happened in the Finals.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e3/7f/e37fce69de0f7227c7f5bf1d63478dd747dae02478464a61ab79408213a57dae.jpg)

The trophy!
