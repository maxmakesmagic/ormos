The effect of *Fate Reforged* on Standard has been quick and powerful. Gone are cards like [Whip of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20150221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos) and [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy). While [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) continues to showcase the power of three-colored cards in *Khans of Tarkir*, plenty of potency is coming from the latest set: [Valorous Stance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valorous+Stance), [Crux of Fate](https://web.archive.org/web/20150221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crux+of+Fate), [Wild Slash](https://web.archive.org/web/20150221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Slash), [Whisperwood Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20150221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whisperwood+Elemental), [Outpost Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20150221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Outpost+Siege), and the enigmatic [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150221000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon) have all made waves in recent weeks. 

Which *Fate Reforged* standouts can you find in the Grand Prix Trial winning decklists below?
