# Deck of the Day

1.  [Jason Simard's Mono-White Eldrazi and Taxes](https://web.archive.org/web/20170817142725/http://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/gpvan17/top-8-decklists-2017-02-19)

I have multiple reasons for showing off this Modern list from [Grand Prix Vancouver](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-vancouver-2017) last month. And they're all fantastic reasons. Just the best reasons.

For one, the Masters of Modern just revealed this morning that [Aven Mindcensor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Mindcensor), a cog in this hateful wheel, is being reprinted in *Amonkhet*. Simard only played one in the main and one in the side, but the "gotcha" feeling of [Aven Mindcensor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Mindcensor) is really high, especially in conjunction with [Ghost Quarter](https://web.archive.org/web/20170330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Quarter).

For two, we have a [Team Unified Modern Grand Prix this weekend in San Antonio](/en/events/2017/gpsa17/fact-sheet-grand-prix-san-antonio), and it's worth noting that this deck is both powerful and steals very few cards other decks might want. [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20170330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile) is played in many places, [Ghost Quarter](https://web.archive.org/web/20170330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Quarter) gets used a bit, [Eldrazi Temple](https://web.archive.org/web/20170330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Temple) goes in other Eldrazi decks, and [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20170330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) is played by Merfolk—but that's about it. It sucks up some powerful sideboard cards ([Stony Silence](https://web.archive.org/web/20170330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stony+Silence) and [Rest in Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20170330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rest+in+Peace)), but any white deck would do that.

Finally, also note how many of these cards are in *Modern Masters 2017 Edition*. [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel), [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20170330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile), [Blade Splicer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blade+Splicer), [Flickerwisp](https://web.archive.org/web/20170330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flickerwisp), and even the sideboarded [Stony Silence](https://web.archive.org/web/20170330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stony+Silence) and [Grafdigger's Cage](https://web.archive.org/web/20170330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grafdigger%27s+Cage).

Given all of this, I wouldn't be surprised to see this deck pop up now and then in San Antonio.
