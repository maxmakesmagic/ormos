I'm not a [mad scientist](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/ftl/159). I'm not [Merv Griffin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/td/158). I'm just a twisted soul unleashing cackles of dark joy. Not quite mad, but not fully sane either, I'm ascending into something beautifully dark.

*Innistrad* is upon us. Horrors, and wonders, lurk within.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/sf/sf161_endlessRanks.jpg)

Endless Ranks of the Dead | Art by [Ryan Yee](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;artist=+%5B%22Ryan+Yee%22%5D)

Of course, I'm being a bit dramatically facetious about all this. The creepy crawlies and shadowy beasts scuttling about are exactly why we're here, and it's time to begin cataloguing our new friends.

As this week is *Innistrad* Week, celebrating the impending destruction of booster packaging worldwide, I wanted to take a different tack on a traditional review article. *Innistrad* features many different things that hunt, and turning that on its head seems like fair play.

It's time for us, and a few choice friends, to go hunting through the freshly filled [Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/innistrad/cig) and find our prey: multiple new cards for gaming with multiple friends!

Self-Flagellating For Fun (and Profit)

There is a cycle of artifacts from *Tempest* I'm rather fond of: [the medallions](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advancedamp;name=+%5BMedallion%5Damp;set=+%5B%22Tempest%22%5D).

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/sf/sf161_5Cards1.jpg)  

Each one reduces the cost of the appropriate color of spells for you, and they work in tandem to make multicolored spells even easier. There have been many subsequent takes on mana reduction, but the most recent one appears in *Innistrad*: Heartless Summoning. All your creatures cost a whopping ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless2.gif) less to cast with the "detriment" of making all your creatures weaker by -1/-1. Flavorfully, you're ripping creatures through the Æther faster, but damaging them in the process.

This might seem like a bad thing to do, but sometimes it's a welcomed *feature*. You can see where this is going.

The goal of the deck is to attack with large, grave-fueled monstrosities. In [last week's Building on a Budget](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/boab/160), Jacob Van Lunen took us for a spin with Splinterfright, and I liked what I saw. However, this is a slightly *different* take on living large from the graveyard. Like Jacob's deck, we want to put cards from our library into the graveyard. Unlike Jacob's deck, though, we're in for a longer haul against more opponents, with slower growth that creeps up on the game.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b1/48/b1482634ebe55cd37dc6d92017edec202caf80398f8095437d21410d26077da4.jpg)  

The aforementioned Heartless Summoning does a few things for us:

> *   [Lord of Extinction](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lord+of+Extinction), Boneyard Wurm, and Splinterfright—all gears of graveyard aggression—are easier to cast.
> *   [Dryad Arbor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dryad+Arbor), [Sylvan Ranger](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Ranger), and [Viridian Emissary](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viridian+Emissary) hop to the graveyard posthaste.
> *   An early Bloodgift Demon or Reaper from the Abyss is deliciously devastating as well.
> *   Just a single mana for [Auramancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auramancer) or [Yavimaya Granger](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yavimaya+Granger) is sweet too.
> *   We can cast a kicked [Citanul Woodreaders](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Citanul+Woodreaders) for just ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless2.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/green.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/green.gif)!

In essence, we get to grow funky interactions into a mass of grungy critters. A few other things we can do:

> *   Use [Verdant Catacombs](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Verdant+Catacombs) to grab a [Dryad Arbor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dryad+Arbor), letting us change combat math in a hurry.
> *   [Auramancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auramancer) can get back any enchantment card, letting us recycle [Sterling Grove](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sterling+Grove) for other enchantments or get back a destroyed Heartless Summoning.
> *   Gutter Grime, another new card from *Innistrad*, watches as our creatures continue to die. The resulting precipitate is handy.
> *   [Nantuko Cultivator](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Cultivator) can even let us cycle away lands we don't need for fresh new cards while netting a fatty to boot.

Pairing new players with old favorites is a failsafe I always fall back to. With [Rhys the Redeemed](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rhys+the+Redeemed), [Jade Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jade+Mage), [Spectral Procession](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectral+Procession), and [Elspeth, Knight-Errant](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Knight-Errant) in the mix making so many tokens, [Hour of Reckoning](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hour+of+Reckoning) and [Retribution of the Meek](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Retribution+of+the+Meek) feel right at home too. [Culling Dais](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Culling+Dais) is a serviceable method of drawing cards, and getting rid of the guys who leave tokens behind upon death. [Noxious Revival](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noxious+Revival) can obviously rebuy something you want, but it does say "target player" and allows you to help (or hurt) other players' draws as well!

If you did decide to dip into blue, the first addition I'd seek out is Snapcaster Mage. Who needs to get the spell all the way back to your hand when you can just give it flashback? Or we could just use even more cards with flashback.

> *   Cackling Counterpart copies a creature you control. Say that five times fast (so you don't forget it like I likely will).

> *   Deranged Assistant is a way to shoehorn in a little mana acceleration, and will certainly provide a speedbump to any invading masses from other players.
> *   Returning favorite [Think Twice](https://web.archive.org/web/20110920000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Think+Twice) gives everyone another chance to reconsider it.
