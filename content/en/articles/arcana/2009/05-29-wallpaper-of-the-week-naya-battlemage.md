People enjoyed [their look at Steve Argyle's process](/en/articles/arcana/2009/05-27-the-argyle-process) so much that there was a positive outcry to have Naya Battlemage as this week's desktop wallpaper. And who are we to argue?

Incidentally, Mr. Argyle himself dropped into the forums and provided [an enormous version of this art](http://forums.gleemax.com/showpost.php?p=18670866&postcount=236). Thanks, Steve! This also gives us an opportunity to mention [SteveArgyle.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20090529000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.steveargyle.com/&origin=), where there are more time-lapses, as well as various sketches and cool stuff. Check out the sketches for Slave of Bolas while you're there!

Enjoy.
