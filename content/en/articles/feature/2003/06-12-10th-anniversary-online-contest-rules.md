1.   Void where prohibited or restricted. Stories and completed entry forms should be sent to Wizards of the Coast Europe. ("Wizards") via magic10thpromotion@hasbro.co.uk or by regular post: Wizards of the Coast, p/a Hasbro Belgium nv – 10th anniversary online contest – ‘t Hofveld 6D – 1702 Groot-Bijgaarden – Belgium (for European residents). US residents should send their entry forms to: Wizards of the Coast, Inc. - Attn: 10th Anniversary Contest - P.O. Box 707 - Renton, WA 98057-0707. Only official entry forms downloaded from www.magicthegathering.com/default.asp?x=magic/promo/10annivstory2 will be accepted.
2.   Winners will be determined by Wizards, whose decisions are final.
3.   This competition is open to persons over the age of 13 who are legal residents of any of the following countries excluding employees of Wizards and its respective affiliates and distributors and the families of such employees, affiliates and distributors: Belgium, Canada, Denmark, France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Norway, Spain, Sweden, United Kingdom, United States.
4.   All stories must be submitted in English in order to be eligible.
5.   Stories must fit within one of the following categories in order to be eligible:
     *   **Category 1:** Tell us about the best game of Magic you ever played and why was it so great.
     *   **Category 2:** Tell us about the best place you have played Magic (i.e. on top of the Eiffel Tower, in the Rain Forest, on an airplane flying to Japan)
     *   **Category 3:** What is the best trip you took to go play Magic?
     *   **Category 4:** What do you think is the best Magic card and why?
6.   There will be 5 prizes per category (1 grand prize and 4 runner-up prizes), for a total of twenty prizes in all.
7.   Each Grand Prize (four total) includes a round-trip coach airfare for the winner and a guest from the major airport nearest winner’s home to Berlin, Germany to attend the **Magic: The Gathering** World Championships between August 6th, 2003 and August 10th, 2003. In case the winner is a minor in his or her home jurisdiction, the 2nd person must be a parent of guardian. This prize includes: transportation costs to and from the airport in Berlin to The World Championships; 3 nights (breakfast included) double-occupancy accommodation at a hotel chosen by Wizards; a free slot to play in the on-site 10th Anniversary Celebration draft on Sunday, August 10th! Except as otherwise specified herein, meals, gratuities, ground transportation and all other expenses are winner's responsibility. A representative of Wizards will contact the winner regarding this prize package. This prize is non-negotiable, non-transferable, and may not be substituted except at Wizards’ sole discretion. Trip must be taken on the dates designated by Wizards, or prize will be forfeited and will not be re-awarded. Specific travel dates and arrangements are subject to availability. Winner and guest must travel together on same itinerary. Approximate retail value of each Grand Prize package is approx. €1.000. Actual value may vary based on point of departure and any airfare fluctuations.
8.   Each Runner-up prize (16 total) includes: one (1) **MagicTM** binder and a display (36 boosters) of random **Magic: The Gathering®** booster packs. A representative of Wizards will contact the winner regarding this prize package. This prize is non-negotiable, non-transferable, and may not be substituted except at Wizards’ sole discretion.
9.   Stories must be postmarked by July 6th, 2003, and received by July 10th, 2003 before midnight (12am), Pacific Standard Time.
10.  All stories will be reviewed by representatives of Wizards. Winners will be determined on or about July 18th, 2003. Winners will be notified before July 25th, 2003.
11.  No purchase or payment of any money is necessary to enter.
12.  One entry per person.
13.  All entries become the exclusive property of Wizards and will not be acknowledged or returned. Wizards assumes no responsibility for lost, late, ineligible, incomplete, or misdirected entries or for technical or other failures, including Internet traffic, which may prevent or restrict access to the web site.
14.  \[All copyright subsisting in the stories submitted to Wizards pursuant to this agreement shall become the property of Wizards, and upon Wizards’ request, will execute any documents necessary to effectuate such ownership.\]
15.  This agreement shall be governed by and interpreted according to the laws of England and the parties submit to the jurisdiction of the English courts. This contest is not running in jurisdictions where similar skill-based games and contests are prohibited by law. Non-compliance with time parameters or return of any prize or prize notification as undeliverable will result in disqualification. Winner may be required to sign affidavit of eligibility/release of liability/prize acceptance ("Affidavit") within five (5) days of receipt of notification, or forfeit prize. If winner is a minor, the winner's legal guardian must co-sign Affidavit. By accepting the prize, winner agrees to the use of his or her name and/or likeness for purposes of advertising, trade, or promotion without further compensation, unless prohibited by law.
16. Winner further acknowledges that Wizards neither has made, nor is in any way responsible, or liable for any warranty, representation, or guarantee, express or implied, in fact or in law, relative to any prize, including but not limited to quality, mechanical condition, or fitness, except as such is contained in the written warranty included within each prize package.
17.  Winners List: winning stories and the names of the winners will be posted on www.magicthegathering.com as of August 15th, 2003.
18.  Sponsor. This contest is sponsored solely by Wizards of the Coast, Inc. (US address).
19.  Eligibility. By participating in this contest, contestants accept these rules and all restrictions contained therein.
