You have perhaps heard that the **Magic Online** [Holiday Cube](/en/articles/magiconline/2013/01-22-the-magic-online-cube-returns) returns [the Power](/en/articles/makingmagic/2012/11-27-power-cubed) introduced last year? Well, with great Power comes great... uh... new art. For the online Cube, we commissioned [Volkan Baga](https://web.archive.org/web/20131217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Volkan+Baga%22%5D) to create all-new images of the iconic and beloved Moxes. Let's take a look!
