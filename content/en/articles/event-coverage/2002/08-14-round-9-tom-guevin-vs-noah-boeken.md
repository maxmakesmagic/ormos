To the surprise of absolutely no one, there was a lot of talk during the match between Noah Boeken and Tom Guevin. Each is used to throwing an opponent off his game with a never-ending stream of meaningless chatter. Each is also having a good day so far, with wins in each of the first two rounds.

Boeken was the aggressor early in Game 1, playing creatures on turns 2, 3, and 4 before Guevin was able to play a single spell. [Violent Eruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Violent+Eruption) has a way of slowing down a weenie deck though – suddenly the only creature in play was Boeken's [Teroh's Faithful](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teroh%27s+Faithful). He enchanted it with [Exoskeletal Armor](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exoskeletal+Armor), turning it into quite a beast, but Guevin had [Second Thoughts](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Second+Thoughts) and that threat was promptly dispatched. Guevin's removal simply never stopped. [Lava Dart](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lava+Dart) killed [Militant Monk](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Militant+Monk). [Ember Shot](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ember+Shot) killed [Aven Cloudchaser](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Cloudchaser). [Morbid Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morbid+Hunger) killed [Suntail Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Suntail+Hawk). [Waste Away](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Waste+Away) killed [Arrogant Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrogant+Wurm). [Anarchist](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anarchist) brought back the Hunger and finally gave Guevin something to attack with. 

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/worlds02/a850.jpg)

Guevin's deck was actually quite interesting: He ran a total of 19 lands to support all his expensive spells (and his three colors). Boeken could not keep anything alive long enough to attack with it – not even with two counters on his [Soulcatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soulcatcher)'s Aerie. Cheering himself on with "Come on, Tommy!" he took a 1-0 lead, threatening to win the table with his 19-land beauty.

Game 2 started with Guevin pile-shuffling Boeken's deck and noticing that it had 41 cards. "41 cards? Oh yeah, you're on that team that believes in that prime numbers bull. I'm surprised, Noah. I mean, if I had two [Battle Screech](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battle+Screech)es I'd want to draw them." The key turn in the game came when Boeken tried to enchant his [Auramancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auramancer) with [Exoskeletal Armor](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exoskeletal+Armor). There were already two creatures in his graveyard and Guevin was sure to kill more. Guevin played a [Lava Dart](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lava+Dart) as a response and the sacrificed a mountain to flash it back. Boeken used [Pay No Heed](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pay+No+Heed) to stop the second point of dart damage and his Armor resoved. "Card #41," he explained, "that 41st card did it." Indeed it did – Guevin was never able to stabilize the board and theywere soon on to game 3.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/worlds02/a849.jpg)

Boeken finally drew a [Battle Screech](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battle+Screech) in game 3 and his turn 3 [Auramancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Auramancer) meant four birds came out to play on turn 4. Those birds dropped Guevin down to 9 before his deck delivered up a [Mystic Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystic+Familiar) that could hold off future beats. That stalled long enough for Guevin to draw a second swamp for his Torturer plus [Morbid Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morbid+Hunger). Suddenly, Guevin was in charge and starting to ham it up as only he can. His Torturer killed 7 different creatures and Guevin was able to mount an offense with a motley crew consisting of barbarian Outcast, [Mystic Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystic+Familiar) and [Shieldmage Advocate](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shieldmage+Advocate). Boeken discarded a card to [Hypochondria](https://web.archive.org/web/20020814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hypochondria) every turn so he was just taking one, but all that did was stall the inevitable and give Guevin more chances to play to the crowd.

**Final Result:** Guevin 2 - Boeken 1
