These are the top 8 players of Grand Prix Denver 2015.

* * *

# Andrew Brown

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpden15/T8%20-%20Brown.jpg)

**Age:** 22  
**Hometown:** Santa Monica, CA  
**Occupation:** Latin Teacher, *Magic* Player 

**Pro Tour/Grand Prix Top 8s:**  
 This one! 

 **Other *Magic* Achievements:**  
 Two Top 16s, 2 Top 32s, 3 Top 100s 

**Record on Day 1/Day 2?**  
 9-0, 3-2-1 

**What deck are you playing today and why?**  
 Blue Black Control, I must play Islands and draw spells. The only bad matchups are Temur and Mono Red. 

**What was your MVP this weekend? Maindeck? Sideboard?**  
[Interpret the Signs](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Interpret+the+Signs) in the main, Blind Flipped [Pearl Lake Ancient](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pearl+Lake+Ancient) and drew seven, won that game, lost that match. 

**What are you most excited about for Fate Reforged?**  
 Crux of Fate! However I can punish Forests!

# Lukas Parson

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpden15/T8%20-%20Parson.jpg)

**Age:** 22  
**Hometown:** Madisonville, KY  
**Occupation:** Student/*Magic* Grinder 

**Pro Tour/Grand Prix Top 8s:**  
 First 

 **Other *Magic* Achievements:**  
 Three PTQ Wins, Top 8 Indy WMCQ 

**Record on Day 1/Day 2?**  
 8-1, 4-1-1 

**What deck are you playing today and why?**  
 Red/Green Aggro. Most consistent mana base with having an excellent nut draw. [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon) is very well-positioned. 

**What was your MVP this weekend? Maindeck? Sideboard?**  
[Goblin Rabblemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Rabblemaster)/[Xenagos, the Reveler](https://web.archive.org/web/20150104000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xenagos%2C+the+Reveler) 

**What are you most excited about for Fate Reforged?**  
 Crux of Fate
