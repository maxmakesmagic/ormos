Czech superstar Martin Juza shares his Modern deck with Rich Hagon. The deck hinges on getting an early infect creature on the board and then striking for all 10 poison damage in one swing thanks to Blazing Shoal.

  
  

Martin Juza 

Pro Tour Philadelphia, Modern Constructed
