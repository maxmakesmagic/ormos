Last week, Amsterdam played host to Worlds Week, a five-day **event** that combined the [World Championship](https://web.archive.org/web/20130809000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/youth-served-flash) and [World **Magic** Cup](https://web.archive.org/web/20130809000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/l%C3%A9vy-du-soleil-en-france)! Congratulations to Shahar Shenhar, our new world champion, and the Raphaël Lévy–led Team France, our new World **Magic** Cup champions! Between those two tournaments, players competed in **seven different formats**. Of particular interest to those of you competing at PTQs this season, Standard was well represented. If you plan on going to a PTQ this season, find one near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#theros). Good luck; have fun!
