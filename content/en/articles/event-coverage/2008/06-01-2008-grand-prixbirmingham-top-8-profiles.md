**Name:** Matthias Künzler  
**Age:** 27  
**Occupation:** Engineer in microtechnics, co-owning a game store  
**Hometown:** Bern, Switherland

**Previous Magic accomplishments:** 

Top 8 GP–Vienna 1999  
 Top 16 PT–Yokohama 2007

**What was your most valuable card this weekend?** 

[Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20080601000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command)

**What do you think about your chances for the top 8?**

Quite okay. Kithkin isn’t that bad a match-up.

**If you were to play your deck in the upcoming PTQs, would you change anything?**

Yes, switch one [Sower of Temptation](https://web.archive.org/web/20080601000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sower+of+Temptation) for a [Familiar’s Ruse](https://web.archive.org/web/20080601000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Familiar%E2%80%99s+Ruse) in the sideboard.

**Age:** 25  
**Occupation:** Student  
**Hometown:** Helsinki, Finland

**Previous Magic accomplishments:** 

4th at PT–London  
*Unhinged* Prerelease champion

**What was your most valuable card this weekend?**

Decent... Happy to be here.
