The **Magic** Invitational is alive and kicking for 2002! For those unaware of the **Magic** Invitational, it's an invite-only event each year where **Magic's** top sixteen players are brought together to compete in a round robin tournament with multiple formats (a few of which are quite quirky). This is **Magic's** all-star game and the winner earns the right to create his very own **Magic** card. (Past cards include Darwin Kastle's Avalanche Riders, Mike Long's Rootwater Thief, Chris Pikula's Meddling Mage, Jon Finkel's Shadowmage Infiltrator, and Olle Rade's Sylvan Safekeeper.)

After six years and five continents, where can you go? There's only one place: Cyberspace. The 2002 **Magic** Invitational will be held live on **Magic** Online. For the first time ever, anyone with a computer (and a copy of **Magic** Online) will be able to watch the action live. The sixteen invitees will come to Wizards' corporate office in Seattle (picking up the Invitational's sixth continent) where they will have a chance to see our home city and get a behind-the-scenes look at Wizards of the Coast.

Formats for the event will be announced when the sixteen invitees are announced later this summer. The fan ballot will begin after the 2002 World Championships in Sydney, Australia.
