I due studiano le rispettive liste e Gionata scopre subito una cosa che non gli piace...   
 "Ma tu giochi i [](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Timely+Reinforcements)Timely Reinforcements di main? Nooo....affrontami da uomo e levale"   
 "Se tu levi le [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide)"   
 "Ok, ci sto!"   
 Ma i judge non sono d'accordo e si parte.

### Game 1

Terra [Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain) per Mattia che ferma con [Spell Pierce](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Pierce) lo [Shrine of Burning Rage](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrine+of+Burning+Rage) di Gionata e risponde con doppio [Squadron Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squadron+Hawk) a [Furnace Scamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Furnace+Scamp). La race la guida quindi Caw Blade, contrariamente ai pronostici, e Gionata deve usare un [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember) per disfarsi di una [Hero of Bladehold](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero+of+Bladehold) che poteva diventare molesta. Ma ormai il vantaggio dato dai falchetti è parecchio, il [Celestial Colonnade](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Celestial+Colonnade) si anima e insieme ai pennuti porta il piemontese a 5, e a nulla serve un [Hero of Oxid Ridge](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero+of+Oxid+Ridge) per portare a casa la prima.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/itnat11/sf-montanari.jpg)

**Dal Farra 0-1 Montanari**

### Game 2

Gio parte sparato con [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) che però rivela [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) dal mazzo, il che mette la partita già abbastanza in salita; una seconda Guida prova a capitalizzare il vantaggio della partenza, ed un [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer) dovrebbe essere sufficiente a riciclare le fecthlands che il piemontese sta mettendo nel cimitero in queste prime fasi di gioco.  [](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze)Searing Blaze scoppia lo [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) dopo che ha bloccato una delle due guide, ma la race viene interrotta da [](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Timely+Reinforcements)Timely Reinforcements, giunti al momento opportuno per parare e temporeggiare un turno perché arrivi Day of Judgement. C'è poco da fare però contro una [Hero of Bladehold](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero+of+Bladehold) che si vede equipaggiare con [Sword of War and Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+War+and+Peace), specialmente con un singolo Lighning Bolt e terre in mano, il game finisce esattamente come il primo.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/itnat11/sf-dal-farra.jpg)

**Dal Farra 0-2 Montanari**

### Game 3

Mulligan per Gionata che vede la strada farsi sempre più ripida davanti: [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer) di primo e passa, per poi nei turni successivi mettere [Shrine of Burning Rage](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrine+of+Burning+Rage) e [Chandra's Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Phoenix), subito mangiata da [Oblivion Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Ring), a cui Mattia fa seguire [Ratchet Bomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ratchet+Bomb), [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) e poi [Squadron Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squadron+Hawk). La situazione sembrerebbe smuoversi quando su un [Revoke Existence](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Revoke+Existence), Gionata fa [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember) sullo [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) e poi detona il santuario sul suo opponent, ma Mattia decide che è il momento di [](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Timely+Reinforcements)Timely Reinforcements e torna su di vite, bloccando con [Flashfreeze](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flashfreeze) una nuova evocazione di [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer). [Gideon Jura](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon+Jura) decide che è il suo turno e scende in campo gettando il piemontese nello sconforto, e mentre il secondo Shrine si carica, la pescata di [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20110731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) dà adito a nuove illusioni, frustrate però dal nuovo attacco del planeswalker. Troppo alto di vite il romano per continuare a sperare nel miracolo, Gionata gira la prima del suo mazzo e tende la mano: Mattia Montanari è il primo finalista di questo campionato nazionale!

**Dal Farra 0-3 Montanari**
