We took a look at the characteristic look of Teferi [back in 2004](/en/articles/arcana/2004/12-15-teferis-appearance-2), but since he’s now a [Teferi, Mage of Zhalfir](https://web.archive.org/web/20061101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Teferi%252C%2BMage%2Bof%2BZhalfir), it’s worth taking another look.

Can you name these cards by their Teferi-involving art?

![Disrupt_640](https://i.ormos.uk/fe/2d/fe2d3bad383a3aa6ee288621fc5ff404578ce481564e8ee3c2a10f0d5b8d71b6.jpg)  
*Art by Paolo Parente*

Reveal answer

![Disrupt](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Disrupt)

Teferi and Urza team up to close the Phyrexian portal on [Disrupt](https://web.archive.org/web/20061101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disrupt).

![Spite_640](https://i.ormos.uk/f3/bf/f3bf77221e6cd24fc110cc825442c2984b89cf87384129dfedeb18e2bcd323f6.jpg)  
*Art by David Martin*

Reveal answer

![Spite  Malice](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Spite++Malice)

Teferi counters a spell on the “Spite” half of [Spite // Malice](https://web.archive.org/web/20061101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spite+%2F%2F+Malice).

![Slow_Motion_640](https://i.ormos.uk/11/b2/11b2bc5c43e17d2da5bfc1ad4b5434547f6f6eff5e91f083c4e479d83fcbf9f6.jpg)  
*Art by Todd Lockwood*

Reveal answer

![Slow Motion](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Slow+Motion)

Yep, that’s Teferi in there. In this scene, the artificer Jhoira looks on as Teferi becomes trapped in a “slow time” bubble, as seen on [Slow Motion](https://web.archive.org/web/20061101000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slow+Motion).

![Wash_Out_640](https://i.ormos.uk/45/d0/45d07797ef8bfb17685b0ca2ee7beab4619fa32f3f59e8092a71496d4ca1c6d8.jpg)  
*Art by Matthew D. Wilson*

Reveal answer

![Wash Out](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Wash+Out)

Teferi “washes out” some kavu. See him in the background on the left?

![Confiscate_640](https://i.ormos.uk/ad/0f/ad0f4490dc7b13d7fc0128621c33e4351e0597f8ede5520f07d4f254049bbeca.jpg)  
*Art by Adam Rex*

Reveal answer

![Confiscate](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Confiscate)

Teferi at age 10! Teferi’s the mischievous student on the left, getting reprimanded by his teacher. He was always a plucky lad.

And then there’s Teferi today, after the events of *Time Spiral*:

![Teferi_Mage_of_Zhalfir_640](https://i.ormos.uk/9b/ca/9bca3b77fee8feb9208073b3bdd792658d68968b95ee9aee32130562b88309ef.jpg)  
*Art by D. Alexander Gregory & Jeremy Jarvis*
