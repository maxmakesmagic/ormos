## 2001 Pro Tour Tokyo Coverage

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c9/6c/c96c69dfac717ce405d52b47b9cb11c56c6027d087fa01cc07a13effa8fd0699.gif)

Zvi Mowshowitz has won Pro Tour Tokyo, defeating Japan's own Tsuyoshi Fujita in the finals! We have total coverage of the event, including [all the decklists](https://web.archive.org/web/20170719035411/http://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/event-coverage/pro-tour-tokyo-decklists-2015-10-13) played on the weekend.
