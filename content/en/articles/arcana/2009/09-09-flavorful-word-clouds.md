Let's play a game! You like games, right? Good!

Here's how this works. We went to [Wordle](https://web.archive.org/web/20090909000000/http://ww2.wizards.com/Site/LeavingWizards.aspx?url=http://www.wordle.net&origin=) and whipped up some word clouds using the flavor text of some **Magic** sets. Your challenge is to identify which set each of these word clouds came out of. Ready? Go!

![](http://www.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/arcana/273_FE.jpg) 

Click here to see what set this is!  
  

It's *Fallen Empires*, of course! The various volumes of *Sarpadian Empires* contribute quite a lot of that set's flavor text.

![](http://www.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/arcana/273_CSP.jpg) 

Click here to see what set this is!  
  

The prominence of "Rimewind" can mean only one thing: this is the flavor text of *Coldsnap*!

![](http://www.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/arcana/273_ALA.jpg) 

Click here to see what set this is!  
  

This one was kind of a surprise. Who knew that the flavor text of *Shards of Alara* had so much more Grixis and Esper than Naya and Bant?

![](http://www.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/arcana/273_ZEN.jpg) 

Click here to see what set this is!  
  

What set could this be? Some of those words look completely unfamiliar! But if you explore the lower left quadrant, one word does kind of jump out at you: *Zendikar*!
