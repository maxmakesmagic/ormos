The captain of the U.S. National Team, Josh Utter-Leyton had quite the World Championships for 2010. In the Extended portion of the event, Josh, who is nicknamed "Wrapter" for his **Magic Online** name, decided to run Five-Color Cruel Control. Named for its powerful [Cruel Ultimatum](https://web.archive.org/web/20101216000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cruel+Ultimatum)s and somewhat neurotic mana base, the deck has proved powerful in previous formats.
