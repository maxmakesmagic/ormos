The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on November 15, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by the League of Dastardly Dooooooooooommmmm!!!

# A Bit of News

1.  Expanding the Black Friday Promotion

Are you enjoying *Unstable* previews so far? I know we are. Quite a bit. So much, we felt a little bad for leaving it off the [Black Friday Treasure Chest Promotion](/en/articles/feature/2017/11-07-black-friday-treasure-chest-promotion) we announced last week. *Iconic Masters*, too. So here's what we're going to do—we're expanding the promotion! Starting November 24 and while supplies last (check with your [local retailers](https://web.archive.org/web/20171115000000/http://locator.wizards.com/) for more information), if you buy a box of *Ixalan*, *Unstable*, or *Iconic* *Masters*, you can get a Buy-a-Box Treasure Chest booster. Heck, if you buy any booster box back to and including *Kaladesh*, you can receive a Buy-a-Box Treasure Chest booster. It's not even limited to one per person!

And if you want to get me a gift for the holidays, that special [Search for Azcanta](https://web.archive.org/web/20171115000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Search+for+Azcanta) sure is nice.

*\[Note: In the APAC region, this promotion applies only to in-store purchases of* Ixalan *booster boxes.\]*
