## *Supreme Vintage Cube* January 12–19

It's time for a bonus week of *Vintage Cube* with *Supreme Vintage Cube*, which is like regular *Vintage Cube*, but every pick is a first pick! You might even end up with a deck that has ten pieces of the Power 9!

For more info on MTGO Supreme Draft Events, click [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2020/08-24-introducing-supreme-draft), and for a full list of the latest changes to *Vintage Cube*, click [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/2016/11-30-vintage-cube-cardlist).

* * *

## *Vintage Cube* Ends Tomorrow

*Vintage Cube* is back for a three-week run through January 12. To see the full cube list and the list of card changes, make sure to check out [the updated Vintage Cube card list page](/en/articles/uncategorised/2016/11-30-vintage-cube-cardlist).

All paid accounts on file as of last week's downtime have been given a Vintage Point—good for a free entry into the *Vintage Cube* Draft League during this three-week run.

![Vintage Cube Entry](https://media.wizards.com/2021/images/daily/img_vintagecubeentry.png)

## Pioneer Super Qualifier Friday

On Friday, January 14, there will be a Pioneer Super Qualifier with a special start time of 2 p.m. PT (22:00 UTC). The entry fee is 40 MOCS Qualifier Points, 40 Event Tickets, or 400 Play Points. The top two finishers qualify for the Neon Dynasty Championship on *MTG Arena*.

Learn more about Super Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-qualifiers-super-qualifiers).

* * *

## Legacy Super Qualifier Saturday

On Saturday, January 15, there will be a Legacy Super Qualifier with a special start time of 5 a.m. PT (13:00 UTC). The entry fee is 40 MOCS Qualifier Points, 40 Event Tickets, or 400 Play Points. The top two finishers qualify for the Neon Dynasty Championship on *MTG Arena*.

Learn more about Super Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-qualifiers-super-qualifiers).

## Modern Super Qualifier Saturday

On Saturday, January 15, there will be a Modern Super Qualifier with a special start time of 3 p.m. PT (23:00 UTC). The entry fee is 40 MOCS Qualifier Points, 40 Event Tickets, or 400 Play Points. The top two finishers qualify for the Neon Dynasty Championship on *MTG Arena*.

Learn more about Super Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-qualifiers-super-qualifiers).

## Limited Super Qualifier Sunday

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format Challenges are the first step toward qualifying for Premier Play through your favorite *Magic* formats and provide excellent prizes for those looking to test their mettle in their favorite formats. [Learn more about Premier Play qualification](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021) to get started today.

*Magic Online*'s Format Challenges run once on Saturdays and once on Sundays for each Constructed format! On weekends featuring Showcase Challenge events, the Format Challenge that starts at either 6 a.m., 8 a.m., or 10 a.m. Pacific time is pre-empted by the Showcase Challenge—Format Challenges scheduled at other times remain in place. *NOTE: Pauper and Standard Challenges are never pre-empted.*

## *Modern Horizons 2* Ends Tomorrow

It's the season for another fan favorite: *Modern Horizons 2* Draft! We're bringing back the non-Phantom Draft League through January 5 with the same prize structure and chance to open a [Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer](https://web.archive.org/web/20220111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ragavan%2C+Nimble+Pilferer) you know and love!

*   Entry Options:
    *   25 Event Tickets
    *   3 *Modern Horizons 2* boosters and 4 Event Tickets
    *   250 Play Points
*   Prizes:
    *   3 Wins – 6 *Modern Horizons 2* boosters, 2 MOCS Season 3 QPs, and a Competitive Trophy
    *   2 Wins – 2 *Modern Horizons 2* boosters

**NOTE**: Limited Preliminary events will use *Modern Horizons 2* product until Wednesday, January 12.

*   Entry Options:
    *   45 Event Tickets
    *   450 Play Points
*   Prizes:
    *   4 Wins – 750 Play Points and 40 QP
    *   3 Wins – 450 Play Points and 20 QP
    *   2 Wins – 150 Play Points and 10 QP
