As you may know, there are tokens in *Return to Ravnica*. But you might *not* know exactly what they look like. Let's fix that!

<table><tbody><tr><td><img alt="Return to Ravnica Bird Token" src="https://i.ormos.uk/c9/43/c9432aa20c0acef0a6e708ad1bff8ba470f6a91ec5c6dfc1bfbeabbf87b91fb5.jpg"></td><td><img alt="Eyes in the Skies" src="https://i.ormos.uk/69/20/6920efc0c4a567525c6b8e55d22e1a14ad681351873ff1196e356d90a781ee99.jpg"></td></tr></tbody></table>

Bird tokens are always popular, right? And one of the cards that creates them is Eyes in the Skies, which gives us an excuse to show a card with populate. See what we're doing? Tokens... populate... it all makes sense!

<table><tbody><tr><td><img alt="Return to Ravnica Knight Token" src="https://i.ormos.uk/26/06/260675ce061a37162255851a03289f8eb90db0b6047027b5a513237121ffc107.jpg"></td><td><img alt="Selesnya Charm" src="https://i.ormos.uk/9c/28/9c28d72ddcf63a75362b75cfb13c3a00cf683db0f5b3114a2afe5aef93ff07be.jpg"></td></tr></tbody></table>

Knight tokens. Yay!

<table><tbody><tr><td><img alt="Return to Ravnica Soldier Token" src="https://i.ormos.uk/51/56/515616f21cc5d65cbbfdd527357aecb3e6345c3727d7103c7030d7d58f758f4c.jpg"></td><td><img alt="Precinct Captain" src="https://i.ormos.uk/bd/60/bd605ec99565caf52193f755b619e71eadce491f3fa96eacbef3653c4cc0ec25.jpg"></td></tr></tbody></table>

Yes, the existence of populate means there are a lot of different token options. Soldiers are slightly smaller than Knights. They're also less vigilant.

<table><tbody><tr><td><img alt="Return to Ravnica Assassin Token" src="https://i.ormos.uk/9f/fa/9ffa82caeef1dd95cd336b4e53c42603d4cd27160092503f5f67ef7eb28d98c5.jpg"></td><td><img alt="Vraska the Unseen" src="https://i.ormos.uk/a1/97/a197b0df71ac709689788ef91ec4ea0954b93ee23d919bd23c4408ed0d415fab.jpg"></td></tr></tbody></table>

If I could take a moment to share something with you, I want you to know that the Assassin token is my favorite token in the set. That's just a great token.

<table><tbody><tr><td><img alt="Return to Ravnica Dragon Token" src="https://i.ormos.uk/fa/70/fa7019372a00c3bd76a9ca47a7d809ee4ff93bd8e91e963be6d19a3cab12a0eb.jpg"></td>&#10;					<td><img alt="Return to Ravnica Goblin Token" src="https://i.ormos.uk/37/d6/37d6a3d150b7933ffeca103ca04ab895076d59f0547b30901d11c1401651a2d4.jpg"></td></tr></tbody></table>

Hmm. We haven't previewed a cards that make Dragon tokens or Goblin tokens. Weird!

<table><tbody><tr><td><img alt="Return to Ravnica Centaur Token" src="https://i.ormos.uk/02/88/028892f4a11a6a5cd1fe4cae4cb9ea03c10e61b84bd3d93611b4d2e471250861.jpg"></td><td><img alt="Call of the Conclave" src="https://i.ormos.uk/63/6b/636b9f7c41c615cfbff114d5589d16345bb64d51ef2e661245be0b7a06820caa.jpg"></td></tr></tbody></table>

Centaurs are traditionally 3/3 creatures. And this one is no exception! Look, it's hard to think of things to say about tokens, okay?

<table><tbody><tr><td><img alt="Return to Ravnica Ooze Token" src="https://i.ormos.uk/12/b9/12b92c004529c1d396f8dd937db873188b429044dabcf538161c985f8db1ecc1.jpg"></td><td><img alt="Slime Molding" src="https://i.ormos.uk/21/c0/21c0b6dae11eb27f83bac1c6b41f2379d14473984acac6fa27fba029f43ce8f4.jpg"></td></tr></tbody></table>

Not all tokens have preprinted powers and toughnesses. Because who knows how big your Ooze is going to be? The answer is in your heart!

![Return to Ravnica Rhino Token](https://i.ormos.uk/34/94/34946c0a9644f0cec2bcd4fcb7f50634202bbeac8bf2bc0476881c0446abca1f.jpg)  

Whoops! It's another mysterious token. There are apparently Rhinos running around in Ravnica. You probably want to watch out for that.

<table><tbody><tr><td><img alt="Return to Ravnica Saproling Token" src="https://i.ormos.uk/a3/14/a31403813b6edc7460e95f545c2002a18d57d010c18fdb8cd7e53d3925b0e71a.jpg"></td><td><img alt="Korozda Guildmage" src="https://i.ormos.uk/0d/be/0dbe69168f5452ad0ba8ffa8c1a5c659e32eb485ed2d841d363dd3f382eb79be.jpg"></td></tr></tbody></table>

Saprolings were introduced to **Magic** back in [Fallen Empires](https://web.archive.org/web/20120918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fallen+Empires) with cards like [Elvish Farmer](https://web.archive.org/web/20120918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Farmer) and [Night Soil](https://web.archive.org/web/20120918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Night+Soil).
