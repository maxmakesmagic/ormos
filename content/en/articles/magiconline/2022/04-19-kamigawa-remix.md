![Kamigawa Remix Logo](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/Q2sd8e3ns.png)

Welcome to *Kamigawa Remix*! This is a new Draft format coming only to *Magic Online* for a week starting April 20 that features cards from all four *Kamigawa* sets! So, if you've ever wanted to open a pack with both [Kabuto Moth](https://web.archive.org/web/20220419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kabuto+Moth) and [Voltage Surge](https://web.archive.org/web/20220419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voltage+Surge), read on!

![Kamigawa Remix Booster](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/N237nsdyw.png)

In *Kamigawa Remix*, the timelines of original *Kamigawa* block and *Kamigawa Neon Dynasty* are colliding! In this format, you'll see an equal representation of cards from *Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty* and the original Kamigawa block of sets *Champions of Kamigawa*, *Betrayers of Kamigawa*, and *Saviors of Kamigawa* collectively. You also won't see every single card in all four of these sets but rather just the cream of the crop from each set for drafting:

*   [![Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Sakura-Tribe+Elder)](https://web.archive.org/web/20220419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sakura-Tribe+Elder)
*   [![Ninja of the Deep Hours](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ninja+of+the+Deep+Hours)](https://web.archive.org/web/20220419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ninja+of+the+Deep+Hours)
*   [![Kiku, Night's Flower](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Kiku%2C+Night%27s+Flower)](https://web.archive.org/web/20220419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kiku%2C+Night%27s+Flower)
*   [![Kaito Shizuki](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Kaito+Shizuki)](https://web.archive.org/web/20220419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kaito+Shizuki)

*Imagine seeing all these cards in the same pack!*

To accommodate all these timelines combining in one pack, packs are overcharged! What this means is that in each pack of *Kamigawa Remix* you will find:

*   1 Rare or mythic rare from *Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty*
*   1 Rare from among *Champions of Kamigawa*, *Betrayers of Kamigawa*, and *Saviors of Kamigawa*
*   3 Uncommons from *Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty*
*   3 Uncommons from among *Champions of Kamigawa*, *Betrayers of Kamigawa*, and *Saviors of Kamigawa*
*   4 Commons from *Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty*
*   4 Commons from among *Champions of Kamigawa*, *Betrayers of Kamigawa*, and *Saviors of Kamigawa*
*   1 Land that has a 50/50 shot of being a basic land or a "gainland" that enters the battlefield tapped from the [Tranquil Cove](https://web.archive.org/web/20220419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tranquil+Cove) cycle

That's seventeen cards in each booster pack! As usual, mythic rares will replace a rare in the *Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty* slot at the normal rate you're accustomed to. But if you were around during the time of the original *Kamigawa* block, mythic rarity hadn't quite been invented yet. However, for *Kamigawa Remix*, a certain Equipment from *Betrayers of Kamigawa* has been given the honorary "mythic rare" treatment and will appear as if it was a mythic rare in the original *Kamigawa* block rare slot.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Umezawa%27s+Jitte)](https://web.archive.org/web/20220419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Umezawa%27s+Jitte)

*Yep—still good.*
