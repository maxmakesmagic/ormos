The *Ravnica* legend **Tolsimir Wolfblood** can tap to put his faithful friend, the intelligent wolf Voja, into play, and grants the token a +2/+2 bonus to boot.

As with any other card that creates tokens, art was commissioned for the Voja token for ***Magic** Online*.
