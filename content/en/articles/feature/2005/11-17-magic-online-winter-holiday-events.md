*   “Nix Tix” Draft Queues

*   Premier Events with Braingeyser Promo Card giveaway

### “Nix Tix” Draft Queues

All draft queues will require 0 event tickets during the times indicated below. "Nix Tix" Draft Queues will have a normal prize payout. 

 ***Thanksgiving***

"Nix Tix" QUEUES BEGIN: Thursday, November 24 9:00 AM  
"Nix Tix" QUEUES END: Monday, November 28 9:00 AM 

 ***Winter Holidays*** 

"Nix Tix" QUEUES BEGIN: Thursday, December 22 9:00 AM  
"Nix Tix" QUEUES END: Monday, December 26 9:00 AM 

 ***New Year*** 

"Nix Tix" QUEUES BEGIN: Thursday, December 29 9:00 AM  
"Nix Tix" QUEUES END: Monday, January 2 9:00 AM 

ADMISSION: 0 Tix, Product

### Premier Events with Promo Card

All Premier Events held during the times indicated below will award the promo card BRAINGEYSER to all participants. Each Premier Event during the times indicated above will award a foil promo BRAINGEYSER to each of the Top 8.

 ***Thanksgiving*** 

Begins: Thursday, November 24 9:00 AM  
Ends: Wednesday, November 30 9:00 AM

 ***Winter Holidays/New Year*** 

Begins: Thursday, December 22 9:00 AM  
Ends: Monday, January 2 9:00 AM

 *Special note regarding the issuing of the promotional card:* 

 *Wizards of the Coast reserves the right to reissue the promo card “Braingeyser” in the future, at its discretion.*
