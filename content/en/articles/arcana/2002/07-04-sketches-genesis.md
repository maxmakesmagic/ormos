This week's Sketches looks at the *Judgment* rare [Genesis](https://web.archive.org/web/20020704000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genesis) from Mark Zug.

### 1\. Art Description

These are the instructions given to the artist:

"This is a green card and should show a jedi in the shape of a centaur made of tree bark. See reference." As [Mark Rosewater has discussed](https://web.archive.org/web/20020704000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/daily/mr20), the incarnations were called "Jedi" during design.
