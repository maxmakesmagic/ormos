Nathan Holiday is mostly known as a constructed player. First rising to prominence after winning Grand Prix San Diego in 2013, he has since added top 8 performances in Phoenix and Minneapolis. However, a 10th place finish at Grand Prix Atlanta proved that Holiday can handle himself in limited as well, and going undefeated on day 1 gave him a strong starting position going into round 10.

Allen Sun is still new to the Grand Prix scene, having played in only one event before GP Salt Lake City. Most of his experience comes from *Magic Online*, and Sun said he has had some difficulty adapting to certain aspects of paper *Magic*. 

Sun played black-red on day 1, one of the least popular color combinations in *Magic 2015* limited. However, he managed to break the mold by not losing a single match in sealed, and when it came time to draft he once again chose to go with black-red. 

Holiday went with a more conventional strategy in draft, building a green-white deck with several copies of [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic) to provide the mana to cast his more powerful cards ahead of schedule. A suite of cheap creatures could allow him to go on the offensive early or buy time until the larger threats took over. 

**Game One** 

Holiday started off with an impressive amount of mana acceleration, casting [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic)s on each of the first three turns. Unfortunately Holiday was lacking a big play to follow them up with, and the Elves ended up doing more attacking than anything else. Fortunately Holiday had a [Sunblade Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sunblade+Elf) to make the 1/1s more effective in combat. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpslc14/Holiday.jpg)

  

The players traded swings, both of them quickly dropping half their life totals. A [Kird Chieftain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kird+Chieftain) from Sun presented a big roadblock for Holiday to get through, even without a Forest on the battlefield. However, a [Titanic Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Titanic+Growth) from Holiday quickly dealt with the problem. 

Sun enchanted [Typhoid Rats](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Typhoid+Rats) with [Burning Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Anger), which started killing one of Holiday's creatures every turn thanks to deathtouch. Sun attacked with an effectively unblockable Krenko's Enforcer to get Holiday into burn range, finishing the game with [Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames) and 1 damage from [Burning Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Anger). 

"Strong combo," said Holiday.

Sun said he was impressed by the amount of mana acceleration in Holiday's deck. Holiday said he wished he had a better top end, then changed his mind.

"I just wish you didn't have [Burning Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Anger)," he said. 

**Nathan Holiday 0-1 Allen Sun** 

**Game Two** 

A [Netcaster Spider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Netcaster+Spider) from Holiday shut down Sun's early attackers. Sun killed it with [Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames), but Holiday tapped it for convoke in response, casting [Chord of Calling](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chord+of+Calling). [Chord of Calling](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chord+of+Calling) put [Yisan, the Wanderer Bard](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yisan%2C+the+Wanderer+Bard) on the battlefield, but Holiday only had time to activate it once. Sun cast [Burning Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Anger) on [Goblin Roughrider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Roughrider) the following turn and tapped it to kill Yisan. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpslc14/Sun.jpg)

  

Now it was Sun who had the dangerous card advantage engine on the battlefield. [Burning Anger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Anger) threatened to deal 3 damage every turn, but Holiday cast [Hunt the Weak](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hunt+the+Weak) to kill the [Goblin Roughrider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Roughrider) at the cost of his [Satyr Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Satyr+Wayfinder). Holiday cast a [Roaring Primadox](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roaring+Primadox) and used it to block Sun's [Gravedigger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravedigger). Sun had had a trick up his sleeve, however. He had finally drawn a second mountain and used it to cast [Cone of Flame](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cone+of+Flame), killing the Primadox along with [Sunblade Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sunblade+Elf). 

Holiday's life total started dropping quickly. Sun used [Rummaging Goblin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rummaging+Goblin) to keep drawing creatures while Holiday saw only lands. [Sungrace Pegasus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sungrace+Pegasus) blocked to keep Holiday alive at 1, but after drawing yet another land, he fell to Sun's army of creatures. 

Holiday said that he was hoping to draw into some of his larger threats, but didn't get there in time. Sun commented on the effectiveness of [Rummaging Goblin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rummaging+Goblin), and Holiday agreed. 

"Card is good," he said.

Nathan Holiday 0-2 Allen Sun

**Holiday dropped to 9-1, Sun advanced to 9-0-1**
