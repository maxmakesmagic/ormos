How would you like to go to the *Return to Ravnica* party at PAX Prime? Well, you can't. It already happened. You're a month late.

But don't feel bad! Part of the experience is still available to you! People at PAX who wanted to go to the party had to solve one of ten guild-themed riddles. And we have them here for you!
