Looking for something new and fun to play in *Shards of Alara* Block play? A\_Webb managed to take this version of a Grixis control deck to second place in a recent Premier Event on **Magic Online**. Cruel Ultimatum, Grixis Charm, Sedraxis Specter; the deck teems with the very best (or worst, considering their plane of existence!) Grixis has to throw at the world. And check out none other than Nicol Bolas, Planeswalker ready to rumble in the sideboard!
