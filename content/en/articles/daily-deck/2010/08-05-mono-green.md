Red and green decks starring Primeval Titan have been getting the lion's share of attention lately in the world of Standard. But at a Concord Pro Tour Qualifier, Roger Samson managed to make the Top 8 with a build featuring only green cards. The deck looks a bit like the Eldrazi Green decks of the past year but with some helpful ***Magic** 2011* additions like the aforementioned Titan and Overwhelming Stampede. One can only imagine few opponents are going to withstand the power of THAT sorcery!
