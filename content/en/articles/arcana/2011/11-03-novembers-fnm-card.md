It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20111103000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=events/magic/fnm)! And since it's the first Friday in November, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

![Go For the Throat](https://i.ormos.uk/80/c0/80c0848ac4c4ddf5c15016d0e5eee5e202aae3721a4c1120d94b4b9914b9999c.jpg)  

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards often feature original flavor text and updated wordings.
