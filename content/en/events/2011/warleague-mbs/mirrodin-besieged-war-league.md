*   ### Choose a side – Mirrodin or Phyrexia!

*   ### Defeat players from the other faction for your own glory and for the benefit of your faction

*   ### Play often – ALL in-store play counts, including Friday Night Magic and Launch Parties

*   ### Update your deck weekly with a new booster pack

Starting on February 4, many stores will be running War Leagues. You simply choose Phyrexia or Mirrodin and you play as often as you like. Each time you win, you mark your win on the poster in the store, and if you defeat someone from the opposing faction you can put a sticker on the poster in an epic game of tug-o-war between the Mirrans and Phyrexians. Each week you can upgrade your deck by purchasing another *Mirrodin Besieged* booster pack.

War Leagues are not sanctioned, so you can play in a more casual environment. They are a great way to meet new people, develop camaraderie or friendly rivalry—depending on their faction. All in-store play counts for the league, so your FNM matches and other games played at the store will all help you achieve victory for your faction.

Stores can run as many as they want through the release of "Action." If your store isn't running a league, ask them to start one.
