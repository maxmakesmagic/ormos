M**irror Gallery** (a *Betrayers of Kamigawa* card [previewed today](/en/articles/houseofcards/2005/01-20-mirror-gallery-yrellag-rorrim) by Mark Gottlieb) depicts the interior of a grand, Soratami cloud palace. It's filled with mirrors reflecting a regal-looking moonfolk in the foreground.

![Mirror Gallery art by Scott M. Fischer](https://i.ormos.uk/65/5a/655a852861d3994c2c4257cef36d66a6d3abff514492957cf6e64f8f0c4ca62b.jpg)  
*Mirror Gallery art by Scott M. Fischer*

That moonfolk is none other than the legendary Meloku the Clouded Mirror, also illustrated by Scott M. Fischer.

![Meloku the Clouded Mirror art by Scott M. Fischer](https://i.ormos.uk/41/c1/41c1b366c8f75af489e35e265c7d9d96a81805cb57eb47507272f9a7d62964ea.jpg)
