You know who we haven't heard from in a few years? Our old friend [Mistform Ultimus](https://web.archive.org/web/20110511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistform+Ultimus)! [Last time](https://web.archive.org/web/20110511000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/mistform-ultimus-watch-conflux-edition-2009-03-02) we saw ol' Ulty, there were 216 creature types in **Magic**. And now? Well, take a look!

The new types since *Conflux* (has it been that long? We were all so young then, weren't we?) are Ally, Eldrazi, Germ, Gremlin, Hippogriff, Praetor, Siren, and Surrakar. Here's a piece of bonus trivia: Gremlin was introduced for [Flensermite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flensermite), but [Phyrexian Gremlins](https://web.archive.org/web/20110511000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Gremlins) got retroactively re-Gremlinized.
