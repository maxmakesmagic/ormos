After 15 hard-fought rounds of Standard, with a total of 1,162 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running. Their Top 8 performance came with invitations and airfare to Pro Tour *Ixalan* in Albuquerque…and the opportunity to fight for the Grand Prix trophy.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpams17/GPAmsterdam_Top-8-Group-Shot.jpg)

Learn more about Grand Prix Amsterdam's Top 8 competitors—including who they are, why they chose their Standard deck, and more—by taking a look through the Top 8 profiles!

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpams17/GPAmsterdam_Top8_Micheal-Maurici.jpg)MICHEAL MAURICI**

**Age**: 28

**Hometown**: Adelaide, Australia

**Occupation:** Accountant / Magic Grinder

**Number of byes:** 2

**Previous Magic accomplishments**: A few Pro Tours and Grand Prix Top 16s.

**What Standard deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?** Esper Aggro – great Marvel and U/R Control matchup. I wanted to play an aggressive deck in this metagame that had permission.

**What were the best cards in your main deck, and why?** [Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar) (single-handedly wins matches) and [Irrigated Farmland](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Irrigated+Farmland) (fixes the flooding and smooths draws).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Skysovereign, Consul Flagship](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skysovereign%2C+Consul+Flagship) – when it resolved it ensured I won the close games.

**What are your most important cards against Temur Marvel decks, and what is the match-up about?** [Metallic Rebuke](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metallic+Rebuke) and [Spell Queller](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Queller) – they allow me to play permission and stop [Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel) or their energy once I have small creatures in play.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpams17/GPAmsterdam_Top8_Arjan-van-Leeuwen.jpg)ARJAN VAN LEEUWEN**

**Age**: 48

**Hometown**: Rotterdam, the Netherlands

**Occupation:** IT

**Number of byes:** 2

**Previous Magic accomplishments**: Two Grand Prix wins.

**What Standard deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?** R/G Pummeler. It's so explosive.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Electrostatic Pummeler](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrostatic+Pummeler).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Magma Spray](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Spray).

**What are your most important cards against Temur Marvel decks, and what is the match-up about?** Just be quick, and hope they miss with [Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel) :)

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpams17/GPAmsterdam_Top8_Benjamin-Luft.jpg)BENJAMIN LUFT**

**Age**: 31

**Hometown**: Hannover, Germany

**Occupation:** Tax Consultant Assistant

**Number of byes:** 2

**Previous Magic accomplishments**: Attended some PPTQs (with little success).

**What Standard deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?** Blue-Red Control. I expected mostly Marvel and U/R on Day 2 and felt comfortable with my deck in those match-ups. My prediction turned out to be wrong, but the deck carried me.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Torrential Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrential+Gearhulk). Because UHulk!

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Kefnet the Mindful](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kefnet+the+Mindful). Blocks everything and single-handedly wins the mirror.

**What are your most important cards against Temur Marvel decks, and what is the match-up about?** Lots of counterspells and card draw. It's about not falling behind and gaining incremental advantages from turn four on.

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpams17/GPAmsterdam_Top8_Lukas-Blohon2.jpg)LUKAS BLOHON**

**Age**: 27

**Hometown**: Prague, Czech Republic

**Occupation:** Magic

**Number of byes:** 3

**Previous Magic accomplishments**: Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon* Champion.

**What Standard deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?** B/G Constrictor. Shaun McLaren built a great deck so Thomas Hendriks, Ivan Floch, and I tested it and played it. Most of the played decks now are good-ish matchups.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Winding Constrictor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Winding+Constrictor).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Grasp of Darkness](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grasp+of+Darkness).

**What are your most important cards against Temur Marvel decks, and what is the match-up about?** Them not hitting [Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulamog%2C+the+Ceaseless+Hunger) on the first spin so you can destroy [Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel) with [Dissenter's Deliverance](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dissenter%27s+Deliverance). It is important to keep pressure on them. [Dispossess](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dispossess) is good in most games.

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpams17/GPAmsterdam_Top8_Jelco-Bodewes2.jpg)JELCO BODEWES**

**Age**: 23

**Hometown**: Zeist, Netherlands

**Occupation:** Master student

**Number of byes:** 1

**Previous Magic accomplishments**: Pro Tour *Kaladesh* 8-8 through RPTQ.

**What Standard deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?** Temur Marvel. Before this Standard, my deck was Saheeli combo. Value-based midrange decks with a strong combo element are my favorite.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ulamog%2C+the+Ceaseless+Hunger), both through [Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel) and via [Shrine of the Forsaken Gods](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrine+of+the+Forsaken+Gods) the card is absurd. I beat U/R by hard-casting three in a row, putting my opponent to one land.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Confiscation Coup](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Confiscation+Coup). When the best cards in your deck are [Tireless Tracker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tracker) and [Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel), stealing them is pretty good in the mirror.

**What are your most important cards against Temur Marvel decks, and what is the match-up about?** As mentioned before, turn-four Ulamog game one, and Trackers and Coup post-board. Pre-board it's a combo race; post-board it's a grind with counterspells and Trackers.

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpams17/GPAmsterdam_Top8_Thomas-Hendriks2.jpg)THOMAS HENDRIKS**

**Age**: 24

**Hometown**: Leiden, Netherlands

**Occupation:** Student

**Number of byes:** 3

**Previous Magic accomplishments**: 2 Grand Prix Top 8s and 1 Pro Tour Top 8.

**What Standard deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?** B/G Tracker because it's a deck with a lot of late game that can also explode and win fast (which is nice versus Marvel).

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Tireless Tracker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tracker). Doesn't matter how it happens, if the game goes longer you are doing great.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Grasp of Darkness](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grasp+of+Darkness). (Thanks to Martin Dang). Makes [Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer) less unbeatable!

**What are your most important cards against Temur Marvel decks, and what is the match-up about?** [Dispossess](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dispossess). Forces the game into a midrange game, with them having bad/dead cards. (Spoiler: B/G often wins those midrange games.)

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpams17/GPAmsterdam_Top8_Alexander-Mertins.jpg)ALEXANDER MERTINS**

**Age**: 26

**Hometown**: Ringe, Germany

**Occupation:** Finished study

**Number of byes:** 0

**Previous Magic accomplishments**: Multiple FNM wins, lol.

**What Standard deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?** I chose B/G because I don't like Marvel and play too slow to execute control decks. I had all cards (mostly) from the previous Standard.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Winding Constrictor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Winding+Constrictor). It makes the whole deck viable with the synergies.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Transgress the Mind](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transgress+the+Mind) and [Dispossess](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dispossess). Taking Marvel on turn 3 feels good.

**What are your most important cards against Temur Marvel decks, and what is the match-up about?** Having a fast clock game one and a good sideboard for games two and three.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpams17/GPAmsterdam_Top8_Raphael-Coors.jpg)RAPHAEL COORS**

**Age**: 30

**Hometown**: Osnabrück, Germany

**Occupation:** Pharmacist

**Number of byes:** 2

**Previous Magic accomplishments**: Several wins of local tournaments, PTQ Top 8, SSS Top 8.

**What Standard deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?** Green-Black Energy. I tested a lot on Blue-Red Control and didn't feel safe on the Marvel match-up. So this was a last-minute choice inspired by Jonathan Rowe.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Glint-Sleeve Siphoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint-Sleeve+Siphoner). It felt like the better [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** [Dispossess](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dispossess). I like Magic and not gambling.

**What are your most important cards against Temur Marvel decks, and what is the match-up about?** [Transgress the Mind](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Transgress+the+Mind) and [Dispossess](https://web.archive.org/web/20170604000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dispossess). Pre-board I lost a lot against them. Post-board it felt a lot better.
