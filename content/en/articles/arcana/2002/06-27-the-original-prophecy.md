If you thought the set *Prophecy* was a little sub-par, check out the *card* Prophecy.

This *Homelands* common lifegain spell (I use the term loosely) is interesting because of it's art. In the center of the eye is reflected the real boss of *Homelands*, Ihsan's Shade. Baron Sengir had nothing on that guy.
