Pauper is a popular **Magic Online** format that allows players to use only commons when building their decks. The result? SlashRus recently used Rats, of all things, to succeed in the format. Both Chittering Rats and Crypt Rats saw play front and center alongside black's most powerful common removal like Tendrils of Corruption. Add some card drawing in the form of Mulldrifter and you've got a recipe for success!
