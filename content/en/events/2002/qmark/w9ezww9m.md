### 

### October 2002: Words

**Tuesday, October 1:**  
 What non-*Unglue*d card mentions the game of **Magic** by name in its rules text?  
 Answer: Shahrazad (from *Arabian Nights*)

**Wednesday, October 2:**  
 There is a card that has only two lines of rules text. Those two lines are identical except two words are swapped with each other in the second line. What card is this?  
**Answer**: Righteous War (from *Visions*): "All white creatures you control gain protection from black. All black creatures you control gain protection from white."

**Thursday, October 3:**  
 What sorcery in *Seventh Edition* has identical rules text as it did in Alpha?  
**Answer**: Stream of Life: "Target players gains X life."\]

**Friday, October 4:**  
 What creature's rules text as written instructs the creature to make a decision? **Answer**: Floral Spuzzem (from *Legends*): "If Floral Spuzzem attacks an opponent and is not blocked, then Floral Spuzzem may choose to destroy a target artifact under that opponent’s control and deal no damage."

**Monday, October 7:**  
 What card has the terms "1/1" and "2/2" appear in the card's text box?  
**Answer**: Broodhatch Nantuko (from *Onslaught*): Whenever Broodhatch Nantuko is dealt damage, you may put that many 1/1 green Insect creature tokens into play. Morph ![2 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/f9/cc/f9cc8a15b009997919d58ad79964384351495d1b6c9231f938316577453038a8.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif) (You may play this face down as a 2/2 creature for ![3 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/7f/5d/7f5d2f1e55eaa9d19a9a23027b63bfbb5b39dbf8642160dda1a477a03df23a6a.gif). Turn it face up any time for its morph cost.) 

Tuesday, October 8:  
 What card was the first one to mention another card in its rules text? I am not counting cards that reference basic land or cards that do not use the full card name (so Alpha Gloom doesn't count.) Note that there are two acceptable answers.  
**Answer:**Camel and Desert Nomads (from *Arabian Nights*). Both reference the card Desert.

**Thursday, October 10**:  
 What was the first card to have a mana symbol in its text box not of the card's color?  
**Answer:**Sedge Troll (from Alpha). It had a black activation.

**Friday, October 11:**  
 What card was printed with the most lines of rules text?  
**Answer:**Ice Cauldron (from *Ice Age*). It has 11 lines of rules text. This goal will never be broken as we no longer allow ourselves to print cards with text that small.

**Monday, October 14:**  
 What card from Alpha if reprinted today using its Oracle wording would have a "X" and "Y" in its casting cost? (And I'm talking about "X" and "Y" as variables not random letters sitting inside a word.)  
**Answer:**Fireball. It now deals X damage to Y creatures. And its casting cost is now ![X Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/b1/6bb1152b0a3305d0a7c2e2eb725b8ebd18620607efc62f6f38b0642b97c8501f.gif)![Y Mana](http://www.wizards.com/images/Symbols/Symbol_Y_mana.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif).

**Tuesday, October 15:**  
There is a counter type \[i.e. the name of a counter such as pin counter\] that exists on only two cards. Both cards are of the same color and card type and both were first printed before 1996. What is the card type and what are the two cards?  
**Answer:** Wind counters; Cyclone (*Arabian Nights*) & Freyalise's Winds (*Ice Age*).

**Thursday, October 17:**  
 What is the largest number to appear in rules text?  
**Answer:** 360 on the card Falling Star (*Legends*). The card must "rotate 360 degrees".

**Friday, October 18:**  
 What was the text of the original Time Walk in playtest before the game was released?  
**Answer:** "Target opponent loses next turn." Charlie Catino got it changed when he commented to Richard, "So I play this and automatically win next turn?

**Monday, October 21:**  
In one expansion, the rules text of every non-land card had the same word appear. What was the word and what was the expansion?  
**Answer:** The word was "artifact" and the expansion was *Antiquities*.
