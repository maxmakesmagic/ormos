![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/ptla05/fm2_shuanton.jpg)*A rematch from the finals of Pro Tour-Nagoya.*

Anton and Shu share a moment in time. Anton had just played Mystic [Restrain](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restrain)ts on Shu's [Order](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Order) of the Sacred Bell. The aura served to keep Anton's hopes of winning Pro Tour-Nagoya alive by tapping down the 4/3 monk. Anton knew his opponent; this was the third time they had played over the weekend. He was down two games to one in the finals, but this was his fifth top eight and he had never been so close to being a Pro Tour champion.

From the Pro Tour-Nagoya coverage by Scott Johns:

  

*   "With one card left in hand, Shu drew what has to be one of the best top decks in Pro Tour Finals history. With [Kodama's Might](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kodama%27s+Might) in hand, he ripped his next card off the top and didn't even try to hide his complete astonishment when he saw what he'd pulled.
     Anton cringed at the reaction, holding a fist of good spells he was finally ready to start casting once he untapped. But that turn never came. [Teller of Tales](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teller+of+Tales) hit Shu's side of the table and the visibly excited Japanese player quickly slapped his [Kodama's Might](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kodama%27s+Might) on the tapped [Order](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Order) of the Sacred Bell, untapping it thanks to the Teller! In almost the same motion Shu untapped the [Order](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Order) and shoved it into the red zone, and suddenly the tournament was over! For a moment the crowd didn't even realize it was done, and a stunned Anton just sat there staring, unable to move, unable to believe it was somehow over . . . just like that."  
  
   

Now almost a year later, they met again. Instead of the finals, though, this time they were both fighting at the 0-1 feature match.

Talking with Anton on Friday morning, he revealed that his play testing for this event consisted of getting together with Johan Sadeghpour. They both live in the same city in Sweden, and Anton only feels comfortable testing live. They ended up getting their green, black and white deck from a friend. Shu worked with legendary deck builder Itaru Ishida. Ishida's deck, [Dredge](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dredge)-atog, combines the power of [Gifts Ungiven](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gifts+Ungiven) with [Life](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Life) from the Loam and cycling lands. The deck tries to take complete control by using the powerful dredge engine to strip and counter the opponent's resources to nothing.

### Game 1

Shu started with a [Polluted Delta](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Polluted+Delta), while Anton had a turn-one [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise). Anton had a turn-two [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) met with a [Force Spike](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+Spike) from Shu. Anton laid out a turn-three [Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top) but when he missed his third land drop, Shu pounced by hitting the [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise) with a [Putrefy](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrefy). Shu's game plan appeared to revolve around keeping Anton manascrewed.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/ptla05/fm2_shu.jpg)*A misplaced sideboard card cost Shu a game loss.*

Shu didn't appear to have much countermagic in his hand but Anton was light on both threats and mana. Anton finally found his third land by searching with the Top and placing on top. Shu, meanwhile, just kept playing out lands.

With five lands out, Shu cast a [Gifts Ungiven](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gifts+Ungiven). He went for [Life](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Life) from the Loam and [Barren Moor](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barren+Moor). While still searching his deck, Shu paused and started to look through again and again. Finally, he looked up and asked for a judge. Shu had realized that a [Last Gasp](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Last+Gasp) from his sideboard was still in his main deck from the previous round.

After some double-checking the judge gave Shu a game loss in a game. After the match Anton said that Shu certainly would have won the game had he not received the penalty, but that his honesty must be applauded.

Anton 1, Shu 0

### Game 2

Shu laid out land for the first few turns of Game 2 while Anton developed his board with a first-turn [Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top), followed by a turn-two [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise). The Top began to find Anton the card's he needed as he sacrificed a [Flooded Strand](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flooded+Strand) to access new cards with the Top.

Anton continued to take the lead when he played a [Cunning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cunning+Wish) to fetch a [Gifts Ungiven](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gifts+Ungiven). When Anton cast a [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy), Shu finally made a move responding with a [Gifts Ungiven](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gifts+Ungiven).

This game, the Gifts didn't mean instant death for Shu as he got a [Night](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Night)mare [Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Void), [Life](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Life) from the Loam, [Barren Moor](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barren+Moor) and [Lonely Sandbar](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lonely+Sandbar). Anton chose to give him the two spells and named [Life](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Life) from the Loam for the Therapy. Shu revealed a [Life](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Life) from the Loam, [Night](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Night)mare [Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Void), [Counterspell](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterspell), [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog), two [Pernicious Deed](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pernicious+Deed)s and a land.

Anton knew he needed to get busy before the [Dredge](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dredge) engine kicked in. He cast a [Gifts Ungiven](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gifts+Ungiven) of his own. After about five minutes Anton pulled out [Cranial Extraction](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Extraction), [Nostalgic Dreams](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nostalgic+Dreams), a Russian 9th edition [Reclaim](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reclaim), and [Eternal Witness](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eternal+Witness). Now it was time for Shu to go into the tank and finally he gave Anton the [Reclaim](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reclaim) and [Nostalgic Dreams](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nostalgic+Dreams).

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/ptla05/fm2_anton.jpg)*Despite never dealing a point of damage, Anton proved victorious.*

With the entirety of card tutoring out of the way, the discard kicked into overdrive. Shu played a [Night](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Night)mare [Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Void) on his turn taking the [Nostalgic Dreams](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nostalgic+Dreams) out of Anton's hand. Anton fought back as he used the [Reclaim](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reclaim) to bring back [Cranial Extraction](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Extraction) and named [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog) ripping one from Shu's hand and two from his deck.

Shu decided to reclaim nightmare void and didn't like what he saw as Anton laid down [Cunning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cunning+Wish), Top and [Genesis](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genesis). Shu took the [Cunning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cunning+Wish). Anton played [Genesis](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genesis) on his turn and sacrificed it to flashback a [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy) hitting [Counterspell](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterspell). When Shu dredged the [Night](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Night)mare [Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Void), he milled [Wonder](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wonder) into his graveyard. Shu realized that he has no ways to win left in his deck (the [Wonder](https://web.archive.org/web/20051028000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wonder) was the last damage source in his deck) and conceded.

Anton won the match without either player attacking or dealing a single point of damage to the other.

Anton Jonsson 2, Shu Komuro 0
