### June 2002: Mono-Blue Cards

**June 3**  
 Q: What card is also known as the "blue boon?"  
 A: Ancestral Recall. It along with Healing Salve, Dark Ritual, Lightning Bolt and Giant Growth are known as the boons. This cycle of five cards from alpha are all cards for a single mana which produces three of something.  
 Winner: Nick Blas

**June 4**  
 Q: What blue creature references itself five times in its rules text?  
 A: Morphling (*Urza's Saga*)  
 Winner: Frank Nelson

**June 5**  
 Q: What blue card was the first to have its rarity changed and be reprinted as an uncommon?  
 A: Sleight of Mind (*Ice Age*). The alpha version of the card was rare while the *Ice Age* version was uncommon.  
 Winner: Jeff Urich

**June 10**  
 Q: What blue card, from an expansion, was the first ever printed without an expansion symbol?  
 A: Reconstruction (*Antiquities*)  
 Winner: Ian McBride

**June 11**  
 Q: What blue card first appeared in *Portal* under the name Cloud Pirates?  
 A: Cloud Sprite (*Mercadian Masques*)\]  
 Winner: Jordan Justman

**June 12**  
 Q: What blue creature is identical to a green creature in every way (identical converted mana cost, card type, rules text, power and toughness) except that the mana in its mana cost is blue instead of green?  
 A: Glacial Wall (*Ice Age*). It is identical to Wall of Ice from alpha. Flying Men and Scryb Sprites have different creature types.  
 Winner: Trevor Barrie

**June 14**  
 Q: What blue card was called "Howling Mind" in design?  
 A: Words of Wisdom (*Odyssey*)  
 Winner: Aaron Porath

**June 17**  
 Q: What card in *Mirage* was originally called Mirage but had to be changed when the set got its name?  
 A: Shimmer (*Mirage*)  
 Winner: Joseph DeMattei

**June 18**  
 Q: What blue art had its artwork's orientation flipped when it was reprinted in the basic set?  
 A: Soldevi Sage (*Alliances*). The orginal artwork was accidentally printed backwards. This mistake was corrected when the card was reprinted in 7th Edition.  
 Winner: Christopher Eby

**June 19**  
 Q: What blue creature was almost released under the name Rootwater Torpedo?  
 A: Rootwater Thief (*Nemesis*)  
 Winner: Jurgen Schroven

**June 20**  
 Q: What was the first blue creature with an enemy color activation?  
 A: Electric Eel (*The Dark*). It had an activation of ![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif).  
 Winner: Puyo

**June 21**  
 Q: What two blue enchantments have titles which reference a nonbasic land printed several years earlier?  
 A: Pendrell Mists (*Weatherlight*) and Pendrell Flux (*Urza's Saga*). The cards reference the legendary land The Tabernacle at Pendrell Vale from *Legends*.  
 Winner: Tom Brown

**June 24**  
 Q: While watching Zvi Mowshowitz play his winning deck from Pro Tour - Paris in the Auction of Champions at the **Magic** Invitational in Sydney, Mike Long said "Why the hell did I play that?" of what blue card?  
 A: Three Wishes (*Visions*)  
 Winner: Rickard Hedlund

**June 25**  
 Q: What blue card can be Shattered?  
 A: Copy Artifact (Alpha). Copy Artifact copies all elements of an artifact including its artifact status.  
 Winner: Laurent Nguyen

**June 26**  
 Q: What Extended-legal blue common card has more lines of text than any other?  
 A: Balduvian Shaman (*Ice Age*). It has eight lines of rules text.  
 Winner: Jeff Boehm

**June 27**  
 Q: What blue card which was later banned was voted as the worst card of its expansion by InQuest Magazine?  
 A: Dream Halls (*Stronghold*)  
 Winner: Chris Waits

**June 28**  
 Q: What blue card played in Extended was knocked out of numerous sets (including Tempest and Urza's Saga) by development teams for being "too weak"?  
 A: Donate (*Urza's Saga*). This card was a pet favorite so I kept submitting it until it got printed. Oops.  
 Winner: Ryan Strand
