## *Rivals of Ixalan* Preview League

If you can't even bear to wait until the Prerelease to try the new cards of *Rivals of Ixalan*, we've got good news! Starting January 11 at 10 a.m. PT, we will be running a 24-hour *Rivals* Sealed Deck league. It's open to all players—no qualifying necessary—and will feature a friendly prize structure that guarantees you'll walk away with at least one *Rivals of Ixalan* booster regardless of your record.

Perhaps you'd rather see how your favorite *Magic* streamers do with the new cards? We've got you covered there, too! For the *entire 24-hour duration* of the Preview League, a rotating cast of beloved streamers from the *Magic* community will be joining the action and streaming it live for your viewing pleasure at any hour of the day (or night). Check the tables below to see who's streaming when.

To learn more about the Preview League, check out [Lee Sharpe's article here](/en/articles/magiconline/2018/01-03-get-ready-for-the-magic-online-rix-preview-league).

## Vintage Cube Runs through Jan. 15

Vintage Cube's longest-ever run is coming to an end, but you've still got almost a week left to draft those powerful, ridiculous, seriously-who-thought-that-was-okay Vintage cards. Use those skills you've built up over the last few weeks between now and Monday, January 15, to put together whatever sort of busted storm, ramp, aggro, or anything-else deck your heart desires.

Learn more about Vintage Cube [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/2017/12-05-vintage-masters-flashbacks-and-vintage-cube).

## Build Notes

Collection | *Magic Online* Player Rewards Packs for December have been granted. You can find out more information regarding this [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20180109000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-player-rewards).

Account | An issue that sometimes prevented the key bindings for "always yes to this" and "always yield to this" from functioning correctly has been fixed.

Vintage, Modern | [Hollow One](https://web.archive.org/web/20180109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hollow+One) now correctly interacts with madness cards such as [Fiery Temper](https://web.archive.org/web/20180109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fiery+Temper) that were discarded but not cast.

Commander | An issue with the art of tokens created by [Omnath, Locus of Rage](https://web.archive.org/web/20180109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Omnath%2C+Locus+of+Rage) has been fixed.

Pauper | Destroying [Oubliette](https://web.archive.org/web/20180109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oubliette) no longer prevents the game from proceeding.

Modern | There is no longer a chance for a game reset when a spell is spliced onto another spell that requires a target.

Collection | Certain booster packs and intro packs are no longer smaller than their kin.

Limited | [Otherworldly Outburst](https://web.archive.org/web/20180109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Otherworldly+Outburst) now gives the targeted creature a delayed trigger shield with the appropriate information.

Collection | [Reprocess](https://web.archive.org/web/20180109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reprocess) from *Urza's Saga* now has art.

Collection | Level up creatures such as [Student of Warfare](https://web.archive.org/web/20180109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Student+of+Warfare) no longer have blue power and toughness unless it is modified.

Duel Scene | Revealing a card as a cost during your opponent's turn no longer appears in your opponent's revealed card zone.

Duel Scene | An issue with targeting arrows pointing in the wrong direction has been fixed.
