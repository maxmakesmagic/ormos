![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpatc15/t8_gau.jpg)Name: Gaudenis Vidugiris

  
 Age: 30

  
 Hometown: Vilnius / Madison / New York

  
 Previous Magic Accomplishments: Three-time Grand Prix champion; Pro Tour Avacyn Restored finalist

What did you play on Saturday, and why?  
 Red-White Aggro with [Citadel Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Citadel+Siege) and two Runemarks. My pool wasn't good and [Citadel Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Citadel+Siege) is amazing, so I tried to be as aggro as possible.

What did you draft today?  
 Red-Green Aggro and Blue-Red Control

  
 What were your best commons or uncommons this weekend? Why?  
[Goblin Heelcutter](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Heelcutter): The card is unbeatable in aggro. [Abzan Runemark](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Runemark): It was the only way my Sealed pool could win tihout drawing [Citadel Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Citadel+Siege).

  
 What do you want open for pack one, pick one? Why?  
 A Dragonlord. They are almost unbeatable. (Also, less thinking for the rest of the draft.)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpatc15/t8_neal.jpg)Name: Stephen Neal

  
 Age: 29

  
 Hometown: Madison, Wisconsin

  
 Previous Magic Accomplishments: Madison and Milwaukee city champion. Top 8 at the Pro Tours in San Juan and Barcelona.

  
 What did you play on Saturday, and why?  
 White-Black with [Citadel Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Citadel+Siege) and [Brutal Hordechief](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brutal+Hordechief), because those cards are unbeatable.

  
 What did you draft today?  
 Blue-Black, 2-1. Black-Red, 2-0-1.

  
 What were your best commons or uncommons this weekend? Why?  
[Rakshasa Gravecaller](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Gravecaller) and [Deadly Wanderings](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deadly+Wanderings), because they weren't in the same deck. [Butcher's Glee](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Butcher%27s+Glee) also overperformed.

  
 What do you want open for pack one, pick one? Why?  
 Any Dragonlord. Failing that, [Rakshasa Gravecaller](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Gravecaller).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpatc15/t8_hayne.jpg)Name: Alexander Hayne

  
 Age: 26

  
 Hometown: Westmount, Canada

  
 Previous Magic Accomplishments: Four Grand Prix wins, three other Top 8s. Pro Tour Avacyn Restored Champion. Rookie of the Year, 2011-2012.

  
 What did you play on Saturday, and why?  
 I played Blue-Black, splash Kolaghan, the Storm's Fury. I prepared by Skype-ing with Limited master Mike Sigrist, who loves Blue-Black, and it was one of the deeper combinations in my pool.

  
 What did you draft today?  
 Draft 1: Green-Blue Conifder Strider / [Taigam's Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Taigam%27s+Strike) combo. 2-1. It was awful. I got lucky.  
 Draft 2: Red-White Aggro. Good curve, good removal, no cares. 3-0.

  
 What were your best commons or uncommons this weekend? Why?  
[Strongarm Monk](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strongarm+Monk). It just makes combat impossible for your opponents.

  
 What do you want open for pack one, pick one? Why?  
[Dragonlord Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar). Blue-Black is great and deep, and having a giant, flying, deathtouch creature, plus your opponent's best creature seems good. Also, I'm a proud Dimir mage.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpatc15/t8_esse.jpg)Name: Zachary Jesse

  
 Age: 30

  
 Hometown: Richmond, Virginia

  
 Previous Magic Accomplishments: One Pro Tour Top 16. Two or three Pro Tour Top 25s. One Grand Prix Top 8.

  
 What did you play on Saturday, and why?  
 Blue-Red Unblockable. My deck had no rares, but a bunch of unblockable creatures and flyers. First time I've every played [Dragonrage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonrage) happily. It killed a lot of people out of nowhere.

  
 What did you draft today?  
 Draft 1: Red-Green Dudes. I had a Descent of the Dragons; never cast it.  
 Draft 2: White-Black, splashing [Boltwing Marauder](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boltwing+Marauder).

  
 What were your best commons or uncommons this weekend? Why?  
[Dragonrage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonrage) won me several games with my Sealed deck that I probably had no business winning. I didn't play it main deck, and I sided it in every game.

  
 What do you want open for pack one, pick one? Why?  
 Besides an insane rare? [Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price) or [Rakshasa Gravecaller](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Gravecaller).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpatc15/t8_calcano.jpg)Name: Christian Calcano

  
 Age: 26

  
 Hometown: New York, NY

  
 Previous Magic Accomplishments: Five Grand Prix Top 8s. Two Pro Tour Top 16s.

What did you play on Saturday, and why?  
 Blue-Black, splashing white. Black was my best color and blue was my best support color. Had two on-color white duals, so I splashed an extra win condition and removal spell.

  
 What did you draft today?  
 Blue-Black (3-0) and Black-Red (2-0-1).

  
 What were your best commons or uncommons this weekend? Why?  
[Ojutai's Breath](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ojutai%27s+Breath) in the first draft won me multiple games I would've lost otherwise. Second draft: [Qal Sisma Behemoth](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Qal+Sisma+Behemoth). A 5/5 for 3 mana is insane.

  
 What do you want open for pack one, pick one? Why?  
[Dragonlord Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar) because it's unfair.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpatc15/t8_scott-vargas.jpg)Name: Luis Scott-Vargas

Age: 32

  
 Hometown: Oakland

  
 Previous Magic Accomplishments: Hall of Fame. Third in Vintage Super League, season two. Second in Vintage Super League, season one.

 Red-Green Midrange, because I had a bunch of removal and a few finishers.

 Two Blue-Back Control decks. Both were top-heavy and full of value cards.

[Aven Surveyor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Surveyor) and [Reach of Shadows](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reach+of+Shadows) were both great at dealing with opposing anything.

[Dragonlord Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar), so I can draft Blue-Black.
