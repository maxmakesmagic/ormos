What began as a rough, pencil-sketched, woodland creature in Greg Staples's sketchbook...

![Sketch by Greg Staples](https://i.ormos.uk/8a/02/8a02295c07975cfb04a0cc0aadd28c0830b559c76ed1f900d4b5f3fec034dc43.jpg)

...grew into an enormous, creaking elemental of the woods...

![Sketch by Greg Staples](https://i.ormos.uk/8c/72/8c727d4c7fda16f2344a99efbfc52eb8fa1ec98a6987fc668df43f21a883868a.jpg)

...and finally into the *Ninth Edition* art for **Force of Nature**.

![Force of Nature art by Greg Staples](https://i.ormos.uk/da/75/da75fb84c9261b7ce5eebe4ea2c101318635a033985a33127b59d16fc1b2370a.jpg)

[*Ninth Edition*](https://web.archive.org/web/20080429041234/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/products/9elinks) releases July 29th. Are you as excited as we are?
