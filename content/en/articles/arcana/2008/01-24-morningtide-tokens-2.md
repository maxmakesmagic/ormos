It's time for everyone's favorite fake game show, "Meet the *Morningtide* Creature Tokens"!

First up, we have a 2/5 Treefolk Shaman:

![Treefolk Shaman](https://i.ormos.uk/4d/a9/4da9060951fb38a57469d81abc3b0b85b45e5f70d35fec2e21491a54c740f613.jpg "Treefolk Shaman")

This happy little fellow will come in handy when you play your Reach of Branches. Next, meet our second contestant. He's a 1/1 Faerie Rogue, he flies, and he enjoys long walks on the beach and Jazzercise:

![Faerie Rogue](https://i.ormos.uk/bb/83/bb8324b09d53bdc107fe48c17a8a24cfb752eb1b0e8122a2f31bc0adf6ff9354.jpg "Faerie Rogue")

The Faerie Rogue is usually seen in the company of Bitterblossom, Notorious Throng, and Violet Pall. Our third creature token is a systems analyst from the Osterman-Price accounting firm—no, sorry, that's wrong. Our third creature token is actually a mighty 5/5 Giant Warrior!

![Giant Warrior](https://i.ormos.uk/b2/ca/b2cacfd945bea2bbec62da9d36d84cc22598094c85ce73b61b88f4da17dc8a09.jpg "Giant Warrior")

The Giant Warrior is a byproduct of Feudkiller's Verdict. That's our *Morningtide* Creature Token lineup. Let's give them all a big hand!

Of course, the *backs* of the creature tokens are not completely without interest. In addition to some fascinating ads, there are also five rules tips:

![Morningtide Rules Tips Cards](https://i.ormos.uk/ab/00/ab0088e7036616192eba4c5a1f2ab951ef65c6948b3483c4160e68e190290c09.jpg "Morningtide Rules Tips Cards")

To get a better look at the rules tips, the best plan would be to show up bright and early at your local [*Morningtide* Launch Party](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-launch-parties-4) and buy yourself some *Morningtide* packs. Select locations in North America are having Midnight Madness events ([USA Schedule](/en/events/2008/releases-na/eventide-launch-parties-north-america#usa1); [Canada Schedule](/en/events/2008/releases-na/eventide-launch-parties-north-america#canada1)) so you can get your cards even earlier!
