Vampires were hot with the release of *Zendikar*, but Caleb Durward is seeing them through a period of resurgence now that *Scars of Mirrodin* is here. He made the Top 4 of the recent StarCityGames.com Standard Open–[Boston](http://www.starcitygames.com/magic/standard/20450_Live_Coverage_of_StarCityGamescom_Open_Series_Boston.html) using this take on the archetype. The key element of his deck? Bloodthrone Vampire. The 1/1 combines with many of the cards in his deck to go big and fast, leaving him with a dead opponent!
