# What's the best card in standard right now?

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppra15/gppra15_qq_floch.jpg)  
*Ivan Floch* 

**Ivan Floch:** [Hangarback Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hangarback+Walker). I haven't really played Standard since the Pro Tour, but from what I've seen the card is played everywhere. If not in the main deck it's in people's sideboards. 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppra15/gppra15_qq_anteri.jpg)  
*Fabrizio Anteri* 

**Fabrizio Anteri:** I have to say [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino). It's the best card in the best archetype, which I think is Hangarback Abzan. 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppra15/gppra15_qq_severin.jpg)  
*Thoralf Severin* 

**Thoralf Severin:** [Hangarback Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hangarback+Walker). Because it's both resilient and flexible. 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppra15/gppra15_qq_mengucci.jpg)  
*Andrea Mengucci* 

**Andrea Mengucci:** The best new card is [Hangarback Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hangarback+Walker), but the best card remains to be [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino). It's a 2-for-1 no matter what and some decks can't deal with it. 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppra15/gppra15_qq_bonde.jpg)  
*Michael Bonde* 

**Michael Bonde:** [Tragic Arrogance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tragic+Arrogance). There are some really good strategies that are either good against green devotion. And for the once that are bad against it [Tragic Arrogance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tragic+Arrogance) solves all of your problems.
