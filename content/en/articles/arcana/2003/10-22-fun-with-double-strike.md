![Fireshrieker](https://i.ormos.uk/ce/b3/ceb35b85f5991a6068f6a8acdc61c3dd0b12ffe3eda758b67d4001397034bf57.jpg)

As [Randy Buehler wrote](https://web.archive.org/web/20031022000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/rb54) in January of 2003, the double strike ability is a player-created mechanic that debuted in *Legions*. A creature with double strike deals first-strike combat damage as well as regular combat damage, which could create some interesting combos with cards that have effects triggered by combat damage. *Mirrodin* brings us the equipment card [Fireshrieker](https://web.archive.org/web/20031022000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fireshrieker), which bestows double strike on a creature. What are some cards that would benefit from triggering on combat damage twice? Turns out there are quite a few. Can you think of more?
