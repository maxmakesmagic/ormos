As we [announced here](/en/articles/arcana/2012/02-29-announcing-from-the-vault-realms) (and reminded people at San Diego Comic-Con), *From the Vault: Realms* will be released on August 31, 2012 with an MSRP of $34.99. It will contain fifteen powerful lands from the history of **Magic**. And here's a look at the box!

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/47/33/473393033e7cefd6bb220e3f4d2bcf96b0e7514960678bd7d4d1604e7fdfd8f8.jpg)
