A format for fun, 100 Card Singleton has a simple rule: each deck has to play 100 cards, and you may only play one copy of each card that's not a basic land. ChrisKool took that concept to a whole new level with a control deck that featured a whopping two creatures. Still, the creatures that were played were significant threats in the form of Simic Sky Swallower and Empyrial Archangel. When your attackers are that big, you don't need a lot of them to win!
