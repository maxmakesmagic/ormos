Day 2 of Grand Prix Shanghai has begun. As mentioned previously, only 118 competitors remain and here is what they have brought to the tables.

# Top Archetypes: The 'Abzan' Decks

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Siege+Rhino)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Abzan+Charm)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Charm)

*   A total of 42 players were playing with [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino)s and [Abzan Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Charm)s today. That represents about 35% of the field.
*   Abzan Aggro's creature base was characterized by [Warden of the First Tree](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warden+of+the+First+Tree), [Rakshasa Deathdealer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Deathdealer), and [Anafenza, the Foremost](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anafenza%2C+the+Foremost).
*   Abzan Control players opted for [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix) and [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion) instead.
*   The Megamorph players chose to play with [Deathmist Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathmist+Raptor) and [Den Protector](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Den+Protector).

# The Next Four: The Sea of Red

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Stormbreath+Dragon)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Thunderbreak+Regent)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thunderbreak+Regent)

<table><thead><tr><th>Deck</th>&#10;			<th>Percentage</th>&#10;		</tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>&#10;			<p>Red Green Dragons</p>&#10;			</td>&#10;			<td>&#10;			<p>12 (10.1%)</p>&#10;			</td>&#10;		</tr><tr><td>&#10;			<p>Mardu Dragons</p>&#10;			</td>&#10;			<td>&#10;			<p>11 (9.3%)</p>&#10;			</td>&#10;		</tr><tr><td>&#10;			<p>Atarka Red</p>&#10;			</td>&#10;			<td>&#10;			<p>10 (8.5%)</p>&#10;			</td>&#10;		</tr><tr><td>&#10;			<p>Green Red Devotion</p>&#10;			</td>&#10;			<td>&#10;			<p>9 (7.6%)</p>&#10;			</td>&#10;		</tr></tbody></table>

*   Many players chose to soar through the skies with dragons this weekend. Approximately 20% of players opted to play with [Stormbreath Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stormbreath+Dragon) and [Thunderbreak Regent](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thunderbreak+Regent).
*   The Red Green Dragons players used fast mana to accelerate their win, while the Mardu Dragons players opted for higher threat density and solid removal.
*   The presence of Atarka was also felt in Day 2. 10 players chose to defeat their opponents with the help of little red men and [Atarka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Atarka%27s+Command), 9 others chose to summon [Dragonlord Atarka](https://web.archive.org/web/20150516000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Atarka) from their Green-based Devotion decks instead.
