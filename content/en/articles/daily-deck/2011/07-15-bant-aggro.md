The Classic format features all of the cards ever printed on **Magic Online** and is a blast to play. It uses a banned & restricted list similar to that of the paper Vintage format, and for players who love playing with older cards it's the perfect format. Check out Timmins's list from a recent Premier Event, which features the best in green, white, and blue to keep the opponent off balance! Timmins went undefeated with the deck list.
