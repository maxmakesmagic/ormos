![Mistform Ultimus](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Mistform+Ultimus)

Well it’s been over a year since our last [Mistform Ultimus](https://web.archive.org/web/20131231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistform+Ultimus) check-in, so I think it’s about time we see how *Return to Ravnica*, ***Magic** 2014*, and *Theros* have been treating it. 

For those of you who might not know what I’m talking about, here’s the deal. Before Changeling was an ability in *Lorwyn* block, [Mistform Ultimus](https://web.archive.org/web/20131231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistform+Ultimus) was the only odd creature to have every creature type. Over the years, we’ve been keeping tabs on [Mistform Ultimus](https://web.archive.org/web/20131231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistform+Ultimus) to see how its type line has changed.

The [last time we checked in](https://web.archive.org/web/20131231000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/mistform-ultimus-watch-magic-2013-edition-2012-08-15) on it was in August, 2013, just after ***Magic** 2013*, when it had 224 types.

The *Return to Ravnica* block didn’t create any new creature types (despite the Simic’s best efforts), same for ***Magic** 2014*, so it is actually here in *Theros* that we get our new additions. 

Obviously, becoming a God is sort of a big deal. But there are two other new types from *Theros*: Sable and Nymph.
