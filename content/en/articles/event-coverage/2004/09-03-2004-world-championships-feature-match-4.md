"We're on the board baby! On the board! Don't call it a comeback when we are on the board." 

Craig Krempels was referring to the fact that Team USA was sitting only eight points behind the first-place Ukrainian team on the team leaderboard. Standing in his way was Cornelissen of Von Dutch -- not the country, but the powerhouse team that he, Jeroen Remie, and Jelger Wiegersma formed to conquer the field at Pro Tour-Seattle.

"11-1, wow that's ridiculous," Krempels remarked on Cornelissen's record. "You must be sitting on top of the world." 

Krempels worked with Team TOGIT for this event. He also talked some with Team USA member Ben Zoz making sure that the team would all have good tech for the *Mirrodin* Block portion of Worlds. 

### Game 1

"So lucky! I'll draw," said Krempels after losing the dice roll. Cornelissen just gave a little smile. 

Cornelissen led with a [Darksteel Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Citadel) and an Aether Vial, showing that he was playing affinity. Krempels dropped out a Vault of the Whispers and [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker). Taking a quick look around the Feature Match area showed that these two were the only affinity decks of the eight players under the spotlights.

[Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager) and [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite) joined Cornelissen's team, while Krempels cast a two-mana [Thoughtcast](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtcast). Cornelissen used the Vial to pop out an [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker). On his own turn, he brought down [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating) and placed it on the [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite). Then he swung with everything. Krempels had too weak of a board position and scooped on his third turn.

Kamiel Cornelissen 1, Craig Krempels 0

### Game 2

Krempels started the second game with a turn-one Aether Vial. This time Cornelissen was the one with the [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker). On his second turn, Krempels brought down [Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault) but had no other play. Cornelissen had an [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager) and a [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite) to get his offense going once again. Krempels used the Vial to bring into play an [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker), but his third turn saw no play as he passed with all three of his lands untapped.

Cornelissen dropped down a [Chromatic Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chromatic+Sphere), a [Relic Barrier](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Relic+Barrier) and a [Myr Enforcer](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Enforcer) before sending in all three of his creatures into the red zone. Krempels traded off the [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker)s.

Krempels seemed discouraged, but his draw of [Thoughtcast](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtcast) helped his spirits slightly. That was followed by a [Blinkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinkmoth+Nexus) before he ended his turn. Cornelissen emptied his hand, playing out a second [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager) and a second [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite). Cornelissen sent his whole team again and Krempels had no blocks.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c1/c2/c1c2310fd4eeac0fd59e7f3ec87b8e617dbe71f96006f92189d0769eb6d58d11.jpg)

Cornelissen had an empty hand but drew [Thoughtcast](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtcast) as his only card. Krempels started to complain about how good Cornelissen's draw had been. 

"Sure you draw that [Thoughtcast](https://web.archive.org/web/20040903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtcast) . . . why wouldn't you, both games your draw has been perfect," Krempels lamented.

"Yeah my draws were just insane," answered Cornelissen.

Krempels couldn't deal with the horde of creatures attacking him and conceded. With the victory, Cornelissen stayed atop of the standings with an impressive 12-1 record.

Final Score  
 Kamiel Cornelissen 2, Craig Krempels 0
