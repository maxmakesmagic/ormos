Both players finished up Day 1 of Grand Prix Beijing with perfect records. Sung-Wook Nam is ably piloting Mono-Black Devotion, a deck he took to victory recently at Grand Prix Melbourne. Tung-Yi Cheng has piloted a R/W Burn deck over the weekend, the blistering pace leaving his opponents scorched and helpless. 

As the match unfolded, Cheng was able to take Nam's life down to a perilously low levels, but was unable to steal any games before the control deck stabilised and rode its powerful creatures to victory. This matchup is a race, pure and simple, between a flurry of early burn and the Black deck's attempts to stabilise their life total and use their superior creatures to take the game.

**Game 1** 

The first game was all about a flurry of early burn from Cheng, as Nam's [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) displayed a panapoly of [Warleader's Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warleader%27s+Helix), [Shock](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shock), [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike) and an [Ash Zealot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ash+Zealot). Once the Zealot hit the bin, Cheng wasted no time in reducing Nam's life total as rapidly as possible. Nam managed to resolve a Nightveil Spectre and followed up with a [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress). Cheng had enough burn to drop Nam to 5 in response, but had nothing for Nam's follow-up [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon). Cheng was forced to look to the top of his deck for answers. None arrived in time, the powerful 6/6 Demon taking the first game in short order. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/r10_cheng.jpg)

*Cheng feels a little Desecrated.* 

  

**Sung-Wook Nam 1 - Tung-Yi Cheng 0** 

**Game 2** 

Nam dominated the early turns of Game 2 with a volley of triple [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress), taking a pair of Cheng's Chained To The Rocks and some burn. Cheng summoned an [Ash Zealot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ash+Zealot) and threw what he had at Nam, who eventually offed the Zealot. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/r10_nam.jpg)

*Nam carefully takes control of the game.* 

  

Nam summoned a [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon), and when Cheng threw a [Mizzium Mortars](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mizzium+Mortars) and a [Searing Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blood) at it, Nam used a [Devour Flesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devour+Flesh) in response to gain a bunch of life and put Cheng even further behind. Nam dropped some more men and mopped up the game, having never been seriously threatened after the opening wave of discard. 

**Sung-Wook Nam 2 - Tung-Yi Cheng 0**
