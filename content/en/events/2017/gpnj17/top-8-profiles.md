At the end of Round 15, only eight players remained. A few new faces joined a Top 8 full of Pro Tour regulars and a pair of Hall-of-Famers. Meet your Grand Prix New Jersey Top 8!

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpnj17/t8_rietzl.jpg)Name:** Paul Rietzl

**Age:** It’s impolite to ask.

**Occupation:** Executive Recruiter

**Hometown:** Scottsdale, AZ

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** 

I won a JSS Challenge at TOGIT in New Jersey in 2001. Outside NJ, Hall of Fame and some Top 8s.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Mardu, because it has all the best cards and a one-mana 3/2.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Unlicensed Disintegration](https://web.archive.org/web/20170312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unlicensed+Disintegration) because it’s the only thing that beats Gideon. Also, Gideon.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

Sorin, because it’s super fun.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you? And what would it mean if you won?**

All I want to do is qualify for Worlds in Boston, where I grew up.

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpnj17/t8_stark.jpg)Name:** Ben Stark

**Age:** 33

**Occupation:** none

**Hometown:** Coral Springs, FL

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

20 GP Top 8s, 4 PT Top 8s

PT Paris Champion

GP Indy Champion

MtG Hall of Fame

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Jund Energy, Scrounger and [Unlicensed Disintegration](https://web.archive.org/web/20170312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unlicensed+Disintegration) are more consistent than in Mardu

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20170312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push) - it kills 4-drops for 1 mana

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Natural Obsolescence](https://web.archive.org/web/20170312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Natural+Obsolescence) - it’s great against Tower/Gearhulk and against Scrounger/[Heart of Kiran](https://web.archive.org/web/20170312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heart+of+Kiran)

**What does this Top 8 mean to you? And what would it mean if you won?**

Top 8 means 4 pro points, and a win means 8!

Helps show other people that playing all formats strengthens your skill sets. Winning would put me much closer to Gold.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpnj17/t8_moran.jpg)Name:** Kyle Moran

**Age:** 18

**Occupation:** high school student

**Hometown:** Larchmont

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Going 5-4 at the last GP . . .

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

4-Color Saheeli because I needed a deck for Standard and my friend had it on hand.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Felidar Guardian](https://web.archive.org/web/20170312000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Felidar+Guardian) because it is literally everything from a removal spell, card selection, and an infinite combo all rolled up into a 1/4 body.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

**What does this Top 8 mean to you? And what would it mean if you won?**

Given that I have never Day 2-ed a GP, I’m half expecting I will wake up from my dream any time now.
