Ask Wizards is a weekly feature that allows you to ask us questions! If you'd like to submit your question please email it to AskWizards@wizards.com. We aren't able to answer every question we receive but if your question is good then it might show up in the coming weeks!

 **Q: I have a question about *Theros*! —Everybody** 

**A: Mike McArtor, copyeditor for DailyMTG:**

Fortunately for you, **Magic** audience, we have the "[*Theros* Release Notes](https://web.archive.org/web/20140218200827/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/faq/ths)" to answer all your questions about *Theros*, its mechanics, and how its cards interact with the game in general.
