Monday was [Plains](https://web.archive.org/web/20090813000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/zendikar-plains-2009-08-10) and Tuesday was [Islands](https://web.archive.org/web/20090813000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/zendikar-islands-2009-08-11), and Wednesday had [Swamps](https://web.archive.org/web/20090813000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/zendikar-swamps-2009-08-12). Today is Mountains.
