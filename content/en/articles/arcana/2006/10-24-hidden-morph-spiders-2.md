The morph mechanic came back in a big way in *Time Spiral*. Many morph creatures have a visual cue in their art that represents the mysteriously uniform “morph shell” from which the morph creature emerges. It’s that spider-shaped creature shell represented in *Onslaught’s* expansion symbol (![](https://i.ormos.uk/a8/4a/a84a50bb06c8db6280a931bb651b3c3820d648c7050b234b118ecae8c171256d.gif)).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f7/95/f79537a3837c368bf806418fd1f56f5986373e6a7477b04defa1ba043ac49a8f.jpg)  
*Break Open art by Alex Horley-Orlandelli – Fatal Mutation art by Heather Hudson*

You can find lots of these “morph spiders” throughout the art of *Time Spiral* morph cards:

![Fortune_Thief_640](https://i.ormos.uk/60/f9/60f9f8db191be01c725c73e72b91f1d67bd2eefb9228fdfe2e818090b98943a4.jpg)  
*Check out the discarded “morph spider” on the left – Fortune Thief art by Christopher Moeller*

  

![Fathom_Seer_640](https://i.ormos.uk/5c/38/5c385351c43faa612d3793a1a7f4f7262d1d0844d28e787b2fc75e4c04cb4bd3.jpg)  
*See it way down below, on the sea floor? – Fathom Seer art by Ralph Horsley*

  

![Weathered_Bodyguards_640](https://i.ormos.uk/da/b0/dab00b2fb1dcf915f17fca56815725c57a471fd55979e2ae4cf3db80abdc8d88.jpg)  
*The morph spider looms behind these cool plains survivors – Weathered Bodyguards art by Wayne Reynolds*
