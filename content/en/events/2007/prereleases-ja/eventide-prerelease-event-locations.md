**Dates/Locations**  
 

*   Refer to the [**Locations**](#eventlist) listing below.

**Entry Fee & Product Mix**  
 

*   All *Eventide* Prerelease tournaments in Japan have an entry fee of ¥3,000.
*   Each player will receive 1 tournament pack of *Shadowmoor* and 3 booster packs of the latest **Magic: The Gathering** expansion, *Eventide*.
*   In addition, some locations may offer 2-person Two-Headed Giant or 3-person team format tournaments. In a Two-Headed Giant format tournament, each team will receive 1 *Shadowmoor* tournament pack and 5 *Eventide* booster packs to build two decks. For 3-person team tournaments, each team will receive 2 *Shadowmoor* tournament packs and 6 *Eventide* booster packs to build three decks.
*   English language packs will be provided to players who cannot read Japanese (while supplies last).

**Tournament Structure**  
 

*   All *Eventide* Prerelease tournaments will be run using standard DCI Limited Sealed Deck rules.
*   The format for all side events run at *Eventide* prerelease tournaments will be single-elimination DCI Limited Booster Draft.

**Tournament Registration**  
 

*   All *Eventide* prerelease tournaments are open. No pre-registration is required. Registration time varies from location to location; see below for details.
*   Some events will be run using the flight system. Registration will be open continuously until capacity at each site is filled; a new prerelease tournament will begin once the specified number of players have signed up.
*   Depending on demand and available space, participants may be asked to wait until their scheduled tournament begins.

**Prizes**  
 

*   Top finishing players will be awarded *Eventide* booster packs as prizes.
*   Each participant in a *Eventide* prerelease tournament will receive a commemorative prerelease card (while supplies last). Even if a player participates in more than one prerelease tournament in a day, he or she will receive only 1 prerelease card.
*   Other items for winners of the main tournament and side tournaments may be awarded.

**Locations**  
 For specific information about each prerelease tournament, please contact your nearest organizer as listed below.
