Ask Wizards is a weekly feature that allows you to ask us questions! If you'd like to submit your question please email it to AskWizards@wizards.com. We aren't able to answer every question we receive but if your question is good then it might show up in the coming weeks!

What are they? —Nicholas C. </b></td>&#10;			<td></td>&#10;			<td><img alt="" src="http://archive.wizards.com/magic/images/magicboxicon_nocolor.jpg"></td>&#10;		</tr><tr><td colspan="3">&#10;			<table><tbody><tr><td>&#10;						<p><b>A: Mike McArtor, copy editor for DailyMTG: </b></p>&#10;&#10;						<p>Heliod wields Khrusor, the Sun Spear. Thassa carries Dekella, the one-time prize at the center of a maze (see <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20140204000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/ur/273">Part 1</a> and <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20140204000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/ur/274">Part 2</a> of an Uncharted Realms story about it). Erebos's powerful whip is named Mastix. The hammer that Purphoros wields is named Akmon. Nylea carries the bow, Ephixis. You can learn more about the weapons borne by the gods (and the gods themselves) in the &quot;<a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20140204000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/feature/261c">Planeswalker's Guide to <i>Theros</i>, Part 1</a>.&quot;</p>&#10;&#10;						<p>Of course, <i>Born of the Gods</i> releases this week, and you can learn more about Ephara, Karametra, Mogis, Phenax, and Xenagos with the &quot;<a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20140204000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/ur/284">The Gods of <i>Born of the Gods</i></a>&quot; and the set's trailer, as well.</p>&#10;&#10;						<p>The <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20140204000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/magazine/archive.aspx?tag=uncharted%20realms&amp;description=uncharted%20realms">Uncharted Realms column</a> is going to continue to tell more stories set in Theros (the plane). And Part 1 of Jenna Helland's Theros eBook, <i>Godsend</i>, <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20140204000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/1411">releases April 22</a>.</p>&#10;						</td>&#10;					</tr></tbody></table></td>&#10;		</tr></tbody></table>
