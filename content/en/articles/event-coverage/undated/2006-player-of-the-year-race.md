*Updated December 13, 2006* 

Congratulations to [Japan's Shouta Yasooka](/en/events/2006/worlds06/feature-shouta-yasooka-player-of-the-year), the 2006 **Magic: The Gathering** Player of the Year! Thanks to a win at Pro Tour-Charleston and no fewer than five Grand Prix Top 8s, Yasooka took home the trophy.

The player with the most pro points over the course of a season is identified as the Pro Player of the Year. In addition to the title, the Pro Player of the Year receives invitations to certain tournaments throughout the next season. That player also receives travel and accommodations to each of the next season’s Pro Tour events and to the World Championships. 

This page shows all players with 10 or more Pro Points in the 2006 season. For a complete list of 2006 Pro Points and current Pro Players Club levels, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20130222011526/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/events/protour?x=protour/standings/proplayersclub06).
