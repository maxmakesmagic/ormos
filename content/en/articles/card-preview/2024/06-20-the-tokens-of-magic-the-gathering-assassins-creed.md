On July 5, [*Magic: The Gathering® – Assassin's Creed®*](https://web.archive.org/web/20240620000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/assassins-creed) freeruns its way onto shelves at your local game store with new cards and alternate-art reprints featuring the characters, settings, and lore from the popular *Assassin's Creed* video games. The set also features 8 tokens in all, including 3 minigame cards, that offer thrilling and iconic *Magic* play!

In Beyond Boosters, you can find 8 full-art tokens and 3 minigame cards. Play three minigames, including Into the Story: Assassin Edition, Beyond Booster Blitz, and Find the Assassin, with the 3 minigame cards from Beyond Boosters shown below.
