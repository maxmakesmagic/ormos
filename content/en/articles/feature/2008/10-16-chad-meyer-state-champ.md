The [State Championships](/en/articles/feature/2008/10-10-states-and-champs-information-update-2) are again on the horizon and in honor of those events and an exciting new product announcement we're looking back at a former champion. When *Lorwyn* was first released, planeswalkers had never been seen before and much of the deckbuilding community wasn't quite sure what to think about them. Chad Meyer wasted no time jumping right into utilizing [Chandra Nalaar](https://web.archive.org/web/20081016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra+Nalaar) to take the top slot at the 2007 Nebraska State Championships. His mono-red control deck, affectionately called "Skred Red," kept his opponent's table clear of creatures with burn spells long enough to allow him to Chandra or [Stuffy Doll](https://web.archive.org/web/20081016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stuffy+Doll)/[Skred](https://web.archive.org/web/20081016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skred) his opponent for the win.
