*Dragons of Tarkir* is almost here! Prereleases start this weekend, and the set releases in stores everywhere March 27. Believe me, we're counting the days. And the Dragons. And all of the other things. Want to reap the benefits of our counting compulsion? Check out some of these early numbers on *Dragons of Tarkir*.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/ACR20150317_Dragons_PieChart.png)

**Biggest Dragon:** Dragonlord Atarka

**Smallest Dragon:** Herdchaser Dragon (and all of the other megamorphing uncommon Dragons)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/2_Dragons.png)
