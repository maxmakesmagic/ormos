Congratulations to Chinese Taipei, your first World **Magic** Cup champions. Yu Min Yang, Tzu-Ching Kuo, and Tung-Yi Cheng played across three Constructed formats to defeat Puerto Rico in the finals. You can see [complete coverage of the World **Magic** Cup here](/en/events/2012/wmc12/chinese-taipei-claims-first-world-magic-cup).
