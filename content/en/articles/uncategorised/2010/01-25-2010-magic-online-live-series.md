**2010 Magic Online Live Series** 

![](http://www.wizards.com/global/images/mtg_daily_other_012510_pic1_en.jpg)

The **2010 Magic Online** **Live Series** provides new and long-time **Magic Online** players the chance to compete for cash prizes and an opportunity to meet the **Magic Online** developers and managers face to face.

Each live event will offer a Single Elimination Challenge tournament with a $5000 cash prize pool. In addition, all new players that create a **Magic Online** account ([eligibility requirements](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=1358) apply) or existing players that complete an on-site survey will receive a foil Crystalline Sliver promo card and the opportunity to play in a free **Magic Online** draft (while supplies last).

**Magic Online Live Series Single Elimination Challenges** 

LIVE EVENTS: 

*   [Pro Tour–San Diego](/en/events/2010/sandiego10/pro-toursan-diego-event-information) (Feb 19 – 21)
*   [PAX East](http://www.paxsite.com/paxeast/index.php) (Mar 26 – 28)
*   [Pro Tour–San Juan](/en/events/2010/sanjuan10/pro-toursan-juan-event-information) (May 28 – 30)
*   [Gen Con](http://www.gencon.com/2010/indy/default.aspx) (Aug 5 – 8)
*   [Pro Tour–Amsterdam](https://web.archive.org/web/20100125000000/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=mtgcom/protour/amstedam10) (Sept 2 – 5)
*   [PAX](http://www.paxsite.com/paxprime/index.php) (Sept 3 – 5)
*   NY Comic Con (Oct 8 – 10) **Unfortunately, this event has been cancelled. We apologize for any inconvenience.**
