The month is nearly over, so once again it's time to take a trip in our Friday Night *Magic* time machine and see what promos we have in store (and in stores!) for the coming months.

**November**

Is it a coincidence that our hottest promos come during some of the coldest months in North America? Stay warm with this Zoltan Boros take on [Roast](https://web.archive.org/web/20151022000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roast).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/c4rd_09b1vGHdZs.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/c4rd4r7_pAtmwPHvaG.jpg)

[Roast](https://web.archive.org/web/20151022000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roast) Promo | Art by [Zoltan Boros](https://web.archive.org/web/20151022000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5B%22Zoltan+Boros%22%5D)

**December**

For many, December is a highly anticipated month full of holidays, presents, the end of one year, and the start of a brand-new one. To celebrate, we're giving you a new tool to see what's ahead with an updated version of [Anticipate](https://web.archive.org/web/20151022000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anticipate) from Volkan Baga. What? You didn't see that coming?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/uzwNQRWP2V.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_OTRZfmm0Lz.jpg)

[Anticipate](https://web.archive.org/web/20151022000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anticipate) Promo | Art by [Volkan Baga](https://web.archive.org/web/20151022000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5B%22Volkan+Baga%22%5D)

**January**

It's a new year, which means it's time for a journey to find yourself...and maybe a few Forests. Nissa's certainly on just such an introspective journey, and you can join her with your copy of [Nissa's Pilgrimage](https://web.archive.org/web/20151022000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%27s+Pilgrimage) featuring art by Christine Choi.
