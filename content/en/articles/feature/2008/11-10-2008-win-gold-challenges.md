[![](https://i.ormos.uk/5e/26/5e26c943e120533bc98964e4afd77ea0c1c7cc76be9c8ea9f059d97266d710e5.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20081110000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=events/magic/worlds)The **WIN GOLD** Challenges tournaments are a series of qualifiers run throughout North America. The top two finishers at each qualifier earn invitations to the **WIN GOLD** Championship tournament, to be held at the 2008 **Magic: the Gathering** World Championship in Memphis, Tennessee on December 14, 2008. Additional qualifiers will be held at the World Championship for the **WIN GOLD** Championship tournament, where $10,000 in 14K GOLD will be awarded to the winner.

*   [Locations of **WIN GOLD** Challenge tournaments](#locations)
*   [Win GOLD Championship Tournament Information](https://web.archive.org/web/20081110000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=events/magic/worlds08-wingold)
*   [Complete schedule of Public Events at the 2008 **Magic: The Gathering** World Championships](https://web.archive.org/web/20081110000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=events/magic/worlds08-publicevents)

*All Universal Tournament Rules and **Magic: The Gathering** Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document*

**Entry Fee**  

*   Contact your local organizer for entry fee.

**Format**  

*   Format determined by organizer (Standard, Extended, Legacy, or Sealed Deck)
*   Deck lists are required
*   Tournaments with less than 8 players will be cancelled.

**Tournament Length**  

*   Swiss rounds - 50 minutes per round
*   Number of Swiss rounds based on attendance according to the following chart:

**Advancement**  

*   All players may participate in all Swiss rounds.
*   The top 8 players after the final Swiss round will advance to the top 8 single-elimination finals.

**Top 8**  

*   Top 8 matches will be best 2 of 3
*   Top 8 matches will have no time limit.
*   Where necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will still be used to determine final order in the standings.

**Prizes**  

*   The top 2 players will win an invitation to the **WIN GOLD** tournament held at the 2008 World Championship on Sunday, December 14, 2008 at 10:00 AM.
*   Product Prizes as determined by the organizer.

**Notes**  

*   16K, REL Regular
*   All Universal Tournament Rules and **Magic: The Gathering** Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document
*   Players must bring pen, paper, card sleeves and appropriate counters.
