Renowned deck builder Adrian Sullivan recently got 2nd at a Pro Tour Qualifier in his native Wisconsin. The deck he was piloting was a standard Sullivan issue Rogue Red, featuring cards as yet unseen in the format like Dwarven Blastminer and Blistering Firecat. But will *Conflux* change everything? To find out how to get a chance to play with the brand new set this weekend, the first it becomes available for sale, visit [this page](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-launch-parties-4).
