Do you have knowledge, wisdom, or experience that you'd like to share with the judge community at large? Do you want your words immortalized on the judge site? Here are a few DO's and DON'T's to observe when writing your article, before you submit it to an editor: 

### DO's

**DO** send the file as a .txt or .doc file. If you don't have access to Word, please let the editor know when you submit your article. 

**DO** send a picture (or two!) to go with your article. If you don't have a picture to include, think of a card image that might be relevant to your subject matter. 

**DO** look through the article archives or talk to the editors about your topic before writing, so that you don't duplicate anyone else's effort. 

**DO** try to submit articles that have general appeal or interest. Most of the judges in the world are Level 1. 

**DO** try to keep your article in the 2-5 page range. 

**DO** have patience. Sometimes the editors get busy. 

**DO** be prepared to work with an editor to prepare your article for posting. 

### DON'T's

**DON'T** submit an event report that just lists the things that happened at your event without teaching or sharing anything else with your readers. If you learned something, and a certain event was a vehicle for that learning process, please write about what you learned and how the event helped. Not about the event with a paragraph about what you learned from it at the end. 

**DON'T** mention future articles that you'll be following up with. 

**DON'T** be afraid of submitting an article. The editors are nice people and will take good care of you. 

**DON'T** worry about your English skills, or that what you have to say isn't important. 

**DON'T** feel the need to include HTML formatting in your article. This can be handled later. 

**DON'T** worry about your level. Judges of every level have knowledge to share. 

Once you've got a finished product that you're happy with, please submit it to an editor. We appreciate your contributions to the judge community and thank you for taking the time to share with everyone.
