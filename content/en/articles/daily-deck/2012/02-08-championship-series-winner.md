Each month in 2012 features a [**Magic Online** Championship Series event](/en/articles/feature/2011/12-20-2012-magic-online-championship-series) (MOCS for short), with the winners of each qualifying for a tournament with $100,000 at stake. The most recent MOCS event happened last weekend, with more than 500 players fighting for first.

The format? Modern, the same exciting new format simultaneously being used in PTQs worldwide. The end result? Botakov's Project Melira deck sitting on top of them all!

The *Shadowmoor* block persist creatures look innocent enough—until Melira, Sylvok Outcast comes out to play. Melira's unique ability prevents persist creatures from receiving –1/–1 counters when they return. Stir in a sacrifice outlet like Viscera Seer and you can sacrifice the same persist creature over and over and over again, reusing Kitchen Finks or Murderous Redcap's enters the battlefield effects in the process! The end result: one *very* dead opponent.

With this deck making waves, players have been looking for answers to the combo by using everything from Torpor Orb to Tormod's Crypt. The problem with that method is, well... this deck doesn't always have to combo you out!

If your opponent has ways to fight the Melira combo plan, the deck is still full of creatures and disruption. Between Birthing Pod and Chord of Calling, the deck can assemble whichever creatures are best for the situation. To fight this deck, you have to fight through disruption, shut down the combo, and then *still* weather the residual assault of creatures. Few decks are equipped for the challenge. Is yours?

With Modern heating up and decks like this rising toward the top, the format is still wide open. Do you have what it takes to succeed? Try your hand at a [Modern PTQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20120208000000/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=mtgcom/protour/barcelona12-qualifiers) and find out for yourself!
