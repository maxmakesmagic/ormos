Ah Elves, favored deck of children and Pro Tour winners alike (Dirk Baberowski loves an Elf deck nearly as much as he loves German soccer player Mehmet Scholl, which is to say, a whole lot), the archetype of small green creatures returned with a vengeance here this weekend. In fact, Elves turned out to be the second most popular archetype here this weekend, so I figured it was time to settle in and take a look at a couple of builds that are performing well so far in the tournament. 

### Take 1

The first comes from Charlie "The Kid" Gindy. Gindy, mostly known as a Team Limited specialist, took an extended hiatus from **Magic** to chase girls for a few years, but won a PTQ for this format and did a bunch of testing on **Magic Online** prior to the event. I caught up with him after he had just defeated Hall of Famer Zvi Mowshowitz to extend his record to 10-3 on the weekend. Since then, he’s cinched a place in the Top 8.



![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/pthol08/Gindy.jpg)**What is the plan?** "You just want to cast Elves, use good removal to get blockers out of the way, and smash face with either [Garruk Wildspeaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20080525000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Wildspeaker) or [Profane Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20080525000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Profane+Command). The deck is very Rock-like."

**What are the key cards?** [Profane Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20080525000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Profane+Command), [Wren’s Run Vanquisher](https://web.archive.org/web/20080525000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wren%E2%80%99s+Run+Vanquisher), and [Chameleon Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20080525000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chameleon+Colossus). "I was testing some builds I found online and there was one I liked that didn’t have the Colossus in the main. I swapped those in and suddenly the deck was very good and very consistent."

**Does it beat Faeries?** "I’m 2-2 on the weekend thus far. Game 1 is not great, but you do get five very strong cards out of the sideboard ([Cloudthresher](https://web.archive.org/web/20080525000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudthresher)s and Squall Lines). I may need to put one more Squall Line in there, though."

**How does it do against the rest of the field?** "It’s very good against the green decks—[Profane Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20080525000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Profane+Command) is insane there. Goblins and most of the red decks you smash, and it’s even competitive against [Reveillark](https://web.archive.org/web/20080525000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reveillark)—it depends on how fast their draw is.

**Why would you play this and would you make any changes?** "I wanted to play something I knew was solid that would give me a good chance to Top 16 if I played well and I really wanted to avoid playing Faerie mirror matches all day long. I won a couple of Premiere Events on **Magic Online** with it, so I was also very comfortable playing this deck for an extended period of time. The main deck is perfect, but you might need that one more Squall Line in the board."

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/pthol08/Warmenhoven.jpg)**What is the Plan?** "Make 2/2s and attack!"

**What are the key cards?** "[Wren’s Run Vanquisher](https://web.archive.org/web/20080525000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wren%E2%80%99s+Run+Vanquisher) and Imperious Prefect are the most important ones, while [Elvish Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20080525000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Champion) is so good against other green decks."

**Does It Beat Faeries?** "It does. That’s why we played it. 2 Squall Lines main plus [Jagged-Scar Archers](https://web.archive.org/web/20080525000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jagged-Scar+Archers) make it tough for them, especially because we can race."

**How does it do against the rest of the field? "**It can lose to the Mono-Red builds that are more controlling and really doesn’t like Damnation and Sulfurous Blast. Fish and the other aggro decks are usually pretty good matchups, but like most aggro decks, [Reveillark](https://web.archive.org/web/20080525000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reveillark) is very bad."

**Why would you play this and would you make any changes?** "We knew there would be lots of Faerie decks running around, and it’s a lot of fun to play. If I had to do it again though, I’d cut some [Elvish Champ](https://web.archive.org/web/20080525000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Champ)s from the main and play [Riftsweeper](https://web.archive.org/web/20080525000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Riftsweeper)s instead."
