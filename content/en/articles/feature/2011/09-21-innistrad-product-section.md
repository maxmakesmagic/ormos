Do you know what day it is? Wednesday, sure, unless it's still Tuesday or already Thursday, or maybe some other day entirely. But more importantly, it's [*Innistrad* Product Section](https://web.archive.org/web/20130623114034/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/products.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/products/innistrad) update day! Every card in *Innistrad* has been revealed, and you can see them all in the Card Image Gallery! There's also a new update to the World of Innistrad section, rules pages to help you brush up before this weekend's Prerelease events, and more!

The [*Innistrad* Product Section](https://web.archive.org/web/20130623114034/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/products.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/products/innistrad) awaits you!
