**Magic: The Gathering**® **Pro Tour**™**–Los Angeles Qualifier**  
October 27-29, 2000

**The Grand Prix tournament series is an international circuit of large-scale Qualifier tournaments featuring cash prizes and multiple invitations to the Pro TourTM.** 

**WHERE**  
 Ramada Inn - Texas Stadium  
 1055 Regal Road  
 Dallas, TX  
 (214)-634-8550 

**WHEN**  
 October 27-29, 2000  
 Side events begin Friday evening. 

**COST**  
 $20 preregistration  
 $25 on site 

For advance registration, contact **Aussiefox Events at (316)-946-1055**. Advance registration will be available until 5:00 P.M. on Wednesday, October 25, 2000. For hotel reservations, call the Ramada Inn - Texas Stadium at (214)-634-8550. Mention "Magic Players Room Block" for $65/night rate. 

For complete Grand Prix-Dallas information, please visit [http://www.DALLASGRANDPRIX.com](http://www.dallasgrandprix.com/) 

**TOURNAMENT** 

*   **Divisions**
     The main tournament at Grand Prix-Dallas is open to **Magic**® players of all ages.

    *In the event that a player who wins an invitation to a Pro Tour stop at a Grand Prix has already qualified for an invitation, the invitation shall be extended to the next-best finisher in that Grand Prix. Previous qualification may be through a Qualifier tournament, through performance at a professional tournament, **or** by being an invited DCITM player (valid upon the date of the official rank announcement for the appropriate professional tournament).*

*   **Tournament Format**
     The format for Grand Prix-Dallas will be Invasion Block Limited-format, with day 2 Rochester Draft. All DCI Limited-format rules and Magic Floor Rules announced on September 1, 2000, along with all subsequent updates, will be enforced.

*   **Tournament Structure**
     The structure for Grand Prix-Dallas will be modified Swiss-style (60-minute rounds, number of rounds determined by number of participants), with the Top 64 players advancing to the second day of competition for prizes and invitations.

*   **Tournament Byes** *(Byes are not cumulative throughout the season or year)*
     Byes will be given to players in the Grand Prix based on the following criteria:

     ***Third-Round Byes***

    *   Players with 24 or more professional points in the Worlds 2000 professional standing.
    *   DCI players with a Limited rating of 2025 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI ratings published approximately six weeks prior to the Grand Prix.

     ***Second-Round Byes***

    *   Players with 12 or more professional points in the Worlds 2000 professional standing.
    *   DCI players with a Limited rating of 1900 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI ratings published approximately six weeks prior to the Grand Prix.

     ***First-Round Byes***

    *   Players with 6 or more professional points in the Worlds 2000 professional standing.
    *   DCI players with a Limited rating of 1800 or higher. Byes do not pass down under any circumstances. Byes are based on DCI ratings published approximately six weeks prior to the Grand Prix.

**PAYOUT**  
 Grand Prix-Dallas will feature the standard "main tournament" prize schedule as well as a special "1700 division" prize schedule. DCI members who have a lifetime highest Limited rating of no greater than 1700 will be eligible for the 1700 division. The 1700 division prizes will be paid to the highest-finishing players *regardless of their standing in the Grand Prix.*
