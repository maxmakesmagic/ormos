Blue-White Control is one of **Magic**'s most classic archetypes. Brett Ripley offered up his take on the deck at his Champs tournament in Nova Scotia, Canada. New additions to the deck include Knight-Captain of Eos, Tidings, and Mistmeadow Witch. That unassuming 1/1 works wonders when combined with Mulldrifter, Reveillark, the Knight-Captain, and Kitchen Finks. Congrats to Brett, and keep it tuned to Daily Decks all week long for a look at what Champs tournaments are bringing to the Standard format!
