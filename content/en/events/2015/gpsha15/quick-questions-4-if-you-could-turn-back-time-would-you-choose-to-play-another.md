# If you could turn back time, would you choose to play another deck this weekend after seeing the Shanghai metagame for yourself?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_Day2QQTerrySoh.jpg)  
**3 Pro Tour Top 8s & Invitational Winner, Terry Soh:** I think I would still have stuck to Abzan Control. It's a fine choice and I am very familiar with this deck.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_Day2QQYuukiIchikawa.jpg)  
**2 Pro Tour Top 8s & Platinum Pro, Yuuki Ichikawa:** I am playing Abzan Control and I think it is great against the other Abzan decks. Since there are so many here in Shanghai, I think I made the right choice. I think Abzan Megamorph is a possible choice too!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_Day2QQFabienLi.jpg)  
**Grand Prix Kuala Lumpur Champion, Fabien Li:** I will still have played Abzan Aggro after looking at the metagame. It's one of the most powerful decks in the format and doesn't really have any bad matchups.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_Day2QQKenYukuhiro.jpg)  
**Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* Top 4 and Grand Prix Singapore Champion, Ken Yukuhiro:** I think Ojutai Bant is well-positioned now. It is good against Abzan Control and Atarka Red, so maybe no change for me.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_Day2QQParkJunYoung.jpg)  
**Grand Prix Minneapolis 2014 Champion, Park Jun Young:** I'm doing ok with Abzan Aggro, but given the choice I would rather play a Megamorph deck. Aside from [Deathmist Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathmist+Raptor) and [Den Protector](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Den+Protector), there is also Stratus Dancer, [Hidden Dragonslayer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hidden+Dragonslayer), and [Rattleclaw Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20150517000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rattleclaw+Mystic). I think there is advantage to be had by playing with Morphs to confuse the opponent. 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsha15/gpShanghai15_Day2QQTiagoChan.jpg)  
**2 Pro Tour Top 8s & Invitational Winner, Tiago Chan:** I played Mardu Dragons and was paired against five Abzan Control yesterday! It's not a very good match up. If I had known, I would have chosen Devotion or Sultai Whip instead.
