It was a weekend of Legacy action across the live and digital **Magic** tournament scene. First at [Grand Prix–Columbus](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/saitos-legacy-continues), **Magic** superstar Tomoharu Saito took home the trophy with a Merfolk deck, defeating Tom Martell and his CounterTop deck in the finals. Over in the world of **Magic Online**, the Season Seven Championship in MOCS was won by Archer. with Ad Nauseam/Tendrils. If Standard's more your scene, check out the Top 8 lists from Italy Nationals featuring multiple archetypes.
