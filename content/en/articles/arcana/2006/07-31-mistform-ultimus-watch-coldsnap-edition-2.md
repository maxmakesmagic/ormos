Welcome again to Mistform Ultimus Watch! In this recurring sub-feature of Magic Arcana we keep track of everybody's favorite bane of trivia to see what creature types it has gained and/or lost.

Last time we checked in with the Ultimus [after the release of Ravnica](/en/articles/arcana/2006/01-05-mistform-ultimus-watch-ravnica-edition-2), just before *Guildpact* previews were getting ready to kick off. Since then *Guildpact*, *Dissension*, and *Coldsnap* have all joined the mix. On top of all that, *Visions* was released on ***Magic** Online* in April, which also brought some changes. So, since we last checked in, the Ultimus has also become a Construct, a Cyclops, a Nephilim, a Weird, and, at long last, a Juggernaut! (Thanks Gruul War Plow!) 

While picking those types up, the Ultimus lost the following:

*   Butterfly (Giant Caterpillar's token is still *named* Butterfly, but the token's type is Insect now, as of the MTGO release of *Visions*.)
*   Lichenthrope (The *Visions* card Lichenthrope was consolidated to type Fungus.)
*   Lion (Savannah Lions (*9th Edition*), Mtenda Lion (*Mirage*) and Jamuraan Lion (*Visions*) have all been reprinted now with type "Cat".)
*   Mindsucker (Urborg Mindsucker from *Visions* was the only creature of its type, so it was consolidated to Horror.)
*   Mistfolk (Only *Ice Age's* card Mistfolk had this creature type. It's now an Illusion like Thalakos Mistfolk.)
*   Necrosavant (Now a Zombie Giant!)
*   Warthog (The *Visions* and *Sixth Edition* card Warthog is now a Beast to match his buddy Giant Warthog.)
*   Wildebeest (Also now a Beast as of *Visions* joining ***Magic** Online*.)

Mistform Ultimus is a Weird Fungus, an Oyster Paratrooper, a Walking-Dead Waiter, an Expansion-Symbol Designer, a Giant Squirrel Farmer, a Banshee Mime, and a Master Gamer. Speaking of Master Gamer, it's also an Archer Assassin Barbarian Cleric Druid Monk Ranger Paladin Thief Ranger Wizard. (Someone's been adventuring a little too long.)
