If you’ve seen the [*Tenth Edition* Starter Game](/en/articles/arcana/2007/06-14-tenth-edition-starter-game-2), you know it comes bundled with a sneak peek of *Lorwyn*, **Magic**’s all-new large set coming in October. In the starter game you’ll find some beautiful basic lands ported directly from the *Lorwyn* set. Today we show off these lands to you, to give you a glimpse of what’s coming in the world of Lorwyn.
