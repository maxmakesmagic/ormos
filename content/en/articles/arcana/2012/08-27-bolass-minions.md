NNicol Bolas, Planeswalker is, of course, in ***Magic** 2013*.

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f3/1b/f31b5bf00581f4a8cf96486f4e281340fb869b24de3c4e121b881fb6683b09ad.jpg) 

Nicol Bolas, Planeswalker | Art by [D. Alexander Gregory](https://web.archive.org/web/20120827000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22d.%20alexander%20gregory%22])

But it isn't like Nicol Bolas is a lone dragon, standing without friends in a cold and unfeeling core set. Both an Augur and a Disciple stand by, each showing the horns of Nicol Bolas to show where their loyalties lie.

It can be a little hard to see the horns on Disciple of Bolas at card size, but when the art is blown up, it's on the disciple's torso.

But the horns aren't the only symbol of Nicol Bolas being carried around ***Magic** 2013*. The chest armor worn by Augur of Bolas matches the armor on Nicol Bolas, Planeswalker. And Nicol Bolas, the Augur, and the Disciple all have a distinctive strip of fabric draped over their shoulders. And that brings us to Mindclaw Shaman.
