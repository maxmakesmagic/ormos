Have you checked out the Modern format lately? [Qualifiers for Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored*](https://web.archive.org/web/20120119000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/640181) are under way, punching tickets to Barcelona in May. With cards dating back to *Eighth Edition* and the original *Mirrodin* block, that's a card pool of more than 6,500 cards with which to build decks! Check out recent PTQ winners below for ideas on how to attack this format.
