Time for another quiz! Last time it was all about artifacts and some of you did quite well on that one! This time it's all about ***Magic** 2014* Core Set, and I hope you're prepared because tomorrow is [the Prerelease](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-prereleases-3) and you'll need to [know the cards](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&set=+%5b%22Magic+2014%22%5d) in the set!

Q**1.** Which two cards with “Giant” in their name are both printed in ***Magic** 2014*?

*(Difficulty: Easy)*

[50/50 Hint](#) - Click this to eliminate two wrong answers.

*   [Giant Albatross & Giant Crab](#) - WRONG - [Giant Albatross](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Giant+Albatross) was printed in *Homelands* while [Giant Crab](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Giant+Crab) was originally printed in *Tempest*. Though a normal albatross dwarfs a crab, here the [Giant Crab](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Giant+Crab) is more than capable of fending off the [Giant Albatross](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Giant+Albatross).
*   [Giant Growth & Giant Spider](#) - RIGHT! - [Giant Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Giant+Growth) returns to the core set for the first time since ***Magic** 2011*, and [Giant Spider](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Giant+Spider) was also absent in last year’s ***Magic** 2013*, so they are happy to be reunited in **Magic**’s latest core set.
*   [Blind-Spot Giant & Giant Dustwasp](#) - WRONG - Both of these cards were printed in *Modern Masters* but neither appear in ***Magic** 2014*.
*   [Giant Solifuge & Oathsworn Giant](#) - WRONG - Both cards appeared in the original *Ravnica* block, but not in any recent sets.

Q**2.** How many cards are in ***Magic** 2014*?

*(Difficulty: Medium)*

[50/50 Hint](#) - Click this to eliminate two wrong answers.

*   [156 cards](#) - WRONG - There were 156 cards in *Dragon’s Maze*.
*   [229 cards](#) - WRONG - There were 229 cards in *Modern Masters*.
*   [249 cards](#) - RIGHT! - Spot on!
*   [290 cards](#) - WRONG - There were 290 cards in **Magic**'s first set, Alpha.

Q**3\.** [Colossal Whale](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Colossal+Whale) is a rare from ***Magic** 2014*. It is one of only four creatures in **Magic** with the creature-type “Whale.” What is the largest power among the Whales?

*(Difficulty: Medium)*

[50/50 Hint](#) - Click this to eliminate two wrong answers.

*   [4](#) - WRONG - Pay attention! [Colossal Whale](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Colossal+Whale) is a 5/5 so this isn't possibly right.
*   [5](#) - RIGHT! - Both [Colossal Whale](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Colossal+Whale) and [Great Whale](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Great+Whale) are tied with a power of 5.
*   [6](#) - WRONG - There are no whales with a six power, though you could get three [Narwhal](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narwhal), which would be 6 power.
*   [7](#) - WRONG - Sorry, but no. There aren't a lot of [7 power creatures in blue](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&color=+@%28+%5BU%5D%29&power=+=%5B7%5D).

Q**4.** Which Planeswalker in ***Magic** 2014* has the same starting loyalty as his or her counterpart from *Lorwyn*?

*(Difficulty: Medium)*

[Hint](#) - It is neither Chandra nor Garruk.

[Reveal the answer](#)

Of the Planeswalkers in ***Magic** 2014* and *Lorwyn*, the only pair which have the same starting loyalty are:

![Ajani, Caller of the Pride](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Ajani,%20Caller%20of%20the%20Pride&options=)

![Ajani Goldmane](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Ajani%20Goldmane&options=)

[Ajani, Caller of the Pride](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ajani%2C+Caller+of+the+Pride), [Chandra, Pyromaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Pyromaster), [Garruk, Caller of Beasts](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk%2C+Caller+of+Beasts), and [Jace, Memory Adept](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Memory+Adept) have a starting loyalty of 4.

Q**5.** Which of the following is the oldest first-time reprint in ***Magic** 2014*? That is, before ***Magic** 2014*, this card had only been printed one time.

*(Difficulty: Hard)*

[50/50 Hint](#) - Click this to eliminate two wrong answers.

*   [Capashen Knight](#) - RIGHT! - Originally printed in *Urza’s Destiny*, it was first printed in 1999.
*   [Coral Merfolk](#) - WRONG - Although it was printed originally in *Urza’s Saga*, making it the oldest card amongst the answers, it has since been printed a handful of times, most recently in ***Magic** 2012*.
*   [Lightning Talons](#) - WRONG - [Lightning Talons](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Talons) was originally printed in *Shards of Alara*, and there are other cards in this list much older.
*   [Shrivel](#) - WRONG - Originally printed in *Rise of the Eldrazi* it is the youngest card on this question!
