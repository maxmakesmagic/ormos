Love Your Local Game Store is back and bigger than before as [the Summer of Legend continues](/en/articles/feature/2021/05-06-welcome-to-the-summer-of-legend)!

# In-Store Retro Frame Promos

If you've shopped in your local game store recently, you may have received this retro frame promotional version of [Fabled Passage](https://web.archive.org/web/20210623000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fabled+Passage).

![Retro Frame Fabled Passage](https://media.wizards.com/2021/images/daily/en_w37sdjsywd.png)

Beginning July 23, there are even more retro frame promos coming to your local game store.

![Retro Frame Aven Mindcensor](https://media.wizards.com/2021/images/daily/U1QYda7BuS.png)![Retro Frame Dig Through Time](https://media.wizards.com/2021/images/daily/8abGGS1Dso.png)

![Retro Frame Bolas's Citadel](https://media.wizards.com/2021/images/daily/ffdGhrKu0N.png)![Retro Frame Goblin Guide](https://media.wizards.com/2021/images/daily/ryhiPzaiBT.png)![Retro Frame Scavenging Ooze](https://media.wizards.com/2021/images/daily/m3LUMjvffU.png)

These five cards ([Aven Mindcensor](https://web.archive.org/web/20210623000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Mindcensor), [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20210623000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time), [Bolas's Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20210623000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bolas%27s+Citadel), [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20210623000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide), and [Scavenging Ooze](https://web.archive.org/web/20210623000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scavenging+Ooze)) will be available by participating in in-store events, plus they come with an *MTG Arena* code to unlock a Japanese alternate-art Planeswalker Sleeve. Check with your local game store for details on how you can enjoy these upcoming cards!

# *Adventures in the Forgotten Realms* Ampersand Promos

In addition to [*Mystery Booster: Convention Edition* coming to in-store events this August](/en/articles/feature/2021/06-21-mystery-booster-convention-edition-returns-with-in-store-events), WPN Premium stores will also receive an amazing series of promotional versions of *Adventures in the Forgotten Realms* rares and mythic rares featuring the iconic *Dungeons & Dragons* ampersand.

![Ampersand Tiamat](https://media.wizards.com/2021/images/daily/YvnrRxuMwn.png)

These ampersand promos are traditional foil with a glossy D&D ampersand overlaying the entire card. Stores can offer these cards in a variety of ways, so be sure to find how you can receive one once they're available this August.
