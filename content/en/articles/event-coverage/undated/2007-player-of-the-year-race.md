*Updated January 18, 2008* 

Congratulations to [Japan's Tomoharu Saito](/en/events/2007/worlds07/player-of-the-year-tomoharu-saito), the 2007 **Magic: The Gathering** Pro Tour Player of the Year! Saito's consistent play saw him finish in the top 37 of four Pro Tours, along with eight top 20 Grand Prix finishes.

The player with the most pro points over the course of a season is identified as the Pro Player of the Year. In addition to the title, the Pro Player of the Year receives invitations to certain tournaments throughout the next season. That player also receives travel and accommodations to each of the next season’s Pro Tour events and to the World Championships. 

This page shows all players who have received at least 10 Pro Points in the 2007 season. For a complete list of current Pro Players Club levels, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20130222011523/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/events/protour?x=protour/standings/proplayersclub07).

\*indicates member of the [**Magic** Pro Tour Hall of Fame](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/tournaments-events-trading-card-game-magic-the-gathering-2), which guarantees at least Level 5 status as a benefit.
