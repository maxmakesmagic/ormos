# Deck of the Day

1.  New Horizons

I've seen a few variations on this deck, but the coolest deck I've seen so far to come out of *Amonkhet* looks to win in one exciting turn where it cycles through possibly its entire deck. It centers around these cards:

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=New+Perspectives)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=New+Perspectives)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Shadow+of+the+Grave)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shadow+of+the+Grave)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Weirding+Wood)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Weirding+Wood)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Vizier+of+Tumbling+Sands)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vizier+of+Tumbling+Sands)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Shefet+Monitor)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shefet+Monitor)

The idea is that if you can get to seven cards in hand and a [New Perspectives](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=New+Perspectives) in play, you can cycle through your entire deck, or at least much of it. From there, [Vizier of Tumbling Sands](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vizier+of+Tumbling+Sands) and [Shefet Monitor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shefet+Monitor) can provide you with some extra mana, [Weirding Wood](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Weirding+Wood) can make Vizier even more potent, and [Shadow of the Grave](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shadow+of+the+Grave) can let you refill once you've run out of cycling cards.

From there, it's basically your choice how you want to win. Do you want to [Drake Haven](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drake+Haven)? Go for it. How about [Approach of the Second Sun](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Approach+of+the+Second+Sun)? Risky, but it's also a single-card win condition.

The fun part is that the deck actually has some play to it. [Shefet Monitor](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shefet+Monitor) can grab Deserts, which means it can grab a [Sunscorched Desert](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sunscorched+Desert) at instant speed to break up the CopyCat combo. Against aggressive decks, [Renewed Faith](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Renewed+Faith) and [Haze of Pollen](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Haze+of+Pollen) can keep you alive until you win on turn five or six. And the bevy of cyclers means finding [New Perspectives](https://web.archive.org/web/20170420000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=New+Perspectives) isn't that hard.

The deck is a bit of a glass cannon, but man is it fun.

Below you'll find Frank Karsten's decklist from [his article on ChannelFireball yesterday](https://www.channelfireball.com/articles/3-combo-brews-with-amonkhet/), but I would also check out [the list on Reddit by Tyler Hill](https://www.reddit.com/r/spikes/comments/66b62k/standard_how_to_lose_friends_and_irritate_people/).
