The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on March 27, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by [*Amonkhet Inovocations*](#4)!

# What You Might Have Missed This Weekend

1.  [Top 5 Moments of Grand Prix Orlando](/en/events/2017/gporl17/top-five-moments) | Corbin Hosler

Didn't have time this weekend to keep up with [Grand Prix Orlando](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-orlando-2017)? Corbin has you covered. Though the item you're looking for is "Pikula makes a long-awaited return."

1.  [Battle to Bolas—Lore Lesson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R2Ch58yaJHo) | Aether Hub | Seiben

Just before we enter *Amonkhet* (the first *Magic Story* is Wednesday!), Seiben takes us through the end of the *Aether Revolt* story and reminds us all just how dangerous Nicol Bolas is.

1.  [Behind the Scenes of Grand Prix Porto Alegre](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/behind-the-scenes-of-grand-prix-porto-alegre/) | ChannelFireball | Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa

It's not often we get this kind of perspective on coverage—someone who hasn't normally done coverage, doing coverage, and bringing you behind the scenes. PVDDR's perspective is a fun one, as are his insights on the format and the tournament.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Jund Midrange](http://sales.starcitygames.com/deckdatabase/displaydeck.php?DeckID=112468)

When in doubt, Jund 'em out.

James Rector did just that at a StarCityGames.com Invitational Qualifier where he finished 6th with a grindy Jund deck in Standard that was reminiscent of things we've seen in the not-too-distant past. [Goblin Dark-Dwellers](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Dark-Dwellers) does a lot of the heavy lifting in this deck, flashing back [Unlicensed Disintegration](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unlicensed+Disintegration), [To the Slaughter](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=To+the+Slaughter), [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push), or the underrated [Pulse of Murasa](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pulse+of+Murasa).

You haven't lived until you've [Pulse of Murasa](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pulse+of+Murasa)-ed back a [Goblin Dark-Dwellers](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Dark-Dwellers) which then gets to [Pulse of Murasa](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pulse+of+Murasa)-ed again. It's a cascade of card advantage and life that's hard to come back from.
