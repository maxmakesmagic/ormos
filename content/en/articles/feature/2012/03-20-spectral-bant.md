At the Legacy Starcitygames.com Open Series in Sacramento this past weekend, Philip Contreras put several unassuming pieces together to create a deck unlike anything Legacy players were used to. A green-white-blue beatdown deck featuring auras alongside vicious creatures to enchant, Philip carved the way for a brand new deck to go all the way to 1st place. The deck makes good use of efficient green acceleration, blue disruption, and... [Spectral Flight](https://web.archive.org/web/20120320000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectral+Flight)?!?

Yes, that's right, the *Innistrad* blue common and the classic aura [Unstable Mutation](https://web.archive.org/web/20120320000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unstable+Mutation) lead the enchantment charge, providing plenty of extra damage at a cheap cost. [Unstable Mutation](https://web.archive.org/web/20120320000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unstable+Mutation) can easily translate to 6 extra damage, and [Spectral Flight](https://web.archive.org/web/20120320000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectral+Flight) sends creatures to the skies while providing a much-needed boost.

Creature auras are generally not the backbone of decks because of how fragile they are on creatures—but not in this deck. Contreras has eight tricky-to-target beaters in [Troll Ascetic](https://web.archive.org/web/20120320000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Troll+Ascetic) and [Geist of Saint Traft](https://web.archive.org/web/20120320000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geist+of+Saint+Traft). The hexproof ability on these creatures caused opponent after opponent to gnash their teeth as creature enchantments carried Contreras's army right past any number of [Swords to Plowshares](https://web.archive.org/web/20120320000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swords+to+Plowshares) and [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20120320000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt)s.

To support the deck's aggressive core, Contreras has eight one-drop accelerators to help ensure a turn-two threat. If dealing with enchanted hexproof creatures wasn't enough for the opposition to worry about, the heavy disruption from [Daze](https://web.archive.org/web/20120320000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Daze) and [Spell Pierce](https://web.archive.org/web/20120320000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Pierce) stops spell-heavy decks in their tracks. Even if an opponent manages to stabilize, [Psionic Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20120320000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psionic+Blast) gives the deck the reach it needs to finish the game.

It looks like [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20120320000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets) isn't the only blue *Innistrad* common making waves in Legacy! If you've been looking for a new Legacy deck to try out, give this unique deck a whirl.
