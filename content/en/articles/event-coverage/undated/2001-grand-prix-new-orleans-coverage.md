## 2001 Grand Prix New Orleans Coverage

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fd/df/fddfee672c07d16fbb35b69d1c892b2453f3a711d8f9fb6f318517601453edca.gif)

Bill Stead burst onto the Pro Tour scene in a championship match that lasted 20 minutes and maybe 12 turns. He upset Michelle Bush 2-0, winning $2,400 for himself along with an invitation to Pro Tour Barcelona and enough Pro Tour points to play in the Masters Gateway tournament once he gets there.
