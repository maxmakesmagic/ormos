This week's Sketches looks at the *Judgment* rare Genesis from Mark Zug.

### 1\. Art Description

These are the instructions given to the artist:

"This is a green card and should show a jedi in the shape of a centaur made of tree bark. See reference." As [Mark Rosewater has discussed](/en/articles/makingmagic/2002/05-13-through-the-riftstone), the incarnations were called "Jedi" during design.
