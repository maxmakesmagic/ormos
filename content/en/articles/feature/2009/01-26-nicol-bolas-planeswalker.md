![Nicol Bolas, Planeswalker](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Nicol+Bolas%2C+Planeswalker)

Congratulations, you've found Nicol Bolas, Planeswalker. 

As part of a special *Conflux* promotion, find all five hidden ![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/mtg/images/multiverse/planeswalkers/5cylx6tlm2.gif) symbols on the **Magic** web site, each corresponding to a specific planeswalker.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b5/90/b590edd8ca15cffcde3551fb2a8d05ed00cbe3428a9ad95612fa0c8bc991f6e3.jpg)  

Print one of these forms, write down the names of all five hidden planeswalkers in the lines provided, and bring it to [a participating store](/en/articles/feature/2009/01-26-participating-store-list) by February 28th, 2009 to receive a free planeswalker spindown counter!\* 

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/mtg/images/multiverse/planeswalkers/jy7t3wvdlh.jpg)  

\- [Click here](/en/articles/feature/2009/01-26-participating-store-list) for a list of participating stores.
