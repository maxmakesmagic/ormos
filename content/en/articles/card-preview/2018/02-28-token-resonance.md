*Masters 25* is filled with incredible reprints from across *Magic*'s 25-year history. As someone who recalls white-bordered cards and the majesty of *Mirage* and *Ice Age* being my introduction to the game, the chance to play with some of the game's most iconic cards creates a mix of nostalgia and excitement I can't turn down.

For starters, *Masters 25* is home to one of the greatest blue four-drops ever created. Not only does it get stronger through [Damnation](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Damnation) and other [wrath](/en/articles/play-design/2018/02-02-the-m-files-rix-edition-white-blue-and-black#wrath) effects, it strikes a visual chord as you go digging for a die or two to make it clear what you have. On top of it all, as a Commander player, it's such a universal recommendation that I'd put it as one of the easiest cards to slot into just about any deck with the color.

![Art by Dan Scott](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/c4rd4r7_tCrhJFNrLi.jpg)

Art by [Dan Scott](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Dan+Scott%22%5d)

Yes indeed! [Reef Worm](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reef+Worm) is finally getting its chance at the premium-foil treatment as a returning card in *Masters 25*.

![](https://media.wizards.com/2018/a25/en_hj0EKZa78U.png)

What? You thought I was talking about [that *other* four-mana blue card everyone is excited about](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=195297)?

# Keeping (up with the) Current

It's easy to look back at *Magic*'s history of sets that were drafted and stop there. After all, nearly all the cards in the game came from something that was once legal in Standard. But that "nearly" is the crux of the problem. Annual *Commander* releases have expanded the mechanics, flavor, and story of the game in amazing ways. Overlooking their additions to *Magic* would be missing a piece of the game's history.

[Reef Worm](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reef+Worm) is a particular kind of card. While it isn't a named character or even tied to a specific story point in *Magic* lore, it reflects the unique and powerful ways many players love to play.

**Even in multiplayer, it's really hard to kill [Reef Worm](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reef+Worm).** Starting small, every time someone tosses out [Wrath of God](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wrath+of+God), [Fleshbag Marauder](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleshbag+Marauder), or even [Earthquake](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Earthquake), our little Worm gets *a lot* bigger. Commander, the multiplayer format it was designed for, asks a lot of creatures planning to stick around. [Reef Worm](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reef+Worm) delivers by scaling up every time someone takes a swing at it.

![](https://media.wizards.com/2018/a25/en_0MZScjB7tc.png)![](https://media.wizards.com/2018/a25/en_JMtRnyeKH4.png)![](https://media.wizards.com/2018/a25/en_fKO9THV3bj.png)

**It plays nice with almost any Commander deck.** While I'd love to see [Reef Worm](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reef+Worm) make its way into a Legacy deck, its most likely use case will be in blue-based Commander decks you build. While it's text lines shine for the format in general, the easy mana requirement of just one blue means you can slot it into any deck running blue you can imagine. Slowing the game down and taking control with [Kefnet the Mindful](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kefnet+the+Mindful)? [Reef Worm](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reef+Worm) is there. Need a handy sacrifice for [Diabolic Edict](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Diabolic+Edict) or [Greater Good](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Greater+Good)? [Reef Worm](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reef+Worm) will help. Just want a pile of value packed into one card? [Reef Worm](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reef+Worm) will answer the call. Whatever your theme, the odds are fair [Reef Worm](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reef+Worm) will help you make it happen.

**When you pair it with another *Masters 25* card, things get downright silly.** Fun fact: [Reef Worm](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reef+Worm) does its thing through triggered abilities. While explaining exactly how these work is an exercise best left for your local judge, the short of it is since these happen "when" the creature dies, they're fair game for [Strionic Resonator](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strionic+Resonator) to add to the value.

# Harmonic Resonance

[Strionic Resonator](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strionic+Resonator) hails from *Magic 2014*, and it's a card that feels like it should have legendary on its type line. It's the sort of card that doesn't tell you what it *does* or what to do with it—like [Reef Worm](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reef+Worm)—but asks you for what's possible and demands you figure it out.

**If you have plenty of triggered abilities already, [Strionic Resonator](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strionic+Resonator) adds to the fun.** There are plenty of popular Commander cards that see play solely because of their triggered abilities. [Wood Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wood+Elves), [Phyrexian Arena](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Arena), [Sword of Fire and Ice](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sword+of+Fire+and+Ice), [Bojuka Bog](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bojuka+Bog), and [Glissa, the Traitor](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glissa%2C+the+Traitor) all just scratch the surface of what [Strionic Resonator](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strionic+Resonator) can do. Powerful triggered abilities already in your decks are easy targets to amplify.

And, yes, every time [Reef Worm](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reef+Worm) dies you can copy the trigger. If someone is clearing the battlefield repeatedly, and you have Resonator at the ready each time, you can grow your army by one every round:

*   One [Reef Worm](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reef+Worm) becomes two 3/3 Fish creature tokens.
*   Two 3/3 Fish tokens can become three 6/6 Whale creature tokens.
*   Three 6/6 Whale creature tokens become *four* 9/9 Kraken creature tokens.

![](https://media.wizards.com/2018/images/daily/gXam46jpgT.jpg)

And if you can totally control every time one of the tokens goes to the graveyard and triggers, the value adds up even more.

**Decks that control their triggers will break the game.** Just like pairing [Rhys the Redeemed](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rhys+the+Redeemed) (and lots of other awesome activated abilities) with [Rings of Brighthearth](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rings+of+Brighthearth), setting up powerful triggers and planning to double it with [Strionic Resonator](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strionic+Resonator) can lead to overpowered moments. [Brago, King Eternal](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brago%2C+King+Eternal) is one way to upend a game; just use something like [Sol Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sol+Ring) to copy Brago's triggered ability, then let the copied ability resolve to flicker [Sol Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sol+Ring) and [Strionic Resonator](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strionic+Resonator) (and anything else you want) out and back into play untapped. (You can repeat that as many times as you want!) [Primordial Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primordial+Hydra) gets of out hand in a hurry, quadrupling in one upkeep. Casting [Prossh, Skyraider of Kher](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prossh%2C+Skyraider+of+Kher) is even better when twice as many Kobold creature tokens running are around afterward. [Zedruu the Greathearted](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zedruu+the+Greathearted) is slightly more selfish for you with a Resonator around. [Etali, Primal Storm](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Etali%2C+Primal+Storm) takes twice as much from everyone.

There are always accidental triggered abilities lying around in your Commander decks, but building one that takes advantage of them will quickly correct any opponents who doubt the power of the Resonator.

# More Than Fishy Fun

*Masters 25* is more than awesome Commander cards—see [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20180228000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor)—and finding things I need for every kind of deck I play is something that makes every *Masters* release special. Getting my hands on cards I haven't been able to open in years is exactly what some of my newest decks need.

After all, it's 25 years of cards I can consider. There's bound to be one I haven't tried yet!
