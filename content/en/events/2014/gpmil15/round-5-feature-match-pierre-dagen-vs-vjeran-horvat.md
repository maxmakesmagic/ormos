These two—Frenchman Pierre Dagen and Croatia's Vjeran Horvat—had made it this far without a loss, but one of them would surely lose now. Dagen could boast three previous Grand Prix Top 8s, including a finals appearance just two weeks ago, whereas Horvat's major accomplishment so far was winning a Modern Grand Prix in Prague earlier this year.

Back then, Horvat had been playing a blue-white-red aggro-control deck featuring [Geist of Saint Traft](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geist+of+Saint+Traft) and [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel) for beatdown. Since then, most players have switched to [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets) and [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer) but Horvat stayed true to his cards. Dagen was playing a version of [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy) similar to the one popularized by deck designer Josh Utter-Leyton and his teammates at the World Championship. So, while both players were running Jeskai builds, the decks couldn't have been more different from each other.

**Game 1**

The match began with Dagen cycling through some cards via [Peek](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peek), [Thought Scour](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thought+Scour), and [Izzet Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Izzet+Charm) discarding [Fatestitcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatestitcher). When Horvat made his first move and cast [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) at the end of Dagen's third turn, Dagen even increased his card selecting and resolved [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time).

Unsatisfied with the development of the game so far, Horvat finally decided to get something done on his turn four: he played [Celestial Colonnade](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Celestial+Colonnade) and tapped out for [Geist of Saint Traft](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geist+of+Saint+Traft). Now it was all in Dagen's hand. If Dagen had the combo, all would be lost for Horvat, but if Horvat would be able to untap with 8 power worth of attackers, things would quickly go downhill for Dagen.

Dagen did have the combo. He unearthed [Fatestitcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatestitcher), cast [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy), and basically just won. The messy reality of it involved a [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe), an [Izzet Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Izzet+Charm), a [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise), and another [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe), all interspersed with various [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy) triggers and the ability of [Fatestitcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatestitcher) untapping lands in between. [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy) allowed Dagen to dump a second [Fatestitcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatestitcher) and a third, which were both unearthed, and when it was obvious Dagen would neither run out of cards nor mana before growing his blue army to lethal proportions, Horvat conceded.

Pierre Dagen 1-0 Vjeran Horvat

**Game 2**

This time neither player had much action, no cantrips or otherwise, until Horvat summoned [Vendilion Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vendilion+Clique) at the end of Dagen's turn three. Dagen stopped it with [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand), or rather delayed it until Horvat's main phase when he re-cast it and took a look at Dagen's hand: two lands, two copies of [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy), two lands, and three [Izzet Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Izzet+Charm)s …

Horvat swallowed, Dagen chuckled: "A very good hand against [Vendilion Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vendilion+Clique), isn't it?"

Horvat took one Ascendancy anyway, then took the other with Wear & Tear after Dagen had cast it. [Vendilion Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vendilion+Clique) died to [Izzet Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Izzet+Charm), but Horvat was able to establish a clock anyway in the form of [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel).

Dagen tried to get his hand back into shape via [Izzet Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Izzet+Charm)s and [Serum Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serum+Visions), but before he found a solution or another threat of his own second [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel) sealed the deal.

Pierre Dagen 1-1 Vjeran Horvat

**Game 3**

Horvat opened on Relic of [Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Progenitus), a card Dagen hadn't seen before in the match and one that would make all of his [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) and [Fatestitcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatestitcher) along with the [Izzet Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141213000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Izzet+Charm) that much worse, especially when Horvat added a second Relic and was able to keep Dagen reliably at zero graveyard cards without ever having to crack one of the artifacts.
