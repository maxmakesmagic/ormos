Legacy is a format full of cards people have forgotten about that are waiting to be discovered. The newest card in this lineage? Copper Gnomes! Jeff Blyden came up with a Kuldotha Forgemaster combo deck for Legacy using four of both cards to accelerate out game-ending creatures like Blightsteel Colossus. Take a look for yourself!

Jeff Blyden's Kuldotha Combo 

Legacy – StarCityGames.com Open in Kansas City
