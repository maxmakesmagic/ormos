Congratulations to Thomas Enevoldsen, winner of [Grand Prix Strasbourg](https://web.archive.org/web/20130419000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/danes-make-their-mark-strasbourg)! Thomas beat out nearly 1,400 other players to show his dominance of the quick and powerful Legacy format. Want to try to play against him at Pro Tour *Theros* later this year but can't make a Grand Prix? No worries! If you win a Pro Tour Qualifier you are in! This season's PTQs run the Standard format, so make sure to keep an eye on Top 8 decks from both those and [WMCQs](/en/events/2012/qualifiers/2012-world-magic-cup-qualifiers) to see what's going on in the Standard metagame. Also, the [*Dragon's Maze* Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20140404015522/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/dragonsmaze/cig) updates daily, so be sure to see what's about to hit the format! Find the nearest PTQ to you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#theros)! Good luck; have fun!
