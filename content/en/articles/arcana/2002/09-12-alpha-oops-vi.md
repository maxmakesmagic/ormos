In the very first **Magic** release, several errors were made. One of which that affected game play was the printing of the wrong card type on Red Elemental Blast.

![Alpha Red Elemental Blast](https://i.ormos.uk/33/7d/337db661eb368cb618a96e150bb5f6fd67008e7eee7311e26c1f53d113f583df.jpg)

Meant to be an interrupt (as indicated by the card type of its blue counterpart), the card was printed as an instant. Under the [old game rules](/en/articles/uncommonknowledge/2002/07-03-rules-interrupted), that meant it could not actually be cast at the right time to counter a blue spell, as only interrupts could be used in such a way.

The type was changed to interrupt for each subsequent printing of the card, from Beta to *Fourth Edition*. Of course, the introduction of Sixth Edition rules forced it to be changed again, via errata…

…back to an instant.
