### Game 1

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpnap02/a905.jpg)

Ruel went on the offensive first, summoning a turn 2 [Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel). Lévy blocked with Dilligent Farmhand and used it to get himself another land, while Ruel summoned a [Nantuko Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Mentor), which could make life very difficult for Lévy unless he drew a direct damage spell.

Each of the players summoned a [Centaur Chieftain](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Centaur+Chieftain) and traded blows over the next turn. At fourteen life and facing a Mongrel/Mentor combo, Lévy could no longer attack. He summoned a [Blessed Orator](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blessed+Orator) and passed the turn. When Ruel attacked, he had his Chieftain block Ruel's as Olivier was one card short of killing Lévy with a Mongrel. Ruel discarded [Obsessive Search](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Obsessive+Search) with Madness to Mongrel, pitched his five cards and activated the Mentor's ability dealing fourteen points of damage for the turn five win.

Game 2 

Ruel did not draw a [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest), thus failing to play his Mongrel on turn 2. He cast [Compulsion](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsion) and began cycling through his library, but several cards in, still had no [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest). However, Lévy was not exerting any serious pressure, having summoned [Werebear](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Werebear) and then [Aven Archer](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Archer) but not damaged Ruel. 

In the meantime, Ruel pitched a [Basking Rootwalla](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basking+Rootwalla) to [Compulsion](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsion), and after drawing a third land, did the [Obsessive Search](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Obsessive+Search)/[Compulsion](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsion) combo to finally find a [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest), cast [Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel), and enchant it with [Ghostly Wings](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghostly+Wings). Ruel kept riding [Compulsion](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Compulsion) hard, discarding another [Basking Rootwalla](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basking+Rootwalla) to it and then casting [Bearscape](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bearscape). He also summoned [Puppeteer](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Puppeteer) and [Metamorphic Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metamorphic+Wurm), making Lévy's position less than secure.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpnap02/a906.jpg)

Lévy was trying to play for his [Vengeful Dreams](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vengeful+Dreams), holding most of his cards in hand and allowing Ruel to put more and more creatures into play. He eventually tapped four mana to cast [Mystic Zealot](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystic+Zealot), leaving two lands untapped. Ruel cast [Churning Eddy](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Churning+Eddy), returning the [Blessed Orator](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blessed+Orator) and one of the lands – thus preventing the Dreams) and attacked with everything. Lévy was forced to lose several creatures in combat, and be left with just [Werebear](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Werebear) and [Mystic Zealot](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystic+Zealot) in play. 

On the next turn, Ruel attacked with the 3/3 Mongrel and [Metamorphic Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metamorphic+Wurm) only. Lévy blocked the Wurm and Ruel gained Threshold by casting [Elephant Ambush](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elephant+Ambush), taking out the Orator. The two players bantered pointing out that Lévy could not win even with [Kirtar's Wrath](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kirtar%27s+Wrath), as that would allow Ruel to make about ten Bear tokens. 

Eventually Ruel got tired of watiting and sent nine creatures into the red zone. Lévy got some vindication by removing six attackers from the game, dropping a [Basking Rootwalla](https://web.archive.org/web/20020407000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Basking+Rootwalla) of his own into play and blocking one of the remaining attackers with a Rootwalla. 

"It's funny that you are still going to lose after this," commented Noah Boeken who was watching the game. 

True to the prediction, Olivier's next attack overwhelmed Lévy, bringing him victory.

**Final Result:** Ruel 2 – Lévy 0
