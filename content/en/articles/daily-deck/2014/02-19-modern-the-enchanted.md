Even though it's only ever been a fringe card, [Zur the Enchanter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur+the+Enchanter) has always been on my radar. After all, being able to both search your deck for a specific card and put it into play for free has historically been one of the best things you can be doing in Magic.

![Zur the Enchanter](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Zur%20the%20Enchanter&options=)

The common options to Zur for include [Steel of the Godhead](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steel+of+the+Godhead) to gain some life, [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) to remove a problematic permanent, [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas) to disrupt your opponent, and [Threads of Disloyalty](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Threads+of+Disloyalty) to take a [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) or [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant). With the release of *Theros*, we now have [Thassa, God of the Sea](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thassa%2C+God+of+the+Sea)! Additionally, [Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom) was recently unbanned, so Zur can add that to his arsenal.

[Meddling Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meddling+Mage) and [Geist of Saint Traft](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geist+of+Saint+Traft) provide a reasonable aggro-control shell and additional [Steel of the Godhead](https://web.archive.org/web/20140219000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steel+of+the+Godhead) targets. The rest of the deck is filled with removal and disruption to ensure Zur's survival. If you get to untap with Zur, you should like your spot!
