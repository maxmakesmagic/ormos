# What Are You Playing & Why?

With a metagame as diverse as Modern, the simple question, “What Are You Playing & Why?” can yield deceptively complex answers.  I talked to many different players and got completely different responses.  Even with format pillars like [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise), [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time), and [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino), there is still tons of play within this crazy-deep format.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpoma15/QQ2-reid-duke.jpg)  
**(4) Reid Duke** – “Blue-Red Delver.  It’s got a good match-up against [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod), and [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) is too powerful not to play with.”

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpoma15/QQ2-ari-lax-and-craig-wescoe.jpg)  
**(10) Ari Lax** – “Domain Zoo.  It plays all the best cards, and the attrition-based decks don’t exist anymore thanks to Delver.” 

**Craig Wescoe** – “Domain Zoo.  It plays [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl)s, and that’s kinda my thing.”

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpoma15/QQ2-pat-cox.jpg)  
**Pat Cox** – “Scapeshift.  Ben S. \[Stark\] tested a lot with it.  He said it’s good against Blue-Red.”

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpoma15/QQ2-josh-utter-leyton.jpg)  
**(16) Josh Utter-Leyton** – “Jeskai Ascendancy with [Pyromancer Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyromancer+Ascension).  It’s really powerful and really resilient, and I think it’s the right build against Delvers.”

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpoma15/QQ2-matthias-hunt.jpg)  
**Matthias Hunt** – “Amulet Bloom Combo.  One year later, I still love the deck.  And [Chalice of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chalice+of+the+Void) is real good.”

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpoma15/QQ2-nathan-holiday.jpg)  
**Nathan Holiday** – “Melira Pod.  [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) is still broken and [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20150110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino) is great.”
