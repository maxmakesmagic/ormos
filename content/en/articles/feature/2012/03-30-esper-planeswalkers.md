At the StarCityGames.com Invitational event last weekend, numerous notable pros battled it out in both Standard and Legacy. **Magic** deck builder Shaheen Soorani brought a new take on the old Solar Flare archetype: Esper Planeswalkers. Carrying him all the way to 3rd place, Soorani's new deck is shaping up to be a major force in Standard.

Named after the white-blue-black shard of Esper from *Shards of Alara* and featuring several of the cards you would expect from that color combination, the big focus of Soorani's deck is on its planeswalkers! [Gideon Jura](https://web.archive.org/web/20120330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon+Jura); [Liliana of the Veil](https://web.archive.org/web/20120330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+of+the+Veil); and [Sorin, Lord of Innistrad](https://web.archive.org/web/20120330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorin%2C+Lord+of+Innistrad) come together to pack a tag-team punch. Serving as the deck's primary route to victory, this trio of planeswalkers helps shut down creature-based assaults on the ground while also being proactive against control decks. If the planeswalker plan doesn't work out, [Consecrated Sphinx](https://web.archive.org/web/20120330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Consecrated+Sphinx) and [Batterskull](https://web.archive.org/web/20120330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Batterskull) can help close out the game.

To back it up, the deck features many of the control elements you would expect to show up in Standard. Three [Curse of Death's Hold](https://web.archive.org/web/20120330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Curse+of+Death%27s+Hold) help shut down the token-based decks, and a plethora of removal helps fight any other creatures that pop up.

Most noteworthy, though, might be Soorani's copies of [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20120330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls)! By using the tokens primarily to buy time, the deck can stutter the opponent's attacks for long enough to find a [Day of Judgment](https://web.archive.org/web/20120330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Day+of+Judgment)—or build up a planeswalker army!

Check out the decklist below, and keep it in mind for Friday Night **Magic** tonight!
