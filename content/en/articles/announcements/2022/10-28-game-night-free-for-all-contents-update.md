The decklist insert in *Game Night: Free-for-All* was mistakenly printed with a list for the Dark Sacrifice deck that included Zombify and 25 Swamps. The printed deck does not include Zombify and is [correct as originally shared here](/en/articles/feature/2022/09-29-game-night-free-for-all-2022-decklists).

Released October 14, 2022, *Game Night: Free-for-All* contains everything you need to challenge friends and family to epic magical battles. [Learn more about *Game Night: Free-For-All* now](/en/articles/feature/2022/09-29-game-night-free-for-all-2022-decklists).
