Every time a new set releases, another **Magic** artist gets his or her first artist credit. Today on Magic Arcana we take a moment to salute these arists that have begun their **Magic** art careers during the *Mirrodin* block. 

Got any favorites among the new recruits? [Go post](https://web.archive.org/web/20040728000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/showthread.php?s=&threadid=268155)! Think you have what it takes to be a **Magic** artist? Check out our [art submission guidelines](https://web.archive.org/web/20040728000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=contactinfo/artsubmission). 

Stay tuned for this fall: there are even more artists getting their first break into **Magic** illustration in the Kamigawa block beginning with *[Champions of Kamigawa](https://web.archive.org/web/20040728000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/606676)*.

Welcome, new **Magic** artists!
