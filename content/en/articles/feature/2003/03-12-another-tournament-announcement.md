### How Many Serra Angels Can Dance On The Head Of A Pin?

* * *

This is a completely philosophical (and silly) question that is, frankly, impossible to answer. There's no way to check for correct answers. So, what is Wizards of the Coast going to do about it?

Nothing.

Instead, we're going to try and find the answer to a question that we can test: How many players can participate in a ***Magic** Online* tournament at the same time? We just need you to help us find out.

On Saturday, March 22, 2003, the normal 5:00 AM, 9:00 AM, 11:00 AM, and 1:00 PM (Pacific Standard Time) tournaments will be cancelled. In their place will be a single, free Standard (with *LegionsTM*) Constructed tournament beginning at 9:00 AM PST. But you have to join early, as we're only allowing the first 4,096 players in.

No, those aren't typos, folks. We want at least 800 players and we're gunning for as many more than that as we can get. And to do that, we're offering free admission to the event and prizes for the top 512 players. We will not, however, provide you with a deck.

Why are we doing it? Imagine driving around in a high-performance sports car every day in regular traffic. Every once and a while, you just get the urge to put the pedal to the floor. Since we don't let development team members play with fast cars (or sharp objects), they have to be satisfied with putting ***Magic** Online* through its paces. And they'd like to see exactly what happens when we cram as many players as possible into one event.
