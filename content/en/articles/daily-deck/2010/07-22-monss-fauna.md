Wizards of the Coast developer Mons Johnson is famous for two things: being the namesake of the card Mons's Goblin Raiders, and building wacky decks for the Future Future League at Wizards of the Coast. In preparation for gunslinging duties at the ***Magic** 2011* Prerelease in Seattle, Washington, Mons focused on Fauna Shaman. Like Zac Hill's take on the deck, which we brought to you [last week](/en/articles/daily-deck/2010/07-13-fauna-shaman), Mons chose Squadron Hawk to help fuel his 2/2. His most powerful tutor targets? Mitotic Slime, Vengevine, and Obstinate Baloth, all of which his flexible mana base allows him to simply cast!
