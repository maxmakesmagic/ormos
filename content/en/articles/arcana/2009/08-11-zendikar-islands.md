As you know if you read [yesterday's Arcana](https://web.archive.org/web/20090811000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/zendikar-plains-2009-08-10), *Zendikar* basic lands come in two flavors. First, there are the Intro Pack variety, seen here in the handsome "[Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20090811000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island)" genus.
