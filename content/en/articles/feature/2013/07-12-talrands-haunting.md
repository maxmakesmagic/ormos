Apparently I'm on a tokens kick this week, but I really like the look of this list. It was played to 3–1 in a Daily Event by HelloHouseplant (hello!), and plays a lot of cards I like playing with.

![Intangible Virtue](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Intangible+Virtue)

Despite playing the same base as many other blue decks ([Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) and [Augur of Bolas](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Augur+of+Bolas)), this deck uses them to power out a variety of token-making cards, finishing the game with [Intangible Virtue](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Intangible+Virtue) and a [Runechanter's Pike](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Runechanter%27s+Pike). 

[Talrand's Invocation](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Talrand%27s+Invocation) in particular is a card I've not seen much of, so getting to play with it is awesome. Between that, [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls), [Midnight Haunting](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Midnight+Haunting), and Sorin, you should rarely be short tokens, especially once you consider how well Augur digs and how well Snapcaster flashes back. 

The removal suite is also atypical, relying on [Tragic Slip](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tragic+Slip) and [Warped Physique](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warped+Physique), both of which are incredibly efficient when used correctly. In this case, chumping with a token lets you [Tragic Slip](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tragic+Slip) anything that isn't [Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Progenitus), and [Warped Physique](https://web.archive.org/web/20130712000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warped+Physique) should often be enough given the hand-filling power of Augur and flashback cards. 

I've played a fair amount with tokens in Standard, but I have yet to try the blue version, and after seeing this deck I think I shall rectify that.
