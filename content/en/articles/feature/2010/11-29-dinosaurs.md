The word "dinosaur" means terrible lizard, and while [Molder Beast](https://web.archive.org/web/20101129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Molder+Beast) and [Alpha Tyrranax](https://web.archive.org/web/20101129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Alpha+Tyrranax) are not proper dinosaurs but Beasts, they are pretty terribly frightening if you're seated across from them at the *Scars of Mirrodin* Limited table. That's what many of Ari Lax's opponents found out this past weekend at Grand Prix–Nashville. There he managed to finish 2nd place using a draft deck based around the green creatures. Ari's name for the deck? Dinosaurs, of course!
