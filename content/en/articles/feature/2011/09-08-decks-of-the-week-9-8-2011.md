Now that's a debut! Pro Tour Philadelphia showed off the Modern format for the first time at a premier-level tournament and the appetite for deck lists and analysis was voracious. Congratulations to [champion Samuele Estratti](https://web.archive.org/web/20110908000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/estratti-starts-modern-revolution-philly) and his Splinter Twin combo deck, which defeated Josh Utter-Leyton and Counter-Cat Zoo in the finals. Check out the full rundown of Modern deck lists and deck techs below for a unique look at the birth of a format.
