* * *

Effective February 19, 2004, Wizards of the Coast will be increasing the prices on most ***Magic** Online* product. Boosters will increase to $3.69, Expert-level expansion theme decks and tournament packs will be $11.29, and *Eighth Edition* theme decks and the Core Game set will be $8.99. The price of new accounts and event tickets will not be changing at this time. The new prices will go live in the store shortly after the usual Thursday maintenance.

To catch you up on other important news, Platinum Angel avatars were awarded last Friday. Congratulations to all of you who won one. We'll be announcing our next release event schedule and formats soon. Don't look for the **Premier Events** room to be up and running for the *Darksteel* release, though. Returning Premier Events to our tournament offerings is a high priority for us and we've committed additional resources to this project, but we'll be taking our time and conducting exhaustive testing before reintroducing these tournaments to the public.

Speaking of *Darksteel*--we're on target for our scheduled March 1, 2004 release date and I anticipate our development team beta testing the set in early February.

The **Magic** Team is working on a new communications and web strategy that we'll be able to introduce within the next couple of weeks. The separate **Magic: The Gathering** and ***Magic** Online* sites will be merged and a point person will be reporting on weekly events for ***Magic** Online.* This individual will sit in on the highest-level planning teams and will work closely with the new **Magic** web team. I'm looking forward to introducing a knowledgeable and enthusiastic ***Magic** Online* advocate within the next couple of weeks. Our objective is to provide consistent, clear and timely information to our community. We know that our performance in this area has been spotty at best. Stay tuned for more details. 

Best wishes for the New Year and many thanks for your support in 2003. I think 2004 is going to be awesome!

Linda Cox  
 Brand Manager - ***Magic** Online*
