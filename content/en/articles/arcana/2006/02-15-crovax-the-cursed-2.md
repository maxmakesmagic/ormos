![Crovax the Cursed](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Crovax+the+Cursed)

The card **[Crovax the Cursed](https://web.archive.org/web/20060215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Crovax%2Bthe%2BCursed)** represents the cursed nobleman Crovax after he has become transformed into a vampire. And then there's [Ascendant Evincar](https://web.archive.org/web/20060215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ascendant+Evincar), which depicts Crovax after he's become the evincar of Rath. But that's not all the cards that depict this former *Weatherlight* crewman's bloody history -- not even close.

![enfeeblement](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=enfeeblement)

[Enfeeblement](https://web.archive.org/web/20060215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enfeeblement) shows Crovax despairing over his betrayal by [Selenia, Dark Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20060215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Selenia%2C+Dark+Angel).

![Slaughter](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Slaughter)

[Slaughter](https://web.archive.org/web/20060215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slaughter) shows him killing [Mirri, Cat Warrior](https://web.archive.org/web/20060215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirri%2C+Cat+Warrior) soon after becoming a vampire.

![Death Stroke](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Death+Stroke)

[Death Stroke](https://web.archive.org/web/20060215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death+Stroke) shows Crovax slaying Selenia in revenge, sealing his doom and fulfilling his curse.

![Gerrard's Verdict](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Gerrard%27s+Verdict)

And [Gerrard's Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20060215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gerrard%27s+Verdict) depicts [Gerrard Capashen](https://web.archive.org/web/20060215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gerrard+Capashen) killing the now cruel and genocidal evincar Crovax.

There are plenty more. What other cards can you find that show off this tortured soul? Check out the [**Magic** Storyline Summaries](https://web.archive.org/web/20060215000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/products/storylinesummaries) page for more stories of Crovax and the *Weatherlight*.
