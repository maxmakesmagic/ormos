Kaleidoscope is a wacky **Magic Online** format that has one deck building requirement: all cards must be multicolored. Th00mor took that constraint to heart, building this deck which features two of the game's very best two-drops: Meddling Mage and Tidehollow Sculler. So, are *you* ready to get started on a Kaleidoscope deck? [**Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20100117134321/http://www.wizards.com/magic/digital/magiconline.aspx?) is available 24/7!
