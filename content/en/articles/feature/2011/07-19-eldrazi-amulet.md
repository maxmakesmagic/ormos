Players around the world have been curious how ***Magic** 2012* would change Standard as we know it.Now, thanks to Japan Nationals, we have our first taste from a large premier event. One card that caught the eye of deckbuilder Toshiyuki Kadooka? [Quicksilver Amulet](https://web.archive.org/web/20110719000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quicksilver+Amulet). The reprinted artifact served Kadooka well, allowing him to skip paying for his giant creatures and avoid getting them countered at the same time!
