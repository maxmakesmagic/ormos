Let's take a Gatherer dive through *Khans of Tarkir* , letter by letter. Some of these might be more of a stretch than others, so bear with me.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/cardart_KTKbear.jpg)

Bear Token | Art by [Kev Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22Kev%20Walker%22])

A is for Ascendancy, [of which there are five](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&set=+[%22Khans+of+Tarkir%22]&name=+[Ascendancy]). A is also for Abzan, the white-black-green clan, which has [30 cards](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&mark=+[%22Abzan%22]) bearing its watermark.

B is for Banner, [one for each clan](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&set=+[%22Khans+of+Tarkir%22]&name=+[Banner]). B is also for bear, because there are an awful lot of them running around Tarkir, and they sure do get [punched](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Punch).

C is for Charm. As you probably know, there are also [five of them](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&set=+[%22Khans+of+Tarkir%22]&name=+[Charm]).

D is for dual lands. There are [ten of them](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&set=[%22Khans+of+Tarkir%22]&type=+[%22Land%22]+![%22Basic%22]&rarity=+[C]), all at common, and they go by several nicknames: "KTK Refuges" (based off the original [cycle of five from *Zendikar*](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&set=[%22Zendikar%22]&type=+[%22Land%22]&name=+[%22Refuge%22]) , each with Refuge in the name), "lifelands," and "KTK duals" are the ones I've seen most. D is also for [delve](/en/articles/feature/2014/08-31-mechanics-of-khans-of-tarkir#anchor3), the Sultai mechanic that returns to us from *Future Sight*, which is [on eleven cards](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&set=[%22Khans%20of%20Tarkir%22]&text=+[delve]).

E is for Elk, because [Elk tribal](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&subtype=+[Elk]) has become a thing in recent sets. You're welcome, [Marshall](http://lrcast.com/).

F is for fetch lands. These [five rare lands](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&type=+[%22Land%22]&text=+[search]+[your]+[library]&set=%7C[%22Khans%20of%20Tarkir%22]) originally saw print in *Onslaught*. Their inclusion in *Khans of Tarkir* makes them legal in the Modern format for the first time. F is also for [ferocious](/en/articles/feature/2014/08-31-mechanics-of-khans-of-tarkir#anchor5), the Temur mechanic, which is [on eleven cards](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&set=[%22Khans%20of%20Tarkir%22]&text=+[ferocious]).

G is for Goblin, a creature type with [a couple of distinctive looks on Tarkir](/en/articles/arcana/2014/09-24-monster-menagerie-goblins).

H is for Honolulu, the [always-popular site](/en/articles/arcana/2014/10-07-hi-again) of Pro Tour *Khans of Tarkir*.

I is for ibex, which pull Anafenza around on her chariot and also serve as mounts [for the Abzan](/en/articles/feature/2014/09-03-planeswalkers-guide-to-khans-of-tarkir-part-1).

J is for Jeskai, the blue-red-white clan. Like Abzan, [30 cards bear the Jeskai watermark](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&mark=+[%22Jeskai%22]).

K is for khans, because of course it is. There are [five legendary creatures](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&set=[%22Khans+of+Tarkir%22]&type=+[%22Legendary%22]+[%22Creature%22]) in the set, each the leader of his or her clan.

L is for Loxodon, as in [Woolly Loxodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Woolly+Loxodon), biggest (power + toughness) common and morph creature in the set. That ties ol' Woolly with [Pearl Lake Ancient](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pearl+Lake+Ancient) (a mythic rare!) for the *second* highest P/T sum at 13. The biggest creature in the set? The rare turtle [Meandering Towershell](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meandering+Towershell), at 14.

M is for Mardu, the red-white-black clan. The Horde claims [29 cards with its watermark](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&mark=+[%22Mardu%22]). M is also for [morph](/en/articles/feature/2014/08-31-mechanics-of-khans-of-tarkir#anchor6), which originated in *Onslaught* and is in all five clans. There are [35 creatures with morph](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&set=[%22Khans%20of%20Tarkir%22]&text=+[morph]).

N is for [Naturalize](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=386617), a common, a reprint, and the only card whose name begins with N not represented by any other link in this article.

O is for [outlast](/en/articles/feature/2014/08-31-mechanics-of-khans-of-tarkir#anchor1), the Abzan mechanic. There are [nine creatures with outlast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&set=[%22Khans%20of%20Tarkir%22]&text=+[outlast]). O is also for Orc, a creature type making its triumphant return to *Magic* [with seven cards](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&set=[%22Khans%20of%20Tarkir%22]&subtype=+[Orc]) in *Khans of Tarkir*.

P is for [prowess](/en/articles/feature/2014/08-31-mechanics-of-khans-of-tarkir#anchor2), the Jeskai mechanic, which appears [on ten creatures](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&set=[%22Khans%20of%20Tarkir%22]&text=+[prowess]).

Q is for [Quiet Contemplation](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quiet+Contemplation). Shh...

R is for [raid](/en/articles/feature/2014/08-31-mechanics-of-khans-of-tarkir#anchor4), the Mardu mechanic that appears not just on creatures, but [on eleven cards](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&set=[%22Khans%20of%20Tarkir%22]&text=+[raid]) of various types. R is also for [Rakshasa](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&set=[%22Khans%20of%20Tarkir%22]&name=+[Rakshasa]), a type of creature from [Hindu mythology](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rakshasa) making its *Magic* debut on Tarkir.

S is for Sultai, the black-green-blue clan, which commands the highest number of clan-related cards with a total of [36 cards bearing its watermark](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&mark=+[%22Sultai%22]).

T is for Temur, the green-blue-red clan, which has [31 cards with its watermark](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&mark=+[%22Temur%22]). T is also for [tri-lands](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&set=[%22Khans+of+Tarkir%22]&type=+[%22Land%22]&rarity=+[U]&text=+[%22tapped%22]), the five lands that tap for clan-specific colors.

U is for Ugin, [who is dead](/en/articles/magic-story/2014/09-24-sorins-revelation). (Spoilers!) But his [Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%27s+Nexus) yet remains.

V is for [Villainous Wealth](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Villainous+Wealth), a very vexing vindication of the vanity of venal value we vouch when villain visits violence upon victim who, vacant the vestige of volition, views vicariously the verdict of victory.

W is for wedge, which is what we call the three-color groupings that make up the clans' colors.

X is for [Empty the Pits](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Empty+the+Pits), or more specifically its casting cost, which is the first black card with XX in its casting cost.

Y is for Yasimin Ankleshank, a female goblin warrior, we are told by the [Planeswalker's Guide](/en/articles/feature/2014/09-10-planeswalkers-guide-to-khans-of-tarkir-part-2#mardu), is "famed for her rash battle expertise."

Z is for [Zurgo Helmsmasher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141009000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zurgo+Helmsmasher), who gets a second callout because he's the only card that starts with Z. Oh, and also because he was a face card for [*Duel Decks: Speed vs. Cunning*](/en/articles/feature/2014/08-18-duel-decks-speed-vs-cunning) .
