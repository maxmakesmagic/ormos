Grand Prix Kuala Lumpur was held on March 4th-5th at Sunway Pyramid Mall in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. 298 players from 13 different countries participated in the Grand Prix with many spectators watching the Grand Prix and the [**Magic** Invitational](https://web.archive.org/web/20210926055213/http://archive.wizards.com/sideboard/eventarticle.asp?event=MTG00&name=welcome), which was held at the same location. This was the last Extended Grand Prix held for the Pro Tour New York round of Qualifiers. The winner was Ryan Soh, an experienced Malaysian player using the powerful Necro-Donate deck.
