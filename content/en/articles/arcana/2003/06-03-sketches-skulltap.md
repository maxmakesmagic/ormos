This week's "Sketches" focuses on Adam Rex's rendition of a [Braids](https://web.archive.org/web/20030603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Braids)-themed discard spell, [Skulltap](https://web.archive.org/web/20030603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skulltap) from *Scourge*. 

### 1\. Art Description

These were the instructions given to the artist:

"Color: Black  
 Location: Aphetto city scene  
 Action: Show Braids, grabbing the head of a dead victim, with the victim’s life force draining out of its head and into Braids." 

The concept here is that [Skulltap](https://web.archive.org/web/20030603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skulltap), which demands the sacrifice of a creature, would be a spell cast by [Braids, Dementia Summoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20030603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Braids%2C+Dementia+Summoner). Therefore the card art needed to carry the feeling of Braids' cruelty and menace.

### 2\. Sketches

Here is a preliminary sketch of [Skulltap](https://web.archive.org/web/20030603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skulltap) submitted by Adam Rex.

We clearly see [Braids](https://web.archive.org/web/20030603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Braids) tapping into the figure's skull. But the art team felt that that the overall picture looked too light or humorous for such a dark effect. They wanted [Braids](https://web.archive.org/web/20030603000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Braids) to have a more active, menacing pose relative to her lifeless victim. The art team got what they were looking for.

### 3\. Final Art

Here is a large version of the final piece. Note how, as Braids clutches the head of her victim, tendrils of "essence" flow out of the victim and up toward Braids, just as in the original art description.
