![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpcle02/a962.jpg)

Pat Chapin has been touting the virtues of his four-color [Mirari's Wake](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirari%27s+Wake) deck since this tournament began, and he has left himself with little breathing room after losing in round 4. His opponent this round is his former teammate, Mike Turian. Turian also has one loss with his [Battle Screech](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battle+Screech) deck, which "splashes" two Cabal Therapies as some tasty targets for [Quiet Speculation](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quiet+Speculation).

As they shuffled, Chapin tried to deduce what deck Turian was playing. "You're not playing [Upheaval](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Upheaval)-Infestation, are you?"  
 "I wish," said Turian. "I'd smash you."

Chapin said that no matter what Turian was playing, Chapin would win. "Yeah?" replied Turian, "What did you lose to?"  
 Chapin: "Myself."  
 Turian: "You idiot."

Turian had to mulligan, and noted that it was his fourth in five games. He lamented not putting another land in his deck.

His new hand gave him a turn-2 [Patrol Hound](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Patrol+Hound) and a turn-3 [Divine Sacrament](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divine+Sacrament), then cast [Quiet Speculation](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quiet+Speculation) for [Battle Screech](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battle+Screech), [Deep Analysis](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deep+Analysis), and [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy). Chapin raised his eyebrows at the Therapy. "That's good beats."

Turian attacked for three with his Hound, the sacrificed to the Therapy and named [Mirari's Wake](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirari%27s+Wake). Chapin's hand contained no such card, just a [Moment's Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moment%27s+Peace), two [Deep Analysis](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deep+Analysis), and a land.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpcle02/a958.jpg)

Turian cast [Breakthrough](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Breakthrough) for two, discarding two [Prismatic Strands](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prismatic+Strands), a [Glory](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glory), and another Therapy, then cast and flashed back two Screeches to fill his side of the table. Chapin, meanwhile, had drawn two more [Moment's Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moment%27s+Peace) and was using them to buy himself a nice amount of time. Eventually he found the elusive Wake, fogged again, then uptapped and cast [Burning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Wish) for [Kirtar's Wrath](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kirtar%27s+Wrath). The sorcery killed all of Turian's army, and gave Chapin two 4/4 flying Spirits (their size owed to the Wake and Turian's thresholded Sacrament.) With Chapin's hand full, Turian conceded.

Chapin 1 – Turian 2

Turian started in with the weenies again in game 2: [Tireless Tribe](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tribe), [Beloved Chaplain](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Beloved+Chaplain), and [Spurnmage Advocate](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spurnmage+Advocate). On his fourth turn, he cast Speculation for [Battle Screech](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battle+Screech) and two Therapies, and used one to make Chapin discard his Wake.

Chapin hadn't cast a single spell until turn five, when he used a [Moment's Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moment%27s+Peace) to protect his life total. Turian used his second Therapy to steal Chapin's [Kirtar's Wrath](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kirtar%27s+Wrath), but was dismayed to find another [Moment's Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moment%27s+Peace) in Chapin's hand.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpcle02/a959.jpg)

Pat didn't do much other than fog over the next few turns, but he managed to stay alive until he could Speculate for two MORE [Moment's Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Moment%27s+Peace) and a [Deep Analysis](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deep+Analysis). The fog was thick, and Turian's creatures couldn't see well enough to ever damage Chapin.

Chapin fogged again, and Speculated all the [Deep Analysis](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deep+Analysis) out of his library, just to get threshold for his [Far Wanderings](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Far+Wanderings). He fogged again, and cast another thresholded Wanderings, going up to 15 lands, He then cast a [Flash of Insight](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flash+of+Insight) out of his graveyard for three. He found his bomb: [Time Stretch](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Time+Stretch),

At 16 mana, he cast [Time Stretch](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Time+Stretch), untapped and dropped a Wake, countered Turian's [Frantic Purification](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frantic+Purification) with a [Circular Logic](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Circular+Logic), and Wished for [Ambassador Laquatus](https://web.archive.org/web/20020907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ambassador+Laquatus). With another turn coming and access to 30+ mana, Chapin had Turian dead to rights. Turian conceded.

**Final Result:** Pat "Beloved" Chapin and his Amazing Fog Machine 2 – Mike Turian 0
