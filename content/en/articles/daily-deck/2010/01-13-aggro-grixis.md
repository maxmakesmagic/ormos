Grixis Control is a popular anti-Jund strategy in standard, but The\_Apologist is looking to make Aggro Grixis even more popular! The **Magic Online** user took this list to the Top 8 of a recent Premier Event. With creatures like Sedraxis Specter and Goblin Ruinblaster disrupting the opponent while Bloodghast proves impossible to kill, The\_Apologist stalls the game. From there, the Grixis hordes take over as powerful bombs like Siege-Gang Commander hit the battlefield. You don't want to be on the wrong end of that crew!
