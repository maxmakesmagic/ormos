The [2015 *Magic Online* Player Rewards (MOPR) page](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2015-magic-online-player-rewards) is now live!

The *Magic: The Gathering Online* Player Rewards program offers players the chance to earn digital promo cards for their monthly *Magic Online* activity. This activity includes playing in tournaments of a sufficient size, as well as making purchases in the *Magic Online* Store. For the current season, November 26, 2014 through January 5, 2015, [Nekusar, the Mindrazer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nekusar%2C+the+Mindrazer) is the event promo, and [Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames) is the store activity promo.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/mtgo/MTGO_12-15-2014_img1.png)

You can earn both standard non-foil versions, as well as premium foil versions of the Magic Online Player Rewards program cards. Check out the table below for the run-down on what activities award which cards.

Promo cards are updated each monthly MOPR period so make sure to add the [2015 page](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2015-magic-online-player-rewards) to your favorites to keep in-the-know on the latest *Magic Online* Player Rewards!
