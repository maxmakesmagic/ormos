With the Eldrazi running rampant in Standard, Polymorph is a reject rare that has been given a lot of second looks recently. But while most people have been following a blue-green path for their Polymorph combo decks, Tim Sussino opted for a different method: blue and red. He uses Dragon Fodder, Goblin Assault, and the powerful Stone Idol Trap to provide himself the targets he needs for his Polymorph, and when it resolves? He picks up a free Iona, Shield of Emeria. The list was strong enough to put him into the Top 8 of a PTQ in Hartford, Connecticut.
