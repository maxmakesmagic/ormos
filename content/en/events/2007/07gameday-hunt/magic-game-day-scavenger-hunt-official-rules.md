By entering the Wizards of the Coast, Inc. ("Wizards") **Magic: The Gathering** Game Day Scavenger Hunt Contest ("Contest"), you agree to be bound by these Official Rules.

**1\. No purchase necessary.** The Contest is open to legal residents of the fifty (50) United States and Canada subject to eligibility requirements below. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED BY LAW. The Contest begins on July 14, 2007 and ends on July 14, 2007 when each participating retailer selects its Contest winner. This Contest is sponsored by Wizards. 

**2\. Eligibility:** The Contest is open to legal residents of the fifty (50) United States and Canada ages thirteen (13) and older as of July 14, 2007. Employees of Wizards, its affiliates, subsidiaries, franchisees, agents, retailers, advertising and promotion agencies, and fulfillment companies, and their immediate families (parents, children, siblings, spouse) and members of their same households (related or not) are not eligible to participate in the Contest.

**3\. How to enter:** To enter this Contest, bring as many of the 30 **Magic: The Gathering**-related items set forth below with you on July 14, 2007 to one (1) participating **Magic: The Gathering** Game Day retailer. Entrants may only enter once. **Entrants must be present at the time of the participating retailer's announcement of the winner in order to be eligible to win.** For a list of participating retailers, go to [www.wizards.com/magicgameday](/en/events/2007/07gameday/magic-the-gathering-worldwide-game-day). All items will be posted here no later than July 2.

1\. *Arabian Nights* Fishliver Oil card  
 2. Empty booster display box of any expansion from *Alpha* to *Mirage*  
 3. An *Alpha*, *Beta*, *Unlimited* or *Revised* Starter Pack deckbox  
 4. Booster pack wrapper of *Arabian Nights*, *Revised*, *Unlimited*, *Beta* or *Alpha*  
 5. **Magic** rulebook with Bog Wraith on the cover  
 6. **Magic** rulebook with Shivan Dragon on the cover  
 7. *Arabian Nights* Mountain card  
 8. *Legends* Rules card  
 9. *Invasion* Reya Dawnbringer card  
 10. *Exodus* Paladin en-Vec card  
11\. *Onslaught* Festering Goblin card  
12\. *Visions* Nekrataal card  
13\. Lord of the Pit card illustrated by Mark Tedin  
 14. *Tempest* Mogg Fanatic card  
15\. *Alliances* Guerilla Tactics card  
16\. *Scourge* Siege Gang Commander card  
17\. Giant Growth card illustrated by Sandra Everingham  
18\. *Visions* Stampeding Wildebeests card  
19\. *Mirrodin* Platinum Angel card  
20\. *Fifth Dawn* Crucible of Worlds card  
21\. *Urza's Legacy* Treetop Village card  
22\. *Ice Age* Adarkar Wastes card  
23\. *Apocalypse* Caves of Koilos card  
24\. Largest collection of 1 single card (basic land excluded)  
 25. Duelist Issue Number 1  
 26. Entrant with Lowest DCI Number  
 27. An officially licensed **Magic** comic book  
 28. Clothing with **Magic** artwork on it  
 29. Four different Friday Night **Magic** cards  
 30. Three spin down **Magic** life counters with different expansion symbols on them  

**4\. Judging:** Each participating **Magic: The Gathering** Game Day retailer will serve as the judge of the Contest for his or her respective store, review all eligible entries, and select one (1) winner on July 14, 2007 who brings in the most of the 30 above-enumerated items. Ties, if any, will be broken by the person who first brought in the most of the above thirty (30) items on July 14, 2007. **Entrants must be present at the time of the participating retailer's announcement of the winner in order to be eligible to win**.

All decisions made by Wizards and its judges/participating retailers on all matters relating to this Contest are binding. By entering the Contest, entrants agree to abide by Wizards' and the judges' decisions and waive any right to appeal.

**5\. Prizes and Approximate Retail Value:** There will be one (1) prize winner per participating **Magic: The Gathering** Game Day retailer. Each such winner will receive a prize consisting of a **Magic: The Gathering** backpack. Approximate retail value of prize is US$15.

Prizes are not transferable. Wizards, in its sole discretion, may substitute a prize for another product of equal value to the prize identified herein, or provide the cash equivalent. All decisions made by Wizards on all matters relating to this Contest are binding. Winners are solely responsible for paying all applicable federal, state and local taxes, and all other expenses associated with any prize not explicitly stated herein. Winners and their guests may be required to sign and return an Affidavit of Eligibility/Release of Liability and Publicity and any other documentation required by Wizards, or prize will be forfeited in its entirety. In the event the winner is a minor, the winner's parent or legal guardian will be required to sign the aforementioned documents. Noncompliance with this requirement, return of prize/prize notification as undeliverable, or failure to comply with any terms of these Official Rules will result in a winner's disqualification.
