Players who compete in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier during 2015 will receive a [Liliana of the Veil](https://web.archive.org/web/20141011000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana+of+the+Veil) promo cards.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/ptktk/rptq_promo.png)

Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifiers, which qualify winners for the Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers, are store-level events. To learn more about what format your local Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifiers will be run in, and to see if your local store is running one, talk to your store. Sanctioning for these events is open now, so be sure to check with your store to see if they are planning to run one!

For more information on the upcoming Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifier season, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20141011000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/protour/pptqvan15).

For more information on the Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers set to take place on April 25, 2015, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20141011000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/protour/rptqvan15).
