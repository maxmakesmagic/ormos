Modern has been heating up on **Magic Online** the past few weeks, and many players have been searching for the best control deck in the format. **Magic Online** player Peruzo has thrown his deck into the ring after a recent 4-0 result in a **Magic Online** Daily Event. Peruzo's control deck of choice: a take on the Mystical Teachings deck, featuring Dralnu, Lich Lord to reuse spells over and over! Check it out!

Peruzo's Blue-black Teachings 

Modern – 4-0 in a Magic Online Daily Event
