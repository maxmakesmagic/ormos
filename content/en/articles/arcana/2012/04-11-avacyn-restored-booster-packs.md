As a large set, *Avacyn Restored* has 244 cards. And it's got five different images that adorn its booster packs. Let's take a look at them!

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/21/95/21951b115aade7e8927611aca1f25b699bda9dfbb360130672bd4cb867e280ed.jpg) 

  

This one can't come as a surprise. It's Avacyn, Angel of Hope.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e4/03/e403b5237f97e2a8f31ba32c0e6ba504a6dea20b571c9ba55a23ec13642eed9e.jpg) 

  

And this, of course, is Avacyn's old Helvault-mate Griselbrand. Everything's in order so far. Perfectly logical.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f0/c1/f0c112457bf26e4c545088aabef72473f74d3a58c0814d95fb85bd2e1603a02c.jpg) 

  

This appears to be an angel of some sort. That's not all that surprising in this set, right?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d2/dc/d2dc8c0bb4825b0000c130a3bdb88638a0950531590a62a73110a206113f7a4c.jpg) 

  

Whoa! This doesn't look familiar at *all*!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/67/a5/67a5d92990cd347fc46adf5131ca50fa088125609c36afca7b0d619cf8692c7b.jpg) 

  

This, again, is an angel. And if you want to know more about it, check out today's [Planeswalker's Guide to *Avacyn Restored*](/en/articles/feature/2012/04-11-planeswalkers-guide-to-avacyn-restored-part-1).
