Looking for a fresh take in the world of Extended? Why not try Jonathan Job's Sun Titan Control list? Jonathan used the deck to make the Top 8 of a Pro Tour Qualifier in Denver, Colorado by casting the best creatures in black, red, and white. He then gets them back later in the game with Sun Titan! As a back-up, Ajani Vengeant is there to save the day should anything go awry. The list was good enough to take Job to the semifinals. For more info about Qualifiers on **Magic Online**, click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20110904022453/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/events/ptqmtgo/nagoya/11).
