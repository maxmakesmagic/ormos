With nearly four thousand players in the main event, and likely another thousand in the room, Grand Prix New Jersey is an impressively huge event. I took a stroll around the convention center during the fourth round in an attempt to fully capture the sheer size of this weekend's Legacy Grand Prix!

I started my mid afternoon walk in front of the stage.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/DSCN0326.JPG)

  

I made my way beyond the feature match area.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/DSCN0328.JPG)

  

"Surely these crowds won't persist." I thought to myself as I walked toward the back of the room.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/DSCN0329.JPG)

  

Could the side event area be this crowded? Yes, it could. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/DSCN0330.JPG)

  

I looped around to the higher numbered tables. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/DSCN0331.JPG)

  

Then I walked back toward the stage. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/DSCN0333.JPG)

  

I was astounded by the massive size of this tournament. The spectacle of it. There was so much *Magic* happening. So much mental energy buzzing through the air. It was incredible that such an unbelievably diverse group this large could be enjoying a shared passion in perfect harmony. *Magic* was bigger and better than ever.
