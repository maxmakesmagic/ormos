### Help Me Prepare For The Invitational

* * *

**Name:** Daniel Myers  
**Occupation:** Editor, ***Magic** Online* Web Site  
**Last Sanctioned Tournament:** *PlaneshiftTM* Prerelease

Okay, admittedly I do play *some* **Magic** games working here at Wizards. But I'd just started playing Sanctioned tournaments after the *InvasionTM* set became legal in Standard. And I played my first draft after I started working here.

So, what do I know about building decks in the current Standard and Extended (with *LegionsTM*) environments?

Not much, and my deckbuilding skills leave a bit to be desired. (Although my white-blue-black *Invasion*-era control deck worked pretty well. How was I to know that Go-Mar wasn't going to be in fashion until after *Planeshift* was legal?)

The one advantage I have in the Invitational is that, while I'd really like to win, I'm out to have fun. And, in the interest of enjoying the imminent beats, I'm asking the ***Magic** Online* community for help. Unlike my esteemed colleagues, I'm not just looking for random assistance. There are some guidelines: 

*   **I'm not really good at control.**
    Granted, I love the *idea* of control decks and can play them, but that's gonna take some serious work on my part. So I'd like to avoid "Mother May I" decks as much as possible.  
  

*   **I like Sligh and Weenie decks.**
    What can I say? Play creature. Attack with creature. 'Tings to the dome. What's not to like?  
  

*   **I really like five-color decks.**
    I suppose it's just a holdover from beginning my short tournament career during *Invasion* block, but I do enjoy playing all five colors when feasible.  
  

*   **I want a Tribal Wars deck that *uses* the creatures.**
    Oh trust me, there's been plenty of talk around here about three color Mirari's Wake decks that use elves or birds for mana acceleration and card drawing. To me, that's not really the spirit of the competition. I'd rather go down fighting with my horde of gobbos any day.  
  

*   **And speaking of Wake decks...**
    We all know that this could be *the* bomb in Standard with *Legions* cards. Again, I'd rather go rogue and play something a little on the edge.

With those things in mind, I'll say that I can be swayed by argument and I'll look at whatever's put in front of me. And no, I don't have something against Mirari's Wake in and of itself. I'm just not terribly interested in blasting someone with Firecat Blitz for 180 points of damage ('tho that can be fun). In fact, I was already looking at a green-white Spirit deck that uses it.

So send your ideas for Tribal Wars, Standard (with *Legions*) decks, and even your theme deck suggestions to mtgonlineeditor@wizards.com. With your help (and a lot of luck) maybe we'll get to see Mirri, Cat Warrior as an avatar.
