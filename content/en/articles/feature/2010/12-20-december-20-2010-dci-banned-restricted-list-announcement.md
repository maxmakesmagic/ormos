**Announcement Date:** December 20, 2010  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2011

***Scars of Mirrodin* Block Constructed**, **Standard, Extended, Vintage**  
 No changes

**Legacy**  
[Survival of the Fittest](https://web.archive.org/web/20101220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Survival+of+the+Fittest) is banned.  
[Time Spiral](https://web.archive.org/web/20101220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Time+Spiral) is unbanned.

**Magic Online** effective dates and changes to **Magic Online**–only formats are now announced monthly in the [**Magic Online** Group](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-gatecrash-qualifier-season-top-8-standard-decklists-43) .

For the complete list of all banned and restricted cards by format, click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424103806/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=judge/resources/banned).

For an explanation of the changes to Legacy from R&D member Erik Lauer, click [here](/en/articles/feature/2010/12-20-explanation-of-december-2010-b-r-changes).
