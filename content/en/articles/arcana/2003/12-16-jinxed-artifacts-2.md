Unless you're the size of a homunculus or are wearing powerful glasses, it can be hard to make out small details of **Magic** card art. 

Have you seen the [little surfer](/en/articles/arcana/2002/06-12-surfs-up) on Flame Wave, for example?

Let's take a zoomed-in view of a few of **Magic**'s "jinxed" items.

![Jinxed Idol](https://i.ormos.uk/76/ff/76ff7ba07dc81ccbbd76a1c6e66667a8bc55efcb37970dd91fa891127af31c2a.jpg)  
  
  
  
You may already know that certain drawback-laden, "jinxed" artifacts flavorfully curse their controllers, and initiate a game of Hot Potato as soon as they hit the board. For example, **Jinxed Idol** from *Tempest* is a creepy stone figurine that stings its bearer for 2 damage a turn. Give it away as soon as you can -- if only to stop seeing its ugly mug on your side of the table.

  

![Jinxed Ring](https://i.ormos.uk/1a/00/1a00c8fb6d61401348dd559114b35f0b1a9e8078e159352c99b92f9ef4783784.jpg)  
  
  
  
  
  
  
And you may have seen that *Stronghold's* **Jinxed Ring** is a piece of jewelry in the shape of Volrath's toothy head that bites the "wearer" for each card he or she loses. How... unwholesome.

  
  

But did you know that *Mirrodin's* **Jinxed Choker** is actually a thin silver snake that slowly tightens its grip, increasing its turn-by-turn damage as the charge counters pile up?

![Jinxed Choker](https://i.ormos.uk/04/de/04de8a1b90291c85bdca017d48f2fb6cdc914b8ebb6697546840250b62b6c4f6.jpg)

Perhaps the Flame Wave [surfer](/en/articles/arcana/2002/06-12-surfs-up) was a slightly less unsettling detail to zoom in on!
