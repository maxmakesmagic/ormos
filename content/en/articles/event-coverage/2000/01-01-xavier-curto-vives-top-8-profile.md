**by Sideboard Staff** 

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/GPAMS01/861.jpg)**Name:** Xavier Curto Vives  
**Age:** 24  
**Location:** Terrassa (Barcelona)  
**Occupation:** Student  
**Played Magic since:** 1995  
**Best accomplishments:**48th 1999 Worlds 

**Q: What made you decide to come to Grand Prix Amsterdam?**  
 A: I won a trial. 

**Q: What were your expectations going into this tournament, and why?**  
 A: To qualify for PT Barcelona 

**Q: Briefly describe your tournament play-wise so far, both day one and day two.**  
 A: Very good decks and medium skill of play. I mean really good decks. 

**Q: What do you think is the main reason for you making top 8 in this tournament, and why?**  
 A: Very good decks on the 2nd day. 

**Q: What does this top 8 performance mean to you?**  
 A: Playing at PT Barcelona. My first PT 

**Q: What are your predictions for the top 8?**  
 A: I really don't know any of the other players. 

**Q: Anything else you'd like to share with us?**  
 A: Thanks to my translators!!
