Rich Hagon and Brian David-Marshall discuss the formats that will be used at the inaugural World Magic Cup, highlighting key cards in each format. This three-day event features national teams from more than 65 countries battling across Standard, Modern, *Innistrad/Avacyn* Restored Block Constructed, *Magic 2013* Booster Draft, and M13 Team Sealed Deck.
