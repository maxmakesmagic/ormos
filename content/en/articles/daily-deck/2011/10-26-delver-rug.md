With Mental Misstep to longer in Legacy, one-drops have been given a new lease on life. It's the perfect time for some new ones to show up—like Delver of Secrets! The innocuous *Innistrad* common fit perfectly in Calosso Fuentes's spell-heavy blue-red-green deck, flying him right into first place at the StarCityGames.com Open Series in Baltimore.

Calosso Fuentes's Blue-Red-Green Counterbalance 

Legacy – StarCityGames.com Open Series in Baltimore, MD
