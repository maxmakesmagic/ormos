### 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7d/8f/7d8ff54118818537df5162c2b230a51bc3935197e65afa73336339f039d3ebf6.gif)Switzerland will be holding its National Championship this weekend. The odds-on favorite is pro player Raphael Gennari. He'll have his work cut out for his, as the Swiss are among Magic's oldest competitors. 1995 World Champion Alexander Blumke is also qualified for the event. See if Gennari or Blumke can lead Switzerland to the 2002 World Championships. Coverage will start Saturday, April 27 and be available [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20041111001632/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.remori.ch/swissmagic/index.htm&origin=sideboard_news_20020424a).
