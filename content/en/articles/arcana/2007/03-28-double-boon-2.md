Several early **Magic** sets had multiple alternate art pieces for the same card. One such set was *Alliances*, in which the card Phyrexian Boon was slated to get two collectible pieces of art. 

Mark Tedin elected to illustrate the two Phyrexian Boon pieces as one continuous mural. Check it out:

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/15/d1/15d1f670b9f8c1069102e96909d9acb750341f74d6c7c00875e565b53a8f106a.jpg)](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/mtgcom/Arcana1000/1300_BoonMuralBig.jpg)

  
*Art by Mark Tedin*
