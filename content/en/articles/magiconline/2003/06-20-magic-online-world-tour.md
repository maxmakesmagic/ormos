### Well, Maybe Just The Midwest

* * *

While ***Magic** Online* can be everywhere the Internet is, I have yet to figure out how to digitally transfer myself. So, instead of being worldwide, I'll be taking a short tour of the Midwest this summer. Well, actually, ***Magic** Online* is making the convention tour and I'm going along for the ride.

### First Stop: 2003 Origins -- Columbus, Ohio

From June 26 through June 29, I'll be doing demos of ***Magic** Online* at [Origins](https://web.archive.org/web/20030620000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.originsgames.com&origin=magic_magiconline_editorial062003) in Columbus, Ohio. Working Origins is a nostalgic experience for me, as it's one of the first gaming conventions I ever attended. (The first being Capcon at The Ohio State University -- I'm not telling what year.) Being back in my hometown talking about my favorite game is going to be a blast.

### Second Stop: 2003 GenCon -- Indianapolis, Indiana

Then, from July 24 through July 27, I'll be working at [GenCon](https://web.archive.org/web/20030620000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.gencon.com&origin=magic_magiconline_editorial062003) in its new home -- Indianapolis, Indiana. I was fortunate enough to attend the final show in Milwaukee, Wisconsin last year, and am looking forward to seeing what Inianapolis has in store for us. From what I've been told about working GenCon, I should be exhausted, sleep-deprived, and have sore feet by the end of it. However, I plan to have fun and enjoy showing off ***Magic** Online* to the crowd.

If you plan on attending either of these events, stop by the Wizards of the Coast area and say, "Hi." See you at the Cons!
