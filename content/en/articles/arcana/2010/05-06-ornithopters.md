Hey! Let's talk Ornithopters! They've been around almost as long as **Magic**, having appeared originally in *Antiquities*, when Amy Weber provided this basic blueprint.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d7/b9/d7b9217256882f06696b6ab79ed090990df94ece9c2351bf1916c57fb49691a8.jpg) 

Ornithopter | Art by [Amy Weber](https://web.archive.org/web/20100506000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22amy+weber%22])

That remained the standard look for Ornithopters from *Fourth Edition* through *Classic Sixth Edition*. Then **Magic** had to live without Ornithopters until *Mirrodin* brought Dana Knutson's version to life.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ad/50/ad50e00958be5adbe4a05f06eb8304a743bbd3a8e30a7b37bf29ad81ee8808b6.jpg) 

Ornithopter | Art by [Dana Knutson](https://web.archive.org/web/20100506000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22dana+knutson%22])

With *Ninth Edition*, Dana Knutson's Ornithopter was in the core set, staying there through *Tenth Edition*. And then ***Magic** 2010* brought the most recent look. Franz Vohwinkel, take it away!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/05/f5/05f572f3a611a3a4a8a3c7a98b2e97261878c2a14bb2ca9b64bf5056888d967e.jpg) 

Ornithopter | Art by [Franz Vohwinkel](https://web.archive.org/web/20100506000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22franz+vohwinkel%22])

Who knows what Ornithopters the future of **Magic** might hold?
