Looking for the new Standard tech? Want to see who the best and brightest players are in your region? Wondering who has taken the first steps on the Road to Worlds? Let the decklists from Regionals be your guide as you explore the new Standard environment.

You can test each decklist thanks to our [Sample Hand Generator](https://web.archive.org/web/20090602023708/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/294). Just click on the card fan in the upper right of each decklist and you can see what your opening hand could be, along with mulligans and the first three cards you'd draw.

[U.S. Nationals](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603154935/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/nationals/us08) will be held July 31-August 3 in Chicago, Illinois. [Canada Nationals](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603103917/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/nationals/canada) will be held August 2-3 in Montreal, Quebec. [Great Britain Nationals](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603161051/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/m-fest) will be held August 15-17 at M-Fest in Birmingham, England. The date and location for [Japan Nationals](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603161051/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/m-fest) is September 19-21 in Yokohama. For more information on all Nationals, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20090601172507/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/nationals).

*Additional decklists to be added as they are submitted by tournament organizers.*
