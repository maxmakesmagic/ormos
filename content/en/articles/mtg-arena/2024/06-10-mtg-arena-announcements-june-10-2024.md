## *Modern Horizons 3* Launches Tomorrow on *MTG Arena*!

The wait is nearly over—*Modern Horizons 3* hits *MTG Arena* full power tomorrow, June 11!

Playable in [Timeless](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://magic.wizards.com/formats/timeless), [Brawl](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://magic.wizards.com/formats/brawl), and [Historica few pre-ban exceptions](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://magic.wizards.com/formats/historic)), these cards promise to amp up the excitement—and you thought *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* brought the Wild West! You can see them all in the complete [*Modern Horizons 3* Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/modern-horizons-3/card-image-gallery).

### Last Day for Preorders!

If you haven't yet, ***this is the last day to [put in your Modern Horizons 3 preorders](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/mtg-arena-announcements-may-28-2024#mh3preorders)!*** There are two bundles to pick from: the [Sorin Pack Bundle](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/mtg-arena-announcements-may-28-2024#sorinpackbundle) and the [Tamiyo Play Bundle](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/mtg-arena-announcements-may-28-2024#tamiyoplaybundle), but don't miss the [Enemy Fetch Lands Anthology](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/mtg-arena-announcements-may-28-2024#enemyfetchlandsanthology) that's also here for a limited time! When the update begins tomorrow (Tuesday, June 11), these will disappear.

*Modern Horizons 3* is the first *Modern Horizons* set to be draftable in *MTG Arena*, and we have lots of Draft and Sealed events coming up for the set:

*   June 11–July 4 – *Modern Horizons 3* Sealed
*   June 11–July 30 – *Modern Horizons 3* Traditional Draft
*   June 11–July 30 – *Modern Horizons 3* Premier Draft
*   June 21–July 2 – *Modern Horizons 3* Quick Draft
*   June 22–23 – [Arena Open: *Modern Horizons 3*](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/arena-open-modern-horizons-3-june-22-23)—all participants receive the Powerbalance sleeve.
*   July 7 – *Modern Horizons 3* Best-of-One Bonus Play-In

## Explorer Qualifier Weekend This Weekend

[![Qualifier Play-In Best-of-Three, June 14, Explorer format](http://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/June%20Bo3%20Play-In%20Explorer%20-%201920x1080.webp)](https://www.magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) 

The June Qualifier Weekend is coming this weekend, kicking off with the [Best-of-Three Qualifier Play-In on Friday, June 14](https://www.magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in), beginning at 6 a.m. PT. This Explorer event is the last one that can get you an invitation to the Qualifier Weekend event if you haven't secured one yet.

With an invitation in hand, the [Qualifier Weekend event](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) begins on Saturday, June 15, at 6 a.m. PT. Also in the [Explorer format](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://magic.wizards.com/formats/explorer), this two-day event is where the competition gets fierce, the stake rise, and you get traction on the path toward tournaments like the Arena Championship and beyond.

Premier Play structure changed a bit recently, so check out the [*MTG Arena* Premier Play in 2024 article](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/mtg-arena-premier-play-in-2024) for complete details.

## *MTG Arena* Rewards for WPN Play in *Modern Horizons 3*

[![Draft three times in WPN stores, get one Draft token on MTG Arena, June 14–July 14, plus score a bonus card style and sleeve](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/MH3_wpn_arena_cross_promo_social_1920x1080.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/announcements/play-on-the-modern-horizons#wpnmh3promo) 

We have another opportunity for you to score *MTG Arena* rewards for playing at your local game store! Between June 14 and July 14, play in Booster Draft or Sealed events at [a WPN retailer](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://locator.wizards.com/) using your [Wizards Account](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://myaccounts.wizards.com/) connected to your *MTG Arena* account and pick up a Kozilek, the Broken Reality card style for participating in one Sealed or Draft event; a Wurmcoil Engine sleeve for your second Sealed or Draft event; and for your third you'll get a Draft token.

Check out [all the details on the WPN *MTG Arena* promo](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/announcements/play-on-the-modern-horizons#wpnmh3promo)!

## Competitive Play Schedule

All times listed are Pacific time (UTC-07:00).

### Premier Play

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events.

Qualifier tokens are delivered to your *MTG Arena* inbox. Remember to claim them before the event starts!

#### June

*   **Qualifier Play-In (Best-of-Three)**
     June 14, 6 a.m. PT–May 15, 3 a.m. PT
    *   Format: Explorer
*   **Qualifier Weekend**
     June 15, 6 a.m. PT–June 16, 4 p.m. PT

*   Format: Explorer

### Arena Open

The Arena Open Day One entry window begins at 6 a.m. PT and closes to new entries the following day at 3 a.m. PT (UTC-07:00). The Day Two entry window is 2 hours only, from 6 a.m. PT until 8 a.m. PT (UTC-07:00).

*   **[Arena Open: *Modern Horizons 3*](https://web.archive.org/web/20240610000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/arena-open-modern-horizons-3-june-22-23)**
    *   June 22: Day One, *Modern Horizons 3* Sealed (Best-of-One and Best-of-Three)
    *   June 23: Day Two, *Modern Horizons 3* Draft (Best-of-Three)
