After ten rounds, there was one team left at undefeated. Matt Sperling, Dave Williams, and fourth-ranked Paul Rietzl. These three are no strangers to success individually, and as a team has finished in both first and second place in only three outings.

Learn a bit more about these three, and what the “Scottsdale Foundation” thing even means. The trio also waxes about their love of Team Draft—which they’re hoping to do in the Top 4 this weekend.
