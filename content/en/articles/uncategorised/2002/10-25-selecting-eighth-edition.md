Selecting Eighth Edition is coming to a close. (Haven't been following along? Review the Selecting Eighth Edition archive [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20041226203910/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/columnarchive&column=SelectingEighthEdition).) We have two steps left… choosing the last three flavor text winners, and then voting on basic land art from a veritable slew of options.

We'll be back next week to go over the land options, and to announce all the winning flavor texts. In the mean time, you can vote on which player-submitted flavor text you want to appear on three more cards.

The three cards we'll be voting on are Verduran Enchantress, Confiscate, and Serra Angel. All of the entries below were submitted by players. Our staff chose the best ones, and those are the options. Confiscate is special in that we asked for real-world quotes.

So read them over and vote for your favorite! The winners will end up on the actual *Eighth Edition* cards!

![Verduran Enchantress, Confiscate, Serra Angel](https://i.ormos.uk/ea/8e/ea8e0953efdf717fdb8f96727aa9b74b1f91b01ce771547cbde5d6f5ff3b5e70.jpg)

After you vote in each poll, use your Back button to return to this page and vote in the next poll. Only one vote for each poll will be recorded.
