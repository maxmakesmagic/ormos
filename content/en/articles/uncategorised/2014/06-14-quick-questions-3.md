**What is the most underrated card in the format?** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/QQ-Marijn-Lybaert.jpg)

*Marijn Lybaert: [Abrupt Decay](https://web.archive.org/web/20140614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrupt+Decay). It's a 2-mana removal that also takes care of all the cards that the black devotion deck has problems with. It kills [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections), [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere), [Domri Rade](https://web.archive.org/web/20140614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Domri+Rade) and it's even good against bestow creatures like [Herald of Torment](https://web.archive.org/web/20140614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Herald+of+Torment).* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/QQ-Alessandro-Portaro.jpg)

 *Alessandro Portaro: [Nyx Fleece Ram](https://web.archive.org/web/20140614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nyx+Fleece+Ram). In the match ups where it is good, it wins games by itself.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/QQ-Tomoharu-Saito.jpg)

*Tomoharu Saito: [Mana Confluence](https://web.archive.org/web/20140614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Confluence) and [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos). Both very powerful.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/QQ-Lee-Shi-Tian.jpg)

 *Lee Shi Tian: [Hall of Triumph](https://web.archive.org/web/20140614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hall+of+Triumph)* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/QQ-Patrick-Dickmann.jpg)

 *Patrick Dickmann: I really wonder why more people aren't playing [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix).*
