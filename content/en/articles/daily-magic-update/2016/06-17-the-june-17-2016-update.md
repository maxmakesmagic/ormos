# Today's Must

1.  [Seven Common Mistakes and How to Correct Them](http://magic.tcgplayer.com/db/article.asp?ID=13341&writer=Craig+Wescoe&articledate=6-17-2016) | TCGPlayer | Craig Wescoe

It must be teaching week in the community, as we've had multiple theory/learning-style articles this week. The latest entry comes from Craig Wescoe, who, coincidentally, is trying to formulize teaching people to play better *Magic*. Today's lesson covers seven deadly sins common mistakes and what to do instead.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Pauper Grixis Affinity](/en/events/2016/pauper-constructed-league-2016-06-16/pauper-constructed-league)

The results from a post-*Eternal Masters* metagame are creeping in, and the most surprising deck has almost nothing to do with *EMA*. *Magic Online* user FredMcFred recently went 5-0 with [Bleak Coven Vampires](https://web.archive.org/web/20160617000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bleak+Coven+Vampires) in his deck.

I'll give you a moment to read the card.

Combined with flicker effects and a million artifacts, this deck is able to take advantage of both strong affinity cards ([Thoughtcast](https://web.archive.org/web/20160617000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtcast), [Galvanic Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20160617000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Blast)) and enters-the-battlefield abilities ([Mulldrifter](https://web.archive.org/web/20160617000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mulldrifter), [Beetleback Chief](https://web.archive.org/web/20160617000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Beetleback+Chief)) for a deck that's almost two decks in its execution.
