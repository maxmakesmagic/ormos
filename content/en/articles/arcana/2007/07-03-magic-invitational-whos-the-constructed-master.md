[![](https://i.ormos.uk/95/e1/95e1ecd747dd66faf2625bfb32f5b00a52e284ff8051513a353b6f7df8b5d20f.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20070703000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/showthread.php?t=876047)Voting for the **Magic** Invitational is going on right now! This year's Invitational will be October 18-21 at Spiel 2007 in Essen, Germany. A new ballot opens each week, and this week **Magic** fans are choosing their favorite Constructed Master. All Invitational voting is being done through the Wizards of the Coast message boards, so if you don't have an account there yet, you'll need to [go sign up](https://web.archive.org/web/20070703000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/register.php). If you haven't voted yet, this is your chance. 

To see the full schedule of upcoming votes (and which players are already in), [click here](/en/events/2007/mi07/2007-magic-invitational-voting). As for this week's Constructed Master vote, you can read profiles of each candidate [here](/en/events/2007/mi07/gabriel-nassif-is-the-2007-constructed-master). Results will be posted in Brian David-Marshall's "The Week That Was" column on July 6. Once you've read up on the candidates and are logged into the message boards, [go here](https://web.archive.org/web/20070703000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/showthread.php?t=876047) to cast your vote, and make sure to keep checking back for the results and to cast your vote for the remaining ballots!
