The banning of Mystical Tutor left many in the world of Legacy believing Reanimator was dead. But Sam Roukas has stumbled upon a different plan of action for cheating gigantic monsters to the battlefield for cheap. How does he do it? Through Show and Tell and Sneak Attack. Enlightened Tutor helps him find the enchantment, and cards like Ancient Tomb help him cast it faster. From there you just need a red mana to plop Emrakul onto the battlefield, a savings of fourteen mana!
