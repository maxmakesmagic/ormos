Hall of Fame member and long-time Pro Tour competitor Raphael Levy and platinum-level player Shahar Shenhar had already played yesterday during the Standard Constructed rounds, where Levy was able to emerge victorious. Now, the two face off once again. The format? *Gatecrash* Booster Draft. Would Shenhar be able to even the score?

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/ptgtc13/r9_levy_shenhar.jpg)  
*Raphael Levy and Shahar Shenhar face off for their second match of the weekend, with Shenhar looking to even the score.* 

"I'll play," Levy said jokingly when Shenhar rolled two ones to see if he'd go first. "You still have to roll," Shenhar said with a laugh, as Levy rolled a 6 on one die and chose to go first.

### Game 1

Levy made the first play of the game with [Prophetic Prism](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prophetic+Prism), while Shenhar came out of the gates with [Cartel Aristocrat](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cartel+Aristocrat), which attacked in on the next turn and was joined by [Kingpin's Pet](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kingpin%27s+Pet). Levy cast [Millennial Gargoyle](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Millennial+Gargoyle), and chose to not block the Aristocrat when it came in, going to 16. [Balustrade Spy](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balustrade+Spy) knocked a land off of Levy's deck, while the French Hall of Famer tapped out for [Adaptive Snapjaw](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Adaptive+Snapjaw).

But when Shenhar put on the clock with a [Shadow Slice](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shadow+Slice) ciphered onto the Spy, attacks from Shenhar quickly put Levy to 6, who was not ready to throw his Gargoyle in front of a flier just yet. The [Snap](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snap)jaw cracked in for 6, and [Scorchwalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scorchwalker) gave Levy another big attacker. [Simic Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simic+Charm) bounced Shenhar's Spy back to his hand, but Shenhar replayed it on the next turn, extorting Levy to 5 with [Kingpin's Pet](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kingpin%27s+Pet), as the Aristocrat and [Scorchwalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scorchwalker) traded blows.

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/ptgtc13/r9_levy.jpg)  
*Levy is left with only a few points of life to play with in his first game against Shenhar.* 

Levy tapped out for [Ruination Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ruination+Wurm), but lost his attacking [Snap](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snap)jaw to [Smite](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smite) after Shenhar blocked with his [Kingpin's Pet](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kingpin%27s+Pet). [Devour Flesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devour+Flesh) caused Levy to sacrifice his Wurm for 6 life, unwilling to lose his [Millennial Gargoyle](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Millennial+Gargoyle) to the removal spell. Shenhar ketp his [Kingpin's Pet](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kingpin%27s+Pet) back, content with extorting Levy for the next few turns. [Balustrade Spy](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balustrade+Spy) attacked in, and was joined by another one as they began to take out chunks of life that Levy gained back from Shenhar's [Devour Flesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devour+Flesh). Levy, who had no answers to the fliers, cast [Towering Thunderfist](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Towering+Thunderfist), hoping to find a moment to push through.

Levy went to 4 after an extorted [Deathcult Rogue](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathcult+Rogue) came down, but Levy wasn't done yet. He sent in his ground attacker, then played [Hellraiser Goblin](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellraiser+Goblin) after attacks. When Shenhar sent in his team, [Homing Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Homing+Lightning) shot down the dual Spies, [Millennial Gargoyle](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Millennial+Gargoyle) finally got to jump in front of [Kingpin's Pet](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kingpin%27s+Pet), and the [Deathcult Rogue](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathcult+Rogue) dropped Levy to 2.

When Levy's next card did not yield a 6 power creature to go with his [Hellraiser Goblin](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellraiser+Goblin), he scooped up his cards.

Levy 0, Shenhar 1

### Game 2

Shenhar was first to act again with [Syndic of Tithes](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syndic+of+Tithes), but Levy was ready with [Mugging](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mugging) for the Orzhov two drop. What Levy was not ready for, however, was stalling on lands, whiffing on his third turn land drop. Shenhar, meanwhile, rebuilt with [Gutter Skulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gutter+Skulk).

Levy found a [Simic Guildgate](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simic+Guildgate) waiting on top for his fourth turn, giving him access to the colors he needed, while Shenhar added another rat to his board and attacked for 2. [Viashino Shanktail](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viashino+Shanktail) from Levy, who was no longer hurting to his land drops, was dealt with via [Devour Flesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devour+Flesh), as Shenhar sent his team in. [Disciple of the Old Ways](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Old+Ways) gave Levy a way to hold back the two [Gutter Skulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gutter+Skulk)s, as Shenhar deployed [Kingpin's Pet](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kingpin%27s+Pet) to take to the skies.

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/ptgtc13/r9_shenhar.jpg)  
*Shenhar is determined to take the fight to the skies.* 

Levy had a second [Mugging](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mugging) to take out the [Kingpin's Pet](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kingpin%27s+Pet), but Shenhar simply replaced it with [Assault Griffin](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Assault+Griffin). Levy's Homing Lighting shot the bird out of the sky on the next turn, but Shenhar replaced that flier with a new [Kingpin's Pet](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kingpin%27s+Pet). Levy, now out of removal for the moment, cast [Slaughterhorn](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slaughterhorn) and passed with the Disciple still on defense.

With the coast finally clear, the [Kingpin's Pet](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kingpin%27s+Pet) started its attacks. [Deathcult Rogue](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathcult+Rogue) let Shenhar extort Levy to 12. The Hall of Famer cast another [Disciple of the Old Ways](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Old+Ways), but he was staring down a three turn clock.

That clock quickly went up a turn as [Screeching Bat](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Screeching+Bat) joined the team (with Shenhar extorting for a point), as the [Kingpin's Pet](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kingpin%27s+Pet) and [Deathcult Rogue](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathcult+Rogue) sent Levy to 7. Levy, out of options, sent in his team. When Shenhar didn't block and had a [Cartel Aristocrat](https://web.archive.org/web/20130216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cartel+Aristocrat) for two points of extort, Levy offered the handshake.

**Levy 0, Shenhar 2**
