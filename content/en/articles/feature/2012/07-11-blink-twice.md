While the [flicker mechanic](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flicker) goes back a long way, it really didn't take its place in the Constructed circle until [Momentary Blink](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Momentary+Blink) was printed and the mechanic came with card advantage attached. [Momentary Blink](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Momentary+Blink) was probably the most powerful flicker ever, but there have been plenty to see print since. In *Avacyn Restored*, both [Cloudshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudshift) and [Ghostly Flicker](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghostly+Flicker) do a pretty good job of showcasing the mechanic in Limited, but [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel) is the big champion here, already taking a firm hold of success in Standard. But that isn't the only place you can find a flicker...

[Venser, the Sojourner](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Venser%2C+the+Sojourner) has been a well-liked card ever since it was spoiled on this very site, but its success stories have been few and far between, as it has existed in a very powerful environment. Well, Llowle on **Magic Online** seems to think the strategy is still a viable one and showcased the Planeswalker in a pretty awesome list that made its way to the Top 8 of a recent Standard Premier event.

This list might not have as much of an emphasis on the blinking action as you would think, but Venser still plays a central role in the deck's game plan. With something as innocent as a [Stonehorn Dignitary](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stonehorn+Dignitary) out, the opponent might not ever get another attack step again! Resetting a [Batterskull](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Batterskull) token or gaining extra life off of a [Pristine Talisman](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pristine+Talisman) is also pretty nifty and turns the underplayed [Drogskol Reaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20120711000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drogskol+Reaver) into an actual card-advantage engine, even before it attacks!

Not a believer? Well, there is only one way to prove the power level of this deck and that is to play. So sleeve up and enjoy this at your local Friday Night **Magic**, or go digital with **Magic Online**. Just be sure to take it easy on all of those aggro decks!
