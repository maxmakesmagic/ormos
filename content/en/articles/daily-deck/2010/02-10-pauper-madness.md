Pauper is a fun format for **Magic Online** that prohibits the use of rares, mythic rares, and uncommon, requiring players to build entire decks using only commons. Baldr7 was up to the task, putting together this black-green madness / graveyard deck. Powerful spells like Wild Mongrel allow madness spells like Grave Scrabbler to hit the battlefield on the cheap.
