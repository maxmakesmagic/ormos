Do you like putting a Sword of Feast and Famine on your Mirran Crusader? How about pumping your elf army with Ezuri, Renegade Leader? Ramping to massive mana for the single-turn Scapeshift/Valakut combo kill? Dropping a turn-one Goblin Guide and never looking back? Or maybe even some serious enters-the-battlefield shenanigans with Flickerwisp, Sun Titan, Wall of Omens, Glimmerpoint Stag, and Lone Missionary? Then Extended may be the format for you. All of those strategies earned invitations to Pro Tour Nagoya last weekend, showing off a diverse metagame stretching across the last four blocks of **Magic**. With only four weekends left to grab your invitation, find a qualifier near you now!
