Looking for a fun combo deck for the Standard format? Open the Vaults is your deck! Cycle cards like Glassdust Hulk and Architects of Will, or draw and discard Filigree Angel with Sphinx of Lost Truths. Then accelerate Open the Vaults onto the battlefield with your Borderposts, and voila! An instant army of artifacts *and* a healthy boost in life. The deck even made the Top 8 of [Pro Tour–San Diego](/en/events/2010/ptsd10/gortzen-goes-all-the-way-in-san-diego) in the hands of Belgian Niels Viaene.
