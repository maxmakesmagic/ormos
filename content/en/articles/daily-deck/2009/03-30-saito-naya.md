Former Pro Player of the Year Tomoharu Saito was the champion at Grand Prix–Singapore. He used his own take on Naya Zoo to earn the top slot. Cutting burn and Sulfuric Vortex, he opted to play a slightly slower version that was more powerful in the mirror match and benefited from Gaddock Teeg maindeck against matchups like Storm. Read more about the Grand Prix [in the official event coverage](/en/events/2009/gpsin09/saito-slaughters-singapore).
