The StarCityGames.com Open Series in Des Moines last weekend produced some great **Magic** action and decklists—including this one from Jeff Hoogland. Glissa, the Traitor is a card players have been trying to break since its release, and Hoogland made Standard deck builders everywhere a little happier by cruising into the Top 8 with a Glissa-fueled control deck!

The core interaction with Glissa is alongside artifacts that destroy creatures. If Ratchet Bomb or Perilous Myr happen to destroy a creature—as they often tend to do—you can immediately return it to your hand. Of course, if you play plenty of strong artifacts, that *also* means you can bring those back as well. Wurmcoil Engine and Batterskull lead the pack as powerful artifacts this deck can close out the game with.

The deck isn't just Glissa-or-bust, either. Five planeswalkers and an assortment of removal spells ensure this deck can just as easily lock down a game and then finish off with a Wurmcoil Engine. It's this kind of versatility that propelled Hoogland to the top.

If you've been waiting for a dedicated Glissa deck that can go toe-to-toe with the top decks in Standard, you won't want to miss this one! Check out the decklist below.

Jeff Hoogland's Glissa Control 

Standard – Top 8, StarCityGames.com Open Series, Des Moines
