Welcome to the second Part of my PT Prague report.

  
  

### POD 4 TABLE 1

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/ptpra06/fm8_oruel.jpg) 1 Da Costa Cabral, Bernardo 24  
 2 Boj Perez, Israel 24  
 3 Ruel, Olivier 24  
 4 Oosawa, Takuya 24  
 5 Crosby, Joseph 24  
 6 Sibast, Rasmus 27  
 7 Donovan, Michael 21  
 8 Hüttenberger, Christian 24  

I know most of the names at the table but there’s only one person I don't want to play against: Bernardo Da Costa Cabral.  
 Pack one was pretty bad with a pick one Galvanic Arc, pick two Terrarion, pick three Surveilling Sprite. It was not a deliberate choice to go Izzet, the packs were just too bad here. I thankfully received a late Skyknight Legionnaire followed by a Thundersong Trumpeteer, but my only two fixers (Selesnya Signet and Dimir Aqueduct) weren't really sexy at that point.  
 The question was to know whether I'd go Izzet or Orzhov in pack two. The great point about being Boros after pack one is the flexibility it offers for pack two.  
 My *Guildpact* pack offered me a choice between Blind Hunter and Belfry Spirit. I went for the uncommon which is just better to me, and allows me to wait for the next packs to decide my second guild. Pack two was a choice between Orzhov Basilica and Gruul Guildmage. I almost went for the dual as I was 99% sure not to play green, but I remembered about my Selesnya Signet and went for the 2/2, assuming one more Signet or green dual would make him good. Then I was passed almost nothing good, except for off color fixers, so I went for Basilica, and two copies of Izzet Boilerworks. My deck was pretty bad then, but I add infinite off color fixers, so I could grab the best non green card of every Dissension booster I would receive.  
 And what did I first pick? Rakdos Signet...  
 Luckily, Wrecking Ball, Court Hussar and Helium Squirter were my next picks, followed by Guardian of the Guildpact, and, divine surprise, Rakdos Pit Dragon pick 6. Two Demon's Jester , Carom and Gobhobbler Rats would finish the deck, which was not good, but definitely ok, maybe it would be enough for 2-1.

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/ptpra06/sibast.jpg) I was paired up against Rasmus Sibast from Denmark, the last undefeated player from the tournament. [Here](/en/events/2006/ptpra06/round-10-will-the-undefeated-fall) is the coverage of the game by Scott Johns  
 After I sided in Reroute at the previous draft against Genki Taru, and misdirected Seal of Doom, I wanted to try the card main deck.  
 It definitely made me win game two VS Rasmus when his Rakdos Guildmage commited suicide.  
 My next match is one of the craziest I've played in 7 years at the PT. I'm down a game against Joseph Crosby, I mulligan to five, miss two land drops, and he's absolutely crushing me. I think about conceding the game, as I can't deal with his Goblin Spelunkers and as he also has two more guys including a Scorched Rusalka, but one card in my hand makes me think I must have an out, Reroute. Joseph sacrifices a 2/2 into my Absorver Thrull to give me lethal damage, but I survive playing Withstand and buy myself a turn. Still no removal, I have to pass. I'm at 4, Spelunkers attacks, put me down to 2. Joseph sacrifices two of his three guys to finish me, including the Rusalka itself, as he didn't except a trick from two red manas untapped. I Reroute the ability, but I'm still facing a 3/1 Imp and a 2/2 Mountainwalk. My next draw gives me the key card I needed, Izzet Boilerworks. I play the bounceland, return my only mountain to my hand, and I add a Belfry Spirit to the board. A new game is starting, life totals are 1-19, and only 5 minutes left on the clock. He draws Halo and equips his flyer, which forces me to race and hope he doesn’t draw any burn or removal. On the second additional turn, Joseph concedes assuming I'll kill him on turn 4.  
 Back from hell, 1-0-1 with a mediocre deck, I'm now only 2-2 from Top 8.  
 Last round I'm facing a German amateur, Christian Hüttenberger. Despite playing pretty bad cards and having nearly no fixers, his card pool is good enough to make him win if he draws his mana properly. He wins game one after a pretty long game thanks to his massive removals spells, and takes the second drawing more removals than me drawing guys. Maybe mulliganing to 4 didn't help me either.

I need to 2-1 table one to make it to the final eight.

Woebringer Demon or Dimir House Guard? Long thinking here again, as I have unfortunately never played the 4/4 before, I don't know how good it is. It seems as good as the 2/3 to me, but I go for the safe pick and grab the card I know.

[Draftviewer](https://web.archive.org/web/20060520000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/magic/draftools/draftviewer.asp?draftid=5_6_2006_6_20_40_0&player=2&pack=1&pick=7&showpick=false)  
Terraformer can be a good mana fixer, while Safeguard is a solid turn three drops, which ability can compensate Moroii loss of life. Dimir House Guard also allows me to kill him anytime, so I go for it, but both picks were ok.

[Draftviewer](https://web.archive.org/web/20060520000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/magic/draftools/draftviewer.asp?draftid=5_6_2006_6_20_40_0&player=2&pack=1&pick=8&showpick=false&alwaysshowpick=false)

 Oracle #2 over Halo, I'll try to use it as a fixer as I'm not being passed any.

![](http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/ptpra06/sf2_huttenberger.jpg) Then I’ve been passed nothing worth being mentioned. Pretty good after 31 picks, my deck seems now empty except for the rares.

My first opponent going in the last draft is Christian Hüttenberger again. Up a game, I lose game two to a huge mana screw. Game three offers me a pretty solid three-land opening hand featuring only one creature, Primordial Sage. I then draw five straight lands before I can eventually drop it, but the game is looking pretty bad already. Christian gives me a big smile, takes his time slowrolling his removal and says, « *what about... Hit your guys and you take six hahaha*». I guess stress approaching the end of the tournament made him forget about common things like respecting an opponent. But my favourite one happened a few turns later, when he is winning whatever happens, put me down to two and goes « *Remember Honolulu? (Big Smile) Lightning Helix you haha.* » So funny indeed… « *I hope I'll see you in top 8* » he says while leaving the table. Well… I don't.

I'm then called in feature match to face Paolo Vitor Da Rosa. Antoine warned me, despite Brazilian Magicians are not too scary these days, I'd better not underestimate Paolo. My brother played against him several times and he apparently impressed Antoine. [Here's](/en/events/2006/ptpra06/round-14-good-start-poor-finish) the coverage of the match.  
 Unfortunately, Paolo is not used to the Feature Match and the stress being in contention for Top8. He did several misplays on a first game he shouldn't have lost, before not drawing land of the all game two.

[Final](https://web.archive.org/web/20060520000000/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgevent/ptpra06/fm15) match for Top 8, VS Shuuhei Nakamura: I seat in the same chair of the feature match area for the 11th time in 16 rounds, with an 8-1-1 record so far.  
  
 Up a game against Nakamura, I realize I only have to win three and a half matches to be level 6 in 2007. After he destroys me in game two and three, I unfortunately have to change my plans.

I end up in 20th place, disappointed I miss the Top 8, but still pretty happy about my tournament as I can see hardworking in Amsterdam in the previous week paid. I also feel like I've played my best limited Magic in a while, which makes my final result less painful. I’ve also caught Mark Herberholz at the PPY race, but I am now one point behind Osawa. It's great to see I have chances to have a good finish at the PPY race at the end of the season. I'll work hard to do so, next stop, Charleston, South Carolina.
