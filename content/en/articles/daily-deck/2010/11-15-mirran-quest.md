Last week we brought you the tale of a Phyrexian-aligned *Scars of Mirrodin* Game Day deck. Fair is fair, so this week we bring you a tale of a Mirran-aligned deck! Kostas Daniilidis used a White Weenie strategy based around Quest for the Holy Relic and Argentum Armor with a twist: the cards in the deck were aligned with the Mirrans. That meant Kostas was able to earn a special preview card from *Mirrodin Besieged* for the deck's effort!
