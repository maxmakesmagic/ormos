There is another Deck Pack for [**Duels of the Planeswalkers** 2013](https://web.archive.org/web/20130519033118/http://www.wizards.com/magic/digital/duelsoftheplaneswalkers.aspx?)! Pack One had [Boros and Simic](/en/articles/arcana/2012/11-07-duels-2013s-deck-pack), and Pack Two had [Orzhov and Gruul](/en/articles/arcana/2012/12-05-duels-of-the-planeswalkers-2013-deck-pack-2-2). The new Deck Pack has Dimir and a five-color deck. And the five-color deck has Progenitus!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ca/34/ca34e885bcdba4352bedaf11073dc33989e601b050eaa27c3552e3c408aa74d2.jpg) 

  

### Rogues' Gallery

Manipulate and infiltrate with the blue and black "Rogues’ Gallery" deck. Disrupt your enemies’ plans with spells, then summon stealthy mages and shadowy undead to deliver the killing blows. 

Rogues' Gallery
