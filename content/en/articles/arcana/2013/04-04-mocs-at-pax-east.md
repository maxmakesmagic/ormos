We held the **Magic Online** Championship at PAX East this year and the finals feature Butakov vs. Malteko in a quick finale to the event. In case you missed it, check out the finals below, and the two top decks. Or go check out the [entire coverage archive](/en/events/2013/mocs12/butakov-tramples-his-way-past-the-opposition) for the event!
