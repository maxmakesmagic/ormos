As I was reading the first installment about the **Magic** Colony I got to thinking about all of the **Magic** players struggling to qualify for a major **Magic** tournament. Having recently made the transition from store owner/tournament organizer to writer/**Magic** player this held dual interest for me. 

Each week I plan to recount my efforts to qualify at a PTQ for Pro Tour-Houston somewhere on the East Coast. I am also going to follow a rotating cast of characters that play at Neutral Ground and other stores in the New York City area.

Before I could start, I would need a deck. I have been known to play with some unusual cards in Constructed formats, as my rating reflects. For this season I wanted to try and play with a good deck while maintaining an open mind as to what makes a card good. 

I looked at all of the information I could find on the format. Fortunately there were a number of Grand Prix Trials and even a PTQ at Origins prior to the first week of the season to draw upon. The Ferrett wrote numerous articles at Star City, Professional Event Services posted decklists from multiple GP Trials and a PTQ, and Andrew Stokinger even explored the idea of a combo deck in the format.

I spoke with Mike Flores often and he urged me to play Monoblack week one and Project X (his super-secret rogue deck that he claims will knock the metagame on its keister) week two.

I really don't like Wonder. I don't think it is good for this format but you cannot play u-g without it. It adds Wonder-screw to the game. Not only do you have to worry about drawing enough land but you have worry about drawing and discarding a Wonder AND having an island in play. It even adds Wonder-flood to the game. A three or four Wonder draw is quite underwhelming against 6/6 flying Player Reward tokens.
