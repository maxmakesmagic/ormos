They say that you can get a pretty good feel for a **Magic** set by looking at its tokens. Well, nobody says that right now, but there's a chance they might start. After all, when you see a pair of tokens like this...

<table><tbody><tr><td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/af/52/af52eb119f302633d57adc2e6fb0993f4387b774006a9dfc04f171b77c6d8b90.jpg"></td><td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/bb/35/bb35604f3da8228c534f83a9bd0ecae08205438ddb1169ad99e002e30723d097.jpg"></td></tr></tbody></table>

...doesn't it make you picture a card like this?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/75/be/75be493983bdbf382a2682d21db6cc140af95ab0f7fc46c77a1b2d827ffec762.jpg)  

Okay, that was kind of a gimme. But let's look at the other tokens of *Scars of Mirrodin* and see what they tell us about the set.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/68/36/683605a07638a916a1e90a4f5185117cfefd28d943f3f323fc1ded5bf0020440.jpg)  

Myr tokens? That brings to mind the Myr Battlesphere!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/12/45/1245ced7196a64505a6ed2bf5941c1a3d8c472778af9131419aec5c02211275f.jpg)  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/3e/7a/3e7a0db91987c762941c3af99ba838bf5ee024213958ccac14f2d201cef7eea2.jpg)  

Ah! That looks like a golem that gets created by the Precursor Golem, which just appeared on the [Visual Spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20100915000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/scarsofmirrodin/spoiler) yesterday!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/02/a4/02a4935b1a3a1d5168246c84ae759b920eb50399a162383b70f489fa5e4c1a18.jpg)  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/0e/92/0e926841aa8cc0d0c53c29564ca7fc256cf8d89c2357032540de7bb29fbe9872.jpg)  

This wolf, of course, is the product of the Sword of Body and Mind.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c5/d2/c5d2c0834eb1e9336b09c61000cf0870077ac02466c3ab12b3777684a89ddc44.jpg)  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/47/dd/47ddb7e3680706e5062a34802d6aed72edf153a5ce377dc9f4706c88fa4d7578.jpg)  

A 1/1 Insect with infect? Hey, that rhymes! And it suggests that maybe someone's been using a Trigon of Infestation around here. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/61/9a/619afc7fee515b50eced6e2c0eeaf2908dc8b18bacbb8ea9184240988ebb073f.jpg)  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/71/76/7176597f231a4d8983487942322d1e51f0182897a9378890918e9f47267f04cd.jpg)  

Where did this Goblin come from? Well, that will have to remain a mystery for now.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/7d/d97d36517f87f7b775265519d6cea6adf10d30576c4e7cec606b620f4da924f3.jpg)  

A 1/1 Soldier? Well, that one's easy: Elspeth Tirel!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f8/2f/f82f6835d3988c0b870b2da60f1776beb8ad36cd102ee98e18fc38c76c19ab7d.jpg)  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/56/53/5653541697cfb1e000a595a653d47613b91731aa72c8c7a6fe04799ee1b2b349.jpg)  

And our last creature token is a mighty Cat, spawned, presumably, by Kemba, Kha Regent.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4c/01/4c012adae98eb80520a5504c77667f28a7b7ee8b0c38e97d400517914b916e0d.jpg)  

There's one more interesting one, but it's not a token card. And no, it's not an emblem either. It's a *counter* card.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d8/d0/d8d02449acedada497764745ef23e2eca251b0a8b467c2d913da9a64d2fcc282.jpg)
