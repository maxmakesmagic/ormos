Like so many gift sequences, it all began with an indestructible fruitcake.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/96/d5/96d53bea11b113ada2928f27f0b81821e5758ae6416b6139d5d9af4f0d20c177.jpg) 

  

It was 2006, and for the first time Wizards of the Coast had released a special promo card to commemorate the holidays. They were given out as gifts to employees and others associated with Wizards.

In 2007, the special surprise was Gifts Given.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/39/ac/39acd655e90b63f055e35f9620ae144a09bd404cea46042919669cbcef52b199.jpg) 

  

For 2008, Wizards of the Coast wanted to remind everybody that it's *definitely* better to give than receive.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ba/f3/baf3edbc297d7b30a2f502ed7d8432b072e7f115a9d2ead0e209ffa566d26dac.jpg) 

  

Here's a higher-resolution look at the "gifts" wrapped up in bows for their fortunate recipient. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f6/17/f6171c0243745f221c6bdc7b780e3a70ffddd606339e4744a955c56e40f7759e.jpg) 

  

We hope you had a great holiday season!
