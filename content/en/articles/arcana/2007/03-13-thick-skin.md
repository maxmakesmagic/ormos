But it also has a protection from red ability that keeps it around when it comes time to pay <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20070313000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Subterranean+Shambler">Subterranean Shambler</a>’s echo cost. ![keeper of kookus](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=keeper+of+kookus)

Although goblins have [a distinctive look](https://web.archive.org/web/20070313000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/time-vs-goblins-2007-03-07) in *Time Spiral* Block, the art for [Thick-Skinned Goblin](https://web.archive.org/web/20070313000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thick-Skinned+Goblin) bears a number of similarities to *Visions*’ [Keeper of Kookus](https://web.archive.org/web/20070313000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Keeper+of+Kookus).

What features can you see that they have in common?

*   [Thick-Skinned Goblin](https://web.archive.org/web/20070313000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thick-Skinned+Goblin) holds an iron rod in the fire that looks the same as [Keeper of Kookus](https://web.archive.org/web/20070313000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Keeper+of+Kookus)’s.
*   [Thick-Skinned Goblin](https://web.archive.org/web/20070313000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thick-Skinned+Goblin) lacks a nose, but you can see some familiar bushy eyebrows.
*   Both have similar gloves and boots held dangerously close to a fire source
*   Both have a certain toothiness to their grins.
*   Check out the flavor text of [Keeper of Kookus](https://web.archive.org/web/20070313000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Keeper+of+Kookus).
