The leader of the Azorius guild is the sphinx Isperia, currently appearing in *Return to Ravnica* as Isperia, Supreme Judge. But Isperia first appeared in *Ravnica: City of Guilds* as Isperia the Inscrutable.

 ![Isperia the Inscrutable](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Isperia%20the%20Inscrutable&options=) 

  

The art for Isperia the Inscrutable was drawn with more background than you see on the card. In fact, it looks like this!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0a/19/0a19b9899f84410cfa43e41c9946c78baa3eff5908cfdb6dd6ae57a726d6c791.jpg) 

Isperia the Inscrutable | Art by [Greg Staples](https://web.archive.org/web/20121029000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22greg%20staples%22])

Back then, the guildmaster was Grand Arbiter Augustin IV. But by the time Isperia came back in *Return to Ravnica*, the sphinx had taken the position and got a new card: Isperia, Supreme Judge.

 ![Isperia, Supreme Judge](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Isperia,%20Supreme%20Judge&options=) 

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/91/b5/91b5408d7b5f35c98f96406948fdf6d7e8947b30386d4a2dc0a393fffc8248b3.jpg) 

Isperia, Supreme Judge | Art by [Scott M. Fischer](https://web.archive.org/web/20121029000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22scott%20m.%20fischer%22])

Isperia's new look also appears on Sphinx's Revelation:

 ![Sphinx's Revelation](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Sphinx%27s%20Revelation&options=) 

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/57/0a/570a23a40405d073a203a774836b038333b95b88c76ec1ec5359f44098bfc919.jpg) 

Sphinx's Revelation | Art by [Slawomir Maniak](https://web.archive.org/web/20121029000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22slawomir%20maniak%22])

There's another place to get a good look at Isperia, but it's not on a card. As seen in [this Arcana](/en/articles/arcana/2012/10-15-new-mtgo-avatars), there's a new **Magic Online** avatar for Isperia, Supreme Judge!
