*What was the first deck you ever built, and how did it work?* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b4/9a/b49a97d847733a73985c5d85979f9c0df999dfc0380369be66282a7035d6c565.jpg)

**Mons Johnson:** The very first deck I built was centered on Ley Druid. Besides the 4 or 5 Ley Druids it had Instill Energy, Wild Growth, Stasis, Llanowar Elves, Unsummon, Counterspell, my one precious Time Walk, some other random green cards (maybe even a Craw Wurm), and an Ornithoper or two. As this was soon after *Antiquities*, they weren't the most efficient creatures available, I had to make do with what was around. The concept was for Ley Druid to tap for one or more mana, either accelerating me into more powerful spells and/or keeping the Stasis going. Instill Energy would occasionally win a game by enchanting an attacker while Stasis held everything for a couple turns.
