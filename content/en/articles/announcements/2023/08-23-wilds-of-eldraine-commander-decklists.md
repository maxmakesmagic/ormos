**EDITOR'S NOTE: This article has been updated to clarify *Wilds of Eldraine* Commander deck token availability. During printing, some tokens intended for the Virtue and Valor (Green-White) Commander deck were accidentally swapped with tokens intended for the Fae Dominion (Blue-Black) deck. The set of tokens found in the Fae Dominion (Blue-Black) deck are correct.**

On September 8, 2023, you'll be able to enter the [*Wilds of Eldraine*](https://web.archive.org/web/20230823000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/wilds-of-eldraine). A terrible curse has fallen upon the kingdom as the Kenrith twins fight for the future of their plane. To join in on the fairy-tale fun, we'll have two *Wilds of Eldraine* Commander decks releasing alongside the set! 

![Fae Dominion (Blue-Black) Commander Deck](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/en_EJ8LfYhQgcg4.png)

Fae Dominion (Blue-Black)

![Virtue and Valor (Green-White) Commander Deck](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/en_qExkyBhoYerA.png)

Virtue and Valor (Green-White)

See the cards from these decks in the new [Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20230823000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/wilds-of-eldraine/card-image-gallery), which features updated information about all of the amazing cards in *Wilds of Eldraine*, such as the [exciting Booster Fun treatments](/en/articles/feature/2023/08-15-collecting-wilds-of-eldraine) in the set. 

Each deck contains 10 new-to-*Magic* cards, 10 double-faced tokens, 2 traditional foil legendary creatures, and 1 foil-etched display Commander. We've got the decklists for these *wild* Commander decks here, and you can preorder them now from [your local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20230823000000/https://locator.wizards.com/), online retailers like [Amazon](https://gtly.to/oDSipyfgz), and elsewhere *Magic* products are sold!
