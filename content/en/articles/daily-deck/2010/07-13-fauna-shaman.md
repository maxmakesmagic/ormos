Our second installment of R&D champion challenge decks starring ***Magic** 2011* and today's deck comes from former Pro Tour star and R&D member Zac Hill. Zac knows a thing or two about deck building, and one card that caught his attention in the new set was Fauna Shaman. The 2/2 is great for turning cards in hand into just the right creatures, and Zac had Squadron Hawk to help provide him plenty of fodder for tutoring with the Shaman. He took on challengers in Atlanta, Georgia.
