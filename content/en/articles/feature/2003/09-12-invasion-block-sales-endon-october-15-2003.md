* * *

The time has come to bid a fond farewell to the beloved *Invasion* block. On October 15, 2003 at 5:00 PM Pacific Daylight Time, *Invasion* block sets *(Invasion, Planeshift,* and *Apocalypse)* will no longer be on sale in the [***Magic** Online* Store](https://web.archive.org/web/20030912000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com&origin=magic_magiconline_news091203). Since this is only the second time we've retired sets from the game, let's go over what this means.

You won't be able to purchase *Invasion, Planeshift, or Apocalypse* product from the [***Magic** Online* Store](https://web.archive.org/web/20030912000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com&origin=magic_magiconline_news091203). No more boosters, tournament packs, or theme decks will be available. The only way you'll be able to get any IPA product will be to trade other players for it.

However, this doesn't affect the playability of IPA product you already own. Boosters and tournament packs can be used in Invasion Block Limited events in the **Casual Play** area. Cards will continue to be legal in Extended Constructed format for quite some time and will always be legal in Open-format games.

Although this doesn't have anything to do with *Invasion* block product not being available, I'll remind you that you still have about three years to redeem *Invasion, Planeshift,* or *Apocalypse* sets. (Sets can be redeemed until four years after they first become available on ***Magic** Online.*) While it'll be harder to collect full sets as the years go by, you still have plenty of time to play if you're thinking about redeeming those sets.

As long as we're on the subject of sets, the *Mirrodin* set will first be available at the [***Magic** Online* Store](https://web.archive.org/web/20030912000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com&origin=magic_magiconline_news091203) in November 2003. We haven't set an exact date for release, but figured you'd want to know we're back on the "month after physical release" schedule for the foreseeable future. Once we have a firm date set, I'll post it here on the website.
