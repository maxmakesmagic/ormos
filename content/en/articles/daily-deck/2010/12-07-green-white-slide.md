When he won the 2004 World Championships in San Francisco, California, Julien Nuijten was the youngest world champ of all time. His prize purse, thanks to a strong team performance, also made him the owner of the largest Worlds payday. At this year's World Championships, Brad Nelson will give Julien a run for his money in Chiba, Japan thanks to the **Magic Online** Championship Series. You'll find live coverage of that tournament [here](/en/articles/event-coverage/2011/10-10-magic-weekend-san-francisco-and-2011-worlds-event-information) this weekend, and you can read about Julien's exploits in San Fran [here](/en/events/2004/worlds04/nuijten-nassif-dominate-worlds).
