Last weekend saw two Constructed formats represented in exciting Grand Prix: Standard at [Grand Prix Nagoya](https://web.archive.org/web/20121214000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/okitas-glorious-rise-nagoya) and Modern at [Grand Prix Toronto](https://web.archive.org/web/20121214000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/edel-tops-toronto). Congratulations to Yuuji Okita for his win in Nagoya and Willy Edel for taking down Toronto! The next Grand Prix, in Indianapolis, will be *Return to Ravnica* Limited, just like the current PTQ season, so to get an idea of what people are doing check out the Top 8 lists from recent tournaments! And find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#gtc). Enjoy!
