The Pro Tour Boston invitation list comprises the following: 

*   Any team of three players with a combination of at least 50 pro points as of the most recent pro standing preceding the Pro Tour event (Worlds 2002). Teams formed in this manner must be announced at player registration before the tournament.
*   The Top 4 finishers (who were previously uninvited) of each Grand Prix tournament that feeds the appropriate Pro Tour event (see list below).
*   Teams (who were previously uninvited) who earn invitations through appropriate Qualifier tournaments (see list below).
*   Any team with a DCI three-person Limited rating of 1750 or more as of the August 14 Ratings-run.

A team is considered to already possess an invitation if the players on the team participating at a team-format Grand Prix or Qualifier event have a total of 50 or more pro points toward the pro standing immediately preceding the Pro Tour event that the Grand Prix or Qualifier tournament feeds. Teams may have also earned invitations through previous Grand Prix or Qualifier tournaments.

Check the status of a tournament not listed with the DCI at [www.thedci.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20020910000000/https://www.wizards.com/dci/)

Brisbane has no final rounds. The organizer is working to correct.

The following event is Delinquent: Brasilia

The Last-Chance Qualifier is the only remaining QT.

Ratings invites require a Team Limited Rating of 1750 or more as of the August 14th ratings publish. Teams and their rating are listed below. For appeals please review the policy at [www.thedci.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20020910000000/https://www.wizards.com/dci/) and complete the online form therein. Be sure to include the team name all required information. For inquiries on appeals email john.grant@wizards.com

Please update your contact information at [http://webapp.wizards.com/dcimember](https://web.archive.org/web/20020910000000/http://webapp.wizards.com/dcimember)
