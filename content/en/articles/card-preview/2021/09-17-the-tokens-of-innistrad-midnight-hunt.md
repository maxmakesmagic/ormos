Shield your candle flame and huddle close to gaze upon the tokens of [*Innistrad: Midnight Hunt*](https://web.archive.org/web/20210917000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/midnight-hunt)! The set and [its associated Commander decks](/en/articles/feature/2021/09-13-innistrad-midnight-hunt-commander-decklists) contain a combined 27 full-art tokens, two emblems, and one double-sided Day // Night token. In Draft and Set Boosters, you can find two emblems, sixteen full-art tokens, and the double-sided Day // Night token (exclusive to Draft and Set Boosters). In Collector Boosters, you can find many of these as traditional foil double-sided tokens.

Commander decks each come with ten double-sided tokens with a combined total of fourteen double-sided variations. Eleven full-art tokens are unique to *Innistrad: Midnight Hunt* Commander products.

If you haven't yet seen the cards that make these tokens, go check out the [Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2021/09-10-innistrad-midnight-hunt) and [Variant Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2021/09-14-innistrad-midnight-hunt-variants)—plus the [*Innistrad: Midnight Hunt* Commander Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2021/09-14-innistrad-midnight-hunt-commander).
