**What Common is the Lynchpin of your Deck?** 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmon14/Duke.jpg)

***(2) Reid Duke** : [Wingsteed Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingsteed+Rider). It's one of the fastest ways to win a game in this format.* 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmon14/Costa.jpg)

***Matt Costa**: [Gods Willing](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gods+Willing). A powerful heroic enabler and a cheap answer to removal, as well as giving my big green creatures a way to push through late.* 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmon14/qq_hayne.jpg)

***(11) Alexander Hayne** : [Bronze Sable](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bronze+Sable). I'm playing two of them because I need to hold the fort early. Twice I've traded it off for Agent of Horizons.* 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmon14/Vidugiris.jpg)

***(23) Gaudenis Vidugiris** : Probably just [Wingsteed Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingsteed+Rider). It's the best common, and it's in my deck, so it's the best common in my deck.* 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmon14/UtterLeyton.jpg)

***(8) Josh Utter-Leyton**: [Sudden Storm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sudden+Storm). Though I'd happily trade it for a Griptide or Voyage's End. It gives me a way to deal with big creatures.* 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmon14/cueno.jpg)

***Andrew Cuneo**: Probably just [Wingsteed Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140315000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingsteed+Rider). I bet a lot of people have said that.*
