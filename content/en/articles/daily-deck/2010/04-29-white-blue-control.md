Popular throughout **Magic's** history, White-Blue Control has popped up most recently on **Magic Online** in the *Zendikar* Block Constructed format. One thing sure to change this particular version is the release of *Rise of the Eldrazi.* So, what cards do you think will change the archetype from the new set? Take a look through the [searchable spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20100704071326/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/riseoftheeldrazi/spoiler) to decide for yourself.
