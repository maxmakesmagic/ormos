Perhaps the most famous Mirrans are the Myr, the lowly but ubiquitous artifact tribe. But did you know that a combo deck exists that seeks to make as many 1/1 Myr tokens as possible, then kills the opponent by turning them into mana and using the mana to activate Goblin Cannon? It's true, and this is one version of the deck created by former Wizards of the Coast R&D member and Pro Tour player Nate Heiss.
