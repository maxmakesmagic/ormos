On the shard of Naya, monsters of mythic proportions rule the land. These gargantuans are so revered they're treated almost as gods; Philadelphian Jordan Berkowitz is also no stranger to respecting Beasts. He used a plethora of the over-sized creatures to earn himself a Top 8 at Pro Tour-Venice in 2003. The deck was nearly entirely lands and Beasts save for four copies of Shock. To learn more about the Beastly inhabitants of Naya, check out the *Shards of Alara* [product page](https://web.archive.org/web/20090520022911/http://wizards.com/magic/tcg/products.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/products/shardsofalara).
