There are two types of *Magic* players—okay, that's not quite true. There are a ton of different types of *Magic* players. We're a diverse group.

But there are two camps when it comes to basic lands, at least. Those who love basic lands for their art, and those who love basic lands because they let them cast spells.

This is for the former group.

*Kaladesh* basic lands are secretly (well, not anymore) my favorite basic lands to come out in a long time. In fact, I will take all of the foil versions of this Johannes Voss Island I can get my hands on.

![Island](http://media.wizards.com/2016/images/daily/c4rd4r7_PWlqwyNQUI.jpg)
