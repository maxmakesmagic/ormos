# Deck of the Day

1.  [Mono-Red Eldrazi](http://www.mtgmintcard.com/articles/writers/lee-shi-tian/4-new-challengers-in-standard)

Is it fair to call this the new kid on the block when the Eldrazi have been around for nearly two years? However you look at it, this new take on Big Red is putting up results in Standard and looks to get even better with the inclusion of Ramunap Ruins, [as Lee Shi Tian points out](http://www.mtgmintcard.com/articles/writers/lee-shi-tian/4-new-challengers-in-standard). Not only is the land an extra piece of burn, but in a red and colorless deck, it's effectively a dual land, as are all the single-color Deserts. Who knew the Eldrazi were such desert-dwellers?
