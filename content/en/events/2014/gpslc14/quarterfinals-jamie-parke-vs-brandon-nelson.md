Jamie Parke is a bit of an enigma within competitive *Magic*. Returned and redoubled to the game in recent times, he found success with Top 8 appearances at both Grand Prix Richmond and Pro Tour *Journey into Nyx*, Parke was on the rise again after his hiatus. He even celebrated in a song he shared with the world. 

Brandon Nelson had been on the cusp for a couple years. With plenty of heartbreak ninth-place finishes at recent events, adding a second Grand Prix Top 8 to his first from Kansas City was another notch in the best of a rising star. Surrounded by friends watching, Nelson looked the part of someone ready to win his first Grand Prix.

**The Decks** 

Nelson's deck was a thing of *Magic* 2015 drafting beauty. With [Resolute Archangel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Resolute+Archangel) to climb back from even the most precarious of life totals, [Spectra Ward](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectra+Ward) with Heliod's Pilgrims to threaten a nigh invulnerable creature, and copies of [Triplicate Spirits](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triplicate+Spirits) and [Sanctified Charge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sanctified+Charge) for good measure, Nelson's plan was powerful. Layering red on top of it, including cards like [Inferno Fist](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inferno+Fist) and [Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames) gave Nelson multiple ways to close out game quickly. 

Parke, too, was on a similar strategy. With copies of [Triplicate Spirits](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triplicate+Spirits), [Sanctified Charge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sanctified+Charge), and [Stoke the Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoke+the+Flames) of his own, Parke wanted to end the game just as fast a Nelson. Instead of white creatures to lay down a base of attack, Parke leaned heavily on red creatures like [Torch Fiend](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torch+Fiend) and Krenko's Enforce to get the average attack done. 

**The Games** 

Nelson began with the one-two combo of [Soulmender](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soulmender) into Ajani's Pridemate, threatening to make a 4/4 on the attack the next turn. He did so, and began delivering the beats. Parke was undaunted, playing one of the best white spells in the format: [Triplicate Spirits](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triplicate+Spirits). Using it to cast [Devouring Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devouring+Light) on the massive Pridemate, Nelson responded in kind by calling on his own trio of flying Spirits. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpslc14/parke_qf.jpg)

  

The battlefield was suddenly a little crowded.

Heliod's Pilgrim helped pull Nelson ahead in the game, putting [Spectra Ward](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectra+Ward) into his hand with a [Resolute Archangel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Resolute+Archangel) in waiting. [Spectra Ward](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectra+Ward) resolved next turn, and Nelson began to take 6 life chunks out of Parke's total using the oversized [Borderland Marauder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Borderland+Marauder). 

It wasn't long before that more than Parke could handle.

**Parke 0 – Nelson 1** 

In the second game Parke led strong with evasive creatures – Krenko's Enforcer and [Sungrace Pegasus](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sungrace+Pegasus) – but Nelson was ready to block with Crowd's Favor off [Goblin Roughrider](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Roughrider). [Brawl](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brawl)er's Plate and [Scrapyard Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scrapyard+Mongrel) replaced Parke's lost Enforcer, though Nelson was content to trade more away to keep Parke's battlefield diminished. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpslc14/nelson_qf.jpg)

  

[Hoarding Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hoarding+Dragon) was Parke's lone bomb, searching out a [Will-Forged Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Will-Forged+Golem) to lay in wait. Having traded away most of his creatures, it was an air force leader that proved more than Nelson could handle. 

**Parke 1 – Nelson 1** 

Nelson moved quickly in the third game, Using Ajani's Pridemate and Heliod's Pilgrim to find and enchant [Divine Favor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divine+Favor), giving him reasonable attacks against Parke's [Preeminent Captain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preeminent+Captain). While Parke had opportunities to attack, he didn't have a supply of soldiers to slip onto the battlefield. Nelson raced ahead, 21 life to Parke's 8, though the opposite side of the battlefield was quickly filled up with Krenko's Enforcer, [Torch Fiend](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torch+Fiend), and [Razorfoot Griffin](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Razorfoot+Griffin) as well. 

[Spectra Ward](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spectra+Ward) took away Parke's ability to block, and Nelson dropped the storied pro down to just 4 life. Parke didn't have an answer on the next turn. 

**Brandon Nelson defeated Jamie Parke, 2-1.**
