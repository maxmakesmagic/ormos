In fact, Top 8 competitor Frank Karsten even built his deck using some complicated math and information he received from Decks of the Week! Read about [how he did it](/en/events/2008/worlds08/2008-worlds-blog-day-2#3).

Of course, it's not a Decks of the Week without the usual suspects. We've got more PTQ-Kyoto Top 8 lists (and of course, there are a few more PTQs left, which you can find out about [here](/en/events/2008/kyoto08-qualifiers/pro-tourkyoto-2009-qualifiers). And as always, the Top 8s from this past weeks **Magic Online** premier events. So read up on the 2008 Worlds Coverage, then find out what all the latest tech is about!
