**Ben Thompson**, a graduate of the Laguna College of Art and Design, first began working for Wizards of the Coast while still a senior in college. He has illustrated many cards for **Magic: The Gathering** beginning with the *Mercadian Masques* expansion. Following graduation, Ben was a professor at both California State University Long Beach and Mt. San Antonio Community College in the Illustration Departments. In the summer of 2004, Ben left both schools after being invited to join the staff of *Sigil Games Online* as a concept artist.

While a complete list of artistic influences would require more space than can be found here, some of the most important include Norman Rockwell, John Jude Palencar, N.C. Wyeth, Brom, J.C. Leyendecker, Jim Dietz. Other art credits include the *Harry Potter Trading Card Game*, Numerous interiors for the **Dungeons and Dragons** role-playing game, and cover art for both **Dungeons and Dragons**, and *Legend of the Five Rings*.

Ben currently lives and works in Laguna Niguel California. Where he finds any spare moment for a good book, and a warm pipe.
