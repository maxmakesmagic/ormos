## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ac/18/ac1814a2b59c331a1c44b55a2cdbb292b1c2595c0065cf2a6b43f3d5af2c9152.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a special Top Deck magazine pullout with pictures of all cards in the set, six 15-card booster packs, two premium cards, and the *Prophecy* novel.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ac/18/ac1814a2b59c331a1c44b55a2cdbb292b1c2595c0065cf2a6b43f3d5af2c9152.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare card. Or you might get lucky and find a premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ac/18/ac1814a2b59c331a1c44b55a2cdbb292b1c2595c0065cf2a6b43f3d5af2c9152.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/82/b2/82b2e24d304fa73d335f33126093cdf4775c14be5d229d6a53d0b00cfd79df78.png)This 143-card set includes randomly inserted foil premium cards and features new mechanics like Wind spells, giant Avatars, and Rhystic spells.

**Set Name:** *Prophecy*  
**Block:** Set 3 of 3 of the *Masques* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 143

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, Chinese Traditional

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719050318/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/expert/prophecy/pr_faq.asp) 

**Design Team:** William Jockusch (lead) with contributions from Bill Rose and Mark Rosewater

**Development Team:** Charlie Hasbrino (lead), Mike Elliott, William Jockusch, Paul Peterson, Henry Stern, Teeuwynn Woodruff with contributions from Beth Moursund
