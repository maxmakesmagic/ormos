Monday, August 29 9:00 am PST, *Ninth Edition* goes on sale in the [Magic Online store](https://web.archive.org/web/20050819000000/https://magiconlinestore.wizards.com/).

NOTE: *Ninth Edition* becomes legal in ALL Constructed formats the day it goes on sale (August 29). All casual rooms and constructed queues will use *Ninth Edition* starting on August 29. Constructed Premier events will not run until September 6 and will use *Ninth Edition* starting on that date.

*Ninth Edition* Release events begin Thursday, September 1 and will have 4 components:

Release Leagues  
 Draft Queues  
 Premier Event Flights (2x and 4x Prizes)  
 Release Championship

***Ninth Edition* Release Leagues**

SIGNUP: September 1, 9:00am (or immediately following the scheduled downtime, whichever is later) – September 6, 9:00am (the final 1 week, 2x league will start no later than 9AM on September 6). Release Leagues will have a maximum of 256 players each and will auto-spawn (as soon as a league reaches 256 players, another will begin registration).

LEAGUE DURATION: 1 week

PRODUCT: 5 *Ninth Edition* Booster Packs.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 2x prizes, Participation Avatar: Hell's Caretaker; Records of 5-0 get Prize Avatar: Maro. 

Avatars will be distributed Approx. 3 weeks after the last league concludes

***Ninth Edition* Draft Queues**

QUEUES BEGIN: September 1, 11:00 am (or 2 hours after the scheduled downtime, whichever is later).

FORMATS: Booster Draft

PRODUCT: 3 9E Boosters

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 4-3-2-2

***Ninth Edition* Premier Events (2x Flights)**

DURATION: September 1 3:00 pm (or 6 hours after the scheduled downtime, whichever is later) – September 6 9:00 am (last starts at 9am). 2x Flights will have 32 players per flight and will start every hour, on the hour. Each flight will have a minimum of 24 players, maximum of 32 players.

PRODUCT: 5 9E booster packs, top 8 booster draft. The booster draft format will be 3 9E boosters.

ADMISSION: 2 Tix, Product

PRIZE SUPPORT: 2x Prizes, Top 8

The winner of the Championship will also receive the framed, original artwork\* for the *Ninth Edition* card Will-O'-The-Wisp by artist Rob Alexander.

Places 2 through 6 will receive his or her choice (in place order) from 5 Limited Edition Canvas Prints depicting Ninth Edition Artwork\*. The prints are reproductions of original magic art. Each piece is signed by the artist, framed, and numbered; and each comes with a certificate of authenticity.

Places 7 through 11 will receive his or her choice (in place order) from 5 Limited Edition matted lithographs depicting Ninth Edition Artwork\*. The lithographs are reproductions of original magic art. Each piece is signed by the artist.

Although players may have multiple accounts qualified for the Championship event, players not permitted to have more than 1 of their accounts entered in the event. Players are not permitted to have secondary accounts played by another person. If a player is discovered to have more than 1 account playing in the Championship either before, during, or after the event, appropriate penalties will be applied (including disqualification from the event with no refund of product or event tickets).

### Important information regarding system crashes and refunds for Premier Events

If during your event the system crashes, 1 of 3 things will happen:

1.  The system will come right back up. In this case:

    *   Events will continue and additional time will be given to allow players to finish the event
    *   No Compensation will be given

2.  The system cannot recover the event and "finishes" (prematurely ends) the event, or a WOTC Tournament Coordinator must "finish" (prematurely end) the event.

    *   We will accept the prize payouts "as is" - This means that whatever prize payouts and invites the system awards, we will not change what the system does.
    *   No changes will be made to these events from what the system automatically pays out as prizes or invitations

    If the event is in TOP 8:

    *   No product or tickets will be refunded
    *   All players keep the product used to enter the event as well as the drafted cards from the TOP 8 draft.

    If the event is still in Swiss Rounds:

    *   Players can [request a refund through Customer Service](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=161).
    *   We will refund your Product and tickets.

3.  The event is cleared or cancelled by the system. If an event is cancelled this means the event can no longer be viewed in the premier event listings.

    *   The system will automatically refund your tickets.
    *   Players can [request a refund through Customer Service](http://wizards.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/wizards.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=161).
    *   We will refund your Product.
