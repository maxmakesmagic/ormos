### ![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/features/feature43c_dotpLogo.jpg)Xbox LIVE® Arcade – Game with Developers – October 24, 2009

Team members from Stainless Games, Ltd., Wizards of the Coast, Microsoft, and other **Magic** developers behind [**Magic: The Gathering Duels of the Planeswalkers**](https://web.archive.org/web/20091021000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Digital/DuelsOfThePlaneswalkers.aspx) Expansion Pack 1 will play Xbox LIVE® Gold Members on Saturday, October 24.

Join us [Saturday, October 24 from 6-9 p.m. ET](http://www.xbox.com/en-US/community/calendar/) for a classic gaming session. Plus, this is your chance to get the XBLA **Spellslinger Achievement** directly from the source by defeating **Duels of the Planeswalkers** developers!
