## Best-of-Three Qualifier Play-In and Qualifier Weekend

[![](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/nud8ivastetr.jpg)](https://www.magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) 

This is a big week with the release of the new set, and this weekend is also big for competitive *Magic*: it's the monthly back-to-back events of the Best-of-Three Qualifier Play-In and the Qualifier Weekend. This one is sure to be exciting, because the just-released cards from *The Lost Caverns of Ixalan* will be in the mix!

This weekend is one of the quickest ways to get into competitive *Magic* play. Start with the [Best-of-Three Qualifier Play-In](https://www.magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) this Friday, November 17, where you'll play in the Historic format to secure your invitation to the Qualifier Weekend.

Then, move right into that [two-day Qualifier Weekend event](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) happening Saturday through Sunday, where you'll face top players in the Historic format, all competing to earn invitations to the Arena Championship and the Pro Tour!

### QUALIFIER EVENTS

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events.

Qualifier tokens are delivered to your *MTG Arena* inbox. Remember to claim them before the event starts!

*Please note: Daylight saving time ends in the U.S. on March 12. UTC times will shift to UTC-08:00 on that date and continue until March 10, 2024.)*
