*We've done this before.*

In 2006, *Time Spiral* was unleashed into the world. It heralded our return to the plane of Dominaria after a then-long hiatus of *three years* since the release of *Scourge*—the last story to take place there. The face of *Magic* cards had changed—updated to the "modern" frame—and we were met with a dystopian future where the world was slowly dying and things got *complicated*.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&multiverseid=108899)](https://web.archive.org/web/20210225000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=108899)

The story of *Time Spiral* block includes many characters, but the most important was [the return—and planeswalking sacrifice—of Teferi](https://web.archive.org/web/20210225000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/planeswalkers/teferi) that played out through the namesake release and its additions: *Planar Chaos* and *Future Sight*. Packed with flavorful callbacks to *Magic*'s earliest days alongside dozens of mechanics, it created a rich, complex Draft format.

*Time Spiral* block would be the last visit to Dominaria until, well, *Dominaria*'s release ten years later. (Things got better.)

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&multiverseid=122080)](https://web.archive.org/web/20210225000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=122080)

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&multiverseid=443151)](https://web.archive.org/web/20210225000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=443151)

It's easy for me to wax philosophic about *Time Spiral* because I lived through the experience. In wonder, I opened cards that looked old but were being printed anew—even if they weren't the splashiest of options.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Squire)](https://web.archive.org/web/20210225000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squire)

I fell in love with the reimagined color pie—a rebalancing of the mechanics and abilities of colors—that played with my *Magic* intuition and appeared only in *Planar Chaos*.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Mycologist)](https://web.archive.org/web/20210225000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mycologist)

I was in awe of the "future frame" that came with some of the cards in *Future Sight*, showing off full-art cards in basic Draft commons.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Blade+of+the+Sixth+Pride)](https://web.archive.org/web/20210225000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blade+of+the+Sixth+Pride)

The era was defined by complicated interactions, interesting combos, and payoffs of some of *Magic*'s wildest competitive moments. But, like all other sets, it was an ephemeral moment.

*Time Spiral Remastered* follows in the footsteps of digital "remastered" sets to bring older gameplay to a modern aesthetic and match it with an experience that's as refined as it is nostalgic.

![TSR Aven Riftwatcher](https://media.wizards.com/2021/tsr/en_v3iv77kces.png)![TSR Pallid Mycoderm](https://media.wizards.com/2021/tsr/en_XHo4U2NUD9.png)![TSR Mycologist](https://media.wizards.com/2021/tsr/en_vUPeODEsDe.png)

Each card in *Time Spiral Remastered* comes from the three sets in the *Time Spiral* block, selected to capture what it was like drafting in that era.

*   [Thallid](https://web.archive.org/web/20210225000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=108825)s! If you can wait three turns you can get *something* good, I think! (You need "time" to make it work. *Get it?!*)
*   Sacrifice outlets! What good is a thallid growing saprolings without some way to munch them up and get something else out of them? (Dominarian historians can explain the dangers of a [Thallid](https://web.archive.org/web/20210225000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=108825)-based economy.)
*   More life gain! [Aven Riftwatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20210225000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=108899) is somewhat efficient, and will get a hit in for you, but it's destined to *vanish* as a win condition. \[Editor's Note: Insert a "time is limited/Limited" joke here.\]

You'll have to wait to see other familiar faces for folks who enjoy taking time to find the win, but *Time Spiral Remastered* also brings one more nod to the past with it.

![WAR Ajani's Pridemate](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?multiverseid=460931&type=card)![TSR Ajani's Pridemate](https://media.wizards.com/2021/tsr/en_Mn79o6nkKM.png)

Every booster pack contains a card from a bonus sheet, select cards originally printed with the modern *Magic* frame now returning with what we're calling the retro frame treatment. Built beginning from the original frame files for *Magic*, these cards flip *Time Spiral*'s "the past returns" convention on its head—and delight players like me who love the throwback *Magic* look.

Gaining life quickly suddenly looks a lot better with [Ajani's Pridemate](https://web.archive.org/web/20210225000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ajani%27s+Pridemate) around. You can be sure that just as *Time Spiral*'s bonus sheet added excitement for the unexpected in drafts, the retro frame cards of *Time Spiral Remastered* bring a new dimension to its battles with familiar faces sliding into the past.
