### 

What was once a minor player in the Extended metagame became an eternal fixture when Bob Maher won Pro Tour-Chicago with his Oath of Druids deck. A powerful utility deck that practically shuts down opposing creature assaults with a single two casting cost enchantment, Oath is sure to be a contender in Extended for a long time to come, despite the changes in Extended. Let's take a look at the classic Oath deck and see how its legacy began. 

4 Tundra  
 4 Tropical Island  
 4 Volcanic Island  
 1 Adarkar Wastes  
 1 Savannah  
 4 Flood Plain

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6a/7b/6a7b1a0a7e40ee409e92ea0787c2857fdb8fdc3ccf7bbcd9ef20e2e371f71ca9.jpg)One of Oath's few weaknesses has always been its precarious mana base, solidly four colors and usually composed of nonbasic lands. Flood Plain helped to smooth out the colors, as well as providing powerful interactions with Brainstorm.

2 Treetop Village  
 4 Wasteland

The deck's utility lands are just as important as its mana base, as Treetop Village allows the deck to present an offense even with Oath of Druids in play, while Wasteland can deal with opposing man-lands, as well as cut off key sources of mana.

1 Morphling  
 1 Spike Weaver  
 1 Spike Feeder

![](https://i.ormos.uk/40/b3/40b3cca43c57dd01cef837a9cc894192b2f689c19cc87f29db4bcd19292d570c.jpg)This was the standard Oath package of creatures. Morphling needs little explanation, as it's perhaps the closest to an unstoppable creature **Magic** has ever seen. Spike Weaver served to shut down the opponent's attack phase, and Spike Feeder fended off burn spells by keeping the Oath player's life total high. All three creatures can be sacrificed at a moment's notice to keep Oath of Druids working properly. 

4 Brainstorm  
 4 Impulse

These two blue instants are far more powerful than first glance might have you believe. Both help improve the deck's consistency, smoothing out the mana and getting Oath of Druids into play more quickly, but Brainstorm in particular also had powerful interactions with Flood Plain and Oath of Druids, allowing the Oath player to put less-useful cards back into his deck and shuffle them away to improve the quality of his hand. 

4 Counterspell  
 4 Force of Will  
 1 Forbid

![](https://i.ormos.uk/02/af/02af3898eaf3df8950fe7d34bee36b0488330045a33c1f2c7bccffbd394acb4d.jpg)Countermagic, plain and simple. Force of Will was particularly powerful in this deck due to its ability to protect an early Oath of Druids from opposing counters or enchantment destruction.

4 Oath of Druids  
 2 Gaea's Blessing

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8d/96/8d96b3bfdf6b0eb6f3ecdf3d7da73b059182c0663c82595a8bb82668ff3af9ef.jpg)The combination around which the deck was based, Oath of Druids and Gaea's Blessing allow for an infinite creature loop. The Oath can single-handedly shut down opposing creature strategies. Gaea's Blessing not only allowed for Oath recursion, but was also a powerful tool in the control game, allowing for important cards to be recycled back into the deck.

4 Swords to Plowshares  
 3 Powder Keg  
 3 Fact of Fiction

Utility at its best. Swords to Plowshares was the most efficient creature removal spell in the game, Fact or Fiction arguably one of the most powerful card drawers and Powder Keg a sweeper for creature rushes or just about whatever else might manage to slip past the deck's countermagic.

### The sideboard:

3 Pyroblast

All-purpose anti-blue hate. Don't leave home without it.

1 Powder Keg  
 2 Aura Blast

For further utility, often in card-advantage-providing form.

3 Hydroblast  
 2 Samite Ministration

Stop Price of Progress Oath of Druids does not. These two cards shore up the holes against decks that can just ignore their creature plan and burn you to a crisp.

4 Call of the Herd

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f6/d0/f6d0e7f3bd2dde245daa5930ce7b41061d8ece55498f7a59c63c51562895ff8f.jpg)When your Oaths aren't getting you anywhere, or you just need to put your opponent on a clock, it's time to call in the Elephants. Also useful against Sligh decks for drawing burn away from your life total, not to mention eating Jackal Pups for lunch.

### Variations:

1\. If your local environment has extremely heavy nonbasic land hate, consider building the deck in just blue and green, cutting Swords to Plowshares and shaving some other numbers to make room for Accumulated Knowledge and Intuitions, which not only make your Accumulated Knowledges more powerful, but allow you to effectively tutor for Oaths or Powder Kegs, as well as providing great synergy with Call of the Herd.

2\. If you prefer a deck more similar to Bob Maher's Tutor-Oath deck, cut two copies of Oath of Druids, two copies of Powder Keg, and the Fact or Fictions, and add four Enlightened Tutor and your favorite enchantments and artifacts, from Sylvan Library and Abundance to Ivory Mask and Null Rod.
