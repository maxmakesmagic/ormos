We announced at the the launch of *Theros* a forthcoming *Theros* Limited Championship and a *Theros* Standard Constructed Championship, happening before *Born of the Gods* is released. Now that we're at the end of 2013, and *Born of the Gods* is on the horizon, the *Theros* Championships are imminent! Both of these championships have dedicated qualifier events during the week leading up to them, and both award more prizes to the top finishers and players overall than in the past. So, during the holiday break, we wanted to post a *Theros* refresher to make sure everyone is in the know! 

In this article: 

*   [*Theros* Championship Details](#details)
*   [Limited Championship Events](#limitedqualifiers)
*   [Constructed Championship Events](#constructedqualifiers)

 

*Theros* Championships Details

Now that you've had time to learn the new *Theros* cards, try out synergies, and refine your favorite strategies, you can put all of that knowledge to the test during the championship events.

Prize awards have expanded so more players can win booster packs. We've pushed the threshold for those who win packs from 64th place all the way down to 128th place, awarding a total of 1,000 booster packs in each championship event. Top 8 finishers will receive booster packs in addition to complete set prizes. Plus, Top 8 finishers in the championship events will now receive enough Qualifier Points to enter **Magic Online** Championship Series preliminary events.

After the Theros Championship concludes, look for a new web page dedicated to the winners and their decks on MTGO.com!
