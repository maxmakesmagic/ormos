We've got something interesting to show you today, if you're in the United States. If you're not, go ahead and skip this Arcana. Go read [Savor the Flavor](/en/articles/savortheflavor/2011/10-26-deathtrap) instead. It's good!

Okay. Monday, as you may know, is Halloween. And this Friday is a special Halloween-themed edition of [Friday Night **Magic**](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6)! While retailer participation may vary, there ought to be cool decorations and special prizes. In particular, we'd like to highlight this item:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/77/3e/773ea89bd7ef1fc8121f3c24e1ed0e88a48cc8e12a664a2540e9b98d1e7bfd0e.jpg) 

That's a bag of official Friday Night **Magic** M&Ms. And they come in **Magic** colors!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/44/72/447257fe2ec9a6c636238b62205490d4246ff06d31520fd4b0dc171bfc58cff3.jpg)
