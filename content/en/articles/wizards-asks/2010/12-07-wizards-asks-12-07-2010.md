*What's your favorite [Johnny](/en/articles/makingmagic/2009/08-03-designing-for-johnny) card?*

![](https://i.ormos.uk/98/ff/98ff84c36158a8091193fe55e8d3028e6e766bb343fd8e5051e08a78fcb62548.jpg)

**Zac Hill:** My favorite Johnny card is probably ... [Rite of Replication](https://web.archive.org/web/20101207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rite+of+Replication)?

![Rite of Replication](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Rite+of+Replication)

 

I love seeing the screen shots and hearing the crazy stories about the stuff you can do with WAAAAAAAY more copies of a guy than you're supposed to have. My favorite target? Well, besides an [Opalescence](https://web.archive.org/web/20101207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Opalescence) [Doubling Season](https://web.archive.org/web/20101207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doubling+Season), I've got to go with good ol' [Precursor Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20101207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Precursor+Golem).

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/activity/da597_golem.jpg)
