Looking for a way to keep warm this winter? Maverick Kimman combined the best burn red has to offer with the most powerful white cards to succeed at the 2009 **Magic** Game Day. Using a control deck featuring nearly zero creatures, the power of Ajani Vengeant; Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle; and Chandra Nalaar helped propel the Minnesota player to a successful Game Day weekend.
