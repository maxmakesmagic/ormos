After reviewing the geographic breakdown and timing of next year's spring and summer Grand Prix, Wizards of the Coast is changing the format of Grand Prix Houston to *Return to Ravnica* Block Sealed Deck and Booster Draft. 

This change is being made to ensure there is an individual Limited Grand Prix in North America that features all three sets from the **Return to Ravnica** block. Grand Prix Houston is scheduled for June 15-16, 2013. Grand Prix in Beijing (May 11-12), Gothenburg (June 8-9), and Bangkok (June 22-23) will also feature the full block with Sealed Deck and Booster Draft formats, and Grand Prix Providence (June 8-9) will feature all three sets in the Team Limited format. 

Here is the updated Grand Prix schedule for 2013. The rest of the 2013 schedule and formats will be announced in December.
