The release of ***Magic** 2013* last Friday means it is now legal in all formats and presages the return of [Pro Tour *Return to Ravnica*](/en/events/2012/seattle12-qualifiers/qualifiers-for-pro-tour-return-to-ravnica-in-seattle) Qualifiers and Grand Prix to the realms of paper **Magic** this weekend. In the meantime, **Magic Online** held another [PTQ this past weekend](https://web.archive.org/web/20130520093755/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Digital/MagicOnlineTourn.aspx?x=mtg/digital/magiconline/tourn/4081279), and the [**Magic Online** ***Magic** 2013* Prerelease](/en/articles/feature/2012/07-10-magic-online-magic-2013-prerelease-events) starts up on July 26—less than a week away! You can find the Top 8 decks for the **Magic Online** PTQ below and can find out about future events with [this handy PTQ list](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list). Good luck!
