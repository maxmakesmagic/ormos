## PRO TOUR BATTLE FOR ZENDIKAR

SUNDAY COVERAGE

Japan is back!

Coming into this weekend, Kazuyuki Takimura had told his fiancé that he would one day Top 8 a Pro Tour. As he is getting married at the end of this year, bringing home a win—and the $40,000 and benefits that come with a victory—would be very welcome.

After two rounds of play on Sunday, Takimura found himself in the finals of Pro Tour *Battle for Zendikar* as he faced off against Ryohichi Tamada, a 2015 World *Magic* Cup representative for Team Japan. Tamada had been waiting for his big breakthrough on the Pro Tour, with his play renowned by some of Japan 's top players. The two ensured that Japan would get its first Pro Tour champion in nearly four years in an all-Japan finals match: Something that has not happened since the 2006 World Championships between Makihito Mihara and Ryo Ogura.

The best-of-five finals came down to one last game, where Takimura's Abzan deck was able to overpower the last real threat that Tamada was able to offer up: Gideon, Ally of Zendikar. When Takimura dispatched the Planeswalker, his path to victory was clear, and in just a few turns, Takimura had accomplished far more than he imagined.

Pro Tour *Battle for Zendikar* has been the defining platform for new Planeswalkers. While Jace, Vryn's Prodigy made his big debut, it was Gideon, Ally of Zendikar that fueled numerous Standard Constructed decks this weekend. Takimura made excellent use of Gideon and many of his Abzan deck's key cards to win match after match, until he was the only one left. His win has earned him plenty of prize money, Platinum status in the Pro Tour Players Club, and an invitation to the 2016 *Magic: The Gathering* World Championship.

SATURDAY COVERAGE

Pro Tour *Battle for Zendikar*’s Top 8 is set with a historic field. Hall of Famers Jon Finkel and Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa achieved career milestones this weekend. Damo da Rosa earned his tenth career Pro Tour Top 8, joining an illustrious elite of only two other players who have achieved the same feat, including fellow Top 8 competitor Jon Finkel who has continued to cement himself as *Magic*’s very best with his *fifteenth* career Top 8.

Joining these two is a diverse field of amazing players. Owen Turtenwald leads the way with his third career Pro Tour Top 8, followed by Ricky Chin from Canada, a newcomer competing in his first Pro Tour. Denmark’s Martin Müller has followed up a stellar season as one of the game’s youngest prodigies with his first Pro Tour Top 8. Joining these players are Kazuyuki Takimura and Ryoichi Tamada from Japan, the latter who will be representing Japan in this year’s World *Magic* Cup, along with Paul Dean from Canada, who earned his way onto the Sunday stage from a *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifier invitation.

With a field ranging from the brand new to the game’s most decorated pro player of all time, who will claim the coveted title of Pro Tour champion? Tune in tomorrow to find out!
