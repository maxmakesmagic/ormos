![Gold Myr art by Kev Walker](https://i.ormos.uk/7b/6f/7b6f6a019cf12f5dc72f95b448664d1146884af449fc58cc6de2b0b931b03201.jpg)

You know that the five [mana myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20041214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/default.asp?term=_myr&fields=%7Cname&format=Mirrodin&color=All&output=summary&sort=power&first=4) from *Mirrodin* all have a distinctive artifact-race look. 

You may not know that they were recently immortalized in three dimensions by 3-D artist [Henry Higgenbotham](https://web.archive.org/web/20041214000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/default.asp?term=Henry_G._Higgenbotham&fields=%7Cartist&format=Allsets&color=All&output=summary&sort=name&first=1) (visit his site at [www.henryh.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20041214000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.henryh.com&origin=mtgcom_arcana_712)). Here are some photos of the statues he created and showed off at [2004's World Championships](/en/events/2004/worlds04/nuijten-nassif-dominate-worlds) in San Francisco.
