*Battle for Zendikar* previews are nearly over (the full set will be revealed this Friday!), which means you're starting to get a sense of the set, the art, the story, and just how the battle for Zendikar will shake out.

It also means I get to show you some tokens today! And by some, I mean all of the tokens.

There are eleven tokens with *Battle for Zendikar*, plus three emblems from the planeswalkers you already know and love. Let's start there, with the cards that create them nearby for reference:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/ECytCRIuXD.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/bfz_8asdifk2Sk2cs/en_PitAVDWBFm.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/BdrcxTuuOn.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/bfz_8asdifk2Sk2cs/en_CrUglvnPJ0.png)

Speaking of Kiora, the ability that creates her emblem also happens to churn out three of these beauties:

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/V2qe0ALQPq.png)

Next up we have three different kinds of Eldrazi Scion tokens, all looking creepy as can be.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/jzkBRlkQ2X.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/pAT2ZVOJYc.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/ERZfWSN2Kh.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/kWadJiABeY.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/bfz_8asdifk2Sk2cs/en_Yuxp4WpiVB.png)

Next up we have a few white tokens. . .

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/okAysNztfA.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/My4kFweDNz.png)

And a few red ones. . .

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/FoyMVxznmy.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/mam91uW2Ld.png)

And a green one. . .

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/VIQOdH0X0n.png)

And even a red AND green one!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/es6ELrarbB.png)![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/bfz_8asdifk2Sk2cs/en_TP7oddZX7Z.png)

That's all of them! You can get your hands on these tokens—not to mention the rest of the set—when *Battle for Zendikar* releases October 2. Or you can get a head-start beginning with the Prerelease on September 26!
