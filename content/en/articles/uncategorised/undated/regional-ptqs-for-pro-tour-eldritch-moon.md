## OVERVIEW

Bring your top strategies with you to a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier, and you may be one of the few to emerge from the event with an invitation and airfare to Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*!

Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers (RPTQs) for Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon* take place May 28 – June 5, 2016.

The Top 4 finishers of a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier with 128 or fewer players receive airfare and invitations to Pro Tour Eldritch Moon, while 1st- through 8th-place finishers receive invitations to the next round of Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers. If the Regional Pro Tour Qualifier is 129 or more players, 1st- through 8th-place finishers receive airfare and invitations to Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon* as well as invitations to the next round of Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers.

**The format for this Regional Pro Tour Qualifier season is *Shadows over Innistrad* Sealed Deck.** 

**As a reminder, players are only allowed to compete in *one* Regional Pro Tour Qualifier if they are qualified to play in one.**

[TENTATIVE LIST OF QUALIFIED PLAYERS](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/regional-ptqs-for-pro-tour-eldritch-moon-rptq-player-invitation-list)

## QUALIFICATION

**Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers are an invitation-only event.** An invitation to participate in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier for Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon* is offered to the following:

*   Players who have a [Pro Tour Players Club](https://web.archive.org/web/20160609111343/http://magic.wizards.com/en/protour/playersclub/standings) Silver level (as of the date of the Regional Pro Tour Qualifier).
*   Players who won a Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifier from January 23, 2016 to April 17, 2016.
*   Players who finished 5th- through 8th-place at a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier for Pro Tour *Shadows over Innistrad* that had 128 or fewer players.
*   Players that have won a Regional Last Chance Qualifier that ties to that Regional Pro Tour Qualifier.

## ELIGIBILITY

The following players may ***not*** compete in Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers for Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*:

*   Players who have previously participated in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier that awards an invitation to Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*.
*   Players who have won a *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifier Final that awards an invitation to Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*.
*   Players who have won a *Magic Online* Championship Series (MOCS) tournament, Player of the Year Championship tournament, or Last Chance Qualifier tournament that awards invitations to Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*.
*   Players who finished in the Top 8 or finished with 39 or more match points in an individual-format Grand Prix that awards invitations to Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*.
*   Players who finished in the Top 4 or finished with 36 or more match points in a team-format Grand Prix that awards invitations to Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*.
*   Players who finished with 33 or more match points at Pro Tour *Shadows over Innistrad*.
*   Players with a Pro Tour Players Club Gold or Platinum level (as of the date of the Regional Pro Tour Qualifier).
*   Players who are members of the Pro Tour Hall of Fame.

If you have any questions as to you eligibility to play in a particular Preliminary Pro Tour Qualifier, please email your inquiry to PremierPlay@wizards.com.

Complete information about invitations to Pro Tours can be found in the [*Magic: The Gathering* Invitation Policy](https://web.archive.org/web/20160609111343/http://wpn.wizards.com/en/document/magic-premier-event-invitation-policy).

## General Information

The structure for Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers is modified Swiss-style. If the tournament has 129 or more players, the Top 8 at the end of Swiss earn invitations and airfare to Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*.

If the tournament has 128 or fewer players, then top finishing players after the Swiss rounds play in one single-elimination playoff match to determine a Top 4.

*   *Shadows over Innistrad* Sealed Deck.
*   6 *Shadows over Innistrad* booster packs per player will be used.
*   Decklists are required.
*   If the tournament has 128 or fewer players, the playoff will consist of a *Shadows over Innistrad* booster draft with 3 *Shadows over Innistrad* booster packs per player.

*   Swiss rounds will be 50 minutes in length.
*   Single-elimination playoff matches will have no time limit.
*   Number of Swiss rounds based on attendance according to the following chart:

*   Playoff will only take place if the tournament is 128 or fewer players.
*   Playoff format will be *Shadows over Innistrad* booster draft with 3 *Shadows over Innistrad* boosters per player.
*   Playoff matches will be best two of three.
*   Playoff matches will be single-elimination.
*   While there are no time limits in the playoff rounds, players are expected to play at a reasonable pace and finish their matches within 60 minutes.
*   Standings after the Swiss rounds will be used to determine final order in the standings for losing players in the quarterfinal and semifinal playoff rounds.
*   For the first game of each match in the playoff, the player who finished higher in the Swiss rounds chooses either to play first or to play second. For subsequent games in each playoff match, the usual Play/Draw rule applies (loser of the previous game decides whether to play first in the next game).
*   The players in the playoff will be seated randomly at the draft table immediately prior to drafting. The playoff bracket will be determined using this random seating.
*   The [*Magic: The Gathering* Tournament Rules](https://web.archive.org/web/20160609111343/http://wpn.wizards.com/en/document/magic-gathering-tournament-rules) and the [*Magic: The Gathering* Infraction Procedure Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20160609111343/http://wpn.wizards.com/en/document/magic-infraction-procedure-guide) are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Fact Sheet.
*   Players are responsible for bringing their own method of tracking life totals and appropriate counters or tokens.

There is no entry fee to compete in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier.

## Magic Online Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers

The round of Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers taking place between May 28 and June 4, 2016 will have two *Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers in which qualified players may participate. The first will take place on May 28 at 3 p.m. PT, and the second one will take place June 4 at 5 a.m. PT.

To register for a *Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifier, a player who is qualified to compete in that round of Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers must send an email to PremierPlay@wizards.com with the following elements:

*   The title of the email should be "Magic Online RPTQ."
*   The email should include your DCI number, Magic Online username, and the date of the Magic Online Regional Pro Tour Qualifier that you wish to participate in.

The deadlines to register are:

*   Wednesday, May 25 at 11:59 PM PT (for the May 28 tournament)
*   Wednesday, June 1 at 11:59 PM PT (for the June 4 tournament)

*Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers will be capped at 212 players, so sign up early if you intend to play.

The Top 8 players will receive invitations to compete in Pro Tour *Eldritch Moon*, as well as an invitation to play in the [Pro Tour Challenge event](https://web.archive.org/web/20160609111343/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers#challenge) at that Pro Tour. The top 8 players will also receive an invitation to the next round of Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers.

The format for these Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers is *Shadows over Innistrad* Sealed Deck.

As a reminder, if you play in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier during a qualifying round, **you cannot play in any other Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers during that qualifying round, including *Magic Online* Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers**. Your invitation to compete in a Regional Pro Tour Qualifier only lets you compete in one Regional Pro Tour Qualifier.
