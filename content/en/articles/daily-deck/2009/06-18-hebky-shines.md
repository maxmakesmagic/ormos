A relative unknown coming into Pro Tour–Honolulu, Czech player Michal Hebky proved he was more than a flash in the pan. He dominated both the Constructed and Limited portions of the Pro Tour, battling his way into the finals where he was felled by Japanese pro Kazuya Mitamura. For more about Pro Tour–Honolulu, including all the top decklists, [read the official event coverage](/en/events/2009/pthon09/mitamura-finds-gold-in-paradise).
