## *Legacy Cube* May 12–26

For two weeks, we're reviving a cube that's been dormant for almost a full year. For those of you who love the power level of *Vintage Cube* without the huge spikes that come with actual power, *Legacy Cube* is the cube for you!

Check out the full card list and most up-to-date changes [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2017/04-05-legacy-cube-cardlist).

* * *

## *Proliferate Cube* Ends Tomorrow

Emma Handy's *Proliferate Cube* ends tomorrow, May 12! One of the most popular spotlight cubes from 2020 is back with an update you don't want to miss. You can *counter* on having a good time and read all about this latest iteration, including a full card list, in [Emma's article here](/en/articles/magiconline/2021/05-04-spotlight-cube-series-proliferate-cube-returns).

* * *

## *Strixhaven* Season Alternative Play Calendar

Classes are now in session, with guest-lecture alternative play events available weekly. Each of these events begins on Wednesday at 10 a.m. Pacific time and continues for one week unless otherwise specified. *Strixhaven* season ends with the MTGO launch of *Modern Horizons 2* in early June.

*   May 26: New to the Spotlight Cube Series – As usual for new spotlight cubes, we'll have more information as we get closer.

* * *

## Modern Qualifier Friday

On Friday, May 14, there will be a Modern Qualifier with a start time of 2 p.m. PT. The event costs 40 QPs, and the winner will earn an invitation to the *Strixhaven* Set Championship event on *MTG Arena*.

Learn more about Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021#ptq).

* * *

## Two Limited Qualifiers Saturday

On Saturday, May 15, there will be two *Strixhaven* Sealed Qualifiers with starts times of 3 a.m. *(yes, early morning in the Americas)* and 2 p.m. PT. Each event costs 40 QPs, and winners will earn an invitation to the *Strixhaven* Set Championship event on *MTG Arena*.

Learn more about Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021#ptq).

* * *

## Modern Super Qualifier Sunday

On Sunday, May 16, there will be a Modern Super Qualifier with a start time of 7 a.m. PT. The entry fee is 40 MOCS Qualifier Points, 40 Event Tickets, or 400 Play Points. The top two finishers qualify for the *Strixhaven* Set Championship on *MTG Arena*.

NOTE: This weekend's events are the last chances you have to reach the *Strixhaven* Championship.

Learn more about Super Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021#sq).

* * *

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format Challenges are the first step toward qualifying for Premier Play through your favorite *Magic* formats and provide excellent prizes for those looking to test their mettle in their favorite formats. To learn more about Premier Play qualification, click [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021).

*Magic Online*'s Format Challenges run once on Saturdays and once on Sundays for each Constructed format! On weekends featuring Showcase Challenge events, the Format Challenge that starts at either 6 a.m., 8 a.m., or 10 a.m. Pacific time is pre-empted by the Showcase Challenge—Format Challenges scheduled at other times remain in place. *NOTE: Pauper and Standard Challenges are never pre-empted.*

## Next Downtime June 2

The next downtime will be on Wednesday, June 2, beginning at 9 a.m. and continuing until noon. This is for the release of *Modern Horizons 2*. All times listed are Pacific time (UTC-7).
