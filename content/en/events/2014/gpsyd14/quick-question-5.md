 **What color combination do you prefer to draft in *Magic 2015*?** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/02_QQ_OliverOks.jpg)

 ***Oliver Oks:** "B/R. I like aggro and [Forge Devil](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forge+Devil) is still underrated."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/02_QQ_JohnPaulKelly.jpg)

 ***John-Paul Kelly:** "W/B. I like how it plays, and white is a deep color."* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/02_QQ_HuangHaoShan.jpg)

 ***Huang Hao-Shan:** "R/W or B/R if I don't draft [Triplicate Spirits](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triplicate+Spirits)."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/02_QQ_JacobWilson.jpg)

 ***Jacob Wilson:** "U/R. Lots of Artifact synergy, and the type of aggressive deck I like."* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/02_QQ_MaitlandCameron.jpg)

 ***Maitland Cameron:** "B/G. I'm looking to play [Undergrowth Scavenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140823000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Undergrowth+Scavenger)s."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/02_QQ_PattyRobertson.jpg)

 ***Patty Robertson:** "G/W or U/R."*
