*Rise of the Eldrazi* is having an impact on all sorts of formats, including Legacy! Andrew Shuggart recently played this innovative deck at the StarCityGames.com Open in Atlanta. The combo centers around drawing Show and Tell or Eureka and using them to plop Emrakul, the Aeons Torn or Progenitus onto the table. From there, you can make short work of your opponent, and Andrew even managed to drop the monsters onto the table as early as the first turn. Powerful!
