We've got this new product coming out today called the [Deck Builder's Toolkit](/en/articles/feature/2010/05-17-deck-builders-toolkit). It's designed for new players who want a base of cards to start building decks from. And as part of the launch (today!) we sent copies out to a few other websites to see what they thought. Because we already know that *we* like it!

**[Trick Jarrett](http://www.mananation.com/deckbuilders-toolkit-twitter-qa/)** (ManaNation.com)

> Having received a care package from our friends at Wizards yesterday, I’ve been fielding questions about the upcoming Deckbuilder’s Toolkit via twitter today ....

[Read More](http://www.mananation.com/deckbuilders-toolkit-twitter-qa/) from ManaNation.com

**[Evan Erwin](http://www.starcitygames.com/magic/misc/19334_The_Magic_Show_188_Deckbuilders_Toolkit_Examined.html)** (The Magic Show, StarCityGames.com)

> We've got the Pro Tour competitor and multiple StarCityGames Open winner Chris Woltereck on board, and together we're going to break apart this Toolkit and see what we can build ....

[Read More](http://www.starcitygames.com/magic/misc/19334_The_Magic_Show_188_Deckbuilders_Toolkit_Examined.html) from StarCityGames.com
