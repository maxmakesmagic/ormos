What’s the best color combination in *Magic 2015* sealed?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gptai14/QQ_Nakamura.jpg)

***Shuhei Nakamura:** – White-Blue or White-Red.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gptai14/QQ_Yukuhiro.jpg)

***Ken Yukuhiro:** – Red Green. [Kird Chieftain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140726000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kird+Chieftain) is more powerful than a lot of rares.* 

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gptai14/QQ_Park.jpg)

***Jun Young Park:** – White / X. White is really good.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gptai14/QQ_Young.jpg)

***Ryan Young:** – Black-Red.* 

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gptai14/QQ_Nam.jpg)

***Nam Sung Wook:** – White-Red.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gptai14/QQ_Chew.jpg)

***Kelvin Chew :** – Red-Green.*
