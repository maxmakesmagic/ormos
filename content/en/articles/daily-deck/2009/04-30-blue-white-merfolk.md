Looking for a change of pace in Standard? Hesturk took the above Blue-White Merfolk list to battle recently in a **Magic Online** Premier Event. He finished in the Top 8, with the powerful members of the fish tribe combined with counterspells keeping many of his opponents under complete control. Sygg keeps the team protected while Merrow Reejerey keeps them beefy enough to get in for some sizable hits. Add a few Path to Exiles and Cryptic Commands and voila! Instant Standard success.
