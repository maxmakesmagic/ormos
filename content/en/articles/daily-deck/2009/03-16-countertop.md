Just one week after winning Pro Tour-Kyoto, Gabriel Nassif is back in the champion's chair! He bested 1,230 players at Grand Prix-Chicago to take the Legacy title. His deck of choice? The infamous CounterTop, which uses a combination of Sensei's Divining Top and Counterbalance to lock an opponent out of the game. But Gabe didn't just run *any* version of the deck. Check out his Highlander sideboard; all one-ofs! To read more about the event, [visit the official web coverage](/en/events/2009/gpchi09/two-victories-in-a-row-for-gabriel-nassif).
