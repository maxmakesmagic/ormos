Murakami thought he was doing fine when he managed to resolve both [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance) and [Monastery Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Mentor). The problem was, he would not live to see his next untap step.

Using [Lion's Eye Diamond](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lion%27s+Eye+Diamond) to check the top card of Murakami's deck, Thiele decided to go for the combo when [Monastery Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Mentor) was revealed. This meant that every spell in his deck could resolve without contest.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkyo15/SF_kaiVSmurakami_EN.jpg)

The first [Lion's Eye Diamond](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lion%27s+Eye+Diamond) resolved, followed by the second one. [Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy) forced Murakami to lose [Force of Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+of+Will), paving the way for [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time).

The [Infernal Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Infernal+Tutor) he was looking for was indeed within those seven cards. Sacrificing both his "[Black Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Lotus)" while [Infernal Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Infernal+Tutor) was on the stack, that enabled him to find [Past in Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Past+in+Flames).

Thiele started to fan out his entire graveyard and Murakami decided it was better to scoop things up and save time. There was no way Thiele could fizzle out.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkyo15/SF_kai_EN.jpg)  
*Kai Thiele punches past [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance) to take Game One.* 

**Kai Thiele 1 – Kazuya Murakami 0** 

[Grafdigger's Cage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grafdigger%27s+Cage) was a decent foil against Thiele's combo deck. It did not nerf most of the cards in his deck, but it did stop the one crucial card the most. [Past in Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Past+in+Flames).

[Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance) was the next barrier, and Murakami looked like it was in firm control of the game. However, he had yet to find the [Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top) that would lock Thiele out of the game.

He dug with [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm) and [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder), and did manage to produce [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic) (along with [Batterskull](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Batterskull), of course).

Thiele tested the waters with [Lotus Petal](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotus+Petal) and Murakami revealed [Seal of Cleansing](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seal+of+Cleansing) at the top of the deck. This meant that [Cabal Ritual](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Ritual), [Burning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burning+Wish), or [Infernal Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Infernal+Tutor) would not resolve this turn. Thiele was forced to pass the turn.

[Meddling Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meddling+Mage), meddling with [Infernal Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Infernal+Tutor), seemed to be the final nail in the coffin. Even though he had K[rosan Grip](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=rosan+Grip) to remove [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance), he had no way to deal with "Chris Pikula".

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkyo15/SF_murakami_EN.jpg)  
*Kazuya Murakami disrupts his opponent with [Grafdigger's Cage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grafdigger%27s+Cage), [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance), and [Meddling Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meddling+Mage).* 

**Kai Thiele 1 – Kazuya Murakami 1** 

The final game played out pretty much similarly to the second. [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance) and [Meddling Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meddling+Mage) caused Thiele much anguish, while [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic) and [Batterskull](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Batterskull) chipped away at his life total.

Murakami knew he was going to win. He was holding [Flusterstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flusterstorm). That card was practically reverse engineered to destroy "Storm" strategies. When Thiele tried to execute his combo, Murakami sent him packing by negating all his efforts for just a single mana.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Flusterstorm)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flusterstorm)

**Kai Thiele 1 – Kazuya Murakami 2** 

**Kazuya Murakami defeats Kai Thiele and advances to the finals!**
