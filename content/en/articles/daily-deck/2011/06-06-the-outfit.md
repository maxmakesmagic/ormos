White Weenie is not new in the world of Standard, and Equipment has seen a resurgence in popularity of late thanks to Stoneforge Mystic. But there's Equipment besides Swords and Batterskulls to fetch up! Savvy [**Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20110816030145/http://www.wizards.com/magic/Digital/MagicOnline.aspx?) player Alloran finished in the Top 8 of a recent Pro Tour Qualifier in the digital playground by building a White Weenie deck that could call forth, amongst other things, Argentum Armor. With Stoneforge Mystic tutoring for the hefty six-drop as well as helping to cheat it onto the battlefield, you simply need a means of getting around the hefty equip cost of ![6 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/2d/12/2d12c8318f11a9188ce9fdd831b87ed97536df32e65841004ce583d399c4b454.gif). That's where Kor Outfitter comes into play. Blowing up your opponent's side of the battlefield while smashing them about the face all for the bargain-basement price of casting creatures you can use to attack them? That's some kind of deal...
