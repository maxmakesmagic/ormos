What is your favorite Morph creature and why? 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Kuo-Tzu-Ching.jpg)

 ***Kuo Tzu Ching:** [Kheru Spellsnatcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kheru+Spellsnatcher). It's great in a standstill.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Tomoharu-Saito.jpg)

 ***Tomoharu Saito:** [Ashcloud Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashcloud+Phoenix). Wins games fast and difficult to kill.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Raymond-Tan.jpg)

 ***Raymond Tan:** [Sagu Mauler](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sagu+Mauler). It's huge and has hexproof, can't really complain!* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Gao-Tan.jpg)

 ***Gao Tan:** [Abzan Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Guide). It is powerful in races.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Huang-Hao-Shan.jpg)

 ***Huang Hao-Shan:** [Ruthless Ripper](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ruthless+Ripper). Trades well with everything with a surprise element.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Lee-Shi-Tian.jpg)

 ***Lee Shi Tian:** [Woolly Loxodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Woolly+Loxodon). It's fat, just like me!* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Ken-Yukuhiro2.jpg)

 ***Ken Yukuhiro:** [Abomination of Gudul](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abomination+of+Gudul). I love to draw cards!* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsha14/QQ-Nam-Sung-Wook.jpg)

 ***Nam Sung Wook:** [Dragon's Eye Savants](https://web.archive.org/web/20141004000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragon%27s+Eye+Savants). I like to look at what my opponents have.*
