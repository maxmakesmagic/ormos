Naya Allies is continuing to do well in Standard! This week, Toolundertow used a version to go 3-1 in a large event on **Magic Online**. The powerful teamwork ability of the Allies work together to create gigantic Hada Freeblades and Kazandu Blademasters, gain tons of life with Ondu Cleric, become unblockable with protection from Kabira Evangel, and more. It's a fun breeze of fresh air in Standard, perfect for your upcoming Friday Night **Magic** event!
