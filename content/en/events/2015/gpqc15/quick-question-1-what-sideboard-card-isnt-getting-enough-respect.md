### What sideboard card isn't getting enough respect?

Thanks to the Pro Tour, players have a good idea of what they'll face this weekend. However, the sideboard that was good at the Pro Tour is not necessarily the best for this more-defined format. The Pros weigh in on some cards that should show up more.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpqc15/GPQC-Manfield.jpg)  
*"[Self-Inflicted Wound](https://web.archive.org/web/20151024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Self-Inflicted+Wound) and [Rending Volley](https://web.archive.org/web/20151024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rending+Volley). They're matchup-specific, but very good in those matchups." – World Champion Seth Manfield* 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpqc15/GPQC-Hayne.jpg)  
*"[Chandra, Fire of Kaladesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20151024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Fire+of+Kaladesh) in Atarka Red. People are relying on [Surge of Righteousness](https://web.archive.org/web/20151024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Surge+of+Righteousness) and [Arashin Cleric](https://web.archive.org/web/20151024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arashin+Cleric), both of which are terrible against her. She's hard to remove once she flips, and even if she doesn't she's a fine threat." – Pro Tour* Avacyn Restored *Champion Alex Hayne* 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpqc15/GPQC-Larsson.jpg)  
*"[Mastery of the Unseen](https://web.archive.org/web/20151024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mastery+of+the+Unseen). I think it's very good in the Jeskai mirror. Of course this is all theory, as I haven't drawn it in any of my games." – Pro Tour* Magic Origins *Champion Joel Larsson* 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpqc15/GPQC-BBD.jpg)  
*"[Quarantine Field](https://web.archive.org/web/20151024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Quarantine+Field). Being able to exile threats is very powerful against Jeskai, shutting down their recursion from Commands. It's not bad even as an expensive [Oblivion Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20151024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Ring), and the ability to scale up is huge." – Two-Time Grand Prix Champion Brian Braun-Duin* 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpqc15/GPQC-Duke.jpg)  
*"[Dragonlord Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar). Very powerful against Gideon and all the green decks. Any deck capable of casting it should have it in the sideboard." – Household Name Reid Duke*
