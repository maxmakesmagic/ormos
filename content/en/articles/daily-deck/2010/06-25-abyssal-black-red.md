Aggressive red decks have always been short one thing: fatties. But Scott Alshansi didn't let that stop him from using Abyssal Persecutor to make up for that fact on his way to the Top 8 of the recent Pro Tour Qualifier held in Madison, Wisconsin. The 6/6 could be blown up with Gatekeeper of Malakir, Terminate, and Sarkhan the Mad giving Scott the time he needed to beat down, then giving him plenty of outs to toss the giant monster!
