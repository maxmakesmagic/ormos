Extended continues to heat up the premier-event field. Former World Champion Katsuhiro Mori steamrolled the 1,122-player field at Grand Prix–Yokohama with his Thopter/Depths deck in a Top 8 that featured six different archetypes. There are only three weekends left in the [Pro Tour–San Juan Qualifier season](https://web.archive.org/web/20100325000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/protour/sanjuan10-qualifiers). There are [three more **Magic Online** qualifiers](https://web.archive.org/web/20100325000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/events/ptqmtgo/sanjuan10), so even if there's not a PTQ in your local area, fire up that Extended deck and take aim at the Pro Tour.
