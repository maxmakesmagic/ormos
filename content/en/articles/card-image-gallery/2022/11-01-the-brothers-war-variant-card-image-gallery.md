Check out the latest Booster Fun variant cards and promos revealed from [*The Brothers' War*](https://web.archive.org/web/20221101000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/the-brothers-war) below. To view the regular cards, check out [*The Brothers' War* Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2022/09-29-the-brothers-war-card-image-gallery). You can also see the retro frame cards featured in *The Brothers' War* Commander decks in our [Commander Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2022/10-28-the-brothers-war-commander-card-image-gallery).

*The Brothers' War* releases November 18 and will be available at [your local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20221101000000/https://locator.wizards.com/), online through [Amazon](https://gtly.to/SrLywky5i), and everywhere *Magic* is sold.
