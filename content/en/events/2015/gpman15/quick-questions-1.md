# What is your favorite Fate Reforged previewed card?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Christian-Calcano.jpg)  
**Christian Calcano:** Temporal Tresspass. After the results of [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20150103000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) and [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20150103000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time), I can't see how it's not broken.  
  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/ujehvsbfrf153/en_p9bhq61qqe.png)

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Lee-Shi-Tian.jpg)  
**Lee Shi Tian:** The new [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150103000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer), Monastery Mentor, looks crazy.  
  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/ujehvsbfrf153/en_lvwdxadj5j.png)

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Terry-Soh.jpg)  
**Terry Soh:** I like Crux of Fate. It gives black mass removal options.  
  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/ujehvsbfrf153/en_ltmv1th2oo.png)

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Jelger.jpg)  
**Jelger Wiegersma:** Ugin, The Spirit Dragon, a colourless Planeswalker!  
  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/ujehvsbfrf153/en_w54r4t295i.png)

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Martin-Juza.jpg)  
**Martin Jůza:** Soulfire Grand Master seems great, it's the 2-drop the Jeskai decks have been looking for. The ability to buyback spells seems strong.  
  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/ujehvsbfrf153/en_lw5yaa6hq8.png)

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpman15/QQ-Nam-Sung-Wook.jpg)  
**Nam Sung Wook:** Monastary Siege - at three mana it looks like it could be an important card in Modern, maybe in the sideboard.  
  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/ujehvsbfrf153/en_uaeht1tvvh.png)
