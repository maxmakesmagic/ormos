### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare card.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7b/f9/7bf9e7fc08fd406a0ed23a7215736d70b6ee4a78e68f22f2ae284e3833f03223.png)

### TWO-PLAYER STARTER GAME

Want to play with a friend right now? Just choose which of the two 40-card decks you’ll each play—and begin! It’s that easy. Also includes a rulebook and play guide.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7b/f9/7bf9e7fc08fd406a0ed23a7215736d70b6ee4a78e68f22f2ae284e3833f03223.png)

### TOURNAMENT STARTER DECKS

Battle like the best with these 75 tournament-ready cards, including 30 land cards and 45 creatures and other spells.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/85/ef/85ef633385d3bd0f752ac380da7011062be283613cd0246d7b9601799c42c47a.png)

This 350-card **Magic** set includes favorites from the *Visions*, *Mirage*, *Alliances*, and *Weatherlight* expansions. Each card is now numbered for collectors, too, and carries an expansion symbol to indicate its rarity.

**Set Name:** *Sixth Edition*  
**Number of Cards:** 350

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, Korean

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718094816/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/advanced/6e/C6EFAQ.txt)

**Design and Development:** Charlie Cantana, Skaff Elias, Mike Elliott, Williman Jockusch, Beth Moursund, Bill Rose, Mark Rosewater, and Henry Stern
