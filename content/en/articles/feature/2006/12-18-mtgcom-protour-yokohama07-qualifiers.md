### Entry Fee

Entry fee varies from location to location. Check with local organizer for specific information.

### Format

*   Extended-constructed format
*   The *Planar Chaos* expansion is not legal for use in the Extended format until February 20, 2007. Events held prior to this date will **not** use the *Planar Chaos* expansion.
*   Deck lists are required

### Tournament Length

*   Swiss rounds will each be 50 minutes in length
*   Top 8 matches will have no time limit
*   Number of Swiss rounds based on attendance according to the following chart:

<table><tbody><tr><td><b>Attendance</b></td><td><b>Number of<br>Swiss rounds</b></td></tr><tr><td>17 - 32</td><td>5</td></tr><tr><td>33 - 64</td><td>6</td></tr><tr><td>65 - 128</td><td>7</td></tr><tr><td>129 - 226</td><td>8</td></tr><tr><td>227 - 410</td><td>9</td></tr><tr><td>411 or higher</td><td>10</td></tr></tbody></table>

### Advancement

*   All players may participate in all Swiss rounds.
*   The top 8 players after the final Swiss round will advance to the top 8 single-elimination finals.

### Top 8

*   Top 8 matches will be best 2 of 3
*   Where necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will still be used to determine final order in the standings.

### Notes

*   32K, REL 3
*   All Universal Tournament Rules and **Magic: the Gathering** Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document
*   Players must bring pen, paper, card sleeves and appropriate counters.

### Invitations, Eligibility, and Prize Money

Players already holding an invitation to Pro Tour–Yokohama are prohibited from participating in any Pro Tour–Yokohama Qualifiers, with the exception of Grand Prix tournaments. Players may have earned invitations through cumulative past performance in the Pro Tour, appropriate Pro Tour Players Club level, winning a previous Qualifier, finishing Top 16 at a Grand Prix tournament, or by being an invited DCI player (valid upon the date of the official ratings invitation announcement for the appropriate professional tournament).

### Prizes

*   Invitations to Pro Tour–Yokohama (exact number of invitations determined by tournament location)
*   Product prizes to be determined by the organizer
*   Qualifier offer airline travel. See individual schedules for details.

### Registration Information

*   Players who want more specific local tournament or registration information should call their local contact phone number.
