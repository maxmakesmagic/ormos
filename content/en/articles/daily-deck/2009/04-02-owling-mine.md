Owling Mine was a Pro Tour Top 8ing anti-control deck from Pro Tour-Honolulu. **Magic Online** user 30to1 has brought the archetype back for a modern world of Standard. Kederekt Parasite serves as a damage source while Jace Beleren and Howling Mine keep both player's hands stocked. Add a small amount of countermagic and some spot removal, and you have a winner!
