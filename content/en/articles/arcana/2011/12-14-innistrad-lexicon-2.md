Welcome to the *Innistrad* edition of the Lexicon, in which we explore the meanings of some of the more unusual words we use in the names of those cards. While many offbeat names in **Magic** (like "[moldgraf](https://web.archive.org/web/20111214000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=moldgraf)") are simply made up, we often dig around dictionaries for real little-used and archaic words for inspiration.

![Abattoir Ghoul](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Abattoir+Ghoul)

**ab•at•toir**  
*noun*

a slaughterhouse.

![Ulvenwald Primordials](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ulvenwald+Primordials)

**pri•mor•di•al**  
*noun*

1.  constituting a beginning; giving origin to something derived or developed; original; elementary: *primordial forms of life*.
2.  *Embryology.* first formed.
3.  pertaining to or existing at or from the very beginning: *primordial matter.*

![Bloodcrazed Neonate](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Bloodcrazed+Neonate)

**ne•o•nate**  
*noun*

a newborn child, or one in its first 28 days.

![Paraselene](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Paraselene)

**par•a•se•le•ne**  
*noun*

a bright moonlike spot on a lunar halo; a mock moon. 

![Trepanation Blade](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Trepanation+Blade)

**tre•pan**  
*noun*

1.  a tool for cutting shallow holes by removing a core.
2.  *Surgery.* an obsolete form of the trephine resembling a carpenter's bit and brace.

![Morkrut Banshee](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Morkrut+Banshee)

**ban•shee**  
*noun*

(in Irish folklore) a spirit in the form of a wailing woman who appears to or is heard by members of a family as a sign that one of them is about to die. 

*Source: dictionary.com*
