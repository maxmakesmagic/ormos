After fifteen rounds of Modern, with a total of 1,735 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

* * *

### Steve Hatto

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpbir17/t8_hatto.jpg)

**Age:** 34

**Occupation:** Public servant

**Hometown:** Itzig, Luxembourg

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Finalist Modern Grand Prix Copenhagen 2015, Top 8 Standard Grand Prix Prague 2015, Gold for 2017–18, lifetime Team Captain Luxembourg.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Black-Green Rock, because people told me it's unplayable.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Tireless Tracker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tracker) because people mostly refuse to play [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt)s.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Fulminator Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fulminator+Mage). Because people do not expect me to go land destruction in non "big mana" matchups.

### Simon Nielsen

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpbir17/t8_nielsen.jpg)

**Age:** 23

**Occupation:** It's complicated ...

**Hometown:** Copenhagen, Denmark

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Two Grand Prix Top 8s, Gold two years in a row.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Red-Green Valakut, simple deck for a simple player. Also I think it's too powerful not to play.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan), duh!

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Spitebellows](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spitebellows)! Okay, it might actually have been our [Witchbane Orb](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Witchbane+Orb) tech.

* * *

### Oscar Christensen

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpbir17/t8_christensen.jpg)

**Age:** 21

**Occupation:** Teacher

**Hometown:** Copenhagen, Denmark

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Grand Prix Gothenburg 2013 win, Grand Prix Manchester 2016 Top 4.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Abzan Company,. because it is my kind of deck—mana dorks, [Kitchen Finks](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kitchen+Finks), and multiple infinite combos. Also chose the deck because Chris Larsen was playing it and I wanted to prove that I am much better than him.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Devoted Druid](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devoted+Druid)—does it all!

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Chord of Calling](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chord+of+Calling) package is so important and makes it possible for you to control most matchups.

### Piotr Głogowski

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpbir17/t8_glogowski.jpg)

**Age:** 22

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Poznań, Poland

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

[Defeat](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Defeat)ed Sam Pardee on Magic Online and Top 8ed Grand Prix Milan in 2014.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Lantern, because it defeats everything besides Mono-Blue Tron. Luckily, Shooktropa wasn't here.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Pyxis of Pandemonium](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyxis+of+Pandemonium) because you can pop it for troll value at the end of the game.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Leyline of Sanctity](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leyline+of+Sanctity), because it's good if you're lucky and I am an extremely lucky person.

### Loïc Le Briand

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpbir17/t8_lebriand.jpg)

**Age:** 33

**Occupation:** Data analysis

**Hometown:** Metz, France

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Top 8 Grand Prix Lille in Legacy, Top 8 Grand Prix Paris in 2014.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Burn with [Shrine of Burning Rage](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrine+of+Burning+Rage). I like the deck, and I found something with which to make it past its bad matchups. [Eidolon of the Great Revel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eidolon+of+the+Great+Revel) is not great at the moment.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Shrine of Burning Rage](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrine+of+Burning+Rage). Completely unexpected, people really aren't prepared for it.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer) and [Destructive Revelry](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Destructive+Revelry) add something to your deck which it lacks at times.

### George Channing

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpbir17/t8_channing.jpg)

**Age:** 30

**Occupation:** Trainee teacher

**Hometown:** Guildford, England

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

Played one Pro Tour. Multiple Game Day champion.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Grixis Shadow as I have a lot of experience with it after my team captain Tom told me to take some time to learn a Modern deck—thanks Tom! Also it is the best deck, probably.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize). The card is absurd. It allows you to take their best card and then sculpt your game plan around what they have.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Engineered Explosives](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engineered+Explosives) was excellent in the aggro matchups, but [Leyline of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leyline+of+the+Void) won me two rounds against [Living End](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+End) and [Dredge](https://web.archive.org/web/20170813000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dredge), so that has to be the winner.
