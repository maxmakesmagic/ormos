The King of Beatdown sits down at his seven person table between Alex Shvartsman and Frederic Courtois. With a 4-2-1 record, he needs a good deck to remain in contention for top 8. Price drafted R/G in both drafts yesterday, and shows a high inclination towards the beatdown. 

The first pack goes decently as he starts with a [Roar of the Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roar+of+the+Wurm). Price is given several chances to delve into Black, with a [Morbid Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morbid+Hunger), Crypt Keeper and [Skeletal Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skeletal+Scrying), but stays firm in mono Green. He seems to want to force Red, but with only a reckless charge and seeing no other good Red cards from the right it may not be the best of ideas. He also shows how high he values [Muscle Burst](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Muscle+Burst) by taking it over the board controlling [Nantuko Disciple](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Disciple).

The second pack does not offer Price any bomb quality cards, so he settles for another [Muscle Burst](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Muscle+Burst). At this point, Price doesn't have many creatures, and none that are aggressive (1-2 casting cost), so the [Muscle Burst](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Muscle+Burst) seems like an interesting choice for an aggressive deck. The key color decision point comes in pack 4 where Price must choose between Red and Blue for his second color. Price decides that [Reckless Charge](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reckless+Charge), Flameburst, and [Lithatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lithatog) are better than [Repel](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Repel), [Escape Artist](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Escape+Artist) and Balashan Griffin/ [Psionic Gift](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psionic+Gift). Once again the second pack is weak on creatures.

After 2 packs, Price's deck is mediocre as he has a lot of creature enhancement spells but very few low casting cost creatures, staples in a R/G deck. *Torment* is generally weak for R/G especially if low casting cost creatures are needed, as the majority of *Torment* picks in R/G will be 4 casting cost. This holds true for Price as he is able to get 1 one casting cost creature and 4 four casting cost creatures. The Llawan that was drafted could also be important as a splash versus Blue Mages.

With a 7 person table, Price is will get at least one win with a bye. Unless it happens the first round, however, a bye would not be a good sign for his top 8 hopes. Slow R/G decks generally do not fare well without bombs to back it up. He also lacks Tier 1 Green creatures such as [Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel), [Springing Tiger](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Springing+Tiger), [Rabid Elephant](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rabid+Elephant), [Gorilla Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20020504000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gorilla+Titan) etc. Price is optimistic though and hopes for at least a 2-2 record for this draft.
