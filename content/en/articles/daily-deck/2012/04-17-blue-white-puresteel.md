At the StarCityGames.com Open Series in Phoenix this past weekend, longtime **Magic** player Ryan Sterbenz brought a deck to Standard that had fallen out of favor among many Standard players: Puresteel Paladin! While many players who once played Puresteel have shifted toward Delver of Secrets decks or Tempered Steel, the stalwart Sterbenz has stuck with it to a Top 16 finish. Could this be the time for Puresteel Paladin to make a comeback?

The core of this deck is its Equipment engine. Puresteel Paladin means that all of your Equipment draws you into more Equipment, quickly overloading your board with artifacts and enabling metalcraft. Once metalcraft is online, the Paladin produces gobs of virtual mana, moving around all of your Swords, Batterskulls, and more at absolutely no cost.

You can always put the Equipment onto the Paladin or a Germ token, but Sterbenz figures that since this deck has metalcraft anyway it is worth putting in some other excellent metalcraft cards. Etched Champion and Indomitable Archangel help protect your side of the board while serving in for immense amounts of damage. Dispatch almost always just exiles a creature, getting any pesky blockers out of the way.

If you've been waiting to try out Puresteel Paladin again, now might be a good time! Dust off those Paladins and give it a try!

Ryan Sterbenz's Blue-White Puresteel 

Standard – StarCityGames.com Open Series, Phoenix, AZ
