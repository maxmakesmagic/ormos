[*Jumpstart 2022* arrives on December 2](https://web.archive.org/web/20221123000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/jumpstart), ready for you to mash up more than 40 wild new theme combinations and jump right into playing!

![Jumpstart 2022 hero image](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/j22_hero.jpg) 

Want to play Blink Cats? You can enter the battlefield with feline ferocity. Perhaps you'd like to see what class is like when Zombies Go to School? Pedagogy for the corpuscularly challenged is in the curriculum. Or maybe you just want to regulate with Urza's Law? It's on the docket! Grab two *Jumpstart 2022* boosters, shuffle together, and play!

![Jumpstart 2022 booster box with 24 boosters](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/j22_booster_displaybox.png)![Jumpstart 2022 unopened booster](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/j22_booster.png) 

Each *Jumpstart 2022* booster contains 20 cards along with all the lands you'll need, plus:

*   1 Theme description card
*   1–2 Rare or mythic rare cards
*   1 Anime-inspired card

If 46 theme combos aren't enough for you, you can expand your palate options by combining *Jumpstart 2022* with [*The Brothers' War* Jumpstart Boosters](/en/articles/feature/2022/11-08-the-brothers-war-jumpstart-booster-contents), [*Dominaria United* Jumpstart Boosters](/en/articles/announcements/2022/08-31-dominaria-united-jumpstart-booster-contents), or any other *Jumpstart* product!

Check out the complete card lists and themes for all the different *Jumpstart 2022* boosters below.
