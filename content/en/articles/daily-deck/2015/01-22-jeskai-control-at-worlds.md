*Editor’s Note: Shuhei submitted his Daily Decks before the* [*Banned and Restricted Announcement*](/en/articles/feature/2015/01-19-january-19-2015-banned-and-restricted-announcement) *and, as such, did not know the contents of the announcement. We still thought it was worth revisiting the format prior to the announcement.*

* * *

For this deck, I would like to introduce the Jeskai (URW) Control piloted by Shaun McLaren, the only person to bring a control deck to the Modern scene at [the World Championship](/en/events/2014/legacy-daily-2014-11-22/legacy-daily).

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Dig+Through+Time)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150122000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time)

Remove, counter, draw, and do it again—the archetype that won him a Pro Tour and a basic *Magic* strategy. The biggest deference since [Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-born-of-the-gods-coverage-events) is that [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20150122000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) has changed to [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20150122000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time). There are no true beatdown decks in the format, so cards like [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20150122000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) are less reliable. Control decks end up doing a lot of one-for-one exchanges and need cards like [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20150122000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) to replenish their hands for two to four mana, instead of mana-consuming [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20150122000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation).

The downside was that you could not play strong anti-graveyard cards such as [Rest in Peace](https://web.archive.org/web/20150122000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rest+in+Peace), since you wanted to use your graveyard. Without as much delve in the format, it might make a return.
