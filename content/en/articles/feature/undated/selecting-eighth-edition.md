### Nostalgia, please have a seat

Go to the "Selecting *Eighth Edition*" promo page [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20021014100639/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/vote8e/home).

* * *

Rabid Wombat is one of those cards that is a little sillier than we normally like to print, but we understand that people have an attachment to it, and we're perfectly willing to put it into *Eighth Edition*. Similarly, Crusade was rotated out of the base set, partially due to power level concerns, but with white needing a kick-start, we could see it coming back.

The dust has settled, and it seems you all have other ideas. Nostalgia, please have a seat.

Yavimaya Enchantress and Glorious Anthem beat out their older siblings, and neither contest was particularly close. I'll leave the "whys" and "hows" to other speculative writers around the 'Net; I'm just here with the facts. (And the fact is, an 0/1 for ![2 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/f9/cc/f9cc8a15b009997919d58ad79964384351495d1b6c9231f938316577453038a8.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif)![Green Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/d9/d5/d9d556256faf839099c06019302393fab57e6e9a77769d6f6813a7c961c4cb59.gif) *is* pretty awful when you think about it.)

Nostalgia saved face by winning both art votes, with *Tempest* versions of Trained Armodon and Boil getting the nod.
