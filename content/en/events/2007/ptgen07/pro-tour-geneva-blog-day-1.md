<table><tbody><tr><td><b>BLOG</b></td>&#10;</tr>&#10;<tr><td><br><br><a name="1"></a><li><b>Friday, Feb 9: 10:21 am - Kicking off the Season</b><div><i>by Tim Willoughby</i></div>&#10;</li><br><br><p>Welcome to Day One of Pro Tour - Geneva! With a day of skiing behind them, players and staff are now ready for a whole weekend drafting the minty fresh <i>Time Spiral</i> - <i>Time Spiral - Planar Chaos</i> format. We've got more coverage than ever, with audio, video and plain old-fashioned words sent direct to you by super high-tech, modern internettery. </p>&#10;<p>Here in the blog we'll be giving you all the latest in terms of news, photos, gossip around the venue, and possibly even some 'falling over in the snow' ski-trip anecdotes. Given that yesterday a fair few of them went skiing, this is entirely appropriate. Pro Tour beanie hats and polo necks were given out to players at this event, and there have also been scarves, ear muffs, and gloves spotted, bearing the Pro Tour stamp or otherwise. </p>&#10;<p>One player that rather stood out this morning, though, was Olivier Ruel. Had he taken the Japanese fashion of wearing a scarf flung jauntily over a shoulder to heart for this event? Actually no. Olivier is in a blue neck brace this Pro Tour, following an entirely un-skiing related mishap, though you could easily be forgiven for mistaking his neckwear for a fashion accessory. He did comment that he was pretty warm with it on most of the time.</p>&#10;<p>More concerningly, though, Olivier has been put to a tricky decision as to what to do with regards to painkillers for this event. His neck is pretty uncomfortable, which could prove a distraction, but his painkillers make him drowsy - also not good for <b>Magic</b>. Going into the first draft, Ruel was going for the 'painful but awake' strategy. It remains to be seen if he will switch to his backup plan later in the weekend.</p><br><br><a name="3"></a><li><b>Friday, Feb 9: 2:10 pm - Crazy Plays</b><div><i>by Kelly Digges</i></div>&#10;</li>&#10;<p>A Pro Tour is the perfect place to catch clever plays and strange interactions, and the eclectic nature of <i>Time Spiral</i> block only accentuates it.</p>&#10;<table><tbody><tr><td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/65/b4/65b44cec90bda938bbb278a79acba1f455a38a19b714807bea68c253b77de30d.jpg"></td><td></td><td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/dd/0a/dd0a617788694ee0e82eed58b0746ab9dbdbdb6e709ee5390dcfb32556a6990e.jpg"></td></tr>&#10;<tr><td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/96/f5/96f5d53e3ef958055c4fdf5bf835be99797bd18449a68a882c96ab3801bae612.jpg"></td><td><a><img alt="Hail Storm" src="https://i.ormos.uk/c9/83/c983f8e056483534f95994ee99ffa81eb81764f8301d5cd8d337ae7f7e1e5cb0.jpg"></a></td><td></td></tr>&#10;<tr><td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/a0/a9/a0a91e69ef087e17e689f758224146196f0a29fad934eb2639d2fb73d4cf3c75.jpg"></td><td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/c0/e2/c0e24bff2fa206011911cb4b5cb20b4559cb82301a77e757b6e86de3c1ae659a.jpg"></td><td><img alt="" src="https://i.ormos.uk/fa/ab/faab219b94a5d6a8405ff77aefe6f815ba2bba52fc447652a633eec85e227836.jpg"></td></tr>&#10;</tbody></table>&#10;<p>I noticed Olivier Ruel with a <a>Vampiric Sliver</a> and <a>Ghost Ship</a> faced down by Anatoli Lightfoot's lonely little <a>Errant Doomsayers</a>. Lightfoot was getting low on life and decided it was time to make a move. He attacked with his <a>Errant Doomsayers</a>, dealt Olivier 1 damage, and played <a>Hail Storm</a> at the end of <i>his own</i> combat step to kill the Doomsayers. Not exactly an orthodox play, but it allowed Lightfoot's newly minted <a>Porphyry Nodes</a> to get to work a turn earlier. &quot;Desperate times,&quot; he said wryly. Of course, the whole thing would have looked a lot better for him if Olivier hadn't suspended a <a>Deep-Sea Kraken</a> a few turns earlier.
