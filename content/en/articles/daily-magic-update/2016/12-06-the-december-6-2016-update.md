The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on December 6, 2016. Today's Update is brought to you by

# Today's Must

1.  [Standard Power Rankings](http://www.channelfireball.com/power-rankings/) | ChannelFireball

So many reasons I put this here. One, I love power rankings. I don't follow basketball, but I follow basketball power rankings. I'll read power rankings for anything. Heck, I'll read power rankings for Power Rangers (Tommy as the White Ranger, obviously).

Two, we've just had a ton of Standard results that altered the metagame once more. Third, with Standard Showdowns in full swing (I keep getting great decklists—keep them coming at standardshowdown@wizards.com), this is a useful tool for surveying the landscape.

But the real reason? ChannelFireball pandered *hard* to me and put [Panharmonicon](https://web.archive.org/web/20161206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panharmonicon) at No. 1. Well played, ChannelFireball. Well played.

# Deck of the Day

1.  Zada and the Wolves

After my gleeful [Panharmonicon](https://web.archive.org/web/20161206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panharmonicon) moment yesterday, I return to Standard Showdown decks (though Panharmonicombo makes a great Standard Showdown deck). Today's deck is one of the cooler ones I received from this past weekend, which I've taken to calling "Zada and the Wolves" because, well, it has Zada. And some Wolves.

The key interaction is between [Zada, Hedron Grinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20161206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zada%2C+Hedron+Grinder) (or [Mirrorwing Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20161206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirrorwing+Dragon)) and [Silverfur Partisan](https://web.archive.org/web/20161206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silverfur+Partisan). With those two pieces on the battlefield, any targeting spell essentially doubles the number of Wolves you have in play. To make the most of that, the deck packs a bunch of one-mana spells to trigger the Partisan as many times as possible, creating an army.

But even if Partisan isn't available, the deck can still do a ton of damage with either Zada or [Mirrorwing Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20161206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirrorwing+Dragon) and just a few creatures. [Rush of Adrenaline](https://web.archive.org/web/20161206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rush+of+Adrenaline) can become [Overrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20161206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overrun). And [Expedite](https://web.archive.org/web/20161206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Expedite) can become [Ancestral Recall](https://web.archive.org/web/20161206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Recall).

The deck is fun and potentially explosive, and it looks like a lot of care has gone into balancing the numbers for the deck, so I would highly recommend this fun, aggressive, combo-ish deck for your next Standard Showdown. Just be sure to bring a lot of Wolf tokens.
