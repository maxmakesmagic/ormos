Judging from the results of Grand Prix Charlotte and Grand Prix Copenhagen, who would have expected an all-Affinity mirror match in the finals amidst a sea of unfair combo decks and midrange decks laden with hate?

The last two men standing at Grand Prix Singapore are both armed with the explosive beatdown deck, spearheaded by [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating), [Etched Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Etched+Champion), [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager) and an assortment of cheap artifacts.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Dispatch)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dispatch)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Galvanic+Blast)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Blast)

Despite largely similar maindecks, the main difference between both decks was the choice of removal. Tan went with a pair of [Dispatch](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dispatch), while Masaaki went with a quad of [Galvanic Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Blast). Despite that, it could be anyone's game!

# Game One

After a mulligan, Tan led with [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge), Memnite and Ornithopher. Kawasaki was not to be outdone, using [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter), [Darksteel Citadel](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Citadel) and double [Mox Opal](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Opal) to power out a turn 1 [Etched Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Etched+Champion).

Tan tried to negate the damage that [Etched Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Etched+Champion) would deal by summoning a second and third [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge) but Masaaki had the [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating) to upset the combat math.

Tan still had a chance to recover if he was allowed to untap with [Steel Overseer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steel+Overseer). That could grow his trio of [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge)s into 2/2 lifelinkers. And into 3/3 lifelinkers. And then 4/4 lifelinkers.

However, one can only hope.

Masaaki was ready with [Galvanic Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Blast) to throw that plan out of the window. When Tan tried to chump with both his [Blinkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinkmoth+Nexus) on to successive turns, a second and third [Galvanic Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Blast) put an end to the plan.

A second [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating) and [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) was the final nail in the coffin for Tan.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsin15/GPSing_Finals_Masaaki.jpg)  
*Hitomi Masaaki takes the first game with triple [Galvanic Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Blast).* 

**Steven Tan 0 – Hitomi Masaaki 1** 

# Game Two

It is sometimes said that the Affinity deck mulligans often but mulligans well. Masaaki would have to disagree. Going down to four cards, he quickly faced pressure from [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter), [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge) and [Steel Overseer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steel+Overseer).

Masaaki did not have the [Galvanic Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Blast) so Tan proceeded to summon [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager) and put +1/+1 counters on his entire army.

Masaaki had [Ancient Grudge](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Grudge) to kill both [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager) and [Steel Overseer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steel+Overseer). Regardless, Tan had the board advantage, backed up with [Dispatch](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dispatch) and [Wear & Tear](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wear+%2F%2F+Tear). It was certain that Masaaki could not recover from that ill-fated series of mulligans.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsin15/GPSing_Finals_Tan.jpg)  
*Steven Tan capitalizes on his opponent's mulligans and equalizes the score.* 

In under five minutes, Tan had forced the rubber game!

**Steven Tan 1 – Hitomi Masaaki 1** 

# Game Three

It seems like a reversal of fortunes would sculpt the final game. It was Tan who went down to five cards.

Kawasaki kickstarted with [Inkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inkmoth+Nexus), [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter), [Springleaf Drum](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Springleaf+Drum) and [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge).

On his turn three, [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating) and [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge) were melded as one. Thankfully for Tan, [Wear & Tear](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wear+%2F%2F+Tear) made its dramatic appearance to buy him a little more time but was still facing a pair of [Signal Pest](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Signal+Pest)s, Memnite, [Vault Skirge](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vault+Skirge) and [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter).

When Tan added [Steel Overseer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steel+Overseer) to the board, Kawasaki decimated his entire board with [Galvanic Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Galvanic+Blast) and [Ancient Grudge](https://web.archive.org/web/20150628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Grudge) (with flashback).

The Singaporean crowd winced and everyone knew it was over. Hitomi Masaaki had denied Singapore of yet another Grand Prix Trophy, bringing glory to Japan.

**Hitomi Masaaki defeats Steven Tan and wins Grand Prix Singapore! Congratulations!** 

**Steven Tan 1 – Hitomi Masaaki 2**
