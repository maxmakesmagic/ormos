Early on Saturday, we asked the Twitterverse for interesting questions to be answered by the pros here at Grand Prix Paris. We selected the following one, because *Modern Masters* certainly is one of the big topics this weekend: 

> [@magicprotour](https://twitter.com/magicprotour) what is the best way to beat Esper Dragons? Or: Which card excites you the most of Modern Masters 2015? 
> 
> — Danny de Rooij (@One2WhoopWhoop) [May 9, 2015](https://twitter.com/One2WhoopWhoop/status/596953962173091840)

And well, we already tackled [Esper Dragons recently](/en/events/2015/gpsao15/seven-ways-to-beat-esper-dragons), so we took a look at the [*Modern Masters 2015* card image gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2015/05-08-modern-masters-2015-card-image-gallery) and asked the pros for their thoughts. 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppar15/gppar15_qq_juza.jpg)  
**Platinum level pro Martin Jůza** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/mm2_9vgauji43t9a/en_zZAEqwgRAp.png)  
 *[Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf). It's the best creature ever printed, so I'm hoping to open a foil one at Grand Prix Utrecht.* 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppar15/gppar15_qq_blohon.jpg)  
**Two-time Pro Tour Top 8 competitor Lukas Blohon** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/mm2_9vgauji43t9a/en_rZIxVRAbYe.png)  
 *[Karn Liberated](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn+Liberated). It's one of the best planeswalkers of all time.* 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppar15/gppar15_qq_larsson.jpg)  
 **Pro Tour *Gatecrash* finalist Joel Larsson** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/mm2_9vgauji43t9a/en_0gYrhYs23Y.png)  
 *[Bitterblossom](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bitterblossom). I've had some fun with that. I used to play Faeries back in the days.* 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppar15/gppar15_qq_melis.jpg)  
**2010 Dutch National Champion Bas Melis** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/mm2_9vgauji43t9a/en_wqrhwi3REi.png)  
 *[Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command). It's a great card, and I love to play it.* 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppar15/gppar15_qq_dolar.jpg)  
**Grand Prix Kraków 2015 finalist Robin Dolar** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/mm2_9vgauji43t9a/en_4sRXIOzwPJ.png)  
 *[Vendilion Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vendilion+Clique). It's a 3/1 flier with flash for 1UU that can put a card from a player's hand on the bottom of his or her library. That's why it's good.* 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppar15/gppar15_qq_anteri.jpg)  
**Gold level pro Fabrizio Anteri** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/mm2_9vgauji43t9a/en_rZIxVRAbYe.png)  
 *[Karn Liberated](https://web.archive.org/web/20150510000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn+Liberated). I've played with it in Modern, and I'd like to play with it in Limited.*
