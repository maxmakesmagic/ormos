In 1998, Pro Tour-Rome was defined by two cards: Time Spiral and Tolarian Academy. One player who decided to make a go of things without either was Frenchman Nicolas Labarre. His Fish build was good enough to get second, powered by Force of Will, Curiosity, and cheap blue creatures. He could plop a few threats onto the battlefield, then counter his opponent's most relevant spells long enough to attack for the win!
