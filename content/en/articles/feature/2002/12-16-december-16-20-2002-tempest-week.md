The theme week for December 16-20, 2002 was "*Tempest* Week".

### Monday

*   **Making Magic: [In a Teapot](/en/articles/makingmagic/2002/12-16-in-a-teapot)**, by Mark Rosewater. Design stories about individual *Tempest* cards.
*   **Arcana: [Jinxed Items](https://web.archive.org/web/20021216000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/jinxed-items-2002-12-16)**. The cycle that became [Jinxed Idol](https://web.archive.org/web/20021216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jinxed+Idol), [Jinxed Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20021216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jinxed+Ring), and ... [Null Brooch](https://web.archive.org/web/20021216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Null+Brooch)?

### Tuesday

*   **Serious Fun: [The New Old School](https://web.archive.org/web/20021216000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/serious-fun/new-old-school-2002-12-17)**, by Anthony Alongi. *Tempest* as the dividing line between "old" and "new".
*   **Feature Article: [Before the Storm](https://web.archive.org/web/20021216000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/storm-2002-12-17)**, by Mark Rosewater. Reprinted from Duelist #20, the inside story behind the creation of *Tempest*.
*   **Arcana: [*Tempest* playtest cards](/en/articles/arcana/2002/12-17-tempest-playtest-cards)**. Early versions of *Tempest* cards.

### Wednesday

*   **Uncommon Knowledge: [Lost in the Tempest](https://web.archive.org/web/20021216000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/lost-tempest-2002-12-18), by Ben Bleiweiss**. New and innovative uses for *Tempest* cards.
*   **Arcana: [Not actually *Tempest* cards...](/en/articles/arcana/2002/12-18-not-actually-tempest-cards)**. *Tempest* playtest cards that ended up in other sets.

### Thursday

*   **House of Cards: [Root for the Funky Fish](https://web.archive.org/web/20021216000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/root-funky-fish-2002-12-19), by Jay Moldenhauer-Salazar**. Wacky decks featuring [Rootwater Matriarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20021216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rootwater+Matriarch) and [Rootwater Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20021216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rootwater+Shaman)
*   **Arcana: [Dirty Medallions](https://web.archive.org/web/20021216000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/dirty-medallions-2002-12-19)**. Close-up scans of a rare *Tempest* misprint.

### Friday

*   **Latest Developments: [Out of the Shadows](/en/articles/latestdevelopments/2002/12-20-out-of-the-shadows), by Randy Buehler**. Why Randy believes that shadow was the worst **Magic** mechanic ever.
*   **Feature: [*Tempest* Storyboard](https://web.archive.org/web/20021216000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/tempest-storyboard-2002-12-20)**. Reprinted from Duelist #20, the entire *Tempest* story as portrayed in its artwork.
*   **Arcana: [Squee's Resolve](https://web.archive.org/web/20021216000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/squees-resolve-2002-12-20)**. The hidden Squee on [Hero's Resolve](https://web.archive.org/web/20021216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Resolve).
