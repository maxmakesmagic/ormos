With nearly a quarter of the field in Mono Black Devotion, it is clear that most players are happy to play with the most powerful cards in the format, mostly consisting of [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) and [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon). However, it is important to note that the five-drop of choice, i.e. [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel), is what makes this deck different from Orzhov Midrange variants With [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) and to a lesser extent [Whip of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos), It is not uncommon to drain your opponent's life by four or six, especially when [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) is on the battlefield churning out an endless supply of cards for the black mage. 

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Sphinx%27s%20Revelation)

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Thoughtseize)

Esper Control is the next most popular archetype, pretty close to what most players would expect. The allure of playing with [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) and hand disruption spells is difficult to resist. 

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Boros%20Charm)

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Skullcrack)

Hoping to prey on the sea of control decks are 17 players, who have chosen to run the blistering quick Boros Burn deck, hoping to end games quickly with a flurry of burn spells and preventing life any life gain with [Skullcrack](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullcrack)s. 

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Polukranos,%20World%20Eater)

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Rakdos%27s%20Return)

Red Green Monster decks are also present in the metagame, but players have evolved the existing archetype to include black for [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), [Rakdos's Return](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos%27s+Return) and assorted spot removal spells like [Dreadbore](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dreadbore) and/or [Doom Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doom+Blade). 

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Thassa,%20God%20of%20the%20Sea)

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Detention%20Sphere)

Mono Blue Devotion seems to have taken a turn for the worst and players are opting to splash [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) and [Ephara, God of the Polis](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ephara%2C+God+of+the+Polis). 

Rounding off the rest of the field are an assortment of rather unique decks. A quad of Ephara & Friends, a midrange Esper deck popularized by Makihito Mihara, has made it to Day two, as are an assortment of green-based aggro decks. The most interesting deck probably goes to Ken Yukuhiro, who is armed with a Blue Black Heroic aggro deck, featuring [Pain Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pain+Seer), [Hidden Strings](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hidden+Strings), [Triton Tactics](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triton+Tactics) and [Agent of the Fates](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Agent+of+the+Fates)!
