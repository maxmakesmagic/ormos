Nissa first appeared in the original *Zendikar* block. We've seen [some of her adventures since then](/en/articles/magic-story/2014/06-25-nissa-worldwaker), but her origin story is told in full for the [first time today](https://web.archive.org/web/20161014062324/http://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/uncharted-realms/nissa%E2%80%99s-origin-home-2015-07-08). Nissa has been a relatively shy Planeswalker until now, appearing on very few cards and pieces of art—but all of that changes with *Magic Origins*.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_NissaRevane.jpg)

[Nissa Revane](https://web.archive.org/web/20150708000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa+Revane) | Art by [Jaime Jones](https://web.archive.org/web/20150708000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Jaime+Jones%22%5D)

When we first met Nissa, she was primarily concerned with her tribe on Zendikar.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_NissasExpedition.jpg)

[Nissa's Expedition](https://web.archive.org/web/20150708000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%27s+Expedition) | Art by [Dan Scott](https://web.archive.org/web/20150708000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Dan+Scott%22%5D)

Since then, she has journeyed quite a bit.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_AnimistsAwakening.jpg)

[Animist's Awakening](https://web.archive.org/web/20150708000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Animist%27s+Awakening) | Art by [Chris Rahn](https://web.archive.org/web/20150708000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Chris+Rahn%22%5D)

She went through Zendikar . . .

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_NissasPilgrimage.jpg)

[Nissa's Pilgrimage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150708000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%27s+Pilgrimage) | Art by [Matt Stewart](https://web.archive.org/web/20150708000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Matt+Stewart%22%5D)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_NissasRevelation.jpg)
