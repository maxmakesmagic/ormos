Looking to explore a brand new format? **Magic Online** players have begun to try out *Innistrad* Block Constructed! With only one set in the mix it's both accessible and interesting as players try and do the most they can with the limited card pool. Over the weekend, Nukesaku took down a Premier Event with this decklist, featuring mana symbols from all five colors among its spells! What does this mean for *Innistrad* Block and the future of Standard? Try out the format now and begin to find out for yourself!

Nukesaku's Block Five-Color Control 

Innistrad Block Constructed – Premier Event on Magic Online
