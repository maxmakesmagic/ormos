The rare *Odyssey* artifact Charmed Pendant is one of a handful of cards that uses an example in its reminder text. The Pendant states that if you reveal a card that costs ![3 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/7f/5d/7f5d2f1e55eaa9d19a9a23027b63bfbb5b39dbf8642160dda1a477a03df23a6a.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Black Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/6b/e16bcf75cffdd1079b6ba1f9f43c9e6e7253bb89521acdf6b3e93d3c2e63dbb5.gif), you add ![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Black Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/6b/e16bcf75cffdd1079b6ba1f9f43c9e6e7253bb89521acdf6b3e93d3c2e63dbb5.gif) to your mana pool.

So what card is referred to in the example? Editing wanted to use a real, unique card when they were devising the example, so they chose the only card with a mana cost listed as ![3 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/7f/5d/7f5d2f1e55eaa9d19a9a23027b63bfbb5b39dbf8642160dda1a477a03df23a6a.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Black Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/6b/e16bcf75cffdd1079b6ba1f9f43c9e6e7253bb89521acdf6b3e93d3c2e63dbb5.gif) -- Spinal Embrace.

One other old card has a functionally identical mana cost: *Legends'* Princess Lucrezia's cost is written as ![3 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/7f/5d/7f5d2f1e55eaa9d19a9a23027b63bfbb5b39dbf8642160dda1a477a03df23a6a.gif)![Black Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/6b/e16bcf75cffdd1079b6ba1f9f43c9e6e7253bb89521acdf6b3e93d3c2e63dbb5.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif). Back then, costs didn't follow the same rules they do now. Currently, all multi-colored mana costs are ordered clockwise around the color wheel. In the [Oracle reference](https://web.archive.org/web/20020312000000/http://www.wizards.com/dci/main.asp?x=oracle), Princess Lucrezia's mana cost appears in the current correct form -- identical to Spinal Embrace's ![3 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/7f/5d/7f5d2f1e55eaa9d19a9a23027b63bfbb5b39dbf8642160dda1a477a03df23a6a.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Blue Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/5d/b75de4f4cec208d24e23ce112a4bb89540d55aa95fe1ef377d1c7c5d2b6bdb50.gif)![Black Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/6b/e16bcf75cffdd1079b6ba1f9f43c9e6e7253bb89521acdf6b3e93d3c2e63dbb5.gif).
