### Flavor text voting - batch 1

Now that our *Onslaught* previews are over, we can get back to the business at hand… Selecting *Eighth Edition*! (Haven't been following along? Review the Selecting *Eighth Edition* archive [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20031004190424/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/columnarchive&column=selectingeighthedition).)

We have a couple of steps left. One is voting on the submitted flavor text for six cards, and the other is voting on which basic land art will appear in the set. This week we'll tackle half of the first part.

The three card's we'll be voting on are Aladdin's Ring, Orcish Artillery, and Persecute. All of the entries below were submitted by players. Our staff chose the best ones, and those are the options. Persecute is special in that we asked for real-world quotes.

So read them over and vote for your favorite! The winners will end up on the actual *Eighth Edition* cards!
