Curious what the world of New Extended looks like? Check out this result from a recent event starring the format on **Magic Online**. Naemen managed to go 3-1 with this white and red beatdown deck. It features some powerful attackers in [Figure of Destiny](https://web.archive.org/web/20100729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Figure+of+Destiny), [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20100729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide), and [Keldon Marauders](https://web.archive.org/web/20100729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Keldon+Marauders). And check out that volley of burn: [Burst Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20100729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burst+Lightning), [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20100729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt), [Incinerate](https://web.archive.org/web/20100729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Incinerate), [Searing Blaze](https://web.archive.org/web/20100729000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blaze), and more! While the format may have changed, the beatdown remains!
