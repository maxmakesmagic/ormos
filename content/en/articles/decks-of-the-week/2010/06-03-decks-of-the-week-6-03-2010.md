Congratulations to Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa, your newest Pro Tour champion. Paulo made his sixth Top 8 at [Pro Tour-San Juan](https://web.archive.org/web/20100603000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/paulo-celebrates-six-beach-san-juan) thanks to an 8-2 record with his Blue-Red-Green *Zendikar* Block Constructed deck and his 5-1 record in Booster Draft. Check out the Block Constructed decks of each player who recorded at least 18 points over the 10 rounds of Swiss, along with the Top 8 Booster Draft decks and a few San Juan Public Event deck lists from the weekend as well.
