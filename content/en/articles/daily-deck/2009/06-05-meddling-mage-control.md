Meddling Mage was one of the most exciting cards released in *Alara Reborn*, and David Daniel has made good on using the powerful 2/2 in Standard! He earned a Regionals Top 8 in Albuquerque with a Blue-White control strategy. The deck drops Meddling Mage into play, then hides behind the 2/2 while protecting itself with Path to Exile, Negate, and Remove Soul. You can even use Peek to make sure you lock down cards in the opponent's hand with the Mage! No doubt Chris Pikula would be proud...
