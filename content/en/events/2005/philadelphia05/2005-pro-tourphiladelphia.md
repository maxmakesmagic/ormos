### regMandatory Registration

Player registration will occur at the tournament site **4pm-8pm on Thursday, May 5th**.

Players must have a signed [waiver and consent form](http://www.wizards.com/dci/downloads/Consent_Form_Philly_2005.pdf) to compete at Pro Tour Philadelphia. Forms can be downloaded at **wizards.com/protour** and will be available onsite. Minors must have a parent or guardian’s signature on these forms.

Bring these forms with you to registration. We will make forms available for competitors at registration, if necessary.

**At registration, all participants must present one of the following forms of identification upon request:**

*   a driver’s license, or
*   a passport, or
*   a copy of their birth certificates *and* photo IDs (school IDs are acceptable).

If you anticipate any difficulty registering during the official registration hours, please contact Andy Heckt at (425) 254-2941 or andy.heckt@wizards.com.

### formatFormat and Schedule

*All Universal Tournament Rules and Magic: the Gathering Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document*

Note: The prize structure for this event differs from the normal payout. See the "Pro Tour - Philadelphia Prize Payout" for details

**Format**

*   *Champions of Kamigawa* Block Constructed format
*   Check [www.thedci.com](http://www.thedci.com/) for Block Constructed format rules.

**Round Length**

*   12 Swiss Rounds total plus Top 8 Single-elimination finals, 75 minutes per round
*   Top 8 single-elimination finals have no time limit
*   When time is called, the active player will finish the turn and five additional turns will be taken
*   Stalling and slow play rules will be strictly enforced during the tournament.

**Advancement**

*   Players play all Swiss rounds until they receive their third match loss or draw, at which time they will be automatically be dropped from the tournament.
*   Match draws will count as losses for purposes of elimination. Draws will not count as losses for ratings.
*   Players will receive 3 points for a match win, 0 points for a draw or loss.
*   Byes, if necessary, will be awarded to a random player in *highest* score group (the players with the most match points) that has not already received a bye in the tournament.
*   The top 8 players after round 12 will advance to Sunday’s top 8 single-elimination finals.

**Top 8**

*   Top 8 matches will be best 3 of 5
*   Where necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will still be used to determine final order in the standings.

**Notes**

*   48K, REL 5
*   Players are responsible for knowing all DCI rules and [high-level tournament rules](http://www.wizards.com/protour/images/HighlevelRules.pdf).
*   Players must bring pen, paper, card sleeves and appropriate counters.
