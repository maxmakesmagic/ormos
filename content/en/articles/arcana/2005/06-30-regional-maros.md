Top 8 decklists for the 2005 Regionals tournaments [are up](/en/events/2005/regionals05/2005-regionals-2); go check them out.

You'll note that the contributions from *Saviors of Kamigawa* to the Standard scene so far isn't just the flexible Pithing Needle and the occasional Arashi. [Alex Etzel](https://web.archive.org/web/20050630000000/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgevent/regionals05/phoenix) ran Shifting Borders in his Monoblue 'Tron deck to meddle with Boseijus and opposing Urzatrons. [Clay Pierce](https://web.archive.org/web/20050630000000/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgevent/regionals05/duluth) ran Sunder from Within to complement his land destruction strategy, and [Arthur Morris](https://web.archive.org/web/20050630000000/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgevent/regionals05/arlington) qualified for Nationals on Infernal Kirin's contribution to his Monoblack control deck, to name a few.

Hand size matters, all right, and in almost opposite ways for these two \*/\* fatties. [Simon Cadorette](https://web.archive.org/web/20050630000000/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgevent/regionals05/montreal) Q'd in Montreal on the back of Kagemaro's board-clearing addition to his G/B Rock deck, as did [Bryan Chu](https://web.archive.org/web/20050630000000/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgevent/regionals05/burnaby) in Burnaby, B.C. with his innovative U/B control deck.

[Ross Freeman and Gabriel Wilson](https://web.archive.org/web/20050630000000/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgevent/regionals05/portland), on the other hand, punished their opponents' slow draws with a fast-out-of-the-gate Adamaro, both qualifying in Portland with Red Deck Wins.

Congratulations to all the Standard warriors who competed, placed and qualified at [2005 Regionals](/en/events/2005/regionals05/2005-regionals-2)!
