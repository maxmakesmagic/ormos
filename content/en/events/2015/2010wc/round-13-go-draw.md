### Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa (Five-Color Control) vs. Guillaume Wafo-Tapa (Five-Color Control)

Coming into the first round of individual competition on Saturday, both Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa and Guillaume Wafo-Tapa were sitting comfortably on 10-2. With six rounds to go, they both realistically had wiggle room for a couple of losses, but not more than that. With that in mind, both players had apparently opted to field strategically similar decks. In this case, Five-Color Control. You’ll have to bear with me, because the grueling nature of this control-on-control battle does not lend itself to a detailed play by play.

**Game 1**

Damo da Rosa rolled well to start the game, but Wafo-Tapa trumped his 10 with an 11, and rushed a main phase [Esper Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Esper+Charm) through while only one of Damo da Rosa’s lands were untapped. Five-Color Control decks being notably mana hungry, keeping up a continuous flow of extra card is important to the deck, and especially important in the mirror.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/worlds10/r13_damodarosa_wafo-tapa.jpg)  
*Guillaume Wafo-Tapa and Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa settle in for a well-spectated control-on-control match-up.*

A few turns later, Wafo-Tapa landed a [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor), after baiting Damo da Rosa with a [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command) during his end of turn step. The players traded Jaces for a few turns, but Wafo-Tapa had drawn three of them to Damo da Rosa’s two, and after checking the top of his deck once, to take the planeswalker out of [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) range, he [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm)ed repeatedly, until Damo da Rosa was but a speck of dust in his rear view mirror. From there, he began fatesealing Damo da Rosa to ensure he wasn’t going to find a way back into the game.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/worlds10/r13_wafo-tapa.jpg)  
*Wafo-Tapa’s name has become synonymous with control.*

As Jace neared his Ultimate, Damo da Rosa finally summoned a [Wurmcoil Engine](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wurmcoil+Engine), and after a [Volcanic Fallout](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Volcanic+Fallout) during Wafo-Tapa’s upkeep, and another later in his turn, the board was suddenly clear of the many copies of [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) that had congregated around the middle of the table. The Engine dropped Jace to 5 loyalty, but Jace simply bounced it during Wafo-Tapa’s turn, who followed it up with a [Cruel Ultimatum](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cruel+Ultimatum) with [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command) back up, to clear out Damo da Rosa’s hand. Wafo-Tapa then summoned a [Sunblast Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sunblast+Angel) and started attacking. A few turns later, they were done with Game 1 ....

Wafo-Tapa 1, Damo da Rosa 0

...Which had taken nearly 28 of the 50 minutes allocated to a round, and as they sideboarded, Damo da Rosa set a timer on his trademarked trusty timepiece with a frown.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/worlds10/r13_damodarosa.jpg)  
*Paulo Vitor keeps an eye on the clock.*

**Game 2**

Damo da Rosa led with a [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), his deck now optimized for a control mirror. Many, many turns later, when Wafo-Tapa tried to summon Jace, Damo da Rosa attacked his hand again, this time with a surprise [Vendilion Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vendilion+Clique), denying Wafo-Tapa the [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command) he’d intended to use to protect the Jace. Damo da Rosa countered it with a [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command) of his own, and untapped, summoning his own Jace and fatesealing Wafo-Tapa to keep him off an answer for the planeswalker. Several turns later, after Wafo-Tapa’s [Cruel Ultimatum](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cruel+Ultimatum) had met with another [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command), the match was evened at one game apiece.

Wafo-Tapa 1, Damo da Rosa 1

As they shuffled quickly for a third game, Wafo-Tapa asked how much time was left. When the answer returned an improbably small figure, both players shook hands with a shrug and a smile.

Final result: Wafo-Tapa 1, Damo da Rosa 1 (DRAW)
