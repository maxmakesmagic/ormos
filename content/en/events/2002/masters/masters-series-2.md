The Masters Series is an invitation-only tournament series that showcases the top players from the Pro Tour circuit in single-elimination competition with large cash prizes. Masters Series tournaments are held approximately four times a year in both individual and team formats. They are run at REL 5, require a level 4 head judge, and are DCI sanctioned with a K-value of 48. They are invitation-only tournaments.

**Invitation Criteria**  
 Each individual-format Masters Series invitation list comprises the following:

*   The winner of the previous season's Masters Series event of the closest format.
*   The Top 27 previously uninvited players as of the most recent pro standing preceding the Masters Series event. Pro point ties are broken using DCI Composite ratings as of the Wednesday following the Pro Tour event that precedes the Masters Series event.
*   The Top 4 finishers of the Gateway tournament preceding each Masters Series event.

**MASTERS SERIES GATEWAY TOURNAMENTS**  
 Each Masters Series event is preceded by a Gateway tournament. Gateway tournaments offer invitations to specific Masters Series events. Gateway tournaments use the single-elimination format. They are run at REL 4, require a level 3 head judge, and are DCI sanctioned with a K-value of 40. They are invitation-only tournaments.

**Invitation Criteria**  
 Each individual-format Gateway tournament invitation list comprises the following:

*   Any player with 7 or more pro points as of the most recent pro standing preceding the Gateway tournament (see below).
*   Any player with a DCI rating of 1900 or higher in the most appropriate format, as of the publish six weeks prior (see dates below).
