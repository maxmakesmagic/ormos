Looking for a fun way to use Mass Polymorph? The powerful new Magic 2011 card was the perfect win condition for Zac Hill's white-blue deck at the Champion Challenge in Atlanta, Georgia. Using Martial Coup and Elspeth, Knight-Errant, he could generate a few Soldier tokens. That meant he could then cast the Mass Polymorph to hunt up Iona, Shield of Emeria, Stormtide Leviathan, and Emrakul, the Aeons Torn. From there, winning was no doubt easy!
