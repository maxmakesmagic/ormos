When *Journey into Nyx* was released, [Mana Confluence](https://web.archive.org/web/20140522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Confluence) was hailed as the missing ingredient for multicolored aggressive decks. Now that we have a few weeks of tournaments, it would appear that [Mana Confluence](https://web.archive.org/web/20140522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Confluence) has indeed given a boost to these multicolored aggressive decks! Jamie Arnold took his Naya Aggro deck to a finals appearance at [StarCityGames Knoxville](http://www.starcitygames.com/events/100514_knoxville.html).

![Brave the Elements](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Brave%20the%20Elements&options=)This is a base-white deck, partially to take advantage of white's strong creature suite, but mostly to take advantage of [Brave the Elements](https://web.archive.org/web/20140522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brave+the+Elements). Multicolored cards, such as [Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion) and [Voice of Resurgence](https://web.archive.org/web/20140522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Voice+of+Resurgence), are potent creatures, but even tougher to handle when they are backed up by a variety of support spells. [Ghor-Clan Rampager](https://web.archive.org/web/20140522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghor-Clan+Rampager) helps boost any attacking creature over a blocker, where [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm) is often used to save your creatures from a card like [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict). However, the most potent combo is when [Ghor-Clan Rampager](https://web.archive.org/web/20140522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghor-Clan+Rampager) and [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm) are combined for what is usually a lethal attack.

While maintaining an aggressive curve, this deck has a lot of tricks up its sleeve and lots of options. Both [Selesnya Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Selesnya+Charm) and [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm) provide many different options, and many of the deck's creatures are hard to remove.
