As we [announced here](https://web.archive.org/web/20120727000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/918) (and reminded people at San Diego Comic-Con), *From the Vault: Realms* will be released on August 31, 2012 with an MSRP of $34.99. It will contain fifteen powerful lands from the history of **Magic**. And here's a look at the box!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/47/33/473393033e7cefd6bb220e3f4d2bcf96b0e7514960678bd7d4d1604e7fdfd8f8.jpg)
