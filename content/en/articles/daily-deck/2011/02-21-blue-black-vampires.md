Mono-black and black-red have been popular color combinations for Vampire decks over the past few years in Standard. For American Christian Calcano, working with a group of European players to come up with something a bit more unique led to the addition of the color blue. With this new take on the deck, Calcano and company can rely on Unified Will to counter spells while Creeping Tar Pit attacks unblocked. For more information on Pro Tour Paris, where Calcano debuted this version of the deck, visit [here](/en/events/2011/ptpar11/stark-flies-high-with-planeswalkers-in-paris).
