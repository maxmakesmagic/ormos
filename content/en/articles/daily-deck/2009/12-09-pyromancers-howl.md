Standard has seen Pyromancer Ascension decks and it has seen Howling Mine decks, but Naoki Sakaguchi, the Japanese National Team's alternate, combined both for his Worlds Standard deck. With Howling Mine making sure he always drew plenty of fuel, his Pyromancer Ascension was always maxed out on effect. Time Warp, Lightning Bolt, Worldly Counsel, whatever spell he wanted to cast—he got a free copy to add insult to injury!
