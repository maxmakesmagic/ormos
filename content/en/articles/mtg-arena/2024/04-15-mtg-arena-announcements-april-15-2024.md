## *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Launches This Week!

![Outlaws of Thunder Junction logo](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/en_logo_otj_ub26hik88xxb.png) 

There's less than a day left to [get your *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* preorders in](https://pex8.adj.st/store/?adj_t=nr03woc&adj_deep_link=mtga%3A%2F%2Fplay.mtgarena.com%2Fstore%2F%3Fmtg_campaign%3DArena_MTGArena_OTJ_Daily%26mtg_adgroup%3Dwizards-web-otj&mtg_campaign=Arena_MTGArena_OTJ_Daily&mtg_adgroup=wizards-web-otj&adj_fallback=https%3A%2F%2Fmagic.wizards.com%2Fen%2Fmtgarena%2Fpreorder%3Futm_source%3Dwizards%26utm_medium%3Dweb%26utm_campaign%3Dmtgarena-otj-preorder&adj_redirect_macos=https%3A%2F%2Fmagic.wizards.com%2Fen%2Fmtgarena%2Fpreorder%3Futm_source%3Dwizards%26utm_medium%3Dweb%26utm_campaign%3Dmtgarena-otj-preorder&adj_redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fmagic.wizards.com%2Fen%2Fmtgarena%2Fpreorder%3Futm_source%3Dwizards%26utm_medium%3Dweb%26utm_campaign%3Dmtgarena-otj-preorder), because the new set arrives tomorrow, April 16!

Get to know the cards in the set with the complete [*Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20240415000000/https://magic.wizards.com/products/outlaws-of-thunder-junction/card-image-gallery).

Then, get a sample of the new set by playing in this week's [*Jump Into Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Midweek *Magic* event](https://web.archive.org/web/20240415000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/announcements/midweek-magic), April 16–18! There's no fee to enter, you can start playing fast, and you can pick up some prizes!

Finally, get your fingers limbered up, because the *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Premier Draft opens tomorrow, April 16, after the release. [Build your season ranking](https://magic.gg/events/ranked-play-season), win prizes, and score [cool card styles](#rankedseason) each season.

## Release Notes for *Outlaws of Thunder Junction*

There are a couple cool features to talk about coming to *MTG Arena* with the release of *Outlaws of Thunder Junction*.

The first is a batch of new phrases! As [part of the *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Mastery Pass](https://web.archive.org/web/20240415000000/https://magic.wizards.com/news/mtg-arena/outlaws-of-thunder-junction-mastery-details), you can add ten new Western-inspired phrases to your emotes repertoire.

We're also adding helpful visual feedback when creatures with reach are on the battlefield. Say goodbye to mistakenly flying into sneaky archers, spiders, and others with reach!

![Battlefield with translucent blue bow and nocked arrow image over the card Freestrider Lookout with the reach ability](https://media.wizards.com/2024/images/daily/screenshot_reach_alert.webp)

## Competitive Play Schedule

All times listed are Pacific time (UTC-07:00).

### Premier Play

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events.

Qualifier tokens are delivered to your *MTG Arena* inbox. Remember to claim them before the event starts!

#### May

*   **Qualifier Play-In (Best-of-One)**
     May 4, 6 a.m. PT

*   Format: *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Limited

*   **Qualifier Play-In (Best-of-Three)**
     May 10, 6 a.m. PT–May 11, 3 a.m. PT

*   Format: *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Limited

*   **Qualifier Weekend**
     May 11, 6 a.m. PT–May 12, 4 p.m. PT

*   Format: *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Limited

### Arena Open

The Arena Open Day One entry window begins at 6 a.m. PT and closes to new entries the following day at 3 a.m. PT (UTC-07:00). The Day Two entry window is 2 hours only, from 6 a.m. PT until 8 a.m. PT (UTC-07:00).

*   **Arena Open: *Outlaws of Thunder Junction***
    *   May 4: Day One, *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Sealed (Best-of-One and Best-of-Three)
    *   May 5: Day Two, *Outlaws of Thunder Junction* Draft (Best-of-Three)
