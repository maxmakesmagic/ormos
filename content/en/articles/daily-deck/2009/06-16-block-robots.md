Fears Naya and Jund would run Pro Tour–Honolulu rampant proved to be unfounded. Brian Kibler and Paul Rietzl both managed to make the Top 8 with an innovative Esper deck that focused on abusing Thopter Foundry to get around the prime removal available in the *Shards of Alara* Block Constructed format. For more about Pro Tour–Honolulu, including all the top decklists, [read the official event coverage](/en/events/2009/pthon09/mitamura-finds-gold-in-paradise).
