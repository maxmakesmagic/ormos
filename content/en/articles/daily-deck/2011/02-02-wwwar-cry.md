White Weenie? Oh yes! The new Standard welcomes back some old favorites. R&D member Zac Hill took this version of the new deck to spellsling at a Prerelease in New York. It stars a host of powerful new threats like Accorder Paladin, Mirran Crusader, and Hero of Bladehold, all from *Mirrodin Besieged*! And did you know you can [follow Zac on Twitter](http://www.twitter.com/ZDCH)? It's true!
