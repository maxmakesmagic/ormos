You sit down to play at a [Grand Prix Minneapolis](https://web.archive.org/web/20140521000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/parks-great-scape-minneapolis), and your opponent is none other than then-reigning [Pro Tour Champion Shaun McLaren](https://web.archive.org/web/20140521000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/mclaren-weaves-victory-valencia). You have him pegged as playing the same deck he won the Pro Tour with, a Red-White-Blue Control deck featuring lots of removal and card drawing. An [Electrolyze](https://web.archive.org/web/20140521000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrolyze) here, a [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20140521000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) there, a [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140521000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) everywhere, and you've got Shaun figured out. You've tested this matchup, and you know how you're going to defeat a Pro Tour Champion. 

![Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Kiki-Jiki%2C+Mirror+Breaker)

"At the end of your turn, [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140521000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel)." 

Interesting. Shaun didn't play [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140521000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel) at Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*! This is a pretty reasonable card to play, and you'll have to be on your toes for the rest of the match. 

"Untap, draw, play Kiki-Jiki, copy [Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140521000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel), blink Kiki-Jiki." 

Oh no, you've been had.

Don't worry, you're not alone. Shaun defeated many players in this way on his way to a semifinals appearance at Grand Prix Minneapolis. Adding a combo element into his control deck gave his potent Pro Tour–winning deck a new dimension and kept his opponents on their toes all tournament long.
