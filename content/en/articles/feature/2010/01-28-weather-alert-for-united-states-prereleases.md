Because of the developing winter storm moving across much of the United States, Wizards of the Coast recommends that all players check with their local organizers for any possible delays or postponements for this weekend's [*Worldwake* Prerelease events](/en/events/2008/prerelease-facts/worldwide-prerelease-tournaments).

The [Regional Prerelease](/en/events/2009/prerelease-regional/alara-reborn-worldwide-regional-prerelease-schedule) scheduled for Richmond, Virginia has been cancelled.
