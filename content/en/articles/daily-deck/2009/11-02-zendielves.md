If you thought Elves in Standard were a thing of the past with the rotation of *Lorwyn* and *Shadowmoor*, think again! Pedestrian used this list successfully on **Magic Online**. Check out the powerful interactions with Nissa Revane and the way Oran-Rief, the Vastwood makes an army of tiny monsters into a far more imposing force. Toss in Great Sable Stag and a touch of white for the sideboard, and you've got a brand new Standard masterpiece!
