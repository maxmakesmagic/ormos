[プロツアー・ホノルル予選　日本語の案内](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603145841/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=protour/honolulu06/qualifiers-japan,,ja) 

### Entry Fee

The entry fee for all Pro Tour—Honolulu Qualifier tournaments in Japan is ¥2,000.

### Format

*   The format for all Pro Tour—Honolulu Qualifiers tournaments is Extended Constructed. For information on this format, please refer to the DCI Universal Tournament Rule and **Magic** Floor Rules documents on the [DCI Document Center](http://www.thedci.com/docs).
*   Deck lists are required.

### Tournament Structure

*   The structure for all Pro Tour—Honolulu Qualifier tournaments will be modified Swiss-style, with the Top 8 players playing in a single-elimination round for prizes and invitations.
*   Each round in the Swiss portion of the tournament will be 50 minutes.
*   The number of Swiss rounds will be determined based on attendance according to the following chart:

### Advancement

*   All players may participate in all Swiss rounds.
*   The top 8 players after the final Swiss round will advance to the Top 8 single-elimination finals.

### Top 8 Finals

*   All Top 8 matches at Pro Tour—Honolulu Qualifier tournaments will be best 2 of 3.
*   Top 8 matches will have no time limit.
*   When necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will be used to determine final order in the standings.

### Notes

*   All Pro Tour—Honolulu Qualifier tournaments are run at Rules Enforcement Level 3, and are 32K.
*   All Universal Tournament Rules and **Magic: The Gathering** Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this format document.
*   Players are responsible for bringing their own pen, paper, card sleeves, and appropriate counters.

### Invitations and Eligibility

Players already holding an invitation to Pro Tour—Honolulu are prohibited from participating in any Pro Tour—Honolulu Qualifier tournaments, with the exception of Grand Prix tournaments. Players may have earned invitations through standing in the Pro Player's Club, winning a previous Qualifier or Grand Prix tournament, or by being an invited DCI player (valid upon the date of the official ratings invitation announcement for the appropriate Pro Tour). Please refer to the [Pro Tour—Honolulu Fact Sheet](/en/events/2005/honolulu06/2006-pro-tourhonolulu) for a list of qualified players.

### Prizes

*   The winning player of each Pro Tour—Honolulu Qualifier tournament will receive an invitation to the Pro Tour and round-trip airfare from his/her closest international airport to the Pro Tour site.
*   Product prizes to be determined by the organizer.

### Registration Information

Players who want more specific local tournament or registration information should contact their local organizer.
