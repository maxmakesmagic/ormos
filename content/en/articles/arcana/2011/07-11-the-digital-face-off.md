What's the Digital Face-Off, you ask?

  
 ![](https://i.ormos.uk/c9/70/c97060f037c53e0392c55df342be846c318a447a5b4b1efe067959992a804888.jpg)

We'll give the basics here, but there's [complete information](https://web.archive.org/web/20110711000000/http://community.wizards.com/magicthegathering/blog/2011/07/07/digital_face_off) available. Basically, eight teams from big digital gaming companies will battle each other in **Duels of the Planeswalkers** 2012. And you (yes, *you*!) get to play along by predicting who will win.
