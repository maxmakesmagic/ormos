A*dolmen* is a construction of enormous stones, usually with two or more vertical stones supporting another, horizontal stone, like a table.

[![Dolmen Gate](https://i.ormos.uk/12/7c/127c6f1f84b2952765a76f1877b1fe50e0af5be6add4df41c52b75654d700956.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071115000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Dolmen%2BGate) *[Dolmen Gate](https://web.archive.org/web/20071115000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dolmen+Gate) art by Richard Sardinha*

Dolmens can be found all over the plane of Lorwyn; they are a favorite architectural technique of giants and other creatures. Check out the art of [Ancient Amphitheater](https://web.archive.org/web/20071115000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancient+Amphitheater) and [Lairwatch Giant](https://web.archive.org/web/20071115000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lairwatch+Giant), as well as [Dolmen Gate](https://web.archive.org/web/20071115000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dolmen+Gate).

![Ancient Amphitheater](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ancient+Amphitheater)

![Lairwatch Giant](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Lairwatch+Giant)

Giants also construct "dolmens" from fallen trees. Note the art of [Axegrinder Giant](https://web.archive.org/web/20071115000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Axegrinder+Giant)—the front door of his lair has three trees in the same pattern as a dolmen.

[![Axegrinder Giant](https://i.ormos.uk/1d/b2/1db2d6dab91b2a466ffdf664fb645fd8f2c60dcdf228e1a5fe36397aec5ba780.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071115000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Axegrinder%2BGiant) *[Axegrinder Giant](https://web.archive.org/web/20071115000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Axegrinder+Giant) art by Warren Mahy*

Dolmens can also be found on Earth! The Lorwyn setting was inspired by the myths and folklore of the British Isles, and some of the world's best-known dolmens are in Ireland. The word "dolmen" comes from a Breton phrase meaning "stone table," but variants on the dolmen stone tomb construction can be found all over the world.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d4/02/d402b99d8067cef2bb06ea39eb7a5a605573b8c3608dc812c1775d9e7405767b.jpg)*Knocknakilla Dolmen on the NW upper slopes of Musherabeg mountain, between Macroom and Millstreet, County Cork, Ireland.*

Check out [Wikipedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolmen) for more information on dolmens.
