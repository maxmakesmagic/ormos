Longtime **Magic Online** player Dracc0n put his own spin on Modern control in a recent Premier Event with an [Isochron Scepter](https://web.archive.org/web/20120130000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Isochron+Scepter) control deck! With a wide array of control elements and several utility spells to put on a Scepter, Dracc0n sliced through the competition and took down the tournament. Could this be a new force in Modern? Check out the deck for yourself!
