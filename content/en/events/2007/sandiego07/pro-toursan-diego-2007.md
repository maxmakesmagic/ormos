San Diego, California is the third stop in the 2007 Pro Tour season. But not only the Pros will be enjoying themselves: the Pro Tour is above all a great weekend of **Magic** that brings you fantastic opportunities. 

Pro Tour–San Diego is a Two-Headed Giant *Time Spiral* Booster Draft event that runs June 29-July 1, 2007. The Last-Chance Qualifier will be run on Thursday, June 28 at the event site, starting at 5 p.m. Registration for the LCQ begins at 3 p.m.

### Tournament Format and Schedule

Pro Tour–San Diego is a three-day Two-Headed Giant Booster Draft format event with a prize purse of $240,500. For more information about the format, schedule and prize payout, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20130628201341/https://www.wizards.com/Default.asp?x=mtgcom/protour/sandiego07-format).

### Site Attractions

**SIDE EVENTS**  
Play **Magic** all weekend long, even if you're not qualified for the Pro Tour! Information on all the events being held at Pro Tour-San Diego can be found in the [Side Event Schedule](/en/events/2007/sandiego07-sides/2007-pro-tour-san-diego-side-events-schedule).

**ARTISTS ON SITE**  
 Pro Tour–San Diego 2007 will feature appearances by Magic artists Lars Grant-West and Alan Pollack! [Click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20130628201341/https://www.wizards.com/Default.asp?x=mtgcom/protour/sandiego07-artists) to read more about these artists. 

**WATCH THE TOP 8**  
 All of Sunday's Top 8 action can be viewed on a projection screen in the hall, with Randy Buehler and Brian David-Marshall providing the play-by-play and color commentary. Sunday's action begins at 2 p.m. local time, which is 5 p.m. ET, and is available as a [webcast](/en/events/2004/webcast/magic-the-gathering-webcast-schedule) from the comfort of your own home.
