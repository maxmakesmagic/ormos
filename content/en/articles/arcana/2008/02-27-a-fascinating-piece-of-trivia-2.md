Let's play a game! We'll name one of the *Morningtide* classes and you guess how many cards' flavor texts that class has ever appeared in. For example, if we said "frog", the correct answer would be "five" (which counts both -mites and -tossers. Like this:

![Mudbutton Torchrunner](https://i.ormos.uk/61/17/61171ec006462cbb615fbf5ef14f3af4a9ffc0c7d0f08693f57b07de7bf90efe.jpg "Mudbutton Torchrunner") 

Do "Rogue" last, though, because your reward will be... a Fascinating Piece of Trivia!
