The all-foil *Shards of Alara* block booster packs went on sale last Friday (for only $11.99), and we thought it would be a good use of our time to show you exactly what's inside one.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2c/49/2c49950f452a6edf66d3cdaf02eebbe70d65d1d2083b41ebbda83e014543873b.jpg)  

Wait. That's what's *outside* one. When you open this up, you get this:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/82/f2/82f2ebf57b493dfb603e10683854b7d6ddb0c4cb56d714db529b88213ef53e67.jpg)  

For fans of Raymond Swanland and booster packaging, here's a close-up.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2d/5c/2d5cf3500acdf56dcf5f4eafcf595acfdc2e71c0998c99b9473209ad96da3d59.jpg)  

Okay. This is an actual, unretouched booster, Let's crack it open and see what we get!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d1/d1/d1d1c1f58434ed6023a560088d27273704fe3877b74f159b31aae00d03cb1de0.jpg)  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/7b/7d/7b7df80f33ee78c19419779659929548d32808a954a7df52566c10105d19879c.jpg)  
![](https://i.ormos.uk/3e/1c/3e1ca5ac0151f0ecde625f95c8f4641177e588b0e66e2607784bb5404e29da16.jpg)  

Okay. We got a rules tip card (not foil), a basic land, one rare, three uncommons, and ten commons. The rare is [Sovereigns of Lost Alara](https://web.archive.org/web/20100114000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sovereigns+of+Lost+Alara), although that [Bloodbraid Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20100114000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodbraid+Elf) makes the uncommons more interesting. There are eight cards from *Shards of Alara* (counting the [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20100114000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest)), three from *Conflux*, and four from *Alara Reborn*. That's just what's in this pack; contents of packs may vary.

Now for the important question: if this were an all-foil draft, what would we pick? Well, we love Esper, so we're kind of leaning toward the [Sanctum Gargoyle](https://web.archive.org/web/20100114000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sanctum+Gargoyle) and trying for an Esper deck. A *shiny* Esper deck. But it might make more sense to grab the [Rhox Charger](https://web.archive.org/web/20100114000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rhox+Charger). Or the [Bloodbraid Elf](https://web.archive.org/web/20100114000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodbraid+Elf). What's *your* pick?
