*Modern Masters* Weekend is nearly here! You can find a full, detailed breakdown of which GPs you can watch and where over the course of the big weekend below!

* * *

**Day One**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/GP_City_Chiba.png)

The live video coverage of *Modern Masters* Weekend begins with Day One, Round 1 of [Grand Prix Chiba](https://web.archive.org/web/20150522000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/fact-sheet-grand-prix-chiba-2015)'s main event on the [Twitch *Magic* channel](http://www.twitch.tv/magic) at the following times, based on your time zone:

> **Friday at 7 p.m. PT  
>  Friday at 10 p.m. ET  
>  Saturday at 2 a.m. UTC  
>  Saturday at 4 a.m. CET  
>  Saturday at 11 a.m. Local Time (JST)**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/GP_City_Utrecht.png)

While Grand Prix Chiba is going on, [Grand Prix Utrecht](https://web.archive.org/web/20150522000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/fact-sheet-grand-prix-utrecht-2015)coverage will be kicking off over on the [Twitch *Magic*2 channel](http://www.twitch.tv/magic2) at the following times, based on your time zone:

> **Saturday at 2 a.m. PT  
>  Saturday at 5 a.m. ET  
>  Saturday at 9 a.m. UTC  
>  Saturday at 11 a.m. Local Time (CET)  
>  Saturday at 6 p.m. JST**

Grand Prix Chiba will still be streaming when GP Utrecht coverage begins, giving you double the *Magic* to watch for a few hours.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/GP_City_LasVegas.png)

Double the *Magic* is taken to a new level with Vegas. Grand Prix Las Vegas is of such an epic size that it will consist of *two* main events rather than one! You can catch all of the action from the first main event starting with Round 1 on the [Twitch *Magic* channel](http://www.twitch.tv/magic) at the following times:

> **Saturday at 11 a.m. Local Time (PT)  
>  Saturday at 2 p.m. ET  
>  Saturday at 6 p.m. UTC  
>  Saturday at 8 p.m. CET  
>  Sunday at 3 a.m. JST**

Meanwhile, you can catch all of the action of the second GP Vegas main event from our friends at [ChannelFireball](http://www.twitch.tv/channelfireball), where they'll be bringing dedicated coverage of that event all weekend.

* * *

**Day Two**

Day Two of the *Modern Masters* Weekend main events will see a bit of a shift of where each event will air, so pay attention!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/GP_City_Chiba.png)

Day Two of Grand Prix Chiba will stream on the [Twitch *Magic*2 channel](http://www.twitch.tv/magic2) (NOTE: that is different from Day One) at the following times:

> **Saturday at 5 p.m. PT  
>  Saturday at 8 p.m. ET  
>  Sunday at 12 a.m. UTC  
>  Sunday at 2 a.m. CET  
>  Sunday at 9 a.m. Local Time (JST)**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/GP_City_Utrecht.png)

Day Two of Grand Prix Utrecht will stream on the Twitch *Magic* channel after the end of Day One of Grand Prix Vegas. This is estimated to be around the following times:

> **Sunday at 1 a.m. PT  
>  Sunday at 4 a.m. ET  
>  Sundayat 8 a.m. UTC  
>  Sunday at 10 a.m. Local Time (CET)  
>  Sunday at 5 p.m. JST**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/GP_City_LasVegas.png)

Meanwhile, Day Two of Grand Prix Las Vegas will stream on the Twitch *Magic*2 channel after the end of Grand Prix Chiba. This is expected to be around the following times:

> **Sunday at 9 a.m. Local Time (PT)  
>  Sunday at 12 p.m. ET  
>  Sunday at 4 p.m. UTC  
>  Sunday at 6 p.m. CET  
>  Monday at 12 a.m. JST**

As we wind down with the other *Modern Masters* Weekend Grand Prix, you can catch the final few hours of Grand Prix Las Vegas on both Magic Twitch channels.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/Week5_BracketRed.jpg)

The [Standard Super League](http://standardsuperleague.com/) continues on the [*Magic* Twitch Channel](http://www.twitch.tv/magic) every Tuesday at 6 p.m. PT/9 p.m. ET/1 a.m. UTC, but this week's a little special as the players head into the *Modern Masters* Weekend home stretch by busting out their Modern Constructed decks for one week only! Don't miss it!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/daily/openSeriesBanner.png)

And don't miss the latest [StarCityGames.com](http://www.starcitygames.com/) Open Series stop in [Worcester](http://www.starcitygames.com/events/230515_worcester_fact_sheet.html) this weekend, hosted by Patrick Sullivan and Cedric Phillips. Coverage starts Saturday at 7:30 a.m. PT/10:30 a.m. ET/2:30 p.m. UTC, and Day Two begins Sunday at 6 a.m. PT/9 a.m. ET/1 p.m. UTC on the [SCGLive Twitch Channel](http://www.twitch.tv/scglive)!
