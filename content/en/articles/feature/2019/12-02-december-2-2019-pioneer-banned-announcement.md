**Announcement Date**:December 2, 2019

**Pioneer**:

[Once Upon a Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20191202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Once+Upon+a+Time)

[Field of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20191202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Field+of+the+Dead)

[Smuggler's Copter](https://web.archive.org/web/20191202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smuggler%27s+Copter)

The list of all banned cards in Pioneer is [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20191202000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/game-info/gameplay/formats/pioneer).

**Effective Date**:December 3, 2019

**Next Pioneer Banned Announcement**: December 9, 2019

* * *

Over the last several days, a series of Pioneer PTQ tournaments were held as a part of *Magic Online*'s Pioneer week. The results of these tournaments, and data from league play, show how the environment has evolved under further competitive pressure.

While the results look promising and changes so far have been moving the format in a good direction, our data shows three outstanding issues that are preventing the metagame from arriving at a better balance. These are:

*   The strength of Mono-Black Aggro and other aggressive decks featuring [Smuggler's Copter](https://web.archive.org/web/20191202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smuggler%27s+Copter)
*   That [Field of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20191202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Field+of+the+Dead) ramp decks are suppressing controlling and reactive decks
*   The general prevalence and dominance of green decks as a group, with [Once Upon a Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20191202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Once+Upon+a+Time) being a key common factor to those decks' success

As a result, we're choosing to ban [Smuggler's Copter](https://web.archive.org/web/20191202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smuggler%27s+Copter), [Field of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20191202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Field+of+the+Dead), and [Once Upon a Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20191202000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Once+Upon+a+Time) in Pioneer. We believe that these changes will result in greater diversity among aggressive decks, a clearer role in the metagame for reactive and controlling decks, and a push toward better color balance among competitive decks.

This weekly, rapid iteration of the banned list in the early days of Pioneer as an alternative to launching the format with a pre-populated ban list has been an interesting and positive experiment thus far. We've been able to use real-world data and results to arrive at what we believe is a smaller and more accurate ban list that we hope will provide a more fun, dynamic metagame compared to a preemptive approach. Our plan remains to slow the pace of changes as we approach the new year, and we hope to sync up Pioneer changes with the regularly scheduled B&R announcements for all formats starting early next year. After that time, we expect the frequency of changes to Pioneer to be closer to that of other nonrotating formats.
