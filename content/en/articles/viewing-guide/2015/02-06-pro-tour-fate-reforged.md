No time for dawdling today. We've got a Pro Tour broadcast to put on. Which reminds me…

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/LOGO_ProTour_FRF.png)

You've got a Pro Tour to watch! In fact, by the time this article goes live, the Pro Tour will have already begun! So join Rich Hagon, Brian David-Marshall, Randy Buehler, Marshall Sutcliffe, Rashad Miller, Ian Duke, the text coverage team, the video crew, and everyone else who makes the Pro Tour broadcast possible as we bring you three days of non-stop *Magic*.

And as I mentioned, it's live. So [tune in on Twitch](http://www.twitch.tv/Magic) or [check out *all* the coverage](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-fate-reforged) now!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/VG20150206_Week2Standings.jpg)

After the Pro Tour, [Vintage Super League](http://vintagesuperleague.com/) resumes after a one-week hiatus for the big event in Washington, DC, with Week #2 of this second season. The highlight match for this show will be Randy Buehler as he faces off against Eric Froehlich. Coming off of his first week with the very first [turn 1 kill in the league](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2AlvMfksj9c&list=PLQQ8jPMFzt7mK5VpGKGjgjtc0XKjRFSVc&index=4)—kudos to [Tolarian Academy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tolarian+Academy) and [Goblin Charbelcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20150206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Charbelcher) for their performance 20 minutes into the video—will Buehler be able to improve on his performance or will Froehlich be the one to emerge with his undefeated record unscathed?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/VG20150206_FeatureMatchup.jpg)

If you're curious to see what the competitors have digitally sleeved up for the league, you can find their decklists [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/02-25-vintage-super-league-season-2-decklists).

Join host Randy Buehler and the rest of the Vintage Super League competitors for all the exciting action, from the Mox-powered openers to the explosive and iconic [Fastbond](https://web.archive.org/web/20150206000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fastbond)-fueled kill turns. It all starts on the [*Magic* Twitch Channel](http://www.twitch.tv/magic) Tuesday, February 10 at 6 pm. PT/9 p.m. ET/2 a.m. GMT.
