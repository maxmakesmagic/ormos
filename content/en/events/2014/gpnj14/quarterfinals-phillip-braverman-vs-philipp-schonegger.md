Philipp Schönegger is someone you can certainly call an expert at playing a Miracles deck in Legacy. Beginning with Grand Prix Ghent, Schönegger has tooled and retooled his favored Jeskai Miracles deck into a state he called "practically perfected." Built to take advantage of the power of Sensei's Diving Top and [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance) alongside miracle triggers from [Entreat the Angels](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Entreat+the+Angels) and [Terminus](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terminus), a bevy card selection, countermagic, and removal spells helped keep its player alive until the pseudo-lock was online. 

It's a package that put him into the Top 8 of Grand Prix Paris and again here.

Phillip Braverman took a different road to the same deck. He had found success with two Top 8s on the StarCityGames Open Series, and had come to the conclusion that card selection would be a good thing for a Legacy Grand Prix. With some sideboard help from his friend, Braverman was proving imitation is the best form of flattery.

Jeskai Miracles, with a dash of [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time), was Braverman's weapon of choice as well. Only one players could carry the controlling deck onto the semifinals. 

**The Games** 

Braverman's plan to take control and force through damage from Stoneforge Mystic hit a snag from Schönegger's Sensei's Divining Top and [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance). Missing plenty of land drops meant when Schönegger had the opportunity to create an Angel with [Entreat the Angels](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Entreat+the+Angels) there wasn't much Braverman could do about it upfront. 

After a battle over [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm), Braverman countered the spell making the creature token before slipping [Batterskull](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Batterskull) onto the battlefield. [Terminus](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terminus) from Schönegger ensured Braverman would have no attack either. Throughout all this, Schönegger used his [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) to reshape his hand as favorably as he could. When Braverman tried for a [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance) of his own, Schönegger was ready with [Force of Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+of+Will). 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/gpnj_qaurter-braverman.jpg)

*Phillip Braverman* 

  

Using Jace to hold control, Schönegger was content to let [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance) and Sensei's Diving Top lock Braverman out. 

"Next game." Braverman said after he had enough. Score one for the European expert.

The second game had Braverman lead [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic) and Sensei's Diving Top to set up a [Batterskull](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Batterskull) offensive, though [Terminus](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terminus) from Schönegger slowed the advance. 

"It's still in the deck!" Schönegger said, admitting his sideboard plan was a little different from what Braverman had speculated between games.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpnj14/gpnj_quarter_Scho%CC%88negger.jpg)

*Philipp Schonegger* 

  

Braverman had a second [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic) though, adding a second copy of Batterskull to his hand. Slipping the first into play with his Mystic, Braverman was ambushed by Schönegger's Wear // [Tear](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tear) though he fought the counterspell war to keep his equipment in play. 

[Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) and [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) gave Schönegger a few options, and he opted to block and protect the Planeswalker against Braverman's attack and [Red Elemental Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Red+Elemental+Blast). However, it slid downhill for Braverman from there. Using Will of the Council and [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) to clear out [Batterskull](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Batterskull)s, Schönegger lost his Jace but set up an [Entreat the Angels](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Entreat+the+Angels) for two 4/4's on an otherwise empty battlefield. 

At 26 life, Braverman had a comfortable padding of life to find an answer in. Stopping a [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20141116000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance)-Sensei's Divining Top duo on Schönegger's side was a start, but falling to 2 life gave Braverman just one more turn to Top his own way out from the Angels. 

He drew one last card and extended the hand instead.

**Phlipp Schönegger defeated Phillip Braverman, 2-0, to advance to the semifinals of Grand Prix New Jersey.**
