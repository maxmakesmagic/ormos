We'd been wondering what we should do with the Akroma avatar, and now we know. After Chuck's Virtual Party is over, the only way you'll be able to get one of these avatars is to trade for it.

### How Many Tournaments Will There Be?

Chuck's Virtual Party Events will run off two queues -- one for *Eighth Edition* Sealed and one for *Onslaught* Block Sealed. With the queues opening at 12:01 AM on October 25, here's how it works:

Each tournament requires 24 players to launch. As long as the 24-player minimum isn't hit, the tournament continues waiting for other players to join. Once 24 players have joined, the system waits to let other players register. As long as players keep registering, the tournament waits to launch. If players stop registering, the tournament will launch after a short wait with the number of players already joined.

Once 48 players join a tournament, the system launches that tournament immediately and spawns a new one.

The last Virtual Party Tournaments will launch on October 26 at 11:59 PM PST. After that, the CVP Tournaments still running will finish and the rest will be history.

### How About A Completely Free Tournament?

If you were one of the folks logging on to ***Magic** Online* from July 28 through September 28, you get a special bonus. Before the day of Chuck's Virtual Party, you'll get some extra product in your collection.

Each player who logged on to ***Magic** Online* during this period of time will get enough product to enter one of the *Onslaught* Block Sealed Deck tournaments for free. That's right: you'll get one *Onslaught* tournament pack, one *Legions* booster, and one *Scourge* booster. With the tournaments not costing any Event Tickets, that means you can play in one for free (although you don't *have* to use the product for an event).

### How Much Would You Pay For All This? But Wait, There's More...

Along with the *Onslaught* block product, if you logged on to ***Magic** Online* between July 28 and September 28, you'll get a special booster in your collection. It will only have one of three cards in it, but we're not sure which three to give away. So, it's time for a little survey.

From now until October 8, you can help us choose what three **pre-*Invasion* cards** we'll be giving out in the goodie bag. When the product arrives in your collection, you'll get one of the three winning cards in a special booster.

**Note:** Voting for the pre-*Invasion* cards has ended. Keep an eye on the ***Magic** Online* [Announcements folder](https://web.archive.org/web/20031008000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://boards1.wizards.com/forumdisplay.php?forumid=217&origin=magic_magiconline_news100103b) for results sometime after October 8, 2003.
