### How many dragons do you think there will be in the Top 8 this weekend?  
 (Pro Tour Dragons of Tarkir had 29)

**Fabrizio Anteri:** "My guess is 13. Two control decks with five in each, and a Green/Red deck with three Dragonlord Atarka."
