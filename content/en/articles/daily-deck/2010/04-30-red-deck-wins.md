While red decks have always existed, this particular version was created for use in the *Zendikar* Block Constructed format on **Magic Online.** It's an archetype sure to be shaken up by the release of the new set *Rise of the Eldrazi*, but it's powerful enough that you could even try it at your Standard-format Friday Night **Magic**! So what new cards will improve it from *Rise?* Take a look through the [searchable spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20100817070909/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/riseoftheeldrazi/spoiler) to decide for yourself.
