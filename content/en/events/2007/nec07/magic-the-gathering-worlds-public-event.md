The Northeast Challenge Qualifiers is a one-day tournament run across the eastern United States. The top two finishers at each qualifier earn invitations to the Northeast Challenge, to be held at the **Magic: The Gathering** World Championships in New York City on December 7. The Northeast Challege is one of 10 qualifier tournaments at Worlds for the [Win A Car Tournament](https://web.archive.org/web/20080622042727/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/worlds-07winacar), where a 2007 Toyota FJ Cruiser will be awarded to the winner.

*   [Locations of Northeast Challenge Qualifier Events](#locations)
*   [Complete schedule of Public Events at **Magic: The Gathering** World Championships](https://web.archive.org/web/20080622180618/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/worlds-07publicevents)  

*All Universal Tournament Rules and Magic: the Gathering Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document.*

### Entry Fee

*   Contact your local organizer for entry fee. The fee should be $15 or less.

### Format

*   Standard-constructed format
*   Deck lists are required

### Tournament Length

*   Swiss rounds - 50 minutes per round
*   Number of Swiss rounds based on attendance according to the following chart:

### Advancement

*   All players may participate in all Swiss rounds.
*   The top 8 players after the final Swiss round will advance to the top 8 single-elimination finals.

### Top 8

*   Top 8 matches will be best 2 of 3
*   Top 8 matches will have no time limit.
*   Where necessary, the standings after the Swiss rounds will still be used to determine final order in the standings.

### Prizes

*   The top 2 players will win an invitation to the Northeast Challenge held at the 2007 World Championships on Friday, December 7, 2007 at 5:00 PM.
*   Product Prizes as determined by the organizer.

### Notes

*   16K, REL Regular
*   All Universal Tournament Rules and Magic: the Gathering Floor Rules are in effect for this event unless specifically overruled by information in this Format Document
*   Players must bring pen, paper, card sleeves and appropriate counters.
