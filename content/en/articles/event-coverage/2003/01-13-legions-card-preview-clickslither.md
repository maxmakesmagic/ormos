I have never been a huge fan of aggressive decks. I am known mostly for playing aggro-control. White Weenie, Sped Red, Merfolk and Rock, these are the decks that appeal to me. Clickslither is a card that can only realistically be classified as aggressive, and its existence may well force me into a more aggressive mode for Constructed.

Clickslither, costing a hefty ![1 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/4c/6b4cf558eab279fb9414f146ebdd0e0705ec92984fec530597dd2a3bce43ce24.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif), pretty much relegates you to monored, but in both Standard and Extended, this guy will shine. Traditional monored decks try to attack you with small creatures early and finish you with burn at the end. When you have a four casting cost spell in your aggressive deck, it had better have a significant impact on the game. Clickslither may have that impact.

If we take a look at the current build of an Extended goblin deck, we can see that cards like Wall of Blossoms, Vine Trellis, and Ravenous Baloth give it headaches. In fact, they cripple your ground force. Once anything with a power greater than 0 hits to back up those walls, your goblins are pretty much out of the running. You now have to finish them with burn. In the meantime, all the creatures you draw are pretty much worthless. This is when the goblin deck’s game falls apart. 

Clickslither gives you an outlet for those dead draws. Much the way Goblin Bombardment was used in the past, Clickslither can be a powerful finisher trampling over those walls for an all-out alpha strike. While he also helps get around some of the red hosers like Absolute Law, he makes you more vulnerable to others like Circle of Protection: Red and Samite Ministration. Also, cards that are normally not a huge deal to red beatdown deck can turn around and wreck you, like Terror and Terminate. He is, however, immune to Smother.

Standard has also bred a modern goblin deck. However, Clickslither is even more vulnerable in an environment where there are Astral Slides and Moment’s Peaces. However, this may be a solid sideboard card against other strategies. He can run over Aquamoebas and Wild Mongrels just as well as he can Wall of Blossoms. To top it all off, 3/3 haste for ![1 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/4c/6b4cf558eab279fb9414f146ebdd0e0705ec92984fec530597dd2a3bce43ce24.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif) is no slouch.

Another issue with Clickslither: where do you pick him in draft? I am glossing over sealed deck, because unless a card outright wins you the game, triple of a specific color is just too hefty. Another problem in sealed is the difficulty in going tribal. You will rarely be able to exploit this guy’s goblin ability in sealed. Draft is another story entirely.

Red was the best color in *Onslaught* and, from the looks of the preview cards revealed thus far, things aren’t changing. Skirk Marauder and Warbreak Trumpeter have made this guy a little juicier in draft. In this format, more than others, I have found myself playing a 10/7 land configuration. This is most likely due to the depth of all the colors (except for blue). You can easily share a color with a neighbor and not even realize it until you notice how significantly your picks are weighted to your other color. This can offset Clickslither’s biggest drawback in Limited.

Another drawback of Clickslither is that he is a *Legions* card. This means until the third set comes out, you will have around 2/3 of your deck already built when you crack this guy. That means that unless you are already tribal in goblins, you may be passing Clickslither along.

Now that all the drawbacks are out of the way, know that there is nothing wrong with picking him first. If you happen to be tribal in goblins already, you simply slam this guy. He can dominate in the midgame and end the whole thing in the late game. If not, he is still a fine card. I may be dating myself here, but back in *Mirage* Block Limited, red was a great color. One card that always found its way into my decks was Talruum Minotaur. Clickslither has the same stats, plus abilities, but a little heavier on the red. So, if you are heavy red, odds are that you will have at least a couple goblins with which to use his other ability.

In red-black, you get even more utility out of him. Festering Goblin, one of the best black commons in *Onslaught*, becomes a little more useful with this guy. If you found yourself at a loss for Nantuko Husks, Clickslither can sub in to perform a similar function. He doesn’t have the advantage of being a zombie, but he is still a powerhouse.

Clickslither, while a situational card in every environment, only fluctuates between good and great. He is a solid addition to a goblin deck in Constructed and a truly dominating card most times in Limited. While a lot of the goblins in *Onslaught* may not be that exciting, look for more ways to exploit this guy in *Legions*.
