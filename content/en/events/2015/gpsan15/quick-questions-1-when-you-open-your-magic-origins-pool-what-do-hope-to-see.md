**When you open your *Magic Origins* Pool, what do hope to see first?**

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/gpsan15_qq_sanchez.jpg)  
*Maximiliano Sanchez*

**Maximiliano Sanchez:** [Thopter Spy Network](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thopter+Spy+Network)!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/gpsan15_qq_denicola.jpg)  
*Nicolás de Nicola*

**Nicolás de Nicola:** I want to see a [Hangarback Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hangarback+Walker).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/gpsan15_qq_arenas.jpg)  
*Christian Arenas*

**Christian Arenas:** I look for the red cards; I want to be aggressive and Boros is the best deck.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/gpsan15_qq_cortez.jpg)  
*Paulo Cortez*

**Paulo Cortez:** A large number of two-drops.
