Grand Prix–Brussels went down in the record books as the largest Standard tournament in the history of **Magic**. Tamas Nagy was one of the semifinalists, taking his Mythic deck to the very last table. The deck, which features a collection of cards from the Bant shard, debuted at Pro Tour–San Diego in the hands of Pro Tour Hall of Famer Zvi Mowshowitz. It is an absolute blast to play, owing to big hitters like Rafiq of the Many, Finest Hour, and Baneslayer Angel. For more information on Grand Prix-Brussels, visit the [official coverage](/en/events/2010/gpbru10/thrinaxes-sprout-for-giusti-in-brussels).
