The *Visions* card Ovinomancer turns creatures into 0/1 sheep tokens (at quite a hefty cost). This card is a tribute to the Blizzard computer game *Warcraft*, in which the spell Polymorph turns opposing units into sheep. The game was an R&D favorite during the *Visions* design period.
