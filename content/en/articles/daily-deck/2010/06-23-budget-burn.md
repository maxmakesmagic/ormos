Legacy is one of **Magic**'s most popular formats, claiming the title of the format for the largest tournament ever held earlier this year with over 2,100 players competing. If you're looking for a deck to play in the format on **Magic Online**, check out MakingSmartPlay's Burn version. The deck was good enough to 3-1 a recent event in the digital world, and with sideboard tech like Faerie Macabre and Mindbreak Trap it can even keep up with the big dogs attempting to do more "broken" things than simply connecting for 20 burn!
