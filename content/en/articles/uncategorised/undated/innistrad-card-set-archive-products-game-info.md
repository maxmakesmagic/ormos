## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7a/9d/7a9dab3daed6b0c7204b9ab175d8925fea862e5209e4c06148f3bd92bf630347.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, two deck boxes, nine 15-card booster packs, 70 land cards, 10 checklist cards for use with double-faced cards, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0c/6b/0c6b79f315fe0af98b45840b24b7bc05e91d61d8d8a0cb3b2f39652427bd696d.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/73/a8/73a82f1b699de5ef2cd58d297cbfd82d61a8bbe92f81b81a7ce6e1ca1e56351f.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 cards, a foil premium rare card, one sealed 15-card booster pack, a strategy insert, and a **Magic** learn-to-play guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719050257/http://magic.wizards.com/node/284376)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4d/94/4d9419538481d3ff0a8cd02a2d214dcd0b255a93e8d9acb099a1736cfc4acac0.png)

### EVENT DECKS

Battle like the best, right out of the box. Includes a tournament-ready 60-card deck plus a 15-card “sideboard” to swap in as needed, depending on what you expect your opponent will play.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719050257/http://magic.wizards.com/node/284371)

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ad/ab/adab51775db3ee2cb26f8ce7a73df37c476d4bebf53bc0ebb0ce2c70b80f77df.png)*Innistrad* features 264 black-bordered cards, including randomly inserted premium versions of cards. It’s the first set to feature double-faced **Magic** cards.

**Set Name:** *Innistrad*  
**Block:** Set 1 of 3 in the *Innistrad* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 264

**Languages:** English, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish

**Official Three-Letter Code:** ISD  
**Twitter Hashtag:** #MTGINN

*Innistrad* [FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719050257/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/faq/isd) (or download as [RTF](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719050257/http://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/MTGISD_FAQ_20110909_EN.rtf))  
  

**Design Team:** Mark Rosewater (lead), Richard Garfield, Jenna Helland, Graeme Hopkins, Tom LaPille

**Development Team:** Erik Lauer (lead), Mark L. Gottlieb, David Humpherys, Tom LaPille, Adam Lee, Kenneth Nagle
