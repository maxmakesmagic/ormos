The art description for the *Stronghold* uncommon Flame Wave reads: "Show a literal wave of flame that travels down the exterior of Volrath’s mountain. The Vec soldiers advancing up the mountainside are being fried by the oncoming wave. They don’t have a chance to escape."

![Flame Wave](https://i.ormos.uk/ea/c0/eac0a4dc5539bf4e5f76ce395f40df58dbbe5ce48ee90c1ae5d289c890ea1c5b.jpg)

Artist Donato Giancola decided one lucky soldier had the means to escape: a surfboard. Hidden in the inferno is a lone surfer hanging 10 on the fire. Duuuuuude!
