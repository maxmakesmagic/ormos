*What's your favorite Spike card?* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ee/e0/eee0d44326785d6cab5488c0c4ad1ad943d8c6ff2b26281945c039e7527772dc.jpg)

**Alexis Janson:** The phrase "favorite Spike card" seems almost a non-sequitur to me. When I'm in Spike mode, my goal is to win—I'm not interested in such trivialities as "favorite cards." The "best" cards completely change based on the board state, metagame, or format.

Trying to answer this from the perspective of "what Spike card have you played with the most?", I came up with Sol Ring, because it goes in almost every deck. But that's not a very interesting answer. In fact, I can't say definitively that I even like Sol Ring anymore, because it has such an unbalancing effect on the game. All the other cards I initially thought of fell into a similar vein—I love playing Dark Ritual, or Balance, or Necropotence, because I can feel the power rushing through my veins as I crush my opponent with such unbalanced effects. Does that make them my favorites? I don't think so.

My goal with **Magic**, first and foremost, is to play a tight, challenging game—one where the advantage swings back and forth, lots of interesting decisions are made, and then I finally squeak out a win via the smallest of advantages. So my favorite Spike card would need to be a card that contributes to that type of game—not one that often decisively wins the game on its own.

 ![3629](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&MultiverseId=3629&options=) 

  

With that in mind, I would nominate Vampiric Tutor as my favorite Spike card. It's got a lot of inherent decision making; its instant speed means you'll have a lot of information to make your decision, but the possibilities are limited only by the cards in your deck. Do you get a land on turn one, or get a utility card on turn three, or get a finisher on turn six? What singletons do you include in your deck once you're running tutor effects? Does this change your sideboarding strategy? I love that tutor effects keep me wondering what the optimal decision is. I'm never sure if I've chosen the optimal time to play them or the perfect card to fetch. A perfect Spike card!
