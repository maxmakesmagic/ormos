### Max Sjöblom

**Age:** 23  
  
**Hometown:** Helsinki  
  
**Occupation:** Student  
  
**How did you qualify for Pro Tour Philadelphia?Magic** Online PTQ  
  
**Number of Grand Prix / Pro Tour Top 8s:**  
 PT: 0  
 GP: 0  
  
**Other previous** ***Magic*** **accomplishments:** Finnish National Champion 2006, Team Worlds 3rd 2006  
  
**What was your record by format?**  
 Modern: 6-2-2  
 Booster Draft: 6-0  
  
**How did you prepare for Pro Tour Philadelphia? Who did you prepare with?**  
 Did a bunch of drafts online and tested with the Finnish guys that were qualified. Switched from Mono G Post to Ascension once I got the list from Ensio.  
  
**How many hours do you spend on** ***Magic*** **Online in an average week?**  
 10-25, depending on my schedule  
  
**What Modern deck did you play and why?**  
[Pyromancer Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20110904000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyromancer+Ascension). Got an initial list from Ensio Ask and tuned it. It just seemed insane against the most popular decks.  
  
**What do you think of the new Modern format?**  
 Very nice, and infinitely better than Extended, glad about the change. Too bad there are no real control decks that are viable.  
  
**Where do you play Friday Night** ***Magic*****, or if you don't go to FNM anymore, where did you play?**  
 Don't really play FNM anymore, used to play at Poromagia and [Safe Haven](https://web.archive.org/web/20110904000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Safe+Haven).

### Sam Black

**Age:** 29  
  
**Hometown:** Madison, WI  
  
**Occupation:** Writer for StarCity Games  
  
**How did you qualify for Pro Tour Philadelphia?** Pro Level 6  
  
**Number of Grand Prix / Pro Tour Top 8s:**  
 PT: 0 GP: 3  
  
**Other previous ***Magic*** accomplishments:** Team World Champion, Win-a-Car Tournament winner at Worlds 2007  
  
**What was your record by format?**  
 Modern: 7-2-1 Booster Draft: 5-1  
  
**How did you prepare for Pro Tour Philadelphia? Who did you prepare with?**  
 Drafting *M12* in Madison all the time. *Magic* Online with John Stolzmann. In person with Zvi Mowshowitz, Gaudenis Vidugiris, Jon Finkel, Tom Martell, and Zaiem Beg.  
  
**How many hours do you spend on ***Magic*** Online in an average week?**  
 Highly variable depending on whether a set has come out physically or online more recently. I probably play *Magic* about 6 hours a day.  
  
**What Modern deck did you play and why?**  
 Mono Blue Poison. John Stolzmann told me he had built a deck he thought might be very good. After some changes, we couldn't find anything that beat it.  
  
**What do you think of the new Modern format?**  
 I don't think this is what it was supposed to look like. Too much combo. I'd ban [Cloudpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20110904000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudpost), maybe [Blazing Shoal](https://web.archive.org/web/20110904000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blazing+Shoal), and unban [Ancestral Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20110904000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Vision), maybe Blossom.  
  
**Where do you play Friday Night ***Magic***, or if you don't go to FNM anymore, where did you play?**  
 Netherworld Games in Madison – the store I founded but no longer own.

### Josh Utter-Leyton

**Age:** 25  
  
**Hometown:** Rohnert Park, CA  
  
**How did you qualify for Pro Tour Philadelphia?** Pro Club Level 7  
  
**Number of Grand Prix / Pro Tour Top 8s:**  
 PT: 2 GP: 2  
  
**Other previous ***Magic*** accomplishments:** 2010 US National Champion  
  
**What was your record by format?**  
 Modern: 8-1-1 Booster Draft: 4-2  
  
**How did you prepare for Pro Tour Philadelphia? Who did you prepare with?**  
 I spent a week in the woods with the [Channel](https://web.archive.org/web/20110904000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Channel)Fireball squad.  
  
**How many hours do you spend on ***Magic*** Online in an average week?**  
 Six.  
  
**What Modern deck did you play and why?**  
 Zoo. Every other deck was either [Cloudpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20110904000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudpost) or a "belcher" deck -- a deck that could kill on turn two or three but was nearly as likely to turn-six fizzle. Zoo was the most stable deck, and had the best sideboard. What do you think about the new Modern format? I was super excited about the change to Modern for this PT. The way the format developed for this PT (so many flimsy combo decks) has tempered that excitement some, but I think the format will look very different now that the best decks are known.  
  
**Where do you play Friday Night ***Magic***, or if you don't go to FNM anymore, where did you play?**  
 At [Channel](https://web.archive.org/web/20110904000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Channel)Fireball's shop, Superstars!
