On January 4, 2003, Zvi Mowshowitz wins Grand Prix New Orleans piloting his pet deck TurboLand. In short (though nothing about TurboLand is short) Zvi combined [Exploration](https://web.archive.org/web/20090109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exploration) and [Horn of Greed](https://web.archive.org/web/20090109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Horn+of+Greed) to generate massive amounts of mana and cards, capping things off with repeating [Time Warp](https://web.archive.org/web/20090109000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Time%2BWarp) thanks to drawing his whole deck and then putting the Warps back in his deck courtesy of [Battlefield Scrounger](https://web.archive.org/web/20090109000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battlefield+Scrounger). Here's the famous deck in all its mad genius glory:
