Standard! Standard!! Standard!!!

With the release of *Khans of Tarkir*, we have entered a new environment.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Elspeth,%20Sun%27s%20Champion)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth,%20Sun%27s%20Champion)

From now on, we need not worry about getting our [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20141007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) accidentally killed, or despairing in the face of a plague of [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20141007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s, or watching helplessly as our creatures are mercilessly judged with a [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20141007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict). Oh, and no more sudden [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20141007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation), either.

To coincide with the rollout of a new Standard, I plan on focusing this week on new decks from that format. Today's deck is one from Card Shop Hareru-ya (more popularly known as "Saitō's shop") in Tōkyō.

At Hareru-ya, they run three FNM tournaments, at 14:00, 17:00, and 20:00. This deck, an Esper control deck by Hiroaki Yamakawa, went [6–0 in games at the 14:00 tournament](http://www.happymtg.com/decks/view/D070268) last week.

I guess this is what Esper looks like in the new Standard world: removal, draw spells, and a little countermagic. The win condition, [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20141007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion), is strong in any environment.

One thing to note is Yamakawa's fresh perspective in including [Singing Bell Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20141007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Singing+Bell+Strike) as removal. Oh, and [Empty the Pits](https://web.archive.org/web/20141007000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Empty+the+Pits). Combined with all the draw spells in the deck, it can lead to an explosive end step.
