After fifteen rounds of Standard, with a total of 1,161 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-standard/gpbir18_t8_pinto.jpg)

**Name:** Goncalo Pinto

**Age:** 32

**Occupation:** Digital marketing analyst

**Hometown:** Barreiro, Portugal

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Top 8 at Nationals.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

I tested a lot for Legacy and didn't practice any Standard, so on Friday I and Márcio thought black-red might be good and asked \_megafone\_ for help.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Goblin Chainwhirler](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Chainwhirler), by far.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Rekindling Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rekindling+Phoenix). Could be main deck.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-standard/gpbir18_t8_southworth.jpg)

**Name:** Luke Southworth

**Age:** 29

**Occupation:** Retail

**Hometown:** Preston, United Kingdom

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Couple of GP cashes, one Pro Tour appearance.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Black-Red Aggro, because [Heart of Kiran](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heart+of+Kiran) is great and Matthew Foulkes put the tuning in.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Heart of Kiran](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heart+of+Kiran).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Siege-Gang Commander](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege-Gang+Commander).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-standard/gpbir18_t8_brown.jpg)

**Name:** Matt Brown

**Age:** 20

**Occupation:** Accountant

**Hometown:** Chelmsford, England

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Played one Pro Tour, a few GP cashes.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Black-Red Aggro because Matt Foulkes told me to.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Scrapheap Scrounger](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scrapheap+Scrounger) with a shoutout to [Goblin Chainwhirler](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Chainwhirler).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

Not [The Eldest Reborn](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Eldest+Reborn).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-standard/gpbir18_t8_busson.jpg)

**Name:** Etienne Busson

**Age:** 24

**Occupation:** *Magic* streamer

**Hometown:** Neuchâtel, Switzerland

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

A couple PTQ Top 8 and GP Day 2 finishes.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Black-Green Constrictor because I felt there would be a lot of Vehicles decks and I like the matchup.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Adventurous Impulse](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Adventurous+Impulse). It adds a lot of consistency.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Liliana, Death's Majesty](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana%2C+Death%27s+Majesty) helped a lot in grindy matchups.

Don't know.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-standard/gpbir18_t8_juza.jpg)

**Name:** Martin Jůza

**Age:** 30

**Occupation:** Professional tourist

**Hometown:** Plzen, Czech Republic

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Hall of Fame class of 2017, 28 Grand Prix Top 8s, three Pro Tour Top 8s.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Black-red because you get to play a lot of powerful cards. I also didn't really have a lot of time to prepare and this was the deck I played at the last Standard GP, so I just stuck with it.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-standard/gpbir18_t8_lahonen.jpg)

**Name:** Leo Lahonen

**Age:** 23

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Espoo, Finland

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

2016 World *Magic* Cup Top 8.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

White-Blue Control. It seemed very streong and sweet in our testing.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Teferi, Hero of Dominaria](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%2C+Hero+of+Dominaria). The card buys time, draws cards, and wins games.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Sorcerous Spyglass](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sorcerous+Spyglass), a card that shuts down multiple copies of a card at an efficient mana cost.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpbir18-standard/gpbir18_t8_nielsen.jpg)

**Name:** Simon Nielsen

**Age:** 23 I think ...

**Occupation:** *Magic* is my life

**Hometown:** Slagelse, Denmark

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Four Grand Prix Top 8s, two times Gold pro, Pro Tour Sydney Top 16, Zen Takahashi's friend, and sleep-in special on both Day 1 and Day 2.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why did you choose it?**

Black-Red Mirrorbreaker. I expected lots of black-red, thanks to twitch.tv/matthewfoulkes. Fabrizio Anteri gave me a list that is great in the mirror. Seemed like an obvious choice.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

Probably [Heart of Kiran](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heart+of+Kiran)? I don't know, they are all so good!

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Heartless Pillage](https://web.archive.org/web/20180513000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heartless+Pillage). Thanks to the cool kids, Piotr Głogowski, Julien Henry, and Louis Deltour, for the tech! It was ... excessively medium.

* * *
