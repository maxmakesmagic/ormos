### THE **MAGIC ONLINE** CUBE RETURNS!

 

With almost 150 cards replaced, this version of the Cube is bound to challenge even the most voracious Cube drafter. If the Cube looks familiar, it's because it was played in the 2012 MOCS Championship by the 16 finalists (check out the [Championship Cube coverage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130416000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/butakov-tramples-his-way-past-opposition)). As you play, keep an eye out for these exciting additions: [Chord of Calling](https://web.archive.org/web/20130416000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chord+of+Calling), [Death Cloud](https://web.archive.org/web/20130416000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death+Cloud), and [Destructive Force](https://web.archive.org/web/20130416000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Destructive+Force). Check out the full list of changes [below](#cubelist), or review the full card list [here](/en/events/2013/mocs2012cubelist/2012-magic-online-championship-cube-draft-card-list).

When you have had your fill of Cube, or just want to mix it up, *Lorwyn* Block Draft Queues will be available in the Limited Queues room. The single elimination Cube queues also include *Lorwyn* Block boosters in their prizes.

The **Magic Online** Cube and the *Lorwyn* Block booster Draft Queues will start after the downtime on Wednesday, May 1, and end with the downtime on Wednesday, May 8.

**THE MAGIC ONLINE CUBE DRAFT QUEUES**  
*START TIMES:* On demand beginning after the downtime on Wednesday, May 1, until the downtime on Wednesday, May 8.  
*LOCATION:* Limited Queues room.  
*ENTRY OPTION(S):* 8 Event Tickets **OR** 2 Event Tickets and 10 Cube Tickets.  
*PRODUCT:* **Magic Online** will provide 3 Phantom **Magic Online** Cube booster packs for this event. Cards that players draft and play with in a Phantom event are NOT added to those players' collections.  
*SIZE:* 8 Players.  
*DURATION:* Drafting and deck building time plus 3 rounds, single elimination **OR** 3 rounds, Swiss pairings, depending on event entered.  
*PRIZES:* Prizes for single-elimination events are based on placement at the end of the tournament. Prizes for Swiss pairing events are based on the total match points at the end of the tournament. Each match win awards 3 points and each match loss awards 0 points.

### **4-BOOSTER SEALED QUEUES RETURN**

It has been several months since we removed the 4-Booster Sealed queues and replaced them with both normal and Phantom 6-Booster queues. While we have been happy with the results of the Phantom queue, the normal 6-Booster queues have not been as popular as we would have liked.

As a result, starting with tomorrow’s downtime, we will bring back a version of the 4-Booster Sealed queues for *Return to Ravnica*, *Gatecrash*, and ***Magic** 2013*. These will be in addition to the other Sealed queues for a week, and then we will remove the normal 6-Booster queues leaving only the Phantom 6-Booster and the 4-Booster Sealed deck queues.

**4-BOOSTER SEALED QUEUES**  
*START TIMES:* On demand beginning after the downtime on Wednesday, April 17.  
*LOCATION:* Limited Queues room.  
*ENTRY OPTION(S):* 18 Event Tickets **OR** 2 Event Tickets plus product.  
*PRODUCT:* 4 booster packs that match the queue format.  
*SIZE:* 8 Players.  
*DURATION:* Drafting and deck building time plus 3 rounds, Swiss pairings.  
*PRIZES:* Prizes for Swiss pairing events are based on the total match points at the end of the tournament. Each match win awards 3 points and each match loss awards 0 points.

### **CLASSIC PRODUCT DRAFTS**

The **Magic Online** Team is always looking for ways to balance organized play to meet the needs of both the players and the business. With events for product that is no longer available in the **Magic Online** store, we tend to be more cautious with our event offerings. Queues that take hours to fire are not good for anyone, so we try to ensure that when they are available we are driving players to them.

Lately, however, we have been getting quite a few requests for more classic product events, so we have decided to bring back the *Shadowmoor* Block drafts for this week. With *Lorwyn* Block drafting coming with the Cube May 1, it seemed like a natural pairing. Enjoy!

***SHADOWMOOR* BLOCK DRAFT QUEUE**  
*START TIMES:* On demand beginning after the downtime on Wednesday, April 17, until the downtime on Wednesday, April 24.  
*LOCATION:* Limited Queues room.  
*ENTRY OPTION(S):* 14 Event Tickets **OR** 2 Event Tickets plus product.  
*PRODUCT:* 2 *Shadowmoor* booster packs and 1 *Eventide* booster pack.  
*SIZE:* 8 Players.  
*DURATION:* Drafting and deck building time plus 3 rounds, single elimination.  
*PRIZES:* Prizes for single-elimination events are based on placement at the end of the tournament.
