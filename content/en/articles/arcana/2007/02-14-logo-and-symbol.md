The *Future* is on the horizon.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e9/02/e9023a24f6f4a881df6c02525dcf3ad738b630baa5f6768ed1548f036df266a2.jpg)  

*Future Sight logo*

The *Future Sight* logo you see above was designed by Wizards' talented graphic artists Robert Rapier, Scott Okamura and Lisa Hanson. There's a lot of squintworthy detail in there-want to [see it in hi-res](https://i.ormos.uk/36/c5/36c5853458228ecfca693df2e71713b0afd00db9a91854fbedc42d5bd528ea71.jpg)?

Below is the expansion symbol for *Future Sight*, its design reminiscent of an eye gazing through a rift portal.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/49/ea/49eae8e1d4027af5b919695f822086c72ffe694090dd669a772cee78fe3940dc.jpg)  

*Future Sight expansion symbol*

*Future Sight*, the third set in the *Time Spiral* Block, releases May 4, 2007. Prerelease events for *Future Sight* begin April 21, 2007. Previews on **Magicthegathering.com** begin April 9, 2007.
