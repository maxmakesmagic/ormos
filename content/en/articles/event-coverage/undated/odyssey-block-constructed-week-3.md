### 

This week's issue will concentrate on the combo potential (however little of it) in OBC. I will also update everyone on the minor changes made to the decks I already have so far.

### Confessor-Lich

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7d/90/7d902164af8a4f7049c03f35856c51e5684b11d2ad2d6baf02e5104e0102e107.jpg)About a million people pointed out that the win condition for this deck is Zombie Infestation. A dozen or so sent in deck lists (you can see one of them by clicking on the Message Boards link at the bottom of this article). I've carefully examined them and tried to come up with my own, but the deck just never got off the ground and to the point where it could be considered even a tier 2. 

The concept is simple enough. Play Nefarious Lich with a Confessor and Zombie Infestation in play. Start discarding cards two at a time to Zombie Infestation. Confessor's life gain ability will allow you to draw the same amount of cards back. Thus you can make 20+ Zombie tokens and still have enough cards in your library not to get decked in the next few turns. Here is a very basic (not teched out at all) sample listing for those who wish to take another crack at making this deck work. I however have not been able to do so.

4 Nefarious Lich
4 Confessor
4 Zombie Infestation
4 Diabolic Tutor
4 Insidious Dreams
4 Teroh's Faithful
4 Innocent Blood
4 Chainer's Edict
4 Mutilate
4 Tainted Fields
14 Swamp
6 Plains

![](https://i.ormos.uk/20/f8/20f8a1ba7bd6ba94d0c1e599bd58351a1f66b45e50879dfe994b3353ab673bb6.jpg)I started out with a 243 card deck but found that despite many cantrips, you are never in danger of falling below 200 cards unless your opponent resolves Haunting Echoes or Traumatize. So I was able to cut 4 Patchwork Gnomes and 2 lands - not a great deal in a deck this size, but anything that gets you closer to the combo helps.

Originally I considered filling out the deck with a number of other combos (Traumatize, Haunting Echoes, Mortal Combat, Ichorid) but most of these cards would be terrible unless you drew Trauamatize, and drawing a Tutor always means going after Battle of Wits anyway. Instead, I built it to try and be able to survive whatever your opponent brings for as long as possible, while waiting to draw one of 8 tutors or Battle of Wits itself. Sometimes the deck even manages to win with its creatures, never drawing a combo. I would not recommend that anyone play this at Pro Tour-Osaka, but it is sure great to play for fun (I now understand the appeal of 5-Color **Magic** a little better).

### All Your Eggs...

Surprisingly, the strongest potential 'combo deck' we've discovered was based on Nantuko Shrine! Former U.S. National Team member Frank Hernandez came up with the following version that he was willing to share with us:

8 Forest 
10 Island 
4 Darkwater Egg 
4 Skycloud Egg 
4 Mossfire Egg 
4 Shadowblood Egg 
4 Sungrass Egg 
4 Peek 
4 Obsessive Search 
4 Moment's Peace 
4 Words of Wisdom 
4 Nantuko Shrine 
2 Circular Logic 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2f/43/2f43fd6c2d111feaef34a8c32112b77cbfb73f49a5dd3188d8fde2e416a5c85f.jpg)The idea here is that you quickly cycle through your library to find a Nantuko Shrine. Once you do, you should easily out-Squirrel your opponent because of how quickly the Eggs and various other cantrips fill out your graveyard. Four Moment's Peace buy you quite a bit of time to do this. I was not at all convinced, but Frank and I played a number of games and although I think I had a winning record in the end, his deck still performed far, far better than I expected it to. 

The deck's greatest problem is its mana base. You can't run too few lands, or you will never get off the ground. And you can't run too many lands, because you will then constantly draw into land pockets mid-game and be unable to generate lots of tokens quickly. Scott Seville had a very interesting idea of adding New Frontiers to the deck. It lets you play more lands initially but search them out of the library so that they do not act as speed bumps later. I forwarded the idea to Frank and hopefully he will share the results with us.

### Other Decks

A quick update on what is going on with the other decks I posted over the last few weeks. (You can find them [here](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/odyssey-block-constructed-week-2).)

The monogreen Squirrel version I posted last week has been testing quite well. I have made no changes to it as of yet, but I can certainly see taking out Gurzigosts (perhaps putting them in the sideboard) as you rarely have a lot of cards in your graveyard, with all the flashback spells. I have not come up with a replacement yet.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e1/0b/e10b72120dae8694be6eab5e61a7252aedaeae943d30162965bf477408f40829.jpg)In monoblack, I strongly contemplate adding one or two maindeck Rancid Earth to help deal with Squirrel's Nest. Although monoblack is by far the most fun deck to play of the ones I have built, it is moving further and further away from being my top choice for Osaka. There are just too many cards it can't handle well - token generators and Braids being on top of that list. I intend to try a different monoblack version, with Mesmeric Fiends and Butchers instead of Innocent Blood. Perhaps it might perform better.

Compulsion has been nothing short of excellent in u-g, and Breakthrough has left the deck in favor of better cards. I still keep changing my version of the deck around and will post an updated one next week. I have been playing more with the Psychatog build and am pretty happy with it so far. Far more testing is needed against u-g though - although that is the most popular deck out there, this is also the matchup I did not get a chance to test enough.
