It's Regionals celebration week on Daily Decks! All week long, we'll be bringing you the newest, coolest archetypes to come out of the 2009 Regionals tournaments held across North America recently. First on the docket? Chris Kelly's innovative take on Jund from Seattle, Washington Regionals. He added the nifty little Sygg, River Cutthroat, allowing him to draw extra cards for doing what his deck loves to do: 3+ damage a turn! To read about more Regionals Top 8 decklists, visit [the Regionals decklists page](/en/events/2009/regionals09/2009-regionals-24).
