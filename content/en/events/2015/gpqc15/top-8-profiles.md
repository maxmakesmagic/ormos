**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpqc15/t8_beldon.jpg)Name:** Omar Beldon

**Age:** 25

**Hometown:** Toronto, ON

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Nothing of note.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Dark Jeskai. Lucas Siow is much smarter than I am. #TeamSiow'sBasement

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon)

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Bant Midrange with Seekers and [Defiant Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Defiant+Strike)

**If you could change one card anywhere in your deck what would it be and why?**  
 Wouldn't change anything. Would try to convince Paul Dean to put down his Wardens and sleeve up Jaces, though!

 The Mirror.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpqc15/t8_magalhaes.jpg)Name:** Edgar Magalhaes

**Age:** 21

**Hometown:** Waterloo, ON

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 Top 8 GP Toronto 2013 & 2015

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Jeskai Black. Sammy T. told me the deck was great.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
 Obviously Jace. The card is busted and this is the best Jace deck. Shoutouts to [Pia and Kiran Nalaar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pia+and+Kiran+Nalaar) though for being insane in the mirror.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
 Probably [Kolaghan's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kolaghan%27s+Command), versatile card advantage engine.

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Jake Mondello's Eldrazi Ramp deck.

**If you could change one card anywhere in your deck what would it be and why?**  
 Wouldn't mind a [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) or two in the 75 somewhere.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpqc15/t8_lanthier.jpg)Name:** Dan Lanthier

**Age:** 28 (it's my birthday!)

**Hometown:** Ottawa, ON

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 GP Vancouver Champion 2015, Canadian National Champion 2008

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Dark Jeskai. I feel it's the best deck and it suits my play style.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy). It's the best card in the format, and the reason that the deck is playable.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Exert Influence](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exert+Influence). It swings creature matchups that are otherwise tough to beat.

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Red-Black Dragons? Nothing was very surprising.

**If you could change one card anywhere in your deck what would it be and why?**  
 I wish I had [Mastery of the Unseen](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mastery+of+the+Unseen) in my sideboard, but I'm unsure what I'd cut to accomodate it.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpqc15/t8_maynard.jpg)Name:** Pascal Maynard

**Age:** 22

**Hometown:** Here!

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 9 GP Top 8's now.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 4-Color [Rally the Ancestors](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rally+the+Ancestors), because I had no idea what to play and it seemed well-positioned.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Catacomb Sifter](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Catacomb+Sifter). It does everything you need.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Merciless Executioner](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merciless+Executioner) / [Fleshbag Marauder](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleshbag+Marauder), since it's the only sideboard card that doesn't make my deck bad.

**What was the most surprising deck you played against this weekend?**  
 The mirror match.

**If you could change one card anywhere in your deck what would it be and why?**  
 I would try to shave a few [Grim Haruspex](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Haruspex), but I have no idea what card I'd play instead.
