These disturbing creatures are "bound spirits" that the Azorius keep around for advice and other tasks. There was one back in the original *Ravnica: City of Guilds*: [Soulsworn Jury](https://web.archive.org/web/20121102000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soulsworn+Jury).
