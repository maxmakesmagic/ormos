Luis Scott-Vargas talks through his deck choice for this Block Constructed Pro Tour: Tempered Steel. Projected to be the boogeyman of the format, the deck uses cheap artifacts to race out to explosive starts. 

  

Luis Scott-Vargas - 28 points 

Pro Tour Nagoya, Block Constructed
