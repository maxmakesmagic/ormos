En este comprometido enfrentamiento tenemos a Lucas Mercader (UR Twin) contra Alejandro Dupuy (UG Milling).

Lucas gana el dado y hace mulligan, Dupuy se queda su mano.

Abren con [Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain) y [Hedron Crab](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hedron+Crab) respectivamente, para el segundo turno, Lucas descubre tres fetchland, un [Evolving Wilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Evolving+Wilds) y dos [Lotus Cobra](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotus+Cobra) en la mano de Dupuy gracias a [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe), y finaliza con [Shrine of Piercing Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrine+of+Piercing+Vision), este último juega Evolving y deckea seis a su rival cayendo un [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch) como carta relevante.

Ya en el tercer turno, Dupuy juega cobra y con [Scald](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scald)ing Tarn deckea tres más, a lo que Lucas responde con [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch) al final del turno, pero se topa con [Into the Roil](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Into+the+Roil) sacrificando la fecth, siguiente turno y siguiente cobra y de nuevo deceiver al final del turno el cual ya no encuentra respuesta.

Lo que si encuentra Lucas es un [Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain) sacrificando su altar de cinco, pone las dos abajo y pasa turno. Seguimos con Lucas que juega [See Beyond](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=See+Beyond) en su siguiente turno, su oponente deckea seis y vemos en el cementerio un exarch y un [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin).

Entonces encuentra Lucas el ansiado [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) gracias a [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder) para combar (quedándole diez cartas en el deck) sin resistencia por parte de Dupuy.

Lucas Mercader gana 1-0.

Ahora es Dupuy quien decide salir y hacer mulligan, lo que no le impide empezar con [Hedron Crab](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hedron+Crab) de primero y otro de segundo deckeando doce cartas con [Terramorphic Expanse](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terramorphic+Expanse) y volcando únicamente un [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch) de entre las cartas que Lucas tiene para combar, pero este respira aliviado barriendo la mesa con [Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm).

Dupuy falla land drop y Lucas juega [Halimar Depths](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Halimar+Depths) y [Shrine of Piercing Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrine+of+Piercing+Vision).

Tras fetchland para UG y [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder) para la UR, Lucas vuelve a su turno jugando otro [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder) y un [Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain) a lo que en respuesta Dupuy juega [Archive Trap](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archive+Trap). Lucas en respuesta sacrifica un altar de seis, se resuelve la trampa y juega otra dejando al jugador de twin con tres cartas en su mazo; sigue respondiendo Dupuy a [Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain) ahora con Surgical [Extraction](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Extraction) a [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) que se encuentra con un [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) pero Dupuy tiene tres manás para pagarlo.Lucas concede.

Alejandro Dupuy logra el 1-1.

Para la partida de desenlace ambos quedan sus manos. Lucas sale de [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder) de primero, [Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm) a [Hedron Crab](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hedron+Crab) de segundo, [Halimar Depths](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Halimar+Depths) con [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) a [Lotus Cobra](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotus+Cobra) de tercero y [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder), [Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain) y [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe) descubriendo tres lands, [Archive Trap](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archive+Trap) y Surgical [Extraction](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Extraction) de cuarto turno.

Al final del sexto turno de Lucas cae [Archive Trap](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archive+Trap) por parte de Dupuy que se ve contrarrestada con [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak). Despues de varios turnos de mana vas por parte de ambos cae al final del turno un [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch), para en su siguiente turno jugar [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) respondiendo Dupuy con *[Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Visions)* From Beyond, robando una carta y extendiendo la mano a favor de Lucas.

**Lucas Mercader gana 2-1**
