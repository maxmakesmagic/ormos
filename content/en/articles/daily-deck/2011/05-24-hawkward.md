Ken Adams quietly builds decks on his own terms and always seems to do well with them. At last weekend's StarCityGames.com Standard Open, he took this mono-white concoction into the Top 16. Loaded with powerful artifacts, the deck benefits from artifact enhancers like Tempered Steel and Steel Overseer. In a pinch, Signal Pest can also juice the team up. For more, read [full coverage](http://www.starcitygames.com/php/news/expandnews.php?Article=21896) of the event at StarCityGames.com.
