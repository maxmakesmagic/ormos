It took a lot of work for [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) to exist. Let's go back to the beginning.

### The Magic Invitational

The **Magic** Invitational started life as the *Duelist* Invitational, but when *Duelist* Magazine stopped existing, the name got changed. It was a sixteen-person tournament with the unique prize of getting to design a **Magic** card. And the winner would then get depicted in the art! Here's the list of winners:

*   **1997**: Olle Rade, creator of [Sylvan Safekeeper](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Safekeeper)
*   **1998**: Darwin Kastle, creator of [Avalanche Riders](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avalanche+Riders)
*   **1999**: Mike Long, creator of [Rootwater Thief](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rootwater+Thief)
*   March, **[2000](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/article.asp?x=INVITATIONAL9900/welcome)**: Chris Pikula, creator of [Meddling Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=26591)
*   November, **[2000](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/event.asp?event=MI00)**: Jon Finkel, creator of [Shadowmage Infiltrator](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=33604)
*   **[2001](/en/articles/event-coverage/2015/09-02-2001-magic-invitational)**: Kai Budde, creator of [Voidmage Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=108811)
*   **[2002](/en/articles/event-coverage/2002/10-18-live-coverage-of-2002-magic-invitational)**: Jens Thoren, creator of [Solemn Simulacrum](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=49434)
*   **[2004](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/live-coverage-2004-magic-invitational)**: Bob Maher, creator of [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant)
*   **[2005](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/soh-rides-online-skills-victory)**: Terry Soh, creator of [Rakdos Augermage](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakdos+Augermage)
*   **[2006](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/ruel-invokes-invitationalists-victory)**: Antoine Ruel, creator of [Ranger of Eos](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ranger+of+Eos)
*   **[2007](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/tiago-triumphant)**: Tiago Chan, creator of [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage)

R&D tried this card out, but they decided that a land that could also be an uncounterable counterspell was too strong to print. So they asked Tiago for another version. And that turned into [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20111024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage)!

So much for the text on the card. Let's move on to the art! Here's the art description that was given to Volkan Baga:

> Color: Blue creature  
>  Action: Start with the reference. Design a bad-ass human mage based on the reference. He is wearing a metal arm enhancement with glass tubing that contains ghost energy. We can see the ghosts swirling around inside the glass. The mage should look smart and powerful.  
>  Focus: The mage  
>  Mood: Powerful, smart, dangerous

Volkan decided to sketch in his own face for the early stages of the piece. Here's the first version:
