Each Thursday on the new site we’ll use this slot to show off all the new winning decks we’ve received from premier events and Pro Tour Qualifiers. And, we're happy to announce that starting very soon we’ll also be able to include **Magic Online** events in these lists as well. 

For this week we’ve got decks from the Vintage Championships, every deck that made Day 2 at Grand Prix–Denver, plus multiple Top 8s from Pro Tour Qualifiers.
