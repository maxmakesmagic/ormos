It is time once again to check in on everyone's favorite Kobold Pincher, [Mistform Ultimus](https://web.archive.org/web/20120815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistform+Ultimus). It, along with the [Changelings](https://web.archive.org/web/20120815000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?name=+%5Bchangeling%5D), has every creature type in **Magic**. And that means it's always changing as new creature types get added. One day it's a perfectly normal Caribou Assassin and then the next day it discovers it's a Germ Eldrazi Ally. Let's take a look at the entire list!
