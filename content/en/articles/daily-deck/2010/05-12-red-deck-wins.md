Red Deck Wins has been around for quite some time, but there's a reason they say it can win! Gabriel Willmon took a look at the new *Rise of the Eldrazi* and immediately made some changes to the archetype for Standard. Kargan Dragonlord and Kiln Fiend now occupy the two-drop slot, and Staggershock has been added to the cadre of burn spells in the deck. But the truly interesting addition? Devastating Summons. On the fourth turn Gabriel can cast it to make two 4/4 creatures while floating two additional mana. What to do with that? How about casting Goblin Bushwhacker with kicker? Take 12, thanks!
