### Notes:

Five Tinker decks were "tainted," meaning that they had black. 

Four Angry Hermit decks were "happy," meaning that they had white. 

Four Replenish decks were "academic," meaning that they had Bargain and Rector. 

### Rogue decks included:

*   Spore Frog
*   Scent of Jasmine
*   Green/Red/White Enchantress
*   White/Black/Green
*   Welder/Tinker
*   White/Blue Tinker-based
*   Blue/White Control Hermit Opposition Rebel
*   White/Green beatdown
*   Suicide Jank
*   White/Blue control
*   Red/Green Land Destruction
