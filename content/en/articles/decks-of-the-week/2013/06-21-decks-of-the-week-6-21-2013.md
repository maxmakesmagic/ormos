Shahar Shenhar showed he's one to watch with his third Grand Prix win at last weekend's [Grand Prix Houston](/en/events/2013/gphou13/shenhar-punishes-his-enemies-in-houston). With that win, Shahar will be in Dublin this October to compete at Pro Tour *Theros*. Would you like to join him there? One way to do it is to win a Pro Tour Qualifier! The current PTQ season uses the Standard format, so check out these Top 8 Standard decks from recent events and find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#theros). Good luck; have fun!
