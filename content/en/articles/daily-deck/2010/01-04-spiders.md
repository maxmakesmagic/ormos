Looking for a fun deck on a theme that has existed in **Magic** since the very first set? We present to you: Spiders! Featuring every creepy crawly member of the tribe (changelings not included), including a few wannabes like Orbweaver Kumo and Spinneret Sliver, Spiders! is a walk down an arachnidan memory lane. Plus, there are even a few Spider-themed spells to help you out of any tight spots you get into!
