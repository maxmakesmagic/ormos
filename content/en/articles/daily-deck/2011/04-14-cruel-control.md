The Extended season is winding down, but players are still innovating right to the very end. Kellen Abel recently finished in the Top 8 of a Pro Tour Qualifier in Boise, Idaho using this take on Cruel Control. With the powerful Cruel Ultimatum as a finishing touch, Kellen overwhelms his opponents. To get to that stage in the game he also relies on early spot removal like Go for the Throat and his creature suite of Inferno Titan and Sedraxis Specter.
