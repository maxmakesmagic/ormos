It's no secret, I'm a fan of our judges. I love meeting judges whenever I travel to the Pro Tour. With judges around the world helping to oversee and facilitate a fun and fair competitive **Magic** scene there are plenty more to meet! 

Most people see them as the referees of the game, which is certainly true, but it's far from the full picture. There is a very vibrant judge community to oversee game play, facilitate events, teach new players, and generally help the **Magic** community continue to grow and be more awesome! 

If you're interested in learning about becoming a judge, here are a few useful links for you:

*   [Official Judge Resource](https://web.archive.org/web/20140516160400/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=judge/welcome) - Start here and read about the program.
*   [How to Become a Certified Judge](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/reference-guide-for-dci-judge-certification-applicants) - A great introduction about how to become a certified judge.
*   [List of Regional Coordinators](/en/articles/event-coverage/2008/03-21-judge-regional-coordinators) - As the judge community has grown, so has the infrastructure. Not sure who to contact? Reach out to your nearest regional coordinator and he or she will be able to help.
*   [Become a Judge](http://wiki.magicjudges.org/en/w/Become_a_judge) - The judge community also maintains an [unofficial wiki](http://wiki.magicjudges.org/en/w/Main_Page) as a reference tool, and it has a number of useful articles. This is just one of them.

Now, let's take a look at these new promos!

First up is [Genesis](https://web.archive.org/web/20130828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genesis) in a new frame. It was originally released in *Judgment*, which used old card frames and even included a small tombstone next to the name to indicate it had abilities in the graveyard. This new printing does not use the tombstone and is just a sexy foil. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ab/ca/abca31f9ce5060cb0af6e9f1fe13526d0cfe55355bce1c0f214285a6f5f2c6db.jpg)  

Next up is a new [Vindicate](https://web.archive.org/web/20130828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vindicate) with new art and flavor text. Vindicate was originally printed in *Apocalypse* and has provided many an Orzhov with an effective removal against anything they might face on the board. 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b8/46/b8468febc4acd375aa959332617fb7ab6b2abfba48c528eb20e6898bb5286a2d.jpg)  

And here's a closer look at this new art by [Karla Ortiz](http://www.karlaortizart.com/) (whom you might remember from *Dragon's Maze*'s [Mending Touch](https://web.archive.org/web/20130828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mending+Touch) and [Teysa, Envoy of Ghosts](https://web.archive.org/web/20130828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teysa%2C+Envoy+of+Ghosts)).
