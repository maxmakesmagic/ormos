You may notice that <a>Oubliette</a>'s printed wording and Oracle wording don't much resemble one another. This clunky “considered out of play” idea, also used on <a>Tawnos's Coffin</a>, worked a lot like being removed from the game, except that counters and enchantments on the creature stayed. Why, that sounds like… Phasing! After <i>Mirage</i> was printed, <a>Oubliette</a>'s Oracle wording received a major facelift employing the new technology, representing a surprisingly small functional change for such a complete change in templating. </td>&#10;</tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

The “at end of turn” clause on the Revised through <i>Fifth Edition</i> printings of <a>Fungusaur</a> is not, as you might assume, the original wording.
