Learn more about Grand Prix Oakland's Top 8 competitors by taking a look through their profiles!  
  

* * *

 

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpoak16/t8_lingelbach.jpg)Name:** Cody Lingelbach

**Age:** 23

**Occupation:** Card game dealer

**Hometown:** Portland, Oregon

**Previous Magic accomplishments**: Multiple StarCityGames.com Open Top 8s; a bunch of Grand Prix Day 2s.

**What deck did you play and why?** Mardu Thopters. The format is full of removal so making thopters is insane.

**What sideboard card was the most important against the field? Why?** All of them. No one individual card overperformed.

**What *Oath of the Gatewatch* card are you most excited about? Why?**  
 I haven’t looked at the spoiler. Did they reprint [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls)?

**Living or dead, who’s your dream Two-Headed Giant teammate? Why?**  
 Brian Weller-Gordon.

* * *

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpoak16/t8_sinclair.jpg)Name:** Brett Sinclair

**Age:** 24

**Occupation:** Environmental engineer/specialist

**Hometown:** Orange

**Previous Magic accomplishments:** Nothing huge. A couple PPTQ and a first-place Grand Prix Trial finish.

**What deck did you play and why?**  
 4C [Rally the Ancestors](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rally+the+Ancestors). I was in on the deck shortly after the Pro Tour *Battle for Zendikar*. It does really busted stuff and has a ton of versatility and decision making.

**What sideboard card was the most important against the field? Why?**  
[Murderous Cut](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Murderous+Cut). That card is great and saves you versus [Anafenza, the Foremost](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anafenza%2C+the+Foremost) and Red combo.

**What *Oath of the Gatewatch* card are you most excited about? Why?**  
 Logic-Knot Seer. It’s hard to say if colorless cards will be constructed playable, but “Vendilion Clique 2.0” looks sweet.

**Living or dead, who’s your dream Two-Headed Giant teammate? Why?**  
 Attila the Hun. He’d scare my opponents into an easy concession. GG. Eh. Probably LSV. He has a good attitude and seems to go deep and know about everything.

* * *

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpoak16/t8_engle.jpg)Name:** Woodrow Engle

**Age:** 32

**Occupation:** Video game artist

**Hometown:** Seattle, Washington

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**  
 RPTQ Top 8; PTQ Top 8

**What deck did you play and why?**  
 Atarka red. I had played an earlier version of the deck last year and felt it was the deck I was the best with.

**What sideboard card was the most important against the field? Why?**  
[Rending Volley](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rending+Volley)—killing [Soulfire Grand Master](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soulfire+Grand+Master) [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy), and [Dragonlord Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Ojutai).

**What *Oath of the Gatewatch* card are you most excited about? Why?**  
 Basic Land – Wastes. It’s just cool!

**Living or dead, who’s your dream Two-Headed Giant teammate? Why?**  
 Marshall Sutcliffe.

* * *

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpoak16/t8_smith.jpg)Name:** Nathaniel Smith

**Age:** 20

**Occupation:** uh … Pro Wizard. Student.

**Hometown:** Omaha, Nebraska + Houston, Texas!

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**  
 Top 8 Grand Prix Dallas 2015; MOCS competitor 2015; 29th Pro Tour *Fate Reforged*  
 A whole lot of Grand Prix Top 16s. Silver Pro statues (last season, and guaranteed this season!)

**What deck did you play and why?**  
 Abzan with an updated mana base because I couldn’t find a reason not to play it. Oh, I cut all the two drops for more removal.

**What sideboard card was the most important against the field? Why?**  
[Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress). All four. No regrets. Why? Because you take the card that beats you!

**What *Oath of the Gatewatch* card are you most excited about? Why?**  
 Matter Reshaper because it’s an exciting effect.

**Living or dead, who’s your dream Two-Headed Giant teammate? Why?**  
 Donnie “the yung god ca$h money $$” Peck, duh!!

* * *

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpoak16/t8_mcclain.jpg)Name:** Josh McClain

**Age:** 26

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Iowa City, Iowa

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**  
 Grand Prix Detroit Champion

**What deck did you play and why?**  
[Rally the Ancestors](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rally+the+Ancestors), the deck just feels like the most powerful and resilient deck in the format by a mile.

**What sideboard card was the most important against the field? Why?**  
[Murderous Cut](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Murderous+Cut). Killing [Anafenza, the Foremost](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anafenza%2C+the+Foremost) is so important.

**What *Oath of the Gatewatch* card are you most excited about? Why?**  
 The 3/3 [Flash](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flash) that when it enter the battlefield you can pay <> to make a spawn for each creature that died this turn \[Vile Redeemer\]. It’s a tricky card, that might be good in Rally.  
  
**Living or dead, who’s your dream Two-Headed Giant teammate? Why?**  
 Adam Tellis, I can’t abandon him now!

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpoak16/t8_nguyen.jpg)Name:** Justin Nguyen

**Age:** 24

**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Yorba Linda, California

**Previous Magic accomplishments:**

**What deck did you play and why?**

**What *Oath of the Gatewatch* card are you most excited about? Why?**  
 The new angel is pretty sweet! \[Linvala, the Preserver\]

**Living or dead, who’s your dream Two-Headed Giant teammate? Why?**  
 My little brother, Cody Nguyen.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpoak16/t8_rubin.jpg)Name:** Ben Rubin

**Age:** 33

**Occupation:** Data Analyst

**Hometown:** San Francisco, California

**Previous Magic accomplishments:** Hall of Fame, etc… a long time ago.

**What deck did you play and why?**  
 “Wet Abzan” (Blue Abzan). I was able to tame it to do unexpected things from an already powerful shell. [Stubborn Denial](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stubborn+Denial) is a very undervalued card.

**What sideboard card was the most important against the field? Why?**  
[Rising Miasma](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rising+Miasma). It’s a great card against Red and good against Tokens.

**What *Oath of the Gatewatch* card are you most excited about? Why?**  
 Can’t name one in particular.

**Living or dead, who’s your dream Two-Headed Giant teammate? Why?**  
 Ken Ho. He’s even better than my current awesome Two-Headed Giant partner Jeff Ciu. True artists.
