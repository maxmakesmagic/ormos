[Bogardan Hellkite](https://web.archive.org/web/20100329000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bogardan+Hellkite), [Hellkite Charger](https://web.archive.org/web/20100329000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellkite+Charger), [Spellbound Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20100329000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellbound+Dragon), and honorary "Dragon" [Sphinx of Jwar Isle](https://web.archive.org/web/20100329000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx+of+Jwar+Isle).
