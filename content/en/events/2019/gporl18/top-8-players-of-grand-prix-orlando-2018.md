After 15 rounds of Standard at Grand Prix Orlando 2018 we were left with just eight players. Let's take a look at who they are, what was their weapon of choice, and which cards performed well for them over the weekend.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gporl18/gpOrlando2018_Top8_Kyle%20Cooper.JPG)

**Name:** Kyle Cooper

**Age:** 19

**Occupation:** Aftercare Monitor

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Day 2 Grand Prix Atlanta 2017.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Mono-Red Flame of Keld. It was well positioned against Nexus (Turbo Fog).

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Wizard's Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wizard%27s+Lightning). It is [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) in Standard.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

Hazoret. It attacks very well and comes in a lot.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gporl18/gpOrlando2018_Top8_Adam%20Fox.JPG)

**Name:** Adam Fox

**Age:** 32

**Occupation:** Self-employed

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Various Pro Tour appearances. Nothing notable.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Grixis Midrange. Everything but Mono-Green felt beatable.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Glint-Sleeve Siphoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint-Sleeve+Siphoner).

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Chandra's Defeat](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Defeat). Very versatile.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gporl18/gpOrlando2018_Top8_Gabriel%20Joglar.JPG)

**Name:** Gabriel Joglar

**Age:** 25

**Occupation:** Bartender

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Some Pro Tours, SCG Top 8 and random other stuff.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

UW Approach. It's good against the [Nexus of Fate](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nexus+of+Fate) deck (Turbo Fog) and I have a good sideboard plan for red-black.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

Teferi is the best planeswalker printed in a long time. ([Seal Away](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seal+Away) is close second.)

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[History of Benalia](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=History+of+Benalia) attacks other decks from other angles.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gporl18/gpOrlando2018_Top8_Andrew%20Calderon.JPG)

**Name:** Andrew Calderon

**Age:** 30

**Occupation:** PPG player

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Won GP Vegas Legacy. TCG Invitational finalist.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Esper Control. I made the deck to beat RB and I did.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Torrential Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrential+Gearhulk). It did the most work and had the most value this weekend.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Arguel's Blood Fast](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arguel%27s+Blood+Fast). It surprised me how much value both sides provided.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gporl18/gpOrlando2018_Top8_Greg%20Sokol.JPG)

**Name:** Greg Sokol

**Age:** 28

**Occupation:** Magic Coach. Limited specialist ("I don't play constructed")

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Won an online PTQ a few months back.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Anti-Red Black. Join 'em (with Harvesters)

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Aethersphere Harvester](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aethersphere+Harvester). 3/5 booty.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Lost Legacy](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lost+Legacy). Silver bullets all day.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gporl18/gpOrlando2018_Top8_Ian%20Plantz.JPG)

**Name:** Ian Plantz

**Age:** 25

**Occupation:** Technical Consultant @ Dell

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

Two-time PPTQ Champion.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

GB Constrictor, because I feel it has a great Control and Turbo Fog matchup.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Thrashing Brontodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thrashing+Brontodon), because I destroyed a lot of [Gift of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gift+of+Paradise) and [Search for Azcanta](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Search+for+Azcanta) this weekend. It's also a wall versus red decks.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress), as the information and utility versus Control and Combo is great.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gporl18/gpOrlando2018_Top8_Corey%20Burkhart.JPG)

**Name:** Corey Burkhart

**Age:** 26

**Occupation:** Game Designer

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

3 GP Top 8's. Member of team Face to Face games. Gold? Platinum? Pro. Who knows.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

UB Midrange. Has the Grixis interaction, but a better mana base. Shout out to former US National Champ Michael Jacob for helping me out with the mana/list.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Censor](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Censor). I countered an unreal number of Teferi and Chandras with it. I also cycled into a [Vraska's Contempt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vraska%27s+Contempt) to an [Angrath, the Flame-Chained](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angrath%2C+the+Flame-Chained) stealing my Scarab God.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Spell Pierce](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Pierce). Shout out to MJ. 1 mana interaction is so key.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gporl18/gpOrlando2018_Top8_Arseniy%20Egorov.JPG)

**Name:** Arseniy Egorov

**Age:** 29

**Occupation:** *Magic: the Gathering* judge

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**

25th Anniversary Pro Tour.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Grixis Control. Dmitry Butakov was playing the same deck this weekend in Brussels, after the PT. I asked him what he would recommend and he sent me this list. It's good against current meta and has good answers against Turns (Turbo Fog).

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Arguel's Blood Fast](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arguel%27s+Blood+Fast) clearly MVP. It helps to stabilize vs Aggro decks (especially with [The Scarab God](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Scarab+God)) and it gives you tons of fuel vs Control and Combo.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20180812000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress). All matches I sided it in I drew all three and won.
