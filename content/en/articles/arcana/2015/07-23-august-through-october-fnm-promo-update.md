The month is nearly over, so once again it’s time to take a trip in our Friday Night Magic time machine and see what promos we have in store for the coming months.

**August**

Do you see? Do you see? Your vision of the future will be 20/20 come August, when [Serum Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20150723000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serum+Visions)—with art by Dan Scott—comes to an FNM near you.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/EN_cardpromo_SerumVisions.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_160402.jpg)

[Serum Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20150723000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serum+Visions) Promo | Art by [Dan Scott](https://web.archive.org/web/20150723000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Dan%20Scott%22%5d)

**September**

The trip back to Standard will be softened with this sturdy Bird Monk, [Orator of Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20150723000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Orator+of+Ojutai)—the Dragoniest [Wall of Omens](https://web.archive.org/web/20150723000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wall+of+Omens) ever. You'll be able to get your copy come September, whether you choose to employ Dragons or some other inferior creatures to do so.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/en_uwqrt40izi.png)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/cardart_s6idki155h.jpg)

[Orator of Ojutai](https://web.archive.org/web/20150723000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Orator+of+Ojutai) Promo | Art by [David Gaillet](https://web.archive.org/web/20150723000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22David%20Gaillet%22%5d)

**October**

What's in the bag? WHAT'S IN THE BAG?!?!

The spookiest month gets a spooky upgrade to [Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20150723000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price) with new art by Scott Murphy. Look away if you're squeamish, because John Doe has the upper hand.
