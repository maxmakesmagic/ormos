![](http://ww2.wizards.com/magic/images/tournamentcenter/2007/citychamps_logo.jpg)

Congratulations to all the winners of the 2008 City Championships Finals! 

Each winner earned a berth to their respective National Championship (either United States or Canada). All other Top 8 finishers earned byes at Regionals. Check out the Top 8 Standard decklists for each city below.

You can test each decklist thanks to our [Sample Hand Generator](https://web.archive.org/web/20090602023708/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/294). Just click on the card fan in the upper right of each decklist and you can see what your opening hand could be, along with mulligans and the first three cards you'd draw.

Thanks to everyone who participated in the 2008 City Championships season.

Note: The incorrect winner was listed for the New York Finals. The correct name is shown below.
