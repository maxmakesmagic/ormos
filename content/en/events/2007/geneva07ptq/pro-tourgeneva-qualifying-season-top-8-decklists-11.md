![](http://www.wizards.com/protour/images/qt_lg.jpg) Refine your Sealed Deck and Booster Draft skills to earn a ticket to Geneva, Switzerland, by winning a Pro Tour Qualifier! 

This season features *Time Spiral* Booster Draft. The decks linked here are the Top 8 decks from North American Pro Tour Qualifiers.

You can test each decklist thanks to our [Sample Hand Generator](https://web.archive.org/web/20130624094147/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/feature/294). Just click on the card fan in the upper right of each decklist and you can see what your opening hand could be, along with mulligans and the first three cards you'd draw.

Decklists will be updated weekly during the PTQ season. The season runs until December 31. To find a PTQ near you, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20130629200418/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/events/protour?x=protour/geneva07/qualifiers). Pro Tour-Geneva is scheduled for February 9-11, 2007, and the format is *Time Spiral* Booster Draft.
