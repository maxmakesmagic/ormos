# Did you play the World Magic Cup Qualifier last weekend?

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppra15/gppra15_qq_floch.jpg)  
*Ivan Floch* 

**Ivan Floch:** "No, since I'm the captain of Slovakia I'm already qualified. A local guy, Robert Gregor won, so I'm happy for him." 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppra15/gppra15_qq_anteri.jpg)  
*Fabrizio Anteri* 

**Fabrizio Anteri:** "I didn't, because I'm the captain for England. It was won by Low Law however, who's an old time PTQ-grinder. I don't think he has played any Pro Tours, but I've played against him and he's a decent player so I'm happy to have him on the team." 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppra15/gppra15_qq_severin.jpg)  
*Thoralf Severin* 

**Thoralf Severin:** "No, I was on vacation with friends, but I probably would have played the same deck as in Prague, which is Blue-White control." 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppra15/gppra15_qq_mengucci.jpg)  
*Andrea Mengucci* 

**Andrea Mengucci:** "I did! I played Hangerback Abzan and I won the WMCQ. The tournament had 229 players in it and was nine rounds of swiss in a two day event." 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gppra15/gppra15_qq_bonde.jpg)  
*Michael Bonde* 

**Michael Bonde:** "Yes. I played Hangerback Abzan with [Tasigur, the Golden Fang](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tasigur%2C+the+Golden+Fang), [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion) and [Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20150829000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc). I started 5-0, but lost the last two rounds and ended up ninth on tie-breakers."
