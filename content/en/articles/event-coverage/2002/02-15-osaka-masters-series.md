The appeals window for Osaka Masters Invites has passed. 

The Qualifications Criteria is as follows:

*   The winner and finalist team of the previous team-format Pro Tour
*   The winner of the previous team-format Masters Series
*   The Top 4 DCI (three-person) Team Limited-ranked teams who were previously uninvited, as of the January 30, 2002 ratings publish.
*   The Gateway tournament-winning team

The Following Teams were invited because of previous finishes:

*   **Phoenix Foundation:** Kai Budde, Marco Blume, and Dirk Baberowski
*   **Les Plus Class:** Nicolas Oliveri, Gabriel Nassif, Amiel Tenenbaum
*   **Panzer Hunter:** Itaru Ishida, Kazuyuki Momose, Reiji Andou.

Panzer Hunter replaced AlphaBetaUnlimited.com because of the suspension of Ryan Fuller.

The Following Teams are invited based on DCI Rating:

*   **www.star-maker.nl/lap** - Top 4 DCI Team Limited-rank: Jelger Wiegersma, Victor van den Broek, Frank Karsten
*   **Your Move Games** - Top 4 DCI Team Limited-rank: Darwin Kastle, Robert Dougherty, David Humphreys
*   **Team Outland** - Top 4 DCI Team Limited-rank: Nicolai Herzog, Bjorn Jocumsen, Eivand Nitter
*   **Slay-Pillage-Massacre** - Top 4 DCI Team Limited-rank: Scott McCord, Jonathan Sonne, Eric Ziegler

Those teams and 1 team from the Masters Gateway in Osaka will play *Odyssey-Odyssey-Torment* Team Rochester Draft for $156,000 in Osaka.
