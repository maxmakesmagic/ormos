It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/friday-night-magic-6)! Since we're now in May, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

![Squadron Hawk](https://i.ormos.uk/bb/0a/bb0ad6e705d1ccdf0aa1542cdff82441b7dc46c6d9d70ce226f2af2ed80ee397.jpg "Squadron Hawk") 

  

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards feature the FNM logo and many have original flavor text and updated wordings.
