## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9a/ed/9aed272f5ecf59d9356034810dbf9b04f35f50658718a2b8f2404acc28eac037.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set as well as two deck boxes, nine 15-card booster packs, 80 basic land cards, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/bd/e1/bde14c20ae594059d2e07198d82cd928c9c0b04a8e1859e52a86f6cb0113ea63.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e8/14/e8141ddbac36985862245eb75a050209d456a86cbf1c58e076796e5bc1b4b00e.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 cards, a foil premium rare card, one sealed 15-card booster pack, a strategy insert, and a **Magic** learn-to-play guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718094531/http://magic.wizards.com/node/286081)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c7/53/c7532097aff08e9abb3d18f85be5a9e088c92aa5a145d1cf98a5748d664427b2.png)

### BOOSTER BATTLE PACKS

Want to play with a friend right now? Just choose which of the two 22-card decks you’ll each play, insert up to five cards from the two included booster packs—and begin! It’s that easy.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/56/39/5639a11d4cc7507dcafeb239e0878ba6068196563c1bee88e5fcd045b7e87c53.png)

### EVENT DECKS

Battle like the best, right out of the box. Includes a tournament-ready 60-card deck plus a 15-card “sideboard” to swap in as needed, depending on what you expect your opponent will play.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718094531/http://magic.wizards.com/node/286086)

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f6/24/f624905bf27b1882c76695004e7fb75c1e98323e440be1f3ada40fee97bc244d.png)

The **Magic: The Gathering***2012 Core Set* features 249 black-bordered cards, including randomly inserted premium versions of all cards in the set.

**Set Name:***2012 Core Set*  
**Number of Cards:** 249

**Languages:** English, Chinese Traditional, German, French, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Japanese, Spanish, Korean

**Official Three-Letter Code:** M12  
**Twitter Hashtag:** #MTGM12

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718094531/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/faq/m12) (or download as [RTF](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718094531/http://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/MTGM12_FAQ_EN.rtf))

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Mark Globus (lead), Doug Beyer, Aaron Forsythe, and Kenneth Nagle

**Final Game Design and Development:** Tom LaPille (lead), Kelly Digges, David Humpherys, Peter Schaefer, Mike Turian, and Steve Warner
