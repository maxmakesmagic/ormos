We've dispatched some of our top reporters across the world to bring you all the action from a big Grand Prix weekend. What changes can be expected to the Extended metagame since Vancouver? How have the developments from the Pro Tour–Hollywood PTQ season changed the gamescape? Tune in all weekend to find out!

### [Grand Prix–Vienna](/en/events/2008/gpvie08/polish-level-blue-polishes-off-dredge)
