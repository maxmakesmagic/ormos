We [promised](/en/articles/arcana/2005/02-23-ninth-edition-core-game) more *Ninth Edition* goodies, so here you go.

As with the [*Eighth Edition*](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603114056/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Article.aspx?x=magic/expansion/8e) of **Magic's** Core Set, *Ninth Edition* features five pre-constructed theme decks, one for each color. 

White's *Ninth Edition* theme deck is called **Army of Justice**, and it looks a little something like this:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/d3/ea/d3eaa977886be6bc985b185d2624f4b012aa5873a12e6fe83ec2561e41dae8dc.jpg)  
 *Ninth Edition theme deck 1 of 5* 

*Ninth Edition*, chock full of [your](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603113844/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/selecting9e/wrapup) card choices, art decisions and flavor text, releases Summer 2005. Tune in next week for more *Ninth Edition* sneak peeks.
