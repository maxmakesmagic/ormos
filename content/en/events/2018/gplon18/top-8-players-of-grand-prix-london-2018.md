After fifteen rounds, with a total of 1,971 others eliminated, only eight players remained in the running for the fame and the fortune, the title and the trophy. Find out more about these people here!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gplon18/t8_freeman.jpg)

**Name:** Marcelino Freeman

**Age:** 31

**Occupation:** Transfer pricing manager at Kreston

**Hometown:** Puebla, Mexico

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 

Two Grand Prix Top 8s.

**What was the best card in your Sealed Deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

Black-red, [Rekindling Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rekindling+Phoenix), 8-1, and [Ravenous Chupacabra](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ravenous+Chupacabra)!

**What was the best card in your first Booster Draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

Blue-black, 3-0, [Kitesail Corsair](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kitesail+Corsair).

**What was the best card in your second Booster Draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

Green-blue, 2-0-1, two [Kitesail Corsair](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kitesail+Corsair)!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gplon18/t8_rubin.jpg)

**Name:** Steve Rubin

**Age:** 25

**Occupation:** Magic, writing, gaming

**Hometown:** Pittsburgh, PA

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 

Eight GP Top 8s, three consecutive Platinum years, two PTQ wins, two SCG wins, one PT win.

**What was the best card in your Sealed Deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

Green-blue. [Hadana's Climb](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hadana%27s+Climb).

**What was the best card in your first Booster Draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

2-1. Black-Red Pirates.

**What was the best card in your second Booster Draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

2-1. Blue-Red Pirates splashing black.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gplon18/t8_manfield.jpg)

**Name:** Seth Manfield

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** *Magic* pro

**Hometown:** Columbia, MD

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 

Five Grand Prix wins, Pro Tour *Ixalan* winner.

**What was the best card in your Sealed Deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

[Ravenous Chupacabra](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ravenous+Chupacabra), was better than [Impale](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Impale). Blue-black splash green.

**What was the best card in your first Booster Draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

Red-Green Dinos. Two [Charging Monstrosaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Charging+Monstrosaur) and [The Immortal Sun](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Immortal+Sun).

**What was the best card in your second Booster Draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

White-Black Vampires. [Forerunner of the Legion](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forerunner+of+the+Legion).

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gplon18/t8_jacobson.jpg)

**Name:** Mark Jacobson

**Age:** 27

**Occupation:** Hardware engineer

**Hometown:** Philadelpthia, PA

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 

12th at Pro Tour *Magic Origins*, 11-5 at Pro Tour *Kaladesh*, Top 4 at Grand Prix Toronto 2015 and Grand Prix Houston 2016.

**What was the best card in your Sealed Deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

8-1, starting on red-white. Boarded into five different color configurations depending on the matchup. Best card was [Arch of Orazca](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arch+of+Orazca).

**What was the best card in your first Booster Draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

2-1 with Blue-Red Aggro and [Storm Fleet Sprinter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Storm+Fleet+Sprinter).

**What was the best card in your second Booster Draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

3-0 Green-Blue Merfolk and two [Merfolk Mistbinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merfolk+Mistbinder). Honorable mention goes to sideboard [Plummet](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plummet). On the penultimate turn of my win-and-in, my opponent put [One With the Wind](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=One+With+the+Wind) on a 3/3 Dino token and then attempted to [Hunt the Weak](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hunt+the+Weak) ...

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gplon18/t8_sajgalik.jpg)

**Name:** Eduardo Sajgalik

**Age:** 30 this weekend

**Occupation:** Community consultant

**Hometown:** Montreal, Canada

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:** 

Two Pro Tour Top 8s (*Return to Ravnica*/*Aether Revolt*). Multiple national teams (UK/England/Canada). Fifth Grand Prix Top 8.

**What was the best card in your Sealed Deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

Black-red. [Charging Monstrosaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Charging+Monstrosaur) was best, [Dark Inquiry](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Inquiry) was MVP. 7-2, or 4-2 plus three byes.

**What was the best card in your first Booster Draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

Black-Red Aggro Pirates. Deck was a team effort, but [Dire Fleet Poisoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dire+Fleet+Poisoner) was best.

**What was the best card in your second Booster Draft deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

White-Blue Fliers with three [Expel from Orazca](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Expel+from+Orazca). MVP was [Snubhorn Sentry](https://web.archive.org/web/20180128000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snubhorn+Sentry), 3/3s for 1 are great.
