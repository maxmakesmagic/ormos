## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/1e/34/1e34a42bdb1e6cd6c4c36aba2698df7c793c4b4c741c8166676893345359f029.png)Each pack of *Vintage Masters* will contain:

*   10 commons
*   3 uncommons
*   1 rare or mythic rare
*   1 premium foil card (any rarity, including the extremely rare premium foil Power Nine cards) **OR** 1 Power Nine card

**Set Name:** *Vintage Masters*   
**Number of Cards:** 325 

**Languages**: English  
**Twitter Hashtag:** [#MTGOVM](https://twitter.com/#!/search/realtime/%23mtgovm)

**Official Three-Letter Code**: VMA

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Max McCall (lead) and Ethan Fleischer

**Final Game Design and Development:** Ian Duke (lead), Max McCall, Adam Prosak, with contributions by Tim Aten
