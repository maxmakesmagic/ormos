Welcome back to "On the Mirrodin Site," where we show off the week's highlights from the [Mirrodin Site](https://web.archive.org/web/20101206125256/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/). This week, the Phyrexians took their invasion to the site itself, allowing people to decide which faction they would stand with.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/activity/da550_thumb.jpg) 

  

To see the Phyrexian version of the site (which comes with its own updates, field reports, wallpapers, screen savers, and so on), just go to the [Mirrodin Site](https://web.archive.org/web/20101206125256/http://www.wizards.com/magic/mirrodin/) and click "Change Faction!"
