The China National Championships were held this past weekend. One of the most eye-opening decks in the Top 8 was played by one Li Meng. He piloted Mono-Black Control, one of the game's most powerful archetypes historically. Does Li's deck signal the latest coming of MBC? Check out the full [China National Championships coverage](/en/events/2011/chnat11/a-champion-is-born) to see how his games played out.
