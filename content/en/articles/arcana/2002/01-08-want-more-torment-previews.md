Can't get enough of *Torment?* Want more advance information? Then check out the current issue of *Sideboard* Magazine... Included are *Torment* previews of Basking Rootwalla by Mike Turian and Nantuko Shade by David Price. Plus, our own Mark Rosewater gives a few more hints (including Mind Sludge) in his "Insider Trading" column.

And, as usual, you get top-notch coverage of major events, profiles of your favorite players, and strategy articles straight from the Pros! The *Sideboard* Magazine is available at all premier stores and premier events.
