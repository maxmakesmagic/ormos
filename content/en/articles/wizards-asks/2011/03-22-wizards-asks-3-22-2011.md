*Do you have a signature deck or decks?* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e0/0c/e00c2900e4532fed44efdbc0223f486ee1518c0cf594bb289867319eeda0f043.jpg)

**Kelly Digges:** Signature decks are a funny thing. They're most common in casual circles (particularly Commander) and in the relatively stable Vintage and Legacy formats, but even in more tumultuous formats, you'll find people associated indelibly with particular decks—off the top of my head, Dave Price with red decks, Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa with Faeries, and of course Jamie Wakefield with the classic Secret Force deck.

I'm an almost obsessive deck builder, and I've logged vastly more hours building decks for Commander, my Constructed format of choice, than I have actually, you know, playing it. At any given time, I have something like a dozen Commander decks of varying completeness and quality. In that mass of decks and relatively steady churn, it's not easy to say that any one of them is my signature deck. But without question, there's one that's nearer and dearer to my heart than any of the others.

 ![Multani, Maro-Sorcerer](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Multani,%20Maro-Sorcerer&options=) 

  

It's my Multani, Maro-Sorcerer deck, which is entirely built around something that, through whatever quirk of personality or experience, is one of my very favorite things to do in **Magic**: drawing cards for green mana.
