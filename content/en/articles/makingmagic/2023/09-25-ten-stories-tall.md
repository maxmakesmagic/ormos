When I talked about the design of *Wilds of Eldraine* ([Part 1](/en/articles/makingmagic/2023/08-15-wilds-story-part-1) and [Part 2](/en/articles/makingmagic/2023/08-21-wilds-story-part-2)), I discussed how we chose to take the ten two-color draft archetypes and attach a story to each one. Today, I'm going to examine each of those ten stories and walk through what exactly the story is (as we enjoy doing our take on the classics) and how we illustrated it on cards. Note that the titles are the fairy-tale stories we were inspired by.

I'm going to start by showing the five stories that are part of the main storyline with Kellan, in the order they appear in that story. I will then talk about the five other stories.

## Sleeping Beauty (Blue-Black)

### Characters

![Talion, the Kindly Lord](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_0284d07b3e.png) ![Obyra, Dreaming Duelist](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_455d78a0f0.png)

*   **Talion** – The leader of the High Fae. Talion is curious, supremely powerful, and otherworldly. They (Talion uses they/them pronouns) regard themselves as the protector of Eldraine.
*   **Obyra** – Talion's daughter and the greatest duelist in Eldraine. She's currently trapped under the effects of the Wicked Slumber, but her finely tuned sword-fighting instincts can't be suppressed—Obyra roams Eldraine, seeking other duelists even while asleep.

![Asinine Antics](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_b94eac1d3d.png) ![Chancellor of Tales](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_d644cd7f8e.png) ![High Fae Negotiator](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_9b1efa7dba.png)

![Likeness Looter](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_6957ac2285.png) ![Mocking Sprite](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_fdad01c4da.png)

![Spell Stutter](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_f142e8f5f6.png) ![Twining Twins](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_a0536d35b2.png)

The High Fae, led by Talion, are a rowdy bunch that enjoy causing mischief. They all returned from a jaunt to the edge of the plane to find the Phyrexians invading. Striking a deal with the three witch sisters, Agatha, Hylda, and Eriette, a massive sleeping spell, known as the Wicked Slumber, spread throughout Eldraine and turned the tides of the invasion.

![Dream Spoilers](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_494846f65e.png) ![Ego Drain](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_9e78e5b472.png) ![Spellscorn Coven](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_cc315d2313.png)

After the Phyrexians were defeated, the Wicked Slumber remained and grew in power.

![Cruel Somnophage](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_600336507b.png) ![Ashiok's Reaper](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_1b3536d137.png)

![Wicked Visitor](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_ce6c8b685e.png) ![Taken by Nightmares](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_3e393666b3.png)

It attracted creatures that feed upon dreams and nightmares.

![Sleep-Cursed Faerie](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_ae77fa8533.png)

Eventually, it took hold in Tuinvale, where the High Fae have taken up residence.

![Faerie Fencing](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_1572dfa5e8.png)

Obyra, Talion's daughter, has fallen asleep and is causing havoc as she sleep-duels her way across the plane.

![Talion's Messenger](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_f29838657d.png) ![Into the Fae Court](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_d5158583d7.png)

Talion calls on Kellan, a boy from the Boundary Lands, to go retrieve the three gifts they gave to the witch sisters (the soul cauldron, the winter crown, and the tempting apple) to break the spell.

![Obyra's Attendants](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_c151d4a9dd.png)

Meanwhile, Obyra's attendants, Beauty, Song, and Blessing, try to keep her out of trouble with whatever means necessary.

## Little Red Riding Hood (Red-Green)

### Characters

![Ruby, Daring Tracker](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_4b40ea7856.png) ![Agatha of the Vile Cauldron](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_2e46e02846.png) ![Agatha's Champion](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_18bf0533ab.png)

*   **Ruby** – A tough, street-smart young woman who would do anything for her family. She is cunning and resourceful.
*   **Agatha** – One of the three witch sisters. Her kind and motherly appearance disguises her bestial nature.
*   **The Wolf** – Ruby's brother and a fearsome warrior. He is enthralled by Agatha and forced to fight his sister.

### Story

![Agatha's Soul Cauldron](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_5468e9c629.png)

The witch Agatha was gifted a magical cauldron by Talion for her role in creating the Wicked Slumber.

![Commune with Nature](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_e53ada1cf4.png)

The cauldron has the power to boil down living creatures into their magical essence. She sets up nearby Edgewall, hoping to trick some poor fools into becoming her next meal.

![Witch's Mark](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_891b8e9dfb.png) ![Witchstalker Frenzy](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_fbf9696aa5.png)

Meanwhile, Ruby's brother has suddenly gone missing. Unbeknownst to Ruby, her brother was taken and ensorcelled by Agatha to be her muscle. Ruby meets Kellan in the streets of Edgewall, who was sent there to retrieve the cauldron and other artifacts for Talion. She agrees to help him find the local witch, hoping that it might lead her to her brother.

![Feral Encounter](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_475f37928b.png) ![Brave the Wilds](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_30ead7e19f.png) ![Bellowing Bruiser](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_099045f6b1.png)

![Territorial Witchstalker](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_e21015f26a.png) ![Two-Headed Hunter](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_805391fae8.png) ![Picnic Ruiner](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_b40d3aee13.png)

![Leaping Ambush](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_5854dcc91d.png) ![Boundary Lands Ranger](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_b11477f434.png) ![Monstrous Rage](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_0647cfd098.png)

After plenty of dangerous encounters in the forests of the Boundary Lands, Ruby and Kellan reach Agatha's hut.

![Feed the Cauldron](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_5faa9cfa95.png)

After a fierce battle, Kellan pushes Agatha into her own cauldron, boiling her alive.

![A Tale for the Ages](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_3d6e5f6925.png)

Finished with his first quest, Kellan journeys onward with Ruby.

## Jack and the Beanstalk (Green-Blue)

### Characters

![Troyan, Gutsy Explorer](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_438f9767db.png) ![Beluna Grandsquall](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_993895164b.png)

![Ruby, Daring Tracker](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_4b40ea7856.png) ![Kellan, the Fae-Blooded](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_856c3ca0db.png)

*   **Troyan** – A vedalken from Ravnica who journeyed here through the Omenpaths.
*   **Beluna Grandsquall** – A giantess noble and the leader of the Stormkeld giants. Also coincidentally a kleptomaniac.
*   **Ruby and Kellan** – This duo from our main story journey up the beanstalk to find the mirror of Vantress, hoping to ask where the ice witch Hylda is located.

### Story

![The Magic Mirror](https://media.wizards.com/2019/eld/en_97D0izHn9u.png)

Ruby and Kellan seek the three gifts that Talion gave to the witch sisters to break the Wicked Slumber. They enlist the help of Troyan, a strange explorer from a distant land, to lead them to the fabled magic mirror Indrelon, the treasure of Castle Vantress, which has been stolen.

![Up the Beanstalk](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_3bcf1b76c9.png) ![Beanstalk Wurm](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_a289add8db.png)

They journey up the beanstalk and arrive in the Stormkeld, home to the storm giants.

![Beluna's Gatekeeper](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_46d718ec2f.png) ![Disdainful Stroke](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_4aba52dd14.png) ![Galvanic Giant](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_4b33b71240.png)

![Howling Galefang](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_3c5819ee39.png) ![Stormkeld Vanguard](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_0ee68859a6.png)

![Skybeast Tracker](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_39fbba4c98.png) ![Tempest Hart](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_33c20b114e.png)

They sneak past many dangers, finding themselves in the parlor of the storm giants—on Beluna's birthday.

![Stormkeld Prowler](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_0fcf8f0dd4.png)

Beluna is slow to trust them. After all, outsiders might be here to steal her many precious treasures (never mind that she stole them all first).

![Collector's Vault](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_b2f8193368.png)

At the insistence of Yorvo, former ruler of Garenbrig, Beluna lets them into her vault to consult the mirror.

![Solitary Sanctuary](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_1391d3c33d.png)

They discover that the witch queen has built a fortress over Loch Larent and head there immediately.

## The Snow Queen (White-Blue)

### Characters

![Hylda of the Icy Crown](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_94fa0b6594.png) ![Sharae of Numbing Depths](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_cfd005d08b.png)

*   **Hylda** – One of the three witch sisters. She is as aloof and cold as the blizzards she commands.
*   **Sharae** – Leader of the merfolk of Loch Larent, who Hylda trapped under the surface of the lake when she formed her icy castle.

### Story

![Hylda's Crown of Winter](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_29d06ab3e2.png) ![Bitter Chill](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_4b42a34522.png) ![Freeze in Place](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_94fa0b6594.png)

As a gift for helping create the Wicked Slumber, Hylda receives a crown of wintery power from Talion capable of conjuring blizzards, snow, and ice.

![Ice Out](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_f3c0b80850.png) ![Solitary Sanctuary](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_1391d3c33d.png) ![Rimefur Reindeer](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_6b355dc08f.png)

She creates a kingdom of ice in the farthest reaches of Dunbarrow, above Loch Larent.

![Succumb to the Cold](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_d759ff1bb3.png) ![Plunge into Winter](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_80d8113488.png) ![Merfolk Coralsmith](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_a8b1ce8fa6.png)

Her ice powers cause problems for the residents of the land and freeze over the local lake, trapping the merfolk in and beneath the ice.

![Farsight Ritual](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_fe618c0872.png)

Kellan and Ruby, who are seeking out the gifts that Talion bestowed, come to Hylda's frozen kingdom searching for the icy crown.

![Frostbridge Guard](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_b35ee43edf.png) ![Icewrought Sentry](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_58ae1abca9.png)

He battles with the ice elemental guardians.

![Kindled Heroism](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_7cdc0f1725.png) ![Kellan's Lightblades](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_f413e23b46.png)

At a critical moment, he discovers his ability to manifest weapons made from ethereal light.

Forming a glowing blade, he strikes an elemental down. Kellan collapses before he can reach Hylda. Ruby carries him nearly to the castle gates.

![Break the Spell](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_30e4ec0eea.png)

In a moment of kindness, Hylda kisses Kellan's forehead. With her blessing, Kellan and Ruby are protected from the cold.

## Snow White (White-Black)

### Characters

![Neva, Stalked by Nightmares](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_688ab78f21.png) ![Eriette of the Charmed Apple](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_3c6029d7e3.png)

*   **Neva** – A young noble who was sealed in a glass casket when the Wicked Slumber took her.
*   **Eriette** – One of the three witch sisters. She is vain and controlling.

### Story

![Glass Casket](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_d55b36ff84.png)

As the Wicked Slumber sweeps over the land, Neva succumbs to the spell and is placed in a glass casket to protect her while the wizards of Vantress study the slumber's effects.

![Eriette's Tempting Apple](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_01240087bd.png) ![Eriette's Whisper](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_884f30ad7e.png) ![Conceited Witch](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_7dbf62f8c9.png)

![Slumbering Keepguard](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_ef4be214f7.png) ![Dutiful Griffin](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_aed0a6ac12.png) ![Hopeless Nightmare](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_6095233820.png)

Meanwhile, the witch sister Eriette uses the charmed apple that Talion gifted her to maintain the curse of the Wicked Slumber long after the invasion was repelled.

![Ashiok's Reaper](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_1b3536d137.png) ![Taken by Nightmares](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_3e393666b3.png) ![Wicked Slumber](https://media.wizards.com/2023/mom/en_953eac9fcb.png)

Neva is stalked in the dream realm by nightmares, some resembling Phyrexians that hunted her in the real world.

![Eerie Interference](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_4aea23e0a4.png)

Eriette meanwhile takes over the ruins of Castle Ardenvale and ensorcells any knights that come to defeat her, using her magic to manipulate those under the Wicked Slumber's curse like puppets.

![Shrouded Shepherd](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_4c83a39f49.png)

Neva is only able to fend off the nightmares due to the influence of a mysterious spectral visitor who saves her life.

![Twisted Fealty](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_93222f2084.png)

Eriette convinces Rowan to join her as an apprentice. When Kellan and Ruby show up at Castle Ardenvale to get the tempting apple, it's Rowan they face.

![Ashiok, Wicked Manipulator](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_e06c2675fe.png)

One witch would be a tough enough opponent—but the pair must also face down the Planeswalker Ashiok. With some help from Will Kenrith, the unlikely heroes manage to save the day.

![Extraordinary Journey](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_9b2988997a.png)

Kellan learns that his real father may be hiding not just somewhere in the far reaches of Eldraine, but on another plane entirely—and sets off to find him.

Now we get to the five stories that are independent of the larger story.

## The Pied Piper of Hamelin (Black-Red)

### Characters

![Totentanz, Swarm Piper](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_95954f48b0.png) ![Lord Skitter, Sewer King](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_9298cc01cd.png)

*   **Totentanz** – A struggling artist trying to make ends meet. His desperation leads him to evil. He's creepy and vindictive.
*   **Lord Skitter** – A demonic rat who lives beneath Edgewall. He is cruel and cunning.

### Story

![Gnawing Crescendo](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_132683e3ef.png)

Totentanz was a struggling musician who could barely make ends meet.

![Twisted Sewer-Witch](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_b9b61eef08.png) ![Redcap Gutter-Dweller](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_6da6cea2da.png) ![Lord Skitter's Butcher](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_e4b4231b2f.png)

While staying out on the streets of Edgewall, he fell into the sewers beneath and was led by the rats to Lord Skitter.

![Lord Skitter's Blessing](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_772dc2aae2.png)

Lord Skitter granted Totentanz powers, specifically the ability to enthrall listeners in a way that his music never could.

![Rat Out](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_daecdf4560.png)

In exchange, Totentanz must lure victims into the sewer so that Skitter and his brood can feast on them.

![Song of Totentanz](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_6dc13fe37e.png)

Totentanz leads droves of hapless Edgewall citizens into the undercity with his music.

![Tangled Colony](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_e3820e9fce.png) ![Voracious Vermin](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_3cc2bbe171.png) ![Warehouse Tabby](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_eb9bd06ecb.png)

Lord Skitter and his brood grow in power, and eventually rats swarm the city.

![Edgewall Pack](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_d21155e231.png)

At first, the people of Edgewall are outwitted by Lord Skitter.

![Ratcatcher Trainee](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_fb41e875f9.png) ![Tattered Ratter](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_3e25b14279.png) ![Harried Spearguard](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_4dafa500de.png)

The townsfolk do the best they can to deal with the rats, but eventually, they band together, steal Totentanz's pipe, and storm the sewers, twisting its wicked magic back onto Lord Skitter and Totentanz, causing them to be devoured by their own rat horde. Without a leader, the rats of Edgewall return to their ordinary ways, though no one knows if Lord Skitter is truly gone for good.

## Beauty and the Beast (Green-White)

### Characters

![Syr Armont, the Redeemer](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_b220d2a820.png) ![Yenna, Redtooth Regent](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_56f4259ac1.png)

*   **Lady Armont** – An Ardenvale knight captain renown for her kindness and beauty. She cannot ignore injustice.
*   **Yenna** – The leader of the werefox elves. She is distrustful and proud.

### Story

Lady Armont, looking for a way to heal the realm after the Phyrexian invasion, journeys deep into the wilds with her band of loyal knights.

![Redtooth Genealogist](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_b8f93b2a5f.png) ![Sentinel of Lost Lore](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_1318f87787.png)

In the forest, by sheer coincidence, she stumbles upon Redtooth Keep where the ancient bloodline of elves live.

![Bestial Bloodline](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_24d9337ad9.png) ![Besotted Knight](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_e9d3ca75aa.png) ![Curse of the Werefox](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_a8828ea488.png)

![Ferocious Werefox](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_ba4ef4ae7d.png) ![Redtooth Vanguard](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_4491c443a9.png) ![Werefox Bodyguard](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_85be277cf4.png)

The elves are cursed to transform into werefoxes at night.

![Hopeful Vigil](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_4b5d9162bd.png)

Lady Armont commits herself to solving this mystery and breaking the curse. Some of her knights, concerned with their own survival, battle against the werefoxes despite Armont's wishes.

![Elvish Archivist](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_f1f1566911.png)

Yanna, leader of the werefoxes, warms up to Armont's kindness and tells her of the enchanted rose that will break the spell.

![Archon of the Wild Rose](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_449f25be9b.png)

It is guarded by an archon in a glade nearby. Armont and Yanna venture to the glade in search of the rose.

## Sorcerer's Apprentice (Blue-Red)

### Characters

![Johann, Apprentice Sorcerer](https://media.wizards.com/2023/woe/en_ec5c90c032.png) ![Gadwick, the Wizened](https://media.wizards.com/2019/eld/en_nlJ8s68cUc.png)

*   **Johann** – A foolish apprentice who unleashes havoc after his master leaves the tower.
*   **Gadwick** – Johann's master, unseen in the story, but a character with a card in *Throne of Eldraine*. He is powerful and mysterious.

### Story
