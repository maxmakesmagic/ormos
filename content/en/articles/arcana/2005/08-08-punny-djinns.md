The [color-counting djinns](https://web.archive.org/web/20050808000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/default.asp?first=1&last=100&term=djinn&Field_Type=on&setfilter=InvasionBlock&colorfilter=All&typefilter=All&output=summary&sort=color&x=24&y=29) from *Invasion* were based on creatures designed for the never-printed *Unglued 2*. This cycle of *Unglued 2* djinns were a bit of templating away from being ready for print -- they already had punny djinn names, art by Edward P. Beard, Jr., and in some cases flavor text! In honor of *Invasion* Week, today we present the full text and art of these proto-*Invasion* cards. (Click each djinn's card name to see the *Invasion* card it inspired.)

![Art by Edward P. Beard, Jr.](https://i.ormos.uk/c9/ed/c9ed538533f010e3888d19efd5fc0546ea7a9b22fe386b75715e931dbf25f2e6.jpg)

**[Ruham Djinn](https://web.archive.org/web/20050808000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Ruham%2BDjinn)**  
 4W  
 Creature — Djinn  
 W: Cotton Djinn gains first strike until end of turn.  
 Cotton Djinn gets +2/+2 as long as white is the most common color among the permanents in play. If white is tied with another color for most common color, Cotton Djinn still gets +2/+2.  
*“Now hold on just a cotton-pickin’ minute...”  
 —Eli Whitney*  
 3/3

![Art by Edward P. Beard, Jr.](https://i.ormos.uk/98/f8/98f83e8e7234e47162e26e087e4e323af0590d40acf77a20e27292ac2b2b4140.jpg)

**[Zanam Djinn](https://web.archive.org/web/20050808000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Zanam%2BDjinn)**  
 4U  
 Creature — Djinn  
 U: Hydro Djinn gains flying until end of turn.  
 Hydro Djinn gets +2/+2 as long as blue is the most common color among the permanents in play. If blue is tied with another color for most common color, Hydro Djinn still gets +2/+2.  
*For every two of these in the water, there must be one Oxy Djinn.*  
 3/3
