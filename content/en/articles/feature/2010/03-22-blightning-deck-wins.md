It was the Jund boogeyman that took six of the Top 8 slots a the recent Grand Prix–Kuala Lumpur featuring the Standard format. But the powerful deck couldn't stand up to Ding Yuan Leong's aggressive red deck. Leong touched just enough black for [Blightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20100322000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blightning) and [Lavaclaw Reaches](https://web.archive.org/web/20100322000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lavaclaw+Reaches), as well as some creature removal in the sideboard, and powered through the Jund menace with ease. Read more about the event in the [official coverage](https://web.archive.org/web/20100322000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/ding-dong-jund-dead-kuala-lumpur).
