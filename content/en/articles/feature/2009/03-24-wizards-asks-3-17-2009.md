*What's your favorite Limited combo of all time?*

![Mike Turian](https://i.ormos.uk/4d/df/4ddf17870927d1fa29f7c99e3b74f59ad7b39456776c837fd321939967d75c7b.jpg)

**Mike Turian:** My favorite Limited combo was during *Odyssey* / *Torment* Draft. I had always loved opening up a pack to see [Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20090324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel). It was such a powerful two-drop (my favorite!) in Limited.

![Wild Mongrel](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Wild+Mongrel)

  

When *Torment* was released, [Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20090324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel) became even stronger with the madness mechanic. After casting a turn-two Mongrel, I loved madnessing out an [Arrogant Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20090324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrogant+Wurm) for only ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/colorless2.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/green.gif). One time I followed up a turn-two [Wild Mongrel](https://web.archive.org/web/20090324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Mongrel) with a turn-three [Arrogant Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20090324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrogant+Wurm), followed by a turn-four [Violent Eruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20090324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Violent+Eruption). That is the type of combo I love!
