Boggarts are naturally drawn to new sensations, which causes them to be sort of accidental kleptomaniacs. They don't *mean* to steal the delicious pie cooling on the kithkin windowsill—they just couldn't help themselves.

But there's one important rule among the boggarts: you have to share any new sensation you find with the rest of your warren.

The art of the card [Hoarder's Greed](https://web.archive.org/web/20071029000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hoarder%27s+Greed) shows a boggart finding a coveted trinket in the woods of Lorwyn. He's definitely not sharing—he's letting his greed take precedence over the rules of his warren's Auntie.

[![Hoarder's Greed](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/cardart/LRW/Hoarders_Greed_640.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071029000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Hoarder%2527s%2BGreed) *[Hoarder's Greed](https://web.archive.org/web/20071029000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hoarder%27s+Greed) art by Pete Venters*

But that's not the end of the story for our little friend. If we look on another card in *Lorwyn*, we can see the same greedy boggart suffering the punishment for not sharing the trinket—a sentence of exile.

[![Exiled Boggart](http://www.wizards.com/magic/images/cardart/LRW/Exiled_Boggart_640.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20071029000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Exiled%2BBoggart) *[Exiled Boggart](https://web.archive.org/web/20071029000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exiled+Boggart) art by Pete Venters*
