## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/06/23/0623f80baf460b0b4826289f7ac47b56cb7864d35f63d38fe02230a00c716d88.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, one 75-card tournament pack, three 15-card booster packs, one foil basic land, one foil common card, special-edition life counter, and the *Odyssey* novel.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/06/23/0623f80baf460b0b4826289f7ac47b56cb7864d35f63d38fe02230a00c716d88.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/06/23/0623f80baf460b0b4826289f7ac47b56cb7864d35f63d38fe02230a00c716d88.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719043948/http://magic.wizards.com/node/976826)  
 (\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/06/23/0623f80baf460b0b4826289f7ac47b56cb7864d35f63d38fe02230a00c716d88.png)

### TOURNAMENT PACKS

The foundation for exciting Sealed Deck play. Includes 45 randomly assorted cards and 30 basic land cards.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/46/81/46819d3472bcc968ff8bf7b64b29a7d7f1f5a7bee7f6235d108a3dea250626f4.png)

This 350-card, black-bordered set includes randomly inserted foil premium cards and two new graveyard-based mechanics: Flashback and Threshold.

**Set Name:** *Odyssey*  
**Block:** Set 1 of 3 of the *Odyssey* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 350

**Languages:** English, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719043948/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/faq/odyssey)

**Game Design:** Mark Rosewater (lead), Michael Donais, Richard Garfield, William Jockusch, and Henry Stern

**Game Development:** Randy Buehler (lead), Michael Donais, Mike Elliott, William Jockusch, and Henry Stern
