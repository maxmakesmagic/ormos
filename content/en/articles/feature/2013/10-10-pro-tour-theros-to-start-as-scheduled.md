Pro Tour *Theros* will begin Friday at 9 a.m. Dublin time as scheduled. The anticipated delays connected to a partial air traffic controllers strike did not significantly affect players' ability to arrive in Dublin on time to register on Thursday.

Tune in for the [Pro Tour *Theros*](/en/events/2013/theros13/pro-tour-theros-event-information) live video stream at [wizards.com/webcast](/en/events/2004/webcast/magic-the-gathering-webcast-schedule) and [twitch.tv/magic](http://www.twitch.tv/magic). Friday begins with the induction ceremony of the 2013 Pro Tour Hall of Fame class, followed by three rounds of *Theros* Booster Draft and five rounds of Standard.
