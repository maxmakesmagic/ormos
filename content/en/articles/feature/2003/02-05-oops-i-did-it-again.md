### Site Editor Soundly Thrashed By R&D

* * *

So, can you tell I was unfamiliar with the tool used to make surveys here at Wizards? First, a missing tag made it impossible to vote. Now, I find out that it doesn't matter, anyway, as I can't put code in the survey answers (i.e., I can't link to the bio page) since it trashes all the data.

Welcome to my Wednesday.

If I simply repair the survey, everyone who's already voted loses out. Bad answer as far as I'm concerned. So, I'm taking down the current one and putting up a fixed version. You'll have to look at the bios by hand, but at least you can tell us who you want in the Invitational.

Sorry again, folks. I'll post everything back up once the survey is ready...

...AND WORKING!
