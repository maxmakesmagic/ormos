### 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/63/9d/639d641230b9245f414e0724dc0c4b1bdff5ed0323a0d6f0b187dfdb329f929c.jpg)Due to school conflicts, Kai Budde (as a student) and Zvi Mowshowitz (as a teacher) have both chosen to decline an invitation to this year's **Magic** Invitational. Kai's slot (for winning last year's Invitational) does not drop down, so the invite goes to the fifth person on the fan ballot, Chris Pikula. Pikula has been contacted and will be attending the event. Pikula now single-handedly holds the record for the most Invitational attendances (six in all; he has attended every Invitational except the first one). Previously he had been tied with Mike Long at five. Zvi's slot drops down to the sixth person on the fan ballot, Tomi Walamies. Walamies has also accepted the invitation. This will be Walamies' first Invitational.
