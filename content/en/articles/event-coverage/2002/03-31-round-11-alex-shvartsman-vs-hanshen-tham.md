![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpkl02/a953.jpg)

Twelve-year-old Hanshen Tham has been taking names this weekend. After swashbuckling the Sealed Deck portion of the event, he's managed to hold his own at the draft tables. Unfortunately, he needs to run his table in order to reach the lofty goal of Top 8. In his way, and playing the role of dreamcrusher, is pro regular Alex Shvartsman. Experience is sure to be a factor.

The only factor in the first game was Shvartsman's ability to spill guys onto the field, and Tham's relative inability. His [Gloomdrifter](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gloomdrifter) got ousted by [Shelter](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shelter), and [Zombie Assassin](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zombie+Assassin) ate [Cephalid Broker](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cephalid+Broker), but the damage had been done. He had nothing on the table, against Shvartsman's [Militant Monk](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Militant+Monk), [Hallowed Healer](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallowed+Healer), [Pilgrim of Virtue](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pilgrim+of+Virtue) and [Millikin](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Millikin).

Tham cast [Breakthrough](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Breakthrough) for three, discarding three land and an [Escape Artist](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Escape+Artist). His newly-found [Mesmeric Fiend](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mesmeric+Fiend) snagged [Second Thoughts](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Second+Thoughts), and [Crippling Fatigue](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crippling+Fatigue) took down the Monk and Healer. Shvartsman was immobile, so things were looking up.

Not for long. Shvartsman hit [Aven Windreader](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Windreader) and [Deep Analysis](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deep+Analysis). Tham had only [Think Tank](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Think+Tank) and [Shadowmage Infiltrator](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shadowmage+Infiltrator). Shvartsman chumped with the [Millikin](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Millikin) to keep Tham off options long enough to kill him.

Shvartsman 1 - Tham 0

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpkl02/a951.jpg)

Tham kept a questionable hand of three islands and black spells for the second game, but drew out of it expertly. He still had just [Treetop Sentinel](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treetop+Sentinel) against [Cephalid Sage](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cephalid+Sage), [Pilgrim of Virtue](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pilgrim+of+Virtue) and [Hallowed Healer](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallowed+Healer). He tried [Crippling Fatigue](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crippling+Fatigue) on the Healer, only to meet [Shelter](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shelter).

Shvartsman's Sage continued to send. Tham blocked with his Sentinel. A [Psionic Gift](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psionic+Gift) on the Pilgrim finished it off. [Churning Eddy](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Churning+Eddy) ran damage control, but [Deep Analysis](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deep+Analysis) kept Shvartsman fuelled up. Tham tried a Finkel. Again the Sage hit the Red Zone, and again Tham bit. This time [Patriarch's Desire](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Patriarch%27s+Desire) finished his monster.

Tham's [Whispering Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whispering+Shade) would have looked more dangerous, considering Shvartsman's swamps, if he weren't already at ten life. [Aven Windreader](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Windreader) sounded the death knell. Tham's only rejoinder was a [Dusk Imp](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dusk+Imp). He had a pair of [Filthy Cur](https://web.archive.org/web/20020331000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Filthy+Cur)s, but they made poor defencemen. It was mercifully short.

Final Result: Alex Shvartsman defeats Hanshen Tham 2-0
