Jeff Hoogland is a player who has had quite a bit of success with under-the-radar decks. If you played against Black-Green Midrange last year or Blue-Red Faeries in Modern this year, you likely played against a Jeff Hoogland creation. Jeff is at it again, building a new deck with *Journey into Nyx* to help him reach the semifinals of a SCG Standard Open. 

![Ajani, Mentor of Heroes](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ajani%2C+Mentor+of+Heroes)

Jeff's deck is a black-green-white deck that takes advantage of a variety of card interactions and borrows strategies from other decks. In some games, you can use [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140520000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) to draw a steady stream of removal spells. In other games, [Archangel of Thune](https://web.archive.org/web/20140520000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archangel+of+Thune) is very easy to trigger multiple times with [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140520000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix) or [Scavenging Ooze](https://web.archive.org/web/20140520000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scavenging+Ooze). I'm sure Jeff caught more than one opponent by surprise with [Advent of the Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140520000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Advent+of+the+Wurm), especially as opponents did not know what to expect. Building new decks can unleash the power of uncertainty on your opponents!
