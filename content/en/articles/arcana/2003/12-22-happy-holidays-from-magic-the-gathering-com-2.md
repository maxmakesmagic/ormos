We here at **MagicTheGathering.com**, and at Wizards of the Coast in general, would like to extend to you our season's greetings (in the form of a 7/7 flying trample – yet oh-so-jolly – demon-mouse from *Unglued*). Have a happy and safe holiday season, and we'll see you again on Monday, January 5th for *Darksteel* previews!

– MagicTheGathering.com staff
