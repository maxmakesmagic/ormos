Enchantress is an archetype that has been around for a long time. Using "[Enchantresses](https://web.archive.org/web/20101202000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&text=+[enchantment]+[draw]+[a]+[card]+[cast]&name=+[Enchantress]&type=+[%22Creature%22])," cards like Verduran Enchantress and Argothian Enchantress, the deck seeks to draw extra cards by casting as many enchantments as possible. For pryite199, the deck was good enough to earn a 3-1 performance in a recent Legacy event on **Magic Online**. Enchantress's Presence and Argothian Enchantress provide the fuel for Solitary Confinement, while cards like Moat and Aura of Silence keep the opponent all tied up.
