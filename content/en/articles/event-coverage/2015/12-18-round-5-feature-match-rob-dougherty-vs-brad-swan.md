![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpatl01/960.jpg)*Rob Dougherty vs. Brad Swan*Rob Dougherty of YMG fame faced off against PT-Barcelona Top 8er Brad Swan in a round 5 feature match. Dougherty, fresh off his Top 16 finish in New Orleans looked to continue his hot streak.

Brad Swan dominated the die-roll metagame, rolling a 12-sided die to a wildly successful "12," while the die's owner, Rob Dougherty managed a meager "1." "Make sure you mention the die roll," joked Swan. Okay Brad, I got it.

On his first turn, Swan got his [Peek](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peek) on, and Rob showed mono-red with 4 Mountains, [Magma Vein](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Vein), [Ember Beast](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ember+Beast), and [Firebolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firebolt). While Rob was casting an Ember Beast, Swan showed that he also is very good at another limited skill; drawing all three colors of mana on the first three turns. Dougherty finally got his second color of mana, black, and began to build some card advantage by [Gravedigger](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravedigger)ing his fallen Ember Beast. Swan missed his fifth-turn land drop but showed excellent defense in the form of [Puppeteer](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Puppeteer) and [Blessed Orator](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blessed+Orator), while [Repel](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Repel)ling, and then Syncopating Rob's third Ember Beast.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpatl01/962.jpg)*Rob Dougherty*The first game dragged as the board was nearly stalled, with Swan keeping control as Dougherty tried to build up some creatures. After several uses of [Phanatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phanatog), Swan got to threshold; killed an Ember Beast with a [Patriarch's Desire](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Patriarch%27s+Desire) and cast a big [Dirty Wererat](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dirty+Wererat). Swan finally drew a board-buster, [Cephalid Broker](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cephalid+Broker). Over the next few turns he rooted through his library and turned up [Morbid Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Morbid+Hunger), and plenty of control to win with his Wererat and a [Caustic Tar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Caustic+Tar).

Swan 1 Dougherty 0

Swan once again dominated the mana draw with blue, white, and black mana in the first three turns, used [Innocent Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Innocent+Blood) to kill Dougherty's Ember Beast and cast a [Standstill](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Standstill). Rob didn't fear Swan's Standstill, and cast a [Famished Ghoul](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Famished+Ghoul) and gave Swan the three cards. With cards filling his hand, and what seemed like plenty of time, Swan started out with defense again with Blessed Orator, Puppeteer, and then a second Puppeteer on successive turns.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c6/5e/c65e7a7fb5e49795ce52d8aa687ccac2589a0230432235943287d231be2f83f1.jpg)*Brad Swan*Dougherty, down on cards, and threats, managed to deal with the Orator with a Firebolt and Patriarch's Desire, and Firebolt out of his graveyard made short work of a Puppeteer. After several land draws, the Bostonian Dougherty appeared to have some offense rolling with a Dirty Wererat on threshold. Dougherty, now flanked with teammates David Humphreys and Chad Ellis, finally got to 9 mana to cast [Valley of Boulders](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valley+of+Boulders), attempting to take out two of Swan's three creatures. Swan, being the tight player that he is, remembered the [Embolden](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Embolden) in his graveyard cast nearly 20 minutes ago, saved his creatures, and swung back to win on the following turn in extra time.

Final result; Brad Swan beat Rob Dougherty 2-0 to remain undefeated.
