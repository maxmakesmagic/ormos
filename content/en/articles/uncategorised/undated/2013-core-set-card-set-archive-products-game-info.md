## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/32/75/3275dd4698949cc721d4da9e995bfdd4a60176a8e4d15add4293a9b440180ddf.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set as well as two deck boxes, nine 15-card booster packs, 80 basic land cards, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2f/d5/2fd574effc51cfb9c8a29ea1e5e753b117f2e8c15132c275079aadfe3bbd9faf.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/33/d3/33d3d3226b3c44cd48393975570b0a869a4a4c8f85061426e6280f6da3cdbf65.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 cards, a foil premium rare card, two sealed 15-card booster packs, a strategy insert, and a **Magic** learn-to-play guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160707021155/http://magic.wizards.com/node/282861)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2e/b0/2eb0a04c1d319f2dbab318cfc7228437eba3d18fe6c9a00370607b77d63999f0.png)

### DECK BUILDER'S TOOLKIT

The support you need to build your own **Magic: The Gathering** decks. Includes 125 semi-randomized cards suitable for building several different decks, four 15-card booster packs from a variety of recent **Magic** sets, 100 basic land cards, a deck builder’s guide, a **Magic** learn-to-play guide, and a card box.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f6/cc/f6cc7f2921476015b19817a305799f34410c2e20e7669651aa1380dd51f7dd0b.png)

### BOOSTER BATTLE PACKS

Want to play with a friend right now? Just choose which of the two 22-card decks you’ll each play, insert up to five cards from the two included booster packs—and begin! It’s that easy.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a5/f1/a5f144a8180e84884bd1295f43575c529c05370e2126490fc8672673c72ef5f4.png)

### EVENT DECKS

Battle like the best, right out of the box. Includes a tournament-ready 60-card deck plus a 15-card “sideboard” to swap in as needed, depending on what you expect your competitor will play.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160707021155/http://magic.wizards.com/node/288481)

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e8/85/e8853a91c9907829da8f9512b8d49f546db0834c38dae29063e454707a6a8089.png)The **Magic** *2013 Core Set* features 249 black-bordered cards, including randomly inserted premium versions of all cards in the set.

**Set Name:** *2013 Core Set*  
**Number of Cards:** 249

**Languages:** English, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish

**Official Three-Letter Code:** M13  
**Twitter Hashtag:** #MTGM13

[2013 Core Set FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160707021155/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/faq/m13) (or download as [DOC](https://web.archive.org/web/20160707021155/http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/tcg/products/m13/EN_MTGM13_FAQ_20120615.doc))

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Doug Beyer (lead), Aaron Forsythe, Graeme Hopkins, Ryan Miller, and Mark Purvis

**Final Game Design and Development:** Zac Hill (lead), Ethan Fleischer, Mark L. Gottlieb, Tom LaPille, and Max McCall, with contributions from Matt Tabak
