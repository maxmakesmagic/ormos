In honor of the start of the Pro Tour Qualifier-Honolulu season, Daily Decks is bringing you some of the hottest Extended decks all week long! While former World Champion and 2008 Pro Tour-Kuala Lumpur victor Jon Finkel is famous for his prowess with blue cards, he took a different path at the 2008 Extended portion of the World Championships. Finkel served with none other than Spark Elemental to get the job done against his opponents, burning to a crisp all who stood in his way. To find an Extended PTQ for Honolulu near you, visit [this link](/en/events/2009/honolulu09-qualifiers/pro-tourhonolulu-2009-qualifiers).
