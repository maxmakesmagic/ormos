Only a few months after tearing up the world of Standard, the Bant Eldrazi Conscription decks are back! This time they're rearing their heads in the world of Extended, the latest Pro Tour Qualifier format. Celldweller took the above build to a 2nd-place finish at a recent PTQ. Where? Why, on [**Magic Online**](https://web.archive.org/web/20110414065035/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Digital/MagicOnline.aspx) of course! That's right, you can qualify to play on the Pro Tour from the comfort of your own living room!
