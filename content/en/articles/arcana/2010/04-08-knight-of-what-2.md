Let's talk for a moment about the *Rise of the Eldrazi* card Knight of Cliffhaven. For those of you who haven't committed it to memory, here it is again.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fd/93/fd93e164bfa8b92a4f1cf040c441ea670f4b19384651062ab42b4c3239350595.jpg) 

So what you've got is a 2/2 knight that can gain flying, vigilance, and a total of +2/+2. Does anything in the art look a little familiar?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b6/b6/b6b6a3d3859ea1bc34605d3cec13f7436a341671fd45039c395b79aafb0dafe3.jpg) 

Knight of Cliffhaven | Art by [Matt Cavotta](https://web.archive.org/web/20100408000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22matt+cavotta%22])

Well, let's consider this logically. If you were a kor knight in Zendikar and you wanted to find something that could fly you around, maybe something that knew it's way around a +2/+2 boost, what would you look for?

See the answer!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/de/40/de40d699b08dcb821765eb5b4a34aa9c2029b28a8c454cf465f2e58af3c6c44f.jpg) 

Windrider Eel | Art by [Austin Hsu](https://web.archive.org/web/20100408000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22austin%20hsu%22])

It's a Windrider Eel!
