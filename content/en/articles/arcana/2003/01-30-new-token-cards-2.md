Two new token cards are already in the mail to Player Reward members.

The first is a 1/1 green Insect token that can be used in conjunction with lots of cards, from [Saber Ants](https://web.archive.org/web/20030130000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Saber+Ants) to the *Onslaught* [Symbiotic Beast](https://web.archive.org/web/20030130000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Symbiotic%2BBeast) to [Wirewood Hivemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20030130000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wirewood+Hivemaster).

The second is a 1/1 colorless Sliver token, which works perfectly with not only [Brood Sliver](https://web.archive.org/web/20030130000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brood+Sliver), but the [Sliver Queen](https://web.archive.org/web/20030130000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sliver+Queen) as well!

Interested in joining Player Reward and getting your own tokens in the mail? Click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20030130000000/http://archive.wizards.com/dci/rewards.asp?x=rewards).
