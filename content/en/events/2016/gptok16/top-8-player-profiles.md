After many rounds of Swiss and some great games of *Magic*, the Top 8 for Grand Prix Tokyo is set! Learn more about this event’s Top 8 competitors, their history at the top tables, and more by taking a look through the Top 8 profiles.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gptok16/gpNagoya16_TOP8_All8Players.jpg)

* * *

### Riku Kumagai

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gptok16/gpNagoya16_TOP8_3Kumagai.jpg)

**Age:** Secret  
**Occupation:** Secret  
**Hometown:** Sendai, Japan  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Two *Magic Online* PTQ Wins

**Number of Byes:** 2 **Score in Day 1:** 8-1 **Score in Day 2:** 5-0-1

**Where do you usually play *Magic*?**  
*Magic Online*!

**What deck did you play and why?**  
 Naya Midrange, I built and tweaked the deck and had great results with it.

**Would you change anything if you were to play this deck again?**  
 If I am not playing Naya Midrange, I would probably end up playing Bant Humans or a Ramp deck.

**What is your favorite card from the plane of Innistrad?**  
[Restoration Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20160508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Restoration+Angel). I like cards with a lot of synergy with other and are solid themselves.

* * *

### Kazushige Suzuki

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gptok16/gpNagoya16_TOP8_8Suzuki.jpg)

**Age:** 24  
**Occupation:** Student  
**Hometown:** Nagoya, Japan  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Finalist at WMCQ 2015 in Nagoya

**Number of Byes:** 1 **Score in Day 1:** 8-1 **Score in Day 2:** 5-1

**Where do you usually play *Magic*?**  
 I grind a lot of PPTQs all over Nagoya.

**What deck did you play and why?**  
 Grixis Control, because it was the only deck which I could build with my own cards.

**Would you change anything if you were to play this deck again?**  
 If I went back to two days ago, I would change a few cards from my sideboard. I would also consider playing Human decks, I just don't know which build.

**What is your favorite card from the plane of Innistrad?**  
[Thalia, Guardian of Thraben](https://web.archive.org/web/20160508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thalia%2C+Guardian+of+Thraben). I've used it in both Standard and Modern and I really like the artwork!

* * *

### Takuma Morofuji

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gptok16/gpNagoya16_TOP8_4Morofuji.jpg)

**Age:** 35  
**Occupation:** Office worker  
**Hometown:** Fukuoka, Japan  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** World Championship 2005 Team Champion, Japanese National Champion 2005, GP Kobe 2015 Winner

**Number of Byes:** 2 **Score in Day 1:** 8-1 **Score in Day 2:** 5-0-1

**Where do you usually play *Magic*?**  
 Baburusu, Yellow Submarine Fukuoka

**What deck did you play and why?**  
 White-Green Tokens, because I'm not good at flipping 6 cards with [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20160508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company).

**Would you change anything if you were to play this deck again?**  
 I would remove [Evolutionary Leap](https://web.archive.org/web/20160508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Evolutionary+Leap) from the maindeck, it was out of line.

**What is your favorite card from the plane of Innistrad?**  
[Declaration in Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20160508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Declaration+in+Stone), because it is almost as strong as [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20160508000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile) is.
