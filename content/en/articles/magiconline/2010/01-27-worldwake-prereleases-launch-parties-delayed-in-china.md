Due to a massive iceberg off the northeastern coast of China, the vessel carrying *Worldwake* product will not reach China in time for this week's Prerelease events and next week's Launch Parties.

The [Prereleases](https://web.archive.org/web/20100127000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prerelease-facts) in China have been rescheduled for February 6-7 and [Launch Parties](https://web.archive.org/web/20100127000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/release-facts) will be held February 16-17. 

Wizards of the Coast apologizes for the inconvenience.
