[Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20111207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets) has made a huge splash recently in Standard and Legacy, but those aren't the only formats the *Innistrad* common has been slowly taking over. It's also legal in Pauper, a format where only common cards are legal! In a recent **Magic Online** Premier Event, a deck featuring the Human Insect buzzed all the way to 2nd place. Take a look for yourself, and give this fun, unique **Magic Online** format a try!
