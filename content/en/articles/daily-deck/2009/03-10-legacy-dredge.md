Grand Prix-Chicago, featuring the Legacy format, is in the books, and here's a look at one of the whispered about decks heading into the event: Dredge. Yuu Fukudome took this build to the championship at the Legacy event held at Pro Tour-Kyoto. The deck seeks to abuse Lion's Eye Diamond to quickly fill the graveyard with dredge creatures which then allow the user to effectively draw numerous extra cards each turn. Of course, the deck struggles against opposing Extirpates, Tormod's Crypts, and other assorted graveyard hate, but that doesn't stop the brave from trying it!
