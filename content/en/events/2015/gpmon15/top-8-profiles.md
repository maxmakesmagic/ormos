### Stephen Whelan

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmon15/t8-whelan.jpg)

**Age:** 24

**Hometown:** Mount Pearl, Newfoundland, Canada

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**  
 PTQ Top 8; Top 64 Canada Nationals

**What did you play Saturday, and how did you do?**  
 Black-Red Aggro with two Dragon bombs and way too many good spells. I went 9-0 and 18-2 (games) on the day. Great pool that built itself.

**What did you draft today, and how did that go?**  
 First draft I was blue-red tempo with no bombs but a great cruve/removal. Went 2-1 losing a close match to a guy (Josh) who played great. Sencond drafy I was 1-1-1 with white-red aggro with little push through. I misread some signals at the table.

**What were your best commons or uncommons this weekend? Why?**  
[Twin Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20150705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Twin+Bolt)/[Tail Slash](https://web.archive.org/web/20150705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tail+Slash)/[Butcher's Glee](https://web.archive.org/web/20150705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Butcher%27s+Glee). First two are obvious the [Butcher's Glee](https://web.archive.org/web/20150705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Butcher%27s+Glee) really wins the grindy games.

**What do you want open for pack one, pick one? Why?**  
 I would like to open a really aggressive bomb. Nothing in particular (I’m not that greedy) just something that puts me in a color and wins me some games.

**What has you most excited about *Magic Origins*?**  
 I have been off and on *Magic* for 14 years. This set looks great and after my finish today I’m jumping back in full force.

### Gautier Cousin

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmon15/t8-cousin.jpg)

**Age:** 26

**Hometown:** Bourg-La-Reine

**Previous *Magic* accomplishments:**  
 PTQ Top 8

**What did you play Saturday, and how did you do?**  
 Played blue-black-white control with [Silumgar, the Drifting Death](https://web.archive.org/web/20150705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silumgar%2C+the+Drifting+Death), his Command, Daghatar and the all-star [Stratus Dancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stratus+Dancer).

**What did you draft today, and how did that go?**  
 First draft went blue-black tempo. Won all my mulligans ot five cards so everything went ok. Got 3-0. Second draft went green-white. Opened [Dromoka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dromoka%27s+Command) got passed a second one. Last pack a got [Temur War Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20150705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temur+War+Shaman) fifth pick and [Abzan Beastmaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20150705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Beastmaster) thirteenth pick. Happy Gautier!

**What were your best commons or uncommons this weekend? Why?**  
[Abzan Beastmaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20150705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abzan+Beastmaster) has been awesome. With some [Glade Watcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20150705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glade+Watcher) it’s insane. [Sight Beyond Sight](https://web.archive.org/web/20150705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sight+Beyond+Sight) has performed well as well.

**What do you want open for pack one, pick one? Why?**  
 I don’t want Dragons. I never open them anyway. I guess [Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20150705000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price).

**What has you most excited about *Magic Origins*?**  
 Seems to be great. Can’t wait for the Prerelease.
