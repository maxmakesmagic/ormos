![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/tcg/products/bng/EN_BNG_BuyABox_Booster_Display.jpg)

Here's an offer sure to appeal to every player: The first 20 (30 for Advanced locations) people that preorder or purchase a **Magic: The Gathering**® *Born of the Gods*™ booster box in your retail store, will get an Alternate Art Premium **FATED CONFLAGRATION** promo card! But hurry, this offer is limited to **ONLY** the first 20 (or 30) people per store who have either preordered or purchase a full booster box! 

  
![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/tcg/products/bng/EN_BNG_BuyABox_Promo_Card.jpg)

*   [List of participating stores (North amp; Latin America)](https://web.archive.org/web/20140116000000/https://magic.wizards.com/Magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=mtg/daily/events/bngbuyaboxpromolocations)

If you have questions or concerns, please contact Wizards at [Wizards.com/CustomerService](http://wizards.custhelp.com/), or (800) 324-6496. For all other countries, please find the global contact information at [Wizards.com/GlobalCS](https://web.archive.org/web/20140116000000/http://company.wizards.com/contactus)
