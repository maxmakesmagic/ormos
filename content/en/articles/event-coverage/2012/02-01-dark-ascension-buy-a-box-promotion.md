Here's an offer that is sure to appeal to every **Magic: The Gathering** player! 

The first 20 (for Core locations) or 30 (for Advanced locations) people to purchase a **Magic: The Gathering** *Dark Ascension* Booster Box at participating locations will get a free alternate-art premium Gravecrawler promo card!

But hurry, this offer is limited to the first 20 or 30 people per store!
