Pro Tour-Berlin has barely wrapped up and Luis Scott-Vargas has been crowned a champion! He played the breakout hit of the tournament in the form of Elves. The deck seeks to generate huge amounts of card and mana advantage through a combination of Glimpse of Nature, Heritage Druid, Nettle Sentinel, and cheap Elves. Six players took varying versions of the concoction to the Top 8, but it was Luis Scott-Vargas who reigned supreme. To read more about the event, check out the coverage from **Magicthegathering.com** [here](/en/events/2008/ptber08/lsv-elves-equals-champion).
