**What's the toughest first-pick decision you've had in *Born of the Gods* / *Theros* draft?** 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpnag14/yasooka.jpg)

***Shouta Yasooka**: For me, there are no difficult picks.* 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpnag14/yamamoto.jpg)

***Kentarou Yamamoto**: [Raised by Wolves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140413000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raised+by+Wolves) vs. [Bolt of Keranos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140413000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bolt+of+Keranos). I hate green, but I couldn't say no to the enchantment.* 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpnag14/kuo.jpg)

***Tzu Ching Kuo**: [Brimaz, King of Oreskos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140413000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brimaz%2C+King+of+Oreskos) vs. [Retraction Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140413000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Retraction+Helix).* 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpnag14/yukuhiro.jpg)

***Ken Yukuhiro**:[Fall of the Hammer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140413000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fall+of+the+Hammer) vs. [Asphyxiate](https://web.archive.org/web/20140413000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Asphyxiate). I used to think it was [Fall of the Hammer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140413000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fall+of+the+Hammer), but now I think [Asphyxiate](https://web.archive.org/web/20140413000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Asphyxiate) is right.* 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpnag14/lee.jpg)

***Shi-Tian Lee**: [Retraction Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140413000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Retraction+Helix) vs. [Glimpse the Sun God](https://web.archive.org/web/20140413000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glimpse+the+Sun+God).* 

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpnag14/mori.jpg)

***Katsuhiro Mori**: (6-0 in draft at the Pro Tour, 3-0 his first draft here) It's all easy!*
