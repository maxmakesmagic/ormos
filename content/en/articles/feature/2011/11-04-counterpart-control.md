Looking for something fun and unique to do with the [Mayor of Avabruck](https://web.archive.org/web/20111104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mayor+of+Avabruck) / [Howlpack Alpha](https://web.archive.org/web/20111104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Howlpack+Alpha) promos from the *Innistrad* Prerelease? Look no further! Smaland built up a cool green-blue deck using [Cackling Counterpart](https://web.archive.org/web/20111104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cackling+Counterpart), [Mayor of Avabruck](https://web.archive.org/web/20111104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mayor+of+Avabruck), and several other new *Innistrad* cards and ran it to 3-1 in a **Magic Online** Daily Event. Take a look!
