**Holiday Cube Update**

Randy Buehler is a longtime *Magic* player, a friend of the Vintage format, co-host of the [Standard Super League](http://standardsuperleague.com/), and a lover of *Magic Online* Cubes everywhere.

Randy and *Magic* R&D have set their sites on updating the *Magic Online* Holiday Cube! Read up on the redesign in the [full article](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/06-22-holiday-cube-cardlist) then check out Lee Sharpe's [latest article](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/06-08-june-2015-magic-online-event-updates#singleelemholiday) for details about the upcoming Holiday Cube Events from July 8 through July 22.

Read about what went into the redesign in the full [article](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/06-22-holiday-cube-updates).

Check out the full, newly-updated Holiday Cube list [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/06-22-holiday-cube-cardlist).

**Modern Festival Starting Starting Soon**

Now's your chance to win one copy of every Modern-legal set in the *Magic Online* [Modern Festival Finals](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/05-11-magic-online-modern-festival#ModernFinals)!

Since the start of [*Modern Masters 2015 Edition* Release Events](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/05-04-modern-masters-2015-edition-event-schedule), the Modern format has taken center stage on *Magic Online*—and the celebration doesn't stop there. Whether you're looking to get into Modern, already love the format, or are just looking for some sweet promos, the upcoming [Modern Festival](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/05-11-magic-online-modern-festival) has something for you. Events kick off after the Wednesday, June 24, downtime!

Get the full [event details](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/05-11-magic-online-modern-festival).

**Extending Some *Modern Masters 2015 Edition* Events**

*Modern Masters 2015 Edition* is available on *Magic Online* now!

In the spirit of the [Modern Festival](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/05-11-magic-online-modern-festival)l, we're extending the duration of *Modern Masters 2015 Edition* events! All events listed below will remain active through Wednesday, July 8, instead of their previous end date of the June 17 downtime. Not only will events keep running, but *Modern Masters 2015* will also remain on sale in the *Magic Online* Store until Wednesday, July 8!

*Modern Masters 2015 Edition* Events that are being extended through Wednesday, July 8:

*   [Modern 3-2-1-1 Single Elimination Queue](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/05-04-modern-masters-2015-edition-event-schedule#prizes)
*   [Modern, Legacy, and Vintage Constructed 2-Player Queue Prize Changes](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/05-04-modern-masters-2015-edition-event-schedule#queueprizes)
*   [Modern, Legacy, and Vintage Daily Event Prize Changes](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/05-04-modern-masters-2015-edition-event-schedule#dailyprizes)
*   [*Modern Masters 2015 Edition* Phantom Draft](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/05-04-modern-masters-2015-edition-event-schedule#MM2draft)
*   [*Modern Masters 2015 Edition* Phantom Sealed](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/05-04-modern-masters-2015-edition-event-schedule#MM2sealed)
*   [*Modern Masters 2015 Edition* 6-2-2-2 Draft](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/05-04-modern-masters-2015-edition-event-schedule#MM26222)
*   [*Modern Masters 2015 Edition* 8-4 Draft](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/05-04-modern-masters-2015-edition-event-schedule#MM284)

Check out the updated event schedule [article](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/05-04-modern-masters-2015-edition-event-schedule) for the full details on Release Events.

**Standard Super League Semifinals Tonight**

Stop by tonight for the Standard Super League semifinals! Over the course of the past season, a commentary team of two *Magic* Hall of Fame members, Randy Buehler and Luis Scott-Vargas, has hosted eight-player single-elimination tournaments every Tuesday night to see which competitor emerges as the first Standard Super League Champion. Don't miss the finals, next Tuesday night at 6:00 p.m. Pacific (UTC-7) on [twitch.tv/magic](http://www.twitch.tv/magic)!

For a recap of what happened in last week's episode of the Standard Super League, head over to Randy Buehler's latest [article](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/06-23-standard-super-league-episode-8-recap).

Remember, we'll be posting each player's deck lists for the upcoming episode of the Standard Super League to [MTGO.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20150623000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/game-info/products/magic-online) at 6:00 p.m. Pacific (UTC-7) so that viewers can follow along at home! Stop by tonight to get the semifinals deck lists.

**Double Pro Tour Qualifier Weekend**

Attention all Pro Tour hopefuls, there's a double Pro Tour Qualifier (PTQ) weekend coming up! Standard Pro Tour Qualifier Preliminaries and the *Dragons of Tarkir-Fate Reforged* Sealed Pro Tour Qualifier Preliminaries begin tomorrow, June 24, and run through the day preceding each Finals Event.

Learn more about PTQs on *Magic Online* and get the full schedule [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers-for-pro-tour-battle-for-zendikar).

**Upcoming Wizards Account System Maintenance for Monday, June 22**

The Wizards Account System will be undergoing maintenance on Monday, June 22, from 7:00 p.m. Pacific (UTC-7) through Tuesday, June 23 7:00 a.m. Pacific. Customers will be able to file for reimbursement and submit bug reports during this time, but responses will be delayed.

*   All tournaments will begin to close Tuesday, June 23, at 11:00 p.m. Pacific (6:00 UTC)
