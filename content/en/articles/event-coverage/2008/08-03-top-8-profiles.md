![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpkob08/T8Tanahashi.jpg)*Masayasu Tanahashi* 

**Masayasu Tanahashi** 

**Hometown:** Niigata  
**Age:** 24  
**Profession:** Company employee

**What deck did you play, who designed it, and what was the best card in it?**  
 I played a Kithkin deck designed by Aikora. My best card was [Windbrisk Heights](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Windbrisk+Heights).

**Which card do you think is currently the most undervalued in *Eventide*?**  
[Drain the Well](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drain+the+Well).

**In your opinion, which *Eventide* card has had the most influence on Constructed?**  
[Figure of Destiny](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Figure+of+Destiny).

**What interests do you have other than *Magic*?**  
 I like the Japanese comedy duo “Sekai no Nabeatsu”.

**What is your most lasting impression from Grand Prix Kobe?**  
 Not getting [Mirrorweave](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirrorweave) off even once in the Swiss.

**What are your other major *Magic* achievements?**  
 None.

**What are your other major non-*Magic* achievements?**  
 None.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpkob08/T8Yoshimori.jpg)*Shou Yoshimori* 

**Shou Yoshimori**

**Hometown:** Chiba  
**Age:** 24  
**Profession:** Company employee

**What deck did you play, who designed it, and what was the best card in it?**  
 I played a traditional [Doran, the Siege Tower](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doran%2C+the+Siege+Tower) I designed myself. My best combo was [Leaf-Crowned Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leaf-Crowned+Elder) with [Doran, the Siege Tower](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doran%2C+the+Siege+Tower).

**Which card do you think is currently the most undervalued in *Eventide*?**  
 I don’t know yet.

**In your opinion, which *Eventide* card has had the most influence on Constructed?**  
[Figure of Destiny](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Figure+of+Destiny) and [Unmake](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unmake).

**What interests do you have other than *Magic*?**  
 Acapella and games in general.

**What is your most lasting impression from Grand Prix Kobe?**  
 In the 8th round, I was playing against Quick and Toast, and managed to pull out a win after getting hit by [Profane Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Profane+Command).

**What are your other major *Magic* achievements?**  
 I made Top 8 at three different GP Kobe Trials. I’ve also played in the Finals.

**What are your other major non-*Magic* achievements?**  
 Performing acapella throughout university.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpkob08/T8Takahashi.jpg)*Yuuta Takahashi* 

**Yuuta Takahashi**

**Hometown:** Tokyo  
**Age:** 22  
**Profession:** Student

**What deck did you play, who designed it, and what was the best card in it?**  
 I used a UB Faerie deck of my own design. All 15 of my sideboard cards were strong.

**Which card do you think is currently the most undervalued in *Eventide*?**  
[Endless Horizons](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Endless+Horizons).

**In your opinion, which *Eventide* card has had the most influence on Constructed?**  
 For Block Constructed, [Stillmoon Cavalier](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stillmoon+Cavalier). For Standard, [Figure of Destiny](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Figure+of+Destiny).

**What interests do you have other than *Magic*?**  
 Jazz, the works of Yukio Mishima.

**What is your most lasting impression from Grand Prix Kobe?**  
 I was playing Shintarou Ishimura. He went first and played [Devoted Druid](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devoted+Druid) followed by [Chameleon Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chameleon+Colossus), then [Cloudthresher](https://web.archive.org/web/20080803000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudthresher), but I still won.

**What are your other major *Magic* achievements?**  
 I’ve lost a lot.  
 (Editor’s Note: Takahashi won Grand Prix Shizuoka 2008 and was a Finalist at PT San Diego.)

**What are your other major non-*Magic* achievements?**  
 None...says a friend of mine.
