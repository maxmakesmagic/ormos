Join players around the world in a celebration of the past, present, and future of **Magic** you won’t want to miss: the 1st Annual **Magic** Game Day. Not only will it be your first chance to pick up and play *Tenth Edition*, but you’ll also receive an exclusive foil promo card of Reya Dawnbringer (while supplies last). You’ll also find league play, contests, tournaments, prizes, and learn-to-play demos. Bring a friend—he or she will get a promo card too.

The format for **Magic** Game Day is Sealed Deck. Each player receives five *Tenth Edition* 15-card booster packs to build their deck (minimum of 40 cards). Both tournament and league play will be supported at **Magic** Game Day.

If you enjoy **Magic** Game Day, you should look into playing at the next [Prerelease Event](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-prereleases-3), along with [Friday Night **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20130609095319/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/fnm). **Magic** Game Day kicks off the "[Summer of **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20130605171934/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/07summerofmagic)," so be on the lookout for many other opportunites to play **Magic** at your local stores.

Wondering what's in *Tenth Edition*? Visit the [product page](https://web.archive.org/web/20130718224306/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Article.aspx?x=magic/expansion/tenthedition) for card spoilers, packaging art, and much more.

### Summer of Magic

The [Summer of **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20130605171934/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/07summerofmagic-eu) will kick off the week of Game Day and last run until September 16. For those 10 weeks, there will be **Magic** being played at stores near you every night of the week. These Limited format tournaments (including Theme deck and Gateway tournaments) will provide you with easy and fun ways to win great prizes based on your participation.

Starting at **Magic** Game Day, every player will receive a participation card. At the end of the Summer of **Magic**, players then turn in their card at the store they played to receive Summer of **Magic** goodies! If you play in 10 matches, you'll receive a *Tenth Edition* deckbox and one booster. Play in 20 matches and your prizes increase to a *Tenth Edition* long-sleeve deckbox and three boosters. In addition, players can win **Magic**-branded sunglasses (while supplies last). 

For more information about the Summer of **Magic**, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20130605171934/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/07summerofmagic-eu).

Join players around the world in a celebration of the past, present, and future of **Magic** you won’t want to miss: the 1st Annual **Magic** Game Day. Not only will it be your first chance to pick up and play *Tenth Edition*, but you’ll also receive an exclusive foil promo card of Reya Dawnbringer (while supplies last). You’ll also find league play, contests, tournaments, prizes, and learn-to-play demos. Bring a friend—he or she will get a promo card too.

The format for **Magic** Game Day is Sealed Deck. Each player receives five *Tenth Edition* 15-card booster packs to build their deck (minimum of 40 cards). Both tournament and league play will be supported at **Magic** Game Day.

If you enjoy **Magic** Game Day, you should look into playing at the next [Prerelease Event](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-prereleases-3), along with [Friday Night **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20130609095319/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/fnm). **Magic** Game Day kicks off the "Summer of **Magic**," so be on the lookout for many other opportunites to play **Magic** at your local stores.

Wondering what's in *Tenth Edition*? Visit the [product page](https://web.archive.org/web/20130718224306/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Article.aspx?x=magic/expansion/tenthedition) for card spoilers, packaging art, and much more.
