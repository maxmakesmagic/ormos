![merfolk of the pearl trident](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=merfolk+of+the+pearl+trident)

Did you know that the [Merfolk of the Pearl Trident](https://web.archive.org/web/20040205000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merfolk+of+the+Pearl+Trident) was the only merfolk in **Magic** for over a year? The second merfolk, [Merfolk Assassin](https://web.archive.org/web/20040205000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Merfolk+Assassin), did not show up until [*The Dark*](/en/articles/makingmagic/2004/02-05-free-association-64). ([Lord of Atlantis](https://web.archive.org/web/20040205000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lord+of+Atlantis) is a lord and not a merfolk.)
