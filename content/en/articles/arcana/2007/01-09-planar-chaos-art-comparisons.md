As you’ll see at the [*Planar Chaos* prerelease](https://web.archive.org/web/20071020005522/http://wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/prerelease/planarchaos), the set plays with the theme of “time” in twisted new ways. Kev Walker’s art of [Damnation](https://i.ormos.uk/54/2a/542a6472a4ff0d0160dedaa4c7123c96cd437a68259296d6231dd049b4eec324.jpg), a virtual mirror of his *Seventh Edition* Wrath of God piece, gives us a visual statement of the idea that not all memories are happy ones.

Mouse over this high-resolution shot of Wrath of God to see the transition to *Planar Chaos’s* interpretation of the spell:

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/5c/a7/5ca74bd48ae17d83af9d1014f4fd562877f8d120526b0a964f640c64021040eb.jpg)](#)  
*Wrath of God and Damnation art by Kev Walker*

Alternate-universe Akroma is now a harbinger of her master’s [chaotic fury](/en/articles/feature/2007/01-08-order-and-planar-chaos) rather than of his righteous wrath. Mouse over Akroma below to see Daren Bader’s reimagining of Ron Spears’s original Akroma, Angel of Wrath into a red-aligned creature:

[![](https://i.ormos.uk/cc/54/cc5407071048154b3c5cbe80a0b32d8bb5d2c352fd2c91d96b8939be386fc7d5.jpg)](#)  
*Akroma, Angel of Wrath art by Ron Spears – Akroma, Angel of Fury art by Daren Bader* 

Stay tuned to **magicthegathering.com** for more of *Planar Chaos’s* twisted reflections and more.
