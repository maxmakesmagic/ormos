### Locations

[**Europe**](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603193730/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/07summerofmagic-eulocations) | [**Great Britain & Ireland**](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603193738/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/storechallenge-locations) | [**United States**](/en/events/2007/07summerofmagic-usa/summer-of-magic-locations) | [**Canada**](/en/events/2007/07summerofmagic-canada/summer-of-magic-locations)

Contact a Summer of **Magic** location near you to find out the specific day they will be hosting their next Summer of **Magic** Tournament. Also, start time, format, product mix, and entry fee. 

### When

If you enjoyed [**Magic** Game Day](/en/events/2007/07gameday/magic-the-gathering-worldwide-game-day), take the next step and stretch out the fun throughout the Summer of **Magic**. Tournaments begin July 21 (the week after **Magic** Game Day) and run until September 22. Playing in **Magic** Game Day release events will give you points for the Summer of **Magic** prizes. 

### Tournament Structure/Formats

Summer of **Magic** consists of Limited format tournaments and will provide players with a Summer of **Magic** point per registered match they play.The format for all Summer of **Magic** events is *Tenth Edition*. All tournaments should be run using Swiss rounds (minimum of 3 rounds of play).

*   *Tenth Edition* Sealed Deck (five booster packs)
*   1 *Tenth Edition* theme deck + 1 *Tenth Edition* booster Sealed Deck
*   *Tenth Edition* Booster Draft (three boosters of *Tenth Edition*)
*   Players build a minimum 40-card deck
*   All players must receive the same product mix![](https://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/magic/images/tournamentcenter/2007/gameday_deckbox.jpg)

### Prizes

Each player receives an Participation Card. The card will explain the details of Summer of **Magic** and how to register the matches they play. You will receive one point for each match played. At the end of the Summer of **Magic**, players then turn in their card at the store they played to receive Summer of **Magic** goodies! 

*   If you have at least 10 points, you receive a *Tenth Edition* deckbox with 1 booster.
*   If you have at least 20, you receive a *Tenth Edition* longsleeve deckbox with 3 boosters.

### Notes

*   All Summer of **Magic** Tournaments are sanctioned by the DCI™
*   Entry Fees includes product used in tournament and any additional prize support.
