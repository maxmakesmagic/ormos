So, maybe the cycle of *Legends* lands that grant banding abilities to certain-colored legends aren't exactly the most powerful cards in the game. They're even "hosed" by another banding-related land from that same set -- Tolaria -- just in case! 

But they are highly flavorful, and they have cool art. Check out some great old-school fantasy art from some unappreciated banding-era cards. Wouldn't your Adun Oakenshield feel right at home in one of these places, banding with his buddies Hunding Gjornersen and Ramirez DePietro?
