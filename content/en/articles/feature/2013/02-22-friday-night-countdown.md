There's a lot of information that comes out of a Pro Tour weekend. Rich Hagon and Brian David-Marshall watched all three days of [Pro Tour *Gatecrash*](/en/events/2013/ptgtc13/martell-calls-down-reckoning-at-pro-tour) unfold from the best seats in the house and now they bring you the key points from the new Standard metagame, tips for drafting each guild, and some of the milestone moments from a Pro Tour full of firsts. If you're looking to arm yourself with the hottest decks and draft strategies for your next Friday Night **Magic**, sit back and soak in the knowledge.
