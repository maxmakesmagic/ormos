This is Nils Hamm's [Illness in the Ranks](https://web.archive.org/web/20130313000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Illness+in+the+Ranks) from *Gatecrash*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/04/ac/04ac796388b95366fc53d3467074666f650bcb34ec0bc7b0b4c25ae2d9c0755f.jpg)

[Illness in the Ranks](https://web.archive.org/web/20130313000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Illness+in+the+Ranks) | Art by [Nils Hamm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Nils%20Hamm%22%5D)

These poor centaurs appear to have been stricken by some sort of horrible disease. Do they look a little familiar? 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/23/09/230959469643d90ba1e49281b9b3f4070e10f683f5d63dbddb0cc64163896806.jpg)

[Coursers' Accord](https://web.archive.org/web/20130313000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Coursers%27+Accord) | Art by [Nils Hamm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130313000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Nils%20Hamm%22%5D)

It's the centaurs from [Coursers' Accord](https://web.archive.org/web/20130313000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Coursers%27+Accord), also illustrated by Nils Hamm, from *Return to Ravnica*! That's what happens when you summon tokens into a world ravaged by anti-token plagues. On the upside, though, those centaurs probably aren't dead. They're just taking a little rest because they're not used to being only 2/2.
