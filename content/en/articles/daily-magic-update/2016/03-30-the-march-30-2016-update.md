The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on March 30, 2016. Today's Update is brought to you by [math](#math).

# Today's Must

1.  [*Magic* Math—Shadow Lands and Battle Lands](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/magic-math-shadowlands-and-battlelands/) | ChannelFireball | Frank Karsten

Confused about how you're going to be combining the new dual lands from *Shadows over Innistrad* with the ones we already have from *Battle for Zendikar*? Fret no more, as Frank Karsten—the mathiest guy in all of *Magic* content—breaks it down.

I mean, how can you beat opening sentences like "After seeing the new cycle of allied-colored dual lands from *Shadows over Innistrad*, I knew that I had to write an article on math and mana bases in the new Standard."

Of course you did, Frank. That's why we love you.

# After That, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [*Shadows over Innistrad* Spoilers: Best of the Rest—Part 1: White!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CL-WbUOTRxE) | The Mana Source | Wedge

Once again, Wedge cranked out a ton of content on new cards for *Shadows over Innistrad*, tracking everything as it was revealed. Well, with everything now revealed (and [fully stocked in Gatherer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&set=+%5b%22Shadows%20over%20Innistrad%22%5d)!), Wedge has started wading through everything else as well. White and blue are already done, and who knows, by the time I finish writing this sentence he might be through black, red, and green as well.

You can check out the [full playlist here](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLGsoeNcK9QA89LtitGX1bRGJsZ7lFI7u0), or just start watching videos below.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Red-White Equipment](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/suit-up-boros-equipment/)

Now that *Shadows over Innistrad* is fully searchable in [Gatherer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/), that means I can start showing off some of the cool new brews in the Deck of the Day section, since the deck lists won't look all wonky. So be prepared for so much Standard.

Today we're going to look at one of the more out-there decks I've seen brewed so far. Even the author, Brian DeMars, called it "one of the stranger decks to come out of my brewing sessions."

You can view the [article on the deck at ChannelFireball](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/suit-up-boros-equipment/).

At its kor—er, *core*—the deck is an aggressive-ish tokens-ish deck that uses Equipment to make all of its various tokens into real threats. [Stone Haven Outfitter](https://web.archive.org/web/20160330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stone+Haven+Outfitter) and [Weapons Trainer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Weapons+Trainer) then make those bonuses even more devastating, and [Reckless Bushwhacker](https://web.archive.org/web/20160330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reckless+Bushwhacker) turns a half-lethal crew whole.

And then there's [Bone Saw](https://web.archive.org/web/20160330000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bone+Saw).

The deck looks oddly synergistic and fun, which is not something I often say about Boros-flavored aggressive decks. It's quirky and new, and maybe we finally have enough pieces to turn this Draft archetype into a real contender.
