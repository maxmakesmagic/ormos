Orcs are one of those races that I believe have gotten short shrift in the game. I think the popularity of goblins has squeezed out space that in the past had been filled by orcs and [dwarves](/en/articles/makingmagic/2004/02-05-free-association-69). Mental note: look for more design space for orcs. And bleeder cards. Oh, and a new version of Feedback.
