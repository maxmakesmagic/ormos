There are three awesome images on *Dragon's Maze* booster packs. And you should already know all three! First is:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3d/ae/3daec3fc67106a1d506bce2f7676b4ce4855ace4fd02b8e1d83bf9c382941223.jpg)

That's Emmara Tandris! She's the Selesnya maze runner who you haven't seen yet, but readers of Doug Beyer's *The Secretist* know who she is.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ec/43/ec43f6c4724810f670f09540e3c1c0253371f130cb54fec156144c0da98f841a.jpg)

Here's the Izzet weird maze runner Melek, Izzet Paragon, Mike Cannon introduced him in [yesterday's From the Lab](/en/articles/from-the-lab/2013/04-08-a-little-weird).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/bb/8c/bb8c62108a3a0a3698c3ad9587baad042a32c81adccfc44df8d3c74fdfa223ae.jpg)

And here's Tajic, Blade of the Legion! The Boros maze runner with his awesome curvy sword.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7b/c3/7bc3040cf5484955a2514008ab1f29a3a189d124e172528f0405f659053ddfa5.jpg)

Also we'll be selling the awesome *Dragon's Maze* Fat Packs. Open the fat pack below to see what's inside.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/34/b0/34b059c287492a2d83626f9fd366706d8f62f6ed7193b0cb538d31299161a4bc.jpg)

Inside the Fat pack is:

*   Player’s Guide with complete visual encyclopedia
*   *Dragon's Maze* card box
*   Nine *Dragon's Maze* booster packs
*   Eighty-card basic land pack
*   Special edition Spindown life counter
*   Two deck boxes
