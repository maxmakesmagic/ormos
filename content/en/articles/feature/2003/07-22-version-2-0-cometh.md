### All The Details About The July 27 Update

* * *

There have been many questions about ***Magic** Online* Version 2.0 in the past month: Is it still coming out with the *Eighth Edition* launch? What's going to be in it? Do I need to buy the new Core Game to get the CD? I'm here to give you the rundown on what's up with Version 2.0.

You'll be happy to know that Grog is very pleased with his inclusion among the standard avatars. He has no current plans to hassle players who voted against him in the survey.

### Version 2.0 Launch Plan

Version 2.0 is still on track to launch on July 28 just before *Eighth Edition* product becomes available on ***Magic** Online.* As you might expect, the size and complexity of this update is going to require some extended downtime. Here's how things are going to happen this coming weekend.

* * *

***July 27***  
**11:00 AM Pacific Daylight Time:** ***Magic** Online* will enter "no pay" mode to give running events time to finish up.

**2:00 PM PDT:** The ***Magic** Online* servers will be taken offline for the Version 2.0 update.

The servers will remain down until the development team has a chance to propagate the Version 2.0 update to the live servers and check that things are running smoothly. We're planning on being done by around Midnight (approx. 10 hours after shutdown), but keep an eye on the [**Announcements Folder** of the ***Magic** Online* Message Boards](https://web.archive.org/web/20030722000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://boards1.wizards.com/forumdisplay.php?forumid=217&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm072203) for the notification that the servers are back up.

***July 28***  
**9:00 AM PDT:** *Seventh Edition* product will be replaced by *Eighth Edition* product at the [***Magic** Online* Store](https://web.archive.org/web/20030722000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm072203).

* * *

As Randy mentioned in [his article](/en/articles/feature/2003/07-22-some-background-thoughts-on-2-0), this is the last week that *Seventh Edition* product will be available in the store, so stock up now. After 9:00 AM on July 28, it's gone for good.

As an aside to this item, the triple-*Seventh Edition* draft queues will be available until the *Eighth Edition* queues open on July 30. During that time, you'll still be able to win *Seventh Edition* boosters in those queues.

### July Championship Delayed

Because of the update, the July Championship will be held on August 3, 2003 at 9:00 AM PDT. This also gives you an extra week to qualify!

### Loading From The Core Game CD

While the ***Magic** Online* CD in the *Eighth Edition* Core Game is a good place for new players to start, current online players shouldn't replace their installed versions of the client with it. Keep in mind that those CDs were printed several months ago, so will need to update for both Version 2.0 and the updates from those months.

What the CD does give you is code you can use at the [***Magic** Online* Store](https://web.archive.org/web/20030722000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://magiconlinestore.wizards.com&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm072203) that will cover the cost of your account and give you digital versions of the physical cards that come with the Core Game. It's a great way for new players to get a leg-up on their collections and to avoid the client [download from the ***Magic** Online* website](https://web.archive.org/web/20030722000000/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/downloads).

### What's New In Version 2.0

Randy talks about most of these features in his article, but I'm going over them here just to make sure you know what to expect after the Version 2.0 update.

***Features List***  

*   New Player and Expert Player Views
*   New **Sanctioned Tournaments** Room
*   Changes to the **Trading Post**
*   **Trophy Room**
*   **1800 Room**
*   Casual Game Auto-Matching
*   *Eighth Edition* Cards

***New Player and Expert Player Views***  
 When you log on for the first time after the update, you may be confused about what you'll see. Instead of the full spread of ***Magic** Online* rooms, you're only going to see six: **Tour**, **Slide Shows**, **Tutorials**, **Practice Room**, **Moderators & Support Staff**, and **Casual Play**. This is because you'll be looking at the **New Player View**.

In the top right corner of the **Main** room is a dropdown list. From it, you can select either the **New Player View**or **Expert Player View**. The **Expert Player View** is where you'll find all the rest of the rooms you're used to seeing: **Casual Play**, **Clans**, **Hall of Champions**, **Trading Post**, **Leagues**, plus the new **Sanctioned Tournaments** room and **1800 Room**.

Whichever view you have selected when you log off ***Magic** Online* is the view you'll see the next time you log on.

***Sanctioned Tournaments Room***  
 The **Sanctioned Tournaments** room is where you'll now find all sanctioned-play options within ***Magic** Online.* That means all Draft queues, Constructed queues, and Premier Events are inside the **Sanctioned Tournaments** area.

***Trading Post***  
 The **Trading Post** now has only three separate rooms: **Casual Trading**, **Message Board**, and **Marketplace**.

The **Casual Trading** room looks like the graphic view of the game rooms. If you have cards to trade, you set up a new table and other players can join your table to initiate a trade with you. Players will have icons over their avatars' heads indicating how many successful trades that player has had in the recent past. The size of the folder on a player's table indicates how many rares that player has open for trade.

The **Message Board** works like the current rooms in the **Trading Post**. You can post a short message describing what you have to trade or what you're looking for. Other players can select your message to initiate a trade with you. Messages will time out after being up for two hours.

The **Marketplace** is similar to the original **Trading Post** chat room, with one significant difference: the pause/play button. You can chat in this room to tell players what you're looking for. If you want to pause the scrolling so you can catch up, click the pause/play button. To resume the scrolling, click the button again.

***Trophy Room***  
 Once Version 2.0 is up, ***Magic** Online* will begin keeping track of your tournament and Premier Event wins within the game. The **Trophy Room** will show the numbers of different victories for each player. To see any player's **Trophy Room**, right-click the player's name and select **Trophies**.

***1800 Room***  
 The **1800 Room** is where players with a Composite rating of 1800 or higher can play against each other. Inside are four rooms: **Casual Constructed**, **Casual Limited**, **8-Player Draft**, and **8-Player Constructed**. The 8-player rooms have a 9-5 payout and cost 2 tickets plus product for Limited and 6 tickets for Constructed events.

If you don't have a Composite rating of 1800 or better, you won't be able to go inside the **1800 Room**. If you've reached 1800, you'll be able to play in the **1800 Room** until your Composite rating drops below 1750.

***Auto-Matching***  
 You won't have to wait for an opponent or shout "OPEN" in any of the **Casual Play** rooms anymore. Instead, when you set up a game, ***Magic** Online* will search the room for another player who wants to play the same format as you do. If there's someone else waiting to play a game in that format, you'll be automatically matched up with that player.

If you don't want to use the auto-matching feature, all you have to do is either type something in the **Comment** field or select **Buddies only** when you set up your game.

**Eighth Edition *Cards***  
 Once the update finishes, you'll be able to see *Eighth Edition* cards on the **Collection** screen; complete with the new art, frames, and overall design. In the **Number owned** dropdowns, select **<=** and **0** to view the cards before you own them.

That covers the major changes you'll find after the Version 2.0 update on Sunday, July 27. Again, after the system goes down, keep your eye on the [**Announcements Folder** of the ***Magic** Online* Message Boards](https://web.archive.org/web/20030722000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://boards1.wizards.com/forumdisplay.php?forumid=217&origin=magic_magiconline_articledm072203) for information on when the servers come back up. And, if you're planning on playing in the [*Eighth Edition* Release Events on July 30-31](/en/articles/eighthedition/2003/07-31-eighth-edition-release-events), make sure you get the Version 2.0 update earlier in the week.
