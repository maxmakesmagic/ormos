The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on August 15, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by *Commander (2017 Edition)* reviews. So many reviews.

# Today's Must

1.  [The *Commander 2017* Review](http://www.starcitygames.com/article/35645_The-Commander-2017-Review.html) | StarCityGames.com | Sheldon Menery

The godfather of Commander reviews the latest *Commander* release. That feels like a slam-dunk must read. Sometimes my job isn't that hard.

# After That, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [Top Ten Cards from *Commander 2017*](http://www.gatheringmagic.com/abesargent-08152017-top-ten-cards-from-commander-2017/) | Gathering Magic | Abe Sargent

He may not be the godfather, but Sargent knows his way around 99 cards, and here he picks out his ten favorites from *Commander (2017 Edition)*.

1.  [*Commander 2017* Set Review](https://www.brotherswarcast.com/2017/08/15/episode-10-commander-2017-set-review/) | The Brothers War | Ryan and AJ Greene

The Brothers War podcast is, appropriately, two brothers who focus on Commander. This week, in their tenth episode, they cover the legendaries of C17. So if you're looking for some ideas on how to use some of the newest, most legendariest cards from C17, give the bros a listen.

1.  [Krenko vs Sram vs Sakashima vs Ezuri EDH / CMDR Gameplay for *Magic: The Gathering*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NeK7bfVbFjA) | MTG Muddstah

If the impending release of *Commander (2017 Edition)* has you itching to watch some Commander play, MTG Muddstah has a unique approach to showing games on YouTube that makes them pretty easy to follow.

1.  [This is 31](http://www.hipstersofthecoast.com/2017/08/this-is-31-birthday-memories/) | Hipsters of the Coast | Zach Barash

Barash reflects on his life through the lens of *Magic*, year-by-year and card-by-card.

Also, it made me realize that Hurricane Sandy and getting trapped in Philadelphia was five years ago. Makes one feel old to see the time pass like that.

1.  [Pro Tour *Hour of Devastation* Afterthoughts](http://www.mtgmintcard.com/articles/writers/lee-shi-tian/pro-tour-hour-of-devastation-afterthoughts) | MTG Mint Card | Lee Shi Tian

It's a reflective sort of day, apparently, as Lee does his own walk through of recent events, turning what looked like a down Pro Tour into a reflection on how amazing this season was for him *and* his team.

1.  [Points Change Effective August 14, 2017](https://canadianhighlander.wordpress.com/2017/08/08/points-change-effective-august-14th-2017/#more-2034) | Canadian Highlander

This is the first time my decks have escaped unscathed in a points update. Here's to not owning any power!

# Top 25

1.  Romão Leaps into Top 25

When just 3 Pro points separate No. 13 from No. 24, you can expect any strong Grand Prix finish to send anyone in that range shooting up the charts—in this case Carlos Romão's Top 8 finish at [Grand Prix São Paulo](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-sao-paulo-2017) sent him skyrocketing up from No. 25 to No. 13—a whopping twelve-place leap.

Beyond Romão, there were few changes. Javier Dominguez went up three spots and Thiago Saporito and Martin Jůza got slight bumps, while a whole slew of folks stepped back to let those three rise.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [AndyGrey's Jeskai Gift](/en/events/2017/competitive-standard-constructed-league-2017-08-15/competitive-standard-constructed-league)

We've clearly reached the "What if we add another color?" stage of brewing with [God-Pharaoh's Gift](https://web.archive.org/web/20170815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=God-Pharaoh%27s+Gift), now sporting red. Red grants the deck a few things—[Cathartic Reunion](https://web.archive.org/web/20170815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cathartic+Reunion) to fill the graveyard, [Insolent Neonate](https://web.archive.org/web/20170815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Insolent+Neonate) to also fill the graveyard, and a semi-transformational sideboard that lets the deck go all [Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer) and [Chandra, Torch of Defiance](https://web.archive.org/web/20170815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Torch+of+Defiance) on some people.

But there are a few non-red innovations as well. [Trophy Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20170815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trophy+Mage) can helpfully search up [Gate to the Afterlife](https://web.archive.org/web/20170815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gate+to+the+Afterlife) while standing in as a serviceable body (and blocking fodder), and [Walking Ballista](https://web.archive.org/web/20170815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Walking+Ballista), previously not seen in this deck, can be played for zero to fill the yard quickly, can just do regular [Walking Ballista](https://web.archive.org/web/20170815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Walking+Ballista) things, or it can even come back as a 4/4 with [God-Pharaoh's Gift](https://web.archive.org/web/20170815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=God-Pharaoh%27s+Gift), ready to load up with counters.
