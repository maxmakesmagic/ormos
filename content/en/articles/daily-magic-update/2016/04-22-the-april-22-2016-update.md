The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on April 22, 2016. Today's Update is brought to you by [Pro Tour *Shadows over Innistrad*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-shadows-over-innistrad), because there is nothing else you should be watching this weekend. *Nothing*.

# Seriously. Nothing.

1.  [Pro Tour *Shadows over Innistrad*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-shadows-over-innistrad)

There's a whole lot going on with the Pro Tour *right now* (well, probably approximately right now, depends on when you're reading this). You could be watching on [twitch.tv/magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic/) right now. You could also read a cool feature on the [rise of East West Bowl](/en/events/2016/ptsoi/east-west-bowls-rise), one of the more successful newcomers to the team scene. Or you could just visit our [coverage page](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-shadows-over-innistrad) and find everything we have to offer. Because we're nice like that.

# After That, Read Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [What We Learn from Thraben Inspector](http://www.toplevelpodcast.com/thraben-inspector/) | Top Level Podcast | Patrick Chapin and Mike Flores

Considering that [Thraben Inspector](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thraben+Inspector) is probably in line to be one of the most played *cards* at the Pro Tour, this seems like a relevant topic.

1.  [Yo! MTG Taps!—The Final Episode](http://mtgcast.com/mtgcast-podcast-shows/active-podcast-shows/yo-mtg-taps/yo-mtg-taps-the-final-episode) | Joey Pasco and Stephen Marshall

Send off Yo! MTG Taps! in style by listening to their final episode.

1.  [Vintage 101: VSL Season Five with Randy Buehler!](http://www.mtggoldfish.com/articles/vintage-101-vsl-season-five-with-randy-buehler) | MTGGoldfish | Islandswamp

With the [newest season of Vintage Super League](/en/articles/uncategorised/2016/04-20-announcing-community-super-league) starting up Tuesday (followed by Community Super League Thursday), now's a great time to learn more about Vintage straight from the maestro of VSL, Randy Buehler.

1.  [You all liked old paperwork more than I anticipated. Here's more—Expansion submission form & sample *Ice Age* form, circa 1995-96](https://www.reddit.com/r/magicTCG/comments/4fwro8/you_all_liked_old_paperwork_more_than_i/)

More old-school paperwork from themisprintguy, these documents show the old-school rules for creating and submitting a *Magic* expansion. You can see an example from *Ice Age*, which has some pretty fantastic commentary about the cards from that era.

# Deck of the Day

1.  Deck of the Day goes on hiatus

As I write this, I'm watching two of my favorite decks at [Pro Tour *Shadows over Innistrad*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-shadows-over-innistrad): Esper Control and Black-Green [Seasons Past](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seasons+Past). I'm also looking directly at their decklists and, seeing as how this is the Pro Tour, I probably shouldn't be sharing those. So, given my intimate knowledge of every deck, it doesn't seem wise to post a decklist today.

Sure, I could write about another, non-Standard format, but that's kind of difficult with [Seasons Past](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seasons+Past) and [Narset Transcendent](https://web.archive.org/web/20160422000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Narset+Transcendent) dancing in my head. So, to get your fill of decklists at this Pro Tour, watch for them to appear on Sunday.
