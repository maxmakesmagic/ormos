### December 3, 2000: **2000 Pro Tour–Chicago**: Kai Budde wins again!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/05/70/057092a8cac8d0998c3dc4c88734e33fdc3914a07db07a6e3ee6a35f7fda526e.jpg)

Serving notice to the rest of the Pro Tour that his 1999 World Championship was no fluke, Kai Budde and his rebel deck took the prize [on December 3, 2000](https://web.archive.org/web/20081205000000/http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/event.asp?event=ptchi00). [Jon Finkel](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/jon-finkel-magic-pro-tour-hall-of-fame), [Zvi Mowshowitz](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/zvi-mowshowitz-magic-pro-tour-hall-of-fame), Brian Kibler, [Rob Dougherty](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/rob-dougherty-magic-pro-tour-hall-of-fame), Kamiel Cornelissen, Michael Pustilnik, and Jay Elarar filled out the star-studded Top 8,

Meanwhile, in the [2000 Chicago Masters](https://web.archive.org/web/20081205000000/http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/event.asp?event=masterschi00) event, Ben Rubin beat Jon Finkel in the finals, drafting green-white-blue with three Glimmering Angels to defeat Finkel's black-red burn deck.
