As the summer of national championships continue, Great Britain crowned its champion this past weekend. Daniel Royde made it to the top with a modified Eldrazi Green list that caused one match in the Top 8 to last nearly four hours! Daniel and the rest of the team will represent Great Britain at the 2011 World Championships. Read more about the tournament in the [official coverage](/en/events/2011/gbnat11/daniel-royde-crowned-king-of-great-britain).
