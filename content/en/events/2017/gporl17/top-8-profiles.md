Eight competitors fought through a field of nearly 1,300 to reach the Top 8, and a final draft of *Aether Revolt-Kaladesh* decided it all. Here's some more about the eight players who reached the top. 

* * *

### Joel Larsson

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gporl17/GP_ORL16_JoelLarsson.jpg)

**Age:** 25 

**Hometown:** Stockholm, Sweden 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Winner at Pro Tour *Magic Origins;* six Grand Prix Top 8s, national champion four times. 

**What colors was your Sealed Deck? Record?** Sultai colors (black-blue-green), 8-1. 

**What was the best card in your Sealed deck?** [Cowl Prowler](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cowl+Prowler). 

**What were your draft decks, and records with them?** Red-White 3-0, then 2-1 with Jund color (green-red-black). 

**What common or uncommon do you most want to open in the Top 8?** [Ridgescale Tusker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ridgescale+Tusker) and [Chandra's Revolution](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Revolution). 

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** A lot. I haven't made Top 8 in a while and I need the Pro Points. 

* * *

### Noah Walker

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gporl17/GP_ORL16_NoahWalker.jpg)

**Age:** 20 

**Hometown:** Amherst, Massachusetts 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Two Grand Prix Top 8s, and Game Day Champion! 

**What colors was your Sealed Deck? Record?** Green-White, went 8-1. 

**What was the best card in your Sealed deck?** [Aethersphere Harvester](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aethersphere+Harvester). 

**What were your draft decks, and records with them?** Two blue-red decks, 2-1 and then 3-0. 

**What common or uncommon do you most want to open in the Top 8?** [Ridgescale Tusker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ridgescale+Tusker) or [Aether Swooper](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aether+Swooper). 

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** Wouldn't have even been able to show up if not for my boy Alex S. Now I'm super close to Gold and going to Japan!! 

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gporl17/GP_ORL16_ChrisPikula.jpg)

* * *

### Zile Yao

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gporl17/GP_ORL16_ZileYao.jpg)

**Age:** 16 

**Hometown:** Anhui Heiei, China. Now studying at Ohio State University in Columbus. 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 31st at SCG Columbus; 10-5 at Grand Prix Pittsburgh. 

**What colors was your Sealed Deck? Record?** Black-Green, went 8-1, lost one because my opponent with two byes had [Duplicant](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duplicant). 

**What was the best card in your Sealed deck?** [Smuggler's Copter](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smuggler%27s+Copter) and [Walking Ballista](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Walking+Ballista). They're some good! 

**What were your draft decks, and records with them?** 2-1 with White-Blue, and then 3-0 with Black-Green. 

**What common or uncommon do you most want to open in the Top 8?** [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20170327000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push). 

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?** My dream was going to the Pro Tour during my time in university – I can't believe I made it when I was a freshman!
