Jun'ya Iyanaga is the 2011 **Magic** World Champion! Iyanaga's Standard deck, Wolf Run Ramp, is a well-established archetype that served him well in the Top 8, dispatching Josh Utter-Leyton, Conley Woods, and Richard Bland on his way to the title. Check out the [full coverage](/en/events/2011/worlds11/japan-stands-tall-at-worlds-again) to see how!
