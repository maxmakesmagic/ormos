Zombies have been in **Magic** since the beginning. Ever since [Scathe Zombies](https://web.archive.org/web/20040518000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scathe+Zombies) were introduced in *Alpha*, Zombies have been a living, non-breathing tribe. In fact Zombies have been so popular that over the years many other creature types have been "zombified" so that your dreams of an all Zombie deck could come true.

This is a tribute to all of the **Magic** creatures that have officially become Zombie:
