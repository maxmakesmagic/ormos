Last week, we [took a look at](https://web.archive.org/web/20111115000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/what-animate-dead-says-2011-11-08) the version of [Animate Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20111115000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Animate+Dead) that appears in *Premium Deck Series: Graveborn*. We mostly talked about the text, but the art is of some interest, too. Let's look at that card again!

Do you recognize the creature bursting its way out of the graveyard? *Do* you? Click here to be reminded of where you've seen it before.

Yes, it's an animated [Sun Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20111115000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sun+Titan).
