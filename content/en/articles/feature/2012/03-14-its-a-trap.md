As the Modern PTQ scene continues to develop, players are finding exciting new ways to merge eras of **Magic**. In a recent **Magic Online** PTQ, HotGoblin1 provided a new spin on a deck that briefly showed up in Extended last year: Green-White [Summoning Trap](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoning+Trap). Merging the best elements of that deck with the power of [Through the Breach](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Through+the+Breach), HotGoblin1 added extra consistency to a deck that was already quite explosive!

How can [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Aeons+Torn) and [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise) fit together in the same deck? With this deck, the name of the game is finding ways to sneak an [Emrakul](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul) onto the battlefield, and as you'll soon find out there are plenty of ways to cheat out the 15/15 Eldrazi.

The two most obvious ways to achieve an Eldrazi attack in this deck is to use [Summoning Trap](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoning+Trap) or [Through the Breach](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Through+the+Breach). Full of plenty of acceleration like [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise) and [Lotus Cobra](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotus+Cobra), this deck can hardcast one of those two cards as early as turn three. Either of those cards make it easy for an [Emrakul](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul) or "merely" a [Terastodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terastodon) to come out to play.

While obvious, those ways aren't even the most deadly. This deck makes use of the *Lorwyn* hideaway lands [Windbrisk Heights](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Windbrisk+Heights) and [Mosswort Bridge](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mosswort+Bridge) as another method to get out its fatties. Between 0/1 attackers and the quickly-growing [Knight of the Reliquary](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+the+Reliquary), the hideaway conditions are easy for this deck to meet. Best of all, when you play a card off of a hideaway land you get to cast it—thanks for the extra turn, [Emrakul](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul)!

In case your opponent comes packing hate, this deck isn't a one-trick pony either. It's full of viable beatdown cards, so if your opponents spend too much time stopping your combo, [Knight of the Reliquary](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+the+Reliquary) and [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan) might just run them over!

If putting [Emrakul](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul) onto the battlefield on turn three sounds fun, this might be the deck for you. The Modern world continues to grow—give it a try at a [Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* PTQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20120314000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/node/640181) near you!
