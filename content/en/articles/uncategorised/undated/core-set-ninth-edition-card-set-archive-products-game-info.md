## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/97/10/9710475f912240f6a072553fd3d354fe037b6da8f32aa06df32bb359e340b947.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set as well as two card boxes, six 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, a special oversized card, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/97/10/9710475f912240f6a072553fd3d354fe037b6da8f32aa06df32bb359e340b947.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/97/10/9710475f912240f6a072553fd3d354fe037b6da8f32aa06df32bb359e340b947.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 40 cards and a strategy guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160718093750/http://magic.wizards.com/node/976071)

(\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/97/10/9710475f912240f6a072553fd3d354fe037b6da8f32aa06df32bb359e340b947.png)

### TWO-PLAYER STARTER GAME

Includes two 30-card decks, one 14-card booster, a collectable premium card, two play guides, rulebook, two play mats with scorekeeping counters, and a CD-ROM with access to Magic Online.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/87/83/878317764720dd5c8e4fb961a641542873ed908d80a45e3ab7d007a6e03096d0.png)Ninth Edition features 350 cards, including some of the greatest **Magic** cards of all time. It also features randomly inserted premium black-bordered versions of all cards in the set.

**Set Name:** *Core Set Ninth Edition*  
**Number of Cards:** 350

**Languages**: English, Italian, French, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), German, Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish
