What Is the *Magic Online* Bug Blog?

While the [Known Issues List](https://web.archive.org/web/20150108000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-known-issues) is simply that—a running list of issues we expect players to encounter most often—this accompanying “Bug Blog” is the list’s companion. Here you’ll find updates regarding emergent new issues, details around issue statuses when applicable, known issue workarounds, and a running tally of Known Issues List updates. The Bug Blog will be updated each Thursday, in conjunction with the [Known Issues List](https://web.archive.org/web/20150108000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-known-issues) on the same day.

**Known Issues List Updates**

There's a little bit of everything this week when it comes to Known Issues List updates. There was one more item from the December 17 deployment that has now been correctly updated to a, "fixed" status. In addition to this, several issues have been added to the Known Issues List. Refer to the full [Known Issues List](https://web.archive.org/web/20150108000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-known-issues) to see the most recent updates.

* * *

**Fixed During the Wednesday, December 17 *Magic Online* Client Update**

**Server**

*   Limited Deckbuilding | Deck submission is not accepted and player is forced to restart client

* * *

**Newly Added Known Issues as of Thursday, January 8**

**Game Play**

*   Exodus | Client crashes when attempting to pay mana to prevent the effect of Fade Away for multiple creatures.
*   General | Cards that have multiple targets to select from such as Arc Lightning or Inferno Titan have the 'ok' improperly grayed out
*   General | Ponder occasionally shuffles library even though user chooses "No"
*   General | Scrying Glass replaces choosing 1 with 0

**Client**

*   Events | Dragging and dropping during drafting or deck building does not always work consistently
