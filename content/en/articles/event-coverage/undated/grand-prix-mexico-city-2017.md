## GRAND PRIX MEXICO CITY 2017

Day 2 Coverage

A total of 148 teams arrived at Mexico City this weekend to compete in the dynamic, robust format of Kaladesh block Team Sealed. Despite the small attendance, it turned out to be one of the most difficult tournaments in recent memory. 17 of the Top 25 Ranked Players were present, as well as 10 Hall-of-Famers and over 20 Platinum Pros.

When all was said and done, Andrew Cuneo, Eric Froehlich, and Ben Stark were the last ones standing. This marks Cuneo's first Grand Prix win and also Froehlich's and Stark's second title! To earn their trophies, they had to defeat the trio of Matthew Nass, Jacob Wilson, and Samuel Pardee who were shooting for their third team Grand Prix title, which would have been an astounding accomplishment should they have succeeded.

Rounding out the top 4 were the seemingly-unstoppable Toru Inoue, Shota Takao and Kazuyuki Takimura, who steamrolled through the competition before falling in the semifinals. Last but not least, Jon Stern, Greg Ogreenc, Stephen Neal also made it to the playoffs but lost to the eventual champions.

Once again, hearty congratulations to Andrew Cuneo, Eric Froehlich, and Ben Stark!

Day 1 Coverage

148 teams arrived at Mexico City today but only 40 will return tomorrow for five more rounds of Kaladesh block Team Sealed. After nine rounds of intense competition, Toru Inoue, Shota Takao and Kazuyuki Takimura are the lone 9-0's at Grand Prix Mexico City! They'll only need to pick up two or three more victories tomorrow to make the Top 4, while the rest of the pack will need to work a little harder!

Second in Swiss are Andrew Cuneo and Hall-of-Famers Eric Froehlich and Ben Stark. They're the only three at 8-1, but there are ten teams with 7-2 records right on their tails, including Maynard-Brain-Duin-Soorani, Baumeister-Larsson-Huang, Turtenwald-Jensen-Duke, Juza-Nakamura-Burkhart and Black-Severa-Cohen.

With so many big names at the top, tomorrow is going to be even more exciting!
