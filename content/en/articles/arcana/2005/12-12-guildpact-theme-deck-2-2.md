The upcoming *[Guildpact](https://web.archive.org/web/20080509092701/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/expansion/guildpact)* set features three pre-constructed theme decks, each themed around one of *Guildpact's* guilds.

The second one is called **Gruul Wilding**, and it looks a little something like this:

![Gruul Wilding](https://i.ormos.uk/fe/92/fe9229fe067466e9ac5230810558358ab0783949bb8b78648ac137d5e6ab61fa.jpg "Gruul Wilding")  
*Guildpact* theme deck 2 of 3

*Guildpact* releases February 4, 2006. Get your first chance to crack open packs at the [*Guildpact* prerelease tournaments](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-prereleases-3), coming to a venue near you January 21-22, 2006. Then don't miss the [*Guildpact* release tournaments](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-launch-parties-4) on the weekend the set hits shelves, February 4-5, 2006.
