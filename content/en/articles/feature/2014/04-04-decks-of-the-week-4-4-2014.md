San Francisco, California, played host last week to the [**Magic Online** Championship](https://web.archive.org/web/20140404000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/mocs13/welcome), won by Limited Player of the Year Lars Dam. Meanwhile, across the Pacific, Yuuya Watanabe won [Grand Prix Beijing](https://web.archive.org/web/20140404000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/gpbei14/welcome). That just happens to be his *seventh* Grand Prix victory (tying him with Kai Budde!) and *twentieth* GP Top 8! Wow! Congratulations, Lars and Yuuya! They will be going to Pro Tour ***Magic** 2015* in Portland this August, and you can join them by winning a Pro Tour Qualifier! The M15 PTQ season is Sealed Deck. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](https://web.archive.org/web/20140404000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtg/event/protour/qualifierlist#m15). Good luck; have fun!
