Ready for another story about the heroes of Kamigawa? Check out the [*Champions of Kamigawa* site](https://web.archive.org/web/20041013000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/displayexpansion.asp?set=chk&page=1) for an update in the "Meet the Champions" area.

[](https://web.archive.org/web/20041013000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/chk/jugan) This week's story is "**[The Dragon Shield](https://web.archive.org/web/20041013000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/chk/jugan)**" by Jay Moldenhauer-Salazar. It's the story of the protective influence of [Jugan, the Rising Star](https://web.archive.org/web/20041013000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jugan%2C+the+Rising+Star), and more generally, the role that a legendary dragon-spirit plays during Kamigawa's war between the kami and the mortals. Enjoy!
