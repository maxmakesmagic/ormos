Hey you! Yes, *you*! You look like someone who'd be interested in seeing what various *Innistrad* promo cards are going to look like! But first, you probably want to look at [our coverage of **Magic** stuff at PAX](https://web.archive.org/web/20110829000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/feature/158), because it's full of *Innistrad* news, and will also explain some of the things you're about to see.

Done? Good. *Now* let's look at promo cards!

### *Innistrad* Prerelease

The *Innistrad* Prerelease Events will be September 24-25, 2011. The full information [is here](https://web.archive.org/web/20110829000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/prerelease-facts), and the foil promo card is right here:

You'll get that card just for participating (while supplies last)! Just like the other one!
