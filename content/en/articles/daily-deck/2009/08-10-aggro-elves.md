Combo Elves have been all the rage of late, using Elvish Archdruid alongside Heritage Druid and Nettle Sentinel to repeatedly cast Primal Command, all in one turn. Apparently no one told Steve Pulliam, who powered into the Top 8 of a Pro Tour Qualifier for Austin in Portland, Oregon with a completely different take on the Elf archetype. He paired the most powerful and cost efficient Elves with a modicum of burn to control the red zone. Particularly cool? His combo of Manamorphose and Talara's Battalion!
