*Kaldheim* releases with two Commander decks, each containing eight all-new *Magic* cards. These decks are designed to introduce and level up newer players exploring the Commander format.

***(Editor's Note: The decklists below pull the newest printing of each card automatically from [Gatherer](https://web.archive.org/web/20210120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Default.aspx), including printings which are not in this product. These decklists are not card-for-card product displays but rather interactive lists of the cards included in each deck.)***
