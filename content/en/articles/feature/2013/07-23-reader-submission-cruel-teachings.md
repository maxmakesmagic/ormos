Using the email link at the bottom, a reader by the name of Aleksi (KaiserMagus on **Magic Online**), submitted an awesome-looking Modern deck. Aleksi played a [Cruel Ultimatum](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cruel+Ultimatum)/[Mystical Teachings](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystical+Teachings) deck to 4–0 in a Daily Event, and seeing as how I'm a big fan of both those cards, I decided to feature the deck. His postscript was what really sold me: 

> P.S. In case you were quick to dismiss the shameless request of spreading the word about my achievements, I played 2 [Cruel Ultimatum](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cruel+Ultimatum) and 2 [Mystical Teachings](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystical+Teachings) in the list. Is that not the stuff Daily Decks are made of? 
> 
> —Aleksi

I've had a long and rewarding relationship with [Cruel Ultimatum](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cruel+Ultimatum), and an even longer one with [Mystical Teachings](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystical+Teachings). The combination of the two is pretty epic, with Teachings giving you access to a toolbox of instants whose only purpose is to keep you alive long enough to cast [Cruel Ultimatum](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cruel+Ultimatum). Plus, you haven't lived until you've Snapcastered a [Cruel Ultimatum](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cruel+Ultimatum). 

![Cruel Ultimatum](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Cruel+Ultimatum)

Despite being based around a seven-mana sorcery and a four-/six-mana instant, the deck has a ton of early interaction. [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek) and [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) give you eight relevant one-drops, and [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) kicks in at two. [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) lets you recur any of the above cards, and even if you get behind, the three [Damnation](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Damnation)s can clean up the mess. 

One reason I really like this style of deck is that it gets to play a ton of different one-ofs, which not only give you outs for almost any situation, but make games play out in vastly different ways. Variety is the spice of life, and [Mystical Teachings](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystical+Teachings) is the spice of **Magic**. Even the creatures have flash, with [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage), [Vendilion Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vendilion+Clique), Teferi, and [Sulfur Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20130723000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sulfur+Elemental) all searchable via Teachings. 

Control decks in this vein have a tough time in the format that is Modern, so seeing this is like a flashback to 2007, which is a time I very much enjoyed. I don't think **Magic** should always play like that, but going back to the draw-go control style every now and then is a fun thing to do.
