### October 14, 1997: *Tempest* Hits

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8b/1c/8b1cb057447b6db015cfa1c2156a862d88aac114091768c064a1868af9e81352.jpg)

Twelve years ago, Gerrard Capashen and the crew of the *Weatherlight* kicked off the Rath Cycle, picking up where *Weatherlight* left off. This set introduced **Magic** players to Slivers, [Cursed Scroll](https://web.archive.org/web/20091016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cursed+Scroll), [Living Death](https://web.archive.org/web/20091016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+Death), and [Tradewind Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20091016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tradewind+Rider). And also [Phyrexian Grimoire](https://web.archive.org/web/20091016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Grimoire), [Phyrexian Hulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20091016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Hulk), and [Phyrexian Splicer](https://web.archive.org/web/20091016000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Splicer), which is all the excuse we need to point out that two days ago, we announced [Duel Decks: Phyrexia vs. The Coalition](/en/articles/arcana/2009/10-14-announcing-duel-decks-phyrexia-vs-the-coalition) on the anniversary of *Tempest*'s release!
