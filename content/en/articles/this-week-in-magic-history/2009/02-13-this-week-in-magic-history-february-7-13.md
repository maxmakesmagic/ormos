### February 9-11, 2007: Mike Hron Wins Pro Tour–Geneva 2007

![Mike Hron, winner of Pro Tour–Geneva](http://wizards.com/sideboard/images/ptgen07/F_Trophy_Hron.jpg "Mike Hron, winner of Pro Tour–Geneva")

It had been over six years since an American had won a Limited Pro Tour. But Wisconsin's Mike Hron turned that around with his virtuoso performance in *Time Spiral* Booster Draft.

The final match against Takuya Osawa was one for the ages, culminating in a rare Osawa misplay when he appeared to misread Spectral Force.

### Also This Week

*   February 4-February 7, 1999: Mike Long takes down the *Duelist* Invitational in Barcelona, defeating Sturla Bingen in the finals. The scene was set for Rootwater Thief to enter the *Nemesis* set!
*   February 4-February 7, 1999: Kai Budde wins Grand Prix–Barcelona. Another time a Grand Prix run at the same time as the Invitational was won by a future Invitationalist!
