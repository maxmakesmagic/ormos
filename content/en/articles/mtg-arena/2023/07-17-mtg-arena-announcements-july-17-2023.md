## Tomorrow: Historic Anthology 7 and Explorer Anthology 3

![](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/haswesw5xifa.jpg) 

Tuesday, July 18—that's tomorrow!—50 new-to-*MTG Arena* cards arrive to expand the Explorer and Historic formats. Start making plans for new deck possibilities by checking out the [Historic Anthology 7 Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/06-30-historic-anthology-7-comes-to-mtg-arena) and the [Explorer Anthology 3 Card Image Galleries](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2023/06-30-explorer-anthology-3-arrives-on-mtg-arena), and then pick up yours tomorrow.

Oh, and one more thing . . .

## Explorer Anthology 2 Returns for a Limited Time!

We're bringing back Explorer Anthology 2! If you missed this anthology of cards the first time around, this is your chance to catch up. Strengthen your Explorer and Historic card repertoire with cards like Eidolon of the Great Revel, Brave the Elements, and Satyr Wayfinder.

![Eidolon of the Great Revel](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/cj8z994waxkw.png) ![Brave the Elements](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/axsmd9s8f5m7.png) ![Satyr Wayfinder](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/yyhkc8wgj5u3.png) 

And boost your land game with Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx.

![Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/93gkwczvzqdk.png) 

Check out all 25 cards in the [Explorer Anthology 2 Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/12-05-explorer-anthology-2-arrives-december-13). You can pick up yours in the *MTG Arena* Store for 4,000 gems or 25,000 gold. But don't let this opportunity slip by—**Explorer Anthology 2 will only be available until September 5!**

## Historic and Explorer Metagame Challenges

New sets releases and more cards open up the metagame, and that means the Metagame Challenge can't be far behind. In this case, *two* are coming soon!

**July 21–24**, take all those new cards from Historic Anthology 7 and Explorer Anthology 3 and prove your metagame mastery in the **Historic Metagame Challenge**.

**July 28–30**, sharpen that mastery and bring it to the digital table for the **Explorer Metagame Challenge**.

Both events bring with them gold and pack prizes. You can win *The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth* packs in the Historic Metagame Challenge, and *Shadows over Innistrad Remastered* packs for the Explorer Metagame Challenge.

## 2023 Rotation and Renewal Is Coming

![Graphical representation of sets that will be rotating in Standard and Alchemy](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/67ljephudutr.jpg) 

It's hard to believe, but we're already closing in on the 2023 Standard rotation and Renewal in *MTG Arena*, and this year introduces changes to how rotation will work going forward. Check out the [Renewal for *MTG Arena*'s 2023 Standard Rotation article](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2023/07-17-renewal-for-mtg-arenas-2023-standard-rotation) for complete details on what to expect as we near September, the release of *Wilds of Eldraine*, and rotation for Standard and Alchemy.

## Premier Play Schedule

All times listed are Pacific time (UTC-07:00).

### Qualifier Events

[Qualifier Play-In events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-play-in) are single-day tournaments in which players compete to earn invitations to that month's Qualifier Weekend events.

[Qualifier Weekend events](https://magic.gg/events/qualifier-weekend) are two-day events in which eligible players compete for invitations to upcoming Arena Championship events. Check out the standings to see [who's qualified for Arena Championship 4](https://magic.gg/standings/arena-championship-4).

Qualifier tokens are delivered in your *MTG Arena* inbox. Remember to claim them before the event starts!
