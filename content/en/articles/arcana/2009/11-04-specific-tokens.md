Sometimes, tokens are generic. The *Zendikar* Bird Token is, frankly, just a bird:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4c/67/4c67cf702c035dc13464a1913899d194a5219489cc39508e590d4865d6afcf81.jpg)  

Other times, however, a token moves from the general to the particular. Take, for example, this *Zendikar* Wolf token:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6a/54/6a540b873cd18f80bdd8e107c200c2910d741b9a73ae0e41568f01c50d60b9e7.jpg)  

You *could* use it with your [Fable of Wolf and Owl](https://web.archive.org/web/20091104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fable+of+Wolf+and+Owl) or [Master of the Wild Hunt](https://web.archive.org/web/20091104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+the+Wild+Hunt). But isn't it especially handsome next to [Turntimber Ranger](https://web.archive.org/web/20091104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Turntimber+Ranger)?

![Turntimber Ranger](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Turntimber+Ranger)

Yep. Same wolf.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/35/f1/35f1c4c6fe760740cdea426a50fc6bcdf8c9a239638bdd5c6e6eb6777eca873c.jpg)[Turntimber Ranger](https://web.archive.org/web/20091104000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Turntimber+Ranger) art by Wayne Reynolds  
  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/52/68/5268ce32cbb41e4096a298292f870810675bbff7d0a426ec793f1ad54023d42e.jpg)*Zendikar* Wolf Token art by Daren Bader
