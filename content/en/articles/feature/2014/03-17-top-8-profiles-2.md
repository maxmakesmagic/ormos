![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmon14/Robertson.jpg)

**Ian Robertson** **Age:** 24  
**Hometown:** Calgary, Alberta  
**Occupation:** Business Analyst

 

**Previous **Magic** Accomplishments:**  
 Almost got Top 8 at a PTQ a few times!

**What was your record in Sealed? What did you play? What was the hardest decision in your build?**  
 8-1. Hardest decision was whether to splash for sick cards or not.

**What was your record in Draft 1? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**

**2-0-1 with Green-Blue. First-picked [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix). It was either that or [Ornitharch](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornitharch). I thought white would be over-drafted... also it's better?** 

**What was your record in Draft 2? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**  
 2-0-1 with Green-Black. First-picked [Asphyxiate](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Asphyxiate). The card is awesome and I like black in this format.

**What's your favorite *Born of the Gods* Draft archetype, and why?**  
 Green-Blue.

**Which *Born of the Gods* card exceeded your expectations in Limited, and why?**  
 Both [Retraction Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Retraction+Helix) and [Arbiter of the Ideal](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arbiter+of+the+Ideal).

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmon14/Parker.jpg)

**Brock Parker** **Age:** 32  
**Hometown:** Silver Springs, MD  
**Occupation:** Poker player

 

**Previous **Magic** Accomplishments:**  
 Team Pro Tour Champion \[Boston 2003 with Huey Jensen and Matt Linde as "The Brockafellars"\], Team Grand Prix Champion \[Turin 2000 with Daniel Clegg and Peter Szigeti as "Team Clegg"\], and Grand Prix Pittsburgh 2013 Champion.

**What was your record in Sealed? What did you play? What was the hardest decision in your build?**  
 8-1 with Blue-Green with [Kiora, the Crashing Wave](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kiora%2C+the+Crashing+Wave), [Polukranos, World Eater](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Polukranos%2C+World+Eater) and [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix). Had to decide which five of seven bad cards to play.

**What was your record in Draft 1? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**  
 3-0 with White-Green Heroic. First-picked [Akroan Skyguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroan+Skyguard). It's great.

**What was your record in Draft 2? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**  
 1-1-1 with Green-White 2 [Aspect of Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aspect+of+Hydra) bad deck. First-Picked [Ghostblade Eidolon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghostblade+Eidolon). The pack was terrible but white is great.

**What's your favorite *Born of the Gods* Draft archetype, and why?**  
 White-based Heroic decks. White has a million great cards that all work well together.

**Which *Born of the Gods* card exceeded your expectations in Limited, and why?**  
[Kiora](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kiora%2C+the+Crashing+Wave), even though my expectations were very high. As far as not-great cards, [Crypsis](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crypsis).

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmon14/Gomes.jpg)

**Ben Gomes** **Age:** 23  
**Hometown:** Livermore, CA  
**Occupation:** Insurance Adjuster

 

**Previous **Magic** Accomplishments:**  
 23rd at GP Oakland 2013, 17th at GP Sacramento 2013.

**What was your record in Sealed? What did you play? What was the hardest decision in your build?**  
 7-2 with a Red-White Aggro deck that played only a single card that cost more than three. The hardest decision was passing on green's [Courser of Kruphix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Courser+of+Kruphix) and Hunter's Prowess.

**What was your record in Draft 1? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**  
 3-0 with Red-White Aggro. I only had five white cards, but one was Elspeth. I first-picked [Hero of Leina Tower](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero+of+Leina+Tower).

**What was your record in Draft 2? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**  
 3-0 with Red-White splashing Blue. I ended up with too few playables so I splashed [Nimbus Naiad](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nimbus+Naiad) off [Astral Cornucopia](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Astral+Cornucopia). I first-picked [Akroan Conscriptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroan+Conscriptor).

**What's your favorite *Born of the Gods* Draft archetype, and why?**  
 Blue-Green with [Griptide](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Griptide)s. That card is unreal.

**Which *Born of the Gods* card exceeded your expectations in Limited, and why?**  
[Fearsome Temper](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fearsome+Temper) has been superb for me.

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmon14/Alt.jpg)

**Judah Alt** **Age:** 36  
**Hometown:** Watertown, NY  
**Occupation:** Writer

 

**Previous **Magic** Accomplishments:**  
 Monied at Pro Tour Philadelphia 2005. Top 8'd States.

**What was your record in Sealed? What did you play? What was the hardest decision in your build?**  
 8-1 with Blue-White Ephara featuring [Akroan Horse](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroan+Horse) and [Plea for Guidance](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plea+for+Guidance). Hardest decision was leaving [Divination](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divination) out of the main deck.

**What was your record in Draft 1? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**  
 3-0 with Blue-White. I first-picked [Temple of Enlightenment](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temple+of+Enlightenment) hoping to go base-white aggro.

**What was your record in Draft 2? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**  
 2-1 with White-Red-Green. It was aggressive, removal-heavy and mostly white, supported by two Temples, [Opaline Unicorn](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Opaline+Unicorn) and [Springleaf Drum](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Springleaf+Drum).

**What's your favorite *Born of the Gods* Draft archetype, and why?**  
 Blue-based decks with [Retraction Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Retraction+Helix) and untapping cards such as [Crypsis](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crypsis), [Kiora](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kiora%2C+the+Crashing+Wave)'s Follower and [Breaching Hippocamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Breaching+Hippocamp).

**Which *Born of the Gods* card exceeded your expectations in Limited, and why?**  
[Mischief and Mayhem](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mischief+and+Mayhem), because on **Magic Online** I always lose to it, yet never win when I play it myself.

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/gpmon14/t8fournier.jpg)

**Daniel Fournier** **Age:** 21  
**Hometown:** Toronto, Ontario  
**Occupation:** Student

 

**Previous **Magic** Accomplishments:**  
 Four Pro Tour Qualifier Top 8s. Six Grand Prix Day 2s. One Grand Prix winner's photo. Once beat "the" Andrew van Leeuwen in a sanctioned match.

**What was your record in Sealed? What did you play? What was the hardest decision in your build?**  
 9-0 with Blue-Red. I struggled with which mediocre two-drop was best in my deck.

**What was your record in Draft 1? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**  
 3-0 with Red-White Aggro. First picked and did not play [Ornitharch](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornitharch). It was the best card in the pack by far.

**What was your record in Draft 2? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**  
 1-0-2 with Blue-Red Aggro. First picked [Kragma Butcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kragma+Butcher) in an otherwise weak pack because I only needed to win one match to make Top 8.

**What's your favorite *Born of the Gods* Draft archetype, and why?**  
 White-Red Aggro or heroic. When it's open it's completely obscene.

**Which *Born of the Gods* card exceeded your expectations in Limited, and why?**  
[Everflame Eidolon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140317000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Everflame+Eidolon). It won me many games. When it falls off it still trades with something on the ground.

 Winner Grand Prix Dallas 2011, three Grand Prix Top 8s, worked with the 40-card Friedman
