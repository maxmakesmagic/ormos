Mons's Goblin Raiders, one of *Magic*'s first cards, was named by creator Richard Garfield after friend and playtester Mons Johnson. With a long career working on *Magic* (and other Wizards of the Coast games, including *Duel Masters*) and a few more Goblins named after him along the way, Mons shared some stories and history behind iconic Goblins featured in his own Pashalik Mons Commander deck.
