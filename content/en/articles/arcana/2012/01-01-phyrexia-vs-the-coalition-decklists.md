As you may be aware, *Phyrexia vs. The Coalition* was played at [Pro Tour–San Diego](https://web.archive.org/web/20120101000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/ptsd10/welcome) this weekend by *Robot Chicken* writers. If you weren't aware of that, we apologize for springing this information on you like that. You can get [the whole story here](https://web.archive.org/web/20120101000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/ptsd10/robotchicken).

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/arcana/391_playing.jpg)

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/arcana/391_allfour.jpg)

And the decks looked like this!

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/arcana/391_deck1cards.jpg)

![](http://archive.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/arcana/391_deck2cards.jpg)
