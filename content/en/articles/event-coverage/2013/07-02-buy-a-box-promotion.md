Here's an offer that is sure to appeal to every player: The first 20 (for Core locations) or 30 (for Advanced locations) people that preorder or purchase a **Magic: The Gathering** *Magic 2014 CORE SET* booster box in your retail store, will get an Alternative Art Premium Ratchet Bomb promo card! But hurry, this offer is limited to only the first 20 or 30 people per store who eithre preorder or purchase a full booster box before July 19!
