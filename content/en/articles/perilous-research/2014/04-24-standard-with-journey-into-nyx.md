Welcome back to Perilous Research! We're just a few days away from the *Journey into Nyx* Prerelease. Prereleases are excellent opportunities to meet other players in our local area. We might learn about new and exciting interactions or be given a chance to share our own discoveries with others. Most importantly, the Prerelease is a chance to be among the very first players to play with *Journey into Nyx*. Today, we'll be taking a break from dissecting **Magic Online** metagames to discuss the potential impact *Journey into Nyx* will have on the Standard metagame.

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/pr/2014/pr_wk16_296_cardart_atheros.jpg)

Athreos, God of Passage | Art by [Ryan Barger](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22ryan%20barger%22%5D)

We can expect aggressive white strategies to experience a resurgence. Godsend; Tethmos High Priest; Deicide; Banishing Light; Dictate of Heliod; Athreos, God of Passage; and Iroas, God of Victory are all strong cards that will undoubtedly revive the fallen archetype. Let's take a look at what some of the new White Weenie decks might look like.

This white-black list has a lot of interesting stuff going on. [Cartel Aristocrat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cartel+Aristocrat) in conjunction with [Xathrid Necromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Xathrid+Necromancer) and Athreos, God of Passage can do some pretty exciting things. That being said, this list is very rough and it seems like it might be trying to play too many angles. Hit the forums and share the decklists you've made that use Athreos, God of Passage!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/89/b4/89b4a06c3abeee8dee9875b72908d2d3177582bf37d4cda8dd57e3b5e5b78ac7.jpg)

Blue Devotion seems to have fallen off the radar coming into *Journey into Nyx*. A lot of players are assuming that the White Weenie decks having access to both [Banisher Priest](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banisher+Priest) and Banishing Light will make [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) a lot worse. They're right, but [Master of Waves](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+of+Waves) gets quite a bit better if we play Hall of Triumph in the sideboard of our Blue Devotion decks.

The most exciting thing for the Blue Devotion strategy is Hypnotic Siren. [Judge's Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge%27s+Familiar) and [Cloudfin Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudfin+Raptor) have long been the targets of ridicule. It seems odd that such weak cards could make the cut in one of Standard's best archetypes. Hypnotic Siren serves as a tremendous replacement. We still get our [Flying Men](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flying+Men) out of the deal, but in the later stages of the game where top decking a [Judge's Familiar](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Judge%27s+Familiar) or [Cloudfin Raptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudfin+Raptor) would be disastrous, top decking [Hypnotic Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hypnotic+Specter) gives us a [Control Magic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Control+Magic) effect that actually pumps the creature we're stealing and gives it evasion. I expect Hypnotic Siren to be universally adopted by Blue Devotion strategies in the coming weeks.
