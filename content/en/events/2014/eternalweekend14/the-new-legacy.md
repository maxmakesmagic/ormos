The current Legacy is one of the most interesting and skill-testing formats in *Magic*'s history. Legacy's sheer card volume usually means the format's development is slow and steady. Recently, the addition of delve cards like [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) and [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) have shaken things up quite a bit, though. Let's talk about the current tier one in Legacy!

Elves has always been a contender in Legacy, but the deck improved a lot with the new Legend rule. Now, the deck can get multiple Gaea's Cradle activations in a single turn and easily produce more than enough mana to end the game with one of its powerful combos. Elves has been amassing a lot of tournament success over the last few months and it looks to be capable of keeping up with the [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) decks.

There are two metagame White Weenie strategies that do particularly well in the new Legacy. Maverick, a Green/White deck that takes advantage of Green Sun's Zenith and Death and Taxes, a single color White creature strategy with a mana denial theme built around cards like [Wasteland](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland), [Rishadan Port](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rishadan+Port), [Thalia, Guardian of Thraben](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thalia%2C+Guardian+of+Thraben), and a mix of other powerful spells. The White Weenie strategies of Legacy won't be able to play with much from Khans of Takir, but [Spirit of the Labyrinth](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spirit+of+the+Labyrinth) and [Gaddock Teeg](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gaddock+Teeg) will likely become a much more focal part of their game plan. 

The new Legacy is wide open. Today, we'll have an opportunity to see a new format in action. Will [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) continue its stay at the top of the world? or will a new dominant force emerge? Stay tuned to continuing coverage of The Legacy Championship to find out!
