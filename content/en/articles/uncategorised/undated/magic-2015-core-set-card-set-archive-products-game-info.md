## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e8/30/e8307e04da4da0d7a5e1c0694d380f7ac19ca1a34066c6d97add1d9a93b6644c.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to help make your deck better, whatever way you play. You might even find a premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/71/04/71049b555b980ca17e422160922f3c8ffb844abedf78453902fb8f71ce779b06.png)

### FAT PACKS

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the pack as well as two full core set deck boxes, nine 15-card booster packs, 80 basic land cards, and a special-edition life counter.

(Not available in all languges.)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/92/a3/92a3c7ec3431011e13f6fb83fe68c0b3bf74f50f46dea8e6e3c6c16aed6ef6f8.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 cards, a foil premium rare card, two sealed 15-card booster packs, a strategy insert, and a Magic learn-to-play guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160712040751/http://magic.wizards.com/node/253001)

(Not available in all languges.)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/23/62/236250544378e874019f0dbaa52d9597541ad0cd02d3ff4306a374af7c9e5186.png)

### Deck Builder's Toolkit

The support you need to build your own **Magic: The Gathering** decks. Includes 125 semi-randomized cards suitable for building several different decks, four 15-card booster packs from a variety of recent **Magic** sets, 100 basic land cards, a deck builder’s guide, a **Magic** learn-to-play guide, and a card box.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b0/44/b044f06e33965d34818bb49aa40eae5fd21b35fd0fe8bc7d3862545708c6bd3d.png)

### CLASH PACKS

Includes two ready-to-play 60-card decks, 6 premium cards with alternative art, a deck box, a Magic rules reference card, and a strategy insert that walks you through one way to combine the two decks into a single deck that is more powerful.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160712040751/http://magic.wizards.com/node/252766)

(Not available in all languges.)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6a/3e/6a3e90e55efef5260e2976638c6f55d35285e6c6d6c43e342237b638ddd7d62a.png)

### Magic Online Deck Builder's Essentials

Designed to complement the cards included with every new *Magic Online* account, the 2015 Core Set Deck Builder's Essentials adds over 125 additional common, uncommon, and rare cards to your collection. Deck Builder's Essentials includes 2 Event Tickets which can be used to enter *Magic Online* Events, where you can compete for prizes.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160712040751/http://magic.wizards.com/node/390386)
