## Daily Activity

The qualifier season for [Pro Tour *Return to Ravnica*](/en/events/2012/seattle12-qualifiers/qualifiers-for-pro-tour-return-to-ravnica-in-seattle) continues apace. If you're interested in reaching the Pro Tour and flying out to Seattle to compete in October, find a PTQ [near you](https://web.archive.org/web/20120629000000/http://locator.wizards.com/) and bring your favorite Standard deck. But don't go there unprepared! Find out what you might be up against with the Top 8 decklists from some of the recent PTQs (including those on **Magic Online**)!
