The "favors" of ***Magic** 2012* ([Divine Favor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divine+Favor) and [Dark Favor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Favor)) have parallel abilities. Although they both boost power and toughness, one focuses on offense and one focuses on defense.

<table><tbody><tr><td><div><div><img alt="Divine Favor" src="http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&amp;name=Divine+Favor"></div></div></td><td><div><div><img alt="Dark Favor" src="http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&amp;name=Dark+Favor"></div></div></td></tr></tbody></table>

The art, of course, demonstrates this duality. [Divine Favor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divine+Favor) shows a wizard with her palms held protectively inward, surrounded by magical shields.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a9/11/a91127da0fc893973e18478d5ef83db2effb1b0c17c016deec48029bd04d52b6.jpg)

[Divine Favor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divine+Favor) | Art by [Allen Williams](https://web.archive.org/web/20110727000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Allen+Williams%22%5D)

[Dark Favor](https://web.archive.org/web/20110727000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Favor), on the other hand, shows a wizard with fists held up, ready to fight. And what's he surrounded by? Magical knives!
