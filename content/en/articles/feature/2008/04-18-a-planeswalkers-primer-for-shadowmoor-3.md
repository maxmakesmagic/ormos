*Shadowmoor*'s prerelease is imminent. But before you go into battle, you need to know what new tricks you'll have up your sleeve. We're sure you've been checking out the [Card Preview Archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20080418000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/shadowmoor/spoiler) every day and devouring every word in our preview articles. To aid in your preparation, we've recorded videos for all the new *Shadowmoor* mechanics so you can see **hybrid**, **wither**, **persist**, **conspire**, and **untap** in action.
