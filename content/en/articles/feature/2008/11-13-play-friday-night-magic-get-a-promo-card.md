Forge your destiny! Play *Shards of Alara* in at least two FNM (Friday Night **Magic**) tournaments between November 14th and December 12th, 2008 and receive one Hypnotic Specter\* promo card in your next MPR mailing! 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b5/f8/b5f8ad915e0e240387d6ff0120ece6bd8c394f3768998937a67764cd13a2faf6.jpg) 

  

Magic Player Rewards is a free program that rewards DCI members for their participation in sanctioned **Magic: The Gathering** tournaments with never-before-seen textless spells. 

All registered MPR members, with up-to-date mailing information on file, are welcome to participate. FNM results will be reported to the DCI by all Tournament Organizers on or before December 18, 2008\*\*. 

To learn more about MPR**,** register, or update your contact information, please go [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20090122233233/http://wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=magic/rewards/welcome).

Click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20090122233212/http://wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=events/magic/fnm) to find a FNM location near you.

* * *

\* Supplies limited. Wizards of the Coast reserve the right to substitute promotional card with item of equal value. Promotional cards will be mailed to qualifying participants with their January MPR mailing.

\*\* Only official Friday Night **Magic** tournament results received by Wizards of the Coast prior to December 18 will count towards promotion eligibility.
