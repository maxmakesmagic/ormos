The Pro Tour is a **Magic** festival, with tons of things to do whether you're qualified to play professionally or just there to take in the sites and sounds of the festivities. Case in point: tons of Public Events featuring formats like Two-Headed Giant, Legacy, and Vintage. Today we look at a Legacy list that did well at an event in California that awarded a set of *Revised* dual lands to the winner! For the full story, visit the [official coverage](/en/events/2010/ptsd10/gortzen-goes-all-the-way-in-san-diego).
