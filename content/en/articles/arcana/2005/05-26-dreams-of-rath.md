S*aviors of Kamigawa* hits shelves in nine days, on June 3rd. But did you see this little number at the prerelease or in the [spoiler](/en/articles/feature/2005/05-23-saviors-of-kamigawa-sortable-spoiler-2)? 

 ![Dreamcatcher](https://i.ormos.uk/5e/39/5e3912afd662608ecdef1b5fa1309ed9ed6bcedc35005d5252d7e8be8ad214a0.jpg) 

Dreamcatcher, a 1/1 kami of daydreams, is shown feeding off of the thoughts of a dozing Kamigawa peasant. In the misty shapes that orbit the kami, you can see a bird, a dragon, and... 

...check this out:

 ![Dreamcatcher art by Mark Tedin](https://i.ormos.uk/6a/2c/6a2c5c2d1767e38a3f9346e6e854f09219040d59dfcd65d2ee652703f1e1ddc7.jpg) 

The Dreamcatcher has leeched a *sliver* -- a creature not native to the plane of Kamigawa -- out of his head. That peasant must have quite the imagination -- and clearly artist Mark Tedin does!
