It has been a busy month for Luis Scott-Vargas. He kicked things off by winning [Pro Tour-Berlin](/en/events/2008/ptber08/lsv-elves-equals-champion), then a few weekends later managed to earn the title of [Grand Prix-Atlanta](/en/events/2008/gpatl08/back-to-back-for-lsv). In between his two titles he *also* managed to place in the Top 4 of the California State Championships using this Merfolk list. You can find LSV’s list and more in the [2008 State Championship archive](/en/events/2008/all/state-championships-2008-top-8-decklists).
