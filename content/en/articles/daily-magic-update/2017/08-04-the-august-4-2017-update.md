# Deck of the Day

1.  [White Eldrazi](/en/events/2017/competitive-standard-constructed-league-2017-08-03/competitive-standard-constructed-league)

We've seen red Eldrazi decks, black Eldrazi decks, and now white Eldrazi-fueled decks have started to pop up. Where for art thou, green and blue?

At any rate, beyond the typical Eldrazi squad of [Thought-Knot Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thought-Knot+Seer) and [Matter Reshaper](https://web.archive.org/web/20170804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Matter+Reshaper) (though note the lack of [Reality Smasher](https://web.archive.org/web/20170804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reality+Smasher)), white also grants you the powerful [Eldrazi Displacer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Displacer) that happens to be able to fairly neatly keep a [Hazoret the Fervent](https://web.archive.org/web/20170804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hazoret+the+Fervent) in check.

In fact, you might notice that this deck has a bunch of ways to keep Hazoret from being terribly effective. [Stasis Snare](https://web.archive.org/web/20170804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stasis+Snare), [Cast Out](https://web.archive.org/web/20170804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cast+Out), and [Declaration in Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20170804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Declaration+in+Stone) all easily handle the indestructible creature, and [Thought-Knot Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thought-Knot+Seer) can sometimes grab her before she even enters the battlefield.

Beyond that, the deck is mostly a pile of midrange white creatures. [Archangel Avacyn](https://web.archive.org/web/20170804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archangel+Avacyn), [Selfless Spirit](https://web.archive.org/web/20170804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Selfless+Spirit), [Thraben Inspector](https://web.archive.org/web/20170804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thraben+Inspector), and [Gideon, Ally of Zendikar](https://web.archive.org/web/20170804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Ally+of+Zendikar) are all pretty much white staples at this point, and [Skysovereign, Consul Flagship](https://web.archive.org/web/20170804000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skysovereign%2C+Consul+Flagship) gives the deck a way to punch through to planeswalkers as well.
