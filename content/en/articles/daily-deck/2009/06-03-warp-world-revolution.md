It's a Revolution! Chicago area native Chris Boomer is intent on bringing Warp World back into power in Standard. He played this list of the deck to a Top 8 at a recent PTQ. The goal of the deck? Building up as many permanents into play as possible, then blowing the world up with Warp World. Thanks to token generators and acceleration like Fertile Ground, he gets to reveal more cards than his opponent, all while fueling his draw engine with Mulldrifter and Elvish Visionary and even getting Warp World back with Nucklavee!
