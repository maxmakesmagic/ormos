This is the *Rise of the Eldrazi* Fat Pack.

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ba/2a/ba2a5fa390e80b29e36dc68140d748c16050aaa43a4b7cd31275784a72596992.jpg) 

  

Here is a handsome display of all the contents.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/07/69/07699102f2ffbf148fe2e9cfca06f3e46da0fa35158f592c64f4a41b60ddfb04.jpg) 

  

Let's hit a few highlights. To the right, we've got a sample chapter of *Zendikar: In the Teeth of the Akoum*. Note that it's a sample chapter, even though it looks like it might be the full book in the picture there. It actually looks like this:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ac/14/ac14dab79b3e2ceceb99d19df77f8ea4c9cde1934ce0f6951e74f0b6c2fea1d1.jpg) 

  

There's also, of course, a spindown counter and eight boosters. For a better look at the boosters, check out [yesterday's Arcana](/en/articles/arcana/2010/03-10-rise-of-the-eldrazi-booster-packs).

There's also a storage box, the wraparound mural, a player's puide, and forty *Rise of the Eldrazi* basic land.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ab/3c/ab3c9219e57f8a4e062d6b986fc3ae055d61856cefd623a51538fd9eff9e93da.jpg)
