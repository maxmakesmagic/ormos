* * *

The *Mirrodin* Release Events will run on a schedule -- three tournaments each hour -- beginning at 9:00 AM (09:00 hours) Pacific Standard Time on Wednesday, December 3, 2003. Since tournaments won't be running off queues, you'll be able to preregister for the events beginning Tuesday, November 25. (We'll post to the [Announcements folder](https://web.archive.org/web/20031106000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://boards1.wizards.com/forumdisplay.php?forumid=217&origin=magic_magiconline_news110803) on the Message Boards when the tournaments are available for preregistration.) Each event will cost 2 Event Tickets, 1 *Mirrodin* tournament pack, and 2 *Mirrodin* boosters (required when you sign up).

Like physical **Magic** Prerelease Tournaments, every player who joins one of the ***Magic** Online Mirrodin* Release Tournaments gets something -- a Bosh, Iron Golem tradeable avatar. As a digital object, it will show in your collection and you can trade it to other players. While an avatar digital object is in your collection, you can use it to represent you while playing ***Magic** Online.*

You get one copy for each of the Release Tournaments you play in, but you have to play through at least one round. If you drop before the tournament starts, you won't get an avatar for it.

After the *Mirrodin* Release Tournaments are over, you won't be able to get a Bosh, Iron Golem avatar unless you trade someone for it.

### How Many Release Tournaments Will There Be?

The *Mirrodin* Release Tournaments will begin at 9:00 AM (09:00 hours) Pacific Standard Time on December 3, with three tournaments launching each hour.

However, since we aren't running the Release Tournaments on queues this time around, we want to make sure everyone has ample opportunity to join one. So, the final three tournaments will launch at 11:00 PM (23:00 hours) Pacific Standard Time on Sunday, December 7.

No, that's not a typo. Release tournaments will run for five straight days.

And, because they're scheduled events instead of queues, preregistration will be available before the events take place. On Tuesday, November 25, keep your eye on the [Announcements folder](https://web.archive.org/web/20031106000000/http://wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://boards1.wizards.com/forumdisplay.php?forumid=217&origin=magic_magiconline_news110803) on the Message Boards to find out when the tournaments have been added to the **Premier Events** schedule.

### Side Events

Also unlike other Release Events, the new draft queue won't start up until after the Sealed Deck tournaments are finished.

The current *Odyssey* block draft queue will be replaced by a *Mirrodin* draft queue on Monday, December 8. Unlike other single-set drafts that run only during the Release Events, this will remain until the *Darksteel* release on ***Magic** Online.*

The draft queue will be standard cost: 2 Event Tickets plus product used.

### *Mirrodin* Reconstructs *Magic Online*

There's only one date to remember:  
  
*December 8, 2003*  
*Mirrodin* leagues will begin.  
*Odyssey* block leagues will no longer be available.  
*Mirrodin* Limited events begin in the **Premier Events** room.  
*Odyssey* block Limited events will no longer be available in the **Premier Events** room.  
 Standard and Extended Constructed sanctioned tournaments will allow *Mirrodin* cards.

As soon as *Mirrodin* cards are available on ***Magic** Online,* you can play both Constructed and Limited events using *Mirrodin* cards in any of the **Casual Play** rooms.

### Summary

So, join the rest of the ***Magic** Online* community in round-the-clock *Mirrodin* Release Events from Wednesday, December 3 at 9:00 AM (09:00 hours) PST through Sunday, December 7 at 11:00 PM (23:00 hours) PST. Play in the Release Tournaments and get the limited-release Bosh, Iron Golem avatar. Then, on Monday, December 8, *Mirrodin* draft queues and leagues will be available.

The *Odyssey* is over. Welcome to *Mirrodin!*
