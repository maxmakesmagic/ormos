Visit Innistrad in the special Full Innistrad Sealed League Event, running on *Magic Online* September 14–21!

Looking for ideas for your next vacation destination? Plan a trip to the beautiful plane of Innistrad! Bask in scenic vistas, from the [Nephalia Drownyard](https://web.archive.org/web/20160907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nephalia+Drownyard) all the way to [Stensia Bloodhall](https://web.archive.org/web/20160907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stensia+Bloodhall). Feeling outdoorsey? Take a trip to Kessig and you just might catch a glimpse of local Spirits, like the [Kindercatch](https://web.archive.org/web/20160907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kindercatch) or the [Permeating Mass](https://web.archive.org/web/20160907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Permeating+Mass)! Whether you’ve come to observe the majestic [Maw of the Mire](https://web.archive.org/web/20160907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Maw+of+the+Mire) or are just trying to keep up on the latest in abomination innovation ([Ludevic has been busy!](https://web.archive.org/web/20160907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=221173)), there’s a little something for everyone on Innistrad.

Players can enter the Full Innistrad Sealed League starting September 14, 10:00 a.m. PT/5:00 p.m. UTC. Entrants get two *Innistrad*, one *Dark Ascension*, two *Shadows over Innistrad*, and one *Eldritch Moon* booster pack to build from. Once your deck is built, play five matches before the League ends on September 21, 7:00 p.m. PT/2:00 a.m. UTC. Remember, this event will be available for one week only, so play while it's still around!

Enjoy your visit to Innistrad, and don’t forget a [Jar of Eyeballs](https://web.archive.org/web/20160907000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jar+of+Eyeballs) to commemorate your trip!

**Closed Time**: September 20 at 7:00 p.m.  
**End Time**: September 21 at 7:00 a.m.

**Entry Options**:

*   28 Event Tickets
*   2 *Innistrad* booster packs, 1 *Dark Ascension* booster pack, 2 *Shadows over Innistrad* booster packs, 1 *Eldritch Moon* booster pack, and 4 Event Tickets
*   280 Play Points

**Format**: Sealed League  
**Structure**: Five matches, each up to 50 minutes
