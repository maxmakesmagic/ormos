Over the last 14 years of Magic's history, many **Magic** decks have met cruel and unusual fates. Whether hurled at 40 mph into a basement wall, toasted in a fire, or eaten by plane-crash survivors in the Andes mountains (okay, we made that one up), decks have met their bitter demise on many occasions.

Today, we have something very special...a minty, clean ball of **Magic** cards. After a 0-2 record, this FFL Land Destruction deck was left in the jacket pocket of Magic Creative Manager Jake Theis. Not only did it fail at destroying its opponent's lands, it failed at surviving its washing machine's saving throw. Tossed into the drink by Jake's wife, this deck not only marinated in the washer; it also was forged in the crucible of the dryer. The result: a card pie.
