## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b3/bd/b3bdd9badc10ee1c949a2a57805079a5c599d014e8ca318e25d787a1c7942e09.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a guide with pictures of all cards in the set, a card box with panoramic art, eight 15-card booster packs, 40 basic lands, a special-edition life counter, and excerpts from *A Planeswalkers Guide to Alara*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f7/c6/f7c6c1426c3efba34c1ab9431b3771df2fd309d1ef790f482d847d54879f1c0e.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2d/c8/2dc83860934e222466efeeecc0f54fe622325ad7893335240dc7bd65206c291e.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes a 41-card preconstructed deck, a rare foil card, a strategy insert, a learn-to-play insert, and one 15-card *Shards of Alara* booster pack.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044817/http://magic.wizards.com/node/961626)

(\* Not available in all languages)

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/82/f9/82f9177c507477a7cf82f6437614d6e9862362ec8114b7dae674e3d3b7e44271.png)*Shards of Alara* is the first set in the *Shards of Alara* block. It’s a 249-card expansion set with randomly inserted premium cards.

**Set Name:** *Shards of Alara*  
**Block:** Set 1 of 3 of the *Shards of Alara* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 249

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044817/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/EN_MTGALA%20FAQ%20092308.doc)

**Game Design and Development:** Bill Rose (design lead), Devin Low (development lead), Aaron Forsythe, Mark Globus, Mark L. Gottlieb, Graeme Hopkins, Alexis Janson, Erik Lauer, Michael Mikaelian, Kenneth Nagle, Matt Place, Mark Rosewater, Brian Tinsman, Mike Turian, and Noah Weil
