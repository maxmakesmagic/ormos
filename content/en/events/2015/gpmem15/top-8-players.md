# Brad Nelson

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmem15/T8-Nelson.jpg)

**Age:** 28  
**Hometown:** Roanoke, VA  
**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Standard! 

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Abzan Control 

**What did you expect to play against? Did you play what you expected to see?**  
 White-Red, Jeskai, and Abzan Aggro 

**What was the best card in your main deck and sideboard, and why?**  
[Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion). Jacob Van Lunen put a few copies in the sideboard earlier this week and I been sideboarding them in every matchup. 

 **How will you travel through time: *Back to the Future* or *Time Cop*?**  
 Back to the Future! 

* * *

# Alex Majlaton

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmem15/T8-Majlaton.jpg)

**Age:** 30  
**Hometown:** Lanham, MD 

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Seven other Grand Prix Top 8s; first Standard Top 8! 

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Abzan Control designed by Steve Rubin. It’s very robust and had a great plan versus every other deck. 

**What did you expect to play against? Did you play what you expected to see?**  
 I expected to play against other Abzan decks, Blue-Black Control, and White-Red/Jeskai decks. I didn;t play against very much Jeskai but otherwise everything was normal. 

**What was the best card in your main deck and sideboard, and why?**  
 Main deck: [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ugin%2C+the+Spirit+Dragon) got me out of some tough spots.  
 Sideboard: [Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion) - The aftr board plan versus basically every deck. 

 **How will you travel through time: *Back to the Future* or *Time Cop*?**  
 Back to the Future. I’d be Doc and Ben Friedman would be Marty.

# Jack Fogle

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmem15/T8-Fogle.jpg)

**Age:** 24  
**Hometown:** Lexington, KY  
**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Two PTQ wins this year; multiple Grand Prix cash finishes 

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Sultai Control “sans clans”. I have an unhealthy affinity for [Planar Cleansing](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Planar+Cleansing) effects.

 Abzan, Jeskai, White-Red, and Red-Green, although way more Abzan than expected

[Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time): It’s the card you hope to draw every game and gives you the tools to wins. 

 **How will you travel through time: *Back to the Future* or *Time Cop*?**  
 Back to the Future

# Eric Rath

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmem15/T8-Rath.jpg)

**Age:** 27  
**Hometown:** Coralville, IA  
**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** 3-1 a Modern Daily Event with a four-of Seeance in my deck 

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 White-Red Aggro. It had pretty even matchups across the board, and I get ot attack people with big, hastey Dragons. 

**What did you expect to play against? Did you play what you expected to see?**  
 I expected a lot of mirror matches and Abzan Aggro. 3-0 versus the mirror and 5-0 versus Abzan Aggro. 

**What was the best card in your main deck and sideboard, and why?**  
 Main Deck: Heliod’s Pilgrim. Northing better than [Chained to the Rocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chained+to+the+Rocks) number five with a body.  
 Sideboad: [Evolving Wilds](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Evolving+Wilds). The deck really wants 25 lands in the postboard games with six 5-drops. Also lets me cut the Mana [Confluence](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Confluence) in the mirror. 

 **How will you travel through time: *Back to the Future* or *Time Cop*?**  
 Time Cop; Jean Claude Van Damme can’t be stopped!

# Chris Fennell

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpmem15/T8-Fennell.jpg)

**Age:** 32  
**Hometown:** Tampa, FL  
**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Seven Limited Grand Prix Top 8s; Top 8 Pro Tour Born of the Gods 

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Abzan Control because it had all the answers, and Steve Rubin and Brad Nelson broke the sideboard. 

**What did you expect to play against? Did you play what you expected to see?**  
 Midrange decks, then White-Red and Jeskai, and Abzan Aggro 

**What was the best card in your main deck and sideboard, and why?**  
 Elspeth, Sun’s Champion in the main deck. It lets you play both offense and defense, and handles problems.  
[Fleecemane Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fleecemane+Lion) in the sideboard because people board out Lighting Strike and [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20150222000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight). 

 **How will you travel through time: *Back to the Future* or *Time Cop*?**  
 Time Cop
