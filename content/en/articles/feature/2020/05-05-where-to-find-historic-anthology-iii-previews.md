Historic, *Magic: The Gathering Arena*'s nonrotating format, is quickly approaching its third release, bringing with it even more cards from *Magic*'s past to unlock new and interesting strategies.

To celebrate, we're revealing every card in Historic Anthology III on May 8 at outlets around the internet. We've already given you a sneak peek at two cards included in Historic last week on Weekly MTG. Take a look:

Or, if you're in a hurry, you can check out the cards here:

![Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger](https://media.wizards.com/2020/images/daily/Ulamog-the-Ceaseless-Hunger-Arena.png)![Phyrexian Obliterator](https://media.wizards.com/2020/images/daily/Phyrexian-Obliterator_Arena.png)

If you want to see more, check out these outlets on May 8. We'll update this list as cards are previewed and include a list of added cards, so you can also check back here to see what you've missed and what is still yet to come.
