Worlds isn't all about the World Championships, you know. It's about a big festival of **Magic**, at which all sorts of wacky things happen. Well, not *all* sorts. That sounds excessive. Nobody spontaneously turned into custard or anything. But there was this:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/bf/74/bf7486b3513e94b7fd76d1f49e8596c64914cbe4d94108fe4d47816b8ec68008.jpg)  

Or if you're not into the idea of sticking your head through a hole so you can pretend to be Jace, there was this:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/10/7c/107cdbf4e3b213db3aead98da3dcb5846eac1879853dd3076ce27b19dbb7b414.jpg)  

Mark Tedin wasn't the only artist there. There was also Chris Rahn, Chippy, and Raymond Swanland. Read all about them [here](/en/events/2010/worlds10/2010-magic-the-gathering-world-championships-feature-the-state-of-the-art-at), as well as Yoshino Himori, who illustrated the Manga-style Jace and Chandra.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8d/5c/8d5c025cd75c02eb60d6dc4b520aaaa0b31d5bf681966ba4b6faea133b761497.jpg)  

There was also a pair of game shows hosted by Mark Rosewater and Koichiro Maki. You can try to get that experience yourself [here](/en/events/2010/worlds10/2010-magic-the-gathering-world-championships-question-mark) and [here](/en/events/2010/worlds10/feature-the-magic-game-show), but it won't be as good as being in Japan.

We could go on for days listing things, but we'll close with Massive **Magic**. Mark Rosewater and Richard Garfield faced off with huge cards (more information and cool pictures [here](/en/events/2010/worlds10/2010-magic-the-gathering-world-championships-feature-massive-magic-mayhem)), and in the course of the game, the following cards were revealed:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/49/84/4984c0f3767d34dbecfee217b33fc13ea6f9dc50ba5b5b3384bf375328451754.jpg)  

Hey ... those are from *Mirrodin Besieged*! Here's a better look at them, especially if you don't speak Japanese.
