As you may be aware, *Phyrexia vs. The Coalition* was played at [Pro Tour–San Diego](https://web.archive.org/web/20100222000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/g%C3%B6rtzen-goes-all-way-san-diego) this weekend by *Robot Chicken* writers. If you weren't aware of that, we apologize for springing this information on you like that. You can get [the whole story here](/en/events/2010/ptsd10/theyre-alive-robot-chicken-and-phyrexia-vs-the-coalition).
