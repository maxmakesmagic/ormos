**Standard PTQ for Pro Tour *Ixalan* Saturday, July 15**

**Build Notes**

The following notes are from the July 5 build:

Limited | [Naga Vitalist](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Naga+Vitalist) is now able to add mana of any color when you also control a [Cascading Cataracts](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cascading+Cataracts).

Limited | [Scarscale Ritual](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scarscale+Ritual) can now correctly only put -1/-1 counters on creatures.

Commander | [Lifeblood Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifeblood+Hydra) no longer hurts you for having negative power.

Limited | [Vastwood Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vastwood+Hydra) distributes the correct number of +1/+1 counters upon death.

Collection | Flip cards can again be searched for by name.

Commander | [Phyrexian Soulgorger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Soulgorger) will now correctly gain more than 1 age counter.

Standard | [Kalitas, Traitor of Ghet](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kalitas%2C+Traitor+of+Ghet) will make the correct number of tokens with [Anointed Procession](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anointed+Procession).

Limited | [Watchers of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Watchers+of+the+Dead) will correctly leave split cards alone if not chosen.

Commander | [Profane Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Profane+Command) now respects the X in its cost both in terms of reanimating, and giving all its legal targets fear.

General | You can now correctly redirect spells that can target a player but originally did not.

Commander | [Strionic Resonator](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strionic+Resonator) can now be resonated. Strionically.

Commander | If you’re lucky enough to control multiple Planeswalker emblems, they will now all display their correct art.

Commander | [Panoptic Mirror](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panoptic+Mirror) now correctly reflects instants and sorceries.

Standard | [Tezzeret the Schemer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tezzeret+the+Schemer)’s Etherium Cells now properly indicate if they were made this turn by right clicking and looking for “Newly Controlled.”

Commander | [Grinning Ignus](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grinning+Ignus) can now correctly only grin at sorcery speed.

General | Damage Prevention shields now display immediately on the permanent or player they are protecting.

Legacy | [Doomsday](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doomsday) now functions correctly with undo. Good luck, Have fun.

General | Stabwhisker is a little less Odious and will no longer crash the game.

Legacy | [Dismember](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismember) no longer gets around [Trinisphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trinisphere) without paying 3 mana.

Duel Scene | Yield All (F8) now respects zero mana plays.

Commander | [Mana Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Bloom) now functions correctly.

General | [Suffer the Past](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Suffer+the+Past) no longer suffers from incorrect mana costs.

General | [Dragonlair Spider](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlair+Spider)’s tokens now use the correct art.

Commander | [Ooze Flux](https://web.archive.org/web/20170711000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ooze+Flux) now correctly secretes oozes.
