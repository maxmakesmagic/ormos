![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/tpc12/header.jpg)

The 2012 *Magic* Players Championship showcases the best players in the world battling against one another for the title of Player of the Year. Sixteen players will be invited to compete across three formats for the title and $40,000 first prize, with a total cash purse exceeding $100,000.

With Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* in the books, all 16 slots are set for this massive tournament. Start following all the Players Championship talk on Twitter with the hashtag [#mtgpc](http://twitter.com/#%21/search/realtime/%23mtgpc) and mark your calendars for three days of can't-miss live coverage of this unique event.
