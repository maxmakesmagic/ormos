**Announcement Date:** December 20, 2010  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2011

 ***Scars of Mirrodin* Block Constructed**, **Standard, Extended, Vintage**  
 No changes

**Legacy**  
 Survival of the Fittest is banned.  
Time Spiral is unbanned.

**Magic Online** effective dates and changes to **Magic Online**–only formats are now announced monthly in the [**Magic Online** Group](https://web.archive.org/web/20101220000000/http://community.wizards.com/magiconline) .

For the complete list of all banned and restricted cards by format, click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20130705231600/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=judge/resources/banned).

For an explanation of the changes to Legacy from R&D member Erik Lauer, click [here](/en/articles/feature/2010/12-20-explanation-of-december-2010-b-r-changes).
