**Announcement Date:** September 18, 2009  
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2009

**Legacy**  
 Dream Halls is no longer banned  
Entomb is no longer banned  
Metalworker is no longer banned

**Standard, Extended, Vintage, *Shards of Alara* Block Constructed**  
 No changes

Changes to **Magic Online**–only formats are now announced monthly in the [Magic Online Group Blog](https://web.archive.org/web/20090918000000/http://community.wizards.com/magiconline/blog/).

For an explanation of this announcement, read the [Latest Developments](/en/articles/latestdevelopments/2009/09-18-this-land-is-your-land) column today, September 18. For the complete list of all banned and restricted cards by format, click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20130408054028/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=judge/resources/banned).
