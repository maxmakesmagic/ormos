Join players from everywhere - for serious **Magic** fun!  
  
 Covering multiple formats and taking place around Europe, Grand Prix are for ALL players, from amateurs to pro players. In addition to the two-day main tournament open to all, Grand Prix are filled with wall-to-wall side events: card signing by **Magic** artists, fun play against professional players, 8-player drafts, Junior - or 2 headed-giant tournaments and a lot more! One thing is for sure: Grand Prix are unique and great memories.  
  
 What are you waiting for? Book your flights now with your friends and get your cards ready.  
  
 Check out for yourself what it is like to be part of a Grand Prix.  
  
 The 2007 Season can be found [here](/en/events/2007/europe2007/european-grand-prix).  
 You can find the Worldwide Grand Prix Information [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20091019040251/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=grandprix).  
  
  

### Prizes and Pro Points

Each Grand Prix tournament has a prize purse of $30,000, with a first-place prize of $3,500, except for the ***Summer Series*** including GP Madrid and GP Copenhagen which will feature increased prize money. To see the complete payout and Pro Point structure, click [here](/en/events/2002/prizes/worldwide-grand-prix-prize-structure)  
  
 You can also earn [Pro Points](https://web.archive.org/web/20080823110553/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=grandprix/points) by finishing high at a Grand Prix. These points count toward your [Pro Players Club levels](/en/events/2006/playersclub/pro-players-club-guidelines-and-procedures) and in the Pro Tour Player of the Year Standing.
