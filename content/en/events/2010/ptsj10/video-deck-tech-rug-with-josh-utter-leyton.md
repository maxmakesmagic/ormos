Top 8 San Juan competitor Josh Utter-Leyton shows off his red-blue-green Block Constucted deck that has been successful since its debut in the **Magic Online** Championship Series back in April won by Brad Nelson. Better known as wrapter on **Magic Online**, Josh earned his invitation to San Juan by winning an online qualifier.
