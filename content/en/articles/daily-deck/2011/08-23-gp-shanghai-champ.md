The latest Grand Prix champion is no stranger to the Daily Decks spotlight. The winner? None other than former Player of the Year Yuuya Watanabe! He took down the title in Shanghai, China with a green-blue draft number that splashed juuuuuuuust enough red for a Fireball. For more, check out [full coverage](/en/events/2011/gpsha11/watanabe-wins-the-showdown-in-shanghai) of the event.
