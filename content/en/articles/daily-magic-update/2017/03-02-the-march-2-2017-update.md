The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on March 2, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by the last day of individual *Modern Masters 2017 Edition* previews.

# Today's Previews

1.  [Ode to Blood Moon—Exclusive MM3 Preview](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/articles/ode-to-blood-moon-exclusive-mm3-preview?src=wizards) | MTGGoldfish | SaffronOlive

SaffronOlive might be the biggest [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20170302000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) fan on the planet, so who else would we get to preview the most hateful of all cards in Modern?

1.  Such Justice. Much Fire.

1.  [Exclusive *Modern Masters 2017* Preview Card for Magic: The Gathering – MTG!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FhQo9suWKNA) | The Mana Source | Wedge

Wedge is beyond excited for this one. His words. Not mine. But, with a preview this sweet, he has good reason to be.
