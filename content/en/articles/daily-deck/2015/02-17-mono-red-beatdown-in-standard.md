With the release of *Fate Reforged*, followed by [Pro Tour *Fate Reforged*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-fate-reforged), the Standard metagame continued to evolve even as pro players prepared for the Modern Pro Tour. I might even say the pro players were the ones left behind. At least I was. I'm spending most of my time now checking new decklists, trying to catch up after being Rip Van Winkle for almost a month.

This week, I will introduce some interesting looking Standard decks I stumbled upon on *Magic Online*, especially ones with cards from *Fate Reforged*. First off is FETUS51's Mono-Red Beatdown, played in the online PTQ.

[Goblin Rabblemaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20150217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Rabblemaster) has been replaced by [Flamewake Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20150217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flamewake+Phoenix) from *Fate Reforged*. Indeed, the Standard metagame's ground is flooding with creatures, but the skies are open.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Flamewake+Phoenix)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flamewake+Phoenix)

[Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20150217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc) can be a pain. However, it's two notches too slow for Mono-Red Beatdown's game plan anyway, so I recommend forgetting it altogether and attacking full speed with the Phoenix. Besides, Mono-Red is often done for by the time [Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20150217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc) hits the table anyway.

The Phoenix is also great because it can attack straight out of the graveyard. Even after falling to a [Lightning Strike](https://web.archive.org/web/20150217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Strike), the Phoenix can revive itself in the attack step with backup from [Dragon Mantle](https://web.archive.org/web/20150217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragon+Mantle) or [Titan's Strength](https://web.archive.org/web/20150217000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Titan%27s+Strength), which is a huge bonus. I can't wait to topdeck a haste creature and attack for 4 plus 2. That's what I call raid.
