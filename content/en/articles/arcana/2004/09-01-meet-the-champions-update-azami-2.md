Ready for another story about the heroes of Kamigawa? Check out the [*Champions of Kamigawa* site](https://web.archive.org/web/20040901000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/displayexpansion.asp?set=chk&page=1) for an update in the "Meet the Champions" area.

This week's story is "**[Everything](https://web.archive.org/web/20040901000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/chk/azami)**" by Gwendolyn Kestrel. It's the story of Azami, a woman so dedicated to the pursuit of knowledge that she was willing to sacrifice -- everything. Enjoy.
