At the StarCityGames.com Open Series in St. Louis this past weekend, an archetype that had fallen out of favor rose to the top: Mono-Red! Dennis Dowty cruised past a sea of Humans, Wolf Run, and Illusions on his way there. His secret? A full set of Volt Charge. Check out his build, and try it out for yourself!

Dennis Dowty's Red Deck Wins 

Standard – Winner, StarCityGames.com Open in St. Louis
