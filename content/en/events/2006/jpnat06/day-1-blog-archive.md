Asahara did manage to land a <a>Sensei's Divining Top</a> on turn one but he was discarding by the second slow <a>Boomerang</a>. He pitched <a>Snow-Covered Plains</a>.</p>&#10;      <p>Oiso wasted no time against the control deck and dropped a 2/2 <a>Magnivore</a>. <a>Azorius Signet</a> for Akira. Oiso grimaced at his hand - he was on four land with many <a>Wildfire</a>s - and decided to 'pump' his Vore with <a>Pyroclasm</a>.</p>&#10;      <p>&#10;        <a>Court Hussar</a> dug Ashara toward land but it was quickly <a>Demolish</a>ed. He found another in <a>Scrying Sheets</a> and regained some lost life with <a>Faith's Fetters</a>. Asahara had all the parts in place for an amazing card advantage engine but when Oiso played a fresh <a>Magnivore</a> he found himself under the gun. Still he had the Hussar to absorb a blow and had to wait one turn to get his <a>Counterbalance</a> going.</p>&#10;      <p>Oiso read the card, &quot;You may?&quot; He <a>Stone Rain</a>ed the Sheets and Ashara declined to activate. <a>Wrath of God</a> cleared the board. A sly grin formed at the edges of Oiso's mouth as he eyed the Mikokoro he was about the play and the <a>Counterbalance</a> that he was growing wary of. His good humor was tempered by the fact that Ashara had two Tops.</p>&#10;      <p>Asahara was well in control now as he topped into a new <a>Scrying Sheets</a>. Suddenly the control slipped as Oiso - who had been digging with his Mikokoro - played Boseiju. Asahara decided this was as good a time as any to cast <a>Enduring Ideal</a> for Zur's Weirding. Oiso's hand: Sleight, <a>Pyroclasm</a>, two <a>Wildfire</a>, and a land. Asahara's hand: Who cares he had already played <a>Enduring Ideal</a> and his kill cards were still in the deck. </p>&#10;      <p>&#10;        <a>Form of the Dragon</a> was in play a turn later. Oiso worked his Mikokoro - most likely just to see as much of Asahara's deck as possible - but the game was already over. </p>&#10;      <h3>Game 2</h3>&#10;      <div>&#10;        <div>&#10;          <a>&#10;            <img alt="Magnivore" src="http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&amp;type=card&amp;name=Magnivore&amp;options=">&#10;          </a>&#10;        </div>&#10;      </div>&#10;      <p>I did not get to see how Asahara sided exactly but while he was shuffling I did catch a glimpse of a spicy little number that I hoped would see some action in the remaining game(s). </p>&#10;      <p>&#10;        <a>Coldsteel Heart</a>, naming blue was the first play from Asahara. He followed up with <a>Counterbalance</a> but without Top he could only hope to synch up with his foe's spells through random chance. He missed on both <a>Stone Rain</a> and <a>Tidings</a> but a Court Husaar soon found him the infernal Champions artifact. Oiso winced and aimed <a>Demolish</a> at an <a>Island</a> - Asahara peeked but chose not to reveal. &quot;Yes,&quot; proclaimed Oiso, who still had nothing in play and was slowly being nibbled to death by the Hussar. </p>&#10;      <p>&#10;        <a>Shattering Spree</a> targeting the Heart and the Top with two copies aimed at the Heart. Akira drew a land with his <a>Scrying Sheets</a>, flipped his Top, and let things go at that. He had dealt with all of Ashara's blue sources once he cleared the Heart. <a>Sleight of Hand</a> was countered by the Top on top but <a>Eye of Nowhere</a> returned <a>Counterbalance</a> with no visible means of replaying it next turn. That's when our spicy number stepped in - Asahara Comandeered the Eye by pitching another Comandeer and Hussar to bounce one of Oiso's lands instead. </p>&#10;      <p>Oiso reolved <a>Tidings</a> on the next turn but he had thrown out his shoulder trying to hoist the <a>Counterbalance</a> the previous turn and could do nothing to stem the inevitability that seemed to be creeping into the game as Asahara's <a>Scrying Sheets</a> revealed a second copy of the powerful rare land. </p>&#10;      <p>&#10;        <a>Stone Rain</a> took down an <a>Island</a> - Asahara's only blue source - and, with threes obviously in the clear, Oiso dug deep with <a>Compulsive Research</a>. <a>Court Hussar</a> bought Asahara a fresh set of cards to Top with but he still could not find a two to deal with <a>Eye of Nowhere</a> on his enchantment. <a>Magnivore</a> came rushing in with no resistance save for the Courth Hussar the fell beneath its wheels. He took eleven from it on the following turn. Oiso stayed true to his game plan of denying Ashara blue mana and they were soon onto the rubber game.</p>&#10;      <h3>Game 3</h3>&#10;      <table>&#10;        <tbody><tr>&#10;          <td>&#10;            <div>&#10;              <img src="http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/jpnat06/R1_Asahara.jpg">&#10;            </div>&#10;            <div>&#10;              <i>Akira Asahara</i>&#10;            </div>&#10;          </td>&#10;        </tr>&#10;      </tbody></table>&#10;      <p>&#10;        <a>Boreal Shelf</a> for Asahara was followed by a <a>Pithing Needle</a> with a slip of paper that simply read 'Top'. <a>Stone Rain</a> took out <a>Scrying Sheets</a> and a second one took down the Shelf but not before Ashara revealed an ominous <a>Commandeer</a> on top of his deck. <a>Magnivore</a> revealed a second <a>Commandeer</a> - without the Top the Counterbalnce was pretty random. <a>Faith's Fetters</a> held the <a>Magnivore</a> at bay. Oiso attampet to <a>Eye of Nowhere</a> the <a>Magnivore</a> and - despite holding two <a>Commandeer</a> - Akira offered no resistance. Reanimation targets include the usual dragons, <a>Simic Sky Swallower</a>, and the Wrath-resistant <a>Protean Hulk</a>. </p>&#10;      <p>Iron Man Ken-ichi Fujita was playing the deck, as was everyone's favorite Masashiro Kuroda.
