Previously, we looked at Stanislav Cifka's [WR heroic beatdown deck](/en/articles/daily-deck/2014/10-02-rw-heroic-from-pro-tour-journey-into-nyx-2) during Block Constructed theme week; Yoshihiko Ikawa has made an impressive Standard version of this deck.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Obelisk%20of%20Urd)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Obelisk%20of%20Urd)

The two obviously bear a lot of similarities to one another, but they act in completely different ways. Ikawa's deck is hoping to get a first-turn [Akroan Crusader](https://web.archive.org/web/20141023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroan+Crusader), followed up by a second-turn [Raise the Alarm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raise+the+Alarm), and then drop [Obelisk of Urd](https://web.archive.org/web/20141023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Obelisk+of+Urd), naturally naming "Soldier." The next turn, playing [Launch the Fleet](https://web.archive.org/web/20141023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Launch+the+Fleet) leads to 18 points of damage (the Obelisk effect plus the heroic triggers plus the [Raise the Alarm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Raise+the+Alarm) tokens plus the Fleet triggers). On the fourth turn, drop a land, target the Crusader to make yet another token and deal 21 damage. Ikawa's version is closer to being a combo deck than a beatdown deck.

If it can get the Obelisk down, even mass removal only slows this deck down. With heroic triggers, [Akroan Crusader](https://web.archive.org/web/20141023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroan+Crusader) and [Vanguard of Brimaz](https://web.archive.org/web/20141023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vanguard+of+Brimaz) allow you to quickly beef up your forces and continue the fight.
