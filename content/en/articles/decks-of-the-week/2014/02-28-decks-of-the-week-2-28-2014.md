Congratulations to Shaun McLaren, [Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*](https://web.archive.org/web/20140228000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/mclaren-weaves-victory-valencia) champion! The Pro Tour was a Modern event and we'll be coming back to Modern in a couple weeks in GP Richmond, but for this weekend, we return to Standard with GP Melbourne (which also happens to be the first Australian Grand Prix with video coverage, so check it out!). Standard is also the format for Pro Tour *Journey into Nyx* Qualifiers, which you can find near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#jou). Good luck; have fun!
