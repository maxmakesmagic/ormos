Congratulations to Stefan Jedlicka, Champion of Grand Prix Prague!

The Top 8 contained such glittering name players as Anton Jonsson, Jelger Wiegersma, Gabriel Nassif, and Raphael Levy. The decks were also frighteningly good. Thomas Gunderson had [Visara](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207210636/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Visara) and two [Infest](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207210636/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Infest). Levy had three [Echo Tracer](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207210636/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Echo+Tracer) and two [Lavamancer's Skill](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207210636/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lavamancer%27s+Skill). Wiegersma had [Dragon Roost](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207210636/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragon+Roost) and [Akroma's Vengeance](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207210636/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Akroma%27s+Vengeance).

Yet through this the unfancied Austrian piloted a solid red-green deck with beats and burn to the final where he took Frenchman Raphael Levy down with a finely aimed [Erratic Explosion](https://web.archive.org/web/20211207210636/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Erratic+Explosion) to the head.

The Austrian takes home $2400 and now has a chance to travel to his first Pro Tour in Yokohama.
