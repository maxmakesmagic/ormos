*Worldwake* Game Day was a smashing success! For Colas Duarte, playing at Dima Game in Buenos Aires, Argentina, the event was an opportunity to use a Goblins deck to make the Top 8. The tribe is often seen in Extended and Legacy, but Colas proved they can be just as powerful in Standard! The thought of a Warren Instigator on the second turn feeding into double Siege-Gang Commander on the third is enough to terrify any **Magic** player. For more Standard decks, check out [all the *Worldwake* Game Day deck lists](/en/events/2010/worldwakegameday/worldwake-game-day).
