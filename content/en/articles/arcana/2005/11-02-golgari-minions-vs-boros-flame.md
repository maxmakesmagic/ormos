We've already seen the key to Guild Golgari's strategy in Ravnica: [plant zombies](/en/articles/arcana/2005/10-27-ravnica-style-guide-golgari) that never seem to die, elven beast-riders that do periodic corpse round-ups for recycling, elementals that taint what little woods Ravnica has left. 

![Woodwraith Strangler, Grave-Shell Scarab, Woodwraith Corrupter](https://i.ormos.uk/73/68/73682e7b833283d37d09afb2985278b3bda243c52b11d8e8314691ac20a3f968.jpg)

But what's the Boros gameplan to deal with such corrupt strategies? What do those justice-minded warriors, led by the fiery angel Razia, do when up against these face-painted bug-riders and rotting tree-freaks?

They burn. Burnnnnnnnn.

![Lightning Helix, Brightflame, Razia's Purification](https://i.ormos.uk/23/44/2344b0f9481b0db51ea070fcfeda9d32e723b8354d82d16b4c03be88447e1569.jpg)

Does it work? Well, see for yourself. Here's the enlarged art of **Brightflame**:

![Brightflame art by Dave Dorman](https://i.ormos.uk/48/34/4834706f3c1c8ac7c03786943e25c50c28430298e4ba9c158aec653591e084b6.jpg)

<table>&#10;<tbody><tr><td>&#10;<h3><i>&quot;Let the pyres of the unbelievers light our way.&quot; - Razia</i></h3>&#10;</td></tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

As you can see in the art, a Golgari elf insect-rider (foreground) and a plant zombie (just behind him, left of frame) are about to get toasted. And way back in the background, another elf and his insect mount are already being consumed by the purifying flames. Ouch. The flavor text (right) sums it up perfectly.
