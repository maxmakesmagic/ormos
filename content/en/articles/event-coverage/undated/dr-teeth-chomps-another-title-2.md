![](https://i.ormos.uk/d6/ec/d6ec9e380c586810f339e5aef9b0108e9d39c7224ad1806f8dea4f9fe2b76409.jpg)

In a weekend filled with sloppy Magic, questionable decisions, and few surprises, a [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog) player managed to snag another title running essentially the same deck that Antoine Ruel took to a Pro Tour victory last weekend. James Zhang, a 19-year-old university student from Sydney, just happened to be the pilot of said [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog) deck and to win he had to overcome Boros, Goblins, and Boros - relatively easy matchups for an experienced master of the 'Tog. Zhang's opponent in the finals was Paul Chalder, a Sydney amateur and one of two players with perfect records at the end of Day 1. Chalder blew Zhang out of the water in game 1 and nearly pulled off the upset in the finals before Zhang struck back in game 2 and Chalder ran out of time, conceding the final game in order to catch his flight.

Other storylines this weekend included Terry Soh also running the table on Saturday before sleeping through round 8 on Sunday morning and getting a match loss. He later took an intentional draw against Tomoharu Saito in round 11 only to have both pros finish 9th and 10th in the standings, shutting the Asian experts out of the elimination rounds. Congratulations go out to all of the great players and staff here in Australia and particularly to James Zhang, winner of Grand Prix-Melbourne 2005.

## BLOG

## Sunday, November 6: 7:07 p.m. - Quarterfinals: Sam Atkinson vs. Tim He

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3d/82/3d821d498d1f63ce0f48925952b4535ac75d8dfa2a5ddbdce01871f301ca4ad1.jpg)*Tim He*

Tim He was the 2004 Australian National Champion and managed to catch another glimpse of the spotlight this weekend by piloting his Affinity deck to the top seed coming out of the swiss. Sitting across from him is amateur Sam Atkinson, who snagged the last Top 8 berth available, keeping both Terry Soh and Tomoharu Saito on the outside looking in. Atkinson's deck is a very funky Macey Rockesque creation that features a host of equipment plus cards like [Loxodon Hierarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Hierarch) and [Iwamori of the Open Fist](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Iwamori+of+the+Open+Fist) plus various twos and three-ofs in combinations that only Adrian Sullivan could love.

Atkinson won the die roll and led off with [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress), snagging [Shrapnel Blast](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrapnel+Blast) out of He's hand. [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager) and [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter) on turn 2 for He gave some decent offense, but [Putrefy](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrefy) got rid of the electric bugaboo before he had too many resources to work with. Undaunted, He brought [Somber Hoverguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Somber+Hoverguard) into play and followed that on the next turn with another Ravager, while Atkinson gummed up the ground with daddy pachyderm [Loxodon Hierarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Loxodon+Hierarch). Unfortunately, the second Ravager, you know, ravaged and Atkinson couldn't find an answer, giving He the early 1-0 lead.

He 1 - Atkinson 0

Before the match, He looked at Atkinson's decklist and noted that Sam had 13 cards to bring in for this matchup, though Tim noted that he wasn't sure exactly what Sam might take out. Atkinson's brow furrowed as he pondered which cards he wanted to take out without losing too much of the aggression in his deck.

Atkinson began game 2 with a second-turn [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant) - a ballsy play against a deck as good at creating fast damage as Affinity. He showed his respect for Bob's card advantage, Firing The Great One during his own mainphase. Nonplussed, Atkinson treated game 2 like a Doritos commercial, suggesting Sam could crunch all he wanted, he'd just make more. The real worry was what He was making, casually dropping [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager), Worker, and a [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite) all on turn 3. Atkinson took a shock to the nug from Bob as TGO revealed [Umezawa's Jitte](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Umezawa%27s+Jitte) during his upkeep and then picked up the pointy stick to swing with it during the combat phase. This was clearly a mistake, since all He did was block with his proletariat, stack damage, and then sacrifice the little Worker to Ravager, making sure the Jitte earned no counters and Bob waved goodbye.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/81/e8/81e8459817f6e74ac080663bc365043bc33a15f79f6de4478def9fd6ec3935ed.jpg)*Sam Atkinson*

He added further robot beaters on his turn, tossing down the burly [Myr Enforcer](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Enforcer) and bashing Atkinson for five. [Putrefy](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrefy) made sure the Ravager went away, and then [Naturalize](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Naturalize) dissipated the [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite) danger, leaving He with a 4/4 on the board to Atkinson's [Birds](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds) plus a Jitte. He reloaded on his own turn, casting a [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite) and then [Thoughtcast](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtcast)ing into another, clearly running the dirty cheats. Oh wait, that's just the Affinity mechanic. Where's Kataki when you need him? Tragically for Atkinson, not in his deck.

Power pachyderm brought Atkinson back up to ten life and the elephant picked up the poking stick, looking to deal some damage and smash some Frogs. [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle) made that impossible, so the Hierarch traded with [Myr Enforcer](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Enforcer), adding two currently unusable counters to the Jitte. [Putrefy](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrefy) removed the Needle, but [Electrostatic Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrostatic+Bolt) zapped Atkinson's [Birds](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds) with the Jitte equip on the stack. He was now down to a single [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite) for offense, but Atkinson was sitting at a very precarious four. Atkinson dropped to three to cast [Troll Ascetic](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Troll+Ascetic) with enough mana up to regenerate it and drop to two, hoping to stabilize if he could just get an untap step. Tim cast an [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker) on his own turn and then [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle), which Atkinson chose not to respond to by regenerating, so He named Troll. Tim then attacked with his [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite) and Atkinson declared no blockers, opening the door for He to Bolt his [Arcbound Worker](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Worker) before damage, making it a 3/3 and putting Tim into the semifinals.

He 2 - Atkinson 0

## Sunday, November 6: 7:50 p.m. - Quarterfinals: David Zhao vs. Hugh Glanville

![Birds of Paradise](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Birds+of+Paradise)

Hugh's success so far this tournament has largely come from equipping the hard-to deal-with [Troll Ascetic](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Troll+Ascetic)s and introducing them to his opponent via the Red Zone. His plan against David was similar, drop dudes, give them toys, turn the sideways and wreck David's board.

David has piloted his goblins near flawlessly, with only one loss from the tournament coming from Tim He's Affinity.

Hugh was playing first and mulliganed a hand so poor he couldn't remember it. His six cards were more appealing, and he then allowed the very soft-spoken David to mulligan.

Hugh's first turn [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise) was answered only with a Skirk Prospecter, allowing 'Bill', one of Hugh's [Call of the Herd](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Call+of+the+Herd) tokens, to make an appearance. David sacked a [Wooded Foothills](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wooded+Foothills) to place an untapped [Sacred Foundry](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sacred+Foundry) into play, and introduced [Goblin Legionnaire](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Legionnaire), one of only 4 white cards in David's main deck.

On Hugh's turn, Bill went into the Red Zone and was met by David's team, which did damage-stacking shenanigans resulting in no men on either side. On David's turn he dropped a [Battlefield Forge](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Battlefield+Forge), and cast [Goblin Matron](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Matron), tutoring for the ubiquitous [Goblin Warchief](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Warchief). Hugh responded by calling 'Ted', Bill's comrade-in-arms, from the herd.

David merely dropped his Warchief and passed. Hugh attempted to capitalize on David's mana situation by [Putrefy](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Putrefy)ing the Warchief while swinging with Ted who was unopposed. David tried to gain some board control with his creature-removal-of-choice, [Sparksmith](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sparksmith). Hugh dropped a [Hypnotic Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hypnotic+Specter) which was dealt with when David's [Sparksmith](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sparksmith) came online. David's [Goblin Ringleader](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Ringleader) was quite advantageous, netting him a second [Sparksmith](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sparksmith), a [Goblin Matron](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Matron), and a mighty [Siege-Gang Commander](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege-Gang+Commander). Ted continued to swing on Hugh's behalf and was double-blocked by the Matron and Ringleader. David's Ring-led Matron found him another Warchief which was again Putrefied. David's second Ringleader was a total waste of time, merely milling three lands and a [Lava Dart](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lava+Dart). Hugh was feeling confident, having 11 life to David's 3, and dropped his MVP of the weekend, [Troll Ascetic](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Troll+Ascetic). A Piledriver and a [Goblin Legionnaire](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Legionnaire) tilted the scales in David's favour, but Hugh still had hope in the form of a Sword of [Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fire)-and-[Ice](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ice) which he asked his deck politely to give him. When he merely drew another land, he packed them up and headed for Game 2.

Both players had altered their lists slightly from the Pro Tour: LA equivalents, with Fleming forsaking [Force Spike](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+Spike) in exchange for a full compliment of [Opt](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Opt)s and [Smother](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smother) from his otherwise faithful U/B "RuelTog" with a modified sideboard. Chalder's R/W contained elements from various decks seen at LA, with 3 [Genju of the Spires](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genju+of+the+Spires) and 6 land destruction spells in the main deck.

## Sunday, November 6: 8:23 p.m. - Semifinals: Paul Chalder vs. Tim He

![](https://i.ormos.uk/21/61/21617929e11efd697c3f759ce251d67883a8ececd9a4e1824b4a59005ca1529f.jpg)*Paul Chalder*

Rumors of Affinity's demise are about as believable and frequent as the headlines in the Weekly World News. Sure, it's *possible* that someone gave birth to a 40-pound lizard baby last week, but until I see its scaly, blubberous body for myself, you can count me among the unbelievers. Tim He's particular robotic concoction would seem to validate this philosophy, as his fourteen-land Affinity deck with a heavy burn plus [Shivan Reef](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Reef) sideboard has torn up a metagame that still had a lot of hate for the deck. Sitting across from He is one of those decks, piloted by one of the Day 1 undefeateds, Paul Chalder. If there's one thing the Boros decks do well, it's smash Affinity, though with how well He is playing, a win for Chalder was far from a guarantee.

Chalder bolted himself with a [Wooded Foothills](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wooded+Foothills) into untapped [Sacred Foundry](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sacred+Foundry) just to cast [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer) and they were off. He's plays were actually irrelevant, since Chalder tossed down a [Barbarian Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barbarian+Ring) on turn 2 and cast [Kataki, War's Wage](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kataki%2C+War%27s+Wage), earning an immediate concession from He. Players everywhere can congratulate R&D for making this Extended season bearable. After six tries or so, they finally seem to have found an answer to the Affinity menace.

Chalder 1 - He 0

He led off game 2 with a cautious [Blinkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinkmoth+Nexus) and [Chromatic Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chromatic+Sphere). Chalder's play was to cast [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle) naming [Arcbound Ravager](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arcbound+Ravager). He's second turn was reasonably explosive with [Great Furnace](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Great+Furnace), [Welding Jar](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Welding+Jar), and [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite) all hitting the table in succession before He cast a [Thoughtcast](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtcast) to keep his grip full, giving him ammo against the dreaded turn 2 legendary spirit. Chalder once again cast exactly that, wiping He's board presence away minus the lands. Chalder made a minor mistake on his own upkeep, forgetting to pay for [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle), before Pillaging a [Blinkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinkmoth+Nexus), forcing the flaming side of [Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fire)/[Ice](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ice) out of He to dispatch the Saviors rare. He then cautiously rebuilt, playing a [Seat of the Synod](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seat+of+the+Synod) and casting [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating) plus another Sphere, while Chalder played draw, go for a bit, either glutted with land (doubtful) or stocked with removal that was merely waiting for a target (likely).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7c/89/7c89f168b480aeb37857bc4e050a718a74b9a682e8f6f72952abc3bf0710de4d.jpg)*Tim He*

He went large on his next turn, casting [Frogmite](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frogmite) and [Myr Enforcer](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Myr+Enforcer), robotic animals immediately turned into a pile of flaming parts by Chalder's burn spells. The game then turned cat and mouse, with each player holding removal to deal with threats and He very careful not to overextend. Chalder did glut on land in the midgame, but was able to maintain board parity with another [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle) (again on Ravager). Maybe, answered his deck, throwing out a rescue dog in Isamaru. More burn got He to use the Jar, but [Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fire) cleared the puppy out of the way, and a single swing did in Chalder, evening the match at one each.

Chalder 1 - He 1

He tossed his hand back three times before game 3 and then earned a full boot to the face from Chalder when he cast Isamaru, Kataki, and then [Pillage](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pillage) on He's only land. Chalder 2 - He 1

Paul Chalder is a 24-year-old amateur from Sydney who toils away in finance during the daytime. Hi bank account will be enriched considerably after this weekend, since Boros Deck Wins has been as good as its name for him, locking up top amateur status with his appearance in the finals and earning a chance at first prize to boot. To win it all, he'll need to defeat James Zhang and Dr. Teeth, a tough task under any circumstances, but one made more so by the fact that Chalder was nervously looking at his watch trying to figure out just how much time he had before he missed his flight.

Chalder won the die roll and immediately parised away his landless seven cards in search of terra firma. He found it with the six and immediately cast Isamaru, Hand of Konda. Zhang ended up stuck on two lands and then three for some time, but that meant hand was full of gas, and he managed to stave off the beatings and land destruction for a bit until Chalder decided to start activating the [Genju of the Spires](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genju+of+the+Spires) he cast way back on turn 2. Zhang had no answers to spirit rocks and died to a [Lightning Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Helix) when he tapped out looking for one. Chalder 1 - Zhang 0

Zhang again got a bit unlucky with his opener, taking a mulligan in search of good stuff and then once again stalling on mana sources. [Counter](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counter)magic and [Darkblast](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darkblast) kept down the early threats, but [Thirst for Knowledge](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thirst+for+Knowledge) yielded only an ironic [Seat of the Synod](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seat+of+the+Synod) as a mana source. Chalder made an interesting play after that, drawing a third land of his own and then casting [Molten Rain](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Molten+Rain) on [Stalking Stones](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stalking+Stones) instead of Zhang's newfound second blue mana source. Even Zhang seemed a little stunned by this, since he had to doublecheck the target of the spell before sending the Stones to the bin. Zhang ate four from [Violent Eruption](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Violent+Eruption) on Chalder's end step, figuring it an easy concession to be able to cast [Fact or Fiction](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fact+or+Fiction). Chalder started sneaking burn in whenever he could, rapidly dropping Zhang to eight while Zhang continued to draw cards, now in search of a clock. A second Fact gave him Dr. Teeth and a land to go along with five other cards in hand and suddenly any [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog) that might hit the board looked rather lethal, minus the fact that there was a [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle) sitting on Chalder's side of the board. Chalder flashed back a [Firebolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firebolt) at the 'Tog and Zhang had to let it die since he had no [Boomerang](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boomerang) in hand to allow him to protect it.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/91/b9/91b9b0bcb6f8a0699334b265140b876289d9a1d5f107add7480cf4beb6e15843.jpg)*James Zhang*

Chalder still lacked pressure when Zhang finally dug himself into Meloku, but the legend was immediately answered by [Pithing Needle](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pithing+Needle) naming the same. Zhang created three illusion tokens and then was so far ahead on cards that the Needle didn't matter. He simply maintained board control just long enough for the Clouded Mirror of Victory to take it home.

Chalder 1 - Zhang 1

At that point, Chalder looked at his watch and packed it so that he could catch his flight, conceding the title to James Zhang and giving [Psychatog](https://web.archive.org/web/20201022205203/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Psychatog) the first two premiere event victories of the new Extended format.

James Zhang is the winner of Grand Prix: Melbourne 2005!
