Welcome back to the Lab, comrades! It's *Modern Masters* week here on Daily MTG, and in honor of the set, today I'll be showing off a pair of combo decks that are Modern-legal. The popularity of the format has continued to grow over the last few years, and many stores are even holding [Friday Night **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=events/magic/fnm) events using the Modern format. Feel free to try out one of these decks at a local tournament or just use them against your friends. 

Yes Zur

[Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) is an odd little card that allows you and your opponent to control each other's draws. You can pay 2 life to stop your opponent from drawing any card, and with all hands revealed, you can determine exactly what to allow and what to pay 2 life for. 

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=2546)](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=2546)

  

Then again, why let your opponent have anything at all?

If you have two [Firemane Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firemane+Angel)s either on the battlefield or in your graveyard, you will gain 2 life every upkeep, allowing you to stop your opponent from drawing cards ever again. Eventually, your opponent will just die to one of the Angels, since you can return them to the battlefield from your graveyard for a mere ten mana. 

[Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) can be a difficult card to play correctly. You can't just slam it down as soon as you hit four mana every game. Since your opponent will be able to stop you from drawing cards as well, you need to be sure that the cards in your hand will deal with the cards in your opponent's hand, as well as anything on the field. 

One of the best tools for obtaining that certainty is [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict). It will destroy any creatures your opponent has on the battlefield, and it can't be stopped by counterspells. It usually provides a fair amount of card advantage and also stops your opponent from killing you, which is generally a good thing. 

[Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile) can be used early to keep you alive long enough to clear the board with a [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict), but it really shines afterward, where it can eliminate your opponent's last creature for only one mana.

[Lightning Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Helix) can be used similarly, and also gives you a bit of extra life to help keep aggressive strategies at bay, or to allow you to pay 2 for [Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) a few more times if you don't have two [Firemane Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firemane+Angel)s yet. 

[Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) buys you some time against faster decks and can be used to protect [Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) if you can wait until you have six mana. It's also a catch-all for making sure you can deal with the spells in your opponent's hand once [Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) has resolved.

[Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command) is an incredibly versatile spell that most often functions as [Dismiss](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dismiss) or [Lost in the Mist](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lost+in+the+Mist), but it can also tap all your opponent's creatures for a turn in combination with any of the other effects. 

[Forbidden Alchemy](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forbidden+Alchemy) and [Thirst for Knowledge](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thirst+for+Knowledge) can dig for [Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) at instant speed, allowing you to leave mana open for your counterspells. You can also put [Firemane Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Firemane+Angel) into your graveyard to start gaining your life. Note that once [Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) is on the battlefield, your opponent can pay 6 life to stop you from drawing any cards with [Thirst for Knowledge](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thirst+for+Knowledge), forcing you to simply discard two cards, so don't cast it unless that's worth it. [Forbidden Alchemy](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forbidden+Alchemy), on the other hand, bypasses [Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) entirely, putting the card straight into your hand.

[Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) also gives the cards put into the graveyard with [Thirst for Knowledge](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thirst+for+Knowledge) and [Forbidden Alchemy](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forbidden+Alchemy) additional value, letting you give flashback to whatever instant or sorcery you need. You can re-cast a [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) to counter a key spell, use [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile) to get rid of your opponent's last creature, or clear the board a second time with [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict).

Ad Nauseam, Subtract Life

[Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam) has been central to a number of combo decks since its printing. My first experience with the card was in a deck that used it to get to 2 or 3 life and draw a large number of cards, then cast multiple copies of [Death's Shadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death%27s+Shadow) along with a kicked [Goblin Bushwhacker](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Bushwhacker) to give them haste and attack for lethal in a single turn. 

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ad+Nauseam)](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam)

  

In this deck, however, we'll be going a bit further. By casting [Angel's Grace](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel%27s+Grace), you prevent yourself from losing the game for one turn. If you then cast [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam), you can put your entire deck into your hand. Even though your life total will be negative, you won't lose the game until the effect of [Angel's Grace](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel%27s+Grace) wears off. You just need to win before that happens. 

Although there are a number of ways to accomplish this, there are two that immediately come to mind. Both of them require three red mana, but [Simian Spirit Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simian+Spirit+Guide) can handle that. Since you'll have your entire deck in your hand, you can exile three Spirit Guides to get the three red mana you need immediately.

The first win condition is [Conflagrate](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Conflagrate). You can cast it with X as 0 to get it into the graveyard, then pay its flashback cost and discard fifty cards or so to deal that much damage to your opponent. The second option is [Lightning Storm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Storm). It's not as reliable as [Conflagrate](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Conflagrate), since if your opponent has gained life, you might not have enough lands to discard to finish him or her off. The upside, however, is that unlike [Conflagrate](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Conflagrate), it is an instant. Since [Angel's Grace](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel%27s+Grace) and [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam) are also instants, you can make the entire combo happen at any time, waiting until your opponent taps out or is about to kill you. Including one copy of each card gives you some flexibility and ensures that your opponent can't win just by getting rid of your [Lightning Storm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Storm) with [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek). 

[Phyrexian Unlife](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Unlife) can substitute for [Angel's Grace](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel%27s+Grace), and in some situations can do the job even better. Although it requires two more mana than [Angel's Grace](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel%27s+Grace), it can be cast in advance, allowing you to combo off with only five mana rather than the six needed to cast both spells. 

[Serum Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serum+Visions) and [Sleight of Hand](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sleight+of+Hand) help you find the mana and combo pieces you need to go off, and only require a single mana. [Peer Through Depths](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peer+Through+Depths) is more expensive but can dig deep to find [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam) or [Angel's Grace](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel%27s+Grace). [Mystical Teachings](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mystical+Teachings) will just grab whichever instant you need and put it in your hand with no fuss, although it charges a hefty four mana for the service.

[Pact of Negation](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pact+of+Negation) will stop anything that tries to mess with your combo, without charging you any mana. You won't even lose the game on your next upkeep, since if you don't win by the end of the turn you'll die from a severely negative life total anyway. 

[Lotus Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotus+Bloom) and [Pentad Prism](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pentad+Prism) give you extra mana with which to cast [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam) and win the game, usually on turn four or so. This acceleration really turns up the heat on an otherwise fairly slow combo.

[Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) played an Island and passed the turn. [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam) cast [Angel's Grace](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel%27s+Grace), then [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam). Zur hit it with a [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command), but [Pact of Negation](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pact+of+Negation) countered the counter, and [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam) drew its entire deck to win the game with [Conflagrate](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Conflagrate).

Game 3

[Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) played [Steam Vents](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steam+Vents) and passed the turn, and [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam) played [Scalding Tarn](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scalding+Tarn), searching up a [Hallowed Fountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallowed+Fountain), and suspended [Lotus Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotus+Bloom). [Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) fetched for a [Hallowed Fountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallowed+Fountain) as well, and [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam) played [Gemstone Mine](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gemstone+Mine) and cast [Pentad Prism](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pentad+Prism). [Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) played an Island and passed. 

[Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam) played another Mine and passed the turn, and [Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) cast [Forbidden Alchemy](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forbidden+Alchemy), getting [Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) and putting a Fireman Angel in the graveyard. [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam) cast [Peer Through Depths](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Peer+Through+Depths) while Zur was tapped out, finding [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam). [Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) paid 2 life for an untapped [Hallowed Fountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallowed+Fountain) and passed the turn. 

[Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam) cast [Lotus Bloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotus+Bloom) off suspend and passed the turn. [Zur's Weirding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zur%27s+Weirding) cast another [Forbidden Alchemy](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forbidden+Alchemy) during the end step, and [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam) responded with [Angel's Grace](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Angel%27s+Grace) and [Ad Nauseam](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ad+Nauseam), winning the game with [Lightning Storm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130527000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Storm) before the Alchemy ever resolved.
