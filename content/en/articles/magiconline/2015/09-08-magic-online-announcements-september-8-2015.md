***Magic Online* *Magic Origins* Championship Winners**

Hundreds of players fought their way through the qualifiers leading up to the *Magic Origins* Championship Finals. Sparks were ignited left and right along the way but, when the smoke cleared, only sixteen Planeswalkers were ready to compete for the title of *Magic Online Magic Origins* Champion.

The final matches came down to Robotique and nyord in the [*Magic Origins* Limited Championship Finals](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/09-07-magic-online-magic-origins-championship-winners#limited), and ryota09 and Sneaky homunkulus in the [*Magic Origins* Standard Championship Finals](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/09-07-magic-online-magic-origins-championship-winners#standard). How did the victors triumph over their competition? Read the [full article](/en/articles/magiconline/2015/09-07-magic-online-magic-origins-championship-winners) to see how each match unfolded.

Who will emerge as the Super League grand champion? Find out by watching along!

***Magic Online* R&D Challenge**

The time has come once again to rumble with the folks in R&D and earn the sweetest prize of all: bragging rights. That's right, another *Magic Online* R&D Challenge is coming up on Friday, September 18, at 12:00 p.m. Pacific (UTC -7). Aside from satisfying clacks on their victory abacuses, R&D competitors will not be eligible to win prizes—their matches will only affect standings. **Community Cup Stakes**

The Community Cup [is coming up](/en/articles/feature/2015/08-12-2015-community-cup) on September 19!

In long-standing Community Cup tradition, all *Magic Online* players will receive a prize at the event's conclusion—the catch is that it will be different depending on which team wins!

If the Wizards Team wins (which they totally will), players who logged in during a specific time frame will be getting a non-premium [Drown in Sorrow](https://web.archive.org/web/20150908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drown+in+Sorrow).

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&multiverseid=378437)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=378437)

*If* the Community Team wins, players will get one-each, premium and non-premium, of the John Avon *Unhinged* basic lands, plus a premium and non-premium version of [Thalia, Guardian of Thraben](https://web.archive.org/web/20150908000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thalia%2C+Guardian+of+Thraben)!

**There Will Be No Downtime for September 9, 2015**

During the past two weeks, we have experienced issues when returning from downtime. We are currently troubleshooting the root cause of these issues and, to avoid further negative experiences for our players, we will not be having our usual Wednesday, September 9, downtime. Because of this, **redemption will be postponed until our next downtime**.

We’ll update players with our downtime plans for September 16 via the *Magic Online* Home Scene, the Weekly Announcements for Tuesday, September 15, and on the [*Magic Online* Twitter account](https://twitter.com/MagicOnline).
