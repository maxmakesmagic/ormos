**What is the most overrated card in the format?** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/QQ-Marijn-Lybaert.jpg)

 *Marijn Lybaert: Probably [Skullcrack](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullcrack). I just don't like it. When I have been playing burn it has simply been bad. You would think it's good at least in the mirror to respond to your opponents [Warleader's Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warleader%27s+Helix), but that never really happens.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/QQ-Alessandro-Portaro.jpg)

 *Alessandro Portaro: [Sylvan Caryatid](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Caryatid). I've never liked it. And against [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) it's a 2-mana do nothing card.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/QQ-Tomoharu-Saito.jpg)

 *Tomoharu Saito: [Divination](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Divination). You can't afford to play it on turn three. And in the late game just drawing one extra card isn't good enough.* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/QQ-Lee-Shi-Tian.jpg)

 *Lee Shi Tian: [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat).* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpmos14/QQ-Patrick-Dickmann.jpg)

 *Patrick Dickmann: [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat), it's got worse with the release of [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight).*
