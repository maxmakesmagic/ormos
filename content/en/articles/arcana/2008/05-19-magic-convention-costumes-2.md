Long ago, when Wizards of the Coast was young, it was harder to drum up excitement at conventions. After all, there are only so many times you can release *Legends*. That's why the convention staff used to dress up in **Magic**-themed costumes. Would you care to see a couple of pictures?

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/magic/images/mtgcom/arcana1000/1593_costumes.jpg)

Back row: [Order of the Ebon Hand](https://web.archive.org/web/20080519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Order+of+the+Ebon+Hand) (Christopher Rush version), [Hurloon Minotaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20080519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hurloon+Minotaur), [Natural Selection](https://web.archive.org/web/20080519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Natural+Selection), Alpha [Prodigal Sorcerer](https://web.archive.org/web/20080519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prodigal+Sorcerer) (possibly holding an Alpha [Icy Manipulator](https://web.archive.org/web/20080519000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Icy%2BManipulator)). Front row: [Counterspell](https://web.archive.org/web/20080519000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Counterspell), [Bird Maiden](https://web.archive.org/web/20080519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bird+Maiden), [Benalish Hero](https://web.archive.org/web/20080519000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Benalish+Hero)
