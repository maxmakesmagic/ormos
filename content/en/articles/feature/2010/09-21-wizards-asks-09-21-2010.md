*What card from the [Visual Spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20140209055955/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/scarsofmirrodin/spoiler) are you most hoping to open at the Prerelease?*

**Ken Nagle:** I'm most excited to open Argentum Armor, mostly because it's my submitted design.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/tcg/products/scarsofmirrodin/9j39nwg1ye_en.jpg)  

To me, nothing is more "Mirrodin" than Equipment, and I felt *Scars of Mirrodin* needed the "biggest Equipment ever," both for my [Godo, Bandit Warlord](https://web.archive.org/web/20100921000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Godo%252C%2BBandit%2BWarlord) EDH deck and for shenanigans with [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20100921000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic) / [Stonehewer Giant](https://web.archive.org/web/20100921000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stonehewer+Giant) (again, my designs).
