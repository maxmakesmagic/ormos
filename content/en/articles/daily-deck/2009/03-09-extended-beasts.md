Beasts aren't exactly a tribe known for the prowess in the Extended format, but Corbett Gray didn't let that stop him from Top 8ing a recent PTQ with the monsters. He took his deck all the way to the finals on the back of powerhouse creatures like Chameleon Colossus and Ravenous Baloth as well as some sneaky hits like Taurean Mauler. Can the deck remain good throughout the rest of the PTQ-Honolulu season? The world waits with baited breath!
