Relative newcomer Justin Robb overcame a field of more than 450 Modern players to win [Grand Prix Brisbane](/en/events/2013/gpbri13/justin-robb-triumphant-in-brisbane). Across the Pacific, Pierre-Christophe Mondon won the first Grand Prix using *Theros* Limited in [Oklahoma City](/en/events/2013/gpokc13/mondon-devoted-to-victory-in-okc). Congratulations, Justin and Pierre-Christophe! And speaking of *Theros* Limited, that is not only the format for this season's PTQs, but it's also on display this weekend at [Pro Tour *Theros*](/en/events/2013/ptths13/dezani-proclaims-allez-les-bleus-at-pro-tour). It's too late to get into that Pro Tour, but you can still qualify for the next one. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#bog)! Good luck; have fun!
