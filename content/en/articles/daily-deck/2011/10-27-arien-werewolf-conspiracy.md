Over on TCGPlayer.com, **Magic** writer Cassidy Silver [brewed up](http://magic.tcgplayer.com/db/article.asp?ID=10078) a red-black-green Commander deck designed to show off the Werewolves and other monsters that populate *Innistrad*. And who better to lead the masquerade than costume-loving commander Xira Arien?

Cassidy Silver's Arien's Werewolf Conspiracy
