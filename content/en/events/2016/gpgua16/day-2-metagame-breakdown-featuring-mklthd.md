Going solo at a Grand Prix isn't always easy. It gets a little lonely. Sometimes, I hallucinate. In those times, I enjoy replying to the imaginary voices in my head. This weekend, it's just me and Mklthd.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Jeering+Homunculus)](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeering+Homunculus)

289 decklists isn't that many, especially when I've got a jeering homunculus here to help me out. Enough chatter. With three Modern Grand Prix taking place this weekend, we've got no time to waste.

**Chapman:** So, just like we suspected. Usual suspects such as Jund, Infect, Affinity, Bant Eldrazi, Tron and Burn are all pretty well-represented. The Modern metagame is still diverse as ever, with a total of 47 archetypes represented in Day 2.

**Mklthd:** So, you forced me to look through all these sheets of paper for nothing?

**Chapman:** Not really. We found that 4 Jund players have opted to play [Grim Flayer](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Flayer) over [Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant). The singleton [Sylvan Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Scrying) in Infect is catching on, as Tomoharu Saito demonstrates. Affinity is same-old, same-old I guess. I mean, it's always been a solid choice.

**Mklthd:** I don't care.

**Chapman:** You should! It's interesting because Bant Eldrazi has also risen in numbers over the past few months even after [Eye of Ugin](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eye+of+Ugin) got banned. R/G Tron is now a great tool to beat other midrange decks by going over the top. Naya Burn is also very popular, but I've also learnt that [Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl) have largely fallen out of favor, showing up only in [Death's Shadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death%27s+Shadow) Aggro lists.

**Mklthd:** I still don't care.

**Chapman:** What if I told you that [Scapeshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scapeshift) is making a huge comeback? There were 11 copies of 4-color [Scapeshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scapeshift) that uses [Bring to Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bring+to+Light) as a tutor. Titan Shift is also doing surprisingly well, because you can also use [Through the Breach](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Through+the+Breach) to cheat in [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan). And of course, there's also 5 traditional builds, making it a total of 26 players running [Scapeshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20160828000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scapeshift) in Day 2.

**Mklthd:** Do you have cookies? I want cookies.

**Chapman:** No, I don't have cookies because you ate them all. You don't care about Modern, do you?

**Mklthd:** I'm not even legal in Modern. What's '*others*' anyway?

**Chapman:** It's basically any archetype with three or less copies in Day 2. There are a lot of interesting decks, but for convenience, we generally just clump them all together.

**Mklthd:** So, is that how you guys usually do things around here? Just hunches and guesses and stuff.

**Chapman:** It's not like that. Most of our viewers just wanna see what the Top 5 or Top 10 decks are. That provides a quick overview of the weekend. Besides, we'll be posting the top decklists later on.

**Mklthd:** So, you're just being lazy.

**Chapman:** No, I'm not. I looked through everything and sorted everything out. You were the one pile-shuffling all the decklists while I wasn't looking.

**Mklthd:** You complain about everything. 'Stop making confetti.' 'Don't eat my decklists.'

**Chapman:** Seriously?

**Mklthd:** I'm a goader. What do you expect?

**Chapman:** Ok, just to get you off my back. Here's the complete data.

**Mklthd:** I don't care. Find someone who does. Too many people in this room. I'm outta here.
