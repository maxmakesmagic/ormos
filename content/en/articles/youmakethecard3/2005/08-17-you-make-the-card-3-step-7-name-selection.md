The next step is to give this little card a name. And [last time we spoke](/en/articles/youmakethecard3/2005/07-11-you-make-the-card-3-step-6-name-submission), I asked all of you to submit names that tied all the elements of the card together. After poring through over *four thousand* entries we ended up with the following ten choices (hand selected by **Magic**’s very own Creative Team).
