This weekend millions of players across the globe will be tearing open brand new booster packs from the new Ravnica: City of Guilds expansion. In Russia things are a little different. Over here we have the 9th Edition release. This is not just any old release. This tournament marks the first expansion of Magic to be released using the Cyrillic alphabet. To commemorate the occasion sixteen of the World's best players have been invited over to take on the best that Russia, Ukraine and Belarus can offer in an exhibition tournament. For the full player list and profiles see here.

In the end the Local Heroes were not enough to stand up to the might of the Best of the Rest. The Best ran out 129-95 winners. But there was still the matter of the rotisserie draft for the top four players of each team.

Julien Nuijten was the top ranked player from Saturday and had the honor of taking the first pick in the Rotisserie draft. This draft featured every card in 9th Edition, in foil. Unsurprisingly, the World Champion took Loxodon Warhammer. It was not enough to save him from Antoine Ruel's clever, almost creature-less, blue-red deck in the semi-finals.

On the other side of the bracket Matvey Linov, perhaps better known as DuSheLov from Magic Online, was dismantling his opponents with an almost mono-blue deck. In the end it was the Russian player who caused the upset in the finals. His Mahamoti Djinn proved to be too tough to kill for the experienced French pro. The Local Heroes team might have been defeated by the Best of the Rest, but they were able to salvage some pride as Matvey Linov came out on top of the Rotisserie draft.

Overall the event was a massive success with many local players showing up to play in the 9th Edition release or to test their skills against the pro's on the gunslinging tables.
