All is quiet on the Mirrodin Site this week, so we'll take this opportunity to direct your attention once again to the [*New Phyrexia* Product Section](https://web.archive.org/web/20110901234902/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/products.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/products/newphyrexia)!

What's in the Product Section, you ask? There's the Product Information page, including collector's highlights, design and development teams, and more. There's also the Mechanics article, which demonstrates the new and returning mechanics of *New Phyrexia* via a number of card previews. The Prerelease Fact Page has a full suite of tools to help you find a Prerelease event going on near you on May 7–8. And the Card Image Gallery contains all of the cards that have been officially previewed to date.
