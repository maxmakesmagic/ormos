**Episode 176**: Tales from Europe (23.5 MB)

This podcast is a spiritual sequel to a podcast called "[Tales from the Boat](https://web.archive.org/web/20141225162322/http://www.wizards.com/drivetoworkpodcast.xml)," where I shared some Pro Tour stories from the early days. This podcast focuses on a few early Pro Tours held in Europe.

*Magic: The Gathering* Head Designer Mark Rosewater shares stories, insights, and more while driving to work. Listen in and learn more about *Magic*!
