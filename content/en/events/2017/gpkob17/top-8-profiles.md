Learn more about Grand Prix Kobe's Top 8 competitors—including who they are, why they chose their Modern deck, and more—by taking a look through the Top 8 profiles!

* * *

### Joe Soh

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpkob17/gpKobe2017_TOP8_8_soh_EN.jpg)

**Age:** 29

**Hometown:** Malaysia

**Occupation:** Self Employed

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Grand Prix Taipei 2014 Top 8, Grand Prix Kobe 2015 finalist, 2003 and 2009 Malaysia National Champion.

**What deck did you play this weekend and how do you feel about your choice?**

White-Black Eldrazi. I liked Eldrazi but I didn't like the Eldrazi decks in the format, so I brewed my own.

**What is your favorite card in Modern?**

[Wasteland Strangler](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland+Strangler) and [Thought-Knot Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thought-Knot+Seer)

**What was the most interesting memory this weekend?**

Turn 2 [Tidehollow Sculler](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tidehollow+Sculler)s into turn 3 [Wasteland Strangler](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland+Strangler) has surprised many people, as well as the maindeck [Relic of Progenitus](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Relic+of+Progenitus).

### Park Bi-o

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpkob17/gpKobe2017_TOP8_4_park_EN.jpg)

**Age:** 34

**Hometown:** South Korea

**Occupation:** Engineer
