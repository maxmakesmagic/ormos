All of the *Coldsnap* Martyrs were concepted as spellcasters blowing dust from their palms into magical energy. The transforming dust represents the card’s activated ability going off, giving a visual cue to the similarities of the mechanical cycle. Each Martyr was illustrated by a different artist: Randy Gallegos, Wayne England, Ralph Horsley, E.M. Gist, and Dan Scott.
