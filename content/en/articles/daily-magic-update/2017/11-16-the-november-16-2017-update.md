Today's Update is brought to you by [Half-Kitten, Half-Kitten, or the more egregious Ninja Constrictor](https://web.archive.org/web/20171116000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/unstable#jumblemorph).

# What to Watch Tomorrow

1.  Wizards of the Coast *Unstable* preview stream | [twitch.tv/magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic) | 1 p.m. PT/4 p.m. ET/8 p.m. UTC

Join myself, Trick Jarrett, and *Unstable* lead developer Ben Hayes as we preview somewhere between one and twelve cards on a stream tomorrow. We're not even sure how many we'll be previewing ourselves, as things might get a little—dicey.
