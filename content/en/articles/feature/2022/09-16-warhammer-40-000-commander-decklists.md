You can check out the cards from the *Warhammer 40,000* Commander decks in the [Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2022/09-16-warhammer-40-000-commander-decks-card-image-gallery). Note that the Collector's Edition versions of *Warhammer 40,000* Commander decks are English-only and include surge foil versions of cards and tokens.

![Surge foil Abaddon the Despoiler](https://media.wizards.com/2022/images/daily/7YwyDbwDrr.jpg)

Look for the *Warhammer 40,000* Commander decks at [your local game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20220916000000/https://locator.wizards.com/), [online through Amazon](https://gtly.to/PPb9Kv0es), and everywhere else you buy your *Magic: The Gathering* products.

***(Editor's Note: The decklists below pull the newest printing of each card automatically from*** [***Gatherer***](https://web.archive.org/web/20220916000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Default.aspx)***, including printings which are not in this product. Decklists are not card-for-card product displays but rather interactive lists of the cards included in each deck. Additionally, card images for new* Warhammer 40,000 *Commander deck cards will appear in decklists at a later time.)***
