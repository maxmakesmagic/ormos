Rich Hagon and Brian David-Marshall run through the major lessons learned at [Pro Tour *Journey into Nyx*](https://web.archive.org/web/20140529000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/ptjou14/welcome). They spotlight the top *Theros* Block cards that you can expect to see making their way into Standard, run through the key changes to the full-block draft environment with Marshall Sutcliffe, and honor long-time pro Patrick Chapin on his first Pro Tour victory.
