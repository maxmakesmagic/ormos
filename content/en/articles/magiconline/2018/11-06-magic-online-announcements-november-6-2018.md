## Last Day of *Innistrad* Flashback Draft

Tomorrow, November 7, is your last day to get in on the spooky action of triple *Innistrad* Flashback Draft. Enjoy frightful fights and the creepiest of creatures while you still can!

* * *

## *Core Set* *Cube*

The next event in the Cube Spotlight Series is almost upon us! A full week of *Core Set Cube* draft starts on November 7. This Cube features iconic cards from every core set on *Magic Online*, from *Seventh Edition* to *Core Set 2019*. Cube designer Kyle O'Neill has more on the cube in his article, available [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2018/10-30-cube-spotlight-series-core-set-cube)!

* * *

## PTQ Saturday and Sunday

There will be a Standard PTQ this Saturday at 9 a.m. PT. Then on Sunday, there will be a Modern PTQ at 9 a.m. PT. Each event costs 30 Event Tickets or 300 Play Points.

Learn more about *Magic Online* Pro Tour Qualifiers [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20181106000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-pro-tour-qualifiers).

## Downtime December 5

The next downtime will be December 5, 3 a.m.–noon PT/-7 from UTC. Please note that there are no more 'extended' or 'normal' downtimes; in the new world with fewer downtimes, they're all the same length of time.

## Build Notes

General | [Devour Flesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20181106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devour+Flesh) and [Geth's Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20181106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geth%27s+Verdict) work as intended.

Play Lobby | A visual issue with avatars has been fixed.

Modern | [Aven Mindcensor](https://web.archive.org/web/20181106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aven+Mindcensor) no longer censors [Archive Trap](https://web.archive.org/web/20181106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archive+Trap)'s alternative cost.

Limited | Both skills of [Lavamancer's Skill](https://web.archive.org/web/20181106000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lavamancer%27s+Skill) can now be skillfully activated.
