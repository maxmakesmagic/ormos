# Deck of the Day

1.  [Reiderrabbit's Legacy Elves](/en/events/2017/competitive-legacy-constructed-league-2017-06-13/competitive-legacy-constructed-league)

Fans will recognize the Reiderrabbit moniker, but it's the deck that's most striking. In the world where Miracles was the top deck, Elves struggled in a field of one-mana [Terminus](https://web.archive.org/web/20170614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Terminus) and [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20170614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance)/Top that could lock out virtually the entire deck. But with [Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20170614000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top) gone, the Elves are free to roam Legacy again.

Could this be one of the big decks that comes out of Las Vegas? Tune in to [twitch.tv/magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic) starting tomorrow to find out!
