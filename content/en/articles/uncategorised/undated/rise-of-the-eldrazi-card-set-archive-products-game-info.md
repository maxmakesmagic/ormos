## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ae/1b/ae1b6c2d0f19e15feb5e614f7fc886a9e04cd24f2020df95243fe730ab3d1bf8.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes player’s guide, card box with panoramic art, eight 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, special-edition life counter, and an excerpt from the novel In the *Teeth of Akoum*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a2/97/a2972e644f5271c2808dd85c92158fd4a238dc6c3728a7928415277ed303b29e.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/23/2d/232d1571e102679b0f2653d0c1cf5f88bc91370fb9b44974ac4741fae8f13a83.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes a 41-card preconstructed deck, a rare foil card, a strategy insert, a learn-to-play insert, and one 15-card *Rise of the Eldrazi* booster pack.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719051013/http://magic.wizards.com/node/961096)

(\* Not available in all languages)

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/2a/bc/2abcdf19e35747bac299b6fc4ba041eb4a36ac54e7b07843b9d9e03fdc50cadc.png)This set features 248 black-bordered cards and marks the debut of colorless non-artifact creatures, instants, and sorceries, which can complement almost any deck.

**Set Name:** *Rise of the Eldrazi*  
**Block:** Set 3 of 3 of the *Zendikar* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 248

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish

**Official Three-Letter Code:** ROE  
**Twitter Hashtag:** #MTGROE

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719051013/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/ROE_FAQ_20100407_EN.rtf)

**Initial Concept and Game Design:** Brian Tinsman (lead), Aaron Forsythe, Graeme Hopkins, Devin Low, Gregory Marques, and Bill McQuillan

**Final Game Design and Development:** Matt Place (lead), Mark Globus, Erik Lauer, Gregory Marques, and Mark Rosewater, with contributions from Mark L. Gottlieb
