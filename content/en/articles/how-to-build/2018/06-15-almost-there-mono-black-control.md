Back when *Torment* was released in 2002, Mono-Black Control was one of my favorite archetypes. Since then, a lot of people have tried to revive it, but they have been mostly unsuccessful because without cards like [Nantuko Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Shade), [Mind Sludge](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind+Sludge), [Cabal Coffers](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Coffers), and [Mutilate](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutilate), there was never a reason to be mono-black as opposed to black and another color. With the printing of [Dread Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dread+Shade) and [Cabal Stronghold](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Stronghold), it may finally be the time for mono-black to make a comeback. This is the deck Hall of Fame pro Willy Edel played to a 7-3 record in the Standard rounds at [Pro Tour *Dominaria*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-dominaria):

Ironically enough, there are no copies of [Cabal Stronghold](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Stronghold) in Willy's deck—though I imagine it started with them in earlier versions. Once you look at what cards are in the deck, however, it makes perfect sense. There aren't a lot of mana sinks (only [Walking Ballista](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Walking+Ballista)), so using [Cabal Stronghold](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Stronghold) to jump from five to seven mana doesn't do much for you, and the requirement of using only basic Swamps is pretty steep because the black utility lands are amazing. [Ifnir Deadlands](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ifnir+Deadlands) is a Standard staple that lets you exchange a land for a removal spell, and [Memorial to Folly](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memorial+to+Folly) rebuys any of the many creatures that have spell-like abilities, such as [Gonti, Lord of Luxury](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gonti%2C+Lord+of+Luxury); [Walking Ballista](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Walking+Ballista); or [Noxious Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noxious+Gearhulk). The other Deserts aren't really necessary, but they're mostly free and can be sacrificed for [Ifnir Deadlands](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ifnir+Deadlands) as well—plus, they can be very handy if you get paired against [God-Pharaoh's Gift](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=God-Pharaoh%27s+Gift) or [The Scarab God](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Scarab+God), on top of randomly exiling [Earthshaker Khenra](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Earthshaker+Khenra)s and [Scrapheap Scrounger](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scrapheap+Scrounger)s. Because the utility lands are so powerful, this deck can play 26 lands without risk of flooding out. I believe there can be a version of mono-black that uses [Cabal Stronghold](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Stronghold) in combination with [Karn, Scion of Urza](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn%2C+Scion+of+Urza) to make all its land drops, and then uses those to fuel big [Walking Ballista](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Walking+Ballista)s and [Josu Vess, Lich Knight](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Josu+Vess%2C+Lich+Knight), but that's a very different build.

The thing I like about this deck is that a lot of the creatures play double duty. Gonti is an excellent blocker that also steals a card from your opponent, which makes it great versus control. [Gifted Aetherborn](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gifted+Aetherborn) trades with any green creature while helping you stay alive versus red. [Noxious Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noxious+Gearhulk) kills something and gains life. [Glint-Sleeve Siphoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint-Sleeve+Siphoner) draws cards. [Knight of Malice](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+Malice) blocks [Scrapheap Scrounger](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scrapheap+Scrounger) every turn and also threatens [Teferi, Hero of Dominaria](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%2C+Hero+of+Dominaria). [Dread Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dread+Shade) brick-walls everything and then kills them in three turns. Having so many utility creatures is great when you have the ability to rebuy them through [Liliana, Death's Majesty](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana%2C+Death%27s+Majesty) and [Memorial to Folly](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memorial+to+Folly), and it's not uncommon to cast three or four Gontis in a game, for example.

[Dread Shade](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dread+Shade) | Art by: [G-host Lee](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&output=spoiler&method=visual&artist=+%5BG-host%5D+%5BLee%5D)

Right now, this deck is tuned to beat control more than it is tuned to beat red, and I think we have to change this. By this point, it's pretty clear that mono-red and black-red are the decks to beat, which means we can't afford having a card like [Glint-Sleeve Siphoner](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint-Sleeve+Siphoner) in the main deck since it just incidentally dies to [Goblin Chainwhirler](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Chainwhirler).

To replace them, I think we should just play more removal and cheap creatures. The deck is already maxing on [Vraska's Contempt](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vraska%27s+Contempt)s and [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push)es, but it only plays one [Cast Down](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cast+Down) right now. [Cast Down](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cast+Down) is also very good against mono-green (it kills anything you need to kill except [Ghalta, Primal Hunger](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghalta%2C+Primal+Hunger)) as well as Black-Green Constrictor. You can also add an extra [Knight of Malice](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Knight+of+Malice), which is a much better blocker versus red as well as being a big nuisance against the white-blue decks. If you resolve one against white-blue (which isn't hard, since it costs only two mana), they can't kill it with [Seal Away](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seal+Away) or [Cast Out](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cast+Out) and they can't stop it with [Gideon of the Trials](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon+of+the+Trials) or [Teferi, Hero of Dominaria](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Teferi%2C+Hero+of+Dominaria), which means they're basically left with [Settle the Wreckage](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Settle+the+Wreckage), [Fumigate](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fumigate), or [Torrential Gearhulk](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Torrential+Gearhulk). It's not going to kill them single-handedly, but it makes sure their planeswalkers won't dominate the game.

I'd also add a couple of [The Eldest Reborn](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Eldest+Reborn) in the sideboard, which is extremely powerful against white-blue since it kills a planeswalker and then gives you that planeswalker two turns later, while also being insurance against [Lyra Dawnbringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20180615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lyra+Dawnbringer).
