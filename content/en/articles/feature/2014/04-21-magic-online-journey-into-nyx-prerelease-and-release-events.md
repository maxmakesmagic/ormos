Magic Online *Journey into Nyx*  
Prerelease and Release Key Dates 

**Spotlight Early Access Begins:** 10AM May 8 

**Prerelease Begins:** 10AM May 9  
**On-Sale:** 10AM May 12  
**Release Events End:** 12PM May 28  
**Redemption Guarantee:** October 31, 2015  
**Redemption Cutoff:** November 2, 2016 

All times are PDT (for UTC, add 7 hours) 

Rival the Gods! 

Theros has become a battlefield. The gods have sent their forces against the mortals to punish the hubris of a few. But the mortals have risen up in defiance, and the time has come when you will be tested against a god. 

[*Journey into Nyx*](https://web.archive.org/web/20140416041816/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/productarticle.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/journeyintonyx/productinfo) Prerelease Events begin Friday, May 9. The set officially goes on sale in **Magic Online** and Release Events begin on Monday, May 12. 

There is a special perk for you with this set: Participants in the Wide Beta Spotlight will gain early access to Prerelease Events, beginning Thursday, May 8, at 10:00 AM PDT!

[Our Wide Beta Spotlight is a special event](/en/articles/feature/2014/04-14-wide-beta-update-spring-fever) in which we put the client through its paces with the full **Magic Online** community of players. Following the downtime on Wednesday, May 7, and continuing until the official start of Prerelease Events at 10:00 AM PDT on Friday, May 9, the current client will be disabled. During this Spotlight period, you are encouraged to play using the [Wide Beta Client](https://web.archive.org/web/20140421000000/http://mtgoclientdepot.onlinegaming.wizards.com/setup.exe). As an added bonus to participating in the Spotlight, we're going to let you into Prerelease Events a full day early. You can find Wide Beta Spotlight details in [last week's Wide Beta Update](/en/articles/feature/2014/04-14-wide-beta-update-spring-fever) by Chris Kiritz. 

**The Wide Beta Spotlight ends with the official start of Prerelease Events at 10:00 AM PDT on Friday, May 9, when both the current client and the Wide Beta Client will be usable as normal.** 

[Download the **Magic Online** Wide Beta Client Now](https://web.archive.org/web/20140421000000/http://mtgoclientdepot.onlinegaming.wizards.com/setup.exe) 

In *Journey into Nyx,* you will forge your legacy as a hero, challenging the gods themselves! [Purchase one of five entry objects](#promos) that reflect your **Magic** color preference from the **Magic Online** store for $5.00 starting Wednesday, May 7, following downtime. These objects grant access to Prerelease Events, plus contain one of the five *Journey into Nyx* [Prerelease promo cards](#promos). 

For Prerelease, we've added two new events we think players will enjoy. The [9-4 Single-Elimination Draft](#pre94draft) provides those who like the more competitive experience of single-elimination play with a Prerelease option to challenge their skills. The [*Journey into Nyx*-ONLY Prerelease Swiss Draft](#prejouonlydraft) gives you the pure *Journey into Nyx* experience of three *Journey into Nyx* packs for drafting. 

We've also made a few small changes to the Premier Swiss Sealed event. Most importantly, we've adjusted the player size to 65-128, which should ensure more events reach capacity and fire, and keep game length manageable. 

![Sage of Hours](https://i.ormos.uk/ee/b8/eeb80cc55895113a838e91deca9a63eb927326d383796a8045a7c2bc1443aa73.jpg) 

You can earn the avatars you want simply by [participating in the events that award them](#avatars). Collect only the ones you want, or collect them all. 

Happy Playing!
