For many players, Team Deadguy member Chris Pikula will always be the Meddling Mage. That's because after winning the Invitational, the 2/2 is the card Chris decided to design as his prize. The nickname didn't stop the East Coaster from coming out of retirement a few years later to Top 8 the Legacy Grand Prix held in Philadelphia. You can read more about the event in [the archives](/en/events/2005/gpphi05/a-legacy-is-established). If you're interested in Legacy, [visit the Grand Prix-Chicago page](/en/events/2009/chicago09/grand-prixchicago-event-information) for information on this year's Legacy Grand Prix.
