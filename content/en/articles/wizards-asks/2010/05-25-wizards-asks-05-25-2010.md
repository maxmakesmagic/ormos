*What's your favorite planeswalker card?* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/73/dc/73dc2c209bef80978eea09781f3075d348fa6472480299108af0945ff889dce7.jpg)

**Ken Nagle:** My favorite planeswalker card is Chandra Nalaar. One of the original planeswalker cycle printed in *Lorwyn* then again in ***Magic** 2010*, Chandra's original version is all about burning stuff to the ground. The card itself is fun, fair, and flavorful (we measure planeswalker cards by the "Three F's" Rule). I'm a fan of "Big Red"-style decks (a.k.a. "slow" red), where its burn focuses on board control until the expensive spells bad clean up. I put Chain Reaction, Comet Storm, and more in *Worldwake* to support slower red decks.

 ![Chandra Nalaar](https://i.ormos.uk/70/ad/70ad0f14c80de3da84143a6445ab8c75d599b7e43bf98bb6e65e512144d46dd0.jpg) 

  

Erik Lauer and I were the design/development team for *Duel Decks: Jace vs Chandra*, and I sure played a lot of the Chandra side!
