**Pierre Canali vs. Guillaume Wafo-Tapa (Draft)**

One of the all-time great **Magic** nations, France has produced three Hall of Famers and multiple Pro Tour champions. Two of those champions sat down to face one another in the round 7 feature match at the 2010 World Championships in Chiba, Japan. Their names? Guillaume Wafo-Tapa and Pierre Canali. Both had had monster first days and found themselves drafting the *Scars of Mirrodin* format at Table 1 to begin competition Friday morning.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9e/50/9e50493a807de5c3d9448012f991bcc37f9d068a1c60ea5ed49ec037f86901d1.jpg)*Frenchmen Pierre Canali and Guillaume Wafo-Tapa*  

### Game 1

[Blight Mamba](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blight+Mamba) led the way for Guillaume, his black and green mana base strongly indicating he was playing the popular poison/infect strategy. An [Ichor Rats](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ichor+Rats) joined his 1/1 Snake while across the table his opponent mustered an [Embersmith](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Embersmith) with which to trade by blocking the Rats. A [Contagious Nim](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Contagious+Nim) hit the battlefield for Wafo-Tapa to reload while his opponent had [Argent Sphinx](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Argent+Sphinx), well short of metalcraft to see it fully powered but happy to have a solid 4/3 flyer for the low price of just four mana.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/48/e8/48e842e08b1f3fbdf7107ead9c4c62106a7af39dc649e580cf898ca845a1005a.jpg)*Pierre Canali is back after a quiet Pro Tour period, with no less charm (but not quite as much hair).*  

The Sphinx stopped the spread of disease from Guillaume and bought Pierre time to build an offense of his own. It started with [Barrage Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barrage+Ogre), the beefy 3/3 leaving the Frenchman feeling confident enough to attack with his Sphinx. After that he had [Liquimetal Coating](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liquimetal+Coating) and [Necrogen Censer](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Necrogen+Censer), the former allowing him to turn any permanent he controlled into a combustible object for his Ogre to throw. The tandem of artifacts also allowed him to gain metalcraft, a fact that granted him the ability to clear his [Argent Sphinx](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Argent+Sphinx) of some -1/-1 counters it had picked up from blocking his opponent's creatures.

The 4/3 Sphinx bashed Wafo-Tapa to 8 life, Guillaume meekly casting a [Tangle Angler](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tangle+Angler) as riposte. [Clone Shell](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clone+Shell) for his opponent made sure the ground was too gummed up for the poison player to get through, and facing a lethal attack from the Sphinx combined with [Barrage Ogre](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barrage+Ogre) activations, he conceded.

Canali 1, Wafo-Tapa 0

### Game 2

Pierre Canali started off the second game far more aggressively than he had the first, casting an [Oxidda Daredevil](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oxidda+Daredevil) on the second turn, then a second copy one turn later. At four mana he found his [Argent Sphinx](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Argent+Sphinx) and in the blink of an eye had dumped 8 points worth of power onto the battlefield. Poor Guillaume, meanwhile, had gambled on a hand featuring two [Swamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swamp)s, but had failed to draw a third mana source. That meant he couldn't cast any spells and had been forced to discard for a turn.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9e/0c/9e0c64b299b53922fb14ffb8f9f516e67a337f43acab992f1392bf4ec2b117ad.jpg)*Wafo-Tapa has a reputation for playing control even in draft, but his aggressive infect deck bucks that trend.*  

A [Saberclaw Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Saberclaw+Golem) added to Canali's lead on the board, but Wafo-Tapa finally managed to draw a [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest) and used it to cast [Cystbearer](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cystbearer). That shut down his opponent's 2/1 [Oxidda Daredevil](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oxidda+Daredevil)s, and a turn later Guillaume came up with a second [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest) to enable [Tangle Angler](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tangle+Angler). Unfortunately for the champ, he was still taking 4-point swings from his opponent's flying [Argent Sphinx](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Argent+Sphinx).

Smelling blood in the water, Canali ignored his opponent's blockers and sent his entire team to the red zone. The swing took Wafo-Tapa to 2 life, and postcombat, Canali revealed a copy of [Arc Trail](https://web.archive.org/web/20101209000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Trail) in his hand to finish the job. Wafo-Tapa's mana screw proved too much for him to fight back in the second game, and he found himself dead in the match to his fellow Frenchman.

Canali 2, Wafo-Tapa 0
