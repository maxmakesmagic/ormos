The story out of Japanese Nationals centered around an explosive Elf deck that generated fast mana and combed Regal Force and Primal Command to win the game. It was Elves that were again the big story out of French Nationals, but Mongilardi Gilles eschewed all the fancy bells and whistles of the green-white versions for the tried and true Rock approach. To read more about the event, visit the [official event coverage](/fr/events/2009/frnat09/mongilardi-leads-france).
