After many rounds of Swiss and some great games of *Magic*, the Top 8 for Grand Prix Charlotte is set! Learn more about this event’s Top 8 competitors, their history at the top tables, and more by taking a look through the Top 8 profiles.

**Hometown:** Louisville, KY

**Occupation:** Clerk at Something 2 Do Games

**Record:** 13-1-1

**What deck are you playing and why?**  
 Kiki Chord. It seemed good.

**What’s the most interesting deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Bant Eldrazi

**In your opinion, what’s the most underrated card in Modern? Your Favorite? Why?**  
[Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy). [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy). I like tapping and untapping the same cards over and over. (See Kiki Chord)

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpcha16/t8_wang.jpg)Jack Wang

**Hometown:** Philiadelphia, PA

**Occupation:** Scientist

**Record:** 13-1-1

**What deck are you playing and why?**  
 Four-Color [Bring to Light](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bring+to+Light) [Scapeshift](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scapeshift). It’s lit.

 A dank Bant Company brew.

**In your opinion, what’s the most underrated card in Modern? Your Favorite? Why?**  
[Spell Snare](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Snare). [Cryptic Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cryptic+Command). Blue too Stronk.

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpcha16/t8_medrano.jpg)Adonnys Medrano

**Hometown:** Burke, Virginia

**Occupation:** Student

**Record:** 13-1-1

**What deck are you playing and why?**  
 Jund. Jund lyfe is best lyfe.

**What’s the most interesting deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Got ruined by Goblins because of [Goblin Grenade](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Grenade). So probably that.

In your opinion, what’s the most underrated card in Modern? Your Favorite? Why?  
 Night of Soul’s [Betrayal](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Betrayal), silver bullet against tons of decks. [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), one of the most powerful cards in Modern.

* * *

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpcha16/t8_ganz.jpg)Andreas Ganz

**Hometown:** Zurich, Switzerland

**Occupation:** Content Manager

**Record:** 13-2

**What deck are you playing and why?**  
 Ad Nauseam. It’s well-positioned against the field (except for Infect), favored vs Jeskai Harbinger, and people aren’t prepared for the deck/don’t know it.

**What’s the most interesting deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Bant Eldrazi, most other decks I faced were stock tier 1 decks.

**In your opinion, what’s the most underrated card in Modern? Your Favorite? Why?**  
 I don’t think there is really a single underrated card in a format as wide as Modern. If anything, there are underrated archetypes. As far as underrated decks go, I would say Green White Death and Taxes.

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpcha16/t8_sigrist.jpg)Mike Sigrist

**Hometown:** Foxboro, MA

**Occupation:** professional Magic player

**Record:** 13-2

**What deck are you playing and why?**  
 Jund. Couldn’t find a deck that was more consistent.

**What’s the most interesting deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Bant Eldrazi. [Sakura-Tribe Elder](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sakura-Tribe+Elder) seemed good in it as well.

**In your opinion, what’s the most underrated card in Modern? Your favorite? Why?**  
[Dark Confidant](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Confidant). It can take over games on its own and it’s not in any decks but Jund.  
[Amulet of Vigor](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Amulet+of+Vigor) is my favorite card in Modern. It was in my favorite deck, I don’t care if the deck doesn’t function anymore.

### ![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpcha16/t8_bolding.jpg)Jon Bolding

**Hometown:** Raleigh, N.C.

**Occupation:** property manager

**Record:** 13-2

**What deck are you playing and why?**  
 Company Zoo - it’s the deck I know the best and familiarity is extremely important in Modern

**What’s the most interesting deck you played against this weekend?**  
 Jund splashing white for Nahiri and [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls)

**In your opinion, what’s the most underrated card in Modern? Your favorite? Why?**  
[Thalia, Guardian of Thraben](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thalia%2C+Guardian+of+Thraben) - it’s naturally strong against a lot of archetypes.  
[Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20160522000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) - it’s the most versatile removal in Modern
