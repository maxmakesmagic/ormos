Pro Tour - Amsterdam 2004 is now underway! The field of competition is nearly 350 strong, with the best drafters from around the world descending on the city to fight for top honors. The gloom of night still shrouded the RAI convention center as the players took their seats for the first draft at nine, but morning soon arrived in full force. Not that any player noticed. The air was one of intense concentration.
