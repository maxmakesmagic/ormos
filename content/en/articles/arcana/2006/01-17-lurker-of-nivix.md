The Parun of the Izzet League, as an grand and brilliant [dragon](/en/articles/savortheflavor/2006/01-11-niv-mizzet-the-fountainhead), deserves an equally grand and brilliant guild-hall. That hall-slash-dragon's-lair is known as Nivix, its name of course inspired by Niv-Mizzet himself.

![Art by Martina Pilcerova](https://i.ormos.uk/1d/02/1d02f8abcaf2c30e4e41dc94913476ae664e1d20e6c79e1593e49346c31d9748.jpg "Art by Martina Pilcerova")  
*Art by Martina Pilcerova*

Martina Pilcerova's submitted art for this card (a card you'll likely get to see in cardboard glory at the [prerelease](https://web.archive.org/web/20070812021340/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/prerelease/guildpact)) originally featured one additional detail. The art team liked that detail but thought it appeared out of scale to the guild-hall so it was removed. Would you like to [see it](#image)? (Or [flip back](#image) again?)
