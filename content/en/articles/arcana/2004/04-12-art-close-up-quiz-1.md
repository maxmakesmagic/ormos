How well do you know your *Mirrodin* block art?

For example, did you know that this little myr hangs out on the art of Ogre Leadfoot?

![Ogre Leadfoot](https://i.ormos.uk/bb/49/bb49dcf88761135481a014fc156ac451d4473227ba2c2fcccb8b2712806ab611.jpg)

Here's a little quiz. Each of the three images below is a small section of the art of a *Mirrodin* or *Darksteel* card, magnified greatly; can you identify the card to which each belongs? *Hint:* They are all *Mirrodin* block commons.

![Are you hovering over the image in the hope that the popup text would give you some kind of clue? Tough, mister!](https://i.ormos.uk/67/da/67dad769df2c62177f461f21912b9294219292c45c443f5736303848b4cc4dd5.jpg)

Discuss your answers on the [Arcana thread](https://web.archive.org/web/20040412000000/http://boards1.wizards.com/forumdisplay.php?s=&forumid=481) on the message boards, and tune in tomorrow for the answers! (If these prove to be too easy, the next ones will be harder.)
