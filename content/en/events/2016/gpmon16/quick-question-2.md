### Which card are people playing in Eldritch Moon / Shadows over Innistrad sealed that they should not?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmon16/qq_Chin.jpg)

**Ricky Chin, Top 8 Pro Tour Battle for Zendikar:** [Lunarch Mantle](https://web.archive.org/web/20160730000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lunarch+Mantle). I don't think it's playable at all.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmon16/qq_Fabiano.jpg)

**Gerard Fabiano, GP Montreal 2014 Champion:** I think people are overvaluing [Ruthless Disposal](https://web.archive.org/web/20160730000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ruthless+Disposal).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmon16/qq_Dean.jpg)

**Paul Dean, Top 4 Pro Tour Battle for Zendikar:** [Succumb to Temptation](https://web.archive.org/web/20160730000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Succumb+to+Temptation).

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmon16/qq_skarren.jpg)

**Frank Skarren, Two-Time Grand Prix Champion:** Any vanilla 2/2 for 2. You need your cards to do more than that.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmon16/qq_Siow.jpg)

**Lucas Siow, Grand Prix Toronto 2015 Champion:** [Murderer's Axe](https://web.archive.org/web/20160730000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Murderer%27s+Axe). You have to have a LOT of good reasons to justify playing this.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2016/events/gpmon16/qq_Parke.jpg)

**Jamie Parke, 4 GP Top 8's:** Any one-toughness creature. There are a lot of cards that make them into liabilities.
