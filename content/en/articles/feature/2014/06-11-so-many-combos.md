What better than one combo deck? Two combo decks! What's better than two combo decks? Three combo decks, of course! **Magic Online** user Bryang has created a truly innovative deck that combines many different combos into one similar deck. 

![Goblin Welder](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Goblin+Welder)

This blue-black-red deck starts with the classic [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20140611000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance) + [Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20140611000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top) combo to lock your opponent out of a majority of spells. In addition, the [Painter's Servant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140611000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Painter%27s+Servant) + [Grindstone](https://web.archive.org/web/20140611000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grindstone) combo will mill your opponent's entire library. Why would you want to play both of these combos in the same deck? [Goblin Welder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140611000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Welder), of course! [Goblin Welder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140611000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Welder) can do nifty tricks with [Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20140611000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top)—tap the Divining Top to draw a card, and use [Goblin Welder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140611000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Welder) to sacrifice it. You still get to draw a card while maintaining the full function of [Goblin Welder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140611000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Welder). By alternating two copies of [Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20140611000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top) (or [Baleful Strix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140611000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baleful+Strix)) between your graveyard and the battlefield, [Goblin Welder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140611000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Welder) functions as an excellent card-drawing engine as well. 

[Imperial Recruiter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140611000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Imperial+Recruiter) and [Trinket Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140611000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trinket+Mage) provide the deck with some flexibility to play one copy of a card and easily access it. This flexibility is why the deck can put so many different combos and synergies into one deck.
