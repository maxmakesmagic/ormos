Over a hundred cards were designed for *Unglued 2*, a set that never saw print but that was full of ideas that led to [many actually-printed cards](/en/articles/arcana/2004/11-10-when-unglued-2-cards-see-print-2). We've also seen that one of the running gags in *Unglued 2* was going to be [anthropomorphized vegetables](https://web.archive.org/web/20050627000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/unglued-2-veggie-creatures-2004-11-09).

In honor of Beatdown Week, today we take a closer look at the **Magic**-slang-slash-angry-veggie-joke card, **Mad Beetdown**.

![Mad Beetdown art by Mark Raabe](https://i.ormos.uk/97/93/97934810105bacc449e36888ec209265771c7d123867992373a15a3000edd625.jpg)

According to Multiverse, Mad Beetdown was going to be a green card with one of the more aggressive poison abilities.

> Mad Beetdown  
>  3G  
>  Creature - Vegetable  
>   
>  Poison: 2  
>  Mad Beetdown cannot be the target of spells or effects.  
>  2/2

The "Poison: 2" in its rules text is a keyworded version of the same ability that [Pit Scorpion](https://web.archive.org/web/20050627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pit+Scorpion) or [Marsh Viper](https://web.archive.org/web/20050627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Marsh+Viper) have. In other words:

> Whenever Mad Beetdown deals damage to an opponent, that player gets two poison counters. *(A player with ten or more poison counters loses the game.)*

Might *Unglued 2* veggies like Mad Beetdown inspire future **Magic** card designs?
