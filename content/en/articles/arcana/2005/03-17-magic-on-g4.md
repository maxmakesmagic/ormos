The video game network [G4TV](https://web.archive.org/web/20050317000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.g4tv.com/&origin=mtgcom_arcana_779) will feature two segments on **Magic: The Gathering** on tonight's episode of [The Screen Savers](https://web.archive.org/web/20050317000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.g4tv.com/screensavers/&origin=mtgcom_arcana_779). In one segment, G4TV follows a team of pros as they make their way through last weekend's [Pro Tour Atlanta](/en/events/2005/ptatl05/nova-burns-brightest-in-atlanta). The other segment is an informative spot introducing viewers to the game.

**The episode airs tonight, March 17, at 4 PM Pacific and again at 9 PM.**

Set your TiVo!
