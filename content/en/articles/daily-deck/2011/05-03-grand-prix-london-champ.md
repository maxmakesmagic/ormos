Grand Prix London is in the bag! Daniel Royde is the champion, becoming the fourth Briton to earn the title of Grand Prix victor. The format was *Scars of Mirrodin* Limited, and Daniel's Sealed Deck from the first day of competition was enough to get him through to the Draft portion on Sunday. That allowed him to strut his stuff with this deck, and he proved he had the goods to take down the title. Read our [official coverage](/en/events/2011/gplon11/royde-king-of-london) of the event for more.
