A vorrac is a green-aligned beast native to the plane of Mirrodin. It's a boarlike creature with huge, curving, coppery tusks, and can often be found flattening the undergrowth of the Tangle. During *Mirrodin* set design, the beast was called a "djeeruk."

[![Copperhoof Vorrac](https://i.ormos.uk/9c/8d/9c8d88ca153bd5c5bee4684476af69f559c82164a21c473b85dcd1a455c4a2bd.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20070514000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Copperhoof%2BVorrac) *[Copperhoof Vorrac](https://web.archive.org/web/20070514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Copperhoof+Vorrac) art by Matt Cavotta*

Artificers on Mirrodin were responsible for the [Clockwork Vorrac](https://web.archive.org/web/20070514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Clockwork+Vorrac), a magically animated metal version of the boar-beast. The Viridian elves of the Tangle hunted this unnatural abomination.

![Clockwork Vorrac](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Clockwork+Vorrac)

![Vorrac Battlehorns](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Vorrac+Battlehorns)

[Vorrac Battlehorns](https://web.archive.org/web/20070514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vorrac+Battlehorns) shows the tusks of a vorrac forged into a hand-held weapon, magically infused with the beast's unstoppable ferocity.

Speaking of ferocity, note that the *Fifth Dawn* "[Giant Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20070514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Giant+Growth)" effect [Ferocious Charge](https://web.archive.org/web/20070514000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ferocious+Charge) depicts a vorrac on the rampage. (Happy Scry Week!)
