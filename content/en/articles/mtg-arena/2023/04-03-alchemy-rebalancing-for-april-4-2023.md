Here are the latest card rebalances and breakdowns for the Alchemy format, releasing April 4, 2023.

## Ninjas

The changes around *Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty*'s Ninjas aim to improve their defensive capabilities. The buffs are primarily focused on adding more 4-toughness Ninjas and decreasing ninjutsu costs.

With lower ninjutsu costs, players have more available mana to cast interactive spells and creatures to block with in the same turn they use ninjutsu. The lower ninjutsu costs and other general buffs to the deck also better reward players for enabling ninjutsu and building around the mechanic.

**Dokuchi Silencer**

*   Changed "discard a creature card" to "discard a card."

![Dokuchi Silencer](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/fri02chid9ls.png)

**Futurist Operative**

*   Cost U (from 3U)
*   Now 0/4 (from 3/4)

![Futurist Operative](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/biwrls1u1reg.png)

**Krydle of Baldur's Gate**

*   Removed the mana cost from the second ability.

![Krydle of Baldur's Gate](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/camas38ch8zo.png)

**Moon-Circuit Hacker**

*   Now scries 2 before drawing a card.

![Moon-Circuit Hacker](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/glce8acada0r.png)

**Nezumi Prowler**

*   Changed "target creature you control gains deathtouch and lifelink until end of turn" to "put a deathtouch counter and a lifelink counter on target creature you control."

![Nezumi Prowler](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/ne0pest0spas.png)

**Nightclub Bouncer**

*   Changed "This spell costs 1 more to cast" to "This spell costs 2 more to cast."

![Nightclub Bouncer](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/cothenoq4xic.png)

**Prosperous Thief**

*   Now 2/4 (from 3/2)

![Prosperous Thief](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/s41rlfumu5he.png)

**Satoru Umezawa**

*   Now 3/4 (from 2/4)
*   Changed "Each creature card in your hand has ninjutsu 2UB" to "Each creature card in your hand has ninjutsu 1UB."

![Satoru Umezawa](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/fegewr9wrech.png)

**Nashi, Moon Sage's Scion**

*   Cost: Ninjutsu 2B (from 3B)

![Nashi, Moon Sage's Scion](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/gos0taxi4rly.png)

**Silver-Fur Master**

*   Cost: Ninjutsu H (a single U/B hybrid mana symbol) (from UB)

![Silver-Fur Master](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/hed65chud8ub.png)

**Swarm Saboteur**

*   Cost: Ninjutsu B (from 1B)

![Swarm Saboteur](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/fropuchoyur9.png)

## Defensive Tools

These buffs aim to improve tools that help counter aggressive decks such as Mono-Red. Increasing toughness to 4 on creatures and increasing the life gain on cards better matches them against Lightning Strike and helps decks reach the late game against aggressive decks.

Knockout Blow's rebalance is intended to buff it in main decks by allowing it to destroy cards like Sheoldred, the Apocalypse; Raffine, Scheming Seer; and Thundering Raiju.

**Dawnbringer Cleric**

*   Now 1/4 (from 1/3)

![Dawnbringer Cleric](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/stu05fihit9i.png)

**Circuit Mender**

*   Changed "you gain 2 life" to "you gain 3 life."

![Circuit Mender](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/wrupat2i3ebr.png)

**Haywire Mite**

*   Changed "you gain 2 life" to "you gain 3 life."
*   Now 1/2 (from 1/1)

![Haywire Mite](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/kohacuslfrl3.png)

**Hinterland Chef**

*   Now 3/4 (from 3/3)

![Hinterland Chef](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/fr2reh4xos0z.png)

**Knockout Blow**

*   Changed "Knockout Blow deals 4 damage to attacking or blocking creature" to "Destroy target tapped creature."

![Knockout Blow](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/g512dunujiji.png)

**Lonely End**

*   Changed "you gain 2 life" to "you gain 3 life."

![Lonely End](https://media.wizards.com/2023/images/daily/vuyiwrug6tr9.png)

Look for these changes with the April 4 release. Also, [check out the *MTG Arena* Announcements](https://web.archive.org/web/20240518005713/https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/mtg-arena) for more news and updates.
