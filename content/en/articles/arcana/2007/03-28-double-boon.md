Several early **Magic** sets had multiple alternate art pieces for the same card. One such set was *Alliances*, in which the card [Phyrexian Boon](https://web.archive.org/web/20070328000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Boon) was slated to get two collectible pieces of art. 

Mark Tedin elected to illustrate the two [Phyrexian Boon](https://web.archive.org/web/20070328000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Boon) pieces as one continuous mural. Check it out:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/15/d1/15d1f670b9f8c1069102e96909d9acb750341f74d6c7c00875e565b53a8f106a.jpg)  
*Art by Mark Tedin*

The card [Serra's Boon](https://web.archive.org/web/20070328000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serra%27s+Boon) from *Planar Chaos*, as Matt Cavotta [reported earlier](https://web.archive.org/web/20070328000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/mc78), contains a particularly close art homage to the left half of Tedin's [Phyrexian Boon](https://web.archive.org/web/20070328000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Boon) art.
