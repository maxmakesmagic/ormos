Modern is the newest format on the block, and if you've been itching to try it, make sure to check it out on **Magic Online**! There you may run across this take on a popular archetype: Affinity with Erayo, Soratami Ascendant. The deck's goal is to use Affinity's many "free" spells in order to flip Erayo. From there, it's extra difficult for opponents to deal with the beatdown headed their way!
