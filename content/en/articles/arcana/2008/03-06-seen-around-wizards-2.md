Mark Rosewater's [80,000 Words](https://web.archive.org/web/20080306000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/mr240) gave you a tour of Wizards of the Coast, including a lot of our statuary and decorations. With the whole building to hit, though, he inevitably missed some awesome stuff. To wit… 

In the lobby, right next to the shelf with a massive library of Wizards games in multiple languages, stands the majestically backlit [Dakkon Blackblade](https://web.archive.org/web/20080306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dakkon+Blackblade). 

![Dakkon Blackblade](https://i.ormos.uk/62/2e/622eff25fe1ccc208360bbe7e9698ba7d594ec079eecf6a3a3b53e7388f7ba9c.jpg)

Just down the hall in our Legal department, there's a freestanding Lego model of a spaceship you probably recognize.

![Millennium Falcon](https://i.ormos.uk/ab/4b/ab4bf0c348cee0654090f294e2a1cc6e46c882788135b6895e63f74aa7ffecda.jpg)

![Cockpit](https://i.ormos.uk/1f/80/1f8084e3599774ad5cbd38bac5f470b9e2c3c86a67050945e58a0c76efcf17d0.jpg)
