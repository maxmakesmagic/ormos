The rarest card of all is the [1996 World Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20020509000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=1996+World+Champion). This card was given out to the winner of Worlds in 1996, and all other copies of it, plus the printing plates, were ceremonially destroyed. The winner of that tournament was Australia's Tom Chanpheng, who, at last word, still has the card at his home in a screwdown plastic case.

  

Chanpheng won the title playing a White Weenie deck:
