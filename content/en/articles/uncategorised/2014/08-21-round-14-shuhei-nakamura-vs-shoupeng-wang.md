Nakamura came into the round on 36 points, needing a win or a draw to lock up Top 8, while Wang, paired up, needed to win to have a chance of making the final cut. Both players were playing Mono-Black, Nakamura with a White splash, Wang with Blue.

**Game 1** 

Nakamura opened with a [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) and took Wang's [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) before casting one of his own. Wang's [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie) got Devour in Flesh'd, as did his follow-up [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon). Nakamura had another [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), which snaffled a [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel). Wang took advantage of Nakamura being tapped out to sneak some damage in with a [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault), knocking the Hall of Famer to 12. 

A second attack was stopped with a [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight), and the two traded a pair of removal spells and a pair of [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault)s the following turns. Nakamura, who had had an active [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) for some time now, was comfortably ahead on lands and cards. 

He exploited this by checking the coast was clear with a [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie) and following up with a [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s, the latter of which Wang promptly shot. Nakamura was unfazed, playing his second copy and passing. The [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s started to multiply. Wang played what defence he could with a pair of Gray Merchants of Asphodel, but Nakamura's Rats punched through for lethal. 

**Shuuhei Nakamura 1 - Shoupeng Wang 0** 

**Game 2** 

Both players traded discard spells back and forth for the first few turns before settling into the mid-game. Nakamura made the first move with an Erebos off the top of his library, Wang keeping pace with an [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections). 

A water-testing [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie) from Wang got killed with a [Devour Flesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devour+Flesh). Sensing an opening, Wang summoned a [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon) he had been sandbagging for several turns. Nakamura tried another [Devour Flesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devour+Flesh) and Wang activated his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) in response, but Nakamura had a [Bile Blight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bile+Blight) to off both the land and the Demon. 

With the board empty, Nakamura switched to an aggressive gear and started swinging with a pair of [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault)s. Wang summoned his own Erebos but had nothing to stop the incoming lands. Nakamura held off attacking for a turn to summon a [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s and a [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel). In response to the Merchant's triggered ability, Wang had a pair of [Dark Betrayal](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Betrayal)s on both his creatures to keep the devotion damage to a single digit from Nakamura's Erebos. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/r14_nakamura.jpg)

*Nakamura shifts gears and begins a land assault.* 

  

Wang attacked with his Erebos (powered off a pair of [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections)), went deep into the tank, and eventually emerged to summon a [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon), who stared across the battlefield at Nakamura's trio of [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault)s. Nakamura, his plan on the ground stymied, and unable to effectively sacrifice his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault)s to race damage through, drew nothing to stave off either the God or the Demon and shuffled up for Game 3. 

**Shoupeng Wang 1 - Shuuhei Nakamura 1** 

Nakamura opened with his third straight [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), drawing a groan of laughter from Wang. He saw a potent hand of Erebos, [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s, [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall) and a [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize). He elected to knock out the discard spell, keeping his own hand safe from meddling. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/r14_wang.jpg)

*Wang works through the [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) math.* 

  

Both players summoned a [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s on their second turn, Nakamura ahead slightly in the race with the first token copy hitting the board. Wang passed with 3 mana open, shooting one of the Rats with a [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall) as Nakamura attacked. Nakamura considered his options and then discarded a [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel) to make another rat, pinging Wang for 2. 

Wang once again passed with mana open. This time, on Nakamura's attack, Wang visibly hesitated before assigning no blockers. Nakamura went to create another token and, again, after a pause, Wang let the 6 damage through, falling to 10. At the end of Nakamura's turn Wang created his own [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat) token before untapping and passing. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/r14_both.jpg)

*The players stopped mid-game for a quick round of Euchre, Wang leading with a strong off-trump. Or they could be [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s.* 

  

Nakamura untapped and swung with his trio of Rats just as time was called. Wang created a third Rat of his own and, before blockers used an [Ultimate Price](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ultimate+Price) to knock out one of the Rats. double-blocking the second and letting the last one through unhindered. Nakamura created another Rat and activated his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault). When the dust settled, both players were left with 2 rats, Nakamura at 18, Wang at 6. 

Wang summoned a [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel), tipping the life totals to 14-10. On his turn, Nakamura attacked with a trio of Rats and as Wang gang-blocked, his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140821000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) pushed the math in his favour to leave Wang stranded without any Rats left on the board. A timely Gray Merchant off the top of his library took Nakamura to 10, but with only one attack step each left, the two players had fought each other to a draw. 

**Shuuhei Nakamura 1 - Shoupeng Wang 1**
