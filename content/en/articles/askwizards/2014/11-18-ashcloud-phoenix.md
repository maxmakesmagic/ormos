Ask Wizards is a monthly feature that gives you a chance to ask us questions and have them answered. If you'd like to submit a question, please email it to AskWizards@wizards.com. We aren't able to answer every question we receive, [but if we can](/en/articles/uncategorised/2014/06-17-ask-wizards-tuesday-june-17), it might show up in the coming months!

This month, Rules Manager Matt Tabak answers two questions about [Ashcloud Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20141118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashcloud+Phoenix).

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ashcloud%20Phoenix)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashcloud%20Phoenix)

* * *

**Q: If I cast [Burn Away](https://web.archive.org/web/20141118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burn+Away) (on my turn) on an opponent's [Ashcloud Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20141118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashcloud+Phoenix), what will happen?** 

A: In this case, [Ashcloud Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20141118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashcloud+Phoenix) dying causes two abilities to trigger: its own and the delayed triggered ability of [Burn Away](https://web.archive.org/web/20141118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burn+Away). Since you control the ability from [Burn Away](https://web.archive.org/web/20141118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burn+Away), it will go on the stack first, followed by [Ashcloud Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20141118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashcloud+Phoenix)'s ability, which will resolve first. The Phoenix returns to the battlefield face down. Then your opponent's graveyard is exiled, although the Phoenix has successfully escaped by then.

**Q: If I use [Twinflame](https://web.archive.org/web/20141118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Twinflame) on [Ashcloud Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20141118000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ashcloud+Phoenix) and then the token it creates dies, does it return to the battlefield face-down, as per the Phoenix's ability?** 

A: Once a token leaves the battlefield, it can't return. It will cease to exist the next time state-based actions are performed.
