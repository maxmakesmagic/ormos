The city-plane of Ravnica is full of guilds. And it's also full of guild symbols. And from that it follows that *Gatecrash*, being set on Ravnica, has guild symbols in card art.

Sometimes, the symbol is prominent, in the fashion of the Boros [Truefire Paladin](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Truefire+Paladin).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f4/64/f464acb3b123f5a385c3b9486dfd8a038913961e54366fc51a715179b28248a8.jpg)

[Truefire Paladin](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Truefire+Paladin) | Art by [Michael C. Hayes](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Michael%20C.%20Hayes%22%5D)

Sometimes, they're being formed out of *pure magic*, like the Simic symbol on [Gyre Sage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gyre+Sage).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/78/ee/78ee524a2b0678fbf14cc7dfaf7337d73cba6d868627590c31fa2d0697418218.jpg)

[Gyre Sage](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gyre+Sage) | Art by [Tyler Jacobson](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Tyler%20Jacobson%22%5D)

Sometimes they're hidden, like the Gruul graffiti on the wall next to [Wasteland Viper](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland+Viper).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/cd/3d/cd3d2a22dbac3aab7727a2a68aa8b6adeb24fdb6eb76f3d84acd88bbdb9c55f0.jpg)

[Wasteland Viper](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland+Viper) | Art by [Lucas Graciano](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Lucas%20Graciano%22%5D)

Sometimes it's chiseled on the wall of an Orzhov mausoleum, as on [Immortal Servitude](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Immortal+Servitude).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/dc/27/dc27be58d18184b198efff2fc1021e19fc6f725ca54adfc29f2c7fc41fcef3a9.jpg)

[Immortal Servitude](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Immortal+Servitude) | Art by [Seb McKinnon](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Seb%20McKinnon%22%5D)

Sometimes they're graffiti on a person! Wait, that's just a Dimir tattoo. Anyway, here's [Coerced Confession](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Coerced+Confession).
