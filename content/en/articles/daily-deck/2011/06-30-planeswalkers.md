The planeswalker lineup in the base set is due for a shakeup, and Magic 2012 is not disappointing! Gideon Jura will take his place in the lineup of powerful planeswalkers in the set. So how do you build a deck with Gideon Jura in mind? Why not start with this take on a Gideon deck built by Lewis Laskin? Lewis used it in days of Standard past, but with Gideon about to make a roaring comeback—and a renewed reason to splash red, [previewed today](/en/articles/top-decks/2011/06-30-ten-mind-blowing-head-turning-bullets-on-chandra-the-firebrand) by Mike Flores—it might be time to take a second look at the deck!
