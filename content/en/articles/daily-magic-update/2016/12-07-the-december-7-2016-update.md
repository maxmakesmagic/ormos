The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on December 7, 2016. Today's Update is brought to you by the [art of Jesper Ejsing](#2).

# Today's Musts

1.  > Last weekend, [@SethManfield](https://twitter.com/SethManfield) made ANOTHER Grand Prix Top 8. Here's the deck that made it happen: [https://t.co/pPAJDP8bng](https://t.co/pPAJDP8bng) [pic.twitter.com/8YoMm11ZjG](https://t.co/8YoMm11ZjG)
    >
    > — Magic.TCGplayer.com (@MTGatTCGplayer) [December 6, 2016](https://twitter.com/MTGatTCGplayer/status/806241306611748868)

Yes, I'm a big [Panharmonicon](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panharmonicon) homer, but this article would probably occupy this spot regardless of my undying love for getting two triggers out of [Cloudblazer](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudblazer). It's an emergent deck in the Standard metagame. The (now) No. 2–ranked player (see below) in the world just took this deck to a Top 8 at last weekend's Grand Prix. And the deck is just cool. And also really, really good. And now the rest of the world knows it too.

1.  [The *Magic* Art of Jesper Ejsing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G31XbLvoY2E) | The Magic Man Sam

The Magic Man Sam doesn't publish new videos very often, but when he does he knocks it out of the park. This is a deep look at the art of one of *Magic*'s most interesting artists, [Jesper Ejsing](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=+%5b%22Jesper+Ejsing%22%5d). This is the best art-centric video you'll watch today, and probably for a while after that. Certainly a must for art lovers.

# After Those, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [Commander at WotC and Atraxa Alterations with Trick Jarrett](http://collected.company/2016/12/06/playing-commander-at-wotc-and-atraxa-alterations-with-trick-jarrett-136/) | The Command Zone | Josh Lee Kwai and Jimmy Wong

This episode of The Command Zone has it all—name dropping me, talk of value, mentions of [Panharmonicon](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panharmonicon), discussion of my sweet mono-white Commander deck—you know, everything you want out of a podcast. I suppose it also talks about updating the Atraxa deck, and Trick Jarrett is there too. But, whatever.

1.  [First Strike #3—From Aetherworks Marvel to *Magic* as an e-Sport](http://manadeprived.com/first-strike-3-aetherworks-marvel-magic-e-sport/) | Mana Deprived | Kar Yung Tom, Bryan Gottlieb, and Robert Lombardi

In the race to get out more sports talk show–like *Magic* shows, First Strike is lapping [In Response](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7gun9MM1LxU&t=2s), with now its third episode. Not that there's an actual race anywhere outside of my head, but in there, the competition is heating up. [Cloudblazer](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudblazer) is there too. It's a mess.

At any rate, in this episode, [Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel) and *Magic*'s status as an e-sport are on the docket, as are Grand Prix Denver controversies, Modern, and Standard talk as well.

1.  [Standard and Mardu Vehicles after the GPs](http://www.channelfireball.com/articles/standard-and-mardu-vehicles-after-the-gps/) | ChannelFireball | Frank Karsten

The article starts out offhandedly discussing using the [Nash equilibrium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nash_equilibrium) (made famous among us common folk thanks to the movie *A Beautiful Mind*) and ends with a lengthy discussion of how Karsten would shape Mardu Vehicles, the deck that won a Grand Prix last weekend. If that's not a recipe to make the Daily Update without mentioning [Cloudblazer](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudblazer) or [Panharmonicon](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panharmonicon), I don't know what is.

# Top 25

1.  Two Grand Prix send shivers up and down the Top 25

The top story, of course, is that [Panharmonicon](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panharmonicon) pushed Seth Manfield up enough points to jump to No. 2 in our Top 25. Or maybe Manfield pushed [Panharmonicon](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Panharmonicon) to new heights. Either way, the pairing has Manfield on top of everyone, save Yasooka.

But there were actually a ton of shifts from the weekend's results. Ondřej Stráský and Samuel Black had the biggest moves, sliding up three spots each. Stephen Rubin moved into the Top 5 after another strong Grand Prix. And Paul Reitzel is back in the Top 25, displacing Thiago Saporito, after another strong Grand Prix performance.

# Deck of the Day

1.  (Nearly) Mono-Blue Artifacts

Here's another Standard Showdown submission that made me smile. The deck is essentially a [Metalwork Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metalwork+Colossus) deck that sticks to almost entirely just blue, but it has a few twists.

The spiciest is the not mono-blue option, [Saheeli Rai](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Saheeli+Rai), who can give you hasty a [Metalwork Colossus](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metalwork+Colossus), scry to set up [Glint-Nest Crane](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint-Nest+Crane), double-up on [Foundry Inspector](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foundry+Inspector) to let you cheat costs for a turn, or let you draw cards with copies of [Hedron Archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hedron+Archive). R&D member Sam Stoddard thinks Saheeli is one of the most underplayed cards in Standard, and this deck shows off a way to make the card shine.

Additionally, the nearly mono-blue nature of the deck makes [Engulf the Shore](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Engulf+the+Shore) a viable way to keep the board clear, and [Gearseeker Serpent](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gearseeker+Serpent) can be basically [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20161207000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) under the right circumstances (just don't draw too many—hence the single copy).

The deck is super fun and sweet, and just the right kind of awesome for a Standard Showdown.
