The [Vintage Super League](http://vintagesuperleague.com/) returns next week, kicking off on Tuesday, January 27, at 6 p.m. PT (one hour earlier than Season 1). In honor of the return of VSL, we thought we'd run one of the decks VSL Season 1 champion Stephen Menendian used to win the championship.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Delver+of+Secrets)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150123000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets)[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Insectile+Aberration)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150123000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Insectile+Aberration)

Tune in next Tuesday to see how a restricted [Treasure Cruise](https://web.archive.org/web/20150123000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Treasure+Cruise) and unrestricted [Gifts Ungiven](https://web.archive.org/web/20150123000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gifts+Ungiven) affects the format.
