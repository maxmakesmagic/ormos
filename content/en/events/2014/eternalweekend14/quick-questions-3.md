**"Tell me about the first piece(s) of Power 9 you acquired."** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/eternalweekend14/QQMaher.jpg)

 ***Hall of Famer, Bob Maher** - "I went to my first *Magic* tournament. There were no formats then, just *Magic* tournaments. I realized pretty quickly that I couldn't compete without the restricted cards. The first singles I ever bought were a full set of power and four copies of [Chaos Orb](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chaos+Orb)."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/eternalweekend14/QQRynkiewicz.jpg)

 ***Grand Prix Providence Champion, James Rynkiewicz** - "When I was thirteen years old, I needed a [Mox Ruby](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Ruby) for my [Burn](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Burn) deck."* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/eternalweekend14/QQLax.jpg)

 ***Pro Tour Champion, Ari Lax** - "I was at Eternal Weekend last year and, after watching some games of Vintage, I knew I wanted to acquire a set of Power 9. I immediately started grabbing every piece I could get my hands on and I had all nine in the next four weeks."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/eternalweekend14/QQWilliams.jpg)

 ***Pro Tour Top 8 Competitor, David Williams** - "They gave everyone a foil [Balduvian Horde](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balduvian+Horde) at a tournament I went to. At the time, it was one of the most highly sought-after cards around. I traded mine for a beat-up copy of [Ancestral Recall](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Recall) and [Mox Sapphire](https://web.archive.org/web/20141026000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Sapphire)."*
