For this week's Commander deck, I went back to the drawing board. I thought of commanders that sounded fun and somehow came back with [Ruric Thar](https://web.archive.org/web/20130731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ruric+Thar). Just the idea of [Ruric Thar](https://web.archive.org/web/20130731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ruric+Thar) running around (vigilantly) and punching anyone who casts a spell amused me, so I decided to take a whack at it. I do understand that playing Ruric runs the risk of drawing the ire of the table, but ask yourself: would [Ruric Thar](https://web.archive.org/web/20130731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ruric+Thar) care?

I started by adding in a ton of good creature-based acceleration. I purposely stayed away from spells, because Ruric, but there still was an ogreabundance of options. Elves, Cobras, and the odd Bird all contribute to making sure you get to cast your awesome commander and the rest of the large creatures that come with him.

Because of all the ramp, I'm pretty light on two- to four-casting-cost creatures that just provide stats. [Thrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20130731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thrun) is one of the few exceptions, because I for some reason have a soft spot for Trolls. The rest of the midrange creatures are utility creatures (and this even extends to the higher casting costs). If you are avoiding spells for the most part, your creatures are going to have to pull double duty and deal with troublesome permanents. 

The large creatures are the meat of the deck, with all sorts of behemoths (including literal Behemoths) providing a wide array of powerful effects. Some draw cards, some attack every other player, others fight or shoot fire at anything they can see, and some are just monstrous. I also like the inclusion of cards like Ezuri and [Craterhoof Behemoth](https://web.archive.org/web/20130731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Craterhoof+Behemoth) that take advantage of all the small creatures in the deck. 

Lastly, there are the spells. I kept it pretty light, but I went with every Garruk imaginable, along with a few card-draw spells to make sure you don't run out of gas. [Lead the Stampede](https://web.archive.org/web/20130731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lead+the+Stampede); [Domri Rade](https://web.archive.org/web/20130731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Domri+Rade); and [Garruk, Caller of Beasts](https://web.archive.org/web/20130731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk%2C+Caller+of+Beasts) all pay you for running this many creatures, so they easily made the cut. 

As with any Commander deck, there is infinite customization available, and I'd encourage you to take any and every part of this that looks fun and work it into a deck that does what you want it to do. If you don't, [Ruric Thar](https://web.archive.org/web/20130731000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ruric+Thar) WILL punch you.
