**Name:** Michael Bernat

**Hometown:** San Jose, CA

**Occupation:** Software Engineer

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Two Grand Prix Top 8 appearances

**What color combination did you play on Day 1, and what was your best card?**  
 White-Black with [Aryel, Knight of Windgrace](https://web.archive.org/web/20180618000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aryel%2C+Knight+of+Windgrace)

**What color combinations did you draft on Day 2, and what were your best cards?**  
 Black-Red [Siege-Gang Commander](https://web.archive.org/web/20180618000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege-Gang+Commander). Then White-Blue with [In Bolas's Clutches](https://web.archive.org/web/20180618000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=In+Bolas%27s+Clutches).

**What's the most exciting play you've made in Dominaria Limited?**  
 Nothing, really.

**What is your favorite part of a MTG event in Vegas?**  
 Good food.

**What would winning the GP mean to you?**  
 Go to more GPs!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gplv18-limited/t8_jacobson.jpg)

**Name:** Mark Jacobson

**Hometown:** San Jose, CA

**Occupation:** Hardware engineer

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Top 8s at Grand Prixs Toronto, Houston and London.

**What color combination did you play on Day 1, and what was your best card?**  
 Black-green splashing white to go 7-2. Multani was by far the best card in the deck and the losses came when it could be answered or ignored.

**What color combinations did you draft on Day 2, and what were your best cards?**  
 3-0 and 3-0 with blue-red. The first was Wizards and the flying Turtles. [Syncopate](https://web.archive.org/web/20180618000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syncopate) played an important role in the end, but [Fight with Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20180618000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fight+with+Fire) was the keystone.

**What's the most exciting play you've made in Dominaria Limited?**  
 Kicking [Fight with Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20180618000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fight+with+Fire) three times.

**What is your favorite part of a MTG event in Vegas?**  
 I enjoy seeing so many of my friends and getting to eat great food.

**What would winning GP Vegas mean to you?**  
 My first trophy. I can't be a proper hardware engineer without some hardware to take home .
