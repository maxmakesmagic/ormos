As they shuffled, Vasovski found his game face. They were off.</p>&#10;      <p>Weiss played first, starting things off with a second-turn Martyr of Sands off snow-covered plains. Vasovski showed <a>Wall of Roots</a> off blue and green lands, making the matchup clear. While Vasovski suspended a Cloudskate and played a morph, Weiss was working his Scrying Sheets. Unable to find white mana of his own, Vasovski's Court Hussar played Impulse.</p>&#10;      <p>Weiss dropped Poryphyry Nodes. It ate the Wall, but the following turn the Cloudskate ticked its last and sent it back to his hand. On this attack, Weiss decided to block and sacrificed his Martyr. He laid down seven white cards.</p>&#10;      <p>&quot;That's not bad, I guess,&quot; said Vasovski as he jotted them down. Three more Martyrs, <a>Story Circle</a>, Debtors' Knell, the Nodes, and a Beacon of Immortality. At first he counted only six.</p>&#10;      <p>&quot;So wait, that Martyr is in your yard?&quot;</p>&#10;      <p>Weiss shook his head and gestured to the fourth Martyr already in the bin. This time it was Venser who sent the enchantment back home. Vasovski sent two of his four ogres. Weiss again laid down his hand for twenty-one life. He had drawn Wrath. On his turn he replayed the Nodes again and Martyr Number Three. An end-of-turn Chord of Calling for six fetched <a>Brine Elemental</a> for Vasovski.</p>&#10;      <p>He attacked and unmorphed a Shapeshifter after Weiss stuck his Martyr in the way. Weiss came out ahead on the exchange, lifewise. A <a>Momentary Blink</a> on the Cloudskate kept the Nodes off the table for another turn. Weiss tried again, now locked out of all but two white mana. Vasovski continued the lock on his turn, taking another big chunk out of Weiss's admittedly towering life total.</p>&#10;      <p>Finally, Weiss had a chance to use his Nodes. Unfortunately, he rushed his decision, offing the morph instead of Venser. He realized it almost immediately. With Blink in the yard and Venser on board he couldn't Wrath. He passed it back, shaking his head. From there, Vasovski's ample mana and Brine Lock proved enough to take the game.</p>&#10;      <p>Vasovski 1 - Weiss 0</p>&#10;      <p>After matching a pair of Coldsteel Hearts with Walls of Roots, Vasovski tried to answer a <a>Wrath of God</a> with <a>Mystic Snake</a> only to have it pointed out that his <a>Nimbus Maze</a> could not produce blue.</p>&#10;      <p>&quot;Well, I screwed myself on that play.&quot;</p>&#10;      <p>It was his second warning of the match. His relief was visible when it resolved, but it was short-lived. Vasovski had Cloudskate in hand and now Weiss was at six.</p>&#10;      <p>His last gasp was a Faith's Fetters, and naturally the morph was <a>Willbender</a>. Krakower made a morph and passed it back.</p>&#10;      <p>&quot;That's a ... &quot; - Michael Thicke</p>&#10;      <p>&quot;Don't ask me, I don't know what I'm playing.&quot; - Gary Krakower</p>&#10;      <p>&quot;Well, let's see what he is,&quot; said Thicke, unleashing the <a>Rift Bolt</a> on it. Krakower made no play on his turn, and when Thicke replayed his <a>Tarmogoyf</a> he threw a Mouth of Ronom at it. Unfortunately, land was the fifth card type in a graveyard. Thicke didn't even have to sacrifice a land to his Gargadon to make the save.</p>&#10;      <img src="https://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/canat07/D1r2Board-%20A%20Beating.jpg">&#10;      <p>Krakower passed again, then summoned Teferi during combat. Thicke sacrificed some lands to remove counters from his Gargadon, leaving it with one, and went forward to his upkeep. When he went to move it into play, Krakower reminded him of Teferi.</p>&#10;      <p>&quot;... Oh.&quot; - Michael Thicke</p>&#10;      <p>&quot;That's why I did that land thing with your <a>Tarmogoyf</a>, to lull you into false confidence. One mistake apiece.&quot; - Gary Krakower</p>&#10;      <p>Thicke swung again, but this time he was interrupted by an unmorph of <a>Thelonite Hermit</a>. Is that a deck I should know about?&quot; - Michael Thicke</p>&#10;      <p>&quot;It's a variation, with my own tweaks. A Seal of Fire, a suspended <a>Rift Bolt</a>, and then an Incinerate on endstep, which was Remanded. That allowed him to make <a>Keldon Marauders</a>. Krakower threw out a morph and used it as a speedbump. With damage on the stack he aimed <a>Pongify</a> at his morph, forcing the Seal off the table. Thicke still hadn't found a third land.</p>&#10;      <p>Krakower untapped and passed it back without a play. Incinerate and the departing Marauders took him to twelve. Thicke's Cryoclasm had Krakower at six, and it looked like he might steal a game.</p>&#10;      <p>Krakower made an endstep morph and hit for five. Thicke's second Cryoclasm put him to three. Krakower smiled and flipped up the Hermit.
