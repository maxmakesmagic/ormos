This weekend's Pro Tour–San Diego marks the beginning of the 2010 Pro Tour season. It won't be the first time the Tour has swung through town, however. In 2002, both the Pro Tour and Masters Series took place there. The Masters, a single-elimination event that awarded Pro Tour sized checks to the top 32 players, used the Standard format, and Ryan Fuller took the title home. His deck of choice? The powerful Psychatog, which ruled the Standard scene throughout its time there. You can follow all of this year's San Diego action, starting Friday morning here on DailyMTG.com!
