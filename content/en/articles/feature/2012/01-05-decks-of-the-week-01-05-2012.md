PTQs for Pro Tour *Avacyn Restored* are [going on now in the Modern format](https://web.archive.org/web/20120105000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/640181). Take a look at the Modern lists from the prestigious Finals tournament from Japan held in December plus lists from **Magic Online** Daily Events, then find a PTQ near you to earn a trip to Barcelona in May!
