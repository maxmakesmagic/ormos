### Josh Utter-Leyton

**Age:** 29  
**Hometown:** Rohnert Park  
**Occupation:** Software Engineer 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
 Five Pro Tour Top 8s, seven Grand Prix Top 8s 

**What was your record in Sealed? What did you play? What was the hardest decision in your build?**  
 8-1. Played straight White-Black, but sideboarded into Sultai most of the time. Sultai's mana wasn't great, but I think I came up on the wrong side of the power versus consistency decision playing the two-color main deck. 

**What was your record in Draft 1? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**  
 3-0 with White-Red Aggro. First picked [Mardu Hordechief](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mardu+Hordechief)--didn't open a great rare or uncommon and it's the best common. 

**What was your record in Draft 2? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**  
 1-0-2 with Sultai. First picked [Rakshasa Deathdealer](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rakshasa+Deathdealer) because it's great. 

 **Which *Khans of Tarkir* card has changed the most in your evaluation for Limited?**  
[Barrage of Boulders](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Barrage+of+Boulders) went from what I thought was a fringe playable to a key card to draft around. 

**What present would you most like to unwrap during your Top 8 draft?**  
[Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc).

### David Foster

**Age:** 19  
**Hometown:** Crofton, MD  
**Occupation:** Student 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
 Won the two Grand Prix Trials I've competed in. 

**What was your record in Sealed? What did you play? What was the hardest decision in your build?**  
 9-0. Abzan splashing Crater's Claws. There were no difficult decisions. The pool built itself. [Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc) OP. 

**What was your record in Draft 1? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**  
 2-1 (11-0 at one point!). I played five-color good stuff. First-picked [Flying Crane Technique](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flying+Crane+Technique) because it wins games. 

**What was your record in Draft 2? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**  
 2-1. I played Abzan splashing red. I first-picked [Arc Lightning](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arc+Lightning) because it's versatile and cheap. 

 **Which *Khans of Tarkir* card has changed the most in your evaluation for Limited?**  
[Dragonscale Boon](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonscale+Boon). When it was spoiled I thought it wasn't that good, but I now know its power. 

**What present would you most like to unwrap during your Top 8 draft?**  
 Foil [Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc).

**Hometown:** Salem, MA

### Chris Fennell

**Age:** 31  
**Hometown:** Tallahassee  
**Occupation:** Attorney-to-be 

 **Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**  
 Five previous Limited Grand Prix Top 8s, with one win at Grand Prix Washington D.C. in 2004. Top 8 at Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*. Top 16 at Pro Tour Dragon's Maze. 

**What was your record in Sealed? What did you play? What was the hardest decision in your build?**  
 8-1. Abzan splash red and blue. Whether to play slightly better cards or worse cards with better mana. 

**What was your record in Draft 1? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**  
 Temur. [Frontier Bivouac](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Frontier+Bivouac). I prefer green and tri-lands help so much. 

**What was your record in Draft 2? What did you play? What did you first-pick and why?**  
 Temur. [Hooded Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hooded+Hydra). Extremely powerful mythic rare. 

 **Which *Khans of Tarkir* card has changed the most in your evaluation for Limited?**  
[Trail of Mystery](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trail+of+Mystery). It is much harder to get lands now. It was easier when the set was released. 

**What present would you most like to unwrap during your Top 8 draft?**  
[Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc).
