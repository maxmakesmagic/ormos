Congratulations to Craig Wescoe, winner of [Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze*](https://web.archive.org/web/20130524000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/voice-raises-wescoe-above-all-pro-tour) . The pro season concluded with the Pro Tour, which means we can also congratulate Josh Utter-Leyton as our 2012-2013 Player of the Year! And since this is Decks of the Week, it seems appropriate to applaud Rob Castellon, who made it to the Top 8 after an incredible undefeated run on Day One. And how did he get to the PT in the first place? Won a PTQ. You can too! Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#theros)! Good luck; have fun!
