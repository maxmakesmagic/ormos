Gunnar Geißler didn't quite make the German World *Magic* Cup team with his Mono-Green Devotion, but 7th place was pretty good anyway, and his deck was certainly noteworthy.

Eschewing a second color, Geißler played some pretty spicy cards in both his main deck and sideboard, including [Sylvan Primordial](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Primordial), [Hornet Nest](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hornet+Nest), and main-deck [Reclamation Sage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reclamation+Sage). Instead, he created a sleek, mono-colored machine that nearly took him to the title.

He also didn't fall for many of the shiny new toys in *Magic 2015*. No [Genesis Hydra;](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genesis+Hydra%3B) no [Chord of Calling;](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chord+of+Calling%3B) and just one [Nissa, Worldwaker](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%2C+Worldwaker) in the sideboard.

There's still one more weekend of [World *Magic* Cup Qualifiers](https://web.archive.org/web/20140908000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/world-magic-cup-qualifiers-event-types-events), so if you want to qualify for your national team, it's time to get on it!
