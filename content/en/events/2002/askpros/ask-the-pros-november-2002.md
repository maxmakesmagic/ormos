Ever wonder what it's like playing on camera in Top 8 of a Pro Tour? Curious about the origins of a word like "mise"? For instance, <a>Smother</a> at <img alt="1 Mana" src="https://i.ormos.uk/6b/4c/6b4cf558eab279fb9414f146ebdd0e0705ec92984fec530597dd2a3bce43ce24.gif"><img alt="Black Mana" src="https://i.ormos.uk/e1/6b/e16bcf75cffdd1079b6ba1f9f43c9e6e7253bb89521acdf6b3e93d3c2e63dbb5.gif"> can handle not only <a>Psychatog</a>, but also <a>Roar of the Wurm</a> tokens, <a>Wild Mongrel</a>, and even an <a>Exalted Angel</a> while it is face down. But <a>Smother</a> and other good cards like <a>Naturalize</a> won't have the dramatic effect you are asking about because they are just more of the same--just another instant way to kill creatures, or just another way to remove artifacts and enchantments. And while there are many very powerful creatures in the set, from <a>Goblin Piledriver</a> to <a>Visara the Dreadful</a>, I hesitate to pick any particular creature as standing head and shoulders above the rest.<p></p>&#10;                  <p>I think if I had to pick a single card, it would be <a>Astral Slide</a>, because it features a new mechanic at a very cheap cost. Because of <a>Astral Slide</a>, token creatures are a lot less powerful. Also, the Slide makes it virtually impossible to kill an <a>Exalted Angel</a>, because any attempt to stop it can be met by phasing it out. Right now, the only Standard deck featuring <a>Astral Slide</a> is a type of red-white control deck, but it certainly seems that this card has more potential with morph creatures, comes-into-play effects, or other tricks.</p>&#10;                </td>&#10;              </tr>&#10;            </tbody></table>&#10;          </td>&#10;        </tr>&#10;      </tbody></table>&#10;    </td>&#10;  </tr>&#10;</tbody></table>
