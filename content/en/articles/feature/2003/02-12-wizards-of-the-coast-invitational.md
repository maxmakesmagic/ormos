### Polls Closed-Results This Friday

Thanks to everyone who participated in choosing the players for the Wizards of the Coast Invitational. Randy Buehler will be announcing the players in his article this Friday here and on the [**Magic: The Gathering** home page](https://web.archive.org/web/20030212000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.magicthegathering.com&origin=magic_magiconline_wotcinvvotingclosed).

### Schedule

*Friday, February 28 (12 noon to 9pm PST)*  
 Standard Constructed (with *Legions*)  
 All-*Legions* Two-Headed Giant (5 boosters per player)

*Saturday, March 1 (10am to 9pm PST)*  
 Extended Constructed (with *Legions*) Tribal Wars (minimum 20 cards with the same creature type)  
 Theme Decks

*Sunday, March 2 (10am to 6pm PST)*  
*Legions* Prerelease (one *OnslaughtTM* tournament pack, three *Legions* boosters)

The finals will take place after the last round finishes, and will consist of Standard (with *Legions*), Extended (with *Legions*) Tribal Wars, and theme decks.

During the finals, the fourteen other players who didn't make the finals will be gunslinging current Standard against all comers.

### How To Watch

Just like the **Magic** Invitational in October, you won't be able to watch the games live, but can watch the replays after each match finishes. (Of course, if Randy makes the finals, we'll have to find another commentator.) Click on the table of the match you want to watch, then select the game to see the replay.

### Why Are We Doing It?

Frankly, once you come on board at Wizards, you don't get the fun of playing in all the events that other **Magic** and ***Magic** Online* players do. The Wizards Invitational gives us a chance to play the game we love, in friendly competition, for a real prize: to have the avatar we want added to the game. Not all of us have what it takes to hold our own on the Pro Tour, but we do love to play.

Remember to check back this Friday for Randy's announcement of who's in.

See you February 28th through March 2nd for the Invitational.
