## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c2/6f/c26f29b96cf15bac6b2e026fc08ce86627017b2fb9a217553685dcba8dc4e599.png)

### FAT PACKS

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, six 15-card booster packs, one foil basic land card, one premium card, a special-edition life counter, and *The Moons of Mirrodin* novel.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c2/6f/c26f29b96cf15bac6b2e026fc08ce86627017b2fb9a217553685dcba8dc4e599.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c2/6f/c26f29b96cf15bac6b2e026fc08ce86627017b2fb9a217553685dcba8dc4e599.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044803/http://magic.wizards.com/node/976516)  
 (\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c2/6f/c26f29b96cf15bac6b2e026fc08ce86627017b2fb9a217553685dcba8dc4e599.png)

### TOURNAMENT PACKS

The foundation for exciting Sealed Deck play. Includes 45 randomly assorted cards and 30 basic land cards.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4f/e7/4fe701dc6d2788a16f1b6765521f04649083f44ae021427859d6b8a81fbf21f9.png)This 306-card, black-bordered set introduces several new mechanics to **Magic** (Imprint, Affinity, and Entwine) as well as the new Equipment card type, adding power and reusability to your deck.

**Set Name:** *Mirrodin*  
**Block:** Set 1 of 3 of the *Mirrodin* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 306

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, Spanish
