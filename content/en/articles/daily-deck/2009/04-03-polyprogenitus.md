Progenitus. Every tournament player's worse nightmare, nigh unkillable. Emale has taken the 10/10 to a whole new level with this **Magic Online** Classic deck. Proteus Staff and Polymorph serve to sneak the gigantic monster into play. Swords to Plowshares, Daze, Counterbalance, and an assortment of removal and counters keep the board clear long enough to pull the feat off!
