**Announcement Date:** March 20, 2009  
**Effective Date:** April 1, 2009  
**Magic Online Effective Date:** April 8, 2009

**Standard, Extended, Legacy**, **Vintage, *Shards of Alara* Block Constructed**  
 No changes

### Magic Online Formats

**Vanguard Avatar Changes**  
 Reaper King  
 Was: -1 card, -5 life  
 Now: -2 cards, -5 life  
 Change: -1 card, +0 life

Nekrataal  
 Was: +0 cards, -4 life  
 Now: -1 card, -4 life  
 Change: -1 card, +0 life

**Online Classic**   
 (and Vanguard Classic)  
 Demonic Tutor is restricted\*

\*Demonic Tutor is currently not available online. It will become available with the release of [*Duel Decks: Divine vs. Demonic*](/en/articles/arcana/2008/12-10-announcing-duel-decks-divine-vs-demonic-2)*.*

**Commander**, **Freeform, Momir Basic, Pauper, Prismatic, Singleton 100**, **Tribal Wars Classic, Tribal Wars Standard**  
 No changes

**Kaleidoscope\*\***  
 Glittering Wish is banned

\*\*Kaleidoscope is a new **Magic Online**–only format that will be available soon. Watch for an announcement called the **Official** **Unofficial Format Review** for the formats rules to come soon in [the **Magic Online** announcements folder](http://forums.gleemax.com/forumdisplay.php?f=667).

For an explanation of this announcement, read the Latest Developments column on Friday, March 20. For the complete list of all banned and restricted cards by format, click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20120427154520/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=judge/resources/banned).
