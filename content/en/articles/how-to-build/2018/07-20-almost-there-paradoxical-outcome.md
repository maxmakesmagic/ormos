[Paradoxical Outcome](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Paradoxical+Outcome) Combo has been a deck for a while but has never broken through the barrier and become an established archetype. With the printing of [Sai, Master Thopterist](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sai%2C+Master+Thopterist), this seems to have changed.

This deck is now very real and can definitely take down a tournament. If you haven't seen it in practice, here is how it works.

The early stages of the game will be centered around setting up. You'll be playing some cantrips ([Prophetic Prism](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prophetic+Prism), [Glint-Nest Crane](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint-Nest+Crane)) and getting a few artifacts in play. At this point, you mostly want to deploy the board and, if possible, dig for [Inspiring Statuary](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inspiring+Statuary) and [Paradoxical Outcome](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Paradoxical+Outcome). You don't need Statuary to win, but it makes things easier.

Once you have Outcome and a decent board presence, you're ready for your big turn. Start by playing some cheap artifacts if you haven't already, and then cast [Paradoxical Outcome](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Paradoxical+Outcome), returning all of them to your hand and drawing a lot of cards. If you have Statuary, then [Paradoxical Outcome](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Paradoxical+Outcome) effectively costs one mana—zero if you have an active [Mox Amber](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Amber). At this point, if you have mana left and if it's not yet in play, you can play [Aetherflux Reservoir](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherflux+Reservoir) (sometimes via the help of [Baral's Expertise](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baral%27s+Expertise)). You can then replay all your cheap artifacts, drawing new cards off [Prophetic Prism](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prophetic+Prism) and [Glint-Nest Crane](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint-Nest+Crane). Often, this is already enough to get the opponent dead.

If it isn't, then you can play another [Paradoxical Outcome](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Paradoxical+Outcome) or a [Baral's Expertise](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baral%27s+Expertise). [Baral's Expertise](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baral%27s+Expertise) can return your own cards back to your hand, which means each [Baral's Expertise](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baral%27s+Expertise) can count as five spells (the [Expertise](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baral%27s+Expertise) itself, three that you're returning, and another one that you cast for free). If you have Statuary in play, then it costs only two mana and can be used to play [Aetherflux Reservoir](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherflux+Reservoir).

This is an example of how a turn like this can go:

You untap turn five with 15 life, four lands, [Renegade Map](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Renegade+Map), and [Inspiring Statuary](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inspiring+Statuary) in play.

Cast [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter).

Cast [Mox Amber](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Amber).

Tap [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter) and an Island to cast [Glint-Nest Crane](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint-Nest+Crane). Find another [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter).

Cast [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter) #2.

Tap Mox, [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter) #2, [Renegade Map](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Renegade+Map), and a land to cast [Paradoxical Outcome](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Paradoxical+Outcome), returning [Renegade Map](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Renegade+Map), [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter), [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter) #2, [Glint-Nest Crane](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint-Nest+Crane), and [Mox Amber](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Amber) to your hand. Draw five cards.

Play a land for the turn.

Recast [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter).

Recast [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter) #2.

Recast [Mox Amber](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Amber).

Tap [Inspiring Statuary](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inspiring+Statuary) and Island to recast [Glint-Nest Crane](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint-Nest+Crane).

Tap your remaining two mana, [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter), [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter) #2, and [Mox Amber](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Amber) to cast [Baral's Expertise](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baral%27s+Expertise). Return [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter), [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter) #2, and [Mox Amber](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Amber) back to your hand. Cast [Aetherflux Reservoir](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherflux+Reservoir) for free.

You've now cast eleven spells. Recast both [Ornithopter](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ornithopter)s and [Mox Amber](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Amber) to gain 39 life, putting you at 54. Activate [Aetherflux Reservoir](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherflux+Reservoir) and kill your opponent.

Of course, turns aren't exactly the same—sometimes you have fewer artifacts and more mana, sometimes you find something off your [Paradoxical Outcome](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Paradoxical+Outcome), and so on—but this is the general idea.

The big addition from *Core Set 2019* for this deck is [Sai, Master Thopterist](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sai%2C+Master+Thopterist). It might not seem very important, given that it didn't even factor in in the example turn, and in fact is not needed at all to combo off, but it does a series of things for the archetype:

*   It provides early defense. A 1/4 is hard to attack through, survives most removal spells, and the 1/1s that you will get naturally just by playing your artifacts do a good job of blocking.
*   It provides a way to pressure your control opponents. If you get [Sai, Master Thopterist](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sai%2C+Master+Thopterist) in play, you can start attacking for 3 or 4 damage a turn, and even if they do remove Sai (which isn't easy, since it doesn't die to [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push), [Magma Spray](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Spray), [Abrade](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abrade), or [Cast Down](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cast+Down)), you get to keep attacking with the Thopters. This ensures they have to act first, and then you can combo them out.
*   It turns on [Mox Amber](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mox+Amber), which is a big boost to your combo turns, particularly once you replay it with [Paradoxical Outcome](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Paradoxical+Outcome).

The first 50 or so cards in any list are pretty standard, but after that there's room for some customization. Personally, I like [Metallic Rebuke](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Metallic+Rebuke) a lot in a deck like this, and I would move some to the main deck alongside [Glint-Nest Crane](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glint-Nest+Crane). In turn, I'd move [The Antiquities War](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Antiquities+War) to the sideboard (I don't think you need a plan B in Game 1), and trim some of the other cards. I'm also a fan of [Karn, Scion of Urza](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn%2C+Scion+of+Urza) in the sideboard, as it's an excellent card to pressure your opponent while also digging for more artifacts, and, very importantly, it can be played completely off [Inspiring Statuary](https://web.archive.org/web/20180720000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inspiring+Statuary).
