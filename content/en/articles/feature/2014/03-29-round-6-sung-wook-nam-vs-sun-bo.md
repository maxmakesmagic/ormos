Both players have unblemished records thus far and intend to keep it that way. Nam Sung-Wook has had the honor of being the first South Korean player to win a Grand Prix, achieving this feat at Melbourne less than a month ago. Running the very same deck that propelled him to victory, Nam is back with Mono Black Devotion, but has tuned his deck to run [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) in the maindeck in view of the shifting metagame. 

His opponent, Sun Bo, is no slouch himself. Known as duotianshi203 on **Magic Online**, he was the powerhouse that almost nearly became the MTGO Player of the Year, narrowly losing to eventual winner Shouta Yasooka by a two mere Qualifying Points. 

Sun laments that it was a heartbreaking defeat, considering he had been leading the entire season for the first ten months before being overtaken. Regardless, he remains one of the most well-known players in Mainland China and will try to disrupt Nam's attempt at perfection.

**Game One** 

After a trip to Paris, Nam opened with [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress), nabbing [Syncopate](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syncopate) from Sun's hand. This left Sun with with just a fistful of lands and a lone [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict). Thankfully, he had a pair of Scrylands at the ready, which enabled him to smooth out his future draws. 

Not having a third untapped land, Nam activated [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault), and swung for 4 damage, before laying [Temple of Deceit](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temple+of+Deceit). This prompted Sun to cast [Jace, Architect of Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Architect+of+Thought) to slow down the bleeding by shrinking the opposing army. 

Sun decided it was time to draw some cards and flipped over [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion), [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) and [Azorius Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azorius+Charm). Nam was clearly very experienced in this matchup, and wasted no time in isolating Elspeth. Sun took the larger pile, with [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) and [Azorius Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azorius+Charm). 

Regardless, double [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s and double [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) crashed into the red zone. Nam activated a third [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault), turning all the [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s into temporary 5/5s, but only for a short moment. Sun blocked the first [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) with his own, and sent the other onto the top of the library. 

[Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) cleared the board and it looked like Sun was in firm control having protected his Jace for upwards of five turns. Sun fired off the first [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation), and then another. Soon [Ætherling](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86therling) reared it's ugly head and even [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) from Nam could not bother Sun as he proceeded to close the game in three attack steps. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/r6_sun.JPG)

 *Sun Bo adeptly survives Nam's [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) and [Pack Rat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pack+Rat)s.* 

  

**Sun Bo 1 – Nam Sung-Wook 0** 

**Game Two** 

Sun's strategy seemed to be keeping land-heavy hands and hoping for good cards on top with the help of the dozen Scrylands in his deck. 

Nam used [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize) to remove [Blood Baron of Vizkopa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Baron+of+Vizkopa), leaving Sun with nothing but land. But being the talented player that he is, he topdecked [Syncopate](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syncopate) to stop Nam's [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter). Nam was unbothered, and resolved [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections). Without [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere), Sun would eventually become buried with the extra card granted per turn. 

The extra cards drawn were pretty useful. [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) forced Sun to lose [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion), paving the way for a very scary [Erebos, God of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Erebos%2C+God+of+the+Dead). When [Blood Baron of Vizkopa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Baron+of+Vizkopa) hit play, Nam was ready with [Devour Flesh](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Devour+Flesh). 

Despite drawing the [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) he required (exiling [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections)), Nam proceeded to draw not one, not two, but three cards from Erebos on the next turn, clearly unbothered about his life total in this matchup. 

Eventually, using [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) to clear the way, he resolved [Desecration Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Desecration+Demon) and finished Sun off in two quick punches, plus a lethal [Gray Merchant of Asphodel](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gray+Merchant+of+Asphodel). 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/r6_nam.JPG)

 *[Erebos, God of the Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Erebos%2C+God+of+the+Dead) ensured that Nam would win the second game.* 

  

**Sun Bo 1 – Nam Sung-Wook 1** 

**Game Three** 

After both players took a mulligan, Nam's double [Duress](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Duress) and [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie) forced Sun to discard [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation), [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) and [Blood Baron of Vizkopa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Baron+of+Vizkopa). 

This reduced Sun to just three lands in play and two more in his grip. Scrying into [Jace, Architect of Thought](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Architect+of+Thought), it was taken down by [Hero's Downfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero%27s+Downfall). When Sun used [Dark Betrayal](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Betrayal) to kill [Lifebane Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifebane+Zombie), Nam replaced it with [Notion Thief](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Notion+Thief), maintaining the pressurizing clock while switching off [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) temporarily. 

Nam continued to draw more cards with [Underworld Connections](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Connections) and [Read the Bones](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Read+the+Bones), burying Sun with card advantage. When Nam presented [Nightveil Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140329000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nightveil+Specter), Sun decided to throw in the towel and extend the hand.

*Drawing a million cards while preventing your opponent from doing so, is a great strategy to secure victory, as demonstrated by Nam.* 

**Sun Bo 1 – Nam Sung-Wook 2** 

**Nam Sung-Wook defeats Sun Bo and moves to 6-0.**
