You're gonna need your tokens handy to brave the [*Streets of New Capenna*](https://web.archive.org/web/20220420000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/streets-of-new-capenna). There are a whopping 53 full-art tokens and seven double-sided punch-out tokens across *Streets of New Capenna* and its Commander decks.

In *Streets of New Capenna* Draft and Set Boosters, there are 17 full-art tokens and two double-sided punch-out tokens to be found. Collector Boosters feature the same 17 full-art tokens as traditional foil double-sided tokens but do not contain the punch-out tokens.

The five *Streets of New Capenna* Commander decks each include ten double-sided tokens and one double-sided punch-out token that supports the needs of your chosen family, with 36 full-art tokens being unique to *Streets of New Capenna* Commander decks.

You'll find all the cards that create these tokens in the [*Streets of New Capenna* Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2022/03-24-streets-of-new-capenna-card-image-gallery), the [Variant Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2022/04-05-streets-of-new-capenna-variant-card-image-gallery), and the [*Streets of New Capenna* Commander Card Image Gallery](/en/articles/card-image-gallery/2022/04-19-streets-of-new-capenna-commander-card-image-gallery)!
