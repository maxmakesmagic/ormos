### What's your Pack 1, Pick 1, from the following pack?

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/QQ3-Cards.jpg)

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/gpsan15_qq_wescoe.jpg)  
*Craig Wescoe*

**Craig Wescoe:** [Avaricious Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avaricious+Dragon) - though [Reave Soul](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reave+Soul) is the best common in the set.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/gpsan15_qq_arenas.jpg)  
*Christian Arenas*

**Christian Arenas:** Voidmage - the [Avaricious Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avaricious+Dragon) is too much of a colour commitment for a first pick.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/gpsan15_qq_saito.jpg)  
*Tomoharu Saito*

**Tomoharu Saito:** [Avaricious Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Avaricious+Dragon) - flying is very strong in this format.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/gpsan15_qq_navas.jpg)  
*Luis Navas*

**Luis Navas:** [Suppression Bonds](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Suppression+Bonds).
