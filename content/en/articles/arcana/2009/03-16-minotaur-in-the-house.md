![](https://i.ormos.uk/f2/1b/f21b62330429ea97dc12f0c219be62f8ba71c4fa270c475dc6bd98206bbdbf25.jpg)  

(Well, that's what it looked like in our old building; we had to [move it across the street](https://web.archive.org/web/20090316000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/wotc-dragon-moves-2005-10-17) a few years ago.)

But here's something you might not know. That dragon was sculpted by Kim Gram, who stopped by the office last week. And here's what *she* looks like:

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b5/ab/b5aba6e990cdd9200db4264bdfce1564dfbc80834f77526cd2bc88e3bc13dc68.jpg)  

So our dragon appears to have been sculpted by a minotaur. Neat!
