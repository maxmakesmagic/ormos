The leader of the Rakdos Cult is a demon named Rakdos, which is easy to remember. When Rakdos first appeared in the *Dissension* set, it was as Rakdos the Defiler:

  

 ![Rakdos the Defiler](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Rakdos%20the%20Defiler&options=) 

  

And here's a closer look!

In the time since the original *Ravnica: City of Guilds* block, many things have changed in the world of Ravnica. But Rakdos is still around! This time, he's Rakdos, Lord of Riots.
