### Mark Rosewater Introduces the Event

* * *

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ce/ee/ceee2366bc57174e43e2d12f94839b446d7fd2eb2ef2d0978e287aeb5361ccee.jpg)![](https://i.ormos.uk/49/a2/49a2dea08e42f0099440b668a0246c2412009f9e352943fdf0ed991849f0865a.jpg)

Welcome to the 2002 **Magic: The Gathering** Invitational. This is our seventh event and sixth continent. But more importantly, the Invitational is going someplace it's never been before: online.

The Invitational is very different from most other premier-level **Magic** events. Don't get me wrong, the Invitational will involve top pros (sixteen of the best from around the world will fly to Seattle to play on ***Magic** Online*) competing for a big prize: the chance to design their very own **Magic** card. But the nature of the event, the mood of the event, the very structure of the event is very different from the run-of-the-mill Pro Tour.

The Invitational is the **Magic** all-star game. The competitors will test their **Magic** skills in [five different formats](https://web.archive.org/web/20021010000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=sideboard/feature/20020910a&origin=magic_magiconline_articlemrMI02intro), three Constructed and two Limited, over fifteen rounds. Instead of normal Swiss or Single Elimination rounds, the Invitational is in Round Robin format, which means each player will play each other player once. You'll be able to see the full schedule [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20021010000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=sideboard/events/mi02&origin=magic_magiconline_articlemrMI02intro) when it goes up. The top two finishers at the end of the fifteen rounds will compete against one another in a best-of-three match final using all three Constructed formats.

The Invitational is designed to challenge the competitors while providing maximum entertainment for the spectators. The formats are as varied as they are fun. From October 18 through October 20, you will see the top players in the world play future formats, [bid for Alphabet decks](https://web.archive.org/web/20021010000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=sideboard/feature/20021009a&origin=magic_magiconline_articlemrMI02intro), and play Two-Headed Giant. Watch as these players enjoy the many facets of the **Magic** game.

At Pro Tour events, we have a special Feature Matches area where we stick the most exciting matches of each round. These matches are watched by numerous spectators and are covered online. The Invitational is the tournament where every match *is* a feature match.

This year, you have an opportunity to not just read about the matches, but actually watch them. Anybody with a ***Magic** Online* account can watch replays of the Invitational games moments after they end, or a week later, or even sometime next year. What happens during the Invitational will be stored permanently on ***Magic** Online.*

So please join us as we make history with the very first premier **Magic** event to be held online. For analysis and further coverage of the event, check out this year's sponsor, [*Sideboard* Online](https://web.archive.org/web/20021010000000/http://www.wizards.com/leaving.asp?url=http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=sideboard&origin=magic_magiconline_articlemrMI02intro) .

Let me just end by wishing all our competitors the best of luck. And most of all, have fun.

Mark Rosewater  
 Creator & Head Judge, **Magic** Invitational
