# ***What Is Your Best Magic Memory From 2014?***

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/qqIchikawa.jpg)  
**No. 23 Yuuki Ichikawa:** At Pro Tour *Magic 2015*, winning my bubble match against the legendary Jon Finkel.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/qqLee.jpg)  
**No. 9 Lee Shi Tian:** Winning the last round of the swiss at Pro Tour *Khans of Tarkir*. The last round, the last game, the last turn, at one life.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/qqYukuhiro2.jpg)  
**Ken Yukuhiro:** Finishing 25th at Pro Tour *Magic 2015*. It got me Gold status. Any lower and I would’ve missed.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/qqWatanabe.jpg)  
**Yuuya Watanabe:** World Championships. I’m not known for deck design, but I did well the whole event with a deck I built myself. At one point Patrick Chapin (a player I greatly respect) complimented me on the deck. That meant a lot to me.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/qqFloch.jpg)  
**No. 2 Ivan Floch:** Even more than winning the Pro Tour, it was making that Top 8. It was my first Pro Tour Top 8, and Saturday was my birthday. I can remember jumping like crazy when they announced it.
