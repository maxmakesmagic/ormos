![Obzedat, Ghost Council](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Obzedat%2C+Ghost+Council)

**[Obzedat, Ghost Council](https://web.archive.org/web/20121231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Obzedat%2C+Ghost+Council)** – *Gatecrash* mythic rare. From the [*Gatecrash* FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20121231000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/634111):

When Obzedat is returned to the battlefield by its last ability, it gains haste indefinitely. This may be relevant if a player gains control of it later using a spell or ability that doesn't give it haste. 

![High Priest of Penance](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=High+Priest+of+Penance)

**[High Priest of Penance](https://web.archive.org/web/20121231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=High+Priest+of+Penance)** – *Gatecrash* rare. From the [*Gatecrash* FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20121231000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/634111):

High Priest of Penance's ability triggers once for each instance of damage dealt to it, not once for each 1 damage. The ability will trigger even if High Priest of Penance is dealt lethal damage. 

![Enter the Infinite](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Enter+the+Infinite)

**[Enter the Infinite](https://web.archive.org/web/20121231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enter+the+Infinite)** – *Gatecrash* mythic rare. From the [*Gatecrash* FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20121231000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/634111):

If you are playing with the top card of your library revealed (due to [Garruk's Horde](https://web.archive.org/web/20121231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk%27s+Horde), for example), you'll reveal each card before you draw it. 

![Call of the Nightwing](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Call+of+the+Nightwing)

**[Call of the Nightwing](https://web.archive.org/web/20121231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Call+of+the+Nightwing)** – *Gatecrash* uncommon. From the [*Gatecrash* FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20121231000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/node/634111):

You can exile [Call of the Nightwing](https://web.archive.org/web/20121231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Call+of+the+Nightwing) encoded on the Horror creature token it just created.

![Experiment One](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Experiment+One)

**[Experiment One](https://web.archive.org/web/20121231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Experiment+One)** – *Gatecrash* uncommon. Frankly, [Experiment One](https://web.archive.org/web/20121231000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Experiment+One) seems pretty successful. It's not every group of biomancers that could get it right the first time. Good job, Simic!
