## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3a/e8/3ae81066f1d3cd88ca87e7d405d3ea3f888a2ad47871af64314f279e42b8036a.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, one 75-card tournament pack, three 15-card booster packs, two premium cards, the *Onslaught* novel, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3a/e8/3ae81066f1d3cd88ca87e7d405d3ea3f888a2ad47871af64314f279e42b8036a.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3a/e8/3ae81066f1d3cd88ca87e7d405d3ea3f888a2ad47871af64314f279e42b8036a.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044808/http://magic.wizards.com/node/976621)  
 (\* Not available in all languages)  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3a/e8/3ae81066f1d3cd88ca87e7d405d3ea3f888a2ad47871af64314f279e42b8036a.png)

### TOURNAMENT PACKS

The foundation for exciting Sealed Deck play. Includes 45 randomly assorted cards and 30 basic land cards.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/89/85/8985695c46a48306e704a8a46f7f203f3a12c3b482aec8a810f4c181d07336cb.png)This black-bordered, 350-card set features randomly inserted foil premium cards and powerful mechanics like Morph, “Creature Types Matter” Cards, Fear, Chain Spells, and Cycling (an old favorite from *Urza's Saga*).

**Set Name:** *Onslaught*  
**Block:** Set 1 of 3 of the *Onslaught* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 350

**Languages:** English, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044808/https://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/faq/onslaught) (or download as [DOC](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044808/https://archive.wizards.com/dci/downloads/FAQ_Onslaught_17sep2002.doc))

**Game Design:** Mike Elliott (lead) and Michael Donais

**Game Development:** Randy Buehler Jr. (lead), Elaine Chase, Michael Donais, Brian Schneider, Henry Stern, and Worth Wollpert
