The **Magic: The Gathering** Pro Tour travels to tropical San Juan, Puerto Rico for Pro Tour–San Juan! Truly the crown in the **Magic** tournament circuit, the Pro Tour is a four-day **Magic** festival offering something for everyone whether you’re new to the game or a seasoned World Champion. 

There will be a whole lot more than a $230,000 tournament taking place! **Magic** fans of all ages and experience levels will find something waiting for them at Pro Tour–San Juan. Try [Public Events](/en/events/2010/sanjuan10-public/pro-toursan-juan-public-event-information), tournaments open to all offering fantastic prizes throughout the weekend. Face off against **Magic** celebrities in the Champions Challenge, or mingle with artists Aleksi Briclot and John Avon as they sign cards for fans all weekend long. Get a glimpse into the pro lifestyle while seeing some of the game’s best in action live and up close. Whether you come for one day or the entire weekend, Pro Tour–San Juan offers you the opportunity for an unforgettable **Magic** experience!

Pro Tour–San Juan is a *Zendikar* Block Constructed / *Rise of the Eldrazi* Booster Draft format event with a *Rise of the Eldrazi* Booster Draft Top 8 that runs May 28-30, 2010. The *Zendikar* Block Constructed format consists of *Zendikar*, *Worldwake*, and *Rise of the Eldrazi*. The Last-Chance Qualifier is Standard Constructed and will be held at 5 p.m. on Thursday, May 27 at the Puerto Rico Convention Center, with the top four finishers earning a spot in the main event. For more information, visit the complete [Public Events schedule](/en/events/2010/sanjuan10-public/pro-toursan-juan-public-event-information).

### About San Juan

San Juan is the capital and largest city in Puerto Rico, an archipelago approximately 1,000 miles southeast of Florida. A self-governing unincorporated territory of the United States, Puerto Rico is known as "La Isla del Encanto," which translates into "The Island of Enchantment." 

Primarily a mountainous island, Puerto Rico has large coastal areas in the north and south. The average temperature is 82 degrees Fahrenheit, remaining fairly constant through the year despite the seasons. Both English and Spanish are official languages of Puerto Rico. Along with the obvious tropical tourist activities, Puerto Rico has many historic military forts such as the Fort de San Felipe del Morro overlooking San Juan Bay. The Arecibo Ionospheric Observatory, located approximately 100 kilometers from San Juan, studies radio emissions from distant galaxies and is the home base for NASA's SETI program (Search for Extra-Terrestrial Intelligence). This radio telescope, the largest of its kind, measures 305 meters in diameter and was featured in *Goldeneye*, *Contact*, and *The X-Files*.

For more about Puerto Rico, visit [gotopuertorico.com](http://www.gotopuertorico.com/).

### Site Attractions

**PUBLIC EVENTS**  
 Come one, come all to participate in four days of events open to the public. Starting Thursday with the Last-Chance Qualifier, there are tournaments, drafts, EDH, *Planechase* events, and open dueling all weekend. Pro Tour–San Juan will also play host to a few extra-special events, such as Massive Multiplayer **Magic**!, a Grand Melee, a Pro Tour–Amsterdam Qualifier, the $3,000 Draft Challenge, the [WPN Championship](/en/events/2010/sanjuan10-wpnchampionship/wpn-championshipsan-juan), Super Friday Night **Magic**, and the **Magic Online** Live Series Championship. Prizes include airfare and accomodations at Pro Tour–Amsterdam, iPads, premium foil sets of all Standard-legal cards, iPods, an Xbox 360 Elite gaming system, Power 9, and much more! 

*   **[Complete Public Events Schedule](/en/events/2010/sanjuan10-public/pro-toursan-juan-public-event-information)**

**CHAMPIONS CHALLENGE**  
 Test your **Magic** skills against Wizards of the Coast R&D members Brian Tinsman, Tom LaPille, Mark Globus, Ken Nagle, and Matt Tabak, along with Pro Tour Hall of Famers Randy Buehler and Alan Comer. Win and you can walk away with cool **Magic** prizes! 

*   [**Champions Challenge Profiles**](https://web.archive.org/web/20100330000000/https://www.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtgcom/protour/sanjuan10-challenge)

**ARTISTS ON SITE**  
 Prominent **Magic** artists Aleksi Briclot and John Avon will be on hand for free signings of your favorite cards, plus you’ll have the opportunity to buy prints and even original artwork. John Avon will be available 9 a.m. - 11 a.m. and 12 p.m. - 2 p.m. Friday-Sunday, and Aleksi Briclot will be available 1 p.m. - 5 p.m. Friday-Sunday.

*   [**Artist Profiles and Card Lists**](/en/events/2010/sanjuan10-artists/pro-toursan-juan-artists)

**WATCH THE TOP 8**  
 All of Sunday's Top 8 action can be viewed on screens in the hall, with Pro Tour Statistician Rich Hagon and Pro Tour Historian Brian David-Marshall providing the commentary. Sunday's action begins at 10:45 a.m. ET, and is available as a [webcast](/en/events/2004/webcast/magic-the-gathering-webcast-schedule) from the comfort of your own home.
