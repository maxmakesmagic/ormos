### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the set, six 15-card booster packs, two premium cards, the *Apocalypse* novel, and a special-edition life counter.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/be/06/be06f1e48b728581d6dbd63a9a406afe035b22469085ac62866af64111619477.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/be/06/be06f1e48b728581d6dbd63a9a406afe035b22469085ac62866af64111619477.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/eb/74/eb74b9cb59d10ef859a7a0c6257536d899398505d070d85915e402e7025dd569.png)

This 143-card set features a host of new mechanics and multicolored madness: Envoys, Siphon Creatures, Enemy Pain Lands, Enemy Split Cards, Disciples, Volvers, and Sanctuaries.

**Set Name:** *Apocalypse*  
**Block:** Set 3 of 3 of the *Invasion* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 143

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional

[FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044248/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/expert/apocalypse/ap_faq.asp)

**Game Design:** Paul Peterson (lead), Randy Buehler, Charlie Cuturza, Mark Rosewater, and Teeuwynn Woodruff

**Game Development:** Mike Elliott (lead), Randy Buehler, Charlie Cuturza, Michael Donais, and Robert Gutschera, with contributions from Brady Dommermuth and Beth Moursund
