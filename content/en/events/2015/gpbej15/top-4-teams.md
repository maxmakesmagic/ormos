After many rounds of Swiss and some great games of *Magic*, the Top 4 teams for Grand Prix Beijing is set! Learn more about this event’s Top 4 teams, their history at the top tables, and more by taking a look through the Top 4 team profiles.

*(Photographs by Masashi Koyama)*

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpbej15/T8_WescoeHoaenHron.jpg)  
*Craig Wescoe, Richard Hoaen and Mike Hron*

**Name:** Craig Wescoe  
**Hometown:** New York City, USA  
**Age:** 32  
**Occupation:** Columnist for TCGplayer.com  
**Day 1 Deck Colors, Score and MVP(s):** 6-3 with White-Red. [Valakut Invoker](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut+Invoker) was my MVP.  
**Day 2 Deck Colors, Score and MVP(s):** 4-1 with Bant. I loved [Roil Spout](https://web.archive.org/web/20151025000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roil+Spout).  
**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze* Champion and 2 other Pro Tour Top 8s. 8 Grand Prix Top 8s.

**What was your most memorable moment this weekend?**  
 The Chinese team was overjoyed when we scooped them into the Top 4 as we were about to beat them.

**Anything nice to say to your teammates?**  
 They're two of the best Limited players of all time!
