Pro Tour–Austin is just around the bend, and with it all professional eyes have turned their attention towards the Extended format. Itilgor used this version of All-In Red to do well in a recent event on **Magic Online**. The deck's game plan is to ritual out a large threat and/or a form of mana disruption in [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20090916000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) and [Magus of the Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20090916000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magus+of+the+Moon). Will it remain a potent player at the Pro Tour in Texas? Tune in to the live coverage in October to find out!
