He was playing a mono-red burn deck with a smidgen of critters and had quietly compiled an 8-1 record. </p>&#10;      <p>His opponent this round was Ai Shimoji. Ai is part of a wave of Japanese Magic girlfriends who do more than just come to the event and root on their beaus. In fact, Ai was playing on Day Two while her boyfriend Ryuchi Arita cheered her on from the sidelines. Ai has been smacking people around all weekend with Black Hand. </p>&#10;      <p>In Game 1, Hard Bitten kept a one land hand that featured three <a>Frostling</a>s and a <a>Pithing Needle</a>. He was also holding a pair of burn spells which began to loom larger when Ai crossed off three of her own life to play a <a>Raving Oni-Slave</a>. Hard Bitten began to draw into land and the <a>Hidetsugu's Second Rite</a> lurked in the weeds like a tripwire. </p>&#10;      <p>Ai had no idea what was coming. She had the opportunity to take her turn in two directions with four mana available and on 12 life. She could have used <a>Distress</a> to see a hand of <a>Flames of the Blood Hand</a> and the Second Rite. Had she gone down that route she could have taken the Flames and played a second Oni-Slave to effectively negate the Second Rite by going to 9. Instead she tapped out to play the ginormous <a>Razorjaw Oni</a>. </p>&#10;      <p>Hard Bitten looked over at the number 12 written under Ai's column on his scoresheet, at the Second Rite he was holding, at the three mana on his board, and finally at the top of his deck pleading for a land. Ai seemed to have no idea what was coming but Ryuchi winced in anticipation hoping her foe would not rip a <a>Mountain</a>. </p>&#10;      <p>Hard Bitten attacked for two, laid the land, and handed Ai the <a>Hidetsugu's Second Rite</a> to read.</p>&#10;      <p>In Game 2 Ai had a land heavy draw with few threats. A pair of <a>Hearth Kami</a>'s made her Jitte useless and <a>Glacial Ray</a> hitched a ride onto a couple of <a>Lava Spike</a>s and a Blind With Anger before <a>Genju of the Spires</a> closed the deal. </p>&#10;      <img src="http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/images/gptai05/b_red2.jpg">&#10;      <p>I asked Hard Bitten about the deck after the match. He chose to play the deck after seeing it at some Taiwanese PTQs. The only problem is that it was in the hands of a formidable opponent. </p>&#10;      <p>Gifts has proven to be very beatable this weekend but generally when it is in the hands of a weaker player. Royce is one of the more formidable Singaporean players with a handful of GP Top8s and plenty of PT experience.
