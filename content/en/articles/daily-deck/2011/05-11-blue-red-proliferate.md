Ken Nagle, Wizards R&D member, used this deck for spellslinging at the *New Phyrexia* Prerelease this past weekend. How does it work? Says Ken, "This deck defends itself with Contagion Clasps and Tumble Magnets until a planeswalker, a Shrine of Burning Rage, or an Inkmoth Nexus poison counter gets enough proliferation to kill the opponent." Ken's favorite *New Phyrexia* art is Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite, and you can follow him on his Twitter feed, @[NorrYtt](https://web.archive.org/web/20110511000000/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/twitter.com/NorrYtt). Interested in playing the set yourself? It goes on sale [this weekend](/en/events/2008/release-facts/worldwide-launch-parties)!
