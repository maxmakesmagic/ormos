The 2009 **Magic** Game Day was a smashing success! All over the world players tried their hands at the new world of *Zendikar* Standard. In Austria, Paul Gelautz opted to battle with Jund, but with an added twist: discard creatures. A twin-set of Specters of the Sedraxis and Hypnotic variety helped keep an opponent's hand devoid of answers while Madrush Cyclops helped make the entire team a turn faster by granting all Paul's creatures haste.
