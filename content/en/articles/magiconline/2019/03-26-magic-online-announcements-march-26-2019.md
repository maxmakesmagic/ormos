## Legacy Cube Begins Tomorrow

Do you like that one card, from that one set, and you just never see it anymore? Fear not, *Legacy Cube* starts tomorrow, March 27, and you may see it again! *Legacy Cube* is full of some of the best cards from across Magic's history, working together in ways you can only experience in a Cube. And the fun doesn't stop for nearly a full month, ending April 25.

For more information on this update of the *Legacy Cube*, check out [designer Paul Cheon's article](/en/articles/magiconline/2019/03-19-march-2019-legacy-cube-overhaul).

* * *

## Vintage Super League Finals!

After nine weeks of fierce competition, only two players are left standing: Rachel Agnes and Andreas Petersen. Each player will submit three decks and then battle to be the first to win three matches. Check out the action starting at 6 p.m. PT on [twitch.tv/magic](https://www.twitch.tv/magic)!

Ready to start playing? [Grab a deck](https://web.archive.org/web/20190326000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/deck-lists-magic-online-products-game-info) and join the Vintage League for matches any time or play for greater rewards with the Vintage Challenge every Saturday at 10 a.m. PT!

## Limited MCQ Finals Saturday

On Saturday, March 30, there will be a Limited MCQ Finals at 7 a.m. PT. The set will be *Ravnica Allegiance*, and the event will cost 1 Limited MCQ Finals Token. The winner will receive an invitation to Mythic Championship Barcelona.

Learn more about Mythic Championship Qualifier events [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20190326000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-mythic-championship-qualifiers).

* * *

## Vintage Playoff Event Saturday

This Saturday, March 30, there will be a Format Playoff on *Magic Online*. It will begin at 10 a.m. PT, and the format will be Vintage. The event requires 35 Format Points in the event's format to enter. Top winners receive prizes along with an invitation to the associated format's Championship.

Learn more about Format Playoffs [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20190326000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2019-magic-online-championship-series#FormatPlayoffs2019).

* * *

## Modern MCQ Finals Sunday

On Sunday, March 31, there will be a Modern Mythic Qualifier Finals at 9 a.m. PT. The event will cost 30 Event Tickets or 300 Play Points, and the winner will receive an invitation to Mythic Championship Barcelona.

Learn more about Mythic Championship Qualifier events [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20190326000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-mythic-championship-qualifiers).

* * *

## Pauper Playoff Event Sunday

This Sunday, March 31, there will be a Format Playoff on *Magic Online*. It will begin at 10 a.m. PT, and the format will be Pauper. The event requires 35 Format Points in the event's format to enter. Top winners receive prizes along with an invitation to the associated format's Championship.

Learn more about Format Playoffs [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20190326000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/content/2019-magic-online-championship-series#FormatPlayoffs2019).

## Build Notes

General | [Death Pit Offering](https://web.archive.org/web/20190326000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Death+Pit+Offering) has been reigned in and no longer causes you to sacrifice all of your permanents.

Standard | If [Nullhide Ferox](https://web.archive.org/web/20190326000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nullhide+Ferox) is granted riot and its activated ability is activated, its controller is rightly able to cast noncreature spells.

General | Creatures that are able to transform now have the summoning sickness animation when they are appropriately sick.

General | Vehicles such as [Smuggler's Copter](https://web.archive.org/web/20190326000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Smuggler%27s+Copter) are now affected by anthem effects such as [Trostani Discordant](https://web.archive.org/web/20190326000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Trostani+Discordant).

Legacy, Modern | [Laboratory Maniac](https://web.archive.org/web/20190326000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Laboratory+Maniac) is no longer able to maniacally end the game with cards in your library and a card with dredge such as [Golgari Thug](https://web.archive.org/web/20190326000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Golgari+Thug) in your graveyard.

General | [The Scarab God](https://web.archive.org/web/20190326000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Scarab+God) now correctly applies Rule 706.9d which means to say that an [Enigma Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20190326000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enigma+Drake) will be a 4/4 creature no matter what cards are in your graveyard.
