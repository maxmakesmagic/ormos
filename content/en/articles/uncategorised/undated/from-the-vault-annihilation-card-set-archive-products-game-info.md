## FROM THE VAULT: ANNIHILATION PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/tcg/products/v14/EN_GAMEINFO_PRODUCTS_PRODUCTARCHIVEHUB_Icon_V14_140619.png) These limited-edition, black-bordered super weapons are legal in many tournament formats. Sweep the battlefield clean with this powerful arsenal and blast the opposition into oblivion.

**Set Name:** *From The Vault: Annihilation*  
**Number of Cards:** 15 premium foil cards, including 6 with new art  
**Includes:** An exclusive Spindown™ life counter and collector's guide  
**Details:** Each card has been printed using a foil process unique to the *From the Vault* series.  
**Languages:** English  
**Official Three-Letter Code:** V14  
**Twitter Hashtag:** [#MTGANH](http://twitter.com/hashtag/mtganh)

**Product Concept and Development:** Gavin Verhey and Jeremy Jarvis
