Sometimes, you want a cleaver. For example, [Ogre's Cleaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20100617000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ogre%27s+Cleaver) from *Rise of the Eldrazi*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6e/5e/6e5e20b07b4972fd85a4a7180e64db42797e063dcb0746a9f17d30de7113f556.jpg)

[Ogre's Cleaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20100617000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ogre%27s+Cleaver) | Art by [Adi Granov](https://web.archive.org/web/20100617000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Adi+Granov%22%5D)

That's a powerful weapon. In fact, it looks like it might be too large for the elf (not a human, even though that's what this paragraph originally said) holding it. It would look better in the hands of an ogre, which makes a certain amount of sense. And this, of course, is where we show you a very similar cleaver as it appeared in the art of an ogre in an earlier set!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a9/d7/a9d7c6ca238faa2360c38384ee7cffe1e96827ee3a51d7e3b2900f943191c125.jpg)
