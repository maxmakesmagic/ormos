# Deck of the Day

1.  The Future of Jeskai in Modern?

With the banning of Splinter Twin, Jeskai-flavored decks are going to start looking elsewhere for inspiration. While Jeskai Ascendancy still exists as a viable combo option, that deck hasn't really proven itself a player on the Modern scene. But maybe LORDMORPETH has the answer? The *Magic Online* player recently finished [5-0 in a Modern League](/en/events/2016/modern-constructed-league-2016-01-17/modern-constructed-league) with a pretty pure control deck featuring cards such as Tidings, Ajani Vengeant, and Supreme Verdict. Now able to better tap out with impunity from turn four on, could decks like this go further? Jace, Architect of Thought? Chandra, Pyromaster? Mulldrifter?!
