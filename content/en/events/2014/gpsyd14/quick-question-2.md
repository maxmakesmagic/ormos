 **What's the most underrated common in *Magic 2015* sealed deck?** 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/01_QQ_TheBenSeck.jpg)

 ***The Ben Seck:** "[Bronze Sable](https://web.archive.org/web/20140822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bronze+Sable), especially good against creatures with intimidate."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/01_QQ_SimonHarnden.jpg)

 ***Simon Harnden:** "[Forge Devil](https://web.archive.org/web/20140822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forge+Devil) for the 2 for 1's on 1 toughness creatures."* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/01_QQ_HuangHaoShan.jpg)

 ***Huang Hao-Shan:** "[Heliod's Pilgrim](https://web.archive.org/web/20140822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Heliod%27s+Pilgrim)."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/01_QQ_MattRogers.jpg)

 ***Matt Rogers:** "[Marked by Honor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Marked+by+Honor)."* 

  
  

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/01_QQ_KelvinChew.jpg)

 ***Kelvin Chew:** "[Festergloom](https://web.archive.org/web/20140822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Festergloom), there's a lot of X/1's in the format."* 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpsyd14/01_QQ_JasonChung.jpg)

 ***Jason Chung:** "[Sanctified Charge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sanctified+Charge). People already like it, but it's even better than people think."*
