*What's on your [**Magic: The Gathering** Fantasy Pro Tour](http://apps.facebook.com/magicfantasyprotour/) roster, and why?*

**Billy Moreno:** 

> **Planeswalker**: [Garruk Relentless](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Relentless)  
> **Large Creature**: [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan)  
> **Medium Creature**: [Phyrexian Metamorph](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Metamorph)  
> **Small Creature**: [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage)  
> **Instant**: [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak)  
> **Sorcery**: [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder)  
> **Enchantment** : [Honor of the Pure](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Honor+of+the+Pure)  
> **Artifact**: [Sphere of the Suns](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphere+of+the+Suns)  
> **Nonbasic Land**: [Seachrome Coast](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seachrome+Coast)  
> **Pro Tour Player**: Patrick Chapin

Quick rundown of my picks:

> *   [Garruk Relentless](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Relentless) might be the most powerful planeswalker in Standard; it is also one of the easiest to cast and fits in with aggro and control plans.
> *   [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan) has been the defining fatty for the entire time it's been around and I don't expect that to change now.
> *   People love Titans and discounts and breaking the color pie—[Phyrexian Metamorph](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Metamorph) does all three while answering [Thrun, the Last Troll](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thrun%2C+the+Last+Troll) and comboing well with [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod).
> *   Admittedly, [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage) is much better in older formats with [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm) and [Ancestral Recall](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ancestral+Recall) to flashback; that doesn't mean it can't be the most powerful card in Standard.
> *   [Seachrome Coast](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seachrome+Coast), [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak), and [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder) are along for the ride in [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage)– and [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets)–powered decks. Of course, [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) and [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder) get played in the controlling blue decks as well.
> *   There are other enchantment options, but [Honor of the Pure](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Honor+of+the+Pure) is the only one that is always a four-of in the decks it's in.
> *   Various swords, pikes, and living weapons are the high-profile artifact options, but [Sphere of the Suns](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphere+of+the+Suns) is a key cog in blue-based control decks and green-based ramp decks.
> *   Finally, Patrick Chapin himself informed me that he hasn't lost a Standard match in three years, so that seemed like an easy pick.
