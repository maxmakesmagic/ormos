Many people—okay, pretty much everyone—took note of the two special *Khans of Tarkir* tri-lands in [*Duel Decks: Speed vs. Cunning*](/en/articles/feature/2014/08-18-duel-decks-speed-vs-cunning), with the clan symbol watermarks, and extrapolated that we'd have one for each of the wedges. Since you're all very smart people, you were (naturally) correct. I give you...all five tri-lands from *Khans of Tarkir*.
