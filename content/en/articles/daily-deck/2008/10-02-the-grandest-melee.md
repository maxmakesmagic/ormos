Fans of the Grand Melee format will be excited to see Dylan Austin’s decklist from GenCon 2008’s Grand Melee tournament. He bested over a hundred other players to repeat as champion from 2007! Using a combination of Rites of Flourishing, Crucible of Worlds, and Walk the Aeons Austin attempted to take an arbitrarily large amount of turns, then kill his opponents with Oona, Queen of the Fae. The plan worked, and he emerged victorious! Find out which cards from the exciting new set Shards of Alara you could add to Dylan’s deck with the [Shards of Alara Visual Spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20090601212653/https://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/shardsofalara/spoiler).
