### 

Last week, we introduced a pack with a voluminous quantity of first picks, and the readers' votes were split relatively evenly among them. Click [here](/en/articles/event-coverage/2003/05-06-limited-information-4) to view last week's edition.

Most of you selected Lavamancer's Skill, Solar Blast, or Whipcorder, with Stag Beetle, Pacifism, and Wellwisher also receiving a relevant number of votes. It seems the conclusion was that Lavamancer's Skill is powerful enough to warrant the inherent risk in selecting what is essentially a gold card first pick. Clearly the public notion is that Whipcorder is better than Pacifism, but not a better first pick than Lavamancer's Skill or Solar Blast. The professionals came to a different conclusion.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/a6/b8/a6b8eb6877068370e8faaf6b94b92b5b5627aa9216aa131925a53df0dc4b8b57.jpg)**Kai Budde**: I'd pick Stag Beetle. There is only one good green card and the remaining pack is going to put the drafters behind me into white-X and red-X with one going red-blue. If it works out that way, you should get some good green in the second pack. If you manage to also cut off the black, you are on your way to a good green-black deck. I am not a huge fan of white. I don't think it's worth fighting for with so many good cards meaning that there will be at least three white players at the table. I don't like to first pick Lavamancer's Skill. 

**Antonino de Rosa**: Well let's try to analyze this pack (warning you that I did go winless in Yokohama). I love white more than most, but I wouldn't touch it since there are three great white cards. You don't want the guy to your left to get the fourth pick 3/3 flier and think it's a signal. I never first pick Lavamancer's Skill, since it commits you to two colors. So the real question is do I take Stag Beetle or Solar Blast. I think Stag Beetle is very good, but since removal is very hard to come by, I would pick Solar Blast and keep my eyes open for a red-black deck since you aren't passing any black cards.

**Frank Karsten**: I don't think there is one "right" pick in this pack, it really only comes down to personal preference. With this in mind, I would take Stag Beetle. My drafting strategies are really awkward; I try to avoid red and black, the colors people try to get their hands on. I wouldn't take a white card because there are already three white cards and that sends a strange signal. If you prefer white, I think the Whipcorder is the best pick. Stag Beetle is a very good card too, about the same power level as Barkhide Mauler. I'm not really sure if it is worth it only based on the power levels on the cards, but this is a pack with a lot of good cards in several of colors, and I have a preference for green. I would be happy with a g-u deck since (a) I really like the color combination and (b) you don't pass a single good green or blue card in this pack if you take the Beetle. Of course, that may all change when you see the second pack...

![](https://i.ormos.uk/4e/3b/4e3b63b4d615d21b8ac404a62ece36b8fe7d4657b724feb18322333a63d40805.jpg)**Ken Krouner**: When looking at a pack with such a high power level, there is much to take into consideration. First, what is the most powerful card in the pack? My vote here would be Whipcorder. I think this card shuts down a lot of strategies and fits in superbly with the aggressive nature of soldiers. The second thing to look at is which color is the most shallow. The answer here (besides blue which is invariably the shallowest) is black. However, the black cards are so weak that it is hardly worth considering. This moves next to green where Stag Beetle is a fine choice. I put these two cards at about equal in terms of making a good pick. The determining factor for me is the fact that green is currently overdrafted. Taking the Whipcorder reduces your chances of getting cut off in the first and third pack.

**Bob Maher**: I would take the Solar Blast. Passing the three white cards seems like it will put you in a good position on the way back. While the Lavamancer's Skill can be extremely powerful, the Solar Blast is the all-around better card because of the added flexibility it gives you. The only other thing I had to think about was the Stag Beetle. If you take the Beetle, the next five picks are probably white and red. Given that people avoid white-green, there would be three white-x players, one red-blue player and 1 red-x player. This would mean that there will be a lot of green on the way back. The problem is that you can not control what other people have opened and that you are trying to plan too far down the line. Beyond the two people on your left, most of the picks will be based on the power of the cards already in their stack, not the cards in the pack you opened. I would take the Solar Blast, and I would be leaning towards red-green or red-black, and definitely avoiding white. 

**Sol Malka**: I'm going to have to go with Stag Beetle. You're virtually assured that no one to your left will take a green card for at least a couple seats, and the Beetle is much more powerful in On/On/Le than in 3xOn because creature counts in decks are much higher. Since most of the other good cards in the pack (notwithstanding the Herald, much-improved with Legions) are white and red, you're in a good position for green-black, a very solid archetype (and not just in Constructed). 

**Gabriel Nassif**: I take Solar Blast because it is the best card and you can go for any second color with first pick Solar Blast. Taking a white card seems fine, because it is unlikely anyone after you is taking a white card with Solar Blast and Lavamancer's Skill left. However, you are still leaving two very good white and red cards, so you can't know for sure what's going to happen. Since things can work out very badly for you regardless of what you take, that makes the safe pick of Solar Blast even better.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6c/1b/6c1b231f1568ca57fb30380beceb972106202cd50d9180e6af1eef7123bbe2bb.jpg)**Bill Stead**: I would take the Solar Blast. In Yokohama, Chris Benafel was faced with a similar situation: Pacifism, Solar Blast, or Lavamancer's Skill. Chris ended up taking Skill and finished the draft with a mediocre red-blue wizard deck. After the draft, he vowed not to force himself into red-blue first pick (or white-blue splash red). Basically, Lavamancer's Skill is a better card than Solar Blast, but if you can't utilize it to its fullest, it is pretty much a 23rd pick. Solar Blast, on the other hand, is good in almost any deck as a solid removal spell or a great splash. Basically, if you are a gambling man, take the Skill and hope, because you are already shipping a lot of white and a Solar Blast, meaning that you're going to have some trouble with the people to your left (this is fine if you get lucky and the person to your right stays clear of your colors ). If not, take the safe pick in Solar Blast, because even if the person on your left cuts red, you will have a great splash.

**Victor van den Broek**: I'd take Whipcorder. I really like Whipcorder a lot, and I'd expect the guy to my left to take Solar Blast or Skill from the booster, because red is still insane. Even if he doesn't, I should be able to cut white from him and corner it.

**David Williams**: Solar Blast, the most powerful one-color card, is the obvious pick. Not only is it better than the white cards, but you avoid the white conflict. I don't like Stag Beetle that much. It's a giant fatty just like any other green card. It's almost the same as casting a Krosan Tusker, good but completely generic in comparison to what else the color has to offer. Removal is also hard to come by, so I jump at it over men. I don't think taking Stag Beetle with the intention of going green-black is a good idea because good green-black decks are determined by what's coming in packs one and three, as you have to have it coming in the Legions pack. You can't decide on two colors because you have no clue about that at this point; you might not even stick with your first pick. You can splash Solar Blast, but there's no splashing a big dumb man. By taking Solar Blast, you still have the option of going green-red because Stag Beetle might be taken anywhere down the line. If you take Stag Beetle, Solar Blast will nearly always go second, and you hurt yourself pack two if you go green-red, so you force yourself to go green-black. If the miser on your right is black, you end up with no deck. 

**Gary Wise**: While my initial response was to take Whipcorder, which I think is the most powerful card in the pack, my opinion has since changed. First, my allegiance wavered between Whipcorder and Pacifism because of the latter's more generic nature lending well to both clerics and soldiers, but then I decided drafting white from this pack would be a mistake. One of the main reasons I like picking the white card is because the color is underdrafted, getting stronger as the packs progress, but in this case, with three very good white cards in the pack, you know white isn't going to be underdrafted in this particular draft. As a result, I think the choices are Solar Blast and Stag Beetle, the two best remaining cards in the pack (I really dislike first pick Skill... committing to two colors when you don't have to seems dumb to me). Blast is the better of the two, though the Beetle can be argued for because it's the only green card in the pack and you should want to be drafting green more than red in a format where half the first picks are red. Ask me tomorrow and I might say Blast, but as I write this, Beetle feels like the better pick to me.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b0/d1/b0d1bbf85aba59c15fe26b914b08732ffa23d122c3b09af86a9451294d52ca98.jpg)**Mark Wraith**: I would definitely take Skill out of this pack. Firstly, I consider red-blue to be the best possible color combination, and secondly, despite the Solar Blast, I think it's a good signal. Imagine you got passed that pack, but with the Skill missing. Which common could I possibly have taken? With a Solar Blast still there, I would say the only commons superior are Shock, Sparksmith, possibly Cruel Revival, and Snarling Undorak. Most green players would take Stag Beetle over the Undorak, which leaves two red cards, and a black card. So, even though there is still a Solar Blast there, it's still most likely that I took a red card. They would know that I certainly hadn't taken a green or a white card, which hopefully would encourage them to pick one of those. Presumably, they would take Stag Beetle. Finally, if the worst comes to the worst, although you are cut off in pack two, you still get first grabs at the very good blue cards in Legions: Keeneye Aven, Echo Tracer, and Mistform Seaswift.

**Mark Zajdner**: I believe that the pick in this pack is Whipcorder. Now at first glance this pack has lots of good cards and the pick doesn't seem so obvious. But when thinking about it, the Gravel Slinger will most likely round the table putting your soldier deck in position to be very strong after one pack. I don't like to take the Skill first because that means you commit to blue-red and if no blue comes, you have wasted your pick. With Whipcorder, you get a solid start to any white deck and give yourself the option to draft tribally.

According to the public, Lavamancer's Skill, Solar Blast, Whipcorder, Pacifism, Stag Beetle, and Wellwisher all have first pick potential. The first step is to determine if any cards can be eliminated from one's decision based on abstract value. Wellwisher can be ruled out immediately, as it is strictly worse than Stag Beetle. For that matter, if one was forced to take Wirewood Herald or Wellwisher first pick, the correct choice would be the Herald. This comes down to a lack of information as to how tribally focused your deck will be. Herald will be good if you end the draft with a couple other good elves, while in that situation, Wellwisher will be mediocre at best. The next dilemma is Whipcorder vs. Pacifism. While Gary brings up a good point about Whipcorder pushing you in the soldier direction while Pacifism leaves you open to soldiers or clerics, I nevertheless think Pacifism can be ruled out. Whipcorder is just much more powerful in the abstract, as it still possesses a great deal of potency in a cleric deck or a non-tribal white deck. 

We're left with Lavamancer's Skill, Solar Blast, Whipcorder, and Stag Beetle. Now one has to examine what impact each selection will have on the expected value of forthcoming picks. Lavamancer's Skill is the most powerful card if one goes blue-red and marginally playable if one does not. So it follows that Lavamancer's Skill is a blue-red gold card. If you are in a position to draft blue-red, you have made the best selection, and every piece of information you have in the pack indicates that pack two will go well for that archetype. However, if the person feeding you has also moved into blue-red, you will at the very least have to give up your first pick entirely, and you may also suffer for having to move into green or white. It's also entirely possible that the player to your left takes Solar Blast after his own first pick Skill or blue card, in which case you are still in a suboptimal color combination. The next most powerful card is Solar Blast, which has a lot going for it. It's a removal spell with inherent value in any red-x combination, even if red is a splash color. One's biggest concern is that the Skill will be taken shortly afterwards and that the next pack's value will suffer consequently (particularly so if you go blue-red after taking the Blast). A similarly powerful card in the abstract is Whipcorder, but this pick is incredibly dangerous with the other three white cards in the pack. Depending on what others have picked before they see your pack, you might wheel the Gravel Slinger, or you might be sharing white with three other players. That's the difference between a 4-0 and a 0-4 draft deck. Stag Beetle is largely considered to be the worst of the four in terms of raw value, yet four of the pros select it. The reasoning here is that a green card will not get selected for at least four picks, and that either green-black or green-blue will be wide open in the second pack. Fans of these archetypes select Stag Beetle because they believe this archetype is most likely to net good cards in this situation. Again, this puts a great deal of emphasis on one's ability to be able to discern what will happen in the draft based on one's own first pack.

When Bob Maher and Kai Budde disagree on a draft pick, it's clearly a tough decision. Unsurprisingly, I think the decision comes down to Solar Blast vs. Stag Beetle. As much faith as I have in the blue-red archetype, and as powerful as Whipcorder is, these two picks are both based upon an overestimation of one's ability to predict the way cards will fall in a draft. This type of consideration is definitely relevant, but one has to remember that other people are being swayed by what they open as well. In this situation, one has to turn down the risk associated with Whipcorder or Lavamancer's Skill, as your draft could be ruined based on surrounding people's first picks. While I feel Solar Blast and Stag Beetle are both worthwhile picks, Bob and Dave make excellent points about the aforementioned predictability of a draft. Solar Blast is a powerful removal spell that will highlight your deck no matter how this unpredictable situation turns out, while Stag Beetle still hopes for the green-black gates to open in the second Onslaught pack and Legions. Escape this erratic situation with the most consistent pick: Solar Blast.

This week we'll explore a decision inspired by a debate I witnessed between Kai Budde and William Jensen. It is once again On/On/Le Booster Draft, and you do not know anything about the players around you. You are playing blue-white, and you have no illusions (Mistform Dreamer, etc.) in your deck thus far. You have just opened the Legions pack. With only this information at your disposal, which card do you draft?
