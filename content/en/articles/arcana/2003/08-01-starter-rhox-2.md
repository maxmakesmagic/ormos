The great monster Rhox from the *Nemesis* expansion has been reprinted in the Core Set with its original art.

A rarer premium version of Rhox—with art based on how he was presented in television ads—was included in the [2000 Starter set](https://web.archive.org/web/20120415192114/http://www.wizards.com/magic/starter/starter/welcome.asp).
