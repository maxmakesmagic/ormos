If you're getting antsy to play *Scars of Mirrodin*, then here's good news: the [Launch Parties](https://web.archive.org/web/20100930000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/events/releases) start this Friday and run the whole weekend!

Obviously, the main lure is the fun of playing **Magic**. But there's also a special promo card that you get just by showing up and playing! While supplies last, that is.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7c/81/7c81ef0fb386f36597caf4ecc026e8db07d2db366e679fbcf72eeb983f931298.jpg)  

That's right, it's a special shiny promo [Steel Hellkite](https://web.archive.org/web/20100930000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steel+Hellkite)! It doesn't look that shiny there, so we went for an angle that's a little more ... *dramatic*.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/21/2c/212cfff663034de3ac63cf9e9a529e2e2932e25e1c95e45bbb07568c04b409de.jpg)  

October 1-4. *Scars of Mirrodin* Launch Parties. Be there!
