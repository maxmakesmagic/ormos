Hollywood, USA is the second stop in the 2008 Pro Tour season. But the pros won't be the only ones enjoying themselves: the Pro Tour is above all a great weekend of **Magic** that brings fantastic opportunities for all players, whether you're qualified for the main event, participating in public events, playing against the people who make **Magic**, or just hanging around with your friends.

Pro Tour–Hollywood is a Standard format event that runs May 23-25, 2008. The Last-Chance Qualifier will be held Thursday at 4 p.m. at the Hyatt Regency Century Plaza. For more information, visit the complete [Public Events schedule](/en/events/2008/hollywood08-public/pro-tourhollywood-2008-public-events-schedule).

### Tournament Format and Schedule

Pro Tour-Hollywood is a three-day Standard format event with a prize purse of $230,795. For more information about the format, schedule, and prize payout, [click here](/en/events/2008/hollywood08-format/pro-tourhollywood-2008-format-schedule-and-prizes).

### Media Requests

Please contact Hélène Bergeot at helene.bergeot@wizards.com if you wish to set up a press credential for Pro Tour–Hollywood. Wizards asks that all media requests be submitted by May 16 (one week prior to the event).

### Site Attractions

**PUBLIC EVENTS**  
Come one, come all to participate in four days of events open to the public. Starting Thursday with the Last-Chance Qualifier, there are tournaments, drafts, and drawings all weekend. Prizes include invitations and airfare to Pro Tour-Berlin, a 160gb Apple TV, an iPod Touch, a set of 30 *Revised* Dual Lands, and a canvas **Magic** art print. Pro Tour-Hollywood also features Super Friday Night **Magic** and an unsanctioned 64-person free-for-all on Saturday night. 

*   **[Complete Public Events Schedule](/en/events/2008/hollywood08-public/pro-tourhollywood-2008-public-events-schedule)**

**GUNSLINGING**  
 Test your **Magic** skills against the guys who make the game Pro Tour–Hollywood 2008 will feature four members of **Magic** R&D: Aaron Forsythe, Mike Turian, Erik Lauer, and Kenneth Nagle. Win and you get to walk away with cool **Magic** prizes! 

**ARTISTS ON SITE**  
 Pro Tour–Hollywood 2008 will feature appearances by rk post and Rob Alexander. [Click here](/en/events/2008/hollywood08-artists/pro-tourhollywood-2008-artists) to read more about these artists. Check back here for additions to the artist schedule. 

**WATCH THE TOP 8**  
 All of Sunday's Top 8 action can be viewed screens in the hall, with Randy Buehler and Brian David-Marshall providing the play-by-play and color commentary. Sunday's action begins at 10:45 a.m. local time, which is 1:45 p.m. ET, and is available as a [webcast](/en/events/2004/webcast/magic-the-gathering-webcast-schedule) from the comfort of your own home.
