Hi everyone! We've got a short week as we wind down the rest of the year in premier play. Today we've got some Regional Pro Tour Qualifier (RPTQ) Top 8 decklists for you, along with a look at what's on the horizon this weekend.

# RPTQ Top 8 Decklists

Last weekend, sixteen stores around the world hosted Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers (RPTQs) that qualify the Top 4 finishers for [Pro Tour *Aether Revolt*](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-aether-revolt-event-information) in Dublin, Ireland, February 3–5, 2017. The remaining sixteen stores from the 32 in total this round will be hosting RPTQs this Sunday, December 11, all around the world. In addition, two *Magic Online* RPTQs have yet to take place. One happens December 10 at 2 p.m. PT, and the other one takes place December 17 at 5 a.m. PT.

The format for this round of Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers is Modern Constructed.

# This Weekend!

Here's a quick rundown of events that are going on this weekend in the world of Organized Play:

**RPTQs**: As mentioned above, the remaining in-store Regional Pro Tour Qualifiers for Pro Tour *Aether Revolt* take place this Sunday all around the world. Look for more Modern decklists next week!

**Grand Prix Milwaukee**: Anyone in North America looking for one last big Limited event will likely be found at [Grand Prix Milwaukee](/en/events/2016/gpmil16/grand-prix-milwaukee-2016-fact-sheet-magic-the-gathering) this weekend. Marc Calderaro and Corbin Hosler will be bringing you the latest as our writer-reporters for the event.

**Standard Showdown**: Week 3 of the Standard Showdown takes place on Saturday in game stores worldwide. Looking for your chance to showcase what you've got in Standard Constructed and a shot at some prize boosters that may have all kinds of awesome? Then head to your [local participating game store](https://web.archive.org/web/20161208000000/http://locator.wizards.com/) on Saturday to participate!

To those of you playing in an event this weekend, best of luck!
