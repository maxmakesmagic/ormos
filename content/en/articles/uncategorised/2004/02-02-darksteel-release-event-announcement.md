### Leagues, Draft Queues, And Set Rotation

It's also time to start thinking about the new *Darksteel* leagues and when the set will rotate into formats. 

*   Traditional four-week *Mirrodin-*block leagues including *Darksteel* cards will begin Monday, March 8 at 9:00 AM (0900 hours) Pacific Standard Time when we stop taking signups for Release Leagues.  
  

*   *Mirrodin-*block draft queues including *Darksteel* cards will begin at the same time as the Release Leagues--Thursday, March 4 after our regularly scheduled downtime. The *Darksteel* set officially rotates into all Limited formats at the same time.  
  

*   On Monday, March 15 (two weeks after it becomes available online), the *Darksteel* set will officially rotate into the Standard Constructed format.

**\***The cost of the leagues were originally incorrectly posted as using only two *Darksteel* boosters instead of three. --Dan
