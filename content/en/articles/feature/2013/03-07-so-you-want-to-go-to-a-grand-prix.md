More people than ever are going to Grand Prix events. And you could be one of them! Here's how:

![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/themes/wiz_mtg/img/interface/video-place-holder.png)[.](http://www.youtube.com/embed/fiakR7NgtF0)

 

 

And now that you know all about how to attend a Grand Prix, you might be interested in knowing that there are Grand Prix events in [Verona, Italy](/en/events/2012/verona13/grand-prix-verona-event-information), and [Rio de Janeiro](/en/events/2013/riodejaneiro13/grand-prix-rio-de-janeiro-event-information-2) this weekend. In fact, let's just take a look at the whole schedule for the rest of the year.
