Darwin Kastle and the crew from team Your Move Games dominated the Extended Pro Tour scene at the turn of the century. Before they finished in the top three slots of Pro Tour–Houston, Kastle joined teammate and fellow Hall of Famer Dave Humpherys in the Top 8 of Pro Tour–New Orleans 2001. Both did it with their Reanimator decks, able to power out a super fatty as early as the second turn. [Read more about the event in the official event coverage archives](https://web.archive.org/web/20090923000000/http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/event.asp?event=ptnor01).
