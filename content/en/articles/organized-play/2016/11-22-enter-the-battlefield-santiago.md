*Enter the Battlefield* heads to South America for the very first time, as documentary producers Nathan Holt and Shawn Kornhauser discover the vibrant atmosphere of Latin America's *Magic* community in a two-part adventure into Santiago, Chile.

There, they talk to locals of the Santiago community, World *Magic* Cup teams, Argentinian national champion Luis Salvatto, and the Brazilian *Magic* legend Carlos Romao, as the two introduce the world to the energetic world of Latin American *Magic*.
