Grab a deck and be a part of the 35th anniversary of Origins Game Fair on June 23-27 in Columbus, Ohio. With five full days of **Magic: The Gathering** tournaments and events, there's sure to be something for you.

The highlights of the **Magic** schedule include a Pro Tour–Amsterdam qualifier, three generic Grand Prix Trials, Super Friday Night **Magic**, and single-elimination four-person *Archenemy* tournaments. Prizes across all events include an Xbox gaming system, a Nintendo Wii gaming system, a Nintendo DSi, iPod shuffles, complete foil sets of *Zendikar*, *Worldwake*, *Rise of the Eldrazi*, ***Magic** 2010*, and more.

Origins Game Fair is held at the Greater Columbus Convention Center. For more information about the full schedule of **Magic: The Gathering** events at Origins, visit [Professional Event Services](http://www.professional-events.com/Magic/Origins10/). For more information about Origins, visit [the official Origins web site](http://www.originsgamefair.com/Home/tabid/2737/Default.aspx).
