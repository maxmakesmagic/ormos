*What's your favorite fatty, and why?* 

![Kenneth Nagle](https://i.ormos.uk/73/dc/73dc2c209bef80978eea09781f3075d348fa6472480299108af0945ff889dce7.jpg "Kenneth Nagle")

**Kenneth Nagle:** My favorite fatty is Mossbridge Troll. During *Shadowmoor* design, we wanted mechanically linked rare creatures to show what iconics were hiding away as Mosswort Bridge, Windbrisk Heights, [and friends](https://web.archive.org/web/20100126000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?text=+[hideaway]) in *Lorwyn*.

We had a few designs:

*   Mossbridge Troll costs 5 less if you control any number of creatures with combined power 10 or more.
*   When Mossbridge Troll enters the battlefield, \[bonus\]. If it was hidden away by a card named Mosswort Bridge \[insane bonus\].

But in the end, we went with a somewhat loose cycle with the goal that "you should play the creature and the hideaway card in the same deck." We gave each creature a conditional ability similar to its hideaway land. I might have designed all five of these at a working lunch; they are definitely my kind of card designs.

Mossbridge Troll sports the biggest size bonus in the game. Since [every Troll in **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20100126000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?type=+[troll]%7C%7Csubtype=+[troll]) regenerates (or persists), we cut trample and added super-regeneration.
