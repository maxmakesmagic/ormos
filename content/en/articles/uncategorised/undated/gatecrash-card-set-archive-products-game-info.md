## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/00/f1/00f11ed5cc5898647a3c07d566b643b3882a7e7fcaf80b3c08df40553d862ca7.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare, mythic rare, or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/53/9b/539b167030babac227989a6adca677c5a8d8f17de9f4fd93943bf7c685e97e0e.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide with pictures of all cards in the pack as well as two full core set deck boxes, nine 15-card booster packs, 80 basic land cards, and a special-edition life counter.

(\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/05/a6/05a6a2fab43766d847c134d00bc3cce3a0ad994a89d49ccaf8fb5416b2ccbe5b.png)

### INTRO PACKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 cards, a foil premium rare card, two sealed 15-card booster packs, a strategy insert, and a **Magic** learn-to-play guide.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044508/http://magic.wizards.com/node/164821)

(\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b6/d9/b6d988efd187bc083c156ff1435e910ef677569b45fab4858a80409254d65755.png)

### BOOSTER BATTLE PACKS

Want to play with a friend right now? Just choose which of the two 22-card decks you’ll each play, insert up to five cards from the two included booster packs—and begin! It’s that easy.

(\* Not available in all languages)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/97/bb/97bb211cf213d66768e3a7b25ea38ebeeaa9081a344ea4829ec470607a4ede2b.png)

### EVENT DECKS

Battle like the best, right out of the box. Includes a tournament-ready 60-card deck plus a 15-card “sideboard” to swap in as needed, depending on what you expect your competitor will play.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160719044508/http://magic.wizards.com/node/164826)

(\* Not available in all languages)
