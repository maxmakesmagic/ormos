<table>&#10;  <tbody><tr>&#10;    <td>&#10;      &#10;    </td>&#10;  </tr>&#10;  <tr>&#10;    <td>&#10;      <b>The Grand Prix Experience</b>&#10;    </td>&#10;  </tr>&#10;</tbody></table>

Grand Prix are easily the biggest open events the DCI offers. They're high-energy and held around the world. Plus, no invitation is necessary – anyone can attend. No pro points? No problem. Playing a good game is all that matters, and with Pro Tour invitations down to 16th place and cash prizes all the way down to 64th place, one thing is certain: a Grand Prix is sure-fire great time. Plus each participant gets an alternate art Chrome Mox! 

Players or teams with high ratings or Pro Players Club levels can earn [byes for Grand Prix tournaments](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-bye-information). You can also earn byes at [Grand Prix Trials](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/grand-prix-trial-tournament-series-3).

To view **the 2009 Grand Prix schedule**, [click here](#1).

### Grand Prix Prizes

Each Grand Prix tournament has a prize purse of $30,000, with a first-place prize of $3,500. For a complete prize breakdown, [click here](/en/events/2002/prizes/worldwide-grand-prix-prize-structure-2). 

You can also earn [Pro Points](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/magic-the-gathering-pro-points-structure) by finishing high at a Grand Prix. These points count toward your [Pro Players Club levels](/en/events/2006/playersclub/pro-players-club-guidelines-and-procedures) and in the Pro Tour Player of the Year Standing.
