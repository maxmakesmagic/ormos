# What Is the *Magic Online* Bug Blog?

The "Bug Blog" is a running list of updates, alongside a list of *Magic Online* known issues. Here you'll find a list of emergent new issues, details around issue statuses when applicable, known issue workarounds, a running tally of known issues updates, as well as the full Known Issues List. The Bug Blog posts to [MTGO.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/game-info/products/magic-online) every other Thursday.

Want to find out when something was fixed? Check out the [Fixed Known Issues Archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-fixed-known-issues-archive).

Expect the next Bug Blog on Thursday, March 24, 2016!

[REPORT A BUG](http://wizards.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/668/~/magic-online%3A-report-an-issue)

# Known Issues List Updates

We've done a bit of a reformat of the bug blog and the known issues list. Sections are now grouped differently, focused around being able to filter and search the comprehensive known issues list. We hope that these changes make the bug blog and the known issues list a better resource for players. As always, we'll be listening to your feedback.

The Known Issues List below has been updated, so refer to that for the most up-to-date statuses on *Magic Online* known issues. If you want to see the history of fixed known issues, refer to the [archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://magic.wizards.com/en/content/magic-online-fixed-known-issues-archive).

## Change Log

# Newly Added Known Issues

**Cardset**

*   If a player casts [Predict](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Predict) or [Foreshadow](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Foreshadow), accurately naming one name of a split card (such as [Fire & Ice](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fire+%2F%2F+Ice)) put into the graveyard by the spell, the player will only draw one card instead of two.
*   Players are currently unable to choose to have [Gemstone Cavern](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gemstone+Cavern) start the game on the battlefield.
*   [Hallowed Moonlight](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hallowed+Moonlight) incorrectly fails to prevent [Kalitas, Traitor of Ghet](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kalitas%2C+Traitor+of+Ghet) from creating [Zombie](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Zombie) tokens.
*   Cardset | If [Vesuva](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vesuva) copies a land with a static ability and then is later turned into a basic land (perhaps by a [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon) or [Spreading Seas](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spreading+Seas)), that static ability will not be removed. Triggered and activated abilities are correctly removed.
*   [Blood Funnel](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Funnel) does not allow its controller to sacrifice a creature during the resolution of its triggered ability.
*   If an Aura becomes attached to a [Bramble Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bramble+Elemental) that causes the Elemental to change controllers, the Elemental's triggered ability is incorrectly controlled by the previous controller of [Bramble Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bramble+Elemental), not the new controller of [Bramble Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bramble+Elemental). Notably for this Limited format, this interaction may occur with [Dream Leash](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dream+Leash).
*   [Gaze of the Gorgon](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gaze+of+the+Gorgon)'s delayed triggered ability fails to destroy any creatures. It appears [Vraska](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=380214) may have lost her touch.

**Client and Gameplay**

*   Duel Scene | The "No Possible Play: Yield All" function, associated with the keyboard shortcut F8 by default, does not work correctly for a player who has a Convoke card in their hand.
*   Duel Scene | The summoning sickness whirl does not appear on the top token in a stack of summoning-sick creature tokens if one is tapped to help cast a Convoke spell.
*   Duel Scene | Unusual mana in a player's pool, such as mana from a snow permanent or mana with a rider attached (as that produced by [Cavern of Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cavern+of+Souls)) cannot be applied to a cost by clicking it. As a temporary workaround, players should produce this mana during the cost payment step in which they want to spend that mana.
*   Duel Scene | If a player makes a selection, then reverses that selection with the "undo" keyboard shortcut (default: CTRL+Z), the game log will sometimes incorrectly show both cards the player selected. Notably, this includes [Oath of Nissa](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oath+of+Nissa) and similar effects that ask a player to put one of a small number of cards into their hand.
*   Duel Scene | In certain rare scenarios, a bug may cause a game to restart. If this occurs, errors in the restarted game may cause cards to appear with incorrect watermarks, lands not appearing on the battlefield, or other visual issues. If you experience this issue, please contact Game Support.

# Fixes Deployed March 3

**Client and Gameplay**

*   Players can now choose to close the window that appears when blocking another player to cancel the reporting process. Additionally, players are no longer required to enter text into this window.
*   Decks purchased from the Store once again automatically create a decklist in the purchasing player’s Collection.
*   Several bugs not listed elsewhere that caused crashes have been resolved.

*   In a Commander game, mana abilities that produce mana based on their controller's commander's color identity, such as that found on [Commander's Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Commander%27s+Sphere) or [Command Tower](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Command+Tower), incorrectly produce no mana when tapped.
*   Players are currently unable to choose to have [Gemstone Cavern](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gemstone+Cavern) start the game on the battlefield.
*   When effects like [Parallax Wave](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Parallax+Wave) and [Exhume](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exhume) end and creatures return to the battlefield, if one of those creatures was a [Containment Priest](https://web.archive.org/web/20160310000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Containment+Priest) then all other creatures are exiled.

**Client and Gameplay**

*   Duel Scene | When casting with Convoke, creatures used for the ability have a green highlight for some turns after casting the spell.
