Let's talk about [Nirkana Cutthroat](https://web.archive.org/web/20100415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nirkana+Cutthroat) for a moment, if you don't mind. It's a creature from *Rise of the Eldrazi* with Level Up.

![Nirkana Cutthroat](https://i.ormos.uk/10/c4/10c402eb5331d41e3c0a16c15e0584274927a22a78b82b80cc128c4395fff023.jpg)

Notice anything familiar there? Perhaps this will help.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/mtg/images/daily/arcana/429_nirkanacutthroat.jpg)

Nirkana Cutthroat | Art by [Erica Yang](https://web.archive.org/web/20100415000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Erica+Yang%22%5D)

Do you see it? Something that would help a young vampire, just starting out on the path to high-level awesomeness?

The cutthroat is carrying a version of the [Blade of the Bloodchief](https://web.archive.org/web/20100415000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blade+of+the+Bloodchief)!
