## Manuel Bucher vs. Bertrand Fagnoni

![](https://i.ormos.uk/9e/e7/9ee7c9910fbc4827fd64007a587067471e478e5bf2597396955697874d0519bd.jpg)

The lone [Tooth and Nail](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tooth+and+Nail) player against the lone Red-Blue player in the Top 8. In a sea of Green with the two Affinity playing against each other one of the Swiss players faces on a French.

### Game 1

Fagnoni took a mulligan. Bucher was the first to blink with a turn two [Sylvan Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Scrying). On his fourth turn Manuel tried [Solemn Simulacrum](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Solemn+Simulacrum) but The French had [Condescend](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Condescend).

Fagnoni dug deep with Thirst of Knowledge discarding two [Electrostatic Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Electrostatic+Bolt)s before playing [Darksteel Ingot](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Ingot). The Swiss used [Reap and Sow](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reap+and+Sow) to blow up one of Fagnoni's lands when the French stumbled.

Bertrand still felt safe at four mana but Manuel Bucher launched another [Reap and Sow](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reap+and+Sow), this time with entwine. Next the little Swiss decided he might as well start attacking.

He played [Leonin Abunas](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Abunas) and tried [Solemn Simulacrum](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Solemn+Simulacrum) again. Fagnoni had [Annul](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Annul). The [Stalking Stones](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stalking+Stones) turned into a creature and the French played [Darksteel Pendant](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Pendant).

Manuel: "[Tooth and Nail](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tooth+and+Nail) with entwine?"  
 Bertrand: "Ok, you win."

Manuel 1-0 Bertrand.

### Game 2

They both kept their hands, although Bertrand seemed anything but happy. On his third turn he played [Darksteel Ingot](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Ingot), but he missed his fourth land drop. Bucher fished with a turn four [Leonin Abunas](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Abunas) but the French had [Condescend](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Condescend).

[Eternal Witness](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eternal+Witness) brought Abunas back, while Fagnoni set up for the long game with [Darksteel Pendant](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Darksteel+Pendant). When he found his fourth land he played a backbreaker: [Acquire](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Acquire).

Bertrand dragged it along trying to figure out what his opponent had in hand by looking at the cards in his deck. He ended up taking a [Solemn Simulacrum](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Solemn+Simulacrum). Bucher played his own Simulacrum.

They traded Thorens and Fagnoni played [March of the Machines](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=March+of+the+Machines). [Sylvan Scrying](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sylvan+Scrying) gave Manuel his second [Cloudpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudpost), before he played Abunas. With the second [Cloudpost](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloudpost) online he tried [Mindslaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindslaver). Bertrand allowed it.

Then young Swiss tried to activate [Mindslaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindslaver) and Fagnoni pointed to his [March of the Machines](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=March+of+the+Machines). [Beacon of Destruction](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Beacon+of+Destruction) took [Leonin Abunas](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Leonin+Abunas) away and [Echoing Truth](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Echoing+Truth) bounced the [Mindslaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindslaver).

The Witness chumped the Pendant, and Bucher used another Witness to fetch back Abunas. Again Fagnoni had the [Condescend](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Condescend) to try to stop the White creature. But this time he only got a chance to scry, because Manuel Bucher had enough mana for the whole Top 8 at this point.

The Swiss tried the [Mindslaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindslaver) again but this time the French had [Annul](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Annul). But when Manuel tried [Oblivion Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Stone) there was no answer. Standing behind his indestructibule artifacts-turned-creatures Bertrand tried desperately to find a way to regain total control of the table.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/sideboard/images/gpzur04/qf1_2.jpg)

Accross the table Manuel Bucher was having the time of his life. Next he resolved [Plated Slagwurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plated+Slagwurm). Now that's something a Blue-Red can't really do much about. Still the French hardly blinked.

Manuel got rid of the [March of the Machines](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=March+of+the+Machines) and Bertrand's defense plan fell like a house of... well, cards. A [Tooth and Nail](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tooth+and+Nail) with entwine convinced Bertrand Fagnoni he might as well concede before the White Keeper started recurring the [Mindslaver](https://web.archive.org/web/20040627000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mindslaver).

Final result:

Manuel 2-0 Bertrand.
