The latest member of **Magic** R&D is Dave Humpherys, and he definitely earned his spot! The Pro Tour Hall of Famer belonged to arguably the game's most famous team, Your Move Games, and has numerous top finishes on his resume. At Pro Tour–Rome in 1999, home to the infamous Tolarian Academy and High Tide decks, Dave took another path and finished 9th with "Free Whaley." The deck centered around a ruling at the time that allowed Recurring Nightmare and Great Whale in the graveyard to generate infinite mana. Once you could do that, you could switch Nightmare targets to Merfolk Traders to draw your deck, Triskelion to kill your opponent, or simply cast Stroke of Genius to deck an unassuming competitor.
