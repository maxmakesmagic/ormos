The 2010 [State Championships](http://www.the2009s.com/) have been played, and in Indiana Charles Morrison's White-Blue Control deck managed to finish third. What's so neat about Charles's deck? The boost it got from *Scars of Mirrodin*! Lux Cannon and Ratchet Bomb allow Charles to control anything an opponent can throw at the deck. Trinket Mage even serves to fetch up powerful cards like Voltaic Key and Everflowing Chalice. Neat!
