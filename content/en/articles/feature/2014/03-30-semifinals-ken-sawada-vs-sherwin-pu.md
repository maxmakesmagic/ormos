Pu and Sawada faced off in the first round of Day 2, where, according to Sawada, Pu crushed him 'Most rapidly'. Sawada headed into the semifinals hoping to avoid the same fate, but acknowledged the matchup was an uphill one. 

**Game 1** 

Sawada led things off with a [Soldier of the Pantheon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soldier+of+the+Pantheon), which Pu [Magma Jet](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Jet)ted. Sawada followed up with a Brimaz, while Pu had a Trouble for 6. Pu tried to mount some pressure with a [Chandra's Phoenix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Phoenix), but Sawada had a [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) for it. A [Magma Jet](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Jet) and [Searing Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blood) took care of the Brimaz, but Sawada had a second to mount more pressure on Pu. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/sf_sawada.jpg)

*Sawada contemplates a fiery end for his creatures* 

  

He followed up with a [Keening Apparition](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Keening+Apparition) and a [Precinct Captain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Precinct+Captain). Su raised an eyebrow and emptied his hand of [Skullcrack](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullcrack) and [Lightning Surge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Surge) at Sawada's dome, dropping him to 3, then flipped the top card of his deck over. A [Searing Blood](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Searing+Blood) did quite nicely, netting Pu the first game. 

**Sherwin Pu 1 - Ken Sawada 0** 

Sawada started the second game with a pair of [Soldier of the Pantheon](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soldier+of+the+Pantheon)s. One of them was promptly [Shock](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shock)ed, but Sawada had a [Lyev Skyknight](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lyev+Skyknight) to keep things ticking along. Pu summoned a [Satyr Firedancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Satyr+Firedancer), which was a too-juicy target for Sawada's [Detention Sphere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Detention+Sphere) to resist. Pu had a [Magma Jet](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Jet) for the Skyknight. 

Sawada attacked with his Soldier, drawing a [Lightning Surge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Surge) from Pu. Sawada followed up with a [Imposing Sovereign](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Imposing+Sovereign), who got [Chained to the Rocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chained+to+the+Rocks). Sawada played a tapped [Watery Grave](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Watery+Grave) and passed, Doom Blading Pu's [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault) when it attacked. A [Whip of Erebos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Whip+of+Erebos) from Sawada was met with a [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm), knocking Sawada to 12 but putting Pu on a clock against the pending lifegain. 

Sawada tried an attack with his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault), which was burned before it could connect. He summoned a [Precinct Captain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Precinct+Captain), which Pu [Chained to the Rocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chained+to+the+Rocks). Sawada, indefatigable, used his Whip or Erebos to return a Soldier, which Pu shot before passing on his turn. Sawada tried th esame thing again the following turn, and Pu had another [Magma Jet](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Jet) to again keep Sawada's life total from rising. 

Sawada finally connected with his Skyknight, going to 15. Sawada destroyed the [Chained to the Rocks](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chained+to+the+Rocks) holding his [Precinct Captain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Precinct+Captain) captive, and when Pu went to burn it, he flashed a [Negate](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Negate) to keep the token generator alive. Pu's back was to the wall with the active Whip on the board, but he tried again with a [Lightning Surge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Surge) and took out the Captain. Sawada simply used his Whip to reanimate it, Pu using a [Skullcrack](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullcrack) to stop the lifegain trigger. 

Pu, fighting a losing battle, visibly slumped when Sawada summoned a [Blood Baron of Vizkopa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Baron+of+Vizkopa). He drew a [Blind Obedience](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blind+Obedience) what was likely a turn too late. A [Warleader's Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warleader%27s+Helix) wasn't enough to deal with the Baron, and although Pu played a tight defensive game for several turns, it wasn't enough to deal with Sawada's relentless life advantage. 

**Ken Sawada 1 - Sherwin Pu 1** 

Pu led with a [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault), cracking in at Sawada on his second turn. Sawada had only lands, taking 4 points of damage before dropping an untapped watery grave to summon a [Sin Collector](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sin+Collector). Pu spread a hand of [Magma Jet](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Jet), [Shock](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shock), [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm), [Blind Obedience](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blind+Obedience) and [Warleader's Helix](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Warleader%27s+Helix), Sawada picking the [Magma Jet](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Magma+Jet). 

![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/events/2014/gpbei14/sf_pu.jpg)

*Pu's only problem is figuring out which burn spell to cast first.* 

  

Pu shocked the [Sin Collector](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sin+Collector) before heading in with his [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault). Sawada summoned a Brimaz, but was on a lowly 12 life. Pu elected to ignore the powerful legend and try to race him with burn. The equation changed a little when Sawada summoned a [Ghost Council of Orzhova](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ghost+Council+of+Orzhova), but Pu was committed. He threw his Helix, a [Lightning Surge](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Surge) and a [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm) at Sawada to knock him to 1, rising to 3 when the Ghost Council came back in. Sawada attacked for 10, but had nothing relevant post-combat to keep him out of [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140330000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm) range. 

**Sherwin Pu 2 - Ken Sawada 1**
