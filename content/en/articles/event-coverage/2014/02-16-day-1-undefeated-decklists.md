While the first nine rounds of the tournament really showcased the immense variety of the Legacy format, there wasn't much of it at the very top. Among the five players to achieve such a feat, three did so playing Blue-White Miracles, including Hall of Famer Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa.

Complementing those three was one BUG [Delver](https://web.archive.org/web/20140216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver) and, what may just be the big story of the tournament so far, Kasper Euser's rogue build, beautifully titled "Farmville." The deck features some regular control elements like [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20140216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) and [Force of Will](https://web.archive.org/web/20140216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Force+of+Will) as well as pretty basic card selection in [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm), [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder), and [Intuition](https://web.archive.org/web/20140216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Intuition), but really runs on a lands engine of [Exploration](https://web.archive.org/web/20140216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exploration), [Crop Rotation](https://web.archive.org/web/20140216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crop+Rotation), [Living Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20140216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Living+Wish), and [Life from the Loam](https://web.archive.org/web/20140216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Life+from+the+Loam) to quickly assemble the combination of [Dark Depths](https://web.archive.org/web/20140216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Depths) and [Thespian's Stage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140216000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thespian%27s+Stage). Copying the former with the latter creates an indestructible 20/20 token (because the copy lacks all ice counters) and usually wins the game shortly after. Check it out!
