Foils are something that catch your eye. Like Wonka's golden ticket you open a booster pack and sometimes you discover one of our premium foils inside the pack. But as you can see, if you look at a premium foil, the card is not uniformly reflective.

We've shown these underlayers a few times before, but now let's look at some of them from ***Magic** 2014 Core Set*. I've picked out a few of my favorites.

With each of the cards below, click on the card image to reveal the foil underlayer.

First up, [Chandra, Pyromaster](https://web.archive.org/web/20130815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Pyromaster).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/70/5a/705a71eeee8b596476b43acfb4c5dd5bc120215b9e319462c9f64c5695c636ca.jpg)![](https://i.ormos.uk/ce/41/ce414c798ec8933491cff767b79a25dad6612fa84b5644606335d0306122b2f7.jpg)

Next, let's take a look at another red card—this time an uncommon: [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20130815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c6/e2/c6e2ab610dda4bc879b874aa733014e3a39257a380efffb90b6839c7a957273d.jpg)![](https://i.ormos.uk/9a/1c/9a1cd66c450a91b985439e74547cb1be4fe84c68863db3a3133a9068bc0372bb.jpg)

Here is a blue uncommon that has some fantastic art and looks even better as a premium foil: [Opportunity](https://web.archive.org/web/20130815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Opportunity).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/bd/7a/bd7a8ca3ab842b0e4227b98c5081fa4153abfedaba3d3c41f0e463f3fe1dd385.jpg)![](https://i.ormos.uk/74/5b/745b79f482a71f7e9bad9202ac37f1c5b2c01ee09f6f150a11a7323d4461685b.jpg)

When I looked through the premium foils of ***Magic** 2014*, I knew I had to highlight this card: [Oath of the Ancient Wood](https://web.archive.org/web/20130815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oath+of+the+Ancient+Wood).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/39/98/3998db6bc6e60f7041d1785c09d043b076d52b24df13da8627f9cb74691c77a0.jpg)![](https://i.ormos.uk/c1/e5/c1e5e0b8971eaf902fc41683fa94eeebad7f7e9688cf8cf1643a3b3254d5ee1e.jpg)

I love the veins on that one and it really shows on the actual card.

But not to be outdone, here is [Garruk, Caller of Beasts](https://web.archive.org/web/20130815000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk%2C+Caller+of+Beasts).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c6/00/c600696952b8d05c4be0b1212f5082c09332268a3c8cb300aaeb079f06d46b2d.jpg)![](https://i.ormos.uk/4d/36/4d3657d778939eadd880753843b937b7caecd1651a4ae2fd33bfc45c1216e2c0.jpg)
