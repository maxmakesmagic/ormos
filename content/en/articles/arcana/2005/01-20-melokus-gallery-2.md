M**irror Gallery** (a *Betrayers of Kamigawa* card [previewed today](https://web.archive.org/web/20050120000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/mg159) by Mark Gottlieb) depicts the interior of a grand, Soratami cloud palace. It's filled with mirrors reflecting a regal-looking moonfolk in the foreground.

  
*[Mirror Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20050120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirror+Gallery) art by Scott M. Fischer* 

That moonfolk is none other than the legendary [Meloku the Clouded Mirror](https://web.archive.org/web/20050120000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Meloku+the+Clouded+Mirror), also illustrated by Scott M. Fischer.
