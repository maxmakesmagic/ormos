This edition of [Milk and Cookies](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/https://magic.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Archive.aspx?tag=Milk%20and%20Cookies) features the Spectacular Jason Chan (much like the Spectacular Spiderman, except Jason was bitten by a radioactive fantasy artist in college).

Jason has created some fantastic images for us over the years, and he's definitely become one of our "go-to" guys when we need really striking characters and awesome composition.

Anyway, enough of my fluff... Here's the scoop!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/ce/a2/cea201fbd46d7f1f9ecfbbb6da062abbd68a3deb7326131668bc26b6ef10d856.png)  

***What got you interested in illustration and fantasy art?***

I've always been a fan of science fiction and fantasy, and I've loved to draw since I was a little kid. I grew up reading comics, playing video games, watching movies and cartoons, and reading sci-fi and fantasy novels, and I loved drawing my favorite characters and creatures and making up my own. In junior high my friends and I began playing **Magic: The Gathering**. I had no idea that I would be able to make art for **Magic** for a living!

Oh! That reminds me... I can't pay you one red cent for this interview, by the way. My budget items only include milk and cookies, and a *Smokey and the Bandit* car for me, of course.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/74/05/74052867e5600365492d6cf9f77521e260151dd7476f9fde6579ec4cd957d5a3.jpg)

[Sunblast Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sunblast+Angel) | Art by [Jason Chan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;artist=%5B%22Jason+Chan%22%5D)

***How long have you been working as a fantasy artist?***

Most of my artwork growing up was very fantasy, sci-fi, and anime influenced. I did my earliest paid fantasy art back in high school, back in the late 90s. It wasn't until late into college, in the early 2000s, that I was making steady pay off of fantasy art, doing artwork for pen-and-paper RPGs and later for **Magic**.

Sounds like you worked your way up. Aspiring fantasy artists take note!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/15/19/151979d94a706bb0e63e6cd622bc4f42fa518ba12e06cf37b441de8931b0e741.jpg)

[Apocalypse Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Apocalypse+Hydra) | Art by [Jason Chan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;artist=%5B%22Jason+Chan%22%5D)

***Who are your artistic influences?***

I have influences from all over the place. Growing up, I was a big fan of Boris Vallejo, Frank Frazetta, and Brom in the fantasy world. I was also into some comic artists and manga artists such as Joe Quesada, Masamune Shirow, and Akira Toriyama.

I was also very interested in things I saw in video games and movies. I was a huge *Warcraft* and *Starcraft* fan, and a huge fan of JRPGs like *Final Fantasy*. In art school I was introduced to more illustrators and fine artists, and I became a huge fan of J. C. Leyendecker and Alphonse Mucha. As the Internet grew, I was introduced to EVEN MORE artists from around the world. I became very interested in a lot of Korean artists and Japanese artists.

I can go on forever—I love looking at new artwork and I love seeing things that look new and unique. I think all of this influences my work, little by little.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/72/c1/72c1d31af1e2157d40b587ef68bedae069a98869e3d4ce62db4a84903e917b3e.jpg)

[Kargan Dragonlord](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kargan+Dragonlord) | Art by [Jason Chan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;artist=%5B%22Jason+Chan%22%5D)

***Was it tough "breaking in" to Magic?***

I was very fortunate to get a personal introduction to Jeremy Jarvis, the art director, during a San Diego Comic Con. It was my first Comic Con and I had just met illustrator Dan Dos Santos, who would quickly become a good friend. He was very good to me and introduced me to a lot of art directors, including Jeremy. I showed Jeremy my portfolio, and later he contacted me with a little work.

That Jeremy guy seems to know talent when he sees it; he hired me as well (which proves he is fallible... an endearing trait for an art director).

![](https://i.ormos.uk/70/6c/706c850939eb03433a254b8efdc7de7f964888792f64d2db18caace10d2f688d.jpg)

[Corrupted Conscience](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Corrupted+Conscience) | Art by [Jason Chan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;artist=%5B%22Jason+Chan%22%5D)

***What was your first Magic illustration?***

My earliest **Magic** illustrations were for [Nimbus Maze](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nimbus+Maze) and [Pact of Negation](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pact+of+Negation) in *Future Sight*. I also did the [Gifts Given holiday card](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/https://magic.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/arcana/340) early on.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/95/1c/951c30b07eec8e9c65086fc19ecde1d8442a0f4459d0103c01846bf443d9a981.jpg)

[Pact of Negation](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pact+of+Negation) | Art by [Jason Chan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoileramp;method=visualamp;action=advancedamp;artist=%5B%22Jason+Chan%22%5D)

***What is your favorite piece you've done for Magic?***

I am pretty happy with [Maul Splicer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Maul+Splicer), [Twilight Shepherd](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Twilight+Shepherd), and [Iona, Shield of Emeria](https://web.archive.org/web/20110615000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Iona%2C+Shield+of+Emeria). I have a hard time picking favorites with my work—I'm my own worst critic.
