Learn more about Pro Tour *Amonkhet*'s Top 8 competitors—including who they are, what they've done in the game, and more—by taking a look through their profiles!

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/ptakh/t8-fennell.jpg)Name** : Chris Fennell

**Age**: 31

**Hometown**: Tampa, FL

**Team**: [Lingering Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20170513000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lingering+Souls)

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments** : Top 8 at Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*. Nine Grand Prix Top 8s.

**Limited Record**: 5-1

**Standard Record**: 9-1

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?** Black-green and green-blue splashing white. I preferred the black-green deck.

**What's the key to drafting *Amonkhet*?** Be in the right colors.

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?** Black-White Zombies. It wins the mirror and does not lose to a resolved [Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170513000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel).

**What card was your MVP for the weekend?** [Anguished Unmaking](https://web.archive.org/web/20170513000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Anguished+Unmaking)

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?** Validation for 20 years of playing. I would always be a Pro Tour Champion.

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/ptakh/t8-yukuhiro.jpg)Name** : Ken Yukuhiro

**Age**: 28

**Hometown**: Tokyo, Japan

**Team**: Musashi, sponsored by Dig.cards

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments** : two Pro Tour Top 8s, seven Grand Prix Top 8s

**Limited Record**: 6-0

**Standard Record**: 7-3

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?** Black-red twice

**What's the key to drafting *Amonkhet?*** Studying all the archetypes in the set and not playing green.

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?** Black-Green Energy because I have mastered it.

**What card was your MVP this weekend?** [Bone Picker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170513000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bone+Picker)

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?** It will be the start of my new *Magic* story.

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/ptakh/t8-tobiasch.jpg)Name** : Marc Tobiasch

**Age**: 29

**Hometown**: Benn, Germany

**Team**: EUreka

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments** : Managing and building [Doomsday](https://web.archive.org/web/20170513000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doomsday) piles in time.

**Limited Record**: 6-0

**Standard Record**: 6-2-2

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?** Black-green and then green-white. The black-green deck was the best.

**What's the key to drafting *Amonkhet?*** Find out what is open and either be aggro or have a good plan to beat it.

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?** Temur Marvel. \[It's a\] midrange deck that has an "I win" button as well as a very good late game.

**What card was your MVP this weekend?** [Tireless Tracker](https://web.archive.org/web/20170513000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tireless+Tracker).

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?** Starting the German tradition of winning all the Pro Tours again, finally. Also, Platinum and the World Championship. Come on!

* * *

  
**![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/ptakh/t8-calcano.jpg)Name** : Christian Calcano

**Age**: 28

**Hometown**: New York, NY

**Team**: MTG Bent Card

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments** : seven Grand Prix Top 8s with two wins, two Pro Tour Top 16s.

**Limited Record**: 5-1

**Standard Record**: 7-1-2

**What archetypes did you draft? Which was your favorite?** White-Blue [Slither Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20170513000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slither+Blade), going 3-0. Then Red-White Aggro going 2-1. Favorite was [Slither Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20170513000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slither+Blade) (obviously).

**What's the key to drafting *Amonkhet?*** Don't play four-drops or higher.

**What Standard deck are you playing and why?** Mono-Black Zombies, because I trust my teammates.

**What card was your MVP this weekend?** [Liliana's Mastery](https://web.archive.org/web/20170513000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana%27s+Mastery)

**What would winning the Pro Tour mean to you?** Everything I've dreamed of, and one step closer to the Hall of Fame.

**What card was your MVP for the weekend?** [Aetherworks Marvel](https://web.archive.org/web/20170513000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Aetherworks+Marvel) and [Chandra, Flamecaller](https://web.archive.org/web/20170513000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Flamecaller)
