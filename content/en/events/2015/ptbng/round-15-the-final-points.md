## Chris Fennell (Storm) vs. Dominik Prosek (Burn)

[Saul Aguado vs. (14) Alexander Hayne](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/ptbng14/fm16)

When Dominik Prosek and Chris Fennell met at the feature match table, they were sitting down for the most important match of their lifes. Sitting at twelve wins and three losses each, a win would put them at 36 points, one intentional draw away from a coveted Top 8 slot.

 

### The Decks

Prosek, a PTQ winner from the Czech Republic, is currently 7-1 with his burn deck. While most of his cards are red, he is splashing white for [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm) and black for [Bump in the Night](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bump+in+the+Night). Taking out his opponents before they can muster a winning board has worked for him so far, but would his unconventional deck choice get him into the Top 8?

Fennell finished in the Top 16 of Pro Tour Dragon's Maze, but most of his success has been on the Grand Prix Circuit. He won GP Washington 2004 with his team, and recently made the finals of GP Indianapolis 2013. Fennell is known for his Limited game, but he is also 7-1 in Modern after joining Team TCGPlayer for this tournament. His deck is as different from Limited **Magic** as it gets: Fennell is on U/R Storm.

This matchup was coming down to a race between Fennell's combo kill and Prosek's burn spells. Would Fennell find a lethal [Grapeshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grapeshot) before the 3- and 4-damage spells of Prosek finished him off?

 

### The Games

Fennell won the die roll and went to 19 to find an Island with [Scalding Tarn](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scalding+Tarn) before casting [Sleight of Hand](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sleight+of+Hand). A suspended [Rift Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rift+Bolt) by Prosek revealed that he was on a burn plan. Fennell had saved two life by fetching out the basic land, but followed up with a [Shivan Reef](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Reef) to cast [Pyromancer Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyromancer+Ascension). This put him to 18, and Prosek made that 12 with [Rift Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rift+Bolt) and [Bump in the Night](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bump+in+the+Night). Another [Shivan Reef](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Reef) allowed Fennell to cast [Faithless Looting](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faithless+Looting) as well as [Goblin Electromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Electromancer), going to 10 in the process. That life total was not for long though, as Prosek used his untapped red source for [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt).

The following turn presented a big decision point for Fennell. Prosek had cast a [Goblin Guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Guide) which revealed a [Faithless Looting](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faithless+Looting) upon attacking. Fennell wanted to untap with his board of three lands, Ascension, and Electromancer, but was only on 7 life. Could he go down to 5 by not blocking, activate one of his [Shivan Reef](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Reef)s and win the following turn? Or should he trade the 2/2s to play around two burn spells, losing the mana advantage?

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/mtg/images/daily/events/ptbng14/r15_fennell.jpg)

*With Fennell's lands damaging his life total, each [Shivan Reef](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Reef) activation was particularly tense in his fight against a burn deck.* 

Fennell knew that this decision would most likely decide the game. Ultimately, he decided to risk it. "No blocks, I'll go to 5", he declared. Prosek passed the turn, but an [Arid Mesa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arid+Mesa) meant that he would be able to cast a lethal [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm) should Fennell be forced to take one from his [Shivan Reef](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Reef). Fennell untapped and drew the previously revealed [Faithless Looting](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faithless+Looting). With a sigh, he tapped his [Shivan Reef](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Reef) for Red. "I go to 4."

Prosek could only Shrug.

Two [Pyretic Ritual](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyretic+Ritual)s and a second [Faithless Looting](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faithless+Looting) activated [Pyromancer Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyromancer+Ascension). A [Past in Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Past+in+Flames) allowed Fennell access to his graveyard, and [Sleight of Hand](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sleight+of+Hand), [Faithless Looting](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faithless+Looting)s, and the copies provided by the Ascension made sure that Fennell found a lethal [Grapeshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grapeshot).

Dominik was on the play for Game 2, with both players again keeping their starting hands. A [Vexing Devil](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vexing+Devil) took Fennell down to 16, who went with a pain-free Island into [Serum Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serum+Visions) for his first turn. A second [Vexing Devil](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vexing+Devil) and [Bump in the Night](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bump+in+the+Night) took Fennell down to 9. Playing a Mountain, Fennell ensured that he would stay at 9 while casting [Pyromancer Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyromancer+Ascension), potentially setting up a turn 3 combo turn. When Prosek only played a tapped [Blood Crypt](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Crypt), it looked like Fennell could take the match next turn. However, he only had a [Scalding Tarn](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scalding+Tarn), a [Sleight of Hand](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sleight+of+Hand), and a [Goblin Electromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Electromancer) to play. Fennell passed the turn.

"End of turn [Skullcrack](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skullcrack)", was Prosek's response. He revealed his hand of two [Shard Volley](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shard+Volley)s to his opponent, and it was on to Game 3.

![Shivan Reef](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Shivan+Reef)

[Shivan Reef](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Reef) made an appearance once again, taking Fennell down to 19 as he cast [Serum Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serum+Visions). Prosek reduced that to 16 with a [Bump in the Night](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bump+in+the+Night), which signaled that he wasn't holding the more powerful [Vexing Devil](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vexing+Devil). For the third game straight, Fennell had a turn two [Pyromancer Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyromancer+Ascension), looking comfortable at 15 life. Prosek dropped an [Arid Mesa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arid+Mesa) but had no play for his turn. He had kept his opening hand, so Fennell could be sure that there would be burnspells waiting for him or his [Goblin Electromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Electromancer), should he cast one.

Fennell went with a [Serum Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serum+Visions), putting the first counter on [Pyromancer Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyromancer+Ascension). "It's crazy, giving you a whole turn is better than playing this untapped", he remarked as he played an untapped [Steam Vents](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steam+Vents) and passed the turn. Would Fennell's conservative life total management lead him to victory?

Prosek went to 14 to cast [Boros Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Charm), taking Fennell down to 10, but simplifying Fennell's task of conjuring a lethal [Grapeshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grapeshot). [Bump in the Night](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bump+in+the+Night) and [Lava Spike](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lava+Spike) meant that Fennell was at 4 heading into his fourth turn, with an [Arid Mesa](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arid+Mesa) representing a lethal 3-damage burnspell should Fennell use his [Shivan Reef](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Reef) for colored mana.

As it turned out, Fennell did not have to. A third [Serum Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serum+Visions), cast off the Island, activated [Pyromancer Ascension](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyromancer+Ascension), and the Mountain allowed for a duplicated [Faithless Looting](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Faithless+Looting). With [Steam Vents](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steam+Vents) and [Shivan Reef](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shivan+Reef) still untapped, Fennell found [Manamorphose](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Manamorphose) to generate the colored mana he needed while drawing into more rituals and card draw. "I'm done with my lands," he commented as he tapped out and started going through with the combo.

A few [Sleight of Hand](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sleight+of+Hand)s later, Fennell had found a lethal [Grapeshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grapeshot), sitting comfortably at 4 life. Prosek was forced to extend his hand in defeat, revealing the [Shard Volley](https://web.archive.org/web/20140222000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shard+Volley) he had been holding.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/mtg/images/daily/events/ptbng14/r15_storm.jpg)

*The final totals, with the fourth life point deciding whether Fennell would be burnt to a crisp or storming up a victory.*

In the end, a single point of life decided the game, the match, and who would most likely advance to the Top 8 of Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*.

**Fennell 2 – Prosek 1**
