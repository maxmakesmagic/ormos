**Format**  
 

*   Booster Draft for Masters - 3 Onslaught packs will be used for drafting.
*   Sealed Deck for Gateway - 1 Onslaught Tournament Deck and 2 Onslaught Boosters
*   Check **www.thedci.com** for Booster Draft rules.

**Round Length**  
 

*   Gateway - 60 minute rounds; Masters - untimed rounds
*   Players have 3 minutes to sideboard between games.
*   Although there is no official time limit, stalling and slow play rules will be very strictly enforced during the tournament regardless.

**Advancement**  
 

*   The tournament is single-elimination.
*   All matches are best two out of three.
*   The top 4 Gateway players will advance to the Masters.

**Notes**  
 

*   Players are responsible for knowing all DCI rules and high-level tournament rules.
*   Players must bring pen, paper, card sleeves and appropriate counters.

**Thursday Schedule**  
 8:00 AM: Gateway Registration  
 9:00 AM: Gateway Begins

The Gateway will begin as sealed deck, with the final 32 players drafting Onslaught to determine the 4 invitations to the Masters.

*Masters Play will begin at 4:00 PM or immediately after the Gateway tournament.* 

4:00 PM: Masters Draft #1 Begins (4 draft pods)  
 5:00 PM: Masters Round #1 (32 players)  
 7:00 PM: Masters Round #2 (16 players)

**Friday Schedule**  
 *Masters Play will begin immediately following the Pro Tour. Estimated time: 5:30 PM.* 

5:30 PM: Masters Draft #2 Begins (1 draft pod)  
 6:30 PM: Masters Round #3 (8 players)

**Saturday Schedule**  
 *Masters Play will begin immediately following the Pro Tour. Estimated time: 5:30 PM.* 

5:30 PM: Masters Round #4 (4 players)

**Sunday Schedule**  
 *Masters Play will begin immediately following the Pro Tour semifinals. Estimated time: 1:00 PM.* 

1:00 PM: Masters Finals (2 players)
