Congratulations to Croatia's Vjeran Horvat, who battled through a Modern field of nearly 1,400 to win [Grand Prix Prague](https://web.archive.org/web/20140117000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/gppra14/welcome)! And if you're interested in Modern decklists, you might like to know that the buzz in Prague was for Jan van der Vegt's [Tin Fists](https://web.archive.org/web/20140117000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/eventcoverage/gppra14/day1#4) deck. This season's Pro Tour Qualifier (PTQ) format is Standard, though, so be sure to check out the [*Born of the Gods* Card Image Gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20140117000000/https://archive.wizards.com/Magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/bornofthegods/cig) to get an early view of what might change in February! Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](https://web.archive.org/web/20140117000000/https://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=mtg/event/protour/qualifierlist#jou)! Good luck; have fun!
