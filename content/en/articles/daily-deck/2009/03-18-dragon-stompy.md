Like playing gigantic red monsters far earlier than you should be able to? Then try James Mink's Dragon Stompy build! He attempts to do exactly that, using Seething Song, City of Traitors, and Ancient Tomb (amongst others) to drop Arc-Slogger on the first or second turn. Of course, when that plan fails, he's happy to just plop a disruptive spell like Magus of the Moon, Blood Moon, or Trinisphere into play to disrupt his opponent until he *can*. To read more about James and the Legacy format, [visit the official web coverage from Grand Prix-Chicago](/en/events/2009/gpchi09/two-victories-in-a-row-for-gabriel-nassif).
