Pro Tour Nagoya has concluded, and European standout Kenny Öberg demonstrated yet again his prowess for building innovative decks. This is his red-blue build from the event, designed in part to take advantage of all of the mono-white decks he anticipated. Does your opponent have Hero of Bladehold? Why not play your own in the form of Phyrexian Metamorph? Or simply use Silver Myr to accelerate into Birthing Pod, starting a chain to find your best threats at a moment's notice.
