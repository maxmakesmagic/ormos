Three years ago, Kei Umehara made his first Top 8 at Grand Prix Yokohama. Today, marks his second Top 8 appearance and Temur Delver was his weapon of choice. Clearly, [Nimble Mongoose](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nimble+Mongoose) and [Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) had served him well and was worth the green splash.

His opponent was not a slouch either, having broke into the Top 8 in the first seed out of 1942 other competitors. Kazuya Murakami's Blue White Miracles homebrew that he has christened "Counter Top Mentor" was aptly named. He was playing with 4 copies of [Monastery Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Mentor), the [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer) on steroids.

# Game One

Murakami received the right to play first. After a mulligan, he tried to resolve a pair of [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance), but both were hit by [Spell Snare](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Snare).

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Counterbalance)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance)

[Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets) appeared on Turn 3, only for it fall to [Swords to Plowshares](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swords+to+Plowshares). Umehara let out a wail when Murakami drew into his third [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance). With no threats on the board, Umehara would need to try to force one into play.

[Tarmogoyf](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tarmogoyf) was countered by the [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic) sitting atop his deck. Umehara had to settle for [Nimble Mongoose](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nimble+Mongoose), the very last card in his hand. However, that was quickly outclassed by [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic) and [Batterskull](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Batterskull) and Umehara reached for his sideboard.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkyo15/QF-Kazuya-Murakami.jpg)  
*Kazuya Murakami is up a game with the help of [Stoneforge Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stoneforge+Mystic) and [Batterskull](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Batterskull).* 

**Kei Umehara 0 – Kazuya Murakami 1** 

# Game Two

Murakami had to go down to six cards again and quickly faced pressure from an opposing [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets). It fell to [Swords to Plowshares](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swords+to+Plowshares) after three attacks as a Human Wizard and one attack as an Human Insect. Ouch.

Umehawa replaced it with [Nimble Mongoose](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nimble+Mongoose), which got in for three more unimpeded attacks, putting Murakami at a dangerously low life. More ouch. 

When Murakami tapped out for [Monastery Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Mentor), it provided a window for Umehara to resolve [Vendillion Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vendillion+Clique). With [Pyroclasm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyroclasm) removed, [Vendillion Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vendillion+Clique) was able to sneak in for the lethal blow. 

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpkyo15/QF-Kei-Umehara.jpg)  
*Kei Umehara equalizes by playing the tempo game.* 

**Kei Umehara 1 – Kazuya Murakami 1** 

# Game Three

Umehara's first move was to counter the opposing [Counterbalance](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Counterbalance) with [Spell Snare](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Snare), before baiting out some countermagic and removal spells for his [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets).

That enabled Umehara to resolve [Vendillion Clique](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vendillion+Clique) once again, and Murakami could only let it resolve, revealing a hand of [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm), [Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top), and [Monastery Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Mentor).

However, that was the turning point of the game. Feeling that he could win the damage race, he chose to remove [Sensei's Divining Top](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sensei%27s+Divining+Top). That turned out to be a grave mistake, as Umehara later admitted.

Naturally, Murakami put [Monastery Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Mentor) into play, and outraced Umehara with a bunch of Monk tokens and prowess triggers.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Monastery+Mentor)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150419000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Mentor)

**Kei Umehara 1 – Kazuya Murakami 2** 

Kazuya Murakami advances to the semifinals.
