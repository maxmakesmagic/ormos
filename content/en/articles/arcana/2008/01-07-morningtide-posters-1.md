Morningtide, as you may have heard, is a class-based set. We don't mean that in the sense of "snooty, monocle-wearing creatures", but in the sense of "everyone's got a job".

Three of those jobs are Rogue, Shaman, and Warrior. Rogues look like this:

![Rogue](https://i.ormos.uk/3a/df/3adf886f2bd5d8d96b4dce54c23c92bb5162c0414c76f6d1eb14ee4f1501d02a.jpg)*[Rogue Poster in PDF (1.7MB)](https://media.ormos.uk/ea/57/ea57c4f87d895d675f6d6c58fdadb76f1dfd1eab683d432e5d507ba3e7ff8d15.pdf)*

Sneaky, aren't they? Shamans look like this:

![Shaman](https://i.ormos.uk/64/81/648153428e5490c617980bfe19aacc1c096279d8bcf3df81dbf94422613e8803.jpg)*[Shaman Poster in PDF (2.1MB)](https://media.ormos.uk/2d/f3/2df3033d48c63d530aab89e6b08645600fe593c7fa1f938436677ea297791c80.pdf)*

And Warriors look like this:

![Warrior](https://i.ormos.uk/34/f9/34f9bbc36fd9002a488646b6c83bf91f484c459d9554896e8abab0beaac695f3.jpg)*[Warrior Poster in PDF (1.8MB)](https://media.ormos.uk/d9/b0/d9b04894a6638b94bd84d582f5810f19d92c9d5869ec07ddbfb015c6324f200d.pdf)*

Tune in tomorrow for the other two classes!
