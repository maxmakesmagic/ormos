Hall of Famer Frank Karsten has established himself as the type of player you should listen to when looking for a new deck in Standard. At Pro Tour Paris, he brought a Mono-White Quest deck. His build could quickly abuse Quest for the Holy Relic, allowing him to transform casting creatures into attacking with something equipped with Argentum Armor. Read more about Pro Tour Paris, part of **Magic** Weekend Paris, [here](/en/events/2011/ptpar11/stark-flies-high-with-planeswalkers-in-paris).
