So Mark Rosewater calls me one morning last November, which is always a good feeling if you’re a **Magic** fan. He asks if I’m busy. I should tell the truth (I am, very), but then I scan my memory for the last time the senior designer of my favorite game called me looking to chat, and I can’t come up with an exact date. So I manage a “no, sure, go ahead, what’s on your mind?”

Mark lays out the vision he’s got for this site, which I imagine he’s done separately to you all by now, and I’m paying attention, really… but I’m also typing up a report that will be used to support Governor Ventura’s Congressional testimony on the reauthorization of the Workforce Investment Act sometime this February, so the two sort of blend together. Best I can tell, I’m now writing for a site that leverages states’ investment in our high-skill workforce through attaining threshold and developing card advantage. Other than that, I can’t tell you much.

Well, I *can* tell you that this weekly column is meant to capture the “Spirit of the Game.” What is this Spirit of the Game, you ask? Last I heard, it was an X/X flyer with trample and haste for ![0 Mana](http://www.wizards.com/images/Symbols/Symbol_0_mana.gif) casting cost, where X equaled how seriously you take being unserious. The stakes in any given Magic game are far lower than most of us remember consistently; and this column is here to suggest ways of jogging your memory.

So this will be the weekly space where we relax the rules on mana curves, where less efficient cards get proposed, where your match results are not nearly as important as what effect you had on the game. Did you enjoy the game? Did your friends enjoy having you in the game? Did something unexpected and fun happen? Those will be our measuring sticks, not Pro Tour qualifications or Grand Prix byes.

That ought to cover it. Now let’s look at a *Torment* card truly worthy of chaotic environments: Radiate.

This is Fork gone insane, a fifty-pronged grill fork for Paul Bunyan. It is, in essence, a method of converting a targeted spell into a global killer. Terminate becomes Wrath of God; Disenchant, Purify; Stone Rain, Armageddon.

But there can be more nuance to it than that – and the fun of the card will be in exploiting those nuances. Terror does *not* become a Wrath of God; it becomes a spell that wipes out every army except your own, since you will be playing with artifact creatures. Active Volcano does *not* just become a pure color hoser; it benefits from your clever decision to include Metathran Transport or Vision Charm. Word of Command does not become a disgusting puppet show…oh, wait, it does.

### RADIANCE A LA MODE

One intriguing use of Radiate will be with any one of a number of charms – they’re instants, they’re cheap, and above all they’re flexible. Radiate turns many of them into gamebreakers. Here’s how some sample charms will now effectively read. Note that you can only do one thing, and it’s based on what the controller chooses to do first. (So if you want to untap all permanents, the first Charm can’t have grounded a flyer.)

*Emerald Charm: destroy all global enchantments, untap all permanents, or all creatures lose flying for the turn.*

*Crosis's Charm: destroy all non-black creatures, destroy all artifacts, or return all permanents to their owners’ hands.*

*Darigaaz's Charm: return target creature card from your graveyard to your hand, pump all creatures, or deal 3 damage to all creatures and players.*

Several times, I’ve heard the controller of a Darigaaz’s Charm say in the middle of combat, “I guess it doesn’t matter if I pump my creature, or damage yours. Pump mine, I guess.”

Well, it matters now, doesn’t it?

### RADIATING CHAOS, TWO DIFFERENT WAYS

With or without Charms, there are an amazing number of cheap instants and sorceries that interact with Radiate. I’ve grouped them into two styles of deck: the “conventional” card-advantage engine, and the more freakish mind-bender. How well your own Radiate deck works – and of course, to be fun, it *should* occasionally work – will depend on how quickly you draw Radiate, how quickly you can develop mana, and how easily you can prepare the next Radiate.

The **card-advantage engine** only has to look back as far as *Invasion* block to find an amazing array of instant cantrips – Aggressive Urge, Repulse, and Fire/Ice would be my favorites out of them. It’s easy to imagine a green-blue-red deck that announced a single Ice, and then followed it up with Radiate to tap the entire board’s worth of permanents and draw that many cards. In this style of multiplayer deck, you’re probably looking for pieces of a combo, or some incredibly strong bombs you can throw down. Perhaps a massively-forked Urza's Rage, or Aether Mutation, is what you’re after. Or perhaps your looking for a Morphling, which is as amazing as always, since you can make it impervious to Radiate’s global effect.

And hey, while you’re playing blue and green, don’t forget that Relearn and Reclaim will help get Radiate back in your hand. (Remember, you can’t Radiate multiple cards in your graveyard.)

The **mind-bender** will be out to play chicken with everyone else, and will use direct damage cards such as Blazing Salvo and Lightning Bolt, as well as some interesting white cards, including Shelter, Tithe, and of course, Swords to Plowshares.

Seize the Day, with its ability to generate an additional combat and main phase for every targetable creature on the board, would be a great surprise finish to this kind of deck, and a memorable threat in repeat games. In a five-player game where everyone has two creatures, you get a total of eleven combat phases off of one “irradiated” Seize the Day (and you haven’t even flashed it back yet). You will, of course, need creatures that do not tap to attack to take full advantage of this: Serra Angel; Radiant, Archangel; even a bit of blue for Lightning Angel will work just fine, here.

### MAXIMIZING RADIATION FROM OTHER SOURCES

All of this is so far has been about your own deck, of course, but consider the possibilities that arise in the typical multiplayer game when you have ![3 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/7f/5d/7f5d2f1e55eaa9d19a9a23027b63bfbb5b39dbf8642160dda1a477a03df23a6a.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif) available and have demonstrated Radiate capability. (I’m not a big fan of just showing everyone the card – too vain, or too supplicant. Why not have more fun by being creative? Play a Wandering Eye, so everyone can see your hand; or use a Bosium Strip so that the first Radiate threatens a return. Or just make the deck good enough so that you can use it enough times for people to anticipate what’s coming.) What signals will ![3 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/7f/5d/7f5d2f1e55eaa9d19a9a23027b63bfbb5b39dbf8642160dda1a477a03df23a6a.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif) buy you?

**“Don’t Tread On Me”:** A single wrong move by an opponent could send the game spiraling out of control for them. If someone would get cute and Backlash your Hill Giant, perhaps they’ll think it over when they’ve got their own Crater Hellion and Pit Raptor sitting in front of them. This works with all sorts of damage spells your opponents may have – culminating in Illuminate with red kicker. Bottom line, you make other red mages your little pets. *Stay, Burnsie! Good red mage. Okay, now attack over there! Attack! Good boy! All right, Burnsie, now go fetch!*

The signal gets even stronger if you have relatively few of the permanents in question. Enjoy the look of frustration on your opponents’ faces as they consider using Terminating your only creature – say, Aboshan, Cephalid Emperor – so that their own massive armies can operate, and then realize that they’d rather have tapped creatures than dead ones.

**“Buy One, Get 38 Free”:** Everyone has been in a situation where they wish their Mirror Strike or Recoil could nail more than one target. Well, now’s their big chance. If they know you can ultra-Fork their modest spell, they’ll be more likely to play it. And the more players there are, the more allure this option will have.

Of course, there’s no guarantee that you’ll follow through on your promise. A great deal of fun with this plan is that someone will play their Humble or Barrin's Unmaking in response to a fatal attack of weenies, look at you with hope in their heart…and then you get to watch their face turn from expectation to despair as they realize you don’t plan to do a darn thing. *Gee, I’m sorry, I don’t appear to have Radiate in my hand right now. Horrible shame.*

**“Let’s Get the Game Moving”:** If several of you find yourself stuck in a quagmire of redundant Stasis copies (“…one more, in case of Disenchant!”…) or endless Lifeline combos (“…at the end of your turn, I’ll sacrifice my Deranged Hermit to Barrin, Master Wizard…”), make sure you have some legal way to show everyone that you have a Radiate. ![](https://i.ormos.uk/d4/aa/d4aad06524cacee48dbc2979fad6c99101acc6ec9feef6569989a77e0b99e6f6.jpg)A single Disenchant, Recoil, or even Hull Breach can suddenly break open a stalled game, and give the control and combo players fits as they watch their entire elaborate scheme crumble.

### LAST OPTION: IRRADIATED CASH

If none of this is to your taste… well, you’re a tough customer, aren’t you? Try this: a mono-red deck with Chance Encounter out. Play Chaotic Strike during your last opponent’s combat. Then, a Radiate with 20 creatures out gives you a 50/50 chance of setting up a winning condition for your next upkeep, all for the low, low price of ![4 Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/36/29/3629288966daa042decf01d3752789ab6676fddd67c3679fa580cd3baecae158.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif)![Red Mana](https://i.ormos.uk/86/2f/862ffc3d21299d18f0315a3f77721a10737fb394e147b0987204ef0496ce1060.gif)!
