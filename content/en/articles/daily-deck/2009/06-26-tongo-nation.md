A surprise hit at Pro Tour-Chicago in 1997, “Tongo Nation” was a mid-range control deck featuring the best spells from blue, white, and red. The deck put two players into the Top 8 and put David Mills into the finals of the Pro Tour. He controlled his opponent’s game plans with cards such as Lightning Bolt and Swords to Plowshares before finishing with a huge Fireball or Earthquake or repeated attacks from the hard-to-kill Frenetic Efreet.
