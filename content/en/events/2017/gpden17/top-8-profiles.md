![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpden17/T8-rolf.jpg)**Name:** John Rolf

**Age:** 28

**Hometown:** Omaha, Nebraska

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 

Top 4 Grand Prix Cleveland 2017

Top 16 U.S. Nationals

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Ramunap Red because of its consistency and ability to go big. Nathan Smith brewed the list and assured me it was broken.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Chandra, Torch of Defiance](https://web.archive.org/web/20170820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Torch+of+Defiance); it's the new Jace.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

The two extra Chandras because value.

**What's your story? What should people know about you?**

Long-time *Magic* *Online* grinder

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

A lot! Shoutout to my brother David, Brandon Ayers, and Nathan Smith for always supporting me!

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpden17/t8-pardee.jpg)**Name:** Sam Pardee

**Age:** 28

Hometown: Denver, Colorado

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 

2 Pro Tour Top 8s

12 Grand Prix Top 8s

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Mono-Red \[Ramunap Red\]. If you can't beat ‘em, join ‘em.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

[Hazoret the Fervent](https://web.archive.org/web/20170820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hazoret+the+Fervent)! It kills them dead.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Chandra, Torch of Defiance](https://web.archive.org/web/20170820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Torch+of+Defiance). Good once the games slow down post-board, and good to kill [Kalitas, Traitor of Ghet](https://web.archive.org/web/20170820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kalitas%2C+Traitor+of+Ghet) with.

**What's your story? What should people know about you?**

I'm a game designer here in Denver. I taught myself to read as a kid to play *Magic* with my father.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

Hopefully not another second place.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2017/events/gpden17/t8-bbd.jpg)**Name:** Brian Braun-Duin

**Age:** 31

**Hometown:** Roanoke, Virginia

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:** 

2016 World Champion

2x Grand Prix Champion. Brad Nelson's better half. Corey Baumeister's worse half.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**

Temur Energy.

Did well for me last weekend. Good versus most top decks.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**

The long tusk of the law. \[[Longtusk Cub](https://web.archive.org/web/20170820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Longtusk+Cub)\]

Love me a cub that gets out of control.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**

[Confiscation Coup](https://web.archive.org/web/20170820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Confiscation+Coup).

I like stealing things.

**What's your story? What should people know about you?**

Well, it all started on a cold and blustery night in Michigan in 1986 ... I was birthed into this world holding a Swamp, a [Mutavault](https://web.archive.org/web/20170820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutavault), and an [Obzedat, Ghost Council](https://web.archive.org/web/20170820000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Obzedat%2C+Ghost+Council) in my hands. The rest, as they say, is history.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

Means I am out of my rut!
