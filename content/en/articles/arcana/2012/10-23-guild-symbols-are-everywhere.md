The guild symbols are all over Ravnica. And that means that they're all over *Return to Ravnica*, too! You might see the Azorius symbol being used as handcuffs on an unruly Ogre....

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/24/1b/241bd908354fc2c51b960ecb8c7ca652f79bf17ba030e5f8257d4d10d96b5ec2.jpg) 

Inaction Injunction | Art by [Wayne Reynolds](https://web.archive.org/web/20121023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22wayne%20reynolds%22])

You might see the Rakdos symbol as a hole in a human's chest....

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e8/e9/e8e954fbfb94c45fadb1e62debdca08d1d6696f24d0d73c2a77640897df9276b.jpg) 

Dreadbore | Art by [Wayne Reynolds](https://web.archive.org/web/20121023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22wayne%20reynolds%22])

Guildgates, of course, are likely to have the symbol of their guilds....

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5e/7b/5e7b8fad22d5e3fe0528cd5b524918c2b6d9684939747ffa05936b6a34021671.jpg) 

Izzet Guildgate | Art by [Noah Bradley](https://web.archive.org/web/20121023000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/pages/search/default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=[%22noah%20bradley%22])

If you look carefully at a priest's breastplate, you might even see a symbol for a guild that isn't even in *Return to Ravnica*!
