*Rise of the Eldrazi's* namesake gigantoids have led many a player to look at Polymorph as a means of cheating them onto the battlefield. Top judge Nicholas Sabin took a turn as a player for a local Friday Night **Magic** playing this version. Awakening Zone and Khalni Garden provide the tokens for targeting with Polymorph. The only creature it can search up? Emrakul, the Aeons Torn. And if you draw the fifteen-drop early, See Beyond will shuffle it back into the library.
