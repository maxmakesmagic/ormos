# Deck of the Day

1.  [Jonathan Job's Mono-Red Aggro](http://sales.starcitygames.com/deckdatabase/displaydeck.php?DeckID=114812)

Placing sixth playing [Ahn-Crop Crasher](https://web.archive.org/web/20170718000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ahn-Crop+Crasher) is nothing to sneeze at, and Jonathan Job certainly crashed the Top 8 of the StarCityGames.com Open last weekend with a red and colorless beatdown deck.

The Crasher was just one odd duck in the deck, as [Eldrazi Obligator](https://web.archive.org/web/20170718000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Obligator) made an appearance, along with [Kari Zev, Skyship Raider](https://web.archive.org/web/20170718000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kari+Zev%2C+Skyship+Raider) and a slew of Deserts.

They key to this list might have been that it went a bit bigger. Sure, it played [Soul-Scar Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20170718000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soul-Scar+Mage) (with only six spells in the main deck) and [Falkenrath Gorger](https://web.archive.org/web/20170718000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Falkenrath+Gorger), but it also played [Chandra, Torch of Defiance](https://web.archive.org/web/20170718000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%2C+Torch+of+Defiance) and [Reality Smasher](https://web.archive.org/web/20170718000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Reality+Smasher) and [Thought-Knot Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170718000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thought-Knot+Seer).

The real fun of the deck, however, is not letting your opponent block, no matter what they have defending their life total. Crasher sneaks past something for a turn, as does [Earthshaker Khenra](https://web.archive.org/web/20170718000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Earthshaker+Khenra) (sometimes). [Eldrazi Obligator](https://web.archive.org/web/20170718000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Eldrazi+Obligator) pulls something to your side, where it's obviously not blocking, and [Glorybringer](https://web.archive.org/web/20170718000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glorybringer) simply kills something out of the way.

This deck is definitely a player, which means one thing: mono-red is back on the menu, boys!
