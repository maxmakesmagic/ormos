![](http://magic.wizards.com/sites/mtg/files/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/novels/pathoftheplaneswalker.jpg)

The *Path of the Planeswalker* graphic novels collect the popular [Planeswalker comics](https://web.archive.org/web/20120913000000/http://staging.wizards.com/magic/multiverse/planeswalkers.aspx?x=mtg/multiverse/webcomics/main) from magicthegathering.com. These graphic novels combine a compelling look into Planeswalkers' motivations with art from **Magic** artists including Mark Texeira, Christopher Moeller, Jason Shawn Alexander, Aleksi Briclot, Kev Walker, Alex Horley-Orlandelli, and more.
