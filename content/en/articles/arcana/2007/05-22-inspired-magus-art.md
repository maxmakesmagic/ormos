The background of Magus of the Future shows the Escherian landscape of multidirectional stairs also seen on the Magus's inspiration card, Future Sight. In the art of that enchantment, Ixidor is shown in his castle seeing visions of future actions: pleading with Akroma, battling the furious Kamahl, facing Phage.

![Magus_of_the_Future_640](https://i.ormos.uk/0d/40/0d40f0d9d952317019689b24c548a32f3db8f1876a8afd2864fe425c21ae04d9.jpg)  
*Magus of the Future art by Anthony Francisco*

  

![Future_Sight_640](https://i.ormos.uk/65/86/65861b3a6d468b39c2ad213d5ddcd1b0ba870b2ba02aa3d0347c7a0219d756ba.jpg)  
*Future Sight art by Matt Cavotta*

Franz Vohwinkel took inspiration from his own *Eighth Edition* Blood Moon art for the background of Magus of the Moon. The flavor text of Magus of the Moon offers an explanation for the look of the *Time Spiral* mountains.

*Magus of the Moon art by Franz Vohwinkel*
