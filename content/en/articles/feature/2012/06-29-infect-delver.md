You've seen White-Blue Delver, you've seen Blue-Red Delver, you've seen Blue-Red-Green Delver, and you've even seen Delver without the Delver—but you might not have seen anything quite like this! At the StarCityGames.com Open Series in Detroit last weekend, Steven Krueger brought something very interesting: Infect Delver! Ending up in 9th place, this new flavor of the deck uses the infect mechanic to its advantage!

Sure, this deck features classic Delver components that make it powerful: [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20120629000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage), [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20120629000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe), and [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20120629000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder) compliment [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20120629000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets) to form the core. But the infect bits are what make it interesting. Instead of [Seachrome Coast](https://web.archive.org/web/20120629000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Seachrome+Coast) and [Glacial Fortress](https://web.archive.org/web/20120629000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Glacial+Fortress), the deck stays mono-blue and uses [Inkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20120629000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inkmoth+Nexus) as its specialty land. Alongside [Blighted Agent](https://web.archive.org/web/20120629000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blighted+Agent), this deck has the capacity to kill with either damage or poison counters!

[Runechanter's Pike](https://web.archive.org/web/20120629000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=244681) can quickly pump up creatures thanks to the high quantity of instants and sorceries in the deck—and infect creatures make great targets to hand a Pike. With Pike on your side, you can easily deal lethal poison in just one or two hits. The deck also features [Mutagenic Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20120629000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutagenic+Growth), which also pours more power on your infect creatures—especially with [Snapcaster Mage](https://web.archive.org/web/20120629000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Snapcaster+Mage)—while also protecting your Delvers while tapped out.

This unique take on Delver is worth a look! Check it out below.
