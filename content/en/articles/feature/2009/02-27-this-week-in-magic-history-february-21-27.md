### February 21-27: Eleven Grand Prix Events!

This was a great week to have a Grand Prix, with eleven events in the last ten years. Let's count them down!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/41/ec/41eca202de4e516b06754ce34fd4d4dcf551260fd3dedaf603f6585fafc3bbf2.jpg)

**February 26-27: 2000 Grand Prix–Cannes**: Team Black Ops (Florent Jeudon, Antoine Ruel, and Olivier Ruel) defeats Team New Wave (Thomas Preyer, Alex Shvartsman, and Bram Snepvangers)

**February 24-25: 2001 Grand Prix–Boston**: [Tom Swan defeats Scott Johns](https://web.archive.org/web/20090227000000/http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/event.asp?event=gpbos01)

**February 24-25: 2001 Grand Prix–Cologne**: [Jim Herold defeats Antoine Ruel](https://web.archive.org/web/20090227000000/http://www.wizards.com/sideboard/event.asp?event=gpcol01)

**February 23-24: 2002 Grand Prix–Tampa**: [Sol Malka defeats Jeff Cunningham](https://web.archive.org/web/20090227000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=sideboard/events/gptam02)

**February 22-23: 2003 Grand Prix–Boston**: [Brian Kibler defeats Matthew Cory](https://web.archive.org/web/20090227000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=sideboard/events/gpbos03)

**February 22-23: 2003 Grand Prix–Sevilla**: [Anton Jonsson defeats Carlos Romão](https://web.archive.org/web/20090227000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=sideboard/events/gpsev03)

**February 21-22: 2004 Grand Prix–Madrid**: [Kai Budde defeats Bernardo Da Costa Cabral](https://web.archive.org/web/20090227000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=sideboard/events/gpmad04)

**February 26-27: 2005 Grand Prix–Eindhoven**: [Sebastien Roux defeats Rogier Maaten](https://web.archive.org/web/20090227000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/roux-rules-over-eindhoven)

**February 24-25: 2007 Grand Prix–Dallas**: [Raphael Levy defeats Paul Cheon](https://web.archive.org/web/20090227000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/hall-famer-lone-star-dallas)

**February 23-24: 2008 Grand Prix–Vancouver**: [Paul Cheon defeats Ben Lundquist](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/cheon-conquers-canada-2)

**February 21-22: 2009 Grand Prix–Rotterdam**: [Arjan van Leeuwen defeats Robert van Medevoort](https://web.archive.org/web/20090227000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/arjan-van-leeuwen-smokes-rotterdam)
