Let's say you're wondering how to dress like a priest devoted to a demon on Innistrad. It doesn't matter why. It's probably for an upcoming convention or something. So here's [Skirsdag Flayer](https://web.archive.org/web/20120307000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skirsdag+Flayer) from *Dark Ascension*.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/923_skirsdagflayer.jpg)

[Skirsdag Flayer](https://web.archive.org/web/20120307000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skirsdag+Flayer) | Art by [Austin Hsu](https://web.archive.org/web/20120307000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Austin+Hsu%22%5D)

See that thing on the flayer's face? It turns out it's kind of a common thing on Innistrad. Check out [Disciple of Griselbrand](https://web.archive.org/web/20120307000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+Griselbrand) from the *Innistrad* set.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/923_discipleofgriselbrand.jpg)

[Disciple of Griselbrand](https://web.archive.org/web/20120307000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+Griselbrand) | Art by [Clint Cearley](https://web.archive.org/web/20120307000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Clint+Cearley%22%5D)

By now, you're probably wondering what that face-thing looks like from the back. Right? Of course you are! And conveniently enough, that's *also* been shown. Specifically, it's on the alternate-art [Ravenous Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20120307000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ravenous+Demon) that was [given out for *Dark Ascension* Prereleases](https://web.archive.org/web/20120307000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/alternate-art-2012-01-24)! As the demon prepares to consume its servant (and transform into [Archdemon of Greed](https://web.archive.org/web/20120307000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Archdemon+of+Greed)), the back of the priest's head is clearly visible. Just before it gets bitten off, probably.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/892_ravenousdemonpre.jpg)
