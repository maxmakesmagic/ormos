Dominic Lo wasn't exactly a household name when he set out to win Grand Prix-Auckland in 2008, but that didn't seem to faze him. Despite competing in a Top 8 with such luminaries as Olivier Ruel, Dominic stayed on target and rode his Bant draft deck, with a hint of red, to the championship trophy. Read more about Grand Prix–Auckland [here](/en/events/2008/gpauc08/lo-reaches-new-heights-in-auckland) and keep your eyes peeled for live coverage from Grand Prix-Hanover as the *Shards of Alara* Limited format returns to premier event action.
