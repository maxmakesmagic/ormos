The Turbo Land deck that's taking Standard by storm claimed a national champion when Ivan Floch took down the top slot at the 2010 Slovak Republic National Championship. The powerful deck accelerates out lots of lands in order to Time Warp and abuse Avenger of Zendikar, swinging with a horde of Plant tokens for the win. Ivan will take the Slovak Republic's national team to battle at the [2010 World Championships](https://web.archive.org/web/20121103022300/http://www.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=events/magic/worlds) to be held in Chiba, Japan in December.
