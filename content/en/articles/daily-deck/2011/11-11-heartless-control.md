Heartless Summoning is a card that was talked about in whispers as people tried to break it when *Innistrad* was released. Alex Mukhar is one such person, and he managed to put his own refined twist on it by using it in a Blue-Black Control deck! His top 16 deck from the StarCityGames.com Open Series threatens to overwhelm you with tons of powerful blue and black control fatties if you don't get Heartless Summoning off the table. Try this fun and powerful Standard deck tonight at a Friday Night **Magic** near you!

Alex Mukhar's Heartless Control 

Standard – StarCityGames.com Open in Las Vegas, NV
