Grand Prix Krakow was last weekend and the tournament was overrun by Dragon Control decks. While looking through the decks that didn't make Top 8, I stumbled across this innovative take on White-Blue Heroic. While Jan Ksandr failed to overtake the Dragons in Krakow, his Bant Heroic deck managed to earn him a very respectable ninth-place finish.

Bant Heroic isn't so much Bant as it is White-Blue Heroic with four [Dromoka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dromoka%27s+Command)s. Splashing for only four cards may not seem worth it, but [Dromoka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dromoka%27s+Command) is one of the best heroic enablers that we've seen in a while. [Dromoka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dromoka%27s+Command) is a modal spell, which means that you can choose what you want it to do. Unlike most modal spells, [Dromoka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dromoka%27s+Command) lets you make two choices instead of the usual one. In a Heroic deck, that's pretty huge. This spell is capable of having multiple targets and can potentially trigger your heroic creatures twice. [Dromoka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dromoka%27s+Command) can also be a removal spell if you choose the fight mode and can even take care of annoying enchantments like [Jeskai Ascendancy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jeskai+Ascendancy) and [Outpost Siege](https://web.archive.org/web/20150428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Outpost+Siege).

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ordeal+of+Heliod)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ordeal+of+Heliod)

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ordeal+of+Thassa)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ordeal+of+Thassa)

One of the most interesting interactions I've seen with this deck is between [Dromoka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dromoka%27s+Command) and the Ordeals. Most of the time when you attack with your heroic creature with an Ordeal attached to it, you will sacrifice it when your creature has three +1/+1 counters. However, the Ordeals read "When you sacrifice this." Your Ordeals will trigger whenever you sacrifice them, no matter how that happens. If you are in a tricky spot where you absolutely need to gain 10 life or draw two cards, you have the option of casting [Dromoka's Command](https://web.archive.org/web/20150428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dromoka%27s+Command) on yourself, forcing you to sacrifice an enchantment and trigger your Ordeal.
