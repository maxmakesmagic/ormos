### Details For And Thanks To The Players

* * *

After the cancellation of the [large tournament last Saturday](/en/articles/feature/2003/03-12-another-tournament-announcement), many players expressed an interest in knowing more about what happened. Without going into the technical details, here is how things played out.

When the free tournament launched on Saturday morning, a bug in the tournament code caused the ***Magic** Online* servers to shut down. The development team isolated the bug and realized that the servers couldn't be successfully restarted with the large tournament in place. As the top priority was to get the servers up and running again, the large tournament had to be cancelled before a restart was attempted. When the servers came back up, the team began the series of replacement small tournaments, as the bug would prevent another large tournament from starting without another system shutdown.

Since the restart on Saturday, the servers have been completely stable. The bug found this weekend has no impact on the normal day-to-day operation of the ***Magic** Online* servers or the [Worlds Qualifier Tournaments](/en/articles/feature/2003/03-24-press-release).

We understand that many of you set aside a great deal of time to be a part of Saturday's event, and hope you were able to enjoy the smaller replacement tournaments. Wizards of the Coast would like to thank everyone who volunteered their time on Saturday to participate in the large event. Because of the large number of players involved (approximately 2,500), we were able to isolate a tournament code bug that would've been difficult to discover without your assistance. We may run a test like this again in a few weeks--we'll keep you posted.

If you have any further concerns or questions, please contact the Wizards of the Coast [Technical Support group](https://web.archive.org/web/20041211074516/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/magiconline/tspage).

Sincerely,  
 The ***Magic** Online* Team
