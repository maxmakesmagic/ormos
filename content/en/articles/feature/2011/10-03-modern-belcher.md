Modern is beginning to flex its muscle as a new format, and recently had a new set of bannings announced. While that will change many Modern decklists, that hasn't stopped players from testing the waters with new creations. Take, for example, this [Goblin Charbelcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20111003000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Charbelcher) list used on **Magic Online** by 1nf0rm4luko. It features just 3 [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20111003000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain)s, but with a host of other mana sources it can cast its combo extremely quickly: [Goblin Charbelcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20111003000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Charbelcher), which is virtually guaranteed to be lethal with so few lands in the deck, or [Grapeshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20111003000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grapeshot) combined with [Pyromancer's Swath](https://web.archive.org/web/20111003000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pyromancer%27s+Swath). How will this deck change with [the bannings](/en/articles/feature/2011/09-20-september-20-2011-dci-banned-restricted-list-announcement) in effect?
