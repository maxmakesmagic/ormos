## Pioneer Super Qualifier Saturday

On Saturday, August 27, there will be a Pioneer Super Qualifier with a start time of 7 a.m. PT (14:00 UTC). The entry fee is 40 MOCS Qualifier Points, 40 Event Tickets, or 400 Play Points. The top four finishers qualify for the tabletop Regional Championship Season 1.

Learn more about [Super Qualifier events](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-qualifiers-super-qualifiers).

* * *

## Modern Qualifier Sunday

On Sunday, August 28, there will be a Modern Qualifier with a special start time of 1 a.m. PT (08:00 UTC). The event costs 40 Season 2 QPs, and the top two finishers will earn an invitation to the tabeltop Regional Championship Season 1.

Learn more about [Qualifier events](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-qualifiers-super-qualifiers).

## August Special Events: *Double Masters 2022* Duels

We have heard plenty of feedback from players regarding adding Limited to the menu of weekly Format Challenge choices.

As an experiment, we are trying out a new event type on weekends this month that has a set number of rounds (like Preliminaries) but has greater prizes (like Challenges). For now, we are working these in around the existing schedule.

***Double Masters 2022*** **Duels**

**Time**: Through August 28, Saturdays and Sundays at 1 a.m. PT (8:00 UTC) and noon PT (19:00 UTC)

*   Replaces Limited Prelims in the following time slots: 1 a.m. PT Saturday, noon PT Saturday, and noon PT Sunday
*   New slot at 1 a.m. PT Sunday
    *   Reminder – Saturday 1 a.m. there is no Duel due to the Modern Qualifier.

**Entry**: 45 Event Tickets or 450 Play Points  
**Format**: *Double Masters 2022* Sealed Deck  
**Min-Max Players**: 65–256  
**Rounds**: 7 (no Playoff)  
**Prizes**:

## *Vintage Cube* Until September 1

*Vintage Cube* is back and runs until *Dominaria United* events begin on September 1! There are no changes from June, and both the regular league and single-elimination queue will be offered. [Check out the full list of cards](/en/articles/uncategorised/2016/11-30-vintage-cube-cardlist).

## *Double Masters 2022* on MTGO

Our newest set, *Double Masters 2022*, is now live in the game. Sealed, Draft, and Phantom Draft Leagues are available. Check out the [release article](/en/articles/magiconline/2022/06-29-double-masters-2022-magic-online-edition) to learn all about events, avatars (both for *Streets of New Capenna* and *Double Masters 2022*), and how Cryptic Spires will work in the game. Be sure to also check out [the updated Treasure Chest info page](/en/articles/magiconline/2020/07-27-magic-online-treasure-chest-information).

## *Magic Online* Format Challenges Every Weekend

Format Challenges are the first step toward qualifying for Premier Play through your favorite *Magic* formats and provide excellent prizes for those looking to test their mettle in their favorite formats. [Learn more about Premier Play qualification](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2021) to get started today.

*Magic Online*'s Format Challenges run once on Saturdays and once on Sundays for each Constructed format! On weekends featuring Showcase Challenge events, the Format Challenge that starts at either 6 a.m., 8 a.m., or 10 a.m. Pacific time is pre-empted by the Showcase Challenge—Format Challenges scheduled at other times remain in place.

*NOTES:* 

*   *Pauper and Vintage Challenges are never pre-empted.*
*   *Saturday Legacy Challenges require 32 players and give prizes to Top 32.*
*   *Sunday Legacy Challenges require 64 players and give prizes to Top 64.*
