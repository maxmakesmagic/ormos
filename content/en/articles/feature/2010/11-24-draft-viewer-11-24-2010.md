Welcome back to the Draft Viewer! Today we've got a classic viewer from the 2009 World Championships. This was the second of two triple-*Zendikar* Booster Drafts from the second day of Worlds and featured not one but two 2010 Pro Tour Hall of Fame inductees: Tomoharu Saito and Bram Snepvangers. The [2010 World Championships](https://web.archive.org/web/20101124000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=events/magic/worlds) begin December 9 in Chiba, Japan.
