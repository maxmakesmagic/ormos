Here are the solutions to [Monday's Magic: The Puzzling puzzles](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/magic-puzzling%E2%80%94guildpact-2006-02-27).

Part I: Replication Aberration 

### Puzzle 1a:

[![Diagram 1a](https://i.ormos.uk/02/b4/02b49f1391ccb02e9b0e46e96ce9da259734e4e19687e9d33d7bd59e442fe519.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/features/314_Diagram1aBig.jpg)

1.  Play the [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island) from your hand.
2.  Tap enough of your lands to add seven Blue and two Red mana to your mana pool. Leave [Steam Vents](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steam+Vents) untapped.
3.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif) to animate [Genju of the Spires](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genju+of+the+Spires).
4.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif) to cast [Rain of Embers](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rain+of+Embers), killing your [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain) and returning [Genju of the Spires](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genju+of+the+Spires) to your hand.
5.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif) to animate [Genju of the Falls](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genju+of+the+Falls).
6.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif) to cast [Genju of the Spires](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genju+of+the+Spires) on [Steam Vents](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steam+Vents).
7.  Attack with your Genju.
8.  After no blockers are declared, use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif) to animate your [Steam Vents](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steam+Vents) via [Genju of the Spires](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genju+of+the+Spires). This makes it a 6/1 that will attack for lethal damage.

### Puzzle 1b:

[![Diagram 1b](https://i.ormos.uk/aa/47/aa472e47bce88b8a412aabb2db91b4410f9a19e7c3a33bb6c9249274b1d9cd74.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/features/314_Diagram1bBig.jpg)

1.  Play the [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island) from your hand.
2.  Tap all your lands for nine Blue and two Red mana.
3.  Cast [Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Niv-Mizzet%252C%2Bthe%2BFiremind) and [Drowned Rusalka](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drowned+Rusalka).
4.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif) to animate [Genju of the Spires](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genju+of+the+Spires).
5.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif) to sacrifice your animated land via your Rusalka. Discard any card and draw a card. Target [Gelectrode](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gelectrode) with [Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Niv-Mizzet%252C%2Bthe%2BFiremind)'s trigger.
6.  With [Gelectrode](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gelectrode) out of the way, sacrifice [Wild Cantor](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Cantor) for ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif) and cast [Surge of Zeal](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Surge+of+Zeal) targeting [Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Niv-Mizzet%252C%2Bthe%2BFiremind).
7.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif) to sacrifice your Rusalka. Discard any card and draw a card. Target your opponent with [Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Niv-Mizzet%252C%2Bthe%2BFiremind)'s trigger.
8.  Attack with [Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Niv-Mizzet%252C%2Bthe%2BFiremind) for the other four damage.

### Puzzle 1c:

[![Diagram 1c](https://i.ormos.uk/1b/8a/1b8ac80456e5c742dcba2a840309d368b4432d1a7ad93458ba69971836622edc.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/features/314_Diagram1cBig.jpg)

1.  Play an [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island).
2.  Tap all of your lands except for one [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island) to add ten Blue and two Red mana to your mana pool. Make sure that the untapped [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island) is not the one you just played.
3.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif) to animate each of your Genjus.
4.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif) to cast [Rain of Embers](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rain+of+Embers), killing your [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain) and returning [Genju of the Spires](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genju+of+the+Spires) to your hand.
5.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif) to cast [Genju of the Spires](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genju+of+the+Spires) on [Steam Vents](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steam+Vents).
6.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif) to animate your [Steam Vents](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Steam+Vents) via [Genju of the Spires](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genju+of+the+Spires). It still has one damage on it, and will go to your graveyard. Both Genjus will return to your hand.
7.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif) to cast [Genju of the Falls](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genju+of+the+Falls) on an untapped [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island).
8.  Use your remaining ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif) to animate your Genju, and attack for the final three damage.

### Puzzle 1d:

[![Diagram 1d](https://i.ormos.uk/75/a1/75a16b1571c5becd6c8a9d7ed41e5f84a8aa7db77064b433f6d822d2a2d3b6be.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/features/314_Diagram1dBig.jpg)

1.  Play a [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain).
2.  Tap enough of your lands to add five Blue and three Red mana to your mana pool. Leave your [Genju of the Spires](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genju+of+the+Spires)-enchanted land untapped.
3.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif) to cast [Gelectrode](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gelectrode).
4.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif) to cast [Surge of Zeal](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Surge+of+Zeal) targeting [Gelectrode](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gelectrode).
5.  Tap [Gelectrode](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gelectrode) targeting [Dripping-Tongue Zubera](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dripping-Tongue+Zubera).
6.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/red.gif) to cast [Rain of Embers](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rain+of+Embers). With that on the stack, [Gelectrode](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gelectrode) will untap. Tap it to destroy the Zubera and put the 1/1 token into play.
7.  [Rain of Embers](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rain+of+Embers) will resolve, kill all of your opponent's potential blockers.
8.  Use ![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif)![](https://magic.wizards.com/sites/all/modules/custom/wiz_autocard/wiz_manacost/manasymbols/blue.gif) to animate [Genju of the Spires](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Genju+of+the+Spires).
9.  Attack for the remaining six damage.

Part II: Ideas Unbound 

### Puzzle 2:

[![Diagram 2](https://i.ormos.uk/4f/5e/4f5eae59ec47cb3dfd3d94eca58cb2b7c5498af14f0a3ed9e78041f04c24721c.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=magic/images/mtgcom/fcpics/features/314_Diagram2Big.jpg)

1.   End your turn.
2.   The rest of the solution occurs as a stack of instant effects during your opponent's upkeep.
3.   Tap all lands for 27 mana.
4.   Tap [Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Niv-Mizzet%252C%2Bthe%2BFiremind) to draw a card. The [Underworld Dreams](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Dreams)' trigger is stacked, then [Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Niv-Mizzet%252C%2Bthe%2BFiremind)'s, targeting Mandark. Let the Mizzet trigger resolve dealing one damage to Mandark.
5.   Cast [Gigadrowse](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gigadrowse), replicate five times, all targeting your Guildmage. (21 mana, six [Gigadrowse](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gigadrowse) on stack)
6.   Cast [Rewind](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rewind), replicate four times, each other targeting a unique [Gigadrowse](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gigadrowse) copy (one being the original).
7.   Upon each resolution of [Rewind](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rewind), untap three [Izzet Boilerworks](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Izzet+Boilerworks) and an [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island), tapping them for eleven mana.
8.   This puts [Rewind](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rewind) and [Gigadrowse](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gigadrowse) into your graveyard and leaves you with 56 mana and a copy of [Gigadrowse](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gigadrowse) on the stack.
9.   Cast [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand) targeting [Gigadrowse](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gigadrowse) and replicate it. Target the original [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand) with the copy.
10.  The copied [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand) returns the original to your hand, and you draw a card. The [Underworld Dreams](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Underworld+Dreams)' trigger is stacked, then [Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Niv-Mizzet%252C%2Bthe%2BFiremind)'s, targeting Mandark. Let the Mizzet trigger resolve, dealing one damage to Mandark.
11.  This leaves a Dreams trigger on the stack and two cards in your library.
12.  Repeat steps 9-10 two times, leaving you with 44 mana. You deal two more damage and draw your entire library.
13.  Cast [Stream of Consciousness](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stream+of+Consciousness) with no targets, replicate once.
14.  Before the replicate trigger resolves, cast [Induce Paranoia](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Induce+Paranoia) on the original Stream. This puts Stream, [Induce Paranoia](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Induce+Paranoia), [Gigadrowse](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gigadrowse) and [Rewind](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rewind) in your grave. (36 mana)
15.  Let the replicate trigger resolve, targeting all four cards in your library with the copy of Stream.
16.  Repeat steps 9-10 four times to draw your library again. (20 mana)
17.  Cast [Gigadrowse](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gigadrowse), replicate three times. (16 mana).
18.  Cast [Rewind](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rewind), replicate three times, each targeting one of the three new [Gigadrowse](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gigadrowse).
19.  After getting the mana from the [Rewind](https://web.archive.org/web/20060301000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rewind)s, you will have 44 mana.
