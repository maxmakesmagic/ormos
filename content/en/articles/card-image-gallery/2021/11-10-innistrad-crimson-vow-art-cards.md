[*Innistrad: Crimson Vow*](https://web.archive.org/web/20211110000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/innistrad-crimson-vow) Set Boosters include an art card showcasing one of 81 amazing illustrations from the set. Even better, 1 in 20 art cards opened are gold stamped with an artist's signature or Planeswalker symbol.

Learn more about Set Boosters from the [*Innistrad: Crimson Vow* Product Overview](/en/articles/feature/2021/10-28-innistrad-crimson-vow-product-overview) and check out the foil-stamped versions of the art cards below (non-stamped versions have the same art without the signature).
