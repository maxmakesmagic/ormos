[Kamahl, Pit Fighter](https://web.archive.org/web/20051121000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Kamahl%252C%2BPit%2BFighter) was not the only character to undergo a [Kamahl, Fist of Krosa](https://web.archive.org/web/20051121000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Kamahl%252C%2BFist%2Bof%2BKrosa) in the *Odyssey* block storyline. 

Kamahl's mentor, the dwarf weaponsmaster **[Balthor the Stout](https://web.archive.org/web/20051121000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Balthor%2Bthe%2BStout)**, had a bit of a change of heart as well.

![Balthor the Stout art by Ron Spears](https://i.ormos.uk/d6/6b/d66bf37f6fa7a23ec35c70cae1052197babb7ac908cbe88f57a878689e6d2499.jpg)  
*[Balthor the Stout](https://web.archive.org/web/20051121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balthor+the+Stout) art by Ron Spears* 

Here is Balthor in his prime: surrounded by the barbarians of the Pardic Mountains, getting them pumped up and ready to fight. A fierce warrior and hero among men. 

But Balthor is killed in battle with the nightmarish [Laquatus's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20051121000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Laquatus%2527s%2BChampion) of the villainous merfolk [Ambassador Laquatus](https://web.archive.org/web/20051121000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Ambassador%2BLaquatus). 

That's not the end of the heroic dwarf's story, however. 

Balthor was later reanimated, his only goal to seek revenge on the evil Laquatus. 

![Balthor the Defiled art by Carl Critchlow](https://i.ormos.uk/05/05/05057c2f77440a81dd4a841be3dfb6f1a62d3cffab9f6f577ae30a56bf9bb8b4.jpg)  
*[Balthor the Defiled](https://web.archive.org/web/20051121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balthor+the+Defiled) art by Carl Critchlow* 

This Balthor is at his most desperate: his undead form is a curse, but a way to get vengeance on Laquatus. Laquatus proves able to control the zombified Balthor, however, due to the remnant of [Laquatus's champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20051121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Laquatus%27s+champion) still lodged in his body. (Ironically, the card [Balthor the Defiled](https://web.archive.org/web/20051121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Balthor+the+Defiled) is quite a nice combo with a graveyard full of [Laquatus's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20051121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Laquatus%27s+Champion)s.)

At the end, Balthor's disciple [Kamahl, Fist of Krosa](https://web.archive.org/web/20051121000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Kamahl%252C%2BFist%2Bof%2BKrosa) does him the favor of slaying him for the second time -- and Kamahl slays Laquatus with his [Mirari](https://web.archive.org/web/20051121000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mirari)-enchanted sword, leaving Balthor finally at peace.

Enjoy Dwarf Week.
