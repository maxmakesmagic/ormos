Ladies and Gentlemen, you have a new deck to beat. Black Devotion decks have been at the top of the Standard pecking order for quite some time. Due to the power of [Temples](https://web.archive.org/web/20140513000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&format=+[%22Theros+Block%22]&type=+[%22Land%22]&name=+[Temple]), both white and red have seen success as splashes in Black Devotion. With *Journey into Nyx*, Temple of Malady opens up the option of splashing green. Andrew Tenjum recently won the [StarCityGames Open in Cincinnati](http://www.starcitygames.com/events/030514_cincinnati.html) with a Black Devotion deck with a light splash of green. 

 ![Gray Merchant of Asphodel](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Gray%20Merchant%20of%20Asphodel&options=) 

The appeal of a green splash becomes apparent when you look at other new options with *Journey into Nyx*. Banishing Light has made its way into all sorts of white decks, and combined with Detention Sphere as an additional target, Abrupt Decay looks very appealing as a removal spell that will rarely lack a target. Vraska the Unseen and Golgari Charm ramp up the enchantment removal, which is important, given the amount of enchantments in Standard now with the introduction of *Journey into Nyx*.
