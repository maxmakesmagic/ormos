Ever since its printing, [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) has been the subject of many brews, thought experiments, and crazy stories. The card is so unique and powerful that it has captured the hearts or minds of many **Magic** players. Over time, with just the printing of a single good creature at some spot along the curve, the entire make-up of [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) might change. It has been everything from Naya to Bant to green-blue-black. Over the past few months, a certain kind of [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) deck, Zombie Pod, has become popular. We saw the deck in action before ***Magic** 2013* was released, but I wanted to take another look at the archetype, to see where it has evolved to.

![Birthing Pod](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Birthing+Pod)

The backbone of any Zombie Pod deck is, in fact, the Zombies. Both [Gravecrawler](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravecrawler) and [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) have great synergy with the namesake card, making them very important to the core of the deck. From there, [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) looks to get value out of a lot of niche one-ofs that it inherently looks to abuse in certain match ups. [Massacre Wurm](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Massacre+Wurm) is a great example of that in this list, providing a one-sided sweeper that might just kill the opponent. You can imagine how bad the Wurm is against Ramp or whatever, but against a tokens deck, it becomes an all-star.

Similar power levels on other one-ofs exist in here as well. [Skinrender](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Skinrender) and [Thragtusk](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thragtusk) are both monsters against aggressive decks. [Phyrexian Metamorph](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Metamorph) is great against Ramp or just as your own enabler. A cool addition to the deck with M13 is [Disciple of Bolas](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+Bolas). Even just sacrificing a [Gravecrawler](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gravecrawler) gives you a reverse [Night's Whisper](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Night%27s+Whisper), which is fine, but it scales from there, hitting [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger) or [Thragtusk](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thragtusk) for extra value. [Blood Artist](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Artist) helps to keep your life total high while [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) does the opposite. This list can really take on any form, going long against a deck through card advantage or trying to win early with an onslaught of one-mana 2/2s. And as a bonus, even though the deck is technically three colors, there isn't a land in the deck that doesn't cast [Geralf's Messenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20120917000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geralf%27s+Messenger)!
