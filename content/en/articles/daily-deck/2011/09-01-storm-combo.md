Modern is on deck for this weekend's Pro Tour Philadelphia, and you can count on having plenty of fun combo options for players tackling the big event. In a recent Magic Online tournament, Gamemaster32 used Storm Combo to get the job done. How does the deck work? By casting lots of cards that draw you another card, to put together enough cards like Pyretic Ritual and Desperate Ritual, to create a storm and mana count large enough to cast both Pyromancer's Swath and Grapeshot in the same turn. With an undefeated performance, this is one deck to keep an eye on this weekend!
