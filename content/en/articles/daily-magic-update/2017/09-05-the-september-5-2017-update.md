# Deck of the Day

1.  [Robin Dolar's Blue-Black Control](https://web.archive.org/web/20210923031817/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/gptur17/top-8-decklists-grand-prix-turin-2017-09-03)

Last week we pointed to [The Scarab God](https://web.archive.org/web/20170905000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=The+Scarab+God) as The Next Big Thing, and this week, it wins a Grand Prix. Coincidence? Probably mostly. But the deck it did it with was a bit of a surprise.

Blue-Black Control has been overshadowed by Blue-Red Control for quite some time and by White-Blue Control (specifically [Approach of the Second Sun](https://web.archive.org/web/20170905000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Approach+of+the+Second+Sun)) lately. But Dolar, who's no stranger to Grand Prix success, took the top spot this past weekend in Turin. The deck is remarkable, like most we've been highlighting lately, in that most of it doesn't rotate.

The main deck loses [Flaying Tendrils](https://web.archive.org/web/20170905000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flaying+Tendrils), [Grasp of Darkness](https://web.archive.org/web/20170905000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grasp+of+Darkness), and seven of its dual lands. [Drowned Catacomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20170905000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drowned+Catacomb)s will replace four of the dual lands, so the mana doesn't take much of a drop. Whether [Flaying Tendrils](https://web.archive.org/web/20170905000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flaying+Tendrils) is necessary is more of a metagame question, though [Grasp of Darkness](https://web.archive.org/web/20170905000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grasp+of+Darkness) is certainly a loss. On the other hand, Vraska's Contempt is certainly a welcome addition to the deck.

The sideboard will certainly have to change, but it always does. So long Kalitas. *Arrivederci* [Liliana, the Last Hope](https://web.archive.org/web/20170905000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Liliana%2C+the+Last+Hope). You will be missed.
