It's not every day you get another shot at something big.

In 2018, we unveiled [*Core Set Cube* on *Magic Online*](/en/articles/magiconline/2018/10-30-cube-spotlight-series-core-set-cube)—a cube with a simple premise: every card had to come from a core set. Would players be interested in something like this? Turns out, yes! It had a clear theme, clever inclusions, a resonant name, and it was a fan favorite.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Resurrection)](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Resurrection)

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Regrowth)](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Regrowth)

However, it was clearly time for a change. It felt like it didn't live up to its full potential. It's been about a year since the last time we've seen *Core Set Cube*, and since then, it's gotten some big upgrades, new perspectives, and a name change.

Hi, I'm Harper O'Neill, designer of ***Alpha Core Set Cube***. I'm the senior retail development specialist for the Wizards Play Network at Wizards of the Coast, and I work closely with your local game stores to bring out their best. I've also organized cube nights at Wizards for almost nine years, and this cube has withstood the test of time. You can find me at [@imharperoneill](https://twitter.com/imharperoneill?lang=en) on Twitter. It's wonderful that I get to reintroduce myself to you with my favorite cube.

***Alpha Core Set Cube*** **comes to *Magic Online* on September 29 and will run for a week!**

# About the Cube

The biggest change is now we have access to **all** core sets from *Magic*'s history instead of limiting players to *Seventh Edition* and onwards. In fact, you'll find over 50 cards in this list that were originally printed in *Limited Edition Alpha*, and the total card list is remarkably different.

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Berserk)](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Berserk)

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ankh+of+Mishra)](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ankh+of+Mishra)

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Ball+Lightning)](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ball+Lightning)

A few things to know about the design:

**It's powerful!**

This cube has a feast of efficient threats and interaction. Don't get caught off guard, and don't underestimate the power of combat tricks like [Giant Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Giant+Growth) or [Feat of Resistance](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Feat+of+Resistance). The land base also got a huge upgrade with the original dual lands.

**It's nostalgic.**

You'll find classic *Magic* archetypes and modern draft standouts here. What are some of your favorite strategies from years gone by? Perhaps Blue Tempo is your style, or Red Sligh! Maybe you just want to ramp into [Grave Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grave+Titan) or load up your creatures with +1/+1 counters.

**It's largely monocolor.**

There aren't many multicolor cards available to core sets in general, so this cube has very few. Getting back to basics means selecting the most iconic cards from *Magic*'s history and having a good, old-fashioned color pie battle. The colors do what you expect them to do.

What you **won't** see:

While *Alpha* is involved, you won't see [Black Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Lotus), [Sol Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sol+Ring), or the Moxen, and many versions of the cards you'll play with aren't from *Alpha* itself. There are a few other notable omissions for power concerns. However, this cube's power level remains relatively high despite only having access to a small portion of *Magic*'s overall history and manages to keep its personality fun and distinct from others.

But one member of The Power Nine can be found in this cube: [Timetwister](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Timetwister)'s time to twist has come!

I've also removed a great number of low-performing cards and archetypes we saw in previous versions. Over 150 cards were removed or added to make this experience more accessible without luring players into draft traps. Sorry, Enchantress fans!

# Fun Things to Try

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Copy+Artifact)](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Copy+Artifact)

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Mana+Flare)](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Flare)

[![](https://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Dark+Ritual)](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Ritual)

Here's some things you could try!

1.   Discard a big Dragon and bring it back with **Resurrection**. Yes, you heard that right: Red-White Reanimator!
2.   Say "no" to combat with a Turbo Fog strategy. Play a control game while you draw your opponent out with **[Howling Mine](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Howling+Mine)** and keep your life total high with **[Fog](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fog)** and board wipes. Finish them off with **[Braingeyser](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Braingeyser)**.
3.   **[Dark Ritual](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dark+Ritual)** into **[Hypnotic Specter](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hypnotic+Specter)**! I'm sure they'll love that.
4.   Create a gigantic **[Chasm Skulker](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chasm+Skulker)** with **[Timetwister](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Timetwister)** or **[Wheel of Fortune](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wheel+of+Fortune)**.
5.   Double (or quadruple) a creature's power and **[Fling](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fling)** it straight to your opponent's face! With **[Berserk](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Berserk)** and **[Unleash Fury](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Unleash+Fury)** in the cube, the capacity for massive damage output is quite high.
6.   Show them the **[Door (to Nothingness)](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Door+to+Nothingness)**. You can pry this *Core Set Cube* strategy from my cold, dead hands.
7.   Create a team of killer robots with cards like **[Scuttling Doom Engine](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Scuttling+Doom+Engine)** and **[Copy Artifact](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Copy+Artifact)**.
8.   Flood the board with a nigh-infinite number of Goblins using **[Krenko, Mob Boss](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Krenko%2C+Mob+Boss)** and **[Intruder Alarm](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Intruder+Alarm)**. Don't have Krenko? **[Ant Queen](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ant+Queen)** and some mana-producing Elves will work just as well.
9.   Cast or copy an insanely big **[Fireball](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fireball)**. Cards like **[Mana Flare](https://web.archive.org/web/20210928000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Flare)** will help fuel huge flashy spells that can end the game in an instant (well, sorcery).
10.  Discover something new!

Have fun! I'll see you in the queues.

Harper
