Remember the symbol of Phyrexia? We mentioned in an [earlier Arcana](/en/articles/arcana/2004/02-16-the-symbol-of-phyrexia) how it crops up in the art of cards like Order of Yawgmoth and Yawgmoth's Edict, and looks a little something like this:

![The symbol of Phyrexia](https://i.ormos.uk/52/02/5202c099ba339c4b8c355e83bf63e36506a9e2dc7c6b6f8f0987a631b22c4941.jpg "The symbol of Phyrexia") 

Yawgmoth Demon follows suit in its Pete Venters-illustrated *Ninth Edition* printing. Check it out.

 ![Yawgmoth Demon art by Pete Venters](https://i.ormos.uk/ae/69/ae69c14ea8415fbed7605f0afc694b09827288b6a28bdd7c7a2cc3d9967743b3.jpg) 

Its head bears the mark of Phyrexia, and its staff has the circle-and-bar design as well. But of course, you don't have to be Phyrexian to order this artifact-hungry demon around. Just open a pack of *Ninth*. 

 ![Yawgmoth Demon](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Yawgmoth%20Demon&options=)
