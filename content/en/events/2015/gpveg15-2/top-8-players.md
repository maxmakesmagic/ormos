### David Heineman

**Age:** 32

**Hometown:** Highland Park, IL

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Pro Tour attendance

**What did you play Saturday, and how did you do?**

I had a scrappy 4-color control/proliferate deck. It was amazing and I had some excellent games.

**What did you draft today, and how did that go?**

I drafted Elementals in the first draft, which was stellar. My second draft was average, but was a very successful green-white tokens deck.

**What were your best commons or uncommons this weekend? Why?**

[Nest Invader](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nest+Invader)! It's power, acceleration, and great at chump blocking.

**What do you want open for pack one, pick one? Why?**

Savaged Twister. It leads me where I want to be.

**What have you done to #MakeMagicHistory?**

I finally have a *Magic* resume for my personal #MagicHistory.

### Brian Richards

**Age:** 25

**Hometown:** Roxana, IL

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** None

**What did you play Saturday, and how did you do?**

An insane pool of two [Comet Storm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Comet+Storm), Elesh Norn, [Precursor Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Precursor+Golem), [Sunforger](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sunforger), and good red and white creatures. I only lost one match when my opponent's [Artisan of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Artisan+of+Kozilek)ed back [Precursor Golem](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Precursor+Golem). I only lost one other game.

**What did you draft today, and how did that go?**

I started the day with a red-white deck that had 2 [Hellkite Charger](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hellkite+Charger)s in it. Then I drafted a green-black tokens deck that had [Necrogenesi](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Necrogenesi)s and 3 [Bone Splinters](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bone+Splinters).

**What do you want open for pack one, pick one? Why?**

[Karn](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Karn), because he is the easiest bomb to cast.

**What have you done to #MakeMagicHistory?**

Top 8ed my first Limited Grand Prix.

**Age:** 28 (completed yesterday!)

**Hometown:** Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Top 8 Pro Tour *Return to Ravnica*

Top 8 Grand Prix Santiago

**What did you play Saturday, and how did you do?**

BWr, because I thought I needed removal with 3 [Gorehorn Minotaurs](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gorehorn+Minotaurs) in my pool. Obviously it was a terrible choice, and I sided into straight red-white every round. 8-1

Ru Elementals, 3-0

BWu Spirits, 2-0-1

**What were your best commons or uncommons this weekend? Why?**

[Mulldrifter](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mulldrifter)

I didn't even have a ticket for this event till Wednesday!

### Shaun McLaren

**Age:** 27

**Hometown:** Edmonton, Alberta

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Winner, Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*

Second place, Pro tour *Khans of Tarkir*

Etc.

**What did you play Saturday, and how did you do?**

RUG Ramp Control with [Primeval Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Primeval+Titan) and [Banefire](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banefire). 8-1

**What did you draft today, and how did that go?**

Four-color Ramp Control without white ([Savage Twister](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Savage+Twister) and [Wildfire](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wildfire)). 3-0

Four-color Durdle without blue. 2-0-1

**What were your best commons or uncommons this weekend? Why?**

[Wayfarer's Bauble](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wayfarer%27s+Bauble). It's colorless fixing and ramp.

Also [Tribal Flames](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tribal+Flames). No one else wants it much.

**What do you want open for pack one, pick one? Why?**

[Wildfire](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wildfire). It completely breaks the game.

**What have you done to #MakeMagicHistory?**

I went back in time and gave Richard Garfield a [Black Lotus](https://web.archive.org/web/20150531000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Lotus).
