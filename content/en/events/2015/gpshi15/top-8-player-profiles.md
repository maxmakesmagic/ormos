The field of 2205 players has been narrowed down to 8. You can learn more about Grand Prix Shizuoka 2015's Top 8 players below.

# Yuki Matsumoto

 ![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpshi15/TOP8_matsumoto-Yuki.jpg)

**Hometown:** Tokyo  
**Age:** 23  
**Occupation:** Student

**Previous Accomplishments:**  
 Nothing.

**Where do you normally play *Magic*?**|  
 Hareruya. I started *Magic* Online this season.

**What was your record in Sealed Deck? What did you play, and did you have any key cards?**  
 7-2 with 1 bye. I played Jeskai with [Arrow Storm](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Arrow+Storm).

**What were your records in the drafts? What did you play, and did you have any key cards?**  
 I played two Temur decks, going 3-0 both times.

**What is the most underrated card in this format?**  
[Blinding Spray](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinding+Spray), [Monastery Flock](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Flock), [Bloodfire Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bloodfire+Mentor), and Dragon’s Eye Savants.

**What is your favorite Draft strategy?**  
 Walls, unblockables, and removals.

**What surprised you the most at Grand Prix Shizuoka?**  
 In the 3rd pack of the 1st draft, I received a 12th-pick [Swiftwater Cliffs](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swiftwater+Cliffs) and 13th-pick [Thornwood Falls](https://web.archive.org/web/20150111000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thornwood+Falls).

**Which *Fate Reforged* preview has excited you the most?**  
 Temporal Trespass and Ugin, the Spirit Dragon.
