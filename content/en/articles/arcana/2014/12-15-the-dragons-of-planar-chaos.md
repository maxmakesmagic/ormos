Prior to the release of *Magic: The Gathering—Commander*, there were few wedge-colored legendary creatures. So few, in fact, that there were but six standard bearers of the wedges. Excepting [Doran, the Siege Tower](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doran%2C+the+Siege+Tower), these five legends of the wedges were all found in *Planar Chaos* as the alternative-history versions of three-color dragon legends *(Note: An earlier version of this article incorrectly called them Elder Dragons. They are not, though they are eldar dragons in the simple English sense of the phrase)*. Little is known about these five mighty dragons that have headlined many a Commander deck over the years, and they remain shrouded in mystery. However, we can shed a little light on them through the art descriptions that gave them birth.

First, each dragon had the same note in its description that tells us what we’re working with:

***Notes:** This dragon should look sentient, like it has a personality and thinks for itself (unlike our normal, predatory red dragons).* 

As for the rest of the description? Read on.

# Numot, the Devastator

**Color:** Red primarily, also blue and white  
**Location:** sky  
**Action:** Show the elder dragon Numot—a legendary, ancient, sentient creature. Numot’s scales are a rich orange-red, and he should seem passionate, engaged, dynamic.  
**Focus:** the dragon legend  
**Mood:** Vibrant, charismatic, powerful

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/cardart/cardart_numotthedevastator.jpg)

Art by [Dan Dos Santos](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Dan+Dos+Santos%22%5D)

# Teneb, the Harvester

**Color:** Black primarily, also green and white  
**Location:** sky  
**Action:** Show the elder dragon Teneb—a legendary, ancient, sentient creature. Teneb has dusky, dark scales and a paler underbelly that almost seems to have algae growth on it. This dragon should seem like a weary schemer.  
**Focus:** the dragon legend  
**Mood:** Brooding, bitter, a little sleepy

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/cardart/cardart_tenebtheharvester.jpg)

Art by [Zoltan Boros & Gabor Szikszai](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Zoltan+Boros+&+Gabor+Szikszai%22%5D)

# Vorosh, the Hunter

**Color:** Green primarily, also blue and black  
**Location:** sky  
**Action:** Show the elder dragon Vorosh—a legendary, ancient, sentient creature. Vorosh has scales that are the color of the darkest storms and waters: dark greens and blues. He is the strongest and largest among these elder dragons.  
**Focus:** the dragon legend  
**Mood:** Roiling and ferocious, all muscle

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/cardart/cardart_voroshthehunter.jpg)

Art by [Mark Zug](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Mark+Zug%22%5D)

# Oros, the Avenger

**Color:** White primarily, also black and red  
**Location:** sky  
**Action:** Show the elder dragon Oros—a legendary, ancient, sentient creature. Oros has scales like a golden pearl, with a cruel look in his eye.  
**Focus:** the dragon legend  
**Mood:** Beautiful and cruel

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/cardart/cardart_orostheavenger.jpg)

Art by [Daren Bader](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Daren+Bader%22%5D)

# Intet, the Dreamer

**Color:** Blue primarily, also red and green  
**Location:** sky  
**Action:** Show the elder dragon Intet—a legendary, ancient, sentient creature. Intet is an idealist and sage among dragons. She has bluish opalescent scales that glint with magenta and green in the light.  
**Focus:** the dragon legend  
**Mood:** Fluid and meditative

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/images/cardart/cardart_intetthedreamer.jpg)

Art by [Dan Scott](https://web.archive.org/web/20141215000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Dan+Scott%22%5D)
