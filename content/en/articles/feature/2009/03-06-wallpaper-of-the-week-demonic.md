This week's desktop wallpaper is *two* wallpapers! As a special preview of *Duel Decks: Divine vs. Demonic*, we're going to show you two of **Magic**'s most demonic cards: [Lord of the Pit](https://web.archive.org/web/20090306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lord+of+the+Pit) (by Chippy) and [Demonic Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20090306000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonic+Tutor) (by Scott Chou)! Remember, you can get your hands on the actual cards with *Duel Decks: Divine vs. Demonic* hits stores on April 10!
