Who needs spells to win in Extended? Matt Boccio's innovative Elves deck plays nineteen land and forty-one(!) creatures in the main deck. No instants, no sorceries, not even a planeswalker. Just lands and creatures ... and smashing face. How can you not love that? Read more on Grand Prix Atlanta from the full coverage [here](/en/events/2011/gpatl11/jason-is-built-ford-tough-in-atlanta).
