The 2012 **Magic** Players Championship showcases the best players in the world battling against one another for the title of Player of the Year. Sixteen players will be invited to compete across three formats for the title and $40,000 first prize, with a total cash purse exceeding $100,000.

Wednesday begins with three rounds of Cube Draft, where the players will be drafting customized booster packs featuring many of the greatest cards in the history of the game. The day concludes with three rounds of Modern.

The live video webcast is going on **NOW**, starting with back-to-back Cube Drafts! You can watch the [enhanced viewer here](#) or check out [twitch.tv/magicprotour](http://www.twitch.tv/magicprotour).
