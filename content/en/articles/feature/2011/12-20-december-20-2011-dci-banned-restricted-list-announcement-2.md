**Announcement Date:** December 20, 2011  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2012  
**Magic Online Effective Date:** December 21, 2011

**Innistrad Block Constructed**, **Standard, Extended, Legacy, Vintage**  
 No changes

**Modern**  
[Punishing Fire](https://web.archive.org/web/20111220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Punishing+Fire) is banned.  
[Wild Nacatl](https://web.archive.org/web/20111220000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wild+Nacatl) is banned.

Changes to **Magic Online**–only formats are now announced monthly in the [**Magic Online** Community Group blog](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-gatecrash-qualifier-season-top-8-standard-decklists-43).

For the complete list of all banned and restricted cards by format, click [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20140424103806/http://www.wizards.com/magic/magazine/article.aspx?x=judge/resources/banned).

To learn more about the Banned & Restricted List changes from R&D member Erik Lauer, click [here](/en/articles/feature/2011/12-20-explanation-of-december-2011-b-r-changes).
