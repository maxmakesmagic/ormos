As the final limited Grand Prix before the release of *Khans of Tarkir*, Grand Prix Salt Lake City is the last time players from around the world will gather to play *Magic 2015* sealed and draft. With weeks to practice with the format in preparation for the tournament, players have had plenty of time to figure out which cards they most want to see when they crack open a pack. We talked to some of the best in the game to find out what they thought. 

**What is your favorite card in *Magic 2015* limited?** 

  
  

(13) Paul Rietzl, Pro Tour Hall of Fame

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Satyr%20Wayfinder)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpslc14/Rietzl.jpg)

"Probably [Satyr Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Satyr+Wayfinder). I like drafting the graveyard deck quite a bit, but I also don't like getting mana screwed." 

**David Ochoa, top 8 Pro Tour Seattle 2012** 

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Mind%20Rot)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpslc14/Ochoa.jpg)

"My favorite card would probably be [Mind Rot](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mind+Rot). I like playing games that are just very long and grindy. It's often overlooked." 

**Jon Stern, winner Grand Prix Atlanta 2014** 

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Elvish%20Mystic)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpslc14/Stern.jpg)

"Probably [Elvish Mystic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elvish+Mystic). Old habits die hard." 

**Craig Wescoe, winner Pro Tour San Diego 2013** 

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Spirit%20Bonds)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpslc14/Wescoe.jpg)

"I think I like [Spirit Bonds](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spirit+Bonds), because if I can play it on turn two, it makes basically all of my other creatures into Triplicate Spirits." 

**(4) William Jensen, Pro Tour Hall of Fame** 

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Soul%20of%20Theros)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpslc14/Jensen.jpg)

"[Soul of Theros](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soul+of+Theros). It's the best card." 

**(2) Owen Turtenwald, winner Grand Prix Albuquerque 2013 and Washington, D.C. 2013** 

![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Soul%20of%20Theros)

![](http://media.wizards.com/2014/events/gpslc14/Turtenwald.jpg)

"[Soul of Theros](https://web.archive.org/web/20140907000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soul+of+Theros). I have the most fun when I cast it." 

"That's because you always win," added Jensen from the side.
