Karplusan Yeti hasn't been in print since the days of *Ice Age* back in 1995 and 1996. When the card was more commonly around, many players gave the card only a casual glance and missed the yeti in the process. (This *is* a yeti we're talking about though, so that seems pretty appropriate.)

In this enlarged version of Quinton Hoover's piece, it's a lot easier to see what's going on. (And that flavor text makes a lot more sense too.)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/c6/58/c65855bbf8b380f7089435c1575969d14d6ad3ad838ef147eee10f68a473f246.jpg)

The players may not have been the only ones that missed what was going on in this piece however. Whoever picked out the packaging art for *Ice Age* may have made the same mistake, leading to what may very well be the only case of **Magic** packaging featuring the "wrong" part of a card's art!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/34/c2/34c2e1dac8a7cffb3ffb1992d21d8eb87880987cc4ad9738b85c1b6cbdfff351.jpg)
