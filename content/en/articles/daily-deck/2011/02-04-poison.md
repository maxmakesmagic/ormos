With the release of *Mirrodin Besieged*, the second set to include the infect mechanic, all eyes are on the latest take on poison. For **Magic** designer Ken Nagle, that means going mostly black. Phyrexian Crusader and Phyrexian Vatmother are powerful new threats for the deck, while Tezzeret, Agent of Bolas allows you to turn Vector Asp, Plague Myr, and Inkmoth Nexus into monstrous 5/5 infecting threats. No one can withstand that type of assault for long!
