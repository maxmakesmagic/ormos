The first [Yotian Soldier](https://web.archive.org/web/20110324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yotian+Soldier) appeared in *Antiquities*. The flavor text explains that Urza created the soldiers to protect his allies while he was busy elsewhere.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e2/ad/e2ad047bed0baf6212bf35691ed5c3a07fb220fabad2bd2f46312524b322c771.jpg)

[Yotian Soldier](https://web.archive.org/web/20110324000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Yotian+Soldier) | Art by [Christopher Rush](https://web.archive.org/web/20110324000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Christopher+Rush%22%5D)

![Yotian Soldier](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Yotian+Soldier)

  

So how could this creature show up in the *Mirrodin* set when it's native to Dominaria? What are the odds of Memnarch creating exactly the same creature as Urza made on a different plane? The flavor text on the *Mirrodin* version explains:

> *Poets dream the verses of otherworldly stories. Artificers dream the blueprints of otherplanar artifacts.*
