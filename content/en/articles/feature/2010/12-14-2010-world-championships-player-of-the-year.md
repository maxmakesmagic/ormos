It's a tie!

Before [Worlds](https://web.archive.org/web/20160325102524/http://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/theres-no-jace-guillaume) this weekend, Brad Nelson was the frontrunner in the Player of the Year race. Combine a rough patch of playing on Brad's part and an amazing run for Guillaume Matignon (he did win the whole thing, after all), and the result? A tie!

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/activity/da602_players.jpg)  

What to do? Let's have them play more **Magic**!

The Player of the Year will be decided in [Pro Tour–Paris](https://web.archive.org/web/20101214000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/pro-tour%E2%80%93paris-qualifier-season-top-8-booster-draft-deck-lists), February 10-13, 2010, after a tie-breaker playoff!

Read the [Player of the Year feature](/en/events/2010/worlds10/2010-magic-the-gathering-world-championships-feature-player-of-the-year) to get the whole story!
