Due to regulations in China, alternate art often needs to be produced for cards depicting the "undead." Below is another example. The top card is the normal version of *Seventh Edition* Scathe Zombies, and the bottom one is the Asian market version.

![Scathe Zombies](http://www.wizards.com/global/images/mtgcom_arcana_93_pic2_en.jpg)

When the Asian market artwork was leaked prior to *Seventh Edition*'s release, many players incorrectly speculated that Portcullis was being reprinted.
