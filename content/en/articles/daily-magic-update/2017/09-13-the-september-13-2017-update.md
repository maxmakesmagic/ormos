The Daily *Magic* Update is a roundup of everything *Magic* you should know on September 13, 2017. Today's Update is brought to you by piles of Treasure and chocolate.

# Today's Must

1.  [Treasured Finds](http://www.starcitygames.com/article/35820_Treasured-Finds.html) | StarCityGames.com | Chris Lansdell

Treasure tokens are delightfully flavorful and versatile tokens in *Ixalan*. Chris goes deep into how they will likely end up impacting different formats once *Ixalan* makes its landing.

# Today's Previews

1.  [Sword-Point Diplomacy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NEIhTWhVtXU) | LoadingReadyRun

Now I want to go around substituting chocolate bars for cards in different effects and see which produce the best ones. [Enter the Infinite](https://web.archive.org/web/20170913000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Enter+the+Infinite) definitely seems like the best choice for maximum chocolating.

1.  [Field of Ruin](http://www.giga.de/spiele/magic-the-gathering-arena/specials/magic-the-gathering-exklusive-preview-karte-zum-neuen-set-ixalan/) | Giga.de | Alexander Gehlsdorf

Hm. This card has a lot more German on it than I understand. I think it says something about lands, cards, games, and libraries, but that's all I've got. Fortunately, we have an image of the card in English here as well.

If this is what Dinosaur-themed *commons* look like, it may be easier than I thought to force Dinosaurs into every Limited deck I build during this block.

# After Those, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [Enter the Arena](http://www.magictheamateuring.com/mta-episode-242-enter-the-arena/) | Magic the Amateuring | Meghan Wolff and Maria Bartholdi

Meghan and Maria dive into the wonders of *Magic*'s next digital adventure. They also discuss some new set called *Iconic Masters* or something? I dunno, sounds pretty cool though.

1.  [*Magic: The Gathering Arena* with Ryan Spain](http://lrcast.com/limited-resources-405-magic-the-gathering-arena-with-ryan-spain/) | Limited Resources | Luis Scott-Vargas and Marshall Sutcliffe

Not to be the only podcast not talking about *Magic: The Gathering Arena*, the Limited Resources guys are joined by Ryan Spain as they discuss *MTG Arena* and everything it has to offer.

1.  [RACES + FACTIONS of *IXALAN*!!!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7sdWeTHAkxQ) | Aether Hub | Seiben

With the [*Ixalan* story](https://web.archive.org/web/20170913000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/ixalan#story) already underway, it's time to make sure you're caught up on all the background information leading into it.

# What People Are Talking About
