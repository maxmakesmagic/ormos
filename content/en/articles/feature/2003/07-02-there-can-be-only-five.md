### Help The Team Pick The Free *Magic Online* Avatars

* * *

One of the reasons many things have changed within ***Magic** Online* over the past year is that we're always learning new things about running a massively multiplayer online game. Putting the **Magic** game on the Internet is, in some ways, a continuing experiment as there are no other games out there quite like it. So, while we've picked up as much as possible from other's successes and failures, there are some aspects of the game that we just have to go through some trial and error with.

The idea of having collectible, tradeable avatars in ***Magic** Online* came late to the game. In the meantime, we were creating avatars anyone could use so as to give players as much customizability as possible. Once we decided to go ahead with the avatar digital object plan, we'd already "flooded" the system with avatars. Then we decided that we'd like the avatars to be the "promotional cards" of the online world.

However, with so many free avatars in the system, it's hard to view the limited-edition avatars as having any special value. Since we want limited-edition avatars to mean something and since we're developing new ones on a regular basis, we need to reduce the number of free avatars in the system. The number of avatars that makes the most sense is five -- one for each **Magic** color.

So we're asking you to take a few minutes and vote for the avatars you want for each color. There are three choices for each color and you can select one for each. Review the avatars below, then **take the survey** to vote for the ones you want to keep.
