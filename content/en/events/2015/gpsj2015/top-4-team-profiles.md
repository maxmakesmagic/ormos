### Sean Gifford, Tyler Blum, Robert Smith

  
![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsj15/t4blum.jpg)

### Tyler Blum

**Age:** 25

**Hometown:** Calgary, AB Canada

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Grand Prix Chicago champion; Canadian National team 2005

**What colors was your first Sealed deck, what was your record, and what was your best card?**

Red-Green Ferocious.  7-2.  [Hooded Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hooded+Hydra).  I never lost when it hit play.

**What colors was your second Sealed deck, what was your record, and what was your best card?**

Abzan.  2-1.  Teammates too good to finish games.

**What was a card that you wanted for a deck, but your teammate needed to play?**

Sean took an [Alabaster Kirin](https://web.archive.org/web/20150201000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Alabaster+Kirin) that I wanted pretty bad.

**What was the most helpful thing your teammates did for you in the tournament?**

Well, Rob went 13-1 in matches, pretty helpful.  Sean booked the hotel room and is the luckiest man alive.
