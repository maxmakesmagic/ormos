Congratulations are in order to Ken Yukuhiro and Jon Stern, winners of Grands Prix in Singapore and Atlantic City, respectively. Ken and Jon are now qualified for Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze*, and you can join them by winning a PTQ! The format for the Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze* PTQ season is Modern. See the kinds of decks players are playing with these Top 8 lists, and find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#dgm)! Good luck; have fun!
