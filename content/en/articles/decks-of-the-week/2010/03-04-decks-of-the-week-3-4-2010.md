The largest **Magic** tournament ever took place last weekend at [Grand Prix–Madrid](https://web.archive.org/web/20100304000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/m%C3%BCller-magical-madrid), and it's only fitting that it featured Legacy and the more than 10,000 cards legal in that format. Check out the Top 8 decks plus the decks that survived the 2,227-player Day One unscathed! Decks of the Week also closes the book on Pro Tour–San Diego with the rest of those Standard lists, shows off *Worldwake*'s impact on Extended in the San Juan PTQ season, and welcomes *Worldwake* to **Magic Online**. Enjoy!
