Since this was the first Premier Event since *Amonkhet*'s release, I took the liberty of poring through the pages, frantically searching for decks which utilized the newest cards. Well, they were digital pages, so it's not all that bad. After hours and hours of scouring, here were some of the cards that we've seen in the most popular decks so far.

Then, there were also some rather unique brews which house some *Amonkhet* treasures. These decks might not have occupied a large percentage of the metagame but a brew is a brew and we absolutely wish to share these decklists with you!

These players may not have made the Top 8 of Grand Prix Kobe, but they certainly were among the eight most interesting brews of the weekend!

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Kefnet+the+Mindful)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kefnet+the+Mindful)

Akira Kinoshita showed up with a "Turbo Fog" strategy except that it was Mono Blue. Using cards like [Gigadrowse](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gigadrowse) and [Exhaustion](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Exhaustion) to buy himself a lot of time, the goal was to take numerous turns with [Time Warp](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Time+Warp), [Part the Waterveil](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Part+the+Waterveil) and [Walk the Aeons](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Walk+the+Aeons) (oh, [Temporal Trespass](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Temporal+Trespass) too!) to eventually win with [Jace, Vryn's Prodigy](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Vryn%27s+Prodigy) or [Azor's Elocutors](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Azor%27s+Elocutors)!

[Kefnet the Mindful](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Kefnet+the+Mindful) in the sideboard does a great job at staying alive against removal-heavy decks, in addition to being a huge beat stick and a card-drawing engine!

Azorius Control might be old tech but it had a great mana base compared to Esper or Jeskai. Despite losing [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) and [Fatal Push](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fatal+Push), Akinori Fujikura made it up with [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) and [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile). [Gideon of the Trials](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon+of+the+Trials), [Gideon Jura](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon+Jura) and [Elspeth, Sun's Champion](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Elspeth%2C+Sun%27s+Champion) were great win conditions, or he could simply prolong the game until he fired off a massive [Sphinx's Revelation](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sphinx%27s+Revelation) or [White Sun's Zenith](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=White+Sun%27s+Zenith)!

For a mere four mana, Chu Huseh Lan was able to use [Madcap Experiment](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Madcap+Experiment) to cheat [Platinum Emperion](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Platinum+Emperion) onto the battlefield. His disruption comes in the form of [Stone Rain](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Stone+Rain) and [Blood Moon](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blood+Moon), which are great against Eldrazi Tron and Titan Shift decks which have been pretty popular all weekend. [Hazoret the Fervent](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hazoret+the+Fervent) was there as an indestructible win condition, as long as it did not encounter [Path to Exile](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Path+to+Exile)!

Tetsuhiro Takahashi did not build his deck around [Nissa, Steward of Elements](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%2C+Steward+of+Elements) but it was a card that could generate a lot of value for him if he needed a tool for the late game. Wanting to run along with [Geist of Saint Traft](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geist+of+Saint+Traft) and [Spell Queller](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Queller)s, he found it difficult to resist the temptation of splashing for [Collected Company](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Collected+Company)! Oh, since we're playing Green, we might as well go with [Noble Hierarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Noble+Hierarch) as well, making the entire deck extremely tempo-oriented.

The condition of [Bontu the Glorified](https://web.archive.org/web/20170528000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bontu+the+Glorified) was easy to fulfill, since he will always have a bunch tokens to sacrifice!
