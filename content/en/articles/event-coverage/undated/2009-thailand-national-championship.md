### Invitation List

**2009 Thailand National Championship Invitations** 

The **Magic: The Gathering** Thailand National Championship is an invitation-only event, open to citizens or foreign players resident in Thailand from Jan 1st 2009 Thailand. Invitations are awarded based on the following criteria:

*   2008 Thailand Worlds Team

    *   Sirichai Chaisuthamporn (Thailand Champion)
*   Top 4 winners from the Regional Qualifying Tournaments on June 6
*   Top 2 winners from National Qualifier on June 26

Regional( 4 invite) and National Qualifier( 2 invite) tournaments take place as follows:
