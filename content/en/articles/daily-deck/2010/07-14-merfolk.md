Continuing our series of decks highlighting the decks R&D champions took to ***Magic** 2011* Prereleases all across the land, today we present a blue and white Merfolk deck played by Ken Nagle. He said the deck got a huge boost from the reprinting of Mana Leak as well as Scroll Thief. If you're looking to give the new cards a spin, trying building Ken's deck for Friday Night **Magic** following the Launch Party for ***Magic** 2011*! Ken was one of the challengers in Seattle, WA.
