# Also, Read, Watch, or Listen to These

1.  [The 13 Scariest Pieces of *Magic* Art](http://www.mtgsalvation.com/articles/49669-the-13-scariest-pieces-of-magic-art) | MTG Salvation | Andrew Weisel

[Brain Maggot](https://web.archive.org/web/20171031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brain+Maggot) and [Macabre Waltz](https://web.archive.org/web/20171031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Macabre+Waltz) made the list, so, yes, this list is accurate. I'm pretty sure my darkest nightmares aren't as dark as [Macabre Waltz](https://web.archive.org/web/20171031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Macabre+Waltz).

Seriously. Those cards freak me out.

It's a spooky episode of Commander VS, with the scariest lineup of all—every deck is playing blue! Truly terrifying!

1.  [The Boogeyman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Myi-jxhxOnM&feature=youtu.be) | TheMagicManSam

So good, it's terrifying. And I could be talking about both the video and [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20171031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor).

In the second Rewind video in the series, Marshall visits with friend of the show Kenji Egashira over one of his most pivotal matches, from way back in 2014. This one doesn't have the benefit of Reid Duke calmly explaining everything, but the concept plays and Kenji is a delight.

1.  [What's the Play?](https://www.channelfireball.com/articles/whats-the-play-7/) | ChannelFireball | Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa

Often, these "what play do you make" articles can be kind of blasé, but this one sucked me in. The voting format is fun, and the scenarios are interesting, even if Paulo does love his Faeries deck a bit too much.

# Deck of the Day

1.  [Standard White-Blue God-Pharaoh's Gift](/dan/events/2022/standard-ptq-2017-10-29/standard-ptq)

We've come almost full-circle with this [God-Pharaoh's Gift](https://web.archive.org/web/20171031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=God-Pharaoh%27s+Gift) thing, as the latest variations ditch trying to search it up with [Gate to the Afterlife](https://web.archive.org/web/20171031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gate+to+the+Afterlife) and go straight to [Refurbish](https://web.archive.org/web/20171031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Refurbish)-ing it out of the graveyard. That lets the deck play fewer enablers ([Minister of Inquiries](https://web.archive.org/web/20171031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Minister+of+Inquiries) is no one's idea of a good *Magic* card) and more—[Sacred Cat](https://web.archive.org/web/20171031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sacred+Cat)s? You know, for a 1/1 lifelink creature, [Sacred Cat](https://web.archive.org/web/20171031000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sacred+Cat) sure is seeing a lot of play these days between this and token strategies.
