## PRODUCTS

![](https://i.ormos.uk/f7/64/f764b5237db5145a3fd3a66d6ee7c01a240edb56ceeec32ffd049be4e3a08eaa.png)

### FAT PACK

Includes a player’s guide, two card boxes, six 15-card booster packs, 40 basic land cards, one random Pro Tour player card, a special-edition life counter, and the *Dissension* novel.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/34/2e/342e94433983e921c3fbc8a6ebfc31456ebef803f019bb49aa00a898c6c6ffa2.png)

### BOOSTER PACKS

Each booster pack includes 15 cards to add to your collection—and you can expect one of those 15 to be a rare or premium foil card!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/88/f6/88f6f9edc14e3c1bb1c03d4311f258000e60513cfd0b9fa65ad0e946a31425f1.png)

### PRECONSTRUCTED THEME DECKS

Everything you need in one ready-made deck. Includes 60 total cards, a customized strategy guide, land cards, creature cards, and other spells.

[DECKLISTS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160707011057/http://magic.wizards.com/node/975946)

## PRODUCT INFORMATION

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6e/2c/6e2cf45b0af610b6edc3273689da1db766edc441d2dd30b68df4c577f8a32234.png)This 180-card deck includes introduces three more Ravnica guilds: Azorius Senate (blue-white), Rakdos Cult (black-red), and Simic Combine (green-blue).

**Set Name:** *Dissension*  
**Block:** Set 3 of 3 of the *Ravnica* Block  
**Number of Cards:** 180  
**Guilds:** Simic Combine, Azorius Senate, Rakdos Cult

**Languages:** English, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Chinese Simplified, Spanish
