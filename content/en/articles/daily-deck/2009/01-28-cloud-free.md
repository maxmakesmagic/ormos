U.S National Champion Michael Jacob is fresh off a team championship at the 2008 World Championships. His follow-up act? Top 8ing Grand Prix-Los Angeles! He used an innovative Black-Green control deck similar to the Death Cloud decks that have become very popular on the Pro Tour Qualifier-Honolulu circuit. One key difference? He cut Death Cloud! For all the official coverage of Grand Prix-Los Angeles, [visit here](/en/events/2009/gpla09/luis-scott-vargas-conquers-in-la). To find out how to get to an Extended Pro Tour Qualifier for Honolulu near you, visit the [official PTQ page](/en/events/2009/honolulu09-qualifiers/pro-tourhonolulu-2009-qualifiers).
