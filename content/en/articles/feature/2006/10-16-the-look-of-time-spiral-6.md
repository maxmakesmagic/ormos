### Green

![](https://i.ormos.uk/de/9f/de9f9ef54785d93237b9393bcea141b4a7df6095d4ec69daaffd0a725f3720d1.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/11/1b/111b651a2044f3f41f6383d111fc1de62a702fb69d2afad2ab6346c286f18bcd.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/78/ae/78aebcb095d20e775e8ff1e203b1cf2fdf18ff74143cc320b54743370153158f.jpg)

It seemed right that Green would have been hardest hit by Dominaria’s past catastrophes. Both magical warfare and the invasion of unnatural, black-infused, half-living abominations run against the values of Green. The trees are reduced to cork ‘skeletons’ of themselves, and the forest’s inhabitants are now a people bereft of resources. Some ‘green’ remains, however. A fungal kudzu blankets the forest floor and clings to the base of the dead trees.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/09/68/09688e466cd31378c47ca1b8f16bc86a4c3c7f2ca4645144ba373ba8861be368.jpg)

The elves and druids remain barefoot to maintain a connection to the last, albeit gross, plant life in their home. The kudzu fungus also gave us a fun angle through which to approach Thallids, which we felt sure would remain in the set.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b7/b8/b7b8519c66cc08512641b4034ad9b5f97262563851c8e91b6920bec4977545b4.jpg)

It is the last resource of the druids and elves. They dry it and weave it into a burlap-like material to fashion clothes and adorn their weapons with. The weapons themselves are meant to reflect the idea of a native people who have lost everything but aren’t taking it lying down.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/64/22/6422ebbe217e07c54afe4a3c07c3b105a6d3ef594fc92034927768fe48179ddb.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0f/a9/0fa9471bb073805525e6a771516412add9636873197dd7bf9afc6b9212ca75c4.jpg)

![](https://i.ormos.uk/80/f6/80f6485a5dbd89a488f9777c09ad7db9742e7a628405b07b895ff0fe1ab77e78.jpg)

![Fungal_Reaches_640](https://i.ormos.uk/1b/7d/1b7dd102563561f8a4f9428137bdb3a0c7c516bc5303fa668d86ab608a447613.jpg)  
*Martina Pilcerova’s Fungal Reaches shows the fungal kudzu broadening its domain and colonizing some monolith mountains.*

![Thrill_of_the_Hunt_640](https://i.ormos.uk/62/5a/625afbd41742efbb42354e010455347ca41f0007d36721b3dd019a889aec2bb1.jpg)  
*Stephen Tappin’s Thrill of the Hunt is probably the poster child for Green’s downtrodden, emaciated look combined with its never-say-die attitude.*

There you have it: an overview of a few of the thought processes and ideas that led to the atmospheric Dominaria-in-ruins of *Time Spiral*.
