![Carlos Romao](https://i.ormos.uk/75/04/7504d7ec205070221b678cb7946c48a1f9be092944e0389c9bb127341c76479e.jpg)Carlos Romao is the 2002 **Magic** World Champion! At the start of the day, he and Argentinean National Champion Diego Ostrovich shared the honor of being the first Latin Americans to make the Top 8 of a Pro Tour. Their hard work had paid off. The Latin Alliance, composed of pros from Uruguay, Argentina, Chile, Brazil and Venezuela, was an unprecedented collaboration between the pro communities of Latin America. They tested endlessly for Worlds, sharing tech and results, and they put two players in the Top 8. You can read full coverage of Worlds 2002 at the [Sideboard](https://web.archive.org/web/20020819000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=sideboard/events/worlds02).
