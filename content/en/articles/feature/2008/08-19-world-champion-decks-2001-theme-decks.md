### ![](https://i.ormos.uk/85/6f/856f23fdedad44880f455a1fe5f0cfcf31437e9ddd977b64d2da9b6162783188.jpg)Tom van de Logt

Tom van de Logt's aggressive, black-red "Machine Head" deck used the good ol' "blow stuff up" method. While creatures like [Plague Spitter](https://web.archive.org/web/20080819000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plague+Spitter) wiped out his opponents' smaller creatures, more ferocious beasts like [Flametongue Kavu](https://web.archive.org/web/20080819000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flametongue+Kavu) aggressively cleared the board of larger threats.
