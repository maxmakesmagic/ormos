For Christian Valenti, Pro Tour Nagoya marked a move from the ranks of the StarCityGames.com Open Series to the grand stage of the Pro Tour. His red-green deck drew the notice of the coverage team and stood out against a field of White Weenie Tempered Steel decks. Rather than try to out-aggro his opponents, however, Christian wanted to control the pace of the game while building his mana base until powerful elbow-drops like Karn Liberated or Red Sun's Zenith could take over.
