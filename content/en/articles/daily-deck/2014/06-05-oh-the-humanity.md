Modern is home to many cheap, efficient instants and sorceries. [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20140605000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt), [Serum Visions](https://web.archive.org/web/20140605000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serum+Visions), and [Remand](https://web.archive.org/web/20140605000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Remand) provide effective ways to take control of the early stages of the game without losing their effectiveness as the game wears on. **Magic Online** user MR\_Thompson takes advantage of these with creatures that want a number of spells to be played. Each creature in the deck gets stronger as the number of instants and sorceries grows.![Young Pyromancer](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Young%20Pyromancer&options=)

This deck also uses its sideboard well to shore up different matchups without changing the core of the deck. [Molten Rain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140605000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Molten+Rain) is a perfect example. [Molten Rain](https://web.archive.org/web/20140605000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Molten+Rain) is effective against [Urza's Tower](https://web.archive.org/web/20140605000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Urza%27s+Tower) and [Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle](https://web.archive.org/web/20140605000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Valakut%2C+the+Molten+Pinnacle), while still allowing this deck to maintain its high spell count and positive tempo. [Shattering Spree](https://web.archive.org/web/20140605000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shattering+Spree) gives additional fuel against [Cranial Plating](https://web.archive.org/web/20140605000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cranial+Plating) and friends while [Combust](https://web.archive.org/web/20140605000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Combust) is strong against [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20140605000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch) and [Pestermite](https://web.archive.org/web/20140605000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pestermite).
