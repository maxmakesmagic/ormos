### Invitations

Invitations to the 2012 Friday Night Magic Championship are awarded in the following manner:

> *   The top eight (8) ranked players from the Asia-Pacific geo-region at the conclusion of the 2012 Friday Night **Magic** Points Season.
> *   The top thirty-one (31) ranked players from the Europe geo-region at the conclusion of the 2012 Friday Night **Magic** Points Season.
> *   The top six (6) ranked players from the Japan geo-region at the conclusion of the 2012 Friday Night **Magic** Points Season.
> *   The top seven (7) ranked players from the Latin America geo-region at the conclusion of the 2012 Friday Night **Magic** Points Season.
> *   The top forty-eight (48) ranked players from the North America geo-region at the conclusion of the 2012 Friday Night **Magic** Points Season.

Complete information about invitations to the 2012 Friday Night **Magic** Championship can be found in the [**Magic** Premier Event Invitation Policy](https://web.archive.org/web/20140604193929/http://www.wizards.com/wpn/Document.aspx?x=Magic_The_Gathering_Premier_Event_Invitation_Policy).
