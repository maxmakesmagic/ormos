Alex Bertoncini is on a roll! After winning the 2010 StarCityGames.com Player of the Year race, Alex is back in the saddle in 2011. He recently made the Top 8 of [Grand Prix Dallas/Fort Worth](/en/events/2011/gpdal11/shiels-stands-alone-in-dallas), and used this list to win a StarCityGames.com Legacy Open event in Boston. That puts him in position to have a breakout 2011 season including his first appearance on the Pro Tour!
