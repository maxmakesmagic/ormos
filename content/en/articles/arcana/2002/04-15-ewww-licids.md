Licids were one of the more interesting twists on creature enchantments. There were twelve licids in all -- five each in *Tempest* and *Stronghold*, and two more in *Exodus*. Below is their (small) entry in the *Tempest* Block Style Guide.

![licids](http://www.wizards.com/global/images/mtgcom_arcana_73_pic2_en.jpg)

In the card art, licids were always show attached to a "host." At least one non-licid card contains a licid: Mage il-Vec from *Exodus*. The John Matson illustration on this card was originally commissioned for a licid. (It's attached to the back of the mage's head.) The *Tempest* team joked that perhaps it was fashionable to wear licids in Rath.

![Mage il-Vec](http://www.wizards.com/global/images/mtgcom_arcana_73_pic3_en.jpg)
