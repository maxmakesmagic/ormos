The creature type vote has proved very interesting with a tight race at the top. So much so that no single creature type garnered a third of the vote. Thus, per our rules, the top three vote getters will now have a run-off.

![Mr. Babycakes](https://i.ormos.uk/6f/8d/6f8d5274152c97c62eb74fd771da6e84ebb86c34f109cc699c96e82cd49ae1e6.jpg)

The top three creature types (in alphabetical order) are:

*   Elemental
*   Fungus
*   Spike

A rundown of how all the creature types faired will be listed when we announce the run-off winner next week.

Deadline for voting for the run-off will be midnight, Monday, June 3rd.
