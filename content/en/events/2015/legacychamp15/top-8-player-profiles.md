### Geroge Flete

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/legacychamp15/ew15_legacy_flete.jpg)

**Age:** 32

**Hometown:** Keene, NH

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Just started played *Magic* again after a ten year break.

**What did you play today, and how did you do?**

Merfolk, 10-0-1

**Why did you choose your deck?**

[Chalice of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chalice+of+the+Void) on 1 plus a fast clock seems good against a lot of decks.

**What were your best cards in your main deck? Why?**

[Chalice of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chalice+of+the+Void), [True-Name Nemesis](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=True-Name+Nemesis), and [Cavern of Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cavern+of+Souls). [True-Name Nemesis](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=True-Name+Nemesis) is almost impossible to remove. [Chalice of the Void](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chalice+of+the+Void) and [Cavern of Souls](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cavern+of+Souls) locks out opponents decks without disrupting your own deck.

**What were the best cards in your sideboard? Why?**

[Back to Basics](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Back+to+Basics). It's usually game over versus decks with greedy manabases.

**What is your favorite card in Legacy?**

[Lord of Atlantis](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lord+of+Atlantis)

### Akash Naidu

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/legacychamp15/ew15_legacy_naidu.jpg)

**Age:** 24

**Hometown:** Boston, MA

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

9th Grand Prix New Jersey; PTQ Top 8

**What did you play today, and how did you do?**

Blue-Red [Omniscience](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Omniscience), 9-1-1

**Why did you choose your deck?**

It has done me well in the past and I can't resist combo decks.

**What were your best cards in your main deck? Why?**

[Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time). It's basically double [Demonic Tutor](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Demonic+Tutor).

**What were the best cards in your sideboard? Why?**

[Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt). I had multiple rounds where I attacked an [Emrakul, the Aeons Torn](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Emrakul%2C+the+Aeons+Torn) and would have died on the swing back if not for [Cunning Wish](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cunning+Wish) into [Lightning Bolt](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lightning+Bolt) for lethal. [Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer). It just makes a lot of bad matchups winnable.

**What is your favorite card in Legacy?**

[Cabal Therapy](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cabal+Therapy). I enjoy the mind games it causes no matter whether I'm playing with it or against it.

### Kevin King

**Age:** 23

**Hometown:** Baltimore, MD

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Always having it, never having it, and blind flipping my [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets).

Lands. Made Top 8.

**Why did you choose your deck?**

The popularity of Noah's [Delver of Secrets](https://web.archive.org/web/20150822000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Delver+of+Secrets) deck pushed me off it.
