## Ludvig Londos vs. Brad Nelson

Coming in to Round 8, both Ludvig Londos and Brad Nelson were undefeated, having navigated a dangerous table 1 pod including Brian Kibler, Kai Budde and Guillaume Wafo Tapa. While Brad is now accustomed to the lights of the feature match arena, his opponent Ludvig Londos may have been a little tired after grinding in in last night's Last Chance Qualifier, which ran well past midnight.

### Game 1

Both players kept, and it was Nelson who got in the first play of the game with a [Llanowar Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Elves). He had a little "Caw!" as Londos dropped a [Squadron Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squadron+Hawk) onto the table, finding two more. Nelson let out a laugh and another caw as he cast [Prodigal Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prodigal+Pyromancer). These birds would not fly for long.

"Did you go to Gothenburg last weekend?" asked Londos.

"Nah, I stayed home, chilled, had a good time." Brad looked fairly chilled and was having a good time in the feature match arena.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/6b/b2/6bb22d409adfb148d9ff80bc717f495cce3e021aab71796643711d2d73ec7ddb.jpg)*After grinding in and going 7-0 on a very short night's sleep, Ludvig Londos is all smiles.*

Londos played out a [Cloud Elemental](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Cloud+Elemental), and got a single hit in with his Hawk before it was shot down in flames. Brad had a [Canyon Minotaur](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Canyon+Minotaur) to start attacking with, and looked pretty happy with his position.

An [Awakener Druid](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Awakener+Druid) provided the next attacker for Nelson, letting a [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest) nip in hastily for more damage. While Londos had a [Serra Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serra+Angel), it looked a little impotent in the face of the power on the other side of the board. Londos dug with [Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain), then played an [Augury Owl](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Augury+Owl), looking for answers. Brad, meanwhile, had a new plan for his [Prodigal Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prodigal+Pyromancer) if he ever ran out of targets on the other side of the board. [Protean Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Protean+Hydra) came down, with X=3. It could easily grow thanks to his pinger.

[Plummet](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plummet) on [Serra Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serra+Angel) was a brutal main-deck answer to the flyer, and with just a few swift swings, Brad Nelson put himself one game away from a perfect score for the day.

Nelson 1, Londos 0

"So, are you jetlagged from travelling from the USA?" asked Londos.

"No," replied Brad. "I love to sleep. I have been sleeping all the time."

"I didn't get to sleep until 3 a.m."

"Why not?"

"I was qualifying for this Pro Tour."

"Maaaaaaaaan! I can't beat you now! Grinder goes to 8-0? What a story!"

### Game 2

The next game started with [Silvercoat Lion](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Silvercoat+Lion) for Londos, while Nelson started with [Goblin Piker](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Goblin+Piker). Stuck on just [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain)s, Nelson had little follow-up, while Londos played [Augury Owl](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Augury+Owl), and [Squadron Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squadron+Hawk) finding two more.

"I once had a deck with four [Squadron Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squadron+Hawk)s and a [Mass Polymorph](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mass+Polymorph) in this format. That was a *fun* deck," remarked Nelson, who eventually drew a [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest) to allow for some Llanoawar Elves.

[Prodigal Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prodigal+Pyromancer) from Nelson was soon shut down by an [Ice Cage](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ice+Cage), but there was little Londos could do about [Triskelion](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triskelion), which was a clear and present danger to his team of Birds.

Londos, however, was far from out of it. A [Vengeful Archon](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vengeful+Archon) from Londos met a small harrumph from Nelson, who tapped out for a 5/5 [Protean Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Protean+Hydra). It got bounced by [Æther Adept](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=%C3%86ther+Adept), and it was on to Game 3.

Londos 1, Nelson 1

### Game 3

A quick keep came from Brad on the play, while Ludwig shuffled his hand nervously before starting with seven. Londos's turn-two [Squadron Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squadron+Hawk) was so eager to get its friends that Londos ended up having to discard, dropping a [Siege Mastodon](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Mastodon) in the bin. Nelson again had the trump in [Prodigal Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prodigal+Pyromancer), and soon followed up with [Juggernaut](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Juggernaut) to launch into the ground and pound.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/5e/42/5e42345f54676062c476df91573621e380f9ed5e010db8585ddaef490f5249b4.jpg)*Let it never be said that Brad Nelson is not a man who loves to sleep.*

After a little thought for his turn (and an attack that ate a [Squadron Hawk](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Squadron+Hawk)), Brad cast a [Fauna Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fauna+Shaman). He looked on as [Serra Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Serra+Angel) came down for Londos, and calmly cast a [Triskelion](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Triskelion) on his next turn that, along with Pyromancer, tag-teamed to take the Angel down.

Attacks soon had Londos at 5, and when he tried again for a [Vengeful Archon](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vengeful+Archon), Brad had quite the response.

"[Fling](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fling) my [Juggernaut](https://web.archive.org/web/20100903000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Juggernaut) at you?"

That will do.

Nelson 2, Londos 1

Both players then slunk off for a nap—Londos because he needed it, and Brad just because he likes to sleep.

Brad Nelson wins 2-1, advancing to 8-0 going into Saturday.
