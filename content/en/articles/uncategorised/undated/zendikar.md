## ZENDIKAR

A tumultuous plane of wild mana, unstable weather, and floating terrain, Zendikar is a world of incomparable natural beauty and danger. A dynamic plane, the landscape is affected by volatile seismic movements known as "the Roil," whose violent shifts make life there precarious, unpredictable, and full of adventure. Rivers cascade down from above the skyline. Craggy peaks lurch to crush those who would scale their heights. Lush forests and murky swamps grow over upturned ruins, which hide ancient secrets. Large and mysterious stone formations known as hedrons, the ancient handiwork of the Lithomancer Nahiri, remain as a reminder of calmer days on the plane. Days before the Eldrazi returned. . .

The Eldrazi titans are inscrutable entities of the Blind Eternities, able to travel between planes to devour worlds. Unable to defeat them, the Planeswalkers Nahiri, Sorin, and Ugin used the hedrons to contain and immobilize the titans. They remained trapped in stasis and hidden for millenia until three new Planeswalkers—Chandra Nalaar, Jace Beleren, and Sarkhan Vol—inadvertently released them.

Freed from their imprisonment, the Eldrazi titans summoned forth their spawn and spread throughout the plane, consuming two of Zendikar's seven continents and leaving dust in their wake. But after their long imprisonment and rapid encroachment, the titans still needed to feed and recover their strength. Sightings of the titans themselves subsided, then ceased entirely. Zendikari inhabitants who survived the destruction then adapted, reformed alliances, and battled the remaining Eldrazi spawn. But in recent days, the waves of spawn have emerged stronger than before, targeting Zendikari settlements and destroying the land itself. Leaderless and hounded by inscrutable and increasingly strong enemies, the Zendikari are now under desperate pressure to find allies, defenses, and the means to survive.

## LEGENDARY CREATURES

### ANOWON, THE RUIN SAGE

This vampire sage spent years searching for the Eye of Ugin, trying to save his people. When Chandra Nalaar showed him the map, he offered to guide her, fully intending to betray her. Instead, Jace tricked him—only to find that Chandra and Sarkhan had beaten them both to the prize.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011257/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=177508)

### EMRAKUL, THE AEONS TORN

Unspeaking creatures of the Blind Eternities, the Eldrazi’s existence is marked only by their constant consumption of all energy and matter around them. Emrakul is the largest of the three Eldrazi titans, able to annihilate anyone in the vicinity by his presence alone.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011257/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=193452)

### KOZILEK, BUTCHER OF TRUTH

Kozilek has a multitude of eye sprouts across his body. Thus, he came to represent mental destruction and the puzzle of insanity. Like the other Eldrazi titans, Kozilek’s motivations are shrouded in mystery.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011257/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=193632)

### ULAMOG, THE INFINITE GYRE

This Eldrazi titan travels across Zendikar on writhing, massive tentacles. Its facial features are covered by a thick bony plate. The Eldrazi may have senses beyond mortal comprehension, but this complete lack of understanding hinders any attempt at communication, reasoning, or peace with their kind.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011257/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=261321)

## SOCIETIES

### AKOUM

Life on the Teeth of Akoum is treacherous, but manageable. Rock slides on this unstable terrain are perhaps the biggest threat. Clinging to what little shelter there is, humans survive by rallying together, protecting each other, and finding the quickest paths to safety.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011257/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&block=+%5b%22Zendikar+Block%22%5d&name=+%5bakoum%5d)

### BALA GED

Defined by its massive trees and lush vegetation, Bala Ged is at the mercy of the jungle. The trees grow so large that some could even encase hedrons! Humans can only survive by either befriending or dominating the massive creatures native to the land.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011257/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&block=+%5b%22Zendikar+Block%22%5d&name=+%5bbala%5d)

### ONDU

Upon discovering that certain healing spells were amplified by the hedrons here, humans were eager to build a settlement nearby. They pass along any information they gain about this phenomenon, diligently recording and storing all of their findings at the Lighthouse of Sea Gate.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011257/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&block=+%5b%22Zendikar+Block%22%5d&name=+%5bondu%5d)

### TAZEEM

The most stable location in Tazeem is the Lighthouse at Sea Gate. There, scholars document everything, from the location of valuable artifacts found inside the hedrons to the numerous deadly predators roaming Zendikar.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011257/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&block=+%5b%22Zendikar+Block%22%5d&color=+%5bR%5d&type=+%5b%22Creature%22%5d+!%5b%22Legendary%22%5d&subtype=+%5b%22Human%22%5d+%5b%22Shaman%22%5d)

## CREATURE RACES

### ELDRAZI

Once released from their prison inside Zendikar, three Eldrazi titans spawned many lesser Eldrazi, consuming all energy and matter along the way. The Eldrazi’s existence and motive are entirely unknown, but it seems they won’t stop until they have annihilated every inch of the plane.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011257/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&block=+%5b%22Zendikar+Block%22%5d&subtype=+%5b%22Eldrazi%22%5d)

### VAMPIRES

The long-lived vampires of Zendikar hid a terrible secret: ages ago, they were enslaved by the Eldrazi. Now that their former masters have returned, many vampires have dedicated themselves to doing everything in their power to prevent a return to subservience.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011257/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&block=+%5b%22Zendikar+Block%22%5d&subtype=+%5b%22vampire%22%5d)

### MERFOLK

Curious and analytical, the merfolk of Zendikar reside in scattered enclaves throughout the plane. Merfolk long believed that three powerful figures watched over the plane: Emeria (sky), Ula (water), and Cosi (land). In fact, these deities were based on dim memories of the three Eldrazi titans Emrakul, Ulamog, and Kozilek.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011257/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&block=+%5b%22Zendikar+Block%22%5d&subtype=+%5b%22merfolk%22%5d)

### GOBLINS

Goblins have a deep fascination with trinkets and artifacts. They often weave their discoveries into their gear, which they display with pride, regardless of its actual power or importance. All Zendikar goblins belong to one of the three major tribes: the Tuktuk, Lavastep, or Grotag.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011257/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&block=+%5b%22Zendikar+Block%22%5d&subtype=+%5b%22goblin%22%5d)

### ELVES

The majority of elves live in the treetops of Ondu and Bela Ged. Constantly adapting and remaining ever prepared for danger, the elves of Zendikar expect the same of all others, often valuing self-preservation above all else.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011257/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&block=+%5b%22Zendikar+Block%22%5d&subtype=+%5b%22Elf%22%5d)

### KOR

These graceful, fearless creatures adventure across the floating hedrons, looking for unknown treasures. Using their hooks and lines to transverse aerial gaps and ensnare foes, they make excellent guides and protectors.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011257/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&block=+%5b%22Zendikar+Block%22%5d&subtype=+%5b%22kor%22%5d)
