**PRO TOUR** 

The Pro Tour circuit is a series of high-level international Magic tournaments with large cash prize awards. They are run at REL 5, require a level 4 head judge, and are DCI sanctioned with a K-value of 48. They are invitation-only tournaments.

**Invitation Criteria** 

*   The current World Champion.
*   The current Pro Player of the Year.
*   The Top 32 players from the previous individual-format Pro Tour event (PT Yokohama).
*   The Top 32 players from Worlds (only for the first individual-format Pro Tour event following Worlds).
*   Players on the Top 10 teams from the previous team-format Pro Tour event (only for the first individual-format Pro Tour event following the team-format Pro Tour event).
*   Players on the Top 4teams from the previous Worlds (only for the first individual-format Pro Tour event following the Worlds).
*   Players with 20 or more pro points as of the most recent pro standing (Boston 03) preceding the Pro Tour event.
*   The Top 8 finishers (who were previously uninvited) of each Grand Prix tournament that feeds the appropriate Pro Tour event.
*   Players who earn invitations through appropriate Qualifier tournaments.
*   The Top 50 DCI-ranked players (who were previously uninvited) in Constructed format as of the publication on Sept 17th, 2003
