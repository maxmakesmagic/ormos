### Did You Make Top 8? Send In Your Deck!

* * *

While the big event didn't go off last weekend, there was a lot of Constructed play going on in the smaller tournaments. A little math shows us how much **Magic** mayhem there was.

Let's look at some stats for this weekend: 

*   17 free Constructed tournaments
*   256 players per tournament
*   204 rounds of Constructed play
*   4352 decks played
*   1271 matches played

I grant you that my numbers may be off here and there (I'm a writer, not a mathematician, Jim), but it's certain that there was a *lot* of Constructed playtesting going on. And while it would be interesting to see all the decks and how they did, I don't have the endurance to put in 4352 decks.

What I *would* like to see (along with other [Worlds Qualifier](/en/articles/feature/2003/03-24-press-release) hopefuls) is what made it to the Top 8 this weekend. We have the tools, we have the technology, all we need are the decks.

So, if you made Top 8 in one of the Constructed tournaments this past weekend, send your information to me and I'll put it on the web. Here's what I need: 

*   Your .dec file.
*   Your ***Magic** Online* user name.
*   The tournament number you played the deck in.
*   What your record was with the deck.

Send all that to mtgonlineeditor@wizards.com and I'll list your deck among the best decks in the ***Magic** Online* world.
