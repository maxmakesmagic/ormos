Naoki Shimizu is a pro player, coverage reporter, and **Magic** writer. The above deck, featuring Acidic Slime in conjunction with Reveillark, was the one he used to make the Top 8 at his local Game Day in Japan. The 2/2 deathtoucher happens to have exactly the right stats to be abused with Reveillark: a powerful enters-the-battlefield ability and power 2 or less. Look for the green creature to be popping up in Reveillark decks at a tournament near you soon!
