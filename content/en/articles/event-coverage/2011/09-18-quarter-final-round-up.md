**Reinaldo da Silva vs. Guilherme Medeiros Merjan  
 Luiz Henrique Miguel vs. Marcus Camargo  
 Allison Abe vs. Igor Sousa  
 Marcus Pacheco vs. Jonathan Melamed**

So here's how this here 'one of me, four matches' thing is going to work. First of all, it's only three matches, since Reinaldo da Silva, having got to 9-0 and paced the field all weekend, can't go to Worlds, and has conceded to Guilherme Medeiros Merjan, making him a very happy man. That leaves three matches to look at. Our good friend Paulo Vitor Damo da Rosa is keeping an eye on Miguel v Camargo and Abe v Sousa, leaving me to concentrate on the match between Jonathan Melamed and Marcus Pacheco. Oh, and don't forget that this is Nationals, so it's best three-out-of five.

Pacheco opened with [Drowned Catacomb](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Drowned+Catacomb)s, while Melamed was straight into action with [Halimar Depths](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Halimar+Depths). [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe) found [Jace Beleren](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace+Beleren), [Solemn Simulacrum](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Solemn+Simulacrum), [Into the Roil](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Into+the+Roil), [Black Sun's Zenith](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Sun%27s+Zenith), [Tectonic Edge](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tectonic+Edge), and [Go for the Throat](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Go+for+the+Throat) in Pacheco's hand. [Spell Pierce](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Pierce) dealt with [Jace Beleren](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace+Beleren) the following turn.

*Already the first game was over in Luiz Miguel v Marcus Camargo. A turn three [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch) followed up by turn four [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) was enough for Miguel to take the opener.*

***Luiz Miguel 1 - 0 Marcus Camargo*** 

Melamed spent his fourth turn casting [Shrine of Piercing Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrine+of+Piercing+Vision), and didn't take long to crack it in search of more goodness for his [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) combo. He added [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) to the board, the only non-land between the two players. At end of turn he aimed for [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch). [Go for the Throat](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Go+for+the+Throat) from Pacheco was met with [Dispel](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dispel). Now Pacheco went for a second [Go for the Throat](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Go+for+the+Throat), and that resolved, killing the [Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=+376301) threat.

Melamed cast [Sea Gate Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sea+Gate+Oracle), now holding six cards in hand. Pacheco too had a grip full of cards. Next up was [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer) from Melamed, which persuaded Pacheco to try [Black Sun's Zenith](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Black+Sun%27s+Zenith) to wipe the board. [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) was a teaser. Pacheco could pay, but then leave only two mana open. He elected to let his board sweeper hit the graveyard instead. Melamed cast [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin), not on an [Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=+376301) as normally happens, but on [Sea Gate Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sea+Gate+Oracle). That was a hideous engine to have to overcome, and virtually forced Pacheco to do something about it. [Into the Roil](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Into+the+Roil) was that something, which Melamed redirected to his [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite). [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) from Pacheco met [Spell Pierce](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Pierce), and the [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) resolved. Advantage Melamed. [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) reappeared too.

Now Pacheco had [Doom Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doom+Blade), causing Melamed to get some value by casting [Into the Roil](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Into+the+Roil) with Kicker on his own [Sea Gate Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sea+Gate+Oracle). He was still six cards to three up.

*Elsewhere, Igor Sousa had taken his opener against Allison Abe. Although Abe had played a cagey game, having used [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe) to see early on that Sousa had a lot of removal, in the end Sousa always had one more removal available than Abe could avoid, and Sousa led 1-0.*

***Igor Sousa 1 - 0 Allison Abe*** 

 ![Consume the Meek](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Consume%20the%20Meek&options=)

Back at Melamed v Pacheco, and Pacheco had landed his first [Grave Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grave+Titan) of the match. Melamed cast [Sea Gate Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sea+Gate+Oracle) once more, a second [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite), and a second [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer). He didn't have an answer to [Grave Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grave+Titan), however. Pacheco attacked Melamed down to five before casting [Solemn Simulacrum](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Solemn+Simulacrum). For Melamed, at least [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer) was shooting down Zombies, but Melamed would need something more. [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) attached itself to [Sea Gate Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sea+Gate+Oracle), and it was back to the Titan Pacheco.

This time he had [Consume the Meek](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Consume+the+Meek). Melamed responded by activating his [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) to see deeper into his deck, but there were no answers. The board was swept away, leaving [Grave Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grave+Titan) to waltz in unopposed to claim the opener.

Jonathan Melamed 0 - 1 Marcus Pacheco

*In Miguel v Camargo, Luiz Miguel had gone for his [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) combo on turn four, but Marcus Camargo was ready with [Nature's Claim](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nature%27s+Claim). Camargo followed up with [Linvala, Keeper of Silence](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Linvala%2C+Keeper+of+Silence), which stops the combo, and Miguel couldn't find a way back. All square once more.*

***Luiz Miguel 1 - 1 Marcus Camargo*** 

Melamed opened game two with [Gitaxian Probe](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gitaxian+Probe). Pacheco didn't want him to see, so cast [Mental Misstep](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mental+Misstep). Melamed really wanted to look, so cast [Mental Misstep](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mental+Misstep) too! He found [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek), [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite), and four land. When Pacheco cast the [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek), he found [Spell Pierce](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Pierce) in the way, so couldn't get the same level of information that Melamed had. Pacheco cast [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite), leaving the way clear for Melamed to begin the Planeswalker battle with [Jace Beleren](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace+Beleren), who promptly drew Melamed a card.

[Despise](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Despise) finally got Pacheco a look at Melamed's hand: [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder), [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak), [Dispel](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dispel), and [Misty Rainforest](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Misty+Rainforest). Little changed over the next few turns, with [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) for Pacheco and [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer) for Melamed, who was nevertheless advantaged by the status quo thanks to his [Jace Beleren](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace+Beleren), which he had restocked to 3 loyalty. Pacheco tried for a Jace of his own, but [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) resolved. At end of turn, Melamed went for [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch). Was the Jace a mana-sapping fatal mistake from Pacheco?

He was down to three mana, and let the [Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=+376301) resolve. Melamed untapped. He drew an extra card with [Jace Beleren](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace+Beleren). Then he attacked with his [Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=+376301) and [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer). Pacheco fell to 16, and then Melamed passed the turn. [Jace Beleren](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace+Beleren) continued to draw him extra cards, pulling further ahead of Pacheco. Even so, the presence of [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) on the board was stopping Melamed from pulling the trigger on his [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin).

Pacheco cast [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek), and again the [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak) trap was set. If Pacheco paid, he would be back down to two mana. He declined to pay, laid a [Creeping Tar Pit](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Creeping+Tar+Pit), and passed back to Melamed. [Sea Gate Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sea+Gate+Oracle) resolved, as Melamed hit the ten mana mark. Jace drew him another card, and the same dance occurred again, [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) blocking [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch), and Pacheco taking one from [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer).

*On the back table, Igor Sousa took a 2-0 lead when he cast [Memoricide](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Memoricide) on Allison Abe, removing all the [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch)s from the deck.* 

***Igor Sousa 2 - 0 Allison Abe*** 

*Meanwhile Luiz Miguel was able to find his [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) combo to take a 2-1 lead over Marcus Camargo.*

***Luiz Miguel 2 - 1 Marcus Camargo*** 

Pacheco once again wanted [Inquisition of Kozilek](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Inquisition+of+Kozilek) to resolve, and this time it did, Pacheco taking [Dispel](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dispel) and finding all four copies of [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) in Melamed's hand. With [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite)[Shatter](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shatter)ed by Melamed, he was in great shape. He cast [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) on his [Sea Gate Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sea+Gate+Oracle), rather than his [Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=+376301), ready to bait Pacheco into a counterspell war. Pacheco responded with [Doom Blade](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doom+Blade), and the [Sea Gate Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sea+Gate+Oracle) was no more. Now Pacheco had three cards. Melamed couldn't cast [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) and pay for a [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak). If Pacheco didn't have [Mana Leak](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mana+Leak), or a way to kill the [Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=+376301) in response, the win was sitting there right before Melamed's eyes.

 ![Splinter Twin](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Splinter%20Twin&options=)

Intriguingly, Melamed reset Jace to three loyalty, getting both players a card, allowing Pacheco one extra card to have an answer. He cast [Shrine of Piercing Vision](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Shrine+of+Piercing+Vision), and passed, having avoided putting the possible win to the test. [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch) turned sideways for one damage, putting Pacheco to 14, and Melamed was done.

Finally Pacheco had a way to deal with Jace, casting one of his own. Melamed, now free of the constraints of a [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) across the table, used [Grim Lavamancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grim+Lavamancer) to drop Pacheco to 12 at end of turn. This time he went for the [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) onto [Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=+376301) combo. Pacheco had [Deprive](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deprive) to stop the combo. The [Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=+376301) attacked, and Pacheco was at 11. That said, Melamed was down to 12 himself...

Pacheco cast [Grave Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20110918000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grave+Titan), Melamed responding with two damage to the face, Pacheco at 9.
