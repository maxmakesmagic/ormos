This week marks Commander columnist Sean McKeown's last weekly Commander article over at StarCityGames, and he takes the opportunity to tackle a rather unloved tribe: Snakes! The *Kamigawa* block focused on the slithery tribe, and over time the creature type has slowly received more and more entries. Sean wraps everything nicely together in a deck-doctor package, pushing the tribal lord Seshiro the Anointed to its maximum potential!

This Commander deck sets up with some early Snakes, ideally ones that ramp your mana. Sakura-Tribe Elder, Sakura-Tribe Scout, Lotus Cobra, and a variety of other accelerators let the deck quickly surge up to six mana. Once the deck is there, it can drop Seshiro and recoup its cards while simultaneously smashing in for massive amounts of damage.

In addition to Seshiro, the deck also sports a vast supply of *Kamigawa* Snake legends. Sachi, Sakiko, Sasaya, Shisato, and Sosuke all add to the tribal fun, enhancing the tribal aspects of the deck.

Trying out a tribal Commander deck can be a lot of fun and adds yet another layer to the already varied format. If you're looking for inspiration, check out [Sean's article over on StarCityGames.com](http://www.starcitygames.com/magic/commander/23846_Dear_Azami_Snakes_On_A_Plane.html) or just take a gander at the decklist below!
