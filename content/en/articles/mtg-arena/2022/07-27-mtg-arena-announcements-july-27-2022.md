# Historic Anthology 6 and Explorer Anthology 1 Available Tomorrow

Tomorrow, July 28, both Historic Anthology 6 and Explorer Anthology 1 hit the *MTG Arena* Store! If you haven't yet, [check out all the cards in Historic Anthology 6](/en/articles/uncategorised/2022/07-27-bridge-your-way-to-new-historic-decks-with-historic-anthology-6) and [Explorer Anthology 1](/en/articles/uncategorised/2022/07-27-new-decks-and-new-play-options-with-explorer-anthology-1). These anthologies open up new deck-building opportunities in both the Historic and the new Explorer formats by adding powerful, iconic, and popular cards from across *Magic*'s history.

These anthology bundles will be available only from July 28 until October 5, 2022, so grab yours today.

# Arena Open This Weekend!

[The Arena Open happens this weekend](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/07-20-july-2022-arena-open-alchemy-horizons-baldurs-gate-limited), July 30–31, with big prizes—up to $2,500 and Qualifier Weekend invitations—plus a new structure for Day 2 events. In past Limited Arena Open events, players who made it to Day 2 would draft a single deck and then play up to nine full matches with it. Although Day 2 is expected to be an intense competition, this many games with a single draft deck made the experience more of a repetitive grind.

To give Day 2 players more potential variety throughout the day, we are changing the structure of this month's Arena Open Day 2. Learn more in [the July 20 *MTG Arena* Announcements](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/07-20-mtg-arena-announcements-july-20-2022#arenaopen) and on [The Arena Open page](/en/articles/mtg-arena/2022/07-20-july-2022-arena-open-alchemy-horizons-baldurs-gate-limited).

And don't forget, all participants will receive the Baldur's Gate Dragon sleeve!
