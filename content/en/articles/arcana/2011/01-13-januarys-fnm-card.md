It's Thursday, which means that tomorrow is Friday. And *that* means that tomorrow night is [Friday Night **Magic**](https://web.archive.org/web/20110113000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=events/magic/fnm)! Since we're now in January, there's a new card being given out at FNM. Check it!

![Everflowing Chalice](https://i.ormos.uk/63/76/6376743000ccee776b53b7ae2687bed389795d2100b531952da5ff3fec72058f.jpg)  

Each month a different foil card will be given out. The cards feature the FNM logo in the text box and many have original flavor text and updated wordings.
