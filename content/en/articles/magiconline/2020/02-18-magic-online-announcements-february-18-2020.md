## *Zendikar* Flashback Draft February 19–26

Land yourself some big wins during this week's *Zendikar* Flashback Draft running through February 26. In addition to the standard entry options of 10 Event Tickets or 100 Play Points, you can also enter this event with 2 Event Tickets plus 2 *Zendikar* boosters. This event is Phantom—the cards you draft will not be added to your collection.

Read more about this season's Alternative Play events [here](/en/articles/magiconline/2020/01-10-theros-beyond-death-magic-online-edition#alt-play).

* * *

## Pauper PTQ Saturday

On Saturday, February 22, there will be a Pauper PTQ at 9 a.m. PT! The entry fee is 1 Red PTQ Finals Token or 40 [MOCS Qualifier Points](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020). The winner qualifies for their choice of events in Series 2 of the [2020 Players Tour](https://magic.gg/players-tour) in May.

Learn more about Players Tour Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020#ptq).

* * *

## Limited PTQ Sunday

On Sunday, February 23, there will be a Limited PTQ at a special start time of 4 a.m. PT! The set will be *Theros Beyond Death*, and the event will cost 1 Red Limited PTQ Token. You can also join with 40 [MOCS Qualifier Points](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020). The winner qualifies for their choice of events in Series 2 of the [2020 Players Tour](https://magic.gg/players-tour) in May.

Learn more about Players Tour Qualifier events [here](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/mtgo-premier-play-2020#ptq).
