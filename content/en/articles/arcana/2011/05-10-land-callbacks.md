As you might remember from last weekend's *New Phyrexia* Prerelease (or from [this Arcana](https://web.archive.org/web/20110510000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/new-phyrexia-basic-lands-2011-04-18)), *New Phyrexia* has basic lands in it. What you might *not* have noticed is that half of them depict the scene inside the Phyrexianized core of Mirrodin, while half of them contain references to lands from *Mirrodin*. Observe!
