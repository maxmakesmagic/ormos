Welcome to the first **[Mistform Ultimus](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistform+Ultimus) Watch**. In this to-be-recurring sub-feature of Magic Arcana, we keep track of what creature types that auto-updating *Legions* legend has recently become, and in effect, what new creature types have recently been added to **Magic**.

But since this is the first occasion of [Mistform Ultimus](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistform+Ultimus) Watch, you may want to do a little background reading on Mr. Ultimus.

*   Want to read Aaron Forsythe's [development stories](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/af5) from when [Mistform Ultimus](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistform+Ultimus) was being created?
*   Want to read all of Rune Horvik's [rulings](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=mtgcom/satschoolarchive&term=mistform_ultimus) about the Ultimus?
*   Want to read Jay Moldenhauer-Salazar's [deck ideas](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/jm54) for [Mistform Ultimus](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistform+Ultimus)?
*   Want to read [player-submitted poetry and stories](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtgcom/daily/af12) about the day the Ultimus became Human?

Okay, enough with the old; you're ready for the new. Today we focus on what the Ultimus has become as the result of *Mirrodin* block: the *Mirrodin*, *Darksteel* and *Fifth Dawn* sets.

*   *Mirrodin* adds **Archer** with [Tel-Jilad Archers](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tel-Jilad+Archers). It adds **Drone** with cards like [Looming Hoverguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Looming+Hoverguard). It adds the high-profile **Human** with eleven cards like [Disciple of the Vault](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disciple+of+the+Vault). The **[Alpha Myr](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?&name=Alpha%2BMyr)** creature type of course also busts onto the scene as of *Mirrodin*. Also new are **Rogue** from cards like [Neurok Spy](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Neurok+Spy) and **Shaman** from cards like [Viridian Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Viridian+Shaman). The creature types **Pentavite** and **Pest** arrive due to the token creature generators [Pentavus](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Pentavus) and [Nuisance Engine](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nuisance+Engine). **Slith** shows up on cards like [Slith Bloodletter](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slith+Bloodletter). Lastly, *Mirrodin* adds **Vedalken** from cards like [Vedalken Archmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vedalken+Archmage).

*   *Darksteel* adds **Artificer** with cards like [Slobad, Goblin Tinkerer](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Slobad%2C+Goblin+Tinkerer). It adds **Blinkmoth** from [Blinkmoth Nexus](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Blinkmoth+Nexus) and **Twin** from [Gemini Engine](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gemini+Engine). Lastly, *Darksteel* brings us **Wirefly** from [Wirefly Hive](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wirefly+Hive).

*   *Fifth Dawn* adds **Bringer** to the Ultimus's virtual type line with cards like [Bringer of the White Dawn](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Bringer+of+the+White+Dawn), and **Pincher** due to [Summoning Station](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Summoning+Station).

That's all for this block. The next time we'll catch up with [Mistform Ultimus](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mistform+Ultimus) Watch is in October, after the release of *Champions of Kamigawa*!
