You make the Duel Deck!

Wizards of the Coast is going to release a *Duel Decks* product on **Magic Online** called "*Mirrodin Pure vs. New Phyrexia*." As you might remember, one of those two is the name of "Action," the third set in *Scars of Mirrodin* block. But this isn't about that. It's about your chance to submit a deck list that will be used in *Duel Decks: Mirrodin Pure vs. New Phyrexia*.

The full details are available [in the contest announcement](https://web.archive.org/web/20110208000000/http://community.wizards.com/go/thread/view/75846/26875685/mirrodin_pure_vs._new_phyrexia_duel_decks_contest), but here are the most important details:

*   You can submit up to one deck for each side.
*   Each deck must be 60 cards.
*   The *Mirrodin Pure* deck will be based on Platinum Angel and the *New Phyrexia* deck will be based on Phyrexian Plaguelord. These are the only mythic rares allowed.
*   No opposite-faction cards are allowed.
*   Each deck should have 4-8 rare cards and 20-28 land cards.
*   Include a 250-word explanation of the deck.

The deadline is 11:59 p.m. (PST) on February 13, 2011. Go read the [full details](https://web.archive.org/web/20110208000000/http://community.wizards.com/go/thread/view/75846/26875685/mirrodin_pure_vs._new_phyrexia_duel_decks_contest) and [official rules](http://www.wizards.com/company/downloads/mtgo_build_a_deck_contest.pdf) and get to work!
