Learn more about Grand Prix Santiago's Top 8 competitors by taking a look through their profiles!

 

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/t8_caceres.jpg)Rodrigo A López Cáceres**

**Age, Hometown, Occupation**: 29 / Santiago

**Previous Accomplishments:** : Top 10 Worlds 2013

**Where do you normally play *Magic*?**: In “La Forja De Stone” and Magic Online.

**What was the best card in any of your decks this weekend, and why?**: [Somberwald Alpha](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Somberwald+Alpha). I played him in Swiss Rounds and in the first draft of Day 2. It won me a lot of rounds.

**What's your favorite *Magic* card in *Origins*?**: [Topan Freeblade](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Topan+Freeblade). I didn't play it in Swiss or either draft, but it's a common that attacks and defends very well.

**What was the most interesting play you made so far this weekend?**: Not playing a land so that my opponent thought I was mana-screwed. This let me [Languish](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Languish) for more creatures. I lost seven life by that play, but it was worth it.

**Age, Hometown, Occupation**: 30 / Santiago / Filmmaker

**Previous Accomplishments:** Second place at a couple of local pre-releases.

**Where do you normally play *Magic*?**: Magic Online.

**What was the best card in any of your decks this weekend, and why?**: [Molten Vortex](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Molten+Vortex), it finishes games out of nowhere.

**What's your favorite *Magic* card in *Origins*?**: [Separatist Voidmage](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Separatist+Voidmage).

**What was the most interesting play you made so far this weekend?**: Bouncing fatties all weekend long, super effective.

* * *

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/t8_flores.jpg)Christian Flores**

**Age, Hometown, Occupation**: 31 / Santiago / Freelance

**Previous Accomplishments:** : 2 WMCQ Top 8

**Where do you normally play *Magic*?**: At Magic Sur Chile.

**What was the best card in any of your decks this weekend, and why?**: [Chandra's Ignition](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Chandra%27s+Ignition). It's kinda unfair.

**What's your favorite *Magic* card in *Origins*?**: [Nissa, Vastwood Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%2C+Vastwood+Seer).

**What was the most interesting play you made so far this weekend?**: End of my turn, destroy my opponent's [Hangarback Walker](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hangarback+Walker). On my turn, I sacrifice all my creatures to make my [Nantuko Husk](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nantuko+Husk) an 8/8 and then cast Chandra's Ignition to clean the board and attack for lethal.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpsan15/t8_ochoa.jpg)Claudio Barrientos Ochoa**

**Age, Hometown, Occupation**: 30 / Both in the north and south of Chile!

**Previous Accomplishments:** : GP Sao Paulo 2015 Top 8, GP Santiago 2014 Top 64, GP Buenos Aires 2014 Top 128, GP San Diego 2015 Top 100

**Where do you normally play *Magic*?**: Local stores in the north of Chile, and I test with my brother and friends, but mostly I test online.

**What was the best card in any of your decks this weekend, and why?**: I think [Sentinel of the Eternal Watch](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sentinel+of+the+Eternal+Watch), wom me 3 rounds of today's first draft by itself.

**What's your favorite *Magic* card in *Origins*?**: [Nissa, Vastwood Seer](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nissa%2C+Vastwood+Seer) and [Abbot of Keral Keep](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abbot+of+Keral+Keep).

**What was the most interesting play you made so far this weekend?**: Play a [Flameshadow Conjuring](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Flameshadow+Conjuring) and then copy a [Woodland Bellower](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Woodland+Bellower) - for two creatures, one of them a [Managorger Hydra](https://web.archive.org/web/20150830000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Managorger+Hydra) … in Sealed!
