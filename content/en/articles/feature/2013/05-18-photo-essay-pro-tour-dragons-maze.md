Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze* wasn't the first Pro Tour in San Diego, and it probably wasn't the last. But like each one before it, what is Pro Tour looked like was unique. Thanks to the sharp eyes and quick lens of Craig Gibson, you too can see it.

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptdgm13/ptdgm1.jpg)  
*There's a reason we kept using "sunny San Diego" in our descriptions.* 

 

* * *

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptdgm13/ptdgm2.jpg)  
*We were the headline show at the downtown San Diego Concourse. It certainly drew its own crowd.* 

 

* * *

 

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptdgm13/ptdgm3.jpg)

![](http://media.wizards.com/legacy/mtg/images/daily/events/ptdgm13/ptdgm4.jpg)
