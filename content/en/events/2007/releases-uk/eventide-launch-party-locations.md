*Updated 2008-07-03* 

### Join the Party!

Bring your friends to a *EventideTM* Launch Party near you. Play, trade, hang out, have fun, and win cool prizes\*.

### When

Parties will be held Friday, July 25th or Saturday, July 26th or Sunday, July 27th. 

### Play Structure/Format/Product Mix

*   The Release Tournament is sanctioned by the DCITM.
*   The structure for the Release Tournament is Sealed Deck. Should be ran modified Swiss-style. Each player receives one *Shadowmoor* Tournament Pack and two *Eventide* Boosters to build a 40-card minimum deck.
*   The structure for League play is Open Duelling. Each player receives one *Eventide* Theme Deck (at random).

### Entry Fee

*   League Play – The entry fee should include the cost of operations and prize product.
*   Release Tournament – The entry fee should include the cost of operations and prize product.

### Prizes

*   **Door Prizes**: *Eventide* Life Counters will be given out randomly through the day (while supplies last).
*   **League Play**: Each participant receives one special foil promo card (while supplies last, card to be announced). The top-four players with the most wins + losses at the end of league play each win a *Eventide* Deckbox.
*   **Release Tournament**: Each participant receives one special foil promo card (while supplies last, card to be announced). The top-four finishers each win a *Eventide* Deckbox.

Check with your local Launch Party location for specific entry fee, and start time information.

\* With event participation. While supplies last.
