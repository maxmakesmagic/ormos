In case you don't click around to our product pages, this is just a reminder that there are *Onslaught* wallpapers and screensavers available for download from our [*Onslaught* Extra Features](https://web.archive.org/web/20021003000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/products/onslaughtextras) page.

There are wallpapers featuring art from [Doubtless One](https://web.archive.org/web/20021003000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Doubtless+One) and [Grinning Demon](https://web.archive.org/web/20021003000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grinning+Demon), plus screensavers showcasing art from several cards. [Go check 'em out!](https://web.archive.org/web/20021003000000/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/products/onslaughtextras)
