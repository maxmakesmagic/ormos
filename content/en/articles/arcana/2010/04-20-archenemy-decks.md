A*rchenemy* is a new way to play **Magic**, in which one player takes on the rest of the table. And when it goes on sale on **June 18**, it will (like *Planechase*) have some preconstructed **Magic** decks. And those decks .... could be described like *this*.

### Bring About the Undead Apocalypse

Reanimate zombie horrors, use big creatures to lethal advantage, and hunt down other Planeswalkers with your aberrant army of the undead.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e7/b2/e7b21ef149abe8d88e03a551aa5d58d892dfc5e1cd1e0a9be779ca7387d71905.jpg) 

  

### Assemble the Doomsday Machine

Use machinery incompreshensible to others to build a doomsday device. Its sole purpose: to destroy the world and any Planeswalker foolish enough to oppose you.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/af/23/af230bd1b63dcf80364bc43db3aa7bd1bc5bded5d2d94cdf69eedcb54577b92c.jpg) 

  

### Trample Civilization Underfoot

When you’re a Planeswalker possessed by a maniacal desire to obliterate civilization by unleashing the forces of nature upon the world, carnage can be the only result.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/29/d8/29d89de6ffbda8fa4210079cd0fb1e881d1e183bdfd0b1c688dfad503f7a8ea5.jpg) 

  

### Scorch the World with Dragonfire

When you use your force of dragons to rain down incendiary destruction upon lesser Planeswalkers, how can they hope to stop you?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/54/3b/543be73a49ec6a59e87ae850157a56e938d6d063ddf9225cbc3e3e00fbdbb9f3.jpg)
