**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpind15/t8_mackl.jpg)Name:** Valentin Mackl  
  
**Age:** 23  
  
**Hometown:** Vienna, Austria  
  
**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** It’s kind of my first GP  
  
**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Bant Megamorph, because I’m one of the few people who listens to Brad Nelson and Patrick Dickmann.  
  
**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc) (won me a lot of games)

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Disdainful Stroke](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Disdainful+Stroke) (makes the bad matchups okay).

**If you could change one card anywhere in your deck what would it be and why?**  
 Adding a second [Prairie Stream](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Prairie+Stream) since I manifested it once and needed to cast two Strokes in one turn.

**What’s the best Halloween costume you’ve ever worn?**  
 I was a squirrel once.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpind15/t8_phelps.jpg)Name:** David Phelps

**Age:** 17

**Hometown:** Saint Charles, IL

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Top 16 at the SCG Open last week.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Bant [Hardened Scales](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hardened+Scales). Green is the game.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Hardened Scales](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hardened+Scales). It enables most of the cards in the deck.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Evolutionary Leap](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Evolutionary+Leap). It carries hard matchups.

**If you could change one card anywhere in your deck what would it be and why?**  
 Change a [Surge of Righteousness](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Surge+of+Righteousness) in the sideboard to another [Evolutionary Leap](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Evolutionary+Leap).

**What’s the best Halloween costume you’ve ever worn?**  
 Donkey Kong.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpind15/t8_perez.jpg)Name:** Ray “FuturePro” Perez, Jr.

**Age:** 25

**Hometown:** Lansing, MI

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** 2013-14 Rookie of the Year; Top 16 at Pro Tour *Theros.*  
  
**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Esper Tokens. \_FAB\_ got second in the *Magic Online* Championship Series with it and once I played it I found out it was insane. The deck has a great Dark Jeskai matchup.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc). It applies so much pressure while having two great bodies to block.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Dragonlord Silumgar](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dragonlord+Silumgar). It steals all the things.

**If you could change one card anywhere in your deck what would it be and why?**  
 I would add another [Wingmate Roc](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wingmate+Roc) main deck or sideboard because fliers are insane.

**What’s the best Halloween costume you’ve ever worn?**  
 I once dressed up normally with a spray bottle of water. When someone asked me what I was, I responded by spraying myself and saying I drowned.

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpind15/t8_kirkwood.jpg)Name:** Scott Kirkwood

**Age:** 21

**Hometown:** Hoffman Estates, IL

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:** Day 2 at Grand Prix Madison

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Red-Green Landfall. It’s a dog to Dark Jeskai but has great matchups against the other top decks while staying consistent with 24 lands and [Den Protector](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Den+Protector)s.

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?** [Den Protector](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Den+Protector). It provides a lot of value while also being a great aggressive 2-drop when combined with pump spells. The amount of games I won with it is insane.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?** I have a special place in my heart for [Rending Volley](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Rending+Volley) for [Mantis Rider](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mantis+Rider)s.

**If you could change one card anywhere in your deck what would it be and why?** I’d probably cut the [Roast](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Roast)s. I brought them in for Tasigur out of Dark Jeskai and for Abzan but I found that I never needed them against Abzan and there are better cards to bring in for Dark Jeskai.

**What’s the best Halloween costume you’ve ever worn?** A bunch of costumes pieced together to have my costume be “a bad Halloween costume.”

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/events/gpind15/t8_chapin.jpg)Name:** Patrick Chapin

**Age:** 34

**Hometown:** Denver, CO

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 Hall of Fame, two-time Worlds finalist, five Pro Tour Top 8s, Pro Tour *Journey into Nyx* champion.

**What did you play this weekend, and why?**  
 Esper Control – [Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time) plus Jace are the two best cards and this is the only deck that seemed good against Dark Jeskai. Team Ultra Pro told me to…

**What was the best card in your main deck, and why?**  
[Dig Through Time](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Dig+Through+Time). It costs less mana than it should, which I assume was a design/development decision.

**What was the best card out of your sideboard, and why?**  
[Monastery Mentor](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Monastery+Mentor). It’s great when people take out some of their removal. It’s great against opponents that go big with Eldrazi or wide with tokens. It’s particularly valuable for avoiding draws.

**If you could change one card anywhere in your deck what would it be and why?**  
 Maybe [Infinite Obliteration](https://web.archive.org/web/20151101000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Infinite+Obliteration), I think another Mentor would have been better.

**What’s the best Halloween costume you’ve ever worn?**  
 I was Frankenstein’s monster as a kid. I fell and bust my head open at breakfast and went to the emergency room and got stitches for it. I won “most realistic costume.”
