After fifteen rounds of Standard, only eight competitors could continue on. Meet your GP Milwaukee Top 8 players!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmil18/sullivan.jpg)

**Name:** Adrian Sullivan

**Hometown:** Madison, WI

**Occupation:** Communications Strategy Ad Consulting

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**

2-time Wisconsin State Champ

Top 8 PT *Dragons of Tarkir*

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Jeskai Control

**What was your best card this weekend, and why?**

Niv-Mizzet

**What was the most exciting play you made this weekend?**

Explosion for 17 off of [Spell Swindle](https://web.archive.org/web/20181118000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spell+Swindle) on [Carnage Tyrant](https://web.archive.org/web/20181118000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Carnage+Tyrant)

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

A long-awaited return to the PT.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmil18/hernandez.jpg)

**Name:** Adam Hernandez

**Hometown:** Canton, MI

**Occupation:** IT

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**

Top 32 SCG Open

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

GB Midrange, because mono-red had a bad Jeskai matchup, and I didn't like URor Jeskai in this field.

**What was your best card this weekend, and why?**

Either the 24th land or [Llanowar Elves](https://web.archive.org/web/20181118000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Llanowar+Elves). Having more keepable hands and consistent access to mana is excellent.

**What was the most exciting play you made this weekend?**

Casting down [Adanto Vanguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20181118000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Adanto+Vanguard) in response to an activation in order to outrace WW in game three.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

FIRST PT INVITE

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmil18/tilk.jpg)

**Name:** Jake "FirstFNM" Tilk

**Hometown:** Grosse Ile, MI

**Occupation:** Twitch streamer (twitch.tv/firstfnm) and student

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**

Way too many GP Top 16s, Pro Tour Day 2 competitor, back-to-back 5k champion.

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Mono-White Aggro. Ian Barber and Slater Claudel told me it was great.

**What was your best card this weekend, and why?**

[Adanto Vanguard](https://web.archive.org/web/20181118000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Adanto+Vanguard), it's unstoppable

**What was the most exciting play you made this weekend?**

I got to draw the best I did all weekend in my feature match!

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

Everything. I've worked so hard for this. I want to thank everyone for supporting me, you're all wonderful. Bryan Carey, Nate Elkus, Anthony Sayre.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmil18/krut.jpg)

**Name:** Isaak Krut

**Hometown:** Kazan, Russia/Toronto, ON

**Occupation:** student

**Previous *Magic* Accomplishments:**

GP Montreal 2018 Top 8

**What deck did you play this weekend, and why?**

Jeskai Control. It seems to have good matchups against most other top archetypes.

**What was your best card this weekend, and why?**

[Deafening Clarion](https://web.archive.org/web/20181118000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deafening+Clarion). I drew it every time I needed one.

**What was the most exciting play you made this weekend?**

[Syncopate](https://web.archive.org/web/20181118000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Syncopate) opponent's Niv-Mizzet for zero to trigger my own Niv-Mizzet. Winning with Teferi -3 when I had no other win conditions left was nice too.

**What does this Top 8 mean to you?**

It takes me Bronze, that gives me two byes at future GPs.
