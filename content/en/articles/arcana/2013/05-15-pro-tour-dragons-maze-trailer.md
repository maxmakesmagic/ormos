Pro Tour *Dragon's Maze* is this weekend in San Diego, California. Hundreds of the world's greatest **Magic** players will come together to battle for prizes and to hoist the champion trophy on Sunday.

Nate Holt and Shawn Kornhauser of [Walking the Planes](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL3rP64NRtmbg0g-A_fwwpfaTpkmmwcwYl) put together this short trailer to get you excited for what's coming up. And if you missed it, read [Monday's preview](/en/articles/feature/2013/05-13-mazes-end-a-pro-tour-dragons-maze-preview) to this weekend's tournament by Rich Hagon.
