[![Tempest](https://i.ormos.uk/b1/6a/b16a66c64e69e82d4dd1bf247dcdf5986a398df4b21b2693bb06b00ab31e0dc8.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20081219000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/themeweek/tempest)  

If you were wondering why the [*Tempest* archive from last month](https://web.archive.org/web/20081219000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/feature/14) didn't include everything from Duelist #20, the answer is that we'd already run those articles six years earlier, along with articles by Mark Rosewater, Randy Buehler, Jay Moldenhauer-Salazar, Anthony Alongi, and Ben Bleiweiss. Take a hop in the time machine back to 2002 and read [*Tempest* Week](https://web.archive.org/web/20081219000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/Magazine/Article.aspx?x=mtg/daily/themeweek/tempest)!
