Chiba, Japan, which will host the 2010 **Magic** World Championships, prepared by participating in the [2009 *Zendikar* Game Day](/en/events/2009/zendikargameday/zendikar-game-day-2). Takagi Yui took an innovative take on green-blue to a solid finish. Nissa's Chosen (powered by Nissa Revane herself) helped hold the ground, allowing Yui plenty of time to set up powerful endgame spells like Sphinx of Lost Truths and Rite of Replication.
