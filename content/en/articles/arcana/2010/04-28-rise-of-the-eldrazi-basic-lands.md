You might have heard—especially if you're the suave, sophisticated sort who reads [**Magic** Arcana](/en/articles/arcana/2010/03-29-rise-of-the-eldrazi-swamps)—that each of the basic land types in *Rise of the Eldrazi* forms a mural when you line up all four different pictures. And if you're the sort who reads [Savor the Flavor](/en/articles/savortheflavor/2010/04-07-the-tale-of-tuktuk), you might already have seen the [Island](https://web.archive.org/web/20100428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Island) mural arranged into a fetching slider that looked something like this:

![Island mural](/flash/df3e0e3e851309814a471f1f8dff162757355675a60759be14fb0b778ecb4f0a)

You might even have wished you could see the other four basic land types similarly arranged. If so ... good news! Here we see the mysterious hedron structures stretching across the [Plains](https://web.archive.org/web/20100428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Plains) of Zendikar:

![Plains mural](/flash/71d0c5f9854e7725db764806559e354b177c58db20c2da66a98ed4e95926965b)

Here are the muck-encrusted cubes of the awakened [Swamp](https://web.archive.org/web/20100428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Swamp)s:

![Swamp mural](/flash/b4fc663d876ed1875e9ddd6323d762f58e21a39004fd817bfca3c35d7cd2773e)

The [Mountain](https://web.archive.org/web/20100428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mountain)s of Zendikar look even more ominous thanks to the "awakened" hedrons:

![Mountain mural](/flash/d9d7821efa7b69705a0898df61b43f21b7c8617fdc111d8a577c73608a4fc35a)

And, lastly, we see the [Forest](https://web.archive.org/web/20100428000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Forest)s stretching off into the distance:

![Forest mural](/flash/72e780f217c45358afa45b1b310add061c83a402d90b3b1855c7527d9edc8a9c)
