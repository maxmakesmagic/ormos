## THEROS

Theros is ruled by an awe-inspiring pantheon of gods. Mortals tremble before them, feel the sting of their petty whims, and live in terror of their wrath. It is a plane where barbaric, cave-dwelling minotaurs descend on wayward travelers. Giants stalk the land, drawing strength from the terrain on which they tread. At sea, massive krakens prowl its depths, and sirens lure its sailors to their demise. Yet amid such colossal perils, mortals have found a way to endure, and it is here that the hero's mantle is raised highest.

Not only have the mortals endured, they have managed to thrive, for Theros is also a plane where civilization is protected behind the walls of great poleis, each city-state a bulwark against the raging monsters that roam the more savage places of Theros. Meletis, the polis of learning, progress, magic, and devotion to the gods, is a testament to the achievements of civilized humanity. It is hemmed in by vast golden wheat fields and the Siren Sea, and defended by the Reverent Army. In the polis of Akros, martial prowess is held in the highest esteem. Akroan warriors have reached near-mythic status throughout Theros. The imposing cliff-top fortress of Akros lies at the center of a network of outposts that serve to protect the rest of Theros, a fact that comforts many Therans. Setessa, the isolated polis surrounded by concentric rings of forest, is fiercely independent. Strong bonds connect its inhabitants, and all in Setessa share a veneration of nature. 

## LEGENDARY CREATURES

### ATHREOS, GOD OF PASSAGE

When mortals die, their souls must cross the Rivers That Ring the World to reach the Underworld. The shrouded god Athreos is their guide, ferrying the dead to their ultimate rest. Silent, bent, and tattered, he patiently fulfills his role.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=380374)

### EPHARA, GOD OF THE POLIS

Ephara is the god of city-states and building. It is believed that her presence keeps a polis safe from beings of the wild; when a marble building or stone wall is erected, some representation of Ephara's face is almost always included.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=378517)

### EREBOS, GOD OF THE DEAD

Wielding a gold-handled whip, Erebos rules the underworld and all who suffer misfortune. His content with his own fate offers hope and guidance to all those who desire peace, yet fight their destiny.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=373589)

### HELIOD, GOD OF THE SUN

Wielding his sun spear, Heliod radiates light upon all of Theros. The embodiment of daylight, he oversees disputes, bringing truth and justice to all. But do not be deceived: Those who raise his ire are dealt with quickly and severely. 

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=373524)

### IROAS, GOD OF VICTORY

The beacon of glory in combat, Iroas takes the form of a muscular, centaur-like being. He governs both personal valor and bravery in battle, and thus he also governs warfare. He is twin to Mogis, god of slaughter, who commands the dark and brutal side of war, and the two spar constantly.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=380441)

### KARAMETRA, GOD OF HARVESTS

Karametra is the god of the harvest, hearth, and protection. She is a serene, wise god who values community, stability, and the balance of nature. As such, she is strongly associated with Setessa, where worship of her is strongest.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=378520)

### KERANOS, GOD OF STORMS

Keranos embodies the fury of the storm and the sudden blaze of epiphany. A god of little patience and less mercy, he dispenses insights and blasts of lightning in equal measure.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=380442)

### KRUPHIX, GOD OF HORIZONS

The most enigmatic of the gods, Kruphix has dominion over the potential, the distant, and the unseen. Thus, he is seen as an oracle of dreams. He also governs navigation, mystery, and the cycles of time.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=380446)

### MOGIS, GOD OF SLAUGHTER

Mogis, the dark twin of the god Iroas, is the horned god of wrath and pain. Both Mogis and his brother govern the domain of war. Sadism, revenge, malice, domination—these are Mogis's spoils, and he has no shortage of worshippers.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=378523)

### NYLEA, GOD OF THE HUNT

Wielding a legendary short bow, Nylea rules over the hunt and seasons of Theros. The most aloof of the gods, she spends much of her time in a calm olive orchard surrounded by peaceful solitude. While not primarily worshiped, any who hunt pay their respects, for fear of being hunted themselves.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=373559)

### PHARIKA, GOD OF AFFLICTION

Pharika is the keeper of apothecary knowledge, the source of dark magic. She is also the mother of all gorgons, and their snaky bodies resemble hers.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=380469)

### PHENAX, GOD OF DECEPTION

Phenax is the god of cheats and liars. He governs gambling, deception, betrayal, isolation, planning, and secrets. He is worshipped by criminals and others who wish to subvert the rules.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=378524)

### PURPHOROS, GOD OF THE FORGE

Wielding a massive hammer called Akmon, Purphoros oversees chaos and destruction as well as the craftsmanship of unique creations. Swift to anger and often frustrated by the limitations of his mortal followers, he will destroy a masterwork, if for no other reason then to inspire advancement.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=373556)

### THASSA, GOD OF THE SEA

Wielding her legendary bident, Thassa rules the sea and all secret knowledge that has been lost to it. Any who travels by ship—no matter how brief a journey—must make an offering or risk her displeasure. Though she often appears as a triton, Thassa does not favor them over mortals or other creatures.

[VIEW CARD](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=373535)

## POLEIS

### AKROS

The polis of Akros is carved into a mountain. Here, the fiercest of Theros live. These warriors prove themselves unparalleled, generation after generation. Most of them diligently travel the countryside, ensuring the safety of the land’s roadways.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=373555)

### MELETIS

Scholars and those who wish to better understand Theros gather in Meletis. Filled with temples to every god, Meletis is the civilized sister to its Akroan and Setessan counterparts. Its well-trained standing force, the Reverent Army, is rumored to augment brute force with spells.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&set=%7c%5b%22Theros%22%5d%7c%5b%22Born+of+the+Gods%22%5d%7c%5b%22Journey+into+Nyx%22%5d&name=+%5bmeletis%5d)

### SETESSA

On the edge of Nistos Forest is the matriarchal polis of Setessa. Populated primarily by women, the safety of Setessa and her children is valued above all else, including territorial conquest or even personal advancement.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?sort=color+&output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&set=%7C[%22Theros%22]%7C[%22Born%20of%20the%20Gods%22]%7C[%22Journey%20into%20Nyx%22]&name=%20[setessan])

## RACES

### CENTAURS

The centaurs have formed two distinct bands. The Lagonnas are merchant travelers who peaceful trade and seek to live in harmony with those around them. The Pheres are wandering nomads who raid for what they need and hunt wherever they venture.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&set=%7c%5b%22Theros%22%5d%7c%5b%22Born+of+the+Gods%22%5d%7c%5b%22Journey+into+Nyx%22%5d&subtype=+%5b%22Centaur%22%5d)

### GODS

Powerful and immortal, the gods of Theros exist to rule their domains and watch over the mortals who worship them. The gods live within the starfield of Nyx, which accompanies them (in part) when they travel Theros.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?sort=abc+&output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&subtype=%20[%22God%22]&set=%7C[%22Theros%22]%7C[%22Born%20of%20the%20Gods%22]%7C[%22Journey%20into%20Nyx%22])

### MERFOLK (TRITONS)

Tritons revere Thassa and resent the humans, who defile their seas by fishing and sailing. That’s why they’ve resorted to raiding the costal regions. Able to spend days out of water, merfolk simply need to keep their gills moist to prevent permanent damage from the atmosphere.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&set=%7c%5b%22Theros%22%5d%7c%5b%22Born+of+the+Gods%22%5d%7c%5b%22Journey+into+Nyx%22%5d&subtype=+%5b%22Merfolk%22%5d)

### MINOTAURS

Although they are among Theros's races, for all intents and purposes they are monsters, seeking only mayhem and meat, killing each other when they're not killing humanoids of other races. Brutish and barely sentient, minotaurs do not revere any gods. Their divine guides are destruction, brutality, and blood—all in the name of conquest and fun. 

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&set=%7c%5b%22Theros%22%5d%7c%5b%22Born+of+the+Gods%22%5d%7c%5b%22Journey+into+Nyx%22%5d&subtype=+%5b%22Minotaur%22%5d)

### NYXBORN

Created by the gods, nyxborn are a wild assortment of creatures that all contain the essence of Nyx within. They don’t require sustenance and spend their lives carrying out the wishes of the gods who made them. Nyxborn are identifiable by the same starfield appearance that the gods cast in shadow.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&set=%7c%5b%22Theros%22%5d%7c%5b%22Born+of+the+Gods%22%5d%7c%5b%22Journey+into+Nyx%22%5d&type=+%5b%22Creature%22%5d+%5b%22Enchantment%22%5d+!%5b%22Legendary%22%5d)

### SATYRS

Always in good spirits and eager to celebrate, satyrs welcome all to their festivities… as long as guests have no desire to leave. Since they see life ending with a journey to the underworld, satyrs choose to spend every second alive in a wild jubilation, needing no other reason then being alive.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&set=%7c%5b%22Theros%22%5d%7c%5b%22Born+of+the+Gods%22%5d%7c%5b%22Journey+into+Nyx%22%5d&subtype=+%5b%22Satyr%22%5d)

### ZOMBIES (RETURNED)

On Theros, anyone who dies goes to the Underworld. But not all stay there. Those who return have found a way to cheat Erebos and escape. In masks of gold, they return to Theros with all their old skills, yet no memory of who they once were.

[VIEW CARDS](https://web.archive.org/web/20160717011037/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&sort=color+&action=advanced&set=%7c%5b%22Theros%22%5d%7c%5b%22Born+of+the+Gods%22%5d%7c%5b%22Journey+into+Nyx%22%5d&subtype=+%5b%22Zombie%22%5d)
