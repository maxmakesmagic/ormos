While both of those Grand Prix were Constructed, the Pro Tour *Gatecrash* PTQ season is *Return to Ravnica* Limited—Sealed and Draft. Find a PTQ near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#gtc).
