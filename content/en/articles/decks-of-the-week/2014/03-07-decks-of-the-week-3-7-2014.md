What a great couple weeks it's been for Christian Seibold, who not only Top 8ed [Pro Tour *Born of the Gods*](https://web.archive.org/web/20140307000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/mclaren-weaves-victory-valencia) on February 23, but last weekend he and the Gräfensteiner brothers, Daniel and Tobias, took down Team Limited [Grand Prix Barcelona](https://web.archive.org/web/20140307000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/bold-band-brothers-best-barcelona). Across the globe, more than 900 players descended on Melbourne to compete in the [largest-ever Grand Prix in Australia](https://web.archive.org/web/20140307000000/https://magic.wizards.com/en/events/coverage/nam-annihilates-melbourne), which was ultimately won by Nam Sung-Wook. Congratulations, Christian, Daniel, Tobias, and Nam! The format for GP Melbourne was Standard, the same format as the Pro Tour *Journey into Nyx* Qualifier season, which ends this weekend. Find out if one of the last PTQs for the season is near you with [this handy link](/en/articles/event-coverage/2012/02-24-pro-tour-qualifier-list#jou). Good luck; have fun!
