Do you like strong creatures? How about lifegain? Or a little combo? Perhaps you just like to play offbeat decks?

If you answered yes to any of those questions, then I have the deck for you today—Ken Yukuhiro's [Pro Tour](/en/articles/event-coverage/undated/pro-tour-khans-of-tarkir-coverage-events) deck.

[![](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Sidisi,%20Brood%20Tyrant)](https://web.archive.org/web/20141024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sidisi,%20Brood%20Tyrant)

While it can follow the well-beaten Abzan path of using mana creatures to pull out a [Siege Rhino](https://web.archive.org/web/20141024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Siege+Rhino), it also breaks new ground by ramping into [Sidisi, Brood Tyrant](https://web.archive.org/web/20141024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sidisi%2C+Brood+Tyrant).

Sidisi is much more than a gold [Hill Giant](https://web.archive.org/web/20141024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hill+Giant). With enough creatures in the deck, she can act like a miniature [Grave Titan](https://web.archive.org/web/20141024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Grave+Titan), and she turns [Satyr Wayfinder](https://web.archive.org/web/20141024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Satyr+Wayfinder) into an amazing two-mana creature that not only gives you a land but makes 2/2 Zombies.

But that's not the only gimmick Yukuhiro thought of. What would happen if Sidisi or the Wayfinder were to turn over a [Soul of Theros](https://web.archive.org/web/20141024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Soul+of+Theros)? You know, the one that gives everything +2+2, first strike, and lifelink?

Do you like decks that turn [Hill Giant](https://web.archive.org/web/20141024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hill+Giant)s into [Baneslayer Angel](https://web.archive.org/web/20141024000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Baneslayer+Angel)s?
