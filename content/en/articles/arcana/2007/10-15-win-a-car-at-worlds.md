Wizards of the Coast is giving away more than a few trophies at the 2007 World Championships this year—we're giving away a car!

That's right, a new 2007 Toyota FJ Cruiser will be awarded to one **Magic** player in the "Win a Car" tournament, to be held Sunday, December 9 in an invitation-only Standard tournament. How can you win the car, you ask? There are two ways to qualify:

**1\. Public Events at Worlds**

There are nine qualifying events to be held at Worlds open to all players designated as qualifiers, with the top two finishers (or top two teams) of each earning spots in the "Win a Car" tournament. There are three events each day (Thursday, Friday, and Saturday) across multiple formats: *Lorwyn* Sealed, Two-Headed Giant *Lorwyn* Sealed, Legacy, and Standard. For a complete schedule of the 2007 Worlds Public Events, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20080622180618/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/worlds-07publicevents).

**2\. The Northeast Magic Challenge**

There will be 100 in-store qualifiers across the northeast United States on Sunday, November 18. These will be Standard format, Swiss with cut to Top 8, and the top two finishers at each challenge event qualify for a special Northeast Worlds Challenge qualifying event at Worlds. That Standard tournament will be held at Worlds on Friday, December 7 at 5 p.m., and the top 8 finishers qualify for the "Win a Car" tournament. Check the World Championships information page for a complete location list of the Northeast Magic Challenge coming soon. Contact individual tournament organizers for more information and entry fee.

So make your plans to be at the [2007 **Magic** World Championships](https://web.archive.org/web/20080506190240/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/worlds) at the Javits Center in New York City on December 6-9 for the biggest **Magic** event of the year!

For complete information about the "Win a Car" tournament, [click here](https://web.archive.org/web/20080422233615/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=events/magic/worlds-07winacar)

See you at Worlds!
