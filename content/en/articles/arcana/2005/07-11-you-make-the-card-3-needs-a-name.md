The card has a type and mana cost, so this week it's the *name submission* step for You Make the Card 3! Behold the card, and then [give us your name submission](/en/articles/youmakethecard3/2005/07-11-you-make-the-card-3-step-6-name-submission)! After all the submissions are in, you'll be able to vote your favorite candidate onto the card.
