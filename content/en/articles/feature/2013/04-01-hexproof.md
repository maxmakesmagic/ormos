Before *Gatecrash* came out, Bant Hexproof was one of the major contenders in Standard. Despite expectations that it could get better with the addition of [Breeding Pool](https://web.archive.org/web/20130401000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Breeding+Pool), the post-*Gatecrash* Standard metagame has largely left the deck behind. 

![Gift of Orzhova](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?type=card&name=Gift+of+Orzhova)

**Magic Online**'s \_Slippery\_ has not forgotten about the deck and has found new tools for it from *Gatecrash* outside of the dual lands, in the form of [Gift of Orzhova](https://web.archive.org/web/20130401000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gift+of+Orzhova) and [Simic Charm](https://web.archive.org/web/20130401000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Simic+Charm) (and [Hands of Binding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130401000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hands+of+Binding) in the sideboard). Outside of those additions, the strategy is the same as it used to be—play a hexproof creature and make it huge and evasive—fortunately, [Invisible Stalker](https://web.archive.org/web/20130401000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Invisible+Stalker) is already evasive, and [Geist of Saint Traft](https://web.archive.org/web/20130401000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Geist+of+Saint+Traft) is already huge, in its way, so that's not as hard as it might seem. 

Despite the improved mana, \_Slippery\_ still uses [Abundant Growth](https://web.archive.org/web/20130401000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Abundant+Growth) because of its synergy with [Ethereal Armor](https://web.archive.org/web/20130401000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ethereal+Armor). 

This deck's primary weakness in the new Standard metagame has been an inability to consistently race the aggressive decks that were never interested in targeting its creatures to begin with, and the prevalence of [Supreme Verdict](https://web.archive.org/web/20130401000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Supreme+Verdict) and sacrifice effects from control decks. [Gift of Orzhova](https://web.archive.org/web/20130401000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gift+of+Orzhova) is an excellent card at winning these races against aggressive decks, and \_Slippery\_ has [Fog](https://web.archive.org/web/20130401000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fog), [Hands of Binding](https://web.archive.org/web/20130401000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hands+of+Binding), and [Nearheath Pilgrim](https://web.archive.org/web/20130401000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Nearheath+Pilgrim) in the sideboard to further swing these tight races. 

Control decks are at a relative low, so this deck could be a great choice.
