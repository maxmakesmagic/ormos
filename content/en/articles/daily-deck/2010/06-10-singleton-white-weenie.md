Building a deck that is 100 cards and features no more than a single copy of each card in it save for basic land is a tall order for some, but Magic Online user Vazdru had no problems piloting this White Weenie list to the Top 5 of a recent Singleton Challenge. The deck is devoid of mana for a second color of spells, meaning Vazdru found the 100 cards he or she wanted to play within just the color white. Neat!
