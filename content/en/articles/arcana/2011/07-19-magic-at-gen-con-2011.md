Gen Con 2011 is going to be held August 4-7, 2011 in Indianapolis, IN. And a lot of **Magic** is going to go on inside Gen Con. Let's take a look!

### Public Events

There will be booster drafts of ***Magic** 2012* or *Scars of Mirrodin* Block available at all times starting at 9:00 AM Thursday. In addition, there will be "Win-A-Box" tournaments in Standard, Legacy, and Vintage. They're called that because the prize for winning is a box of 36 booster packs of ***Magic** 2012*!

There are other **Magic** events going constantly throughout Gen Con. A quick look at [the Gen Con Public Event schedule](https://web.archive.org/web/20110719000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=events/magic/gencon2012) will answer all questions!

### Championships! Championships! Championships!

That's three championships, right? Friday has the [Vintage Championships](https://web.archive.org/web/20110719000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=events/magic/vintagechamps) and Saturday has the [Legacy Championship](https://web.archive.org/web/20110719000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/TCG/Events.aspx?x=events/magic/legacychamps), and the whole weekend has the 2011 [US National Championships](https://web.archive.org/web/20110719000000/http://archive.wizards.com/magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/nationals/unitedstates)!

And that doesn't even include the 2011 Block Championship! Or the 2011 Midwest Masters Series Championship! Or all the Mini-Master tournaments! Or the Grand Melee! Look, maybe you should just look at the schedule, because there's a lot there.

*   **[The 2011 Gen Con Public Event schedule](https://web.archive.org/web/20110719000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=events/magic/gencon2012)**

Oh! One more thing! There are a lot of **Magic Online** events planned, too! Again, the [event schedule](https://web.archive.org/web/20110719000000/http://archive.wizards.com/Magic/tcg/events.aspx?x=events/magic/gencon2012#3) has all the information.

### *Magic: The Gathering Commander* Panel

Want to learn more about ***Magic: The Gathering** Commander*? See how this panel grabs you:

**Saturday Noon – 2pm. Marriott Indiana Ballroom G**

> Join members of the ***Magic: The Gathering** Commander* design team and the mtgcommander.net rules committee for a Q&A about the latest multiplayer Magic product. Panelists include Ken Nagle (Commander Design Team Lead), Mark Gottlieb (Commander Design Team), Sheldon Menery (mtgcommander.net Rules Committee), and Scott Larabee (Commander Design Team, mtgcommander.net Rules Committee).

### Spell Slinging

In addition to all that, Mark Gottlieb, Ken Nagle and Sheldon Menery will be on-site to play Commander and other formats against *you!* Go get 'em!
