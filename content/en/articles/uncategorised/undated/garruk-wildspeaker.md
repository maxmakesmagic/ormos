Congratulations, you've found Garruk Wildspeaker. 

As part of a special *Conflux* promotion, find all five hidden ![](http://www.wizards.com/mtg/images/multiverse/planeswalkers/5cylx6tlm2.gif) symbols on the **Magic** web site, each corresponding to a specific planeswalker.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b5/90/b590edd8ca15cffcde3551fb2a8d05ed00cbe3428a9ad95612fa0c8bc991f6e3.jpg) 

  

Print one of these forms, write down the names of all five hidden planeswalkers in the lines provided, and bring it to [a participating store](/en/articles/uncategorised/undated/participating-store-list) by February 28th, 2009 to receive a free planeswalker spindown counter!\* 

![](http://www.wizards.com/mtg/images/multiverse/planeswalkers/jy7t3wvdlh.jpg)
