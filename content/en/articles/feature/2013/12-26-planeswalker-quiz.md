**2\. Six of a Kind** - There are six Planeswalkers with \[0\] cost abilities. Among them, what color isn't represented?  
  
*Difficulty: Medium*

[White](#) - WRONG Nope, both [Gideon Jura](https://web.archive.org/web/20131226000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon+Jura) and [Gideon, Champion of Justice](https://web.archive.org/web/20131226000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Gideon%2C+Champion+of+Justice) have zero activation abilities.

[Blue](#) - WRONG Sorry, not here either. [Jace, Memory Adept](https://web.archive.org/web/20131226000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+Memory+Adept) mills a player for ten cards and of course [Jace, the Mind Sculptor](https://web.archive.org/web/20131226000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Jace%2C+the+Mind+Sculptor) allows you to [Brainstorm](https://web.archive.org/web/20131226000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Brainstorm) for zero.

[Black](#) - WRONG [Sarkhan the Mad](https://web.archive.org/web/20131226000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sarkhan+the+Mad) reveals the top card for zero.

[Red](#) - WRONG [Sarkhan the Mad](https://web.archive.org/web/20131226000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sarkhan+the+Mad) reveals the top card for zero.

[Green](#) - WRONG [Garruk Relentless](https://web.archive.org/web/20131226000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Relentless) has only zero activation abilities.

[None](#) - Correct! [Sarkhan the Mad](https://web.archive.org/web/20131226000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sarkhan+the+Mad) covers red and black, [Garruk Relentless](https://web.archive.org/web/20131226000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Garruk+Relentless) is green, and then Gideon and Jace are white and blue respectively.
