</li>&#10;      <p>Compared to drafting with the full <i>Ravnica</i> block, building a Sealed Deck is easy. With the guilds spread out through the packs as they are, some draft combinations come together earlier than others. For instance, if you end up drafting a blue/green/white deck, you'll get some hot, hot Selesnya loving in pack one, but will be left wanting until pack three, where you'll suddenly be flush with Simic and Azorius goodness. A black/green/white deck will most likely be filled out by the time you're finished with pack two, and pack three with have but a garnish for your Selesnya/Golgari/Orzhov salad. </p>&#10;      <p>&#10;        </p><table>&#10;          <tbody><tr>&#10;            <td>&#10;              <div>&#10;                <img src="https://i.ormos.uk/e4/71/e471a67a58b6dd64650a475ad8a9884092ccceb33a1771dd5c91267189d1ae7a.jpg">&#10;              </div>&#10;              <div>&#10;                <i>Quentin Martin secretly helps Tsumura build his deck with his telepathic super powers.</i>&#10;              </div>&#10;            </td>&#10;          </tr>&#10;        </tbody></table>Three people managed to pull through Day One with eight wins and no loses to their names, and the biggest name of the three was none other than Kenji Tsumura. Tsumura snapped up the Player of the Year title last year through his tireless roaming of the globe, beating the stuffing out of people in Magic tournaments, and he's in a prime position to do it again here Sunday.<p></p>&#10;      <p>As I stood behind Tsumura, I noticed that Eugene Levin was seated a mere two seats downstream, and had a pleading &quot;watch me, watch me!&quot; look in his eyes. Well it was that, or his latte kicking in. Levin was in seventh place at the end of day one with his great deck (with Quentin Martin at 14th, maybe both players were right about the <a href="/en/events/2006/gpkl06/day-1-blog-archive#4">quality of their decks</a>) and was also in great position to make a run for the playoffs himself. </p>&#10;      <p>Tsumura was rewarded with a Bottle Cloister in his opening pack, and was soon in black for <a>Keening Banshee</a> and <a>Dark Confidant</a>. Levin whipped out a first-pick <a>Galvanic Arc</a>, and matched it in Golgari with a <a>Civic Wayfinder</a> and both the <a>Golgari Rotwurm</a> and Rot Farm. Tsumura branched into blue for a fifth-pick <a>Szadek, Lord of Secrets</a>, which I guess he would hope never to reveal with the Confidant. </p>&#10;      <p>In pack two, both players ventured into Red; Gruul for Levin, and Izzet for Tsumura. Levin was clearly happy to see a fifth-pick <a>Ghor-Clan Savage</a>, and barely managed to stay in his chair when he got another one sixth. After a first-pick Debtors' Knell, Tsumura seemed less happy with how things were going.
