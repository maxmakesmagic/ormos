Welcome to March 2014! Let's take a look ahead and see what FNM Promo you can earn if you hit your local game store this Friday, as well as a peek further into the future to see what is coming down the pipe.

March 2014

Introduced in ***Magic** 2014* here is [Banisher Priest](https://web.archive.org/web/20140303000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Banisher+Priest). It was a new spin on the [Oblivion Ring](https://web.archive.org/web/20140303000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Oblivion+Ring) effect.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b4/21/b421cd7fa5ef4c36edf87c2aa7de905ee8a3d5bde6354630dc25b09d5d5b082b.jpg)  

April 2014

Next is [Encroaching Wastes](https://web.archive.org/web/20140303000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Encroaching+Wastes), also introduced in ***Magic** 2014*. It's a rarely seen land destruction land, while not quite on the power level of [Strip Mine](https://web.archive.org/web/20140303000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Strip+Mine) or [Wasteland](https://web.archive.org/web/20140303000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland) it provided an easy to get land for players who enjoyed that strategy to use.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/b6/67/b6675636216cb01f96aabf43735259f3b5f0465b18e3d9bc0f2ae7395f508706.jpg)  

May 2014

Now for the new FNM Promo which you haven't seen before! It is the *Theros* card [Tormented Hero](https://web.archive.org/web/20140303000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tormented+Hero)!
