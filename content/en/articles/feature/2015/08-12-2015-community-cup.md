The Community Cup is coming back this year, taking place Sept. 19–20. Today we want to introduce you to our two teams. We will be releasing further information about the event, the formats, and more in the coming weeks.

This year, you might have noticed there wasn't a community vote to choose these guys and gals. Boo, right? But here's the thing. This list is chock full of awesome people, and after holding the Community Cup for years, we wanted to get some new and awesome blood in here to lose terribly to challenge the Wizards team. After we started brainstorming people to put on the list, we looked at our initial pass and thought "Wouldn't this be an awesome group to bring in?" And they are an awesome group. So we're bringing them in.

Without further ado, the Community Team:

**Kenji Egashira**

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/FEAT20150812CommCup_Kenji.jpg)**

Kenji hails from Lynnwood, Washington, and has been playing *Magic* for over sixteen years. He returns to the Community team for the second time. You can follow him on Twitter at [@NumottheNummy](http://www.twitter.com/NumottheNummy) and watch his [Twitch](http://www.twitch.tv/numotthenummy) streams.

> * * *

**Joel Larsson**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/FEAT20150812CommCup_Joel.jpg)

Joel is the Pro Tour *Magic* *Origins* Champion and a Platinum pro. Follow him on Twitter at [@JoelLarsson1991](http://www.twitter.com/JoelLarsson1991).

> * * *

**Meghan Wolff** and **Maria Bartholdi**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/FEAT20150812CommCup_Meghan-Maria.png)

Hosts of the [*Magic* the Amateuring](http://www.magictheamateuring.com/) podcast from Minneapolis! Both of these wonderful ladies will be part of our Community team. Follow them on Twitter [@MtACast](http://www.twitter.com/MtACast).

> * * *

**Gabe Reale**

**![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/FEAT20150812CommCup_Gabe.jpg)**

Also known as "SGDoc," Reale is a law student in New York City. He started playing *Magic* back with *Mirage*, and these days you can catch him streaming *Magic* on his [Twitch channel](http://www.twitch.tv/sgdoc). Follow his Twitter at [@MtgoDoc](http://www.twitter.com/MtgoDoc).

> * * *

**Jimmy Wong**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/FEAT20150812CommCup_Jimmy.jpg)

Living in LA, Jimmy Wong is a YouTuber and member of the [Command Zone podcast](http://www.rocketjump.com/podcasts#thecommandzone). He started slinging spells back around *Ice Age*. Check out his [channel](https://www.youtube.com/user/jimmy) and his [Twitter](http://www.twitter.com/jfwong).

> * * *

**Adam Styborski**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/FEAT20150812CommCup_Adam.jpg)

Command Tower author, [Gathering*Magic*](http://www.gatheringmagic.com/) content manager, and coverage writer. We are excited to have "The Stybs" joining the Community team this year. Follow him on Twitter [@the\_stybs](http://www.twitter.com/the_stybs).

> * * *

**Anthony D'Aconto** aka **"Wedge"**

![](http://media.wizards.com/2015/images/daily/FEAT20150812CommCup_Anthony.jpg)

Host of [The Mana Source](https://www.youtube.com/user/TheManaSource) on YouTube and on Twitch.

*   Worth Wollpert—Director of Digital Product Management, *Magic Online*

*   Mike Turian—Digital Product Manager, *Magic Online*

*   Ryan Spain—Design Manager, *Magic* R&D

*   Alli Medwin—Editor, *Magic* R&D

*   Robert Schuster—Game Designer, *Magic* R&D

*   Melissa DeTora—Associate Designer, *Magic* R&D

Look for more about the event and the competitors in the coming weeks! Until then, mark your calendars for Sept. 19–20 to tune in and watch!
