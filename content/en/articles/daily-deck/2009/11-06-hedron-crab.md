The global phenomenon of **Magic** Game Day has come and gone for 2009. In the British Isles, Glenn Goldsworthy opted for a different path to victory: decking the opponent. Using Hedron Crab, Mind Funeral, and Archive Trap,cards quickly spin from the top of the opponent's library to the graveyard. Angelsong and Day of Judgment keep the ground protected long enough to finish the job!
