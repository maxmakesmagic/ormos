What an awesome week last week was. Five days of **Magic** from Amsterdam, the Netherlands, culminating on Sunday with the crowning of Team France as the World **Magic** Cup Champion, and Shahar Shenhar as the new World Champion.

![](http://staging.wizards.com/mtg/images/daily/events/wmc13/trophy.jpg) 

  
 ![](http://media.wizards.com/images/magic/daily/arcana/arc1291_craiggibson.jpg)

At Pro Tour events we bring in a professional photographer named Craig Gibson (pictured to the right). He's been working with us for several years now and I wanted to take today's Arcana as a chance to highlight some of his awesome photos.

Here are only a handful of the hundreds of photos he took over the course of the week. But be sure to look through the official event coverage and maybe take a little more notice of the awesome photos he took over the course of the event.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/71/d4/71d48c218e570bbf018b00f901d1445ac66f331fc4fe4f8bef036724e03160c3.jpg) 

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/21/ea/21ea0c3aca22c12d032000c58934393d1282aa9bca8cf3db4d7457450c15e2b9.jpg) 

*Sometimes Craig adds subtle effects to his photos, such as this look at Brian Kibler with slightly blurred hands to show his constant card shuffling.* 

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/06/9e/069e260bddd3fcdf36183fe20004151844c2b9344c63b185ac5940fb49bf66fa.jpg) 

*Other times he doesn't need any effects and captures the deep thought moments by players such as Josh "Wrapter" Utter-Leyton* 

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/32/22/3222ac63288a050425c3f0bc9d26a2216c80ac8e3172bb895e27bca1ca319e82.jpg) 

*He scouts out good spots for photos that give unique perspectives, such as this top-down look at the World **Magic** Cup competition.* 

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/02/a2/02a2a8201c67bcec1b5c04fddac4bc8f0f27b0b1d6ed3848940a1f2b7005daf5.jpg) 

*He also captures the moments between players, such as here where two members of the Australian team confer strategy during their match.*
