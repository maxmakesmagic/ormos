Our last gunslinging deck list of the week comes from Dave Guskin, who used it at the *Scars of Mirrodin* Prerelease in Bellevue, Washington. The Wizards R&D member said, "It's a fun way to utilize Time Reversal!" How? Just generate lots of artifact mana before casting the powerful spell, then cast as many zero casting cost artifacts as you can afterwards! Bonus points if you can get Riddlesmith on the battlefield first! You can follow Dave on Twitter—@[DaveTron](http://www.twitter.com/DaveTron).
