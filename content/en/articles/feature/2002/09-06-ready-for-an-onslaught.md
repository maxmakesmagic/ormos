### Wizards Announces Online Release Date and New Play Formats

* * *

### *OnslaughtTM* Cards and New Play Formats Coming to *Magic® Online* November 1

*Onslaught,* the new **Magic: The Gathering®** expansion, will go live in ***Magic** Online* November 1. *Onslaught* cards will be available immediately in all casual-play formats and will be legal in all sanctioned tournaments within a few weeks. Sanctioned Limited play begins November 3 with *Onslaught* release tournaments beginning at noon Pacific Standard Time. The *Onslaught* set will rotate into Standard starting November 14.

With the release of the *Onslaught* set, Wizards of the Coast will introduce an exciting new play format for ***Magic** Online.* Extended play will be available November 1 in the **Casual Play** area and Sanctioned Extended play will begin November 14. 

For those new to **Magic**, the Extended format allows players to use card sets released over the past several years, while Standard play entails the current core set and expansion sets from the last two years--which can be a good route for players who haven't amassed an extensive collection or just prefer the newer blocks.

The *Onslaught* set will feature Tribal cards that mark the return of some of the most popular creature types in **Magic** history, including goblins and elves, among others. And stay tuned for details on the *Onslaught* set's new morph mechanic. In the meantime, check out [Randy Buehler's article](/en/articles/latestdevelopments/2002/09-06-online-rotations) on the new play formats coming to ***Magic** Online* with the release of *Onslaught!*
