After fifteen rounds of play, the Legacy field at Grand Prix Seattle has been narrowed down to just the Top 8. Below are the GP Seattle Legacy Top 8 profiles!

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpsea18-legacy/gpsea18l_keatonwood.jpg)

**Name:** Keaton Wood

**Hometown:** San Diego

**Occupation:** Student

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

Queuing for multiple RPTQs

11-4 at GP Vegas

**What deck are you playing and why?**

Miracles because I like cantrips and didn't want to play [Deathrite Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20180408000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathrite+Shaman).

**What was your best card this weekend and why?**

[Back to Basics](https://web.archive.org/web/20180408000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Back+to+Basics) because a bunch of decks can’t beat a resolved one.

**What do you enjoy most about Legacy?**

0-ing Jace.

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**

A dream come true.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpsea18-legacy/gpsea18l_walker.jpg)

**Name:** Noah Walker

**Twitter Handle:** @EsperNoah

**Hometown:** Amherst, MA

**Occupation:** Magic pro and Chipotle burrito roller

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

3 GP Top 8s

**What deck are you playing and why?**

Delver, it’s the best deck

**What was your best card this weekend and why?**

[Young Pyromancer](https://web.archive.org/web/20180408000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Young+Pyromancer), it’s so much value in the fair match-ups.

**What do you enjoy most about Legacy?**

The game play, games are so intricate and skill intensive.

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**

Points toward gold and some good $$$.

* * *

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpsea18-legacy/gpsea18l_keith.jpg)

**Name:** Miranda Keith

**Twitter Handle:** @StarMonkey42

**Hometown:** Seattle, WA

**Occupation:** Student

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

-

**What deck are you playing and why?**

Maverick. I knew going in that I was playing a Thalia deck, and decided on Maverick over Death & Taxes because I like its Delver match-up better. Also it’s just super fun.

**What was your best card this weekend and why?**

[Ramunap Excavator](https://web.archive.org/web/20180408000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ramunap+Excavator). I don’t think I lost a game when it resolved. Recurring [Wasteland](https://web.archive.org/web/20180408000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wasteland)s and Horizon Canopies is incredibly powerful!

**What do you enjoy most about Legacy?**

Everything. Legacy is basically the only format I play, and I really can’t say enough good things about it, so I’ll just say that Thalia is better here than anywhere else.

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**

A lot. I’ve definitely had dreams about it.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpsea18-legacy/gpsea18l_dezani.jpg)

**Name:** Jeremy Dezani

**Twitter Handle:** @jdezani

**Hometown:** Paris/Tokyo

**Occupation:** MTG pro player, Hareruya writer

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**

12 GP Top 8s

POY 2014

PT Theros Champion

**What deck are you playing and why?**

Sultai Leovold. I think the deck has a chance against everything. Feels like the old Jund Modern with [Deathrite Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20180408000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathrite+Shaman).

**What was your best card this weekend and why?**

[Deathrite Shaman](https://web.archive.org/web/20180408000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deathrite+Shaman), a Planeswalker for 1 mana.

**What do you enjoy most about Legacy?**

Fair matches are so complicated and interesting, and are super interactive most of the time.

**What would winning the Grand Prix mean to you?**

I always say Legacy is my worst format. In Limited I have a chance, but in Legacy, it would be crazy.
