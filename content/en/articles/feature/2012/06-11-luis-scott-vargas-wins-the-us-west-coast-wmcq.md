Congratulations to Luis Scott-Vargas for winning this weekend's World **Magic** Cup Qualifier in Oakland, California. He joins the US Captain Brian Kibler, and last week's WMCQ winner Alex Binek.

You can see Luis Scott-Vargas' deck tech with ChannelFireball.com's Tristan Gregson on [their YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nZEit3FZSKs).
