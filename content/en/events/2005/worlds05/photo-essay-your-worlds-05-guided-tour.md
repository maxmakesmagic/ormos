 Welcome to Worlds 2005, the 12th World Championships in the game's history. This is the second time Worlds has come to Japan. The first five Worlds events were held in the United States, but starting with1999 the event began traveling the globe, starting with Tokyo, Japan. Since then we've been to Brussels, Toronto, Sydney, Berlin, San Francisco, and now Worlds is back in the Land of the Rising Sun. We've come a long way from [Worlds '94](/en/articles/feature/2004/08-23-magics-first-world-championships), held in the Seattle Red Lion (really!). Modern Worlds events are visual spectacles, as you can see here just from the entryway that greets your arrival.
