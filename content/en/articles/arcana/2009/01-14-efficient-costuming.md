We're always impressed by people who [make costumes](/en/articles/feature/2008/11-10-form-of-the-vanguard) to look like **Magic** cards. And of course we're always on the lookout for things to do [with extra **Magic** cards](/en/articles/arcana/2008/04-29-more-menger).

Are you thinking what we're thinking? What if somehow someone could create a **Magic** costume *out of **Magic** cards*?

Hmm. What card would lend itself to that? Well... click here.

 ![Cardboard Carapace](https://i.ormos.uk/1c/2d/1c2d406657d1a99820d0475ba1c659bd2297b1c86f68a774dea07fcd5410cd2c.jpg) 

Yes! Perfect! Now all we have to do is—wait. What?

You'll never believe this, folks, but I've just been informed that someone at PAX 2008 beat us to it. Check it out!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/27/dd/27dda592950b69ac0ad611f7c441c649e52b63596f6fc08a2308788f85154c02.jpg) 

  

Incidentally, we're impressed that this convention costume seamlessly incorporates the PAX badge. Too many times, you see someone with an incredibly elaborate outfit with a plasting badge holder hanging off in an unseemly manner.
