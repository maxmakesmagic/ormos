Cruel Ultimatum was one of the most powerful cards to come out of *Shards of Alara*, and it's even seeing new life with *Rise of the Eldrazi*. Anthony Gabrielli took this take to the Top 8 of a PTQ for Amsterdam. Check out a full playset of Consuming Vapors, perfectly for munching the fatties in a deck like Mythic, and Consume the Meek, perfect for munching the *non*-fatties in a deck like Jund! Find a Pro Tour Qualifier near you on the [Amsterdam Qualifiers page](/en/events/2010/amsterdam10-qualifiers/pro-touramsterdam-2010-qualifiers).
