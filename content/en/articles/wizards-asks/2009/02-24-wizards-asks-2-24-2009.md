*What is your favorite win condition and what card do you love to use to get there?* 

![](https://i.ormos.uk/e0/0c/e00c2900e4532fed44efdbc0223f486ee1518c0cf594bb289867319eeda0f043.jpg)  

**Kelly Digges:** I already gave a shout-out to Overrun-type effects, which I absolutely love—especially with a nice big army of Saproling tokens. Other than the original, I like Tromp the Domains, Marton Stromgald, and especially Titanic Ultimatum.

Honorable mention goes to Mindslaver. Keep your Control Magics and Enslaves—I like to use my own cards to win—but I love the feeling of grabbing an opponent's hand and surveying just how much damage I can do in a turn. It's like ***Magic**: The Puzzling* in reverse. And in multiplayer, when "how much damage I can do" includes making my newfound minion attack my other enemies? Even better!
