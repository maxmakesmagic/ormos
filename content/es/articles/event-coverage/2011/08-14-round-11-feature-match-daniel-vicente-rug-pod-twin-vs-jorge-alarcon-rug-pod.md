Dicen que el umbral de puntos necesario para ingresar en el top8 está en 28, por lo que al ganador de este duelo le bastaría con un empate en su siguiente ronda para lograrlo. El perdedor se despide automáticamente de los viajes al mundial.

Jorge tiene en mesa [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) en su segundo turno gracias a los [Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise) que jugó en el primero. [Sea Gate Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sea+Gate+Oracle) se convierte en [Vengevine](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vengevine) por arte de magia, la cosa parece ir rápida. Daniel tiene en mesa [Overgrown Battlement](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overgrown+Battlement), lo que le ayuda a jugar [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch) para impedir a Jorge activar [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) a su gusto este turno.

Un nuevo [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) cae en mesa ese mismo turno, seguido de [Urabrask the Hidden](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Urabrask+the+Hidden) a la salud de [Vengevine](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Vengevine). En su siguiente turno, el quinto, Jorge comienza una cadena de sacrificios de criaturas y búsquedas en su baraja con [Lotus Cobra](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lotus+Cobra) – [Sea Gate Oracle](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sea+Gate+Oracle) – [Hero of Oxid Ridge](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero+of+Oxid+Ridge) – [Phyrexian Metamorph](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Metamorph).. Dani sólo puede girar una de las amenazas de su rival por medio de un segundo [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch). Demasiado arroz para tan poco pollo.

Daniel 0 – Jorge 1

 

### Game Two

 

Largo y tendido han estado ambos jugadores dándole vueltas a sus banquillos y cabezas. Al menos parece que las manos iniciales son bastante buenas.

[Birds of Paradise](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birds+of+Paradise) – Spellkite – [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder) frente a [Overgrown Battlement](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Overgrown+Battlement) en los dos primeros turnos. ¡Da gusto ver partidas dinámicas! Daniel toma ventaja jugando [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod), quien trae un [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch) consigo, pero Jorge rápidamente remueve la amenaza con [Tuktuk Scrapper](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Tuktuk+Scrapper). [Master Thief](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+Thief) convierte al Spellkite de Alarcón en un chaquetero de proporciones importantes.

Daniel se tira a la piscina, con el flotador que representa Spellkite, intentando 'combar' con [Splinter Twin](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Splinter+Twin) sobre su [Deceiver Exarch](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Deceiver+Exarch), pero Jorge tiene listo un [Combust](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Combust) para [Master Thief](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Master+Thief), lo que devuelve Spellkite a su legítimo dueño. Ahora Jorge podrá cambiar objetivos a su antojo.

Vicente busca soluciones con [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder), y parece encontrarlas con [Acidic Slime](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Acidic+Slime) – [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod) – Consacrated Sphinx. Jorge usa [Phantasmal Image](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phantasmal+Image) para copiar la esfinge y lo que acontece ahora es digno de mención. Ambos jugadores se enzarzan en un duelo de robos provocados por las esfinges de cada uno hasta que Jorge decide cesarlo. Daniel profundiza en su mazo con [Ponder](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Ponder) y [Preordain](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Preordain), pero parece no tener suerte.

Con 17 – 14 a favor de Daniel, Act of Agression toma prestada la Sphinx para restar ocho vidas por el aire, dejando la cosa en 9-10. La esfinge robada es ofrecida a los dioses del [Birthing Pod](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Birthing+Pod). [Thrun](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thrun), the last Troll, [Spellskite](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Spellskite) y [Solemn Simulacrum](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Solemn+Simulacrum) intentan en vano detener una ofensiva letal similar a la de la primera partida: [Hero of Oxid Ridge](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hero+of+Oxid+Ridge) – [Phyrexian Metamorph](https://web.archive.org/web/20110814000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Phyrexian+Metamorph).

Jorge Alarcón se encuentra tan solo un punto de conseguir una plaza en el Top8 del Campeonato de España 2011.

**Daniel 0 – Jorge 2**
