From the land of Japan comes Erika Sudou’s Top 8 Rampant Growth control deck. Featuring some of the game’s most powerful spells, the deck is able to power them onto the table early thanks to a triple set of both Rampant Growth and Fertile Ground. After using Broodmate Dragon, Primal Command, or Cloudthresher? Why, Vengeful Rebirth just allows Erika to reload and do it again!
