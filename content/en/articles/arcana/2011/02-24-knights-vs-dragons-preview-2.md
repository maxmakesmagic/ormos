There may have been some confusion about *Duel Decks: Knights vs. Dragons*, which we announced [last October](/en/articles/arcana/2010/10-11-announcing-duel-decks-knights-vs-dragons). Specifically, some people appear to believe it to be a joke, just because it's being released on April 1.

Allow us to be clear: this is a real product that just happens to be coming out on April 1. April 1 is the day we make jokes, not the day we make jokes *about*. Look, we even have a preview of the alternate art for Knight of the Reliquary!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/bc/a2/bca280fdfe16a2c2d8cd349e18cafd04cf70eddb039f4d9f38d2b2e593e4bac2.jpg) 

  

*Duel Decks: Knights vs. Dragons* will be available in English, French, Italian, German, and Spanish on April 1, 2011 with an MSRP of $19.99.
