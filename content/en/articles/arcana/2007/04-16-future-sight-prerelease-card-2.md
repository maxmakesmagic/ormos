Interested in *Future Sight* spoilers? Here's one: Dakkon Blackblade*loses* the famous soul-sucking sword, the one whose name he took as his own. 

 ![Dakkon Blackblade](http://gatherer.wizards.com/Handlers/Image.ashx?size=small&type=card&name=Dakkon%20Blackblade&options=) 

So who wields Blackblade after Dakkon loses it? This guy:

[](/en/events/2007/prereleases-fut/future-sight-worldwide-prerelease-fact-sheet)

[![Korlash, Heir to Blackblade](https://i.ormos.uk/5b/50/5b50d6508082fe48aadb82d8ff2b488132ec314b53e2b25023fcf795f97c34e2.jpg "Korlash, Heir to Blackblade")](/en/events/2007/prereleases-fut/future-sight-worldwide-prerelease-fact-sheet) 

[](/en/events/2007/prereleases-fut/future-sight-worldwide-prerelease-fact-sheet)

Blackblade can be yours next. Get your Korlash, Heir to Blackblade at a [*Future Sight* prerelease](/en/events/2007/prereleases-fut/future-sight-worldwide-prerelease-fact-sheet) just for showing up to play. In fact, trade for as many as you can—grandeur only makes Korlash grander. 

The prerelease card features alternate art of Korlash, Heir to Blackblade by Richard Kane-Ferguson, the original artist of Dakkon Blackblade.

Find *Future Sight* prerelease [locations near you](/en/events/2007/prereleases-fut/future-sight-worldwide-prerelease-fact-sheet).
