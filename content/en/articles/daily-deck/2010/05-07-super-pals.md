Looking for a fun new deck for your Friday Night **Magic** starring some of the new power players from *Rise of the Eldrazi*? Try Lewis Laskin's tournament-winning control strategy. He uses twelve planeswalkers, including Gideon Jura, to overwhelm his opponent's forces. Wall of Omens does blocking duty, while board-sweepers, spot removal, and Spreading Seas keep opponents off balance. It's a great new deck to try for your big tournament this week!
