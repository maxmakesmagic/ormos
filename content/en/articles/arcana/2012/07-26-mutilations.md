The card [Mutilate](https://web.archive.org/web/20120726000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=35194) made its appearance in *Torment*, where it looked like this.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/0f/2e/0f2ef955aab28ef860dab2d361555b0f07d453bfa3425c508bc7f282bc5bd0f7.jpg)

[Mutilate](https://web.archive.org/web/20120726000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=35194) | Art by [Eric Peterson](https://web.archive.org/web/20120726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?output=spoiler&method=visual&action=advanced&artist=%5B%22Eric+Peterson%22%5D)

Then it went away for a long time, because it was very powerful. It made a reappearance in *Duel Decks: Garruk vs. Liliana*, where it looked like this.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/1024_mutilate_dd.jpg)

[Mutilate](https://web.archive.org/web/20120726000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?multiverseid=201819) | Art by [Zoltan Boros & Gabor Szikszai](https://web.archive.org/web/20120726000000/http://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Search/Default.aspx?action=advanced&artist=+%5Bzoltan%5D+%5Bgabor%5D)

But since its first apperance in *Torment* back in 2002, [Mutilate](https://web.archive.org/web/20120726000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Mutilate) hasn't been in a regular set. Until ***Magic** 2013*! And now it looks like this.

![](https://media.magic.wizards.com/image_legacy_migration/images/magic/daily/arcana/1024_mutilate_m13.jpg)
