[![Core Set](https://i.ormos.uk/bc/5b/bc5bd12c90c3fca751c9af380294de6047a700e1c673b2369c8a168ba5798b2d.jpg "Core Set")](https://web.archive.org/web/20080424040324/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/expansion/9e)

R&D designs the Core Set (currently in its *[Ninth Edition](https://web.archive.org/web/20080424040324/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/expansion/9e)*) both as a learning tool for new **Magic** players and as a source of staple cards for both Constructed formats and casual play. But since it's packed with spells hailing from Alpha through (in the case of *Ninth*) *Betrayers of Kamigawa*, it's also a 350-card tour through the game's past. Take a look below at the art of some *Ninth Edition* cards (in no particular order), and enjoy a planeswalkers'-eye view of events in **Magic**'s history.

  

Furnace of Rath

The airship *Weatherlight* navigates the deadly Furnace of Rath...  
![Furnace of Rath](https://i.ormos.uk/c4/52/c452509940bd1a4ce426c4c67fb751cecf83720c24bdf8c5f6284605c754096f.jpg "Furnace of Rath")  
*Furnace of Rath art by John Matson*

  

Lumengrid Warden

A Neurok human overlooks the spires rising from Mirrodin's Quicksilver Sea...  
![Lumengrid Warden](https://i.ormos.uk/82/f1/82f1da2494238b2eda073461aa982444c0d4d88080aaff6dd30010ec72cd4412.jpg "Lumengrid Warden")  
*Lumengrid Warden art by Francis Tsai*

  

Mending Hands

A kitsune healer puts her Mending Hands to two samurai in Kamigawa...  
![Mending Hands](https://i.ormos.uk/c5/40/c540a6256c1db2b3123df1d3694a1bbf669b22da3bd1a13ee25d4f1d061ab0c1.jpg "Mending Hands")  
*Mending Hands art by Douglas Schuler*

  

Slay

Uncaring Phyrexian minions Slay a host of Llanowar elves...  
![Slay](https://i.ormos.uk/07/7e/077e605364408361e0849b3ca237726a816ec66fc1c56de6ea00d02064802b0a.jpg "Slay")  
*Slay art by Ben Thompson*

  

Biorhythm

In Otaria, Kamahl realizes his destiny lies with the rhythms of Nature...  
![Biorhythm](https://i.ormos.uk/4d/aa/4daa73780c50e53614d3a1d1fb3b7816167fb28abba7f9ad06f817cd03652144.jpg "Biorhythm")  
*Biorhythm art by Ron Spears*

The Core Set isn't just a source of Grizzly Bears and Flights for teaching your little brother a **Magic** lesson, and isn't just a source of Hypnotic Specters and Greater Goods for *teaching your older brother a lesson*; it's also a feast of storyline content drawn from all points in the timeline. No doubt the Core Set's *Tenth Edition* will showcase even more of the history of the **Magic** multiverse. After all, we've visited an entirely new [plane](https://web.archive.org/web/20080428162930/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=magic/expansion/rav) since *Ninth*...
