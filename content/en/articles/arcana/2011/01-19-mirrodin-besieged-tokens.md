As you might expect, there will be tokens in *Mirrodin Besieged*. And sure enough, token cards will appear in packs. Well, some packs, anyway. The ones that don't have Rules Tip cards. Look, the point is that if you open a *Mirrodin Besieged* pack, you could be faced with this happy little face:

  

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3b/f9/3bf94be32625090abd7e1c7195315cdd8a44ca3a9015e4a3fa02a51656e961d9.jpg) 

  

Okay, so what does that tell us about the set? Presumably that there are Thopters in it. Or at any rate, Thopter *creators*. Let's see the next one!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/18/4a/184a7e48ad945593b68e4937e90cffddd3dc656b44899f51d4cf12959f98b5f0.jpg) 

  

Ah! This is a fascinating specimen. Incapable of surviving on its own, the Germ token is normally seen in the company of a [living weapon](https://web.archive.org/web/20110611021630/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/mirrodinbesieged/mechanics#3) equipment. Next!

![](https://i.ormos.uk/7d/02/7d0293104d2b56546562b8848b23c0d801944436689f78c3344954ca53d84844.jpg) 

  

Yikes! This thing is scary! Well, maybe not if you just saw it on its own like this. But it's ordinarily seen right after a Phyrexian Rebirth has cleansed the battlefield of impurities. By which we mean "creatures."

![](https://i.ormos.uk/8c/68/8c688b8e0b38e0d3490b39e34bbdf72c911169d901bc0b50f2a111d45b4af94f.jpg) 

  

Phyrexian Rebirth appeared on the [*Mirrodin Besieged* Visual Spoiler](https://web.archive.org/web/20110529053521/http://www.wizards.com/magic/tcg/article.aspx?x=mtg/tcg/mirrodinbesieged/spoiler) today. That thing is *full* of fascinating cards! Anyway, let's move on.

![](https://i.ormos.uk/fe/03/fe039e8a3e5d477213a89ede5c1b131b0a052de16df57a1ca4f14c66e908bd9a.jpg) 

  

Right. Sure. A 2/2 Zombie. That makes sense. But surely we have a token sitting around that could let us close with a sense of mystery and anticipation? Something to suggest an unknown card?

![](https://i.ormos.uk/3f/c9/3fc97480074253ead1cb55e38c92a960d42f2aaa2db5158eb706d86e074549c5.jpg) 

  

That'll do nicely.
