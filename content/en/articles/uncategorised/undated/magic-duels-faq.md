## Magic Duels FAQ

# GENERAL QUESTIONS

 

**Q: What is *Magic Duels*?**  
**A:** *Magic Duels* is the best way to start playing the world's best strategy card game, *Magic: The Gathering*, offering multiple play modes, including offline solo play, story campaigns, 2-player battles, and Two-Headed Giant (2v2). *Magic Duels* is a free play experience, in which all gameplay content (including cards) is earnable.

**Q: What does free play mean?**  
**A:** It means that all of the content within the game, including cards, can be unlocked through gameplay. There is no "pay wall" in *Magic Duels*.

**Q: What platforms is *Magic Duels* available for?**  
**A:** *Magic Duels* is available for iOS, Xbox One, and Windows PC via Steam.

**Q: When will *Magic Duels* release on PlayStation 4?**  
**A:** Development of a PlayStation 4 version of *Magic Duels* is on hold as we work to provide the best possible experience for players on our current platforms. We are working toward a release on PlayStation 4 in 2016.

**Q: Where do I go for help?**  
**A:** Within the game go to Help & Options >> Help. Outside of the game simply contact the friendly members of our [Game Support](https://web.archive.org/web/20160628181753/http://company.wizards.com/contact/customer-support) team.

**Q: Who do I contact for help with achievements or trophies (if there are any)?**  
**A:** If it is an in-game achievement, please contact [Game Support](https://web.archive.org/web/20160628181753/http://company.wizards.com/contact/customer-support). If it is a console-specific achievement, please contact your console platform's support team.

**Q: I think I found a bug! What do I do?**  
**A:** Tell us about it! Help us to continue to improve *Magic Duels* by contacting our [Game Support](https://web.archive.org/web/20160628181753/http://company.wizards.com/contact/customer-support) team.

**Q: How do I get *Magic Duels*?**  
**A:** Just go here to play free now!

**Q: Will I be able to connect to players playing on different platforms?**  
**A:** Cross-platform multiplayer gameplay is not currently supported.

**Q: Do I need an Internet connection to play *Magic Duels*?**  
**A:** An Internet connection is not necessary to play *Magic Duels*, but it is strongly recommended. An Internet connection is required in order to play some of gameplay modes and for saving your game progress to the cloud. While playing offline, coins cannot be earned and gameplay is restricted to Solo Battles and deck building.

# iOS QUESTIONS

**Q: Nothing is happening when I try pressing a button/option in *Magic Duels* on my iPhone. What should I do?**  
**A:** Setting your device display to Standard instead of Zoom should correct any touch or display issues you experience with *Magic Duels*. To switch to Standard display mode, go to the Settings on your device, then select "Display & Brightness" and change the View option to Standard. If you are still experiencing issues after doing this, please contact our [Game Support](https://web.archive.org/web/20160628181753/http://company.wizards.com/contact/customer-support) Team. 

**Q: I am unable to connect to *Magic Duels* on my iPhone or iPad. What should I do?**  
**A:** Please try updating to the newest version of the game. If your connectivity issues persist, please contact our [Game Support](https://web.archive.org/web/20160628181753/http://company.wizards.com/contact/customer-support) Team. 

**Q: On which iOS devices is *Magic Duels* supported?**  
**A:** *Magic Duels* can be played on an iPad Mini 2 or newer, iPad Air or newer, and iPhone 5s or newer. 

**Q: Will I keep my game progress, card collection, decks, coins, etc. if I switch back and forth between my iPhone and iPad?**  
**A:** As long as you are logged in to the same Game Center account and are connected to the Internet on each device, your game progress and everything you've unlocked will be synchronized across devices. 

**Q: How do I set up Game Center?**  
**A:** If you need help getting started with Game Center on your iOS device please check here. 

**Q: The game seems to be running slowly on my iPhone or iPad. What can I do to speed things up?**  
**A:** If *Magic Duels* seems to a little slower than usual, we recommend closing any other open apps on your device. To do this, double-tap the Home button on the bottom-front of your device, then swipe up on any app to close it.  
**A:** Another tip is to turn off the Combat Animations in *Magic Duels*. To do this in-game, go to Help & Options >> Settings >> Gameplay and uncheck the "Combat Animations" option.

# MULTIPLAYER QUESTIONS

**Q: When I try to invite my friend to a match, one or both of us receive an "Unable to join game" or "Network error occurred" message. What should I do?**  
**A:** First, make sure you and your friend are both properly logged into *Magic Duels* and connected online. We are researching other factors that may cause network errors. We ask that if your issue is not resolved to contact our [Game Support](https://web.archive.org/web/20160628181753/http://company.wizards.com/contact/customer-support) Team. 

**Q: When I try to play Two-Headed Giant with a friend, they're put on the opposite team. What should I do?**  
**A:** This is a known issue that we are researching. For the time being, we recommend conceding your Two-Headed Giant game and trying to create a new match. 

**Q: What is Two-Headed Giant?**  
**A:** Two-Headed Giant is a 2v2 multiplayer mode in which you and your teammate work together to defeat an opposing team of two. Two-Headed Giant is a unique play mode in which you can take actions as a team instead of individually. 

**Q: How does the Two-Headed Giant multiplayer mode work?**  
**A:** When starting a Two-Headed Giant match, you choose the type of teammate you would like to play with: a player you've invited to join your team, a player selected for you at random, or an AI teammate. No matter which type of teammate you choose, you'll then face off against another randomly selected human/AI or AI/AI team. For PC and console players you can also be randomly paired against human/human teams. 

**Q: How do I play multiplayer matches against friends?**  
**A:** Under "Versus Battle," choose the option "Battle a Friend" and then select Continue. You will then be prompted with a friend invite list from which you can choose the friend you want to invite to your match.

# CARD/CONTENT QUESTIONS

**Q: I am unable to purchase the last pack needed to finish my collection in a set or my final purchased pack is not entering my collection. What should I do?**  
**A:** Please contact our [Game Support](https://web.archive.org/web/20160628181753/http://company.wizards.com/contact/customer-support) Team to help troubleshoot the issue. 

**Q: Does *Magic Duels* use the same cards as the *Magic Origins* paper card set?**  
**A:** Yes. The launch content for *Magic Duels* features a significant number of cards from the *Magic Origins* card set. 

**Q: Will additional downloadable content be available?**  
**A:** *Magic Duels* will have regular content updates that reflect Magic's latest paper card sets. 

**Q: Can I build and customize my own decks?**  
**A:** In *Magic Duels* you have complete freedom to create, customize, and save up to 30 decks. Newer players are encouraged to use the Deck Wizard feature, which provides 10 standard deck archetypes and step-by-step guidance through the deck building process. More experienced players may prefer using the Deck Builder feature, which allows complete deck building freedom. 

**Q: Where do I go to build a deck?**  
**A:** To build a deck, go to Battle Mode >> My Decks. You can then select the "Create New Deck" option. You may then use either the guided Deck Wizard or the free-form Deck Builder to build your deck. 

**Q: Can I use one of my own decks while playing Story mode?**  
**A:** Throughout Story mode you take on the role of five iconic Planeswalker characters while experiencing their individual origin stories. You won't need to prepare anything prior to playing Story mode. For each of the Planeswalkers' five story chapters (25 chapters in all), you are provided a pre-made deck of cards specifically built around the challenges at hand. 

**Q: Why does my deck keep changing in Story mode?**  
**A:** Throughout Story mode you take on the role of five iconic Planeswalker characters while experiencing their individual origin stories. For each of the Planeswalkers' five story chapters (25 chapters in all), you are provided a pre-made deck of cards specifically built around the challenges at hand. These pre-made Story decks cannot be modified. 

**Q: Are items available for purchase in *Magic Duels*?**  
**A:** In *Magic Duels*, players can earn in-game currency, called coins, through gameplay. Coins can also be purchased in the Store section of the game. Within the Store section of the game, coins can also be exchanged for game content, like 6-card boosters. 

**Q: What are boosters?**  
**A:** Within *Magic Duels* a booster refers to a pack of 6 cards. Boosters are sometimes given as rewards as part of the game, or you can exchange in-game currency, called coins, for boosters in the in-game Store. 

**Q: How do I get more boosters?**  
**A:** Boosters can be earned through gameplay, or you can exchange in-game currency, called coins, for boosters in the in-game Store. 

**Q: I didn't open a booster after earning/buying it. Where do I go to find it now?**  
**A:** If you have any unopened boosters, the next time you start playing *Magic Duels* you will begin the game on the booster opening screen where you can open your remaining boosters. 

**Q: What is the rarity ratio of cards within the in-game boosters?**  
**A:** The 6-card boosters in *Magic Duels* include commons, uncommons, and a rare card. With every booster you open, there is a chance that the rare card will be a mythic rare card instead. 

**Q: How do I get foil versions of cards?**  
**A:** You can use coins you have earned or purchased to turn your cards into foil versions. To turn an individual card into a foil version, go to your "Card Collection" from the main menu, then zoom in on the card you would like to foil. Once zoomed in you will see a small foil card icon with a coin next to it. Selecting that icon will give you the option to turn all copies of that card that you own (now or in the future) into foil versions.  
**A:** Alternatively, you can turn all of the cards in one of your decks into foil versions. To do so, go to Battle Mode >> My Decks, then select the deck you would like to foil. Once you have selected the deck to foil, choose the FOIL option and follow the on-screen prompts. NOTE: Once a card is turned into a foil version, all copies of that card that you own, currently or in the future, will be a foil version, even if that card is part of another deck you have created. Once a card is turned into a foil version it cannot be turned back into a regular version of that card.

# GAMEPLAY QUESTIONS

**Q: How do Daily Quests work?**  
**A:** Daily Quests are a great way for players to earn additional coins. They update once per day, and each day players can earn up to one new quest. Players can have a maximum of three active Daily Quests at any given time. If you complete all of your quests in a single day, at the next update you will receive a single new Daily Quest. If you experience any issues seeing new Daily Quests, please contact us here. 

**Q: How do Community Quests work?**  
**A:** Each week *Magic Duels* will feature a new Community Quest. Currently, Community Quests refresh on Wednesday evenings, UTC. If a player contributes to the completion of the community quest during that week, and the community as a whole reaches the threshold for completing the quest, that player will receive the quest's reward. Rewards are given when the Community Quest refreshes. Players whose first contribution to the quest occurs after the community has reached the threshold for completion will receive the reward. 

**Q: Do I have to know how to play *Magic: The Gathering* or other trading card games before playing *Magic Duels*?**  
**A:** Not at all. *Magic Duels* is ideal for gamers regardless of their familiarity with *Magic: The Gathering* or other trading card games. *Magic Duels* is the best way to start playing the world's best strategy card game. 

**Q: Will *Magic Duels* be challenging for experienced players?**  
**A:** Of course. *Magic Duels* features an advanced AI and multiple difficulty levels that can test the skills of even the most experienced Magic player. Along with play modes such as Two-Headed Giant, online multiplayer (including ranked matches), and Story Mode, there are also achievements, trophies, and the daily and community quests to keep your experience fresh. 

**Q: What game modes are available in *Magic Duels*?**  
**A:** The current game modes include Story Mode, Solo Battle Mode, Versus Battle Mode, and Two-Headed Giant. 

**Q: Do I have to go through the tutorial before I can play the rest of the game?**  
**A:** You will need to play through the first four Skill Quest tutorials, which should only take a few minutes. This allows you to experience the basics of gameplay. The remaining tutorials are optional. 

**Q: How do I gain access to all of the different game modes and content?**  
**A:** Story Mode - The first story, Gideon, is immediately available for you to play. The subsequent stories of the other Planeswalker characters are unlocked after you play at least one Solo Battle.  
**A:** Battle Mode - This section of the main menu contains Solo Battle, Versus Battle, Two-Headed Giant, and My Decks.  
**A:** Solo Battle - Solo Battle is immediately available for you to play. When you start your first Solo Battle, we provide you with a Starter Collection of cards and walk you through building your first deck. Completing a Solo Battle unlocks access to all other game modes.  
**A:** Multiplayer Modes - All multiplayer modes (Versus Battle and Two-Headed Giant) are unlocked after you complete at least one Solo Battle.  
**A:** Quests - Quests are available after completing your first Solo Battle. A new Quest is added each day and provides and interesting challenge for you to try to complete. 

**Q: Are there achievements or trophies and, if so, what type?**  
**A:** Yes, the availability of achievements or trophies varies by platform.

# Battle for Zendikar

**Q: How do I access the new *Battle for Zendikar* storyline?**  
**A:** You may unlock the new *Battle for Zendikar* storyline by first completing the Gideon Jura campaign and playing at least one game in Battle Mode. 

**Q: How do I access the new *Battle for Zendikar* Deck Wizard Archetype decks and new content from the Starter Box?**  
**A:** You may access the new content by completing the *Battle for Zendikar* campaign, or by using coins to purchase *Battle for Zendikar* booster packs. 

**Q: Are new booster packs available for purchase with the *Battle for Zendikar* update?**  
**A:** Yes! *Magic Origins* and *Battle for Zendikar* booster packs are now available for purchase in the *Magic Duels* Store. 

**Q: When will I be able to begin manually tapping lands for mana?**  
**A:** With the release of the *Battle for Zendikar* update, we have introduced expanded *Magic: The Gathering* mana pool functionality. This includes manual tapping of lands, creatures, and other permanents that tap to generate mana-features never before seen in the Duels franchise. 

**Q: I downloaded the *Battle for Zendikar* update, but I am still unable to connect to *Magic Duels* on my iPhone or iPad. What should I do?**  
**A:** Please contact our [Game Support](https://web.archive.org/web/20160628181753/http://company.wizards.com/contact/customer-support) Team for help troubleshooting your connection. 

**Q: I downloaded the *Battle for Zendikar* update and my account suddenly reverted to a starting account (I lost all my coins, cards, and story progress). What should I do?**  
**A:** Our [Game Support](https://web.archive.org/web/20160628181753/http://company.wizards.com/contact/customer-support) Team can assist you in recovering lost coins, cards, and progress.
