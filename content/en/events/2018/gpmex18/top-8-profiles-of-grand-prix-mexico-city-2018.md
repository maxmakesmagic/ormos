![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmex18/t8-grezes.jpg)**Name/Nombre:** Felix Grezes

**Age:** 30

**Occupation:** PhD student / College teacher

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 Pauper Standard World Champion

**What did you play in Sealed on Day 1? Why? What was your record?**

Dimir (splash [Hypothesizzle](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Hypothesizzle)) 7-2 (2 byes).

**What was your hardest choice building your Sealed deck?**

\- splashing Red.

**What did you Draft on Day 2? Why? What were your records?**

Boros 3-0  
 Izzet Combo (Gravitic Punch, [Sonic Assault](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Sonic+Assault), [Wee Dragonauts](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wee+Dragonauts), etc...) 2-1

**What card(s) surprised you this weekend, and why?**

[Lazav, the Multifarious](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lazav%2C+the+Multifarious) (from good to great)  
 Gravitic Punch (from bad to build-around).

**What does making this Top 8 mean to you?**

Making my first Pro Tour.

**What's your guild affiliation are you in real life, why? (Be honest)**

Gruul.

![](http://media.wizards.com/2018/events/gpmex18/t8-nettles.jpg)

**Name/Nombre:** Logan Nettles

**Age:** 30

**Occupation:** Magic

**Previous Magic Accomplishments:**  
 Grand Prix Los Angeles winner < 2 months ago

**What did you play in Sealed on Day 1? Why? What was your record?**

Izzet because the best cards in my pool were [Niv-Mizzet, Parun](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Niv-Mizzet%2C+Parun) and Expansion//Explosion. 7-2 record.

**What was your hardest choice building your Sealed deck?**

Whether to splash black for two removal spells with Niv-Mizzet in my deck. I did not and ususally boarded in black.

**What did you Draft on Day 2? Why? What were your records?**

3-0 Izzet because I saw a Blue-Red Guildmage pick three and I had two red cards.  
 2-0-1 Boros because I opened [Boros Challenger](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Boros+Challenger), [Izoni, Thousand-Eyed](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Izoni%2C+Thousand-Eyed), and Golgari Finebroker, so I took the standalone card and stuck to it.

**What card(s) surprised you this weekend, and why?**

Invert \[Invert//Invent\]. I attacked Vedalkan Mesmerist into [Wishcoin Crab](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Wishcoin+Crab) and inverted the crab and an 0/4 [Crackling Drake](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Crackling+Drake).

**What does making this Top 8 mean to you?**

One step closer to Gold and the bigtime.

**What’s your guild affiliation are you in real life, why? (Be honest)**  
  
 Golgari. [Thoughtseize](https://web.archive.org/web/20181007000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Thoughtseize), Goyf, Lily, gg.
