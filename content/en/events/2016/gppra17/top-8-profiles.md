**Occupation:** Student

**Hometown:** Vantaa, Finland

**What was the best card in your sealed deck, what color combination did you play, and what was your record?**

[Fabrication Module](https://web.archive.org/web/20170129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Fabrication+Module), blue-red, 3-0.

[Lifecraft Cavalry](https://web.archive.org/web/20170129000000/https://gatherer.wizards.com/Pages/Card/Details.aspx?name=Lifecraft+Cavalry), red-green, 2-1.
