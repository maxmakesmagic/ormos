In the *Invasion* block, cards with the kicker ability usually contained a part of the [Coalition Symbol](/en/articles/arcana/2002/02-14-kicker-sacrifice-land-2) in their artwork—usually the part associated with the color of the kicker.

Cards with colorless kickers, however, usually contain the entire symbol in their art. Below are close-ups of four cards from Invasion that have colorless kickers. The Coalition Symbol in the art has been pointed out, but do you know what the cards are? Answers below.

![Invasion colorless kicker](https://i.ormos.uk/c3/af/c3af33092053f27a72ff7e707178333381cf2de6dd123646d620ef0335c3a6e7.jpg)  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  

**Answers:** Ardent Soldier, Vodalian Serpent, Kavu Aggressor, Urborg Skeleton.
